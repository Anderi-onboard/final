Mark fields

Three reference compositions rebuilt from the forty-two phenomena in assets/marks.js. No motif was added or redrawn — only placement, scale, count and colour. Colour is on the live 114-group rotation, so what you see is one colourway of many.

A · all-over field — one motif, hundreds of times, and a rare opposite

Crowns are peak shingled up the slope; the pale verticals are bamboo at roughly one to every twenty-five crowns. One diagonal, off-centre, is the only large-scale event; everything above it is flat sky. Recession is chroma and value compression, not less drawing.

B · triptych — one window repeated, ABA

Outer plates: pine massed above, tide laid flat below, split at 42%. The birds are wave at 1% of the area and the highest value in the picture. The middle plate is the same scene at a different scale of attention — glow turned into shafts, star for the flare — and it is only legible because the two literal plates flank it.

C · figure and ground — one mass, cropped, against a flat sky

The mass is leaf at one scale, thousands of them, each turned to face out of the canopy; the shadow scallops are valley at four times that scale, so the inside repeats the silhouette's own language. Sky is one flat value with a single haze low and to the left.
