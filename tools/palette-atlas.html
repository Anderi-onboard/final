Every colour the

The site ships 180 colour groups of ten steps each — 1,800 hand-picked values. Almost none of them reach the screen as authored. Every colour passes through a clamp on its way to a CSS token, and the clamp is doing far more work than the catalogue is.

What the measurement found

90.6%

Ridge colours flattened

1,630 of 1,800 exceed the saturation cap and are set to exactly 0.26. The catalogue's mean saturation is 0.675; the screen's is 0.251.
100%

Skies at one lightness

Every group's sky is clamped to L 0.82 and S 0.14. Across all 180 groups the sky varies in hue only — never in value.
57%

Water clamped in value

40% hit the lightness floor of 0.42 and 17% the ceiling of 0.64, so the water band is nearly a constant tone.
45 min

Full cycle

180 groups at 15s each. Not the 900s the notes claim.

The pipeline

Four of the eleven fields in each catalogue entry are rendered. The rest — pattern, harmony, families, breaks — are metadata that no code reads.

1 · Source

color-groups.json

180 groups. Ten rows, a sky, a water, and a gem index are read; everything else is unused.

2 · Clamp

mutedHex(hex, maxL, maxS, minL)

Converts to HSL, clamps lightness and saturation, converts back. Hue is never touched — it is the only channel that survives intact.

3 · Tokens

--bw-palette-1 … -10, -cloud, -water, -gem

Rows land on ten ridge planes 400ms apart, each interpolating for 1.5s; water follows at 4.4s.

4 · Surfaces

fill · stroke 42–44% · water .68

Contours mix the same colour to 42–44%, so the line work is a lighter tint of its own plane rather than a separate ink.

Hue survives; nothing else does

Because the clamp never touches hue, the colour identity of a group is carried entirely by where it sits on the wheel. This is the distribution across all 1,800 ridge values — and it is the one axis with real range left in it.

Two things the notes get wrong

The play order is not alternating

CLAUDE.md §4 describes a sequence that alternates near-white and colour 1:1, with a clash bias that promotes high-contrast groups. The code does neither. buildPaletteSchedule sorts by tier and then by id, so the site sits in one tier for many minutes before stepping to the next.

The cycle is 2,700s, not 900s

180 groups at a 15s dwell. A visitor who stays five minutes sees twenty groups drawn from one tier — never the range the catalogue implies.

What this means if you want more colour

The catalogue is not the constraint. Raising the ridge saturation cap from 0.26 is the single change that would let the 180 groups read as 180 groups; 90.6% of the values are waiting behind it. The sky cap is the second: at one fixed lightness it can only ever shift hue, which is why the upper half of the screen looks the same in every group. Both were set to keep glare down, and both are one number.
