## 总则(Principles: the Subject, the Self and Other, yin and yang)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/婚姻/总则.md`

- **LY.MARRY.BSYG.01** 妻、妾和财是同一类，都看财爻。占婚姻和求财，这一爻都要旺相有气，不落空亡。
  Wife, concubine and wealth are the same kind, all read from the Wealth line. In divining a marriage or seeking wealth alike, this line must be strong and have qi, and must not fall Void.
- **LY.MARRY.BSZZ.01** 《易林补遗》一概拿内外卦、世应爻、阴阳财鬼来论夫妇，不管是谁在占、为谁占，这是大谬。王洪绪主张：世爻是我家、应爻是对方家；女人自己占嫁人，以官爻为丈夫，官世相生相合、官阳世阴才叫得地；男人自己占娶妻，以财爻为妻子，财世相生相合、财阴世阳才叫得地；父母尊长为子孙的婚事占，以子孙爻为用神，世上的子孙或阳象是我家的男孩，应上的子孙或阴象是对方的女孩；无冲破而有生合就可配、夫妻和谐，无生合而有冲克就夫妻不睦，休囚或受克就不长寿；为弟弟娶妻、妹妹出嫁占，都以兄弟爻为用神。
  The Yilin Buyi judges husband and wife by the inner and outer trigrams, the Self and Other lines, and yin, yang, Wealth and Pressure alike, without regard to who divines and for whom; this is a great mistake. Wang Hongxu holds: the Self line is my family and the Other line the other family; when a woman divines for herself about marrying, Pressure is the husband, and only if Pressure and Self generate and combine, with Pressure yang and Self yin, is it called being in place; when a man divines for himself about marrying, Wealth is the wife, and only if Wealth and Self generate and combine, with Wealth yin and Self yang, is it in place; when parents or elders divine the marriage of a child, Output is the Subject, with Output on Self or on a yang image the boy of my family and Output on Other or on a yin image the girl of the other family; with generation and combination and no clash or break they can be matched and are harmonious, with clash and control and no generation or combination they are at odds, and resting or controlled they will not live long; for a younger brother's marriage or a younger sister's, take the Peer line as the Subject.
- **LY.MARRY.DYTJ.18** 六爻全都安静的卦，要看用神和日辰：日辰克用神或者冲刑它，事情要谨慎；再推究世应，忌神千万不要加临，世应临用神和原神，做事一定昌盛。娶妻买妾、屋宅坟田、住庵谒人、失物理冤这些事，用爻宜静，有气为先。
  When all six lines of a hexagram are still, look at the Subject and the Day: if the Day controls, clashes with or punishes the Subject, the matter calls for caution; then examine Self and Other: the Adversary must on no account come upon them, and if Self and Other sit on the Subject and the Support spirit, the undertaking will certainly flourish. For taking a wife or buying a concubine, houses and graves, living in a hermitage or calling on people, lost property and righting a wrong, the Subject line should be still and having qi comes first.
- **LY.MARRY.DYTJ.20** 官用取官，私用取财。占病（鬼祟）、占失（看贼）、求官事、官司词讼、占婚姻问丈夫，这些都看官爻；占买卖、家事、奴婢事、求财事、婚姻事，这些都看财爻。
  For an official matter take Pressure, for a private matter take Wealth. Illness (haunting), loss (look for the thief), seeking office, lawsuits, and marriage when asking after the husband: all of these look at the Pressure line; trade, household affairs, servant matters, seeking wealth and marriage matters: all of these look at the Wealth line.
- **LY.MARRY.DYTJ.29** 占婚姻，忌财爻死绝；占生产，看阴阳。
  In a marriage divination, Wealth dead is disliked; in childbirth, look at yin and yang.
- **LY.MARRY.HDY.02** 占妻子看财爻，宜动；占丈夫要有鬼爻，宜静。
  To divine a wife look at the Wealth line, which should move; to divine a husband the Pressure line must be present and should be still.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.01** 夫妻要和谐，先看阴阳配不配：世属阳、应属阴，鬼（官）属阳、财属阴，内卦属阳、外卦属阴；各得其位，才是夫妇之道，日后有利。男家占，宜世阳应阴；女家占，宜世阴应阳。
  For a couple to be harmonious, first see whether yin and yang match: the Self is yang and the Other yin, Pressure is yang and Wealth yin, the inner trigram is yang and the outer yin; each in its place is the way of husband and wife, and later days will be favourable. When the man's family divines, the Self should be yang and the Other yin; when the woman's family divines, the Self should be yin and the Other yang.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.18** 财、鬼两爻是占婚姻的用神，逢空亡本不吉，但不可死守：男占女以财为主，鬼空不妨；女占男以鬼为主，财空不妨；要紧的是用神没有损害。财鬼空亡而干支相合、世应相生、阴阳得位，是真姻缘，只是半辈子同床、不能白头到老，那是天命，不是刑克；逢冲遇克叫避空，反而不用担心。长辈占子侄、兄占弟，就看子孙爻、兄弟爻，不能一律用财官。
  The Wealth and Pressure lines are the Subject in a marriage divination and Void on them is by nature unlucky, but it must not be followed rigidly: when a man divines about a woman Wealth is the main thing and Pressure Void does no harm; when a woman divines about a man Pressure is the main thing and Wealth Void does no harm; what matters is that the Subject is undamaged. If Wealth and Pressure are Void but the stems and branches combine, the Self and Other generate each other and yin and yang are in their places, it is a real marriage bond, only that they share a pillow for half a lifetime and do not grow old together, which is decreed by Heaven and not the work of punishment or control; meeting a clash or control is called escaping the Void and there is on the contrary nothing to fear. When elders divine for a son or nephew, or an elder brother for a younger, look at the Output line or the Peer line, and do not always use Wealth and Pressure.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.45** 一卦的吉凶，要细察其中精微曲折；夫妇百年，才知道到底能否团圆。王洪绪特别说：这一章只是讲男子占女方婚事、女子占男方姻缘；今天的术士不辨清楚，凡择婿、择媳、嫁妹、娶嫂，都不按用神断，一概以官为夫、财为妇，大大误人；应当按用神的吉凶来推断，不可一概只论财官。
  The good and ill of a hexagram is to be examined for its fine and winding detail; only over a lifetime as husband and wife does one know whether they come together in the end. Wang Hongxu adds especially: this chapter speaks only of a man divining about a bride or a woman divining about a bridegroom; today's practitioners, not seeing the distinction, for choosing a son-in-law or daughter-in-law, marrying off a younger sister or taking a brother's wife, do not judge by the Subject but always take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife, which misleads people greatly; one should judge by the good or ill of the Subject and never speak of Wealth and Pressure alike in every case.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.01** 婚姻喜欢卦相合，世爻、应爻宜静，财爻、官爻旺相，婚姻就可以成。世应有动就不成。男家娶妻看财爻（代人占也一样），女家嫁丈夫用官鬼爻；用神忌讳动，出现时怕被冲；旺相可成，世应互相克则不长久。世爻代表丈夫，应爻代表妻子。还要看是谁占：占丈夫忌子孙爻发动，子孙持世就不成；占妻子忌兄弟爻发动，兄弟持世就不成。间爻是媒人，父母爻是三堂，子孙爻是子嗣，都宜静。卦里没有子孙爻，不欢喜。
  A marriage likes a hexagram that combines; the Self and Other lines should be still, and with Wealth and Pressure strong the marriage can be made. If the Self or Other line moves, it will not succeed. When the man's family takes a wife look at the Wealth line (the same for a divination on someone's behalf); when the woman's family marries her to a husband use the Pressure line. The Subject fears moving, and when it appears it fears being clashed; if strong it can be made, but if the Self and Other control each other it will not last. The Self line is the husband and the Other line the wife. Look also at who is divining: for a husband, fear a moving Output line, and an Output line on the Self means it will not succeed; for a wife, fear a moving Peer line, and a Peer line on the Self means it will not succeed. The between lines are the go-between, the Resource line is the 'three halls', the Output line is the heir; all should be still. A hexagram without an Output line is not a happy one.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.04** 占妻子看财爻，占丈夫看官鬼爻，都宜安静。阳宫的人端正，阴宫的人丑陋：落在飞神上，应在头面四肢；落在伏神下，应在笨拙、不稳当。男子占得震卦、巽卦，主再婚；女子占得坎卦、离卦，主再嫁。
  For a wife look at the Wealth line and for a husband at the Pressure line; both should be still. A yang palace means upright looks and a yin palace ugly ones: on the flying spirit it answers in the head, face and limbs, and under it, in clumsiness and unsteadiness. A man who gets Zhen or Xun will marry again; a woman who gets Kan or Li will marry again.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.09** 内边（内卦、身）是阳、鬼爻是阳，丈夫就在这一边；外边（外卦、应）是阴、财爻是阴，总是妻子。世爻、应爻相生，婚姻大吉；世应比和，配成夫妻相宜。青龙、六合扶持就美，三合再加子孙爻临上更妙。应爻动而遇三刑，就是刑，不用再问；外卦交来六害，害是无疑的。
  With the inner side, the Body, yang and Pressure yang, the husband holds there; with the outer side, the Other line, yin and Wealth yin, that is always the wife. The Self and Other lines generating each other: the marriage is greatly auspicious; the Self and Other in accord, the pairing is fitting. Helped by the Azure Dragon and a Six-combine it is fair; a three-harmony with the Output line present is more wondrous still. If the Other line moves and meets a three-punishment, there is punishment, ask no further; if the outer side meets a six-harm, harm is beyond doubt.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.11** 男女同心合意，是咸、泰、益这些卦；夫妻反目，是革、睽、离这些卦。夬卦一宫阳气壮盛，妻子难保；姤卦阴气强，家里不齐整。「不有躬」到蒙卦里去判断，「夫征不复」到渐卦里去推算。
  Man and woman in agreement: hexagrams like Xian, Tai and Yi; husband and wife falling out: Ge, Kui and Li. In the palace of Guai, yang is strong and the wife is hard to keep; in the hexagram Gou, yin is strong and the household is not whole. 'Not to have a body' is judged in Meng; 'the husband goes on the expedition and does not return' is reckoned in Jian.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.18** 阴阳各得其位，都称吉利；全是阴的，白白费心费力；全是阳的，有退悔，成不了婚；即使勉强成了，终究郁闷不乐。
  Yin and yang each in its place: all is called auspicious; all yin: heart and strength spent in vain; all yang: regret and withdrawal, and the marriage is not made; even if forced through, it ends in gloom.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.25** 占婚原本要阴阳相配；动的时候一起动也算好。阳爻都不动，只有一个财爻、一个鬼爻，应爻是阴、世爻是阳，最吉祥。再得世爻在内卦，男女团圆，百年昌盛。
  A marriage divination needs yin with yang; when they move, moving together is also good. If the yang lines do not move and there is just one Wealth and one Pressure, with the Other line yin and the Self line yang, it is the most auspicious. Better still if the Self line sits in the inner trigram: man and woman are reunited and prosper for a hundred years.
- **LY.MARRY.TXF.01** 世爻代表男家，应爻代表女家，要相生相合；官鬼是丈夫，妻财是妻子，最怕互相害、互相刑。生合的地方要防冲克，恐怕快成了又被破坏；冲克的地方若得生扶，反而有人从中撮合。财、官都要旺相才吉，最怕三刑六害发动。
  The Self line stands for the man's family and the Other line for the woman's family, and they should generate and combine with each other; Pressure is the husband and Wealth is the wife, and what is most disliked is their harming and punishing each other. Where there is generation and combination guard against a clash, for fear that what is about to be made is broken up; where there is clash and control, if support comes, someone steps in to help the match along. Wealth and Pressure should both be strong to be lucky, and most disliked are the three punishments and six harms when they move.
- **LY.MARRY.TXF.02** 阴阳各在其位为好，夫妻双方都在（财、官俱全）就和谐吉利。阴阳得位有两种：卦象外阴内阳，或者应爻阴、世爻阳。占婚以财、官为首要的用爻，俱全大吉；男占女以财为主，女占男以官鬼为主。财、官不上卦或落空，婚事难成，成了也不吉。
  Yin and yang each in its place is good, and husband and wife both present (Wealth and Pressure complete) is harmonious and lucky. Being in place takes two forms: a hexagram with yin outside and yang inside, or the Other line yin and the Self line yang. In a marriage divination Wealth and Pressure are the first Subject lines, and both present is very lucky; a man divining a woman takes Wealth as the main line, and a woman divining a man takes Pressure. If Wealth and Pressure are absent from the hexagram or fall Void, the marriage is hard to make, and even if made it is not lucky.
- **LY.MARRY.TXF.18** 咸、恒、节、泰四卦，取它们阴阳得位，如果又有日辰生合、吉神发动，就一直欢喜和谐，像鱼得水；但用爻落空、有凶神冲克时，不能一概断吉。睽、革、解、离一见如炭遇冰：睽是乖，火上泽下、金火同居，必有伤克；革是变，占婚遇到，有争婚改嫁；解是散，占婚宜合不宜分；离是六冲卦，纯阴之象，不吉。
  Xian, Heng, Jie and Tai are taken for their yin and yang being in place, and if the Day also generates or combines and lucky spirits move, they are harmonious to the end, like fish in water; but if the Subject line falls Void or is clashed and controlled by fierce spirits, one cannot judge them all as lucky. Kui, Ge, Xie and Li at first sight are like charcoal meeting ice: Kui is at odds, with fire above and marsh below and Metal and Fire dwelling together, so there is injury and control; Ge is change, and meeting it in a marriage divination there will be a contested marriage or remarriage; Xie is dispersal, and in a marriage divination it should be together and not apart; Li is a six-clash hexagram, an image of pure yin, and unlucky.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.04** 论婚姻，先看阴阳，再凭财爻、官爻。卦如果纯阳或者没有财爻，一定伤妻；纯阴的卦或者缺少官爻，丈夫要早死。
  In judging marriage, first look at yin and yang, then rely on Wealth and Pressure. If the hexagram is pure yang or has no Wealth line, the wife will surely be harmed; a pure yin hexagram, or one lacking a Pressure line, means the husband dies early.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.05** 夫唱妇随，卦里阴阳要得位；男女情投意合，爻里财、鬼要齐全。内卦是夫、外卦是妇，又以世爻为夫、应爻为妇：内阳外阴，或世阳应阴，都算得位，婚姻遇到百年和合。内阴外阳，或世阴应阳，是阴阳交错，夫妻只有一半称心。世应得位而内外卦纯阴纯阳，或内外卦得位而世应纯阴纯阳，都只是半吉。内外卦和世应全是纯阳或纯阴，不成夫妇——自古纯阴不生、纯阳不化，要一阴一阳才成配偶。再看：鬼爻是夫、财爻是妇；有鬼无财是有夫无妇，有财无鬼是有妇无夫；财、鬼齐全，夫妇才能和谐到老。
  For the husband to lead and the wife to follow the hexagram must have yin and yang in their places; for man and woman to love and delight in each other, the lines must have Wealth and Pressure complete. The inner trigram is the husband and the outer the wife, and likewise the Self line is the husband and the Other line the wife: inner yang with outer yin, or the Self line yang with the Other yin, count as in their places, and a marriage meeting this is in harmony for a hundred years. Inner yin with outer yang, or the Self yin with the Other yang, is yin and yang crossed, and husband and wife have their wish only by half. If the Self and Other lines are in their places but the inner and outer trigrams are pure yin or pure yang, or the reverse, it is only half auspicious. If the inner and outer trigrams and the Self and Other lines are all pure yang or pure yin, they do not become husband and wife: since antiquity pure yin does not give birth and pure yang does not transform, and only one yin with one yang makes a pair. Then: the Pressure line is the husband and the Wealth line the wife; Pressure without Wealth is a husband without a wife, Wealth without Pressure a wife without a husband; only when both are complete can husband and wife grow old in harmony.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.06** 男子的婚事，财爻的象要兴旺，才能早晚一同欢悦；女子出嫁，官鬼爻要旺相，才能朝夕同欢。男占妻子，以财爻为主，决不可没有；女占丈夫，以鬼爻为先，道理相仿。
  For a man's marriage the sign of Wealth must flourish, so that they delight together morning and evening; for a woman's marriage the Pressure line must be strong, so that they share joy morning and evening. A man divining a wife takes the Wealth line as the main thing, and it must never be absent; a woman divining a husband puts the Pressure line first; the principle is the same.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.18** 凡是婚配，要得到长久和合，只凭奇偶（阴阳），自然顷刻之间就分明。
  In any marriage match, to gain lasting harmony, rely only on odd and even (yang and yin), and it becomes clear in an instant.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.19** 女子占男方的婚事，以官鬼爻为主，以应爻的象为凭。财爻兴旺、子孙爻安静，是好姻缘。官旺为佳，鬼爻空就不要用；又嫌子孙爻动伤官鬼，喜的是财爻兴旺来帮助鬼爻。
  A woman divining a man's marriage takes the Pressure line as the main thing and the sign of the Other line as evidence. With Wealth flourishing and Output still, it is a good match. A strong Pressure line is good, and if it is Void do not use it; a moving Output line hurting Pressure is disliked, and what is welcome is a flourishing Wealth line helping the Pressure.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.20** 看妻子看内爻，看丈夫看外爻；世爻是女方本人，应爻是男方。女子占男方，反过来取内卦为妇、外卦为夫，又以世爻为妇、应爻为夫。也就是说，占卦的人这一边总是内卦和世爻。
  For the wife look at the inner lines, for the husband at the outer; the Self line is the woman herself and the Other line the man. When a woman divines about a man, take the inner as the wife and the outer as the husband, the reverse of the usual, and the Self line as the wife and the Other line as the husband. That is, the divining party's own side is always the inner trigram and the Self line.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.21** 男子择妻，应爻属阴才好；女子择夫，应爻属阳才欢喜。阴阳得位才和合，即使阴阳交错，也长久快乐。男子娶妻，世爻属阳、应爻属阴算得位；女子嫁男子，世爻属阴、应爻属阳算得位；内阴外阳，也一样。得位则欢喜无穷，即使交错，也主欢乐和谐。
  When a man chooses a wife, a yin Other line is fair; when a woman chooses a husband, a yang Other line brings joy. Yin and yang in their places make harmony; even if they are crossed, there is long joy. For a man taking a wife, the Self line yang and the Other yin count as in their places; for a woman marrying a man, the Self yin and the Other yang count as in their places; inner yin with outer yang is the same. In their places, joy is endless; even crossed, there is still gladness and harmony.
- **LY.MARRY.YM.01** 克我的是官鬼，代表丈夫、男子；我克的是妻财，代表妻子、女人：官是男、财是女，这是定理。男方占女方的婚事，以妻财为要；女方占男方的婚事，以官鬼为先；就算是父母、兄弟、朋友替他们占，也是把财当作妻、把官当作夫。所以官爻旺相，是女方家的吉占；妻财兴旺，是男子的好配偶；官爻落空、断绝，女子择夫多半落得孤身一人；妻财破散，男子择妻会终身无妻。
  What controls me is Pressure, which stands for the husband, the man; what I control is Wealth, which stands for the wife, the woman: Pressure is male and Wealth is female, a fixed principle. A man divining a woman's marriage takes Wealth as the key; a woman divining a man's puts Pressure first; even when parents, brothers or friends divine for them, Wealth is still taken as the wife and Pressure as the husband. So a strong Pressure line is a lucky divination for the woman's family and a thriving Wealth line a good match for the man; if Pressure is Void or cut off, a woman choosing a husband is often left alone, and if Wealth is broken or scattered, a man choosing a wife stays wifeless to the end.
- **LY.MARRY.YM.03** 《易冒》主张占婚姻先看卦、后看爻：得咸、恒、节、泰四卦为吉，只是遇到合冲、变冲就不适宜；得睽、革、解、离四卦为凶，即使用神齐备、又化出合，也不吉。小注说：得咸恒节泰而没有遇到合冲、变冲的，即使财、官衰弱陷落，或者随官入墓、助鬼伤身，都不算凶，这就是「用神比不上卦验」。婚姻忌三冲，遇到了就不必再去查用神的旺衰。这些都是凭卦断、不用爻的，前人往往有应验。

## 动变(Moving lines and what they turn into)

- **LY.MARRY.BSZZ.02** 合处逢冲有三种：六合卦变成六冲卦；日辰、月建冲爻；动爻变成冲。冲中逢合也有三种，方向相反。合处逢冲，谋划虽然成了最后还是散；冲中逢合，事情已经散了又成。占婚姻时，卦是六合，可是世爻被日辰冲、应爻月破，也是合处逢冲，不说吉；书中例：辰月丁酉日自占婚姻得否卦，当月自己大病，未月世爻和财爻入墓，那个女子病死。
  There are three kinds of a combination meeting a clash: a six-combine hexagram changing into a six-clash hexagram; the Day or Month clashing a line; a moving line changing into a clash; and three kinds of a clash meeting a combination, in the opposite direction. With a combination meeting a clash, the plan is made but in the end scatters; with a clash meeting a combination, the matter has scattered and is then made. In a marriage divination, if the hexagram is six-combine but Self is clashed by the Day and the Other line is under a Month-break, that too is a combination meeting a clash and is not called lucky; the case in the book: on a Ding-You day in the Chen month a man divined for himself about a marriage and got Pi; that month he fell gravely ill himself, and in the Wei month, Self and Wealth entering the tomb, the girl died of illness.
- **LY.MARRY.DYTJ.02** 用爻遇绝，却有一个生它的爻发动来生，又变得有用，叫绝处逢生。占婚姻遇到世爻、应爻绝处逢生：事情快要散的时候又成了，心意先淡后浓；或者可以说男女两家贫乏无力，但得到人扶助，这件事也能成。
  A line that meets extinction but has a moving line generate it becomes useful again: this is birth in a place of extinction. In a marriage divination, if Self and Other meet birth in a place of extinction, the matter is about to fall apart and then is made, and the intent is at first cool and then warm; or it may be said that the two families are poor and powerless but helped and supported by someone, the matter can still be made.
- **LY.MARRY.DYTJ.03** 六爻相合的卦，遇到日辰冲世应，叫合处逢冲。占婚姻遇到：一定被人说破，两边互相诋毁，快成的时候又散了。
  A hexagram whose lines all combine but whose Self or Other line is clashed by the Day is a combination meeting a clash. In a marriage divination, meeting this, the match is sure to be talked to pieces by others, both sides slander each other, and what is about to be made falls apart.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.03** 占婚姻，卦宜安静，安静则家庭和睦、没有争斗；如果财动，和公婆不和；鬼动，和妯娌不和；父动，和子侄不和；兄动，夫妻不和；再加月建、日辰，不但不和，还有克制。
  In divining marriage the hexagram should be quiet, and when quiet the household is at peace without strife; if Wealth moves, discord with the parents-in-law; Pressure moving, with the brothers' wives; Resource moving, with the children and nephews; Peer moving, between husband and wife; with the Month or Day added there is not only discord but also control.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.12** 世动生合应爻，男家愿成；应动生合世爻，女家愿嫁；都是容易成的象；只怕变入空亡，一定有退悔的意思；如果有日辰、动爻扶助，虽悔也可成。
  The Self moving to generate or combine with the Other means the man's family is willing; the Other moving to generate or combine with the Self means the woman's family is willing to give her; both are images of easy success; only turning into Void is feared, which means a wish to withdraw and regret; if the Day or a moving line supports it, it can still succeed despite regret.

- **LY.MARRY.YM.24** 占得六冲卦，叫卦冲，无论到哪里都散，话多乱、事多变、行多违、谋多阻。卦化成六冲，叫变冲：占事没有下文，约婚不能到底，结义不守信，谋求不能如愿。卦冲叫前冲，变冲叫后冲；如果前面是六合、后面是六冲，更加厉害。
  A divination that gives a six-clash hexagram is called a hexagram clash: wherever it goes it scatters; words are muddled, matters change often, actions go against, plans meet obstruction. A hexagram that changes into a six-clash hexagram is called a change-clash: a matter divined has no lasting result, a betrothal is not carried through, sworn brotherhood lacks good faith, a pursuit is not fulfilled. The hexagram clash is the front clash and the change-clash the rear clash; if the front is six-combine and the rear six-clash it is worse still.
- **LY.MARRY.YM.25** 六合卦而世爻或应爻被日辰冲害的，叫合冲，好比功业快成却败了，财已到手却失了，婚事快要答应却违了，谋划快要成功却变了，不可居守。不叫「冲合」的缘故是：已经败了就不能再成，已经解开了就不能再交合，已经失去了就不能再得到，即使得到也是应在别处了。另外爻的合处遇到冲：财爻、官鬼被冲害，忌婚姻；父母、官爻被冲害，忌功名；子孙、妻财被冲害，忌谋生；但比起冲害落在世应上的就稍轻，所以合处遇冲，前人专门以世应为主。
  A six-combine hexagram whose Self or Other line is clashed or harmed by the Day is called a combination-clash: like a feat nearly done that fails, wealth once gained that is lost, a marriage nearly agreed that goes against, a scheme nearly achieved that changes; one cannot rest on it. It is not called a 'clash-combination' because what has been spoiled cannot then be made, what has come apart cannot then be joined, and what has been lost cannot then be regained, and even if regained it answers elsewhere. Besides this there is a line's combination meeting a clash: Wealth and Pressure clashed or harmed is bad for marriage, Resource and Pressure clashed or harmed for office and fame, Output and Wealth clashed or harmed for making a living; but these are lighter than the clash falling on Self and Other, so for a combination meeting a clash the worthies of old looked chiefly at Self and Other.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.15** 财化财，不一定是结两次婚；鬼化鬼，很难说能相守百年。《黄金策》说「财爻叠叠，重作新人」，作者屡次试过：男家占女家，财爻重叠、旺相而生合世爻的，多主有贤妻美妾；财化财的，或者应在娶两个，或者应在婢女妾室一起来，或者妻妾众多，或者嫁妆丰厚；只怕爻中兄弟动，或者日月冲克财爻，那样免不了再办喜事。女家占男家，遇到财化财、财爻重叠而生世的，聘礼一定丰厚；不宜鬼动化鬼，那样必主丈夫亡故；鬼爻重叠克世，不是争婚夺娶，就一定反复不定、有灾祸是非。男方占遇到这种情况也主有变更，克世的就该停手，世爻化鬼的更该停手。
  Wealth changing into Wealth does not necessarily mean two marriages; Pressure changing into Pressure can hardly be said to mean keeping company for a hundred years. The Huangjin Ce says 'with Wealth lines upon Wealth lines, one becomes a bridegroom anew', but the author has tried it many times: when the man's family divines about the woman and the Wealth line is doubled, strong, and generates or combines with the Self line, it mostly means a virtuous wife and a fair concubine; where Wealth changes into Wealth, it may answer as taking two brides, or a maid or concubine coming along, or many wives and concubines, or a rich dowry; only beware a moving Peer line, or the Day and Month clashing and controlling Wealth, for then the wedding candles cannot be avoided again. When the woman's family divines about the man and gets Wealth changing into Wealth, with doubled Wealth lines generating the Self, the betrothal gifts will surely be rich; it is not good if Pressure moves and changes into Pressure, which surely means the husband's death, and doubled Pressure lines controlling the Self mean, if not a fight over the marriage or a snatched bride, then surely repeated disaster and dispute. A man who meets this also faces change; where it controls the Self one should stop, and where the Self line changes into Pressure one should stop all the more.

- **LY.MARRY.ZS.02** 这门婚事妨不妨父母：古书只说妻财发动才伤克父母，觉子驳它——春天占，财爻是木、父母爻是土，当令的木一定克得住休囚的土，旺和动一样能伤。他的办法是分占：先要财、官旺相，得到吉卦，再单独占一卦，问这门婚事会不会妨克父母。
  Whether the marriage harms the parents: the old books say only a moving Wealth line harms and controls the parents, and Juezi rejects this; in a spring divination Wealth is Wood and the Resource line is Earth, and Wood in season surely controls resting Earth, so strength harms just as movement does. His method is to divine separately: first Wealth and Pressure must be strong and the hexagram auspicious, then cast a separate hexagram on whether the marriage harms or controls the parents.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.18** 问这门婚事对父母好不好：专用父母爻做用神，父母爻旺也好、衰也好，都不忌；所忌的是财爻发动，那就一定是这门婚事有妨碍。比如财化父母、父母化财、鬼化父母、父母爻动而化成墓、绝、空、破，以及回头克，动而破散，或者日月克父母爻，那是父母暮年的时候了，就算不成这门婚事，也是难免的。
  Whether the marriage suits the parents: take the Resource line alone as the Subject; whether the Resource line is strong or weak is no matter, what is feared is a moving Wealth line, which surely means the marriage is harmful. For example Wealth changing into Resource, Resource changing into Wealth, Pressure changing into Resource, Resource moving and changing into tomb, extinction, Void or break, or into something that controls it, moving and then broken or scattered, or the Day or Month controlling the Resource line: this is the autumn of the parents' twilight years, and even if this marriage is not made it cannot be avoided.
