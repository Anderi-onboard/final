## 总则(Principles: the Subject, the Self and Other, yin and yang)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/婚姻/总则.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.01** 夫妻要和谐，先看阴阳配不配：世属阳、应属阴，鬼（官）属阳、财属阴，内卦属阳、外卦属阴；各得其位，才是夫妇之道，日后有利。男家占，宜世阳应阴；女家占，宜世阴应阳。
  For a couple to be harmonious, first see whether yin and yang match: the Self is yang and the Other yin, Pressure is yang and Wealth yin, the inner trigram is yang and the outer yin; each in its place is the way of husband and wife, and later days will be favourable. When the man's family divines, the Self should be yang and the Other yin; when the woman's family divines, the Self should be yin and the Other yang.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.18** 财、鬼两爻是占婚姻的用神，逢空亡本不吉，但不可死守：男占女以财为主，鬼空不妨；女占男以鬼为主，财空不妨；要紧的是用神没有损害。财鬼空亡而干支相合、世应相生、阴阳得位，是真姻缘，只是半辈子同床、不能白头到老，那是天命，不是刑克；逢冲遇克叫避空，反而不用担心。长辈占子侄、兄占弟，就看子孙爻、兄弟爻，不能一律用财官。
  The Wealth and Pressure lines are the Subject in a marriage divination and Void on them is by nature unlucky, but it must not be followed rigidly: when a man divines about a woman Wealth is the main thing and Pressure Void does no harm; when a woman divines about a man Pressure is the main thing and Wealth Void does no harm; what matters is that the Subject is undamaged. If Wealth and Pressure are Void but the stems and branches combine, the Self and Other generate each other and yin and yang are in their places, it is a real marriage bond, only that they share a pillow for half a lifetime and do not grow old together, which is decreed by Heaven and not the work of punishment or control; meeting a clash or control is called escaping the Void and there is on the contrary nothing to fear. When elders divine for a son or nephew, or an elder brother for a younger, look at the Output line or the Peer line, and do not always use Wealth and Pressure.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.45** 一卦的吉凶，要细察其中精微曲折；夫妇百年，才知道到底能否团圆。王洪绪特别说：这一章只是讲男子占女方婚事、女子占男方姻缘；今天的术士不辨清楚，凡择婿、择媳、嫁妹、娶嫂，都不按用神断，一概以官为夫、财为妇，大大误人；应当按用神的吉凶来推断，不可一概只论财官。
  The good and ill of a hexagram is to be examined for its fine and winding detail; only over a lifetime as husband and wife does one know whether they come together in the end. Wang Hongxu adds especially: this chapter speaks only of a man divining about a bride or a woman divining about a bridegroom; today's practitioners, not seeing the distinction, for choosing a son-in-law or daughter-in-law, marrying off a younger sister or taking a brother's wife, do not judge by the Subject but always take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife, which misleads people greatly; one should judge by the good or ill of the Subject and never speak of Wealth and Pressure alike in every case.

## 成否(Will it succeed: easy or hard to bring about)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/婚姻/成否.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.04** 六合卦一阴一阳配合成象，世应相生、财官相合，容易成而且吉；六冲卦不是纯阴就是纯阳，像两个女人同住、两个男人并处，志向合不来，难成，成了也不利。
  A Six-Combine hexagram is one yin matched with one yang, with the Self and Other generating each other and Wealth and Pressure combining, and is easy to bring about and auspicious; a Six-Clash hexagram is pure yin or pure yang, like two women living together or two men side by side whose aims cannot agree, hard to bring about, and unfavourable even if it succeeds.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.06** 世和应相合、应和世相合，是男女两家都愿意的象，容易成，以后也吉利。
  The Self and Other combining with each other is the image of both families willing; it is easy to bring about and auspicious later as well.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.08** 应爻生合世爻，是女家贪图男家，容易成；世刑克应爻，男家不会早来求；世旺应衰，是倚仗富有欺负贫弱、用强抢娶。世上见鬼和螣蛇、财爻旺相、青龙得地，多半是看上了女子的姿色，想设谋娶她；世上见玄武、兄弟，是因为缺钱还债，想谋娶这个女子。
  The Other line generating or combining with the Self means the woman's family covets the man's and it is easy to bring about; the Self punishing or controlling the Other means the man's family would not have come early to ask; the Self thriving and the Other weak is relying on wealth to bully the poor and taking her by force. With the Self carrying Pressure and the Coiling Snake, Wealth thriving and the Azure Dragon well placed, it is probably because the girl is good-looking and someone means to scheme to marry her; with the Self carrying the Dark Tortoise or Peer, it is because the family lacks money to repay a debt and wants to take the girl in.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.14** 女方占男方，最不宜鬼爻刑克世爻，也不宜应爻克世，都主不成，男家不愿结婚；如果他有制约、我有扶助、大象又吉，也可成，但他家终究无意；若得官爻生合世爻，或合财爻，或应爻生合世爻，才主容易成。
  When the woman's side divines about the man, the most unfavourable thing is a Pressure line punishing or controlling the Self line, and also the Other line controlling the Self; both mean it will not succeed and the man's family is unwilling; if he is restrained, I am supported and the overall image is auspicious, it can still succeed, though his family in the end has no intention; if Pressure generates or combines with the Self line, or combines with the Wealth line, or the Other generates or combines with the Self, then it is easy to bring about.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.23** 世空，是自己不想成；强行成了，终究不如意；应空，是对方不想成；成了以后两家一定不来往。
  The Self Void means one does not wish it oneself, and if forced through it never turns out as wished; the Other Void means the other side does not wish it, and if it comes about the two families will surely not visit each other afterwards.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.29** 父母爻是主婚人；不上卦、或落空亡，必定没有主婚的人，恐怕是那个女子自己作主，不然这件事难以成就。
  The Resource line is the officiant of the marriage; if it is absent from the hexagram or falls Void there is surely no one to preside, and the woman may be deciding for herself, or else the matter is hard to bring about.

## 动变(Moving lines and what they turn into)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/婚姻/动变.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.03** 占婚姻，卦宜安静，安静则家庭和睦、没有争斗；如果财动，和公婆不和；鬼动，和妯娌不和；父动，和子侄不和；兄动，夫妻不和；再加月建、日辰，不但不和，还有克制。
  In divining marriage the hexagram should be quiet, and when quiet the household is at peace without strife; if Wealth moves, discord with the parents-in-law; Pressure moving, with the brothers' wives; Resource moving, with the children and nephews; Peer moving, between husband and wife; with the Month or Day added there is not only discord but also control.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.12** 世动生合应爻，男家愿成；应动生合世爻，女家愿嫁；都是容易成的象；只怕变入空亡，一定有退悔的意思；如果有日辰、动爻扶助，虽悔也可成。
  The Self moving to generate or combine with the Other means the man's family is willing; the Other moving to generate or combine with the Self means the woman's family is willing to give her; both are images of easy success; only turning into Void is feared, which means a wish to withdraw and regret; if the Day or a moving line supports it, it can still succeed despite regret.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.27** 占婚遇财化财，一定有仆人陪嫁（赠嫁）；财化子，有小孩带来（带幼聘）；化子逢空虽来不长寿；化财遇冲以后会走失；退神逢冲，以后背夫改嫁或回娘家。鬼化鬼，不论进退神，凡事反复不定，占婚一定不容易，即使可成也迟滞，女家多有变化，缓急全听对方。
  In a marriage divination, Wealth changing into Wealth surely means servants come with the bride as a gift; Wealth changing into Output means a small child is brought along, called a betrothal with a child; changing into Output and meeting Void, the child comes but will not live long; changing into Wealth and meeting a clash, she will later run away; changing into a retreating spirit and meeting a clash, she will later leave her husband and remarry, or go back to her family. Pressure changing into Pressure, whether advancing or retreating, means everything is reversed and unsettled, and the marriage surely will not be easy, and even if it can succeed it is delayed; the woman's family will change its mind often, and easy or hard, slow or quick, all is at its pleasure.

## 媒妁(Go-betweens and the between lines)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/婚姻/媒妁.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.09** 世生应，是男方求女方；应生世，是女方求男方；相合也一样。世应比和，是两家互相成全。想知是谁赞成，看日辰合到哪一爻；间爻发动、与日辰相合，就是媒人。
  The Self generating the Other means the man is seeking the woman; the Other generating the Self means the woman is seeking the man; combining is the same. The Self and Other in harmony means the two families are helping each other. To know who approves, see which line the Day combines with; a between line that moves and combines with the Day is the go-between.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.33** 占庚帖，以间爻为媒人；如果是专为媒人这件事占，就不按间爻论，一定以应爻为媒。
  In divining the horoscope card, the between lines are the go-between; if the divination is solely about the go-between, do not go by the between lines but take the Other line as the go-between.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.34** 间爻与世生合，多是男家的亲戚；与应生合，多是女家的亲戚；世应都被生合，两家都有亲；旺相是新亲，休囚是旧眷，本宫是至亲，他宫是外亲。
  A between line generating or combining with the Self is mostly kin of the man's family, with the Other mostly kin of the woman's, and with both, both families are related to it; thriving means new relatives, weak means old ones, the home palace means nearest kin, another palace means outside kin.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.35** 间爻阳是男媒，阴是女媒；间爻有两个时，按衰旺、动静来取；衰爻动、旺爻静，取动的；两爻都旺或都衰，取被冲而动的；没有冲，取被生合扶起的；一旺一空，取不空的；都不空，用长生的办法；务必归到一个，就不杂乱。
  A yang between line is a male go-between and a yin one a female; when there are two between lines, choose by strength and movement; if the weak one moves and the thriving one is still, take the moving one; if both thrive or both are weak, take the one clashed into movement; with no clash, the one raised by generation or combination; if one thrives and one is Void, the one that is not Void; if neither is Void, use the Long Life method; always come down to one, and then it is not confused.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.36** 交、重（老阴老阳）为老年人，单、拆（少阳少阴）为少年人；胎、养、长生是少年，临官、帝旺是中年，墓、绝是老年；墓化旺，是老年人先来说；阴化阳，是女人先来说；这爻动空化空而自冲另一爻，是原来的媒人有事推辞，别人来做媒。
  The doubled and crossed marks (old yin and old yang) mean an old person and the single and broken marks (young yang and young yin) a young one; Womb, Nurture and Long Life mean youth, Official and Emperor's Prime middle age, Tomb and Cut-off old age; Tomb changing to thriving means an old person spoke first; yin changing to yang means a woman spoke first; if this line moves, is Void and turns Void and itself clashes another line, the original go-between has an excuse to decline and someone else acts as go-between.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.38** 间爻遇到螣蛇、朱雀和兄弟的，那人一定伶牙俐齿，一向靠做媒获利，旺相就一定奸诈。
  A between line that meets the Coiling Snake, the Vermilion Bird or Peer marks a person with a ready tongue who has always profited from matchmaking, and if thriving, one who is surely crafty.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.39** 间爻安静，被应、世两爻冲动，或者被生扶合起，或被日辰冲并起来的，那个人无心做媒，一定是一边请他去说；间爻自己发动的，不要这样断。
  A quiet between line clashed into motion by the Other and the Self, or raised by generation, support or combination, or by a clash from the Day, belongs to a person who has no mind to act as go-between and has certainly been asked by one side to speak; a between line that moves of itself is not to be judged this way.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.40** 两个间爻都动，一定有两个媒人，临兄弟或兄化兄、动出两个鬼，主有争着做媒的，要看空亡衰旺、有制无制，才知道谁掌权；间爻安静又都空亡，一定没有媒人来通好；空动而化出兄鬼，是媒人想要谢礼而作梗不来，不是没有媒人。
  Two between lines both moving means two go-betweens, and if they sit on Peer, or Peer changes into Peer, or two Pressure lines move out, they compete to be go-between, and one must see Void, thriving or weakness, and whether there is restraint to know which holds the power; between lines that are quiet and both Void mean no go-between comes to bring the two sides together; between lines that are Void and moving and change into Peer or Pressure mean the go-between wants his thank-you gift and is holding back, not that there is no go-between.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.41** 世应相冲相克，本来难成亲，如果得间爻生合来解救，终归要靠媒人两边说合来通好，事也可以成；解救的办法，比如应动克世，间爻合住应爻、生扶世爻，其余类推。
  The Self and Other clashing or controlling each other makes the match hard, but if a between line rescues them by generating or combining, in the end the go-betweens must speak to both sides to bring them together, and then it can succeed; the way of rescue is, for example, when the Other moves to control the Self, a between line combines with the Other and generates the Self, and the rest by analogy.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.42** 想请媒人说合求亲，必须应爻生合间爻，媒人的话才容易入耳、被听信；如果间爻反被应爻冲克，即使甜言蜜语，对方也不听从。
  To ask a go-between to arrange a match, the Other line must generate or combine with the between line, and then his words go in easily and are believed; if the between line is instead clashed or controlled by the Other, even honeyed words are not heeded.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.43** 间爻如果被日辰、动爻刑冲克害，那个媒人一定被两家怨恨：世爻冲克，男家有怨；应爻冲克，女家有怨；或者世来生合而财爻冲克，男家虽吉，日后女家一定怨；财、官都冲克，夫妻双方都有怨。
  If the between line is punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Day or a moving line, the go-between is surely resented by both families: the Self line clashing or controlling, the man's family resents; the Other line clashing or controlling, the woman's family resents; or if the Self generates or combines while the Wealth line clashes or controls, the man's family is fine but later the woman's family will resent; if Wealth and Pressure both clash or control, both husband and wife resent.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.44** 世应、日辰带财福、青龙，生合间爻，媒人一定得到两家的酬谢，旺相多、休囚少；带兄、鬼、螣蛇、玄武生合的，不过巧言虚礼，没有实惠；化出财福生合也吉；男看世爻，女看应爻；世应都生合，两家都有酬谢；世旺男家多，应旺女家多。
  When the Self and Other or the Day carry Wealth, Output or the Azure Dragon and generate or combine with the between line, the go-between surely receives thanks from both families, more if thriving and less if weak; if what generates or combines carries Peer, Pressure, the Coiling Snake or the Dark Tortoise, it is no more than fine words and empty courtesy without real benefit; changing out into Wealth or Output that generates or combines is also auspicious; a man looks at the Self line and a woman at the Other line; the Self and Other both generating or combining means both families give; the Self thriving means more from the man's family and the Other thriving more from the woman's.

## 对方(The other party: character, looks, family, age)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/婚姻/对方.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.17** 世应相生相合或比和，财、鬼又在同一宫，是亲上加亲；不带三合，虽是亲戚也不相识；带三合，一定是见过面的。
  The Self and Other generating, combining or in harmony, with Wealth and Pressure also in one palace, is kin upon kin; without a three-harmony frame they are relatives who do not know each other, and with one they have surely met.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.22** 卦里有官、财两爻，而财爻伏在空亡的鬼爻下，那个女子先前受过聘，未婚夫死了，俗称望门寡；再加白虎发动来克，就是嫁了以后丈夫死、带着孝服的；如果伏在不空的鬼下，是有丈夫的妇人，被日辰、动爻提起而刑克世爻，日后要防争讼。
  If the hexagram has both Pressure and Wealth lines and the Wealth line is hidden under a Void Pressure line, the woman was once betrothed and her fiancé died, commonly called a widow at the door; with the White Tiger moving and controlling as well, she married and her husband died and she wears mourning; if she is hidden under a Pressure line that is not Void, she is a woman with a husband, and if the Day or a moving line raises her and it punishes or controls the Self line, guard later against a lawsuit.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.25** 想知男女的性情容貌，看财、鬼两爻：月令旺身体胖，日时旺相貌美；月令衰身体瘦，日时衰相貌丑；月令旺而日时衰，身胖貌不扬；月令衰而日时旺，身瘦貌美；衰而有扶，丑却有才能；旺而入墓，虽美而愚拙；只看性情，男看鬼，女看财。
  To know the temperament and looks of the man or woman, look at the Wealth and Pressure lines: thriving in the month means a stout body, thriving on the day and hour means good looks; weak in the month means a thin body, weak on the day and hour means plain looks; thriving in the month but weak on the day and hour, a stout body and unremarkable looks; weak in the month but thriving on the day and hour, a thin body and handsome looks; weak but supported, plain but capable; thriving but entering the tomb, handsome but dull; for temperament alone, look at Pressure for a man and Wealth for a woman.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.26** 本命爻生旺有气，日后有荣华；休囚死绝，没有发达的日子。财旺命衰，妻子相貌虽美命却平常；鬼衰命旺，丈夫虽愚蠢衣食丰足。命临父母好技艺，加青龙好诗书，临兄好赌博、爱花钱、不好学，临官喜迎官府，临财福善于持家。王洪绪反对拿四季论旺衰，主张看本命爻是否临财福、青龙、贵人，或得日辰动爻生扶。
  When the natal line is born or thriving and has qi, glory lies ahead; when it is weak, dead or cut off, there is no rise. With Wealth thriving and the natal line weak, the wife is handsome but her fate is ordinary; with Pressure weak and the natal line thriving, the husband is dull but well fed and clothed. The natal line on Resource means a liking for skills, with the Azure Dragon added for poetry and books, on Peer for gambling, spending and no love of study, on Pressure a welcome to officials, on Wealth or Output a good housekeeper. Wang Hongxu objects to judging thriving and weakness by the four seasons and looks instead at whether the natal line sits on Wealth, Output, the Azure Dragon or a noble, or is generated and supported by the Day or a moving line.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.37** 要看男女的贫富美恶：问妇看财爻，问夫的身看鬼爻，问男家看世爻，问女家看应爻；应旺而财衰，是女家虽富，女子相貌不出众；维持和拆台的旁人，用刑冲生合的爻来推。
  To judge the wealth, poverty, looks and character of the man or woman: to ask about the bride look at the Wealth line, about the bridegroom look at the Pressure line, about the man's family the Self line, about the woman's family the Other line; the Other thriving and Wealth weak means the woman's family is rich but her looks are unremarkable; the bystanders who support or wreck it are judged by the lines that punish, clash, generate or combine.

## 聘嫁(Betrothal gifts, dowry, and the two families)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/婚姻/聘嫁.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.07** 求庚帖（女方的八字帖），要应爻安静不空、又生合世爻，才一定应允；应爻发动、空亡或冲克世爻，都主不答应。论聘礼，世爻见螣蛇、兄弟，主男家吝啬、礼不多；应爻见它们，主女家妆奁淡薄；旺相发动，也是克妻的兆头。
  To obtain the horoscope card (the woman's family's eight-character card), the Other line must be quiet, not Void, and must generate or combine with the Self, and then consent is sure; the Other moving, falling Void, or clashing with or controlling the Self all mean refusal. As to betrothal gifts, the Coiling Snake or Peer on the Self line means the man's family is stingy and the gifts few; on the Other line, the woman's dowry is meagre; thriving and moving, it is also a sign of harm to the wife.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.32** 看妆奁要看财爻：财爻生合世爻，又有日月、动爻扶助，一定有妆奁；临勾陈，有妆奁田；临青龙，妆奁漂亮；临螣蛇、白虎、败、病等爻，是旧东西；财没有生助，不可乱说；想推多少，看生合妻财那一爻的衰旺；财的衰旺只能推女子的相貌美丑，不能推妆奁厚薄。
  To judge the dowry look at the Wealth line: Wealth generating or combining with the Self and supported by the Month, Day or a moving line means there will be a dowry; on Hook Earth, dowry land; on the Azure Dragon, a handsome dowry; on the Coiling Snake, the White Tiger, or the ruined or sick lines, old household goods; if Wealth has no support, do not speak rashly; to reckon how much, judge by the strength of the line that generates or combines with Wealth; the strength of Wealth can only tell the woman's looks, not the richness of the dowry.
