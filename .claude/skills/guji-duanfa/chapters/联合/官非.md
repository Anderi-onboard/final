## 总则(Principles: who is who in a lawsuit)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/官非/总则.md`

- **LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.01** 怎么知道人家的官司了结：官鬼落空亡又休囚。怎么知道人家官司多：朱雀、白虎持世，官鬼来扶助。怎么知道人家多争执：朱雀、兄弟推动世爻应爻。怎么知道人家有小人：玄武、官鬼发动临在身爻。怎么知道人家灾祸到：官鬼临在应爻来克世爻。
  How do we know a household's suit is over? Pressure is Void and also resting or trapped. How do we know a household has many suits? The Vermilion Bird or the White Tiger holds the Self and Pressure comes to support it. How do we know a household has many quarrels? The Vermilion Bird and the Peer line push on the Self and the Other. How do we know a petty person has appeared? The Dark Tortoise and Pressure move and sit on the Body. How do we know calamity is coming? Pressure sits on the Other line and comes to control the Self.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.02** 子孙持世，官司从今了结就罢休；财爻持世，求官问讼适宜用财去托人，发动变出兄弟、官鬼，事事皆凶。
  With Output holding the Self, the lawsuit ends and rests from now on; with Wealth holding the Self, in seeking office or asking about a suit it is fit to entrust the matter with money, and if Peer or Pressure moves or changes out, everything is ill.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.01** 小的怨气不加惩戒，必然争长论短；大的亏已经吃了，怎能不告状申冤？要定输赢，必须详察世爻和应爻。卦中的世应，就是状纸上的原告和被告，详察它们，两边的胜负就可以知道。
  A small grievance not checked leads to quarrelling over who is greater; a great wrong already suffered, how can one not sue to set it right? To settle who wins and who loses, one must examine the Self and the Other. The Self and the Other in the hexagram are the plaintiff and the defendant in the plaint; by examining them one knows the success or failure of both sides.

## 起讼(Whether a suit is lodged and accepted)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/官非/起讼.md`

- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.10** 卦身是事情的根本缘由，落空就情节虚假。卦身关系到词讼的根由：旺，事大；衰，事小；动，事急；静，事缓；如果空或伏，都是编造的故事；飞神伏神都没有，一丝一毫都不是实情。
  The Body is the root cause and substance of the matter, and if it is Void the facts are false. The Body concerns the root of the lawsuit: strong, the matter is great; weak, small; moving, urgent; still, slow; if it is Void or hidden, it is a made-up story; if neither flying nor hidden spirit is present, not a hair of it is true.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.19** 上下卦都有父母，状词要递两次才能成。官鬼、父母两爻不适宜重复出现，主有转变不定的象，事情一定缠绵，最终难以了结。比如占告状，遇到这个象，必定再告一次才成。
  With Resource in both upper and lower trigrams, the plaint must be lodged twice before it succeeds. The Pressure and Resource lines should not appear twice over, for that means an unsettled image of change; the matter will surely drag on and in the end be hard to finish. For example, in divining for lodging a complaint, meeting this image, it must be lodged once more before it succeeds.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.20** 官鬼和父母两爻都强，词讼和表章都能被受理；妻财一发动，申呈和诉告全是白费。凡是要上表、申奏、递呈子、告诉这类事，都要官鬼和父母两样齐全，有气不空，就能受理；最怕财爻动伤父母，一定不成。
  With Pressure and Resource both strong, lawsuits and memorials alike are accepted; once Wealth moves, petitions and complaints are all in vain. For anything such as presenting a memorial, submitting a report, lodging a petition or making a complaint, Pressure and Resource must both be present, with breath and not Void, and then it is accepted; most to be feared is Wealth moving to injure Resource, in which case it surely fails.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.26** 父母临在应爻上，他想要起诉。父母是文书，临在世爻上，是我想告理；临在应爻上，是他想申诉；发动是想要进行，安静就不会提起。
  With Resource on the Other, he wants to file a suit. Resource is the papers; on the Self, I want to lodge the case; on the Other, he wants to file his plea; moving, it is going ahead; still, it will not be raised.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.27** 父母发动而官鬼化出福神，事情将成时偶然遇到有人拉着劝；父母落空而身爻临刑煞，状词还没批准就先挨笞刑。凡是占告状，遇到官鬼、父母两爻都动，官司可以成；如果父母有气，或官鬼化出子孙，就是人到公门将要投状，有人在旁边劝；如果父母化出空亡墓绝，官鬼刑克世爻，或者被日辰刑冲克害，状告不准，先遭杖责。
  With Resource moving and Pressure changing into Output, just as the matter is about to be done someone happens to pull one back and urge restraint; with Resource Void and the Body on a punishing star, the plaint is not yet accepted and one is beaten first. In divining for lodging a complaint, if Pressure and Resource both move, the suit can go ahead; if Resource has breath, or Pressure changes into Output, then when one reaches the court door about to submit the plaint someone persuades one against it; if Resource changes into the Void, tomb or extinction, Pressure punishes or controls the Self, or is punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Day, the complaint is not accepted and one is first beaten with the rod.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.05** 官鬼和父母同时发动，在公堂上有理；父母、官鬼克世爻，受屈含冤。世爻化退神、世爻空或破、卦变成六冲、世爻被刑冲，终究不成讼；财爻和子孙同时发动，克破文书和官鬼，也主不成。
  Pressure and Resource moving together, one is in the right in the public hall; Resource and Pressure controlling the Self, one suffers wrong and swallows the grievance. The Self changing into a Retreating spirit, the Self Void or broken, the hexagram changing into six-clash, or the Self punished or clashed, in the end no suit is brought; Wealth and Output moving together to control and break the papers and Pressure also means it is not brought.

## 胜负(Who wins and who loses)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/官非/胜负.md`

- **LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.05** 月破的爻：神机显现在破；祸福的机缘根源在动，动而有生无克的破爻，有出破、填实、合破的办法；安静而有克无生的破爻，就是破了。书中例：戌月丁卯日占官司，得泰卦：卦里爻逢六合，官事必定要审；不适宜的是戌月冲世爻，这是月破，卯日又克世爻，必输无疑；果然被杖责。这是应在日克月破的缘故。
  On a line struck by the Month-break: the working of the spirits shows in the break; the root of luck and misfortune lies in movement, and a broken line that moves and has life without control has the ways of leaving the break, being filled, or being combined out of the break, while a still broken line with control and no life is broken. Worked example: in the Xu month, on a Dingmao day, a man divined about a lawsuit and got Tai: the lines meet six-combine, so the official matter must come to trial; what is unfit is the Xu month clashing the Self line, which is the Month-break, and the Mao day also controlling the Self, so he surely loses; indeed he was beaten with the rod. This answers because the Day controls the Month-break.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.02** 应爻是对头，要休囚死绝才好；世爻是自己，适宜帝旺长生。不论原告被告，占的人都以世爻为自己，应爻为对头：应爻旺而世爻衰，他强我弱；世爻旺而应爻衰，他弱我强。
  The Other is the opponent, and it is best resting, trapped, dead or extinct; the Self is oneself, and it is fit for Emperor Strength or Long Life. Whether one is plaintiff or defendant, the questioner takes the Self as himself and the Other as the opponent: the Other strong and the Self weak, he is strong and I weak; the Self strong and the Other weak, he is weak and I strong.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.03** 相冲相克，是欺负凌辱的象。世爻刑克应爻，不算我胜，是欺负他的象；必须是官鬼爻克应爻，才算我胜，动爻和月建日辰克它也一样。
  Clash and control are the image of bullying and insult. The Self punishing or controlling the Other does not mean I win; it is the image of bullying him. Only if the Pressure line controls the Other does it mean I win, and so too if a moving line, the Month or the Day controls it.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.08** 应爻发动，他就多有机变。世爻发动，我必定用心谋划，如果化出官鬼或兄弟回头克制，反而失策；应爻发动，他必定有谋划，如果加上月建，必有贵人依靠，克世爻就不吉。
  If the Other moves, he has many tricks and changes. If the Self moves, I surely lay plans with care, but if it changes into Pressure or Peer that turns back to control it, the plan misfires; if the Other moves, he surely has a scheme, and if the Month is added he surely has a noble to lean on, and if it controls the Self it is unlucky.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.14** 遇到财爻，就理直气壮。以财爻为道理：临在世爻，我有理；临在应爻，他有理；官鬼来刑害，虽然有理，官府不听，兄弟发动，不容分辩。如果是递状告人，财爻就是忌神。
  Meeting Wealth, one's reason is just and one's spirit bold. Wealth stands for being in the right: on the Self, I am in the right; on the Other, he is in the right; if Pressure comes to punish and harm, though one is in the right the officials do not listen, and if Peer moves, one is not allowed to argue. If one is lodging the plaint, Wealth is the Adversary.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.21** 父母旺、官鬼衰，是雀角鼠牙的官司。父母旺相，官鬼休囚，状词说得大、事实很小，所以是雀角鼠牙之类的讼事。
  Resource strong and Pressure weak is a lawsuit of sparrow's horn and rat's tooth. With Resource strong and Pressure resting or trapped, the plaint talks big and the facts are small, so it is a petty suit of that kind.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.22** 变成衰弱、发动时旺，是虎头蛇尾的人。凡是世爻应爻旺而发动，有并吞六国的气势；如果变入墓绝空亡，就是先强后弱、虎头蛇尾的象。世指自己说，应指对方说。
  Changing into weakness after moving strong is a man all tiger-head and snake-tail. Whenever the Self or the Other is strong and moves, it has the force to swallow up six states; if it changes into the tomb, extinction or Void, it is the image of strong first and weak after, tiger-head and snake-tail. The Self speaks of oneself, the Other of the opponent.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.23** 世爻如果逢生，应当有贵人倚靠；应爻衰弱没有帮助，必定没有奸恶刁钻的人唆使。世爻衰弱，遇到月建、日辰、动爻生合，必定有贵人扶持，对方也无可奈何；应爻遇到这些则相反。
  If the Self meets life, there should be a noble to lean on; if the Other is weak and without help, there are surely no wicked and crafty men to egg him on. A weak Self that meets the Month, the Day or a moving line giving it life or combining with it surely has a noble to support it, and the opponent can do nothing; the Other meeting these is the reverse.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.24** 没有合没有生，即使旺又怎么样，不过是独脚的老虎；有刑有克又逢空，应当学缩头的乌龟。应爻旺而发动、没有生合的，他虽然刚强，是独脚虎不值得怕；世爻没有生合，又遇到日月动爻刑克，应当学缩头龟，暂且不要和他对理。
  With no combination and no life, however strong, what is it but a one-legged tiger? With punishment and control, and also in the Void, one should act like a tortoise drawing in its head. An Other that is strong and moving but has no life or combination is, for all its hardness, a one-legged tiger not worth fearing; a Self with no life or combination that also meets punishment or control from the Month, the Day or a moving line should act like a tortoise drawing in its head and for the time being not argue the case with him.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.03** 彼此相争，要看世爻应爻：不适宜冲克，喜欢相生；日月克身，我必定受辱；动爻克应爻，他必定遭刑；应爻克世爻他胜，世爻克应爻我赢。世爻空、世爻破、世爻动而化退，我心里胆怯；应爻动、应爻旺、应爻化进神，他得势了。卦遇六冲一定散，卦逢六合一定成。
  When the two sides contest, look to the Self and the Other: clash and control are not fit and giving life is welcome; if the Month or the Day controls the Body, I surely suffer insult; if a moving line controls the Other, he surely suffers punishment; the Other controlling the Self means he wins, the Self controlling the Other means I win. The Self Void, the Self broken, or the Self moving and changing into a Retreating spirit means I am timid at heart; the Other moving, the Other strong, or the Other changing into an Advancing spirit means he has the upper hand. A hexagram meeting six-clash surely breaks up; a hexagram meeting six-combine surely comes to terms.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.04** 总不离子孙爻：子孙发动、子孙持世，争斗打不起来；日月和动爻适宜克应爻，不适宜克世爻；世爻适宜旺相发动去克应爻，以及动而化出吉的，官事一定赢。
  It never leaves the Output line: Output moving or holding the Self means the fight does not come off; the Month, the Day and moving lines are fit to control the Other and unfit to control the Self; the Self is fit to be strong and moving to control the Other, or to move and change into something lucky, and then the official matter is surely won.

## 和解(Settlement and reconciliation)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/官非/和解.md`

- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.04** 相生相合，最终成就和好的情意。世爻应爻相生相合，原告被告有和解的意思：世爻生应爻，是我想求和；应爻生世爻，是他想求和；应爻世爻发动落空或化空的，都是假意说和。
  Giving life and combining, they end in a feeling of reconciliation. The Self and the Other giving life to and combining with each other means the parties have a mind to settle: the Self giving life to the Other means I want peace; the Other giving life to the Self means he wants peace; the Other or the Self moving into the Void or changing into the Void means a false pretence of peace.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.05** 世应比和而官鬼发动，恐怕官府捉打这场官司。世应比和是和解的象，如果官鬼发动来克，就是官府要办这场官司，不愿意和议；子孙也发动，最终才能成就和议。
  With the Self and the Other in harmony but Pressure moving, I fear the authorities will take up the case and press it. The Self and the Other in harmony is the image of settlement, but if Pressure moves to control, the authorities will pursue the case and do not want a private settlement; only if Output also moves is a settlement in the end achieved.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.06** 卦爻安静而子孙发动，喜得亲友劝和这件官事。六爻安静，世应虽然不相生合，但子孙发动，必定有亲戚邻居来劝和。
  With the lines still and Output moving, one is glad to have relatives and friends mediate the official matter. With all six lines still, though the Self and the Other do not give life to or combine with each other, if Output moves there will surely be relatives and neighbours who come to mediate.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.07** 世爻空，是我想平息争端。世爻空是我想平息争端，应爻空是他想平息争端，世爻应爻都空，双方的怨气都消了。
  If the Self is Void, I want to end the dispute. The Self Void means I want to end the dispute, the Other Void means he wants to end it, and both Void means the grievances on both sides are gone.

If a line that clashes or controls me instead gives life to or combines with the Other, beware of witnesses plotting together to frame me; if it is controlled by the Pressure line or clashed and controlled by the Day, the officials will not listen to its words and I come to no harm. If a between line is punished or controlled, the witness is surely beaten with the rod; the one near the Self is surely my witness, and the one near the Other is his witness.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.12** 官鬼是审问的官，克应爻则他遭杖责。官鬼是审理词讼的官，发动去克应爻，官司必定我胜；克世爻，我败。
  Pressure is the examining magistrate; if it controls the Other, he is beaten with the rod. Pressure is the official who hears the case; if it moves to control the Other, I surely win the suit; if it controls the Self, I lose.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.13** 日辰是书吏，伤了世身则我要受刑罚。日辰既能救事也能坏事，如果鬼发动克世，自然有刑罚；得到日辰制住鬼、冲掉鬼，必有旁人一句话来解释，问案的官必能宽恕我。
  The Day is the clerk, and if it injures the Self and Body I suffer punishment. The Day can both save and spoil a matter: if Pressure moves and controls the Self there is naturally punishment; if the Day restrains or clashes away Pressure, someone is sure to put in a word of explanation, and the magistrate will surely pardon me.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.17** 官鬼逢太岁，必定不是州县的案子。官鬼在第五爻，如果值太岁，这件事必定牵涉朝廷；遇月建，必定涉及台宪。
  Pressure meeting Tai Sui surely means this is not a case for a prefecture or county. If Pressure is on the fifth line and sits on Tai Sui, the matter surely concerns the court; meeting the Month, it surely involves the censorate and the provincial authorities.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.18** 内外卦都有官鬼，事情牵涉一个衙门也终究了不了。官鬼不上卦，没有官员主张；内外都有官鬼，权力不归一人，主事情反复，必定经过两个衙门然后了事。
  With Pressure both inside and outside, a matter that touches one office cannot be finished in the end. If Pressure is not on the hexagram there is no official to take charge; with Pressure inside and outside, authority is not in one hand, which means the matter goes back and forth and must pass through two offices before it is settled.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.25** 兄弟在间爻，事情必然牵涉众人。兄弟在间爻，状词里牵涉的人很多；发动，作证的人就会贪图索取贿赂；克应爻，会索取赔偿的钱财；克世爻，须用财物托人才能安稳。
  With a Peer line among the between lines, the matter surely involves many people. A Peer line among the between lines means many are implicated in the plaint; if it moves, the witnesses grasp at bribes; if it controls the Other, it demands compensation money; if it controls the Self, one must buy security by entrusting money to someone.

## 罪名(The charge and the sentence)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/官非/罪名.md`

- **LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.03** 凡遇回头克的，彻底克尽。书中例：午月丁未日占弟弟被人告了吉凶如何，得困卦变恒卦：酉金兄弟爻为用神，午月克它，未日生它，似乎可以相抵；但是动出午火，和月建相克，是大凶之象；他问凶在什么时候，我说今年是辰年，太岁相合没有妨碍，化出退神于申，恐怕危险在午年申月；果然到午年申月被判重刑。
  Whatever meets a turning-back control is controlled through and through. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Dingwei day, a man divined how things stood for his younger brother, who had been sued, and got Kun changing to Heng: the Peer line You-metal is the Subject, controlled by the Wu month and given life by the Wei day, which seems to set them even; but it moves out Wu-fire, which, controlled by the Month, is a sign of great ill. He asked when the ill would fall. I said: this year, a Chen year, Tai Sui combines with it and there is no harm, but it changes into a Retreating spirit at Shen, and I fear danger in the Shen month of a Wu year; indeed in the Shen month of the Wu year he was given a heavy punishment.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.04** 凡遇回头克的，彻底克尽。书中例：卯月戊辰日有人占父亲的官事，得萃卦变同人卦：断语说，外卦未土被卯月克它，何况土在春令气败，又会成亥卯未木局克它，全无救助，必定到重罪；后来果然被斩。
  Whatever meets a turning-back control is controlled through and through. Worked example: in the Mao month, on a Wuchen day, a man divined about his father's official matter and got Cui changing to Tongren: the Judgement said that the Wei-earth of the outer trigram is controlled by the Mao month, and moreover Earth is spent in the spring command and Hai, Mao and Wei combine into a Wood frame that controls it, with no rescue at all, so it must come to a grave charge; afterwards he was indeed beheaded.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.29** 世爻发动变成官鬼，必定因为官司丢了性命。世爻持鬼，是我失理；应爻持鬼，是他失理；世爻变鬼恐怕因官事丧身，应爻变鬼就按对方来断。
  The Self moving and changing into Pressure surely means losing one's life over the lawsuit. The Self holding Pressure means I am in the wrong; the Other holding Pressure means he is in the wrong; the Self changing into Pressure, I fear one may die from the official matter, and the Other changing into Pressure is judged for the opposite party.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.32** 如果问罪名，必须详察官鬼。凡是占罪名轻重，用官鬼爻来定：旺，罪重；衰，罪轻。再加刑、白虎、旺而发动克世爻，火主受极刑，金主充军，木主笞杖，水主从犯罪。要按衰旺、有制无制来断，不可拘泥。
  If asking about the charge, one must examine Pressure. In judging how heavy or light the charge is, fix it by the Pressure line: strong, the charge is heavy; weak, light. With punishment and the White Tiger added, strong and moving to control the Self, Fire means the extreme penalty, Metal means military exile, Wood means the cane or rod, Water means being an accessory. Judge by strength and weakness and whether it is held in check or not, and do not be rigid.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.06** 子孙适宜旺相，最忌官鬼兴盛。子孙持世、子孙发动，已经送到法场，终究会赦免；官鬼克世爻、或官鬼持世，又被日月动爻冲克，或者世爻发动化出凶的，虽然判的是轻罪也会拘押改判为重罪。
  Output should be strong, and Pressure flourishing is most feared. Output holding the Self or moving, even one already taken to the execution ground is in the end pardoned; Pressure controlling the Self or holding the Self and also clashed or controlled by the Month, the Day or a moving line, or the Self moving and changing into something ill, means that though a light sentence is fixed he is held and it is changed to a heavy one.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.07** 蛇雀木鬼犯官刑，临火须防焚害；蛇木又言缢绞，勾陈土鬼牢狱。书中例：未月癸亥日占判定什么罪，得中孚卦变临卦：木鬼临白虎，绞罪是定了；但幸好世爻临月建，木鬼虽然发动，又被酉金回头之克，外卦反吟，事情一定反复，虽然定了绞罪，终究会有改动；果然议的是绞罪，后来蒙驳回重审减轻。
  The Coiling Snake or Vermilion Bird with Wood Pressure offends against the official penalty, and with Fire beware of burning; the Coiling Snake and Wood also speak of hanging or strangling, and the Hook Earth with Earth Pressure of jail. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Guihai day, a man divined what charge would be fixed and got Zhongfu changing to Lin: Wood Pressure on the White Tiger means a sentence of strangulation is fixed; but fortunately the Self sits on the Month, and though the Wood Pressure moves it is controlled by a turning-back control from You-metal, and the outer trigram is Reversed Chant, so the matter will surely go back and forth; though strangulation is fixed there will in the end be a change; indeed strangulation was recommended, and later the case was sent back for retrial and the sentence reduced.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.10** 用神、元神，适宜逢生：有月建生、日建生、动爻生、动变回头生。书中例：卯月己卯日，弟弟占哥哥已经被判重罪，母亲来问能不能救，得复卦变震卦：兄弟爻为用神，丑土兄动，被日月克，明显大罪难脱，幸好兄爻丑土化出午火父母，回头相生；断说：赶快去办！化出父母去生兄爻，相生为吉，神灵告知得很明显，后来果然蒙恩免死。
  The Subject and the Support should meet life: life from the Month, from the Day, from a moving line, or from a turning-back life by change. Worked example: in the Mao month, on a Jimao day, a younger brother divined for his elder brother, who had already been given a grave sentence, with the mother asking whether he could be saved, and got Fu changing to Zhen: the Peer line is the Subject, Chou-earth moves and is controlled by the Month and the Day, so it is plain that he cannot escape the great charge, but fortunately the Chou-earth Peer line changes into Wu-fire as Resource that turns back to give life; I judged: go and do it quickly! Resource changing out to give life to the Peer line, mutual life, is lucky and the spirits spoke clearly; afterwards he was indeed spared death by grace.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.11** 忌神、仇神，适宜逢克：有月克、日克、动爻克、用神动化回头克。这四种，用神、元神只要逢一克，别处又见不到生扶的，就是凶兆；占吉事，乐极生悲；占凶事，须赶紧回避。书中例：卯月戊辰日占父亲的官事已经拟了重罪，得萃卦变同人卦：外卦未土父母，被卯月克它，内卦亥卯未合成木局又相克制，月克日刑，全无救助，果然被重刑。
  The Adversary and the enemy spirit should meet control: control by the Month, by the Day, by a moving line, or a turning-back control from the Subject's own change. In these four, if the Subject or the Support meets even one control and sees no life or support elsewhere, it is an ill sign; in divining for a lucky matter, joy brings sorrow; in divining for an ill matter, hurry to avoid it. Worked example: in the Mao month, on a Wuchen day, a man divined about his father's official matter, for which a grave sentence had already been drafted, and got Cui changing to Tongren: the Resource Wei-earth of the outer trigram is controlled by the Mao month, and the inner trigram Hai, Mao and Wei combine into a Wood frame that controls it again; controlled by the Month and punished by the Day, with no rescue at all, he was indeed severely punished.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.12** 同一天妹妹占哥哥的官事，和前一案一样，也拟了重罪，得否卦变讼卦：申金兄弟爻为用神，巳火官鬼发动，刑克申金，重罪是定了；幸好辰日冲动戌土父母，暗动去生申金，克处逢生，如果有父母，可以救他；后来因为他父亲八十岁了，援例告留，免了死罪。
  On the same day a younger sister divined about her elder brother's official matter, the same case as before, and a grave sentence had likewise been drafted; she got Pi changing to Song: the Peer line Shen-metal is the Subject, Pressure Si-fire moves and punishes or controls Shen-metal, so the grave charge is fixed; fortunately the Chen day clashes the Resource Xu-earth into secret motion, which gives life to Shen, life met within a control, and if there is a parent he can be saved; afterwards, because his father was eighty years old, an appeal for retention under the precedent saved him from death.

## 狱囚(Prison and release)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/官非/狱囚.md`

- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.16** 世爻入墓，难免被关进牢狱。世爻入墓、化墓，或临在鬼墓上，卦象凶的必有牢狱之灾；临白虎，在狱中生病。
  If the Self enters the tomb, imprisonment can hardly be avoided. The Self entering the tomb, changing into the tomb, or sitting on the tomb of Pressure, with an ill hexagram, surely means the calamity of prison; on the White Tiger, he falls ill in prison.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.31** 墓逢日德和刑冲，眼下就该出狱；太岁带着福神来生合，狱中必遇天恩。世墓、鬼墓发动，都是入狱的象，如果得到日辰刑冲克破，眼下就该出狱。在狱中占卦，最喜太岁生合世爻，主有天恩赦免；月建生合，上司审出来；日辰生合，有关官员饶恕；父母爻生合，必须申诉才能免。
  The tomb meeting the Day's virtue and punishment or clash, he should leave prison at once; Tai Sui bringing Output to give life and combine, in prison he surely meets imperial grace. The tomb of the Self or of Pressure moving is the image of entering prison, and if the Day punishes, clashes, controls or breaks it, he should be out at once. Divining in prison, best of all is Tai Sui giving life to or combining with the Self, which means imperial pardon; the Month giving life or combining means the higher office trials him out; the Day giving life or combining, the officials spare him; Resource giving life or combining, he must plead to be let off.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.08** 占狱讼的，世爻旺相能够脱离，休囚空破的免不了凶；占讼事，世爻旺相的得理，空破休囚又被克的，必定遭受刑罚牢狱。
  In divining for prison and lawsuits, a strong Self can get free, while a resting, trapped, Void or broken Self cannot avoid ill; in divining for a suit, a strong Self is in the right, and a Void, broken, resting or trapped Self that is also controlled will surely suffer punishment and prison.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.09** 我屡次验证而得验的，只有世爻入墓三种：一是世爻随鬼入日墓，一是世爻入动墓，一是世爻动而化墓。这三种墓，自占看世爻，世爻旺相的不是真；代占看用神，用神旺相的不是真；只有世爻、用爻休囚被克，又入墓的才是；墓神被日月动爻冲破的，也不是真。因为墓破就像破网，容易出来。书中例：未月戊辰日占已经判定的重罪能不能蒙赦免，得蛊卦变损卦：世爻随鬼入动墓，又动而化墓，古法认为凶，我断为吉：日月生世，丑墓被月破，破罗破网，容易出来，断明年酉年一定蒙赦；果然次年辰月蒙赦出来。
  What I have found borne out by repeated trial is that the Self entering the tomb is borne out in only three ways: the Self following Pressure into the tomb of the Day, the Self entering a moving tomb, and the Self moving and changing into the tomb. For these three tombs, in divining for oneself look at the Self, and a strong Self is not the real thing; in divining for someone else look at the Subject, and a strong Subject is not the real thing; it is real only when the Self or the Subject is resting, trapped or controlled and also enters the tomb; a tomb spirit clashed and broken by the Month or a moving line is not real either. For a broken tomb is like a broken net, and it is easy to get out. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Wuchen day, a man divined whether a grave sentence already fixed could be pardoned, and got Gu changing to Sun: the Self follows Pressure into a moving tomb and also moves and changes into the tomb, which the old method calls ill, but I judged lucky: the Month and the Day give life to the Self, the Chou tomb is broken by the Month, a net broken is easy to leave, and I judged that next year, a You year, he would surely be pardoned; indeed in the Chen month of the next year he was pardoned and let out.

## 消散(When the matter fades away)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/官非/消散.md`

- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.30** 子孙在身边，到底不能结成证据；官鬼伏在世下，诉讼的根子还没有除掉。卦身临福德出现并发动，马上就会消散；只怕官鬼伏在世爻下，那么讼根常在，目前虽然不成讼，等官鬼旺相透出来时就会被举发。
  With Output beside the Body, a case can never be made into proof; with Pressure hidden under the Self, the root of the lawsuit has not been pulled out. The Body sitting on Output, appearing and moving, is scattered at once; the only fear is Pressure hidden under the Self, in which case the root of the suit is always there, and though no suit is formed for now, when Pressure is strong and comes out it will be brought up.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.33** 要知道怎样消散，应当看子孙。如果福神发动、官鬼安静，按生旺的日月断消散；官鬼发动、福神安静，按官鬼入墓的日月断消散。卦象既然成了，胜负明明白白；一部讼庭的是非，判得清清楚楚。
  To know how the matter will melt away, look at Output. If Output moves and Pressure is still, judge the day it melts by the months and days of life and strength; if Pressure moves and Output is still, judge it by the months and days when Pressure enters the tomb. Once the hexagram is formed, who wins and who loses is plain; the rights and wrongs of the whole lawcourt are decided clearly.

- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.01** 担心官司的，喜欢官鬼落在空亡或月破，最适宜子孙发动、子孙持世，就没有是非；官鬼和朱雀一起发动，口舌免不了；螣蛇官鬼克世，灾祸忧患相侵；玄武官鬼，必定因盗贼和阴人；白虎官鬼，必定有伤痕见血；世爻动而克应爻，是我兴词告人；应爻克世爻，是他有讼要告我。
  In worrying about a lawsuit, one is glad that Pressure is Void or broken, and most fit is Output moving or holding the Self, which means no trouble; Pressure and the Vermilion Bird moving together mean quarrels cannot be avoided; the Coiling Snake and Pressure controlling the Self mean calamity and distress press in; the Dark Tortoise and Pressure surely come from thieves and women; the White Tiger and Pressure surely mean wounds and blood; the Self moving to control the Other means I file a plaint; the Other controlling the Self means he has a suit against me.
- **LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.02** 子孙发动、子孙持世，必定不成是非；官鬼持世、官鬼克世、应爻发动克世，一定成讼。
  Output moving or holding the Self surely means no trouble arises; Pressure holding the Self, Pressure controlling the Self, or the Other moving to control the Self surely means a suit arises.
