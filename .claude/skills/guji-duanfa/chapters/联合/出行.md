## 总则(Principles: which line is the traveller, the destination, the baggage and the purse)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/出行/总则.md`

- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.01** 占出行，父母爻是行李：旺相就多，休囚就少，带着刑害就是破损的旧东西；妻财爻是盘缠（路费、本钱）：旺相就丰足，休囚就微少，从兄弟爻变出来的是合伙的本钱或借来的。为名为利出门免不了奔波，路上的休咎，神灵能给准信。
  In divining for a journey the Resource line is the baggage — much if strong, little if resting or trapped, worn and damaged things if it carries punishment or harm — and the Wealth line is the travelling money and capital — full if strong, slight if resting or trapped, and a joint stake or borrowed money if it comes out of the Peer line by change. One who goes out for fame or gain cannot avoid the roads, and the spirits give a sure report of good and ill on the way.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.02** 世爻代表出门的自己：世爻生旺，身体健壮；世爻衰弱（休囚），人就经不起路上风餐水宿的劳苦。
  The Self line is the traveller himself: if it is strong he is robust; if it is weak (resting or trapped) he cannot bear the toil of eating in the wind and sleeping on the water.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.23** 官鬼主祸灾，所以整个卦最好没有鬼煞；即使出现了，只要安静或者有制伏，见到也无妨。
  Pressure governs calamity, so the best hexagram has no baleful Pressure at all; even if it appears, if it is still or restrained there is no harm in seeing it.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.24** 子孙爻是福德，又是解除灾祸的神；如果不上卦，或者落空亡，就制不住鬼，鬼煞专权，恐怕有灾祸，所以出行六爻里不见子孙就难以称心。
  The Output line is virtue and fortune and also the spirit that loosens calamity; if it is not in the hexagram or falls into Void it cannot control Pressure, then the baleful Pressure holds sole power and one fears calamity, so a journey whose six lines show no Output can hardly go as one wishes.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.26** 出行如果财爻旺相、临在月建上，生合持世爻，又不受刑克，一定满载而归；只要能趋吉避凶，就不必担心登高涉险。
  In travelling, if the Wealth line is strong and stands on the Month, gives life to or combines with the Self, and is not punished or controlled, one will surely return home with a full load; if one can seek the lucky and avoid the ill, there is no need to fear climbing high or crossing danger.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.27** 占行人（在外的人），先找主象，也就是用神：占官员看官爻，占晚辈看子孙爻，占妻子、奴仆看财爻，占兄弟朋友看兄爻，占尊长看父爻，不在六亲之中的人看应爻；要决断他的归期，就看这个主象。
  In divining for an absent traveller one first finds the principal image, which is the Subject: for an official look at the Pressure line, for the young at the Output line, for a wife or servant at the Wealth line, for brothers and friends at the Peer line, for elders at the Resource line, and for anyone outside the six relations at the Other line; to decide his day of return one looks at this principal image.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.07** 福神发动，祸患灾殃就消散。出行时，遇到子孙发动、子孙持世、世爻化出子孙，走万里路程，百种祸患都暗中消散；子孙克世的也吉。
  When Output moves, misfortune and calamity melt away. In travelling, if Output moves, Output holds the Self, or the Self changes into Output, then through a journey of ten thousand li a hundred misfortunes quietly vanish; Output controlling the Self is also lucky.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.16** 问行人什么时候回来，有远有近：近的应在日，远的应在月；也有离开很久、眼下就该回来的，只用日来断。问行人平安与否，要另外占一卦，不可以一卦兼断。
  In asking when a traveller will return, some returns are far and some near: a near one falls on a day, a far one on a month; there are also those who have long been away and are due back very soon, and these too are judged by the day alone. In asking whether the traveller is faring well or ill, divine separately; do not judge both from a single hexagram.

## 去留(Go or stay: whether one can set out, delays and detentions)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/出行/去留.md`

- **LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.02** 出行适宜世爻发动；归魂卦不出疆界；用爻发动又遇三合，行人立刻回到家。
  Setting out is fit when the Self moves; a Returning Soul hexagram does not leave the border; when the Subject moves and meets a three-harmony, the traveller is back home at once.
- **LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.08** 独静独发只能看事情成败的快慢，至于吉凶要推用神，舍掉用神去决断的，迂腐而且错误。书中例：午月丙午日占亲自去寻找父亲能不能回来，得大有卦变离卦：一位懂易的朋友拿着这一卦问我，寅木一爻独发，正月能见到吗？我说不是的，卦中父爻持世，被寅木克制，自己不能动，父亲也见不到，想让自己动身见到父亲，必须等到冲克寅木的年月；让他再占一卦合起来决断，得革卦变既济卦：这一卦正合前一卦，前一卦应在冲开寅木的申，这一卦世爻化出申金回头生，也应在申；果然在申年八月寻父回了家，应在申年，是前卦冲去忌神、后卦化出申金父母用神生世的缘故。
  Sole still and sole moving only show how fast or slow the matter succeeds or fails; luck and ill must be reckoned from the Subject, and to decide while setting the Subject aside is pedantic and wrong. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Bingwu day, a man divined about going himself to seek his father and bringing him back, and got Dayou changing to Li: a friend who knew the Changes held up this hexagram and asked me whether Yin-wood moving alone meant he would see his father in the first month. I said no; the Resource line holds the Self and is controlled by Yin-wood, so he cannot move himself and cannot see his father; to set out and see his father, he must wait for a year or month that clashes or controls Yin-wood. I told him to divine again to settle it together, and he got Ge changing to Jiji: this hexagram agrees exactly with the former; the former answered at Shen, which clashes Yin open, and this one has the Self changing into Shen-metal that turns back to give life, which also answers at Shen. Indeed in the eighth month of a Shen year he went to seek his father and came home with him. It answered in the Shen year because the former hexagram clashed away the Adversary and the latter changed into Shen-metal, the Resource and Subject, to give life to the Self.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.03** 应爻代表所去的地方，最怕落空亡：空了，那个地方冷落寂寞，谋事难成，不能称心如意地回来。
  The Other line is the place one goes to and most fears Void: if it is Void the place is desolate and quiet, the plan is hard to make good, and one cannot return satisfied.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.06** 世爻克应位，是我制住对方，所去的方向通达，去了没有阻隔，不论远近都适宜出行；应爻克世爻，所去的方向闭塞，不论公事私事都不利，再遇到动爻、日辰刑克，更不吉利。
  The Self controlling the Other position is my controlling the other side: the way I go is open and I go without hindrance, and it is fit to set out however far or near; the Other controlling the Self closes the way I go and is unfavourable for public and private business alike, and with a moving line or the Day punishing or controlling as well it is the more inauspicious.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.09** 世爻发动，行期就定了；世爻、应爻都动宜赶快走；世爻动而变出鬼爻，去后必遭祸患，等于自投罗网；鬼爻持世，是犹豫畏缩、想走又不走的样子；鬼爻化出子孙，即使有灾患也不足畏惧，最终能脱身。
  When the Self moves the date of departure is fixed; if the Self and the Other both move it is fit to set out quickly; if the Self moves and changes into a Pressure line, one meets misfortune after going and walks into the net oneself; Pressure holding the Self is the look of hesitation and shrinking, wanting to go and not going; and if Pressure changes into Output, though there is calamity it is not to be feared, and one at last escapes.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.10** 世爻安静而被日辰、动爻暗冲，是别人托他去的，不是为自己谋事；世爻发动而被日辰、动爻合住，是将走而有牵绊，不能起程，只有间爻合住才是同伴把他留住。要断起程的日期，要逢到冲开的日子。
  If the Self is still and is stirred in secret by the clash of the Day or a moving line, someone else has asked him to go and it is not for his own business; if the Self moves and is held by a combination from the Day or a moving line, he is about to go but is tied down and cannot set out, and only when a between line holds it is a companion keeping him back. To fix the day of departure one must wait for the day that clashes it open.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.11** 世爻逢空，出行去不成，勉强去了也终究难以得意，白白奔波；只有九流、技艺和公门里的人，空着手去求利，反而吉。
  If the Self meets Void the journey cannot be made, and going by force one still finds it hard to succeed and runs about in vain; only for people of the nine trades, arts and crafts, and the yamen, going empty-handed to seek profit, is it on the contrary lucky.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.25** 别人替人占出行，要看是谁出行：占子孙就看子孙爻，出行的人（主人）就是用神；用神发动遇到空亡，走到半路又回来，发动化出退神也是这样。
  If someone divines on another's behalf one must see who is setting out: for a child look at the Output line, and the one who goes (the principal) is the Subject; if the Subject moves and meets Void, he goes halfway and returns, and moving and changing into a Retreating spirit is the same.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.01** 世爻是出行的人，旺相有气就适宜出行；发动化出吉的爻、化出子孙更吉；休囚空破，或者发动化出凶的，就不宜出行。应爻是目的地，又是依傍的人，应爻空亡就该停下；应爻空破墓绝，或者发动变鬼、变绝、化出回头克，去了也没有益处。
  The Self line is the one who goes: if it is strong and has breath it is fit to set out, it is better still if it moves and changes into something lucky or into Output, and it is unfit to set out if it is resting or trapped, Void or broken, or moves and changes into something ill. The Other line is the destination and also the person one leans on: if the Other is Void one should stop, and if it is Void, broken, in the tomb or extinct, or moves and changes into Pressure, into extinction or into a line that turns back to control it, going is of no use.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.02** 世爻克伤应位，不论远近都适宜出行；应爻克世爻，公事私事都主不利。我去克他，所去的方向通达；应来刑冲克世，不可出行。
  The Self injuring the Other position is fit whether the journey is far or near; the Other controlling the Self is unfavourable for public and private business alike. My going to control him means the way I go is open; if the Other comes to punish, clash or control the Self, one must not go.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.05** 世爻安静而遇到日冲，一定去；发动而被合住，就留下。世爻暗中发动的，一定去；世爻旺相而安静的，逢到冲的日子一定去；世爻发动而化出合，或者被日、月、动爻合住的，必有事故，被阻滞不能走。觉子说：我曾试验过，也有应验在冲开的那天才走的。
  If the Self is still and meets the clash of the Day, one surely goes; if it moves and is held by a combination, one stays. If the Self moves in secret, one surely goes; if the Self is strong and still, one surely goes on the day that clashes it; if the Self moves and changes into a combination, or is held by a combination from the Day, the Month or a moving line, there is surely some affair, and one is obstructed and does not go. Juezi says: I have tried it and found that it sometimes answers by going on the day that clashes it open.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.47** 游魂卦是各宫的第七卦，归魂卦是各宫的第八卦。古人认为游魂可以走千里，我做这件事想要久的，游魂却不能久，心里没有定向，迁改不常：占身命，一生没有安家乐业的地方；占行人，游遍他乡；占出行，行止没有定准；占家宅，搬迁变动不常；占坟茔，亡灵不能安妥。野鹤说：必须以用神为主，然后拿这个来参考；如果舍掉用神、执着这个来断，就错了。归魂卦不出疆界，诸事拘泥不行，与游魂卦相反来断就可以。
  A Wandering Soul hexagram is the seventh hexagram of each palace and a Returning Soul hexagram the eighth. The ancients held that a Wandering Soul can travel a thousand li, but if I am undertaking something that I want to last, the Wandering Soul cannot last; the heart has no settled direction and changes are frequent. In divining for one's life, there is no place to settle and thrive all one's life; for a traveller, he roams other lands; for setting out, going and stopping are unfixed; for the house, moves and changes are frequent; for a tomb, the spirits of the dead cannot be at rest. Yehe says: take the Subject as the main thing first and then use this as a reference; to set the Subject aside and judge by this alone is a mistake. A Returning Soul hexagram does not leave the border and things are stuck and do not go; judge it as the opposite of a Wandering Soul hexagram.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.49** 占出仕、出行，世爻旺相得地，冲开墓库的月日遂心；世爻如果空破休囚，多半是去了不回来。占行人，用神化墓，或入动墓的，用神旺相的一定回来；用神空破休囚的，不是病在他乡，就是流落在异地。
  In divining for taking office or setting out, if the Self is strong and well placed, matters go as wished in the month or day that clashes the tomb open; if the Self is Void, broken, resting or trapped, he mostly goes and does not return. In divining for a traveller, a Subject that changes into a tomb or enters a moving tomb returns for certain if it is strong; if it is Void, broken, resting or trapped, he is either ill in a strange land or adrift in a foreign place.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.50** 动爻化进神是好事：世爻临寅木发动化出进神，按理应当就走，但因为月破，眼下未必能走，要出了这个月才行。书中例：申月乙卯日占出行，得屯卦变节卦：后来到亥月才动身，由燕地到粤地，第二年八月回来。我问：路上平安吗？他说：来去都托庇平安。我是因为子孙化进神，知道他平安，所以才问。应在亥月出行，是月破逢合的月份，这是动爻被月破、又化出日辰，怎么能说它不能进呢？
  A moving line changing into an Advancing spirit is a good thing: the Self sits on Yin-wood and moves and changes into an Advancing spirit, so by rights he should go at once, but because of the Month-break he may not be able to go now and can go only after the month is out. Worked example: in the Shen month, on a Yimao day, a man divined about setting out and got Zhun changing to Jie: he set out only in the Hai month, going from Yan to Yue, and came back in the eighth month of the next year. I asked: was the road safe? He said: coming and going I was sheltered and safe. I asked only because Output changes into an Advancing spirit, and I knew he would be safe. That it answered in the Hai month is the month in which a broken line meets a combination; this is a moving line broken by the Month and changing into the Day, so how can one say it cannot advance?

## 同行(Companions: the between lines, fellow travellers, sharing a boat)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/出行/同行.md`

- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.08** 间爻如果空，主途中没有阻滞；间爻又代表同伴，两个间爻都落旬空，宜自己单独走，免得被同伴拖累。
  If a between line is Void there is no obstruction on the way; the between lines also stand for companions, and if both are in the Void of their ten-day period it is fit to travel alone, so as to escape being burdened by companions.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.18** 子孙爻持世最吉，主碰上好同伴，路上平安；但如果是为了拜见贵人而出行就不适宜，因为子孙发动叫伤官，反而不利。
  Output holding the Self is the best omen: one meets good companions and the road is safe; but if one travels to call on a patron it is unfit, since Output moving is called wounding the official and is on the contrary unfavourable.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.21** 鬼爻在间爻上发动，不是同伴之间不和，就是同伴里有人生病；如果克世，自己会后悔。
  If Pressure moves on a between line, either the companions are at odds or one of them is ill; if it controls the Self, one will regret it.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.04** 两个间爻是同行、搭载的人：发动克世，一定遭他们的害；发动临着兄爻，一定破我的财；生合我的就吉；如果两个间爻同时空，不是中途受阻，就一定没有伴、一个人走。
  The two between lines are the people who travel with one or are carried along: moving to control the Self, they surely do one harm; moving with a Peer line, they surely cost me money; those that give me life or combine with me are lucky; and if both between lines are Void, either one is obstructed on the road or one travels alone without company.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.15** 同行、同住，以应爻为尊，同路、同船一样论：应爻伤了世位，会遭他的害；应爻入墓、空、绝、破，是辜负我的恩情；只适宜生合、互相扶助，永远靠他维持，像至亲一样。三合同路，事事遂意；六冲，走到半路就灰心。日常起居为害，看兄弟、官鬼发动；水路和睦清平，看子孙发动。占别人，以应为用；占亲戚，看所属的用神；不宜兄、鬼发动克世，最宜相合相生。
  For those who travel and stay together, the Other is the honoured one, and those on the same road or in the same boat are judged alike: if the Other injures the Self position, one suffers harm from him; if the Other is in the tomb, Void, extinct or broken, he is ungrateful for my kindness; it is fit only that they give life, combine and support each other, so that one can always rely on his help as on one's own kin. Three-harmony on the same road, everything goes as wished; six-clash, one loses heart halfway. For harm in daily life and lodging, look to a moving Peer or Pressure; for peace and harmony on the water, look to a moving Output. In divining for another person, the Other is the Subject; for a relative, look at that relative's own Subject; it is unfit for Peer or Pressure to move and control the Self, and best when they combine and generate.

## 路途(The road: dangers, thieves, storms, losses and gains on the way)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/出行/路途.md`

- **LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.04** 五爻是道路，官鬼落在五爻发动，路上一定多有惊险，不适宜出外，要知道是什么灾咎，按它所临的六神来断，白虎是风波，玄武是盗贼。二爻是住宅，在家求财，鬼爻动在二爻必然不利，按它所临的五行断，火鬼忌火烛之类；得子孙持世发动，差不多就没有害，如果没有子孙发动，宜迁换店铺来化解。
  The fifth line is the road: when Pressure sits on it and moves, there will surely be many frights and dangers on the way, and it is unfit to go out; to know what calamity, judge by the six spirits it carries, the White Tiger meaning storms and the Dark Tortoise thieves. The second line is the dwelling: for seeking wealth at home, Pressure moving on this line is surely unfavourable, judged by the element it carries, a Fire Pressure fearing fire and candles and the like; with Output holding the Self and moving there is hardly any harm, and with no Output moving it is resolved by moving the shop.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.04** 间爻代表往来经过的地方：动了，途中有阻滞；安静，往来平安没有阻碍；间爻临着财爻或福神发动，途中谋求的反比到了地头还好。
  The between lines are the places passed through going and coming: if they move there is obstruction on the way; if they are still, the going and coming is safe and unobstructed; if a between line carrying Wealth or Output moves, what is sought on the road is better than at the destination.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.14** 官鬼临着玄武，本来就是盗贼；如果和世爻相刑克，免不了为盗贼担惊受忧。
  Pressure on the Dark Tortoise is by nature thieves; if it punishes or controls the Self, one cannot escape fright and worry over thieves.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.15** 兄弟爻加上白虎、或忌神发动，或者鬼爻在巽宫发动来克世，都有风波险阻。
  If Peer with the White Tiger, or an Adversary, moves, or if Pressure in the Xun palace moves and controls the Self, there are storms and perilous obstruction.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.20** 兄弟爻主劫财，如果加上螣蛇又发动，必有光棍抢劫、损耗财物；没有制伏要提防，有制伏就无妨。
  Peer governs robbery of wealth; if with the Coiling Snake it moves, rogues will surely rob and damage one's goods; without restraint be on guard, with restraint there is no harm.
- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.22** 兄弟爻主耗费钱财：持世，多半是白白花费；不在世位上、从别人那里发动，是损耗我的钱。
  The Peer line governs the wasting of money: holding the Self it means mostly useless expense; if it is not on the Self but moves from another place, it wastes my money.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.06** 官鬼发动重叠，灾祸就重：带玄武动，担忧盗贼；带朱雀发动，提防官司口舌；带白虎，疾病缠身；带螣蛇，风波惊险；带勾陈发动，事情牵连纠缠；带青龙发动，要戒嫖赌。鬼爻发在震、乾，有车马之患；发在兑、坎，要虑风波；坤、艮的鬼爻，在山间岭野遇灾殃；离、巽的官爻，在炉场、树林、窑窖里遭难。
  Pressure moving in force means heavy calamity: with the Dark Tortoise moving, worry about thieves; with the Vermilion Bird, guard against lawsuits and quarrels; with the White Tiger, illness that lingers; with the Coiling Snake, storms and frightening danger; with Hook Earth moving, entanglement in others' affairs; with the Azure Dragon moving, beware of whoring and gambling. ## 方位(Direction: which way to go and which to avoid)

- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.44** 避乱要避到生世的方向和子孙的方向，还要按旺衰来分辨。书中例：巳月丙戌日同乡的人占避乱，得乾卦变大有卦：众人认为子孙属水，有金动来生它，北方为吉。我说：不对。兄爻动化出进神，是破财之象；午火当令，生助世爻，往南避才吉。众人信了我的话，都往南避。后来贼果然从北边来，放火把村子烧光，房屋稻谷都成了灰烬，这是申金兄爻发动破财的缘故。所以生世的方向吉，子孙的方向也吉；又要用旺衰来分别，火虽然是鬼的方向，生我又有什么妨碍。
  In avoiding disorder, go to the direction that gives life to the Self and the direction of Output, and tell them apart by strength and weakness. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Bingxu day, men of the same village divined which way to flee the disorder and got Qian changing to Dayou. The company held that Output belongs to Water, and Metal moves to give it life, so the north is lucky. I said: no. The Peer line moves and changes into an Advancing spirit, which is a sign of losing wealth; Wu-fire is in command and gives life to the Self, so it is lucky to flee south. They believed me and all fled south. Afterwards the bandits did come from the north, set fire to the village and left, and the houses and the rice were all ashes, which was the loss of wealth from the moving Shen-metal Peer. So the direction that gives life to the Self is lucky, and the direction of Output is also lucky; and one must also distinguish by strength and weakness: though Fire is the direction of Pressure, what harm is there in its giving life to me.

- **LY.TRAVEL.HJC.103** 财爻如果受克，一定要伤财；如果又遇到兄弟发动，必定破费钱财。
  If the Wealth line is controlled, money is surely lost; if a Peer line also moves, there will surely be expense and loss of money.
- **LY.TRAVEL.ZS.53** 以上都是防灾虑患：只要得子孙持世，安稳如泰山，该走就走，有吉无凶；如果得官鬼持世，忧疑难解，要加意防止，即使该走，也宜暂时停下。只有陈言谏诤的，又当别论；如果真是为国计民生，捐躯报主，即使官鬼持世，也应该去。前面说的各种事，以子孙持世为吉，官鬼持世为忧。这两个如果卦中都不出现，只能再占；只要有一个出现，就不必再占了。然而事情有缓急的不同：假如江海开船，如果得官鬼持世，难道会永远不开行吗？哪里知道早开一天遇凶，晚走半个时辰就免祸；如果今天占得官鬼持世，暂且不要开船，明天再占，后天又占，只要遇到子孙持世，马上开船。事情有相似的，照这个办。
  All the above are cases of guarding against calamity and worry: if Output holds the Self, one is as safe as Mount Tai, and goes if one should, with luck and no ill; if Pressure holds the Self, the worry cannot be resolved, one must take extra care, and even if one should go it is fit to stop for the time being. Only those who offer advice and remonstrate are to be judged otherwise: if it is truly for the nation and the people and one gives one's life to repay the sovereign, then even with Pressure holding the Self one should go. In the matters just discussed, Output holding the Self is lucky and Pressure holding the Self is a worry. If neither shows in the hexagram, one can only divine again; if one of them appears, there is no need to divine further. But matters differ in urgency: suppose a boat is to set out on a river or the sea and one gets Pressure holding the Self, is it that it will never sail? One does not realise that setting out a day early meets ill, and leaving half an hour later escapes disaster; if today one gets Pressure holding the Self, do not sail for now; divine again tomorrow, and again the day after, and as soon as Output holds the Self, sail. In similar matters, do the same.
