## 总则(Principles: how the day is found)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/应期/总则.md`

- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.17** 要知道原神。先看用神是哪个爻，生用神的爻就是原神。如果用神旬空、月破、衰弱，或者伏藏不现，得到原神发动来生它，或者日辰月建作原神生它，必须等用神出旬、出破、得令、值日，所求的才一定顺遂。如果用神旺相，原神休囚不动，或者动而变墓、变绝、变克、变破、变退，或者被日辰月建克制，都不能生用神，是用神的根基被伤了，不但没有益处，反而有损。
  One must know the Support. First see which line is the Subject; the line that gives life to the Subject is the Support. If the Subject is Void, struck by the Month-break, weak, or hidden and not appearing, and the Support moves to give it life, or the Day or the Month acts as the Support to give it life, one must wait until the Subject comes out of the period, out of the break, gets its season or meets its ruling day, and then what is sought will surely come. If the Subject is strong, but the Support is resting and still, or moves and changes into the tomb, extinction, control, break or retreat, or is controlled by the Day or the Month, none of these can give life to the Subject: the root of the Subject is injured, and it is not only no help but a loss.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.19** 卦理只论得中和之道：乱动就要搜寻独静的爻，安静就要看逢冲的一日，月破要出破、填合，旬空要出旬、值日，动的等合，静的等冲，克处逢生、绝处逢生、冲中逢合、合处逢冲，这些法则就是「太过的损掉它才成，不及的补上它才利」。
  The principle of the hexagram speaks only of the way of balance: if lines move wildly, search for the sole still line; if all are still, look to the one day that brings a clash; the Month-break must leave the break or be filled and combined; the Void must leave the period or meet its ruling day; a moving line waits for a combination and a still one waits for a clash; life met in a control, life met in extinction, a combination met in a clash, and a clash met in a combination: these rules are what "what is excessive succeeds by being reduced, what is lacking profits by being supplied" means.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.23** 如果遇到休囚，必待生旺才成事。断日期的方法不能执着于一种，应当用活法推断，才不会有差错。如果用爻被合住，就以冲它的日期来断。或者用爻休囚，必在生旺的时期才能成事，所以无气的当以旺相月来断。如果用爻旺相不动，就以冲动它的月日来断。如果用爻有气发动，就以合它的日子来断。或者有气发动合日辰，或日辰临它而动，或日辰临它而动来生合世爻身爻，就以本日来断。如果用爻受制，就以制煞它的日月来断。如果用爻多得时旺、发动，又遇生扶，这是太旺，应当以墓库的日月来断。如果用爻无气发动而遇生扶，就以生扶它的月日来断。如果用爻入墓，应当以冲墓、冲用的月日来断。如果用爻旬空安静，就以出旬逢冲的日子来断。如果用爻旬空发动，就以出旬值日来断。如果用爻发动旬空被合，就以出旬冲日来断。如果用爻旬空安静被冲，就以出旬合日来断。如果用爻旬空发动逢冲，叫冲实，就以本日来断。以上的断法只是撮其大要，其中最微妙的道理，学的人自己应当融会贯通、灵活变化，分清轻重，辨别用神和忌神，就一定没有差错了。
  If it meets resting or trapped, it succeeds only in a strong season. The method of judging the day cannot be held to one rule, and should be worked out flexibly, so that there is no mistake. If the Subject line is combined shut, judge by the day that clashes it. If the Subject is resting or trapped, it can succeed only in a period of life and strength, so one without breath is judged by a strong month. If the Subject is strong and still, judge by the month or day that clashes it into motion. If the Subject has breath and moves, judge by the day that combines it. If it has breath and moves to combine with the Day, or the Day sits on it and it moves, or the Day sits on it and it moves to give life to or combine with the Self or Body line, judge by that very day. If the Subject is controlled, judge by the month or day of the star that restrains the controller. If the Subject is in season, strong and moving, and also meets life and support, it is too strong, and should be judged by the month or day of the tomb or storehouse. If the Subject has no breath, moves and meets life and support, judge by the month or day that gives it life and support. If the Subject enters the tomb, judge by the month or day that clashes the tomb or the Subject. If the Subject is Void and still, judge by the day it meets a clash after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and moving, judge by its ruling day after coming out of the period. If the Subject moves and is Void and combined, judge by the clashing day after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and still and is clashed, judge by the combining day after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and moving and meets a clash, which is called clashed solid, judge by that very day. The above only picks out the main points; as for the most subtle principle in it, the student should digest it and turn it flexibly, tell heavy from light and distinguish the Subject from the Adversary, and then there will surely be no mistake.
- **LY.TIMING.ZS.07** 元神来帮助来扶持，要紧的是看用神的衰旺；忌神来冲来克，必须看元气的兴衰：详见元神忌神章。
  When the Support comes to help and support, what matters is the strength or weakness of the Subject; when the Adversary comes to clash and control, one must look at the rise or fall of the Support: see the chapter on the Support and the Adversary.

## 静动(Still and moving lines)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/应期/静动.md`

- **LY.TIMING.ZS.01** 主事的爻安静而遇到值日、遇到冲：比如主事爻落在子水不动，后来遇到子日、午日就应验；其余照这个类推。主事的爻发动而遇到合、遇到值日：比如主事爻落在子水发动，后来遇到子日、丑日就应验；其余照这个类推。
  The line that rules the matter, still, meets its ruling day or a clash: for example, if the line ruling the matter sits on Zi-water and does not move, it answers on a later Zi day or Wu day; the rest follow this. The line ruling the matter, moving, meets a combination or its ruling day: for example, if the line ruling the matter sits on Zi-water and moves, it answers on a later Zi day or Chou day; the rest follow this.

## 旺衰(Strength and weakness)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/应期/旺衰.md`

- **LY.TIMING.ZS.02** 太旺的，遇到墓、遇到冲：比如主事爻落在午火，又遇到巳午月日占卦，或者卦中巳午爻太多，后来遇到亥子日应验，又有戌日应验，那是火入墓。衰绝的，遇到生、遇到旺：比如主事爻属金，在巳午月日占卦，就是休囚无气，后来遇到土月土日，或到秋令当时，就旺了；其余照这个类推。
  A line that is too strong meets the tomb or a clash: for example, if the line ruling the matter sits on Wu-fire and the divination falls in a Si or Wu month or day, or there are too many Si or Wu lines in the hexagram, it answers on a later Hai or Zi day, and also on a Xu day, which is Fire entering the tomb. A line that is weak or extinct meets life or strength: for example, if the line ruling the matter is Metal and the divination falls in a Si or Wu month or day, it is resting and without breath; later, in an Earth month or day, or when autumn takes command, it is strong; the rest follow this.

## 墓库(The tomb and storehouse)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/应期/墓库.md`

- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.21** 墓中的人，不冲就不发。大概用爻入墓就多有阻滞，诸事费力难成，必须等日辰、动爻冲它，或冲克它的墓爻，才有用。古书说：「冲空就起，破墓就开。」
  A man in the tomb does not rise without a clash. Generally a Subject line entering the tomb is much hindered, and all matters are done with effort and hard to complete; one must wait for the Day or a moving line to clash it or to clash and control its tomb line before it is of use. The old books say: "a clash raises the Void, and breaking the tomb opens it."
- **LY.TIMING.ZS.03** 入了三墓，都喜欢被冲开：比如主事爻落在午火，火的墓在戌，后来遇到辰日就应验；其余照这个类推。
  A line that has entered one of the three tombs is glad to be clashed open: for example, if the line ruling the matter sits on Wu-fire, whose tomb is Xu, it answers on a later Chen day; the rest follow this.

## 合冲(Combination and clash)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/应期/合冲.md`

- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.20** 动而遇到合就被绊住。凡是动爻没遇到合才算动，如果有合就被绊住不能动了；既然不能动，就不能生物克物了。如果日辰合它，必须等冲它本爻的日子到，才能应事的吉凶；如果旁边的爻发动来合它，必须等冲那个旁爻的日子到，才能应事的吉凶。假如用丑土财被子日合住，等到未日应事；子爻合它的，等午日应事。又比如子孙爻发动而被日辰合住，就不能生财，要等冲动子孙的时候到，才有财；其余照这个类推。
  A line that moves and meets a combination is held fast. A moving line counts as moving only if it meets no combination; if there is a combination it is held fast and cannot move, and being unable to move it cannot give life to or control anything. If the Day combines it, one must wait for the day that clashes its own line before the luck or ill of the matter answers; if a neighbouring line moves to combine it, one must wait for the day that clashes that neighbouring line. For example, if the Wealth is Chou-earth and the Zi day combines it, wait for the Wei day to answer; if a Zi line combines it, wait for the Wu day to answer. Again, if the Output line moves and is combined shut by the Day, it cannot give life to Wealth, and one must wait for the time that clashes the Output into motion before there is wealth; the rest follow this.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.22** 如果逢合住，必须冲破才成功。卦中用神、忌神遇到日辰合，或自己化出合，或有动爻来合它，不论吉凶，都不见效，必须等冲破的日期才应事的吉凶。假如用爻动来生世，凡事容易成，如果遇到合住，又变得阻滞，必须等冲它的日子，事情才有成。以下都是断日期的办法。
  If it meets a combination that holds it shut, it must be clashed open before it succeeds. When the Subject or the Adversary in the hexagram meets a combination with the Day, or changes into a combination of itself, or a moving line comes to combine it, whether luck or ill, it has no effect, and the luck or ill of the matter answers only on the day it is clashed open. For example, if the Subject line moves to give life to the Self, everything is easily accomplished, but if it meets a combination that holds it shut it becomes blocked, and one must wait for the day that clashes it before the matter succeeds. What follows are all methods of judging the day.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.24** 三合成局，遇到日冲叫暗动（指静爻被日冲），叫填实（指动爻落空被日辰冲），叫破（指月建冲）。它被冲破，必须等到相合的日期到，才应事的吉凶。如果一爻静、两爻发的，必须等那一爻静的值日应事。如果一爻静而逢空，或动而逢空，或变化而逢空的，等到它出空的日期应事的吉凶。如果空而逢合，静而逢合，动而合的，必须等冲的日期到，才应事的吉凶。如果自己化合或与日辰合，或自己化墓或墓在日辰的，必须等到冲的日期（这是说三爻齐发、其中两爻没有病，指自化的那一爻说）。如果自己化绝或绝在日辰的，必须等到生的日期（也是指有一爻有病的说）。
  In a three-harmony frame, meeting a clash from the Day is called secret motion (a still line clashed by the Day), being filled solid (a moving Void line clashed by the Day), or breaking (clashed by the Month). When it is clashed or broken, the luck or ill of the matter answers only when the time of combination comes. If one line is still and two move, one must wait for the still line to meet its ruling day. If one line is still and Void, or moves and is Void, or changes into the Void, wait for the time it comes out of the Void. If it is Void and meets a combination, or still and meets a combination, or moves and combines, one must wait for the time of the clash. If it changes into a combination of itself or combines with the Day, or changes into the tomb of itself or is in the tomb of the Day, one must wait for the time of the clash (this is said of three lines all moving where two have no ailment, and means the one that changes of itself). If it changes into extinction of itself or is extinct on the Day, one must wait for the time of life (also said of the case where one line has an ailment).
- **LY.TIMING.ZS.04** 遇到六合，也适宜相互冲击：主事爻与日月作合，或发动与别的爻合，或发动而化出合，不管是凶是吉，必定等到冲开它的月日才应验。就像主象落在子，与丑作合，后来遇到午日、未日应验。其余照这个类推。
  Meeting a six-combine, it is likewise fit to be struck by a clash: if the line ruling the matter forms a combination with the Month or the Day, or moves and combines with another line, or moves and changes into a combination, whether it is ill or lucky it answers only in the month or day that clashes it open. For example, if the principal image sits on Zi and forms a combination with Chou, it answers on a later Wu or Wei day. The rest follow this.

## 空破(The Void and the Month-break)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/应期/空破.md`

- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.01** 旬空的爻，没有生扶又被克，到底是空；有生扶没有克，等到时候就有用。书中例：巳月戊戌日占求财，得益卦：辰土财爻持世，因为落旬空，戌日冲它，叫冲空则起，当天就得到；果然应验。应在当天，是因为戌日也是财星来冲我。这是冲空有用。
  A Void line with no life and under control is empty in the end; one with life and no control is useful when its time comes. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined for wealth and got Yi: the Wealth Chen-earth holds the Self and falls into the Void, and the Xu day clashes it, which is "a clash raises up the Void", so he gets it that very day; and it was borne out. It answered on the same day because the Xu day is also the Wealth star clashing me. This is a Void that is useful when clashed.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.03** 旬空的爻有生扶无克，等到时候就有用。书中例：申月丁卯日占谒见贵人求财，得同人卦：亥水官星持世落旬空，出了这旬到亥日一定见到；月建财爻生世，财利如意；果然应验。这是出空待用。
  A Void line with life and no control is useful when its time comes. Worked example: in the Shen month, on a Dingmao day, a man divined about seeing a noble to seek wealth and got Tongren: the Pressure Hai-water holds the Self and falls into the Void, so once the ten-day period is out he will surely see him on the Hai day; the Month and the Wealth line give life to the Self, so gain comes as hoped; and it was borne out. This is waiting for the Void to be out and then being useful.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.04** 空的爻发动变出生它的爻，就不算空。书中例：子月癸酉日有人自己占婚事，得恒卦变鼎卦：世爻官、应爻财，算得地；现在戌土财爻落旬空，化出巳火回头生它，动而被生就不空，第二天去求一定答应；果然第二天巳时答应了婚事。
  A Void line that moves and changes into a line that gives it life is not counted as Void. Worked example: in the Zi month, on a Guiyou day, a man divined for his own marriage and got Heng changing to Ding: the Self is Pressure and the Other is Wealth, which counts as well placed; now the Wealth Xu-earth falls into the Void but changes into Si-fire which gives it life in return, and a line that moves and is given life is not Void, so if he asks the next day it will surely be granted; indeed the next day, in the Si hour, the marriage was agreed.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.15** 卦中的爻遇到旬空，是神机在这里显现。如果旺相而旬空，或休囚而发动，有日辰生扶、动爻生扶，动爻变空，伏而旺相，这些旬空到底有用，不过等它出旬值日：有合空、冲起、冲实、填补的办法，后面各卷的占验里注明。如果休囚安静，或被日辰克、动爻克，伏而被克，静而逢月破，遇到这样的旬空，叫做真空，到底是空了。
  A line in the Void in the hexagram is where the working of the spirits shows. A line that is strong and Void, or resting and moving, with life and support from the Day and from moving lines, or moving to change into the Void, or hidden but strong, has a Void that is in the end useful, and only waits to come out of the period and meet its ruling day: there are the ways of combining the Void, clashing it up, clashing it solid and filling it, set out in the verified examples in the later volumes. A line that is resting and still, or controlled by the Day or a moving line, or hidden and controlled, or still and meeting the Month-break, with such a Void is called a true Void and is empty in the end.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.16** 卦中月破的爻，是关键因由所现。动的也能生克别的爻，变出的也能生克本爻——目下虽然破，出了这个月就不破；今天虽然破，到值日就不破。月破最喜欢逢合、填实，远的应在年月，近的应在日时。如果破而安静，又碰上旬空衰弱，遇到动爻、月建、日辰克害，这样的月破，叫做真破，到底是破了！
  A line struck by the Month-break is where the pivot of the matter shows. A moving one can also give life to or control other lines, and a changed one can give life to or control its own line: though it is broken now, it is not broken once the month is out; though it is broken today, it is not broken on its ruling day. The Month-break most loves to meet a combination or be filled; far answers by year and month, near by day and hour. If it is broken and still, and also meets the Void and weakness, and is harmed by moving lines, the Month or the Day, such a Month-break is called a true break and is broken in the end!
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.18** 卦中月破的爻，发动旺相，或者遇到动爻生合、日辰生合，或者化出回头生合，不过是在月内不能成吉凶，出了月、值日、合补，也能吉能凶。张星元说月破没法解救，一概当凶推。卦中旬空的爻，或者旺相安静，休囚发动，日辰生合或冲它，或是变出的，或者伏着而有提拔的，屡试屡验，应在出旬。
  A line struck by the Month-break that moves and is strong, or meets life or combination from a moving line or the Day, or changes into a turning-back life or combination, is merely unable to make luck or ill within the month; once the month is out, or on its ruling day, or when filled and combined, it can bring luck or ill. Zhang Xingyuan says the Month-break cannot be rescued and judges it ill across the board. A line in the Void that is strong and still, or resting and moving, or given life, combined or clashed by the Day, or one that is changed out, or hidden with someone to lift it, has been borne out again and again to answer when the period is out.
- **LY.TIMING.ZS.05** 月破的，喜欢遇到填实或合：比如子月占卦，主事爻落在午火，是月破，后来遇到未日应验，叫做破而逢合；又有遇到午月应验，填实了就不破了；其余照这个类推。旬空的最喜欢被填实或冲：详见旬空章。
  A line struck by the Month-break is glad to meet a filling or a combination: for example, if the divination falls in the Zi month and the line ruling the matter sits on Wu-fire, it is broken by the Month, and it answers on a later Wei day, which is called a break that meets a combination; it may also answer in the Wu month, when it is filled and no longer broken; the rest follow this. A Void line most loves to be filled or clashed: see the chapter on the Void.
- **LY.TIMING.ZS.11** 世爻空了而元神发动的，必须等元神遇到值日：比如甲辰旬里占求财，得困卦变坎卦，断亥日得财；其余照这个类推。世爻衰而元神安静的，必须等元神被冲：比如秋天占谋划的事，得困卦，后来遇到巳日事情成了；其余照这个类推。
  When the Self is Void and the Support moves, one must wait until the Support meets its ruling day: for example, in a Jiachen decade a man divined for wealth and got Kun changing to Kan, and it was judged he would get wealth on a Hai day; the rest follow this. When the Self is weak and the Support is still, one must wait until the Support is clashed: for example, in autumn a man divined about a scheme and got Kun, and the matter was accomplished on a later Si day; the rest follow this.
- **LY.TIMING.ZS.12** 诸书都把用神临月破叫做悖时，像枯根朽木，逢生生不起，逢伤伤得更重；虽然现在卦里，有也像没有；伏在卦中，终究难透出来；就算有日辰生它，也不能生；动而作忌神的，也不能为害，作变爻的，也不能伤克动爻。野鹤说：我验证过，动就能伤爻，变就能伤动，为什么？神灵的兆示机缘在动，事情没有吉凶就不会动；既然动了，就有祸福的根基；目下虽然破，出了这个月就不破；今天虽然破，到实破的日子就不破，逢合的日子不破，近的应在日时，远的应在年月；只有静而不动，又没有日辰动爻生助的，才到底破了。
  All the books call a Subject on the Month-break "out of season", like a withered root or rotten wood, which if given life is not raised and if injured is injured the more; though it is on the hexagram it is as if absent; hidden in the hexagram, it is hard to bring out in the end; even given life by the Day, it cannot be raised; moving as the Adversary, it cannot do harm; as a changed line, it cannot injure the moving line. Yehe says: I have found it borne out that it can injure a line if it moves and can injure the moving line if it changes into it. Why? The spirits give their sign in moving, and if a matter had neither luck nor ill it would not move; once it moves, there is a basis of fortune or misfortune. Though it is broken now, it is not broken once the month is out; though it is broken today, it is not broken on the day that fills the break, nor on the day it is combined; near answers by day and hour, far by year and month. Only one that is still, does not move, and has no life or support from the Day or a moving line is broken to the end.
- **LY.TIMING.ZS.13** 野鹤说：旺的不算空，动的不算空，动而化空、伏而旺相，都不算空，有日建动爻生扶的，也不算空；月破算空，有气但不动也算空，伏而被克也算空，真空是空。（真空的意思：春天的土、夏天的金、秋天的木、整个冬天遇火，是真空。）我刚学占卜时，凡是遇到旬空，茫然分不清，想断成到底全空，却又应在填实的日子而不空，当作不空，却又到底全空；后来得到多占的办法，凡是遇到旬空，叫他再占一卦，卦吉的，许他出旬就不空；卦凶的，许他是空了。
  Yehe says: a strong line is not Void, a moving line is not Void, a line that moves and changes into the Void or is hidden and strong is not Void, and one given life and support by the Day Branch or a moving line is not Void; a line struck by the Month-break counts as Void, a line with breath that does not move also counts as Void, a hidden line that is controlled counts as Void, and a true Void is Void. (A true Void: Earth in spring, Metal in summer, Wood in autumn, and Fire in all three winter months.) When I first learned divination I was at a loss whenever I met the Void: I wanted to judge it wholly empty, yet it answered on the day of filling and was not empty; I took it as not empty, yet it was wholly empty in the end. Later I got the method of divining several times: whenever the Void is met, have the man divine again; if the hexagram is lucky, promise that it is not Void once the period is out; if the hexagram is ill, promise that it is Void.

## 天时(Weather and sky)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/应期/天时.md`

- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.12** 原神旬空安静，要等出旬逢冲。书中例：未月戊戌日因大旱占哪天有雨，得观卦：月建未土父母爻是用神，日辰帮扶，有雨一定大；但巳火官爻是原神，落旬空安静，静了必须冲它，空了必须等出旬；断辛亥日得大雨；果然到那天申酉时得雨五寸。

Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined on what day there would be rain in a great drought and got Guan: the Resource line Wei-earth of the Month is the Subject, with the Day helping it, so the rain will surely be heavy; but the Pressure Si-fire is the Support, Void and still, and being still it must be clashed and being Void it must wait for the period to be out; it was judged that heavy rain would come on the Xinhai day; and on that day, in the Shen and You hours, five inches of rain fell.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.14** 用神被空爻回头克，要等那个空爻出空，还要看克它的是谁。书中例：未月甲辰日占哪天有大雨，得小过卦变革卦：日辰辰土父母爻是用神，发动，月建帮它，又值土旺用事，辰土旺得没法说，下雨一定不小；但不宜化出卯木旬空、卯木回头之克；虽然有申金化出酉金进神克木救土，可是卯木旬空，空叫做畏避，躲开金的克，以致辰土父爻终究被卯木所病；必定到甲寅旬乙卯日卯木出空值日，叫出头难避，戌土暗动助金克它；而卯木已经受金克，不能为害辰土，必定等甲寅旬乙卯日下雨。到期竟然没有，到立秋辛酉日申时才下雨。应在立秋后的酉日，为什么？明显是申化酉，就是申月酉日。卯木出空值日到底被克不尽，交申月克它，酉日又冲它，才得下雨。我因为这一卦，学问又进了一层！

Mao-wood coming out of the Void and ruling was in the end not wholly controlled; the Shen month controls it and the You day clashes it, and only then did the rain fall. Through this hexagram my learning went up another level!

## 病程(The course of an illness)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/应期/病程.md`

- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.05** 用神旬空，有原神相生，等到出空的时候。书中例：午月癸丑日占妻子的病哪天好，得萃卦变比卦：卯木财爻是用神，落旬空，有亥水原神生它，第二天一定好；旁边有人问：卯爻旬空该断卯日，为什么说寅日？我说：你不懂这个办法，到了甲寅日卯木已经出空了，寅木也是用星；果然先好了一天，应在甲寅日。
  A Subject in the Void with the Support giving it life waits until the Void is out. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Guichou day, a man divined on what day his wife's illness would be cured and got Cui changing to Bi: the Wealth Mao-wood is the Subject and falls into the Void, with the Support Hai-water giving it life, so she will surely be better the next day. Someone beside him asked: a Void Mao line should be judged on the Mao day, so why the Yin day? I said: you do not know the method; when the Jiayin day comes Mao-wood is already out of the Void, and Yin-wood is also a star of the Subject; indeed she got better a day early, answering on the Jiayin day.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.06** 近病遇空就好，但用神化出的又是空，要等出空的日子。书中例：寅月庚戌日占儿子的病什么时候好，得姤卦变无妄卦：亥水子孙化出寅木旬空，近病遇空就好，但嫌亥水化出的寅木落旬空，受不到酉金的生，必须等寅日才好；果然应验，这是化空出空。
  A near illness meets the Void and is cured, but when what the Subject changes into is also Void, wait for the day it comes out. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Gengxu day, a man divined when his son's illness would be cured and got Gou changing to Wuwang: the Output Hai-water changes into Yin-wood, which is Void; a near illness meeting the Void is cured, but the trouble is that Yin-wood, into which Hai-water changes, is Void and cannot receive life from You-metal, so he must wait for the Yin day; and it was borne out, a case of changing into the Void and then coming out of it.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.08** 近病遇空就好，遇合就死。书中例：酉月庚辰日占岳父的近病，得师卦变升卦：酉金岳父爻落旬空，近病遇空就好；日辰合它，近病遇合就死；又不宜忌神来克它，这个病必定危险；问危险在哪天，我说：午火自己化旬空，旬空克不了它，近病遇空不死，旬内不死，要防乙酉日；果然在乙酉日卯时死了。
  A near illness meeting the Void is cured, and meeting a combination means death. Worked example: in the You month, on a Gengchen day, a man divined about his father-in-law's recent illness and got Shi changing to Sheng: the father-in-law line You-metal falls into the Void, and a near illness meeting the Void is cured; but the Day combines it, and a near illness meeting a combination means death; moreover it is unfit that the Adversary holds and controls it, so this illness is surely dangerous. Asked on what day the danger falls, I said: Wu-fire itself changes into the Void, and a Void cannot control it; a near illness meeting the Void does not die, and he will not die within the ten-day period, so guard against the Yiyou day; indeed he died on the Yiyou day in the Mao hour.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.09** 伏神得不到提拔，终究是徒劳。书中例：酉月壬辰日占儿子的病，得大过卦：午火子孙伏在世爻亥水之下，月建生助它，亥水克它，眼下用神落旬空不受克，到甲午日难逃了；果然死在午日午时，这叫伏无提拔。
  A hidden spirit not lifted up is in vain in the end. Worked example: in the You month, on a Renchen day, a man divined about his son's illness and got Daguo: the Output Wu-fire is hidden under the Self line Hai-water; the Month gives it life and Hai-water controls it; for now the Subject is Void and is not hurt by the control, but on the Jiawu day he cannot escape; and indeed he died on the Wu day in the Wu hour, which is what is meant by a hidden spirit with no lift.
- **LY.TIMING.BSZZ.11** 近病遇空不死，遇六合就死。书中例：丑月甲午日有人占父亲的近病，得复卦变噬嗑卦：巳火父母是用神，落旬空，日辰拱它，近病遇空不死，但不利六合卦，六合就死；我想用神空和六合可以相抵，偏偏不宜世上忌神暗动，外卦巳酉丑合成金局去助水克它，此病必凶；他问：凶在哪天？我说：巳日亥日克冲巳火，还在旬空里没有妨碍，恐怕危险在出旬的辛亥日；果然到那天死了，这是应在出旬又被冲克。

Worked example: in the Chou month, on a Jiawu day, a man divined about his father's recent illness and got Fu changing to Shihe: the Resource Si-fire is the Subject and falls into the Void, with the Day embracing it; a near illness meeting the Void does not die, but a six-combine hexagram is unfavourable, and a six-combine means death. I thought the Void Subject and the six-combine could offset each other, but what is unfit is the Adversary on the Self line moving secretly, and the outer trigram Si, You and Chou combining into a Metal frame that helps the water to control it, so this illness is surely ill. He asked: on what day does the ill fall? I said: on a Si or a Hai day it is controlled or clashed, but while it is still in the Void there is no harm; I fear the danger lies on the Xinhai day when it comes out of the ten-day period; and indeed he died that day.
