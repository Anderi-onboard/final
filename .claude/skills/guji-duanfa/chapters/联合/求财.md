## 总则(Principles: what stands for money)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/求财/总则.md`

- **LY.WEALTH.CA.01** 求财做买卖，以财为主；财发动而临身，钱财一定拿得到；卦里没有财，或者财落空，一切谋求都靠不住。
  In seeking wealth and trade Wealth is the chief; Wealth moving and on the Body, the money is sure to be taken; if the hexagram has no Wealth, or Wealth falls void, no scheme of any kind can be relied on.
- **LY.WEALTH.CA.18** 凡占卦问求财：父母动就艰难，官鬼动就有灾；子孙旺、财明现，一定得利；兄弟兴旺，迟滞和是非就来。
  Whoever casts to ask about money: Resource moving means hardship and Pressure moving means disaster; Output strong and Wealth showing, profit is sure; Peer rising, delay and quarrels come.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.01** 占财以妻财为用神，子孙为原神（财的来源）；吉凶先看这两爻。
  For money, the Wealth line is the Subject and Output is its Support, the source of money; judge the outcome from these two lines first.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.43** 《黄金策》求财章的结语：谋生的门路多，占法要随所问之事变；以诚敬之心求卦。
  The closing words of the Golden Strategy's chapter on wealth: there are many ways to make a living, and the reading must fit the particular matter; ask with sincerity and reverence.
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.01** 公事用官爻，私事用财爻：占买卖钱财、占家事、占婚姻、占求财，都看财爻。天下的事总起来不出财、官两个字；占官必用父母，占财必用子孙；兄弟是破财的人，既不是主也不是辅，不必去看。
  Public business takes the Pressure line and private business the Wealth line: trade and money, household affairs, marriage and money-seeking all look to the Wealth line. The affairs of the world come down to the two words Wealth and Pressure; seeking office always uses Resource and seeking wealth always uses Output; Peer is the one who breaks Wealth, neither chief nor helper, and need not be looked at.
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.08** 有本钱求利：要财爻持世，应旺相有气，才大吉；财爻没气，有也不多；财爻空亡，那财一定没有，还要防破失；财爻生旺可以加倍，休囚减半；遇冲，快到手时有阻碍。
  Seeking profit with capital: the Wealth line must sit on the Self and the Other be strong and with breath for great good fortune; a Wealth line without breath gives little even if there is some; a Wealth line in Void means there is surely none, and beware of breakage and loss; born or strong, it can double, resting or trapped, it halves; clashed, there is an obstruction just as it reaches the hand.
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.09** 空手求财：虽有财爻，还要官鬼旺相才算全吉；财爻旺相而卦里没有官鬼，财虽可求却实际得不到；有官鬼没有财，即使有高明的手艺也得不到财；两样都要齐全，才是大吉。
  Seeking money empty-handed: even with a Wealth line, Pressure must also be strong to be fully auspicious; a strong Wealth line with no Pressure in the hexagram can be sought yet in fact cannot be had; Pressure with no Wealth gives no money even to a fine skill; both must be there for great good fortune.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.01** 求财本身艰险，卜卦是为了决疑；出外求财重在分祸福，在家求财重在分得失。见妻财、子孙为吉，见兄弟、官鬼为凶。
  Seeking money is hazardous, and we divine to settle doubt; for money sought abroad the question is danger or safety, at home it is gain or loss. Wealth and Output are friends; Peer and Pressure are enemies.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.02** 将本求财看妻财：财爻旺相、克世或生世吉，世克财凶；卦有两财，外旺内冲本少利多，内旺外冲只能保本；内外财死绝、财不上卦或空亡则折本；财绝逢生仍有利。空手求财（百工技艺）看官鬼：鬼旺财厚；《断易天机》本说官鬼所临六神指财的来路（青龙馈送、朱雀文书口舌、勾陈稳当、螣蛇靠人带挈、白虎诈取、玄武暗昧）。
  Trading with capital: read the Wealth line; thriving and controlling or generating the Self line is good, the Self line controlling Wealth is bad; with two Wealth lines, outer strong and inner clashed means small capital and large profit, inner strong and outer clashed means only the capital is kept; both dead, absent or void means losing the capital; Wealth at extinction meeting birth still profits. Earning without capital (crafts and trades): read the Pressure line; strong Pressure, ample money. The Duanyi Tianji text adds that the spirit on the Pressure line shows where the money comes from (Azure Dragon gifts, Vermilion Bird documents or talk, Hook Earth steady, Coiling Snake through others, White Tiger fraud, Dark Tortoise hidden dealings).
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.01** 有本钱去求财，以财爻为用神：不能空无，逢旬空月破，不但没有利，连本也亏；财旺来克世爻，利息滚滚，生世，财源滚滚。子孙发动是生财之神，会青龙是生财的大道；兄弟动而子孙不动，本利都没有；父母动、兄弟不动再加白虎，克伤子孙这个元神，财得不到生助，是断利的根源。福德发动财源增，兄弟发动资本亏。
  Seeking money with capital, Wealth is the Subject: it must not be void or absent; with Void or Month-break there is no profit and the capital is lost too. Wealth strong and controlling the Self brings interest in a flood, and generating the Self, a flowing source of wealth. Output moving is the spirit that gives birth to Wealth, and meeting the Azure Dragon it is the great road to wealth; Peer moving with Output still leaves neither capital nor profit; Resource moving with Peer still and the White Tiger added controls Output, the Support of Wealth, so Wealth has nothing to feed it and the root of profit is cut. Output moving increases the source of wealth; Peer moving wounds the capital.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.02** 空手（没本钱）问利，是九流、工匠、靠手艺吃饭一类人的事：以官爻为主，要旺相并生世、合世，又喜财爻兴旺来扶助，最吉；财爻是官的元神，不能空；子孙发动伤官，用神就没用了；父母发动，空手求财没有妨碍，有本钱的却遭殃。
  Seeking profit empty-handed is for the trades, craftsmen and people who work for their food: Pressure is the chief; it must be strong and generate or combine with the Self, and it is best of all if a strong Wealth comes to support it; Wealth is the Support of Pressure and must not be void; Output moving hurts Pressure and leaves the Subject useless; Resource moving does the empty-handed no harm but is a curse to those with capital.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.01** 占求财要分六问：有无、难易、多少、得于何人、得何财、何日得；靠人说合的也要占。鬼谷分爻：初爻己身、二爻伴侣、三爻行李、四爻车马、五爻道路、六爻店舍。
  A money question breaks into six: whether there is money, easy or hard, how much, from whom, what kind, and on what day; where it depends on someone putting in a word, ask about that too. Guigu's assignment of the lines: first, oneself; second, companions; third, baggage; fourth, carts and horses; fifth, the road; sixth, the lodging.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.04** 问的是什么就看什么：谒贵能否见到，看世爻与官鬼相生相合；能否得名、得利要另起一卦（名看父母、官鬼两旺，利看妻财、子孙两旺）。为求名而起的卦里财爻持世，是破文书的忌神，不能读作「不成名也得利」。
  Read what was asked: whether you will be received depends on the Self line and Pressure generating or combining; whether it brings a post or gain needs a separate casting (a post wants Resource and Pressure strong, gain wants Wealth and Output). In a hexagram cast for a post, Wealth on the Self line is the Adversary that breaks the documents; do not read it as 'no post, but money'.

## 有无(Is there money, and how much)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/求财/有无.md`

- **LY.WEALTH.CA.02** 财爻持世，或克世，一买一卖，利润百倍；克身又有青龙扶助，金玉堆积得算不清。
  Wealth on the Self or controlling it brings a hundredfold profit on one purchase and one sale; controlling the Body as well and supported by the Azure Dragon, gold and jade pile up beyond counting.
- **LY.WEALTH.CA.12** 世应是财，求就容易得到；卦里没有财，就少有把握。财爻化成官鬼是凶象，官鬼化成财一定是吉神。财落在空亡，难以把握；再遇二杀（劫杀、天贼杀），贼来相侵。
  The Self or the Other being Wealth, seeking is easily rewarded; with no Wealth in the hexagram there is little to go on. Wealth turning into Pressure is an ill sign, and Pressure turning into Wealth is surely an auspicious spirit. Wealth fallen into Void is hard to seize, and if the two killers (the Robbery star and the Heavenly Thief star) are also met, thieves encroach.
- **LY.WEALTH.CA.14** 各爻都没有财的象，子孙动、青龙交，也能求到；世应相合最吉，子孙来合就是福德；月德、月建来相扶，求财终究能得数倍。
  If none of the lines shows the image of Wealth, Output moving and the Dragon joining still lets you seek it; Self and Other combining is most auspicious, and Output combining is fortune and virtue; the Month's Virtue and the Month supporting it, seeking wealth surely brings several times the sum.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.02** 财爻旺相、子孙发动：公事私事求财都如意。财、福落空、临墓绝（或受伤克而无救）：做什么营生都不如意。野鹤另加两条：宜动而化吉、宜生合世爻；忌动而变凶、忌日月冲破。
  Wealth thriving and Output moving: money comes as wished, public or private. Wealth and Output void, in tomb or extinction (or harmed with no rescue): no business goes as wished. Yehe adds: they should turn into something favourable and generate or combine with the Self line; bad if they turn into something harmful or are clashed and broken by the Day or Month.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.03** 妻财不上卦（或不动）而子孙在，兄弟发动反而有望：兄弟生子孙，子孙生财，只是来得慢。财爻空而兄动叫「避空」，过旬、到财爻生旺之日可得。财爻不空不伏却被兄弟劫，是下等卦。王洪绪、野鹤都要求子孙也动。
  With Wealth absent (or still) but Output present, a moving Peer line actually gives hope: Peer feeds Output and Output feeds Wealth, only slowly. Wealth void while Peer moves is called 'hiding in the void': the money comes after the ten-day cycle, on a day that births or empowers Wealth. Wealth neither void nor hidden yet robbed by Peer is the lowest grade. Wang Hongxu and Yehe both require Output to be moving too.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.04** 卦有财而子孙不现，本来一见兄弟就被劫；此时官鬼发动（或日辰为官鬼）克住兄弟，仍可求财，在官鬼生旺日得。卦中若有子孙，官鬼动反成阻滞。野鹤把条件改成「兄、财同动」时官鬼动可制兄。
  If Wealth is present but Output is not, any Peer line would rob it; a moving Pressure line (or a Pressure Day branch) that holds Peer down still makes the money obtainable, arriving on a day that births or empowers Pressure. If Output is present, a moving Pressure line becomes an obstruction instead. Yehe restates the condition as Peer and Wealth moving together, with a moving Pressure line restraining Peer.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.05** 妻财、子孙都不上卦，或上卦而休囚、空破、墓绝、受克冲：求财无望，不如按兵不动。例外（觉子）：日辰或月建本身是财，或财爻伏藏而旺，仍可求。
  If neither Wealth nor Output is in the hexagram, or they are there but weak, void, broken, entombed, extinguished, controlled or clashed, there is no hope in pursuing money; better to hold still. Exception (Juezi): the Day or Month is Wealth itself, or the hidden Wealth line is strong.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.07** 卦中无财而月建是财：这个月内必有财，月建旺财纵遇兄弟也不怕；财再出现在卦上更好。王洪绪：月建扶起伏藏的财，到那一爻值日可得。
  If the hexagram has no Wealth but the Month is Wealth, money will come within the month, and a Wealth this strong fears no Peer line; better still if Wealth also shows in the hexagram. Wang Hongxu: the Month lifts the hidden Wealth line, and the money arrives on the day that branch comes round.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.09** 财爻太多（三五重，或临日月、动变俱是财）为太过，反复难求；要有财的墓库来收：见财的墓库爻发动或持世（旧注、王洪绪），或等财爻入墓之日（野鹤）。财化财也主反复。
  Too much Wealth (three to five lines, or coinciding with Day and Month, or moving and changed lines all Wealth) is excess and goes back and forth; it needs Wealth's tomb to gather it in: Wealth's tomb line moving or on the Self line (old commentary, Wang Hongxu), or the day Wealth enters its tomb (Yehe). Wealth turning into Wealth also means back and forth.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.19** 三合成财局而子孙在局中动，或成子孙局而财在局中动，并来生合世爻：财利不绝，财旺可许厚利（经文说万倍，野鹤说百倍）；野鹤要世爻在局中或局来生世。合成父局辛劳、兄局破耗、鬼局阻隔口舌，克世更凶。
  A three-harmony Wealth frame with Output moving inside it, or an Output frame with Wealth moving inside it, generating or combining with the Self line: profit without end, and with a strong Wealth a great profit (the classic says ten-thousandfold, Yehe a hundredfold); Yehe requires the Self line to be in the frame or to be generated by it. A Resource frame means toil, a Peer frame losses, a Pressure frame obstruction and quarrels, worse if it controls the Self line.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.41** 六爻（内外卦）无财，本宫财爻又伏在空亡之地：缺本钱，勉强经营也无利；若动爻化出财来，有小利。野鹤说屡试屡验。
  No Wealth among the six lines, and the home palace's Wealth line hidden in a void place: no capital, and forcing a venture brings no profit; if a moving line turns into Wealth, a small profit. Yehe says this has proved true again and again.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.42** 父母、兄弟有气（或发动刑冲克害）而财爻弱、临绝：利少，恐怕折本，不如不做。
  Resource and Peer strong (or moving to punish, clash, control or harm) while Wealth is weak or extinguished: little profit and a risk to the capital; better not to go ahead.
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.07** 求财：财来扶世，求起来不难；财空、官鬼旺，隔着千山万水。与吉神合、并与世应相合，六爻上有财就可以得到。白虎临财、临应，先生气；比和无关；官鬼动就要费力经营。应生世，没有财也可以求；外生内、应生世，或比和不落空，现在没有，将来还有钱回来。死气的财在长生日得到。外卦克内卦宜出钱，内卦克外卦宜进钱。
  Seeking wealth: Wealth coming to support the Self, seeking is not hard; Wealth void and Pressure strong, a thousand rivers and ten thousand hills lie in the way. Combining with an auspicious spirit and with the Self and the Other, and with Wealth among the six lines, it can be had. The White Tiger on Wealth or on the Other is angry first; in harmony it does not matter; Pressure moving, you must work at it. The Other generating the Self, even with no Wealth it can be sought; the outer generating the inner and the Other generating the Self, or harmony without void: none yet, but money still comes back. Wealth in the dead state is got on a Birth day. The outer trigram controlling the inner suits paying out; the inner controlling the outer suits bringing in.
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.11** 子孙是财的源头，再加青龙发动，不管财爻旺衰，一定可以谋求，是大吉之兆；父母动则子孙受伤，不能生财；财源已断再遇白虎同上，凶；即使财旺、生合世爻，也只能得一次，不能再谋。
  Output is the source of Wealth; with the Azure Dragon added and moving, then whether the Wealth line is strong or weak it can surely be sought, a sign of great good fortune; Resource moving hurts Output, which then cannot generate Wealth; the source already cut and the White Tiger joining is ill; even if Wealth is strong and generates or combines with the Self line it allows one time only and cannot be sought again.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.06** 子孙是财源：卦无财而子孙旺动仍可求，临青龙等同有财；鬼化财或化子孙最利；子孙化子孙，财从两处来，宜两处求或与人同求；财逢长生、子孙临官，绵绵不绝；财旺而子孙死绝，只得一次；子孙旺而被父母克（父母发动临白虎），断绝财源，日辰合子孙可免。
  Output is the source of money: with no Wealth in the hexagram, a strong moving Output still allows the venture, and with the Azure Dragon it counts as having money; Pressure turning into Wealth or into Output is the best of luck; Output turning into Output, money from two places, so seek in two places or with a partner; Wealth at Birth and Output at its Official stage, the money keeps coming; Wealth strong but Output dead or extinguished, only once; Output strong but controlled by Resource (Resource moving with the White Tiger), the source is cut, unless the Day combines with Output.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.09** 妻财、子孙得生扶拱合，财货丰盈。《断易天机》本细分：日辰生财福吉、生鬼杀凶；财生世易求、世生财难求；应生世是人来就我，世生应是我赶他人；世应相生逢冲仍有阻；财福拱我得人相助，我拱兄弟是他人把权；合财福吉、合鬼杀凶，合处逢生十拿九稳。
  Wealth and Output helped by generation, support, propping and combination: goods and money abound. The Duanyi Tianji text details it: the Day generating Wealth or Output is good, generating Pressure or a killing star bad; Wealth generating the Self line is easy money, the Self generating Wealth hard; the Other generating the Self means they come to me, the reverse means I run after them; Self and Other generating each other can still be blocked by a clash; Wealth or Output propping me means help from others, my propping Peer means others hold the power; combining with Wealth or Output is good, with Pressure or a killing star bad; a combination that also meets generation is a sure thing.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.02** 有财：六合卦；外生内、应生世；内外世应比和旺相不空破；卦无财而月日为财生合世；世下伏财得日辰扶起；应动化财生合世；子孙旺而兄动生之；日辰与世应三合财局；应财虽衰而临日辰长生。无财：六冲卦；内克外、世克应；应、财空破；财化鬼、化空破绝；财动去合应、兄或旁爻；卦身克动财。财旺而子孙空绝，得一次难再。
  There is money with: a Six-Combine hexagram; outer generating inner, Other generating Self; inner and outer, Self and Other in harmony, strong, not void or broken; no Wealth in the hexagram but a Wealth Month or Day generating or combining with the Self; hidden Wealth under the Self lifted by the Day; the Other moving into Wealth that generates or combines with the Self; Output strong and fed by a moving Peer; the Day forming a Wealth frame with Self and Other; the Other's weak Wealth at its Birth stage under the Day. No money with: a Six-Clash hexagram; inner controlling outer, Self controlling Other; Other and Wealth void or broken; Wealth turning into Pressure, void, a break or extinction; Wealth moving off to combine with the Other, Peer or another line; the Body controlling a moving Wealth. Wealth strong but Output void or extinguished: once, and not again.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.04** 估数目：数大的用财爻纳甲数（甲己子午九、乙庚丑未八、丙辛寅申七、丁壬卯酉六、戊癸辰戌五、巳亥四），把卦中所有的财（两财、伏财、变财、四直之财、卦宫之财）加总，旺相加倍、休照数、囚死减半；数小的用财爻地支五行数（水一、火二、木三、金四、土五）随旺衰增减。
  Estimating the sum: for a large one, use the stem-and-branch numbers of the Wealth line (Jia Ji Zi Wu nine, Yi Geng Chou Wei eight, Bing Xin Yin Shen seven, Ding Ren Mao You six, Wu Gui Chen Xu five, Si Hai four), add up every Wealth in the reading (two Wealth lines, hidden Wealth, changed Wealth, Wealth among the four rulers, the palace's own Wealth), doubling the strong, keeping the resting as they are, halving the trapped and dead; for a small one, use the element number of the Wealth branch (Water one, Fire two, Wood three, Metal four, Earth five), raised or lowered by strength.

## 难易(Easy or hard to get; who holds it)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/求财/难易.md`

- **LY.WEALTH.CA.03** 财爻在外卦发动，比较艰难；财在内卦兴旺，人会把钱送上门；外卦生内卦、应生世，即使财轻也容易。
  A Wealth line moving in the outer trigram is harder; Wealth thriving in the inner trigram, people bring the money to you; the outer generating the inner and the Other generating the Self, even if Wealth is light it is easy.
- **LY.WEALTH.CA.04** 应克世、世克身，就算有财，终究难以成就；世应比和、卦身又安稳，资财平稳，没有忧愁阻滞。
  The Other controlling the Self and the Self controlling the Body: even with Wealth it is hard to bring about in the end; Self and Other in harmony and the Body at ease: the funds are steady, with no worry or delay.
- **LY.WEALTH.CA.13** 难求的，怕财爻在外卦发动；容易求到的，是财在内卦兴旺。世如果克财，别开口去求；财来克世，能得千金。
  Hard to get is a Wealth line moving in the outer trigram; easy to find is Wealth thriving in the inner; if the Self controls Wealth, do not open your mouth to ask; Wealth coming to control the Self brings a thousand gold.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.10** 官鬼能制兄弟：卦中无官鬼则兄弟专权、有人分争；官鬼发动又主阻隔。兄弟动时喜官鬼动来制；兄弟静时官鬼不宜动，动则泄财气、有口舌（觉子）。官鬼被克、冲散或合住，阻而无妨。
  Pressure keeps Peer in check: without Pressure, Peer takes over and there is fighting over shares; yet a moving Pressure line means obstruction. When Peer moves, a moving Pressure line is welcome to restrain it; when Peer is still, Pressure should not move, since it drains Wealth and brings quarrels (Juezi). If the Pressure line is controlled, clashed apart or combined, its obstruction does no harm.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.11** 一个兄弟旺动最不利。兄弟重叠太过（月日动变俱兄）时，经文说反不克财：旧注说到财爻生旺日可得；王洪绪说须见子孙发动；觉子不同意，认为到兄弟入墓或受损克之日仍会劫财。
  A single strong moving Peer line is the worst case. When Peer is in excess (Month, Day, moving and changed lines all Peer) the classic says it does not rob after all: the old commentary says the money comes on a day that births or empowers Wealth; Wang Hongxu says only if Output moves; Juezi disagrees: excessive Peer still robs on the day it enters its tomb or is cut down.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.12** 兄弟临卦身或持世，求财难有指望（卦身临财福吉、临父辛劳、临官利公门之财）。野鹤只看世爻，并认为兄弟持世而世逢月破、旬空、化墓，或日月为财冲克世爻，或世上兄弟化出财爻，仍可得财。
  Peer on the Body line or the Self line makes the money hard to get (Wealth or Output on the Body line is good, Resource means toil, Pressure favours official money). Yehe reads only the Self line, and holds that Peer on the Self line still gets the money if the Self line is under a Month-break, void, or turns into its tomb, if the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line, or if the Peer on the Self line turns into Wealth.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.13** 财爻生、合、克世爻或持世，为「财来就我」，容易得；财爻虽动而与世爻无关，为「我去寻财」，难得。野鹤：不可拘泥，兄弟持世、财不生合世爻而照样得财的卦也有。
  The Wealth line generating, combining with, controlling or sitting on the Self line is 'the money coming to me': easy to get. A moving Wealth line with no tie to the Self line is 'me going after the money': hard. Yehe: do not cling to this; there are hexagrams with Peer on the Self line and no Wealth reaching it where the money came all the same.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.14** 世爻持财：即使无气也容易得，旺相更好；世爻持兄弟：安静也难得，发动更难。
  Wealth on the Self line: easy to get even without force, better still if thriving. Peer on the Self line: hard even when still, harder when moving.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.21** 世爻空：有财难得；应爻空：别人靠不住；世应俱空：谋事无准。空而动又相合（或化空），为「虚约」，口头答应而不实。
  Self line void: even existing money is hard to get. Other line void: others cannot be relied on. Both void: nothing certain. Void and moving yet combining (or turning void) is an 'empty promise': agreed in words, not in fact.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.22** 去求某人得财，以代表他的那一爻为「主人」（求贵人看官鬼，求妇人看妻财，不知是谁看应爻）。这一爻被日辰、动爻刑克，或空、化空：见不到，见了也无益；生合世爻、化出财爻生合世爻：最吉。
  When the money depends on someone you go to, the line standing for that person is the 'patron' (Pressure for a person of rank, Wealth for a woman, the Other line if you cannot tell). If that line is punished or controlled by the Day or a moving line, or is void or turns void, you will not find them and meeting would not help; if it generates or combines with the Self line, or turns into Wealth that does, it is best.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.23** 官鬼持世，财来生鬼，本来求财容易；若世上的官鬼（或妻财）落空，是自己迟疑退缩，所以不成。
  With Pressure on the Self line, Wealth feeds it and money should come easily; if that Pressure (or a Wealth) on the Self line is void, it is your own hesitation that makes it fail.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.24** 财爻动来生合世爻本易得；若被日辰或动爻合住，财被人把持：合它的那一爻（卦无财则看财伏在哪一爻下）就是把持的人，如父母为长辈、官鬼为贵人；到冲开之日才到手（王洪绪）。
  A moving Wealth line that generates or combines with the Self line is easy money; but if the Day or a moving line combines with and holds it, someone is keeping it back. The holding line (or, if there is no Wealth, the line the hidden Wealth lies under) shows who: Resource an elder, Pressure a person of rank. It arrives on the day that clashes the combination open (Wang Hongxu).
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.12** 世是我：财来生我、克我，都吉，是容易得到的象；我克财爻叫克退，财静还可以，财动就像下面追高处，追不上；世安静、财爻发动来生我、克我，是财追着我的象，一定容易求。
  The Self is me: Wealth coming to generate or control me is auspicious, the image of easy gain; my controlling the Wealth line is called driving it back, and if Wealth is still it is tolerable but if it moves it is like chasing something above from below and you cannot reach it; the Self still and the Wealth line moving to generate or control me is the image of wealth coming after me, and it is sure to be easy to seek.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.07** 财来生、克、合世爻或持世：财来就我，伸手可得；世爻去克发动的财：我去追财，难得（即使日辰合财也不得）；世生动财而日辰合住，或许可得。《断易天机》本：财在内卦动易，在外卦动难。
  Wealth generating, controlling or combining with the Self line, or sitting on it: the money comes to me, reach out and take it. The Self line controlling a moving Wealth: I chase the money and do not catch it (even if the Day combines with it). The Self line generating a moving Wealth that the Day holds by combination: it may be had. The Duanyi Tianji text: Wealth moving in the inner trigram is easy, in the outer hard.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.08** 兄弟、官鬼是求财的忌神，它们受克、害、刑、冲就灾消、财有望。《断易天机》本细分：吉神克我吉、凶神克我凶，我克兄鬼愁散、我克财福无财；害财则财有妨，害鬼则鬼无作，还要看衰旺；刑鬼煞吉、刑财福凶，刑世伤我、刑应伤彼，应合世而被刑难成；日辰冲世应或冲财难成，冲散财则无财；吉神逢冲凶，凶神逢冲吉。
  Peer and Pressure are the adversaries of money; when they are controlled, harmed, punished or clashed, trouble disperses and money becomes possible. The Duanyi Tianji text details it: a good spirit controlling me is good, a baleful one bad; my controlling Peer or Pressure dispels worry, my controlling Wealth or Output means no money; harming Wealth hinders it, harming Pressure disarms it, strength decides; punishing Pressure or a killing star is good, punishing Wealth or Output bad, punishing the Self line hurts me, the Other line the other side, and an Other line combining with the Self but punished will not close; the Day clashing Self, Other or Wealth makes it hard, clashing Wealth apart leaves no money; a good spirit clashed is bad, a baleful one clashed is good.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.03** 易：旺财临世身，或旺动生合克世身，子化财生合世身。鬼化财生合世：从无中得；鬼化子生合身：儿子送财；父化财：先难后得（父旺财衰力多财少，父衰财旺力少财多）；兄化财：费口舌而得；子化子：两处得财。财动又化财：化去难得；财生世又化兄：得而复失或与人分；财合逢冲：成而后变；财旺而被月日动变冲刑克害：求难得薄；财冲破而世得生扶：人安财散；世逢虎鬼大杀或持墓化墓而财旺被合住：财存人亡；世遇虎鬼刃劫克伤而财空绝破：人财两失。
  Easy: a strong Wealth on the Self or Body line, or moving to generate, combine with or control them; Output turning into Wealth that generates or combines with them. Pressure into Wealth generating the Self: money from nothing; Pressure into Output generating the Body: a son brings it; Resource into Wealth: hard first, gain later (Resource strong and Wealth weak, much effort and little money; the reverse, the reverse); Peer into Wealth: gained after much talk; Output into Output: money from two places. Wealth moving into Wealth: it changes away, hard to get; Wealth generating the Self but turning into Peer: gained and lost, or shared; Wealth combined then clashed: settled, then changed; a strong Wealth hit by Month, Day, moving or changed lines: hard to get and little of it; Wealth clashed and broken while the Self is supported: you are safe, the money scatters; the Self with the White Tiger on Pressure or the Great Killing star, or entombed, while a strong Wealth is held by combination: the money survives, the person does not; the Self attacked by Tiger, Pressure, Blade or Robbery while Wealth is void, extinguished or broken: both lost.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.08** 靠人说合（仗托吹嘘）看间爻（中人、保人）：死绝受伤则中保无能；生合世爻善成全，刑克世爻多欺骗；带兄鬼玄武冲伤为奸人阻隔，带贵人生合为贵人做主；应克间，对方起疑；世伤间，我心怀怨；两间兄动，争做中人；朱雀兄弟旁动伤世克间，有人破坏；间爻空，没有中保。
  When it depends on someone putting in a word, read the between lines (go-between and guarantor): dead or hurt, they are useless; generating or combining with the Self, they see it through; punishing or controlling it, they cheat; with Peer, Pressure or the Dark Tortoise clashing, villains block it; with the Noble star generating, a person of rank takes charge; the Other controlling them, the other side grows suspicious; the Self hurting them, you bear a grudge; both between lines Peer and moving, people compete to be the go-between; the Vermilion Bird on a moving Peer elsewhere hurting the Self and controlling the between line, someone spoils it; a void between line, no go-between.

## 动变(Moving lines and what they turn into)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/求财/动变.md`

- **LY.WEALTH.CA.08** 兄弟化成财，虽有财，要忌落入别人的圈套；折本伤财，只因妻财化成兄弟；子孙化成财，利润宜多，财化成子孙更不是小利；青龙落在水上，登山涉海多得利润。
  Peer turning into Wealth, though there is money, take care not to fall into another's trap; capital and money are lost only because Wealth turned into Peer; Output turning into Wealth brings large profit, and Wealth turning into Output is better still; with the Azure Dragon on Water, climbing mountains and crossing seas brings much profit.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.06** 父母、兄弟同时发动：父母克子孙（断财源），兄弟克妻财（劫财），求财必不可得；父化兄、兄化父同论。
  If Resource and Peer both move, Resource cuts off Output (the source of money) and Peer robs Wealth: the money cannot be had. Resource turning into Peer, or Peer into Resource, is judged the same.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.15** 子孙发动（生财），利长久；子孙化财、财化子孙，而且生合世爻、卦身，财源不绝。
  A moving Output line (which feeds Wealth) means lasting profit; Output turning into Wealth, or Wealth into Output, especially generating or combining with the Self and Body lines, means money that keeps coming.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.17** 父化财：辛劳后得。兄化财：先散后聚（后利前不利）。官化财：利公门、谒贵、九流手艺求财；私财先惊后喜、先阻后得；若官鬼克世，为「助鬼伤身」，不吉（王洪绪）。
  Resource turning into Wealth: gain after toil. Peer turning into Wealth: first scattered, then gathered (good later, not at first). Pressure turning into Wealth: favours official money, patrons and people who live by a trade; for private money, fright then joy, obstruction then gain; if Pressure controls the Self line it is 'feeding the ghost that harms you', unlucky (Wang Hongxu).
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.18** 财化官鬼最凶：损折虚耗、有惊险，伤世则因财致祸。财化兄弟：与人分利、先聚后散（王洪绪把它和财化鬼并列为最凶）。财化父母：得后艰辛或只一次。财化死墓空绝：有名无实；若生合世爻，宜抢先去取。
  Wealth turning into Pressure is the worst: losses, waste and danger, and disaster brought on by the money if it harms the Self line. Wealth turning into Peer: sharing the profit, gathered then scattered (Wang Hongxu ranks it with Wealth-into-Pressure as the worst). Wealth turning into Resource: hardship after the gain, or only once. Wealth turning into death, tomb, void or extinction: a name without substance; if it generates or combines with the Self line, act at once.
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.10** 父母化成财，先难后易；财化成父母，先易后难。财化成兄弟，先聚后散；兄弟化成财，先散后聚。本卦有财、变卦无财，赶快谋求有得，迟了就没有；本卦无财、变卦有财，迟一些取才有，眼下还不当值。钱财多少，要凭爻的衰旺来定。书中例：九二的财动，求就一定有；九五的比肩爻动，被阻拦还没得到；买卖同样推。
  Resource turning into Wealth: hard first, easy later; Wealth turning into Resource: easy first, hard later. Wealth turning into Peer: gathered first, scattered later; Peer turning into Wealth: scattered first, gathered later. Wealth in the first hexagram and none in the later one: act quickly and you get it, late and you do not; no Wealth in the first and Wealth in the later one: take it late and you have it, it is not on duty now. How much money there is must be decided by the strength or weakness of the line. Case in the book: the Wealth on the second line moves, so seeking will surely bring it; the Peer on the fifth line moves, so it is blocked and not yet obtained; trade is worked out the same way.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.03** 官鬼化财（鬼送财）而来克世：大吉；若世爻去克官鬼则无财。财化官鬼：从有到无，再逢空为全凶。《断易天机》本：得日辰扶合、动爻生扶为「财入鬼手」，可无伤；旺财化衰鬼，财不全；衰财化旺鬼，防加倍折财。财化财：本卦财旺而变出的财无气，宜速求；反之宜待。
  Pressure turning into Wealth ('the ghost bringing money') that controls the Self line: very lucky; if the Self line controls the Pressure line instead, no money. Wealth turning into Pressure: something becomes nothing, and void as well it is wholly unlucky. The Duanyi Tianji text: with the Day supporting or combining, or a moving line generating it, it is 'money in the ghost's hand' and may do no harm; strong Wealth into weak Pressure, the money is not whole; weak Wealth into strong Pressure, beware of double loss. Wealth into Wealth: a strong original turning into a forceless one, seek quickly; the reverse, wait.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.04** 父母化财：先难后得；父旺财衰，辛苦多财少；父衰财旺，用力少财多。财化兄弟：有侵夺，积蓄也要分散（本就与人分利则无妨）。兄弟化财：先散后聚。《断易天机》本：父母、兄弟化财，虽辛苦亦吉；父爻带贵人有依靠，带太岁凶煞防因财染病。
  Resource turning into Wealth: difficulty first, gain later; Resource strong and Wealth weak, much toil and little money; the reverse, little effort and much money. Wealth turning into Peer: encroachment, savings scattered (no harm if you were sharing the profit anyway). Peer turning into Wealth: scattered first, gathered later. The Duanyi Tianji text: Resource or Peer turning into Wealth is lucky however hard the way; the Resource line with the Noble star means someone to lean on, with the Tai Sui's killing star beware of falling ill over the money.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.25** 财是根本，不能变动：化成官鬼、变成兄弟，财就被耗散，两条路都没有好处，终究难有结果；财化成子孙，则利禄滚滚，永远没有边际。
  Wealth is the root and must not change: turning into Pressure or Peer, Wealth is consumed and scattered, neither way does any good, and in the end there is no result; Wealth turning into Output, profit and emolument flow on without limit.

## 应期(Timing: when the money or the price comes)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/求财/应期.md`

- **LY.WEALTH.CA.11** 想问钱财哪一天得到：应在卦身和财爻生旺的日子；比如乾宫以木为财，就以亥、卯的日子为准。
  To ask on what day the money is got: it answers on a day when the Body and Wealth are born or strong; for instance, if the Qian palace takes Wood as its Wealth, the days Hai and Mao are the standard.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.08** 财爻旺相、生合世爻或持世，本是必得；若日辰克财，当天得不到，要过了这一天才得。
  A thriving Wealth line that generates, combines with or holds the Self line means certain gain; if the Day controls it, nothing comes that day; it comes after the day has passed.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.25** 定得财之日看财爻状态：有气→合日或本日；太旺、太过→墓库日；休囚、逢绝→生旺日；被合、入墓→冲开之日；逢冲→合日；动逢月破→填实或合日；静→冲日；旬空→出旬；伏藏→出现（提起）之日。财死绝而有子孙扶，按子孙断；卦无财而兄弟生子孙，按兄弟断。
  Time the money by the state of the Wealth line: with force, the day that combines with it or its own day; over-strong or in excess, its tomb day; resting, trapped or extinguished, a day that births or empowers it; combined or entombed, the day that clashes it open; clashed, the combining day; moving under a Month-break, the day that fills it in or combines with it; still, the clashing day; void, once the cycle passes; hidden, the day it appears. If Wealth is dead but propped up by Output, time it by Output; if there is no Wealth and Peer feeds Output, time it by Peer.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.26** 货物何时得价：旧注按财爻五行当令的季节（木春、土夏而六月更好，同类中取更旺之月）；王洪绪说不能一概按五行，还要用冲合、生绝、墓开之法并看子孙爻。两家都说：财坐日辰的长生，一天比一天贵；坐帝旺，眼下正好，迟则贱。
  When goods will fetch a price: the old commentary goes by the season of the Wealth line's element (Wood in spring, Earth in summer and best in the sixth month, choosing the stronger month within a season); Wang Hongxu says not to rely on the element alone but also on clash and combination, birth and extinction, the opening of tombs, and the Output line. Both agree: Wealth at its Birth stage under the Day gains value day by day; at its Peak, now is the time, and later it cheapens.
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.13** 看得财的日子，要和日辰相合才能到手；旺相的财，墓日可以得到；没有气的财，要生旺的日子才得到。
  To find the day money is got, it must combine with the Day to come into the hand; a strong Wealth can be had on a tomb day; a Wealth without breath is had only on a day of Birth or strength.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.10** 本卦有财、变卦无财：宜速求，迟则难得；本卦无财、变卦有财：先难后得，宜等时机，或等财爻旺相的年月。
  Wealth in the original hexagram but not in the changed one: seek quickly, for later it is hard to get. No Wealth in the original but Wealth in the changed one: hardship first, gain later; wait for the moment, or for the year and month in which Wealth is strong.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.11** 得财之日：财与日辰相合之日（或财爻值日）、财爻入墓之日；《断易天机》本细分：财旺逢入墓之日得，财衰逢生旺之日得。
  The day the money comes: the day that combines with Wealth (or Wealth's own day), or the day Wealth enters its tomb. The Duanyi Tianji text: a strong Wealth on its tomb day, a weak one on a day that births or empowers it.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.18** 求财想知道哪一天到手：看日辰生合财爻的那一天。经上说，财爻合日辰，才能入手。
  To know on what day money arrives in any money question: it is the day on which the Day generates or combines with the Wealth line. The classic says: only when Wealth combines with the Day can it come into the hand.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.19** 财爻落在衰、绝之地，要日辰来生扶，那一天才是能得到的日期；财爻太旺太多，必定要到墓日才有。经上说，财逢墓库便可归还，多财反复，必须墓库收藏。
  Wealth in decline or extinction needs the Day to generate or support it, and that day is when it can be had; Wealth too strong and too many comes on a tomb day. The classic says: Wealth meeting the tomb-store can be brought home; abundant Wealth is unsteady and needs the tomb-store to hold it.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.20** 卦里有福神（子孙）而没有财：六爻里没有财出现，即使有子孙也还得不到，一定要遇到财爻当值的日子，钱才进口袋。
  A hexagram with Output but no Wealth: with no Wealth appearing among the six lines, even Output gives nothing yet; only on the day the Wealth line is on duty does the money go into the purse.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.07** 何日得财：旺相而生合世身的财→六合之日；财多太旺→墓日；死绝→生旺之日；空墓→逢冲之日；子孙旺而卦无财→财值之日。书中五例：伏财靠月建扶起而得；伏财因飞神旬空透出、逢长生而得；财化绝待绝处逢生之日；用神不透待财值之日；伏克飞神「出暴」、次日兄空而得。
  The day the money comes: a strong Wealth generating or combining with the Self or Body, the day that combines with it; too much or over-strong, its tomb day; dead or extinguished, a day of birth or strength; void or entombed, the clashing day; Output strong and no Wealth, the day of Wealth's branch. Five cases in the book: hidden Wealth lifted by the Month; hidden Wealth showing through when its flying line is void, and coming on its Birth day; Wealth turning into extinction, coming on the day it is reborn; a Subject that does not show, coming on the day of its branch; a hidden line controlling its flying line ('breaking out') and coming the next day when Peer is void.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.02** 谒贵：世爻动而化空、化破，不作凶论，是应期——应在到达那里的月日（书中例：官星持世而空，出空之日见到，财利如意）。
  Calling on the great: the Self line moving into the void or into a Month-break is not bad; it marks the month or day you arrive (case in the book: Pressure on the Self line was void, and the meeting came on the day it left the void, with the money as wished).

In the case from the book, hexagram Xu with no moving line looked auspicious because Output on Shen Metal held the Self line; the author judged that Metal was void in the fourth month and could not generate Wealth, and that four Peer lines clashed each other away, so the buyers left when they saw the goods.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.15** 囤货卖货看财爻四时旺衰：财衰宜囤，财旺宜卖（如春天木财旺宜速卖，金水土财宜囤，火财为相也不宜囤）；忌财空破、动而变回头克。觉子：财衰可买，但要查它有没有根——有根将来再涨，无根终难卖出。
  Stockpiling or selling goes by the seasonal strength of the Wealth line: weak Wealth, stockpile; strong Wealth, sell (in spring Wood Wealth is thriving, sell fast; Metal, Water or Earth Wealth, stockpile; Fire is strong in spring, do not stockpile). Void or broken Wealth, or Wealth turning into its own control, is bad. Juezi: weak Wealth can be bought, but check its root; rooted, it will rise again; rootless, it will never sell.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.16** 有固定旺季的货（麻葛夏、棉花冬）凭时令断价；没有固定时节的货（木料、绸缎）要指明求利单独占。
  Goods with a set season (hemp and linen in summer, cotton in winter) are priced by the season; goods without one (timber, silk) need a casting that asks specifically about the profit.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.17** 财化进神：价正在涨，不可收购囤积；财化退：价将落，有货速卖。兄弟持世或兄弟发动：破财之兆。
  Wealth turning Advancing: the price is rising, do not buy to store; turning Retreating: the price will fall, sell what you hold at once. Peer on the Self line or Peer moving: a sign of losing money.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.22** 内卦财衰、外卦财旺，或外卦财旺而生合世爻、持世：宜到外地卖。财不生合世爻或世爻动而化凶：外地再好，我去也遇不上。
  Wealth weak in the inner trigram and strong in the outer, or strong in the outer and generating, combining with or sitting on the Self line: sell elsewhere. If Wealth does not generate or combine with the Self line, or the Self line moves into harm, however good the other place, you will not find your spring there.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.23** 卖货守还是动：旧说六冲宜另图、六合宜坐守；野鹤屡验：六冲而财旺不必改、财衰改也无益，应再占「更改如何」，财世两旺许改，衰则耐守；六合也看财爻：财持世而动不可坐守，衰而静则守待旺时。
  Selling, stay or move: the old rule says Six-Clash, change plans; Six-Combine, stay put. Yehe tested it: with a Six-Clash and Wealth strong there is no need to change, with Wealth weak changing does not help; cast again asking 'what if I change', and allow it only if Wealth and the Self line are both strong, otherwise wait. With a Six-Combine, look at Wealth too: on the Self line and moving, do not sit still; weak and still, wait for its season.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.24** 财衰动变旺：眼下贱、将来涨；财旺动变衰：卖要急、买不可收。财爻旺时卖，衰时买。内卦财衰外卖，世临旺财近卖；财化进神往外发卖，世爻化退回近处出脱。
  A weak Wealth moving into strength: cheap now, dearer later; a strong Wealth moving into weakness: sell fast, do not buy. Sell when Wealth is strong, buy when it is weak. Wealth weak in the inner trigram, sell elsewhere; a strong Wealth on the Self line, sell near home; Wealth turning Advancing, go further afield; the Self line turning Retreating, come back and sell nearby.
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.25** 往何方买卖：古法有推方位的说法，野鹤屡试不验；要指明去哪个地方单独起卦（书中例：往楚买豆，财休囚在外卦主那里货贱，日建生世主厚利，间爻兄弟旺主耗费；后果如此）。
  Which way to trade: the old methods have rules for finding the direction, but Yehe found they fail; name the place and cast for it (case: going to Chu to buy beans, Wealth resting in the outer trigram meant cheap goods there, the Day generating the Self line meant rich profit, a strong between-line Peer meant heavy expenses; all came true).
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.26** 买什么货：古法以财爻五行、六神定货类（金珠宝、水鱼盐、火陶冶果实、木竹木、土五谷；青龙喜庆、白虎丧葬屠宰等），野鹤认为是揣摩之论、屡试不验；要指定某一货物单独占（书中例：旅卦六合、世应相生，只断此行顺遂，不断买何物，后买檀香翠羽都获大利）。
  Which goods to buy: the old method reads the goods from the element and spirit of the Wealth line (Metal jewels, Water fish and salt, Fire pottery and fruit, Wood timber, Earth grain; Azure Dragon festive goods, White Tiger funeral and butchery goods, and so on); Yehe holds this speculation that fails when tested; name a particular kind of goods and cast for it (case: the hexagram Lü in Six-Combine with Self and Other generating, read only as 'the journey will go well'; he bought sandalwood and kingfisher feathers and profited on both).

## 开店(Opening a shop, partnership, brokers)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/求财/开店.md`

- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.27** 合伙：世、应都是财爻最好；兄弟安静（或临卦身而世爻静）不嫌，因为合伙本要分财；兄弟发动则不宜。世应两动或落空：合不长久；相冲克：日后不和；化出兄官：多私心；世应生合而被刑冲克害：有人挑拨。野鹤：世、应临兄弟仍不吉，只有日月为财冲克世时反吉。
  Partnership: best if both the Self and Other lines are Wealth; a still Peer (or Peer on the Body line with the Self line still) is no objection, since partners share the money anyway; a moving Peer is bad. Self and Other both moving or void: it will not last; clashing or controlling each other: they will fall out; turning into Peer or Pressure: self-interest; combining but punished, clashed or controlled by others: someone will sow discord. Yehe: Peer on the Self or Other line is still unlucky, except when the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.32** 开行（牙行、铺面）：世应不空，财福齐全，官鬼有气，父兄衰静，为上吉；再得月建、日辰、动爻生合世爻，客人远近来投。卦无财而世爻只得生合：门口热闹、无实利（鬼旺财空同）；动出兄官多是非；世应空，开不成。
  Opening a brokerage or shop: Self and Other not void, Wealth and Output both present, Pressure with force, Resource and Peer weak and still is first-class; if the Month, the Day and moving lines also generate or combine with the Self line, clients come from near and far. With no Wealth, support for the Self line is only a busy doorway without profit (the same for strong Pressure and void Wealth); Peer or Pressure moving means quarrels; Self and Other void, it will not open.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.04** 开店做买卖最怕六冲卦：一冲，主顾聚不拢，货物销不动。
  A shop and its business fear a Six-Clash hexagram above all: with a clash, customers are hard to gather and the goods do not move.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.10** 开张店业：用神不要交重（发动）；只要财爻安静，如果发动，一定败坏或迁移。
  Opening a shop: the chief image must not be moving; all that is needed is a still Wealth, and if it moves it will surely fail or have to move away.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.21** 有本钱开张：卦里要财和福都齐全，福德临门则顾客盈门，妻财持世则利润绵绵；以财为用神，财子两全，财旺则财源无穷，子孙动则顾客不断；不宜兄弟动又世克财，那样即使勤谨也终无利息。
  Opening a shop with capital: the hexagram needs Wealth and Output both complete; Output at the gate fills the shop with customers and Wealth on the Self brings unbroken profit. Wealth is the Subject and Wealth and Output must both be there: Wealth thriving, its source is endless; Output moving, customers keep coming; but Peer moving while the Self controls Wealth is bad, and even a diligent person ends with no interest. With capital, Wealth and Pressure each want to be still.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.22** 空手开张：以官爻为主，一定要财和官鬼同时有；财动就帮官鬼，官鬼动就发财；九流的手艺无中生有、空手得财，所以经上说，空手问利，官爻喜欢遇到兴旺。
  Opening a shop empty-handed: Pressure is the chief; both Wealth and Pressure must be present; Wealth moving helps Pressure and Pressure moving makes money; the skills of the trades make something from nothing, so the classic says: empty-handed, Pressure is glad to meet a thriving state.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.23** 开张遇六冲又兄弟发动，一定开不满一年：卦是六冲，不论有本钱还是空手，到头来成不了，成了也不长久；只有兄弟发动，有本钱的还嫌不好，空手的没有妨碍。
  A Six-Clash with Peer moving means the shop will not last a year: in a Six-Clash hexagram, with capital or empty-handed, it comes to nothing or does not last; with only Peer moving, it is still unwelcome with capital but no taboo for the empty-handed.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.24** 世代表自己，不宜发动，一动心里变化多端；应代表伙伴，也宜安静，一动就怀了背叛的心，空就没有能力也不长久，动而克世，更要防被欺害、分离；应空，合伙的人还是换掉为好。
  The Self stands for oneself and should not move: moving, the mind changes in many ways. The Other stands for the partner and should also be still: moving, he harbors a heart of betrayal; void, he has no ability and will not last; moving and controlling the Self, expect to be cheated and harmed and to part. With the Other void, the partner should be replaced.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.20** 找牙行（中间人）：应爻、官爻旺相，又生合世爻，千金也可托付；休囚落空、或冲伤世，各样货物都难托付。应爻发动克世，或应上的财化成兄弟，难免被侵吞；应上的财发动生合世、或化成子孙，或应上的官鬼化成财，办事肯替你周旋。
  Choosing a broker: the Other and Pressure lines strong and generating or combining with the Self, and a thousand gold can be entrusted to him; resting or void, or clashing with and hurting the Self, and no goods can be relied upon to him. The Other moving to control the Self, or the Other's Wealth turning into Peer: you cannot escape being fleeced. The Other's Wealth moving to generate or combine with the Self, or turning into Output, or the Other's Pressure turning into Wealth: he handles matters with care and works on your behalf.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.22** 开店要依次问六件事：能不能开成；本钱多少、利润厚薄；合伙人好不好；店面大小；店里的货物；能不能放账收账。管辂分爻（六爻各管一件事）：上爻财路，五爻主人，四爻店屋，三爻基业，二爻伴侣，初爻心事。
  Opening a shop asks six things in order: can it be opened; how much capital and how thick the profit; is the partner worthy; how big is the shopfront; what of the goods in the shop; and may credit be given and collected. Guan Lu's assignment of the lines: top, the money road; fifth, the master; fourth, the shop building; third, the foundation of the business; second, companions; first, the mind's intent.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.23** 能不能开：本宫、世爻、财爻带福、禄、龙、喜而旺相，财或子孙持世旺相且不犯刑害克破，应上的财旺而发动生合世，或四直临财生合世，可开。本宫、世爻、财爻逢死墓绝胎、空破刑害，或四直和应爻带兄弟官鬼、旺而发动克世，不可开；四直全是兄弟而世爻旺相，兄弟受制，开张安稳。六冲卦开不成，开成也不久；太岁生合世而有动爻冲太岁，开不满一年；月生合世而有动爻冲月，开不满一月。财化子孙，宜守老本行。
  Whether it can be opened: if the home palace, the Self line and the Wealth line carry Output, Emolument, the Dragon or Joy and are strong, or Wealth or Output on the Self is strong and free of punishment, harm, control and breaking, or the Other's Wealth is strong and moving to generate or combine with the Self, or the four rulers on Wealth generate or combine with the Self, it can be opened. If the home palace, the Self and the Wealth line meet death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, void, broken, punished or harmed, or the four rulers and the Other carry Peer or Pressure, strong and moving to control the Self, it cannot; but if the four rulers are all Peer while the Self is strong, Peer is held in check and the opening is safe. A Six-Clash hexagram cannot be opened, or if opened will not last; the Tai Sui generating or combining with the Self but a moving line clashing the Tai Sui: it will not last a year; the Month generating or combining with the Self but a moving line clashing the Month: not a month. Wealth turning into Output: keep to the old business.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.24** 本钱和利润：卦旺财旺，本钱多；卦衰财衰，本钱少；财落在胎、墓，本钱少；财落空、绝，缺本钱。得利：世带旺财，财得生而库旺，财在绝地逢生，外卦的财生合内卦的世，财落在三爻或四爻（门户）旺而发动，三传临财发动生合世，财伏在世爻或子孙下得生得旺，财发动化成子孙、化长生、化旺，子孙发动化成财、化长生、化旺。折本：财不上卦，财空破死绝，三传临兄弟或劫煞发动，兄弟旺动克世，父母发动帮兄弟克世，应爻刑害克破世。财伏在父母下，失一半；伏在官鬼下，耗损多；伏在兄弟下，谨防口舌。
  Capital and profit: hexagram and Wealth both strong, much capital; both weak, little; Wealth in the womb or tomb stage, little; Wealth void or extinct, no capital. Profit comes when: the Self carries a strong Wealth; Wealth is generated and its store strong; Wealth in extinction meets Birth; outer Wealth generates or combines with the inner Self; Wealth on the Gate or Door line (the third and fourth), strong and moving; the three rulers on Wealth, moving to generate or combine with the Self; Wealth hidden under the Self or under Output and generated or strong; Wealth moving into Output, into Birth or into strength; Output moving into Wealth, into Birth or into strength. Capital is lost when: Wealth is absent from the hexagram, or void, broken, dead or extinct; the three rulers on Peer or the Robbery star, moving; Peer strong and moving to control the Self; Resource moving to help Peer control the Self; or the Other line punishes, harms, controls or breaks the Self. Wealth hidden under Resource: half is lost; under Pressure: heavy losses; under Peer: beware of quarrels.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.25** 合伙：应生合世，对我有益；应刑冲克害世，对我有损。世生应、内生外，是成全别人；世克应、内克外，是借别人的力。世应比和，两人同心；世应六冲，两情不协；世应六合，两意相投；应静而逢冲，心意多变；世空，自己心疏懒；应空，对方心松弛；世应俱空，两家都要退悔；应遭刑害克破，对方多灾病。应带龙喜，交易和气；应临白虎兄弟刻傲，螣蛇兄弟琐碎，玄武兄弟欺诈，勾陈兄弟拙钝，朱雀兄弟多是非。应上的财伏在官鬼下、或应上的财发动化兄弟，必被侵占。游魂卦应临驿马发动，人不守店；归魂卦应临驿马发动，常在家。福神带朱雀加咸池发动，爱吹爱喝；应合财加咸池，有私情。离宫火应带月盲杀克金财，分不出银子成色。应旺，勤俭多能；应衰，疲弱无才；应先合后化冲，有始无终。卦无兄弟，店里没人掌管；兄弟以旺相安静为吉。与内外亲属合伙，以用爻为凭。

The Self generating the Other, inner generating outer: helping another succeed; the Self controlling the Other, inner controlling outer: drawing on another's strength. Self and Other in harmony: two of one mind; in a Six-Clash: feelings do not agree; in a Six-Combine: minds meet; the Other still but clashed: his mind keeps changing; the Self void: my heart is slack; the Other void: his intent is loose; both void: both sides withdraw and regret; the Other punished, harmed, controlled or broken: he has many mishaps and illnesses. The Other with the Dragon and Joy stars: dealings are gracious; on the White Tiger as Peer: sharp and arrogant; Coiling Snake as Peer: petty; Dark Tortoise as Peer: deceitful; Hook Earth as Peer: clumsy and dull; Vermilion Bird as Peer: many quarrels. The Other's Wealth hidden under Pressure, or moving into Peer: he will surely take your share. A Wandering-Soul hexagram with the Other on the Post Horse, moving: he does not stay in the shop; a Returning-Soul hexagram: he is often at home. Output on the Vermilion Bird with the Salt Pool star, moving: fond of boasting and drinking; the Other combining with Wealth and carrying the Salt Pool star: a private affair. In the Li palace, the Other on Fire with the Moon-Blind star, controlling Metal Wealth: cannot tell the fineness of silver. The Other strong: diligent, thrifty and capable; weak: feeble and without talent; combining then turning into a clash: a start with no finish. No Peer in the hexagram: the shop has no one in charge; Peer is auspicious when strong and still. For a partnership with relatives, inside or outside the family, take the Subject line as your guide.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.26** 看店和货：父母代表店，妻财代表货。父旺而静，店里热闹平安；财旺而动，货物畅销。父旺是大店，父衰是小店；父逢冲开不长，父遇生合能长久。有财无父是有货无店，有父无财是有店无货；父旺财衰，店大利薄；父衰财旺，店小利厚。两个财：内卦被冲、外卦旺，本少利多；内卦旺、外卦被冲，本多利少。卦身和世爻跟官鬼入墓，白费力，利归别人。财爻遇冲暗动，带龙喜暗中得利，带玄武劫煞被骗失财。官鬼临财库发动，不是官司就是灾祸。父母带杀刃亡劫发动，或日雀会月雀发动，招争讼。珠宝古董杂货店以财为用，书纸巾帽衣服店以父为用，酒肴牲畜店以子为用；用神旺、财衰，货多利薄。另有郭雍说：开书籍纸货古董店，财旺而父母被克，吉；开当铺，父母旺而财被克，吉。

Wealth without Resource is goods without a shop; Resource without Wealth is a shop without goods; Resource strong and Wealth weak: a big shop, thin profit; the reverse: a small shop, thick profit. Pressure moving on the tomb-store of Wealth: a lawsuit or a disaster. Guo Yong adds: for a shop of books, paper goods and antiques, Wealth strong while Resource is controlled is auspicious; for a pawnshop, Resource strong while Wealth is controlled is auspicious.

  Opening a shop: the Self line is oneself, the Other the other party, and they should combine; Wealth is the capital, Output the foundation, and both should flourish. Juezi: who the Other line stands for depends on what is asked. Asking about a partner, it is the partner (generating or combining, of one mind; Other generating Self, he benefits me; Self generating Other, I benefit him; clashing or controlling, goodwill changes; Other controlling Self, he deceives me; Self controlling Other, he goes along; Other on the Dark Tortoise and Peer, secret fraud; Other on Pressure controlling Self, open trouble; Wealth on Self and Peer on Other, he drags me down; Wealth on Self and Output on Other, he helps; both void yet combining, both insincere). Asking about the trade, it is the customer (generating or combining with the Self line, strangers become regulars; clashing or controlling, old friends become enemies).
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.09** 开店：官鬼发动招是非，克世最忌（鬼现爻中是非日有，鬼摇克世灾祸连绵）；财为资本，忌衰墓空破、动而变凶、日月刑冲克害；世爻与财爻落空，开不成。

- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.29** 买卖、饲养六畜（家畜野兽）以子孙为用神，兼看财爻：财持世而子孙动来生、日月生助，或财化子孙，大利；子孙持世、财合世也有利。防鬼化子、子化鬼、父化子、子化父、父兄持世、财临空破（书中例：子孙动变鬼、又临月破，贩马多死，幸财暗动生世，未大亏本）。
  Livestock, bought, sold or raised, domestic or wild: Output is the Subject, with Wealth considered too. Wealth on the Self line fed by a moving Output or by the Day and Month, or Wealth turning into Output, great profit; Output on the Self line with Wealth combining, profit too. Beware of Pressure turning into Output or the reverse, Resource turning into Output or the reverse, Resource or Peer on the Self line, Wealth void or broken (case: Output moving into Pressure and under a Month-break; most of the horses died on the road, but a hidden-stirred Wealth generating the Self line saved the capital).

## 风险(Risks: theft, disputes, losses, a bad year)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/求财/风险.md`

- **LY.WEALTH.CA.06** 财爻不动，钱财平安；遇凶杀、逢空，终究破费。外卦的官鬼把玄武牵来，官灾、盗贼，重重后悔。劫杀临身又克身，暗昧私下的事必须忌讳。
  Wealth still, the money is safe; meeting an ill star or void, in the end it is spent. A Pressure outside bringing in the Dark Tortoise: official trouble and thieves, regrets on regrets. The Robbery star on the Body and controlling it: secret and shady dealings must be avoided.
- **LY.WEALTH.CA.09** 勾陈、官鬼发动，必被耽搁滞留；运货时官鬼临身，货被贼偷；妻财忽然化成官鬼，虽然获利，却被官府收去；行商坐贾经营钱财，切忌官爻落在本库上，不是官司送来祸事，就一定被贼偷走。
  Hook Earth and Pressure moving, you are surely held up; carrying goods with Pressure on it, thieves steal them; Wealth suddenly turning into Pressure, though profit is made the officials take it; for travelling merchants and shopkeepers, the Pressure line falling on its own store is above all to be avoided: it is either a lawsuit bringing trouble or thieves who surely steal it away.
- **LY.WEALTH.CA.17** 外卦的爻没有气、应又克身，若再遇空亡，要担心生病；土爻持世，怎么离得开；阴爻持世，主是阴人（女人）。小注：土爻持世主有阻隔，阴爻持世主被女人耽搁滞留。
  The outer lines without breath and the Other controlling the Body, and if void besides, fear illness; an Earth line on the Self, how can you get away; a yin line on the Self points to a woman. The note: an Earth line on the Self means obstruction, a yin line on the Self means being held up by a woman.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.16** 兄弟、官鬼同时发动（王洪绪），或兄弟动而官鬼克世：主口舌是非，父母再动则成官讼。兄弟动变官鬼是回头克，兄弟被制，不能劫财克世（王洪绪、觉子驳旧注）；但野鹤举例，问财而见兄化鬼，可能应在兄弟（亲人）身上。
  Peer and Pressure moving together (Wang Hongxu), or Peer moving while Pressure controls the Self line: quarrels and trouble, and a lawsuit if Resource moves too. Peer moving into Pressure is a turn-back control that disables Peer, so it cannot rob or attack the Self line (Wang Hongxu and Juezi against the old commentary); but Yehe gives a case where Peer turning into Pressure in a money question showed up as a brother's death.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.20** 占长期买卖看太岁：太岁临兄弟（劫杀），一年无利，兄弟上卦发动必有损耗；临官鬼一年惊忧，临父母一年艰辛，临财福一年顺利。
  For long-term trade, look at the Tai Sui: on Peer (the robber), no profit all year, and losses if that Peer line shows and moves; on Pressure, a year of worry; on Resource, a year of hardship; on Wealth or Output, a smooth year.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.35** 外出贩货：世应生合、官鬼空伏、动爻日辰不伤世爻则平安，财旺福兴大吉。兄鬼发动又玄武发动，遇劫窃；再克世，大祸；世空可避；官鬼动在第五爻（路），途中小心。王洪绪：官临玄武动来克世，必遭盗贼。
  Travelling to trade: Self and Other generating or combining, Pressure void or hidden, and no moving line or Day harming the Self line means safety; with Wealth strong and Output flourishing, very good. Peer and Pressure moving with the Dark Tortoise moving: robbers or thieves; if they also control the Self line, disaster; a void Self line may escape; Pressure moving on the fifth line (the road): take care on the way. Wang Hongxu: Pressure on the Dark Tortoise moving to control the Self line means certain robbery.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.39** 第五爻为道路：临官鬼发动，途中多惊险，不宜出外求财，灾祸看所临六神（白虎风波、玄武盗贼）；道路爻空亡也不宜出外；临财、福则吉。
  The fifth line is the road: Pressure on it and moving means many dangers on the way, so do not go abroad for money; the kind of trouble shows in its spirit (White Tiger, storms; Dark Tortoise, thieves); a void road line is also against travel; Wealth or Output on it is good.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.40** 第二爻为宅：在家求财而官鬼在宅爻发动，必不利，按官鬼五行断灾（火鬼火灾、水鬼盗贼）；子孙持世发动可解，否则宜迁店铺（王洪绪）；宅爻空亡，只宜在外开铺。
  The second line is the house: seeking money at home with Pressure moving on the house line goes badly; judge the trouble by its element (Fire, fire; Water, thieves). Output on the Self line and moving resolves it, otherwise move the shop (Wang Hongxu); a void house line means trade only from a shop outside.
- **LY.WEALTH.TXF.05** 凶神（如白虎、螣蛇）冲克财爻：出外行船防风波，世爻临吉神则人无事而财散，虎蛇克世则财命难保；在家求财则主恶人搅扰或因财惹官非，有解神无事。劫杀（神煞）临财，财不可得；临发动的兄弟，得财后防失，再加玄武有盗劫；贵人临官鬼持世，或官鬼动而制住兄弟，可免；官鬼无气则兄弟仍为祸。
  A baleful spirit (such as the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake) clashing or controlling the Wealth line: abroad, beware of storms on the water; with a good spirit on the Self line you are safe but the money drifts off; the Tiger or Snake controlling the Self line endangers both money and life. At home it means troublemakers or the money dragging you into an official matter; a relieving spirit ends it. The Robbery star on Wealth: the money cannot be had; on a moving Peer: beware of losing it afterwards, and with the Dark Tortoise, of robbery; the Noble star on a Pressure line holding the Self line, or a moving Pressure restraining Peer, averts it; a forceless Pressure line cannot.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.05** 走江湖水路求利：官爻衰弱就没有险难，风浪不凶险，全靠官爻衰。
  Seeking profit on rivers and lakes: a weak Pressure means no danger; that the wind and waves are not dangerous depends wholly on Pressure being weak.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.51** 卖产业：兄弟兴旺、财爻安静，或又空又衰，一定难脱手，不必多疑；官鬼鼓动、朱雀噪闹，必有官司拖累、是非招来，牵缠不安宁。
  Selling property: Peer rising with the Wealth line still, or void and weak besides, is surely hard to unload, no need to doubt it; Pressure stirring and the Vermilion Bird clamouring bring lawsuits, burdens and quarrels, and entanglement with no peace.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.59** 把财货寄放在别人那里：要财爻安静，官鬼不动、子孙旺相最好，货放在他那里无妨；兄弟发动不好；世被冲、应落空亡，财物终究被消耗，这样寄托不好。
  Leaving money and goods at another's house: the Wealth line must be still, best of all with Pressure not moving and Output strong, and there is no harm in storing the goods there; Peer moving is not good; the Self clashed and the Other void, the goods will in the end be consumed and such entrusting is not good.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.60** 寄放：世是我、应是对方，世不可空；旺相的应伤克衰弱的世，他一定不是善良之辈；世跟着官鬼入墓，我不吉祥。货物一概以财爻为主象，忌落空亡、没有气；财明现而沉静，就长久没有损失，一兴发就有变动的兆头。
  Leaving goods with someone: the Self is me and the Other is him, and the Self must not be void; a strong Other hurting and controlling a weak Self, he is surely no good sort; the Self following Pressure into the tomb, it is not auspicious for me. Goods are all judged with the Wealth line as the main image, which must not fall void or lack breath; Wealth showing and still, there is no loss for a long time, and once it stirs there is a sign of change.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.61** 寄放：财爻化成绝、化成空，终究亏损；化成兄弟，财被剥削砍伐，化成官鬼，财被侵占谋算，哪能不受伤害。
  Leaving goods: Wealth turning into extinction or void ends in loss; turning into Peer, Wealth is stripped and felled, turning into Pressure, Wealth is encroached upon and schemed against - how could there be no harm.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.62** 寄放：财化成子孙、化成财，都吉庆；化生、化旺，永远平安。子孙是福神，是生财的母亲；财爻是主宰，管着安全寄放。
  Leaving goods: Wealth turning into Output or Wealth is all auspicious; turning into Birth or strength, lasting peace. Output is the spirit of fortune, the mother that gives birth to Wealth; the Wealth line is the master in charge of safekeeping.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.63** 寄放：财化成水，要防水神玄冥，上面漏、下面潮，以致腐烂；亥子、父母发动也是这样。

- **LY.WEALTH.YL.64** 寄放：财化成火，要防火神祝融，里面走火、外面延烧；巳午、火性的官鬼发动也是这样。
  Leaving goods: Wealth turning into Fire, guard against the fire god Zhurong, fire breaking out within and spreading without; Si, Wu and a Fire Pressure moving mean the same.

  Leaving goods: meeting the Dark Tortoise, always fear thieves coming to steal; meeting the Vermilion Bird, often fear quarrels.
- **LY.WEALTH.YL.66** 寄放：六合的爻，或日辰合世、合应，最适合寄存财物，因为彼此心意相投；六冲的爻，或日辰冲用神，不能把财货安放托付给他，以后会有变卦。
  Leaving goods: with Six-Combine lines, or the Day combining with the Self and the Other, it is best to leave money and goods, because the two hearts agree; with Six-Clash lines, or the Day clashing the Subject, goods and money cannot be safely entrusted to him, for later there will be a change.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.28** 把东西寄放在别人那里：先要托给可托的人，因为人心难测，有见物起欺心的，有收藏不严招盗的，有安放不当弄坏东西的。卦六合可寄，六冲不可寄；月日和动爻合财、合世应可寄，冲财、冲世应不可寄。财旺静，或财动化子孙、化长生、化旺，无失；财衰动，或动化兄弟、官鬼、空破绝，有失。财化土朽腐，化木微黷，化火遭火灾，化水受潮漏，化金封识被拆开。世应空破、随官鬼入墓、被兄弟官鬼动伤，应爻动化兄鬼空破绝，都有失。应爻动，他心变；应动助鬼伤身世财，他与人合谋劫骗。应临玄武动偷窃，临朱雀动口舌，临勾陈动牵连，临螣蛇动惊恐，临白虎动吞没，临青龙动则善加保护。
  Leaving goods with someone: first choose a person who can be trusted, for hearts are hard to fathom: some see the goods and turn deceitful, some store them carelessly and invite thieves, some put them in the wrong place and let them spoil. A Six-Combine hexagram: you may leave things; a Six-Clash: you may not. The Month, the Day and moving lines combining with Wealth, the Self or the Other: you may; clashing with them: you may not. Wealth strong and still, or Wealth moving into Output, into Birth or into strength: nothing is lost; Wealth weak and moving, or moving into Peer, Pressure, void, break or extinction: loss. Wealth turning into Earth: rot and decay; into Wood: slightly soiled; into Fire: fire; into Water: damp and leaks; into Metal: the seals broken open. Self and Other void or broken, following Pressure into the tomb, hurt by moving Peer or Pressure, or the Other moving into Peer, Pressure, void, break or extinction: loss. The Other moving: his mind has changed; the Other moving to help Pressure hurt the Body, the Self and the Wealth line: he plots with others to rob and cheat you. The Other moving on the Dark Tortoise: theft; on the Vermilion Bird: quarrels; on Hook Earth: entanglement; on the Coiling Snake: fright; on the White Tiger: devouring; on the Azure Dragon: good safekeeping.

## 心术(The asker's intent)

### 六爻 → `chapters/六爻/求财/心术.md`

- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.18** 义取之财神告显然，不义之财神不告：品行不端的人占财，卦卦兄弟持世、发动，只示破财，不示利与不利（觉子的例子：亏待伙计的人占卖货，占了又占都是兄弟持世，终于折本流落）。
  For money honestly sought the spirits answer plainly; for ill-gotten money they do not: a man of bad conduct asking about money gets Peer on the Self line and moving every time, showing only loss and saying nothing of profit (Juezi's example: a man who cheated his partners asked about selling, got Peer on the Self line again and again, and ended ruined and adrift).
- **LY.WEALTH.ZS.32** 行险求财（不义之财）不占：古法以青龙临财为吉，觉子认为这是丧心之事，神不告不义，卜亦不验，本章不注。
  Money sought by risky or dishonest means is not divined: the old method counted the Azure Dragon on Wealth as good, but Juezi calls it heartless business; the spirits do not answer the unjust, divination does not work for it, and the chapter is left without comment.
