## LY.MARRY.HDY.05 妻在间爻女有亲为主婚、夫在间爻男有亲为主婚，得时旺相则成，出现忌日冲月破；世动男不肯、应动女生疑；用神发动成也分离；父伤损长亲，间动阻隔或媒人作鬼

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻子（财爻）在间爻上，女方有亲人做主婚人；丈夫（鬼爻）在间爻上，男方有亲人做主婚人。得时旺相就能成；用神出现时，忌日冲、月破。世爻动，男方不肯；应爻动，女方起疑。用神发动，即使成了也见分离。父母爻受伤，损害长辈；间爻动，有阻隔；或者媒人又化作鬼爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the wife (the Wealth line) is on a between line, a relative of the woman's side presides at the marriage; if the husband (the Pressure line) is on a between line, a relative of the man's side presides. If it is in season and strong it can be made; when the Subject appears, it fears a clash from the Day and a Month-break. If the Self line moves the man will not agree; if the Other line moves the woman grows doubtful. If the Subject moves, even if made it ends in separation. If the Resource line is harmed the elders are injured; a moving between line means obstruction; or the go-between turns into Pressure again.

**《海底眼·婚姻》原文** — 《海底眼》第 603–605 行 · *Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Marriage", base text; Haidi Yan, lines 603–605*

## LY.MARRY.HJC.09 如日合而世应比和，因人成事：世生应男求女、应生世女求男；日辰合爻定谁赞成；间爻动合是媒人

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世生应，是男方求女方；应生世，是女方求男方；相合也一样。世应比和，是两家互相成全。想知是谁赞成，看日辰合到哪一爻；间爻发动、与日辰相合，就是媒人。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self generating the Other means the man is seeking the woman; the Other generating the Self means the woman is seeking the man; combining is the same. The Self and Other in harmony means the two families are helping each other. To know who approves, see which line the Day combines with; a between line that moves and combines with the Day is the go-between.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.33 欲通媒妁，须论间爻：占庚帖以间爻为媒人；专占媒人则以应爻为媒

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占庚帖，以间爻为媒人；如果是专为媒人这件事占，就不按间爻论，一定以应爻为媒。

## LY.MARRY.HJC.34 应或相生，乃女家之瓜葛；世如相合，必男室之葭莩：间爻与世生合多男家亲、与应生合多女家亲；旺相新亲、休囚旧眷、本宫至亲、他宫外亲

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:间爻与世生合，多是男家的亲戚；与应生合，多是女家的亲戚；世应都被生合，两家都有亲；旺相是新亲，休囚是旧眷，本宫是至亲，他宫是外亲。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A between line generating or combining with the Self is mostly kin of the man's family, with the Other mostly kin of the woman's, and with both, both families are related to it; thriving means new relatives, weak means old ones, the home palace means nearest kin, another palace means outside kin.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.35 先观卦象之阴阳，则男女可决：阳男媒、阴女媒；间爻有二，以衰旺动静、冲动、生合扶起、不空、长生来定

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:间爻阳是男媒，阴是女媒；间爻有两个时，按衰旺、动静来取；衰爻动、旺爻静，取动的；两爻都旺或都衰，取被冲而动的；没有冲，取被生合扶起的；一旺一空，取不空的；都不空，用长生的办法；务必归到一个，就不杂乱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A yang between line is a male go-between and a yin one a female; when there are two between lines, choose by strength and movement; if the weak one moves and the thriving one is still, take the moving one; if both thrive or both are weak, take the one clashed into movement; with no clash, the one raised by generation or combination; if one thrives and one is Void, the one that is not Void; if neither is Void, use the Long Life method; always come down to one, and then it is not confused.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.36 次看卦爻之动静，则老幼堪推：交重二爻为老年人，单拆二爻为少年人；胎养长生少年、临官帝旺中年、墓绝老年

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:交、重（老阴老阳）为老年人，单、拆（少阳少阴）为少年人；胎、养、长生是少年，临官、帝旺是中年，墓、绝是老年；墓化旺，是老年人先来说；阴化阳，是女人先来说；这爻动空化空而自冲另一爻，是原来的媒人有事推辞，别人来做媒。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The doubled and crossed marks (old yin and old yang) mean an old person and the single and broken marks (young yang and young yin) a young one; Womb, Nurture and Long Life mean youth, Official and Emperor's Prime middle age, Tomb and Cut-off old age; Tomb changing to thriving means an old person spoke first; yin changing to yang means a woman spoke first; if this line moves, is Void and turns Void and itself clashes another line, the original go-between has an excuse to decline and someone else acts as go-between.

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Self and Other or the Day carry Wealth, Output or the Azure Dragon and generate or combine with the between line, the go-between surely receives thanks from both families, more if thriving and less if weak; if what generates or combines carries Peer, Pressure, the Coiling Snake or the Dark Tortoise, it is no more than fine words and empty courtesy without real benefit; changing out into Wealth or Output that generates or combines is also auspicious; a man looks at the Self line and a woman at the Other line; the Self and Other both generating or combining means both families give; the Self thriving means more from the man's family and the Other thriving more from the woman's.

## LY.MARRY.HZL.05 妻在间爻由女方亲人主婚，夫在间爻由男方亲人主婚，得时旺相皆许成；出现忌日冲，世动男不肯，应动女生疑；用神动成也分离；间动有阻隔

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻子（财爻）在间爻上，女方有亲人做主婚人；丈夫（官鬼爻）在间爻上，男方有亲人做主婚人；只要得时旺相，都许可成。用神出现时怕被日辰冲；世爻动，男方不肯；应爻动，女方起疑。用神如果发动，即使成了也会分离。间爻动有阻隔，或者是媒人化作鬼爻；如果占的是女子的美丑，另看下一条。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the wife (Wealth) is on a between line, a relative of the woman's side presides at the marriage; if the husband (Pressure) is on a between line, a relative of the man's side presides; so long as it is in season and strong, all may be made. The Subject, when it appears, fears being clashed by the Day; if the Self line moves the man will not agree, if the Other line moves the woman grows doubtful. If the Subject moves, even if made it ends in separation. A moving between line means obstruction, or the go-between turning into Pressure; if the question is the woman's looks, see the next unit.

## LY.MARRY.YL.01 嫁聚当求媒妁人，应为月老忌冲刑：卜媒人以应爻为主象，忌空冲破绝、喜扶合旺生；应克世媒心刁诈，应生世是良媒

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚以间爻为主，单问媒人以应爻的象为凭。这时应爻是主要的象：不能落在空、冲、破、绝的地方，最喜逢扶、合、旺、生。应爻克世爻，媒人心怀刁滑欺诈；应爻来生世爻，就是好媒人。应爻空而陷是没有功效，旺而有生扶才有力量。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a marriage divination the between lines come first, and when the go-between alone is asked about, rely on the sign of the Other line. Then the Other line is the chief sign: it must not fall into Void, clash, break or extinction, and is most glad to meet support, combination, strength and generation. If the Other line controls the Self line the go-between is cunning and deceitful; if it comes to generate the Self line, the go-between is a good one. If the Other line is Void and fallen it achieves nothing; strong and supported, it has power.

## LY.MARRY.YL.03 占婚又不观其应，二间为媒细审情：单占媒人看应爻；在婚姻卦里推就不凭应爻，反取间爻为媒，旺则有力、空则无能

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚时不看应爻，把两个间爻当作媒人，仔细审察实情。只占媒人时看应爻；要是在婚姻卦里推，就不凭应爻，反过来取间爻当媒人：旺就有力，空就无能。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a marriage divination one does not look at the Other line; take the two between lines as the go-betweens and examine the facts closely. ## LY.MARRY.YM.05 以间分其媒妁：近世的间爻是我方传信的媒、近应的是对方传信的妁，空散不得力、破绝不为情，发动则有变更；间爻作用神宜生助不宜空破

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用间爻分辨媒人。靠近世爻的间爻，是替我这边传话的媒；靠近应爻的间爻，是替对方传话的妁。间爻落空、散，就不得力；破、绝，就不肯出情；帮助还是欺克，两边都是这样；只有发动，才会出现或易或难的变更。间爻作用神，要有生助，不宜落空、破：有生助就有力，空、破就无功，靠近哪一边，就断它偏向哪一边。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Use the between lines to tell the go-betweens apart. The between line near Self is the go-between who carries my side's word; the one near Other is the matchmaker who carries the other side's. Void or scattered, it has no strength; broken or cut off, it does not act from feeling; whether it supports or cheats, it is so for both sides; only when it moves does a change come, easy or hard. When a between line serves as the Subject it should be supported and not Void or broken: supported, it is strong; Void or broken, it achieves nothing; judge by which side it is near.

> **Note:** 'Support, control or cheating' is said of the between line: which side it is helping and which it is cheating. 'Near them or near me' means whether the between line is next to the Other line or the Self line.

## LY.MARRY.YM.13 倩媒同于托人，用应爻：冲则不能合、克世则有私；应爻空则不力、破则不专，旺相为我则重、休囚助己则轻，冲世误事、克世存欲，合处逢冲成而复解

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:请媒人和托人办事是一样的，用应爻作用神：应爻被冲就不能相合，克世就有私心。应爻是被托付的人，所以落空就不出力，破就不专心；旺相而向着我就看重，休囚而帮着我就看轻；冲世爻是误事，克世爻是存着自己的私欲；合处遇到冲，成了又散。这几条，是请媒人时要引以为戒的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Engaging a go-between is the same as entrusting someone, so use the Other line: if it is clashed it cannot combine, and if it controls Self it has private motives. The Other line is the one entrusted, so if Void it does not exert itself and if broken it is not devoted; strong and on my side it takes the matter seriously, resting or trapped though helping me it takes it lightly; clashing Self it spoils the matter, controlling Self it keeps desires of its own; a combination that meets a clash is made and then undone. These are the cautions for asking a go-between.

## LY.MARRY.YY.18 媒约：以间爻为用；世生合间男家之亲、应生合间女家之亲、世应俱生合间两家皆亲；间阳男媒间阴女媒；衰墓老媒生旺少媒；间阴化阳女人先说；间空化空更人为媒；间兄两动二人争媒；蛇武兄动冰人诡诈；朱雀旺空媒多诳语

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以间爻为用神。世爻生合间爻，是男家的亲戚；应爻生合间爻，是女家的亲戚；世应都生合间爻，两家都是亲戚。间爻属阳是男媒人，属阴是女媒人；衰、入墓是老媒人，生旺是年轻的媒人。间爻属阴变阳，是女人先开口；间爻空又化空，换人做媒；间爻和兄弟爻都动，两个人争着做媒。螣蛇、玄武、兄弟爻动，媒人诡诈；朱雀旺相或空，媒人多说谎话。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Take the between lines as the Subject. The Self line generating or combining with a between line: a relative of the man's family; the Other line doing so: a relative of the woman's family; the Self and Other both doing so: a relative of both. A yang between line is a male go-between, a yin one a female go-between; weak or in a tomb, an old go-between; in birth or strength, a young one. A yin between line changing into yang: a woman speaks first; a between line Void and changing into Void: another person becomes the go-between; the between line and a Peer line both moving: two people compete to be the go-between. The Coiling Snake and Dark Tortoise with a moving Peer line: the go-between is crafty and deceitful; the Vermilion Bird strong or Void: he tells many lies.
