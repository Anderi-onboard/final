## LY.MARRY.BSQS.03 孤辰寡宿：婚姻犯之，主无子；亥子丑忌寅戌、寅卯辰忌巳丑、巳午未忌申辰、申酉戌忌亥未

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:孤辰寡宿：婚姻犯了它，主没有子女。歌诀：亥子丑忌寅、戌，寅卯辰找巳、丑，巳午未忌申、辰，申酉戌是亥、未来临。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Solitary Star and the Widowhood Star: if a marriage offends them, it means no children. The rhyme: for Hai, Zi and Chou beware Yin and Xu; for Yin, Mao and Chen look to Si and Chou; for Si, Wu and Wei beware Shen and Chen; for Shen, You and Xu, Hai and Wei arrive.

**《卜筮全书·神煞歌例》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 10124 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Rhymes on the spirit stars", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 10124*

**《卜筮全书·神煞歌例》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 10125 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Rhymes on the spirit stars", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 10125*

> **按**:每一句前一个地支按通行的排法是孤辰、后一个是寡宿（亥子丑年孤辰在寅、寡宿在戌，余类推），书里没有写明。「无子」在这里是没有子女的意思，书里没有区分有儿有女。
>
> **Note:** In each line, by the usual layout, the first branch is the Solitary Star and the second the Widowhood Star (for Hai, Zi and Chou years the Solitary Star is at Yin and the Widowhood Star at Xu, and so on); the book does not say so. 'No son' here means no offspring, and the book does not tell sons from daughters.

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.09 子孙用·占婚：子孙为子息，卦中无子必无儿，但不宜动，动则异日伤夫

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:我所生的是子孙爻，是卦中最吉的神：遇到它没有不好的，背离它就没法带来福；卦里没有父母爻它就不受克，有兄弟爻它就有依靠；发动就生财、克伤官鬼。占婚姻，子孙爻代表子息：卦中没有子孙，一定没有儿子；但不宜发动，动了日后要伤夫。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** What I generate is the Output line, the most auspicious spirit in a hexagram: meeting it, all is fair, and whatever turns its back on it cannot bring fortune; with no Resource line in the hexagram it is not controlled, and with Peer among the lines it has support; moving, it generates Wealth and controls and harms Pressure. In a marriage divination the Output line stands for children: if there is no Output in the hexagram there will surely be no son; but it should not move, for moving it will harm the husband some day.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 764 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 764*

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.12 父母用变·婚姻：父母爻发动克子孙，婚姻子息应难得

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻一发动就克子孙：病人无药、沉昏；婚姻和子息都难得；买卖劳心，利不存。

## LY.MARRY.HJC.10 若父动而子孙墓绝，为嗣求婚：父旺动、子孙墓绝，子息少；大人占，是无子而娶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚遇到父爻旺相发动，子孙墓绝，日后子息一定少；如果占的是长辈（大人）的婚姻，有这种象，是因为没有儿子而再娶；父爻持身、持世的也一样。

## LY.MARRY.YL.09 子云后代，遇绝无儿：子孙宜静不宜动（动则刑夫）、宜旺不宜空（空则无子）；临绝又主无儿，绝中得生扶反主儿孙多

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙爻代表后代，遇到绝就没有儿子。子孙爻宜静、不宜动，动了刑克丈夫；宜旺、不宜空，空了没有儿子；落在绝地又没有儿子；绝中如果得到生扶，反而儿孙多育。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Output line is the descendants, and meeting extinction there is no son. The Output line should be still and not moving, since moving it punishes the husband; it should be strong and not Void, since Void means no son; in a place of extinction, again no son; but if in extinction it receives generation and support, on the contrary there are many sons and grandsons.

## LY.MARRY.YL.16 孤辰值鬼夫必无儿，寡宿临财妻当少子；父旺少兄妨儿女，妻强无鬼损公姑；子摇刑夫财兴有解，兄动克妇子发无妨

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:孤辰星落在鬼爻上，丈夫必定没有儿子；寡宿星临在财爻上，妻子该少子息。男子怕孤辰临官鬼，女子嫌寡宿落妻财，遇到其中一样，以后没有子息，不仅儿女受伤，更主夫妻相克。父母爻旺、兄弟爻少，妨碍儿女；妻财强、没有鬼爻，损害公婆。子孙爻动固然刑克丈夫，但财爻兴旺就有化解；兄弟爻动虽说克妻，但子孙发动就无妨。父母爻动而兄弟爻不动，一定伤儿女；财爻动而鬼爻不动，一定损公婆。子孙动伤夫，财动去生官鬼反而吉；兄弟兴旺害妻，子孙一同发动就无妨。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Solitary Star on the Pressure line: the husband surely has no son; the Widowhood Star on the Wealth line: the wife should have few children. A man fears the Solitary Star on Pressure and a woman dislikes the Widowhood Star on Wealth; meeting either, there will be no offspring later, and not only are the children harmed, husband and wife also harm each other. A strong Resource line with few Peer lines hinders the children; a strong Wealth line without Pressure harms the parents-in-law. When the Output line moves it indeed punishes the husband, but if Wealth flourishes there is a remedy; when a Peer line moves it is said to harm the wife, yet if Output moves there is no harm. When the Resource line moves and the Peer line does not, the children are surely harmed; when Wealth moves and Pressure does not, the parents-in-law are surely hurt. When Output moves it harms the husband, but if Wealth moves to generate Pressure it is on the contrary auspicious; when a Peer line flourishes it harms the wife, but if the Output line moves together with it, there is no harm.

**《易林补遗·婚姻嫁娶章第十》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 541 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 10: marriage and giving in marriage", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 541*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

## LY.MARRY.YL.24 欲知向后生儿否，切忌福德犯空亡：子孙不论阴阳衰旺，在卦又不旬空月破，必有子孙；落空亡寻伏象，伏再空看动爻化子，更无则绝嗣；出见早生、伏藏迟育；旺相或动来速、休囚或静来迟
