## LY.MARRY.HJC.10 若父动而子孙墓绝，为嗣求婚：父旺动、子孙墓绝，子息少；大人占，是无子而娶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚遇到父爻旺相发动，子孙墓绝，日后子息一定少；如果占的是长辈（大人）的婚姻，有这种象，是因为没有儿子而再娶；父爻持身、持世的也一样。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining marriage, if the Resource line is thriving and moving and Output is in the tomb or cut off, children later will surely be few; if it is an elder's marriage that is divined, this image means he is marrying because he has no son; Resource holding the Body or the Self is the same.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6340 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6340*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 若父动而子孙墓绝，为嗣求婚。 | 如果父母爻发动而子孙墓绝，是为了子嗣而求婚。 | If Resource moves and Output falls into the tomb or is cut off, the marriage is sought for the sake of an heir. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6341 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6341*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占婚，遇父旺动，子孙若墓绝，异日子息必少。 | 占婚姻，遇到父爻旺相发动，子孙如果墓绝，日后子息一定少。 | In divining marriage, if Resource is thriving and moving and Output is in the tomb or cut off, children later will surely be few. |
| 若卜大人婚姻，有此象，乃为无子而娶也。 | 如果占的是长辈的婚姻，有这种象，是因为没有儿子而娶。 | If it is an elder's marriage that is divined, this image means he is marrying because he has no son. |
| 父持身世者亦然。 | 父爻持身、持世的也一样。 | Resource holding the Body or the Self is the same. |

## LY.MARRY.HJC.16 文书若动当权，子嗣必然萧索：父母旺动克子孙；子孙旬空反可得子，不空受伤则无子息

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻当权发动，子孙躲在空亡里，是事情还没定；如果子孙不空，而是死绝无气，就被父母克，日后一定克子，没有子息。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Resource line holds power and moves while Output hides in the Void, the matter is not yet settled; if Output is not Void but dead, cut off and without qi, it is controlled by Resource, and later the children will surely be harmed, leaving no offspring.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6361 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6361*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 文书若动当权，子嗣必然萧索。 | 文书爻如果发动当权，子嗣一定稀少冷落。 | If the document line moves and holds power, offspring will surely be few and lonely. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6362 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6362*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父母当权发动，子孙在空避之地，乃是事体未定。 | 父母爻当权发动，子孙在空亡躲避之处，那是事情还没有定。 | If the Resource line holds power and moves while Output hides in a Void place, the matter is not yet settled. |
| 若不空，而死绝无气，则被其克，异日必然克子。 | 如果不空，而是死绝无气，就被它克，日后一定克子。 | If it is not Void but dead, cut off and without qi, it is controlled by Resource, and later the children will surely be harmed. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2016 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2016*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父母旺动，子孙旬空反可得子，至子孙出空之年亦难免克； | 父母爻旺相发动，子孙旬空反而可以得子，到子孙出空的那一年也难免被克； | With Resource thriving and moving, Output in the ten-day Void can on the contrary have a child, though in the year Output leaves the Void it will still inevitably be controlled; |
| 若不空现受其伤，主无子息。 | 如果不空、现身而受伤，主没有子息。 | if it is not Void but shows and is harmed, there will be no offspring. |

## LY.MARRY.HZL.10 三爻并偶生成少，六位纯阳养育稀

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:三个爻并排全是偶（阴），生下的子女少；六个位置全是纯阳，养育的子女稀少。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Three lines side by side all even (yin): few children are born; all six places pure yang: children are seldom raised.

**《火珠林·婚姻章》原文** — 《火珠林》第 1176 行 · *Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Chapter on marriage (verse)", base text; Huozhu Lin, line 1176*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 三爻并偶生成少，六位纯阳养育稀。 | 三个爻都是偶（阴），生成的子女少；六个位置全是纯阳，养育的子女稀少。 | Three lines all even (yin): few children are born; six places all pure yang: children are seldom raised. |

他本同文、异文(程序比对,只比本文):

- 《卜筮大全》:同文 第 3650 行:三爻并偶生成少，六位纯阳养育稀
- 《周易尚占》:异文(相似 0.86) 第 644 行:三爻并偶生成少，立位纯阳生育稀

相关:LY.MARRY.HJC.10、LY.MARRY.HJC.16
