## LY.MARRY.BSQS.07 神杀断例·天喜与咸池：问喜宜用天喜，消愁宜天解星，大杀不要用，咸池星不要用来问婚

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问喜事，要看天喜星；消愁解忧，要看天解星；大杀不能用；有咸池星的时候不要问婚姻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask about a joyful event, look to the Tianxi star; to dispel sorrow, look to the Tianjie star; do not use the Great Killing star; when the Xianchi star is present, do not ask about marriage.

**《卜筮全书·二十二、神杀断例》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3161 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "No. 22, judgments by the spirit stars", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3161*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 问喜宜天喜， | 问喜事，适宜用天喜星， | To ask about joy, the Tianxi star is fitting, |
| 消愁天解星。 | 消愁解忧，要用天解星。 | to dispel sorrow, use the Tianjie star. |
| 大杀休施用， | 大杀星不要施用， | The Great Killing star is not to be used, |
| 咸池莫问婚。 | 咸池星不要用来问婚姻。 | and the Xianchi star is not to be used for asking about marriage. |

> **按**:这一句后面还有天医、血忌、往亡、三丘五墓等，都是问病、求雨、出入的口诀，与婚姻无关，没有收。咸池在婚姻里的具体说法见 LY.MARRY.BSQS.01。
>
> **Note:** The verse goes on with the Tianyi, Xueji and Wangwang stars, the Three Mounds and Five Tombs and the like, all rhymes on illness, rain-seeking and going out, which have nothing to do with marriage and are not included. What the Xianchi star means in marriage is set out at LY.MARRY.BSQS.01.

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:喜庆的事看青龙。青龙属木，春天旺；它如果和子孙一起发动，或者持世有气、不落空亡，主有婚姻、官禄、财帛，添人进口的喜事。

## LY.MARRY.BSYG.03 月建青龙：青龙在内象，月内有婚姻进人口之喜；在外象，求官进职吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:月建上的青龙：在内象，这个月里有婚姻、添人进口的喜事；在外象，求官、升职吉利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Azure Dragon of the Month: in the inner image, that month brings the joy of a marriage and of people added to the household; in the outer image, seeking office and promotion is lucky.

## LY.MARRY.BSYG.05 推占来情：旺相则婚姻官职、休囚则争财退位；内外卦俱旺相者大则求官、次则嫁娶；卦与世爻同克日则必是六畜及婚姻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要知道占卜的人为什么事来：先看旺相休囚的气，旺相是婚姻、官职的事，休囚是争财、丢官位的事。注说：春天是艮、震，夏天是巽、离，秋天是坤、兑，冬天是乾、坎；占卜一定要先看八卦的休旺之气，内外卦都旺相的，大的是求官，其次是嫁娶；内外都休囚的，则相反。卦和世爻同克日辰，来问的一定是六畜和婚姻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know what the diviner has come about, first look at whether the qi is strong, resting or trapped: strong, it is marriage and office; resting or trapped, it is quarrels over money and an official losing his place. The note says: spring is Gen and Zhen, summer Xun and Li, autumn Kun and Dui, winter Qian and Kan; a divination must first look at the resting or strong qi of the eight trigrams, and when inner and outer are both strong, the great matter is seeking office and next comes marriage; when inner and outer are both resting or trapped, it is the reverse. If the hexagram and the Self line both control the Day, it must be livestock and marriage.

**《卜筮元龟·总例门》原文** — 《大易断例卜筮元龟目》第 462 行 · *Bushi Yuangui (The Great Tortoise of Divination), "The general rules", base text; Dayi Duanli Bushi Yuangui Mu, line 462*

>
> **Note:** 'Rest and birth' looks like a slip for 'rest and strength', as the context speaks of resting and strong qi, and is translated as written; 'has a regular tunnel' is not explained and is rendered as having a regular course.

## LY.MARRY.YL.30 青龙动处无不为祥：管嫁娶喜庆善事生育，问喜即成、问财即得；但不宜冲（冲则失半）、不宜空（空则永无嘉兆）；月建青龙与戊辰青龙同断

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:青龙这个神，管僧道、嫁娶喜庆、善事、生育等。它如果遇到发动的地方，没有不吉祥的，即使带着凶神恶煞也不会因此止歇；问喜事就成，问钱财就得。但是不宜被冲，被冲就失去一半；也不宜落空，落空就永远没有好兆头。月建上的青龙和戊辰的青龙，断法相同。书上说：卦中吉利的神将叫青龙，做事求财喜气浓，求名和嫁娶都遂意，即使有忧愁的事也没有凶险。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Azure Dragon governs monks and Taoists, marriages and celebrations, good deeds and childbirth, and the like. If it meets a place where it moves, nothing fails to be auspicious, and even carrying ill spirits and fierce stars it is not stopped by them; ask about joy and it is made, ask about wealth and it is gained. But it must not be clashed, for clashed it loses half, and it must not be Void, for Void there is never a good omen. The Azure Dragon of the Month and that of a Wuchen day are judged alike. The books say: the lucky general in a hexagram is called the Azure Dragon; in undertakings and seeking wealth joy is thick, for fame and marrying all goes as wished, and even with a worrying matter there is no ill.
