## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.22 明六神诀·青龙：属木属阳，持世及交重者为财喜、为婚姻、为孕育、为姻亲，附于六爻无有不吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:青龙属木、属阳，春天旺，亥月长生，卯月最旺，在未、申墓绝。它持世或发动，主财喜、婚姻、孕育、姻亲；附在哪一爻上都吉而亨通。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Azure Dragon belongs to Wood and is yang; it thrives in spring, is born at Hai, peaks at Mao, and is in the tomb and extinct at Wei and Shen. If it holds Self or moves, it means joy over wealth, marriage, pregnancy and in-law relations; attached to any of the six lines it is lucky and smooth.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 462 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 462*

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.23 六神彖象歌·青龙入木：值旺来生世、不值空亡尽可为，求婚须得财须有

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:青龙落在木爻上，旺相、来生世爻、不落空亡，做什么都可以；求婚要有财爻；占行人一定回来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With the Azure Dragon on a Wood line, strong and coming to generate Self and not meeting Void, all may be done; to seek a marriage Wealth must be present; asking after a traveler, he will surely return.

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.24 何知章·青龙：青龙进钱财，住宅婚姻进口来

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:青龙进钱财，住宅和婚姻都会进人口来；出入求财大有所获，造作添修也没有灾。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Azure Dragon brings in money; for a house and for a marriage, people are added to the household; going in and out seeking wealth, one will gain greatly, and building or repairs bring no calamity.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 979 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 979*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 青龙进钱财， | 青龙主进钱财， | The Azure Dragon brings in money; |
| 住宅婚姻进口来， | 住宅、婚姻，人口物件都进来； | for house and marriage, people and goods come in; |
| 出入求财当大获， | 出门入门求财，该有大收获； | going out and coming in to seek wealth, there should be a great haul; |
| 造作添修不入灾。 | 建造修缮不遇灾。 | building and repairs meet no calamity. |

> **按**:「进口」是添人口：住宅添人口，婚姻娶进新人。
>
> **Note:** 'Bringing in mouths' means adding people to the household: for a house, more members; for a marriage, a new member brought in.

相关:LY.MARRY.DYTJ.22、LY.MARRY.HJC.32、LY.MARRY.HZL.20

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.25 神煞表·天月德、青龙：喜庆婚姻，求财合吉；公私吉庆，婚姻财吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:神煞表按月排：天月德按月依次是丁、坤、壬、辛、乾、甲、癸、艮、丙、乙、巽、庚，主喜庆婚姻，求财也合吉；青龙按月依次是寅、卯、辰、巳、午、未、申、酉、戌、亥、子、丑，主公私吉庆，婚姻和财都吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The table of spirit stars is laid out by month. The Heavenly-Month Virtue runs month by month as Ding, Kun, Ren, Xin, Qian, Jia, Gui, Gen, Bing, Yi, Xun, Geng, and means joyful events and marriage, and suits seeking wealth; the Azure Dragon runs month by month as Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou, and means good fortune in public and private matters, with marriage and wealth both lucky.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 366 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 366*

| 求财合吉。 | 求财也合吉。 | and it suits seeking wealth. |

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.30 占来意：旺相则来问婚姻官职，休囚则是争财官退位；卦与世爻同与日则必是六畜及婚姻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要知道占卜的人为什么事来：先看旺相休囚，旺相是婚姻、官职的事，休囚是争财、丢官的事。卦、世爻和日辰相同的话，来问的一定是六畜和婚姻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know what the diviner has come about, first look at whether the qi is strong, resting or trapped: strong, it is marriage and office; resting or trapped, it is quarrels over money and an official losing his place. If the hexagram and the Self line are the same as the Day, it must be livestock and marriage.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 502 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 502*
