# 六爻 · 婚姻 · 应期

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Marriage and relationships · Timing: when the match is made or the wedding held*

2 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.MARRY.HJC.31 日逢父合，已期合卺于三星：日辰与父爻相合，或日辰自带文书，成婚的日期已经定下

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:日辰与父爻相合，或者日辰自己带着文书（父母），主成婚的日期已经预先定下。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Day combining with the Resource line, or the Day itself carrying the document (Resource), means the wedding date has already been fixed in advance.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6413 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6413*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 日逢父合，已期合卺于三星； | 日辰与父爻相合，已经在三星（出现）的时候约好了成婚。 | The Day combining with the Resource line: the wedding is already set for the time of the Three Stars. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6414 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6414*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 日辰与父爻作合，或日辰自带文书，主成婚日期，已预定矣。 | 日辰与父爻相合，或者日辰自己带着文书，主成婚的日期已经预先定下了。 | The Day combining with the Resource line, or the Day itself carrying the document, means the wedding date has already been fixed in advance. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2050 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2050*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 日辰与父爻作合，或日辰自带文书，主成婚日期已选定。 | 日辰与父爻相合，或者日辰自己带着文书，主成婚的日期已经选定。 | The Day combining with the Resource line, or the Day itself carrying the document, means the wedding date has been chosen. |

> **按**:「三星」出自《诗经·唐风·绸缪》的「三星在天」「三星在隅」「三星在户」，是婚期的意象；「合卺」是新婚夫妇共饮交杯酒，指成婚。
>
> **Note:** 'The Three Stars' is from the Book of Odes (Tang Feng, 'Chou Miu'), 'the Three Stars are in the sky', 'in the corner', 'at the door', an image for the wedding season; 'sharing the nuptial cup' means the wedding itself.

他本同文、异文(程序比对,只比本文):

- 《卜筮正宗》:同文 第 2050 行:日逢父合，已期合卺于三星
- 《黄金策》:异文(相似 0.91) 第 814 行:日逢父合，己期合卺于三星

## LY.MARRY.YY.20 欲知过聘下定之月，以月卦临直为期；单占媒人贤奸，以应爻为用

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:想知道过聘、下定在哪个月，就以「月卦」临值的时候为期。只占媒人是贤是奸，就以应爻为用神。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know the month of the betrothal exchange and the fixing of the engagement, take the time when the 'month hexagram' is in charge. To divine alone whether the go-between is worthy or crafty, take the Other line as the Subject.

**《易隐·婚姻占·媒约》原文** — 《易隐》第 2438 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining marriage: the go-between", base text; Yi Yin, line 2438*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 欲知过聘下定之月，则以月卦临直为期也。 | 想知道过聘、下定的月份，就以月卦临值为期。 | To know the month of the betrothal exchange and the fixing of the engagement, take the time when the month hexagram is in charge as the date. |
| 又单占媒人贤奸者，则以应爻为用也。 | 又，单独占媒人是贤是奸的，就以应爻为用神。 | Again, to divine alone whether the go-between is worthy or crafty, take the Other line as the Subject. |

> **按**:「月卦临直为期」里的「月卦」是什么，原文没有解释（可能是按月排的卦或月建），译文照字面，没有替书选一种读法。
>
> **Note:** What the 'month hexagram' in 'take the time when the month hexagram is in charge' is, is not explained (perhaps a hexagram assigned to each month, or the Month branch); it is translated literally without choosing a reading for the book.

相关:LY.MARRY.YL.01、LY.MARRY.HJC.31
