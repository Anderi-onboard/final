## LY.MARRY.HJC.01 欲谐伉俪，必定阴阳：阳奇阴偶，世阳应阴、鬼阳财阴、内阳外阴，阴阳相得成夫妇之道

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:夫妻要和谐，先看阴阳配不配：世属阳、应属阴，鬼（官）属阳、财属阴，内卦属阳、外卦属阴；各得其位，才是夫妇之道，日后有利。男家占，宜世阳应阴；女家占，宜世阴应阳。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For a couple to be harmonious, first see whether yin and yang match: the Self is yang and the Other yin, Pressure is yang and Wealth yin, the inner trigram is yang and the outer yin; each in its place is the way of husband and wife, and later days will be favourable. When the man's family divines, the Self should be yang and the Other yin; when the woman's family divines, the Self should be yin and the Other yang.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.18 如逢财鬼空亡，乃婚姻之大忌；苟遇阴阳得位，实天命之所关：男占女用财、女占男用鬼，用神无损害为要，空亡不可执法推

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财、鬼两爻是占婚姻的用神，逢空亡本不吉，但不可死守：男占女以财为主，鬼空不妨；女占男以鬼为主，财空不妨；要紧的是用神没有损害。财鬼空亡而干支相合、世应相生、阴阳得位，是真姻缘，只是半辈子同床、不能白头到老，那是天命，不是刑克；逢冲遇克叫避空，反而不用担心。长辈占子侄、兄占弟，就看子孙爻、兄弟爻，不能一律用财官。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Wealth and Pressure lines are the Subject in a marriage divination and Void on them is by nature unlucky, but it must not be followed rigidly: when a man divines about a woman Wealth is the main thing and Pressure Void does no harm; when a woman divines about a man Pressure is the main thing and Wealth Void does no harm; what matters is that the Subject is undamaged. If Wealth and Pressure are Void but the stems and branches combine, the Self and Other generate each other and yin and yang are in their places, it is a real marriage bond, only that they share a pillow for half a lifetime and do not grow old together, which is decreed by Heaven and not the work of punishment or control; meeting a clash or control is called escaping the Void and there is on the contrary nothing to fear. When elders divine for a son or nephew, or an elder brother for a younger, look at the Output line or the Peer line, and do not always use Wealth and Pressure.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6367 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6367*

| 苟遇阴阳得位，实天命之所关。 | 如果遇到阴阳得位，实在是天命所关。 | meeting yin and yang in their places is truly a matter of the decree of Heaven. |

## LY.MARRY.HJC.45 一卦吉凶，细察精微委曲：此章只论男卜女婚、女卜男姻；择婿择媳嫁妹娶嫂，要按用神断，不可一概以官为夫、财为妇

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:一卦的吉凶，要细察其中精微曲折；夫妇百年，才知道到底能否团圆。王洪绪特别说：这一章只是讲男子占女方婚事、女子占男方姻缘；今天的术士不辨清楚，凡择婿、择媳、嫁妹、娶嫂，都不按用神断，一概以官为夫、财为妇，大大误人；应当按用神的吉凶来推断，不可一概只论财官。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The good and ill of a hexagram is to be examined for its fine and winding detail; only over a lifetime as husband and wife does one know whether they come together in the end. Wang Hongxu adds especially: this chapter speaks only of a man divining about a bride or a woman divining about a bridegroom; today's practitioners, not seeing the distinction, for choosing a son-in-law or daughter-in-law, marrying off a younger sister or taking a brother's wife, do not judge by the Subject but always take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife, which misleads people greatly; one should judge by the good or ill of the Subject and never speak of Wealth and Pressure alike in every case.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6458 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6458*

| 百年夫妇，方知到底团圆。 | 百年夫妇，才知道到底能不能团圆。 | only over a lifetime as husband and wife does one know whether they come together in the end. |

> **打架**:这一章的财官各指谁：旧注一路按「官为夫、财为妇」讲（见 HJC.11、HJC.24）；王洪绪在章末明说这是「男卜女婚、女卜男姻」的意思，别人代占、兄占弟婚之类要按用神断，不可一概；觉子（《增删卜易》）也说男家占女以财为用、女家占男以官为用，不拘父母亲朋代占都同。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Whom Wealth and Pressure stand for in this chapter: the old commentary throughout speaks by 'Pressure the husband, Wealth the wife' (see HJC.11, HJC.24); Wang Hongxu says at the chapter's end that this is what applies when a man divines about a bride or a woman about a bridegroom, and that divinations made on another's behalf or by an elder brother for a younger must be judged by the Subject and not all alike; Juezi (Zengshan Buyi) also says that the man's family divining about the woman takes Wealth as the Subject and the woman's family divining about the man takes Pressure, the same whether parents or relatives divine on their behalf.
