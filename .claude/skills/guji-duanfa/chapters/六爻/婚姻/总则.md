## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.18 六爻俱静类：卦遇六爻安静须看用与日辰，日辰克用及冲刑宜谨慎，世应临用神及原神则昌盛；娶妻买妾用爻宜静、有气为先

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六爻全都安静的卦，要看用神和日辰：日辰克用神或者冲刑它，事情要谨慎；再推究世应，忌神千万不要加临，世应临用神和原神，做事一定昌盛。娶妻买妾、屋宅坟田、住庵谒人、失物理冤这些事，用爻宜静，有气为先。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When all six lines of a hexagram are still, look at the Subject and the Day: if the Day controls, clashes with or punishes the Subject, the matter calls for caution; then examine Self and Other: the Adversary must on no account come upon them, and if Self and Other sit on the Subject and the Support spirit, the undertaking will certainly flourish. For taking a wife or buying a concubine, houses and graves, living in a hermitage or calling on people, lost property and righting a wrong, the Subject line should be still and having qi comes first.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 652 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 652*

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.20 占公私用事：官用取官、私用取财；占婚问夫看官爻，占婚姻事看财爻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官用取官，私用取财。占病（鬼祟）、占失（看贼）、求官事、官司词讼、占婚姻问丈夫，这些都看官爻；占买卖、家事、奴婢事、求财事、婚姻事，这些都看财爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For an official matter take Pressure, for a private matter take Wealth. Illness (haunting), loss (look for the thief), seeking office, lawsuits, and marriage when asking after the husband: all of these look at the Pressure line; trade, household affairs, servant matters, seeking wealth and marriage matters: all of these look at the Wealth line.

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.29 总断门：占婚嫌财死，占产看阴阳

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚姻，忌财爻死绝；占生产，看阴阳。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a marriage divination, Wealth dead is disliked; in childbirth, look at yin and yang.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 2846 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 2846*

## LY.MARRY.HDY.02 占妻看财宜动，占夫有鬼宜静

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占妻子看财爻，宜动；占丈夫要有鬼爻，宜静。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To divine a wife look at the Wealth line, which should move; to divine a husband the Pressure line must be present and should be still.

**《海底眼·婚姻》原文** — 《海底眼》第 597 行 · *Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Marriage", base text; Haidi Yan, line 597*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占妻看财宜动，占夫有鬼宜静。 | 占妻子看财爻，宜动；占丈夫要有鬼爻，宜静。 | To divine a wife look at the Wealth line, which should move; to divine a husband the Pressure line must be present and should be still. |

> **打架**:占妻看财爻：《海底眼》说宜动；《火珠林》说「占妻看财爻，宜静」（LY.MARRY.HZL.04）；《易林补遗》也说要静。占夫看鬼爻，两家都说宜静。这一处是《海底眼》独异，也可能是转录之误，书里无从核对。
>
> **Where they disagree:** For divining a wife by the Wealth line, the Haidi Yan says it should move; the Huozhulin says 'for a wife look at the Wealth line, which should be still' (LY.MARRY.HZL.04); the Yilin Buyi also asks for stillness. For a husband by the Pressure line both say it should be still. Here the Haidi Yan stands alone; it may be a transcription slip, and there is nothing in the books to check it against.

相关:LY.MARRY.HZL.04、LY.MARRY.YL.19

## LY.MARRY.HJC.01 欲谐伉俪，必定阴阳：阳奇阴偶，世阳应阴、鬼阳财阴、内阳外阴，阴阳相得成夫妇之道

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:夫妻要和谐，先看阴阳配不配：世属阳、应属阴，鬼（官）属阳、财属阴，内卦属阳、外卦属阴；各得其位，才是夫妇之道，日后有利。男家占，宜世阳应阴；女家占，宜世阴应阳。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For a couple to be harmonious, first see whether yin and yang match: the Self is yang and the Other yin, Pressure is yang and Wealth yin, the inner trigram is yang and the outer yin; each in its place is the way of husband and wife, and later days will be favourable. When the man's family divines, the Self should be yang and the Other yin; when the woman's family divines, the Self should be yin and the Other yang.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.18 如逢财鬼空亡，乃婚姻之大忌；苟遇阴阳得位，实天命之所关：男占女用财、女占男用鬼，用神无损害为要，空亡不可执法推

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财、鬼两爻是占婚姻的用神，逢空亡本不吉，但不可死守：男占女以财为主，鬼空不妨；女占男以鬼为主，财空不妨；要紧的是用神没有损害。财鬼空亡而干支相合、世应相生、阴阳得位，是真姻缘，只是半辈子同床、不能白头到老，那是天命，不是刑克；逢冲遇克叫避空，反而不用担心。长辈占子侄、兄占弟，就看子孙爻、兄弟爻，不能一律用财官。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Wealth and Pressure lines are the Subject in a marriage divination and Void on them is by nature unlucky, but it must not be followed rigidly: when a man divines about a woman Wealth is the main thing and Pressure Void does no harm; when a woman divines about a man Pressure is the main thing and Wealth Void does no harm; what matters is that the Subject is undamaged. If Wealth and Pressure are Void but the stems and branches combine, the Self and Other generate each other and yin and yang are in their places, it is a real marriage bond, only that they share a pillow for half a lifetime and do not grow old together, which is decreed by Heaven and not the work of punishment or control; meeting a clash or control is called escaping the Void and there is on the contrary nothing to fear. When elders divine for a son or nephew, or an elder brother for a younger, look at the Output line or the Peer line, and do not always use Wealth and Pressure.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6367 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6367*

## LY.MARRY.YL.05 夫唱妇随卦必阴阳得位，男情女喜爻当财鬼俱全：内卦为夫外卦为妇、世为夫应为妇，内阳外阴或世阳应阴为得位；有鬼无财有夫无妇，有财无鬼有妇无夫

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:夫唱妇随，卦里阴阳要得位；男女情投意合，爻里财、鬼要齐全。内卦是夫、外卦是妇，又以世爻为夫、应爻为妇：内阳外阴，或世阳应阴，都算得位，婚姻遇到百年和合。内阴外阳，或世阴应阳，是阴阳交错，夫妻只有一半称心。世应得位而内外卦纯阴纯阳，或内外卦得位而世应纯阴纯阳，都只是半吉。内外卦和世应全是纯阳或纯阴，不成夫妇——自古纯阴不生、纯阳不化，要一阴一阳才成配偶。再看：鬼爻是夫、财爻是妇；有鬼无财是有夫无妇，有财无鬼是有妇无夫；财、鬼齐全，夫妇才能和谐到老。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For the husband to lead and the wife to follow the hexagram must have yin and yang in their places; for man and woman to love and delight in each other, the lines must have Wealth and Pressure complete. Inner yin with outer yang, or the Self yin with the Other yang, is yin and yang crossed, and husband and wife have their wish only by half. If the Self and Other lines are in their places but the inner and outer trigrams are pure yin or pure yang, or the reverse, it is only half auspicious. If the inner and outer trigrams and the Self and Other lines are all pure yang or pure yin, they do not become husband and wife: since antiquity pure yin does not give birth and pure yang does not transform, and only one yin with one yang makes a pair. ## LY.MARRY.ZS.05 以应爻为男家是死法：财官为重，世应为轻，女家占男也要财爻不失陷

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:女家占男，用官鬼，把应爻当男家——这是古法，也是死法，断卦要活。官鬼旺相持世（婚该成）而应爻受克（男家像是不肯）时怎么断？觉子的判法：财、官为重，世、应为轻；就算是女家占男，也要财爻不失陷。男女两个人的事，财爻被回头克、又被丑土克，就生不了卯木官鬼，男女不能相合相生，婚姻即使成了也会有变。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the woman's family divines about the man, Pressure is the Subject and the Other line stands for the man's family; that is the old method, and a dead one, and a hexagram must be judged with flexibility. What if a strong Pressure line holds the Self line (the marriage should be made) while the Other line is controlled (the man's family seems unwilling)? Juezi's judgment: Wealth and Pressure carry the weight, the Self and Other lines matter less; even when the woman's family divines about the man, the Wealth line must not fall into ruin. The matter concerns both the man and the woman: if Wealth is controlled by what it turns into and again by Chou Earth, it cannot generate the Mao Wood Pressure, the two cannot combine or generate each other, and even if the marriage is made it will meet change.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.14 咸临节泰吉、暌革解离凶的古说不可执：仍用财官世应断；有疑再占一卦，以后一卦为准

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:古人把咸、临、节、泰当作占婚的吉卦，把暌、革、解、离当作凶卦。觉子引《易冒》：得咸、临、节、泰，只要不遇到合冲、变冲，即使财官衰陷也不算凶；暌、革、解、离是凶兆，用神齐全也不算吉。觉子自己试了：得泰卦，可是兄弟持世、财爻月破旬空又被世爻克、日辰刑，他不敢拿别人的婚姻大事去试；让人再占一卦得坤卦，六冲大凶，才知道前面的泰卦不是吉，还是以财、官、世、应来断为对。有疑问不要死执古法，以再占的卦为准：后卦吉就按吉断，后卦凶就按凶推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The ancients took Xian, Lin, Jie and Tai as auspicious hexagrams for marriage and Kui, Ge, Xie and Li as ill ones. Juezi quotes the Yi Mao: with Xian, Lin, Jie or Tai, unless it meets a combination-clash or a change into a clash, it is not ill even if Wealth and Pressure are weak and fallen; Kui, Ge, Xie and Li are ill omens, and it is not auspicious even if the Subject is complete. Juezi tried it himself: he obtained Tai, but with a Peer line on the Self, the Wealth line under a Month-break and Void and also controlled by the Self line and punished by the Day, and he dared not test it on someone's great matter of marriage; when the person divined again and got Kun, a Six-clash of great ill, he knew the earlier Tai was not auspicious, and that judging by Wealth, Pressure, Self and Other remains right. When in doubt do not cling to the old methods; decide by the hexagram divined again: if the later one is auspicious judge it auspicious, if it is ill reckon it ill.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.23 占夫妇要先分清问什么：妻寿、偕老、和睦、刑伤破败；妻、妾、婢一卦只占一人，不可统问

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占夫妻，先要分清楚问的是哪一件：妻子的寿数，夫妻能不能白头偕老，夫妻和睦不和睦，或者妻子有没有刑伤破败。占妻、占妾、占婢女，一卦只占一个人，不可以合在一起问。
