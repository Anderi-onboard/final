## LY.MARRY.BSYG.01 妻妾财同此：妻、妾和财都看同一个财爻；凡婚姻及求财，皆要此爻旺相有气，不落空亡

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻、妾和财是同一类，都看财爻。占婚姻和求财，这一爻都要旺相有气，不落空亡。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wife, concubine and wealth are the same kind, all read from the Wealth line. In divining a marriage or seeking wealth alike, this line must be strong and have qi, and must not fall Void.

## LY.MARRY.BSZZ.01 辟《易林补遗》婚姻嫁娶之谬：用神要按占的人与被占的人是什么关系来取，不能一概以内外卦、世应、财鬼论夫妇

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:《易林补遗》一概拿内外卦、世应爻、阴阳财鬼来论夫妇，不管是谁在占、为谁占，这是大谬。王洪绪主张：世爻是我家、应爻是对方家；女人自己占嫁人，以官爻为丈夫，官世相生相合、官阳世阴才叫得地；男人自己占娶妻，以财爻为妻子，财世相生相合、财阴世阳才叫得地；父母尊长为子孙的婚事占，以子孙爻为用神，世上的子孙或阳象是我家的男孩，应上的子孙或阴象是对方的女孩；无冲破而有生合就可配、夫妻和谐，无生合而有冲克就夫妻不睦，休囚或受克就不长寿；为弟弟娶妻、妹妹出嫁占，都以兄弟爻为用神。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Yilin Buyi judges husband and wife by the inner and outer trigrams, the Self and Other lines, and yin, yang, Wealth and Pressure alike, without regard to who divines and for whom; this is a great mistake. Wang Hongxu holds: the Self line is my family and the Other line the other family; when a woman divines for herself about marrying, Pressure is the husband, and only if Pressure and Self generate and combine, with Pressure yang and Self yin, is it called being in place; when a man divines for himself about marrying, Wealth is the wife, and only if Wealth and Self generate and combine, with Wealth yin and Self yang, is it in place; when parents or elders divine the marriage of a child, Output is the Subject, with Output on Self or on a yang image the boy of my family and Output on Other or on a yin image the girl of the other family; with generation and combination and no clash or break they can be matched and are harmonious, with clash and control and no generation or combination they are at odds, and resting or controlled they will not live long; for a younger brother's marriage or a younger sister's, take the Peer line as the Subject.

> **打架**:这是王洪绪对《易林补遗》婚姻说法的正面批评（LY.MARRY.YL.05、YL.06、YL.20、YL.21）：《易林补遗》一概按内外卦、世应、财鬼论夫妇，王洪绪要求先分清是谁在占、替谁占，再按这层关系取用神。《增删卜易》也讲代占与自占取用不同（LY.MARRY.ZS.04）。

> **Where they disagree:** This is Wang Hongxu's direct attack on the Yilin Buyi's teaching on marriage (LY.MARRY.YL.05, YL.06, YL.20 and YL.21): the Yilin Buyi judges husband and wife throughout by the inner and outer trigrams, Self and Other, and Wealth and Pressure, whereas Wang Hongxu requires first telling who is divining and for whom, and choosing the Subject by that relation. The Zengshan Buyi likewise distinguishes taking the Subject for a proxy from taking it for oneself (LY.MARRY.ZS.04).

> **按**:张星元即《易林补遗》的作者张世宝（字星元）。「先天之妙旨」指易的本意，书里没有再解释。第三、四、五段是两个卦例，卦图（六爻的排列）不收，只收卦图前后的文字；第二个卦例的前一句在卦图之前。
>
> **Note:** Zhang Xingyuan is the author of the Yilin Buyi, Zhang Shibao. 'The fine purport of the primal Way' means the original intent of the Changes and is not explained further. The third to fifth passages belong to two cases, whose hexagram diagrams (the arrangement of the six lines) are not included, only the text before and after the diagrams; the second case's first sentence comes before its diagram.

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.18 六爻俱静类：卦遇六爻安静须看用与日辰，日辰克用及冲刑宜谨慎，世应临用神及原神则昌盛；娶妻买妾用爻宜静、有气为先

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六爻全都安静的卦，要看用神和日辰：日辰克用神或者冲刑它，事情要谨慎；再推究世应，忌神千万不要加临，世应临用神和原神，做事一定昌盛。娶妻买妾、屋宅坟田、住庵谒人、失物理冤这些事，用爻宜静，有气为先。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When all six lines of a hexagram are still, look at the Subject and the Day: if the Day controls, clashes with or punishes the Subject, the matter calls for caution; then examine Self and Other: the Adversary must on no account come upon them, and if Self and Other sit on the Subject and the Support spirit, the undertaking will certainly flourish. For taking a wife or buying a concubine, houses and graves, living in a hermitage or calling on people, lost property and righting a wrong, the Subject line should be still and having qi comes first.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.18 如逢财鬼空亡，乃婚姻之大忌；苟遇阴阳得位，实天命之所关：男占女用财、女占男用鬼，用神无损害为要，空亡不可执法推

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财、鬼两爻是占婚姻的用神，逢空亡本不吉，但不可死守：男占女以财为主，鬼空不妨；女占男以鬼为主，财空不妨；要紧的是用神没有损害。财鬼空亡而干支相合、世应相生、阴阳得位，是真姻缘，只是半辈子同床、不能白头到老，那是天命，不是刑克；逢冲遇克叫避空，反而不用担心。长辈占子侄、兄占弟，就看子孙爻、兄弟爻，不能一律用财官。

If Wealth and Pressure are Void but the stems and branches combine, the Self and Other generate each other and yin and yang are in their places, it is a real marriage bond, only that they share a pillow for half a lifetime and do not grow old together, which is decreed by Heaven and not the work of punishment or control; meeting a clash or control is called escaping the Void and there is on the contrary nothing to fear. ## LY.MARRY.YL.05 夫唱妇随卦必阴阳得位，男情女喜爻当财鬼俱全：内卦为夫外卦为妇、世为夫应为妇，内阳外阴或世阳应阴为得位；有鬼无财有夫无妇，有财无鬼有妇无夫

## LY.MARRY.YM.03 咸恒节泰为吉、睽革解离为凶，惟合冲变冲则不宜；婚姻忌三冲则不索用神之衰旺，用神未胜于卦验

## LY.MARRY.ZS.04 男卜女姻财要旺，女占男配鬼宜兴：代占以应爻为对方家，自占以应爻为女身；财为重，应为附和

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:男方占，妻财要旺；女方占，官鬼要兴旺。代占的：男家占女，用财爻，应爻算女家；女家占男，用官鬼，应爻算男家。财、官要旺相，动了要化出吉象；应爻不该空、破，也不该克世（女家代占的应爻还不该入墓、绝）。男子自占娶妻：也用财爻，应爻兼算女子本人；财爻和应爻生世、持世、合世都吉，最忌破、墓、绝、空；财旺生世而应爻破空还可取，应爻生世而财爻破绝就不要求。野鹤补一句：财、应一起来生合世更吉；大体上财为重，应只是附和。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A man divining wants Wealth strong; a woman divining wants Pressure flourishing. For a divination on someone's behalf: when the man's family asks about the woman, use the Wealth line and take the Other line as her family; when the woman's family asks about the man, use the Pressure line and take the Other line as his family. Wealth or Pressure should be strong, and if it moves it should change into good; the Other line should not be Void or broken, nor control the Self line (in the woman's family's divination it should not be in a tomb or extinguished either). A man divining his own wife-to-be also uses the Wealth line and takes the Other line as the woman herself; Wealth and the Other line are good when they generate, hold or combine with the Self line, and most to be feared when broken, entombed, extinguished or Void; strong Wealth generating the Self while the Other line is broken or Void can still be taken, but if the Other line generates the Self while Wealth is broken or extinguished, do not seek it. Yehe adds: it is better still when Wealth and the Other line both come to generate or combine with the Self line; in general Wealth carries the weight and the Other line only follows along.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.05 以应爻为男家是死法：财官为重，世应为轻，女家占男也要财爻不失陷

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:女家占男，用官鬼，把应爻当男家——这是古法，也是死法，断卦要活。官鬼旺相持世（婚该成）而应爻受克（男家像是不肯）时怎么断？觉子的判法：财、官为重，世、应为轻；就算是女家占男，也要财爻不失陷。男女两个人的事，财爻被回头克、又被丑土克，就生不了卯木官鬼，男女不能相合相生，婚姻即使成了也会有变。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the woman's family divines about the man, Pressure is the Subject and the Other line stands for the man's family; that is the old method, and a dead one, and a hexagram must be judged with flexibility. What if a strong Pressure line holds the Self line (the marriage should be made) while the Other line is controlled (the man's family seems unwilling)? Juezi's judgment: Wealth and Pressure carry the weight, the Self and Other lines matter less; even when the woman's family divines about the man, the Wealth line must not fall into ruin. The matter concerns both the man and the woman: if Wealth is controlled by what it turns into and again by Chou Earth, it cannot generate the Mao Wood Pressure, the two cannot combine or generate each other, and even if the marriage is made it will meet change.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.14 咸临节泰吉、暌革解离凶的古说不可执：仍用财官世应断；有疑再占一卦，以后一卦为准

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:古人把咸、临、节、泰当作占婚的吉卦，把暌、革、解、离当作凶卦。觉子引《易冒》：得咸、临、节、泰，只要不遇到合冲、变冲，即使财官衰陷也不算凶；暌、革、解、离是凶兆，用神齐全也不算吉。觉子自己试了：得泰卦，可是兄弟持世、财爻月破旬空又被世爻克、日辰刑，他不敢拿别人的婚姻大事去试；让人再占一卦得坤卦，六冲大凶，才知道前面的泰卦不是吉，还是以财、官、世、应来断为对。有疑问不要死执古法，以再占的卦为准：后卦吉就按吉断，后卦凶就按凶推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The ancients took Xian, Lin, Jie and Tai as auspicious hexagrams for marriage and Kui, Ge, Xie and Li as ill ones. Juezi quotes the Yi Mao: with Xian, Lin, Jie or Tai, unless it meets a combination-clash or a change into a clash, it is not ill even if Wealth and Pressure are weak and fallen; Kui, Ge, Xie and Li are ill omens, and it is not auspicious even if the Subject is complete. Juezi tried it himself: he obtained Tai, but with a Peer line on the Self, the Wealth line under a Month-break and Void and also controlled by the Self line and punished by the Day, and he dared not test it on someone's great matter of marriage; when the person divined again and got Kun, a Six-clash of great ill, he knew the earlier Tai was not auspicious, and that judging by Wealth, Pressure, Self and Other remains right. When in doubt do not cling to the old methods; decide by the hexagram divined again: if the later one is auspicious judge it auspicious, if it is ill reckon it ill.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.32 反吟有冲有克重于伏吟，两者都要看用神旺衰：伏吟用神旺相则冲开之年月志伸，休囚则不过忧郁；不可一概以反伏为畏途

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:伏吟和反吟比，反吟有冲有克，用神再受克，祸不轻。伏吟的卦，用神旺相，在冲开的年月，心志得以伸展；用神休囚，在冲开的年月，不过是忧郁罢了。李我平说：《易林补遗》讲「爻有伏吟不吉」「卦有反吟最凶」，是不看用神的旺衰，竟把反吟、伏吟当成要躲开的路；野鹤的办法是看用神，教人多么透彻明快。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Compared with the Reversed Chant, the Hidden Chant is the lighter, since the Reversed Chant has clash and control, and when the Subject is controlled the misfortune is not slight. In a Hidden Chant hexagram, if the Subject is strong, then in the year or month when it is clashed open one's aims are carried out; if the Subject is resting or trapped, then in that year or month there is only gloom. Li Woping says the Yilin Buyi's 'a line with a Hidden Chant is not auspicious' and 'a hexagram with a Reversed Chant is the worst' do not look at whether the Subject is strong or weak and simply treat Reversed and Hidden Chant as roads to be dreaded; the Old Man of Yehe's way, judging by the Subject, is thoroughly clear and incisive.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.35 占身命不可一卦兼断：夫妻偕老要另占一卦；「兄动妻亡」这类一条通断，世上贫寒之士岂都是丧偶之人

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:各书占身命，说妻子、钱财、儿女、官禄一个卦都能包，寿命长短、穷困通达六爻一起断。野鹤不同意：五个六亲各有互相忌讳互相伤害的地方，一个卦兼着断，就会出笑话——父母爻旺相说是「双庆」，又说「父旺伤子」，难道世上有父母的人都没有子嗣？兄弟爻旺持世，是克妻，是耗财，还是手足无伤？《易林补遗》说「兄动妻亡财耗散」，照这样断，世上的穷人和寒士岂不都是丧偶的人？觉子的办法是分开占：占父母、占兄弟各占一卦；终身的财福、终身的功名、夫妻能不能偕老、终身的子嗣和寿数，都应分开占。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The books on divining one's person and fate say that wife, wealth, children and salary can all be covered by one hexagram, and length of life and poverty or success judged together from the six lines. The Old Man of Yehe disagrees: the five relations each have things that fear and harm one another, and judging them all together from one hexagram produces absurdities: a strong Resource line is called 'double celebration' and it is also said that 'a strong Resource harms Output', but is everyone in the world who has parents childless? A strong Peer line on the Self line: does it harm the wife, waste money, or leave the brothers unharmed? The Yilin Buyi says 'a moving Peer line: the wife dies and wealth is scattered'; holding to this, are all the poor and humble scholars of the world people who have lost their spouses? Juezi's method is to divine separately: one hexagram for parents and one for brothers; and lifelong wealth and fortune, lifelong office and fame, whether husband and wife will grow old together, and lifelong children and length of life should each be divined separately.
