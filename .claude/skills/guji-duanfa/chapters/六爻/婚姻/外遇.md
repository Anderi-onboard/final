## LY.MARRY.HJC.11 财官动合，先私而后公：财官两爻动合，先通后娶；临玄武眉目传情未通；财与旁爻合，与他人有情

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以官为丈夫、财为妻子；两爻都动而相合，一定是先私通后成婚；遇冲克，外人已经知道；临玄武，只是眉来眼去、还没有通情意；本人自占，财和世爻动而相合，也是这样；财爻与旁爻相合，是与别人有情；财遇合多、又化出子孙，一定是妓家出身，因为化子孙有从良的象。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife; if both lines move and combine, they were surely intimate in secret before they married; if a clash or control meets them, outsiders already know; on the Dark Tortoise, it is only glances and looks and feelings have not yet been exchanged; when one divines for oneself, Wealth and the Self line moving and combining is the same; if the Wealth line combines with another line, she has feelings for someone else; Wealth meeting many combinations and turning into Output means she is surely from a brothel, since turning into Output carries the image of leaving that life to marry.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.21 父合财爻，异日有新台之行；世临妻位，他时无就养之心：财父二爻带玄武动合，有翁淫子媳之事；财临世身，妇不善事公婆

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚遇到财、父两爻，世带玄武，动而相合的，日后有公公与儿媳私通的事；如果财临世身，那个妇人一定不善于侍奉公婆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining marriage, when the Wealth and Resource lines with the Self carrying the Dark Tortoise move and combine, there will later be an affair between the father-in-law and his son's wife; if Wealth sits on the Self or the Body, the woman will surely not serve her parents-in-law well.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6379 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6379*

## LY.MARRY.YY.24 妻爻带玄武咸池红艳杀加驿马动必贪淫；合应爻旁爻必与外人私通，进神动来生合淫无度，退神动来合制眼去眉来无实，合逢冲被人撞见；兄合财兄私弟妇、父合财翁妇相通、子合财义男共枕；福（子）动化鬼合财亦然；此断须带咸池红艳杀

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻爻带玄武、咸池、红艳煞，又加驿马而动，一定放纵贪淫。妻爻去合应爻旁边的爻，一定和外人私通：遇进神动来生合，淫乱没有节度；遇退神动来合住，只是眉目传情、没有实事（合又逢空也是这样）；合又逢冲，被人撞见，虽然淫乱但不泛滥。妻爻暗动、去合应爻旁的爻，要防私奔。兄弟爻合财爻，是哥哥私通弟媳；父母爻合财爻，是公公与儿媳相通；子孙爻合财爻，是义子与她同枕；福德爻动化鬼合财、财动化鬼合福德，也是这样——但这些都要带咸池、红艳煞才可以断。应爻与妻爻临兄弟爻，动化财爻来合世的，是姨妹一同的事。《黄金策》说合多而刑煞临身，女子必为娼；《涯泉摘锦》说贵人多是舞裙歌扇，合多是暗约偷期。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The wife's line carrying the Dark Tortoise, the Xianchi star and the Red Allure star, with the Post Horse added and moving: she is surely willful and lustful. If the wife's line combines with a line beside the Other line, she surely has an affair with an outsider: if an Advancing spirit moves to generate or combine with it, her licentiousness is without measure; if a Retreating spirit moves to combine with and restrain it, there are only glances and looks with no actual act (a combination meeting Void is the same); if the combination meets a clash and she is caught by someone, although licentious she is not indiscriminate. If the wife's line stirs in secret and combines with a line beside the Other line, beware of an elopement. A Peer line combining with Wealth: an elder brother has an affair with his younger brother's wife; the Resource line combining with Wealth: a father-in-law and daughter-in-law are involved; Output combining with Wealth: an adopted son shares a pillow with her; Output moving, changing into Pressure and combining with Wealth, or Wealth moving, changing into Pressure and combining with Output, is the same — but all this may be judged only where the Xianchi and Red Allure stars are carried. The Other line and the wife's line on a Peer line, moving and changing into Wealth to combine with the Self line: the wife's younger sister is involved too. The Huangjin Ce says that many combinations with punishment and baleful stars on the Body make the woman a prostitute; the Yaquan Zhaijin says that many Noblemen mean entertainers and many combinations mean secret trysts.

**《易隐·断妻妾》原文** — 《易隐》第 1256 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Judging wives and concubines", base text; Yi Yin, line 1256*
