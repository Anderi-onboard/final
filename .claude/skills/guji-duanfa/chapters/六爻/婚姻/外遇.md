## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.31 占行人·咸池临玄武恐逢觅水之欢：用爻加咸池玄武则行人有外情牵惹不归，二星动合应爻或用爻是觅水之欢，青龙动合世应必娶妾

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占在外的人（行人）：咸池星临在玄武上，恐怕他会遇上寻欢的事。小注展开说：用爻如果加上咸池、玄武，行人在外有别的情爱牵住，不回来；咸池、玄武发动，去合应爻或用爻，是在外寻欢；青龙发动去合世爻和应爻，他一定会娶妾。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Divining a traveller: if the Xianchi star sits on the Dark Tortoise, he may meet a dalliance. The note goes on: if the Subject line has the Xianchi star and the Dark Tortoise added, the traveller has an outside affair holding him and does not return; if the Xianchi star and the Dark Tortoise move and combine with the Other line or the Subject line, it is a dalliance; if the Azure Dragon moves and combines with Self and Other, he will surely take a concubine.

**《断易天机·占行人第二十二》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1459 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 22: Divining a traveller", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 1459*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 咸池若临玄武， | 咸池星如果临在玄武上， | If the Xianchi star sits on the Dark Tortoise, |
| 恐逢觅水之欢。 | 恐怕会遇上寻欢作乐的事。 | he may meet with the pleasures of seeking the water (a dalliance). |

**《断易天机》注(注者不详)** — 《断易天机》第 1460 行 · *Duanyi Tianji commentary (commentator unknown); Duanyi Tianji, line 1460*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 用爻若加咸池、玄武，行人主有外情牵惹，不归。 | 用爻如果加上咸池、玄武，行人在外有别的情爱牵住，不回来。 | If the Subject line has the Xianchi star and the Dark Tortoise added, the traveller has an outside affair holding him and does not return. |
| 咸池、玄武动合应爻或用爻，觅水之欢。 | 咸池、玄武发动去合应爻或用爻，是寻欢之事。 | If the Xianchi star and the Dark Tortoise move and combine with the Other line or the Subject line, it is the pleasure of seeking the water. |
| 或青龙动合世应，必娶妾。 | 或者青龙发动去合世爻和应爻，一定娶妾。 | Or if the Azure Dragon moves and combines with Self and Other, he will surely take a concubine. |

> **按**:「觅水之欢」的「水」指咸池：咸池又叫桃花，是水星，主男女私情，「觅水之欢」是在外面找私情的欢会。这一段是占出门在外的人（行人），不是占本地的婚姻；这里收的是它里面和男女私情、娶妾有关的三句，前面讲梦的话和别的行人断法没有收。「用爻」这里是行人本人所在的爻。
>
> **Note:** 'Seeking the pleasure of water' refers to the Xianchi star: Xianchi, also called the peach blossom, is a water star and rules illicit love between man and woman, and 'seeking the pleasure of water' is going outside for a liaison. This passage divines a traveller away from home and not a marriage at home; what is taken here are the three sentences in it that concern illicit love and taking a concubine, and the words before them about dreams and the other rules for judging travellers are not taken. The 'Subject line' here is the line that stands for the traveller himself.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.11 财官动合，先私而后公：财官两爻动合，先通后娶；临玄武眉目传情未通；财与旁爻合，与他人有情

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以官为丈夫、财为妻子；两爻都动而相合，一定是先私通后成婚；遇冲克，外人已经知道；临玄武，只是眉来眼去、还没有通情意；本人自占，财和世爻动而相合，也是这样；财爻与旁爻相合，是与别人有情；财遇合多、又化出子孙，一定是妓家出身，因为化子孙有从良的象。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife; if both lines move and combine, they were surely intimate in secret before they married; if a clash or control meets them, outsiders already know; on the Dark Tortoise, it is only glances and looks and feelings have not yet been exchanged; when one divines for oneself, Wealth and the Self line moving and combining is the same; if the Wealth line combines with another line, she has feelings for someone else; Wealth meeting many combinations and turning into Output means she is surely from a brothel, since turning into Output carries the image of leaving that life to marry.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.59 妻与应爻相合，外有私通：妻的身命爻与应爻相合又带咸池玄武桃花，主妻有外情；夫带日辰克妻与应爻，主捉到妻子的奸情

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻子的身命爻与应爻相合，又带咸池、玄武、桃花，主妻子在外有私情；丈夫带日辰克妻子和应爻，主捉到妻子的奸情。妻子带财生应爻，是妻子用钱引诱外人；应爻带财生妻爻，是外人用钱引诱妻子；应爻从外宫来，是远方的人，从内来，是近亲的人。世爻动带鬼隔断，是家里人阻隔；应爻动带鬼隔断，是外人阻隔他们的感情。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the wife's Body or Life line combines with the Other line and carries the Xianchi star, the Dark Tortoise and the Peach Blossom star, the wife has a lover outside; if the husband, with the Day, controls the wife and the Other line, he catches his wife in adultery. If the wife carries Wealth and generates the Other line, the wife lures the outsider with money; if the Other line carries Wealth and generates the wife's line, the outsider lures the wife with money; if the Other line comes from an outer palace the man is from far away, if from within, a near relative. If the Self line moves carrying Pressure to cut things off, family members stand in the way; if the Other line moves carrying Pressure to cut things off, outsiders block the affection.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.60 男临女子互爻，内多淫欲：男的身爻临女的命爻、女的身爻临男的命爻叫互，尊卑失序主淫乱；夫妻互相合主先奸后娶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:男子的身爻起来临到女子的命爻，或女子的身爻起来临到男子的命爻，叫做互，尊卑失了次序，主有淫乱的事；如果夫妻互相合，主先奸后娶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the man's Body line rises onto the woman's Life line, or the woman's Body line rises onto the man's Life line, this is called mutual: seniority and rank lose their order, and it means lewd conduct; if husband and wife combine with each other, it means adultery first and marriage after.

The Other line with Xianchi and the Dark Tortoise means the wife is licentious; the Self line with them, the husband. ## LY.MARRY.YY.24 妻爻带玄武咸池红艳杀加驿马动必贪淫；合应爻旁爻必与外人私通，进神动来生合淫无度，退神动来合制眼去眉来无实，合逢冲被人撞见；兄合财兄私弟妇、父合财翁妇相通、子合财义男共枕；福（子）动化鬼合财亦然；此断须带咸池红艳杀

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻爻带玄武、咸池、红艳煞，又加驿马而动，一定放纵贪淫。妻爻去合应爻旁边的爻，一定和外人私通：遇进神动来生合，淫乱没有节度；遇退神动来合住，只是眉目传情、没有实事（合又逢空也是这样）；合又逢冲，被人撞见，虽然淫乱但不泛滥。妻爻暗动、去合应爻旁的爻，要防私奔。兄弟爻合财爻，是哥哥私通弟媳；父母爻合财爻，是公公与儿媳相通；子孙爻合财爻，是义子与她同枕；福德爻动化鬼合财、财动化鬼合福德，也是这样——但这些都要带咸池、红艳煞才可以断。应爻与妻爻临兄弟爻，动化财爻来合世的，是姨妹一同的事。《黄金策》说合多而刑煞临身，女子必为娼；《涯泉摘锦》说贵人多是舞裙歌扇，合多是暗约偷期。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The wife's line carrying the Dark Tortoise, the Xianchi star and the Red Allure star, with the Post Horse added and moving: she is surely willful and lustful. If the wife's line combines with a line beside the Other line, she surely has an affair with an outsider: if an Advancing spirit moves to generate or combine with it, her licentiousness is without measure; if a Retreating spirit moves to combine with and restrain it, there are only glances and looks with no actual act (a combination meeting Void is the same); if the combination meets a clash and she is caught by someone, although licentious she is not indiscriminate. If the wife's line stirs in secret and combines with a line beside the Other line, beware of an elopement. A Peer line combining with Wealth: an elder brother has an affair with his younger brother's wife; the Resource line combining with Wealth: a father-in-law and daughter-in-law are involved; Output combining with Wealth: an adopted son shares a pillow with her; Output moving, changing into Pressure and combining with Wealth, or Wealth moving, changing into Pressure and combining with Output, is the same — but all this may be judged only where the Xianchi and Red Allure stars are carried. The Other line and the wife's line on a Peer line, moving and changing into Wealth to combine with the Self line: the wife's younger sister is involved too. The Huangjin Ce says that many combinations with punishment and baleful stars on the Body make the woman a prostitute; the Yaquan Zhaijin says that many Noblemen mean entertainers and many combinations mean secret trysts.
