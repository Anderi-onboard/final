# 六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Marriage and relationships · Infidelity and improper relations*

2 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.MARRY.HJC.11 财官动合，先私而后公：财官两爻动合，先通后娶；临玄武眉目传情未通；财与旁爻合，与他人有情

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以官为丈夫、财为妻子；两爻都动而相合，一定是先私通后成婚；遇冲克，外人已经知道；临玄武，只是眉来眼去、还没有通情意；本人自占，财和世爻动而相合，也是这样；财爻与旁爻相合，是与别人有情；财遇合多、又化出子孙，一定是妓家出身，因为化子孙有从良的象。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife; if both lines move and combine, they were surely intimate in secret before they married; if a clash or control meets them, outsiders already know; on the Dark Tortoise, it is only glances and looks and feelings have not yet been exchanged; when one divines for oneself, Wealth and the Self line moving and combining is the same; if the Wealth line combines with another line, she has feelings for someone else; Wealth meeting many combinations and turning into Output means she is surely from a brothel, since turning into Output carries the image of leaving that life to marry.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6343 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6343*

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2006 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2006*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 夫占以财为妇，世与动合，是必先通而后娶，财与世爻动合亦然。 | 男子占，以财为妻子，世和它动而相合，一定是先私通后娶，财和世爻动而相合也是这样。 | A man divining takes Wealth as the wife; if the Self combines with it by moving, they were intimate in secret before marrying, and the same if Wealth and the Self line combine by moving. |

> **按**:旧注把「财官」读作男女两方（官为夫，财为妇）；这一条只对男占女、女占男这一类占法成立，用神照占者身份定，见 HJC.45。
>
> **Note:** The old commentary reads 'Wealth and Pressure' as the two parties (Pressure the husband, Wealth the wife); this holds only for the ordinary man-divining-woman or woman-divining-man question, and the Subject is fixed by who divines, as HJC.45 says.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.21 父合财爻，异日有新台之行；世临妻位，他时无就养之心：财父二爻带玄武动合，有翁淫子媳之事；财临世身，妇不善事公婆

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚遇到财、父两爻，世带玄武，动而相合的，日后有公公与儿媳私通的事；如果财临世身，那个妇人一定不善于侍奉公婆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining marriage, when the Wealth and Resource lines with the Self carrying the Dark Tortoise move and combine, there will later be an affair between the father-in-law and his son's wife; if Wealth sits on the Self or the Body, the woman will surely not serve her parents-in-law well.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6379 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6379*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父合财爻，异日有新台之行； | 父爻合财爻，日后有像「新台」那样的丑行； | The Resource line combining with the Wealth line: later there will be conduct like that of the New Terrace; |
| 世临妻位，他时无就养之心。 | 世临妻位，将来没有就养（奉养公婆）的心。 | the Self sitting on the wife's place: in time she will have no heart to support her parents-in-law. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6380 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6380*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占婚遇财父二爻，世带玄武，动来相合者，异日有翁淫子媳之事。 | 占婚遇到财、父两爻，世带玄武，动而相合的，日后有公公与儿媳私通的事。 | In divining marriage, when the Wealth and Resource lines with the Self carrying the Dark Tortoise move and combine, there will later be an affair between the father-in-law and his son's wife. |
| 若财临世身，其妇必不善事翁姑。 | 如果财临世身，那个妇人一定不善于侍奉公婆。 | If Wealth sits on the Self or the Body, the woman will surely not serve her parents-in-law well. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2028 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2028*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占婚遇财父二爻带玄武动合者，有翁淫子媳之事； | 占婚遇到财、父两爻带玄武动而相合的，有公公与儿媳私通的事； | In divining marriage, Wealth and Resource carrying the Dark Tortoise, moving and combining, mean an affair between the father-in-law and his son's wife; |
| 若财临世身，玄武不动合者，其妇必不善事公姑。 | 如果财临世身，玄武不动而相合的，那个妇人一定不善于侍奉公婆。 | if Wealth sits on the Self or the Body and the Dark Tortoise combines without moving, the woman will surely not serve her parents-in-law well. |

> **按**:「新台」出自《诗经·邶风·新台》，讲卫宣公在河上筑新台，夺了儿子的新娘；旧注把它直接解作「翁淫子媳」。
>
> **Note:** 'The New Terrace' is from the Book of Odes (Bei Feng, 'Xin Tai'), about Duke Xuan of Wei building a new terrace on the river and seizing his son's bride; the old commentary reads it directly as a father-in-law's affair with his son's wife.

- 《黄金策》:同文 第 781 行:父合财爻，异日有新台之行;同文 第 781 行:世临妻位，他时无就养之心
