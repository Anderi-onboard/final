## LY.MARRY.HJC.17 若在一宫，当有通家之好；若加三合，曾叨会面之亲：世应生合比和、财鬼同宫是亲上亲；带三合则曾见过面

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世应相生相合或比和，财、鬼又在同一宫，是亲上加亲；不带三合，虽是亲戚也不相识；带三合，一定是见过面的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self and Other generating, combining or in harmony, with Wealth and Pressure also in one palace, is kin upon kin; without a three-harmony frame they are relatives who do not know each other, and with one they have surely met.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.22 空鬼伏财，必是望门之寡妇；动财值虎，定然带服之婆娘：财伏空鬼之下，其女先曾受聘、未婚夫死；加白虎动，是已嫁夫亡带孝

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里有官、财两爻，而财爻伏在空亡的鬼爻下，那个女子先前受过聘，未婚夫死了，俗称望门寡；再加白虎发动来克，就是嫁了以后丈夫死、带着孝服的；如果伏在不空的鬼下，是有丈夫的妇人，被日辰、动爻提起而刑克世爻，日后要防争讼。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the hexagram has both Pressure and Wealth lines and the Wealth line is hidden under a Void Pressure line, the woman was once betrothed and her fiancé died, commonly called a widow at the door; with the White Tiger moving and controlling as well, she married and her husband died and she wears mourning; if she is hidden under a Pressure line that is not Void, she is a woman with a husband, and if the Day or a moving line raises her and it punishes or controls the Self line, guard later against a lawsuit.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.25 夫若才能，官位占长生之地；妻如丑拙，财爻落墓库之乡：男女性情容貌看财鬼二爻，月令旺身肥、日时旺貌美

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:想知男女的性情容貌，看财、鬼两爻：月令旺身体胖，日时旺相貌美；月令衰身体瘦，日时衰相貌丑；月令旺而日时衰，身胖貌不扬；月令衰而日时旺，身瘦貌美；衰而有扶，丑却有才能；旺而入墓，虽美而愚拙；只看性情，男看鬼，女看财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know the temperament and looks of the man or woman, look at the Wealth and Pressure lines: thriving in the month means a stout body, thriving on the day and hour means good looks; weak in the month means a thin body, weak on the day and hour means plain looks; thriving in the month but weak on the day and hour, a stout body and unremarkable looks; weak in the month but thriving on the day and hour, a thin body and handsome looks; weak but supported, plain but capable; thriving but entering the tomb, handsome but dull; for temperament alone, look at Pressure for a man and Wealth for a woman.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.26 命旺则荣华可拟，时衰则发达难期：本命爻生旺有气则荣华，休囚死绝则无发达；本命爻所临六亲六神各主一种性好

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:本命爻生旺有气，日后有荣华；休囚死绝，没有发达的日子。财旺命衰，妻子相貌虽美命却平常；鬼衰命旺，丈夫虽愚蠢衣食丰足。命临父母好技艺，加青龙好诗书，临兄好赌博、爱花钱、不好学，临官喜迎官府，临财福善于持家。王洪绪反对拿四季论旺衰，主张看本命爻是否临财福、青龙、贵人，或得日辰动爻生扶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the natal line is born or thriving and has qi, glory lies ahead; when it is weak, dead or cut off, there is no rise. With Wealth thriving and the natal line weak, the wife is handsome but her fate is ordinary; with Pressure weak and the natal line thriving, the husband is dull but well fed and clothed. The natal line on Resource means a liking for skills, with the Azure Dragon added for poetry and books, on Peer for gambling, spending and no love of study, on Pressure a welcome to officials, on Wealth or Output a good housekeeper. Wang Hongxu objects to judging thriving and weakness by the four seasons and looks instead at whether the natal line sits on Wealth, Output, the Azure Dragon or a noble, or is generated and supported by the Day or a moving line.

## LY.MARRY.YY.26 妻为闺女为再醮：卦中一财不见鬼是闺女；财鬼相合或财下伏鬼已见一夫，一财二鬼相合或财下伏鬼又化鬼已见二夫；二鬼一空一旺必再醮，二鬼旺而财被刑冲克害生离改嫁；《黄金策》妻克世身重合应妻必重婚；床帐香闺法

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask whether the wife is a maiden or has married before: a hexagram with a single Wealth line and no Pressure means a maiden. If Wealth and Pressure combine, or Pressure is hidden beneath Wealth, she has already had one husband; one Wealth line combining with two Pressure lines, or Pressure hidden beneath Wealth and again changing into Pressure, means two husbands. A hexagram with two Pressure lines, one Void and one strong, means she has surely married again; two strong Pressure lines while the Day, Month and moving lines punish, clash, control or harm Wealth mean parting alive and remarriage. The Huangjin Ce says: the wife's line controlling the Self (Body) and doubly combining with the Other line, the wife will surely marry twice; the Self, Other and wife's lines forming a three-harmony, she takes both a secondary and a principal husband; many Pressure lines with no ill omens touching them, the woman should remarry. The Mingrui manuscript says: a man takes the line the hexagram Body generates as the bed-curtains, a woman takes the line the Body controls as the fragrant chamber. If the fragrant chamber is in a tomb or extinct, the match is not yet made; if the bed-curtains are Void, he has not yet taken a wife. This method is wonderfully accurate; do not pass it to the unworthy.

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问妻子的富贵贫贱：还没娶，按她父亲那边断；出嫁了，按丈夫这边断。没娶的时候，只看外卦本宫的父母爻；外卦的父母爻不出现，就看伏爻；不现又不伏，就把生妻爻的那个爻当妻母，克妻母的爻当岳父，和真的父母一样分阴阳、真假来断。妻父妻母爻带禄、马、贵人，是官宦人家的女儿；带财、禄、德、福、生气，是富家女；带咸池、沐浴、玄武和休囚死气，是贫贱人家的女儿；带白虎和贵人，武职的女儿；带白虎、刃、劫杀，军人工匠的女儿；带勾陈、土旺相，农家女；带朱雀、火、财旺，牙人的女儿；带玄武、红艳，行为放荡人家的女儿；带刑害亡劫又无气，低贱人的女儿；再加金、白虎、刑、刃，是不务农而追逐末业者的女儿。螣蛇逢生旺，是九流人的女儿；螣蛇逢冲并、墓、合，是有艺术技能的人的女儿，按五行定是哪一种技艺。衰败就贫，空绝就断绝，生合世爻能得她家的荫庇，刑害冲克世爻就被她家欺凌。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask the wealth, rank, poverty or lowliness of the wife: before the marriage, judge from her father's side; once married, from the husband's. Before the marriage, look only at the Resource line of the home palace in the outer trigram; if it does not appear, look at the hidden line; if it neither appears nor is hidden, take the line that generates the wife's line as the wife's mother and the line that controls the wife's mother as the father-in-law, and judge them like real parents by dividing yin and yang, true and false. The wife's father and mother lines carrying Emolument, the Horse and the Nobleman: a daughter of an official family; carrying Wealth, Emolument, Virtue, Fortune and the Birth-breath: a rich family; carrying the Xianchi star, the Bathing stage, the Dark Tortoise and the breaths of rest, trap and death: a poor and lowly family; the White Tiger and the Nobleman: a military officer's daughter; the White Tiger, the blade and the Robbery star: a soldier's or artisan's; Hook Earth with strong Earth: a farmer's; the Vermilion Bird and Fire with strong Wealth: a broker's; the Dark Tortoise and the Red Allure star: a licentious household's; punishment, harm, the Loss and Robbery stars without breath: a low person's; with Metal, the White Tiger, punishment and blade added: one who does not farm but pursues the lesser trades. The Coiling Snake in birth or strength: a daughter of the nine schools; the Coiling Snake meeting clash, merging, tomb or combination: a craftsman's or artist's, the Five Phases deciding which craft. Weak and ruined means poor; Void or extinct means cut off; generating or combining with the Self line, one receives their protection; punishing, harming, clashing with or controlling the Self line, one is bullied by them.

## LY.MARRY.YY.30 归藏易「妻临克位号重财」：财爻所带六神克它所在的宫（震宫带白虎、坤艮带青龙、乾兑带雀、离带武、坎带勾蛇）叫重财；四直贵马德合聚一爻旺相居二五爻得太岁扶无刑破必为驸马仪宾；出嫁后妇人贵贱贫富从夫同世爻断

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:《归藏易》说：妻爻临在克位，叫重财——像金爻进入震宫；贵人、煞会合又正旺相，因妻子而受禄，可比三公。比如震宫的财爻带白虎，白虎属金又克震木，所以叫重财；坤、艮宫的财爻带青龙，青龙属木又克坤艮土，也是重财；乾、兑宫的财爻带朱雀，离宫的财爻带玄武，坎宫的财爻带勾陈、螣蛇，都是。如果四直、贵人、驿马、德神、合聚在同一个爻上，又旺相居二爻或五爻，再得太岁扶持，没有刑破，一定是驸马、仪宾（公主、郡主的丈夫）。出嫁以后，妇人的贵贱贫富跟从丈夫，都同世爻一样来断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Guicang Yi says: the wife's line on a controlling position is called doubled wealth, like a Metal line entering Zhen; with the Nobleman and baleful stars meeting and in strength, one receives emolument through the wife, comparable to the Three Ministers. For example, the Wealth line in the Zhen palace carrying the White Tiger: the White Tiger is Metal and controls Zhen's Wood, so it is called doubled wealth; the Wealth line in the Kun or Gen palace carrying the Azure Dragon: the Azure Dragon is Wood and controls the Earth of Kun and Gen, so this too is doubled wealth; in the Qian or Dui palace with the Vermilion Bird, in the Li palace with the Dark Tortoise, in the Kan palace with Hook Earth or the Coiling Snake: all are so. If the four rulers, the Nobleman, the Post Horse, Virtue and combination gather on one line, strong and on the second or fifth line, and helped by Tai Sui, without punishment or break, he is surely an imperial son-in-law or a consort of a princess. After she marries, the woman's rank and wealth follow her husband and are all judged like the Self line.

## LY.MARRY.YY.31 妻家远近、娶妻迟早、妻年长幼：财持世或出现住近娶早，伏藏住远娶迟；财与世同在一卦近亲女，被月日动爻变爻隔断外郡女；八卦定方二十八宿定郡；财在二爻坐宅必养媳妇否亦邻女；财合世身命娶必早；妻阳世阴妻年长、世阳妻阴妻年幼；妻居辰戌丑未长女、子午卯酉中女、寅申巳亥少女

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问妻家远近、娶妻早晚、妻子年长年幼：妻财爻持世或出现，一定住得近、娶得早；伏藏不现，住得远、娶得迟。财爻和世爻同在一个卦，是近亲的女儿；财爻和世爻被月日、动爻、变爻隔断的，一定是外郡人的女儿——比如乾卦戌世、寅财，而申、午、辰爻动，或月日并动申、午、辰爻，都是隔断。用八卦定方位，用二十八宿定是哪个郡；卦空爻空，就用财爻的长生方位来定。财爻在二爻（二爻是宅爻）叫坐宅，必是养媳妇，不然也是紧邻的女子。财爻合世爻、身爻、命爻的，娶妻一定早。妻爻属阳、世爻属阴，妻子年长；世爻属阳、妻爻属阴，妻子年幼。妻爻在辰、戌、丑、未是长女，在子、午、卯、酉是中女，在寅、申、巳、亥是少女。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask how far the wife's family lives, how soon he will marry and whether the wife is older or younger: if the Wealth line holds the Self line or appears, she surely lives near and the marriage comes early; if it is hidden and does not appear, she lives far and the marriage comes late. Wealth and the Self line in the same trigram: a daughter of a close relative; cut off from each other by the Month, Day, moving or changed lines: surely a daughter of someone from another prefecture (for example in Qian with the Self on Xu and Wealth on Yin, a moving Shen, Wu or Chen line, or the Month and Day together moving on Shen, Wu or Chen, all cut them off). Use the eight trigrams to fix the direction and the twenty-eight lodges to fix the prefecture; if the hexagram or line is Void, fix it by the direction of the Wealth line's Birth stage. Wealth on the second line (the house line) is called sitting in the house: surely a child bride raised in the family, or else a girl from next door. Wealth combining with the Self, Body or fate line: he marries early. A yang wife's line with a yin Self line: the wife is older; a yang Self line with a yin wife's line: the wife is younger. The wife's line on Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei: the eldest daughter; on Zi, Wu, Mao or You: a middle daughter; on Yin, Shen, Si or Hai: the youngest daughter.

## LY.MARRY.YY.32 占女婿：以本宫出现的官鬼爻为主（不现看伏神，再不现取克女之爻）；本宫官鬼为正婿、他宫出者为旁婿；鬼伏父下伶俐尊重、伏子下性善、伏兄下贪淫好赌、伏财下能掌财帛；鬼化鬼男家未定、兄化鬼斗狠贪淫、财化鬼克妻损财、子化鬼夫妇和谐、鬼化子与妻相益、鬼化兄伤妻嫖赌、鬼化财能设施

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占女婿：以本宫里出现的官鬼爻为主，不论内卦外卦（因为女婿是半个儿子，有家里人的意思）；不出现，就看本宫的伏神；不现又无伏，就取克女儿的那个爻当女婿。本宫的官鬼是正婿，年月日时上别的宫出来的是旁婿；正宫有两个鬼爻，阳爻得位的是正婿，阴爻失位的是旁婿。正婿的鬼爻在巳、午，火命的吉，女儿命属土的吉（火能生土）。鬼爻伏在父母爻下，伶俐庄重；伏在子孙爻下，性善不损物，能容纳；伏在兄弟爻下，贪淫好赌，不诚实；伏在财爻下，能管钱财，做事分明，夫妻好合（财鬼带辰午酉亥自刑的，主先私通后娶）。鬼化鬼，男家没定准，或停妻再娶；兄化鬼，好斗凶狠贪淫（鬼带刑刃也是）；财化鬼，克妻损财；子化鬼，带青龙、喜神、德神、合，夫妇和谐，带华盖、刑、刃，是僧道还俗；鬼化子，与妻互相有益；鬼化兄，伤妻、嫖赌；鬼化财，能有设施。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Divining a son-in-law: take the Pressure line that appears in the home palace as the main one, whether in the inner or outer trigram (since a son-in-law is a half-son, with the sense of a member of the household); if it does not appear, look at the home palace's hidden spirit; if it neither appears nor is hidden, take the line that controls the daughter as the son-in-law. The home palace's Pressure is the principal son-in-law, and one that comes from another palace through the year, month, day or hour is a collateral one; if the home palace has two Pressure lines, the yang line in its place is the principal son-in-law and the yin line out of place the collateral one. If the principal Pressure line is on Si or Wu, a man of the Fire fate is auspicious and a daughter whose fate is Earth is auspicious (Fire can generate Earth). Pressure hidden beneath the Resource line: clever and dignified; beneath Output: good-natured, does not waste goods, tolerant; beneath a Peer line: lustful, gambling and dishonest; beneath Wealth: able to manage money, clear in affairs, the couple well matched (if Wealth and Pressure carry the self-punishing branches Chen, Wu, You or Hai: illicit relations first, marriage after). Pressure changing into Pressure: the man's family is unsettled, or he sets aside his wife and marries again; a Peer line changing into Pressure: quarrelsome, fierce and lustful (Pressure carrying punishment and blade is the same); Wealth changing into Pressure: harms the wife and loses money; Output changing into Pressure, with the Azure Dragon, Joy, Virtue and combination: the couple is harmonious, with the Canopy star, punishment and blade: a monk or priest returned to lay life; Pressure changing into Output: mutually beneficial with his wife; into a Peer line: harms his wife, whores and gambles; into Wealth: able to make arrangements.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.03 《黄金策》《易冒》既以应爻为妻，又以应爻为女家父母：两说并用，贤良之女都出自富贵之家，荒谬

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:觉子驳一种推法：把应爻当作妻，要它旺相才是贤良的女子；同时又把应爻当作女家的父母，旺相就是富贵人家、休囚就是贫寒人家。两个说法一起用，就推出「贤良的女子都出自富贵人家」，说不通——一个爻不能同时担当两个角色。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Juezi attacks a way of reading in which the Other line is both the wife (she must be strong to be a virtuous woman) and the woman's parents (strong means a rich family, resting means a poor one). Used together, the two readings lead to 'every virtuous woman comes from a rich family', which is absurd: one line cannot carry two roles at once.

**觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 11576 行 · *Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 11576*

## LY.MARRY.ZS.22 占婚不要求容貌才情十全：官占长生、财落墓库近理，以八宫推容貌、六神定才情不可；用财官兼世应断足矣

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:李我平批评古法占婚多浮辞：《黄金策》说的「丈夫有才能，官鬼占长生的位置；妻子丑拙，财爻落在墓库」还算近理；《易冒》用八宫推容貌、用六神定才情就不行——同一卦，按八宫读是端正聪明的淑女，按六神读财爻临白虎又成了悖逆之星，两种读法彼此打架。婚姻有一定的缘分，财旺官兴，就会夫唱妇随；相生相合，自然有讨人喜欢的容貌；吉的就求，凶的就弃，用财、官兼看世、应来断就够了。要是事事求全——命不入墓、六亲不相妨、父母不沉、子孙不溺、财不动、官不没、夫妇不二、阴阳不悖——世上没有这样的女子，六十四卦里也没有这样的卦。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Li Woping criticizes the old methods of divining marriage as full of showy words: what the Huangjin Ce says, that a husband with ability has the Pressure line in the position of Birth and an ugly, clumsy wife has the Wealth line in the tomb or storehouse, is still close to reason; but the Yi Mao inferring looks from the eight palaces and settling talent by the six spirits will not do, since the same hexagram read by palace gives an upright and clever virtuous woman, and read by spirit gives Wealth with the White Tiger, a star of perversity, so the two readings contradict each other. Marriage has its appointed bond: with Wealth strong and Pressure flourishing, husband leads and wife follows; where they generate and combine she will naturally have looks that please; seek what is auspicious and abandon what is ill, and judging by Wealth and Pressure together with the Self and Other lines is enough. If one demands perfection in everything (fates not entering a tomb, six kin not harming each other, parents not sinking, children not lost, Wealth not moving, Pressure not lost, no second spouse, yin and yang not at odds), there is no such woman in the world and no such hexagram among the sixty-four.
