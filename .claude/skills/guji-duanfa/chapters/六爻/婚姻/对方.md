## LY.MARRY.HJC.17 若在一宫，当有通家之好；若加三合，曾叨会面之亲：世应生合比和、财鬼同宫是亲上亲；带三合则曾见过面

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世应相生相合或比和，财、鬼又在同一宫，是亲上加亲；不带三合，虽是亲戚也不相识；带三合，一定是见过面的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self and Other generating, combining or in harmony, with Wealth and Pressure also in one palace, is kin upon kin; without a three-harmony frame they are relatives who do not know each other, and with one they have surely met.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.22 空鬼伏财，必是望门之寡妇；动财值虎，定然带服之婆娘：财伏空鬼之下，其女先曾受聘、未婚夫死；加白虎动，是已嫁夫亡带孝

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里有官、财两爻，而财爻伏在空亡的鬼爻下，那个女子先前受过聘，未婚夫死了，俗称望门寡；再加白虎发动来克，就是嫁了以后丈夫死、带着孝服的；如果伏在不空的鬼下，是有丈夫的妇人，被日辰、动爻提起而刑克世爻，日后要防争讼。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the hexagram has both Pressure and Wealth lines and the Wealth line is hidden under a Void Pressure line, the woman was once betrothed and her fiancé died, commonly called a widow at the door; with the White Tiger moving and controlling as well, she married and her husband died and she wears mourning; if she is hidden under a Pressure line that is not Void, she is a woman with a husband, and if the Day or a moving line raises her and it punishes or controls the Self line, guard later against a lawsuit.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.25 夫若才能，官位占长生之地；妻如丑拙，财爻落墓库之乡：男女性情容貌看财鬼二爻，月令旺身肥、日时旺貌美

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:想知男女的性情容貌，看财、鬼两爻：月令旺身体胖，日时旺相貌美；月令衰身体瘦，日时衰相貌丑；月令旺而日时衰，身胖貌不扬；月令衰而日时旺，身瘦貌美；衰而有扶，丑却有才能；旺而入墓，虽美而愚拙；只看性情，男看鬼，女看财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know the temperament and looks of the man or woman, look at the Wealth and Pressure lines: thriving in the month means a stout body, thriving on the day and hour means good looks; weak in the month means a thin body, weak on the day and hour means plain looks; thriving in the month but weak on the day and hour, a stout body and unremarkable looks; weak in the month but thriving on the day and hour, a thin body and handsome looks; weak but supported, plain but capable; thriving but entering the tomb, handsome but dull; for temperament alone, look at Pressure for a man and Wealth for a woman.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.26 命旺则荣华可拟，时衰则发达难期：本命爻生旺有气则荣华，休囚死绝则无发达；本命爻所临六亲六神各主一种性好

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:本命爻生旺有气，日后有荣华；休囚死绝，没有发达的日子。财旺命衰，妻子相貌虽美命却平常；鬼衰命旺，丈夫虽愚蠢衣食丰足。命临父母好技艺，加青龙好诗书，临兄好赌博、爱花钱、不好学，临官喜迎官府，临财福善于持家。王洪绪反对拿四季论旺衰，主张看本命爻是否临财福、青龙、贵人，或得日辰动爻生扶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the natal line is born or thriving and has qi, glory lies ahead; when it is weak, dead or cut off, there is no rise. With Wealth thriving and the natal line weak, the wife is handsome but her fate is ordinary; with Pressure weak and the natal line thriving, the husband is dull but well fed and clothed. The natal line on Resource means a liking for skills, with the Azure Dragon added for poetry and books, on Peer for gambling, spending and no love of study, on Pressure a welcome to officials, on Wealth or Output a good housekeeper. Wang Hongxu objects to judging thriving and weakness by the four seasons and looks instead at whether the natal line sits on Wealth, Output, the Azure Dragon or a noble, or is generated and supported by the Day or a moving line.

| 如命临父母主好技艺，若加青龙主好诗礼，临兄弟则爱赌好贱，临财福必善作家，临官鬼带凶神，主疾病官刑，不加凶神乃公门人役，带贵人则贵。 | 命临父母主好技艺，如果加青龙主好诗书，临兄弟则爱赌好贱，临财福必善持家，临官鬼带凶神主疾病官刑，不加凶神则是公门人役，带贵人则贵。 | The natal line on Resource means a liking for skills, with the Azure Dragon a liking for poetry and rites, on Peer a love of gambling and cheapness, on Wealth or Output surely a good housekeeper, on Pressure with ominous spirits illness and official punishment, without ominous spirits a runner in a government office, and with a noble, high rank. |
| 学者宜以类推。 | 学的人应当类推。 | Learners should reason by analogy. |

> **打架**:旺衰怎么论：旧注按月令、日时（四季）；王洪绪明说古法按四季论旺衰是错的，改看本命爻临什么六亲六神、有没有日辰动爻生扶；两家在这一条上直接不同。
>
> **Where they disagree:** How to judge thriving and weakness: the old commentary goes by the month and by the day and hour (the seasons); Wang Hongxu says outright that the old method of judging by the four seasons is wrong, and looks instead at what relation and spirit the natal line sits on and whether the Day or a moving line supports it; the two differ directly here.

- 《黄金策》:同文 第 796 行:命旺则荣华可拟，时衰则发达难期

## LY.MARRY.HJC.37 论贫富，当究身命；决美恶，可验性情：问妇看财爻、问夫看鬼爻、问男家看世爻、问女家看应爻；应旺财衰，女家富而女貌不扬

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要看男女的贫富美恶：问妇看财爻，问夫的身看鬼爻，问男家看世爻，问女家看应爻；应旺而财衰，是女家虽富，女子相貌不出众；维持和拆台的旁人，用刑冲生合的爻来推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To judge the wealth, poverty, looks and character of the man or woman: to ask about the bride look at the Wealth line, about the bridegroom look at the Pressure line, about the man's family the Self line, about the woman's family the Other line; the Other thriving and Wealth weak means the woman's family is rich but her looks are unremarkable; the bystanders who support or wreck it are judged by the lines that punish, clash, generate or combine.
