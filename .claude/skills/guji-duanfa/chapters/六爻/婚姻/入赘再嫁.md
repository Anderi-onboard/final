## LY.MARRY.HJC.05 阴而阳阳而阴，偏利牵丝之举：世官宜阳反阴、应财宜阴反阳，娶妇多不利，惟入赘吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世和官本宜阳却变成阴，应和财本宜阴却变成阳，这样的卦，占娶妻多半不利，只有入赘（男子嫁入女家）才吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Self and Pressure, which should be yang, turn out yin, and the Other and Wealth, which should be yin, turn out yang, then in divining the taking of a wife it is mostly unfavourable, and only marrying into the bride's family is auspicious.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6321 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6321*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 阴而阳阳而阴，偏利牵丝之举； | 阴变阳、阳变阴，偏偏利于「牵丝」的事； | Yin turning to yang and yang to yin favour only the 'drawing of the thread'; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6322 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6322*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 世与官，宜阳反阴； | 世和官，本宜阳，却反成了阴； | The Self and Pressure should be yang but have become yin; |
| 应与财，宜阴反阳。 | 应和财，本宜阴，却反成了阳。 | the Other and Wealth should be yin but have become yang. |
| 占娶妇多不利。 | 占娶妻，大多不利。 | In divining the taking of a wife it is mostly unfavourable. |
| 惟入赘则吉。 | 只有入赘就吉。 | Only marrying into the bride's family is auspicious. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1994 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1994*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 世与用宜阳反阴，应与财宜阴反阳，占娶妻多为不利，唯入赘最吉。 | 世和用神本宜阳却反成阴，应和财本宜阴却反成阳，占娶妻多半不利，只有入赘最吉。 | The Self and the Subject should be yang but are yin, and the Other and Wealth should be yin but are yang; in divining the taking of a wife it is mostly unfavourable, and only marrying into the bride's family is best. |

> **按**:「牵丝」字面是牵红线成亲；旧注和王洪绪的解释都落在「惟入赘则吉」上，本库照此。
>
> **Note:** 'Drawing the thread' literally means drawing the red thread of a match; both the old commentary and Wang Hongxu resolve it into 'only marrying into the bride's family is auspicious', and this library follows that.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.24 财官叠见，重为一度之新人：男占女卦有两财、女占男卦有两鬼，是再娶再嫁

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:男占女，卦里有两个财；女占男，卦里有两个鬼；一定是再娶再嫁。两个财都没有损伤，一正一偏；一个旺一个空，前妻已死；两鬼发动，有两家争娶；一旺一空，前夫已死；鬼伏财下，男方家里有妻；财伏鬼下，女方有夫；鬼不空而被动爻日辰冲克妻财，是生离改嫁；世克鬼而动爻日辰生合妻财，是逐婿嫁女。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A man divining about a woman with two Wealth lines in the hexagram, or a woman divining about a man with two Pressure lines, means a remarriage; two Wealth lines both unharmed are one principal and one secondary; one thriving and one Void means the former wife has died; two Pressure lines moving means two families contending to marry her; one thriving and one Void, the former husband has died; Pressure hidden under Wealth means the man has a wife at home; Wealth hidden under Pressure means the woman has a husband; Pressure not Void while a moving line or the Day clashes with or controls Wealth means a living separation and remarriage; the Self controlling Pressure while a moving line or the Day generates or combines with Wealth means driving out the son-in-law and marrying off the daughter.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6388 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6388*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 财官叠见，重为一度之新人。 | 财、官两爻重叠出现，是再做一次新人。 | Wealth and Pressure appearing twice over: to be a new bride or groom a second time. |

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 男占女卦有两财，女占男卦有两鬼，必是再娶再嫁，重为一度新人。 | 男占女，卦里有两个财；女占男，卦里有两个鬼；一定是再娶再嫁，再做一次新人。 | A man divining about a woman with two Wealth lines, or a woman divining about a man with two Pressure lines, surely means a remarriage, to be a new bride or groom a second time. |
| 两鬼发动，必有两家争娶； | 两鬼发动，一定有两家争娶； | Two Pressure lines moving mean two families contending to marry her; |

> **按**:这一条的前提是「男占女看财、女占男看鬼」；另一方向的伏（鬼伏财下、财伏鬼下），旧注和王洪绪的说法正好相反：旧注是鬼伏财下男有妻、财伏鬼下女有夫，王洪绪是财伏鬼下男有妻、鬼伏财下女有夫。
>
> **Note:** The premise is 'a man divining about a woman looks to Wealth, a woman divining about a man looks to Pressure'. On the direction of hiding, the old commentary and Wang Hongxu are opposite: the old commentary says Pressure hidden under Wealth means the man has a wife and Wealth hidden under Pressure means the woman has a husband, while Wang Hongxu says Wealth hidden under Pressure means the man has a wife and Pressure hidden under Wealth means the woman has a husband.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.19 纳宠（娶妾）：为子嗣的用子孙爻，同占子嗣；否则专看财爻，同占婚

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:娶妾：为了子嗣的，用子孙爻做用神，和前面占子嗣一样断；不是为了子嗣的，专看财爻，和占婚一样断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Taking a concubine: if it is for the sake of an heir, take the Output line as the Subject and judge as in the earlier divination on children; otherwise look at the Wealth line alone and judge as in a marriage divination.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 11807 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 11807*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 为子嗣者，以子孙为用神，与前占子嗣同断，否则专看财爻，与占婚同断。 | 为了子嗣的，用子孙爻做用神，和前面占子嗣一样断，不然就专看财爻，和占婚一样断。 | If it is for the sake of an heir, take the Output line as the Subject and judge as in the earlier divination on children; otherwise look at the Wealth line alone and judge as in a marriage divination. |

> **按**:这一节后面的「乾按」是后人加的，不引。

## LY.MARRY.ZS.20 配仆：财爻为用；财爻持世或动而生合世、化进化生、财化财，得忠诚出力之人；散破绝空非懒即病；克世冲世忘恩负义；化鬼化绝化墓化克及鬼化财，贫穷夭折

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:配仆（安排仆人）：以财爻为用神。财爻持世，或者动了来生合世爻，或者动而化进神、化生，或者财化财，都会得到忠诚肯出力的人；财爻散、破、绝、空，不是懒就是病；财爻克世、冲世，是忘恩负义的人；财爻化成鬼、绝、墓、克，以及鬼爻化成财爻，是贫穷短命之类的人。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Taking on a servant: the Wealth line is the Subject. With the Wealth line on the Self line, or moving to generate or combine with the Self line, or moving and changing into an Advancing spirit or a generator, or Wealth changing into Wealth, you gain someone loyal who serves with effort; if the Wealth line is scattered, broken, extinguished or Void, the person is either lazy or ill; if the Wealth line controls or clashes with the Self line, the person betrays kindness and forgets favors; if Wealth changes into Pressure, extinction, tomb or a controller, or Pressure changes into Wealth, the person is of the poor and short-lived sort.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 11812 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 11812*

> **按**:「配仆」原是婚姻章里的一小节（安排、聘用仆人），原书说它和婚姻看法一样（那句「乾按」是后人加的，不引）。收在这里是因为它排在婚姻章里，不是因为它是婚姻。
