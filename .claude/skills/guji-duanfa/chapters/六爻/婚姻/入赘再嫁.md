## LY.MARRY.HJC.05 阴而阳阳而阴，偏利牵丝之举：世官宜阳反阴、应财宜阴反阳，娶妇多不利，惟入赘吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世和官本宜阳却变成阴，应和财本宜阴却变成阳，这样的卦，占娶妻多半不利，只有入赘（男子嫁入女家）才吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Self and Pressure, which should be yang, turn out yin, and the Other and Wealth, which should be yin, turn out yang, then in divining the taking of a wife it is mostly unfavourable, and only marrying into the bride's family is auspicious.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.24 财官叠见，重为一度之新人：男占女卦有两财、女占男卦有两鬼，是再娶再嫁

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:男占女，卦里有两个财；女占男，卦里有两个鬼；一定是再娶再嫁。两个财都没有损伤，一正一偏；一个旺一个空，前妻已死；两鬼发动，有两家争娶；一旺一空，前夫已死；鬼伏财下，男方家里有妻；财伏鬼下，女方有夫；鬼不空而被动爻日辰冲克妻财，是生离改嫁；世克鬼而动爻日辰生合妻财，是逐婿嫁女。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A man divining about a woman with two Wealth lines in the hexagram, or a woman divining about a man with two Pressure lines, means a remarriage; two Wealth lines both unharmed are one principal and one secondary; one thriving and one Void means the former wife has died; two Pressure lines moving means two families contending to marry her; one thriving and one Void, the former husband has died; Pressure hidden under Wealth means the man has a wife at home; Wealth hidden under Pressure means the woman has a husband; Pressure not Void while a moving line or the Day clashes with or controls Wealth means a living separation and remarriage; the Self controlling Pressure while a moving line or the Day generates or combines with Wealth means driving out the son-in-law and marrying off the daughter.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6388 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6388*

On the direction of hiding, the old commentary and Wang Hongxu are opposite: the old commentary says Pressure hidden under Wealth means the man has a wife and Wealth hidden under Pressure means the woman has a husband, while Wang Hongxu says Wealth hidden under Pressure means the man has a wife and Pressure hidden under Wealth means the woman has a husband.

## LY.MARRY.HZL.07 占婢妾专以财爻为主象：财旺吉；动出官主病讼、动出兄主口舌；兄爻合财主外情；财化子善、化官带疾、化兄淫荡、化父老成；定妇人女子看财福二爻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婢妾：专以财爻为主要的象。财爻旺相就吉；动出官鬼爻，主生病、招官司；动出兄弟爻，主口舌。兄弟爻和财爻相合而合住，主有不正当的外情。有别的爻和财爻三刑六害，一定因此打官司。财化子孙，性善；财化官鬼，带病；财化兄弟，淫荡不良；财化父母，老成；财化子孙，性情迟缓不管事。分辨是妇人还是女子：看财、福两个爻，生扶身、世而没有冲克的是女子；财福去生官鬼、兄弟，或者官鬼、兄弟旺而动的是妇人。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Divining a maid or concubine: take the Wealth line alone as the chief sign. Strong Wealth is auspicious; a Pressure line moving out means illness and a lawsuit; a Peer line moving out means quarrels. If a Peer line and the Wealth line combine and hold each other, there is an improper outside attachment. If any line forms a three-punishment or six-harm with the Wealth line, a lawsuit will surely arise from it. Wealth changing into Output: good-natured; into Pressure: carries an illness; into a Peer line: licentious and of bad conduct; into Resource: mature and steady; into Output: slow by nature and takes no responsibility. To tell a married woman from a maiden, look at the Wealth and Output lines: if they generate the Body and Self lines with no clash or control, she is a maiden; if Wealth and Output generate Pressure or a Peer line, or Pressure or a Peer line is strong and moving, she is a married woman.

**《火珠林·占婚姻》原文** — 《火珠林》第 251–252 行 · *Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Divining marriage", base text; Huozhu Lin, lines 251–252*

>
> **Note:** 'Wealth changing into Output' appears twice, once judged 'good-natured' and once 'slow and takes no responsibility'. The heading 'Appendix: divining maids and concubines' runs into the first sentence, so the quotation starts at 'take the Wealth line alone'. 'Married woman' and 'maiden' are taken from the context.

## LY.MARRY.HZL.08 占雇取仆从亦用财爻：财不可太过也不可无财或空亡；子化财纯善、鬼化财带疾、兄化财不诚实、父化财稳重、财化兄淫荡；身爻是鬼有疾、是父识字、是兄说谎、是子慈善、是财最好

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:雇用、聘取仆从，也以财爻为主要的象：财爻不能太过，也不能没有财爻或者落空亡，那样的仆人慵懒不上进。子孙化财，为人纯朴善良；鬼爻化财，带病；兄弟化财，不诚实，爱说谎、瞒骗别人；父母化财，性情稳重，做事稳当；财化兄弟，多淫荡，难以托付钱财。还要看身爻：身爻是鬼，主有病；是父母，主识字；是兄弟，多说谎；是子孙，主慈善；是财爻最好。如果化出鬼爻，主生病、招口舌；化出兄弟爻，主口舌、不稳当。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For hiring or taking on a servant, too, take the Wealth line as the chief sign: it must be neither excessive nor absent or Void, or the servant is idle and unambitious. Output changing into Wealth: simple and kind; Pressure changing into Wealth: carries an illness; a Peer line changing into Wealth: dishonest, lies and cheats people; Resource changing into Wealth: steady in nature and reliable in work; Wealth changing into a Peer line: mostly licentious and cannot be trusted with money. Look also at the Body line: Pressure means illness, Resource means literacy, a Peer line means lying, Output means kindness and charity, and Wealth is best. If it changes out into Pressure, illness and quarrels; into a Peer line, quarrels and unreliability.

## LY.MARRY.YL.22 将女招夫继子祥，必须兼看结婚章，内阴外阳称大吉，鬼旺财生夫妇昌：赘婿同继子，与婚姻章同看，须一阴一阳，纯阴纯阳不可，财鬼不全也不宜

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:招女婿入赘和过继儿子一样吉祥，要和婚姻章一起看：内阴外阳是大吉，鬼爻旺、财爻生，夫妇昌盛。卦中的内外卦或者世应，必须一阴一阳才是好配偶；遇到纯阴或纯阳，决不可成；财、鬼不齐全，也不合适。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Taking a son-in-law into the household is as auspicious as adopting a son, and it is to be read together with the marriage chapter: inner yin with outer yang is greatly auspicious, and with a strong Pressure line and Wealth generating, husband and wife prosper. In the hexagram the inner and outer trigrams, or the Self and Other lines, must have one yin and one yang to make a good match; if it is pure yin or pure yang it must never be made; if Wealth and Pressure are not both complete, that too is unsuitable.

**《易林补遗·婚姻嫁娶章第十·赘婿》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 557 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 10: marriage and giving in marriage (taking a son-in-law)", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 557*

## LY.MARRY.YL.23 父母忌神愁发动，子孙用象怕空亡，世应变空难送老，六冲之卦岂绵长：赘婿占子孙为赘婿、世为己应为婿，皆不可空；世应皆动后必有更，六冲丝毫莫用

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:招婿入赘：父母爻是忌神，不动为吉；子孙爻代表入赘的女婿，空了就不确定，又不宜动，动了还怕妨碍丈夫。子孙爻虽然动，但财爻也动来帮助鬼爻，就无妨。世爻是自己、应爻是女婿，都不可空：世空，自己有打发他回去的意思；应空，女婿没有久留的心；世应都动，以后必有变更；遇到六冲，丝毫不要用。合处逢冲，或变出六冲，起初和谐，后来分拆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Taking a son-in-law in: the Resource line is the Adversary and is auspicious when it does not move; the Output line stands for the son-in-law, and if Void it is unsettled, and it should not move either, since moving may still harm the husband. Even if Output moves, if Wealth also moves and helps the Pressure line there is no harm. The Self line is oneself and the Other line the son-in-law, and neither may be Void: if the Self line is Void, one has a mind to send him back; if the Other line is Void, the son-in-law has no heart to stay long; if both move, there will surely be change later; a Six-clash is not to be used at all. A combination meeting a clash, or changing into a Six-clash, means harmony at first and separation afterwards.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.20 配仆：财爻为用；财爻持世或动而生合世、化进化生、财化财，得忠诚出力之人；散破绝空非懒即病；克世冲世忘恩负义；化鬼化绝化墓化克及鬼化财，贫穷夭折

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:配仆（安排仆人）：以财爻为用神。财爻持世，或者动了来生合世爻，或者动而化进神、化生，或者财化财，都会得到忠诚肯出力的人；财爻散、破、绝、空，不是懒就是病；财爻克世、冲世，是忘恩负义的人；财爻化成鬼、绝、墓、克，以及鬼爻化成财爻，是贫穷短命之类的人。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Taking on a servant: the Wealth line is the Subject. With the Wealth line on the Self line, or moving to generate or combine with the Self line, or moving and changing into an Advancing spirit or a generator, or Wealth changing into Wealth, you gain someone loyal who serves with effort; if the Wealth line is scattered, broken, extinguished or Void, the person is either lazy or ill; if the Wealth line controls or clashes with the Self line, the person betrays kindness and forgets favors; if Wealth changes into Pressure, extinction, tomb or a controller, or Pressure changes into Wealth, the person is of the poor and short-lived sort.
