## LY.MARRY.BSZZ.04 世临阴位女为政，财爻持世赘为姻；二爻冲克破，当家夫妻少恩情（王洪绪注：财爻持世居五爻阴位是入赘成婚，二爻冲克是夫妇各主张，不是赘婚）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卜筮正宗论家宅六爻的位置时，有两联和夫妻有关。世爻落在阴位（比如五爻是阴爻），家里是妇人当家；但如果是财爻持世在这个位置，是那个男人入赘去成婚，就不是女人主事。二爻如果被冲、被克、被破，当家的夫妻之间少恩爱，王洪绪注：那是夫妇各有各的主张，不是赘婚的说法。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In its treatment of the positions of the six lines in a household reading, the Bushi Zhengzong has two couplets touching husband and wife. Self on a yin position (for example the fifth line being a yin line): the woman rules the household; but if it is the Wealth line that holds Self in that place, the man has gone in to marry as a son-in-law, and a woman is not in charge. If the second line is clashed, controlled or broken, the couple who run the household have little affection; Wang Hongxu's note says that then husband and wife each hold their own views, and it is not the reading of a marriage taken in.

**《卜筮正宗·论家宅六爻位置》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3243 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "On the positions of the six lines in a household reading", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3243*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 世临阴位女为政，财爻持世赘为姻 | 世爻落在阴位，女人当家主事；财爻持世，就是入赘成婚。 | Self on a yin position, the woman runs the household; Wealth holding Self, the marriage is one taken in (zhui). |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3243 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 3243*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 如世居五爻，爻是阴位，主其家内降为政主持家事。 | 比如世爻在五爻，这一爻是阴位，主这家里的妇人当家，主持家里的事。 | If Self stands on the fifth line and it is a yin position, the woman in the household rules and manages the family's affairs. |
| 财爻持世居此位，其人赘去为婚，又非女人主事也。 | 财爻持世，又在这个位置，那个男人是去入赘成婚，就不是女人主事了。 | If Wealth holds Self in this place, the man has gone off to marry in as a son-in-law, and it is then not a woman in charge. |

**《卜筮正宗·论家宅六爻位置》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3245 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "On the positions of the six lines in a household reading", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3245*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 若是二爻冲克破，当家夫妻少恩情 | 如果二爻被冲、被克、被破，当家的夫妻之间少恩爱。 | If the second line is clashed, controlled or broken, the couple who run the household have little affection. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3245 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 3245*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 二爻如逢冲克，主夫妇各主张，又非赘婚之论也。 | 二爻如果遇上冲克，主夫妻各有各的主张，那就不是入赘婚的说法了。 | If the second line meets clash or control, husband and wife each hold their own views, and it is then not a judgment of marrying in. |

> **按**:这两联是家宅里的六爻分位诗（五爻是一家之主、二爻是宅和主妇）中的两句；这一组的其余各联讲屋宇、田、畜、坟、水等，与婚姻无关，没有收。注里的「主其家内降为政」的「降」字在这里说不通，大概是「妇」的误字，译文按「妇人当家」译。
>
> **Note:** These are two lines of the rhyme on the positions of the six lines in a household reading (the fifth line as the head of the house, the second as the dwelling and the mistress); the other couplets in the group, on buildings, land, livestock, graves and water, have nothing to do with marriage and are not taken. The word 'jiang' in the note's 'zhu qi jia nei jiang wei zheng' does not read here and is probably a slip for 'fu' (woman); it is translated as the woman ruling the household.

## LY.MARRY.HDY.04 男占得震主再婚，女占得坎主再嫁

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:男子占得震卦，主再婚；女子占得坎卦，主再嫁。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A man who gets Zhen will marry again; a woman who gets Kan will marry again.

**《海底眼·婚姻》原文** — 《海底眼》第 601 行 · *Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Marriage", base text; Haidi Yan, line 601*

> **Note:** The Huozhulin has 'a man who gets Zhen or Xun... a woman who gets Kan or Li...', one trigram name more on each side.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.05 阴而阳阳而阴，偏利牵丝之举：世官宜阳反阴、应财宜阴反阳，娶妇多不利，惟入赘吉

## LY.MARRY.HJC.24 财官叠见，重为一度之新人：男占女卦有两财、女占男卦有两鬼，是再娶再嫁

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:男占女，卦里有两个财；女占男，卦里有两个鬼；一定是再娶再嫁。两个财都没有损伤，一正一偏；一个旺一个空，前妻已死；两鬼发动，有两家争娶；一旺一空，前夫已死；鬼伏财下，男方家里有妻；财伏鬼下，女方有夫；鬼不空而被动爻日辰冲克妻财，是生离改嫁；世克鬼而动爻日辰生合妻财，是逐婿嫁女。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A man divining about a woman with two Wealth lines in the hexagram, or a woman divining about a man with two Pressure lines, means a remarriage; two Wealth lines both unharmed are one principal and one secondary; one thriving and one Void means the former wife has died; two Pressure lines moving means two families contending to marry her; one thriving and one Void, the former husband has died; Pressure hidden under Wealth means the man has a wife at home; Wealth hidden under Pressure means the woman has a husband; Pressure not Void while a moving line or the Day clashes with or controls Wealth means a living separation and remarriage; the Self controlling Pressure while a moving line or the Day generates or combines with Wealth means driving out the son-in-law and marrying off the daughter.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6388 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6388*

> **Note:** The premise is 'a man divining about a woman looks to Wealth, a woman divining about a man looks to Pressure'. ## LY.MARRY.HJC.49 交重兄弟克妻身，再理丝弦：兄弟爻发动克伤妻的身命，或丈夫的命临兄弟爻发动克妻身命，主妻亡而续弦

| 入福，好善，能成物，值雀，好诵经。 | 入福德爻（子孙），好善，能成事；遇朱雀，好念经。 | Entering Output (the fortune-and-virtue line): fond of good deeds and able to accomplish things; with the Vermilion Bird, fond of chanting scripture. |
| 值龙，临三爻，持三官齐，临五爻，持观音齐也。 | 遇青龙，临在三爻，持三官斋；临在五爻，持观音斋。 | With the Azure Dragon on the third line, he keeps the Three Officials' fast; on the fifth line, the Guanyin fast. |
| 入财性温和，能主家司出纳。 | 入财爻，性情温和，能管家里的出纳。 | Entering Wealth: mild in nature and able to manage the household's income and expenditure. |
| 有财帛，旺相，则多材识也。 | 有钱财；旺相的，多才识。 | He has money; if strong, he has much talent and knowledge. |
| 入马酷毒好杀，自身带疾。 | 入驿马，残酷狠毒好杀，自己带病。 | Entering the Horse: cruel, venomous, fond of killing, and ill himself. |
| 旺相加贵马，则为官，衰乃下流之辈也。 | 旺相又加贵人、驿马的，能做官；衰弱就是下流之辈。 | If strong with the Nobleman and Horse added, he becomes an official; if weak, he is of the lowest sort. |
| 其余贫、富、贵、贱、带疾、祸福俱与世爻同断。 | 其余贫、富、贵、贱、带病、祸福，都同世爻一样来断。 | For the rest, poverty, wealth, rank, lowliness, illness, calamity and fortune, judge as with the Self line. |
| 又凡占女婿者，遇男女临世爻，与应鬼居二爻，(二为宅爻)，或应鬼合内财者，俱主入养也。 | 又凡是占女婿的，遇到男女临在世爻上，和应爻的鬼爻居在二爻（二爻是宅爻），或者应爻的鬼爻合内卦的财爻的，都主入赘养。 | Again, in divining a son-in-law, if a 'man-woman' sits on the Self line, and the Other line's Pressure sits on the second line (the second is the house line), or the Other line's Pressure combines with the inner Wealth: all mean he is taken in and supported by the family. |

> **按**:「入父」「入兄」「入福」「入财」「入马」都是「飞爻入……」的省写；「持三官齐」「持观音齐」的「齐」是「斋」的转录之误（斋戒）；「男女临世爻」的意思原文没有解释，照字面译。飞爻入什么，是指飞爻所在的地支进入哪一类六亲。
>
> **Note:** 'Entering the Resource', 'entering a Peer line' and so on are shortened from 'the flying line enters ...'; 'qi' in 'keeps the ... fast' is a transcription slip for 'zhai' (fast); what 'man-woman on the Self line' means is not explained and it is translated literally. What the flying line enters means which kin the flying line's branch enters.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.19 纳宠（娶妾）：为子嗣的用子孙爻，同占子嗣；否则专看财爻，同占婚

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:娶妾：为了子嗣的，用子孙爻做用神，和前面占子嗣一样断；不是为了子嗣的，专看财爻，和占婚一样断。

## LY.MARRY.ZS.20 配仆：财爻为用；财爻持世或动而生合世、化进化生、财化财，得忠诚出力之人；散破绝空非懒即病；克世冲世忘恩负义；化鬼化绝化墓化克及鬼化财，贫穷夭折

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:配仆（安排仆人）：以财爻为用神。财爻持世，或者动了来生合世爻，或者动而化进神、化生，或者财化财，都会得到忠诚肯出力的人；财爻散、破、绝、空，不是懒就是病；财爻克世、冲世，是忘恩负义的人；财爻化成鬼、绝、墓、克，以及鬼爻化成财爻，是贫穷短命之类的人。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Taking on a servant: the Wealth line is the Subject. With the Wealth line on the Self line, or moving to generate or combine with the Self line, or moving and changing into an Advancing spirit or a generator, or Wealth changing into Wealth, you gain someone loyal who serves with effort; if the Wealth line is scattered, broken, extinguished or Void, the person is either lazy or ill; if the Wealth line controls or clashes with the Self line, the person betrays kindness and forgets favors; if Wealth changes into Pressure, extinction, tomb or a controller, or Pressure changes into Wealth, the person is of the poor and short-lived sort.
