## 总则(Principles: the Subject, the Self and Other, yin and yang)→ `总则.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.01** 夫妻要和谐，先看阴阳配不配：世属阳、应属阴，鬼（官）属阳、财属阴，内卦属阳、外卦属阴；各得其位，才是夫妇之道，日后有利。男家占，宜世阳应阴；女家占，宜世阴应阳。(《黄金策》)
  For a couple to be harmonious, first see whether yin and yang match: the Self is yang and the Other yin, Pressure is yang and Wealth yin, the inner trigram is yang and the outer yin; each in its place is the way of husband and wife, and later days will be favourable. When the man's family divines, the Self should be yang and the Other yin; when the woman's family divines, the Self should be yin and the Other yang.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.18** 财、鬼两爻是占婚姻的用神，逢空亡本不吉，但不可死守：男占女以财为主，鬼空不妨；女占男以鬼为主，财空不妨；要紧的是用神没有损害。财鬼空亡而干支相合、世应相生、阴阳得位，是真姻缘，只是半辈子同床、不能白头到老，那是天命，不是刑克；逢冲遇克叫避空，反而不用担心。长辈占子侄、兄占弟，就看子孙爻、兄弟爻，不能一律用财官。(《黄金策》)
  The Wealth and Pressure lines are the Subject in a marriage divination and Void on them is by nature unlucky, but it must not be followed rigidly: when a man divines about a woman Wealth is the main thing and Pressure Void does no harm; when a woman divines about a man Pressure is the main thing and Wealth Void does no harm; what matters is that the Subject is undamaged. If Wealth and Pressure are Void but the stems and branches combine, the Self and Other generate each other and yin and yang are in their places, it is a real marriage bond, only that they share a pillow for half a lifetime and do not grow old together, which is decreed by Heaven and not the work of punishment or control; meeting a clash or control is called escaping the Void and there is on the contrary nothing to fear. When elders divine for a son or nephew, or an elder brother for a younger, look at the Output line or the Peer line, and do not always use Wealth and Pressure.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.45** 一卦的吉凶，要细察其中精微曲折；夫妇百年，才知道到底能否团圆。王洪绪特别说：这一章只是讲男子占女方婚事、女子占男方姻缘；今天的术士不辨清楚，凡择婿、择媳、嫁妹、娶嫂，都不按用神断，一概以官为夫、财为妇，大大误人；应当按用神的吉凶来推断，不可一概只论财官。(《黄金策》)
  The good and ill of a hexagram is to be examined for its fine and winding detail; only over a lifetime as husband and wife does one know whether they come together in the end. Wang Hongxu adds especially: this chapter speaks only of a man divining about a bride or a woman divining about a bridegroom; today's practitioners, not seeing the distinction, for choosing a son-in-law or daughter-in-law, marrying off a younger sister or taking a brother's wife, do not judge by the Subject but always take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife, which misleads people greatly; one should judge by the good or ill of the Subject and never speak of Wealth and Pressure alike in every case.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.01** 婚姻喜欢卦相合，世爻、应爻宜静，财爻、官爻旺相，婚姻就可以成。世应有动就不成。男家娶妻看财爻（代人占也一样），女家嫁丈夫用官鬼爻；用神忌讳动，出现时怕被冲；旺相可成，世应互相克则不长久。世爻代表丈夫，应爻代表妻子。还要看是谁占：占丈夫忌子孙爻发动，子孙持世就不成；占妻子忌兄弟爻发动，兄弟持世就不成。间爻是媒人，父母爻是三堂，子孙爻是子嗣，都宜静。卦里没有子孙爻，不欢喜。(《火珠林》)
  A marriage likes a hexagram that combines; the Self and Other lines should be still, and with Wealth and Pressure strong the marriage can be made. If the Self or Other line moves, it will not succeed. When the man's family takes a wife look at the Wealth line (the same for a divination on someone's behalf); when the woman's family marries her to a husband use the Pressure line. The Subject fears moving, and when it appears it fears being clashed; if strong it can be made, but if the Self and Other control each other it will not last. The Self line is the husband and the Other line the wife. Look also at who is divining: for a husband, fear a moving Output line, and an Output line on the Self means it will not succeed; for a wife, fear a moving Peer line, and a Peer line on the Self means it will not succeed. The between lines are the go-between, the Resource line is the 'three halls', the Output line is the heir; all should be still. A hexagram without an Output line is not a happy one.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.04** 占妻子看财爻，占丈夫看官鬼爻，都宜安静。阳宫的人端正，阴宫的人丑陋：落在飞神上，应在头面四肢；落在伏神下，应在笨拙、不稳当。男子占得震卦、巽卦，主再婚；女子占得坎卦、离卦，主再嫁。(《火珠林》)
  For a wife look at the Wealth line and for a husband at the Pressure line; both should be still. A yang palace means upright looks and a yin palace ugly ones: on the flying spirit it answers in the head, face and limbs, and under it, in clumsiness and unsteadiness. A man who gets Zhen or Xun will marry again; a woman who gets Kan or Li will marry again.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.09** 内边（内卦、身）是阳、鬼爻是阳，丈夫就在这一边；外边（外卦、应）是阴、财爻是阴，总是妻子。世爻、应爻相生，婚姻大吉；世应比和，配成夫妻相宜。青龙、六合扶持就美，三合再加子孙爻临上更妙。应爻动而遇三刑，就是刑，不用再问；外卦交来六害，害是无疑的。(《火珠林》)
  With the inner side, the Body, yang and Pressure yang, the husband holds there; with the outer side, the Other line, yin and Wealth yin, that is always the wife. The Self and Other lines generating each other: the marriage is greatly auspicious; the Self and Other in accord, the pairing is fitting. Helped by the Azure Dragon and a Six-combine it is fair; a three-harmony with the Output line present is more wondrous still. If the Other line moves and meets a three-punishment, there is punishment, ask no further; if the outer side meets a six-harm, harm is beyond doubt.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.11** 男女同心合意，是咸、泰、益这些卦；夫妻反目，是革、睽、离这些卦。夬卦一宫阳气壮盛，妻子难保；姤卦阴气强，家里不齐整。「不有躬」到蒙卦里去判断，「夫征不复」到渐卦里去推算。(《火珠林》)
  Man and woman in agreement: hexagrams like Xian, Tai and Yi; husband and wife falling out: Ge, Kui and Li. In the palace of Guai, yang is strong and the wife is hard to keep; in the hexagram Gou, yin is strong and the household is not whole. 'Not to have a body' is judged in Meng; 'the husband goes on the expedition and does not return' is reckoned in Jian.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.18** 阴阳各得其位，都称吉利；全是阴的，白白费心费力；全是阳的，有退悔，成不了婚；即使勉强成了，终究郁闷不乐。(《火珠林》)
  Yin and yang each in its place: all is called auspicious; all yin: heart and strength spent in vain; all yang: regret and withdrawal, and the marriage is not made; even if forced through, it ends in gloom.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.25** 占婚原本要阴阳相配；动的时候一起动也算好。阳爻都不动，只有一个财爻、一个鬼爻，应爻是阴、世爻是阳，最吉祥。再得世爻在内卦，男女团圆，百年昌盛。(《火珠林》)
  A marriage divination needs yin with yang; when they move, moving together is also good. If the yang lines do not move and there is just one Wealth and one Pressure, with the Other line yin and the Self line yang, it is the most auspicious. Better still if the Self line sits in the inner trigram: man and woman are reunited and prosper for a hundred years.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.04** 论婚姻，先看阴阳，再凭财爻、官爻。卦如果纯阳或者没有财爻，一定伤妻；纯阴的卦或者缺少官爻，丈夫要早死。(《易林补遗》)
  In judging marriage, first look at yin and yang, then rely on Wealth and Pressure. If the hexagram is pure yang or has no Wealth line, the wife will surely be harmed; a pure yin hexagram, or one lacking a Pressure line, means the husband dies early.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.05** 夫唱妇随，卦里阴阳要得位；男女情投意合，爻里财、鬼要齐全。内卦是夫、外卦是妇，又以世爻为夫、应爻为妇：内阳外阴，或世阳应阴，都算得位，婚姻遇到百年和合。内阴外阳，或世阴应阳，是阴阳交错，夫妻只有一半称心。世应得位而内外卦纯阴纯阳，或内外卦得位而世应纯阴纯阳，都只是半吉。内外卦和世应全是纯阳或纯阴，不成夫妇——自古纯阴不生、纯阳不化，要一阴一阳才成配偶。再看：鬼爻是夫、财爻是妇；有鬼无财是有夫无妇，有财无鬼是有妇无夫；财、鬼齐全，夫妇才能和谐到老。(《易林补遗》)
  For the husband to lead and the wife to follow the hexagram must have yin and yang in their places; for man and woman to love and delight in each other, the lines must have Wealth and Pressure complete. The inner trigram is the husband and the outer the wife, and likewise the Self line is the husband and the Other line the wife: inner yang with outer yin, or the Self line yang with the Other yin, count as in their places, and a marriage meeting this is in harmony for a hundred years. Inner yin with outer yang, or the Self yin with the Other yang, is yin and yang crossed, and husband and wife have their wish only by half. If the Self and Other lines are in their places but the inner and outer trigrams are pure yin or pure yang, or the reverse, it is only half auspicious. If the inner and outer trigrams and the Self and Other lines are all pure yang or pure yin, they do not become husband and wife: since antiquity pure yin does not give birth and pure yang does not transform, and only one yin with one yang makes a pair. Then: the Pressure line is the husband and the Wealth line the wife; Pressure without Wealth is a husband without a wife, Wealth without Pressure a wife without a husband; only when both are complete can husband and wife grow old in harmony.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.06** 男子的婚事，财爻的象要兴旺，才能早晚一同欢悦；女子出嫁，官鬼爻要旺相，才能朝夕同欢。男占妻子，以财爻为主，决不可没有；女占丈夫，以鬼爻为先，道理相仿。(《易林补遗》)
  For a man's marriage the sign of Wealth must flourish, so that they delight together morning and evening; for a woman's marriage the Pressure line must be strong, so that they share joy morning and evening. A man divining a wife takes the Wealth line as the main thing, and it must never be absent; a woman divining a husband puts the Pressure line first; the principle is the same.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.18** 凡是婚配，要得到长久和合，只凭奇偶（阴阳），自然顷刻之间就分明。(《易林补遗》)
  In any marriage match, to gain lasting harmony, rely only on odd and even (yang and yin), and it becomes clear in an instant.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.19** 女子占男方的婚事，以官鬼爻为主，以应爻的象为凭。财爻兴旺、子孙爻安静，是好姻缘。官旺为佳，鬼爻空就不要用；又嫌子孙爻动伤官鬼，喜的是财爻兴旺来帮助鬼爻。(《易林补遗》)
  A woman divining a man's marriage takes the Pressure line as the main thing and the sign of the Other line as evidence. With Wealth flourishing and Output still, it is a good match. A strong Pressure line is good, and if it is Void do not use it; a moving Output line hurting Pressure is disliked, and what is welcome is a flourishing Wealth line helping the Pressure.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.20** 看妻子看内爻，看丈夫看外爻；世爻是女方本人，应爻是男方。女子占男方，反过来取内卦为妇、外卦为夫，又以世爻为妇、应爻为夫。也就是说，占卦的人这一边总是内卦和世爻。(《易林补遗》)
  For the wife look at the inner lines, for the husband at the outer; the Self line is the woman herself and the Other line the man. When a woman divines about a man, take the inner as the wife and the outer as the husband, the reverse of the usual, and the Self line as the wife and the Other line as the husband. That is, the divining party's own side is always the inner trigram and the Self line.
- **LY.MARRY.YL.21** 男子择妻，应爻属阴才好；女子择夫，应爻属阳才欢喜。阴阳得位才和合，即使阴阳交错，也长久快乐。男子娶妻，世爻属阳、应爻属阴算得位；女子嫁男子，世爻属阴、应爻属阳算得位；内阴外阳，也一样。得位则欢喜无穷，即使交错，也主欢乐和谐。(《易林补遗》)
  When a man chooses a wife, a yin Other line is fair; when a woman chooses a husband, a yang Other line brings joy. Yin and yang in their places make harmony; even if they are crossed, there is long joy. For a man taking a wife, the Self line yang and the Other yin count as in their places; for a woman marrying a man, the Self yin and the Other yang count as in their places; inner yin with outer yang is the same. In their places, joy is endless; even crossed, there is still gladness and harmony.
- **LY.MARRY.YY.01** 婚姻是做人的头一件大事，占它不能不慎重。求婚总是男方先开口，所以挑妻子的这一占尤其要慎重。占的次序：先占门第、德行、性情容貌、有没有子嗣，再占入门以后的吉凶、聘礼和嫁妆的厚薄，然后才占成不成；没有媒人就得不到，所以媒人（月老）也要占。鬼谷的分爻法把爻对到：祖宗、父母、外氏、婿、妇、媒妁、自身。(《易隐》)
  Marriage is the first business of human life, and its divination must not be undertaken carelessly. It is always the man's side that seeks first, so the divination for choosing a wife calls for still more care. The order of divining: first her family standing, virtue, nature and looks, and whether there will be children; then the good or ill of her entering the house and the richness of the betrothal gifts and dowry; only then whether the marriage will be made. Without a go-between it cannot be had, so the go-between (the Old Man under the Moon) must be divined too. Guiguzi's division of the lines assigns them to: ancestors, parents, the mother's family, the son-in-law, the wife, the go-between and oneself.
- **LY.MARRY.YY.09** 游魂卦主离别，八纯卦主反目。咸、恒、节、泰的和谐，睽、革、解、离的乖违。女子得坎、离的再嫁，男子得震、巽的重婚。坤变坎，男子破体；乾变离，女子不真。丈夫阳气壮盛就伤妻子，妻子阴气强就欺丈夫。蒙卦是「不有其躬」，渐卦是「夫征不复」。(《易隐》)
  A Wandering Soul hexagram means separation, and a pure eight-palace hexagram falling out. Xian, Heng, Jie and Tai are harmonious; Kui, Ge, Xie and Li are discordant. A woman who gets Kan or Li marries again; a man who gets Zhen or Xun marries again. Kun changing into Kan: the man's body is broken; Qian changing into Li: the woman is not genuine. A husband with strong yang harms his wife; a wife with strong yin overrides her husband. Meng is 'not to have a body'; Jian is 'the husband goes on the expedition and does not return'.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.01** 占婚姻：男家占女家，用妻财爻；女家占男家，用官鬼爻；父母、亲戚朋友代占也一样。官鬼有生有扶，或者妻财旺相，就可以答应成婚。(《增删卜易》)
  In a marriage divination, when the man's side asks about the woman take the Wealth line as the Subject, and when the woman's side asks about the man take the Pressure line; parents, relatives and friends divining on their behalf are the same. If the Pressure line is supported, or the Wealth line is strong, the marriage may be granted.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.04** 男方占，妻财要旺；女方占，官鬼要兴旺。代占的：男家占女，用财爻，应爻算女家；女家占男，用官鬼，应爻算男家。财、官要旺相，动了要化出吉象；应爻不该空、破，也不该克世（女家代占的应爻还不该入墓、绝）。男子自占娶妻：也用财爻，应爻兼算女子本人；财爻和应爻生世、持世、合世都吉，最忌破、墓、绝、空；财旺生世而应爻破空还可取，应爻生世而财爻破绝就不要求。野鹤补一句：财、应一起来生合世更吉；大体上财为重，应只是附和。(《增删卜易》)
  A man divining wants Wealth strong; a woman divining wants Pressure flourishing. For a divination on someone's behalf: when the man's family asks about the woman, use the Wealth line and take the Other line as her family; when the woman's family asks about the man, use the Pressure line and take the Other line as his family. Wealth or Pressure should be strong, and if it moves it should change into good; the Other line should not be Void or broken, nor control the Self line (in the woman's family's divination it should not be in a tomb or extinguished either). A man divining his own wife-to-be also uses the Wealth line and takes the Other line as the woman herself; Wealth and the Other line are good when they generate, hold or combine with the Self line, and most to be feared when broken, entombed, extinguished or Void; strong Wealth generating the Self while the Other line is broken or Void can still be taken, but if the Other line generates the Self while Wealth is broken or extinguished, do not seek it. Yehe adds: it is better still when Wealth and the Other line both come to generate or combine with the Self line; in general Wealth carries the weight and the Other line only follows along.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.05** 女家占男，用官鬼，把应爻当男家——这是古法，也是死法，断卦要活。官鬼旺相持世（婚该成）而应爻受克（男家像是不肯）时怎么断？觉子的判法：财、官为重，世、应为轻；就算是女家占男，也要财爻不失陷。男女两个人的事，财爻被回头克、又被丑土克，就生不了卯木官鬼，男女不能相合相生，婚姻即使成了也会有变。(《增删卜易》)
  When the woman's family divines about the man, Pressure is the Subject and the Other line stands for the man's family; that is the old method, and a dead one, and a hexagram must be judged with flexibility. What if a strong Pressure line holds the Self line (the marriage should be made) while the Other line is controlled (the man's family seems unwilling)? Juezi's judgment: Wealth and Pressure carry the weight, the Self and Other lines matter less; even when the woman's family divines about the man, the Wealth line must not fall into ruin. The matter concerns both the man and the woman: if Wealth is controlled by what it turns into and again by Chou Earth, it cannot generate the Mao Wood Pressure, the two cannot combine or generate each other, and even if the marriage is made it will meet change.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.14** 古人把咸、临、节、泰当作占婚的吉卦，把暌、革、解、离当作凶卦。觉子引《易冒》：得咸、临、节、泰，只要不遇到合冲、变冲，即使财官衰陷也不算凶；暌、革、解、离是凶兆，用神齐全也不算吉。觉子自己试了：得泰卦，可是兄弟持世、财爻月破旬空又被世爻克、日辰刑，他不敢拿别人的婚姻大事去试；让人再占一卦得坤卦，六冲大凶，才知道前面的泰卦不是吉，还是以财、官、世、应来断为对。有疑问不要死执古法，以再占的卦为准：后卦吉就按吉断，后卦凶就按凶推。(《增删卜易》)
  The ancients took Xian, Lin, Jie and Tai as auspicious hexagrams for marriage and Kui, Ge, Xie and Li as ill ones. Juezi quotes the Yi Mao: with Xian, Lin, Jie or Tai, unless it meets a combination-clash or a change into a clash, it is not ill even if Wealth and Pressure are weak and fallen; Kui, Ge, Xie and Li are ill omens, and it is not auspicious even if the Subject is complete. Juezi tried it himself: he obtained Tai, but with a Peer line on the Self, the Wealth line under a Month-break and Void and also controlled by the Self line and punished by the Day, and he dared not test it on someone's great matter of marriage; when the person divined again and got Kun, a Six-clash of great ill, he knew the earlier Tai was not auspicious, and that judging by Wealth, Pressure, Self and Other remains right. When in doubt do not cling to the old methods; decide by the hexagram divined again: if the later one is auspicious judge it auspicious, if it is ill reckon it ill.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.23** 占夫妻，先要分清楚问的是哪一件：妻子的寿数，夫妻能不能白头偕老，夫妻和睦不和睦，或者妻子有没有刑伤破败。占妻、占妾、占婢女，一卦只占一个人，不可以合在一起问。(《增删卜易》)
  In a divination on husband and wife, first tell apart what is being asked: the wife's lifespan, whether the couple will grow old together, whether they will be in harmony, or whether the wife will suffer injury or ruin. For a wife, a concubine or a maid, one hexagram covers one person only; do not ask about them together.

## 成否(Will it succeed: easy or hard to bring about)→ `成否.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.04** 六合卦一阴一阳配合成象，世应相生、财官相合，容易成而且吉；六冲卦不是纯阴就是纯阳，像两个女人同住、两个男人并处，志向合不来，难成，成了也不利。(《黄金策》)
  A Six-Combine hexagram is one yin matched with one yang, with the Self and Other generating each other and Wealth and Pressure combining, and is easy to bring about and auspicious; a Six-Clash hexagram is pure yin or pure yang, like two women living together or two men side by side whose aims cannot agree, hard to bring about, and unfavourable even if it succeeds.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.06** 世和应相合、应和世相合，是男女两家都愿意的象，容易成，以后也吉利。(《黄金策》)

The line controlled by the Self line is the wife, and the line controlled by the wife's line is the concubine; in a divination for oneself one may also take the Other line as the principal wife and the line it controls as the concubine. Wealth on a yang palace and yang line, strong and with auspicious spirits: she is surely beautiful and chaste in conduct and good at running the house; on a yin palace and yin line, weak, in a tomb and with the punishment, blade, Loss and Robbery stars: she is surely ugly and incapable, and dies young.
- **LY.MARRY.YY.26** 问妻子是闺女还是再嫁：卦中只有一个财爻、不见鬼爻的，是闺女。财鬼相合，或财爻下伏着鬼爻的，已经有过一个丈夫；一个财爻和两个鬼爻相合，或财爻下伏鬼又化出鬼的，已经有过两个丈夫。卦里有两个鬼爻，一个空一个旺的，一定是再嫁；两个鬼爻都旺，而日月、动爻刑冲克害财爻的，主生离改嫁。《黄金策》说：妻爻克世身、又重复合应爻，妻子必定重婚；世、应、妻爻三合，该招偏正两种丈夫；官爻多而没有凶煞冲犯，妇人该再嫁。《明睿抄本》说：男子取卦身所生的爻为床帐（卦身所生之爻），女子取卦身所克的爻为香闺（卦身所克之爻）。香闺入墓、绝，还没配成；床帐空亡，还没娶妻。这个办法灵验，不要传给不该传的人。(《易隐》)
  To ask whether the wife is a maiden or has married before: a hexagram with a single Wealth line and no Pressure means a maiden. If Wealth and Pressure combine, or Pressure is hidden beneath Wealth, she has already had one husband; one Wealth line combining with two Pressure lines, or Pressure hidden beneath Wealth and again changing into Pressure, means two husbands. A hexagram with two Pressure lines, one Void and one strong, means she has surely married again; two strong Pressure lines while the Day, Month and moving lines punish, clash, control or harm Wealth mean parting alive and remarriage. The Huangjin Ce says: the wife's line controlling the Self (Body) and doubly combining with the Other line, the wife will surely marry twice; the Self, Other and wife's lines forming a three-harmony, she takes both a secondary and a principal husband; many Pressure lines with no ill omens touching them, the woman should remarry. The Mingrui manuscript says: a man takes the line the hexagram Body generates as the bed-curtains, a woman takes the line the Body controls as the fragrant chamber. If the fragrant chamber is in a tomb or extinct, the match is not yet made; if the bed-curtains are Void, he has not yet taken a wife. This method is wonderfully accurate; do not pass it to the unworthy.
- **LY.MARRY.YY.27** 问妻妾的品德容色：妻爻临金，肤色白净，身体瘦小，性情刚烈；临木，肤色青，身材长，妖娆多姿，性情宽缓；临火，红脸，身矮，性急躁；临土，肤色黄，身体又胖又矮，性温和，做事迟慢；临水，肤色紫黑，身体灵活敏捷，性情温和宽厚，衰就急躁，动就多机变，冲就没有主见，合就没有知觉。妻爻持父母爻，长寿伶俐，能当家，会读书算账；持兄弟爻，性情损物耗财，留不住奴婢，旺相的败家好赌，和妯娌不和；持子孙爻，性善、见识多，旺相的能当家、生贵子，带朱雀的常念经；持财爻，貌美、安和，能当家开店，旺相的旺夫有财；持官鬼爻，貌丑、性狠毒，旺相的好杀，带贵人的有封赠荫庇。(《易隐》)

The wife's line holding the Resource line: long-lived, clever, able to run the house and to write and reckon; holding a Peer line: she wastes goods and squanders money and keeps no servants, and if strong ruins the house, gambles and is at odds with her sisters-in-law; holding Output: good-natured with much insight, and if strong able to run the house and bear a noble son, with the Vermilion Bird often chanting scripture; holding Wealth: fair, calm and gentle, able to run the house and a shop, and if strong benefits her husband and has money; holding Pressure: ugly and cruel, if strong given to killing, and with the Nobleman having honors and protection.
- **LY.MARRY.YY.28** 财爻伏在父母爻下，为人庄重不苟；伏在子孙爻下，性善不损物，喜打扮；伏在兄弟爻下，貌丑，贪淫好赌；伏在官鬼爻下，性情残酷恶劣，有病，但夫妻大吉。阳卦阳爻，工巧无双；阴卦阴爻，丑拙第一。阳变入阴，小时灵巧、长大笨拙；阴变入阳，小时丑陋、长大美好。详见性情容貌篇。(《易隐》)
  Wealth hidden beneath the Resource line: dignified and not careless; beneath Output: good-natured, does not waste goods, likes to dress up; beneath a Peer line: ugly, lustful and given to gambling; beneath Pressure: cruel and bad-tempered, ill, yet the couple is greatly auspicious. A yang trigram and yang line: skilled and clever beyond compare; a yin trigram and yin line: the ugliest and clumsiest. Yang changing into yin: clever when young and clumsy when grown; yin changing into yang: plain when small and lovely when grown. See the section on nature and looks.
- **LY.MARRY.YY.29** 问妻子的富贵贫贱：还没娶，按她父亲那边断；出嫁了，按丈夫这边断。没娶的时候，只看外卦本宫的父母爻；外卦的父母爻不出现，就看伏爻；不现又不伏，就把生妻爻的那个爻当妻母，克妻母的爻当岳父，和真的父母一样分阴阳、真假来断。妻父妻母爻带禄、马、贵人，是官宦人家的女儿；带财、禄、德、福、生气，是富家女；带咸池、沐浴、玄武和休囚死气，是贫贱人家的女儿；带白虎和贵人，武职的女儿；带白虎、刃、劫杀，军人工匠的女儿；带勾陈、土旺相，农家女；带朱雀、火、财旺，牙人的女儿；带玄武、红艳，行为放荡人家的女儿；带刑害亡劫又无气，低贱人的女儿；再加金、白虎、刑、刃，是不务农而追逐末业者的女儿。螣蛇逢生旺，是九流人的女儿；螣蛇逢冲并、墓、合，是有艺术技能的人的女儿，按五行定是哪一种技艺。衰败就贫，空绝就断绝，生合世爻能得她家的荫庇，刑害冲克世爻就被她家欺凌。(《易隐》)

The wife's father and mother lines carrying Emolument, the Horse and the Nobleman: a daughter of an official family; carrying Wealth, Emolument, Virtue, Fortune and the Birth-breath: a rich family; carrying the Xianchi star, the Bathing stage, the Dark Tortoise and the breaths of rest, trap and death: a poor and lowly family; the White Tiger and the Nobleman: a military officer's daughter; the White Tiger, the blade and the Robbery star: a soldier's or artisan's; Hook Earth with strong Earth: a farmer's; the Vermilion Bird and Fire with strong Wealth: a broker's; the Dark Tortoise and the Red Allure star: a licentious household's; punishment, harm, the Loss and Robbery stars without breath: a low person's; with Metal, the White Tiger, punishment and blade added: one who does not farm but pursues the lesser trades. The Coiling Snake in birth or strength: a daughter of the nine schools; the Coiling Snake meeting clash, merging, tomb or combination: a craftsman's or artist's, the Five Phases deciding which craft. Weak and ruined means poor; Void or extinct means cut off; generating or combining with the Self line, one receives their protection; punishing, harming, clashing with or controlling the Self line, one is bullied by them.

- **LY.MARRY.YY.31** 问妻家远近、娶妻早晚、妻子年长年幼：妻财爻持世或出现，一定住得近、娶得早；伏藏不现，住得远、娶得迟。财爻和世爻同在一个卦，是近亲的女儿；财爻和世爻被月日、动爻、变爻隔断的，一定是外郡人的女儿——比如乾卦戌世、寅财，而申、午、辰爻动，或月日并动申、午、辰爻，都是隔断。用八卦定方位，用二十八宿定是哪个郡；卦空爻空，就用财爻的长生方位来定。财爻在二爻（二爻是宅爻）叫坐宅，必是养媳妇，不然也是紧邻的女子。财爻合世爻、身爻、命爻的，娶妻一定早。妻爻属阳、世爻属阴，妻子年长；世爻属阳、妻爻属阴，妻子年幼。妻爻在辰、戌、丑、未是长女，在子、午、卯、酉是中女，在寅、申、巳、亥是少女。(《易隐》)
  To ask how far the wife's family lives, how soon he will marry and whether the wife is older or younger: if the Wealth line holds the Self line or appears, she surely lives near and the marriage comes early; if it is hidden and does not appear, she lives far and the marriage comes late. Wealth and the Self line in the same trigram: a daughter of a close relative; cut off from each other by the Month, Day, moving or changed lines: surely a daughter of someone from another prefecture (for example in Qian with the Self on Xu and Wealth on Yin, a moving Shen, Wu or Chen line, or the Month and Day together moving on Shen, Wu or Chen, all cut them off). Use the eight trigrams to fix the direction and the twenty-eight lodges to fix the prefecture; if the hexagram or line is Void, fix it by the direction of the Wealth line's Birth stage. Wealth on the second line (the house line) is called sitting in the house: surely a child bride raised in the family, or else a girl from next door. Wealth combining with the Self, Body or fate line: he marries early. A yang wife's line with a yin Self line: the wife is older; a yang Self line with a yin wife's line: the wife is younger. The wife's line on Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei: the eldest daughter; on Zi, Wu, Mao or You: a middle daughter; on Yin, Shen, Si or Hai: the youngest daughter.
- **LY.MARRY.YY.32** 占女婿：以本宫里出现的官鬼爻为主，不论内卦外卦（因为女婿是半个儿子，有家里人的意思）；不出现，就看本宫的伏神；不现又无伏，就取克女儿的那个爻当女婿。本宫的官鬼是正婿，年月日时上别的宫出来的是旁婿；正宫有两个鬼爻，阳爻得位的是正婿，阴爻失位的是旁婿。正婿的鬼爻在巳、午，火命的吉，女儿命属土的吉（火能生土）。鬼爻伏在父母爻下，伶俐庄重；伏在子孙爻下，性善不损物，能容纳；伏在兄弟爻下，贪淫好赌，不诚实；伏在财爻下，能管钱财，做事分明，夫妻好合（财鬼带辰午酉亥自刑的，主先私通后娶）。鬼化鬼，男家没定准，或停妻再娶；兄化鬼，好斗凶狠贪淫（鬼带刑刃也是）；财化鬼，克妻损财；子化鬼，带青龙、喜神、德神、合，夫妇和谐，带华盖、刑、刃，是僧道还俗；鬼化子，与妻互相有益；鬼化兄，伤妻、嫖赌；鬼化财，能有设施。(《易隐》)

The home palace's Pressure is the principal son-in-law, and one that comes from another palace through the year, month, day or hour is a collateral one; if the home palace has two Pressure lines, the yang line in its place is the principal son-in-law and the yin line out of place the collateral one. Pressure hidden beneath the Resource line: clever and dignified; beneath Output: good-natured, does not waste goods, tolerant; beneath a Peer line: lustful, gambling and dishonest; beneath Wealth: able to manage money, clear in affairs, the couple well matched (if Wealth and Pressure carry the self-punishing branches Chen, Wu, You or Hai: illicit relations first, marriage after). - **LY.MARRY.ZS.03** 觉子驳一种推法：把应爻当作妻，要它旺相才是贤良的女子；同时又把应爻当作女家的父母，旺相就是富贵人家、休囚就是贫寒人家。两个说法一起用，就推出「贤良的女子都出自富贵人家」，说不通——一个爻不能同时担当两个角色。(《增删卜易》)

Used together, the two readings lead to 'every virtuous woman comes from a rich family', which is absurd: one line cannot carry two roles at once.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.22** 李我平批评古法占婚多浮辞：《黄金策》说的「丈夫有才能，官鬼占长生的位置；妻子丑拙，财爻落在墓库」还算近理；《易冒》用八宫推容貌、用六神定才情就不行——同一卦，按八宫读是端正聪明的淑女，按六神读财爻临白虎又成了悖逆之星，两种读法彼此打架。婚姻有一定的缘分，财旺官兴，就会夫唱妇随；相生相合，自然有讨人喜欢的容貌；吉的就求，凶的就弃，用财、官兼看世、应来断就够了。要是事事求全——命不入墓、六亲不相妨、父母不沉、子孙不溺、财不动、官不没、夫妇不二、阴阳不悖——世上没有这样的女子，六十四卦里也没有这样的卦。(《增删卜易》)
  Li Woping criticizes the old methods of divining marriage as full of showy words: what the Huangjin Ce says, that a husband with ability has the Pressure line in the position of Birth and an ugly, clumsy wife has the Wealth line in the tomb or storehouse, is still close to reason; but the Yi Mao inferring looks from the eight palaces and settling talent by the six spirits will not do, since the same hexagram read by palace gives an upright and clever virtuous woman, and read by spirit gives Wealth with the White Tiger, a star of perversity, so the two readings contradict each other. Marriage has its appointed bond: with Wealth strong and Pressure flourishing, husband leads and wife follows; where they generate and combine she will naturally have looks that please; seek what is auspicious and abandon what is ill, and judging by Wealth and Pressure together with the Self and Other lines is enough. If one demands perfection in everything (fates not entering a tomb, six kin not harming each other, parents not sinking, children not lost, Wealth not moving, Pressure not lost, no second spouse, yin and yang not at odds), there is no such woman in the world and no such hexagram among the sixty-four.

## 聘嫁(Betrothal gifts, dowry, and the two families)→ `聘嫁.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.07** 求庚帖（女方的八字帖），要应爻安静不空、又生合世爻，才一定应允；应爻发动、空亡或冲克世爻，都主不答应。论聘礼，世爻见螣蛇、兄弟，主男家吝啬、礼不多；应爻见它们，主女家妆奁淡薄；旺相发动，也是克妻的兆头。(《黄金策》)

## 子嗣(Children in the marriage)→ `子嗣.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.10** 占婚遇到父爻旺相发动，子孙墓绝，日后子息一定少；如果占的是长辈（大人）的婚姻，有这种象，是因为没有儿子而再娶；父爻持身、持世的也一样。(《黄金策》)
  In divining marriage, if the Resource line is thriving and moving and Output is in the tomb or cut off, children later will surely be few; if it is an elder's marriage that is divined, this image means he is marrying because he has no son; Resource holding the Body or the Self is the same.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.16** 父母爻当权发动，子孙躲在空亡里，是事情还没定；如果子孙不空，而是死绝无气，就被父母克，日后一定克子，没有子息。(《黄金策》)
  If the Resource line holds power and moves while Output hides in the Void, the matter is not yet settled; if Output is not Void but dead, cut off and without qi, it is controlled by Resource, and later the children will surely be harmed, leaving no offspring.

- **LY.MARRY.YL.09** 子孙爻代表后代，遇到绝就没有儿子。子孙爻宜静、不宜动，动了刑克丈夫；宜旺、不宜空，空了没有儿子；落在绝地又没有儿子；绝中如果得到生扶，反而儿孙多育。(《易林补遗》)

- **LY.MARRY.YL.16** 孤辰星落在鬼爻上，丈夫必定没有儿子；寡宿星临在财爻上，妻子该少子息。男子怕孤辰临官鬼，女子嫌寡宿落妻财，遇到其中一样，以后没有子息，不仅儿女受伤，更主夫妻相克。父母爻旺、兄弟爻少，妨碍儿女；妻财强、没有鬼爻，损害公婆。子孙爻动固然刑克丈夫，但财爻兴旺就有化解；兄弟爻动虽说克妻，但子孙发动就无妨。父母爻动而兄弟爻不动，一定伤儿女；财爻动而鬼爻不动，一定损公婆。子孙动伤夫，财动去生官鬼反而吉；兄弟兴旺害妻，子孙一同发动就无妨。(《易林补遗》)
