## 总则(Principles: the Subject, the Self and Other, yin and yang)→ `总则.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.01** 夫妻要和谐，先看阴阳配不配：世属阳、应属阴，鬼（官）属阳、财属阴，内卦属阳、外卦属阴；各得其位，才是夫妇之道，日后有利。男家占，宜世阳应阴；女家占，宜世阴应阳。(《黄金策》)
  For a couple to be harmonious, first see whether yin and yang match: the Self is yang and the Other yin, Pressure is yang and Wealth yin, the inner trigram is yang and the outer yin; each in its place is the way of husband and wife, and later days will be favourable. When the man's family divines, the Self should be yang and the Other yin; when the woman's family divines, the Self should be yin and the Other yang.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.18** 财、鬼两爻是占婚姻的用神，逢空亡本不吉，但不可死守：男占女以财为主，鬼空不妨；女占男以鬼为主，财空不妨；要紧的是用神没有损害。财鬼空亡而干支相合、世应相生、阴阳得位，是真姻缘，只是半辈子同床、不能白头到老，那是天命，不是刑克；逢冲遇克叫避空，反而不用担心。长辈占子侄、兄占弟，就看子孙爻、兄弟爻，不能一律用财官。(《黄金策》)
  The Wealth and Pressure lines are the Subject in a marriage divination and Void on them is by nature unlucky, but it must not be followed rigidly: when a man divines about a woman Wealth is the main thing and Pressure Void does no harm; when a woman divines about a man Pressure is the main thing and Wealth Void does no harm; what matters is that the Subject is undamaged. If Wealth and Pressure are Void but the stems and branches combine, the Self and Other generate each other and yin and yang are in their places, it is a real marriage bond, only that they share a pillow for half a lifetime and do not grow old together, which is decreed by Heaven and not the work of punishment or control; meeting a clash or control is called escaping the Void and there is on the contrary nothing to fear. When elders divine for a son or nephew, or an elder brother for a younger, look at the Output line or the Peer line, and do not always use Wealth and Pressure.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.45** 一卦的吉凶，要细察其中精微曲折；夫妇百年，才知道到底能否团圆。王洪绪特别说：这一章只是讲男子占女方婚事、女子占男方姻缘；今天的术士不辨清楚，凡择婿、择媳、嫁妹、娶嫂，都不按用神断，一概以官为夫、财为妇，大大误人；应当按用神的吉凶来推断，不可一概只论财官。(《黄金策》)
  The good and ill of a hexagram is to be examined for its fine and winding detail; only over a lifetime as husband and wife does one know whether they come together in the end. Wang Hongxu adds especially: this chapter speaks only of a man divining about a bride or a woman divining about a bridegroom; today's practitioners, not seeing the distinction, for choosing a son-in-law or daughter-in-law, marrying off a younger sister or taking a brother's wife, do not judge by the Subject but always take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife, which misleads people greatly; one should judge by the good or ill of the Subject and never speak of Wealth and Pressure alike in every case.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.01** 婚姻喜欢卦相合，世爻、应爻宜静，财爻、官爻旺相，婚姻就可以成。世应有动就不成。男家娶妻看财爻（代人占也一样），女家嫁丈夫用官鬼爻；用神忌讳动，出现时怕被冲；旺相可成，世应互相克则不长久。世爻代表丈夫，应爻代表妻子。还要看是谁占：占丈夫忌子孙爻发动，子孙持世就不成；占妻子忌兄弟爻发动，兄弟持世就不成。间爻是媒人，父母爻是三堂，子孙爻是子嗣，都宜静。卦里没有子孙爻，不欢喜。(《火珠林》)
  A marriage likes a hexagram that combines; the Self and Other lines should be still, and with Wealth and Pressure strong the marriage can be made. If the Self or Other line moves, it will not succeed. When the man's family takes a wife look at the Wealth line (the same for a divination on someone's behalf); when the woman's family marries her to a husband use the Pressure line. The Subject fears moving, and when it appears it fears being clashed; if strong it can be made, but if the Self and Other control each other it will not last. The Self line is the husband and the Other line the wife. Look also at who is divining: for a husband, fear a moving Output line, and an Output line on the Self means it will not succeed; for a wife, fear a moving Peer line, and a Peer line on the Self means it will not succeed. The between lines are the go-between, the Resource line is the 'three halls', the Output line is the heir; all should be still. A hexagram without an Output line is not a happy one.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.04** 占妻子看财爻，占丈夫看官鬼爻，都宜安静。阳宫的人端正，阴宫的人丑陋：落在飞神上，应在头面四肢；落在伏神下，应在笨拙、不稳当。男子占得震卦、巽卦，主再婚；女子占得坎卦、离卦，主再嫁。(《火珠林》)
  For a wife look at the Wealth line and for a husband at the Pressure line; both should be still. A yang palace means upright looks and a yin palace ugly ones: on the flying spirit it answers in the head, face and limbs, and under it, in clumsiness and unsteadiness. A man who gets Zhen or Xun will marry again; a woman who gets Kan or Li will marry again.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.09** 内边（内卦、身）是阳、鬼爻是阳，丈夫就在这一边；外边（外卦、应）是阴、财爻是阴，总是妻子。世爻、应爻相生，婚姻大吉；世应比和，配成夫妻相宜。青龙、六合扶持就美，三合再加子孙爻临上更妙。应爻动而遇三刑，就是刑，不用再问；外卦交来六害，害是无疑的。(《火珠林》)
  With the inner side, the Body, yang and Pressure yang, the husband holds there; with the outer side, the Other line, yin and Wealth yin, that is always the wife. The Self and Other lines generating each other: the marriage is greatly auspicious; the Self and Other in accord, the pairing is fitting. Helped by the Azure Dragon and a Six-combine it is fair; a three-harmony with the Output line present is more wondrous still. If the Other line moves and meets a three-punishment, there is punishment, ask no further; if the outer side meets a six-harm, harm is beyond doubt.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.11** 男女同心合意，是咸、泰、益这些卦；夫妻反目，是革、睽、离这些卦。夬卦一宫阳气壮盛，妻子难保；姤卦阴气强，家里不齐整。「不有躬」到蒙卦里去判断，「夫征不复」到渐卦里去推算。(《火珠林》)
  Man and woman in agreement: hexagrams like Xian, Tai and Yi; husband and wife falling out: Ge, Kui and Li. In the palace of Guai, yang is strong and the wife is hard to keep; in the hexagram Gou, yin is strong and the household is not whole. 'Not to have a body' is judged in Meng; 'the husband goes on the expedition and does not return' is reckoned in Jian.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.18** 阴阳各得其位，都称吉利；全是阴的，白白费心费力；全是阳的，有退悔，成不了婚；即使勉强成了，终究郁闷不乐。(《火珠林》)
  Yin and yang each in its place: all is called auspicious; all yin: heart and strength spent in vain; all yang: regret and withdrawal, and the marriage is not made; even if forced through, it ends in gloom.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.25** 占婚原本要阴阳相配；动的时候一起动也算好。阳爻都不动，只有一个财爻、一个鬼爻，应爻是阴、世爻是阳，最吉祥。再得世爻在内卦，男女团圆，百年昌盛。(《火珠林》)
  A marriage divination needs yin with yang; when they move, moving together is also good. If the yang lines do not move and there is just one Wealth and one Pressure, with the Other line yin and the Self line yang, it is the most auspicious. Better still if the Self line sits in the inner trigram: man and woman are reunited and prosper for a hundred years.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.01** 占婚姻：男家占女家，用妻财爻；女家占男家，用官鬼爻；父母、亲戚朋友代占也一样。官鬼有生有扶，或者妻财旺相，就可以答应成婚。(《增删卜易》)
  In a marriage divination, when the man's side asks about the woman take the Wealth line as the Subject, and when the woman's side asks about the man take the Pressure line; parents, relatives and friends divining on their behalf are the same. If the Pressure line is supported, or the Wealth line is strong, the marriage may be granted.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.04** 男方占，妻财要旺；女方占，官鬼要兴旺。代占的：男家占女，用财爻，应爻算女家；女家占男，用官鬼，应爻算男家。财、官要旺相，动了要化出吉象；应爻不该空、破，也不该克世（女家代占的应爻还不该入墓、绝）。男子自占娶妻：也用财爻，应爻兼算女子本人；财爻和应爻生世、持世、合世都吉，最忌破、墓、绝、空；财旺生世而应爻破空还可取，应爻生世而财爻破绝就不要求。野鹤补一句：财、应一起来生合世更吉；大体上财为重，应只是附和。(《增删卜易》)
  A man divining wants Wealth strong; a woman divining wants Pressure flourishing. For a divination on someone's behalf: when the man's family asks about the woman, use the Wealth line and take the Other line as her family; when the woman's family asks about the man, use the Pressure line and take the Other line as his family. Wealth or Pressure should be strong, and if it moves it should change into good; the Other line should not be Void or broken, nor control the Self line (in the woman's family's divination it should not be in a tomb or extinguished either). A man divining his own wife-to-be also uses the Wealth line and takes the Other line as the woman herself; Wealth and the Other line are good when they generate, hold or combine with the Self line, and most to be feared when broken, entombed, extinguished or Void; strong Wealth generating the Self while the Other line is broken or Void can still be taken, but if the Other line generates the Self while Wealth is broken or extinguished, do not seek it. Yehe adds: it is better still when Wealth and the Other line both come to generate or combine with the Self line; in general Wealth carries the weight and the Other line only follows along.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.05** 女家占男，用官鬼，把应爻当男家——这是古法，也是死法，断卦要活。官鬼旺相持世（婚该成）而应爻受克（男家像是不肯）时怎么断？觉子的判法：财、官为重，世、应为轻；就算是女家占男，也要财爻不失陷。男女两个人的事，财爻被回头克、又被丑土克，就生不了卯木官鬼，男女不能相合相生，婚姻即使成了也会有变。(《增删卜易》)
  When the woman's family divines about the man, Pressure is the Subject and the Other line stands for the man's family; that is the old method, and a dead one, and a hexagram must be judged with flexibility. What if a strong Pressure line holds the Self line (the marriage should be made) while the Other line is controlled (the man's family seems unwilling)? Juezi's judgment: Wealth and Pressure carry the weight, the Self and Other lines matter less; even when the woman's family divines about the man, the Wealth line must not fall into ruin. The matter concerns both the man and the woman: if Wealth is controlled by what it turns into and again by Chou Earth, it cannot generate the Mao Wood Pressure, the two cannot combine or generate each other, and even if the marriage is made it will meet change.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.14** 古人把咸、临、节、泰当作占婚的吉卦，把暌、革、解、离当作凶卦。觉子引《易冒》：得咸、临、节、泰，只要不遇到合冲、变冲，即使财官衰陷也不算凶；暌、革、解、离是凶兆，用神齐全也不算吉。觉子自己试了：得泰卦，可是兄弟持世、财爻月破旬空又被世爻克、日辰刑，他不敢拿别人的婚姻大事去试；让人再占一卦得坤卦，六冲大凶，才知道前面的泰卦不是吉，还是以财、官、世、应来断为对。有疑问不要死执古法，以再占的卦为准：后卦吉就按吉断，后卦凶就按凶推。(《增删卜易》)
  The ancients took Xian, Lin, Jie and Tai as auspicious hexagrams for marriage and Kui, Ge, Xie and Li as ill ones. Juezi quotes the Yi Mao: with Xian, Lin, Jie or Tai, unless it meets a combination-clash or a change into a clash, it is not ill even if Wealth and Pressure are weak and fallen; Kui, Ge, Xie and Li are ill omens, and it is not auspicious even if the Subject is complete. Juezi tried it himself: he obtained Tai, but with a Peer line on the Self, the Wealth line under a Month-break and Void and also controlled by the Self line and punished by the Day, and he dared not test it on someone's great matter of marriage; when the person divined again and got Kun, a Six-clash of great ill, he knew the earlier Tai was not auspicious, and that judging by Wealth, Pressure, Self and Other remains right. When in doubt do not cling to the old methods; decide by the hexagram divined again: if the later one is auspicious judge it auspicious, if it is ill reckon it ill.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.23** 占夫妻，先要分清楚问的是哪一件：妻子的寿数，夫妻能不能白头偕老，夫妻和睦不和睦，或者妻子有没有刑伤破败。占妻、占妾、占婢女，一卦只占一个人，不可以合在一起问。(《增删卜易》)
  In a divination on husband and wife, first tell apart what is being asked: the wife's lifespan, whether the couple will grow old together, whether they will be in harmony, or whether the wife will suffer injury or ruin. For a wife, a concubine or a maid, one hexagram covers one person only; do not ask about them together.

## 成否(Will it succeed: easy or hard to bring about)→ `成否.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.04** 六合卦一阴一阳配合成象，世应相生、财官相合，容易成而且吉；六冲卦不是纯阴就是纯阳，像两个女人同住、两个男人并处，志向合不来，难成，成了也不利。(《黄金策》)
  A Six-Combine hexagram is one yin matched with one yang, with the Self and Other generating each other and Wealth and Pressure combining, and is easy to bring about and auspicious; a Six-Clash hexagram is pure yin or pure yang, like two women living together or two men side by side whose aims cannot agree, hard to bring about, and unfavourable even if it succeeds.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.06** 世和应相合、应和世相合，是男女两家都愿意的象，容易成，以后也吉利。(《黄金策》)
  The Self and Other combining with each other is the image of both families willing; it is easy to bring about and auspicious later as well.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.08** 应爻生合世爻，是女家贪图男家，容易成；世刑克应爻，男家不会早来求；世旺应衰，是倚仗富有欺负贫弱、用强抢娶。世上见鬼和螣蛇、财爻旺相、青龙得地，多半是看上了女子的姿色，想设谋娶她；世上见玄武、兄弟，是因为缺钱还债，想谋娶这个女子。(《黄金策》)
  The Other line generating or combining with the Self means the woman's family covets the man's and it is easy to bring about; the Self punishing or controlling the Other means the man's family would not have come early to ask; the Self thriving and the Other weak is relying on wealth to bully the poor and taking her by force. With the Self carrying Pressure and the Coiling Snake, Wealth thriving and the Azure Dragon well placed, it is probably because the girl is good-looking and someone means to scheme to marry her; with the Self carrying the Dark Tortoise or Peer, it is because the family lacks money to repay a debt and wants to take the girl in.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.14** 女方占男方，最不宜鬼爻刑克世爻，也不宜应爻克世，都主不成，男家不愿结婚；如果他有制约、我有扶助、大象又吉，也可成，但他家终究无意；若得官爻生合世爻，或合财爻，或应爻生合世爻，才主容易成。(《黄金策》)
  When the woman's side divines about the man, the most unfavourable thing is a Pressure line punishing or controlling the Self line, and also the Other line controlling the Self; both mean it will not succeed and the man's family is unwilling; if he is restrained, I am supported and the overall image is auspicious, it can still succeed, though his family in the end has no intention; if Pressure generates or combines with the Self line, or combines with the Wealth line, or the Other generates or combines with the Self, then it is easy to bring about.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.23** 世空，是自己不想成；强行成了，终究不如意；应空，是对方不想成；成了以后两家一定不来往。(《黄金策》)
  The Self Void means one does not wish it oneself, and if forced through it never turns out as wished; the Other Void means the other side does not wish it, and if it comes about the two families will surely not visit each other afterwards.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.29** 父母爻是主婚人；不上卦、或落空亡，必定没有主婚的人，恐怕是那个女子自己作主，不然这件事难以成就。(《黄金策》)
  The Resource line is the officiant of the marriage; if it is absent from the hexagram or falls Void there is surely no one to preside, and the woman may be deciding for herself, or else the matter is hard to bring about.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.02** 为什么世应一动就不成？世爻动，男家进退不定；应爻动，女家不肯；世应逢空也一样。所忌的「二字动」，忌的是财、官两个爻动。「三堂」是父母、兄弟、子孙三种爻。(《火珠林》)
  Why does it fail if the Self or Other line moves? If the Self line moves, the man's family wavers; if the Other line moves, the woman's family will not agree; the same if the Self or Other line is Void. The 'two' whose moving is feared are the Wealth and Pressure lines. The 'three halls' are the Resource, Peer and Output lines.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.12** 一阳一阴（一奇一偶），成亲顺利；两个鬼爻、两个财爻，婚配违逆不合。凶煞遇到妻财，妻子有厄；凶煞临到官鬼，丈夫悲哀（有灾）。(《火珠林》)
  One odd and one even: the match goes smoothly; two Pressure lines or two Wealth lines: the pairing goes against. A baleful star meeting the Wealth line brings the wife distress; a baleful star on the Pressure line brings the husband sorrow.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.22** 凡是占卦问婚姻，财爻、鬼爻两样齐全就可以成。鬼爻是丈夫，愁的是子孙爻动；财爻是妻子，也怕兄弟爻旺。(《火珠林》)
  Whenever someone divines a marriage, with both Wealth and Pressure complete it can be made. The Pressure line is the husband, and he fears a moving Output line; the Wealth line is the wife, and she fears a flourishing Peer line.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.06** 妻财休囚、破、散，或官鬼衰、入墓、绝、空，都难成婚。财爻、官爻不该落墓、绝、空、破，也不该动了以后化成破、散、克、鬼——那是短命贫寒的命。男方占，忌财爻犯这些；女方占，忌官爻犯；男女自己占，忌世爻犯，勉强成婚，很快就会不和。(《增删卜易》)
  If Wealth is resting, trapped, broken or scattered, or Pressure is weak, entombed, extinguished or Void, the marriage can hardly be made. Neither the Wealth line nor the Pressure line should fall into tomb, extinction, Void or break, nor move and change into a break, a scattering, a controller or Pressure: that is a fate of early death and poverty. For a man's divination, fear that the Wealth line commits these faults; for a woman's, the Pressure line; when a man or woman divines for themselves, the Self line; if the marriage is forced through, discord shows at once.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.07** 世爻静而落空亡，或者动了变成退神，终究要失望。世爻动而落空的，到填实空亡的月份、日子一定成；静而落空的，终究不成。世爻动：化成进神一定成，化成退神难成。应爻静而空、破，或者化成退神，也照这个推。(《增删卜易》)
  A Self line that is still and Void, or that moves and changes into a Retreating spirit, ends in disappointment. If it moves and is Void, it succeeds in the month or day that fills the Void; if it is still and Void, it never succeeds. If the Self line moves, changing into an Advancing spirit it surely succeeds, changing into a Retreating spirit it is hard to accomplish. A still Other line that is Void or broken, or that changes into a Retreating spirit, is judged the same way.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.08** 应爻静而旬空、财爻化成进神，是早就有的好姻缘。应爻空，是对方不踏实；如果应爻动了又化成退神，对方必有反悔的心。如果财爻动、化成进神而来生世，虽然会有退悔，终究是好姻缘。世爻、应爻动了又逢破，也应在破被填实的时候。(《增删卜易》)
  A still Other line in the Void of its ten-day cycle with Wealth changing into an Advancing spirit is a good match long fated. If the Other line is Void, the other party is not sincere; if it moves and changes into a Retreating spirit, they surely have thoughts of going back on it. If Wealth moves, changes into an Advancing spirit and generates the Self line, then although there is withdrawal and regret, it is in the end a good match. If the Self and Other lines move and are broken, it too answers at the time the break becomes real.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.09** 世爻、应爻都空，是白费力气；卦变成反吟，翻来覆去难成。野鹤补充：如果是空而逢旺，财爻和官鬼互相生，起先虽然不答应，后来终究还是成。(《增删卜易》)
  With the Self and Other lines both Void, effort is wasted; a hexagram that turns into a Reversed Chant flips back and forth and is hard to accomplish. Yehe adds: if the Void meets strength and Wealth and Pressure generate each other, although at first it is refused, in the end it still succeeds.

## 动变(Moving lines and what they turn into)→ `动变.md`

When the woman's family divines about the man and gets Wealth changing into Wealth, with doubled Wealth lines generating the Self, the betrothal gifts will surely be rich; it is not good if Pressure moves and changes into Pressure, which surely means the husband's death, and doubled Pressure lines controlling the Self mean, if not a fight over the marriage or a snatched bride, then surely repeated disaster and dispute. A man who meets this also faces change; where it controls the Self one should stop, and where the Self line changes into Pressure one should stop all the more.

To tell a married woman from a maiden, look at the Wealth and Output lines: if they generate the Body and Self lines with no clash or control, she is a maiden; if Wealth and Output generate Pressure or a Peer line, or Pressure or a Peer line is strong and moving, she is a married woman.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.08** 雇用、聘取仆从，也以财爻为主要的象：财爻不能太过，也不能没有财爻或者落空亡，那样的仆人慵懒不上进。子孙化财，为人纯朴善良；鬼爻化财，带病；兄弟化财，不诚实，爱说谎、瞒骗别人；父母化财，性情稳重，做事稳当；财化兄弟，多淫荡，难以托付钱财。还要看身爻：身爻是鬼，主有病；是父母，主识字；是兄弟，多说谎；是子孙，主慈善；是财爻最好。如果化出鬼爻，主生病、招口舌；化出兄弟爻，主口舌、不稳当。(《火珠林》)
  For hiring or taking on a servant, too, take the Wealth line as the chief sign: it must be neither excessive nor absent or Void, or the servant is idle and unambitious. Output changing into Wealth: simple and kind; Pressure changing into Wealth: carries an illness; a Peer line changing into Wealth: dishonest, lies and cheats people; Resource changing into Wealth: steady in nature and reliable in work; Wealth changing into a Peer line: mostly licentious and cannot be trusted with money. Look also at the Body line: Pressure means illness, Resource means literacy, a Peer line means lying, Output means kindness and charity, and Wealth is best. If it changes out into Pressure, illness and quarrels; into a Peer line, quarrels and unreliability.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.19** 娶妾：为了子嗣的，用子孙爻做用神，和前面占子嗣一样断；不是为了子嗣的，专看财爻，和占婚一样断。(《增删卜易》)
  Taking a concubine: if it is for the sake of an heir, take the Output line as the Subject and judge as in the earlier divination on children; otherwise look at the Wealth line alone and judge as in a marriage divination.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.20** 配仆（安排仆人）：以财爻为用神。财爻持世，或者动了来生合世爻，或者动而化进神、化生，或者财化财，都会得到忠诚肯出力的人；财爻散、破、绝、空，不是懒就是病；财爻克世、冲世，是忘恩负义的人；财爻化成鬼、绝、墓、克，以及鬼爻化成财爻，是贫穷短命之类的人。(《增删卜易》)
  Taking on a servant: the Wealth line is the Subject. With the Wealth line on the Self line, or moving to generate or combine with the Self line, or moving and changing into an Advancing spirit or a generator, or Wealth changing into Wealth, you gain someone loyal who serves with effort; if the Wealth line is scattered, broken, extinguished or Void, the person is either lazy or ill; if the Wealth line controls or clashes with the Self line, the person betrays kindness and forgets favors; if Wealth changes into Pressure, extinction, tomb or a controller, or Pressure changes into Wealth, the person is of the poor and short-lived sort.

## 风险(Risks: obstruction, broken promises, bad omens)→ `风险.md`

- **LY.MARRY.HJC.13** 世应相生相合本是吉兆，但遇动爻、日辰冲克，两边必有人说坏话，事难成；世应相冲相克本不吉，但遇动爻、日辰生合，两边必有人说好话，也可成；想知道是谁，按五类推，比如父母爻是叔伯尊长一类，外宫、他卦上的是外人。(《黄金策》)
  The Self and Other generating and combining is by nature an auspicious sign, but if a moving line or the Day clashes or controls it, there will be people on both sides speaking against it and the matter is hard to bring about; the Self and Other clashing or controlling each other is by nature not auspicious, but if a moving line or the Day generates or combines with them, there will be people on both sides speaking well of it and it can succeed; to know who they are, judge by the five kinds: the Resource line is the uncles and elders, and lines in the outer palace or another trigram are outsiders.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.28** 鬼爻不动，而兄弟又临玄武、螣蛇，来刑冲世身的，要防其中奸诈，设计骗财；如果世应生合、阴阳得位、财鬼无伤，也一定要大费钱财才能成。(《黄金策》)
  With the Pressure line quiet and Peer sitting on the Dark Tortoise or the Coiling Snake and punishing or clashing with the Self or the Body, guard against deceit and a scheme to swindle money; if the Self and Other generate and combine, yin and yang are in their places and Wealth and Pressure are undamaged, it too must cost a great deal of money to bring about.
- **LY.MARRY.HJC.30** 卦里两个父爻动，或父化父，有两个人主婚，不然就是两家的庚帖；两个鬼都动，有两家争着求婚，不然事体多变；一动一静、一旺一空则无此说；官化父、父化官，或官父都动，恐怕有争讼；文书、兄弟动，一定有口舌。(《黄金策》)
  Two Resource lines moving in the hexagram, or Resource changing into Resource, means two people presiding or two families' horoscope cards; two Pressure lines both moving means two families contending for the match, or else the affair changes often; one moving and one still, or one thriving and one Void, does not carry this reading; Pressure changing into Resource, Resource into Pressure, or both moving, may bring a lawsuit; Resource or Peer moving surely brings quarrels.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.14** 白虎临在财爻上，不要问婚事；勾陈加在官鬼位上，克夫身。凶煞临在兄弟爻上，多有争执；朱雀临身（爻），因官司而起。勾陈带阴煞，不要求女方；玄武加在财爻上，不要问亲事。(《火珠林》)
  The White Tiger on the Wealth line: do not ask about the marriage; Hook Earth added to the Pressure position controls the husband's person. A baleful star on a Peer line: many quarrels; the Vermilion Bird on the Body: a cause of public lawsuits. Hook Earth with a yin baleful star: do not seek the woman; the Dark Tortoise added to Wealth: do not ask about the match.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.17** 卦里有亡神、劫煞，都没有用；没有子孙爻、没有财爻，总是不堪。(《火珠林》)
  With the Loss star or the Robbery star present, all is useless; without an Output line and without Wealth, it is altogether unbearable.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.21** 如果遇到天寡煞、天鳏煞，要知道夫妻不会长久。(《火珠林》)
  If one meets the Heavenly Widow or Heavenly Widower baleful star, know that the couple will not last long.
- **LY.MARRY.HZL.24** 鬼爻旺而克财爻，妻子娶不成；子孙爻旺而伤丈夫，这事可疑。八纯卦动的，主生离别；五世卦、游魂卦，损伤小孩。(《火珠林》)
  A strong Pressure line controlling Wealth: the wife cannot be got; a strong Output line harming the husband: the matter is doubtful. A pure eight-palace hexagram that moves means parting alive; a fifth-generation or Wandering Soul hexagram harms the children.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.16** 兄弟爻带玄武，要防被劫、被骗；鬼爻带白虎，会遇到凶丧之事。兄弟爻、鬼爻动而带玄武，要防局中有人奸诈骗钱，即使世应相生、财官无害，也要大破费。鬼爻带白虎而动，或是还没过门，或是过门不久，一定见丧事。(《增删卜易》)
  A Peer line with the Dark Tortoise: beware of robbery and cheating; a Pressure line with the White Tiger: you will meet a death. When a Peer or Pressure line moves with the Dark Tortoise, beware of a treacherous scheme to cheat you of money; even if the Self and Other lines generate each other and Wealth and Pressure are unharmed, there will still be great expense. A moving Pressure line with the White Tiger means that, whether before she has entered the house or not long after, a funeral will surely be seen.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.21** 娶离了婚的妇人、或从娼门出来的妇人：要防祸患，用子孙爻——子孙持世、发动，没有忧患；财爻持世、子孙动来相生更吉；鬼爻或兄弟爻克世、随墓助伤、鬼爻持世，婚事虽成，祸患跟着生；财爻克世、兄弟持世，是落进别人设的局，一定人财两空。要防有人拦阻：兄弟爻动就不成，鬼爻动就有祸。兄弟持世，到底难成；财爻动来相生，不用谋划就成。六冲变冲是凶，三合、六合的吉。六合变冲，成了不长久；六冲变合，最终一定成。强抢的婚事、私下的约定，既不吉祥也不合义，不要去告问神明。(《增删卜易》)

## 家人(Parents and in-laws: whether the match harms them)→ `家人.md`

- **LY.MARRY.ZS.02** 这门婚事妨不妨父母：古书只说妻财发动才伤克父母，觉子驳它——春天占，财爻是木、父母爻是土，当令的木一定克得住休囚的土，旺和动一样能伤。他的办法是分占：先要财、官旺相，得到吉卦，再单独占一卦，问这门婚事会不会妨克父母。(《增删卜易》)
  Whether the marriage harms the parents: the old books say only a moving Wealth line harms and controls the parents, and Juezi rejects this; in a spring divination Wealth is Wood and the Resource line is Earth, and Wood in season surely controls resting Earth, so strength harms just as movement does. His method is to divine separately: first Wealth and Pressure must be strong and the hexagram auspicious, then cast a separate hexagram on whether the marriage harms or controls the parents.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.18** 问这门婚事对父母好不好：专用父母爻做用神，父母爻旺也好、衰也好，都不忌；所忌的是财爻发动，那就一定是这门婚事有妨碍。比如财化父母、父母化财、鬼化父母、父母爻动而化成墓、绝、空、破，以及回头克，动而破散，或者日月克父母爻，那是父母暮年的时候了，就算不成这门婚事，也是难免的。(《增删卜易》)
  Whether the marriage suits the parents: take the Resource line alone as the Subject; whether the Resource line is strong or weak is no matter, what is feared is a moving Wealth line, which surely means the marriage is harmful. For example Wealth changing into Resource, Resource changing into Wealth, Pressure changing into Resource, Resource moving and changing into tomb, extinction, Void or break, or into something that controls it, moving and then broken or scattered, or the Day or Month controlling the Resource line: this is the autumn of the parents' twilight years, and even if this marriage is not made it cannot be avoided.
