## LY.MARRY.HJC.04 六合则易而且吉，六冲则难而又凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六合卦一阴一阳配合成象，世应相生、财官相合，容易成而且吉；六冲卦不是纯阴就是纯阳，像两个女人同住、两个男人并处，志向合不来，难成，成了也不利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Six-Combine hexagram is one yin matched with one yang, with the Self and Other generating each other and Wealth and Pressure combining, and is easy to bring about and auspicious; a Six-Clash hexagram is pure yin or pure yang, like two women living together or two men side by side whose aims cannot agree, hard to bring about, and unfavourable even if it succeeds.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.06 世合应应合世，终成种玉之缘：世应相合是两愿之象，易成而后吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世和应相合、应和世相合，是男女两家都愿意的象，容易成，以后也吉利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self and Other combining with each other is the image of both families willing; it is easy to bring about and auspicious later as well.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.08 应生世，悦服成亲；世克应，用强劫娶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:应爻生合世爻，是女家贪图男家，容易成；世刑克应爻，男家不会早来求；世旺应衰，是倚仗富有欺负贫弱、用强抢娶。世上见鬼和螣蛇、财爻旺相、青龙得地，多半是看上了女子的姿色，想设谋娶她；世上见玄武、兄弟，是因为缺钱还债，想谋娶这个女子。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Other line generating or combining with the Self means the woman's family covets the man's and it is easy to bring about; the Self punishing or controlling the Other means the man's family would not have come early to ask; the Self thriving and the Other weak is relying on wealth to bully the poor and taking her by force. With the Self carrying Pressure and the Coiling Snake, Wealth thriving and the Azure Dragon well placed, it is probably because the girl is good-looking and someone means to scheme to marry her; with the Self carrying the Dark Tortoise or Peer, it is because the family lacks money to repay a debt and wants to take the girl in.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.14 鬼克飞爻，果信绿窗之难嫁；官合财位，方知绮席之易婚：女占男，鬼刑克世、应克世皆不成；官生合世或合财、应生合世则易成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:女方占男方，最不宜鬼爻刑克世爻，也不宜应爻克世，都主不成，男家不愿结婚；如果他有制约、我有扶助、大象又吉，也可成，但他家终究无意；若得官爻生合世爻，或合财爻，或应爻生合世爻，才主容易成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the woman's side divines about the man, the most unfavourable thing is a Pressure line punishing or controlling the Self line, and also the Other line controlling the Self; both mean it will not succeed and the man's family is unwilling; if he is restrained, I am supported and the overall image is auspicious, it can still succeed, though his family in the end has no intention; if Pressure generates or combines with the Self line, or combines with the Wealth line, or the Other generates or combines with the Self, then it is easy to bring about.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.23 世应俱空，难遂百年之连理：世空自己不想成，应空对方不想成
