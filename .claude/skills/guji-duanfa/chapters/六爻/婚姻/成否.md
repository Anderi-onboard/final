## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.01 无鬼无气·占婚：官鬼无形而有用，卦中不可无，宜静不宜动；占婚无鬼婚难成，纵成夫当夭折

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼这一爻没有形体却有用处，卦里不能没有，宜静不宜动；带吉神动也能有福，加凶煞动没有不成祸的。占婚姻而卦中没有官鬼，婚事难成，即使成了，丈夫也会早死。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure has no form but has its use; it must not be absent from the hexagram, and should be still and not move; moving with a lucky spirit it can also bring fortune, moving with a fierce star it always brings calamity. In a marriage divination with no Pressure in the hexagram, the marriage is hard to make, and even if it is made the husband will die young.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 43 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 43*

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.05 逢冲暗动·占婚：吉则暗中有人网维、凶则暗中有人破说

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六爻安静的卦，被日辰冲而动，叫暗动；暗动有吉有凶，各有各的用处，不可一概而论。占婚姻遇到暗动：吉的话暗中有人维系撮合；凶的话暗中有人说破。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a hexagram whose six lines are still, a line stirred by the Day's clash is a hidden movement; hidden movement brings good or ill, each with its own use, and cannot be judged all the same. In a marriage divination, meeting hidden movement, if lucky someone quietly holds the match together; if unlucky someone quietly talks it apart.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 102 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 102*

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.13 子孙用变·婚姻：子孙发动伤官鬼，婚姻喜美是姻缘

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙爻发动伤克官鬼：占病求医身体就痊愈；行人、买卖身体安康；婚姻喜庆美满，是好姻缘。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Output line moves it injures and controls Pressure: in illness and seeking a doctor the body recovers; travelers and trade are in good health; a marriage is happy and fair, a good match.

## LY.MARRY.HDY.01 娶妻先向财中觅，嫁夫可类鬼爻推：旺相得时成合配，休囚刑害不相宜；兄旺克妻妻不就，子旺伤夫夫有疑；八纯动者主离别，五世游魂损小儿

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:娶妻先看财爻，嫁夫也照样看鬼爻。旺相得时就能配成，休囚或被刑害就不合适。兄弟爻旺克妻，妻子成不了；子孙爻旺伤夫，丈夫有疑。八纯卦动主离别，五世卦、游魂卦损害小孩。眉批的意思相同：财、鬼旺相成合，休囚不宜，兄旺克妻，子动伤夫。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To take a wife look first at the Wealth line; to marry a husband, look likewise at the Pressure line. If it is strong and in season the match is made; if resting, trapped, punished or harmed it is unsuitable. A strong Peer line controls the wife and she cannot be had; a strong Output line harms the husband and he is in doubt. A pure eight-palace hexagram that moves means parting; a fifth-generation or Wandering Soul hexagram harms the children. The marginal comment says the same: Wealth and Pressure strong, they combine; resting or trapped, unsuitable; a strong Peer line controls the wife; Output moving harms the husband.

## LY.MARRY.HDY.08 辰日合酉、午合未、亥日寅爻六合神：用神旺相长生皆可顺，财鬼婚姻便结亲

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:日辰和用神爻六合（辰日合酉、午合未、亥日合寅），用神又旺相或长生，事情都顺；占婚姻时，妻财和官鬼爻遇上这样的六合，婚事就结成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Day six-combines the Subject line (a Chen day combines You, Wu combines Wei, a Hai day combines Yin), and the Subject is strong or in its birth state, things all go smoothly; in a marriage divination, when the Wealth and Pressure lines meet such a six-combine, the match is made.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.30 反吟卦内外不安、成而败败而成：占婚姻反复难成，允了又悔；但应爻临财动而生世，仍在生扶之月成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:反吟（内卦或外卦动变之后，每一爻的地支都变成和自己相冲的地支，例如丑亥酉变成未巳卯）主里外不安，事情成了又败、败了又成。占婚姻，就是反反复复，难以成功：先答应又反悔。书中一例：巳月戊子日占婚事对方答不答应，得恒之晋，内卦反吟，断为答应了又后悔；问将来能不能成，断为应爻是财爻、动而生世，八九月间一定成，果然酉月成婚。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Reversed Chant (after moving and changing, the branch of every line of the inner or outer trigram becomes the one that clashes with it, for example Chou-Hai-You becoming Wei-Si-Mao) means unease within and without and things that succeed and then fail, and fail and then succeed. ## LY.MARRY.ZS.33 世爻入墓只验三种（随鬼入日墓、入动墓、动而化墓），且世爻用爻休囚被克才是真；占婚姻，世旺得地或财爻有气生世，冲墓之月日成，空破休囚断难许允

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻入墓，野鹤试验后只有三种应验：世爻随鬼入日墓、世爻入动爻的墓、世爻动而化入墓。自己占的看世爻，世爻旺相的不是真入墓；替别人占的看用神，用神旺相的也不是真。只有世爻、用爻休囚被克又入墓的才是；墓被日月和动爻冲破的，也不是真，因为墓破就像网破，很容易出来。占婚姻：世爻旺、得地，或者财爻有气去生世爻，在冲开墓的月日婚事成；世爻空、破、休囚的，断定难以答应。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Of the Self line entering a tomb, only three kinds have proved true on repeated trial: the Self line following Pressure into the Day's tomb, the Self line entering a moving line's tomb, and the Self line moving and turning into a tomb. In a divination for oneself look to the Self line, and if it is strong it is not the real thing; in a divination for another look to the Subject, and if it is strong it is not the real thing either. Only when the Self or Subject line is resting or trapped and controlled and also enters a tomb is it real; if the tomb is clashed and broken by the Day, Month or a moving line it is not real either, since a broken tomb is like a torn net and one gets out easily. In divining a marriage: if the Self line is strong and well placed, or the Wealth line has qi and generates the Self line, the match is made in the month or day that clashes the tomb open; if the Self line is Void, broken, resting or trapped, judge that consent is hard to obtain.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.36 三合成局吉凶各有所宜：占婚姻、夫妇宜财官旺而合局，占久远喜庆之事宜成局；用神旺、世爻在局或局生世才吉，局克世则凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:申子辰合水局、巳酉丑合金局、寅午戌合火局、亥卯未合木局；三合有四种成局的方式，有吉有凶。占祖坟、家宅，宜父母爻合成局；占婚姻、夫妇，宜财官旺而合成局；占长久的喜庆事，宜成局，永远牢固；占官司、忧疑的事而成局，一辈子心里结着解不开的结。用三合局要看条件：用神旺就无不吉，世爻在局里最好；世爻不在局里，局也要生世爻才断吉；局克世爻，按凶推。日建、月建有一个在局里，叫局旺，更吉。三爻只有两爻动不算成局，要等以后的月日补上，叫虚一待用；一爻明动一爻暗动也算两爻动；局中有一爻逢空、破的，等填实的日月才成；有一爻入墓的，等冲开墓的日子才成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Shen-Zi-Chen combine into a Water frame, Si-You-Chou into Metal, Yin-Wu-Xu into Fire and Hai-Mao-Wei into Wood; a three-harmony frame can form in four ways, and includes ill ones and auspicious ones. For ancestral graves and the household, it suits the Resource line forming a frame; for marriage and husband and wife, it suits Wealth and Pressure being strong and forming a frame; for a long-lasting celebration, forming a frame suits and is firm forever; for a lawsuit or a matter of worry and doubt, a frame that forms leaves a knot in the heart for life. How to use a three-harmony frame depends on conditions: with a strong Subject none fails to be auspicious, and best is the Self line inside the frame; if the Self line is outside, the frame must generate the Self line before it is judged auspicious; if the frame controls the Self line, reckon it ill. If the Day or Month is inside the frame it is called a strong frame, better still. If only two of the three lines move it is not a formed frame, and one waits for a later month or day to supply the third, which is called 'leaving one empty, awaiting use'; one line moving openly and one moving in secret counts as two moving; if one line of the frame is Void or broken, it forms on the day or month that fills it; if one has entered a tomb, it forms on the day that clashes the tomb open.
