## LY.MARRY.HJC.07 欲求庚帖，不宜应动应空；若论聘仪，不可世临螣蛇、兄弟

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求庚帖（女方的八字帖），要应爻安静不空、又生合世爻，才一定应允；应爻发动、空亡或冲克世爻，都主不答应。论聘礼，世爻见螣蛇、兄弟，主男家吝啬、礼不多；应爻见它们，主女家妆奁淡薄；旺相发动，也是克妻的兆头。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To obtain the horoscope card (the woman's family's eight-character card), the Other line must be quiet, not Void, and must generate or combine with the Self, and then consent is sure; the Other moving, falling Void, or clashing with or controlling the Self all mean refusal. As to betrothal gifts, the Coiling Snake or Peer on the Self line means the man's family is stingy and the gifts few; on the Other line, the woman's dowry is meagre; thriving and moving, it is also a sign of harm to the wife.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6327 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6327*

## LY.MARRY.HJC.32 世获财生，终得妆奁于百两：财爻生合世爻又得日月动爻扶助必有妆奁；临勾陈有妆奁田、临青龙美丽、临螣蛇白虎败病是旧什物

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看妆奁要看财爻：财爻生合世爻，又有日月、动爻扶助，一定有妆奁；临勾陈，有妆奁田；临青龙，妆奁漂亮；临螣蛇、白虎、败、病等爻，是旧东西；财没有生助，不可乱说；想推多少，看生合妻财那一爻的衰旺；财的衰旺只能推女子的相貌美丑，不能推妆奁厚薄。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To judge the dowry look at the Wealth line: Wealth generating or combining with the Self and supported by the Month, Day or a moving line means there will be a dowry; on Hook Earth, dowry land; on the Azure Dragon, a handsome dowry; on the Coiling Snake, the White Tiger, or the ruined or sick lines, old household goods; if Wealth has no support, do not speak rashly; to reckon how much, judge by the strength of the line that generates or combines with Wealth; the strength of Wealth can only tell the woman's looks, not the richness of the dowry.
