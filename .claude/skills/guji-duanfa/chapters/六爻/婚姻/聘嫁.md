## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.07 父母用·占婚：父母爻为主婚人，空则必无主者及无聘礼

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻是生我的，能吉能凶，各有各的用处：遇财就伤自身，遇鬼反而增光；发动就克伤子孙、生扶兄弟。占婚姻，父母爻是主婚人：空亡了，就一定没有人主婚，也没有聘礼。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Resource line is what generates me and can bring good or ill, each with its own use: meeting Wealth it harms its own body, meeting Pressure it grows in radiance; moving, it controls and harms Output and supports Peer. In a marriage divination the Resource line is the presider at the wedding; when it is Void there is surely no presider and no betrothal gifts.

**《断易天机·散见各章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 727 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 727*

## LY.MARRY.HJC.07 欲求庚帖，不宜应动应空；若论聘仪，不可世临螣蛇、兄弟

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求庚帖（女方的八字帖），要应爻安静不空、又生合世爻，才一定应允；应爻发动、空亡或冲克世爻，都主不答应。论聘礼，世爻见螣蛇、兄弟，主男家吝啬、礼不多；应爻见它们，主女家妆奁淡薄；旺相发动，也是克妻的兆头。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To obtain the horoscope card (the woman's family's eight-character card), the Other line must be quiet, not Void, and must generate or combine with the Self, and then consent is sure; the Other moving, falling Void, or clashing with or controlling the Self all mean refusal. As to betrothal gifts, the Coiling Snake or Peer on the Self line means the man's family is stingy and the gifts few; on the Other line, the woman's dowry is meagre; thriving and moving, it is also a sign of harm to the wife.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6327 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6327*

## LY.MARRY.HJC.32 世获财生，终得妆奁于百两：财爻生合世爻又得日月动爻扶助必有妆奁；临勾陈有妆奁田、临青龙美丽、临螣蛇白虎败病是旧什物

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看妆奁要看财爻：财爻生合世爻，又有日月、动爻扶助，一定有妆奁；临勾陈，有妆奁田；临青龙，妆奁漂亮；临螣蛇、白虎、败、病等爻，是旧东西；财没有生助，不可乱说；想推多少，看生合妻财那一爻的衰旺；财的衰旺只能推女子的相貌美丑，不能推妆奁厚薄。

## LY.MARRY.YL.12 旺财与旺应生身妆奁厚实，衰世并衰官克应聘礼轻微：应为妻宅、财乃嫁资，二者旺而生世倍得妆奁；世作男家、鬼作夫主，休囚克应聘礼轻微

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:旺相的财爻和旺相的应爻来生扶自身，嫁妆丰厚；世爻衰、官爻也衰又克应爻，聘礼轻薄。应爻是妻子家，财爻是嫁资，两样旺相而生世，加倍得到嫁妆；世爻是男家，鬼爻是丈夫，如果休囚而克应爻，自然聘礼轻薄。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A strong Wealth line and a strong Other line generating the Self bring a rich and solid dowry; a weak Self line and a weak Pressure line controlling the Other line bring slight betrothal gifts. The Other line is the wife's house and Wealth the marriage portion: if both are strong and generate the Self, the dowry is doubled; the Self line is the man's family and Pressure the husband: if they are resting and trapped and control the Other line, the betrothal gifts are naturally slight.
