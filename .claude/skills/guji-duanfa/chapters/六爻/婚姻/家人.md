# 六爻 · 婚姻 · 家人

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Marriage and relationships · Parents and in-laws: whether the match harms them*

2 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.MARRY.ZS.02 都说财动伤克父母，财旺一样能克：先要财官旺相得吉卦，再另占一卦看妨不妨父母

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:这门婚事妨不妨父母：古书只说妻财发动才伤克父母，觉子驳它——春天占，财爻是木、父母爻是土，当令的木一定克得住休囚的土，旺和动一样能伤。他的办法是分占：先要财、官旺相，得到吉卦，再单独占一卦，问这门婚事会不会妨克父母。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whether the marriage harms the parents: the old books say only a moving Wealth line harms and controls the parents, and Juezi rejects this; in a spring divination Wealth is Wood and the Resource line is Earth, and Wood in season surely controls resting Earth, so strength harms just as movement does. His method is to divine separately: first Wealth and Pressure must be strong and the hexagram auspicious, then cast a separate hexagram on whether the marriage harms or controls the parents.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.18 此婚有宜于父母否：专以父母爻为用神，旺衰皆不忌，只忌财爻发动；财化父、父化财、鬼化父、父动化墓绝空破及回头克、日月克父母，则此婚有妨

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问这门婚事对父母好不好：专用父母爻做用神，父母爻旺也好、衰也好，都不忌；所忌的是财爻发动，那就一定是这门婚事有妨碍。比如财化父母、父母化财、鬼化父母、父母爻动而化成墓、绝、空、破，以及回头克，动而破散，或者日月克父母爻，那是父母暮年的时候了，就算不成这门婚事，也是难免的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whether the marriage suits the parents: take the Resource line alone as the Subject; whether the Resource line is strong or weak is no matter, what is feared is a moving Wealth line, which surely means the marriage is harmful. For example Wealth changing into Resource, Resource changing into Wealth, Pressure changing into Resource, Resource moving and changing into tomb, extinction, Void or break, or into something that controls it, moving and then broken or scattered, or the Day or Month controlling the Resource line: this is the autumn of the parents' twilight years, and even if this marriage is not made it cannot be avoided.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 11802 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 11802*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

> **按**:原文最后一句「不成此婚亦难免也」含糊：可以读作「父母暮年之事，不成婚也躲不开」；这里照字面译，没有添补。这一节后面的「乾按」是后人加的，不引。
>
> **Note:** The last clause 'even if the marriage is not made it cannot be avoided' is terse and could be read as 'a matter of the parents' old age that cannot be dodged even without the match'; it is translated literally without filling in. The 'Qian note' after this section is a later addition and is not quoted.
