## LY.MARRY.TXF.10 财动则伤克翁姑，父兴则难为子息：财爻持世必不能奉事翁姑，静还可、动则刑伤；父母发动难于子嗣，带凶杀生后有损

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻发动会伤克公婆；财爻持世，一定不能侍奉公婆，静还可以，动就是刑伤。父母爻发动，子嗣艰难；带凶煞，生后有损伤，得救还可以，逢冲则无害。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When Wealth moves it injures the parents-in-law: with the Wealth line on Self she surely cannot serve them; if still it may pass, and if it moves it means injury. When the Resource line moves, heirs come with difficulty; with a fierce star there is harm after the birth, which if rescued may pass, and if clashed does no harm.

**《天玄赋·伉俪章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 4258 行 · *Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "The chapter on husband and wife", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 4258*

## LY.MARRY.YM.18 娶疑妨害：用神旺相则我人之福，财神空破则此妇之刑；占用有二，为父母病看父母、为夫妻病娶媳看妻财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:娶亲而怀疑她会带来妨害：用神旺相，就是我家里人的福气；财神落空、破，就是这个女人带来的刑克。小注说，这类占用神有两种：为父母生病而娶妻，怀疑妻子妨害，如果父母爻旺，就是父母的福气，不是她的妨害；为夫妻生病而娶儿媳，怀疑儿媳妨害，如果妻财旺，就是儿媳的贤良，不是她的刑克。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Marrying while doubting that she will bring harm: if the Subject is strong, she is a blessing to my people; if the Wealth spirit is Void or broken, the scourge comes from this woman. The note says there are two Subjects to use in such a divination: when marrying a wife because a parent is ill, and doubting that she will do harm, if the Resource line is strong it is the parents' blessing and not harm from her; when marrying a daughter-in-law because husband and wife are ill, and doubting that she will do harm, if Wealth is strong it is the daughter-in-law's goodness and not her scourge.

**《易冒·婚姻章第五十一》原文** — 《易冒》第 1112 行 · *Yi Mao, "Chapter 51: Marriage", base text; Yi Mao, line 1112*

| 用神旺相， | 用神旺相， | if the Subject is strong, |
| 则我人之福， | 就是我家里人的福气； | she is a blessing to my people; |
| 财神空破， | 财神落空、破， | if the Wealth spirit is Void or broken, |
| 则此妇之刑。 | 就是这个女人带来的刑克。 | the scourge comes from this woman. |

> **按**:「娶媳为夫妻病」的「夫妻」书里没有说是谁，可能指家里的长辈夫妇；译文照字面写作 husband and wife。「我人」译作「我家里人」。
>
> **Note:** The book does not say whose 'husband and wife' are ill in 'marrying a daughter-in-law because husband and wife are ill'; it may mean the elder couple of the house, and it is translated literally. 'My people' is rendered as the people of my household.

## LY.MARRY.YY.07 动爻冲克父则尊长不全，冲克财则分离耗财帛奴婢无力，冲克子则无子，冲克兄则姑嫂妯娌兄弟不睦，冲克鬼则不利丈夫

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:动爻冲克父母爻，尊长不齐全；冲克财爻，分离、耗财物，奴婢不得力；冲克子孙爻，没有子，有也不多；冲克兄弟爻，姑嫂、妯娌、兄弟不和睦；冲克鬼爻，不利于丈夫。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A moving line clashing with and controlling the Resource line: the elders are not all present; Wealth: separation, loss of money and servants of no help; Output: no son, or not many if there are any; a Peer line: the sisters-in-law and brothers are at odds; Pressure: bad for the husband.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.02 都说财动伤克父母，财旺一样能克：先要财官旺相得吉卦，再另占一卦看妨不妨父母

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:这门婚事妨不妨父母：古书只说妻财发动才伤克父母，觉子驳它——春天占，财爻是木、父母爻是土，当令的木一定克得住休囚的土，旺和动一样能伤。他的办法是分占：先要财、官旺相，得到吉卦，再单独占一卦，问这门婚事会不会妨克父母。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whether the marriage harms the parents: the old books say only a moving Wealth line harms and controls the parents, and Juezi rejects this; in a spring divination Wealth is Wood and the Resource line is Earth, and Wood in season surely controls resting Earth, so strength harms just as movement does. His method is to divine separately: first Wealth and Pressure must be strong and the hexagram auspicious, then cast a separate hexagram on whether the marriage harms or controls the parents.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.18 此婚有宜于父母否：专以父母爻为用神，旺衰皆不忌，只忌财爻发动；财化父、父化财、鬼化父、父动化墓绝空破及回头克、日月克父母，则此婚有妨

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问这门婚事对父母好不好：专用父母爻做用神，父母爻旺也好、衰也好，都不忌；所忌的是财爻发动，那就一定是这门婚事有妨碍。比如财化父母、父母化财、鬼化父母、父母爻动而化成墓、绝、空、破，以及回头克，动而破散，或者日月克父母爻，那是父母暮年的时候了，就算不成这门婚事，也是难免的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whether the marriage suits the parents: take the Resource line alone as the Subject; whether the Resource line is strong or weak is no matter, what is feared is a moving Wealth line, which surely means the marriage is harmful. For example Wealth changing into Resource, Resource changing into Wealth, Pressure changing into Resource, Resource moving and changing into tomb, extinction, Void or break, or into something that controls it, moving and then broken or scattered, or the Day or Month controlling the Resource line: this is the autumn of the parents' twilight years, and even if this marriage is not made it cannot be avoided.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 11802 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 11802*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 专以父母为用神，父母或旺或衰，皆所不忌，所忌者，财爻发动，必此婚之有妨也。 | 专以父母爻为用神，父母爻旺也好衰也好，都不忌，所忌的是财爻发动，那就一定是这门婚事有妨碍。 | Take the Resource line alone as the Subject; whether the Resource line is strong or weak is no matter; what is feared is a moving Wealth line, which surely means the marriage is harmful. |
| 如财化父、父化财、鬼化父、父动化墓绝空破，及回头克，动而破散，或日月克父母，乃桑榆暮景之秋，不成此婚亦难免也。 | 比如财化父母、父母化财、鬼化父母、父母爻动而化墓、绝、空、破，以及回头克，动而破散，或者日月克父母爻，那是父母暮年的时候了，就算不成这门婚事，也是难免的。 | For example Wealth changing into Resource, Resource changing into Wealth, Pressure changing into Resource, Resource moving and changing into tomb, extinction, Void or break, or into something that controls it, moving and then broken or scattered, or the Day or Month controlling the Resource line: this is the autumn of the parents' twilight years, and even if this marriage is not made it cannot be avoided. |

>
> **Note:** The last clause 'even if the marriage is not made it cannot be avoided' is terse and could be read as 'a matter of the parents' old age that cannot be dodged even without the match'; it is translated literally without filling in. The 'Qian note' after this section is a later addition and is not quoted.
