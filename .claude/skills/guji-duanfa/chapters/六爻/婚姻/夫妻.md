## LY.MARRY.HJC.02 阴阳交错，难期琴瑟之和鸣：世阴应阳、鬼阴财阳、内阴外阳，成婚后夫妻反目

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:阴阳交错是指世阴应阳、鬼阴财阳、内阴外阳，这是反象；成婚后，丈夫欺凌妻子、妻子欺侮丈夫，整天吵架，不得和顺；如果变出的财、鬼不空，主象又安静，也还可用。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Yin and yang crossed means the Self yin and the Other yang, Pressure yin and Wealth yang, the inner trigram yin and the outer yang; this is an inverted image, and after the marriage the husband bullies the wife and the wife slights the husband, quarrelling all day and never in harmony; if the changed Wealth and Pressure are not Void and the principal image is quiet, it can still be used.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.15 财鬼如无刑害，夫妻定主和谐

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财、鬼受刑冲克害，夫妻必然不和睦；没有这种象，才能如鱼得水，到老和谐。

## LY.MARRY.HJC.19 应财世鬼，终须夫唱妇随；应鬼世财，不免夫权妻夺：世持鬼应持财是阴阳得位，反之夫权被夺，惟赘婿反吉

## LY.MARRY.HJC.20 妯娌不和，只为官爻发动；翁姑不睦，定因妻位交重：官动克兄弟则妯娌不和，财动克父母则公婆不睦

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚以兄弟为妯娌、父母为公婆；官动克兄弟，妯娌之间不利；财动克父母，公婆不和睦；如果旺相而没有制约，或父爻衰弱抵挡不住，双亲就有刑克。觉子反驳：财旺时同样克父母，不必等到动才说。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining marriage Peer stands for the brothers' wives and Resource for the parents-in-law; Pressure moving controls Peer and is unfavourable among the brothers' wives; Wealth moving controls Resource and the parents-in-law are at odds; if it is thriving with no restraint, or Resource is weak and cannot resist, the parents suffer punishment and control. Juezi objects that a thriving Wealth harms Resource just the same, and one need not wait for it to move.

>
> **Where they disagree:** The old commentary and Wang Hongxu both say 'Wealth moving controls Resource'; Juezi (Zengshan Buyi) holds that what harms the parents is Wealth being thriving, with no need to wait for it to move, and that the ancients spoke of moving only to avoid being challenged; his method is to divine first whether Wealth and Pressure are thriving and, having got a good hexagram, to divine separately whether the parents will be harmed.

## LY.MARRY.HZL.23 鬼爻持动女妨害，妇意嫌夫不相爱；财爻持动夫妨妻，终身不泰

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼爻持世而动，对女方有妨害，妻子心里嫌丈夫，夫妻不相爱；财爻持世而动，丈夫妨碍妻子，这样一辈子都不会安泰。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Pressure line that holds the Self and moves harms the woman: the wife dislikes her husband and they do not love each other; a Wealth line that holds the Self and moves means the husband hinders the wife, and all their life will not be at peace.

**《火珠林·婚姻章》原文** — 《火珠林》第 1180 行 · *Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Chapter on marriage (verse)", base text; Huozhu Lin, line 1180*

## LY.MARRY.YY.22 兄持世动、兄独发、有兄无财、财无故自空俱克妻否则分离；财旺自刑夫妻不睦终生离；财动冲克世、世应刑害夫妇无情；世动夫凌妻、应动妻欺夫；财临死墓绝胎加刑刃兼兄克刑克三妻；旺相无伤亦妻不贤

Wealth moving to clash with and control the Self line, or the Self and Other punishing and harming each other: the couple have no affection. Wealth on death, tomb, extinction or womb stages with the punishment and blade stars added and a Peer line moving to control it: three wives are punished and harmed; with generation and combination from the Month and Day she may live on with illness. Wealth on the flying line entering the Peer's branch, or the Other line holding a Peer line and moving, meeting punishment, harm, control or break from the Month and Day: she dies. Even strong and unharmed, the wife is not virtuous: she stirs up quarrels, is at odds with her sisters-in-law and not of one mind with her husband, often steals goods, hides them for herself and leaks them to another house.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.25 财动化凶失履遗簪，兄弟持世鼓盆箕踞：财旺兄衰终反目，克财财退必生离

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻动而化成凶象（化鬼、化回头克、化退神、化绝、化墓、化破、化空这一类），不管问什么，都是玉碎珠沉，有丧妻之叹。兄弟爻持世，或者兄弟爻在卦中发动，或者日月临兄弟爻来伤财，或者兄弟爻化成财爻，不管问妻问妾，都不长久。财旺兄衰，最终要反目；克财而财爻退，必定生离。兄弟爻持世而财爻旺的，克不了妻，但夫妻不和，见面如仇人；兄弟爻持世、财爻化成退神的，也是这样，不是反目就是生离。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When Wealth moves and changes into ill (into Pressure, into a returning control, a Retreating spirit, extinction, tomb, break, Void and the like), whatever is asked, the jade is shattered and the pearl sunk, with the sigh of losing a wife. A Peer line on the Self line, or a moving Peer line in the hexagram, or the Day or Month being a Peer and harming Wealth, or a Peer changing into Wealth: whether one asks about a wife or a concubine, she will not live long. Wealth strong and the Peer weak, in the end the couple fall out; Wealth controlled and retreating surely means parting alive. A Peer line on the Self line with Wealth strong cannot kill the wife, but the couple are at odds and meet like enemies; a Peer line on the Self line with Wealth changing into a Retreating spirit means the same: either falling out or parting alive.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.26 应财受制原配难偕老，动妻旺相续弦可白头；妻财克世不刑则悍，应财生世非内助即外财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦中财爻出现几个、要分正室和偏房时，以应爻上的财爻作正妻的位置：被日月、动爻冲克，或者动而化成凶象，就是原配妻子有伤；如果别的爻上的财旺相，或动而化吉，或者别的爻变出来的财旺相来生合世爻，就是续娶的妻子反而能白头到老。财爻不在应爻上，就用本卦的财当正妻，变爻里的财当再娶的。妻财克身，不是刑伤就一定是妒悍的妻；妻财克世而世爻空、破、休囚，或入墓、绝，或动而变凶，或随鬼入墓，只要碰上一条，丈夫就短命早亡；如果是旺财克世而世爻得地，虽然没有刑伤，也一定遇上悍妇。应爻上的财爻生世，不是得到贤内助，就是得到外财；财爻生合世爻也不能只按和美来断，间或有应在得到妻家财力帮助的，不然的话妻子一定善于持家。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When several Wealth lines appear and one must tell the principal wife from the secondary ones, take the Wealth on the Other line as the principal wife's position: if it is clashed or controlled by the Day, Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into ill, the wife of first marriage is harmed; if the Wealth on another line is strong or moves and changes into good, or a Wealth line changed out from another line is strong and generates or combines with the Self line, the wife of a second marriage, on the contrary, fulfils the wish of growing old together. If the Wealth line is not on the Other line, take the Wealth in the original hexagram as the principal wife and the Wealth in the changed lines as the second marriage. If the wife (Wealth) controls the Self, it is either injury or a jealous and fierce wife; if Wealth controls the Self line while the Self line is Void, broken, resting or trapped, or in tomb or extinction, or moves and changes into ill, or follows Pressure into a tomb, meeting any one of these means the husband dies young; but if a strong Wealth line controls the Self and the Self line holds its ground, there is no injury but he will surely meet a shrew. Wealth on the Other line generating the Self means either a helpful wife at home or wealth from outside; when a Wealth line generates or combines with the Self, do not judge it simply as harmony, for at times it answers as gaining the wife's family's financial help, and otherwise the wife will be good at running the household.
