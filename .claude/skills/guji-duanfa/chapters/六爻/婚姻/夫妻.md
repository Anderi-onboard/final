## LY.MARRY.BSQS.08 诸爻持世诀：父母持世夫妻相克不和同、到老要求他姓之子；兄弟持世克妻财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻持世，事情忧虑阻塞，身上带着文书和官鬼，夫妻互相克、不和睦，到老要去求别姓的儿子（过继）。兄弟爻持世，克妻财，官司之忧没了结又来，鬼旺要防口舌，自身强反而损失钱财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With the Resource line holding the Self line, matters are worrying and blocked, one carries documents and Pressure, husband and wife control each other and are not in accord, and in old age one must seek a son of another surname (by adoption). With the Peer line holding the Self line, it controls Wealth, the wife; worry over official matters is not over before more comes; with strong Pressure guard against quarrels; with the Self strong one loses money instead.

**《卜筮全书·三、诸爻持世诀》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3040 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "No. 3, rhyme on each line holding the Self", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3040*

## LY.MARRY.BSQS.11 身命章：财临阴位妻交（动）妻妾宜；世、命、应、身相刑相克，不伤自己即伤妻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻落在阴位、又动，妻妾相宜；世、命、应、身彼此又刑又克，不伤到自己就伤到妻子。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth on a yin position and moving suits wife and concubine; if the Self, Life, Other and Body punish and control one another, either oneself or one's wife is harmed.

| 阴位妻交妻妾宜。 | 阴位，妻交，妻妾宜。 | on a yin position, with the wife line moving, wife and concubine are fitting. |

**《卜筮全书·阐奥歌章·身命章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3637 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Rhyme chapter on person and fate (Chan Ao Ge Zhang)", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3637*

## LY.MARRY.BSZZ.03 何知章·妻：妻有灾（虎临兄弟动伤财）、妻有孕（青龙财临喜神）、有妻妾（内外两财旺相）、损妻房（财爻带鬼落空亡）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:「何知章」是一问一答的口诀，问的是一家人的各样事。其中四问是关于妻子的：妻子有灾，是白虎临着兄弟爻发动，伤克财爻；妻子有孕，是青龙、财爻临着喜神；这家有妻有妾，是内外卦两个财爻都旺相；这家要损妻房（丧妻），是财爻带着官鬼又落空亡。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The 'how to know' rhyme is a rhyme of questions and answers on the affairs of a household. Four of its questions concern the wife: the wife has misfortune when the White Tiger sits on a moving Peer line and injures Wealth; the wife has conceived when the Azure Dragon and Wealth arrive with a spirit of joy; the household has a wife and concubines when the two Wealth lines, inside and outside, are both strong; the household will lose its wife when the Wealth line carries Pressure and falls into Void.

**《卜筮正宗·赋文三篇·何知章》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3566 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Three rhapsodies: the "how to know" rhyme", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3566*

| 虎临兄弟动伤财； | 白虎临着兄弟爻发动，伤克财爻； | The Tiger on a Peer line, moving, injures Wealth; |
| 何知人家妻有孕？ | 怎么知道这家的妻子有孕？ | How can one know that the wife has conceived? |
| 青龙财临添喜神； | 青龙、财爻临着，再加上喜神； | the Azure Dragon and Wealth arrive with a spirit of joy added; |

> **按**:「何知章」一共问了三四十件事（父母有病、有没有子孙、讼事、财富、田地、屋宇、坟墓、六畜、口舌、盗贼……），都是家宅的事；这里只收关于妻子的四问，其余与婚姻无关，不收。「损妻房」就是丧妻。《卜筮大全》本第三问作「青龙财临天喜神」，第四问的头一个字作「休」（应是「何」字之误），《卜筮正宗》本作「添喜神」「何」。

> **Note:** The 'how to know' rhyme asks thirty or forty things (illness of the parents, whether there are children, lawsuits, wealth, land, houses, graves, livestock, quarrels, thieves and more), all about the household; only the four questions about the wife are taken here, and the rest have nothing to do with marriage. 'Loses its wife' means bereavement. The Bushi Daquan text has 'the Azure Dragon and Wealth arrive with the Tianxi spirit' for the second, and 'xiu' for the first word of the fourth question (a slip for 'he'); the Bushi Zhengzong text has 'an added spirit of joy' and 'he'.

## LY.MARRY.HDY.10 六亲持世断夫妻：父母持世夫妻不同床、老来终求他姓之子；兄弟持世克妻财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:《海底眼》的「六亲持世」诗里，和夫妻有关的两句：父母爻持世，夫妻不合、不同床（书里写的是「相合」，疑是「相克」之误），到老终究要过继别姓的儿子（小注：父母必克子孙）；兄弟爻持世，克妻财（小注：兄弟必克财）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Two lines of the Haidi Yan's poem on 'the relatives on Self' touch husband and wife: with the Resource line on Self, husband and wife do not share a bed (the book writes 'combine', probably a slip for 'control'), and in the end one must seek a son of another surname (annotation: Resource must control Output); with the Peer line on Self, Wealth is controlled (annotation: Peer must control Wealth).

**《海底眼·六亲持世》原文** — 《海底眼》第 324 行 · *Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "The relatives on Self", base text; Haidi Yan, line 324*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父母持世身忧苦（父主难宰）， | 父母爻持世，自身忧愁辛苦（小注：父母主难以主持）， | With Resource on Self one is anxious and toiling (annotation: Resource is hard to manage), |
| 身带文书及官鬼（父赖官生）， | 自身带着文书和官鬼（小注：父母靠官鬼生）， | carrying documents and Pressure (annotation: Resource depends on Pressure to be generated), |
| 夫妻相合不同床（夫妇不宜）， | 夫妻相合却不同床（小注：夫妇不相宜）， | husband and wife 'combine' yet do not share a bed (annotation: husband and wife are not suited), |
| 到者终求他姓子（父必克子）。 | 到者（疑为「到老」）终究要求别姓的儿子（小注：父母必克子）。 | at the last (probably 'in old age') one must in the end seek a son of another surname (annotation: Resource must control Output). |

**《海底眼·六亲持世》原文** — 《海底眼》第 336 行 · *Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "The relatives on Self", base text; Haidi Yan, line 336*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 兄弟持世克妻财（兄必克财）， | 兄弟爻持世，克妻财（小注：兄弟必克财）， | With a Peer line on Self, Wealth is controlled (annotation: Peer must control Wealth), |

> **按**:「相合不同床」读不通：相合而不同床，自相矛盾。《卜筮全书》的「诸爻持世诀」同一句作「夫妻相克不和同、到老要求他姓之子」（LY.MARRY.BSQS.08），这里的「相合」「到者」像是「相克」「到老」抄错了，《火珠林》所附歌诀第 945 行也作「夫妻相克不和同，到老用求他姓子」。译文照原文译，括号里注出。第 336 行后面三句讲官司、口舌、损财，不属婚姻，没有收。
>
> **Note:** 'Combine yet do not share a bed' is self-contradictory. The Bushi Quanshu's poem on the lines holding Self has the same line as 'husband and wife control each other and do not agree, in old age one must seek a son of another surname' (LY.MARRY.BSQS.08); 'combine' and 'at the last' here look like miscopies of 'control' and 'in old age'. The verse appended to the Huozhulin, at line 945, also reads 'husband and wife control each other and do not agree, in old age one must seek a son of another surname'. The translation follows the text as written, with the doubt marked in brackets. The three lines after line 336 speak of lawsuits, quarrels and loss of money, not marriage, and are not taken.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.02 阴阳交错，难期琴瑟之和鸣：世阴应阳、鬼阴财阳、内阴外阳，成婚后夫妻反目

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:阴阳交错是指世阴应阳、鬼阴财阳、内阴外阳，这是反象；成婚后，丈夫欺凌妻子、妻子欺侮丈夫，整天吵架，不得和顺；如果变出的财、鬼不空，主象又安静，也还可用。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Yin and yang crossed means the Self yin and the Other yang, Pressure yin and Wealth yang, the inner trigram yin and the outer yang; this is an inverted image, and after the marriage the husband bullies the wife and the wife slights the husband, quarrelling all day and never in harmony; if the changed Wealth and Pressure are not Void and the principal image is quiet, it can still be used.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.15 财鬼如无刑害，夫妻定主和谐

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财、鬼受刑冲克害，夫妻必然不和睦；没有这种象，才能如鱼得水，到老和谐。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth and Pressure punished, clashed, controlled or harmed means the couple will surely not be at peace; without this image they are like fish in water and are in harmony into old age.

**《黄金策·婚姻》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6358 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6358*

## LY.MARRY.ZS.24 占妻以财爻为重、应爻次之：财旺应破仍吉，应旺财破即凶；《黄金策》专以应为妻是因身命章一卦要断六亲

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占妻子：财爻为重，应爻其次，应爻只是附和。财爻旺相、应爻空破，仍按吉断；应爻旺相、财爻空破，就按凶推；应旺财旺吉上加吉，应破财破凶上加凶。《黄金策》专把应爻当妻，是因为它的身命章一卦要断父子、兄弟、妻财、官鬼各样，财爻不好又用来当妻，又拿什么爻当钱财，不得不用应爻当妻；虽然如此，它也兼看财爻，不是完全没道理。觉子补充：财爻旺、相，或者临日月，或者被日月动爻生扶，以及动而化吉，又和世爻相生相合的，占白头偕老一定白头相守，占和睦一定举案齐眉，占妻子命好不好、进门以后夫家随之兴旺；占妾占婢，指名单问的，也照这样断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining a wife, the Wealth line carries the weight and the Other line comes second, only following along. If Wealth is strong while the Other line is Void or broken, still judge it auspicious; if the Other line is strong while Wealth is Void or broken, reckon it ill; both strong is good and better, both broken is ill and worse. The Huangjin Ce takes the Other line alone as the wife because its person-and-fate chapter judges father and son, brothers, Wealth and Pressure all from one hexagram, and if Wealth were used again and again for the wife some other line would be needed for money; so it had no choice but to use the Other line for the wife, though it too looks at Wealth, so it is not wholly unreasonable. Juezi adds: where the Wealth line is strong or prospering, or is the Day or Month, or is generated and helped by the Day, Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into good, and generates or combines with the Self line, then in a divination about growing old together they will surely keep company to white hair, about harmony surely treat each other with mutual respect, and about whether the wife's fate is good, her husband's family prospers with her once she enters; for a concubine or maid asked about by name, judge the same way.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.25 财动化凶失履遗簪，兄弟持世鼓盆箕踞：财旺兄衰终反目，克财财退必生离

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻动而化成凶象（化鬼、化回头克、化退神、化绝、化墓、化破、化空这一类），不管问什么，都是玉碎珠沉，有丧妻之叹。兄弟爻持世，或者兄弟爻在卦中发动，或者日月临兄弟爻来伤财，或者兄弟爻化成财爻，不管问妻问妾，都不长久。财旺兄衰，最终要反目；克财而财爻退，必定生离。兄弟爻持世而财爻旺的，克不了妻，但夫妻不和，见面如仇人；兄弟爻持世、财爻化成退神的，也是这样，不是反目就是生离。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When Wealth moves and changes into ill (into Pressure, into a returning control, a Retreating spirit, extinction, tomb, break, Void and the like), whatever is asked, the jade is shattered and the pearl sunk, with the sigh of losing a wife. A Peer line on the Self line, or a moving Peer line in the hexagram, or the Day or Month being a Peer and harming Wealth, or a Peer changing into Wealth: whether one asks about a wife or a concubine, she will not live long. Wealth strong and the Peer weak, in the end the couple fall out; Wealth controlled and retreating surely means parting alive. A Peer line on the Self line with Wealth strong cannot kill the wife, but the couple are at odds and meet like enemies; a Peer line on the Self line with Wealth changing into a Retreating spirit means the same: either falling out or parting alive.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.26 应财受制原配难偕老，动妻旺相续弦可白头；妻财克世不刑则悍，应财生世非内助即外财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦中财爻出现几个、要分正室和偏房时，以应爻上的财爻作正妻的位置：被日月、动爻冲克，或者动而化成凶象，就是原配妻子有伤；如果别的爻上的财旺相，或动而化吉，或者别的爻变出来的财旺相来生合世爻，就是续娶的妻子反而能白头到老。财爻不在应爻上，就用本卦的财当正妻，变爻里的财当再娶的。妻财克身，不是刑伤就一定是妒悍的妻；妻财克世而世爻空、破、休囚，或入墓、绝，或动而变凶，或随鬼入墓，只要碰上一条，丈夫就短命早亡；如果是旺财克世而世爻得地，虽然没有刑伤，也一定遇上悍妇。应爻上的财爻生世，不是得到贤内助，就是得到外财；财爻生合世爻也不能只按和美来断，间或有应在得到妻家财力帮助的，不然的话妻子一定善于持家。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When several Wealth lines appear and one must tell the principal wife from the secondary ones, take the Wealth on the Other line as the principal wife's position: if it is clashed or controlled by the Day, Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into ill, the wife of first marriage is harmed; if the Wealth on another line is strong or moves and changes into good, or a Wealth line changed out from another line is strong and generates or combines with the Self line, the wife of a second marriage, on the contrary, fulfils the wish of growing old together. If the Wealth line is not on the Other line, take the Wealth in the original hexagram as the principal wife and the Wealth in the changed lines as the second marriage. If the wife (Wealth) controls the Self, it is either injury or a jealous and fierce wife; if Wealth controls the Self line while the Self line is Void, broken, resting or trapped, or in tomb or extinction, or moves and changes into ill, or follows Pressure into a tomb, meeting any one of these means the husband dies young; but if a strong Wealth line controls the Self and the Self line holds its ground, there is no injury but he will surely meet a shrew. Wealth on the Other line generating the Self means either a helpful wife at home or wealth from outside; when a Wealth line generates or combines with the Self, do not judge it simply as harmony, for at times it answers as gaining the wife's family's financial help, and otherwise the wife will be good at running the household.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.29 爻逢六合已是吉兆，动而又变六合终始佳祥：占婚姻白头相守；宜合则吉不宜合则凶，用神旺临日月吉上加吉，用神受克更为凶兆

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:爻逢六合，已经是吉兆；动了又变成六合，从头到尾都好。占婚姻，就是白头相守。合是吉是凶，要看这件事适不适合聚合：婚姻、合伙、功名、钱财、兄弟同居这类事遇合是吉，诉讼、冤仇、犹疑的怪事、生产遇合反而不利（怀孕倒是安稳）。以上各种占问，用神旺又临日月的，吉上加吉；用神被克，更是凶兆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line meeting Six-combine is already an auspicious sign, and if it moves and changes into a Six-combine again it is fortunate from start to finish. For a marriage that is keeping company to white hair. Whether a combination is good depends on whether the matter suits gathering: marriage, partnership, office, money and brothers living together are helped by a combination, whereas a lawsuit, a grudge, a doubtful or strange matter and childbirth are hindered by it (pregnancy, however, is secure). In all of these, where the Subject is strong and is the Day or Month itself it is good and better still; where the Subject is controlled it is an even worse omen.
