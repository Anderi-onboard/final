## LY.MARRY.HJC.03 内外互摇，定见家庭之挠括：婚姻卦宜安静；财动不和公婆，鬼动不和妯娌，父动不和子侄，兄动不和夫妻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚姻，卦宜安静，安静则家庭和睦、没有争斗；如果财动，和公婆不和；鬼动，和妯娌不和；父动，和子侄不和；兄动，夫妻不和；再加月建、日辰，不但不和，还有克制。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining marriage the hexagram should be quiet, and when quiet the household is at peace without strife; if Wealth moves, discord with the parents-in-law; Pressure moving, with the brothers' wives; Resource moving, with the children and nephews; Peer moving, between husband and wife; with the Month or Day added there is not only discord but also control.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.12 世应化空，始成而终悔：世动生合应、应动生合世皆易成，怕变入空亡；日辰动爻扶助则悔而可成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世动生合应爻，男家愿成；应动生合世爻，女家愿嫁；都是容易成的象；只怕变入空亡，一定有退悔的意思；如果有日辰、动爻扶助，虽悔也可成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self moving to generate or combine with the Other means the man's family is willing; the Other moving to generate or combine with the Self means the woman's family is willing to give her; both are images of easy success; only turning into Void is feared, which means a wish to withdraw and regret; if the Day or a moving line supports it, it can still succeed despite regret.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.27 财化财，一举两得；鬼化鬼，四复三番：财化财有赠嫁，财化子有带来的幼子；鬼化鬼凡事反复

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占婚遇财化财，一定有仆人陪嫁（赠嫁）；财化子，有小孩带来（带幼聘）；化子逢空虽来不长寿；化财遇冲以后会走失；退神逢冲，以后背夫改嫁或回娘家。鬼化鬼，不论进退神，凡事反复不定，占婚一定不容易，即使可成也迟滞，女家多有变化，缓急全听对方。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a marriage divination, Wealth changing into Wealth surely means servants come with the bride as a gift; Wealth changing into Output means a small child is brought along, called a betrothal with a child; changing into Output and meeting Void, the child comes but will not live long; changing into Wealth and meeting a clash, she will later run away; changing into a retreating spirit and meeting a clash, she will later leave her husband and remarry, or go back to her family. Pressure changing into Pressure, whether advancing or retreating, means everything is reversed and unsettled, and the marriage surely will not be easy, and even if it can succeed it is delayed; the woman's family will change its mind often, and easy or hard, slow or quick, all is at its pleasure.

## LY.MARRY.YM.24 得六冲叫卦冲无往而不散；卦化六冲叫变冲，占事无后，约婚而不终、结义而不信、营求而不遂

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占得六冲卦，叫卦冲，无论到哪里都散，话多乱、事多变、行多违、谋多阻。卦化成六冲，叫变冲：占事没有下文，约婚不能到底，结义不守信，谋求不能如愿。卦冲叫前冲，变冲叫后冲；如果前面是六合、后面是六冲，更加厉害。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A divination that gives a six-clash hexagram is called a hexagram clash: wherever it goes it scatters; words are muddled, matters change often, actions go against, plans meet obstruction. A hexagram that changes into a six-clash hexagram is called a change-clash: a matter divined has no lasting result, a betrothal is not carried through, sworn brotherhood lacks good faith, a pursuit is not fulfilled. The hexagram clash is the front clash and the change-clash the rear clash; if the front is six-combine and the rear six-clash it is worse still.

**《易冒·合冲章第三十四》原文** — 《易冒》第 578 行 · *Yi Mao, "Chapter 34: Combination and clash", base text; Yi Mao, line 578*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 筮得六冲，其名卦冲，无往而不散也，言多乱，事多变，行多违，谋多阻也。 | 占得六冲卦，名叫卦冲，无论去哪里都散，话多乱，事多变，行多违，谋多阻。 | If the divination gives a six-clash hexagram, it is called a hexagram clash: wherever it goes it scatters; words are muddled, matters change often, actions go against, plans meet obstruction. |
| 筮化六冲，其名变冲，占事主无后也，约婚而不终，结义而不信，营求而不遂也。 | 卦化成六冲，名叫变冲：占事没有下文，约婚不能到底，结义不守信，谋求不能如愿。 | If the hexagram changes into a six-clash hexagram, it is called a change-clash: for a matter divined there is no lasting result, a betrothal is not carried through, sworn brotherhood lacks good faith, and a pursuit is not fulfilled. |
| 卦冲谓之前冲，变冲谓之后冲，倘前六合而后六冲，又其甚已。 | 卦冲叫做前冲，变冲叫做后冲，如果前面六合而后面六冲，就更加厉害了。 | The hexagram clash is called the front clash and the change-clash the rear clash; if the front is six-combine and the rear six-clash, it is worse still. |

The passage speaks of all kinds of matters, and marriage is only the clause about a betrothal not carried through.

## LY.MARRY.YM.25 六合的卦被日辰冲害在世应上叫合冲：功之几成而败、婚之几从而违；合处逢冲，财鬼冲害忌婚姻，冲在世应上者稍轻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六合卦而世爻或应爻被日辰冲害的，叫合冲，好比功业快成却败了，财已到手却失了，婚事快要答应却违了，谋划快要成功却变了，不可居守。不叫「冲合」的缘故是：已经败了就不能再成，已经解开了就不能再交合，已经失去了就不能再得到，即使得到也是应在别处了。另外爻的合处遇到冲：财爻、官鬼被冲害，忌婚姻；父母、官爻被冲害，忌功名；子孙、妻财被冲害，忌谋生；但比起冲害落在世应上的就稍轻，所以合处遇冲，前人专门以世应为主。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A six-combine hexagram whose Self or Other line is clashed or harmed by the Day is called a combination-clash: like a feat nearly done that fails, wealth once gained that is lost, a marriage nearly agreed that goes against, a scheme nearly achieved that changes; one cannot rest on it. It is not called a 'clash-combination' because what has been spoiled cannot then be made, what has come apart cannot then be joined, and what has been lost cannot then be regained, and even if regained it answers elsewhere. Besides this there is a line's combination meeting a clash: Wealth and Pressure clashed or harmed is bad for marriage, Resource and Pressure clashed or harmed for office and fame, Output and Wealth clashed or harmed for making a living; but these are lighter than the clash falling on Self and Other, so for a combination meeting a clash the worthies of old looked chiefly at Self and Other.

'Wealth-Pressure', 'Resource-Pressure' and 'Output-Wealth' are the book's abbreviations for pairs of relations.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.15 财化财未必两度婚，鬼化鬼难相守百年：男家占女财爻重叠旺相生合世多贤妻美妾，财化财或双娶、婢妾同来、妆奁丰；女家占男财化财生世聘礼丰厚，鬼化鬼必夫亡或反复灾非

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财化财，不一定是结两次婚；鬼化鬼，很难说能相守百年。《黄金策》说「财爻叠叠，重作新人」，作者屡次试过：男家占女家，财爻重叠、旺相而生合世爻的，多主有贤妻美妾；财化财的，或者应在娶两个，或者应在婢女妾室一起来，或者妻妾众多，或者嫁妆丰厚；只怕爻中兄弟动，或者日月冲克财爻，那样免不了再办喜事。女家占男家，遇到财化财、财爻重叠而生世的，聘礼一定丰厚；不宜鬼动化鬼，那样必主丈夫亡故；鬼爻重叠克世，不是争婚夺娶，就一定反复不定、有灾祸是非。男方占遇到这种情况也主有变更，克世的就该停手，世爻化鬼的更该停手。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth changing into Wealth does not necessarily mean two marriages; Pressure changing into Pressure can hardly be said to mean keeping company for a hundred years. The Huangjin Ce says 'with Wealth lines upon Wealth lines, one becomes a bridegroom anew', but the author has tried it many times: when the man's family divines about the woman and the Wealth line is doubled, strong, and generates or combines with the Self line, it mostly means a virtuous wife and a fair concubine; where Wealth changes into Wealth, it may answer as taking two brides, or a maid or concubine coming along, or many wives and concubines, or a rich dowry; only beware a moving Peer line, or the Day and Month clashing and controlling Wealth, for then the wedding candles cannot be avoided again. When the woman's family divines about the man and gets Wealth changing into Wealth, with doubled Wealth lines generating the Self, the betrothal gifts will surely be rich; it is not good if Pressure moves and changes into Pressure, which surely means the husband's death, and doubled Pressure lines controlling the Self mean, if not a fight over the marriage or a snatched bride, then surely repeated disaster and dispute. A man who meets this also faces change; where it controls the Self one should stop, and where the Self line changes into Pressure one should stop all the more.

> **Where they disagree:** The Huangjin Ce: with Wealth lines upon Wealth lines, one becomes a bridegroom anew (remarriage; see LY.MARRY.HJC.24).
