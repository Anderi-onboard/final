## LY.MARRY.HJC.13 六合而动象刑伤，必多破阻；世冲而日辰扶助，当有吹嘘：动爻日辰冲克则有人破阻、生合则有人吹嘘

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世应相生相合本是吉兆，但遇动爻、日辰冲克，两边必有人说坏话，事难成；世应相冲相克本不吉，但遇动爻、日辰生合，两边必有人说好话，也可成；想知道是谁，按五类推，比如父母爻是叔伯尊长一类，外宫、他卦上的是外人。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self and Other generating and combining is by nature an auspicious sign, but if a moving line or the Day clashes or controls it, there will be people on both sides speaking against it and the matter is hard to bring about; the Self and Other clashing or controlling each other is by nature not auspicious, but if a moving line or the Day generates or combines with them, there will be people on both sides speaking well of it and it can succeed; to know who they are, judge by the five kinds: the Resource line is the uncles and elders, and lines in the outer palace or another trigram are outsiders.

## LY.MARRY.HJC.30 两父齐兴，必有争盟之象；双官俱动，斯为竞娶之端：两个父爻动主有两人主婚或两家庚帖，两个官爻动主两家争娶；官化父、父化官恐有争讼

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里两个父爻动，或父化父，有两个人主婚，不然就是两家的庚帖；两个鬼都动，有两家争着求婚，不然事体多变；一动一静、一旺一空则无此说；官化父、父化官，或官父都动，恐怕有争讼；文书、兄弟动，一定有口舌。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Two Resource lines moving in the hexagram, or Resource changing into Resource, means two people presiding or two families' horoscope cards; two Pressure lines both moving means two families contending for the match, or else the affair changes often; one moving and one still, or one thriving and one Void, does not carry this reading; Pressure changing into Resource, Resource into Pressure, or both moving, may bring a lawsuit; Resource or Peer moving surely brings quarrels.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.16 兄临玄武防劫骗，鬼临白虎遇凶丧：兄鬼动临玄武防局中奸诈骗财，世应相生财官无害也要大破费；鬼临白虎动，未过门或过门不久必见丧事

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟爻带玄武，要防被劫、被骗；鬼爻带白虎，会遇到凶丧之事。兄弟爻、鬼爻动而带玄武，要防局中有人奸诈骗钱，即使世应相生、财官无害，也要大破费。鬼爻带白虎而动，或是还没过门，或是过门不久，一定见丧事。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Peer line with the Dark Tortoise: beware of robbery and cheating; a Pressure line with the White Tiger: you will meet a death. When a Peer or Pressure line moves with the Dark Tortoise, beware of a treacherous scheme to cheat you of money; even if the Self and Other lines generate each other and Wealth and Pressure are unharmed, there will still be great expense. A moving Pressure line with the White Tiger means that, whether before she has entered the house or not long after, a funeral will surely be seen.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.21 取离妇跳娼妇：防患者用子孙，防拦阻者看兄鬼；六冲变冲凶，三合六合吉；强夺婚、私约不祥不义，勿告神

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:娶离了婚的妇人、或从娼门出来的妇人：要防祸患，用子孙爻——子孙持世、发动，没有忧患；财爻持世、子孙动来相生更吉；鬼爻或兄弟爻克世、随墓助伤、鬼爻持世，婚事虽成，祸患跟着生；财爻克世、兄弟持世，是落进别人设的局，一定人财两空。要防有人拦阻：兄弟爻动就不成，鬼爻动就有祸。兄弟持世，到底难成；财爻动来相生，不用谋划就成。六冲变冲是凶，三合、六合的吉。六合变冲，成了不长久；六冲变合，最终一定成。强抢的婚事、私下的约定，既不吉祥也不合义，不要去告问神明。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Marrying a woman who has left her husband, or one who has left the brothel: to guard against harm, use the Output line; with the Output line on the Self line or moving there is nothing to worry about; with the Wealth line on the Self line and a moving Output line generating it, better still; if Pressure or a Peer line controls the Self line, or there is following into a tomb or aiding of harm, or Pressure holds the Self line, although the marriage is made calamity follows; if Wealth controls the Self line and a Peer line holds it, you have fallen into someone's trap and will lose both the person and the money. To guard against obstruction: if a Peer line moves it will not succeed, and if Pressure moves there will be disaster. With a Peer line on the Self line it is in the end hard to accomplish; with Wealth moving to generate, it comes about without scheming. A Six-clash changing into a clash is ill, a three-harmony or Six-combine is auspicious; a Six-combine changing into a clash is made but does not last, a Six-clash changing into a combine will surely succeed in the end. Forcing a marriage or fulfilling a secret pact is neither auspicious nor righteous; do not put it to the spirits.

> **Note:** The word 'tiao' ('to leap') in the section title is uncertain (it may mean leaving the trade, being bought out, or something else) and no other book has a section of the same name; it is translated as a woman who has left prostitution without claiming more. The section ends with the sentence 'Sending away a concubine or wife is not what one wants...', which looks like the start of the next section and is not included.
