## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.04 随官入墓·占婚：世随鬼入墓，男家贫乏、男财不备；三种入墓不问占何事皆非吉兆

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:随官入墓有三种：卦身随鬼入墓、世爻随鬼入墓、本命随鬼入墓；这三种入墓，不管占什么事都不是吉兆。占婚姻遇到世爻随鬼入墓，男方家里贫乏，男方的钱财没有准备好。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Following Pressure into the tomb comes in three kinds: the Body, the Self line, or the Life following Pressure into the tomb; whatever the matter divined, none of the three is a lucky sign. In a marriage divination, if Self follows Pressure into the tomb, the man's family is poor and the man's wealth is not ready.

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.06 助鬼伤身·占婚：鬼克世而妻财动来生助，纵成其妇也不贤不孝公姑、搬唆夫主、割户分门、不和兄弟

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:生助官鬼的只有妻财，不宜发动，动了就把弱的鬼变成旺鬼。卦中鬼爻克身世，妻财再动来生助，凶更重；没有财，凶有限；两个财都动，大凶。占婚姻遇到：世爻是男家，官鬼是丈夫，鬼克世是自己克自家，本来就不好；再遇财爻发动、生助鬼爻，即使成了，那个妻子也一定不贤、不孝敬公婆、挑唆丈夫、分家另过、不和兄弟。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The only line that generates and supports Pressure is Wealth, and it should not move, for moving turns a weak ghost into a strong one. When the Pressure line in a hexagram controls the Body or Self and Wealth also moves to generate and support it, the ill is all the worse; with no Wealth the ill is limited; with two Wealth lines both moving the calamity is very great. In a marriage divination: Self is the man's family and Pressure is the husband, so Pressure controlling Self is a house controlling itself, which is not fine to begin with; if Wealth then moves and generates and supports Pressure, even if the match is made the wife will surely be unworthy, unfilial to her parents-in-law, egg her husband on, split the household and be at odds with his brothers.

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.08 兄弟用·占婚：兄弟为费财、诡诈、虚妄；大象可成发动不过破财，不可成则临世发动他日伤妻，间爻动则媒人诡诈

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟爻是与我五行相同的，不能带来福，也不至于大凶，不过是破败、克剥、阻滞之神；怕官鬼发动来制它，喜欢父母兴旺让它有依靠；发动就伤克妻财、扶持子孙。占婚姻，兄弟爻是耗费钱财、诡诈、虚妄：大象能成的，发动不过是破财；不能成的，兄弟临世发动，日后必伤妻；在间爻上发动，一定是媒人诡诈虚妄。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Peer is what shares my element; it cannot bring fortune and cannot bring great ill, being nothing but the spirit of ruin, plunder and obstruction; it fears Pressure moving to hold it in check and is glad when Resource thrives and gives it support; moving, it injures and controls Wealth and supports Output. In a marriage divination Peer means expense, trickery and falsehood: if the overall image allows the match, its moving is no more than a loss of money; if it cannot be made, Peer moving on Self surely means the wife will be harmed some day, and moving on a between line means the go-between is tricky and false.

## LY.MARRY.DYTJ.15 兄弟用变·婚姻：兄弟交重克了财，买婢求妻事不谐

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟爻交重克了财：病人难愈、灾未离；考试雷同是大忌；官非、暗贼耗钱财；若带吉神有助，出路的行人还没来，做买卖折本；买婢女、求妻，事情不和谐。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Peer line moves and controls Wealth: a sick person is hard to cure and the calamity has not left; in exams sameness with others is a great taboo; lawsuits and hidden thieves drain money. If it carries a lucky spirit and helps, a traveler on the road has not yet come, trade loses its capital, and buying a maid or seeking a wife does not go smoothly.

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻化成官鬼，男方的尊长阻隔；父母爻化成财爻，女方的尊长阻隔。父母爻空，没有主婚的人，只能苟且成合。父母爻克世应，父母阻挠；兄弟爻冲世应，兄弟阻挠；兄弟爻加煞而动，一定争斗。鬼爻化鬼爻，男家反复不定；兄弟爻化兄弟爻，遇到阻碍才能成。两个父母爻一起发动，主婚的不止一人。财官动而相合，先私通后娶（自刑的应验）。两个官爻一起动，两家来求娶；两个鬼爻克应爻，女方许给了两家。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Resource changing into Pressure: the man's elders obstruct; Resource changing into Wealth: the woman's elders obstruct. With the Resource line Void there is no one to preside at the wedding, and the union is made irregularly. The Resource line controlling the Self and Other lines: the parents obstruct; a Peer line clashing the Self and Other: the brothers obstruct; a Peer line with a baleful star and moving: quarrels are certain. Pressure changing into Pressure: the man's family goes back and forth; a Peer line changing into a Peer line: made only after meeting obstruction. Two Resource lines moving together: more than one person presides at the wedding. Wealth and Pressure moving and combining: illicit relations first, marriage after (it proves true where there is self-punishment). Two Pressure lines moving together: two families ask for the bride; two Pressure lines controlling the Other line: the woman is promised to two families.

## LY.MARRY.ZS.21 取离妇跳娼妇：防患者用子孙，防拦阻者看兄鬼；六冲变冲凶，三合六合吉；强夺婚、私约不祥不义，勿告神

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:娶离了婚的妇人、或从娼门出来的妇人：要防祸患，用子孙爻——子孙持世、发动，没有忧患；财爻持世、子孙动来相生更吉；鬼爻或兄弟爻克世、随墓助伤、鬼爻持世，婚事虽成，祸患跟着生；财爻克世、兄弟持世，是落进别人设的局，一定人财两空。要防有人拦阻：兄弟爻动就不成，鬼爻动就有祸。兄弟持世，到底难成；财爻动来相生，不用谋划就成。六冲变冲是凶，三合、六合的吉。六合变冲，成了不长久；六冲变合，最终一定成。强抢的婚事、私下的约定，既不吉祥也不合义，不要去告问神明。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Marrying a woman who has left her husband, or one who has left the brothel: to guard against harm, use the Output line; with the Output line on the Self line or moving there is nothing to worry about; with the Wealth line on the Self line and a moving Output line generating it, better still; if Pressure or a Peer line controls the Self line, or there is following into a tomb or aiding of harm, or Pressure holds the Self line, although the marriage is made calamity follows; if Wealth controls the Self line and a Peer line holds it, you have fallen into someone's trap and will lose both the person and the money. To guard against obstruction: if a Peer line moves it will not succeed, and if Pressure moves there will be disaster. With a Peer line on the Self line it is in the end hard to accomplish; with Wealth moving to generate, it comes about without scheming. A Six-clash changing into a clash is ill, a three-harmony or Six-combine is auspicious; a Six-combine changing into a clash is made but does not last, a Six-clash changing into a combine will surely succeed in the end. Forcing a marriage or fulfilling a secret pact is neither auspicious nor righteous; do not put it to the spirits.

> **Note:** The word 'tiao' ('to leap') in the section title is uncertain (it may mean leaving the trade, being bought out, or something else) and no other book has a section of the same name; it is translated as a woman who has left prostitution without claiming more.
