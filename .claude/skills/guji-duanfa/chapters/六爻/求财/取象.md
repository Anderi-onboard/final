## LY.WEALTH.CA.10 地头克身，事未周全，阴爻主女人滞留；身爻克地头，满载荣归

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:地头（所到之地）克身，事情没办周全，阴爻则是女人把你留住；身爻克地头，就能满船载宝、荣耀而归。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The place you go to controlling the Body, matters are not fully done, and a yin line means a woman keeps you; the Body line controlling the place brings you home in glory with a boat full of treasure.

**《阐奥歌章·求财买卖章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3856 行 · *Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on seeking wealth and trade", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3856*

**《阐奥歌章·求财买卖章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3857 行 · *Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on seeking wealth and trade", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3857*

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.01 求财六爻各主一样：初己身、二伴侣、三行李、四车门、五道路、六店舍

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.11 买卖六爻各主一样：初货物、二己身、三伴侣、四中途、五店舍、六地头

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.16 求财买卖往哪个方向：避开本宫绝命和墓绝之方，走生气、长生和世爻旺处

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求财买卖往哪个方向走吉：本宫的绝命方、入墓的方位、加上鬼的墓在同一边，墓绝的方位最凶，想经营就避开这条路；生气、长生的方位吉，世爻旺的地方好，八卦的方位都照这个定，终身没有病也没有灾。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Which direction is auspicious for seeking wealth and trade: the Life-severing direction of the home palace, the tomb direction, and the tomb of Pressure on the same side, the directions of tomb and severance are the worst, and one who means to run a business avoids that road; the directions of Vital Qi and Long Life are auspicious, a place where the Self is thriving is good, and the directions of the eight trigrams are all fixed this way; then one has no illness and no calamity for life.

**《断易天机·占买卖第二十》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1330 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 20: Divining for buying and selling", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 1330*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 本宫绝命及入墓， | 本宫的绝命方，以及入墓的方位， | The Life-severing direction of the home palace, and the tomb direction, |
| 兼之鬼墓同厢顾， | 再加上鬼的墓也在同一边， | together with the tomb of Pressure on the same side: |
| 墓绝之方最为凶， | 墓、绝的方位最凶， | the directions of tomb and severance are the worst; |
| 若欲经营避此路。 | 想经营就要避开这条路。 | if you mean to run a business, avoid that road. |
| 生气长生是吉方， | 生气、长生的方位是吉方， | The directions of Vital Qi and Long Life are the auspicious ones; |
| 世爻旺处即为良， | 世爻旺的地方就是好的； | where the Self is thriving is good; |
| 八卦之方同此定， | 八卦的方位都照这个来定， | the directions of the eight trigrams are all fixed this way, |
| 终身无病亦无秧。 | 终身没有病，也没有灾。 | and for life there is no illness and no calamity. |

> **按**:「绝命」「生气」是八宅游年里的方位名；本书没有在这一条里讲怎么排，本库不补。「无秧」的「秧」可能是「殃」的误字，原文如此。
>
> **Note:** 'Life-severing' and 'Vital Qi' are direction names from the house-facing system; this passage does not explain how they are laid out and this library does not add it. 'Yang' in the last line may be a miscopy of 'calamity'; the original reads as given.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.36 来情·冲克妻财爻：所问多是是非、占财、铺店丝绵买物，或阴人和合事，金银铜铁器交易

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看来意：妻财爻被冲克，所问的多是是非、占财、开铺店、丝绵、买东西，也可能是有女人参与的合作事，或者金银铜铁器的交易。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To tell what the visit is about: when the Wealth line is clashed or controlled, the matter asked is often disputes, wealth, shops, silk floss and buying things, and it may be a partnership involving a woman, or a trade in gold, silver, copper and iron ware.

**《断易天机·来情章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 944 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "What the visit is about", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 944*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 冲克妻财爻： | 冲克妻财爻： | The Wealth line clashed or controlled: |
| 冲克即是非占财， | 冲克它，就是：是非、占财， | a clash or control means: disputes, and divining wealth, |
| 铺店丝绵买物来， | 铺店、丝绵、买东西的事， | shops, silk floss, the buying of things, |
| 或有阴人和合事， | 或者有女人参与的合作事， | or a joint matter involving a woman, |
| 金银铜铁器和交。 | 金银铜铁器的交易。 | the trading of gold, silver, copper and iron ware. |

> **按**:「冲克即是非占财」这一句读法不定：可读作「冲克即是：是非、占财」，这里就是这样译的；但《海底眼》同一句作「冲妻即是系占财」（见 HDY.13），意思是「关系到占财」，那样读更顺。「非」和「系」哪一个是原字，书里没有别的证据，本库不改原文，也不裁。「阴人」原指女子，「和合事」指结合、合作的事，这里译得宽。
>
> **Note:** How to read 'a clash or control is disputes and divining wealth' is not settled: it can be read as a list, which is how it is translated here; but the Haidi Yan has the same line as 'a clash on Wealth is tied to divining wealth' (see HDY.13), which reads more smoothly. The books give no further evidence about whether 'disputes' or 'tied to' is the original character, and this library neither changes the original nor decides. 'A yin person' means a woman and 'union' is rendered loosely as a joint matter.

他本同文、异文(程序比对,只比本文):

- 《海底眼》:异文(相似 0.86) 第 550 行:或有阴人成合事

## LY.WEALTH.YY.06 得何财：看财入何宫、属何支

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问得什么财：看财爻落在哪个卦宫（乾金玉珠宝骡马、坤田土布帛五谷牛、震竹木茶、巽山林园圃、坎池沼鱼盐酒醋猪、离窗灶炉冶书吏、艮坟石山路、兑姬妾缸瓷羊五金），属哪个地支（子池荡石灰布帛……亥楼台仓库绸绢鱼介猪）。野鹤认为这类以五行定物的说法屡试不验（见 LY.WEALTH.ZS.26）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** What kind of money: see which palace the Wealth line lies in (Qian gold, jade, jewels, mules and horses; Kun land, cloth, grain, cattle; Zhen bamboo, timber, tea; Xun forests and gardens; Kan ponds, fish and salt, wine and vinegar, pigs; Li stoves, furnaces, clerks' work; Gen graves, stone, mountain paths; Dui concubines, jars, sheep, metals) and which branch it falls on (Zi ponds, lime, cloth ... Hai towers, granaries, silk, fish, pigs). Yehe held such element-based readings of goods unreliable (see LY.WEALTH.ZS.26).

**《易隐·求财占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2878 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining for wealth", base text; Yi Yin, line 2878*

## LY.WEALTH.YY.17 世下伏财者，彼先有物欲抵此债

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:讨债时世爻下伏财：对方有东西想拿来抵债；按财所在的卦宫和地支推是什么东西（乾马金银杯镜冠玉、兑羊五金缸坛、离绸缎书画帘屏、震鼓竹茶笋、坎酒醋鱼盐猪、艮狗石瓷瓜叶、坤牛马田土布帛锅；子池荡大豆珠玉……亥猪伞笠笔墨）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In collecting a debt, Wealth hidden under the Self line means the debtor has goods to offer instead; tell what they are from the palace and branch of that Wealth (Qian horses, gold and silver, mirrors, jade; Dui sheep, metalware, jars; Li silks, paintings, screens; Zhen drums, bamboo, tea; Kan wine, vinegar, fish, salt, pigs; Gen dogs, stone, porcelain; Kun oxen, land, cloth, pots; Zi ponds, soybeans, jewels ... Hai pigs, umbrellas, brushes and ink).
