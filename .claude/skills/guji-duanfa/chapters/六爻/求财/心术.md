## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.02 辨君子小人求财：小人求财得乾卦，心里无良，行劫害之事

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:辨君子和小人求财：小人求财，占到乾卦，心里没有善念，做的是抢夺伤害人的事。后面几句按巳酉丑、寅午戌、申子辰三组，说泰卦里的劫杀和驿马。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** On telling a gentleman from a petty person seeking wealth: a petty person who gets Qian has no goodness in the heart and does robbery and harm. The remaining lines take the three groups Si-You-Chou, Yin-Wu-Xu and Shen-Zi-Chen and speak of the Robbery star and the Post Horse in Tai.

**《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1260 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 1260*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 小人求财得乾卦， | 小人求财，占到乾卦， | A petty person seeking wealth who gets the Qian hexagram: |
| 心底无良行劫害（1）， | 心里没有善念，做的是抢夺伤害人的事。 | no goodness in the heart, he does robbery and harm. |
| 巳酉丑兮卜得泰， | 逢巳、酉、丑而占到泰卦， | Meeting Si, You and Chou and getting Tai: |
| 寅午戌兮劫杀害， | 逢寅、午、戌，劫杀带来祸害； | meeting Yin, Wu and Xu, the Robbery star brings harm; |
| 申子辰兮驿马寅， | 逢申、子、辰，驿马在寅； | meeting Shen, Zi and Chen, the Post Horse is at Yin; |
| 财与寅兮六合亥。 | 财与寅，在亥上六合。 | Wealth and Yin form a six-harmony at Hai. |

> **按**:这首歌诀省得很厉害，后四句怎么读，书里没有解释。整理本后面有今人写的注，把最后一句读作「贵人求财」；那段注文不是古籍，本库不引，也不跟着下结论，只照字译。原文里的「（1）」是整理本给那段注文编的号。
>
> **Note:** The verse is very terse and the book does not explain its last four lines. The transcription appends a modern note that reads the last line as a nobleman seeking wealth; that note is not a classic, so this library neither cites it nor draws the conclusion, and only translates the words. The '(1)' in the Chinese is the number the transcription gave to that note.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.18 算甚命？问甚卜？亏人是祸，饶人是福

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:义取之财神告显然，不义之财神不告：品行不端的人占财，卦卦兄弟持世、发动，只示破财，不示利与不利（觉子的例子：亏待伙计的人占卖货，占了又占都是兄弟持世，终于折本流落）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For money honestly sought the spirits answer plainly; for ill-gotten money they do not: a man of bad conduct asking about money gets Peer on the Self line and moving every time, showing only loss and saying nothing of profit (Juezi's example: a man who cheated his partners asked about selling, got Peer on the Self line again and again, and ended ruined and adrift).

**觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 11347 行 · *Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 11347*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 觉子曰：算甚命？问甚卜？亏人是祸，饶人是福。 | 觉子说：算什么命？问什么卜？亏待别人就是祸，宽待别人就是福。 | Juezi says: why tell fortunes? Why divine? To wrong others is misfortune; to spare them is fortune. |
| 财将义取者，神告显然。财之不义者，神不告矣。 | 以正当手段求财的，神明会清楚地告诉；不义之财，神明不告诉。 | For money sought honestly, the spirits answer plainly; for money sought unjustly, they do not answer. |
| 昔有一人亏损伙计，独得其利，人人知而詈骂， | 从前有个人亏待伙计，独吞利益，人人都知道、都骂他； | There was once a man who cheated his partners and kept the profit for himself; everyone knew and cursed him. |
| 因占货当卖否，卦得兄爻持世，又占不卖何如，又是兄爻持世，占过又占，不离兄动， | 他占货该不该卖，卦得兄弟持世；又占不卖怎样，又是兄弟持世；占了又占，总离不开兄弟发动。 | He asked whether to sell the goods: the Peer line on the Self line; he asked what if he did not sell: the Peer line on the Self line again; ask as he might, Peer kept moving. |
| 余亦知其此人亏损伙计，而得货抑买，素行不端。 | 我也知道这个人亏待伙计，货是压价强买来的，平素品行不端。 | I too knew he had cheated his partners and got the goods by forced buying, a man of bad conduct. |
| 余悟兄动者破财之兆也，有利无利，神不告之， | 我明白了：兄弟动是破财之兆，至于有利无利，神明不告诉他。 | I understood: the moving Peer was the sign of losing money, and whether there would be profit the spirits would not tell him. |
| 后此人不卖者，其价一日贱似一日，不得不卖，及至急急卖毕，其价大长， | 后来这人不卖，价格一天比一天便宜，不得不卖；等他急急忙忙卖完，价格却大涨； | Afterwards he held on and the price fell day by day until he had to sell; as soon as he had hurriedly sold out, the price shot up; |
| 折本漂流，难回故里，所以丧心之人何须占卜！ | 他亏了本钱，流落在外，回不了家乡。所以丧尽良心的人何必占卜！ | he lost his capital and drifted, unable to go home. So what need has a heartless man of divination! |

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.32 行险求财：丧心之事，卜亦不验，故不注

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:行险求财（不义之财）不占：古法以青龙临财为吉，觉子认为这是丧心之事，神不告不义，卜亦不验，本章不注。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Money sought by risky or dishonest means is not divined: the old method counted the Azure Dragon on Wealth as good, but Juezi calls it heartless business; the spirits do not answer the unjust, divination does not work for it, and the chapter is left without comment.

**觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 11567 行 · *Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 11567*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 觉子曰：古以青龙财爻为吉。 | 觉子说：古法以青龙临财爻为吉。 | Juezi says: the old method counted the Azure Dragon on the Wealth line as good. |
| 余以此非营生之本，必丧心之事也。 | 我认为这（行险求财）不是谋生的正道，一定是丧尽良心的事。 | I hold that this is no proper livelihood but a heartless business. |
| 行此丧心之事，亦敢告之于神耶？ | 做这种丧良心的事，还敢拿去问神吗？ | Would you dare put such a thing before the spirits? |
| 既告之于神，而神若以吉凶而告者，乃神之教人而作丧心之事也。 | 既然问了神，神如果回答吉凶，那就是神教人去做丧良心的事了。 | If you did, and the spirits answered with good or bad fortune, they would be teaching people to do heartless things. |
| 可谓大道之易卦耶？可谓之伏羲大圣之神耶？ | 这还能叫大道的易卦吗？还能叫伏羲大圣的神明吗？ | Could that be called the Changes of the great Way? Could those be the spirits of the sage Fuxi? |
| 然不独此，凡占非礼非义之事，不忠不孝之谋，一念之恶，恶神随之。 | 不只这一类，凡是占非礼非义的事、不忠不孝的图谋，一念起恶，恶神就跟上了。 | And not only this: whenever one divines about something improper or unjust, a disloyal or unfilial scheme, the moment of evil intent brings evil spirits with it. |
| 即不告之于神，亦遭天谴。 | 即使不去问神，也会遭天谴。 | Even without asking the spirits, one will be punished by Heaven. |
| 《尚书》曰：“惠迪吉，从逆凶，何须问卜？” | 《尚书》说：「顺道就吉，从逆就凶，何必问卜？」 | The Book of Documents says: 'Follow the way and prosper, follow the wrong and suffer; what need to divine?' |
| 所以卜亦不验，故不注。 | 所以卜了也不灵，这一章不作注。 | So divination does not work for it, and this chapter is left without comment. |

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.34 八宫章·占财秘法·非理之求神不报此而应彼：占炼汞烧丹世无此理，即使妻财持世也应在别处的财；书中例占烧丹得戌土财爻持世午火子孙动，只说戌月成丹，却是九月妻妾子女从数千里外来团聚；占地下忽见异物，妻财持世必有金银，兄弟持世不独无财且有破财，官鬼持世必是妖邪

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占炼汞烧丹，世上没有这个道理，卦不必占，念头也不要起，即使卦得妻财持世，也应在另外地方的财。曾经有人占烧丹，得戌土财爻持世，午火子孙发动相生，只以为戌月一定成丹，哪知道九月妻子、妾、子女从几千里外来，异乡团聚。戌土妻财，应妻妾；午火子孙，应子女。这是占这件应那件，《易》是为君子谋的，难道不可信吗？所以不合道理的求取，神灵不报这一件而应到那一件上。占地下忽然见到有光彩的异物，或者现出黄白奇形，怀疑有财的，如果妻财持世，必有金银；兄弟持世，不但没有财，而且有破财的事；如果官鬼持世，必是妖邪。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Divining for refining mercury and burning cinnabar: there is no such principle in the world, so the hexagram need not be divined and the thought should not even be raised; even if the hexagram gives Wealth holding the Self, it answers on wealth elsewhere. Someone once divined about burning cinnabar and got Wealth Xu-earth holding the Self with Output Wu-fire moving to give life to it, and thought it meant that the cinnabar would surely be done in the Xu month; he did not know that in the ninth month his wife, his concubine and his children would come from thousands of li away and be reunited with him in a strange land. Xu-earth Wealth answers to wife and concubine; Wu-fire Output answers to children. This is divining one matter and having another answer; the Changes are for the benefit of the noble man, and can it be disbelieved? So for a seeking that is against reason the spirits do not report that matter but answer on another. In divining when one suddenly sees a strange shining object in the ground, or a yellow or white form, and suspects there is treasure, if Wealth holds the Self there is surely gold or silver; if Peer holds the Self, there is not only no wealth but a matter of losing money; if Pressure holds the Self, it is surely an evil spirit.
