## LY.WEALTH.HJC.30 六畜血财，尤喜福兴持世

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占六畜（贩卖或饲养）以子孙为主：子孙旺相不空、持世临身则吉；父母发动有伤损；化出土鬼防瘟死；子孙旺而财空，畜好无利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For livestock, bought and sold or raised, Output is the main line: Output thriving, not void, on the Self or Body line is good; a moving Resource line means losses; turning into an Earth Pressure line, beware of plague; Output strong but Wealth void, the animals do well but there is no profit.

## LY.WEALTH.HZL.15 占畜养：财福上卦，虎动鬼兴防损失；鬼持初爻鸡鸭不吉，官坐五爻牛马难安

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占畜养：财旺、福神相，牲畜有益；虎动、鬼兴，要防损失。占畜养先定体：关键要财、福上卦，没有就不利；官鬼在初爻，鸡鸭不吉；官鬼在五爻，牛马难安；还要配合六神来断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Livestock: Wealth strong and Output thriving, the animals do well; the Tiger moving and Pressure rising, guard against loss. First fix the body of the question: the essential is Wealth and Output in the hexagram, and without them it is unfavourable; Pressure on the first line, poultry do badly; Pressure on the fifth line, cattle and horses are unsettled; and the six spirits are consulted together.

## LY.WEALTH.HZL.16 畜养：最忌兄弟官鬼；官鬼五行各主一样病；本命爻临财福无伤则吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:畜养最忌兄弟、官鬼。官鬼落在上爻（或说以五爻为主）：金鬼，牛极瘦；木鬼，脚疼或肚子受风；水鬼，牲畜散失；火鬼，中热；土鬼，发痒、瘟疫、发黄。所属牲畜的本命爻临在财、福上、没有受伤，就吉；比如兄弟、官鬼临三爻本来不好，但有亥爻这个本命临财福，就吉，不为害，其余照此类推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Livestock most fear Peer and Pressure. With Pressure on the top line (or, some say, the fifth line as the chief): a Metal Pressure, the ox grows very thin; Wood, sore feet or wind in the belly; Water, the animals scatter; Fire, heat stroke; Earth, itching, plague and jaundice. The animal's own natal line on Wealth or Output and unharmed is auspicious; for instance Peer or Pressure on the third line is bad in itself, but if the Hai line, the natal one, sits on Wealth or Output it is auspicious and does no harm; the rest follow from this.

**《火珠林·占畜养》原文** — 《火珠林》第 527 行 · *Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Divining for livestock", base text; Huozhu Lin, line 527*

| 如鬼值上爻或曰五爻为主： | 比如官鬼落在上爻（或说以五爻为主）： | with Pressure on the top line (or, some say, the fifth line as the chief): |

## LY.WEALTH.HZL.17 占渔猎：以世为主、以财为物，财子旺相大吉；财在四爻遇兔豕，鬼在六爻防虎豹；内克外、世克应、青龙财动不空可得

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占渔猎：以世为主，以财为猎物；福神兴旺、财旺，前程可望；财、官鬼落空，一无所获。财和子孙都旺相大吉；财在四爻，可遇兔和猪；官鬼在六爻，要防虎豹。内克外、内旺相、世克应，有青龙、财爻发动又不空亡，猎物可得；恶杀临在旺相的财上，发动克世，主有野兽伤人，凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Fishing and hunting: the Self is the chief and Wealth the quarry; Output rising and Wealth strong, the prospect is bright; Wealth and Pressure falling void, nothing is caught. Wealth and Output both strong is greatly auspicious; Wealth on the fourth line, hares and boars may be met; Pressure on the sixth line, beware of tigers and leopards. The inner controlling the outer, the inner strong and the Self controlling the Other, with the Azure Dragon and the Wealth line moving and not void, the quarry can be had; the ill stars on a strong Wealth, moving and controlling the Self, mean a wound from a beast: ill.

## LY.WEALTH.YL.17 开矿淘沙取藏：取伏财为用，要有气才可获

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:开矿、淘沙、掘藏：地下的宝物藏而不露，只取伏着的财为用神，伏财要有气才能得到，福神成双跟着来；劈山开矿、破石寻珍，是同一个道理。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Mining, panning sand and digging for buried treasure: treasure in the ground lies hidden, so take the hidden Wealth as the Subject; it must have breath to be had, and Output follows alongside; splitting mountains, opening mines and breaking rock to find gems are judged by the same principle.

## LY.WEALTH.YL.56 渔猎：财官两备才有收获，缺一或遇空，鱼飞鸟走

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:渔翁猎户占，只取财和官为用神：卦里缺一个就不好，要财旺官兴同时出现，才能打到大鱼、擒到大兽；财、鬼两爻有一个落空亡，就像鹅飞上天、鱼跳进深渊，白白张弓设网也捉不住。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When fishermen and hunters ask, take only Wealth and Pressure as the Subject: if either is missing from the hexagram it is not good; a strong Wealth and a thriving Pressure must both appear, and then the fisherman lands great fish and the hunter seizes big game; if either the Wealth or the Pressure line falls void, the goose flies to the sky and the fish leaps into the deep, and drawing bow and spear is in vain.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.29 不拘家禽野兽，皆以子孙为用神，兼看财爻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:买卖、饲养六畜（家畜野兽）以子孙为用神，兼看财爻：财持世而子孙动来生、日月生助，或财化子孙，大利；子孙持世、财合世也有利。防鬼化子、子化鬼、父化子、子化父、父兄持世、财临空破（书中例：子孙动变鬼、又临月破，贩马多死，幸财暗动生世，未大亏本）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Livestock, bought, sold or raised, domestic or wild: Output is the Subject, with Wealth considered too. Wealth on the Self line fed by a moving Output or by the Day and Month, or Wealth turning into Output, great profit; Output on the Self line with Wealth combining, profit too. Beware of Pressure turning into Output or the reverse, Resource turning into Output or the reverse, Resource or Peer on the Self line, Wealth void or broken (case: Output moving into Pressure and under a Month-break; most of the horses died on the road, but a hidden-stirred Wealth generating the Self line saved the capital).
