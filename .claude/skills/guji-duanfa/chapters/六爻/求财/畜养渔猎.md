## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.47 六畜爻位：初爻鸡鸭，二爻猫犬；遇青龙旺盛，加白虎两难全

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六畜里，鸡鸭在初爻，猫犬在二爻；遇到青龙就四季旺盛，加上白虎就鸡鸭猫犬两样难保全。注：初爻带鬼或落空亡，难养鸡鸭，加大小耗有狐狸黄鼠伤害，临青龙大吉；二爻的猫犬，寅动克戌是虎伤犬，戌克二爻是犬伤猫，猫爻带吉煞善捕鼠，带鬼煞家有恶犬，逢空不利，加白虎必有伤，带青龙必旺相。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Among the six kinds of livestock, chickens and ducks are on the first line and cats and dogs on the second; meeting the Azure Dragon they thrive through the four seasons, and with the White Tiger added the two cannot both be kept. The commentary adds: the first line with Pressure or Void makes chickens and ducks hard to keep, with the Great and Small Waste stars foxes and weasels hurt them, and on the Azure Dragon it is greatly auspicious; for cats and dogs on the second line, Yin moving against Xu is the tiger hurting the dog, Xu controlling the second line is the dog hurting the cat, a cat line with an auspicious star catches rats well, with Pressure and a baleful star the house has a fierce dog, falling Void is bad, with the White Tiger added there is surely harm, and with the Azure Dragon added it surely thrives.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.48 三四爻猪羊怕官杀兄弟；五六爻牛马喜福德妻财；各爻带什么看盈亏

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:三爻猪、四爻羊，怕见官杀、兄弟；五爻牛、六爻马，喜遇福德、妻财。注：猪带子孙妻财必旺，带兄弟不长，带官逢空有损；羊带财福旺，带鬼杀损失，逢空无羊；牛带子孙有子母牛，遇青龙财福吉，遇鬼杀白虎凶；驴马一爻照牛爻断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The third line is the pig and the fourth the sheep, which fear Pressure, baleful stars and Peer; the fifth is the ox and the sixth the horse, which like Output and Wealth. ## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.49 最嫌本命逢冲，须忌分宫受克；六畜本命：丑牛、寅虎（作猫）、午马、未羊、酉鸡、戌犬、亥猪

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:最怕的是六畜的本命爻逢冲，也忌分宫爻受克。注：丑爻是牛，寅爻是虎（也当猫爻断），午马、未羊、酉鸡、戌犬、亥猪；本命怕遇鬼煞、大小耗相刑相克，也嫌空亡，都主损伤；另一说，初鸡鸭、二猫犬、三猪、四羊、五牛、六马和驴骡，是分宫；本命上卦以分宫为主，吉神来生则吉，凶煞来克则凶，日辰扶持亦吉，日辰刑冲则凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** What is most disliked is the livestock's natal line meeting a clash, and the line of its own palace being controlled. The commentary says: Chou is the ox, Yin the tiger (also judged as the cat line), Wu the horse, Wei the sheep, You the chicken, Xu the dog, Hai the pig; the natal line fears Pressure, baleful stars and the Great and Small Waste stars punishing and controlling it, and dislikes Void, all of which mean damage; another saying gives first chicken and duck, second cat and dog, third pig, fourth sheep, fifth ox, sixth horse, donkey and mule as the palace division; when the natal line is in the hexagram, the palace division is the main thing, an auspicious spirit coming to generate it is auspicious, a baleful one coming to control it is ominous, the Day's support is auspicious and the Day's punishment or clash is ominous.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.50 命逢生旺当别吉凶；爻值刑冲须分表里：旺处逢冲则损，绝处逢冲则散，空处逢冲则不空

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:本命逢生旺，要分吉凶：临财福、旺相则吉，临鬼煞、大小耗则凶，反不如不旺；分宫爻遇刑冲，也要分表里：会财福、青龙逢刑冲则凶，临鬼煞、白虎逢刑冲反吉；旺处逢冲则损，绝处逢冲则散，空处逢冲则不空。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the natal line meets a thriving state one must tell auspicious from ominous: on Wealth or Output and thriving it is auspicious, on Pressure, baleful stars or the Great and Small Waste stars it is ominous and worse than not thriving; when the palace line meets punishment or clash one must tell the outside from the inside: joined with Wealth, Output or the Azure Dragon it is ominous, on Pressure, baleful stars or the White Tiger it is on the contrary auspicious; thriving and clashed it is damaged, severed and clashed it is scattered, Void and clashed it is no longer Void.
