## LY.WEALTH.HJC.31 世应同人，放债必然连本失

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:放债：世、应都是兄弟（同人），讨不回来；财爻再绝，连本俱失；世应落空亦然。应爻生合世爻、妻财有气、子孙发动则吉；间爻兄鬼发动防经手人抽分；卦无财而官鬼化出财，要打官司才讨得回。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Lending: Peer on both the Self and Other lines means the money cannot be recovered; with Wealth extinguished as well, the principal is lost; the same if Self and Other are void. Good if the Other line generates or combines with the Self line, Wealth has force and Output moves. A moving Peer or Pressure on the between lines: the middleman skims a share. No Wealth, but Pressure turning into it: recoverable only through a lawsuit.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.33 应落空亡，索借者失望

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:向人讨要、借贷：应爻空（见不到人、不实）或动（对方变卦）都不宜；应爻生合世爻则对方慷慨。代表所借之物的爻也要不空：衣服书籍看父母，六畜酒器看子孙，财物（食物）看妻财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Asking for something or borrowing it: the Other line should be neither void (you will not find them, nothing is real) nor moving (they change their mind); if it generates or combines with the Self line they give generously. The line for the thing itself must also not be void: Resource for clothes and books, Output for livestock and wine vessels, Wealth for money, goods and food.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7874 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7874*

| 应落空亡， | 应爻落空亡， | If the Other line is void, |
| 索借者失望。 | 去讨要、去借的人要失望。 | whoever goes to ask or borrow will be disappointed. |
