## LY.WEALTH.HJC.31 世应同人，放债必然连本失

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:放债：世、应都是兄弟（同人），讨不回来；财爻再绝，连本俱失；世应落空亦然。应爻生合世爻、妻财有气、子孙发动则吉；间爻兄鬼发动防经手人抽分；卦无财而官鬼化出财，要打官司才讨得回。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Lending: Peer on both the Self and Other lines means the money cannot be recovered; with Wealth extinguished as well, the principal is lost; the same if Self and Other are void. Good if the Other line generates or combines with the Self line, Wealth has force and Output moves. A moving Peer or Pressure on the between lines: the middleman skims a share. No Wealth, but Pressure turning into it: recoverable only through a lawsuit.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7867 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7867*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.33 应落空亡，索借者失望

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:向人讨要、借贷：应爻空（见不到人、不实）或动（对方变卦）都不宜；应爻生合世爻则对方慷慨。代表所借之物的爻也要不空：衣服书籍看父母，六畜酒器看子孙，财物（食物）看妻财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Asking for something or borrowing it: the Other line should be neither void (you will not find them, nothing is real) nor moving (they change their mind); if it generates or combines with the Self line they give generously. The line for the thing itself must also not be void: Resource for clothes and books, Output for livestock and wine vessels, Wealth for money, goods and food.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.10 借贷求财：财持世生合世而不空破绝

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:借贷：财持世或生合世而不空破绝，财逢生合之日得；应生合世而兄静财伏，或财伏而兄子同动，财值日得；父空无借据，父化父改契；世应空合，虚约；应受刑伤，求而不遇；应生合世而财空绝，想借无钱；财生合世而应冲克世，借而不情愿；财动生世而日合财，有人把持拖延；应动合世而日冲应，有人说破不成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Borrowing: Wealth on or generating or combining with the Self line and not void, broken or extinguished, the money comes on a day that generates or combines with it; the Other generating the Self with Peer still and Wealth hidden, or Wealth hidden with Peer and Output both moving, it comes on Wealth's day; Resource void, no written note; Resource into Resource, the contract rewritten; Self and Other void yet combining, an empty promise; the Other hurt, you will not find him; the Other generating the Self but Wealth void, willing but empty-pursed; Wealth generating the Self but the Other clashing it, lent grudgingly; Wealth moving to the Self but held by the Day, someone delays it; the Other combining with the Self but clashed by the Day, someone talks him out of it.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.11 纠会、摇会求财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:纠会（发起互助会）：财、应生合世易成；财应空破绝或应刑冲克世不成；应空对方推辞，世空自己倦怠，应空合世虚诺；应生合世带退悔杀或化退神，先允后却；带破碎杀，零星交财；应财化财，一人两脚；应兄化财，两人合脚；六合变六冲不成。摇会：财旺动助官生合世得；兄持世动、兄弟独发、无财鬼、世财空破死绝受刑克则失；六合得六冲失；财合世而兄克财，得而复失；财合应而应生世，彼得让我；财生世又生应，谁衰伤谁失；世应俱旺，两人分得。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Gathering a savings society: Wealth and the Other generating or combining with the Self, it forms; Wealth or the Other void, broken or extinguished, or the Other attacking the Self, it does not; Other void, they refuse; Self void, you tire of it; Other void yet combining, an empty promise; the Other generating the Self but moving with the Regret star or turning Retreating, agreed then withdrawn; with the Breaking star, paid in dribs and drabs; the Other's Wealth turning into Wealth, one person taking two shares; the Other's Peer turning into Wealth, two sharing one; Six-Combine changing into Six-Clash, it does not form. Drawing: a strong moving Wealth feeding Pressure and generating the Self wins; Peer moving on the Self or alone, no Wealth or Pressure, or Self and Wealth void or hurt, loses; Six-Combine wins, Six-Clash loses; Wealth combining with the Self but controlled by Peer, won then lost; Wealth combining with the Other and the Other generating the Self, he wins and hands it to you; Wealth generating both, whichever is weaker loses; both strong, they share.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.16 索债求财：外生内、应生世者还

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:讨债：外生内、应生世、内外世应比和，还；外克内、应克冲刑害世，不还；应生合世而无财，想还没钱；应冲伤世而财旺，能还不肯；应持兄鬼动伤世，嘴甜心狠；应持兄动而财空破绝，被骗；应空，穷或逃；应入墓胎，见不到人；财化空破绝，全无；财动助应而鬼伤世，借势耍赖；鬼动化财，告状才还；世持财子旺而兄衰静，本利俱全；应持兄鬼安静不伤世，或持勾陈父母不伤世，只是拖延。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Collecting a debt: outer generating inner, the Other generating the Self, inner and outer, Self and Other in harmony, repaid; outer controlling inner, the Other attacking the Self, not repaid; the Other generating the Self but no Wealth, willing but unable; the Other hurting the Self with Wealth strong, able but unwilling; the Other on Peer or Pressure moving against the Self, sweet words, bad heart; the Other on a moving Peer with Wealth void, swindled; Other void, too poor or hiding; entombed or embryonic, not to be found; Wealth turning void or extinguished, nothing; Wealth feeding the Other while Pressure hurts the Self, he leans on power to welsh; Pressure into Wealth, repaid only after a lawsuit; a strong Wealth or Output on the Self with Peer weak and still, principal and interest; the Other on a still Peer or Pressure, or on Hook Earth and Resource, not hurting the Self, only delay.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.27 世逢兄，何须开口；应空破，难遂我心

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:借贷：世临兄弟不必开口；应爻空破难遂意；财空破休指望；子孙兴、财发动可求；世应都不宜月破旬空；兄弟不可临世或发动。书中例：兄动化退，而明日辰日合住兄爻，借款于辰日到手。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Borrowing: Peer on the Self line, do not bother asking; the Other line void or broken, you will not get your wish; Wealth void or broken, no hope; Output stirring and Wealth moving, ask; neither Self nor Other should be under a Month-break or void; Peer must not sit on the Self line or move. Case: the moving Peer turned Retreating, and the next day, a Chen day, combined with and held it; the loan came that day.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.28 兄临世，放则无还；应临兄，索则不获

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:放债、索债：兄弟持世，放出去收不回；应临兄弟，去讨讨不到（「世应同人」两头都忌）；应临官鬼克世，防负义，好意成恶意；应临财生世，对方守信。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Lending and collecting: Peer on the Self line, what you lend does not come back; Peer on the Other line, what you ask for you do not get ('both Self and Other Peer' is feared at either end); the Other on Pressure controlling the Self line, beware of ingratitude, goodwill turning to ill will; the Other on Wealth generating the Self line, he will keep faith.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.31 请会须宜财旺，更宜世应相生

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:请会：宜财旺、世应相生；六冲则财旺也不久，六冲变合（即使世应相克）可成，六合变冲有始无终，财破绝宜速止。摇会：旺财持世必得；何时得：破空→填实之时，伏藏→出现之时，太旺→逢墓之时，衰绝→逢生旺之时；财化回头克、衰静破空又六冲，会散，终无得会之期。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Starting a savings society: Wealth strong and Self and Other generating; a Six-Clash means it will not last even with strong Wealth; clash changing into combination forms (even with Self and Other controlling); combination changing into clash, a start with no finish; Wealth broken or extinguished, stop at once. Winning the draw: a strong Wealth on the Self line wins; when: broken or void, when filled in; hidden, when it appears; over-strong, at its tomb; weak or extinguished, at its birth or strength; Wealth turning into its own control, or weak, still, broken or void in a Six-Clash hexagram, the society breaks up and your turn never comes.
