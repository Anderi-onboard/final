## LY.WEALTH.HJC.31 世应同人，放债必然连本失

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:放债：世、应都是兄弟（同人），讨不回来；财爻再绝，连本俱失；世应落空亦然。应爻生合世爻、妻财有气、子孙发动则吉；间爻兄鬼发动防经手人抽分；卦无财而官鬼化出财，要打官司才讨得回。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Lending: Peer on both the Self and Other lines means the money cannot be recovered; with Wealth extinguished as well, the principal is lost; the same if Self and Other are void. Good if the Other line generates or combines with the Self line, Wealth has force and Output moves. A moving Peer or Pressure on the between lines: the middleman skims a share. No Wealth, but Pressure turning into it: recoverable only through a lawsuit.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7867 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7867*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.33 应落空亡，索借者失望

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:向人讨要、借贷：应爻空（见不到人、不实）或动（对方变卦）都不宜；应爻生合世爻则对方慷慨。代表所借之物的爻也要不空：衣服书籍看父母，六畜酒器看子孙，财物（食物）看妻财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Asking for something or borrowing it: the Other line should be neither void (you will not find them, nothing is real) nor moving (they change their mind); if it generates or combines with the Self line they give generously. The line for the thing itself must also not be void: Resource for clothes and books, Output for livestock and wine vessels, Wealth for money, goods and food.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7874 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7874*

## LY.WEALTH.YL.16 合会：忌世应空；应生合世易成，应空、应被冲破、兄弟动冲，被人阻挠；世应两空或六冲，彼此灰心

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:合会（亲友凑钱互助）：怕世应落空。应爻生世、合世，容易成、容易得；应空则对方推却；应被日冲月破或兄弟动来冲，被人搅扰办不成；世空，自己起不来；世应两空或六冲，双方灰心，事情难成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pooling money with kin and friends (a savings society): what is feared is the Self or the Other void. The Other generating or combining with the Self makes it easy to form and to receive; the Other void, and he declines; the Other clashed by the Day or broken by the Month, or Peer moving to clash it, and someone interferes and blocks it; the Self void, and I cannot get it going; both void or a Six-Clash, and both sides lose heart and the affair fails.

**《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 1944 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 1944*

## LY.WEALTH.YL.32 放债借出：有子孙、妻财旺相生世合世，有财有利；官鬼、兄弟发动冲世克世，多耗多是非

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:借出本钱：卦里有子孙，遇到善良的客人；有官鬼，遇到强横的对手。妻财最喜欢落在生旺之地；兄弟只怕克世和卦身。爻上有子孙、妻财，旺相，或生世、合世、克世，有财有利；官鬼、兄弟发动，冲世、克世，多耗损多是非，即使只是发动也不是吉象，要忌讳。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Lending out capital: Output in the hexagram means meeting a good customer; Pressure means meeting a domineering guest. Wealth is most glad to sit in a place of Birth or strength; Peer only worries about controlling the Self and the Body. Output or Wealth on the lines, strong, or generating, combining with or controlling the Self, means money and profit; Pressure or Peer moving to clash with or control the Self brings many losses and quarrels, and even merely moving is not auspicious and must be avoided.

**《易林补遗·托本求利章第一百七》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 1985 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 107 - Lending capital for interest", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 1985*

## LY.WEALTH.YL.33 世空我心变，应陷他意不真；应克世爻、外伤内卦，对方狠心欺赖、忘恩负义

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世空，我自己不情愿；应空，对方灰心；世应两空，彼此都算了。应克世爻，他必定狠着心欺赖；外卦伤内卦，也是对方不好，必然忘恩负义。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Self void: I am unwilling; Other void: he loses heart; both void: both are done with it. The Other controlling the Self line: he will harden his heart and cheat and default; the outer trigram hurting the inner is also bad on his part, and he will forget kindness and betray your trust.

## LY.WEALTH.YL.34 上下彼我不相克、内外比和：应生世、外生内信义不忘，世生应、内生外义中取利

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:借贷双方上下、彼我没有相克，内外比和、世应相合，是两个「我」和顺，和同胞兄弟没有两样；应生世、外生内，对方不忘信义，利钱不少；世生应、内生外，或者比和，也是情义中取利，两边心意缠绵长久。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With no control between above and below, between him and me, inner and outer in harmony and Self and Other combined, the two sides are like two selves in accord, no different from brothers. The Other generating the Self and the outer generating the inner: he keeps faith and the interest is not small; the Self generating the Other and the inner generating the outer, or harmony: profit taken within good faith, the two minds entwined and lasting.
