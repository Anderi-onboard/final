## LY.WEALTH.HJC.28 公门何虑官爻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:一般占财忌官鬼发动（阻隔）；求公门（官府）之财反要官鬼：无鬼或鬼空，财旺也难到手；官鬼旺相、生合世爻卦身则吉；刑克世爻主责罚、祸害立至；官鬼死绝也不济事，须财、官两全才大吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Money questions usually fear a moving Pressure line (obstruction), but money from official quarters needs Pressure: with no Pressure line, or a void one, even a strong Wealth is hard to get; Pressure thriving and generating or combining with the Self and Body lines is good; Pressure punishing or controlling the Self line means punishment and immediate trouble; a dead or extinguished Pressure line is no use either; only with both Wealth and Pressure sound is it very good.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7855 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7855*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.29 九流术士，偏宜鬼动生身

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:空手求财（百工九流）以官鬼为主顾：无官鬼最不利；官鬼出现发动、生合世爻则如意，化财更有大利；官鬼刑克世爻或化兄主是非；应爻空更不吉。但仍须财爻旺相、兄弟不动，才有实利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Earning without capital (craftsmen and the trades) takes the Pressure line as the client: no Pressure is the worst; Pressure appearing, moving and generating or combining with the Self line goes as wished, and turning into Wealth brings a large profit; Pressure punishing or controlling the Self line, or turning into Peer, means trouble; a void Other line is worse still. Yet there is real profit only if Wealth is also thriving and Peer still.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7858 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7858*

## LY.WEALTH.YL.06 祝巫：鬼旺有神通；官旺合真人之应，鬼空则道不通彻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:圆光召将、画符念咒的太保、师巫这类人求财，一定靠官鬼兴旺，灵验才像回声一样快；官旺合真人之应，法术高明；官鬼落空，就没有野祟那样的灵验，道法不通透。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For crystal-gazers who summon spirit generals and for the Taibao and shamans who draw talismans and utter spells, everything rests on Pressure being strong, and then the response is as prompt as an echo: a strong Pressure fits the response of a true adept and the art is lofty; with Pressure void there is none of the efficacy of the wild spirits and the art is not fully effective.

## LY.WEALTH.YL.11 梨园：不忌官和兄弟；官鬼是用爻，兄弟是伴侣，两个都动才全美

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:戏班、走戏、傀儡这类行当：不是一两个人做得了的事，又要有主顾来找；兄弟是他们的伙伴，官鬼是用爻，两个都发动才十全十美，所以说不怕官兴、兄发。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For theatre troupes, touring players and puppet shows: it is not work for one or two people and patrons must come to hire them; Peer is their companion and Pressure the Subject line, and only if both move is it perfect, hence the saying that there is no fear of Pressure rising and Peer moving.

## LY.WEALTH.YM.09 巫医、优娼以官鬼为专；工技忌应克世；僧道缘募恶应爻空；行次迎客、脱产求交，应空则无人来、无人与

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:巫医的利在名誉，专看官鬼；优娼的利在鼓舞，也以官鬼为务；工技之人重情好，忌应克世；僧道的缘募重悦从，恶应爻空；出门迎客，对方空了谁来；卖产业求成交，应空了谁来买；成交有指定的对象，就和谋事一样。注：有的兼用官，有的兼用应，而财神不可脱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For shamans and doctors the profit lies in reputation and the Pressure line is the specialist; for actors and courtesans the profit lies in drumming and dancing, and Pressure is again the business; for craftsmen good feeling counts and the Other controlling the Self is avoided; for monks and priests raising alms, willing agreement counts and the Other line falling Void is disliked; in going out to meet guests, if the other side is Void who will come; in selling property to close a deal, if the Other is Void who will take it; if the deal has a named counterpart it is the same as scheming a matter. The note adds: some also use Pressure, some also use the Other, but the Wealth spirit must not be lost.
