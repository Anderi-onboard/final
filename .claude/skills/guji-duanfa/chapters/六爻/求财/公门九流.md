## LY.WEALTH.HJC.28 公门何虑官爻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:一般占财忌官鬼发动（阻隔）；求公门（官府）之财反要官鬼：无鬼或鬼空，财旺也难到手；官鬼旺相、生合世爻卦身则吉；刑克世爻主责罚、祸害立至；官鬼死绝也不济事，须财、官两全才大吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Money questions usually fear a moving Pressure line (obstruction), but money from official quarters needs Pressure: with no Pressure line, or a void one, even a strong Wealth is hard to get; Pressure thriving and generating or combining with the Self and Body lines is good; Pressure punishing or controlling the Self line means punishment and immediate trouble; a dead or extinguished Pressure line is no use either; only with both Wealth and Pressure sound is it very good.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7855 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7855*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.29 九流术士，偏宜鬼动生身

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:空手求财（百工九流）以官鬼为主顾：无官鬼最不利；官鬼出现发动、生合世爻则如意，化财更有大利；官鬼刑克世爻或化兄主是非；应爻空更不吉。但仍须财爻旺相、兄弟不动，才有实利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Earning without capital (craftsmen and the trades) takes the Pressure line as the client: no Pressure is the worst; Pressure appearing, moving and generating or combining with the Self line goes as wished, and turning into Wealth brings a large profit; Pressure punishing or controlling the Self line, or turning into Peer, means trouble; a void Other line is worse still. Yet there is real profit only if Wealth is also thriving and Peer still.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7858 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7858*

## LY.WEALTH.YY.09 空手求财：但以官鬼为主

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:空手求利（百工九流、到官员任所打秋风）以官鬼为主：官鬼旺动生合身世吉，财爻旺动助鬼生合身世更吉；子孙旺动伤官、兄持世动或兄弟独发、官鬼刑克世身、官财空破死墓绝胎，少利。求财方向：世爻所克之方为财方，世爻生旺之方利，死墓绝胎或受刑克之方凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Earning without capital (craftsmen, the trades, visitors soliciting gifts at an official's post) reads the Pressure line: a strong moving Pressure generating or combining with the Self and Body is good, better still with a strong moving Wealth feeding it; Output strong and moving against Pressure, Peer on the Self line moving or Peer moving alone, Pressure punishing or controlling the Self or Body, Pressure or Wealth void, broken, dead, entombed, extinguished or embryonic: little profit. Direction: the element the Self line controls is the direction of money; where the Self is born or strong is good; where it dies or is hurt is bad.

**《易隐·空手求财占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2900–2902 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Earning without capital", base text; Yi Yin, lines 2900–2902*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡百工九流之人，与任所抽丰之客，皆空拳求利者，但以官鬼为主。 | 凡是百工九流的人，以及到官员任所去打秋风（求馈赠）的客人，都是空手求利的，只以官鬼为主。 | Craftsmen and people of the trades, and visitors who go to an official's post to solicit gifts, all earn without capital; for them Pressure is the main line. |
| 官爻旺动生合身世者吉， | 官鬼旺而发动、生合身世，吉； | A strong moving Pressure line generating or combining with the Body and Self lines is good; |
| 则爻旺动助鬼生合身世者，尤吉也。 | 财爻（原文作「则爻」）旺而发动去助官鬼、生合身世，更吉。 | a strong moving Wealth (the text reads ze, 'then') that feeds Pressure and generates or combines with the Body and Self lines is better still. |
| 子旺动伤官，或兄持世动，与兄弟独发者， | 子孙旺动伤官鬼，或兄弟持世发动，或只有兄弟发动， | Output strong and moving to hurt Pressure, or Peer on the Self line and moving, or Peer moving alone; |

| 或官爻，财爻，逢空破死墓绝胎，与化空破死墓绝胎者，少利也。 | 或官鬼、财爻逢空、破、死、墓、绝、胎，或化出空破死墓绝胎，利就少。 | or the Pressure and Wealth lines void, broken, dead, entombed, extinguished or embryonic, or turning into these: little profit. |
| 问往何方可求，则世爻所克是财方也。 | 问往哪个方向去求，世爻所克的方位就是财方。 | Which direction to seek in: the direction of the element the Self line controls is the direction of money. |

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.01 世克官，官生世，须宜谒见

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:谒贵求财：世爻克官鬼或官鬼生世，宜去拜见；财临月破、官鬼克世，白跑。野鹤：求名以官鬼为用，求利以妻财为用，用神持世或生合世爻为吉；逢空破墓绝、动而变凶，徒劳；世爻化鬼、化回头克，或随鬼入墓、助鬼伤身，不宜去，去则到彼或途中遇祸。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Calling on the great for money: the Self line controlling Pressure, or Pressure generating the Self line, go; Wealth under a Month-break or Pressure controlling the Self line, a wasted journey. Yehe: for a post take Pressure, for gain take Wealth; the Subject on or generating or combining with the Self line is good; void, broken, entombed, extinguished, or turning harmful, the trip is for nothing; the Self line turning into Pressure or into its own control, following the ghost into the tomb or feeding the ghost that harms it, do not go, or trouble meets you there or on the way.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.05 六合六冲须看用，反吟化退枉奔劳

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六合、六冲不能单独定吉凶：六合要用神旺相，或世与财、官相合，才吉；六冲要用神受克失陷，才凶。反吟、世动化退：难以成行，勉强去也空手回。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Six-Combine and Six-Clash do not decide the matter by themselves: a Six-Combine is good only with the Subject strong or the Self line combining with Wealth or Pressure; a Six-Clash is bad only if the Subject is controlled and falls. Reversed Chant, or the Self line moving into Retreating: hard to set out, and back empty-handed if you force it.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.06 贵人谒贵宜世应相生，平人见贵宜官贵相合

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:谒贵分身份：贵人见贵人看世应相生相合；平民见贵人看官鬼与世爻相生相合，或官鬼持世。问能否得见，官鬼生合世即见；问功名，官鬼持世、生世才得名（觉子：勿把所见贵人之官当功名）。世应冲克、财伏而空，去无益；官鬼克世、世化鬼化克，凶，平人勿去；财官生世、持世吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** It depends on who is calling: rank calling on rank reads Self and Other generating or combining; a commoner calling on the great reads Pressure generating or combining with the Self line, or sitting on it. If the question is only whether you will be received, Pressure generating or combining with the Self line says yes; if it is office, Pressure must sit on or generate the Self line (Juezi: do not take the great man's Pressure line for office). Self and Other clashing or controlling with Wealth hidden and void, no use going; Pressure controlling the Self line, or the Self turning into Pressure or into its own control, bad, and a commoner should not go; Wealth or Pressure generating or sitting on the Self line is good.

**《增删卜易·谒贵求财章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 11284–11286 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Calling on the great for money", base text; Zengshan Buyi, lines 11284–11286*

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.07 最重财官两旺，不宜世应皆空

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:替贵人奔走效力求财：财、官两旺为要，世应俱空不宜；世临财、应临福，我得益；应临官、世逢破，他得益；兄弟持世，不如不去；应克世，防对方反目。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Earning by serving a great man: Wealth and Pressure both strong is what matters; Self and Other both void is bad. Wealth on the Self line and Output on the Other, I gain; Pressure on the Other and the Self line broken, he gains; Peer on the Self line, better not to go; the Other controlling the Self, beware of him turning on you.
