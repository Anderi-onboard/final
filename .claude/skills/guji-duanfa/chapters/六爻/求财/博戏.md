## LY.WEALTH.HJC.34 世遭刑克，赌博者必输

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:赌博：世旺应衰、世动应静为好；世克应我胜，应克世他胜。兄鬼动来刑克世爻、世临兄弟或世空：不胜；世应静空：赌不成；世坐官鬼：防合谋骗局；间爻动出兄鬼：争斗；内外无财：难胜；世应空动：赊账赌；兄鬼化财先败后胜，财化兄鬼先胜后败；卦身临兄：终是输兆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Gambling: best with the Self line strong and the Other weak, the Self moving and the Other still; Self controlling Other, I win; Other controlling Self, they win. Peer or Pressure moving to punish or control the Self line, Peer on the Self line, or a void Self line: no win; Self and Other still and void: no game; Pressure on the Self line: beware of a conspiracy; Peer or Pressure moving on the between lines: a fight; no Wealth in either trigram: hard to win; Self and Other void yet moving: gambling on credit; Peer or Pressure turning into Wealth, lose then win; Wealth turning into Peer or Pressure, win then lose; Peer on the Body line: a losing sign whatever you do.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7877 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7877*

## LY.WEALTH.YL.12 搏戏：嫌官鬼、兄弟兴旺；兄隆鬼旺必全输

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:下棋赌钱、争采取胜，是钱财往来来决输赢的事：兄弟耗财，官鬼克削，兄弟隆盛、官鬼旺相，必然输光。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Board games, betting and contests for prizes are matters where money passes back and forth and decides who wins: Peer wastes money and Pressure strips it, and with Peer thriving and Pressure strong you will lose everything.

## LY.WEALTH.YL.52 博戏：忌兄弟兴旺（被抽头暗耗）；子孙绝、财落空，钱如雪投汤

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:赌钱博弈：兄弟兴旺，会被人抽头、暗中耗去，手气不顺、心里不快，钱财该输；子孙和财不空不绝，只是一般；一遇空，即使有万贯钱财，也像雪投进热汤。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Gambling for money: Peer thriving means being skimmed in the open and drained in secret, luck running against you and an unhappy heart, and the money is lost; with Output and Wealth neither void nor extinct it is only slight; once void, even ten thousand strings of cash is like snow dropped into hot soup.

## LY.WEALTH.YL.53 博戏：世克应爻我胜，应伤世我输；旺克休、静受克动难伤；世旺生休应带财子大胜；世应比和胜负无偏

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:赌博输赢看世应：世克应爻，我全胜而归；应伤世，我大败，钱袋倒空。克的规矩：旺相能克休囚，休囚不能克旺相；安静的受克，发动的难伤，所以经上说静而休囚的要受克、兴旺的决难伤。世旺生休囚的应，再带财或子孙，大胜；不带，只得几文；应旺生衰弱的世，对方来追随我，没有不吉利的；世应比和相生，彼此谦让，胜负没有偏向。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Win or lose in gambling by the Self and the Other: the Self controlling the Other line, I win outright and come home content; the Other hurting the Self, I lose badly and my purse is emptied. The rules of control: the strong can control the resting and trapped, and the resting and trapped cannot control the strong; one that is still takes the control and one that moves is hard to hurt, so the classic says that still and resting takes the control and thriving cannot be hurt. A strong Self generating a resting Other and carrying Wealth or Output is a great win, without them only a few coins; a strong Other generating a weak Self, the other side comes to follow me and all is auspicious; Self and Other in harmony and generating each other, both yield and win and loss lean neither way.

**《易林补遗·博戏求财章第一百十二》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 2049 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 112 - Gambling for money", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 2049*

## LY.WEALTH.YY.12 赌博求财：内克外、世克应胜，外克内、应克世输

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:赌博：内克外、世克应，或财子旺持世生合世，胜（旺世克衰应小胜，旺应生衰世大胜）；外克内、应克世、财官俱无、世空破墓绝，输；世空动缺庄家，应空无对手；世应比和不分胜负，俱空两无所得；世上子孙化父因骄而败，化兄得助而赢；世居阴卦阴爻宜守，阳爻阳卦宜争先。应爻所临六神与六亲定对方手段：内外两鬼动伤世合局骗，间爻兄动抽头多，应带龙喜生世得人提携，虎刃克世斗殴，玄兄伤世设局偷筹，勾鬼伤世牵连不清，蛇火鬼伤世药骰，雀兄伤世因财争斗，雀兄化鬼伤世有人告发；世持雀鬼化父或雀父化鬼，因赌成讼，加太岁讼经年。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Gambling: inner controlling outer, Self controlling Other, or a strong Wealth or Output on or generating the Self, a win (strong Self over weak Other a small win, strong Other generating weak Self a big one); outer controlling inner, Other controlling Self, no Wealth or Pressure, or the Self void, broken, entombed or extinguished, a loss; Self void and moving, no banker; Other void, no opponent; in harmony, no winner; both void, nothing gained; Output on the Self turning into Resource, pride loses; into Peer, help wins; the Self yin in a yin trigram, hold back; yang in yang, press on. The Other's spirit and relation show how they play you: two Pressure lines against the Self, a set-up; a moving between Peer, a heavy house cut; the Other with Dragon and Joy generating the Self, someone helps you; Tiger and Blade controlling it, a brawl; Tortoise and Peer, a rigged game or swapped counters; Hook and Pressure, dragged into something murky; Snake and Fire Pressure, loaded dice; Bird and Peer, a fight over money; Bird and Peer turning into Pressure, someone informs; the Self on Bird and Pressure turning into Resource, or Bird and Resource into Pressure, gambling ends in a lawsuit, a year long with the Tai Sui.

**《易隐·空手求财占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2904 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Earning without capital", base text; Yi Yin, line 2904*

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.30 技斗力斗首重世应，禽鸟斗者专重子孙

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:博戏：比技、比力看世应，赌钱兼看财爻；世临日月、财得日月生扶、世动化吉、应带财生世，我胜，反之他胜；兄动破财，鬼克世有灾。斗禽专看子孙：旺者胜；休囚空破、受克害刑冲，或卦中父母动，多败（书中例：父母当令的巳午时斗，大败）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Contests: skill or strength, read Self and Other; with money staked, the Wealth line too. The Self on the Day or Month branch, Wealth supported by them, the Self moving into good, the Other carrying Wealth and generating the Self: I win; the reverse, he wins. Peer moving, money lost; Pressure controlling the Self, disaster. Bird fights: Output alone; strong wins; resting, trapped, void, broken, controlled, clashed, or Resource moving in the hexagram, mostly loses (case: fighting at the hours when Resource was in season, a heavy defeat).
