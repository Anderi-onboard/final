## LY.WEALTH.CA.12 世应是财求易得，卦无财少准凭；财化鬼凶、鬼化财吉；财落空亡难把捉，遇二杀贼侵

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世应是财，求就容易得到；卦里没有财，就少有把握。财爻化成官鬼是凶象，官鬼化成财一定是吉神。财落在空亡，难以把握；再遇二杀（劫杀、天贼杀），贼来相侵。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self or the Other being Wealth, seeking is easily rewarded; with no Wealth in the hexagram there is little to go on. ## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.05 出入财：内阴、外阴，财也阴的吉；纯阳的多散失

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看钱进钱出：内卦阳、财空、又变阳，不宜进财，多散失；内卦阴、财爻也变阴，如期变吉；外卦阴、财阴，出钱吉；外卦阳、财阳，宜藏好；总之逢阴为好，纯阳多散失。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Money coming in and going out: an inner yang with Wealth void and changing to yang is no good for taking money in and mostly scatters; an inner yin whose Wealth line changes to yin turns auspicious on time; an outer yin with yin Wealth is good for spending; an outer yang with yang Wealth should be stored away; yin is always the good sign, pure yang mostly loses.

## LY.WEALTH.HDY.06 卦中无财：福能生财，福爻旺相必遂；无财无福，月令有财亦成，日辰是财当日亦有，谓之无中生有

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里没有财，别忘了福德能生财，只要福爻旺相，一定如愿；卦里既没有财也没有福，月令里有财也能成；日辰是财，当天也有；这叫无中生有。凡是有财的卦也是这样，不可错过，要谨慎。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If a hexagram has no Wealth, remember that Output can generate Wealth, and if the Output line is thriving it is sure to be fulfilled; where the hexagram has neither Wealth nor Output, Wealth held by the month can still bring it about; if the Day is Wealth there is money that day; this is called producing something from nothing. The same holds for hexagrams that do have Wealth; it should not be missed, and one should be careful.

No money with: a Six-Clash hexagram; inner controlling outer, Self controlling Other; Other and Wealth void or broken; Wealth turning into Pressure, void, a break or extinction; Wealth moving off to combine with the Other, Peer or another line; the Body controlling a moving Wealth. Wealth strong but Output void or extinguished: once, and not again.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.04 多少：取财爻纳甲之数，旺相加倍，囚死减半

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:估数目：数大的用财爻纳甲数（甲己子午九、乙庚丑未八、丙辛寅申七、丁壬卯酉六、戊癸辰戌五、巳亥四），把卦中所有的财（两财、伏财、变财、四直之财、卦宫之财）加总，旺相加倍、休照数、囚死减半；数小的用财爻地支五行数（水一、火二、木三、金四、土五）随旺衰增减。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Estimating the sum: for a large one, use the stem-and-branch numbers of the Wealth line (Jia Ji Zi Wu nine, Yi Geng Chou Wei eight, Bing Xin Yin Shen seven, Ding Ren Mao You six, Wu Gui Chen Xu five, Si Hai four), add up every Wealth in the reading (two Wealth lines, hidden Wealth, changed Wealth, Wealth among the four rulers, the palace's own Wealth), doubling the strong, keeping the resting as they are, halving the trapped and dead; for a small one, use the element number of the Wealth branch (Water one, Fire two, Wood three, Metal four, Earth five), raised or lowered by strength.
