## LY.WEALTH.HJC.02 财旺福兴，财空福绝

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻旺相、子孙发动：公事私事求财都如意。财、福落空、临墓绝（或受伤克而无救）：做什么营生都不如意。野鹤另加两条：宜动而化吉、宜生合世爻；忌动而变凶、忌日月冲破。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth thriving and Output moving: money comes as wished, public or private. Wealth and Output void, in tomb or extinction (or harmed with no rescue): no business goes as wished. Yehe adds: they should turn into something favourable and generate or combine with the Self line; bad if they turn into something harmful or are clashed and broken by the Day or Month.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7764 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7764*

| --- | --- | --- |
| 财旺福兴，不问公私皆称意； | 妻财旺相、子孙兴旺，不论公事私事都能如意； | When Wealth is thriving and Output flourishing, things go as wished, public or private; |
| 财空福绝，不拘营运总违心。 | 妻财落空、子孙衰绝，不论做什么营生都不如意。 | when Wealth is void and Output extinguished, whatever the business, it goes against your wishes. |

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.03 有福无财，兄弟交重偏有望

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻财不上卦（或不动）而子孙在，兄弟发动反而有望：兄弟生子孙，子孙生财，只是来得慢。财爻空而兄动叫「避空」，过旬、到财爻生旺之日可得。财爻不空不伏却被兄弟劫，是下等卦。王洪绪、野鹤都要求子孙也动。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Wealth absent (or still) but Output present, a moving Peer line actually gives hope: Peer feeds Output and Output feeds Wealth, only slowly. Wealth void while Peer moves is called 'hiding in the void': the money comes after the ten-day cycle, on a day that births or empowers Wealth. Wealth neither void nor hidden yet robbed by Peer is the lowest grade. Wang Hongxu and Yehe both require Output to be moving too.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7767 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7767*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 有福无财， | 子孙在、妻财不在， | With Output present but no Wealth, |
| 兄弟交重偏有望； | 兄弟发动反而有希望； | a moving Peer line actually gives hope; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7768 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7768*

| 若得兄弟发动，无财可劫，则反生扶福德，财源有气，仍旧有望，但主迟滞耳。 | 但如果兄弟发动，卦里没有财可劫，它反而去生扶子孙，财源有了力量，仍然有希望，只是来得慢。 | But if the Peer line moves and finds no Wealth to rob, it generates and supports Output instead; the source of money gains force and there is still hope, only slow. |

| 过旬生旺日，财亦可得。 | 过了这一旬，到财爻生旺的日子，财也能得到。 | After the ten-day cycle passes, on a day that births or empowers Wealth, the money can still be had. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2659–2661 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, lines 2659–2661*

| 若财爻、动爻、子孙爻俱动于卦中者，更为可喜， | 如果财爻、兄爻（原文作「动爻」）、子孙爻都在卦中发动，更可喜， | If the Wealth line, the Peer line (the text says 'the moving line') and the Output line all move in the hexagram, better still, |

> **打架**:「有福无财」怎么读：旧注读作「财不上卦」，只要兄弟发动去生子孙就有望；王洪绪把「有、无」读成「动、伏」，而且认为有望全靠子孙也在动；野鹤也要子孙和兄弟同动，并说财、兄同动主阻隔。
>
> **Where they disagree:** How to read 'Output present, no Wealth': the old commentary takes it as Wealth absent from the hexagram, with hope as long as the moving Peer feeds Output; Wang Hongxu reads 'present/absent' as 'moving/hidden' and says the hope depends on Output moving as well; Yehe also wants Output and Peer moving together, and says Wealth and Peer moving together means obstruction.

> **按**:野鹤那句「若财爻、动爻、子孙爻俱动」，按下文「兄动生子、子动生财」，「动爻」应是「兄爻」；两个本子都作「动爻」，这里不改原文，只在译文里注明。
>
> **Note:** In Yehe's 'if the Wealth line, the moving line and the Output line all move', the next clause ('Peer feeds Output, Output feeds Wealth') shows 'the moving line' should be 'the Peer line'. Both editions read 'moving line'; we leave the text as it is and flag it in the translation.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.04 有财无福，官爻发动亦堪求

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦有财而子孙不现，本来一见兄弟就被劫；此时官鬼发动（或日辰为官鬼）克住兄弟，仍可求财，在官鬼生旺日得。卦中若有子孙，官鬼动反成阻滞。野鹤把条件改成「兄、财同动」时官鬼动可制兄。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Wealth is present but Output is not, any Peer line would rob it; a moving Pressure line (or a Pressure Day branch) that holds Peer down still makes the money obtainable, arriving on a day that births or empowers Pressure. If Output is present, a moving Pressure line becomes an obstruction instead. Yehe restates the condition as Peer and Wealth moving together, with a moving Pressure line restraining Peer.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.05 财福俱无，何异守株而待兔

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻财、子孙都不上卦，或上卦而休囚、空破、墓绝、受克冲：求财无望，不如按兵不动。例外（觉子）：日辰或月建本身是财，或财爻伏藏而旺，仍可求。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If neither Wealth nor Output is in the hexagram, or they are there but weak, void, broken, entombed, extinguished, controlled or clashed, there is no hope in pursuing money; better to hold still. Exception (Juezi): the Day or Month is Wealth itself, or the hidden Wealth line is strong.
