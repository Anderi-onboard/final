## LY.WEALTH.CA.06 财爻不动平安，遇杀逢空破费；外鬼牵玄武，官灾盗贼；劫杀临身克身，忌暗昧

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻不动，钱财平安；遇凶杀、逢空，终究破费。外卦的官鬼把玄武牵来，官灾、盗贼，重重后悔。劫杀临身又克身，暗昧私下的事必须忌讳。

## LY.WEALTH.CA.17 外爻无气应克身、遇空亡忧病；土爻持世有阻隔，阴爻持世女人淹滞

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:外卦的爻没有气、应又克身，若再遇空亡，要担心生病；土爻持世，怎么离得开；阴爻持世，主是阴人（女人）。小注：土爻持世主有阻隔，阴爻持世主被女人耽搁滞留。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The outer lines without breath and the Other controlling the Body, and if void besides, fear illness; an Earth line on the Self, how can you get away; a yin line on the Self points to a woman. The note: an Earth line on the Self means obstruction, a yin line on the Self means being held up by a woman.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.06 财库：入财时财库化成鬼，是折财耗害；如坎卦以火为财，火墓在戌

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:进财时，财的库（墓）化成了鬼，是折本耗损之象；比如坎卦以火为财，火墓在戌，就是这个例子。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When money is coming in but the store of Wealth turns into Pressure, that is loss of capital and wasting harm; take the Kan trigram, whose Wealth is Fire and whose Fire tomb is at Xu, as the example.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.08 海底眼：财逢月破空亡不可凭，伏财被飞爻克制白留意；最后两句只看财生不生命

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财遇月破、空亡，不可凭信；伏着的财被飞爻克制，白白留意；最后「争困撼钱财」几个字含义不明，只能读出：看财有没有生到命上。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth meeting Month-break or Void cannot be relied on; a hidden Wealth controlled by its flying line is attended to in vain; the last two lines, with their unclear words about contending, being pressed and shaking money, can only be read as: look at whether Wealth generates the natal line.

**《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1294 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 1294*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 月破空亡未可凭， | 财逢月破、空亡，不能凭信； | Wealth meeting Month-break or Void cannot be relied on; |
| 飞爻克制徒留意， | 伏着的财被飞爻克制，白白留意； | a hidden Wealth controlled by the flying line is attended to in vain; |
| 若求争困撼钱财， | 如果是求争、困、撼一类的钱财， | if the money sought is of the sort called contending, pressed or shaken loose, |
| 但看财生命者是。 | 只看财有没有生到命上就是了。 | look only at whether Wealth generates the natal line. |

> **按**:「争困撼钱财」四个字，「撼钱」在本书别处（六爻俱静类）和「赌斗」放在一起，可能指赌斗得来的钱；「争」「困」两字含义不明。这里照字译，不解释。「命」按本书「随官入墓」一节里「命随鬼入墓」的用法，指问卜人生年地支所在的爻。
>
> **Note:** The words about contending, being pressed and shaking loose money are unclear. 'Shaking money' stands beside 'gambling and contests' elsewhere in this book (the section on six quiet lines) and may mean money won in contests; the other two words are unclear. They are translated literally without explanation. 'The natal line' follows the sense of 'the natal line following Pressure into the tomb' elsewhere in this book: the line of the asker's birth-year branch.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.22 助鬼伤身：求财遇鬼带大小耗，财到手后非官即病，耗散费用

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:能生助官鬼的只有妻财；它不宜发动，一动，衰鬼变成旺鬼，旺鬼更凶。求财遇到鬼带大耗、小耗，这笔财到手以后，不是惹官司就是生病，钱都花耗了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The only line that can generate and help Pressure is Wealth; it should not move, for once it moves a weak Pressure becomes strong and a strong Pressure becomes more fierce. In seeking wealth, meeting Pressure carrying the Great or Small Waste stars, after this money is gained there is either a lawsuit or an illness and the money is spent away.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.28 寄物：卦六合、财旺静可寄；财衰动、空破、应动带凶神则有失

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:把东西寄放在别人那里：先要托给可托的人，因为人心难测，有见物起欺心的，有收藏不严招盗的，有安放不当弄坏东西的。卦六合可寄，六冲不可寄；月日和动爻合财、合世应可寄，冲财、冲世应不可寄。财旺静，或财动化子孙、化长生、化旺，无失；财衰动，或动化兄弟、官鬼、空破绝，有失。财化土朽腐，化木微黷，化火遭火灾，化水受潮漏，化金封识被拆开。世应空破、随官鬼入墓、被兄弟官鬼动伤，应爻动化兄鬼空破绝，都有失。应爻动，他心变；应动助鬼伤身世财，他与人合谋劫骗。应临玄武动偷窃，临朱雀动口舌，临勾陈动牵连，临螣蛇动惊恐，临白虎动吞没，临青龙动则善加保护。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Leaving goods with someone: first choose a person who can be trusted, for hearts are hard to fathom: some see the goods and turn deceitful, some store them carelessly and invite thieves, some put them in the wrong place and let them spoil. A Six-Combine hexagram: you may leave things; a Six-Clash: you may not. The Month, the Day and moving lines combining with Wealth, the Self or the Other: you may; clashing with them: you may not. Wealth strong and still, or Wealth moving into Output, into Birth or into strength: nothing is lost; Wealth weak and moving, or moving into Peer, Pressure, void, break or extinction: loss. Wealth turning into Earth: rot and decay; into Wood: slightly soiled; into Fire: fire; into Water: damp and leaks; into Metal: the seals broken open. Self and Other void or broken, following Pressure into the tomb, hurt by moving Peer or Pressure, or the Other moving into Peer, Pressure, void, break or extinction: loss. The Other moving: his mind has changed; the Other moving to help Pressure hurt the Body, the Self and the Wealth line: he plots with others to rob and cheat you. The Other moving on the Dark Tortoise: theft; on the Vermilion Bird: quarrels; on Hook Earth: entanglement; on the Coiling Snake: fright; on the White Tiger: devouring; on the Azure Dragon: good safekeeping.
