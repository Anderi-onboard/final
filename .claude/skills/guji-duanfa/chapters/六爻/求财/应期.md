## LY.WEALTH.CA.11 何日得钱财：应在身、财生旺之日；如乾以木为财，亥卯日为准

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:想问钱财哪一天得到：应在卦身和财爻生旺的日子；比如乾宫以木为财，就以亥、卯的日子为准。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask on what day the money is got: it answers on a day when the Body and Wealth are born or strong; for instance, if the Qian palace takes Wood as its Wealth, the days Hai and Mao are the standard.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.04 求财何日得：死财等财生的日月，生财不要拿财合当日期；空亡加退度，说得再多也要迟疑

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求财哪天到手：财爻衰死的，等财逢长生的日月才得；财爻得令的，不要拿合日当日期。上下相生的，可以取相克的时候；内克外的，白说合日；再遇空亡、退度，怎么说能得，也要迟疑。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** On which day the money arrives: for a dead Wealth, the day or month that gives it life; for a living Wealth, do not take the combining day as the date. When above and below generate each other one may take a time of control; when the inner controls the outer it is idle to speak of a combining day; with Void and a retreating degree added, however much one says it will come, hesitate.

**《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1264 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 1264*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡占求财何日得， | 凡是占求财哪天能得， | Whenever you divine on which day the money will come, |
| 死财财生日月得（5）， | 死财，等财逢生的日月能得； | a dead Wealth comes on the day or month that gives it life; |
| 生财财合莫为期， | 生财，财合的日子不要当日期， | a living Wealth: do not take the combining day as the date; |
| 以此推之必无惑（6）。 | 照这样推，一定没有疑惑。 | reasoned this way, there will be no doubt. |
| 上下相生可克时， | 上下相生的，可以取相克的时候； | When above and below generate each other, the time of control may be taken; |
| 克外徒言相合期， | 克外的，白白说相合的日期； | when the inner controls the outer, it is idle to name the day of combining; |
| 更想空亡兼退度， | 再想到空亡，加上退度， | and think further of Void together with a retreating degree: |
| 百般云得亦迟疑。 | 怎么说能得，也要迟疑。 | however one says it will come, still hesitate. |

> **打架**:《天玄赋》说「财合日辰，方能入手」，合日是入手的条件；这里说生财「财合莫为期」，合日不能当日期。两处不好并成一说：一处讲财与日辰合，一处讲财自己得令时的合日。整理本后面今人注把「财合」读成「入墓」，原文写的是「合」，本库照原文，不改。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The Tianxuan Fu says that when Wealth combines with the Day the money can be taken in hand, making the combining day a condition; this verse says that for a living Wealth 'Wealth combining' is not to be taken as the date. The two do not merge into one statement. A modern note in the transcription reads 'combining' as 'entering the tomb'; the original says 'combining', and this library follows the original.

> **按**:「上下相生可克时」一句，字面很难落实；这里照字译，不加解释。原文里的（5）（6）是整理本给今人注文编的号。
>
> **Note:** The line about 'above and below generate each other, a time of control is possible' is hard to pin down from its words; it is translated literally here without explanation. The (5) and (6) in the Chinese are numbers the transcription gave to modern notes.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.37 期日章·求财：财从长生到帝旺，再加墓库同归；空手求财看鬼爻同断；博戏看生旺

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求财的应期：财爻从长生到帝旺的时候，再加上墓库、同归的时候；空手求财，鬼爻也照这样断；博戏，看生旺也是这样推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The date for wealth: from Long Life to Emperor's Prime, and in addition the times of the tomb and of returning together; for seeking wealth with empty hands, judge the Pressure line the same way; for gambling, the thriving state is reckoned in the same way.

**《断易天机·期日章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 962 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The chapter on dates", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 962*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 求财长生至帝旺， | 求财，（财爻）从长生到帝旺， | For wealth, [Wealth] from Long Life through to Emperor's Prime, |
| 更加墓库及同归， | 再加上墓库和同归的时候； | and in addition the times of the tomb and of returning together; |
| 空手鬼爻同此断， | 空手求财，鬼爻也照这样断； | for seeking wealth with empty hands, the Pressure line is judged the same way; |
| 博戏生旺亦同推（1）。 | 博戏，看生旺也这样推。 | for gambling, the thriving state is reckoned in the same way. |

> **按**:「同归」书里没有解释，这里照字译。这首歌诀后面还有行人、病、失物、婚姻、求官、官司、阴宅、药、天时各条，属于别的问题域。原文里的（1）是整理本给今人注文编的号。
>
> **Note:** 'Returning together' is not explained in the book and is translated literally. The verse goes on with travellers, illness, lost things, marriage, seeking office, lawsuits, graves, medicine and weather, which belong to other domains. The (1) in the Chinese is a number the transcription gave to a modern note.

## LY.WEALTH.HDY.05 财旺相出现，日克之必有；日辰旺相，不克伏财而冲散飞爻，其财必有

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻旺相、又出现在卦上，日辰克它，钱一定有；日辰旺相，却不要克财，要看财伏在哪一爻下，日辰冲散那个飞爻，这笔钱一定有。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Wealth line is thriving and showing in the hexagram, the Day controlling it means the money is sure to come; when the Day is thriving it should not control the Wealth, and one looks at which line the Wealth is hidden under: if the Day clashes and scatters that flying line, the money is sure to come.

**《海底眼·求财》原文** — 《海底眼》第 656 行 · *Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Seeking wealth", base text; Haidi Yan, line 656*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡财爻旺相出现者， | 凡是财爻旺相、又出现在卦上的， | Whenever the Wealth line is thriving and showing in the hexagram, |
| 日克之必有。 | 日辰克它，一定有财。 | the Day controlling it means there is surely money. |

**《海底眼·求财》原文** — 《海底眼》第 658 行 · *Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Seeking wealth", base text; Haidi Yan, line 658*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 日辰旺相， | 日辰旺相， | When the Day is thriving, |
| 却不要克他， | 却不要去克它（财）， | it should not control it [the Wealth]; |
| 看财伏于何爻， | 看财伏在哪一爻下， | look at which line the Wealth is hidden under; |
| 日辰冲散飞爻， | 日辰冲散了飞爻， | if the Day clashes and scatters the flying line, |
| 其财必有。 | 这笔钱一定有。 | the money is sure to come. |

> **打架**:这里说「财旺相出现，日克之必有」；《黄金策》说「日伤妻位，财虽旺，而当日应无」（见 HJC.08）。两句都讲日辰克旺财，结论相反。一种可能是两处说的应期不同（这里似是日克之时财才发，那里是克日当天无财），书里没有明说，本库不裁。
>
> **Where they disagree:** This says 'a thriving Wealth that shows, the Day controlling it, surely has money'; the Huangjin Ce says 'the Day harming the Wealth place, though Wealth is thriving, has none on that day' (see HJC.08). Both speak of the Day controlling a thriving Wealth and reach opposite results. The two may be speaking of different dates, but the books do not say so and this library does not decide.

> **按**:第二段里的「他」我读作财；这一段说的是财伏藏的情形：日辰不要去克伏着的财，而要冲散压着它的飞爻。
>
> **Note:** In the second passage 'it' is read as the Wealth; the passage is about a hidden Wealth: the Day should not control the hidden Wealth but should clash and scatter the flying line that presses on it.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.25 要知何日得财，不离旺衰生合

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:定得财之日看财爻状态：有气→合日或本日；太旺、太过→墓库日；休囚、逢绝→生旺日；被合、入墓→冲开之日；逢冲→合日；动逢月破→填实或合日；静→冲日；旬空→出旬；伏藏→出现（提起）之日。财死绝而有子孙扶，按子孙断；卦无财而兄弟生子孙，按兄弟断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Time the money by the state of the Wealth line: with force, the day that combines with it or its own day; over-strong or in excess, its tomb day; resting, trapped or extinguished, a day that births or empowers it; combined or entombed, the day that clashes it open; clashed, the combining day; moving under a Month-break, the day that fills it in or combines with it; still, the clashing day; void, once the cycle passes; hidden, the day it appears. If Wealth is dead but propped up by Output, time it by Output; if there is no Wealth and Peer feeds Output, time it by Peer.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7837 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7837*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.26 欲决何时有利，但详春夏秋冬

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:货物何时得价：旧注按财爻五行当令的季节（木春、土夏而六月更好，同类中取更旺之月）；王洪绪说不能一概按五行，还要用冲合、生绝、墓开之法并看子孙爻。两家都说：财坐日辰的长生，一天比一天贵；坐帝旺，眼下正好，迟则贱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When goods will fetch a price: the old commentary goes by the season of the Wealth line's element (Wood in spring, Earth in summer and best in the sixth month, choosing the stronger month within a season); Wang Hongxu says not to rely on the element alone but also on clash and combination, birth and extinction, the opening of tombs, and the Output line. Both agree: Wealth at its Birth stage under the Day gains value day by day; at its Peak, now is the time, and later it cheapens.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7845 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7845*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 欲决何时有利， | 要断定什么时候有利， | To decide when there will be profit, |
| 但详春夏秋冬。 | 只要细看春夏秋冬（四时的旺衰）。 | look closely at spring, summer, autumn and winter. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7846–7848 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), lines 7846–7848*

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.20 财逢合而当遇

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财逢合正当时：应爻发动而在逢合之月得价，或财衰静而占时得日月生合，价眼下正好（书中例：贲卦，财逢月合，速去卖，得价一倍多）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth meeting a combination: the moment has come; either the moving Other line gets its price in the month that combines with it, or a weak still Wealth is generated or combined by the Day and Month at the time of casting and the price is right now (case: the hexagram Bi, Wealth combined by the Month; he went at once and sold at more than double).

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.21 财逢冲而当起

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财旺而静或旺而空，被占日的日辰冲：价渐涨；财静而休囚被日冲是日破，不作起价论。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A strong Wealth that is still, or strong but void, clashed by the Day of casting: the price will gradually rise. A still, resting or trapped Wealth clashed by the Day is broken, and must not be read this way.
