## LY.WEALTH.HJC.08 日伤妻位，财虽旺而当日应无

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻旺相、生合世爻或持世，本是必得；若日辰克财，当天得不到，要过了这一天才得。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A thriving Wealth line that generates, combines with or holds the Self line means certain gain; if the Day controls it, nothing comes that day; it comes after the day has passed.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.25 要知何日得财，不离旺衰生合

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:定得财之日看财爻状态：有气→合日或本日；太旺、太过→墓库日；休囚、逢绝→生旺日；被合、入墓→冲开之日；逢冲→合日；动逢月破→填实或合日；静→冲日；旬空→出旬；伏藏→出现（提起）之日。财死绝而有子孙扶，按子孙断；卦无财而兄弟生子孙，按兄弟断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Time the money by the state of the Wealth line: with force, the day that combines with it or its own day; over-strong or in excess, its tomb day; resting, trapped or extinguished, a day that births or empowers it; combined or entombed, the day that clashes it open; clashed, the combining day; moving under a Month-break, the day that fills it in or combines with it; still, the clashing day; void, once the cycle passes; hidden, the day it appears. If Wealth is dead but propped up by Output, time it by Output; if there is no Wealth and Peer feeds Output, time it by Peer.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7837 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7837*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 要知何日得财， | 要知道哪天得财， | To know on what day the money comes, |
| 不离旺衰生合； | 离不开旺、衰、生、合； | go by strength, weakness, generation and combination; |

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.26 欲决何时有利，但详春夏秋冬

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:货物何时得价：旧注按财爻五行当令的季节（木春、土夏而六月更好，同类中取更旺之月）；王洪绪说不能一概按五行，还要用冲合、生绝、墓开之法并看子孙爻。两家都说：财坐日辰的长生，一天比一天贵；坐帝旺，眼下正好，迟则贱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When goods will fetch a price: the old commentary goes by the season of the Wealth line's element (Wood in spring, Earth in summer and best in the sixth month, choosing the stronger month within a season); Wang Hongxu says not to rely on the element alone but also on clash and combination, birth and extinction, the opening of tombs, and the Output line. Both agree: Wealth at its Birth stage under the Day gains value day by day; at its Peak, now is the time, and later it cheapens.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7845 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7845*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 欲决何时有利， | 要断定什么时候有利， | To decide when there will be profit, |
| 但详春夏秋冬。 | 只要细看春夏秋冬（四时的旺衰）。 | look closely at spring, summer, autumn and winter. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7846–7848 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), lines 7846–7848*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡占货物何时得价，以财临五行断之。 | 凡是占货物什么时候卖得上价，看财爻的五行来断。 | In asking when goods will fetch a good price, judge by the element of the Wealth line. |
| 如木财，春月得价，一阳后亦好。 | 比如财属木，春天的月份得价，冬至（一阳生）以后也好。 | Wood Wealth, for instance, sells well in the spring months, and is good after the winter solstice too (when the first yang returns). |
| 土财，夏月有利，六月更美。余仿此。 | 财属土，夏天的月份有利，六月更好。其余照此类推。 | Earth Wealth profits in the summer months, best in the sixth month. The rest by analogy. |
| 又如财临辰土，二月不如三月；财临酉金，七月不如八月。余亦仿此。 | 又比如财是辰土，二月不如三月；财是酉金，七月不如八月。其余也照此类推。 | Again, Wealth on Chen Earth: the second month is not as good as the third; Wealth on You Metal: the seventh month is not as good as the eighth. The rest by analogy. |
| 又如财主日下长生之地，此货一日得价一日； | 再比如财爻处在日辰的长生之地，这货一天比一天值钱； | Again, if the Wealth line sits at its Birth stage under the Day, the goods gain value day by day; |
| 若坐帝旺，眼下正及时，稍迟则贱而无利。 | 如果坐帝旺，眼下正是时候，稍晚就便宜了、没有利。 | if it sits at its Peak, now is exactly the time; a little later the price drops and there is no profit. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2707 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2707*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡占货物何时得价，不可概以财临五行断之，如木财断春冬得价。 | 凡是占货物什么时候得价，不能一概按财爻的五行来断，比如木财就断春、冬得价。 | In asking when goods will fetch a price, do not simply go by the element of the Wealth line, as in judging that Wood Wealth sells in spring and winter. |
| 又宜以冲待合、合待冲、绝逢生、墓待开等法，又宜以子孙爻断。 | 还要用「冲等合、合等冲、绝逢生、墓等开」这些方法，也要看子孙爻来断。 | Use also the methods of a clashed line waiting for its combination, a combined line waiting for its clash, extinction meeting birth, and a tomb waiting to be opened; judge by the Output line as well. |
| 又如财坐长生之地，一日得一日价， | 又比如财爻坐长生之地，一天比一天值钱； | Again, Wealth at its Birth stage gains value day by day; |
| 若从帝旺，目下正及时，迟则贱而无利。 | 如果在帝旺，眼下正当时，晚了就便宜、没有利。 | at its Peak, now is the time; later the price drops and there is no profit. |

> **打架**:旧注主张按财爻五行的当令季节断得价之时；王洪绪说不能一概而论，还要用冲合、生绝、墓开的方法，并看子孙爻。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The old commentary times the price by the season of the Wealth line's element; Wang Hongxu says that is too simple and adds clash-and-combination, birth-and-extinction, tomb-opening and the Output line.
