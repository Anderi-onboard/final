## LY.WEALTH.HJC.27 合伙不嫌兄弟

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:合伙：世、应都是财爻最好；兄弟安静（或临卦身而世爻静）不嫌，因为合伙本要分财；兄弟发动则不宜。世应两动或落空：合不长久；相冲克：日后不和；化出兄官：多私心；世应生合而被刑冲克害：有人挑拨。野鹤：世、应临兄弟仍不吉，只有日月为财冲克世时反吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Partnership: best if both the Self and Other lines are Wealth; a still Peer (or Peer on the Body line with the Self line still) is no objection, since partners share the money anyway; a moving Peer is bad. Self and Other both moving or void: it will not last; clashing or controlling each other: they will fall out; turning into Peer or Pressure: self-interest; combining but punished, clashed or controlled by others: someone will sow discord. Yehe: Peer on the Self or Other line is still unlucky, except when the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7850 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7850*

> **打架**:兄弟到底嫌不嫌：经文说合伙不嫌兄弟；旧注、王洪绪说兄弟静可以、动不宜；野鹤引了经文，却接着说世、应临兄弟不吉、兄弟动也不吉，只有日月为财冲克世爻时反吉。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Is Peer really no objection? The classic says so for partnerships; the old commentary and Wang Hongxu allow a still Peer but not a moving one; Yehe quotes the classic but then says Peer on the Self or Other line is unlucky and a moving Peer too, the only exception being a Wealth Day or Month clashing with or controlling the Self line.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.32 日月相合，开行定主有人投

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:开行（牙行、铺面）：世应不空，财福齐全，官鬼有气，父兄衰静，为上吉；再得月建、日辰、动爻生合世爻，客人远近来投。卦无财而世爻只得生合：门口热闹、无实利（鬼旺财空同）；动出兄官多是非；世应空，开不成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Opening a brokerage or shop: Self and Other not void, Wealth and Output both present, Pressure with force, Resource and Peer weak and still is first-class; if the Month, the Day and moving lines also generate or combine with the Self line, clients come from near and far. With no Wealth, support for the Self line is only a busy doorway without profit (the same for strong Pressure and void Wealth); Peer or Pressure moving means quarrels; Self and Other void, it will not open.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7870 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7870*

## LY.WEALTH.YY.20 牙行：应爻、官爻旺相生合世，千金可托；休空冲伤世，百货难依

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:找牙行（中间人）：应爻、官爻旺相，又生合世爻，千金也可托付；休囚落空、或冲伤世，各样货物都难托付。应爻发动克世，或应上的财化成兄弟，难免被侵吞；应上的财发动生合世、或化成子孙，或应上的官鬼化成财，办事肯替你周旋。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Choosing a broker: the Other and Pressure lines strong and generating or combining with the Self, and a thousand gold can be entrusted to him; resting or void, or clashing with and hurting the Self, and no goods can be relied upon to him. The Other moving to control the Self, or the Other's Wealth turning into Peer: you cannot escape being fleeced. The Other's Wealth moving to generate or combine with the Self, or turning into Output, or the Other's Pressure turning into Wealth: he handles matters with care and works on your behalf.

**《易隐·贸易占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2928 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining for trade", base text; Yi Yin, line 2928*

| 休空冲伤世者，百货难依也。 | 衰弱落空、或冲伤世的，各样货物都难以托付。 | Resting or void, or clashing with and hurting the Self: no goods can be relied upon to him. |

## LY.WEALTH.YY.22 开店占大略：先问可开否，再问本利、合伙、店面、货物、收放

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:开店要依次问六件事：能不能开成；本钱多少、利润厚薄；合伙人好不好；店面大小；店里的货物；能不能放账收账。管辂分爻（六爻各管一件事）：上爻财路，五爻主人，四爻店屋，三爻基业，二爻伴侣，初爻心事。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Opening a shop asks six things in order: can it be opened; how much capital and how thick the profit; is the partner worthy; how big is the shopfront; what of the goods in the shop; and may credit be given and collected. Guan Lu's assignment of the lines: top, the money road; fifth, the master; fourth, the shop building; third, the foundation of the business; second, companions; first, the mind's intent.

**游南子(《易隐》引)** — 《易隐》第 2942 行 · *Master Younan (quoted in the Yi Yin); Yi Yin, line 2942*

| 先问可开否， | 先问开不开得成， | first asks whether it can be opened at all, |
| 次论本之多少，利之厚薄， | 再论本钱多少、利润厚薄， | then weighs how much capital and how thick or thin the profit, |
| 合伙之贤否， | 合伙人好不好， | whether the partner is worthy, |
| 店面之大小， | 店面大小， | how big the shopfront, |

## LY.WEALTH.YY.23 可开否：本宫、世爻、财爻带福禄龙喜旺相可开；死墓绝胎空破刑害不可开；六冲开不成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:能不能开：本宫、世爻、财爻带福、禄、龙、喜而旺相，财或子孙持世旺相且不犯刑害克破，应上的财旺而发动生合世，或四直临财生合世，可开。本宫、世爻、财爻逢死墓绝胎、空破刑害，或四直和应爻带兄弟官鬼、旺而发动克世，不可开；四直全是兄弟而世爻旺相，兄弟受制，开张安稳。六冲卦开不成，开成也不久；太岁生合世而有动爻冲太岁，开不满一年；月生合世而有动爻冲月，开不满一月。财化子孙，宜守老本行。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whether it can be opened: if the home palace, the Self line and the Wealth line carry Output, Emolument, the Dragon or Joy and are strong, or Wealth or Output on the Self is strong and free of punishment, harm, control and breaking, or the Other's Wealth is strong and moving to generate or combine with the Self, or the four rulers on Wealth generate or combine with the Self, it can be opened. If the home palace, the Self and the Wealth line meet death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, void, broken, punished or harmed, or the four rulers and the Other carry Peer or Pressure, strong and moving to control the Self, it cannot; but if the four rulers are all Peer while the Self is strong, Peer is held in check and the opening is safe. A Six-Clash hexagram cannot be opened, or if opened will not last; the Tai Sui generating or combining with the Self but a moving line clashing the Tai Sui: it will not last a year; the Month generating or combining with the Self but a moving line clashing the Month: not a month. Wealth turning into Output: keep to the old business.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.24 本利：卦旺财旺本多，卦衰财衰本少；财旺生扶得利；财不上卦、空破死绝，或兄劫动克世，折本

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:本钱和利润：卦旺财旺，本钱多；卦衰财衰，本钱少；财落在胎、墓，本钱少；财落空、绝，缺本钱。得利：世带旺财，财得生而库旺，财在绝地逢生，外卦的财生合内卦的世，财落在三爻或四爻（门户）旺而发动，三传临财发动生合世，财伏在世爻或子孙下得生得旺，财发动化成子孙、化长生、化旺，子孙发动化成财、化长生、化旺。折本：财不上卦，财空破死绝，三传临兄弟或劫煞发动，兄弟旺动克世，父母发动帮兄弟克世，应爻刑害克破世。财伏在父母下，失一半；伏在官鬼下，耗损多；伏在兄弟下，谨防口舌。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Capital and profit: hexagram and Wealth both strong, much capital; both weak, little; Wealth in the womb or tomb stage, little; Wealth void or extinct, no capital. Profit comes when: the Self carries a strong Wealth; Wealth is generated and its store strong; Wealth in extinction meets Birth; outer Wealth generates or combines with the inner Self; Wealth on the Gate or Door line (the third and fourth), strong and moving; the three rulers on Wealth, moving to generate or combine with the Self; Wealth hidden under the Self or under Output and generated or strong; Wealth moving into Output, into Birth or into strength; Output moving into Wealth, into Birth or into strength. Capital is lost when: Wealth is absent from the hexagram, or void, broken, dead or extinct; the three rulers on Peer or the Robbery star, moving; Peer strong and moving to control the Self; Resource moving to help Peer control the Self; or the Other line punishes, harms, controls or breaks the Self. Wealth hidden under Resource: half is lost; under Pressure: heavy losses; under Peer: beware of quarrels.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.25 合伙：应生合世益我，刑冲克害世损我；世应比和同心，六冲不协；兄弟旺静为吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:合伙：应生合世，对我有益；应刑冲克害世，对我有损。世生应、内生外，是成全别人；世克应、内克外，是借别人的力。世应比和，两人同心；世应六冲，两情不协；世应六合，两意相投；应静而逢冲，心意多变；世空，自己心疏懒；应空，对方心松弛；世应俱空，两家都要退悔；应遭刑害克破，对方多灾病。应带龙喜，交易和气；应临白虎兄弟刻傲，螣蛇兄弟琐碎，玄武兄弟欺诈，勾陈兄弟拙钝，朱雀兄弟多是非。应上的财伏在官鬼下、或应上的财发动化兄弟，必被侵占。游魂卦应临驿马发动，人不守店；归魂卦应临驿马发动，常在家。福神带朱雀加咸池发动，爱吹爱喝；应合财加咸池，有私情。离宫火应带月盲杀克金财，分不出银子成色。应旺，勤俭多能；应衰，疲弱无才；应先合后化冲，有始无终。卦无兄弟，店里没人掌管；兄弟以旺相安静为吉。与内外亲属合伙，以用爻为凭。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Partnership: the Other generating or combining with the Self helps me; the Other punishing, clashing with, controlling or harming the Self costs me. The Self generating the Other, inner generating outer: helping another succeed; the Self controlling the Other, inner controlling outer: drawing on another's strength. Self and Other in harmony: two of one mind; in a Six-Clash: feelings do not agree; in a Six-Combine: minds meet; the Other still but clashed: his mind keeps changing; the Self void: my heart is slack; the Other void: his intent is loose; both void: both sides withdraw and regret; the Other punished, harmed, controlled or broken: he has many mishaps and illnesses. The Other with the Dragon and Joy stars: dealings are gracious; on the White Tiger as Peer: sharp and arrogant; Coiling Snake as Peer: petty; Dark Tortoise as Peer: deceitful; Hook Earth as Peer: clumsy and dull; Vermilion Bird as Peer: many quarrels. The Other's Wealth hidden under Pressure, or moving into Peer: he will surely take your share. A Wandering-Soul hexagram with the Other on the Post Horse, moving: he does not stay in the shop; a Returning-Soul hexagram: he is often at home. Output on the Vermilion Bird with the Salt Pool star, moving: fond of boasting and drinking; the Other combining with Wealth and carrying the Salt Pool star: a private affair. In the Li palace, the Other on Fire with the Moon-Blind star, controlling Metal Wealth: cannot tell the fineness of silver. The Other strong: diligent, thrifty and capable; weak: feeble and without talent; combining then turning into a clash: a start with no finish. No Peer in the hexagram: the shop has no one in charge; Peer is auspicious when strong and still. For a partnership with relatives, inside or outside the family, take the Subject line as your guide.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.26 店面货物：父母为店，妻财为货；父旺店大，财旺货流；书纸店以父为用，宝珠古董店以财为用

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看店和货：父母代表店，妻财代表货。父旺而静，店里热闹平安；财旺而动，货物畅销。父旺是大店，父衰是小店；父逢冲开不长，父遇生合能长久。有财无父是有货无店，有父无财是有店无货；父旺财衰，店大利薄；父衰财旺，店小利厚。两个财：内卦被冲、外卦旺，本少利多；内卦旺、外卦被冲，本多利少。卦身和世爻跟官鬼入墓，白费力，利归别人。财爻遇冲暗动，带龙喜暗中得利，带玄武劫煞被骗失财。官鬼临财库发动，不是官司就是灾祸。父母带杀刃亡劫发动，或日雀会月雀发动，招争讼。珠宝古董杂货店以财为用，书纸巾帽衣服店以父为用，酒肴牲畜店以子为用；用神旺、财衰，货多利薄。另有郭雍说：开书籍纸货古董店，财旺而父母被克，吉；开当铺，父母旺而财被克，吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Reading the shop and its goods: Resource stands for the shop, Wealth for the goods. Resource strong and still: the shop is busy and peaceful; Wealth strong and moving: the goods circulate. Resource strong is a big shop, weak a small one; Resource clashed will not stay open long, generated or combined it stays open for good. Wealth without Resource is goods without a shop; Resource without Wealth is a shop without goods; Resource strong and Wealth weak: a big shop, thin profit; the reverse: a small shop, thick profit. Two Wealth lines, the inner clashed and the outer strong: little capital, much profit; the inner strong and the outer clashed: much capital, little profit. The Body and Self lines following Pressure into the tomb: labor in vain, the profit goes to others. A Wealth line clashed into hidden motion profits in secret with the Dragon and Joy stars, and is cheated of money with the Dark Tortoise or the Robbery star. Pressure moving on the tomb-store of Wealth: a lawsuit or a disaster. Resource moving with the killing stars, the Goat Blade, the Loss or the Robbery star, or the Day's Vermilion Bird meeting the Month's Vermilion Bird, moving: it invites lawsuits. Jewel, antique and sundry shops take Wealth as the Subject; shops of books, paper, scarves, hats and clothes take Resource; wine, food and livestock shops take Output; the Subject strong but Wealth weak: many goods, thin profit. Guo Yong adds: for a shop of books, paper goods and antiques, Wealth strong while Resource is controlled is auspicious; for a pawnshop, Resource strong while Wealth is controlled is auspicious.
