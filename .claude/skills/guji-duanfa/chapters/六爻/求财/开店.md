## LY.WEALTH.HJC.27 合伙不嫌兄弟

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:合伙：世、应都是财爻最好；兄弟安静（或临卦身而世爻静）不嫌，因为合伙本要分财；兄弟发动则不宜。世应两动或落空：合不长久；相冲克：日后不和；化出兄官：多私心；世应生合而被刑冲克害：有人挑拨。野鹤：世、应临兄弟仍不吉，只有日月为财冲克世时反吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Partnership: best if both the Self and Other lines are Wealth; a still Peer (or Peer on the Body line with the Self line still) is no objection, since partners share the money anyway; a moving Peer is bad. Self and Other both moving or void: it will not last; clashing or controlling each other: they will fall out; turning into Peer or Pressure: self-interest; combining but punished, clashed or controlled by others: someone will sow discord. Yehe: Peer on the Self or Other line is still unlucky, except when the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7850 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7850*

> **打架**:兄弟到底嫌不嫌：经文说合伙不嫌兄弟；旧注、王洪绪说兄弟静可以、动不宜；野鹤引了经文，却接着说世、应临兄弟不吉、兄弟动也不吉，只有日月为财冲克世爻时反吉。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Is Peer really no objection? The classic says so for partnerships; the old commentary and Wang Hongxu allow a still Peer but not a moving one; Yehe quotes the classic but then says Peer on the Self or Other line is unlucky and a moving Peer too, the only exception being a Wealth Day or Month clashing with or controlling the Self line.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.32 日月相合，开行定主有人投

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:开行（牙行、铺面）：世应不空，财福齐全，官鬼有气，父兄衰静，为上吉；再得月建、日辰、动爻生合世爻，客人远近来投。卦无财而世爻只得生合：门口热闹、无实利（鬼旺财空同）；动出兄官多是非；世应空，开不成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Opening a brokerage or shop: Self and Other not void, Wealth and Output both present, Pressure with force, Resource and Peer weak and still is first-class; if the Month, the Day and moving lines also generate or combine with the Self line, clients come from near and far. With no Wealth, support for the Self line is only a busy doorway without profit (the same for strong Pressure and void Wealth); Peer or Pressure moving means quarrels; Self and Other void, it will not open.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.08 世为己，应为人，大宜相合；财为本，福为基，最喜同兴

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:开行开店：世为自己、应为对方，宜相合；财为本、福为基，宜同旺。觉子：应爻代表谁，看问者本意——问伙计，应为伙计（世应生合同心；应生世他益我，世生应我益他；冲克生变，应克世我被瞒，世克应他从我；应临玄武兄爻暗中盗骗，应临官鬼克世明降灾非；世财应兄受累，世财应子得力；世应空合彼此虚诈）；问买卖，应为顾客（应生合世，生客成熟客；世应冲克，熟人成仇）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Opening a shop: the Self line is oneself, the Other the other party, and they should combine; Wealth is the capital, Output the foundation, and both should flourish. Juezi: who the Other line stands for depends on what is asked. Asking about a partner, it is the partner (generating or combining, of one mind; Other generating Self, he benefits me; Self generating Other, I benefit him; clashing or controlling, goodwill changes; Other controlling Self, he deceives me; Self controlling Other, he goes along; Other on the Dark Tortoise and Peer, secret fraud; Other on Pressure controlling Self, open trouble; Wealth on Self and Peer on Other, he drags me down; Wealth on Self and Output on Other, he helps; both void yet combining, both insincere). Asking about the trade, it is the customer (generating or combining with the Self line, strangers become regulars; clashing or controlling, old friends become enemies).

**《增删卜易·开行开店及各项铺面章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 11298 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Opening a brokerage, a shop or any storefront", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 11298*

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.09 鬼作灾非须忌动，财为活计畏刑冲

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:开店：官鬼发动招是非，克世最忌（鬼现爻中是非日有，鬼摇克世灾祸连绵）；财为资本，忌衰墓空破、动而变凶、日月刑冲克害；世爻与财爻落空，开不成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Opening a shop: a moving Pressure invites disputes, and controlling the Self line is worst (Pressure present, disputes daily; Pressure moving against the Self, one disaster after another); Wealth is the capital and fears being weak, entombed, void or broken, turning harmful, or being punished, clashed or controlled by the Day or Month; the Self line and Wealth void, the shop will not open.

| 鬼动招非，克世最忌。 | 官鬼动就招是非，来克世最忌。 | A moving Pressure invites disputes; controlling the Self line is the worst. |

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.10 鬼兄发动，有制何妨；随墓助伤，多凶少吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟、官鬼发动，有制则无妨（子孙制鬼，或日月冲克鬼爻）；兄、鬼同动时，官鬼反能制兄、护财（觉子）。忌世随鬼入墓、世动变凶；做买卖虽宜财动，官鬼克世时财动反成「助鬼伤身」。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Peer and Pressure moving do no harm if restrained (Output holding Pressure down, or the Day or Month clashing or controlling it); when Peer and Pressure move together, Pressure actually restrains Peer and shields the money (Juezi). Beware of the Self line following the ghost into the tomb or moving into harm; in trade a moving Wealth is welcome, but with Pressure controlling the Self line it only 'feeds the ghost that harms you'.

| 随墓助伤，多凶少吉。 | 随鬼入墓、助鬼伤身，多凶少吉。 | following the ghost into the tomb, or feeding the ghost that harms you: mostly bad, seldom good. |
