## LY.WEALTH.CA.08 兄弟化财须防圈套；妻财化兄弟折本伤财；子孙化财、财化子孙利多；青龙水上来登山涉海饶利

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟化成财，虽有财，要忌落入别人的圈套；折本伤财，只因妻财化成兄弟；子孙化成财，利润宜多，财化成子孙更不是小利；青龙落在水上，登山涉海多得利润。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Peer turning into Wealth, though there is money, take care not to fall into another's trap; capital and money are lost only because Wealth turned into Peer; Output turning into Wealth brings large profit, and Wealth turning into Output is better still; with the Azure Dragon on Water, climbing mountains and crossing seas brings much profit.

**《阐奥歌章·求财买卖章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3853 行 · *Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on seeking wealth and trade", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3853*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 兄弟化财虽有财， | 兄弟化成财，虽然有财， | Peer turning into Wealth, though there is money, |
| 切须忌落人圈圆。 | 千万要忌讳落入别人的圈套； | take great care not to fall into another's trap; |
| 折本伤财为甚么？ | 折本伤财是为什么？ | why are capital and money lost? |
| 只为妻财化兄弟。 | 只因为妻财化成了兄弟； | only because Wealth turned into Peer; |
| 子孙化财宜利多， | 子孙化成财，利润宜多； | Output turning into Wealth, profit should be large; |
| 财化子孙更非细。 | 财化成子孙，更不是小利； | Wealth turning into Output is better still; |
| 却又青龙水上来， | 又有青龙在水上来， | and if the Azure Dragon comes upon Water, |
| 登山涉海多饶利。 | 登山渡海多得利润。 | climbing mountains and crossing seas brings much profit. |

> **按**:「圈圆」是「圈圈」（圈套）的另一种写法，周易尚占作「圈圚」。「水上来」译作落在水爻上，是照字面取的读法。

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.14 四五处求财买卖，哪处得财：财旺相不空为吉，财初动克身居首；无财放弃；鬼化财易得，财化鬼失财；财化兄弟克，兄弟化财得，化子孙无忧

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:几处都想求财做买卖，看哪处得财：财爻旺相、不落空亡就吉；财爻刚发动又克身，这处最好；卦里没有财（如遁、蹇一类），说白了就是放弃；鬼化为财，财容易得；财化为鬼，有失财之忧；财化兄弟，终究被克；兄弟化财，终究能得；化为子孙，也没有忧。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With four or five places in mind for seeking wealth and trade, which one gets the money: Wealth thriving and not falling Void is auspicious; a Wealth line just moving and controlling the Body makes that place the first; a hexagram without Wealth, such as Dun or Jian, says plainly to give it up; Pressure changing into Wealth, the money is easy to get; Wealth changing into Pressure, worry over losing it; Wealth changing to Peer is finally controlled, Peer changing to Wealth is finally gained, and changing to Output is free of worry.

**《断易天机·占买卖第二十》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1326 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 20: Divining for buying and selling", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 1326*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 欲得财爻临旺相， | 想得财，要财爻临着旺相， | To get the money, the Wealth line must sit on a thriving state, |
| 不落空亡便为吉， | 不落空亡就是吉； | and not falling Void is auspicious; |
| 财爻初动兼克身， | 财爻刚发动，又克身， | a Wealth line just moving and controlling the Body: |
| 此处得云为第一。 | 这个地方说是第一。 | that place is called the first. |
| 卦里无财遁蹇流， | 卦里没有财，像遁、蹇这一类， | A hexagram with no Wealth, of the Dun and Jian kind, |
| 直说须云抛却休， | 直说就是：放弃算了； | says in plain words: give it up; |
| 鬼化为财财易得， | 鬼化为财，财容易得； | Pressure changing into Wealth, the money is easy to get; |
| 财化为鬼失财忧。 | 财化为鬼，有失财之忧； | Wealth changing into Pressure, there is worry over losing it; |
| 财化兄弟终言克， | 财化兄弟，终究说是被克； | Wealth changing into Peer, it is in the end said to be controlled; |
| 兄弟化财终可得， | 兄弟化财，终究能得； | Peer changing into Wealth, it can in the end be gained; |
| 化为子位亦无忧， | 化成子孙，也没有忧； | changing into Output, there is likewise no worry; |
| 以此推之必无惑。 | 照这样推断，一定没有疑惑。 | reasoned this way, there will be no doubt. |

> **按**:题目的「四五处」就是四五个候选的地方；「财爻初动」可读作「财爻刚发动」，也可读作「初爻上的财发动」，原文没有分明，这里取前一种。
>
> **Note:** The heading's 'four or five places' means four or five candidate places. 'The Wealth line first moving' can be read as 'just beginning to move' or as 'the Wealth on the first line moving'; the text does not settle it, and this entry takes the first.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.18 绝处逢生：求财遇妻财绝处逢生，财易求，利厚，不比旺相之财有限

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻被日辰绝了，又有生它的爻发动来相生，叫绝处逢生。求财遇到财爻绝处逢生，钱容易求、利也厚；不像旺相的财有个限度，新生出来的财没有穷尽。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Subject line is cut off by the Day but a line that gives it life moves to generate it, that is 'meeting life where there was severance'. In seeking wealth, Wealth meeting life where it was cut off is easy to seek and its profit is rich; unlike a thriving Wealth, which has a limit, newly born Wealth has none.

**《断易天机·绝处逢生》原文** — 《断易天机》第 56 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Meeting life where there was severance", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 56*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 且如申日占，卦遇用爻属木， | 比如在申日占卦，卦里遇到的用爻属木， | Suppose the divination is on a Shen day and the Subject line is Wood: |
| 木见申则绝，木爻无用矣。 | 木遇到申就绝了，木爻没有用了； | Wood meets Shen and is cut off, and the Wood line is of no use; |
| 此同无也。 | 这等于没有。 | this is the same as having none. |
| 若得水爻发动，水长生动来相生，木爻仍复有用。 | 如果有水爻发动，水在申长生，动来相生，木爻又重新有用了。 | But if a Water line moves, Water being born at Shen and coming to generate it, the Wood line is useful again. |
| 譬如人当困穷之际，得遇贵人扶持， | 就像人在穷困的时候，遇上贵人扶持， | It is like a person at a time of poverty and hardship who meets a noble patron's help: |
| 必有寒谷回春之象，此乃是绝处逢生也。 | 一定有寒谷回春的样子，这就是绝处逢生。 | there is surely the look of spring returning to a cold valley; this is 'meeting life where there was severance'. |

**《断易天机·绝处逢生》原文** — 《断易天机》第 61 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Meeting life where there was severance", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 61*

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.19 合处逢冲：占财遇之，财将入手而不得

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里本来世应相生、六爻相合，事事可成，却在冲它的日子占到，叫合处逢冲；占财遇到，钱将要到手却拿不到。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When a hexagram in which Self and Other generate each other and the six lines combine, so that everything can succeed, is divined on a day that clashes it, that is 'meeting a clash where there was combination'; in divining for wealth, the money is about to come to hand and is not gained.

**《断易天机·合处逢冲》原文** — 《断易天机》第 68 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Meeting a clash where there was combination", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 68*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 且如占得雷地豫卦。 | 比如占到雷地豫卦。 | Suppose the divination gets the Yu hexagram, Thunder over Earth. |
| 世应相生，六爻相合，吉无不利，乃事事可成之象也。 | 世应相生，六爻相合，吉利没有不顺的，是事事可成的样子。 | Self and Other generate each other and the six lines combine; all is auspicious and nothing goes against it; it is the look of everything succeeding. |
| 却在子日占得， | 却是在子日占到的， | But it is got on a Zi day, |
| 子冲应上午、害世上未， | 子冲应爻上的午、又害世爻上的未， | and Zi clashes the Wu on the Other line and harms the Wei on the Self line; |
| 此乃合处逢冲也。 | 这就是合处逢冲。 | this is 'meeting a clash where there was combination'. |

**《断易天机·合处逢冲》原文** — 《断易天机》第 71 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Meeting a clash where there was combination", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 71*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占财遇之， | 占财遇到这种情形， | In divining for wealth, meeting this: |
| 财将入手而不得。 | 钱将要到手却拿不到。 | the money is about to come to hand and is not gained. |

> **按**:书里紧接着说：占讼和占病遇到合处逢冲反而好，因为合则事成、冲则灾散。这一条只取求财。
>
> **Note:** The book goes on to say that in divining a lawsuit or an illness, this same pattern is welcome, since combination brings the matter about and a clash scatters the calamity. This entry takes only wealth.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.44 财爻发动，买卖交官财吉利；财化子好望财，财化兄财少成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻发动，克文书，应试求官都落空，做买卖、结交官府对财有利；财化成子孙有望得财，化成兄弟钱财少成、熟人之间要避嫌；财化成官鬼要防走失，化成文书办事和谐。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Wealth line moving and controlling documents: exams and office come to nothing, but trading and dealing with officials favour the money; Wealth changing into Output gives hope of gaining money, into Peer little money is made and with acquaintances one should avoid suspicion; into Pressure guard against loss, into Resource matters go smoothly.

## LY.WEALTH.TXF.03 鬼化为财克世大吉，财爻化鬼逢空全凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼化财（鬼送财）而来克世：大吉；若世爻去克官鬼则无财。财化官鬼：从有到无，再逢空为全凶。《断易天机》本：得日辰扶合、动爻生扶为「财入鬼手」，可无伤；旺财化衰鬼，财不全；衰财化旺鬼，防加倍折财。财化财：本卦财旺而变出的财无气，宜速求；反之宜待。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure turning into Wealth ('the ghost bringing money') that controls the Self line: very lucky; if the Self line controls the Pressure line instead, no money. Wealth turning into Pressure: something becomes nothing, and void as well it is wholly unlucky. The Duanyi Tianji text: with the Day supporting or combining, or a moving line generating it, it is 'money in the ghost's hand' and may do no harm; strong Wealth into weak Pressure, the money is not whole; weak Wealth into strong Pressure, beware of double loss. Wealth into Wealth: a strong original turning into a forceless one, seek quickly; the reverse, wait.

**《天玄赋·求财章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 4566 行 · *Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 4566*

## LY.WEALTH.TXF.04 父化妻爻当涉艰辛，财连兄位积聚当分

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母化财：先难后得；父旺财衰，辛苦多财少；父衰财旺，用力少财多。财化兄弟：有侵夺，积蓄也要分散（本就与人分利则无妨）。兄弟化财：先散后聚。《断易天机》本：父母、兄弟化财，虽辛苦亦吉；父爻带贵人有依靠，带太岁凶煞防因财染病。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Resource turning into Wealth: difficulty first, gain later; Resource strong and Wealth weak, much toil and little money; the reverse, little effort and much money. Wealth turning into Peer: encroachment, savings scattered (no harm if you were sharing the profit anyway). Peer turning into Wealth: scattered first, gathered later. The Duanyi Tianji text: Resource or Peer turning into Wealth is lucky however hard the way; the Resource line with the Noble star means someone to lean on, with the Tai Sui's killing star beware of falling ill over the money.

**《天玄赋·求财章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 4569 行 · *Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 4569*

## LY.WEALTH.YL.25 财是根本不可变：化鬼化兄则耗散无结果，化子孙则利禄津津

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财是根本，不能变动：化成官鬼、变成兄弟，财就被耗散，两条路都没有好处，终究难有结果；财化成子孙，则利禄滚滚，永远没有边际。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth is the root and must not change: turning into Pressure or Peer, Wealth is consumed and scattered, neither way does any good, and in the end there is no result; Wealth turning into Output, profit and emolument flow on without limit.

## LY.WEALTH.YM.12 脱货宜动，留货宜静；发伏藏宜伏而现，造作宜变而建；财临变爻又值日月建为吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:脱货宜动，留货宜静；发掘伏藏的宜伏着的财出现，造作的宜财变出来而得建旺。注：发伏，如开矿淘沙取宝藏；造作，如木匠、土工、针线、织机；财临变爻，再遇日建或月建，就吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To sell off goods, movement suits; to hold goods, stillness suits; for unearthing hidden things, a hidden Wealth should come to show, and for fabrication, a Wealth that changes should be raised by the month or day.
