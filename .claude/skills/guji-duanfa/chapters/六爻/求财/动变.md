## LY.WEALTH.HJC.06 父兄皆动，无殊缘木以求鱼

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母、兄弟同时发动：父母克子孙（断财源），兄弟克妻财（劫财），求财必不可得；父化兄、兄化父同论。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Resource and Peer both move, Resource cuts off Output (the source of money) and Peer robs Wealth: the money cannot be had. Resource turning into Peer, or Peer into Resource, is judged the same.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7776 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7776*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父兄皆动， | 父母、兄弟都发动， | When Resource and Peer both move, |
| 无殊缘木以求鱼。 | 和爬到树上去捉鱼没有两样。 | it is like climbing a tree to catch fish. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7777 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7777*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父母为绝源杀，兄弟为劫财神。 | 父母是断绝财源的煞，兄弟是劫财的神。 | Resource is the killer of the source; Peer is the robber of wealth. |
| 二者发动，财福俱伤。 | 这两爻发动，妻财和子孙都受伤。 | When both move, Wealth and Output are both harmed. |
| 用神既损，求之必如缘木求鱼，必不可得。 | 用神已经受损，去求就像缘木求鱼，一定得不到。 | With the Subject damaged, seeking the money is climbing a tree for fish: it cannot be had. |
| 父化兄，兄化父，亦然。 | 父母化兄弟、兄弟化父母，也一样。 | Resource turning into Peer, or Peer into Resource, is the same. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2667 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2667*

**野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 11088 行 · *The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 11088*

| 父动克福神，兄动克财爻，此二爻动于卦中，凡图无济。 | 父母动克子孙，兄弟动克财爻，这两爻在卦中发动，凡是谋求都没用。 | Resource moving controls Output, Peer moving controls the Wealth line; when these two move in the hexagram, no plan succeeds. |

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.15 福变财生，滚滚财源不竭

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙发动（生财），利长久；子孙化财、财化子孙，而且生合世爻、卦身，财源不绝。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A moving Output line (which feeds Wealth) means lasting profit; Output turning into Wealth, or Wealth into Output, especially generating or combining with the Self and Body lines, means money that keeps coming.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7803 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7803*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 福变财生， | 子孙动而变出财来， | When Output turns into Wealth, |
| 滚滚财源不竭； | 财源滚滚，用之不竭； | the money rolls in and never runs dry; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7804 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7804*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占财，得子孙发动，利必久远。 | 占财时，子孙发动，利一定长久。 | In a money question, a moving Output line means a lasting profit. |
| 更化财爻，生合身世，乃绵绵不绝之象，尽求则尽有也。 | 再变出财爻，生合卦身、世爻，是绵绵不绝之象，要多少有多少。 | If it also turns into a Wealth line that generates or combines with the Body and Self lines, the money never stops: whatever you ask for, you get. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2685 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2685*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.17 父化财，必辛勤而有得

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父化财：辛劳后得。兄化财：先散后聚（后利前不利）。官化财：利公门、谒贵、九流手艺求财；私财先惊后喜、先阻后得；若官鬼克世，为「助鬼伤身」，不吉（王洪绪）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Resource turning into Wealth: gain after toil. Peer turning into Wealth: first scattered, then gathered (good later, not at first). Pressure turning into Wealth: favours official money, patrons and people who live by a trade; for private money, fright then joy, obstruction then gain; if Pressure controls the Self line it is 'feeding the ghost that harms you', unlucky (Wang Hongxu).

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7811 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7811*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父化财， | 父母化出妻财， | Resource turning into Wealth: |
| 必辛勤而有得； | 一定要辛勤才有所得； | gain comes only through hard work; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7812 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7812*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 父化财，不自然而得，必勤劳后得。 | 父母化财，财不会自然到手，一定要勤劳之后才得。 | Resource turning into Wealth: the money does not come of itself; it comes after hard work. |
| 兄化财，先散后聚；或利于后，不利于前。 | 兄弟化财，先散后聚；或者后面有利，前面不利。 | Peer turning into Wealth: first scattered, then gathered; or favourable later, not at first. |
| 官化财，最利公门谒贵，及九流艺术之人，求财十分有望。 | 官鬼化财，最利于在官府、拜见权贵求财，以及九流、手艺之人，求财很有希望。 | Pressure turning into Wealth is best for money sought in official quarters or from patrons, and for people who live by a trade or an art; the money is very likely. |
| 私财，则先惊后喜，或先阻后得。 | 如果是私人的财，就先惊后喜，或先有阻碍后得到。 | For private money, first a fright and then joy, or first obstruction and then gain. |

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.18 财化鬼，防耗折而惊忧

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财化官鬼最凶：损折虚耗、有惊险，伤世则因财致祸。财化兄弟：与人分利、先聚后散（王洪绪把它和财化鬼并列为最凶）。财化父母：得后艰辛或只一次。财化死墓空绝：有名无实；若生合世爻，宜抢先去取。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth turning into Pressure is the worst: losses, waste and danger, and disaster brought on by the money if it harms the Self line. Wealth turning into Peer: sharing the profit, gathered then scattered (Wang Hongxu ranks it with Wealth-into-Pressure as the worst). Wealth turning into Resource: hardship after the gain, or only once. Wealth turning into death, tomb, void or extinction: a name without substance; if it generates or combines with the Self line, act at once.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7814 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7814*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 财化鬼， | 妻财化出官鬼， | Wealth turning into Pressure: |
| 防耗折而惊忧。 | 要防耗损折本，还有惊忧。 | beware of losses, and of fright and worry. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7815 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7815*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 财化官最凶。主损折虚耗，自有惊险。 | 财化官鬼最凶，主亏损虚耗，还有惊险。 | Wealth turning into Pressure is the worst: losses and waste, and danger besides. |
| 更伤世爻，切恐因财致祸。 | 如果再伤世爻，就怕因财惹祸。 | If it also harms the Self line, beware of disaster brought on by the money. |
| 财化兄，主与人分利；或先聚后散；或利于前，不利于后。 | 财化兄弟，主和别人分利；或先聚后散；或前面有利、后面不利。 | Wealth turning into Peer: sharing the profit with others; or first gathered, then scattered; or favourable at first, not later. |
| 财化父，主得后艰辛；或只许一度。 | 财化父母，主得财以后很辛苦；或者只有一次。 | Wealth turning into Resource: hardship after the gain; or it happens only once. |
| 财化死墓空绝，是有虚名，无实利之象。 | 财化入死、墓、空、绝，是有虚名、无实利之象。 | Wealth turning into death, tomb, void or extinction: a name without real profit. |
| 若得生合世爻，则上前有功，稍迟则无也。 | 如果变出的爻生合世爻，就是抢先去做有收获，稍晚就没了。 | If the changed line generates or combines with the Self line, going at once pays; a little later there is nothing. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2691 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2691*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 财化官鬼 或化兄最凶，主损折驳耗， | 财化官鬼或化兄弟最凶，主亏损、折耗， | Wealth turning into Pressure or into Peer is the worst: losses and waste; |
| 更见世爻有伤，恐因财致祸。 | 再见世爻受伤，恐怕因财惹祸。 | if the Self line is harmed as well, beware of disaster brought on by the money. |

> **打架**:财化兄弟有多凶：旧注说是与人分利、先聚后散；王洪绪把它和财化官鬼并列为「最凶」。
>
> **Where they disagree:** How bad is Wealth turning into Peer? The old commentary: sharing the profit, gathered then scattered. Wang Hongxu puts it alongside Wealth turning into Pressure as 'the worst'.
