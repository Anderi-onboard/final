## LY.WEALTH.CA.08 兄弟化财须防圈套；妻财化兄弟折本伤财；子孙化财、财化子孙利多；青龙水上来登山涉海饶利

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟化成财，虽有财，要忌落入别人的圈套；折本伤财，只因妻财化成兄弟；子孙化成财，利润宜多，财化成子孙更不是小利；青龙落在水上，登山涉海多得利润。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Peer turning into Wealth, though there is money, take care not to fall into another's trap; capital and money are lost only because Wealth turned into Peer; Output turning into Wealth brings large profit, and Wealth turning into Output is better still; with the Azure Dragon on Water, climbing mountains and crossing seas brings much profit.

**《阐奥歌章·求财买卖章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3853 行 · *Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on seeking wealth and trade", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3853*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 兄弟化财虽有财， | 兄弟化成财，虽然有财， | Peer turning into Wealth, though there is money, |
| 切须忌落人圈圆。 | 千万要忌讳落入别人的圈套； | take great care not to fall into another's trap; |
| 折本伤财为甚么？ | 折本伤财是为什么？ | why are capital and money lost? |
| 只为妻财化兄弟。 | 只因为妻财化成了兄弟； | only because Wealth turned into Peer; |
| 子孙化财宜利多， | 子孙化成财，利润宜多； | Output turning into Wealth, profit should be large; |
| 财化子孙更非细。 | 财化成子孙，更不是小利； | Wealth turning into Output is better still; |

> **Note:** 'Quan yuan' is another way of writing the word for a trap; the Zhouyi Shangzhan has yet another variant. 'Comes upon Water' is read literally as falling on a Water line.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.06 父兄皆动，无殊缘木以求鱼

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母、兄弟同时发动：父母克子孙（断财源），兄弟克妻财（劫财），求财必不可得；父化兄、兄化父同论。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Resource and Peer both move, Resource cuts off Output (the source of money) and Peer robs Wealth: the money cannot be had. Resource turning into Peer, or Peer into Resource, is judged the same.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.15 福变财生，滚滚财源不竭

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙发动（生财），利长久；子孙化财、财化子孙，而且生合世爻、卦身，财源不绝。

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.17 父化财，必辛勤而有得

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父化财：辛劳后得。兄化财：先散后聚（后利前不利）。官化财：利公门、谒贵、九流手艺求财；私财先惊后喜、先阻后得；若官鬼克世，为「助鬼伤身」，不吉（王洪绪）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Resource turning into Wealth: gain after toil. Peer turning into Wealth: first scattered, then gathered (good later, not at first). Pressure turning into Wealth: favours official money, patrons and people who live by a trade; for private money, fright then joy, obstruction then gain; if Pressure controls the Self line it is 'feeding the ghost that harms you', unlucky (Wang Hongxu).

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.18 财化鬼，防耗折而惊忧

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财化官鬼最凶：损折虚耗、有惊险，伤世则因财致祸。财化兄弟：与人分利、先聚后散（王洪绪把它和财化鬼并列为最凶）。财化父母：得后艰辛或只一次。财化死墓空绝：有名无实；若生合世爻，宜抢先去取。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth turning into Pressure is the worst: losses, waste and danger, and disaster brought on by the money if it harms the Self line. Wealth turning into Peer: sharing the profit, gathered then scattered (Wang Hongxu ranks it with Wealth-into-Pressure as the worst). Wealth turning into Resource: hardship after the gain, or only once. Wealth turning into death, tomb, void or extinction: a name without substance; if it generates or combines with the Self line, act at once.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7814 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7814*

## LY.WEALTH.HZL.10 动变：父母化财先难后易，财化父母先易后难；财化兄弟先聚后散，兄弟化财先散后聚；前卦有财速谋，前卦无财迟取

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母化成财，先难后易；财化成父母，先易后难。财化成兄弟，先聚后散；兄弟化成财，先散后聚。本卦有财、变卦无财，赶快谋求有得，迟了就没有；本卦无财、变卦有财，迟一些取才有，眼下还不当值。钱财多少，要凭爻的衰旺来定。书中例：九二的财动，求就一定有；九五的比肩爻动，被阻拦还没得到；买卖同样推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Resource turning into Wealth: hard first, easy later; Wealth turning into Resource: easy first, hard later. Wealth turning into Peer: gathered first, scattered later; Peer turning into Wealth: scattered first, gathered later. Wealth in the first hexagram and none in the later one: act quickly and you get it, late and you do not; no Wealth in the first and Wealth in the later one: take it late and you have it, it is not on duty now. How much money there is must be decided by the strength or weakness of the line. Case in the book: the Wealth on the second line moves, so seeking will surely bring it; the Peer on the fifth line moves, so it is blocked and not yet obtained; trade is worked out the same way.

## LY.WEALTH.TXF.03 鬼化为财克世大吉，财爻化鬼逢空全凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼化财（鬼送财）而来克世：大吉；若世爻去克官鬼则无财。财化官鬼：从有到无，再逢空为全凶。《断易天机》本：得日辰扶合、动爻生扶为「财入鬼手」，可无伤；旺财化衰鬼，财不全；衰财化旺鬼，防加倍折财。财化财：本卦财旺而变出的财无气，宜速求；反之宜待。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure turning into Wealth ('the ghost bringing money') that controls the Self line: very lucky; if the Self line controls the Pressure line instead, no money. Wealth turning into Pressure: something becomes nothing, and void as well it is wholly unlucky. The Duanyi Tianji text: with the Day supporting or combining, or a moving line generating it, it is 'money in the ghost's hand' and may do no harm; strong Wealth into weak Pressure, the money is not whole; weak Wealth into strong Pressure, beware of double loss. Wealth into Wealth: a strong original turning into a forceless one, seek quickly; the reverse, wait.

**《天玄赋·求财章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 4566 行 · *Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 4566*

## LY.WEALTH.TXF.04 父化妻爻当涉艰辛，财连兄位积聚当分

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母化财：先难后得；父旺财衰，辛苦多财少；父衰财旺，用力少财多。财化兄弟：有侵夺，积蓄也要分散（本就与人分利则无妨）。兄弟化财：先散后聚。《断易天机》本：父母、兄弟化财，虽辛苦亦吉；父爻带贵人有依靠，带太岁凶煞防因财染病。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Resource turning into Wealth: difficulty first, gain later; Resource strong and Wealth weak, much toil and little money; the reverse, little effort and much money. Wealth turning into Peer: encroachment, savings scattered (no harm if you were sharing the profit anyway). Peer turning into Wealth: scattered first, gathered later. The Duanyi Tianji text: Resource or Peer turning into Wealth is lucky however hard the way; the Resource line with the Noble star means someone to lean on, with the Tai Sui's killing star beware of falling ill over the money.

## LY.WEALTH.YL.25 财是根本不可变：化鬼化兄则耗散无结果，化子孙则利禄津津

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财是根本，不能变动：化成官鬼、变成兄弟，财就被耗散，两条路都没有好处，终究难有结果；财化成子孙，则利禄滚滚，永远没有边际。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth is the root and must not change: turning into Pressure or Peer, Wealth is consumed and scattered, neither way does any good, and in the end there is no result; Wealth turning into Output, profit and emolument flow on without limit.
