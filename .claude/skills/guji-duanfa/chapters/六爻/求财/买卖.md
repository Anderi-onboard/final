## LY.WEALTH.HJC.36 间兴害世，置货者当虑牙人

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:买货：应爻生合世爻易成，刑克世爻难买。代表货物的爻（六畜看子孙、五谷看妻财、布帛看父母）太过货多、不及货少、空伏无货。兄鬼发动防骗；兄鬼在间爻是牙人虚诈、口舌；间爻伤克世爻防牙人谋财；出门买货而应爻空，多不顺。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Buying goods: the Other line generating or combining with the Self line makes it easy; punishing or controlling it makes it hard. The line for the goods (Output for livestock, Wealth for grain, Resource for cloth) in excess means plenty, falling short means few, void or hidden means none. Peer and Pressure moving: beware of swindlers; on the between lines, a false broker or quarrels; a between line harming or controlling the Self line: the broker is after your goods; travelling to buy with the Other line void, it mostly goes badly.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7883 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7883*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.37 停榻者，喜财安而鬼静

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:囤货：财爻不宜动、不宜空，不宜被动爻日辰克劫；财坐胎、养、长生，日后得利。兄官交变或同动防盗；兄弟独发多耗折；水爻父母（王洪绪：父化官鬼）刑克世爻，货被雨水淹腐；财化死墓空绝，日后价贱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Holding goods in store: Wealth should neither move nor be void, nor be attacked by moving lines or the Day; Wealth at the Embryo, Nurture or Birth stage means profit later. Peer and Pressure turning into each other or moving together: beware of theft; Peer moving alone: waste and loss; a Water Resource line (Wang Hongxu: Resource turning into Pressure) punishing or controlling the Self line: the goods are spoiled by rain; Wealth turning into death, tomb, void or extinction: the price will fall.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7887 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7887*

| 官鬼若动，兴灾作祸莫测； | 官鬼若发动，会兴起难以预料的灾祸； | If Pressure moves, it brings unforeseeable trouble; |

>
> **Note:** Wang Hongxu's edition reads 'holding and disposing' in the base text, and his note ends 'so for goods to be disposed of, all six lines should be still', which contradicts the very next line ('for goods to be sold off, Wealth should move'). The old-commentary edition reads 'goods held in store'. From the context this rule is about stockpiling, and 'dispose' in Wang's edition is probably a corruption. We quote it as it stands.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.38 脱货者，宜财动而身兴

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卖货：财动易脱、世动易卖；应空无买主，世空自己卖不成；动变日辰来生合，有人争买；遇刑冲多阻；财在内卦动（生世）本地卖，在外卦动外地卖（财持世而子孙在外卦动，也宜外卖）；动而遇合将成不成，动而逢空欲卖不卖；卦无官鬼难成交。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Selling goods: a moving Wealth means they go easily, a moving Self line that they sell easily; a void Other line, no buyer; a void Self line, you fail to sell; moving and changed lines and the Day generating or combining, buyers compete; punishment or clash, obstacles; Wealth moving in the inner trigram (generating the Self line), sell locally; in the outer, sell elsewhere (also when Wealth is on the Self line and Output moves in the outer trigram); moving but combined, almost closed yet not; moving but void, wanting to sell yet not; no Pressure in the hexagram, hard to close.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.15 财爻衰者宜停脱，妻位当时可脱之

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:囤货卖货看财爻四时旺衰：财衰宜囤，财旺宜卖（如春天木财旺宜速卖，金水土财宜囤，火财为相也不宜囤）；忌财空破、动而变回头克。觉子：财衰可买，但要查它有没有根——有根将来再涨，无根终难卖出。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Stockpiling or selling goes by the seasonal strength of the Wealth line: weak Wealth, stockpile; strong Wealth, sell (in spring Wood Wealth is thriving, sell fast; Metal, Water or Earth Wealth, stockpile; Fire is strong in spring, do not stockpile). Void or broken Wealth, or Wealth turning into its own control, is bad. Juezi: weak Wealth can be bought, but check its root; rooted, it will rise again; rootless, it will never sell.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.16 货有定时，须凭时令；物无定者，指利而占

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:有固定旺季的货（麻葛夏、棉花冬）凭时令断价；没有固定时节的货（木料、绸缎）要指明求利单独占。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Goods with a set season (hemp and linen in summer, cotton in winter) are priced by the season; goods without one (timber, silk) need a casting that asks specifically about the profit.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.17 财化进神，其价正涨；兄爻持世，破败之端

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财化进神：价正在涨，不可收购囤积；财化退：价将落，有货速卖。兄弟持世或兄弟发动：破财之兆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth turning Advancing: the price is rising, do not buy to store; turning Retreating: the price will fall, sell what you hold at once. Peer on the Self line or Peer moving: a sign of losing money.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.23 六冲须要别图，六合还宜坐守

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卖货守还是动：旧说六冲宜另图、六合宜坐守；野鹤屡验：六冲而财旺不必改、财衰改也无益，应再占「更改如何」，财世两旺许改，衰则耐守；六合也看财爻：财持世而动不可坐守，衰而静则守待旺时。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Selling, stay or move: the old rule says Six-Clash, change plans; Six-Combine, stay put. Yehe tested it: with a Six-Clash and Wealth strong there is no need to change, with Wealth weak changing does not help; cast again asking 'what if I change', and allow it only if Wealth and the Self line are both strong, otherwise wait. With a Six-Combine, look at Wealth too: on the Self line and moving, do not sit still; weak and still, wait for its season.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.24 财衰变旺，先屈后伸；财旺变衰，先金后土

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财衰动变旺：眼下贱、将来涨；财旺动变衰：卖要急、买不可收。财爻旺时卖，衰时买。内卦财衰外卖，世临旺财近卖；财化进神往外发卖，世爻化退回近处出脱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A weak Wealth moving into strength: cheap now, dearer later; a strong Wealth moving into weakness: sell fast, do not buy. Sell when Wealth is strong, buy when it is weak. Wealth weak in the inner trigram, sell elsewhere; a strong Wealth on the Self line, sell near home; Wealth turning Advancing, go further afield; the Self line turning Retreating, come back and sell nearby.
