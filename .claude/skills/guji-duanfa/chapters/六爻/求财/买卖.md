## LY.WEALTH.CA.05 财生库旺聚钱财；财休囚货贱如尘宜措置，财旺相货贵如金宜乘早弃

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.12 郭璞论买卖：财爻克世最好，身旺相与财并、无煞不空；贞旺悔囚先赢，悔旺贞休后赢

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:贩运买卖，财爻克世最好；卦身旺相又与财并，再没有凶煞，不落空亡，就能成；贞旺悔囚，起初买卖就赢，悔旺贞休，要后来才赢；卦里上下互相刑克，即使有财爻，也只是空有名声。「贞」「悔」指内外卦还是本卦之卦，见「打架」。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For trading goods, a Wealth line controlling the Self is best; the Body thriving and joined with Wealth, with no baleful stars and not falling Void, it succeeds; Zhen thriving and Hui imprisoned, the first trades win, Hui thriving and Zhen resting, the win comes later; if above and below punish and control each other, even with a Wealth line it is only a name. Whether Zhen and Hui mean the inner and outer trigrams or the original and resulting hexagrams: see the dispute.

**《断易天机·占买卖第二十》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1316 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 20: Divining for buying and selling", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 1316*

| 兼身旺相与财并， | 卦身旺相，又和财并在一起； | the Body thriving and joined with Wealth; |
| 更看役此无诸煞， | 再看这件事上有没有各种凶煞； | then see whether any baleful stars attend the matter; |
| 不落空亡有所成。 | 不落空亡，就有成就。 | not falling Void, there is success. |

| 卦中上下相刑克， | 卦中上下互相刑克， | if above and below in the hexagram punish and control each other, |

**他书本文(引来对照)** — 《易林补遗》第 64 行 · *Base text of another book (quoted for comparison); Yilin Buyi, line 64*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡有动爻，便有之卦。 | 凡是有动爻，就有之卦； | Wherever there is a moving line, there is a resulting hexagram; |
| 以本卦为贞，之卦为悔， | 以本卦为贞，之卦为悔； | the original hexagram is Zhen, the resulting hexagram is Hui; |
| 凡爻静无之者，便取内卦为贞，外卦为悔， | 凡是爻都静、没有之卦的，就取内卦为贞、外卦为悔； | where the lines are all still and there is no resulting hexagram, the inner trigram is Zhen and the outer trigram is Hui; |
| 以贞为始，以悔为终。 | 以贞为开始，以悔为终结。 | Zhen is the beginning and Hui is the end. |

> **打架**:这首歌里的「贞」「悔」，有两种读法。一种是内卦为贞、外卦为悔；一种是本卦为贞、之卦为悔。《易林补遗》两种都记：有动爻时，本卦是贞、之卦是悔；爻都静、没有之卦时，才取内卦为贞、外卦为悔，并且「以贞为始，以悔为终」。歌诀自己没有说是哪一种。「贞旺悔囚初买卖，悔旺贞休后始赢」里的「初」和「后」有先后，和「以贞为始，以悔为终」对得上；但这不是歌诀的明文，本库不裁。
>
> **Where they disagree:** 'Zhen' and 'Hui' in this verse can be read in two ways: the inner trigram as Zhen and the outer as Hui, or the original hexagram as Zhen and the resulting hexagram as Hui. The Yilin Buyi records both: when there is a moving line, the original hexagram is Zhen and the resulting hexagram is Hui; only when all lines are still and there is no resulting hexagram does it take the inner trigram as Zhen and the outer as Hui, and 'Zhen is the beginning and Hui is the end.' The verse itself does not say which. The words 'first' and 'later' in 'Zhen thriving and Hui imprisoned, the first trades; Hui thriving and Zhen resting, only later a win' have a before and after that fits 'Zhen is the beginning, Hui the end'; but that is not the verse's own statement, and this library does not decide.

> **按**:「更看役此无诸煞」的「役此」字义不明，可能有讹字；这里照字面译作「这件事上」，不确定。
>
> **Note:** The words 'yi ci' in the third line are unclear and may be miscopied; they are translated loosely as 'in this matter', without certainty.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.21 脱货：以财爻生旺的月日为脱手之期；财化五行各防一样货损；应动合财易脱

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:脱货：财爻生旺的月份、日子是脱手的日期。应发动合财、或财发动合应，易脱；应落空落破，一定没有买主；财临勾陈土，货难脱。财化水防漏和潮，化火防枯干和火灾，化木防微烂，化土防朽腐，化金又临螣蛇防生虫；财落在辰午酉亥自刑的地支上，货差难脱。世应相冲又带退悔杀发动，或应动合财却化成退神，多半中途作罢；应动合财而被日辰或动爻冲破，有人破坏；日辰合住动财，旁人把持；官鬼动伤世，牙人阻挠；卦里没有官鬼，买卖不成；财动生世生应而官鬼入墓，要等冲开墓的日子成交。书中例：需卦安静，申金子孙持世，人人说吉，作者断四月金空生不了财、又有四个兄弟互相冲脱，买主看了货就散了。

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:往何方买卖：古法有推方位的说法，野鹤屡试不验；要指明去哪个地方单独起卦（书中例：往楚买豆，财休囚在外卦主那里货贱，日建生世主厚利，间爻兄弟旺主耗费；后果如此）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Which way to trade: the old methods have rules for finding the direction, but Yehe found they fail; name the place and cast for it (case: going to Chu to buy beans, Wealth resting in the outer trigram meant cheap goods there, the Day generating the Self line meant rich profit, a strong between-line Peer meant heavy expenses; all came true).

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.26 买何货为吉：揣摩五行之论，屡试不验

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:买什么货：古法以财爻五行、六神定货类（金珠宝、水鱼盐、火陶冶果实、木竹木、土五谷；青龙喜庆、白虎丧葬屠宰等），野鹤认为是揣摩之论、屡试不验；要指定某一货物单独占（书中例：旅卦六合、世应相生，只断此行顺遂，不断买何物，后买檀香翠羽都获大利）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Which goods to buy: the old method reads the goods from the element and spirit of the Wealth line (Metal jewels, Water fish and salt, Fire pottery and fruit, Wood timber, Earth grain; Azure Dragon festive goods, White Tiger funeral and butchery goods, and so on); Yehe holds this speculation that fails when tested; name a particular kind of goods and cast for it (case: the hexagram Lü in Six-Combine with Self and Other generating, read only as 'the journey will go well'; he bought sandalwood and kingfisher feathers and profited on both).

**野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 11437–11438 行 · *The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, lines 11437–11438*
