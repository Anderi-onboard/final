## LY.WEALTH.HJC.36 间兴害世，置货者当虑牙人

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:买货：应爻生合世爻易成，刑克世爻难买。代表货物的爻（六畜看子孙、五谷看妻财、布帛看父母）太过货多、不及货少、空伏无货。兄鬼发动防骗；兄鬼在间爻是牙人虚诈、口舌；间爻伤克世爻防牙人谋财；出门买货而应爻空，多不顺。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Buying goods: the Other line generating or combining with the Self line makes it easy; punishing or controlling it makes it hard. The line for the goods (Output for livestock, Wealth for grain, Resource for cloth) in excess means plenty, falling short means few, void or hidden means none. Peer and Pressure moving: beware of swindlers; on the between lines, a false broker or quarrels; a between line harming or controlling the Self line: the broker is after your goods; travelling to buy with the Other line void, it mostly goes badly.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7883 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7883*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.37 停榻者，喜财安而鬼静

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:囤货：财爻不宜动、不宜空，不宜被动爻日辰克劫；财坐胎、养、长生，日后得利。兄官交变或同动防盗；兄弟独发多耗折；水爻父母（王洪绪：父化官鬼）刑克世爻，货被雨水淹腐；财化死墓空绝，日后价贱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Holding goods in store: Wealth should neither move nor be void, nor be attacked by moving lines or the Day; Wealth at the Embryo, Nurture or Birth stage means profit later. Peer and Pressure turning into each other or moving together: beware of theft; Peer moving alone: waste and loss; a Water Resource line (Wang Hongxu: Resource turning into Pressure) punishing or controlling the Self line: the goods are spoiled by rain; Wealth turning into death, tomb, void or extinction: the price will fall.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7887 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7887*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.38 脱货者，宜财动而身兴

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卖货：财动易脱、世动易卖；应空无买主，世空自己卖不成；动变日辰来生合，有人争买；遇刑冲多阻；财在内卦动（生世）本地卖，在外卦动外地卖（财持世而子孙在外卦动，也宜外卖）；动而遇合将成不成，动而逢空欲卖不卖；卦无官鬼难成交。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Selling goods: a moving Wealth means they go easily, a moving Self line that they sell easily; a void Other line, no buyer; a void Self line, you fail to sell; moving and changed lines and the Day generating or combining, buyers compete; punishment or clash, obstacles; Wealth moving in the inner trigram (generating the Self line), sell locally; in the outer, sell elsewhere (also when Wealth is on the Self line and Output moves in the outer trigram); moving but combined, almost closed yet not; moving but void, wanting to sell yet not; no Pressure in the hexagram, hard to close.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.18 贸易占大略：先问时价，再问牙行，后问脱货；六爻各管一事

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:买卖要问三件事：现在的价钱是贵是贱、以后是涨是落；牙行（中间人）是好是坏；货好不好脱手。六爻各管一事（鬼谷分爻）：上爻店舍，五爻道路，四爻舟车马，三爻行李，二爻伴侣，初爻己身。哪一爻旺相、带财福龙德贵禄、又扶着卦身和世爻，那件事就顺；哪一爻带鬼、伏鬼、化鬼来刑冲克害卦身和世爻，或落在空死墓绝胎、被四直和动爻冲伤，疏漏就出在那件事上。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A trade question asks three things: is the price now dear or cheap, and will it later rise or fall; is the broker honest or crooked; and will the goods sell easily. The six lines each govern one matter (Guigu's assignment): top, the lodging; fifth, the road; fourth, carts, boats and horses; third, baggage; second, companions; first, oneself. A line that is strong, carries Wealth, Output, the Azure Dragon, Virtue, Noble or Emolument, and supports the Body and Self lines means that matter goes well; a line that holds, hides or turns into Pressure and punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Body and Self lines, or falls in void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, or is clashed and hurt by the four rulers and moving lines, is where things go wrong.

Here the source runs 'carts, boats and horses' and 'baggage' together with no break; they are read as the fourth and third lines.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.19 买卖：官鬼旺静兄弟弱为吉；按买卖的季节配财爻五行；财旺价贵，财死价贱

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:买卖看价：官鬼旺相安静、兄弟无气，吉。财爻旺相有生助，价贵；死败，价贱；财属火价一天天涨，属水一天天落。按季节看财爻的五行：冬收夏卖看火财，春收秋卖看金财，秋收春卖看木财，夏收冬卖看水财。财变兄弟或官鬼、子孙变父母，先贵后贱；财化子孙、官鬼化财，先贱后贵。内卦财旺外卦财衰，别处贱；外卦财旺内卦财衰，以后贵。本卦有财而变卦无，晚买便宜；本卦财旺而变卦财衰，先卖的卖得贵。内卦财空可卖，外卦财空难买；外卦的财生合世易买，应爻合内卦的财易卖，世爻落在财的墓上难卖。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Reading the price in a trade: Pressure strong and still, with Peer lacking strength, is auspicious. Wealth strong and fed means dear; Wealth dead or spent means cheap; Wealth on Fire, the price climbs day by day; on Water, it falls. By season, look at the element of Wealth: goods bought in winter and sold in summer want a Fire Wealth, spring to autumn Metal, autumn to spring Wood, summer to winter Water. Wealth turning into Peer or Pressure, or Output turning into Resource: dear first, cheap later; Wealth turning into Output, or Pressure into Wealth: cheap first, dear later. Wealth strong inside and weak outside: cheap elsewhere; strong outside and weak inside: dear later. Wealth in the main hexagram but not in the changed one: buy late and it is cheaper; strong in the main and weak in the change: whoever sells first gets the dear price. Inner Wealth void: it can be sold; outer Wealth void: hard to buy; outer Wealth generating or combining with the Self: easy to buy; the Other combining with the inner Wealth: easy to sell; the Self standing on the tomb of Wealth: hard to sell.

> **Note:** The sentence 'goods bought to be sold in the four seasons' is terse; both 'four seasons' and 'times' have other readings, and here the four seasons are read as the four season-end months Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei. Also, this passage pairs the season of buying and selling with the element of Wealth; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi works differently: sell when Wealth is strong and stock up when it is weak, judge goods with a fixed season by the season, cast separately for goods without one, and he says the old element-by-goods method never held up in his trials.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.21 脱货：以财爻生旺的月日为脱手之期；财化五行各防一样货损；应动合财易脱

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:脱货：财爻生旺的月份、日子是脱手的日期。应发动合财、或财发动合应，易脱；应落空落破，一定没有买主；财临勾陈土，货难脱。财化水防漏和潮，化火防枯干和火灾，化木防微烂，化土防朽腐，化金又临螣蛇防生虫；财落在辰午酉亥自刑的地支上，货差难脱。世应相冲又带退悔杀发动，或应动合财却化成退神，多半中途作罢；应动合财而被日辰或动爻冲破，有人破坏；日辰合住动财，旁人把持；官鬼动伤世，牙人阻挠；卦里没有官鬼，买卖不成；财动生世生应而官鬼入墓，要等冲开墓的日子成交。书中例：需卦安静，申金子孙持世，人人说吉，作者断四月金空生不了财、又有四个兄弟互相冲脱，买主看了货就散了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Selling off goods: the month or day when the Wealth line is born or strong is the time to unload. The Other moving to combine with Wealth, or Wealth moving to combine with the Other: easy to sell; the Other void or broken: surely no buyer; Wealth on Hook Earth: hard to sell. Wealth turning into Water: beware leaks and damp; into Fire: drying out and fire; into Wood: slight rot; into Earth: decay; into Metal with the Coiling Snake: worms; Wealth on Chen, Wu, You or Hai, the self-punishing branches: poor goods, hard to sell. Self and Other clashing with the Regret star moving, or the Other moving to combine with Wealth but turning into a Retreating line: often broken off midway; the Other moving to combine with Wealth but clashed and broken by the Day or a moving line: someone wrecks it; the Day combining with a moving Wealth: a bystander holds it back; Pressure moving to hurt the Self: the broker obstructs; no Pressure in the hexagram: the trade does not close; Wealth moving to generate both Self and Other while Pressure has gone into its tomb: it closes on the day the tomb is clashed open. In the case from the book, hexagram Xu with no moving line looked auspicious because Output on Shen Metal held the Self line; the author judged that Metal was void in the fourth month and could not generate Wealth, and that four Peer lines clashed each other away, so the buyers left when they saw the goods.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.15 财爻衰者宜停脱，妻位当时可脱之

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:囤货卖货看财爻四时旺衰：财衰宜囤，财旺宜卖（如春天木财旺宜速卖，金水土财宜囤，火财为相也不宜囤）；忌财空破、动而变回头克。觉子：财衰可买，但要查它有没有根——有根将来再涨，无根终难卖出。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Stockpiling or selling goes by the seasonal strength of the Wealth line: weak Wealth, stockpile; strong Wealth, sell (in spring Wood Wealth is thriving, sell fast; Metal, Water or Earth Wealth, stockpile; Fire is strong in spring, do not stockpile). Void or broken Wealth, or Wealth turning into its own control, is bad. Juezi: weak Wealth can be bought, but check its root; rooted, it will rise again; rootless, it will never sell.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.16 货有定时，须凭时令；物无定者，指利而占

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:有固定旺季的货（麻葛夏、棉花冬）凭时令断价；没有固定时节的货（木料、绸缎）要指明求利单独占。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Goods with a set season (hemp and linen in summer, cotton in winter) are priced by the season; goods without one (timber, silk) need a casting that asks specifically about the profit.
