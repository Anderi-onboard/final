## LY.WEALTH.CA.03 财爻外动较难，财在内兴人送至；外生内、应生世，财轻也容易

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻在外卦发动，比较艰难；财在内卦兴旺，人会把钱送上门；外卦生内卦、应生世，即使财轻也容易。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Wealth line moving in the outer trigram is harder; Wealth thriving in the inner trigram, people bring the money to you; the outer generating the inner and the Other generating the Self, even if Wealth is light it is easy.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.10 无鬼分争，又怕交重而阻滞

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼能制兄弟：卦中无官鬼则兄弟专权、有人分争；官鬼发动又主阻隔。兄弟动时喜官鬼动来制；兄弟静时官鬼不宜动，动则泄财气、有口舌（觉子）。官鬼被克、冲散或合住，阻而无妨。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure keeps Peer in check: without Pressure, Peer takes over and there is fighting over shares; yet a moving Pressure line means obstruction. When Peer moves, a moving Pressure line is welcome to restrain it; when Peer is still, Pressure should not move, since it drains Wealth and brings quarrels (Juezi). If the Pressure line is controlled, clashed apart or combined, its obstruction does no harm.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7788 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7788*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.11 兄如太过，反不克财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:一个兄弟旺动最不利。兄弟重叠太过（月日动变俱兄）时，经文说反不克财：旧注说到财爻生旺日可得；王洪绪说须见子孙发动；觉子不同意，认为到兄弟入墓或受损克之日仍会劫财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A single strong moving Peer line is the worst case. When Peer is in excess (Month, Day, moving and changed lines all Peer) the classic says it does not rob after all: the old commentary says the money comes on a day that births or empowers Wealth; Wang Hongxu says only if Output moves; Juezi disagrees: excessive Peer still robs on the day it enters its tomb or is cut down.

>
> **Where they disagree:** Does excessive Peer rob or not? The old commentary: excess means it does not rob, and the money comes on a day that births or empowers Wealth. Wang Hongxu: it does not rob only if Output moves, and then the profit is endless; with Output still it is unlucky. Juezi: excessive Peer still robs on the day it enters its tomb or is cut down, and gives a case where the loss came in the ninth month when Peer entered its tomb.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.12 身或兄临，必难求望

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟临卦身或持世，求财难有指望（卦身临财福吉、临父辛劳、临官利公门之财）。野鹤只看世爻，并认为兄弟持世而世逢月破、旬空、化墓，或日月为财冲克世爻，或世上兄弟化出财爻，仍可得财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Peer on the Body line or the Self line makes the money hard to get (Wealth or Output on the Body line is good, Resource means toil, Pressure favours official money). Yehe reads only the Self line, and holds that Peer on the Self line still gets the money if the Self line is under a Month-break, void, or turns into its tomb, if the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line, or if the Peer on the Self line turns into Wealth.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.03 难易：财旺临身世易，父化财先难后获

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:易：旺财临世身，或旺动生合克世身，子化财生合世身。鬼化财生合世：从无中得；鬼化子生合身：儿子送财；父化财：先难后得（父旺财衰力多财少，父衰财旺力少财多）；兄化财：费口舌而得；子化子：两处得财。财动又化财：化去难得；财生世又化兄：得而复失或与人分；财合逢冲：成而后变；财旺而被月日动变冲刑克害：求难得薄；财冲破而世得生扶：人安财散；世逢虎鬼大杀或持墓化墓而财旺被合住：财存人亡；世遇虎鬼刃劫克伤而财空绝破：人财两失。

> **打架**:财化财怎么断，三家不同：《易隐》说「化去而难得」；《黄金策》旧注说「反复不定」；《天玄赋》注（《断易天机》本）说「本来是吉」，只是要分本卦、变卦的财孰旺孰衰。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Wealth turning into Wealth is read three ways: the Yi Yin says it 'changes away and is hard to get'; the Golden Strategy's old commentary says 'back and forth, uncertain'; the Tianxuan Fu commentary (Duanyi Tianji text) says 'normally lucky', judged by which of the two Wealths is stronger.

## LY.WEALTH.YY.08 仗托吹嘘：看间爻（中保）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:靠人说合（仗托吹嘘）看间爻（中人、保人）：死绝受伤则中保无能；生合世爻善成全，刑克世爻多欺骗；带兄鬼玄武冲伤为奸人阻隔，带贵人生合为贵人做主；应克间，对方起疑；世伤间，我心怀怨；两间兄动，争做中人；朱雀兄弟旁动伤世克间，有人破坏；间爻空，没有中保。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When it depends on someone putting in a word, read the between lines (go-between and guarantor): dead or hurt, they are useless; generating or combining with the Self, they see it through; punishing or controlling it, they cheat; with Peer, Pressure or the Dark Tortoise clashing, villains block it; with the Noble star generating, a person of rank takes charge; the Other controlling them, the other side grows suspicious; the Self hurting them, you bear a grudge; both between lines Peer and moving, people compete to be the go-between; the Vermilion Bird on a moving Peer elsewhere hurting the Self and controlling the between line, someone spoils it; a void between line, no go-between.
