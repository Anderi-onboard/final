## LY.WEALTH.HJC.10 无鬼分争，又怕交重而阻滞

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼能制兄弟：卦中无官鬼则兄弟专权、有人分争；官鬼发动又主阻隔。兄弟动时喜官鬼动来制；兄弟静时官鬼不宜动，动则泄财气、有口舌（觉子）。官鬼被克、冲散或合住，阻而无妨。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure keeps Peer in check: without Pressure, Peer takes over and there is fighting over shares; yet a moving Pressure line means obstruction. When Peer moves, a moving Pressure line is welcome to restrain it; when Peer is still, Pressure should not move, since it drains Wealth and brings quarrels (Juezi). If the Pressure line is controlled, clashed apart or combined, its obstruction does no harm.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7788 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7788*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.11 兄如太过，反不克财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:一个兄弟旺动最不利。兄弟重叠太过（月日动变俱兄）时，经文说反不克财：旧注说到财爻生旺日可得；王洪绪说须见子孙发动；觉子不同意，认为到兄弟入墓或受损克之日仍会劫财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A single strong moving Peer line is the worst case. When Peer is in excess (Month, Day, moving and changed lines all Peer) the classic says it does not rob after all: the old commentary says the money comes on a day that births or empowers Wealth; Wang Hongxu says only if Output moves; Juezi disagrees: excessive Peer still robs on the day it enters its tomb or is cut down.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7791 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7791*

| --- | --- | --- |
| 兄如太过， | 兄弟如果太多太旺， | If Peer is in excess, |
| 反不克财； | 反而不克财； | it does not rob Wealth after all; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7792 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7792*

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.12 身或兄临，必难求望

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟临卦身或持世，求财难有指望（卦身临财福吉、临父辛劳、临官利公门之财）。野鹤只看世爻，并认为兄弟持世而世逢月破、旬空、化墓，或日月为财冲克世爻，或世上兄弟化出财爻，仍可得财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Peer on the Body line or the Self line makes the money hard to get (Wealth or Output on the Body line is good, Resource means toil, Pressure favours official money). Yehe reads only the Self line, and holds that Peer on the Self line still gets the money if the Self line is under a Month-break, void, or turns into its tomb, if the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line, or if the Peer on the Self line turns into Wealth.

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.13 财来就我终须易，我去寻财必是难

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻生、合、克世爻或持世，为「财来就我」，容易得；财爻虽动而与世爻无关，为「我去寻财」，难得。野鹤：不可拘泥，兄弟持世、财不生合世爻而照样得财的卦也有。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Wealth line generating, combining with, controlling or sitting on the Self line is 'the money coming to me': easy to get. A moving Wealth line with no tie to the Self line is 'me going after the money': hard. Yehe: do not cling to this; there are hexagrams with Peer on the Self line and no Wealth reaching it where the money came all the same.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7797 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7797*

| --- | --- | --- |
| 财来就我，终须易； | 财来找我，终究容易； | When the money comes to me, it is easy in the end; |
| 我去寻财，必是难。 | 我去找财，一定困难。 | when I go looking for the money, it is sure to be hard. |

## LY.WEALTH.HJC.22 主人一位刑伤，往求不遇
