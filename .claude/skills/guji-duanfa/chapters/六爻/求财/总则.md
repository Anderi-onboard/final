## LY.WEALTH.HJC.01 要问吉凶，但看财福

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占财以妻财为用神，子孙为原神（财的来源）；吉凶先看这两爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For money, the Wealth line is the Subject and Output is its Support, the source of money; judge the outcome from these two lines first.

**《黄金策·求财》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7761 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7761*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 居货曰贾，行货曰商，总为资生之计； | 坐店卖货叫「贾」，贩运货物叫「商」，说到底都是谋生的办法； | Keeping goods in a shop is called gu, carrying goods to trade is called shang; both are ways of making a living; |
| 蓍所以筮，龟所以卜，莫非就利之谋。 | 人们用蓍草来筮、用龟甲来卜，问的无非是怎么得利。 | we divine with yarrow stalks and with the tortoise shell, and what we ask is how to profit. |
| 要问吉凶，但看财福。 | 要问求财的吉凶，只看妻财和子孙（福）。 | To know whether it turns out well or badly, look only at Wealth and its source, Output (fu). |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7762 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7762*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 财为利息，福为财源。 | 妻财代表利润，子孙（福）代表财的来源。 | Wealth is the profit; Output (fu) is the source of the money. |
| 二者占财用神。 | 这两爻是占财时要取的用神。 | These two are the Subject lines of a money question. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2655 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2655*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 财为本，福为利， | 妻财是本钱，子孙是利润， | Wealth is the capital, Output is the profit; |
| 二者不可损坏。 | 两者都不能受损。 | neither may be damaged. |
| 卦中子孙之爻，称曰福神。 | 卦里的子孙爻，叫作「福神」。 | The Output line in a hexagram is called the spirit of fortune (fushen). |

**野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 11083 行 · *The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 11083*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 上至国课，下至营谋，无不以财为用， | 大到国家赋税，小到个人生意，没有不以财为用的； | From the state's taxes down to a private venture, everything turns on money; |
| 公占私卜，皆以财为用神。 | 无论为公事还是私事占问，都以妻财为用神。 | whether the question is public or private, Wealth is the Subject. |
| 子孙者，乃生助财爻之元神也， | 子孙是生助财爻的原神。 | Output is the Support that generates and helps the Wealth line. |

> **打架**:财、福各指什么，两家注说得相反：旧注说妻财是利润、子孙是财源；王洪绪说妻财是本钱、子孙是利润。野鹤只说子孙是生财的原神。三家都以妻财为用神、子孙为最要紧的帮手。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The two commentaries gloss the pair oppositely: the old commentary makes Wealth the profit and Output its source; Wang Hongxu makes Wealth the capital and Output the profit. Yehe only says Output is the Support that generates Wealth. All three take Wealth as the Subject and Output as its chief help.

- 《黄金策》:同文 第 1805 行:居货曰贾，行货曰商。总为资生之计;同文 第 1805 行:蓍所以筮，龟所以卜，莫非就利之谋;同文 第 1805 行:要问吉凶，但看财福

## LY.WEALTH.TXF.01 饥寒起盗心，富贵享遐福

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求财本身艰险，卜卦是为了决疑；出外求财重在分祸福，在家求财重在分得失。见妻财、子孙为吉，见兄弟、官鬼为凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Seeking money is hazardous, and we divine to settle doubt; for money sought abroad the question is danger or safety, at home it is gain or loss. Wealth and Output are friends; Peer and Pressure are enemies.

**《天玄赋·求财章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 4557–4558 行 · *Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), lines 4557–4558*

## LY.WEALTH.TXF.02 将本求财，妻位偏宜旺相；空拳问利，官爻喜遇兴隆

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:将本求财看妻财：财爻旺相、克世或生世吉，世克财凶；卦有两财，外旺内冲本少利多，内旺外冲只能保本；内外财死绝、财不上卦或空亡则折本；财绝逢生仍有利。空手求财（百工技艺）看官鬼：鬼旺财厚；《断易天机》本说官鬼所临六神指财的来路（青龙馈送、朱雀文书口舌、勾陈稳当、螣蛇靠人带挈、白虎诈取、玄武暗昧）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Trading with capital: read the Wealth line; thriving and controlling or generating the Self line is good, the Self line controlling Wealth is bad; with two Wealth lines, outer strong and inner clashed means small capital and large profit, inner strong and outer clashed means only the capital is kept; both dead, absent or void means losing the capital; Wealth at extinction meeting birth still profits. Earning without capital (crafts and trades): read the Pressure line; strong Pressure, ample money. The Duanyi Tianji text adds that the spirit on the Pressure line shows where the money comes from (Azure Dragon gifts, Vermilion Bird documents or talk, Hook Earth steady, Coiling Snake through others, White Tiger fraud, Dark Tortoise hidden dealings).

**《天玄赋·求财章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 4561 行 · *Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 4561*

| 将本求财，妻位偏宜旺相； | 拿本钱求财，妻财尤其宜旺相； | Trading with capital: the Wealth line above all should be thriving; |
| 空拳问利，官爻喜遇兴隆。 | 空手求利，喜欢官鬼兴旺。 | earning without capital: the Pressure line is welcome to flourish. |

## LY.WEALTH.YY.01 占求财六问：有无、难易、多少、何人、何财、何日

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占求财要分六问：有无、难易、多少、得于何人、得何财、何日得；靠人说合的也要占。鬼谷分爻：初爻己身、二爻伴侣、三爻行李、四爻车马、五爻道路、六爻店舍。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A money question breaks into six: whether there is money, easy or hard, how much, from whom, what kind, and on what day; where it depends on someone putting in a word, ask about that too. Guigu's assignment of the lines: first, oneself; second, companions; third, baggage; fourth, carts and horses; fifth, the road; sixth, the lodging.

**游南子(《易隐》引)** — 《易隐》第 2850–2852 行 · *Master Younan (quoted in the Yi Yin); Yi Yin, lines 2850–2852*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 游南子曰，占求财者，求其求之有无， | 游南子说：占求财，要问的是求得到求不到， | Master Younan says: in a money question, ask whether there is anything to get, |
| 占其得之难易，占其数之多寡， | 得来容易还是困难，数目多少， | whether it comes easily or hard, how much, |
| 与夫得于何人，得为何财，得于何日而已。 | 从谁那里得、得的是什么财、哪一天得，就这些。 | from whom, what kind of money, and on what day; that is all. |
| 若乃吹嘘成就，全藉乎人，亦不可以不占也。 | 至于靠别人说好话促成的，全凭人力，也不能不占。 | And where it depends on someone putting in a word for you, it rests entirely on people, so that too must be asked. |

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.04 为名宜父动，因利忌兄兴

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问的是什么就看什么：谒贵能否见到，看世爻与官鬼相生相合；能否得名、得利要另起一卦（名看父母、官鬼两旺，利看妻财、子孙两旺）。为求名而起的卦里财爻持世，是破文书的忌神，不能读作「不成名也得利」。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Read what was asked: whether you will be received depends on the Self line and Pressure generating or combining; whether it brings a post or gain needs a separate casting (a post wants Resource and Pressure strong, gain wants Wealth and Output). In a hexagram cast for a post, Wealth on the Self line is the Adversary that breaks the documents; do not read it as 'no post, but money'.

**《增删卜易·谒贵求财章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 11274 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Calling on the great for money", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 11274*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 为名宜父动，因利忌兄兴。 | 为求名，宜父母发动；为求利，忌兄弟兴动。 | For a post, a moving Resource is welcome; for gain, a stirring Peer is feared. |
| 凡占谒贵，既得世与官星相生相合，必然得见。 | 凡是占拜见贵人，只要世爻和官鬼相生相合，一定能见到。 | When asking about calling on the great, if the Self line and Pressure generate or combine with each other, you will be received. |
