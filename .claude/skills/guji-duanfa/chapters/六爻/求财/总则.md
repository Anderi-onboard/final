## LY.WEALTH.CA.01 求财交易以财为主，发动临身必取；卦无财或落空，一切营谋不行

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求财做买卖，以财为主；财发动而临身，钱财一定拿得到；卦里没有财，或者财落空，一切谋求都靠不住。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In seeking wealth and trade Wealth is the chief; Wealth moving and on the Body, the money is sure to be taken; if the hexagram has no Wealth, or Wealth falls void, no scheme of any kind can be relied on.

**《阐奥歌章·求财买卖章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3853 行 · *Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on seeking wealth and trade", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3853*

| 发动临身财必取。 | 财发动而临身，钱财一定拿得到； | Wealth moving and on the Body, the money is sure to be taken; |
| 卦若无财及落空， | 卦里如果没有财，或者财落空， | if the hexagram has no Wealth, or Wealth falls void, |

## LY.WEALTH.CA.18 求财四句：父动艰难，鬼动灾；子旺财明得利，兄兴迟滞是非

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.07 海底眼：财旺相喜持世，有生扶可乘势；卖货要用神兴，开店要财爻安静

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:海底眼说：占财，财旺相，喜欢它持世；财出现在卦上又有生扶，可以乘势而为；卖货求财要用神兴旺；开店要财爻安静才有利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea says: when divining for wealth, Wealth thriving is best when it holds the Self; Wealth showing and supported may be ridden while it lasts; for selling goods the Subject must be thriving; for opening a shop the Wealth line should be quiet.

**《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1294 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 1294*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占财旺相喜持世， | 占财，财旺相，喜欢它持世； | Divining for wealth, Wealth thriving, it is glad to hold the Self; |
| 出现生扶可乘势， | 财出现在卦上又有生扶，可以乘势； | Wealth showing and supported may be ridden while it lasts; |
| 脱货求财要用兴， | 卖货求财，要用神兴旺； | to sell goods for money the Subject must thrive; |
| 开店财爻安者利。 | 开店，财爻安静的有利。 | for a shop, the one whose Wealth line is quiet profits. |

> **按**:这几句在《海底眼》原书里也有（见 HDY.01），第二句字面不同：这里作「出现生扶可乘势」，原书作「出现生扶司秉劳」。

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.17 求财无鬼：兄弟争权，替别人趁财，财也薄；鬼是财之主，财旺也要有主张才聚得起来

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求财而卦里没有鬼：兄弟一定来争权，等于在别人手下赚钱，钱也少；鬼是财的主人，财再旺也要有人作主才聚得起来，没有鬼就没有主，一定破耗多端、钱聚不起来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Seeking wealth with no Pressure in the hexagram: the Peer lines will surely contend for control, which means earning under another's hand, and the money is thin; Pressure is the master of Wealth, and however thriving Wealth is it needs someone in charge before it can gather, so with no Pressure there is no master, and there will surely be many kinds of waste and the funds will not gather.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.24 六爻都静：将本求财须用神静，脱货占来动才正；放债抽拈、买卖停场、赌斗撼钱，用爻宜静，有气为先

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六爻都安静的卦：带本钱去求人，用神要静；卖货来占，用神动才正；放债、抽拈、买卖歇了场、赌斗博钱这几样，用爻都宜静，有气最要紧。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a hexagram with all six lines quiet: to go to someone with capital, the Subject should be still; in divining on selling goods, movement is the proper sign; for lending, drawing lots, trade gone quiet, and gambling or contests for money, the Subject line should be still, and having qi comes first.

**《断易天机·六爻俱静类》原文** — 《断易天机》第 654 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Hexagrams with six quiet lines", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 654*

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.30 妻财用·求财：财是用爻、一卦的主宰，宜生世克世，大吉；无气必难求；诸事逢之皆吉，遇兄弟有损，遇福德更佳

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻财是我克的那个六亲，诸事遇到它没有不吉的，只有占父母、文书不宜见它；遇兄弟有损，遇福德更好，遇官鬼泄我的气；动静都吉。占求财，它是用爻，一卦的主宰，宜生世、克世，大吉，没有气就一定难求。

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.32 六亲变用：妻财宜旺相出现；兄弟为隔神；子孙为散神，旺相生世可求财，休囚发动只宜散财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻财宜旺相、出现在卦上，伏在世下的要生世；动而不当，宜脱货；兄弟叫隔神，忌动而不当；子孙叫散神，旺相生世可以求财，休囚发动就只宜散财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth should be thriving and showing in the hexagram, and if hidden under the Self it should generate the Self; if it moves out of place, sell off goods; Peer is called the Separating spirit and should not move out of place; Output is called the Scattering spirit, and thriving and generating the Self one may seek wealth, but weak and moving it suits only scattering money.

## LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.33 用财六看：世下财有无、日辰生扶、伏在哪一爻下、忌兄弟持世、独发要生世

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用财（以财为用神）的几个要点：财伏在世下，忌伏官；要妻财或生财的子孙值日；财喜伏在子、父之下，忌伏在鬼、兄之下；忌兄弟持世，须日辰旺相、用爻透出才不怕；用财旺相，伏动都要生世。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Points for using Wealth as the Subject: Wealth hidden under the Self should not be hidden under Pressure; the Wealth line or Output that gives it life should rule the day; Wealth likes to be hidden under Output or Resource and dislikes being hidden under Pressure or Peer; Peer holding the Self is to be avoided unless the Day is thriving and the Subject shows; a thriving Wealth, hidden or moving, must generate the Self.

## LY.WEALTH.ZS.33 八宫章·占财秘法：占钱粮、买卖经营、开张店面、远处求财、见贵求财，妻财持世宜行、兄弟持世速止；已经营久的妻财持世愈久愈丰、兄弟持世渐渐衰败；囤货妻财持世可买；放债妻财持世始终两好、兄弟持世有借无还；取债妻财持世终归偿还、屡占仍兄弟持世终成画饼、得官鬼持世必要经官；开矿买山伐木园林盐池鱼沼妻财持世物阜财丰、兄弟持世破财折本

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占财的，问钱粮能不能征收足额？领钱粮能不能全额？妻财持世就丰足，兄弟持世要包赔。问买卖经营、开张店面，以及远处求财、见贵人求财，妻财持世适宜去，兄弟持世赶快停止。占经商贸易、开张店面，如果已经做了很久，问将来兴废？妻财持世，愈久愈丰；兄弟持世，慢慢衰败。占囤货的，妻财持世可以买，兄弟持世不要收。占放债的，妻财持世，始终两好；兄弟持世，有借无还。占讨债的，妻财持世，即使目下得不到，最终会偿还；兄弟持世，另日再占；如果屡占仍是兄弟持世，终成画饼；屡占得官鬼持世，必要告官。占开矿以及煤窑祇山，以及买山、伐木、园林、盐池、鱼沼，凡是靠山岗江海取利的，妻财持世，物阜财丰；兄弟持世，破财折本。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining for wealth: asked whether the grain and money levies can be collected in full, or whether the stipend can be drawn in full, Wealth holding the Self means full and abundant, and Peer holding the Self means making up the loss oneself. Asked about trade and management, opening a shop, seeking wealth far away or seeking wealth by seeing a noble, Wealth holding the Self is fit to go, and Peer holding the Self means stop quickly. In divining for trade and commerce or a shop already long in business, asking whether it will prosper or fail in the future: Wealth holding the Self, the longer the richer; Peer holding the Self, it gradually decays. In divining for hoarding goods, Wealth holding the Self means one may buy, Peer holding the Self means do not take in. In divining for lending money, Wealth holding the Self is good for both throughout, and Peer holding the Self means loans without repayment. In divining for calling in a debt, Wealth holding the Self means that even if not got now it is repaid in the end, and Peer holding the Self means divine again another day; if on repeated divinations it is still Peer holding the Self, it ends as a painted cake, and if repeated divinations give Pressure holding the Self, one must go to law. In divining for mining, coal pits and mountain tenures, buying hills, felling timber, orchards, salt pans and fish ponds, anything that profits from hills, rivers or seas, Wealth holding the Self means goods in plenty and wealth abundant, and Peer holding the Self means loss of money and ruin.
