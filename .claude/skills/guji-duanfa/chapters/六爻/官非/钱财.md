## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.15 词讼·遇兄则财散人离：兄弟爻在世身爻上，事必牵连众人，发动则要广花钱财；加白虎必倾家荡产；临应爻则按赔偿来断

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:遇到兄弟爻，就财散人离。兄弟爻如果在世爻身爻上，事情必然牵连众人，发动就要大量花费钱财；如果再加白虎，必定倾家荡产；临在应爻上，就按赔偿来断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Meeting a Peer line, wealth is scattered and people part. If the Peer line is on the Self or Body line, the matter surely involves many people, and if it moves a great deal of money will be spent; if the White Tiger is added, it surely means the ruin of the household; on the Other line, judge it as compensation.

**《黄金策·词讼》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2865 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lawsuits", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2865*

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.28 词讼·妻动生官须用赀财嘱托：官司已成而卦有财爻发动，必须花钱托人打点官府；如果遇到子孙冲官鬼，即使花钱也没有用

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻财发动生官鬼，须用钱财嘱托。如果官司已经成立，卦里有财爻发动，必须用钱财去嘱托官府；如果遇到子孙冲官鬼，即使花钱也没有用。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth moving to give life to Pressure means money must be used to entrust the matter to someone. If the suit has already been brought and the hexagram has a moving Wealth line, money must be used to entrust the officials; if Output meets and clashes Pressure, spending money is useless.

**《黄金策·词讼》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2893 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lawsuits", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2893*
