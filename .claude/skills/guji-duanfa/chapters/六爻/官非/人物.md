## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.09 词讼·间伤世位须防硬证同谋、鬼克间爻且喜有司明见：间爻是中证人，生世合世向着我，生应合应向着他；与世冲克的与我有仇；若冲克我的爻反去生合应爻，要防证人同谋陷害；有鬼爻克制或日辰冲克，官府不听他的话，我无事；间爻受刑克中证必遭杖责

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:间爻伤害世位，要防备硬证人同谋；鬼爻克间爻，还喜得官府看得明白。间爻是作中证的人：生世合世，必定向着我；生应合应，必定向着他；与世爻冲克，与我有仇；与应爻冲克，与他有隙。如果旺爻生应、衰爻合世，是帮他的有力、帮我的没有功效；或者应爻发动克世，是向着他的人，即使不上前怪我，偏偏会出面。如果冲克我的爻反去生应合应，须防证人同谋陷害；如果得到鬼爻克制或被日辰冲克，是官府不听他的话，我无事。间爻如果受刑克，作证的人必遭杖责；靠近世爻的必是我的干证，靠近应爻的是他的干证。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If a between line injures the Self position, beware of hard witnesses in a plot; if Pressure controls the between line, one is glad that the court sees clearly. The between lines are the witnesses: one giving life to or combining with the Self surely favours me; one giving life to or combining with the Other surely favours him; one clashing or controlling the Self has a feud with me; one clashing or controlling the Other has a rift with him. If a strong line gives life to the Other and a weak line combines with the Self, those helping him are powerful and those helping me are ineffective; or if the Other moves to control the Self, those on his side, though they do not step forward to blame me, will come out in the open all the same. If a line that clashes or controls me instead gives life to or combines with the Other, beware of witnesses plotting together to frame me; if it is controlled by the Pressure line or clashed and controlled by the Day, the officials will not listen to its words and I come to no harm. If a between line is punished or controlled, the witness is surely beaten with the rod; the one near the Self is surely my witness, and the one near the Other is his witness.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.12 词讼·鬼作问官克应则他遭杖责：官鬼是审案的官，动来克应爻，官司必定我胜；克世爻我败

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼是审问的官，克应爻则他遭杖责。官鬼是审理词讼的官，发动去克应爻，官司必定我胜；克世爻，我败。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure is the examining magistrate; if it controls the Other, he is beaten with the rod. Pressure is the official who hears the case; if it moves to control the Other, I surely win the suit; if it controls the Self, I lose.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.13 词讼·日为书吏伤身则我受刑名：日辰既能救事也能坏事；鬼动克世自然有刑；得日辰制鬼冲鬼，必有旁人一句话解释，问官宽恕我

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:日辰是书吏，伤了世身则我要受刑罚。日辰既能救事也能坏事，如果鬼发动克世，自然有刑罚；得到日辰制住鬼、冲掉鬼，必有旁人一句话来解释，问案的官必能宽恕我。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Day is the clerk, and if it injures the Self and Body I suffer punishment. The Day can both save and spoil a matter: if Pressure moves and controls the Self there is naturally punishment; if the Day restrains or clashes away Pressure, someone is sure to put in a word of explanation, and the magistrate will surely pardon me.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.17 词讼·官逢太岁必非州县之词：官鬼在第五爻，若值太岁，这事必牵涉朝廷；遇月建必涉及台宪

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼逢太岁，必定不是州县的案子。官鬼在第五爻，如果值太岁，这件事必定牵涉朝廷；遇月建，必定涉及台宪。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure meeting Tai Sui surely means this is not a case for a prefecture or county. If Pressure is on the fifth line and sits on Tai Sui, the matter surely concerns the court; meeting the Month, it surely involves the censorate and the provincial authorities.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.18 词讼·内外有官事涉一司终不了：官鬼不上卦就没有主事的官；内外卦都有官鬼，权不归一，事情反复，必经两个衙门才了结

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:内外卦都有官鬼，事情牵涉一个衙门也终究了不了。官鬼不上卦，没有官员主张；内外都有官鬼，权力不归一人，主事情反复，必定经过两个衙门然后了事。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Pressure both inside and outside, a matter that touches one office cannot be finished in the end. If Pressure is not on the hexagram there is no official to take charge; with Pressure inside and outside, authority is not in one hand, which means the matter goes back and forth and must pass through two offices before it is settled.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.25 词讼·兄在间中事必干众：兄弟爻在间爻，状词牵涉的人很多；发动则作证的人贪图贿赂，克应爻会索取赔偿的钱财，克世爻必须花钱托人才能安稳

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟在间爻，事情必然牵涉众人。兄弟在间爻，状词里牵涉的人很多；发动，作证的人就会贪图索取贿赂；克应爻，会索取赔偿的钱财；克世爻，须用财物托人才能安稳。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With a Peer line among the between lines, the matter surely involves many people. A Peer line among the between lines means many are implicated in the plaint; if it moves, the witnesses grasp at bribes; if it controls the Other, it demands compensation money; if it controls the Self, one must buy security by entrusting money to someone.
