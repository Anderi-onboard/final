## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.04 词讼·相生相合终成和好之情：世应相生相合，原被告有和解的意思；世生应是我想求和，应生世是他想求和；世应发动落空或化空，都是假意说和

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:相生相合，最终成就和好的情意。世爻应爻相生相合，原告被告有和解的意思：世爻生应爻，是我想求和；应爻生世爻，是他想求和；应爻世爻发动落空或化空的，都是假意说和。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Giving life and combining, they end in a feeling of reconciliation. The Self and the Other giving life to and combining with each other means the parties have a mind to settle: the Self giving life to the Other means I want peace; the Other giving life to the Self means he wants peace; the Other or the Self moving into the Void or changing into the Void means a false pretence of peace.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.05 词讼·世应比和官鬼动恐公家捉打官司：世应比和是和解之象，若官鬼发动来克，则官府要办这场官司、不让和议；子孙也动才终成和议

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世应比和而官鬼发动，恐怕官府捉打这场官司。世应比和是和解的象，如果官鬼发动来克，就是官府要办这场官司，不愿意和议；子孙也发动，最终才能成就和议。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With the Self and the Other in harmony but Pressure moving, I fear the authorities will take up the case and press it. The Self and the Other in harmony is the image of settlement, but if Pressure moves to control, the authorities will pursue the case and do not want a private settlement; only if Output also moves is a settlement in the end achieved.

**《黄金策·词讼》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2845 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lawsuits", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2845*

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.06 词讼·卦爻安静子孙兴喜亲友劝和公事：六爻安静，世应虽不生合，而子孙发动，必有亲戚邻居出来劝和

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦爻安静而子孙发动，喜得亲友劝和这件官事。六爻安静，世应虽然不相生合，但子孙发动，必定有亲戚邻居来劝和。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With the lines still and Output moving, one is glad to have relatives and friends mediate the official matter. With all six lines still, though the Self and the Other do not give life to or combine with each other, if Output moves there will surely be relatives and neighbours who come to mediate.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.07 词讼·世空则我欲息争：世空是我想息争，应空是他想息争，世应都空两边的怨气都消了

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻空，是我想平息争端。世爻空是我想平息争端，应爻空是他想平息争端，世爻应爻都空，双方的怨气都消了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Self is Void, I want to end the dispute. The Self Void means I want to end the dispute, the Other Void means he wants to end it, and both Void means the grievances on both sides are gone.
