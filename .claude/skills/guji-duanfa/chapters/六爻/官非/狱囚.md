## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.16 词讼·世入墓爻难免狱囚之系：世爻入墓、化墓或临鬼墓，卦象凶的必有牢狱之灾；临白虎，在狱中有病

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻入墓，难免被关进牢狱。世爻入墓、化墓，或临在鬼墓上，卦象凶的必有牢狱之灾；临白虎，在狱中生病。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Self enters the tomb, imprisonment can hardly be avoided. The Self entering the tomb, changing into the tomb, or sitting on the tomb of Pressure, with an ill hexagram, surely means the calamity of prison; on the White Tiger, he falls ill in prison.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.31 词讼·墓逢日德刑冲目下即当出狱、岁挈福神生合狱中必遇天恩：世墓、鬼墓动是入狱之象，得日辰刑冲克破眼下就出狱；狱中占卦最喜太岁生合世爻有天恩赦宥，月建生合上司审出，日辰生合有司饶恕，父母生合申诉得免

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:墓逢日德和刑冲，眼下就该出狱；太岁带着福神来生合，狱中必遇天恩。世墓、鬼墓发动，都是入狱的象，如果得到日辰刑冲克破，眼下就该出狱。在狱中占卦，最喜太岁生合世爻，主有天恩赦免；月建生合，上司审出来；日辰生合，有关官员饶恕；父母爻生合，必须申诉才能免。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The tomb meeting the Day's virtue and punishment or clash, he should leave prison at once; Tai Sui bringing Output to give life and combine, in prison he surely meets imperial grace. The tomb of the Self or of Pressure moving is the image of entering prison, and if the Day punishes, clashes, controls or breaks it, he should be out at once. Divining in prison, best of all is Tai Sui giving life to or combining with the Self, which means imperial pardon; the Month giving life or combining means the higher office trials him out; the Day giving life or combining, the officials spare him; Resource giving life or combining, he must plead to be let off.

## LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.08 随鬼入墓章·占狱讼世旺脱离、占讼事世旺者得理：世旺的脱离牢狱，休囚空破的不免于凶；世旺的占讼得理，空破休囚被克的必遭刑狱

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占狱讼的，世爻旺相能够脱离，休囚空破的免不了凶；占讼事，世爻旺相的得理，空破休囚又被克的，必定遭受刑罚牢狱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining for prison and lawsuits, a strong Self can get free, while a resting, trapped, Void or broken Self cannot avoid ill; in divining for a suit, a strong Self is in the right, and a Void, broken, resting or trapped Self that is also controlled will surely suffer punishment and prison.

## LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.09 随鬼入墓章·书中例，占已定重罪可蒙赦免否：世爻随鬼入动墓又动而化墓，古法断凶，作者断吉——日月生世、丑墓月破，破罗破网容易出来，断明年酉年蒙赦，果然次年辰月蒙赦而出；世爻入墓只验三种，旺相的不真，墓被日月动爻冲破的也不真

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:我屡次验证而得验的，只有世爻入墓三种：一是世爻随鬼入日墓，一是世爻入动墓，一是世爻动而化墓。这三种墓，自占看世爻，世爻旺相的不是真；代占看用神，用神旺相的不是真；只有世爻、用爻休囚被克，又入墓的才是；墓神被日月动爻冲破的，也不是真。因为墓破就像破网，容易出来。书中例：未月戊辰日占已经判定的重罪能不能蒙赦免，得蛊卦变损卦：世爻随鬼入动墓，又动而化墓，古法认为凶，我断为吉：日月生世，丑墓被月破，破罗破网，容易出来，断明年酉年一定蒙赦；果然次年辰月蒙赦出来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** What I have found borne out by repeated trial is that the Self entering the tomb is borne out in only three ways: the Self following Pressure into the tomb of the Day, the Self entering a moving tomb, and the Self moving and changing into the tomb. For these three tombs, in divining for oneself look at the Self, and a strong Self is not the real thing; in divining for someone else look at the Subject, and a strong Subject is not the real thing; it is real only when the Self or the Subject is resting, trapped or controlled and also enters the tomb; a tomb spirit clashed and broken by the Month or a moving line is not real either. For a broken tomb is like a broken net, and it is easy to get out. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Wuchen day, a man divined whether a grave sentence already fixed could be pardoned, and got Gu changing to Sun: the Self follows Pressure into a moving tomb and also moves and changes into the tomb, which the old method calls ill, but I judged lucky: the Month and the Day give life to the Self, the Chou tomb is broken by the Month, a net broken is easy to leave, and I judged that next year, a You year, he would surely be pardoned; indeed in the Chen month of the next year he was pardoned and let out.
