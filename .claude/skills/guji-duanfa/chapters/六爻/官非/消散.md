## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.30 词讼·子在身边到底不能结证、官伏世下讼根犹未芟除：卦身临福德出现发动，随即消散；怕官鬼伏在世爻下，讼根还在，眼下虽不成讼，到官鬼旺相透出时会被举发

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙在身边，到底不能结成证据；官鬼伏在世下，诉讼的根子还没有除掉。卦身临福德出现并发动，马上就会消散；只怕官鬼伏在世爻下，那么讼根常在，目前虽然不成讼，等官鬼旺相透出来时就会被举发。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Output beside the Body, a case can never be made into proof; with Pressure hidden under the Self, the root of the lawsuit has not been pulled out. The Body sitting on Output, appearing and moving, is scattered at once; the only fear is Pressure hidden under the Self, in which case the root of the suit is always there, and though no suit is formed for now, when Pressure is strong and comes out it will be brought up.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.33 词讼·要知消散当看子孙：福神发动官鬼安静，按生旺的日月断；官鬼发动福神安静，按官鬼入墓的日月断；卦象一成，胜负明明白白，一部讼庭的是非判得清清楚楚

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要知道怎样消散，应当看子孙。如果福神发动、官鬼安静，按生旺的日月断消散；官鬼发动、福神安静，按官鬼入墓的日月断消散。卦象既然成了，胜负明明白白；一部讼庭的是非，判得清清楚楚。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know how the matter will melt away, look at Output. If Output moves and Pressure is still, judge the day it melts by the months and days of life and strength; if Pressure moves and Output is still, judge it by the months and days when Pressure enters the tomb. Once the hexagram is formed, who wins and who loses is plain; the rights and wrongs of the whole lawcourt are decided clearly.

**《黄金策·词讼》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2903 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lawsuits", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2903*
