## LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.03 十八问答·第二问·书中例，占弟被讼：酉金兄弟为用神，午月克它、未日生它似可相敌，但动出午火月建相克是大凶象；问凶在何时，说今年辰年太岁相合无妨，化退神于申，恐危在午年申月，果然午年申月被重刑

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:凡遇回头克的，彻底克尽。书中例：午月丁未日占弟弟被人告了吉凶如何，得困卦变恒卦：酉金兄弟爻为用神，午月克它，未日生它，似乎可以相抵；但是动出午火，和月建相克，是大凶之象；他问凶在什么时候，我说今年是辰年，太岁相合没有妨碍，化出退神于申，恐怕危险在午年申月；果然到午年申月被判重刑。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whatever meets a turning-back control is controlled through and through. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Dingwei day, a man divined how things stood for his younger brother, who had been sued, and got Kun changing to Heng: the Peer line You-metal is the Subject, controlled by the Wu month and given life by the Wei day, which seems to set them even; but it moves out Wu-fire, which, controlled by the Month, is a sign of great ill. He asked when the ill would fall. I said: this year, a Chen year, Tai Sui combines with it and there is no harm, but it changes into a Retreating spirit at Shen, and I fear danger in the Shen month of a Wu year; indeed in the Shen month of the Wu year he was given a heavy punishment.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.29 词讼·世兴变鬼必因官讼亡身：世持鬼是我失理，应持鬼是他失理；世爻变鬼恐怕因为官事丧身，应爻变鬼就按对方来断

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻发动变成官鬼，必定因为官司丢了性命。世爻持鬼，是我失理；应爻持鬼，是他失理；世爻变鬼恐怕因官事丧身，应爻变鬼就按对方来断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self moving and changing into Pressure surely means losing one's life over the lawsuit. The Self holding Pressure means I am in the wrong; the Other holding Pressure means he is in the wrong; the Self changing into Pressure, I fear one may die from the official matter, and the Other changing into Pressure is judged for the opposite party.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.32 词讼·若问罪名须详官鬼：卜罪名轻重看官鬼爻，旺则罪重衰则罪轻；加刑白虎旺动克世，火主极刑、金主充军、木主笞杖、水主从罪；要按衰旺和有制无制来断，不可拘泥

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:如果问罪名，必须详察官鬼。凡是占罪名轻重，用官鬼爻来定：旺，罪重；衰，罪轻。再加刑、白虎、旺而发动克世爻，火主受极刑，金主充军，木主笞杖，水主从犯罪。要按衰旺、有制无制来断，不可拘泥。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If asking about the charge, one must examine Pressure. In judging how heavy or light the charge is, fix it by the Pressure line: strong, the charge is heavy; weak, light. With punishment and the White Tiger added, strong and moving to control the Self, Fire means the extreme penalty, Metal means military exile, Wood means the cane or rod, Water means being an accessory. Judge by strength and weakness and whether it is held in check or not, and do not be rigid.

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙适宜旺相，最忌官鬼兴盛。子孙持世、子孙发动，已经送到法场，终究会赦免；官鬼克世爻、或官鬼持世，又被日月动爻冲克，或者世爻发动化出凶的，虽然判的是轻罪也会拘押改判为重罪。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Output should be strong, and Pressure flourishing is most feared. **规则(整理,不是原文)**:蛇雀木鬼犯官刑，临火须防焚害；蛇木又言缢绞，勾陈土鬼牢狱。书中例：未月癸亥日占判定什么罪，得中孚卦变临卦：木鬼临白虎，绞罪是定了；但幸好世爻临月建，木鬼虽然发动，又被酉金回头之克，外卦反吟，事情一定反复，虽然定了绞罪，终究会有改动；果然议的是绞罪，后来蒙驳回重审减轻。

## LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.10 五行相生章·书中例，弟占兄已得重罪母问能救否：兄弟爻为用神丑土兄动被日月克，明显大罪难脱，幸好丑土化出午火父母回头相生，化父母生兄爻相生为吉，断快快去办，后来蒙恩免死

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神、元神，适宜逢生：有月建生、日建生、动爻生、动变回头生。书中例：卯月己卯日，弟弟占哥哥已经被判重罪，母亲来问能不能救，得复卦变震卦：兄弟爻为用神，丑土兄动，被日月克，明显大罪难脱，幸好兄爻丑土化出午火父母，回头相生；断说：赶快去办！化出父母去生兄爻，相生为吉，神灵告知得很明显，后来果然蒙恩免死。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Subject and the Support should meet life: life from the Month, from the Day, from a moving line, or from a turning-back life by change. Worked example: in the Mao month, on a Jimao day, a younger brother divined for his elder brother, who had already been given a grave sentence, with the mother asking whether he could be saved, and got Fu changing to Zhen: the Peer line is the Subject, Chou-earth moves and is controlled by the Month and the Day, so it is plain that he cannot escape the great charge, but fortunately the Chou-earth Peer line changes into Wu-fire as Resource that turns back to give life; I judged: go and do it quickly! Resource changing out to give life to the Peer line, mutual life, is lucky and the spirits spoke clearly; afterwards he was indeed spared death by grace.
