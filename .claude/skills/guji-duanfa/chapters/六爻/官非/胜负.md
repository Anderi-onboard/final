## LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.05 十八问答·第八问·书中例，占讼事：爻逢六合官事必审，但戌月冲世是月破、卯日克世必输无疑，果然被杖责——应在日克月破

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:月破的爻：神机显现在破；祸福的机缘根源在动，动而有生无克的破爻，有出破、填实、合破的办法；安静而有克无生的破爻，就是破了。书中例：戌月丁卯日占官司，得泰卦：卦里爻逢六合，官事必定要审；不适宜的是戌月冲世爻，这是月破，卯日又克世爻，必输无疑；果然被杖责。这是应在日克月破的缘故。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** On a line struck by the Month-break: the working of the spirits shows in the break; the root of luck and misfortune lies in movement, and a broken line that moves and has life without control has the ways of leaving the break, being filled, or being combined out of the break, while a still broken line with control and no life is broken. Worked example: in the Xu month, on a Dingmao day, a man divined about a lawsuit and got Tai: the lines meet six-combine, so the official matter must come to trial; what is unfit is the Xu month clashing the Self line, which is the Month-break, and the Mao day also controlling the Self, so he surely loses; indeed he was beaten with the rod. This answers because the Day controls the Month-break.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.02 词讼·应乃对头要休囚死绝、世为自己宜帝旺长生：不论原告被告，占的人以世爻为自己，应爻为对头；应旺世衰他强我弱，世旺应衰他弱我强

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:应爻是对头，要休囚死绝才好；世爻是自己，适宜帝旺长生。不论原告被告，占的人都以世爻为自己，应爻为对头：应爻旺而世爻衰，他强我弱；世爻旺而应爻衰，他弱我强。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Other is the opponent, and it is best resting, trapped, dead or extinct; the Self is oneself, and it is fit for Emperor Strength or Long Life. Whether one is plaintiff or defendant, the questioner takes the Self as himself and the Other as the opponent: the Other strong and the Self weak, he is strong and I weak; the Self strong and the Other weak, he is weak and I strong.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.03 词讼·相冲相克乃是欺凌之象：世爻刑克应爻不一定是我胜，是欺他之象；必须鬼爻克应爻才是我胜，动爻和月建日辰克它也是一样

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:相冲相克，是欺负凌辱的象。世爻刑克应爻，不算我胜，是欺负他的象；必须是官鬼爻克应爻，才算我胜，动爻和月建日辰克它也一样。

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.08 词讼·应动则他多机变：世动我必用心谋划，若化官鬼兄弟回头克制反为失计；应动他必有谋，加月建必有贵人依靠，克世则不吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:应爻发动，他就多有机变。世爻发动，我必定用心谋划，如果化出官鬼或兄弟回头克制，反而失策；应爻发动，他必定有谋划，如果加上月建，必有贵人依靠，克世爻就不吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Other moves, he has many tricks and changes. ## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.24 词讼·无合无生纵旺何如独脚虎、有刑有克逢空当效缩头龟：应爻旺动但没有生合，他虽刚强是独脚虎不足畏；世爻没有生合又遇日月动爻刑克，应当学缩头龟，暂且别和他对理

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:没有合没有生，即使旺又怎么样，不过是独脚的老虎；有刑有克又逢空，应当学缩头的乌龟。应爻旺而发动、没有生合的，他虽然刚强，是独脚虎不值得怕；世爻没有生合，又遇到日月动爻刑克，应当学缩头龟，暂且不要和他对理。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With no combination and no life, however strong, what is it but a one-legged tiger? With punishment and control, and also in the Void, one should act like a tortoise drawing in its head. An Other that is strong and moving but has no life or combination is, for all its hardness, a one-legged tiger not worth fearing; a Self with no life or combination that also meets punishment or control from the Month, the Day or a moving line should act like a tortoise drawing in its head and for the time being not argue the case with him.

## LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.03 斗殴争竞章·彼此相争看世应：不宜冲克，喜相生；日月克身我必受辱，动爻克应他必遭刑；应克世他胜，世克应我赢；世空世破世动化退我胆怯，应动应旺应化进神他得势；六冲必散，六合必成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:彼此相争，要看世爻应爻：不适宜冲克，喜欢相生；日月克身，我必定受辱；动爻克应爻，他必定遭刑；应爻克世爻他胜，世爻克应爻我赢。世爻空、世爻破、世爻动而化退，我心里胆怯；应爻动、应爻旺、应爻化进神，他得势了。卦遇六冲一定散，卦逢六合一定成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the two sides contest, look to the Self and the Other: clash and control are not fit and giving life is welcome; if the Month or the Day controls the Body, I surely suffer insult; if a moving line controls the Other, he surely suffers punishment; the Other controlling the Self means he wins, the Self controlling the Other means I win. The Self Void, the Self broken, or the Self moving and changing into a Retreating spirit means I am timid at heart; the Other moving, the Other strong, or the Other changing into an Advancing spirit means he has the upper hand. A hexagram meeting six-clash surely breaks up; a hexagram meeting six-combine surely comes to terms.

## LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.04 斗殴争竞章·总不离乎子孙爻：子孙发动、子孙持世，争斗不成；日月动爻宜克应不宜克世；世爻宜旺动克应及动而化吉，官事必赢

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:总不离子孙爻：子孙发动、子孙持世，争斗打不起来；日月和动爻适宜克应爻，不适宜克世爻；世爻适宜旺相发动去克应爻，以及动而化出吉的，官事一定赢。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** It never leaves the Output line: Output moving or holding the Self means the fight does not come off; the Month, the Day and moving lines are fit to control the Other and unfit to control the Self; the Self is fit to be strong and moving to control the Other, or to move and change into something lucky, and then the official matter is surely won.
