## LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.01 何知章·讼事与争竞的几句：讼事休是空亡官鬼又休囚，讼事多是朱雀白虎持世而鬼来扶，多争竞是朱雀兄弟推世应，小人生是玄武官鬼动临身，灾祸至是鬼临应爻来克世

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:怎么知道人家的官司了结：官鬼落空亡又休囚。怎么知道人家官司多：朱雀、白虎持世，官鬼来扶助。怎么知道人家多争执：朱雀、兄弟推动世爻应爻。怎么知道人家有小人：玄武、官鬼发动临在身爻。怎么知道人家灾祸到：官鬼临在应爻来克世爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** How do we know a household's suit is over? Pressure is Void and also resting or trapped. How do we know a household has many suits? The Vermilion Bird or the White Tiger holds the Self and Pressure comes to support it. How do we know a household has many quarrels? The Vermilion Bird and the Peer line push on the Self and the Other. How do we know a petty person has appeared? The Dark Tortoise and Pressure move and sit on the Body. How do we know calamity is coming? Pressure sits on the Other line and comes to control the Self.

## LY.LAWSUIT.BSZZ.02 诗诀·官讼和问讼的句子：子孙持世则官讼从今了便休，占官问讼宜财托而动变兄官万事凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙持世，官司从今了结就罢休；财爻持世，求官问讼适宜用财去托人，发动变出兄弟、官鬼，事事皆凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Output holding the Self, the lawsuit ends and rests from now on; with Wealth holding the Self, in seeking office or asking about a suit it is fit to entrust the matter with money, and if Peer or Pressure moves or changes out, everything is ill.

**《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·三十三、诸爻持世诀》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 198 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 33: the verse on lines holding the Self", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 198*

**《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·三十三、诸爻持世诀》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 200 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 33: the verse on lines holding the Self", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 200*

> **按**:这两句出在诸爻持世诀的两联：官讼一句在「子孙持世」那一联，问讼一句在「财爻持世」那一联；每联开头的持世爻没有拆进引文里。
>
> **Note:** The two lines come from two couplets of the verse on lines holding the Self: the line on lawsuits is in the couplet for Output holding the Self, and the line on asking about a suit is in the couplet for Wealth holding the Self; the line named at the start of each couplet is not split into the quotation.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.01 词讼·小忿不惩必至争长竞短、大亏既负宁不诉枉申冤，欲定输赢须详世应：卦中世应就是状子里的原告被告，看它们就知两边胜负

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:小的怨气不加惩戒，必然争长论短；大的亏已经吃了，怎能不告状申冤？要定输赢，必须详察世爻和应爻。卦中的世应，就是状纸上的原告和被告，详察它们，两边的胜负就可以知道。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A small grievance not checked leads to quarrelling over who is greater; a great wrong already suffered, how can one not sue to set it right? To settle who wins and who loses, one must examine the Self and the Other. The Self and the Other in the hexagram are the plaintiff and the defendant in the plaint; by examining them one knows the success or failure of both sides.

**《黄金策·词讼》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2837 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lawsuits", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2837*
