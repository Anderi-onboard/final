## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.10 词讼·身乃根因事体空则情虚：卦身是词讼的根由，旺则事大衰则事小，动则急静则缓；空伏都是编造的虚事，飞伏都没有就一点不实

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦身是事情的根本缘由，落空就情节虚假。卦身关系到词讼的根由：旺，事大；衰，事小；动，事急；静，事缓；如果空或伏，都是编造的故事；飞神伏神都没有，一丝一毫都不是实情。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Body is the root cause and substance of the matter, and if it is Void the facts are false. The Body concerns the root of the lawsuit: strong, the matter is great; weak, small; moving, urgent; still, slow; if it is Void or hidden, it is a made-up story; if neither flying nor hidden spirit is present, not a hair of it is true.

**《黄金策·词讼》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2855 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lawsuits", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2855*

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.19 词讼·上下有父词兴两度始能成：官鬼和父母两爻不宜重复出现，主有转变不定的象，事情缠绵，最终难了结；占告状遇到这个象，必要再告一次才能成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:上下卦都有父母，状词要递两次才能成。官鬼、父母两爻不适宜重复出现，主有转变不定的象，事情一定缠绵，最终难以了结。比如占告状，遇到这个象，必定再告一次才成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Resource in both upper and lower trigrams, the plaint must be lodged twice before it succeeds. The Pressure and Resource lines should not appear twice over, for that means an unsettled image of change; the matter will surely drag on and in the end be hard to finish. For example, in divining for lodging a complaint, meeting this image, it must be lodged once more before it succeeds.

**《黄金策·词讼》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2873 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lawsuits", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2873*

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.20 词讼·官父两强词讼表章皆准理、妻财一动申呈诉告总徒劳：凡是要上表、申奏、递状、告诉，官鬼和父母都要有气不空才准理；最怕财爻动伤父母，必不成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼和父母两爻都强，词讼和表章都能被受理；妻财一发动，申呈和诉告全是白费。凡是要上表、申奏、递呈子、告诉这类事，都要官鬼和父母两样齐全，有气不空，就能受理；最怕财爻动伤父母，一定不成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Pressure and Resource both strong, lawsuits and memorials alike are accepted; once Wealth moves, petitions and complaints are all in vain. For anything such as presenting a memorial, submitting a report, lodging a petition or making a complaint, Pressure and Resource must both be present, with breath and not Void, and then it is accepted; most to be feared is Wealth moving to injure Resource, in which case it surely fails.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.26 词讼·父临应上彼欲兴词：父母是文书，临世是我想告理，临应是他想申诉；发动是要进行，安静就不会提起

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母临在应爻上，他想要起诉。父母是文书，临在世爻上，是我想告理；临在应爻上，是他想申诉；发动是想要进行，安静就不会提起。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Resource on the Other, he wants to file a suit. Resource is the papers; on the Self, I want to lodge the case; on the Other, he wants to file his plea; moving, it is going ahead; still, it will not be raised.

## LY.LAWSUIT.HJC.27 词讼·父动而官化福爻事将成而偶逢兜劝、父空而身临刑煞词未准而先被笞刑：占告状遇官父两动讼事可成；父有气或官化子孙，是人到公门将投状时有人劝止；父化空亡墓绝、官鬼刑克世爻或被日辰刑冲克害，状子不准先挨板子

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母发动而官鬼化出福神，事情将成时偶然遇到有人拉着劝；父母落空而身爻临刑煞，状词还没批准就先挨笞刑。凡是占告状，遇到官鬼、父母两爻都动，官司可以成；如果父母有气，或官鬼化出子孙，就是人到公门将要投状，有人在旁边劝；如果父母化出空亡墓绝，官鬼刑克世爻，或者被日辰刑冲克害，状告不准，先遭杖责。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Resource moving and Pressure changing into Output, just as the matter is about to be done someone happens to pull one back and urge restraint; with Resource Void and the Body on a punishing star, the plaint is not yet accepted and one is beaten first. In divining for lodging a complaint, if Pressure and Resource both move, the suit can go ahead; if Resource has breath, or Pressure changes into Output, then when one reaches the court door about to submit the plaint someone persuades one against it; if Resource changes into the Void, tomb or extinction, Pressure punishes or controls the Self, or is punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Day, the complaint is not accepted and one is first beaten with the rod.

## LY.LAWSUIT.ZS.05 兴词举讼章·官父同兴公庭有理、父官克世受屈含冤：世化退神、世空世破、卦变六冲、世被刑冲，终不成讼；财与子孙同动克破文书官鬼，也不成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼和父母同时发动，在公堂上有理；父母、官鬼克世爻，受屈含冤。世爻化退神、世爻空或破、卦变成六冲、世爻被刑冲，终究不成讼；财爻和子孙同时发动，克破文书和官鬼，也主不成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure and Resource moving together, one is in the right in the public hall; Resource and Pressure controlling the Self, one suffers wrong and swallows the grievance. The Self changing into a Retreating spirit, the Self Void or broken, the hexagram changing into six-clash, or the Self punished or clashed, in the end no suit is brought; Wealth and Output moving together to control and break the papers and Pressure also means it is not brought.
