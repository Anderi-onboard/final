## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.17 出行·父遭风雨之淋漓舟行尤忌：父母爻是辛劳之神又代表船，动来刑克世爻，必被风雨所阻，坐船尤其忌

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻是辛勤劳苦的神，动了就要跋涉路程、不能安稳；刑克世爻，必定被风雨阻住；父母爻又代表船，克世，行船不利，所以坐船出行尤其忌讳。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Resource line is the spirit of toil and hardship; if it moves one must trudge the road and cannot be at ease; if it punishes or controls the Self, one will surely be stopped by wind and rain; the Resource line is also the boat, and controlling the Self makes travel by boat unfavourable, so it is especially to be avoided when travelling by boat.

**《黄金策·出行》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3457 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3457*

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占买船和雇船，断法相同，按船户（船的主人）的办法断；如果是船家自己占，就要按船家的家宅来断，不是这里的断法。

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.58 舟船·六亲持世可推新旧之由：看船新旧用六亲持世——财福是新，父母是旧，兄弟半新半旧，官鬼多灾惊；兼看衰旺

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:推断船的新旧，看哪一个六亲持世：财爻、福神持世是新船，父母爻持世是旧船，兄弟爻持世是半新半旧，官鬼持世则多灾多惊；还要兼看衰旺。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To tell whether a boat is new or old, see which of the six relations holds the Self: Wealth or Output is new, Resource is old, Peer is half new and half old, and Pressure means many calamities and frights; strength and weakness are also taken into account.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.59 舟船·诸鬼动临可识节病之处：金鬼钉少，土鬼灰少，木鬼有缝，水鬼有漏，火鬼有燥裂——船的毛病看哪一行的鬼爻发动

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:船的毛病看是哪一行的鬼爻发动：金鬼是钉子少，土鬼是灰（油灰）少，木鬼是板缝，水鬼是漏水，火鬼是木头干燥裂开。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The boat's faults are read from the element of the moving Pressure line: Metal Pressure means too few nails, Earth Pressure too little putty, Wood Pressure seams, Water Pressure leaks, and Fire Pressure dry cracks.

**《黄金策·舟船》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3548 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Boats and ships", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3548*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 诸鬼动临， | 各种鬼爻发动临到（船上）， | When the various Pressure lines move and arrive, |
| 可识节病之处 | 可以知道毛病所在的地方 | one can tell where the faults lie |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3548 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 3548*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 金鬼钉少，土鬼灰少，木鬼有缝，水鬼有漏，火鬼有燥裂。 | 金鬼是钉子少，土鬼是灰少，木鬼有缝，水鬼有漏，火鬼有干燥裂开。 | Metal Pressure means too few nails, Earth Pressure too little putty, Wood Pressure has seams, Water Pressure leaks, and Fire Pressure dry cracks. |

> **按**:「节病」是船体各节、各处的毛病；「灰」指填船缝用的油灰。这一句按鬼爻所属五行断船的毛病，和疾病章按鬼爻五行断病位是同一种取法。
>
> **Note:** "Faults" here are the defects in the parts and joints of the hull, and "putty" is the caulking used to fill the seams. Reading a boat's faults by the element of Pressure is the same kind of reading as judging the seat of an illness by the element of Pressure in the chapters on illness.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.38 行人章·书中例，连日占风：上爻寅鬼化酉金子孙则明日酉时有风；次日再占不再报，因为已知；子水子孙与世爻相合若被合住须冲破，明日午时顺风必大，舟行三百余里

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When Output moves, that day has wind; when Output combines with the Self and is held fast, it needs to be clashed open to succeed. Someone asked: you said yesterday there would be wind at the You hour today, so why does today's hexagram not show it? I said: the spirits take it that I already know, and do not report it again. There will indeed be wind at the You hour today, but it will certainly not be strong; what the spirits report is tomorrow's great wind. Output Zi-water combines with the Self, and when it is held fast by a combination it must be clashed open before it succeeds; tomorrow at the Wu hour the fair wind will certainly be strong.
