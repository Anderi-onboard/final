## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.08 出行·两间齐空独行则吉：间爻代表同伴，两间都落旬空，宜自己单独走

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:间爻如果空，主途中没有阻滞；间爻又代表同伴，两个间爻都落旬空，宜自己单独走，免得被同伴拖累。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If a between line is Void there is no obstruction on the way; the between lines also stand for companions, and if both are in the Void of their ten-day period it is fit to travel alone, so as to escape being burdened by companions.

**《黄金策·出行》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3439 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3439*

| --- | --- | --- |
| 两间齐空独行则吉 | 两个间爻都空，独自一人走就吉 | If the two between lines are both Void, travelling alone is lucky |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3439 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 3439*

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.18 出行·福遇和同之伴侣谒贵反凶：子孙持世最吉，遇好同伴路上平安；但为谒贵而出行则不宜，子动伤官反不利

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙爻持世最吉，主碰上好同伴，路上平安；但如果是为了拜见贵人而出行就不适宜，因为子孙发动叫伤官，反而不利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Output holding the Self is the best omen: one meets good companions and the road is safe; but if one travels to call on a patron it is unfit, since Output moving is called wounding the official and is on the contrary unfavourable.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.21 出行·鬼动间中不谐同侣：鬼爻在间爻发动，不是同伴不和，就是同伴里有人生病，克世则自己会后悔

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼爻在间爻上发动，不是同伴之间不和，就是同伴里有人生病；如果克世，自己会后悔。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure moves on a between line, either the companions are at odds or one of them is ill; if it controls the Self, one will regret it.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.04 出行章·两间爻是同行附载之人：发动克世必遭其害，动临兄爻必破我财，生合我者吉；两间齐空则不是中途梗阻就是无伴单行

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:两个间爻是同行、搭载的人：发动克世，一定遭他们的害；发动临着兄爻，一定破我的财；生合我的就吉；如果两个间爻同时空，不是中途受阻，就一定没有伴、一个人走。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The two between lines are the people who travel with one or are carried along: moving to control the Self, they surely do one harm; moving with a Peer line, they surely cost me money; those that give me life or combine with me are lucky; and if both between lines are Void, either one is obstructed on the road or one travels alone without company.

| 动临兄爻，必破我财； | 发动临着兄爻，一定破我的财； | moving with a Peer line, they surely cost me money; |
| 生合我者则吉。 | 生合我的就吉。 | those that give me life or combine with me are lucky. |
| 倘两间齐空，若非中途梗阻，定然无伴单行。 | 如果两个间爻同时空，不是中途受阻，就一定没有伴、一个人走。 | If both between lines are Void, either one is obstructed on the road or one travels alone without company. |

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.15 同舟共行章·同行共处应为尊、同路同舟一样论：应伤世位遭他害，只宜生合相扶助；三合同途皆遂意、六冲半路便灰心；占他人以应为用，占亲戚看所用用神，不宜兄鬼动克世

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:同行、同住，以应爻为尊，同路、同船一样论：应爻伤了世位，会遭他的害；应爻入墓、空、绝、破，是辜负我的恩情；只适宜生合、互相扶助，永远靠他维持，像至亲一样。三合同路，事事遂意；六冲，走到半路就灰心。日常起居为害，看兄弟、官鬼发动；水路和睦清平，看子孙发动。占别人，以应为用；占亲戚，看所属的用神；不宜兄、鬼发动克世，最宜相合相生。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For those who travel and stay together, the Other is the honoured one, and those on the same road or in the same boat are judged alike: if the Other injures the Self position, one suffers harm from him; if the Other is in the tomb, Void, extinct or broken, he is ungrateful for my kindness; it is fit only that they give life, combine and support each other, so that one can always rely on his help as on one's own kin. Three-harmony on the same road, everything goes as wished; six-clash, one loses heart halfway. For harm in daily life and lodging, look to a moving Peer or Pressure; for peace and harmony on the water, look to a moving Output. In divining for another person, the Other is the Subject; for a relative, look at that relative's own Subject; it is unfit for Peer or Pressure to move and control the Self, and best when they combine and generate.
