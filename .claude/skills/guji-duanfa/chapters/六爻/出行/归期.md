## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.05 辩贵人禄马之谬·卜行人当察用神：用神临驿马动则归期可定，驿马动而用爻受伤则不可以驿马断其来——驿马是行动之宿，不是用神

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占行人要先看用神：用神临着驿马发动，回来的日期可以定；如果驿马发动而用爻受了伤，不可以拿驿马来断他会来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining for a traveller, look first at the Subject: if the Subject sits on the Post Horse and moves, the day of return can be fixed; if the Post Horse moves but the Subject line is injured, one cannot judge his coming from the Post Horse.

## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.06 十八问答·第二问·书中例，占仆何日回：亥水财爻为用神，不谓月破，却怕重重土动伤它，「双拳不敌四手」，不仅难盼归期，还防途中不测，后来午月火旺合未助土，中途遇害

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:回头克的道理是：凡遇回头克，彻底克尽。书中例：巳月丁亥日有人占仆人哪天回来，得夬卦变履卦：亥水财爻是用神，亥水虽然是月建，不叫月破，但是不宜重重土爻发动来伤它。谚语说「双拳不敌四手」，不但难盼他回来，还要提防途中不测；后来到午月火旺、合未助土的时候，仆人在中途遇害了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** On the turning-back control: whatever meets a turning-back control is controlled through and through. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Dinghai day, a man divined on what day his servant would return and got Guai changing to Lü (Treading): the Wealth Hai-water is the Subject; though Hai-water is the Month it is not called Month-break, but it must not be injured by crowded movement of Earth lines. As the saying goes, two fists are no match for four hands; there is not only little hope for his return but beware of mishap on the road. Later, in the Wu month, when Fire was strong, combined Wei and helped Earth, the servant was killed on the way.

**《卜筮正宗·十八问答·第二问·回头之克》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3651 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Eighteen questions, no. 2: the turning-back control", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3651*

| 原用二神遇之则凶，忌仇二神遇之反吉也。 | 原用二神遇到它就凶，忌神仇神遇到它反而吉。 | The original two spirits (the Subject and its Support) are ill when they meet it, while the Adversary and the enemy spirit are lucky when they meet it. |

## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.09 十八问答·第十五问·书中例，占父远出何日回：外卦伏吟在外有忧愁之象，内卦辰土父母化巳火回头之生、世爻午火化卯木助火生之，并无害；第四爻午火独静，五月必归；后三四月父在湖广生理，省城兵乱，五月方归

## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.10 十八问答·第十八问·书中例，占伯父何日回：用神伏藏受克而不来，若问平安则父母卯木被日月动爻冲克必不安，问来不来不能这样断；后来果然不来，在外平安（与《增删卜易》同一卦，被占的人是母亲）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:书中例：酉月戊申日占伯父哪天回来，得旅卦变艮卦：这一卦如果问伯父平安与否，卯木父母伏而不现，被日月动爻冲克，必定不平安；现在问的是回不回来，不这样断，只能断用神伏藏受克所以不来；后来果然没有来，他在外面平安。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Worked example: in the You month, on a Wushen day, a man divined on what day his uncle would come back and got Lü (the Traveller) changing to Gen: had this hexagram been asked about the uncle's safety, the Resource Mao-wood is hidden and does not appear, clashed and controlled by the Month, the Day and the moving lines, and he would surely be unwell; but the question is whether he comes back, so it is not judged that way, and one can only judge that the Subject is hidden and controlled and so does not come; in the end he did not come, and he was safe abroad.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.28 行人·主象交重身已动、用爻安静未思归：主象发动行人已动身，用爻安静又无冲并则安居异乡还没想回家

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:主象（用爻）发动，行人已经动身；用爻安静，又没有日辰、动爻来冲来并，他就安居在异乡，还没起回家的念头。

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.29 行人·克速生迟、我若制他难见面：用爻克世或世落空亡人很快到，生合世爻回得迟，最忌世爻动克用爻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻发动克世爻，或者世爻落空亡，人一定很快就到；用爻生合世爻，人回得迟；最忌世爻发动去克用爻，那是回不来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject moves and controls the Self, or the Self falls into Void, the man will surely arrive soon; if the Subject gives life to or combines with the Self, he returns late; most to be feared is the Self moving to control the Subject line, which means he cannot come back.

| 我若制他难见面 | 我如果制住他，就难以见面 | if I control him, it is hard to meet him |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3485 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 3485*

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.46 千金赋注·觉子论用克世：旧注说用神克世不作凶看、是事来就我，觉子以为不尽然——求财财爻克世必得、行人用神克世即归、医药子孙克世即愈，其余各占都不宜用神克世，占功名官鬼克世更是非祸即灾

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用克世就是用神来克世爻。旧注说不要当凶看，因为主事的爻动来克世，是这件事来就我，事必易成，我虽被克又有什么妨碍，所以说克世的不算凶。觉子认为这个说法不完全对。他验证过的：占求财，财爻克世一定得到；占行人，用神克世马上回来；占医药，子孙克世即愈。除了这几种占，其他都不宜用神克世。如果占功名，官鬼克世，不是祸就是灾，不能一概说不要当凶看。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** "The Subject controls the Self" means the Subject comes to control the Self. The old commentary says not to take it as ill: the line that rules the matter moves to control the Self, which means the matter comes to me and will easily be done, so what harm is there in my being controlled? Hence it is said that a controller of the Self is not taken as ill. Juezi thinks this is not entirely right. What he has found borne out is this: in divining for wealth, a Wealth line that controls the Self is surely gained; in divining for a traveller, a Subject that controls the Self returns at once; in divining for medicine, Output controlling the Self means a cure. Apart from these few divinations, the Subject controlling the Self is unfit in all others. If one divines for office, Pressure controlling the Self is not misfortune but calamity, and it cannot be said across the board not to take it as ill.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.52 动散章·书中例，占父出外一载无音：卯木父爻动而生世、又化子水回头生，断在外平安、世空者速至、交春即归；果于二月回——卯动被酉日冲也没有冲散

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The ancients called a moving line clashed by the Day "scattered", and also said a moving line clashing another line can scatter it. Worked example: in the Chou month, on a Dingyou day, a man divined about his father who had gone abroad and sent no word for a year, and got Huan changing to Kan: the Resource Mao-wood moves and gives life to the Self, and changes into Zi-water that turns back to give life, so he was promised that his father was safe abroad, that a Void Self means a quick arrival, and that he would return at the turn of spring.
