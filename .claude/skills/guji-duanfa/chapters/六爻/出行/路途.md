## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.04 总断千金赋·路上有官休出外、宅中有鬼勿居家：五爻为道路，官鬼在五爻发动，途中惊险，不宜出外，灾咎按所临六神断；二爻为住宅，鬼动二爻不利，得子孙持世发动才无害，不然宜迁店铺

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:五爻是道路，官鬼落在五爻发动，路上一定多有惊险，不适宜出外，要知道是什么灾咎，按它所临的六神来断，白虎是风波，玄武是盗贼。二爻是住宅，在家求财，鬼爻动在二爻必然不利，按它所临的五行断，火鬼忌火烛之类；得子孙持世发动，差不多就没有害，如果没有子孙发动，宜迁换店铺来化解。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The fifth line is the road: when Pressure sits on it and moves, there will surely be many frights and dangers on the way, and it is unfit to go out; to know what calamity, judge by the six spirits it carries, the White Tiger meaning storms and the Dark Tortoise thieves. The second line is the dwelling: for seeking wealth at home, Pressure moving on this line is surely unfavourable, judged by the element it carries, a Fire Pressure fearing fire and candles and the like; with Output holding the Self and moving there is hardly any harm, and with no Output moving it is resolved by moving the shop.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.04 出行·间爻安静往来一路平安：间爻是往来经过的地方，动则途中阻滞，安静则平安无阻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:间爻代表往来经过的地方：动了，途中有阻滞；安静，往来平安没有阻碍；间爻临着财爻或福神发动，途中谋求的反比到了地头还好。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The between lines are the places passed through going and coming: if they move there is obstruction on the way; if they are still, the going and coming is safe and unobstructed; if a between line carrying Wealth or Output moves, what is sought on the road is better than at the destination.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.06 出行章·官鬼交重灾祸重：武鬼忧盗贼、朱鬼防讼非、虎鬼疾病淹缠、蛇鬼风波惊险、勾鬼事勾连、青鬼戒嫖赌；鬼发震乾车马患，兑坎虑风波，坤艮山岭逢殃，离巽炎厂林窖被难

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.09 出行章·兄冲世位花月耗破灾非：财持世而兄爻冲克，有的应在月夕花朝无端浪费，有的应在奸徒小辈暗骗明欺，酿成灾非，破耗百出

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟爻冲克世位，主在花前月下耗费钱财、引来灾非：财爻持世而被兄爻冲克的，有的应在月夜花朝无缘无故地浪费，有的应在奸诈之徒、小人暗骗明欺，以致酿成灾非，破耗百出。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Peer line clashing with or controlling the Self position means money wasted amid flowers and moonlight and trouble brought on: when Wealth holds the Self and a Peer line clashes with or controls it, it sometimes answers in pointless waste on moonlit nights and flowery mornings, and sometimes in treacherous rogues and petty men cheating in secret and deceiving openly, so that trouble is brewed and losses of every kind appear.
