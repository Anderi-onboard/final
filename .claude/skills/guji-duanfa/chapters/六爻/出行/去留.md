## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.02 通玄赋·出行宜世动、归魂不出疆，用神发动值三合则行人立回：出行要世爻动；归魂卦不出边疆；用爻发动逢三合，行人马上回来

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:出行适宜世爻发动；归魂卦不出疆界；用爻发动又遇三合，行人立刻回到家。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Setting out is fit when the Self moves; a Returning Soul hexagram does not leave the border; when the Subject moves and meets a three-harmony, the traveller is back home at once.

**《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·三十一、通玄赋》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 187 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 31: the Tongxuan rhapsody", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 187*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 出行宜世动， | 出行适宜世爻发动， | Setting out is fit when the Self moves, |
| 归魂不出疆； | 归魂卦不出疆界； | a Returning Soul hexagram does not leave the border; |
| 用动值三合， | 用爻发动又遇三合， | when the Subject moves and meets a three-harmony, |
| 行人立回庄。 | 行人立刻回到家。 | the traveller is back home at once. |

> **打架**:《增删卜易》三合章说：占出行，用神在三合之内，被合而留；占行人，用神在三合之内，被合不返。这里却说用爻发动值三合，行人立回。两处都有「三合」，但一说被合住不回、一说立回，没有一本说明区别。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The chapter on three-harmony in the Zengshan Buyi says that in setting out a Subject inside a three-harmony is combined shut and stays, and for a traveller it is combined shut and does not return; this verse says that a Subject that moves and meets a three-harmony means the traveller returns at once. Both name three-harmony, one saying the traveller is held and the other that he is back, and neither book explains the difference.

## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.08 十八问答·第十五问·书中例，占自去寻父回：寅木独发克父爻持世，自身不能动、父也见不到，要等冲克寅木之年月；再占得革之既济，世化申金回头生，果于申年八月寻父回家

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:独静独发只能看事情成败的快慢，至于吉凶要推用神，舍掉用神去决断的，迂腐而且错误。书中例：午月丙午日占亲自去寻找父亲能不能回来，得大有卦变离卦：一位懂易的朋友拿着这一卦问我，寅木一爻独发，正月能见到吗？我说不是的，卦中父爻持世，被寅木克制，自己不能动，父亲也见不到，想让自己动身见到父亲，必须等到冲克寅木的年月；让他再占一卦合起来决断，得革卦变既济卦：这一卦正合前一卦，前一卦应在冲开寅木的申，这一卦世爻化出申金回头生，也应在申；果然在申年八月寻父回了家，应在申年，是前卦冲去忌神、后卦化出申金父母用神生世的缘故。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Sole still and sole moving only show how fast or slow the matter succeeds or fails; luck and ill must be reckoned from the Subject, and to decide while setting the Subject aside is pedantic and wrong. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Bingwu day, a man divined about going himself to seek his father and bringing him back, and got Dayou changing to Li: a friend who knew the Changes held up this hexagram and asked me whether Yin-wood moving alone meant he would see his father in the first month. I said no; the Resource line holds the Self and is controlled by Yin-wood, so he cannot move himself and cannot see his father; to set out and see his father, he must wait for a year or month that clashes or controls Yin-wood. I told him to divine again to settle it together, and he got Ge changing to Jiji: this hexagram agrees exactly with the former; the former answered at Shen, which clashes Yin open, and this one has the Self changing into Shen-metal that turns back to give life, which also answers at Shen. Indeed in the eighth month of a Shen year he went to seek his father and came home with him. It answered in the Shen year because the former hexagram clashed away the Adversary and the latter changed into Shen-metal, the Resource and Subject, to give life to the Self.

**《卜筮正宗·十八问答·第十五问·独静独发》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 5075 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Eighteen questions, no. 15: sole still and sole moving", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 5075*

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.06 出行·世伤应位不拘远近总宜行、应克世爻无问公私皆不利：世克应则所向通达，应克世则所向闭塞

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻克应位，是我制住对方，所去的方向通达，去了没有阻隔，不论远近都适宜出行；应爻克世爻，所去的方向闭塞，不论公事私事都不利，再遇到动爻、日辰刑克，更不吉利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self controlling the Other position is my controlling the other side: the way I go is open and I go without hindrance, and it is fit to set out however far or near; the Other controlling the Self closes the way I go and is unfavourable for public and private business alike, and with a moving line or the Day punishing or controlling as well it is the more inauspicious.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.09 出行·世动订期变鬼则自投罗网、官临畏宿化福则终脱樊笼：世动则行期已定，变鬼爻去后遭祸，鬼持世是想走不走，鬼化子孙虽有灾不足畏

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻发动，行期就定了；世爻、应爻都动宜赶快走；世爻动而变出鬼爻，去后必遭祸患，等于自投罗网；鬼爻持世，是犹豫畏缩、想走又不走的样子；鬼爻化出子孙，即使有灾患也不足畏惧，最终能脱身。

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.10 出行·静遇日冲必为他人而去、动逢间合定因同伴而留：世静被日冲是替别人去，世动被合住是有牵绊，间爻合住才是同伴挽留
