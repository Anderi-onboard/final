## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.01 六亲发动诀·行人句：父动则行人书信动，子孙发动行人买卖身康泰，官鬼克兄弟出外逃亡定见灾，财爻发动行人在外身将动，兄弟交重克财若带吉神有助则出路行人便未来

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六亲发动的诀里，和行人、出外有关的几句：父母爻发动，盼望的行人有书信的动静；子孙发动，行人、做买卖的人身体康泰；官鬼克兄弟，出外或逃亡的人必定见灾；财爻发动，在外的行人将要动身；兄弟交重去克财，如果带着吉神有助，出路上的行人还没有来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The lines of the verse on the six relations that bear on travellers and going out: when the Resource line moves, the letters of the traveller one is watching for begin to stir; when Output moves, travellers and traders are well and at ease; when Pressure controls the Peer, those who go out or flee will surely meet disaster; when the Wealth line moves, the traveller abroad is about to set out; when the Peer lines are crowded with movement and control Wealth, if they carry an auspicious spirit and are helped, the traveller on the road has not yet come.

## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.07 十八问答·第十二问·书中例，占子在楚生理何日回、叔占侄在外平安：前卦子孙伏于世爻丑土墓库之下又墓于日辰，后卦子孙现而化墓、寅木原神真破真空，申金月建官鬼临道路发动，两卦并看是不祥之兆；后来果然传来五月长江履舟而死的口信

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:四生墓绝有三种：生墓绝于日辰是一，生墓绝于飞爻是二，动而变出来的是三。用神墓绝，有救就无凶。书中例：申月癸丑日有人占儿子在楚地做生意哪天回，得损卦：申金子孙伏在世爻丑土的墓库之下，本来就不宜，怎能又墓在日辰？令郎恐怕有大患；他说，近来有信，里面说八月起身，所以才占他来不来，我说这一卦难以断他的归期；他的叔叔说，我来占侄子在外平安与否，又得无妄卦变颐卦：前一卦子孙不现且入墓，后一卦现身却化墓，何况原神寅木是真破真空、没有生助，又有申金月建官鬼临在道路上发动，两卦并看，是不祥之兆；他说，前些日子有口信传来说五月在长江翻船死了，这个信息我已经听到，卦理很明白，所以才开玩笑去占。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Life, Tomb and Extinction come in three kinds: in the Day is one, in the flying line is two, and what comes out by moving and changing is three. A Subject in the tomb or extinct is not ill if there is rescue. Worked example: in the Shen month, on a Guichou day, a man divined on what day his son, trading in Chu, would return and got Sun: Output Shen-metal is hidden under the tomb store Chou-earth of the Self line, which is already unfit, and how can it be in the tomb of the Day as well? Your son may meet a great misfortune. He said: a letter came recently saying he would set out in the eighth month, so I asked whether he would come; I said this hexagram could not fix his day of return. His uncle said: I came to divine whether my nephew is safe abroad, and he got Wuwang changing to Yi: in the former hexagram Output is hidden and in the tomb; in the latter it appears but changes into a tomb, and moreover the Support Yin-wood is truly broken and Void with no help, and Pressure Shen-metal, the Month, sits on the road and moves; read together, the two hexagrams are an ominous sign. He said: a few days ago word came that he died in the fifth month when his boat capsized on the Yangtze; I had already heard it, and the hexagram principle is very clear, so I divined as a jest.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.35 行人·世克用而俱动转往他方：不宜世克用爻，用爻安静受克仍在旧处，用爻发动人已起程，世动克它而用爻也动则转往别处

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:不宜世爻克用爻：用爻安静受克，人还在原来的地方；用爻发动，人已经起程；被发动的世爻克它而用爻也动，就转往别处。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** It is unfit for the Self to control the Subject line: if the Subject is still and controlled, the man is still in the old place; if it moves, he has already set out; if a moving Self controls it and the Subject also moves, he turns and goes elsewhere.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.37 行人·远行最怕用爻伤、尤嫌入墓：用神出现不受伤克、不落旬空月破则在外吉利归迟无妨，遇墓绝及日月动爻变刑克皆不吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占远行，用神出现、不受伤克、不落旬空月破，主他在外吉利，回来迟也无妨；如果遇到墓、绝，或者日月、动爻、变爻的刑克，都主不吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining a distant journey, if the Subject appears, is not injured or controlled, and is not in the Void of its ten-day period or under a Month-break, he does well abroad and there is no harm if his return is late; if it meets the tomb or extinction, or the punishment or control of the Day, the Month, a moving line or a changed line, all are inauspicious.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.22 行人章·用爻无病可断归期、有病则在外不安：野鹤说用神墓绝空破受伤才叫有病；问平安要看有病无病，问归期只看来与不来

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻没有病，可以断回来的日期；用爻有病，他在外面不安。野鹤说：用神入墓、绝、空、破、受伤，叫做有病。来人问行人在外平安与否，要看用神有病没病；如果问回来的日期，只看卦象来断来不来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject line is free of illness, the day of return can be judged; if it is ill, he is uneasy abroad.
