## LY.TRAVEL.BSZZ.11 总断千金赋·出行最怕往亡，如系吉神终获利：往亡是凶煞，出行遇到它即使大象吉利也断凶是因为它主死，但它若临所喜之爻、发动生扶拱合世身用爻，就当利不当害——神煞的分量轻，五行的分量重

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:出行最怕往亡，但如果往亡落在吉神上，终究获利。往亡是凶煞，出行遇到它，即使整个卦象吉利，也断为凶，因为它主死；但如果它落在所喜的爻上、发动来生扶拱合世身和用爻，我一定认为有利而不认为有害。因为神煞的权重轻、五行的权重重。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Setting out most fears Wangwang, but if it sits on an auspicious spirit one gains in the end. Wangwang is a baleful star: when setting out meets it, even if the whole hexagram looks lucky it is judged ill, because it speaks of death; but if it sits on a welcome line and moves to give life to, support, embrace or combine with the Self, the Body and the Subject, I must take it as profit and not as harm, because the weight of the stars is light and the weight of the five elements is heavy.

**《卜筮正宗·总断千金赋》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1280 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "The Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 1280*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 出行最怕往亡， | 出行最怕往亡， | Setting out most fears Wangwang, |
| 如系吉神终获利。 | 如果是吉神终究获利。 | but if it is an auspicious spirit one gains in the end. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1282 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1282*

> **Note:** The first half of the couplet speaks of the White Tiger, the Dragon, the Dark Tortoise, the Vermilion Bird and the Tianxi star for illness; only the line on setting out is taken here. Wangwang is a baleful star of the old day-selection lore, held to make setting out unfavourable.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.05 出行·太岁克冲行止终年挠括：太岁发动冲克世爻，出外终年不利，再加白虎更非吉兆

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:太岁发动，冲克世爻，这个人出外一年到头不顺利；再加上白虎这样的凶煞，更不是吉兆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Tai Sui moves and clashes with or controls the Self, the man abroad has ill fortune the whole year; with the White Tiger, a baleful star, added, it is the more inauspicious.

**《黄金策·出行》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3433 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3433*

| --- | --- | --- |
| 太岁克冲， | 太岁冲克世爻， | If the Tai Sui clashes with or controls the Self, |
| 行止终年挠括 | 走动和停留一年到头都受阻碍 | going and staying are hampered all year |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3433 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 3433*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 太岁发动冲克世爻，其人出外终年不利，更加白虎凶煞，尤非吉兆也。 | 太岁发动冲克世爻，这个人出外一年到头不顺利；再加上白虎这样的凶煞，尤其不是吉兆。 | If the Tai Sui moves and clashes with or controls the Self, the man abroad has ill fortune the whole year; with the White Tiger, a baleful star, added, it is the more inauspicious. |

> **按**:「挠括」是受阻碍、被束缚的意思。《增删卜易》的千金赋一节讲太岁时说，太岁只在占官事、年运、乡会试、仕宦等几类事里才要紧，出行不在其内（LY.EXAM.ZS.59）；这里却把太岁克冲世爻当作出外终年不利的信号。
>
> **Note:** "Hampered" translates a word meaning being obstructed and bound. The Zengshan Buyi, in its section on the Thousand-Gold Rhapsody, says the Tai Sui matters only in a few kinds of divination — official affairs, the fortune of the year, the examinations, taking office — and travel is not among them (LY.EXAM.ZS.59); here the Tai Sui clashing with or controlling the Self is taken as a sign of ill fortune abroad all year.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.16 出行·妻来克世莫贪无义之财、财合变官勿恋有情之妇：财动克世恐因财致祸，世财相合而财变鬼克恐因色致殃
