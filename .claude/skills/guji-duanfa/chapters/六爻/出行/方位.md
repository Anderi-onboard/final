## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.12 出行·土如遇福偏宜陆地行程：卦中火土爻是陆路，水木爻是水路，临财福则吉、临兄鬼则凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦中火爻、土爻是陆路，水爻、木爻是水路；走哪条路，要看那条路上的爻：临着财爻、福神就吉，临着兄弟、官鬼就凶。土爻遇到福神，特别适宜走陆路。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In the hexagram the Fire and Earth lines are the land road and the Water and Wood lines the water road; which road to take depends on the lines on it: if they carry Wealth or Output it is lucky, if Peer or Pressure it is ill. Earth meeting Output is especially fit for going by land.

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.13 出行·鬼地墓乡岂宜践履、财方父向动可登临：官鬼所在方位和它的墓方、世的墓方、克世的方位是凶方不可去，宜去财福所在的方位

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:自己占出行，方位都从世爻所在的位置说：官鬼所在的方位，鬼爻的墓方、世爻的墓方，以及克世的方位，都是凶方，不可去踏足；应当去财爻、福神所在的方位，大吉利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In one's own divination for a journey the quarters are all spoken of from the position of the Self: the quarter of Pressure, the quarter of Pressure's tomb, the quarter of the Self's tomb and the quarter that controls the Self are ill quarters where one must not tread; it is fit to go to the quarter of Wealth and Output, which is greatly auspicious.

**《黄金策·出行》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3449 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3449*

| 财方父向动可登临 | 财爻所在的方向、父母所向的方位，可以动身去登临 | The direction of Wealth and the quarter faced by Resource: one may set out and climb there |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3449 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 3449*

## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.74 避乱·鬼逢冲散何须克制之乡、福遇空亡莫若生扶之地：子孙之方固吉；福静官动而卦内有冲散官爻者取冲散之方；子孙空伏衰静受制又无冲散者取生世合世之方

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙所在的方位固然吉，因为它能制鬼，发动就取它；如果福静官动，而卦里有冲散官爻的，就以冲散它的方位为吉；如果子孙空亡、伏藏、衰弱安静受制，而鬼爻又没有被冲散，宜取生世、合世的方位为吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The quarter of Output is of course lucky because it controls Pressure, and if it moves one takes it; if Output is still and Pressure moves, and something in the hexagram clashes the Pressure line apart, take the quarter that clashes it apart as lucky; if Output is Void, hidden, weak and still and controlled, and the Pressure line is not clashed apart, take the quarter that gives life to or combines with the Self as lucky.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.40 趋避章·书中例，占流年：寅木动爻临月建化进神克世，六七月木衰墓时防土木之厄，宜出外避；目下去宜往西，六七月去反宜往东；此人七月初八往东，二十八地震房塌，独他得免

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:克世的神动了，宜出外避；去的方向要看克世的神旺衰：它旺的时候去西，它衰的时候反而宜往东。书中例：寅月丁卯日占流年，得噬嗑卦变睽卦：寅木动爻临月建，化出进神克世爻，这时木盛、贪着自己的旺，不来克世；六、七月是木衰墓的时候，防土木之灾；问可不可以避，宜出外才能避；问往哪个方向吉，目下去的宜往西，六、七月去的宜往东，因为六七月木衰，反而宜东。这个人竟没有出门，拖到七月初七又做了凶梦，初八就往东去了，二十八日地震房塌，人口受伤，只有他幸免。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the controlling spirit moves, it is fit to go out to avoid it; the direction depends on that spirit's strength: while it is strong go west, while it is weak go east instead. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Dingmao day, a man divined the year ahead and got Shihe changing to Kui: the moving Yin-wood line sits on the Month and changes into an Advancing spirit that controls the Self; at this time the Wood is thriving and greedy for its own flourishing, so it does not come to control the Self, but in the sixth and seventh months, when Wood is weak and in the tomb, beware calamity from earth and timber. Asked whether it could be avoided, I said it could, only by going out; asked which direction was lucky, I said: if you go now, go west; if you go in the sixth or seventh month, go east, because in those months Wood is weak and east becomes the fit direction. The man did not leave and put it off until the seventh day of the seventh month, when he had an ill dream again; on the eighth he went east, and on the twenty-eighth there was an earthquake, the house fell and people were hurt, and he alone escaped.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.42 趋避章·避患于生世之方、趋吉于福神之地：避兵、避盗、避瘟、养病、避是非，都宜避到「生世」之方和「子孙」之方；子孙若在卦中发动，不论克世、生世、持世都吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:避祸要避到生助世爻的那个方向，求吉要去福神所在的地方。凡是避兵、避盗、避瘟疫、养病、避是非，都适宜避到「生世」的方向和「子孙」的方向才吉。子孙如果在卦中发动，不论克世、生世还是持世，都吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To avoid harm, go to the direction that gives life to the Self; to seek luck, go to the place of Output. In avoiding soldiers, avoiding thieves, avoiding plague, recuperating from illness and avoiding quarrels alike, it is lucky to avoid to the direction that gives life to the Self and the direction of Output. If Output moves in the hexagram, it is lucky whether it controls, gives life to or holds the Self.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.44 趋避章·书中例，同乡占避乱往哪个方向：众人以子孙属水、金动生之，主往北；作者断兄动化进神是破财之象，午火得令生世，往南避为吉；众人从南避，后来贼从北来放火烧村，所以生方为吉，子孙之方亦吉，要以旺衰分别

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:避乱要避到生世的方向和子孙的方向，还要按旺衰来分辨。书中例：巳月丙戌日同乡的人占避乱，得乾卦变大有卦：众人认为子孙属水，有金动来生它，北方为吉。我说：不对。兄爻动化出进神，是破财之象；午火当令，生助世爻，往南避才吉。众人信了我的话，都往南避。后来贼果然从北边来，放火把村子烧光，房屋稻谷都成了灰烬，这是申金兄爻发动破财的缘故。所以生世的方向吉，子孙的方向也吉；又要用旺衰来分别，火虽然是鬼的方向，生我又有什么妨碍。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In avoiding disorder, go to the direction that gives life to the Self and the direction of Output, and tell them apart by strength and weakness. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Bingxu day, men of the same village divined which way to flee the disorder and got Qian changing to Dayou. The company held that Output belongs to Water, and Metal moves to give it life, so the north is lucky. I said: no. The Peer line moves and changes into an Advancing spirit, which is a sign of losing wealth; Wu-fire is in command and gives life to the Self, so it is lucky to flee south. They believed me and all fled south. Afterwards the bandits did come from the north, set fire to the village and left, and the houses and the rice were all ashes, which was the loss of wealth from the moving Shen-metal Peer. So the direction that gives life to the Self is lucky, and the direction of Output is also lucky; and one must also distinguish by strength and weakness: though Fire is the direction of Pressure, what harm is there in its giving life to me.
