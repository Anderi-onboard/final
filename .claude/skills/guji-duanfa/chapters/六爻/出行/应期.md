## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.56 行人·欲决归期之远近须详主象之兴衰：断归期全在合待冲、冲待合、空待出旬、破待补合、绝待逢生、墓待冲开；远近兼看兴衰

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:断归期，要用这些方法推详：被合住的等冲、被冲的等合、空的等出旬、破的等补合、绝的等逢生、入墓的等冲开；远近还要兼看主象的兴衰。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To judge the return, work it out by these methods: a combined line waits for a clash, a clashed line waits for a combination, a Void line waits for the end of the ten-day period, a broken line waits for repair or combination, an extinct line waits for life, and a tombed line waits to be clashed open; whether it is far or near is also judged by the rise and fall of the principal image.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.12 舟行章·登舟问何日到：世爻发动，合日、值日而到；子孙持世或子孙他爻发动，值日而到；世化退及反吟卦，中途而返

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:上船问哪天到：世爻发动，在合的日子或值日那天到；子孙持世，或者子孙在别的爻发动，值日那天到；世爻化退神，以及反吟卦，中途返回。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Boarding and asking on what day one arrives: if the Self moves, one arrives on the combining day or the ruling day; if Output holds the Self, or Output moves in another line, one arrives on the ruling day; if the Self changes into a Retreating spirit, or the hexagram is Reversed Chant, one turns back on the way.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.21 行人章·用神三合则冲开之月日来、六冲则行人无定而不返：用爻静的冲动之日来，墓的开墓之期来

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神三合，在冲开三合的月日回来；卦遇六冲，行人去向不定，不会回来。用爻安静的，在冲动它的日子回来；用爻入墓的，在开墓的时候回来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Subject is in a three-harmony, he returns in a month or day that clashes the harmony open; when the hexagram meets six-clash, the traveller's course is unsettled and he does not return. A still Subject returns on the day that clashes it into motion; a Subject in the tomb returns at the time the tomb is opened.
