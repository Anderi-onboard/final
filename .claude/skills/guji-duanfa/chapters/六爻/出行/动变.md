## LY.TRAVEL.HJC.07 出行·八纯乱动到处皆凶：八纯卦就是六冲卦，六爻不和又乱动，没有吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:八纯卦就是六冲卦，六爻不和，再碰上乱动，到处都凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A pure-palace hexagram is a six-clash hexagram, its six lines at odds, and with disorderly movement besides, everywhere is ill.

**《黄金策·出行》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3437 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3437*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 八纯乱动到处皆凶 | 八纯卦六爻乱动，到处都凶 | In a pure-palace hexagram with the lines moving in disorder, everywhere is ill |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3437 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 3437*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 八纯乃六冲之卦，六爻不和又遇乱动，何吉之有？ | 八纯卦就是六冲卦，六爻不和，又碰上乱动，哪里还有吉？ | A pure-palace hexagram is a six-clash hexagram, its six lines at odds, and meeting disorderly movement besides, where is the luck? |

> **按**:「八纯」是八个纯卦（乾为天、坤为地、震为雷、巽为风、坎为水、离为火、艮为山、兑为泽），上下卦相同，都是六冲卦。
>
> **Note:** The "eight pure" hexagrams are the eight hexagrams doubled from one trigram (Qian, Kun, Zhen, Xun, Kan, Li, Gen, Dui), whose upper and lower trigrams are the same; all are six-clash hexagrams.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.03 出行章·八纯乱动在处皆凶、两间齐空独行则吉；六冲卦及动变冲、世爻休囚被克者终不能去，有始无终，去亦诸般不就

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:八纯卦乱动，到处都凶；两个间爻同时空，一个人走才吉。六冲卦，以及发动变成冲的、世爻休囚被克的，终究去不成，有始无终，去了也诸事不成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Reversed Chant, or changing into a Retreating spirit, means turning back on the road; six-clash, or entering the tomb with Pressure, is ill from start to finish. If the hexagram is Hidden Chant and the Self moves, one surely sets out in the month or on the day that clashes it open; if it is Reversed Chant, even on getting halfway one turns back; the Self meeting clash and control is greatly ill; in a six-clash hexagram with the Self still or Void it is judged that one cannot go; entering the tomb with Pressure, if the Self is resting or trapped, is the sign of not returning.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.19 行人章·用神动化进神不返、化退神必归，动逢合有事阻隔、动化鬼在外危灾：最忌动而化克，还防卦变反吟

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神发动化出进神，不回来；用神发动化出退神，一定回来。发动遇到合，有事阻隔在外；发动化出官鬼，在外有危险灾祸。最怕发动而化出克它的爻，还要提防卦变成反吟。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Subject that moves and changes into an Advancing spirit does not return; one that changes into a Retreating spirit surely returns. A moving Subject that meets a combination is held up by some affair; one that changes into Pressure meets danger and calamity abroad. Most to be feared is a movement that changes into a controlling line, and beware too of the hexagram changing into Reversed Chant.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 11998 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 11998*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 动化进神不返，用神化退必归； | 动化进神不返，用神化退神必定回来； | A moving Subject that changes into an Advancing spirit does not return, and one that changes into a Retreating spirit surely returns; |
| 动逢合，有事阻隔； | 发动遇到合，有事阻隔； | a moving Subject that meets a combination is held up by some affair; |
| 动化鬼，在外危灾。 | 发动化出官鬼，在外有危险灾祸。 | one that changes into Pressure meets danger and calamity abroad. |
| 最忌动而化克，还防卦变反吟。 | 最怕发动而化出克它的爻，还要提防卦变成反吟。 | Most to be feared is a movement that changes into a controlling line, and beware too of the hexagram changing into Reversed Chant. |

> **打架**:《黄金策》说用爻化进神，行人很快就回，化退神则来了又返回或又去别处（见黄金策·行人）；这里相反：化进神不回，化退神必回。两书对进退神的读法正好相反，没有哪一本交代理由。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The Huangjin Ce says that a Subject changing into an Advancing spirit means the traveller hurries home, and changing into a Retreating spirit means he comes and then goes back or goes elsewhere (see the Huangjin Ce on travellers); this chapter says the opposite, that an Advancing change does not return and a Retreating change surely returns. The two books read Advancing and Retreating in opposite ways, and neither gives its reason.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.28 行人章·书中例，占父何时来：初爻丑父与子合、五爻未父化进神，皆不来，须午年方来，果然午年戌月到

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神发动化进神，不回来；发动的遇合则被事绊住，逢合之年才来。书中例：辰年未月丁丑日占父亲什么时候来，得大有卦变井卦：初爻丑土父母与子水相合，不来；五爻未土父母化出进神，不来；必须到午年才来；果然午年戌月到。应在午年，是因为未爻的父母发动，午年合它，正是发动的爻逢合之年；又因为初爻子水与丑土相合，合住要冲开，午年冲开了它。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Subject that moves and changes into an Advancing spirit does not return; a moving one that meets a combination is held up, and comes in the year it meets the combination. Worked example: in a Chen year, in the Wei month, on a Dingchou day, a man divined when his father would come and got Dayou changing to Jing: the Resource Chou-earth in the first line is combined with Zi-water, so he does not come; the Resource Wei-earth in the fifth line changes into an Advancing spirit, so he does not come; he must come only in a Wu year; and he arrived in a Wu year, in the Xu month. It answered in the Wu year because the Wei-line Resource moves and the Wu year combines it, the year in which a moving line meets a combination; and also because Zi in the first line combines with Chou, and a combination must be clashed open, which the Wu year does.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 11998 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 11998*

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.29 行人章·书中例，占主人往楚平安否、迁往他省否：连占三卦，前两卦用神真空是凶亡之兆，报的是未年之死，第三卦申父生世才报眼前所问（四月必归）；野鹤：占远应近、占近应远，不能一卦竟断

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神入墓、绝、空、破，回来的消息杳无音信。书中例：寅月庚寅日仆人占主人往楚地平安与否、是否迁往别省，得观卦：父母遇真空，是不祥之兆，不敢直接断，再叫亲人占；同日占兄长，得中孚卦变临卦：前一卦仆人占主人，父母为用神，父母真空；这一卦弟弟占兄长，兄弟为用神，又落真空，是大凶之兆；再叫亲人占；次日辛卯日家人又占，得坎卦变困卦，作者说：得这一卦，现在可以替你放心了——前两卦用神真空是凶亡的兆头，这一卦申金父母生世爻，四月必归；问前面父、兄真空是为什么，是因为此公寿数无多，神灵报的是未年的死，没有说眼下的事；未年死的根据是占主人时父母临未土、占兄长时兄临未土，都被日月克害，因为在旬空所以暂时没有灾祸，未年出空，被日月伤，岂能不危险。辰年占的，果然未年死。野鹤说：连占两卦都报的是没有问的事，第三卦才报眼前所问，所以感叹前人用一卦竟断吉凶，不知道把「占远应近、占近应远」的卦错断了这么多。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Subject in the tomb, extinct, Void or broken gives no word of a return. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Gengyin day, a servant divined whether his master, gone to Chu, was safe and whether he would move to another province, and got Guan: the Resource met true Void, an ominous sign, and I did not dare judge outright, so the household was told to divine again; the same day, divining for the elder brother, they got Zhongfu changing to Lin: in the former hexagram the servant divined for the master with the Resource as the Subject, and the Resource was truly Void; in this one the younger brother divined for the elder with the Peer as the Subject, and it too fell into true Void, a sign of great ill; so they were told to divine again; the next day, a Xinmao day, the family divined once more and got Kan changing to Kun. I said: with this hexagram I can set your mind at ease. The Subject in the first two was truly Void, a sign of death; in this one the Resource Shen-metal gives life to the Self, and he will surely return in the fourth month. When asked why the Resource and the Peer were truly Void before, I said: because this gentleman has not long to live, the spirits report his death in a Wei year and do not speak of what is before us. When asked how I knew it was a Wei year, I said: in divining for the master the Resource sits on Wei-earth, and in divining for the brother the Peer sits on Wei-earth, both controlled and harmed by the Month and the Day; being in the Void they cause no harm for now, but in the Wei year they come out of Void and are injured by the Month and Day, and how could that not be dangerous? Divined in a Chen year, he did die in the Wei year. Yehe says: two hexagrams in a row reported what had not been asked, and only the third reported the present question; hence I sigh that the former worthies judged luck and ill-luck from a single hexagram, not knowing how many hexagrams of "far matter answered near, near matter answered far" they have misjudged.

## LY.TRAVEL.ZS.48 三合章·占出行用神在三合之内则被合而留，占行人用神在三合之内则被合不返

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占出行，用神在三合局之内，会被合住而留下来；占行人，用神在三合局之内，被合住不回来。同一章里，占功名三合官局生世利于我、生应利于他，占财财局生世利于我、生应利于他，也是同一个看法。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining for setting out, a Subject inside a three-harmony frame is combined shut and stays; in divining for a traveller, a Subject inside a three-harmony frame is combined shut and does not return. In the same chapter, in divining for office a Pressure frame giving life to the Self is good for me and giving life to the Other is good for him, and a Wealth frame in divining for wealth is read the same way.
