## LY.CAREER.ZS.19 在任吉凶·世临官而遇马，差遣烦劳（官星持世，明动暗动加临驿马必有差遣；日月动爻作财生扶，差中得利；兄爻发动日月动爻刑克世，辛苦赔财；盼差遣者世动即有差）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻临官星又遇驿马，会有差遣，烦劳辛苦：官星持世，明动、暗动又加临驿马的，必定有差遣；日月、动爻作财来生扶的，在差遣中能得到利益；卦中兄爻发动，日月、动爻刑克世爻的，辛苦还要赔钱；心里盼着得到差遣的人，不用等驿马加临，只要世爻发动就有差遣了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Self sits on the star of office and meets the Post Horse, there will be commissions and toil: if the star of office holds the Self and moves openly or in secret upon the Post Horse, there will surely be a commission; if the Day, Month or a moving line acts as Wealth and generates or supports it, one profits from the commission; if a Peer line moves in the hexagram and the Day, Month or a moving line punishes or controls the Self, one toils and loses money as well; for one who hopes in his heart for a commission, there is no need to wait for the Post Horse, for as soon as the Self moves there is a commission.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.27 署印谋差·财与官星不可有一而不旺，子孙兄弟不可有一而动摇；世陷财空不如守己，官空世破且让他人；衰世随鬼入墓、世动化克化鬼、子孙兄弟持世，自投罗网

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:谋署理印务、谋差事：财星和官星不能有一个不旺相，子孙和兄弟不能有一个发动摇动；世爻失陷、财落空，不如守住自己；官星落空、世爻被破，暂且让给别人；用钱去谋的，和援例一样推断。衰弱的世爻随官鬼入墓，世爻发动变克、变鬼，子孙或兄弟持世，这时去谋署印、营求差事，是自己投进罗网。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** On seeking to act in a seal-holding post or to scheme for a commission: of the star of Wealth and the star of office neither may fail to be thriving, and of Output and Peer neither may move or sway; if the Self is lost and Wealth is Void, it is better to keep to oneself; if the star of office is Void and the Self is broken, let others have it for now; for one who seeks it with money, judge as for buying office by precedent. If a weak Self follows Pressure into the tomb, or the Self moves and turns into a control or into Pressure, or Output or Peer holds the Self, then seeking to act in a seal-holding post or to scheme for a commission is walking into the net oneself.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.48 营造公务防患·福摇子动无忧，鬼克兄冲有患（子孙持世或动于卦中，事无忧虑有始有终；官鬼克世受累，兄克兄动赔财，鬼兄持世同推）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:修陵修河等营造公务防患：福神摇动、子孙发动没有忧虑，官鬼克、兄弟冲就有祸患；子孙持世或在卦中发动，事情没有忧虑，有始有终；官鬼克世会受累，兄弟克世、发动会赔钱，官鬼、兄弟持世的也同样推断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Guarding against trouble in works such as repairing tombs and rivers: with the lucky spirit swaying and Output moving there is nothing to worry about, and with Pressure controlling and Peer clashing there is trouble; if Output holds the Self or moves in the hexagram, the matter has nothing to worry about and has a beginning and an end; if Pressure controls the Self one is burdened, if Peer controls the Self or moves one loses money, and the same judgment holds when Pressure or Peer holds the Self.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.49 营造公务防患·父世官生，因公加爵（父母持世，官星动而生合世爻，必因公事加官）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母持世，官星发动生合世爻，必定因为办公事而加官。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Resource holds the Self and the star of office moves to generate or combine with the Self, one will surely gain office through public business.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.50 营造公务防患·财兴官旺，利获名荣（旺官持世财动相生，因公得利、远许名荣；旺财持世、世动化财生世同断）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财兴旺、官星旺相，既得利益又得荣名：旺相的官星持世，财发动来生它，不但因公事获利，还将得到名誉荣耀；旺相的财持世，或者世爻发动变出财来生世，也同样断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Wealth flourishing and the star of office thriving, one wins both gain and honour: if a thriving star of office holds the Self and Wealth moves to generate it, one not only gains from public business but will in due time win honour; the same judgment holds when a thriving Wealth line holds the Self, or the Self moves and turns into Wealth to generate the Self.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.52 营造公务防患·野鹤：初入工程防患宜子孙动，已见疏虞防患则子动伤官；觉子：初入工程兄动虑赔财，已见疏虞兄兴防罚俸

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占公务防患要分两个阶段：刚开始进入工程而防备祸患的，适合子孙动摇，子孙发动就没有忧虑；如果已经出现了疏漏失误而防备祸患的，必然是在为功名担忧，子孙发动反而伤官星，怎么能说子孙动就没有忧虑呢。觉子补充：刚开始进入工程，兄弟发动要担心赔钱；已经出现疏漏，兄弟兴旺要防备罚俸。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining public business and guarding against trouble one must tell two stages apart: for one who has just begun a works project and guards against trouble, Output swaying is good, for when Output moves there is nothing to worry about; but for one who has already seen a lapse and guards against trouble, he is surely anxious for his rank, and Output moving then injures the star of office, so how could it be said that when Output moves there is nothing to worry about? Juezi adds: at the start of a works project, when Peer moves one fears losing money; once a lapse has appeared, when Peer flourishes guard against a fine of salary.
