## LY.CAREER.EL.106 谋差顶役：以官爻为主、世象为凭；书辩捕兵民皂卒谋差顶役世爻详，各忌六冲官害世、皆宜财鬼旺为强；一应衙门生意顶役谋差若遇六冲决难成就纵成不久，官鬼若伤身世终遭罪责难逃，得财官两旺其中定见兴家

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:谋差使、顶替衙门的役，以官爻为主，以世爻为凭据。书办、捕快、兵、民壮、皂隶、差役这些人谋差顶役，要细看世爻；都忌六冲、官爻害世爻，都适宜财爻、官鬼旺相才强。凡是衙门里的差事、生意、顶役、谋差，如果遇到六冲，一定难以成就，即使成了也不长久；官鬼如果伤了身爻、世爻，终究遭到罪责，难以逃脱；得到财、官两旺在其中，一定见到兴家。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In seeking a commission or taking over a post in a yamen's service, take the Pressure line as the chief and the Self image as the ground. For clerks, scribes, constables, soldiers, militiamen, lictors and runners seeking a commission or taking over a service, examine the Self line in detail; all fear a six-clash and the star of office harming the Self, and all are fitly strong when Wealth and Pressure are thriving. For every job in a yamen, whether a livelihood, taking over a service or seeking a commission, if it meets a six-clash it will surely be hard to accomplish, and even if it is accomplished it will not last; if Pressure injures the Body or the Self, one will in the end be blamed and punished and cannot escape; if Wealth and the star of office are both thriving within it, one will surely see the household prosper.

## LY.CAREER.EL.107 谋差顶役：更愁身命随官墓、世坐空亡谁赞襄，无鬼莫来谋此役、官临身世却宜当；身世本命随官入墓祸不可当，世值空亡焉能久远，卦中无鬼所作不成纵成始终无益，若得官临身世本命又遇生扶宜充此役

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:更怕身、命随着官爻入墓，世爻坐空亡，谁来帮助？没有官鬼，就不要来谋这份役；官爻临在身爻、世爻上，却适宜担当。具体说：身爻、世爻、本命随着官爻入墓，祸就挡不住；世爻落在空亡，哪能长久？卦中没有官鬼，所做的不成，即使成了也始终无益；如果官爻临在身爻、世爻上，本命又遇到生扶，就适宜充任这份役。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Still more fear the Body and the fate following the star of office into the tomb, and with the Self sitting on Void who will assist? With no Pressure do not come to seek this service; with the star of office on the Body or the Self it is on the contrary fitting to take it up. In detail: if the Body, the Self and the native fate follow the star of office into the tomb, the calamity cannot be withstood; if the Self falls on Void, how can it last long? If the hexagram has no Pressure, what one does will not succeed, and even if it succeeds it is of no benefit from first to last; if the star of office rests on the Body or the Self and the native fate meets generation and support, it is fitting to fill this service.

## LY.CAREER.EL.108 谋差顶役：财空鬼弱兄爻动但得虚名利不昌、世不逢冲官不绝差成役就乐欢肠；财落空中官居衰地事虽见就必主无财，卦中纵有衰财或逢兄动亦不为祥，世象不冲不破官爻不绝不空便言谋中得成求财得利

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻落空、官鬼衰弱、兄爻发动，只得到虚名，利益不旺；世爻不遇冲、官爻不绝，差使办成、役位到手，心里欢喜。财爻落在空中、官爻居衰地，事情虽看似成了，一定没有钱财；卦中即使有衰弱的财爻，或者遇到兄动，也不吉祥；世爻不冲不破、官爻不绝不空，就可以说谋求能成、求财得利。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Wealth Void, Pressure weak and the Peer line moving, one gets only an empty name and profit does not flourish; if the Self does not meet a clash and the star of office is not extinct, the commission is won and the service is taken up, and the heart is glad. If Wealth falls into Void and the star of office sits in a weak place, then although the matter seems to be accomplished there will surely be no money; even if there is a weak Wealth line in the hexagram, or Peer moves, it is not auspicious; if the Self image is not clashed or broken and the Pressure line is not extinct or Void, one may say that the scheme will succeed and that seeking wealth will bring gain.

## LY.CAREER.EL.109 户役轻重：以官鬼为主、生克为凭；民当户役有轻重之不同，卦出官爻取旺衰之可验；官旺则役重、官衰则役轻，克世则厚而非薄、伤身则重而不轻，带青龙而吉、加白虎而凶；或空或绝皆主无忧，鬼纵休囚克世还当繁重，官虽旺相生世赔费还轻，值青龙终无责罚临白虎必犯官刑

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:百姓承当户役，有轻重的不同；卦里出现的官爻，看它的旺衰就可以验证。官爻居于旺相，役一定高大强劲；休囚，才细小；官克世爻，役厚而不薄；伤身爻，役重而不轻；带青龙是吉，加白虎是凶。要知道户役的轻重，必须看官爻的衰旺：旺就重大，衰就轻微；或者落空，或者入绝，都主没有忧虑；官鬼即使休囚，如果克世，还是繁重；官爻虽然旺相，但生世爻，赔费还轻；值青龙，终究没有责罚；临白虎，必定触犯官刑。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The people bear household services of lighter and heavier kinds, and the Pressure line that appears in the hexagram can be tested by its strength or weakness. If it is in a thriving place the service will certainly be high and strong; if it is resting or imprisoned it is small and fine; if it controls the Self the service is thick and not thin; if it hurts the Body it is heavy and not light; carrying the Azure Dragon it is called auspicious, and adding the White Tiger it is called ominous. To know whether the household service is light or heavy, look at the strength of the Pressure line: if thriving it is heavy and large, if weak it is light and slight; if Void or extinct, in either case there is nothing to worry about; even if Pressure is resting or imprisoned, if it controls the Self the burden will still be heavy; even if the star of office is thriving, if it generates the Self the cost of making up shortfalls is still light; on the Azure Dragon there is in the end no blame or punishment, and on the White Tiger one will surely run into official punishment.

'Xixiao' and 'gaoqiang' are the size and weight of the service.

## LY.CAREER.EL.110 户役轻重：月建临官应佥魁首、日辰克鬼当审轻微，官爻值世估高名、福德加身登下榜；官临月建至重之差，鬼值世爻次重之役，鬼逢日月克伤轻徭可必，子孙世或发动其户亦轻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:月建临在官爻上，应被派作魁首（最重的役）；日辰克官鬼，应审定为轻微。官爻值世，估定是高名（高等户，役重）；福德加身，登下榜（下等户，役轻）。具体说：官临月建，是最重的差；鬼值世爻，是次重的役；官鬼遇到日月克伤，役轻是可以断定的；子孙持世或者发动，这个户也轻。

In detail: the star of office on the Month is the heaviest commission; Pressure on the Self line is the next heaviest service; if Pressure meets the control and injury of the Day and the Month, lightness can be affirmed; if Output holds the Self or moves, the household too is light.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.50 营造公务防患·财兴官旺，利获名荣（旺官持世财动相生，因公得利、远许名荣；旺财持世、世动化财生世同断）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财兴旺、官星旺相，既得利益又得荣名：旺相的官星持世，财发动来生它，不但因公事获利，还将得到名誉荣耀；旺相的财持世，或者世爻发动变出财来生世，也同样断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Wealth flourishing and the star of office thriving, one wins both gain and honour: if a thriving star of office holds the Self and Wealth moves to generate it, one not only gains from public business but will in due time win honour; the same judgment holds when a thriving Wealth line holds the Self, or the Self moves and turns into Wealth to generate the Self.

| 财兴官旺，利获名荣。 | 财兴旺、官星旺相，获得利益和名誉。 | With Wealth flourishing and the star of office thriving, one wins both gain and honour. |

## LY.CAREER.ZS.52 营造公务防患·野鹤：初入工程防患宜子孙动，已见疏虞防患则子动伤官；觉子：初入工程兄动虑赔财，已见疏虞兄兴防罚俸

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占公务防患要分两个阶段：刚开始进入工程而防备祸患的，适合子孙动摇，子孙发动就没有忧虑；如果已经出现了疏漏失误而防备祸患的，必然是在为功名担忧，子孙发动反而伤官星，怎么能说子孙动就没有忧虑呢。觉子补充：刚开始进入工程，兄弟发动要担心赔钱；已经出现疏漏，兄弟兴旺要防备罚俸。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining public business and guarding against trouble one must tell two stages apart: for one who has just begun a works project and guards against trouble, Output swaying is good, for when Output moves there is nothing to worry about; but for one who has already seen a lapse and guards against trouble, he is surely anxious for his rank, and Output moving then injures the star of office, so how could it be said that when Output moves there is nothing to worry about? Juezi adds: at the start of a works project, when Peer moves one fears losing money; once a lapse has appeared, when Peer flourishes guard against a fine of salary.
