## LY.CAREER.HJC.26 仕宦·奏陈谏诤，那堪太岁刑冲（旧注：奏对陈疏上章谏诤赴召面君皆忌动爻冲克太岁亦忌刑克世爻；太岁月建生合世爻必见详允，冲克须防不测之祸；岁君衰静不带刑害虎蛇不见取用非大害；动空化空虚惊；有制伏冲散合住必得大臣申救；应动冲克世更防人奏劾）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:上奏陈情、进呈奏章、上书、进谏，以及奉召面君这类事，都忌讳动爻冲克太岁，也忌讳世爻被刑克；如果太岁、月建生合世爻，必定得到详细审议并获准；一旦遇到冲克，须提防意外的祸患；太岁衰弱安静又不带刑、害、白虎、螣蛇，只是不被采用，没有大害；动爻空亡或化空，也只是虚惊；有制伏、冲散、合住的，必有大臣出来申救；应爻发动冲克世爻，更要提防有人上奏弹劾。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For anything such as a memorial in audience, presenting a petition, submitting a written report, remonstrating, or going to an audience with the sovereign at his summons, it is feared that a moving line clashes or controls the Tai Sui, and also that the Self is punished or controlled; if the Tai Sui or the Month generates or combines with the Self, it will surely be examined in detail and approved; once a clash or control comes, beware of an unforeseen misfortune; if the Tai Sui is weak and still and carries no punishment, harm, White Tiger or Coiling Snake, one is only not adopted and there is no great harm; if the moving line is Void or turns into Void, it is only a false alarm; if there is something that subdues, clashes away or combines it, a high minister will surely come forward to plead and rescue; and if the Other moves and clashes or controls the Self, beware all the more of someone submitting an impeachment.

> **按**:「奏对」是面奏回答皇帝，「陈疏」「上章」是上书，「谏诤」是进言规劝，「赴召面君」是奉召去见皇帝，「申救」是出面陈说、营救，「奏劾」是上奏弹劾。旧注「亦忌刑克世爻」没有说谁刑克世爻，王洪绪补明是「太岁刑克世爻」。旧注比王洪绪多出的几句（太岁衰静不带刑害虎蛇只是不被采用、动空化空是虚惊、有制伏冲散合住有大臣申救、应动冲克世防人弹劾），是在「冲克」这个凶象上分轻重：太岁衰静无煞只是不被采纳，有煞才是祸；动爻空或化空是虚惊；有人制住冲克的爻，就有大臣出来救。这一联是「面君」类事的判据，所以放在「国朝」一题下。
>
> **Note:** 'Audience' is replying to the emperor in person, 'a petition' and 'a written report' are writing to the throne, 'remonstrating' is offering advice and admonition, 'going to an audience at his summons' is being summoned to see the emperor, 'plead and rescue' is coming forward to speak and save, and 'impeachment' is submitting a memorial of impeachment. The old commentary's 'it is also feared that the Self is punished or controlled' does not say by whom, and Wang Hongxu makes it clear that it is 'the Tai Sui punishing or controlling the Self'. The extra sentences of the old commentary (the Tai Sui weak and still with no punishment, harm, Tiger or Snake is only not being adopted; a moving line Void or turning into Void is a false alarm; with something that subdues, clashes away or combines it, a minister pleads and rescues; the Other moving and clashing the Self, beware impeachment) grade the ominous sign of clash or control by weight: the Tai Sui weak and still without killers is only not being adopted, and only with killers is there misfortune; the moving line Void or turned Void is a false alarm; and when something holds in check the line that clashes or controls, a minister comes out to rescue. This couplet is the criterion for matters of facing the sovereign, hence it is placed under 'the court and the state'.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.63 国朝·君恕则臣忠，共济明良之会；国泰则民乐，当推祸福之原；国之治乱兴衰，卦理推详剖决（总论）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占国家的兴衰，是把整个卦当作国运来读。国家有始有终，必有兴有废；国君宽厚，臣子就忠心；国家太平，百姓就安乐；这些祸福的来由，都可以用卦理推到根上、剖开判决。哪一爻代表什么，见下一条。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A question about the fortunes of a state reads the whole hexagram as the fortune of the realm. A state has a beginning and an end and must rise and fall; when the ruler is forgiving the ministers are loyal, and when the state is at peace the people are happy; the source of such blessings and disasters can be traced and decided by the principles of the hexagram. Which line stands for what is in the next rule.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.64 国朝·本宫旺相，周文王创八百年之基；大象休囚，秦始皇遗二世主之祸（旧注：本宫为国爻，太岁为君、岁合为后、月建为臣、日建为东宫、子孙为黎庶、父爻为国；本宫旺相国祚长，休囚而大象又凶则速亡）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占国运，先分派各爻：本宫是国爻，太岁是君，和太岁相合的是后，月建是臣，日建是东宫（太子），子孙是百姓，父爻是国家。开国时占得本宫旺相，国运绵长，像周文王那样子孙享国八百年；本宫休囚、整个卦象又凶，国运短促，像秦始皇那样到二世就亡国。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask about the fortune of a state, first assign the lines: the home palace is the line of the state, Tai Sui is the ruler, the branch combining with Tai Sui is the empress, the Month is the minister, the Day is the Eastern Palace (the crown prince), Output is the common people and Resource is the realm. If a state is being founded and the home palace comes out thriving or strong, its fortune is long, as with King Wen of Zhou, whose descendants held the realm for eight hundred years; if the home palace is resting or trapped and the whole image is also bad, its fortune is short, as with the First Emperor of Qin, whose state was lost with the second reign.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.65 国朝·九五逢阳，当遇仁明之主；四爻值福，必多忠义之臣（旧注：五爻为君位，逢阳象又遇青龙财福是仁明之君；四爻为臣位，子孙旺相克世是敢谏之臣，兄鬼生合世爻是阿谀佞臣）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:第五爻是君位：逢到阳象，又遇到青龙、妻财、子孙，是仁慈英明的君主；世爻临阳象、带吉神也一样。第四爻是臣位：子孙旺相、克世爻，是敢于直言进谏的臣子；兄弟、官鬼生合世爻，是阿谀奉承的佞臣。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The fifth line is the ruler's seat: if it meets a yang image and also meets the Azure Dragon, Wealth and Output, the ruler is benevolent and enlightened, and so it is if the Self sits in a yang image with auspicious spirits.
