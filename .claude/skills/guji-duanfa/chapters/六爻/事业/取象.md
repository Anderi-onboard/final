## LY.CAREER.EL.141 平生学艺：金银铜钱之工值乾兑酉申而最吉，竹木芦藤之匠得震巽寅卯以为佳，造贩鱼盐酒醋利入坎宫，裁成袖绢绫罗喜临离象，采石樵山须见艮、土工泥作要逢坤

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:做金、银、铜、钱的工匠，遇到乾、兑、酉、申最吉；做竹、木、芦、藤的匠人，得到震、巽、寅、卯为好；制造、贩卖鱼、盐、酒、醋，利益进入坎宫；裁制衣袖、绢帛、绫罗的，喜欢遇到离象；采石、上山砍柴，须见艮；做土工、泥作，要逢坤。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For metalworkers in gold, silver, copper and coin, meeting Qian, Dui, You and Shen is the most auspicious; for craftsmen in bamboo, wood, reed and rattan, getting Zhen, Xun, Yin and Mao is good; in making and selling fish, salt, wine and vinegar, profit enters the Kan palace; for cutting garments and silks, one is glad to meet the Li image; for quarrying stone and cutting wood on the mountain one must see Gen; for earthwork and plastering one must meet Kun.

**《易林补遗·平生学艺章第二十三》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 680 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 23: Learning a craft or art for one's whole life", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 680*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 金银铜钱之工，值乾兑酉申而最吉。 | 做金、银、铜、钱的工匠，遇到乾、兑、酉、申最吉； | For metalworkers in gold, silver, copper and coin, meeting Qian, Dui, You and Shen is the most auspicious; |
| 竹木芦藤之匠，得震巽寅卯以为佳。 | 做竹、木、芦、藤的匠人，得到震、巽、寅、卯为好； | for craftsmen in bamboo, wood, reed and rattan, getting Zhen, Xun, Yin and Mao is good; |
| 造败鱼盐酒醋，利入坎宫。 | 制造、贩卖鱼、盐、酒、醋，利益进入坎宫； | in making and selling fish, salt, wine and vinegar, profit enters the Kan palace; |
| 裁成袖绢绫罗，喜临离象， | 裁制衣袖、绢帛、绫罗的，喜欢遇到离象； | for cutting garments and silks, one is glad to meet the Li image; |

> **按**:这一联把行业对到卦宫：金属工匠对乾、兑和酉、申，竹木匠对震、巽和寅、卯，鱼盐酒醋对坎，裁缝绫罗对离，采石樵山对艮，土工泥作对坤。书里没有说缘故：金、木、水、土几项是按五行对的，裁缝对离书里没有解释。「造败鱼盐酒醋」的「败」疑是「贩」，译作「制造贩卖」；「樵山」是上山砍柴。这一联后面没有解说。
>
> **Note:** This couplet matches trades to the palaces: metalworkers to Qian and Dui and You and Shen, bamboo and wood craftsmen to Zhen and Xun and Yin and Mao, fish, salt, wine and vinegar to Kan, tailoring and silks to Li, quarrying and woodcutting to Gen, earthwork and plastering to Kun. The book does not give the reason: the metal, wood, water and earth items go by the five elements, and the book does not explain tailoring going with Li. The 'bai' of 'zao bai yu yan jiu cu' is presumably 'fan', to trade, and it is translated 'making and selling'; 'qiaoshan' is going up the mountain to cut wood. No explanation follows this couplet.

## LY.CAREER.EL.143 平生学艺：凡习经书父须生旺、若求官职鬼莫空无、祝巫大利官兴僧道惟宜子旺；习儒业者父母为用，求功名者官鬼为用，祝巫迎神召将照马关亡之类亦用官爻，僧道之流子孙为用，凡用爻皆宜旺相各忌空亡；诸般道术一应生涯皆把财为衣禄养命之源兼推子乃福神发家之主，诸般艺业皆宜财神兴隆财若空亡利资必绝望

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:学经书，父爻必须生旺；求官职，官鬼不可空无；祝巫大利官爻兴旺，僧道只适宜子孙旺。学儒业的，父母爻是用爻；求功名的，官鬼是用爻；祝巫一类，迎神召将、照马、关亡之类，也用官爻；只有僧道一流，以子孙为用。凡是用爻都适宜旺相，各自忌空亡：旺就事事有成，空就样样不成。各种道术、一切生计，都把财当作衣食俸禄、养命的源头，并且要推子孙是福神、发家的主；各种艺业，都适宜财神（财爻）兴隆；财如果空亡，利润资财一定没有指望了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In studying the classics, Resource must be thriving; in seeking office, Pressure must not be empty; for diviner-priests and shamans it is greatly favourable for the star of office to flourish, and for monks and Daoists only Output thriving is fitting. For the scholarly calling, Resource is the Subject line; for those seeking fame and office, Pressure is the Subject; for shamans, those who greet gods and summon generals, 'look at the horse' and 'close the gate on the dead' and the like, the Pressure line is also used; only for the monks and Daoists is Output the Subject. Every Subject line should be thriving and each fears Void: if thriving everything succeeds, and if Void nothing is accomplished. For every kind of Daoist art and every livelihood, Wealth is taken as the source of food, clothing, pay and the sustaining of life, and Output must also be reckoned as the fortune spirit and the master of making a household prosper; in every kind of art and trade it is fitting for Wealth to flourish, and if Wealth is Void, profit and funds are surely without hope.

>
> **Note:** 'Zhuwu' is the callings of invocation and healing by charms and of shamanism; 'zhaoma' and 'guanwang' are two kinds of rite of the folk religion, and the book does not go into them. 'Ruye' is the calling of studying for examinations. This entry looks at what Subject each calling takes: the scholarly calling takes Resource, fame and office take Pressure, shamans take the Pressure line, monks and Daoists take Output, and every other livelihood takes Wealth. That monks and Daoists take Output is the same method as 'for monk, Daoist and medical officials, how could the document move' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.27) and the Zengshan Buyi on monk-officials and medical officials (ZS.53).

## LY.CAREER.HJC.19 仕宦·官旺父衰，职任高而衙门冷落（旧注：父母旺相衙门大、休囚衙门小；官旺父衰是冷落闲静衙门；官衰父旺职卑而在大衙门；官父俱衰职卑衙小）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母旺相，衙门一定大；休囚，衙门一定小。官鬼旺而父母衰，不是小去处，而是冷落清闲的衙门，因为官旺所以职位高；官鬼衰而父母旺，职位虽然卑微，却在大衙门里办事；官和父都衰，职位低、衙门小，必定不是风宪之地。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Resource is thriving, the office is surely large; if resting or trapped, the office is surely small. With Pressure thriving and Resource weak, it is not a small place but a cold and quiet office, the post being high because Pressure is thriving; with Pressure weak and Resource thriving, the post, though lowly, is in a large office; with both Pressure and Resource weak, the post is low and the office small, and it is surely not a place in the censorate.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.20 仕宦·职居风宪，皆因月值官爻（旧注：官鬼旺相不临月建定非风宪；临月建又扶出世爻决是风宪之任必非府县官；带刑爻多是镇守边陲、掌兵权或居刑部；在外是司刑之职）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼旺相而不临月建，一定不是风宪的职位；如果临月建，又得到扶助而出现在世爻上，一定是风宪的任命，必不是府县的官，再在日辰的生旺之地，更是风宪；如果带刑爻，多是镇守边陲的职位，或者掌握兵权，或者在刑部，在外地也是主管刑狱的职位。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure is thriving but does not rest on the Month, it is surely not a post in the censorate; if it rests on the Month and is supported so as to appear on the Self, it is surely an appointment in the censorate and not an officer of a prefecture or county, all the more if it also stands in a thriving place of the Day; if it carries a punishment line, it is mostly a post guarding the frontier, or holding military power, or in the Board of Punishments, and outside the capital a post in charge of punishments.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7724 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7724*

| 若临月建，又得扶出世爻，决是风宪之任，必非州县之官。 | 如果临月建，又得到扶助而出现在世爻上，一定是风宪的任命，必不是州县的官； | if it rests on the Month and is supported so as to appear on the Self, it is surely an appointment in the censorate and not an officer of a prefecture or county; |

> **按**:「风宪」是监察、执法的官署（御史、按察使一类），「刑部」是掌管刑狱的部，「镇守边陲」是驻守边疆。旧注写「府县官」，王洪绪写「州县之官」，是同一类的地方官。王洪绪把旧注的「带刑爻」改成「带白虎刑爻」，并且只说「镇守边陲，职掌兵权」，没有旧注那两个出路（刑部、在外司刑）。这一联和 LY.CAREER.HJC.07 里「月建扶出官爻世爻者必是风宪之职」是同一个判据，在那里是顺带提到，这里是专门讲职位的类别。
>
> **Note:** 'The censorate' is the office that supervises and enforces the law (censors and surveillance commissioners), 'the Board of Punishments' is the ministry in charge of punishments, and 'guarding the frontier' is being stationed on the border. The old commentary writes 'an officer of a prefecture or county' and Wang Hongxu 'an officer of a department or county', local officers of the same kind. Wang Hongxu changes the old commentary's 'a punishment line' into 'the White Tiger or a punishment line' and says only 'guarding the frontier and holding military power', without the old commentary's other two outcomes (the Board of Punishments, and punishments outside). The criterion 'if the Month supports Pressure or the Self, it is surely a post in the censorate' is also in LY.CAREER.HJC.07, where it comes up in passing, and here the couplet speaks directly of what kind of post it is.

## LY.CAREER.YM.49 概占当得何官：专察官爻，以八卦别其属之部曹——乾天官（冢宰）管禁内杂职厩马、坎冬官（司空）管河漕台谏水利、艮阍寺管晨门防卫狱禁矿藏宝库钱局、震春官（宗伯）管东宫师傅试务宗庙礼乐文章、巽天使管上林与奉命四方之上差、离夏官（司马）管耳目纠弹修史、坤地官（司徒）管农事疆场社稷母后内臣、兑秋官（司寇）管言语刑罚章疏上膳

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To divine in general what office one will get, look only at the Pressure line, and tell by the eight trigrams the department it belongs to: Qian is the Heavenly Office (anciently called the Grand Steward), governing miscellaneous posts within the forbidden palace and the stable horses; Kan is the Winter Office (anciently the Minister of Works), governing the affairs of the river-conservancy and grain-transport directorates and the censors and remonstrators, officials of the waterworks of the realm; Gen is the palace gatekeeper, governing the officers of the dawn gate, the officials of defence and guard, the management of prisons, and things like mines, treasuries and mints; Zhen is the Spring Office (anciently the Elder of the Ancestral Temple), the tutors of the Crown Prince's Eastern Palace, or chief examiners and chief compilers, and the officials who manage the ancestral temples, sacrifices, rites, music and letters; Xun is the Heavenly Envoy, and those who manage the Shanglin park and the assistants of senior envoys sent with orders to the four quarters; Li is the Summer Office (anciently the Marshal), governing the power of ears and eyes and the work of impeachment, or also the supporting ministers of the Duan Gate and the civilised office of compiling histories; Kun is the Earth Office (anciently the Minister of the Multitude), governing the farming of the realm, officials with land, who carry the charge of the borders and the altars of the state, and the inner servants of the Empress Dowager; Dui is the Autumn Office (anciently the Minister of Crime), governing speech, in charge of punishments, passing memorials, and having the charge of the sovereign's meals.

## LY.CAREER.YM.50 概占何官（六爻位）：以六爻辨位之高卑——官在初爻为散缺（或本乡官守）、二爻为县邑（或谏议大臣相国学士）、三爻为守牧（或三司首领及大衙门随从）、四爻为司道（或九卿六曹近君之臣）、五爻君象惟代天巡狩与钦差者当之、六爻为贵戚之乡及宗庙功勋高尚辅相之臣；武途封职其义相类

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用六爻来分辨官位的高低：官爻在初爻，是散缺（闲散的缺额），或是本乡的官守；在二爻，是县邑，或是谏议大臣、相国学士；在三爻，是守牧（知府、知州一类），或是三司的首领，以及大衙门里随从的官；在四爻，是司道，或是九卿六曹，以及近君的臣子；五爻是君象，不是臣子所居，只有代天巡狩的官和奉钦命的钦差才当得起；六爻是贵戚之乡，以及宗庙功勋之臣、高尚之士、辅相王府的官、四围的股肱。武途的封职，意义相类似。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** By the six lines one tells the height or lowness of the rank: the Pressure line in the first line is a scattered vacancy (an idle post), or an official guard of his home district; in the second line, a county or town, or a remonstrating minister, a chief minister or academician; in the third line, a prefect or governor, or the head of the Three Offices, and officials attending in a great yamen; in the fourth line, a commissioner or circuit intendant, or the Nine Ministers and the Six Boards, and ministers near the sovereign; the fifth is the sovereign's image and not where a subject dwells, and only an official who makes tours of inspection in Heaven's stead or one sent by imperial command can fill it; the sixth line is the home of the noble kin, and of ministers of merit to the ancestral temple, men of lofty virtue, officials who assist a prince's household, and the limbs and trunk on all four sides. For appointments on the military path the meaning is similar.

## LY.CAREER.YM.51 概占何官（五行职掌）：以五行分其官之职掌——金官司兵事或执刑衡、木官司工务礼乐苑囿车舆舟楫及桑麻制造、水官司江淮河海及水利盐务税饷漕储、火官司文章参劾祭祀及冶铸喉舌之权、土官司土地农田社稷边疆及陵寝屯守之柄

## LY.CAREER.YM.53 概占何官（总括）：日月象之、世应度之、君臣位之；官爻独发论宫、一位论爻位、两现求所属、俱无考伏与变（伏占飞神、变占八宫）；官临日月建非社稷之臣即直节科道、持世为正印临应为左右、五爻为君二爻为大臣；虽代有更名理不远

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:日、月来象征它，世、应来度量它，君、臣来定它的位置。所以官爻独发的论宫，官爻只有一位的论爻位，官爻两现的求它所属，都没有的就考伏和变：伏的占飞神，变的占八宫。伏、变的办法也可以分出位置和所属。要知道当世的官守，用象去求，即使各代名称有更改，道理相差不远。官临日辰月建，不是社稷之臣，一定是直节的科道官；官爻持世是正印，临应是左右辅佐之职；五爻是君，不是御史就是当宠幸之臣；二爻是大臣，是宰相，是国家的仪表。卦里没有官鬼，就求伏下的飞神，用位和所属来分辨；卦有动象，就求所变的官爻，用八宫来区别。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Day and Month image it, the Self and the Other measure it, ruler and minister fix its place. To know the offices of the present age, one seeks them by the image, and though the names have been changed from age to age, the principle is not far off. When Pressure sits on the Day or Month branch, if he is not a minister of the altars of the state, he is surely an upright censor or supervising official; the Pressure line on the Self is the principal seal, and on the Other it is a post at the side, an assistant; the fifth line is the sovereign, and if not a censor he is a minister favoured by the throne; the second line is a great minister, a chief minister, the model of the state. If there is no Pressure line in the hexagram, one seeks the flying line above the hidden one and tells it by position and department; if there is a moving image, one seeks the Pressure line it changes into and tells it by the eight palaces.

## LY.CAREER.YY.03 官禄占·何官（一）：四直贵马聚身世更德合相扶在官印阳爻者宰辅，加刑刃大杀出将入相；太岁五爻生合世身朝仕，冲克外任；内卦动外任外卦动朝仕；阳宫阳爻文职阴宫阴爻武弁；世持岁破朝仕六部、外任布按都司；持月建巡方安抚，持日建郡邑之宰

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To tell what office it is: if the four rulers, the Noble and the Horse gather on the Body and Self lines, and Virtue and combination also support them, and they sit on a yang line of the star of office and seal, it is surely the post of chief minister; with the punishing blade and great baleful spirits added, one goes out as a general and comes in as chancellor; with Tai Sui or the fifth line generating or combining, he is surely honoured with a gift of banquet. If the star of office and the Noble hold the Self in a yang palace and on a yang line it is a civil post, and in a yin palace and on a yin line a military officer. If the Self holds the Year-break, at court it is in the great offices such as the Six Ministries, and outside it is in the provincial administration, judicial or military commissions; holding the Month it is an inspecting commissioner, a pacification commissioner or an envoy; holding the Day it is the head of a prefecture or county.

## LY.CAREER.YY.25 官禄占·贪廉（一）：官印皆旺财爻不见者廉；世持兄旺动者贪；世下伏兄化兄者贪

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:为官贪还是廉：官星、印星都旺相，财爻不出现的，清廉；世爻持兄爻、旺相发动的，贪；世爻下伏着兄爻、又变成兄爻的，贪。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Greed or integrity in office. If the star of office and the seal are both thriving and the Wealth line does not appear, he is incorruptible; if the Self holds a Peer line that is thriving and moving, he is greedy; if a Peer line lies hidden under the Self and turns into a Peer line, he is greedy.

## LY.CAREER.YY.26 官禄占·贪廉（二）：世持虎鬼加刑刃劫杀动者酷；世持鬼加玄武咸池荒芜四废杀动者罢软；世持虎鬼加雷火卒暴杀动者浮躁；世加天解天赦喝散动者昭雪滞狱

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:为官的品性：世爻持白虎、官鬼，再加刑、刃、劫杀发动的，酷；世爻持官鬼，再加玄武、咸池、荒芜、四废杀发动的，疲软无能；世爻持白虎、官鬼，再加雷火、卒暴杀发动的，浮躁；世爻带天解、天赦、喝散发动的，能为积压的冤狱昭雪。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The character of the official. If the Self holds the Tiger and Pressure and the punishing, blade and Robbery spirits are added and move, he is cruel; if the Self holds Pressure and the Dark Tortoise, Xianchi, Huangwu and the Four Wastes baleful spirits are added and move, he is feeble and soft; if the Self holds the Tiger and Pressure and the Thunder-Fire and Sudden-Violence baleful spirits are added and move, he is rash and restless; if the Self carries Tianjie, Tianshe and Hesan and they move, he clears and redresses cases long held up in prison.

**《易隐·官禄占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2652–2654 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining office and salary", base text; Yi Yin, lines 2652–2654*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 世持虎鬼，加刑刃劫杀动者，酷也。 | 世爻持白虎、官鬼，再加刑、刃、劫杀发动的，酷。 | If the Self holds the Tiger and Pressure and the punishing, blade and Robbery spirits are added and move, he is cruel. |
| 世持鬼，加玄武咸池荒芜四废杀动者，罢软也。 | 世爻持官鬼，再加玄武、咸池、荒芜、四废杀发动的，疲软无能。 | If the Self holds Pressure and the Dark Tortoise, Xianchi, Huangwu and the Four Wastes baleful spirits are added and move, he is feeble and soft. |
| 世持虎鬼，加雷火卒暴杀动者，浮躁也。 | 世爻持白虎、官鬼，再加雷火、卒暴杀发动的，浮躁。 | If the Self holds the Tiger and Pressure and the Thunder-Fire and Sudden-Violence baleful spirits are added and move, he is rash and restless. |
| 世加天解天赦喝散动者，昭雪滞狱也。 | 世爻带天解、天赦、喝散发动的，能为积压的冤狱昭雪。 | If the Self carries Tianjie, Tianshe and Hesan and they move, he clears and redresses cases long held up in prison. |

> **按**:第一句「刑刃劫杀」分在两行里，中间被断行隔开（「加刑刃」在上一行末，「劫杀动者」在下一行首），这里连起来读。「罢软」的「罢」读作疲。天解、天赦、喝散、荒芜、四废、卒暴、咸池等都是神煞，书里没有逐个解释，译文只音译或按字面译。「滞狱」是拖着没有判决的案子。
>
> **Note:** In the first sentence 'xing ren jie sha' is split across two lines, 'adding the punishment and blade' at the end of the earlier line and 'Robbery moving' at the start of the next, and is read here joined up. In 'ruan' of 'baruan', the character 'ba' is read as 'pi', weary. Tianjie, Tianshe, Hesan, Huangwu, the Four Wastes, Cubao, Xianchi and the others are spirit-stars that the book does not explain one by one, and the translation only gives their sounds or translates them by the letter. 'Zhiyu' is a case dragging on without judgment.

## LY.CAREER.YY.27 官禄占·贪廉（三）：财空鬼旺廉名振而囊槖空；父空官旺爵位高而琴鹤随；父母为行李；初爻为吏民，初爻克官克世主贤明，世官克初主苛刻，生初主慈爱

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:清名与属吏：财爻落空、官鬼旺相的，清廉的名声很响，而口袋是空的；父爻落空、官星旺相的，爵位高，身边只有琴和鹤相随；父母爻是行李。初爻代表属吏和百姓：初爻克官星、克世爻的，主这个官贤明，属吏百姓亲近他；世爻、官爻克初爻的，主这个官苛刻，属吏百姓畏惧躲避他；生初爻的，主这个官慈爱属吏百姓。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A clean name, and the clerks and people. If Wealth is Void and Pressure thriving, his name for incorruptibility rings out while his purse is empty; if the seal line is Void and the star of office thriving, his rank is high while only the zither and the crane follow him; the Resource line is the baggage. The first line stands for clerks and people: if the first line controls the star of office or the Self, he is wise and enlightened and the clerks and people are close to him; if the Self line or the Pressure line controls the first line, he is harsh and the clerks and people fear and avoid him; if it generates the first line, he is kind and loving towards them.

**《易隐·官禄占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2654 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining office and salary", base text; Yi Yin, line 2654*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 财空鬼旺者，廉名振而囊槖空也。 | 财爻落空、官鬼旺相的，清廉的名声很响，而口袋是空的。 | If Wealth is Void and Pressure is thriving, his name for incorruptibility rings out while his purse is empty. |
| 父空官旺者，爵位高而琴鹤随也。 | 父爻落空、官星旺相的，爵位高，而身边只有琴和鹤相随。 | If the seal line is Void and the star of office thriving, his rank is high while only the zither and the crane follow him. |
| (父母为行李)。 | （父母爻是行李。） | (The Resource line stands for baggage.) |
| 又初爻为吏民，初爻克官克世者，主贤明，吏民亲迩也。 | 又，初爻代表属吏和百姓；初爻克官星、克世爻的，主这个官贤明，属吏和百姓亲近他。 | Again, the first line stands for clerks and people; if the first line controls the star of office or the Self, it means he is wise and enlightened, and the clerks and people are close to him. |
| 世爻官爻克初者，主苛刻，吏民畏避。 | 世爻、官爻克初爻的，主这个官苛刻，属吏和百姓畏惧躲避他。 | If the Self line or the Pressure line controls the first line, he is harsh, and the clerks and people fear and avoid him. |
| 生初爻者，主慈爱吏民也。 | 生初爻的，主这个官慈爱属吏和百姓。 | If it generates the first line, he is kind and loving towards the clerks and people. |

> **按**:「琴鹤」用的是宋代赵抃只带一张琴、一只鹤去赴任的典故，比喻做官清廉；「囊槖」是口袋。「父空官旺」的父单字，按上面的用法译成印星；括号里的「父母为行李」是说父母爻代表行李（见 YY.13 赴任的分爻）。最后一句的主语省了，承上一句，是世爻、官爻。
>
> **Note:** 'Qin he' is the allusion to Zhao Bian of the Song, who went to his post taking only one zither and one crane, an image of an incorruptible official; 'nangtuo' is a bag. The single character 'father' in 'father Void, official thriving' is rendered as the seal line as above; the bracket 'the Resource line is baggage' says that the Resource line stands for baggage (see the division of lines for going to the post in YY.13). The subject of the last sentence is left out, following on from the previous one, and is the Self line and the Pressure line.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.53 僧官医官·亦喜官文发动（旧文谓僧道医官占功名以子孙为用神、不宜父动，作者以为非：他人占僧道医以子孙为用神，自占功名仍看官爻；官星持世或父母持世得旺官生，名必成；阴阳杂职嫌子动；书中例：屯之既济，子孙持世官临应，果退职）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:僧官、道纪、医官、杂职、阴阳等官：别人占僧人、道士、医生，以子孙为用神，子孙旺相则道业高强，父母兴旺则平庸愚笨、懦弱；他们自己占自己的道术，也以子孙为用神，子孙旺相能降伏鬼神。但他们自己占功名时，子孙发动就克官星了，不能再当用神，应当仍然看官爻：官星持世，或日月动爻来生，或父母持世、有旺相的官星来生，功名必定成；子孙发动或子孙持世，一定不成名；阴阳官和各种杂职官尤其嫌子孙发动；其余的和普通人求名同样断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Monastic, Daoist, medical, minor and yin-yang officials: when another person divines about a monk, Daoist or doctor, Output is the Subject, and if Output is thriving his practice is high and strong, if Resource flourishes he is mediocre, dull and timid; when they divine about their own art, Output is again the Subject, and a thriving Output can subdue ghosts and spirits. But when they divine about their own rank, a moving Output controls the star of office and can no longer be the Subject, so one must still look at the Pressure line: if the star of office holds the Self, or the Day, Month or a moving line generates it, or Resource holds the Self and a thriving star of office generates it, the rank will surely be made; if Output moves or holds the Self, the name will surely not be made; yin-yang officials and all minor posts especially dislike a moving Output; otherwise judge as for an ordinary man seeking a name.

> **打架**:黄金策仕宦章讲僧道医官「岂可文书发动」，旧注解作僧道医官的功名以子孙为用神、父母发动克子孙必有灾悔（LY.CAREER.HJC.27）；增删卜易引了这句旧文，说「余以为非也」，并且主张自占功名时看官爻、不看子孙。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The Huangjin Ce's chapter on holding office says of monastic, Daoist and medical officials 'how can the document move', and the old commentary explains it as: their rank takes Output as the Subject, and a moving Resource that controls Output surely brings misfortune and regret (LY.CAREER.HJC.27); the Zeng Shan Bu Yi quotes this old text and says 'I think this is wrong', holding that when they divine about their own rank one looks at the Pressure line and not at Output.

> **按**:「僧官、道纪、医官、阴阳」是明清官署里管僧道、医、阴阳的官职；书里没有解释。「鬼服龙降」是说道术高强、能降伏鬼神。
>
> **Note:** Monastic officials, Daoist registrars, medical officials and yin-yang officials were the officers who administered monks, Daoists, medicine and divination in the Ming and Qing offices; the book does not explain them. 'Ghosts submit and dragons are tamed' says that the art is high and can subdue ghosts and spirits.
