## LY.CAREER.HJC.19 仕宦·官旺父衰，职任高而衙门冷落（旧注：父母旺相衙门大、休囚衙门小；官旺父衰是冷落闲静衙门；官衰父旺职卑而在大衙门；官父俱衰职卑衙小）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母旺相，衙门一定大；休囚，衙门一定小。官鬼旺而父母衰，不是小去处，而是冷落清闲的衙门，因为官旺所以职位高；官鬼衰而父母旺，职位虽然卑微，却在大衙门里办事；官和父都衰，职位低、衙门小，必定不是风宪之地。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Resource is thriving, the office is surely large; if resting or trapped, the office is surely small. With Pressure thriving and Resource weak, it is not a small place but a cold and quiet office, the post being high because Pressure is thriving; with Pressure weak and Resource thriving, the post, though lowly, is in a large office; with both Pressure and Resource weak, the post is low and the office small, and it is surely not a place in the censorate.

>
> **Note:** This couplet reads the size of the office from Resource (Resource is the documents and the yamen) and the height of the post from Pressure, two axes looked at separately. 'Yamen' is a government office, and 'cold' means one that is not busy, with not many people at work. The old commentary has the additional case 'Pressure weak and Resource thriving' (a lowly post in a large office), which Wang Hongxu does not have.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.20 仕宦·职居风宪，皆因月值官爻（旧注：官鬼旺相不临月建定非风宪；临月建又扶出世爻决是风宪之任必非府县官；带刑爻多是镇守边陲、掌兵权或居刑部；在外是司刑之职）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼旺相而不临月建，一定不是风宪的职位；如果临月建，又得到扶助而出现在世爻上，一定是风宪的任命，必不是府县的官，再在日辰的生旺之地，更是风宪；如果带刑爻，多是镇守边陲的职位，或者掌握兵权，或者在刑部，在外地也是主管刑狱的职位。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure is thriving but does not rest on the Month, it is surely not a post in the censorate; if it rests on the Month and is supported so as to appear on the Self, it is surely an appointment in the censorate and not an officer of a prefecture or county, all the more if it also stands in a thriving place of the Day; if it carries a punishment line, it is mostly a post guarding the frontier, or holding military power, or in the Board of Punishments, and outside the capital a post in charge of punishments.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7724 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7724*

| 若临月建，又得扶出世爻，决是风宪之任，必非州县之官。 | 如果临月建，又得到扶助而出现在世爻上，一定是风宪的任命，必不是州县的官； | if it rests on the Month and is supported so as to appear on the Self, it is surely an appointment in the censorate and not an officer of a prefecture or county; |

>
> **Note:** 'The censorate' is the office that supervises and enforces the law (censors and surveillance commissioners), 'the Board of Punishments' is the ministry in charge of punishments, and 'guarding the frontier' is being stationed on the border. The old commentary writes 'an officer of a prefecture or county' and Wang Hongxu 'an officer of a department or county', local officers of the same kind. Wang Hongxu changes the old commentary's 'a punishment line' into 'the White Tiger or a punishment line' and says only 'guarding the frontier and holding military power', without the old commentary's other two outcomes (the Board of Punishments, and punishments outside). The criterion 'if the Month supports Pressure or the Self, it is surely a post in the censorate' is also in LY.CAREER.HJC.07, where it comes up in passing, and here the couplet speaks directly of what kind of post it is.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.21 仕宦·官在贰司，只为鬼临旁位（旧注：鬼在世应爻上或带月建日辰是掌印正官，被世合或在旁爻是佐贰之职；六爻无鬼而动爻化出者亦然；王洪绪：官临子午卯酉是正任、寅申巳亥是佐二、辰戌丑未是杂职）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:旧注说：官鬼在世爻、应爻上，或者带月建、日辰的，必是掌印的正官；如果被世爻合住，或者在旁边的爻上，就是佐贰的职位；六爻没有官鬼而动爻化出官鬼的，也是这样。王洪绪说：官鬼临子午卯酉是正任的官，临寅申巳亥是佐贰的职官，临辰戌丑未是杂职官；如果临月建、日辰，就是掌印的官。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The old commentary says: if Pressure is on the Self or the Other, or carries the Month or the Day, it is surely the principal officer who holds the seal; if it is combined by the Self or is on a side line, it is a post as an assistant; and if there is no Pressure among the six lines but a moving line turns into Pressure, it is the same. Wang Hongxu says: Pressure on Zi, Wu, Mao and You is a principal officer, on Yin, Shen, Si and Hai an assistant post, and on Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei a miscellaneous post; and if it rests on the Month or the Day, it is the officer who holds the seal.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7727 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7727*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 官在贰司，只为鬼临旁位。 | 官员在副职的衙司，只是因为官鬼临在旁边的爻位上。 | That the officer is in a secondary office is only because Pressure sits in a side line. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7728 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7728*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 鬼在世应爻上，或带月建日辰者，必是掌印正官。 | 官鬼在世爻、应爻上，或者带月建、日辰的，必是掌印的正官。 | If Pressure is on the Self or the Other, or carries the Month or the Day, it is surely the principal officer who holds the seal. |
| 若被世合，或在旁爻，则是佐贰之职。 | 如果被世爻合住，或者在旁边的爻上，就是佐贰的职位。 | If it is combined by the Self or is on a side line, it is a post as an assistant. |
| 六爻无鬼，而动爻有化出者，亦然。 | 六爻没有官鬼，而动爻化出官鬼的，也是这样。 | If there is no Pressure among the six lines but a moving line turns into Pressure, it is the same. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1968 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1968*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 官临子午卯酉是正任官也，官临寅申巳亥乃佐二职官，临辰戌丑未乃杂职官，如临月建日辰，乃掌印之官也。 | 官鬼临子午卯酉是正任的官，临寅申巳亥是佐贰的职官，临辰戌丑未是杂职官，如果临月建、日辰，就是掌印的官。 | Pressure on Zi, Wu, Mao and You is a principal officer; on Yin, Shen, Si and Hai it is an assistant post; on Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei it is a miscellaneous post; and if it rests on the Month or the Day, it is the officer who holds the seal. |

> **打架**:两家判断「正官还是佐贰」的标准不一样：旧注看官鬼在卦里的位置（在世应爻上或带月建日辰是正官，被世爻合住或在旁爻是佐贰）；王洪绪看官鬼所临的地支（四仲子午卯酉是正官，四孟寅申巳亥是佐贰，四季辰戌丑未是杂职），再加上带月建日辰是掌印官。两个标准在同一个卦里可能给出相反的结果，书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The two commentators use different criteria for 'principal officer or assistant': the old commentary looks at the position of Pressure in the hexagram (on the Self or the Other, or carrying the Month or the Day, a principal officer; combined by the Self or on a side line, an assistant), while Wang Hongxu looks at the branch it rests on (the four cardinal branches Zi, Wu, Mao and You, a principal officer; the four initial branches Yin, Shen, Si and Hai, an assistant; the four closing branches Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei, a miscellaneous post), and adds that one carrying the Month or the Day is the officer who holds the seal. The two criteria may give opposite results in the same hexagram, and the books do not decide.

> **按**:「贰司」是副职的衙司，「佐贰」是副职官（同知、通判一类的辅佐），「杂职」是不入流的杂项官职（典史、巡检一类），「掌印」是主管官署印信的正官。经文只说「鬼临旁位」，所以旧注讲位置；王洪绪的地支标准书里没有说理由。
>
> **Note:** 'A secondary office' is the office of a deputy, 'assistant' is a deputy officer (an assistant prefect or sub-prefect and the like), 'miscellaneous post' is a lesser, unranked post (a jail warden or a patrol inspector and the like), and 'holds the seal' is the principal officer in charge of the office's seal. The verse says only 'Pressure sits in a side line', so the old commentary speaks of position; the book gives no reason for Wang Hongxu's criterion by branch.

- 《黄金策》:同文 第 1782 行:官在贰司，只为鬼临旁位

## LY.CAREER.HJC.27 仕宦·僧道医官，岂可文书发动（旧注：僧道医官及阴阳官皆要子孙出现有气不空，父母发动必有灾悔；父带岁月日非身有灾病乃外来祸；子孙自空亦有大难，然子孙只宜安静、官鬼不宜空伏；王洪绪：僧道医官以子孙为用，父动则伤、用药不灵）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:僧官、道官、医官和阴阳官，都要子孙出现、有气不空才吉。父母发动，必有灾祸悔恨；父母带太岁、月建、日辰，就不是自身有灾病，而是外来的祸；子孙自己空亡，也有大难；但子孙只适宜安静，官鬼不适宜空伏，两样都要顾到，要仔细。王洪绪说：僧官、道官、医官都以子孙为用，父母发动就伤害他们，用药不灵，反而不好。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Monk-officials, Daoist officials, physician-officials and officials of yin-yang divination all need Output to appear, with breath and not Void, to be auspicious. If Resource moves, there will surely be calamity and regret; if Resource carries the Tai Sui, the Month or the Day, it is not calamity or illness in oneself but a misfortune from outside; if Output is Void by itself, there is also a great calamity; but Output should only be still and Pressure should not be Void or hidden, and both must be satisfied, with care. Wang Hongxu says: monk-officials, Daoist officials and physician-officials all take Output as the Subject, and if Resource moves it injures them, medicines do not work and on the contrary it is bad.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7748 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7748*

> **Note:** This is the last couplet of the chapter on a school post: the earlier ones speak of whether one gets the post and how much the fee is, and the last speaks of what becomes of the person after doing the work. For 'teaching and learning grow together' see the old commentary.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.53 僧官医官·亦喜官文发动（旧文谓僧道医官占功名以子孙为用神、不宜父动，作者以为非：他人占僧道医以子孙为用神，自占功名仍看官爻；官星持世或父母持世得旺官生，名必成；阴阳杂职嫌子动；书中例：屯之既济，子孙持世官临应，果退职）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:僧官、道纪、医官、杂职、阴阳等官：别人占僧人、道士、医生，以子孙为用神，子孙旺相则道业高强，父母兴旺则平庸愚笨、懦弱；他们自己占自己的道术，也以子孙为用神，子孙旺相能降伏鬼神。但他们自己占功名时，子孙发动就克官星了，不能再当用神，应当仍然看官爻：官星持世，或日月动爻来生，或父母持世、有旺相的官星来生，功名必定成；子孙发动或子孙持世，一定不成名；阴阳官和各种杂职官尤其嫌子孙发动；其余的和普通人求名同样断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Monastic, Daoist, medical, minor and yin-yang officials: when another person divines about a monk, Daoist or doctor, Output is the Subject, and if Output is thriving his practice is high and strong, if Resource flourishes he is mediocre, dull and timid; when they divine about their own art, Output is again the Subject, and a thriving Output can subdue ghosts and spirits. But when they divine about their own rank, a moving Output controls the star of office and can no longer be the Subject, so one must still look at the Pressure line: if the star of office holds the Self, or the Day, Month or a moving line generates it, or Resource holds the Self and a thriving star of office generates it, the rank will surely be made; if Output moves or holds the Self, the name will surely not be made; yin-yang officials and all minor posts especially dislike a moving Output; otherwise judge as for an ordinary man seeking a name.

> **打架**:黄金策仕宦章讲僧道医官「岂可文书发动」，旧注解作僧道医官的功名以子孙为用神、父母发动克子孙必有灾悔（LY.CAREER.HJC.27）；增删卜易引了这句旧文，说「余以为非也」，并且主张自占功名时看官爻、不看子孙。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The Huangjin Ce's chapter on holding office says of monastic, Daoist and medical officials 'how can the document move', and the old commentary explains it as: their rank takes Output as the Subject, and a moving Resource that controls Output surely brings misfortune and regret (LY.CAREER.HJC.27); the Zeng Shan Bu Yi quotes this old text and says 'I think this is wrong', holding that when they divine about their own rank one looks at the Pressure line and not at Output.

> **按**:「僧官、道纪、医官、阴阳」是明清官署里管僧道、医、阴阳的官职；书里没有解释。「鬼服龙降」是说道术高强、能降伏鬼神。
>
> **Note:** Monastic officials, Daoist registrars, medical officials and yin-yang officials were the officers who administered monks, Daoists, medicine and divination in the Ming and Qing offices; the book does not explain them. 'Ghosts submit and dragons are tamed' says that the art is high and can subdue ghosts and spirits.
