**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求官时，初爻代表吏；官鬼落在初爻、来克世身，说明这人将来是廉明、慈惠、贤明的官员。

## LY.CAREER.HJC.21 仕宦·官在贰司，只为鬼临旁位（旧注：鬼在世应爻上或带月建日辰是掌印正官，被世合或在旁爻是佐贰之职；六爻无鬼而动爻化出者亦然；王洪绪：官临子午卯酉是正任、寅申巳亥是佐二、辰戌丑未是杂职）

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The old commentary says: if Pressure is on the Self or the Other, or carries the Month or the Day, it is surely the principal officer who holds the seal; if it is combined by the Self or is on a side line, it is a post as an assistant; and if there is no Pressure among the six lines but a moving line turns into Pressure, it is the same. > **打架**:两家判断「正官还是佐贰」的标准不一样：旧注看官鬼在卦里的位置（在世应爻上或带月建日辰是正官，被世爻合住或在旁爻是佐贰）；王洪绪看官鬼所临的地支（四仲子午卯酉是正官，四孟寅申巳亥是佐贰，四季辰戌丑未是杂职），再加上带月建日辰是掌印官。两个标准在同一个卦里可能给出相反的结果，书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The two commentators use different criteria for 'principal officer or assistant': the old commentary looks at the position of Pressure in the hexagram (on the Self or the Other, or carrying the Month or the Day, a principal officer; combined by the Self or on a side line, an assistant), while Wang Hongxu looks at the branch it rests on (the four cardinal branches Zi, Wu, Mao and You, a principal officer; the four initial branches Yin, Shen, Si and Hai, an assistant; the four closing branches Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei, a miscellaneous post), and adds that one carrying the Month or the Day is the officer who holds the seal. The two criteria may give opposite results in the same hexagram, and the books do not decide.

> **按**:「贰司」是副职的衙司，「佐贰」是副职官（同知、通判一类的辅佐），「杂职」是不入流的杂项官职（典史、巡检一类），「掌印」是主管官署印信的正官。经文只说「鬼临旁位」，所以旧注讲位置；王洪绪的地支标准书里没有说理由。
>
> **Note:** 'A secondary office' is the office of a deputy, 'assistant' is a deputy officer (an assistant prefect or sub-prefect and the like), 'miscellaneous post' is a lesser, unranked post (a jail warden or a patrol inspector and the like), and 'holds the seal' is the principal officer in charge of the office's seal. The verse says only 'Pressure sits in a side line', so the old commentary speaks of position; the book gives no reason for Wang Hongxu's criterion by branch.

## LY.CAREER.YM.50 概占何官（六爻位）：以六爻辨位之高卑——官在初爻为散缺（或本乡官守）、二爻为县邑（或谏议大臣相国学士）、三爻为守牧（或三司首领及大衙门随从）、四爻为司道（或九卿六曹近君之臣）、五爻君象惟代天巡狩与钦差者当之、六爻为贵戚之乡及宗庙功勋高尚辅相之臣；武途封职其义相类

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用六爻来分辨官位的高低：官爻在初爻，是散缺（闲散的缺额），或是本乡的官守；在二爻，是县邑，或是谏议大臣、相国学士；在三爻，是守牧（知府、知州一类），或是三司的首领，以及大衙门里随从的官；在四爻，是司道，或是九卿六曹，以及近君的臣子；五爻是君象，不是臣子所居，只有代天巡狩的官和奉钦命的钦差才当得起；六爻是贵戚之乡，以及宗庙功勋之臣、高尚之士、辅相王府的官、四围的股肱。武途的封职，意义相类似。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** By the six lines one tells the height or lowness of the rank: the Pressure line in the first line is a scattered vacancy (an idle post), or an official guard of his home district; in the second line, a county or town, or a remonstrating minister, a chief minister or academician; in the third line, a prefect or governor, or the head of the Three Offices, and officials attending in a great yamen; in the fourth line, a commissioner or circuit intendant, or the Nine Ministers and the Six Boards, and ministers near the sovereign; the fifth is the sovereign's image and not where a subject dwells, and only an official who makes tours of inspection in Heaven's stead or one sent by imperial command can fill it; the sixth line is the home of the noble kin, and of ministers of merit to the ancestral temple, men of lofty virtue, officials who assist a prince's household, and the limbs and trunk on all four sides. For appointments on the military path the meaning is similar.

## LY.CAREER.YM.53 概占何官（总括）：日月象之、世应度之、君臣位之；官爻独发论宫、一位论爻位、两现求所属、俱无考伏与变（伏占飞神、变占八宫）；官临日月建非社稷之臣即直节科道、持世为正印临应为左右、五爻为君二爻为大臣；虽代有更名理不远

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:日、月来象征它，世、应来度量它，君、臣来定它的位置。所以官爻独发的论宫，官爻只有一位的论爻位，官爻两现的求它所属，都没有的就考伏和变：伏的占飞神，变的占八宫。伏、变的办法也可以分出位置和所属。要知道当世的官守，用象去求，即使各代名称有更改，道理相差不远。官临日辰月建，不是社稷之臣，一定是直节的科道官；官爻持世是正印，临应是左右辅佐之职；五爻是君，不是御史就是当宠幸之臣；二爻是大臣，是宰相，是国家的仪表。卦里没有官鬼，就求伏下的飞神，用位和所属来分辨；卦有动象，就求所变的官爻，用八宫来区别。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Day and Month image it, the Self and the Other measure it, ruler and minister fix its place. **规则(整理,不是原文)**:断是什么官：四直、贵人、驿马聚在身爻、世爻上，又有德神、合神相扶，落在官星、印星的阳爻上，必是宰相之位；再加刑、刃、大杀，是出将入相；再加太岁或五爻生合，必荣获赐宴。太岁与五爻生合世身，是在朝为官；冲克世身，是外任；世爻、官爻在内卦发动是外任，在外卦发动是朝官（五爻是君位，四爻六爻都挨近君位）。官星、贵人持世，在阳宫阳爻的是文职，在阴宫阴爻的是武官。世爻持岁破，在朝是六部等大衙门，外任是布政司、按察司、都司；持月建，是巡方、安抚、行人这类；持日建，是郡县的长官。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To tell what office it is: if the four rulers, the Noble and the Horse gather on the Body and Self lines, and Virtue and combination also support them, and they sit on a yang line of the star of office and seal, it is surely the post of chief minister; with the punishing blade and great baleful spirits added, one goes out as a general and comes in as chancellor; with Tai Sui or the fifth line generating or combining, he is surely honoured with a gift of banquet. If Tai Sui and the fifth line generate or combine with the Self and Body, it is an official at court; if they clash with or control the Self and Body, a post outside; if the Self or the Pressure line moves in the inner trigram it is a post outside, and in the outer trigram a court official (the fifth is the ruler's seat, and the fourth and sixth are both near the ruler). If the star of office and the Noble hold the Self in a yang palace and on a yang line it is a civil post, and in a yin palace and on a yin line a military officer. If the Self holds the Year-break, at court it is in the great offices such as the Six Ministries, and outside it is in the provincial administration, judicial or military commissions; holding the Month it is an inspecting commissioner, a pacification commissioner or an envoy; holding the Day it is the head of a prefecture or county.

> **打架**:「占事」一行，周易尚占的六位例是：六爻国事、五爻心事、四爻官事、三爻家事、二爻身事、初爻人事；断易天机的「鬼谷辨爻法·占求事」是：初心事、二身事、三家事、四人事、五官事、六国事。国事（六）、家事（三）、身事（二）两处位置相同，心事、官事、人事的位置不同：心事一个在五爻、一个在初爻，官事一个在四爻、一个在五爻，人事一个在初爻、一个在四爻。两书都没有解释，本库各照原书录，不裁定。另外，「仕宦」一行是按王公、诸侯、大夫的等级排的，断易天机的「占仕宦」（初吏人、二曹官、三长官、四监司、五朝仕、六执政）是按官署的层级排的，是两种分法。
>
> **Where they disagree:** On the row for matters divined, the table of the Zhouyi Shangzhan reads: the sixth line the state, the fifth the heart, the fourth officials, the third the household, the second oneself, the first dealings with people; the Duanyi Tianji's 'Guigu method for divining an undertaking' reads: the first the heart, the second oneself, the third the household, the fourth dealings with people, the fifth officials, the sixth the state. The state (sixth), household (third) and oneself (second) stand in the same places, while the heart, officials and dealings with people differ: the heart is on the fifth in one and the first in the other, officials on the fourth in one and the fifth in the other, and dealings with people on the first in one and the fourth in the other. Neither book explains it, and each is recorded as it stands, without adjudication. The row for official careers is ordered by the rank of princes, dukes and grandees, while the Duanyi Tianji's 'divining official careers' (first clerk, second bureau officer, third head of office, fourth supervising commissioner, fifth court official, sixth minister in power) is ordered by the levels of the offices; these are two different schemes.

>
> **Note:** This is a table, which the book sets out as a heading and six cells, the six cells corresponding in order to the sixth, fifth, fourth, third, second and first lines (the first row, 'read across', gives this order).
