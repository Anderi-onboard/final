## LY.CAREER.EL.84 官员荐奖：鬼化子爻荐贤未听、官之财象奖励能从；荐与奖皆以官为主旺者来速衰则来迟，动化子孙虽荐未能听信，财来助鬼奖得如心

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼化成子孙爻，推荐贤能就不被听从；官爻变成财的象，奖励就能被应允。荐举和奖励，都以官爻为主：旺的来得快，衰的来得迟；官爻发动化成子孙，即使有人推荐也不被听信；财爻来帮助官鬼，奖励就能如愿。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure changes into an Output line, the recommendation of the worthy is not heeded; if the star of office changes into the Wealth image, the award can be granted. Both recommendation and award take the star of office as the chief: if it is thriving it comes quickly, if weak it comes late; if it moves and changes into Output, even if one is recommended it is not heeded and believed; if Wealth comes to aid Pressure, the award is obtained as one wishes.

**《易林补遗·官员荐奖章第三十三》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 822 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 33: Recommendation and commendation of officials", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 822*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 鬼化子爻荐贤未听。 | 官鬼化成子孙爻，推荐贤能就不被听从。 | If Pressure changes into an Output line, the recommendation of the worthy is not heeded. |
| 官之财象，奖励能从。 | 官爻变成财的象，奖励就能被应允。 | If the star of office changes into the Wealth image, the award can be granted. |

**《易林补遗》诀下的解说** — 《易林补遗》第 823 行 · *Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse; Yilin Buyi, line 823*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 荐与奖，皆以官为主，旺者来速，衰则来迟。 | 荐举和奖励，都以官爻为主：旺的来得快，衰的来得迟。 | Both recommendation and award take the star of office as the chief: if it is thriving it comes quickly, if weak it comes late. |
| 动化子孙，虽荐未能听信。 | 官爻发动化成子孙，即使有人推荐也不被听信。 | If it moves and changes into Output, even if one is recommended it is not heeded and believed. |
| 财来助鬼，奖得如心。 | 财爻来帮助官鬼，奖励就能如愿。 | If Wealth comes to aid Pressure, the award is obtained as one wishes. |

> **按**:「荐贤」是荐举贤能。这一条与前面仕宦升迁「官化子孙非降即调」「官若化财平升品级」（EL.56）取同一个关系，这里换到荐举、奖励上：官变子孙是失，官变财是得。
>
> **Note:** 'Jianxian' is recommending the worthy. This entry takes the same relation as 'if Pressure changes into Output it is demotion or transfer' and 'if Pressure changes into Wealth, a level promotion' (EL.56) in the earlier chapter on official promotion, here moved to recommendation and award: Pressure changing into Output is a loss and Pressure changing into Wealth is a gain.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.06 升选候补·随官入墓，世旺者官升；助鬼伤身，身衰者祸至（古以随官入墓、助鬼伤世为凶，作者屡试：世旺反升、世衰不吉，助鬼伤身而元神同动者官必升，世休囚必有祸）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:古人把「随官入墓」「助鬼伤世」一概当凶，作者多次试验，结果是：世爻旺相的反而升官；世爻衰弱的不吉；助鬼伤身而元神同时发动的，官职必定升；世爻如果休囚，必有灾祸。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The ancients took 'following the star of office into the tomb' and 'helping Pressure to injure the Self' as ominous without exception; the author tested it many times and found: where the Self is thriving the man is promoted instead; where the Self is weak it is not auspicious; where one helps Pressure to injure the Body but the Support moves at the same time, the office is surely raised; if the Self is resting or trapped, disaster is sure to come.

**《增删卜易·升选候补章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10423–10424 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Chapter on promotion, selection and waiting for a post", base text; Zengshan Buyi, lines 10423–10424*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 随官入墓，世旺者官升。 | 随着官星入墓，世爻旺相的，官职升迁。 | If the Self follows the star of office into the tomb and the Self is thriving, the office is raised. |
| 助鬼伤身，身衰者祸至。 | 帮助官鬼来伤害自身，自身衰弱的，灾祸就到。 | If one helps Pressure to injure the Body and the Body is weak, disaster arrives. |
| 古以随官入墓、助鬼伤世，皆以为凶。 | 古人认为随官入墓、助鬼伤世，都是凶。 | The ancients took following the star of office into the tomb, and helping Pressure to injure the Self, both as ominous. |
| 余屡试之，世旺者竞升； | 我多次试验，世爻旺相的，竟然都升了； | I tested it many times: where the Self was thriving, the man was promoted; |
| 世衰者不吉； | 世爻衰弱的，不吉； | where the Self was weak, it was not auspicious; |
| 助鬼伤身，元神同动者，其官必升； | 助鬼伤身，但元神同时发动的，他的官职必定升； | where one helps Pressure to injure the Body but the Support moves at the same time, his office will surely be raised; |

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 寅木财爻生助午火之鬼，火鬼克世，乃为助鬼伤身，幸辰土生申金，午火贪生忘克，今年冬月必升。 | 寅木财爻生助午火官鬼，火官鬼克世爻，这就是助鬼伤身；幸好辰土生申金，午火贪着去生而忘了去克，所以今年冬月必定升官。 | The Yin Wood Wealth line generates and helps the Wu Fire Pressure line, and the Fire Pressure line controls the Self, which is helping Pressure to injure the Body; fortunately Chen Earth generates Shen Metal, and Wu Fire, greedy to generate, forgets to control, so he will surely be promoted this winter month. |
| 果得升于冬月。 | 结果果然在冬月升了官。 | He was indeed promoted in the winter month. |
| 应冬月者，辰土化子水空亡，至十一月则不空矣。 | 应在冬月，是因为辰土变成子水，而子水落空亡，到十一月就不空了。 | It came due in the winter month because Chen Earth turned into Zi Water, which was Void, and in the eleventh month it is no longer Void. |

> **打架**:古人把「随官入墓」「助鬼伤世」都当作凶；增删卜易说自己多次试验，世爻旺的反而升官，元神同时发动的官必升，只有世爻休囚才有祸。这是作者对古说的修正，不是两本书的分歧。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The ancients took 'following the star of office into the tomb' and 'helping Pressure to injure the Self' as ominous; the Zeng Shan Bu Yi says that after many tests the man whose Self was thriving was promoted instead, that where the Support moves at the same time the office is surely raised, and that only a resting or trapped Self brings disaster. This is the author's correction of the old teaching, not a disagreement between two books.

> **按**:原文第二个例子的月份写作「戍月」，应是「戌月」，译文按戌月。
>
> **Note:** The second example gives the month as 'Shu' in the text, which should be 'Xu'; the translation reads the Xu month.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.16 在任吉凶·爻反吟，入三墓，反复昏庸（卦得反吟，世官旺相必升，世官衰必降罚，世官被冲克有不测之祸；世休囚被克化墓入墓立见灾非，世有气化墓入墓不过昏聩）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:爻遇反吟或入三墓，主反复不定、昏庸糊涂。卦得反吟：世爻、官爻旺相，一定升迁；世爻、官星衰弱，必遭降职处罚，降后还要焦虑；世爻或官星被冲被克，有想不到的灾祸。世爻休囚被克、变墓或入墓，立刻见到灾祸是非；世爻有气而变墓、入墓，不过是糊里糊涂、昏聩罢了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Lines in Reversed Chant, or entering the three tombs, mean going back and forth, muddle and dullness. If the hexagram gets Reversed Chant: with the Self and the Pressure line thriving one is surely promoted; with the Self and the star of office weak one is demoted and punished and anxious after the demotion; if the Self or the star of office is clashed or controlled, there is unforeseen calamity. If the Self is resting or trapped and controlled, turns into the tomb or enters the tomb, disaster and trouble appear at once; if the Self has strength and turns into the tomb or enters the tomb, it is no more than being hazy and befuddled.
