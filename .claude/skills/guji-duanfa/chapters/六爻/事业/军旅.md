## LY.CAREER.HJC.24 仕宦·出征剿捕，福德兴而寇贼歼亡（旧注：将帅平居卜问不宜子孙发动主降调贬责、不宜应动克世；岁君月建冲动官鬼或世爻有敕命征讨；临敌卜问喜子孙发动必成剿捕之功；官鬼当贼寇论，世克应亦吉）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:担任将帅或征讨之官的人，平时占问，不适宜子孙发动，主有降职调任、贬责，也不适宜应爻发动克世爻，主有意想不到的变故；太岁、月建冲动官鬼或世爻，主有敕命征讨的事。如果临阵对敌占问，就喜欢子孙发动，必成剿捕的功绩，再得到太岁、月建生合，还有升赏；这时官鬼不当作爵位，要当作贼寇来论，世克应也吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For one holding the post of commander or an officer of punitive expeditions, in divining in ordinary times it is not fitting that Output moves, which means demotion, transfer and rebuke, nor that the Other moves and controls the Self, which means an unforeseen change; if the Tai Sui or the Month clashes Pressure or the Self into motion, there will be an imperial order for a punitive expedition. If the divination is made facing the enemy, it is welcome that Output moves, and one will surely achieve merit in suppressing the bandits, and if the Tai Sui and the Month also generate or combine, there will be promotion and reward as well; Pressure is then not taken as rank but as the bandits, and the Self controlling the Other is also auspicious.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7736 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7736*

| 更得岁君月建生合世爻，主有升赏。 | 再得到太岁、月建生合世爻，主有升赏。 | and if the Tai Sui and the Month also generate or combine with the Self, there will be promotion and reward. |

> **按**:这一联要分两种问法：平时问（还没有开战，问自己的仕途）和临阵问（敌人当前，问这一仗）。平时问，子孙是「伤官之杀」，发动就降调贬责；临阵问，子孙是「克鬼」的神（官鬼这时是贼寇），发动就能灭贼。所以同一个子孙发动，平时凶、临阵吉，这和 LY.CAREER.HJC.03（子孙发动剥官削职）不矛盾，是用神换了：官鬼不再是你的官，而是你的敌人。「敕命征讨」是皇帝下令出兵，「剿捕」是围剿捕捉。
>
> **Note:** This couplet must be read for two kinds of question: in ordinary times (no war has begun, and one asks about one's own career) and facing the enemy (the enemy is before one, and one asks about this battle). In ordinary times Output is 'the killer that harms office', and moving brings demotion and rebuke; facing the enemy Output is the spirit that controls Pressure (which is then the bandits), and moving can wipe them out. So the same moving Output is ominous in ordinary times and auspicious facing the enemy, which does not contradict LY.CAREER.HJC.03 (Output moving strips the office): the Subject has changed, and Pressure is no longer your office but your enemy. 'An imperial order for a punitive expedition' is the emperor ordering out the army, and 'suppress' is surrounding and seizing.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.25 仕宦·镇守边陲，卦爻静而华夷安泰（旧注：镇守不拘文武皆宜六爻安静，世应生合比和日辰月建不冲克则安然无警，世应空亡亦吉；官鬼发动世应冲克必多侵扰）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:镇守一方，不论文官武职，都适宜六爻安静；世应生合比和，日辰月建不互相冲克，就能安睡在边庭，安然没有警报，世应空亡也吉；如果遇到官鬼发动、世应冲克，必定多有侵扰，要通变着断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In guarding a region, whether in a civil or military post, it is fitting that the six lines are still; if the Self and the Other generate, combine or are in harmony and the Day and the Month do not clash or control each other, one sleeps soundly at the border, at ease and without alarm, and the Self and the Other Void is also auspicious; if Pressure moves and the Self and the Other clash or control each other, there will surely be many incursions, and one should judge flexibly.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7742 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7742*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 若遇官鬼发动，世应冲克，必多骚扰。 | 如果遇到官鬼发动，世应冲克，必定多有骚扰。 | if Pressure moves and the Self and the Other clash or control each other, there will surely be many disturbances. |

> **按**:「华夷」是中原和边地的民族，「安泰」是平安。「鼾睡边庭」是说在边疆睡得安稳，不用戒备。「世应空亡亦吉」只有旧注写了：世应都空，双方都不动，边境无事。这一联讲的是「守」，和上一联 LY.CAREER.HJC.24 的「攻」（出征剿捕）正好相反：守要静，攻要动。旧注结尾「宜通变断之」、王洪绪「用宜通变推之」，都是提醒不要死守这条。
>
> **Note:** 'The Chinese and the border peoples' are the peoples of the heartland and of the border lands, and 'at peace' is peace and safety. 'Sleeps soundly at the border' says one sleeps easily on the frontier without having to be on guard. 'The Self and the Other Void is also auspicious' is only in the old commentary: when both the Self and the Other are Void, neither side moves and there is nothing on the border. This couplet speaks of 'defending', the very opposite of the preceding couplet, LY.CAREER.HJC.24, on 'attacking' (campaigning and suppressing bandits): defending wants stillness, and attacking wants movement. The old commentary's closing 'judge it flexibly' and Wang Hongxu's 'in use it should be inferred flexibly' both warn against holding to this rule rigidly.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.86 征战·观世应之旺衰，以决两家之胜负；将福官之强弱，以分彼我之军师（旧注：世为我、应为彼，世旺克应则胜、应旺克世则负；子孙为我之将军、官鬼为彼之敌帅）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占征战，世爻是我方，应爻是对方：世爻旺相、克住应爻就胜，应爻旺相、克住世爻就败。子孙是我方的将军，官鬼是对方的主帅，两边军师的强弱，就从这两个爻上分。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a question about a campaign the Self is our side and the Other is the enemy: if the Self is thriving or strong and controls the Other we win, and if the Other is thriving and controls the Self we lose. Output is our general and Pressure is the enemy commander, and the strength of the two sides' leaders is told apart from these two lines.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.87 征战·父母兴隆，立望旌旗之蔽野；金爻空动，侧听金鼓之喧天（旧注：父母为旌旗，旺动主兵起；金空动则闻金鼓声，金空则响故也）
