## LY.CAREER.HJC.08 仕宦·世动逢空，官居不久（旧注：未任世动必无京官牧守、出巡之职反顺利；已任遇之居官不久，日辰动爻相冲必不久任政事）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻发动又遇空亡：没做官的，卦中世爻发动，必定得不到京官、牧守一类的职位，不过如果是出巡的职位，反而顺利；已经做官的遇到这种情形，居官不久，再遇日辰、动爻相冲，必定不能长久任职理政。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Self moves and meets Void: for one not yet in office, with the Self moving in the hexagram, one will surely get no post as a capital official or a governor or magistrate, though if it is a post of touring and inspecting it goes smoothly on the contrary; for one already in office, meeting this means one will not stay long in office, and if the Day or a moving line also clashes it, one will surely not hold the post and govern for long.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7683 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7683*

| --- | --- | --- |
| 世动逢空，官居不久； | 世爻发动又遇空亡，做官居位不久； | If the Self moves and meets Void, one will not stay long in office; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7684 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7684*

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1940 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1940*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 若是出巡之职，世动逢空反利己任； | 如果是出巡的职位，世爻发动逢空反而有利于已经任职的； | If it is a post of touring and inspecting, the Self moving into Void is on the contrary favourable to one already in office; |
| 遇日辰动爻相冲，必不久任政事。 | 遇到日辰、动爻相冲，必定不能长久任职理政。 | if the Day or a moving line clashes it, one will surely not hold the post and govern for long. |

> **按**:「牧守」是牧民之官，即州郡的长官；「京官」是在京城任职的官。「出巡」是出外巡视的差使（见 LY.CAREER.HJC.23）。这里的两种读法：旧注说出巡的职位「反为顺利」（没分已任未任），王洪绪说「反利己任」，「己」按上下文是「已」的误写，译作「有利于已经任职的」；两家差一点：旧注是出巡这类差使世动逢空都顺，王洪绪限在已经任职的人身上。
>
> **Note:** 'Governor or magistrate' is the officer who shepherds the people, that is, the head of a prefecture or commandery, and 'capital official' is one holding office in the capital. 'Touring and inspecting' is a commission to go out on a tour of inspection (see LY.CAREER.HJC.23). The two commentators read the last point a little differently: the old commentary says that a post of touring 'goes smoothly on the contrary' (without distinguishing already in office from not yet), while Wang Hongxu writes 'is on the contrary favourable to the one in office', where the printed character for 'in office' is a misprint of the similar character 'already' and is translated 'one already in office'; the old commentary says that for this kind of commission the Self moving into Void goes smoothly in general, and Wang Hongxu confines it to the person already in office.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.10 仕宦·鬼化福冲当代职（旧注：出巡官宜鬼动、牧守官宜鬼静；鬼动化子必有别官代职；子动化鬼先难后易；官福皆动亦主有官替代）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:出巡的官，适合官鬼发动；牧守一类的官，适合官鬼安静。如果官鬼发动、化出子孙，必有别的官员来代理职务，不然也会被别人抢在前面；子孙发动化出官鬼，就是先难后易，或者先凶后吉；官鬼和子孙都发动，也主有官员来接替。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For an officer on tour, it is fitting that Pressure moves; for a governor or magistrate, it is fitting that Pressure is still. If Pressure moves and turns into Output, another officer will surely come to take over the post, or one is otherwise forestalled by someone else; if Output moves and turns into Pressure, it is hard first and easy later, or ominous first and fortunate later; if both Pressure and Output move, it likewise means an officer comes to replace one.

> **按**:经文的「福冲」是说官鬼动化出子孙（福）、又被冲；「当代职」是「会有人来替代」。出巡官与牧守官对官鬼动静的要求相反：出巡是外出差使，官鬼动才有事可办；牧守是坐镇一地，官鬼静才稳。同一个判据又见 LY.CAREER.HJC.22（牧守）和 LY.CAREER.HJC.23（出巡）。
>
> **Note:** 'Turns into Output and is clashed' in the verse means Pressure moving and turning into Output (the 'blessing' line) and then being clashed, and 'another takes over the post' means that someone will come to replace one. An officer on tour and a governor or magistrate need opposite things of Pressure: touring is a commission outside, where Pressure must move for there to be business to do, and a magistrate is posted in one place, where Pressure must be still for the post to be steady. The same criteria are met again in LY.CAREER.HJC.22 (governing) and LY.CAREER.HJC.23 (touring).

## LY.CAREER.HJC.14 仕宦·兄爻化鬼无情，同僚不协（旧注：兄弟为僚属之官，鬼动化兄冲克世爻或兄化鬼刑冲克害、兄带三刑六害伤世主同僚不和；世克兄爻是我欺他而不和）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟是僚属，也就是同僚、下属一类的官。卦中官鬼发动、化出兄弟，冲克世爻，主同僚不和；或者兄弟化出官鬼而刑冲克害，或者兄弟带三刑六害伤害世爻，也都是这样；世爻克兄弟，是我欺负他，因而不和。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Peer stands for the officers of one's staff, that is, colleagues and subordinates. If in the hexagram Pressure moves and turns into Peer and clashes or controls the Self, it means discord among colleagues; or if Peer turns into Pressure and punishes, clashes, controls or harms, or if Peer carries the three punishments or the six harms and injures the Self, it is the same; if the Self controls Peer, it is I who bully him, and so there is discord.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.16 仕宦·卦静世空，退休之兆；身空杀动，避祸之征（旧注：世空未任无选期；已任六爻安静月日岁君无伤是休官改政；鬼发动被月日岁君伤克是避祸脱灾；卦静日冲欲归不放、杀动日合欲避不能）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦静而世爻空亡，是退休的兆头；身爻空亡而煞发动，是躲避祸事的征兆。世爻空亡，没做官的没有选用的日期；已经做官的，如果六爻安静、月建日辰太岁没有伤它，就是休官改任的象；如果官鬼发动、被月建日辰太岁伤克，是躲避祸患、脱离灾难的兆头；卦静而日辰冲，想回家却被留住；煞动而日辰合，想躲避却躲不开。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A still hexagram with the Self Void is a sign of retirement; the Body Void and a killer moving is a sign of avoiding misfortune. When the Self is Void, one not yet in office has no date of selection; for one already in office, if the six lines are still and the Month, the Day and the Tai Sui do not injure it, it is the image of retiring from office and changing post; if Pressure moves and the Month, the Day or the Tai Sui injures or controls it, it is a sign of avoiding misfortune and escaping calamity; if the hexagram is still and the Day clashes, one wants to go home but is not let go; if a killer moves and the Day combines, one wants to escape but cannot.

The old commentary has two further sentences, 'if the hexagram is still and the Day clashes, one wants to go home but is not let go; if a killer moves and the Day combines, one wants to escape but cannot', meaning that one wishes to retire but is held by the Day, or wishes to flee but is combined by the Day, and Wang Hongxu does not have these two.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.17 仕宦·身边伏鬼若非空，头上乌纱终不脱（旧注：遇凶兆，鬼临身持世或本宫鬼伏世下虽见责罚官职犹在；不临持身世、不伏世下或伏而遇空亡者必革官为民）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:遇到凶兆的时候，如果官鬼爻临身持世，或者本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，虽然被责罚，官职还在；如果不临身持世，也不伏在世爻下面，或者虽然伏着仍遇到空亡，必定被革去官带贬为平民，不只是责罚而已。
