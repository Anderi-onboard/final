## LY.CAREER.EL.54 求馆开设：那日扶持生合世便教宾主两相逢；要知那日成关还详父母——卦中无父但逢值日方在，有父爻旺求墓合之时，衰取旺生之日，又看何日生世、空世亦可成关

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要知道哪一天定下这个馆，要细看父母爻：卦里有父爻而且旺相，取它入墓、逢合的时候；父爻衰弱，取它旺相、得生的日子；卦里没有父爻，要等到它当值的那一天才算在。还要看哪一天生世爻，世爻落空的也可以在那一天成。到了扶助、生合世爻的那一天，宾（师）主（东家）两方就会相见。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know on which day the engagement is settled, one must look closely at Resource: if Resource is in the hexagram and thriving, take the time when it enters the tomb or meets a combination; if it is weak, take the day when it thrives or is generated; if it is not in the hexagram, it is present only when the day on which it is on duty comes. Look also for the day that generates the Self; even a Void Self can then be settled. On the day that aids, generates or combines with the Self, guest and host come to meet each other.

**《易林补遗·求馆开设章第二十六》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 725 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 26: Seeking and opening a school post", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 725*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 那日扶持生合世，便教宾主两相逢。 | 到了扶助、生、合世爻的那一天，就会让宾（师）主（东家）两方相见。 | On the day that aids, generates or combines with the Self, guest and host are made to meet each other. |

**《易林补遗》诀下的解说** — 《易林补遗》第 726 行 · *Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse; Yilin Buyi, line 726*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 要知那日成关，还详父母。 | 要知道是哪一天定下这个馆，还要细看父母爻。 | To know on which day the engagement is settled, one must still look closely at Resource. |
| 卦中无父，但逢值日方在。 | 卦中没有父爻的，要等到它当值的日子才算在。 | If there is no Resource in the hexagram, it is present only when the day on which it is on duty comes. |
| 如有父爻旺，求墓合之时。 | 如果有父爻而且旺相，就要找它入墓、逢合的时候。 | If Resource is present and thriving, look for the time when it enters the tomb or meets a combination. |
| 衰取旺生之日，又看何日生世、空世亦可成关。 | 父爻衰弱的，取它旺相、得生的日子；又要看哪一天生世爻；世爻落空的，也可以成就这个馆。 | If it is weak, take the day when it thrives or is generated; look also for the day that generates the Self; even with the Self Void the engagement can be settled. |

> **按**:「成关」的「关」应是关书（聘请塾师的聘书），「成关」就是把聘定下来；书里没有解释，这里按这个意思译。「卦中无父，但逢值日方在」按字面译：卦里没有父爻，要等到它当值的日子才算在；书里没有说「当值」按什么取，这里不加解释。「求墓合之时」的「墓」是父爻的墓库，「合」是与父爻相合的地支（见术语表）。末句「空世亦可成关」字面是世爻落空也能成；前面说世空遇不到尊从，这里说的是逢到生世的日子仍可成就，两处并不相冲。这一条与易隐觅馆占「关约何日来」（YY.81）取同一个法：旺则墓合日、衰则生旺日、无父则值日，又看生合世之日。
>
> **Note:** The 'guan' of 'cheng guan' is presumably the 'guanshu', the letter of engagement by which a tutor is hired, and 'cheng guan' is the engagement being settled; the book does not explain it, and the translation takes it so. 'If there is no Resource in the hexagram, it is present only when the day on which it is on duty comes' is translated literally: the book does not say by what the 'duty' is reckoned, and nothing is added here. In 'qiu mu he zhi shi' the 'tomb' is the tomb of the Resource line and the 'combination' is the branch that combines with it (see the glossary). The last sentence, 'a Void Self too can settle the engagement', literally says that a Void Self can still be settled; earlier it said that a Void Self meets no one who honours and follows one, and what is said here is that on a day that generates the Self it can still be accomplished, so the two places do not clash. This entry takes the same method as 'on what day the engagement comes' in the Yi Yin divination for seeking a post (YY.81): a thriving line by the tomb or combination day, a weak one by a generating or thriving day, a missing one by its duty day, and also the day that generates or combines with the Self.

## LY.CAREER.EL.61 仕宦升迁：欲知何月官升处、遇值逢生喜报祥；欲决何时迁转，推评官鬼旺衰——旺则高升在近，衰则待生旺之期；若六爻无鬼，查那月临官，便知迁升决矣

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要知道官职在哪个月升，遇到官爻当值、得生的月份，就有喜报。要断定什么时候迁转，就推断官鬼的旺衰：官鬼旺相，高升就在眼前；衰弱，就等它得生、旺相的时候。如果六爻里没有官鬼，再查哪个月临到官，就能知道升迁的决断了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know in which month one is promoted, when the line meets its own month or the month that generates it, joyful news is reported. To decide when the promotion or transfer will come, weigh the strength of Pressure: if it is thriving, the high promotion is near at hand; if weak, wait for the time when it is generated or thriving. If there is no Pressure among the six lines, then check which month falls on the star of office, and one will know the decision of the promotion.

**《易林补遗·仕宦升迁章第二十九》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 767 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 29: Official career and promotion", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 767*

> **按**:「官陛处」的「陛」是「升」。「高承升在尔」按字面不顺，「尔」疑是「迩」（近），译作「高升就在近前」；「承」不译。「那月临官」按文意是官爻（官星）当值的那个月，书里没有细说。这一条与易隐官禄占「候选（二）·何日得官」（YY.08）取同一个法：官旺者看当值或合的年月日，官衰者看生旺的时候，官伏藏者看临值的时候。
>
> **Note:** The 'bi' of 'guan bi chu' is 'sheng', to rise. 'Gao cheng sheng zai er' does not run smoothly as written, and 'er' is presumably 'er', near, so it is translated 'the high promotion is near at hand'; 'cheng' is left untranslated. 'The month falls on the star of office' is, by the sense, the month in which the line of the star of office is on duty, which the book does not go into. This entry takes the same method as 'on what day one gets office' in the Yi Yin divination on awaiting selection (YY.08): for a thriving star of office look at the year, month or day on duty or in combination, for a weak one at the time it is generated or thriving, and for a hidden one at the time it comes on duty.

## LY.CAREER.EL.85 官员荐奖：如卜官凭但愿文书逢旺气、若占到日远寻父母遇生方；又附占文凭到日须看父母之爻候临生临旺之期此凭决到，卦无父须待父爻值日方得凭来，父若衰而又空纵然等候而未至

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占官凭（官员的委任凭据），只要文书爻逢上旺相的气；占凭据哪一天到，要远远去找父母爻遇上生它的时候。另外附带占文凭到的日子，要看父母爻，等它临到生、旺的时候，这份凭据一定到；卦里没有父爻，要等到父爻当值的日子，凭据才会来；父爻如果衰弱又落空，即使等候，凭据也还没有到。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To divine about the official credentials, the document line need only meet a thriving qi; to divine on what day they arrive, look far for Resource meeting the time that generates it. Also appended: for the day the credentials arrive, look at the Resource line and wait for the time when it meets generation or thriving, and the credentials will surely arrive; if there is no Resource in the hexagram, one must wait for the day when the Resource line is on duty, and only then will the credentials come; if Resource is weak and also Void, even if one waits the credentials have not arrived.

**《易林补遗·官员荐奖章第三十三》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 824 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 33: Recommendation and commendation of officials", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 824*

| 如卜官凭，但原文书逢旺气。 | 如果卜官凭（委任的凭据），只要文书爻逢上旺相的气。 | To divine about the official credentials, the document line need only meet a thriving qi. |
| 若占到日，远寻父母遇生方。 | 如果占凭据哪一天到，要远远去找父母爻遇上生它的时候。 | To divine on what day they arrive, look far for Resource meeting the time that generates it. |

> **按**:「官凭」是官员到任的凭证（委任文凭）。「但原文书」的「原」疑是「愿」，译作「只要文书逢旺气」，不论怎么读，要的都是文书爻旺相。解里「此凭决到卦无父」书里没有断句，这里按文意断作「此凭决到；卦无父，须待父爻值日，方得凭来」。「值日」与求馆章「卦中无父，但逢值日方在」（EL.54）同，书里没有说按什么取。这一条与易隐官禄占「领凭」（YY.10）取同一类法：都以父母爻（文书）为用，看它的生旺和当值之日。
>
> **Note:** 'Guanping' is the credentials with which an official takes up his post (the letter of appointment). The 'yuan' of 'dan yuan wenshu' is presumably 'yuan', to wish, and it is translated 'the document line need only meet a thriving qi'; whichever way it is read, what is wanted is the document line thriving. In the explanation 'ci ping jue dao gua wu fu' has no punctuation in the book, and it is broken here by the sense as 'the credentials will surely arrive; if there is no Resource in the hexagram, one must wait for the day when Resource is on duty, and then the credentials will come'. 'Zhiri', the duty day, is the same as in 'if there is no Resource in the hexagram, it is present only when its duty day comes' in the chapter on seeking a school post (EL.54), and the book does not say by what it is reckoned. This entry takes the same kind of method as the 'receiving the credentials' section of the Yi Yin divination on official salary (YY.10): in both, Resource (the document) is the Subject and its thriving and its duty day are looked at.

## LY.CAREER.YM.09 国事：占享国几何以岁为纪，有岁则一位一元、再之亦一位一元，然必考其本宫之旺衰、鬼象之动静方可推而定；岁司年历国祚，如己亥年得丁亥太岁有六元之运，又以内卦参之、鬼动权之方可推

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占国运能延续多久，以太岁为纪年：有太岁就是一位一元，再有也是一位一元；但必须考察本宫的旺衰、官鬼之象的动静，才能推定。太岁管年历国运：比如己亥年，得丁亥太岁，有六元的运数，还要用内卦参看，以官鬼发动来权衡，才可以推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To divine how long the dynasty will last, take the Year as the reckoning: if the Tai Sui is present it is one position, one cycle; with another it is again one position, one cycle; but one must still examine the strength or weakness of the home palace and the movement or stillness of the Pressure image before it can be reckoned and settled. The Year governs the calendar and the dynasty's fortune: for example, in a Jihai year, getting the Dinghai Tai Sui, there is a destiny of six cycles, and one also consults the inner trigram and weighs it by Pressure moving, and only then can it be reckoned.

**《易冒·国事章第四十二》原文** — 《易冒》第 829 行 · *Yi Mao, "Chapter 42: State affairs", base text; Yi Mao, line 829*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 若占享国几何，以岁为纪，有岁则一位一元，再之亦一位一元，然必考其本宫之旺衰、鬼象之动静，方可推而定也。 | 如果占国运能延续多久，以太岁为纪年：有太岁就是一位一元，再有也是一位一元；但必须考察本宫的旺衰、官鬼之象的动静，才能推定。 | To divine how long the dynasty will last, take the Year as the reckoning: if the Tai Sui is present it is one position, one cycle; with another it is again one position, one cycle; but one must still examine the strength or weakness of the home palace and the movement or stillness of the Pressure image before it can be reckoned and settled. |

>
> **Note:** 'Xiangguo' is the number of years a dynasty holds the throne and its fortune lasts. How 'one position, one cycle' and 'a destiny of six cycles' are counted is not explained in the book ('yuan' looks like a unit for counting years), so it is translated literally with no explanation added, and the book does not unfold how the 'Jihai year' and 'Dinghai Tai Sui' of the note correspond. This entry asks the same question as 'counting the reigns and the years' in the Yi Yin divination on the court (YY.49), but the method of counting differs and the two must not be mixed.

## LY.CAREER.YM.31 卜馆·问得馆何时：实世、生世、合世之日就是它的日期

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问什么时候能得到馆，看填实世爻（世爻落空时）、生世、合世的日子，那就是它的日期。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In asking when a post will be obtained, the days that fill the Self, generate the Self or combine with the Self are its date.

**《易冒·卜馆章第六十一》原文** — 《易冒》第 1358 行 · *Yi Mao, "Chapter 61: Divining a teaching post", base text; Yi Mao, line 1358*

| 则其期也。 | 就是它的日期。 | are its date. |

> **按**:「实世」按文意是填实世爻：世落空时，逢到把它填实的日子叫实；「生世」「合世」是生它、合它的日子。书里只有这一句，没有展开。易林补遗求馆（EL.54）和易隐觅馆占（YY.81）把它细讲了：以父母为用，按旺衰取日。

> **Note:** 'Shishi' is, by the sense, filling the Self line: when the Self is Void, a day that meets and fills it is called 'real'; 'generating the Self' and 'combining with the Self' are days that generate or combine with it. The book has only this sentence and does not expand on it. The Yilin Buyi on seeking a post (EL.54) and the Yi Yin divination of a post (YY.81) treat it in detail: Resource is the Subject, and the day is taken by thriving or declining.

## LY.CAREER.YM.43 升何时：值月为法，逢生旺则会推、遇扶助则高擢；再筮是期世实官崇；两官一强一弱以弱者值月为期，一官必求值月，长生临官有气亦可升、生助之月亦然，而以值月为最、长生次之

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:它什么时候升？以官爻自己的月份（值月）为法：逢生旺就会被会推（联名推举），遇扶助就高升。再占确切的日期：世爻实、官爻崇旺，就不是别的日子，就是那一天。如果有两个官爻、一强一弱，那个弱的值月就升，例如亥月壬戌日占调迁得丰卦，到季冬才升。只有一个官爻，一定要求它值月，可以占验；官在长生、临官而有气，也可以升迁，生它、助它的月份也一样，但都不如值月最好，长生次之。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When will the promotion come? The rule is to take the month in which the Pressure line is on duty (its own month): meeting generation and thriving, one is put forward by joint recommendation; meeting support, one is raised high. To divine again for the exact date: when the Self is solid and Pressure exalted, it is no other day, but that very one. If there are two Pressure lines, one strong and one weak, the promotion comes when the weak one meets its month; for example in a Hai month on a Ren-Xu day, divining about a transfer, one got Feng, and the promotion came only in the last month of winter. With a single Pressure line one must seek its own month, and it can then be tested; if it is at Birth or Taking Office with force, one can also be promoted, and the months that generate and support it are the same, but none is as good as its own month, with Birth second.

**《易冒·功名章第六十二》原文** — 《易冒》第 1403 行 · *Yi Mao, "Chapter 62: Fame and office", base text; Yi Mao, line 1403*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |

## LY.CAREER.YY.49 朝廷占·卜世卜年（易学主青）：天子自占以世为主，世爻动变则从世爻数到变爻定世与年，卦变世不动则以正变二世爻数之，六爻安静则从所卜之年数至世爻，臣民代占以五爻为主；附古占验——全寅占英宗北狩复位、陈搏对宋太祖享国长短

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占国家传几世、延续几年（易学主青）：天子自己占，以世爻为主。世爻发动变出的，就从世爻数到变爻，来定他传几世、几年：比如纯乾卦世爻壬戌发动，变成泽天夬卦的丁未爻，就从壬戌数到丁未，是四十六世，年数就是四十六年。卦变了、世爻不动的，就用本卦和变卦这两个世爻来数：比如乾变姤，从壬戌数到辛丑世爻，是四十世，年数就是四十年。六爻安静的，就从占卜那一年数到世爻：比如庚寅年占，从庚寅数到壬戌，是三十三世，年数就是三十三年。臣子百姓代占的，以五爻为主：比如甲子年占得乾卦，五爻是壬申，从甲子数到壬申，占世是九世，占年是九年。其余照这样推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Divining how many reigns or years the dynasty will last (Yixue Zhuqing). When the Son of Heaven divines for himself, take the Self as the chief. If all six lines are still, count from the year of the divination to the Self line: divining in a Gengyin year, from Gengyin to Renxu is thirty-three reigns and thirty-three years. When ministers and commoners divine on his behalf, take the fifth line as the chief: divining in a Jiazi year and getting Qian, the fifth line is Renshen, and from Jiazi to Renshen is nine reigns and nine years. The rest follow this.

## LY.CAREER.YY.81 觅馆占·关约何日来：以父母为用；卦无父死值之日来，父旺合日来，父衰生旺日来，父空墓逢冲之日来；又父生世合世之日亦可成关得关

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:想知道聘书（关约）什么时候来：以父母爻为用神。卦里没有父爻的，在（父爻）死、当值的日子来；父母爻旺相的，在合它的日子来；衰弱的，在它生旺的日子来；空、墓的，在冲它的日子来。又，父母爻生世爻、合世爻的日子，也可以成关、得到聘约。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To know when the engagement (contract) will come: take Resource as the Subject. If the hexagram has no Resource line, it comes on the day it dies and rules; if Resource is thriving, on the day that combines with it; if weak, on the day it is born or thriving; if Void or in tomb, on the day that clashes with it. Again, on the day Resource generates or combines with the Self, the engagement can also be made and obtained.

## LY.CAREER.YY.88 谋望占·迟速：以用爻为主；动速静迟，旺相出现速、囚死伏藏迟；阳变阴速、阴变阳迟；动临震巽速、临坎艮迟；用临卯酉速、辰戌迟；初二爻动速、三四犹豫、五动迟、六动更迟；用临太岁不出一年、月将不出一月、日辰本日、日建本时；尹逢头断法

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:快慢：以用爻为主。发动的快，安静的慢；旺相、出现的快，囚、死、伏藏的慢；阳变阴的快，阴变阳的慢；发动临在震、巽的快，临在坎、艮的慢；用爻临卯、酉的快（卯酉是日月出入的门户），临辰、戌的慢（辰戌是天罗地网，是天涯海角）；初爻、二爻发动的快，三爻、四爻发动的犹豫迟疑，五爻发动的慢，六爻发动的更慢。用爻临太岁，不出一年；临月将，不出一月；临日辰，就在当日；临日建，就在当时。尹逢头的断法说：用爻旺相，还要等入墓的日子来定；用爻休囚，在生旺的日子应当成；用爻伏藏，只看用爻当值的日子；发动而被合住的，等冲它的日辰。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Slow or fast. Take the Subject line as the chief. Moving is quick, still is slow; thriving and appearing is quick, imprisoned, dead and hidden is slow; yang changing into yin is quick, yin changing into yang is slow; moving on Zhen or Xun is quick, on Kan or Gen is slow; the Subject on Mao or You is quick (Mao and You are the gates of sun and moon), on Chen or Xu is slow (Chen and Xu are the net of heaven and the snare of earth, the ends of the earth); a first or second line moving is quick, a third or fourth moving hesitates and delays, a fifth moving is slow, and a sixth moving slower still. If the Subject sits on Tai Sui, it is within a year; on the Month General, within a month; on the Day, that same day; on the Day branch, that very hour. The Chuan Yin Feng method of judgment says: if the Subject is thriving, fix it by the day it enters tomb; if resting, it should be done on the day it is born or thriving; if hidden, look only at the day the Subject rules; if moving and held by a combination, wait for the day that clashes it.
