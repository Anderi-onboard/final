## LY.CAREER.HJC.05 仕宦·父母空亡，休望差除宣敕（旧注：父母为印绶文书诰牒宣敕，持太岁生合世爻有朝廷宣召，加月建是敕制或上司奖励；冲空化空多不实，衰静空亡无宣敕、卦无父母休望迁选）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻是印绶、文书、诰牒、宣敕、奏疏、表章，卦里不能没有它，宜旺不宜衰，扶助世爻最吉。它持太岁、有气、生合世爻，主有朝廷的宣召；再加月建，多是敕令制命和上司的奖励；最怕冲、空、化空，那就多半不实；衰弱安静又空亡，必定没有宣敕，也没有差遣任命；卦里没有父母，别指望迁升选用。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Resource is the seal and ribbon, documents, patents, imperial edicts, memorials and petitions. It cannot be missing from the hexagram, should be thriving and not weak, and supporting the Self is the most auspicious. If it holds the Tai Sui with breath and generates or combines with the Self, there will be a summons from the court; with the Month added, it is mostly an edict or a commendation from superiors; what is most feared is a clash, Void or turning into Void, and then it is mostly unreal; if weak, still and Void, there will surely be no edict and no appointment; if Resource is not in the hexagram, do not hope for promotion or selection.

This entry is about the 'documents' of an official career: with no Resource there is no appointment and no promotion.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.35 求馆·父为书馆，岂可逢空（旧注：占馆不以父母为师、当作书馆论；旺相有好馆，无父或落空必无书馆；占其年有无全要此爻出现有气；应来生合世自有人请，世生合应及父须自去访求；无父而动爻化出有人推荐，父化父有两处书馆，本宫化他宫由近及远）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占馆不把父母当作老师，要当作书馆来看。父母旺相有气，就有好书馆；卦里没有父母或者落空，必定没有书馆，事情也难成。占那一年有没有书馆，全要这一爻出现、有气不空；应爻来生合世爻，自然有人来聘请，世爻生合应爻和父爻，就必须自己去访求；卦里没有父母而动爻化出父母的，意想不到有人推荐；父化父必有两处书馆；本宫化入他宫由近及远，他宫化入本宫从外到内。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining a school post, Resource is not taken as the teacher but as the school itself. If Resource is thriving and has breath, there is a good school; if there is no Resource in the hexagram or it falls Void, there will surely be no school and the matter is hard to bring about. To divine whether there will be a school that year everything depends on this line appearing with breath and not Void; if the Other comes to generate or combine with the Self, someone naturally comes to engage one, and if the Self generates or combines with the Other and with Resource, one must go and seek on one's own; if there is no Resource in the hexagram but a moving line turns into it, someone unexpectedly recommends one; Resource turning into Resource means two schools; from the home palace into another palace is from near to far, and from another palace into the home palace is from outside to inside.

>
> **Note:** In this chapter 'Resource' is not the teacher (the teacher is the Self) but the 'school' itself (the school building, the schoolhouse). This differs from the usage in examinations and documents, where Resource is documents and the degree. 'From near to far' and 'from outside to inside' use the home palace and other palaces to see where the school is: changing within the home palace is near, and changing into another palace is far. The old commentary says more than Wang Hongxu: to divine whether there will be a school that year one looks for this line to appear; the difference between being engaged and going to seek oneself; and Resource turning into Resource meaning two schools.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.37 求馆·兄兴临应，决多同类侵谋（旧注：应持兄动必有同道争谋其馆，兄临卦身亦然；间爻动冲克世则有人破说其事难成）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占学馆，应爻持兄弟发动，必有同行的人来争谋这个馆，兄弟临卦身也是这样；如果在间爻上发动、冲克世爻，就主有人从中说坏话，事情难成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining a school post, if the Other holds Peer and it moves, there will surely be fellow teachers contending for the post, and likewise if Peer rests on the Body; if it moves on a between line and clashes or controls the Self, it means someone undermines it by talk, and the matter is hard to bring about.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.38 求馆·官如藏伏，应无督集之人（旧注：官鬼能生父母，占馆以此爻为纠率子弟之人；不出现或落空必无人聚生徒成学馆又无人推荐；空而逢冲、伏而提起须央浼其人方肯出来）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼能生扶父母，所以占馆以官鬼这一爻代表招集率领学生的人。官鬼不出现或者落空，必定没有人聚集学生来成学馆，事情难成，也没有人推荐；如果空而逢冲、伏而被提起，必须央求那个人，他才肯出来招集。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure can generate and support Resource, so in divining a school post the Pressure line stands for the person who gathers and leads the pupils. If Pressure does not appear or falls Void, there will surely be no one to gather pupils to make up a school, the matter is hard to bring about, and no one will recommend one; if it is Void and meets a clash, or is hidden and drawn up, one must beg that person before he is willing to come forward and gather them.

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Resource appears in the hexagram and the Other comes to generate or combine while the Self is Void, it is not that the school will not come about but that one does not step forward oneself, or that even if it comes about one does not go; if the Other does not generate or combine, Resource is doubled and the Self is Void, there is another school, and so this one does not come about; if the Self falls Void by itself without cause, it will surely not come about, and even if it does it is in the end not as one wishes.

> **打架**:同一个象（父母出现、应来生合、世爻空亡），旧注把原因讲清楚：是自己不肯去，或者另有别的馆；王洪绪只写「求馆不成」，没有讲这是为什么。这不是说两家判断不同，是旧注比王洪绪多了一层理由，按旧注读，「不成」不是外面不给，而是自己不去。
