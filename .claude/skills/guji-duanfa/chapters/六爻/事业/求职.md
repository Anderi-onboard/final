## LY.CAREER.EL.52 求馆开设：以世应为主、财福为凭；觅馆招贤怕世空，应空岂得遇东翁，父空书馆终非美，才陷修仪甚不丰，世应不空须有望，变成冲散定无终；世为自己、应为东家、父为书馆、子为书生、财为束修、鬼为荐馆之人、兄乃同胞之士，逢空各有所缺

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求馆（谋求教书的馆席），以世爻、应爻为主，以妻财、子孙为凭据。求馆、受聘最怕世爻落空，应爻落空就遇不到东家；父爻落空，这个馆终究不是好馆；财爻陷入空亡，束修（馆金）很不丰厚。世爻、应爻都不落空，就必定有希望；如果变成冲散，就一定做不长久。六亲各取一象：世是自己，应是东家，父是书馆，子是书生，财是束修，鬼是荐馆的人，兄是同胞之士。落空各有所缺：世空遇不到尊重、听从自己的人，应空没有东家接纳，父空难遇好馆，财空束修菲薄，子空学生不多，鬼空无人举荐，兄空无人来争，身空住处不安；其余的空还可以稍微放过，只要世应不空，终究有希望。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In seeking a school post, take the Self and the Other as the chief and Wealth and Output as the ground. To seek a post and be engaged, the Self falling Void is the thing most to be feared, and if the Other is Void one cannot meet the host; if Resource is Void the school is in the end no fine one; if Wealth is sunk into Void the fee is very meagre. If neither the Self nor the Other is Void there must be hope; if it changes into a scattering clash there will surely be no lasting end. The six kin each take one image: the Self is oneself, the Other the host, Resource the school, Output the pupils, Wealth the fee, Pressure the one who recommends a post, Peer the men of one's own kind. A Void in each leaves something lacking: if the Self is Void one meets no one who honours and follows one, if the Other is Void there is no host to take one in, if Resource is Void it is hard to meet a good post, if Wealth is Void the fee is slight, if Output is Void the pupils are few, if Pressure is Void no one recommends one, if Peer is Void no one competes, and if the Body is Void the place one lives is unsettled; the other Voids may be set aside somewhat, and if the Self and the Other are not Void there will in the end be hope.

>
> **Note:** 'Tu shuguan' means to plan for a school post (a tutor's place) and not a library as the words mean today; 'dongweng' and 'dongjia' are the master who engages a tutor, 'shuxiu' is the fee that pupils give a teacher, here the pay of the post, and 'the one who recommends one to a post' is the person who introduces one to it. The 'cai' of 'cai xian xiuyi' in the verse is presumably 'cai', Wealth, and the 'xiuti' of 'cai kong xiuti qingwei' in the explanation is presumably 'xiuyi', the fee; the translation reads them so, and the 'tui' of 'zhu kong shao ke tui' is read as 'set aside'. From 'if Peer is Void no one competes for the post' later on, 'the men of one's own kind' means those in the same line who compete for the post. The kin images here are the same set as in the Huangjin Ce on seeking a post (from HJC.29) and in the Yi Yin divination for seeking a post (YY.79): the Self is oneself, the Other the host, Resource the school, Output the pupils, Wealth the pay, Pressure the one who recommends, Peer the one who schemes to take it.

## LY.CAREER.EL.53 求馆开设：凡值六冲之卦或无官鬼定无馆也；如合处逢冲或变出六冲虽见成就其年定主不终；游魂迁改它图吉、归魂乃旧胜西东；问迁更游魂大利、占守旧反要归魂

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:凡是遇到六冲的卦，或者卦里没有官鬼，一定没有馆席；合的地方遇到冲，或者变出六冲，即使看着已经成了，那一年也一定做不到头。游魂卦，改换去别处另谋是吉；归魂卦，是守着旧馆，胜过东奔西走。所以问迁移更换的，游魂卦大为有利；占守旧馆的，反倒要归魂卦。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whenever the hexagram is a six-clash, or there is no Pressure line in it, there will surely be no post; if where there is a combination it meets a clash, or it changes out a six-clash, then even if the post seems to be settled, that year it will surely not last to the end. With a Wandering Soul hexagram, to move and change and plan for another post is auspicious; a Returning Soul hexagram is for the old post, better than going east and west. So for asking about moving to a change, a Wandering Soul is greatly favourable; for divining about keeping the old post, a Returning Soul is on the contrary what is wanted.

## LY.CAREER.EL.71 援监纳吏总纲：以官爻为主、财象为凭；援监纳吏并一应奉例纳银求官者皆把世爻与官爻为主俱不宜空，空则不成纵成不久；世值旬空非久远、福神静旺永无殃；子孙虽为福德亦不可动动则伤官，又不可无无则少利

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:援监、纳吏，以及一切依照成例交银买官的，以官爻为主，以财爻为凭据，又都把世爻和官爻当主要来看，两个都不适宜落空：落空就办不成，即使办成也不长久。世爻落在旬空，不能长久；福神（子孙）安静而旺相，永远没有祸患。子孙虽然是福德，也不可以发动，一动就伤克官爻；也不可以没有，没有就少得利益。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In purchasing a place in the Academy, purchasing a clerkship, and every case of buying office by paying silver according to the statute, take the Pressure line as the chief and the Wealth image as the ground, with the Self line and the Pressure line as the principal things, and neither should be Void: if Void it does not succeed, and even if it succeeds it does not last. If the Self falls on the ten-day Void it will not be long-lasting; if Output, the fortune spirit, is quiet and thriving there is never any calamity. Output, though it is Fortune Virtue, must not move, for if it moves it hurts the star of office; and it must not be absent, for without it there is little profit.

## LY.CAREER.EL.72 援监纳吏：鬼克身爻遭责罚、财生世象必荣昌；身世之爻若被鬼来冲克反受其殃，如得鬼爻持世再加财动来生决主名成利就

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼克制身爻，就遭责罚；财爻生世爻，就一定荣昌。身爻、世爻这两爻，如果被官鬼来冲克，反而受它的祸害；如果官鬼爻持世，再加上财爻发动来生它，一定名成利就。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure controls the Body line one suffers reproof and punishment; if Wealth generates the Self image one will surely prosper. As for the Body and Self lines, if Pressure comes to clash or control them, one suffers its calamity instead; if the Pressure line holds the Self and Wealth moves to generate it, one will surely win name and profit.

> **按**:「身世之爻」是身爻（卦身）和世爻。官鬼本来是求官的用神，克世身又要受责罚，书里要分的是：官鬼冲克世身是祸，官鬼持世而财爻来生是福。第一段解末尾的两句与这里解的第二、三句文字相同，是书里重复排印的。这一条与黄金策仕宦「鬼杀伤身」（HJC.13）取同一个关系，官鬼旺、财爻发动来生，与增删卜易「妻摇鬼旺，定然执王拜丹墀」（ZS.23）相合。
>
> **Note:** 'Shen shi zhi yao' is the Body line (the Body of the hexagram) and the Self line. Pressure is the Subject for seeking office, yet when it controls the Self or Body one suffers reproof, and what the book is distinguishing is this: Pressure clashing or controlling the Self or Body is calamity, while Pressure holding the Self with Wealth generating it is fortune. The two sentences at the end of the first explanation are the same words as the second and third sentences of the explanation here, which the book prints twice. **规则(整理,不是原文)**:参房求利，适宜财爻旺相，官鬼之象不要来克世爻；选缺求名，怕的是福德（子孙）发动，以及随着官爻入墓，恐怕遭殃。参房和选缺看法不同：参房如果官和财都旺相，所得一定不寻常，官鬼克世身，难逃罪责；选缺也要官爻兴旺，最嫌子孙发动，官鬼克世身反而不必畏忌；只是遇到随官入墓，恐怕遭灾患。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For joining a section and seeking gain, Wealth should be thriving and the Pressure image must not come to control the Self; for selection to a vacancy and seeking a name, what is dreaded is Output, the fortune virtue, moving, and following the star of office into the tomb, for fear of calamity. Judging joining a section differs from judging a selection to a vacancy: for joining a section, if the star of office and Wealth are both thriving, what is gained will surely be extraordinary, and if Pressure controls the Self or the Body one cannot escape blame; for a selection to a vacancy the star of office must also flourish and Output moving is most disliked, whereas Pressure controlling the Self or the Body need not be feared; but if one meets following the star of office into the tomb, calamity is to be feared.

## LY.CAREER.EL.117 鬻身投主总纲：以世应为主、父母为凭；命运乖违必致鬻身延岁月、年时饥馑还须投主度晨昏，要决平生之事当寻持世之爻，不破不冲百年可辅、落空落陷一载难从；鬻身者出乎不得已须择良善之主，世爻旺相生扶可以聊生，世落空亡或值日冲月破虽暂时安置终身落寞终是不久

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要卖身投靠主人（鬻身投主），以世爻、应爻为主，以父母爻为凭据。命运不顺，必定要卖身来延续岁月；年成饥荒，还得投靠主人来度过早晚。要决断一生的事，应当寻找持世的爻：不破不冲，一百年都可以得到辅助；落空落陷，一年也难以相从。卖身投主的人是出于不得已，必须选择良善的主人：世爻旺相、得到生扶，可以勉强过活；世爻落空亡，或者遇到日冲、月破，虽然是暂时安顿，毕竟一生落寞，即使勉强维持，终究不久。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In selling oneself into service under a master, take the Self and the Other as the chief and Resource as the ground. When one's fate runs against one, one must sell oneself to prolong one's days; in a famine year one must still take a master to get through morning and evening. To decide the affairs of a lifetime, one should seek the line that holds the Self: if it is neither broken nor clashed, a hundred years can be aided; if it falls to Void or into a pit, it is hard to follow even for one year. Those who sell themselves do so out of necessity and must choose a good and kind master: if the Self line is thriving and supported one can eke out a living; if the Self falls on Void, or meets the Day's clash or Month-break, then although one is temporarily settled, in the end one is desolate all one's life, and even if one makes do it will not last.

## LY.CAREER.YM.95 道业·择师、受徒传法与云游：择师同延师占——父临日月为名师、世空恐吾不能受教；受徒传法同继嗣——子逢生旺为法器、命墓恐彼不能传持；云游同出行而有访道求利之别

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:所以择师，同（延）师占：父临日辰、月建，是名师；世空，恐怕我不能受教。收徒传法，同继嗣占：子逢生旺，是法器（可造之材）；命墓，恐怕他不能传持（继承）。云游同出行占，但有访道和求利的区别，各自兼用相应的象。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** So choosing a teacher is divined like hiring a teacher: the father line on the Day or Month is a famous teacher; the Self Void, I fear I cannot be taught. Taking disciples and transmitting the teaching is divined like continuing a line of descent: the child line meeting generation and thriving is a vessel of the Dharma (a promising disciple); the life line in the tomb, I fear he cannot carry it on and transmit it. Wandering as a cloud is divined like travelling, but there is a difference between seeking the Way and seeking profit, and each also uses its corresponding image.

**《易冒·道业章第九十》原文** — 《易冒》第 1969 行 · *Yi Mao, "Chapter 90: The monastic vocation", base text; Yi Mao, line 1969*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 故择师，同师占也， | 所以择师，同（延）师占： | So choosing a teacher is divined like hiring a teacher: |
| 父临日月为名师，世空恐吾不能受教。 | 父临日辰、月建，是名师；世空，恐怕我不能受教。 | the father line on the Day or Month is a famous teacher; the Self Void, I fear I cannot be taught. |
| 受徒传法，同继嗣也， | 收徒传法，同继嗣占： | Taking disciples and transmitting the teaching is divined like continuing a line of descent: |
| 子逢生旺为法器，命墓恐彼不能传持。 | 子逢生旺，是法器；命墓，恐怕他不能传持。 | the child line meeting generation and thriving is a vessel of the Dharma; the life line in the tomb, I fear he cannot carry it on and transmit it. |

**《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)** — 《易冒》第 1970 行 · *Bracketed small note in the Yi Mao (author not named in the book); Yi Mao, line 1970*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 云游同出行， | 云游同出行占， | Wandering as a cloud is divined like travelling, |

| 各兼用象。 | 各自兼用相应的象。 | and each also uses its corresponding image. |

> **按**:「择师」是选老师（同延师占，见本书延师章），「受徒传法」是收徒弟、传授道法（同继嗣，即过继、立嗣），「云游」是僧道四处游方。「名师」是有名的好老师，「法器」是可以承载佛法、承继衣钵的人才。「命墓」是世爻（命）入墓。「传持」是传承并持守。这一条是把道业的几问分别比到世俗已有的占法上去：择师比延师，收徒比继嗣，云游比出行。比到别的占法，就用那一边的断法。
>
> **Note:** 'Zeshi' is choosing a teacher (divined like hiring a teacher, see the chapter on hiring a teacher in this book), 'shoutu chuanfa' is taking disciples and handing on the teaching (like continuing a line of descent, that is adoption and establishing an heir), and 'yunyou' is a monk or Daoist wandering from place to place. 'Mingshi' is a famous good teacher, and 'faqi' is a person who can carry the Dharma and inherit the robe and bowl. 'The life line in the tomb' is the Self line (the life) entering the tomb. 'Chuanchi' is handing on and keeping. This entry matches each question of the monastic vocation to a divination already known in the worldly sphere: choosing a teacher to hiring a teacher, taking disciples to continuing a line of descent, wandering to travelling. Once matched to another divination, the judgments of that side are used.

## LY.CAREER.YY.79 觅馆占·六亲取象：世为己、应为东主，父为书馆、子为生徒、财为束修、鬼为荐人、兄为谋夺之人；世应父空绝无馆，父值墓胎无好馆，财空墓绝胎修薄，子空墓绝胎徒少，鬼空墓绝胎无荐人，兄空墓绝胎无谋夺人

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:觅馆（找教书的馆），六亲的取象：世爻是自己，应爻是东家；父爻是书馆，子孙是学生，财爻是束修（馆金），官鬼是推荐人，兄弟是谋夺（这个馆）的人。世爻、应爻、父爻落空、绝的，没有馆；父爻值墓、胎，没有好馆；财爻空、墓、绝、胎的，束修菲薄；子孙空、墓、绝、胎的，学生少；官鬼空、墓、绝、胎的，没有推荐人；兄弟空、墓、绝、胎的，没有人来谋夺。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Seeking a teaching post, the images of the six kin. The Self is oneself and the Other is the host; the Resource line is the schoolroom, Output the pupils, Wealth the fee, Pressure the one who recommends, and Peer the one who schemes to take the post. If the Self, the Other and the Resource line are Void or extinct, there is no post; if Resource meets tomb or womb, there is no good post; if Wealth is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, the fee is slight; if Output is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, the pupils are few; if Pressure is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, there is no one to recommend; if Peer is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, there is no one to scheme for the post.

**《易隐·附觅馆占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2518 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Appendix: seeking a teaching post", base text; Yi Yin, line 2518*

'Dongxiu' is by the letter the fee given by the host.

## LY.CAREER.YY.80 觅馆占·卦象与进退：游魂宜别图，归魂宜仍旧；六冲无馆、六合有馆，合逢冲被人破，合变冲初允终拒；官加贵三合世应贵人荐；兄动克世有人争夺；财动化兄荐人分修；世动化鬼央人转荐；鬼动化兄先索谢仪；子合逢冲聚而复散；子衰得助徒少后增

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:觅馆，卦象和进退：游魂卦，宜另作打算；归魂卦，宜仍留旧馆；六冲卦没有馆，六合卦有馆；六合卦遇冲的，被人破坏；六合变六冲的，起初应承、最后拒绝。官星加贵人三合世爻、应爻的，有贵人推荐；兄弟发动克世爻的，有人争夺；财爻发动变兄弟的，推荐的人要分走束修；世爻发动变官鬼的，要央求别人转荐；官鬼发动变兄弟的，推荐人先索要谢礼；子孙爻合而遇冲的，学生聚了又散；子孙衰弱而得到帮助的，学生起初少、后来增多。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Seeking a teaching post, the hexagram and advance or retreat. In a Wandering Soul hexagram it is fit to look elsewhere; in a Returning Soul hexagram, to stay in the old post; a six-clash hexagram has no post and a six-combine hexagram has one; a six-combine meeting a clash is broken by someone; a six-combine changing into a clash is agreed at first and refused in the end. If the star of office with the Noble forms a three-harmony with the Self and Other, a patron recommends; if Peer moves to control the Self, someone contends for the post; if Wealth moves and turns into Peer, the one who recommends takes a share of the fee; if the Self moves and turns into Pressure, one must ask another to pass it on; if Pressure moves and turns into Peer, the recommender first demands a gift of thanks; if Output combines and meets a clash, the pupils gather and then scatter; if Output is weak but helped, the pupils are few at first and grow later.
