# 六爻 · 事业 · 求职

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Career and office · Finding work: a tutor's post, an apprenticeship, hiring*

2 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.CAREER.YY.79 觅馆占·六亲取象：世为己、应为东主，父为书馆、子为生徒、财为束修、鬼为荐人、兄为谋夺之人；世应父空绝无馆，父值墓胎无好馆，财空墓绝胎修薄，子空墓绝胎徒少，鬼空墓绝胎无荐人，兄空墓绝胎无谋夺人

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:觅馆（找教书的馆），六亲的取象：世爻是自己，应爻是东家；父爻是书馆，子孙是学生，财爻是束修（馆金），官鬼是推荐人，兄弟是谋夺（这个馆）的人。世爻、应爻、父爻落空、绝的，没有馆；父爻值墓、胎，没有好馆；财爻空、墓、绝、胎的，束修菲薄；子孙空、墓、绝、胎的，学生少；官鬼空、墓、绝、胎的，没有推荐人；兄弟空、墓、绝、胎的，没有人来谋夺。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Seeking a teaching post, the images of the six kin. The Self is oneself and the Other is the host; the Resource line is the schoolroom, Output the pupils, Wealth the fee, Pressure the one who recommends, and Peer the one who schemes to take the post. If the Self, the Other and the Resource line are Void or extinct, there is no post; if Resource meets tomb or womb, there is no good post; if Wealth is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, the fee is slight; if Output is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, the pupils are few; if Pressure is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, there is no one to recommend; if Peer is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, there is no one to scheme for the post.

> **按**:「修」「脩」都是束脩，即教书先生的酬金。这一节的取象和黄金策求馆章（HJC.29–38）几乎相同，只是黄金策以世爻为西席、应爻为东家，这里世爻是「己」。「东修」按字面是东家给的束修。
>
> **Note:** 'Xiu' and 'xiu' are both the tuition fee, the teacher's pay. The images in this section are nearly the same as those in the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a teaching post (HJC.29-38), the difference being that the Huangjin Ce takes the Self as the teacher's seat and the Other as the host, while here the Self is 'oneself'. 'Dongxiu' is by the letter the fee given by the host.

## LY.CAREER.YY.80 觅馆占·卦象与进退：游魂宜别图，归魂宜仍旧；六冲无馆、六合有馆，合逢冲被人破，合变冲初允终拒；官加贵三合世应贵人荐；兄动克世有人争夺；财动化兄荐人分修；世动化鬼央人转荐；鬼动化兄先索谢仪；子合逢冲聚而复散；子衰得助徒少后增

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:觅馆，卦象和进退：游魂卦，宜另作打算；归魂卦，宜仍留旧馆；六冲卦没有馆，六合卦有馆；六合卦遇冲的，被人破坏；六合变六冲的，起初应承、最后拒绝。官星加贵人三合世爻、应爻的，有贵人推荐；兄弟发动克世爻的，有人争夺；财爻发动变兄弟的，推荐的人要分走束修；世爻发动变官鬼的，要央求别人转荐；官鬼发动变兄弟的，推荐人先索要谢礼；子孙爻合而遇冲的，学生聚了又散；子孙衰弱而得到帮助的，学生起初少、后来增多。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Seeking a teaching post, the hexagram and advance or retreat. In a Wandering Soul hexagram it is fit to look elsewhere; in a Returning Soul hexagram, to stay in the old post; a six-clash hexagram has no post and a six-combine hexagram has one; a six-combine meeting a clash is broken by someone; a six-combine changing into a clash is agreed at first and refused in the end. If the star of office with the Noble forms a three-harmony with the Self and Other, a patron recommends; if Peer moves to control the Self, someone contends for the post; if Wealth moves and turns into Peer, the one who recommends takes a share of the fee; if the Self moves and turns into Pressure, one must ask another to pass it on; if Pressure moves and turns into Peer, the recommender first demands a gift of thanks; if Output combines and meets a clash, the pupils gather and then scatter; if Output is weak but helped, the pupils are few at first and grow later.

> **按**:第五句「合变冲者，初尤而终拒也」的「尤」疑是「允」，译文按「起初应承」译。「凂人」是央求别人。「合」在这里指六合卦，「合逢冲」是六合卦被日月或动爻冲，「合变冲」是六合卦变成六冲卦。「官加贵三合世应」按字面是官星加贵人，与世、应形成三合。
>
> **Note:** In the fifth sentence the 'you' of 'chu you er zhong ju' is presumably 'yun', agree, and the translation reads 'agreed at first'. 'Mei ren' is begging another. 'Combine' here means a six-combine hexagram: 'combine meeting a clash' is a six-combine hexagram clashed by the Month, Day or a moving line, and 'combine changing into a clash' is a six-combine turning into a six-clash. 'The star of office with the Noble in a three-harmony with the Self and Other' is by the letter the star of office carrying the Noble and forming a three-harmony with the Self and Other.
