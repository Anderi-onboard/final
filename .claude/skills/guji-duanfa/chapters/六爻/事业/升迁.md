## LY.CAREER.HJC.06 仕宦·官爻隐伏，莫思爵位升迁（旧注：官爻临持身世或生合世爻不受月日冲克必称意；不上卦、落空亡、墓绝无气、受克制皆不如意，世身冲克亦凶）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官爻是占官的象。它临持身世，或者来生合世爻，又不受月建、日辰冲克，凡有谋求必然称心；如果不上卦，或者落空亡，或者虽然出现却墓绝无气、受克制，都不如意；世爻、身爻被冲克，也凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure is the image in divining an office. If it settles on the Body or the Self, or comes to generate or combine with the Self, and is not clashed or controlled by the Month or the Day, whatever is sought will surely turn out to one's liking; if it is absent from the hexagram or falls Void, or even though it appears is in a tomb or extinction and without breath, or controlled, nothing will be as one wishes; a clash or control on the Self or the Body is also ominous.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7677 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7677*

> **按**:这一条是「占官」的基本判据：官爻（官鬼）是用神，看它在不在、有没有气、被不被冲克，再看它和世身的关系。旧注把不如意的几种情形都列出来（不上卦、落空亡、墓绝无气、受克制），王洪绪只写了吉的一半。
>
> **Note:** This entry is the basic criterion of 'divining an office': the Pressure line is the Subject, and one sees whether it is present, whether it has breath, whether it is clashed or controlled, and then its relation to the Self and the Body. The old commentary lists the ways it can go wrong (absent from the hexagram, Void, in a tomb or extinction without breath, controlled), and Wang Hongxu writes only the auspicious half.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.08 升迁何方·官金而应西土，木官必应东方，水北火南，土升中土（野鹤：此古法也，验过的有出入：木官应山东亦有应广东，金官应山西亦有江西、广西，又有以住处分东西南北）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:按官星的五行断去哪个方位上任：金官应西，木官应东，水应北，火应南，土官升到中土。野鹤说这是古法，他验证过的有出入：木官应在山东，也有应在广东的；金官应在山西，也有应在江西、广西的；还有按所住的地方来分东西南北的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To judge in which direction the post lies from the element of the star of office: a Metal one is due in the west, a Wood one in the east, Water in the north, Fire in the south, and an Earth one is promoted to the central land. The Old Man of Yehe says this is the old method and that what he has confirmed does not quite agree: a Wood star of office is due in Shandong, but sometimes in Guangdong; a Metal one in Shanxi, but sometimes in Jiangxi or Guangxi; and sometimes the directions are divided according to the place where one lives.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.09 升迁何方·初爻为内地，五六为边方（野鹤：世与官星都在初爻为应内地；世在五、官在六，或相反，或官星持世同在五六爻为应边方；在二三四爻仍以五行决之）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看是内地还是边远：初爻代表内地，五爻、六爻代表边远地方。野鹤解释：「应在初爻」是世爻和官星都在初爻；「应在五、六爻」是世在五爻官在六爻，或世在六爻官在五爻，或官星持世、一起在五、六爻上；如果在二、三、四爻，仍然按五行来决断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To tell whether the post is inland or on the frontier: the first line is the inner land and the fifth and sixth lines are the frontier. The Old Man of Yehe explains: 'due in the first line' means the Self and the star of office are both in the first line; 'due in the fifth or sixth line' means the Self is in the fifth line and the star of office in the sixth, or the Self in the sixth and the star of office in the fifth, or the star of office holds the Self and both are in the fifth or sixth line; if they are in the second, third or fourth line, one still decides by the five elements.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.10 升迁何方·野鹤：问地方不如指其缺而问（此缺我得否？我得词林、部属、科道、正印否？官星持世、官动生合世、三合官局生世皆必得；世空世破、官破官空、子孙持世或发动、官临应爻、官局生应皆不得）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占升选，与其问「升到哪个方向」，不如指明具体的缺来问：这个缺我能得吗？我能进翰林吗？我能做部属吗？我能做科道官吗？我能做正印官吗？官星持世、官星发动生合世爻、三合官局生世，都主必得；世爻落空落破、官星落破落空、子孙持世、子孙发动、官星临在应爻上、三合官局生应爻，都主得不到。指明了缺就已经知道在哪里，不必多此一举。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a question about promotion, rather than asking 'in which direction', it is better to name the post and ask: can I get this post? Can I enter the Hanlin Academy? Can I get a post in a ministry? Can I become a supervising censor? Can I become the chief official holding the seal? If the star of office holds the Self, or the star of office moves to generate or combine with the Self, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates the Self, it will surely be had; if the Self is Void or broken, the star of office is broken or Void, Output holds the Self, Output moves, the star of office sits on the Other, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates the Other, it will not be had. Once the post is named its place is already known, and the extra step is unnecessary.

**野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 10532–10534 行 · *The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, lines 10532–10534*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 野鹤曰：大概问其地方，虽则有验，然不如指其地而问之，此缺我得否？ | 野鹤说：大致问是哪个地方，虽然也有应验，但不如指明那个地方来问：这个缺我能得吗？ | The Old Man of Yehe says: asking in general which place does sometimes prove right, but it is better to name the place and ask: can I get this post? |
| 我得词林否？ | 我能进翰林院吗？ | Can I enter the Hanlin Academy? |
| 我得部属否？ | 我能做部属吗？ | Can I get a post in one of the ministries? |

| 如官星持世者、官动生合世爻者、三合官局而生世者，皆主必得； | 像官星持世的、官星发动生合世爻的、三合成官局而生世的，都主必得； | Where the star of office holds the Self, or the star of office moves to generate or combine with the Self, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates the Self, all mean it will surely be had; |
| 世空世破、官破官空、子孙持世、子孙发动、官临应爻、三合官局而生应爻，皆主不得。 | 世爻落空落破、官星落破落空、子孙持世、子孙发动、官星临在应爻上、三合成官局而生应爻的，都主得不到。 | where the Self is Void or broken, the star of office is broken or Void, Output holds the Self, Output moves, the star of office sits on the Other, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates the Other, all mean it will not be had. |
| 野鹤曰：指其缺而问之，不失为便捷之法。 | 野鹤说：指明那个缺来问，不失为一个简便快捷的办法。 | The Old Man of Yehe says: naming the post and asking is not a bad quick method. |
| 指其缺即已知其缺在何方，而不必多此一举也。 | 指明了那个缺，就已经知道那个缺在哪个方位，不必再多此一举。 | Once the post is named the place of the post is already known, and there is no need for the extra step. |

> **按**:词林、部属、科道、正印是清朝官的类别：词林是翰林院，部属是各部的属官，科道是六科给事中和各道监察御史，正印是主官（掌印的正职）。书里没有逐个解释，这里是按通行的意思注的。原文这一段最后还有一句「今人测占工作调动，职务升迁何方亦可仿此」，是今人加的话，不收。
>
> **Note:** Cilin, bushu, kedao and zhengyin are categories of official in the Qing: the Hanlin Academy, the subordinate officials of the ministries, the supervising secretaries and investigating censors, and the chief officials who hold the seal. The book does not explain them one by one; this is the usual sense. The paragraph ends in the text with a sentence 'for people today who divine job transfers and the direction of promotion the same method may be followed', which is a modern editor's addition and is not quoted.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.15 在任吉凶·化进神，冲变合，加官晋爵（世旺官化进神、官旺世化进神、世官得地财化进神，皆进爵之征）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:在任时，变出进神，或冲变成合，都是加官晋爵的征兆：世爻旺相、官星变进神；官星旺相、世爻变进神；世爻与官星都得地、财爻变进神。

## LY.CAREER.ZS.54 功名到何品级·水一、火二、木三、金四、土五，官临火者二品，官星属水一品之尊（野鹤：古法不够用，改用指占：我能公侯将相否、一品否、鼎甲否；旺官临日月指日可期，官破刑冲终身失望，子孙持世止渴望梅，官动生身探囊取物；书中例：小过官至佥事，节之蹇世变辰官应辰年鼎甲）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:断官能做到几品：古法按五行的成数，水一、火二、木三、金四、土五，官星临在火上官位二品，官星属水是一品的尊位。野鹤说这个古法不够用：水只有一个数，官位到公侯的怎么确定？官分九品，数只到五，六品、七品的怎么确定？他的办法是指明了去占：我能做公侯将相吗？我能做一品大员吗？我能中鼎甲吗？旺相的官星临日月，指日可待；官星被破或被刑冲，终身失望；子孙持世，就像望梅止渴；官星发动生身，恰似探囊取物。问鼎甲这类具体的事，要另占一卦指明了问：古人以蹇卦为不吉，他只看用神，世临巳火、卯月生申日合、青龙持世，中鼎甲无疑；世爻变出辰土之官，应在辰年，后来果然在甲辰科殿试得传胪第一。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To judge to what grade an official will rise: the old method uses the numbers of the five elements, Water one, Fire two, Wood three, Metal four, Earth five, so that a star of office on Fire gives the second grade and a Water star of office the honoured first grade. The Old Man of Yehe says this old method is not enough: Water has only the number one, so how is the rank of dukes and marquises to be fixed? There are nine grades of office and the numbers stop at five, so how are the sixth and seventh to be fixed? His method is to name the matter and divine: can I become a duke, marquis, general or chancellor? Can I become a first-grade minister? Can I take a place among the top three? A thriving star of office on the Day or Month can be expected any day; if the star of office is broken, punished or clashed, one is disappointed for life; if Output holds the Self, it is like quenching thirst by gazing at plums; if the star of office moves to generate the Body, it is as easy as taking something from a bag. For a concrete matter such as the top three, another hexagram is cast with the matter named: the ancients took Jian as ominous, and he looked only at the Subject, where the Self on Si Fire generated by the Mao month and combined by the Shen day with the Azure Dragon holding the Self left no doubt of a top place; the Self turned into the Chen Earth star of office, so it fell in a Chen year, and at the palace examination of the Jiachen session he did come first at the announcement.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.56 子占父功名·李我平：问何时问何地须分占，据一卦兼断时地之谬；八宫、二十八宿、禄定方、游魂远归魂近，《易冒》以五行六神八宫为经诸星为纬，可说不可行

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占升迁，问什么时候和问什么地方，必须分开占；凭一个卦兼着断，假如官星是申金，必定七月升迁，按分野申是西方晋地，难道七月升迁的缺就一定在西方？这是古法的荒谬。又有按八宫来定的（如离南坎北），有用二十八宿定分野的，有用禄来定方位的，有说游魂卦远、归魂卦近的，都靠不住。《易冒》以五行、六神、八宫为经，众星为纬，可以讲但不能行：五行、八宫、二十八宿还算近理，如果用六神再加星煞，丁日占得恒卦，酉金官星持世，必掌兵权；六神得螣蛇，螣蛇是奔走差遣的吏；丁日以酉为文昌，文昌是翰林院；按这个方法断，就成了又掌兵权、又在翰林、又听差遣的人了，岂不是胡说。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining about promotion, when it comes and where it is must be divined separately; judging both from one hexagram, suppose the star of office is Shen Metal, one would be promoted in the seventh month, and by the division of regions Shen is the land of Jin in the west, but does a promotion in the seventh month have to be a post in the west? This is the absurdity of the old method. Then there is fixing it by the eight palaces (such as Li south and Kan north), or by the twenty-eight lunar mansions, or by the Emolument, or saying a Wandering Soul hexagram is far and a Returning Soul hexagram near, none of which can be relied on. The Yi Mao takes the five elements, the six spirits and the eight palaces as the warp and the stars as the weft, which can be talked about but cannot be practised: the five elements, the eight palaces and the twenty-eight mansions are rather more reasonable, but if the six spirits are joined with the stars and baleful spirits, then on a Ding day with Heng and the You Metal star of office holding the Self he must hold military power; the spirit is the Coiling Snake, an official who runs on commissions; on a Ding day You is the Wenchang star, which is the Hanlin Academy; by this method he would be a man who holds military power, sits in the Hanlin and awaits commissions all at once, and is that not idle talk?
