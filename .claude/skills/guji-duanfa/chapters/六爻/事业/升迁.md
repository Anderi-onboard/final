## LY.CAREER.BSQS.10 断易总论·月建临于用爻，求官必定迁高职

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:月建落在用爻上，求官一定升到高的职位。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Month falls on the Subject line, one seeking office will surely be promoted to a high post.

**《卜筮全书·通玄妙论·八、断易总论》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 2890 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 8: general discussion of Duanyi", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 2890*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 月建临于用爻， | 月建落在用爻上， | When the Month falls on the Subject line, |
| 求官必定迁高职。 | 求官一定升到高的职位。 | one seeking office will surely be promoted to a high post. |

> **按**:「用爻」就是用神，求官时是官鬼；月建临它就是官鬼临月建（旺而有力）。「迁高职」是升到更高的职位。这一行的下一句「月破临于用爻，作事无成病不痊」放在疾病域（LY.ILL.BSQS.22）。仕宦章里「官鬼临月建」的说法见 LY.CAREER.HJC 各条和阐奥歌章。
>
> **Note:** The "Subject line" is the Subject, which in seeking office is the Pressure line; the Month falling on it means Pressure standing on the Month, strong and powerful. "Promoted to a high post" means rising to a higher office. The next line, "if the Month-break falls on the Subject line, nothing done comes to completion and illness is not cured", is in the illness domain (LY.ILL.BSQS.22).

## LY.CAREER.CA.14 官鬼临唐符国印最佳，可作朝中辅翼；符印二爻发动，诏书立至；宜静而动必升迁，宜动而静多掩抑

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼落在唐符、国印上最好，日后可在朝中做辅佐；唐符、国印这两爻在上卦发动，一定符印到手，不久随诏书起用；「宜静而动必升迁，宜动而静多掩抑」。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure standing on the Tangfu or the State Seal is the best, one who will serve as a support at court; when these two lines move in the upper trigram the tally and seal are surely in hand and one will soon be raised by imperial edict; 'what should be still but moves will surely rise; what should move but stays still is mostly held down'.

| 鬼临符印最为佳， | 官鬼落在唐符、国印上，最好， | Pressure standing on the Tangfu or the State Seal is the best, |
| 当作朝中资辅翼。 | 可以在朝中做辅佐。 | one who will serve as a support at court. |
| 宜静而动必升迁， | 该静的偏动，必定升迁； | What should be still but moves will surely rise in post; |
| 宜动而静多掩抑。 | 该动的偏静，多半被压住。 | what should move but stays still is mostly held down. |

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

## LY.CAREER.CA.20 再得雷火杀重重（逢动）：大吉，名位将至王公；雷火杀有两种起法，旧注并存

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里再得雷火杀、重重叠叠，又逢发动，是大吉的征兆，眼看名位将到王公。旧注给了雷火杀的起法，但前后有两种说法并存。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the hexagram further gets the Thunder-Fire baleful spirit, layer upon layer, and it moves, it is a sign of great fortune, and soon rank and position reach prince and duke. The note gives the method for finding the Thunder-Fire spirit, but two accounts of it stand side by side.

## LY.CAREER.DYTJ.02 丘寺丞易鉴歌（一）：求官鬼吏克身为贤官；驿马扶身官职迁；坎离宜冬三月、震巽应申酉年；世应与驿马相合吉；坎咸复卦主狐疑；申酉辰戌持世吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求官时，官鬼落在吏位（初爻）来克身，是贤官之象；驿马相扶，官职迁动；坎、离二卦宜在冬天三个月，震、巽二卦应在申、酉年；世、应和驿马相合是吉；坎、咸、复三卦主狐疑；申、酉、辰、戌持世是吉，做官又遇到禄，好施展作为。

## LY.CAREER.ZSZ.13 儒业章·功名仕途：青龙持世功名大、天喜同官道业亨、朱雀并财宜作馆；世应和身旺上书献策必成、日辰龙德生旺县宰州官力主盟；禄马贵人临月建公侯任责；朝君忌屯蹇、面圣宜晋升、天符持木高迁重任禄光荣

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:青龙持世，功名大；天喜同官，道业亨通；朱雀并财，宜开馆；勾陈同鬼，不要出游。世、应既和睦、身又旺，上书献策必定能成；日辰、龙德都得生旺，县令、州官会出力相助。禄、马、贵人落在月建上，公侯担任重责，官职不轻；天符值土，应当没有阻碍；月建持金，必有惊恐。父母最宜逢六合，世、身切忌犯三刑；刑、杀克身又克世，会陷身受辱、耽误一生。朝见君主，不可遇上屯、蹇；面圣偏适宜见到晋、升；更何况天符持木德，高升、担重任，俸禄光荣。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With the Azure Dragon holding the Self, fame and rank are great; with the Joy star together with the office, the Way and its work go through; the Vermilion Bird joined to Wealth suits opening a school; Hook Earth with Pressure, do not go travelling about. With the Self and the Other in accord and the Body strong as well, submitting a memorial or a plan is sure to be accomplished; with the Day and Dragon Virtue both born and strong, the county magistrate and prefect will lend their strength. With the Emolument, the Horse and the Noble standing on the Month, dukes and marquises bear heavy charge and the post is weighty; with the Year Tally on Earth there is probably no hindrance; with the Month holding Metal there is sure to be fright. Resource is best meeting the six combinations, and the Self and the Body must avoid the three punishments; punishment and baleful spirits controlling the Body and the Self, one falls into disgrace and ruins one's life. To attend the sovereign one must not meet Zhun or Jian; to face the sage ruler it suits meeting Jin or Sheng; and moreover the Year Tally holding Wood virtue, there is high promotion, heavy charge and glorious emolument.
