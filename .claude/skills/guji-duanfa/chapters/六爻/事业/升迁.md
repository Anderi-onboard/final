## LY.CAREER.EL.55 仕宦升迁：以官爻为主、世象为凭；官职升迁子莫刚，唯求官鬼动为良，值生值旺当迁转，临陷临空且守常；子乃忌神不宜发动，官为主象最利交重，若临生旺定主高升，如值空亡未能迁转

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占官职升迁，以官爻为主，以世爻为凭据。子孙是官鬼的忌神，不宜刚旺发动；只求官鬼发动才好。官鬼得生、得旺，就该迁转升调；官鬼落陷、落空，就暂且守着原职、照常。官鬼是主象，最有利的是它发动；如果临生旺，一定主高升；如果遇到空亡，就不能迁转。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining promotion in office, take the Pressure line as the chief and the Self image as the ground. Output is the Adversary of Pressure and must not be strong or move; one asks only that Pressure move, which is good. If Pressure is generated or thriving, one should expect to be promoted and transferred; if Pressure is sunk or Void, for the time being one keeps to the usual and holds one's post. Pressure is the chief image and is most favoured when it moves; if it meets generation or thriving, it surely means a high promotion; if it meets Void, there will be no promotion or transfer.

>
> **Note:** In this chapter 'sheng', to rise, is written 'bi' ('gao bi', 'ping bi', 'bi qian' and so on), which is presumably a miswriting of 'sheng', and the translation reads 'promotion' throughout. The 'gang' of 'zi mo gang' is strong and moving with force. 'Jiaozhong' are the marks of moving lines in a hexagram ('zhong' is old yang and 'jiao' old yin, both lines that change), and here mean a line moving in general. 'Qianzhuan' is the promotion and transfer of officials. The 'xian' of 'lin xian' is a fall into a bad place such as Void, tomb or extinction, and the book does not go into it. This entry takes the same relation as 'a flourishing Pressure settles one in the first rank' and 'if Output moves, beware of being stripped of office' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.02, HJC.03) and as the Zengshan Buyi on promotion and selection (ZS.01, ZS.02): Pressure is the chief and Output the Adversary.

## LY.CAREER.EL.56 仕宦升迁：鬼化子字忧调降、官连财位沐恩光；官化子孙非降即调，卦中财子动亦然，若还财子同兴转助官爻又不降调，官若化财平升品级，官化进气官爻又主重超美职

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼化成子孙，就要担忧被调、被降；官鬼连着财位（化成财），就沐浴恩光。具体说：官鬼化成子孙，不是降职就是调走，卦里财爻、子孙发动也是这样；但如果财爻、子孙一起发动，转过来帮助官爻，就又不降不调；官鬼化成财爻，是在原品级上平升；官爻化成进气的官爻，又主连升、超升到好的职位。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure changes into Output, fear being transferred or demoted; if Pressure is joined to the Wealth place, one bathes in the glow of favour. In detail: if Pressure changes into Output it is either demotion or transfer, and the same if Wealth and Output move in the hexagram; but if Wealth and Output move together and turn to aid the Pressure line, there is then no demotion or transfer; if Pressure changes into Wealth, one is raised on a level in grade; if Pressure changes into an advancing-qi Pressure line, it again means a double promotion to a fine post.

| 若还财子同兴转助官爻，又不降调，官若化财平陛品级，官化进气官爻，又主重超美职。 | 如果财爻、子孙一起发动，转过来帮助官爻，就又不降不调；官鬼如果化成财爻，是在原品级上平升；官爻化成进气的官爻，又主连升、超升到好的职位。 | If Wealth and Output move together and turn to aid the Pressure line, there is then no demotion or transfer; if Pressure changes into Wealth, one is raised on a level in grade; if Pressure changes into an advancing-qi Pressure line, it again means a double promotion to a fine post. |

> **按**:「官连财位」按解里的「官若化财」译，指官鬼变成财爻。「平陛品级」的「平」疑是在原来的品级上升，即不加品级的平升；「重超」是连升、越级升；「进气」是进神（见术语表）。财爻、子孙一起发动转助官爻就不降调：按五行相生，子孙生财、财生官，子孙的力量经财爻传给官鬼，它就不再是克官的忌神——这是据五行相生推的，书里没有明说。这一条与易隐官禄占「官化财平升」（YY.22）、增删卜易「化进神加官晋爵」（ZS.15）是同一类断语。
>
> **Note:** 'Guan lian cai wei' is translated after 'if Pressure changes into Wealth' in the explanation, and means Pressure changing into the Wealth line. The 'ping' of 'ping sheng pinji' is presumably a rise on the same grade, that is a level promotion with no grade added; 'chong chao' is repeated or over-ranked promotion; 'jin qi' is an advancing spirit (see the glossary). That Wealth and Output moving together and turning to aid Pressure means no demotion is read by the generation of the five elements: Output generates Wealth and Wealth generates Pressure, so the force of Output passes through Wealth to Pressure and Output is no longer the Adversary that controls the star of office; this is inferred from five-element generation and the book does not say it. This entry is the same kind of judgment as 'Pressure changes into Wealth, level promotion' in the Yi Yin divination for official salary and rank (YY.22) and 'changing into an advancing spirit adds office and rank' in the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.15).

## LY.CAREER.EL.57 仕宦升迁：财之福象加爵禄、妻变兄爻减俸粮；妻财为俸禄，财化子孙加衔赐禄，变成兄弟罢俸减粮

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:妻财是俸禄：财爻化成福象（子孙），就加官衔、赐俸禄；财爻变成兄爻（兄弟），就罢俸、减粮。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wealth is the salary: if Wealth changes into the fortune image, Output, rank is added and pay is granted; if Wealth changes into a Peer line, salary is stopped and grain is cut.

## LY.CAREER.EL.58 仕宦升迁：卦得归魂还复任、游魂迁转在他方；若卜归魂还当复任，归魂化归魂理应致仕，如遇游魂官又旺者必升别省，游魂化游魂升后再升远处

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦得归魂，是回来再任原职；卦得游魂，是迁转到别的地方去。具体说：卜得归魂，还会恢复原任；归魂化成归魂，按理应当退休（致仕）；遇到游魂而官又旺相的，必定升到别的省；游魂化成游魂，升了之后再升到远处。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Returning Soul hexagram means returning to the former post; a Wandering Soul means being moved and transferred elsewhere. In detail: if a Returning Soul is cast, one will return to the former post; a Returning Soul changing into a Returning Soul means that by right one should retire from office; if one meets a Wandering Soul and the star of office is again thriving, one is surely raised to another province; a Wandering Soul changing into a Wandering Soul means being promoted and then promoted again to a far place.

**《易林补遗·仕宦升迁章第二十九》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 763 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 29: Official career and promotion", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 763*

> **Note:** 'Wandering Soul' and 'Returning Soul' are the seventh and eighth hexagrams of each of the eight palaces (see the glossary). ## LY.CAREER.EL.59 仕宦升迁：世空未得高升任、财动还须佐帝皇；世爻为自身本官，自卜当察世爻、世若空亡未能迁转，他人代卜又不取世爻为主专看官爻、若得财动生官自然显达

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻是自己本人的官职：自己占卜，要察看世爻，世爻落空，就不能升迁；替别人占卜，就不取世爻为主，专看官爻，如果得到财爻发动生官爻，自然显达，能去辅佐帝王。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self line is one's own post: in divining for oneself, look at the Self line, and if the Self is Void one cannot be promoted or transferred; in divining on behalf of another, do not take the Self as the chief but look only at the Pressure line, and if Wealth moves to generate Pressure, one naturally rises to eminence and goes on to assist the sovereign.

| 世空未得高升任，财动还须佐帝皇， | 世爻落空，就得不到高升的官职；财爻发动，还要去辅佐帝王。 | If the Self is Void one does not get a high post; if Wealth moves, one will go on to assist the emperor. |

The book does not say who is divining 'on behalf of another'; by the sense it is divining another person's promotion, so the Self is no longer the person asked about and the Pressure line is looked at instead; this is a reading from the sense. This entry looks at the Self as the Huangjin Ce does in 'if the Self moves and meets Void, one does not stay long in office' (HJC.08): in divining for oneself, look at the Self.

## LY.CAREER.EL.60 仕宦升迁：鬼变退神宜致仕、化居墓绝早还乡；鬼爻若化退气或变入墓绝之中，莫望升迁，远归田里

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼变成退神，适宜退休（致仕）；变入墓、绝，就早早回乡。官鬼爻如果化成退气，或者变入墓、绝之中，就不要指望升迁，只能远远地回到乡里。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure changes into a retreating spirit it is fitting to retire from office; if it changes into tomb or extinction, one returns home early. If the Pressure line changes into a retreating qi, or changes into tomb or extinction, do not hope for promotion, but go far away and return to the countryside.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.56 子占父功名·李我平：问何时问何地须分占，据一卦兼断时地之谬；八宫、二十八宿、禄定方、游魂远归魂近，《易冒》以五行六神八宫为经诸星为纬，可说不可行

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占升迁，问什么时候和问什么地方，必须分开占；凭一个卦兼着断，假如官星是申金，必定七月升迁，按分野申是西方晋地，难道七月升迁的缺就一定在西方？这是古法的荒谬。又有按八宫来定的（如离南坎北），有用二十八宿定分野的，有用禄来定方位的，有说游魂卦远、归魂卦近的，都靠不住。《易冒》以五行、六神、八宫为经，众星为纬，可以讲但不能行：五行、八宫、二十八宿还算近理，如果用六神再加星煞，丁日占得恒卦，酉金官星持世，必掌兵权；六神得螣蛇，螣蛇是奔走差遣的吏；丁日以酉为文昌，文昌是翰林院；按这个方法断，就成了又掌兵权、又在翰林、又听差遣的人了，岂不是胡说。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining about promotion, when it comes and where it is must be divined separately; judging both from one hexagram, suppose the star of office is Shen Metal, one would be promoted in the seventh month, and by the division of regions Shen is the land of Jin in the west, but does a promotion in the seventh month have to be a post in the west? This is the absurdity of the old method. Then there is fixing it by the eight palaces (such as Li south and Kan north), or by the twenty-eight lunar mansions, or by the Emolument, or saying a Wandering Soul hexagram is far and a Returning Soul hexagram near, none of which can be relied on. The Yi Mao takes the five elements, the six spirits and the eight palaces as the warp and the stars as the weft, which can be talked about but cannot be practised: the five elements, the eight palaces and the twenty-eight mansions are rather more reasonable, but if the six spirits are joined with the stars and baleful spirits, then on a Ding day with Heng and the You Metal star of office holding the Self he must hold military power; the spirit is the Coiling Snake, an official who runs on commissions; on a Ding day You is the Wenchang star, which is the Hanlin Academy; by this method he would be a man who holds military power, sits in the Hanlin and awaits commissions all at once, and is that not idle talk?
