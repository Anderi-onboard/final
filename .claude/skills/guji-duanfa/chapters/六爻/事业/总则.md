## LY.CAREER.EL.79 官员荐奖总纲：推荐以官文为主、旌奖以财鬼为凭；官吏贤能望宪台之荐奖、黎民良善赖邑宰之旌扬；所喜者鬼旺龙兴，所忌者世空子动；凡占奖荐各要官爻旺相青龙得地，大忌世值空亡子孙发动

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占推荐，以官爻、文书爻为主；占旌奖（表彰奖励），以财爻、官鬼为凭据。官吏贤能，盼望上司宪台的荐举嘉奖；百姓善良，依靠县令的旌表褒扬。所喜欢的是官鬼旺相、青龙兴旺；所忌讳的是世爻落空、子孙发动。凡是占嘉奖、荐举，都要官爻旺相、青龙得位，最忌世爻落在空亡、子孙发动。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For a recommendation, take the Pressure line and the document line as the chief; for a commendation or award, take Wealth and Pressure as the ground. Officials who are worthy and able look to the supervising authorities for recommendation and award; common people who are good depend on the county magistrate for commendation and praise. What is welcomed is Pressure thriving and the Dragon flourishing; what is feared is the Self Void and Output moving. In every divination about award or recommendation, the Pressure line should be thriving and the Azure Dragon in its place, and what is most feared is the Self falling on Void and Output moving.

## LY.CAREER.EL.88 官员荐奖结语：上察官情之喜怒皆由四象之兴衰，下推人事之亲疏不出两仪之变化

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Observing above the pleasure or anger of the officials all depends on the rise and fall of the four images; reckoning below the closeness or distance of human affairs does not go beyond the changes of the two modes.

## LY.CAREER.EL.103 谋望成事总纲：以世应为主、内外为凭；成事须将世应查，世为本主应为他，空冲破绝临其世凡去谋为莫起牙，冲破绝空居应位他心不合枉嗟呀；凡成事体一应谋为皆取世应为主，或世或应如临绝地或犯旬空月破日冲皆难成就，次将内卦为谋事之人不宜空破，如临旺相定主亨通，再得内克外卦或内外相生比和俱为美

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:谋求成事，以世爻、应爻为主，以内卦、外卦为凭据。要成事，必须查世、应：世是本主（自己），应是他人；空、冲、破、绝临到世爻，凡是去谋求，就不要动手；冲、破、绝、空落在应位，对方的心思不合，白白叹息。凡是成事办事、一切谋求，都取世、应为主：不论世还是应，如果落在绝地，或者犯旬空、月破、日冲，都难成就。其次把内卦当作谋事的人，不适宜空、破；如果旺相，一定亨通；再得到内克外卦，或者内外相生以及比和，都是美好的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In scheming to accomplish an affair, take the Self and the Other as the chief and the inner and outer trigrams as the ground. To accomplish an affair one must examine the Self and the Other: the Self is the principal and the Other is the other party; if Void, clash, break or extinction sits on the Self, do not set about scheming; if clash, break, extinction or Void is in the Other's place, the other party's mind does not agree and one sighs in vain. In every business and every scheme, take the Self and the Other as the chief: if either the Self or the Other falls on a place of extinction, or runs into the ten-day Void, Month-break or the Day's clash, it is hard to accomplish. Next, take the inner trigram as the one who schemes the affair, which should not be Void or broken; if it meets thriving it surely means success; if in addition the inner controls the outer trigram, or the inner and outer generate each other or are in accord, all is fine.

**《易林补遗·谋望成事章第三十九》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 884 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 39: Scheming and accomplishing affairs", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 884*

## LY.CAREER.HJC.01 仕宦·旺相妻财，必得千钟之粟（旧注：未仕不要财爻、已仕要见财爻；财旺俸禄多，财空财伏未得俸禄，财动逢冲减俸，冲财刑世官有停俸罢职之患）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占做官，妻财就是俸禄。还没做官而求功名的人，不要财爻；已经做官的人，要见财爻，因为有爵位就有俸禄，没有没俸禄就能做官的。妻财旺相，俸禄一定多；休囚就微薄；卦里没有财或财落空亡，还没有俸禄；财动遇冲，任职以后会因事减俸；日辰月建冲财、又刑害世爻和官爻，恐怕有停发俸禄、罢职的祸患。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining an office, Wealth is the salary. For one not yet in office who is seeking a name, Wealth is not wanted; for one already in office, Wealth is wanted, since where there is rank there is a stipend, and no one gets an office with no pay. When Wealth is thriving the salary is surely large; resting or trapped, it is meagre; if there is no Wealth in the hexagram or it falls Void, no salary has yet been received; if Wealth moves and meets a clash, the salary is cut after taking up the post, on account of some matter; and if the Day or the Month clashes Wealth while also punishing or harming the Self and Pressure, there may be the calamity of salary being stopped and dismissal from office.

A 'zhong' is an ancient measure of grain, and 'a thousand zhong' means a large salary. 'For one not yet in office who is seeking a name, Wealth is not wanted' says that for someone not yet in office seeking a name, Wealth on the contrary is not wanted (Wealth controls Resource and harms the documents), the same reasoning as 'if Wealth moves heavily, do not hope to be chosen for the blue coins' in the chapter on seeking a name of this book (that chapter belongs to the domain of examinations); for one already in office there must be Wealth, since an office carries a stipend. 'The salary that sustains' is living and keeping a household on the stipend. The Bushi Zhengzong runs the comment on the couplet 'with Pressure flourishing' together with the comment on this couplet, so the last two sentences of Wang Hongxu's passage (Pressure thriving; moving to generate or combine with the Self) are placed in LY.CAREER.HJC.02.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.02 仕宦·兴隆官鬼，定居一品之尊（旧注：鬼为官爵，旺相官高爵大、休囚死绝官小职卑；发动生合世爻、得月日生扶必有升擢；安静被克害无声名）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼代表官职和爵位：旺相有气，官高爵大；休囚死绝，官小职卑；发动去生合世爻，或者得到月建、日辰的生扶，一定有升迁提拔，名声远播京城；安静不动而被月建、日辰克害，或者被世爻刑冲克害，一定没有声名威望。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure stands for office and rank: thriving and with breath, the office is high and the rank great; resting, trapped, dead or in extinction, the office is small and the post low; moving to generate or combine with the Self, or generated and supported by the Month or the Day, there will surely be promotion and the name spreads far to the capital; still, and controlled or harmed by the Month or the Day, or punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Self, there will surely be no fame or standing.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.01 升选候补·官爵荣身，须宜持世；养廉财禄，最喜生身（旺官持世，日月动爻作官星生合世爻，或世动化官生世，皆吉；财动生官得美缺，官临日月必超迁）

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a question about promotion or appointment the star of office should be thriving and hold the Self, and Wealth should generate the Body: a thriving star of office holding the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acting as the star of office to generate or combine with the Self, or the Self moving and turning into the star of office to generate the Self, are all auspicious; Wealth moving to generate the star of office wins a fine post; the star of office on the Day or Month means promotion beyond one's rank.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.07 升选候补·野鹤：古以晋、升为吉，屯、蹇为凶，晋升也要世爻得地，屯蹇世旺无妨

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看升选不能只看卦名：古人以晋、升两卦为吉，屯、蹇两卦为凶；野鹤说晋、升两卦确实屡次应验，但也要世爻得地，世爻失陷也不吉；屯、蹇两卦，只要世爻旺相就没有妨碍，他有一次世爻旺相占到屯卦，照样越级升迁。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Promotion cannot be read from the hexagram name alone: the ancients took Jin and Sheng as auspicious and Zhun and Jian as ominous; the Old Man of Yehe says Jin and Sheng have indeed proved right many times, but the Self must also be well placed, and a Self that is lost or sunk is not auspicious; for Zhun and Jian there is no harm so long as the Self is thriving, and once he got Zhun with a thriving Self and was promoted beyond rank all the same.
