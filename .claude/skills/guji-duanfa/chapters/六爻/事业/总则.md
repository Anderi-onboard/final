## LY.CAREER.CA.26 羽士缁流占：身安世静最先；身爻克世可谋用、世应比和自然乐；世克身当退守、财克世好求缘；六爻不动为清福、一位交重事变迁

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:道士、僧人来问占：身安稳、世安静最要紧；身爻克世，可以去谋划；世、应比和，自然安乐；世去克身，应当退守；财来克世，适合去求缘；六爻不动是清福；有一位交重发动，事情就有变迁。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For Taoist priests and monks asking by divination: the Body at ease and the Self still comes first; the Body line controlling the Self, one may plan and act; Self and Other in accord, ease comes of itself; the Self controlling the Body, one should withdraw and hold; Wealth coming to control the Self, it is good to seek alms; the six lines still is pure blessing; one line moving means change in the matter.

## LY.CAREER.EL.79 官员荐奖总纲：推荐以官文为主、旌奖以财鬼为凭；官吏贤能望宪台之荐奖、黎民良善赖邑宰之旌扬；所喜者鬼旺龙兴，所忌者世空子动；凡占奖荐各要官爻旺相青龙得地，大忌世值空亡子孙发动

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占推荐，以官爻、文书爻为主；占旌奖（表彰奖励），以财爻、官鬼为凭据。官吏贤能，盼望上司宪台的荐举嘉奖；百姓善良，依靠县令的旌表褒扬。所喜欢的是官鬼旺相、青龙兴旺；所忌讳的是世爻落空、子孙发动。凡是占嘉奖、荐举，都要官爻旺相、青龙得位，最忌世爻落在空亡、子孙发动。

## LY.CAREER.EL.103 谋望成事总纲：以世应为主、内外为凭；成事须将世应查，世为本主应为他，空冲破绝临其世凡去谋为莫起牙，冲破绝空居应位他心不合枉嗟呀；凡成事体一应谋为皆取世应为主，或世或应如临绝地或犯旬空月破日冲皆难成就，次将内卦为谋事之人不宜空破，如临旺相定主亨通，再得内克外卦或内外相生比和俱为美

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:谋求成事，以世爻、应爻为主，以内卦、外卦为凭据。要成事，必须查世、应：世是本主（自己），应是他人；空、冲、破、绝临到世爻，凡是去谋求，就不要动手；冲、破、绝、空落在应位，对方的心思不合，白白叹息。凡是成事办事、一切谋求，都取世、应为主：不论世还是应，如果落在绝地，或者犯旬空、月破、日冲，都难成就。其次把内卦当作谋事的人，不适宜空、破；如果旺相，一定亨通；再得到内克外卦，或者内外相生以及比和，都是美好的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In scheming to accomplish an affair, take the Self and the Other as the chief and the inner and outer trigrams as the ground. To accomplish an affair one must examine the Self and the Other: the Self is the principal and the Other is the other party; if Void, clash, break or extinction sits on the Self, do not set about scheming; if clash, break, extinction or Void is in the Other's place, the other party's mind does not agree and one sighs in vain. In every business and every scheme, take the Self and the Other as the chief: if either the Self or the Other falls on a place of extinction, or runs into the ten-day Void, Month-break or the Day's clash, it is hard to accomplish. Next, take the inner trigram as the one who schemes the affair, which should not be Void or broken; if it meets thriving it surely means success; if in addition the inner controls the outer trigram, or the inner and outer generate each other or are in accord, all is fine.

**《易林补遗·谋望成事章第三十九》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 883 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 39: Scheming and accomplishing affairs", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 883*

**《易林补遗·谋望成事章第三十九》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 884 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 39: Scheming and accomplishing affairs", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 884*

This entry takes the same method as 'whether it can be done and whether it succeeds' in the Yi Yin divination on scheming (YY.83, YY.84): the Self and the Other are the chief and the inner trigram is the one who schemes the affair.

## LY.CAREER.YM.28 军机结语：决策在人而成谋在天——蓍草所从的是鬼神之情，喜直而恶曲、辅仁而伐暴，占者要以此为鉴

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** It is often said that decisions rest with men and the success of schemes with Heaven. ## LY.CAREER.YM.40 仕途大象：已食禄于朝而筮仕途，皆以官为用神，喜居日月旺相、生扶拱合，忌破空散绝（空失职、破败事、绝解任、散削籍）；子孙动防公议、兄弟发减俸粮；官临长生帝旺临官则宦途远达升迁、衰墓死绝则官道否；进神升爵、退神降级；泄气动而元神静则升信杳然、元神发而用象摇则迁音乃至

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:已经在朝廷食禄的人占仕途，都以官为用神：喜它在日、月上，人们仰仗它的照临；喜它旺相，像万物沾到及时雨；喜它逢生、扶、拱、合，如同倚着长城奔驰在康庄大道上；忌它逢破、空、散、绝——空就失职，破就败事，绝就解任，散就削籍。子孙动，要防公议，按衰旺定轻重；兄弟发，要减俸粮，按喜忌说吉凶。官临长生，知道宦途远大通达；逢帝旺、临官，知道仕路有升迁；逢衰、墓、死、绝，知道官道不通；进神就升爵升阶，退神就降禄降级。变墓绝而动爻得生扶、被日月克而动爻变生，都是安稳之象。泄气的爻动而元神静，升迁的消息杳无；元神发动、用象摇动，调迁的音信就到了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Having already eaten the Son of Heaven's salary, in divining the official career one takes Pressure as the Subject throughout: it is good when it sits on the Day or the Month, for people look up to its shining down; good when it is thriving or strong, like things receiving the timely rain; good when it meets generation, support, enclosing and combination, like leaning on a great wall while racing along a broad highway; bad when it meets broken, Void, scattered or extinct: Void loses office, broken ruins affairs, extinct dissolves the post, scattered strikes the name from the rolls. Output moving, guard against public criticism, and tell heavy from light by decline and thriving; Peer issuing cuts the salary grain, and good or ill is told by what is welcome and what is feared. Pressure at Birth, one knows the official road will go far and clear; at Peak or at Taking Office, one knows there will be promotion; at decline, tomb, death or extinction, one knows the official way is blocked; Advancing means rank and grade go up, Retreating means salary and grade go down. Changing into tomb or extinction while the moving line is generated and supported, or being controlled by the Day and Month while the moving line changes into generation, are all signs of safety. If the line that drains it moves while its Support is still, news of promotion is faint; if the Support issues and the image of the Subject stirs, word of a transfer arrives.

## LY.CAREER.YM.64 用人总纲：古之治国莫难于知人，知人善任而庶事有济，营家治生者亦然；任仆以财为用、任人以应为尊，三冲则鲜克有终、克世则中怀不利

## LY.CAREER.YM.84 户役·总括：役而趋利好财官、役而免害恶鬼旺及动官克世；避役喜失其鬼、谋役脱役不喜失其鬼，用神无执一（谋役脱役无鬼难为主）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:大抵当役而求利的，喜财、官；当役而求免害的，厌鬼旺，以及官动、官克世。避役的喜欢失去鬼，谋役、脱役的却不喜欢失去鬼。用神不要死守一个爻。谋役、脱役，没有鬼就难以做主。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Broadly, for one who serves a duty to seek profit, Wealth and Pressure are welcome; for one who serves a duty and seeks to escape harm, Pressure thriving is hated, and so are Pressure moving and Pressure controlling the Self. In seeking a duty or being freed from one, without Pressure it is hard to take charge.

'The Subject is not held to one' is that the Subject cannot be held to one line but is taken according to whether the question is seeking or avoiding: this is the general principle of the whole chapter (service, and households and holdings). Read against the sections of the Yilin Buyi on service (EL.109-116).

## LY.CAREER.YM.88 营谋·来去、求己求人与谋之主：谋事之来者谋望并求用神、谋事之去者谋望而不求用神；求于己者世、求于人者应；有交忌冲、无交不忌冲；官鬼乃谋之主俱不可失

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:凡是谋事要「来」的（要得到什么），办法是谋望，再同时求用神；谋事要「去」的（要摆脱什么），办法是谋望而不求用神。求于自己的看世，求于别人的看应；有交接的忌冲，没有交接的不忌冲；只有官鬼是谋事的主，都不可失去。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For a scheme in which something is to 'come' (something is to be gained), the method is the scheme and hope, and also seeking the Subject; for a scheme in which something is to 'go' (something is to be shaken off), the method is the scheme and hope without seeking the Subject. ## LY.CAREER.YM.101 道业·修行吉占与圣学：惟得复咸艮无妄而安宁者为修行之吉占；孔孟之学几成功名之捷径、自姚江而后道风渺然，若有豪杰继绝学明圣道亦以此章占；四卦专释心学不拘福鬼冲合直言其吉（安静则然变动复非）；圣学无求道故道业止详二氏三教道同占同

## LY.CAREER.YM.102 官鬼的名与用：或曰阳为官阴为鬼、旺为官衰为鬼、官鬼为系爻制爻；大凡喜之为官、恶之为鬼，名异而用同，卦不应无亦不宜动（官鬼不必以旺衰阴阳分，用则为官忌则为鬼，官亦可凶鬼亦可吉）

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Some say that yang is the official and yin is the demon; some say that thriving is the official and weak is the demon; some say that Pressure is the 'binding' line and the 'controlling' line. The hexagram should not lack it, and it should not move. Pressure need not be told apart by strength and weakness or by yin and yang: if it is taken for use it is the official, and if it is feared it is the demon; yet the official may be ominous, and the demon may be auspicious.

## LY.CAREER.ZS.01 升选候补·官爵荣身，须宜持世；养廉财禄，最喜生身（旺官持世，日月动爻作官星生合世爻，或世动化官生世，皆吉；财动生官得美缺，官临日月必超迁）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占升选补缺，官星要旺相、持世，妻财要来生身：旺相的官星持世，或者日月、动爻作官星来生合世爻，或者世爻发动变出官星来生世，都是吉兆；妻财发动生官星，能得到好缺；官星临在日月上，必定越级升迁。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a question about promotion or appointment the star of office should be thriving and hold the Self, and Wealth should generate the Body: a thriving star of office holding the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acting as the star of office to generate or combine with the Self, or the Self moving and turning into the star of office to generate the Self, are all auspicious; Wealth moving to generate the star of office wins a fine post; the star of office on the Day or Month means promotion beyond one's rank.

**《增删卜易·升选候补章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10299–10300 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Chapter on promotion, selection and waiting for a post", base text; Zengshan Buyi, lines 10299–10300*

| 养廉财禄，最喜生身。 | 靠清廉养身的俸禄钱财，最喜欢来生扶自身。 | for the salary that keeps an official honest, nothing is better than that it generate the Body. |

## LY.CAREER.ZS.07 升选候补·野鹤：古以晋、升为吉，屯、蹇为凶，晋升也要世爻得地，屯蹇世旺无妨

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看升选不能只看卦名：古人以晋、升两卦为吉，屯、蹇两卦为凶；野鹤说晋、升两卦确实屡次应验，但也要世爻得地，世爻失陷也不吉；屯、蹇两卦，只要世爻旺相就没有妨碍，他有一次世爻旺相占到屯卦，照样越级升迁。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Promotion cannot be read from the hexagram name alone: the ancients took Jin and Sheng as auspicious and Zhun and Jian as ominous; the Old Man of Yehe says Jin and Sheng have indeed proved right many times, but the Self must also be well placed, and a Self that is lost or sunk is not auspicious; for Zhun and Jian there is no harm so long as the Self is thriving, and once he got Zhun with a thriving Self and was promoted beyond rank all the same.

**野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 10480–10483 行 · *The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, lines 10480–10483*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 野鹤曰：古以晋、升为吉，屯、蹇为凶，余于晋升二卦，果得屡验，亦要世爻得地，若失陷，亦不为吉。 | 野鹤说：古人认为晋、升两卦吉，屯、蹇两卦凶；我对晋、升两卦，确实屡次应验，但也要世爻得地，如果世爻失陷，也不吉。 | The Old Man of Yehe says: the ancients took Jin and Sheng as auspicious and Zhun and Jian as ominous; for Jin and Sheng I have indeed found them confirmed many times, but the Self must also be well placed, and if it is lost or sunk, they are not auspicious either. |
| 屯蹇二卦，世爻旺者何妨。 | 屯、蹇两卦，世爻旺相的有什么妨碍。 | For Zhun and Jian, what harm is there if the Self is thriving? |
| 前篇亥月丙寅日之占验，世爻旺者，竟得超升，岂非屯卦耶？ | 前一篇亥月丙寅日的占验，世爻旺相，竟然得了越级升迁，那不就是屯卦吗？ | In the divination of the Hai month on a Bingyin day in the earlier section the Self was thriving and he was promoted beyond rank after all; was that not the Zhun hexagram? |

## LY.CAREER.ZSZ.10 僧道章（周易尚占）·道士缁流问卜：身安世静最先；身爻克世可用、世应比和自然乐；世克身退守、财克世好求缘；六爻不动清福、一位交重事变迁；官鬼交好谒官员；世临白虎官司挠、身遇勾陈心事缠；父母克身师接引、青龙持世贵周全；勾陈带杀多魔障；兄弟勾陈同位动防法眷勾连；父母重重身受克被俗家牵；财空注疏徒劳、鬼旺营谋枉费钱

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:道士、僧人来问卜：身安稳、世安静最要紧；身爻克世，可以信用；世、应比和，自然安乐；世去克身，当退守；财来克世，好求缘；六爻不动是清福，一位交重，事就变迁；财扶助世，能够施舍行利；官鬼交动，偏好去谒见官员；世临白虎，官司纠缠扰乱；身遇勾陈，心事牵缠；父母克身，有师父接引；青龙持世，有贵人周全；勾陈带杀，多有魔障；官鬼临在财上，被贼人冤害；兄弟和勾陈同位发动，谨防法眷在外勾连；贵人和父母交动，师父通达圣道；青龙并上子孙，徒弟贤能；父母重重、身受克，断定是被俗家牵累；财空，注解经疏徒劳力；鬼旺，谋求营运白费钱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For Taoist priests and monks asking by divination: the Body at ease and the Self still comes first; the Body line controlling the Self, one may be trusted and used; Self and Other in accord, ease comes of itself; the Self controlling the Body, one should withdraw and hold; Wealth coming to control the Self is good for seeking alms; the six lines still is pure blessing, one line moving means change; with Wealth supporting the Self one can dispense benefit; Pressure moving is especially good for calling on officials; the Self on the White Tiger, a lawsuit disturbs one; the Body meeting Hook Earth, worries entangle the heart; Resource controlling the Body, a master receives and leads one on; the Azure Dragon holding the Self, a patron sees one through; Hook Earth carrying a baleful spirit, there are many demonic obstructions; Pressure on Wealth, one is wronged by thieves; Peer and Hook Earth moving together in the same place, beware of Dharma kin colluding with outsiders; the Noble and Resource moving together, the master is versed in the sacred; the Dragon joined to Output, the disciples are worthy; Resource upon Resource and the Body controlled, it is certain one is dragged back by one's lay family; Wealth void, annotating the scriptures is wasted labour; Pressure strong, scheming is money wasted.
