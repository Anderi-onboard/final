## LY.CAREER.EL.80 官员荐奖：臣沐君恩必得岁君生世；欲求恩宠须看五爻为天子、太岁为朝廷，此二象内若得一爻生世或生官便沾天泽，如值空亡恩先莫望从，不值空如来克世伤官原不为福

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:臣子蒙受君王的恩典，必须得到岁君（太岁）来生世爻。要求恩宠，要看五爻是天子、太岁是朝廷：这两个象里如果有一个来生世爻或者生官爻，就沾到天子和朝廷所给的恩泽；如果落在空亡，就不要指望得到恩宠；不落空而来克世爻、伤克官爻的，本来就不是福。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A minister who receives the sovereign's favour must have Tai Sui, the sovereign of the year, generating the Self. To seek favour, look at the fifth line as the Son of Heaven and Tai Sui as the court: if either of these two images generates the Self or generates the star of office, one shares in the grace given by the Son of Heaven and the court; if it falls on Void, do not hope for favour; if it is not Void but comes to control the Self or to hurt the star of office, it was never a blessing.

**《易林补遗·官员荐奖章第三十三》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 816 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 33: Recommendation and commendation of officials", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 816*

| 臣沐君恩，必得岁君生世。 | 臣子蒙受君王的恩典，必须得到岁君（太岁）来生世爻。 | A minister bathed in the sovereign's favour must have Tai Sui, the sovereign of the year, generating the Self. |

> **按**:「五爻为天子」是第五爻代表君王，「太岁为朝廷」是太岁代表朝廷，与黄金策国朝「九五逢阳，当遇仁明之主」（HJC.65）、「太岁为君」（HJC.64）同取。「岁君」就是太岁。「天泽」是上天的恩泽，这里指天子的恩典。「恩先莫望从」文字疑有误，按文意译作「不要指望得到恩宠」。
>
> **Note:** 'The fifth line is the Son of Heaven' is the fifth line standing for the sovereign, and 'Tai Sui is the court' is Tai Sui standing for the court, taken as in 'the ninth-fifth meeting yang, one meets a benevolent and enlightened sovereign' (HJC.65) and 'Tai Sui is the sovereign' (HJC.64) in the Huangjin Ce on the state. 'Suijun' is Tai Sui. 'Tianze' is the bounty of heaven and here means the favour of the Son of Heaven. 'En xian mo wang cong' looks corrupt in its characters and is translated by the sense as 'do not hope for favour'.

## LY.CAREER.EL.81 官员荐奖：下叨上荐须求外鬼扶身；如占举荐专看外卦官爻，外象如无内官可用、内鬼再空不须谋望，不论内外卦中若有官来生身生世决叨提拔之恩

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:下级要承蒙上级的荐举，要求外卦的官鬼来扶持卦身。占荐举，专看外卦的官爻；外卦没有，内卦的官爻也可以用；内卦的官鬼再落空，就不必谋求指望；不论内卦外卦，卦中如果有官爻来生卦身、生世爻，一定承蒙提拔的恩典。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A subordinate who is to receive a recommendation from above must seek the outer Pressure line to support the Body. In divining for a recommendation, look only at the Pressure line in the outer trigram; if the outer trigram has none, the inner star of office can be used; if the inner Pressure too is Void, there is no need to scheme and hope; whether inner or outer, if the hexagram has a star of office that generates the Body or the Self, one will surely receive the grace of being raised.

**《易林补遗·官员荐奖章第三十三》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 816 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 33: Recommendation and commendation of officials", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 816*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 下叨上荐，须求外鬼扶身。 | 下级承受上级的荐举，要求外卦的官鬼来扶持卦身。 | A subordinate receiving a recommendation from above must seek the outer Pressure line to support the Body. |

**《易林补遗》诀下的解说** — 《易林补遗》第 817 行 · *Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse; Yilin Buyi, line 817*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 如占举荐，专看外卦官爻。 | 如果占荐举，专看外卦的官爻。 | If divining for a recommendation, look only at the Pressure line in the outer trigram. |
| 外象如无，内官可用，内鬼再空，不须谋望。 | 外卦如果没有，内卦的官爻可以用；内卦的官鬼再落空，就不必谋求指望。 | If the outer image has none, the inner star of office can be used; if the inner Pressure too is Void, there is no need to scheme and hope. |
| 不论内外，卦中若有官来生身生世，决叨提拔之恩。 | 不论内卦外卦，卦中如果有官爻来生卦身、生世爻，一定承蒙提拔的恩典。 | Whether inner or outer, if the hexagram has a star of office that generates the Body or the Self, one will surely receive the grace of being raised. |

> **按**:「叨」是承受（多用作谦辞）。「外鬼」是外卦的官鬼。这一条与易隐官禄占「官贵旺动生合世目下蒙荐」（YY.17）、黄金策求馆「鬼动合身，须得贵人推荐」（HJC.36）取同一个关系：官鬼来生合世身，有人荐举。
>
> **Note:** 'Tao' is receiving (mostly used as a modest word). 'Waigui' is the Pressure line of the outer trigram. This entry takes the same relation as 'if the star of office and the Noble are thriving and moving and generate or combine with the Self, one is recommended at once' in the Yi Yin divination on official salary (YY.17) and 'if Pressure moves and combines with the Body, one must have a noble recommend one' in the Huangjin Ce on seeking a school post (HJC.36): Pressure generating or combining with the Self or Body means that someone recommends one.

## LY.CAREER.EL.82 官员荐奖：卦有二官六部重推重举、爻无一鬼三司不奖不褒；如得旺官迭见又来生合世爻必得重重之荐，六爻内如无鬼者定无推举之官

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里有两个官爻，六部就一再推荐、一再举用；爻里一个官鬼也没有，三司就不奖励也不褒扬。如果旺相的官爻接连出现，又来生合世爻，一定得到一重又一重的荐举；六爻里如果没有官鬼，一定没有推举的官员。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the hexagram has two stars of office, the Six Boards recommend and raise one again and again; if the lines have not even one Pressure, the Three Offices neither commend nor praise. If thriving stars of office appear one after another and come to generate or combine with the Self line, one will surely receive recommendation upon recommendation; if there is no Pressure among the six lines, there is surely no official to recommend one.

**《易林补遗·官员荐奖章第三十三》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 816 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 33: Recommendation and commendation of officials", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 816*

| 卦有二官六部，重推重举。 | 卦里有两个官爻，六部就一再推荐、一再举用。 | If the hexagram has two stars of office, the Six Boards recommend and raise one again and again. |
| 爻无一鬼三司，不奖不褒。 | 爻里一个官鬼也没有，三司就不奖励也不褒扬。 | If the lines have not even one Pressure, the Three Offices neither commend nor praise. |

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 如得旺官迭见又来生合世爻，必得重重之荐。 | 如果旺相的官爻接连出现，又来生合世爻，一定得到一重又一重的荐举。 | If thriving stars of office appear one after another and come to generate or combine with the Self line, one will surely receive recommendation upon recommendation. |
| 六爻内如无鬼者，定无推举之官。 | 六爻里如果没有官鬼，一定没有推举的官员。 | If there is no Pressure among the six lines, there is surely no official to recommend one. |

> **按**:「六部」是朝廷的吏、户、礼、兵、刑、工六部；「三司」是省一级的布政使司、按察使司、都指挥使司。「卦有二官六部」「爻无一鬼三司」书里没有展开，这里按字面对仗译：有两个官，六部再三推荐；一个官鬼也没有，三司都不奖励。
>
> **Note:** 'Liubu', the Six Boards, are the Boards of Personnel, Revenue, Rites, War, Justice and Works at court; 'sansi', the Three Offices, are the provincial commissions for administration, for judicial matters and for military command. The book does not unfold 'the hexagram has two stars of office, the Six Boards' and 'the lines have not one Pressure, the Three Offices', and they are translated here literally as a matched pair: with two stars of office the Six Boards recommend again and again; with not one Pressure the Three Offices do not commend.

## LY.CAREER.EL.94 恩封诰命总纲：以文书为主、官鬼为凭；欲知紫诰赠何人，卦内还须分六亲——父作皇封世作己，官为吏部五为君，更将太岁为天子，乾卦当称帝王尊，君位怕临空与绝，五爻忌克世和身；凡占诰命父母为用爻，不绝不空终须有望

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占恩封诰命，以文书爻为主，以官鬼为凭据。想知道诰命要赠给谁，卦里要分六亲：父爻当作皇帝的封诰，世爻是自己，官爻是吏部，五爻是君王，再把太岁当作天子，乾卦应当称作帝王的尊位；君位怕落空和绝，五爻忌克制世爻和身爻。凡是占诰命，父母爻是用爻，不绝不空，终究有指望；又看世爻是自己、官爻是吏部、五爻是天子、太岁是朝廷，都不要落空亡；代表君位的爻，千万不要伤身、克世；乾卦也代表君王，不论内卦外卦得到这个象的，一定受到恩封。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining about an honorific patent, take the document as the chief and Pressure as the ground. To know on whom the imperial patent will be conferred, one must distinguish the six kin in the hexagram: Resource is taken as the imperial patent, the Self is oneself, the star of office is the Board of Personnel, the fifth line is the sovereign, and Tai Sui is further taken as the Son of Heaven; the Qian hexagram is called the honoured emperor; the sovereign's position fears Void and extinction, and the fifth line must not control the Self and the Body. In every divination about a patent, Resource is the Subject line, and if it is not extinct and not Void there must in the end be hope; look also at the Self as oneself, the star of office as the Board of Personnel, the fifth line as the Son of Heaven and Tai Sui as the court, none of which may be Void; the line of the sovereign's position must on no account hurt the Body or control the Self; the Qian hexagram also stands for the sovereign, and whoever gets this image, inner or outer, will surely receive the conferment.

## LY.CAREER.EL.95 恩封诰命：君爻生合何爻处便见天恩赐那人——财象遇生封妻室、子爻逢合荫儿孙、世身若得君相合品级加增作大臣；五爻与太岁生合父母必赠椿萱，生合财爻必封妻室，生扶福德当荫儿孙，生合世身再加官爻旺相自当品职高升

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:君爻生合哪一爻，就看到天恩赐给那个人：财爻遇生，封赠妻子；子爻逢合，荫及儿孙；世爻、身爻如果与君爻相合，品级加增，做到大臣。具体说：五爻和太岁生合父母爻，一定赠封父母（椿萱）；生合财爻，一定封赠妻室；生扶福德（子孙），应当荫及儿孙；生合世爻、身爻，再加官爻旺相，自然品级职位高升。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wherever the sovereign's line generates or combines, there one sees heaven's favour conferred on that person: if the Wealth image meets generation, the wife is enfeoffed; if the Output line meets combination, descendants receive privilege; if the Self or Body is combined with the sovereign's line, one's rank is increased and one becomes a great minister. In detail: if the fifth line and Tai Sui generate or combine with Resource, the parents will surely be honoured with a title; if they generate or combine with Wealth, the wife will surely be enfeoffed; if they generate and support Output, the fortune virtue, descendants should receive privilege; if they generate or combine with the Self or Body and the Pressure line is further thriving, one's rank and post will naturally be raised high.
