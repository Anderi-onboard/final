The note finds the Tianyi Noble by the month: Si in the first month, Shen in the second, Hai in the third, Yin in the fourth, Si again in the fifth and ninth, the three places turning on after that; if it moves it is surely auspicious, and descendants will be dukes and ministers generation after generation.

> **打架**:旧注里的天乙贵人按月份取（巳申亥寅轮转），而卜筮大全另一处、卜筮正宗、增删卜易、易林补遗等讲的天乙贵人都是按日干取：甲戊（庚）牛羊、乙己鼠猴、丙丁猪鸡、壬癸兔蛇、庚辛（六辛）马虎。两种起法不同，本库各照原书录，不裁定。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The note here finds the Tianyi Noble by the month (turning through Si, Shen, Hai, Yin), while elsewhere in the Bushi Daquan itself, and in the Bushi Zhengzong, the Zengshan Buyi and the Yilin Buyi, the Tianyi Noble is found by the day stem: Jia and Wu (and Geng) ox and sheep, Yi and Ji rat and monkey, Bing and Ding pig and rooster, Ren and Gui hare and snake, Geng and Xin (or the six Xin) horse and tiger. The two methods differ; each is recorded as its book gives it and neither is adjudicated.

> **按**:「天纲」在这里没有交代是谁；断易天机的今人注读作袁天纲。本库照字面译成 Tiangang，不替它定。
>
> **Note:** 'Tiangang' is not identified here; a modern note in the Duanyi Tianji reads it as Yuan Tiangang. It is rendered literally as Tiangang and nothing is decided.

The outer trigram is originally the Subject line: if it flourishes and appears, one gets to meet; with no Body and no Pressure, one does not meet. In every call on a noble, what is most disliked is the outer trigram falling on Void; if the outer trigram appears and is thriving, one will surely meet the person; if all three lines of the outer trigram move, there will surely be a change; if one or two lines move, it is not judged so, but one must also take the Body and the Pressure line, and if one of these is lacking, one again does not meet.

## LY.CAREER.HZL.03 占谒贵（一）：官鬼为主、世我应彼、世应相生得遇和喜；以外卦取——外阳爻可见外阴爻不见、阴鬼阳世再见阳鬼阴世已外出、出现在家忌外卦独发、伏藏应动皆不见

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining about calling on a great man, Pressure is the master; the Self is me and the Other is he: if the Self and the Other generate each other, one meets with harmony and pleasure. In all divinations about calling on a great man, take it from the outer trigram: a yang line in the outer trigram means he can be seen, a yin line in the outer trigram means he cannot. With a yin Pressure line and a yang Self, one will see him later; with a yang Pressure line and a yin Self, he has already gone out. If it appears, he is at home; the outer trigram moving alone is feared. If it is hidden, or the Other moves, he is not seen in either case.

> **Note:** 'Yegui' is going to call on a person of standing. **Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Self and the Other generate or combine with each other it is auspicious, and if they control each other it is surely ominous. The Other controlling the Self, or the Subject line controlling the Self, he is willing to see one, but there is also the worry of punishment.
