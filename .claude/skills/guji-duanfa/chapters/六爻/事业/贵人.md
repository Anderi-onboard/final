## LY.CAREER.HJC.07 仕宦·月建生身当际风云之会，岁君合世必承雨露之恩（旧注：太岁为君、月建为执政之官，生合世身必有好处，冲克世身必遭贬谪；月建扶出官爻世爻是风宪之职，太岁加父母扶出官鬼及世爻必有天恩）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:太岁是君主的象，月建是执政的官。它们如果生合世爻、身爻，必有好处；只怕冲克世身，必定遭到贬谪。月建扶出官爻、世爻，必是风宪的职位；太岁加在父母上、扶出官鬼和世爻的，必有天恩，再得日辰在生旺之地更好；衰绝、遇空就没有用。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Tai Sui is the image of the sovereign, and the Month is the officer who holds government. If they generate or combine with the Self or the Body, there will surely be benefit; what is feared is that they clash or control the Self or the Body, and then one will surely be demoted and exiled. If the Month supports Pressure or the Self, it is surely a post in the censorate; if the Tai Sui is added to Resource and supports Pressure and the Self, there will surely be the Emperor's favour, better still if the Day then stands in a thriving place; if weak, in extinction or Void, it is of no use.

**《黄金策·仕宦》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7680 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7680*

| 月建生身，当际风云之会； | 月建生身，正逢风云际会； | When the Month generates the Body, one meets the gathering of wind and cloud; |
| 岁君合世，必承雨露之恩。 | 岁君合世，必然承受雨露的恩泽。 | when the Tai Sui (the Year Lord) combines with the Self, one will surely receive the favour of rain and dew. |

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 太岁乃君象，月建是执政之官，若得生合世爻者，必是风宪之职； | 太岁是君的象，月建是执政的官，如果生合世爻，必是风宪的职位； | The Tai Sui is the image of the sovereign and the Month is the officer who holds government; if they generate or combine with the Self, it is surely a post in the censorate; |
| 太岁加父母扶出官爻及世爻者，必有天恩，更得生旺尤美。 | 太岁加在父母上，扶出官爻和世爻的，必有天恩，再得生旺更美。 | if the Tai Sui is added to Resource and supports Pressure and the Self, there will surely be the Emperor's favour, and better still if it is thriving. |

> **按**:「风云之会」是君臣相遇、得志的时候，「雨露之恩」是君主的恩泽。「风宪」是负责监察、执法的官署（御史台、按察司一类）。这一联里「太岁」是君、「月建」是执政的官，也就是做官的人头上的两层上司，所以放在「贵人」这一题下。「贬谪」是降职并外放。注里「月建扶出官爻世爻者必是风宪之职」「太岁加父母扶出官鬼及世爻者必有天恩」两句，是占某一类官的象，见 LY.CAREER.HJC.19 以下几条。
>
> **Note:** 'The gathering of wind and cloud' is the time when a ruler and his minister meet and the minister achieves his aim, and 'the favour of rain and dew' is the sovereign's grace. 'The censorate' is the office that supervises and enforces the law (the Censorate, the Surveillance Commission and the like). In this couplet the Tai Sui is the sovereign and the Month the officer who holds government, that is, the two levels of superiors above an official, and so the entry is placed under 'patrons and superiors'. The two sentences in the commentary 'if the Month supports Pressure or the Self, it is surely a post in the censorate' and 'if the Tai Sui is added to Resource and supports Pressure and the Self, there will surely be the Emperor's favour' say what kind of post, which are taken up again in the entries from LY.CAREER.HJC.19 onwards.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.36 求馆·鬼动合身，须得贵人推荐（旧注：官鬼发动当有间阻；来生合世身则其馆必得贵人推荐而后可成；应如克世、父或落空虽荐不成）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼发动，应当有阻隔；但如果它来生合世爻、身爻，又主这个馆必得贵人推荐才能成；应爻如果克世，或者父母落空，即使有人推荐也不成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure moves there will be obstruction; but if it comes to generate or combine with the Self or the Body, it also means the school can be had only with a noble's recommendation; if the Other controls the Self, or Resource is Void, then even with a recommendation it does not come through.

**《黄金策·求馆(附束修)》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 8545 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 8545*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 鬼动合身，须得贵人推荐； | 官鬼发动而合到世身上，须得有贵人推荐； | If Pressure moves and combines with the Self and the Body, a noble's recommendation is needed; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 8546 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 8546*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 官鬼发动，当有间阻。 | 官鬼发动，应当有阻隔。 | If Pressure moves, there will be obstruction. |
| 然来生合世身，又主其馆必得贵人推荐，而后可成； | 但如果它来生合世爻、身爻，又主这个馆必得贵人推荐，然后才能成； | But if it comes to generate or combine with the Self or the Body, it also means the school will be had only with a noble's recommendation, and then it can come through; |
| 应如克世，父或落空，虽荐不成。 | 应爻如果克世，父母或者落空，即使有人推荐也不成。 | if the Other controls the Self, or Resource is Void, then even with a recommendation it does not come through. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2755 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2755*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 官鬼发动当有间阻，若来生合世身，必得贵人推荐可成。 | 官鬼发动应当有阻隔，如果来生合世爻、身爻，必得到贵人推荐才能成。 | If Pressure moves there should be obstruction, and if it comes to generate or combine with the Self or the Body, one must have a noble's recommendation for it to come through. |

> **按**:这一联的「官鬼」同时有两面：发动就有阻隔（鬼是阻碍），但生合世身时又是「贵人」（鬼是官，官是推荐人）。书里没有说怎么分清它是阻碍还是贵人，只说要看它和世身的关系：生合是推荐，克世是阻。这和 LY.CAREER.HJC.06（官爻临持身世是称意）是同一个道理。
>
> **Note:** In this couplet Pressure has two faces at once: moving, it obstructs (Pressure is the obstacle), but generating or combining with the Self and the Body it is the 'noble' (Pressure is an official, and an official is the one who recommends). The book does not say how to tell whether it is an obstacle or a noble, only that one looks at its relation to the Self and the Body: generating or combining is recommendation, and controlling the Self is obstruction. This is the same principle as LY.CAREER.HJC.06 (Pressure resting on the Self or the Body is to one's liking).

## LY.CAREER.HJC.44 求馆·鬼或化兄，备礼先酬乎荐馆（旧注：鬼爻动出兄弟必得礼物先酬荐馆之人则可成；兄临世身亦主先费财物；兄临应上是东家好利必得礼仪馈送而后可成）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼发动化出兄弟，必须拿出礼物先酬谢推荐馆的人，才能成；兄弟临世身，也主要先花费财物；兄弟临在应爻上，是东家好利，必须有礼品馈送，然后才能成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure moves and turns into Peer, one must give gifts first to repay the person who recommended one to the school, and then it can come through; Peer resting on the Self or the Body likewise means that one must first spend money and goods; if Peer rests on the Other, the host is fond of profit, and gifts must be sent before it can come through.

**《黄金策·求馆(附束修)》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 8572 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 8572*

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 8573 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 8573*

| 兄临世身，亦主先费财物。 | 兄弟临世爻、身爻，也主要先花费财物。 | Peer resting on the Self or the Body likewise means that one must first spend money and goods. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2765 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2765*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡遇鬼爻动出兄弟，必得礼物先酬荐馆之人，则可成就，兄临世身亦然。 | 凡是遇到官鬼爻发动化出兄弟，必须拿出礼物先酬谢推荐馆的人，才能成就，兄弟临世身也是这样。 | Whenever Pressure moves and turns into Peer, one must give gifts first to repay the person who recommended one to the school, and then it can be accomplished, and likewise if Peer rests on the Self or the Body. |

> **按**:兄弟是劫财的神，在求馆里就是「要花钱」：鬼（荐馆人）化兄弟，要先谢荐馆人；兄弟临世身，自己先破费；兄弟临应，东家好利要礼。王洪绪只写了前两种，没有写兄弟临应的一种。
>
> **Note:** Peer is the spirit that robs wealth, and in a school post it means 'money must be spent': Pressure (the one who recommends) turning into Peer means the recommender must be thanked first; Peer on the Self or the Body means one spends first; Peer on the Other means the host is fond of profit and wants gifts. Wang Hongxu writes only the first two and not the case of Peer on the Other.

## LY.CAREER.HJC.45 求馆·世如变鬼，央人转荐于东家（旧注：鬼爻出现而世又化鬼须再得推荐乃成；卦无官而动爻化出者初无人荐亦待央人荐之；鬼化鬼父化父反复难成）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼爻出现而世爻又化出官鬼的，必须再得到推荐才能成；卦里没有官鬼而动爻化出官鬼的，当初没有人推荐，也必须等央求人去推荐才能成；官鬼化官鬼、父母化父母，又主反复难成。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure appears and the Self also turns into Pressure, one must obtain a further recommendation before it can come through; if there is no Pressure in the hexagram but a moving line turns into Pressure, no one recommended one at first and one must wait for someone to be begged to recommend one before it can come through; if Pressure turns into Pressure or Resource into Resource, it also means going back and forth and being hard to bring about.
