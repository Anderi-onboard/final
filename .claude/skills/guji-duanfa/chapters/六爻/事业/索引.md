## 总则(Principles: which line is the post, the document, the patron; strong and weak)→ `总则.md`

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.01** 占做官，妻财就是俸禄。还没做官而求功名的人，不要财爻；已经做官的人，要见财爻，因为有爵位就有俸禄，没有没俸禄就能做官的。妻财旺相，俸禄一定多；休囚就微薄；卦里没有财或财落空亡，还没有俸禄；财动遇冲，任职以后会因事减俸；日辰月建冲财、又刑害世爻和官爻，恐怕有停发俸禄、罢职的祸患。(《黄金策》)
  In divining an office, Wealth is the salary. For one not yet in office who is seeking a name, Wealth is not wanted; for one already in office, Wealth is wanted, since where there is rank there is a stipend, and no one gets an office with no pay. When Wealth is thriving the salary is surely large; resting or trapped, it is meagre; if there is no Wealth in the hexagram or it falls Void, no salary has yet been received; if Wealth moves and meets a clash, the salary is cut after taking up the post, on account of some matter; and if the Day or the Month clashes Wealth while also punishing or harming the Self and Pressure, there may be the calamity of salary being stopped and dismissal from office.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.02** 官鬼代表官职和爵位：旺相有气，官高爵大；休囚死绝，官小职卑；发动去生合世爻，或者得到月建、日辰的生扶，一定有升迁提拔，名声远播京城；安静不动而被月建、日辰克害，或者被世爻刑冲克害，一定没有声名威望。(《黄金策》)
  Pressure stands for office and rank: thriving and with breath, the office is high and the rank great; resting, trapped, dead or in extinction, the office is small and the post low; moving to generate or combine with the Self, or generated and supported by the Month or the Day, there will surely be promotion and the name spreads far to the capital; still, and controlled or harmed by the Month or the Day, or punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Self, there will surely be no fame or standing.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.28** 占仕宦，要按所任的职位和职分来推断，才能看出仕途的顺逆。这是仕宦章的结语：前面各联是按官职的种类分的（风宪、牧民、将帅、出巡、谏官、僧道医官），用的时候先判断问的是哪一类职位，再取相应的一联。(《黄金策》)
  In divining official posts, one must infer according to the post held and its duties, and then the smoothness or blockage of an official career can be known. This is the closing line of the chapter on holding office: the couplets before it are divided by the kind of office (censorate, shepherding the people, commander, touring, remonstrance, monk, Daoist and physician officials), and in use one first judges what kind of post is being asked about and then takes the corresponding couplet.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.29** 占书馆（去当塾师），以世爻代表西席，也就是教书的先生。世爻临父母，这位先生一定是通晓经书的人；世在乾、坤、离三宫也是这样；世临官爻又带贵人，或者本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，多半是秀才。(《黄金策》)
  In divining a school post, the Self stands for the tutor's seat, that is, the teacher. If the Self rests on Resource, the teacher is surely versed in the classics, and so too if the Self is in the Qian, Kun or Li palace; if it rests on Pressure with a noble, or the home palace's Pressure lies hidden under the Self, he is mostly a licentiate.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.01** 占升选补缺，官星要旺相、持世，妻财要来生身：旺相的官星持世，或者日月、动爻作官星来生合世爻，或者世爻发动变出官星来生世，都是吉兆；妻财发动生官星，能得到好缺；官星临在日月上，必定越级升迁。(《增删卜易》)
  In a question about promotion or appointment the star of office should be thriving and hold the Self, and Wealth should generate the Body: a thriving star of office holding the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acting as the star of office to generate or combine with the Self, or the Self moving and turning into the star of office to generate the Self, are all auspicious; Wealth moving to generate the star of office wins a fine post; the star of office on the Day or Month means promotion beyond one's rank.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.07** 看升选不能只看卦名：古人以晋、升两卦为吉，屯、蹇两卦为凶；野鹤说晋、升两卦确实屡次应验，但也要世爻得地，世爻失陷也不吉；屯、蹇两卦，只要世爻旺相就没有妨碍，他有一次世爻旺相占到屯卦，照样越级升迁。(《增删卜易》)
  Promotion cannot be read from the hexagram name alone: the ancients took Jin and Sheng as auspicious and Zhun and Jian as ominous; the Old Man of Yehe says Jin and Sheng have indeed proved right many times, but the Self must also be well placed, and a Self that is lost or sunk is not auspicious; for Zhun and Jian there is no harm so long as the Self is thriving, and once he got Zhun with a thriving Self and was promoted beyond rank all the same.

## 成否(Will it come: seeking a post, a commission, a recommendation; easy or hard)→ `成否.md`

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.05** 父母爻是印绶、文书、诰牒、宣敕、奏疏、表章，卦里不能没有它，宜旺不宜衰，扶助世爻最吉。它持太岁、有气、生合世爻，主有朝廷的宣召；再加月建，多是敕令制命和上司的奖励；最怕冲、空、化空，那就多半不实；衰弱安静又空亡，必定没有宣敕，也没有差遣任命；卦里没有父母，别指望迁升选用。(《黄金策》)
  Resource is the seal and ribbon, documents, patents, imperial edicts, memorials and petitions. It cannot be missing from the hexagram, should be thriving and not weak, and supporting the Self is the most auspicious. If it holds the Tai Sui with breath and generates or combines with the Self, there will be a summons from the court; with the Month added, it is mostly an edict or a commendation from superiors; what is most feared is a clash, Void or turning into Void, and then it is mostly unreal; if weak, still and Void, there will surely be no edict and no appointment; if Resource is not in the hexagram, do not hope for promotion or selection.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.35** 占馆不把父母当作老师，要当作书馆来看。父母旺相有气，就有好书馆；卦里没有父母或者落空，必定没有书馆，事情也难成。占那一年有没有书馆，全要这一爻出现、有气不空；应爻来生合世爻，自然有人来聘请，世爻生合应爻和父爻，就必须自己去访求；卦里没有父母而动爻化出父母的，意想不到有人推荐；父化父必有两处书馆；本宫化入他宫由近及远，他宫化入本宫从外到内。(《黄金策》)
  In divining a school post, Resource is not taken as the teacher but as the school itself. If Resource is thriving and has breath, there is a good school; if there is no Resource in the hexagram or it falls Void, there will surely be no school and the matter is hard to bring about. To divine whether there will be a school that year everything depends on this line appearing with breath and not Void; if the Other comes to generate or combine with the Self, someone naturally comes to engage one, and if the Self generates or combines with the Other and with Resource, one must go and seek on one's own; if there is no Resource in the hexagram but a moving line turns into it, someone unexpectedly recommends one; Resource turning into Resource means two schools; from the home palace into another palace is from near to far, and from another palace into the home palace is from outside to inside.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.37** 占学馆，应爻持兄弟发动，必有同行的人来争谋这个馆，兄弟临卦身也是这样；如果在间爻上发动、冲克世爻，就主有人从中说坏话，事情难成。(《黄金策》)
  In divining a school post, if the Other holds Peer and it moves, there will surely be fellow teachers contending for the post, and likewise if Peer rests on the Body; if it moves on a between line and clashes or controls the Self, it means someone undermines it by talk, and the matter is hard to bring about.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.38** 官鬼能生扶父母，所以占馆以官鬼这一爻代表招集率领学生的人。官鬼不出现或者落空，必定没有人聚集学生来成学馆，事情难成，也没有人推荐；如果空而逢冲、伏而被提起，必须央求那个人，他才肯出来招集。(《黄金策》)
  Pressure can generate and support Resource, so in divining a school post the Pressure line stands for the person who gathers and leads the pupils. If Pressure does not appear or falls Void, there will surely be no one to gather pupils to make up a school, the matter is hard to bring about, and no one will recommend one; if it is Void and meets a clash, or is hidden and drawn up, one must beg that person before he is willing to come forward and gather them.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.39** 应爻空亡，没有人来聘请；再如果父母不出现或者落空，必定难成；应爻动空、化空，是说空话、假装作主，不然也不能把事做完。父母临身，或者动爻来生合世身，是大吉的兆头，这个馆容易成；有这种象而应空、官鬼动，虽然有招集学生的人，却没有聘请的主人，适合自己开馆；官鬼空伏而应来生合，虽然有聘请的人家，却没有招集学生的人，适合自己去招来学生。(《黄金策》)
  If the Other is Void, no one comes to engage one; if Resource also does not appear or falls Void, the matter will surely be hard to bring about; if the Other moves into Void or turns into Void, he only says it in words and pretends to be the master, and otherwise the matter is not seen through to the end. If Resource rests on the Body, or a moving line comes to generate or combine with the Self or the Body, it is a greatly auspicious sign and the school comes easily; with this image but the Other Void and Pressure moving, though there is someone to gather pupils there is no master to engage one, and it is fitting to open a school oneself; if Pressure is Void and hidden while the Other comes to generate or combine, though there is a household that would engage one there is no one to gather pupils, and it is fitting to draw in pupils oneself.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.40** 文书（父母）是占馆的用神，遇到财爻发动就被克坏：没成的不能成，已经成的也不称意；世爻、身爻、日辰、月建都忌讳临着财爻；如果父母持月建，或者临在生旺之地，或许还能成。(《黄金策》)
  Resource, the document, is the Subject in divining a school post, and if Wealth moves it is controlled and spoiled: what has not yet come about cannot, and what has come about is not to one's liking; the Self, the Body, the Day and the Month all dislike resting on Wealth; if Resource holds the Month or rests in a place of birth or thriving, it may perhaps still come through.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.41** 卦里父母出现、应爻来生合，而世爻空亡的，不是馆不成，是自己不肯上前，或者虽然成了却不去；应爻不生合、父母重叠而世爻空亡的，是另有别的馆，所以不成这一个；世爻无缘无故自己空亡，必定不能成，即使成了也终究不如意。(《黄金策》)
  If Resource appears in the hexagram and the Other comes to generate or combine while the Self is Void, it is not that the school will not come about but that one does not step forward oneself, or that even if it comes about one does not go; if the Other does not generate or combine, Resource is doubled and the Self is Void, there is another school, and so this one does not come about; if the Self falls Void by itself without cause, it will surely not come about, and even if it does it is in the end not as one wishes.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.43** 间爻（世应之间的爻）发动，事情多有阻隔，纵然卦象吉也难成。(《黄金策》)
  If a between line (a line between the Self and the Other) moves, the matter is often obstructed, and even if the hexagram is auspicious it is hard to bring about.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.02** 子孙持世，或者在卦中发动，升迁选官没有指望；官星落在空、绝、墓、破上，或者发动变出凶爻，补缺升迁还早得很。(《增删卜易》)
  If Output holds the Self or moves in the hexagram, promotion and selection have no hope; if the star of office falls on Void, extinction, tomb or break, or moves and turns into a bad line, it is still early for an appointment or promotion.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.03** 世爻与官星三合成官局或财局，或者这个局生世爻，或者世爻在局中，或者官星持世、又与日月动爻相合，得到六合卦，以及发动变成六合卦的，都是得到皇恩的日子。但如果官局生的是应爻、不是世爻，这叫「出现无情」，这个缺得不到。(《增删卜易》)
  If the Self and the star of office form a three-harmony Pressure frame or Wealth frame, or the frame generates the Self, or the Self is within the frame, or the star of office holds the Self and combines with the Day, the Month or a moving line, or one gets a Six-Combine hexagram or a hexagram that moves into Six-Combine, it is the day of receiving the ruler's favour. But if the Pressure frame generates the Other and not the Self, this is called 'appearing but without feeling', and the post cannot be had.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.04** 时机未到的征兆：世爻、官星都不旺，又得六冲卦，或者官星被日月相冲，或者六冲变六冲；元神衰弱安静，或者泄元神之气的爻发动，问升选就没有日期。反过来，世爻发动、官星变成进神，升迁就在眼前；世爻发动或变吉，或禄、马、贵人临世，或官星发动变吉、生合世爻，都可以收拾行装等着。(《增删卜易》)
  Signs that the moment has not come: the Self and the star of office are not thriving and the hexagram is Six-Clash, or the star of office is clashed by the Day or Month, or a Six-Clash hexagram turns into Six-Clash; or the Support is weak and still, or the line that drains it moves, and then promotion has no date. The reverse: if the Self moves and the star of office turns into an Advancing line, promotion is at hand; if the Self moves, or turns auspicious, or Emolument, the Horse and a Noble sit on the Self, or the star of office moves, turns auspicious and generates or combines with the Self, one may pack one's bags and wait.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.05** 世爻或官星被破就不要指望，世爻上的子孙落空却也能升。世爻落在月破上，即使官星持世也升不了，还要防别的变故；子孙持世又遇旬空，也不该升。但如果占的是两三天之内的事，可以答应一定得到，因为子孙还没有出空、没有克官星。作者占过三个人，都得了官，得官后随即死了。(《增删卜易》)
  If the Self or the star of office is broken, give up hope; if the Self sits on an Output line that is Void, one is promoted all the same. If the Self is Month-broken, one cannot be promoted even when the star of office holds the Self, and one must guard against other changes; if Output holds the Self and meets Void, one should not be promoted either. But if the question concerns the next two or three days, one may grant that it will surely be had, since Output has not yet left the Void and does not yet control the star of office. The author divined for three men; all got office, and all died soon after getting it.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.22** 用钱买官（援例）：妻财旺相、世爻旺相，但官星落在月破、旬空上，或被日月冲克，以及发动变凶，官星像朽木枯枝，有再旺相的财也生不起它，到头来只是个有钱的平民，吃不上国家的俸禄。(《增删卜易》)
  Buying office by precedent: if Wealth and the Self are thriving but the star of office falls in Month-break or Void, or is clashed or controlled by the Day or Month, or moves and turns bad, the star of office is like a rotten log and a dry branch: though Wealth is thriving it cannot raise it, and in the end one is only a rich man in a commoner's house who cannot eat the heavenly salary of the royal house.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.23** 援例捐官，世爻、官星、财星这三样不能有一样失陷：妻财、官星都旺而世爻失陷，终究得了官也没有福气安享；官星、世爻都旺而财星失陷，是有例可援却没有钱。(《增删卜易》)
  In buying office by precedent, of the Self, the star of office and the star of Wealth, not one may be lost: if Wealth and the star of office are both thriving but the Self is lost, one may in the end get office but has no fortune to enjoy it in peace; if the star of office and the Self are both thriving but the star of Wealth is lost, there is a precedent to appeal to but no money.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.24** 援例捐官，子孙持世，不管财爻动不动，都不可以去办；财爻落空落破，也不可以去办；但子孙、财、官一起发动又可以去办，因为子孙动生财、财动生官。(《增删卜易》)
  In buying office by precedent, if Output holds the Self, whether or not the Wealth line moves it should not be done; if Wealth falls in Void or break, it should not be done either; but if Output, Wealth and the star of office all move together, it may be done, because Output moves to generate Wealth and Wealth moves to generate the star of office.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.25** 援例捐官，兄弟爻持世，或者兄爻发动，是破耗的神，白花钱财，功名终究不成；如果和官星一起发动，又可以去办，因为官鬼能制住兄弟。野鹤说：卦中官星不动，只要遇到兄弟持世、兄爻和父母爻发动、以及财爻持世变出兄弟，百件里没有一件成，即使成了也必有别的缘故，到头来没有地方吃俸禄。(《增删卜易》)
  In buying office by precedent, if the Peer line holds the Self or a Peer line moves, it is a spirit of waste and loss: the money is spent for nothing and the rank is never gained; if it moves together with the star of office it may be done, since Pressure restrains Peer. The Old Man of Yehe says: if the star of office does not move in the hexagram, and Peer holds the Self, or Peer and Resource lines move, or the Wealth line holds the Self and turns into Peer, not one in a hundred succeeds, and even if it succeeds there will surely be some other cause, and in the end one has no place to eat a salary.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.28** 上书奏事，父母爻就是文书、奏章：旺相的父母爻持世、合世，奏章必定被批准；再得到太岁、五爻和日月、动爻来生合的，被采用无疑。(《增删卜易》)
  In submitting a memorial the Resource line is the document and the memorial: if a thriving Resource line holds or combines with the Self, the memorial will surely be approved, and if Tai Sui, the fifth line, the Day, the Month or a moving line also generates or combines with it, it will be adopted without doubt.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.29** 兄弟临身时，喜欢父母爻变吉并来拱合：兄弟持世，有日月生它，或者世爻、父母爻发动变吉，或者世爻变出父母爻来生世，都能使皇上龙颜大悦，因为奏章被采用。(《增删卜易》)
  If Peer sits on the Body, it is good that the Resource line turns auspicious and holds up and combines: if Peer holds the Self and the Day and Month generate it, or the Self or the Resource line moves and turns auspicious, or the Self turns into Resource to generate the Self, all of these please the dragon countenance, and one is employed because of the document.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.33** 条陈将相、诤谏君过、弹劾奸臣这类事，和一般的奏事不一样：这里子孙要当福神用，所以反而喜欢子孙持世，即使当面诤谏也可以保证没有祸。但有官职的人如果只是为了保全功名，子孙发动时千万不要去做。古人以子孙在乾宫发动为吉，是不对的，子孙是制住官鬼的福神，没有听说只在乾宫才是福神。(《增删卜易》)
  For proposals about the generals and chancellors, remonstrance against the ruler's faults and the impeachment of a treacherous minister, the rule differs from ordinary memorials: here Output is used as the lucky spirit, so Output holding the Self is welcome instead, and even if one contends to the ruler's face one can be sure of no misfortune. But an official who only wants to preserve his rank must not do it when Output moves. The ancients took Output moving in the Qian palace as auspicious, which is wrong: Output is the lucky spirit that restrains Pressure, and it has never been heard that it is so only in the Qian palace.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.36** 上书求赠爵封官：父母旺相、官星生它，或者旺相的官星持世，或者旺相的父母持世、官星发动生它，或者旺官持世、有日月生它，做官的就官上加官，读书人就位至台阁，平民得到这个卦，立刻平步青云。野鹤说：断卦最看重的是来占的人心里的那个念头；借上书当阶梯来求功名的人，官星旺相而文书不旺，也可以答应他得到赠官封职，因为他本来的念头是为了得官，文书只是敲门砖，所以看重官星而不看重父母。(《增删卜易》)
  On submitting a memorial to win a bestowed rank or office: if Resource is thriving and the star of office generates it, or a thriving star of office holds the Self, or a thriving Resource holds the Self and the star of office moves to generate it, or a thriving star of office holds the Self and the Day and Month generate it, an official gets office added to office, a scholar reaches the high ministries, and a commoner who gets it at once climbs to the clouds. The Old Man of Yehe says: the most important thing in judging a hexagram is the one thought of the person who comes; for one who uses a memorial as a ladder to seek a name, if the star of office is thriving though the document is not, one may still grant that he will be given a post and title, because his first thought was to gain office and the document is only the tile to knock at the door, so the star of office is weighed and not Resource.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.51** 看工程牢不牢：工程都以父爻为用神；用神不旺，或者受冲克，又得六冲卦的，必定不坚固长久，变冲的也一样；还要看世爻旺不旺，世爻如果休囚被克，将来终究要遭它的害；只有父爻旺相、又得六合卦的，才永远坚固牢靠。(《增删卜易》)
  To tell whether a work will be firm: in construction the Resource line is always the Subject; if the Subject is not thriving, or is clashed or controlled, and the hexagram is also Six-Clash, the work will surely not be firm and lasting, and so also when it turns into a clash; one must also see whether the Self is thriving, for if the Self is resting or trapped and controlled one will in the end suffer harm from it; only when the Resource line is thriving and the hexagram is also Six-Combine will it be firm and secure for ever.

## 升迁(Promotion, transfer, posting and rank: which way, how far)→ `升迁.md`

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.06** 官爻是占官的象。它临持身世，或者来生合世爻，又不受月建、日辰冲克，凡有谋求必然称心；如果不上卦，或者落空亡，或者虽然出现却墓绝无气、受克制，都不如意；世爻、身爻被冲克，也凶。(《黄金策》)

If it settles on the Body or the Self, or comes to generate or combine with the Self, and is not clashed or controlled by the Month or the Day, whatever is sought will surely turn out to one's liking; if it is absent from the hexagram or falls Void, or even though it appears is in a tomb or extinction and without breath, or controlled, nothing will be as one wishes; a clash or control on the Self or the Body is also ominous.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.08** 按官星的五行断去哪个方位上任：金官应西，木官应东，水应北，火应南，土官升到中土。野鹤说这是古法，他验证过的有出入：木官应在山东，也有应在广东的；金官应在山西，也有应在江西、广西的；还有按所住的地方来分东西南北的。(《增删卜易》)

- **LY.CAREER.ZS.09** 看是内地还是边远：初爻代表内地，五爻、六爻代表边远地方。野鹤解释：「应在初爻」是世爻和官星都在初爻；「应在五、六爻」是世在五爻官在六爻，或世在六爻官在五爻，或官星持世、一起在五、六爻上；如果在二、三、四爻，仍然按五行来决断。(《增删卜易》)
  To tell whether the post is inland or on the frontier: the first line is the inner land and the fifth and sixth lines are the frontier. The Old Man of Yehe explains: 'due in the first line' means the Self and the star of office are both in the first line; 'due in the fifth or sixth line' means the Self is in the fifth line and the star of office in the sixth, or the Self in the sixth and the star of office in the fifth, or the star of office holds the Self and both are in the fifth or sixth line; if they are in the second, third or fourth line, one still decides by the five elements.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.10** 占升选，与其问「升到哪个方向」，不如指明具体的缺来问：这个缺我能得吗？我能进翰林吗？我能做部属吗？我能做科道官吗？我能做正印官吗？官星持世、官星发动生合世爻、三合官局生世，都主必得；世爻落空落破、官星落破落空、子孙持世、子孙发动、官星临在应爻上、三合官局生应爻，都主得不到。指明了缺就已经知道在哪里，不必多此一举。(《增删卜易》)

Can I become a supervising censor? Can I become the chief official holding the seal? If the star of office holds the Self, or the star of office moves to generate or combine with the Self, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates the Self, it will surely be had; if the Self is Void or broken, the star of office is broken or Void, Output holds the Self, Output moves, the star of office sits on the Other, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates the Other, it will not be had. Once the post is named its place is already known, and the extra step is unnecessary.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.15** 在任时，变出进神，或冲变成合，都是加官晋爵的征兆：世爻旺相、官星变进神；官星旺相、世爻变进神；世爻与官星都得地、财爻变进神。(《增删卜易》)

- **LY.CAREER.ZS.54** 断官能做到几品：古法按五行的成数，水一、火二、木三、金四、土五，官星临在火上官位二品，官星属水是一品的尊位。野鹤说这个古法不够用：水只有一个数，官位到公侯的怎么确定？官分九品，数只到五，六品、七品的怎么确定？他的办法是指明了去占：我能做公侯将相吗？我能做一品大员吗？我能中鼎甲吗？旺相的官星临日月，指日可待；官星被破或被刑冲，终身失望；子孙持世，就像望梅止渴；官星发动生身，恰似探囊取物。问鼎甲这类具体的事，要另占一卦指明了问：古人以蹇卦为不吉，他只看用神，世临巳火、卯月生申日合、青龙持世，中鼎甲无疑；世爻变出辰土之官，应在辰年，后来果然在甲辰科殿试得传胪第一。(《增删卜易》)

The Old Man of Yehe says this old method is not enough: Water has only the number one, so how is the rank of dukes and marquises to be fixed? There are nine grades of office and the numbers stop at five, so how are the sixth and seventh to be fixed? His method is to name the matter and divine: can I become a duke, marquis, general or chancellor? Can I become a first-grade minister? Can I take a place among the top three? A thriving star of office on the Day or Month can be expected any day; if the star of office is broken, punished or clashed, one is disappointed for life; if Output holds the Self, it is like quenching thirst by gazing at plums; if the star of office moves to generate the Body, it is as easy as taking something from a bag. For a concrete matter such as the top three, another hexagram is cast with the matter named: the ancients took Jian as ominous, and he looked only at the Subject, where the Self on Si Fire generated by the Mao month and combined by the Shen day with the Azure Dragon holding the Self left no doubt of a top place; the Self turned into the Chen Earth star of office, so it fell in a Chen year, and at the palace examination of the Jiachen session he did come first at the announcement.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.56** 占升迁，问什么时候和问什么地方，必须分开占；凭一个卦兼着断，假如官星是申金，必定七月升迁，按分野申是西方晋地，难道七月升迁的缺就一定在西方？这是古法的荒谬。又有按八宫来定的（如离南坎北），有用二十八宿定分野的，有用禄来定方位的，有说游魂卦远、归魂卦近的，都靠不住。《易冒》以五行、六神、八宫为经，众星为纬，可以讲但不能行：五行、八宫、二十八宿还算近理，如果用六神再加星煞，丁日占得恒卦，酉金官星持世，必掌兵权；六神得螣蛇，螣蛇是奔走差遣的吏；丁日以酉为文昌，文昌是翰林院；按这个方法断，就成了又掌兵权、又在翰林、又听差遣的人了，岂不是胡说。(《增删卜易》)

This is the absurdity of the old method. Then there is fixing it by the eight palaces (such as Li south and Kan north), or by the twenty-eight lunar mansions, or by the Emolument, or saying a Wandering Soul hexagram is far and a Returning Soul hexagram near, none of which can be relied on. ## 任所(In office: how the post goes, the term, dismissal, impeachment, leaving office)→ `任所.md`

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.10** 出巡的官，适合官鬼发动；牧守一类的官，适合官鬼安静。如果官鬼发动、化出子孙，必有别的官员来代理职务，不然也会被别人抢在前面；子孙发动化出官鬼，就是先难后易，或者先凶后吉；官鬼和子孙都发动，也主有官员来接替。(《黄金策》)

If Pressure moves and turns into Output, another officer will surely come to take over the post, or one is otherwise forestalled by someone else; if Output moves and turns into Pressure, it is hard first and easy later, or ominous first and fortunate later; if both Pressure and Output move, it likewise means an officer comes to replace one.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.14** 兄弟是僚属，也就是同僚、下属一类的官。卦中官鬼发动、化出兄弟，冲克世爻，主同僚不和；或者兄弟化出官鬼而刑冲克害，或者兄弟带三刑六害伤害世爻，也都是这样；世爻克兄弟，是我欺负他，因而不和。(《黄金策》)

If in the hexagram Pressure moves and turns into Peer and clashes or controls the Self, it means discord among colleagues; or if Peer turns into Pressure and punishes, clashes, controls or harms, or if Peer carries the three punishments or the six harms and injures the Self, it is the same; if the Self controls Peer, it is I who bully him, and so there is discord.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.16** 卦静而世爻空亡，是退休的兆头；身爻空亡而煞发动，是躲避祸事的征兆。世爻空亡，没做官的没有选用的日期；已经做官的，如果六爻安静、月建日辰太岁没有伤它，就是休官改任的象；如果官鬼发动、被月建日辰太岁伤克，是躲避祸患、脱离灾难的兆头；卦静而日辰冲，想回家却被留住；煞动而日辰合，想躲避却躲不开。(《黄金策》)

When the Self is Void, one not yet in office has no date of selection; for one already in office, if the six lines are still and the Month, the Day and the Tai Sui do not injure it, it is the image of retiring from office and changing post; if Pressure moves and the Month, the Day or the Tai Sui injures or controls it, it is a sign of avoiding misfortune and escaping calamity; if the hexagram is still and the Day clashes, one wants to go home but is not let go; if a killer moves and the Day combines, one wants to escape but cannot.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.17** 遇到凶兆的时候，如果官鬼爻临身持世，或者本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，虽然被责罚，官职还在；如果不临身持世，也不伏在世爻下面，或者虽然伏着仍遇到空亡，必定被革去官带贬为平民，不只是责罚而已。(《黄金策》)

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.18** 官鬼发动，来生合世爻，日辰、月建、动爻的变化又没有冲克它的，做官必定有声名威望；再得到财爻生扶合助、有气不空，就既会做官又会赚钱，内里实际贪图贿赂，外面不丧失名声；如果财爻空亡、伏藏或者临死绝，就是声名虽有，贿赂却没有。(《黄金策》)

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.33** 世应两爻刑冲克害，日后宾主一定不合，再加上兄弟、官鬼发动，是大凶的兆头；世应生合比和，情意必定相投；生而化出克，开始和睦、最后不和；冲而化出合，开始疏远、后来亲密；世爻和子孙也适宜生合，师生之间就恩义兼尽，见到冲克也多不睦。(《黄金策》)

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.46** 应爻不克世爻，父母不空，兄弟、官鬼不发动，而月建、日辰、动爻都不生合世爻的，这个事可以成，只是主人不敬重，都用白眼对待。(《黄金策》)
