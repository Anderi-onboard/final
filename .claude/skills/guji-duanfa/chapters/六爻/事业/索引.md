## 总则(Principles: which line is the post, the document, the patron; strong and weak)→ `总则.md`

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.01** 占做官，妻财就是俸禄。还没做官而求功名的人，不要财爻；已经做官的人，要见财爻，因为有爵位就有俸禄，没有没俸禄就能做官的。妻财旺相，俸禄一定多；休囚就微薄；卦里没有财或财落空亡，还没有俸禄；财动遇冲，任职以后会因事减俸；日辰月建冲财、又刑害世爻和官爻，恐怕有停发俸禄、罢职的祸患。(《黄金策》)
  In divining an office, Wealth is the salary. For one not yet in office who is seeking a name, Wealth is not wanted; for one already in office, Wealth is wanted, since where there is rank there is a stipend, and no one gets an office with no pay. When Wealth is thriving the salary is surely large; resting or trapped, it is meagre; if there is no Wealth in the hexagram or it falls Void, no salary has yet been received; if Wealth moves and meets a clash, the salary is cut after taking up the post, on account of some matter; and if the Day or the Month clashes Wealth while also punishing or harming the Self and Pressure, there may be the calamity of salary being stopped and dismissal from office.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.02** 官鬼代表官职和爵位：旺相有气，官高爵大；休囚死绝，官小职卑；发动去生合世爻，或者得到月建、日辰的生扶，一定有升迁提拔，名声远播京城；安静不动而被月建、日辰克害，或者被世爻刑冲克害，一定没有声名威望。(《黄金策》)
  Pressure stands for office and rank: thriving and with breath, the office is high and the rank great; resting, trapped, dead or in extinction, the office is small and the post low; moving to generate or combine with the Self, or generated and supported by the Month or the Day, there will surely be promotion and the name spreads far to the capital; still, and controlled or harmed by the Month or the Day, or punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Self, there will surely be no fame or standing.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.28** 占仕宦，要按所任的职位和职分来推断，才能看出仕途的顺逆。这是仕宦章的结语：前面各联是按官职的种类分的（风宪、牧民、将帅、出巡、谏官、僧道医官），用的时候先判断问的是哪一类职位，再取相应的一联。(《黄金策》)
  In divining official posts, one must infer according to the post held and its duties, and then the smoothness or blockage of an official career can be known. This is the closing line of the chapter on holding office: the couplets before it are divided by the kind of office (censorate, shepherding the people, commander, touring, remonstrance, monk, Daoist and physician officials), and in use one first judges what kind of post is being asked about and then takes the corresponding couplet.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.29** 占书馆（去当塾师），以世爻代表西席，也就是教书的先生。世爻临父母，这位先生一定是通晓经书的人；世在乾、坤、离三宫也是这样；世临官爻又带贵人，或者本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，多半是秀才。(《黄金策》)
  In divining a school post, the Self stands for the tutor's seat, that is, the teacher. If the Self rests on Resource, the teacher is surely versed in the classics, and so too if the Self is in the Qian, Kun or Li palace; if it rests on Pressure with a noble, or the home palace's Pressure lies hidden under the Self, he is mostly a licentiate.
- **LY.CAREER.YY.01** 官禄占分两种情形：还没做官的，占将做什么官、哪个月选官、哪天领到凭证、任所在哪个方向；已经做官的，占赴任的吉凶、在任上的顺逆、哪天升迁任命、什么时候被人替代。至于做官是贫还是廉，以及失去职位、补缺任职、遭父母之丧、起复，是官禄占附带的占问。(《易隐》)
  The divination of office and salary has two cases. For one not yet in office: what office he will take, in which month he will be selected, on what day he will receive his credentials, and in which direction the post lies. For one already in office: the good or ill of his journey to the post, how his term goes, on what day he will be promoted or appointed, and when he will be replaced. Whether an official is poor or incorruptible, and losing a post, being given a post to fill, mourning for a parent and being recalled to office, are subsidiary questions of this divination.
- **LY.CAREER.YY.82** 谋望占总述：想做一件事又不敢马上去做，就有了谋；想得到又不能一定得到，就有了望。所以先占它行不行，既然行了，再占它成不成，既然成了，再占它是快是慢，大致就是这些。分爻是：国事、官事、人事、家事、身事、心事。(《易隐》)
  Overview of divining a scheme. When one wants to do a thing and does not dare to do it at once, there is a scheme; when one wants to get a thing and cannot be sure of getting it, there is a hope. So first divine whether it is feasible; once feasible, whether it will succeed; once successful, whether it will be slow or fast; that is the general outline. The division of the lines: affairs of state, official affairs, affairs of others, family affairs, personal affairs and affairs of the heart.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.01** 占升选补缺，官星要旺相、持世，妻财要来生身：旺相的官星持世，或者日月、动爻作官星来生合世爻，或者世爻发动变出官星来生世，都是吉兆；妻财发动生官星，能得到好缺；官星临在日月上，必定越级升迁。(《增删卜易》)
  In a question about promotion or appointment the star of office should be thriving and hold the Self, and Wealth should generate the Body: a thriving star of office holding the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acting as the star of office to generate or combine with the Self, or the Self moving and turning into the star of office to generate the Self, are all auspicious; Wealth moving to generate the star of office wins a fine post; the star of office on the Day or Month means promotion beyond one's rank.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.07** 看升选不能只看卦名：古人以晋、升两卦为吉，屯、蹇两卦为凶；野鹤说晋、升两卦确实屡次应验，但也要世爻得地，世爻失陷也不吉；屯、蹇两卦，只要世爻旺相就没有妨碍，他有一次世爻旺相占到屯卦，照样越级升迁。(《增删卜易》)
  Promotion cannot be read from the hexagram name alone: the ancients took Jin and Sheng as auspicious and Zhun and Jian as ominous; the Old Man of Yehe says Jin and Sheng have indeed proved right many times, but the Self must also be well placed, and a Self that is lost or sunk is not auspicious; for Zhun and Jian there is no harm so long as the Self is thriving, and once he got Zhun with a thriving Self and was promoted beyond rank all the same.

## 成否(Will it come: seeking a post, a commission, a recommendation; easy or hard)→ `成否.md`

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.05** 父母爻是印绶、文书、诰牒、宣敕、奏疏、表章，卦里不能没有它，宜旺不宜衰，扶助世爻最吉。它持太岁、有气、生合世爻，主有朝廷的宣召；再加月建，多是敕令制命和上司的奖励；最怕冲、空、化空，那就多半不实；衰弱安静又空亡，必定没有宣敕，也没有差遣任命；卦里没有父母，别指望迁升选用。(《黄金策》)
  Resource is the seal and ribbon, documents, patents, imperial edicts, memorials and petitions. It cannot be missing from the hexagram, should be thriving and not weak, and supporting the Self is the most auspicious. If it holds the Tai Sui with breath and generates or combines with the Self, there will be a summons from the court; with the Month added, it is mostly an edict or a commendation from superiors; what is most feared is a clash, Void or turning into Void, and then it is mostly unreal; if weak, still and Void, there will surely be no edict and no appointment; if Resource is not in the hexagram, do not hope for promotion or selection.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.35** 占馆不把父母当作老师，要当作书馆来看。父母旺相有气，就有好书馆；卦里没有父母或者落空，必定没有书馆，事情也难成。占那一年有没有书馆，全要这一爻出现、有气不空；应爻来生合世爻，自然有人来聘请，世爻生合应爻和父爻，就必须自己去访求；卦里没有父母而动爻化出父母的，意想不到有人推荐；父化父必有两处书馆；本宫化入他宫由近及远，他宫化入本宫从外到内。(《黄金策》)
  In divining a school post, Resource is not taken as the teacher but as the school itself. If Resource is thriving and has breath, there is a good school; if there is no Resource in the hexagram or it falls Void, there will surely be no school and the matter is hard to bring about. To divine whether there will be a school that year everything depends on this line appearing with breath and not Void; if the Other comes to generate or combine with the Self, someone naturally comes to engage one, and if the Self generates or combines with the Other and with Resource, one must go and seek on one's own; if there is no Resource in the hexagram but a moving line turns into it, someone unexpectedly recommends one; Resource turning into Resource means two schools; from the home palace into another palace is from near to far, and from another palace into the home palace is from outside to inside.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.37** 占学馆，应爻持兄弟发动，必有同行的人来争谋这个馆，兄弟临卦身也是这样；如果在间爻上发动、冲克世爻，就主有人从中说坏话，事情难成。(《黄金策》)
  In divining a school post, if the Other holds Peer and it moves, there will surely be fellow teachers contending for the post, and likewise if Peer rests on the Body; if it moves on a between line and clashes or controls the Self, it means someone undermines it by talk, and the matter is hard to bring about.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.38** 官鬼能生扶父母，所以占馆以官鬼这一爻代表招集率领学生的人。官鬼不出现或者落空，必定没有人聚集学生来成学馆，事情难成，也没有人推荐；如果空而逢冲、伏而被提起，必须央求那个人，他才肯出来招集。(《黄金策》)
  Pressure can generate and support Resource, so in divining a school post the Pressure line stands for the person who gathers and leads the pupils. If Pressure does not appear or falls Void, there will surely be no one to gather pupils to make up a school, the matter is hard to bring about, and no one will recommend one; if it is Void and meets a clash, or is hidden and drawn up, one must beg that person before he is willing to come forward and gather them.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.39** 应爻空亡，没有人来聘请；再如果父母不出现或者落空，必定难成；应爻动空、化空，是说空话、假装作主，不然也不能把事做完。父母临身，或者动爻来生合世身，是大吉的兆头，这个馆容易成；有这种象而应空、官鬼动，虽然有招集学生的人，却没有聘请的主人，适合自己开馆；官鬼空伏而应来生合，虽然有聘请的人家，却没有招集学生的人，适合自己去招来学生。(《黄金策》)
  If the Other is Void, no one comes to engage one; if Resource also does not appear or falls Void, the matter will surely be hard to bring about; if the Other moves into Void or turns into Void, he only says it in words and pretends to be the master, and otherwise the matter is not seen through to the end. If Resource rests on the Body, or a moving line comes to generate or combine with the Self or the Body, it is a greatly auspicious sign and the school comes easily; with this image but the Other Void and Pressure moving, though there is someone to gather pupils there is no master to engage one, and it is fitting to open a school oneself; if Pressure is Void and hidden while the Other comes to generate or combine, though there is a household that would engage one there is no one to gather pupils, and it is fitting to draw in pupils oneself.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.40** 文书（父母）是占馆的用神，遇到财爻发动就被克坏：没成的不能成，已经成的也不称意；世爻、身爻、日辰、月建都忌讳临着财爻；如果父母持月建，或者临在生旺之地，或许还能成。(《黄金策》)
  Resource, the document, is the Subject in divining a school post, and if Wealth moves it is controlled and spoiled: what has not yet come about cannot, and what has come about is not to one's liking; the Self, the Body, the Day and the Month all dislike resting on Wealth; if Resource holds the Month or rests in a place of birth or thriving, it may perhaps still come through.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.41** 卦里父母出现、应爻来生合，而世爻空亡的，不是馆不成，是自己不肯上前，或者虽然成了却不去；应爻不生合、父母重叠而世爻空亡的，是另有别的馆，所以不成这一个；世爻无缘无故自己空亡，必定不能成，即使成了也终究不如意。(《黄金策》)
  If Resource appears in the hexagram and the Other comes to generate or combine while the Self is Void, it is not that the school will not come about but that one does not step forward oneself, or that even if it comes about one does not go; if the Other does not generate or combine, Resource is doubled and the Self is Void, there is another school, and so this one does not come about; if the Self falls Void by itself without cause, it will surely not come about, and even if it does it is in the end not as one wishes.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.43** 间爻（世应之间的爻）发动，事情多有阻隔，纵然卦象吉也难成。(《黄金策》)
  If a between line (a line between the Self and the Other) moves, the matter is often obstructed, and even if the hexagram is auspicious it is hard to bring about.
- **LY.CAREER.YY.07** 候选（等待选官）——能选上：官星临在身爻、世爻上，财爻单独发动；或者世爻、官星加文书、贵人，带青龙或朱雀发动；或者太岁、月建带着官星、印星来生合世身。选得迟：官星伏在世爻下，或伏着驿马、印绶。选在下等：世爻持官星、贵人遇升爻，但休囚受克。选不上：卦里没有官星、印星，或它们空绝，或身爻世爻空绝，或子孙持世旺动、子孙单独发动；官鬼变官鬼，事情多反复；官鬼发动而财空绝，选官的衙门没有力量；官星入绝但逢生，事虽有阻挠，终得贵人之力而成；身爻世爻随官鬼入墓，选不成，成了也不如意；遇丁未、戊戌二杀之墓，即使选了官、到了任，也要带着亲人回乡；官鬼带亡神劫杀大杀克世身，选了官也不得赴任；有官星而无父母，或父母空死墓绝胎，没有任所。(《易隐》)
  Awaiting selection. Selected: the star of office sits on the Body or the Self and the Wealth line alone moves; or the Self and the star of office carry the document and the Noble and move with the Azure Dragon or the Vermilion Bird; or Tai Sui and the Month carry the star of office and seal and generate or combine with the Self and Body. Late selection: the star of office or the Post Horse or seal lies hidden under the Self. A low selection: the Self holds the star of office and the Noble on a promotion line but is resting or trapped and controlled. Not selected: the hexagram has no star of office or seal, or they are Void or extinct, or the Body and Self are Void or extinct, or Output holds the Self and is thriving and moving, or Output alone moves. Pressure turning into Pressure: the matter goes back and forth; Pressure moves while Wealth is Void or extinct: the selecting office has no power; the star of office extinct but meeting generation: the matter meets obstruction but is in the end achieved through a patron's help; the Body and Self following Pressure into the tomb: the selection fails, and if it succeeds it is not as wished; meeting the tombs of the two baleful spirits Dingwei and Wuxu: though selected and arrived at the post, he travels home with his kin; Pressure carrying the Lost Spirit, Robbery and great baleful spirits and controlling the Self and Body: though selected he cannot go to his post; the star of office present but no Resource, or Resource Void, dead, in tomb, extinct or in the womb stage: there is no post.
- **LY.CAREER.YY.10** 领凭证：以文书（父母）为用神。文书旺相，到它入墓的日子可以领；衰弱，到它生旺的日子可以领；伏藏，到它当值的日子领。卦里没有父母，或者父母临空、死、墓、绝、胎，等很久也领不到；父母变出父母，文凭不实在。(《易隐》)
  Collecting the credentials: the document line is the Subject. If it is thriving, the credentials can be collected on the day it enters the tomb; if weak, on a day of its birth or strength; if hidden, on the day in which it rules. If the hexagram has no Resource line, or Resource sits on Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, one waits long and does not get them; if Resource turns into Resource, the credentials are not genuine.
- **LY.CAREER.YY.29** 起复，重新起用：官星遇到三传（太岁、月建、日辰）生扶的，必定起复；世爻下伏着官星，或伏着印星、驿马的，必定在官星、印星当值的年月日起复；官星、印星持身爻、世爻，旺相安静的，必定在冲动官星、印星的年月日起复；旁边的爻带贵人、驿马，旺相发动，来生身爻、世爻上的官星、印星的，必得贵人提拔，在相生的年月日起复；旁边的兄弟爻旺相发动，带亡神、劫杀、隔神来刑、冲、克、害身爻、世爻上的官星、印星的，必有小人从中阻隔、坏事。(《易隐》)
  Being recalled to office. If the star of office meets generation and support from the three rulers, he is surely recalled; if the star of office lies hidden under the Self, or the seal and the Horse lie hidden, he is recalled in the year, month or day when the star of office and seal rule; if the star of office and seal hold the Body and Self, thriving and still, he is recalled in the year, month or day that clashes them into motion; if a side line carrying the Noble and the Horse is thriving and moving and generates the star of office and seal on the Body and Self, he gets a patron's promotion and is recalled in the year, month or day of generation; if a side Peer line is thriving and moving, carrying the Lost Spirit, Robbery and the obstructing spirit, and punishes, clashes, controls or harms the star of office and seal on the Body and Self, there will surely be bad men who stand between and spoil the matter.
- **LY.CAREER.YY.71** 文书的占问，有公事和私事的分别：虽然都以父母爻为用神，但公事占以官星旺相为助，私事占以子孙旺相为助。三传（太岁、月建、日辰）刑、害、克、破父母爻的，不成；卦里没有官星、父爻，或官星、父爻临空、绝，以及化成空、绝的，不是发不下来，就是遗失；父爻值墓、胎，以及化成墓、胎，或者加勾陈、土爻而旺相发动的，必定被阻滞耽搁拖延月日。在官的人占文凭、诰敕、公文、差委的札付，只要身爻、世爻坐着禄、驿马、贵人、官星、印绶，或者三传值官星、印星发动来生合身爻、世爻的，必定可以得到。为经商占盐引、钞文，以及居家占契券、田地凭帖，只要父母爻旺相发动生合世爻、身爻，而财爻、子孙旺相安静的，有；应爻空、绝、墓、胎，以及世爻持财爻加玄武发动的，没有（财爻克文书，玄武克朱雀）。(《易隐》)
  Documents differ between public and private matters: though Resource is the Subject in both, in a public matter the star of office being thriving helps, and in a private matter Output being thriving helps. If the three rulers punish, harm, control or break Resource, it does not come about; if the hexagram has no star of office or seal line, or they sit on Void or extinction or turn into Void or extinction, it is either not issued or lost; if the seal line meets tomb or womb, or turns into tomb or womb, or carries Hook Earth and an Earth line and is thriving and moving, it will surely be blocked and delayed for months and days. For one in office divining credentials, imperial edicts, official letters and orders of appointment, if the Body and Self sit on Emolument, the Horse, the Noble, the star of office and the seal and ribbon, or the three rulers on the star of office and seal move to generate or combine with the Body and Self, they can surely be had. - **LY.CAREER.YY.83** 谋事能不能成，以世爻、应爻为主，内卦是谋事的人。内卦克外卦、世爻克应爻，外卦、应爻生内卦、世爻，或者内外卦、世应都旺相比和的，事情可以谋，谋也容易成；月建、日辰合世、应的，对方心里虽然不愿意，也会答应成全；间爻、官鬼发动生合世、应的，有人劝说成全。谋官，逢官星旺相生合世身、子孙衰弱安静的，成；谋财，逢财爻旺相生合世身、兄弟衰弱安静的，成；谋文书，逢父爻旺相生合世身、财爻衰弱安静的，成。凡事都嫌兄弟发动，但如果兄弟带着贵人、禄一起生世爻，就是替你吹嘘推荐的人；凡事都喜欢福神发动，求名（功名）却忌讳它。这是可谋而成的占。(《易隐》)
  Whether a scheme can succeed takes the Self and Other as the chief, and the inner trigram is the man who schemes. If the inner controls the outer, the Self controls the Other, the outer and the Other generate the inner and Self, or the inner and outer and the Self and Other are all thriving and in harmony, the matter can be planned and is easily done; if the Month and Day combine with the Self and Other, though the other side is unwilling at heart he will agree and see it through; if a line between or Pressure moves to generate or combine with the Self and Other, someone persuades him to see it through. Scheming for office, if the star of office is thriving and generates or combines with the Self and Body while Output is weak and still, it succeeds; scheming for wealth, if Wealth is thriving and generates or combines with the Self and Body while Peer is weak and still, it succeeds; scheming for a document, if Resource is thriving and generates or combines with the Self and Body while Wealth is weak and still, it succeeds. ## 任所(In office: how the post goes, the term, dismissal, impeachment, leaving office)→ `任所.md`

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.08** 世爻发动又遇空亡：没做官的，卦中世爻发动，必定得不到京官、牧守一类的职位，不过如果是出巡的职位，反而顺利；已经做官的遇到这种情形，居官不久，再遇日辰、动爻相冲，必定不能长久任职理政。(《黄金策》)
  If the Self moves and meets Void: for one not yet in office, with the Self moving in the hexagram, one will surely get no post as a capital official or a governor or magistrate, though if it is a post of touring and inspecting it goes smoothly on the contrary; for one already in office, meeting this means one will not stay long in office, and if the Day or a moving line also clashes it, one will surely not hold the post and govern for long.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.10** 出巡的官，适合官鬼发动；牧守一类的官，适合官鬼安静。如果官鬼发动、化出子孙，必有别的官员来代理职务，不然也会被别人抢在前面；子孙发动化出官鬼，就是先难后易，或者先凶后吉；官鬼和子孙都发动，也主有官员来接替。(《黄金策》)

If Pressure moves and turns into Output, another officer will surely come to take over the post, or one is otherwise forestalled by someone else; if Output moves and turns into Pressure, it is hard first and easy later, or ominous first and fortunate later; if both Pressure and Output move, it likewise means an officer comes to replace one.
- **LY.CAREER.HJC.14** 兄弟是僚属，也就是同僚、下属一类的官。卦中官鬼发动、化出兄弟，冲克世爻，主同僚不和；或者兄弟化出官鬼而刑冲克害，或者兄弟带三刑六害伤害世爻，也都是这样；世爻克兄弟，是我欺负他，因而不和。(《黄金策》)

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.16** 卦静而世爻空亡，是退休的兆头；身爻空亡而煞发动，是躲避祸事的征兆。世爻空亡，没做官的没有选用的日期；已经做官的，如果六爻安静、月建日辰太岁没有伤它，就是休官改任的象；如果官鬼发动、被月建日辰太岁伤克，是躲避祸患、脱离灾难的兆头；卦静而日辰冲，想回家却被留住；煞动而日辰合，想躲避却躲不开。(《黄金策》)

- **LY.CAREER.HJC.17** 遇到凶兆的时候，如果官鬼爻临身持世，或者本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，虽然被责罚，官职还在；如果不临身持世，也不伏在世爻下面，或者虽然伏着仍遇到空亡，必定被革去官带贬为平民，不只是责罚而已。(《黄金策》)

Juezi says: first understand the intent of the person who comes; for one asking for rank and profit this is how to judge; if he asks whether, not yet employed, he can be taken on, the star of office is the officer who takes him on: if it comes to generate or combine with the Self he will surely be taken on, and if the Pressure line controls the Self or is Void or broken he will not be enrolled.

  When Output holds the Self or moves in a memorial: Output is the spirit that controls the star of office, and for an official with a post who submits a memorial it is not good to see it: at the lightest there is demotion and punishment, at the worst dismissal; for a man without office one must look at the thought of the one who comes: if he does this in order to seek a name, seeing Output means he will surely not gain a name; if he fears that it may bring him misfortune, seeing Output means there will surely be none.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.32** 上书时世爻临空、临破，难以挽回圣意：世爻临旬空、月破，以及临绝、变绝、入三墓的，得不到批准，应当早点察觉时机；世爻发动变凶，或者太岁、五爻、日月、动爻冲克世爻的，不但难以挽回，而且要防备想不到的祸患。(《增删卜易》)
  In a memorial, if the Self falls on Void or break, it is hard to turn heaven round: if the Self falls on Void or Month-break, or on extinction or turns into extinction, or enters the three tombs, the request will not be approved, and one should read the moment early; if the Self moves and turns bad, or Tai Sui, the fifth line, the Day, the Month or a moving line clashes with or controls the Self, it is not only hard to turn heaven round but one must guard against unforeseen misfortune.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.34** 上书最忌官爻克世爻，也嫌弃帮助官鬼伤害自身：官鬼克世已经预兆凶象，再加上财发动来帮助官鬼的，灾祸难免；世爻变出官鬼、变出回头克、随官鬼入墓、卦得反吟、卦变成绝、世爻被冲克的，事情如果不是重大的，要赶紧停止。(《增删卜易》)
  In a memorial what is most to be avoided is the Pressure line controlling the Self, and also disliked is helping Pressure to injure the Body: when Pressure controls the Self it already foretells ill, and if Wealth also moves to help Pressure the misfortune cannot be avoided; if the Self turns into Pressure, or into a returning control, or follows Pressure into the tomb, or the hexagram gets Reversed Chant or changes into extinction, or the Self is clashed or controlled, unless the matter is grave it should be stopped at once.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.35** 太岁生世必须在卦爻里出现才算数，不在卦爻中就生不了世；五爻生世当作吉的，也要防它受克、落空、衰弱、被破、入绝，有生的名义没有生的实际；六爻恍惚不定就不要上书。野鹤说：真有益于国的事，舍身为国也不辞，不必占卜；事情可做可不做之间的，必须世爻与父爻都没有刑冲克破，而且太岁、五爻、日月相生，才可以做；爻中乱动、用神元神非旺非衰、克多生少的，就应当停止。(《增删卜易》)
  For Tai Sui to generate the Self it must appear among the lines, and if it is not among the lines it cannot generate the Self; taking the fifth line generating the Self as auspicious, one must also guard against its being controlled, Void, weak, broken or extinct, which would give the name of generation and not the substance; if the six lines are vague and unsettled, do not submit the memorial. The Old Man of Yehe says: if the matter truly benefits the state one gives oneself for the country and refuses nothing, and there is no need of divination; for a matter that could go either way, it may be done only if the Self and the Resource line are free of punishment, clash, control and break and Tai Sui, the fifth line, the Day and the Month generate them; if the lines move in disorder, the Subject and Support are neither thriving nor weak, or controls outnumber generations, it should be stopped.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.37** 为求功名而上书，忌讳子孙发动，忌讳兄弟临身：父母持世、文书再旺，只要子孙一动伤官，为名的人文书旺也没用；旺相的文书生世、但兄弟持世，上司的奏章可以上去，自己的钱财终究要破，销账不准。(《增删卜易》)
  When one submits a memorial to seek a name, Output moving is to be avoided and Peer on the Body is to be avoided: if Resource holds the Self and the document is extremely thriving, once Output moves to injure the star of office a thriving document is of no use to one who seeks a name; if a thriving document generates the Self but Peer holds the Self, the superior's memorial goes up but one's own money is bound to be lost and the write-off is not approved.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.38** 防备被参劾、担心大计考核、案子还没结的人：世爻与官星都旺相，再有日月、动爻生扶，或者世爻、官爻临在日月上，或者发动变吉，就没有什么可忧虑的：防参劾的无忧，已被参劾而未结案的无忧，担心大计的不但无忧，还一定被评为卓异；凡是旺相的官星持世，也同样推断。(《增删卜易》)
  For one who guards against impeachment, fears the great evaluation, or has a case not yet closed: if the Self and the star of office are both thriving, and the Day, Month or a moving line also generates and supports them, or the Self or the Pressure line sits on the Day or Month, or they move and turn auspicious, there is nothing to fear: one guarding against impeachment has nothing to fear, one already impeached whose case is not closed has nothing to fear, and one who fears the great evaluation not only has nothing to fear but will surely be rated outstanding; whenever a thriving star of office holds the Self, judge in the same way.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.39** 子孙持世、子孙在卦中发动、世爻发动变出子孙，官星被破或落空，以及发动变出回头克，都主被罢职、休官。(《增删卜易》)
  If Output holds the Self, or Output moves in the hexagram, or the Self moves and turns into Output, or the star of office is broken or Void, or it moves and turns into a returning control, all mean dismissal and removal from office.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.40** 被参劾的情形：子孙发动、财落空，减俸、罚俸；兄弟持世或发动，或者财被破、财落空，以及财发动变凶，如果官爻旺相，不过罚俸而已；官星如果失陷，那么利益和功名都丧失了。(《增删卜易》)
  In the case of impeachment: with Output moving and Wealth Void, there is a cut in salary and a fine; if Peer holds the Self or moves, or Wealth is broken or Void, or Wealth moves and turns bad, then if the Pressure line is thriving it comes to no more than a fine; if the star of office is lost, both gain and rank are lost.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.41** 世爻衰弱、官鬼来克，遭贬责、受凌辱。世爻衰弱又得不到生扶，又有太岁、五爻、日月、动爻刑冲克世，或者官星发动克世，或者世爻随官鬼入墓，世爻发动变鬼、变克，以及白虎、螣蛇刑克世爻的，轻的贬官受罚，重的坐牢。轻重要看世爻有没有救：世爻有救，是轻的；世爻休囚又被刑伤冲克，是重的。(《增删卜易》)
  With the Self weak and Pressure controlling it, there is demotion, reproof and abuse. If the Self is weak and gets no generation or support, and Tai Sui, the fifth line, the Day, the Month or a moving line punishes, clashes with or controls the Self, or the star of office moves to control the Self, or the Self follows Pressure into the tomb, or the Self moves and turns into Pressure or into a control, or the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake punishes or controls the Self, then at the lightest there is demotion and punishment, at the worst prison. Light or heavy depends on whether the Self has a rescue: it is light when the Self has a rescue, and heavy when the Self is resting or trapped and is also punished, injured, clashed or controlled.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.43** 世爻失陷但遇到生扶，凶煞兴旺也没有妨碍。古人有「身空煞动是避祸的征兆」的说法，增删卜易认为不对：忌神在卦中发动，世爻落空，等到世爻出空的月份日子，必定遭它的害，怎么能避开？只有世爻落空而遇到元神发动来生它，卦中即使有忌神发动也没有妨碍，因为等到世爻出空那天，忌神反而去生元神，元神再生世爻。(《增删卜易》)
  If the Self is lost but meets generation, there is no harm even when baleful spirits flourish. The ancients said 'a Void Body and a moving baleful spirit are a sign of avoiding disaster', which the Zeng Shan Bu Yi holds to be wrong: when the Adversary moves in the hexagram and the Self is Void, in the month or on the day the Self leaves the Void it will surely be harmed, so how could it avoid it? Only when the Self is Void and meets the Support moving to generate it is there no harm even though the Adversary moves, because by the day the Self leaves the Void the Adversary instead generates the Support and the Support generates the Self.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.44** 官星兴旺而世爻失陷，自身受辱但官位还在：凡是遇到凶兆，如果官爻持世，或者官星伏在世爻下面而且旺相，虽然受到责罚，官职还保留（古说：身边伏鬼若不落空，官职还在）。野鹤说：世爻被五爻所克，只要官星旺相、生合世爻，或者旺相的官星持世，虽然遭到羞辱，仍然会有官职。(《增删卜易》)
  With the star of office flourishing and the Self lost, one is humiliated but keeps the office: whenever an ill omen comes, if the Pressure line holds the Self, or the star of office lies hidden under the Self and is thriving, one is censured and punished but the office remains (the old saying: if Pressure lies hidden beside the Body and is not Void, the office remains). The Old Man of Yehe says: if the Self is controlled by the fifth line but the star of office is thriving and generates or combines with the Self, or a thriving star of office holds the Self, one meets disgrace but still has office.
- **LY.CAREER.ZS.45** 自身实在、官星失陷，官位丢了但人平安：世爻旺相、不受伤克，而官星被破、落空，或被刑冲克害，或发动变凶，官职俸禄虽然丢了，人不会受伤害。(《增删卜易》)
  With the Body solid and the star of office lost, the post goes but the person is safe: if the Self is thriving and not injured or controlled, while the star of office is broken or Void, or punished, clashed, controlled or harmed, or moves and turns bad, the office and salary are lost but the person is not harmed.
