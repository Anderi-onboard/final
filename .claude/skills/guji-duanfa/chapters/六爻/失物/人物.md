## LY.LOST.HJC.08 逃亡·动合伏财必拐妇人而去：用爻发动去合本宫的财爻，那人必定拐带妇人逃走；财伏在世爻下是妻妾，伏在应爻下是邻家妇女

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻发动，去和伏着的财爻相合，一定是拐带妇人逃走。用爻发动来与本宫的财爻合，那人必定拐带妇人逃去；财爻如果伏在世爻之下，必是自己的妻妾；伏在应爻之下，是邻居家的妇女。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject moves and combines with a hidden Wealth line, he has surely run off with a woman. A Subject that moves to combine with the Wealth line of its own palace means he has run off with a woman; if Wealth is hidden under the Self, it is surely his own wife or concubine, and if under the Other, a woman of a neighbouring family.

**《黄金策·逃亡》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2923 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Runaways", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2923*

## LY.LOST.HJC.11 逃亡·合起合住若非容隐即相留：用爻静逢合是合起、动逢合是合住，日辰动爻合起用神，必有窝藏容留的人，看合爻定是什么人，合爻与世冲克的决不会来报

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:合起或合住，不是容留隐藏就是挽留他。用爻静遇合是合起，用爻动遇合是合住；如果日辰或动爻合起用神，必有窝藏容忍留住他的人。要知道留他的是什么人，用合爻来定，比如合爻在子孙是僧道，在父母是尊长一类。合爻与世爻冲克的，决不会来报信。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Combined up or combined shut, either he is sheltered and hidden or he is held. A still Subject meeting a combination is combined up; a moving Subject meeting a combination is combined shut; if the Day or a moving line combines the Subject up, someone must be sheltering, tolerating and keeping him. To know who keeps him, fix it by the combining line: on Output it is a monk or Daoist, on Resource an elder, and so on. A combining line that clashes or controls the Self will never come to report.

**《黄金策·逃亡》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2929 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Runaways", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2929*

## LY.LOST.HJC.13 逃亡·动爻刑克有人阻彼登程、日建生扶有伴纠他同去：用爻逢冲是被人喝破，遇克是被人捉住，有扶有并有生是有人约他同去；这些刑克之爻与世有情的必定来报信

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:动爻刑克用爻，有人阻止他上路；日建生扶用爻，有同伴拉他一起走。用爻遇冲，是被人喝破；遇克，是被人捉住；有扶、有并、有生，是有人约他同去。以上这些刑克的爻如果和世爻有情，必定来告诉我。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If a moving line punishes or controls the Subject, someone stops him from setting out; if the Day Branch gives life to or supports it, a companion draws him along. A Subject meeting a clash means he is shouted down; meeting control, he is caught; with support, with parity, or with life, someone invites him to go along. If the punishing and controlling lines above have feeling toward the Self, they will surely come to tell me.

## LY.LOST.HJC.14 逃亡·间爻作合原中必定知情：间爻是原来的保人，没有保人就看邻里，间爻与用爻相合必知内情，再与世爻冲克，必是这个人诱他走的

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:间爻与用爻相合，当初的中人必定知道内情。间爻是原来的保人，没有保人，就按邻居来断。间爻如果和用爻相合，必定知道内情；再和世爻冲克，一定是这个人把他诱走的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If a line between the Self and the Other combines with the Subject, the original go-between surely knows what happened. The between lines are the original guarantor; where there was no guarantor, judge by the neighbours. If a between line combines with the Subject, it surely knows the inside story; and if it also clashes or controls the Self, this person must be the one who lured him away.

## LY.LOST.HJC.47 失脱·与世刑冲必是冤仇相聚、与福交变必然僧道同谋：鬼爻与世爻刑冲，贼是有仇怨的人；与世生合，是沾亲带故的人；鬼化子孙、子孙化鬼，必有僧道夹在其中

## LY.LOST.ZS.02 六神章·玄武主盗贼，朱雀主是非：吉凶之外，六神是附和之神，向吉的遇青龙更吉，卦凶的遇蛇虎更凶，玄武主盗贼没有不验的

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六神不是不灵，是附和的神：原来吉的遇到青龙更吉，卦凶的遇到螣蛇白虎更凶。玄武主盗贼，朱雀主是非，没有不灵验的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The six spirits are not unreliable but are spirits that chime in: what was lucky is more so when it meets the Azure Dragon, and an ill hexagram is more so when it meets the Coiling Snake or the White Tiger. The Dark Tortoise governs thieves and the Vermilion Bird governs quarrels, and neither fails to be borne out.
