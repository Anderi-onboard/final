## LY.LOST.BSZZ.02 六亲发动诀、诸爻持世诀·失物句：父动失物逃亡要诉论，官鬼克兄弟失脱难寻多暗昧，财爻发动失物静安家未出，鬼爻持世财物时时忧失脱

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻发动，丢了东西和逃走的人要靠告状追论；官鬼克兄弟，失脱的东西难找、多半说不清；财爻发动，失物安静不动，没有出家门；鬼爻持世，财物时常担忧失脱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Resource line moves, lost things and runaways must be pursued by lodging a complaint; when Pressure controls the Peer line, lost goods are hard to find and mostly murky; when the Wealth line moves and the lost thing is still and safe, it has not left the house; when Pressure holds the Self, one is forever worried about losing goods.

## LY.LOST.HJC.09 逃亡·内近外远生世则终有归期：用爻在本宫内卦人在本地，本宫外卦在别府别县，他宫内卦在外县交界，他宫外卦在他府外县，在六爻则去远方；最喜生合持世，人虽去，日后自己回来，寻也容易见到

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:内卦近、外卦远；用爻生世爻，终究有回来的日子。用爻在本宫内卦，人在本地；在本宫外卦，在别的府县；在他宫内卦，在外县交界处；在他宫外卦，在他府外县；如果在上爻，已经去了远方。最喜欢用爻生合持世，那人虽然走了，日后会自己回来，去找也容易找到。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The inner trigram is near and the outer far; if the Subject gives life to the Self, there is in the end a day of return. A Subject in the inner trigram of its own palace means he is still in the locality; in the outer trigram of its own palace, in another prefecture or county; in the inner trigram of another palace, at the border of another county; in the outer trigram of another palace, in another prefecture and county; if on the top line, he has gone far away. Best of all is the Subject giving life to or combining with the Self or holding the Self: though he has gone, he will come back of himself later, and a search finds him easily.

## LY.LOST.HJC.10 逃亡·静易动难坐空则必无寻路：用爻不动人容易找，动了就迁移无常、指东说西或改名换姓，难找；落旬空则杳无踪迹

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻静容易找，动则难找；用爻落空就一定没有找的门路。用爻不动，那人容易找到；发动则迁移不定，指东说西，甚至改名换姓，必然难找。如果落了空亡，杳无踪迹。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A still Subject is easy and a moving one hard; a Subject that falls into the Void leaves no way to search at all. If the Subject does not move, the man is easy to find; if it moves, he shifts about without fixed course, pointing east and saying west or even changing his name, and he is surely hard to find. If it falls into the Void, there is no trace of him.

## LY.LOST.HJC.16 逃亡·无冲无破居六位则一去不回、有克有生在五爻则半途仍走：用爻不受刑冲克害又不生合世爻、世爻也不克它，逃者不想回、找的人也见不到；动爻日辰克制用爻是可擒之兆，变出的爻反生合用爻则被捕后仍逃走

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻没有冲没有破、落在上爻，就一去不回；有克有生、在五爻，就半路上仍然会走。用爻不受刑冲克害，又不生合世爻，世爻也不克它，是逃的人不想念着回来、找的人见不到的象，是一去不回之象。如果动爻或日辰克制用爻，是能擒获的兆头；如果变出的爻反过来生合用爻，捕获之后仍会逃走。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With no clash and no break and the Subject on the top line, he goes and never returns; with control and life and the Subject on the fifth line, he slips away again halfway. A Subject that suffers no punishment, clash, control or harm, gives no life or combination to the Self, and is not controlled by the Self, is the image of a runaway who does not think of returning and a searcher who cannot see him, the image of going and never coming back. If a moving line or the Day controls the Subject, it is a sign he can be seized; if a changed line gives life to or combines with the Subject, he escapes again even after being caught.

## LY.LOST.HJC.19 逃亡·归魂卦用遇生合不捕而自回、游魂卦应又交重能潜而会遁：得归魂卦他回家心切，生合世爻他自己会回来，寻也容易见；得游魂卦他没有回来的意思，能躲会逃，寻必难见

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:归魂卦、用神遇到生合，不用捕他自己会回来；游魂卦、应爻又交重发动，他能潜伏，会逃遁。得归魂卦，他回来的心意急切，如果生合世爻，他一定自己回来，找他容易见到；遇到游魂卦，那人必定没有回来的意思，能潜伏会逃遁，找他必定难见。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a Returning Soul hexagram, if the Subject meets life or combination, he comes back by himself without being caught; in a Wandering Soul hexagram, with the Other also crowded with movement, he can lie low and slip away. ## LY.LOST.HJC.27 逃亡·世应空亡独发徒费乎心：世空则去找的不紧，用空则找也找不到，世应都空必定白跑一趟；兄弟独发是虚假不实，也找不到

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世应都空亡、独自发动，是白费心思。世爻空，去找的人不上心；用爻空，找也找不到；世应都空，必定没有可找的地方，空回一趟。兄弟独自发动，是虚假不实，也找不到。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With the Self and the Other both Void and one moving alone, it is a waste of effort. If the Self is Void, the searcher does not press the search; if the Subject is Void, he is not found however one searches; with both Void there is surely no place to search and he returns empty-handed. A Peer line moving alone means falsehood and nothing real, and he is not found either.

## LY.LOST.ZS.05 反伏章·占盗贼、官非，见而又见：反吟卦聚散不常，占盗贼官非的是见了还会再见

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Reversed Chant hexagram gathers and scatters without constancy. In divining for thieves and lawsuits, it is seen and seen again.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 2403 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 2403*
