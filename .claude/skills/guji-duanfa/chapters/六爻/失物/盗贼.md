## LY.LOST.BSZZ.01 何知章·失脱与盗贼的几句：遭贼是玄武临财而鬼旺有扶，见失脱是玄武带鬼而应爻发动，失衣裳是勾陈玄武入财乡，损六畜是白虎带鬼临所属，失牛是五爻丑鬼落空，失鸡是初爻带鬼玄武欺

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:怎么知道人家遇上贼：玄武临在财爻，鬼爻又旺又有扶助。怎么知道人家丢了东西：玄武带着鬼爻，应爻发动。怎么知道人家丢了衣服：勾陈、玄武落在财的位置。怎么知道人家损了牲畜：白虎带着鬼爻，临在所属的位置。怎么知道人家丢了牛：五爻丑土鬼爻落空，令人发愁。怎么知道人家丢了鸡：初爻带鬼，又有玄武欺它。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** How do we know a household has met thieves? The Dark Tortoise sits on Wealth and Pressure is strong and supported. How do we know the household has lost things? The Dark Tortoise carries Pressure and the Other line moves. How do we know they have lost clothes? The Hook Earth and the Dark Tortoise enter the Wealth position. How do we know livestock will be lost? The White Tiger carries Pressure and sits on the line it belongs to. How do we know an ox is lost? The Pressure of the fifth line at Chou falls into the Void, and there is cause for worry. How do we know chickens are lost? The first line carries Pressure and the Dark Tortoise preys on it.

## LY.LOST.HJC.39 失脱·鬼在空中世动则自家所失：卦里没有官鬼或官鬼落空，而世爻发动，是自己遗失的，不是被人偷去

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼落空、世爻发动，就是自己家里丢失的。卦里没有官鬼或官鬼落空而世爻发动，是自己遗失，不是被人偷去。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure falls into the Void and the Self moves, it is lost by oneself. When the hexagram has no Pressure or Pressure falls into the Void and the Self moves, one lost it oneself and it was not stolen by anyone.

## LY.LOST.HJC.40 失脱·财伏应下世合则假贷于人：官鬼或空或伏或死绝不动，而财临应上或伏应下，是自己借给了别人；借给谁用应爻所临的六亲定

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻伏在应爻下、又与世爻合，是借给别人了。官鬼落空、或伏藏、或死绝不动，而财爻临在应爻上或伏在应爻下，就是自己借给别人的。要知道借给了谁，用应爻所临的六亲来定，比如临子孙，是年幼晚辈一类。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Wealth is hidden under the Other and combines with the Self, it has been lent to someone. If Pressure is Void, hidden, or dead or extinct and still, while Wealth sits on the Other or is hidden under it, one lent it to someone oneself. To know whom, fix it by the relation the Other sits on; on Output, for example, it is a younger person.

## LY.LOST.HJC.43 失脱·财伏逢冲必是移物动：财爻伏在卦中，遇动爻日辰暗冲，若鬼爻衰弱安静，是东西被人挪动了，不是被偷

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财伏遇冲，一定是东西被人挪动。财爻伏在卦中，遇到动爻或日辰暗中冲它，如果鬼爻衰弱安静，东西是被人移动了，不是人偷的。

## LY.LOST.HJC.44 失脱·鬼兴出现定为贼窃人偷：鬼不上卦或落空、衰绝不动都不是人偷，游魂卦多是忘记，鬼爻变动才是人偷

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼发动出现，一定是贼偷人窃。官鬼不上卦或落了空亡，或衰绝不动，都不是人偷的；游魂卦，多半是忘记了；如果鬼爻变动，才是被人偷了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure appearing and moving means it was surely stolen by a thief. If Pressure is not on the hexagram, falls into the Void, or is weak, extinct and still, it was not stolen; in a Wandering Soul hexagram it is mostly forgotten; only if the Pressure line changes and moves was it stolen by someone.

## LY.LOST.HJC.50 失脱·木克六爻逾墙而入、金伤三位穿壁而来：看鬼爻五行克哪一处就知从哪里进来——木鬼克土逾墙掘洞、金鬼克木割壁穿篱、火鬼克金劈环开锁、水鬼克火灌水灭灯、土鬼克水涉渎跳涧、木火交化明火执仗；木鬼克上爻翻墙而入，克初爻后门掘洞

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:木克上爻，是翻墙而入；金伤三爻，是穿壁而来。木鬼克土，翻墙挖洞；金鬼克木，割开板壁穿过篱笆；火鬼克金，劈开环扣、撬开锁；水鬼克火，泼水灭灯；土鬼克水，涉过水渠跳过山涧；木火交相变化，是明火执仗。要知道从哪里能进来，用鬼爻克到的地方来定：木鬼克上爻，翻墙而入；克初爻，从后门挖洞而进。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Wood controlling the top line means he came in over the wall; Metal injuring the third place means he came through the partition. Wood Pressure controlling Earth: over the wall or through a dug hole; Metal Pressure controlling Wood: cutting through the planking or the hedge; Fire Pressure controlling Metal: splitting the ring and opening the lock; Water Pressure controlling Fire: dousing the lamp with water; Earth Pressure controlling Water: wading the channel or leaping the gully; Wood and Fire changing into each other: robbers with open torches. To know where he came in, fix it by where the Pressure controls: Wood Pressure controlling the top line, over the wall; controlling the first line, through a hole dug at the back door.

## LY.LOST.ZS.03 千金赋·玄武为盗贼之事亦必官爻、朱雀为口舌之神必须兄弟，作者说此论欠当：玄武、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇动而不克世的没有妨碍，动而克世的都是凶，不必再分兄弟官鬼

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:千金赋说：玄武主盗贼的事，也一定要有官鬼爻；朱雀是口舌之神，一定要有兄弟爻。作者说：这个说法不妥当。玄武、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇发动而不克世爻的，没有妨碍；发动而克世爻的，都算凶，何必再分什么兄弟、官鬼呢？

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Qianjin Fu says: the Dark Tortoise governs matters of thieves, but it too must have the Pressure line; the Vermilion Bird is the spirit of quarrels but it must have the Peer line. The author says: this view is not proper. When the Dark Tortoise, Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth or Coiling Snake move without controlling the Self, there is no harm; when they move and control the Self, all are taken as ill; why distinguish Peer from Pressure at all?

**《增删卜易·千金赋》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 5509 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The Qianjin Fu, as edited in this book", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 5509*

> **打架**:千金赋（黄金策的总断）说玄武主盗贼要有官鬼爻同临才成，朱雀主口舌要兄弟爻同临；增删卜易的作者认为这个说法不当，只要六神发动克世就凶，不分兄弟官鬼。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The Qianjin Fu (the general judgement of the Huangjin Ce) says that the Dark Tortoise governs thieves only with Pressure sitting together, and the Vermilion Bird governs quarrels only with Peer; the author of the Zengshan Buyi holds this improper: a six-spirit that moves and controls the Self is ill whether or not Peer or Pressure is present.
