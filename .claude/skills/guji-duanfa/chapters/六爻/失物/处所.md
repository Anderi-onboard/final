## LY.LOST.HJC.02 逃亡·若临午地必往南方、或化寅爻转移东北：用爻安静以所临之地定方向，用爻发动以变爻定方向

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻若落在午，逃的人必往南方；或者化出寅爻，就转移到东北。用爻安静，以它所临的地支作逃去的方向，所以说临午是南方；用爻发动，以变出的爻定方向；转移的意思是转往另一方，比如午变寅爻，一定先往正南，后往东北。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject line sits on Wu, the runaway has gone south; or if it changes into a Yin line, he moves on to the northeast. A still Subject takes the branch it sits on as the direction of flight, hence Wu means south; a moving Subject takes the changed line to fix the direction; moving on means turning toward another quarter, so that Wu changing to Yin means he surely goes due south first and then to the northeast.

**《黄金策·逃亡》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2909 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Runaways", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2909*

## LY.LOST.HJC.03 逃亡·木属震宫都邑京城之内、金居兑象庵观寺院之中：用爻临震宫木爻必在都邑，临九五必往京城，临兑宫金爻必躲在庵院寺观

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻属木、在震宫，人在都邑京城之内；属金、居兑宫，人在庵观寺院之中。用神如果临震宫木爻，必在都邑；如果临九五，必往京城；如果临兑宫金爻，必躲在庵院寺观之中。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Subject of Wood in the Zhen palace means he is in a city or the capital; of Metal in the Dui palace, in a nunnery, temple or monastery. ## LY.LOST.HJC.05 逃亡·如逢四库当究五行：四库是辰戌丑未，辰是水土之库必在水边，戌是火库在寺庙旁，丑是金库在银铁匠家，未是木库在园林柴草间或木工竹匠家；遇墓爻要等冲破墓爻的日月才见得到

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:如果遇到四库，要追究它的五行。四库就是辰戌丑未四个地支，用爻属木，卦里有未动一类。辰是水和土的库，人必在水边；戌是火库，在寺庙旁边；丑是金库，在银铁匠家；未是木库，在园林柴草之间，或木工竹匠家。凡是占逃亡盗贼，如果遇到墓爻，一定很难找到，要等冲破墓爻的日月才能见到。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the four storehouses are met, examine their element. The four storehouses are the branches Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei; the Subject line is of Wood, and the hexagram has something like an unmoved Wei. Chen is the storehouse of Water and Earth, and he must be by the water; Xu is the storehouse of Fire, by a temple; Chou is the storehouse of Metal, at the house of a silver or iron smith; Wei is the storehouse of Wood, among gardens, woods and brushwood, or at the house of a carpenter or bamboo worker. In divining for a runaway or a thief, if a tomb line is met he is surely hard to find, and he can be seen only in the month or day that clashes the tomb line open.

## LY.LOST.HJC.06 逃亡·倘伏五乡岂宜一类：卦里没有用神就看它伏在哪一爻之下，伏在鬼、父母、兄弟、财、子孙下，以及伏在鬼墓、财库下，各是不同的去处

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:如果用神伏在五种六亲之下，怎么能一概而论。卦里没有用神，就要看它伏在哪一爻之下，才知道那个人在什么地方。伏在鬼爻下，在官仓官库之中，旺相又逢月建，在官户家，休囚无气，在公差吏役家；伏在父母下，在叔伯父母家，不然在手艺人家；伏在兄弟下，在兄弟姊妹和熟识朋友家；伏在财爻下，在奴婢妻妾家；伏在子孙下，在寺观和年幼辈那里。又比如伏在鬼墓之下，不在庙宇中就在寺庵内；伏在财库之下，不在仓库中，就在富豪家。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject is hidden under one of the five relations, how can they all be judged alike? When the hexagram has no Subject, look at which line it is hidden under, and one learns where the person is. Hidden under Pressure, he is in a government granary or storehouse; if strong and also on the Month, in the house of an official family, if resting or without breath, in the house of a clerk or runner. Hidden under Resource, he is at the house of an uncle or parent, otherwise at a craftsman's. Hidden under Peer, he is at the house of brothers and sisters or of acquaintances and friends. Hidden under Wealth, he is at the house of servants, wives or concubines. Hidden under Output, he is at a temple or among the young. Again, hidden under the tomb of Pressure, he is either in a temple or in a nunnery; hidden under the storehouse of Wealth, he is either in a warehouse or at a wealthy family's.

## LY.LOST.HJC.18 逃亡·本宫化入本宫去应不远：本宫卦变成本宫卦（如乾变姤、遁、否、观），人逃在本地，必不远走；用爻在他宫发动又化入他宫，是走远了又转了方向

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:本宫变入本宫，逃去的地方应当不远。本宫仍然变成本宫的卦，比如乾卦变姤、遁、否、观等本宫卦，主那人逃在本地，必不会走远；如果用爻在他宫发动，又变入他宫，是远去之后又转了方向。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the palace changes into its own palace, the place he fled to should not be far. A palace changing into a hexagram of its own palace, for example Qian changing into Gou, Dun, Pi or Guan and the like, means the man fled within the locality and surely has not gone far; if the Subject moves in another palace and changes into yet another palace, he has gone far and then turned in another direction.

## LY.LOST.HJC.30 失脱·内卦本宫搜索家庭可见、他宫外卦追来邻里能知：财在本宫内卦物未出家门，在他宫外卦物已出外难得，在间爻可到邻居家寻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财在本宫内卦，在家里搜索可以找到；财在他宫外卦，到邻居那里追问能知道下落。财在本宫内卦，东西没有出家门，可以找到；财在他宫外卦，东西已经出外，难得回；财在间爻，邻居人家可以去找。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With Wealth in the inner trigram of its own palace, a search of the household can find it; with Wealth in the outer trigram of another palace, inquiring among the neighbours can learn where it went. Wealth in the inner trigram of its own palace means the thing has not left the house and can be found; Wealth in the outer trigram of another palace means the thing has gone out and is hard to get back; Wealth on a between line means the neighbours' houses can be searched.

**《黄金策·失脱》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2969 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lost goods, with theft, catching and arresting thieves", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2969*
