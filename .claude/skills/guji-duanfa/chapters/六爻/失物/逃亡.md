## LY.LOST.BSZZ.03 十八问答·第九问·书中例，占逃仆：占仆以财爻为用神，卯木财伏在二爻午火之下，申金持世克制卯木故终不能逃，伏去生飞是泄气，盗去的财物都窝在炉火人家，断甲子日拿获，果然子日在窝赌的铁匠家申时拿获——应子日是冲克午火飞神、生起卯木伏神

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神不上卦，看伏神在哪一爻之下，能出不能出，各有四种情形：日月生它、日月持它是一；飞神生伏神、动爻生它是二；日月动爻冲克飞神是三；飞神空破休囚、墓绝在日是四；这四种是有用的伏神。书中例：辰月丁巳日占逃走的仆人，得蹇卦：占仆人以财爻为用神，兑宫二爻卯木伏在本卦二爻午火之下，午火是飞神，卯木是伏神；现在申金持世克制卯木，终究不能逃走；但因为伏神去生飞神，叫做泄气，偷去的财物必定全窝在炉火人家，许他甲子日拿获；果然子日得到消息，在窝赌的铁匠家，申时拿到。应在子日，是冲克午火飞神、生起卯木伏神的缘故。《黄金策》说「伏无提挈终徒尔，飞不推开亦枉然」，这一卦的应验就是这样。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Subject does not appear, look at which line the hidden spirit lies under; whether it can come out comes in four ways: the Month or the Day gives it life or holds it, which is one; the flying spirit gives life to the hidden one or a moving line gives it life, two; the Month, Day or a moving line clashes or controls the flying spirit, three; the flying spirit is Void, broken, resting, trapped, or in the tomb or extinct on the Day, four; these four are useful hidden spirits. Worked example: in the Chen month, on a Dingsi day, a man divined about a runaway servant and got Jian: in divining for a servant the Wealth line is the Subject; Mao-wood in the second line of the Dui palace is hidden under Wu-fire in the second line of the present hexagram, Wu-fire being the flying spirit and Mao-wood the hidden spirit; now Shen-metal holds the Self and controls Mao-wood, so he cannot in the end get away; but because the hidden spirit gives life to the flying one, which is called draining, the stolen goods are all harboured in a household with a furnace fire, and it was promised that he would be caught on the Jiazi day; indeed on the Zi day word came that he was at the house of a blacksmith who harboured gamblers, and he was taken in the Shen hour. It answered on the Zi day because Zi clashes and controls Wu-fire, the flying spirit, and raises up Mao-wood, the hidden spirit. The Huangjin Ce says "a hidden spirit without a lift is in vain in the end, and a flying spirit not pushed aside is futile all the same", and this hexagram bore it out.

## LY.LOST.HJC.07 逃亡·木兴水象定乘舟楫而逃：用爻属木在坎宫发动必乘船逃去，木化水、水化木、木在水宫动、水在木宫动都是

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:木发动而见水象，一定是乘船逃走。用爻属木在坎宫发动的，必定乘船逃去；木化水，水化木，或者木在水宫发动，水在木宫发动，都是这样。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Wood moves and a Water image appears, he has surely fled by boat. A Subject of Wood moving in the Kan palace means he has gone by boat; so too Wood changing to Water, Water changing to Wood, Wood moving in a Water palace, or Water moving in a Wood palace.

**《黄金策·逃亡》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2921 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Runaways", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2921*

## LY.LOST.HJC.21 逃亡·子孙发动当有维持：子孙临身世，逃走自然顺利，得日辰生合世爻必有人维持，即使有逆事也不能为害——这是逃亡者自己占

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙发动，会有人维持。子孙临在身爻或世爻，逃去自然顺利；如果得到日辰生合世爻，必定有人维持，即使有不顺的事也不能为害。这是说逃亡的人自己占卜。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When Output moves there will be support. Output on the Body or the Self means his flight goes smoothly of itself; if the Day gives life to or combines with the Self, someone will surely support him, and even adverse things cannot harm him. This speaks of the runaway divining for himself.

**《黄金策·逃亡》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2949 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Runaways", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2949*

## LY.LOST.HJC.22 逃亡·众煞伤身窃恐反遭刑辱：动爻、变爻、日月刑冲克害世爻是重煞伤身，反而遭受刑罚，不逃的好——逃亡者自己占

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:许多凶煞伤害身，恐怕反而遭受刑罚和羞辱。动爻、变爻、日月刑冲克害世爻，叫做重煞伤身，反而遭受刑罚，不逃为吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With many baleful influences injuring the Body, one fears he will instead suffer punishment and disgrace. When moving lines, changed lines, the Month and the Day punish, clash, control or harm the Self, it is called heavy baleful influences injuring the Body, and he will instead suffer punishment; it is lucky not to flee.

## LY.LOST.HJC.23 逃亡·动兄持世必然广费赀财：兄弟持世，费钱才能寻到、也要费钱才能逃；再加玄武旺而发动克世，要防有人劫骗

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟动而持世，一定要花费很多钱财。兄弟持世，花钱可以找到，花钱可以逃走；如果再加上玄武旺而发动、克世爻，要防有人劫掠欺骗。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If a Peer line moves and holds the Self, it will surely cost a great deal of money. With a Peer line holding the Self, spending money lets him be found and spending money lets him flee; if the Dark Tortoise is added, strong and moving to control the Self, beware of someone robbing or cheating.
