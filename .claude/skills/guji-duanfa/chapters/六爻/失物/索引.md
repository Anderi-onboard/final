## 总则(Principles: what stands for the lost thing)→ `总则.md`

- **LY.LOST.HJC.01** 宽容待人，众人会生出侮慢；严厉管人，逃亡就会发生，所以即使大圣人有容人的度量，也说小人难养。要知道逃走的人的实际去向，必须察看用爻。用爻，比如占奴仆、妻妾逃亡，就看财爻这一类。(《黄金策》)
  Treat people with leniency and the crowd turns insolent; manage them with severity and flight begins; hence even the great sage, for all his tolerance, said that petty people are hard to keep. To learn where the runaway has really gone, one must examine the Subject line; for example, in divining for the flight of a servant or a wife or concubine, one looks at the Wealth line and the like.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.28** 百姓饥寒，常有翻墙钻洞的人，不要忘了检点约束，也多有遗失的忧虑。要知道其中的得失，必须详察卦上的妻财。财爻是所失之物的主象：如果得到冲中逢合，所失一定可以得回；如果是合处逢冲，既然失去就不能再得回了。(《黄金策》)
  Common people suffer hunger and cold, and there are often those who climb walls and dig through them; never forget to check and restrain, for there is also a worry of loss. To know the gain or loss in it, one must examine the Wealth line on the hexagram. The Wealth line is the principal image of the thing lost: if a combination is met within a clash, what is lost can surely be got back; if a clash is met at a combination, once lost it cannot be got back.
- **LY.LOST.ZS.01** 占乱臣、贼盗、邪祟，也以官鬼爻为用神；官鬼这一类事物很多，一切拘束我身的都是。(《增删卜易》)
  In divining for rebellious ministers, thieves and robbers, and evil spirits, the Pressure line is also the Subject; Pressure covers many kinds of things, and everything that constrains my person is of it.
- **LY.LOST.ZS.04** 凡是六合卦变成六冲卦，各种占都是先合后离，先亲后疏，先浓后淡，先荣后悴，先好后迟，先通后塞，得到又失去，成功又败坏，事情做成又变。只有占官非、盗贼、了结断绝的事最适宜。(《增删卜易》)
  Whenever a six-combine hexagram changes into a six-clash hexagram, every divination first combines and then parts, first close and then distant, first warm and then cool, first flourishing and then withered, first good and then slow, first open and then blocked, gained and then lost, achieved and then ruined, the matter done and then changed again. Only in divining for lawsuits, thieves, and matters of settling or breaking off is it best.

## 寻得(Whether it can be found)→ `寻得.md`

- **LY.LOST.BSZZ.02** 父母爻发动，丢了东西和逃走的人要靠告状追论；官鬼克兄弟，失脱的东西难找、多半说不清；财爻发动，失物安静不动，没有出家门；鬼爻持世，财物时常担忧失脱。(《卜筮正宗》)
  When the Resource line moves, lost things and runaways must be pursued by lodging a complaint; when Pressure controls the Peer line, lost goods are hard to find and mostly murky; when the Wealth line moves and the lost thing is still and safe, it has not left the house; when Pressure holds the Self, one is forever worried about losing goods.
- **LY.LOST.BSZZ.04** 合是聚、冲是散：冲中逢合，先散后聚，先失后得，先淡后浓；合处逢冲则相反。书中例：寅月戊戌日占丢失的银子和东西能不能失而复得，得巽卦变讼卦：卦得六冲，未土财爻化出午火，回头生合，是失而复得的象；旁人说，应爻持着白虎金鬼，玄武临在财爻，难说能得回；我说，应爻是他人，被午火回头克制，财爻是用神，冲中逢合，日辰又合它，管保一定得到；他问应在哪一天，我说，巳火青龙原神旬空，毛病在巳，必须等乙巳日原神出空值日，会得回；果然应验。(《卜筮正宗》)
  A combination gathers and a clash scatters: a combination met within a clash first scatters and then gathers, first loses and then gains, first cool and then warm; a clash met at a combination is the reverse. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined whether lost silver and goods could be recovered and got Xun changing to Song: the hexagram has six-clash, the Wealth Wei-earth changes into Wu-fire which gives life and combines in return, the image of lost and then regained; onlookers said that the Other holds the White Tiger and Metal Pressure and the Dark Tortoise sits on Wealth, so it is hard to say it will be recovered; I said that the Other is another person, controlled by Wu-fire in return, that Wealth is the Subject, met with a combination within a clash, with the Day also combining it, so it is bound to be had. He asked on what day, and I said: the Support, the Azure Dragon Si-fire, is Void, so the trouble lies in Si, and one must wait for the Yisi day when the Support comes out of the Void and rules; and indeed it was borne out.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.09** 内卦近、外卦远；用爻生世爻，终究有回来的日子。用爻在本宫内卦，人在本地；在本宫外卦，在别的府县；在他宫内卦，在外县交界处；在他宫外卦，在他府外县；如果在上爻，已经去了远方。最喜欢用爻生合持世，那人虽然走了，日后会自己回来，去找也容易找到。(《黄金策》)
  The inner trigram is near and the outer far; if the Subject gives life to the Self, there is in the end a day of return. A Subject in the inner trigram of its own palace means he is still in the locality; in the outer trigram of its own palace, in another prefecture or county; in the inner trigram of another palace, at the border of another county; in the outer trigram of another palace, in another prefecture and county; if on the top line, he has gone far away. Best of all is the Subject giving life to or combining with the Self or holding the Self: though he has gone, he will come back of himself later, and a search finds him easily.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.10** 用爻静容易找，动则难找；用爻落空就一定没有找的门路。用爻不动，那人容易找到；发动则迁移不定，指东说西，甚至改名换姓，必然难找。如果落了空亡，杳无踪迹。(《黄金策》)
  A still Subject is easy and a moving one hard; a Subject that falls into the Void leaves no way to search at all. If the Subject does not move, the man is easy to find; if it moves, he shifts about without fixed course, pointing east and saying west or even changing his name, and he is surely hard to find. If it falls into the Void, there is no trace of him.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.16** 用爻没有冲没有破、落在上爻，就一去不回；有克有生、在五爻，就半路上仍然会走。用爻不受刑冲克害，又不生合世爻，世爻也不克它，是逃的人不想念着回来、找的人见不到的象，是一去不回之象。如果动爻或日辰克制用爻，是能擒获的兆头；如果变出的爻反过来生合用爻，捕获之后仍会逃走。(《黄金策》)
  With no clash and no break and the Subject on the top line, he goes and never returns; with control and life and the Subject on the fifth line, he slips away again halfway. A Subject that suffers no punishment, clash, control or harm, gives no life or combination to the Self, and is not controlled by the Self, is the image of a runaway who does not think of returning and a searcher who cannot see him, the image of going and never coming back. If a moving line or the Day controls the Subject, it is a sign he can be seized; if a changed line gives life to or combines with the Subject, he escapes again even after being caught.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.19** 归魂卦、用神遇到生合，不用捕他自己会回来；游魂卦、应爻又交重发动，他能潜伏，会逃遁。得归魂卦，他回来的心意急切，如果生合世爻，他一定自己回来，找他容易见到；遇到游魂卦，那人必定没有回来的意思，能潜伏会逃遁，找他必定难见。(《黄金策》)
  In a Returning Soul hexagram, if the Subject meets life or combination, he comes back by himself without being caught; in a Wandering Soul hexagram, with the Other also crowded with movement, he can lie low and slip away. In a Returning Soul hexagram his mind is eager to return, and if it gives life to or combines with the Self he surely comes back himself and is easily found; in a Wandering Soul hexagram the man surely has no mind to return, he can lie low and slip away, and a search for him is surely in vain.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.20** 父母爻落空亡，杳无音信。父母爻主音信，遇空就没有信息；如果父母发动来生世爻、合世爻，一定有报信的人来。(《黄金策》)
  If the Resource line falls into the Void, there is no word at all. The Resource line governs news, and meeting the Void it brings no news; if it moves to give life to or combine with the Self, someone will surely come to bring word.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.27** 世应都空亡、独自发动，是白费心思。世爻空，去找的人不上心；用爻空，找也找不到；世应都空，必定没有可找的地方，空回一趟。兄弟独自发动，是虚假不实，也找不到。(《黄金策》)
  With the Self and the Other both Void and one moving alone, it is a waste of effort. If the Self is Void, the searcher does not press the search; if the Subject is Void, he is not found however one searches; with both Void there is surely no place to search and he returns empty-handed. A Peer line moving alone means falsehood and nothing real, and he is not found either.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.29** 用爻自己空、或变成空，都应当搁下不问；财爻日旺月旺，东西总是没有散失，可以得回。用爻自己落空，或发动而化空，都难找到；如果财爻值月令，或在日辰生旺之地，这件东西没有散失，可以找。(《黄金策》)
  A Subject that is Void itself or changes into the Void should be set aside and not pursued; a Wealth line strong on the Day and the Month means the thing has not been scattered and can be got back. A Subject that is Void itself, or moves and changes into the Void, is hard to find; if the Wealth line is on the Month command, or on a place of life and strength on the Day, the thing has not been scattered and can be sought.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.35** 安静而临在世爻上，东西还在，有什么妨碍。占失脱，用爻不适宜发动，发动就有更变；如果得到安静持世，生世合世，东西都主没有散失，必定容易找到，生旺不空尤其好。(《黄金策》)
  Still and sitting on the Self, the thing is still there, so what harm? In divining for lost goods the Subject should not move, for moving brings change; if it is still and holds the Self, or gives life to or combines with the Self, the thing is not scattered and is surely easy to find, and best of all if it is strong and not Void.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.54** 兄弟发动劫财，如果占起获赃物就不必去找了。占起赃和寻物，只要见兄弟发动，都主财物失散，终究难找。(《黄金策》)
  A Peer line moving robs Wealth; if one divines for recovering the stolen goods, there is no need to look. In divining for recovering stolen goods or searching for things, if a Peer line is seen moving, the goods are scattered and in the end hard to find.
- **LY.LOST.ZS.05** 反吟卦聚散没有常规。占盗贼、官非，是见了又见。(《增删卜易》)
  A Reversed Chant hexagram gathers and scatters without constancy. In divining for thieves and lawsuits, it is seen and seen again.
- **LY.LOST.ZS.06** 捕获遇到它，贼深藏难找；失脱遇到它，东西暗藏看不见。(《增删卜易》)
  In catching a thief, meeting it means the thief is deeply hidden and hard to find; in lost goods, meeting it means the thing is hidden away and not seen.

## 处所(Where it is: place and distance)→ `处所.md`

- **LY.LOST.HJC.02** 用爻若落在午，逃的人必往南方；或者化出寅爻，就转移到东北。用爻安静，以它所临的地支作逃去的方向，所以说临午是南方；用爻发动，以变出的爻定方向；转移的意思是转往另一方，比如午变寅爻，一定先往正南，后往东北。(《黄金策》)
  If the Subject line sits on Wu, the runaway has gone south; or if it changes into a Yin line, he moves on to the northeast. A still Subject takes the branch it sits on as the direction of flight, hence Wu means south; a moving Subject takes the changed line to fix the direction; moving on means turning toward another quarter, so that Wu changing to Yin means he surely goes due south first and then to the northeast.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.03** 用爻属木、在震宫，人在都邑京城之内；属金、居兑宫，人在庵观寺院之中。用神如果临震宫木爻，必在都邑；如果临九五，必往京城；如果临兑宫金爻，必躲在庵院寺观之中。(《黄金策》)
  A Subject of Wood in the Zhen palace means he is in a city or the capital; of Metal in the Dui palace, in a nunnery, temple or monastery. If the Subject sits on a Wood line of the Zhen palace, he must be in a city; if it sits on the ninth in the fifth place, he must have gone to the capital; if it sits on a Metal line of the Dui palace, he must be hiding in a nunnery, temple or monastery.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.04** 用爻带着鬼墓、交重发动，人在庙宇中隐藏；用爻休囚死绝，人在坟陵左右潜藏。用爻持鬼墓，人必隐在庙宇之中；用爻临死绝，必藏身在坟墓左右。(《黄金策》)
  A Subject carrying the tomb of Pressure and moving means he hides within a temple; a Subject resting, trapped, dead or extinct means he lurks about the graves and mounds. A Subject holding the tomb of Pressure means he is hidden in a temple; a Subject on death or extinction means he is hiding by the graves.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.05** 如果遇到四库，要追究它的五行。四库就是辰戌丑未四个地支，用爻属木，卦里有未动一类。辰是水和土的库，人必在水边；戌是火库，在寺庙旁边；丑是金库，在银铁匠家；未是木库，在园林柴草之间，或木工竹匠家。凡是占逃亡盗贼，如果遇到墓爻，一定很难找到，要等冲破墓爻的日月才能见到。(《黄金策》)
  If the four storehouses are met, examine their element. The four storehouses are the branches Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei; the Subject line is of Wood, and the hexagram has something like an unmoved Wei. Chen is the storehouse of Water and Earth, and he must be by the water; Xu is the storehouse of Fire, by a temple; Chou is the storehouse of Metal, at the house of a silver or iron smith; Wei is the storehouse of Wood, among gardens, woods and brushwood, or at the house of a carpenter or bamboo worker. In divining for a runaway or a thief, if a tomb line is met he is surely hard to find, and he can be seen only in the month or day that clashes the tomb line open.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.06** 如果用神伏在五种六亲之下，怎么能一概而论。卦里没有用神，就要看它伏在哪一爻之下，才知道那个人在什么地方。伏在鬼爻下，在官仓官库之中，旺相又逢月建，在官户家，休囚无气，在公差吏役家；伏在父母下，在叔伯父母家，不然在手艺人家；伏在兄弟下，在兄弟姊妹和熟识朋友家；伏在财爻下，在奴婢妻妾家；伏在子孙下，在寺观和年幼辈那里。又比如伏在鬼墓之下，不在庙宇中就在寺庵内；伏在财库之下，不在仓库中，就在富豪家。(《黄金策》)
  If the Subject is hidden under one of the five relations, how can they all be judged alike? When the hexagram has no Subject, look at which line it is hidden under, and one learns where the person is. Hidden under Pressure, he is in a government granary or storehouse; if strong and also on the Month, in the house of an official family, if resting or without breath, in the house of a clerk or runner. Hidden under Resource, he is at the house of an uncle or parent, otherwise at a craftsman's. Hidden under Peer, he is at the house of brothers and sisters or of acquaintances and friends. Hidden under Wealth, he is at the house of servants, wives or concubines. Hidden under Output, he is at a temple or among the young. Again, hidden under the tomb of Pressure, he is either in a temple or in a nunnery; hidden under the storehouse of Wealth, he is either in a warehouse or at a wealthy family's.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.18** 本宫变入本宫，逃去的地方应当不远。本宫仍然变成本宫的卦，比如乾卦变姤、遁、否、观等本宫卦，主那人逃在本地，必不会走远；如果用爻在他宫发动，又变入他宫，是远去之后又转了方向。(《黄金策》)
  If the palace changes into its own palace, the place he fled to should not be far. A palace changing into a hexagram of its own palace, for example Qian changing into Gou, Dun, Pi or Guan and the like, means the man fled within the locality and surely has not gone far; if the Subject moves in another palace and changes into yet another palace, he has gone far and then turned in another direction.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.26** 世应比和而且不空，他必定潜伏在这里。凡是占逃走的人在不在这里，必须世爻应爻生合、比合用爻，用爻出现而且不落空，他必然潜伏在这里。(《黄金策》)
  If the Self and the Other are in harmony and not Void, he is surely lying low here. In divining whether a runaway is at this place, the Self and the Other must give life to, combine with or match the Subject, and the Subject must appear and not fall into the Void; then he is surely lying low here.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.30** 财在本宫内卦，在家里搜索可以找到；财在他宫外卦，到邻居那里追问能知道下落。财在本宫内卦，东西没有出家门，可以找到；财在他宫外卦，东西已经出外，难得回；财在间爻，邻居人家可以去找。(《黄金策》)
  With Wealth in the inner trigram of its own palace, a search of the household can find it; with Wealth in the outer trigram of another palace, inquiring among the neighbours can learn where it went. Wealth in the inner trigram of its own palace means the thing has not left the house and can be found; Wealth in the outer trigram of another palace means the thing has gone out and is hard to get back; Wealth on a between line means the neighbours' houses can be searched.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.31** 五爻是道路，四爻是门，上爻是栋梁和阁楼上。这是就六爻说个大概：用神在五爻，要到道路上找；在四爻，到门户那里找；在上爻，到梁上、阁上找。学的人不可拘泥，应当灵活。(《黄金策》)
  The fifth line is the road and the fourth the door, and the top line is the beams and the loft. This speaks of the six lines in rough outline: with the Subject on the fifth line, search the road; on the fourth, the doors; on the top line, the beams and the loft. The student must not be rigid but should be flexible.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.32** 初爻是水井，二爻是灶，三爻是内室房中。比如用神临初爻子、亥，可以在水井中找；在二爻，可以在灶前找；在三爻，可以在房内找；如果伏在三爻官鬼之下，可以在神堂内找。(《黄金策》)
  The first line is the well, the second the hearth, and the third the inner chamber. For example, a Subject on the first line at Zi or Hai can be sought in the well; on the second line, before the hearth; on the third, in the room; if hidden under the Pressure of the third line, in the shrine hall.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.34** 用爻发动入墓中，财物深藏而看不见。如果用爻入墓、化墓，或伏在墓爻之下，东西必定在器物之中，想知道哪天找到，要等到冲开墓的日子。(《黄金策》)
  If the Subject moves into the tomb, the goods are deeply hidden and not seen. If the Subject enters the tomb, changes into the tomb, or is hidden under the tomb line, the thing is surely inside some container, and to know the day it turns up, wait for the day that clashes the tomb open.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.36** 鬼爻的墓爻临上，东西必定在坟边墓侧。用神临鬼墓，东西必定在寺庙中，无气则在坟墓内，如果是本宫内卦，就在棺旁，或在座席上；再加螣蛇，恐怕在神像佛像之前；在三爻，在香火堂中之类。(《黄金策》)
  If the tomb line of Pressure appears, the thing is surely beside a grave or tomb. A Subject on the tomb of Pressure means the thing is in a temple, or if without breath, in a tomb; if it is in the inner trigram of its own palace, beside a coffin or on a seat; with the Coiling Snake added, perhaps before a picture of a god or a Buddha image; on the third line, in an incense hall and the like.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.37** 日辰合住，东西一定被器物掩盖遮藏。用爻发动遇到日辰合住，必定有东西遮藏着；冲中逢合一定得到，合处逢冲难找。(《黄金策》)
  If the Day combines it shut, the thing is surely covered and hidden by some vessel. A Subject that moves and meets the Day combining it shut is surely covered and hidden by something; a combination met within a clash is surely got, a clash met at a combination is hard to find.
- **LY.LOST.HJC.38** 子孙变成妻财，要去探老鼠洞；酉地遇到福德，应当查看鸡窝。财化福、福化财，东西必定在禽兽的窝巢里：值子爻，是老鼠衔去，更在初爻，在地洞里；寅是猫衔走，丑在牛栏，午在马厩，未在羊圈，酉在鸡窝，亥在猪圈。有合，在里面；没有合，在旁边。(《黄金策》)
  Output changing into Wealth, one must probe the rat holes; Output meeting You ground, one should check the chicken roost.
