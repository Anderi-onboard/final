## LY.LOST.HJC.33 失脱·水失于池、木乃柴薪之内、土埋在地、金为砖石之间：财临水在池沼，临木在竹木柴薪内，临金旺相在铜锡铁器中、休囚在缸缺罐瓶内，外卦旺相在砖石内、休囚在瓦砾中

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:属水的失在水池，属木的在柴薪之内，属土的埋在地里，属金的在砖石之间。财爻临水，东西在池沼；临木，在竹木树林柴薪内；临金旺相，在铜锡铁器中，休囚，在缸口破损的罐瓶内；外卦旺相，在砖石内，休囚，在瓦砾中，诸如此类。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** What is of Water is lost in a pool, what is of Wood is among firewood, what is of Earth is buried in the ground, and what is of Metal is between bricks and stones. Wealth on Water means the thing is in a pond or marsh; on Wood, among bamboo, timber, trees and firewood; on Metal, strong, among copper, tin and iron vessels, resting or trapped, in a chipped jar or bottle; in the outer trigram, strong, in bricks and stones, resting or trapped, in rubble and the like.

## LY.LOST.HJC.53 失脱·子动丑宫问牧童定知消息、福兴酉地是酒客可探情由：子孙发动，必有人撞见，问那人可知消息，看子孙所临的地支，分别问哪一种人

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙发动在丑宫，问牧童一定知道消息；子孙兴旺在酉地，是酒客可以打探到情由。子孙发动，必定有人撞见，问他可知消息。如果在子爻，可以问不戴帽子的男子或捕鱼人；在丑爻，问牧童、筑墙等人；在寅爻是木客、木匠、挑竹木器的人；在卯爻，问织席卖鞋的、挑柴砍草的人；在辰爻，问凿井的、傍河的、锄地的人；在巳爻，问穿红衣的女子，或耍蛇的乞丐；在午爻，问烧窑的、骑马的、讨火的、提灯的人；在未爻，问挑灰的、耕种的、放羊的人；在申爻，问铜铁匠或耍猴的人；在酉爻，问针线工、酒客、捉鸡的人；在戌爻，问挑泥的、锄地的、牵狗的人；在亥爻，问挑水的、踏水车的、洗衣的、洗澡的人。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Output moving in the Chou palace: ask the herd boy and you will surely learn the news; Output flourishing on You ground: a drinker can be sounded out for the facts. When Output moves, someone surely saw it, and asking him will give news. On Zi, one can ask a bareheaded man or a fisherman; on Chou, herd boys, wall builders and the like; on Yin, woodsmen, carpenters, and people carrying bamboo and wooden things; on Mao, mat weavers, shoe sellers, those carrying firewood or cutting grass; on Chen, well diggers, people by the river, and those hoeing the ground; on Si, a woman in red or a snake-charming beggar; on Wu, kiln burners, riders, those begging a light or carrying a lamp; on Wei, those carrying ash, ploughing or herding sheep; on Shen, copper and iron smiths or monkey handlers; on You, needleworkers, drinkers and chicken catchers; on Xu, those carrying mud, hoeing, or leading a dog; on Hai, water carriers, those treading a water wheel, washing clothes or bathing.

## LY.LOST.HJC.64 失脱·倘失舟车衣服不宜妻位交重、或亡走兽飞禽切忌父爻发动：失舟车、衣帽、文书、奏章以父母爻为用神所以怕财爻动；失飞禽走兽以子孙为用神所以怕父爻发动，被克就难找

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:如果丢了船、车、衣服，不适宜妻财爻位交重发动；或者丢了走兽飞禽，切忌父母爻发动。失脱不能专门拿财当用神：如果丢的是船、车、衣帽、文书、奏章，就以父母爻为用神，所以忌讳财爻动；如果丢的是飞禽走兽，就以子孙爻为用神，所以忌讳父母爻发动，被克就难找。学习要通达变化。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If a boat, cart or clothes are lost, it is unfit for the Wealth position to be crowded with movement; or if a beast or bird has run off, the Resource line moving is to be strictly avoided. In lost goods one cannot take Wealth alone as the Subject: if what is lost is a boat, cart, clothes, a cap, documents or memorials, the Resource line is the Subject, so a moving Wealth line is feared; if what is lost is a bird or beast, Output is the Subject, so a moving Resource line is feared, and if it is controlled the animal is hard to find. One should learn to be adaptable.

**《黄金策·失脱》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 3034 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lost goods, with theft, catching and arresting thieves", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 3034*
