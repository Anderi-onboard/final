## LY.LOST.HJC.01 逃亡·须察用爻方知实迹：占奴婢妻妾逃亡，用爻就是财爻，看它才知道人的实际去向

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:宽容待人，众人会生出侮慢；严厉管人，逃亡就会发生，所以即使大圣人有容人的度量，也说小人难养。要知道逃走的人的实际去向，必须察看用爻。用爻，比如占奴仆、妻妾逃亡，就看财爻这一类。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Treat people with leniency and the crowd turns insolent; manage them with severity and flight begins; hence even the great sage, for all his tolerance, said that petty people are hard to keep. To learn where the runaway has really gone, one must examine the Subject line; for example, in divining for the flight of a servant or a wife or concubine, one looks at the Wealth line and the like.

**《黄金策·逃亡》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2907 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Runaways", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2907*

## LY.LOST.HJC.28 失脱·要知失物得失须详妻财：财爻是所失之物的主象，冲中逢合所失必可得，合处逢冲既失不能复得

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:百姓饥寒，常有翻墙钻洞的人，不要忘了检点约束，也多有遗失的忧虑。要知道其中的得失，必须详察卦上的妻财。财爻是所失之物的主象：如果得到冲中逢合，所失一定可以得回；如果是合处逢冲，既然失去就不能再得回了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Common people suffer hunger and cold, and there are often those who climb walls and dig through them; never forget to check and restrain, for there is also a worry of loss. To know the gain or loss in it, one must examine the Wealth line on the hexagram. The Wealth line is the principal image of the thing lost: if a combination is met within a clash, what is lost can surely be got back; if a clash is met at a combination, once lost it cannot be got back.

**《黄金策·失脱》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2964 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Lost goods, with theft, catching and arresting thieves", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2964*

## LY.LOST.ZS.01 用神章·占乱臣、贼盗、邪崇也以官鬼爻为用神：凡是拘束我身的都是官鬼

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占乱臣、贼盗、邪祟，也以官鬼爻为用神；官鬼这一类事物很多，一切拘束我身的都是。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining for rebellious ministers, thieves and robbers, and evil spirits, the Pressure line is also the Subject; Pressure covers many kinds of things, and everything that constrains my person is of it.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 433 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 433*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 占乱臣、贼盗、邪崇，亦以官鬼爻为用神。 | 占乱臣、贼盗、邪祟，也以官鬼爻为用神。 | In divining for rebellious ministers, thieves and robbers, and evil spirits, the Pressure line is also the Subject. |
| 物类亦多，一切拘束我身者是也。 | 官鬼这一类事物很多，一切拘束我身的都是。 | Pressure covers many kinds of things, and everything that constrains my person is of it. |

> **按**:这是从「谁是贼」的一头取用神：官鬼代表贼。被偷的东西则取财爻（失脱章里分别说），所以占失物要分清问的是人还是物。
>
> **Note:** This takes the Subject from the side of who the thief is: Pressure stands for the thief. The thing stolen is read from the Wealth line (given separately in the chapter on lost goods), so in divining for lost goods one must distinguish whether one asks about the person or the thing.

相关:LY.LOST.HJC.28、LY.LOST.HJC.61

## LY.LOST.ZS.04 六冲章·合变六冲：凡是六合卦变六冲，诸占先合后离、先亲后疏、得而复失、成而复败，只有占官非、盗贼、结绝之事最宜

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:凡是六合卦变成六冲卦，各种占都是先合后离，先亲后疏，先浓后淡，先荣后悴，先好后迟，先通后塞，得到又失去，成功又败坏，事情做成又变。只有占官非、盗贼、了结断绝的事最适宜。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whenever a six-combine hexagram changes into a six-clash hexagram, every divination first combines and then parts, first close and then distant, first warm and then cool, first flourishing and then withered, first good and then slow, first open and then blocked, gained and then lost, achieved and then ruined, the matter done and then changed again. Only in divining for lawsuits, thieves, and matters of settling or breaking off is it best.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 1842 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, line 1842*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 凡得六合变六冲者，诸占先合后离，先亲后疏，先浓后淡，先荣后悴，初好后迟，先亨后否，得而复失，成而复败，事就而又变也。 | 凡是六合卦变成六冲卦的，各种占都是先合后离，先亲后疏，先浓后淡，先荣后悴，开头好后来迟，先通后塞，得到又失去，成功又败坏，事情做成又变。 | Whenever a six-combine hexagram changes into a six-clash hexagram, every divination first combines and then parts, first close and then distant, first warm and then cool, first flourishing and then withered, good at first and slow later, first open and then blocked, gained and then lost, achieved and then ruined, the matter done and then changed again. |
| 惟占官非、盗贼、结绝事者最宜。 | 只有占官非、盗贼、了结断绝的事最适宜。 | Only in divining for lawsuits, thieves, and matters of settling or breaking off is it best. |
