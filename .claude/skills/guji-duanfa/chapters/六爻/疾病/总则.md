# 六爻 · 疾病 · 总则

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Illness · Principles: whose illness, which line stands for the patient, strong and weak*

2 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.ILL.HJC.01 占病以官爻为恶杀：症候轻重、得病根由皆系此爻，独发之爻亦可推之

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病时把官鬼爻当作凶恶的煞：病的症状轻重、得病的根由，都看这一爻；卦里只有一爻独自发动的，也可以照它来推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining illness the Pressure line is taken as the malign killer: how heavy or light the symptoms are, and how the illness was contracted, are both read from this line; a line that moves alone in the hexagram can also be reasoned from in the same way.

**《黄金策·疾病》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6763 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6763*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 人孰无常，疾病无常； | 人谁没有无常的时候？疾病就是无常的； | Who among men is without impermanence? Illness is impermanent; |
| 事孰为大，死生为大。 | 什么事最大？生死最大。 | what matter is greatest? Life and death are the greatest. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6764 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6764*

| 独发之爻，亦可以此推之。 | 只有一爻独自发动的，也可以照这个办法推。 | A line that moves alone can also be reasoned out in the same way. |

## LY.ILL.HJC.33 次看主象（就是用神）：怕遇克害刑冲；占夫以官为用神、占妻以财为用神；旧注另说自占以世为主、代占以应为主；得生扶必不至死

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:再看主象，也就是用神，就是病人：占丈夫用官鬼，占妻子用妻财。旧注还说，自己占以世爻为主象，替别人占以应爻为主象。主象怕遇到刑、冲、克、害，那就是病人被病折磨；被克害的地方如果得到生扶（旧注：得世爻生合、旁边的爻扶持），一定不至于死。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Next look at the main image, which is the Subject and stands for the patient: in divining a husband take Pressure, in divining a wife take Wealth. The old commentary adds that in a divination for oneself the Self line is the main image, and in a divination for another the Other line is. The main image fears meeting punishment, clash, control or harm, which is the patient being ground down by the illness; but if the place of harm is generated and supported (the old commentary: by the Self line generating or combining with it and the lines beside it supporting it), he will surely not die.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6882 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6882*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 次观主象，怕逢克害刑冲。 | 接着看主象，怕遇到克、害、刑、冲。 | Next look at the main image: it fears meeting control, harm, punishment or clash. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6883–6884 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), lines 6883–6884*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 主象即病人。 | 主象就是病人。 | The main image is the patient. |
| 如占夫，以官为主； | 比如占丈夫，拿官爻做主象； | For example, in divining a husband, the Pressure line is the main image; |
| 占妻，以财为主之类。 | 占妻子，拿财爻做主象，这一类。 | in divining a wife, the Wealth line is the main image, and so on. |
| 又自占，以世为主； | 另外，自己占，拿世爻做主象； | Again, in a divination for oneself, the Self line is the main image; |
| 代占，以应为主； | 替别人占，拿应爻做主象； | in a divination for another, the Other line is the main image; |
| 亦是主象。 | 这也是主象。 | these too are the main image. |
| 刑冲克害，即病人受症候折磨，故怕见之。 | 刑、冲、克、害，就是病人被症候折磨，所以怕见到它们。 | Punishment, clash, control and harm mean the patient being ground down by the symptoms, so they are feared. |
| 若得世爻生合，旁爻扶持，必不至死。 | 如果得到世爻来生、来合，旁边的爻来扶持，一定不至于死。 | If it is generated or combined by the Self line and supported by the lines beside it, he will surely not die. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2364 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2364*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 主象即用神也，如占夫以官为用神，占妻以财为用神类。 | 主象就是用神，比如占丈夫拿官爻做用神，占妻子拿财爻做用神，这一类。 | The main image is the Subject: in divining a husband the Pressure line is the Subject, in divining a wife the Wealth line, and so on. |
| 如遇刑冲克害，即病人受病磨折，故怕见之。 | 如果遇到刑、冲、克、害，就是病人被病磨折，所以怕见到。 | If it meets punishment, clash, control or harm, the patient is worn down by the illness, so these are feared. |
| 克害处若得生扶，必不至死。 | 被克害的地方如果得到生扶，一定不至于死。 | If the place that is controlled and harmed is generated and supported, he will surely not die. |

> **打架**:替别人占病时取哪一爻做主象：旧注在六亲取用（占夫看官、占妻看财）之外，又说「代占以应为主」；王洪绪只讲六亲取用，没有把应爻当主象。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Which line is the main image in a divination on behalf of another: besides taking the relation (Pressure for a husband, Wealth for a wife) the old commentary also says 'in a divination for another the Other line is the main image'; Wang Hongxu speaks only of taking the relation and does not make the Other line the main image.

> **按**:「主象」就是「用神」，王洪绪直接说了这一点。前面 LY.ILL.HJC.32 说的子孙，是解神；这里的主象，是病人自己那一爻。
>
> **Note:** The 'main image' is the 'Subject', which Wang Hongxu says outright. The Output of LY.ILL.HJC.32 is the relieving spirit; the main image here is the line of the patient himself.

- 《黄金策》:同文 第 1200 行:次观主象怕逢克害刑冲
