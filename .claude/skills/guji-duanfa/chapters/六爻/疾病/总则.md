## LY.ILL.CA.01 疾病先将身命看：逢龙见子放心宽；妻财入命多沉重、官鬼临身更不堪；身犯勾陈凶有准、命加玄武瘥应难；螣蛇白虎忧丧事、身命空亡定入棺；金鬼值身伤骨节、水鬼血浓攒、火鬼疮痍恼、木鬼百骨酸、土鬼肿胀；杀刑加命命摧残

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:疾病先要看身和命：遇到青龙、见到子孙，可以放心。妻财入了命，多半沉重；官鬼临在身上，更加不堪。身犯勾陈，凶是有准的；命上加了玄武，痊愈就难。螣蛇、白虎，忧的是丧事；身命落空亡，一定入棺。金鬼值身，伤骨节；身临水鬼，血液浓稠聚积；贴着身的火鬼，疮痍烦恼；木鬼在身宫，百骨酸痛；土鬼在身，身体肿胀；杀和刑加在命上，命被摧残。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In illness first look at the Body and the natal line: meeting the Dragon and seeing Output, set the heart at ease. Wealth entering the natal line: mostly heavy; the Pressure line sitting on the Body: still worse. The Body offending Hook Earth: ill is certain; the natal line with the Dark Tortoise added: recovery is surely hard. The Coiling Snake and White Tiger: worry of a funeral; the Body and natal line Void: he will surely enter the coffin. A Metal ghost on the Body: injury to the bone joints; the Body on a Water ghost: blood thick and gathered; a Fire ghost close to the Body: the vexation of sores; a Wood ghost in the Body palace: all the bones ache; an Earth ghost on the Body: the body swells; a killing and punishment added to the natal line: life is ruined.

## LY.ILL.CA.04 疾病歌(二)：凡占疾病先从卦中寻本命、仍看来者是何人（克者是何加世应）；福德持世鬼暂退、官旺之日又加进；卦中无鬼病难医、鬼旺财兴难保命；鬼杀临身五墓侵鬼神不应药无灵；一为五脏二皮肉三为体骨腰并足四肺心经五六头；游魂恍惚如神触、归魂渐向安

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:凡是有人占疾病，先从卦里找本命，还要看来占的是什么人，克它的又是什么、加在世应上。福德持世，鬼暂时退去；官鬼旺相的日子又进一步。卦里没有鬼，病难医；鬼旺、财兴，难保命。鬼杀临在身上、又有五墓侵犯，鬼神不应验，药也无灵。第一爻是五脏，第二爻是皮肉，第三爻是体骨、腰和足；第四爻是肺和心经，第五、第六爻是头。游魂卦恍惚，像被神鬼触到；只有归魂卦，渐渐向安；吉凶我细细地为你嘱咐。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If someone divines an illness, first look in the hexagram for the natal line, and still look at who has come, and what it is that controls, added to the Self and Other. Output holding the Self: the ghost retreats for a time; on a day when the Pressure line is strong it advances again. With no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to treat; with the ghost strong and Wealth rising life is hard to keep. A ghost-killing sitting on the Body with the Five Tombs invading: the ghosts and spirits do not respond and medicine has no effect. The first line is the five viscera, the second skin and flesh, the third body, bones, waist and feet; the fourth is the lungs and heart channel, the fifth and sixth the head. The Wandering Soul hexagram: dazed, as if touched by spirits; only the Returning Soul gradually turns to peace; good and ill I tell you in detail.

> **打架**:哪一爻管身体哪一处，几本书的说法差得很远。阐奥歌章这一首（LY.ILL.CA.04）是「一爻五脏、二爻皮肉、三爻体骨腰并足、四爻肺心经、五六爻头」，断易天机的孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）同；断易天机卜筮元龟一段（见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.11）是「初爻两足、二爻双腿、三爻腰背、四爻心腹及肚肠、五爻肾脏、六爻头」；断易天机鬼谷辨爻法的表（LY.ILL.DYTJ.01）是「初足、二腿股、三腰、四脊、五心、六头」；易隐引鬼谷分爻（LY.ILL.YY.02）是「初爻足、二爻腿股、三爻肝肾、四爻脾脏、五爻心肺、六爻头脑」；卜筮大全卜筮全书歌诀里的六位取象表（见 LY.ILL.BSQS.01）疾病一行是「足、腿肢、（缺）、（缺）、心肺、头脑」。卜筮元龟、鬼谷辨爻法的表、易隐、六位表都是「初足、二腿、上头」，只有阐奥歌章和孙膑歌把初爻配成五脏、二爻配成皮肉。书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Which line governs which part of the body is put very differently in several books. This poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.04) has 'the first line the five viscera, the second skin and flesh, the third body, bones, waist and feet, the fourth lungs and heart channel, the fifth and sixth the head', and Sun Bin's song in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) is the same; the Bushi Yuangui passage in the Duanyi Tianji (see LY.ILL.DYTJ.11) has 'the first line the two feet, the second both legs, the third waist and back, the fourth heart, belly and bowels, the fifth the kidneys, the sixth the head'; the table headed Guigu's method of telling the lines apart in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.01) has 'first the feet, second the legs and thighs, third the waist, fourth the spine, fifth the heart, sixth the head'; the Yi Yin, quoting Guigu's division of the lines (LY.ILL.YY.02), has 'first the feet, second the legs and thighs, third liver and kidneys, fourth the spleen, fifth heart and lungs, sixth the head and brain'; and the table of the six positions in the verses of the Bushi Quanshu in the Buyi Daquan (see LY.ILL.BSQS.01) has for illness 'feet, legs and limbs, (missing), (missing), heart and lungs, head and brain'. The Bushi Yuangui, Guigu's table, the Yi Yin and the table of the six positions all run 'first line the feet, second the legs, top the head', and only the Chan'ao Gezhang and Sun Bin's song match the first line with the viscera and the second with skin and flesh. The books do not settle it.

> **按**:这是第二首疾病歌，它在断易天机里作「孙膑断疾病歌」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.02），字句略有出入。「先从卦中寻本命」的「本命」是病人的本命爻（生年属相所在的爻）；「克者是何加世应」文字含混，按字面译。「福德持世鬼暂退，官旺之日又加进」是说子孙持世时鬼暂时退去，但官鬼旺相的日子又会进一步。「五墓」是五墓煞（春未、夏戌、秋丑、冬辰，见 LY.ILL.EL.51）。「游魂恍惚如神触」的「游魂」「归魂」是卦的名称（游魂卦、归魂卦），「恍惚」是神志不清。整首和易林补遗的「卦无用象」（LY.ILL.EL.10）、海底眼的「疾病」一节（LY.ILL.HDY.01）是同一类判据：先找本命、看来者何人、看鬼有无。
>
> **Note:** This is the second illness poem, which appears in the Duanyi Tianji as 'Sun Bin's song on judging illness' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02), with slight differences in wording. The 'natal line' of 'first look in the hexagram for the natal line' is the sick person's natal line (the line where the birth animal falls); 'what it is that controls, added to the Self and Other' is loose in wording and is rendered literally. 'Output holding the Self, the ghost retreats for a time; on a day when the Pressure line is strong it advances again' says that when Output holds the Self the ghost retreats for a time, but on a day when the Pressure line is strong it advances again. The 'Five Tombs' is the Five Tombs killing (Wei in spring, Xu in summer, Chou in autumn, Chen in winter; see LY.ILL.EL.51). The 'Wandering Soul' and 'Returning Soul' of 'the Wandering Soul hexagram, dazed as if touched by spirits' are names of hexagrams (the Wandering Soul hexagram and the Returning Soul hexagram), and 'dazed' is clouded consciousness. The whole poem is on the same kind of criteria as the Yilin Buyi's 'when the hexagram has no Subject image' (LY.ILL.EL.10) and the Haidi Yan's section on illness (LY.ILL.HDY.01): first find the natal line, look at who has come, and look at whether there is a ghost.

## LY.ILL.DYTJ.02 孙膑断疾病歌(一)：凡占疾病先从卦中寻本命、仍看来占是何人；福德持世鬼暂退、爻旺之日又加进；卦中无鬼病难医、鬼旺财兴命难保；鬼煞临身五墓加、鬼神休祷药无灵

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占疾病，先在卦里找出病人的本命爻，再看来占的是什么人；福德持世，鬼暂时退去，可是爻旺的日子又进一步；卦里没有鬼，病难医；鬼旺、财兴，命难保；鬼煞临在身上又加了五墓，向鬼神祷告也不必了，药也无灵。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining illness, first find the sick person's natal line in the hexagram and look at who has come to divine. With Output holding the Self the ghost retreats for a time, but on a day when the line is strong it advances again. With no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to treat; with the ghost strong and Wealth rising life is hard to keep. A ghost-killing sitting on the Body with the Five Tombs added: there is no use praying to ghosts and spirits, and medicine has no effect.

## LY.ILL.EL.08 疾病专看用爻：用爻有气、元神发动、忌神安静便无妨；忌神动而元神同发则转助用爻；元神不动闲神休囚又被月建日辰克也主倾危

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以用神为主，元神为凭。占疾病，必须求用象兴旺；元神旺相又发动，一定亨通；忌神千万不要在发动的位置上；日、月到来，要配合它的克与生；动的要看变爻才知道去留；没有（用象）就找伏象，才知道亏盈。注说：凡是占疾病，专看用爻，卦里如果用爻有气、元神发动、忌神安静，就没有妨碍；忌神怕动，但如果元神同时发动，转过来帮助用爻，病反而安稳；卦里元神不动，闲神又休囚，再遇上月建或日辰来克，也主倾危。

## LY.ILL.EL.13 休把卦名推祸福，莫将神煞决忧祯：其中有论卦名者切不可；论神煞者亦非；用神旺则墓门煞大煞动亦不死、用神既伤则月解天医同发亦死；五行为重，神煞难凭，星家专忌本命空亡非正道

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:不要用卦名去推祸福，不要拿神煞去断忧祯。注说：其中有拿卦名来论的，切不可；经里说，易卦的渊源是论五行阴阳的道理，本来生生不息，可怜愚昧无知的人颠倒阴阳去论卦名，如今有人拿神煞来论的也不对；经里又说，易卦阴阳在变通，五行生克妙无穷，现在的人不明白阴阳的道理，拿神煞来定吉凶。比如儿子占父亲的病，父爻旺相，墓门煞或大煞又动，用爻有气，怎么会死？又如丈夫占妻子的病，财爻无气，父爻兄弟爻都动，纵然月解、天医同时发动，用爻既然受了伤，怎么能不推断为死？所以以五行为重，神煞难以凭信，星家专门忌讳本命空亡，这不是正道。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Do not push fortune and misfortune by the names of hexagrams, and do not decide grief and omen by spirit-stars. The commentary says: those who judge by hexagram names must on no account be followed; the classic says the source of the Yi hexagrams is to judge by the principles of the five phases and yin and yang, which by nature give birth without end, and pitiable ignorant people turn yin and yang upside down and judge by hexagram names, and those who now judge by spirit-stars are also wrong; the classic says again that in the hexagrams yin and yang lie in change, and the generation and control of the five phases are marvellous without end, but people now do not understand the principle of yin and yang and use the spirit-stars to fix fortune. For example, if a son divines a father's illness and the Resource line is strong, and the Tomb Gate star or the Great Killing also moves, the Subject line having qi, how could he die? Again, if a husband divines a wife's illness and the Wealth line has no qi, and the Resource and Peer lines both move, even if Month Release and Heavenly Doctor move together, the Subject being already wounded, how can one judge otherwise than death? Therefore the five phases are weighty and the spirit-stars hard to rely on, and that astrologers particularly fear the Void of the natal branch is not the right way.

## LY.ILL.EL.17 占自己病断之二：先凭世象次凭卦身；怕月破旬空，喜生扶拱合；克世为忌客、生世为元神，随官入墓灾难瘥、助鬼伤身命必沉；世落空辨缓急、休囚被克无救则天年命尽、旺相无生助亦无所害；他人病身世随官入墓不必忌，命随鬼入墓即凶；卦身墓绝于月建或变爻决无救

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:自己占病，先凭世爻的象，再凭卦身，怕遇上月破、旬空，喜欢见到生、扶、拱、合；克世的爻是忌客，来生世的象是元神；随官入墓，灾难痊愈；助鬼伤身，命必沉沦。注说：自己占自己的病，专看世，爻如果临月破，不论新病旧病，命也难保全。世如果落空，要分缓急：急症还能医，旧病就不救；如果空中有气，眼下无妨，后来难保。世象不临旬空月破，再看旺衰：世如果休囚，又被动爻来克、或遭日月来伤、或变成死绝，毫无救助的爻，怎么会不是天年命尽？世即使休囚，如果得到动爻生助、或遇日月扶持、或变为有气，纵然临危也不会死；世如果旺相，虽然没有生助的爻，也没有什么害处。凡是克世的叫忌象，宜静不宜动；生世的叫元神，宜旺不宜空。占他人的病，身、世随官入墓，不必忌讳，命随鬼入墓就凶了。只有自己占身、世，命随鬼入墓的命不会回生，助鬼伤身也是同样的道理；又看卦身，如果墓、绝于月建，或者墓、绝于变爻，一定没有救了。

If the Self image does not sit in Void or Month-break, look next at strength and weakness: if the Self is resting or trapped and is controlled by a moving line, or wounded by the Day or Month, or changes into death or Extinction, with no line of rescue at all, how can it be anything but the end of one's allotted years? Though the Self is resting or trapped, if a moving line generates and helps it, or the Day and Month support it, or it changes into qi, even at the brink it does not die; if the Self is strong, even without a generating line there is no harm. Whatever controls the Self is called an adversary image and should be still, not moving; whatever generates the Self is called the Support and should be strong, not Void. In divining another's illness, the Body and Self following the Pressure line into a tomb need not be feared, but the natal branch following the ghost into a tomb is ominous. Only in divining for oneself, with the Body and Self, does a natal branch following the ghost into a tomb mean life will not be restored, and helping the ghost to harm the Self is the same in principle; look also at the Body: if it is in a tomb or Extinction under the Month, or in a tomb or Extinction under the changed line, there is surely no rescue.

## LY.ILL.HDY.10 用爻被飞神所克：不动者须日辰旺生出、冲出飞神；有动者在克日旺生出亦可取——占妻病例，申日比之坤、五爻动、癸亥伏在戌下，本不好却得日辰生扶，死中得生

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻伏着、被上面的飞神克制，如果用爻不动，就要日辰旺，把它生出来或者冲出来，飞神无气的日子是生不出来的；用爻有动的，在克它的那一日旺相、生出它，也可以取。书里的例子：占妻的病，申日得比之坤，五爻发动，癸亥伏在戌的下面，本来不好，却得到日辰能生扶它出来，死中得生。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject line is hidden and controlled by the flying line above it, then if it is still it needs the Day to be strong to generate it out or clash it out, and on a day when the flying line has no qi it cannot be generated; if it moves, being strong on the day that controls it and generated out can also be taken. The book's example: divining a wife's illness, on a Shen day the hexagram Bi changing to Kun, the fifth line moves, and Guihai lies hidden under Xu; it was originally not good, but the Day was able to generate and support it out, and life was found in death.

## LY.ILL.HZL.01 占疾病·应药世身、若坐墓鬼病主昏沉；卦有三墓（宫墓、鬼墓、财墓），世爻逢墓鬼主自身合灾（暴病未可、久病必死）；应为药忌坐鬼、本宫墓鬼得之主无药；最怕申酉持世、木爻独发

## LY.ILL.HZL.04 病忌官鬼·以财为禄、以鬼为祟；鬼爻旺相独发大忌；占妇人病喜子孙旺相持世安静、忌财伏鬼下兄弟持世独发；世克应内克外主吐、应克世外克内主泻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以财为禄，以鬼为祟；鬼爻旺相、独发，大忌。占妇人病，喜子孙旺相、持世、安静；忌财伏在鬼下、兄弟持世、兄弟独发。世克应、内克外，主吐；应克世、外克内，主泻。问答：妇人病喜子孙旺相、世安，是用财而以子孙辅助的道理；忌财伏鬼、兄弟持世，是用财伏在兄弟下的道理。内克外为什么主吐？内是腹、外是口；外克内主泻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Take Wealth as the salary and the ghost as the curse; the Pressure line strong and moving alone is a great taboo. The Self controlling the Other, the inner controlling the outer, means vomiting; the Other controlling the Self, the outer controlling the inner, means diarrhoea. Questions and answers: that in a woman's illness Output strong and the Self at peace is welcome is the principle of taking Wealth as the Subject with Output to help it; that Wealth hidden under the ghost and Peer holding the Self are taboo is the principle of the Subject Wealth hidden under Peer. Why does the inner controlling the outer mean vomiting? The inner is the belly and the outer is the mouth; the outer controlling the inner means diarrhoea.

## LY.ILL.HZL.07 占医药·占病必察用爻（占父母必要父母有气）；纵遇凶卦但主沉重不致丧亡、用爻空亡及不上卦更逢凶杀决主不起；用爻无气得旁爻动来生扶同生旺；凶杀临父母或父母空可言双亲有病；鬼爻持世沉重绝日轻可；鬼化鬼病进退；鬼带杀持世为瘵病

## LY.ILL.TXF.01 疾病章总纲：养生非道终有疾病、请祷能占便见死生；杀临父母忧堂上之亲、空及妻财虑闺中之妇；占疾病先看来者何人——子占父母要父母上卦、妻占夫看鬼、夫占妻看财、父占子看子、占兄弟朋友看兄弟、占奴仆看子孙；用爻不上卦或空亡多凶少吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:国家的治乱，靠的是风俗纪纲；人的寿夭，不在身体强弱。养生不得法，终究会有疾病；请祷能占卜，就能看出死生的决断。凶杀临在父母爻上，要为堂上的双亲担忧；妻财爻落空，要为闺中的妻子担心灾祸。注说：占疾病，先看来占的是什么人：儿子占父母，要父母爻上卦；妻子占丈夫看鬼爻；丈夫占妻子看财爻；父亲占儿子看子孙；占兄弟、占朋友都看兄弟爻；占奴仆也看子孙爻。用爻不上卦或空亡的，多凶少吉；哪一爻空亡、哪一爻受克，吉凶就照这个来断。

## LY.ILL.YM.09 既疾必筮其吉凶而调剂之：慎于未病是君子之道；命随鬼墓是不治之疾（例：卦中子孙旺相病不死，亥命随官入墓，半年内溺水亡）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:生死有命，不是祈祷能改的；疾病在于人，不可以忽视；没生病之前就谨慎，是君子的做法。已经生了病，能不占问它的吉凶而加以调理吗？本命跟着鬼入了墓，是治不好的病。比如戌月甲辰日，占儿子的病，他生于己亥年，得同人卦，卦中子孙旺相、六爻安静，病应当不会死，但是亥命随官入了墓，按法大凶；后来他的病没有死，却不到半年溺水而死。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Life and death have their appointed lot and cannot be prayed away; illness lies with the person and must not be neglected; to take care before falling ill is the way of the gentleman. Once ill, can one fail to divine its fortune and adjust the treatment? If the natal branch follows the ghost into a tomb, it is an untreatable illness. For example, in the Xu month on the Jiachen day, someone divined a son's illness; he was born in a Jihai year and the hexagram was Tongren, with Output strong and the six lines still, so the illness should not kill; but the Hai natal branch followed Pressure into a tomb, which by the method is a great ill omen; afterwards he did not die of the illness, but within half a year he drowned.

## LY.ILL.YM.20 筮己疾世乃用神、筮人疾应乃用神：己疾随墓、助伤、墓绝、反伏，虽旺遇一则凶；人疾随墓、反伏、墓绝，虽生一不可遇

## LY.ILL.YY.01 占疾病要慎而详：大端有四——占死生、占病症、占医药、占鬼崇；病何日起、病何处得是疾病的附占（游南子）

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When a person is unfortunate enough to fall ill and divines about it, one must be careful and thorough. ## LY.ILL.YY.03 占病取用爻：自占以世爻、身爻、命爻为用，代占以应爻为用；占家亲祖妣用官鬼、父兄妻子各以所属；五属不上卦从《黄金策》分爻取用；奴占家主用五爻、占主母用二爻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病取用爻：自己占，拿世爻、身爻、命爻做用；替别人占，拿应爻做用。占家里的亲人，祖父祖母用官鬼，父亲、兄弟、妻子、子女各用自己所属的那一类爻；这五类亲属如果不上卦，就按《黄金策》分爻的办法取用。奴仆丁口占家主，拿五爻做用；占主母，拿二爻做用。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Taking the Subject line in divining illness: in divining for oneself, the Self line, the Body line and the destiny line are the Subject; in divining for another, the Other line is the Subject. In divining for kin of the household, grandfather and grandmother use the Pressure line, and father, brother, wife and child each use the relation to which they belong; if these five kinds of relative do not appear in the hexagram, take the Subject by the Huangjin Ce's division of the lines. A servant or hired hand divining for the master of the house takes the fifth line as the Subject, and for the mistress the second line.

## LY.ILL.ZS.14 用临日月休寻伏：卦无用神而日月作了用神的，不必寻伏神，许之即愈；伏神衰弱、用神不现，当再占（例：占友痔病，应爻为用神被月刑日冲，令亲人再占）

## LY.ILL.ZS.39 来人所问决定看什么：问病的吉凶，鬼持世或动是忧神、子孙持世或动是解忧喜悦之神；问病犯何鬼神，看鬼爻五行（金鬼冲犯武神、水鬼得罪河神）；问病源，看鬼爻五行脏腑（火鬼心经、水鬼肾部）——李我平

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼是忧神、子孙是喜悦解忧之神，虽然是古法，用起来很恰当；但也有不把鬼当忧神的，全看来问的人心里想问什么。他问病的吉凶：鬼爻持世、鬼在卦里发动，是忧神；子孙持世、子孙发动，是解忧喜悦的神。他问病是犯了哪路鬼神：卦里出现金鬼，一定冲犯了武神；水鬼，一定得罪了河神这一类。他问病源：卦里出现火鬼，一定属心经；水鬼，一定在肾部。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** That the Pressure line is the spirit of worry and Output the spirit of joy that relieves worry, though an old method, is very apt in use; but there are cases in which Pressure is not taken as the spirit of worry, and it all depends on what the asker has in mind to ask. If he asks about the fortune of the illness: Pressure holding the Self line or moving in the hexagram is the spirit of worry, and Output holding the Self line or moving is the spirit of joy that relieves worry. If he asks which ghost or spirit the illness offends: a Metal Pressure appearing in the hexagram means he has surely offended a martial god, and a Water Pressure that he has offended the river god and the like. If he asks about the source of the illness: a Fire Pressure appearing in the hexagram surely belongs to the Heart channel, and a Water Pressure surely lies in the Kidney.

> **按**:李我平在这里把三种看法各归到它回答的问题上：野鹤的「忧神/喜悦神」回答病的吉凶，觉子的五行鬼神（LY.ILL.ZS.23）回答犯了什么，黄金策疾病章开头的五行脏腑（LY.ILL.HJC.02）回答病在哪里。所以野鹤删掉黄金策的首论（LY.ILL.ZS.22），李我平认为不是不对，是问题不同。
>
> **Note:** Here Li Woping assigns each of three ways of looking to the question it answers: the Old Man's 'spirit of worry and spirit of joy' answers the fortune of the illness, Juezi's ghosts and spirits by phase (LY.ILL.ZS.23) answers what has been offended, and the organs by phase at the opening of the Huangjin Ce's chapter on illness (LY.ILL.HJC.02) answers where the illness lies. So when the Old Man deletes the opening discussion of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.ZS.22), Li Woping holds that it is not wrong but answers a different question.
