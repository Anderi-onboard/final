## LY.ILL.HJC.01 占病以官爻为恶杀：症候轻重、得病根由皆系此爻，独发之爻亦可推之

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病时把官鬼爻当作凶恶的煞：病的症状轻重、得病的根由，都看这一爻；卦里只有一爻独自发动的，也可以照它来推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining illness the Pressure line is taken as the malign killer: how heavy or light the symptoms are, and how the illness was contracted, are both read from this line; a line that moves alone in the hexagram can also be reasoned from in the same way.

**《黄金策·疾病》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6763 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6763*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 人孰无常，疾病无常； | 人谁没有无常的时候？疾病就是无常的； | Who among men is without impermanence? Illness is impermanent; |
| 事孰为大，死生为大。 | 什么事最大？生死最大。 | what matter is greatest? Life and death are the greatest. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6764 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6764*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 占病以官爻为恶杀。 | 占病，把官鬼爻当作凶恶的煞。 | In divining illness, the Pressure line is taken as the malign killer. |

| 独发之爻，亦可以此推之。 | 只有一爻独自发动的，也可以照这个办法推。 | A line that moves alone can also be reasoned out in the same way. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2301 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2301*

| 得病根由，独发之爻亦可推之。 | 得病的根由，独自发动的爻也可以推。 | for the cause of the illness, a line moving alone can also be reasoned from. |
