## LY.ILL.EL.08 疾病专看用爻：用爻有气、元神发动、忌神安静便无妨；忌神动而元神同发则转助用爻；元神不动闲神休囚又被月建日辰克也主倾危

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以用神为主，元神为凭。占疾病，必须求用象兴旺；元神旺相又发动，一定亨通；忌神千万不要在发动的位置上；日、月到来，要配合它的克与生；动的要看变爻才知道去留；没有（用象）就找伏象，才知道亏盈。注说：凡是占疾病，专看用爻，卦里如果用爻有气、元神发动、忌神安静，就没有妨碍；忌神怕动，但如果元神同时发动，转过来帮助用爻，病反而安稳；卦里元神不动，闲神又休囚，再遇上月建或日辰来克，也主倾危。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Subject is the main line, and the Support is the ground. In illness one must seek a thriving Subject image; the Support strong and moving surely means success; the Adversary must on no account be in a moving position; when the Day and Month arrive, match them with their control and generation; for a moving line look at the changed line to know whether it goes or stays; if there is none, search for a hidden image to sense its waning and waxing. The commentary says: in divining illness look only at the Subject line; if in the hexagram the Subject line has qi, the Support moves and the Adversary is still, there is no obstruction; the Adversary should not move, but if the Support moves with it, it turns to help the Subject line and the illness on the contrary settles; if the Support in the hexagram does not move, the idle spirits are resting or trapped, and the Month or Day also controls it, danger of collapse is indicated.

> **按**:这一段没有章题；小标「以用神为主，元神为凭」说的是整章的取法：占病主看用神，凭据是元神。「用象」「用爻」「主象」在这一段里是用神的别称。「闲神」是与用神既不相生也不相克的爻。「无寻伏象」的「无」是没有用象时才去找伏象。与黄金策「主衰得助重亦何妨」（LY.ILL.HJC.48）、增删卜易「用绝逢生危而有救」（LY.ILL.ZS.13）是同一路读法。

> **Note:** This passage has no chapter title; the subtitle 'the Subject is the main line, the Support the ground' gives the method for the whole: in divining illness the Subject is the chief, and the Support is what one relies on. 'Subject image', 'Subject line' and 'chief image' are other names for the Subject in this passage. 'Idle spirits' are lines that neither generate nor control the Subject. 'If there is none, search for a hidden image' means the hidden image is looked for only when there is no Subject image. It reads in the same way as the Huangjin Ce's 'the chief weak but helped, however heavy what matter' (LY.ILL.HJC.48) and the Zengshan Buyi's 'the Subject in Extinction meeting generation, danger yet rescue' (LY.ILL.ZS.13).

## LY.ILL.EL.10 卦无用象：先察伏神，再推日月，再查互卦；互中体用二爻亦无用神方言无救；主卦无用象而伏出无伤决难损命；伏出遭刑克但日月互卦有一用爻亦无害

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里如果没有用象，就要察看伏神，再推日、月；日、月也没有主象、伏神又被刑伤的，再查互卦；互卦里体、用两爻也没有用神的，才说没有救。主卦虽然没有用象，如果伏神出来而没有受伤，一定不会损命；伏神出来即使遭刑克，再远查日、月和互卦之中有一个用爻，也没有害处。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the hexagram has no Subject image, examine the hidden line, and further the Day and Month; if the Day and Month too lack the chief image and the hidden line is wounded by punishment, then examine the nuclear trigrams; only if the body and use lines within the nuclear trigrams also have no Subject can one say there is no rescue. Though the main hexagram has no Subject image, if the hidden line comes out unharmed, life is surely not lost; even if the hidden line, when it comes out, is punished or controlled, if further examination of the Day, Month and nuclear trigrams finds one Subject line, there is no harm.

> **打架**:卦里没有用神先查什么：易林补遗的次序是先察伏神，再推日月，日月也没有主象、伏神又被刑伤，才查互卦，互卦里也没有用神才说无救；增删卜易说日、月作了用神的，不必去找伏神，断他马上就好，伏神衰弱、用神不现，就再占一次（LY.ILL.ZS.14）；易冒说卦无用神，元神动则不死，忌神动而用神伏反吉，也必须得日月来救（LY.ILL.YM.17）；黄金策讲的是用爻不上卦要等它值日露出来（LY.ILL.HJC.39）。几家的次序不同，书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** What to look at first when the hexagram has no Subject: the Yilin Buyi's order is the hidden line first, then the Day and Month, and only when the Day and Month lack the chief image and the hidden line is punished, the nuclear trigrams, and only if these too have no Subject is there no rescue; the Zengshan Buyi says that if the Day or Month serves as the Subject there is no need to look for the hidden line, and one may promise recovery at once, and that if the hidden line is weak and the Subject does not appear, one divines again (LY.ILL.ZS.14); the Yi Mao says that when the hexagram has no Subject, if the Support moves he does not die, and if the Adversary moves and the Subject is hidden it is on the contrary auspicious, but rescue by the Day and Month is still required (LY.ILL.YM.17); the Huangjin Ce speaks of waiting for a Subject line absent from the hexagram to come out on its day (LY.ILL.HJC.39). The orders differ among the books, and none gives a ruling.

> **按**:「互卦」是从本卦二、三、四爻和三、四、五爻各取三爻组成的两个卦。「体用二爻」在原文里没有解释；六爻里的「体」「用」在这里疑是指互卦里的「世」和「用神」，读法没有把握。「伏出」是伏神出现（被飞神冲开或生出）。这一条讲的是三层找法：先看伏神（用神不上卦时藏在哪一爻下），再看日、月能不能作用神，再看互卦。
>
> **Note:** The 'nuclear trigrams' are the two trigrams formed by taking the second, third and fourth lines and the third, fourth and fifth lines of the hexagram. The 'body and use lines' are not explained in the original; here 'body' and 'use' in the six-line method probably mean the Self and the Subject within the nuclear hexagram, though the reading is uncertain. 'The hidden line comes out' is the hidden line appearing (opened or brought out by the flying line). The entry describes three layers of searching: first the hidden line (under which line the Subject lies when it is absent from the hexagram), then whether the Day or Month can serve as the Subject, then the nuclear trigrams.

## LY.ILL.EL.13 休把卦名推祸福，莫将神煞决忧祯：其中有论卦名者切不可；论神煞者亦非；用神旺则墓门煞大煞动亦不死、用神既伤则月解天医同发亦死；五行为重，神煞难凭，星家专忌本命空亡非正道

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:不要用卦名去推祸福，不要拿神煞去断忧祯。注说：其中有拿卦名来论的，切不可；经里说，易卦的渊源是论五行阴阳的道理，本来生生不息，可怜愚昧无知的人颠倒阴阳去论卦名，如今有人拿神煞来论的也不对；经里又说，易卦阴阳在变通，五行生克妙无穷，现在的人不明白阴阳的道理，拿神煞来定吉凶。比如儿子占父亲的病，父爻旺相，墓门煞或大煞又动，用爻有气，怎么会死？又如丈夫占妻子的病，财爻无气，父爻兄弟爻都动，纵然月解、天医同时发动，用爻既然受了伤，怎么能不推断为死？所以以五行为重，神煞难以凭信，星家专门忌讳本命空亡，这不是正道。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Do not push fortune and misfortune by the names of hexagrams, and do not decide grief and omen by spirit-stars. The commentary says: those who judge by hexagram names must on no account be followed; the classic says the source of the Yi hexagrams is to judge by the principles of the five phases and yin and yang, which by nature give birth without end, and pitiable ignorant people turn yin and yang upside down and judge by hexagram names, and those who now judge by spirit-stars are also wrong; the classic says again that in the hexagrams yin and yang lie in change, and the generation and control of the five phases are marvellous without end, but people now do not understand the principle of yin and yang and use the spirit-stars to fix fortune. For example, if a son divines a father's illness and the Resource line is strong, and the Tomb Gate star or the Great Killing also moves, the Subject line having qi, how could he die? Again, if a husband divines a wife's illness and the Wealth line has no qi, and the Resource and Peer lines both move, even if Month Release and Heavenly Doctor move together, the Subject being already wounded, how can one judge otherwise than death? Therefore the five phases are weighty and the spirit-stars hard to rely on, and that astrologers particularly fear the Void of the natal branch is not the right way.

> **打架**:卦名、神煞能不能断病的生死：易林补遗说五行为重、神煞难凭，卦名不可论，星家专忌本命空亡不是正道；增删卜易的野鹤同样说卦名、卦身世身兼用、本命丧门、蛇虎星煞都不验，只看用神，只有卦变六冲例外（LY.ILL.ZS.01）；易冒也说四灭没、十卦、凶煞皆不死，神煞尤轻（LY.ILL.YM.11）；易隐则把黄泉杀、四灭卦、四没卦、棺椁杀列为占病逢之必死，又把三坵五墓、丧门吊客、死符丧车等列为动而临用必死的凶煞（LY.ILL.YY.05、YY.08）。前三家一边，易隐一边。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Whether hexagram names and spirit-stars can judge life and death in illness: the Yilin Buyi says the five phases weigh most and the spirit-stars are hard to rely on, that hexagram names cannot be used, and that astrologers' special fear of the Void of the natal branch is not the right way; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise says that hexagram names, using the Body and Self together, the mourning stars on the natal branch, and the Snake and Tiger stars are all unproved, that only the Subject is looked at, with the Six-Clash changing into Six-Clash the one exception (LY.ILL.ZS.01); the Yi Mao also says the Four Extinguishing and Submerging, the ten hexagrams and the malign stars do not mean death, and that spirit-stars weigh lighter still (LY.ILL.YM.11); the Yi Yin, on the other hand, lists the Yellow Springs, the Four Extinguishing, the Four Submerging and the Coffin killers as hexagrams that mean death for one who divines illness, and lists Three Mounds and Five Tombs, Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence, Death Tally and Funeral Carriage and the like as malign stars that mean death when they move and sit on the Subject (LY.ILL.YY.05, YY.08). The first three stand on one side and the Yi Yin on the other.

> **按**:这一段的两个「经云」是评注者引用的，原文没有说明出自哪部经，这里照录、没有指认出处。「墓门煞」「大煞」「月解」「天医」都是神煞名（月解、天医是吉神，墓门煞、大煞是凶煞）。评注者用两个反例把「神煞能救」「神煞能杀」都打掉：用爻有气，凶煞动也不死；用爻受伤，吉神动也死。「星家」指按命理星命来断的人。
>
> **Note:** The two 'the classic says' in this passage are quoted by the commentator, and the original does not say from which classic; they are copied as they stand and no source is asserted. 'Tomb Gate', 'Great Killing', 'Month Release' and 'Heavenly Doctor' are names of spirit-stars (Month Release and Heavenly Doctor being auspicious, Tomb Gate and Great Killing malign). The commentator uses two counter-examples to knock down both 'a spirit-star can save' and 'a spirit-star can kill': with the Subject line in qi a malign star moving does not kill, and with the Subject wounded an auspicious star moving does not save. 'Astrologers' are those who judge by the astrology of fate.

## LY.ILL.EL.17 占自己病断之二：先凭世象次凭卦身；怕月破旬空，喜生扶拱合；克世为忌客、生世为元神，随官入墓灾难瘥、助鬼伤身命必沉；世落空辨缓急、休囚被克无救则天年命尽、旺相无生助亦无所害；他人病身世随官入墓不必忌，命随鬼入墓即凶；卦身墓绝于月建或变爻决无救

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:自己占病，先凭世爻的象，再凭卦身，怕遇上月破、旬空，喜欢见到生、扶、拱、合；克世的爻是忌客，来生世的象是元神；随官入墓，灾难痊愈；助鬼伤身，命必沉沦。注说：自己占自己的病，专看世，爻如果临月破，不论新病旧病，命也难保全。世如果落空，要分缓急：急症还能医，旧病就不救；如果空中有气，眼下无妨，后来难保。世象不临旬空月破，再看旺衰：世如果休囚，又被动爻来克、或遭日月来伤、或变成死绝，毫无救助的爻，怎么会不是天年命尽？世即使休囚，如果得到动爻生助、或遇日月扶持、或变为有气，纵然临危也不会死；世如果旺相，虽然没有生助的爻，也没有什么害处。凡是克世的叫忌象，宜静不宜动；生世的叫元神，宜旺不宜空。占他人的病，身、世随官入墓，不必忌讳，命随鬼入墓就凶了。只有自己占身、世，命随鬼入墓的命不会回生，助鬼伤身也是同样的道理；又看卦身，如果墓、绝于月建，或者墓、绝于变爻，一定没有救了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining one's own illness, first rely on the image of the Self, then on the Body; it fears meeting Month-break or Void, and is glad to see generation, support, flanking and combination; the line that controls the Self is the adversary guest, and the image that comes to generate it is the Support; following the Pressure line into a tomb, the illness is hard to cure; helping the ghost to harm the Self, life surely sinks. The commentary says: in divining one's own illness, look at the Self alone; if the Self line sits in Month-break, whether the illness is new or old, life is hard to preserve. If the Self is Void, distinguish slow from urgent: an urgent illness can still be treated, an old illness is not saved; if there is qi in the Void, there is no harm for the moment, but later it is hard to keep. If the Self image does not sit in Void or Month-break, look next at strength and weakness: if the Self is resting or trapped and is controlled by a moving line, or wounded by the Day or Month, or changes into death or Extinction, with no line of rescue at all, how can it be anything but the end of one's allotted years? Though the Self is resting or trapped, if a moving line generates and helps it, or the Day and Month support it, or it changes into qi, even at the brink it does not die; if the Self is strong, even without a generating line there is no harm. Whatever controls the Self is called an adversary image and should be still, not moving; whatever generates the Self is called the Support and should be strong, not Void. In divining another's illness, the Body and Self following the Pressure line into a tomb need not be feared, but the natal branch following the ghost into a tomb is ominous. Only in divining for oneself, with the Body and Self, does a natal branch following the ghost into a tomb mean life will not be restored, and helping the ghost to harm the Self is the same in principle; look also at the Body: if it is in a tomb or Extinction under the Month, or in a tomb or Extinction under the changed line, there is surely no rescue.

## LY.ILL.YM.09 既疾必筮其吉凶而调剂之：慎于未病是君子之道；命随鬼墓是不治之疾（例：卦中子孙旺相病不死，亥命随官入墓，半年内溺水亡）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:生死有命，不是祈祷能改的；疾病在于人，不可以忽视；没生病之前就谨慎，是君子的做法。已经生了病，能不占问它的吉凶而加以调理吗？本命跟着鬼入了墓，是治不好的病。比如戌月甲辰日，占儿子的病，他生于己亥年，得同人卦，卦中子孙旺相、六爻安静，病应当不会死，但是亥命随官入了墓，按法大凶；后来他的病没有死，却不到半年溺水而死。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Life and death have their appointed lot and cannot be prayed away; illness lies with the person and must not be neglected; to take care before falling ill is the way of the gentleman. Once ill, can one fail to divine its fortune and adjust the treatment? If the natal branch follows the ghost into a tomb, it is an untreatable illness. For example, in the Xu month on the Jiachen day, someone divined a son's illness; he was born in a Jihai year and the hexagram was Tongren, with Output strong and the six lines still, so the illness should not kill; but the Hai natal branch followed Pressure into a tomb, which by the method is a great ill omen; afterwards he did not die of the illness, but within half a year he drowned.

## LY.ILL.YY.01 占疾病要慎而详：大端有四——占死生、占病症、占医药、占鬼崇；病何日起、病何处得是疾病的附占（游南子）

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When a person is unfortunate enough to fall ill and divines about it, one must be careful and thorough. ## LY.ILL.YY.03 占病取用爻：自占以世爻、身爻、命爻为用，代占以应爻为用；占家亲祖妣用官鬼、父兄妻子各以所属；五属不上卦从《黄金策》分爻取用；奴占家主用五爻、占主母用二爻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病取用爻：自己占，拿世爻、身爻、命爻做用；替别人占，拿应爻做用。占家里的亲人，祖父祖母用官鬼，父亲、兄弟、妻子、子女各用自己所属的那一类爻；这五类亲属如果不上卦，就按《黄金策》分爻的办法取用。奴仆丁口占家主，拿五爻做用；占主母，拿二爻做用。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Taking the Subject line in divining illness: in divining for oneself, the Self line, the Body line and the destiny line are the Subject; in divining for another, the Other line is the Subject. In divining for kin of the household, grandfather and grandmother use the Pressure line, and father, brother, wife and child each use the relation to which they belong; if these five kinds of relative do not appear in the hexagram, take the Subject by the Huangjin Ce's division of the lines. A servant or hired hand divining for the master of the house takes the fifth line as the Subject, and for the mistress the second line.

| 尔问我阻风于此，焦心如焚，能解我辈之忧者，乃子孙之爻耳，此卦子孙持世而化空，连朝风雨。 | 你问的是我们被风阻在这里，心急如焚，能解我们这些人的忧愁的，是子孙爻，这一卦子孙持世又变空，连着几天风雨。 | What you ask is that we are held up here by the wind, burning with anxiety, and what can relieve our worry is the Output line; in this hexagram Output holds the Self line and changes into Void, so there will be wind and rain for several days. |
| 只待出空而后晴，子孙出空之日，开船解我之忧矣。 | 只等出空以后才会晴，子孙出空的那天，开船解了我们的忧愁。 | Only after it leaves the Void will the weather clear; on the day Output leaves the Void the boat sails and our worry is relieved. |
| 果于乙巳日大晴，风恬浪静，尚无顺风。 | 果然在乙巳日大晴，风平浪静，还没有顺风。 | Indeed on the Yisi day it was fully clear, the wind was still and the waves calm, but there was not yet a fair wind. |
| 次日丙午，一日顺风，竟过湖矣。 | 第二天丙午，一整天顺风，就过了湖。 | On the next day, Bingwu, there was a fair wind all day and we crossed the lake. |
| 顺风顺水，畅饮开怀，得非子孙之力，释我之忧耶？ | 顺风顺水，畅饮开怀，这不是子孙的力量，解了我们的忧愁吗？ | With fair wind and current we drank freely and made merry; was this not the power of Output, relieving our worry? |
| 彼曰：卜书从来无此论，必有秘传。 | 他说：卜书从来没有这个说法，一定有秘传。 | He said: the divination books have never had this saying; there must be a secret transmission. |
| 余曰：昔亦以木兄而断，屡试不验，因见子孙值日以开舟而风顺者，比比皆然，遂悟出此理耳。 | 我说：我从前也是按木爻、兄弟爻来断，多次试都不灵，因为看到子孙值日开船而顺风的，比比皆是，才悟出这个道理。 | I said: I too used to judge by the Wood and Peer lines and it failed again and again; because I saw that boats setting sail on a day when Output rules the day have fair wind time after time, I came to understand this principle. |
| 彼又问：子水子孙化巳空，巳日已开舟矣，何故至丙午日而始得顺风？ | 他又问：子水子孙变出巳空，巳日已经开船了，为什么到丙午日才得到顺风？ | He asked again: the Zi Water Output line changes into Si, which is Void; the boat sailed on the Si day, so why was the fair wind got only on the Bingwu day? |
| 余曰：此卦占于丑日，子水到巳日虽则出空，还被日辰合住，故必待丙午日冲开，方遇顺风也。 | 我说：这一卦占在丑日，子水到巳日虽然出了空，还被日辰合住，所以一定要等到丙午日冲开，才遇到顺风。 | I said: this hexagram was divined on a Chou day, and though Zi Water leaves the Void on the Si day, it is still held by the Day's combination, so one had to wait until the Bingwu day to clash it open before meeting a fair wind. |
| 此子孙者，不犹夫疾病之篇以子孙为解忧之神者，理一而已。 | 这个子孙，不就像疾病篇里把子孙当作解忧之神一样吗，道理是同一个。 | This Output line is just like the Output of the chapter on illness taken as the spirit that relieves worry: the principle is one. |

> **按**:这一条讲的是天时行舟，不是病；野鹤拿它作类比，证明「子孙是解忧之神」这条道理通用。占的时候人已经在船上，所以不再按占天时的通例（木爻、兄弟爻主风云）去断。出行的判断另见其他章。
>
> **Note:** This entry is about weather and a voyage, not illness; the Old Man uses it as an analogy to show that 'Output is the spirit that relieves worry' holds generally. The people were already on board when they divined, and so he did not judge by the usual rule for weather (the Wood and Peer lines govern wind and cloud). The judgment of travel is treated in other chapters.

## LY.ILL.ZS.39 来人所问决定看什么：问病的吉凶，鬼持世或动是忧神、子孙持世或动是解忧喜悦之神；问病犯何鬼神，看鬼爻五行（金鬼冲犯武神、水鬼得罪河神）；问病源，看鬼爻五行脏腑（火鬼心经、水鬼肾部）——李我平

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼是忧神、子孙是喜悦解忧之神，虽然是古法，用起来很恰当；但也有不把鬼当忧神的，全看来问的人心里想问什么。他问病的吉凶：鬼爻持世、鬼在卦里发动，是忧神；子孙持世、子孙发动，是解忧喜悦的神。他问病是犯了哪路鬼神：卦里出现金鬼，一定冲犯了武神；水鬼，一定得罪了河神这一类。他问病源：卦里出现火鬼，一定属心经；水鬼，一定在肾部。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** That the Pressure line is the spirit of worry and Output the spirit of joy that relieves worry, though an old method, is very apt in use; but there are cases in which Pressure is not taken as the spirit of worry, and it all depends on what the asker has in mind to ask. If he asks about the fortune of the illness: Pressure holding the Self line or moving in the hexagram is the spirit of worry, and Output holding the Self line or moving is the spirit of joy that relieves worry. If he asks which ghost or spirit the illness offends: a Metal Pressure appearing in the hexagram means he has surely offended a martial god, and a Water Pressure that he has offended the river god and the like. If he asks about the source of the illness: a Fire Pressure appearing in the hexagram surely belongs to the Heart channel, and a Water Pressure surely lies in the Kidney.
