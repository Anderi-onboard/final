## LY.ILL.EL.22 缠身与脱体：鬼兴鬼动定缠绵、随官入墓助鬼伤身终难脱、世受官伤成痼疾、子孙一动立时安；六冲旦夕灾殃散、无鬼病不缠、鬼空无疾厄、忌神旺发患多年、鬼化福神他日解、用象兴隆祸不干

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以福神为主，用象为凭。身处灾难、相貌残损，鬼兴鬼动，一定缠绵不去；随官入墓，终究难脱；助鬼伤身，永不痊愈；世爻受官鬼所伤，成为痼疾；子孙一动，立刻安好。六冲的卦，灾殃旦夕就散；没有鬼，病终究不缠；鬼如果空亡，没有疾厄；忌神旺相发动，病患延续多年；鬼变出福神，他日就解；用象兴隆，祸事不相干。注说：占带不带疾，最嫌官鬼兴隆，官鬼如果旺相，病一定缠身，纵然不旺相，发动也是这样；若遇上随官入墓、或助鬼伤身，必成痼疾；鬼克世爻也难脱体；如果得到子孙旺相、或发动、或持世、或临日月，病就离身。六冲的卦，这个病容易消，合处逢冲的后来才解；鬼不上卦以及落空亡，永远没有殃疾；纵然有鬼象，如果变出福神，眼下虽然凶，后来当解脱；虽然凭官鬼，也要看用爻，用爻遇旬空月破，病也难痊愈；土象休囚，又被忌神发动，岂不成凶？用爻纵然弱，如果遇上动出元神，虽然有这个灾，决不至损寿；用爻父母若得兴隆，官鬼又居衰地，起初虽然见病，终究不成殃。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The fortune spirit is the main line, and the Subject image is the ground. The body in trouble, the features worn and disfigured; the ghost thriving and moving surely clings and lingers; following the Pressure line into a tomb, it is hard in the end to shake off; helping the ghost to harm the Self, it never heals; the Self wounded by Pressure becomes a chronic illness; when Output moves, ease comes at once. A Six-Clash hexagram disperses calamity by morning or evening; with no ghost the illness in the end does not cling; if the ghost is Void there is no illness; the Adversary strong and moving makes an affliction of many years; a ghost that changes into the fortune spirit is resolved some day later; the Subject image thriving, misfortune does not touch. The commentary says: for whether one carries an illness or not, most feared is a thriving Pressure line; if the Pressure line is strong the illness surely clings to the body, and even if it is not strong the same is true if it moves; meeting following into a tomb or helping the ghost to harm the Self, it will surely become a chronic illness; a ghost controlling the Self line also can hardly be shaken off; if Output is strong, or moves, or holds the Self, or sits on the Day or Month, the illness leaves the body. A Six-Clash hexagram makes this ailment easy to dispel, and a clash met in a combination is resolved only later; if the ghost is absent from the hexagram or falls into Void, there will never be affliction; even if there is a ghost image, if it changes into the fortune spirit, though it is ominous for the moment it will be resolved later; though one relies on the Pressure line, one must also look at the Subject line, and if the Subject meets Void or Month-break the illness too is hard to heal; if an Earth image is resting or trapped and the Adversary moves, how can it fail to be ominous? Though the Subject line is weak, if the Support comes out moving, then although there is this calamity it does not cost life; if the Resource line as Subject is thriving and the Pressure line sits in a weak place, then although at first there is illness, in the end it does no harm.

> **按**:这一章问的是「病会不会缠身、能不能脱体」。章首小标是「以福神为主」（福神是子孙），这和上一大段以用神为主不同：这里要看的是病能不能被解掉，而解病的是子孙。「相貌残」是形容病人的身形样貌受损。「土象休囚，更被忌神发动」的「土象」是用神属土（占父母尊长等），读法没有把握。最后一句的「用父」是用爻为父母爻。与黄金策「世持鬼爻病纵轻而难疗」（LY.ILL.HJC.34）、易隐的轻重（LY.ILL.YY.16、YY.19）同一路读法。
>
> **Note:** This chapter asks whether the illness will cling to the body and whether it can leave it. The chapter's subtitle is 'the fortune spirit is the main line' (the fortune spirit being Output), which differs from the previous large passage, where the Subject was the main line: here what is looked at is whether the illness can be dissolved, and it is Output that dissolves it. 'Features worn and disfigured' describes the sick person's frame and appearance being damaged. In 'an Earth image resting or trapped and the Adversary moving' the 'Earth image' is the Subject being Earth (as in divining a parent or elder), the reading being uncertain. The 'Resource as Subject' in the last sentence means the Subject line is the Resource line. It reads in the same way as the Huangjin Ce's 'the Self holding the ghost, though the illness be light it is hard to treat' (LY.ILL.HJC.34) and the Yi Yin's light and heavy (LY.ILL.YY.16, YY.19).

## LY.ILL.HDY.02 鬼在内外夜重夜轻、内外有鬼旧病再发：鬼在内夜重、鬼在外夜必轻、内外有鬼人昏困要防旧病再来；代占最怕应持鬼、官鬼持身命亦倾；病因怕鬼旺相、子孙不宜克世、财忌飞克、父母交重病沉、大怕世空、又嫌月破、更嫌身世入墓

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼在内卦，夜里病重；鬼在外卦，夜里一定较轻；内外都有鬼，人昏沉困顿，要提防旧病再来侵扰。替别人占，最怕应爻持鬼；官鬼持了身，命也会倾倒。眉批说：鬼在内，夜里重；鬼在外，灾厄解除；内外都有鬼，危险；代占时应爻有鬼、又逢官鬼的墓，命一定倾倒。后面的散句又把它重述了一遍：病因怕（鬼）旺相；子孙是药，不宜克世；财是禄命，忌被飞爻克；父母交重病重；最怕世空，又嫌月破，更嫌身世入墓；内外有鬼旧病再发；代占应爻持鬼凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A ghost in the inner trigram makes the illness heavy at night; a ghost in the outer trigram, at night it is surely lighter; with ghosts both inside and outside the person is faint and trapped, and one must beware of the old illness coming again to invade. In divining for another, most to be feared is the Other line holding the ghost; if the Pressure line holds the body, life too topples. The marginal comment says: ghost inside, heavy at night; ghost outside, calamity released; ghosts both inside and outside, dangerous; in divining for another, the Other with the ghost and the official tomb, life surely topples. The prose that follows restates it: for the cause of illness the ghost is feared when strong; Output is the medicine and should not control the Self; Wealth is the salary and life and fears the flying line controlling it; Resource crossing makes the illness heavy; most feared are the Self Void and Month-break, and more disliked still the body and Self entering a tomb; ghosts both inside and outside bring the old illness back; in divining for another, the Other line holding the ghost is ominous.

> **打架**:病在昼夜哪一头重，几家的依据不同。海底眼（LY.ILL.HDY.02）、黄金策的旧注和王洪绪（LY.ILL.HJC.12）都按官鬼在内卦还是外卦来分：鬼在内卦夜里重，鬼在外卦白天重（海底眼说「夜必轻」），内外都有鬼昼夜都不轻；易隐（LY.ILL.YY.15）则按官鬼的阴阳分：鬼属阴白天轻、夜里重，鬼属阳白天重、夜里轻。两个标准可以给出相反的结论（比如阳爻的鬼在内卦：前一种夜里重，后一种白天重），书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Whether an illness is heavier by day or by night: the books use different criteria. The Haidi Yan (LY.ILL.HDY.02), the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu (LY.ILL.HJC.12) all divide by whether the Pressure line is in the inner or the outer trigram: a ghost in the inner trigram is heavy at night, a ghost in the outer trigram heavy by day (the Haidi Yan says 'at night it is surely lighter'), and with ghosts both inside and outside neither day nor night is light; the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.15) divides by the yin or yang of the ghost: a yin ghost is light by day and heavy at night, a yang ghost heavy by day and light at night. The two criteria can give opposite conclusions (for example, a yang ghost in the inner trigram is heavy at night by the first and heavy by day by the second), and the books do not settle it.

> **按**:下面一段散句（「疾病病因，怕逢旺相……」）是对上面两首歌诀（LY.ILL.HDY.01、这一条）的复述，几乎逐句对应。「怕逢旺相」缺主语，按上下文（眉批「鬼怕旺相」）补作「（鬼）」。「更嫌身世入墓内犹重，官外尽凶」文字含混：「内犹重」按「里面的更重」译，「官外尽凶」按字面译作「官在外面就全凶」，和上一首歌诀眉批的「鬼外解厄」看上去不合，书里没有解释，这里不强作调和。「旧病再发」同黄金策「官藏世下，病起如前」（LY.ILL.HJC.07）是同一件事的另一个说法。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> **Note:** The prose that follows ('the cause of illness, fear meeting strength ...') restates the two verses above (LY.ILL.HDY.01 and this entry), almost sentence by sentence. 'Fears meeting strength' has no subject, and from the context (the marginal comment 'the ghost fears strength') it is supplied as 'the ghost'. 'Still more disliked is the body and Self entering a tomb, inside heavier still, with the official outside all ominous' is loose: 'inside heavier still' is rendered 'what is inside is heavier still', and 'with the official outside all ominous' is rendered literally; it looks at odds with the marginal comment on the previous verse, 'ghost outside, calamity released', and the book does not explain, so no reconciliation is forced here. 'The old illness flares again' is another way of saying the Huangjin Ce's 'the Pressure line hidden under the Self, the illness rises as before' (LY.ILL.HJC.07). The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.

## LY.ILL.HJC.12 上实下空，夜轻日重：鬼在内卦夜重、在外卦日重，内外皆有鬼昼夜不轻；二鬼一旺一空或一动一静则分上下断日夜

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼在内卦，病夜里重；鬼在外卦，病白天重；内外都有鬼，昼夜都不轻。如果卦里虽然有两个鬼，但一个旺一个空、或者一个动一个静，就是白天轻夜里重，或者白天重夜里轻，要分上下来断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure in the inner trigram, the illness is heavy at night; Pressure in the outer trigram, heavy by day; Pressure both inside and outside, not light by day or night. If the hexagram has two Pressure lines but one is strong and one Void, or one moving and one still, it is light by day and heavy at night, or heavy by day and light at night, and one judges by upper and lower.

The commentary says: on a day when the Pressure line is strong the illness is heavy, on its store day it is trapped and hard, on its Extinction day it is light and tolerable. The ghost changing into a ghost: the illness advances and retreats, or there is a change of symptoms, or an old illness flares, or the symptoms are mixed. The ghost holding the Self: the illness is hard to root out; the Pressure line carrying a killing and holding the Self is a wasting illness. If the Pressure line in the hexagram is strong on the Day, it is a sudden illness; if strong on the Month, an illness of a full month; a Pressure line without qi sitting on the body is a long illness that can hardly be cured at once. With no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to settle; the ghost moving and wounding the body, the symptoms are urgent. In every divination, a strong ghost that does not kill means an easy cure, a weak ghost that does not kill means an illness hard to treat.

'An illness of a full month' is translated as 'an illness of a full month'. 'A strong ghost that does not kill means an easy cure; a weak ghost that does not kill means an illness hard to treat' means that if a strong ghost has not killed, the cure comes fast, because a ghost strong yet not fatal shows the Subject line can bear it, and if a weak ghost has not killed the illness on the contrary drags on — the original does not explain, and only the literal sense is translated. ## LY.ILL.YM.24 鬼者病之源、祟之状：鬼旺则重、衰则轻、动则骤而变、合则染而缠、散则疴将解、空则病未真

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼是病的来源，是祟作怪的样子：所以鬼旺病就重，鬼衰病就轻；鬼动，病来得急而且会变；鬼被合住，灾就染上身、缠着不放；鬼被冲散，病快要解了；鬼落空，病还不是真的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The ghost is the source of illness and the shape of the haunting: so when the ghost is strong the illness is heavy, and when the ghost is weak it is light; when the ghost moves the trouble comes on suddenly and changes; when the ghost combines the calamity clings and lingers; when the ghost scatters the ailment is about to be resolved; when the ghost is Void the illness is not yet real.

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用日破、月破来分病是成了一半；用鬼在内、在外来分病在里还是在表；用鬼在变爻上还是伏着来分病是新的还是旧的。注说：问有没有病的，病算成了一半；问是不是没有病的，病算解了一半；内是里、外是表，变出来的是新病，伏着的是旧病，就是这个意思。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Day-break and Month-break are used to tell a half-formed illness; being in the inner or in the outer to tell inside from surface; sitting in a change or in hiding to tell new from old. The note says: asked whether there is illness, it is half formed; asked whether there is none, the illness is half resolved; the inner is inside and the outer is surface, the changed is new and the hidden is old: this is the meaning.

| 倘占父母之灾，官鬼为父母之元神； | 如果占父母的灾病，官鬼是父母的元神； | but if one divines a parent's affliction, Pressure is the Support of the Resource line; |
| 妻占夫病，鬼为用神，岂宜子孙动以伤耶？ | 妻子占丈夫的病，鬼是用神，怎么可以子孙动来伤它呢？ | and if a wife divines her husband's illness, Pressure is the Subject, so how can Output be right to move and harm it? |
| 不惟不能解忧，反添忧矣。 | 不但不能解忧，反而添了忧愁。 | It not only cannot relieve the worry, it adds to it. |

> **按**:野鹤先重写黄金策的「世持鬼爻，病纵轻而难疗」，再拆成两种说法：自占和代占。黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.34）泛说占病怕鬼持世，王洪绪只限自占；野鹤的第一种和王洪绪一致，第二种是他另加的。觉子的补充和 LY.ILL.HJC.32 里王洪绪说的「占父母、丈夫病不宜子孙发动」是一个意思。
>
> **Note:** The Old Man first rewrites the Huangjin Ce's line 'Pressure holding the Self line: though light, hard to treat' and then splits it into two views, one for divining oneself and one for divining another. The old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.34) says in general that in divining illness Pressure holding the Self line is feared, and Wang Hongxu limits it to divining one's own; the Old Man's first view agrees with Wang Hongxu and the second is his own addition. Juezi's addition says the same as Wang Hongxu's remark in LY.ILL.HJC.32 that in divining a parent's or a husband's illness Output should not move.
