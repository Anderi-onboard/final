## LY.ILL.HJC.12 上实下空，夜轻日重：鬼在内卦夜重、在外卦日重，内外皆有鬼昼夜不轻；二鬼一旺一空或一动一静则分上下断日夜

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼在内卦，病夜里重；鬼在外卦，病白天重；内外都有鬼，昼夜都不轻。如果卦里虽然有两个鬼，但一个旺一个空、或者一个动一个静，就是白天轻夜里重，或者白天重夜里轻，要分上下来断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure in the inner trigram, the illness is heavy at night; Pressure in the outer trigram, heavy by day; Pressure both inside and outside, not light by day or night. If the hexagram has two Pressure lines but one is strong and one Void, or one moving and one still, it is light by day and heavy at night, or heavy by day and light at night, and one judges by upper and lower.

## LY.ILL.HJC.13 动生变克，暮热朝凉：动爻为始变爻为终，动爻生扶用爻而变爻刑克用爻则朝凉暮热、日轻夜重，动克变生反之

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占卦时动爻是开头、变爻是结尾。卦中动爻生扶用爻、而变爻刑克用爻，病一定早上凉快、晚上发热，或者白天轻夜里重；动爻克用爻、变爻生用爻，反过来断。鬼爻化出所喜的爻、所喜的爻化出鬼爻，也是这样。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In every divination the moving line is the beginning and the changed line the end. If the moving line in the hexagram generates and supports the Subject while the changed line punishes and controls it, the illness is surely cool in the morning and hot in the evening, or light by day and heavy at night; if the moving line controls the Subject and the changed line generates it, judge the reverse. Where a Pressure line changes into a welcome line, or a welcome line changes into Pressure, it is the same.

**《黄金策·疾病》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6806 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6806*

## LY.ILL.HJC.34 世持鬼爻病纵轻而难疗：鬼持世必难脱体，大象纵轻也不易愈；用爻有气、子孙当权则眼前可医、日后必不断根

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼爻持世，病就是轻，也难医治：一定难以脱身。整个卦象再轻，也不容易痊愈。就算用爻有气、子孙当权，眼前能医好，以后也一定断不了根。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When a Pressure line holds the Self line, even a light illness is hard to treat: it is surely hard to shake off. However light the whole image, it does not easily heal. Even if the Subject line has force and Output holds power, so that it can be cured for now, the root will surely not be cut later.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6886 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6886*

## LY.ILL.HJC.36 用壮有扶切恐太刚则折：占病不喜旺相，人在病中必衰弱，得时有气又遇生扶反是太刚则折，到生旺日必凶；有日辰动爻刑克则不嫌其旺

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:别的占都喜欢旺相，只有占病不喜欢：人在病里一定衰弱，平时再强健的人这时也瘦了，哪来的旺？所以用爻得时有气、又遇到日辰、动爻来生扶，是「太刚则折」的兆头，到生旺的日子一定凶；如果有动爻、日辰来刑克它，就不嫌它旺。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Every other divination likes strength, but divining illness does not: a person in illness is surely weak, and even one who is usually robust grows thin by then, so where is the strength? Hence a Subject line that is in season and has force and also meets generation or support from the Day or a moving line is a sign of 'too rigid, it snaps', and on the day of birth or strength it is surely unlucky; if a moving line or the Day punishes or controls it, its strength is not held against it.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6892 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6892*

| 用壮有扶，切恐太刚则折； | 用爻强壮又有生扶，千万怕太刚硬而折断； | A Subject that is strong and supported: above all fear that what is too rigid snaps; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6893–6894 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), lines 6893–6894*

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2370 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2370*

| 若有日辰动爻刑克，则不嫌其旺矣。 | 如果有日辰、动爻来刑克，就不嫌它旺了。 | if the Day or a moving line punishes or controls it, its strength is not held against it. |
| 所谓“太过者损之斯成”也。 | 这就是所谓「太过的，损掉一些才成」。 | This is what is meant by 'what is excessive is made complete by taking from it'. |

> **打架**:什么样的旺才是「太刚则折」：旧注说凡是得时有气、又遇日辰动爻生扶的都是；王洪绪的条件更窄：用神要临月建，又得日辰生扶拱合，再遇动爻生扶，最怕原神也值月建。
>
> **Where they disagree:** What sort of strength is 'too rigid, it snaps': the old commentary says whatever is in season and has force and also meets generation or support from the Day or a moving line; Wang Hongxu's condition is narrower: the Subject must sit on the Month, get generation, support, embracing or combining from the Day, and then meet generation or support from a moving line, and most feared is the Support also ruling the Month.

> **按**:「太刚则折」的意思是：太硬的东西容易折断。王洪绪引的「太过者损之斯成」，是说过头的东西要减掉一些才成。旧注说「至生旺日必凶」，书里没有说「生旺日」指哪些日子，按上下文是用爻长生、帝旺的日子。旧注这一条和 LY.ILL.HJC.48 是一对：这里说太旺要折，那里说太弱体虚。
>
> **Note:** 'Too rigid, it snaps' means that what is too hard breaks easily. Wang Hongxu's 'what is excessive is made complete by taking from it' says that a thing that has gone too far must be reduced before it is complete. The old commentary says 'on the day of birth or strength it is surely unlucky' and the book does not say which days these are; from the context they are the days on which the Subject line is at Birth or Peak. This entry and LY.ILL.HJC.48 are a pair: here too strong snaps, there too weak and the body frail.

## LY.ILL.HJC.68 鬼带日辰定非久病：鬼带日辰又动出卦中的是近来暴得之病；日辰虽是官鬼却不现卦中，则病眼下正炽，须过此才可用药

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼带着日辰、又动出在卦里的，一定是近来突然得的病，不是久病。如果日辰虽然是官鬼，却没有出现在卦里，就不是这样；可以说这病眼下正厉害，必须等这一阵过去，才可以用药。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Pressure line that carries the Day and moves out in the hexagram is surely an illness got suddenly in these days, not a long one. If the Day is Pressure but does not appear in the hexagram, it is not so; one can say the illness is at its fiercest just now, and only after this has passed can medicine be used.

## LY.ILL.YY.15 昼夜与先后：鬼属阴日轻夜重、属阳日重夜轻；外鬼旺内鬼衰先轻后重；动爻克变爻生先重后轻；游魂鬼用言语恍惚、归魂鬼用昏闷不语

## LY.ILL.YY.16 鬼伏鬼化与轻重之日：鬼伏藏病来不觉；鬼化鬼膏肓之疾；鬼伏鬼、鬼化鬼非变症则两病交作；鬼生旺日重、墓胎日困、死绝日轻；元神值日轻、忌神值日重；六冲乱动者重；用持龙福被动爻伤虽重不死；鬼动伤用而月日冲鬼者免

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼伏藏的，病来了不觉得；鬼变鬼的，是膏肓的重病；鬼伏在鬼下、鬼变出鬼的，不是病症有变，就是两种病同时发作；用爻下面伏着鬼的，鬼旺时复发。世爻持着子孙、下面伏着鬼的，子孙旺的那天病退，鬼旺的那天病重。鬼生旺的日子病重，鬼入墓、胎的日子病困，鬼死、绝的日子病轻；原神当值的日子一定轻，忌神当值的日子一定重；财爻入墓、绝的日子也重。卦是六冲又乱动的，重。用爻带着青龙、福德而被动爻伤到的，虽然重也不会死；鬼发动伤到用爻，而月、日冲鬼的，临到凶险也能免。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the ghost is hidden, the illness comes unnoticed; a ghost changing into a ghost is a disease lodged in the vitals; a ghost hidden beneath a ghost, or a ghost changing into a ghost, is either a change of symptoms or two illnesses at once; a ghost hidden beneath the Subject line makes it recur when the ghost is strong. If the Self holds Output with a ghost hidden beneath it, the illness retreats on the day Output is strong and worsens on the day the ghost is strong. It is heavy on days the ghost is born or strong, wearied on days the ghost is in a tomb or Womb, and light on days it is in Death or Extinction; on days when the Support is on duty it is surely light and when the Adversary is on duty surely heavy; it is also heavy on days when the Wealth line is in a tomb or Extinction. A Six-Clash hexagram with lines moving in disorder is heavy. If the Subject line carries the Dragon and Fortune and is harmed by a moving line, it is heavy but he does not die; if the ghost moves to harm the Subject line but the Month and Day clash the ghost, he escapes even at the brink of danger.

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼持世，病虽轻也难治，有两种情形。自己占病：官鬼持世，病难痊愈；就算子孙发动、克掉身边的鬼，眼前好了，终究断不了根，不然就又生别的病，叫「病不离身，身不离药」；官鬼持世又临帝旺、长生的，一定得长久的病。替别人占六亲的病：官鬼持世是忧郁的样子，只喜欢子孙发动、克掉这份忧郁，就算用神衰弱也管保平安。觉子补充：这个办法用于占自己和兄弟、儿子、妻子；占父母的病，官鬼是父母的元神；妻子占丈夫的病，鬼是用神，子孙一动反而伤它。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Pressure line holding the Self line means that even a light illness is hard to treat, and there are two cases. In divining one's own illness: with Pressure holding the Self line the illness is hard to heal; even if Output moves and controls away the Pressure by one's side, so that he recovers for the present, the root is never cut, or else another illness grows, which is called 'the illness does not leave the body, the body does not leave medicine'; if Pressure holds the Self line and sits on Peak or Birth, he surely gets a long-lasting illness. In divining a relation's illness for another: Pressure holding the Self line is the picture of worry and gloom, and all that is wanted is Output moving to control away this gloom, and then even if the Subject is weak, peace is assured. Juezi adds: this method serves for divining oneself and a brother, son or wife; in divining a parent's illness Pressure is the Support of the Resource line, and when a wife divines her husband's illness Pressure is the Subject, so a moving Output harms it instead.
