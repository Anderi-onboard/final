## LY.ILL.DYTJ.18 火珠林(断易天机引)·占疾病何日愈：爻须旺相子扶身、龙蛇动处当轻利、狼虎过伤是重辰；己身自病何时瘥福德来临愈必真

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病问哪一天痊愈，爻要旺相，子孙扶身；青龙、螣蛇动的地方，当有轻快顺利；狼、虎过来伤，就是病重的时辰。重象不妨身带困顿，一交上恐怕气就离身；自己身上的病，什么时候好？福德来临的时候，一定真的痊愈。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask on what day an illness is cured: the line must be strong and Output support the Body; where the Azure Dragon and the Coiling Snake move it should be light and smooth, and where the Wolf and the Tiger pass and injure is the heavy time. In a heavy image there is no harm if the body is worn, but on meeting it one must fear the breath leaves the body; for one's own illness, when will it be cured? When Output arrives the cure is certain.

**《断易天机·占疾病第二十四·火珠林》原文** — 《断易天机》第 1721–1722 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Section 24, on illness: Huozhu Lin", base text; Duanyi Tianji, lines 1721–1722*

The two lines 'in a heavy image there is no harm if the body is worn; on meeting it one must fear the breath leaves the body' are not certain and are rendered literally. 'When Output arrives the cure is certain' says that the month or day on which Output (Fortune-and-Virtue) appears is the day of recovery; Output governing the timing is also said in the Chan'ao Gezhang and other books (around LY.ILL.CA.07).

## LY.ILL.DYTJ.42 期日章·占病应期：子孙生旺为病瘥、鬼爻墓旺病身危；药爻福德寻生旺、日辰遇着是神医

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看病愈的日子：子孙生旺的时候，病好；鬼爻入墓、旺的时候，病人危险。药爻（福德）要找它生旺的日子，遇到那个日辰，就是遇到神医。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For the day of recovery from illness: when Output is born strong, the illness is cured; when the Pressure line enters a tomb or is strong, the sick person is in danger. For the medicine line (Output) look for the day on which it is born strong, and meeting that day is meeting a divine physician.

**《断易天机·期日章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 962 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The chapter on the day of the outcome", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 962*

| 鬼爻墓旺病身危， | 鬼爻入墓、旺的时候，病人危险。 | when the Pressure line enters a tomb or is strong, the sick person is in danger. |

**《断易天机·期日章》原文** — 《断易天机》第 962 行 · *Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The chapter on the day of the outcome", base text; Duanyi Tianji, line 962*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 药爻福德寻生旺， | 药爻（福德）要找它生旺的日子， | For the medicine line (Output) look for the day on which it is born strong, |
| 日辰遇着是神医（6）。 | 遇到那个日辰，就是遇到神医。 | and meeting that day is meeting a divine physician. |

> **按**:这是《期日章》这首歌里说到病的两联：歌里一联管一件事的应期，求财、六甲（怀孕生产）、行人、病、失物、婚姻、求官、公事、阴宅、医药、天气各一联。「子孙生旺为病瘥」是子孙爻长生或帝旺的日子病好；「鬼爻墓旺病身危」是鬼爻入墓或旺的日子病重。「药爻福德寻生旺，日辰遇着是神医」是药爻（子孙福德）生旺的日辰，医药有效，「神医」按字面译。歌后有今人写的「（3）」「（6）」注号，那几条注不收。这两联和孙膑歌的「福德持世鬼暂退」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.02）、火珠林「占疾病何日愈」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.18）里子孙福德临月日愈病是同一个道理。
>
> **Note:** These are the two couplets on illness in the song of the 'chapter on the day of the outcome': in the song each couplet governs the timing of one matter: wealth, the six jia (pregnancy and birth), a traveller, illness, lost things, marriage, seeking office, official business, a yin house (a grave), medicine and the weather one couplet each. 'When Output is born strong the illness is cured' is that the illness gets better on the day when the Output line is at Long Life or Emperor's Prime; 'when the Pressure line enters a tomb or is strong the sick person is in danger' is that the illness is heavy on the day when the Pressure line enters a tomb or is strong. 'For the medicine line (Output) look for the day on which it is born strong; meeting that day is meeting a divine physician' is the day branch on which the medicine line (Output) is born strong, when the medicine is effective, and 'divine physician' is rendered literally. After the song there are annotation marks such as '(3)' and '(6)' written by a modern hand, and those annotations are not quoted. These two couplets are the same principle as Sun Bin's song, 'Output holding the Self, the ghost retreats for a time' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02), and the Huozhu Lin's 'on which day an illness will be cured' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.18), in which Output arriving on the month or day cures the illness.

相关:LY.ILL.DYTJ.02、LY.ILL.DYTJ.18

## LY.ILL.EL.19 占何日病退：用临生旺体当安、元神值日灾须减、忌象遭伤病必痊、助鬼伤身逢福解、随官入墓见冲欢、主象临绝待生时；元神旺相忌客休囚取用爻值日、用爻衰弱取生旺之期、用爻太旺反喜入墓之时、不太旺取元神值日、忌神动逢冲克忌神之日

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:凡是占病人哪一天痊愈，用爻临在生旺上，身体应当安好；元神值日，灾应减轻；忌象受伤，病必痊愈；助鬼伤身，遇到福解（福神来解）；随官入墓，见冲就欢喜；如果主象落在绝地，就等它逢生的时候，免去祸愆。注说：占病哪一天痊愈，要推主象。卦里元神旺相、忌客休囚而有用爻的，就取用爻值日的那天安好；如果用爻衰弱，才取生旺的时期。倘若用爻重重太旺，反而喜欢它入墓的时候；如果不太旺，又取元神值日；忌神如果动，必须遇上冲克忌神的日子，才能安康。若遇助鬼伤身，又利于子孙值日。随官入墓，还要求冲开墓的日辰。用象如果临在绝地而逢生，一定主平安。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To divine on what day a patient recovers: with the Subject line on generation or strength, the body should be well; on the day of the Support the calamity should lessen; when the adversary image is wounded the illness surely heals; helping the ghost to harm the Self is resolved by meeting the fortune spirit; following the Pressure line into a tomb is glad to meet a clash; if the chief image sits in Extinction, wait for the time it is born, and misfortune is avoided. The commentary says: to divine on what day the illness is cured, work out the chief image. If in the hexagram the Support is strong and the adversary guest resting or trapped and there is a Subject line, take the day on which the Subject line is on duty for ease; if the Subject line is weak, take a period of generation or strength. If the Subject line is far too strong, on the contrary it is glad of the time it enters a tomb; if it is not too strong, take the day of the Support; if the Adversary moves, one must meet the day that clashes and controls the Adversary before health returns. Meeting helping the ghost to harm the Self, the day of Output on duty is again favourable. Following the Pressure line into a tomb, seek further a day that clashes the tomb open. If the Subject image sits in Extinction and meets generation, it surely means peace.

**《易林补遗·疾病生克论》原文** — 《易林补遗》第 1312 行 · *Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "On generation and control in illness", base text; Yilin Buyi, line 1312*

>
> **Note:** 'Resolved by meeting the fortune spirit' means Output moving or on duty resolves the ill of helping the ghost to harm the Self; the 'body' in 'the body should be well' is the physical body. 'The Subject line far too strong is on the contrary glad of the time it enters a tomb' means that an over-strong Subject line needs to enter a tomb to be drained of its excess, the same reasoning as the Huangjin Ce's 'the Subject strong and supported, fearing it is too rigid and breaks' (LY.ILL.HJC.36): in divining illness one does not welcome too much strength. This entry is the same matter as the Huangjin Ce's timing (LY.ILL.HJC.57), the Zengshan Buyi's timing (LY.ILL.ZS.16), the Yi Yin's days of recovery (LY.ILL.YY.04) and the Yi Mao's days of ease (LY.ILL.YM.19), and the books agree in outline on how to take the day: the day the Subject (or Support) is strong and on duty, the day that clashes and controls the Adversary, the day that clashes the tomb open.

## LY.ILL.EL.20 占何日病凶：用爻无气更遭伤、忌爻逢生助之日便主身危；忌神旺动仇人发而无用爻则待用神值日难避；月破用爻不拘衰旺命必归阴；元神被克灾加重、忌客逢生定受殃——书中九例(观之益、涣之垢、剥、萃、师、艮、归妹……)

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:病人来占，要细看凶日：用爻无气，又遭伤克，忌爻哪天逢到生助，便主身危，立在孝堂；忌神旺动、仇神发动，用神失位反而无妨；变爻怕日辰临在用爻的位置，如果挨过去就免于凄惶；元神被克灾更重，忌客逢生一定受殃；月破用爻，天命就止，纵然旺相也必死。注说：论病哪一天出现凶险，不过看用爻的生克：用爻如果无气、被日辰克的就凶，忌神动、用爻很衰的时候，遇上再生忌神的日子就死；又说卦里元神不发，忌神兴旺、仇神都动，唯独没有用爻，眼前无事，等到日后用神值日就难以回避，一定入黄泉；倘若忌神、仇神兴旺而元神同时发动，也没有主象，等到用爻值日反而无妨；再说卦里用象既衰，全靠元神相救，担心忌客来伤，日辰克制元神，灾就沉重；忌象如果逢生旺的时候，一定成凶咎；主象如果逢月破，不论衰旺，命必归阴。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For a patient who comes to divine, examine the ominous days closely: with the Subject line lacking qi and moreover wounded, on the day the adversary line meets generation and support the body is in danger, standing in the house of mourning; the Adversary strong and moving and the enemy spirit rising, the Subject having lost its place is on the contrary all right; a change fears the Day sitting on the Subject's place, and if it is got past there is freedom from dismay; the Support controlled makes the calamity heavier, and the adversary guest meeting generation surely brings disaster; the Subject line in Month-break ends one's allotted span, and even if strong it will die. The commentary says: to judge on what day the illness turns ominous is nothing but looking at the generation and control of the Subject line: if the Subject line has no qi and is controlled by the Day it is ominous, and when the Adversary moves and the Subject line is very weak, meeting a day that again generates the Adversary means death; again, if in the hexagram the Support does not move, the Adversary thrives and the enemy spirit all move, and there is only no Subject line, nothing happens for now, but when the Subject's day comes it is hard to avoid and he surely enters the Yellow Springs; if the Adversary and enemy spirit thrive and the Support moves with them, and there is also no chief image, then waiting until the Subject line's day is on the contrary harmless; moreover, if the Subject image in the hexagram is already weak, everything depends on the Support to rescue it, and one worries that the adversary guest will come to wound it, and if the Day controls the Support the calamity will be heavy; if the adversary image meets a time of generation or strength it is sure to make misfortune; if the chief image meets Month-break, whether strong or weak, life must return to the shades.

## LY.ILL.HJC.39 主象伏藏则病迁延：用爻不上卦其病难安（旧注：生扶无处着落，病恍惚朦胧不爽快），要等它值日出露、或久病等它值年值月才愈（王洪绪）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻不上卦，这病一定拖着难好：出现在卦里的爻，日辰、动爻才能生它、合它，不上卦，就算有生扶也没有地方落脚，这种病也是恍惚朦胧、不爽快的病。要等用爻值日露出来，或者久病等它值年、值月才好，所以说一定拖延日子和月份。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Subject line that is absent from the hexagram means the illness drags on and cannot settle: a line that appears can be generated and combined by the Day and moving lines, but if it is not in the hexagram, where would generation and support land? Such an illness is vague and hazy, one that never feels clear. One must wait until the Subject line rules the day and comes into the open, or in a long illness wait for the year or month that it rules, before it heals; hence it is said to drag on for days and months.

## LY.ILL.YM.18 问危时其法有三：官鬼当值的日月（占父母不论）、忌仇动而用神潜伏遇用神出现之时、用神月破日破失气亡援之时

## LY.ILL.YY.07 生死·死日：忌神长生之日死（土用亥日、木用巳日、火用申日、水用巳午日、金用寅日）；土鬼动者鬼爻长生之日死；六冲卦用爻败死墓绝之日死；用空无气者元神绝忌神生之日死；用逢空冲者初病即愈久病难痊；土旺动克用者克用爻之日死；卦有三无救（无火无财无子）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:死的日子：忌神长生的那天死（土做用的，亥日死；木的，巳日死；火的，申日死；水的，巳日、午日死；金的，寅日死）；土鬼发动的，鬼爻长生的那天死；卦是六冲的，用爻败、死、墓、绝的那天死；用爻落空又没有气的，原神绝、忌神生的那天死；土旺盛发动克到用爻的，克用爻的那天死。用爻遇空又被冲，刚起的病马上会好，久病却难好。卦里有三种没有救的：没有火、没有财、没有子孙。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The days of death: he dies on the day the Adversary is born (for Earth as the Subject, a Hai day; Wood, a Si day; Fire, a Shen day; Water, a Si or Wu day; Metal, a Yin day); if an Earth ghost moves, on the day the Pressure line is born; if the hexagram is Six-Clash, on a day when the Subject line is at ruin, death, tomb or Extinction; if the Subject line is Void and without force, on the day the Support is extinct and the Adversary is born; if strong moving Earth controls the Subject line, on the day that controls it. If the Subject line meets Void and is clashed, a recent illness is cured at once while a long illness is hard to cure. A hexagram has three kinds of no rescue: no Fire, no Wealth, no Output.

## LY.ILL.ZS.16 近病逢空、化空即愈，若两卦俱值三合必成久病而终：应在填实墓库的年月（例：妻占夫近病，卯官化未土空亡，两卦俱三合，明年未月卒）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:近病：用神落空、或变成空，到出空的日子就好；可是如果两个卦都值三合，就会拖成久病，最后死掉，死期应在把它的墓填实的年月。
