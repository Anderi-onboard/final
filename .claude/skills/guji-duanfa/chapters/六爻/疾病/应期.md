# 六爻 · 疾病 · 应期

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Illness · Timing: when the illness lifts or worsens, and when death may come*

2 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.ILL.HJC.39 主象伏藏则病迁延：用爻不上卦其病难安（旧注：生扶无处着落，病恍惚朦胧不爽快），要等它值日出露、或久病等它值年值月才愈（王洪绪）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻不上卦，这病一定拖着难好：出现在卦里的爻，日辰、动爻才能生它、合它，不上卦，就算有生扶也没有地方落脚，这种病也是恍惚朦胧、不爽快的病。要等用爻值日露出来，或者久病等它值年、值月才好，所以说一定拖延日子和月份。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Subject line that is absent from the hexagram means the illness drags on and cannot settle: a line that appears can be generated and combined by the Day and moving lines, but if it is not in the hexagram, where would generation and support land? Such an illness is vague and hazy, one that never feels clear. One must wait until the Subject line rules the day and comes into the open, or in a long illness wait for the year or month that it rules, before it heals; hence it is said to drag on for days and months.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6903 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6903*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 主象伏藏，定主迁延乎日月； | 主象伏藏不出，一定主拖延日子和月份； | A main image hidden away: it surely means dragging on through days and months; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6904–6905 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), lines 6904–6905*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 用爻不上卦，其病必难安。 | 用爻不上卦，这病一定难以安稳。 | A Subject line absent from the hexagram: the illness is surely hard to settle. |
| 盖凡出现之爻，日辰动爻能生能合； | 因为凡是出现在卦里的爻，日辰、动爻能生它、能合它； | For a line that appears in the hexagram can be generated and combined by the Day and moving lines; |
| 若不上卦，虽有生扶，着于何地？ | 如果不上卦，就算有生扶，落在哪里？ | if it is not in the hexagram, then though there is generation and support, where does it land? |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2376 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2376*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 用爻不上卦，纵有提拔扶引者，必待其值日出露，或久病必待其值年值月方愈，故曰定主迁延日月也。 | 用爻不上卦，就算有提拔、扶引它的，一定要等它值日的时候露出来，或者久病一定要等它值年、值月才好，所以说一定拖延日子和月份。 | A Subject line absent from the hexagram: even if something would raise and lead it out, one must wait for the day it rules, when it comes into the open, or in a long illness wait for the year or month it rules before it heals; hence it is said to drag on for days and months. |

> **按**:两家讲的是同一个卦象的两面：旧注讲这种病是什么样子（恍惚朦胧、不爽快），王洪绪讲它什么时候好（值日、值年、值月）。王洪绪引的经文是「迁延乎岁月」，而他自己的注里说「迁延日月」，和卜筮大全的「迁延乎日月」一致，「岁月」大概是印错了。
>
> **Note:** The two speak of two sides of the same image: the old commentary of what sort of illness this is (vague, hazy, never clear), Wang Hongxu of when it heals (on the day, year or month the line rules). The verse as Wang Hongxu quotes it reads 'dragging on through years and months', while his own note says 'dragging on through days and months', in agreement with the Bushi Daquan's 'days and months'; 'years' is probably a printing slip.

## LY.ILL.HJC.57 欲决病痊当究福神之动静、要知命尽须详鬼杀之旺衰：旧注按子孙、扶持之爻、所喜之爻、制伏恶杀之爻断痊愈之期，按鬼爻墓绝日或用爻生旺日断死期；王洪绪说不可以词害义，福神指原神、鬼煞指忌神

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要断病能不能好，看福神的动静；要知道命数什么时候尽，看鬼杀的旺衰。福神、鬼杀指哪一爻，两家读法不同（见分歧）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To decide whether an illness will heal, study whether the fortune spirit moves or is still; to know when life is used up, examine whether the Pressure killer is strong or weak. Which lines the 'fortune spirit' and the 'Pressure killer' are, the two commentaries read differently (see the dispute).

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6968 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6968*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 欲决病痊，当究福神之动静； | 要判断病能不能痊愈，应当研究福神是动还是静； | To decide whether the illness will heal, one should study whether the fortune spirit (Output) moves or is still; |
| 要知命尽，须详鬼杀之旺衰。 | 要知道命数是否已尽，必须仔细看鬼杀是旺还是衰。 | to know whether life is used up, one must examine whether the Pressure killer is strong or weak. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6969–6971 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), lines 6969–6971*

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2412 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2412*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 读是篇者不可以词害义。 | 读这一篇的人不可以拿字面害了本意。 | One who reads this piece must not let the words hurt the meaning. |
| 福神者，其义轻于子孙而重于原神也； | 福神这个词，它的分量比子孙轻，而比原神重； | The 'fortune spirit' is lighter in meaning than Output and heavier than the Support; |
| 鬼煞者，其理在于忌神而不在于官鬼也。 | 鬼煞这个词，它的道理在忌神，而不在官鬼。 | the principle of the 'Pressure killer' lies in the Adversary and not in the Pressure line. |
| 凡占病，如遇原神旺动，即使用神空破伏藏者，其病可愈； | 凡是占病，如果遇到原神旺相发动，就算用神落空、被破、伏藏，这病也能好； | In divining illness, if the Support is strong and moving, then even if the Subject is Void, broken or hidden, the illness can heal; |
| 如遇忌神旺动，即使用神出现不空破者，禄命当尽矣！ | 如果遇到忌神旺相发动，就算用神出现在卦里、不空不破，禄命也该尽了！ | if the Adversary is strong and moving, then even if the Subject appears in the hexagram and is neither Void nor broken, his allotted life is at its end! |

> **打架**:「福神」「鬼杀」指哪一爻：旧注按字面，福神是子孙、鬼杀是官鬼和忌爻，并且定出按哪一爻断痊愈的日子、按哪一天断死期；王洪绪说不可以拿字面害了本意，福神比子孙轻、比原神重，鬼煞的道理在忌神、不在官鬼：原神旺动，用神空破伏藏也能好；忌神旺动，用神好好出现也要命尽。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Which lines the 'fortune spirit' and the 'Pressure killer' are: the old commentary takes them by the letter, the fortune spirit as Output and the Pressure killer as the Pressure line and the Adversary line, and fixes by which line to judge the day of healing and by which day the date of death; Wang Hongxu says one must not let the words hurt the meaning, the fortune spirit being lighter than Output and heavier than the Support and the principle of the Pressure killer lying in the Adversary and not in Pressure: with the Support strong and moving, even a Void, broken or hidden Subject can heal; with the Adversary strong and moving, even a Subject that appears whole is at the end of its life.

> **按**:王洪绪这一条和他在 LY.ILL.HJC.44 里的话是一个意思：鬼煞的道理在忌神，不在官鬼。旧注最后讲「用爻生旺之日断死期」，和常理不太顺（死期为什么取生旺日），书里没有别的解释，译文按字面。「禄命」是命里注定的禄和寿。这一条讲的是应期，即什么时候好、什么时候不行，和总则、生死两个主题也相通。
>
> **Note:** Wang Hongxu's entry says the same as his words in LY.ILL.HJC.44: the principle of the 'Pressure killer' lies in the Adversary and not in the Pressure line. The old commentary's last point, 'judge the date of death by the days on which the Subject line is born or strong', does not sit easily with common sense (why take a day of strength for the date of death), and the book gives no other explanation, so the translation follows the letter. 'Allotted life' is the emolument and span of life fixed by fate. This entry speaks of timing, that is, when the patient recovers and when he fails, and so also touches the general and the life-and-death topics.
