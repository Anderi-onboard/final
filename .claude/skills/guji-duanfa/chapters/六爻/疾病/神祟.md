## LY.ILL.HJC.50 日辰带鬼急为祈祷：日辰带鬼生合世爻，看世爻带的是哪一亲就知道该用什么治（父母经箓符醮、子孙用药、兄弟戒怒节食、财静养、官鬼祈祷）；认哪路神鬼，旧注按日辰地支、王洪绪按生合的六神

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:日辰带着鬼、来生合世爻，就该祈祷。看世爻带的是哪一亲，就知道该用什么办法治：带父母，要靠经、箓、符、醮来保扶；带子孙，要用药调理；带兄弟，要戒暴怒、节制饮食；带财爻，要好好休养；带官鬼，要祈祷鬼神。要知道该求哪一路神鬼，两家的办法不同，见分歧。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Day carries Pressure and generates or combines with the Self line, one should pray. What the Self line carries shows the cure: Resource, scriptures, registers, talismans and rites; Output, medicine; Peer, curbing temper and moderating food; Wealth, good rest; Pressure, prayer to the ghosts and spirits. Which spirit or ghost to seek is found differently by the two commentaries (see the dispute).

> **打架**:怎样认该求哪一路神鬼：旧注用日辰的地支推（子是北斗或北阴，亥是溺死鬼，要察看是河伯、还是划龙船的），再按世爻带的六亲定治法；王洪绪看生合世爻或用爻的那一爻带的六神（青龙、勾陈、朱雀、螣蛇、白虎、玄武各是一路），并且说阳象阳爻是神、阴象阴爻是鬼。
>
> **Where they disagree:** How to tell which spirit or ghost to seek: the old commentary reasons from the branch of the Day (Zi is the Northern Dipper or the North Yin, Hai a drowned ghost, to be looked into as the River Earl or dragon-boat rowing) and fixes the cure by the relation the Self line carries; Wang Hongxu looks at the six spirits carried by the line that generates or combines with the Self or Subject line (the Azure Dragon, Hook Earth, Vermilion Bird, Coiling Snake, White Tiger and Dark Tortoise each a different sort), and says that a yang image on a yang line is a god and a yin image on a yin line a ghost.

> **按**:「箓」是道士画的符箓，「醮」是道士设坛祭神的仪式，「保扶」是保护、扶助。「花幡香愿」「香灯口愿」是向神许过愿、还愿时献的花幡、香灯。「伤司」「五道」是主伤亡、管冥途的神，「玄帝」是玄天上帝，「北阴」是北方的阴神。亥里藏着壬水和甲木，所以旧注说除了河伯（水），还要看有没有划龙船的（木船）。王洪绪说的《鬼神章》是本书后面专讲鬼神的一章。
>
> **Note:** A 'lu' is a register or talisman drawn by a Daoist, a 'jiao' a rite in which a Daoist sets up an altar and offers to the spirits, and 'protect and sustain' is to protect and support. 'A vow of flower banners and incense' and 'a vow of incense and lamps spoken aloud' are vows made to a god and the flower banners, incense and lamps offered in fulfilling them. 'The Office of Injury' and 'the Five Ways' are gods over injury and death and the road to the underworld, the 'Dark Emperor' is the Supreme Emperor of the Dark Heaven, and the 'North Yin' is the yin spirit of the north. Hai holds Ren Water and Jia Wood, so the old commentary says that besides the River Earl (Water) one must see whether there is dragon-boat rowing (a wooden boat). The Chapter on Ghosts and Spirits that Wang Hongxu mentions is the chapter later in the book that treats ghosts and spirits alone.

## LY.ILL.HJC.85 福鬼俱空当不治而自愈、子官皆动宜内补而外修：子孙官鬼都空是吉兆（或俱衰静无冲无并）病自愈不用服药；两爻都动不是药不对病而是神祟作祸，须服汤药又祈祷鬼神才愈，俗谓外修内补

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病，子孙、官鬼两爻都空，是吉兆；或者两爻都衰弱安静、没有冲也没有并，这病自己会好，不用吃药。如果两爻都动，不是药不对病，而是有神祟隔在中间（王洪绪：神祟在作祸），所以没有功效，必须又吃汤药、又祈祷鬼神才会痊愈，俗话叫「外修内补」。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining illness, when the Output and Pressure lines are both Void it is a lucky sign; or if both are weak and still, with neither clash nor joining, the illness heals of itself and no medicine need be taken. If both lines move, it is not that the medicine does not fit the illness but that spirits or a haunting stand between (Wang Hongxu: are working mischief), and so it does no good; he must take decoctions and also pray to the ghosts and spirits to be cured, which the common saying calls 'cultivate without and nourish within'.

## LY.ILL.ZS.23 占鬼神看鬼爻的五行与旺衰：鬼休囚或空破墓绝都不是鬼神作祟；旺相是神、休囚是鬼——金是武神西方神/刀剑亡鬼，木是文神东方神/依草附木之妖或刑杖悬梁之鬼，水是河海北方神/水死之鬼，火是火神南方神雷公电母窖灶神/汤火焚烧之鬼，土是土神中央神山川社稷神/墙倒屋塌土死之鬼

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占鬼神，卦里的鬼要是休囚，或者空、破、墓、绝，都不是鬼神在作祟。鬼爻旺相是神，休囚衰弱是鬼：临金，旺相是武神和西方的神，衰弱是死于刀剑的鬼；临木，旺相是文神和东方的神，衰弱是依草附木的妖、或被杖责悬梁而死的鬼；临水，旺相是河海和北方的神，衰弱是死在池井江湖里的鬼；临火，旺相是火神和南方的神、雷公电母、窖灶的神，衰弱是被汤火烧死的鬼；临土，旺相是土神和中央的神、掌管山川社稷的神，衰弱是墙倒屋塌压死的鬼。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining ghosts and spirits, if the Pressure line in the hexagram is resting or trapped, or Void, broken, in a tomb or extinct, it is not the harm of ghosts or spirits. A strong Pressure line is a god and a weak one a ghost: on Metal, if strong a martial god and a god of the west, if weak a ghost that died by sword or blade; on Wood, if strong a civil god and a god of the east, if weak a goblin clinging to grass and trees or a ghost that died by the rod or by hanging from a beam; on Water, if strong a god of river and sea and of the north, if weak a ghost that drowned in a pond, well, river or lake; on Fire, if strong the god of fire and a god of the south, the Duke of Thunder and Lady Lightning, the gods of the cellar and the stove, if weak a ghost that died by scalding or burning; on Earth, if strong the god of the soil and a god of the centre and a god in charge of mountains, rivers and the altars of the land and grain, if weak a ghost that died buried under a fallen wall or collapsed house.

> **打架**:怎样认是哪一路神鬼：黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.50）按日辰的地支推（子是北斗或北阴，亥是溺死鬼），王洪绪按生合世爻或用爻的那一爻带的六神认；觉子这里按鬼爻自己的五行和旺衰认（旺相是神，休囚是鬼），又说鬼休囚或空破墓绝根本不是鬼神作祟。三家的办法各不相同。
>
> **Where they disagree:** How to tell which sort of spirit or ghost it is: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.50) reasons from the branch of the Day (Zi is the Northern Dipper or the North Yin, Hai a drowned ghost), and Wang Hongxu from the six spirits carried by the line that generates or combines with the Self or Subject line; Juezi here tells by the phase and strength of the Pressure line itself (strong is a god, resting or trapped a ghost), and adds that Pressure that is resting or trapped, Void, broken, in a tomb or extinct is not the harm of ghosts and spirits at all. The three ways all differ.

> **按**:「武神」是关公、岳飞一类的神；「文神」是文昌、孔子一类，书里没有列名单，译文按字面。「刑杖悬梁」是被杖打死、吊死。「雷公电母」是司雷电的神，「窖灶」是地窖和灶，「社稷」是土神和谷神。
>
> **Note:** 'Martial gods' are gods such as Guan Yu and Yue Fei, and 'civil gods' are of the kind of Wenchang and Confucius; the book gives no list, and the translation follows the letter. 'The rod or hanging from a beam' is being beaten to death or hanged. The 'Duke of Thunder and Lady Lightning' are the gods of thunder and lightning, 'cellar and stove' are the underground store and the hearth, and 'the altars of the land and grain' are the gods of soil and grain.

## LY.ILL.ZS.24 不必按五行硬指哪路神：金官指为关公岳公等武神不对，武神有封有未封各地不同；须问病人许愿未酬或污秽了哪座庙，把所疑的神占一卦，鬼爻旺或动就是此神，不上卦再占别处；得罪正神用香花纸马祭，招惹邪祟用浆纸钱夜静时送（金鬼向西、水鬼向北）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:古时候按五行分是哪路神，比如金官就说是天将、关公、金刚、伍公、岳公这些武神，觉子认为不对：武神有朝廷敕封的、有没有封的，各地风俗又不同，书里没记的更多，不可能一座庙一座庙地祭。该问病人是不是在哪座武神庙里许了愿没有还、或者在哪座庙里弄脏糟蹋了；凡是心里怀疑哪位神，就指着那位神占一卦，鬼爻旺或发动就是这位神，不上卦再占别处的神。鬼临木火水土的，都这样办。得罪了正神的，用香花纸马祭；招惹了邪祟的鬼，用浆水和纸钱，夜深人静时送走，金鬼向西送，水鬼向北送。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In ancient times the gods were told apart by the five phases, for example a Metal Pressure was said to be a heavenly general, Guan Gong, a Vajra, Wu Gong, Yue Gong and the other martial gods; Juezi thinks this wrong: martial gods include those enfeoffed by imperial decree and those not, local customs differ, and those not recorded in books are still more, so one cannot sacrifice at temple after temple. One should ask the patient whether he vowed something in the temple of some martial god and has not paid it, or defiled and abused some temple; whatever god he suspects in his heart, point to that god and divine, and if the Pressure line is strong or moving it is that god, and if it is not in the hexagram divine the god of some other place. Pressure on Wood, Fire, Water or Earth is treated in the same way. If he has offended an orthodox god, sacrifice with incense, flowers and paper horses; if he has provoked an evil haunting ghost, use rice-water and paper money and send it off in the stillness of the night, a Metal ghost to the west and a Water ghost to the north.

## LY.ILL.ZS.25 祭送不是万灵药：时灾瘟癖照法祭送屡验，疯痨气蛊是自己失调、与鬼神无关；野鹤见滇黔蜀粤病不服药专信鬼神、杀牲救命反害命；李我平驳《易冒》《易林补遗》指定关岳、说不祭降灾是邪神，并说此书疾病章言鬼神是从俗

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:祭送鬼神，对时行灾病、瘟疫、癖积一类的病有用（野鹤多次验过）；疯、痨、气、蛊是自己没有调养好、酒色伤了身，和鬼神没有关系，祭送没有益处，该做的是节制饮食、远离色欲、平息怒气、养护精神。有病不吃药、专靠杀牲祭神的，反而害了性命。李我平批评有的书指定哪位神（关帝、岳王、关圣），又说祭了降福、不祭降灾，那是作威作福的邪神。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Sacrificing and sending off the ghosts and spirits is of use for seasonal calamities, epidemics and hard lumps (the Old Man of Yehe verified it many times); madness, consumption, qi disorder and drum-belly come from failing to look after oneself and from wine and lust damaging the body, have nothing to do with ghosts and spirits, and gain nothing from sacrifice, what is to be done being to moderate food, keep away from lust, calm anger and nourish the spirit. Those who take no medicine when ill and rely wholly on slaughtering animals to the gods only harm life. Li Woping criticises books that name a particular god (Emperor Guan, King Yue, the Sage Guan) and say that those who sacrifice are blessed and those who do not are sent calamity, which he calls evil gods who lord it and dispense favour.
