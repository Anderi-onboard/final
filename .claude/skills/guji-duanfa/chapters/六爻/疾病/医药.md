## LY.ILL.HJC.32 先看子孙：能克制官鬼，古人叫解神、又名福德，占病里就是医药；卦中无它则鬼无制伏、服药不效祷神不灵；要生旺有气，日辰动爻生扶则吉、冲坏则凶；占父母丈夫病不宜它发动

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:断病先看子孙爻。子孙能克制官鬼这个凶煞，古人叫它解神，又叫福德，占病时它又是医药。卦里没有子孙，鬼就没有东西制伏，吃药没有效果，祷告求神也不灵。子孙要得令有力，不要衰弱、被困、死、绝；日辰、动爻来生它、扶它就吉，来冲它、破坏它就凶。替父母或丈夫占病，子孙不宜发动：动了会伤克夫星，又克伤父母爻的原神。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In judging illness look first at Output. Output can control and restrain the Pressure killer; the ancients called it the relieving spirit and also Output-virtue, and in divining illness it is also the medicine. If there is no Output in the hexagram, Pressure has nothing to restrain it: medicine has no effect and prayer to the spirits is not answered. Output should be in season and have force, not resting, trapped, dead or extinct; if the Day or a moving line generates and supports it, it is lucky, and if they clash and spoil it, unlucky. In divining the illness of a parent or a husband, Output should not move: moving, it harms and controls the husband star and also harms the Support of the Resource line.

> **按**:「解神」是能解除灾厄的神，子孙克官鬼，所以叫解神；「福德」是子孙的另一个名字。「生扶拱合」是帮用神的四种办法：生是五行相生，扶和拱是同一五行的两个地支互相帮衬（《卜筮正宗》在别处举的例子：亥扶子、子拱亥），合是二合、三合、六合。旧注的「官杀」「鬼杀」，王洪绪写作「鬼煞」，是同一个意思。王洪绪比旧注多说了一条：替父母或丈夫占病，子孙不宜发动。丈夫用官鬼，父母的原神也是官鬼（官鬼生父母），所以子孙一动就克到病人依靠的那一爻；旧注没有这个限定。「财鬼无制」，旧注只说「鬼无制伏」，王洪绪多了一个财。
>
> **Note:** The 'relieving spirit' is the spirit that can relieve disaster; Output controls Pressure, hence the name. 'Output-virtue' (fude) is another name for Output. 'Generating, supporting, embracing and combining' are four ways of helping the Subject: generating is the generation of the five phases; supporting and embracing are two branches of the same phase helping each other (the Bushi Zhengzong gives elsewhere the examples Hai supports Zi and Zi embraces Hai); combining is the two-combine, the three-combine and the six-combine. The old commentary's 'guan sha' and 'gui sha' are written 'gui sha' by Wang Hongxu, and mean the same. Wang Hongxu adds one thing to the old commentary: in divining the illness of a parent or a husband, Output should not move. A husband takes Pressure, and the Support of the Resource line is also Pressure (Pressure generates Resource), so a moving Output controls the very line the patient depends on; the old commentary has no such limit. Of 'Wealth and Pressure unrestrained' the old commentary says only 'Pressure has nothing to restrain it', and Wang Hongxu adds Wealth.

## LY.ILL.HJC.35 身临福德势虽险而堪医：「身」是一卦之体（旧注说是用爻，王洪绪说是月卦身），子孙临之决然无虞，纵然凶险用药必愈

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:一卦的「身」是整个卦的主体：身上坐着子孙（福德），一定没有忧患，病势再凶险也有救，用药医治一定痊愈。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The 'body' is the substance of the whole hexagram: if Output (Output-virtue) sits on it, there is surely nothing to fear; even if the illness looks dangerous it can be saved, and treated with medicine it will surely be cured.

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Output appears in the hexagram but is Void, and the fifth line moves and changes out an Output that is not Void, there is no need to seek a famous doctor and take medicine: a rustic doctor met on the road can cure him instead. If Output does not appear and none of the six lines changes it out, but the Day carries Output and generates or combines with it, a doctor will come to treat him unexpectedly.

**《黄金策·医药》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 7085 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Doctors and medicine", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7085*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 五兴化福，可用路遇医人。 | 第五爻发动、变出子孙，可以用路上遇到的医生。 | The fifth line rising and changing into Output: a doctor met on the road may be used. |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 7086 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 7086*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 子孙出现逢空，而五爻变出子孙，却不空者，不须选求名医服药，不如过路草医，反能治之。 | 子孙出现却落空，而第五爻变出子孙、不落空的，不用选求名医服药，不如路过的乡野医生，反而能治好。 | If Output appears but is Void, while the fifth line changes out Output that is not Void, there is no need to seek a famous doctor and take medicine: a rustic doctor who passes by can cure him instead. |
| 若子孙不出现，六爻不化出，而日辰带子孙生合者，不意中自有医来治也。 | 如果子孙不出现，六爻也没有变出子孙，而日辰带着子孙来生合，想不到的时候自然有医生来治。 | If Output does not appear and none of the six lines changes it out, but the Day carries Output and generates or combines with it, a doctor will come to treat him unexpectedly. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2480 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2480*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 如卦中第五爻变出子孙，不须选医服药，不如路遇草医能治。 | 如果卦里第五爻变出子孙，不用选医生服药，不如路上遇到的乡野医生能治好。 | If the fifth line of the hexagram changes out Output, there is no need to choose a doctor and take medicine; a rustic doctor met on the road can cure him. |

> **Note:** The old commentary's condition is finer than Wang Hongxu's: Output appears and is Void, and the Output changed out at the fifth line is not Void. Wang Hongxu says only that the fifth line changes out Output. A 'rustic doctor' is a country doctor of no repute.

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻、官鬼都发动，势头已经凶险，子孙又落空，吃药没有益处，就算有好医生也一定没有功效，不如不吃，病反而渐渐好起来。

## LY.ILL.HJC.92 占医药须诚心默祷，问用何人的药有效无效，不必说明姓氏；卜家据此章而断，自无荐医之弊（王洪绪）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:凡是占医药，要诚心默默祷告，问用哪个人的药有效还是无效，不必说出医生的姓氏。卜家依照这一章来断，自然没有替医生推荐的毛病，心诚没有不感通的，卦没有不灵验的，这不是彼此都安心吗？

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whoever divines about medicine must pray silently and sincerely, asking whether the medicine of a certain person will or will not be effective, and need not state the doctor's surname. A diviner who judges by this chapter will have no abuse of touting doctors; sincerity never fails to reach, and the hexagram never fails to prove true; is that not peace of mind for both sides?

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2483 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2483*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡占医药者，须诚心默祷，用何人药有效无效，不必说明姓氏。 | 凡是占医药的，要诚心默默祷告，问用哪个人的药有效还是无效，不必说出他的姓氏。 | Whoever divines about medicine must pray silently and sincerely, asking whether the medicine of a certain person will or will not be effective, and need not state his surname. |
| 卜家据此章而断，自无荐医之弊，则诚无不格，卦无不验矣，岂非彼此安心乎？ | 卜家依照这一章来断，自然没有替医生推荐的毛病，那么诚心没有不感通的，卦没有不灵验的，这不是彼此都安心吗？ | A diviner who judges by this chapter will have no abuse of touting doctors; sincerity then never fails to reach, and the hexagram never fails to prove true; is that not peace of mind for both sides? |

> **按**:这一段是王洪绪自己加在医药章末尾的话，卜筮大全没有对应的文字。「荐医之弊」是占卜的人借断卦替某位医生说好话、推荐他的毛病。
>
> **Note:** This passage is Wang Hongxu's own addition at the end of the chapter on medicine, and the Bushi Daquan has no matching text. The 'abuse of touting doctors' is the fault of a diviner who uses his judgment of the hexagram to speak well of a certain doctor and recommend him.

相关:LY.ILL.HJC.58
