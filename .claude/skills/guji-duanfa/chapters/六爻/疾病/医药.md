## LY.ILL.HJC.32 先看子孙：能克制官鬼，古人叫解神、又名福德，占病里就是医药；卦中无它则鬼无制伏、服药不效祷神不灵；要生旺有气，日辰动爻生扶则吉、冲坏则凶；占父母丈夫病不宜它发动

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:断病先看子孙爻。子孙能克制官鬼这个凶煞，古人叫它解神，又叫福德，占病时它又是医药。卦里没有子孙，鬼就没有东西制伏，吃药没有效果，祷告求神也不灵。子孙要得令有力，不要衰弱、被困、死、绝；日辰、动爻来生它、扶它就吉，来冲它、破坏它就凶。替父母或丈夫占病，子孙不宜发动：动了会伤克夫星，又克伤父母爻的原神。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In judging illness look first at Output. Output can control and restrain the Pressure killer; the ancients called it the relieving spirit and also Output-virtue, and in divining illness it is also the medicine. If there is no Output in the hexagram, Pressure has nothing to restrain it: medicine has no effect and prayer to the spirits is not answered. Output should be in season and have force, not resting, trapped, dead or extinct; if the Day or a moving line generates and supports it, it is lucky, and if they clash and spoil it, unlucky. In divining the illness of a parent or a husband, Output should not move: moving, it harms and controls the husband star and also harms the Support of the Resource line.

> **按**:「解神」是能解除灾厄的神，子孙克官鬼，所以叫解神；「福德」是子孙的另一个名字。「生扶拱合」是帮用神的四种办法：生是五行相生，扶和拱是同一五行的两个地支互相帮衬（《卜筮正宗》在别处举的例子：亥扶子、子拱亥），合是二合、三合、六合。旧注的「官杀」「鬼杀」，王洪绪写作「鬼煞」，是同一个意思。王洪绪比旧注多说了一条：替父母或丈夫占病，子孙不宜发动。丈夫用官鬼，父母的原神也是官鬼（官鬼生父母），所以子孙一动就克到病人依靠的那一爻；旧注没有这个限定。「财鬼无制」，旧注只说「鬼无制伏」，王洪绪多了一个财。
>
> **Note:** The 'relieving spirit' is the spirit that can relieve disaster; Output controls Pressure, hence the name. 'Output-virtue' (fude) is another name for Output. 'Generating, supporting, embracing and combining' are four ways of helping the Subject: generating is the generation of the five phases; supporting and embracing are two branches of the same phase helping each other (the Bushi Zhengzong gives elsewhere the examples Hai supports Zi and Zi embraces Hai); combining is the two-combine, the three-combine and the six-combine. The old commentary's 'guan sha' and 'gui sha' are written 'gui sha' by Wang Hongxu, and mean the same. Wang Hongxu adds one thing to the old commentary: in divining the illness of a parent or a husband, Output should not move. A husband takes Pressure, and the Support of the Resource line is also Pressure (Pressure generates Resource), so a moving Output controls the very line the patient depends on; the old commentary has no such limit. Of 'Wealth and Pressure unrestrained' the old commentary says only 'Pressure has nothing to restrain it', and Wang Hongxu adds Wealth.

## LY.ILL.HJC.35 身临福德势虽险而堪医：「身」是一卦之体（旧注说是用爻，王洪绪说是月卦身），子孙临之决然无虞，纵然凶险用药必愈

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:一卦的「身」是整个卦的主体：身上坐着子孙（福德），一定没有忧患，病势再凶险也有救，用药医治一定痊愈。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The 'body' is the substance of the whole hexagram: if Output (Output-virtue) sits on it, there is surely nothing to fear; even if the illness looks dangerous it can be saved, and treated with medicine it will surely be cured.

## LY.ILL.ZS.26 问卜求神、延医服药有没有用：术不精则不效；病要分有根无根，有根者可救、无根者不能（像移花接木，上盖下浇自能渐活，无根之木浇盖无益）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问卜求神、请医吃药是有道理的：不灵验有两个原因，一是术不精，精了没有不灵验的；二是病要分有根无根，有根的可以救，无根的救不了，就像移栽花木，有根的上面遮盖、下面浇灌，自然慢慢活过来，无根的树、无蒂的花，浇了盖了也没有用。人不懂这个，一次不灵验就不信神和药，是冤枉了它。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** There is reason in consulting the oracle and praying to the spirits, calling a doctor and taking medicine: when it fails there are two causes, one that the art is not refined, for if refined nothing fails, and the other that an illness must be told as rooted or rootless, the rooted being savable and the rootless not, as with moving a flowering plant, where one that has roots is covered above and watered below and revives gradually, while a tree without root or a flower without stem gains nothing from watering and covering. People who do not understand this lose faith in the spirits and medicine at the first failure, and wrong them.

## LY.ILL.ZS.27 鬼是忧神、子孙是喜悦解忧之神：鬼持世或动，得子孙动而制之，此医可请手到成功；子孙临应爻是明医，因为子孙克去的是忧不是邪魔（例：三十余人泪眼相对，子水兄爻旬空，半夜子时退灾）

## LY.ILL.ZS.31 医克用爻近病即愈：自占病应爻克世、应爻克用神是克制我的病；不宜久病和体弱的人，克病也伤身；应爻临兄鬼而克用神或克世爻，不拘久近必遭其害

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:医生克用爻，近病就好。自己占病，应爻克世爻，是克制我的病；应爻克用神，也这样断。但是不适宜久病和体弱的人，因为它不光克掉了病，身体也受到伤害。应爻临着兄弟、官鬼而去克用神，以及克世爻的，不管久病近病，一定受它的害。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The doctor controlling the Subject line: a recent illness heals at once. In divining one's own illness, if the Other line controls the Self line, it restrains my illness; if the Other line controls the Subject, judge the same. It is unsuitable only for one long ill or of weak body, for it controls not only the illness but harms the body too. If the Other line sits on Peer or Pressure and controls the Subject, or controls the Self line, whether the illness is long or recent, harm from it is certain.

## LY.ILL.ZS.33 子不代父占药、妻不代夫卜医：子占父宜父爻旺，占药占医又以子孙为用神，父旺伤子，一爻不能两用；妻占夫宜官鬼旺，占医药又以子孙为用神，子旺伤夫，所以不可代占医药

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:儿子不替父亲占药，妻子不替丈夫卜医。儿子占父亲的病，宜父爻旺相；可是占药、占医，又要拿子孙当用神，父爻旺就会伤子孙，一个爻不能同时当两样用。妻子占丈夫的病，宜官鬼旺相；占医占药，也拿子孙当用神，子孙旺又会伤丈夫，所以不可以替他占医药。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A son does not divine medicine for his father, nor a wife a doctor for her husband. A son divining his father's illness wants the Resource line strong; but in divining medicine or a doctor Output is taken as the Subject, and a strong Resource line harms Output, and one line cannot serve two uses. A wife divining her husband's illness wants the Pressure line strong; in divining a doctor or medicine Output is also taken as the Subject, and a strong Output harms the husband, so one may not divine medicine or a doctor on his behalf.

## LY.ILL.ZS.34 延医于子孙之方、治病以应爻为定：子孙旺则往子孙所在方位请（午火请南方医）；子孙休囚空破则看应爻旺相生合用神，往应爻之方请；有一爻独发生用神，往此方请，姓名按五行——水点水、火象火、木草头木旁、土有土、金有金

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:请医生要往子孙所在的方位去请，治病则以应爻来定。卦里子孙爻旺相的，从子孙所在的方位请医生，比如午火子孙，旺相或发动，就请南方的医生。子孙休囚、空、破的，再看应爻旺不旺，如果应爻旺相、又生合用神，就往应爻所在的方位请，比如应爻临申金，名医一定在西南。卦里有一个爻单独发动、而且生用神的，就从这个爻的方位请，并用五行来定医生的姓名：水爻动，姓名带三点水；火爻动，姓名像火；木爻动，带草字头、木字旁；土爻动，姓名里有土；金爻动，姓名里有金。比如占妻子的病，妻爻属水，得申金发动来生它，就请姓钱的医生。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Call the doctor from the direction of Output; for treatment, the Other line decides. If the Output line in the hexagram is strong, call the doctor from the direction of Output; for example, with Wu Fire Output, strong or moving, call a doctor from the south. If Output is resting, trapped, Void or broken, look at whether the Other line is strong; if the Other line is strong and generates or combines with the Subject, call from the direction of the Other line; for example, with the Other line on Shen Metal, a famous doctor is surely in the south-west. If one line in the hexagram moves alone and generates the Subject, call from the direction of that line, and fix the doctor's name by the five phases: a Water line moving, a name with the water radical; Fire, a name resembling fire; Wood, a name with the grass or wood radical; Earth, a name with earth in it; Metal, a name with metal in it. ## LY.ILL.ZS.36 医生自占往治与否：世爻旺相、子孙财爻持世、世生应爻、世动化进、化回头生，日月动爻临财星子孙生世，皆主手到病除；近病应空或化空、卦逢六冲等宜速往，久病应旺相或生世宜往，此外勿往治

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:医生自己占要不要去治，和替别人占卜差不多：世爻是自己，应爻是病人。世爻旺相，子孙或财爻持世，世爻生应爻，世动变进神、变回头生，日月和动爻临着财星或子孙来生世爻，都主手到病除。近病：应爻值旬空，或动了变空、变回头生、变旺、变退，或日月、动爻生应爻，以及卦逢六冲、卦变六冲的，都该赶快去救，别让别人先到而成了功。久病：应爻旺相，或遇到日月、动爻来生，或动了变生、变旺，应爻临子孙、临财爻，动而生世爻的，虽然是久病，我一定能除根。除了这些，都不要去治，白白损了名声。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A doctor divining for himself whether to go and treat is much like divining for another: the Self line is oneself and the Other line is the patient. If the Self line is strong, Output or Wealth holds the Self line, the Self line generates the Other line, the Self line moves and changes into an Advancing spirit or into a returning generation, or the Day, Month or a moving line sits on the Wealth star or Output and generates the Self line, all mean that the hand arrives and the illness is gone. For a recent illness: if the Other line falls into Void, or moves and changes into Void, a returning generation, strength or retreat, or the Day, Month or a moving line generates the Other line, or the hexagram is Six-Clash or changes into Six-Clash, one should hurry to the rescue and not let others arrive first and succeed. For a long illness: if the Other line is strong, or is generated by the Day, Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into generation or strength, or sits on Output or Wealth, or moves and generates the Self line, then though the illness is long I shall surely remove the root. Apart from these, do not go to treat, for it only harms one's reputation.

## LY.ILL.ZS.37 占药能不能服以子孙为用神：医生治危症想用某药、行舟远行无处寻医得一奇方、半夜来不及请医，都要占；子孙持世或发动服之立可解忧；子孙不拘五行，旺或动即喜悦之神，不可因金临子孙用针、火属子孙用炙（例：热极用附子肉桂、人参、牛黄）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占药能不能服，拿子孙当用神。医生治垂危的病、想用某味药，可以起死回生，又怕一有闪失；泛海行船、长途旅行没有地方找医生、有人传来奇方；半夜来不及请医、手边的古方或家里的药散确实对这个病却不敢擅用，都要占。只要子孙持世或发动，服了立刻可以解忧，必定见效。子孙不管是金木水火土哪一行，旺相或发动就是喜悦的神；不可以又因为金临子孙就用针、火属子孙就用灸。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To divine whether a medicine may be taken, take Output as the Subject. It should be divined when a doctor treating a dying patient wants a certain medicine that may bring him back from death, and fears one failure; when one is crossing the sea or on a long journey with nowhere to find a doctor and someone passes on a rare prescription; when in the depth of night there is no time to call a doctor and the old prescriptions or powders kept at home truly fit the illness but one does not dare use them on one's own authority. If Output holds the Self line or moves, taking it at once relieves the worry, and it will surely work. Whatever phase Output is, metal, wood, water, fire or earth, so long as it is strong or moving it is the spirit of joy; one must not take Metal on Output to mean the needle, or Fire as Output to mean moxibustion.

## LY.ILL.ZS.38 子孙持世也要不受克：前面各篇都说子孙持世病即愈，野鹤补一条——必要子孙不受克才行（例：杨梅疮占医人，子孙持世应为医人，但初爻巳火动克申金，巳日添灾）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:前面几篇都说子孙持世，病就马上好，但一定要子孙不受克才行，后来的人不可以不知道。比如有人生了杨梅疮，占这位医生好不好：虽然是子孙持世、应爻是医生，但初爻巳火发动克申金，又有卯木生火，世爻虽旺，巳日一定添灾；他不听，还是请了这位医生，卯日、辰日服药还平安，巳日忽然病症变了，全身疼痛难以忍受。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The earlier passages all say that with Output holding the Self line the illness heals at once, but it is so only when Output is not controlled, which later people must know. For example, a man with syphilitic sores divined whether a certain doctor was a good one: though Output held the Self line and the Other line was the doctor, the first line, Si Fire, moved and controlled Shen Metal, with Mao Wood also generating Fire, so that though the Self line was strong there would be added trouble on the Si day; he did not listen and called the doctor, and on the Mao and Chen days the medicine kept him safe, but on the Si day the symptoms suddenly changed and pain all over the body was unbearable.

## LY.ILL.ZS.40 子孙不是针灸之药，火鬼忌热水鬼忌寒是拘泥：子孙旺动则不药自愈，前贤以金生子孙用针、火持子孙用炙是错；诸书说火鬼旺宜大寒药、水鬼旺宜大热药，李我平举土用神、妻占夫二例驳之，说胶柱决之坑陷人命

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙旺相又发动的，不吃药就好，并不是教人拿这个子孙当治病的药；前辈贤人常常看到子孙旺动，占病病好、占医医良，就把子孙爻当成治病的药，金生子孙就断用针，火持子孙就断用灸，不知道子孙旺相病自己就好了，哪里用得着针灸，子孙落空被破，针灸又有什么益处。各种书又说火鬼忌热、水鬼忌寒，水鬼在生旺之地要用大热的药，火鬼旺相宜用大寒的药去攻；李我平不知道他们从哪里看出来的：假使用神属土、儿子占父亲的病，父爻临土，火鬼发动是生用爻的元神，怎么能用大寒的药去治元神？妻子占丈夫的病，鬼是用神，丈夫临水鬼，又怎么能用大热的药去攻用神？死板地照大寒大热去断，坑害了不知多少性命。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Where Output is strong and moving, one recovers without medicine, and this does not teach us to take that Output as the medicine that treats the illness; the worthies of old often saw Output strong and moving, found that in divining illness the illness healed and in divining a doctor the doctor was good, and so took the Output line as the medicine, judging the needle where Metal generates Output and moxibustion where Fire holds Output, without realising that if Output is strong the illness heals of itself, so what need is there of needle and moxa, and if Output is Void or broken what good would they do. The books also say that a Fire Pressure fears heat and a Water Pressure fears cold, that a Water Pressure in a place of birth or strength must be treated with very hot medicine and a strong Fire Pressure attacked with very cold; Li Woping does not know where they saw this: suppose the Subject is Earth, as when a son divines his father's illness and the Resource line sits on Earth, a moving Fire Pressure is the Support that generates the Subject line, and how could one treat the Support with very cold medicine? When a wife divines her husband's illness, Pressure is the Subject, and if the husband sits on a Water Pressure, how could one attack the Subject with very hot medicine? To judge rigidly by very cold and very hot has sunk who knows how many lives in the pit.

> **打架**:用药看什么：黄金策旧注、王洪绪（LY.ILL.HJC.76、HJC.81、HJC.84）按官鬼的五行定寒热（火鬼旺用大寒、水鬼旺用大热），按子孙的五行定针灸（子孙属金用刀针、属火用灸）；李我平、野鹤说这是拘泥：子孙旺动本来就不药而愈，不是针灸之药；火鬼可能是用神的元神，不能拿大寒药去攻；死板照大寒大热去断，是坑人性命。
>
> **Where they disagree:** What to look at in fixing the medicine: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu (LY.ILL.HJC.76, HJC.81, HJC.84) fix cold or hot by the phase of the Pressure line (a strong Fire Pressure very cold, a strong Water Pressure very hot) and needle or moxa by the phase of Output (Metal Output knife and needle, Fire Output moxa); Li Woping and the Old Man call this rigid: Output strong and moving means recovery without medicine and is not the medicine of needle and moxa, a Fire Pressure may be the Support of the Subject and cannot be attacked with very cold medicine, and to judge rigidly by very cold and very hot sinks lives.

> **按**:这是整个疾病域里两派分歧最尖锐的一条。李我平末尾说「此书一出，而寒热之药不敢轻用」，是说《增删卜易》出来以后，占卜的人不该再拿五行定寒热。「炙」「灸」在书里写法不一，都指艾灸。「元神」的用法见 LY.ILL.ZS.08。
>
> **Note:** This is the sharpest split between the two schools in the whole illness domain. Li Woping's closing 'once this book is out, no one will dare to use cold or hot medicines lightly' means that after the Zengshan Buyi diviners should no longer fix cold and hot by the five phases. 'Zhi' and 'jiu' are written differently in the book and both mean moxibustion. For the use of the 'Support' see LY.ILL.ZS.08. The 'Qian note' at the end of the chapter is a modern addition and is not quoted here.
