## LY.ILL.HJC.02 火属心经、水归肾部、金肺木肝、土侵脾胃：鬼爻的五行定哪一脏受病；衰轻旺重，动则煎迫

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼爻的五行，定哪一脏受病：火是心经（发热、咽干、口燥），水是肾（怕冷、盗汗、遗精白浊），金是肺（咳嗽、虚怯、气喘），木是肝（感冒风寒、四肢不和），土是脾胃（虚黄、浮肿、瘟疫时气）。鬼爻衰弱病轻、旺相病重；静则安卧，动则煎熬躁急。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The phase of the Pressure line fixes which organ is ill: Fire is the Heart channel (fever, dry throat, dry mouth), Water the Kidney (aversion to cold, night sweats, seminal emission and cloudy urine), Metal the Lung (cough, weak timidity, panting), Wood the Liver (catching wind-cold, limbs out of harmony), Earth the Spleen and Stomach (sallow weakness, swelling, epidemic and seasonal disease). A weak Pressure line means a light illness and a strong one a heavy illness; still, the patient lies quiet, and moving, he is tormented and restless.

## LY.ILL.HJC.03 鬼在坎宫中满血虚兼湿毒，在离卦内虚眼赤及尿黄

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼爻在坎宫，病是中满、血虚，兼有湿毒；在离卦，病是内虚、眼红、尿黄。旧注分得更细：坎宫属水，火鬼主血虚、水鬼主湿毒；离宫属火，火鬼主眼红、水鬼主尿黄。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With the Pressure line in the Kan palace, the illness is fullness in the middle and blood deficiency, together with damp toxin; in the Li hexagram it is inner deficiency, red eyes and yellow urine. The old commentary divides it more finely: the Kan palace belongs to Water, where a Fire Pressure means blood deficiency and a Water Pressure damp toxin; the Li palace belongs to Fire, where a Fire Pressure means red eyes and a Water Pressure yellow urine.

**《黄金策·疾病》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6770 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6770*

## LY.ILL.HJC.04 坤腹乾头、兑喉风咳嗽、艮足震手、巽瘫痪肠风：鬼在八宫的病位；以鬼在坤宫为例，五行之鬼各主一病

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼爻落在哪一宫，病就在哪个部位：坤是腹、乾是头，兑是喉风咳嗽，艮和震是手足，巽是瘫痪、肠风。以鬼在坤宫为例：火鬼腹痈，水鬼腹痛，动化财爻或财化水鬼是泻痢，土鬼是癖块食积或沙胀蛊症，木鬼是绞肠痧痛或大肠有病，金鬼是胁肋痛（在上胸痛、在下腰痛）。别的卦照此推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Where the Pressure line falls in the palaces fixes where the illness is: Kun is the belly and Qian the head; Dui is throat wind and cough; Gen and Zhen are the hand and foot; Xun is paralysis and intestinal wind. Taking Pressure in the Kun palace as the example: a Fire Pressure is an abdominal abscess, a Water Pressure pain in the belly; moving and changing into a Wealth line, or Wealth changing into a Water Pressure, is diarrhea and dysentery; an Earth Pressure is abdominal masses and food accumulation, or sha bloating and gu-swelling; a Wood Pressure is twisting colic or trouble in the large intestine; a Metal Pressure is pain in the flanks (chest if high, loins if low). The other palaces follow by analogy.

## LY.ILL.HJC.05 六神临鬼各主一症：螣蛇心惊、青龙酒色过度、勾陈肿胀、朱雀言语颠狂、白虎损伤血崩血晕、玄武忧郁阴虚

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼爻临着哪个六神，就是哪一种病：螣蛇鬼坐卧不安、心神不定；青龙鬼酒色过度、瘦弱无力；勾陈鬼胸腹胀满、脾胃不和；朱雀鬼狂言乱语、身热面赤；白虎鬼跌打损伤，女人则血崩血晕、产后各症（白虎是血神）；玄武鬼色欲过度、忧闷在心，在本宫主阴虚。最后一句叫人变通着断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Which of the six spirits the Pressure line sits on fixes the kind of illness: Coiling Snake Pressure, unable to sit or lie at ease and the mind unsettled; Azure Dragon Pressure, excess of wine and lust, thin and without strength; Hook Earth Pressure, fullness and swelling, Spleen and Stomach out of harmony; Vermilion Bird Pressure, wild talk, hot body and red face; White Tiger Pressure, falls and blows and injury, and in a woman flooding and blood-faintness and the ailments after childbirth (the White Tiger is the spirit of blood); Dark Tortoise Pressure, excess of lust and gloom pent in the heart, and in the home palace yin deficiency. The last sentence tells one to judge flexibly.

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:有人问病症发作是不是真的，鬼爻实就是真的，鬼爻虚就不是。有人占担心会不会成病，遇到随墓、助伤，病就已经成了；用神如果坏了，这个病怎么能治好呢？占在外面养病的，遇到随墓、助伤，世爻受损、鬼动，不行；在这里避暑的，也是这个道理。注：官鬼空、破、散、绝叫做虚，旺相、有生扶叫做实；用神空、破、绝、散就会成病，旺相有生扶就不会成病。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If someone asks whether the outbreak of symptoms is real: when the ghost is full it is real, when the ghost is empty it is not. If someone divines whether a worry will become an illness, following into the tomb and helping to harm mean the illness has already formed; if the Subject is spoiled, how can the illness be cured? For one who divines convalescing away from home, following into the tomb, helping to harm, the Self broken and the ghost moving will not do; for one escaping the summer heat here, it is the same.
