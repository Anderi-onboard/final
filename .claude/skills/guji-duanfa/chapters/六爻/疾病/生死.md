## LY.ILL.BSZZ.36 何知章·病要死：卜筮正宗答用神无救又入墓；卜筮大全答身命世鬼入墓

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:怎么知道这家有人病得要死：卜筮正宗说是用神没有救护又入了墓；卜筮大全说是身、命、世、鬼入了墓，照这样推断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** How to know that someone in a household is ill unto death: the Bushi Zhengzong says the Subject has no rescue and enters a tomb; the Bushi Daquan says the Body, the destiny, the Self and the ghost enter a tomb, and one judges by that.

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 何知人家病要死？ | 怎么知道这家有人病得要死？ | How can one know that someone in a household is ill unto death? |
| 用神无救又入墓； | 用神没有救护，又入了墓； | the Subject has no rescue and enters a tomb; |

**《卜筮正宗·赋文三篇·何知章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 9845 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Three rhapsodies: the "how to know" rhyme", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 9845*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 何知人家病要死？ | 怎么知道这家有人病得要死？ | How can one know that someone in a household is ill unto death? |
| 身命世鬼入墓推。 | 身、命、世、鬼入了墓，就这样推断。 | If the Body, the destiny, the Self and the ghost enter a tomb, judge it so. |

> **打架**:凭什么断病要死：卜筮正宗答「用神无救又入墓」，只看用神一爻；卜筮大全答「身命世鬼入墓推」，看身、命、世、鬼四样入墓。两本不一样，书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** What decides that the illness is unto death: the Bushi Zhengzong answers 'the Subject has no rescue and enters a tomb', looking only at the Subject line; the Bushi Daquan answers 'the Body, the destiny, the Self and the ghost enter a tomb', looking at four things entering a tomb. The two texts differ and the books do not decide.

> **按**:「何知章」一问一答，一共问了三四十件事（父母子孙的病、有没有子孙、讼事、财富、田地、屋宇、坟墓、六畜、口舌、盗贼……），都是一家的事；这里只收与病、死、鬼怪有关的。卜筮大全里它是「十六、何知章」，卜筮正宗里它在《赋文三篇》中；两本字句略有出入。「身命世鬼」的「身」按其他章的说法是卦身，「命」在这里没有再解释，按字面译作命。用神入墓的说法在增删卜易里有更多的讨论（LY.ILL.ZS.07：动墓绝、化墓绝须逢生旺）。
>
> **Note:** The 'how to know' rhyme asks and answers some thirty or forty things (illnesses of parents and children, whether there are children, lawsuits, wealth, land, houses, graves, livestock, quarrels, thieves and more), all affairs of a household; only those to do with illness, death and ghostly happenings are taken here. In the Bushi Daquan it is chapter 16, 'the how-to-know rhyme'; in the Bushi Zhengzong it is among the three rhapsodies; the two texts differ a little in wording. The 'Body' of 'Body, destiny, Self and ghost' is, by the other chapters' usage, the hexagram Body, and 'destiny' is not explained here and is rendered literally. The Subject entering a tomb is discussed at more length in the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.07: moving into or changing into a tomb or extinction needs generation and strength).

## LY.ILL.EL.11 用爻遇旬空：暴病逢空可救、久病逢空必死；虽空分衰旺——空而旺相久病无妨、空而休囚遇日冲亦不死；立时空又值旬空无不倾亡；不空而月破必伤身

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:再说用爻上卦正好遇旬空，要看病是近病还是久病：暴病遇空可救，久病遇空必死。虽然遇空亡，还要分衰旺：空的用爻如果旺相，纵然是久病也没有妨碍；空的用爻如果休囚，只要遇上日冲也不会死。倘若是立时空又遇上旬空，不论近病久病，没有不死的。用爻虽然不落空，如果临月破，必定伤身。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Again, when the Subject line is in the hexagram and falls exactly on Void, look at whether the illness is recent or long: a sudden illness meeting Void can be saved, a long illness meeting Void surely dies. Though it meets Void, one still divides strong from weak: if the Void line is strong, even a long illness is no hindrance; if the Void line is resting or trapped, meeting a Day clash it still does not die. If it is Void at the moment and also falls into the ten-day Void, then whether the illness is recent or long, none escapes collapse. Though the Subject line is not Void, if it sits in Month-break it will surely harm the body.

**《易林补遗》诀下的解说** — 《易林补遗》第 1285 行 · *Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse; Yilin Buyi, line 1285*

> **打架**:用神落空要不要分近病久病：易林补遗分：暴病逢空可救、久病逢空必死，而且空了还要分衰旺——旺相的久病也无妨，休囚的遇日冲也不死；增删卜易的野鹤同样分近久，近病落空到冲空、实空的日子就好，久病用神落旬空月破则旺相也无法治（LY.ILL.ZS.04、ZS.05）；黄金策旧注和《海底眼》说主象落空占病大忌，要有动爻日辰冲克才有救，不分近久（LY.ILL.HJC.37）。易林补遗的「空旺相久病也无妨」和增删卜易的「久病用神空破，旺相也无法医治」在久病旺相的空爻上说法相反。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Whether a Void Subject calls for a division between recent and long illness: the Yilin Buyi divides them: a sudden illness meeting Void can be saved and a long one surely dies, and beyond that a Void line is divided by strength — a strong one is no hindrance even in a long illness, a resting or trapped one does not die if it meets a Day clash; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise divides recent from long, a recent illness with the Subject Void being well on the day the Void is clashed or filled, while for a long illness a Subject that is Void or in Month-break cannot be treated even if strong (LY.ILL.ZS.04, ZS.05); the old commentary on the Huangjin Ce and the Haidi Yan say that a Void chief image is a great taboo in divining illness and that a moving line or a clash or control by the Day is needed for rescue, without dividing recent from long (LY.ILL.HJC.37). On a strong Void line in a long illness the Yilin Buyi ('strong Void, even a long illness is no hindrance') and the Zengshan Buyi ('long illness, Subject Void or broken, even strong it cannot be treated') say opposite things.

> **按**:「立时空」原文没有解释，大概指当下、此时就落空（与旬空的空相对），读法没有把握，这里按字面译作「立时空」。「日冲」是日辰冲它：冲空则实。这一条在「暴病逢空可救、久病逢空必死」上与增删卜易同，与黄金策旧注不同；但在空而旺相的久病是否无妨上，与增删卜易相反（见上面的分歧）。
>
> **Note:** 'Void at the moment' (li shi kong) is not explained in the original; it probably means Void right now, as against the Void of the ten-day cycle, but the reading is uncertain and it is translated literally. A 'Day clash' is the Day clashing it: a clash fills a Void. This entry agrees with the Zengshan Buyi on 'a sudden illness meeting Void can be saved and a long illness surely dies' and differs from the old commentary on the Huangjin Ce, but on whether a strong Void line makes a long illness harmless it is opposed to the Zengshan Buyi (see the disagreement above).

## LY.ILL.EL.14 空身空命皆非忌，无鬼无财岂足凭：甲子旬空戌亥不等于属猪属犬人皆命绝；病人无鬼必死之说不准，无鬼不过无神祟；无财只是眼下不思饮食，惟夫占妻、主占仆忌财空

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:空身、空命都不必忌讳，无鬼、无财哪里够得上凭据。注说：比如甲子旬占，空的是戌、亥，天下万万属猪、属狗的人，难道都命绝了？前人又说病人无鬼必死，难道没有验证过吗？《天玄赋》里虽然说占病无鬼，就没有叩告的门路，是天年命尽，这病不能好。可是比如哥哥占弟弟的病，鬼是忌神，怎么该在卦里？又如父亲占儿子的病，鬼是仇人，怎么可以用它？如果按道理论病，只取用爻的旺衰生克，就能断定存亡。凡是卦里无鬼，不过是没有神祟罢了，难道就是天年命尽？何况现在屡次试验没有官鬼的卦，也未必死。又辨别没有财的说法更加差错：财爻虽然是饮食，没有财，不过是眼下不吃东西，怎么能丧命？只有丈夫占妻子、主人占仆人来看，才忌讳财空，其余占的都不忌讳。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Void of the Body and the Void of the natal branch need not be feared, and no ghost or no Wealth is hardly enough to rely on. The commentary says: for example, when a divination is made in the Jiazi decade the Void branches are Xu and Hai, and of the innumerable people under heaven born in the year of the pig or the dog, are all to have their lives cut off? The men of old also said that a patient with no ghost must die, and was there no proof? Although the Tianxuan Fu says that in divining illness with no ghost there is no door to knock on and appeal, it is the end of one's allotted years, and the illness will not be cured. But for instance, if an elder brother divines a younger brother's illness, the ghost is the Adversary, and how should it be in the hexagram? Or if a father divines a son's illness, the ghost is an enemy, and how can it be used? If the illness is discussed on principle, only the strength and the generation and control of the Subject line are taken, and life or death is decided. A hexagram with no ghost only lacks a haunting, and is it therefore the end of one's allotted years? Moreover, no-Official hexagrams have been tried many times and the person did not necessarily die. Again the claim about lacking Wealth is even more mistaken: though the Wealth line is food and drink, lacking it only means that for the moment he does not eat, how can that cost his life? Only in a husband divining a wife or a master a servant is a Void Wealth line feared, and in all other divinations it is not feared.

>
> **Where they disagree:** Whether a patient's hexagram with no ghost or no Wealth means certain death: the Yilin Buyi says no ghost only lacks a haunting and does not mean the end of one's allotted years, and no Wealth only means he does not want to eat for the moment, a Void Wealth line being feared only in a husband divining a wife or a master a servant; what it criticises is the Tianxuan Fu's 'in divining illness with no ghost there is no door to knock on, it is the end of one's allotted years'; the Yi Mao likewise says that no ghost and no Wealth, Void of the Body or of the natal branch, do not mean death (LY.ILL.YM.11); the Huangjin Ce, however, says that although the Pressure line is a malign killer it cannot be absent, that when it is absent from the hexagram one must look for it hidden, and that if the hidden one is also Void the illness cannot be cured (LY.ILL.HJC.49), and the old commentary and Wang Hongxu differ on the scope of that saying; in the Yi Yin, Chao Yidao says that one whose Subject line or natal-branch line is absent from the hexagram dies, and that men fear Void ghosts and women Void Wealth (LY.ILL.YY.10); the Huangjin Ce also says that emolument belongs to Wealth and long life to the Resource line, and that a Void or absent Wealth means no appetite (LY.ILL.HJC.38). The books differ, and none gives a ruling.

> **按**:这一条把上一条歌诀的最后两句「空身空命皆非忌，无鬼无财岂足凭」拿出来单讲：空命（本命落旬空）、无鬼（卦无官鬼）、无财（卦无妻财）三种说法，评注者一个一个驳。「叩告之门」是可以向鬼神叩告的门路，出自《天玄赋》所说的「占病无鬼必无叩告之门」（黄金策也用了这句，见 LY.ILL.HJC.49）。「兄占弟病，鬼乃忌神」是说占兄弟的病，用神是兄弟，官鬼克兄弟，所以是忌神；「父占子病，鬼作仇人」是说占子孙的病，子孙的仇神是官鬼——所以这些卦里官鬼不该出现，何必因为无鬼就断死。
>
> **Note:** This entry takes the last two lines of the verse in the last entry, 'the Void of the Body and of the natal branch need not be feared, no ghost or no Wealth is hardly enough to rely on', and discusses them separately: three sayings, Void natal branch (the natal branch falling on the ten-day Void), no ghost (the hexagram lacks a Pressure line) and no Wealth (it lacks a Wealth line), and the commentator refutes them one by one. The 'door to knock on and appeal' is the way of appealing to ghosts and spirits, from the Tianxuan Fu's 'in divining illness with no ghost there is no door to knock on' (the Huangjin Ce uses this sentence too; see LY.ILL.HJC.49). 'An elder brother divining a younger brother's illness, the ghost is the Adversary' means that in divining a brother's illness the Subject is the Peer line, and the Pressure line controls the Peer, so it is the Adversary; 'a father divining a son's illness, the ghost is an enemy' means that for a son's illness the enemy of Output is the Pressure line; so in such hexagrams the Pressure line ought not to appear, and there is no reason to judge death because the ghost is absent.

## LY.ILL.EL.15 六冲分缓急：初灾遇六冲当全瘥、久病逢之命必伤，合处逢冲同此意，不凭主象弱和强；土爻临鬼动多凶少吉；近病逢冲则愈、重病逢冲则死；占尊长鬼为元神动则祈让可疗

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:只有六冲这一种，能分缓急，病的来源要自己斟酌衡量：刚起的灾遇上它应当全好，久病遇上它命必伤；合处逢冲也是这个意思，不看主象是弱还是强；再看土爻临鬼发动，凶多吉少，灾祸难以禳解。注说：病得六冲、六冲变冲、合处逢冲，都要审察病是远是近、是轻是重：暴病来占，朝夕之间就应当痊愈；如果久病来占，用象虽然旺相，也主身亡。又说近病逢冲就好，重病逢冲就死；卦纵然不冲，用爻也旺，凡是遇上财爻、鬼爻发动的，都可以断死；只有占尊长，鬼是元神，发动了就祈祷禳解可以治疗。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Only the Six-Clash tells slow from urgent, and the source of the illness must be weighed by oneself: a fresh calamity meeting it should be wholly cured, a long illness meeting it surely brings harm to life; a clash met in a combination has the same meaning, without regard to whether the chief image is weak or strong; and further, an Earth line on a moving ghost is mostly ominous and seldom auspicious, and the misfortune is hard to exorcise. The commentary says: when an illness has a six-clash, or a six-clash changing into a clash, or a clash met in a combination, one must examine whether it is near or far, light or heavy: a sudden illness divined should be cured by morning or evening; if a long illness is divined, though the Subject image is strong it still means death. It says also that a recent illness meeting a clash is cured and a heavy illness meeting a clash dies; even if the hexagram is not clashing and the Subject line is strong, wherever the Wealth or Pressure line moves one may say death; only in divining an elder, the ghost being the Support, moving it can be treated by prayer and exorcism.

'A six-clash, a six-clash changing into a clash, a clash met in a combination' are three kinds of clash: the hexagram being a Six-Clash hexagram, a Six-Clash hexagram changing into another, and a Six-Combine hexagram (or a combining line) meeting a clash. It is the same matter as the Zengshan Buyi's 'a Six-Clash changing into a clash makes a long illness hard to treat' (LY.ILL.ZS.02) and 'a change into Extinction or control also means danger in a fresh illness' (LY.ILL.ZS.03), the Yi Mao's 'a hexagram that is Reversed, Hidden, in a tomb or in Extinction is untreatable; a recent illness is cured by the three clashes, a long one dies' (LY.ILL.YM.10) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.07); the books agree that a long illness meeting a clash surely dies, and differ slightly on a recent illness meeting a clash (here it is cured).

## LY.ILL.HJC.38 禄系妻财、寿属父母：财是食禄、父母是寿命，卦中不可无、宜静不宜动；财空或无财不思饮食，父母动占病大忌（克制福神、官杀肆虐、服药不效、反促天年）；主象遇绝，财父入绝为禄绝、寿绝

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病，妻财是食禄，父母是寿命，卦里不能没有，宜静不宜动。卦里没有财爻或者财爻落空，是不想吃东西；父母爻动是占病的大忌，因为它克制子孙（福神），官鬼这个凶煞就能肆意逞凶，吃药不见效、祷告不灵，所以说「反促天年」。旧注还说：主象遇到绝，或变到绝地，财爻遇到叫禄绝，父母爻遇到叫寿绝，整个卦象如果凶，这是死的兆头。

## LY.ILL.HJC.54 命入幽明两墓泉世之人：身、命、世随鬼入墓，有卦墓、鬼墓、世墓、主墓；动出在卦中的是明墓、变入的是幽墓，不拘幽明皆非吉兆；大象凶又逢墓动、或世爻主象俱入墓者必死，动爻日辰冲破墓爻则无事

## LY.ILL.YM.11 考之占验：四灭没、十卦、无鬼无财、身空命空、凶煞皆不死，死者必用神死则死、用神生则生（神煞尤轻）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:从占验来看，四灭、四没的卦不死，十卦不死，没有鬼、没有财的不死，身空、命空的不死，遇到凶煞的不死；那么人之所以死，一定是用神死了他就死，用神生旺他就活。灭没：春天需、蒙，夏天观、蛊，秋天剥、节，冬天旅、临。十卦：明夷、观、贲、大畜、丰、同人、蛊、夬、需、临。凶煞：三坵五墓、丧车这一类。比如占父亲的病，六月得观卦，凭什么会死？用爻当日、月，卦里没有财、鬼，有什么凶？因为用爻坏而没有鬼，即使祈祷也得不到保佑；用爻旺而没有鬼，不祈祷也安康；没有财而用爻绝，天禄已尽；没有财而用爻旺，只是吃得少。甲子旬空亥，难道亥年生的人就不能问病？寅月破申，难道申年生的人就不能占病？所以应爻（空）不必作戒；身空的，有世爻、卦身可以凭据；本命不出现的，有变卦、伏爻可以取用。大体上专心看用爻来定生死安危，才真正得到《易》的宗旨，神煞就更轻了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Examining what divinations have proved: with the Four Extinguishing and Submerging hexagrams he does not die; with the ten hexagrams he does not die; with no ghost and no Wealth he does not die; with the Body Void or the destiny Void he does not die; with malign stars he does not die; so what he dies of must be that he dies when the Subject dies and lives when the Subject lives. The Extinguishing and Submerging: in spring Xu and Meng, in summer Guan and Gu, in autumn Bo and Jie, in winter Lü and Lin. The ten hexagrams: Mingyi, Guan, Bi (Adornment), Daxu, Feng, Tongren, Gu, Guai, Xu and Lin. Malign stars are such as Three Mounds and Five Tombs and the Funeral Carriage. For example, in divining a father's illness, if in the sixth month one obtains Guan, from what would he die? The Subject line sits on the Day and Month and the hexagram has neither Wealth nor ghost: what ill omen is there? For if the Subject line is spoiled and there is no ghost, prayer brings no protection; if strong and no ghost, he is well without praying; with no Wealth and the Subject in Extinction the heavenly emolument has ended; with no Wealth and the Subject strong he merely takes little food. If the Jiazi decade leaves Hai Void, may a person of the Hai natal branch not ask about illness? If the Yin month is a Month-break against Shen, may a person of the Shen natal branch not divine illness? So the Other line need not be a warning; if the Body is Void, the Self and Body can be relied on; if the natal branch does not appear, the changed hexagram and hidden lines can be taken. In general, to concentrate on the Subject line to fix life and death, safety and danger, truly grasps the intent of the Yi; the spirit-stars weigh lighter still.

## LY.ILL.YM.15 非危之象：飞爻用神绝而伏爻用神生、内卦用神亡而外卦用神存、卦爻无用神而日月有用神，皆非危

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:飞爻上的用神绝了，但伏爻上的用神生旺，不危险；内卦的用神没了，但外卦的用神还在，不危险；卦爻里没有用神，但日、月里有用神，不危险。注举例：寅月丙寅日，占兄弟，得乾变需，申飞在用爻上，日破月破，却不知道酉伏在戌下，火生土而土生金，所以说不危险；辰月辛卯日，占儿子本人，得未济，外卦的未虽旬空，内卦的辰当月建，后来病好了；午月戊寅日，占妻子本人，得既济，卦里虽然没有用爻，但月是午、日是寅，原神和用神都出现，当月病就好了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject on the flying line is in Extinction but the Subject on the hidden line is born, there is no danger; if the Subject in the inner trigram is lost but the Subject in the outer trigram remains, there is no danger; if there is no Subject among the lines of the hexagram but the Day or Month holds the Subject, there is no danger. The note's examples: in the Yin month on the Bingyin day, a brother was divined and Qian changing into Xu obtained: Shen stood on the Subject line as the flying line, in Day-break and Month-break, but it was not noticed that You lay hidden beneath Xu, and Fire generates Earth and Earth generates Metal, so it is said there is no danger; in the Chen month on the Xinmao day, a son himself was divined and Weiji obtained: Wei in the outer trigram was in Void, but Chen in the inner trigram stood on the Month, and later he recovered; in the Wu month on the Wuyin day, a wife herself was divined and Jiji obtained: though there was no Subject line in the hexagram, the month was Wu and the day Yin, and the Support and the Subject both appeared, and that month she recovered.

## LY.ILL.YM.16 用神有一生则生、有一死则死：日月动爻克用而用得化生扶则非危；日月动爻不生不克而用神化克则危；变爻亦宜无病乃真有救

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:日、月、动爻克用爻，这叫做不得救，但用爻变出生它、扶它的，不危险；日、月、动爻对用神不生也不克，而用神变出克它的，就危险了。所以说：用神有一个生它的就活，有一个死的就死。注：这是说用神变生、变克是最要紧的办法，但变出的爻也应当没有毛病，才是真有救。比如未月甲辰日，占儿子本人，得咸变蹇，日、月克用爻，大凶，但用爻变出兄弟长生，所以不死；戌月戊申日，占父亲本人，得艮变蛊，用爻独自发动本该活，因为变出去在亥绝，就救不了了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Day, Month and moving lines control the Subject line, this is called no rescue, but if the Subject line changes into what generates or supports it, there is no danger; if the Day, Month and moving lines neither generate nor control the Subject, but the Subject changes into what controls it, it is in danger. So it is said: if the Subject has one source of life it lives, and if it has one death it dies. The note: this says that the Subject changing into generation or control is the most important method, but the changed line must also be without flaw for the rescue to be real: for example, in the Wei month on the Jiachen day, a son himself was divined and Xian changing into Jian (Obstruction) obtained; the Day and Month control the Subject line, a great ill omen, but the Subject changed into Peer at Birth, so he did not die; in the Xu month on the Wushen day, a father himself was divined and Gen changing into Gu obtained: the Subject line moved alone and should have lived, but it changed into Extinction at Hai and so could not be saved.

## LY.ILL.YM.17 卦无用神：元神动则不死（趋生）；忌神动而用神伏反吉（避克）；亦必得日月之救

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里没有用神，说是凶兆，但得到原神发动就不会死，因为它是在趋向生路；忌神发动而用神伏藏，反而吉，因为它避开了克；虽然如此，也必须得到日、月的救助。注举例：酉月戊申日，占妻子本人，得睽变损，卦里没有用爻，本来不是吉兆，喜的是子孙发动，到冬天病好了，这叫趋生；亥月甲申日，也是占妻子本人，得姤变升，忌神发动，本来是凶兆，喜的是用神不出现，伏在月建下面，后来到子月壬辰日，病势逐渐缓解，因为申子辰会成水局，忌神合成了原神，这叫避克。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A hexagram without the Subject is said to be an ill omen, but if the Support moves he does not die, because he is heading toward life; if the Adversary moves and the Subject is hidden it is instead auspicious, because it avoids the control; even so, it must also gain the rescue of the Day and Month. The note's examples: in the You month on the Wushen day, a wife herself was divined and Kui changing into Sun obtained: the hexagram had no Subject line and so was not auspicious, but happily Output moved, and by winter she recovered — this is 'heading toward life'; in the Hai month on the Jiashen day, again a wife herself was divined and Gou changing into Sheng obtained: the Adversary moved, which was an ill omen, but happily the Subject did not appear and was hidden beneath the Month, and later on the Renchen day of the Zi month the illness gradually eased, for Shen, Zi and Chen meet to form a Water frame and the Adversary is combined into the Support — this is 'avoiding control'.

## LY.ILL.ZS.01 断病生死全凭用神：卦名、卦身世身兼用、本命丧门、蛇虎星煞都不验，只有卦变六冲例外

## LY.ILL.ZS.03 卦变绝克新疾亦主危亡：六冲变六冲近病也危，是因为化回头克（巽木变乾金、艮坤变震巽）；化比和、化克去、化回头生，近病吉久病凶（六冲之故）；此外必看用神

## LY.ILL.ZS.04 用遇旬空近病不忧、用逢月破久病难安：自占病世为用，占父母兄弟以其为用；近病用神旬空或化空，无日月动爻冲克则冲空实空之日即愈，逢冲克病虽重不死；月破看用神衰旺；久病用神空破，旺相也无法医治

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:自己占病，世爻是用神；占父母、兄弟的病，拿父母、兄弟做用神。近病：用神落旬空或变空，如果没有日月和动爻来冲克，冲空、实空的日子病就好；如果遇到冲克，病再重也不会死。用神逢月破，要看用神旺衰：旺的，到实破那天或出了这个月就好；衰又受克的，一定危险。久病：用神落旬空或月破的，就算用神旺相，也没法医治。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining one's own illness the Self line is the Subject; in divining a parent or brother, the Resource or Peer line is the Subject. A recent illness whose Subject falls into Void or changes into Void heals on the day the Void is clashed or filled if no Day, Month or moving line clashes or controls it; if it meets clash or control, even a heavy illness does not lead to death. If the Subject meets Month-break, look at whether it is strong or weak: if strong, the patient recovers on the day the break is filled or once the month has passed; if weak and controlled, he is surely in danger. In a long illness, if the Subject falls into Void or Month-break, then even if it is strong nothing can be done to treat it.

## LY.ILL.ZS.13 用绝逢生危而有救、主衰得助重亦何妨：用爻受克制刑冲但有日月动爻一个生扶，是绝处逢生临危有救；用神不宜太弱，得生扶拱合重病也不至死；觉子：用神也不宜太旺，过犹不及（旺极的老人卦全胜，壬戌日死）

## LY.ILL.ZS.35 不起之症卦中不见其医、立愈之灾爻中不报用药：用神化鬼、鬼化用神、忌神化六亲、六亲化忌神、用神化绝化克化墓、久病逢冲逢空、随鬼入墓，都是无药可医；近病六冲或用神旬空或化回头生、自占子孙持世，不药而愈，也不现医

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占医生的卦里看不到医生，有两种情形。一种是治不好的病，神不让医生出现：用神变鬼、鬼变用神，忌神变六亲、六亲变忌神，用神变绝、变克、变墓，久病遇冲遇空，随着鬼入墓，这些都是没有药可医的病。另一种是马上就好的病，神也不让医生出现：近病如果不是卦得六冲，就是用神值旬空、或用神变回头生；自己占病，子孙持世，这些都是不吃药就好的。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a hexagram that divines a doctor, the doctor may fail to appear, in two cases. One is an illness that cannot be cured, where the spirit does not let the doctor appear: the Subject changing into Pressure or Pressure into the Subject, the Adversary into a relation or a relation into the Adversary, the Subject changing into extinction, into a control or into a tomb, a long illness meeting a clash or Void, following Pressure into the tomb; all these are illnesses that no medicine can treat. The other is an illness that heals at once, where the spirit also does not let the doctor appear: a recent illness is either in a Six-Clash hexagram, or has its Subject in Void or changing into a returning generation; in divining one's own illness, Output holds the Self line; in all these the patient recovers without medicine.
