## LY.ILL.HJC.37 主空无救须防中道而殂：主象落空占病大忌，要有动爻日辰冲克才有救（冲空则实、克空为用）；王洪绪说不独指空，墓绝空破有救者无妨、无救者必死，救指生扶拱合

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:主象落空（王洪绪：或入墓、绝、被破），占病是大忌；要有救才行，没救就会死在半路。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A main image that is Void (Wang Hongxu: or in a tomb, in extinction, or broken) is a great taboo in divining illness; it must have rescue, and without rescue the patient will die on the way.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6896 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6896*

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6897 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6897*

| 盖冲空则实，克空为用，故也。 | 因为冲了落空的爻它就实了，克了落空的爻它就有了用。 | For clashing a Void line makes it real, and controlling a Void line puts it to use. |
| 否则必死。 | 不然一定死。 | Otherwise he will surely die. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2372 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2372*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 非独指空而言也。 | 这一句不光是指落空说的。 | This does not speak of Void alone. |
| 凡主象墓绝空破，有救者无妨，无救者必死。 | 凡是主象入墓、入绝、落空、被破，有救的没关系，没有救的一定死。 | Whenever the main image is in a tomb, in extinction, Void or broken, if there is rescue it does not matter, and if there is none he will surely die. |
| 救者，生扶拱合也。 | 所说的救，就是生、扶、拱、合。 | By rescue is meant generating, supporting, embracing and combining. |

> **打架**:怎样才算「有救」：旧注说主象落空，要动爻、日辰来冲它、克它才有救（冲空则实，克空为用），不然必死；王洪绪说不光是落空，入墓、绝、被破也算，救是生、扶、拱、合。一个用冲克把它叫起来，一个用生扶合去帮它。
>
> **Where they disagree:** What counts as 'rescue': the old commentary says a Void main image is rescued only when a moving line or the Day clashes or controls it (clashing a Void line makes it real, controlling a Void line puts it to use), otherwise he surely dies; Wang Hongxu says the line speaks not of Void alone but of tomb, extinction and being broken as well, and rescue is generating, supporting, embracing and combining. One wakes the line by clash and control, the other helps it by generation, support and combination.

## LY.ILL.HJC.38 禄系妻财、寿属父母：财是食禄、父母是寿命，卦中不可无、宜静不宜动；财空或无财不思饮食，父母动占病大忌（克制福神、官杀肆虐、服药不效、反促天年）；主象遇绝，财父入绝为禄绝、寿绝

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病，妻财是食禄，父母是寿命，卦里不能没有，宜静不宜动。卦里没有财爻或者财爻落空，是不想吃东西；父母爻动是占病的大忌，因为它克制子孙（福神），官鬼这个凶煞就能肆意逞凶，吃药不见效、祷告不灵，所以说「反促天年」。旧注还说：主象遇到绝，或变到绝地，财爻遇到叫禄绝，父母爻遇到叫寿绝，整个卦象如果凶，这是死的兆头。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining illness, Wealth is the food-emolument and Resource is the span of life; neither may be missing from the hexagram, and both should be still and not moving. If there is no Wealth line or it is Void, he has no wish to eat; a moving Resource line is a great taboo, because it controls Output (the spirit of fortune) and lets the Pressure killer run wild, so medicine has no effect and prayer is not answered, hence 'it shortens the span of life instead'. The old commentary adds: if the main image meets extinction or changes into a place of extinction, then Wealth meeting it is the emolument cut off and Resource meeting it the life span cut off, and if the whole image is bad this is a sign of death.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6899 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6899*

| 禄系妻财，空则不思饮食； | 食禄归妻财管，落空就不想吃东西； | The emolument belongs to Wealth: if it is Void, he has no wish to eat; |
| 寿属父母，动则反促天年。 | 寿命归父母管，一动反倒缩短寿数。 | the life span belongs to Resource: if it moves, it shortens the span of life instead. |

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 父母爻动，占病所忌，以其克制福神，官煞能肆其虐故也，主服药无效，故云反促天年。 | 父母爻动，是占病所忌讳的，因为它克制子孙（福神），官鬼这个凶煞就能肆意逞凶，主吃药没有效果，所以说反而缩短寿数。 | A moving Resource line is what divining illness taboos, because it controls Output (the spirit of fortune) and the Pressure killer can then run wild; it means medicine has no effect, hence it is said to shorten the span of life instead. |
| 占兄弟病反宜动也。 | 占兄弟的病，反而宜于动。 | In divining the illness of a brother (the Peer line), moving is on the contrary right. |

> **按**:「占兄弟病反宜动也」这一句是王洪绪独有的，书里没有说道理。按六亲，父母生兄弟，占兄弟的病时父母爻是原神，它动起来反倒是帮忙，大概是这个缘故。「福神」是子孙爻的另一个叫法，「食禄」是口粮、吃的。旧注比王洪绪多讲了「禄绝」「寿绝」。
>
> **Note:** The sentence 'in divining a brother's illness moving is on the contrary right' is Wang Hongxu's alone, and the book gives no reason. By the six relations Resource generates Peer, so in divining a brother's illness the Resource line is the Support, and its moving is on the contrary a help; that is probably the reason. The 'spirit of fortune' is another name for the Output line, and 'food-emolument' is one's ration, what one eats. The old commentary speaks of the 'emolument cut off' and the 'life span cut off', which Wang Hongxu does not.

## LY.ILL.HJC.41 世上临鬼不可随官入墓：世持官鬼本非吉兆，遇日辰带墓爻、本命爻随官入墓、世爻用爻化入鬼墓、带鬼动化入墓、持鬼墓发动，皆主大凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻持着官鬼，本来就不是吉兆；如果再遇到日辰带着墓爻，就叫「随官入墓」；本命爻随官入墓也凶。世爻、用爻变入鬼的墓，以及世爻、命爻带着鬼动、变入墓的，还有用爻、命爻、世爻持着鬼墓发动的，都主大凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Pressure line holding the Self line is in itself no lucky sign; if it also meets a tomb line carried by the Day, this is called 'following Pressure into the tomb'; a natal line following Pressure into the tomb is also unlucky. The Self or Subject line changing into the tomb of Pressure, the Self or natal line carrying Pressure, moving and changing into a tomb, and the Subject, natal or Self line holding the tomb of Pressure and moving, all mean great misfortune.

## LY.ILL.HJC.44 用连鬼杀目前必见倾危：旧注是用爻发动又化出鬼爻（病人变鬼），无子孙日辰解救必死；王洪绪说「连」当作「变」，鬼煞是忌神，指用神化回头克，有日月动爻解救则不危，并驳「用神变官鬼必死」

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神连着鬼煞，眼前一定看到危险：没有子孙、日辰、动爻来解救，危险就在眼前。

## LY.ILL.HJC.47 用绝逢生危而有救：用爻不宜在死绝之地（一克则倒），卦中动爻生助叫绝处逢生，凶中回吉，病危而有救

## LY.ILL.HJC.54 命入幽明两墓泉世之人：身、命、世随鬼入墓，有卦墓、鬼墓、世墓、主墓；动出在卦中的是明墓、变入的是幽墓，不拘幽明皆非吉兆；大象凶又逢墓动、或世爻主象俱入墓者必死，动爻日辰冲破墓爻则无事

Whether dark or bright, neither is a lucky sign: even if the whole image does not show death, the illness is dangerous and hard; if the whole image is already bad and a tomb also moves, or the Self line and the main image both enter a tomb, he will surely die. ## LY.ILL.ZS.04 用遇旬空近病不忧、用逢月破久病难安：自占病世为用，占父母兄弟以其为用；近病用神旬空或化空，无日月动爻冲克则冲空实空之日即愈，逢冲克病虽重不死；月破看用神衰旺；久病用神空破，旺相也无法医治

> **按**:书中例里，儿子占病，用神是子孙，「鬼变子孙」就是官鬼变成用神。子孙落旬空，所以近病出空之日就好了；但「用化鬼」这个凶兆没有消失，后来在午年死于痘症。
>
> **Note:** In the case, a son's illness is divined, so the Subject is Output, and 'Pressure changing into Output' is Pressure changing into the Subject. Output being in Void, the recent illness healed on the day the Void was out; but the ill omen of 'the Subject changing into Pressure' did not go away, and he later died of smallpox in the Wu year.

## LY.ILL.ZS.13 用绝逢生危而有救、主衰得助重亦何妨：用爻受克制刑冲但有日月动爻一个生扶，是绝处逢生临危有救；用神不宜太弱，得生扶拱合重病也不至死；觉子：用神也不宜太旺，过犹不及（旺极的老人卦全胜，壬戌日死）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻在绝地遇到生，危险却有救；主象衰弱得到帮助，病重又有什么妨碍。用爻受到克制、刑冲，只要日月、动爻有一个来生扶它，就是绝处逢生，临危有救。用神不宜太弱，太弱就体虚，一时难以痊愈，但只要得到生扶拱合，就算得重病，也不至于死。觉子补充：用神也不宜太旺，易理贵在中庸，过头和不及一样。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Subject meeting life in extinction is in danger yet rescued; a weak main image that is helped, however heavy the illness, what does it matter? If the Subject line is controlled, punished or clashed, but the Day, Month or a moving line has one that generates and supports it, this is meeting life in a place of extinction, and in danger it is rescued. The Subject should not be too weak; if too weak the body is frail and cannot quickly heal, but if it gets generation, support, embracing or combining, then even in a heavy illness he does not die. Juezi adds: the Subject should not be too strong either; the principle of the Changes prizes the mean, and going too far is like falling short.
