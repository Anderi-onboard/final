## LY.ILL.BSZZ.34 何知章·亲人的病灾：父母疾（白虎临爻兼刑克）、父母殃（财爻发动煞神伤）、子孙疾（父母爻动相克）、子孙灾、小儿死（子孙空亡加白虎）、兄弟亡（用落空亡白虎伤）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:「何知」口诀里关于亲人病灾的六问：父母有病，是白虎临着（父母）这一爻，再加刑克；父母有灾殃，是财爻发动、煞神来伤；子孙有病，是父母爻发动来克制子孙；子孙有灾，卜筮大全说是白虎落到福德（子孙）上，卜筮正宗说是父母落到福德上；小孩要死，是子孙落空亡再加白虎；兄弟去世，是用神落空亡又被白虎所伤。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Six questions of the 'how to know' rhyme about illness and calamity among kin. A parent is ill when the White Tiger sits on the (Resource) line together with punishment and control; a parent meets calamity when the Wealth line moves and a killing spirit wounds; a child is ill when the Resource line moves and controls Output; a child meets misfortune when, in the Bushi Daquan, the White Tiger arrives upon Output, or, in the Bushi Zhengzong, Resource arrives upon Output; a small child will die when Output is Void and the White Tiger is added; a brother dies when the Subject falls into Void and the White Tiger wounds it.

> **打架**:子孙有灾看什么：卜筮大全答「白虎当临福德来」，白虎落到子孙爻上；卜筮正宗答「父母当临福德来」，父母爻落到子孙爻上。两本一个说白虎、一个说父母，书里没有说哪个对（白虎是六神，父母是六亲，两条不是一回事）。
>
> **Where they disagree:** What to look at for a child's misfortune: the Bushi Daquan answers 'the White Tiger arrives upon Output', the White Tiger falling on the Output line; the Bushi Zhengzong answers 'Resource arrives upon Output', the Resource line falling on the Output line. One says the White Tiger and the other Resource, and the books do not say which is right (the White Tiger is one of the six spirits and Resource one of the six relations, so the two are not the same thing).

> **按**:「何知章」一问一答，一共问了三四十件事（父母子孙的病、有没有子孙、讼事、财富、田地、屋宇、坟墓、六畜、口舌、盗贼……），都是一家的事；这里只收与病、死、鬼怪有关的。卜筮大全里它是「十六、何知章」，卜筮正宗里它在《赋文三篇》中；两本字句略有出入。「杀神」「煞神」是同一个字的两种写法。「用落空亡」的「用」是用神。
>
> **Note:** The 'how to know' rhyme asks and answers some thirty or forty things (illnesses of parents and children, whether there are children, lawsuits, wealth, land, houses, graves, livestock, quarrels, thieves and more), all affairs of a household; only those to do with illness, death and ghostly happenings are taken here. In the Bushi Daquan it is chapter 16, 'the how-to-know rhyme'; in the Bushi Zhengzong it is among the three rhapsodies; the two texts differ a little in wording. 'Sha' in the two forms of 'killing spirit' is one word written two ways. The 'yong' of 'yong falls into Void' is the Subject.

## LY.ILL.CA.05 占病先须问六亲：父母空亡防父母、财爻无气损妻身、子孙化鬼须遭死、兄弟杀临定不贞

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病先要问是哪一层亲人，吉凶必须问清楚。父母爻空亡，要防父母；财爻无气，妻子受损；子孙化成鬼，必定遭死；兄弟爻上有杀，一定不吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining illness first ask which of the relations it is; good and ill must be made clear. Resource Void: guard the parents; the Wealth line without qi: the wife is harmed; Output changing into a ghost will meet death; Peer with a killing sitting on it is surely not steadfast.

> **按**:这是第三首。「父母空亡防父母，财爻无气损妻身」是说占哪一层亲人就看哪一层的爻（父母爻、财爻）有没有气，和天玄赋「杀临父母，当忧堂上之亲；空及妻财，灾虑闺中之妇」（LY.ILL.TXF.01）、易林补遗「谁爻化鬼谁人犯」（LY.ILL.EL.02）是同一个思路。「不贞」按字面译作「not steadfast」，意思是不吉利、靠不住。
>
> **Note:** This is the third poem. 'Resource Void: guard the parents; the Wealth line without qi: the wife is harmed' says that one looks at the line of the relation asked about (the Resource line, the Wealth line) to see whether it has qi, on the same line of thought as the Tianxuan Fu's 'a killing sitting on Resource, worry for the parents in the hall; Void reaching Wealth, fear calamity for the woman in the inner chamber' (LY.ILL.TXF.01) and the Yilin Buyi's 'whose line changes into a ghost, that person is struck' (LY.ILL.EL.02). 'Bu zhen' is rendered literally as 'not steadfast', and means not auspicious and not to be relied on.

## LY.ILL.CA.07 六亲占病六首：子占亲病（子要兴隆父要安、兄动缠绵、子兴财发入黄泉）；父占子病（兄兴子动死还生、鬼摇父动难保、财动病增）；夫问妻疾（财明子动便安然、鬼爻发动沉重、父发兄摇定断弦）；妻占夫病（鬼静财兴可放心、兄子交重恩义绝、父摇难愈）；兄弟占病（父兴兄静自安全、鬼摇财动黄泉客、子动留连数日）；奴婢疾病（鬼摇更连绵、财安子发无事、父动兄兴不得痊）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子占父母的病：什么时候好？子孙要兴隆，父母要安。兄动，缠绵难脱体；子孙兴、财发，入黄泉。父占子病：兄兴、子动，死里逃生；鬼摇、父动，终究难保；财动，缠绵，病反增。夫问妻的病：财明、子动，便安然；鬼爻发动，还沉重；父发、兄摇，定断弦。妻占夫的病：鬼静、财兴，可以放心；兄、子交重，恩义断绝；父摇，难愈，病沉沉。兄弟来占病：父兴、兄静，自然安全；鬼摇、财动，黄泉客；子动，留连几日卧床。奴婢的病：鬼摇，病更连绵；财安、子发，没有别的事；父动、兄兴，不得痊愈。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A son divining a parent's illness, when will it be cured? Output must thrive and Resource must be at peace; Peer moving, it drags on and is hard to shake off; Output rising and Wealth moving, it enters the Yellow Springs. A parent divining a child's illness: Peer rising and Output moving, one dies and yet lives; the ghost shaking and Resource moving, in the end he cannot be kept; Wealth moving, it drags on and the illness increases. A husband asking of his wife's illness: Wealth clear and Output moving, at once she is at ease; the Pressure line moving, it is still heavy; Resource issuing and Peer shaking, surely the string is cut. A wife divining her husband's illness: the ghost still and Wealth rising, one may set the heart at rest; Peer and Output crossing heavily, love and duty are cut; Resource shaking, hard to cure, the illness deep. Brothers coming to divine an illness: Resource rising and Peer still, of itself safe; the ghost shaking and Wealth moving, a guest of the Yellow Springs; Output moving, it lingers several days in bed. A servant's illness: the ghost shaking, the illness drags on the more; Wealth at peace and Output issuing, no other matter; Resource moving and Peer rising, no recovery.

>
> **Where they disagree:** Which line must thrive when a son divines a parent's illness differs in the two copies. The Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.07) has 'a son divining a parent's illness, when will it be cured: Output must thrive and Resource must be at peace' — Output thriving and Resource at peace; the Duanyi Tianji (see LY.ILL.DYTJ.08) has the same poem as 'the ghost must thrive and Resource must be at peace' — the Pressure line thriving and Resource at peace. Output being strong means the medicine is effective and can control the ghost, while the Pressure line thriving means the illness is heavy: the first agrees better with the logic of illness, and the second may be a miswriting of 'ghost' for 'Output', which the book does not explain, and here the two are only set side by side.

> **按**:这六首（第六到第十一首）是按病人和占卜人的关系分的六亲占病歌：子占父母、父占子、夫占妻、妻占夫、兄弟占、奴婢占。每首都是「哪一爻动、哪一爻静、哪一爻兴」几个组合的吉凶。「断弦」是妻子去世的委婉说法（琴弦断了）；「黄泉客」是死亡；「财明」是财爻明显出现；「恩义绝」是夫妻情义断绝（兄弟克财而说兄弟伤妻的说法，原文没有解释）。这六首在断易天机里作「经验论家人占病」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.08），文字略有出入（除了上面「打架」里那一处，还有「妻问夫病」等）。用爻表是：子占父母看父母、父占子看子孙、夫占妻看财、妻占夫看鬼（见 LY.ILL.TXF.01）。第十首「兄弟来占疾病缠」的「兄弟」是兄弟这个亲人，不是兄弟爻。
>
> **Note:** These six poems (the sixth to the eleventh) are poems for divining illness by relations, divided by the relation between the sick person and the diviner: a son about a parent, a parent about a child, a husband about a wife, a wife about a husband, a brother, and a servant. Each is the good or ill of a few combinations of 'which line moves, which stays still, which rises'. 'Duan xian' (the string cut) is a delicate way of saying the wife has died (a broken zither string); 'guest of the Yellow Springs' is death; 'Wealth clear' is the Wealth line clearly appearing; 'love and duty cut' is the bond between the couple cut off (the original does not explain why Peer and Output should cut a husband's love from his wife). In the Duanyi Tianji these six appear as 'verses from experience on divining illness by family members' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.08), with slight differences in wording (besides the one place in the 'dispute' above, also 'the wife asks of her husband's illness' and so on). The table of the Subject line: a son about a parent looks at Resource, a parent about a child at Output, a husband about a wife at Wealth, a wife about a husband at the ghost (see LY.ILL.TXF.01). The 'brothers' of the tenth poem, 'brothers come to divine an illness that clings', are brothers as relatives and not the Peer line.

'Duan xian' (the string cut) is a delicate way of saying the wife has died (a broken zither string); 'guest of the Yellow Springs' is death; 'Wealth clear' is the Wealth line clearly appearing. The second line of the first poem is 'the ghost must thrive and Resource must be at peace', where the Chan'ao Gezhang has 'Output must thrive and Resource must be at peace', see the dispute. The table of the Subject line: a son about a parent looks at Resource, a parent about a child at Output, a husband about a wife at Wealth, a wife about a husband at the ghost (see LY.ILL.TXF.01). The 'brother' of 'divining a brother's illness' is a brother as a relative and not the Peer line.

## LY.ILL.EL.02 谁爻化鬼谁人犯、那命临官那个遭：父爻化鬼灾至椿萱、兄化官祸延手足、子化官殃从儿女、财之用象病在妻奴、世化官病临自己、应位为鬼妻妾不宁；本命临官必为灾；问病有无，忌神不动、鬼不交重、用神不空破便言平安

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:哪个爻变出鬼，是哪个人犯的；哪个人的本命落在官鬼上，是哪个人遭殃；再看六亲里谁受克，轻重就能判断得分毫不差。再说全家有没有人生病，要去找用象：父爻变出鬼，灾落到父母身上；兄弟变出官，祸延及兄弟姐妹；子孙变出官爻，灾殃从儿女来；财爻的用象，病在妻子和仆人；世爻变出官爻，病落在自己身上；应位是鬼，妻妾不安宁。再查每个人的本命，落在官鬼上的，一定成灾，比如占得某卦，午火临官，就说属马的人受患；鬼如果空亡，不这样断，要再看六亲的象，不能受伤。财爻动，祸事忧及长辈；鬼兴旺，灾殃波及兄弟；兄弟摇动，妻子生病；父爻动，儿女有灾；如果妻子来占，子孙动，丈夫受患；其余占问，子孙兴旺都是平安。问有没有病，卦里忌神不动、鬼不发动、用神不遇旬空月破，就说平安。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Whichever line changes into a ghost, that person has offended; whichever natal branch sits on the Pressure line, that person suffers; moreover see which of the six relations is controlled, and severity can be judged to the finest degree. Again, to judge whether anyone in the whole household falls ill, one must find the Subject image: if the Resource line changes into a ghost, calamity reaches the father and mother; if Peer changes into Pressure, misfortune spreads to brothers and sisters; if Output changes into a Pressure line, misfortune comes through the children; for the Wealth image, the illness lies with the wife and servants; if the Self changes into a Pressure line, the illness falls on oneself; if the Other's place is the ghost, wife and concubines are unsettled. Then check each person's natal branch: whoever's falls on the Pressure line will surely suffer calamity, so that if a certain hexagram is obtained and Wu Fire sits on the Pressure line, one says the person born in the year of the Horse suffers; if the ghost is Void, do not judge this way, but look further at the image of the six relations, which must not be harmed. Wealth moving, misfortune worries the elders; the ghost thriving, calamity reaches the brothers; the Peer stirring, the wife falls ill; the Resource moving, the children have calamity; if the wife divines and Output moves, the husband suffers; in all other questions Output thriving means peace. To ask whether there is illness: if the Adversary is still, the ghost does not move, and the Subject does not meet Void or Month-break, say all is well.

## LY.ILL.EL.18 占他人病断之三：代占他人以应爻为用——临月破旬空其命难保，衰弱遭动象日月伤或变墓绝便凶，应纵休囚得变生旺或动爻日月相扶命还有救；生应为元神宜动、克应为忌客宜空；鬼临应上病必难痊；卦身不可临月破、不可成墓绝之乡

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:替别人问，看应爻，如果临月破最难逃过；遇冲遇克，人难救；逢旺逢生，病必消；生应的元神宜发动，克它的忌象怕交重；卦身有气，还是吉；应位遇上官鬼，祸必招来。注说：替别人占病，应作用爻，如果临月破、旬空，他的命难保；应如果衰弱，又遭动象或日月来伤、或应变成墓绝，就主凶危；应即使休囚，如果变为生旺、或有动爻和日月相扶，命还有救。凡是生应的爻是元神，宜动；克应的象是忌客，宜空。鬼临在应上，病一定难痊。再察卦身，也不可临月破，又不可成墓绝之乡；卦身或墓、或绝于月建之中，都是凶兆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In asking for another, look at the Other line; if it sits in Month-break, it is hardest to escape; meeting clash or control, the person is hard to save; meeting strength or generation, the illness surely dissolves; the Support that generates the Other should move, and the adversary image that controls it fears moving; if the Body has qi it is still auspicious; if Pressure meets the place of the Other, misfortune is sure to be invited. The commentary says: in divining another's illness, the Other is the Subject line; if it sits in Month-break or Void, his life is hard to keep; if the Other is weak and is wounded by a moving image or by the Day and Month, or the Other changes into a tomb or Extinction, it means danger; though the Other is resting or trapped, if it changes into generation or strength, or a moving line and the Day and Month support it, life can still be saved. Whatever generates the Other is the Support and should move; whatever controls the Other is the adversary guest and should be Void. With the ghost on the Other, the illness is surely hard to cure. Then examine the Body: it must not sit in Month-break, nor become a place of tomb or Extinction; if the Body is in a tomb or in Extinction under the Month, both are ominous signs.

## LY.ILL.EL.21 各类所占之人的用神、元神、忌神、仇神、泄气：占父母尊长——父母为用官鬼为元妻财为忌子孙为仇兄弟泄；占兄弟朋友、占子孙卑幼、占妻妾奴婢、占夫主官员各有其表；自己取世、他人取应；此法非惟占病，凡看卦无不用之

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占父母及家主尊长之类，取父母爻为用神，官鬼为元神，妻财为忌神，子孙为仇神，兄弟为泄气；占兄弟朋友之类，兄弟为用神，父母为元神，官鬼为忌神，妻财为仇神，子孙为泄气；占子孙卑幼之类，子孙为用神，兄弟为元神，父母为忌神，官鬼为仇神，妻财为泄气；占妻妾、弟媳子媳奴婢之类，妻财为用神，子孙为元神，兄弟为忌神，父母为仇神，官鬼为泄气；占丈夫、官员之类，官鬼为用神，妻财为元神，子孙为忌神，兄弟为仇神，父母为泄气；占自己取世作用爻，生世的为元神，克世的为忌神；占他人取应作用爻，生应的为元神，克应的为忌神；这个办法不光占病，凡是看卦，没有不用它的。

For example, in divining a parent the Subject is the Resource line, the Pressure line that generates it is the Support, the Wealth line that controls it is the Adversary, Output which generates Wealth is the enemy spirit, and Peer which it generates is the drain. 'In divining oneself take the Self, in divining another the Other' is the general rule; the five kinds above subdivide it by the status of the one divined. Compared with the Huangjin Ce's way of taking the Subject (LY.ILL.HJC.33), the Yi Mao's (LY.ILL.YM.21 to YM.23) and the Yi Yin's line for the Subject (LY.ILL.YY.03), its division into five kinds agrees with the usual method, but it does not go on, as the Yi Mao does, to treat seniority and closeness separately, as in 'a master, though young, takes the Resource line'. In its first item, 'a parent, or the master of the house or an elder', the master also takes the Resource line, as in the Yi Mao (LY.ILL.YM.21), and unlike the Yi Yin's 'servants divining the master of the house take the fifth line' (LY.ILL.YY.03).

## LY.ILL.YM.21 用神之法：主虽少用父母、仆虽老用妻财、宗按尊卑（称叔用父称侄用子）、表姓用应（岳父母舅父母用父）、现任用官、师傅用父

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:取用神的办法，看的是关系，不看年纪和亲疏：主人即使年纪小，用父母爻；仆人即使年纪老，用妻财爻；同宗的人即使远到五服，称叔的用父爻，称侄的用子孙爻；异姓的表亲即使近到三党之内，也用应爻，只有岳父母、舅父母才用父爻；占现任的官，即使关系亲密，也用官爻而不用应爻；占师傅，即使关系疏远，也用父爻而不用应爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The method of taking the Subject goes by the relation, not by age or closeness: a master, though young, takes the Resource line; a servant, though old, takes the Wealth line; a clansman, even as distant as the five mourning grades, takes the Resource line if addressed as uncle and the Output line if addressed as nephew; kin of another surname, even as near as the three clans, take the Other line, and only a father- or mother-in-law or a maternal uncle or aunt takes the Resource line; a serving official, however close, takes the Pressure line and not the Other; a teacher, however distant, takes the Resource line and not the Other.

Both speak of the same pair, servant and master, but take different lines. >

The note says that for a clansman one takes 'addressed as uncle, the Resource; addressed as nephew, the Output', that is, by his generation relative to oneself and however remote the blood; relatives of another surname are all taken by the Other line, and only a father- or mother-in-law or a maternal uncle or aunt counts as a senior generation and takes the Resource line.

## LY.ILL.YM.23 君臣、阍寺、将卒、师弟取用：臣占君用九五、君占臣用应爻、阍寺用财、将占卒用子孙、师弟谊用子；宗人之臣从朝廷长幼取用（太子臣用子、皇弟兄臣用弟兄、太后臣用父母）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:臣子占君主，用九五爻，不论是皇亲贵戚、异姓的臣子还是平民；宗室的官员占他自己的本主，就按本主在朝廷里的长幼辈分来取：太子的臣子占太子，用朝廷的储君当子，取子孙爻；皇帝弟兄的臣子占皇弟皇兄，用朝廷的枝叶当兄弟，取兄弟爻；太后的臣子占太后，取父母爻。君主占臣子，用应爻，因为那是辅佐我的人。对守门的宦官用财爻；将领占士卒用子孙爻，为的是制住鬼；师徒的情谊用子爻。取用神这件事，占卜的人屡屡弄错，不能不审慎；占筮的人取用神常有两种困惑，以致吉凶不灵验，所以在这里说得详细，其他的取用也照这样推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A minister divining his sovereign takes the ninth-five line, whether he is of the imperial kin, of another surname or a commoner. An official of the imperial clan divining his own lord takes by the lord's seniority at court: the Crown Prince's minister divining the Crown Prince takes the court's heir apparent as the Output line; the minister of the emperor's brothers divining an imperial brother takes the branches of the court as brothers, the Peer line; the Empress Dowager's minister divining the Empress Dowager takes the Resource line. A sovereign divining his minister takes the Other line, for he is my aide. For palace gate-keepers and eunuchs the Wealth line is taken; a general divining his soldiers takes Output, in order to control the ghost; and in the bond of teacher and pupil the Output line is taken. In taking the Subject diviners have repeatedly erred, and one cannot but be careful; diviners often fall into two kinds of confusion in taking the Subject, so that fortune and misfortune do not prove true, and therefore it is set out in detail here, and other cases may be reckoned by it.

> **按**:「阍寺」是守宫门的人和宫里的宦官。「宗人之臣，代君而用」注里解作：宗室的官员占他自己的本主，不用九五，而是照本主在朝廷里排的辈分取六亲。注里说的「两惑」没有点明是哪两种；从上下文看，一是不知道该取哪一亲，二是把年纪、亲疏当成取用的依据，前面的「主虽少而用父母」等句就是纠正后一种，这是推的，书里没有写。
>
> **Note:** 'Gate-keepers and eunuchs' are those who guard the palace gates and the eunuchs within. Of 'the ministers of the clan take in place of the sovereign', the note explains that an official of the imperial clan divining his own lord does not take the ninth-five line but takes the six relations by the lord's rank in the court's generations. The note does not say which two 'confusions' are meant; from the context they seem to be not knowing which relation to take and taking age or closeness as the ground of choice, the latter being what the earlier sentences such as 'a master, though young, takes the Resource line' correct, but this is inferred and not written in the book.

## LY.ILL.YM.33 爻变之鬼亦然：占父病鬼动于坤其母随之而疾、占子病鬼化于巽其女继之而灾；何宫鬼动及鬼变何宫，言其人亦防灾病（例：辛酉日占女巽之涣、丙辰日占次女痘革之咸）；疾病之章不离用爻鬼象

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:还有占父亲的病而鬼在坤宫发动，他的母亲跟着也生病；占儿子的病而鬼变到巽宫，他的女儿接着遭灾：这就是说，爻变出来的鬼也是同样。所以疾病这一章离不开用爻和鬼的象。注：鬼在哪一宫发动，以及鬼变到哪一宫，就说那一宫所代表的人也要提防灾病。举了三个例子：辛酉日占女儿的病，巽变涣，子孙遇长生，这个女儿不会死，但鬼动变入坎宫，次子接着生病，后来还死了；丙辰日占二女儿出痘，革变咸，子孙值月建，二女儿无事，但内卦变到艮，官鬼落在少男宫里，后来小儿子也出了痘。总之占疾病，生死靠用爻，趋避、变通、救治的门路，都在于详细考察鬼爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Again, where a father's illness is divined and the ghost moves in Kun, his mother falls ill after him; where a son's illness is divined and the ghost changes into Xun, his daughter suffers calamity in turn: so the ghost that appears by change is also the same. So the chapter on illness never leaves the Subject line and the image of the ghost. The note says: whichever palace the ghost moves in, and whichever palace it changes into, say that the person that palace stands for should also guard against calamity and illness. It gives cases: on a Xinyou day a woman's illness was divined, Xun changing into Huan, Output met Birth and the woman did not die, but the ghost moved into the Kan palace and the middle son fell ill in succession and later died; on a Bingchen day the pox of a second daughter was divined, Ge changing into Xian, Output stood on the Month and the second daughter came to no harm, but the inner trigram changed into Gen, the Pressure line sat in the palace of the youngest son, and afterwards the youngest son also had the pox. In short, in divining illness life and death rest on the Subject line, and the gates of seeking and avoiding, altering and remedying all depend on a careful examination of the Pressure line.

> **按**:这一条讲的是牵连：病的鬼落在哪一宫，那一宫代表的家人就会跟着有事——坤是母，巽是长女，坎是中男，艮是少男。与痘疹章「考六亲化鬼及鬼化六亲」（LY.ILL.YM.06）是同一路读法，只是那里是问谁会出痘，这里是问谁会跟着有病。注里第二例「及甲申旬化午子空」一句，原文的次序和断句都不清楚（甲申旬空的是午未，而不是子），这里按字面译，不改。「及甲寅旬……及甲申旬……」两个旬，说的是同一个鬼在两个旬里应验的情形不同：头一旬是有病，后一旬是死。
>
> **Note:** This entry is about knock-on effects: into whichever palace the ghost of the illness falls, the family member that palace stands for will be affected in turn — Kun is the mother, Xun the eldest daughter, Kan the middle son, Gen the youngest son. It reads in the same way as the chapter on pox's 'examine the relations that turn into ghosts and the ghosts that turn into relations' (LY.ILL.YM.06), except that there the question was who would have the pox and here it is who would fall ill in turn. In the note's second case, the sentence 'in the Jiashen decade changing into Wu with Zi Void' is unclear in both order and punctuation in the original (the Void branches of the Jiashen decade are Wu and Wei, not Zi), and is translated literally without correction. The two decades, Jiayin and Jiashen, show the same ghost coming true differently in each: illness in the first, death in the second.
