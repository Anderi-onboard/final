## LY.ILL.BSQS.12 禾苗田地章·六爻为田夫：六爻鬼克忧病疾

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占庄稼的歌里，第六爻代表种田的人，主灾祸；第六爻被官鬼克，担忧病疾。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In the verse on crops, the sixth line stands for the farmer and means calamity; if Pressure controls the sixth line, there is worry over illness.

**《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·禾苗田地章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3456 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses: the chapter on crops and fields", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3456*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 六是田夫主灾咎。 | 第六爻是种田的人，主灾祸。 | The sixth line is the farmer and means calamity. |

**《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·禾苗田地章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3456 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses: the chapter on crops and fields", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3456*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 六爻鬼克忧病疾。 | 第六爻被鬼克，担忧病疾。 | With a ghost controlling the sixth line, there is worry over illness. |

> **按**:这是占耕种的歌（禾苗田地章第四首）。歌里爻位依次是：初爻田、二爻种、三爻主长、四爻苗秀、五爻禾（收成）、六爻田夫；每一爻被鬼克都各有一句，只有六爻这一句讲到病。「六爻鬼克」是说官鬼克第六爻（和前面「初爻鬼克田瘦瘠」的句式一样）。这条是爻位配人的一个例子：六爻配的是种田的人。
>
> **Note:** This is a verse on farming (the fourth poem of the chapter on crops and fields). Its positions run: the first line is the field, the second the seed, the third the growth, the fourth the fine seedlings, the fifth the grain (the harvest), the sixth the farmer; each line controlled by a ghost has its own sentence, and only the one on the sixth line mentions illness. 'A ghost controls the sixth line' means Pressure controls the sixth line, in the same pattern as 'a ghost controls the first line: the field is lean'. This is an example of a line position matched to a person: the sixth line is matched to the farmer.

## LY.ILL.BSYG.04 月建直符·破为白虎：月内有人疾，阴女阳男；子孙兄弟父母妻财并可预断；带杀同鬼主孝服

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:月建直符这一段说：月破的那个位置是白虎，这个月里有人生病，阴是女的、阳是男的；子孙、兄弟、父母、妻财这几类亲人，都可以预先判断是谁；如果又带着杀、和鬼在一起，主孝服；在外象，是出远路的人的事。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The passage on the on-duty spirit of the Month says: the break of the Month is the White Tiger, and within that month someone is ill, yin being a woman and yang a man; the relatives of the four kinds Output, Peer, Resource and Wealth can all be judged in advance; if it also carries a killing and sits with the ghost, it means mourning dress; in the outer image it concerns a traveller on a far road.

**《卜筮元龟·六神门·月建六神吉凶》原文** — 《大易断例卜筮元龟目》第 410 行 · *Bushi Yuangui (The Great Tortoise of Divination), "The Six Spirits: Omens of the Spirits of the Month", base text; Dayi Duanli Bushi Yuangui Mu, line 410*

| 阴女阳男。 | 阴是女的，阳是男的。 | yin is a woman, yang a man. |

| 并可预断之。 | 都可以预先判断。 | all can be judged in advance. |
| 若带杀同鬼， | 如果带着杀、又和鬼在一起， | if it carries a killing and sits with the ghost, |

> **按**:「破」是和月建相冲的那个地支：这一段前面有一张月建六神表（本库没有收表），白虎在正月是申、二月是酉……正好是月建寅、卯……的对冲，所以「破为白虎」就是月破那一位是白虎。「阴女阳男」按字面译，书里没有说阴阳是指爻还是别的；「子孙兄弟父母妻财并可预断之」是说生病的是哪一类亲人，可以按这四类六亲来断。「内象」「外象」书里没有解释。「若带杀同鬼」的「鬼」可以是官鬼，也可以是鬼神，书里没有标明。这一段前面讲月建青龙的一句（婚姻、添人进口）在 LY.MARRY.BSYG.03。
>
> **Note:** 'Break' is the branch that clashes with the Month: before this passage the book has a table of the six spirits by month (the table is not included in this library), in which the White Tiger is at Shen in the first month, You in the second and so on, exactly the clash of the Month branches Yin, Mao and so on, so 'the break is the White Tiger' means the place of the Month-break is the White Tiger. 'Yin is a woman, yang a man' is translated literally and the book does not say whether yin and yang refer to lines or something else; 'Output, Peer, Resource, Wealth can all be judged in advance' says that the relation of the person who is ill can be judged by these four of the six relations. 'Inner image' and 'outer image' are not explained in the book. The 'ghost' of 'sits with the ghost' could be Pressure or a ghost of the spirits, and the book does not say which. The line before this passage on the Azure Dragon of the Month (marriage, people added to the household) is in LY.MARRY.BSYG.03.

## LY.ILL.DYTJ.38 六神与疾病：白虎属金持世及交重者为患病丧死破财；白虎内惊忧疾病；白虎临财损病忧；玄武入土家中有疾病；白虎空亡病无事

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:白虎属金，兑旺乾相，旺在秋，属阴，是西方之神；它若持世和交重，是患病、丧死、破财，附在六爻上没有不凶的。日六神内动：白虎内动，惊而忧疾病。白虎临在财上，损害、病和忧愁。玄武入土，家中有疾病。白虎空亡，病没有事。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The White Tiger belongs to Metal, strong in Dui and helped by Qian, strong in autumn, yin, and the spirit of the west; if it holds the Self or is crossed and heavy it means illness, mourning and death, and loss of wealth, and attached to the six lines it is never not ominous. The day spirit moving inside: the White Tiger moving inside frightens and worries about illness. The White Tiger on Wealth: harm, illness and worry. The Dark Tortoise entering Earth: illness in the house. The White Tiger Void: the illness comes to nothing.

> **按**:这里从六神几处的歌里把说到病的句子集在一起。头一段（「白虎属金……」）是六神各自的性质：白虎属金、旺在秋、属阴、西方之神，持世或交重时是患病、丧死、破财；后四句是别处歌诀里的单句：「日六神内动」歌的「白虎内惊忧疾病」（其后的「（1）」是今人注号，不收）、「六神有气吉凶」歌的「白虎临财损病忧」、「六神彖象歌」的「玄武入土家中有疾病」、「六神空亡」歌的「白虎空亡病无事」。「附于六爻而无不凶也」是说白虎附在哪一爻上都凶。「白虎临财损病忧」的「病」是病，「忧」是忧愁，「损」是损害，这一句字面译。这几句和天玄赋「青龙得位终见安康、白虎伤身必成凶咎」（LY.ILL.TXF.04）、火珠林的白虎临父母（LY.ILL.HZL.08）是同一类。
>
> **Note:** Here the sentences that speak of illness are gathered from several of the six-spirit songs. The first passage ('The White Tiger belongs to Metal ...') is the nature of each of the six spirits: the White Tiger belongs to Metal, is strong in autumn, yin and the spirit of the west, and holding the Self or crossed and heavy means illness, mourning and death, and loss of wealth; the last four are single lines from other songs: 'the White Tiger moving inside frightens and worries about illness' from the song of the day spirits moving inside (the '(1)' after it is a modern annotation mark and is not quoted), 'the White Tiger on Wealth: harm, illness and worry' from the song on the six spirits having qi and being lucky or ill, 'the Dark Tortoise entering Earth: illness in the house' from the song of the images of the six spirits, and 'the White Tiger Void: the illness comes to nothing' from the song of the six spirits Void. 'Attached to the six lines it is never not ominous' says the White Tiger is ominous on whichever line it is attached to. In 'the White Tiger on Wealth: harm, illness and worry' the words are rendered literally. These lines are of the same kind as the Tianxuan Fu's 'the Azure Dragon in place finally sees health, the White Tiger injuring the Body surely becomes calamity' (LY.ILL.TXF.04) and the Huozhu Lin's 'the White Tiger sitting on Resource' (LY.ILL.HZL.08).

## LY.ILL.DYTJ.41 六亲类象·疾病与医药：福德类象有医药；官鬼独发主病灾、官鬼神邪病崇盗；冲克子孙爻来情为医药

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:福德（子孙）所主的类象里，有缉捕、医药、闲人、鱼贩。官鬼是鬼爻，独发主病灾，是损失的兆；官鬼是神、邪、病、祟、盗。来情看冲克哪一爻：冲克子孙爻，是冲福瑞的物件和医药。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Among the images governed by Output (Fortune-and-Virtue) are arrests, medicine, idlers and fishmongers. The Pressure line is the ghost line, and moving alone it means illness and calamity, a sign of loss; the Pressure line is gods, evil, illness, curses and thieves. The reason for the divination is looked at by which line is clashed or controlled: a clash on the Output line is a clash with lucky things and medicine.

> **按**:这是另一套办法：不在六爻里找六亲，而是拿八卦对家人（乾父坤母，震坎艮是三个儿子，巽离兑是三个女儿）。按年龄取卦是给「用卦不出现」和代占者用的备用办法。这一条和上面六爻取用（LY.ILL.YY.03）不是一回事，书里没有说两者冲突时怎么办。「僧一行」是唐代的一行和尚，书里没有另作介绍。
>
> **Note:** This is another method: instead of looking for the six relations among the lines, the eight trigrams are matched to the family (Qian father, Kun mother, Zhen, Kan and Gen the three sons, Xun, Li and Dui the three daughters). Taking the trigram by age is a fallback for when the trigram to be used does not appear and for divining for another. It is not the same as taking the Subject among the six lines (LY.ILL.YY.03), and the book does not say what to do when the two conflict. 'The monk Yixing' is the Tang monk Yixing, whom the book does not otherwise introduce.
