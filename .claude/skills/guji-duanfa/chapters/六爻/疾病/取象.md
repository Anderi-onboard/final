# 六爻 · 疾病 · 取象

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Illness · Reading the images: colour, direction and kind of person or spirit*

2 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.ILL.YY.02 鬼谷分爻：六爻各配身体部位和一个吉凶名——六爻头脑黄泉、五爻心肺棺椁、四爻脾脏福德、三爻肝肾哭声、二爻腿股吊客、初爻足丧门

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼谷把六爻这样分：六爻是头脑，配黄泉；五爻是心肺，配棺椁；四爻是脾脏，配福德；三爻是肝肾，配哭声；二爻是腿和大腿，配吊客；初爻是足，配丧门。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Guigu divides the six lines as follows: the sixth line is the head and brain, matched with the Yellow Springs; the fifth is the heart and lungs, with the Coffin; the fourth is the spleen, with Fortune; the third is the liver and kidneys, with Wailing; the second is the legs and thighs, with the Guest of Condolence; the first is the feet, with the Mourning Gate.

**《易隐·疾病占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2978–2985 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining illness", base text; Yi Yin, lines 2978–2985*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 鬼谷分爻 | 鬼谷分爻（表）： | Guigu's division of the lines (table): |
| 头脑黄泉 | 头脑，黄泉； | head and brain, the Yellow Springs; |
| 心肺棺椁 | 心肺，棺椁； | heart and lungs, the Coffin; |
| 脾脏福德 | 脾脏，福德； | spleen, Fortune; |
| 肝肾哭声 | 肝肾，哭声； | liver and kidneys, Wailing; |
| 腿股吊客 | 腿股，吊客； | legs and thighs, the Guest of Condolence; |
| 足丧门 | 足，丧门。 | feet, the Mourning Gate. |

> **按**:表是分两栏排的，书里没有说是从哪一爻排起。按后面「又分宫」一段（初爻是足，二爻股膝，三爻腹腰，四爻胸胃乳，五爻面项手，六爻头脑），头脑在六爻、足在初爻，可知表是从六爻往下排到初爻；右栏的黄泉、棺椁、福德、哭声、吊客、丧门书里没有解释，按字面译。「福德」在这里是这一爻配的名目，不是六亲里的子孙。
>
> **Note:** The table is set out in two columns and the book does not say which line it starts from. By the later passage 'again by the division of the lines' (first line the feet, second the thighs and knees, third the belly and waist, fourth the chest, stomach and breasts, fifth the face, neck and hands, sixth the head and brain), the head is on the sixth line and the feet on the first, so the table runs from the sixth line down to the first; the right column — Yellow Springs, Coffin, Fortune, Wailing, Guest of Condolence, Mourning Gate — is not explained in the book and is rendered literally. 'Fortune' (fude) here is the name matched to that line and is not the Output line of the six relations.

## LY.ILL.YY.09 僧一行占病法：从八卦取用——父乾母坤、长男震次男坎三男艮、长女巽次女离三女兑；用卦不现或代占者按年龄：一至二十男艮女兑、二十一至四十男坎女离、四十一至六十男震女巽、六十以上男乾女坤；正卦无用象取伏卦；主象化死墓绝克破皆死

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:僧一行占病的办法，从八卦里取用：父亲用乾，母亲用坤，长子用震，次子用坎，三子用艮，长女用巽，次女用离，三女用兑。如果所用的卦不出现，或者是替别人占，就按年龄：一岁到二十岁，男的用艮，女的用兑；二十一岁到四十岁，男的用坎，女的用离；四十一岁到六十岁，男的用震，女的用巽；六十岁以上，男的用乾，女的用坤。正卦里没有用的象，就取伏卦。主象变出死、墓、绝，或者变出克它、破它的，都主死。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The monk Yixing's method of divining illness takes the Subject from the eight trigrams: father takes Qian, mother Kun, eldest son Zhen, second son Kan, third son Gen, eldest daughter Xun, second daughter Li, third daughter Dui. If the trigram to be used does not appear, or one is divining for another, go by age: from one to twenty, a male uses Gen and a female Dui; from twenty-one to forty, a male Kan and a female Li; from forty-one to sixty, a male Zhen and a female Xun; over sixty, a male Qian and a female Kun. If the main hexagram has no image to use, take the hidden hexagram. If the main image changes into Death, tomb or Extinction, or into what controls or breaks it, in every case it means death.

**《易隐·疾病占》原文** — 《易隐》第 2993 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining illness", base text; Yi Yin, line 2993*

| 六十以上者，男用乾，女用坤也。 | 六十岁以上，男的用乾，女的用坤。 | Over sixty, a male uses Qian and a female Kun. |
| 若正卦无用象者，则取伏卦。 | 如果正卦里没有用的象，就取伏卦。 | If the main hexagram has no image to use, take the hidden hexagram. |
| 若主象化死墓绝，化克破者，皆主死也。 | 如果主象变出死、墓、绝，或者变出克它、破它的，都主死。 | If the main image changes into Death, tomb or Extinction, or into what controls or breaks it, in every case it means death. |

> **按**:这是另一套办法：不在六爻里找六亲，而是拿八卦对家人（乾父坤母，震坎艮是三个儿子，巽离兑是三个女儿）。按年龄取卦是给「用卦不出现」和代占者用的备用办法。这一条和上面六爻取用（LY.ILL.YY.03）不是一回事，书里没有说两者冲突时怎么办。「僧一行」是唐代的一行和尚，书里没有另作介绍。
>
> **Note:** This is another method: instead of looking for the six relations among the lines, the eight trigrams are matched to the family (Qian father, Kun mother, Zhen, Kan and Gen the three sons, Xun, Li and Dui the three daughters). Taking the trigram by age is a fallback for when the trigram to be used does not appear and for divining for another. It is not the same as taking the Subject among the six lines (LY.ILL.YY.03), and the book does not say what to do when the two conflict. 'The monk Yixing' is the Tang monk Yixing, whom the book does not otherwise introduce.

相关:LY.ILL.YY.03、LY.ILL.YY.10
