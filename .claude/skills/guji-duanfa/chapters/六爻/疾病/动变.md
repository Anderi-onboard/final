## LY.ILL.DYTJ.28 逢冲暗动·占病：六爻安静不遇冲则不动、日辰相冲名曰暗动；凶煞暗动伤身克世百事不佳、吉神暗动合世生身事事吉；占病遇之吉则病人有默佑、凶则脉阴隙所致、病人不病须防暗有伤

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六爻安静，不遇到冲就不动；如果日辰来冲它，叫作暗动。暗动有吉有凶，各有各的用处，不可以一概而论：凶煞暗动、伤身克世，百事都不好；吉神暗动、合世生身，事事都吉。我推究它的意思：暗的一定不是明的，是阴私、潜藏的样子，福来了却不知道，祸来了却没有察觉；爻吉，暗中就有补益，爻凶，暗中就有伤害。占病遇到它：吉，就说病人有暗中的保佑；凶，就说是脉络里暗中的空隙造成的；病人若还没有发病，也要防暗中有伤。这一章宜细细玩味，就万无一失。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the six lines are still, they do not move unless they meet a clash; if the Day clashes with one, it is called a hidden movement. A hidden movement has luck and ill, each with its own use, and it cannot be judged all alike: a fierce killing moving hidden, injuring the Body and controlling the Self, is not good in all things; a lucky spirit moving hidden, combining with the Self and generating the Body, is lucky in everything. I work out its sense: what is hidden is surely not open, it is of the private and the concealed, fortune comes and one does not know it, calamity comes and one does not notice it; if the line is lucky there is help in secret, and if ominous there is injury in secret. Divining illness and meeting it: if lucky it is said the sick person has a silent protection; if ominous it is said to be caused by a hidden gap in the pulse; and if the sick person is not yet sick one must guard against a hidden injury. This chapter should be studied closely, and nothing will fail.

> **按**:「暗动」是静爻遇日辰冲而动（见增删卜易的「暗动」）。这一章占婚、占产、占官、占讼、失脱、求财、出行、行人、家宅各有一句，这一条收占病那一句。「凶则言脉阴隙所致也」的「脉阴隙」意思读不确定，「阴隙」是暗中的缝隙，这里按「脉络里暗中的空隙」字面译；「病人不病须防暗有伤」的「病人不病」按「病人还没有发病」译，原文没有解释。「吉则言病人有默佑」的「默佑」是暗中的神佑。这一节和「合处逢冲」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.26）是冲在病里的两面。「（暗动）遇凶煞伤身克世」的「身」是卦身。
>
> **Note:** A 'hidden movement' is a still line moving on meeting a clash from the Day (see the Zengshan Buyi on hidden movement). This chapter has one sentence each for marriage, childbirth, office, lawsuit, losses, wealth, travel, a traveller and the house, and this entry takes the sentence on illness. The 'mai yin xi' of 'if ominous it is said to be caused by a hidden gap in the pulse' is not certain in sense; 'yin xi' is a hidden crack, and it is rendered literally as 'a hidden gap in the pulse'; 'the sick person not sick' of 'the sick person not sick must guard against a hidden injury' is rendered 'the sick person is not yet sick', and the original does not explain it. The 'silent protection' of 'if lucky it is said the sick person has a silent protection' is protection by a spirit in secret. This section and 'a clash where there is a combination' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.26) are the two sides of a clash in illness. The 'Body' of 'injuring the Body and controlling the Self' is the hexagram Body.

## LY.ILL.DYTJ.40 六亲用变·疾病：父动当头克子孙病人无药主沉昏、父化子纵然忧病反为吉；子孙发动伤官鬼占病求医身便痊、子化官占病忧疑；财爻发动病人伤脾腹胃虚；兄弟交重克财病人难愈、兄化官占病难医须见哭；官化官病未安、官鬼病困门庭祸祟来

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母动而克子孙，病人没有药，主沉重昏迷；父母化子孙，宜退散，纵然忧病，反而是吉。子孙发动、伤克官鬼，占病求医，身体就痊愈；子孙化官鬼，要防祸患，占病的忧疑都不准。财爻发动，病人伤脾胃、腹胃虚。兄弟交重克财，病人难愈、没有离灾；兄化官，不要下状，占病难医、须见哭。官鬼发动：病困在门庭、祸祟来；官化官，病未安。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Resource line moving and controlling Output: the sick person has no medicine, and it means heaviness and stupor; Resource changing into Output: it is fit to retreat and scatter, and though there is worry about illness, it is on the contrary lucky. Output moving and injuring the Pressure line: in divining illness and seeking a doctor, the body is cured at once; Output changing into the Pressure line: guard against calamity, and the worries about illness are all off the mark. The Wealth line moving: the sick person injures the spleen and the belly and stomach are deficient. Peer crossing heavily and controlling Wealth: the sick person is hard to cure and has not left the calamity; Peer changing into the Pressure line: do not bring a suit, and divining illness it is hard to treat and weeping will be seen. The Pressure line moving: the illness is trapped at the gate, curses of calamity come; the Pressure line changing into the Pressure line: the illness is not yet at ease.

> **按**:「用变」是每一类六亲（父母、子孙、妻财、兄弟、官鬼）发动、以及它们变成别的六亲时的断语，每一类有两首歌，这里只收说到病的句子。「父动当头克子孙，病人无药主沉昏」是父母爻发动克子孙（药）：没有药，病人昏沉；「子孙发动伤官鬼，占病求医身便痊」是子孙克鬼：治病有效。「财爻发动……病人伤脾腹胃虚」的「腹胃虚」原文如此，「财」（土）克水，所以「伤脾」这里按字面译，不作解释。「兄弟交重克了财，病人难愈未离灾」是兄弟克财（禄命），病人难愈。「兄化官兮休下状，占病难医须见哭」是兄弟化官鬼。「官鬼发动，病困门庭，祸祟来」是官鬼动：病困在门庭里。这些句子多与前面几章（孙膑歌的六亲、家人占病歌 LY.ILL.DYTJ.05、DYTJ.08）是同一个意思。父母、子孙的用变在求财里也有（LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.32 一带）。
>
> **Note:** 'Uses by change' are the judgments when each kind of relation (Resource, Output, Wealth, Peer, Pressure) moves and when it changes into another relation, with two songs for each kind, and here only the sentences that speak of illness are taken. 'The Resource line moving and controlling Output head on: the sick person has no medicine, heaviness and stupor' is Resource moving and controlling Output (the medicine): with no medicine the sick person is in a stupor; 'Output moving and injuring the Pressure line: in divining illness and seeking a doctor the body is cured at once' is Output controlling the ghost: the treatment is effective. In 'the Wealth line moving ... the sick person injures the spleen and the belly and stomach are deficient' the 'belly and stomach are deficient' is as in the original, and it is rendered literally with no explanation. 'Peer crossing heavily and controlling Wealth: the sick person is hard to cure and has not left the calamity' is Peer controlling Wealth (salary and life): the sick person is hard to cure. 'Peer changing into the Pressure line: do not bring a suit, divining illness it is hard to treat and weeping will be seen' is Peer changing into the Pressure line. 'The Pressure line moving, the illness trapped at the gate, curses of calamity come' is the Pressure line moving: the illness is trapped in the gate and courtyard. These sentences mostly agree with the earlier chapters (the six relations in Sun Bin's song and the verses on a family member, LY.ILL.DYTJ.05 and DYTJ.08). The uses by change of Resource and Output are also in the wealth domain (around LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.32).

## LY.ILL.EL.09 用象强弱与变化：用象虽弱逢日月生扶无咎；用静不必细详，用动推变化（变生扶吉、变墓绝凶）；用爻有气元神动忌神纵发不须惊；主象休囚元神静忌神动或被月日伐则后必伤躯；用象临旺遇生扶有起死回生之兆
