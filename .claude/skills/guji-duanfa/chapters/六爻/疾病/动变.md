## LY.ILL.HJC.09 官化官新旧两病，鬼化鬼迁变百端：官鬼化进神病增、化退神病减

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼爻化出官鬼爻，是新旧两种病；鬼爻化出鬼爻，病情迁变，花样百出。旧注说：本宫官鬼还伏在别宫官鬼下面，是旧病，或者旧病没有谨慎、变出别的病，或者新旧两种病；鬼化鬼是病有进有退，或者变了症候，或者药石杂乱，或者忽冷忽热。王洪绪补充：官鬼化进神病加重，化退神病减轻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure changing into Pressure means two illnesses, new and old; Pressure changing into Pressure shifts and changes in a hundred ways. The old commentary says: if the home palace's Pressure is still hidden beneath another palace's Pressure, it is an old illness, or through carelessness with the old illness another disease has grown from it, or it is two kinds of illness, new and old; Pressure changing into Pressure means the illness advances and retreats, or the syndrome changes, or the drugs and needles are muddled, or chills and fever are uncertain. Wang Hongxu adds: Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit, the illness grows; into a Retreating spirit, it lessens.

**《黄金策·疾病》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6792 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6792*

## LY.ILL.HJC.23 鬼在死绝其病本轻，逢日辰动爻生扶是绝处逢生，病要好了又重、安而复作；财生鬼则因饮食或女色加重

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼爻落在死、绝的爻位上，病本来轻。遇到日辰或动爻来生扶，叫绝处逢生，病一定是先轻后重、快好了又犯。如果是财爻生鬼，一定是因为饮食使病加重，或者因为又和女人同房。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Pressure line on a death or extinction position means a light illness. When the Day or a moving line generates or supports it, this is called meeting life in a place of extinction, and the illness is surely light and then heavy again, settling and then flaring. If Wealth generates Pressure, the illness is surely made heavier by food and drink, or by being with a woman again.

**《黄金策·疾病》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6839 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6839*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 鬼绝逢生，病体安而复作； | 鬼爻在绝处逢到生，病体安稳了又发作； | A Pressure line meeting life where it was extinct: the body settles and then the illness starts again; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6840 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6840*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 遇日辰动爻生扶，谓绝处逢生，其病必既轻复重，将安复作也。 | 遇到日辰或动爻来生扶，叫绝处逢生，这病一定是轻了又重，快好了又犯。 | If it meets the Day or a moving line that generates or supports it, this is called meeting life in a place of extinction; the illness is surely light and then heavy again, on the point of settling and then flaring. |
| 若财生鬼，必因饮食加重，或因妇人重合。 | 如果是财爻生鬼，一定是因为吃喝使病加重，或者因为又和女人同房。 | If Wealth generates Pressure, the illness is surely made heavier by food and drink, or by being with a woman again. |
| 故耳。 | 就是这个缘故。 | That is the reason. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2341 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2341*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 官鬼逢绝，其病必轻，如遇生扶，谓之绝处逢生，其病必将复作。 | 官鬼逢绝，这病一定轻，如果再遇上生扶，叫绝处逢生，这病一定还要犯。 | A Pressure line meeting extinction: the illness is surely light; if it then meets generation or support, this is called meeting life in a place of extinction, and the illness will surely flare again. |

> **按**:「妇人重合」的「重合」字面是「再合」。财爻既管饮食，又是男人的妻和女人，旧注这一句按上下文读作又和女人同房；书里没有再解释。「财生鬼」这一句王洪绪没有收。
>
> **Note:** 'Chong he' in 'a woman, chong he' literally means 'coming together again'. Wealth governs food and drink and is also the wife and woman for a man, so the old commentary is read from the context as being with a woman again; the book does not explain it further. Wang Hongxu does not take the sentence on 'Wealth generating Pressure'.
