## LY.ILL.BSQS.06 六亲发动诀·疾病：父动克子孙病人无药昏沉；子孙发动伤官鬼占病求医即痊；官鬼发动克兄弟病因门庭祸祟；财爻发动克文书病人伤胃伤脾；兄弟交重克财病人难愈

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六亲发动诀里讲病的句子：父母爻发动，当头克子孙，病人没有药可用，昏沉；子孙爻发动伤官鬼，占病求医，人就痊愈；官鬼爻发动克兄弟，病因是门庭里有祸祟；财爻发动克文书，病人伤胃，更伤脾；兄弟交重克了财，病人难愈，灾没有离开。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The lines on illness in the verse on the six relations moving: with Resource moving and controlling Output head-on, the sick person has no medicine to use and sinks into stupor; with Output moving and injuring Pressure, in divining illness and seeking a doctor one is cured at once; with Pressure moving and controlling Peer, the cause of the illness is misfortune from the gate and courtyard; with Wealth moving and controlling documents, the sick person has harm to the stomach and still more to the spleen; with Peer moving together and controlling Wealth, the sick person is hard to cure and has not left misfortune.

## LY.ILL.BSQS.07 六亲变化歌·疾病：子孙化鬼忧病产；官鬼化财占病凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六亲变化歌里讲病的两句：子孙爻动，变出官鬼，担忧疾病和生产；官鬼爻动，变出财爻，占病主凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The two lines on illness in the verse on what each relation turns into: when the Output line moves and turns into Pressure, worry over illness and childbirth; when the Pressure line moves and turns into Wealth, divining illness is ominous.

**《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·二十六、六亲变化歌》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3211 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses, no. 26: what each relation turns into", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3211*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 子孙化子孙， | 子孙爻化出子孙， | Output turning into Output, |
| 人财两称情； | 人和财两样都称心； | both people and money are as wished; |
| 化父田蚕旺， | 化出父母，田地蚕桑兴旺； | turning into Resource, fields and silkworms flourish; |
| 化财加倍荣； | 化出财，加倍荣盛； | turning into Wealth, doubly prosperous; |
| 化鬼忧病产， | 化出官鬼，担忧疾病和生产； | turning into Pressure, worry over illness and childbirth; |
| 兄弟必相争。 | 化出兄弟，必定互相争夺。 | turning into Peer, there will surely be quarrelling between brothers. |

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 官化官为禄， | 官爻化出官爻，是得俸禄， | Pressure turning into Pressure is a salary, |
| 求官宜疾速； | 求官宜要快； | in seeking office one should be quick; |
| 化财占病凶， | 化出财，占病主凶； | turning into Wealth, divining illness is ominous; |

> **按**:歌里每一句的格式是「甲化乙」：甲爻发动，变出乙爻（动是事情的开头，变是事情的结局）。全诀按「父母化」「子孙化」「官化」「妻财化」「兄弟化」一段一段排，这里只收有病的两段，整段带着。「化鬼忧病产」的「产」是生产。
>
> **Note:** Every line of the verse has the form 'A turns into B': line A moves and changes into line B (the moving line is the start of the matter, the changed line its end). The whole verse runs in stanzas on Resource, Output, Pressure, Wealth and Peer turning; only the two stanzas with illness in them are taken, each complete. In 'turning into Pressure, worry over illness and childbirth' the word rendered 'childbirth' is giving birth.

## LY.ILL.BSQS.08 六神歌断·白虎：交重丧恶事、官司病患必成凶；持金坐世妨人口，遇火临空便不同

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The White Tiger stanza in the verse judging by the six spirits: with the White Tiger line moving, there is mourning and ill fortune, and lawsuits and illness are surely ominous; if it is Metal and sits on the Self line it harms the household members; if it meets Fire or falls Void, things are different.

The verse has six stanzas, one for each spirit; only the White Tiger is taken here.

## LY.ILL.BSQS.09 月建直符·白虎：对宫为白虎凡占当忌，外动有忧惊、内摇生疾苦

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:月建直符里说：月建对宫的那一位是白虎，凡是占问都要避忌它；它在外卦发动主忧虑惊恐，在内卦发动生出疾病和痛苦。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The verse on the Month as duty-star says: the palace opposite the Month is the White Tiger and is to be shunned in any divination; moving outside it means anxiety and fright, moving inside it brings illness and suffering.

**《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·二十四、月建直符》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3193 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses, no. 24: the Month as duty-star", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3193*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 对宫为白虎， | 对宫是白虎， | The opposite palace is the White Tiger, |
| 凡占当忌取； | 凡是占问都应当避忌它； | to be shunned in any divination; |
| 外动有忧惊， | 在外发动，有忧虑惊恐； | moving outside, there is anxiety and fright; |
| 内摇生疾苦。 | 在内发动，生出疾病痛苦。 | moving inside, it brings illness and suffering. |

> **按**:月建直符是另一种排六神的办法：拿当月的月建作青龙，月建往前数三位是朱雀，往后数三位是玄武，月建对冲的那一宫（对宫）是白虎，再往后一位是勾陈，螣蛇从正月的辰起、每月逆行一位。歌里的「内」「外」指内卦、外卦。这里的「对宫」就是月建对冲的那个地支，也就是月破的位置；歌没有说这一点，是从前面几句的排法读出来的。原书在歌后加了一句小注：「正月寅爻动是也，主管一月之事」，说的是月建所在的那一爻发动，管这个月的事。
>
> **Note:** The Month as duty-star is another way of setting the six spirits: the Month branch of the current month is the Azure Dragon, three places ahead of it is the Vermilion Bird, three places behind is the Dark Tortoise, the palace opposite the Month branch (the clash) is the White Tiger, one place further back is Hook Earth, and the Coiling Snake starts from Chen in the first month and moves one place backward each month. 'Inside' and 'outside' in the verse are the inner and outer trigrams. Here 'the opposite palace' is the branch that clashes the Month, which is where the Month-break falls; the verse does not say so, and this is read from the way the earlier lines set things out. The original adds a small note after the verse: 'in the first month it is a moving Yin line, and it governs that month's affairs', meaning the line in the Month's place moves and governs the matters of that month.

## LY.ILL.BSQS.21 通玄妙论·逢冲暗动：占家宅暗动吉则暗中有补、凶则暗中有害；占病亦随其所遇之神而定其吉凶

## LY.ILL.DYTJ.28 逢冲暗动·占病：六爻安静不遇冲则不动、日辰相冲名曰暗动；凶煞暗动伤身克世百事不佳、吉神暗动合世生身事事吉；占病遇之吉则病人有默佑、凶则脉阴隙所致、病人不病须防暗有伤

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六爻安静，不遇到冲就不动；如果日辰来冲它，叫作暗动。暗动有吉有凶，各有各的用处，不可以一概而论：凶煞暗动、伤身克世，百事都不好；吉神暗动、合世生身，事事都吉。我推究它的意思：暗的一定不是明的，是阴私、潜藏的样子，福来了却不知道，祸来了却没有察觉；爻吉，暗中就有补益，爻凶，暗中就有伤害。占病遇到它：吉，就说病人有暗中的保佑；凶，就说是脉络里暗中的空隙造成的；病人若还没有发病，也要防暗中有伤。这一章宜细细玩味，就万无一失。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the six lines are still, they do not move unless they meet a clash; if the Day clashes with one, it is called a hidden movement. Divining illness and meeting it: if lucky it is said the sick person has a silent protection; if ominous it is said to be caused by a hidden gap in the pulse; and if the sick person is not yet sick one must guard against a hidden injury. This chapter should be studied closely, and nothing will fail.

## LY.ILL.EL.09 用象强弱与变化：用象虽弱逢日月生扶无咎；用静不必细详，用动推变化（变生扶吉、变墓绝凶）；用爻有气元神动忌神纵发不须惊；主象休囚元神静忌神动或被月日伐则后必伤躯；用象临旺遇生扶有起死回生之兆

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用象虽然落在弱地，如果遇上日、月来生扶，一定没有灾咎；用象如果安静，不必细究，如果发动，就必须推它的变化，变出生扶就吉，变成墓、绝就凶。歌诀说：用爻有气、元神发动，忌神纵然发动也不必惊慌；主象休囚，又加上被克被破，身体即使暂时没病也会倾覆；用神旺相又遇扶助，病即使到了危险的地步，反而主生。注说：得到用爻旺相、元神又动，日、月即使来伤它，也永远不受克，忌神虽然发动，也不成灾殃；主象如果休囚、元神又静，忌神又正在发动、或者用爻被月建、日辰所伐，身体虽然只是小病，后来一定伤及身躯；用象如果落在旺地又遇生扶，一定有起死回生的征兆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Though the Subject image sits in a weak place, if the Day and Month generate and support it, there is surely no fault; if the Subject is still, there is no need to examine it closely, and if it moves, one must work out its change, a change into generation or support being auspicious and a change into a tomb or Extinction ominous. The verse says: with the Subject line in qi and the Support moving, even if the Adversary moves there is no need for alarm; with the chief image resting or trapped and also controlled and broken, though the body is for the moment unharmed it will still collapse; with the Subject strong and meeting support, though the illness reaches danger it on the contrary means life. The commentary says: if the Subject line is strong and the Support moves too, then even if the Day and Month come to wound it, it is never controlled, and though the Adversary moves, it does no harm; if the chief image is resting or trapped, the Support is still, and the Adversary is moreover moving or the Subject is attacked by the Month or Day, then though the body has only a minor illness, in the end the body will be harmed; if the Subject image is in a strong place and meets generation and support, there is surely a sign of rising from death to life.

## LY.ILL.EL.12 忌变生而用变克，分毫之疾恐伤刑；用之旺者忌之绝，沉重之灾即刻轻：疾病卦中忌神旺动又变生扶、主象衰摇化成死绝、元神克破则无救；用神居衰位而化出帝旺长生、忌神动化克伤死绝之乡则旦夕可安

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:忌神变生、用神变克，一丝一毫的病也恐怕要伤刑；用神旺、忌神绝，沉重的灾也立刻转轻。像这样断定，可以留传万古，何须再到海外去求明白。注说：疾病卦里，忌神旺又发动、又变出生扶，主象衰弱摇动、变成死绝，元神又被克破，怎么还有救？用神即使居于衰位，如果化出帝旺、长生，又遇上扶助，忌神虽然发动而变成克伤死绝之乡，不能制用神，病虽然沉重，旦夕可以安好，这样推没有差错。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Adversary changes into generation and the Subject changes into control, even the slightest ailment may bring harm; if the Subject is strong and the Adversary in Extinction, a heavy calamity lightens at once. What is decided thus can be handed down for ten thousand ages; why then seek clarity overseas? The commentary says: in an illness hexagram, if the Adversary is strong and moving and also changes into generation and support, the chief image is weak and swaying and changes into death or Extinction, and the Support is controlled and broken, how could there be rescue? Though the Subject sits in a weak place, if it changes into Peak or Birth and meets support, then although the Adversary moves and changes into a place of control, wounding, death or Extinction and cannot restrain the Subject, the illness, however heavy, may be at ease by morning or night, and there is no error in reckoning so.

> **Note:** This entry is another way of putting LY.ILL.HJC.45 in the chapter on the patient's condition (Output changing into an Adversary line, the illness worse after a slight improvement): the Huangjin Ce speaks of the patient becoming careless as soon as he is a little better, while the Old Man speaks of Output moving and changing into a control, so that the illness breaks out again after he has recovered, and he also counts the Reversed Chant hexagram.
