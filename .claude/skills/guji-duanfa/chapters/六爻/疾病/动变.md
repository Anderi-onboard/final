## LY.ILL.HJC.09 官化官新旧两病，鬼化鬼迁变百端：官鬼化进神病增、化退神病减

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼爻化出官鬼爻，是新旧两种病；鬼爻化出鬼爻，病情迁变，花样百出。旧注说：本宫官鬼还伏在别宫官鬼下面，是旧病，或者旧病没有谨慎、变出别的病，或者新旧两种病；鬼化鬼是病有进有退，或者变了症候，或者药石杂乱，或者忽冷忽热。王洪绪补充：官鬼化进神病加重，化退神病减轻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pressure changing into Pressure means two illnesses, new and old; Pressure changing into Pressure shifts and changes in a hundred ways. The old commentary says: if the home palace's Pressure is still hidden beneath another palace's Pressure, it is an old illness, or through carelessness with the old illness another disease has grown from it, or it is two kinds of illness, new and old; Pressure changing into Pressure means the illness advances and retreats, or the syndrome changes, or the drugs and needles are muddled, or chills and fever are uncertain. Wang Hongxu adds: Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit, the illness grows; into a Retreating spirit, it lessens.

## LY.ILL.HJC.23 鬼在死绝其病本轻，逢日辰动爻生扶是绝处逢生，病要好了又重、安而复作；财生鬼则因饮食或女色加重

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼爻落在死、绝的爻位上，病本来轻。遇到日辰或动爻来生扶，叫绝处逢生，病一定是先轻后重、快好了又犯。如果是财爻生鬼，一定是因为饮食使病加重，或者因为又和女人同房。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Pressure line on a death or extinction position means a light illness. When the Day or a moving line generates or supports it, this is called meeting life in a place of extinction, and the illness is surely light and then heavy again, settling and then flaring. If Wealth generates Pressure, the illness is surely made heavier by food and drink, or by being with a woman again.

**《黄金策·疾病》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6839 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6839*

## LY.ILL.HJC.43 鬼化长生日下沉重：鬼动病重，鬼化入长生（或在长生爻上发动）一日重似一日，直过帝旺日才轻减；化衰病则一日轻似一日，福化长生也吉

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼爻发动，病势一定重。如果鬼变入长生，就是一天比一天重的象，一直要过了帝旺的日子，才会减轻；鬼在长生的爻上发动，也是这样。如果变入衰、病，就是一天比一天轻，和子孙（福）变入长生一样，都吉。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When a Pressure line moves, the illness is surely heavy. If Pressure changes into Birth, it is the image of growing heavier each day, and it is only after the day of its Peak has passed that it lightens; a Pressure line moving while on a Birth line is the same. If it changes into Decline or Sickness, it grows lighter each day, and this, like Output changing into Birth, is all lucky.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6916 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6916*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 鬼化长生，日下正当沉重； | 鬼爻变到长生，眼下正是沉重的时候； | Pressure changing into Birth: at present the illness is at its heaviest; |

**旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)** — 《卜筮大全》第 6917 行 · *Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown); Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6917*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 鬼爻发动，病势必重。 | 鬼爻发动，病势一定重。 | When a Pressure line moves, the illness is surely heavy. |
| 若鬼化入长生，乃一日重一日之象，直过帝旺日，然后轻减。 | 如果鬼变入长生，是一天比一天重的象，一直要过了帝旺的日子，才会减轻。 | If Pressure changes into Birth, it is the image of growing heavier each day, and only after the Peak day has passed does it lighten. |
| 鬼在长生爻上发动，亦然。 | 鬼在长生的爻上发动，也是这样。 | A Pressure line moving while on a Birth line is the same. |
| 若化衰病，一日轻一日，与福化长生皆吉。 | 如果变入衰、病，一天比一天轻，和子孙（福）变入长生一样，都吉。 | If it changes into Decline or Sickness, it grows lighter each day; and this, like Output changing into Birth, is all lucky. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2384 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2384*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 鬼爻发动，病势必重，若鬼化入长生，乃一日重一日之象。 | 鬼爻发动，病势一定重，如果鬼变入长生，是一天比一天重的象。 | When a Pressure line moves, the illness is surely heavy; if Pressure changes into Birth, it is the image of growing heavier each day. |

> **按**:长生是十二长生的第一位（长生、沐浴、冠带、临官、帝旺、衰、病、死、墓、绝、胎、养），帝旺是第五位。鬼变入长生，是鬼在往盛的方向走，病就一天天重下去，一直到它旺到顶才回头。旧注还讲了变入衰、病是一天轻一天的相反情形；王洪绪只收了前半。
>
> **Note:** Birth is the first of the twelve life stages (Birth, Bath, Cap, Office, Peak, Decline, Sickness, Death, Tomb, Extinction, Womb, Nurture) and Peak is the fifth. A Pressure line changing into Birth is Pressure moving toward strength, so the illness grows heavier day by day until Pressure reaches its peak and turns back. The old commentary also gives the opposite case, changing into Decline or Sickness, where it grows lighter each day; Wang Hongxu takes only the first half.

## LY.ILL.HJC.45 福化忌爻病势增加于小愈：子孙发动制伏官鬼病必减，若化出忌爻反来伤克、或子孙化官爻，必因病稍好不谨慎而复重

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙发动制伏官鬼，病一定减轻；如果子孙变出忌爻反过来伤克，或者子孙变成官爻，一定是病稍微好了一点、不谨慎，结果又加重。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When Output moves and restrains Pressure the illness surely lessens; but if Output changes into an Adversary line that turns back to harm, or Output changes into a Pressure line, it is surely because the illness has slightly eased and the patient is not careful, so that it grows heavy again.

## LY.ILL.HJC.51 动化父来冲克劳役堪忧：动爻变出父母（文书）冲克世爻要少劳碌否则病复重；变财冲克防食复（加玄武防色复）、变子孙冲克被药误、变兄弟冲克恼气即复；日辰带刑冲则不依此断

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦里的动爻变出父母（文书）来冲克世爻，要自己放宽心、少劳碌，不然病马上复发，反而加重。变出财爻来冲克，要提防饮食引起复发；再加玄武，要提防女色引起复发；变出子孙来冲克，是被药耽误了；变出兄弟来冲克，一生气就复发。以上这些，如果日辰带着刑、冲，就不能照这个断，只能说是因为这个而又加重，因为日辰上就是鬼。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When a moving line in the hexagram changes out the Resource line (documents) that clashes or controls the Self line, he should keep at ease and toil little, or the illness returns at once and grows heavier. If it changes out Wealth that clashes or controls, beware of a relapse through food; with the Dark Tortoise added, beware of a relapse through lust; if it changes out Output that clashes or controls, he has been misled by medicine; if it changes out Peer that clashes or controls, anger brings the relapse. If in all this the Day carries punishment or clash, one may not judge this way: one can say only that it grows heavier because of this, for the Day is Pressure there.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6944 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6944*
