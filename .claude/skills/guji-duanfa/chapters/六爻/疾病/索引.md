## 总则(Principles: whose illness, which line stands for the patient, strong and weak)→ `总则.md`

- **LY.ILL.HJC.01** 占病时把官鬼爻当作凶恶的煞：病的症状轻重、得病的根由，都看这一爻；卦里只有一爻独自发动的，也可以照它来推。(《黄金策》)
  In divining illness the Pressure line is taken as the malign killer: how heavy or light the symptoms are, and how the illness was contracted, are both read from this line; a line that moves alone in the hexagram can also be reasoned from in the same way.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.33** 再看主象，也就是用神，就是病人：占丈夫用官鬼，占妻子用妻财。旧注还说，自己占以世爻为主象，替别人占以应爻为主象。主象怕遇到刑、冲、克、害，那就是病人被病折磨；被克害的地方如果得到生扶（旧注：得世爻生合、旁边的爻扶持），一定不至于死。(《黄金策》)
  Next look at the main image, which is the Subject and stands for the patient: in divining a husband take Pressure, in divining a wife take Wealth. The old commentary adds that in a divination for oneself the Self line is the main image, and in a divination for another the Other line is. The main image fears meeting punishment, clash, control or harm, which is the patient being ground down by the illness; but if the place of harm is generated and supported (the old commentary: by the Self line generating or combining with it and the lines beside it supporting it), he will surely not die.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.14** 卦里没有用神，而日、月作了用神的，不必去找伏神，断他马上就好；伏神衰弱的，应当再占一次。凡是用神不出现，都应当再占。(《增删卜易》)
  When the hexagram has no Subject but the Day or Month acts as the Subject, one need not look for the hidden line; promise recovery at once. If the hidden line is weak, one should divine again. Whenever the Subject does not appear, one should divine again.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.15** 一个卦看不清、或者问到应期的时候，不要硬断，叫问卜的人再占几个卦，把几个卦合起来详细看，吉凶发生在哪个月就能看出来。(《增删卜易》)
  When one hexagram is unclear, or when the time of fulfilment is asked, do not press a judgment: have the asker divine several more hexagrams and read them together in detail, and the month in which good or ill fortune falls can be known.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.22** 占病时不靠猜症候，先单占一卦问吉凶：卦吉就说调理调理就好，不必吃药求神；卦凶就叫他请医生；卦里鬼动克用神的，再占鬼神，或者占家宅、占坟墓，一定要弄清楚是犯了什么、用什么办法治。拿五行脏腑、六神去猜发冷发热这类症候，病人本来就知道，对他没有用处。(《增删卜易》)
  In divining illness do not rely on guessing the symptoms, but first divine a hexagram on good or ill fortune alone: if it is lucky, say that with care he will recover and there is no need for medicine or prayer; if ill, tell him to call a doctor; if the hexagram shows Pressure moving to control the Subject, divine the ghosts and spirits again, or the house or the tomb, and be sure to find out what he has offended and how to treat it. To guess symptoms such as chills and fever from the five phases, the organs and the six spirits tells the patient nothing he does not already know, and is of no use to him.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.28** 野鹤再拿船上被风阻住的例子来说明：子孙是解忧的神。占什么时候顺风，别人按木爻、兄弟爻断风云，他看的是子孙：子孙持世又变空，连着几天风雨，等它出空那天才晴，再等到被日辰合住的地方被冲开的那天才顺风。他从前也按木、兄弟去断，屡试不验，后来看到子孙值日开船就顺风的比比皆是，才悟出这个道理，和疾病篇把子孙当解忧之神是一个理。(《增删卜易》)
  The Old Man takes the case of a boat held up by the wind to show that Output is the spirit that relieves worry. In divining when the wind would be fair, another judged the wind and cloud by the Wood and Peer lines, but he looked at Output: Output holding the Self line and changing into Void meant wind and rain for several days, clearing on the day it leaves the Void, and a fair wind only on the day the place held by the Day's combination is clashed open. He too used to judge by Wood and Peer and it failed again and again, and only after seeing that boats setting sail on a day when Output rules the day have fair wind time after time did he come to understand the principle, which is the same as taking Output as the spirit that relieves worry in the chapter on illness.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.39** 官鬼是忧神、子孙是喜悦解忧之神，虽然是古法，用起来很恰当；但也有不把鬼当忧神的，全看来问的人心里想问什么。他问病的吉凶：鬼爻持世、鬼在卦里发动，是忧神；子孙持世、子孙发动，是解忧喜悦的神。他问病是犯了哪路鬼神：卦里出现金鬼，一定冲犯了武神；水鬼，一定得罪了河神这一类。他问病源：卦里出现火鬼，一定属心经；水鬼，一定在肾部。(《增删卜易》)
  That the Pressure line is the spirit of worry and Output the spirit of joy that relieves worry, though an old method, is very apt in use; but there are cases in which Pressure is not taken as the spirit of worry, and it all depends on what the asker has in mind to ask. If he asks about the fortune of the illness: Pressure holding the Self line or moving in the hexagram is the spirit of worry, and Output holding the Self line or moving is the spirit of joy that relieves worry. If he asks which ghost or spirit the illness offends: a Metal Pressure appearing in the hexagram means he has surely offended a martial god, and a Water Pressure that he has offended the river god and the like. If he asks about the source of the illness: a Fire Pressure appearing in the hexagram surely belongs to the Heart channel, and a Water Pressure surely lies in the Kidney.

## 症候(Symptoms: which organ or part is ill, by phase, trigram, position and spirit)→ `症候.md`

- **LY.ILL.HJC.02** 官鬼爻的五行，定哪一脏受病：火是心经（发热、咽干、口燥），水是肾（怕冷、盗汗、遗精白浊），金是肺（咳嗽、虚怯、气喘），木是肝（感冒风寒、四肢不和），土是脾胃（虚黄、浮肿、瘟疫时气）。鬼爻衰弱病轻、旺相病重；静则安卧，动则煎熬躁急。(《黄金策》)
  The phase of the Pressure line fixes which organ is ill: Fire is the Heart channel (fever, dry throat, dry mouth), Water the Kidney (aversion to cold, night sweats, seminal emission and cloudy urine), Metal the Lung (cough, weak timidity, panting), Wood the Liver (catching wind-cold, limbs out of harmony), Earth the Spleen and Stomach (sallow weakness, swelling, epidemic and seasonal disease). A weak Pressure line means a light illness and a strong one a heavy illness; still, the patient lies quiet, and moving, he is tormented and restless.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.03** 官鬼爻在坎宫，病是中满、血虚，兼有湿毒；在离卦，病是内虚、眼红、尿黄。旧注分得更细：坎宫属水，火鬼主血虚、水鬼主湿毒；离宫属火，火鬼主眼红、水鬼主尿黄。(《黄金策》)
  With the Pressure line in the Kan palace, the illness is fullness in the middle and blood deficiency, together with damp toxin; in the Li hexagram it is inner deficiency, red eyes and yellow urine. The old commentary divides it more finely: the Kan palace belongs to Water, where a Fire Pressure means blood deficiency and a Water Pressure damp toxin; the Li palace belongs to Fire, where a Fire Pressure means red eyes and a Water Pressure yellow urine.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.04** 鬼爻落在哪一宫，病就在哪个部位：坤是腹、乾是头，兑是喉风咳嗽，艮和震是手足，巽是瘫痪、肠风。以鬼在坤宫为例：火鬼腹痈，水鬼腹痛，动化财爻或财化水鬼是泻痢，土鬼是癖块食积或沙胀蛊症，木鬼是绞肠痧痛或大肠有病，金鬼是胁肋痛（在上胸痛、在下腰痛）。别的卦照此推。(《黄金策》)
  Where the Pressure line falls in the palaces fixes where the illness is: Kun is the belly and Qian the head; Dui is throat wind and cough; Gen and Zhen are the hand and foot; Xun is paralysis and intestinal wind. Taking Pressure in the Kun palace as the example: a Fire Pressure is an abdominal abscess, a Water Pressure pain in the belly; moving and changing into a Wealth line, or Wealth changing into a Water Pressure, is diarrhea and dysentery; an Earth Pressure is abdominal masses and food accumulation, or sha bloating and gu-swelling; a Wood Pressure is twisting colic or trouble in the large intestine; a Metal Pressure is pain in the flanks (chest if high, loins if low). The other palaces follow by analogy.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.05** 官鬼爻临着哪个六神，就是哪一种病：螣蛇鬼坐卧不安、心神不定；青龙鬼酒色过度、瘦弱无力；勾陈鬼胸腹胀满、脾胃不和；朱雀鬼狂言乱语、身热面赤；白虎鬼跌打损伤，女人则血崩血晕、产后各症（白虎是血神）；玄武鬼色欲过度、忧闷在心，在本宫主阴虚。最后一句叫人变通着断。(《黄金策》)
  Which of the six spirits the Pressure line sits on fixes the kind of illness: Coiling Snake Pressure, unable to sit or lie at ease and the mind unsettled; Azure Dragon Pressure, excess of wine and lust, thin and without strength; Hook Earth Pressure, fullness and swelling, Spleen and Stomach out of harmony; Vermilion Bird Pressure, wild talk, hot body and red face; White Tiger Pressure, falls and blows and injury, and in a woman flooding and blood-faintness and the ailments after childbirth (the White Tiger is the spirit of blood); Dark Tortoise Pressure, excess of lust and gloom pent in the heart, and in the home palace yin deficiency. The last sentence tells one to judge flexibly.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.14** 卦里水爻动化火、火爻动化水，不论是不是鬼爻，只要冲犯世爻或主象的，都是忽冷忽热的症候；水、火两爻都动，也是这样。水旺火衰是冷多热少；水受伤、火得助，是常热而偶尔发冷。坎宫火动是里冷外热，离宫水动是皮冷骨热。再带上日辰，一定是疟疾。(《黄金策》)
  In the hexagram, a Water line moving and changing into Fire, or a Fire line changing into Water, whether or not it is the Pressure line, if it offends the Self line or the main image, is a syndrome of alternating chills and fever; if a Water line and a Fire line both move, it is the same. Water strong and Fire weak means more chill and less fever; Water harmed and Fire helped means constant heat with an occasional chill. Fire moving in the Kan palace is cold inside and hot outside; Water moving in the Li palace is cold skin and hot bones. If it also carries the Day, it is surely malaria.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.15** 上下卦都有鬼，病一定是里外两处感受。两个鬼都动、或者都静，是一起受病；两个鬼互相冲，刚感受了邪就受了伤。内卦的鬼动来冲外卦的鬼，是先受伤后感邪；外卦的鬼动来冲内卦的鬼，是先感邪后受伤。要分轻重，用刑、合来断。(《黄金策》)
  With Pressure in both the upper and lower trigrams, the illness is surely a double contagion of inside and outside. Both Pressure lines moving, or both still, means falling ill together; two Pressure lines clashing each other means hardly is the evil felt than harm is done. If the inner Pressure moves and clashes the outer, it is first harm and then contagion; if the outer Pressure moves and clashes the inner, first contagion and then harm. To tell heavy from light, judge by punishment and combination.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.16** 火的性子向上，财爻代表饮食。占病遇到火鬼动来克外卦的财爻，一定是呕吐，重的是反胃、吃不下饭。财爻带着螣蛇，吐出来的还带虫；财爻在间爻，是消渴、容易饿的症候。(《黄金策》)
  Fire's nature is to flare upward and Wealth stands for food and drink. In divining illness, a Fire Pressure moving to control the outer Wealth line is surely vomiting, and when heavy, a stomach that turns and cannot take food. If Wealth carries the Coiling Snake, worms are brought up as well; if Wealth is on the between line, it is the syndrome of thirst and quick hunger.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.17** 水行的官鬼动了，变出土来回头克它，又落在本宫的内卦（下三爻）：小便不通；这一爻属阴，大便也不通；阳宫里是阴的象、阴宫里是阳的象，大小便都不通。内卦的水爻起克、又动、又加白虎：阳爻是尿血，阴爻是泻血（白虎是血神）；再带刑是痔漏。(《黄金策》)
  A Water Pressure line that moves and changes into Earth, which turns back to control it, and falls in the inner trigram of the home palace (the lower three lines): the urine is blocked; if the line is yin, the stool is blocked too; a yang palace with a yin image, or a yin palace with a yang image, blocks both. If the Water line in the inner trigram controls, moves and has the White Tiger added: a yang line means blood in the urine, a yin line bloody stool (the White Tiger is the spirit of blood); with punishment added it is piles and fistula.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.18** 鬼爻在兑宫，病在口里。金鬼变出火、或者火爻变成金鬼，必生牙疔；没有变到火爻，只是牙痛。金鬼遇冲，牙一定松动。(《黄金策》)
  A Pressure line in the Dui palace puts the illness in the mouth. A Metal Pressure changing out into Fire, or a Fire line changing into a Metal Pressure, surely brings a tooth carbuncle; with no change into a Fire line it is only toothache. A Metal Pressure that meets a clash surely loosens the teeth.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.19** 鬼在震宫，病在脚上：加勾陈，脚一定肿；加白虎，一定摔断打伤、破损。土鬼变成木，得脚气；木鬼是酸痛麻木，水鬼是湿气，火鬼主疮毒，金鬼是踝、膝疼痛和骨头痛，或者被刀刃所伤。(《黄金策》)
  A Pressure line in the Zhen palace puts the illness in the foot: with Hook Earth added the foot surely swells, with the White Tiger it is surely broken, wounded or torn. An Earth Pressure changing into Wood is beriberi; a Wood Pressure is aching, soreness and numbness, a Water Pressure is damp, a Fire Pressure means sores and toxin, and a Metal Pressure is pain in the ankle joints, the knees and the bones, or a wound from a blade.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.20** 离宫的鬼变成水（或水爻动变成鬼）是痰火的症候；乾宫的鬼变成木（或木爻动变成鬼）是头风、眩晕。震宫的鬼在外卦，不要按脚上的病断，可以说他坐卧不安、心神恍惚（震是主动的卦）；再加螣蛇发动，是癫狂、惊痫，小孩是惊风；遇冲有翻墙上屋的乱动之患。艮宫的鬼遇上火鬼，一定生痈疽；变出土鬼，可以说浮肿、蛊胀一类。其余照此类推。(《黄金策》)
  A Pressure line of the Li palace changing into Water (or a moving Water line changing into Pressure) is the syndrome of phlegm-fire; a Pressure line of the Qian palace changing into Wood (or a moving Wood line changing into Pressure) is headwind and dizziness. A Zhen Pressure line in the outer trigram is not judged as a foot illness: say the patient cannot sit or lie at ease and his mind wanders (Zhen governs movement); with the Coiling Snake added and moving it is mania and fright-epilepsy, in a child convulsions; meeting a clash there is the danger of climbing over walls and onto roofs. A Gen Pressure line meeting a Fire Pressure surely brings carbuncles; if an Earth Pressure changes out, one can speak of swelling and drum-like distension. The rest follows by analogy.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.21** 财爻管饮食。卦里没有财爻，吃不下东西：财爻落空，吃不下；不上卦，不想吃。财爻动而变成官鬼，这病一定是吃喝来的；财爻临在身爻、世爻上，或者变出财爻，病再重也还能吃。(《黄金策》)
  The Wealth line governs food and drink. With no Wealth in the hexagram, food and drink are not taken: if the Wealth line falls into Void, nothing goes down; if it is absent from the hexagram, he has no wish to eat. A Wealth line that moves and changes into Pressure means the illness came from drink and food; a Wealth line sitting on the Body or the Self line, or a change into a Wealth line, means that even in a heavy illness he can still eat.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.22** 世爻和应爻中间的位置，是病人胸膈的地方。官鬼临在这里，胸膈一定堵闷不通：金鬼是胸肋骨头痛，土鬼是胀饱憋闷、不舒畅，木鬼是心里发痒、胃里嘈杂，水鬼是痰饮堵塞，火鬼多半是心口痛。如果变出财爻，或财爻变成鬼，一定是隔夜的食物没消化，弄得胸膈不舒服。(《黄金策》)
  The place between the Self and Other lines is where the patient's chest and diaphragm are. With a Pressure line on it the chest is surely stuffed and blocked: a Metal Pressure is pain in the ribs and chest bones, an Earth Pressure a full, oppressive feeling with no relief, a Wood Pressure an itching heart and a gnawing stomach, a Water Pressure phlegm and fluid choking it up, and a Fire Pressure mostly heart pain. If it changes into a Wealth line, or a Wealth line changes into Pressure, it is surely food from before left undigested, so that the chest and diaphragm are not at ease.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.24** 世爻衰弱又落进墓里，病人昏沉困倦。世爻有气（旺相），不过是懒得动；衰弱就不说话：怕亮喜暗，不想吃东西，爱睡，怕起床，懒得睁眼。再坐在阴宫上，一定是阴症。用爻入墓也这样断；鬼爻持世入墓、鬼的墓临在用爻上，都照这个断。(《黄金策》)
  A weak Self line falling into the tomb makes the patient drowsy and clouded. If the Self line is strong and has force, he is only too listless to move; if it is weak, he does not speak: he shrinks from the light and likes the dark, has no wish to eat, loves to sleep, dreads getting up and is too listless even to open his eyes. If it also sits in a yin palace it is surely a yin syndrome. A Subject line entering the tomb is judged the same way; so are a Pressure line on the Self line entering the tomb and the tomb of Pressure falling on the Subject line.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.27** 卦里没有鬼爻，就看谁来刑、冲、克、害用爻来断。用爻被金伤，四肢一定酸痛；用爻遭木克，皮和骨一定伤残；火是仇敌，主喘咳的灾病；水来相害，主恍惚的症候。动的金克过来，木爻受伤，四肢关节酸痛；动的木克过来，土爻受制，皮肉伤损。其余照此类推。(《黄金策》)
  With no Pressure line in the hexagram, judge by whatever punishes, clashes, controls or harms the Subject line. A Subject line wounded by Metal: the limbs surely ache and are sore; controlled by Wood: skin and bones are surely injured and maimed; with Fire as the enemy, the trouble of panting and cough; with Water coming to harm, a dazed, wandering state. So when a moving Metal line controls, the Wood line is wounded and the limbs and joints ache; when a moving Wood line controls, the Earth line is held down and flesh and skin are injured. The rest follows by analogy.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.28** 第三爻管腰。第三爻无缘无故落空，是腰软；有缘故而空，或者旺相而空，是腰痛；不空，但遇到动爻、日辰、官鬼来克或冲它，是闪了腰的痛；动而空的也是这样。鬼爻就在第三爻上，也主腰痛。(《黄金策》)
  The third line is the waist. If the third line is Void of itself without cause, the waist is weak; if it is Void with a cause, or Void while strong, the waist hurts; if it is not Void but a moving line, the Day or a Pressure line controls or clashes it, it is a strained waist; a moving Void line is the same. A Pressure line on the third line also means waist pain.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.29** 病不只在鬼爻所坐的地方，被鬼克到的地方也有病痛：克到最上面一爻（上六），主头痛；克到间爻，或者鬼坐在间爻上，主胸膈不舒服；忌神也一样。其余照此类推。(《黄金策》)
  The illness is not only where the Pressure line sits; the place it wounds also has pain. Controlling the top line brings headache; controlling a between line, or sitting on one, makes the chest and diaphragm uneasy; the Adversary does the same. The rest follows by analogy.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.30** 财爻在卦中动：动在上卦，主呕吐；动在下卦，主腹泻。如果被合住，就想吐吐不出、想泻泻不出，像呕逆恶心、里急后重一类。(《黄金策》)
  When the Wealth line moves in the hexagram: moving in the upper trigram it means vomiting, and in the lower trigram diarrhoea. If it is held by a combination, he wants to vomit and cannot, wants to purge and cannot, as in retching and nausea, or urgency with straining at stool.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.31** 木爻旺在世爻上发动，病不是发痒就是发疼：寅木主痛，卯木主痒；如果又在本宫的内卦，还有气不顺的毛病。(《黄金策》)
  When a Wood line rises on the Self line, the illness is either itching or pain: a Yin Wood line means pain, a Mao Wood line itching; if it is also in the inner trigram of the home palace, the qi is not flowing smoothly.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.46** 兄弟爻持世，饮食一定比平时少，这病也是因为吃得太多得的。旧注补充：如果兄弟不动、又没有气，而卦里财爻、子孙爻旺，就不是这样，那是气短力弱的症候，不容易痊愈。(《黄金策》)
  When a Peer line holds the Self line the patient surely eats less than usual, and the illness was also got through eating too much. The old commentary adds: if the Peer line does not move and has no force, while Wealth and Output are strong in the hexagram, it is not so; it is then a syndrome of short breath and weak strength, not easily healed.

## 病因(Cause: how the illness was contracted, and from what)→ `病因.md`

- **LY.ILL.HJC.06** 占病时，本宫的官鬼出现并且发动，得病之初就知道根由；本宫的官鬼不出现、而别的宫有官鬼出现，是病早就伏在身上，这一回因七情四气感冒才发出来；本宫和别的宫都没有官鬼，是不知不觉得的病，起初谁也说不出为什么。旧注又附了两组按卦名的忌讳：明夷、观、贲、需、临最忌世爻和身爻入墓；大畜、丰、同人、蛊、夬不要遇上财爻鬼爻一起兴旺。(《黄金策》)
  In divining illness: if the Pressure of the home palace appears and moves, the source is known as soon as the illness begins; if the home palace's Pressure does not appear but another palace's Pressure does, the illness has long lain hidden in the body and now breaks out through the seven emotions and the four airs; if neither the home palace nor another palace has Pressure, the illness was contracted unseen and at first no one can say why. The old commentary adds two groups of taboos by hexagram name: for Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin, beware above all of Self and Body entering the tomb; for Daxu, Feng, Tongren, Gu and Guai, do not meet Wealth and Pressure both stirring.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.07** 本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，一定是旧病重新发作。如果伏着的鬼不伤用爻、用爻反而被世爻克，以前病轻、现在反倒沉重；如果伏着的鬼刑伤用爻、而用爻反而得到世爻的生扶，以前病重、现在轻。用爻要看问的是谁：比如占妻子的病就看财爻。(《黄金策》)
  If the home palace's Pressure is hidden beneath the Self line, it is surely an old illness breaking out again. If the hidden Pressure does not harm the Subject line but the Subject is controlled by Self, the illness was light before and is heavier now; if the hidden Pressure punishes and harms the Subject but the Subject is generated and supported by Self, it was heavy before and is lighter now. The Subject line depends on whom one asks about: for a wife's illness, for example, look at the Wealth line.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.08** 鬼伏在哪个六亲下面，就是怎么得的病：伏在妻财下，是饮食不节、饥饱失度，或者因为钱财、妇人得病；伏在子孙下，是喝酒过度、放纵房事，夏天露宿贪凉、冬天穿得太暖，或者吃多了养生炼丹的药；伏在父母下，是劳心劳力、忧虑伤神，或者因为动土修造、尊长得病；伏在兄弟下，是口舌争竞、停食生气，或者有人诅咒、因为赌钱争财得病。(《黄金策》)
  Which relative the Pressure is hidden beneath shows how the illness was got: beneath Wealth, eating without restraint and hunger and surfeit, or illness through money or a woman; beneath Output, too much drinking and unrestrained sexual indulgence, sleeping out in the cool in summer, dressing too warmly in winter, or too many drugs for nourishing and refining; beneath Resource, toil of mind and body and worry that harms the spirit, or breaking ground and building, or illness through an elder; beneath Peer, quarrels and rivalry, food stopped and anger felt, or a curse laid, or gambling and struggling for money.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.10** 鬼爻化出哪个六亲，就是在哪里、怎么得的病：化出父母，是在修造的地方得病，在五爻又属水，是在路上淋了雨；化出兄弟，是口舌呕气或者伤食，在三爻又属木，是在房里脱衣露体着了风寒；化出子孙，是在僧道寺院，或者打鱼打猎游戏时得的；化出财爻，多是伤食，或者因为妻子奴仆、买卖交易得病。六亲化出鬼爻，也照这样断。(《黄金策》)
  Which relative the Pressure line changes into shows where and how the illness was got: changing into Resource, it was got at a place of repair or building, and if it is on the fifth line and belongs to Water, it was got braving rain on the road; changing into Peer, it is from quarrels and vexation or harm by food, and if on the third line and Wood, from undressing and baring the body in the room and catching wind and cold; changing into Output, it was got at a temple of monks and Daoists, or while fishing, hunting and at play; changing into Wealth, it is mostly harm by food, or through wife or servants, or through buying and selling. Where a relative line changes into Pressure, judge in the same way.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.11** 鬼在本宫，是在家里得的病；鬼在别的宫（别的卦），是在外面染上的。鬼在下面三爻，一定是内伤的症候，不然病也在里面；鬼在外宫、又在上三爻，一定是外感风邪，不然也是外科的症候；上下都有鬼，是内伤兼外感，症候不止一种。(《黄金策》)
  Pressure in the home palace means the illness was got at home; Pressure in another palace or hexagram means it was caught elsewhere. Pressure in the lower three lines is surely a syndrome of inner damage, or else the illness is in the inside; Pressure in the outer palace and in the upper three lines is surely outer wind evil from without, or else a surgical syndrome; with Pressure both above and below it is inner damage together with outer contagion, and the syndrome is mixed.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.25** 应爻上坐着官鬼，来刑、克、合世爻（王洪绪说是用爻），一定是因为探望亲友的病而被缠染上。鬼爻属土的，是流行的时疫。用爻临在应爻上，一定病倒在别人家里。(《黄金策》)
  When a Pressure line sits on the Other line and punishes, controls or combines with the Self line (Wang Hongxu says the Subject line), the illness was surely caught by entanglement through visiting a sick relative or friend. If the Pressure line is Earth, it is an epidemic of the season. If the Subject line sits on the Other line, the patient surely lies ill in someone else's house.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.26** 官鬼爻持世，病本来有旧根；再伤到用爻，一定是旧病又犯。不然也难脱身，要靠祈禳保佑才吉。卦身上坐着鬼，也是旧病。(《黄金策》)
  When a Pressure line holds the Self line, the illness surely has an old root; if it also wounds the Subject line, it is an old illness breaking out again. Otherwise it is hard to shake off, and protective rites are needed to be lucky. A Pressure line on the hexagram Body is also an old illness.

## 轻重(Light or heavy, new or old, better or worse by day and night)→ `轻重.md`

- **LY.ILL.HJC.12** 鬼在内卦，病夜里重；鬼在外卦，病白天重；内外都有鬼，昼夜都不轻。如果卦里虽然有两个鬼，但一个旺一个空、或者一个动一个静，就是白天轻夜里重，或者白天重夜里轻，要分上下来断。(《黄金策》)
  Pressure in the inner trigram, the illness is heavy at night; Pressure in the outer trigram, heavy by day; Pressure both inside and outside, not light by day or night. If the hexagram has two Pressure lines but one is strong and one Void, or one moving and one still, it is light by day and heavy at night, or heavy by day and light at night, and one judges by upper and lower.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.13** 占卦时动爻是开头、变爻是结尾。卦中动爻生扶用爻、而变爻刑克用爻，病一定早上凉快、晚上发热，或者白天轻夜里重；动爻克用爻、变爻生用爻，反过来断。鬼爻化出所喜的爻、所喜的爻化出鬼爻，也是这样。(《黄金策》)
  In every divination the moving line is the beginning and the changed line the end. If the moving line in the hexagram generates and supports the Subject while the changed line punishes and controls it, the illness is surely cool in the morning and hot in the evening, or light by day and heavy at night; if the moving line controls the Subject and the changed line generates it, judge the reverse. Where a Pressure line changes into a welcome line, or a welcome line changes into Pressure, it is the same.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.34** 官鬼爻持世，病就是轻，也难医治：一定难以脱身。整个卦象再轻，也不容易痊愈。就算用爻有气、子孙当权，眼前能医好，以后也一定断不了根。(《黄金策》)
  When a Pressure line holds the Self line, even a light illness is hard to treat: it is surely hard to shake off. However light the whole image, it does not easily heal. Even if the Subject line has force and Output holds power, so that it can be cured for now, the root will surely not be cut later.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.36** 别的占都喜欢旺相，只有占病不喜欢：人在病里一定衰弱，平时再强健的人这时也瘦了，哪来的旺？所以用爻得时有气、又遇到日辰、动爻来生扶，是「太刚则折」的兆头，到生旺的日子一定凶；如果有动爻、日辰来刑克它，就不嫌它旺。(《黄金策》)
  Every other divination likes strength, but divining illness does not: a person in illness is surely weak, and even one who is usually robust grows thin by then, so where is the strength? Hence a Subject line that is in season and has force and also meets generation or support from the Day or a moving line is a sign of 'too rigid, it snaps', and on the day of birth or strength it is surely unlucky; if a moving line or the Day punishes or controls it, its strength is not held against it.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.68** 官鬼带着日辰、又动出在卦里的，一定是近来突然得的病，不是久病。如果日辰虽然是官鬼，却没有出现在卦里，就不是这样；可以说这病眼下正厉害，必须等这一阵过去，才可以用药。(《黄金策》)
  A Pressure line that carries the Day and moves out in the hexagram is surely an illness got suddenly in these days, not a long one. If the Day is Pressure but does not appear in the hexagram, it is not so; one can say the illness is at its fiercest just now, and only after this has passed can medicine be used.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.09** 官鬼持世，病虽轻也难治，有两种情形。自己占病：官鬼持世，病难痊愈；就算子孙发动、克掉身边的鬼，眼前好了，终究断不了根，不然就又生别的病，叫「病不离身，身不离药」；官鬼持世又临帝旺、长生的，一定得长久的病。替别人占六亲的病：官鬼持世是忧郁的样子，只喜欢子孙发动、克掉这份忧郁，就算用神衰弱也管保平安。觉子补充：这个办法用于占自己和兄弟、儿子、妻子；占父母的病，官鬼是父母的元神；妻子占丈夫的病，鬼是用神，子孙一动反而伤它。(《增删卜易》)
  A Pressure line holding the Self line means that even a light illness is hard to treat, and there are two cases. In divining one's own illness: with Pressure holding the Self line the illness is hard to heal; even if Output moves and controls away the Pressure by one's side, so that he recovers for the present, the root is never cut, or else another illness grows, which is called 'the illness does not leave the body, the body does not leave medicine'; if Pressure holds the Self line and sits on Peak or Birth, he surely gets a long-lasting illness. In divining a relation's illness for another: Pressure holding the Self line is the picture of worry and gloom, and all that is wanted is Output moving to control away this gloom, and then even if the Subject is weak, peace is assured. Juezi adds: this method serves for divining oneself and a brother, son or wife; in divining a parent's illness Pressure is the Support of the Resource line, and when a wife divines her husband's illness Pressure is the Subject, so a moving Output harms it instead.

## 生死(Life or death: will the patient recover, and the signs of danger)→ `生死.md`

- **LY.ILL.BSZZ.36** 怎么知道这家有人病得要死：卜筮正宗说是用神没有救护又入了墓；卜筮大全说是身、命、世、鬼入了墓，照这样推断。(《卜筮正宗》)

- **LY.ILL.HJC.37** 主象落空（王洪绪：或入墓、绝、被破），占病是大忌；要有救才行，没救就会死在半路。(《黄金策》)
  A main image that is Void (Wang Hongxu: or in a tomb, in extinction, or broken) is a great taboo in divining illness; it must have rescue, and without rescue the patient will die on the way.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.38** 占病，妻财是食禄，父母是寿命，卦里不能没有，宜静不宜动。卦里没有财爻或者财爻落空，是不想吃东西；父母爻动是占病的大忌，因为它克制子孙（福神），官鬼这个凶煞就能肆意逞凶，吃药不见效、祷告不灵，所以说「反促天年」。旧注还说：主象遇到绝，或变到绝地，财爻遇到叫禄绝，父母爻遇到叫寿绝，整个卦象如果凶，这是死的兆头。(《黄金策》)

If there is no Wealth line or it is Void, he has no wish to eat; a moving Resource line is a great taboo, because it controls Output (the spirit of fortune) and lets the Pressure killer run wild, so medicine has no effect and prayer is not answered, hence 'it shortens the span of life instead'. The old commentary adds: if the main image meets extinction or changes into a place of extinction, then Wealth meeting it is the emolument cut off and Resource meeting it the life span cut off, and if the whole image is bad this is a sign of death.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.41** 世爻持着官鬼，本来就不是吉兆；如果再遇到日辰带着墓爻，就叫「随官入墓」；本命爻随官入墓也凶。世爻、用爻变入鬼的墓，以及世爻、命爻带着鬼动、变入墓的，还有用爻、命爻、世爻持着鬼墓发动的，都主大凶。(《黄金策》)
  A Pressure line holding the Self line is in itself no lucky sign; if it also meets a tomb line carried by the Day, this is called 'following Pressure into the tomb'; a natal line following Pressure into the tomb is also unlucky. The Self or Subject line changing into the tomb of Pressure, the Self or natal line carrying Pressure, moving and changing into a tomb, and the Subject, natal or Self line holding the tomb of Pressure and moving, all mean great misfortune.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.44** 用神连着鬼煞，眼前一定看到危险：没有子孙、日辰、动爻来解救，危险就在眼前。(《黄金策》)
  When the Subject is joined to the Pressure killer, danger is surely in view: with no Output, Day or moving line to relieve it, the danger is immediate.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.47** 用爻不宜落在死绝的地方，因为一克它就倒。卦里有动爻来生它、帮它，叫绝处逢生，是凶里转吉的象，病虽危险也有救。(《黄金策》)
  The Subject line should not be in a place of death or extinction, because one control topples it. If a moving line in the hexagram generates and helps it, this is called meeting life in a place of extinction, an image of turning to luck out of misfortune: the illness is in danger, but there is rescue.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.48** 用爻虽然不宜太旺，也不宜太弱。太弱，病人就体虚力怯，一时难以痊愈。如果得到日辰、动爻来生、合、扶助，最吉，就算是十分重的病，也不至于死。(《黄金策》)
  Though the Subject line should not be too strong, it should not be too weak either. If weak, the patient is frail and timid in strength and cannot quickly be healed. If it gets generation, combination and support from the Day and moving lines it is most lucky: even a most heavy illness will not lead to death.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.49** 官鬼这一爻虽然是恶煞，也不能没有：得病的原因、症候的虚实、疾病的安危、鬼神的情状，都由它来决定。它不上卦，就要去找它伏在哪里才能推测；如果伏着的爻又落空，这病一定好不了：就算有好医生，也找不到病源；就算有鬼神，也没有可以叩告的门路，是天年命尽。病轻的不在此论。(《黄金策》)

If it is not in the hexagram one must look for where it lies hidden in order to reason it out; if the hidden line is also Void, the illness surely cannot be cured: even a good doctor cannot reach its source, even the spirits have no door to be petitioned, and the span of life is used up. A light illness is not counted here.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.53** 卦身上坐着鬼，而本宫的官鬼又伏在世爻下面；或者世爻持着官鬼，而本宫的官鬼伏在用爻下面；或者自己占，遇到世爻的飞爻、伏爻上下都是官鬼：总叫双官夹身，最不利。就算大局不到死，也是沉重缠困、拖到寿终的病。王洪绪补充：身指卦身和用神；鬼夹在身爻或用爻的前后，或者世爻上有鬼、身爻上又有鬼，都叫双官夹用夹身。(《黄金策》)

Wang Hongxu adds: the 'body' means the hexagram Body and the Subject; Pressure lines flanking the body line or the Subject line before and after, or Pressure on the Self line and also on the body line, are all called double Pressure clamping the Subject and the body.

  Six questions of the 'how to know' rhyme about illness and calamity among kin. A parent is ill when the White Tiger sits on the (Resource) line together with punishment and control; a parent meets calamity when the Wealth line moves and a killing spirit wounds; a child is ill when the Resource line moves and controls Output; a child meets misfortune when, in the Bushi Daquan, the White Tiger arrives upon Output, or, in the Bushi Zhengzong, Resource arrives upon Output; a small child will die when Output is Void and the White Tiger is added; a brother dies when the Subject falls into Void and the White Tiger wounds it.
