# 六爻 · 疾病 · 病因

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Illness · Cause: how the illness was contracted, and from what*

## LY.ILL.HJC.06 鬼伏卦中，病来莫觉：本宫鬼出现发动则得病之初就知根源；本宫无而他宫有是久伏在身今始发；都没有则隐然得病

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病时，本宫的官鬼出现并且发动，得病之初就知道根由；本宫的官鬼不出现、而别的宫有官鬼出现，是病早就伏在身上，这一回因七情四气感冒才发出来；本宫和别的宫都没有官鬼，是不知不觉得的病，起初谁也说不出为什么。旧注又附了两组按卦名的忌讳：明夷、观、贲、需、临最忌世爻和身爻入墓；大畜、丰、同人、蛊、夬不要遇上财爻鬼爻一起兴旺。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining illness: if the Pressure of the home palace appears and moves, the source is known as soon as the illness begins; if the home palace's Pressure does not appear but another palace's Pressure does, the illness has long lain hidden in the body and now breaks out through the seven emotions and the four airs; if neither the home palace nor another palace has Pressure, the illness was contracted unseen and at first no one can say why. The old commentary adds two groups of taboos by hexagram name: for Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin, beware above all of Self and Body entering the tomb; for Daxu, Feng, Tongren, Gu and Guai, do not meet Wealth and Pressure both stirring.

> **按**:旧注最后两句按卦名给忌讳，没有说明理由，王洪绪没有收。「七情」是喜怒忧思悲恐惊，「四气」指寒热温凉（或风寒暑湿），书里没有说是哪一种，译文保留原词。
>
> **Note:** The last two sentences of the old commentary give taboos by hexagram name without reasons, and Wang Hongxu does not take them. The 'seven emotions' are joy, anger, worry, longing, grief, fear and fright, and the 'four airs' are cold, hot, warm and cool (or wind, cold, heat and damp); the book does not say which, and the translation keeps the original words.

## LY.ILL.HJC.07 官藏世下，病起如前：官鬼伏在世爻下是旧病重发；伏鬼不伤用而用被世克则前轻今重，伏鬼伤用而用得世生则前重今轻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，一定是旧病重新发作。如果伏着的鬼不伤用爻、用爻反而被世爻克，以前病轻、现在反倒沉重；如果伏着的鬼刑伤用爻、而用爻反而得到世爻的生扶，以前病重、现在轻。用爻要看问的是谁：比如占妻子的病就看财爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the home palace's Pressure is hidden beneath the Self line, it is surely an old illness breaking out again. If the hidden Pressure does not harm the Subject line but the Subject is controlled by Self, the illness was light before and is heavier now; if the hidden Pressure punishes and harms the Subject but the Subject is generated and supported by Self, it was heavy before and is lighter now. The Subject line depends on whom one asks about: for a wife's illness, for example, look at the Wealth line.

## LY.ILL.HJC.08 鬼伏六亲之下各有病因：伏财伤饥失饱、伏子酒色、伏父劳心、伏兄口舌争竞

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼伏在哪个六亲下面，就是怎么得的病：伏在妻财下，是饮食不节、饥饱失度，或者因为钱财、妇人得病；伏在子孙下，是喝酒过度、放纵房事，夏天露宿贪凉、冬天穿得太暖，或者吃多了养生炼丹的药；伏在父母下，是劳心劳力、忧虑伤神，或者因为动土修造、尊长得病；伏在兄弟下，是口舌争竞、停食生气，或者有人诅咒、因为赌钱争财得病。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Which relative the Pressure is hidden beneath shows how the illness was got: beneath Wealth, eating without restraint and hunger and surfeit, or illness through money or a woman; beneath Output, too much drinking and unrestrained sexual indulgence, sleeping out in the cool in summer, dressing too warmly in winter, or too many drugs for nourishing and refining; beneath Resource, toil of mind and body and worry that harms the spirit, or breaking ground and building, or illness through an elder; beneath Peer, quarrels and rivalry, food stopped and anger felt, or a curse laid, or gambling and struggling for money.

## LY.ILL.HJC.10 鬼爻化出六亲各有得病之处：化父母在修造处（五爻水则途中遇雨）、化兄弟口舌伤食（三爻木则房中伤风）、化子孙寺院渔猎、化财伤食妻奴买卖

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼爻化出哪个六亲，就是在哪里、怎么得的病：化出父母，是在修造的地方得病，在五爻又属水，是在路上淋了雨；化出兄弟，是口舌呕气或者伤食，在三爻又属木，是在房里脱衣露体着了风寒；化出子孙，是在僧道寺院，或者打鱼打猎游戏时得的；化出财爻，多是伤食，或者因为妻子奴仆、买卖交易得病。六亲化出鬼爻，也照这样断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Which relative the Pressure line changes into shows where and how the illness was got: changing into Resource, it was got at a place of repair or building, and if it is on the fifth line and belongs to Water, it was got braving rain on the road; changing into Peer, it is from quarrels and vexation or harm by food, and if on the third line and Wood, from undressing and baring the body in the room and catching wind and cold; changing into Output, it was got at a temple of monks and Daoists, or while fishing, hunting and at play; changing into Wealth, it is mostly harm by food, or through wife or servants, or through buying and selling. Where a relative line changes into Pressure, judge in the same way.

## LY.ILL.YY.18 病因·鬼伏六亲下或六亲化鬼：伏父或父动化鬼忧心得病、衣服寒暖失时；伏兄或兄动化鬼赌博争财；伏财或财动化鬼饮食不节，财在阳宫动主吐、阴宫动主泻、加土动吐、加水动泻；伏子或子动化鬼外情牵引酒色（加龙嗜酒、加武好色）；子爻空绝调理无资；间爻鬼动胸膈不宽

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼伏在父母爻下、或父母爻发动变出鬼的，是忧心得病，或者衣服冷暖穿脱不及时得病；鬼伏在兄爻下、或兄爻发动变出鬼的，是因为赌博争财得病；鬼伏在财爻下、或财爻发动变出鬼的，是饮食没有节制而起：财爻在阳宫发动主呕吐，在阴宫发动主泄泻，财带土发动的吐，财带水发动的泻；鬼伏在子孙爻下、或子孙发动变出鬼的，是被外面的情事牵引，或者酒色致病（带青龙是嗜酒，带玄武是好色）；子孙爻空亡、绝的，没有钱调理；间爻上的鬼发动，胸膈不舒畅。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A ghost hidden beneath the Resource line, or Resource moving and changing into a ghost: the illness is caught from anxiety of heart, or from clothing not changed in time for cold and warmth. Beneath the Peer line, or Peer moving and changing into a ghost: from gambling and quarrels over money. Beneath Wealth, or Wealth moving and changing into a ghost: from intemperate eating and drinking — Wealth moving in a yang palace means vomiting, in a yin palace diarrhea, Wealth with Earth and moving vomiting, Wealth with Water and moving diarrhea. Beneath Output, or Output moving and changing into a ghost: from being drawn away by outside affairs, or wine and women (with the Dragon a taste for drink, with the Dark Tortoise a love of women); if the Output line is Void or in Extinction there are no means for treatment and care; a ghost moving on a between line makes the chest and diaphragm tight.

**《易隐·疾病占》原文** — 《易隐》第 3003 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining illness", base text; Yi Yin, line 3003*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 又鬼伏父下，与父动化鬼者，忧心得病，或衣服寒暖失时得病也。 | 又，鬼伏在父母爻下、和父母爻发动变出鬼的，是忧心而得病，或者衣服冷暖穿脱不及时而得病。 | Also a ghost hidden beneath the Resource line, or Resource moving and changing into a ghost: the illness is caught from anxiety of heart, or from clothing not changed in time for cold and warmth. |
| 鬼伏兄下，与兄动化鬼者，因赌博争财得病也。 | 鬼伏在兄爻下、和兄爻发动变出鬼的，是因为赌博、争财而得病。 | A ghost hidden beneath the Peer line, or Peer moving and changing into a ghost: the illness is caught from gambling and quarrels over money. |
| 鬼伏财下，与财动化鬼者，饮食不节而起也。 | 鬼伏在财爻下、和财爻发动变出鬼的，是饮食没有节制而起。 | A ghost hidden beneath Wealth, or Wealth moving and changing into a ghost: it arises from intemperate eating and drinking. |
| 财在阳宫动，主吐。 | 财爻在阳宫发动，主呕吐。 | Wealth moving in a yang palace means vomiting. |
| 在阴宫动，主泻。 | 在阴宫发动，主泄泻。 | Moving in a yin palace means diarrhea. |
| 财加土动者吐，财加水动者泻也。 | 财带土发动的吐，财带水发动的泻。 | Wealth with Earth and moving: vomiting; Wealth with Water and moving: diarrhea. |
| 鬼伏子下，与子动化鬼者，因外情牵引，或酒色致病也(加龙则嗜酒，加武则好色)。 | 鬼伏在子孙爻下、和子孙发动变出鬼的，是被外面的情事牵引，或者酒色致病（带青龙就是嗜酒，带玄武就是好色）。 | A ghost hidden beneath Output, or Output moving and changing into a ghost: the illness comes from being drawn away by outside affairs, or from wine and women (with the Dragon, a taste for drink; with the Dark Tortoise, a love of women). |
| 子爻空绝者，调理无资也。 | 子孙爻空亡、绝的，是没有钱调理养病。 | If the Output line is Void or in Extinction, there are no means for treatment and care. |
| 间爻鬼动者，胸膈不宽也。 | 间爻上的鬼发动，胸膈不舒畅。 | A ghost moving on a between line: the chest and diaphragm feel tight. |

> **打架**:财爻动，吐还是泻看什么：易隐说财在阳宫动主吐、在阴宫动主泻，又说财带土动吐、财带水动泻；黄金策说财爻动在上卦（外卦）主吐、动在下卦（内卦）主泻（LY.ILL.HJC.30）。一个看阴阳宫，一个看上下卦，两个分法不一样；书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** What decides vomiting or diarrhea when Wealth moves: the Yi Yin says Wealth moving in a yang palace means vomiting and in a yin palace diarrhea, and also Wealth with Earth vomiting and with Water diarrhea; the Huangjin Ce says Wealth moving in the upper (outer) trigram means vomiting and in the lower (inner) trigram diarrhea (LY.ILL.HJC.30). One looks at yang and yin palaces and the other at the upper and lower trigrams, so the two divide differently; the books do not decide.

> **按**:「间爻」是世、应之间的两爻。黄金策讲伏鬼在六亲下的病因（LY.ILL.HJC.08：伏财伤饥失饱、伏子酒色、伏父劳心、伏兄口舌争竞）和这里大体一致；这里多了「六亲发动化出鬼」的情形，和 LY.ILL.HJC.10（鬼爻化出六亲各有得病之处）是反过来的一组。
>
> **Note:** The 'between lines' are the two lines between the Self and the Other. The Huangjin Ce's causes when the ghost is hidden beneath a relation (LY.ILL.HJC.08: beneath Wealth hurt by hunger and overeating, beneath Output wine and women, beneath Resource worry of mind, beneath Peer quarrels) are broadly the same; here there is in addition the case of a relation moving and changing into a ghost, the reverse of LY.ILL.HJC.10 (the Pressure line changing into a relation, each with its own place where the illness was caught).

## LY.ILL.YY.33 病起何方：以动爻断——火南水北木东金西，不论鬼之有无；有鬼不动取外卦；卦安静无鬼看鬼伏何爻之下、以飞神定（伏子北、丑寅东北、卯东、辰巳东南、午南、未申西南、酉西、戌亥西北）；乱动而鬼动以鬼所居之卦断；官鬼独发以鬼爻所值之支断；鬼在本宫内卦是本地得病、他宫外卦是他方得病

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:病是从哪个方向来的，以卦里的动爻来断：火是南，水是北，木是东，金是西，不管有没有鬼。卦里有鬼但不动的，就取外卦来断（乾西北、坤西南、巽东南、艮东北、离南、坎北、震东、兑西）。卦是安静的又没有鬼的，就看鬼伏在哪一爻下面，拿鬼上面的飞神来定灾祸的地方：伏在子下是北方，丑、寅下是东北，卯下是东，辰、巳下是东南，午下是南，未、申下是西南，酉下是西，戌、亥下是西北。卦乱动而鬼也动的，也拿鬼所在的卦来断；官鬼独自发动的，又拿鬼爻所值的地支来断。另外，鬼在本宫的内卦，就说是在本地得病；在别的宫、外卦，就说是在别处得病。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Where the illness came from is judged by the moving lines in the hexagram: Fire is south, Water north, Wood east, Metal west, whether or not there is a ghost. If the hexagram has a ghost that does not move, judge by the outer trigram (Qian northwest, Kun southwest, Xun southeast, Gen northeast, Li south, Kan north, Zhen east, Dui west). If the hexagram is still and has no ghost, see under which line the ghost is hidden and use the flying line above the ghost to fix the place of the affliction: hidden beneath Zi, the north; beneath Chou or Yin, the northeast; beneath Mao, the east; beneath Chen or Si, the southeast; beneath Wu, the south; beneath Wei or Shen, the southwest; beneath You, the west; beneath Xu or Hai, the northwest. If the hexagram has lines moving in disorder and the ghost moves, judge by the trigram in which the ghost stands; if the Pressure line moves alone, judge by the branch that the Pressure line holds. Also a ghost in the inner trigram of the home palace: the illness was caught at home; in the outer trigram of another palace: caught elsewhere.

| 在他宫外卦，则言他方得病也。 | 在别的宫、外卦，就说是在别处得病。 | In the outer trigram of another palace: the illness was caught elsewhere. |

> **Note:** The directions rest on the element directions (Fire south, Water north, Wood east, Metal west) and the later-heaven trigram directions (Qian northwest ... The Yilin Buyi's chapter on where the illness was caught (from line 1240) treats the same matter with a finer division, and is taken with the Yilin Buyi batch.

## LY.ILL.YY.34 病起何年月日：以鬼爻长生之年月日定；土鬼动火生土，午年五月午日得病；土长生在申，或申年七月申日；鬼临辰戌丑未上，辰戌丑未年月日得病；金木水火鬼仿此

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:病是哪年哪月哪天得的，以鬼爻长生的年、月、日来定：比如土鬼发动、火生土，是午年、五月、午日得的病；土的长生在申，或者是申年、七月、申日得的病；鬼落在辰、戌、丑、未上，或者是辰、戌、丑、未的年、月、日得的病；其他金、木、水、火的鬼，都照这样推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the illness was caught is fixed by the year, month or day in which the Pressure line is born: for example, if an Earth ghost moves and Fire generates Earth, the illness was caught in a Wu year, the fifth month, on a Wu day; Earth is born at Shen, or the illness was caught in a Shen year, the seventh month, on a Shen day; a ghost on Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei, the illness was caught in a year, month or day of Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei; for the Metal, Wood, Water and Fire ghosts, work by the same pattern.

**《易隐·疾病占》原文** — 《易隐》第 3029 行 · *Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining illness", base text; Yi Yin, line 3029*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 以鬼爻长生之年月日定之也。 | 以鬼爻长生的年、月、日来定。 | It is fixed by the year, month or day in which the Pressure line is born. |
| 又如土鬼动，火生土，在午年五月午日得病也。 | 又比如土鬼发动，火生土，是在午年、五月、午日得的病。 | Again, for example, if an Earth ghost moves and Fire generates Earth, the illness was caught in a Wu year, the fifth month, on a Wu day. |
| 土长生在申，或申年七月申日得病也。 | 土的长生在申，或者是申年、七月、申日得的病。 | Earth is born at Shen, or the illness was caught in a Shen year, the seventh month, on a Shen day. |
| 鬼临辰戌丑未上，或辰戌丑未年月日得病也。 | 鬼落在辰、戌、丑、未上，或者是辰、戌、丑、未的年、月、日得的病。 | A ghost on Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei: the illness was caught in a year, month or day of Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei. |

> **按**:原文举的例子里，土鬼「火生土」在午年五月午日：午是火的旺位，火生土，所以取午；「土长生在申」是按水土同宫的长生（申）。这一条是「附占」，不是易隐疾病占里的主要事项。
>
> **Note:** In the original's example the Earth ghost 'Fire generates Earth' falls in a Wu year, the fifth month, a Wu day: Wu is Fire's strong place and Fire generates Earth, so Wu is taken; 'Earth is born at Shen' follows the birth (Shen) of Water and Earth together. This entry is a subsidiary matter, not one of the main heads of the Yi Yin's section on illness.
