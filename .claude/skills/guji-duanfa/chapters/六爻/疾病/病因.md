# 六爻 · 疾病 · 病因

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Illness · Cause: how the illness was contracted, and from what*

## LY.ILL.HJC.06 鬼伏卦中，病来莫觉：本宫鬼出现发动则得病之初就知根源；本宫无而他宫有是久伏在身今始发；都没有则隐然得病

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占病时，本宫的官鬼出现并且发动，得病之初就知道根由；本宫的官鬼不出现、而别的宫有官鬼出现，是病早就伏在身上，这一回因七情四气感冒才发出来；本宫和别的宫都没有官鬼，是不知不觉得的病，起初谁也说不出为什么。旧注又附了两组按卦名的忌讳：明夷、观、贲、需、临最忌世爻和身爻入墓；大畜、丰、同人、蛊、夬不要遇上财爻鬼爻一起兴旺。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining illness: if the Pressure of the home palace appears and moves, the source is known as soon as the illness begins; if the home palace's Pressure does not appear but another palace's Pressure does, the illness has long lain hidden in the body and now breaks out through the seven emotions and the four airs; if neither the home palace nor another palace has Pressure, the illness was contracted unseen and at first no one can say why. The old commentary adds two groups of taboos by hexagram name: for Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin, beware above all of Self and Body entering the tomb; for Daxu, Feng, Tongren, Gu and Guai, do not meet Wealth and Pressure both stirring.

> **按**:旧注最后两句按卦名给忌讳，没有说明理由，王洪绪没有收。「七情」是喜怒忧思悲恐惊，「四气」指寒热温凉（或风寒暑湿），书里没有说是哪一种，译文保留原词。
>
> **Note:** The last two sentences of the old commentary give taboos by hexagram name without reasons, and Wang Hongxu does not take them. The 'seven emotions' are joy, anger, worry, longing, grief, fear and fright, and the 'four airs' are cold, hot, warm and cool (or wind, cold, heat and damp); the book does not say which, and the translation keeps the original words.

## LY.ILL.HJC.07 官藏世下，病起如前：官鬼伏在世爻下是旧病重发；伏鬼不伤用而用被世克则前轻今重，伏鬼伤用而用得世生则前重今轻

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，一定是旧病重新发作。如果伏着的鬼不伤用爻、用爻反而被世爻克，以前病轻、现在反倒沉重；如果伏着的鬼刑伤用爻、而用爻反而得到世爻的生扶，以前病重、现在轻。用爻要看问的是谁：比如占妻子的病就看财爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the home palace's Pressure is hidden beneath the Self line, it is surely an old illness breaking out again. If the hidden Pressure does not harm the Subject line but the Subject is controlled by Self, the illness was light before and is heavier now; if the hidden Pressure punishes and harms the Subject but the Subject is generated and supported by Self, it was heavy before and is lighter now. The Subject line depends on whom one asks about: for a wife's illness, for example, look at the Wealth line.

## LY.ILL.HJC.08 鬼伏六亲之下各有病因：伏财伤饥失饱、伏子酒色、伏父劳心、伏兄口舌争竞

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼伏在哪个六亲下面，就是怎么得的病：伏在妻财下，是饮食不节、饥饱失度，或者因为钱财、妇人得病；伏在子孙下，是喝酒过度、放纵房事，夏天露宿贪凉、冬天穿得太暖，或者吃多了养生炼丹的药；伏在父母下，是劳心劳力、忧虑伤神，或者因为动土修造、尊长得病；伏在兄弟下，是口舌争竞、停食生气，或者有人诅咒、因为赌钱争财得病。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Which relative the Pressure is hidden beneath shows how the illness was got: beneath Wealth, eating without restraint and hunger and surfeit, or illness through money or a woman; beneath Output, too much drinking and unrestrained sexual indulgence, sleeping out in the cool in summer, dressing too warmly in winter, or too many drugs for nourishing and refining; beneath Resource, toil of mind and body and worry that harms the spirit, or breaking ground and building, or illness through an elder; beneath Peer, quarrels and rivalry, food stopped and anger felt, or a curse laid, or gambling and struggling for money.

## LY.ILL.HJC.10 鬼爻化出六亲各有得病之处：化父母在修造处（五爻水则途中遇雨）、化兄弟口舌伤食（三爻木则房中伤风）、化子孙寺院渔猎、化财伤食妻奴买卖

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼爻化出哪个六亲，就是在哪里、怎么得的病：化出父母，是在修造的地方得病，在五爻又属水，是在路上淋了雨；化出兄弟，是口舌呕气或者伤食，在三爻又属木，是在房里脱衣露体着了风寒；化出子孙，是在僧道寺院，或者打鱼打猎游戏时得的；化出财爻，多是伤食，或者因为妻子奴仆、买卖交易得病。六亲化出鬼爻，也照这样断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Which relative the Pressure line changes into shows where and how the illness was got: changing into Resource, it was got at a place of repair or building, and if it is on the fifth line and belongs to Water, it was got braving rain on the road; changing into Peer, it is from quarrels and vexation or harm by food, and if on the third line and Wood, from undressing and baring the body in the room and catching wind and cold; changing into Output, it was got at a temple of monks and Daoists, or while fishing, hunting and at play; changing into Wealth, it is mostly harm by food, or through wife or servants, or through buying and selling. Where a relative line changes into Pressure, judge in the same way.

## LY.ILL.HJC.25 应爻带官鬼刑克合世：是探望亲友的病时缠染上的，鬼属土是时疫，用爻临应则病卧他家
