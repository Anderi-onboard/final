## LY.ILL.BSZZ.01 占痘疹先察用神旺衰、再究神的动静：用神被生扶拱合则痘长灵根，被克害刑冲则花遭毒雨；用神原神忌神仇神都要分清

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:痘、痧、疮、疹是随着六气五行的流转一阵一阵流行的，想问平安还是危险，要凭易卦。先看用神是旺是衰，再考究各爻是动是静：用神被生、扶、拱、合，痘就长得有根；被克、害、刑、冲，花就遭到毒雨。凡占痘痧，一定要先分清用神、原神的衰旺，再考究忌神、仇神的动静。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Pox, measles, sores and eruptions come and go with the turning of the six qi and the five phases; to ask whether it will be safe or dangerous one must rely on the hexagram. First look at whether the Subject is strong or weak, then at whether the lines move or are still: if the Subject is generated, supported, embraced or combined, the pox grows a living root; if it is controlled, harmed, punished or clashed, the flowers are struck by poison rain. In all divining of pox and measles one must first tell how strong or weak the Subject and the Support are, then examine how the Adversary and the enemy spirit move or stand still.

**《卜筮正宗·新增痘疹》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2233 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Added chapter on smallpox", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2233*

## LY.ILL.BSZZ.02 出花的是谁就按谁取用神：兄弟出花以兄弟爻为用神、父母爻为原神；子侄以子孙爻为用神、父动克子是凶；妻奴婢妾以财爻为用神、兄动克财不宜

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:看是谁出花来取用神：兄弟出花，拿兄弟爻做用神、父母爻做原神，父母爻发动来生它就吉；子侄出花，拿子孙爻做用神，父母爻发动就克制子孙，是凶，而兄弟爻发动对子侄反而是好的；妻子、奴婢、妾出花，拿财爻做用神，兄弟爻发动克制财爻，也不合适。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Subject is taken by who is having the pox. For a brother, the Peer line is the Subject and the Resource line the Support, and if the Support moves and generates it, it is lucky. For a child or nephew, the Output line is the Subject, and if Resource moves it controls Output, which is unlucky, while a moving Peer is welcome; for a wife, servant, maid or concubine, the Wealth line is the Subject, and a moving Peer controls Wealth, which is likewise unsuitable.

**《卜筮正宗·新增痘疹》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2233 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Added chapter on smallpox", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2233*

| 兄兴则为难于妻奴，子侄喜其相遇。 | 兄弟爻兴旺，就为难妻子、奴婢，而子侄却喜欢碰到它。 | if Peer rises, it makes trouble for wives and servants, while children and nephews are glad to meet it. |

> **打架**:用神取哪一爻：王洪绪按出花的是谁分开取，兄弟取兄弟爻、子侄取子孙爻、妻奴婢妾取财爻，官鬼爻则是痘花本身；增删卜易的野鹤把出花的几件事分开占，问什么时候出花拿鬼爻当用神（LY.ILL.ZS.19），问吉凶大体上全看子孙爻，不管来问的是谁（LY.ILL.ZS.21）。两家取用神的办法不一样，书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Which line is the Subject: Wang Hongxu takes it by who has the pox — the Peer line for a brother, the Output line for a child or nephew, the Wealth line for a wife, servant, maid or concubine — and the Pressure line is the pox flowers themselves; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi divides the matters of the pox into separate divinations, taking the Pressure line as the Subject for when the pox will come out (LY.ILL.ZS.19) and looking, on the whole, only at the Output line for fortune, whoever is asking (LY.ILL.ZS.21). The two differ on how the Subject is taken, and the books do not decide.

> **按**:经文说的是「父动则护持乎兄弟」：父母爻发动，护着兄弟（生兄弟），这对占兄弟出花是好事，但父母爻克子孙，所以对儿孙不合适；兄弟发动克财，所以妻奴受难，而子孙不怕兄弟。
>
> **Note:** The verse says 'if Resource moves it protects the Peer': a moving Resource line generates Peer, which is good for a divination about a brother, but Resource controls Output, so it is unsuitable for children and grandchildren; a moving Peer controls Wealth, so wives and servants suffer, while Output has nothing to fear from Peer.

## LY.ILL.BSZZ.03 最吉者官安用旺、最凶者鬼旺忌兴：官鬼为痘花，不宜伤损也不宜动；遇刑冲克害伏藏是险逆之症，用神旺相而官鬼安静得生扶拱合是顺症

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:最吉的是官爻安静、用神旺相，最凶的是鬼爻旺相、忌神兴旺发动。官鬼就是痘花，不适合受到伤损，也不适合发动，动了恐怕会变坏；如果遇到刑、冲、克、害、伏藏这些象，是凶险不顺的症候，即使勉强出得起来，也难以善终；用神旺相、官鬼安静又得到生、扶、拱、合的，是顺症，不用忧虑。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Most auspicious is Pressure at rest and the Subject strong; most ominous is Pressure strong and the Adversary rising. The Pressure line is the pox flowers themselves: it should not be harmed and should not move, for if it moves it may turn bad; if it meets punishment, clash, control, harm or hiding, it is a dangerous and adverse condition, and even if it barely erupts it is hard to bring to a good end; if the Subject is strong and the Pressure line is at rest and is generated, supported, embraced or combined, it is a favourable condition and there is nothing to fear.

## LY.ILL.BSZZ.04 用得长生百年之内保无虑：用神在日辰上长生、或变出长生，一生都无忧，眼下痘疹更不必虑

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神在日辰上得长生，或者变出长生，即使一百年之内也没有忧虑，眼下的痘疹又何必担心。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject is born at the Day, or changes into Birth, then even within a hundred years there is nothing to worry about, so why worry now about the pox.

## LY.ILL.BSZZ.05 原临死绝一月之外终有害：用神休囚受伤克又遇原神居死绝，旦夕难延；用神旺相而原神静逢死绝或动化伤克，眼下当令有效，出月退气仍有不测之害

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:原神落在死、绝的地方，一个月之外终究有害处：用神休囚又受伤克，再遇上原神落在死、绝之处，一朝一夕都难拖延；用神出现又旺相，而原神安静地遇到死、绝，或者发动却变出伤克的，眼下当令虽然看得到收功，出了这个月、退了气，仍然有想不到的祸害。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Support that falls on Death or Extinction brings harm in the end after a month: if the Subject is resting or trapped and is also harmed and controlled, and the Support meets a place of Death or Extinction, it can hardly last from morning to evening; if the Subject is present and strong but the Support is still and meets Death or Extinction, or moves and changes into harm or control, then for now, in season, one may see it come to completion, but once the month is out and its force retreats there is still an unforeseen harm.

## LY.ILL.BSZZ.06 官强而痘难开朗、福旺则花必稀疏：官鬼为痘症宜静不宜动，静而衰痘稀、旺而动痘密；父母为痘花之主亦宜安静有气；福旺官衰则痘花稀朗好收功

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官爻强，痘就难以开朗；福神旺，花一定稀疏。官鬼爻是痘症本身，适合安静、不适合发动，动了就有变化；安静而衰的痘稀，旺而发动的痘密。父母爻是痘花的主，也适合安静有气，最忌讳发动后变出伤克。如果福神旺、官鬼衰，痘花一定稀疏明朗，一定能好好收功。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If Pressure is strong the pox will hardly open up clear; if Output is strong the flowers will surely be sparse. The Pressure line is the pox itself; it should be at rest and not move, for if it moves there is change: at rest and weak the pox is sparse, strong and moving the pox is dense. The Resource line is the master of the pox flowers and should likewise be at rest and have force, and most dreaded is moving and changing into harm or control. If Output is strong and Pressure weak, the pox flowers are sure to be sparse and clear, and will surely come to a good completion.

**《卜筮正宗·新增痘疹》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2239 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Added chapter on smallpox", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 2239*

## LY.ILL.BSZZ.18 闷而不发皆缘用伏加伤、发而不浆只为官空增制：用神出现不受伤克大吉；用神伏藏又受伤克难过四五朝；官爻旬空月破又遇日辰动爻克害，七八朝内恐不上浆

## LY.ILL.BSZZ.19 原神若坏纵用现不祥、主象受伤得救护无碍：原神旬空或伏藏受伤、真空真破、化回头克，用神无根不吉；主象受伤而原神临月建日辰动爻救护，痘虽险可断不妨

## LY.ILL.BSZZ.24 身上虎须向五行言带疾：卦身临福德吉神主无痘毒，临官鬼必有结毒成疾之处——金鬼肺经鼻孔生疮或耳疾、木鬼肝经两目或耳疾、水鬼肾经耳枯唇疾、火鬼心经舌焦目疾、土鬼脾经口如鱼口鼻梁疾；休囚见福神发动可用药愈，无福神克制加白虎是终身之疾

## LY.ILL.YM.03 当岁而索鬼象之得失：官鬼值太岁或遇空破休囚则言未出，不可以太岁为鬼而断当年应痘；卦有两鬼一空一动取其动、一强一弱取其强，皆应当出

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问哪一年出痘，要探究鬼象的得失；卦里有两个鬼时，要考察其中一爻的虚实。官鬼如果值太岁，或者遇到空、破、休、囚，就说还没出，后来即使日、月会合，也不能说就有：比如己亥年辰月辛未日，占有没有痘，得同人卦，亥鬼旬空，整年平安，可见不能因为太岁就是鬼就断当年应痘。如果卦里有两个鬼，一个空一个动，就取动的；一个强一个弱，就取强的，都应当出痘；这就是所说的考察一爻的虚实。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For the year, seek the gain or loss of the ghost's image; where there are two ghosts, examine the emptiness or fullness of one line. If the Pressure line falls on the Tai Sui, or meets Void, breaking, resting or trapped, say it has not yet come out; even if later the Day and Month meet, one cannot say there is pox: for example, in the Jihai year, the Chen month, on the Xinwei day, someone divined whether there would be pox and obtained Tongren; the Hai ghost was in Void and all year long he was well and at peace, so one cannot take the Tai Sui being the ghost as meaning the pox answers that year. If the hexagram has two ghosts, one Void and one moving, take the moving one; if one strong and one weak, take the strong one; in each case the pox should come out; this is what 'examine the emptiness or fullness of one line' means.

## LY.ILL.YM.04 出痘由己招还是由人召、本乡还是他处：鬼在世由己招、在应由人召，在内卦本乡行灾、外卦他处流祸；往避得痘游魂鬼摇、来归不得痘归魂鬼静；伏鬼有气亦见

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:鬼落在世爻上，是自己招来的；落在应爻上，是别人招来的；在内卦，是本乡流行的灾，在外卦，是别处流传来的祸。出去躲避反而得了痘，是游魂卦、鬼发动；回来却没得痘，是归魂卦、鬼安静；伏着的鬼有气的，痘也会出现。官鬼一动，天花就开了：在世爻是单独发出来的，在应爻是被人传染缠上的，在六爻是童仆起的根，在三爻是弟兄接着种下的；内卦是本地，外卦是外乡；游魂是出去，归魂是回来。鬼在内卦空，本地没事；鬼在外卦空，到外乡可以躲过；鬼动而卦是归魂，回家有痘；鬼空而卦是游魂，出去就能免。大体上鬼爻旺相，即使伏藏也会萌发，鬼爻休囚，即使现出来也会平息。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** On the Self the pox is called down by oneself; on the Other, called by others; in the inner trigram it is a passing calamity of the home district, in the outer trigram a spreading misfortune from elsewhere. Going away to avoid it and yet catching the pox is a Wandering Soul hexagram with the ghost moving; coming home and not catching it is a Returning Soul hexagram with the ghost still; a hidden ghost that has force also shows. When the Pressure line moves the heavenly flowers open: on the Self it is a lone outbreak, on the Other it is caught by contagion, on the sixth line the root is from a servant boy, on the third line brothers sow it in succession; the inner is the home district and the outer another place; Wandering Soul is going away and Returning Soul is coming back. If the ghost is Void in the inner trigram the home district is safe; if Void in the outer trigram one can avoid it by going elsewhere; if the ghost moves in a Returning Soul hexagram, there is pox on returning home; if the ghost is Void in a Wandering Soul hexagram, going out escapes it. In general, if the Pressure line is strong, even hidden it will sprout; if it is resting or trapped, even openly present it will subside.

## LY.ILL.YM.05 占花疏密、部位、颜色：鬼旺日月生扶花稠密、空破休囚死绝痘疏朗；鬼动何宫定花丛何处（艮手臂、兑口颊、坎离上身、震巽下身）、旺在初爻先于足、六爻发于面；火鬼红紫、金鬼虚白、水鬼黑陷、木杂细、土肿大；须论用神生死定吉凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占花的稀密，问鬼的旺衰，用八卦分手足，用六爻定身形，用五行定颜色。鬼临日、月，旺相又有生扶，花一定稠密；遇到空、破、休、囚、死、绝，痘一定疏朗。鬼在哪个宫发动，花就集中在哪里；不动的，就看哪一爻旺：发在艮宫的手臂上密，动在兑宫的口和脸颊上多，坎、离在上身，震、巽在下身；旺在初爻，先出在脚上，旺在六爻，开始发在脸上，五爻在胸背，二爻在腿膝，三、四爻在腰、大腿、心口、肚子。火鬼发的，颜色红紫；金鬼动的，虚白；水鬼发的，黑而塌陷；木是杂而细，土是肿而大。但是一定要论用神的生死才能定吉凶，所以水鬼动而主象旺的，痘出现黑钉，后来仍能收功。痘症多变，假如遇到用象破空，或者某个鬼发动，就变成那种病而死，比如未月甲辰日，占孩子的痘，得到屯卦变革卦，到戊申日变成脾病而治不好。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To divine whether the flowers are sparse or dense, ask whether the ghost is strong or weak; the eight trigrams divide hands and feet, the six lines fix the parts of the body, the five elements bring out the colours. If the ghost sits on the Day or Month, is strong and supported, the flowers are surely dense; if it meets Void, breaking, resting, trapped, death or extinction, the pox is surely sparse and clear. Wherever the ghost moves, in which palace, that fixes where the flowers cluster; if it does not move, see which line is strong: from the Gen palace, dense on the arms; moving in the Dui palace, many on the mouth and cheeks; Kan and Li the upper body, Zhen and Xun the lower; strong on the first line, first on the feet; on the sixth line, starting on the face; the fifth the chest and back, the second the legs and knees, the third and fourth the waist, thighs, heart and belly. A Fire ghost gives red-purple, a Metal ghost hollow white, a Water ghost black and sunken; Wood is mixed and fine, Earth swollen and large. But one must judge whether the Subject lives or dies before fixing fortune, so when a Water ghost moves and the main image is strong, the pox shows black nails and yet comes to completion. Pox often changes: if the image of the Subject is broken or Void, or some ghost moves, it changes into that illness and he dies — for example, in the Wei month on the Jiachen day a child's pox was divined and Zhun changing into Ge obtained, and on the Wushen day it turned into a spleen illness that could not be cured.

## LY.ILL.YM.06 问痘见何人：鬼当本命、鬼化卦宫者应之；考六亲化鬼及鬼化六亲，必鬼旺动；官鬼值何人本命则是人有痘，鬼动艮宫少男、化离象中女、财爻化鬼奴仆、鬼化子孩提

## LY.ILL.YM.08 既痘而筮大象：七日之内三冲是戒（花未开先谢）；卦属反伏墓绝与命随鬼墓虽用旺难治，四忌之外再考用神；鬼旺用旺花丛根固吉、鬼坏用坏花悴根枯凶；生旺用神之日有成、冲鬼象之辰有变；感不感在鬼、害不害在用，鬼化鬼痘后痍、交加动瘄痘相参

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:已经出痘，再占整体的吉凶：七天之内，要戒备三冲，就是花还没开就先谢了。卦是反吟、伏吟、入墓、入绝的，和本命随鬼入墓的，即使用爻旺也难治；这四种忌讳之外，再考察用神。鬼旺而用爻旺，花丛茂盛、根牢固，是吉；鬼坏而用爻坏，花憔悴、根枯，是凶。生旺用神的日子是有成的时候，冲鬼象的时辰是有变的日期。大体上，感染不感染取决于鬼，有害没害取决于用爻；鬼变鬼的，痘后有疮痍；交错发动的，是瘄和痘掺杂。注说：这是专就用爻来说吉凶，不再论鬼的衰旺；如果用爻有生扶救助，鬼旺不过是花密；鬼爻被填实、被冲动，不过是要防变；鬼变鬼和用爻变鬼，不过是痘后余毒；鬼交错发动，或者有别的病夹杂，也没到大害的地步。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Once the pox has come and one divines the great image's fortune, within seven days the three clashes are the thing to guard against, meaning the flower withers before it has opened. The day that strengthens the Subject is the time of completion, the hour that clashes the ghost image the time of change. In general whether the child catches it depends on the ghost, whether it does harm on the Subject line; a ghost changing into a ghost means sores after the pox; lines moving in tangles mean measles and pox mixed together. The note says: this speaks of fortune purely by the Subject line and no longer discusses whether the ghost is strong or weak; if the Subject line has generation, support and rescue, a strong ghost only means dense flowers; if the Pressure line is filled or stirred by a clash, it only means guarding against change; a ghost changing into a ghost, or the Subject line changing into a ghost, only means residual poison after the pox; ghosts moving in tangles, or another illness riding along, does not amount to great harm.

## LY.ILL.YY.35 痘疹占：以官鬼为用，鬼不上卦或鬼化死墓绝胎空、被刑害克破者不出，鬼衰死而化生旺、无鬼而月建临官、鬼静而日当生旺、火鬼加蛇旺动者皆出；出期以官鬼生旺之年月日断；鬼衰子旺或子持身世必稀朗、鬼旺子衰或鬼持身世必稠密；父动者重、兄动者轻；福临空死墓绝胎或受刑害克冲者命难保

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以官鬼为用爻。鬼不上卦，或者鬼变出死、墓、绝、胎、空，以及被刑、害、克、破的，都不出痘。如果鬼虽然衰、死、受伤而变出生旺，或者没有鬼而月建临官，或者鬼安静而日正当生旺，以及火鬼带螣蛇旺盛发动的，都会出痘。想知道出痘疹的日期，以官鬼生旺的年、月、日来断。想知道痘疹的稀密：鬼衰、子孙旺，以及子孙持身、世的，一定稀疏明朗；鬼旺、子孙衰，以及鬼持身、世的，一定稠密。父母爻动的重，兄爻动的轻。福神临在空、死、墓、绝、胎上，以及受到刑、害、克、冲的，性命一定难保。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Pressure line is the Subject. If the ghost does not appear in the hexagram, or changes into Death, tomb, Extinction, Womb or Void, or is punished, harmed, controlled or broken, the pox will not come out. If the ghost is weak, dead or wounded but changes into Birth or strength, or there is no ghost but the Month stands at Office, or the ghost is still and the day is exactly its Birth or strength, or a Fire ghost with the Snake is strong and moving, the pox will come out. To know when the pox will come out, judge by the year, month or day in which the Pressure line is born or strong. To know whether it will be sparse or dense: if the ghost is weak and Output strong, or Output holds the Body or the Self, it will surely be sparse and clear; if the ghost is strong and Output weak, or the ghost holds the Body or the Self, it will surely be dense. If Resource moves it is heavy, if Peer moves it is light. If Fortune falls on Void, Death, tomb, Extinction or Womb, or is punished, harmed, controlled or clashed, his life can hardly be preserved.

> **打架**:痘的稀密看什么：易隐是「鬼衰子旺（或子持身世）则稀朗，鬼旺子衰（或鬼持身世）则稠密」，和卜筮正宗「官强而痘难开朗，福旺则花必稀疏」（LY.ILL.BSZZ.06）、增删卜易「鬼旺相临日月有生扶则花稠密，空破休囚墓绝则痘疏朗」（LY.ILL.ZS.20）是同一个方向，三家一致。取用的办法则不同：易隐以官鬼为用（出不出、何时出、稀密），吉凶另看子孙（福）是否受克；卜筮正宗按谁出花取用（兄弟取兄弟爻、子侄取子孙、妻奴取财），官鬼是痘花本身（LY.ILL.BSZZ.02）；增删卜易问何时出花以鬼为用、问吉凶全看子孙（LY.ILL.ZS.19、LY.ILL.ZS.21）。易隐这一条在取用上和增删卜易相近。「父动者重，兄动者轻」在另两家没有。
>
> **Where they disagree:** What decides whether the pox is sparse or dense: the Yi Yin says 'ghost weak and Output strong (or Output holding the Body or Self), sparse and clear; ghost strong and Output weak (or the ghost holding the Body or Self), dense', in the same direction as the Bushi Zhengzong's 'Pressure strong and the pox hardly clears; Output strong and the flowers are sparse' (LY.ILL.BSZZ.06) and the Zengshan Buyi's 'a strong Pressure line on the Day or Month with support brings dense flowers; Void, broken, resting, trapped, entombed or extinct, sparse pox' (LY.ILL.ZS.20); the three agree. The way of taking the Subject differs: the Yi Yin takes Pressure as the Subject (whether the pox comes out, when, sparse or dense) and looks separately at whether Output (Fortune) is controlled for fortune; the Bushi Zhengzong takes it by who has the pox (Peer for a brother, Output for a child, Wealth for a wife or servant) with Pressure the pox flowers themselves (LY.ILL.BSZZ.02); the Zengshan Buyi takes Pressure as the Subject for when the pox comes out and looks wholly at Output for fortune (LY.ILL.ZS.19 and LY.ILL.ZS.21). In taking the Subject the Yi Yin is close to the Zengshan Buyi. 'Resource moving is heavy, Peer moving light' is found in neither of the others.

> **按**:原文「月建临官」的「官」是十二长生里的临官位，不是官鬼；「火鬼加蛇旺动」是火鬼带螣蛇旺盛发动（何知章里的「螣蛇入火」，LY.ILL.BSZZ.35，是同一个看法）。「福」是福神，即子孙。这一条在易隐里单独一节，题作《痘疹占》，在《小儿占》一类的后面。
>
> **Note:** The 'Office' of 'the Month stands at Office' is the Office stage of the twelve life stages, not the Pressure line; 'a Fire ghost with the Snake, strong and moving' is a Fire ghost carrying the Coiling Snake strong and moving (the 'Snake entering Fire' of the how-to-know rhyme, LY.ILL.BSZZ.35, is the same view). 'Fortune' is the spirit of blessing, that is Output. In the Yi Yin this is a section of its own, titled 'Divining pox', placed after the section on children.

## LY.ILL.ZS.19 问何时出花以鬼爻为用（鬼静逢值逢冲、鬼动逢值逢合、鬼衰逢生旺之年、鬼旺鬼多逢墓库之岁、鬼空鬼破在填实之时、鬼伏在出现之岁）；见花问吉凶忌六冲、子孙化鬼、鬼化子、父化子、子化父、子孙伏而空破
