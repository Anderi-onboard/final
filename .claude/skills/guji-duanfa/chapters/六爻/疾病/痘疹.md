## LY.ILL.ZS.18 断是不是出痘：旧法以官鬼旺者是花痘，野鹤试之不验，只看子孙衰旺，子孙旺者出花无碍；日月世爻克子孙、卦得六冲，花未开而先谢（例：占子发热是痘否，兑为泽，次日再占大过之涣）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问发热是不是出痘：旧法说官鬼旺的就是出花痘，野鹤试过不灵验，说那是揣摩出来的说法，只看子孙的衰旺就行，子孙旺的，出花也没有妨碍。子孙被日月、世爻克，卦又是六冲，是花还没开就先谢了，是不祥的兆头。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask whether a fever is smallpox: the old method says a strong Pressure line means smallpox is flowering; the Old Man tried it and it did not prove true, saying it was a guess, and one need only look at whether Output is strong or weak: if Output is strong, no harm comes even if the flowers come out. Output controlled by the Day, the Month and the Self line, with the hexagram Six-Clash, means the flowers fade before they open, an ill omen.

> **打架**:怎样断是不是出痘：旧法（书里没有指名）以官鬼旺相的为花痘；野鹤试了不验，说是揣摩之说，只看子孙衰旺。
>
> **Where they disagree:** How to judge whether it is smallpox: the old method (which the book does not name) takes a strong Pressure line as smallpox in flower; the Old Man tried it without result, calls it guesswork, and looks only at whether Output is strong or weak.

> **按**:「花痘」「出花」都是出天花、痘疹。这个卦例的后半（医人从哪个方位请，请到杨姓医生）在 LY.ILL.ZS.34，那里用的是同一个孩子的后几个卦。痘疹章（LY.ILL.ZS.19 起）里另有以鬼爻为用神断出花的办法。
>
> **Note:** 'Flowering' and 'coming into flower' both mean the coming out of smallpox and pox eruptions. The second half of this case (from which direction to call the doctor, and calling a doctor with the surname Yang) is in LY.ILL.ZS.34, which uses the later hexagrams of the same child. The chapter on smallpox (from LY.ILL.ZS.19) has a separate way of judging the coming of the flowers with the Pressure line as the Subject.

## LY.ILL.ZS.19 问何时出花以鬼爻为用（鬼静逢值逢冲、鬼动逢值逢合、鬼衰逢生旺之年、鬼旺鬼多逢墓库之岁、鬼空鬼破在填实之时、鬼伏在出现之岁）；见花问吉凶忌六冲、子孙化鬼、鬼化子、父化子、子化父、子孙伏而空破

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:平常问什么时候出花，拿鬼爻当用神：鬼爻静的，逢到值日、逢到冲的时候；鬼爻动的，逢到值日、逢到合；鬼爻衰的，在生旺的年份；鬼旺、鬼多的，在墓库的年岁；鬼空、鬼破的，在填实的时候；鬼伏藏的，在它出现的年岁。已经见花来问吉凶的，卦忌六冲，还忌子孙变鬼、鬼变子、父变子、子变父、子孙伏藏又空又破，都不是吉兆。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In ordinary times, when asking when the flowers will come out, the Pressure line is the Subject: if it is still, at a day it rules or a clash; if moving, when it rules the day or meets a combination; if weak, in a year of birth or strength; if strong or many, in the year of its tomb; if Void or broken, at the time it is filled; if hidden, in the year it appears. For one who asks about fortune when the flowers have already come out, the hexagram must avoid Six-Clash, and also Output changing into Pressure, Pressure into Output, Resource into Output, Output into Resource, and Output hidden and also Void and broken: none of these is a lucky sign.

**《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)** — 《增删卜易》第 13183–13185 行 · *Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked); Zengshan Buyi, lines 13183–13185*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 平时，问男女何时出花者，以鬼爻为用。 | 平常问男孩女孩什么时候出花，拿鬼爻当用神。 | In ordinary times, when asking when a boy or girl will break out in flower, the Pressure line is the Subject. |
| 鬼爻静者，逢值逢冲； | 鬼爻静的，逢到值日、逢到冲就出； | If the Pressure line is still, it comes out when it rules the day or meets a clash; |
| 鬼爻动者，逢值逢合； | 鬼爻动的，逢到值日、逢到合就出； | if the Pressure line moves, when it rules the day or meets a combination; |
| 鬼爻衰者，逢生旺之年； | 鬼爻衰的，逢到生旺的年份就出； | if the Pressure line is weak, in a year of birth or strength; |
| 鬼旺鬼多者，逢墓库之岁； | 鬼旺、鬼多的，逢到墓库的年岁就出； | if Pressure is strong or many, in the year of its tomb; |
| 鬼空鬼破者，在填实之时； | 鬼落空、鬼被破的，在填实的时候； | if Pressure is Void or broken, at the time it is filled; |
| 鬼若伏藏者，在出现之岁。 | 鬼如果伏藏着，在它出现的年岁。 | if Pressure is hidden, in the year it appears. |
| 见花而问吉凶者，卦忌六冲，兼忌子孙化鬼，及鬼化子、父化子、子化父、子孙伏而空破，皆非吉兆。 | 已经见花来问吉凶的，卦忌六冲，还忌子孙变鬼，以及鬼变子、父变子、子变父、子孙伏藏而又空又破，都不是吉兆。 | For one who asks about fortune when the flowers have already come out, the hexagram must avoid Six-Clash, and also Output changing into Pressure, Pressure into Output, Resource into Output, Output into Resource, and Output hidden and also Void and broken: none of these is a lucky sign. |

> **按**:「花」是痘花，出花就是出痘。「墓库」是鬼爻五行的墓；「填实」是落空或被破的爻等到它的地支值年、值月、值日，把空破填上。这一条问的是出花的时间，和 LY.ILL.ZS.18 里问「发热是不是出痘」不同。
>
> **Note:** The 'flowers' are the flowers of the pox, and to 'flower' is to have the pox come out. The 'tomb' is the tomb of the phase of the Pressure line, and 'filling' is for a Void or broken line to wait until its branch rules the year, month or day and fills the void or break. This entry asks when the flowers come out, which is different from asking in LY.ILL.ZS.18 whether a fever is smallpox.

## LY.ILL.ZS.20 问花的疏密、部位、颜色：鬼爻旺临日月又有生扶花必稠密，空破休囚墓绝痘疏朗；鬼在乾多生头、坤多腹、艮多手、兑多口、坎离上身、震巽下身；火鬼紫红、金鬼虚白、水鬼防黑陷、木鬼杂细、土鬼肿大

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:问花出得稀还是密：鬼爻旺相、临着日月、又有动爻生扶的，花一定稠密；如果遇到空、破、休囚、入墓入绝，痘就稀疏明朗。鬼在乾宫发动，多长在头上；鬼在坤卦摇动，肚子上成丛；艮宫多在手上，兑宫多在口上，坎、离在上半身，震、巽在下半身。颜色：火鬼紫红，金鬼虚白，水鬼要防发黑塌陷，木鬼又杂又细，土鬼肿大。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To ask whether the flowers will be sparse or dense: if the Pressure line is strong, sits on the Day or Month and is generated and supported by a moving line, the flowers are surely dense; if it meets Void or a break, or is resting, trapped, in a tomb or extinct, the pox are sparse and clear. Pressure moving in the Qian palace: mostly on the head; stirring in Kun: clusters on the belly; Gen, more on the hands; Dui, more on the mouth; Kan and Li, the upper body; Zhen and Xun, the lower body. As to colour: a Fire Pressure, purple-red; a Metal Pressure, pale white; a Water Pressure, beware of black and sunken pox; a Wood Pressure, mixed and fine; an Earth Pressure, swollen and large.

## LY.ILL.ZS.21 出花吉凶全在子孙爻：所问各事应分占，不可一卦兼断；子孙临日月或被日月动爻生扶、动而化吉、不受刑冲克、爻不乱动，花虽密可全生，痘虽陷仍收功效

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:上面问的几件事应当分开占，不能用一个卦兼着断，大体上全看子孙爻。子孙临着日月、又被日月和动爻生扶，动了变出吉的，不受刑伤冲克，爻又不乱动的，花虽然密，也可以保全性命；痘就算塌陷了，仍然能收到效果。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The matters asked above should be divined separately and cannot be judged from one hexagram together; in general everything depends on the Output line. If Output sits on the Day or Month, is generated and supported by the Day, Month or a moving line, moves and changes into something lucky, suffers no punishment, harm, clash or control, and the lines do not move wildly, then though the flowers are dense the child can survive whole, and even if the pox sink, the treatment still takes effect.

>
> **Note:** This entry brings the several matters of the coming of the flowers (when, how dense, fortune) back to the Output line: LY.ILL.ZS.19 uses the Pressure line for the time, but the fortune returns to Output. The 'Qian note' at the end of the chapter on smallpox is a modern addition and is not quoted here.
