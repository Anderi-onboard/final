## LY.ILL.BSQS.11 神杀断例·四般休问病：三丘、五墓、飞帘、浴盆四煞不要问病

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:神杀断例说：三丘、五墓、飞帘、浴盆这四样煞，不要拿来问病；占怀孕见了它们，也主忧虑惊恐。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The verse of examples on judging by spirit-stars says: do not ask about illness when the Three Mounds, the Five Tombs, the Flying Curtain and the Bathing Basin are present; in divining a pregnancy too they bring worry and fright.

**《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·二十二、神杀断例》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3165–3167 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses, no. 22: examples of judging by spirit-stars", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), lines 3165–3167*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 三丘并五墓， | 三丘和五墓， | The Three Mounds together with the Five Tombs, |
| 飞帘及浴盆。 | 飞帘和浴盆。 | the Flying Curtain and the Bathing Basin: |
| 四般休问病， | 这四样不要问病， | of these four, do not ask about illness, |
| 占孕亦忧惊。 | 占怀孕也忧虑惊恐。 | and in divining a pregnancy there is worry and fright too. |

> **按**:「四般」指前两句点名的四样：三丘、五墓、飞帘、浴盆。三丘五墓在断易天机里有按季节的取法（春丑、夏辰、秋未、冬戌是三丘，五墓从对宫取，LY.ILL.DYTJ.31），卜筮全书的神煞歌例里也有（LY.ILL.BSQS.52）。「飞帘」「浴盆」这里只点了名，没有给位置；周易尚占的神杀例给了浴盆的位置（春辰夏未秋戌冬丑，LY.ILL.ZSZ.01）。
>
> **Note:** 'The four kinds' are the four named in the first two lines: the Three Mounds, the Five Tombs, the Flying Curtain and the Bathing Basin. The Duanyi Tianji gives a seasonal way of finding the Three Mounds and Five Tombs (Chou in spring, Chen in summer, Wei in autumn and Xu in winter are the Three Mounds, and the Five Tombs are taken from the opposite palace, LY.ILL.DYTJ.31), and the verses on spirit-stars in the Bushi Quanshu have them as well (LY.ILL.BSQS.52). The Flying Curtain and the Bathing Basin are only named here without positions; the Zhouyi Shangzhan's table of spirit-stars gives the positions of the Bathing Basin (Chen in spring, Wei in summer, Xu in autumn, Chou in winter, LY.ILL.ZSZ.01).

## LY.ILL.BSQS.17 移徒章·朱雀又临官鬼位、克世兼持世：病遭危困、讼遭囚

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占迁居的歌里说：朱雀又临在官鬼爻上，官司口舌就有来由；再克世、又持世，生病会遇到危险困厄，打官司会被囚禁。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The verse on moving house says: if the Vermilion Bird sits on a Pressure line, there is cause for lawsuits and quarrels; if it also controls and holds the Self line, illness meets danger and distress, and a lawsuit ends in imprisonment.

**《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·移徒章》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 3609 行 · *Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses: the chapter on moving house", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 3609*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 朱雀又临官鬼位， | 朱雀又临在官鬼位上， | If the Vermilion Bird also sits on the Pressure position, |
| 官司口舌有来由。 | 官司口舌有来由。 | there is cause for lawsuits and quarrels. |
| 那更克世兼持世， | 再克世又持世， | and if it also controls and holds the Self line, |
| 病遭危困讼遭囚。 | 生病遇到危险困厄，打官司遇到囚禁。 | illness meets danger and distress, and a lawsuit meets imprisonment. |

> **按**:这是占迁居（移徒章第一首）里的一联，讲朱雀、官鬼、世爻三样凑在一起。「克世兼持世」是同一个神既克世爻又坐在世爻上，照字面写成「控制并占住」在汉语里说不通，译文写作「又克世、又持世」，保留歌里两个动作并列的样子。
>
> **Note:** This couplet is in the verse on moving house (the first poem of the chapter on moving), where the Vermilion Bird, Pressure and the Self line come together. 'Controls and holds the Self line' describes one spirit that both controls the Self line and sits on it; the translation keeps the two actions side by side as in the verse.

## LY.ILL.BSQS.51 神煞歌例·岁前神煞：从太岁起顺轮十二位，三丧门主孝服、六死符、十一天狗吊客、十二病符切莫逢

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:岁前神煞：十二个神煞都从太岁起，顺着一位一位轮下去：太岁、剑锋、伏尸同位，第二位太阳、天空，第三位丧门主孝服，第四位勾绞、贯索，第五位官符、五鬼，第六位死符、小耗，第七位栏干、大耗，第八位暴败、天厄，第九位飞廉、白虎，第十位福德、卷舌，第十一位天狗、吊客，第十二位病符，千万不要遇上。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The stars before the Year: all twelve start from Tai Sui and go round forward place by place: Tai Sui, Sword Edge and Hidden Corpse in one place; second Great Yang and Heavenly Emptiness; third the Mourning Gate, meaning mourning dress; fourth Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through; fifth Official Tally and the Five Ghosts; sixth Death Tally and Small Loss; seventh Railing and Great Loss; eighth Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress; ninth Feilian and the White Tiger; tenth Fortune Virtue and the Curled Tongue; eleventh the Heavenly Dog and the Guest of Condolence; twelfth the Sickness Tally, which one must not meet.

> **打架**:十二位岁煞各家的名单不一样。卜筮全书的岁前神煞是：一太岁剑锋伏尸、二太阳天空、三丧门、四勾绞贯索、五官符五鬼、六死符小耗、七栏干大耗、八暴败天厄、九飞廉白虎、十福德卷舌、十一天狗吊客、十二病符；断易天机的「四利三元」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.50）是：一太岁、二太阳、三丧门、四太阴、五官符、六死符、七岁破、八龙德、九白虎、十福德、十一吊客、十二病符。第一、二、三、五、六、九、十、十一、十二位的主名两本一致；第四位（勾绞贯索对太阴）、第七位（栏干大耗对岁破）、第八位（暴败天厄对龙德）不同，而且卜筮全书每一位后面还并带了一两颗别的星。书里没有裁决。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The books' lists of the twelve year-stars differ. The stars before the Year in the Bushi Quanshu are: first Tai Sui with Sword Edge and Hidden Corpse, second Great Yang with Heavenly Emptiness, third Mourning Gate, fourth Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through, fifth Official Tally with the Five Ghosts, sixth Death Tally with Small Loss, seventh Railing with Great Loss, eighth Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress, ninth Feilian with the White Tiger, tenth Fortune Virtue with the Curled Tongue, eleventh the Heavenly Dog with the Guest of Condolence, twelfth Sickness Tally; the Duanyi Tianji's 'Four Gains and Three Origins' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.50) runs: first Tai Sui, second Great Yang, third Mourning Gate, fourth Great Yin, fifth Official Tally, sixth Death Tally, seventh Year-break, eighth Dragon Virtue, ninth White Tiger, tenth Fortune Virtue, eleventh Guest of Condolence, twelfth Sickness Tally. The principal names agree in the first, second, third, fifth, sixth, ninth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth places; the fourth (Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through against Great Yin), the seventh (Railing and Great Loss against Year-break) and the eighth (Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress against Dragon Virtue) differ, and the Bushi Quanshu joins one or two further stars to each place. The books do not decide.

> **按**:这十二位是流年神煞：子年时太岁、剑锋、伏尸在子，太阳、天空在丑，丧门在寅，依次往下，病符在亥。占病要看的是名字里带丧和病的几位：丧门（主孝服）、死符、吊客、病符（「切莫逢」），歌里只对这几位给了明白的话，其余几位只点名。原文「褔德」的褔是福的异写，这里的福德是一颗神煞，不是子孙爻。这一组里其余的神煞（天哭、四季小杀等）和本条的区别是：它们按月取，这一条按年取。
>
> **Note:** These twelve are year-stars: in a Zi year Tai Sui, Sword Edge and Hidden Corpse are at Zi, Great Yang and Heavenly Emptiness at Chou, the Mourning Gate at Yin, and so on, with the Sickness Tally at Hai. In divining illness the ones to look at are those with mourning and illness in their names: the Mourning Gate (mourning dress), the Death Tally, the Guest of Condolence and the Sickness Tally ('not to be met'); the verse speaks plainly only of these, and the rest are merely named. The 'Fortune' of 'Fortune Virtue' is written in a variant character in the original, and the Fortune Virtue here is a spirit-star, not the Output line. This entry differs from the other spirit-stars in this group (the Weeping, the small killing of the four seasons and so on) in being placed by the year, while those are placed by the month.

## LY.ILL.BSQS.52 神煞歌例·占病大忌的煞：天哭杀、四季小杀、三丘五墓、勾陈杀(占病及讼)、白虎杀

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:神煞歌例点明占病大忌的五个煞：天哭杀（正五九月羊、二六十月猴、三七十一月鸡、四八十二月犬）；四季小杀（春羊夏龙秋牛冬犬，临身命，轻灾也凶）；三丘五墓（春丑夏辰秋未冬戌为三丘，五墓取对宫，病人作福也难留）；勾陈杀（从辰逆行，占病及讼大忌）；白虎杀（从申顺行，占病大忌）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The verses on spirit-stars name five killings that are great taboos in divining illness: the Weeping killing (the sheep in the first, fifth and ninth months, the monkey in the second, sixth and tenth, the rooster in the third, seventh and eleventh, the dog in the fourth, eighth and twelfth); the small killing of the four seasons (the sheep in spring, the dragon in summer, the ox in autumn, the dog in winter; on the Body or the natal line even a light misfortune is ominous); the Three Mounds and Five Tombs (Chou in spring, Chen in summer, Wei in autumn, Xu in winter are the Three Mounds, the Five Tombs are taken from the opposite palace, and a sick person can hardly be kept even by making offerings); the Hook Earth killing (backward from Chen, a great taboo for illness and lawsuits); the White Tiger killing (forward from Shen, a great taboo for illness).

The last sentence, 'Output moves and a resolving spirit comes to rescue', says that with Output moving to control the ghost and a resolving spirit to untie, calamity may be turned into fortune; but if, while Output moves, a Wealth line also moves to generate the ghost, the case is different, and the original does not go on to say so. Wealth moving to generate the ghost, so that an illness is hard to cure, is the same principle as the Huozhu Lin's 'Wealth moving alone generates the ghost and injures the father; asking about illness it is hard to cure' (LY.ILL.HZL.18) and the Duanyi Tianji's 'ghost strong and Wealth rising, life is hard to keep' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02).

## LY.ILL.EL.01 御避灾患：防灾避患忌官爻——安静休囚祸不招，遇旺遇生灾速起，无或落空陷则病潜消，不伤身世无魔瘴，日月制之殃咎散，子孙一动灭邪妖；无鬼便无灾

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:这一章讲防灾避患，吉的事用子孙爻为主，凶的事用官鬼爻为凭。防灾避患最忌官爻：官爻安静、休囚，祸招不来；遇到旺、遇到生，灾很快就起；官爻没有或落陷，病就悄悄消了；官爻不伤世和卦身，就没有魔障；如果发动，就难逃；日、月制住它，殃咎就散；子孙一动，邪妖就灭。注说：占自己有没有病，要仔细看官鬼，鬼逢生旺，灾一定来得快；落空亡或不上卦，永远不会成灾；鬼发动怎么会不生灾？不克世和卦身，终究没有疾厄；官爻虽然动，只要子孙同时发动、或者子孙临日月、或者子孙在世爻上，万祸都悄悄消了；没有鬼就没有灾。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** This chapter is about guarding against calamity and avoiding trouble: for good outcomes take Output as the main line, for bad ones the Pressure line as the ground. To guard against calamity one fears the Pressure line: still, resting or trapped, it brings no misfortune; meeting strength or generation, calamity rises quickly; absent or fallen, the illness quietly fades; if it does not harm the Self and the Body there is no demonic obstruction; if it moves, it can hardly be escaped; if the Day and Month control it, the calamity disperses; when Output moves, evil and demons are wiped out. The commentary says: in divining whether one has illness, examine the Pressure line closely; if the ghost meets generation or strength, calamity is sure to come quickly; if it falls into Void or is absent from the hexagram, it will never cause trouble; if the ghost moves, how could it not breed calamity? But if it does not control the Self and the Body, in the end there is no illness; even if the Pressure line moves, if Output moves with it, or Output sits on the Day or Month, or Output is on the Self, ten thousand misfortunes quietly fade; no ghost, no calamity.

## LY.ILL.EL.23 却灾避暑、养病悟道、修真：四者皆宜子动、各忌官兴；占己世爻休墓绝、问他用体怕空亡；子孙旺相或发动参禅避暑必如愿、官鬼交重旺相心求安反遭不测；用神遇月破旬空或临墓绝不惟无福反惹非殃

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:以用爻为主，福德为凭。天行酷暑，难道没有避暑的办法？人染了患灾，也有躲灾的地方。悟道要选清闲的地方，修真要访幽僻的住处。这四样都宜子孙发动，各样都忌官鬼兴旺；占自己，怕世爻休囚墓绝，问别人，用爻和体怕空亡。注说：凡是占避暑、养病、悟道、修真这些事，都宜福德发动，各自忌讳官爻发动；占自己，以世爻为主，卜别人，以应象为凭；如果问亲人，要推用象，若临旺相、或遇生扶，却灾能脱身、悟道能成功；用神如果遇月破、旬空、或临墓绝，不光没有福，反而招来祸殃。子孙旺相或发动的，参禅打坐没有不成功的，避暑却灾必定如愿；官鬼交重或旺相的，心里想求安，反而遭遇不测。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Subject line is the main line, and the fortune spirit is the ground. When the heat of the season rages, is there not a way to escape it? When a person is infected with calamity, there is a place to ward it off. To realise the Way, choose a quiet and leisured place; to cultivate the truth, visit a secluded dwelling. For all four Output moving is favourable, and in each a thriving Pressure line is feared; in divining oneself, the Self line resting, in a tomb or in Extinction is feared, and in asking about another, the Subject and the body fearing Void. The commentary says: in all matters of escaping the heat, convalescing, realising the Way and cultivating the truth, the fortune spirit moving is favourable, and in each the Pressure line moving is feared; in divining oneself the Self line is the main line, and in divining another the Other image is the ground; if asking about a relative, work out the Subject image, and if it sits on strength or meets generation and support, one escapes calamity and the Way is achieved; if the Subject meets Month-break or Void, or sits in a tomb or Extinction, there is not only no blessing but a misfortune is brought on. With Output strong or moving, sitting in meditation is never without success, and escaping the heat and warding off calamity are sure to be as wished; with the Pressure line moving or strong, the heart seeks peace and on the contrary meets the unforeseen.

## LY.ILL.EL.24 避暑养病往何处：父母生世宜投书馆尊长、子孙生世利僧道卑幼、兄弟生世往弟兄朋友、妻财生世往妻族仆隶、官鬼生世宦宅；一卦皆安方是修行之路、六爻尽破岂为养静之窝；用爻旺遇青龙宜往、忌象兴加白虎莫往；随寓而安、六冲往返不定

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻扶身，宜去书馆；福神生世，利于去僧堂。注说：父母爻生世，宜去文墨的地方和长辈的家；子孙生世，利于住在僧道的门下和晚辈小孩的地方；兄弟生世，宜去兄弟朋友的家；妻财生世，应去妻族亲友、仆隶下人的住处；官鬼生世，偏宜在官宦人家安身。一卦都安，才是修行的路；六爻都破，怎么是养静的窝？用爻旺的遇上青龙，宜去这个地方；忌象兴旺又加白虎，就不要去那一家。注说：各爻都占得随住处而安；一卦六冲，往来不定；用爻有气，又值青龙，利于有所往；忌象交重，又临白虎，这个地方不要去。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Resource line supporting the Self suits going to a schoolroom, and the fortune spirit generating the Self favours the monks' hall. The commentary says: the Resource line generating the Self suits places of letters and the homes of elders; Output generating the Self favours living with monks and Daoists and among the young; Peer generating the Self suits going to the homes of brothers and friends; Wealth generating the Self, go to the dwellings of the wife's kin, friends, servants and underlings; the Pressure line generating the Self suits above all lodging in an official's house. When the whole hexagram is at peace, that is the road of cultivation; when all six lines are broken, how could it be a nest for quiet nurture? When the Subject line is strong and meets the Azure Dragon, it suits going to this place; when the adversary image thrives and adds the White Tiger, do not go to that house. The commentary says: if all lines are divined, one is at peace wherever one lodges; a hexagram of six clashes means going and returning are unsettled; if the Subject line has qi and also holds the Azure Dragon, it is favourable to go; if the adversary image moves and sits on the White Tiger, do not go to this place.

'A hexagram of six clashes means going and returning are unsettled' is a different matter from the Six-Clash of LY.ILL.EL.15: here it is that the lodging is unsettled, not that the illness is fast or slow.

## LY.ILL.YM.32 鬼动为致祸之由、福动为远灾之要，福神可趋、祸神可避：鬼发坎莫贪酒食、发兑莫迷欲色、震忌登舟、巽忌入园、乾不好僧室、艮不悦山居、坤弗食牛、离因嗜炙；子孙发于是宫则反视

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:所以鬼动是招来祸的缘由，福动是远离灾的要领；福神可以趋近，祸神可以躲开。于是鬼在坎宫发动，就不要贪酒食；在兑宫发动，就不要迷恋欲色；震宫忌坐船，巽宫忌进园子，乾宫不可以喜欢去僧房，艮宫不可以喜欢住山里，坤宫不吃牛，离宫是因为爱吃烤炙的东西。注说：如果是子孙在这一宫发动，这个地方反而有利有福，和前面说的正好相反；乾宫利于僧堂，坤宫利于田地，坎宫适合江海，艮宫适合山，离宫乐于文章，兑宫爱好酒，震宫喜欢舟车，巽宫喜欢园囿。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** So a moving ghost is the cause that brings misfortune, and a moving Output is the key to keeping calamity away: the fortune spirit can be sought and the misfortune spirit avoided. When the ghost moves in Kan, do not be greedy for wine and food; in Dui, do not be lost in desire and lust; for Zhen avoid boarding a boat, for Xun avoid entering a garden; for Qian one must not favour monks' rooms, for Gen one must not delight in mountain dwellings; for Kun do not eat beef, and for Li it is from a fondness for roasted food. The note says: if Output moves in the same palace, that place is advantageous and blessed, the reverse of what was said before; Qian favours the monks' hall and Kun the fields, Kan suits river and sea and Gen the mountain, Li delights in writing and Dui in wine, Zhen likes boats and carriages and Xun gardens and parks.

## LY.ILL.ZS.17 避瘟之法：瘟病卦里子水克世、月建克世是大凶，幸有寅日相生，可迁到南方、夜卧南床使火旺水枯以避；不肯移床的死于亥日

## LY.ILL.ZSZ.01 四时吉凶神杀例·占病要忌的三个煞：五墓(春未夏戌秋丑冬辰)发动持凶；浴盆(春辰夏未秋戌冬丑)临水神水爻并凶；三丘(春丑夏辰秋未冬戌)发动兄鬼主死

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:周易尚占旁通部的「四时吉凶神杀例」表列了三个占病要忌的煞，位置按春、夏、秋、冬的次序排：五墓是未、戌、丑、辰，发动、持（世）都凶；浴盆是辰、未、戌、丑，落在水神（玄武）或水爻上都凶；三丘是丑、辰、未、戌，发动、又有兄弟或官鬼，主死。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The table of auspicious and ominous spirit-stars of the four seasons in the Parallel Tables of the Zhouyi Shangzhan lists three stars to avoid in asking about illness, set out in the order spring, summer, autumn, winter: the Five Tombs at Wei, Xu, Chou, Chen, ominous when they move or hold the Self; the Bathing Basin at Chen, Wei, Xu, Chou, ominous when it falls on a Water spirit (the Dark Tortoise) or a Water line; the Three Mounds at Chou, Chen, Wei, Xu, which mean death when they move together with Peer or Pressure.

The 'hold' of 'if it moves or holds, it is ominous' is not followed by 'the Self' in the book; the translation reads it in the usual way as holding the Self, and puts that in square brackets; neither 'falling on a Water spirit or a Water line, both ominous' nor 'if it moves, with Peer or Pressure, it means death' has a subject in the book, and the translation supplies 'it'. 'Water spirit' is, by the earlier section 'Section 11: Fixing the Six Spirits' in the same part, with its 'Azure Dragon is the Wood spirit, Vermilion Bird the Fire spirit, Hook Earth the Earth spirit, White Tiger the Metal spirit, Dark Tortoise the Water spirit' (line 461), the Dark Tortoise; 'Peer or Pressure' stands for the two characters meaning 'brother' and 'ghost'. The table of combining, punishing and killing stars before it in the same part (six combinations, three harmonies, three punishments, post horse, robbery, loss, bathing pool) is not about illness and is not taken here.
