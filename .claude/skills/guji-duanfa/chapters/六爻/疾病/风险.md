## LY.ILL.HJC.55 应合而变财伤勿食馈来之物：应爻动来生合世爻是有人问安，带财福必有馈送、兄弟只是清访；若应生合而变出妻财反来刑克，馈送切须戒食否则反受伤害，占长辈尤宜忌

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:应爻发动来生、合世爻，是有人来问安：带财爻、子孙的，一定有送东西的；带兄弟的，只是来看望。如果应爻虽然生合，却变出妻财反过来刑克世爻（或者应爻生合世爻而刑克主象），要是有人送东西，千万不要吃，不然反而受伤害。占长辈的病，尤其要忌。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Other line moves to generate or combine with the Self line, someone will come to ask after him: if it carries Wealth or Output, there will surely be gifts; if Peer, only a plain visit. But if the Other line, though generating or combining, changes out Wealth that turns to punish or control the Self line (or the Other line generates and combines with the Self line while punishing or controlling the main image), then any gift sent should on no account be eaten, or it does harm instead; in divining an elder it is even more to be avoided.

**《黄金策·病体》原文** — 《卜筮大全》第 6961 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text; Bushi Daquan (= Bushi Quanshu), line 6961*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 2408 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 2408*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 若应虽生合，而用爻或变妻财，或被财爻刑冲克害用神者，倘馈送切宜忌食，否则反生伤害。 | 如果应爻虽然生合，而用爻变出妻财，或者被财爻刑冲克害用神的，要是有人送东西，千万要忌口不吃，不然反而受到伤害。 | If the Other line, though generating and combining, has the Subject line change into Wealth, or a Wealth line punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Subject, then any gift sent must on no account be eaten, or it brings harm instead. |
| 若占长辈，尤宜忌之。 | 如果占的是长辈，更要忌讳。 | If one is divining an elder, it is even more to be avoided. |

> **打架**:变出妻财的是哪一爻：旧注是应爻变出妻财、反过来刑克世爻（另有应爻生合世爻却刑克主象的一种）；王洪绪是用爻自己变出妻财，或者有财爻刑冲克害用神。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Which line changes into Wealth: in the old commentary it is the Other line changing out Wealth that in turn punishes and controls the Self line (with a second case, the Other line generating and combining with the Self line while punishing or controlling the main image); in Wang Hongxu it is the Subject line itself changing into Wealth, or a Wealth line punishing, clashing with, controlling or harming the Subject.

> **按**:「清访」是清淡的探望，不带东西。这一条防的是有人借「送东西」害人，或者因为妻财克伤了病人依靠的那一爻，吃了送来的东西反倒加重；书里没有把机理讲得更细。
>
> **Note:** 'A plain visit' is a bare call, bringing nothing. This entry guards against a gift that does harm, or against eating what is sent when Wealth harms the very line the patient depends on, which makes him worse; the book does not spell the mechanism out further.

## LY.ILL.YM.28 初筮防疾：用坏病起、鬼发病作；时灾专用于鬼，发动则疫扰其室；何命犯鬼是天祸、何神化鬼是人作、鬼化何人是流灾；随墓助伤大凶可畏

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:一开始占筮来预防疾病的：用神坏了，病就起来；鬼发动，病就发作。遇到时行的灾病，专门看鬼，鬼发动，就是瘟疫来扰他的家，只能修身反省、等着它过去。哪个人的本命冲犯了鬼，是天降的祸；哪个六亲变出了鬼，是人自己招的；鬼变成哪个人，是流行的灾。随墓、助伤，大凶，可怕。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a first divination to guard against illness: when the Subject line is spoiled the illness arises, and when the ghost moves the illness breaks out. When a seasonal calamity is going about, look at the ghost alone; if it moves, an epidemic is troubling his household, and there is nothing to do but cultivate oneself, reflect and wait. Whichever natal branch offends the ghost, it is a heavenly misfortune; whichever relation turns into the ghost, it is man-made; into which person the ghost turns, that is a spreading calamity. Following into the tomb and helping to harm are greatly ominous, and to be feared.

**《易冒·疾病章第五十五》原文** — 《易冒》第 1231 行 · *Yi Mao, "Chapter 55: Illness", base text; Yi Mao, line 1231*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 初筮之防疾也，用坏病起，鬼发病作。 | 一开始占筮来预防疾病的，用爻坏了，病就起来；鬼发动，病就发作。 | In a first divination to guard against illness, when the Subject line is spoiled the illness arises, and when the ghost moves the illness breaks out. |
| 时灾之行，专用于鬼，发动疫扰其室，惟修省以俟。 | 遇到时行的灾病，专门看鬼，鬼发动，就是瘟疫来扰他的家，只能修身反省、等着它过去。 | When a seasonal calamity is going about, look at the ghost alone; if it moves, an epidemic is troubling his household, and there is nothing to do but cultivate oneself, reflect and wait. |
| 何命犯鬼，是天祸也，何神化鬼，是人作也，鬼化何人，是流灾也。 | 哪个人的本命冲犯了鬼，是天降的祸；哪个六亲变出了鬼，是人自己招的；鬼变成哪个人，是流行的灾。 | Whichever natal branch offends the ghost, it is a heavenly misfortune; whichever relation turns into the ghost, it is man-made; into which person the ghost turns, that is a spreading calamity. |
| 随墓助伤，大凶可畏。 | 随墓、助伤，大凶，可怕。 | Following into the tomb and helping to harm are greatly ominous, and to be feared. |

> **按**:「初筮」在这里是没病之前先占一卦，防着病来，不是说头一次占。「何命犯鬼」是哪个人的本命地支犯了鬼（与 LY.ILL.YM.09「命随鬼墓」里的「命」是同一个意思）。「天祸」「人作」「流灾」是三种来路：天降、自己招、传染。时行的灾病，书里叫「时灾」，与痘疹章说的「传染」「流行」（LY.ILL.YM.04）是同一路。
>
> **Note:** 'First divination' here means divining before any illness, to guard against its coming, not the first divination made. 'Whichever natal branch offends the ghost' means whose natal earthly branch runs into the ghost (the same 'natal branch' as in 'the natal branch follows the ghost into a tomb', LY.ILL.YM.09). 'Heavenly misfortune', 'man-made' and 'spreading calamity' are three roads by which it comes: sent from heaven, brought on by oneself, caught by contagion. What the book calls a 'seasonal calamity' is of the same kind as the 'passing calamity' and 'spreading' of the chapter on pox (LY.ILL.YM.04).

## LY.ILL.YM.32 鬼动为致祸之由、福动为远灾之要，福神可趋、祸神可避：鬼发坎莫贪酒食、发兑莫迷欲色、震忌登舟、巽忌入园、乾不好僧室、艮不悦山居、坤弗食牛、离因嗜炙；子孙发于是宫则反视

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:所以鬼动是招来祸的缘由，福动是远离灾的要领；福神可以趋近，祸神可以躲开。于是鬼在坎宫发动，就不要贪酒食；在兑宫发动，就不要迷恋欲色；震宫忌坐船，巽宫忌进园子，乾宫不可以喜欢去僧房，艮宫不可以喜欢住山里，坤宫不吃牛，离宫是因为爱吃烤炙的东西。注说：如果是子孙在这一宫发动，这个地方反而有利有福，和前面说的正好相反；乾宫利于僧堂，坤宫利于田地，坎宫适合江海，艮宫适合山，离宫乐于文章，兑宫爱好酒，震宫喜欢舟车，巽宫喜欢园囿。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** So a moving ghost is the cause that brings misfortune, and a moving Output is the key to keeping calamity away: the fortune spirit can be sought and the misfortune spirit avoided. When the ghost moves in Kan, do not be greedy for wine and food; in Dui, do not be lost in desire and lust; for Zhen avoid boarding a boat, for Xun avoid entering a garden; for Qian one must not favour monks' rooms, for Gen one must not delight in mountain dwellings; for Kun do not eat beef, and for Li it is from a fondness for roasted food. The note says: if Output moves in the same palace, that place is advantageous and blessed, the reverse of what was said before; Qian favours the monks' hall and Kun the fields, Kan suits river and sea and Gen the mountain, Li delights in writing and Dui in wine, Zhen likes boats and carriages and Xun gardens and parks.

**《易冒·疾病章第五十五》原文** — 《易冒》第 1239 行 · *Yi Mao, "Chapter 55: Illness", base text; Yi Mao, line 1239*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 故鬼动为致祸之由，福动为远灾之要，福神可趋，祸神可避。 | 所以鬼动是招来祸的缘由，福动是远离灾的要领；福神可以趋近，祸神可以躲避。 | Therefore a moving ghost is the cause that brings misfortune, and a moving Output is the key to keeping calamity away; the Output (fortune) spirit can be sought and the misfortune spirit avoided. |
| 是以发坎则莫贪于酒食，发兑则莫迷于欲色，震忌登舟，巽忌入园，乾不可以好僧室，艮不可以悦山居，坤弗食牛，离因嗜炙也。 | 所以鬼在坎宫发动，就不要贪酒食；在兑宫发动，就不要迷恋欲色；震宫忌坐船，巽宫忌进园子，乾宫不可以喜欢去僧房，艮宫不可以喜欢住山里，坤宫不吃牛，离宫是因为爱吃烤炙的东西。 | So when it moves in Kan, do not be greedy for wine and food; in Dui, do not be lost in desire and lust; for Zhen avoid boarding a boat, for Xun avoid entering a garden; for Qian one must not favour monks' rooms, for Gen one must not delight in mountain dwellings; for Kun do not eat beef, and for Li it is from a fondness for roasted food. |

**《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)** — 《易冒》第 1240 行 · *Bracketed small note in the Yi Mao (author not named in the book); Yi Mao, line 1240*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 若子孙发动于是宫，则此处利福，与前反视。 | 如果是子孙在这一宫发动，这个地方反而有利有福，和前面说的正好相反。 | If Output moves in the same palace, then that place is advantageous and blessed, the reverse of what was said before. |
| 乾利僧堂坤利田，坎宜江海艮宜山，离乐文章兑嗜酒，震喜舟车巽囿园。 | 乾宫利于僧堂，坤宫利于田地，坎宫适合江海，艮宫适合山，离宫乐于文章，兑宫爱好酒，震宫喜欢舟车，巽宫喜欢园囿。 | Qian favours the monks' hall and Kun the fields; Kan suits river and sea and Gen suits the mountain; Li delights in writing and Dui in wine; Zhen likes boats and carriages and Xun gardens and parks. |

> **按**:这一条是上一条（LY.ILL.YM.30）的反面：上一条从病因往回推，这一条向前讲怎么躲。同一个宫，鬼动时忌的东西，子孙动时反而是宜的东西——坎宫鬼动忌酒食，子孙动时宜江海；乾宫鬼动不可好僧室，子孙动时利僧堂。「福神可趋」的「福神」在这里是子孙（Output），「祸神」是鬼。「离因嗜炙」的「嗜炙」是爱吃烤炙的东西，取火象；「坤弗食牛」取坤为牛。
>
> **Note:** This entry is the reverse of the last (LY.ILL.YM.30): that reasons back from the cause of the illness, and this looks forward to how to avoid it. In the same palace, what is avoided when the ghost moves is what is favoured when Output moves: with the ghost moving in Kan wine and food are avoided, and with Output moving Kan suits river and sea; with the ghost moving in Qian one must not favour monks' rooms, and with Output moving Qian favours the monks' hall. The 'fortune spirit' in 'the fortune spirit can be sought' is Output, and the 'misfortune spirit' is the ghost. 'Fondness for roasted food' in Li takes the image of Fire, and 'do not eat beef' in Kun takes Kun as the ox.

## LY.ILL.ZS.17 避瘟之法：瘟病卦里子水克世、月建克世是大凶，幸有寅日相生，可迁到南方、夜卧南床使火旺水枯以避；不肯移床的死于亥日

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占瘟病，卦里子水克世爻、月建也克世爻，是大凶的兆头；要避开，就搬到南方，夜里睡南面的床，让火旺、水枯，可以保平安。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining an epidemic illness, when Zi Water controls the Self line and the Month controls it too, it is a sign of great misfortune; to avoid it, move to the south and sleep at night on a south-facing bed, so that Fire is strong and Water dries up, and he may be kept safe.

**野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 13071 行 · *The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 13071*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 存后向者，使知趋避之法也。 | 留下后面这一卦，是要让人知道趋避的办法。 | The latter hexagram is kept so that one may know the method of avoiding and evading. |

**书中卦例** — 《增删卜易》第 13043 行 · *Case from the book; Zengshan Buyi, line 13043*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 又如：子月丙寅日(旬空：戌亥)，自占瘟病，得“节之中孚” | 又如：子月丙寅日（旬空：戌、亥），自己占瘟病，得节卦变中孚卦。 | Another: in the Zi month, on a Bingyin day (void: Xu and Hai), a person divined his own epidemic illness and got Jie changing into Zhongfu. |

**书中卦例** — 《增删卜易》第 13070 行 · *Case from the book; Zengshan Buyi, line 13070*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 断曰：子水克世，月建克世，大凶之兆，幸有寅日相生，若肯避瘟于南方，夜卧南床，使火旺而水枯，可保无虞。 | 断语：子水克世，月建也克世，是大凶的兆头，幸好有寅日来生，如果肯到南方避瘟，夜里睡南面的床，让火旺水枯，可以保证没有忧患。 | Judgment: Zi Water controls the Self line, and the Month also controls the Self line, a sign of great misfortune; luckily the Yin day generates it, and if he is willing to avoid the epidemic in the south and sleep at night on a south-facing bed, so that Fire is strong and Water dries up, he can be kept free from harm. |
| 果依此行，亥日病势危急，得遇良医，寅日而愈。 | 他果然照这个办，亥日病势危急，遇到了好医生，寅日就好了。 | He did as told; on the Hai day the illness became critical, but he met a good doctor and recovered on the Yin day. |
| 彼时瘟疫流行，家家传染，一人同日亦得此卦，余亦传此法以避之。 | 那时瘟疫流行，家家传染，有一个人同一天也得到这一卦，我也把这个办法传给他来避瘟。 | At that time an epidemic was going round and every household was infected; a man got this same hexagram on the same day, and I also passed on this method to him for avoiding it. |
| 因此人病重，又信旁言病人不可移床，未肯迁移，卒于亥日。 | 因为这个人病得重，又听信旁人说病人不可以移床，不肯迁移，死在亥日。 | But this man was gravely ill and believed what others said, that a patient must not have his bed moved, and would not move; he died on the Hai day. |

> **按**:这一条是趋避：卦里克世的是水，就让病人搬到属火的南方去睡，书里只在这一个卦例里用它，没有把它写成对一切五行都通用的规则。「移床」是把病人的床换到别的方位。野鹤把这一卦和上一个久病逢冲必死的卦（LY.ILL.ZS.02）放在一起，说一个是让人知道死，一个是让人知道避。
>
> **Note:** This entry is avoidance: when the line that controls the Self line in the hexagram is Water, the patient is moved to sleep in the south, which belongs to Fire; the book uses it only in this one case and does not write it as a rule for all five phases. 'Moving the bed' is to change the patient's bed to another direction. The Old Man sets this hexagram beside the previous one on a long illness meeting a clash being fatal (LY.ILL.ZS.02), saying that one teaches how to know death and the other how to avoid it.

相关:LY.ILL.ZS.02、LY.ILL.ZS.14
