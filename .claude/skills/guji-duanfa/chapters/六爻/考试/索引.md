## 总则(Principles: which line is the candidate, the writing, the official star; strong and weak)→ `总则.md`

- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.01** 考试这件事里，先要定下来看哪一爻：问功名（考中、做官），用神是官鬼爻；问老师、文章、奏章、读书的馆舍，用神是父母爻；问学生（门生），用神是子孙爻；问结拜兄弟、同僚和知交朋友，用神是兄弟爻。(《卜筮正宗》)
  In an examination reading one first settles which line to watch: for success in the examinations and in office the Subject is the Pressure line; for a teacher, an essay, a memorial or the study hall the Subject is the Resource line; for pupils the Subject is the Output line; for sworn brothers, colleagues and close friends the Subject is the Peer line.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.15** 求师不只指讲道学的老师，学百工技艺、拜僧人道士为师也都是；自己占，以世爻看学习的人；别人代占，要问是什么人，朋友兄弟就以兄弟爻为主；总要师弟相生相合才吉，相冲相克就凶。(《黄金策》)
  Seeking a teacher does not mean only a teacher of moral learning: learning a craft or skill, or taking a monk or Taoist as teacher, all count; when one divines for oneself, the learner is read from the Self line; when another divines on one's behalf one must ask who he is, and for a friend or brother Peer is the main line; in every case teacher and pupil giving life to and combining with each other is auspicious, and clashing and controlling each other inauspicious.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.16** 占功名，父爻是文章，官鬼是官职，这两样是一卦的主体，伤了一样就不成；父爻旺相文章必好，官鬼得地功名有望，泥金报喜。(《黄金策》)
  In divining for fame and rank, the Resource line is the writing and Pressure the post; these two are the masters of the hexagram, and if either is harmed it will not succeed; with the Resource line thriving the writing is surely fine, and with Pressure well placed fame and rank are within hope and the gilt-ink news of joy comes.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.42** 再要看本主的爻神，才能论这个人的命运：本主是本人的主爻，自己占以世爻论，子侄占看子孙爻等，这一爻最怕被伤克、变坏。(《黄金策》)
  Study further the line that is the principal of the person, and then one may judge his fortune: the principal is the person's own chief line, which for oneself is the Self, and for a son or nephew the Output line, and so on; this line most fears being harmed, controlled or changed for the worse.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.03** 学业上世爻最怕落空、受破、入墓、逢绝或休囚，否则诸事无成；世爻休囚，又跟着官鬼入墓，或被日月、动爻冲克，或发动变官鬼、变绝、变克，不但学业难成，还要提防意外。(《增删卜易》)
  In study the Self line most fears being Void, broken, in the tomb, extinct, or resting and trapped, or nothing will succeed; if the Self is resting and trapped and moreover follows Pressure into the tomb, or is clashed and controlled by the Day, the Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into Pressure, extinction or control, then not only will study be hard to complete but one must guard against the unforeseen.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.45** 衰、旺、克、冲的轻重要仔细玩味：用神衰的要有根，旺的宜适中，被克的要有救，被冲的要遇合。(《增删卜易》)
  The weight of weakness and strength, control and clash must be weighed with care: if the Subject is weak it must have roots, if strong it should be moderate, if controlled it must have rescue, if clashed it must meet a combination.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.50** 占功名有两种一眼就能断的卦：官爻旺相持世、或者日月动爻作官星来生合世爻，求名像弯腰拾起一根草那样容易；子孙持世、或者子孙在卦中发动，不管问入场（考试）还是升迁，都像水中捞月，捞不着。(《增删卜易》)
  In divining for a title there are two hexagrams that can be read at a glance: a strong Pressure line holding the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acting as the Pressure star and giving life to or combining with the Self, and seeking a name is as easy as picking up a blade of grass; Output holding the Self, or Output moving in the hexagram, and whether one asks about entering the examination hall or about promotion it is like scooping the moon from the water.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.51** 考试里要看的东西各有各的用神：问功名，用神是官鬼爻，最忌落空落破，宜有相生、怕被相克，月日动变四处的生克和别的用神同样看；问师长、奏章、文书、文章、书馆，用神是父母爻；问门徒（学生），用神是子孙爻。(《增删卜易》)
  In an examination reading each thing to be watched has its own Subject: for a title the Subject is the Pressure line, which most fears Void and break, is glad of generation and fears control, and whose generation and control from the four places (Month, Day, moving line, changed line) are read as for any other Subject; for teachers, memorials, documents, essays and the study hall the Subject is the Resource line; for pupils the Subject is the Output line.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.52** 子孙爻是福神：占别的事见到它是喜，只有占功名要忌它，因为子孙制住官鬼、剥去官位、引走职位。(《增删卜易》)
  The Output line is the spirit of blessing: for every other matter seeing it is welcome, but for a title it is the one to avoid, because Output controls Pressure, strips office and leads away a post.

## 成否(Will one pass or be chosen: signs for and against)→ `成否.md`

- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.02** 父母爻（文书）发动，当头克制子孙时，读书人应科举能登上金榜。同一诀里还说，父母发动时，等远行的人、盼书信会有动静；打官司下状子，先把是非分清；失物、逃亡要靠告状评理才能了结。(《卜筮正宗》)
  When the Resource line (the document line) moves and squarely controls Output, a scholar sitting the civil examinations is placed on the golden list. The same verse adds that with Resource moving, a traveller or a letter being waited for shows movement, a plaint filed in a lawsuit first sorts out right from wrong, and lost goods or a runaway have to be pleaded for in court.
- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.03** 子孙爻发动，会伤官鬼；这时拜见贵人、求功名都不宜往前进取，诀劝人守住本分、听凭天意。同一诀里，子孙发动对别的事是吉的：病人求医就痊愈，出门人和做买卖的平安，婚姻美满，产妇生子好养，官司私下和解不到官府。(《卜筮正宗》)
  When the Output line moves it wounds Pressure; then calling on a patron or seeking a title should not press forward, and the verse urges one to keep to one's lot and leave it to Heaven. The same verse treats a moving Output as good for other matters: the sick who seek a physician recover, travellers and traders are safe, a marriage is a happy one, a woman in labour bears a child easy to rear, and a lawsuit is settled privately without reaching the court.
- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.04** 财爻发动，克住父母文书：应考、求名总是落空。同一诀里，财动对别的事是吉的：拿本钱经营大吉，亲戚婚姻如意。(《卜筮正宗》)
  When the Wealth line moves and controls the document line, sitting the examinations and seeking a title all come to nothing. The same verse says a moving Wealth line is good elsewhere: trading with capital is greatly auspicious and marriages and family ties go as one wishes.
- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.05** 兄弟爻发动、克住财爻时：病人难好，灾还没有走；应举时，同来夺魁首的人是最要提防的客人；官司和暗中的盗贼会耗损钱财。(《卜筮正宗》)
  When the Peer line moves and controls Wealth: the sick are hard to cure and the calamity has not left; in sitting the examinations, those who contend for the top place are the guests most to be avoided; and lawsuits and secret thieves waste money.
- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.06** 诸爻持世诀里和考试有关的三句：父母爻持世，官爻发动、财爻旺相，适合去赴考；子孙爻持世，求名最忌（子孙坐在世位上）；官鬼爻持世，别的事难安，只有求功名最喜欢世爻当权。(《卜筮正宗》)
  Three lines of the verse on lines holding the Self bear on examinations: with Resource holding the Self, if Pressure moves and Wealth is strong it is a good time to go and sit the examination; with Output holding the Self, seeking a title above all should avoid it; with Pressure holding the Self, other matters are uneasy, but for success in the examinations the best thing is that the Self holds power.
- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.08** 六冲、六合怎么断：人所厌恶的事宜冲，所喜欢的事宜合，只有占病要分近病、久病：近病遇冲就好、久病遇冲就死，六合则相反。六冲卦里，有日辰相合、变爻相合的，叫冲中逢合；六合卦里，有日辰相冲、变爻相冲的，叫合处逢冲。冲掉忌神、合住用神，叫去煞留恩，事事说吉；冲掉用神、合住忌神，叫留煞害命，件件皆凶。书中例：辰月庚午日占会试，得观卦变否卦，未土持世，变出的午火官星正是日辰，生着合着世爻，断为「鼎甲在掌」，结果中了探花。(《卜筮正宗》)
  How the Six-Clash and Six-Combine hexagrams are judged: what a person hates is better clashed and what he likes is better combined, and only in divining illness is there the distinction between a recent and a long illness: a recent illness that meets a clash is cured, a long one dies, and with a combination it is the reverse. In a Six-Clash hexagram, if the Day or the changed line combines with it, it is called a clash meeting a combination; in a Six-Combine hexagram, if the Day or the changed line clashes with it, it is called a combination meeting a clash. If the Adversary is clashed and the Subject combined, it is called removing the baleful and keeping the gracious and every matter is called lucky; if the Subject is clashed and the Adversary combined, it is called keeping the baleful to harm life and every matter is ill. The case in the book: on a Geng-Wu day in the Chen month a man divined the metropolitan examination and got Guan changing to Pi; Wei Earth held the Self and changed out into the Wu Fire Pressure star, which is the Day itself, giving it life and combining with it, so the judgement was that the top three places lay in the palm of his hand, and he indeed passed as Tanhua.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.17** 财爻发动，别指望被选中；福神（子孙）发动，难指望金榜题名。只有占功名时，财、福反而是恶煞，因为财克父、子孙克官鬼；官爻持世又得财爻动来生它，财就没有忌讳，子孙则本来就是忌客。(《黄金策》)
  If Wealth moves, do not hope to be chosen; if Output moves, it is hard to hope for one's name on the Golden List. Only in divining for fame and rank are Wealth and Output on the contrary baleful spirits, for Wealth controls Resource and Output controls Pressure; if the Pressure line holds the Self and Wealth moves to give it life, Wealth is no taboo, but Output is by nature the hostile guest.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.18** 求名时见到兄弟爻，就是和自己同治一经的人；兄弟发动，或月建、日辰都带兄弟，同经的人多，必定夺走自己的魁首，纵然大局可成，名次也落后。(《黄金策》)
  Seeing the Peer line in seeking a name means men who study the same classic as oneself; if it moves, or the Month and the Day both carry Peer, there are many who share one's classic and they will surely snatch one's prize, and even if the overall picture can succeed one's rank falls behind.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.19** 父母爻、官鬼无气，得日辰扶起、克制恶煞，仍然有望，叫辅德；世爻衰、静、空亡，得日辰生扶或冲实，有亲友资助路费，帮他去应考求名。(《黄金策》)
  If the Resource and Pressure lines have no breath but the Day raises them up and restrains the baleful spirit, there is still hope, which is called supporting virtue; if the Self is weak, still and Void but the Day gives it life and support or clashes it solid, relatives and friends will help with the travelling expenses and aid his going to seek a name.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.21** 六爻都争着发动，功名恍惚难成：六爻都喜安静，只要官鬼有气不空、月建日辰不来伤克就吉；凡动就有变，变出的爻又有死、墓、绝、空、刑、克的说法，都是破败，所以乱动的卦大概不吉。(《黄金策》)
  If all six lines contend to move, fame and rank are dim and hard to achieve: the six lines all like stillness, and as long as the Pressure line has breath and is not Void and the Month and Day do not come to harm or control it, it is auspicious; whatever moves changes, and the line it changes into is again judged by death, tomb, extinction, Void, punishment and control, all of which are ruin, so a hexagram of disordered movement is on the whole inauspicious.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.23** 世爻是求名的人：持官鬼或得官鬼生合，功名有望；世爻临子孙，就克制官鬼，仕途不通，白去求谋也没有用。(《黄金策》)
  The Self is the man seeking a name: if it holds Pressure or is given life or combination by Pressure, fame and rank are within hope; if it stands on Output it controls Pressure, the path of office is not open, and seeking and scheming in vain is of no avail.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.24** 父母爻空亡，如果财爻发动来生扶官鬼，侥幸可以成；如果财、官两爻都动而父爻在旬空里，反而不宜用这个说法，父爻不空才有希望。(《黄金策》)
  If the Resource line is Void but the Wealth line moves to give life and support to Pressure, one may succeed by luck; if Wealth and Pressure both move and the Resource line is in the Void, this saying must not be used, and there is hope only if the Resource line is not Void.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.25** 本卦没有官鬼，如果子孙变出官鬼，和世爻、卦身生合，文书有气，功名有望，但不能高中。(《黄金策》)
  If the original hexagram has no Pressure but Output changes into Pressure and gives life to or combines with the Self and Body, and the document line has breath, fame and rank are within hope, but one cannot pass high.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.26** 官爻、父爻出现在卦中却无情：不持临身、世，反而临在应爻上，或者发动却去生别的爻、不来生合世爻和卦身，或者被破坏、入墓、逢绝，虽在卦中，对我没有益处，难遂青云之志。(《黄金策》)
  Present but without feeling: the Pressure and Resource lines are in the hexagram but do not hold or stand on the Body or Self and instead stand on the Other, or move but give life to another line and do not give life to or combine with the Self and Body, or are spoiled, in the tomb or extinct; though in the hexagram they do one no good, and it is hard to fulfil the ambition to rise to the clouds.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.27** 官爻不出现，看它伏在哪里；如果是有用的官爻，到它自己地支值年的那一年，就会告别平民的白屋。(《黄金策》)
  If the Pressure line does not appear, look where it lies hidden; if it is a useful Pressure line, in the year of its own branch one will leave the white cottage.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.29** 父爻、官星三合相遇，接连考中：卦中三合会成官局，必定接连及第，会成父局也吉。(《黄金策》)
  If the Resource line and the official star meet in a three-harmony one passes in successive examinations: a three-harmony forming a Pressure frame in the hexagram means one will surely pass in successive examinations, and forming a Resource frame is also auspicious.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.30** 青龙、白虎两爻都动，一考就成名：两样都在卦中、动来生合世爻，必定中魁选；若又持官爻、父爻或持身、世就更好。(《黄金策》)
  If the Azure Dragon and White Tiger lines both move, one makes a name at a single stroke: if both are in the hexagram and move to give life to or combine with the Self, one will surely be chosen at the head, and it is better still if they hold the official star or Resource, or hold the Body or Self.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.32** 太岁加在有气的官爻上，终究登上黄甲：太岁最喜有情，临官鬼爻是臣子面见君主的象，再得生旺有气，名姓必然高高标榜。(《黄金策》)
  If Tai Sui is added to a Pressure line that has breath, one will in the end enter the Yellow List: Tai Sui most wants to be favourable, standing on the Pressure line is the image of a minister before his sovereign, and if it is further given life and strength and has breath, one's name will surely be set high.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.34** 大局已吉，再得青龙动来生合世爻和卦身，不但成名，必定另有喜事；如果是空而动，出空的月、日有喜。(《黄金策》)
  If the overall figure is already auspicious and the Azure Dragon moves to give life to and combine with the Self and Body, not only will one win a name but there will surely be other joy besides; if it is Void and moving, the joy comes in the month or day it leaves the Void.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.36** 卦里有不成的兆头，但卦身、世爻变出有气的官鬼、父母爻又没有坏，闭门苦读（下帷）可以考中，要等下一次考试才成。(《黄金策》)
  If the hexagram shows a sign of failure but the Body or Self line changes into a Pressure line that has breath and the Resource line is not spoiled, one may pass by shutting oneself away to study, and success comes only on the next sitting.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.37** 占功名，得六冲卦一定难成，得六合卦一定容易得到。(《黄金策》)
  In divining for fame and rank, a Six-Clash hexagram is surely hard to achieve and a Six-Combine hexagram surely easy to gain.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.38** 父母爻、官鬼都宜有力而没有损伤才可成功名：父爻旺相而官鬼空亡或不上卦，文字再好也不能中式；父爻衰弱而官爻旺动、扶起文书，文字平常也可许他成名。(《黄金策》)
  The Resource and Pressure lines should both be strong and unharmed for fame and rank to be achieved: with the Resource line thriving but Pressure Void or absent from the hexagram, however fine the writing one cannot pass; with the Resource line weak but the Pressure line strong and moving and raising the document line up, one may be granted a name even if the writing is ordinary.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.39** 应爻来合、日辰来生，必靠推荐；动爻受伤、被日辰克，还得守着鸡窗读书。父爻、官星变绝，名必不成；如果应爻、动爻或月建、日辰扶起官鬼，必须托人推荐，或者用钱财去求才可成。(《黄金策》)
  If the Other combines and the Day gives life, one will surely rely on a recommendation; if the moving line is harmed and the Day controls it, one must go on keeping to the chicken window. If the Resource line or the official star changes into extinction, the name will surely not be made; if the Other line, a moving line, the Month or the Day raises the Pressure line up, one must ask someone to recommend one or use money to seek it, and then it can succeed.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.05** 占所学的经：文章（父母）旺、官星兴旺是国家栋梁；世爻落空、子孙动是寒士。官持世而父动相生，或父母持世、官动相生、日月生世、世动化吉，都是飞腾之兆；世空世破、动变凶、子孙财爻持世、父母官鬼空破，宜另学一部经，诚心再占一次。(《增删卜易》)
  Divining the classic one studies: with the writing (Resource) strong and the official star thriving one is a pillar of the realm; with the Self Void and Output moving one is a poor scholar. Pressure holding the Self with Resource moving to give it life, or Resource holding the Self with Pressure moving to give life, or the Day or Month giving life to the Self, or the Self moving into an auspicious line, are all signs of soaring; if the Self is Void or broken, or moves into ill, or Output or Wealth holds the Self, or Resource and Pressure are Void or broken, one should study another classic and divine again with sincerity.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.27** 乡试、会试：父爻旺、官星兴，名上虎榜；父爻或官爻持世而旺相，或临日月，或得日月动爻生扶，或动而变吉，名登金榜。子孙动、财爻动，或子孙、财爻持世、在卦中发动，一定落第。(《增删卜易》)
  In the provincial and metropolitan examinations: with the Resource line strong and the official star thriving one's name goes on the Tiger List; if the Resource or Pressure line holds the Self and is thriving, or stands on the Day or the Month, or is given life and support by the Day, the Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into an auspicious line, one's name appears on the Golden List. If Output stirs and Wealth moves, or Output or Wealth holds the Self or moves in the hexagram, one will surely fail.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.28** 兄弟爻在考试里被叫做夺标的恶客，俗传兄爻持世或发动都不吉，其实有喜有忌：财爻、父爻同兴时喜兄动制财；父、官两动时喜兄弟持世、父动生世；兄弟持世临日月或旺相、官文两旺，必能中式，只是不能夺魁。觉子补充：官鬼、子孙同动，官旺得生扶、子孙衰弱被克，再有父动最好，最怕兄兴。(《增删卜易》)
  The Peer line is called the unwelcome guest who snatches the prize; common lore says Peer holding the Self or moving is ill, but there are welcome cases and feared cases: when Wealth and Resource both flourish it is a joy that Peer moves to control Wealth; when Resource and Pressure both move it is welcome that Peer holds the Self and the moving Resource gives it life; with Peer holding the Self and standing on the Day or the Month or thriving, and the official star and the writing both strong, one will surely pass, but cannot take first place. Juezi adds: when Pressure and Output both move, with Pressure strong and given life and Output weak and controlled, a moving Resource line is best, and Peer flourishing is most to be feared.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.29** 官星、父爻、世爻里有一个被动爻克制，或变成回头克，只要日月冲制了那个克它的神，也许能得意，所以日月叫制服恶煞的将军；世爻旺相而官爻、父爻有一个伏藏不现，日月冲制飞神、提出伏神，也许高中；三者有一个不旺，得日月生扶，同此。(《增删卜易》)
  If one of the official star, the Resource line and the Self is controlled by a moving line or changes into a line that turns back to control it, one may still succeed provided the Day or the Month clashes and restrains the controlling spirit, which is why the Day and Month are called the generals who subdue baleful spirits; if the Self is thriving but one of the Pressure and Resource lines lies hidden, and the Day or the Month clashes and restrains the flying line and brings the hidden line out, one may pass high; if one of the three is not strong but the Day or the Month gives it life and support, the same applies.

## 动变(Moving lines and what they turn into)→ `动变.md`

- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.07** 动爻变出来的神有进神、退神，吉凶要分喜忌：吉神喜欢化进神，忌神喜欢化退神。进有三种：旺相的趁势就进；休囚的等到得时才进；动爻或变爻有一个遇到空、破、冲、合的，等到填补、合住、冲开的时候才进。退也有三种：旺相的有日月或动爻生扶，占近期的事暂时不退；休囚的当时就退；动变爻有一个遇空破冲合的，等到那个时候填补、合冲了才退。书中例：申月癸卯日占乡试，得恒之大过卦，酉金官星持世，日辰冲而暗动，又得第五爻官化进神拱扶，断为不但今秋中举，来春也定占鳌头；结果登了乡榜，第二年辰年连中——「化进神」应在今秋，「联捷」应在辰年冲而逢合。(《卜筮正宗》)
  The spirits that come out of a moving line when it changes are Advancing and Retreating spirits, and their luck divides into the welcome and the feared: an auspicious spirit is glad to change into an Advancing spirit, and an Adversary is glad to change into a Retreating spirit. There are three ways of advancing: a strong spirit advances by riding its momentum; a resting or trapped one advances by waiting for its season; and if the moving line or the changed line meets Void, break, clash or combination, it advances by waiting for the period that fills, combines or clashes it. There are also three ways of retreating: a strong spirit given life and support by the Day, the Month or a moving line does not retreat for the time being when a near matter is divined; a resting or trapped one retreats at once; and if the moving line or the changed line meets Void, break, clash or combination, it retreats when the period comes that fills, combines or clashes it. The case in the book: on a Gui-Mao day in the Shen month a man divined the provincial examination and got Heng changing to Daguo; the Pressure star in You Metal held the Self and was stirred in secret by the clash of the Day, and the fifth line's Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit supported it, so the judgement was that not only would he pass in the autumn but next spring he would surely take first place; he made the provincial list and succeeded again in the Chen year after: the change into an Advancing spirit answered in this autumn, and the consecutive success answered in the Chen year, a clash that meets a combination.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.40** 本是必中的卦，但世爻发动变入墓、绝，恐怕成名之后不能享福；游魂卦的死在路上，归魂卦的到家才死，入墓绝的爻就是太岁的，过一年就死。(《黄金策》)

- **LY.EXAM.ZS.17** 父爻衰而发动变旺、变回头生、变进神，文章越写越好，后胜于前；最不宜变退神：旺的变退神，前面顺利后劲不足；衰的变退神，以及变克、变空破墓绝、变回头克，是窗下千日用功、临场一日不济。(《增删卜易》)

- **LY.EXAM.ZS.57** 动爻变出来的爻是进神或退神，吉凶要分喜忌：所喜的宜化进神，所忌的宜化退神。进神有八对：亥化子、寅化卯、巳化午、申化酉、丑化辰、辰化未、未化戌、戌化丑，退神是它们的反方向；进神像春天的树木越长越旺、像有源头的水，有长久的样子，退神像秋天的花木渐渐凋零。书中例一：申月癸卯日占乡试，得恒卦变大过卦，酉金官星持世、旺相当令，卯日冲动它，又有第五爻官爻化进神扶助，断为今秋折桂、明春占鳌头，后来果然联捷。例二：酉月乙丑日占乡试中不中，得兑卦变讼卦，世爻未土化进神、巳火官爻发动生世，吉兆分明，断为坐等报喜，果然戊寅日揭榜中第八名；书上说古法认为动爻遇破、遇散、变到日月上还不能进，这个卦动爻散了又化空，也照样考中。(《增删卜易》)
  A line that comes out of a moving line when it changes is an Advancing or Retreating spirit, and its luck divides into the welcome and the feared: what is welcome is better changing into an Advancing spirit, and what is feared is better changing into a Retreating spirit. There are eight pairs of Advancing spirits (Hai into Zi, Yin into Mao, Si into Wu, Shen into You, Chou into Chen, Chen into Wei, Wei into Xu, Xu into Chou), and the Retreating spirits are their reverse; an Advancing spirit is like spring trees coming into leaf or water that has a source, the look of what lasts long, and a Retreating spirit is like flowers and trees in autumn withering little by little. Second case: on a Yi-Chou day in the You month a man divined whether he would pass the provincial examination and got Dui changing to Song; Wei Earth at the Self changed into an Advancing spirit and the Si Fire Pressure line moved to give life to the Self, an omen plainly good, so the judgement was to sit and wait for the news, and the list was indeed posted on a Wu-Yin day with him at number eight; the book says that by the old rule a moving line that meets a break or a scattering, or changes onto the Day or Month, still cannot advance, yet here the moving line was scattered and changed into Void and he passed all the same.

## 延师(Teachers and schools: engaging, following or keeping a teacher)→ `延师.md`

- **LY.EXAM.BSZZ.10** 占别人的事，不能一概拿应爻当用神：交往疏远的人、没有尊卑名分的人，才以应爻代表他人；占师傅、一家之主、父亲的朋友这类长辈，用神是父母爻；占子孙的朋友，用神是子孙爻；占妻妾奴婢，用神是妻财爻。父亲的朋友、自己的朋友和子孙的朋友，虽然都是别人，要按老幼、称呼、名分来取用神，不能一概用应位断错。(《黄金策》)
  In divining another person's matter one must not always take the Other line as the Subject: it stands for the other person only when he is a distant acquaintance or has no rank of seniority; for a teacher, the head of a household or an older friend of one's father, the Subject is the Resource line; for the friend of one's descendants, the Output line; for wives, concubines, maids and servants, the Wealth line. A father's friend, one's own friend and the friend of one's descendants are all other people, but the Subject must be taken according to age, form of address and status, and one must not always misjudge by the Other position.
- **LY.EXAM.HJC.01** 求老师先看文章（父母爻）：父母爻代表书籍、学馆和老师的学问。老师没有尊卑称呼的，以应爻为用神；有尊卑名分的，不看应爻，按名分论；学生占投师，不论老少，都以父母爻为师长。用神休囚，老师畏缩拘束，不能做楷模；旺相有气，魁梧雄伟，可敬可学。(《黄金策》)
  In seeking a teacher look first to the writing (the Resource line): the Resource line stands for the books, the school and the teacher's learning. If the Subject is resting and trapped the teacher is timid and cramped and cannot be a model; if thriving and having breath he is tall and imposing and worthy of respect and imitation.

## 应期(Timing: when the list is posted and the result known)→ `应期.md`

- **LY.EXAM.ZS.24** 占考试结果几时公布（发案、挂榜），以父母爻为用神：衰的，应在它得生、得旺的日子；旺而静的，应在值日或冲日；动的，应在值日或合日；落空和入墓的，要等冲开；被破和旬空的，要等填实。(《增删卜易》)
  To divine when the result will be issued and the board posted, the Resource line is the Subject: if weak, it answers on the day that gives it life or strength; if strong and still, on the day of its own branch or the day that clashes it; if moving, on the day of its own branch or the day that combines with it; if Void or in the tomb, one must wait until it is clashed open; if broken or in the Void, one must wait until it is filled.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.25** 心里忧虑害怕的，如果子孙持世，在子孙值日的那天发案；心里欢喜的，如果官星持世，在官鬼值日的那天挂榜。(《增删卜易》)
  For one whose heart is anxious and fearful, if Output holds the Self, the list is issued on the day of Output; for one whose heart is glad, if the official star holds the Self, the board is posted on the day of Pressure.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.46** 占考试没有不问应期的，所以讲应期的句子要熟记，自然能触类旁通。(《增删卜易》)
  No divination for an examination fails to ask about the time of fulfilment, so the lines on timing should be learned by heart, and one will understand by analogy and decide.
- **LY.EXAM.ZS.59** 太岁平常和别的爻一样看，只有占官事、占年运、占乡试会试、做官、面圣、上书、叩阍、诤谏、请封请荫这几类事才要紧：宜太岁生合世爻，动而生世更吉，最忌刑冲克世。原因是太岁虽管一年的令，但尊而不亲，吉凶都比不上日月。书中例：巳月壬子日占乡试，得水地比卦，巳火父爻当月建、文星旺，只嫌官星持世落旬空，许他卯科才能得意；子年占，果然卯年登科，应在卯年，是太岁把空填实了。(《增删卜易》)
  The Tai Sui is read like any other line, and is important only in a few kinds of divination: official affairs, the fortune of the year, the provincial and metropolitan examinations, taking office, an audience with the emperor, submitting a memorial, beating the drum at the palace gate, remonstrating, and petitioning for a title; there it is good for the Tai Sui to give life to or combine with the Self line, better still if it moves to give life to the Self, and most of all one fears it punishing, clashing with or controlling the Self. The reason is that though the Tai Sui rules the command of a year it is honoured but not close, and its luck and ill never match those of the Day and Month. The case in the book: on a Ren-Zi day in the Si month a man divined the provincial examination and got Bi; the Si Fire Resource line stood on the Month and the star of writing was strong, and only the Pressure star held the Self while Void in its ten-day period, so I promised him that the Mao-year examination would bring success; he divined in the Zi year and indeed passed in the Mao year, answering in the Mao year because the Tai Sui filled the void.
