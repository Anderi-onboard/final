*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Examinations · Timing: when the list is posted and the result known*

4 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.EXAM.ZS.24 发案挂榜章·应期：父母为用神，衰者于生旺之日，旺而静者逢值逢冲之日，动而逢值逢合之日，空与入墓必待冲开，破与旬空须期填实

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占考试结果几时公布（发案、挂榜），以父母爻为用神：衰的，应在它得生、得旺的日子；旺而静的，应在值日或冲日；动的，应在值日或合日；落空和入墓的，要等冲开；被破和旬空的，要等填实。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To divine when the result will be issued and the board posted, the Resource line is the Subject: if weak, it answers on the day that gives it life or strength; if strong and still, on the day of its own branch or the day that clashes it; if moving, on the day of its own branch or the day that combines with it; if Void or in the tomb, one must wait until it is clashed open; if broken or in the Void, one must wait until it is filled.

**《增删卜易·发案挂榜章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10092 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on issuing the list and posting the board", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 10092*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父母为用神，衰者，于生旺之日； | 以父母爻为用神，衰的，在它得生、得旺的日子应验； | The Resource line is the Subject; if weak, it is fulfilled on the day that gives it life or strength; |
| 旺而静者，逢值逢冲之日； | 旺而静的，在逢值、逢冲的日子； | if strong and still, on the day when it is met by its own branch or clashed; |
| 动而逢值逢合之日； | 动的，在逢值、逢合的日子； | if moving, on the day when it is met by its own branch or combined; |
| 空与入墓，必待冲开； | 落空和入墓的，一定要等冲开； | if Void or in the tomb, one must wait until it is clashed open; |
| 破与旬空，须期填实。 | 被破和旬空的，要等到填实。 | if broken or in the Void, one must wait until it is filled. |

> **按**:「发案」是考官公布取中的名单（案），「挂榜」是把榜张挂出来。「值」是爻的地支恰好是当日的地支；「填实」是等到落空的地支那一日。这一章后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> **Note:** "Issuing the list" is the examiner publishing the list of those taken, and "posting the board" is hanging the board up. "Meeting its own branch" is the day whose branch is the same as the line's branch; "filled" means waiting for the day whose branch is the one that is Void. The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.25 发案挂榜章·心有忧惧者得子孙持世则值子孙之日发案；心有欢喜者得官星持世则逢官鬼之日挂榜（野鹤验得）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:心里忧虑害怕的，如果子孙持世，在子孙值日的那天发案；心里欢喜的，如果官星持世，在官鬼值日的那天挂榜。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For one whose heart is anxious and fearful, if Output holds the Self, the list is issued on the day of Output; for one whose heart is glad, if the official star holds the Self, the board is posted on the day of Pressure.

**《增删卜易·发案挂榜章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10093 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on issuing the list and posting the board", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 10093*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 余又得验：心有忧惧者，若得子孙持世，值子孙之日而发案； | 我又验证到：心里有忧虑、害怕的，如果得子孙持世，在子孙值日的那天发案； | I have also found by experience: for one whose heart is anxious and fearful, if Output holds the Self, the list is issued on the day of Output; |
| 心有欢喜者，若得官星持世，逢官鬼之日而挂榜。 | 心里欢喜的，如果得官星持世，在官鬼值日的那天挂榜。 | for one whose heart is glad, if the official star holds the Self, the board is posted on the day of Pressure. |

> **按**:「心有忧惧」「心有欢喜」是占的人当时的心情，书里没有再解释；这一条按字面译，是作者自己试出来的验法，与上一条按用神定应期的办法并用。
>
> **Note:** "The heart anxious and fearful" and "the heart glad" are the asker's mood at the time, which the book does not explain further; this is translated as written. It is a rule the author found by trial, used alongside the previous entry, which fixes the time by the Subject.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.46 乡试会试章·凡占无不以应期为问，应期章句宜熟记之，触类旁通而决

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占考试没有不问应期的，所以讲应期的句子要熟记，自然能触类旁通。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** No divination for an examination fails to ask about the time of fulfilment, so the lines on timing should be learned by heart, and one will understand by analogy and decide.

**《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10293 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 10293*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 凡占，无不以应期为问，故应期章句宜熟记之，自能触类旁通而决矣。 | 凡是占卦，没有不问应期的，所以应期章的句子要熟记，自然能触类旁通而断。 | In every divination one asks about the time of fulfilment, so the lines of the chapter on timing should be memorised, and one will understand by analogy and decide. |

> **按**:增删卜易有专讲应期的章节；这一条只是提醒要熟记，本身没有具体规则。放在这里，是因为发案挂榜的应期已经在 LY.EXAM.ZS.24、25 里。
>
> **Note:** The Zengshan Buyi has a chapter devoted to timing; this entry only urges one to memorise it and gives no specific rule itself. It is placed here because the timing for issuing the list and posting the board is already in LY.EXAM.ZS.24 and 25.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.59 千金赋·岁君：太岁只在占官事、年运、乡会试、仕宦、面圣上书等事里才要紧，宜生合世爻、最忌刑冲克世，其余按他爻看；例：巳月壬子日占乡试，官星持世旬空，许之卯科，子年占果于卯年登科（太岁填实）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:太岁平常和别的爻一样看，只有占官事、占年运、占乡试会试、做官、面圣、上书、叩阍、诤谏、请封请荫这几类事才要紧：宜太岁生合世爻，动而生世更吉，最忌刑冲克世。原因是太岁虽管一年的令，但尊而不亲，吉凶都比不上日月。书中例：巳月壬子日占乡试，得水地比卦，巳火父爻当月建、文星旺，只嫌官星持世落旬空，许他卯科才能得意；子年占，果然卯年登科，应在卯年，是太岁把空填实了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Tai Sui is read like any other line, and is important only in a few kinds of divination: official affairs, the fortune of the year, the provincial and metropolitan examinations, taking office, an audience with the emperor, submitting a memorial, beating the drum at the palace gate, remonstrating, and petitioning for a title; there it is good for the Tai Sui to give life to or combine with the Self line, better still if it moves to give life to the Self, and most of all one fears it punishing, clashing with or controlling the Self. The reason is that though the Tai Sui rules the command of a year it is honoured but not close, and its luck and ill never match those of the Day and Month. The case in the book: on a Ren-Zi day in the Si month a man divined the provincial examination and got Bi; the Si Fire Resource line stood on the Month and the star of writing was strong, and only the Pressure star held the Self while Void in its ten-day period, so I promised him that the Mao-year examination would bring success; he divined in the Zi year and indeed passed in the Mao year, answering in the Mao year because the Tai Sui filled the void.

| 岁君乃当年之太岁，或明动暗动冲克世爻，占官事者，必于朝廷； | 岁君就是当年的太岁，不管明动还是暗动，冲克世爻，占官事的，一定牵涉朝廷； | The Tai Sui is the year's own Tai Sui; whether it moves openly or in secret, if it clashes with or controls the Self line, one divining an official matter will surely be drawn to the court; |

The "star of writing" is the Resource line (the essay). The hexagram diagram is not included. What follows in the book (the Tai Sui's break and combination, strength and weakness) is outside examinations and is not included.
