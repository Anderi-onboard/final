## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.02 启蒙节要·六亲发动诀·父母发动：父动当头克子孙，士人科举登金榜

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父母爻（文书）发动，当头克制子孙时，读书人应科举能登上金榜。同一诀里还说，父母发动时，等远行的人、盼书信会有动静；打官司下状子，先把是非分清；失物、逃亡要靠告状评理才能了结。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Resource line (the document line) moves and squarely controls Output, a scholar sitting the civil examinations is placed on the golden list. The same verse adds that with Resource moving, a traveller or a letter being waited for shows movement, a plaint filed in a lawsuit first sorts out right from wrong, and lost goods or a runaway have to be pleaded for in court.

## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.03 启蒙节要·六亲发动诀·子孙发动：子孙发动伤官鬼，谒贵求名休进用，劝君守分听乎天

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙爻发动，会伤官鬼；这时拜见贵人、求功名都不宜往前进取，诀劝人守住本分、听凭天意。同一诀里，子孙发动对别的事是吉的：病人求医就痊愈，出门人和做买卖的平安，婚姻美满，产妇生子好养，官司私下和解不到官府。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Output line moves it wounds Pressure; then calling on a patron or seeking a title should not press forward, and the verse urges one to keep to one's lot and leave it to Heaven. The same verse treats a moving Output as good for other matters: the sick who seek a physician recover, travellers and traders are safe, a marriage is a happy one, a woman in labour bears a child easy to rear, and a lawsuit is settled privately without reaching the court.

## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.04 启蒙节要·六亲发动诀·财爻发动：财爻发动克文书，应举求名总是虚

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:财爻发动，克住父母文书：应考、求名总是落空。同一诀里，财动对别的事是吉的：拿本钱经营大吉，亲戚婚姻如意。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Wealth line moves and controls the document line, sitting the examinations and seeking a title all come to nothing. The same verse says a moving Wealth line is good elsewhere: trading with capital is greatly auspicious and marriages and family ties go as one wishes.

**《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·四十六、六亲发动诀》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 257 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 46: the verse on the moving of the six relations", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 257*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 财爻发动克文书， | 财爻发动，克住文书， | The Wealth line moves and controls the document line: |
| 应举求名总是虚， | 应考、求名，总是落空； | sitting the examinations and seeking a title always come to nothing; |
| 将本经营为大吉， | 拿本钱去经营，大吉； | trading with capital is greatly auspicious; |
| 亲姻如意乐无虞， | 亲戚婚姻如意，快乐无忧。 | marriages and family ties go as one wishes, glad and free of worry. |

> **按**:诀文说的是财克父母文书的一般情形。《黄金策》另讲了一种例外：父母爻空亡，而财爻发动生扶官鬼，侥幸可以成（LY.EXAM.HJC.24）。
>
> **Note:** The verse speaks of the usual case of Wealth controlling the Resource line (the document line). The Huangjin Ce gives one exception: when the Resource line is Void and the Wealth line moves to give life and support to Pressure, one may succeed by luck (LY.EXAM.HJC.24).

## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.05 启蒙节要·六亲发动诀·兄弟交重克财：应举夺标为忌客，官非阴贼耗钱财

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:兄弟爻发动、克住财爻时：病人难好，灾还没有走；应举时，同来夺魁首的人是最要提防的客人；官司和暗中的盗贼会耗损钱财。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the Peer line moves and controls Wealth: the sick are hard to cure and the calamity has not left; in sitting the examinations, those who contend for the top place are the guests most to be avoided; and lawsuits and secret thieves waste money.

**《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·四十六、六亲发动诀》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 258 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 46: the verse on the moving of the six relations", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 258*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 兄弟交重克了财， | 兄弟爻发动，克了财， | The Peer line moves and controls Wealth: |
| 病人难愈未离灾， | 病人难好，灾还没有走； | the sick are hard to cure and the calamity has not left; |
| 应举夺标为忌客， | 应举时，夺魁的人是要忌的客人； | in sitting the examinations, those who contend for the top place are the guests to be avoided; |
| 官非阴贼耗钱财， | 官司和暗中的盗贼，耗损钱财。 | lawsuits and secret thieves waste money. |

> **打架**:诀文把兄弟发动克财看成应举的大忌。《增删卜易》（LY.EXAM.ZS.28）却说兄爻持世或发动不是一概不吉：财、父两爻都动时，兄动制住财反而是喜；父、官两动时，喜兄弟持世。《黄金策》（LY.EXAM.HJC.18）和这句诀一致，把兄弟看成同经的竞争者，必夺魁首。
>
> **Where they disagree:** The verse makes a moving Peer line that controls Wealth a great taboo for sitting the examinations. The Zengshan Buyi (LY.EXAM.ZS.28) says that Peer holding the Self or moving is not ill throughout: when Wealth and Resource both move, Peer moving to control Wealth is on the contrary welcome, and when Resource and Pressure both move, Peer holding the Self is welcome. The Huangjin Ce (LY.EXAM.HJC.18) agrees with this verse in treating Peer as rivals who share one's classic and will snatch first place.

- 《断易天机》:同文 第 845 行:兄弟交重克了财;同文 第 845 行:病人难愈未离灾;同文 第 845 行:官非阴贼耗钱财

## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.06 启蒙节要·诸爻持世诀·考试三句：父母持世，官动财旺宜赴试；子孙持世，求名切忌；鬼爻持世，功名最喜世当权

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:诸爻持世诀里和考试有关的三句：父母爻持世，官爻发动、财爻旺相，适合去赴考；子孙爻持世，求名最忌（子孙坐在世位上）；官鬼爻持世，别的事难安，只有求功名最喜欢世爻当权。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Three lines of the verse on lines holding the Self bear on examinations: with Resource holding the Self, if Pressure moves and Wealth is strong it is a good time to go and sit the examination; with Output holding the Self, seeking a title above all should avoid it; with Pressure holding the Self, other matters are uneasy, but for success in the examinations the best thing is that the Self holds power.

| 鬼爻持世事难安， | 官鬼爻持世，事情难安， | Pressure holding the Self, matters are uneasy; |

> **按**:诀文没有解释「官动财旺」为什么宜赴试；读的时候要和「财爻发动克文书，应举求名总是虚」（LY.EXAM.BSZZ.04）分开看：这里的前提是父母爻持世、官爻发动，那一句只说财动克文书。引文里夹着的求嗣、谋利、占身、失脱几句说的是别的事，因为引文要连着引、中间不能断，所以留在了里面。
>
> **Note:** The verse does not explain why Pressure moving and Wealth strong should be fit for sitting the examination. Read it apart from "Wealth moves and controls the document line, so sitting exams and seeking a title all come to nothing" (LY.EXAM.BSZZ.04): the premise here is Resource holding the Self with Pressure moving, whereas that line speaks only of Wealth moving against the document line. The clauses about heirs, profit, the body and loss that sit inside the quotations are other matters; they are kept because each quotation must run on without a gap.

## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.08 十八问答·第十三问·六冲六合：所恶宜冲、所好宜合；冲中逢合、合处逢冲分日辰与变爻；冲忌神合用神为去煞留恩，冲用神合忌神为留煞害命；例：会试未土持世，化出日辰午火官星生合，果中探花

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:六冲、六合怎么断：人所厌恶的事宜冲，所喜欢的事宜合，只有占病要分近病、久病：近病遇冲就好、久病遇冲就死，六合则相反。六冲卦里，有日辰相合、变爻相合的，叫冲中逢合；六合卦里，有日辰相冲、变爻相冲的，叫合处逢冲。冲掉忌神、合住用神，叫去煞留恩，事事说吉；冲掉用神、合住忌神，叫留煞害命，件件皆凶。书中例：辰月庚午日占会试，得观卦变否卦，未土持世，变出的午火官星正是日辰，生着合着世爻，断为「鼎甲在掌」，结果中了探花。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** How the Six-Clash and Six-Combine hexagrams are judged: what a person hates is better clashed and what he likes is better combined, and only in divining illness is there the distinction between a recent and a long illness: a recent illness that meets a clash is cured, a long one dies, and with a combination it is the reverse. If the Adversary is clashed and the Subject combined, it is called removing the baleful and keeping the gracious and every matter is called lucky; if the Subject is clashed and the Adversary combined, it is called keeping the baleful to harm life and every matter is ill. The case in the book: on a Geng-Wu day in the Chen month a man divined the metropolitan examination and got Guan changing to Pi; Wei Earth held the Self and changed out into the Wu Fire Pressure star, which is the Day itself, giving it life and combining with it, so the judgement was that the top three places lay in the palm of his hand, and he indeed passed as Tanhua.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.53 八宫章·占功名秘法（士子）：先占今科，再占下科，再占终身；官鬼持世金榜标名、子孙持世今科失望；孝廉占会试、童生占入学同此法；要节节问去，先问眼前再问久远

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:读书人占功名，要一步一步问：先占今科中不中，官鬼持世就金榜标名，子孙持世就知道今科没有指望；改天再占下一科，再另选一天占终身能不能中，官鬼持世终能奋发，仍是子孙持世就改行另谋出路。举人占会试、童生占入学，照这个办法。占事要节节问下去，先问眼前的疑问，不要先问一生久远的事，因为神灵回报的多半是近事。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A scholar divining for a title should ask step by step: first whether he will pass this year's examination, where Pressure holding the Self means his name is on the golden list and Output holding the Self means this year is hopeless; on another day whether he will pass the next, and on yet another day whether he will ever pass in his life, where Pressure holding the Self means he will in the end rise to success and Output holding the Self again means he should change his occupation. A provincial graduate divining for the metropolitan examination and a boy candidate divining for entry into the school follow the same method. One must ask step by step, the doubt at hand first and the distant future afterwards, because what the spirit reports is mostly the near matter.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.60 随鬼入墓章·占功名：世旺得地，冲开墓库之年月成名；世若休囚空破，始终难成；例：戌月甲寅日占会试能联捷，世爻随官入三墓而日月合成官局，辰年辰月冲开三墓，联捷鼎甲

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻入墓只验三种（世爻随鬼入日墓、入动墓、动而化墓），自占看世爻、代占看用神，旺相的不是真入墓，只有休囚被克又入墓才是，墓神被日月动爻冲破的也不算。占功名：世爻旺相得地，冲开墓库的年月成名；世爻休囚空破，始终难成。书中例：戌月甲寅日占会试能不能联捷，得小过卦变艮卦，世爻随官爻入三个墓（动墓、化墓、入月建之墓），断为明年辰年、发榜之时又在辰月，冲开三个墓，不但联捷，必定名列鼎甲，因为日月合成官局、旺相当令，卦象十全；结果果然传胪三唱。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Self entering the tomb proves true in only three ways (the Self entering the tomb of the Day with Pressure, entering a moving tomb, and moving and changing into a tomb); in one's own divination watch the Self and in a proxy's watch the Subject; a strong one is not truly entering the tomb, and only a resting or controlled one that also enters the tomb is the real thing, and a tomb spirit broken by the Day, the Month or a moving line does not count either. In divining for a title: if the Self is strong and in place, the name is made in the year or month that clashes open the tomb; if the Self is resting, trapped, Void or broken, success is hard from start to finish. The case in the book: on a Jia-Yin day in the Xu month a man divined whether he would pass the metropolitan examination in consecutive rounds and got Xiaoguo changing to Gen; the Self followed Pressure into three tombs (a moving tomb, a changing tomb and the Month's tomb), and the judgement was that next year is the Chen year and the posting of the list falls again in the Chen month, clashing open all three tombs, so he would not only pass in consecutive rounds but be among the top three, since the Day and Month combine into a Pressure frame, strong and in command, the hexagram perfect; and indeed his name was called out at the palace announcement.
