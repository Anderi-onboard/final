## LY.EXAM.HJC.02 求师·临刑临害好施夏楚之威（带刑害白虎则师性暴少慈好笞挞）；逢岁逢身业擅束修之养（用爻卦身持太岁则专以严训为业、务得束修养家）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用爻临刑、临害，老师爱用戒尺显威，带着刑害白虎的，性情暴躁少慈，必定爱打人，旺而动更甚；用爻逢太岁、逢卦身，老师以严格训导为业，一定要拿到束修来养家。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Subject line stands on punishment or harm, the teacher loves to show his severity with the rod, and one carrying punishment, harm and the White Tiger is violent and short of kindness and surely loves to flog, the more so if strong and moving; if the Subject line meets Tai Sui or the Body, the teacher makes strict instruction his trade and must have the tuition fee to keep his family.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.03 求师·兑金震巽杂学堪推、离火乾坤专经可断：父母爻落震巽艮坎兑五卦为杂学，落离乾坤三宫为专经

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:推断老师是专治一经还是学问杂：父母爻落在震、巽、艮、坎、兑五卦里的，是杂学；落在离、乾、坤三宫里的，是专经。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** To judge whether a teacher specialises in one classic or has a miscellaneous learning: a Resource line in the five trigrams Zhen, Xun, Gen, Kan and Dui means miscellaneous learning; in the three palaces Li, Qian and Kun it means a single classic.

| 离火乾坤， | 离火、乾、坤， | Li Fire, Qian and Kun: |
| 专经可断 | 可以断定他专治一经 | one may judge a specialist in a single classic |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1821–1822 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, lines 1821–1822*

A "single classic" is a lifelong specialisation in one classic and "miscellaneous learning" is a spread across many kinds of learning.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.04 求师·本象同乡在内则离家不远、他宫异地在外则隔属须遥（用象在本宫居外卦本处人而住远，在他宫居内卦外郡人而住近）；与世相生非亲则友（与世爻生合有亲道，不同宫者相识朋友）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:老师是哪里的人、住得远近：用神在本宫，是同乡，在内卦离家不远；在别宫，是异地人，在外卦就隔得很远；注补充：本宫而居外卦是本地人但住得远，他宫而居内卦是外郡人却住得近。用神和世相生，不是亲戚就是朋友：与世爻生合有亲谊，不同宫的是相识的朋友。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Where the teacher is from and how far he lives: the Subject in its own palace means a fellow villager, and in the inner trigram he lives not far from home; in another palace he is from another place, and in the outer trigram he lives across a jurisdiction and far away; the commentary adds that in its own palace but in the outer trigram he is a local man who lives far, and in another palace but in the inner trigram he is from another prefecture but lives near. If the Subject and the Self give each other life he is a relative or a friend: giving life to or combining with the Self means kinship, and of a different palace an acquaintance and friend.

**《黄金策·求师》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1824 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a teacher", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 1824*

| 他宫异地， | 在别的宫，是异地人， | in another palace, he is from another place; |
| 在外则隔属须遥 | 在外卦就隔着辖区、相当远。 | in the outer trigram he lives across a jurisdiction and far away. |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1824 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1824*

## LY.EXAM.HJC.08 求师·母化子孙必主能诗能赋（父化福善作杂文，带刑害病败虽能作文多破绽，子带月建加青龙出口成章）；鬼连兄煞定然多诈多奸（兄动化鬼、鬼动化兄皆奸诈）；口是心非临空亡而发动（用爻宜静不宜动、宜旺不宜空）

> **按**:「首唱无疑」大意是名列第一没有疑问；卦图不收，这一段结尾的「又如」以后是下一个例子，不收。《卜筮正宗》里也有一个观卦变否卦的会试例（LY.EXAM.BSZZ.08），日期和断语都不一样。

> **Note:** The phrase "first place is beyond doubt" means in sense that coming first is not in question; the hexagram diagram is not included, and what follows "another" at the end of this passage belongs to the next case and is not included. The Bushi Zhengzong also has a metropolitan-examination case of Guan changing to Pi (LY.EXAM.BSZZ.08), with different dates and a different judgement.
