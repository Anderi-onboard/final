# 六爻 · 考试 · 小考

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Examinations · The minor examinations: county, prefecture, circuit, annual and qualifying, the stipend*

5 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.EXAM.ZS.13 求名章·占小考须先占县考、再占府考、又占道考，都得吉卦才许榜上有名；内有一卦不吉，勿望道考成名（野鹤）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占小考（县考、府考、道考这几关），神应近不应远，一卦吉只应在府县有名；所以要先占县考，再占府考，又占道考，三卦都吉才可以说榜上有名，有一卦不吉，就别指望道考成名。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For the minor examinations (the county, prefecture and circuit examinations): the spirits answer what is near and not what is far, so one auspicious hexagram answers only for the name being on the county or prefecture list; one must divine the county examination first, then the prefecture, then the circuit, and only when all three are auspicious may one say the name will be on the list; if one of them is not auspicious, do not hope for fame at the circuit examination.

**野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 9916 行 · *The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 9916*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 野鹤曰：或问凡占小考，尝见卜得吉卦，后竟道考无名者，何也？ | 野鹤说：有人问，凡是占小考，常常看到占得吉卦，后来竟然道考没有名字，这是为什么？ | The Old Man of Yehe says: someone asked, "In divining for the minor examinations I have often seen an auspicious hexagram obtained and then the name still did not appear at the circuit examination; why is that?" |
| 余曰：神每应近而不应远，已吉卦者，乃应府县之有名也，故凡占小考者，必须先占县考，再占府考，又占道考，俱得吉卦者，许之榜上有名。 | 我说：神常常应近的、不应远的，已经得吉卦的，是应在府县有名；所以凡是占小考的，必须先占县考，再占府考，又占道考，都得吉卦的，才许他榜上有名。 | I said: the spirits usually answer what is near and not what is far; an auspicious hexagram already obtained answers that the name will be on the county and prefecture lists; so for the minor examinations one must first divine the county examination, then the prefecture examination, then the circuit examination, and only if all give auspicious hexagrams may one say that the name will be on the list. |
| 内有一卦而不吉者，勿望道考而成名也。 | 其中有一卦不吉的，不要指望道考能成名。 | If any one of them is not auspicious, do not hope for fame at the circuit examination. |

> **按**:「小考」是科举里考秀才之前的考试：县考、府考、道考（道是提学道，管一省学政的官），考过才得进县学成生员。这一条是野鹤自己的说明，[乾按]说「若以一卦而断数考必大谬」，是今人按语，不收。
>
> **Note:** The "minor examinations" are the examinations before the one that makes a licentiate: the county, prefecture and circuit examinations (the circuit being the education intendant who ran the schools of a province); passing them lets one enter the county school and become a student. This is the Old Man of Yehe's own explanation; the [Qian's note] saying that judging several examinations by one hexagram is a great mistake is a modern comment and is not quoted.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.14 童试章·父旺官兴堪期首选、官衰父发亦论抡收；官星持世得日月动爻生合首选无疑；父爻持世得生合还要官星有气始许成名

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:童试：父爻旺、官星兴，可期名列榜首；官衰而父爻发动，也算能被选中；官星持世，得日月动爻相生相合，首选没有疑问；父爻持世得生合，一定要官星有气，才许成名。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In the youth examinations: with the Resource line strong and the official star thriving one may hope to be first; with the official star weak but the Resource line moving one is still counted as selected; with the official star holding the Self and the Day, the Month and moving lines giving it life and combining with it, being first is beyond doubt; with the Resource line holding the Self and given life and combination, the official star must also have breath before one may win a name.

**《增删卜易·童试章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 9921 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the youth examinations", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 9921*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 父旺官兴，堪期首选，官衰父发，亦论抡收。 | 父爻旺、官星兴，可以期望名列榜首；官衰而父爻发动，也算能被选中。 | With the Resource line strong and the official star thriving, one may hope to be chosen first; with the official star weak but the Resource line moving, one is still counted as selected. |
| 官星持世，得日月动爻相生相合，首选无疑。 | 官星持世，得到日月、动爻相生相合，名列榜首没有疑问。 | If the official star holds the Self and the Day, the Month and the moving lines give it life and combine with it, being first on the list is beyond doubt. |
| 父爻持世，得日月动爻生合者，必要官星有气，始许成名。 | 父爻持世，得到日月、动爻生合的，一定要官星有气，才许成名。 | If the Resource line holds the Self and the Day, the Month and moving lines give life to and combine with it, the official star must also have breath before one may win a name. |

> **按**:「童试」是考秀才之前的各级考试（县试、府试、院试）；「首选」「抡收」是被选中、取中。官星就是官鬼。
>
> **Note:** The "youth examinations" are the examinations before the one that makes a licentiate; "chosen first" and "selected" mean being picked and admitted. The official star is the Pressure line.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.15 童试章·子兴财发于卦中难游泮水；子动克官、财动克父、或持于世爻者皆不宜

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:童试时子孙兴旺、财爻发动在卦中，难以入县学；子孙动克官星、财爻动克父爻，或者子孙、财爻持在世爻上，都不适宜。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In the youth examinations, if Output flourishes and Wealth moves in the hexagram it is hard to enter the county school; Output moving and controlling Pressure, or Wealth moving and controlling Resource, or either of them holding the Self line, is unfavourable in every case.

**《增删卜易·童试章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 9922 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the youth examinations", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 9922*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 子兴财发于卦中，难游泮水。 | 子孙兴旺、财爻发动在卦中，难以入县学。 | If Output flourishes and Wealth moves in the hexagram, it is hard to enter the county school. |
| 子动克官，财动克父，或持于世爻者，皆为不宜。 | 子孙动克官，财爻动克父爻，或者持在世爻上的，都不适宜。 | Output moving and controlling Pressure, or Wealth moving and controlling the Resource line, or either holding the Self line, is unfavourable in every case. |

> **按**:「游泮水」是入县学成生员（旧时县学前有泮池，新生入学要行礼，叫「游泮」）。子孙克官星、财爻克父爻是考试里最忌的两种克：官星管功名，父爻管文章。
>
> **Note:** "Swimming in the Pan pond" is entering the county school and becoming a student (the county school had a ceremonial pond, and new students performed a rite there). Output controlling the official star and Wealth controlling the Resource line are the two most feared controls in examinations: the official star governs rank and the Resource line governs the writing.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.16 童试章·世破身空兼墓绝再奋芸窗；文旺遭伤收而复弃，冲中逢合弃而仍收；父母旺相化休囚空破墓绝、回头克或被冲克则始取终弃（附例：官星月破伏藏、飞神合破，择三月考之必取）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世爻被破、卦身落空又入墓逢绝，不能遂愿，只好再苦读；文章旺却遭伤害，先收取后舍弃；父母爻旺相却变休囚、空破、墓绝、回头克或被冲克，是开始被取、最后被弃；世爻、父爻动而遇冲，若得日月动爻相合，叫冲中逢合，一定续取。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** With the Self broken, the Body Void and moreover in the tomb or extinct, one cannot fulfil one's aim and must work hard again at the study window; with the writing strong but harmed, one is taken and then cast aside; if the Resource line is thriving but changes into resting and trapped, Void or broken, tomb, extinction or a line that turns back to control it, or is clashed and controlled, one is taken at first and cast aside in the end; if the Self and Resource lines move and meet a clash but the Day, the Month or a moving line combines with them, this is a combination within a clash and one will surely be taken afterwards.

**《增删卜易·童试章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 9923–9924 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the youth examinations", base text; Zengshan Buyi, lines 9923–9924*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 世破身空兼墓绝，再奋芸窗。 | 世爻被破、卦身落空，又兼入墓、逢绝，只好再发奋读书。 | With the Self broken, the Body Void, and moreover in the tomb or extinct, one must work hard again at the study window. |
| 世爻空破墓绝，或被日月动爻冲克，及动而变凶者，不能遂志。 | 世爻落空、被破、入墓、逢绝，或者被日月、动爻冲克，以及发动变凶的，不能遂愿。 | If the Self is Void, broken, in the tomb or extinct, or is clashed and controlled by the Day, the Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into an ill line, one cannot fulfil one's aim. |
| 文旺遭伤，收而复弃，冲中逢合，弃而仍收。 | 文章旺却遭伤害，先收取后又舍弃；冲中逢合，舍弃了还会再收取。 | If the writing is strong but harmed, one is taken in and then cast aside; with a combination found within a clash, one cast aside is taken in again. |
| 父母旺相，若化休囚、空破、墓绝，化回头克，及被冲克者，始取终弃。 | 父母爻旺相，如果变成休囚、落空被破、入墓逢绝，变出回头克，以及被冲克的，开始被取、最终被弃。 | If the Resource line is thriving but changes into resting and trapped, Void or broken, tomb or extinction, or into a line that turns back to control it, or is clashed and controlled, one is taken at first and cast aside in the end. |
| 世与父爻，动而逢冲，若得日月动爻相合者，谓之冲中逢合，定获续取。 | 世爻和父爻发动后遇冲，如果又得日月、动爻来合，叫做冲中逢合，一定能获得续取。 | If the Self and Resource lines move and meet a clash, but the Day, the Month or a moving line combines with them, this is called a combination within a clash, and one will surely be taken in afterwards. |

**书中卦例** — 《增删卜易》第 9963 行 · *Case from the book; Zengshan Buyi, line 9963*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 又如：卯月壬子日(旬空：寅卯)，占道考，得“风天小畜” | 再如：卯月壬子日（旬空：寅、卯），占道考，得「风天小畜」 | Another example: on a Ren-Zi day in the Mao month (Void: Yin and Mao), divining for the circuit examination, I obtained "Wind over Heaven, Xiaochu". |

**书中卦例** — 《增删卜易》第 9990 行 · *Case from the book; Zengshan Buyi, line 9990*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 世子水父母临日建，旺文持世，佳作定然得意，但不宜酉金官星临月破，伏藏不露，幸得辰土作飞神，破而逢合，若在三月考之，必取。 | 世爻是子水，父母爻临日建，旺相的文章持世，佳作一定得意；只是不宜酉金官星临月破、又伏藏不现；幸好有辰土做飞神，破了又逢合，如果在三月考，必定录取。 | The Self is Zi Water and the Resource line stands on the Day; a strong writing holds the Self and a fine essay will surely be admired; but it is unfortunate that the Pressure line of You Metal stands on the Month-break and lies hidden and unseen; luckily Chen Earth is its flying line, so what is broken meets a combination, and if the examination is held in the third month he will surely be taken. |
| 果考于三月，取之。 | 果然考试在三月，被录取了。 | The examination was indeed held in the third month and he was taken. |

> **按**:「冲中逢合」是被冲的爻又遇到来合它的日月或动爻，冲的力量被合化解。例子里说官星酉金月破又伏藏，辰土（飞神）和酉合（辰酉合），所以破而逢合，断为「在三月考之必取」。第 2 句里「再奋芸窗」「始取终弃」是榜上被取、后又被除名的情形。
>
> **Note:** A "combination within a clash" is a clashed line meeting a Day, Month or moving line that combines with it, the combination resolving the clash. In the example the official star You Metal is on the Month-break and hidden, and Chen Earth, its flying line, combines with You (Chen and You combine), so what is broken meets a combination, and the verdict is that if the examination is in the third month he will be taken. "Taken at first and cast aside in the end" is the case of being admitted and later struck from the list.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.23 考遗才章·官爻持世生世则必蒙收，父母空破墓绝则不见用；世受克、空破墓绝、动化凶、子孙持世或动则不录；父母持世生世吉，休囚空破凶（凡告考同例）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:考遗才（补考漏掉的人）和告考：官爻持世或生世，一定被收录；父母爻空破墓绝，不会被用；世爻受克或空破墓绝、动变凶、子孙持世或子孙发动，都主不被录取；父母持世生世吉，休囚空破凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For the supplementary examination (taking those who were missed) and for petitioning to sit an examination: the Pressure line holding or giving life to the Self means one will surely be admitted; the Resource line Void, broken, in the tomb or extinct means one will not be used; the Self controlled, or Void, broken, in the tomb or extinct, or moving into ill, or Output holding the Self or moving, all mean one will not be taken; the Resource line holding or giving life to the Self is auspicious, and resting and trapped, Void or broken is inauspicious.

**《增删卜易·考遗才章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10087 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the supplementary examination", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 10087*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 官爻持世生世，我必蒙收； | 官爻持世或生世，我一定被收录； | If the Pressure line holds or gives life to the Self, I will surely be admitted; |
| 父母空破墓绝，他不见用。 | 父母爻空、破、入墓、逢绝，他不会被录用。 | if the Resource line is Void, broken, in the tomb or extinct, he will not be taken into use. |
| 如世爻受克，及空破墓绝，或动而化凶，或子孙持世，或子孙动于卦中，皆主不录。 | 如果世爻受克，以及空、破、墓、绝，或者发动变凶，或者子孙持世，或者子孙在卦中发动，都主不被录取。 | If the Self is controlled, or Void, broken, in the tomb or extinct, or moves and changes into ill, or Output holds the Self, or Output moves in the hexagram, all indicate he will not be taken. |
| 父母持世生世者，皆吉； | 父母爻持世、生世的，都吉； | Resource holding or giving life to the Self is auspicious in every case; |

> **按**:「考遗才」是学官为没赶上考试的人再开的补考；「告考」是自己具呈请求准许应考，章名下注「凡告考同例」。「他不见用」的「他」是主考的人（书里没有明说）。后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> **Note:** The "supplementary examination" is a later examination the school official holds for those who missed the main one; "petitioning to sit" is submitting a petition to be allowed to sit, and the chapter title notes that the same rules apply. The "he" in "he will not be taken into use" is the examiner (the book does not say so). The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
