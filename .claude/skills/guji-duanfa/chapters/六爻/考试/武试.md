# 六爻 · 考试 · 武试

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Examinations · The military examination*

3 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.EXAM.ZS.47 武试章·文试官文两重，武试首重官爻；武场虽不以父为重，官文两旺者竟得抡元

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:文试官星、文章两样都重；武试首先看官爻，武场虽然不把父爻看得重，但官星、文章两旺的，竟然夺得第一名。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In the civil examination the official star and the writing both matter; in the military examination the Pressure line matters first; though the military field does not count the Resource line as important, one with the official star and the writing both strong actually takes first place.

**《增删卜易·武试章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10690 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the military examination", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 10690*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 文试者，官文两重； | 考文试的，官星和文章两个都要紧； | For the civil examination the official star and the writing both matter; |
| 武试者，首重官爻。 | 考武试的，最要紧的是官爻。 | for the military examination, the Pressure line matters first. |
| 武场虽不以父为重，余每得验。 | 考武场虽然不把父爻看得重，但我每次都得到应验： | Though the military field does not count the Resource line as important, I find it confirmed every time: |
| 官文两旺者，竟得抡元。 | 官星、文章两旺的，居然能夺得第一。 | those with the official star and the writing both strong actually take first place. |

> **按**:章名下注「亦可与文试参看」。「抡元」是取得第一名。「文章」是父爻，武试里文章不重，但官、文都旺的仍然能夺魁。
>
> **Note:** The title notes "this may also be read together with the civil examination". "Taking first place" is winning the top spot. The "writing" is the Resource line; in the military examination the writing is not weighty, but one with both the official star and the writing strong can still take the top place.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.48 武试章·世破官空休指望、官兴财旺亦堪图：世与官星有一遇空破不须指望，然亦有财动生官竟得中者，或其中有隐事

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:武试：世爻被破、官星落空，不要指望；官星兴旺、财旺，也可以一图。世爻和官星有一个遇空破的，不必指望；不过也有财动生官星而竟然中了的，作者推测其中或有隐情。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In the military examination, with the Self broken and the official star Void do not hope; with the official star flourishing and Wealth strong one may still try. If either the Self or the official star meets Void or break there is no need to hope; yet there are cases where Wealth moved to give life to the official star and the man passed after all, and the author suspects some hidden matter in it.

**《增删卜易·武试章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10692 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the military examination", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 10692*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 世破官空休指望，官兴财旺亦堪图。 | 世爻被破、官星落空，不要指望；官星兴旺、财爻旺，也可以图谋。 | With the Self broken and the official star Void, do not hope; with the official star flourishing and Wealth strong, one may still try. |
| 世与官星，有一而逢空破者，不须指望。 | 世爻和官星，有一个逢空、逢破的，不必指望。 | If either the Self or the official star meets Void or break, there is no need to hope. |
| 然又有财动生官竟得中者，何也？ | 然而又有财动生官星竟然中了的，这是为什么？ | Yet there are cases where Wealth moved to give life to the official star and the man passed after all; why is that? |
| 或其中有隐事乎？ | 或者其中有什么隐情吧？ | Perhaps there was some hidden matter in it? |

> **按**:作者对「财动生官竟得中」这种例外自己也不确定，只说「或其中有隐事」（比如有人情、钱财的运作），没有给出规则；这里照录，不当作规则。
>
> **Note:** The author is himself unsure about the exception where Wealth moved to give life to the official star and the man passed, and says only that there may have been some hidden matter in it (some favour or money at work) without giving a rule; it is kept as written and not treated as a rule.

相关:LY.EXAM.ZS.47、LY.EXAM.ZS.49

## LY.EXAM.ZS.49 武试章·空赴武闱皆为子孙持世、子孙发动，即使力能扛鼎百步穿杨亦无益；试期病阻乃官鬼伤身，官克世、日月动爻克世、世动化鬼化克，非临场病阻即灾祸临身

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:武试白去，都是因为子孙持世或发动，力气再大、箭法再好也没用；考期因病受阻，是官鬼伤身：官星克世、日月和动爻克世、世动变鬼变克，不是临场病阻，就是灾祸临身。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Going to the military examination in vain is always a case of Output holding the Self or moving, and even strength to lift a tripod and a shot that pierces a willow leaf at a hundred paces are of no help; being blocked by illness at the examination is Pressure harming the Body: the official star controlling the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line controlling the Self, or the Self moving and changing into Pressure or control, means either being blocked by illness at the examination or disaster coming upon one.

**《增删卜易·武试章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10693 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the military examination", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 10693*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 空赴武闱，皆为子孙持世。 | 白去武场的，都是因为子孙持世。 | Those who go to the military examination in vain are all cases of Output holding the Self. |
| 子孙持世，子孙发动，即使力能扛鼎，百步穿杨，亦无益于事矣。 | 子孙持世、子孙发动，即使力气大到能举鼎、百步穿杨，也无益于事。 | With Output holding the Self or Output moving, even if one's strength can lift a tripod and one can pierce a willow leaf at a hundred paces, it is of no help. |
| 试期病阻，乃因官鬼伤身。 | 考期因病受阻，是因为官鬼伤身。 | Being blocked by illness on the examination date is because Pressure harms the Body. |
| 官克世、日月动爻克世、世动化鬼化克，非临场病阻，即灾祸临身。 | 官星克世、日月和动爻克世、世爻发动变鬼变克，不是临场病阻，就是灾祸临身。 | If the official star controls the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line controls the Self, or the Self moves and changes into Pressure or control, then either one is blocked by illness at the examination or disaster comes upon one. |

> **按**:「扛鼎」是举起大鼎，「百步穿杨」是百步外射中柳叶，都是武试的本事；子孙克官星，所以武试里子孙持世、发动最坏。这一条后面的[提要]（今人所加）不收。
>
> **Note:** "Lifting a tripod" and "piercing a willow leaf at a hundred paces" are the skills tested in the military examination; Output controls the official star, so Output holding the Self or moving is the worst in the military examination. The [summary] after it (added by a modern editor) is not quoted.

相关:LY.EXAM.ZS.47
