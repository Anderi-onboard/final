## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.01 十八论·用神分类定例：考试里要看的人和物各有各的用神——功名官府取官鬼，师长、文章、馆室取父母，门生取子孙，结盟同寅和知交朋友取兄弟

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:考试这件事里，先要定下来看哪一爻：问功名（考中、做官），用神是官鬼爻；问老师、文章、奏章、读书的馆舍，用神是父母爻；问学生（门生），用神是子孙爻；问结拜兄弟、同僚和知交朋友，用神是兄弟爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In an examination reading one first settles which line to watch: for success in the examinations and in office the Subject is the Pressure line; for a teacher, an essay, a memorial or the study hall the Subject is the Resource line; for pupils the Subject is the Output line; for sworn brothers, colleagues and close friends the Subject is the Peer line.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.15 求师·搜索六爻无过求理、思量万事莫贵读书：求师不专指道学之师，百工技艺拜僧道为师皆是；自占以世爻看学者，隔手来占须问是何人；师弟相生相合则吉相冲相克则凶

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求师不只指讲道学的老师，学百工技艺、拜僧人道士为师也都是；自己占，以世爻看学习的人；别人代占，要问是什么人，朋友兄弟就以兄弟爻为主；总要师弟相生相合才吉，相冲相克就凶。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Seeking a teacher does not mean only a teacher of moral learning: learning a craft or skill, or taking a monk or Taoist as teacher, all count; when one divines for oneself, the learner is read from the Self line; when another divines on one's behalf one must ask who he is, and for a friend or brother Peer is the main line; in every case teacher and pupil giving life to and combining with each other is auspicious, and clashing and controlling each other inauspicious.

**《黄金策·求师》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1859 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a teacher", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 1859*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |

| 无过求理， | 不过是求得其中的理； | it is no more than seeking the principle in them; |
| 思量万事， | 思量万事， | ponder all affairs, |
| 莫贵读书 | 没有比读书更可贵的 | and nothing is more precious than study |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1859 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1859*

| 但师之主象，自占不以父母； | 但是老师的主象，自己占时不用父母爻； | But the main image of the teacher, when one divines for oneself, is not the Resource line; |
| 而学者主象，自占当以世爻看之。 | 而学习者的主象，自己占时要用世爻来看。 | and the main image of the learner, when one divines for oneself, is to be read from the Self line. |

One possible reading is that for teachers of crafts, monks and Taoists the teacher is not taken from the Resource line but from the Other or another line, but the text does not say so.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.16 求名·父爻旺相其文掷地金声、鬼位兴隆家报泥金捷喜：凡占功名以父爻为文章、鬼为官职，二者一卦之主，伤一则不成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占功名，父爻是文章，官鬼是官职，这两样是一卦的主体，伤了一样就不成；父爻旺相文章必好，官鬼得地功名有望，泥金报喜。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining for fame and rank, the Resource line is the writing and Pressure the post; these two are the masters of the hexagram, and if either is harmed it will not succeed; with the Resource line thriving the writing is surely fine, and with Pressure well placed fame and rank are within hope and the gilt-ink news of joy comes.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.42 求名·更详本主之爻神方论其人之命运：本主者本人之主爻，自占以世爻论，子侄看子孙爻，此爻最怕伤克变坏

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:再要看本主的爻神，才能论这个人的命运：本主是本人的主爻，自己占以世爻论，子侄占看子孙爻等，这一爻最怕被伤克、变坏。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Study further the line that is the principal of the person, and then one may judge his fortune: the principal is the person's own chief line, which for oneself is the Self, and for a son or nephew the Output line, and so on; this line most fears being harmed, controlled or changed for the worse.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.03 学业章·世爻忌空破墓绝休囚；世休囚又随鬼入墓、受冲克、动化鬼化绝化克，不唯学业难成还防不测

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:学业上世爻最怕落空、受破、入墓、逢绝或休囚，否则诸事无成；世爻休囚，又跟着官鬼入墓，或被日月、动爻冲克，或发动变官鬼、变绝、变克，不但学业难成，还要提防意外。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In study the Self line most fears being Void, broken, in the tomb, extinct, or resting and trapped, or nothing will succeed; if the Self is resting and trapped and moreover follows Pressure into the tomb, or is clashed and controlled by the Day, the Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into Pressure, extinction or control, then not only will study be hard to complete but one must guard against the unforeseen.

**《增删卜易·学业章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 9815 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on study", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 9815*

## LY.EXAM.ZS.45 乡试会试章·衰旺克冲轻重须宜细玩：凡遇用神，衰要有根，旺宜中和，克要有救，冲要逢合

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:衰、旺、克、冲的轻重要仔细玩味：用神衰的要有根，旺的宜适中，被克的要有救，被冲的要遇合。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The weight of weakness and strength, control and clash must be weighed with care: if the Subject is weak it must have roots, if strong it should be moderate, if controlled it must have rescue, if clashed it must meet a combination.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 衰旺克冲，轻重须宜细玩。 | 衰、旺、克、冲的轻重，要仔细玩味。 | The weight of weak and strong, control and clash must be savoured with care. |
| 凡遇用神，衰要有根，旺宜中和，克要有救，冲要逢合。 | 凡遇到用神，衰的要有根，旺的宜适中，被克要有救，被冲要遇合。 | Whenever one meets the Subject: if weak it must have roots, if strong it should be moderate, if controlled it must have rescue, if clashed it must meet a combination. |

> **按**:这是乡试会试章的结尾总则，适用于所有取用神的断法，不限考试。「有根」是衰的爻有生扶、或在长生、禄旺之地，不会被一冲就断。
>
> **Note:** This closes the chapter with a general rule that applies to every Subject reading and is not limited to examinations. "Having roots" means a weak line has life and support or stands on a Birth or Emolument stage, so that a single clash does not cut it off.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.50 序·占功名一眼可断：官旺持世或日月、动爻作官星生合世爻，求名如拾芥；子孙持世或子孙动于卦中，占入场、升迁如水中掬月

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占功名有两种一眼就能断的卦：官爻旺相持世、或者日月动爻作官星来生合世爻，求名像弯腰拾起一根草那样容易；子孙持世、或者子孙在卦中发动，不管问入场（考试）还是升迁，都像水中捞月，捞不着。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining for a title there are two hexagrams that can be read at a glance: a strong Pressure line holding the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acting as the Pressure star and giving life to or combining with the Self, and seeking a name is as easy as picking up a blade of grass; Output holding the Self, or Output moving in the hexagram, and whether one asks about entering the examination hall or about promotion it is like scooping the moon from the water.

**野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 24 行 · *The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 24*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 假令占功名，若得旺官持世，或日月、动爻作官星生合世爻，求名如拾芥耳； | 譬如占功名，如果得到旺相的官爻持世，或者日月、动爻作官星来生合世爻，求名就像弯腰拾起一根草那样容易； | Suppose one divines for a title: if a strong Pressure line holds the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acts as the Pressure star and gives life to or combines with the Self, seeking a name is as easy as picking up a blade of grass; |
| 倘遇子孙持世，或子孙动于卦中，不拘占入场、占升迁，悉如水中掬月。 | 如果遇到子孙持世，或者子孙在卦中发动，不管占入场还是占升迁，全都像水中捞月。 | if Output holds the Self, or Output moves in the hexagram, whether one divines for entering the examination hall or for promotion, all is like scooping the moon from the water. |

> **按**:「拾芥」是弯腰拾起一根草，比喻毫不费力；「掬月」是用手去捧水里的月亮，比喻白费力气。这一句后面接着讲占求财，不属于考试，没有收。
>
> **Note:** Picking up a blade of grass means doing something without effort, and scooping the moon means trying to cup the moon in the water with one's hands, that is, labouring in vain. The next sentences go on to divining for wealth and are outside examinations, so they are not included.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.51 用神章：占功名以官鬼爻为用神（忌空破，宜生怕克）；师长、章奏、文书、文章、书馆以父母爻为用神；门徒以子孙爻为用神

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:考试里要看的东西各有各的用神：问功名，用神是官鬼爻，最忌落空落破，宜有相生、怕被相克，月日动变四处的生克和别的用神同样看；问师长、奏章、文书、文章、书馆，用神是父母爻；问门徒（学生），用神是子孙爻。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In an examination reading each thing to be watched has its own Subject: for a title the Subject is the Pressure line, which most fears Void and break, is glad of generation and fears control, and whose generation and control from the four places (Month, Day, moving line, changed line) are read as for any other Subject; for teachers, memorials, documents, essays and the study hall the Subject is the Resource line; for pupils the Subject is the Output line.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.52 用神章·子孙乃福神，诸事见之为喜，独占功名者忌之（子孙是福德之神、制鬼之神、解忧之神，又是剥官引职之神）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:子孙爻是福神：占别的事见到它是喜，只有占功名要忌它，因为子孙制住官鬼、剥去官位、引走职位。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Output line is the spirit of blessing: for every other matter seeing it is welcome, but for a title it is the one to avoid, because Output controls Pressure, strips office and leads away a post.
