## LY.EXAM.HJC.28 求名·月建克身当被责、财如生世必帮粮：月建在身爻发动刑克世而官爻失时必遭杖责；官爻持世而财爻发动生合世必有帮粮之喜

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:月建在身爻上、发动刑克世爻，而官爻失时，必遭杖责；卦中官爻持世、财爻发动生合世爻，必有得帮粮的喜事。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Month is on the Body line and moves to punish and control the Self while the Pressure line is out of season, one will surely be caned; if the Pressure line holds the Self in the hexagram and the Wealth line moves to give life to and combine with the Self, there will surely be the joy of a grain allowance.

| 财如生世必帮粮 | 财爻如果生世，必有帮粮（的好处） | if Wealth gives life to the Self, one will surely get the grain allowance |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1890 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1890*

> **按**:「帮粮」是廪生之外官府另给的补助口粮（增删卜易岁考章里「优等帮粮」是同一件事，LY.EXAM.ZS.18）。「失时」是爻没有得月令（休囚）。
>
> **Note:** A "grain allowance" is the supplementary ration the government gave in addition to the stipend (the "excellent grade with a grain allowance" in the Zengshan Buyi chapter on the annual examination is the same thing, LY.EXAM.ZS.18). "Out of season" means the line has not got the month's command (it is resting or trapped).

## LY.EXAM.ZS.18 岁考科考章·文宜旺相最忌休囚、官要生身不宜克世：父、世旺相又得生扶全无破绽定考超等，旺而逢生上等，有一遇刑冲又次之，世父同受克劣等无疑（野鹤、觉子；附例）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:岁考、科考按父爻和世爻的旺衰分等第：两爻旺相又得日月动爻生扶、全无破绽的考超等；旺而逢生上等；旺而无生、或相而遇扶、没有刑冲克破的次之；两爻有一个遇刑冲的又次之；世爻受克要受责罚，世爻父爻同受克必定劣等。野鹤说要变通，以六冲变冲、反吟伏吟、变克化绝为破绽；觉子说《易冒》以进神为上等、退神为下等，没有进退之神时就按旺衰定。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In the annual and qualifying examinations the grade is set by how strong the Resource and Self lines are: both thriving and given life and support by the Day, the Month and moving lines, with no flaw at all, is the top grade; strong and meeting life is the upper grade; strong without life, or thriving and given support, with no punishment, clash, control or breaking, is next; one of the two meeting punishment or clash is lower still; if the Self is controlled one will be flogged, and if the Self and Resource are both controlled it is surely the lowest grade. The Old Man of Yehe says to be flexible, and takes a Six-Clash, a clash by change, a Reversed or Hidden Chant, or a change into control or extinction as flaws; Juezi says the Yi Mao takes an Advancing line as the upper grade and a Retreating line as the lower, and that when there is neither one decides by strength and weakness.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.19 岁考科考章·六冲变冲或六爻乱动即使文旺世旺也只考平等；飞神无助身无克荣辱无施；伏藏旺相也遭伤赏罚并见（野鹤；附例：泰卦，巳午之火非文旺乃字佳）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦成六冲、变冲或六爻乱动，即使文章旺、世爻旺，也只考平等；文章衰受刑克、世爻休囚被克、变凶的，必定劣等；飞神得不到帮助、卦身不受克，无荣无辱；父爻出现、不旺不衰、没有生扶也没有克制、世爻不被冲克的，保得住平等；伏藏的爻即使旺相也遭伤，赏罚一起来。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the hexagram is a Six-Clash or changes into a clash, or the six lines move in disorder, then even with the writing strong and the Self strong one takes only the middle grade; with the writing weak and punished or controlled, or the Self resting and trapped and controlled, or changing into ill, it is surely the lowest grade; if the flying line gets no help and the Body is not controlled there is neither honour nor disgrace; if the Resource line appears, neither strong nor weak, with neither support nor control and the Self not clashed or controlled, the middle grade is assured; a hidden line, even though thriving, is harmed, and reward and punishment appear together.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.20 岁考科考章·总括：父、世旺相遇生扶为上，略欠次之，刑伤有救又次之，刑伤无救而轻者又次之，世父被刑冲克破而重者劣等；善卜者通变而断（觉子）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:岁考科考的等第次序：父爻世爻旺相又得生扶最上；略有欠缺的次之；受刑伤但有救的又次之；受刑伤无救但程度轻的又次之；世、父被刑冲克破而且重的，必定劣等。会占的人要把吉凶和用神衰旺的轻重合起来，灵活变通地断。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The order of grades in the annual and qualifying examinations: the Resource and Self lines thriving and given life and support is the top; one with a slight lack is next; one punished or harmed but with rescue is lower again; one punished or harmed with no rescue but lightly is lower again; if the Self and Resource are punished, clashed, controlled or broken, and badly, it is surely the lowest grade. One skilled at divining must weigh the signs together with how heavy or light the strength or weakness of the Subject is, and judge flexibly.

| 善卜者，须以向之吉凶兼用神衰旺之轻重，通变而断之。 | 善于占卜的人，要把先前所说的吉凶，连同用神衰旺的轻重一起考虑，灵活变通地来断。 | One skilled at divining must take the auspicious and inauspicious signs spoken of above together with how heavy or light the strength or weakness of the Subject is, and judge flexibly. |

>
> **Note:** This sums up the chapter; the examples are under LY.EXAM.ZS.18 and 19. In this chapter the "Subject" is the Resource and Self lines. The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.21 占廪章·占廪以财爻为用神：财是文章（父母）的忌神，占岁考与问廪不可并在一卦，先占岁考、另卜一卦问廪；财爻持世或动而生世为吉，断同求财章（觉子）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占廪（廪生的名额和官给口粮）以财爻为用神，而财是文章（父母爻）的忌神，所以不能把岁考和占廪放在一卦里问：先占岁考的吉凶，另外再卜一卦问廪；财爻持世，或动而生世为吉，断法同求财章。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining for the stipend (a stipend place and the government ration) the Wealth line is the Subject, but Wealth is the adversary of the writing (the Resource line), so the annual examination and the stipend must not be asked in one hexagram: first divine the fortune of the annual examination, then divine another hexagram for the stipend; Wealth holding the Self, or moving to give life to the Self, is auspicious, and it is judged as in the chapter on seeking wealth.

**觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 10080 行 · *Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 10080*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 觉子曰：占廪若不得其决，而望验者，未之有也。 | 觉子说：占廪生名额，如果不得其中的诀窍，却指望灵验，是没有的事。 | Juezi says: in divining for the stipend, one who does not have the secret of it and yet hopes for accuracy has never been seen. |
| 盖占廪用财，财乃文章之忌神，不知其法，卜岁考而兼问廪，财爻若旺，父母受伤，财若休囚，如何得廪？ | 因为占廪要用财，而财是文章（父母）的忌神；不知道这个办法，占岁考同时又问廪，财爻如果旺，父母受伤，财如果休囚，又怎么能得廪？ | For in divining for the stipend Wealth is used, and Wealth is the adversary of the writing; if one does not know the method and asks about the annual examination and the stipend together, then if Wealth is strong the Resource line is harmed, and if Wealth is resting and trapped how can one obtain the stipend? |
| 余有法焉，先占岁考之吉凶，另卜一卦而问廪，即其秘也。 | 我有个办法：先占岁考的吉凶，另外再卜一卦来问廪，这就是它的秘诀。 | I have a method: first divine the fortune of the annual examination, then divine a separate hexagram to ask about the stipend; that is the secret. |

**《增删卜易·占廪章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 10081 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the stipend", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 10081*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 廪以财爻为用神，财爻持世，或动而生世为吉，与求财章同断。 | 廪以财爻为用神，财爻持世，或者发动来生世为吉，按求财章的断法同断。 | For the stipend the Wealth line is the Subject; Wealth holding the Self, or moving to give life to the Self, is auspicious, and it is judged as in the chapter on seeking wealth. |

> **按**:「廪」是廪膳生，考岁考得一等才补；「占廪」是占能不能补上廪生的名额。财是文章的忌神（财克父），所以占考试时财是坏的，占廪时财却是用神，正反要分开占，书里叫「秘」。后面的[乾按]不收。

> **Note:** A "stipend student" was a licentiate who drew a government grain ration, appointed on a first-grade result in the annual examination; "divining for the stipend" is asking whether one will be given a place. Wealth is the adversary of the writing (Wealth controls Resource), so in an examination Wealth is bad, while in the stipend it is the Subject; the two must be divined separately, which the book calls the "secret". The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
