# 六爻 · 考试 · 学业

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Examinations · Study: whether study will come to anything, which classic to take up*

2 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.EXAM.ZS.01 学业章·儒业：世爻、父母爻同旺，日月动爻相生，必成大器；财动卦中青灯不久，子孙持世皓首无成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:学儒业的人，世爻和父母爻都旺，日月、动爻再来生扶，将来一定成大器；反过来，财爻在卦中发动，读书会半途而废，子孙爻持世或发动，一辈子成不了功名。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For one studying the scholar's career: if the Self and Resource lines are both strong and the Day, the Month and the moving lines add support, he will surely become a great vessel; the other way round, if Wealth moves in the hexagram he will give up study halfway, and if Output holds the Self or moves he will not win fame and rank in his whole life.

**《增删卜易·学业章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 9807–9811 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on study", base text; Zengshan Buyi, lines 9807–9811*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 儒业者，父母世爻同旺，终须变化成龙，日月动爻相生，定是王家储器。 | 学儒业的人，父母爻和世爻都旺，终究会变化成龙；日月、动爻再来相生，一定是朝廷储备的人才。 | For one who studies the scholar's career, if the Resource and Self lines are both strong, he will in the end transform into a dragon; and if the Day, the Month and the moving lines give them life, he is surely a vessel stored up for the royal house. |
| 世爻、父母爻皆宜旺相，或动而变吉，或父母持世，日月动爻生合世爻，攀龙附凤，指日可期。 | 世爻、父母爻都宜旺相，或者发动变出吉象，或者父母爻持世、日月和动爻生合世爻，那就是攀龙附凤，指日可待。 | The Self and Resource lines should both be thriving, or move and change into an auspicious line, or the Resource line should hold the Self with the Day, the Month and the moving lines giving life to or combining with the Self; then he will cling to the dragon and the phoenix, a matter of days. |
| 财动卦中，青灯不久； | 财爻在卦中发动，青灯苦读的日子不会长； | If Wealth moves in the hexagram, the green lamp will not burn long; |
| 子孙持世，皓首无成。 | 子孙爻持世，白了头也没有成就。 | if the Output line holds the Self, one grows white-haired with nothing achieved. |
| 财动克文章，读书中道而废； | 财动克了文章（父母），读书半途而废； | if Wealth moves and controls the writing (the Resource line), one gives up study halfway; |
| 子动无官之象，皓首必不成功名。 | 子孙动，就没有官星的象，白了头也成不了功名。 | if Output moves there is no sign of the official star, and even in white hair one will not achieve fame and rank. |

> **按**:「王家储器」是为朝廷储备的人才；「青灯」是夜里读书的灯；「财动克文章」里的文章就是父母爻（父母爻管文书、文章）。这一章后面的[乾按]是今人按语，不收。
>
> **Note:** "A vessel stored up for the royal house" is a talent kept in reserve for the court; the "green lamp" is the lamp one studies by at night; the "writing" that Wealth controls is the Resource line (Resource governs documents and compositions). The "[Qian's note]" at the end of the chapter is a modern comment and is not quoted.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.02 学业章·九流、百工：财兴官旺则道重人钦，兄动世衰则有毁无誉；百工以财为用神、子孙为元神，最忌兄爻持世；天师驱邪治鬼之家反以子孙持世为吉（觉子）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:学九流技艺要财爻兴旺、官爻旺相；学百工手艺以财爻为用神、子孙爻为元神，最忌兄弟持世或旺而发动；觉子补充：天师、驱邪治鬼的、师巫和医家，反而要子孙持世或发动才吉，因为靠子孙制鬼。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** For learning the nine schools of skill, Wealth should be thriving and Pressure strong; for learning a craft, Wealth is the Subject and Output the Support, and what is most feared is Peer holding the Self or being strong and moving; Juezi adds that for Heavenly Masters, exorcists, shamans and physicians, it is the other way round: Output holding the Self or moving is auspicious, since they control ghosts by Output.

**《增删卜易·学业章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 9812 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on study", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 9812*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 九流者，财兴官旺，道重人钦； | 学九流技艺的，财爻兴旺、官爻旺相，所学的道就受人看重、人人佩服； | For the nine schools, with Wealth thriving and Pressure strong, one's way is valued and people admire one; |
| 兄动世衰，有毁无誉。 | 兄弟动而世爻衰，只有诋毁、没有赞誉。 | if Peer moves and the Self is weak, there is censure and no praise. |
| 财持世、官持世，财官世爻皆宜得地； | 财爻持世也好，官爻持世也好，财、官、世爻都要得地； | Whether Wealth or Pressure holds the Self, Wealth, Pressure and the Self line should all be well placed; |
| 兄动卦中，兄爻持世，只可糊口，难许成家。 | 兄弟在卦中发动，或者兄弟爻持世，只能勉强糊口，难以成家立业。 | if Peer moves in the hexagram, or the Peer line holds the Self, one can only just make a living and can hardly set up a household. |

**觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)** — 《增删卜易》第 9814 行 · *Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi); Zengshan Buyi, line 9814*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 觉子曰：古以九流皆以官鬼为吉，殊不知天师家，及驱邪治鬼家、师巫医家，必以子孙持世及子孙发动为吉，盖皆以子旺能于治鬼也。 | 觉子说：古人以九流都以官鬼为吉，却不知道天师一类的，以及驱邪治鬼的、师巫和医家，必须以子孙持世以及子孙发动为吉，因为这些人都靠子孙旺，才能治服鬼。 | Juezi says: the ancients took Pressure as the auspicious line for all nine schools, not knowing that for Heavenly Masters, exorcists who drive out evil and subdue ghosts, and shamans and physicians, it must be Output holding the Self and Output moving that is auspicious, because all of them can control ghosts only when Output is strong. |

**《增删卜易·学业章》原文** — 《增删卜易》第 9815 行 · *Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on study", base text; Zengshan Buyi, line 9815*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 百工皆以财为用神，子孙为元神，最忌兄爻持世，及兄爻旺动者，劳碌终身。 | 各种工匠都以财爻为用神、子孙爻为元神，最忌兄弟爻持世，以及兄弟爻旺而发动，会劳碌一生。 | For all the crafts, Wealth is the Subject and Output is the Support; what is most feared is the Peer line holding the Self, or the Peer line strong and moving, and one toils all one's life. |
| 余以求财章同断。 | 我用求财章的断法一并来断。 | I judge them together by the rules of the chapter on seeking wealth. |

> **按**:九流是各种技艺、行当（书里没有列名单），百工是各行手艺人。觉子这一条是纠正「九流皆以官鬼为吉」：靠制服鬼神吃饭的人，官鬼是他要制的东西，所以反以子孙为吉。「余以求财章同断」的「余」是野鹤老人。
>
> **Note:** The "nine schools" are the various skills and trades (the book gives no list) and the "hundred crafts" are the manual trades. Juezi's remark corrects the claim that Pressure is auspicious for all the nine schools: for those who live by subduing ghosts and spirits, Pressure is what they are to subdue, so Output is auspicious for them instead. The "I" in "I judge them together by the chapter on seeking wealth" is the Old Man of Yehe.
