## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.09 十八问答·第十八问·诚明不验：占的人心诚、断的人精明仍有不验，缘故在占的人——事涉机密难对人说，或问的是这件、心里想的是那件；例：问家宅实为乡试功名（自己头场落榜、业师中第四名），十二岁的孩子被父命占功名实为父占子（父动克子而亡）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占的人心诚、断的人精明，有时仍然不验，缘故在占的人，不在断的人：占的人的心意虽然诚，但有的事是机密，难对人说，或者问的是这件事，心里真正想的却是另一件事，所以才有不验。书中例一：申月乙亥日「占家宅」得井卦变节卦，断为应爻临父母、必有外姓长者同住，内卦合成鬼局、宅内不安，兄弟月破伏藏、兄弟家不利，对方一句句说没有，最后才说，占家宅其实是想和家里的老师一同去赴乡试；王洪绪说功名和家宅天远地隔，功名以官鬼为官星，家宅以鬼为祸害，既然占功名，你的功名不许，你的老师必定高中——官局生应、不来生世，叫出现无情，与你无干；后来到八月酉金实空之月，他自己头场就被贴出，他的老师中了第四名。例二：午月辛酉日占功名，得萃卦变遁卦，是一个十二岁的孩子，父亲命他占功名；若按孩子自占，官爻持世、夏火当令、未土父母爻是文章又化进神，功名有望，谁知是父亲叫孩子占，这个心意发自父亲的诚，所以是父占子，父母爻发动来克子孙，未月戌日孩子就去世了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** When the one who divines is sincere and the one who judges is shrewd, the result is sometimes still not borne out, and the cause lies in the one who divines, not the one who judges: however sincere his intent, the matter may be a secret that is hard to tell anyone, or he asks about one thing while his mind is really on another, and so it fails to come true. First case: in the Shen month, on a Yi-Hai day, a man divined "the household" and got Jing changing to Jie; the judgement was that the Other line sat in the second line carrying Resource, so an elder of another surname must be living with them, that the inner trigram combined into a Pressure frame, so the house was uneasy, and that Peer was Month-broken and hidden, so the brothers' family was unlucky; he denied each point in turn and at last said that he had divined the household but really wanted to go to the provincial examination together with the tutor of his family; Wang Hongxu replied that examination success and the household are as far apart as heaven and earth, that for examination success Pressure is the official star while for the household Pressure is calamity, and that since it was examination success that was asked, his own was not promised and his tutor would surely pass high: the Pressure frame gave life to the Other and not to the Self, which is called appearing without feeling and has nothing to do with him; later, in the eighth month, when the Void You Metal was filled, he himself was struck off in the first session and his tutor passed as number four. Second case: in the Wu month, on a Xin-You day, a twelve-year-old boy, ordered by his father to divine his examination prospects, got Cui changing to Dun; judging it as the boy's own divination, Pressure held the Self, Fire was in command in summer and Wei Earth, the Resource line, was the essay and changed into an Advancing spirit, so success looked hopeful; but the father had told the son to divine, so the intent arose from the father's sincerity and it was the father divining for the son; Resource moved to control Output, and in the Wei month, on a Xu day, the boy died.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.10 求师·鬼化文书克世则讼由乎学（鬼动化父母刑克世爻异日必争论，父化鬼爻或官爻皆动伤世亦然）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼变出文书（父母）来克世，会因学业上的事引出官司；官鬼动变父母刑克世爻，日后一定有争论；父爻变官鬼，或官爻都动来伤世，也一样。

## LY.EXAM.ZS.09 延师章·鬼化文书克世则讼由乎学：鬼动化父母冲克世，日后必有争讼；纵有日月动爻制服也不宜，鬼动克世、世动变凶皆不宜从

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:官鬼变成父母来冲克世爻，日后一定因学业上的事引出官司；就算日月、动爻能制住官鬼也不适宜，因为眼下制住了，日后官鬼逢生旺还是灾祸；凡官鬼动克世、世动变凶，都不宜跟这位老师学。

## LY.EXAM.ZS.55 八宫章·功名只可自己占：自占功名只有一点功名之念才灵验，有官爵者心怀二念，代人占功名难取用神；例：代卜长辈功名，巽木化乾金回头克，午月削职、七月而终

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占功名和防灾虑患一样，只能自己占、不能替别人占：自己占功名，心里只有一点功名的念头，才自然灵验；若既有怕出事的心、又有求名的念，就难以决断。有官爵的人「心怀二念」（既怕失官又怕获罪），得子孙持世时，说他无事又怕神灵报的是失名，说功名有碍又怕神灵报的是无忧，所以说「自占必验，代卜不灵，不是卦不灵，是别人的心不专于一」。书中例：有人替长辈卜功名，野鹤说功名必须亲占、代占难取用神，从不敢断，幸而这个卦显而易见——巽木化出乾金，是变出来的爻回头来克，为绝卦，不必问功名，寿数也不长；果然午月被削职，七月去世。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Divining for a title, like divining against calamity, should be done only for oneself and not by proxy: when one divines one's own title with only a single thought of the title in mind, the divination is naturally borne out; if one has both a fear of mishap and a wish for fame, it is hard to decide. A man holding rank is "of two minds" (afraid of losing office and afraid of blame): if Output holds the Self, to promise him safety risks the spirit reporting the loss of his name, and to call his title obstructed risks the spirit reporting no worry; hence the saying "one's own divination is always borne out, a proxy's is not; it is not that the hexagram fails, but that the other person's mind is not set on one thing". The case in the book: someone divined an elder's title by proxy; Yehe said a title must be divined in person, since by proxy the Subject is hard to choose, and he never dares to judge, but luckily this hexagram was plain: Xun Wood changing into Qian Metal is a changed line coming back to control, a hexagram of cutting-off, so there was no need to ask about the title and life would not be long; indeed the man was stripped of rank in the Wu month and died in the seventh month.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.61 占此应彼·举意先后：念头发自谁、卦就应谁，父叫子占功名仍作父占子（例：十五岁孩子，父动克子，妻死申月、子卒未年）；叫人代占须先告知、待他忘怀后自己想起再占；孝廉不明说占会试而混问功名是昧己

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦应的是动念的那个人的念头：别人的心事，他没有动念去占，是你提醒他、叫他占的，那就是你的念头，卦应你不应他；所以父亲叫孩子占功名，仍按父亲占孩子来断。想让别人为自己的事占，可以先告诉他，等他忘怀了、自己想起来再另起念头去占。占的时候要照直说出来，不要含混：孝廉不明说占会试而混问功名，就是欺瞒自己。书中例：午月辛酉日占功名，得萃卦变遁卦，是一个年仅十五的孩子，父亲叫他占将来有没有功名，若按官星持世、父爻是文章又化进神，功名有望，但因为是父亲叫儿子占，念头是父亲的，仍作父占子：父爻发动克子、兄弟爻发动伤妻，妻子死在申月，儿子死在未年。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A hexagram answers the thought of the person from whom the thought arose. If a matter is on another's mind but he has not thought of divining it, and I remind him and tell him to divine, then the thought is mine, and the hexagram answers me and not him; so when a father tells his son to divine for a title, it is still judged as the father divining for the son. If one wants someone to divine his own matter, one may first tell him and let him divine later with a fresh thought of his own after he has forgotten it. And one must state the question plainly: a provincial graduate who does not say he is divining the metropolitan examination but asks vaguely about a title is deceiving himself. The case in the book: on a Xin-You day in the Wu month a boy of fifteen, told by his father to divine whether he would have a title in future, got Cui changing to Dun; by the Pressure star holding the Self, with the Resource line as the essay changing into an Advancing spirit, success looked hopeful, but since it was the father who told the son to divine, the thought was the father's and it was read as the father divining for the son: Resource moved and controlled the son, Peer moved and wounded the wife, and the wife died in the Shen month and the son in the Wei year.
