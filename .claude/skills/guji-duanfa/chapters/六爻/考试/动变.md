## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.07 十八问答·第十问·进神喜忌：吉神宜化进、忌神宜化退；进有三种（旺相乘势、休囚待时、逢空破冲合待期），退也有三种；例：乡试官星持世，第五爻官化进神，今秋中式、次年辰年联捷

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:动爻变出来的神有进神、退神，吉凶要分喜忌：吉神喜欢化进神，忌神喜欢化退神。进有三种：旺相的趁势就进；休囚的等到得时才进；动爻或变爻有一个遇到空、破、冲、合的，等到填补、合住、冲开的时候才进。退也有三种：旺相的有日月或动爻生扶，占近期的事暂时不退；休囚的当时就退；动变爻有一个遇空破冲合的，等到那个时候填补、合冲了才退。书中例：申月癸卯日占乡试，得恒之大过卦，酉金官星持世，日辰冲而暗动，又得第五爻官化进神拱扶，断为不但今秋中举，来春也定占鳌头；结果登了乡榜，第二年辰年连中——「化进神」应在今秋，「联捷」应在辰年冲而逢合。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The spirits that come out of a moving line when it changes are Advancing and Retreating spirits, and their luck divides into the welcome and the feared: an auspicious spirit is glad to change into an Advancing spirit, and an Adversary is glad to change into a Retreating spirit. There are three ways of advancing: a strong spirit advances by riding its momentum; a resting or trapped one advances by waiting for its season; and if the moving line or the changed line meets Void, break, clash or combination, it advances by waiting for the period that fills, combines or clashes it. There are also three ways of retreating: a strong spirit given life and support by the Day, the Month or a moving line does not retreat for the time being when a near matter is divined; a resting or trapped one retreats at once; and if the moving line or the changed line meets Void, break, clash or combination, it retreats when the period comes that fills, combines or clashes it. The case in the book: on a Gui-Mao day in the Shen month a man divined the provincial examination and got Heng changing to Daguo; the Pressure star in You Metal held the Self and was stirred in secret by the clash of the Day, and the fifth line's Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit supported it, so the judgement was that not only would he pass in the autumn but next spring he would surely take first place; he made the provincial list and succeeded again in the Chen year after: the change into an Advancing spirit answered in this autumn, and the consecutive success answered in the Chen year, a clash that meets a combination.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.40 求名·世动化空用旺则豹变翻成蝴蝶：必中之卦世爻发动变入墓绝，恐成名之后不能享福；游魂卦死于途中、归魂卦到家而死，墓绝爻本太岁逾年而死

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:本是必中的卦，但世爻发动变入墓、绝，恐怕成名之后不能享福；游魂卦的死在路上，归魂卦的到家才死，入墓绝的爻就是太岁的，过一年就死。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If one has a hexagram of sure success but the Self moves and changes into the tomb or extinction, one fears that after winning the name one cannot enjoy the blessing; with a Wandering Soul hexagram one dies on the road, with a Returning Soul hexagram one dies on reaching home, and if the line that enters the tomb or extinction is Tai Sui itself, one dies after a year.

**《黄金策·求名》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1916 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a name", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 1916*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 世动化空用旺， | 世爻动而变空、用神旺， | If the Self moves and changes into Void while the Subject is strong, |
| 则豹变翻成蝴蝶 | 豹变（的好事）反而成了蝴蝶梦 | the leopard's change turns instead into a butterfly dream |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1916 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1916*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 若得必中之卦，如遇世爻发动变入墓绝，恐成名之后不能享福。 | 如果得的是必中的卦，但遇到世爻发动变入墓、绝，恐怕成名之后不能享福。 | If one has obtained a hexagram of sure success but the Self moves and changes into the tomb or extinction, one fears that after winning the name one cannot enjoy the blessing. |
| 游魂死于途中，归魂卦到家而死，墓绝爻本太岁，逾年而死也。 | 游魂卦（的人）死在路上，归魂卦（的人）到家才死，入墓、入绝的爻如果就是太岁，过了一年就会死。 | With a Wandering Soul hexagram one dies on the road, with a Returning Soul hexagram one dies on reaching home, and if the line that enters the tomb or extinction is Tai Sui itself, one dies after a year. |

> **打架**:这里（王洪绪注）把「世动化空（入墓绝）」读作成名后不得享福。增删卜易（觉子）引同一联时指出：旧说都是就卦身讲的，野鹤试过不灵，只验于世爻；并举例世爻午火官星化出丑土、官文两旺而丑父旬空，午年入场、丑年中了甲榜——「化空者必应实空之年」，并没有成蝴蝶梦（LY.EXAM.ZS.40）。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Here (Wang Hongxu's commentary) reads "the Self moves into Void (tomb or extinction)" as one winning a name but being unable to enjoy it. The Zengshan Buyi (Juezi), quoting the same couplet, points out that the old sayings all speak of the Body, that the Old Man of Yehe found them inaccurate and true only of the Self line, and gives an example in which the Self, Wu Fire the official star, changed into the Chou Earth writing with the official star and writing both strong and the Chou Resource line in the Void, entered the examination in a Wu year and passed in a Chou year: "a change into Void is answered in the year that fills the Void", and it did not turn into a butterfly dream (LY.EXAM.ZS.40).

> **按**:「游魂」「归魂」是八宫卦里的两种卦（每宫第七、第八卦），游魂主漂泊、归魂主回家。这一联的「豹变」是君子像豹那样变化（比喻考中后的飞黄腾达）。
>
> **Note:** A "Wandering Soul" and a "Returning Soul" are two kinds of hexagram in the eight palaces (the seventh and eighth of each palace), the first meaning drifting and the second coming home. The "leopard's change" is the gentleman changing like a leopard (a figure for rising after passing).

## LY.EXAM.HJC.41 求名·身官化鬼月扶则鹏程连步蟾宫：卦身为事体功名尤宜见之怕临财福；官爻临之必有成望，发动化官爻得月建生合必连科及第

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦身或官爻变出官鬼又有月建扶助，接连登科：卦身是事情的体，占功名尤其宜见到它，怕它临财、福；官爻临在它上面必有成功的希望，若发动变出官爻又得月建生合，必主连科及第。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Body or the official star changes into Pressure with the Month's support, one passes in succession: the Body is the substance of the matter, and in divining for fame and rank it is especially welcome to see it and one fears it standing on Wealth or Output; with the Pressure line on it there is surely hope of success, and if it moves and changes into the Pressure line and the Month gives life to and combines with it, one will surely pass in successive examinations.

**《黄金策·求名》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1918 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a name", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 1918*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 身官化鬼月扶， | 卦身、官星变出官鬼又有月建扶助， | If the Body or official star changes into Pressure with the Month's support, |
| 则鹏程连步蟾宫 | 就像鹏鸟程程高升，接连登步蟾宫 | it is like the roc rising stage after stage, going on step by step in the Toad Palace |

**王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1918 行 · *Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong); Bushi Zhengzong, line 1918*

| 原文 | 译文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 卦身为事体，功名尤宜见之，怕临财福。 | 卦身是事情的体，占功名尤其宜见到它，怕它临财、福。 | The Body is the substance of the matter; in divining for fame and rank it is especially welcome to see it, and one fears it standing on Wealth or Output. |
| 如得官爻临之，必有成望，更若发动化官爻，而得月建生合者，必主连科及第。 | 如果有官爻临在它上面，必有成功的希望；再如果发动变出官爻，而得月建生合，必主连科及第。 | If the Pressure line stands on it there is surely hope of success; and if it moves and changes into the Pressure line and the Month gives life to and combines with it, one will surely pass in successive examinations. |

> **打架**:这里（王洪绪注）认为卦身得官爻临、发动化官爻又有月建生合就必连科及第。增删卜易（觉子）举了一个卦：巳为卦身，世爻变出子水官星，又得月建生合，正合这一说，却没有连捷，反而因变出的鬼回头克世，后来客死途中——结论是这类讲卦身的旧说「试不验，惟验于世爻」（LY.EXAM.ZS.40）。
>
> **Where they disagree:** Here (Wang Hongxu's commentary) holds that a Body stood on by the Pressure line, or moving and changing into the Pressure line with the Month giving life and combining, will surely pass in successive examinations. The Zengshan Buyi (Juezi) gives a hexagram in which Si was the Body, the Self changed into the Zi Water official star and the Month gave life and combined, exactly as this saying has it, yet the man did not pass in succession and, because the ghost he changed into turned back to control the Self, died on the road later; the conclusion is that of such old sayings about the Body "when tried they were not accurate, and hold true only of the Self line" (LY.EXAM.ZS.40).

> **按**:「蟾宫」是月宫，旧时以「蟾宫折桂」比喻考中；「鹏程」是大鹏飞行的路程，比喻前程远大。
>
> **Note:** The "Toad Palace" is the moon palace, and "breaking a laurel in the Toad Palace" was the figure for passing the examination; the "roc's flight" is the course of the great roc, a figure for a great future.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.17 童试章·父衰变旺愈出愈奇、文化退神日长日短：父衰动化旺、回头生、进神则后胜于前，皆不宜化退神；旺化退神前篇稍遂后劲不力，衰化退神窗下千日之长临场一日之短（附例）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:父爻衰而发动变旺、变回头生、变进神，文章越写越好，后胜于前；最不宜变退神：旺的变退神，前面顺利后劲不足；衰的变退神，以及变克、变空破墓绝、变回头克，是窗下千日用功、临场一日不济。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Resource line is weak and moves to change into strength, into a line that turns back to give it life, or into an Advancing line, the writing grows better as it goes and the later is better than the earlier; a change into a Retreating line is what is most unwelcome: a strong line changing into a Retreating line goes well at first but lacks force later; a weak line changing into a Retreating line, or into control, or into Void, break, tomb or extinction, or into a line that turns back to control it, means a thousand days of work at the window and one day that falls short in the hall.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.57 进神退神章：喜者宜化进神、忌者宜化退神，进神八对、退神八对；例：乡试官星持世、五爻官化进神，今秋折桂、明春联捷；乡试世爻未土化进神、巳火官动生世，揭榜中第八名

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:动爻变出来的爻是进神或退神，吉凶要分喜忌：所喜的宜化进神，所忌的宜化退神。进神有八对：亥化子、寅化卯、巳化午、申化酉、丑化辰、辰化未、未化戌、戌化丑，退神是它们的反方向；进神像春天的树木越长越旺、像有源头的水，有长久的样子，退神像秋天的花木渐渐凋零。书中例一：申月癸卯日占乡试，得恒卦变大过卦，酉金官星持世、旺相当令，卯日冲动它，又有第五爻官爻化进神扶助，断为今秋折桂、明春占鳌头，后来果然联捷。例二：酉月乙丑日占乡试中不中，得兑卦变讼卦，世爻未土化进神、巳火官爻发动生世，吉兆分明，断为坐等报喜，果然戊寅日揭榜中第八名；书上说古法认为动爻遇破、遇散、变到日月上还不能进，这个卦动爻散了又化空，也照样考中。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line that comes out of a moving line when it changes is an Advancing or Retreating spirit, and its luck divides into the welcome and the feared: what is welcome is better changing into an Advancing spirit, and what is feared is better changing into a Retreating spirit. There are eight pairs of Advancing spirits (Hai into Zi, Yin into Mao, Si into Wu, Shen into You, Chou into Chen, Chen into Wei, Wei into Xu, Xu into Chou), and the Retreating spirits are their reverse; an Advancing spirit is like spring trees coming into leaf or water that has a source, the look of what lasts long, and a Retreating spirit is like flowers and trees in autumn withering little by little. First case: on a Gui-Mao day in the Shen month a man divined the provincial examination and got Heng changing to Daguo; the Pressure star in You Metal held the Self, strong and in command, and was stirred by the clash of the Mao day, and the fifth line's Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit supported it, so the judgement was that he would break the laurel that autumn and take first place the next spring, and he indeed passed in consecutive rounds. Second case: on a Yi-Chou day in the You month a man divined whether he would pass the provincial examination and got Dui changing to Song; Wei Earth at the Self changed into an Advancing spirit and the Si Fire Pressure line moved to give life to the Self, an omen plainly good, so the judgement was to sit and wait for the news, and the list was indeed posted on a Wu-Yin day with him at number eight; the book says that by the old rule a moving line that meets a break or a scattering, or changes onto the Day or Month, still cannot advance, yet here the moving line was scattered and changed into Void and he passed all the same.

> **Note:** For "breaking the laurel", "taking first place" and "consecutive success" see the note at LY.EXAM.BSZZ.07; the hexagram diagrams of the two cases are not included, and the second case's opening sentence comes before its diagram. The terse phrase in the second case, "moving, broken, scattered, changing into Day or Month, still cannot advance", means in sense that a moving line which meets a break or is scattered by a clash, or whose changed line falls on a Void or break of the Day or Month, was held by the old rule not yet to count as Advancing; the translation follows this reading, taken from the context, and the book does not explain it further. In the list of eight pairs the text writes "Xu" with a wrong character in two places and is reproduced as it stands. The first case is the same hexagram as the one in the Bushi Zhengzong's tenth question (LY.EXAM.BSZZ.07), though the wording of the judgement differs.
