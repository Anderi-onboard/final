## LY.EXAM.BSZZ.10 总断千金赋·应位遭伤：占别人的事要看是什么名分——占师傅、家主、父友这类长辈用神是父母爻，不一概以应位论

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:占别人的事，不能一概拿应爻当用神：交往疏远的人、没有尊卑名分的人，才以应爻代表他人；占师傅、一家之主、父亲的朋友这类长辈，用神是父母爻；占子孙的朋友，用神是子孙爻；占妻妾奴婢，用神是妻财爻。父亲的朋友、自己的朋友和子孙的朋友，虽然都是别人，要按老幼、称呼、名分来取用神，不能一概用应位断错。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In divining another person's matter one must not always take the Other line as the Subject: it stands for the other person only when he is a distant acquaintance or has no rank of seniority; for a teacher, the head of a household or an older friend of one's father, the Subject is the Resource line; for the friend of one's descendants, the Output line; for wives, concubines, maids and servants, the Wealth line. A father's friend, one's own friend and the friend of one's descendants are all other people, but the Subject must be taken according to age, form of address and status, and one must not always misjudge by the Other position.

**《黄金策·总断千金赋》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1167 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 1167*

> **Note:** This takes the line "the Other position is injured, unfavourable to other people's matters" from the Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements together with Wang Hongxu's note; it says the same thing as the Huangjin Ce's seeking-a-teacher chapter (LY.EXAM.HJC.01), "where there is rank of seniority the Other line is not consulted, and a pupil going to a teacher takes the Resource line for the teacher". The last sentence of the note concerns one's own matters and is kept together without being split off.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.01 求师·凡求师傅先究文章（文书既父母，父爻为书籍、为学馆、为学分）；用居弱地不范不模、若在旺乡可矜可式（有尊卑名分者不看应爻，门人投师以父母爻为师长）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:求老师先看文章（父母爻）：父母爻代表书籍、学馆和老师的学问。老师没有尊卑称呼的，以应爻为用神；有尊卑名分的，不看应爻，按名分论；学生占投师，不论老少，都以父母爻为师长。用神休囚，老师畏缩拘束，不能做楷模；旺相有气，魁梧雄伟，可敬可学。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In seeking a teacher look first to the writing (the Resource line): the Resource line stands for the books, the school and the teacher's learning. If the teacher has no title of seniority, the Other line is the Subject; if he has a rank of seniority, the Other line is not looked at and one judges by the rank; when a pupil divines about taking a teacher, whether he is old or young the Resource line is the teacher. If the Subject is resting and trapped the teacher is timid and cramped and cannot be a model; if thriving and having breath he is tall and imposing and worthy of respect and imitation.

**《黄金策·求师》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1815 行 · *Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a teacher", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 1815*

"The weight of learning" is not explained, but from "his learning is deficient" later (LY.EXAM.HJC.07) it means learning and scholarship.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.06 求师·静合福爻喜遇循循之善诱、动加龙德怕逢凛凛之威严：用神与子孙作合最吉，师徒契合；父动克子孙加白虎刑害则师严毅方正难为子弟

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神静而和子孙爻相合最吉，老师博文约礼、循循善诱，师徒投契；怕父爻动而克子孙，再加白虎、刑、害，老师就严厉刚毅、凛然不可犯，子弟难受。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The Subject still and combining with the Output line is most auspicious: the teacher is learned and restrained and draws one on step by step, and teacher and pupil are in accord; what is feared is the Resource line moving and controlling Output, with the White Tiger, punishment and harm besides, when the teacher is stern, resolute and so awesome that one dare not offend him, and the pupils fare hard.

## LY.EXAM.HJC.09 求师·彼迁此请持世应而兴隆（世应俱动主有家延请，两爻俱空俱不成）；母值应而世生须知假馆（父临应上世动来生合，必馆他家而欲附学）；父在外而福合必是担囊（自占以世为徒、不看福爻，父兄占以子弟为徒、不看世爻；父在外卦他宫安静而子孙动去相会则游学他方）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:世、应都动，有人家来聘请；两爻都空，都不成。父爻临在应爻上、世爻动来生合它，老师在别人家设馆，自己想去附学。自己占以世爻为徒、不看福爻，父兄代占以子弟为徒、不看世爻；父爻在外卦又是别宫、安静，而子孙动去相会，必是到别处游学。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If the Self and Other both move, some household will engage him; if both lines are Void, neither comes about. If the Resource line stands on the Other and the Self moves to give life to or combine with it, the teacher keeps his school at another's house and one wishes to attach oneself to it. When one divines for oneself the Self is the pupil and the Output line is not looked at; when a father or elder brother divines for him the son or younger brother is the pupil and the Self line is not looked at; if the Resource line is in the outer trigram, of another palace and still, and Output moves to meet it, he will surely go to study in another place.

## LY.EXAM.ZS.12 延师章·为子延师：应为西席、世为东家，宜世应相生合；应生世我得益、应空破延之不来、应动他心有变、世空我意不专；父旺与子孙合名实兼收，父动伤子孙、子动化克不宜延、子动化鬼速谢辞（觉子；附例）

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:为儿子请老师：应爻是老师、世爻是东家，宜世应相生相合，不宜相克相冲；应生世我得益，应空破请不来，应动他有变心，世空我自己心意不专。觉子补充：父爻旺又和子孙相合，名实都有；父动伤克子孙，有损无益；子孙动变克，不宜聘请；子孙动变官鬼，应赶快辞退。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In engaging a teacher for one's son: the Other is the teacher and the Self is the host; the Self and Other should give life to and combine with each other and not control or clash; if the Other gives life to the Self I gain, if the Other is Void or broken he will not come, if the Other moves he has a change of mind, if the Self is Void my own mind is not set. Juezi adds: a strong Resource line combining with Output gains both name and substance; if Resource moves and harms Output there is harm and no gain; if Output moves and changes into control the man should not be engaged; if Output moves and changes into Pressure he should be thanked and dismissed quickly.

> **按**:「西席」「东家」是旧时请私塾先生的说法：老师坐西席，主人是东家。这里主人是父亲，所以世爻是父亲（东家），儿子拜师时看子孙爻。前面「延师受教，以父母爻为用神」是自己拜师的取法；这一条是父亲为儿子请老师，多看世应，再加子孙爻。后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> **Note:** "The west seat" and "the host" are the old way of speaking of hiring a private-school teacher: the teacher sits in the west seat and the master of the house is the host. Here the master is the father, so the Self is the father; when the son takes a teacher, the Output line is also looked at. The earlier line, "in engaging a teacher for instruction take the Resource line as the Subject", is for a man taking a teacher himself; this one is for a father engaging a teacher for his son, so the Self and Other are looked at first, with the Output line added. The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
