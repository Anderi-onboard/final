## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.17 原忌仇神论·用神旬空月破衰弱或伏藏而得原神动来生，必待用神出旬出破得令值日才所求必遂；用神旺相而原神休囚不动、变墓变绝变克变破变退，或被日辰月建克制，都不能生用

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:要知道原神。先看用神是哪个爻，生用神的爻就是原神。如果用神旬空、月破、衰弱，或者伏藏不现，得到原神发动来生它，或者日辰月建作原神生它，必须等用神出旬、出破、得令、值日，所求的才一定顺遂。如果用神旺相，原神休囚不动，或者动而变墓、变绝、变克、变破、变退，或者被日辰月建克制，都不能生用神，是用神的根基被伤了，不但没有益处，反而有损。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** One must know the Support. First see which line is the Subject; the line that gives life to the Subject is the Support. If the Subject is Void, struck by the Month-break, weak, or hidden and not appearing, and the Support moves to give it life, or the Day or the Month acts as the Support to give it life, one must wait until the Subject comes out of the period, out of the break, gets its season or meets its ruling day, and then what is sought will surely come. If the Subject is strong, but the Support is resting and still, or moves and changes into the tomb, extinction, control, break or retreat, or is controlled by the Day or the Month, none of these can give life to the Subject: the root of the Subject is injured, and it is not only no help but a loss.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.19 总断千金赋·月破要出破填合，旬空要出旬值日，动待合、静待冲，克处逢生、绝处逢生、冲中逢合、合处逢冲：乱动就搜独静的爻，安静就看逢冲的一日

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦理只论得中和之道：乱动就要搜寻独静的爻，安静就要看逢冲的一日，月破要出破、填合，旬空要出旬、值日，动的等合，静的等冲，克处逢生、绝处逢生、冲中逢合、合处逢冲，这些法则就是「太过的损掉它才成，不及的补上它才利」。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The principle of the hexagram speaks only of the way of balance: if lines move wildly, search for the sole still line; if all are still, look to the one day that brings a clash; the Month-break must leave the break or be filled and combined; the Void must leave the period or meet its ruling day; a moving line waits for a combination and a still one waits for a clash; life met in a control, life met in extinction, a combination met in a clash, and a clash met in a combination: these rules are what "what is excessive succeeds by being reduced, what is lacking profits by being supplied" means.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.23 总断千金赋·若遇休囚必生旺而成事——断日期的总法：用爻合住以冲日断，休囚以旺相月断，旺相不动以冲动月日断，有气发动以合日断，受制以制煞日月断，太旺以墓库日月断，无气发动遇生扶以生扶月日断，入墓以冲墓冲用月日断，旬空安静以出旬逢冲日断，旬空发动以出旬值日断，发动旬空被合以出旬冲日断，旬空安静被冲以出旬合日断，旬空发动逢冲谓冲实以本日断

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:如果遇到休囚，必待生旺才成事。断日期的方法不能执着于一种，应当用活法推断，才不会有差错。如果用爻被合住，就以冲它的日期来断。或者用爻休囚，必在生旺的时期才能成事，所以无气的当以旺相月来断。如果用爻旺相不动，就以冲动它的月日来断。如果用爻有气发动，就以合它的日子来断。或者有气发动合日辰，或日辰临它而动，或日辰临它而动来生合世爻身爻，就以本日来断。如果用爻受制，就以制煞它的日月来断。如果用爻多得时旺、发动，又遇生扶，这是太旺，应当以墓库的日月来断。如果用爻无气发动而遇生扶，就以生扶它的月日来断。如果用爻入墓，应当以冲墓、冲用的月日来断。如果用爻旬空安静，就以出旬逢冲的日子来断。如果用爻旬空发动，就以出旬值日来断。如果用爻发动旬空被合，就以出旬冲日来断。如果用爻旬空安静被冲，就以出旬合日来断。如果用爻旬空发动逢冲，叫冲实，就以本日来断。以上的断法只是撮其大要，其中最微妙的道理，学的人自己应当融会贯通、灵活变化，分清轻重，辨别用神和忌神，就一定没有差错了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If it meets resting or trapped, it succeeds only in a strong season. The method of judging the day cannot be held to one rule, and should be worked out flexibly, so that there is no mistake. If the Subject line is combined shut, judge by the day that clashes it. If the Subject is resting or trapped, it can succeed only in a period of life and strength, so one without breath is judged by a strong month. If the Subject is strong and still, judge by the month or day that clashes it into motion. If the Subject has breath and moves, judge by the day that combines it. If it has breath and moves to combine with the Day, or the Day sits on it and it moves, or the Day sits on it and it moves to give life to or combine with the Self or Body line, judge by that very day. If the Subject is controlled, judge by the month or day of the star that restrains the controller. If the Subject is in season, strong and moving, and also meets life and support, it is too strong, and should be judged by the month or day of the tomb or storehouse. If the Subject has no breath, moves and meets life and support, judge by the month or day that gives it life and support. If the Subject enters the tomb, judge by the month or day that clashes the tomb or the Subject. If the Subject is Void and still, judge by the day it meets a clash after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and moving, judge by its ruling day after coming out of the period. If the Subject moves and is Void and combined, judge by the clashing day after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and still and is clashed, judge by the combining day after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and moving and meets a clash, which is called clashed solid, judge by that very day. The above only picks out the main points; as for the most subtle principle in it, the student should digest it and turn it flexibly, tell heavy from light and distinguish the Subject from the Adversary, and then there will surely be no mistake.

**《卜筮正宗·总断千金赋》原文** — 《卜筮正宗》第 1263 行 · *Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "The Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements", base text; Bushi Zhengzong, line 1263*

## LY.TIMING.ZS.07 元神来助来扶贵看用神衰旺、忌神来冲来克须观元气兴衰：元神帮助用神，要看用神是衰是旺；忌神冲克用神，要看元神是兴是衰；详见元神忌神章

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:元神来帮助来扶持，要紧的是看用神的衰旺；忌神来冲来克，必须看元气的兴衰：详见元神忌神章。
