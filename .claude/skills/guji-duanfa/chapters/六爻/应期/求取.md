## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.02 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占仆何日回：伏午火财为用神，世空者迟至，忌神旬空待冲，旬内必到，乙巳日必回；应在巳日，是巳火也是财星，冲其飞露其伏

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:空下的伏神容易提拔出来。书中例：亥月甲子日占仆人哪天回，得革卦：伏在下面的午火财是用神，被日月克，问吉凶就是凶；现在问哪天回来，世爻空的到得慢，忌神旬空，等到冲它，旬内一定到，乙巳日必回；后来果然应验。应在巳日，是因为巳火也是财星，冲掉飞神、显露伏神。《黄金策》说「空下伏神易于引拔」，说的就是这个。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A hidden spirit under a Void line is easily drawn out. Worked example: in the Hai month, on a Jiazi day, a man divined on what day his servant would come back and got Ge: the hidden Wu-fire Wealth is the Subject and is controlled by the Month and the Day, so asked about luck it is ill; but asked on what day he returns, a Void Self arrives late, the Adversary is Void and waits to be clashed, so he surely comes within the ten-day period, and surely returns on the Yisi day; and afterwards it was borne out. It answered on the Si day because Si-fire is also Wealth, clashing away the flying spirit and exposing the hidden one. The Huangjin Ce says "a hidden spirit under a Void line is easily drawn out", and this is it.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.07 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占求财何日到手：未月持财月内必有，问哪天到手看卦中旬空的辰土，断辰日得财——舍未土之不空而应辰土之空

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:旬空的爻是事情的关键所现。书中例：未月庚子日占求财哪天到手，得小畜卦：未月财爻持世，月内必定有；现在问哪天到手，卦中辰土落旬空，必定是关键所现，断辰日得财；果然应验。这是舍弃未土的不空，而应在辰土的空上。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Void line is where the pivot of the matter shows. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Gengzi day, a man divined on what day wealth would come into his hands and got Xiaoxu: Wealth holds the Self in the Wei month, so he will surely have it within the month; but asked on what day it comes into his hands, the Chen-earth in the hexagram falls into the Void and is surely where the pivot shows, so it was judged that he would get the wealth on the Chen day; and it was borne out. This is passing over Wei-earth that is not Void and answering on the Void Chen-earth.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.10 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占弟死太湖尸首可见否：占尸首以鬼爻为用神，寅木鬼旬空、亥水得令暗动合它，尸首在大水之中，被合须待出旬逢冲，断庚申日可见；没见到，到丑月甲子旬壬申日才浮起——寅鬼出旬而亥水大旺，丑土制水，亥水旬空如水退，木在水中非冲不起

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神旬空被合，要等出旬逢冲。书中例：子月乙巳日有人占弟弟死在太湖，尸首能不能见到，得复卦：占尸首以鬼爻为用神，现在寅木爻落旬空，而亥水是得令之水，暗动去合它，分明尸首在大水之中；但寅木旬空被合，必须等出旬逢冲，庚申日可以见到；后来到庚申日没有见到，直到丑月甲子旬内的壬申日，尸首浮起才见到，为什么？总之寅鬼出旬后亥水大旺，到了丑月丑土制水，甲子旬里亥水旬空，水空就像水退了；木在水中不冲就浮不起来，所以应验在甲子旬壬申日，岂不是神灵的奇报？学的人不能不深究。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Subject in the Void and combined shut must wait for the ten-day period to be out and then be clashed. Worked example: in the Zi month, on a Yisi day, a man divined whether the body of his brother, who had died in Lake Tai, could be seen, and got Fu: in divining for a body the Pressure line is the Subject; now the Yin-wood line falls into the Void, while Hai-water is water in command and secretly moves to combine it, so the body is plainly in deep water; but Yin-wood is Void and combined shut, and must wait for the period to be out and then be clashed, so it can be seen on the Gengshen day; afterwards it was not seen on the Gengshen day, and only on the Renshen day within the Jiazi decade in the Chou month did the body float up and was found; why? In short, after Yin Pressure came out of the Void Hai-water was very strong, and when the Chou month came Chou-earth held the water in check, and in the Jiazi decade Hai-water is Void, and Void water is like water that has receded; wood in water does not float without a clash, so it was borne out on the Renshen day of the Jiazi decade; is this not a wondrous report from the spirits? The student must study it deeply.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.13 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占交疏之人何日来：占至交朋友以兄弟为用神，占交情疏的人以应爻为用神；应爻旬空得日辰冲起，必待甲辰日到，果验

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:旬空的爻被日辰冲起，要等到相应的日子。书中例：未月戊戌日占交情疏远的人哪天来，得蹇卦：凡是占至交朋友以兄弟爻为用神，现在占交情疏远的人，应当以应爻为用神；现在应爻落旬空，得日辰冲起，必须等甲辰日到；果然应验。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Void line clashed into motion by the Day waits for the corresponding day. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined on what day a casual acquaintance would come and got Jian: in divining for an intimate friend the Peer line is the Subject, but now divining for a casual acquaintance the Other line should be the Subject; now the Other line falls into the Void and is clashed into motion by the Day, so he must wait for the Jiachen day; and it was borne out.
