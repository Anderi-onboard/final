## LY.TIMING.ZS.02 太旺者逢墓逢冲、衰绝者遇生遇旺：太旺的在入墓或被冲的日子应，休囚无气的要等逢生或逢旺的月日才应

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:太旺的，遇到墓、遇到冲：比如主事爻落在午火，又遇到巳午月日占卦，或者卦中巳午爻太多，后来遇到亥子日应验，又有戌日应验，那是火入墓。衰绝的，遇到生、遇到旺：比如主事爻属金，在巳午月日占卦，就是休囚无气，后来遇到土月土日，或到秋令当时，就旺了；其余照这个类推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line that is too strong meets the tomb or a clash: for example, if the line ruling the matter sits on Wu-fire and the divination falls in a Si or Wu month or day, or there are too many Si or Wu lines in the hexagram, it answers on a later Hai or Zi day, and also on a Xu day, which is Fire entering the tomb. A line that is weak or extinct meets life or strength: for example, if the line ruling the matter is Metal and the divination falls in a Si or Wu month or day, it is resting and without breath; later, in an Earth month or day, or when autumn takes command, it is strong; the rest follow this.
