# 六爻 · 应期 · 进退

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Timing · Advancing and Retreating spirits*

1 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.TIMING.ZS.08 化进神逢值逢合、化退神忌值忌冲：化进神的在值日或合日应，有应在申月日的也有应在巳月日的；化退神的怕值日怕冲；也有应在独发独静的，详见独发章；有应在变爻动爻的日子

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:变成进神的，遇到值日、遇到合：比如申发动变出酉，是进神，不管是福是祸，有应在申月申日的，有应在巳月巳日的；其余照这个类推。变成退神的，忌讳值日、忌讳冲：比如酉发动变出申，是退神，不管是凶是吉，有应在申月申日的，有应在寅月寅日的；其余照这个类推。偶尔也有应在独发独静上的，详见独发章。偶尔也有应在变爻、动爻上的：比如爻落在戌土，变出酉金，它的吉凶有应在戌日的，有应在酉日的；其余照这个类推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line that changes into an Advancing spirit meets its ruling day or a combination: for example, Shen moving and changing into You is an Advancing spirit, and whether it brings fortune or misfortune, sometimes it answers in a Shen month or day and sometimes in a Si month or day; the rest follow this. A line that changes into a Retreating spirit fears its ruling day and fears a clash: for example, You moving and changing into Shen is a Retreating spirit, and whether it is ill or lucky, sometimes it answers in a Shen month or day and sometimes in a Yin month or day; the rest follow this. Now and then it answers on a sole moving or sole still line: see the chapter on sole movement. Now and then it answers on the changed line or the moving line: for example, if a line sits on Xu-earth and changes into You-metal, its luck or ill sometimes answers on a Xu day and sometimes on a You day; the rest follow this.
