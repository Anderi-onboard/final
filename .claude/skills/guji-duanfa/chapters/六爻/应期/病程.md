## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.05 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占妻病何日愈：卯木财爻为用神旬空，亥水原神生它，次日必愈；有人说空爻应断卯日，答甲寅日卯木已出空、寅木也是用星，果然先好一日

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:用神旬空，有原神相生，等到出空的时候。书中例：午月癸丑日占妻子的病哪天好，得萃卦变比卦：卯木财爻是用神，落旬空，有亥水原神生它，第二天一定好；旁边有人问：卯爻旬空该断卯日，为什么说寅日？我说：你不懂这个办法，到了甲寅日卯木已经出空了，寅木也是用星；果然先好了一天，应在甲寅日。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Subject in the Void with the Support giving it life waits until the Void is out. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Guichou day, a man divined on what day his wife's illness would be cured and got Cui changing to Bi: the Wealth Mao-wood is the Subject and falls into the Void, with the Support Hai-water giving it life, so she will surely be better the next day. Someone beside him asked: a Void Mao line should be judged on the Mao day, so why the Yin day? I said: you do not know the method; when the Jiayin day comes Mao-wood is already out of the Void, and Yin-wood is also a star of the Subject; indeed she got better a day early, answering on the Jiayin day.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.06 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占子病何时愈：亥水子孙化寅木旬空，近病逢空即愈，但亥水化出的寅木旬空受不到酉金之生，必等寅日愈，果验——化空出空

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:近病遇空就好，但用神化出的又是空，要等出空的日子。书中例：寅月庚戌日占儿子的病什么时候好，得姤卦变无妄卦：亥水子孙化出寅木旬空，近病遇空就好，但嫌亥水化出的寅木落旬空，受不到酉金的生，必须等寅日才好；果然应验，这是化空出空。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A near illness meets the Void and is cured, but when what the Subject changes into is also Void, wait for the day it comes out. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Gengxu day, a man divined when his son's illness would be cured and got Gou changing to Wuwang: the Output Hai-water changes into Yin-wood, which is Void; a near illness meeting the Void is cured, but the trouble is that Yin-wood, into which Hai-water changes, is Void and cannot receive life from You-metal, so he must wait for the Yin day; and it was borne out, a case of changing into the Void and then coming out of it.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.08 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占岳父近病：酉金岳父旬空近病逢空即愈，日辰合之近病逢合即死，加忌神克它病必危；午火自化旬空克不了它，旬内不死，防乙酉日，果于乙酉日卯时死

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:近病遇空就好，遇合就死。书中例：酉月庚辰日占岳父的近病，得师卦变升卦：酉金岳父爻落旬空，近病遇空就好；日辰合它，近病遇合就死；又不宜忌神来克它，这个病必定危险；问危险在哪天，我说：午火自己化旬空，旬空克不了它，近病遇空不死，旬内不死，要防乙酉日；果然在乙酉日卯时死了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A near illness meeting the Void is cured, and meeting a combination means death. Worked example: in the You month, on a Gengchen day, a man divined about his father-in-law's recent illness and got Shi changing to Sheng: the father-in-law line You-metal falls into the Void, and a near illness meeting the Void is cured; but the Day combines it, and a near illness meeting a combination means death; moreover it is unfit that the Adversary holds and controls it, so this illness is surely dangerous. Asked on what day the danger falls, I said: Wu-fire itself changes into the Void, and a Void cannot control it; a near illness meeting the Void does not die, and he will not die within the ten-day period, so guard against the Yiyou day; indeed he died on the Yiyou day in the Mao hour.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.09 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占子病：午火子孙伏在世爻亥水之下，月建生它、亥水克它，目下用神旬空不受其克，甲午日难逃，果死于午日午时——伏无提拔

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:伏神得不到提拔，终究是徒劳。书中例：酉月壬辰日占儿子的病，得大过卦：午火子孙伏在世爻亥水之下，月建生助它，亥水克它，眼下用神落旬空不受克，到甲午日难逃了；果然死在午日午时，这叫伏无提拔。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A hidden spirit not lifted up is in vain in the end. Worked example: in the You month, on a Renchen day, a man divined about his son's illness and got Daguo: the Output Wu-fire is hidden under the Self line Hai-water; the Month gives it life and Hai-water controls it; for now the Subject is Void and is not hurt by the control, but on the Jiawu day he cannot escape; and indeed he died on the Wu day in the Wu hour, which is what is meant by a hidden spirit with no lift.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.11 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占父近病：巳火父母为用神旬空，日辰拱它，近病逢空不死，但不利六合卦、六合即死；世上忌神暗动、外卦巳酉丑合成金局助水克它，此病必凶，危于出旬辛亥日，果至其日死——出旬又被冲克

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:近病遇空不死，遇六合就死。书中例：丑月甲午日有人占父亲的近病，得复卦变噬嗑卦：巳火父母是用神，落旬空，日辰拱它，近病遇空不死，但不利六合卦，六合就死；我想用神空和六合可以相抵，偏偏不宜世上忌神暗动，外卦巳酉丑合成金局去助水克它，此病必凶；他问：凶在哪天？我说：巳日亥日克冲巳火，还在旬空里没有妨碍，恐怕危险在出旬的辛亥日；果然到那天死了，这是应在出旬又被冲克。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A near illness meeting the Void does not die, and meeting a six-combine dies. Worked example: in the Chou month, on a Jiawu day, a man divined about his father's recent illness and got Fu changing to Shihe: the Resource Si-fire is the Subject and falls into the Void, with the Day embracing it; a near illness meeting the Void does not die, but a six-combine hexagram is unfavourable, and a six-combine means death. I thought the Void Subject and the six-combine could offset each other, but what is unfit is the Adversary on the Self line moving secretly, and the outer trigram Si, You and Chou combining into a Metal frame that helps the water to control it, so this illness is surely ill. He asked: on what day does the ill fall? I said: on a Si or a Hai day it is controlled or clashed, but while it is still in the Void there is no harm; I fear the danger lies on the Xinhai day when it comes out of the ten-day period; and indeed he died that day. This is answering at coming out of the period and being clashed and controlled.
