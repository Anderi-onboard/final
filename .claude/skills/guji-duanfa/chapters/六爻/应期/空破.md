## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.01 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占求财：辰土财爻持世旬空，戌日冲之，冲空则起，本日即得

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:旬空的爻，没有生扶又被克，到底是空；有生扶没有克，等到时候就有用。书中例：巳月戊戌日占求财，得益卦：辰土财爻持世，因为落旬空，戌日冲它，叫冲空则起，当天就得到；果然应验。应在当天，是因为戌日也是财星来冲我。这是冲空有用。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Void line with no life and under control is empty in the end; one with life and no control is useful when its time comes. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined for wealth and got Yi: the Wealth Chen-earth holds the Self and falls into the Void, and the Xu day clashes it, which is "a clash raises up the Void", so he gets it that very day; and it was borne out. It answered on the same day because the Xu day is also the Wealth star clashing me. This is a Void that is useful when clashed.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.04 十八问答·第七问·书中例，一人自占婚：戌土财爻旬空化巳火回头之生，动生不空，次日求必允，果于次日巳时允婚

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:空的爻发动变出生它的爻，就不算空。书中例：子月癸酉日有人自己占婚事，得恒卦变鼎卦：世爻官、应爻财，算得地；现在戌土财爻落旬空，化出巳火回头生它，动而被生就不空，第二天去求一定答应；果然第二天巳时答应了婚事。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A Void line that moves and changes into a line that gives it life is not counted as Void. Worked example: in the Zi month, on a Guiyou day, a man divined for his own marriage and got Heng changing to Ding: the Self is Pressure and the Other is Wealth, which counts as well placed; now the Wealth Xu-earth falls into the Void but changes into Si-fire which gives it life in return, and a line that moves and is given life is not Void, so if he asks the next day it will surely be granted; indeed the next day, in the Si hour, the marriage was agreed.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.15 旬空论·旬空之爻是神机发现处：旺相旬空、休囚发动、有日辰动爻生扶、动爻变空、伏而旺相，到底有用，只待出旬值日，有合空、冲起、冲实、填补之法；休囚安静、被日辰动爻克、伏而被克、静逢月破的是真空到底空

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦中的爻遇到旬空，是神机在这里显现。如果旺相而旬空，或休囚而发动，有日辰生扶、动爻生扶，动爻变空，伏而旺相，这些旬空到底有用，不过等它出旬值日：有合空、冲起、冲实、填补的办法，后面各卷的占验里注明。如果休囚安静，或被日辰克、动爻克，伏而被克，静而逢月破，遇到这样的旬空，叫做真空，到底是空了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line in the Void in the hexagram is where the working of the spirits shows. A line that is strong and Void, or resting and moving, with life and support from the Day and from moving lines, or moving to change into the Void, or hidden but strong, has a Void that is in the end useful, and only waits to come out of the period and meet its ruling day: there are the ways of combining the Void, clashing it up, clashing it solid and filling it, set out in the verified examples in the later volumes. A line that is resting and still, or controlled by the Day or a moving line, or hidden and controlled, or still and meeting the Month-break, with such a Void is called a true Void and is empty in the end.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.16 月破论·月破之爻是关因所现：动的也能生克他爻，变的也能生克本爻，目下虽破出月不破，今日虽破值日不破；月破最喜逢合填实，远应年月近应日时；破而安静又旬空衰弱遇克害的是真破到底破

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦中月破的爻，是关键因由所现。动的也能生克别的爻，变出的也能生克本爻——目下虽然破，出了这个月就不破；今天虽然破，到值日就不破。月破最喜欢逢合、填实，远的应在年月，近的应在日时。如果破而安静，又碰上旬空衰弱，遇到动爻、月建、日辰克害，这样的月破，叫做真破，到底是破了！

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line struck by the Month-break is where the pivot of the matter shows. A moving one can also give life to or control other lines, and a changed one can give life to or control its own line: though it is broken now, it is not broken once the month is out; though it is broken today, it is not broken on its ruling day. The Month-break most loves to meet a combination or be filled; far answers by year and month, near by day and hour. If it is broken and still, and also meets the Void and weakness, and is harmed by moving lines, the Month or the Day, such a Month-break is called a true break and is broken in the end!

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.18 辟《易林补遗》月破旬空之谬：月破的爻发动旺相或有生合，只是在月内不能为吉凶，出月值日合补也能吉能凶；旬空的爻或旺相安静、休囚发动、被日辰生合冲、变出、伏而有提拔，屡试屡验应在出旬

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:卦中月破的爻，发动旺相，或者遇到动爻生合、日辰生合，或者化出回头生合，不过是在月内不能成吉凶，出了月、值日、合补，也能吉能凶。张星元说月破没法解救，一概当凶推。卦中旬空的爻，或者旺相安静，休囚发动，日辰生合或冲它，或是变出的，或者伏着而有提拔的，屡试屡验，应在出旬。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line struck by the Month-break that moves and is strong, or meets life or combination from a moving line or the Day, or changes into a turning-back life or combination, is merely unable to make luck or ill within the month; once the month is out, or on its ruling day, or when filled and combined, it can bring luck or ill. Zhang Xingyuan says the Month-break cannot be rescued and judges it ill across the board. A line in the Void that is strong and still, or resting and moving, or given life, combined or clashed by the Day, or one that is changed out, or hidden with someone to lift it, has been borne out again and again to answer when the period is out.

## LY.TIMING.ZS.05 月破喜逢填合、旬空最爱填冲：月破的在逢合日或填实的月日应；旬空的详见旬空章

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:月破的，喜欢遇到填实或合：比如子月占卦，主事爻落在午火，是月破，后来遇到未日应验，叫做破而逢合；又有遇到午月应验，填实了就不破了；其余照这个类推。旬空的最喜欢被填实或冲：详见旬空章。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line struck by the Month-break is glad to meet a filling or a combination: for example, if the divination falls in the Zi month and the line ruling the matter sits on Wu-fire, it is broken by the Month, and it answers on a later Wei day, which is called a break that meets a combination; it may also answer in the Wu month, when it is filled and no longer broken; the rest follow this. A Void line most loves to be filled or clashed: see the chapter on the Void.

## LY.TIMING.ZS.12 月破章·用神月破虽破而动则能伤爻、变则能伤动：目下虽破出月不破，今日虽破值日不破、逢合之日不破，近应日时远应年月，只有静而不动又无生助的才到底破了

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:诸书都把用神临月破叫做悖时，像枯根朽木，逢生生不起，逢伤伤得更重；虽然现在卦里，有也像没有；伏在卦中，终究难透出来；就算有日辰生它，也不能生；动而作忌神的，也不能为害，作变爻的，也不能伤克动爻。野鹤说：我验证过，动就能伤爻，变就能伤动，为什么？神灵的兆示机缘在动，事情没有吉凶就不会动；既然动了，就有祸福的根基；目下虽然破，出了这个月就不破；今天虽然破，到实破的日子就不破，逢合的日子不破，近的应在日时，远的应在年月；只有静而不动，又没有日辰动爻生助的，才到底破了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** All the books call a Subject on the Month-break "out of season", like a withered root or rotten wood, which if given life is not raised and if injured is injured the more; though it is on the hexagram it is as if absent; hidden in the hexagram, it is hard to bring out in the end; even given life by the Day, it cannot be raised; moving as the Adversary, it cannot do harm; as a changed line, it cannot injure the moving line. Yehe says: I have found it borne out that it can injure a line if it moves and can injure the moving line if it changes into it. Why? The spirits give their sign in moving, and if a matter had neither luck nor ill it would not move; once it moves, there is a basis of fortune or misfortune. Though it is broken now, it is not broken once the month is out; though it is broken today, it is not broken on the day that fills the break, nor on the day it is combined; near answers by day and hour, far by year and month. Only one that is still, does not move, and has no life or support from the Day or a moving line is broken to the end.

## LY.TIMING.ZS.13 旬空章·野鹤论旬空：旺不为空、动不为空、动而化空、伏而旺相、有日建动爻生扶都不为空；月破为空、有气不动为空、伏而被克为空、真空为空；遇旬空命再占，卦吉的许出旬不空，卦凶的许空

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:野鹤说：旺的不算空，动的不算空，动而化空、伏而旺相，都不算空，有日建动爻生扶的，也不算空；月破算空，有气但不动也算空，伏而被克也算空，真空是空。（真空的意思：春天的土、夏天的金、秋天的木、整个冬天遇火，是真空。）我刚学占卜时，凡是遇到旬空，茫然分不清，想断成到底全空，却又应在填实的日子而不空，当作不空，却又到底全空；后来得到多占的办法，凡是遇到旬空，叫他再占一卦，卦吉的，许他出旬就不空；卦凶的，许他是空了。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Yehe says: a strong line is not Void, a moving line is not Void, a line that moves and changes into the Void or is hidden and strong is not Void, and one given life and support by the Day Branch or a moving line is not Void; a line struck by the Month-break counts as Void, a line with breath that does not move also counts as Void, a hidden line that is controlled counts as Void, and a true Void is Void. (A true Void: Earth in spring, Metal in summer, Wood in autumn, and Fire in all three winter months.) When I first learned divination I was at a loss whenever I met the Void: I wanted to judge it wholly empty, yet it answered on the day of filling and was not empty; I took it as not empty, yet it was wholly empty in the end. Later I got the method of divining several times: whenever the Void is met, have the man divine again; if the hexagram is lucky, promise that it is not Void once the period is out; if the hexagram is ill, promise that it is Void.
