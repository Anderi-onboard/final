## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.21 总断千金赋·墓中人不冲不发：用爻入墓多阻滞，诸事费力难成，要等日辰动爻冲它或冲克它的墓爻才有用——古书说「冲空则起，破墓则开」

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:墓中的人，不冲就不发。大概用爻入墓就多有阻滞，诸事费力难成，必须等日辰、动爻冲它，或冲克它的墓爻，才有用。古书说：「冲空就起，破墓就开。」

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A man in the tomb does not rise without a clash. Generally a Subject line entering the tomb is much hindered, and all matters are done with effort and hard to complete; one must wait for the Day or a moving line to clash it or to clash and control its tomb line before it is of use. The old books say: "a clash raises the Void, and breaking the tomb opens it."

## LY.TIMING.ZS.03 入三墓俱喜冲开：主事爻入墓，在冲开墓的日子应——主事爻午火，火墓在戌，后逢辰日应

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:入了三墓，都喜欢被冲开：比如主事爻落在午火，火的墓在戌，后来遇到辰日就应验；其余照这个类推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line that has entered one of the three tombs is glad to be clashed open: for example, if the line ruling the matter sits on Wu-fire, whose tomb is Xu, it answers on a later Chen day; the rest follow this.
