## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.20 总断千金赋·动值合而绊住：动爻有合就被绊住而不能动，不能生物克物；日辰合它要等冲它的本爻之日应事，旁爻来合要等冲那个旁爻之日；用丑土财子日合之待未日应，子孙动被日辰合住不能生财要等冲动子孙之期

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:动而遇到合就被绊住。凡是动爻没遇到合才算动，如果有合就被绊住不能动了；既然不能动，就不能生物克物了。如果日辰合它，必须等冲它本爻的日子到，才能应事的吉凶；如果旁边的爻发动来合它，必须等冲那个旁爻的日子到，才能应事的吉凶。假如用丑土财被子日合住，等到未日应事；子爻合它的，等午日应事。又比如子孙爻发动而被日辰合住，就不能生财，要等冲动子孙的时候到，才有财；其余照这个类推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** A line that moves and meets a combination is held fast. A moving line counts as moving only if it meets no combination; if there is a combination it is held fast and cannot move, and being unable to move it cannot give life to or control anything. If the Day combines it, one must wait for the day that clashes its own line before the luck or ill of the matter answers; if a neighbouring line moves to combine it, one must wait for the day that clashes that neighbouring line. For example, if the Wealth is Chou-earth and the Zi day combines it, wait for the Wei day to answer; if a Zi line combines it, wait for the Wu day to answer. Again, if the Output line moves and is combined shut by the Day, it cannot give life to Wealth, and one must wait for the time that clashes the Output into motion before there is wealth; the rest follow this.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.22 总断千金赋·如逢合住须冲破以成功：卦中用神忌神遇日辰合、自化合或动爻来合，不论吉凶都不见效，要等冲破的日期才应事；用爻动来生世本来易成，遇合住又阻滞，须待冲之日才有成

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:如果逢合住，必须冲破才成功。卦中用神、忌神遇到日辰合，或自己化出合，或有动爻来合它，不论吉凶，都不见效，必须等冲破的日期才应事的吉凶。假如用爻动来生世，凡事容易成，如果遇到合住，又变得阻滞，必须等冲它的日子，事情才有成。以下都是断日期的办法。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** If it meets a combination that holds it shut, it must be clashed open before it succeeds. When the Subject or the Adversary in the hexagram meets a combination with the Day, or changes into a combination of itself, or a moving line comes to combine it, whether luck or ill, it has no effect, and the luck or ill of the matter answers only on the day it is clashed open. For example, if the Subject line moves to give life to the Self, everything is easily accomplished, but if it meets a combination that holds it shut it becomes blocked, and one must wait for the day that clashes it before the matter succeeds. What follows are all methods of judging the day.

## LY.TIMING.BSZZ.24 十八问答·第四问·三合成局的应期：冲破三合要等相合之期；一爻静二爻发的等静的那一爻值日；一爻静而逢空、动而逢空、化而逢空的，等出空之期；空而逢合、静而逢合、动而合的，等冲期；自化合或与日合、自化墓或墓于日的，等期冲；自化绝或绝于日的，等期生

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:三合成局，遇到日冲叫暗动（指静爻被日冲），叫填实（指动爻落空被日辰冲），叫破（指月建冲）。它被冲破，必须等到相合的日期到，才应事的吉凶。如果一爻静、两爻发的，必须等那一爻静的值日应事。如果一爻静而逢空，或动而逢空，或变化而逢空的，等到它出空的日期应事的吉凶。如果空而逢合，静而逢合，动而合的，必须等冲的日期到，才应事的吉凶。如果自己化合或与日辰合，或自己化墓或墓在日辰的，必须等到冲的日期（这是说三爻齐发、其中两爻没有病，指自化的那一爻说）。如果自己化绝或绝在日辰的，必须等到生的日期（也是指有一爻有病的说）。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** In a three-harmony frame, meeting a clash from the Day is called secret motion (a still line clashed by the Day), being filled solid (a moving Void line clashed by the Day), or breaking (clashed by the Month). When it is clashed or broken, the luck or ill of the matter answers only when the time of combination comes. If one line is still and two move, one must wait for the still line to meet its ruling day. If one line is still and Void, or moves and is Void, or changes into the Void, wait for the time it comes out of the Void. If it is Void and meets a combination, or still and meets a combination, or moves and combines, one must wait for the time of the clash. If it changes into a combination of itself or combines with the Day, or changes into the tomb of itself or is in the tomb of the Day, one must wait for the time of the clash (this is said of three lines all moving where two have no ailment, and means the one that changes of itself). If it changes into extinction of itself or is extinct on the Day, one must wait for the time of life (also said of the case where one line has an ailment).

## LY.TIMING.ZS.04 遇六合亦宜相击：主事爻与日月作合、或动与爻合、或动而化合，不管吉凶都要等冲开它的月日应——主象临子与丑作合，后逢午未日应

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:遇到六合，也适宜相互冲击：主事爻与日月作合，或发动与别的爻合，或发动而化出合，不管是凶是吉，必定等到冲开它的月日才应验。就像主象落在子，与丑作合，后来遇到午日、未日应验。其余照这个类推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** Meeting a six-combine, it is likewise fit to be struck by a clash: if the line ruling the matter forms a combination with the Month or the Day, or moves and combines with another line, or moves and changes into a combination, whether it is ill or lucky it answers only in the month or day that clashes it open. For example, if the principal image sits on Zi and forms a combination with Chou, it answers on a later Wu or Wei day. The rest follow this.
