# 六爻 · 应期 · 静动

*Liu Yao (six-line coin divination) · Timing · Still and moving lines*

1 条。原文逐字核对过 `functions/_lib/doctrine/books/` 下的文本,行号是那份整理本的行号,不是原刻本的页码。「规则(整理)」是本库的归纳,引用时引原文。

## LY.TIMING.ZS.01 静而逢值逢冲、动而逢合逢值：主事爻安静的，逢它值日或被冲的日子应；主事爻发动的，逢合日或值日应

**规则(整理,不是原文)**:主事的爻安静而遇到值日、遇到冲：比如主事爻落在子水不动，后来遇到子日、午日就应验；其余照这个类推。主事的爻发动而遇到合、遇到值日：比如主事爻落在子水发动，后来遇到子日、丑日就应验；其余照这个类推。

**Rule (our summary, not the source text):** The line that rules the matter, still, meets its ruling day or a clash: for example, if the line ruling the matter sits on Zi-water and does not move, it answers on a later Zi day or Wu day; the rest follow this. The line ruling the matter, moving, meets a combination or its ruling day: for example, if the line ruling the matter sits on Zi-water and moves, it answers on a later Zi day or Chou day; the rest follow this.
