| 妻财 | qīcái | Wealth | 财爻、妻位、财神、财星、妻爻、财 | 妻财、财爻 | 六亲之一,我克者。求财以它为用神;男占婚姻也以它为妻。 | One of the six relations: the element the Self controls. The Subject line for money questions (and the wife in a man's marriage question). |
| 子孙 | zǐsūn | Output | 福神、福德、子爻、福爻、福 | 子孙、福神、福德 | 六亲之一,我生者。它生妻财,所以求财时叫「财源」「福神」,是财的原神;又克官鬼,能解灾。 | One of the six relations: the element the Self generates. | 兄弟 | xiōngdì | Peer | 兄爻、弟兄、兄 | 兄弟、兄爻 | 六亲之一,与我同类者。它克妻财,占财最忌,书上叫「劫财」「劫杀」;也主同伴、竞争者、耗费。 | One of the six relations: the same element as the Self. | 官鬼 | guānguǐ | Pressure | 鬼爻、官爻、官星、鬼、官 | 官鬼、鬼爻、官爻 | 六亲之一,克我者。求财时一般主阻隔、官非、盗贼;但它克兄弟,能护财;空手求财、公门求财时反而以它为主。 | One of the six relations: the element that controls the Self. In money questions it usually means obstruction, officials, lawsuits or thieves; but it controls Peer and so can protect the money, and for skill-based or official money it becomes the main line. |
| 父母 | fùmǔ | Resource | 父爻、父 | 父母、父爻 | 六亲之一,生我者。它克子孙,断了财的来源,占财时叫「绝源杀」;又主辛劳、文书、契约。 | One of the six relations: the element that generates the Self. It controls Output and so cuts off the source of money (the classics call it the killer of the source); it also means toil, documents and contracts. |

## 时令

| 日辰 | rìchén | Day | 日建、日 | 日辰 | 起卦那一天的地支,能生克冲合各爻,也定旬空。 | The branch of the day of casting; it can generate, control, clash with or combine any line, and fixes which branches are void. |

| 三传 | sānchuán | three rulers |  | 三传 | 太岁、月建、日辰三个当值的干支。《黄金策》旧注:「三传太岁月建日辰」;《卜筮正宗》:「三传,年月日也」。 | The Year (Tai Sui), the Month and the Day: the three rulers of the moment. Not the three transmissions of Da Liu Ren. |

## 动变

| 术语 | 拼音 | English | 别名 | 核对 | 释义 | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 动爻 | dòngyáo | moving line | 动、发动、交重、交、重 | 动爻 | 摇出老阴(交)、老阳(重)的爻,会变。书上说「交重」就是发动。 | A line cast as old yin (jiao) or old yang (zhong); it changes. The classics' jiaozhong simply means moving. |
| 变爻 | biànyáo | changed line | 化、变出、化出 | 变爻 | 动爻变出来的那一爻。「甲化乙」就是甲爻动,变出乙。动为事之始,变为事之终。 | The line a moving line turns into. 'A turns into B' means line A moves and becomes B. The moving line is how the matter starts; the changed line is how it ends. |
| 回头克 | huítóukè | turns back to control | 回头之克 |  | 动爻变出来的爻反过来克它自己。 | The changed line controls the very line that moved into it. |
| 进神 | jìnshén | Advancing | 化进神 | 进神 | 同一五行往前变(如寅化卯),主越来越盛。 | A line turns into the next branch of the same element (e.g. Yin into Mao): growing stronger. |
| 退神 | tuìshén | Retreating | 化退神、化退 | 退神 | 同一五行往回变(如卯化寅),主消退。 | A line turns back into the previous branch of the same element (e.g. Mao into Yin): fading. |
