## 打架 · Where the classics disagree(135 条)

- **LY.ILL.BSQS.33 疾病论·八卦鬼与病位：巽鬼股痛、震官足疾不瘳；离宫疮痍、火鬼心目之灾；兑官面病、坎鬼腹中灾；乾官头痛、坤鬼肚疼；坎水耳疾、艮土手灾**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/症候.md`):八卦各管身体哪一处，几处说法不同。疾病论这一段是「乾头、坤肚腹、坎耳（另一联又说腹中）、艮手、震足、巽股、离疮痍和心目、兑面」；卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.12）是「乾头、坤腹、坎耳、离目、兑口、艮手、震足、巽股」，和这一段在巽股、艮手上一致；孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）是巽肠、艮手；阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.02）是巽手、艮鼻。不同的地方有三处：兑，这里是面，别处是口；坎，这一段本身就有两说，一联说耳、一联说腹中；离，这里多了疮痍和心，别处只有目。书里没有裁决。
  The books differ on which part of the body each trigram governs. This passage of the chapter on illness gives 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ear (and in another couplet the inside of the belly), Gen the hand, Zhen the foot, Xun the thigh, Li sores and heart and eyes, Dui the face'. The Bushi Yuangui passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.12) gives 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ear, Li the eye, Dui the mouth, Gen the hand, Zhen the foot, Xun the thigh', which agrees with this passage on Xun the thigh and Gen the hand; Sun Bin's verse (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) has Xun the intestines and Gen the hand; the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.02) has Xun the hand and Gen the nose. There are three places of difference: Dui is the face here and the mouth elsewhere; Kan has two readings within this one passage, the ear in one couplet and the inside of the belly in another; and Li has sores and the heart added here, where elsewhere there is only the eye. The books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSQS.35 疾病论·死生之兆(一)：财为禄命怕空亡；十死卦春忌需蒙、夏嫌观蛊、秋忧剥节、冬畏旅临；禄命随官难救、世身入墓难医；鬼入用爻当主死**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):哪几个卦占病最凶，几本书的名单不同。这一段按季节给：春需、蒙，夏观、蛊，秋剥、节，冬旅、临；阐奥歌章第三首（LY.ILL.CA.03）是丰、蛊、困、明夷、夬、同人一例，加冬旅、春需、夏观、秋剥，和这一段在旅、需、观、剥四卦的季节上一致；卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.15）是丰、观、需、剥、节、旅、贲、明夷、蛊、夬、同人「十死分明」；天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.06）是明夷、观、贲、需、临（世身入墓）和大畜、丰、同、蛊、夬（财鬼俱兴）。蒙只有这一段有，临只有这一段和天玄赋有；另外这一段点名的只有八个卦，却称「十死卦」，名目和数目对不上。书里没有裁决。
  The books differ on which hexagrams are the worst for illness. This passage gives them by season: Xu and Meng in spring, Guan and Gu in summer, Bo and Jie in autumn, Lu and Lin in winter. The Chan'ao Gezhang's third poem (LY.ILL.CA.03) gives Feng, Gu, Kun, Mingyi, Guai and Tongren together, plus Lu in winter, Xu in spring, Guan in summer and Bo in autumn, agreeing with this passage on the seasons of Lu, Xu, Guan and Bo; the Bushi Yuangui passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.15) gives Feng, Guan, Xu, Bo, Jie, Lu, Bi, Mingyi, Gu, Guai and Tongren, 'the ten deaths are clear'; the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.06) gives Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin (the Self and Body entering the tomb) and Daxu, Feng, Tong, Gu and Guai (Wealth and Pressure both rising). Meng is found only in this passage, and Lin only in this passage and the Tianxuan Fu; moreover this passage names only eight hexagrams yet calls them 'the ten fatal hexagrams', so the name and the number do not match. The books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSQS.37 疾病论·神鬼之由(一)：乾宫愿欠天庭、坤鬼动地府、艮鬼路神作祸、震宫克世狱司降灾；鬼临五位路头神、官在三爻家内崇；坎鬼溺水之魂、巽鬼悬梁之崇；坎宫船车之鬼、艮象路狱之魂**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):鬼爻落在哪一爻是什么神，几处说法不同。这一段是「五位路头神、三爻家内崇」；卜筮全书的六位取象表（LY.ILL.BSQS.02）是初爻井神、二爻司命、三爻家先、四爻土地、五爻社司、六爻天神，三爻家先和这里的「家内崇」相通，五爻则是社司，不是路头神。另两种排法见 LY.ILL.HZL.11 和 LY.ILL.EL.37。书里没有裁决。
  The books differ on what spirit a ghost on each line stands for. This passage has 'the fifth place the Road-Head spirit, the third line a haunting within the house'; the table of the positions of the lines in the Bushi Quanshu (LY.ILL.BSQS.02) has the first line the well spirit, the second the Life-Governing Spirit, the third the family ancestors, the fourth the Earth God, the fifth the village altar spirit and the sixth the heavenly spirit; the third line, family ancestors, is akin to 'a haunting within the house' here, but the fifth line is the village altar spirit there and not the Road-Head spirit. Two other arrangements are in LY.ILL.HZL.11 and LY.ILL.EL.37. The books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSQS.43 五行论病歌：金鬼牙与头、更兼白虎血脓流；木鬼风疾头眩眼暗；水兴寒热多因疟、痢疾伤风；火鬼虚劳弱症眼疼；土鬼脾胃虚黄肿、间动膈病**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/症候.md`):五行的鬼各主什么病，几家说法不同。这一首是「金鬼牙与头（加白虎血脓）、木鬼风疾头眩眼暗、水寒热疟痢、火鬼虚劳眼痛、土鬼脾胃虚黄肿」；阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.02）是水腰肾、金肺、火心经、木肝、土脾胃；卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.12）是木鬼身体疼痛、水土鬼痈肿、金鬼牙骨痛；同一本书的疾病论（LY.ILL.BSQS.32）是金木动骨骼疼酸、水火兴皮肤寒热、丑未伤脾。土配脾胃、金配牙，几家相近；火配心（阐奥歌章）、配虚劳眼痛（这一首）、配皮肤寒热（疾病论），木配肝（阐奥歌章）、配风疾头眩（这一首）、配身体疼痛（卜筮元龟），各不相同。书里没有裁决。
  The books differ on what illness each element's ghost governs. This verse has 'Metal ghost teeth and head (with the White Tiger, blood and pus), Wood ghost wind illness, dizziness and dim eyes, Water chills and fever with malaria and dysentery, Fire ghost consumption and eye pain, Earth ghost weak spleen and stomach with yellow swelling'; the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.02) has Water the waist and kidneys, Metal the lungs, Fire the heart channel, Wood the liver, Earth the spleen and stomach; the Bushi Yuangui passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.12) has a Wood ghost bodily pain, a Water or Earth ghost swollen abscesses, a Metal ghost pain in the teeth and bones; and the chapter on illness in the same book (LY.ILL.BSQS.32) has Metal or Wood moving, aching bones and joints, Water or Fire rising, chills and fever of the skin, Chou or Wei moving, harm to the spleen. Earth matched to the spleen and stomach and Metal to the teeth are close in several; Fire is matched to the heart (Chan'ao Gezhang), to consumption and eye pain (this verse) and to chills and fever of the skin (the chapter on illness), and Wood to the liver (Chan'ao Gezhang), to wind illness and dizziness (this verse) and to bodily pain (Bushi Yuangui), all different. The books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSQS.51 神煞歌例·岁前神煞：从太岁起顺轮十二位，三丧门主孝服、六死符、十一天狗吊客、十二病符切莫逢**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/风险.md`):十二位岁煞各家的名单不一样。卜筮全书的岁前神煞是：一太岁剑锋伏尸、二太阳天空、三丧门、四勾绞贯索、五官符五鬼、六死符小耗、七栏干大耗、八暴败天厄、九飞廉白虎、十福德卷舌、十一天狗吊客、十二病符；断易天机的「四利三元」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.50）是：一太岁、二太阳、三丧门、四太阴、五官符、六死符、七岁破、八龙德、九白虎、十福德、十一吊客、十二病符。第一、二、三、五、六、九、十、十一、十二位的主名两本一致；第四位（勾绞贯索对太阴）、第七位（栏干大耗对岁破）、第八位（暴败天厄对龙德）不同，而且卜筮全书每一位后面还并带了一两颗别的星。书里没有裁决。
  The books' lists of the twelve year-stars differ. The stars before the Year in the Bushi Quanshu are: first Tai Sui with Sword Edge and Hidden Corpse, second Great Yang with Heavenly Emptiness, third Mourning Gate, fourth Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through, fifth Official Tally with the Five Ghosts, sixth Death Tally with Small Loss, seventh Railing with Great Loss, eighth Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress, ninth Feilian with the White Tiger, tenth Fortune Virtue with the Curled Tongue, eleventh the Heavenly Dog with the Guest of Condolence, twelfth Sickness Tally; the Duanyi Tianji's 'Four Gains and Three Origins' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.50) runs: first Tai Sui, second Great Yang, third Mourning Gate, fourth Great Yin, fifth Official Tally, sixth Death Tally, seventh Year-break, eighth Dragon Virtue, ninth White Tiger, tenth Fortune Virtue, eleventh Guest of Condolence, twelfth Sickness Tally. The principal names agree in the first, second, third, fifth, sixth, ninth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth places; the fourth (Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through against Great Yin), the seventh (Railing and Great Loss against Year-break) and the eighth (Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress against Dragon Virtue) differ, and the Bushi Quanshu joins one or two further stars to each place. The books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSQS.53 神煞歌例·占病见死的煞：墓门杀病人不死也伤财；丧车杀人来占病无他断、教君作急买棺材；沐浴杀发动病人亡**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):丧车杀的四季位置，两本不一样。卜筮全书这一首是「春鸡夏鼠秋兔冬马」；断易天机（LY.ILL.DYTJ.32）是「春鸡夏鼠秋马冬羊」；春、夏两季一致，秋、冬不同。另外断易天机的旌旗杀（LY.ILL.DYTJ.33）是「春兔夏鼠秋鸡冬马」，和卜筮全书这一首的丧车只差春、秋两季的鸡兔互换。书里没有裁决。
  The seasonal positions of the Mourning Cart differ between the two books. This verse in the Bushi Quanshu has 'the rooster in spring, the rat in summer, the rabbit in autumn, the horse in winter'; the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.32) has 'the rooster in spring, the rat in summer, the horse in autumn, the sheep in winter'; spring and summer agree, autumn and winter do not. Moreover the Banner killing of the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.33) has 'the rabbit in spring, the rat in summer, the rooster in autumn, the horse in winter', which differs from the Mourning Cart of this verse only in the rooster and rabbit swapped between spring and autumn. The books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSZZ.02 出花的是谁就按谁取用神：兄弟出花以兄弟爻为用神、父母爻为原神；子侄以子孙爻为用神、父动克子是凶；妻奴婢妾以财爻为用神、兄动克财不宜**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/痘疹.md`):用神取哪一爻：王洪绪按出花的是谁分开取，兄弟取兄弟爻、子侄取子孙爻、妻奴婢妾取财爻，官鬼爻则是痘花本身；增删卜易的野鹤把出花的几件事分开占，问什么时候出花拿鬼爻当用神（LY.ILL.ZS.19），问吉凶大体上全看子孙爻，不管来问的是谁（LY.ILL.ZS.21）。两家取用神的办法不一样，书里没有裁决。
  Which line is the Subject: Wang Hongxu takes it by who has the pox — the Peer line for a brother, the Output line for a child or nephew, the Wealth line for a wife, servant, maid or concubine — and the Pressure line is the pox flowers themselves; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi divides the matters of the pox into separate divinations, taking the Pressure line as the Subject for when the pox will come out (LY.ILL.ZS.19) and looking, on the whole, only at the Output line for fortune, whoever is asking (LY.ILL.ZS.21). The two differ on how the Subject is taken, and the books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSZZ.25 爻中煞当凭八卦论终身：白虎鬼居乾宫带疾在头、兑在面、震在足、巽在股、坎在耳、离在目、艮在手、坤在腹；福神克制则易医，加白虎持临是终身之疾**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/痘疹.md`):兑宫的部位，同一章里有两说：LY.ILL.BSZZ.23 说兑宫毒结在口，这一条说兑象带疾在面。书里没有裁决。
  The place of the Dui palace has two readings in this one chapter: LY.ILL.BSZZ.23 says the poison lodges in the mouth in the Dui palace, and this entry says the ailment lingers in the face in the Dui image. The book does not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSZZ.34 何知章·亲人的病灾：父母疾（白虎临爻兼刑克）、父母殃（财爻发动煞神伤）、子孙疾（父母爻动相克）、子孙灾、小儿死（子孙空亡加白虎）、兄弟亡（用落空亡白虎伤）**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/他人病.md`):子孙有灾看什么：卜筮大全答「白虎当临福德来」，白虎落到子孙爻上；卜筮正宗答「父母当临福德来」，父母爻落到子孙爻上。两本一个说白虎、一个说父母，书里没有说哪个对（白虎是六神，父母是六亲，两条不是一回事）。
  What to look at for a child's misfortune: the Bushi Daquan answers 'the White Tiger arrives upon Output', the White Tiger falling on the Output line; the Bushi Zhengzong answers 'Resource arrives upon Output', the Resource line falling on the Output line. One says the White Tiger and the other Resource, and the books do not say which is right (the White Tiger is one of the six spirits and Resource one of the six relations, so the two are not the same thing).
- **LY.ILL.BSZZ.36 何知章·病要死：卜筮正宗答用神无救又入墓；卜筮大全答身命世鬼入墓**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):凭什么断病要死：卜筮正宗答「用神无救又入墓」，只看用神一爻；卜筮大全答「身命世鬼入墓推」，看身、命、世、鬼四样入墓。两本不一样，书里没有裁决。
  What decides that the illness is unto death: the Bushi Zhengzong answers 'the Subject has no rescue and enters a tomb', looking only at the Subject line; the Bushi Daquan answers 'the Body, the destiny, the Self and the ghost enter a tomb', looking at four things entering a tomb. The two texts differ and the books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.BSZZ.37 何知章·怪与死：多梦寐（螣蛇带鬼持世）、出鬼怪（螣蛇白虎临门）、人投水（玄武入水煞临鬼）、有吊颈（螣蛇木鬼临世爻）、孝服来（白虎临鬼；大全作丧门吊客临鬼）**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):孝服来（有丧事）看什么：卜筮正宗答「交重白虎临鬼排」，白虎发动排在鬼爻上；卜筮大全答「丧门吊客临鬼排」，丧门、吊客两颗神煞排在鬼爻上。一个用六神白虎，一个用神煞丧门吊客，书里没有裁决。
  What tells that mourning is coming: the Bushi Zhengzong answers 'the White Tiger moving, set upon the ghost', the White Tiger of the six spirits moving on the Pressure line; the Bushi Daquan answers 'Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence set upon the ghost', two spirit-stars on the Pressure line. One uses the White Tiger among the six spirits and the other the spirit-stars Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence, and the books do not decide.

- **LY.ILL.DYTJ.46 卜筮元龟·占医人善恶：欲占医人善恶详无过贞悔可斟量；子爻在土宜丸药、水宜服汤、木青丸药、金火宜灸灼；子爻旺相好医人、无气杀冲还恶；卦无子爻何以遂、旺相相生药无崇、内外不和服药凶、更遇杀冲须早惧**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):用药按谁的五行选，几本书的做法不同。断易天机卜筮元龟这一首按子爻（药）的五行：子爻属土宜丸药、属水宜汤、属木宜青丸药、属金火宜灸灼；洞林秘诀（LY.ILL.DYTJ.47）说「药色五行自斟酌」，注也是按子孙爻的五行取药色；天玄赋注（LY.ILL.TXF.13）、火珠林（LY.ILL.HZL.09）、易隐（LY.ILL.YY.25）按鬼爻的五行：火鬼宜凉剂、水鬼宜温剂或丸药、木鬼宜针、金鬼宜灸、土鬼宜锉散。两个体系都在：一个看药本身（子孙）的五行，一个看病（鬼）的五行，并且结论不同（比如金爻：子爻属金宜灸灼，金鬼也宜灸，一致；水：子爻是水宜服汤，水鬼宜温剂或丸药，不一致）。书里没有裁决。
  Whose phase to use in choosing medicine is done differently in different books. The Bushi Yuangui song of the Duanyi Tianji takes the phase of the Output line (the medicine): Output on Earth pills, Water a decoction, Wood green pills, Metal and Fire moxibustion and cautery; the Dongling Mijue (LY.ILL.DYTJ.47) says 'the colour of the medicine is for oneself to weigh by the five phases', taking the colour of the medicine also from the phase of the Output line; the commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.13), the Huozhu Lin (LY.ILL.HZL.09) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.25) take the phase of the ghost line: a Fire ghost a cooling remedy, a Water ghost a warming remedy or pills, a Wood ghost the needle, a Metal ghost moxa, an Earth ghost cut-and-ground powders. The books do not settle it.

- **LY.ILL.EL.10 卦无用象：先察伏神，再推日月，再查互卦；互中体用二爻亦无用神方言无救；主卦无用象而伏出无伤决难损命；伏出遭刑克但日月互卦有一用爻亦无害**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/总则.md`):卦里没有用神先查什么：易林补遗的次序是先察伏神，再推日月，日月也没有主象、伏神又被刑伤，才查互卦，互卦里也没有用神才说无救；增删卜易说日、月作了用神的，不必去找伏神，断他马上就好，伏神衰弱、用神不现，就再占一次（LY.ILL.ZS.14）；易冒说卦无用神，元神动则不死，忌神动而用神伏反吉，也必须得日月来救（LY.ILL.YM.17）；黄金策讲的是用爻不上卦要等它值日露出来（LY.ILL.HJC.39）。几家的次序不同，书里没有裁决。

- **LY.ILL.EL.11 用爻遇旬空：暴病逢空可救、久病逢空必死；虽空分衰旺——空而旺相久病无妨、空而休囚遇日冲亦不死；立时空又值旬空无不倾亡；不空而月破必伤身**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):用神落空要不要分近病久病：易林补遗分：暴病逢空可救、久病逢空必死，而且空了还要分衰旺——旺相的久病也无妨，休囚的遇日冲也不死；增删卜易的野鹤同样分近久，近病落空到冲空、实空的日子就好，久病用神落旬空月破则旺相也无法治（LY.ILL.ZS.04、ZS.05）；黄金策旧注和《海底眼》说主象落空占病大忌，要有动爻日辰冲克才有救，不分近久（LY.ILL.HJC.37）。易林补遗的「空旺相久病也无妨」和增删卜易的「久病用神空破，旺相也无法医治」在久病旺相的空爻上说法相反。
  Whether a Void Subject calls for a division between recent and long illness: the Yilin Buyi divides them: a sudden illness meeting Void can be saved and a long one surely dies, and beyond that a Void line is divided by strength — a strong one is no hindrance even in a long illness, a resting or trapped one does not die if it meets a Day clash; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise divides recent from long, a recent illness with the Subject Void being well on the day the Void is clashed or filled, while for a long illness a Subject that is Void or in Month-break cannot be treated even if strong (LY.ILL.ZS.04, ZS.05); the old commentary on the Huangjin Ce and the Haidi Yan say that a Void chief image is a great taboo in divining illness and that a moving line or a clash or control by the Day is needed for rescue, without dividing recent from long (LY.ILL.HJC.37). On a strong Void line in a long illness the Yilin Buyi ('strong Void, even a long illness is no hindrance') and the Zengshan Buyi ('long illness, Subject Void or broken, even strong it cannot be treated') say opposite things.
- **LY.ILL.EL.13 休把卦名推祸福，莫将神煞决忧祯：其中有论卦名者切不可；论神煞者亦非；用神旺则墓门煞大煞动亦不死、用神既伤则月解天医同发亦死；五行为重，神煞难凭，星家专忌本命空亡非正道**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/总则.md`):卦名、神煞能不能断病的生死：易林补遗说五行为重、神煞难凭，卦名不可论，星家专忌本命空亡不是正道；增删卜易的野鹤同样说卦名、卦身世身兼用、本命丧门、蛇虎星煞都不验，只看用神，只有卦变六冲例外（LY.ILL.ZS.01）；易冒也说四灭没、十卦、凶煞皆不死，神煞尤轻（LY.ILL.YM.11）；易隐则把黄泉杀、四灭卦、四没卦、棺椁杀列为占病逢之必死，又把三坵五墓、丧门吊客、死符丧车等列为动而临用必死的凶煞（LY.ILL.YY.05、YY.08）。前三家一边，易隐一边。
  Whether hexagram names and spirit-stars can judge life and death in illness: the Yilin Buyi says the five phases weigh most and the spirit-stars are hard to rely on, that hexagram names cannot be used, and that astrologers' special fear of the Void of the natal branch is not the right way; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise says that hexagram names, using the Body and Self together, the mourning stars on the natal branch, and the Snake and Tiger stars are all unproved, that only the Subject is looked at, with the Six-Clash changing into Six-Clash the one exception (LY.ILL.ZS.01); the Yi Mao also says the Four Extinguishing and Submerging, the ten hexagrams and the malign stars do not mean death, and that spirit-stars weigh lighter still (LY.ILL.YM.11); the Yi Yin, on the other hand, lists the Yellow Springs, the Four Extinguishing, the Four Submerging and the Coffin killers as hexagrams that mean death for one who divines illness, and lists Three Mounds and Five Tombs, Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence, Death Tally and Funeral Carriage and the like as malign stars that mean death when they move and sit on the Subject (LY.ILL.YY.05, YY.08). The first three stand on one side and the Yi Yin on the other.
- **LY.ILL.EL.14 空身空命皆非忌，无鬼无财岂足凭：甲子旬空戌亥不等于属猪属犬人皆命绝；病人无鬼必死之说不准，无鬼不过无神祟；无财只是眼下不思饮食，惟夫占妻、主占仆忌财空**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):病人卦里无鬼、无财是不是必死：易林补遗说无鬼不过是没有神祟，不等于天年命尽；无财不过眼下不思饮食，只有夫占妻、主占仆才忌财空；它批评的是《天玄赋》的「占病无鬼必无叩告之门，乃天年命尽」；易冒同样说无鬼无财、身空命空皆不死（LY.ILL.YM.11）；黄金策却说官鬼虽是恶煞不可无，不上卦要寻伏，伏又空则病必不能痊（LY.ILL.HJC.49），黄金策旧注、王洪绪对这句的范围也不一致；易隐里晁以道说用爻、命爻不上卦者死，男忌鬼空、女忌财空（LY.ILL.YY.10）；黄金策又说禄系妻财、寿属父母，财空无财则不思饮食（LY.ILL.HJC.38）。几家说法不同，书里没有裁决。
  Whether a patient's hexagram with no ghost or no Wealth means certain death: the Yilin Buyi says no ghost only lacks a haunting and does not mean the end of one's allotted years, and no Wealth only means he does not want to eat for the moment, a Void Wealth line being feared only in a husband divining a wife or a master a servant; what it criticises is the Tianxuan Fu's 'in divining illness with no ghost there is no door to knock on, it is the end of one's allotted years'; the Yi Mao likewise says that no ghost and no Wealth, Void of the Body or of the natal branch, do not mean death (LY.ILL.YM.11); the Huangjin Ce, however, says that although the Pressure line is a malign killer it cannot be absent, that when it is absent from the hexagram one must look for it hidden, and that if the hidden one is also Void the illness cannot be cured (LY.ILL.HJC.49), and the old commentary and Wang Hongxu differ on the scope of that saying; in the Yi Yin, Chao Yidao says that one whose Subject line or natal-branch line is absent from the hexagram dies, and that men fear Void ghosts and women Void Wealth (LY.ILL.YY.10); the Huangjin Ce also says that emolument belongs to Wealth and long life to the Resource line, and that a Void or absent Wealth means no appetite (LY.ILL.HJC.38). The books differ, and none gives a ruling.
- **LY.ILL.EL.43 搜决神鬼章结语：细查何象临官便议何神作祟；请祷则福无不至、祈禳则祸无不消；作此篇为却灾度命，恐人妄断而好杀费财，万不可轻言，须将六兽配五行细论方判无私**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):要不要指到某一位神、祭祀要不要杀牲：易林补遗在本章前面列了大量神名，这里的收束是「恐人妄断，恒忧好杀而费财，万不可轻言」，一样一样细论、把六兽配五行才可判，而它随后的「解禳通用法」（LY.ILL.EL.54）更是劝人不要杀牲、多用斋戒念经；易冒也在结尾说「必实指鬼神而泥之则诬矣」，并且「祭祀莫善于解禳，解禳莫善于修身」（LY.ILL.YM.63、YM.64）；增删卜易的李我平批评指定神名（LY.ILL.ZS.25），野鹤反对杀牲救命（LY.ILL.ZS.25）。这几家在收束上意见接近。
  Whether to pin it to a particular god, and whether sacrifice should involve slaughter: the Yilin Buyi lists a great many names of gods earlier in the chapter, but its closing here is 'fearing people will judge at random, always worrying that they love killing and waste money, one must by no means speak lightly', so that each point must be discussed in detail and the six beasts matched with the five phases before one may judge, and its following 'general methods of exorcism' (LY.ILL.EL.54) go further and urge people not to slaughter but to use fasting and the recitation of scriptures; the Yi Mao also closes with 'to point insistently at a particular ghost or spirit and cling to it is to make a false claim' and 'of sacrifice none is better than exorcism, and of exorcism none is better than cultivating oneself' (LY.ILL.YM.63, YM.64); Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi criticises pinning names (LY.ILL.ZS.25), and the Old Man of Yehe opposes slaughter to save a life (LY.ILL.ZS.25). These books are close in their closing views.
- **LY.ILL.EL.48 总论星煞值鬼诀(一)：看神司先推官鬼（属阳男伤、属阴女鬼），再看临何星何煞；青龙带天喜（正月起戌顺行十二位）是花煞之神；白虎带丧门（正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑循环）是丧煞；朱雀带天火（子卯午酉循环）天烛（正月起巳顺行至辰）是五显灵官、灶神；玄武官爻带天贼（按十二属相）或天盗（亥寅巳申循环）是草野三郎**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):丧门、天喜怎么排，各书不同。①丧门：易林补遗（LY.ILL.EL.48）是正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑，四个位置周而复始；断易天机的注释引鬼谷子的杀例，同样是正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑，并说「只此四位，轮十二月」；火珠林（卜筮大全里抄了同一段）的丧门杀例是正月未、二月辰、三月丑、四月戌，也说「只此四位轮十二月」——四个位置一样，起点差一位。②天喜：易林补遗是正月从戌起、顺行十二位（戌、亥、子、丑、寅、卯、辰、巳、午、未、申、酉），周易尚占的逐月吉凶神杀表也是这样；卜筮大全的神煞篇是「春戌夏丑为天喜，秋辰冬未」，按季不按月。这几本书都没有说谁对谁错，按哪一家排，同一个月里的位置会不同。
  How Mourning Gate and Heavenly Joy are set differs from book to book. (1) Mourning Gate: the Yilin Buyi (LY.ILL.EL.48) has the first month Xu, the second Wei, the third Chen, the fourth Chou, the four positions cycling; the commentary in the Duanyi Tianji quotes the rule of the killings of Guiguzi, which is likewise the first month Xu, the second Wei, the third Chen, the fourth Chou, and says 'only these four positions, rotating through twelve months'; the rule of the Mourning Gate killing in the Huozhulin (whose same passage is copied in the Buyi Daquan) is the first month Wei, the second Chen, the third Chou, the fourth Xu, and also says 'only these four positions rotating through twelve months': the same four positions, with the starting point one place off. (2) Heavenly Joy: the Yilin Buyi has the first month starting from Xu and running forward through twelve places (Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You), and the Zhouyi Shangzhan's monthly table of lucky and unlucky stars agrees; the chapter on killings in the Buyi Daquan says 'spring Xu, summer Chou, autumn Chen, winter Wei is Heavenly Joy', by season and not by month. None of these books says which is right, and which one is followed changes the position in the same month.
- **LY.ILL.EL.50 总论星煞值鬼诀(三)：朱雀官鬼值官符（正月从午顺行十二位）是总管作祟；鬼带刀砧（正月从午顺行十二位）或逢羊刃（甲卯乙辰丙戊午丁己未庚酉辛戌壬子癸丑）是伤司；鬼值太岁带黄旌（正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑循环）是后土（土皇）；官临贵人（甲戊庚牛羊、乙己鼠猴、丙丁猪鸡、壬癸蛇兔、六辛马虎）或天喜是萧堂五圣**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):天乙贵人和羊刃怎么定，各书不同。①天乙贵人：易林补遗（LY.ILL.EL.50）是「甲戊庚牛羊，乙己鼠猴乡，丙丁猪鸡位，壬癸蛇兔藏，六辛逢马虎」，庚日的贵人在牛羊（丑未）；卜筮大全的神煞篇是「甲戊兼牛羊，乙己鼠猴乡，丙丁猪鸡位，壬癸兔蛇藏，庚辛逢马虎」，庚日和辛日一样在马虎（午寅）；周易尚占的逐日表里庚日的贵人也是午。②羊刃：易林补遗是每个日干都取「禄前一位」（甲卯、乙辰、丙戊午、丁己未、庚酉、辛戌、壬子、癸丑）；易冒讲诸星一章的注明说阳干顺行、阴干逆行（甲禄寅取卯、乙禄卯取寅、丙戊禄巳取午、丁己禄午取巳，「乙逢寅动当为刃，辛见申摇岂不凶，丁己忌巳癸忧亥」），并批评「近世不分阴阳顺逆，概以禄前一位为刃，至有误认乙禄到卯、辰为羊刃之说」——易林补遗的表正是这种一律取禄前一位的排法（乙日在辰），易冒没有点名。两边差在乙、丁、己、辛、癸五个阴干上，书里没有裁决。
  How the Tianyi Noble and the Goat Blade are fixed differs from book to book. (1) Tianyi Noble: the Yilin Buyi (LY.ILL.EL.50) has 'Jia, Wu and Geng the ox and sheep; Yi and Ji the land of the rat and monkey; Bing and Ding the places of the pig and rooster; Ren and Gui the snake and hare lie hidden; the six Xin meet the horse and tiger', so that the Noble of a Geng day is at the ox and sheep (Chou, Wei); the chapter on killings in the Buyi Daquan has 'Jia and Wu together the ox and sheep; Yi and Ji the land of the rat and monkey; Bing and Ding the places of the pig and rooster; Ren and Gui the hare and snake lie hidden; Geng and Xin meet the horse and tiger', so that a Geng day is, like a Xin day, at the horse and tiger (Wu, Yin); in the table by day in the Zhouyi Shangzhan the Noble of a Geng day is also Wu. (2) Goat Blade: the Yilin Buyi takes, for every day stem, 'the place one step ahead of the Salary' (Jia Mao, Yi Chen, Bing and Wu Wu, Ding and Ji Wei, Geng You, Xin Xu, Ren Zi, Gui Chou); the note of the Yi Mao in its chapter on the stars says plainly that yang stems go forward and yin stems go backward (Jia with its Salary at Yin takes Mao, Yi with its Salary at Mao takes Yin, Bing and Wu with the Salary at Si take Wu, Ding and Ji with the Salary at Wu take Si, 'Yi meeting Yin moving is surely the blade, Xin seeing Shen shake is not ominous, Ding and Ji fear Si and Gui worries about Hai'), and it criticises that 'in recent times people do not tell yang from yin, forward from backward, and take in every case the place one step ahead of the Salary as the blade, even to the mistaken saying that Yi with its Salary at Mao has Chen as the Goat Blade': the Yilin Buyi's table is exactly this way of taking the place one step ahead of the Salary in every case (Yi on Chen), though the Yi Mao does not name it. The two differ on the five yin stems Yi, Ding, Ji, Xin and Gui, and the books do not settle it.
- **LY.ILL.HDY.02 鬼在内外夜重夜轻、内外有鬼旧病再发：鬼在内夜重、鬼在外夜必轻、内外有鬼人昏困要防旧病再来；代占最怕应持鬼、官鬼持身命亦倾；病因怕鬼旺相、子孙不宜克世、财忌飞克、父母交重病沉、大怕世空、又嫌月破、更嫌身世入墓**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/轻重.md`):病在昼夜哪一头重，几家的依据不同。海底眼（LY.ILL.HDY.02）、黄金策的旧注和王洪绪（LY.ILL.HJC.12）都按官鬼在内卦还是外卦来分：鬼在内卦夜里重，鬼在外卦白天重（海底眼说「夜必轻」），内外都有鬼昼夜都不轻；易隐（LY.ILL.YY.15）则按官鬼的阴阳分：鬼属阴白天轻、夜里重，鬼属阳白天重、夜里轻。两个标准可以给出相反的结论（比如阳爻的鬼在内卦：前一种夜里重，后一种白天重），书里没有裁决。
  Whether an illness is heavier by day or by night: the books use different criteria. The Haidi Yan (LY.ILL.HDY.02), the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu (LY.ILL.HJC.12) all divide by whether the Pressure line is in the inner or the outer trigram: a ghost in the inner trigram is heavy at night, a ghost in the outer trigram heavy by day (the Haidi Yan says 'at night it is surely lighter'), and with ghosts both inside and outside neither day nor night is light; the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.15) divides by the yin or yang of the ghost: a yin ghost is light by day and heavy at night, a yang ghost heavy by day and light at night. The two criteria can give opposite conclusions (for example, a yang ghost in the inner trigram is heavy at night by the first and heavy by day by the second), and the books do not settle it.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.15 上冲下下冲上，内外感伤：上下有鬼内外两感；内鬼动冲外鬼先伤后感，外鬼动冲内鬼先感后伤**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/症候.md`):两鬼互冲的结果，旧注和王洪绪相反。旧注说「适感而遂伤也」，刚感受了邪就受了伤；卜筮正宗写「适感而适愈也」，刚感受就好了。书里没有再解释，也没有别家的话可对。
  The two disagree on what follows when two Pressure lines clash each other. The old commentary says 'hardly is the evil felt than harm is done', while the Bushi Zhengzong writes 'hardly is it felt than it is cured'. The book explains no further and there is no other voice to check against.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.17 水官化土回头克、落在内卦是小便不通：属阴则大便也不通，阴阳错配二便俱闭；内卦水克加白虎，阳爻尿血、阴爻泻血，带刑是痔漏**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/症候.md`):水官化土在哪里才是小便不通：旧注说在「本宫内卦」（下三爻），王洪绪说在「本宫初爻」（最下面一爻）。范围一个宽一个窄，书里没有说哪个对。
  Where a Water Pressure line changing into Earth means blocked urine: the old commentary says in the 'inner trigram of the home palace' (the lower three lines), Wang Hongxu says on the 'first line of the home palace' (the lowest line). One range is wide and one narrow, and the book does not say which is right.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.25 应爻带官鬼刑克合世：是探望亲友的病时缠染上的，鬼属土是时疫，用爻临应则病卧他家**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/病因.md`):被应鬼刑克合的是哪一爻：旧注说是世爻，而且世爻要在发动的位置上；王洪绪说是用爻，没有发动这个条件。
  Which line the Pressure on the Other line punishes, controls or combines with: the old commentary says the Self line, which must also be in a moving position; Wang Hongxu says the Subject line, without the condition of moving.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.33 次看主象（就是用神）：怕遇克害刑冲；占夫以官为用神、占妻以财为用神；旧注另说自占以世为主、代占以应为主；得生扶必不至死**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/总则.md`):替别人占病时取哪一爻做主象：旧注在六亲取用（占夫看官、占妻看财）之外，又说「代占以应为主」；王洪绪只讲六亲取用，没有把应爻当主象。
  Which line is the main image in a divination on behalf of another: besides taking the relation (Pressure for a husband, Wealth for a wife) the old commentary also says 'in a divination for another the Other line is the main image'; Wang Hongxu speaks only of taking the relation and does not make the Other line the main image.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.34 世持鬼爻病纵轻而难疗：鬼持世必难脱体，大象纵轻也不易愈；用爻有气、子孙当权则眼前可医、日后必不断根**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/轻重.md`):是「占病」还是「占自病」：旧注说占病怕鬼持世，泛指所有的病；王洪绪限定成占自己的病（自己占自己的病）。
  Whether it is 'illness' or 'one's own illness': the old commentary says that in divining illness Pressure holding the Self line is feared, taking in every illness; Wang Hongxu limits it to divining one's own illness.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.35 身临福德势虽险而堪医：「身」是一卦之体（旧注说是用爻，王洪绪说是月卦身），子孙临之决然无虞，纵然凶险用药必愈**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):「身」指哪一爻：旧注这里说用爻就是身；王洪绪说是月卦身（按月起的卦身）。这一章的旧注在 LY.ILL.HJC.26 里另外讲「卦身持鬼」，把卦身当成另一样东西，所以旧注自己在这个字上也不一致。
  Which line the 'body' is: the old commentary here says the Subject line is the body; Wang Hongxu says it is the month's hexagram Body (the Body set by the month). In LY.ILL.HJC.26 the old commentary of this chapter speaks separately of 'a Pressure line on the hexagram Body' and treats the hexagram Body as something else, so the old commentary is itself not consistent about this word.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.36 用壮有扶切恐太刚则折：占病不喜旺相，人在病中必衰弱，得时有气又遇生扶反是太刚则折，到生旺日必凶；有日辰动爻刑克则不嫌其旺**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/轻重.md`):什么样的旺才是「太刚则折」：旧注说凡是得时有气、又遇日辰动爻生扶的都是；王洪绪的条件更窄：用神要临月建，又得日辰生扶拱合，再遇动爻生扶，最怕原神也值月建。
  What sort of strength is 'too rigid, it snaps': the old commentary says whatever is in season and has force and also meets generation or support from the Day or a moving line; Wang Hongxu's condition is narrower: the Subject must sit on the Month, get generation, support, embracing or combining from the Day, and then meet generation or support from a moving line, and most feared is the Support also ruling the Month.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.37 主空无救须防中道而殂：主象落空占病大忌，要有动爻日辰冲克才有救（冲空则实、克空为用）；王洪绪说不独指空，墓绝空破有救者无妨、无救者必死，救指生扶拱合**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):怎样才算「有救」：旧注说主象落空，要动爻、日辰来冲它、克它才有救（冲空则实，克空为用），不然必死；王洪绪说不光是落空，入墓、绝、被破也算，救是生、扶、拱、合。一个用冲克把它叫起来，一个用生扶合去帮它。
  What counts as 'rescue': the old commentary says a Void main image is rescued only when a moving line or the Day clashes or controls it (clashing a Void line makes it real, controlling a Void line puts it to use), otherwise he surely dies; Wang Hongxu says the line speaks not of Void alone but of tomb, extinction and being broken as well, and rescue is generating, supporting, embracing and combining. One wakes the line by clash and control, the other helps it by generation, support and combination.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.44 用连鬼杀目前必见倾危：旧注是用爻发动又化出鬼爻（病人变鬼），无子孙日辰解救必死；王洪绪说「连」当作「变」，鬼煞是忌神，指用神化回头克，有日月动爻解救则不危，并驳「用神变官鬼必死」**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):「用连鬼杀」是什么意思：旧注读作用爻发动、又变出官鬼，叫病人变成鬼，没有子孙、日辰解救就一定死；王洪绪说「连」字应当读作「变」，「鬼煞」是忌神、不是官鬼，句子是说用神变出回头克，没有日月、动爻解救，眼前才危险，并且明说术家「用神变官鬼必死」的断法是拿字面害了本意。
  What 'the Subject joined to the Pressure killer' means: the old commentary reads it as the Subject line moving and also changing out a Pressure line, called the patient turning into a ghost, and with no Output or Day to rescue him he surely dies; Wang Hongxu says the character 'lian' should be read as 'change', that the 'Pressure killer' is the Adversary and not the Pressure line, that the line means the Subject changing into a returning control, and only with no Month, Day or moving line to relieve it is the danger before one's eyes; and he says outright that the practitioners' judgment 'the Subject changing into Pressure is surely fatal' lets the words hurt the meaning.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.49 鬼伏空亡早备衣冠防不测：官鬼是恶杀却不可无（得病根由、虚实、安危、鬼神情状都决于它），不上卦要寻伏，伏爻又空则病必不能痊；王洪绪说这两句只指占父母、官府、丈夫的病**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):这两句用在哪些病上：旧注说官鬼是一切病的根由，不能没有，伏而又空则病必不能痊（病轻的除外）；王洪绪说这两句只讲父母、官府、丈夫的病，遇到官爻伏而又空才要提防不测。
  Which illnesses these two lines apply to: the old commentary says the Pressure line is the root of every illness and cannot be missing, and if it is hidden and also Void the illness surely cannot be cured (a light illness excepted); Wang Hongxu says the two lines speak only of the illness of a parent, an official or a husband, and only when the Pressure line is hidden and Void must one beware of the unforeseen.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.50 日辰带鬼急为祈祷：日辰带鬼生合世爻，看世爻带的是哪一亲就知道该用什么治（父母经箓符醮、子孙用药、兄弟戒怒节食、财静养、官鬼祈祷）；认哪路神鬼，旧注按日辰地支、王洪绪按生合的六神**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):怎样认该求哪一路神鬼：旧注用日辰的地支推（子是北斗或北阴，亥是溺死鬼，要察看是河伯、还是划龙船的），再按世爻带的六亲定治法；王洪绪看生合世爻或用爻的那一爻带的六神（青龙、勾陈、朱雀、螣蛇、白虎、玄武各是一路），并且说阳象阳爻是神、阴象阴爻是鬼。
  How to tell which spirit or ghost to seek: the old commentary reasons from the branch of the Day (Zi is the Northern Dipper or the North Yin, Hai a drowned ghost, to be looked into as the River Earl or dragon-boat rowing) and fixes the cure by the relation the Self line carries; Wang Hongxu looks at the six spirits carried by the line that generates or combines with the Self or Subject line (the Azure Dragon, Hook Earth, Vermilion Bird, Coiling Snake, White Tiger and Dark Tortoise each a different sort), and says that a yang image on a yang line is a god and a yin image on a yin line a ghost.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.53 身上飞伏双官膏肓之疾：卦身持鬼、本宫官鬼又伏世爻或用爻之下、世爻飞伏上下皆官鬼，或鬼夹在用爻卦身前后，叫双官夹身，大象不死也是沉困考终之疾**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):「身」指什么、哪些情形算双官夹身：旧注讲三种（卦身持鬼而官鬼伏世下，世持鬼而官鬼伏用爻下，自占世爻飞伏上下皆鬼）；王洪绪先说「身」是卦身和用神，又把鬼夹在身或用爻前后、世爻和身爻都有鬼也算进来，比旧注宽。
  What the 'body' is and which cases count as double Pressure clamping the body: the old commentary gives three (the hexagram Body holds Pressure while Pressure is hidden under the Self line; the Self line holds Pressure while Pressure is hidden under the Subject line; in a divination for oneself the Self line flying and hidden has Pressure above and below); Wang Hongxu first says the 'body' is the hexagram Body and the Subject, and also counts Pressure flanking the body or the Subject before and after, and Pressure on both the Self line and the body line, which is wider than the old commentary.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.54 命入幽明两墓泉世之人：身、命、世随鬼入墓，有卦墓、鬼墓、世墓、主墓；动出在卦中的是明墓、变入的是幽墓，不拘幽明皆非吉兆；大象凶又逢墓动、或世爻主象俱入墓者必死，动爻日辰冲破墓爻则无事**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):墓有几种、什么情形必死：旧注列四种墓（卦墓、鬼墓、世墓、主墓，「以日时看」），必死的条件是大象凶又逢墓动、或世爻主象俱入墓；王洪绪列三种（鬼墓、世墓、主墓，分别从卦、世爻、用神看），必死的条件是世爻用爻被官鬼两头夹着、或有两重鬼墓夹身。
  How many kinds of tomb there are and what makes death certain: the old commentary lists four (the tomb of the hexagram, of Pressure, of the Self and of the main image, 'viewing by the day and hour') and makes death certain when the whole image is bad and a tomb moves, or when the Self line and the main image both enter a tomb; Wang Hongxu lists three (the tombs of Pressure, of the Self and of the main image, seen from the hexagram, the Self line and the Subject) and makes death certain when the Self or Subject line is clamped by Pressure lines on both sides or two layers of Pressure tombs clamp the body.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.55 应合而变财伤勿食馈来之物：应爻动来生合世爻是有人问安，带财福必有馈送、兄弟只是清访；若应生合而变出妻财反来刑克，馈送切须戒食否则反受伤害，占长辈尤宜忌**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/风险.md`):变出妻财的是哪一爻：旧注是应爻变出妻财、反过来刑克世爻（另有应爻生合世爻却刑克主象的一种）；王洪绪是用爻自己变出妻财，或者有财爻刑冲克害用神。

- **LY.ILL.HJC.57 欲决病痊当究福神之动静、要知命尽须详鬼杀之旺衰：旧注按子孙、扶持之爻、所喜之爻、制伏恶杀之爻断痊愈之期，按鬼爻墓绝日或用爻生旺日断死期；王洪绪说不可以词害义，福神指原神、鬼煞指忌神**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/应期.md`):「福神」「鬼杀」指哪一爻：旧注按字面，福神是子孙、鬼杀是官鬼和忌爻，并且定出按哪一爻断痊愈的日子、按哪一天断死期；王洪绪说不可以拿字面害了本意，福神比子孙轻、比原神重，鬼煞的道理在忌神、不在官鬼：原神旺动，用神空破伏藏也能好；忌神旺动，用神好好出现也要命尽。

- **LY.ILL.HJC.64 官化官病变不一：医药卦中官鬼化官鬼病必有更变，症候不一、病势不定，用药不见效；王洪绪说是官爻化进神，化退神反此断**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):「官化官」指什么变化：旧注泛指官鬼变官鬼，病必有更变；王洪绪限定成官爻化进神（症候不一、病势不定），化退神则反过来断。

- **LY.ILL.HJC.65 子化子药杂不精：子孙是占药的用神，只喜一位出现、有气不空、日辰动爻扶持；卦中重叠太过或子孙重化子孙是医不精、用药杂而无功；王洪绪读作子孙化进神有效、化退神与伏吟卦不可服**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):「子化子」是好是坏：旧注说子孙重叠、或子孙又变子孙，是医生不精、用药杂；王洪绪说子孙变进神药有效，变退神和伏吟卦不可服，没有把子化子当成坏事。
  Whether 'Output changing into Output' is good or bad: the old commentary says that Output doubled, or Output changing again into Output, means an unskilled doctor and mixed medicines; Wang Hongxu says that with Output changing into an Advancing spirit the medicine is effective, and with a Retreating spirit or in a Hidden Chant hexagram it may not be taken, and he does not treat Output changing into Output as a bad thing.
- **LY.ILL.HJC.69 应爻所临定医人是谁：持父母是僧道、兄弟是庸医、财爻是富贵医人或女医、子孙是专门医士，持太岁是世医，持日辰而被太岁刑克是初学，持月建是官医，更临子孙用药神效**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):持日辰的医生是哪一种：旧注说持日辰而被太岁刑克的是刚学医的，持月建的是官医；王洪绪说持月建、日辰的一律是官医，没有「刚学医」这一档。
  What sort of doctor one who holds the Day is: the old commentary says one who holds the Day and is punished or controlled by Tai Sui is a beginner, and one who holds the Month is an official physician; Wang Hongxu says those who hold the Month or the Day are all official physicians and has no class of 'beginner'.

  Whether meeting the ill-omened hexagrams and stars means death: the Yi Mao says that the Four Extinguishing and Submerging hexagrams, the ten hexagrams and the malign stars (Three Mounds and Five Tombs, the Funeral Carriage) do not mean death, that death rests only on whether the Subject lives or dies, and that the spirit-stars weigh lighter still; the Yi Yin, on the other hand, lists the Yellow Springs, the Four Extinguishing, the Four Submerging and the Coffin killers as hexagrams that mean death for one who divines illness, and lists Three Mounds and Five Tombs, Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence, Death Tally and Funeral Carriage and the like as malign stars that mean death when they move and sit on or control the Subject (LY.ILL.YY.05 and LY.ILL.YY.08). The same hexagram names and stars, and the two books' conclusions are opposite; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise says hexagram names, the Body and the Snake, Tiger and other stars are not reliable and only the Subject counts (LY.ILL.ZS.01), and stands with the Yi Mao.
- **LY.ILL.YM.21 用神之法：主虽少用父母、仆虽老用妻财、宗按尊卑（称叔用父称侄用子）、表姓用应（岳父母舅父母用父）、现任用官、师傅用父**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/他人病.md`):家里的主人是用哪一爻：易冒说主人即使年纪小，也用父母爻，占仆人用妻财爻（作者自序里也说「老奴占幼主必用父母，少君占衰仆必用妻财」）；易隐说奴仆丁口占家主用五爻，占主母用二爻（LY.ILL.YY.03）。两本书说的都是仆人和主人这一对关系，取的爻却不同。易冒对君臣另有一条：臣占君用九五（LY.ILL.YM.23），所以在易冒里，九五只给君主、不给家主。
  Which line stands for the master of a household: the Yi Mao says a master, however young, takes the Resource line, and a servant takes the Wealth line (the author's own preface says the same: 'an old servant divining a young master must take the Resource, a young lord divining a feeble servant must take the Wealth'); the Yi Yin says servants and household hands divining the master of the house take the fifth line, and divining the mistress take the second line (LY.ILL.YY.03). Both speak of the same pair, servant and master, but take different lines. The Yi Mao has a separate rule for ruler and subject: a minister divining the sovereign takes the ninth-five line (LY.ILL.YM.23), so in the Yi Mao the ninth-five line is for the sovereign only, not for the master of a household.
- **LY.ILL.YM.37 占药占医取象：水子旺宜汤、土子旺宜丸、火灸金针木末；火鬼喜凉药、木鬼喜风药、土脾金肺水肾；占医考子孙之属，木东金西乾僧坤妇——注：子为药鬼为病，象只用来问何药何人，问吉凶仍看子孙衰旺**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):用药要不要按五行来配：易冒把「水子旺宜汤、土子旺宜丸、火灸金针木末」「火鬼喜凉药、木鬼喜风药」当作象，但注马上限定，象只用来问「用何药、是何病」，问吉凶还是看子孙衰旺，不要认死「子孙属土就用丸药」；黄金策旧注、王洪绪和易隐把五行当作用药的依据（火鬼旺用大寒药、水鬼旺用大热药、金鬼宜灸、木鬼宜针，见 LY.ILL.HJC.76、HJC.81、HJC.84、YY.25）；增删卜易的李我平则批评这类说法拘泥，说子孙不是针灸之药、火鬼旺不一定该用大寒药（LY.ILL.ZS.40）。易冒站在中间：象可以用，但不作凭据。
  Whether medicine should be matched by the phases: the Yi Mao takes 'a strong Water Output suits a decoction, an Earth Output pills, Fire moxibustion, Metal needles, Wood powders' and 'a Fire ghost likes cooling medicine, a Wood ghost wind medicine' as images, but the note at once limits them: images are used only for 'which medicine to use and what the illness is', while for whether it is auspicious one still reads the strength of Output and should not hold rigidly that 'an Earth Output means pills'; the old commentary and Wang Hongxu on the Huangjin Ce and the Yi Yin take the phases as the ground for the choice of medicine (a strong Fire ghost takes a very cold medicine, a strong Water ghost a very hot one, a Metal ghost moxibustion, a Wood ghost needles; see LY.ILL.HJC.76, HJC.81, HJC.84, YY.25); Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi criticises such sayings as rigid, saying Output is not a medicine for needles and moxibustion and that a strong Fire ghost does not necessarily call for a very cold medicine (LY.ILL.ZS.40). The Yi Mao stands in the middle: the images may be used, but not as the ground.
- **LY.ILL.YM.43 官鬼属阳则阳神、属阴则阴神：阴神如王母、大悲、水母、太山、天妃、蚕母、子母、催生、太君娘娘之属，要因地（同神异名）、因神（男女）、因事（问蚕得阴官乃蚕母）而考**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):阴阳是分「神」和「鬼」，还是分男神女神：易冒说官鬼属阳是阳神、属阴是阴神，举的阴神是王母、天妃、蚕母这些女神，阴的仍是神，不是鬼；易隐说「阳鬼为神、阴官为鬼」，卜筮正宗的王洪绪说「阳为神为男，阴为鬼为女」（LY.ILL.YY.27、LY.ILL.HJC.94），阴就是鬼；黄金策旧注和增删卜易的觉子则按旺衰分，旺相是神、休囚是鬼（LY.ILL.HJC.94、LY.ILL.ZS.23）。易冒在这里分的是阳神和阴神，神与鬼另在后面「生前之性」几段里分（LY.ILL.YM.49 起）。
  Whether yin and yang divide 'spirit' from 'ghost' or male deity from female: the Yi Mao says a yang Pressure line is a yang spirit and a yin one a yin spirit, and its yin examples are goddesses such as the Queen Mother, the Heavenly Consort and the Silkworm Mother, so yin is still a spirit and not a ghost; the Yi Yin says 'a yang ghost is a spirit, a yin Pressure is a ghost', and Wang Hongxu of the Bushi Zhengzong says 'yang is a spirit and male, yin is a ghost and female' (LY.ILL.YY.27, LY.ILL.HJC.94), so yin is a ghost; the old commentary on the Huangjin Ce and Jue Zi of the Zengshan Buyi divide by strength, strong being a spirit and resting or trapped a ghost (LY.ILL.HJC.94, LY.ILL.ZS.23). The Yi Mao here divides yang spirits from yin spirits, and divides spirit from ghost later, in the paragraphs on how each was in life (from LY.ILL.YM.49).
- **LY.ILL.YM.44 官鬼属金则武神：天将、关帝、伍公、岳王、玄坛、总官、七煞、金刚、伤司、丧煞之属，及称元帅将军金甲神、威武斗勇成神者**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):要不要把金鬼指成关帝、岳王这样的具体某位神：易冒举天将、关帝、伍公、岳王等作例子，但同一章的末尾说「必实指鬼神而泥之则诬」（LY.ILL.YM.63）；增删卜易的李我平点名批评《易冒》指定关岳（LY.ILL.ZS.25），觉子也认为金官指为关公岳公等武神不对，武神有封有未封、各地不同，要问病人许愿未酬的是哪座庙（LY.ILL.ZS.24）。易冒自己也只把这些当作例子，不当作定论。
  Whether a Metal ghost should be pinned to a particular god such as Emperor Guan or Prince Yue: the Yi Mao gives the Heavenly Generals, Emperor Guan, Lord Wu, Prince Yue and the rest as examples, but at the end of the same chapter says 'to point insistently at a particular ghost or spirit and cling to it is to make a false claim' (LY.ILL.YM.63); Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi names the Yi Mao in criticising the pinning to Guan and Yue (LY.ILL.ZS.25), and Jue Zi also holds that calling a Metal Pressure line Guan Gong or Yue Gong is wrong, since martial spirits are enfeoffed or not and differ from place to place, and one should ask which temple the patient vowed to without redeeming (LY.ILL.ZS.24). The Yi Mao itself takes these only as examples, not as conclusions.
- **LY.ILL.YM.57 官鬼独发按八宫断神鬼：乾天神寺庙父老僧人王侯之灵；坤地祗坟墓母妪后妃；震九天之神蚕桑舟车辱身折足鬼雷火部；巽风波镇海花果园林长妇女；坎北斗北极百川四海；离荧惑香火灶中女；艮山神五岳荒塚童儿；兑释之宗咒咀尸祝师尼酒色**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):八宫各配什么鬼神，黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.95）和易冒大体同路，细目不同：乾都是天神一路；坤都有坟墓、地祇；坎、离都是水火之神。不同的地方——艮，黄金策是城隍、宅土，易冒是山神、五岳、荒坟、童子之鬼；巽，黄金策是缢死或颠仆的阴人，易冒是掌管风浪镇海的神、花果园林之祟、长女妇人之魄；兑，黄金策是口愿、伤神，易冒是佛家宗主、咒诅、巫祝之诳、僧尼之鬼、酒色之邪；震，黄金策是树神或杖死的男鬼，易冒是九天之神、蚕桑舟车之司、辱身折足之鬼、雷火部的威灵。两本书都没有说谁对谁错。
  What each palace stands for among ghosts and spirits: the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.95) and the Yi Mao run roughly the same way, with differences of detail: in Qian both have the heavenly spirits; in Kun both have graves and the earthly deity; in Kan and Li both have the spirits of water and fire. Where they differ: for Gen, the Huangjin Ce has the City God and the earth of the house, the Yi Mao the mountain spirit, the Five Peaks, a wild grave and the ghost of a boy; for Xun, the Huangjin Ce has a woman who hanged herself or fell in a fit, the Yi Mao a spirit in charge of wind and waves and pacifying the sea, the haunting of flowers, fruit, gardens and groves, and the soul of an eldest daughter or a woman; for Dui, the Huangjin Ce has a spoken vow or an injuring spirit, the Yi Mao the head of the Buddhists, curses, the deceit of impersonators and invokers, the ghost of a monk or nun and the evil of wine and lust; for Zhen, the Huangjin Ce has a tree spirit or a male ghost beaten to death with a staff, the Yi Mao a spirit of the Nine Heavens, one in charge of silkworms, boats and carts, the ghost of one disgraced or with a broken leg, and the awesome spirit of the Thunder and Fire department. Neither book says which is right.
- **LY.ILL.YM.63 察鬼神但以五行八宫变化而像之，必实指鬼神而泥之则诬：四穹之内无处非鬼神，各乡所尊不同，千古之上未有之神何所知，千古之下再成之神何所拟**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):要不要落实到某一位神：易冒在本章前面（LY.ILL.YM.44 到 YM.62）列了大量神名，这里却说「必实指鬼神而泥之，则诬矣」，前后的关系是：名单是举例，办法是用五行八宫去取象。增删卜易的觉子和李我平批评的是把名单当成定论，即金官就指关公岳公（LY.ILL.ZS.24、ZS.25），而且李我平点名批评了《易冒》指定关岳、并说本书疾病章讲鬼神是「从俗」；从这一段看，易冒自己在这里已经说了不能泥于某一位。黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.93 到 HJC.104）和易隐（LY.ILL.YY.28）则直接给出神名和鬼的死因，没有这样的收束。
  Whether to pin it to a particular god: the Yi Mao lists a large number of names earlier in the chapter (LY.ILL.YM.44 to YM.62), yet here says 'to point insistently at a particular ghost or spirit and cling to it is to make a false claim'; the relation between the two is that the lists are examples and the method is to take the image by the five phases and the eight palaces. What Jue Zi and Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi criticise is taking the list as a conclusion, that is, naming Guan Gong or Yue Gong at once for a Metal Pressure line (LY.ILL.ZS.24, ZS.25), and Li Woping names the Yi Mao for pinning Guan and Yue and says its talk of ghosts and spirits in the illness chapter 'follows the common custom'; from this passage the Yi Mao itself already says here that one must not cling to a particular one. The Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.93 to HJC.104) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.28) give names of gods and the causes of death of ghosts directly, without such a closing qualification.
- **LY.ILL.YM.64 神多为祐、鬼多为祟，灾病多因自孽：不洗心悔过而聚巫陈牲媚鬼神，鬼神未必享；炰杀生命以祈己命，益重其孽；祭祀莫善于解禳，解禳莫善于修身**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):祭祀送鬼有没有用：易冒说灾病多因自孽，不修身而只靠巫祝牲牢献媚鬼神，鬼神未必享用，杀生祈命更是加重罪孽，祭祀最好不过解禳、解禳最好不过修身；增删卜易的野鹤同样反对靠杀牲救命（LY.ILL.ZS.25），但承认时灾瘟癖照法祭送屡验；黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.93、HJC.50）和易隐（LY.ILL.YY.27、YY.31）则把祈禳的对象、祭品、方法一项项讲出来，把祭祀当作要认真做的事。三家不是互相否定，重点不同：易冒重修身，野鹤按病分，黄金策和易隐重办法。
  Whether sacrifice and sending away ghosts is of use: the Yi Mao says calamity and illness mostly come from one's own doing, that without cultivating oneself, currying favour with ghosts and spirits by means of shamans and animal victims is not necessarily partaken of, that slaughtering to pray for life makes the fault heavier, and that of sacrifice none is better than exorcism and of exorcism none is better than cultivating oneself; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise opposes slaughter to save a life (LY.ILL.ZS.25), but admits that sacrifice and sending away by the method has been proved many times for seasonal calamities and abdominal masses; the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.93, HJC.50) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.27, YY.31) set out object, offering and method of prayer and exorcism item by item and treat sacrifice as something to be done with care. The three do not deny one another; their emphasis differs: the Yi Mao stresses self-cultivation, the Old Man of Yehe distinguishes by illness, the Huangjin Ce and the Yi Yin stress procedure.
- **LY.ILL.YY.03 占病取用爻：自占以世爻、身爻、命爻为用，代占以应爻为用；占家亲祖妣用官鬼、父兄妻子各以所属；五属不上卦从《黄金策》分爻取用；奴占家主用五爻、占主母用二爻**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/总则.md`):代占取哪一爻：易隐说代占以应爻为用，但占家里的亲人又各用所属的六亲；黄金策的旧注和王洪绪对这一点也不一致，旧注说自占以世为主、代占以应为主，王洪绪说占夫用官、占妻用财、按所占的人取（见 LY.ILL.HJC.33）。增删卜易则按所占的人取用神，不取应爻。
  Which line to take in divining for another: the Yi Yin says the Other line is the Subject in divining for another, yet for kin of the household it takes each relation as its own; the old commentary and Wang Hongxu on the Huangjin Ce also disagree on this, the old commentary saying the Self is the master in divining for oneself and the Other in divining for another, and Wang Hongxu taking Pressure for a husband and Wealth for a wife, by the person divined (see LY.ILL.HJC.33). The Zengshan Buyi takes the Subject by the person divined and does not take the Other line.
- **LY.ILL.YY.12 按爻位分身体：初爻足、二爻股膝、三爻腹小腹腰臀肛门小便、四爻胸胃乳、五爻面项手（水口火目土鼻水左耳金右耳）、六爻头脑；遇鬼伏、鬼化、鬼动来伤之处必带疾**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/症候.md`):左耳属哪一行：同一段里，上一条（LY.ILL.YY.11）说木是筋骨、左耳，这里说「水左耳，金右耳」，两处对左耳的五行不一致；书里没有说明，应是传抄的差异。易隐别处（第 1447 行讲相貌处）是「木聋左耳，金虎右耳」，与这里说水的一处不同。
  Which element the left ear belongs to: within the same passage, the previous entry (LY.ILL.YY.11) says Wood is the sinews and bones and the left ear, and here it says 'Water the left ear, Metal the right ear', so the two disagree on the left ear's element; the book gives no explanation and it is likely a difference in transmission. Elsewhere the Yi Yin (line 1447, on appearance) has 'Wood deaf in the left ear, Metal and Tiger the right ear', which differs from this one place that says Water.
- **LY.ILL.YY.18 病因·鬼伏六亲下或六亲化鬼：伏父或父动化鬼忧心得病、衣服寒暖失时；伏兄或兄动化鬼赌博争财；伏财或财动化鬼饮食不节，财在阳宫动主吐、阴宫动主泻、加土动吐、加水动泻；伏子或子动化鬼外情牵引酒色（加龙嗜酒、加武好色）；子爻空绝调理无资；间爻鬼动胸膈不宽**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/病因.md`):财爻动，吐还是泻看什么：易隐说财在阳宫动主吐、在阴宫动主泻，又说财带土动吐、财带水动泻；黄金策说财爻动在上卦（外卦）主吐、动在下卦（内卦）主泻（LY.ILL.HJC.30）。一个看阴阳宫，一个看上下卦，两个分法不一样；书里没有裁决。
  What decides vomiting or diarrhea when Wealth moves: the Yi Yin says Wealth moving in a yang palace means vomiting and in a yin palace diarrhea, and also Wealth with Earth vomiting and with Water diarrhea; the Huangjin Ce says Wealth moving in the upper (outer) trigram means vomiting and in the lower (inner) trigram diarrhea (LY.ILL.HJC.30). One looks at yang and yin palaces and the other at the upper and lower trigrams, so the two divide differently; the books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.YY.25 医药·治法与用药：官鬼属金宜灸不宜丸药、属木宜针不宜汤药、属水宜温药丸药不宜针、属火宜凉药汤药不宜草头药饮片、属土宜咬咀不宜灸（取克鬼者、忌生鬼者）；鬼伤二间宜宽胸膈；财外动合用宜吐、内动宜泻；鬼在外卦宜外表、伏内卦宜内表**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):治法与药性要不要按鬼的五行来选：易隐说得很具体——鬼属金宜灸、属木宜针、属水宜温药丸药、属火宜凉药汤药、属土宜咬咀，原则是用克鬼的、避开生鬼的；黄金策也有火鬼旺宜大寒药、水鬼旺宜大热药的说法（LY.ILL.HJC.82、LY.ILL.HJC.84），方向一致。增删卜易的李我平驳这些说法拘泥，说子孙不是针灸之药，胶柱鼓瑟会坑陷人命（LY.ILL.ZS.40）。三家意见不同，书里没有裁决。
  Whether the way of treatment and the nature of the drug is chosen by the element of the ghost: the Yi Yin is very specific — Metal, moxibustion; Wood, acupuncture; Water, warm medicines and pills; Fire, cool medicines and decoctions; Earth, crushed herbs — the principle being to use what controls the ghost and avoid what generates it; the Huangjin Ce also has the sayings that a strong Fire ghost suits very cold medicine and a strong Water ghost very hot medicine (LY.ILL.HJC.82 and LY.ILL.HJC.84), in the same direction. Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi rejects these as rigid, saying Output is not the medicine of acupuncture and moxibustion and that sticking to the letter pits and drowns lives (LY.ILL.ZS.40). The three differ and the books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.YY.26 医药·受病在此所治在彼（周杰）：金虎鬼冲伤用是肝受病然治肺不治肝，木虎鬼脾受病治肝，水虎鬼心受病治肾，火虎鬼肺受病治心，土虎鬼肾受病治脾；颗师：子孙动者误服药**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):子孙发动是好是坏：颗师说「子孙动者误服药」，子孙一动就是吃错了药，断例（剥之观）也照这个断，子孙发动冲克官爻、又变出巳绝，药不对症；黄金策、卜筮正宗、增删卜易都是另一个方向——子孙发动制鬼，是药能见效、甚至不药自愈（LY.ILL.HJC.32、LY.ILL.BSZZ.12、LY.ILL.ZS.30）。同一个爻象，两个相反的说法，书里没有裁决。周杰讲的「受病在此所治在彼」则是易隐这一组里独有的说法，别处没有。
  Whether a moving Output line is good or bad: Master Ke says 'when Output moves, the wrong medicine has been taken', and the case (Bo into Guan) is judged the same way — Output moving to clash with and control Pressure and then changing into Si in Extinction, the medicine did not fit; the Huangjin Ce, the Bushi Zhengzong and the Zengshan Buyi go the other way — Output moving to restrain the ghost means the medicine will take effect, even that he recovers without medicine (LY.ILL.HJC.32, LY.ILL.BSZZ.12 and LY.ILL.ZS.30). The same line-image has two opposite sayings and the books do not decide. Zhou Jie's 'the illness is received here and the treatment lies there' is a saying peculiar to this group in the Yi Yin and is found nowhere else.
- **LY.ILL.YY.27 鬼崇·祈禳：以官鬼为用，无鬼或鬼空绝则无鬼崇；官鬼生合世用宜祈祷、官持世用先祷后医、鬼动克世用而福动救助宜禳保、世用持财逢火鬼动宜众保；阳鬼为神、阴官为鬼、鬼带书贵为神**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):怎么分神和鬼：易隐说「阳鬼为神，阴官为鬼」，按鬼爻是阴爻还是阳爻分；这和卜筮正宗的王洪绪「阳为神为男，阴为鬼为女」一致（LY.ILL.HJC.94），而黄金策的旧注和增删卜易的觉子都是按旺衰分——旺相是神、休囚是鬼（LY.ILL.HJC.94、LY.ILL.ZS.23），觉子还说鬼休囚或空破墓绝根本不是鬼神作祟。两种分法不同，书里没有裁决。
  How to tell a spirit from a ghost: the Yi Yin says 'a yang ghost is a spirit, a yin Pressure line is a ghost', by whether the Pressure line is a yin or a yang line; this agrees with Wang Hongxu of the Bushi Zhengzong, 'yang is a spirit and male, yin a ghost and female' (LY.ILL.HJC.94), while the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Juezi of the Zengshan Buyi both go by strength — strong is a spirit, resting or trapped a ghost (LY.ILL.HJC.94 and LY.ILL.ZS.23), and Juezi adds that if the ghost is resting, trapped, Void, broken, in a tomb or in Extinction it is not a ghost or spirit making trouble at all. The two divide differently and the books do not decide.
- **LY.ILL.YY.28 鬼崇·神与鬼的名目：阳金鬼庵堂关圣（在虎为七煞）、阴金鬼刀伤自刎痨瘵喘嗽虎伤之魂；阳木鬼东岳五圣家先、阴木鬼自缢疯疾跌扑刑杖死；阳水鬼观音真武三官水神河泊、阴水鬼投河服卤呕血；阳火鬼玄坛五福火神灶司、阴火鬼疮毒痨疫带血心疼烧死；阳土鬼城隍土谷太岁皮场大王、阴土鬼瘟疫咽喉臌胀黄疸压死**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):要不要把五行落实到具体的神：易隐按阳鬼、阴鬼列了很具体的名目（阳金鬼是庵堂关圣，阳木鬼是东岳五圣，阳水鬼是观音真武三官……），黄金策的旧注也列过（灶君、伤司、河伯江神，LY.ILL.HJC.97）；增删卜易的觉子认为不必把金官指为关公岳公这样的神，要问病人心里疑的是哪位再占一卦（LY.ILL.ZS.24），李我平更批评《易冒》《易林补遗》指定关岳（LY.ILL.ZS.25）。指名派和不指名派，书里没有裁决。
  Whether to fix the elements to particular named spirits: the Yi Yin gives very specific names by yang and yin ghost (a yang Metal ghost the temples and Guan Sheng, a yang Wood ghost the Eastern Peak and the Five Sages, a yang Water ghost Guanyin, Zhenwu and the Three Officials...), and the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce also lists names (the Kitchen God, the Injuring Office, the Lord of the River and river god, LY.ILL.HJC.97); Juezi of the Zengshan Buyi holds that one should not name Metal Pressure as Guan Gong, Yue Gong and the like but ask which spirit the patient has in mind and divine again (LY.ILL.ZS.24), and Li Woping criticises the Yi Mao and the Yilin Buyi for naming Guan and Yue (LY.ILL.ZS.25). The naming school and the non-naming school are not settled by the books.
- **LY.ILL.YY.35 痘疹占：以官鬼为用，鬼不上卦或鬼化死墓绝胎空、被刑害克破者不出，鬼衰死而化生旺、无鬼而月建临官、鬼静而日当生旺、火鬼加蛇旺动者皆出；出期以官鬼生旺之年月日断；鬼衰子旺或子持身世必稀朗、鬼旺子衰或鬼持身世必稠密；父动者重、兄动者轻；福临空死墓绝胎或受刑害克冲者命难保**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/痘疹.md`):痘的稀密看什么：易隐是「鬼衰子旺（或子持身世）则稀朗，鬼旺子衰（或鬼持身世）则稠密」，和卜筮正宗「官强而痘难开朗，福旺则花必稀疏」（LY.ILL.BSZZ.06）、增删卜易「鬼旺相临日月有生扶则花稠密，空破休囚墓绝则痘疏朗」（LY.ILL.ZS.20）是同一个方向，三家一致。取用的办法则不同：易隐以官鬼为用（出不出、何时出、稀密），吉凶另看子孙（福）是否受克；卜筮正宗按谁出花取用（兄弟取兄弟爻、子侄取子孙、妻奴取财），官鬼是痘花本身（LY.ILL.BSZZ.02）；增删卜易问何时出花以鬼为用、问吉凶全看子孙（LY.ILL.ZS.19、LY.ILL.ZS.21）。易隐这一条在取用上和增删卜易相近。「父动者重，兄动者轻」在另两家没有。
  What decides whether the pox is sparse or dense: the Yi Yin says 'ghost weak and Output strong (or Output holding the Body or Self), sparse and clear; ghost strong and Output weak (or the ghost holding the Body or Self), dense', in the same direction as the Bushi Zhengzong's 'Pressure strong and the pox hardly clears; Output strong and the flowers are sparse' (LY.ILL.BSZZ.06) and the Zengshan Buyi's 'a strong Pressure line on the Day or Month with support brings dense flowers; Void, broken, resting, trapped, entombed or extinct, sparse pox' (LY.ILL.ZS.20); the three agree. The way of taking the Subject differs: the Yi Yin takes Pressure as the Subject (whether the pox comes out, when, sparse or dense) and looks separately at whether Output (Fortune) is controlled for fortune; the Bushi Zhengzong takes it by who has the pox (Peer for a brother, Output for a child, Wealth for a wife or servant) with Pressure the pox flowers themselves (LY.ILL.BSZZ.02); the Zengshan Buyi takes Pressure as the Subject for when the pox comes out and looks wholly at Output for fortune (LY.ILL.ZS.19 and LY.ILL.ZS.21). In taking the Subject the Yi Yin is close to the Zengshan Buyi. 'Resource moving is heavy, Peer moving light' is found in neither of the others.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.01 断病生死全凭用神：卦名、卦身世身兼用、本命丧门、蛇虎星煞都不验，只有卦变六冲例外**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):断生死看什么：黄金策的病体章除了用神，还讲卦身、世爻、本命爻（LY.ILL.HJC.35、HJC.41、HJC.53、HJC.54）；野鹤说这些并用是舛错，断生死全凭用神，只有卦变六冲例外。
  What to look at in judging life and death: besides the Subject the Huangjin Ce's chapter on the patient's condition also speaks of the hexagram Body, the Self line and the natal line (LY.ILL.HJC.35, HJC.41, HJC.53, HJC.54); the Old Man of Yehe says that using them together is error, that life and death rest wholly on the Subject, and that a hexagram changing into Six-Clash is the only exception.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.05 主空无救不分近久非经验之说：野鹤驳《海底眼》《黄金策》，近病久病要分；觉子验得近病旬空又逢三合六合必成久病而终**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):主象落空要不要紧：黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.37）和《海底眼》说主象落空就没救，不分近病久病；野鹤说这不是经验之谈，近病落空到冲空、实空的日子就好（LY.ILL.ZS.04）。觉子又补一条：近病落旬空遇三合、六合会拖成久病而死。
  Whether a Void main image matters: the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.37) and the Haidi Yan say that a Void main image has no rescue, without distinguishing recent from long illness; the Old Man of Yehe says this is not drawn from experience, and that a recent illness that falls into Void heals on the day the Void is clashed or filled (LY.ILL.ZS.04). Juezi adds a point: a recent illness in Void that meets a three-harmony or a six-combine drags into a long illness and ends in death.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.06 用化鬼、鬼化用慎防不测，忌化用、用化忌最难调治：自占病世爻不宜变鬼或化回头克，占兄弟妻子不宜鬼与其用神互变，又不宜兄变财财化兄父化子子化父；父动化鬼是化生，轻病即愈，久病鬼化父、父化鬼皆主危亡**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/生死.md`):「用化鬼」是不是必死：黄金策旧注读作用爻发动又变出鬼爻、没有解救必死（LY.ILL.HJC.44），王洪绪反对拿「用神变官鬼必死」来断（同条）；野鹤的说法在两者之间：用化鬼是凶兆，轻病（父动化鬼是化生）和近病落旬空的可以愈，久病则鬼化父、父化鬼都主危亡。
  Whether 'the Subject changing into Pressure' is surely fatal: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce reads it as the Subject line moving and also changing out a Pressure line, and with no rescue he surely dies (LY.ILL.HJC.44), while Wang Hongxu rejects judging 'the Subject changing into Pressure is surely fatal' (same entry); the Old Man of Yehe stands between the two: the Subject changing into Pressure is an ill omen, but a light illness (Resource moving into Pressure is a changing into generation) and a recent illness whose Subject falls into Void can recover, and in a long illness Pressure changing into Resource or Resource into Pressure always means danger of death.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.18 断是不是出痘：旧法以官鬼旺者是花痘，野鹤试之不验，只看子孙衰旺，子孙旺者出花无碍；日月世爻克子孙、卦得六冲，花未开而先谢（例：占子发热是痘否，兑为泽，次日再占大过之涣）**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/痘疹.md`):怎样断是不是出痘：旧法（书里没有指名）以官鬼旺相的为花痘；野鹤试了不验，说是揣摩之说，只看子孙衰旺。
  How to judge whether it is smallpox: the old method (which the book does not name) takes a strong Pressure line as smallpox in flower; the Old Man tried it without result, calls it guesswork, and looks only at whether Output is strong or weak.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.22 占病先分占吉凶、不靠猜症候：野鹤称《黄金策》占疾病首论（五行脏腑、六神症）是「撞门槌」，无益之论悉删；他的办法是先占吉凶，卦吉许以调理即愈，卦凶命延医，鬼动克用神再占鬼神、家宅、坟茔**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/总则.md`):占病开头该讲什么：黄金策的疾病章开头（LY.ILL.HJC.02 到 HJC.05）拿鬼爻的五行、宫和六神去断症候，并且用同一卦兼断死生；野鹤说这是撞门槌，只是猜发冷发热，对病人没有用，全部删掉，改成先占吉凶、再占治法。
  What the divination of illness should begin with: the opening of the Huangjin Ce's chapter on illness (LY.ILL.HJC.02 to HJC.05) judges the symptoms from the phase of the Pressure line, the palace and the six spirits, and judges life and death from the same hexagram; the Old Man of Yehe says this is a battering ram, only a guess at chills and fever, of no use to the patient, and deletes it all, replacing it with first divining good or ill fortune and then divining the treatment.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.23 占鬼神看鬼爻的五行与旺衰：鬼休囚或空破墓绝都不是鬼神作祟；旺相是神、休囚是鬼——金是武神西方神/刀剑亡鬼，木是文神东方神/依草附木之妖或刑杖悬梁之鬼，水是河海北方神/水死之鬼，火是火神南方神雷公电母窖灶神/汤火焚烧之鬼，土是土神中央神山川社稷神/墙倒屋塌土死之鬼**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):怎样认是哪一路神鬼：黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.50）按日辰的地支推（子是北斗或北阴，亥是溺死鬼），王洪绪按生合世爻或用爻的那一爻带的六神认；觉子这里按鬼爻自己的五行和旺衰认（旺相是神，休囚是鬼），又说鬼休囚或空破墓绝根本不是鬼神作祟。三家的办法各不相同。
  How to tell which sort of spirit or ghost it is: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.50) reasons from the branch of the Day (Zi is the Northern Dipper or the North Yin, Hai a drowned ghost), and Wang Hongxu from the six spirits carried by the line that generates or combines with the Self or Subject line; Juezi here tells by the phase and strength of the Pressure line itself (strong is a god, resting or trapped a ghost), and adds that Pressure that is resting or trapped, Void, broken, in a tomb or extinct is not the harm of ghosts and spirits at all. The three ways all differ.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.25 祭送不是万灵药：时灾瘟癖照法祭送屡验，疯痨气蛊是自己失调、与鬼神无关；野鹤见滇黔蜀粤病不服药专信鬼神、杀牲救命反害命；李我平驳《易冒》《易林补遗》指定关岳、说不祭降灾是邪神，并说此书疾病章言鬼神是从俗**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/神祟.md`):要不要指定是哪位神：《易冒》以鬼临金为关帝、岳王，《易林补遗》以青龙为关圣（李我平点了这两部书）；李我平说这些说法不通，还说「祭者降福、不祭者降灾」是作威作福的邪神。野鹤对祭送的态度是：时灾瘟癖照法祭送灵验，疯痨气蛊与鬼神无关。
  Whether to name a particular god: the Yi Mao takes Pressure on Metal as Emperor Guan and King Yue, and the Yilin Buyi takes the Azure Dragon as the Sage Guan (Li Woping names the two books); Li Woping says this does not hold, and that 'blessing to those who sacrifice, calamity to those who do not' would make evil gods who lord it and dispense favour. The Old Man's attitude to sacrifice is that for seasonal calamities and epidemics sacrifice by the method proves true, and that madness, consumption, qi disorder and drum-belly have nothing to do with ghosts and spirits.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.27 鬼是忧神、子孙是喜悦解忧之神：鬼持世或动，得子孙动而制之，此医可请手到成功；子孙临应爻是明医，因为子孙克去的是忧不是邪魔（例：三十余人泪眼相对，子水兄爻旬空，半夜子时退灾）**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):子孙是什么：黄金策旧注、王洪绪都说子孙是医药（LY.ILL.HJC.32）；野鹤说官鬼不是鬼也不是病，是一家人的忧神，子孙不是药，是解忧的喜悦之神，所以「子孙旺动，不药而愈」。
  What Output is: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu both say Output is the medicine (LY.ILL.HJC.32); the Old Man says the Pressure line is neither ghost nor illness but the spirit of worry of the whole household, and that Output is not medicine but the spirit of joy that relieves worry, so that 'with Output strong and moving, one recovers without medicine'.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.30 子孙制鬼最喜旺相生扶：不受刑冲克害、不逢破墓绝空药必见效；子动化鬼化克药不精、化空实空之日有效、化生化旺化进神更灵；古以子动化子药必杂，野鹤说非也，子动化旺子孙是改换药品即比仙丹**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):「子化子」是好是坏：黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.65）说子孙重叠、或子孙又变子孙，是医生不精、用药杂；王洪绪读作子孙化进神药有效、化退神与伏吟卦不可服；野鹤直接说「古以子动化子，其药必杂，非也」，变成旺相的子孙是另换药品，好比仙丹。
  Whether 'Output changing into Output' is good or bad: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.65) says that Output doubled, or Output changing again into Output, means an unskilled doctor and mixed medicines; Wang Hongxu reads it as Output changing into an Advancing spirit making the medicine effective, and a change into a Retreating spirit or a Hidden Chant hexagram meaning it may not be taken; the Old Man says outright that 'the ancients judged Output moving and changing into Output as a surely mixed medicine, and this is wrong', and that a change into a strong Output means the medicine is changed, as good as an elixir.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.32 父爻持世妙药难调：自占病父持世药不见功宜静养；兄持世有子孙动可延医，嫌鬼旺鬼兴主误服药；财爻持世勿误食肥甘；旺相遇生扶良医有觅，休囚逢冲克无药可医**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):父母持世怎么办：黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.63）说父母持世可以许经醮保禳、祈祷之后再服药；野鹤说药不见功，宜静养、远色欲、息气恼。兄弟持世饮食减省见 LY.ILL.HJC.46。
  What to do when Resource holds the Self line: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.63) says one may vow scriptures, offerings and protective rites and take medicine after praying; the Old Man says the medicine shows no effect and one should rest quietly, keep from lust and calm anger. For Peer holding the Self line reducing food, see LY.ILL.HJC.46.
- **LY.ILL.ZS.37 占药能不能服以子孙为用神：医生治危症想用某药、行舟远行无处寻医得一奇方、半夜来不及请医，都要占；子孙持世或发动服之立可解忧；子孙不拘五行，旺或动即喜悦之神，不可因金临子孙用针、火属子孙用炙（例：热极用附子肉桂、人参、牛黄）**(`chapters/六爻/疾病/医药.md`):占药看什么：黄金策旧注、王洪绪讲用药（LY.ILL.HJC.76 起）是看官鬼的五行、子孙的五行来定药；李我平、野鹤说占某味药能不能服，拿子孙当用神，子孙持世或发动服了就见效，不拘五行。同样是儿子占父亲的药，一卦看父爻（人参）、一卦看子孙（牛黄），野鹤说要看哪一个动、哪一个持世，在人通变。

- **LY.MARRY.YM.03 咸恒节泰为吉、睽革解离为凶，惟合冲变冲则不宜；婚姻忌三冲则不索用神之衰旺，用神未胜于卦验**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/总则.md`):这一条和《增删卜易》直接相反。觉子引了《易冒》这一段（LY.MARRY.ZS.14），自己占了一卦，得泰卦，但兄弟持世、财爻月破旬空，他不敢拿别人的婚姻大事去试，让人再占一卦，得坤卦六冲大凶，才知道泰卦不可信，结论是仍以财、官、世、应来断，有疑再占一卦。《易隐》（LY.MARRY.YY.09）、《火珠林》（LY.MARRY.HZL.11）里有按卦名断婚的口诀，和《易冒》站在同一边。本书文件末尾附的提要也批评这一章「不根五行生克，不究用神衰旺，惟据卦名之美恶而论」。
  This is the direct opposite of the Zengshan Buyi. Juezi quotes this passage of the Yi Mao (LY.MARRY.ZS.14) and tried it himself: he got Tai, yet with a Peer line on Self and the Wealth line Month-broken and Void; he dared not stake another person's marriage on it, had a second divination cast and got Kun, a six-clash hexagram and very unlucky, and so learned that Tai was not to be trusted; his conclusion is to keep judging by Wealth, Pressure, Self and Other, and to divine again if in doubt. The Yi Yin (LY.MARRY.YY.09) and the Huozhulin (LY.MARRY.HZL.11) have rhymes that judge marriage by hexagram names and stand on the same side as the Yi Mao. The summary printed at the end of this book's file also faults this chapter for 'not rooting in the generating and controlling of the five phases, not examining the strength of the Subject, and going only by whether the hexagram names are fair or foul'.
- **LY.MARRY.YM.06 以八卦推容貌：男占女看财、女占男看官所居的卦宫；乾正坤稳、震威巽胜、坎巧离烈、艮笃兑柔，六子有短长、二老无偏倚，再以用爻衰旺参之**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/对方.md`):李我平批评过这一读法（LY.MARRY.ZS.22）：同一个卦，按八宫读是端正聪明的淑女，按六神读财爻临白虎又成了悖逆之星，两种读法彼此打架；他主张用财、官兼看世、应来断就够了，不要求容貌才情十全。
  Li Woping objected to this way of reading (LY.MARRY.ZS.22): the same hexagram read by the eight palaces gives an upright, intelligent lady, and read by the six spirits a Wealth line with the White Tiger becomes a star of perversity, so the two readings quarrel with each other; he holds that judging by Wealth and Pressure together with Self and Other is enough, and that one should not demand a perfect face and talent.
- **LY.MARRY.YM.07 以六神察才情：财值青龙形骸秀丽、蛇临妻位情性虚浮，白虎悖逆、玄武风流、朱雀巧辞饶舌、勾陈持重寡言；官位同论；先考用神，官旺临玄武为杰士、官绝临玄武为奸人**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/对方.md`):李我平批评过这一读法（LY.MARRY.ZS.22）：同一卦，用八宫读是端正聪明，用六神读却可能是悖逆之星，两种读法互相打架。《易冒》自己也加了一道限制：一定先看用神，然后才把六神附上去。
  Li Woping objected to this way of reading (LY.MARRY.ZS.22): the same hexagram read by the eight palaces is upright and intelligent, and read by the six spirits may be a star of perversity, so the two readings quarrel. The Yi Mao itself adds a limit: the Subject must be examined first, and only then are the six spirits attached.
- **LY.MARRY.YM.09 父母不可沦、子孙不可溺、妻财不可摇、官鬼不可没、世应不可空、比福不可动、夫妇不可二、阴阳不可悖**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/总则.md`):李我平不认这八条（LY.MARRY.ZS.22）：要事事求全——命不入墓、六亲不相妨、父母不沉、子孙不溺、财不动、官不没、夫妇不二、阴阳不悖——世上没有这样的女子，六十四卦里也没有这样的卦。
  Li Woping does not accept these eight (LY.MARRY.ZS.22): to demand everything at once, with the Life not in the tomb, the six relations not harming one another, Resource not sunk, Output not drowned, Wealth not moving, Pressure not lost, the couple not two and yin and yang not at odds, no such woman exists in the world and no such hexagram among the sixty-four.
- **LY.MARRY.YY.14 卦六合者成、六冲者不成；前卦合变冲成后复退、冲化合退后复成；六冲或世应动被日辰合住欲退不得；世应财鬼冲克而日辰动爻生合不成而得人赞成、生合而日辰动爻冲克成而被破；虽生合而临死墓绝胎被刑冲克害，在世鬼则男家不允、在应财则女家不允**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/成否.md`):「冲变合」：这里说「前卦冲而化合，退后复成」（先退后成）；《易林补遗》说未配之前得冲中化合「决不相谐」（LY.MARRY.YL.15），《增删卜易》说「六冲变六合者尤吉，求婚者目下不允，久必成之」（LY.MARRY.ZS.13）。《易隐》和《增删卜易》一致：不是不成，是先退后成。
  For a clash changing into a combination this text says 'withdrawn, then made again'; the Yilin Buyi says that before the match is made it 'surely will not be harmonious' (LY.MARRY.YL.15), and the Zengshan Buyi that 'a Six-clash changing into a Six-combine is better still: for one seeking a marriage, although refused for now, it will be made in time' (LY.MARRY.ZS.13). The Yi Yin agrees with the Zengshan Buyi: not that it fails, but that it is withdrawn first and made afterwards.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.04 男卜女姻财要旺，女占男配鬼宜兴：代占以应爻为对方家，自占以应爻为女身；财为重，应为附和**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/总则.md`):「应为百岁之妻」这句在《黄金策》「身命」章（占一生的那一章）的注里，不在它的婚姻章里。野鹤驳的是把它拿来占婚：重应而不重财是错的。夫妇章的正文替《黄金策》解释了为什么那样定（身命章一个卦要断六亲，财爻另有用处），也说占妻以财为重、应次之。两边都承认要兼看财爻，争的是谁为主。
  The line 'the Other line is the wife of a hundred years' stands in the commentary of the Huangjin Ce's chapter on one's person and fate, not in its marriage chapter. Yehe attacks using it for marriage: stressing the Other line and not Wealth is wrong. The main text of the Zengshan Buyi's chapter on husband and wife explains why the Huangjin Ce fixed it that way (the person-and-fate chapter judges the six kin from a single hexagram, so Wealth had other work to do) and also says that in divining a wife Wealth carries the weight and the Other line comes second. Both sides agree that Wealth should be looked at as well; what they dispute is which comes first.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.15 财化财未必两度婚，鬼化鬼难相守百年：男家占女财爻重叠旺相生合世多贤妻美妾，财化财或双娶、婢妾同来、妆奁丰；女家占男财化财生世聘礼丰厚，鬼化鬼必夫亡或反复灾非**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/动变.md`):《黄金策》：财爻叠叠，重作新人（再娶，见 LY.MARRY.HJC.24）。野鹤说不一定是两次婚姻：他屡次试过，财爻重叠、财化财多是贤妻美妾、双娶、婢妾同来或妆奁丰厚；只有兄弟爻动、或日月冲克财爻的时候，再办喜事才免不了。
  The Huangjin Ce: with Wealth lines upon Wealth lines, one becomes a bridegroom anew (remarriage; see LY.MARRY.HJC.24). The author says it is not necessarily two marriages: in his many trials, doubled Wealth or Wealth changing into Wealth mostly meant a virtuous wife and fair concubine, two brides, a maid or concubine coming along, or a rich dowry; only when a Peer line moves, or the Day and Month clash and control Wealth, can the repeated wedding not be avoided.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.24 占妻以财爻为重、应爻次之：财旺应破仍吉，应旺财破即凶；《黄金策》专以应为妻是因身命章一卦要断六亲**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/夫妻.md`):「应为百岁之妻」出自《黄金策》身命章的注（见 LY.MARRY.ZS.04）。这一章的正文替它解释：身命章一个卦要断六亲，财爻另有用处，所以只好用应爻当妻；同时说占妻仍以财为重、应次之。争论只在「谁为主」：《黄金策》重应，增删卜易重财。
  'The Other line is the wife of a hundred years' comes from the commentary in the Huangjin Ce's person-and-fate chapter (see LY.MARRY.ZS.04). The main text here explains it: that chapter judges the six kin from a single hexagram and Wealth had other work, so the Other line had to stand for the wife; it also says that in divining a wife Wealth still carries the weight and the Other line comes second. The dispute is only over which is primary: the Huangjin Ce stresses the Other line, the Zengshan Buyi stresses Wealth.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.32 反吟有冲有克重于伏吟，两者都要看用神旺衰：伏吟用神旺相则冲开之年月志伸，休囚则不过忧郁；不可一概以反伏为畏途**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/总则.md`):《易林补遗》把反吟、伏吟一概当成凶：「爻有伏吟不吉」「卦有反吟最凶」，别处还说「伏吟反吟犯者，急须回避」（见该书第 54、332 行）；增删卜易看用神旺衰：反吟重在有冲有克、用神受克，伏吟用神旺相时冲开之年月反而志伸。李我平明确站在野鹤一边。他引的《易林补遗》原文是「术者未开」「星家谁觉」，和这里的「术者未闻」「卜家谁觉」各差一个字。
  The Yilin Buyi takes Reversed and Hidden Chant as ill without condition: 'a line with a Hidden Chant is not auspicious', 'a hexagram with a Reversed Chant is the worst', and elsewhere 'those who meet Hidden or Reversed Chant must hurry to avoid it' (lines 54 and 332 of that book); the Zengshan Buyi looks at whether the Subject is strong: a Reversed Chant is heavy through clash and control and a controlled Subject, and a Hidden Chant with a strong Subject actually carries out its aims in the year or month it is clashed open. Li Woping sides plainly with the Old Man of Yehe. The Yilin Buyi's own words are 'no practitioner has opened it' and 'which astrologer has noticed', each differing by one character from the 'has heard' and 'which diviner' quoted here.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.34 间爻在婚姻里是媒妁：旧注说间爻动则多阻隔，野鹤说动而生合世应反得其力、只忌克世克应，觉子说婚姻以间爻为媒妁，日月冲克可解；书中例：间爻鬼化退神虽阻无力**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/媒妁.md`):旧注说间爻一动就多阻隔；野鹤明说不可一概这样断，间爻动而生合世应反得其力，只有克世、克应才是阻碍。觉子只说婚姻里间爻就是媒妁，没有说动了是吉是凶，又说日月冲克可以化解阻隔。书中例里间爻官鬼动，答的是鬼化退神，有阻而无力，和野鹤「看它生合还是克」是一路。
  The old commentary says that when the between lines move there are many obstructions; the Old Man of Yehe says plainly that this is not to be judged across the board: between lines that move to generate or combine with the Self and Other lines help, and only those that control the Self or Other line obstruct. Juezi says only that in marriage the between lines are the go-betweens, without saying whether their moving is lucky or not, and adds that the Day or Month clashing or controlling them resolves the obstruction. In the book's case a moving Pressure between line is answered by 'Pressure turning into Retreating, obstructing but without strength', which is on the same road as the Old Man's 'look at whether it generates and combines or controls'.
- **LY.MARRY.ZS.35 占身命不可一卦兼断：夫妻偕老要另占一卦；「兄动妻亡」这类一条通断，世上贫寒之士岂都是丧偶之人**(`chapters/六爻/婚姻/总则.md`):野鹤批评的是「诸书」的一卦兼断，其中特别点了《易林补遗》一句「兄动妻亡财耗散」。这句在本库的《易林补遗》里没有原样出现，意思相近的是「兄动，妻灾奴仆患，资财耗散事无成」（该书第 109 行）和「兄动损妻，财无积聚」（第 441 行），应是转述。
  What the Old Man of Yehe criticizes is judging everything from one hexagram as 'the books' do, and he singles out the Yilin Buyi line 'a moving Peer line, the wife dies and wealth is scattered'. That sentence does not appear word for word in the Yilin Buyi in this library; the close ones are 'Peer moving: trouble for wife and servants, wealth scattered and nothing accomplished' (line 109 of that book) and 'Peer moving harms the wife, wealth does not gather' (line 441), so it is presumably a paraphrase.
- **LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.04 求财何日得：死财等财生的日月，生财不要拿财合当日期；空亡加退度，说得再多也要迟疑**(`chapters/六爻/求财/应期.md`):《天玄赋》说「财合日辰，方能入手」，合日是入手的条件；这里说生财「财合莫为期」，合日不能当日期。两处不好并成一说：一处讲财与日辰合，一处讲财自己得令时的合日。整理本后面今人注把「财合」读成「入墓」，原文写的是「合」，本库照原文，不改。
  The Tianxuan Fu says that when Wealth combines with the Day the money can be taken in hand, making the combining day a condition; this verse says that for a living Wealth 'Wealth combining' is not to be taken as the date. The two do not merge into one statement. A modern note in the transcription reads 'combining' as 'entering the tomb'; the original says 'combining', and this library follows the original.
- **LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.12 郭璞论买卖：财爻克世最好，身旺相与财并、无煞不空；贞旺悔囚先赢，悔旺贞休后赢**(`chapters/六爻/求财/买卖.md`):这首歌里的「贞」「悔」，有两种读法。一种是内卦为贞、外卦为悔；一种是本卦为贞、之卦为悔。《易林补遗》两种都记：有动爻时，本卦是贞、之卦是悔；爻都静、没有之卦时，才取内卦为贞、外卦为悔，并且「以贞为始，以悔为终」。歌诀自己没有说是哪一种。「贞旺悔囚初买卖，悔旺贞休后始赢」里的「初」和「后」有先后，和「以贞为始，以悔为终」对得上；但这不是歌诀的明文，本库不裁。
  'Zhen' and 'Hui' in this verse can be read in two ways: the inner trigram as Zhen and the outer as Hui, or the original hexagram as Zhen and the resulting hexagram as Hui. The Yilin Buyi records both: when there is a moving line, the original hexagram is Zhen and the resulting hexagram is Hui; only when all lines are still and there is no resulting hexagram does it take the inner trigram as Zhen and the outer as Hui, and 'Zhen is the beginning and Hui is the end.' The verse itself does not say which. The words 'first' and 'later' in 'Zhen thriving and Hui imprisoned, the first trades; Hui thriving and Zhen resting, only later a win' have a before and after that fits 'Zhen is the beginning, Hui the end'; but that is not the verse's own statement, and this library does not decide.
- **LY.WEALTH.HDY.05 财旺相出现，日克之必有；日辰旺相，不克伏财而冲散飞爻，其财必有**(`chapters/六爻/求财/应期.md`):这里说「财旺相出现，日克之必有」；《黄金策》说「日伤妻位，财虽旺，而当日应无」（见 HJC.08）。两句都讲日辰克旺财，结论相反。一种可能是两处说的应期不同（这里似是日克之时财才发，那里是克日当天无财），书里没有明说，本库不裁。
  This says 'a thriving Wealth that shows, the Day controlling it, surely has money'; the Huangjin Ce says 'the Day harming the Wealth place, though Wealth is thriving, has none on that day' (see HJC.08). Both speak of the Day controlling a thriving Wealth and reach opposite results. The two may be speaking of different dates, but the books do not say so and this library does not decide.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.01 要问吉凶，但看财福**(`chapters/六爻/求财/总则.md`):财、福各指什么，两家注说得相反：旧注说妻财是利润、子孙是财源；王洪绪说妻财是本钱、子孙是利润。野鹤只说子孙是生财的原神。三家都以妻财为用神、子孙为最要紧的帮手。
  The two commentaries gloss the pair oppositely: the old commentary makes Wealth the profit and Output its source; Wang Hongxu makes Wealth the capital and Output the profit. Yehe only says Output is the Support that generates Wealth. All three take Wealth as the Subject and Output as its chief help.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.03 有福无财，兄弟交重偏有望**(`chapters/六爻/求财/有无.md`):「有福无财」怎么读：旧注读作「财不上卦」，只要兄弟发动去生子孙就有望；王洪绪把「有、无」读成「动、伏」，而且认为有望全靠子孙也在动；野鹤也要子孙和兄弟同动，并说财、兄同动主阻隔。
  How to read 'Output present, no Wealth': the old commentary takes it as Wealth absent from the hexagram, with hope as long as the moving Peer feeds Output; Wang Hongxu reads 'present/absent' as 'moving/hidden' and says the hope depends on Output moving as well; Yehe also wants Output and Peer moving together, and says Wealth and Peer moving together means obstruction.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.04 有财无福，官爻发动亦堪求**(`chapters/六爻/求财/有无.md`):野鹤把经文前半句「有财无福」改成「兄兴财振」：适用的卦从「子孙不现」换成了「兄、财同动」。两种说法的理由一样——官鬼克兄弟、护住财——但管的是不同的卦。
  Yehe rewrote the first half: 'Wealth present, no Output' became 'Peer and Wealth both stirring'. The reason is the same in both (Pressure controls Peer and so shields the money), but they apply to different hexagrams.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.09 多财反复，必须墓库以收藏**(`chapters/六爻/求财/有无.md`):「墓库收藏」三家读法不同：旧注要卦里有财的墓库爻发动；王洪绪要财的库爻持世或临卦身；野鹤不看卦里有没有库爻，改成应期——等财爻入墓的那一天。
  They read 'gathered in by the tomb' three ways: the old commentary wants Wealth's tomb line moving in the hexagram; Wang Hongxu wants that tomb line on the Self or Body line; Yehe ignores whether the hexagram has one and turns it into timing: the money comes on the day Wealth enters its tomb.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.11 兄如太过，反不克财**(`chapters/六爻/求财/难易.md`):兄弟太过劫不劫财，三家说法不同：旧注说太过反而不劫，到财生旺日可得；王洪绪说要见子孙发动才不劫、利无穷，子孙静则不吉；觉子说太过的兄弟到入墓或受损克之日照样劫财，并举了一个九月兄弟入墓破财的例子。
  Does excessive Peer rob or not? The old commentary: excess means it does not rob, and the money comes on a day that births or empowers Wealth. Wang Hongxu: it does not rob only if Output moves, and then the profit is endless; with Output still it is unlucky. Juezi: excessive Peer still robs on the day it enters its tomb or is cut down, and gives a case where the loss came in the ninth month when Peer entered its tomb.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.12 身或兄临，必难求望**(`chapters/六爻/求财/难易.md`):看卦身还是看世爻：旧注、王洪绪看卦身（兄临卦身难求，持世亦然）；野鹤认为卦身不验，只看世爻，并列出兄弟持世仍能得财的几种情形。
  Body line or Self line? The old commentary and Wang Hongxu read the Body line (Peer on it means hardship, and the same on the Self line); Yehe holds the Body line unreliable, goes by the Self line alone, and lists the cases in which Peer on the Self line still gets the money.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.16 兄连鬼克，纷纷口舌难逃**(`chapters/六爻/求财/风险.md`):「兄连鬼克」怎么读：旧注读作兄弟变出官鬼来克世，主口舌、官讼；王洪绪和觉子都驳它——兄弟变官鬼是回头克，兄弟自身难保，不能再去克世；王洪绪改读为兄弟、官鬼同时发动才有口舌。野鹤把经文改成「兄伤鬼克」，并举例说兄化鬼虽然不劫财，却应在问卜人的弟弟身亡。
  How to read 'Peer together with Pressure': the old commentary takes it as Peer turning into Pressure and attacking the Self line, meaning quarrels and lawsuits. Wang Hongxu and Juezi both reject this: Peer turning into Pressure is a turn-back control, Peer cannot save itself, let alone attack the Self line; Wang reads it instead as Peer and Pressure moving at the same time. Yehe rewrites the line as 'Peer harming, Pressure attacking', and gives a case where Peer turning into Pressure did not rob the money but showed up as the death of the asker's younger brother.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.18 财化鬼，防耗折而惊忧**(`chapters/六爻/求财/动变.md`):财化兄弟有多凶：旧注说是与人分利、先聚后散；王洪绪把它和财化官鬼并列为「最凶」。
  How bad is Wealth turning into Peer? The old commentary: sharing the profit, gathered then scattered. Wang Hongxu puts it alongside Wealth turning into Pressure as 'the worst'.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.26 欲决何时有利，但详春夏秋冬**(`chapters/六爻/求财/应期.md`):旧注主张按财爻五行的当令季节断得价之时；王洪绪说不能一概而论，还要用冲合、生绝、墓开的方法，并看子孙爻。
  The old commentary times the price by the season of the Wealth line's element; Wang Hongxu says that is too simple and adds clash-and-combination, birth-and-extinction, tomb-opening and the Output line.
- **LY.WEALTH.HJC.27 合伙不嫌兄弟**(`chapters/六爻/求财/开店.md`):兄弟到底嫌不嫌：经文说合伙不嫌兄弟；旧注、王洪绪说兄弟静可以、动不宜；野鹤引了经文，却接着说世、应临兄弟不吉、兄弟动也不吉，只有日月为财冲克世爻时反吉。
  Is Peer really no objection? The classic says so for partnerships; the old commentary and Wang Hongxu allow a still Peer but not a moving one; Yehe quotes the classic but then says Peer on the Self or Other line is unlucky and a moving Peer too, the only exception being a Wealth Day or Month clashing with or controlling the Self line.
- **LY.WEALTH.HZL.01 公私用事：占买卖、占求财看财爻；占财必用子孙；兄弟是破财之人，不主不辅**(`chapters/六爻/求财/总则.md`):兄弟要不要看：《火珠林》说兄弟是破财的人、不主不辅，不必去看；《黄金策》《易隐》《增删卜易》里却有大量条目专看兄弟动不动、旺不旺、持世还是克世（如兄弟交重、兄弟持世、兄弟克世）。
  Whether to look at Peer: the Huozhu Lin says Peer is only the one who breaks Wealth, neither chief nor helper, and need not be looked at; the Huangjin Ce, the Yi Yin and the Zengshan Buyi devote a great many entries to whether Peer moves, is strong, sits on the Self or controls it.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.03 难易：财旺临身世易，父化财先难后获**(`chapters/六爻/求财/难易.md`):财化财怎么断，三家不同：《易隐》说「化去而难得」；《黄金策》旧注说「反复不定」；《天玄赋》注（《断易天机》本）说「本来是吉」，只是要分本卦、变卦的财孰旺孰衰。
  Wealth turning into Wealth is read three ways: the Yi Yin says it 'changes away and is hard to get'; the Golden Strategy's old commentary says 'back and forth, uncertain'; the Tianxuan Fu commentary (Duanyi Tianji text) says 'normally lucky', judged by which of the two Wealths is stronger.
- **LY.WEALTH.YY.26 店面货物：父母为店，妻财为货；父旺店大，财旺货流；书纸店以父为用，宝珠古董店以财为用**(`chapters/六爻/求财/开店.md`):书籍纸张店该看谁，同一段里有两个说法：前面说书、纸、巾、帽、衣服店以父母为用神，父母旺才好；郭雍却说开书籍纸货古董店，要财旺、父母被克才吉；当铺则反过来，父母旺、财被克才吉。原文并列，没有分出高下。
  For a bookshop the same paragraph gives two readings: first, shops of books, paper, scarves, hats and clothes take Resource as the Subject, so Resource should be strong; Guo Yong says a shop of books, paper goods and antiques does well when Wealth is strong and Resource controlled, while a pawnshop is the other way round, Resource strong and Wealth controlled. The source sets them side by side and does not rank them.
