# 仕宦·月建生身当际风云之会，岁君合世必承雨露之恩（旧注：太岁为君、月建为执政之官，生合世身必有好处，冲克世身必遭贬谪；月建扶出官爻世爻是风宪之职，太岁加父母扶出官鬼及世爻必有天恩）

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁是君主的象，月建是执政的官。它们如果生合世爻、身爻，必有好处；只怕冲克世身，必定遭到贬谪。月建扶出官爻、世爻，必是风宪的职位；太岁加在父母上、扶出官鬼和世爻的，必有天恩，再得日辰在生旺之地更好；衰绝、遇空就没有用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Tai Sui is the image of the sovereign, and the Month is the officer who holds government. If they generate or combine with the Self or the Body, there will surely be benefit; what is feared is that they clash or control the Self or the Body, and then one will surely be demoted and exiled. If the Month supports Pressure or the Self, it is surely a post in the censorate; if the Tai Sui is added to Resource and supports Pressure and the Self, there will surely be the Emperor's favour, better still if the Day then stands in a thriving place; if weak, in extinction or Void, it is of no use.

> [!note] 按
> 「风云之会」是君臣相遇、得志的时候，「雨露之恩」是君主的恩泽。「风宪」是负责监察、执法的官署（御史台、按察司一类）。这一联里「太岁」是君、「月建」是执政的官，也就是做官的人头上的两层上司，所以放在「贵人」这一题下。「贬谪」是降职并外放。注里「月建扶出官爻世爻者必是风宪之职」「太岁加父母扶出官鬼及世爻者必有天恩」两句，是占某一类官的象，见 LY.CAREER.HJC.19 以下几条。
>
> *Note:* 'The gathering of wind and cloud' is the time when a ruler and his minister meet and the minister achieves his aim, and 'the favour of rain and dew' is the sovereign's grace. 'The censorate' is the office that supervises and enforces the law (the Censorate, the Surveillance Commission and the like). In this couplet the Tai Sui is the sovereign and the Month the officer who holds government, that is, the two levels of superiors above an official, and so the entry is placed under 'patrons and superiors'. The two sentences in the commentary 'if the Month supports Pressure or the Self, it is surely a post in the censorate' and 'if the Tai Sui is added to Resource and supports Pressure and the Self, there will surely be the Emperor's favour' say what kind of post, which are taken up again in the entries from LY.CAREER.HJC.19 onwards.
