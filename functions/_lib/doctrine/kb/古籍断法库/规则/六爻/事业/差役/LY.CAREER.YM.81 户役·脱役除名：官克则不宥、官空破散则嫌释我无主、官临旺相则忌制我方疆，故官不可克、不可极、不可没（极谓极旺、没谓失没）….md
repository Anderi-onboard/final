# 户役·脱役除名：官克则不宥、官空破散则嫌释我无主、官临旺相则忌制我方疆，故官不可克、不可极、不可没（极谓极旺、没谓失没）；补遗云官鬼克身难脱役、子孙持世易除名

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 脱役、除名：官克（世），就不被宽宥；官空、破、散，又嫌没有主事的人放我；官临旺相，又忌它制住我的范围。所以凡是官，不可被克，不可太旺（极），不可失去（没）。「极」是极旺，「没」是失没。补遗说：官鬼克身，难脱役；子孙持世，易除名。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For being freed from duty and struck off the rolls: Pressure controlling the Self, one is not pardoned; Pressure Void, broken or scattered, one fears that there is no one in charge to release me; Pressure thriving, one fears it holds my bounds in check. So Pressure must not be controlled, must not be extreme, and must not be lost. 'Extreme' is extremely thriving, and 'lost' is vanished. The Buyi says: when Pressure controls the Body, it is hard to be freed from duty; when Output holds the Self, it is easy to be struck off.

> [!note] 按
> 「脱役」是免掉差役，「除名」是把名字从役册上除去。「不宥」是不被宽宥。「释我无主」是没有主事的官放我走。「制我方疆」是限制我的范围。官既不能克（压着我）、也不能极旺（管得太严）、也不能失没（无人放我），这是脱役的『既要有官、又不能太强』的要求。书里自注引「补遗」（易林补遗除名脱役 EL.114）：官鬼克身难脱役，子孙持世易除名，与这里的「官不可克」一致。
>
> *Note:* 'Tuoyi' is being exempted from duty, and 'chuming' is having one's name struck from the register of service. 'Buyou' is not being pardoned. 'Shi wo wu zhu' is that there is no official in charge to let me go. 'Zhi wo fangjiang' is restricting my bounds. Pressure must not control (press on me), must not be extremely thriving (govern too strictly) and must not be lost (no one to release me), which is the requirement for being freed from duty that there must be an official and he must not be too strong. The book's own note quotes the 'Buyi' (the Yilin Buyi on being struck off and freed from duty, EL.114): Pressure controlling the Body makes it hard to be freed from duty, and Output holding the Self makes it easy to be struck off, in agreement with 'Pressure must not control' here.
