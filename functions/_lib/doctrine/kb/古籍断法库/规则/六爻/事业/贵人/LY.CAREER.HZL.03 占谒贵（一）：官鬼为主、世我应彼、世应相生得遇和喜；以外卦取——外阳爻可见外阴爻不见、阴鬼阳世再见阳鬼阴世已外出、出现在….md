# 占谒贵（一）：官鬼为主、世我应彼、世应相生得遇和喜；以外卦取——外阳爻可见外阴爻不见、阴鬼阳世再见阳鬼阴世已外出、出现在家忌外卦独发、伏藏应动皆不见

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占谒见贵人，以官鬼为主，世是我、应是他：世应相生，就能遇到和气喜悦。凡占谒贵，以外卦来取：外卦阳爻，见得到；外卦阴爻，见不到。阴的鬼遇阳的世，再来相见；阳的鬼遇阴的世，他已经外出。（用爻）出现，是在家；忌外卦独发。伏藏、应动，都见不到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about calling on a great man, Pressure is the master; the Self is me and the Other is he: if the Self and the Other generate each other, one meets with harmony and pleasure. In all divinations about calling on a great man, take it from the outer trigram: a yang line in the outer trigram means he can be seen, a yin line in the outer trigram means he cannot. With a yin Pressure line and a yang Self, one will see him later; with a yang Pressure line and a yin Self, he has already gone out. If it appears, he is at home; the outer trigram moving alone is feared. If it is hidden, or the Other moves, he is not seen in either case.

> [!note] 按
> 「谒贵」是去拜见有地位的人。「以外卦取」是用外卦（对方、外面）来看对方的情形，外卦阳爻是显出、阴爻是隐没。「阴鬼阳世再见，阳鬼阴世已外出」按文意是鬼属阴、世属阳时，他此刻不在、要再来；鬼属阳、世属阴时，他已经出门了。「出现在家」是用爻出现在卦里表示对方在家；「伏藏应动，皆不见」是用爻伏藏、或应爻发动，都见不到。这一节是火珠林里最简的谒贵断法，与易林补遗趋谒贵人（EL.99–102）、易隐谒贵占的见否（YY.74–YY.75）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Yegui' is going to call on a person of standing. 'Taking it from the outer trigram' is using the outer trigram (the other side, the outside) to see the other side's situation: a yang line in the outer trigram shows, a yin line hides. 'With a yin Pressure line and a yang Self, one will see him later; with a yang Pressure line and a yin Self, he has already gone out', by the sense: when Pressure is yin and the Self yang, he is not in just now and one must come again; when Pressure is yang and the Self yin, he has already gone out. 'If it appears, he is at home' is the Subject line appearing in the hexagram meaning that the other side is at home; 'if hidden, or the Other moves, he is not seen' is the Subject line hidden, or the Other line moving, in either case one does not see him. This section is the simplest method of calling on a great man in the Huozhulin; read against the Yilin Buyi on calling on the great (EL.99-102) and the Yi Yin on calling on the great, whether one is seen (YY.74-75).
