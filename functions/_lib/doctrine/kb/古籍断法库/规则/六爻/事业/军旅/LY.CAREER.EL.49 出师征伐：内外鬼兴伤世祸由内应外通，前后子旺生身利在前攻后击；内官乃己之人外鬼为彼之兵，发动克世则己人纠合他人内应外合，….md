# 出师征伐：内外鬼兴伤世祸由内应外通，前后子旺生身利在前攻后击；内官乃己之人外鬼为彼之兵，发动克世则己人纠合他人内应外合，世旺鬼衰难以伤克，世变子或鬼变子则鬼受制无大害，本卦子孙旺而生世先攻后取全功，内官旺相外鬼兴隆则彼此相抗难断胜负

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内外两个官鬼兴起伤世爻，祸患由内应外通而起；前后（内外）的子孙旺相生身，利于前攻后击。内卦的官鬼是自己人，外卦的官鬼是对方的兵：如果发动而克世爻，祸患出自自己人勾结外人，这就是内应外合的残害；如果世旺鬼衰，虽然发动也难以伤克；如果世爻变子孙或官鬼变子孙，就是官鬼受制，谋划不会决裂，没有夭折的祸害，经书说「有人制鬼，鬼动何妨」；本卦子孙之卦，子孙旺相而生世爻的，先攻后取，顺着心意行军，都获得全功；如果内卦的官鬼旺相、外卦的官鬼兴旺，就彼此对抗，难以判断胜负。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure rises both inside and outside and injures the Self, the disaster comes from collusion between inside and outside; if Output is thriving before and behind and generates the Body, it favours attacking in front and striking behind. The inner Pressure is one's own men and the outer Pressure is the enemy's troops: if they move and control the Self, the disaster is one's own men joining others, which is the injury of inside and outside in collusion; if the Self is thriving and Pressure weak, though they move it is hard for them to injure or control; if the Self turns into Output or Pressure turns into Output, Pressure is restrained, the plot does not break out and there is no untimely harm, as the classic says 'if someone restrains Pressure, what harm is there in its moving'; if in the original hexagram Output is thriving and generates the Self, one attacks first and takes after, and moving troops as one wishes one gains complete success; if the inner Pressure is thriving and the outer Pressure flourishing, they resist each other and it is hard to judge victory.

> [!note] 按
> 「内官」「外鬼」是内卦的官鬼和外卦的官鬼。「有人制鬼，鬼动何妨」是引的另一句经书，与黄金策「官化兄反、子孙制鬼」一类同义，书里没有说出处。「内应外合」是内外勾结。第一联「前后子旺」的「前后」按解里应是内外；译文写成「前后（内外）」。这一条与易隐征战占「内外两鬼动克世者，防内应外合」（YY.56）相同。
>
> *Note:* 'Inner official' and 'outer Pressure' are the Pressure lines of the inner and of the outer trigram. 'If someone restrains Pressure, what harm is there in its moving' is quoted from another classic that the book does not name, and has the same sense as 'Output restrains Pressure' sayings in the Huangjin Ce. 'Nei ying wai he' is inside and outside colluding. The 'front and back' of 'Output thriving before and behind' should be inside and outside by the explanation, and the translation writes 'before and behind (inside and outside)'. This entry is the same as 'if Pressure in both the inner and outer trigram moves to control the Self, guard against collusion between inside and outside' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.56).
