# 求馆·动遇冲开，时减执经之子（旧注：子孙动被日辰动爻冲散其徒必有背师而去；被世冲散是先生叱退其徒；子孙动变空亡弟子半途而废是歇学非叛去）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻发动，本来是吉兆，如果被日辰、动爻冲散，他的学生必定有背弃老师而去的；如果被世爻冲散，是先生呵斥退了学生，不是弟子自己背弃老师；子孙动而变出空亡，弟子中有半途而废的，那是歇学，不是叛离。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Output line moves, it is of itself an auspicious sign, but if it is clashed apart by the Day or a moving line, some of his pupils will surely turn their backs on him and leave; if it is clashed apart by the Self, it is the teacher who scolds his pupils away, and not the disciples who abandon their teacher; if Output moves and changes into Void, some of the disciples give up halfway, which is leaving off study and not defection.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2777 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙爻动，若被日辰动爻冲散，其徒必背师而去； | 子孙爻发动，如果被日辰、动爻冲散，他的学生必定背弃老师而去； | If the Output line moves and is clashed apart by the Day or a moving line, his pupils will surely turn their backs on him and leave; |
> | 如被世冲，是先生叱退其徒。 | 如果被世爻冲，是先生呵斥退了他的学生。 | if it is clashed by the Self, it is the teacher who scolds his pupils away. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联分三种走法：被日辰动爻冲散（学生背师）、被世冲散（先生赶走学生）、动变空亡（学生中途歇学），区别在于谁的责任。「执经」是拿着经书受业（指学生）。王洪绪只写了前两种。
>
> *Note:* This couplet separates three courses by who is at fault: clashed apart by the Day or a moving line (the pupils turn on the teacher), clashed apart by the Self (the teacher drives the pupils off), and moving into Void (the pupils break off their studies halfway). 'Carry the classics' is going to study with the classics in hand (meaning pupils). Wang Hongxu writes only the first two.
