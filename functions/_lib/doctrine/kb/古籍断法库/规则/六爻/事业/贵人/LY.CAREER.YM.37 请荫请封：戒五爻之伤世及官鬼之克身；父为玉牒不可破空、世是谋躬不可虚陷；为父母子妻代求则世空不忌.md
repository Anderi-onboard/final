# 请荫请封：戒五爻之伤世及官鬼之克身；父为玉牒不可破空、世是谋躬不可虚陷；为父母子妻代求则世空不忌

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 请荫（请求让子孙凭父祖的官阶得官）、请封（请求给父母妻室封赠诰命），要戒备五爻伤世，以及官鬼克身。父爻是玉牒（封荫的凭据），不可破、空；世是为自身谋划的，不可虚、陷。如果是替父母、子、妻求的，世空就不忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking for hereditary privilege (a descendant entering office on the strength of a forefather's rank) or for the conferment of titles (honours for parents and wife), guard against the fifth line harming the Self and against Pressure controlling the Body. The Resource line is the jade register (the document on which such grants rest), and must not be broken or Void; the Self is the one who plans for himself, and must not be empty or fallen. If it is sought on behalf of parents, sons or wife, the Self being Void is not feared.

> [!note] 按
> 「请荫」是请求让子孙凭父祖的官阶得到官职，「请封」是请求给父母、妻室封赠诰命（恩封诰命另见易林补遗 EL.94 起）。「五爻」是君位，五爻伤世是君上不予恩典。「玉牒」本是皇室的谱牒，这里指封荫所凭的文书，所以以父爻为玉牒。「谋躬」是为自身谋划。书里自注：替父母、子、妻求时，世空不忌，因为这时世不是为自己求。这一章前半（L1375–1389）讲求名、发案、廷试、武试，那些归考试；从「请荫请封」起讲的是官职荣典，归事业。
>
> *Note:* 'Qing yin' is asking that a descendant obtain office on the strength of a forefather's rank, and 'qing feng' is asking for the conferment of titles on parents and wife (conferment of titles is also treated in the Yilin Buyi from EL.94). The 'fifth line' is the sovereign's place, and the fifth line harming the Self is the sovereign withholding his favour. 'Yupie' was the imperial genealogy, and here it means the document on which such grants rest, so the Resource line is taken as the jade register. 'Mougong' is planning for oneself. The book's own gloss: when it is sought for parents, sons or wife, the Self being Void is not feared, because then the Self is not seeking for himself. The first half of this chapter (L1375–1389) deals with seeking a name, the posting of results, the palace examination and the military examination, and belongs under Examinations; from 'asking for hereditary privilege and conferment' on it is about official honours and belongs under Career.
