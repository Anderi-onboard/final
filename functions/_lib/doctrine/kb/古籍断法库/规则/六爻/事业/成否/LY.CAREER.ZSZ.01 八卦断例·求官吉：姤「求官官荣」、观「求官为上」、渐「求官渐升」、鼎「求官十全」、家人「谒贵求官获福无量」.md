# 八卦断例·求官吉：姤「求官官荣」、观「求官为上」、渐「求官渐升」、鼎「求官十全」、家人「谒贵求官获福无量」

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占求官时，遇到姤卦（求官，官位荣耀）、观卦（求官最好）、渐卦（求官渐渐升迁）、鼎卦（求官十全）、家人卦（谒见贵人、求官，获福无量），都是求官有利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking office, meeting the hexagram Gou ('seeking office: the office is glorious'), Guan ('seeking office: the best'), Jian ('seeking office: gradual promotion'), Ding ('seeking office: complete in all ten parts') or Jiaren ('calling on nobles and seeking office, one wins blessing beyond measure') is favourable to seeking office.

## 《周易尚占·八卦断例章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 884 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Chapter of judgments by example for the eight palaces", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 媾者遇也 | 媾（姤），就是相遇。 | Gou (Meeting) means to meet. |
> | 谋必有遇 | 谋事一定会有所遇。 | Any plan is sure to meet with an encounter. |
> | 求官官荣 | 求官，官位荣耀。 | Seeking office: the office is glorious. |
> | 求财财遂 | 求财，财能如愿。 | Seeking wealth: the wealth comes as wished. |
> | 病者难安 | 病人难得平安。 | The sick will find it hard to be at peace. |
> | 婚姻休娶 | 婚姻，不要娶。 | Marriage: do not take the bride. |
> | 卜筮行人 | 占问出门在外的人， | As for divining about a traveller, |
> | 端的在路 | 的确还在路上。 | he is indeed on the road. |
