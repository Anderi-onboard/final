# 洞林秘诀·求官（一）：驿马、龙德与青龙最旺加迁禄位荣；飞伏六爻宜细取、须知四直用心攻；更须印绶并诸德，生旺值时必显通（例：道明占剥之晋，癸酉无期、丙子逢）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 驿马、龙德和青龙，最旺的时候，加官升迁、禄位荣耀；飞、伏的六爻要细细取用，要知道四直，得用心去研究；还要有印绶和各种德神，逢生旺、值时，必定显达亨通。例：从前道明占得剥之晋卦，癸酉（日）无望，丙子（日）才遇上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Post Horse, Dragon Virtue and the Azure Dragon, at their strongest, bring promotion and glory of emolument and rank; the six lines, flying and hidden, should be taken with care, and one must study the four rulers closely; besides, with the seal and ribbon and the various Virtues, meeting birth and strength at the right time one is sure to rise to prominence. For example, in the past Daoming got the hexagram Bo changing to Jin, with no prospect on Guiyou and meeting it on Bingzi.
