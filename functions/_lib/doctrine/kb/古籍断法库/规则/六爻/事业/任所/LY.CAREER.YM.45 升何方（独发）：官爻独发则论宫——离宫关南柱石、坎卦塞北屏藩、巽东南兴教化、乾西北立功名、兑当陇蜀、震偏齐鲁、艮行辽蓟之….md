# 升何方（独发）：官爻独发则论宫——离宫关南柱石、坎卦塞北屏藩、巽东南兴教化、乾西北立功名、兑当陇蜀、震偏齐鲁、艮行辽蓟之漠、坤来秦晋之疆；独发以八宫断方、其余以五行断地利，举天下而言不拘乡任

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官爻单独发动，就按它所在的宫来论方位：独发在离宫，是关南的柱石；单动在坎卦，是塞北的屏藩；在巽，在东南兴教化；在乾，在西北立功名；兑当陇蜀，震偏齐鲁，艮往辽蓟的沙漠，坤到秦晋的疆土。凡独发，用八宫来断方位；其他的情形用五行来断地利；这是就天下而言，不拘泥于本乡、本任。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Pressure line moves alone, direction is discussed by the palace it is in: moving alone in the palace of Li, one is a pillar south of the passes; moving alone in Kan, a screen and bulwark north of the frontier; in Xun, one spreads teaching in the south-east; in Qian, one makes one's name in the north-west; Dui corresponds to Long and Shu, Zhen leans to Qi and Lu, Gen goes to the deserts of Liao and Ji, and Kun comes to the borders of Qin and Jin. When a line moves alone, one judges the direction by the eight palaces; in other cases one judges the lie of the land by the five phases; this speaks of the whole realm, and is not tied to the home district or the present post.

> [!note] 按
> 「关南」「塞北」是关隘之南、边塞之北，「柱石」「屏藩」是撑国家的柱石和护卫的藩篱。「陇蜀」是甘肃、四川，「齐鲁」是山东，「辽蓟」是辽东和蓟州一带，「秦晋」是陕西、山西。八宫的方位是后天八卦：离南、坎北、巽东南、乾西北、兑西、震东、艮东北、坤西南，再配各地的古名。「独发」是卦里只有这一个官爻在动。书里自注：独发用八宫断方位、其余情形（上一条 YM.44）用五行，而且这是就全国而言，不拘泥于原籍或现任所在。
>
> *Note:* 'Guannan' and 'saibei' are south of the passes and north of the frontier, and 'zhushi' and 'pingfan' are the pillar that holds up the state and the screen that guards it. 'Long and Shu' are Gansu and Sichuan, 'Qi and Lu' is Shandong, 'Liao and Ji' are Liaodong and the region of Jizhou, and 'Qin and Jin' are Shaanxi and Shanxi. The directions of the eight palaces are those of the later-heaven arrangement: Li south, Kan north, Xun south-east, Qian north-west, Dui west, Zhen east, Gen north-east, Kun south-west, with the old names of the regions assigned to them. 'Moving alone' is when this is the only Pressure line moving in the hexagram. The book's own gloss: for a line moving alone use the eight palaces for direction, in other cases (the previous entry, YM.44) use the five phases, and it speaks of the whole country and is not tied to the home registry or the present post.
