# 营造公务防患·野鹤：初入工程防患宜子孙动，已见疏虞防患则子动伤官；觉子：初入工程兄动虑赔财，已见疏虞兄兴防罚俸

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占公务防患要分两个阶段：刚开始进入工程而防备祸患的，适合子孙动摇，子孙发动就没有忧虑；如果已经出现了疏漏失误而防备祸患的，必然是在为功名担忧，子孙发动反而伤官星，怎么能说子孙动就没有忧虑呢。觉子补充：刚开始进入工程，兄弟发动要担心赔钱；已经出现疏漏，兄弟兴旺要防备罚俸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining public business and guarding against trouble one must tell two stages apart: for one who has just begun a works project and guards against trouble, Output swaying is good, for when Output moves there is nothing to worry about; but for one who has already seen a lapse and guards against trouble, he is surely anxious for his rank, and Output moving then injures the star of office, so how could it be said that when Output moves there is nothing to worry about? Juezi adds: at the start of a works project, when Peer moves one fears losing money; once a lapse has appeared, when Peer flourishes guard against a fine of salary.
