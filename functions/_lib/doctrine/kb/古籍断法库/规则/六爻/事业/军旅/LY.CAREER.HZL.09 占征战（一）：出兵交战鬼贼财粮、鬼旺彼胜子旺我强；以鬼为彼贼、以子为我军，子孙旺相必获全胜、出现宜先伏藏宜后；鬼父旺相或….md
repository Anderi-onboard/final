# 占征战（一）：出兵交战鬼贼财粮、鬼旺彼胜子旺我强；以鬼为彼贼、以子为我军，子孙旺相必获全胜、出现宜先伏藏宜后；鬼父旺相或独发或持世身大败；六爻安静世旺克应必胜

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出兵交战，鬼是敌人（贼），财是粮草；鬼旺，敌人胜；子旺，我方强。以鬼为对方的贼寇，以子为我军；子孙旺相，必定获得全胜；（子孙）出现，宜先出战，伏藏，宜后出战；卦内凡是鬼、父旺相，或者独发，或者持世、持身，是大败之兆。如果六爻安静，世旺、克应，必胜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In sending out troops to give battle, Pressure is the enemy and Wealth is the provisions; when Pressure is thriving the enemy wins, and when Output is thriving I am strong. Take Pressure as the bandits on the other side and Output as my army; with Output thriving, one is sure to win entirely; if it appears it is right to go first, and if it is hidden it is right to go after; within the hexagram, if Pressure and Resource are thriving, or move alone, or hold the Self or the Body, it is a sign of great defeat. If the six lines are still, and the Self is thriving and controls the Other, one will surely win.

> [!note] 按
> 「鬼贼财粮」是鬼为贼（敌人），财为粮。「彼」是对方，「我」是自己这一边。「出现宜先，伏藏宜后」是子孙在卦里出现的宜先出战，伏藏的宜后出战。「内凡鬼父旺相或是独发，或持世身，大败之兆」是官鬼、父母旺相，或官鬼独发，或持世、持身，都是大败之兆。这一节是征战占的总纲：胜负看官鬼和子孙的旺衰，以及世应的强弱。与易隐征战占的彼己（YY.54–56）、易林补遗出师征伐（EL.12 起）、本书军机（YM.11 起）对着看：都是『世我应彼、子兵鬼寇』这一套。
>
> *Note:* 'Gui zei cai liang' is Pressure as the bandits (the enemy) and Wealth as the grain. 'Bi' is the other side, and 'wo' is oneself, my side. 'If it appears it is right to go first, if hidden after' is that Output appearing in the hexagram means going first to battle, and hidden means going after. 'Nei fan gui fu wangxiang huo shi du fa, huo chi shi shen, da bai zhi zhao' is Pressure and Resource thriving, or Pressure moving alone, or holding the Self or the Body, in each case a sign of great defeat. This section is the general rule for divining war: victory and defeat are seen by the strength and weakness of Pressure and Output, and by the strength of the Self and the Other. Read against the 'we and they' of the Yi Yin on war (YY.54-56), the Yilin Buyi on sending out troops (from EL.12) and the military chapter of this book (from YM.11): all use the same set of 'the Self is me, the Other is they; Output is the troops, Pressure the bandits'.
