# 君臣世应上下交：世为君、应为臣，上为君、下为臣，上下相生则君臣相得；世应相生而奉命训练出行，往则必建功

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世是君、应是臣，上卦是君、下卦是臣；如果上下相生，君臣唱和，满堂和气；卦中世、应相生，又有奉命去训练士卒的差事，去了必定建功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self is the lord and the Other the minister, the upper trigram the lord and the lower the minister; if upper and lower generate each other, lord and ministers answer one another in song and the whole hall is in warm accord; when Self and Other generate each other and there is an order to go and drill troops, going will surely win merit.
