# 卜筮元龟·趋谒（三）：应克世则君主爱；内外相生吉最；世应比和吉无凶；官鬼旺身乐无碍

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应克世，是说主人喜爱你；内外相生，吉利最好；世、应比和，有吉无凶；官鬼旺、身（卦身）旺，欢乐无阻碍。注文说：内外相生、世应比和、官鬼生世爻，都是吉利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other controlling the Self means the host is fond of you; inner and outer generating each other is the best auspice; Self and Other in accord is auspicious without ill; with Pressure strong and the Body strong, joy is without obstruction. The note says: inner and outer generating each other, Self and Other in accord, and Pressure generating the Self line are all auspicious.
