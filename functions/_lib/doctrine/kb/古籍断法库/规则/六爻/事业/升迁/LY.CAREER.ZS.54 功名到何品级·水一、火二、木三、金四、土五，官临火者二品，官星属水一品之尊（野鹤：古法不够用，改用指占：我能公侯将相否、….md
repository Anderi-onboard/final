# 功名到何品级·水一、火二、木三、金四、土五，官临火者二品，官星属水一品之尊（野鹤：古法不够用，改用指占：我能公侯将相否、一品否、鼎甲否；旺官临日月指日可期，官破刑冲终身失望，子孙持世止渴望梅，官动生身探囊取物；书中例：小过官至佥事，节之蹇世变辰官应辰年鼎甲）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断官能做到几品：古法按五行的成数，水一、火二、木三、金四、土五，官星临在火上官位二品，官星属水是一品的尊位。野鹤说这个古法不够用：水只有一个数，官位到公侯的怎么确定？官分九品，数只到五，六品、七品的怎么确定？他的办法是指明了去占：我能做公侯将相吗？我能做一品大员吗？我能中鼎甲吗？旺相的官星临日月，指日可待；官星被破或被刑冲，终身失望；子孙持世，就像望梅止渴；官星发动生身，恰似探囊取物。问鼎甲这类具体的事，要另占一卦指明了问：古人以蹇卦为不吉，他只看用神，世临巳火、卯月生申日合、青龙持世，中鼎甲无疑；世爻变出辰土之官，应在辰年，后来果然在甲辰科殿试得传胪第一。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To judge to what grade an official will rise: the old method uses the numbers of the five elements, Water one, Fire two, Wood three, Metal four, Earth five, so that a star of office on Fire gives the second grade and a Water star of office the honoured first grade. The Old Man of Yehe says this old method is not enough: Water has only the number one, so how is the rank of dukes and marquises to be fixed? There are nine grades of office and the numbers stop at five, so how are the sixth and seventh to be fixed? His method is to name the matter and divine: can I become a duke, marquis, general or chancellor? Can I become a first-grade minister? Can I take a place among the top three? A thriving star of office on the Day or Month can be expected any day; if the star of office is broken, punished or clashed, one is disappointed for life; if Output holds the Self, it is like quenching thirst by gazing at plums; if the star of office moves to generate the Body, it is as easy as taking something from a bag. For a concrete matter such as the top three, another hexagram is cast with the matter named: the ancients took Jian as ominous, and he looked only at the Subject, where the Self on Si Fire generated by the Mao month and combined by the Shen day with the Azure Dragon holding the Self left no doubt of a top place; the Self turned into the Chen Earth star of office, so it fell in a Chen year, and at the palace examination of the Jiachen session he did come first at the announcement.

> [!note] 按
> 「方面」「佥事」「方伯」「开府」都是明清官职或提法，书里没有解释：方面是一省或一方的大员，佥事是按察司的属官，方伯是布政使，开府是出任总督巡抚、自立官署。「鼎甲」是殿试一甲三名（状元、榜眼、探花），「传胪」是殿试后二甲头名的唱名传呼。这里都是按通行的意思注。最后这个例子也讲科举，放在考试域里会再引到。
>
> *Note:* 'Fangmian', 'qianshi', 'fangbo' and 'kaifu' are Ming and Qing offices and terms that the book does not explain: a regional post is a senior office for a province or region, a qianshi is a subordinate of the provincial judicial commission, a fangbo is a provincial administration commissioner, and kaifu is to take up a governor-generalship or governorship with one's own office; 'dingjia' is the top three places of the palace examination (the first, second and third of the first class), and 'chuanlu' is the announcement of the head of the second class after it. They are all glossed here in the usual sense. The last example is also about the examinations and will be cited again in the examinations domain.
