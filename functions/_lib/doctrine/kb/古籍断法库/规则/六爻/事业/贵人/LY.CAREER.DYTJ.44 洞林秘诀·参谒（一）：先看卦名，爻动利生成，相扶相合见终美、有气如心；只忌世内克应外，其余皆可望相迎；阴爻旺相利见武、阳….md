# 洞林秘诀·参谒（一）：先看卦名，爻动利生成，相扶相合见终美、有气如心；只忌世内克应外，其余皆可望相迎；阴爻旺相利见武、阳爻升旺见文人

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 参见拜谒，先要看卦名；稍遇爻动，利于生成；相扶相合，见面终究美好；有气，就如心遂意。只忌世在内卦克应在外卦，其余都可以指望受到迎接；阴爻旺相，利于见武职的人；阳爻升旺，利于见文人。注文说：世爻克应爻、内卦克外卦，都不利相见；应生世、外克内，则他欢喜、盼我去求；阴爻旺相利见武职和平辈，阳爻旺相利见文职和尊长。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In calling on someone, first look at the name of the hexagram; a little movement of a line favours bringing it about; with mutual support and combination the meeting is in the end fair, and with qi it goes as the heart wishes. Only avoid the Self in the inner trigram controlling the Other in the outer; all the rest can hope to be welcomed; a strong yin line favours seeing military men and a rising, strong yang line favours seeing literati. The notes say: the Self line controlling the Other line, or the inner trigram controlling the outer, is unfavourable for meeting; if the Other generates the Self or the outer controls the inner, he is glad and hopes I will come and ask; a strong yin line favours seeing military officials and equals, a strong yang line civil officials and elders.
