# 朝廷国事：初爻属黎元吉曜当权培国本，二爻为士子文昌得令焕文章

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 初爻是百姓，吉星当权就能培固国家的根本；二爻是士人，文昌得令，文章就焕发光彩。初爻的百姓是国家的根本，喜欢临吉星；二爻的士人属文明，喜欢值文昌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The first line is the common people; if an auspicious star holds power, the root of the state is nurtured. The second line is the scholars; if Wenchang is in season, their writings shine. The people of the first line are the root of the state and like to sit on an auspicious star; the scholars of the second line belong to civilization and like to meet Wenchang.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 初爻为民、二爻为士，与易隐朝廷占的分配（初民、二士，YY.31）相同。文昌是一颗主文运的神煞。
>
> *Note:* The first line as the people and the second as the scholars is the same assignment as in the Yi Yin court divination (the first the people, the second the scholars, YY.31). Wenchang is a spirit-star of literary fortune.
