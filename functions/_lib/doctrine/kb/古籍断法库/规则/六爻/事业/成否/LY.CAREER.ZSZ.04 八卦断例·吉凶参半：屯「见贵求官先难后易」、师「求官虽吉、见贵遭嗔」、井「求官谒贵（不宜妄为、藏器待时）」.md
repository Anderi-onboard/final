# 八卦断例·吉凶参半：屯「见贵求官先难后易」、师「求官虽吉、见贵遭嗔」、井「求官谒贵（不宜妄为、藏器待时）」

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 遇到屯卦，见贵人、求官先难后易；遇到师卦，求官虽吉，见贵人却会遭到嗔怪；遇到井卦，不宜妄动，求官谒贵要藏好器具等待时机。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Meeting the hexagram Zhun, in seeing a noble and seeking office it is first hard, then easy; meeting Shi, seeking office is auspicious but seeing a noble draws his anger; meeting Jing, rash action does not suit, and in seeking office and calling on nobles one should keep the tools hidden and wait for the time.

## 《周易尚占·八卦断例章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 1066 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Chapter of judgments by example for the eight palaces", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 井者静也 | 井，就是静。 | Jing (the Well) means stillness. |
> | 不宜妄为 | 不宜乱作为。 | Rash action does not suit. |
> | 求官谒贵 | 求官、拜见贵人， | Seeking office, calling on nobles: |
> | 问病稽迟 | 问病，拖延迟缓。 | Asking about illness: delay and slowness. |
> | 田蚕半得 | 田蚕得一半。 | Fields and silkworms yield half. |
> | 行人未归 | 在外的人没有回来。 | The traveller has not returned. |
> | 占婚阻滞 | 占婚事，阻滞。 | Asking about marriage: obstruction and delay. |
> | 藏器待时 | 把本事藏起来，等待时机。 | Keep the tools hidden and wait for the time. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 井卦那一行里「求官谒贵」后面没有断语，同一行的前后是「不宜妄为」「藏器待时」，这里按整行的口气读成要等待时机，不确定。
>
> *Note:* In the line for Jing nothing follows 'seeking office, calling on nobles', and the same line has 'rash action does not suit' before it and 'keep the tools hidden and wait for the time' after it, so it is read here as meaning to wait for the time, which is uncertain.
