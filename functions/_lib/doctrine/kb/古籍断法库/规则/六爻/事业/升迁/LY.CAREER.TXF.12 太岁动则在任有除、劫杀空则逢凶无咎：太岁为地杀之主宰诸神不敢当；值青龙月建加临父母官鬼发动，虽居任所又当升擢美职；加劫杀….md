# 太岁动则在任有除、劫杀空则逢凶无咎：太岁为地杀之主宰诸神不敢当；值青龙月建加临父母官鬼发动，虽居任所又当升擢美职；加劫杀则当贬责；落空亡逢凶无咎

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁发动，在任的人就有任命、调动；劫杀落空，遇凶也没有祸咎。太岁是地杀的主宰，各神都不敢当它；如果碰上青龙、月建，加在父母、官鬼上发动，即使已在任所，也该升迁到好职位；如果加劫杀，就该受贬责；如果落空亡，遇凶也无咎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Tai Sui moves, one in office gets an appointment or transfer; when the Robbery Killing is void, meeting calamity brings no fault. Tai Sui is the master of the earthly baleful spirits, which no spirit dares withstand; if it meets the Azure Dragon and the Month, added to Resource and Pressure and moving, even one already at his post will be promoted to a fine office; if the Robbery Killing is added, one is to be demoted and reprimanded; if it falls Void, meeting calamity brings no fault.

> 卜筮大全本作「加临父母官鬼」「贬责」，断易天机本作「加临父官」「贬谪」。

> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan text reads 'added to Resource and Pressure' and 'demoted and reprimanded'; the Duanyi Tianji text reads 'added to father and official' and 'banished'.
