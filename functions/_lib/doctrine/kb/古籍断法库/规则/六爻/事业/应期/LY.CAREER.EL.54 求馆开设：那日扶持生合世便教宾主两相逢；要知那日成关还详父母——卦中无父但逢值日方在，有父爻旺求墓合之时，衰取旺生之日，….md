# 求馆开设：那日扶持生合世便教宾主两相逢；要知那日成关还详父母——卦中无父但逢值日方在，有父爻旺求墓合之时，衰取旺生之日，又看何日生世、空世亦可成关

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道哪一天定下这个馆，要细看父母爻：卦里有父爻而且旺相，取它入墓、逢合的时候；父爻衰弱，取它旺相、得生的日子；卦里没有父爻，要等到它当值的那一天才算在。还要看哪一天生世爻，世爻落空的也可以在那一天成。到了扶助、生合世爻的那一天，宾（师）主（东家）两方就会相见。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know on which day the engagement is settled, one must look closely at Resource: if Resource is in the hexagram and thriving, take the time when it enters the tomb or meets a combination; if it is weak, take the day when it thrives or is generated; if it is not in the hexagram, it is present only when the day on which it is on duty comes. Look also for the day that generates the Self; even a Void Self can then be settled. On the day that aids, generates or combines with the Self, guest and host come to meet each other.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 726 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 要知那日成关，还详父母。 | 要知道是哪一天定下这个馆，还要细看父母爻。 | To know on which day the engagement is settled, one must still look closely at Resource. |
> | 卦中无父，但逢值日方在。 | 卦中没有父爻的，要等到它当值的日子才算在。 | If there is no Resource in the hexagram, it is present only when the day on which it is on duty comes. |
> | 如有父爻旺，求墓合之时。 | 如果有父爻而且旺相，就要找它入墓、逢合的时候。 | If Resource is present and thriving, look for the time when it enters the tomb or meets a combination. |
> | 衰取旺生之日，又看何日生世、空世亦可成关。 | 父爻衰弱的，取它旺相、得生的日子；又要看哪一天生世爻；世爻落空的，也可以成就这个馆。 | If it is weak, take the day when it thrives or is generated; look also for the day that generates the Self; even with the Self Void the engagement can be settled. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「成关」的「关」应是关书（聘请塾师的聘书），「成关」就是把聘定下来；书里没有解释，这里按这个意思译。「卦中无父，但逢值日方在」按字面译：卦里没有父爻，要等到它当值的日子才算在；书里没有说「当值」按什么取，这里不加解释。「求墓合之时」的「墓」是父爻的墓库，「合」是与父爻相合的地支（见术语表）。末句「空世亦可成关」字面是世爻落空也能成；前面说世空遇不到尊从，这里说的是逢到生世的日子仍可成就，两处并不相冲。这一条与易隐觅馆占「关约何日来」（YY.81）取同一个法：旺则墓合日、衰则生旺日、无父则值日，又看生合世之日。
>
> *Note:* The 'guan' of 'cheng guan' is presumably the 'guanshu', the letter of engagement by which a tutor is hired, and 'cheng guan' is the engagement being settled; the book does not explain it, and the translation takes it so. 'If there is no Resource in the hexagram, it is present only when the day on which it is on duty comes' is translated literally: the book does not say by what the 'duty' is reckoned, and nothing is added here. In 'qiu mu he zhi shi' the 'tomb' is the tomb of the Resource line and the 'combination' is the branch that combines with it (see the glossary). The last sentence, 'a Void Self too can settle the engagement', literally says that a Void Self can still be settled; earlier it said that a Void Self meets no one who honours and follows one, and what is said here is that on a day that generates the Self it can still be accomplished, so the two places do not clash. This entry takes the same method as 'on what day the engagement comes' in the Yi Yin divination for seeking a post (YY.81): a thriving line by the tomb or combination day, a weak one by a generating or thriving day, a missing one by its duty day, and also the day that generates or combines with the Self.
