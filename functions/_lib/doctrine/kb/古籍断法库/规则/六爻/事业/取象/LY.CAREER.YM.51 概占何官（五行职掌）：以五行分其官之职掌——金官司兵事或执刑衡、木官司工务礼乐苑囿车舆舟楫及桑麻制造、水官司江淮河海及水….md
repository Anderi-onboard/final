# 概占何官（五行职掌）：以五行分其官之职掌——金官司兵事或执刑衡、木官司工务礼乐苑囿车舆舟楫及桑麻制造、水官司江淮河海及水利盐务税饷漕储、火官司文章参劾祭祀及冶铸喉舌之权、土官司土地农田社稷边疆及陵寝屯守之柄

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用五行来分这个官的职掌：金官必管兵事，或执掌刑罚法度；木官必管工务、礼乐、苑囿、车舆舟楫，以及桑麻、制造之事；水官必管江淮河海，以及水利盐务、税饷漕储；火官必管文章、弹劾、祭祀，以及冶铸、喉舌的权柄；土官必管土地农田、社稷边疆，以及陵寝、屯守的权柄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* By the five phases one divides the duties of the office: a Metal official must govern military affairs, or hold the scales of punishment; a Wood official must govern public works, rites and music, parks, carriages and boats, and the affairs of mulberry, hemp and manufacture; a Water official must govern the rivers, the Huai, the Yellow River and the seas, and waterworks, the salt administration, tax and army supplies, and grain transport; a Fire official must govern letters, impeachment and sacrifice, and the powers of smelting and casting and of being the sovereign's mouthpiece; an Earth official must govern land and farmland, the altars of the state and the borders, and the powers over imperial tombs and garrison farms.

> [!note] 按
> 「刑衡」是刑罚的轻重衡量，即执法。「苑囿」是皇家园林。「车舆舟楫」是车和船。「桑麻」是农桑。「江淮河海」是长江、淮河、黄河、海。「盐务」是盐政，「税饷」是税收和军饷，「漕储」是漕运和仓储。「参劾」是弹劾。「冶铸」是冶炼铸造。「喉舌」是替君主传达政令的权。「陵寝」是帝王陵墓。「屯守」是屯田和守卫。这一条和黄金策仕宦的取象（HJC.20）、易隐官禄占的分爻、何官里按五行定部门的一条（YY.05）可对着看，各家配的行业不尽相同。
>
> *Note:* 'Xingheng' is the weighing of the lightness or heaviness of punishments, that is, enforcing the law. 'Yuanyou' are the imperial gardens. 'Chejuzhouji' are carriages and boats. 'Sangma' are farming and sericulture. 'Jianghuaihehai' are the Yangtze, the Huai, the Yellow River and the sea. 'Yanwu' is salt administration, 'shuixiang' is taxes and army pay, and 'caochu' is grain transport and granaries. 'Canhe' is impeachment. 'Yezhu' is smelting and casting. 'Houshe' is the power of conveying the ruler's orders. 'Lingqin' are imperial tombs. 'Tunshou' is garrison farming and guarding. This entry can be read against the images in the Huangjin Ce on official careers (HJC.20) and the entry in the Yi Yin that fixes the department by phase (YY.05); the trades each school assigns are not entirely the same.
