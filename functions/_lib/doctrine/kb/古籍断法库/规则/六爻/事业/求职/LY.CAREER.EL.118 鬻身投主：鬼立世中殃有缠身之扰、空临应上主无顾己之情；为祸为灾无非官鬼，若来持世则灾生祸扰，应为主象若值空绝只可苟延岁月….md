# 鬻身投主：鬼立世中殃有缠身之扰、空临应上主无顾己之情；为祸为灾无非官鬼，若来持世则灾生祸扰，应为主象若值空绝只可苟延岁月是无作养厚情

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼立在世爻上，灾殃缠身、多受烦扰；空亡临在应爻上，主人没有照顾自己的情意。招祸招灾的，无非是官鬼；如果它来持世，就灾生祸扰。应爻是主人的象，如果遇到空、绝，只能苟延岁月，没有养育厚待的情分。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure stands on the Self, calamity entangles one's person with trouble; if Void rests on the Other, the master has no feeling of caring for oneself. What brings misfortune and disaster is nothing but Pressure, and if it comes to hold the Self then disaster arises and misfortune troubles one; the Other is the image of the master, and if it meets Void or extinction one can only drag out one's days, for there is no generous feeling of nurture.

> [!note] 按
> 「缠身之扰」是灾祸缠在身上。「作养」是养育、提携。官鬼在这一章是灾祸之象（「为祸为灾，无非官鬼」），不是官职，与前面求官各章里官鬼是用神、是官职不同。
>
> *Note:* 'Chanshen zhi rao' is calamity clinging to one's person. 'Zuoyang' is nurture and support. In this chapter Pressure is the image of calamity ('what brings misfortune and disaster is nothing but Pressure') and not an official post, which differs from the earlier chapters on seeking office where Pressure is the Subject and the official post.
