# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（九）：更得雷火杀重重，名位至王公；天乙贵人更相合，代代为官受赠封；注：鬼谷子雷火杀例——正月起寅、二月起丑、三月起子……逆数十二位

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里再得雷火杀、重重叠叠，眼看名位将到王公；天乙贵人再来相合，世世代代做官、受封赠。这是天纲玄妙的法门，请你仔细抽绎，不要匆忙。注文给了鬼谷子的雷火杀例：雷火在寅、亥、申、巳里找；它的算法是正月起寅、二月起丑、三月起子、四月起亥、五月起戌、六月起酉、七月起申、八月起未、九月起午、十月起巳、十一月起辰、十二月起卯，逆数十二位。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram further gets the Thunder-Fire baleful spirit, layer upon layer, soon rank and position reach prince and duke; if the Tianyi Noble further combines, one holds office and receives imperial honours generation after generation. This is the profound method of Tiangang, and I ask you to unravel it carefully and not in haste. The note gives the Guigu-zi rule for the Thunder-Fire spirit: look for it among Yin, Hai, Shen and Si; its method is Yin in the first month, Chou in the second, Zi in the third, Hai in the fourth, Xu in the fifth, You in the sixth, Shen in the seventh, Wei in the eighth, Wu in the ninth, Si in the tenth, Chen in the eleventh and Mao in the twelfth, counting backwards through the twelve places.

> [!note] 按
> 这条注把雷火杀的起法完整写了出来（逆数十二位，正月寅、二月丑……十二月卯）；它和卜筮大全旧注里「正月起寅，二月丑，逆数十二位」是同一种起法，说明旧注前面按「寅亥申巳」四孟轮转的那一句与它不是一套。诗里这一句，卜筮大全作「四孟从之遍一周」，断易天机作「四孟先知惊恐忧」。这一本也没有给天乙贵人的起法（卜筮大全旧注按月份取巳申亥寅）。「请君绎」缺一个字，卜筮大全作「劝君紬绎」。
>
> *Note:* This note writes out the method for the Thunder-Fire spirit in full (counting backwards: Yin in the first month, Chou in the second, down to Mao in the twelfth); it is the same method as the 'begin at Yin in the first month, Chou in the second, counting backwards through the twelve places' of the note in the Bushi Daquan, which shows that the earlier sentence there, running through the four Meng branches Yin, Hai, Shen and Si, is not the same scheme. This line of the song reads in the Bushi Daquan 'follow the four Meng until a full circuit is made' and in the Duanyi Tianji 'among the four Meng first know of fright and worry'. This book also gives no method for the Tianyi Noble (the note in the Bushi Daquan finds it by the month through Si, Shen, Hai, Yin). 'Qing jun yi' lacks a character, which the Bushi Daquan has in 'quan jun chouyi'.
