# 求馆·出现不伤，旺相生身，名曰吉；入空无救，休囚化绝，号为凶（旧注：占束修财爻出现旺相不空、月日动爻不带兄弟伤克则不缺欠，财生世合世大吉；财被克劫、居绝地、空亡、变死墓绝皆不遂意；世持兄克尤难取讨；子孙旺动亦好）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占束修，财爻出现、旺相不空，月建、日辰、动爻又不带兄弟来伤克，就不缺欠，再得财爻生世、合世，是大吉的兆头；如果财爻虽然出现，却被克被劫，或处在绝地，或空亡，或变出死、墓、绝的爻，都不如意；世爻临着兄弟来克财，尤其难讨；得子孙旺相发动，也好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining the tuition fee, if Wealth appears, thriving and not Void, and the Month, the Day and the moving lines do not carry Peer to injure or control it, there is no shortfall, and if Wealth also generates or combines with the Self it is a greatly auspicious sign; if Wealth appears but is controlled or robbed, or stands in a place of extinction, or is Void, or turns into a line of death, tomb or extinction, nothing is as one wishes; if the Self rests on Peer and it controls Wealth, collecting is especially hard; and if Output is thriving and moves, that is also good.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一联的经文是求馆章收尾处对「吉凶」的总括（出现不伤旺相是吉、空无救休囚化绝是凶）；卜筮正宗没有这一联，它的相应意思并在 LY.CAREER.HJC.55 的王洪绪一段里。旧注「世持兄克，尤难取讨」是说世爻临着兄弟而兄弟又克财，不是说世克兄弟。
>
> *Note:* The verse is a summary of good and ill at the end of the chapter's account of the tuition fee (appearing, uninjured and thriving is auspicious; Void, unrescued, resting or turning into extinction is ominous); the Bushi Zhengzong does not have this couplet, and its sense is in Wang Hongxu's passage in LY.CAREER.HJC.55. The old commentary's 'the Self holds Peer and it controls Wealth, collecting is especially hard' means the Self rests on Peer and Peer controls Wealth, not that the Self controls Peer.
