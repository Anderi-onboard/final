# 求馆·阳卦阳爻居养位，座前有刘恕之神童；阴宫阴象化财爻，帐后列马融之女乐（旧注：子孙在阳宫阳爻临胎养及金水旺相不空有扶者，其徒有出类拔萃；在阴宫阴爻又化财必有女儿受学，不化财而在兑宫亦有女徒）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙在阳宫阳爻，又临胎、养以及金、水两爻，旺相不空，有扶助的，他的学生必有出类拔萃的，像刘恕那样的神童在门下；如果在阴宫阴爻，又化出财爻的，必有女孩来受学，不化出财爻而在兑宫的，也有女学生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output is in a yang palace on a yang line and rests on the Embryo or Nurture stage or on a Metal or Water line, thriving and not Void and supported, there will surely be an outstanding pupil among them, a child prodigy like Liu Shu at the door; if it is in a yin palace on a yin line and also turns into Wealth, there will surely be girls who come to study, and if it does not turn into Wealth but is in the Dui palace, there are also girl pupils.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2781 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙在阳宫阳爻，而临金水旺相不空，有拱扶者，其徒必有出类拔萃，如刘恕之神童在门； | 子孙在阳宫阳爻，而临金、水旺相不空，有拱扶的，他的学生必有出类拔萃的，像刘恕那样的神童在门下； | If Output is in a yang palace on a yang line and rests on Metal or Water, thriving and not Void and flanked and supported, there will surely be an outstanding pupil among them, a child prodigy like Liu Shu at the door; |
> | 若阴宫阴爻，主有女徒受学。 | 如果是阴宫阴爻，主有女学生来受学。 | if it is in a yin palace on a yin line, there will be girl pupils who come to study. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「刘恕」是宋代以博学著称的人（从小聪颖，十三岁就通经史），「马融」是东汉经学家，讲学时帐后常有女乐，所以用来比有女学生；这里的「女乐」是借典故说「有女徒」，不是说真的有歌女。「胎、养」是十二长生里的两个阶段（胎、养是幼小的阶段），所以「居养位」是说子孙爻落在幼小的阶段，正好对应「神童」。王洪绪没有写胎养、不化财而在兑宫这几句。
>
> *Note:* 'Liu Shu' was a man of the Song famed for his learning (clever from childhood and master of the classics and histories at thirteen), and 'Ma Rong' was a classicist of the Eastern Han who had women musicians behind the curtain when he lectured, so he is used for having girl pupils; the 'women musicians' here borrow the story to say 'there are girl pupils' and do not mean singing girls. 'Embryo' and 'Nurture' are two stages of the twelve life stages (the stages of the very young), so 'in the Nurture stage' means the Output line falls in a stage of youth, which fits a 'child prodigy'. Wang Hongxu does not write the Embryo and Nurture stages or the case of not turning into Wealth but being in the Dui palace.
