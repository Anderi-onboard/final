# 道业·六神所主：螣蛇尘障不除、勾陈夙缘不断、白虎元武灾盗之由、朱雀青龙才名之士，必因鬼动而云凶、福动而云吉（如朱雀摇鬼为是非、动福为名誉）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 螣蛇，是尘世的障碍不除；勾陈，是旧缘不断；白虎、元武，是灾祸盗贼的由头；朱雀、青龙，是有才有名的人；大概必须因鬼动才说凶，福动才说吉。例如朱雀带着摇动的鬼，是是非；朱雀动福，是名誉，之类。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Coiling Snake means the hindrances of the dust of the world are not removed; Hook Earth means old ties are not cut; the White Tiger and the Dark Tortoise are the cause of calamity and theft; the Vermilion Bird and the Azure Dragon are men of talent and name; generally one says ominous only because Pressure moves, and auspicious only because Output moves. For example, the Vermilion Bird with a moving Pressure is disputes; the Vermilion Bird with Output moving is renown, and so on.

> [!note] 按
> 「尘障」是尘世的障碍，「夙缘」是前世留下的缘分。「元武」是玄武。「灾盗之由」是灾祸、盗贼的由头，「才名之士」是有才华、有名望的人。「必因鬼动而云凶，福动而云吉」是六神自己的吉凶，要等鬼动或福动才算数：六神只是底色，说凶说吉由动的是鬼还是福来定。书里自注的例子：朱雀带动的鬼是是非口舌，朱雀动福是名誉。六神的本义见术语表，这里是把它们取到修道人的处境上。
>
> *Note:* 'Chenzhang' is the hindrances of the dust of the world, and 'suyuan' is the ties left from earlier lives. 'Yuanwu' is the Dark Tortoise. 'The cause of calamity and theft' is the source of calamity and thieves, and 'men of talent and name' are people of talent and reputation. 'One says ominous only because Pressure moves, and auspicious only because Output moves' is that the luck of the six spirits themselves counts only when Pressure or Output moves: the six spirits are only the ground colour, and whether one speaks of ominous or auspicious is fixed by whether it is Pressure or Output that moves. The book's own example: the Vermilion Bird with a moving Pressure is disputes and talk, and the Vermilion Bird with Output moving is renown. The original meanings of the six spirits are in the glossary; here they are taken onto the circumstances of one who practises.
