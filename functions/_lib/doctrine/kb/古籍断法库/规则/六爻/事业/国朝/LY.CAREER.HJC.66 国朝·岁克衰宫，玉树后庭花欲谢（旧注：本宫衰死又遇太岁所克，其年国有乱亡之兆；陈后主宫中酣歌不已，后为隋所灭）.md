# 国朝·岁克衰宫，玉树后庭花欲谢（旧注：本宫衰死又遇太岁所克，其年国有乱亡之兆；陈后主宫中酣歌不已，后为隋所灭）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本宫衰弱死绝，又被太岁克，这一年国家有动乱灭亡的征兆，就像陈后主在宫里日夜歌舞饮宴，后来被隋朝灭掉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the home palace is weak or dead and is also controlled by Tai Sui, the state shows signs of disorder and ruin that year, like the last ruler of Chen, who gave himself to songs and revels in the palace and was later destroyed by Sui.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5822 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 岁克衰宫，玉树后庭花欲谢； | 太岁克制衰弱的本宫，就像《玉树后庭花》将要凋谢； | If Tai Sui controls a weak home palace, it is as if 'Jade Trees and Flowers of the Rear Court' were about to fade; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5823 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 本宫衰死，更遇太岁所克，其年国有乱亡之兆。 | 本宫衰弱死绝，又遇到太岁来克，这一年国家有动乱灭亡的征兆。 | If the home palace is weak or dead and meets control from Tai Sui, the state shows signs of disorder and ruin that year. |
> | 昔陈将亡，后主使诸妃嫔，及女乐与侠客，共赋诗，互相赠答。 | 从前陈朝将要灭亡的时候，陈后主让众妃嫔，连同歌女乐伎和游侠宾客，一起作诗，互相赠答。 | Long ago, when Chen was about to fall, its last ruler had his consorts, together with the singing women and the wandering gallant guests, compose poems together and exchange them with one another. |
> | 采其尤艳丽者，被以新声； | 挑出其中最艳丽的诗句，配上新曲； | They picked the most gorgeous of the verses and set them to new tunes; |
> | 选宫女千余，习而歌之，分部迭奏。 | 选了一千多名宫女，学着歌唱，分成几部轮流演奏。 | they chose over a thousand palace women to learn and sing them, and the groups performed in turn. |
> | 其曲有玉树后庭花，临春乐等，大略皆美诸嫔妃之容色。 | 那些曲子有《玉树后庭花》《临春乐》等，大致都是赞美各位妃嫔的容貌姿色。 | Among the pieces were 'Jade Trees and Flowers of the Rear Court' and 'Linchun Le', which in the main all praised the beauty of the consorts. |
> | 君臣酣歌，自夕达旦，以此为常。 | 君臣尽兴唱歌饮酒，从晚上到天亮，一直这样成了常事。 | Ruler and ministers sang and drank to their heart's content from evening until dawn, and made it a habit. |
> | 后为隋所灭。 | 后来陈被隋灭掉了。 | Later Chen was destroyed by Sui. |
> | 故曰：玉树后庭花欲谢也。 | 所以说：《玉树后庭花》将要凋谢了。 | That is why it says: 'Jade Trees and Flowers of the Rear Court' is about to fade. |

^q2
