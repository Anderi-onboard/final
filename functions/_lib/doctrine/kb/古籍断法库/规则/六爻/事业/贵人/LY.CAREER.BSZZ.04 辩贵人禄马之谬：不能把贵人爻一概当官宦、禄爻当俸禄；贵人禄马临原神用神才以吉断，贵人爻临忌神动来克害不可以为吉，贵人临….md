# 辩贵人禄马之谬：不能把贵人爻一概当官宦、禄爻当俸禄；贵人禄马临原神用神才以吉断，贵人爻临忌神动来克害不可以为吉，贵人临官鬼爻持世也不可把官鬼当祸患言凶

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 现在的人多拿贵人的爻当官宦，拿禄爻当俸禄，拿驿马当来人，一概这样论，不知道贵人禄马临在原神、用神上，才应当以吉断。比如占终身，白虎临官爻持世，又有贵人临它，应当许他武职功名；如果没有贵人并临，应当断为病患强暴。如果贵人的爻临在忌神上动来克害，就不能把贵人当吉；如果贵人临在官鬼爻持世，就不能把官鬼爻当祸患说凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* People today mostly take the Noble line for an official, the Salary line for a stipend and the Post Horse for a visitor, and judge across the board; they do not know that a Noble, Salary or Post Horse is judged lucky only when it sits on the Support or the Subject. For example, in divining for a whole life, the White Tiger sits on the Pressure line holding the Self and a Noble sits on it too, so a military office and name should be promised; if no Noble sits with it, it should be judged as illness and violence. If the Noble line sits on the Adversary and moves to control and harm, the Noble cannot be taken as lucky; if the Noble sits on the Pressure line holding the Self, the Pressure line cannot be called a source of calamity and ill.
