# 援监纳吏：夫化子孙难出仕、妻之兄弟利源伤；官爻若化子孙又难出仕，妻财如变兄弟或卦中兄爻发动不惟无所得反主亏伤

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官（夫）爻化成子孙，就难以出来做官；妻财变成兄弟，财路就被伤。官爻如果化成子孙，又难出仕；妻财如果变成兄弟，或者卦中兄爻发动，不但没有所得，反而主亏损受伤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the husband line, Pressure, changes into Output, it is hard to come out into office; if Wealth, the wife, changes into Peer, the source of profit is hurt. If the Pressure line changes into Output it is again hard to come out into office; if Wealth changes into Peer, or a Peer line moves in the hexagram, one gains nothing, and on the contrary suffers loss and injury.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 诀里的「夫」按解里的「官爻」译：六亲里官鬼是夫、妻财是妻，诀把两个放成一对，解里就写成「官爻」「妻财」。「利源」是财路。这一条与前面仕宦升迁「官化子孙非降即调」（EL.56）、「财化兄弟减俸粮」（EL.57）取同一个关系。
>
> *Note:* The 'fu', husband, in the verse is translated after 'the Pressure line' in the explanation: among the six kin Pressure is the husband and Wealth the wife, and the verse puts the two as a pair, while the explanation writes them as 'the Pressure line' and 'Wealth'. 'Liyuan' is the source of profit. This entry takes the same relations as 'if Pressure changes into Output it is demotion or transfer' (EL.56) and 'Wealth changing into Peer cuts salary and grain' (EL.57) in the preceding chapter on official promotion.
