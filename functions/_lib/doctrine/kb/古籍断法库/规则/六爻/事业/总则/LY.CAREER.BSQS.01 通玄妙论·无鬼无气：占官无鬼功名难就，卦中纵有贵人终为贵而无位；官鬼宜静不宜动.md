# 通玄妙论·无鬼无气：占官无鬼功名难就，卦中纵有贵人终为贵而无位；官鬼宜静不宜动

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求官的卦里不能没有官鬼：没有官鬼，功名难成，卦里纵然有贵人，也只是有贵人而没有位子。官鬼这一爻宜安静不宜发动：带着吉神动是福，加上凶煞动是祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A divination for office must not lack the Pressure line: without it name and rank are hard to achieve, and even with a Noble in the hexagram one is a noble without a post. The Pressure line should be still and not moving: moving with an auspicious spirit it is a blessing, moving with a baleful one it is a calamity.

> | 卦中不可无， | 卦里不可以没有它， | in a hexagram it must not be missing, |

> | 亦能为福； | 也能成为福； | it can also be a blessing; |
> | 加凶煞动， | 加上凶煞发动， | moving with a baleful spirit, |

## 《卜筮全书·通玄妙论·一、无鬼无气》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2799 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 1: no Pressure, no spirit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占官无鬼， | 占求官的时候，卦里没有官鬼， | In divining for office, when there is no Pressure line, |
> | 功名难就； | 功名难以成就； | name and rank are hard to achieve; |
> | 卦中纵有贵人， | 卦里纵然有贵人， | even if the hexagram holds a Noble, |
> | 终为贵而无位。 | 到头来也只是有贵人而没有位子。 | he is in the end a noble without a post. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 同一段的下文还讲到占身、占婚、求财、占宅、占病、占失脱没有官鬼是什么样，那几件不是事业，没有收；求财无鬼那一句里「主在他人手下趁财」是给人做事挣钱的意思，放在求财域。这里的「功名」是求官职、求名位。
>
> *Note:* The rest of the passage says what it means to lack the Pressure line when divining for one's person, marriage, wealth, the house, illness and lost goods; those are not career matters and are not included. In the line on seeking wealth, "making money under someone else's hand" means working for another and belongs to the wealth domain. "Name and rank" here means seeking an official post and standing.
