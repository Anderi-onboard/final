# 听轰雷之战鼓，金爻发动逢空；看如雨之炮砖，土位交重逢旺：土为炮石旺相临未上发动方可言，带大杀必遭伤害、遇退神防自损；金空则鸣动处逢冲愈鸣，发动必然金鼓轰雷须防战斗；金爻发动不空亡刀刃森森明似雪，内动我器械备具外动彼兵刃整齐；金爻发动逢刑克兵甲虽坚不损人、金爻无气逢刑克甲胄干戈不整齐

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要听战鼓如轰雷，就看金爻发动逢空；要看炮石砖块如雨，就看土位交重逢旺。土是炮石，要旺相、落在未上发动，才可以这样说；带大杀，必遭伤害；遇退神，要防自损。金空就鸣，发动之处逢冲鸣得更厉害；发动，必然金鼓轰雷，要防战斗。金爻发动而不空亡，刀刃森森明亮如雪；内卦动，我方器械齐备；外卦动，对方兵刃整齐。金爻发动逢刑克，兵甲虽坚却伤不了人；金爻无气又逢刑克，甲胄干戈不整齐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To hear war drums like rolling thunder, look for the Metal line moving and meeting the void; to see cannon stones and bricks like rain, look for the Earth place moving heavily and meeting strength. Earth is the catapult stone, and only when it is strong and moves while standing on Wei can this be said; if it carries the Great Baleful Spirit one is sure to suffer harm; if it meets a Retreating spirit, guard against harming oneself. Metal rings when void, and rings all the more where it moves and meets a clash; if it moves, gongs and drums are sure to thunder, and one must guard against fighting. A Metal line moving and not Void: blades gleam in rows, bright as snow; moving inside, our weapons are fully equipped; moving outside, their blades are in good order. A Metal line moving and meeting punishment or control: the arms and armour, though sturdy, injure no one; a Metal line without qi and meeting punishment or control: helmets, armour and halberds are not in order.
