# 出师征伐：杀不克身值交重无大害，杀如伤世虽居安静且休征；杀即大煞（劫煞亡神小白虎大白虎，官鬼亦作杀神）；诸煞旺动不克身世无大害，不旺不动而所克害身世必生祸，旺动克身世祸骤来；杀并日辰冲克静世名暗动，有阴谋窃劫之患；大煞劫煞亡神小白虎大白虎的起例

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 煞不克身，即使重叠发动也没有大害；煞如果伤世，虽然在安静中也暂且不要出征。「杀」就是大煞，有劫煞，也有亡神，又有小白虎、大白虎，官鬼也当作杀神。如果各煞或旺相或发动、但不克身爻、世爻，必定没有大害；如果不旺不动，而所克的（爻）有害于身爻、世爻，必定生祸，要谨慎提防；如果各煞或旺相或发动来克身、世，祸会骤然来到，绝非小事。如果煞和日辰一起冲克安静的世爻，叫作暗动，必有阴谋窃劫的祸患；想察觉什么时候来害，须推官鬼和煞生旺的时期。所以经书说「福来而不知，祸来而不觉」，正是说这个。大煞、劫煞的起例已经注在前面；亡神煞的起例说：正月起亥顺行十二位；小白虎，就是庚、辛日起白虎；大白虎，是正月起申顺行十二位。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a baleful spirit does not control the Body, even if it moves in double there is no great harm; if a baleful spirit injures the Self, even if it is still, for the time being do not set out. 'Sha' is the great baleful spirit, and there are Robbery, the Lost Spirit, the Lesser White Tiger and the Greater White Tiger, and Pressure too is counted as a baleful spirit. If the various baleful spirits are thriving or moving but do not control the Body and Self, there is surely no great harm; if they are neither thriving nor moving but what they control does harm to the Body and Self, disaster surely arises and one must guard carefully; if the baleful spirits are thriving or moving and come to control the Body and Self, the disaster comes suddenly and is no small matter. If a baleful spirit joins the Day in clashing with and controlling a still Self, it is called secret motion, and there will surely be the danger of plots and plunder; to find out when the harm will come, work out the period when Pressure and the baleful spirit are born or thriving. So the classic says 'fortune comes and one does not know, disaster comes and one does not feel', which says exactly this. The setting out of the great baleful spirit and Robbery is noted earlier; the setting out of the Lost Spirit says: from the first month start at Hai and go through the twelve places; the Lesser White Tiger is the White Tiger starting on Geng and Xin days; the Greater White Tiger starts at Shen in the first month and goes through the twelve places.

> [!note] 按
> 「身世」是身爻和世爻。「暗动」是静爻被日辰冲动（见术语表）。「小白虎」「大白虎」是两种神煞，书里写了起例：小白虎庚辛日起白虎（日干在庚、辛时白虎位置的算法），大白虎正月起申顺行。第二个「若诸杀」那一句里「不旺不动而所克」的主语省略，译文按文意写成「所克的（爻）」。这一条与易隐征战占「彼己」里的「鬼暗动加大杀劫杀刑刃克世防刺客」（YY.56）取同一件事。
>
> *Note:* 'Shishen' is the Body and Self lines. 'Secret motion' is a still line clashed into motion by the Day (see the glossary). The 'Lesser White Tiger' and the 'Greater White Tiger' are two spirit-stars whose setting out the book writes: the Lesser starts on Geng and Xin days (the rule for the position of the White Tiger when the day stem is Geng or Xin), and the Greater starts at Shen in the first month. The subject of 'neither thriving nor moving but what it controls' in the second sentence is left out, and the translation follows the sense as 'what it controls'. This entry takes up the same matter as 'if Pressure moves in secret with a great baleful spirit, Robbery, punishment and the blade and controls the Self, guard against assassins' in the Yi Yin 'them and us' section (YY.56).
