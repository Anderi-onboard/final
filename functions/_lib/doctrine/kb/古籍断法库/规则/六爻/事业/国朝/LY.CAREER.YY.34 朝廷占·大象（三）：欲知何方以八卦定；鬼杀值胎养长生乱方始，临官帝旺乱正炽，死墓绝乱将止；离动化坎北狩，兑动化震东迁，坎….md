# 朝廷占·大象（三）：欲知何方以八卦定；鬼杀值胎养长生乱方始，临官帝旺乱正炽，死墓绝乱将止；离动化坎北狩，兑动化震东迁，坎动化离南渡，震动化兑西征；逢六合安静太平，逢六冲乱动多变故

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大象，方位、祸乱的阶段和迁徙：想知道事情在哪个方向，用八卦来定。官鬼和凶煞值胎、养、长生，祸乱刚开始；值临官、帝旺，祸乱正炽盛；值死、墓、绝，祸乱将要平息。离卦发动变坎卦，君主将要北狩；兑卦发动变震卦，想往东迁；坎卦发动变离卦，想往南渡；震卦发动变兑卦，必定西征。卦遇六合，安静的太平；遇六冲、乱动的，变故很多。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The great image, direction, the stage of the disorder, and moving the court. To know in which direction, fix it by the eight trigrams. If Pressure and a baleful spirit are in the stages of Tai, Yang or Birth, the disorder is just beginning; in Official Post or Emperor's Peak, the disorder is at its height; in death, tomb or extinction, the disorder is about to cease. Li moving and turning into Kan: the sovereign will go on a northern hunt; Dui moving and turning into Zhen: he thinks of moving east; Kan moving and turning into Li: he wishes to cross to the south; Zhen moving and turning into Dui: he will surely march west. If the hexagram is a six-combine and still, there is peace; if it meets the six-clash and moves wildly, there are many upheavals.

> [!note] 按
> 「北狩」是天子被迫离开京城、出走或被掳往北方的委婉说法（宋徽宗、钦宗和明英宗都被说成北狩）；「南渡」是朝廷迁往南方；「东迁」「西征」按字面。四句是按八卦方位配的：离在南、坎在北、兑在西、震在东，变成的那一卦所在的方位，就是去的方向。这个读法是书里没有明写的，译文只照字面译四句。「胎养长生」「临官帝旺」是十二长生的位置，「六合」「六冲」指卦的六合、六冲（见术语表）。
>
> *Note:* 'Beishou', the northern hunt, is the euphemism for the Son of Heaven being driven out of the capital, fleeing or being carried off to the north (the Huizong and Qinzong emperors of the Song and the Yingzong emperor of the Ming are all said to have gone on a northern hunt); 'nandu' is the court crossing to the south; 'eastward move' and 'westward march' are by the letter. The four sentences are matched with the directions of the eight trigrams: Li is south, Kan north, Dui west and Zhen east, and the direction of the trigram it changes into is the direction he goes. This reading is not written out in the book, and the translation follows only the letter of the four sentences. 'Tai, Yang, Birth' and 'Official Post, Emperor's Peak' are positions of the twelve stages, and 'six-combine' and 'six-clash' are the hexagram patterns (see the glossary).
