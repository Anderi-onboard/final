# 国朝·君位克伤四位，商纣害比干之忠（旧注：四爻为臣，持财子而被世爻动克，是犯颜直谏之臣遇剖心之主，如比干尽忠被纣王所杀）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 第四爻是臣。四爻持着妻财或子孙，却被世爻发动来克，是敢于冒犯君主、当面直谏的臣子，遇到了剖人心的君主，就像比干尽忠而被商纣王杀害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The fourth line is the minister. If the fourth line holds Wealth or Output and is moved and controlled by the Self, it is a minister who dares to offend the ruler's face with plain advice meeting a ruler who cuts out hearts, like Bi Gan, who was loyal to the end and was put to death by King Zhou of Shang.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5879 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 君位克伤四位，商纣害比干之忠。 | 君位克伤第四爻，就像商纣王加害比干的忠心。 | If the ruler's place controls and injures the fourth place, it is like King Zhou of Shang harming the loyalty of Bi Gan. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1502 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 四爻为臣，持财子而被君爻克伤，如比干之尽忠，而被纣王之诛也。 | 第四爻是臣，持着财、子孙而被君爻克伤，就像比干尽忠，却被纣王杀害。 | The fourth line is the minister; if it holds Wealth or Output and is controlled and injured by the ruler's line, it is like Bi Gan, who was loyal to the end and was put to death by King Zhou. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说四爻「被世爻动克」，卜筮正宗说「被君爻克伤」；前面分派各爻时，君爻是太岁，世爻不是，两处用的不是同一爻。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says the fourth line is 'moved and controlled by the Self', the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says it is 'controlled and injured by the ruler's line'; in the assignment of the lines the ruler's line is Tai Sui and not the Self, so the two do not use the same line.
