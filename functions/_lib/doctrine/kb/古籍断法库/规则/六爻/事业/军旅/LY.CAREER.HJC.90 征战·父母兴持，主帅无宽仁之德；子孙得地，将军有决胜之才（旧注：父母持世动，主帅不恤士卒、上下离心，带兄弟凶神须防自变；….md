# 征战·父母兴持，主帅无宽仁之德；子孙得地，将军有决胜之才（旧注：父母持世动，主帅不恤士卒、上下离心，带兄弟凶神须防自变；子孙持世身旺动，将军必决胜千里外）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻持世发动，主帅不体恤士兵，上下离心，如果又带着兄弟或凶神，要防备内部自己生变；子孙持世、旺相发动，将军一定能在千里之外决定胜负。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Resource holds the Self and moves, the commander has no kindness for his soldiers and high and low are of different minds, and if it also carries Peer or a baleful spirit one must guard against a change from within; if Output holds the Self and is thriving and moves, the general will surely decide the victory from a thousand li away.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5915 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母兴持，主帅无宽仁之德； | 父母爻兴旺而持世，主帅没有宽厚仁爱的德行； | If Resource is thriving and holds the Self, the commander lacks the virtue of leniency and kindness; |
> | 子孙得地，将军有决胜之才。 | 子孙得地，将军就有决胜的才能。 | if Output gains its ground, the general has the talent to decide the battle. |

^q1

> | 父母持世动，乃主帅不恤士卒，上下离心，若带兄弟官鬼，须防自变； | 父母持世发动，是主帅不体恤士兵，上下离心，如果又带着兄弟、官鬼，要防备内部自己生变； | If Resource holds the Self and moves, the commander does not care for his soldiers and high and low are of different minds, and if it also carries Peer or Pressure, one must guard against a change from within; |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说「带兄弟凶神」，卜筮正宗说「带兄弟官鬼」。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says 'carrying Peer or a baleful spirit'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says 'carrying Peer or Pressure'.
