# 国朝·本宫旺相，周文王创八百年之基；大象休囚，秦始皇遗二世主之祸（旧注：本宫为国爻，太岁为君、岁合为后、月建为臣、日建为东宫、子孙为黎庶、父爻为国；本宫旺相国祚长，休囚而大象又凶则速亡）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占国运，先分派各爻：本宫是国爻，太岁是君，和太岁相合的是后，月建是臣，日建是东宫（太子），子孙是百姓，父爻是国家。开国时占得本宫旺相，国运绵长，像周文王那样子孙享国八百年；本宫休囚、整个卦象又凶，国运短促，像秦始皇那样到二世就亡国。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask about the fortune of a state, first assign the lines: the home palace is the line of the state, Tai Sui is the ruler, the branch combining with Tai Sui is the empress, the Month is the minister, the Day is the Eastern Palace (the crown prince), Output is the common people and Resource is the realm. If a state is being founded and the home palace comes out thriving or strong, its fortune is long, as with King Wen of Zhou, whose descendants held the realm for eight hundred years; if the home palace is resting or trapped and the whole image is also bad, its fortune is short, as with the First Emperor of Qin, whose state was lost with the second reign.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 本宫为国爻。 | 本宫是代表国家的爻。 | The home palace is the line of the state. |
> | 以太岁为君爻，岁合为后爻； | 拿太岁当君主的爻，和太岁相合的当皇后的爻； | Take Tai Sui as the ruler's line and the branch that combines with Tai Sui as the empress's line; |
> | 月建为臣爻，日建为东宫； | 月建当臣子的爻，日建当东宫（太子）； | the Month as the ministers' line and the Day as the Eastern Palace (the crown prince); |
> | 子孙为黎庶，父爻为国。 | 子孙当百姓，父爻当国家。 | Output as the common people and Resource as the realm. |
> | 若建国时，卜得本宫旺相，如周文王，子孙享国八百年之久也。 | 如果是开国之时占卜，占得本宫旺相，就像周文王那样，子孙享有国运长达八百年。 | If the divination is made when a state is being founded and the home palace comes out thriving or strong, it is like King Wen of Zhou, whose descendants enjoyed the realm for as long as eight hundred years. |
> | 史记周武王定鼎于洛，卜云：传位三十，历年七百。 | 《史记》记载周武王在洛邑定都，占卜的结果是：传位三十代，经历七百年。 | The Records of the Grand Historian say that when King Wu of Zhou fixed the tripods at Luo, the divination read: thirty successions, seven hundred years. |
> | 后至赧王亡，共算八百余年。 | 后来到周赧王时灭亡，一共算下来有八百多年。 | Later the dynasty perished with King Nan, and in all it came to more than eight hundred years. |
> | 故曰：周过其历也。 | 所以说：周朝超过了卜到的年数。 | Hence it is said: Zhou went beyond its reckoned span. |
> | 若本宫休囚，大象又凶，则如秦始皇，二世亡国。 | 如果本宫休囚，整个卦象又凶，就像秦始皇那样，到二世就亡国。 | If the home palace is resting or trapped and the whole image is also bad, it is like the First Emperor of Qin, whose state was lost with the second reign. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1462 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如臣卜以本宫为国朝，以太岁为君爻，岁合为后爻，月建为臣爻，日建为东宫，子孙为黎庶，父爻为国。 | 臣子占卜时，以本宫为国朝，以太岁为君爻，岁合为后爻，月建为臣爻，日建为东宫，子孙为百姓，父爻为国家。 | When a minister casts, take the home palace as the court of the state, Tai Sui as the ruler's line, the branch combining with Tai Sui as the empress's line, the Month as the ministers' line, the Day as the Eastern Palace, Output as the common people and Resource as the realm. |
> | 卜得本宫旺相，如周文王子孙享国八百年之久； | 占得本宫旺相，就像周文王的子孙享有国运八百年之久； | If the home palace comes out thriving or strong, it is like the descendants of King Wen of Zhou holding the realm for as long as eight hundred years; |
> | 若本宫休囚，大象又凶，则如秦始皇二世亡国。 | 如果本宫休囚，整个卦象又凶，就像秦始皇到二世就亡国。 | if the home palace is resting or trapped and the whole image is also bad, it is like the First Emperor of Qin, whose state was lost with the second reign. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗把这段分派写成「如臣卜」（臣子占国朝），并在后面「年伤弱世」一联说「以下指言国君自卜」，把这一章分成臣子占国运和国君自占两种占法；卜筮大全的旧注没有这样标明。
>
> *Note:* The Bu Shi Zheng Zong writes this assignment as what applies 'when a minister casts', and at the couplet 'the year injures a weak Self' it says 'what follows speaks of the ruler casting for himself', so that it divides the chapter into a minister asking about the realm and the ruler asking about himself; the old commentary in the Bu Shi Da Quan does not mark this.
