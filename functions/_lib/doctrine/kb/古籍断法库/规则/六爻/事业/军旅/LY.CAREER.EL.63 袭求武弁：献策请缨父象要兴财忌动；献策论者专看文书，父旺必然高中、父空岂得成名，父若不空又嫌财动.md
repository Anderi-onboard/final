# 袭求武弁：献策请缨父象要兴财忌动；献策论者专看文书，父旺必然高中、父空岂得成名，父若不空又嫌财动

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 向朝廷献策、自请从军，要看父母爻：父爻旺相，一定高中；父爻落空，怎么能成名？父爻即使不空，还忌讳财爻发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To submit a plan to the court and volunteer for service, look at Resource: if Resource is thriving, one will surely pass high; if Resource is Void, how can one make a name? Even if Resource is not Void, one still dislikes Wealth moving.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 献策请缨，父象要兴财忌动。 | 献策、请缨，父爻之象要兴旺，财爻忌发动。 | To submit a plan and ask for the tassel, the image of Resource must flourish and Wealth must not move. |

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 775 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 献策论者，专看文书，父旺必然高中，父空，岂得成名？ | 献策、论事的人，专看文书爻：父爻旺相，一定高中；父爻落空，怎么能成名？ | For those who present plans and discuss policy, look especially at the document line: if Resource is thriving, one will surely pass high; if Resource is Void, how can one make a name? |
> | 父若不空又嫌财动。 | 父爻如果不空，又嫌财爻发动。 | If Resource is not Void, one still dislikes Wealth moving. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「请缨」出自终军「愿受长缨，必羁南越王而致之阙下」，是自请出征；「献策」是向朝廷上策论。武职这条路上献策请缨看文书爻（父母爻），因为文书就是策论、章奏。财爻忌动，是因为财克父（五行相克），书里没有明说。「高中」是考中高第。
>
> *Note:* 'Qingying', to ask for the tassel, comes from Zhong Jun's 'I wish to receive a long tassel and surely bind the king of Southern Yue and bring him to the palace', and means volunteering for a campaign; 'xiance' is presenting a policy memorial to the court. On this path to a military post, presenting a plan and volunteering is read from the document line, Resource, because the document is the memorial and the policy paper. That Wealth must not move is because Wealth controls Resource (five-element control); the book does not say so. 'Gao zhong' is passing with a high place.
