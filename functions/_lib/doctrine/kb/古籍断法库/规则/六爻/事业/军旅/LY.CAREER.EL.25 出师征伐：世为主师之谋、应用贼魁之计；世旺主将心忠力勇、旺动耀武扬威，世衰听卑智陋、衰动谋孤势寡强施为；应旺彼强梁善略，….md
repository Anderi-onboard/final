# 出师征伐：世为主师之谋、应用贼魁之计；世旺主将心忠力勇、旺动耀武扬威，世衰听卑智陋、衰动谋孤势寡强施为；应旺彼强梁善略，应休囚彼懦无能

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻是主帅的谋略，应爻是贼首的计策。交兵对敌必须分清彼我，才能决定输赢，所以以世爻为主将，应爻为贼首。世旺，主将忠心有力、勇敢，如果旺相又发动，就耀武扬威、勤于厮杀；世衰弱，就见闻低下、智谋浅陋；世衰而动，谋划孤立、势力单薄，却勉强行事。应旺相，对方必定强横而善于谋略；应休囚，对方就怯懦无能。所以经书说：一卦中间的主宰，无非凭世、应。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self is the commander's strategy and the Other is the bandit chief's scheme. In engaging and facing the enemy one must tell them from us before deciding victory or defeat, so the Self is the commander and the Other is the bandit chief. If the Self is thriving, the commander is loyal, strong and brave, and if thriving and moving he parades his prowess and is diligent in slaughter; if the Self is weak, his knowledge is low and his wit shallow; if the Self is weak and moves, his plans are lonely and his strength thin, yet he forces himself to act. If the Other is thriving, they are surely violent and good at strategy; if the Other is resting or trapped, they are cowardly and incapable. So the classic says: what rules a hexagram is nothing other than the Self and the Other.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「听卑」字面不顺，按上下文是见闻低下；「强施为」是勉强去做。「贼魁」是贼首，即对方的主帅。这一条与易隐征战占「彼己」（YY.54）的「世为我帅，应为彼将」是同一个取法。
>
> *Note:* 'Ting bei' does not run smoothly by the letter and by the context means low knowledge; 'qiang shi wei' is forcing oneself to act. 'Zeikui' is the bandit chief, the enemy's commander. This entry takes the same view as 'the Self is my commander and the Other the enemy's general' in the Yi Yin 'them and us' section (YY.54).
