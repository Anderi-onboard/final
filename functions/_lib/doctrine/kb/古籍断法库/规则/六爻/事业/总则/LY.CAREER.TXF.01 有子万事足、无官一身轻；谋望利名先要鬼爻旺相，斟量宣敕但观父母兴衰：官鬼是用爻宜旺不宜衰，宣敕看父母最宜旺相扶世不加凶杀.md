# 有子万事足、无官一身轻；谋望利名先要鬼爻旺相，斟量宣敕但观父母兴衰：官鬼是用爻宜旺不宜衰，宣敕看父母最宜旺相扶世不加凶杀

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求官，官鬼爻是用爻，宜旺不宜衰，喜欢日辰扶持拱卫，怕刑、害、克、冲；宣敕（任命的诏书）看父母爻，卦中不可没有，最好旺相、扶助世爻、不加凶杀；若被刑冲，根基不正；加临大耗，要多花钱财。太岁、月建旺的，多半是「宣」；贵人、禄、马同在一处的，不过是「敕」；虽有吉神却散漫不当权的，无非是吏员的文书。冲世凶，生世吉；只要不落空亡，再辛苦费力，终归能成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking office the Pressure line is the Subject line and should be strong, not weak; it likes the Day to support and gather round it and fears punishment, harm, control and clash. The proclamation and decree are read from Resource, which cannot be missing from the hexagram and is best strong, supporting the Self and with no baleful spirit added; if it is punished or clashed the footing is not sound, and if Great Loss is added much money will be spent. If Tai Sui and the Month are strong it is mostly a 'proclamation'; if the Noble, the Emolument and the Horse share one place it is no more than a 'decree'; if there are auspicious spirits but they are scattered and without authority it is no more than a clerk's papers. A clash on the Self is bad and a generating of the Self is good; so long as it does not fall Void, however hard and wearing the labour, it will in the end be accomplished.

> [!note] 按
> 「仪俨侃称」是宋代窦禹钧五个儿子（仪、俨、侃、偁、僖）的名字，他们相继登科，这是个典故；注文没有解释。「冲世凶，生克世吉」卜筮大全本如此，断易天机本作「生世吉」，并多出「若冲则为仇人所害，克者事不相容，刑者势不两立」一句；这两处另引在后面。
>
> *Note:* 'Yi Yan Kan Cheng' are the names of the five sons of Dou Yujun of the Song (Yi, Yan, Kan, Cheng and Xi), who passed the examinations one after another; it is an allusion and the commentary does not explain it. The Bushi Daquan text reads 'a clash on the Self is bad; generating or controlling the Self is good', while the Duanyi Tianji text reads 'generating the Self is good' and adds the sentence 'if it is clashed one is harmed by an enemy; if controlled the matter cannot be accommodated; if punished the two cannot stand together'; both are quoted after it.
