# 通玄妙论·随官入墓·求官遇之事体难成，虽成终不能振，若入杀墓更为大凶

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 随官入墓是身、世或本命跟着官鬼入了墓。求官遇到它，事情难成，就算成了也振作不起来；入的如果是杀墓（丁未、戊戌），就更是大凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Following Pressure into the tomb means the Body, the Self or one's birth year follows the Pressure line into its tomb. In seeking office, meeting it makes the matter hard to achieve, and even if achieved one cannot rise; if it enters a killing tomb (Ding-Wei or Wu-Xu) it is greatly inauspicious.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2824 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 4: following Pressure into the tomb", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 随官入墓，其目有三：有身随鬼入墓，有世随鬼入墓，有命随鬼入墓。 | 随官入墓有三种：有身爻跟着官鬼入墓的，有世爻跟着官鬼入墓的，有本命跟着官鬼入墓的。 | Following Pressure into the tomb has three kinds: the Body following the Pressure line into the tomb, the Self following it, or one's birth year following it. |

> [!note] 按
> 这一段的例子是：丑日占得雷水解，身和鬼同在五爻是身随鬼入墓；未日占得山天大畜，世在二爻属木是世随鬼入墓；未日寅生人占得地雷复、卯生人占得火泽睽，本命在鬼爻是命随鬼入墓（这几个例子没有收）。虎易注说这里的「身」是世身，不是卦身，那是今人的注，这里不收。后面讲占身、占婚、占产、占讼、求财、出行、家宅、占病各遇此如何，放在别的问题域。
>
> *Note:* The examples in the passage are: a Chou day with Water over Thunder, Jie, where the Body and Pressure are both on the fifth line, is the Body following Pressure into the tomb; a Wei day with Mountain over Heaven, Daxu, where the Self is on the second line and is Wood, is the Self following it; a Wei day with Earth over Thunder, Fu, for someone born in Yin, or Fire over Marsh, Kui, for someone born in Mao, where the birth year falls on the Pressure line, is the birth year following it (these examples are not quoted). Hu Yi's note says the "body" here is the Self-body and not the hexagram Body; that is a modern note and is not quoted. What follows on one's person, marriage, childbirth, lawsuits, wealth, travel, the house and illness goes to other domains.
