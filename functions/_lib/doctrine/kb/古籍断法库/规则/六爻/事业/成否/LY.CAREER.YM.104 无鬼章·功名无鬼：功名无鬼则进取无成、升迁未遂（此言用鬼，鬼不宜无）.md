# 无鬼章·功名无鬼：功名无鬼则进取无成、升迁未遂（此言用鬼，鬼不宜无）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 功名没有官鬼（官鬼不上卦），进取就没有成就，升迁也不能如愿。这是说取官鬼为用的占问，官鬼不宜没有。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking a name and office with no Pressure line in the hexagram, advancement comes to nothing and promotion is not achieved. This speaks of divinations in which Pressure is taken as the Subject, and Pressure should not be absent.

> [!note] 按
> 「功名无鬼」是占功名时官鬼不上卦（官鬼是功名的用神，见 YM.40）。「进取无成」是想进取没有成就，「升迁未遂」是想升迁不能如愿。书里自注：这是说取鬼（官鬼）为用的占问，鬼不宜没有。「无」要分真无和伏藏：同一章稍前（L550）说，卦中虽然没有、但伏在世爻或动爻之下，或者变出来临在日辰月建之上，就「无犹不无」，所以这里说的是官鬼既不上卦、又没有伏变的真无。同一句里还有身命、家宅、谋望无鬼的断法，分属别的问题域，这里只收功名的一半。和 YM.42 的「毋谓官伏，得助亦升」对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Gongming wu gui' is Pressure being absent from the hexagram when divining about a name and office (Pressure is the Subject of fame and office, see YM.40). 'Jinqu wu cheng' is that advancement comes to nothing, and 'shengqian wei sui' is that promotion is not achieved. The book's own gloss: this speaks of divinations in which Pressure is taken as the Subject, and Pressure should not be absent. 'Absent' must be told apart as truly absent and hidden: slightly earlier in the same chapter (L550) it says that though it is not in the hexagram, if it is hidden under the Self line or a moving line, or if it changes out and sits on the Day or Month branch, then 'absent is as if not absent', so what is meant here is Pressure truly absent, neither in the hexagram nor hidden or changed. The same sentence also has judgments for no Pressure in a person's fate, the household and scheming, which belong to other domains, and only the half about fame and office is taken here. Read against 'do not say Pressure hidden means no promotion: with help it is promoted too' in YM.42.
