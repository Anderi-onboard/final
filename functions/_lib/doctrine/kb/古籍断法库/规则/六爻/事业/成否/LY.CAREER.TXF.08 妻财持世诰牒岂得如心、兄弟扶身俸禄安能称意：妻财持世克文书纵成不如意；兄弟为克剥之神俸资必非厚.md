# 妻财持世诰牒岂得如心、兄弟扶身俸禄安能称意：妻财持世克文书纵成不如意；兄弟为克剥之神俸资必非厚

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财持世，任命的文书哪能称心？兄弟扶身，俸禄哪能称意？妻财持世克了文书（父母），就算成了也不如意；兄弟是克剥的神，俸禄必定不丰厚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Wealth holding the Self, how can the patent of appointment go as one wishes? With Peer supporting the self, how can the salary be as one wishes? Wealth holding the Self and controlling the documents (Resource) means that even if it succeeds it is not as one wishes; Peer is the spirit that strips and takes, so the salary will surely not be generous.

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 2631 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占官，文书克我则吉，我克文书则凶。 | 凡是占官，文书克我就吉，我克文书就凶。 | In every divination for office, if the documents control me it is auspicious, and if I control the documents it is bad. |
> | 妻财持世克文书，纵使成就，文辞亦不如意。 | 妻财持世克文书，即使成就，文辞也不如意。 | With Wealth holding the Self and controlling the documents, even if it succeeds, the wording too is not as one wishes. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 断易天机本多出「凡占官，文书克我则吉，我克文书则凶」一句，又把「纵成，不如意」写成「纵使成就，文辞亦不如意」。
>
> *Note:* The Duanyi Tianji text adds the sentence 'in every divination for office, if the documents control me it is auspicious, and if I control the documents it is bad', and writes 'even if it succeeds, it is not as one wishes' as 'even if it succeeds, the wording too is not as one wishes'.
