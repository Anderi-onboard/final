# 投充兵卒：土官克世遭坑陷、金鬼冲身刀箭伤、水鬼定然逢波险、火官必主犯红光、木爻值鬼多刑责，福德交重最吉祥；克世之官大凶之象须分五属可以拒避，子孙为和解之神须得一发则万祸自消

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 土属的官克世，遭坑陷；金属的官鬼冲身，被刀箭所伤；水属的官鬼，一定遇到风波险阻；火属的官，必主触犯火光；木爻值官鬼，多有刑责；福德（子孙）发动，最吉祥。克世的官是大凶之象，必须分它属于五行中的哪一类，才可以躲避：官鬼属土，要防对方挖陷坑，又要防疾病；金属的官鬼，忌讳锋利的刀箭，也防跌扑；水属的官鬼，必然设下背水的计谋，又被风波险阻；火属的官鬼，只要逃火患，又要顾虑营屯被烧的劫难；木属的官鬼，会有鞭打杖责之刑。子孙是和解之神，只要发动一次，万般祸患自然消散。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If an Earth star of office controls the Self, one falls into a pit-trap; if a Metal Pressure clashes with the Body, one is hurt by knife and arrow; if a Water Pressure, one will surely meet the danger of waves; if a Fire star of office, one will surely run into the red glare; if a Wood line holds Pressure, there are many beatings and punishments; Output, the fortune virtue, moving is most auspicious. The star of office that controls the Self is an image of great ill, and one must tell which of the five kinds it belongs to in order to ward it off: if Pressure is of Earth, guard against the other side digging a trap-pit for one to fall into, and against illness; a Metal Pressure fears sharp blades and arrows, and one must guard against falls; a Water Pressure surely sets a back-to-the-river stratagem, and one is also blocked by wind and waves; with a Fire Pressure one need only flee the danger of fire and also fear the calamity of the camp being burned; a Wood Pressure brings the punishment of whip and cane. Output is the spirit of reconciliation, and if it moves even once, ten thousand calamities melt away of themselves.

> [!note] 按
> 「五属」是官鬼所属的五行。「坑陷」是陷阱。「红光」是火光，指火灾。「背水之略」是背水结阵、没有退路的战法，用的是韩信背水一战的典故。「烧屯」是烧营寨、屯粮之所。「鞭朴」是鞭打杖责。这一条与易隐征战占「五行」一组（YY.63 起：木为舟楫、火营寨、土炮石、金刀刃、水水泉）取同一类五行取象，但这里是按官鬼所属的五行看灾：土陷坑、金刀箭、水波险、火火患、木鞭朴。「和解之神」是能化解凶的神，即子孙（福神）。
>
> *Note:* 'Five kinds' are the five elements to which Pressure belongs. 'Kengxian' is a pit-trap. 'Hongguang' is the red glare, meaning fire. 'Beishui zhi lüe' is the stratagem of drawing up with the back to the water with no way to retreat, and alludes to Han Xin's battle with his back to the river. 'Shaotun' is burning the camp and the place where grain is stored. 'Bianpu' is the whip and cane. This entry takes the same kind of five-element images as the 'five elements' group of the Yi Yin war divination (from YY.63: Wood is boats, Fire is the camp, Earth is catapult stones, Metal is blades, Water is water and springs), but here the disaster is read by the element of Pressure: Earth the pit, Metal blade and arrow, Water the danger of waves, Fire the fire, Wood the whip and cane. 'The spirit of reconciliation' is the spirit that can dissolve ill, that is Output (the fortune spirit).
