# 卜馆·问得馆何时：实世、生世、合世之日就是它的日期

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问什么时候能得到馆，看填实世爻（世爻落空时）、生世、合世的日子，那就是它的日期。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking when a post will be obtained, the days that fill the Self, generate the Self or combine with the Self are its date.

## 《易冒·卜馆章第六十一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1358 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 61: Divining a teaching post", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 问得馆何时， | 问什么时候能得到馆， | In asking when the post will be obtained, |
> | 实世生世合世， | 实世、生世、合世的日子， | the days that fill the Self, generate the Self or combine with the Self |
> | 则其期也。 | 就是它的日期。 | are its date. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「实世」按文意是填实世爻：世落空时，逢到把它填实的日子叫实；「生世」「合世」是生它、合它的日子。书里只有这一句，没有展开。易林补遗求馆（EL.54）和易隐觅馆占（YY.81）把它细讲了：以父母为用，按旺衰取日。
>
> *Note:* 'Shishi' is, by the sense, filling the Self line: when the Self is Void, a day that meets and fills it is called 'real'; 'generating the Self' and 'combining with the Self' are days that generate or combine with it. The book has only this sentence and does not expand on it. The Yilin Buyi on seeking a post (EL.54) and the Yi Yin divination of a post (YY.81) treat it in detail: Resource is the Subject, and the day is taken by thriving or declining.
