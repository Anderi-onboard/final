# 雇工·去来与防夺、防非、防病：欲其去应克世而不去、欲其来应受阻而弗来；人可信矣防被夺——应克世而被争；人可用矣防是非——官动官克而被讼；人可用矣防病——命墓、应坏、鬼动而患病

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想要他走，应克世，他就不走；想要他来，应受阻，他就不来。这个人可以信任了，会不会被人夺走？应克世，就被人争。这个人可以用了，会不会遭受是非？官动、官克，就被告。这个人可以用了，会不会生病？命墓、应坏、鬼动，就患病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If one wants him to go, with the Other controlling the Self he does not go; if one wants him to come, with the Other obstructed he does not come. The man can be trusted: will he not be taken from me? With the Other controlling the Self, he is fought over. The man can be used: will he not meet with slander and trouble? With Pressure moving and Pressure controlling, he is sued. The man can be used: will he not fall ill? With the life line in the tomb, the Other damaged and Pressure moving, he falls ill.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「去」「来」是问他走不走、来不来。「不为夺乎」是问会不会被别人夺走。「不受非乎」是问会不会遭是非。「官动官克而被讼」是官鬼发动克世，就被告上官府。「命墓」是世爻（命）入墓。「人可信矣」「人可用矣」是三个问法的开头：前一个防被夺，后两个防是非、防病，就是章首目录里的「防夺」「防非」「防病」三问。
>
> *Note:* 'Qu' and 'lai' are asking whether he will go or come. 'Bu wei duo hu' asks whether he will be taken away by others. 'Bu shou fei hu' asks whether he will meet with slander and trouble. 'Pressure moving and Pressure controlling, and he is sued' is Pressure moving to control the Self, so that he is taken to court. 'The life line in the tomb' is the Self line (the life) entering the tomb. 'The man can be trusted' and 'the man can be used' open three kinds of question: the first guards against being robbed of him, the other two against slander and against illness, which are the three questions 'guarding against being robbed', 'against slander' and 'against illness' in the index at the head of the chapter.
