# 投充兵卒：官爻持世无冲克必作先锋佐帝皇；官爻最宜持世又宜安静旺相则有奇能美爵，若值冲克必有奸佞窃夺功权之祸

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官爻持世、没有冲克，必定做先锋，辅佐帝王。官爻最适宜持世，又适宜安静旺相，这样就有奇特的才能和美好的爵位；如果遇到冲克，必有奸佞之人窃夺功劳权柄的祸患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line holds the Self with no clash or control, one will surely be a vanguard and assist the sovereign. The Pressure line is best holding the Self, and also best quiet and thriving, and then one has rare ability and a fine rank; if it meets clash or control, there will surely be the misfortune of treacherous men stealing one's merit and power.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条说官爻持世是吉：投军这件事里，官鬼既是随军出战的大忌（EL.125、EL.126），又是持世作先锋、有爵位的吉象。两处不冲突：忌的是官鬼冲克世、随官入墓、助鬼伤身，喜的是官爻持世、安静旺相、不受冲克。这是投充兵卒一章的收尾。
>
> *Note:* This entry says that the Pressure line holding the Self is auspicious: in joining the army, Pressure is both the great taboo of going out to battle (EL.125, EL.126) and the auspicious image of holding the Self as a vanguard and winning rank. The two do not clash: what is feared is Pressure clashing with or controlling the Self, following the star of office into the tomb, and helping Pressure to injure the Body; what is welcomed is the Pressure line holding the Self, quiet and thriving, and not clashed or controlled. This closes the chapter on enlisting as a soldier.
