# 仕宦·身空无救，命尽当危（旧注：世爻无故自空，不拘已任未任必有大难甚至死亡，求谋干事则不成）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻无缘无故自己落空亡又没有救助，不管已经任职还是没有任职，必有大难，甚至死亡；想谋求办事的，主不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self falls Void by itself without cause and there is no rescue, whether one is already in office or not, there will surely be a great calamity, even death; and if one wants to seek or manage something, it will not succeed.

## 《黄金策·仕宦》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7686 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Holding office", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身空无救，命尽当危。 | 世爻空亡又得不到救助，命数将尽，就有危险。 | When the Self is Void with no rescue, one's life is spent and in danger. |

> [!note] 按
> 「无故自空」是指世爻落空亡，而日辰、月建、动爻都没有伤克它（不是因为被伤克而空）。这个说法在黄金策总断千金赋里就是「无故勿空」，两家读法相反，见 LY.ILL.HJC.151：旧注说无故自空是大凶，王洪绪说旧注把意思弄反了。这里的旧注和王洪绪都取「大凶」的读法，没有分歧。
>
> *Note:* 'Void by itself without cause' means the Self falls into Void while the Day, the Month and the moving lines have not injured it (it is not Void because it was injured). This is the line 'without cause, do not be Void' in the Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements, which the two commentators read in opposite ways, see LY.ILL.HJC.151: the old commentary says that Void without cause is greatly ominous, and Wang Hongxu says the old commentary has the sense backwards. Here the old commentary and Wang Hongxu both take the 'greatly ominous' reading, and there is no disagreement.
