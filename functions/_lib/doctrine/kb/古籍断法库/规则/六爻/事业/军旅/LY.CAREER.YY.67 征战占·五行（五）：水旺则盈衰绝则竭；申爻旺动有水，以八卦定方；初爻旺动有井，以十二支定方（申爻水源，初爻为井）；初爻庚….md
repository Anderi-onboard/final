# 征战占·五行（五）：水旺则盈衰绝则竭；申爻旺动有水，以八卦定方；初爻旺动有井，以十二支定方（申爻水源，初爻为井）；初爻庚子水屋下有井、甲子水门前有井；初木动树下有井；初值土水浊，值火井干，土空有一池

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水看水源和井：水旺相，水就满；衰、绝，水就干。申爻旺相发动的，有水，方位用八卦来定；初爻旺相发动的，有井，方位用十二地支来定（申爻是水源，初爻是井）。初爻是庚子水的，屋下有井；是甲子水的，门前有井。初爻是木爻发动的，树下有井；初爻值土，水浑浊；值火，井干；初爻是土爻落空的，有一个池塘。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Water, springs and wells. If Water is thriving it is full; if weak or extinct, it runs dry. If the Shen line is thriving and moving, there is water, its direction fixed by the eight trigrams; if the first line is thriving and moving, there is a well, its direction fixed by the twelve branches (the Shen line is the source of water and the first line is the well). If the first line is Gengzi Water, there is a well under the house; if Jiazi Water, there is a well in front of the gate. If the first line is Wood and moves, there is a well beneath a tree; if the first line is on Earth, the water is muddy; on Fire, the well is dry; if the first line is Earth and Void, there is a pond.

> [!note] 按
> 第三、四句在书里被句号切成「申爻旺动者，有水。以八卦定其何方，初爻旺动者，有井。以十二支神定其何方也」，按文意应读成「申爻旺动，有水，以八卦定其方；初爻旺动，有井，以十二支神定其方」，译文照这个读法把括号补在句首。「初爻庚子水」「甲子水」是初爻的干支再加上子的五行（水），书里没有解释，译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* In the book the third and fourth sentences are cut by full stops as 'the Shen line thriving and moving, there is water. Fix its direction by the eight trigrams; the first line thriving and moving, there is a well. Fix its direction by the twelve branch spirits', and by sense they should be read as 'the Shen line thriving and moving, there is water, its direction fixed by the eight trigrams; the first line thriving and moving, there is a well, its direction fixed by the twelve branch spirits'; the translation follows this reading and supplies the bracketed words at the head of each sentence. 'The first line Gengzi Water' and 'Jiazi Water' are the stem-branch of the first line followed by the element of Zi (Water), which the book does not explain, and the translation follows the letter.
