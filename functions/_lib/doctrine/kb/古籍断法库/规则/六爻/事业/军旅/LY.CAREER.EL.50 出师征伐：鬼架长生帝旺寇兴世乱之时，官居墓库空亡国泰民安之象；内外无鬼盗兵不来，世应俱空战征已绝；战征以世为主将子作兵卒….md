# 出师征伐：鬼架长生帝旺寇兴世乱之时，官居墓库空亡国泰民安之象；内外无鬼盗兵不来，世应俱空战征已绝；战征以世为主将子作兵卒、以应为彼官鬼为贼，官鬼值长生帝旺则盗寇纵横，卦中内外无官或逢墓绝世应皆空则干戈偃息国泰民安

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼乘着长生、帝旺，是贼寇兴起、世道大乱的时候；官鬼落在墓库、空亡，是国泰民安的景象；内外都没有官鬼，盗兵不来；世爻、应爻都空，战事已经断绝。战征的事，以世爻为主将，子孙当兵卒；以应爻为对方，官鬼为贼：如果官鬼值在长生、帝旺的位置，就盗寇纵横、西夷肆虐；如果卦中内外没有官鬼，或者逢墓、绝，世、应都空，就干戈止息，国泰民安了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure rides Birth or Emperor's Peak, it is a time of bandits rising and the world in disorder; if Pressure sits in the tomb or in Void, it is the image of a state at peace and a people secure; if there is no Pressure inside or outside, bandit troops do not come; if the Self and Other are both Void, war has ceased. In matters of war and campaign, the Self is the commander and Output is the soldiers; the Other is the enemy and Pressure is the bandits: if Pressure sits in the position of Birth or Emperor's Peak, bandits run wild and the western barbarians rage; if the hexagram has no Pressure inside or outside, or it meets tomb or extinction, and the Self and Other are both Void, arms are laid down and the state is at peace and the people secure.

> | 以应为彼，官鬼为贼，如官鬼值长生帝旺之乡，则盗寇纵横，酉夷肆虐，若卦中内外无官、或逢墓绝、世应皆空，则干戈眼偃息，国泰民安矣！ | 以应爻为对方，官鬼为贼：如果官鬼值在长生、帝旺的位置，就盗寇纵横、西夷肆虐；如果卦中内外没有官鬼，或者逢墓、绝，世、应都空，就干戈止息，国泰民安了！ | The Other is the enemy and Pressure is the bandits: if Pressure sits in the position of Birth or Emperor's Peak, bandits run wild and the western barbarians rage; if the hexagram has no Pressure inside or outside, or it meets tomb or extinction, and the Self and Other are both Void, arms are laid down and the state is at peace and the people secure! |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「酉夷」疑是「西夷」，「干戈眼偃息」的「眼」疑是「顿」，译文按「西夷」「干戈止息」译。「架」是乘、骑。这一条与易隐征战占「官鬼旺动敌势盛」「内外无鬼敌必遁去」「世应俱空两将休兵」（YY.62、YY.54）取同一类断语。
>
> *Note:* 'You yi' is presumably 'xi yi', the western barbarians, and the 'yan' of 'ganqe yan yanxi' is presumably 'dun', suddenly; the translation reads 'the western barbarians' and 'arms are laid down'. 'Jia' is to ride. This entry takes the same kind of judgment as 'Pressure thriving and moving, the enemy's force is strong', 'no Pressure inside or outside, the enemy surely slips away' and 'the Self and Other both Void, the two commanders rest their troops' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.62, YY.54).
