# 六亲属类·父母主文书吏、印绶、官职事业、图籍号令、差劄条敕；官鬼主君主、职清、公庭

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻代表尊长、文书、吏员，印绶、衣服、轿车船，契约、本事，还有文章、印绶、官职事业、图籍号令、差遣的札子和条文敕令；官鬼爻代表神邪、疾病、鬼祟、盗贼，失物、输赢、灾祸，还有君主、职位清要、公庭。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line stands for elders, documents and clerks, the seal and ribbon, clothing, sedan chairs, carts and boats, contracts and skills, and also essays, the seal and ribbon, office and career, maps and registers, orders and commands, dispatch notes, regulations and decrees; the Pressure line stands for spirits and evil, illness, hauntings and thieves, lost things, winning and losing and disaster, and also the sovereign, clear and weighty posts, and the public court.

> [!note] 按
> 这是一份各类事物归属六亲的名目表，各问题通用；这里只引和官职有关的几句，其余几行（财、福、兄）见求财、婚姻等篇。海底眼另有一段「此尾五句应在官鬼上，未知是否，存考」的小注，不引。
>
> *Note:* This is a general list assigning things to the six relations, common to every kind of question; only the lines bearing on office are quoted here, and the other lines (Wealth, Output, Peer) are in the chapters on wealth, marriage and so on. The Haidi Yan also has a small note saying that the last five phrases may belong to Pressure but this is uncertain and kept for study, which is not quoted.
