# 军机：飞檄求援应疾则不援克世则不轨，上书求济六冲五疑君象冲克世爻则不允济，同仇相援则如用师；应空破散则无周急之情，应克世爻非惟不援尚有他奸，五爻空破散绝谓之五疑盖五爻为君克冲世爻恐逆耳不允，彼困我救断如用师

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 发檄文求救兵，应爻有疾（坏）就不救，应克世就是不轨。上书求济，卦是六冲、五疑，君象冲克世爻，就不允济。同仇相援，就如同用兵来断。应爻空、破、散，就没有周济急难的情意；应克世爻，不只是不救援，还有别的奸谋。五爻空、破、散、绝，叫做五疑，因为五爻是君，冲克世爻，恐怕逆耳不被允准。对方有困难、我去救援，断法如同用兵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In sending a dispatch by fast courier to ask for relief troops, if the Other is sick no help comes, and if it controls the Self it is disloyal. In petitioning in writing for aid, with a six-clash and the five doubts, if the sovereign image clashes with or controls the Self line, the aid is not granted. Mutual help between those who share a hatred is judged like using troops. If the Other is Void, broken or scattered, there is no feeling for relieving distress; if the Other line controls the Self, there is not only no help but some other treachery. If the fifth line is Void, broken, scattered or extinct, it is called the five doubts, because the fifth line is the sovereign and if it clashes with or controls the Self line, I fear what I say jars on his ear and is not granted. If the other side is in difficulty and I go to its rescue, it is judged like using troops.

> [!note] 按
> 「飞檄」是紧急的檄文，「求济」是请求接济。「周急」是救济急难。「五疑」书里自己解释：五爻空、破、散、绝。「不轨」是不守本分、有二心。「同仇相援」是有共同敌人的两方互相救援，「彼困我救」是对方有难我去救，都按用兵断。
>
> *Note:* 'Feixi' is an urgent dispatch, and 'qiuji' is asking for aid. 'Zhouji' is relieving distress. The 'five doubts' are explained by the book itself: the fifth line Void, broken, scattered or extinct. 'Bugui' is not keeping to one's place and having a divided heart. 'Tongchou xiangyuan' is two sides with a common enemy helping each other, and 'bi kun wo jiu' is going to the rescue of the other side in difficulty, and both are judged like using troops.
