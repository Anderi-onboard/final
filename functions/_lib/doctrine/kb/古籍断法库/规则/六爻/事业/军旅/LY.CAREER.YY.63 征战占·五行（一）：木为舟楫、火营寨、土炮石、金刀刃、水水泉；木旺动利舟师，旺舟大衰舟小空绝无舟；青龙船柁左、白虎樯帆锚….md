# 征战占·五行（一）：木为舟楫、火营寨、土炮石、金刀刃、水水泉；木旺动利舟师，旺舟大衰舟小空绝无舟；青龙船柁左、白虎樯帆锚链右、勾陈平基跳板中仓、螣蛇索缆、朱雀烟灶船头、玄武挠头挡浪后艄；旺相新休囚旧空无冲破漏受刑害克及木持动鬼木下伏鬼必损伤；伏土鬼湊浅、伏火鬼燥裂、伏金鬼钉眼损；木带鬼贼船

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 五行配兵器和舟船：木是舟船，火是营寨，土是炮石，金是刀刃，水是水源泉水。木爻旺相发动，利于水军；木旺，船大；木衰，船小；木落空、绝，没有船。船的各部分：青龙是船舵，在左；白虎是桅杆、帆、锚链，在右；勾陈是平基、跳板，在中舱；螣蛇是缆索；朱雀是烟灶，在船头；玄武是挠头、挡浪，在船尾。旺相，船新；休囚，船旧；落空，没有；被冲，破漏；受刑、害、克，以及木爻持发动的官鬼、木爻下伏着官鬼的，必定损伤。木下伏土爻官鬼，船搁浅；伏火爻官鬼，船干燥开裂；伏金爻官鬼，钉眼处损坏。又，木爻带官鬼的，是贼船。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The five elements and arms and boats. Wood is boats and oars, Fire is the camp, Earth is catapult stones, Metal is blades, and Water is springs. If Wood is thriving and moving, it favours the navy; thriving Wood means a big boat, weak Wood a small boat, Void or extinct Wood no boat. The parts of the boat: the Azure Dragon is the rudder, on the left; the White Tiger is the mast, sail and anchor chain, on the right; Hook Earth is the flat base and gangplank, the middle hold; the Coiling Snake is the cables; the Vermilion Bird is the smoke and stove, at the bow; the Dark Tortoise is the scull-heads and wave-guard, at the stern. Thriving, the boat is new; resting or trapped, old; Void, there is none; clashed, it leaks; punished, harmed or controlled, or if Wood holds a moving Pressure line, or Pressure lies hidden under Wood, it is surely damaged. Earth Pressure hidden under it, the boat runs into the shallows; Fire Pressure, it dries and splits; Metal Pressure, the nail holes are damaged. Again, Wood carrying Pressure is the enemy's boat.

> [!note] 按
> 船的各部分和六神的配法，书里只列了名目，没有解释：「平基」「挠头」「后稍（艄）」按字面译成 flat base、scull-heads、stern；「舟湊浅」的「湊」疑是「搁」（搁浅），译文按搁浅译。「舟楫」是船和桨。
>
> *Note:* The book only lists the parts of the boat and their assignment to the six spirits without explaining them: 'pingji', 'naotou' and 'hou shao' are translated by the letter as the flat base, the scull-heads and the stern; in 'zhou cou qian' the 'cou' is presumably 'ge', so that it is running aground, and the translation reads 'runs into the shallows'. 'Zhouji' is boats and oars.
