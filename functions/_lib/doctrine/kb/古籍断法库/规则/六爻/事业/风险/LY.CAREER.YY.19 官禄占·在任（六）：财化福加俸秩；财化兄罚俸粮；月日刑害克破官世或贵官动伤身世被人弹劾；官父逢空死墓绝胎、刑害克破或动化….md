# 官禄占·在任（六）：财化福加俸秩；财化兄罚俸粮；月日刑害克破官世或贵官动伤身世被人弹劾；官父逢空死墓绝胎、刑害克破或动化福空死墓绝胎刑害克破俱降职；世官空动将失职；官化退神

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任，俸禄、弹劾和降职：财爻变出子孙，加俸加秩；财爻变出兄爻，被罚俸禄钱粮；月建、日辰刑、害、克、破官爻、世爻，或者贵人、官星发动伤身爻、世爻的，被人弹劾；官星、印星遇到空、死、墓、绝、胎，或被刑、害、克、破，或者发动变出子孙、变成空、死、墓、绝、胎、变成刑、害、克、破的，都降职；世爻、官星落空而发动的，将要失去职位；官星变成退神（括号里是退神的四个干支）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In office, salary, impeachment and demotion. If Wealth turns into Output, his salary and rank are raised; if Wealth turns into a Peer line, his salary and grain are docked; if the Month and Day punish, harm, control or break the Pressure line or the Self line, or the Noble and star of office move to injure the Body and Self, he is impeached; if the star of office and the seal meet Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, or are punished, harmed, controlled or broken, or move and turn into Output, or into Void, death, tomb, extinction, the womb stage, punishment, harm, control or breaking, he is demoted; if the Self and the star of office are Void and moving, he is about to lose his post; the star of office turning into a Retreating spirit is also listed, followed in brackets by the four ganzhi of the Retreating spirit.

> [!note] 按
> 「福」是福神（子孙），「官化退神」后面的括号（丁丑、丁未、壬辰、壬戌）是退神的干支：《卜筮全书》写「退神者，壬戌、壬辰、丁丑、丁未」，《断易天机》也写「丁丑、丁未、壬辰、壬戌，四者为退神」，和这里一致。这一句在书里只写了条件、没有写结果，和前后句一起列在失职之后，译文照录，不替它补结论。附断例是《易隐》官禄占末尾的一例，放在最相关的这一条下。「季空」书里没有解释，按字面是当季的空。「官不蒞事」是官员不能到任办事。
>
> *Note:* 'Fu' is the fortune spirit (Output), and the bracket after 'the star of office turns into a Retreating spirit', (Dingchou, Dingwei, Renchen, Renxu), gives the ganzhi of the Retreating spirit: the Bushi Quanshu writes 'the Retreating spirit is Renxu, Renchen, Dingchou and Dingwei', and the Duanyi Tianji also writes 'Dingchou, Dingwei, Renchen, Renxu, these four are the Retreating spirit', in agreement with this. The sentence gives only a condition and no result in the book, and stands with the sentences before and after it after the loss of a post; the translation records it and does not supply a conclusion. The example is one of those appended at the end of this chapter of the Yi Yin, and is placed under the entry it illustrates most. 'Jikong' is not explained in the book and is read literally as the Void of the season. 'Guan bu li shi' means the official cannot take up his duties.
