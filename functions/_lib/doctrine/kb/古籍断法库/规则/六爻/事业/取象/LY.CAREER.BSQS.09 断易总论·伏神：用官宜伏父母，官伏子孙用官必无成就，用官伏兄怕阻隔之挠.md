# 断易总论·伏神：用官宜伏父母，官伏子孙用官必无成就，用官伏兄怕阻隔之挠

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用官鬼做用神，最好它伏在父母爻下；官鬼伏在子孙爻下，就一定没有成就；伏在兄弟爻下，怕有阻隔的阻挠。用父母做用神伏在妻财下都是祸患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Pressure is the Subject, it does best hidden under Resource; hidden under Output, it will have no accomplishment; hidden under Peer, one fears the hindrance of obstruction. When Resource is the Subject, hidden under Wealth is all trouble.

## 《卜筮全书·通玄妙论·八、断易总论》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2885–2887 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 8: general discussion of Duanyi", base text

> | 用财宜伏子孙。 | 用妻财做用神，宜它伏在子孙爻下。 | when Wealth is the Subject it does well hidden under Output. |
> | 官伏子孙， | 官鬼伏在子孙爻下， | Pressure hidden under Output |

> | 用财一半亏伤。 | 用妻财做用神就要损伤一半。 | means that when Wealth is the Subject half is lost and harmed. |
> | 用财伏官， | 用妻财做用神而伏在官鬼爻下， | When Wealth is the Subject and is hidden under Pressure, |
> | 怕侵匿之扰。 | 怕有侵吞藏匿的搅扰。 | one fears the trouble of embezzlement and concealment. |
> | 用官伏兄， | 用官鬼做用神而伏在兄弟爻下， | When Pressure is the Subject and is hidden under Peer, |

> | 用父伏财皆为患， | 用父母做用神而伏在妻财爻下，都是祸患； | When Resource is the Subject and is hidden under Wealth, it is all trouble; |
> | 用财伏兄总不中。 | 用妻财做用神而伏在兄弟爻下，总不合用。 | when Wealth is the Subject and is hidden under Peer, it is never of use. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 伏神是卦里没有出现的六亲，伏在本宫首卦的某一爻（飞神）下面；飞神生伏神为得长生，所以官伏在父母下是「宜」（父母生官鬼），伏在子孙下是克（子孙克官鬼）。「用官」是拿官鬼当用神。这一段里用财的几句是求财，同在这里是因为它们和用官的句子连着，没有拆开。
>
> *Note:* A hidden line is a relation that does not appear in the hexagram and lies under some line (the flying line) of the home palace's first hexagram; when the flying line gives life to the hidden line it is well placed, so Pressure hidden under Resource is "fitting" (Resource gives life to Pressure) while hidden under Output is controlled (Output controls Pressure). "Using the official" means taking Pressure as the Subject. The lines on wealth in this passage are about seeking wealth and are kept here because they run on from the lines on Pressure and are not separated.
