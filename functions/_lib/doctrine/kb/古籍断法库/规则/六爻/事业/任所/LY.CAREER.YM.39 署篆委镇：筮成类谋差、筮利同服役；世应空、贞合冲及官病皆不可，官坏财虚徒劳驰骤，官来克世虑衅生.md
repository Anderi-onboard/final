# 署篆委镇：筮成类谋差、筮利同服役；世应空、贞合冲及官病皆不可，官坏财虚徒劳驰骤，官来克世虑衅生

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 代理印信、受委派镇守的人，占成不成，类似谋差使；世应空、本卦合处逢冲，以及官爻有病，都不可。占有没有利，和服役相同：官坏、财虚，只是白费奔走；官来克世，还要担心（此处一字不清）生出事端。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For one acting with the seal or commissioned to a garrison, divining whether it comes off is like seeking a commission; the Self and the Other Void, a combination in the original hexagram being clashed, and Pressure being sick are all unacceptable. Divining about profit is the same as for serving in a duty: with Pressure damaged and Wealth empty one only wears oneself out galloping about; if Pressure comes to control the Self, one must also fear that (one character is unclear) strife arises.

> [!note] 按
> 「署篆」是代理印信，暂代某官的职务；「委镇」是被委派去镇守一处。「谋差」「服役」是前面谋差顶役（EL.106 起）里的两种占：谋差是求差使，服役是当差。「贞合冲」按文意是本卦里本来合住的，又被冲。「驰骤」是奔走。「且虑？衅生」里的「？」是书里自己标的存疑字，按文意译作「还要担心（此处一字不清）生出事端」。
>
> *Note:* 'Shuzhuan' is acting with the seal, standing in for an official for a time, and 'weizhen' is being commissioned to hold a post as garrison. 'Seeking a commission' and 'serving in a duty' are the two divinations in the earlier entries on seeking a commission and taking a runner's place (from EL.106): seeking a commission is asking for the post, and serving is doing the duty. 'Zhen he chong', by the sense, is a combination in the original hexagram that is then clashed. 'Chizhou' is running about. The '?' in 'qie lu ? xin sheng' is a doubtful character marked by the book itself, and by the sense it is translated 'one must also fear that (one character is unclear) strife arises'.
