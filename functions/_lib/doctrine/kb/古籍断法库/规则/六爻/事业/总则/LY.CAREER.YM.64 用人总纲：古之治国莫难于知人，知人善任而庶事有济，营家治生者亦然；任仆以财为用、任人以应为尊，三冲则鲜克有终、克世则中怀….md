# 用人总纲：古之治国莫难于知人，知人善任而庶事有济，营家治生者亦然；任仆以财为用、任人以应为尊，三冲则鲜克有终、克世则中怀不利

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 古代治国的人，没有比识人更难的：认得清人又用得对，各种事情才办得成，何况是经营家业、谋生的人呢？任用仆人以财爻为用，任用一般的人以应爻为尊；三冲，很少能有始有终；应克世，心里怀着对我不利的念头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Of those who governed a state in antiquity, nothing was harder than knowing men: if one knows men and employs them well, all affairs are carried through, and how much more for one who runs a household and makes a living? To employ a servant the Wealth line is the Subject, and to employ anyone else the Other line is honoured; with the three clashes it is rare to see a thing through to the end, and with the Other controlling the Self he bears in his heart what is not to my advantage.

> [!note] 按
> 「知人善任」是认得清人、又用得对。「任仆以财为用」是用仆人取妻财爻，「任人以应为尊」是用一般的人取应爻。「三冲」（见 YM.07 注）是散、是变，所以很少有始有终；「克世则中怀不利」是应克世，他心里存着对我不利的念头。这一章的『用人』是雇用、任用仆人、帮手、伙计，和上一章讲的做官任职不是一回事，归事业里的差役一类。与易林补遗用人藏否（EL.131–133）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhiren shanren' is knowing men clearly and employing them rightly. 'Employing a servant takes Wealth as the Subject' is taking the Wealth line for a servant, and 'for anyone else the Other is honoured' is taking the Other line for ordinary people. The 'three clashes' (see the note to YM.07) are scattering and changing, hence it is rare to see a thing through; 'controlling the Self, he bears in his heart what is not to my advantage' is the Other controlling the Self, so that he carries in his heart a thought against me. The 'employing people' of this chapter is hiring and employing servants, helpers and hands, which is not the same as holding office as in the previous chapter, and belongs to the runners-and-servants group in Career. Read against the Yilin Buyi on employing people and whether they hide (EL.131-133).
