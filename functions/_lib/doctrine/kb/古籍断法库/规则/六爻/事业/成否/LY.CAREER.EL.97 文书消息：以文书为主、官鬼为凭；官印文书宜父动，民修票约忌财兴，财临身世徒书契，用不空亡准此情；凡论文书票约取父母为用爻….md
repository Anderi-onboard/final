# 文书消息：以文书为主、官鬼为凭；官印文书宜父动，民修票约忌财兴，财临身世徒书契，用不空亡准此情；凡论文书票约取父母为用爻，旺则有成空则无用，财爻为动或值世身空劳纸笔枉费神思

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 论文书、消息，以文书爻为主，以官鬼为凭据。官府的印信文书，适宜父爻发动；民间立的票据契约，忌讳财爻兴旺；财爻临在身爻、世爻上，书契就白写；用爻不落空亡，就按这个情形断。凡是论文书、票据、契约，取父母爻为用爻：旺相就有成，落空就无用；财爻发动，或者世爻、身爻落空，就白费纸笔、枉费心思。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In judging documents and tidings, take the document as the chief and Pressure as the ground. For official seals and documents it is fitting that Resource move; for bills and contracts drawn up by the people, avoid Wealth flourishing; if Wealth sits on the Body or the Self, the writing of the deed is in vain; if the Subject is not Void, hold to this situation. In judging documents, bills and contracts, take Resource as the Subject line: if thriving it succeeds, if Void it is useless; if the Wealth line moves, or the Self or Body is Void, the paper and brush are toiled for in vain and thought is wasted.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 865 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡论文书票约，取父母为用爻，旺则有成，空则无用，财爻为动或值世身空，劳纸笔枉费神思。 | 凡是论文书、票据、契约，取父母爻为用爻：旺相就有成，落空就无用；财爻发动，或者世爻、身爻落空，就白费纸笔、枉费心思。 | In judging documents, bills and contracts, take Resource as the Subject line: if thriving it succeeds, if Void it is useless; if the Wealth line moves, or the Self or Body is Void, the paper and brush are toiled for in vain and thought is wasted. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「票约」是票据和契约，「书契」是写下来的契约文书，「官印文书」是官府盖印的文书。「周不空亡准此情」的「周」疑是「用」（用神，这里是父母爻），译作「用爻不落空亡」。这一条与易隐文书占「文书占·公私之辨与成否」（YY.71）取同一个关系：都以父母为用，公家的文书看官旺，私下的契约看财怕克父。
>
> *Note:* 'Piaoyue' are bills and contracts, 'shuqi' are the written contract documents, and 'guanyin wenshu' are documents sealed by the authorities. The 'zhou' of 'zhou bu kongwang zhun ci qing' is presumably 'yong', the Subject (here the Resource line), and it is translated 'if the Subject is not Void'. This entry takes the same relation as 'public and private divinations and success' in the Yi Yin divination on documents (YY.71): both take Resource as the Subject, and for the document of the state one looks at the star of office thriving, for a private contract one fears Wealth controlling Resource.
