# 卜馆总纲：问馆有无成否，喜六合生世、恶三冲应空；本章十三问——何地、何时、就馆、开馆、附学、代馆、辞馆、荐馆、攘馆、选刻、卜徒、幕馆、商馆

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问馆（教书的馆席）有没有、成不成，喜六合、喜生世，恶三冲、恶应空。本章分十三问：在什么地方、什么时候、就馆、开馆、附学、代馆、辞馆、荐馆、攘馆、选文刻书、卜徒、幕馆、商馆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking whether a teaching post exists and whether it will come off, Six-Combine and generation of the Self are welcome, while the three clashes and a Void Other are hated. The chapter takes thirteen questions in turn: where, when, taking up a post, opening a school, pupils coming to attach themselves, standing in for another, resigning, recommending, snatching a post, choosing texts for cutting, divining a disciple, a secretary's post, and a merchant's post.

> [!note] 按
> 「馆」是教书的馆席，也就是请先生的聘约。「就馆」是应聘上任，「开馆」是自己设帐招生，「附学」是学生来附读（下文「问附来者」），「代馆」是替人坐席，「辞馆」是辞去，「荐馆」是荐人去，「攘馆」是被人谋夺馆席，「选刻」是选文刻书，「卜徒」是看收的徒弟，「幕馆」是衙门里的幕友，「商馆」是在商家、富豪处共事。这一章的馆是私塾先生、幕友、商家账房一类的聘职，按职业归入事业；上一章末尾（L1350–1352）讲学生附学、延师、访师的那一半是读书求学，另归考试。「三冲」是贞冲（本卦六冲）、悔冲（变卦六冲）、合处逢冲这三种（见 YM.07 的注）。
>
> *Note:* 'Guan' is the teaching post, that is, the engagement of a teacher. 'Jiuguan' is taking up an engagement, 'kaiguan' is setting up a school oneself, 'fuxue' is pupils coming to attach themselves (the text below, 'asking about those who come to attach'), 'daiguan' is standing in for another, 'ciguan' is resigning, 'jianguan' is recommending someone, 'rangguan' is the post being plotted against and snatched, 'xuanke' is choosing texts and cutting blocks, 'butu' is looking at the disciples taken on, 'muguan' is a secretary in a government office, and 'shangguan' is working in the house of a merchant or rich man. The posts in this chapter are those of a village-school teacher, a secretary and a merchant's accountant, and are placed under Career by occupation; the other half, at the end of the previous chapter (L1350–1352), about pupils attaching themselves, hiring a teacher and seeking a teacher, is about study and belongs under Examinations. The 'three clashes' are the original hexagram being a six-clash, the changed hexagram being a six-clash, and a combination meeting a clash (see the note to YM.07).
