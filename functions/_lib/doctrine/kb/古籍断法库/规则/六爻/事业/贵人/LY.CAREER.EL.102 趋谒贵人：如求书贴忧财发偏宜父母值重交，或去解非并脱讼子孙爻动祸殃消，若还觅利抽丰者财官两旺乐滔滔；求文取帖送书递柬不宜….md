# 趋谒贵人：如求书贴忧财发偏宜父母值重交，或去解非并脱讼子孙爻动祸殃消，若还觅利抽丰者财官两旺乐滔滔；求文取帖送书递柬不宜父母空亡更忌妻财发动，解词息讼反宜官鬼休囚又怕应来伤世，假公济私或抽丰利己须财官两旺切嫌兄弟交重

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 去求书信、帖文，忧的是财爻发动，偏宜父母爻发动；去排解是非、脱出官司，子孙爻动，祸殃就消；求利、打抽丰，财爻、官爻两旺，就乐得很。具体说：去求文、取帖，以及送书、递柬，不适宜父母爻空亡，更忌妻财发动；想排解词讼、平息官司，反而适宜官鬼休囚，又怕应爻来伤世爻；遇到子孙持世或发动，祸一定消散；如果借公事来成全私情、或者打抽丰对自己有利，必须财、官两旺，切忌兄弟发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If one seeks a letter or a note, one worries about Wealth moving and it is fitting for Resource to move; if one goes to settle a dispute or escape a lawsuit, when the Output line moves calamity is dispelled; if one seeks gain by drawing a rich man's bounty, Wealth and the star of office both thriving make one overflow with joy. In detail: in going to ask for a text or take a note, and in sending a letter or handing in a card, it is not fitting for Resource to be Void, and still more Wealth moving is avoided; if one wants to clear up a dispute and stop a lawsuit, it is on the contrary fitting for Pressure to be resting or imprisoned, and one fears the Other coming to hurt the Self; if Output holds the Self or moves, the calamity will surely vanish; if one makes use of a public affair to serve a private feeling, or draws a bounty for one's own benefit, Wealth and the star of office must both be thriving, and Peer moving is by all means to be avoided.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「书贴」是书信、帖文；「解非脱讼」是排解是非、脱出官司；「打抽丰」是去拜访有钱有势的人，求他资助；「递柬」是递送名帖。「祸必触消」的「触」疑是「顿」，译作「一定消散」。解词息讼时官鬼休囚反而好。这一条与易隐谒贵占「所求不同用神不同」（YY.77）取同一个法：求书恳荐以父母为用，息讼以子孙为用，各看各的。
>
> *Note:* 'Shutie' are letters and notes; 'jiefei tuosong' is settling a dispute and getting out of a lawsuit; 'da chou feng' is calling on a rich and powerful man to ask for his help; 'dijian' is handing in a visiting card. The 'chu' of 'huo bi chu xiao' is presumably 'dun', at once, and it is translated 'will surely vanish'. In clearing up a dispute and stopping a lawsuit, Pressure being resting or imprisoned is on the contrary good. This entry takes the same method as 'what is sought differs, the Subject differs' in the Yi Yin divination on calling on a noble (YY.77): for asking for a letter of recommendation Resource is the Subject, for stopping a lawsuit Output is the Subject, and each is looked at on its own.
