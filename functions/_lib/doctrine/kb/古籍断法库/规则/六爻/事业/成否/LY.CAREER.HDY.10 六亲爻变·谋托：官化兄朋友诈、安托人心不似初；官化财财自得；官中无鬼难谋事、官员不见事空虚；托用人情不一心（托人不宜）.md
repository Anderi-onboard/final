# 六亲爻变·谋托：官化兄朋友诈、安托人心不似初；官化财财自得；官中无鬼难谋事、官员不见事空虚；托用人情不一心（托人不宜）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官化兄，朋友有欺诈，安心托付的人心思已不像当初；官化财，钱财自然得到，赌博、抽签拈阄总不如意；官员里没有官鬼，难以谋事，官员不出现，事情落空；托人情办事，人不一条心（不适宜托人）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure turning into Peer, friends are deceitful and the one trusted with a favour no longer has the heart he had at first; Pressure turning into Wealth, money comes of itself, but gambling and drawing lots are never as one wishes; with no Pressure in the office, planning a matter is hard, and with the official not appearing the matter is empty; relying on favours, hearts are not as one (it is not suitable to entrust it to others).
