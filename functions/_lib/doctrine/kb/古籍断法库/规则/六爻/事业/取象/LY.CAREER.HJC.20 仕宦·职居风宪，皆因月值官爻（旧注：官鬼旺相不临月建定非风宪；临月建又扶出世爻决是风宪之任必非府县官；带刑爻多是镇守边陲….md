# 仕宦·职居风宪，皆因月值官爻（旧注：官鬼旺相不临月建定非风宪；临月建又扶出世爻决是风宪之任必非府县官；带刑爻多是镇守边陲、掌兵权或居刑部；在外是司刑之职）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼旺相而不临月建，一定不是风宪的职位；如果临月建，又得到扶助而出现在世爻上，一定是风宪的任命，必不是府县的官，再在日辰的生旺之地，更是风宪；如果带刑爻，多是镇守边陲的职位，或者掌握兵权，或者在刑部，在外地也是主管刑狱的职位。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure is thriving but does not rest on the Month, it is surely not a post in the censorate; if it rests on the Month and is supported so as to appear on the Self, it is surely an appointment in the censorate and not an officer of a prefecture or county, all the more if it also stands in a thriving place of the Day; if it carries a punishment line, it is mostly a post guarding the frontier, or holding military power, or in the Board of Punishments, and outside the capital a post in charge of punishments.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1966 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼不临月建，定非风宪之职； | 官鬼不临月建，一定不是风宪的职位； | If Pressure does not rest on the Month, it is surely not a post in the censorate; |
> | 若临月建，又得扶出世爻，决是风宪之任，必非州县之官。 | 如果临月建，又得到扶助而出现在世爻上，一定是风宪的任命，必不是州县的官； | if it rests on the Month and is supported so as to appear on the Self, it is surely an appointment in the censorate and not an officer of a prefecture or county; |
> | 如带白虎刑爻，主镇守边陲，职掌兵权。 | 如果带白虎、刑爻，主镇守边陲，职掌兵权。 | if it carries the White Tiger or a punishment line, it means guarding the frontier and holding military power. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「风宪」是监察、执法的官署（御史、按察使一类），「刑部」是掌管刑狱的部，「镇守边陲」是驻守边疆。旧注写「府县官」，王洪绪写「州县之官」，是同一类的地方官。王洪绪把旧注的「带刑爻」改成「带白虎刑爻」，并且只说「镇守边陲，职掌兵权」，没有旧注那两个出路（刑部、在外司刑）。这一联和 LY.CAREER.HJC.07 里「月建扶出官爻世爻者必是风宪之职」是同一个判据，在那里是顺带提到，这里是专门讲职位的类别。
>
> *Note:* 'The censorate' is the office that supervises and enforces the law (censors and surveillance commissioners), 'the Board of Punishments' is the ministry in charge of punishments, and 'guarding the frontier' is being stationed on the border. The old commentary writes 'an officer of a prefecture or county' and Wang Hongxu 'an officer of a department or county', local officers of the same kind. Wang Hongxu changes the old commentary's 'a punishment line' into 'the White Tiger or a punishment line' and says only 'guarding the frontier and holding military power', without the old commentary's other two outcomes (the Board of Punishments, and punishments outside). The criterion 'if the Month supports Pressure or the Self, it is surely a post in the censorate' is also in LY.CAREER.HJC.07, where it comes up in passing, and here the couplet speaks directly of what kind of post it is.
