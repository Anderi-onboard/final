# 官禄占·丁忧起复：财带白衣杀或财加虎杀会月建动刑克父母阳丁父艰阴丁母艰；二爻五爻带白衣杀旺动伤世克官二母忧五父忧；后遇生合官印之年月起复

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丁忧起复，遭父母之丧和重新起用：财爻带白衣杀，或财爻加白虎杀，又会合月建，发动来刑克父母爻的，阳爻是遭父亲的丧，阴爻是遭母亲的丧；二爻、五爻（二爻是母，五爻是父）带白衣杀旺相发动，伤世爻、克官星的，二爻是母亲的丧事，五爻是父亲的丧事；后来遇到生合官星、印星的年月，必定起复，或者官星、印星生旺的年月起复做官。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Mourning and recall. If the Wealth line carries the White Garment baleful spirit, or Wealth carries the Tiger spirit and meets the Month, and moves to punish or control the Resource line, then if it is yang he is in mourning for his father and if yin for his mother; if the second or fifth line (the second is the mother and the fifth the father) carries the White Garment spirit, thriving and moving, and injures the Self and controls the star of office, the second line means mourning for the mother and the fifth line mourning for the father; later, in the year or month that generates or combines with the star of office and seal, he is surely recalled, or in the year or month when the star of office and seal are born or thriving he is recalled to office.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「丁忧」「丁父艰」「丁母艰」都是遭父母之丧，官员须去职回家守丧（「丁制」，见 YY.01）；「白衣杀」是主孝服的神煞。「阳丁父艰，阴丁母艰」是看被刑克的那一爻是阳还是阴。
>
> *Note:* 'Dingyou', 'ding fu jian' and 'ding mu jian' all mean mourning for a parent, when an official must leave his post and go home to keep mourning ('dingzhi', see YY.01); 'White Garment' is the spirit-star that signifies mourning dress. 'Yang for the father's mourning, yin for the mother's' depends on whether the line being punished or controlled is yang or yin.
