# 官鬼临唐符国印最佳，可作朝中辅翼；符印二爻发动，诏书立至；宜静而动必升迁，宜动而静多掩抑

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼落在唐符、国印上最好，日后可在朝中做辅佐；唐符、国印这两爻在上卦发动，一定符印到手，不久随诏书起用；「宜静而动必升迁，宜动而静多掩抑」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure standing on the Tangfu or the State Seal is the best, one who will serve as a support at court; when these two lines move in the upper trigram the tally and seal are surely in hand and one will soon be raised by imperial edict; 'what should be still but moves will surely rise; what should move but stays still is mostly held down'.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3782 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 符印既动，诏书立至。 | 符、印既然发动，诏书立刻就到。 | Once the tally and seal move, the edict arrives at once. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 「宜静而动」「宜动而静」没有交代是哪一爻，旧注也没有解释，本库不替它补主语。「就多符印两爻权」的「就多」疑是「就中」之误（断易天机的同一句作「就中苻印两爻推」），这里按旧注的解释读成「再用符、印两爻推」。
>
> *Note:* 'What should be still but moves' and 'what should move but stays still' do not say which line, and the note does not explain, so no subject is supplied here. The 'jiu duo' of 'jiu duo fu yin liang yao quan' looks like a slip for 'jiu zhong' (the same line in the Duanyi Tianji reads 'from among these, reckon the two lines tally and seal'), and it is read here as 'reckon from the two lines tally and seal', following the note.
