# 仕宦·僧道医官，岂可文书发动（旧注：僧道医官及阴阳官皆要子孙出现有气不空，父母发动必有灾悔；父带岁月日非身有灾病乃外来祸；子孙自空亦有大难，然子孙只宜安静、官鬼不宜空伏；王洪绪：僧道医官以子孙为用，父动则伤、用药不灵）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 僧官、道官、医官和阴阳官，都要子孙出现、有气不空才吉。父母发动，必有灾祸悔恨；父母带太岁、月建、日辰，就不是自身有灾病，而是外来的祸；子孙自己空亡，也有大难；但子孙只适宜安静，官鬼不适宜空伏，两样都要顾到，要仔细。王洪绪说：僧官、道官、医官都以子孙为用，父母发动就伤害他们，用药不灵，反而不好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Monk-officials, Daoist officials, physician-officials and officials of yin-yang divination all need Output to appear, with breath and not Void, to be auspicious. If Resource moves, there will surely be calamity and regret; if Resource carries the Tai Sui, the Month or the Day, it is not calamity or illness in oneself but a misfortune from outside; if Output is Void by itself, there is also a great calamity; but Output should only be still and Pressure should not be Void or hidden, and both must be satisfied, with care. Wang Hongxu says: monk-officials, Daoist officials and physician-officials all take Output as the Subject, and if Resource moves it injures them, medicines do not work and on the contrary it is bad.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1980 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 僧道医官皆以子孙为用，如父动则伤僧道医官，则用药不灵，反为不美。 | 僧官、道官、医官都以子孙为用，如果父母发动就伤害僧官、道官、医官，用药不灵，反而不好。 | Monk-officials, Daoist officials and physician-officials all take Output as the Subject, and if Resource moves it injures the monk, Daoist or physician official, medicines do not work and on the contrary it is bad. |
> | 克冲须防是非。 | 克冲要提防是非。 | With a control or clash, beware of dispute. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「僧官、道官」是管理僧道的官（僧纲、道纪一类），「医官」是太医院、医学的官，「阴阳官」是管天文、历数、风水的官。这几种官都是以子孙为用神：子孙是福德，也是僧道医药的象（见疾病域 LY.ILL.HDY.13 子孙是医药僧道）。父母是文书，发动就克子孙，所以不宜。旧注里的「官鬼不宜空伏」是说子孙虽好，也不能没有官鬼，因为这毕竟是占官。王洪绪只讲子孙为用、父动则伤。
>
> *Note:* 'Monk-officials and Daoist officials' are the officials who manage monks and Daoists (the Buddhist and the Daoist registrars), 'physician-officials' are officials of the Imperial Academy of Medicine and the medical offices, and 'officials of yin-yang divination' are those in charge of astronomy, the calendar and geomancy. For all of these kinds of officials Output is the Subject: Output is the blessing, and it is also the image of monks, Daoists, physicians and medicine (see in the domain of illness LY.ILL.HDY.13, Output is medicine, monks and Daoists). Resource is the documents, and moving it controls Output, which is why it is unsuitable. The old commentary's 'Pressure should not be Void or hidden' says that however good Output is, Pressure cannot be missing either, since this is after all divining an office. Wang Hongxu says only that Output is the Subject and that Resource moving injures it.
