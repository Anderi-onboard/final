# 道业·何年道成与何地法隆：世临日月之期道成、世临生合之地法隆；乱动非清修之象、游魂无戒定之心（道业成身专凭世位，世病乱动游魂俱忌）

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 哪一年道成？世临日、月的时期。哪个地方法事兴隆？世临生合的地方。卦象乱动，不是清修之象；游魂，没有戒定（守戒、入定）的心。道业成就自身，专凭世的位置；世病、乱动、游魂，都忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In which year is the Way accomplished? In the period when the Self is on the Day or the Month. In which place does the teaching flourish? In the place where the Self meets generation and combination. A hexagram of disorderly movement is not the image of pure practice; Wandering Soul has no heart of precepts and meditation. The monastic vocation making the man depends wholly on the position of the Self; a sick Self, disorderly movement and Wandering Soul are all feared.

> [!note] 按
> 「道成」是修道有成，「法隆」是弘法兴盛。「世临日月之期」是世爻所属的地支正逢当年、当月（日、月）的时候；「世临生合之地」是世爻得生、得合的方位。「乱动」是卦里动爻多而乱；「游魂」是游魂卦，主游荡不定。「戒定」是佛家的戒和定（守戒律、入禅定）。书里自注：道业成就一个人，专看世爻的位置，世病、乱动、游魂都是忌。这是本章里讲应期和地点的一条，与 YM.94 的『刻期取证』相配。
>
> *Note:* 'Daocheng' is succeeding in cultivating the Way, and 'falong' is the teaching flourishing. 'The Self on the Day or the Month' is the branch of the Self line meeting the branch of the year or the month (day or month); 'the place where the Self meets generation and combination' is the direction where the Self line is generated and combined. 'Disorderly movement' is a hexagram with many moving lines and in disorder; 'Wandering Soul' is a Wandering Soul hexagram, which means drifting without settling. 'Jieding' is the Buddhist precepts and concentration (keeping the rules and entering meditation). The book's own note: the vocation making the man depends wholly on the position of the Self line, and a sick Self, disorderly movement and Wandering Soul are all feared. This entry deals with timing and place in this chapter, and goes with 'fixing a date for realisation' in YM.94.
