# 国事占大杀为灾：金主兵、水动主洪水之灾、土动主修治城垒；震离坎兑四方见应在边方、坤艮宫中事未宁；六爻不动卦无杀则天下太平

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国事占里，大杀是灾星：金主兵戈，水动主洪水之灾，土动主有修治城垒（或攻城）的事；大杀见于震、离、坎、兑四宫，应在边远的地方；见于坤、艮宫，国内的事情还没安宁；六爻不动、卦里没有杀，天下人民歌唱太平。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining affairs of state the Great Baleful spirit brings calamity: Metal rules weapons, Water moving means flood, Earth moving means the repair of city walls (or the assault on a city); when it appears in the four palaces Zhen, Li, Kan and Dui, it falls on the border regions; in the palaces Kun and Gen affairs at home are not yet settled; with the six lines still and no baleful spirit in the hexagram, the people of the world sing of peace.
