# 占朝国（二）：五爻为至尊——加吉神太岁仁慈之主、带杀白虎暴虐之君、与吉神生合必亲贤任能远佞去奸；初爻安静吉神持世生世万民悦服；二爻为侍臣、四爻会吉神克世生世必上忠君下安黎庶

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 五爻是至尊，加上吉神、太岁，是仁慈的君主；带煞、白虎，是暴虐的君主；（五爻）与吉神生合，必定亲近贤人、任用能人，远离佞臣、去除奸邪。初爻安静，吉神持世或生世，万民心悦诚服。本象的二爻是侍臣，带吉神，左右必定得到贤人；加凶煞的，多是奸邪谄佞。四爻会合吉神，克世或生世，必定向上忠于君主，向下安定百姓。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The fifth line is the supreme one; with an auspicious spirit and the Tai Sui added, he is a benevolent ruler; carrying a baleful star and the White Tiger, he is a tyrannical ruler; if it generates and combines with an auspicious spirit, he will surely draw near the worthy and employ the able, keep far from flatterers and dismiss the treacherous. If the first line is still and an auspicious spirit holds or generates the Self, the people are all pleased and submit. In the image, the second line is the attending minister; with an auspicious spirit, he will surely have worthy men about him; with a baleful star added, they are mostly treacherous and flattering. If the fourth line meets an auspicious spirit and controls or generates the Self, he will be loyal to the sovereign above and bring peace to the common people below.

> [!note] 按
> 「至尊」是君主。五爻在卦里是君位，「加吉神太岁」是五爻带吉神、又带当年太岁，为仁慈之君；「带杀白虎」是带凶煞和白虎，为暴虐之君。「亲贤任能，远佞去奸」是亲近贤人、任用能人，疏远奸佞。「万民悦服」是百姓心悦诚服。「侍臣」是君主身边的臣子，本书（YM.01、YM.50）里也以二爻为大臣、近臣。「吉神」「凶杀」是神煞。四爻是近君之位，会吉神又克世或生世，上忠下安。这一节是把爻位配成君臣百姓的细则，可与易冒功名章按六爻辨官位的高卑（YM.50）对着看：五爻是君、二爻是大臣、四爻近君之臣。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhizun' is the sovereign. The fifth line is the sovereign's place in a hexagram; 'with an auspicious spirit and the Tai Sui added' is the fifth line carrying an auspicious spirit and also the Tai Sui of the year, a benevolent ruler; 'carrying a baleful star and the White Tiger' is carrying a baleful star and the White Tiger, a tyrannical ruler. 'Qin xian ren neng, yuan ning qu jian' is drawing near the worthy and employing the able, keeping away from the flatterers and the treacherous. 'Wan min yue fu' is the people submitting gladly. 'Shichen' is the ministers beside the sovereign, and in this book (YM.01, YM.50) the second line is likewise the great minister or near minister. 'Auspicious spirits' and 'baleful stars' are spirit-stars. The fourth line is the place near the sovereign, and meeting an auspicious spirit while controlling or generating the Self is loyalty above and peace below. This section is the detailed rule that matches the line positions to ruler, ministers and people, and can be read against the way the chapter on fame and office in the Yimao tells the height of a post by the six lines (YM.50): the fifth line is the sovereign, the second a great minister, and the fourth a minister near the sovereign.
