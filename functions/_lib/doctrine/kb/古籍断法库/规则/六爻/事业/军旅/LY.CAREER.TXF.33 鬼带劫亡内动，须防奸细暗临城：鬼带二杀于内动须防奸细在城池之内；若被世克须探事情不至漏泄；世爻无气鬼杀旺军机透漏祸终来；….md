# 鬼带劫亡内动，须防奸细暗临城：鬼带二杀于内动须防奸细在城池之内；若被世克须探事情不至漏泄；世爻无气鬼杀旺军机透漏祸终来；内外鬼爻俱发动克世必有内应外合之患子动必无妨；鬼爻不带杀为害亦不凶

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼带劫杀、亡神在内卦发动，要防奸细暗中混进城来。官鬼带两种杀在内发动，要防城池之内有奸细之人；若被世克，要去探听情况，不至于泄漏；世爻无气而鬼、杀旺，军机泄漏，祸终究要来；内外的鬼爻都发动克世，必有内应外合的祸患，子孙动就一定无妨；鬼爻不带杀，为害也不凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure carrying the Robbery and Lost-Spirit killings and moving inside: guard against spies secretly entering the city. If Pressure carries the two baleful spirits and moves inside, guard against spies within the city; if it is controlled by the Self, one must scout out the situation so that nothing leaks; if the Self line has no qi and Pressure and the baleful spirits are strong, military secrets leak and in the end disaster comes; if the Pressure lines inside and outside both move and control the Self, there is sure to be the trouble of insiders answering outsiders, and if Output moves it is surely harmless; if the Pressure line carries no baleful spirit its harm is not fierce either.
