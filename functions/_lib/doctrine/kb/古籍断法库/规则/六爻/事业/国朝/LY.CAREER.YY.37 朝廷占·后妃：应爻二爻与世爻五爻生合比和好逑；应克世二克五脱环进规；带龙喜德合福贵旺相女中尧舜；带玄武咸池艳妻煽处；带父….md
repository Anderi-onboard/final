# 朝廷占·后妃：应爻二爻与世爻五爻生合比和好逑；应克世二克五脱环进规；带龙喜德合福贵旺相女中尧舜；带玄武咸池艳妻煽处；带父母旺相攻书史；带子孙旺相繁螽斯；子动生财能逮下；应居五爻阳位加太岁紫微华盖贵马旺相聚一爻垂帘听政

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 后妃：应爻、二爻与世爻、五爻相生、相合、比和的，是好配偶；应爻克世爻，二爻克五爻的，皇后摘下环佩进言规劝；应爻、二爻带青龙、喜神、德神、合神、福、贵人，旺相的，是女中的尧舜；带玄武、咸池的，是艳妻煽动祸乱当权的格局；带父母爻旺相的，专攻书史；带子孙爻旺相的，像螽斯一样子孙众多；子孙爻发动生财爻的，能恩惠及下；应爻落在五爻的阳位上，又加太岁、紫微、华盖、贵人、驿马，旺相，都聚在一爻的，垂帘听政。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Consort and empress. If the Other and the second line generate, combine or are in harmony with the Self and the fifth line, they are a fine match; if the Other controls the Self and the second line controls the fifth, the empress takes off her ring and gives admonition; if the Other and the second line carry the Dragon, Joy, Virtue, Combination, Fortune and the Noble and are thriving, she is a Yao and Shun among women; if they carry the Dark Tortoise and Xianchi, it is the pattern of the beautiful wife who stirs up trouble and holds sway; if they carry Resource thriving, she devotes herself to books and histories; if they carry Output thriving, her descendants are as many as katydids; if Output moves to generate Wealth, she is bounteous to those below; if the Other sits on a yang position of the fifth line with Tai Sui, Ziwei, Canopy, the Noble and the Horse added, thriving and all gathered in one line, she rules from behind the curtain.

> [!note] 按
> 「脱环进规」按字面是摘下环佩、进言规劝，书里没有解释，译文照字面。「艳妻煽处」出自《诗经·十月之交》「艳妻煽方处」，说美貌的妻子煽动祸乱、当权。「螽斯」出自《诗经·螽斯》，比喻子孙众多；「逮下」出自《诗经·樛木》，指恩惠及于下人；「垂帘听政」是太后（皇后）隔着帘子代皇帝处理政事。「好逑」是好配偶。紫微是一颗神煞。
>
> *Note:* 'Tuo huan jin gui' is by the letter taking off the ring and giving counsel, which the book does not explain, and the translation follows the letter. 'Yan qi shan chu' comes from the Odes, 'Shi yue zhi jiao', 'the beautiful wife stirs up trouble and holds sway'. 'Zhongsi', the katydid, is from the Odes, 'Zhongsi', an image of many descendants; 'daixia' is from the Odes, 'Jiumu', and means favour reaching those below; 'chuilian tingzheng' is the empress dowager handling affairs behind a curtain in place of the emperor. 'Haoqiu' is a good match. Ziwei is a spirit-star.
