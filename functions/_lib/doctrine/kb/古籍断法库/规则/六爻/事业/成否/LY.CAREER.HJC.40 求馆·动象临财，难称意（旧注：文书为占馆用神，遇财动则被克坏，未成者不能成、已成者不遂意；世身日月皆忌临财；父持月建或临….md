# 求馆·动象临财，难称意（旧注：文书为占馆用神，遇财动则被克坏，未成者不能成、已成者不遂意；世身日月皆忌临财；父持月建或临生旺庶亦可成）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 文书（父母）是占馆的用神，遇到财爻发动就被克坏：没成的不能成，已经成的也不称意；世爻、身爻、日辰、月建都忌讳临着财爻；如果父母持月建，或者临在生旺之地，或许还能成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource, the document, is the Subject in divining a school post, and if Wealth moves it is controlled and spoiled: what has not yet come about cannot, and what has come about is not to one's liking; the Self, the Body, the Day and the Month all dislike resting on Wealth; if Resource holds the Month or rests in a place of birth or thriving, it may perhaps still come through.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联和 LY.CAREER.HJC.01 是一个道理的两面：仕宦里要有财（俸禄），求馆里财动克父（文书）就坏事。这一章里的「父母」是书馆（见 LY.CAREER.HJC.35），所以财克父，就是财克了书馆。「用神」在这里指父母爻（书馆）。王洪绪这一行里同时写了「动象临财」和「空爻持世」两联的注，这一条取前一联。
>
> *Note:* This couplet and LY.CAREER.HJC.01 are two faces of one principle: in holding office one must have Wealth (the salary), and in a school post Wealth moving to control Resource (the document) spoils the matter. In this chapter 'Resource' is the school (see LY.CAREER.HJC.35), so Wealth controlling Resource is Wealth controlling the school. 'The Subject' here means the Resource line (the school). Wang Hongxu's line carries the comments on both couplets 'a moving sign meets Wealth' and 'a Void line holds the Self', and this entry takes the former.
