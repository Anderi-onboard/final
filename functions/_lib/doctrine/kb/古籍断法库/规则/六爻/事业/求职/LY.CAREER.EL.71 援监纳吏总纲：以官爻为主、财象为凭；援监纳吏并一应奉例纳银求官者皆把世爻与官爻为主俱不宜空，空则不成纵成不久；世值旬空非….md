# 援监纳吏总纲：以官爻为主、财象为凭；援监纳吏并一应奉例纳银求官者皆把世爻与官爻为主俱不宜空，空则不成纵成不久；世值旬空非久远、福神静旺永无殃；子孙虽为福德亦不可动动则伤官，又不可无无则少利

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 援监、纳吏，以及一切依照成例交银买官的，以官爻为主，以财爻为凭据，又都把世爻和官爻当主要来看，两个都不适宜落空：落空就办不成，即使办成也不长久。世爻落在旬空，不能长久；福神（子孙）安静而旺相，永远没有祸患。子孙虽然是福德，也不可以发动，一动就伤克官爻；也不可以没有，没有就少得利益。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In purchasing a place in the Academy, purchasing a clerkship, and every case of buying office by paying silver according to the statute, take the Pressure line as the chief and the Wealth image as the ground, with the Self line and the Pressure line as the principal things, and neither should be Void: if Void it does not succeed, and even if it succeeds it does not last. If the Self falls on the ten-day Void it will not be long-lasting; if Output, the fortune spirit, is quiet and thriving there is never any calamity. Output, though it is Fortune Virtue, must not move, for if it moves it hurts the star of office; and it must not be absent, for without it there is little profit.

> [!note] 按
> 「援监」是援例捐监，即捐钱取得国子监生的资格；「纳吏」是捐钱买一个吏的名额；「奉例纳银」是依照朝廷的捐纳成例交银。「旬空」是该旬里落空的两个地支（见术语表）。「福神」「福德」都是子孙。第一段解末尾两句（「身世之爻，若被鬼来冲克……决主名成利就」）和后面诀下解的头两句文字相同，是书里重复排印的，只在下一条引一处。这一条与增删卜易「援例」一组（ZS.22 起）取同一个关系：财、官、世三样缺一不可。
>
> *Note:* 'Yuanjian' is entering the Imperial Academy by contribution, that is obtaining the standing of an Academy student by paying; 'naji' is buying a place as a clerk; 'feng li na yin' is paying silver by the statute of contributions. 'Xunkong' is the two branches that fall Void in that ten-day week (see the glossary). 'Fushen' and 'fude' are both Output. The last two sentences of the first explanation ('If the Body and Self lines are clashed or controlled by Pressure ... one will surely win name and profit') are the same words as the first two sentences of the explanation after the next verse; the book prints them twice, and they are quoted only once, in the next entry. This entry takes the same relation as the 'contribution' group of the Zengshan Buyi (from ZS.22): of Wealth, the star of office and the Self, none can be lacking.
