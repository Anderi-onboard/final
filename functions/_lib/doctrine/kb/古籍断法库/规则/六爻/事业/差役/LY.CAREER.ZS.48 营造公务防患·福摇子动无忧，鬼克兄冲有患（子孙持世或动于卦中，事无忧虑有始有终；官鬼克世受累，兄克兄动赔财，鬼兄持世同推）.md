# 营造公务防患·福摇子动无忧，鬼克兄冲有患（子孙持世或动于卦中，事无忧虑有始有终；官鬼克世受累，兄克兄动赔财，鬼兄持世同推）

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 修陵修河等营造公务防患：福神摇动、子孙发动没有忧虑，官鬼克、兄弟冲就有祸患；子孙持世或在卦中发动，事情没有忧虑，有始有终；官鬼克世会受累，兄弟克世、发动会赔钱，官鬼、兄弟持世的也同样推断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Guarding against trouble in works such as repairing tombs and rivers: with the lucky spirit swaying and Output moving there is nothing to worry about, and with Pressure controlling and Peer clashing there is trouble; if Output holds the Self or moves in the hexagram, the matter has nothing to worry about and has a beginning and an end; if Pressure controls the Self one is burdened, if Peer controls the Self or moves one loses money, and the same judgment holds when Pressure or Peer holds the Self.
