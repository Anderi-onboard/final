# 征战占·方向（三）：出战内旺外衰可战、内衰外旺宜守、内克外可战、外克内宜守；内宫之方可战可攻；世爻生旺之方可遁可避；应爻鬼爻生旺之方宜避其锐，败死绝之方击其懈，墓胎之方防其伏

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出战还是固守，以及往哪个方位：内卦旺相、外卦衰弱的，可以出战；内卦衰弱、外卦旺相的，宜固守；内卦克外卦的，可以出战；外卦克内卦的，宜固守。内宫所在的方位，可战可攻；世爻生旺的方位，可遁走可躲避；应爻、官鬼爻生旺的方位，适宜避开对方的锋芒；败、死、绝的方位，适宜打击对方松懈之处；墓、胎的方位，适宜提防对方的埋伏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To fight or hold, and in which direction. If the inner trigram is thriving and the outer weak, one can go out and fight; if the inner is weak and the outer thriving, hold the line; if the inner controls the outer one can fight; if the outer controls the inner, hold the line. In the direction of the inner palace one can fight and attack; in the direction where the Self is born or thriving one can flee or evade; in the direction where the Other and the Pressure line are born or thriving, avoid their sharp edge; in the direction of spent, dead or extinct, strike where they are slack; in the direction of tomb or womb, guard against their ambush.

> | 又凡出战者，内旺外衰者，可战。 | 又，凡是出战的，内卦旺相、外卦衰弱的，可以出战。 | Again, whoever goes out to fight, if the inner is thriving and the outer weak, can fight. |

> | 内宫之方，可战可攻。 | 内宫所在的方位，可战可攻。 | In the direction of the inner palace one can fight and attack. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 前四句里「内旺外衰」「内克外」的「内」「外」指内卦、外卦，译文按内、外写；「内宫之方」指内卦所在的那一宫的方位（见 YY.51）。
>
> *Note:* In the first four sentences the 'inner' and 'outer' of 'inner thriving, outer weak' and 'inner controls outer' mean the inner and outer trigrams; 'the direction of the inner palace' means the direction of the palace that the inner trigram belongs to (see YY.51).
