# 官禄占·官职退复（一）：子旺动父休空官墓绝必退职；欲知退职岁时以卦身为主，从卦身数到占卜之月，数几位失职几月

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官职退复，失去职位：子孙旺相发动，父爻休囚落空，官星入墓入绝的，必定退职。想知道哪年哪月退职，只以卦身为主：从卦身所在的地支顺数到占卜的那个月，数过几个位置，就失职几个月——丑月占得噬嗑，卦身在戌，戌、亥、子，三位，失职三个月；午月占得艮，卦身在巳，一位，一个月；辰月占得蛊，卦身在寅，两位，两个月。其余照这样推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Loss and recovery of office, losing the post. If Output is thriving and moving, the seal line is resting and Void, and the star of office is in tomb or extinct, he surely loses his post. To know the year and time of losing it, take the hexagram Body as the main thing: count from the branch where the Body lies to the month of the divination, and the number of places passed is the number of months of the post lost. In a Chou month, getting Shihe with the Body at Xu: Xu, Hai, Zi, three places, the post is lost for three months; in a Wu month, getting Gen with the Body at Si: one place, one month; in a Chen month, getting Gu with the Body at Yin: two places, two months. The rest follow this.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 三个例子数的都是从卦身的地支顺着地支次序数到占卜月的地支：戌、亥、子、丑（过三位），巳、午（过一位），寅、卯、辰（过两位）。这三个卦身也和通行的起法相符：噬嗑世在五爻、阴世从午起，数到五爻是戌；艮世在上爻、阳世从子起，数到上爻是巳；蛊世在三爻、阳世从子起，数到三爻是寅。「退度」的「退」是退职的退，不是倒着数。
>
> *Note:* In all three examples the count runs forward along the order of the branches from the branch of the Body to the branch of the month of the divination: Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou (three places passed), Si, Wu (one place), Yin, Mao, Chen (two places). The three Bodies also agree with the usual way of finding it: in Shihe the Self is on the fifth line and a yin Self counts from Wu, which reaches Xu at the fifth; in Gen the Self is on the top line and a yang Self counts from Zi, which reaches Si at the top; in Gu the Self is on the third line and a yang Self counts from Zi, which reaches Yin at the third. The 'retreat' of 'retreating through places' is the retreat of retiring from office and not counting backwards.
