# 国朝·应带咸池临九五，武后革唐命而为周（旧注：应爻为皇后，带咸池其后必淫，更居九五是后居尊位，如吕后临朝；再旺动刑冲克害世爻、世或空亡，则如武后废中宗革唐为周）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻是皇后。应爻带咸池，皇后一定不检点；又落在第五爻，就是皇后居于至尊之位，像汉朝的吕后临朝称制；如果它再旺相发动、刑冲克害世爻，世爻又逢空亡，就像唐朝的武后废掉中宗、降为庐陵王，改唐为周。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other line is the empress. If it carries Xianchi, the empress is sure to be licentious; if it also sits in the fifth place, the empress holds the seat of honour, like Empress Lü of Han presiding at court and issuing edicts; if it is also thriving and moving, and punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Self, while the Self may be Void, it is like Empress Wu of Tang, who deposed Emperor Zhongzong, made him Prince of Luling, and turned the Tang mandate into Zhou.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 应带咸池临九五，武后革唐命而为周。 | 应爻带着咸池，又落在九五，就像武后改变了唐的国运、改唐为周。 | If the Other line carries Xianchi and sits in the fifth place, it is like Empress Wu who changed the mandate of Tang and made it Zhou. |

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5834 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应乃皇后之位。 | 应爻是皇后的位置。 | The Other line is the empress's place. |
> | 若带咸池，其后必淫。 | 如果带着咸池，那位皇后必定淫乱。 | If it carries Xianchi, the empress is sure to be licentious. |
> | 更居九五，乃是后居尊位。 | 再落在九五，就是皇后居于至尊之位。 | If it also sits in the fifth place, the empress holds the seat of honour. |
> | 如汉吕后，临朝称制。 | 就像汉朝的吕后，临朝执政、代行皇帝的号令。 | It is like Empress Lü of Han, who presided at court and issued edicts in the ruler's place. |
> | 更有旺动，刑冲克害世爻，世或空亡，必如唐武后废中宗为庐陵王，革唐命为周。 | 如果它再旺相发动，刑、冲、克、害世爻，世爻又落空亡，必定像唐朝的武后那样，废掉中宗、降为庐陵王，改唐的国运为周。 | If it is also thriving and moving, and punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Self, and the Self may be Void, it is surely like Empress Wu of Tang, who deposed Emperor Zhongzong, made him Prince of Luling, and changed the Tang mandate into Zhou. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1474 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 君自卜以应爻为皇后，若带咸池，其后必淫，更居九五尊位克世，如唐武后废中宗为庐陵王，革唐命为周。 | 国君自己占卜时，以应爻为皇后，如果带着咸池，那位皇后必定淫乱，再落在九五的尊位又来克世，就像唐朝的武后废掉中宗、降为庐陵王，改唐的国运为周。 | When the ruler casts for himself, take the Other line as the empress; if it carries Xianchi the empress is sure to be licentious, and if it also sits in the honoured seat of the fifth place and controls the Self, it is like Empress Wu of Tang, who deposed Zhongzong, made him Prince of Luling, and changed the Tang mandate into Zhou. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 两处条件不同：旧注要应爻再旺相发动、刑冲克害世爻，并说「世或空亡」；卜筮正宗只说应爻居九五尊位、克世，没有提世爻落空。
>
> *Note:* The conditions differ: the old commentary adds that the Other line is thriving and moving and punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Self, and says 'the Self may be Void'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says only that the Other line sits in the honoured fifth place and controls the Self, and does not mention the Self being Void.
