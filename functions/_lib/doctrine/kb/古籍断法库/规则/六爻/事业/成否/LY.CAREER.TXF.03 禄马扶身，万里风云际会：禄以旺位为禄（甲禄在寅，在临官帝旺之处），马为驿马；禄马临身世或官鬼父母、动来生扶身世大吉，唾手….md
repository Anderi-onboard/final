# 禄马扶身，万里风云际会：禄以旺位为禄（甲禄在寅，在临官帝旺之处），马为驿马；禄马临身世或官鬼父母、动来生扶身世大吉，唾手成名；大忌空亡与刑害

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 禄、马扶助自身，万里风云际会。禄是以旺位为禄（如甲的禄在寅，在临官、帝旺的地方），比喻人长大后食禄；马是驿马，落在身、世，或官鬼、父母上，以及发动来生扶身、世，是大吉，唾手成名的象；最忌空亡和刑、害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Emolument and the Horse supporting the self there is a meeting of wind and cloud across ten thousand li. The Emolument takes strength as the Emolument (for Jia it is at Yin, at the Office and Peak stages), likening a person grown up and eating a salary; the Horse is the Post Horse, and standing on the Body or the Self, or on Pressure or Resource, or moving to generate or support the Body and Self, it is greatly auspicious, the image of making a name as easily as spitting into one's palms; Void and punishment or harm are the great taboos.
