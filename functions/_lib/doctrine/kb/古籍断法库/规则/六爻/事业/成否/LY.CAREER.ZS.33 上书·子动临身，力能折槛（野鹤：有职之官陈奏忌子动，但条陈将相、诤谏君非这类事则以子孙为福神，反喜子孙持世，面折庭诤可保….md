# 上书·子动临身，力能折槛（野鹤：有职之官陈奏忌子动，但条陈将相、诤谏君非这类事则以子孙为福神，反喜子孙持世，面折庭诤可保无祸；古以子发乾宫为吉，非也；书中例：旅卦，劾奏权奸，果蒙准行）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 条陈将相、诤谏君过、弹劾奸臣这类事，和一般的奏事不一样：这里子孙要当福神用，所以反而喜欢子孙持世，即使当面诤谏也可以保证没有祸。但有官职的人如果只是为了保全功名，子孙发动时千万不要去做。古人以子孙在乾宫发动为吉，是不对的，子孙是制住官鬼的福神，没有听说只在乾宫才是福神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For proposals about the generals and chancellors, remonstrance against the ruler's faults and the impeachment of a treacherous minister, the rule differs from ordinary memorials: here Output is used as the lucky spirit, so Output holding the Self is welcome instead, and even if one contends to the ruler's face one can be sure of no misfortune. But an official who only wants to preserve his rank must not do it when Output moves. The ancients took Output moving in the Qian palace as auspicious, which is wrong: Output is the lucky spirit that restrains Pressure, and it has never been heard that it is so only in the Qian palace.

> [!note] 按
> 「折槛」是汉朝朱云当面直谏、攀折殿槛的典故，书里没有讲，译文是按通行的意思注的。前一条（ZS.31）说有官职的人上奏忌见子孙，这一条说子孙持世反吉，野鹤自己的解释是：要看事情的大小。
>
> *Note:* 'Breaking the railing' alludes to Zhu Yun of Han, who remonstrated to the emperor's face and clung to and broke the palace railing; the book does not tell it, and the translation gives the usual sense. The previous entry (ZS.31) says an official with a post should not see Output in a memorial and this one says Output holding the Self is good instead; the Old Man's own explanation is that it depends on the size of the matter.
