# 升选候补·世官破而休望，世福空而亦升（世临月破纵官星持世亦不能升；子孙持世值旬空亦不当升，但占两三日之内者许必得，因子孙未出空、不克官；书中例三个）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻或官星被破就不要指望，世爻上的子孙落空却也能升。世爻落在月破上，即使官星持世也升不了，还要防别的变故；子孙持世又遇旬空，也不该升。但如果占的是两三天之内的事，可以答应一定得到，因为子孙还没有出空、没有克官星。作者占过三个人，都得了官，得官后随即死了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self or the star of office is broken, give up hope; if the Self sits on an Output line that is Void, one is promoted all the same. If the Self is Month-broken, one cannot be promoted even when the star of office holds the Self, and one must guard against other changes; if Output holds the Self and meets Void, one should not be promoted either. But if the question concerns the next two or three days, one may grant that it will surely be had, since Output has not yet left the Void and does not yet control the star of office. The author divined for three men; all got office, and all died soon after getting it.

> [!note] 按
> 「世福空而亦升」按注文的意思是：世爻持子孙又落旬空，一般不该升，只有占两三天之内的事才许得，因为子孙没出空就克不了官。「前卦点差」「又悟前占卦之差」是说第一例让他看出了自己断错的地方，后两例才断准。
>
> *Note:* 'The Self on a Void Output line is promoted all the same' means, by the note, that when the Self holds Output and it falls in Void one is generally not due for promotion, and may be granted it only for a question of two or three days, since Output cannot control the star of office while it has not left the Void. 'The former hexagram pointed out the error' and 'also realized the error of the earlier divination' mean that the first example showed him where his own judgment had been wrong, and he judged the next two correctly.
