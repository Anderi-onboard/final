# 除名脱役：世空自退无忧虑、应陷他非上籍丁，世应俱空官又动傍人顶役两无刑；世值空亡自当解散，应值空亡他难顶替，世应皆空官又动者自能解脱，彼不克当必有傍人代役已免其忧

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻落空，自己就能退役，没有忧虑；应爻落陷，别人就不能上籍当丁；世应都空而官又发动，旁人顶役，两边都没有刑害。世爻值空亡，自然解散；应爻值空亡，他难以顶替；世、应都空、官又发动的，自己能解脱；他担当不了，一定有旁人代役，自己就免了忧虑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self is Void one retires of oneself and has no worry; if the Other is sunk, the other man cannot be entered on the rolls as an able-bodied adult; if the Self and the Other are both Void and the star of office moves, another man takes over the service and neither side is punished. If the Self falls on Void, one is naturally released; if the Other falls on Void, he can hardly take one's place; if the Self and the Other are both Void and the star of office moves, one can free oneself; if he cannot bear it, another man will stand in for the service, and one is free of the worry.

> [!note] 按
> 「上籍丁」是登记入役籍的成年男丁。「顶役」「代役」都是由别人顶替服役。「傍人」是旁人。「已免其忧」的「已」疑是「己」，译作「自己免了忧虑」。
>
> *Note:* 'Shang ji ding' is an adult male entered on the service roll. 'Dingyi' and 'daiyi' both mean another man serving in one's place. 'Bangren' is another person. The 'yi' of 'yi mian qi you' is presumably 'ji', oneself, and it is translated 'one is free of the worry'.
