# 户役轻重：以官鬼为主、生克为凭；民当户役有轻重之不同，卦出官爻取旺衰之可验；官旺则役重、官衰则役轻，克世则厚而非薄、伤身则重而不轻，带青龙而吉、加白虎而凶；或空或绝皆主无忧，鬼纵休囚克世还当繁重，官虽旺相生世赔费还轻，值青龙终无责罚临白虎必犯官刑

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 百姓承当户役，有轻重的不同；卦里出现的官爻，看它的旺衰就可以验证。官爻居于旺相，役一定高大强劲；休囚，才细小；官克世爻，役厚而不薄；伤身爻，役重而不轻；带青龙是吉，加白虎是凶。要知道户役的轻重，必须看官爻的衰旺：旺就重大，衰就轻微；或者落空，或者入绝，都主没有忧虑；官鬼即使休囚，如果克世，还是繁重；官爻虽然旺相，但生世爻，赔费还轻；值青龙，终究没有责罚；临白虎，必定触犯官刑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The people bear household services of lighter and heavier kinds, and the Pressure line that appears in the hexagram can be tested by its strength or weakness. If it is in a thriving place the service will certainly be high and strong; if it is resting or imprisoned it is small and fine; if it controls the Self the service is thick and not thin; if it hurts the Body it is heavy and not light; carrying the Azure Dragon it is called auspicious, and adding the White Tiger it is called ominous. To know whether the household service is light or heavy, look at the strength of the Pressure line: if thriving it is heavy and large, if weak it is light and slight; if Void or extinct, in either case there is nothing to worry about; even if Pressure is resting or imprisoned, if it controls the Self the burden will still be heavy; even if the star of office is thriving, if it generates the Self the cost of making up shortfalls is still light; on the Azure Dragon there is in the end no blame or punishment, and on the White Tiger one will surely run into official punishment.

> [!note] 按
> 「户役」是民户承当的徭役，应指里甲、粮长、解户一类（明代的户役）；「解户」是押送钱粮解往官府的户，「赔」是赔补短欠。章名在书里缺，按内容拟为「户役」（见谋差顶役一条的说明）。这一章与前面各章不同：官鬼在这里就是役，旺则役重、衰则役轻，「重」「轻」说的是负担，不是吉凶；青龙带吉、白虎带凶才说吉凶。「细小」「高强」是役的大小轻重。
>
> *Note:* 'Huyi', household service, is the corvée borne by a household, and presumably means the kind of service of the neighbourhood-and-tithing system, grain captains and conveyor households (household service under the Ming); 'jiehu' is a household that conveys money and grain to the authorities, and 'pei' is making up a shortfall. The chapter title is missing in the book and is supplied as 'household service' from the content (see the note to the entry on seeking a commission). This chapter differs from the earlier ones: Pressure here is the service itself, so when thriving the service is heavy and when weak it is light; 'heavy' and 'light' speak of the burden and not of good or ill, and it is the Azure Dragon and the White Tiger that carry good and ill. 'Xixiao' and 'gaoqiang' are the size and weight of the service.
