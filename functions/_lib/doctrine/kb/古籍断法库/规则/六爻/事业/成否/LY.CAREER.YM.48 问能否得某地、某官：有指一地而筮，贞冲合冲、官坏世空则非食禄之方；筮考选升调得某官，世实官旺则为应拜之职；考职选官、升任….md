# 问能否得某地、某官：有指一地而筮，贞冲合冲、官坏世空则非食禄之方；筮考选升调得某官，世实官旺则为应拜之职；考职选官、升任调缺、指是地是官同此法

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有指定一个地方来占，问能不能得到这个地方：本卦六冲、合处逢冲、官坏世空，就不是食禄的地方。占考选、升调，问能不能得到某个官职：世爻实、官旺，就是应当拜受的职位。考职选官、升任调缺，或者指定哪个地方、哪个官来占，都用这个办法。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If one points to a particular place and divines whether one will get that place: if the original hexagram is a six-clash, a combination is clashed, Pressure is damaged and the Self is Void, it is not a place to draw salary. If one divines about selection by examination, promotion or transfer and asks whether one will get a certain office: when the Self is solid and Pressure thriving, it is the post one is due to be appointed to. Taking a post by examination, selecting an official, being promoted into or transferred to a vacancy, or pointing to a certain place or a certain office, are all divined by this same method.

> [!note] 按
> 「食禄之方」是能领俸禄的地方，也就是任所。「贞冲合冲」是本卦（贞）是六冲、以及合处逢冲，这是三冲里的两种（见 YM.07 的注）。「应拜之职」是该受任命的职位。这一条是『指名问』：升迁（YM.42）问的是升不升，这里问的是是不是这个地、这个官，所以要世实官旺、不犯冲坏。和易隐官禄占的任所、候选（YY.07、YY.11）可对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Shilu zhi fang' is a place where one can draw salary, that is, the place of the post. 'Zhenchong hechong' is the original hexagram (zhen) being a six-clash and a combination meeting a clash, two of the three clashes (see the note to YM.07). 'Ying bai zhi zhi' is the post one is due to be appointed to. This entry is 'asking by name': promotion (YM.42) asks whether one is promoted, and here the question is whether it is this place or this office, so the Self must be solid and Pressure thriving, with no clash or damage. Compare the place of the post and the candidacy in the Yi Yin on official careers (YY.07, YY.11).
