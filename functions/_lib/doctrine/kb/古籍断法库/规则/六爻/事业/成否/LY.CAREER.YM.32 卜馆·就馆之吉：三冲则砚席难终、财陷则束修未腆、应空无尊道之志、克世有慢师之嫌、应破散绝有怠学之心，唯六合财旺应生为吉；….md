# 卜馆·就馆之吉：三冲则砚席难终、财陷则束修未腆、应空无尊道之志、克世有慢师之嫌、应破散绝有怠学之心，唯六合财旺应生为吉；馆定之后随墓助伤不可犯、世用空破散绝不可遇、官鬼不可动，动则以五行八宫六神诸星详之，游魂不久三冲不定

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 已经聘定，问去就馆吉不吉：三冲，教席难以做满；财陷，酬金不丰；应空，东家没有尊师重道的志向；应克世，有怠慢先生的嫌疑；应破、散、绝，有懈怠学业的心；只有六合、财旺、应生世才是吉。馆已经定了，要问我在那里能不能身安心定：随墓、助伤不可犯；世用空、破、散、绝不可遇；官鬼不可动；动了就用五行、八宫、六神、诸星细看。游魂主不长久，三冲主不安定。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Once the engagement is made, in asking whether taking up the post is auspicious: the three clashes, and the seat at the inkstone is hard to keep to the end; Wealth fallen, and the tuition gift is not generous; the Other Void, and the family has no will to honour the teacher and the Way; the Other controlling the Self, and there is a suspicion of slighting the teacher; the Other broken, scattered or extinct, and there is a heart of idleness about study; only Six-Combine, thriving Wealth and the Other generating the Self are auspicious. The post is settled; in asking whether I shall be at ease in body and settled in mind there, following into the tomb and helping the injury must not be committed, the Self or the Subject Void, broken, scattered or extinct must not be met, and Pressure must not move; if it moves, examine it by the five phases, the eight palaces, the six spirits and the stars. Wandering Soul does not last, and the three clashes are unsettled.

> [!note] 按
> 「砚席」是教书的席位，「束修」是给先生的酬金，「未腆」是不丰厚。「就馆」是应聘上任。「随墓助伤」书里没有再注，是前几章反复出现的忌：随着官鬼入墓、帮着官鬼伤世（见 YM.13）。「世用」是世爻和用神。「游魂不久」是游魂主变动不长久。书里的注自己说明那三组（八宫、五行诸星、六神）都只是大略举例：八宫取象，艮是少男，所以祸由童仆起，兑是少女，所以话从妇女生，乾坤是老父老母，所以防老人，震巽是长男长女，所以防小工匠，坎主盗、离主火；五行金主丧、火主是非、土主病、水主失、木主折；诸星里咸池主淫、天贼主盗、官符主讼、驿马主迁；六神里龙主酒、虎主兵丧。「趄咨」疑是「趑趄咨嗟」（迟疑叹息），「遁闷」是退缩郁闷。「官鬼动」的忌点原文只说「鬼静无戒，鬼动有忌」，后面的举例是各宫各神各自带来的具体事。
>
> *Note:* 'Yanxi' is the seat of a teacher, 'shuxiu' is the tuition gift to the teacher, and 'weitian' is not generous. 'Jiuguan' is taking up an engagement. The book does not gloss 'following into the tomb and helping the injury', which is a taboo recurring in earlier chapters: following Pressure into the tomb and helping Pressure to injure the Self (see YM.13). 'Self or Subject' is the Self line and the Subject. 'Wandering Soul does not last' is that Wandering Soul means change and no lasting. The note in the book says itself that the three groups (the eight palaces, the five phases with the stars, and the six spirits) are only rough listings: by the images of the eight palaces, Gen is the youngest son, so trouble starts from young servants, Dui is the youngest daughter, so talk comes from women, Qian and Kun are the old father and mother, so guard against old people, Zhen and Xun are the eldest son and daughter, so guard against petty craftsmen, Kan governs thieves and Li governs fire; of the five phases Metal governs mourning, Fire disputes, Earth illness, Water loss and Wood breakage; among the stars the Peach Blossom governs lewdness, the Heavenly Thief theft, the Official Charge lawsuits and the Post Horse moving away; among the six spirits the Dragon governs wine and the Tiger soldiers and mourning. 'Quzi' is presumably 'zijuzi jie', hesitating and sighing, and 'dunmen' is shrinking back in gloom. For Pressure moving, the main text says only 'Pressure still, nothing to guard against; Pressure moving, there is something to avoid', and the examples after are the particular troubles each palace and each spirit brings.
