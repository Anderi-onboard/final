# 谒贵占·馈送：有馈送而谒贵以应爻为用；应逢空死墓绝胎人不遇、遇亦不投；财带退神动、财动化退神物不纳；应财旺动生合世馈薄答厚；应动刑害克冲世而间爻又动被间情疏

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 带着礼物去谒见贵人：以应爻为用神。应爻逢空、死、墓、绝、胎的，人遇不到，遇到了也不投合；财爻带退神发动，以及财爻发动变成退神的，礼物不被收纳；应爻的财旺相发动、生合世爻的，送得薄而回报厚；应爻发动刑、害、克、冲世爻，而间爻又发动的，被人从中挑拨而情分疏远。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Calling on a noble with a gift. Take the Other as the Subject. If the Other meets Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, the man is not met, and if met he does not take to the visitor; if Wealth carries the Retreating spirit and moves, or Wealth moves and turns into a Retreating spirit, the gift is not accepted; if the Wealth of the Other is thriving and moving and generates or combines with the Self, the gift is slight and the return generous; if the Other moves to punish, harm, control or clash with the Self and a line between also moves, someone comes between them and the feeling cools.

## 《易隐·谒贵占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2711 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining a call on a noble", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 有馈送而谒贵者，以应爻为用也。 | 带着礼物去谒见贵人的，以应爻为用神。 | For one calling on a noble with a gift, take the Other as the Subject. |
> | 应逢空死墓绝胎者，人不遇遇亦不投也。 | 应爻逢空、死、墓、绝、胎的，人遇不到，遇到了也不投合。 | If the Other meets Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, the man is not met, and if met he does not take to the visitor. |
> | 财带退神动，(丁丑丁未壬辰壬戌)，与财动化退神者，物不纳也。 | 财爻带退神发动（丁丑、丁未、壬辰、壬戌），以及财爻发动变成退神的，礼物不被收纳。 | If Wealth carries the Retreating spirit (Dingchou, Dingwei, Renchen, Renxu) and moves, or Wealth moves and turns into a Retreating spirit, the gift is not accepted. |
> | 应财旺动生合世者，馈薄而答厚也。 | 应爻的财旺相发动、生合世爻的，送得薄而回报厚。 | If the Wealth of the Other is thriving and moving and generates or combines with the Self, the gift is slight and the return generous. |
> | 应动刑害克冲世，而间爻又动者，被间而情踈也。 | 应爻发动刑、害、克、冲世爻，而间爻又发动的，被人从中挑拨而情分疏远。 | If the Other moves to punish, harm, control or clash with the Self and a line between also moves, someone comes between them and the feeling cools. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「财带退神动」后面的括号（丁丑、丁未、壬辰、壬戌）是退神的干支，《卜筮全书》写「退神者，壬戌、壬辰、丁丑、丁未」（见 YY.19）；所以这句的两半说的是两件事：财爻自己带着退神的干支发动，和财爻发动后变成退神。「间爻」是世爻和应爻之间的爻（见术语表）。
>
> *Note:* The bracket after 'Wealth carrying the Retreating spirit and moving', (Dingchou, Dingwei, Renchen, Renxu), gives the ganzhi of the Retreating spirit; the Bushi Quanshu writes 'the Retreating spirit is Renxu, Renchen, Dingchou and Dingwei' (see YY.19). The two halves of the sentence therefore speak of two things: Wealth itself carrying a Retreating ganzhi and moving, and Wealth moving and then turning into a Retreating spirit. 'A line between' is a line between the Self and the Other (see the glossary).
