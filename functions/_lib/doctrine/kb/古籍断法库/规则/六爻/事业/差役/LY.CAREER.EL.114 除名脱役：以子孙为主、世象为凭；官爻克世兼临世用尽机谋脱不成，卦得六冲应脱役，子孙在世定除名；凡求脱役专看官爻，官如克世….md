# 除名脱役：以子孙为主、世象为凭；官爻克世兼临世用尽机谋脱不成，卦得六冲应脱役，子孙在世定除名；凡求脱役专看官爻，官如克世临世欲退其役难以推开，若得六冲或子孙持世或子孙交重皆主役去名消

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求脱役（解除差役），以子孙为主，以世爻为凭据。官爻克世爻、又临在世爻上，用尽机谋也脱不成；卦得六冲，应该能脱役；子孙在世爻上，一定除名。凡是求脱役，专看官爻：官如果克世、临世，想退掉这份役，难以推开；如果得到六冲，或者子孙持世，或者子孙发动，都主役免名消。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking release from service, take Output as the chief and the Self image as the ground. If the Pressure line controls the Self and also rests on the Self, however much scheming one uses one cannot get free; if the hexagram is a six-clash, one should be freed from the service; if Output is on the Self, one will surely be struck from the rolls. In every seeking of release from service, look especially at the Pressure line: if it controls the Self or rests on the Self, it is hard to push off the service one wants to leave; if one gets a six-clash, or Output holds the Self, or Output moves, in every case the service goes and the name is cleared.

> [!note] 按
> 「脱役」是摆脱差役，「除名」是从役籍里除去名字。章名在书里写了两次，头一次写作「除吊脱役」，「吊」是「名」的误写。「推开」是推脱开。「交重」是动爻的记号，泛指爻发动。这一章要看子孙，与前面谋差顶役（要官爻）正好相反：要脱役，官爻是缠住你的，子孙才是解脱的。
>
> *Note:* 'Tuoyi' is getting free of a service and 'chuming' is having one's name removed from the service roll. The chapter title is written twice in the book, the first time as 'chudiao tuoyi', where the 'diao' is a miswriting of 'ming'. 'Tuikai' is pushing off. 'Jiaozhong' is the mark of a moving line and here means a line moving in general. This chapter looks at Output, which is exactly the opposite of the earlier chapter on seeking a commission (which wanted the Pressure line): to be released from a service, the Pressure line is what holds one fast and Output is what frees one.
