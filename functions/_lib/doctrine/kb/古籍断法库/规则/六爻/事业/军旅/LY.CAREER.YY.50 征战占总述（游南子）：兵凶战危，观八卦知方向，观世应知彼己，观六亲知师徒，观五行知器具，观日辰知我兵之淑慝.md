# 征战占总述（游南子）：兵凶战危，观八卦知方向，观世应知彼己，观六亲知师徒，观五行知器具，观日辰知我兵之淑慝

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 征战占总述：用兵凶险，打仗危险，关系人命生死和国家安危，所以要慎重。看八卦来知道方向；看世爻、应爻来知道彼此；看六亲来知道将帅和士卒；看五行来知道器械用具；看日辰来知道我方兵士的好坏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Overview of divining war. Arms are ill-omened and war is perilous, bearing on life and death and the safety of the state, so one must be careful. Look at the eight trigrams to know the direction; at the Self and Other to know them and us; at the six kin to know the commanders and the troops; at the five elements to know the arms and gear; and at the Day to know whether my soldiers are good or bad.

## 游南子(《易隐》引)

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2013 行 · Master Younan (quoted in the Yi Yin)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 游南子曰，兵凶战危，古人慎之，谓其关人命之生死，系宗稷之安危也。 | 游南子说：兵事凶险，战争危险，古人对它很慎重，是因为它关系到人命的生死，牵系着宗庙社稷的安危。 | Master Younan says: arms are ill-omened and war is perilous, and the ancients were careful of it, because it bears on the life and death of men and hangs on the safety of the ancestral temple and the altars of the state. |
> | 故观八卦，以知方向。 | 所以看八卦，来知道方向。 | So look at the eight trigrams to know the direction. |
> | 观世应以知彼己，观六亲以知师徒，观五行以知器具，观日辰，以知我兵之淑慝。 | 看世爻、应爻来知道彼此，看六亲来知道将帅和士卒，看五行来知道器械用具，看日辰来知道我方兵士的善恶。 | Look at the Self and Other to know them and us, at the six kin to know the commanders and the troops, at the five elements to know the arms and gear, and at the Day to know whether my soldiers are good or bad. |
> | 盖绳绳乎其慎之也。 | 大概是要时时谨慎地对待它。 | It is, after all, to be treated with constant care. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是征战占的提纲，后面五节（方向、彼己、六亲、五行、日辰）就是按这个顺序排的。「绳绳」书里写作「盖绳绳乎其慎之也」，字面不很清楚，译文取「时时、连绵不断」的意思。
>
> *Note:* This section is the outline of the divination of war, and the five sections after it (direction, them and us, the six kin, the five elements and the Day) follow this order. In 'sheng sheng hu qi shen zhi ye', the sense of 'sheng sheng' is not clear from the letter, and the translation takes the sense of 'constantly, without a break'.
