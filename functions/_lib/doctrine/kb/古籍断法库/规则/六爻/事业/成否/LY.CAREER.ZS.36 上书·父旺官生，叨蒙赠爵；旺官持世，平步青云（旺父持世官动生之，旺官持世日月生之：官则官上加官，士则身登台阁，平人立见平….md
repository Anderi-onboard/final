# 上书·父旺官生，叨蒙赠爵；旺官持世，平步青云（旺父持世官动生之，旺官持世日月生之：官则官上加官，士则身登台阁，平人立见平步登云；野鹤：断卦最重来人之一念，借上书作阶梯求名者，官旺文不旺亦可许封官，因文乃敲门瓦）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上书求赠爵封官：父母旺相、官星生它，或者旺相的官星持世，或者旺相的父母持世、官星发动生它，或者旺官持世、有日月生它，做官的就官上加官，读书人就位至台阁，平民得到这个卦，立刻平步青云。野鹤说：断卦最看重的是来占的人心里的那个念头；借上书当阶梯来求功名的人，官星旺相而文书不旺，也可以答应他得到赠官封职，因为他本来的念头是为了得官，文书只是敲门砖，所以看重官星而不看重父母。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On submitting a memorial to win a bestowed rank or office: if Resource is thriving and the star of office generates it, or a thriving star of office holds the Self, or a thriving Resource holds the Self and the star of office moves to generate it, or a thriving star of office holds the Self and the Day and Month generate it, an official gets office added to office, a scholar reaches the high ministries, and a commoner who gets it at once climbs to the clouds. The Old Man of Yehe says: the most important thing in judging a hexagram is the one thought of the person who comes; for one who uses a memorial as a ladder to seek a name, if the star of office is thriving though the document is not, one may still grant that he will be given a post and title, because his first thought was to gain office and the document is only the tile to knock at the door, so the star of office is weighed and not Resource.
