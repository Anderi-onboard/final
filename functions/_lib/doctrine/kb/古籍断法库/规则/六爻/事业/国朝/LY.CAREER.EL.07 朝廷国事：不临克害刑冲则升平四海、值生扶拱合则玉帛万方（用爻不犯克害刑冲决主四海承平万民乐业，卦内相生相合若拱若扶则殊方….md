# 朝廷国事：不临克害刑冲则升平四海、值生扶拱合则玉帛万方（用爻不犯克害刑冲决主四海承平万民乐业，卦内相生相合若拱若扶则殊方绝域皆来朝贡）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻不遇克、害、刑、冲，天下升平；遇到生、扶、拱、合，四方都来献玉帛。克害刑冲各有另章的起例；用爻不犯这些的，必定是四海承平、万民乐业；卦内相生相合、有拱有扶的，连远方绝域也都来朝贡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line does not meet control, harm, punishment or clash, the realm is in peace; if it meets generation, support, flanking or combination, all quarters bring jade and silk. The rules for control, harm, punishment and clash are set out in other chapters; if the Subject line is not hit by them, the four seas are at peace and the people content in their work; if within the hexagram there is generation and combination, flanking and support, even distant lands and remote regions come to pay tribute.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「拱」是两个爻夹着另一个爻（拱夹）。「玉帛」是诸侯朝会的礼物。「用爻」是这一联里没有写出、解里补出的主语。
>
> *Note:* 'Gong', flanking, is two lines holding another between them. 'Jade and silk' are the gifts of the feudal lords at a court assembly. The subject, the Subject line, is not written in the couplet and is supplied by the explanation.
