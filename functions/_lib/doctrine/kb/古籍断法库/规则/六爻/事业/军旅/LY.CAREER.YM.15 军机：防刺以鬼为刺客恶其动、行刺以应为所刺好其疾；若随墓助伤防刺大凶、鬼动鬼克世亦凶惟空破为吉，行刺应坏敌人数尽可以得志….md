# 军机：防刺以鬼为刺客恶其动、行刺以应为所刺好其疾；若随墓助伤防刺大凶、鬼动鬼克世亦凶惟空破为吉，行刺应坏敌人数尽可以得志，或世坏随墓助伤敌人虽灭我亦随亡，或鬼兴而世旺敌虽刺我我数未终，吉内藏凶凶中有吉辨之

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 防备刺客，以官鬼为刺客，厌恶它发动；去行刺，以应为被刺的人，喜欢它有疾（坏）。如果随墓、助伤，防刺就大凶；官鬼动、官鬼克世也凶，只有空、破才是吉。行刺时应坏，敌人命数尽了，可以得志；或者世坏、随墓助伤，敌人虽然灭了，我也跟着亡；或者官鬼兴旺而世旺，敌人虽然来刺我，我的命数还没有终结。吉里藏着凶，凶中有吉，要分辨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In guarding against assassins, Pressure is the assassin and its moving is disliked; in carrying out an assassination, the Other is the man to be stabbed and it is liked to be sick. If one follows into the tomb or helps Pressure to injure, guarding against an assassin is greatly ominous; Pressure moving, or Pressure controlling the Self, is also ominous, and only Void or break is auspicious. In carrying out an assassination, if the Other is damaged the enemy's number is used up and one can achieve one's aim; or if the Self is damaged and follows into the tomb or helps Pressure to injure, although the enemy is wiped out, I too die with him; or if Pressure flourishes and the Self is thriving, although the enemy strikes at me my number is not yet at its end. Ill lies hidden within good, and good within ill, so one must tell them apart.

> [!note] 按
> 「刺」「剌」在书里混用，都是行刺。「数」是命数、气数。「防刺」是防备被刺，「行刺」是我方去刺敌。这一条与易隐征战占「鬼暗动加大杀劫杀刑刃克世防刺客，遇福动必擒」（YY.56）取同一类象：官鬼暗动克世是刺客来；福神动能擒拿。
>
> *Note:* 'Ci' and 'la' are used interchangeably in the book, both meaning assassination. 'Shu' is one's allotted number, one's allotted span. 'Fang ci' is guarding against being stabbed, and 'xing ci' is my side going to stab the enemy. This entry takes the same kind of image as 'if Pressure moves hidden with great baleful spirits, Robbery and the blade controlling the Self, guard against an assassin, and if Output moves he is surely seized' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.56): Pressure moving in the dark and controlling the Self is the assassin coming, and Output moving can seize him.
