# 出师征伐：兄象单兴恐有绝粮之患，父爻独发岂无伤卒之忧；兄弟专主奸险，卦中独发旺带白虎则行凶劫掠，衰监玄武必被阴谋抽窃，得日辰克制兄弟则不失脱；父母能克子孙单动则我兵大忌，旺带凶煞决被刑伤，休加吉神则彼势亦不可小视

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄象单独兴起，恐怕有断粮的忧患；父爻独自发动，难道没有伤兵卒的忧虑？兄弟专主奸险的事，如果在卦中独自发动，旺相又带白虎，对方就行凶劫掠；衰弱而临玄武，必被阴谋抽窃；如果得到日辰克制兄弟，即使见到有人行劫，也决不会失脱，就像伏着木牛流马一样稳固。父母能克子孙，如果在爻间单独发动，对我的兵大为忌讳，又旺相带凶煞，必定被刑伤；如果休囚又加吉神，对方的势头稍微（强）也不可小看啊。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Peer image rises alone, fear the danger of cut-off grain; if the Resource line moves alone, how can there be no worry of injured soldiers? Peer governs crafty and dangerous matters, and if it moves alone in the hexagram, thriving and carrying the White Tiger, the enemy commits violence and plunder; if weak and sitting on the Dark Tortoise, there will surely be secret plots and pilfering; if the Day controls Peer, even if one sees plundering there will surely be no loss, as firm as if wooden oxen and gliding horses were hidden away. Resource can control Output, and if it moves alone among the lines, it is a great taboo for my troops, and if thriving and carrying baleful spirits, they are surely punished and injured; if it is resting yet carries auspicious spirits, even a slight strength on the enemy's side must not be made light of.

> [!note] 按
> 第二句在书里被断行隔开（「若得」在上一行末，「日辰克制兄弟」在下一行首），这里连起来读。「木牛流马」是诸葛亮造的运粮工具，这里比喻粮草稳固不失。「行劫声劫」书里写作「行凶声劫」，「声」疑是「抢」，译作「行凶劫掠」。这一条的前半和易隐征战占「兄为埋伏」（YY.61）、「兄动克财督饷侵渔」（YY.59）是同一类断语。
>
> *Note:* The second sentence is split by a line break in the book ('if one gets' at the end of one line and 'the Day controls Peer' at the start of the next) and is read here joined up. 'Wooden oxen and gliding horses' are the grain transport devices that Zhuge Liang made, an image here of provisions held firm and not lost. 'Xing xiong sheng jie' is written as such in the book, and 'sheng' is presumably 'qiang', to snatch; it is translated 'commits violence and plunder'. The first half of this entry is the same kind of judgment as 'Peer is the ambush' (YY.61) and 'a moving Peer controls Wealth and the supply official embezzles' (YY.59) in the Yi Yin war divination.
