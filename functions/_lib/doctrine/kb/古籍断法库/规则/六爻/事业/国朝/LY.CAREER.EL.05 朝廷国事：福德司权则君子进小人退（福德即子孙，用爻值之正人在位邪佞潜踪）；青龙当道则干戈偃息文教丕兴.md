# 朝廷国事：福德司权则君子进小人退（福德即子孙，用爻值之正人在位邪佞潜踪）；青龙当道则干戈偃息文教丕兴

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 福德（子孙）当权，君子被进用、小人退下：福德就是子孙，用爻值在子孙上，正直的人在位，邪佞的人隐藏踪迹。青龙当道，战事止息，文教大兴。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Fortune Virtue holds power, gentlemen advance and petty men retire: Fortune Virtue is Output, and if the Subject line sits on it, upright men are in office and the crafty and flattering hide their traces. If the Azure Dragon holds the road, arms are laid down and culture and teaching flourish.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

## 《易林补遗·朝廷国事章第四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 296 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 4: Affairs of the court and state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 青龙当道，干戈偃息文教丕兴。 | 青龙当道，战事止息，文教大兴。 | If the Azure Dragon holds the road, arms are laid down and culture and teaching flourish. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「干戈偃息」是兵器收起、战事停止；「丕兴」是大兴。这一联后面书里没有解说。
>
> *Note:* 'Gan ge yan xi' is weapons put away and war stopped; 'pi xing' is flourishing greatly. The book gives no explanation after this couplet.
