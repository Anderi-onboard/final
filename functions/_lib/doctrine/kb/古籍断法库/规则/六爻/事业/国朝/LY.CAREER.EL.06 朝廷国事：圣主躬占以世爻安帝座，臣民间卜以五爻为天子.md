# 朝廷国事：圣主躬占以世爻安帝座，臣民间卜以五爻为天子

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 君主自己占，用世爻来安放帝座；臣民代占，还是用五爻来定君王。当今皇帝亲自占的，以世爻安帝座；臣民推占的，仍以五爻为天子；各有各的道理，断然不会出错。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the sovereign divines for himself, set the throne by the Self line; when ministers and commoners divine on his behalf, fix the ruler by the fifth line. When the present emperor divines in person, the throne is set by the Self; when ministers and commoners divine for him, the fifth line is still taken as the Son of Heaven; each has its principle, and there will surely be no error.

## 《易林补遗·朝廷国事章第四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 297 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 4: Affairs of the court and state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 圣主躬占，当以世爻安帝座； | 圣明的君主亲自占，当以世爻安放帝座； | When the wise sovereign divines in person, the throne is set by the Self line; |
> | 臣民间卜，还将五位定君王。 | 臣民代占，还是用五爻来定君王。 | when ministers and commoners divine on his behalf, the ruler is still fixed by the fifth line. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 298 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 云当今躬卜者，当以世爻而安帝座。 | 说的是当今亲自占卜的，当以世爻来安放帝座。 | It means that one who in the present age divines in person should set the throne by the Self line. |
> | 臣庶推占，仍以五爻而为天子，各得具理断无外误。 | 臣民推占的，仍以五爻为天子，各有各的道理，断然不会出错。 | Ministers and commoners who divine on his behalf still take the fifth line as the Son of Heaven; each has its principle, and there will surely be no error. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一条与易隐朝廷占总述（YY.31）的「主上自占以世爻为用，臣庶代卜以五爻为用」完全一致。「间卜」的「间」字义不明，疑是「闻」或「代」，译文按「代占」译。
>
> *Note:* This entry agrees exactly with the Yi Yin court overview (YY.31): 'when the sovereign divines himself, take the Self as the Subject; when ministers and commoners divine on his behalf, take the fifth line as the Subject'. The character 'jian' in 'jian bu' is unclear and is presumably 'wen' or 'dai', and the translation reads 'divine on his behalf'.
