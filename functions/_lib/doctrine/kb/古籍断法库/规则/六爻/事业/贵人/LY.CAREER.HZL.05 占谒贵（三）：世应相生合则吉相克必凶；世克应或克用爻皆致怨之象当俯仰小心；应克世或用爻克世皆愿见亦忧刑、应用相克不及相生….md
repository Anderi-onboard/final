# 占谒贵（三）：世应相生合则吉相克必凶；世克应或克用爻皆致怨之象当俯仰小心；应克世或用爻克世皆愿见亦忧刑、应用相克不及相生；卦有身相见更看用爻、用爻生身尤好；卦无身又无用爻或用爻空亡终不见

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应相生、相合就吉，相克必然凶。世克应，或克用爻，都是招致怨恨之象，应当处处小心。应克世，或用爻克世，（他）都愿意见，但也担忧（受）刑责。应和用爻相克，不如相生。卦有身，见面时更要看用爻，用爻生身尤其好；卦无身，又无用爻，或者用爻空亡，终究见不到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self and the Other generate or combine with each other it is auspicious, and if they control each other it is surely ominous. The Self controlling the Other, or controlling the Subject line, are all signs of drawing resentment, and one should be careful in every move. The Other controlling the Self, or the Subject line controlling the Self, he is willing to see one, but there is also the worry of punishment. The Other and the Subject line controlling each other is not as good as generating each other. If the hexagram has a Body, in the meeting one looks further at the Subject line, and the Subject line generating the Body is especially good; if the hexagram has no Body and no Subject line, or the Subject line is Void, one is never seen.

> [!note] 按
> 「致怨」是招来怨恨，「俯仰小心」是一举一动都小心。「应克世」「用爻克世」是对方克我：对方愿意见，但因为克我，仍担心受到责罚（「忧刑」）。「应用相克不及相生」是对方的应爻和他的用爻之间，相生比相克好。「卦有身」是卦里有卦身（见术语表），相见时还要看用爻，用爻生卦身尤其好；「卦无身又无用爻」是卦身和用爻都没有，或用爻空亡，就终究见不到。这一节与易林补遗趋谒贵人的世应内外（EL.100）、世空应空（EL.101）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhiyuan' is drawing resentment, and 'fuyang xiaoxin' is being careful in every movement. 'The Other controlling the Self' and 'the Subject line controlling the Self' are the other side controlling me: the other side is willing to see me, but because he controls me there is still the worry of reproof ('worry of punishment'). 'The Other and the Subject line controlling each other is not as good as generating each other' is that between the Other line and his Subject line, generation is better than control. 'The hexagram has a Body' is the hexagram having a hexagram Body (see the glossary), in the meeting one looks further at the Subject line, and the Subject line generating the hexagram Body is especially good; 'the hexagram has no Body and no Subject line' is neither the Body nor the Subject line being present, or the Subject line being Void, and one is never seen. Read this section against the Self and the Other, inner and outer, in the Yilin Buyi on calling on the great (EL.100) and against the Self Void and the Other Void (EL.101).
