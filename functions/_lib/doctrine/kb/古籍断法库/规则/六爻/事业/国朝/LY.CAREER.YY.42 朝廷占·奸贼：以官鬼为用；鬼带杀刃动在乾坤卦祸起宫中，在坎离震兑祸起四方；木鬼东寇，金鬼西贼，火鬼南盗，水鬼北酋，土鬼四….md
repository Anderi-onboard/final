# 朝廷占·奸贼：以官鬼为用；鬼带杀刃动在乾坤卦祸起宫中，在坎离震兑祸起四方；木鬼东寇，金鬼西贼，火鬼南盗，水鬼北酋，土鬼四方散起

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 奸贼：以官鬼为用神。官鬼带凶煞和刃发动，在乾卦、坤卦的，祸乱起于宫中；在坎、离、震、兑的，祸乱起于四方。木爻的官鬼，是东方的寇盗；金爻的，是西方的贼；火爻的，是南方的盗；水爻的，是北方的酋首；土爻的，是四方散起的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Traitors and bandits. Take Pressure as the Subject. If Pressure carrying baleful spirits and the blade moves in Qian or Kun, the trouble arises within the palace; in Kan, Li, Zhen or Dui, it arises in the four quarters. A Wood Pressure line is brigands from the east; Metal, rebels from the west; Fire, robbers from the south; Water, a chieftain from the north; Earth, risings in scattered places on all sides.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 第二句「祸起官中」的「官」，按文意应是「宫」（宫中，即皇宫之内），因为乾坤是君与后，译文按「宫」译。
>
> *Note:* In the second sentence the character for 'official' in 'trouble arises in the official middle' is presumably a miscopy for 'palace' (within the palace), since Qian and Kun are the sovereign and the empress, and the translation follows 'palace'.
