# 卜筮元龟·趋谒（一）：贞悔相生吉、内外相凌凶且伤；世克应则群长怒、更相鬼爻为不祥

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 拜谒，内卦（贞）和外卦（悔）相生，吉利恰当；内外互相欺凌（相克、相冲），凶而且受伤；世克应，是众位尊长发怒，再加官鬼爻，就不祥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a call, the inner trigram (zhen) and the outer (hui) generating each other is fitting and auspicious; inner and outer bullying each other (controlling or clashing) is bad and brings harm; the Self controlling the Other means the elders are angry, and with the Pressure line besides it is inauspicious.

> | 内外相凌凶且伤（1）， | 内外互相欺凌，凶而且有伤（1）； | inner and outer bullying each other is bad and brings harm (1); |
> | 世克应云群长怒， | 世克应，是说众位尊长发怒； | the Self controlling the Other means the elders are angry; |
> | 更相鬼爻为不祥（2）。 | 再加上官鬼爻，就不祥（2）。 | and with the Pressure line besides, it is inauspicious (2). |
