# 升何方（远近）：归魂屡复旧任、游魂尝履新途；六冲官伏藏则远、六合官出现则近；内近地外远方、初爻接壤六爻边鄙；一官以内外分远近、两官以卦象分遐迩

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 归魂卦，常常回到旧任；游魂卦，常常走上新路。六冲而官伏藏，就远；六合而官出现，就近。内卦是近处，外卦是远方；初爻在接壤的近处，六爻在边远之地。卦中只有一个官爻，在内卦为近、在外卦为远，初爻近、六爻远，其余爻照这样类推；若有内外两个官爻，就看卦：游魂、六冲、官伏藏，应在远；归魂、六合、官出现，应在近。因为一个官爻以内外分远近，两个官爻以卦象分远近。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Returning Soul hexagram often goes back to the old post; a Wandering Soul hexagram often walks a new road. A six-clash with Pressure hidden is far; a six-combine with Pressure showing is near. The inner trigram is near, the outer far; the first line borders on the near, the sixth is the remote frontier. When there is only one Pressure line in the hexagram, in the inner trigram it is near and in the outer far, the first line near and the sixth far, the other lines by analogy; if there are two Pressure lines, inner and outer, one looks at the hexagram: Wandering Soul, a six-clash and Pressure hidden answer far; Returning Soul, a six-combine and Pressure showing answer near. For one Pressure line distance is divided by inner and outer; for two Pressure lines it is divided by the image of the hexagram.

> [!note] 按
> 「归魂」「游魂」是八宫卦序里的两个卦（第八个归魂、第七个游魂）：游魂主迁动、归魂主回返，所以归魂常复旧任、游魂常换新地方。「履新途」是走上新的路。「六冲伏藏则远，六合出现则近」：冲散、隐藏主远，合聚、显现主近。「接壤」是和本地相连，「边鄙」是边远。「遐迩」是远近。书里自注：只有一个官爻按内外、爻位定远近；两个官爻内外分不出，就改看卦象（游魂、六冲、伏藏则远，归魂、六合、出现则近）。内外卦和初六爻位的远近，与易隐官禄占任所（YY.11）「世在一二位近、三四位远、五六位更远」是同一个意思。
>
> *Note:* 'Returning Soul' and 'Wandering Soul' are two hexagrams in the order of the eight palaces (the eighth is Returning Soul, the seventh Wandering Soul): Wandering Soul governs shifting and Returning Soul going back, so Returning Soul often resumes the old post and Wandering Soul often changes to a new place. 'Walking a new road' is setting out on a new road. 'A six-clash with Pressure hidden is far, a six-combine with Pressure showing is near': scattering and hiding mean far, gathering and showing mean near. 'Jierang' is bordering on one's home place and 'bianbi' is the remote frontier. 'Xia er' is far and near. The book's own gloss: with only one Pressure line distance is fixed by inner and outer and by the line position; with two Pressure lines, where inner and outer cannot tell, one looks at the image of the hexagram instead (Wandering Soul, six-clash and hidden: far; Returning Soul, six-combine and showing: near). The distance by inner and outer trigram and by the first to sixth line is the same idea as 'the Self in the first or second place is near, in the third or fourth far, in the fifth or sixth farther still' in the Yi Yin on the place of the post (YY.11).
