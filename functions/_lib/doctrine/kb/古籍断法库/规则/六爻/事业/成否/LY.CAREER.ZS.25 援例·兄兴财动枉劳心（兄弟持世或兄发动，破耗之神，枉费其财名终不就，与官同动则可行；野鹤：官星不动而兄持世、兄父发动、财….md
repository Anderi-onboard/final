# 援例·兄兴财动枉劳心（兄弟持世或兄发动，破耗之神，枉费其财名终不就，与官同动则可行；野鹤：官星不动而兄持世、兄父发动、财持世化兄，百无一成）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 援例捐官，兄弟爻持世，或者兄爻发动，是破耗的神，白花钱财，功名终究不成；如果和官星一起发动，又可以去办，因为官鬼能制住兄弟。野鹤说：卦中官星不动，只要遇到兄弟持世、兄爻和父母爻发动、以及财爻持世变出兄弟，百件里没有一件成，即使成了也必有别的缘故，到头来没有地方吃俸禄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In buying office by precedent, if the Peer line holds the Self or a Peer line moves, it is a spirit of waste and loss: the money is spent for nothing and the rank is never gained; if it moves together with the star of office it may be done, since Pressure restrains Peer. The Old Man of Yehe says: if the star of office does not move in the hexagram, and Peer holds the Self, or Peer and Resource lines move, or the Wealth line holds the Self and turns into Peer, not one in a hundred succeeds, and even if it succeeds there will surely be some other cause, and in the end one has no place to eat a salary.
