# 天乙贵人再来相合：不止自己享禄，子孙代代为官受封赠（旧注的天乙贵人按月份取巳申亥寅轮转）

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 天乙贵人再来相合，不只自己享俸禄，而且世世代代都能做官、受封赠。旧注里的天乙贵人按月份取：正月在巳、二月在申、三月在亥、四月在寅，五、九月仍在巳，往后三位轮转；逢动一定吉，子孙代代做公卿。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Tianyi Noble further combines, one enjoys the emolument oneself and, generation after generation, holds office and receives imperial honours. The note finds the Tianyi Noble by the month: Si in the first month, Shen in the second, Hai in the third, Yin in the fourth, Si again in the fifth and ninth, the three places turning on after that; if it moves it is surely auspicious, and descendants will be dukes and ministers generation after generation.

> [!warning] 打架
> 旧注里的天乙贵人按月份取（巳申亥寅轮转），而卜筮大全另一处、卜筮正宗、增删卜易、易林补遗等讲的天乙贵人都是按日干取：甲戊（庚）牛羊、乙己鼠猴、丙丁猪鸡、壬癸兔蛇、庚辛（六辛）马虎。两种起法不同，本库各照原书录，不裁定。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The note here finds the Tianyi Noble by the month (turning through Si, Shen, Hai, Yin), while elsewhere in the Bushi Daquan itself, and in the Bushi Zhengzong, the Zengshan Buyi and the Yilin Buyi, the Tianyi Noble is found by the day stem: Jia and Wu (and Geng) ox and sheep, Yi and Ji rat and monkey, Bing and Ding pig and rooster, Ren and Gui hare and snake, Geng and Xin (or the six Xin) horse and tiger. The two methods differ; each is recorded as its book gives it and neither is adjudicated.

> [!note] 按
> 「天纲」在这里没有交代是谁；断易天机的今人注读作袁天纲。本库照字面译成 Tiangang，不替它定。
>
> *Note:* 'Tiangang' is not identified here; a modern note in the Duanyi Tianji reads it as Yuan Tiangang. It is rendered literally as Tiangang and nothing is decided.
