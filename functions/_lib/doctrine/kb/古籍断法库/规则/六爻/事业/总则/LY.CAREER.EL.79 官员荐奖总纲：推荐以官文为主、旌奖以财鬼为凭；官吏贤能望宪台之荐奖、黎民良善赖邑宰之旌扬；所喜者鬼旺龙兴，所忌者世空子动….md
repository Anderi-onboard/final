# 官员荐奖总纲：推荐以官文为主、旌奖以财鬼为凭；官吏贤能望宪台之荐奖、黎民良善赖邑宰之旌扬；所喜者鬼旺龙兴，所忌者世空子动；凡占奖荐各要官爻旺相青龙得地，大忌世值空亡子孙发动

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占推荐，以官爻、文书爻为主；占旌奖（表彰奖励），以财爻、官鬼为凭据。官吏贤能，盼望上司宪台的荐举嘉奖；百姓善良，依靠县令的旌表褒扬。所喜欢的是官鬼旺相、青龙兴旺；所忌讳的是世爻落空、子孙发动。凡是占嘉奖、荐举，都要官爻旺相、青龙得位，最忌世爻落在空亡、子孙发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a recommendation, take the Pressure line and the document line as the chief; for a commendation or award, take Wealth and Pressure as the ground. Officials who are worthy and able look to the supervising authorities for recommendation and award; common people who are good depend on the county magistrate for commendation and praise. What is welcomed is Pressure thriving and the Dragon flourishing; what is feared is the Self Void and Output moving. In every divination about award or recommendation, the Pressure line should be thriving and the Azure Dragon in its place, and what is most feared is the Self falling on Void and Output moving.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 小标里的「官文」是官爻和文书爻（父母），「财鬼」是财爻和官鬼。「宪台」是上级的监察、巡抚、按察一类官署；「邑宰」是县令；「旌扬」是旌表、褒扬。「奖荐」是荐举嘉奖。诀里的「龙」是青龙。这一章看荐举和奖励两件事：荐举看官和文书，奖励看财和官。
>
> *Note:* 'Guanwen' in the subtitle is the Pressure line and the document line (Resource), and 'caigui' is Wealth and Pressure. 'Xiantai' is the supervisory offices of higher level such as the censors, governors and provincial judges; 'yizai' is the county magistrate; 'jingyang' is official commendation and praise. 'Jiangjian' is recommendation and award. The 'Dragon' in the verse is the Azure Dragon. The chapter looks at two things, recommendation and award: recommendation looks at the star of office and the document, award at Wealth and the star of office.
