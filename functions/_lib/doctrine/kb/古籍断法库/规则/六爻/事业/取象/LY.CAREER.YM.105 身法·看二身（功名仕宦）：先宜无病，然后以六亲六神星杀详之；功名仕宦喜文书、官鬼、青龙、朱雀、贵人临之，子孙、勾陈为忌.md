# 身法·看二身（功名仕宦）：先宜无病，然后以六亲六神星杀详之；功名仕宦喜文书、官鬼、青龙、朱雀、贵人临之，子孙、勾陈为忌

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡看（卦身、世身）二身，先要没有病，然后用六亲、六神、星煞来详看；功名仕宦，喜欢文书、官鬼、青龙、朱雀、贵人临在身上，而子孙、勾陈是忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In looking at the two bodies, they should first be free of sickness, and only then are the six relations, the six spirits and the stars examined in detail; for fame and office, it is welcome for Resource, Pressure, the Azure Dragon, the Vermilion Bird and the Noble to rest on the body, and Output and Hook Earth are taboo.

> [!note] 按
> 「二身」是卦身（由卦立，又叫月卦）和世身（由世立，看身居哪一爻），见本书身法章（L198 起）。「文书」是父母爻，「贵人」是神煞。这一句在身法章里是分类总说各类占问对身的喜忌：功名仕宦、财利经营、狱讼、忧患四类。事业取的是第一类：功名仕宦喜文书、官鬼、青龙、朱雀、贵人临在身上，忌子孙、勾陈。子孙克官鬼、勾陈主迟滞，所以是忌（按象义推，书里没有说理由）。「先宜无病」是身本身要没有病（空、破、动冲等，见身法章前文）。其余三类（财利、狱讼、忧患）分属求财、官非、风险各域，这里不收。
>
> *Note:* The 'two bodies' are the hexagram body (set by the hexagram, also called the month hexagram) and the Self body (set by the Self, by which line the body sits on), see the chapter on the two bodies in this book (from L198). 'Wenshu' is the Resource line, and the 'Noble' is a spirit-star. In the chapter on the two bodies this sentence lists by class what each kind of question welcomes and fears in the body: fame and office, profit and business, lawsuits, and worries. Career takes the first class: for fame and office the Resource line, Pressure, the Azure Dragon, the Vermilion Bird and the Noble resting on the body are welcome, and Output and Hook Earth are feared. Output controls Pressure and Hook Earth governs delay, so they are taboo (inferred from the images, the book gives no reason). 'It should first be free of sickness' is that the body itself should be free of sickness (Void, broken, moving and clashed, and so on, see the earlier part of that chapter). The other three classes (profit, lawsuits, worries) belong to Wealth, Lawsuits and Risk and are not taken here.
