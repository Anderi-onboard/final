# 出师征伐：鬼藏世下克世爻切虑自兵谋主，子伏身中合应象谨离我将降夷；世下伏鬼克世则家兵杀主，世旺鬼衰奸心已蓄无隙可乘、世衰鬼旺则逆节潜谋乘虚而入（张飞受范疆之刺）；世下伏子合应则军心已离各怀去意，世衰应旺他国强我军弱（匈奴迫李陵之降）；日辰生子又加雀虎必被阴谮阳唆背义从仇

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼藏在世爻下面克世爻，切记忧虑自己的兵谋害主将；子孙伏在身爻中合应象，当心离开我的将领、投降夷人。世爻下面伏着官鬼、而又克世爻的，就是家兵杀主：如果世旺鬼衰，奸心已经存着、无隙可乘；世衰鬼旺，则叛逆潜谋，乘虚而入，所以张飞受到范疆的刺杀。如果世爻下面伏着子孙而合应爻，就是军心已经离散，各怀去意；如果世衰应旺，就是他国强横、我军卑弱，所以匈奴逼迫李陵投降。如果日辰生子孙，又加朱雀、白虎临在上面，必定被人暗中进谗、公开唆使，背弃道义、投靠仇人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure lies hidden under the Self and controls the Self, take care to worry about one's own troops plotting against the commander; if Output lies hidden in the Body and combines with the Other image, beware of the generals leaving me and surrendering to the foreigners. If a Pressure line lies hidden under the Self and also controls the Self, it is household troops killing their master: if the Self is thriving and Pressure weak, treacherous thoughts have been stored but no gap is open; if the Self is weak and Pressure thriving, the rebellion plots in secret and enters where there is a gap, as Zhang Fei was stabbed by Fan Jiang. If Output lies hidden under the Self and combines with the Other, the hearts of the army have already left and each harbours thoughts of leaving; if the Self is weak and the Other thriving, the other state is strong and my army low, as the Xiongnu forced Li Ling to surrender. If the Day generates Output and the Vermilion Bird and White Tiger sit on it, one will surely be slandered in secret and incited in the open, betraying righteousness and following the enemy.

> [!note] 按
> 「范疆」就是刺杀张飞的范疆、张达（《三国志》作范强、张达），「李陵」是汉武帝时兵败降匈奴的将领；这两个典故书里自己写出，和黄金策征战「子化官爻克世，张飞遭范张之诛」「外宫子动化绝爻，李陵所以降虏」（HJC.110、HJC.105）用的是同一批。「雀虎」是朱雀、白虎的省称；「背羲」是「背义」。「夷」是外族。
>
> *Note:* 'Fan Jiang' is Fan Jiang, who with Zhang Da assassinated Zhang Fei (the Records of the Three Kingdoms writes Fan Qiang and Zhang Da), and 'Li Ling' is the general of Emperor Wu of the Han who was defeated and surrendered to the Xiongnu; the book writes out both allusions itself, and they are the same pair that the Huangjin Ce uses in 'Output turns into the Pressure line and controls the Self, Zhang Fei suffers the execution by Fan and Zhang' and 'Output moves in the outer palace and turns into an extinct line, which is why Li Ling surrendered to the captors' (HJC.110, HJC.105). 'Que hu' is an abbreviation for the Vermilion Bird and the White Tiger; 'bei xi' is 'bei yi', betraying righteousness. 'Yi' is the foreign peoples.
