# 出师征伐：细究何爻克应、再查那象伤官，用此奇方妙计自然剿恶锄强；鬼空惟取应、应陷可从官；行兵之法须求克应伤官，应受伤彼遭屠戮，鬼披克贼必生擒；火爻临应或鬼须求水制火用黑旗为号并临水战；鬼空亡评应象，应落陷看官爻

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 细究是哪一爻克应爻，再查哪一个象伤官鬼；用这种奇方妙计，自然剿灭凶恶、锄除强梁。官鬼落空，只取应爻；应爻陷落，可以从官鬼（入手）。要懂得行兵的办法，必须找出克应爻、伤官鬼的爻：应爻如果受伤，对方就遭屠戮；官鬼如果被克，贼必定生擒。比如火爻临在应爻或官鬼上，须找水来制火，用黑旗为号，并且临水作战，自然得胜，其余的照这样推。官鬼如果空亡，就评断应象；应爻如果落陷，必须看官爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Examine closely which line controls the Other, and then see which image injures Pressure; with this marvellous method and clever plan, one naturally destroys the evil and weeds out the strong. If Pressure is Void, take only the Other; if the Other is sunk, one can start from Pressure. To know the method of moving troops, one must find the line that controls the Other and injures Pressure: if the Other is injured, the enemy is slaughtered; if Pressure is controlled, the bandits are surely taken alive. For example, if a Fire line sits on the Other or on Pressure, one must seek Water to control the Fire, use black flags as the signal and fight beside water, and one naturally wins; the rest follow this. If Pressure is Void, judge the Other image; if the Other is sunk, one must look at the Pressure line.

> | 鬼若空亡，却评应象。 | 官鬼如果空亡，就评断应象。 | If Pressure is Void, judge the Other image. |
> | 应如落陷，必看官爻。 | 应爻如果落陷，必须看官爻。 | If the Other is sunk, one must look at the Pressure line. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「披克」是被克；「奇方」是出奇的办法。「用黑旗为号」是水属黑色的取象。这一条与易隐征战占里「应空取鬼为用」（YY.54）一类：对方的将（应）和兵（官鬼）互为补位。
>
> *Note:* 'Pi ke' is being controlled; 'qi fang' is an unusual method. 'Using black flags as the signal' takes the image that Water is black. This entry is of the kind of 'if the Other is Void take Pressure as the Subject' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.54): the enemy's general (the Other) and troops (Pressure) stand in for each other.
