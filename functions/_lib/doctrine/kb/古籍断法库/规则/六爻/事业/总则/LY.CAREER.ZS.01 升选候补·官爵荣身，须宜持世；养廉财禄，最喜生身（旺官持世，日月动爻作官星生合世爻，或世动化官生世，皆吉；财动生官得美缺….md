# 升选候补·官爵荣身，须宜持世；养廉财禄，最喜生身（旺官持世，日月动爻作官星生合世爻，或世动化官生世，皆吉；财动生官得美缺，官临日月必超迁）

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占升选补缺，官星要旺相、持世，妻财要来生身：旺相的官星持世，或者日月、动爻作官星来生合世爻，或者世爻发动变出官星来生世，都是吉兆；妻财发动生官星，能得到好缺；官星临在日月上，必定越级升迁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a question about promotion or appointment the star of office should be thriving and hold the Self, and Wealth should generate the Body: a thriving star of office holding the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acting as the star of office to generate or combine with the Self, or the Self moving and turning into the star of office to generate the Self, are all auspicious; Wealth moving to generate the star of office wins a fine post; the star of office on the Day or Month means promotion beyond one's rank.
