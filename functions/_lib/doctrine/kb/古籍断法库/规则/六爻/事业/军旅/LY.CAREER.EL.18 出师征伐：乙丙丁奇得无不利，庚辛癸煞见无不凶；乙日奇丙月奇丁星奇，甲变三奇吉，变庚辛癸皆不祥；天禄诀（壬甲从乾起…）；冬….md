# 出师征伐：乙丙丁奇得无不利，庚辛癸煞见无不凶；乙日奇丙月奇丁星奇，甲变三奇吉，变庚辛癸皆不祥；天禄诀（壬甲从乾起…）；冬至后阳令、夏至后阴升，乾内甲外壬/内壬外甲，坤内乙外癸/内癸外乙；举例

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 乙、丙、丁三奇，遇到没有不利的；庚、辛、癸三煞，遇到没有不凶的。乙是日奇，丙是月奇，丁是星奇，甲变成三奇自然吉利；如果变成庚、辛、癸之象，都不吉祥。卦配十干，要看规则：天禄诀说「壬甲从乾起，乙癸向坤求，丙向艮方取，丁出兑家游，戊自坎中觅，己用离为头，庚震及辛巽」。诀里震、巽、坎、离、艮、兑各属一个干，只有乾、坤两卦每个属于两个干，又要接合阴阳之道：冬至后阳令之时，乾是内卦甲、外卦壬，坤是内卦乙、外卦癸；夏至后阴升的节令，乾是内壬外甲，坤是内癸外乙。又说用兵：冬至后，卜得乾变遁卦，就是甲变成月奇；乾变履卦，就是甲变成星奇；乾变否卦，就是甲变成日奇；这样的卦爻得到大胜。又说夏至后，卜得否变归妹，甲被庚所伤；乾变巽卦，甲遭辛所克；凡是得到这类卦爻，人宜出战（原文如此）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The three Wonders Yi, Bing and Ding are never unfavourable when met; the three baleful stems Geng, Xin and Gui are never other than ominous when seen. Yi is the Sun Wonder, Bing the Moon Wonder, Ding the Star Wonder, and when Jia changes into the three Wonders it is naturally auspicious; if it changes into the images of Geng, Xin and Gui, none is auspicious. How hexagrams match the ten stems must be seen from the rule: the Tianlu rhyme says 'Ren and Jia start from Qian, Yi and Gui seek Kun, Bing is taken from Gen, Ding goes out to Dui, Wu is found in Kan, Ji takes Li as head, Geng is Zhen and Xin is Xun'. In the rhyme Zhen, Xun, Kan, Li, Gen and Dui each belong to one stem, and only Qian and Kun each belong to two, and the way of yin and yang must also be joined: after the Winter Solstice, in the season when yang commands, Qian has Jia inside and Ren outside and Kun Yi inside and Gui outside; after the Summer Solstice, in the season when yin rises, Qian has Ren inside and Jia outside and Kun Gui inside and Yi outside. Again for troops: after the Winter Solstice, if one casts Qian changing into Dun, Jia has changed into the Moon Wonder; Qian changing into Lu, Jia has changed into the Star Wonder; Qian changing into Pi, Jia has changed into the Sun Wonder; such lines bring great victory. Again after the Summer Solstice, if one casts Pi changing into Guimei, Jia is injured by Geng; Qian changing into Xun, Jia is controlled by Xin; whoever gets such lines, men are fit to go out and fight (as the text reads).

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的天干是纳甲：乾纳甲壬、坤纳乙癸、艮纳丙、兑纳丁、坎纳戊、离纳己、震纳庚、巽纳辛。三奇乙丙丁分别是日奇、月奇、星奇。书里几处字面有问题：「数丙天禄诀」的「数丙」不明；「各属一千」的「千」是「干」；「二下」是「二干」。最后一句「甲遭辛克，凡得此等卦爻，人宜出战者也」按上下文，甲被庚、辛所伤是不吉的，而前面说变庚辛癸皆不祥，所以这里「宜出战」很可能脱了「不」字（应是「不宜出战」），译文照字面写出并标明「原文如此」，请读者不要照字面当作可以出战的依据。「遁」「履」「否」「归妹」「巽」是卦名，书里写作「乾之遁卦」「乾之履卦」等。
>
> *Note:* The stems in this entry are those of the Najia system: Qian takes Jia and Ren, Kun Yi and Gui, Gen Bing, Dui Ding, Kan Wu, Li Ji, Zhen Geng and Xun Xin; the three Wonders Yi, Bing and Ding are the Sun, Moon and Star Wonders. Several wordings in the book are defective: the 'shu Bing' of 'shu Bing Tianlu jue' is unclear; the 'qian', thousand, of 'ge shu yi qian' is 'gan', stem; and 'er xia' is 'er gan', two stems. In the last sentence, 'Jia is controlled by Xin; whoever gets such lines, men are fit to go out and fight', by the context Jia being injured by Geng and Xin is not auspicious, and the passage before says that a change into Geng, Xin or Gui is never auspicious, so 'fit to go out and fight' very probably lacks the character 'not' (it should be 'not fit to go out and fight'); the translation writes it by the letter and marks it 'as the text reads', and the reader should not take it literally as a ground for fighting. 'Dun', 'Lu', 'Pi', 'Guimei' and 'Xun' are hexagram names, which the book writes as 'Qian changing into Dun' and so on.
