# 福神克世，重观折槛之朱云：子孙发动伤克世爻主有直臣奏事如朱云折槛，与日辰相生合上必允其奏、刑冲则龙颜大怒终遭贬责；子孙临太岁月建动克四爻而生五爻、子孙旺日必有御史劾权臣；四爻鬼杀旺相不受克须防有变

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 福神（子孙）克世，又见到折槛直谏的朱云。子孙发动、伤克世爻，主有正直的大臣上奏，很像朱云折槛的风骨；若与日辰相生相合，上面一定准他的奏章；与日辰刑冲，皇帝大怒，终究遭到贬责。子孙落在太岁、月建上，发动克四爻、生五爻，在子孙旺的日子，必有御史弹劾权臣；四爻的鬼、杀旺相而不受克，要防备有变。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output controlling the Self, one sees again Zhu Yun who broke the railing. If Output moves and injures and controls the Self line, there is an upright minister presenting a memorial, much like Zhu Yun breaking the railing; if it is generated or combined by the Day, the sovereign will surely grant the memorial, and if it is punished or clashed by the Day the dragon countenance is greatly angered and he is in the end demoted and reprimanded. If Output stands on Tai Sui or the Month and moves to control the fourth line and generate the fifth, on a day when Output is strong there will surely be a censor impeaching a powerful minister; if the fourth line's ghost and baleful spirit are strong and not controlled, guard against a change.

> [!note] 按
> 朱云是汉成帝时的直臣，当面弹劾权贵，被拖下殿时攀住殿槛，槛折；「折槛」就是这个典故，注文没有解释。
>
> *Note:* Zhu Yun was an upright minister under Emperor Cheng of Han who impeached a powerful man to his face and, as he was dragged from the hall, clung to the railing until it broke; 'breaking the railing' is that allusion, and the commentary does not explain it.
