# 僧道占：子动妻交还俗汉；忽然负结、财身动免不了填还俗债；世应不和身妄动，了身达命待驴年

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动、妻财交动，是要还俗的人；忽然有负债纠结、财和身一起发动，免不了去偿还俗世的债和冤；世、应不和、身又乱动，想了身达命要等到驴年（也就是永远等不到）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving with Wealth also moving: a man who will return to lay life; if suddenly there is a debt-knot and Wealth and the Body move, one cannot avoid paying back worldly debts and grievances; with Self and Other at odds and the Body moving wildly, to finish with the body and reach one's destiny one must wait for the Year of the Donkey (that is, for ever).

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「负结」原文难解，这里按上下文读作「负债纠结」；「驴年」的十二支里没有驴，意思是永远等不到。
>
> *Note:* 'Fu jie' is hard to read in the text and is taken from the context as 'debts and entanglement'; there is no donkey among the twelve branches, so the 'Year of the Donkey' means never.
