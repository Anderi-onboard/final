# 卜筮元龟·占欲见人可出不可出：阴兴之时得阴卦宜见小人、阳生之后得阳卦宜见士贤；阴卦遇阳时灭没出则被小人害、阳卦遇阴不顺出亦难同君子爱

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占想去见人该不该出门：阴气兴起的时候得阴卦，适宜见小人；阳气生起之后得阳卦，适宜见士人、贤者；阴卦遇上阳的时节就灭没，出门会被小人所害；阳卦遇上阴的时节不顺，出门也难得到君子的喜爱。注文说：夏至后阴气生，得阴卦是小人得时；冬至后阳气生，得阳卦是君子得时；得时则吉，失时则凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On divining whether one who wishes to see someone may go out or not: when yin rises and one gets a yin hexagram it suits seeing petty people; after yang is born and one gets a yang hexagram it suits seeing scholars and the worthy; a yin hexagram meeting a yang season is extinguished, and going out one will be harmed by petty people; a yang hexagram meeting a yin season is not in accord, and going out one will hardly win the regard of the gentleman. The notes say: after the Summer Solstice yin qi is born, and getting a yin hexagram is the petty person's time; after the Winter Solstice yang qi is born, and getting a yang hexagram is the gentleman's time; in its time it is auspicious, out of its time it is bad.
