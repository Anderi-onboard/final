# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（一）：求官鬼吏克身为贤官；驿马扶身官职迁；坎离宜冬三月、震巽应申酉年；世应与驿马相合吉；坎咸复卦主狐疑；申酉辰戌持世吉

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求官时，官鬼落在吏位（初爻）来克身，是贤官之象；驿马相扶，官职迁动；坎、离二卦宜在冬天三个月，震、巽二卦应在申、酉年；世、应和驿马相合是吉；坎、咸、复三卦主狐疑；申、酉、辰、戌持世是吉，做官又遇到禄，好施展作为。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking office, Pressure on the clerk line (the first) and controlling the self is the sign of a worthy official; the Post Horse lending support, the post is moved up; the two trigrams Kan and Li suit the three winter months and Zhen and Xun the years of Shen and You; Self and Other combining with the Post Horse is auspicious; the hexagrams Kan, Xian and Fu bring doubt and hesitation; Shen, You, Chen and Xu holding the Self is auspicious, and taking office and meeting the Emolument gives room to act well.

> [!note] 按
> 这一首和卜筮大全阐奥歌章仕宦章是同一首歌诀，这一段的字句基本相同；断易天机把它归在「丘寺丞易鉴歌」名下，卜筮大全归在阐奥歌章名下，作者的归属两书不同。断易天机里的数字注号（1）到（10）是整理时编的。
>
> *Note:* This is the same song as the chapter on official careers in the Chan'ao Ge of the Bushi Daquan, and in this stretch the wording is nearly the same; the Duanyi Tianji assigns it to the 'Qiu Sicheng Song of the Yi Mirror' and the Bushi Daquan to the Chan'ao Ge, so the two books differ on its authorship. The numbered notes (1) to (10) in the Duanyi Tianji were numbered in editing.
