# 出师征伐：世陷我军有难、应空贼寇罹危；世为主将宜雄强对垒不宜空陷；值旬空月破不可妄自争先，先则有害、迟则有益；世居阳象兵贵神速速行大利

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻陷落，我军有难；应爻落空，贼寇陷于危险。世是主将，要靠雄强来对垒折冲，哪里适宜落空陷落？如果值旬空，或者临月破的宫位，不可妄自争先，争先就有害，迟缓就有益。单爻属阳，发动成为老阳，主过去的缘由；又说阳主迅速，所以世居阳象，用兵贵在神速，迅速行动大利，迟缓就大害。这是大道理，所以不再琐碎细说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self sinks, my army is in trouble; if the Other is Void, the bandits fall into danger. The Self is the commander, and must face the enemy by strength and stoutness; how can it be fit for it to be Void or sunk? If it meets decade Void or sits in a Month-break palace, one must not recklessly press ahead, for pressing ahead brings harm and delay brings benefit. A single line that is yang becomes old yang when it moves and governs the reasons of the past; again it is said that yang governs speed, so when the Self sits on a yang image, in using troops swiftness is prized, and acting swiftly is greatly favourable and delay greatly harmful. This is a great principle, so it is not detailed trivially.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 354 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡世为主将，惟伏雄强对垒折衡，岂宜空陷于？ | 凡是世爻为主将，要靠雄强来对垒折冲，哪里适宜落空陷落？ | Whenever the Self is the commander, he must face the enemy by strength and stoutness; how can it be fit for it to be Void or sunk? |
> | 若值旬空，或临月破宫，不可妄自争先，先则有害，犀则有益。 | 如果值旬空，或者临月破的宫位，不可妄自争先，争先就有害，迟缓就有益（「犀」疑是「迟」）。 | If it meets decade Void or sits in a Month-break palace, one must not recklessly press ahead, for pressing ahead brings harm and delay brings benefit ('xi' is presumably 'chi'). |
> | 单爻属阳，发为老阳，而主过去之由，又云阳主迅速，故世居阳象，兵贵神速，速行大利，犀则大害。 | 单爻属阳，发动成为老阳，主过去的缘由；又说阳主迅速，所以世居阳象，用兵贵在神速，迅速行动大利，迟缓就大害（「犀」疑是「迟」）。 | A single line that is yang becomes old yang when it moves and governs the reasons of the past; again it is said that yang governs speed, so when the Self sits on a yang image, in using troops swiftness is prized, and acting swiftly is greatly favourable and delay greatly harmful ('xi' is presumably 'chi'). |
> | 此系大理，故不琐耳。 | 这是大道理，所以不再琐碎细说。 | This is a great principle, so it is not detailed trivially. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「犀」在第二、三句里两次出现，和「先」对着，应是「迟」（迟则有益、迟则大害）；译文按「迟缓」译并标出。「老阳」是阳爻发动的叫法（老阳变阴）。「折衡」即「折冲」，抵挡敌人。「惟伏」疑是「惟须」，译文按文意译。这一条与易隐征战占「世空，我有难；应空，彼必伤」（YY.54）取同一件事，但这里多了「不可争先」。
>
> *Note:* 'Xi' appears twice, in the second and third sentences, set against 'xian', ahead, and is presumably 'chi', slow (delay brings benefit, delay brings great harm); the translation reads 'delay' and marks it. 'Old yang' is the name for a yang line that moves (old yang changes into yin). 'Zhe heng' is 'zhe chong', turning back the enemy. 'Wei fu' is presumably 'wei xu', one must, and is translated by sense. This entry takes up the same matter as 'if the Self is Void I am in trouble; if the Other is Void they will surely be hurt' in the Yi Yin 'them and us' section (YY.54), but adds 'one must not press ahead'.
