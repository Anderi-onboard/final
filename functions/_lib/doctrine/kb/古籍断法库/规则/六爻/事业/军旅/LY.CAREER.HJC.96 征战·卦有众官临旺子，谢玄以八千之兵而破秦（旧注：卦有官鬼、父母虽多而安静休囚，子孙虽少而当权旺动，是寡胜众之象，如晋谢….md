# 征战·卦有众官临旺子，谢玄以八千之兵而破秦（旧注：卦有官鬼、父母虽多而安静休囚，子孙虽少而当权旺动，是寡胜众之象，如晋谢玄刘牢之以八千兵渡江破秦王苻坚九十万众）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里官鬼、父母虽然多，却安静休囚；子孙虽然少，却当令旺相而发动，这是以少胜多的象，就像晋朝的谢玄、刘牢之，以八千兵马渡江，击破秦王苻坚的九十万大军。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram has many Pressure and Resource lines that are still and resting or trapped, while Output is few but holds the season and is thriving and moving, it is the image of the few overcoming the many, like Xie Xuan and Liu Laozhi of Jin, who crossed the river with eight thousand men and broke the nine hundred thousand of Fu Jian, King of Qin.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5934 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦有众官临旺子，谢玄以八千之兵而破秦。 | 卦里有很多官鬼，而子孙旺相，就像谢玄凭八千兵马击破了秦国。 | If the hexagram has many Pressure lines while Output is thriving, it is like Xie Xuan who with eight thousand soldiers broke Qin. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5935 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦有官鬼，父母虽多，而安静休囚； | 卦里有官鬼，父母虽然多，却安静休囚； | If the hexagram has Pressure, and Resource lines are many but still and resting or trapped; |
> | 子孙虽少，而当权旺动。 | 子孙虽然少，却当令旺相而发动。 | and Output is few but holds the season and is thriving and moving. |
> | 此乃寡胜众之象。 | 这就是以少胜多的象。 | This is the image of the few overcoming the many. |
> | 如晋谢玄、刘牢之，以八千兵渡江，破秦王符坚九十万众也。 | 就像晋朝的谢玄、刘牢之，以八千兵马渡江，击破了秦王符坚的九十万大军。 | It is like Xie Xuan and Liu Laozhi of Jin, who crossed the river with eight thousand soldiers and broke the nine hundred thousand of Fu Jian, King of Qin. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1532 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官父虽多而安静，子孙虽少而旺动，必寡可胜众也，如晋谢玄、刘牢以八千兵破秦王苻坚九十万众也。 | 官鬼、父母虽然多却安静，子孙虽然少却旺相发动，必定是以少胜多，就像晋朝的谢玄、刘牢以八千兵马击破秦王苻坚的九十万大军。 | Though Pressure and Resource lines are many, they are still, and Output is few but thriving and moving; the few will surely overcome the many, like Xie Xuan and Liu Lao of Jin who with eight thousand soldiers broke the nine hundred thousand of Fu Jian, King of Qin. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 人名写法不同：旧注作「刘牢之」「符坚」，卜筮正宗作「刘牢」「苻坚」；译文按通行写法。
>
> *Note:* The names are written differently: the old commentary has 'Liu Laozhi' and a variant character for 'Fu', the Bu Shi Zheng Zong has 'Liu Lao' and the usual 'Fu'; the translation follows the common forms.
