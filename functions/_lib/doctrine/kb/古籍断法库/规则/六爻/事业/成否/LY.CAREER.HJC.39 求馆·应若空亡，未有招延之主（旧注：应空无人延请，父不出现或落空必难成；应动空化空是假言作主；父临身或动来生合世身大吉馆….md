# 求馆·应若空亡，未有招延之主（旧注：应空无人延请，父不出现或落空必难成；应动空化空是假言作主；父临身或动来生合世身大吉馆易成；应空鬼动宜自开馆、鬼空伏应生合宜自招致）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻空亡，没有人来聘请；再如果父母不出现或者落空，必定难成；应爻动空、化空，是说空话、假装作主，不然也不能把事做完。父母临身，或者动爻来生合世身，是大吉的兆头，这个馆容易成；有这种象而应空、官鬼动，虽然有招集学生的人，却没有聘请的主人，适合自己开馆；官鬼空伏而应来生合，虽然有聘请的人家，却没有招集学生的人，适合自己去招来学生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Other is Void, no one comes to engage one; if Resource also does not appear or falls Void, the matter will surely be hard to bring about; if the Other moves into Void or turns into Void, he only says it in words and pretends to be the master, and otherwise the matter is not seen through to the end. If Resource rests on the Body, or a moving line comes to generate or combine with the Self or the Body, it is a greatly auspicious sign and the school comes easily; with this image but the Other Void and Pressure moving, though there is someone to gather pupils there is no master to engage one, and it is fitting to open a school oneself; if Pressure is Void and hidden while the Other comes to generate or combine, though there is a household that would engage one there is no one to gather pupils, and it is fitting to draw in pupils oneself.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联把「应」（聘请的人）、「官鬼」（招集学生的人）、「父母」（书馆）三样放在一起：缺哪样，就要自己补哪样（没有人聘请就自己开馆，没有人招集学生就自己招）。「招延」是招聘、聘请，「延请」同义。旧注后面两段（「父母临身……」「有此象而应空鬼动……」）王洪绪没有写。
>
> *Note:* This couplet puts three things together: the Other (the one who engages), Pressure (the one who gathers pupils) and Resource (the school), and what is missing one must make up oneself (open a school oneself if no one engages one, draw in pupils oneself if no one gathers them). 'Engage' and 'invite' are synonyms. The last two paragraphs of the old commentary ('Resource rests on the Body ...' and 'with this image but the Other Void and Pressure moving ...') are not in Wang Hongxu.
