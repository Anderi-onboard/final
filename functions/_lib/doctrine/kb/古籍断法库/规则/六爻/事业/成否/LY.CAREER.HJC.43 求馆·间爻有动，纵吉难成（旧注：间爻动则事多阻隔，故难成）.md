# 求馆·间爻有动，纵吉难成（旧注：间爻动则事多阻隔，故难成）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻（世应之间的爻）发动，事情多有阻隔，纵然卦象吉也难成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a between line (a line between the Self and the Other) moves, the matter is often obstructed, and even if the hexagram is auspicious it is hard to bring about.

## 《黄金策·求馆(附束修)》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8569 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 间爻有动，纵吉难成。 | 间爻发动，纵然吉也难成。 | If a between line moves, then even if it is auspicious it is hard to bring about. |

^q1

> | 间爻动，事多阻隔，故难成。 | 间爻发动，事情多半有阻隔，所以难成。 | When a between line moves, the matter is often obstructed, and so it is hard to bring about. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2764 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 间爻动克，事多阻隔，故难成也。 | 间爻发动来克，事情多有阻隔，所以难成。 | A between line moving to control the matter is often obstructed, and so it is hard to bring about. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「间爻」是世爻和应爻之间的爻，在求馆里就是先生和东家之间的人（中人、荐馆人）。间爻动，是中间的人插了手，所以多阻隔。这个判据在别的章里也常见，如占婚的「间爻」（见 LY.MARRY 一带）。
>
> *Note:* 'A between line' is a line between the Self and the Other, and in a school post it is the person between the teacher and the host (the go-between, the one who recommends). When it moves, the go-between has stepped in, and so there is obstruction. This criterion is common in other chapters too, as with the 'between lines' in divining marriage (see around LY.MARRY).
