# 道业·刻期取证、指日还丹：世空无成、六冲多阻、世病则九仞井弃、鬼发则一丈魔高，唯世旺福兴而丹成悟彻（止问求道，世空世病六冲鬼动为四戒）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 限定日期取证（证道）、指日还丹（炼丹成功）：世空就无成，六冲就多阻碍，世病就像掘井九仞而放弃，鬼发就是魔高一丈；只有世旺、福兴，才丹成悟彻。只问求道，世空、世病、六冲、鬼动，这是四戒。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For fixing a date for attaining realisation and counting the days to the return of the elixir: the Self Void, and nothing comes of it; a six-clash, and there are many obstructions; a sick Self, and it is digging a well nine fathoms deep and giving it up; Pressure issuing, and the devil stands ten feet high; only with the Self thriving and Output rising is the elixir completed and the realisation thorough. In asking only about seeking the Way, the Self Void, the Self sick, a six-clash and Pressure moving are the four warnings.

> [!note] 按
> 「刻期」是限定日期，「取证」是修行证道（得到证悟），「指日还丹」是指着日子炼成还丹（道家内丹的成功）。「九仞井弃」出自「掘井九仞而不及泉，犹为弃井也」，是功亏一篑；「一丈魔高」是「道高一尺、魔高一丈」，修行越深障碍越大。「丹成悟彻」是丹炼成、悟透彻。书里自注：只问求道的时候，世空、世病、六冲、鬼动是四个要戒的。与 YM.91 的三戒对着看，这里多了一个六冲。
>
> *Note:* 'Keqi' is fixing a date, 'quzheng' is attaining realisation in practice, and 'zhiri huandan' is counting the days to the completion of the returned elixir (success in Daoist inner alchemy). 'Digging a well nine fathoms deep and giving it up' comes from 'digging a well nine fathoms and not reaching water is still an abandoned well', failing for want of the last basket of earth; 'the devil stands ten feet high' is 'the Way is a foot high, the devil is ten feet high', the deeper the practice the greater the obstacle. 'Dancheng wuche' is the elixir completed and the realisation thorough. The book's own note: when asking only about seeking the Way, the Self Void, the Self sick, a six-clash and Pressure moving are the four to be warned against. Read against the three warnings of YM.91; here a six-clash is added.
