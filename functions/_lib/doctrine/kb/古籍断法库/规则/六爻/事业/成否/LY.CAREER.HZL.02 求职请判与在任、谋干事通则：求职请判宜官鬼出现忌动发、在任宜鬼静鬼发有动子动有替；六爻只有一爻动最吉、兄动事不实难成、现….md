# 求职请判与在任、谋干事通则：求职请判宜官鬼出现忌动发、在任宜鬼静鬼发有动子动有替；六爻只有一爻动最吉、兄动事不实难成、现有气可速成怕落空；官与文书俱旺相亦要持世方可成、应不克事体分明；乾兑坎宫谋事不一，见官用动其人多出见亦生嗔

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求职、请上司批判，宜官鬼出现，忌发动；已在任的，宜鬼静，鬼发动就有变动，子动就有人来替。如果六爻中只有一爻动，最吉。兄动，事情不实在，难成。如果（用爻）出现而有气，可以很快成，怕落空。易说：动爻急如火。其次，是文书和贵人出现。但如果卦中本来没有、或不入卦、或落空，那件事就难。官和文书都旺相，也要持世才可以成。应又不克世，事情就清楚。乾、兑、坎宫，谋事不专一；见官爻发动，那个人多半外出，见了也生气。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking a post or asking a superior for a ruling, it is right for Pressure to appear and wrong for it to move; for one in office, it is right for Pressure to be still, Pressure moving means a change, and Output moving means someone to take his place. If only one of the six lines moves, it is most auspicious. If Peer moves, the matter is not solid and is hard to accomplish. If it appears and has force, it can be accomplished quickly, but fear Void. The Yi says: a moving line is urgent as fire. Next come the document line and the Noble appearing. But if the hexagram originally lacks it, or it does not enter the hexagram, or it is Void, the matter is hard. If Pressure and Resource are both strong, they must also hold the Self before it can be accomplished. If the Other does not control the Self either, the matter stands clear. In the palaces of Qian, Dui and Kan, the scheming is not single-minded; when Pressure is seen moving, the man is mostly out, and if one sees him he flares up in anger.

> [!note] 按
> 「请判」是请上司批判、批示。「宜官鬼出现、忌动发」是求职请批时官鬼要出现、不要发动，否则事情生变；「在任宜鬼静」是已在任时官鬼要静，「鬼发有动」是鬼一发动就有变动，「子动有替」是子孙动就有人来接替。后面一组是谋干事的通则：六爻只有一爻动最吉（目标集中），兄动不实，用爻有气可速成（「易云动爻急如火」是引《易》），怕落空；文书、贵人出现是次一等的好；原本没有、不入卦、落空，事难；官和文书都旺相，还要持世才成；应不克世，事情才清楚。最后一句「乾兑坎宫……」是说这几宫谋事不专一，官爻发动则所要见的人多外出、见了也发怒；书里没有再展开。
>
> *Note:* 'Qingpan' is asking a superior for a ruling or an instruction. 'It is right for Pressure to appear and wrong for it to move' is that in seeking a post and asking for a ruling Pressure should appear and not move, otherwise the matter changes; 'for one in office, it is right for Pressure to be still' is that for one already in office Pressure should be still; 'Pressure moving means a change' is that as soon as Pressure moves there is a change; 'Output moving means someone to take his place' is Output moving meaning that someone will succeed him. The group after it is a general rule for seeking business: only one line of the six moving is most auspicious (the aim is concentrated), Peer moving is not solid, a Subject line with force can succeed quickly ('the Yi says a moving line is urgent as fire' quotes the Yi) but fears Void; the document line and the Noble appearing are second best; if originally absent, not in the hexagram, or Void, the matter is hard; if Pressure and Resource are both strong, they must also hold the Self to succeed; the Other not controlling the Self, the matter is clear. The last sentence, 'in the palaces of Qian, Dui and Kan ...', says that in these palaces the scheming is not single-minded, and with Pressure moving the man one wants to see is mostly out and angry when seen; the book does not expand on it.
