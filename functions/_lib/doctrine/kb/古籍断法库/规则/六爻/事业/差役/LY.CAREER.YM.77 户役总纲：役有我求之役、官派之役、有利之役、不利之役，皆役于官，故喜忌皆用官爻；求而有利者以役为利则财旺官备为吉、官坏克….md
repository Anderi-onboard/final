# 户役总纲：役有我求之役、官派之役、有利之役、不利之役，皆役于官，故喜忌皆用官爻；求而有利者以役为利则财旺官备为吉、官坏克世为凶

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 役有不同：有我自己求的役，有官府派的役，有有利的役，有不利的役，都是替官府当役。所以喜忌都用官爻：求而有利的，以役为利，就以财旺、官备为吉，以官坏、克世为凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Service differs: there is service one seeks oneself, service the authorities assign, service that pays, and service that does not, all of it service to the authorities. So what is welcome and what is feared are all taken from the Pressure line: for service one seeks and which pays, taking the service as the profit, thriving Wealth and a complete Pressure line are auspicious, and damaged Pressure and Pressure controlling the Self are ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 「役」是官府摊派的差役、徭役。「我求之役」是自己谋求的差使（如衙门里的差事），「官派之役」是官府强派的徭役，「有利之役」「不利之役」是有油水的和白当的。「财旺官备」是财爻旺、官爻齐备（有官有俸）。这一章是易林补遗户役（EL.109 起）、帮户（EL.112 起）、除名脱役（EL.114 起）的同类，用爻也是官鬼。章首目录：谋差、顶役、脱役、助役、卸役、讼役、避役、防患八问。
>
> *Note:* 'Yi' is service, duties and corvée assigned by the government. 'Service one seeks oneself' is a duty one seeks (such as a post in a yamen), 'service the authorities assign' is corvée forced by the government, and 'service that pays' and 'service that does not' are those with perquisites and those done for nothing. 'Thriving Wealth and a complete Pressure line' is the Wealth line thriving and the Pressure line complete (an office with salary). This chapter is of a kind with the Yilin Buyi on service (from EL.109), on helping households (from EL.112) and on being struck off and freed from duty (from EL.114), and the line used is likewise Pressure. The index at the head of the chapter lists eight questions: seeking a duty, taking another's place, being freed from a duty, getting help, unloading, litigating, avoiding, and guarding against trouble.
