# 仗托人力结语：诸般之重托惟凭一卦之端详，百里倾心千金重托全凭一诺之寄皆系六爻之定

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 各种重大的托付，只凭一卦的细细审详；百里之外倾心相托、千金的重托，全凭一句承诺的寄托，都系于六爻的断定。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For every kind of weighty entrusting, one relies only on the careful examination of one hexagram; a heart given from a hundred li away and a thousand pieces of gold entrusted, depending wholly on one man's promise, all hang on the verdict of the six lines.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1647 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 百里倾心千金重托，全凭一诺之寄，皆系六爻之定。 | 百里之外倾心相托、千金的重托，全凭一句承诺的寄托，都系于六爻的断定。 | A heart given from a hundred li away and a thousand pieces of gold entrusted, depending wholly on one man's promise, all hang on the verdict of the six lines. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这是仗托人力一章的结语，后面没有解说。「一诺」是一句承诺；「百里」是百里之外。
>
> *Note:* This is the closing of the chapter on relying on others' strength, and the book gives no explanation after it. 'Yi nuo' is one promise; 'bai li' is a hundred li away.
