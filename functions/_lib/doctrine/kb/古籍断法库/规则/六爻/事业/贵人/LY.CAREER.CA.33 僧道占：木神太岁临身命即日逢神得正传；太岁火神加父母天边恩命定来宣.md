# 僧道占：木神太岁临身命即日逢神得正传；太岁火神加父母天边恩命定来宣

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木神、太岁落在身和命上，当天就遇上神人、得到真传；太岁、火神加在父母上，天边的恩命一定会来宣布。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wood spirit and Tai Sui on the Body and one's fate: on that very day one meets a divine person and receives the true transmission; Tai Sui and the Fire spirit added to Resource: a favour commanded from the edge of heaven will surely be proclaimed.
