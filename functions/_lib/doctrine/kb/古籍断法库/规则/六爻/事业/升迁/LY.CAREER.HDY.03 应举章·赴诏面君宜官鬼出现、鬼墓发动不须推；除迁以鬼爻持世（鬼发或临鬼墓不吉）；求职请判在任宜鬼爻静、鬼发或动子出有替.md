# 应举章·赴诏面君宜官鬼出现、鬼墓发动不须推；除迁以鬼爻持世（鬼发或临鬼墓不吉）；求职请判在任宜鬼爻静、鬼发或动子出有替

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赴诏、面见君主，宜官鬼出现；官鬼的墓发动，不必再推。除迁（任命调迁），以官鬼爻持世为宜，官鬼发动、或落在鬼墓上，就不吉利。求职、请求考核评定，在任的人宜官鬼爻安静；官鬼发动，或子孙发动出来，就有人接替。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For going to court on summons or seeing the sovereign, it is fitting that Pressure appear; the tomb of Pressure moving needs no further reckoning. For appointment and transfer it is right that the Pressure line hold the Self, and if Pressure moves or stands on the tomb of Pressure it is not auspicious. For seeking a post or an appraisal, one in office should have the Pressure line still; if Pressure moves, or Output moves out, there is a replacement.

> [!note] 按
> 这三句在「应举」一段里，但说的是做官的人：赴诏、除迁、求职请判、在任，不是考试。第三句和火珠林占科举之前「求职请判」一段（HZL.02）是同一句话。「鬼墓发动不须推」没有说为什么不用再推，按字面译。
>
> *Note:* These three passages stand in the section on taking the examination but speak of men holding office: going on summons, appointment and transfer, seeking a post or appraisal, and being in office, not of examinations. The third is the same sentence as the 'seeking a post or appraisal' passage in the Huozhulin (HZL.02). 'The tomb of Pressure moving needs no further reckoning' does not say why, and is translated literally.
