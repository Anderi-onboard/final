# 子占父功名·父旺官动相生皆许成名，财克父、子动伤官、父官变凶、日月克官克父、父官衰墓破绝皆主无益（书中例：涣之夬，寅年起用、终于未年；观之涣，父在京候补，未土父持世巳官生之，补四川）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 儿子占父亲的功名：父母旺相、官星发动相生，或日月作官星来生父母，或父母旺相变出官星，或父母、官星发动变吉，都可以答应成名；财克父母、子孙发动伤官星、父母和官星发动变凶、日月克官星克父母，或者父爻、官爻衰弱入墓被破入绝，都主没有益处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A son divining about his father's career: if Resource is thriving and the star of office moves to generate it, or the Day or Month acts as the star of office and generates Resource, or a thriving Resource turns into the star of office, or Resource and the star of office move and turn auspicious, he may be granted a name; if Wealth controls Resource, Output moves to injure the star of office, Resource and the star of office move and turn bad, the Day or Month controls the star of office and Resource, or the Resource line or the Pressure line is weak, in tomb, broken or extinct, all mean no gain.

> | 又因用神重叠，逢墓库以收藏也。 | 又因为用神重叠，遇到墓库就被收藏了。 | Also, because the Subject was doubled, meeting the tomb it was stored away. |

> [!note] 按
> 「起用」是被重新任用；「候补」是等候补缺。原文这一章在两个例子之间有一段今人的评语（「居士评」），不收。
>
> *Note:* 'Qiyong' is being given a post again and 'houbu' is waiting for a vacancy. Between the two examples this chapter has a modern comment ('the lay scholar's comment'), which is not quoted.
