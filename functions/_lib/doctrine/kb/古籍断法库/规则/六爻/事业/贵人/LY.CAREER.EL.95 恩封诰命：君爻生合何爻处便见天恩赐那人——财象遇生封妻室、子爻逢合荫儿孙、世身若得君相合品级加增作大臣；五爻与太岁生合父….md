# 恩封诰命：君爻生合何爻处便见天恩赐那人——财象遇生封妻室、子爻逢合荫儿孙、世身若得君相合品级加增作大臣；五爻与太岁生合父母必赠椿萱，生合财爻必封妻室，生扶福德当荫儿孙，生合世身再加官爻旺相自当品职高升

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 君爻生合哪一爻，就看到天恩赐给那个人：财爻遇生，封赠妻子；子爻逢合，荫及儿孙；世爻、身爻如果与君爻相合，品级加增，做到大臣。具体说：五爻和太岁生合父母爻，一定赠封父母（椿萱）；生合财爻，一定封赠妻室；生扶福德（子孙），应当荫及儿孙；生合世爻、身爻，再加官爻旺相，自然品级职位高升。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wherever the sovereign's line generates or combines, there one sees heaven's favour conferred on that person: if the Wealth image meets generation, the wife is enfeoffed; if the Output line meets combination, descendants receive privilege; if the Self or Body is combined with the sovereign's line, one's rank is increased and one becomes a great minister. In detail: if the fifth line and Tai Sui generate or combine with Resource, the parents will surely be honoured with a title; if they generate or combine with Wealth, the wife will surely be enfeoffed; if they generate and support Output, the fortune virtue, descendants should receive privilege; if they generate or combine with the Self or Body and the Pressure line is further thriving, one's rank and post will naturally be raised high.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「椿萱」是父母（椿指父、萱指母）。「荫儿孙」是朝廷凭官员的品级给子孙入学、授官的恩典。「封妻室」是封赠妻子为诰命夫人。「君相合」的「君相」疑是「君象」，指五爻、太岁这些代表君王的象。这一条与易隐官禄占「太岁五爻生合父母椿萱受赠；生合妻财室人受诰；生合子孙儿孙得荫」（YY.18）是同一套：君爻生合哪一个六亲，恩典就落在哪一个人身上。
>
> *Note:* 'Chunxuan' is the parents ('chun' for the father, 'xuan' for the mother). 'Yin er sun' is the privilege by which the court gives descendants a place in the school or an office according to the official's rank. 'Feng qishi' is the wife being given a patent as a titled lady. The 'xiang' of 'jun xiang he' is presumably 'xiang', image, the images that stand for the sovereign such as the fifth line and Tai Sui. This entry is the same set as 'Tai Sui and the fifth line generate or combine with Resource, the parents are honoured; with Wealth, the wife receives a patent; with Output, the children receive privilege' in the Yi Yin divination on official salary (YY.18): whichever of the six kin the sovereign's line generates or combines with, the favour falls on that person.
