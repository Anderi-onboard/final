# 卜筮元龟·占居官有疾病否：卦变为囚死乡居官身病至衰亡；无气带杀终有祸、更值空亡必有丧；鬼动逢休废地则退位终无位；胎没并之世自微、加刑杀为衰

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占在任的官有没有疾病：卦变成囚、死之地，在任的人身体有病以至衰亡；无气又带杀，终究有祸，再遇上空亡必有丧事；官鬼发动而遇休、废之地，这个人退位后终究没有官位；胎、没再并上，世就自然衰微，再加刑、杀，就是衰败。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On divining whether an official in post will fall ill: when the hexagram changes into a trapped or dead place the one in office falls ill, even to decline and death; without qi and carrying a baleful spirit there is in the end calamity, and meeting Void as well there is surely a death; if Pressure moves and meets a place of rest or abandonment, the man leaves his post and in the end has none; with Embryo and Sunk joined to it the Self is naturally weak, and with punishment and baleful spirits added it is decline.
