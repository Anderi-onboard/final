# 求馆·官如藏伏，应无督集之人（旧注：官鬼能生父母，占馆以此爻为纠率子弟之人；不出现或落空必无人聚生徒成学馆又无人推荐；空而逢冲、伏而提起须央浼其人方肯出来）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼能生扶父母，所以占馆以官鬼这一爻代表招集率领学生的人。官鬼不出现或者落空，必定没有人聚集学生来成学馆，事情难成，也没有人推荐；如果空而逢冲、伏而被提起，必须央求那个人，他才肯出来招集。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure can generate and support Resource, so in divining a school post the Pressure line stands for the person who gathers and leads the pupils. If Pressure does not appear or falls Void, there will surely be no one to gather pupils to make up a school, the matter is hard to bring about, and no one will recommend one; if it is Void and meets a clash, or is hidden and drawn up, one must beg that person before he is willing to come forward and gather them.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章里的官鬼有两个用处：发动合身是「贵人推荐」（LY.CAREER.HJC.36），不动而在卦是「招集学生的人」（这一条）。「纠率子弟」是召集并带领学生。「央浼」是恳求。
>
> *Note:* In this chapter Pressure has two uses: moving and combining with the Body is 'a noble's recommendation' (LY.CAREER.HJC.36), and present in the hexagram and not moving it is 'the person who gathers the pupils' (this entry). 'Gather and lead' is calling the pupils together and leading them. 'Beg' is entreating.
