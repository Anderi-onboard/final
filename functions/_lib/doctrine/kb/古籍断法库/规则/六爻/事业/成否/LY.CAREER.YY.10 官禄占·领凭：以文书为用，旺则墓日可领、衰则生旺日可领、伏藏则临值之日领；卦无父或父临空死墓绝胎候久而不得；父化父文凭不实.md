# 官禄占·领凭：以文书为用，旺则墓日可领、衰则生旺日可领、伏藏则临值之日领；卦无父或父临空死墓绝胎候久而不得；父化父文凭不实

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 领凭证：以文书（父母）为用神。文书旺相，到它入墓的日子可以领；衰弱，到它生旺的日子可以领；伏藏，到它当值的日子领。卦里没有父母，或者父母临空、死、墓、绝、胎，等很久也领不到；父母变出父母，文凭不实在。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Collecting the credentials: the document line is the Subject. If it is thriving, the credentials can be collected on the day it enters the tomb; if weak, on a day of its birth or strength; if hidden, on the day in which it rules. If the hexagram has no Resource line, or Resource sits on Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, one waits long and does not get them; if Resource turns into Resource, the credentials are not genuine.
