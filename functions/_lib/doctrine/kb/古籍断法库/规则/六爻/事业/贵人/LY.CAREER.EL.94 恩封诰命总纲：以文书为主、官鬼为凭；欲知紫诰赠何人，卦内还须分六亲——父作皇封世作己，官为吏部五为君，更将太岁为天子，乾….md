# 恩封诰命总纲：以文书为主、官鬼为凭；欲知紫诰赠何人，卦内还须分六亲——父作皇封世作己，官为吏部五为君，更将太岁为天子，乾卦当称帝王尊，君位怕临空与绝，五爻忌克世和身；凡占诰命父母为用爻，不绝不空终须有望

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占恩封诰命，以文书爻为主，以官鬼为凭据。想知道诰命要赠给谁，卦里要分六亲：父爻当作皇帝的封诰，世爻是自己，官爻是吏部，五爻是君王，再把太岁当作天子，乾卦应当称作帝王的尊位；君位怕落空和绝，五爻忌克制世爻和身爻。凡是占诰命，父母爻是用爻，不绝不空，终究有指望；又看世爻是自己、官爻是吏部、五爻是天子、太岁是朝廷，都不要落空亡；代表君位的爻，千万不要伤身、克世；乾卦也代表君王，不论内卦外卦得到这个象的，一定受到恩封。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about an honorific patent, take the document as the chief and Pressure as the ground. To know on whom the imperial patent will be conferred, one must distinguish the six kin in the hexagram: Resource is taken as the imperial patent, the Self is oneself, the star of office is the Board of Personnel, the fifth line is the sovereign, and Tai Sui is further taken as the Son of Heaven; the Qian hexagram is called the honoured emperor; the sovereign's position fears Void and extinction, and the fifth line must not control the Self and the Body. In every divination about a patent, Resource is the Subject line, and if it is not extinct and not Void there must in the end be hope; look also at the Self as oneself, the star of office as the Board of Personnel, the fifth line as the Son of Heaven and Tai Sui as the court, none of which may be Void; the line of the sovereign's position must on no account hurt the Body or control the Self; the Qian hexagram also stands for the sovereign, and whoever gets this image, inner or outer, will surely receive the conferment.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「诰命」是皇帝授予官员及其亲属的封赠文书，「紫诰」是用紫泥封的诏书；「史部」的「史」是「吏」的误写，译作「吏部」，管官员任免的部。「皇封」是皇帝的封诰。「乾卦」是乾，天和君的象。「五爻为天子，太岁为朝廷」与上书、面君两章同取（EL.89、EL.91）。「用爻」就是用神：这里是父母爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Gaoming' is the document by which the emperor confers titles on officials and their kin, and 'zigao', the purple patent, is an edict sealed with purple clay; the 'shi' of 'shi bu' is a miswriting of 'li', and it is translated 'Board of Personnel', the board in charge of appointing and dismissing officials. 'Huangfeng' is the emperor's patent of enfeoffment. 'The Qian hexagram' is Qian, the image of heaven and the sovereign. 'The fifth line is the Son of Heaven and Tai Sui is the court' is taken as in the chapters on submitting memorials and on an audience (EL.89, EL.91). 'Yongyao', the Subject line, is the Subject: here the Resource line.
