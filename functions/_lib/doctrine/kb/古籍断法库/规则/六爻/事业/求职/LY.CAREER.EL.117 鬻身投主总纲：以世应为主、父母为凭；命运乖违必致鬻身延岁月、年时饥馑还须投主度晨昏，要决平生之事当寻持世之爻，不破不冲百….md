# 鬻身投主总纲：以世应为主、父母为凭；命运乖违必致鬻身延岁月、年时饥馑还须投主度晨昏，要决平生之事当寻持世之爻，不破不冲百年可辅、落空落陷一载难从；鬻身者出乎不得已须择良善之主，世爻旺相生扶可以聊生，世落空亡或值日冲月破虽暂时安置终身落寞终是不久

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要卖身投靠主人（鬻身投主），以世爻、应爻为主，以父母爻为凭据。命运不顺，必定要卖身来延续岁月；年成饥荒，还得投靠主人来度过早晚。要决断一生的事，应当寻找持世的爻：不破不冲，一百年都可以得到辅助；落空落陷，一年也难以相从。卖身投主的人是出于不得已，必须选择良善的主人：世爻旺相、得到生扶，可以勉强过活；世爻落空亡，或者遇到日冲、月破，虽然是暂时安顿，毕竟一生落寞，即使勉强维持，终究不久。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In selling oneself into service under a master, take the Self and the Other as the chief and Resource as the ground. When one's fate runs against one, one must sell oneself to prolong one's days; in a famine year one must still take a master to get through morning and evening. To decide the affairs of a lifetime, one should seek the line that holds the Self: if it is neither broken nor clashed, a hundred years can be aided; if it falls to Void or into a pit, it is hard to follow even for one year. Those who sell themselves do so out of necessity and must choose a good and kind master: if the Self line is thriving and supported one can eke out a living; if the Self falls on Void, or meets the Day's clash or Month-break, then although one is temporarily settled, in the end one is desolate all one's life, and even if one makes do it will not last.

> [!note] 按
> 「鬻身投主」是把自己卖给主人做奴仆、投靠主人过活。这一章问的是卖身投靠主人，所以世爻是自己，应爻、父母爻是主人（后文「应为主象」「父为主世为我」）。「良善之生」的「生」疑是「主」，译作「良善的主人」。「落陷」是落入空、墓、绝一类凶地（见前）。「聊生」是勉强过活。「日冲」是日辰冲世，「月破」是月建冲破。
>
> *Note:* 'Yushen touzhu' is selling oneself to a master as a servant and attaching oneself to a master to live. The chapter asks about selling oneself into a master's service, so the Self is oneself and the Other and Resource are the master (by the later 'the Other is the image of the master' and 'Resource is the master and the Self is me'). The 'sheng' of 'liangshan zhi sheng' is presumably 'zhu', master, and it is translated 'a good and kind master'. 'Falling into a pit' is falling into a bad place such as Void, tomb or extinction (see earlier). 'Liaosheng' is making a bare living. 'Day clash' is the Day clashing the Self, and 'Month-break' is the Month breaking it.
