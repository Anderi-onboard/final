# 官禄占·在任（二）：父受伤财空绝地瘠民贫；父身动兄世动政繁赋缺；日并兄动伤世吏多舞文；兄化子孙克世民多梗化

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任，任所的民情和属吏：印星受伤而财爻落空、绝的，任所地瘠民贫；印星、身爻发动，而兄爻、世爻也发动的，政事繁杂而赋税不足；日辰合着发动的兄爻伤世爻的，属吏多半舞文弄法；兄爻变出子孙来克世爻的，百姓多半顽梗，不服教化。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In office, the people and the clerks of the post. If the seal line is injured and Wealth is Void or extinct, the land is barren and the people poor; if the seal line and the Body move, and the Peer line and the Self also move, government business is heavy and taxes fall short; if the Day joins a moving Peer line in injuring the Self, the clerks mostly twist the written law; if a Peer line turns into Output and that controls the Self, the people are mostly stubborn and refuse to be reformed.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「舞文」是玩弄法令文书，「梗化」是顽固不服从教化。第一句里「父」是单字，这里按上一条译成印星；印星受伤可以读作官府的庇护不足，但书里没有解释，译文不加。
>
> *Note:* 'Wuwen' is twisting laws and documents, and 'genghua' is stubbornly refusing reform. The single character 'father' in the first sentence is rendered as the seal line, as in the previous entry; an injured seal line could be read as too little protection from the government, but the book does not explain it, and the translation adds nothing.
