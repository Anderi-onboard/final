# 卜筮元龟·占得见贵人否：欲见贵人占月卦（卦身），官鬼生身尤益；卦若无身又无鬼则欲见临行必止；官鬼落空亡则终不得见

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要见贵人，看月卦（月的卦身）：卦里有着落，就能相见；占卦要见到官鬼爻，官鬼生身更有益；卦里如果没有身、又没有鬼，想去见人临出发一定又作罢；卦中官鬼落空亡，终究见不到。注文举例：假如正月占得泰卦，月令寅和卦身上的官鬼寅并在一起，就是和那个人平等相交。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To see a noble, look at the monthly hexagram and its Body: if the hexagram has a footing in it one can meet; in divining one must see the Pressure line, and Pressure generating the Body is all the more helpful; if the hexagram has no Body and no Pressure, one who wishes to go and see will surely stop again as he sets out; if Pressure in the hexagram falls Void, in the end one cannot see him. The note gives an example: suppose in the first month one gets the hexagram Tai, and the month branch Yin joins the Pressure Yin on the Body of the hexagram: one meets that person as an equal.

> [!note] 按
