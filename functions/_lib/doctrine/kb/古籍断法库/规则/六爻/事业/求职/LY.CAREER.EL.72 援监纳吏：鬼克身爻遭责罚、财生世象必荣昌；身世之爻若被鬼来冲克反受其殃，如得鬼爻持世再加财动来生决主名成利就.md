# 援监纳吏：鬼克身爻遭责罚、财生世象必荣昌；身世之爻若被鬼来冲克反受其殃，如得鬼爻持世再加财动来生决主名成利就

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼克制身爻，就遭责罚；财爻生世爻，就一定荣昌。身爻、世爻这两爻，如果被官鬼来冲克，反而受它的祸害；如果官鬼爻持世，再加上财爻发动来生它，一定名成利就。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure controls the Body line one suffers reproof and punishment; if Wealth generates the Self image one will surely prosper. As for the Body and Self lines, if Pressure comes to clash or control them, one suffers its calamity instead; if the Pressure line holds the Self and Wealth moves to generate it, one will surely win name and profit.

> [!note] 按
> 「身世之爻」是身爻（卦身）和世爻。官鬼本来是求官的用神，克世身又要受责罚，书里要分的是：官鬼冲克世身是祸，官鬼持世而财爻来生是福。第一段解末尾的两句与这里解的第二、三句文字相同，是书里重复排印的。这一条与黄金策仕宦「鬼杀伤身」（HJC.13）取同一个关系，官鬼旺、财爻发动来生，与增删卜易「妻摇鬼旺，定然执王拜丹墀」（ZS.23）相合。
>
> *Note:* 'Shen shi zhi yao' is the Body line (the Body of the hexagram) and the Self line. Pressure is the Subject for seeking office, yet when it controls the Self or Body one suffers reproof, and what the book is distinguishing is this: Pressure clashing or controlling the Self or Body is calamity, while Pressure holding the Self with Wealth generating it is fortune. The two sentences at the end of the first explanation are the same words as the second and third sentences of the explanation here, which the book prints twice. This entry takes the same relation as 'a baleful Pressure injuring the Body' in the Huangjin Ce on official careers (HJC.13), and Pressure thriving with Wealth moving to generate it agrees with 'when Wealth sways and Pressure thrives, one will surely bow at the vermilion steps of the court' in the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.23).
