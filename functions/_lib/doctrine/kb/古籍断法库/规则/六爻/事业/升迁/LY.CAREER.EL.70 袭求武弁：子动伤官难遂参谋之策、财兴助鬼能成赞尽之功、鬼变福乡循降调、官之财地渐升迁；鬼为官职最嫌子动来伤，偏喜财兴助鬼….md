# 袭求武弁：子动伤官难遂参谋之策、财兴助鬼能成赞尽之功、鬼变福乡循降调、官之财地渐升迁；鬼为官职最嫌子动来伤，偏喜财兴助鬼，官变子孙非降即调，鬼之财象迭迭高升

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动伤克官爻，难以实现参谋的计策；财爻兴旺帮助官鬼，能成就辅佐到底的功劳。官鬼变到福乡（子孙），就顺着降职、调动；官爻变到财地（财爻），就渐渐升迁。官鬼是官职，最怕子孙发动来伤它，偏偏喜欢财爻兴旺帮助官鬼；官鬼变成子孙，不是降职就是调走；官鬼变成财的象，就一再高升。细细玩味占易道理的玄妙精微，就可以断定武官的吉凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output moves to hurt the star of office, it is hard to carry out one's counsel as a staff adviser; if Wealth flourishes and aids Pressure, one can accomplish the merit of assisting to the end. If Pressure changes into the fortune quarter, Output, it follows that one is demoted or transferred; if the star of office changes into the Wealth ground, one is gradually promoted. Pressure is the official post, and it most dislikes Output moving to hurt it but particularly likes Wealth flourishing to aid it; if Pressure changes into Output, it is either demotion or transfer; if Pressure changes into the Wealth image, one is promoted again and again. By savouring the subtle principles of the Yi divination, one can decide the good and ill of a military officer.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 786 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse

> | 玩占易理玄微，可决戎官之休咎。 | 细细玩味占易道理的玄妙精微，就可以断定武官的吉凶。 | By savouring the subtle principles of the Yi divination, one can decide the good and ill of a military officer. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「福乡」是子孙（福神）所在，「财地」是财爻所在。「赞尽之功」书里就是这几个字，按字面译作「辅佐到底的功劳」。「戎官」是武官。末句是全章的结语。这一条与前面仕宦升迁章「官化子孙非降即调」「官若化财平升品级」（EL.56）是同一类断语，这里换到武职上。
>
> *Note:* 'Fuxiang', the fortune quarter, is where Output (the fortune spirit) stands, and 'caidi', the Wealth ground, is where the Wealth line stands. 'Zanjin zhi gong' is exactly these characters in the book and is translated literally as 'the merit of assisting to the end'. 'Rongguan' is a military officer. The last sentence is the closing line of the whole chapter. This entry is the same kind of judgment as 'if Pressure changes into Output it is demotion or transfer' and 'if Pressure changes into Wealth, a level promotion' in the preceding chapter on official promotion (EL.56), here moved to military posts.
