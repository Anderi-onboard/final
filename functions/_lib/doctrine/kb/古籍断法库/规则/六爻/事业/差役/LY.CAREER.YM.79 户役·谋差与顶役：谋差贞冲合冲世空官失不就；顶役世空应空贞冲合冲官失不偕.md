# 户役·谋差与顶役：谋差贞冲合冲世空官失不就；顶役世空应空贞冲合冲官失不偕

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占谋差使能不能成：本卦六冲、合处逢冲、世空、官失（官爻失去），就是不成。顶替差役能不能成：世空、应空、本卦六冲、合处逢冲、官失，就是合不到一起、办不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining whether a bid for a duty will come off: the original hexagram clashed, a combination clashed, the Self Void, or Pressure lost, mean it does not come off. Whether taking another's place in a duty will come off: the Self Void, the Other Void, the original hexagram clashed, a combination clashed, or Pressure lost, mean the two do not tally and it cannot be done.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「谋差」是谋求差使，「顶役」是顶替别人的差役（两样另见易林补遗谋差顶役 EL.106–108）。「贞冲」是本卦（贞）是六冲卦，「合冲」是合处逢冲，是三冲里的两种（见 YM.07 注）。「官失」是官爻失去（不上卦、落空或被破）。「不偕」是不能相合、不能一同成事：顶役要两边（我和被顶替的人）合得上才成，所以世、应都不能空。
>
> *Note:* 'Moucai' is seeking a duty, and 'dingyi' is taking another's place in a duty (the two are also treated in the Yilin Buyi on seeking a commission and taking a place, EL.106-108). 'Zhenchong' is the original hexagram (zhen) being a six-clash hexagram, and 'hechong' is a combination meeting a clash, two of the three clashes (see the note to YM.07). 'Guanshi' is the Pressure line being lost (absent from the hexagram, Void or broken). 'Buxie' is not tallying and not accomplishing the matter together: taking another's place needs the two sides (myself and the man replaced) to fit, so neither the Self nor the Other may be Void.
