# 仕宦·财空鬼动，声名振而囊箧空虚（旧注：官鬼动生合世无冲克则声名闻望；财爻生扶合助有气不空则既会做官又会赚钱、内实贪赂外不丧名；财空伏死绝则声名有而贿赂无）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动，来生合世爻，日辰、月建、动爻的变化又没有冲克它的，做官必定有声名威望；再得到财爻生扶合助、有气不空，就既会做官又会赚钱，内里实际贪图贿赂，外面不丧失名声；如果财爻空亡、伏藏或者临死绝，就是声名虽有，贿赂却没有。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure moves to generate or combine with the Self and the changes of the Day, the Month and the moving lines do not clash or control it, one will surely have fame and standing in office; if Wealth also generates, supports and helps it, with breath and not Void, one both knows how to hold office and how to make money, taking bribes within while not losing one's name without; if Wealth is Void, hidden, or in death or extinction, one has the fame but not the bribes.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> | 凡官动生合世爻，日月动爻又无冲克者，为官必有声名闻望； | 凡是官鬼发动生合世爻，日辰、月建、动爻又没有冲克的，做官必定有声名威望； | Whenever Pressure moves to generate or combine with the Self and the Day, the Month and the moving lines do not clash or control it, one will surely have fame and standing in office; |
> | 更得财爻生扶合助，则内实贪赂，外不丧名； | 再得到财爻生扶合助，就是内里实际贪图贿赂，外面不丧失名声； | if Wealth also generates, supports and helps it, one takes bribes within and does not lose one's name without; |
> | 若世爻空伏，财爻死绝，声名虽有，贿赂却无也。 | 如果世爻空亡或伏藏，财爻死绝，就是声名虽有，贿赂却没有。 | if the Self is Void or hidden and Wealth is in death or extinction, one has the fame but no bribes. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文题目说的是「财空鬼动」，即财爻空亡而官鬼发动：有名无利。旧注、王洪绪的注都是顺着「官动有名」再看财的有无，所以「内实贪赂，外不丧名」是说财有气时的情形（官贪而有名），不是说书劝人如此，只是描写这种卦象。「囊箧」是口袋和箱子，「囊箧空虚」是没钱。王洪绪把旧注的「财爻或空或伏、或临死绝」改成「世爻空伏，财爻死绝」，多了世爻这一条。
>
> *Note:* The verse speaks of 'Wealth Void and Pressure moving', that is, fame without profit. Both commentaries go from 'Pressure moving gives fame' and then look at whether there is Wealth, so 'taking bribes within while not losing one's name without' describes the case where Wealth has breath (an official who takes bribes and yet has a name), and is not the book recommending it, only describing this kind of hexagram. 'Purse and chest' are the bag and the box, and 'the purse is empty' means there is no money. Wang Hongxu changes the old commentary's 'Wealth Void or hidden, or in death or extinction' into 'the Self Void or hidden and Wealth in death or extinction', adding the Self.
