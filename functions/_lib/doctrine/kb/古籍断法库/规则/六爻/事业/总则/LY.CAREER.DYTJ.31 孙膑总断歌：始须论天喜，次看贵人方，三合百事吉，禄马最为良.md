# 孙膑总断歌：始须论天喜，次看贵人方，三合百事吉，禄马最为良

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断卦一开始先看天喜，其次看贵人所在，三合则百事都吉，禄、马最好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In judging a hexagram first look at the Joy star, next at where the Noble is; three-harmony makes all things auspicious, and the Emolument and the Horse are the best.

## 《断易天机·占仕宦第十一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 2751 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 11: Divining official careers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 始须论天喜， | 开头先要看天喜， | First one must look at the Joy star (Tianxi), |
> | 次看贵人方， | 其次看贵人所在的方位， | next look at where the Noble is, |
> | 三合百事吉， | 三合，百事都吉； | three-harmony: all things are auspicious; |
> | 禄马最为良。 | 禄、马最好。 | the Emolument and the Horse are the best. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这首总断歌是各种问题通用的；求官只是其中一用。歌后面括注的说明里混着今人的话，本库只引歌诀。
>
> *Note:* This general song serves every kind of question and seeking office is only one use of it. The parenthetical explanations after the song are mixed with a modern editor's words, so only the song itself is quoted.
