# 儒业章·功名仕途：青龙持世功名大、天喜同官道业亨、朱雀并财宜作馆；世应和身旺上书献策必成、日辰龙德生旺县宰州官力主盟；禄马贵人临月建公侯任责；朝君忌屯蹇、面圣宜晋升、天符持木高迁重任禄光荣

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙持世，功名大；天喜同官，道业亨通；朱雀并财，宜开馆；勾陈同鬼，不要出游。世、应既和睦、身又旺，上书献策必定能成；日辰、龙德都得生旺，县令、州官会出力相助。禄、马、贵人落在月建上，公侯担任重责，官职不轻；天符值土，应当没有阻碍；月建持金，必有惊恐。父母最宜逢六合，世、身切忌犯三刑；刑、杀克身又克世，会陷身受辱、耽误一生。朝见君主，不可遇上屯、蹇；面圣偏适宜见到晋、升；更何况天符持木德，高升、担重任，俸禄光荣。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Azure Dragon holding the Self, fame and rank are great; with the Joy star together with the office, the Way and its work go through; the Vermilion Bird joined to Wealth suits opening a school; Hook Earth with Pressure, do not go travelling about. With the Self and the Other in accord and the Body strong as well, submitting a memorial or a plan is sure to be accomplished; with the Day and Dragon Virtue both born and strong, the county magistrate and prefect will lend their strength. With the Emolument, the Horse and the Noble standing on the Month, dukes and marquises bear heavy charge and the post is weighty; with the Year Tally on Earth there is probably no hindrance; with the Month holding Metal there is sure to be fright. Resource is best meeting the six combinations, and the Self and the Body must avoid the three punishments; punishment and baleful spirits controlling the Body and the Self, one falls into disgrace and ruins one's life. To attend the sovereign one must not meet Zhun or Jian; to face the sage ruler it suits meeting Jin or Sheng; and moreover the Year Tally holding Wood virtue, there is high promotion, heavy charge and glorious emolument.

> [!note] 按
> 儒业章前半（「凡占儒业究诸身……」到「青龙持世功名大」之前）讲读书、学业、功名，归考试一类，这里不收；从「青龙持世功名大」起，讲到上书、县宰州官、公侯任责、朝君面圣、高迁重任，是仕途，收在这里。「天符」是年建（见 ZSZ.08）。
>
> *Note:* The first part of the chapter on the scholar's calling (from 'in divining the scholar's calling, examine the Body' up to the line before 'with the Azure Dragon holding the Self, fame and rank are great') speaks of study, learning and examination success and is for the examination domain, not taken here; from 'with the Azure Dragon holding the Self' on it speaks of memorials, magistrates and prefects, dukes and marquises, attending and facing the sovereign and high promotion, which is a career in office and is taken here. The 'Year Tally' is the year branch (see ZSZ.08).
