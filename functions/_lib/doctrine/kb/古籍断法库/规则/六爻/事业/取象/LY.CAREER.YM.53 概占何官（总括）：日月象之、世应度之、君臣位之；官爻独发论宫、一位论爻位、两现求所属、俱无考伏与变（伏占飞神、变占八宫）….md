# 概占何官（总括）：日月象之、世应度之、君臣位之；官爻独发论宫、一位论爻位、两现求所属、俱无考伏与变（伏占飞神、变占八宫）；官临日月建非社稷之臣即直节科道、持世为正印临应为左右、五爻为君二爻为大臣；虽代有更名理不远

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日、月来象征它，世、应来度量它，君、臣来定它的位置。所以官爻独发的论宫，官爻只有一位的论爻位，官爻两现的求它所属，都没有的就考伏和变：伏的占飞神，变的占八宫。伏、变的办法也可以分出位置和所属。要知道当世的官守，用象去求，即使各代名称有更改，道理相差不远。官临日辰月建，不是社稷之臣，一定是直节的科道官；官爻持世是正印，临应是左右辅佐之职；五爻是君，不是御史就是当宠幸之臣；二爻是大臣，是宰相，是国家的仪表。卦里没有官鬼，就求伏下的飞神，用位和所属来分辨；卦有动象，就求所变的官爻，用八宫来区别。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Day and Month image it, the Self and the Other measure it, ruler and minister fix its place. So when the Pressure line moves alone one discusses the palace; when there is only one Pressure line one discusses the line position; when two Pressure lines show one seeks the department they belong to; when there is none one examines the hidden and the changed: for a hidden one one divines the flying line, for a changed one the eight palaces; this method of hidden and changed lines can also separate out the place and the department. To know the offices of the present age, one seeks them by the image, and though the names have been changed from age to age, the principle is not far off. When Pressure sits on the Day or Month branch, if he is not a minister of the altars of the state, he is surely an upright censor or supervising official; the Pressure line on the Self is the principal seal, and on the Other it is a post at the side, an assistant; the fifth line is the sovereign, and if not a censor he is a minister favoured by the throne; the second line is a great minister, a chief minister, the model of the state. If there is no Pressure line in the hexagram, one seeks the flying line above the hidden one and tells it by position and department; if there is a moving image, one seeks the Pressure line it changes into and tells it by the eight palaces.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是 YM.49–52 的总括：先看官爻在卦里的情形（独发、只有一位、两现、全无），再决定用哪一把尺：宫、爻位、所属、伏神的飞神、变出的八宫。「社稷之臣」是关系国家安危的重臣，「直节」是刚直有节操，「科道」是六科给事中和各道监察御史。「正印」是掌印的正官，「左右之职」是辅佐之职。「宠幸之臣」是受君主宠信的近臣。「伏变这法」的「这」疑是「之」之误，按文意译作「伏、变的办法」。「代有更名」是历代官名有改变，用象求就不受名称变化影响。注里「官临日月建」里的「月建」是当月的月建（见术语表）。
>
> *Note:* This entry sums up YM.49–52: first look at how the Pressure line stands in the hexagram (moving alone, only one, two showing, none), then decide which ruler to use: the palace, the line position, the department, the flying line of a hidden one, or the eight palaces of a changed one. 'Shejizhichen' is a leading minister on whom the safety of the state depends, 'zhijie' is upright and of principle, and 'kedao' are the supervising secretaries of the six departments and the censors of the circuits. 'Zhengyin' is the principal official who holds the seal, and 'zuoyouzhizhi' is a post of assistance. 'Chongxingzhichen' is a close minister favoured by the sovereign. The 'zhe' of 'fu bian zhe fa' is presumably a slip for 'zhi', and by the sense it is translated 'the method of hidden and changed lines'. 'Dai you geng ming' is that official titles have changed from age to age, and seeking by the image is not affected by the change of names. The 'Month' of 'Pressure sits on the Day or Month branch' in the note is the Month branch of the current month (see the glossary).
