# 投充兵卒：官鬼若还冲克世不如不去反为良；军旅之忌惟以官鬼，若来冲世不过被彼克伐未决输赢，若来克世大有刑伤乃未沾利禄先遭戕戮，不如安分且守故乡

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼如果冲克世爻，不如不去，反而是好。军旅的忌讳，只在官鬼：它来冲世，不过是被对方克伐，还没有决出输赢；它如果来克世，大有刑伤，就是还没有沾到利禄，就先遭杀戮；不如安守本分，暂且守着故乡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure clashes with or controls the Self, it is better not to go, which is on the contrary good. The taboo of the army is nothing but Pressure: if it comes to clash with the Self, one is merely attacked by the other side and victory or defeat is not yet decided; if it comes to control the Self, there is much punishment and injury, and before one has shared in any pay or rank one is struck down; it is better to keep to one's lot and hold one's native place for now.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 诀里「曾如不去反为良」的「曾如」按文意是「不如」，译作「不如不去」。「选遭戕戮」的「选」疑是「先」，译作「先遭杀戮」。这一条与前面一条（EL.125）取同一个关系：官鬼是随军出战的大忌。
>
> *Note:* The 'zeng ru' of 'zeng ru bu qu fan wei liang' in the verse is by the sense 'bu ru', better not, and it is translated 'it is better not to go'. The 'xuan' of 'xuan zao qianglu' is presumably 'xian', first, and it is translated 'before one has shared in any pay or rank one is struck down'. This entry takes the same relation as the previous one (EL.125): Pressure is the great taboo of going out to battle with the army.
