# 卜筮元龟·占官高卑及何方赴任：卦看生旺定高低，旺相名高官贵、休囚位劣徒劳；一二三世咫尺、四五六世千里；官鬼在乾则西北；注：鬼爻逢长生帝旺官职高、逢衰病死墓绝官职卑

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道任所的好坏和官职的高低，看卦的生旺来定分寸：旺相，名声高、官尊贵；休囚，位置低劣，更是白劳。一、二、三世卦，说是咫尺之近；四、五、六世卦，是千里之外的驿程；若问注定在哪里做官，官鬼爻落在乾，就是去西北。注文说：鬼爻逢长生、帝旺，官职高；逢衰、病、死、墓、绝，官职卑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know whether the post is good or bad and whether it is high or low, look at the strength of the hexagram to settle the degree: strong, the name is high and the office honoured; at rest or trapped, the position is poor and effort is all the more in vain. The First, Second and Third Generations are said to be a hand-span away, the Fourth, Fifth and Sixth a post-road of a thousand li; if one asks where one is destined to hold office, with the Pressure line in Qian one goes to the northwest. The note says: if the Pressure line meets the Birth or Peak stage the post is high, and if it meets Decline, Sickness, Death, Tomb or Extinction the post is low.
