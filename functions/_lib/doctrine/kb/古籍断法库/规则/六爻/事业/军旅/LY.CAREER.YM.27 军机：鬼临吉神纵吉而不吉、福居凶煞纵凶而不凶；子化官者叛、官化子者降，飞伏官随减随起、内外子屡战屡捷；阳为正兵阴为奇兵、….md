# 军机：鬼临吉神纵吉而不吉、福居凶煞纵凶而不凶；子化官者叛、官化子者降，飞伏官随减随起、内外子屡战屡捷；阳为正兵阴为奇兵、以子孙官鬼别其利；合则结冲则绝、静应守动应攻

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 所以鬼（官鬼）临在吉神上，即使是吉也不吉；福（子孙）落在凶煞上，即使是凶也不凶。还有：子孙化官鬼的，是叛变；官鬼化子孙的，是投降；官鬼伏在飞爻下的，随灭随起；内外卦都有子孙的，屡战屡捷。阳是正兵，阴是奇兵，用子孙、官鬼来分辨它们的利弊。合就是结，冲就是绝；静就该守，动就该攻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* So when Pressure stands on an auspicious spirit it is not auspicious even though it seems so, and when Output sits on an evil star it is not ominous even though it seems so. Moreover, Output turning into Pressure is mutiny, and Pressure turning into Output is surrender; Pressure hidden beneath a flying line wanes as it rises and rises as it wanes, and Output in both inner and outer trigram means repeated battles and repeated victories. Yang is the regular troops and yin the irregular troops, and Output and Pressure tell where the advantage lies. Combining is binding and clashing is severing; when still, one should hold, and when moving, one should attack.

> [!note] 按
> 「子化官者叛，官化子者降」：子孙是我的兵，变成官鬼，是兵变成了敌，所以叛；官鬼是敌，变成子孙，是敌变成了兵，所以降。「飞伏官」是官鬼伏在飞爻下面；「随减随起」是刚消下去又起来，反复。「正兵」是正面交战的兵，「奇兵」是出奇制胜的兵。「静应守，动应攻」的「应」在这里按「该」读（静就该守、动就该攻）；若把它读成应爻（应静则守、应动则攻：对方静就是守、动就是攻），也讲得通，书里没有注明。「合则结」指合住、结盟，「冲则绝」指冲开、断绝。
>
> *Note:* 'Output turning into Pressure is mutiny, Pressure turning into Output is surrender': Output is my troops, and turning into Pressure it is troops turned enemy, hence mutiny; Pressure is the enemy, and turning into Output it is the enemy turned troops, hence surrender. 'Hidden Pressure beneath a flying line' is Pressure hidden under a flying line; 'wanes as it rises' is rising just after dying down, back and forth. 'Zhengbing' is troops that fight face to face, and 'qibing' is troops that win by surprise. The 'ying' of 'still should hold, moving should attack' is read here as 'should'; read as the Other line (when the Other is still it holds, when it moves it attacks, speaking of the enemy) it also makes sense, and the book does not say which. 'Combining is binding' is being held fast or allied, and 'clashing is severing' is being clashed apart or cut off.
