# 在任吉凶·官合龙兴，恩命至（青龙官星持世，或世临日月，或日月动爻生合世，或世临岁五或岁五生合世，或世官三合，皆吉；大位皇恩特用，平位加级超升）

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官星相合、青龙兴旺，恩命就到了：青龙官星持世，或世爻临日月，或日月、动爻生合世爻，或世爻临太岁、五爻，或太岁、五爻生合世爻，或世爻与官星三合，都是吉兆；居高位的蒙皇上特别重用，职位一般的加级越级升迁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the star of office combines and the Dragon flourishes, a gracious order arrives: the star of office on the Azure Dragon holding the Self, or the Self on the Day or Month, or the Day, Month or a moving line generating or combining with the Self, or the Self on Tai Sui or the fifth line, or Tai Sui or the fifth line generating or combining with the Self, or the Self and the star of office in three-harmony, are all auspicious signs; for one in high office the emperor's favour brings a special employment, and for one in an ordinary post a rise in grade and promotion beyond rank.
