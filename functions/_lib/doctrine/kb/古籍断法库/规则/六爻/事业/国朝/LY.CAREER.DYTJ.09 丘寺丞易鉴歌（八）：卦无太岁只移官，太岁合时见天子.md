# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（八）：卦无太岁只移官，太岁合时见天子

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有太岁，只是移官调动；太岁相合的时候，能见天子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With no Tai Sui in the hexagram, only a transfer of office; when Tai Sui combines, one sees the Son of Heaven.

## 《断易天机·占仕宦第十一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 2585 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 11: Divining official careers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦无太岁只移官， | 卦里没有太岁，只是移官调动； | with no Tai Sui in the hexagram, only a transfer of office; |
> | 太岁合时见天子。 | 太岁相合的时候，能见天子。 | when Tai Sui combines, one sees the Son of Heaven. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全本作「卦无太岁只升迁」，而它自己的旧注却说「若无，但能迁改」；「迁改」和断易天机本的「只移官」相合，所以「升迁」很可能是「移官」的另一种写法或误写。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan text reads 'with no Tai Sui in the hexagram, only promotion', yet its own note says 'if there is none, one can only be moved or changed'; 'moved or changed' agrees with the 'only a transfer of office' of the Duanyi Tianji text, so 'promotion' is very likely another way of writing or a slip for 'transfer'.
