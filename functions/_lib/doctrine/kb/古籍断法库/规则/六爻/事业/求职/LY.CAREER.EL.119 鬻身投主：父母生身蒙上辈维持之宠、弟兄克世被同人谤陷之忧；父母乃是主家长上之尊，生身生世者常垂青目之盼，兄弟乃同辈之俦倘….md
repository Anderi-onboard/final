# 鬻身投主：父母生身蒙上辈维持之宠、弟兄克世被同人谤陷之忧；父母乃是主家长上之尊，生身生世者常垂青目之盼，兄弟乃同辈之俦倘来克世则彼肆嫉妨之谋

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻生身，蒙受上辈的维持和宠爱；兄弟克世，有被同辈诽谤陷害的忧虑。父母爻是主人家里的长辈尊者，如果生身、生世，常常被青眼相看；兄弟是同辈的伙伴，如果来克世，他们就放肆地谋划嫉妒、妨碍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Resource generates the Body, one receives the favour of support from one's elders; if Peer controls the Self, one has the worry of being slandered and trapped by one's fellows. Resource is the honoured elder of the master's household, and if it generates the Body or the Self, one is constantly looked on with a favourable eye; Peer are fellows of one's own rank, and if it comes to control the Self, they give free rein to jealous plots to hinder one.

> [!note] 按
> 「上辈」「长上」是主人家里的长辈。「同人」「同辈之俦」是同为仆役的人。「青目」是青眼，表示好感。「谤陷」是诽谤陷害。
>
> *Note:* 'Shangbei' and 'zhangshang' are the elders of the master's household. 'Tongren' and 'tongbei zhi chou' are those who are servants like oneself. 'Qingmu' is the green eye, a look of good will. 'Bangxian' is slandering and entrapping.
