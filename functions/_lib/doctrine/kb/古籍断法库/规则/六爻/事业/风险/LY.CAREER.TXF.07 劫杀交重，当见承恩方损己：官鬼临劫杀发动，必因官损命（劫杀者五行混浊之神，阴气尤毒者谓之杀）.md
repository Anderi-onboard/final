# 劫杀交重，当见承恩方损己：官鬼临劫杀发动，必因官损命（劫杀者五行混浊之神，阴气尤毒者谓之杀）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 劫杀交重发动，正当受恩的时候反而损伤自己；官鬼落在劫杀上发动，必定因为做官而损命。劫杀见后面神杀章；它是五行混浊的神，阴气特别毒的叫做杀，遇到都不是吉兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Robbery Killing moving heavily, just when one is to receive favour one is harmed; if Pressure stands on it and moves, one is sure to lose one's life through office. The Robbery Killing is dealt with in the later chapter on baleful spirits; it is a spirit of the five phases muddied and turbid, yin qi that is especially poisonous being called a 'killing', and meeting it is never an auspicious sign.

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全本作「损已」，应是「损己」之误（断易天机本作「损己」），这里按「损己」译。后三句是断易天机本多出来的注。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan text writes 'harm oneself' with a wrong character (the one for 'already' in place of the one for 'oneself'); the Duanyi Tianji text has it right, and it is translated as 'harm oneself'. The last three sentences are an extra note found only in the Duanyi Tianji text.
