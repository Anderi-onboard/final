# 征战·子化官爻克世，张飞遭范张之诛（旧注：子孙化官生合应而克害身世，是我兵卒谋杀主帅而欲降敌之象，如后汉张翼德素不恤士卒，被小卒范强、张达枭首帐下，顺流投孙权）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙变出官鬼，去生合应爻、克害世爻或世身，是我方的士兵谋杀主帅、想要投降敌人的象，就像后汉的张翼德，平时不体恤士兵，有一天被小兵范强、张达在帐下砍了头，顺流而下投奔孙权。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output turns into Pressure that generates or combines with the Other and controls and harms the Self or Body, it is the image of our soldiers murdering their commander in order to surrender to the enemy, like Zhang Yide of the Later Han, who never cared for his soldiers and one day had his head cut off in his tent by the common soldiers Fan Qiang and Zhang Da, who went down the river to Sun Quan.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1559 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙化官鬼生合应爻，反来克害身世者，是我兵卒杀主降敌，如后汉张翼德被部卒范疆、张达之刺帐下，因之而投孙权也。 | 子孙变出官鬼、去生合应爻，反过来克害世爻或世身的，是我方的士兵杀主帅投降敌人，就像后汉的张翼德被部下范疆、张达在帐下刺杀，他们因此投奔孙权。 | If Output turns into Pressure that generates or combines with the Other and in turn controls and harms the Self or Body, it is our soldiers killing their commander to surrender to the enemy, like Zhang Yide of the Later Han, stabbed in his tent by his men Fan Jiang and Zhang Da, who therefore went over to Sun Quan. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 范强、范疆是同一个人的两种写法：旧注作「范强」，卜筮正宗作「范疆」。
>
> *Note:* 'Fan Qiang' and 'Fan Jiang' are two ways of writing the same man's name: the old commentary has the first and the Bu Shi Zheng Zong the second.
