# 平生学艺总纲：以妻财为主、世应为凭；业无大小贤愚岂不从师，事有败成去就还须问卜；凡习三教之流师同伯叔，椿萱旺相法必训详，逢旺相或来合世生身者必获全传；习诸般技艺先观兄弟次看应爻，此二象若一空亡便无承受

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 学艺（学一门手艺、行业），以妻财为主，以世爻、应爻为凭据。行业没有大小、贤愚之分，哪能不拜师；事情有败有成、有去有留，还须占卜。凡是学三教一类的，师父如同伯叔：如果椿萱（父母爻）旺相，教法必定讲解详细；如果逢旺相，或者来合世、生身，必定得到全传。学各种技艺，先看兄弟爻，再看应爻，这两个象如果有一个空亡，就没有所承受。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In learning a craft or art, take Wealth as the chief and the Self and the Other as the ground. No trade is great or small, wise or foolish, yet how can one not follow a teacher; matters have failure and success, leaving and staying, and one must still consult the oracle. In learning among the Three Teachings, the teacher is like an uncle: if Resource, the parents, is thriving, the method will surely be taught in detail; if it meets thriving, or comes to combine with the Self or generate the Body, one will surely receive the whole transmission. In learning the various crafts, look first at Peer and then at the Other line; if either of these two images is Void, there is nothing to be received.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 凡习三教之流师同伯叔，如得椿萱旺相，法必训详父田，如逢旺相或来合世生身者，必获全传。 | 凡是学三教一类的，师父如同伯叔；如果椿萱（父母爻）旺相，教法必定讲解详细；如果逢旺相，或者来合世、生身，必定得到全传。 | In learning among the Three Teachings the teacher is like an uncle; if Resource, the parents, is thriving, the method will surely be taught in detail; if it meets thriving, or comes to combine with the Self or generate the Body, one will surely receive the whole transmission. |
> | 或习诸般技艺，先观兄弟次看应爻，此二象若一空亡便无承受。 | 或者学各种技艺，先看兄弟爻，再看应爻，这两个象如果有一个空亡，就没有所承受。 | In learning the various crafts, look first at Peer and then at the Other line; if either of these two images is Void, there is nothing to be received. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「三教之流」是儒、释、道三教的学问和行当。「椿萱」是父母。「法必训详父田」文字有误，「父田」不通，这里按「法必训详」译，不译「父田」。「全传」是完整的传授。这一章问的是终身学一门手艺、行业（「平生学艺」），所以要拜师，取妻财为主；后面的五经定肄、求师训迪讲的是读书应考，是另一件事。
>
> *Note:* 'Sanjiao zhi liu' is the learning and callings of the three teachings of Confucians, Buddhists and Daoists. 'Chunxuan' is the parents. 'Fa bi xunxiang futian' is corrupt in its characters and 'futian' makes no sense, so it is translated as 'the method will surely be taught in detail' and 'futian' is left untranslated. 'Quanchuan' is the complete transmission. This chapter asks about learning a craft or trade for one's whole life, so one must follow a teacher and Wealth is taken as the chief; the following chapters on settling on one of the five Classics and on seeking a teacher to instruct are about studying for examinations, which is another matter.
