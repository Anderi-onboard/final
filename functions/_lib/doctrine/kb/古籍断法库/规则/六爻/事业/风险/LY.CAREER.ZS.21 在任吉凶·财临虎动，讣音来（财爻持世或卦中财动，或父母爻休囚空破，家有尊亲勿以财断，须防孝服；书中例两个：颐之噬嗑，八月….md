# 在任吉凶·财临虎动，讣音来（财爻持世或卦中财动，或父母爻休囚空破，家有尊亲勿以财断，须防孝服；书中例两个：颐之噬嗑，八月丁艰；中孚之损，六月火灾、闻讣）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻持世，或者卦中财爻发动，或者父母爻休囚、落空、被破的，如果家里有尊长亲人，不要只当财来断，要防备孝服（父母之丧）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Wealth line holds the Self or moves in the hexagram, or the Resource line is resting or trapped, Void or broken, then if one has elders and parents, do not judge it as wealth alone, but guard against mourning dress (the death of a parent).

> | 财临虎动，讣音来。 | 财爻临白虎而发动，会有讣告传来。 | When Wealth sits on the White Tiger and moves, news of a death arrives. |
> | 财爻持世及卦中财动，或父母爻休囚空破者，若有尊亲，切勿以财断之，须防孝服。 | 财爻持世以及卦中财动，或者父母爻休囚、落空、被破的，如果有尊长亲人，千万不要用财来断，要防备孝服。 | If the Wealth line holds the Self or moves in the hexagram, or the Resource line is resting or trapped, Void or broken, then if one has elders and parents, do not judge it as wealth, but guard against mourning dress. |

> | 余曰：世爻戌土，虽则休囚，得午火生之，自身无碍，须防孝服。 | 我说：世爻是戌土，虽然休囚，但有午火生它，你自己没有妨碍，要防备孝服。 | I said: the Self is Xu Earth, which though resting or trapped is generated by Wu Fire, so you yourself are not harmed, but guard against mourning dress. |
> | 彼曰：财非临虎，如何主孝服？ | 他说：财爻没有临白虎，怎么会主孝服？ | He said: Wealth does not sit on the White Tiger, so how can it mean mourning dress? |

> | 果至八月丁艰。 | 结果到八月遭了父母之丧（丁艰）。 | And in the eighth month he did suffer the death of a parent. |

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10604 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼克世，幸而不动，在任可保无凶。 | 官鬼克世爻，幸好不发动，在任上可以保证没有凶险。 | Pressure controls the Self but fortunately does not move, so one is safe from ill in the post. |
> | 但不宜兄爻持世，丑日冲动，今冬身必动摇，身动则耗费不小。 | 但是兄爻持世不好，丑日冲动了它，今年冬天自身必定动摇，身动的话耗费不小。 | But a Peer line holding the Self is unfavourable, and the Chou day clashes it into motion, so this winter one is sure to be unsettled, and being unsettled costs a good deal. |
> | 彼问：因何事而动？ | 他问：因为什么事而动？ | He asked: what will cause the move? |
> | 余曰：火被水伤，冬令须防孝服，恐因此而动也。 | 我说：火被水伤，冬天要防备孝服，恐怕是因为这个而动。 | I said: Fire is injured by Water, so in winter guard against mourning dress; it is probably for this that you will be unsettled. |
> | 不意六月公署遭回禄，科月闻讣音。 | 没想到六月官署遭了火灾，考试的月份听到了讣告。 | Unexpectedly in the sixth month the government office suffered a fire, and in the month of the examinations news of a death came. |
> | 回禄者，兄爻持世暗动破财； | 火灾，是因为兄爻持世暗动破财； | The fire was because the Peer line held the Self and moved in secret, wasting money; |
> | 伤父者，巳火父化子水克也。 | 伤到父亲，是因为巳火父母爻变出子水来克。 | the harm to the father was because Si Fire the Resource line turned into Zi Water, which controls it. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 「丁艰」是遭父母之丧；「回禄」是火神，指火灾。「科月」书里没有解释，译文按字面读作考试的月份。
>
> *Note:* 'Dingjian' is the mourning for a parent's death, and 'Huilu' is the fire god, here meaning a fire. The book does not explain 'the month of the examinations', and the translation reads it literally.
