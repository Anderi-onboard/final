# 朝廷占·大象（一）：世爻所居之卦为国——旺相国治天下平，胎没次之，死囚休废国祚陵夷；空亡巡游无度；冲破疆围不固；刑克奸宄窃发；受害臣庶离心；岁生旺宫国运炎隆，岁克衰卦宗社将倾

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大象，国运的总势：世爻所在的那个单卦就是国家。它旺相，国家治理得好，天下太平；胎、没次一等；死、囚、休、废，国运就衰落下去。它落空亡，君主巡游没有节制；被冲破，疆界不稳固；被刑克，奸邪作乱的人暗中起事；受害，臣子百姓离心。太岁生旺这一宫，国运炽盛；太岁克制衰弱的卦，国家的宗庙社稷将要倾覆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The great image, the general trend of the state's fortune. The trigram in which the Self line sits is the state. If it is thriving, the state is well governed and the world at peace; the stages Tai and Mo come next; if it is dead, imprisoned, resting or abandoned, the dynasty declines. If it falls into Void, the sovereign tours without restraint; if it is clashed or broken, the borders are not secure; if it is punished or controlled, treacherous rebels rise in secret; if it is harmed, ministers and commoners lose heart. If Tai Sui generates a thriving palace, the state's fortune is blazing; if Tai Sui controls a weak trigram, the ancestral temple and altars of the state are about to fall.

> [!note] 按
> 「旺相胎没死囚休废」是单卦随季节的八种状态，书里没有逐一解释（征战占「方向」把八个卦在八个节气里的状态逐项列了出来，见 YY.51），译文把前四个当作由好到差的顺序，后四个当作衰败。第八句「岁生旺官」的「官」，和后面「旺宫」对照应是「宫」字抄错，译文按「宫」译；第九句「岁克衰卦」正是同一对仗。「宗社」是宗庙和社稷，代指国家。
>
> *Note:* The eight states, thriving, assisting, Tai, Mo, dead, imprisoned, resting and abandoned, are the states of a trigram through the seasons; the book does not explain each (the 'direction' section of the divination of war sets out the state of each of the eight trigrams in the eight solar terms, see YY.51), and the translation takes the first four as a descent from good to poor and the last four as decline. In the eighth sentence the character for 'official' in 'thriving official' is, compared with 'thriving palace' later on, presumably a miscopy for 'palace', and the translation follows 'palace'; the ninth sentence, 'Tai Sui controls a weak trigram', is the same pair. 'Zongshe' is the ancestral temple and the altars of soil and grain, standing for the state.
