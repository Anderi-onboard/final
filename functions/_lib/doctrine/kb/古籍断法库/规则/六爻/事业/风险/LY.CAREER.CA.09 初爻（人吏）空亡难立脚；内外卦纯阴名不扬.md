# 初爻（人吏）空亡难立脚；内外卦纯阴名不扬

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 初爻是人吏，落空亡，即使做官清正，吏员没力量，谁替他奉行，所以难以立足；内外卦全是阴，阴柔立不起来，怎么振作？所以名声不扬。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The first line is the clerk; if it falls Void, even an upright official has clerks without strength to carry out his orders, like a tree without branches, and it is hard to stand firm; if the inner and outer trigrams are both wholly yin, the soft cannot stand up and the name is not spread abroad.
