# 鬻身投主：多是多非盖为动爻临朱雀、常来常往皆因主象值游魂；朱雀主口舌若值动爻则终朝咭聒寝食不宁，游魂为游荡若卜此卦乃流荡忘返萍踪浪迹之徒；再推之卦之合冲方决始终之遐迩，之卦者变卦也细辨其中之冲合可断终身之吉凶

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 多是多非，是因为动爻临朱雀；常来常往，是因为主人的象值游魂。朱雀主口舌，如果值动爻，就整天被吵闹、睡吃不安；游魂是游荡，如果卜得这个卦，就是流荡忘返、萍踪浪迹的人。再推之卦（变卦）的合冲，才能决断这件事从头到尾是远是近；「之卦」就是变卦，细细辨别其中的冲合，可以断一生的吉凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Many quarrels and much wrangling are because a moving line sits on the Vermilion Bird; constant coming and going is because the master image falls on a Wandering Soul. The Vermilion Bird governs wrangling, and if it falls on a moving line one is clamoured at all day long and neither sleep nor food is in peace; the Wandering Soul is wandering, and if this hexagram is cast one is a drifter who wanders and forgets to return, like duckweed's trace and a wave's track. Then reckon the combination or clash in the changed hexagram, and only then decide how far or near it is from beginning to end; the 'zhi gua' is the changed hexagram, and by carefully distinguishing the clash and combination in it one can judge the good or ill of one's whole life.

> [!note] 按
> 「咭聒」是吵闹。「萍踪浪迹」是像浮萍、浪花一样没有定处。「之卦」是变卦（发动的爻变出来的卦）。「游魂」是八宫卦里每宫的第七卦（见术语表）。「主象值游魂」是应、父等代表主人的象落在游魂卦上，书里没有细说。「始终之遐迩」是从头到尾是远是近（时间的久暂）。这是鬻身投主一章的收尾。
>
> *Note:* 'Jigua' is clamour. 'Pingzong langji' is having no fixed place, like duckweed and the foam of the waves. 'Zhigua' is the changed hexagram (the hexagram that the moving lines turn into). 'Wandering Soul' is the seventh hexagram of each of the eight palaces (see the glossary). 'The master image falls on a Wandering Soul' is the images that stand for the master, such as the Other and Resource, falling on a Wandering Soul hexagram, and the book does not go into it. 'Shizhong zhi xia'er' is how far or near from beginning to end, meaning how long or short in time. This closes the chapter on selling oneself into service.
