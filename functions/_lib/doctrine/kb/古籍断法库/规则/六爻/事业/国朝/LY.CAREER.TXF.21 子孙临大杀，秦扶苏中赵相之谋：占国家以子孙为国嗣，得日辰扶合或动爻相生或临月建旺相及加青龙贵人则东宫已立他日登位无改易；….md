# 子孙临大杀，秦扶苏中赵相之谋：占国家以子孙为国嗣，得日辰扶合或动爻相生或临月建旺相及加青龙贵人则东宫已立他日登位无改易；子孙带咸池非正宫所生；福德临劫杀大杀恐有变易；更被四爻伤克必有赵高谋扶苏之事；卦无子孙无国嗣

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占国家，以子孙为国家的继承人。子孙得日辰扶助相合，或动爻来生它，或落在月建上旺相，以及加青龙、贵人，如果东宫（太子）已立，将来要登宝位，就不会再有更改；子孙带咸池，不是正宫所生；福德落在劫杀、大杀上，恐怕有变更，再被四爻伤克，必有赵高谋害扶苏那样的事；卦里没有子孙，就没有国家的继承人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for the state, Output stands for the heir to the state. If Output gets support and combination from the Day, or a moving line generates it, or it stands on the Month and is strong, or the Azure Dragon or the Noble is added, then if the Eastern Palace (the crown prince) has already been established, when he is due to ascend the throne there will be no further change; if Output carries Xianchi, he is not born of the principal consort; if Output stands on the Robbery Killing or the Great Baleful Spirit there may be a change, and if it is further injured and controlled by the fourth line there will surely be a matter like Zhao Gao's plot against Fusu; if there is no Output in the hexagram, there is no heir to the state.
