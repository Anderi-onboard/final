# 六亲爻动·求官谒贵：子动谒贵求官休进用（主用被伤）、守旧当占可自然；妻财动克文书应举求官总是虚；官鬼动更改动谋皆不行

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动，谒见贵人、求官不要进取（主用爻被伤），守旧倒还可以顺其自然；妻财发动克了文书，应举、求官总是落空（文书受伤）；官鬼发动，更改、动谋都不行（鬼动，所以受阻隔）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Output moving, do not press forward in calling on a noble or seeking office (the Subject line is injured), and holding to the old can be left to take its course; with Wealth moving and controlling the documents, taking the examination or seeking office is all empty (the documents are injured); with Pressure moving, neither changes nor schemes will do (Pressure moving brings obstruction).

> [!note] 按
> 「守旧当占可自然」意思不清，这里按「守着旧的、就此占问，可顺其自然」译，不确定。这一段是各问题通用的歌，每句括号里的小注是原书的。
>
> *Note:* 'Hold to the old, in such a divination it is natural' is unclear in sense and is translated as 'holding to the old, in such a divination it can be left to take its course', which is uncertain. This is a song common to all kinds of questions, and the small notes in brackets after each line are the book's own.
