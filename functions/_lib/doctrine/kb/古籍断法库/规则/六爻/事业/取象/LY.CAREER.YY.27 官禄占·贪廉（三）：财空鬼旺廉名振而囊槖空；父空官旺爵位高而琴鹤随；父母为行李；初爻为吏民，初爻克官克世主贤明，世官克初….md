# 官禄占·贪廉（三）：财空鬼旺廉名振而囊槖空；父空官旺爵位高而琴鹤随；父母为行李；初爻为吏民，初爻克官克世主贤明，世官克初主苛刻，生初主慈爱

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 清名与属吏：财爻落空、官鬼旺相的，清廉的名声很响，而口袋是空的；父爻落空、官星旺相的，爵位高，身边只有琴和鹤相随；父母爻是行李。初爻代表属吏和百姓：初爻克官星、克世爻的，主这个官贤明，属吏百姓亲近他；世爻、官爻克初爻的，主这个官苛刻，属吏百姓畏惧躲避他；生初爻的，主这个官慈爱属吏百姓。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A clean name, and the clerks and people. If Wealth is Void and Pressure thriving, his name for incorruptibility rings out while his purse is empty; if the seal line is Void and the star of office thriving, his rank is high while only the zither and the crane follow him; the Resource line is the baggage. The first line stands for clerks and people: if the first line controls the star of office or the Self, he is wise and enlightened and the clerks and people are close to him; if the Self line or the Pressure line controls the first line, he is harsh and the clerks and people fear and avoid him; if it generates the first line, he is kind and loving towards them.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「琴鹤」用的是宋代赵抃只带一张琴、一只鹤去赴任的典故，比喻做官清廉；「囊槖」是口袋。「父空官旺」的父单字，按上面的用法译成印星；括号里的「父母为行李」是说父母爻代表行李（见 YY.13 赴任的分爻）。最后一句的主语省了，承上一句，是世爻、官爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Qin he' is the allusion to Zhao Bian of the Song, who went to his post taking only one zither and one crane, an image of an incorruptible official; 'nangtuo' is a bag. The single character 'father' in 'father Void, official thriving' is rendered as the seal line as above; the bracket 'the Resource line is baggage' says that the Resource line stands for baggage (see the division of lines for going to the post in YY.13). The subject of the last sentence is left out, following on from the previous one, and is the Self line and the Pressure line.
