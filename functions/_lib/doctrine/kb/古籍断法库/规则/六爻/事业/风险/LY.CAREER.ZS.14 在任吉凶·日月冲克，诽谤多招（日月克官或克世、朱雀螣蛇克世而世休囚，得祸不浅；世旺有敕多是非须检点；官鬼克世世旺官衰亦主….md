# 在任吉凶·日月冲克，诽谤多招（日月克官或克世、朱雀螣蛇克世而世休囚，得祸不浅；世旺有敕多是非须检点；官鬼克世世旺官衰亦主诽谤；世衰鬼旺临蛇雀必挂弹章）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日月来冲克，在任上多招诽谤：日月克官星或克世爻，或者朱雀、螣蛇克世而世爻休囚，招来的祸不小；世爻旺相、有敕命的，也多招是非，要检点、勤勉谨慎处理政事，以免过失；官鬼克世爻，但世旺官衰的，也主有诽谤；世爻衰弱、官鬼旺相，如果又临螣蛇、朱雀，必定被弹劾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Day and Month clash with or control, slander is much invited in office: if the Day and Month control the star of office or the Self, or the Vermilion Bird or the Coiling Snake controls the Self while the Self is resting or trapped, the misfortune is not slight; if the Self is thriving and one holds an imperial edict there is still much gossip and trouble, so one should be careful and diligent and prudent in affairs of government to avoid fault; if Pressure controls the Self though the Self is thriving and Pressure is weak, there is slander too; if the Self is weak and Pressure thriving and it also sits on the Coiling Snake or the Vermilion Bird, one will surely be named in an impeachment.
