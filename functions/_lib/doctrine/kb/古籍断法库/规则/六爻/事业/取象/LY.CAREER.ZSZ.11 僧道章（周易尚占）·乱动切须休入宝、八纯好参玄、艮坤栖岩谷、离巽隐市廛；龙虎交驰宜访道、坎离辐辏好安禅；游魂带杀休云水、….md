# 僧道章（周易尚占）·乱动切须休入宝、八纯好参玄、艮坤栖岩谷、离巽隐市廛；龙虎交驰宜访道、坎离辐辏好安禅；游魂带杀休云水、一世休囚莫置田

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 爻乱动，务必不要入宝；八纯卦倒好去参玄；艮、坤只好栖身岩穴山谷；离、巽偏适合隐居市井；龙虎交驰，适合访道；坎、离汇聚，适合安心坐禅；游魂卦带杀，别去云游；一世卦休囚，不要置买田产。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With lines moving in disorder, by all means do not enter the 'treasure'; an Eight-Pure hexagram is on the contrary good for studying the mysteries; Gen and Kun are fit only for dwelling among crags and valleys; Li and Xun are better for hiding in the marketplace; the Dragon and Tiger racing against each other suit seeking the Way; Kan and Li converging suit settling into meditation; a Wandering Soul hexagram with a baleful spirit, do not go wandering like cloud and water; a First-Generation hexagram resting or trapped, do not buy fields.

> [!note] 按
> 「乱动切须休入宝」的「入宝」不可解，卜筮大全作「乱动坐关为大忌」，「入宝」疑是「入定」一类的误字，这里照字面译，不确定。「栖岩谷」卜筮大全作「栖岩壑」。
>
> *Note:* The 'enter the treasure' of 'with lines moving in disorder do not enter the treasure' cannot be made out; the Bushi Daquan reads 'with lines moving in disorder, going into retreat is the great taboo', and 'enter the treasure' looks like a wrong character for something like entering meditation, but it is translated literally and this is uncertain. 'Dwell among crags and valleys' reads in the Bushi Daquan 'among crags and ravines'.
