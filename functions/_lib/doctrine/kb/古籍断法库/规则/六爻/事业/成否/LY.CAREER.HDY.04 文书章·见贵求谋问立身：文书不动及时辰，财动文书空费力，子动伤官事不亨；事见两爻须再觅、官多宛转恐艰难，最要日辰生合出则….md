# 文书章·见贵求谋问立身：文书不动及时辰，财动文书空费力，子动伤官事不亨；事见两爻须再觅、官多宛转恐艰难，最要日辰生合出则知音处处得前程

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 拜见贵人、求谋、问立身：文书（父母）不动、又赶上时辰，可成；财动，文书白费力；子孙动，伤了官鬼，事情不亨通。事情现出两个爻，要再去找；官多，辗转曲折，恐怕艰难；最要紧的是日辰生合它、让它出来，那就处处遇到知音、得到前程。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For calling on a noble, scheming for a post and asking about establishing oneself: with the documents (Resource) still and in step with the time it can be done; Wealth moving, the documents are spent in vain; Output moving injures Pressure and the matter does not go through. If the matter shows two lines, seek again; with many Pressure lines it winds and turns and may be hard; what matters most is that the Day generates or combines and brings it out, and then one finds those who know one everywhere and a future.
