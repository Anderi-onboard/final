# 金为武职之官掌生杀之重柄、火乃文章之士探礼义之根源：金有断制之义故取为武职，火乃文明之象必职司儒业

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金是武职的官，掌握生杀的大权；火是文章之士，探求礼义的根源。金有决断制裁的意思，所以取为武职；火是文明的象，必定职掌儒业。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Metal is the military officer, holding the heavy handle of life and death; Fire is the man of letters, who probes the source of ritual and righteousness. Metal has the sense of judging and restraining, so it is taken for military office; Fire is the image of civilization and brightness, so the office is surely over the Confucian scholarly profession.
