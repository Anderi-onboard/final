# 官员荐奖：欲求坊扁方光辉必藉官爻之旺相，世空绝望、子动难谋、财动还须嘱托、鬼空不必祈求；凡求牌坊扁额光辉门闾先看官爻次推世象二者若有一空始终绝望，子孙动亦不能求，财动生官谋为遂意

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想求得牌坊、匾额来光耀门庭，必须凭官爻旺相。世爻落空就绝望，子孙发动就难谋求，财爻发动还要托人嘱托，官鬼落空就不必祈求了。凡是求牌坊、匾额来光耀门庭的，先看官爻，再推世爻；这两个只要有一个落空，始终没有指望；子孙发动，也求不到；财爻发动去生官爻，谋求就遂心如意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To seek an honorific archway or plaque that will bring glory to one's door, one must rely on the Pressure line being thriving. If the Self is Void there is no hope, if Output moves it is hard to arrange, if Wealth moves one must still entrust the matter to someone, and if Pressure is Void there is no need to pray for it. In every seeking of an archway or plaque to bring glory to one's gate, look first at the Pressure line and then reckon the Self image; if either of the two is Void, there is no hope to the end; if Output moves, it cannot be obtained either; if Wealth moves to generate the star of office, the scheming turns out as one wishes.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 827 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡求牌坊扁额光辉门闾，先看官爻，次推世象，二者若有一空，始终绝望。 | 凡是求牌坊、匾额来光耀门庭的，先看官爻，再推世爻；这两个如果有一个落空，始终没有指望。 | In every seeking of an archway or plaque to bring glory to one's gate, look first at the Pressure line and then reckon the Self image; if either of the two is Void, there is no hope to the end. |
> | 子孙动，亦不能求。 | 子孙发动，也求不到。 | If Output moves, it cannot be obtained either. |
> | 财动生官，谋为遂意。 | 财爻发动去生官爻，谋求就遂心如意。 | If Wealth moves to generate the star of office, the scheming turns out as one wishes. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「坊扁」是牌坊和匾额，「光辉门闾」是光耀门庭；官府给牌坊匾额是一种表彰，所以看官爻。「嘱托」是托人关照。诀里「财动还须嘱托」和解里「财动生官，谋为遂意」并不冲突：财动生官是成事的路子，但事要有人去办。
>
> *Note:* 'Fangbian' are the honorific archway and the plaque, and 'guanghui menlü' is bringing glory to one's gate; an archway or plaque given by the authorities is a kind of commendation, so the Pressure line is looked at. 'Zhutuo' is entrusting a matter to someone. The verse's 'if Wealth moves one must still entrust the matter to someone' and the explanation's 'if Wealth moves to generate the star of office, the scheming turns out as one wishes' do not clash: Wealth moving to generate the star of office is the road by which the matter is done, but someone must be sent to see to it.
