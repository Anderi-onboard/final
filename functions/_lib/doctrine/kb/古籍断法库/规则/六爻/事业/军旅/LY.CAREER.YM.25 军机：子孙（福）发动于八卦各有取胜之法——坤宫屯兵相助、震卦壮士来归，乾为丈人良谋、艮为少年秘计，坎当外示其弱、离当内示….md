# 军机：子孙（福）发动于八卦各有取胜之法——坤宫屯兵相助、震卦壮士来归，乾为丈人良谋、艮为少年秘计，坎当外示其弱、离当内示其虚、兑当饰以卑词、巽当勇于趋进，皆发子孙而取胜

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙动在坤宫，有屯驻的兵来相助；福神兴在震卦，有壮士来归；乾是老成人的良谋，艮是少年的秘计；坎宫应当对外示弱，离宫应当对内示虚，兑宫应当用卑下的言辞来修饰，巽宫应当勇于进取。这些都是子孙发动才能取胜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving in the palace of Kun, stationed troops come to help; Output rising in the trigram Zhen, brave men come over to me; Qian is the good plan of an elder, Gen the secret scheme of a youth; in Kan one should show weakness outwardly, in Li one should show emptiness inwardly, in Dui one should dress up one's words humbly, in Xun one should be bold in pressing forward. All of these win when Output moves.

> [!note] 按
> 「丈人」是老成的长者（乾为老父）。「餙」是「饰」的异体，「餙以卑词」是用谦卑的言辞包装、掩饰自己。「示其弱」「示其虚」是对敌人装作弱、装作虚，引他来。坤宫、震卦、乾、艮、坎、离、兑、巽各给一个方略，是本章把八卦象义用在『我这一边』的一条，和上面讲敌人的（YM.22–23）对着读。书里自注：兵占里福神（子孙）临到都吉，属什么五行、落在什么宫，各按象去推。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhangren' is a seasoned elder (Qian is the aged father). 'Shi' is a variant of 'shi', to dress up, and 'dress up with humble words' is to wrap and disguise oneself in humble speech. 'Showing weakness' and 'showing emptiness' are pretending to the enemy to be weak or empty so as to draw him on. Each of Kun, Zhen, Qian, Gen, Kan, Li, Dui and Xun gets a plan; this is the entry in the chapter that uses the images of the eight trigrams on 'my side', to be read against the entries above on the enemy (YM.22–23). The book's own gloss: in a military divination Output arriving is always auspicious, and which phase it belongs to and which palace it stands in is worked out by the image.
