# 父加虎杀外兴，莫纵军兵来出阵：子孙为我兵卒、官鬼为彼贼徒，子衰兵卒怯鬼旺贼军强，鬼空他必灭子旺我必昌；怕父母带白虎外动必克子孙宜守不宜战；父母持世动主将不能养士卒上下相离，带凶神须防自变；亡神杀正月从亥起顺行

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母加白虎、杀，在外卦兴起，不要放纵军兵出阵。子孙是我方兵卒，官鬼是对方贼徒：子衰兵卒怯懦，鬼旺贼军强大；鬼空他必覆灭，子旺我必昌盛。怕的是父母带白虎在外卦动，必克子孙，宜守不宜战，战必伤军卒。父母若持世发动，主将不能抚养士卒，上下离散，心里没有相安之道；若带凶神，要防内部生变。亡神杀，正月从亥起顺着排；劫杀见后面。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource carrying the Tiger and a baleful spirit and rising in the outer trigram: do not let the troops go out to the battle line. Output is our soldiers and Pressure is the enemy's bandits: with Output weak the soldiers are timid, with Pressure strong the enemy army is mighty; with Pressure void they are sure to be destroyed, with Output strong we are sure to prosper. What is feared is Resource carrying the White Tiger and moving outside, which surely controls Output; it is right to defend and not to fight, for fighting surely wounds the troops. If Resource holds the Self and moves, the commanding general cannot nurture his troops, high and low drift apart and hearts have no way to rest easy together; if a baleful spirit is carried, guard against a change from within. The Lost Spirit baleful spirit: begin at Hai in the first month and run forward in order; the Robbery Killing is given later.
