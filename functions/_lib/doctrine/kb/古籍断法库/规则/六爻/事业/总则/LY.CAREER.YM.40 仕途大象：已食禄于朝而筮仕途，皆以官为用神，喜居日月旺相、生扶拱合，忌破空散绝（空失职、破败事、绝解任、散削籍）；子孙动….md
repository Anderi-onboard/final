# 仕途大象：已食禄于朝而筮仕途，皆以官为用神，喜居日月旺相、生扶拱合，忌破空散绝（空失职、破败事、绝解任、散削籍）；子孙动防公议、兄弟发减俸粮；官临长生帝旺临官则宦途远达升迁、衰墓死绝则官道否；进神升爵、退神降级；泄气动而元神静则升信杳然、元神发而用象摇则迁音乃至

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 已经在朝廷食禄的人占仕途，都以官为用神：喜它在日、月上，人们仰仗它的照临；喜它旺相，像万物沾到及时雨；喜它逢生、扶、拱、合，如同倚着长城奔驰在康庄大道上；忌它逢破、空、散、绝——空就失职，破就败事，绝就解任，散就削籍。子孙动，要防公议，按衰旺定轻重；兄弟发，要减俸粮，按喜忌说吉凶。官临长生，知道宦途远大通达；逢帝旺、临官，知道仕路有升迁；逢衰、墓、死、绝，知道官道不通；进神就升爵升阶，退神就降禄降级。变墓绝而动爻得生扶、被日月克而动爻变生，都是安稳之象。泄气的爻动而元神静，升迁的消息杳无；元神发动、用象摇动，调迁的音信就到了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Having already eaten the Son of Heaven's salary, in divining the official career one takes Pressure as the Subject throughout: it is good when it sits on the Day or the Month, for people look up to its shining down; good when it is thriving or strong, like things receiving the timely rain; good when it meets generation, support, enclosing and combination, like leaning on a great wall while racing along a broad highway; bad when it meets broken, Void, scattered or extinct: Void loses office, broken ruins affairs, extinct dissolves the post, scattered strikes the name from the rolls. Output moving, guard against public criticism, and tell heavy from light by decline and thriving; Peer issuing cuts the salary grain, and good or ill is told by what is welcome and what is feared. Pressure at Birth, one knows the official road will go far and clear; at Peak or at Taking Office, one knows there will be promotion; at decline, tomb, death or extinction, one knows the official way is blocked; Advancing means rank and grade go up, Retreating means salary and grade go down. Changing into tomb or extinction while the moving line is generated and supported, or being controlled by the Day and Month while the moving line changes into generation, are all signs of safety. If the line that drains it moves while its Support is still, news of promotion is faint; if the Support issues and the image of the Subject stirs, word of a transfer arrives.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「食禄于天家」是在朝廷领俸禄，也就是已经在官位上的人（和未仕求官的相对）。「日月」是日辰、月建。「照临」是日月照下来，这里指官爻受到日、月的临照。「拱」是两爻夹拱出中间一位。「康庄」是四通八达的大路。「削籍」是除名。「公议」是舆论和同僚的议论，「罚俸」是扣俸禄；「傣粮」疑是「俸粮」之误。「长生」「帝旺」「临官」是十二长生里的位置（临官是第四位，不是指官爻）；「进神」「退神」见术语表。「泄气动」按五行生克是泄官鬼之气的爻动：官鬼生父母，所以是父母爻（书里没有点明）；「元神」是生官鬼的妻财。注里「子乃官之忌神」是说子孙克官鬼，子旺官衰官就受祸；「官得三象贪生」按文意是：子孙动本来克官，但它动了要去生妻财、妻财又生官，子孙『贪生』而忘了克，官反得生，所以大吉；「三象」书里没有再注，这里按字面译。这一条与黄金策仕宦的总则（HJC.01、HJC.02）、易林补遗仕宦升迁（EL.55 起）取同一套：官鬼是官，妻财是俸，子孙伤官，兄弟减俸。
>
> *Note:* 'Shilu yu tianjia' is receiving salary from the imperial house, that is, someone already in office (as opposed to one not yet in office who seeks it). 'Day and Month' are the Day branch and the Month branch. 'Zhaolin' is the Day and Month shining down, here the Pressure line receiving the shining of the Day and Month. 'Gong' is two lines enclosing the one between them. 'Kangzhuang' is a broad road open in every direction. 'Xueji' is being struck from the register. 'Gongyi' is public opinion and the criticism of colleagues, and 'fafeng' is a fine of salary; 'tiliang' is presumably a slip for 'fengliang', salary grain. Birth, Peak and Taking Office are places in the twelve stages of life (Taking Office is the fourth, and does not mean the Pressure line); Advancing and Retreating are in the glossary. 'The line that drains it moves', by the generating and controlling of the phases, is the line that drains Pressure moving: Pressure generates Resource, so it is the Resource line (the book does not say so); the 'Support' is Wealth, which generates Pressure. The note's 'Output is the Adversary of Pressure' says that Output controls Pressure, and when Output is thriving and Pressure weak Pressure suffers harm; 'Pressure obtains the three images coveting generation' is, by the sense: Output moving would control Pressure, but it moves to generate Wealth and Wealth in turn generates Pressure, so Output 'covets generating' and forgets to control, Pressure is instead generated, and it is greatly auspicious; the book does not gloss the 'three images', and it is translated by the letter. This entry takes the same set as the general principles of the Huangjin Ce on official careers (HJC.01, HJC.02) and the Yilin Buyi on promotion (from EL.55): Pressure is the office, Wealth is the salary, Output injures the office, Peer cuts the salary.
