# 援监纳吏：吏典财官宜旺相、贡生父鬼忌空亡、太学父兴官旺处定显声名播四方；论恩贡选贡岁贡监生等类，虽用官爻再凭父母，此二象俱不可空，若得父官有气定然名播京都

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 吏典，财爻、官爻适宜旺相；贡生，父爻、官鬼最忌空亡；入太学，父爻兴旺、官爻旺相的地方，一定声名显扬、传遍四方。恩贡、选贡、岁贡、监生这一类，虽然用的是官爻，还要凭父母爻，这两个象都不可落空；如果父爻、官爻有气，一定名传京城。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a clerk of the establishment, Wealth and the star of office should be thriving; for a tribute student, Resource and Pressure must avoid Void; for the Imperial Academy, where Resource flourishes and the star of office is thriving, fame will surely be made manifest and spread in all quarters. For the grace tribute, selected tribute, annual tribute, Academy students and the like, though the Pressure line is the one used, one must rely also on Resource, and these two images must not be Void; if Resource and the star of office have qi, the name will surely be known throughout the capital.

> [!note] 按
> 「吏典」是衙门里的书吏；「贡生」是从地方学校选送到国子监的生员，有恩贡、选贡、岁贡等名目；「太学」「监生」是国子监的学生。所以援监就是捐钱取得监生的资格。这里三种身份分别取象：吏典取财、官，贡生取父、鬼，太学取父、官；解里把贡生、监生合起来说，只说官爻加父母。
>
> *Note:* 'Lidian' are the clerks of a yamen; 'gongsheng' are students sent up from the local schools to the Imperial Academy, under the names of grace tribute, selected tribute, annual tribute and so on; 'taixue' and 'jiansheng' are the students of the Imperial Academy. So 'yuanjian' is obtaining the standing of an Academy student by paying. The three kinds of standing here each take their own image: the clerk takes Wealth and the star of office, the tribute student Resource and Pressure, and the Academy student Resource and the star of office; the explanation speaks of the tribute students and Academy students together and says only the Pressure line with Resource.
