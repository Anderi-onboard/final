# 军机：问城攻守、兵行水陆、战利迟速、遣将用计皆以世应占之，朝廷拜将及择人用间乃三军大任必仁勇达变当以宾应辨；六占皆论胜负忌世坏及应克世、随官入墓助鬼伤身主覆军之败，用间类于嘱托应爻空破散绝则机谋泄漏、应爻克冲世位乃反奸之徒

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问攻守城池、兵走水路还是陆路、作战的利钝快慢，以及遣将用计，志在必胜，都以世应来占。如果朝廷拜将，以及选人用间，这是三军的大任，必须仁、勇、通达应变，应当用宾位的应来辨别。以上六种占问，都是论胜负，只忌世坏和应克世，而随官入墓、助鬼伤身，主有覆军的失败。只有用间不同，类似嘱托：应爻空、破、散、绝，机谋就泄漏；应爻克冲世位，就是反间叛奸的人。君王用蓍草任命将领，也像用间的办法。古来用兵，没有比求将更大的，没有比用间更难的：或者内部结为骨肉，或者情谊兼有师友，所以用宾位的应。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about attacking or defending a city, whether the troops go by water or land, the speed and advantage of a battle, and sending out commanders and using stratagems, with the aim of certain victory, all are divined by the Self and the Other. If the court appoints a general, or a man is chosen to act as a spy, this is the great charge of the three armies, and he must be benevolent, brave and flexible; it should be judged by the Other, the guest's position. In these six kinds of question, victory and defeat are discussed, and only the Self being damaged and the Other controlling the Self are feared, while following the star of office into the tomb and helping Pressure to injure the Body mean the defeat of the army. Only using spies is different, being like entrusting a task: if the Other is Void, broken, scattered or extinct, the scheme leaks out; if the Other clashes with or controls the Self's place, he is a man who turns traitor. When the sovereign appoints a general by the stalks, it is also like the method of using spies. In the use of troops of old, nothing was greater than seeking a general and nothing harder than using spies: either one binds them as kin from within, or the feeling is that of teacher and friend, and so the Other, the guest's position, is used.

> [!note] 按
> 「宾应」是应爻，在这里它是被用的将、被用的间，处在宾的位置。「用间」是派间谍、离间敌人。「君蓍任将」是君王用占筮来选将。「随官入墓、助鬼伤身」见增删卜易对它们的另一说法（ZS.06）；这里是军事占，取凶。这一条与易林补遗出师征伐里「用间」「择将」的取法（EL.12 起）和黄金策「将福官之强弱」（HJC.86）取同一类象。
>
> *Note:* 'Bin ying', the guest Other, is the Other line, which here is the general or spy who is employed, in the guest's position. 'Yongjian' is sending spies and sowing dissension in the enemy. 'Jun shi ren jiang' is the sovereign choosing a general by divination. 'Following the star of office into the tomb' and 'helping Pressure to injure the Body' have another account in the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.06); here, in a military divination, they are taken as ominous. This entry takes the same kind of images as the choosing of generals and use of spies in the Yilin Buyi on sending out troops (from EL.12) and 'weigh the strength of Output and the star of office' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.86).
