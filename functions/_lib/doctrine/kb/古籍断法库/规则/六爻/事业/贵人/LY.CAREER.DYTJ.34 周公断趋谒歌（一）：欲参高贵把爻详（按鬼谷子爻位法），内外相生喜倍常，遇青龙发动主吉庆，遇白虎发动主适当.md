# 周公断趋谒歌（一）：欲参高贵把爻详（按鬼谷子爻位法），内外相生喜倍常，遇青龙发动主吉庆，遇白虎发动主适当

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想去参见高贵的人，要把爻位仔细推详（按鬼谷子爻位法推：如贵人落在初爻，宜谒乡里的贵人）；内、外卦相生，喜事加倍；如果遇到青龙发动，主有吉庆；如果遇到白虎发动，主拜谒适当。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To call on a person of high rank, examine the lines closely (by the Guigu line-position method: if the noble stands on the first line it is fitting to call on a noble of the township); inner and outer generating each other, joy is doubled; if the Azure Dragon moves there is good fortune; if the White Tiger moves, the call is fitting.

> [!note] 按
> 白虎一般主凶，这里却说白虎发动「很当」「趋谒适当」，是原文的说法，本库照录，不改。数字注号（1）到（10）是整理时编的。
>
> *Note:* The White Tiger generally stands for ill, yet here it is said that the White Tiger moving is 'very fitting' for a call, as the text has it; it is recorded as it stands and not altered. The numbered notes (1) to (10) were numbered in editing.
