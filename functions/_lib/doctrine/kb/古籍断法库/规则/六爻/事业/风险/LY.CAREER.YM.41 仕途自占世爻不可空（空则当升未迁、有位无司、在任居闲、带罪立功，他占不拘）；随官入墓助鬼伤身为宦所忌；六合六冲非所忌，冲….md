# 仕途自占世爻不可空（空则当升未迁、有位无司、在任居闲、带罪立功，他占不拘）；随官入墓助鬼伤身为宦所忌；六合六冲非所忌，冲而旺相淹三月、合而休囚滞五年；晋升为吉屯蹇为凶亦不可遗

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 自己占的，世爻不可空：空了，就是该升而没有迁、有位而无职司、在任而闲居、带着罪而立功；替别人占就不是这样。随官入墓、助鬼伤身，是仕宦所忌的，不论衰旺都凶。六合、六冲不是仕宦所忌的：冲而旺相，只是三个月的耽搁；合而休囚，就是五年的停滞。还有晋、升两卦为吉，屯、蹇两卦为凶，也不可遗漏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for oneself the Self line must not be Void: if it is Void, one who should be promoted is not moved, one holds rank without office, is in post but idle, or serves to atone while under a charge; for another it is not so. Following Pressure into the tomb and helping Pressure to injure the Body are what an official career fears, and whether weak or thriving they are ominous. Six-Combine and six-clash are not what an official career fears: if clashed but thriving, it is only a delay of three months; if combined but resting or imprisoned, it is a stagnation of five years. Moreover, Jin and Sheng are auspicious and Zhun and Jian are ominous, and these must not be left out either.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这一条把晋、升、屯、蹇四个卦象直接当作断语，注里还说「无论官鬼」，和易林补遗朝天面旨（EL.92）一样；增删卜易野鹤（ZS.07）的看法不同：晋、升也要世爻得地，屯、蹇只要世爻旺相就没有妨碍。也就是说：易冒、易林补遗把卦名放在前面，野鹤把卦名放在世爻旺衰的后面。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This entry takes the four images Jin, Sheng, Zhun and Jian directly as verdicts, and its note adds 'regardless of Pressure', as does the Yilin Buyi on the audience at court (EL.92); the Zengshan Buyi of Yehe (ZS.07) differs: Jin and Sheng also need the Self to be well placed, while Zhun and Jian do no harm so long as the Self is thriving. That is: the Yimao and the Yilin Buyi put the hexagram name first, while Yehe puts the hexagram name behind the strength of the Self.

> [!note] 按
> 「自占世爻」是为自己占，世爻代表自己；「他占则否」是替别人占，世空不算数。「有位而无司」是有官位而没有职掌，「带罪立功」是身上有处分、立了功才免。「淹」是久留不动。「随官入墓、助鬼伤身」是官鬼入墓、再帮官鬼伤自己（见 YM.13）。「六合六冲，非仕宦之所忌」是说仕途不以合冲为凶：冲了只是耽搁三个月，合而休囚是停滞五年，耽搁的长短由旺衰定。「晋」「升」「屯」「蹇」是四个卦名：晋是前进，升是上升，屯、蹇是艰难。和易林补遗仕宦升迁的世空一条（EL.59）、黄金策的世动逢空（HJC.08）可对照。
>
> *Note:* 'Divining for oneself the Self line' is divining for oneself, the Self line standing for oneself; 'for another it is not so' is that when divining for another a Void Self does not count. 'Holding rank without office' is having an official place but no charge, and 'serving to atone' is being under a penalty that is lifted only on achieving merit. 'Yan' is being kept long without moving. 'Following Pressure into the tomb and helping Pressure to injure the Body' is Pressure entering the tomb and then helping Pressure to injure oneself (see YM.13). 'Six-Combine and six-clash are not what an official career fears' says that in an official career combining and clashing are not taken as ominous: clashed it is only a delay of three months, combined but resting it is a stagnation of five years, and the length of the delay is set by strength. Jin, Sheng, Zhun and Jian are four hexagram names: Jin is advance, Sheng is rising, and Zhun and Jian are hardship. Compare the entry on the Self Void in the Yilin Buyi on promotion (EL.59) and the Huangjin Ce on the Self moving into Void (HJC.08).
