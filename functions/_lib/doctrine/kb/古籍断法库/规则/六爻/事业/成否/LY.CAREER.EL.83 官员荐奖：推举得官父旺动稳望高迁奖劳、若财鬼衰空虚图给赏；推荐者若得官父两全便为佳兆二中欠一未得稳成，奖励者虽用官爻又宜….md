# 官员荐奖：推举得官父旺动稳望高迁奖劳、若财鬼衰空虚图给赏；推荐者若得官父两全便为佳兆二中欠一未得稳成，奖励者虽用官爻又宜财旺、财鬼如空定无奖勤给赏之美

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推举时，官爻、父爻旺相发动，稳稳地盼得高升、奖励功劳；如果财爻、官鬼衰弱落空，想谋求赏赐也是白费。具体说：推荐的，官爻、父爻两样齐全才是好兆头，两样缺一，就不能稳稳办成；论奖励的，虽然用官爻，还适宜财爻旺相；财爻、官鬼如果落空，就一定没有奖励勤劳、给予赏赐的美事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a recommendation, if the star of office and Resource are thriving and moving, one can securely hope for high promotion and an award for service; if Wealth and Pressure are weak or Void, to plan for a reward is vain. In detail: for a recommendation, to have both the star of office and Resource is a good omen, and if one of the two is lacking it is not securely accomplished; for an award, though the Pressure line is the one used, Wealth should also be thriving, and if Wealth and Pressure are Void there will surely be no fine thing like an award for diligence and the granting of a reward.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 819–821 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 推荐者若得官父两全，便为佳兆。 | 推荐的，如果官爻、父爻两样齐全，就是好兆头。 | For a recommendation, if both the star of office and Resource are present, it is a good omen. |
> | 二中欠一，未得稳成。 | 两样当中缺一样，就不能稳稳办成。 | If one of the two is lacking, it is not securely accomplished. |
> | 奖动者虽用官爻，又宜财旺。 | 论奖励的，虽然用官爻，还适宜财爻旺相。 | For an award, though the Pressure line is the one used, Wealth should also be thriving. |
> | 财鬼如空，定无奖勤给赏之美也。 | 财爻、官鬼如果落空，一定没有奖励勤劳、给予赏赐的美事。 | If Wealth and Pressure are Void, there will surely be no fine thing like an award for diligence and the granting of a reward. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「奖动者」疑是「奖勤者」（后一句有「奖勤给赏」），译作「论奖励的」。「官父」是官爻和父母爻，「财鬼」是财爻和官鬼。「高迁」是高升。小标说推荐看官文、旌奖看财鬼，这一条把两样合起来说：推荐要官父齐，奖励要财官俱旺。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiang dong zhe' is presumably 'jiang qin zhe', for the next sentence has 'award for diligence and granting of reward', and it is translated 'for an award'. 'Guanfu' is the Pressure line and the Resource line, 'caigui' is Wealth and Pressure. 'Gaoqian' is high promotion. The subtitle said that recommendation looks at the star of office and the document and commendation at Wealth and Pressure, and this entry puts the two together: a recommendation needs both the star of office and Resource, and an award needs Wealth and the star of office both thriving.
