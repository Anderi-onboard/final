# 朝廷国事结语：祸福攸关于大象，吉凶不离五行衰旺；筮者当察其衰旺、变通活泼、相机而断

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 祸福关系在大象，要变通地洞察细微；吉凶离不开五行，要剖析精研衰旺。人能推究事物、获得知识，报应比影子、回声还快。祸福吉凶所关，易的大象离不开卦的五行，千姿百态哪里能都说尽？筮者应当审察五行的衰旺，自然要变通灵活，相机判断，没有不灵验的，就像回声应着声音。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fortune and misfortune turn on the great image, and one must adapt and perceive the faintest signs; good and ill do not leave the five elements, and one must analyse and study their strength and weakness. If a man can investigate things and gain knowledge, retribution is faster than shadow or echo. What fortune and misfortune turn on is that the great image of the Yi does not leave the five elements of the hexagram; of the thousand shapes and ten thousand states, how can all be told? The diviner should examine their strength and weakness and naturally adapt and be flexible, judging as the occasion offers, and there will be none that fail to prove true, as an echo answers a sound.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 结语三联在书里没有标点（「祸福攸关于大象变通洞烛机微」），按对仗读成「祸福攸关于大象，变通洞烛机微」「吉凶不离于五行，剖精研衰旺」。「格物致知」出自《大学》，是推究事物、获得知识；「悉战」疑是「悉载」，译文按「说尽」译。
>
> *Note:* The three closing couplets are unpunctuated in the book ('huofu youguan yu daxiang biantong dongzhu jiwei'), and by their parallelism are read as 'fortune and misfortune turn on the great image; adapt and perceive the faintest signs' and 'good and ill do not leave the five elements; analyse and study strength and weakness'. 'Gewu zhizhi' is from the Daxue, investigating things to gain knowledge; 'xi zhan' is presumably 'xi zai', to record fully, and the translation reads 'tell all'.
