# 谒贵占总述（游南子）与鬼谷分爻：人之谒贵必有所求所托，始恐不得见，见恐不如所请；看所谒之贵是何分爻，旺动生合身世者往必得见见必如请，伏藏空死墓绝胎而刑害克冲世身者往必不见见必不遂

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 拜谒显贵的占问：人去拜谒显贵，必定有所求、有所托，起初担心见不到，见到了又担心他不照所请求的办，所以不能不占。看所拜谒的那位贵人是哪一爻（大贵、中贵、朝贵、州郡、省贵、县贵、乡贵），这一爻出现、旺相发动、生合身爻、世爻的，去了必定见得到，见了必定如我所请；如果伏藏、空、死、墓、绝、胎，又刑、害、克、冲世爻、身爻的，去了必定见不到，见了必定不遂我的求。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining a call on a noble. A man calling on a noble surely has something to ask and something to entrust, and at first fears he will not be seen, and once seen fears the noble will not do as asked, so one cannot do without a divination. Look at which line the noble called on is (the great noble, the middle noble, the court noble, prefecture and commandery, the provincial noble, the county noble, the village noble): if this line appears, thriving and moving, and generates or combines with the Body and Self, going he will surely be seen and once seen it will surely be as I ask; if it lies hidden or is Void, dead, in tomb, extinct or in womb, and punishes, harms, controls or clashes with the Self and Body, going he will surely not be seen and if seen it will surely not fulfil my request.

> [!note] 按
> 鬼谷分爻表里的名目是「大贵、中贵、朝贵、州郡、省贵、县贵、乡贵」，书里没有写各是哪一爻，也没有写到底是六个还是七个，读法同 YY.02：从上爻写到初爻。「中贵」在明代常指宫中有权势的宦官，这里也可能只是「中等的贵人」，书里没有说明，译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* The names in the Guigu table are 'the great noble, the middle noble, the court noble, the prefecture and commandery, the provincial noble, the county noble, the village noble'; the book does not say which line each is, nor whether there are six or seven, and it is read as in YY.02, from the top line down to the first. 'Zhonggui' in the Ming often meant the powerful eunuchs of the palace, though here it may be only 'a noble of middle rank'; the book does not explain, and the translation follows the letter.
