# 文书占·公私之辨与成否（游南子）：虽俱以父母为用，公占以官旺为助，私占以子旺为助；三传刑害克破父母不成；卦无官父或官父临空绝化空绝非不发则遗失；父值墓胎、化墓胎、加勾土旺动必阻滞；在官占文凭诰敕公文差札身世坐禄马贵官印或三传值官印动生合身世必得；商占盐引钞文私居占契券田贴父旺动生合世身而财子旺静者有；应空绝墓胎、世持财加武动者无

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 文书的占问，有公事和私事的分别：虽然都以父母爻为用神，但公事占以官星旺相为助，私事占以子孙旺相为助。三传（太岁、月建、日辰）刑、害、克、破父母爻的，不成；卦里没有官星、父爻，或官星、父爻临空、绝，以及化成空、绝的，不是发不下来，就是遗失；父爻值墓、胎，以及化成墓、胎，或者加勾陈、土爻而旺相发动的，必定被阻滞耽搁拖延月日。在官的人占文凭、诰敕、公文、差委的札付，只要身爻、世爻坐着禄、驿马、贵人、官星、印绶，或者三传值官星、印星发动来生合身爻、世爻的，必定可以得到。为经商占盐引、钞文，以及居家占契券、田地凭帖，只要父母爻旺相发动生合世爻、身爻，而财爻、子孙旺相安静的，有；应爻空、绝、墓、胎，以及世爻持财爻加玄武发动的，没有（财爻克文书，玄武克朱雀）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Documents differ between public and private matters: though Resource is the Subject in both, in a public matter the star of office being thriving helps, and in a private matter Output being thriving helps. If the three rulers punish, harm, control or break Resource, it does not come about; if the hexagram has no star of office or seal line, or they sit on Void or extinction or turn into Void or extinction, it is either not issued or lost; if the seal line meets tomb or womb, or turns into tomb or womb, or carries Hook Earth and an Earth line and is thriving and moving, it will surely be blocked and delayed for months and days. For one in office divining credentials, imperial edicts, official letters and orders of appointment, if the Body and Self sit on Emolument, the Horse, the Noble, the star of office and the seal and ribbon, or the three rulers on the star of office and seal move to generate or combine with the Body and Self, they can surely be had. For a merchant divining salt vouchers and notes of credit, or a householder divining contracts, deeds and land certificates, if the Resource line is thriving and moving and generates or combines with the Self and Body while Wealth and Output are thriving and still, there will be one; if the Other is Void, extinct, in tomb or in womb, or the Self holds Wealth with the Dark Tortoise added and moving, there will be none (Wealth controls the document and the Dark Tortoise controls the Vermilion Bird).

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 两个例子都是《易隐》文书占末尾附的断例，放在最相关的这一条下。第二例里有几处字面对不上通行的说法，译文都照字面：「月辰巳火六合」按字面是月建辰和巳火，而申的六合只有巳，辰与申是申子辰的半三合；「又犯月破」按通行的月破（月建所冲的支）庚辰月破的是戌，不是亥，这里大概是说亥水被月建辰土克破；「病于岁建寅，申墓于月建辰，申绝于日建巳」说的是亥水兄弟（受托的人）：水病在寅、墓在辰、绝在巳，「申」是借申来指水长生之位。「三传」是太岁、月建、日辰（见术语表）。
>
> *Note:* Both examples are appended at the end of this chapter on documents in the Yi Yin, and are placed under the entry they illustrate most. In the second example several wordings do not match the usual way of speaking, and the translation follows the letter: 'six-combination with the Month Chen and Si Fire' is by the letter the Month Chen and Si Fire, whereas Shen's six-combination is only Si, and Chen and Shen are a half three-harmony of Shen, Zi and Chen; 'also suffers Month-break' by the usual Month-break (the branch the Month clashes) would be Xu in a Gengchen month and not Hai, and here it presumably means that Hai Water is controlled and broken by the Month Chen Earth; 'ill at the Year Yin, Shen in tomb at the Month Chen, Shen extinct in the Day Si' speaks of the Hai Water Peer line (the man entrusted): Water is ill at Yin, in tomb at Chen and extinct at Si, and 'Shen' is used for the position where Water is born. The 'three rulers' are Tai Sui, the Month and the Day (see the glossary).
