# 上书·旺父持世，宜日月岁五以维持（父母为文书奏章，持世合世奏必见准，再得岁五日月动爻生合，见用无疑）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上书奏事，父母爻就是文书、奏章：旺相的父母爻持世、合世，奏章必定被批准；再得到太岁、五爻和日月、动爻来生合的，被采用无疑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In submitting a memorial the Resource line is the document and the memorial: if a thriving Resource line holds or combines with the Self, the memorial will surely be approved, and if Tai Sui, the fifth line, the Day, the Month or a moving line also generates or combines with it, it will be adopted without doubt.
