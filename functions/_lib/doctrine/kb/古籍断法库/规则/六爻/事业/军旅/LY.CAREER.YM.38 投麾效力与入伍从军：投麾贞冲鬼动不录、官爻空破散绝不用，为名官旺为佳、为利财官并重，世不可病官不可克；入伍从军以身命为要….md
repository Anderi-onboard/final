# 投麾效力与入伍从军：投麾贞冲鬼动不录、官爻空破散绝不用，为名官旺为佳、为利财官并重，世不可病官不可克；入伍从军以身命为要，世象强良则冒锋镝成功、官爻旺相则拔行伍拜爵，官克世助伤随墓弗宜见

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 投到将帅麾下效力：本卦六冲、鬼动，就不被录用；官爻空、破、散、绝，就不被任用；为名，官旺才好；为利，财、官并重；世不可病，官不可被克。入伍从军，以身体性命为要：世象强而好，冒着锋镝也能成功；官爻旺相，从行伍中被提拔、拜爵；官爻克世、助伤随墓，都不宜见到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In going to serve under a commander's banner: if the original hexagram is a six-clash or Pressure moves, one is not taken on; if the Pressure line is Void, broken, scattered or extinct, one is not employed; for the sake of a name, thriving Pressure is good; for profit, Wealth and Pressure count equally; the Self must not be sick, and Pressure must not be controlled. In enlisting in the ranks, body and life are what matter: if the Self image is strong and good, one braves blades and arrows and succeeds; if the Pressure line is thriving, one is raised from the ranks and granted rank; the Pressure line controlling the Self, helping the injury and following into the tomb should not be seen.

> [!note] 按
> 「投麾」是投到将帅的麾（军旗）下效力。「贞冲」是本卦（贞）是六冲卦。「鬼动则不录」的「鬼」就是官鬼：要被录用，官鬼要静、要旺，动了反而不稳。「锋镝」是刀锋和箭头，「行伍」是军队的队列，「拜爵」是授予爵位。「助伤随墓」是帮着官鬼伤世、跟着官鬼入墓，与本章军机的「随官入墓、助鬼伤身」（YM.13）同一类忌。和易林补遗袭求武弁（EL.62 起）、黄金策仕宦的出征剿捕（HJC.24）可对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Touhui' is going to serve under the 'hui', the banner, of a commander. 'Zhenchong' is the original hexagram (zhen) being a six-clash hexagram. The 'gui' of 'Pressure moving is not taken on' is the Pressure line: to be taken on, Pressure should be still and thriving, and moving it is unsteady. 'Fengdi' is blade and arrowhead, 'hangwu' is the ranks of an army, and 'baijue' is being granted a title of rank. 'Helping the injury and following into the tomb' is helping Pressure to injure the Self and following Pressure into the tomb, the same taboo as 'following Pressure into the tomb and helping Pressure to injure the Body' in the military chapter (YM.13). Compare the Yilin Buyi on seeking military office (from EL.62) and the Huangjin Ce on marching out to subdue (HJC.24).
