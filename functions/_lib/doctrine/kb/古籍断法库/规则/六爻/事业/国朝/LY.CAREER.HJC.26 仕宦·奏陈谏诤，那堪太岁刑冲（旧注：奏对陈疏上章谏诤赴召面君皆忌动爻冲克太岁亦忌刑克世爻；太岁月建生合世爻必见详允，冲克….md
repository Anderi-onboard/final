# 仕宦·奏陈谏诤，那堪太岁刑冲（旧注：奏对陈疏上章谏诤赴召面君皆忌动爻冲克太岁亦忌刑克世爻；太岁月建生合世爻必见详允，冲克须防不测之祸；岁君衰静不带刑害虎蛇不见取用非大害；动空化空虚惊；有制伏冲散合住必得大臣申救；应动冲克世更防人奏劾）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上奏陈情、进呈奏章、上书、进谏，以及奉召面君这类事，都忌讳动爻冲克太岁，也忌讳世爻被刑克；如果太岁、月建生合世爻，必定得到详细审议并获准；一旦遇到冲克，须提防意外的祸患；太岁衰弱安静又不带刑、害、白虎、螣蛇，只是不被采用，没有大害；动爻空亡或化空，也只是虚惊；有制伏、冲散、合住的，必有大臣出来申救；应爻发动冲克世爻，更要提防有人上奏弹劾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For anything such as a memorial in audience, presenting a petition, submitting a written report, remonstrating, or going to an audience with the sovereign at his summons, it is feared that a moving line clashes or controls the Tai Sui, and also that the Self is punished or controlled; if the Tai Sui or the Month generates or combines with the Self, it will surely be examined in detail and approved; once a clash or control comes, beware of an unforeseen misfortune; if the Tai Sui is weak and still and carries no punishment, harm, White Tiger or Coiling Snake, one is only not adopted and there is no great harm; if the moving line is Void or turns into Void, it is only a false alarm; if there is something that subdues, clashes away or combines it, a high minister will surely come forward to plead and rescue; and if the Other moves and clashes or controls the Self, beware all the more of someone submitting an impeachment.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1978 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡遇奏对、陈疏、上章、谏诤及赴召面君类，皆忌动爻冲克，并忌太岁刑克世爻。 | 凡遇到奏对、陈疏、上章、谏诤和奉召面君这类事，都忌讳动爻冲克，也忌讳太岁刑克世爻。 | In anything such as an audience with a memorial, presenting a petition, submitting a written report, remonstrating, or going to an audience at the sovereign's summons, a moving line clashing or controlling is feared, and the Tai Sui punishing or controlling the Self is also feared. |
> | 若太岁月建生合世爻，必见谕允； | 如果太岁、月建生合世爻，必定得到准许； | If the Tai Sui or the Month generates or combines with the Self, one will surely receive approval; |
> | 如来冲克，须防不测之祸。 | 如果来冲克，须提防意外的祸患。 | if a clash or control comes, beware of an unforeseen misfortune. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「奏对」是面奏回答皇帝，「陈疏」「上章」是上书，「谏诤」是进言规劝，「赴召面君」是奉召去见皇帝，「申救」是出面陈说、营救，「奏劾」是上奏弹劾。旧注「亦忌刑克世爻」没有说谁刑克世爻，王洪绪补明是「太岁刑克世爻」。旧注比王洪绪多出的几句（太岁衰静不带刑害虎蛇只是不被采用、动空化空是虚惊、有制伏冲散合住有大臣申救、应动冲克世防人弹劾），是在「冲克」这个凶象上分轻重：太岁衰静无煞只是不被采纳，有煞才是祸；动爻空或化空是虚惊；有人制住冲克的爻，就有大臣出来救。这一联是「面君」类事的判据，所以放在「国朝」一题下。
>
> *Note:* 'Audience' is replying to the emperor in person, 'a petition' and 'a written report' are writing to the throne, 'remonstrating' is offering advice and admonition, 'going to an audience at his summons' is being summoned to see the emperor, 'plead and rescue' is coming forward to speak and save, and 'impeachment' is submitting a memorial of impeachment. The old commentary's 'it is also feared that the Self is punished or controlled' does not say by whom, and Wang Hongxu makes it clear that it is 'the Tai Sui punishing or controlling the Self'. The extra sentences of the old commentary (the Tai Sui weak and still with no punishment, harm, Tiger or Snake is only not being adopted; a moving line Void or turning into Void is a false alarm; with something that subdues, clashes away or combines it, a minister pleads and rescues; the Other moving and clashing the Self, beware impeachment) grade the ominous sign of clash or control by weight: the Tai Sui weak and still without killers is only not being adopted, and only with killers is there misfortune; the moving line Void or turned Void is a false alarm; and when something holds in check the line that clashes or controls, a minister comes out to rescue. This couplet is the criterion for matters of facing the sovereign, hence it is placed under 'the court and the state'.
