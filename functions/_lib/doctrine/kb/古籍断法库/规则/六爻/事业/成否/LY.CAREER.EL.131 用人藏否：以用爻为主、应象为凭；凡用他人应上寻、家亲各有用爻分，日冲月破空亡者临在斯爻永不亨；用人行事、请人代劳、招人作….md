# 用人藏否：以用爻为主、应象为凭；凡用他人应上寻、家亲各有用爻分，日冲月破空亡者临在斯爻永不亨；用人行事、请人代劳、招人作生涯、托付图利，用外人以应爻为主，占亲戚以用象为凭；用爻遇日辰冲月建破或落旬空皆主不成纵成无力又不久远切莫用之

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用人，以用爻为主，以应象为凭据。凡是用别人，就在应爻上找；亲属各有各的用爻划分；日冲、月破、空亡落在这个爻上，就永远不亨通。凡是用人办事，或者请人代自己劳作，或者招人做生意，或者托付别人图利益，如果用的是外人，以应爻为主；如果是亲戚，以用象为凭据。用爻如果遇到日辰冲、月建破，或者落旬空，都主不成；即使成了，也没有力量，又不长久，千万不要用这个人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In employing a man, take the Subject line as the chief and the Other image as the ground. In employing an outsider, look to the Other; for one's own kin, each has a Subject line to itself; if the Day's clash, Month-break or Void sits on this line, it never prospers. Whenever one employs a man to act, or asks a man to labour in one's place, or takes on a man for a business, or entrusts something to another for gain, if one employs an outsider the Other line is the chief; if it is a relative, the Subject image is the ground. If the Subject line meets the Day's clash, the Month's break, or falls on the ten-day Void, in every case it does not succeed, and even if it succeeds it has no strength and does not last, so by no means employ him.

> [!note] 按
> 「用人」是雇用、托付别人办事。「藏否」就是「臧否」，好坏。「家亲各有用爻分」是用亲属时按六亲各取那一爻。「斯爻」是这一爻。这一章看的是被用的这个人：外人取应爻，亲属取他那个六亲的爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Yong ren' is employing and entrusting a man with an affair. 'Zangpi' is written 'cang fou' and means good and bad. 'Jiaqin ge you yongyao fen' is that in employing a relative, each of the six kin takes its own line. 'Siyao' is this line. This chapter looks at the man who is employed: for an outsider, the Other line; for a relative, the line of his own kin.
