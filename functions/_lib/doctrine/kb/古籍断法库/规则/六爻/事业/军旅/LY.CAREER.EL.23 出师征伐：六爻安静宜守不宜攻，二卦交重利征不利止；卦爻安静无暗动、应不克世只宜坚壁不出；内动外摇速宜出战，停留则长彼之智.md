# 出师征伐：六爻安静宜守不宜攻，二卦交重利征不利止；卦爻安静无暗动、应不克世只宜坚壁不出；内动外摇速宜出战，停留则长彼之智

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻安静，宜守不宜攻；内外两卦都发动，利于出征、不利于停留。卦爻安静、又没有暗动的，应爻又不克世爻，只宜坚壁不出；「二卦」就是内外卦，如果内卦动、外卦摇，要赶快出战，停留下来就增长了对方的智谋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the six lines are still, it is fit to hold and not to attack; if both trigrams, inner and outer, move in double, it favours going out and does not favour staying. If the lines are still and nothing moves secretly and the Other does not control the Self, it is only fit to hold the walls and not go out; the 'two trigrams' are the inner and outer, and if the inner moves and the outer shakes, one should go out to fight quickly, since staying only increases the enemy's wit.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 340 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦爻安静更无暗动者、应又不克世爻，只宜坚壁不出。 | 卦爻安静、又没有暗动的，应爻又不克世爻，只宜坚壁不出。 | If the lines are still and nothing moves secretly and the Other does not control the Self, it is only fit to hold the walls and not go out. |
> | 二卦者，内外也。 | 「二卦」，就是内外卦。 | The 'two trigrams' are the inner and outer. |
> | 若内动外摇，速宜出战，停留则长彼之智矣！ | 如果内动外摇，要赶快出战，停留下来就增长了对方的智谋！ | If the inner moves and the outer shakes, one should go out to fight quickly, since staying only increases the enemy's wit! |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「应又不克世爻」指应爻不克世爻。「暗动」是日辰冲动静爻（见 EL.28）。这一条与易隐征战占「方向」里的「内旺外衰可战、内衰外旺宜守」（YY.53）是同一类取法，但这里看的是动静，那里看的是旺衰。
>
> *Note:* 'The Other does not control the Self' means the Other line does not control the Self line. 'Secret movement' is a still line clashed into motion by the Day (see EL.28). This entry takes the same kind of view as 'if the inner is thriving and the outer weak one can fight, if the inner is weak and the outer thriving hold' in the Yi Yin 'direction' section (YY.53), but this one looks at motion and stillness, and that one at strength and weakness.
