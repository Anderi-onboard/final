# 仕宦·镇守边陲，卦爻静而华夷安泰（旧注：镇守不拘文武皆宜六爻安静，世应生合比和日辰月建不冲克则安然无警，世应空亡亦吉；官鬼发动世应冲克必多侵扰）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 镇守一方，不论文官武职，都适宜六爻安静；世应生合比和，日辰月建不互相冲克，就能安睡在边庭，安然没有警报，世应空亡也吉；如果遇到官鬼发动、世应冲克，必定多有侵扰，要通变着断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In guarding a region, whether in a civil or military post, it is fitting that the six lines are still; if the Self and the Other generate, combine or are in harmony and the Day and the Month do not clash or control each other, one sleeps soundly at the border, at ease and without alarm, and the Self and the Other Void is also auspicious; if Pressure moves and the Self and the Other clash or control each other, there will surely be many incursions, and one should judge flexibly.

> [!note] 按
> 「华夷」是中原和边地的民族，「安泰」是平安。「鼾睡边庭」是说在边疆睡得安稳，不用戒备。「世应空亡亦吉」只有旧注写了：世应都空，双方都不动，边境无事。这一联讲的是「守」，和上一联 LY.CAREER.HJC.24 的「攻」（出征剿捕）正好相反：守要静，攻要动。旧注结尾「宜通变断之」、王洪绪「用宜通变推之」，都是提醒不要死守这条。
>
> *Note:* 'The Chinese and the border peoples' are the peoples of the heartland and of the border lands, and 'at peace' is peace and safety. 'Sleeps soundly at the border' says one sleeps easily on the frontier without having to be on guard. 'The Self and the Other Void is also auspicious' is only in the old commentary: when both the Self and the Other are Void, neither side moves and there is nothing on the border. This couplet speaks of 'defending', the very opposite of the preceding couplet, LY.CAREER.HJC.24, on 'attacking' (campaigning and suppressing bandits): defending wants stillness, and attacking wants movement. The old commentary's closing 'judge it flexibly' and Wang Hongxu's 'in use it should be inferred flexibly' both warn against holding to this rule rigidly.
