# 卜筮元龟·占居官安否：世宫为官应为民；子爻来克鬼则在位彷徨忧退位；旺相相生六位安、居官无事吏民欢；财动必忧亡失位；休囚墓杀细推看

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占在任的官安不安：世宫是官，应是百姓；主首、监临，世就是自身；如果有子孙爻来克官鬼，在位的人心神彷徨，担忧退位；旺相相生，六位都安，居官平安无事、吏民欢喜；财动，必定担忧丢失官位；遇休囚、墓、杀，要仔细推看。注文说：父母爻是主首，月卦是自己的身；得度就安，退度就失。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On divining whether an official in post is secure: the Self palace is the official and the Other is the people; for the headman and the overseer the Self is oneself; if Output comes to control Pressure, the one in post is uneasy and worries about leaving his place; strong and generating one another, all six places are secure, the official in post is without trouble and clerks and people are glad; with Wealth moving one is sure to worry about losing the post; meeting rest, trapped, tomb or baleful spirits, reckon with care. The note says: the Resource line is the headman and the monthly hexagram is oneself; gaining the degree is secure and the retreating degree is loss.
