# 官禄占·何官（三）：世持官贵禄马不空刑克破，按所在爻的五行定部门——金：司马司寇大理(旺)、尚宝武选车驾职方武库(衰)、外任总制抚按总兵；木：司空(旺)营缮虞衡(衰)、外任关津主税；水：家宰(旺)文选稽勋验封都水(衰)、外任盐漕河道；火：宗伯翰林(旺)中书(衰)、加雀科道、午爻司马、外任文昌驿马月建督学；上爻：司农京兆巡城仓院、外任方伯屯田

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻持官星、贵人、禄、驿马，不落空、不被刑克破，再按它所在爻的五行定部门：金爻旺相，京官是兵部尚书、刑部尚书、大理寺和都察院中丞，衰弱的是尚宝司和兵部的武选、车驾、职方、武库各司郎官，武职是五军都督府和锦衣卫，外任旺相的是总制、巡抚、按察、观察、总兵，衰弱的是恤刑、司理、参将游击、把总；木爻旺相，京官是工部尚书，衰弱的是营缮司、虞衡司，外任旺相的是关津主税，衰弱的是税课、茶水之职；水爻旺相，京官是吏部尚书，衰弱的是文选、稽勋、验封、都水各司郎官，外任旺相的是盐政、漕运、河道、操江，衰弱的是水利、盐运、粮运之职；火爻旺相，京官是礼部尚书、詹事府、学士、国子监祭酒司业、太常寺、翰林这类官，衰弱的是中书，旺相又加朱雀的是科道官，衰弱的是鸿胪寺，在午爻上的旺相是兵部尚书、衰弱的是苑马寺，外任带文昌、驿马、又加月建的，不是主考官，必是督学官，衰弱的是学官和炉冶坑场之职；上爻旺相，京官是户部、京兆尹、巡城御史、仓场院，加青龙德神的是光禄寺，外任旺相的是布政使、屯田督运督粮，衰弱的是知府、知县和开矿、采石、垦荒的差使。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Self holds the star of office, the Noble, Emolument and the Horse, and is not Void, punished, controlled or broken, the department is fixed by the element of the line it sits on. On a Metal line, thriving: a capital official is the Minister of War, the Minister of Justice, or the head of the Court of Judicial Review or a vice censor-in-chief; weak: the Seal Office and the directors of the bureaus of the Ministry of War, and for the military the Five Commissions and the Embroidered-Uniform Guard; serving outside, thriving: a governor-general, governor, judicial commissioner, intendant or regional commander; weak: a commissioner for mercy in punishments, a judicial clerk, a deputy or flying column commander, a company captain. On a Wood line, thriving: the Minister of Works; weak: the Yingshan and Yuheng bureaus; outside, thriving: a customs and tax master; weak: a post for taxes and the tea and water duties. On a Water line, thriving: the Minister of Personnel; weak: the directors of the Wenxuan, Jixun, Yanfeng and Dushui bureaus; outside, thriving: salt, grain transport, river works or the river patrol; weak: a post for water conservancy, salt transport or grain transport. On a Fire line, thriving: the Minister of Rites, the Supervisorate of the Heir, a scholar, the Imperial Academy, the Court of Imperial Sacrifices or the Hanlin Academy; weak: a secretary of the Secretariat; thriving with the Vermilion Bird added: a supervising secretary or censor; weak: the Court of State Ceremonial; on the Wu line, thriving: the Minister of War, weak: the Pasturage Court; outside, carrying the Wenchang star, the Post Horse and the Month: not an examiner but surely an education commissioner; weak: a school instructor or a post in the furnaces, smelting works and mines. On the top line, thriving: a capital official is the Minister of Revenue, the governor of the capital prefecture, the city-patrol censor or the granary office, and with the Dragon and Virtue added, the Court of Banqueting; outside, thriving: a provincial administration commissioner or an officer of military farms, transport and grain; weak: a prefect, a county magistrate, or an envoy to open mines, quarry stone or clear land.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的官名都是明代的官署和职务，书里没有解释，译文按通行的叫法对译，个别地方不一定准：「大理中丞」应是大理寺和都察院的长官；「尚宝乡武选车驾职方武库郎」里的「乡」像是衍字；「五府衣使」按文意译作五军都督府和锦衣卫；「家宰」按文意是吏部尚书（冢宰）。「文昌」是一颗主文运的星煞。「午爻」指地支为午的那一爻。
>
> *Note:* The titles in this entry are Ming offices and posts that the book does not explain, and the translation renders them by the usual names; a few may not be exact: 'Dali zhongcheng' is presumably the heads of the Court of Judicial Review and the Censorate; the character 'xiang' in the string of bureau names looks superfluous; 'wufu yishi' is rendered by sense as the Five Commissions and the Embroidered-Uniform Guard; 'jiazai' is by sense the Minister of Personnel (the chief steward). 'Wenchang' is a spirit-star of literary fortune, and 'the Wu line' means the line whose branch is Wu.
