# 用人·八宫五行星煞推人：用爻独发论八宫（乾刚健/骄恣、坤柔顺/阿逢、震正直/暴戾、巽伶俐/风从、坎智巧/奸险、离明识/浮躁、艮笃实/疑滞、兑和悦/谄佞），五行六神诸星论用爻所值（水智/贼、火急/纵、木慈/懦、金刚毅/强横、土敦信/愚蠢；驿马勤趋走/数往来、天财善生息/善贪攘、官符长智谋/长讼斗、咸池能工技/溺酒色）；生世有益于己为贤、克世有损于己为不肖

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻动在八宫，就察八宫的性情；坐在五行，就推五行的性质；星煞详细看，衰旺仔细考，这个人是贤还是不肖，就可以借蓍草得知。八宫只论用爻单独发动：乾，生世是刚健，克世是骄恣；坤，生世是柔顺，克世是阿谀逢迎；震，生世是正直，克世是暴戾；巽，生世是伶俐，克世是随风倒；坎，生世是智巧，克世是奸险；离，生世是明白识事，克世是浮躁；艮，生世是笃实，克世是疑滞；兑，生世是和悦，克世是谄媚奸佞。五行、六神、诸星只论用爻所落的：水之贤叫智，不肖叫贼；火之贤叫急，不肖叫纵；木之贤叫慈，不肖叫懦；金之贤叫刚毅，不肖叫强横；土之贤叫敦信，不肖叫愚蠢。驿马生世，勤于奔走，克世，频繁来往；天财生世，善于生息，克世，善于贪占；官符生世，长于智谋，克世，长于讼斗；咸池生世，能于工技，克世，沉溺酒色。生世则对自己有益而是贤，克世则对自己有损而是不肖。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject line moves in an eight-palace trigram, one examines the nature of that palace; when it sits in a phase, one infers the character of the phase; the stars and spirit-stars are examined in detail and strength and weakness carefully weighed, and whether a man is worthy or unworthy can be learned by the stalks. The eight palaces are discussed only when the Subject line moves alone: Qian, generating the Self is firm and strong, controlling the Self is arrogant and wilful; Kun, generating the Self is gentle and yielding, controlling the Self is fawning; Zhen, generating the Self is upright, controlling the Self is violent and perverse; Xun, generating the Self is clever, controlling the Self is going with the wind; Kan, generating the Self is shrewd and skilful, controlling the Self is treacherous and dangerous; Li, generating the Self is clear-sighted, controlling the Self is flighty and rash; Gen, generating the Self is solid and honest, controlling the Self is suspicious and stuck; Dui, generating the Self is harmonious and pleasant, controlling the Self is flattering and glib. The phases, the six spirits and the stars are discussed only by where the Subject line stands: Water, as worthy called wisdom, as unworthy called thievery; Fire, as worthy called ardour, as unworthy called licence; Wood, as worthy called kindness, as unworthy called weakness; Metal, as worthy called firmness, as unworthy called brutality; Earth, as worthy called steadfast good faith, as unworthy called stupidity. The Post Horse generating the Self is diligent in running about, controlling it is coming and going often; Heavenly Wealth generating is good at making money breed, controlling it is good at grabbing; the Official Charge generating is strong in wit and plans, controlling it is strong in lawsuits and quarrels; the Peach Blossom generating is able in crafts, controlling it is sunk in wine and women. Generating the Self, it benefits oneself and he is worthy; controlling the Self, it harms oneself and he is unworthy.

> [!note] 按
> 「八宫专论独发」是只有用爻单独发动时才论八宫；五行、六神、诸星则只论用爻所落的。八宫的性格：乾刚健（过则骄恣）、坤柔顺（过则阿逢）、震正直（过则暴戾）、巽伶俐（过则风从）、坎智巧（过则奸险）、离明识（过则浮躁）、艮笃实（过则疑滞）、兑和悦（过则谄佞）；每一宫都是生世取它的好处，克世取它的过。「风从」是随风倒、没有定见。五行各有贤与不肖两面。「驿马」「天财」「官符」「咸池」是神煞：驿马主奔走、天财主生息、官符主智谋讼斗、咸池主工技酒色。总原则：生世对我有益就是贤，克世对我有损就是不肖。原文这一大段注的末尾没有收尾的「）」，这里照原文取到「不肖」为止。
>
> *Note:* 'The eight palaces are discussed only for a line moving alone' means that the eight palaces are discussed only when the Subject line moves by itself; the phases, the six spirits and the stars are discussed only by where the Subject line stands. The characters of the eight palaces: Qian is firm and strong (overdone, arrogant), Kun gentle and yielding (overdone, fawning), Zhen upright (overdone, violent), Xun clever (overdone, going with the wind), Kan shrewd (overdone, treacherous), Li clear-sighted (overdone, flighty), Gen solid (overdone, stuck), Dui pleasant (overdone, flattering); in each palace generating the Self takes its good side and controlling the Self takes its excess. 'Fengcong' is falling with the wind, without settled views. Each phase has a worthy and an unworthy side. The Post Horse, Heavenly Wealth, the Official Charge and the Peach Blossom are spirit-stars: the Post Horse governs running about, Heavenly Wealth governs breeding money, the Official Charge governs wit and plans and lawsuits, and the Peach Blossom governs crafts, wine and women. The general principle: generating the Self benefits me and he is worthy, controlling the Self harms me and he is unworthy. The long note in the original has no closing bracket at its end, and here it is taken as in the original up to 'unworthy'.
