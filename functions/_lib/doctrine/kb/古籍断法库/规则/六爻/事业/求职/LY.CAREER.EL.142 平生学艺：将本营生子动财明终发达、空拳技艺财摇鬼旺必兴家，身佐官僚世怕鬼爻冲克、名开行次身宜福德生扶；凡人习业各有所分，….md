# 平生学艺：将本营生子动财明终发达、空拳技艺财摇鬼旺必兴家，身佐官僚世怕鬼爻冲克、名开行次身宜福德生扶；凡人习业各有所分，将本者财为主象子作元神，空拳者官是用爻财为助主，若在官人役虽宜官鬼兴隆冲克世身大忌，牙行生理最要子孙旺则广招千里空则门可罗雀，卦中虽有子孙应若逢空亦无客至

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 靠本钱做生意谋生的，子孙发动、财爻分明，终究发达；靠空手手艺谋生的，财爻发动、官鬼旺相，必定兴家；给官僚当助手的，世爻怕官鬼爻冲克；在行会里开名头的，身爻适宜福德生扶。凡人学业各有各的分别：靠本钱的，财是主象，子孙是元神；空手的，官是用爻，财爻帮助主象；如果是在官府当差役的，虽然适宜官鬼兴旺，但官鬼冲克世身是大忌；牙行生意，最要子孙旺：旺就能广招千里的客人，空就门可罗雀；卦中虽然有子孙，应爻如果逢空，也没有客人上门。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Those who live by trade with capital will in the end prosper if Output moves and Wealth is clear; those who live by bare-handed skill will surely make the household prosper if Wealth sways and Pressure is thriving; for one who serves as an assistant to officials, the Self fears the Pressure line clashing with or controlling it; for one who opens his name in a trade guild, the Body should be generated and supported by Output, the fortune virtue. In learning a business each person has his own division: for those with capital, Wealth is the chief image and Output the Support; for those with bare hands, the star of office is the Subject line and Wealth aids the chief image; if one serves as a runner in an office, though it is fitting for Pressure to flourish, Pressure clashing with or controlling the Self or the Body is the great taboo; in the brokerage trade, Output thriving is what matters most: if thriving one draws guests from a thousand miles, and if Void the gate is empty enough to net sparrows; even if there is Output in the hexagram, if the Other meets Void no customer comes.

> [!note] 按
> 「将本」是拿本钱做生意，「空拳」是空手（靠手艺）。「身佐官僚」是在官员身边做佐助；「名开行次」是在行会里挂名开业；「牙行」是替买卖双方说合、抽佣的行当（经纪）。「郑在官人役」的「郑」疑是「若」，「门纲雀罹」疑是「门网雀罗」，即门可罗雀，按文意译。「元神」是生用神的爻（见术语表）：这里财是主象，生财的子孙是元神。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiang ben' is doing business with capital, and 'kongquan' is bare-handed (living by a skill). 'Shen zuo guanliao' is serving as an assistant beside officials; 'ming kai hangci' is hanging out one's name and opening business in a trade guild; 'yahang' is the brokerage trade of bringing buyer and seller together for a commission. The 'zheng' of 'zheng zai guan ren yi' is presumably 'ruo', if, and 'men gang que li' is presumably 'men wang que luo', a gate where sparrows can be netted, that is a deserted door, translated by the sense. 'Yuanshen', the Support, is the line that generates the Subject (see the glossary): here Wealth is the chief image and Output, which generates Wealth, is the Support.
