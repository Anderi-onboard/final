# 求馆·鬼或化兄，备礼先酬乎荐馆（旧注：鬼爻动出兄弟必得礼物先酬荐馆之人则可成；兄临世身亦主先费财物；兄临应上是东家好利必得礼仪馈送而后可成）

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动化出兄弟，必须拿出礼物先酬谢推荐馆的人，才能成；兄弟临世身，也主要先花费财物；兄弟临在应爻上，是东家好利，必须有礼品馈送，然后才能成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure moves and turns into Peer, one must give gifts first to repay the person who recommended one to the school, and then it can come through; Peer resting on the Self or the Body likewise means that one must first spend money and goods; if Peer rests on the Other, the host is fond of profit, and gifts must be sent before it can come through.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2765 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡遇鬼爻动出兄弟，必得礼物先酬荐馆之人，则可成就，兄临世身亦然。 | 凡是遇到官鬼爻发动化出兄弟，必须拿出礼物先酬谢推荐馆的人，才能成就，兄弟临世身也是这样。 | Whenever Pressure moves and turns into Peer, one must give gifts first to repay the person who recommended one to the school, and then it can be accomplished, and likewise if Peer rests on the Self or the Body. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 兄弟是劫财的神，在求馆里就是「要花钱」：鬼（荐馆人）化兄弟，要先谢荐馆人；兄弟临世身，自己先破费；兄弟临应，东家好利要礼。王洪绪只写了前两种，没有写兄弟临应的一种。
>
> *Note:* Peer is the spirit that robs wealth, and in a school post it means 'money must be spent': Pressure (the one who recommends) turning into Peer means the recommender must be thanked first; Peer on the Self or the Body means one spends first; Peer on the Other means the host is fond of profit and wants gifts. Wang Hongxu writes only the first two and not the case of Peer on the Other.
