# 洞林秘诀·求官（三）：太岁求官常赴诏；太岁与官爻并于九五或持世有威声；更得印绶相符合，圣人常挂记君名

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁（扶）求官，常常赴诏入朝；如果太岁在宫来，得台省之力；太岁和官爻并在九五，或者持世，有威名声势；再得印绶相符合，圣人常常挂念记着你的名字。注文说：官爻有气，遇升印卦利武职、升阳卦利文官；官鬼爻和印绶相生相扶，就是得地；如果杀神和世爻发动克官鬼，白劳一场、空手而返；太岁动扶官，主赴诏。太岁在九五与官爻并，或者持世，这人威名显赫；再得印绶相辅，君王时常记挂他。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Tai Sui supporting one seeking office, one is often summoned to court by edict; if Tai Sui comes in the palace, one gets the help of the censorate and secretariat; with Tai Sui and the Pressure line joined on the fifth line, or holding the Self, one has authority and fame; and if the seal and ribbon are also in accord, the sage ruler often keeps your name in mind. The notes say: with the Pressure line having qi, meeting a 'rising seal' hexagram favours military office and a 'rising yang' hexagram civil office; the Pressure line and the seal and ribbon generating and supporting each other is being well placed; if a baleful spirit and the Self line move to control Pressure, one labours in vain and returns empty-handed; Tai Sui moving to support the Pressure line means going to court on summons. Tai Sui on the fifth line together with the Pressure line, or holding the Self, makes the man famous and eminent, and with the seal and ribbon also helping, the sovereign often keeps him in mind.
