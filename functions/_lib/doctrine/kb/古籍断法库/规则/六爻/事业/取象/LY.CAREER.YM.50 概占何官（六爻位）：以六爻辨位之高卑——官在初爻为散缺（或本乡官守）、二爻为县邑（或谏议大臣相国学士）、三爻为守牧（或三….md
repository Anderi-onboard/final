# 概占何官（六爻位）：以六爻辨位之高卑——官在初爻为散缺（或本乡官守）、二爻为县邑（或谏议大臣相国学士）、三爻为守牧（或三司首领及大衙门随从）、四爻为司道（或九卿六曹近君之臣）、五爻君象惟代天巡狩与钦差者当之、六爻为贵戚之乡及宗庙功勋高尚辅相之臣；武途封职其义相类

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用六爻来分辨官位的高低：官爻在初爻，是散缺（闲散的缺额），或是本乡的官守；在二爻，是县邑，或是谏议大臣、相国学士；在三爻，是守牧（知府、知州一类），或是三司的首领，以及大衙门里随从的官；在四爻，是司道，或是九卿六曹，以及近君的臣子；五爻是君象，不是臣子所居，只有代天巡狩的官和奉钦命的钦差才当得起；六爻是贵戚之乡，以及宗庙功勋之臣、高尚之士、辅相王府的官、四围的股肱。武途的封职，意义相类似。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* By the six lines one tells the height or lowness of the rank: the Pressure line in the first line is a scattered vacancy (an idle post), or an official guard of his home district; in the second line, a county or town, or a remonstrating minister, a chief minister or academician; in the third line, a prefect or governor, or the head of the Three Offices, and officials attending in a great yamen; in the fourth line, a commissioner or circuit intendant, or the Nine Ministers and the Six Boards, and ministers near the sovereign; the fifth is the sovereign's image and not where a subject dwells, and only an official who makes tours of inspection in Heaven's stead or one sent by imperial command can fill it; the sixth line is the home of the noble kin, and of ministers of merit to the ancestral temple, men of lofty virtue, officials who assist a prince's household, and the limbs and trunk on all four sides. For appointments on the military path the meaning is similar.

> [!note] 按
> 「散缺」是闲散的缺；「守牧」是知府、知州一类的地方长官（「守」是太守，「牧」是州牧）；「司道」是布政使、按察使一类的司和各道的道员；「三司」是明代的布政使司、按察使司、都指挥使司；「九卿六曹」是朝廷的九卿和六部。「代天巡狩」是代天子巡视各地。「钦差」是奉皇帝之命外出办事的官。「贵戚之乡」是皇亲国戚居的位置。「武途封职」是武官一路的封授。每一爻后面的「一为」都是「又一说」。这一条和易隐官禄占的分爻（YY.02，鬼谷把执政、朝仕、监司、长官、曹官、吏人对应到六爻）是同一类办法，两家的对应并不完全一样，这里按易冒自己的说法。
>
> *Note:* 'Sanque' is an idle vacancy; 'shoumu' is a local head such as a prefect or a department magistrate ('shou' is a governor, 'mu' is a provincial shepherd); 'sidao' is the provincial commissioners such as the financial and judicial commissioners, and the intendants of circuits; 'the Three Offices' are the Ming provincial administration, surveillance and military commissions; 'the Nine Ministers and Six Boards' are the nine ministers of the court and the six ministries. 'Making a tour of inspection in Heaven's stead' is making a tour of the regions on the Son of Heaven's behalf. 'Qinchai' is an official sent out on the emperor's order to do a task. 'Home of the noble kin' is the place where the imperial relatives live. 'Appointments on the military path' is the conferring of posts along the military track. The 'yi wei' after each line is 'another view'. This entry is of a kind with the division by line in the Yi Yin on official careers (YY.02, where Guiguzi matches the ruler, court official, circuit supervisor, head official, bureau official and clerk to the six lines), and the two schools' correspondences are not wholly alike; here the Yimao's own saying is followed.
