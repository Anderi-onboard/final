# 出师征伐总纲：以世应为主、福德为凭；折杀执俘全凭勇略，观时制变各用机谋；传受三略六韬、八门九遁，虽识奇门之胜负，还凭易卦之吉凶

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出师征伐，以世爻、应爻为主，福德（子孙）为凭据。杀敌擒俘全凭勇气谋略，观察时机、应付变化各用机智谋划，传自黄石公、姜太公的《三略》《六韬》，诸葛孔明推演的八门九遁；虽然知道奇门的胜负，还要凭易卦的吉凶来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In sending out troops and campaigning, take the Self and the Other as the chief and Fortune Virtue (Output) as the ground. Killing the enemy and taking prisoners rest wholly on courage and strategy, and watching the moment and mastering change each use wit and planning; the Three Strategies and the Six Secret Teachings handed down from Huang Shigong and Jiang Taigong and the Eight Gates and Nine Escapes spread out by Zhuge Kongming are known, but though one may know the victories and defeats of Qimen, one still relies on the good and ill of the hexagram.

> [!note] 按
> 小标「以世应为主，福德为凭」是这一章的取法：世爻、应爻定彼己，子孙（福德）是兵将。第二句「三略者，黄遁全上略」的「黄遁全」文字有误，按文意是黄石公。第一联里「全」字在「全凭勇略」的前面，书里没有标点，译文按语气分成三组。「韬略」是兵法。这一章的头一联就把易卦和奇门并提，后面各条也常常两门并用。
>
> *Note:* The subtitle, 'the Self and the Other as chief, Fortune Virtue as the ground', gives the method of this chapter: the Self and the Other fix them and us, and Output (Fortune Virtue) is the troops and generals. The 'Huang Dun Quan' in the second sentence is a miswriting, and by sense is Huang Shigong. The couplets of the verse are unpunctuated in the book and are divided here into three groups by their rhythm. 'Taolue' is the art of war. The very first couplet of this chapter names the hexagram and Qimen together, and the entries after it often use the two together.
