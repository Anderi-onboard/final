# 诸星·天马：天马即乾马、正月午始循环行之，此星惟利升迁之卜（正月用乾四爻、二月五爻申；正七午、二八申、三九戌、四十子、五十一寅、六十二辰）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 天马，就是乾马，正月起在午，循环着走；这颗星只利于升迁的占问。正月用乾卦四爻为天马，二月用五爻（申），依次循环使用。口诀说：正月、七月在午，二月、八月在申，三月、九月在戌，四月、十月在子，五月、十一月在寅，六月、十二月在辰。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Heavenly Horse is the horse of Qian; it starts at Wu in the first month and goes round in turn; this star is of benefit only in divinations about promotion. In the first month the fourth line of Qian is taken as the Heavenly Horse, in the second month the fifth line, Shen, and so on in turn. The formula says: first and seventh months at Wu, second and eighth at Shen, third and ninth at Xu, fourth and tenth at Zi, fifth and eleventh at Yin, sixth and twelfth at Chen.

> [!note] 按
> 「天马」是神煞，「即乾马」是乾卦象马（乾为马）。「正月午始，循环行之」是从正月起在午，以后逐月循环。书里自注给了推法：正月用乾卦四爻（壬午）为天马，二月用五爻（壬申），以后循环；口诀的意思是正月、七月在午，二月、八月在申，三月、九月在戌，四月、十月在子，五月、十一月在寅，六月、十二月在辰（每两个月一组，相差两位）。「惟利升迁之卜」是这颗星只对升迁有利。驿马是另一颗星（主行人、出行），和天马不同（原文在这句之后紧接着讲驿马）。这里只收天马和它的推法。注文里接下去还有「天马文人用上，丧病者嗔」等句，是天马在文人、丧病中的用法，不属事业，没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Tianma' is a spirit-star, and 'ji Qian ma' is that the Qian trigram has the horse as its image (Qian is the horse). 'Starts at Wu in the first month and goes round in turn' is that from the first month it stands at Wu and then goes round month by month. The book's own note gives the way to find it: in the first month the fourth line of Qian (Ren-Wu) is taken as the Heavenly Horse, in the second month the fifth line (Ren-Shen), and so on in turn; the formula means first and seventh months at Wu, second and eighth at Shen, third and ninth at Xu, fourth and tenth at Zi, fifth and eleventh at Yin, sixth and twelfth at Chen (each group of two months, two places apart). 'Of benefit only in divinations about promotion' is that this star is favourable only to promotion. The Post Horse is another star (governing travellers and travelling) and differs from the Heavenly Horse (the original goes on to speak of the Post Horse right after this sentence). Only the Heavenly Horse and the way to find it are taken here. The note goes on with 'the Heavenly Horse is used above by literary men, and those in mourning or ill are angry' and so on, which are uses of the Heavenly Horse for literary men and for mourning and illness, not Career, and are not taken.
