# 用人·任人取爻：任长以父、任幼以子、任士以福、任臣以应；生世为忠良、克世为奸恶，空则无能或不为我力、散则无心或不从我用、破不一向、绝不先劳、纵生世不得其力；任文以父、任贾以财、任畜牧以子孙、任交游以兄弟，应象皆不可病；应为根本艺为枝叶

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 任用年长的用父爻，任用年幼的用子爻，任用读书人用福爻，任用臣属用应爻，依他所担的任来取用爻。生世的是忠良，克世的是奸恶；空就无能，或者不为我出力；散就无心，或者不听我用；破就不一心向着我，绝就不肯先出力，纵然生世也得不到他的力。任用文人用父爻，任用商人用财爻，任用养牲畜的用子孙，任用交游往来的用兄弟，而应象都不可有病。因为应是根本，技艺是枝叶，应空、应破，即使父爻旺，也做不成文事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For employing an elder, use the father line; for a youth, the child line; for a scholar, the Output line; for a subordinate, the Other line: the line is taken according to the charge he is given. One that generates the Self is loyal and good, one that controls the Self is treacherous and wicked; Void means incapable, or not exerting himself for me; scattered means no heart, or not following my use; broken means not wholly turned toward me; extinct means not willing to labour first, and even if it generates the Self one does not get his strength. For a literary man use the Resource line, for a merchant the Wealth line, for a herdsman the Output line, and for a man of social contacts the Peer line, and in all of these the Other image must not be sick. For the Other is the root and the skill is the branches and leaves: if the Other is Void or broken, even with a thriving Resource line he cannot do literary work.

> [!note] 按
> 「任长」是用年长的人，「任幼」是用年幼的，「任士」是用读书人，「任臣」是用臣属、属下；「福」是福神，即子孙。「忠良」「奸恶」是好坏。「不为我力」是不为我出力，「不一向」是不一心向着我，「不先劳」是不肯先出力。「文」是文事，「贾」是商人，「畜牧」是养牲畜的人，「交游」是交际往来的人。「应为根本，艺为枝叶」是说应爻是这个人本身，他的才艺只是枝叶，所以应空、应破时，即使父母旺（有文才），人也成不了文事。这一条是用爻的选法（按所用的人取爻）再加上生、克、空、散、破、绝对这个人的品性、出力的判断。
>
> *Note:* 'Renzhang' is employing an older person, 'renyou' a younger, 'renshi' a scholar, and 'renchen' a subordinate or underling; 'fu' is the fortune spirit, that is, Output. 'Zhongliang' and 'jian'e' are good and bad. 'Bu wei wo li' is not exerting himself for me, 'bu yi xiang' is not wholly turning toward me, and 'bu xian lao' is not being willing to labour first. 'Wen' is literary work, 'gu' is a merchant, 'xumu' is one who raises livestock, and 'jiaoyou' is one who keeps social company. 'The Other is the root and skill is the branches and leaves' says that the Other line is the man himself, and his skills are only branches and leaves, so when the Other is Void or broken, even if Resource is thriving (he has the literary talent) the man cannot do literary work. This entry is both the way of choosing the line (taking the line according to the man employed) and the judging of the man's character and effort by generation, control, Void, scattered, broken and extinct.
