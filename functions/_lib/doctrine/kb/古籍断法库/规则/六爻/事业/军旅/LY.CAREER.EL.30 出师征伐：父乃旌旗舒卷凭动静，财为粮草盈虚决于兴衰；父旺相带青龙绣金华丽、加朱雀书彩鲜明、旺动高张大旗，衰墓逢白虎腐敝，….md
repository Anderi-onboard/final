# 出师征伐：父乃旌旗舒卷凭动静，财为粮草盈虚决于兴衰；父旺相带青龙绣金华丽、加朱雀书彩鲜明、旺动高张大旗，衰墓逢白虎腐敝，空绝则少无；三军未发粮草先行，财旺兵粮丰盛，玄武兴防偷窃、兄弟兴抢夺，财衰墓粮不多，卦无财子化财必借贷邻邦，空绝则失无

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻是旌旗，舒展卷收要凭它的动静；财爻是粮草，盈满亏缺可以由它的兴衰来判断。营中的旌旗以父爻为用爻，旺相又带青龙，就绣金华丽；加朱雀，就色彩鲜明；旺相又发动，就大旗高张、导引飘飘。如果衰、墓而逢白虎，就腐朽破旧；（父爻）空而又绝，旗帜或少或没有，即使有也必然损坏；只要得到安静，世事就都安宁了。谚语说：三军未发，粮草先行，所以卦中的妻财当作车马的粮草：财爻旺相，是兵粮丰盛、马草有余；玄武兴起，要谨防偷窃；兄弟兴起，必定抢夺；如果财爻衰、墓，粮不多、草不肥；卦内没有财，如果有子孙变出财爻，必去邻邦借贷；这一爻如果空、绝，粮草就或者丢失或者没有。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line is the banners, and whether they are unfurled or furled depends on its motion or stillness; Wealth is the grain and fodder, and fullness and lack can be decided by its flourishing or decline. The banners in the camp take the Resource line as the Subject line; if it is thriving and carries the Azure Dragon, they are embroidered with gold and splendid; with the Vermilion Bird added, the colours are bright and clear; thriving and moving, great banners are raised high and wave about to lead the way. If it is weak or in tomb and meets the White Tiger, they are rotten and worn; if Void and extinct as well, the banners are few or none, and even if there are some they are sure to be damaged; if it is only still, all will be at peace. The proverb says: before the three armies set out, grain and fodder go ahead, so the Wealth in the hexagram is taken as the grain and fodder of carts and horses: if the Wealth line is thriving, the troops' grain is plentiful and the horses' fodder has surplus; if the Dark Tortoise rises, guard against theft; if Peer rises, there will surely be plunder; if Wealth is weak or in tomb, the grain is not much and the fodder not fat; if the hexagram has no Wealth and Output turns into a Wealth line, one must go to a neighbouring state to borrow; if this line is Void or extinct, the grain and fodder are either lost or lacking.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和易隐征战占「六亲」里的旌旗（YY.58）、粮草（YY.59）取法相同，只是这里更详细地写了旌旗的色彩和粮草的盈虚。第四句「世熊咸宁」疑是「世态咸宁」，译作「世事就都安宁」；「粼邦」是「邻邦」。「导引飘飘」是大旗在前引导、飘扬。
>
> *Note:* This entry takes the same view as the banners (YY.58) and provisions (YY.59) in the Yi Yin war divination 'six kin' section, only describing in more detail the colours of the banners and the fullness of the provisions. In the fourth sentence 'shi xiong xian ning' is presumably 'shi tai xian ning', translated 'all will be at peace'; 'lin bang' is 'lin bang', neighbouring state. 'Dao yin piaopiao' is the great banners leading in front and fluttering.
