# 羽士缁流占：身安世静最先；身爻克世可谋用、世应比和自然乐；世克身当退守、财克世好求缘；六爻不动为清福、一位交重事变迁

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 道士、僧人来问占：身安稳、世安静最要紧；身爻克世，可以去谋划；世、应比和，自然安乐；世去克身，应当退守；财来克世，适合去求缘；六爻不动是清福；有一位交重发动，事情就有变迁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For Taoist priests and monks asking by divination: the Body at ease and the Self still comes first; the Body line controlling the Self, one may plan and act; Self and Other in accord, ease comes of itself; the Self controlling the Body, one should withdraw and hold; Wealth coming to control the Self, it is good to seek alms; the six lines still is pure blessing; one line moving means change in the matter.
