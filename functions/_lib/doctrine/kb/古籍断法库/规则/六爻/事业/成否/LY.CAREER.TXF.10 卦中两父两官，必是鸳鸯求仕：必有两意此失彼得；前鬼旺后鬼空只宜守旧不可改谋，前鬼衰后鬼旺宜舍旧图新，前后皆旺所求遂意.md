# 卦中两父两官，必是鸳鸯求仕：必有两意此失彼得；前鬼旺后鬼空只宜守旧不可改谋，前鬼衰后鬼旺宜舍旧图新，前后皆旺所求遂意

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有两个父母、两个官鬼，一定是「鸳鸯求仕」：心里有两个打算，是此失彼得的兆头。前面的官鬼旺、后面的官鬼空，只宜守旧，不可改谋；前面的衰而后面的旺，宜舍旧图新；前后都旺，所求遂意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With two Resource lines and two Pressure lines in the hexagram it is surely 'mandarin ducks seeking office': there are two intentions, a sign of losing here and gaining there. If the former Pressure is strong and the latter void, one should only hold to the old and not change the plan; if the former is weak and the latter strong, give up the old and plan for the new; if both are strong, what is sought is attained as wished.
