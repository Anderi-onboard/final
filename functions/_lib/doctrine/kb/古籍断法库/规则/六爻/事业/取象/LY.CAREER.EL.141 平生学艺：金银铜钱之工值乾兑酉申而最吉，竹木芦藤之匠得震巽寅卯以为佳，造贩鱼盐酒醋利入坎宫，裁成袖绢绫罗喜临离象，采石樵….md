# 平生学艺：金银铜钱之工值乾兑酉申而最吉，竹木芦藤之匠得震巽寅卯以为佳，造贩鱼盐酒醋利入坎宫，裁成袖绢绫罗喜临离象，采石樵山须见艮、土工泥作要逢坤

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 做金、银、铜、钱的工匠，遇到乾、兑、酉、申最吉；做竹、木、芦、藤的匠人，得到震、巽、寅、卯为好；制造、贩卖鱼、盐、酒、醋，利益进入坎宫；裁制衣袖、绢帛、绫罗的，喜欢遇到离象；采石、上山砍柴，须见艮；做土工、泥作，要逢坤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For metalworkers in gold, silver, copper and coin, meeting Qian, Dui, You and Shen is the most auspicious; for craftsmen in bamboo, wood, reed and rattan, getting Zhen, Xun, Yin and Mao is good; in making and selling fish, salt, wine and vinegar, profit enters the Kan palace; for cutting garments and silks, one is glad to meet the Li image; for quarrying stone and cutting wood on the mountain one must see Gen; for earthwork and plastering one must meet Kun.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联把行业对到卦宫：金属工匠对乾、兑和酉、申，竹木匠对震、巽和寅、卯，鱼盐酒醋对坎，裁缝绫罗对离，采石樵山对艮，土工泥作对坤。书里没有说缘故：金、木、水、土几项是按五行对的，裁缝对离书里没有解释。「造败鱼盐酒醋」的「败」疑是「贩」，译作「制造贩卖」；「樵山」是上山砍柴。这一联后面没有解说。
>
> *Note:* This couplet matches trades to the palaces: metalworkers to Qian and Dui and You and Shen, bamboo and wood craftsmen to Zhen and Xun and Yin and Mao, fish, salt, wine and vinegar to Kan, tailoring and silks to Li, quarrying and woodcutting to Gen, earthwork and plastering to Kun. The book does not give the reason: the metal, wood, water and earth items go by the five elements, and the book does not explain tailoring going with Li. The 'bai' of 'zao bai yu yan jiu cu' is presumably 'fan', to trade, and it is translated 'making and selling'; 'qiaoshan' is going up the mountain to cut wood. No explanation follows this couplet.
