# 官禄占·贪廉（一）：官印皆旺财爻不见者廉；世持兄旺动者贪；世下伏兄化兄者贪

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 为官贪还是廉：官星、印星都旺相，财爻不出现的，清廉；世爻持兄爻、旺相发动的，贪；世爻下伏着兄爻、又变成兄爻的，贪。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Greed or integrity in office. If the star of office and the seal are both thriving and the Wealth line does not appear, he is incorruptible; if the Self holds a Peer line that is thriving and moving, he is greedy; if a Peer line lies hidden under the Self and turns into a Peer line, he is greedy.
