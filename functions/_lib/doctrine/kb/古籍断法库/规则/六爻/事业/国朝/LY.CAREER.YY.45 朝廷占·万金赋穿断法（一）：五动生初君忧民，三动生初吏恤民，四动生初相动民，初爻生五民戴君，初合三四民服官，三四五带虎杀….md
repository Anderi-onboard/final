# 朝廷占·万金赋穿断法（一）：五动生初君忧民，三动生初吏恤民，四动生初相动民，初爻生五民戴君，初合三四民服官，三四五带虎杀动克初民受害，初冲克三四五民胥谗

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《万金赋》穿断法，看君、官、民：五爻发动生初爻，君主忧虑百姓；三爻发动生初爻，官吏体恤百姓；四爻发动生初爻，宰相打动百姓；初爻生五爻，百姓拥戴君主；初爻合三爻、四爻，百姓服从官员；三爻、四爻、五爻带白虎、凶煞发动克初爻的，百姓受害；初爻冲克三爻、四爻、五爻的，百姓一同进谗毁谤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Chuanduan method of the Wanjin Fu, reading ruler, officials and people. If the fifth line moves to generate the first, the sovereign worries for the people; if the third line moves to generate the first, the officials care for the people; if the fourth moves to generate the first, the minister moves the people; if the first generates the fifth, the people support the sovereign; if the first combines with the third and fourth, the people submit to the officials; if the third, fourth or fifth line carrying the Tiger and baleful spirits moves to control the first, the people suffer harm; if the first clashes with or controls the third, fourth or fifth, the people all slander.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一段是《易隐》引的《万金赋》里的「穿断法」，书里没有说明「穿断」的意思，译文按音译，不解释。「相动民」按字面是宰相打动百姓，「民胥谗」按字面是百姓都在进谗毁谤，两处文意都不顺，译文照字面，不替它改。
>
> *Note:* This passage is the 'chuanduan' method of the Wanjin Fu as quoted in the Yi Yin, and the book does not say what 'chuanduan' means; the translation gives its sound and does not explain it. 'The minister moves the people' and 'the people all slander' are by the letter, and in both the sense does not run smoothly; the translation follows the letter and does not alter it.
