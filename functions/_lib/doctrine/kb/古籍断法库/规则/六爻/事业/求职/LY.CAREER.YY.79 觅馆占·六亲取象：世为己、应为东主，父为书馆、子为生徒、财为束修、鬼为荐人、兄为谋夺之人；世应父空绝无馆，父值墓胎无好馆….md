# 觅馆占·六亲取象：世为己、应为东主，父为书馆、子为生徒、财为束修、鬼为荐人、兄为谋夺之人；世应父空绝无馆，父值墓胎无好馆，财空墓绝胎修薄，子空墓绝胎徒少，鬼空墓绝胎无荐人，兄空墓绝胎无谋夺人

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 觅馆（找教书的馆），六亲的取象：世爻是自己，应爻是东家；父爻是书馆，子孙是学生，财爻是束修（馆金），官鬼是推荐人，兄弟是谋夺（这个馆）的人。世爻、应爻、父爻落空、绝的，没有馆；父爻值墓、胎，没有好馆；财爻空、墓、绝、胎的，束修菲薄；子孙空、墓、绝、胎的，学生少；官鬼空、墓、绝、胎的，没有推荐人；兄弟空、墓、绝、胎的，没有人来谋夺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking a teaching post, the images of the six kin. The Self is oneself and the Other is the host; the Resource line is the schoolroom, Output the pupils, Wealth the fee, Pressure the one who recommends, and Peer the one who schemes to take the post. If the Self, the Other and the Resource line are Void or extinct, there is no post; if Resource meets tomb or womb, there is no good post; if Wealth is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, the fee is slight; if Output is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, the pupils are few; if Pressure is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, there is no one to recommend; if Peer is Void, in tomb, extinct or in womb, there is no one to scheme for the post.

> [!note] 按
> 「修」「脩」都是束脩，即教书先生的酬金。这一节的取象和黄金策求馆章（HJC.29–38）几乎相同，只是黄金策以世爻为西席、应爻为东家，这里世爻是「己」。「东修」按字面是东家给的束修。
>
> *Note:* 'Xiu' and 'xiu' are both the tuition fee, the teacher's pay. The images in this section are nearly the same as those in the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a teaching post (HJC.29-38), the difference being that the Huangjin Ce takes the Self as the teacher's seat and the Other as the host, while here the Self is 'oneself'. 'Dongxiu' is by the letter the fee given by the host.
