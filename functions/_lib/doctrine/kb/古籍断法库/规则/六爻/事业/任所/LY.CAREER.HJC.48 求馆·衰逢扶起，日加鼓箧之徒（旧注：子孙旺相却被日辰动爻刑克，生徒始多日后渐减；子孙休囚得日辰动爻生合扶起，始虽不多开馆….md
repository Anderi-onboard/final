# 求馆·衰逢扶起，日加鼓箧之徒（旧注：子孙旺相却被日辰动爻刑克，生徒始多日后渐减；子孙休囚得日辰动爻生合扶起，始虽不多开馆后日渐增进）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙旺相却被日辰、动爻刑克，学生开始时虽然很多聚集在馆，日后渐渐减少；子孙休囚却被日辰、动爻生合扶起，开始时虽然不多，开馆后日渐增加。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output is thriving but is punished or controlled by the Day or a moving line, then although many pupils gather in the school at first, they gradually dwindle later; if Output is resting or trapped but is generated, combined or raised up by the Day or a moving line, then though there are not many at first, they increase day by day after the school opens.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2775 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙衰弱，得日辰动爻生合扶起，学徒始虽不多，开馆后日渐增举益也。 | 子孙衰弱，得到日辰、动爻生合扶起，学徒开始时虽然不多，开馆后日渐增加。 | If Output is weak and is generated, combined and raised up by the Day or a moving line, then though the pupils are not many at first, they increase day by day after the school opens. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「鼓箧」是古时入学的仪式（击鼓召集学生、打开书箱），这里指学生入学。旧注说的是两种相反的走势：先盛后衰（旺而被克）和先少后多（衰而被扶）；王洪绪只写了后一种。「开馆」是开始办馆。
>
> *Note:* 'Open their book-boxes at the drum' is an old school ceremony (a drum is struck to summon the pupils and the book-boxes are opened), here meaning pupils entering school. The old commentary gives two opposite courses: many first and then few (thriving but controlled) and few first and then many (weak but raised up); Wang Hongxu writes only the second. 'Open the school' means start running the school.
