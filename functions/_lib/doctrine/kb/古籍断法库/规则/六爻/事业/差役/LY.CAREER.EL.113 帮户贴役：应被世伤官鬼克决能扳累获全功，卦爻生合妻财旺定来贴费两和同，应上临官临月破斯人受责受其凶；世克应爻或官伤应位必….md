# 帮户贴役：应被世伤官鬼克决能扳累获全功，卦爻生合妻财旺定来贴费两和同，应上临官临月破斯人受责受其凶；世克应爻或官伤应位必是如心，世应相生相合财逢生旺之乡不必官扳自然津贴，官爻值应或应临月破者官必佥帮违选遭责

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻被世爻伤、被官鬼克，一定能把役扳累过去，获得全功；卦爻相生相合、妻财旺相，一定来贴补费用，双方和同；应爻上临官爻、临月破，这个人受责、遭凶。世克应爻，或者官伤应位，必定如意；世应相生相合、财爻逢生旺之乡，不必官府来扳，自然来津贴；官爻值应、或者应临月破的，官府一定佥派帮户，违背选定的就遭责罚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Other is hurt by the Self and controlled by Pressure, one can surely drag the service onto the other and gain complete success; if the lines generate and combine and Wealth is thriving, they will surely come with a subsidy for the expenses and both sides will be in accord; if the Other rests on the star of office or on Month-break, that man is reproved and meets ill. If the Self controls the Other line, or the star of office hurts the Other's place, it will surely be as one wishes; if the Self and the Other generate and combine with each other and Wealth meets a place of generation and thriving, there is no need for the officials to drag anyone in, and a subsidy comes of itself; if the Pressure line is on the Other, or the Other rests on Month-break, the officials will surely assign helper households, and whoever goes against the selection is reproved.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「扳累」是把累赘攀扯到别人身上；「贴费」是补贴费用。「斯人」指应（被扳的一方）：解里「官爻值应或应临月破者，官必佥帮违选遭责」说的是同一件事，「违选」按文意是违背官府的选定。「官伤应位」「官必佥帮」书里没有展开。
>
> *Note:* 'Banlei' is saddling another with a burden; 'tiefei' is subsidising the expense. 'Siren', that man, is the Other (the party who is dragged in): the explanation's 'if the Pressure line is on the Other or the Other rests on Month-break, the officials will surely assign helper households and whoever goes against the selection is reproved' speaks of the same thing, and 'wei xuan' is by the sense going against the authorities' selection. 'The star of office hurts the Other's place' and 'the officials will surely assign helpers' are not unfolded in the book.
