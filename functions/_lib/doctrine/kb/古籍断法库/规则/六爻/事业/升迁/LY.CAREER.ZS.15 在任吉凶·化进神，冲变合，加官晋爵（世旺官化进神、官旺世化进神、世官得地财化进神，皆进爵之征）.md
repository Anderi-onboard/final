# 在任吉凶·化进神，冲变合，加官晋爵（世旺官化进神、官旺世化进神、世官得地财化进神，皆进爵之征）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任时，变出进神，或冲变成合，都是加官晋爵的征兆：世爻旺相、官星变进神；官星旺相、世爻变进神；世爻与官星都得地、财爻变进神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In office, turning into an Advancing line, or a clash turning into a combination, are signs of added office and higher rank: the Self thriving and the star of office turning into an Advancing line; the star of office thriving and the Self turning into an Advancing line; or the Self and the star of office both well placed and Wealth turning into an Advancing line.
