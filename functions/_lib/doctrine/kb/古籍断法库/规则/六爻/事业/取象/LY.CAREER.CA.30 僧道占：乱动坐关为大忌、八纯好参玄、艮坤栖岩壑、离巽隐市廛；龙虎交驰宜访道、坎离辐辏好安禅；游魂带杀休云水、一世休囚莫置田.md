# 僧道占：乱动坐关为大忌、八纯好参玄、艮坤栖岩壑、离巽隐市廛；龙虎交驰宜访道、坎离辐辏好安禅；游魂带杀休云水、一世休囚莫置田

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 爻乱动而去坐关（闭关），是大忌；八纯卦倒好去参玄；艮、坤只能栖身岩穴山谷；离、巽适合隐居市井；龙虎交驰，适合访道；坎、离汇聚，适合安心坐禅；游魂卦带杀，别去云游；一世卦遇休囚，不要置买田产。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With lines moving in disorder, going into retreat is the great taboo; an Eight-Pure hexagram is on the contrary good for studying the mysteries; Gen and Kun suit only a dwelling among crags and ravines; Li and Xun suit hiding in the marketplace; the Dragon and Tiger racing against each other suit seeking the Way; Kan and Li converging suit settling into meditation; a Wandering Soul hexagram with a baleful spirit, do not go wandering like cloud and water; a First-Generation hexagram resting or trapped, do not buy fields.

> [!note] 按
> 「一世」原文没有展开；这里按「一世卦」（世在初爻）读，也可能指世这一爻本身休囚，本库不裁定。
>
> *Note:* 'Yi shi' is not expanded in the text; it is read here as a First-Generation hexagram (the Self on the first line), though it could mean the Self line itself being at rest or trapped, and this is not decided.
