# 国事结语：以正得国、以德守国者常，以力得国、以暴守国者不常；天命之矣宁俟筮哉；天授之学者仁知、人授之学者象数，安能以人授之学测天命之微，唯述旧闻以备昭格，占者慎之

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 听先贤说过：以正道得国、以德行守国的，国运常久；以武力得国、以暴虐守国的，不能长久。这是天命注定的，哪里还要等占筮？而且天所授的学问是仁与智，人所授的学问是象数，怎么能用人教的学问去测度天命的精微？这里只是转述旧时的见闻，以备昭示之用，占的人要谨慎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* I have heard the worthies of old say: those who gain the state by what is right and keep it by virtue endure; those who gain the state by force and keep it by violence do not endure. It is decreed by Heaven, so why wait for the yarrow stalks? Moreover, what Heaven teaches is benevolence and wisdom, and what men teach is images and numbers; how can one use what men have taught to fathom the subtlety of Heaven's decree? I merely relate what was heard of old, to be ready for clear manifestation; let the diviner take care.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这是国事章的结语。作者在这里说：得国守国靠的是德行，天命不是占筮能完全测度的，章中所讲只是转述旧说，占的人要谨慎。「昭格」是明白昭示、神明降临，这里指占卜的显示。「知」同「智」。
>
> *Note:* This is the closing of the chapter on state affairs. The author says here that gaining and keeping a state depends on virtue, that Heaven's decree cannot be fully fathomed by divination, that what the chapter says is only the relaying of old views, and that the diviner should take care. 'Zhaoge' is clear manifestation and the descent of the spirits, here meaning what divination reveals. 'Zhi' is the same as 'zhi', wisdom.
