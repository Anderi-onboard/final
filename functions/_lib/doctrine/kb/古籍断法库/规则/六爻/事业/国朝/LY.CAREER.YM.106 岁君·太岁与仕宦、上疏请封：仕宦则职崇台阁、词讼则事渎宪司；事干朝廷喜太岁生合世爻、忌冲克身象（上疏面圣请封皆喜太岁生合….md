# 岁君·太岁与仕宦、上疏请封：仕宦则职崇台阁、词讼则事渎宪司；事干朝廷喜太岁生合世爻、忌冲克身象（上疏面圣请封皆喜太岁生合世爻则上合君心、太岁冲身象则忧严谴）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁值福或值鬼的年份：身命有福神持世，一生平安快乐；家宅有鬼会合，岁月多艰难；仕宦，官职高到台阁；词讼，事情惊动宪司。事情关系朝廷，喜太岁生合世爻，忌冲克身象。凡是上疏、面圣、请封等事，都喜太岁生合世爻，就上合君心；太岁冲身象，就担忧受严厉的谴责。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Tai Sui sits on Output or on Pressure: for a person's fate, with the fortune spirit holding the Self one's whole life is peaceful and happy; for the household, with Pressure joining, the months and years are hard; for an official career, the post rises to the cabinet; for a lawsuit, the matter reaches the censorate. For a matter that concerns the court, it is welcome for the Tai Sui to generate or combine with the Self line, and it is feared for it to clash or control the body image. For memorials to the throne, audience with the sovereign, and asking for conferment of titles, it is always welcome for the Tai Sui to generate or combine with the Self line, which accords above with the sovereign's heart; the Tai Sui clashing the body image is a cause to fear severe censure.

> [!note] 按
> 「太岁」是当年的地支（岁君）。这一句接在太岁值福、值鬼之后（「值福」是太岁临在子孙上，「值鬼」是临在官鬼上），分说几类占问受到的影响：「身命福持而生平安乐」是身命占里福神持世、一生平安快乐，「家宅鬼会而岁月迍邅」是家宅占里官鬼会合、岁月多艰难，这两样另属身命、家宅各域；「仕宦则职崇台阁」是官职高到台阁（中枢），「词讼则事渎宪司」是官司惊动了宪司（监察、司法的衙门）。「事干朝廷」是事情关系朝廷，这时要太岁生合世爻、忌太岁冲克身象。书里自注：上疏、面圣、请封这类事，都喜太岁生合世爻，就上合君心；太岁冲身象，就忧严谴。与易林补遗朝天面旨（EL.91–93）、本书上书（YM.57）、黄金策仕宦的月建生身（HJC.07）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Taisui' is the branch of the current year (the Year Lord). This sentence follows the Tai Sui sitting on Output or on Pressure ('sitting on Output' is the Tai Sui resting on the Output line, 'sitting on Pressure' is resting on the Pressure line), and says how several kinds of divination are affected: 'with the fortune spirit holding the Self one's whole life is peaceful and happy' is Output holding the Self in a divination about a person's fate, one's whole life peaceful and happy, and 'with Pressure joining, the months and years are hard' is Pressure joining in a divination about the household, the months and years hard, these two belonging to the domains of personal fate and the household; 'the post rises to the cabinet' is the office rising to the cabinet (the centre of government), and 'the matter reaches the censorate' is the lawsuit stirring up the censorate (the supervising and judicial offices). 'Shi gan chaoting' is a matter that concerns the court, in which it is welcome for the Tai Sui to generate or combine with the Self line and feared for it to clash or control the body image. The book's own gloss: memorials, audience with the sovereign and asking for titles are all welcome with the Tai Sui generating or combining with the Self, which accords above with the sovereign's heart, and the Tai Sui clashing the body image is a cause to fear severe censure. Read against the Yilin Buyi on the audience at court (EL.91-93), the memorials in this book (YM.57) and the Month generating the body in the Huangjin Ce on official careers (HJC.07).
