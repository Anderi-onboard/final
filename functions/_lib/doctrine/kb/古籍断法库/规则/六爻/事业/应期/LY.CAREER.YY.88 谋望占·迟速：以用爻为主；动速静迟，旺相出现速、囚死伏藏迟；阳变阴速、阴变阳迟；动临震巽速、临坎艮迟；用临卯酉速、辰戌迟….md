# 谋望占·迟速：以用爻为主；动速静迟，旺相出现速、囚死伏藏迟；阳变阴速、阴变阳迟；动临震巽速、临坎艮迟；用临卯酉速、辰戌迟；初二爻动速、三四犹豫、五动迟、六动更迟；用临太岁不出一年、月将不出一月、日辰本日、日建本时；尹逢头断法

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 快慢：以用爻为主。发动的快，安静的慢；旺相、出现的快，囚、死、伏藏的慢；阳变阴的快，阴变阳的慢；发动临在震、巽的快，临在坎、艮的慢；用爻临卯、酉的快（卯酉是日月出入的门户），临辰、戌的慢（辰戌是天罗地网，是天涯海角）；初爻、二爻发动的快，三爻、四爻发动的犹豫迟疑，五爻发动的慢，六爻发动的更慢。用爻临太岁，不出一年；临月将，不出一月；临日辰，就在当日；临日建，就在当时。尹逢头的断法说：用爻旺相，还要等入墓的日子来定；用爻休囚，在生旺的日子应当成；用爻伏藏，只看用爻当值的日子；发动而被合住的，等冲它的日辰。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Slow or fast. Take the Subject line as the chief. Moving is quick, still is slow; thriving and appearing is quick, imprisoned, dead and hidden is slow; yang changing into yin is quick, yin changing into yang is slow; moving on Zhen or Xun is quick, on Kan or Gen is slow; the Subject on Mao or You is quick (Mao and You are the gates of sun and moon), on Chen or Xu is slow (Chen and Xu are the net of heaven and the snare of earth, the ends of the earth); a first or second line moving is quick, a third or fourth moving hesitates and delays, a fifth moving is slow, and a sixth moving slower still. If the Subject sits on Tai Sui, it is within a year; on the Month General, within a month; on the Day, that same day; on the Day branch, that very hour. The Chuan Yin Feng method of judgment says: if the Subject is thriving, fix it by the day it enters tomb; if resting, it should be done on the day it is born or thriving; if hidden, look only at the day the Subject rules; if moving and held by a combination, wait for the day that clashes it.

> [!note] 按
> 「月将」是太阳所在的月份地支（神煞，不是月建），「日建」是当日的地支，书里没有解释；「天罗地网」是辰、戌两个位置，「天涯海角」比喻远。「尹逢头」是一位断卦的人，书里没有说他是谁，「断法」这里是他的断语。第五、六句的括号小注是对前一句「卯酉」「辰戌」的说明。「动临震巽速」按字面是发动的爻落在震、巽两个卦里。
>
> *Note:* The 'Month General' is the branch of the month in which the sun stands (a spirit-star, not the Month branch), and the 'Day branch' is the branch of the day; the book does not explain them. 'The net of heaven and the snare of earth' are the two positions Chen and Xu, and 'the ends of the earth' is an image of far away. 'Yin Fengtou' is a diviner, and the book does not say who he was; his 'method of judgment' here is his saying. The bracketed notes in the fifth and sixth sentences explain 'Mao and You' and 'Chen and Xu' of the sentence before. 'Moving on Zhen or Xun' is by the letter the moving line falling in the Zhen or Xun trigram.
