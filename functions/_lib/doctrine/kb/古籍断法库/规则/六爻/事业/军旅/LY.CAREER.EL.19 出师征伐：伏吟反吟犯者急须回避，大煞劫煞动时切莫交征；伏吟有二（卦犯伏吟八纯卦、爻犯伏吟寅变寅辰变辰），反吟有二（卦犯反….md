# 出师征伐：伏吟反吟犯者急须回避，大煞劫煞动时切莫交征；伏吟有二（卦犯伏吟八纯卦、爻犯伏吟寅变寅辰变辰），反吟有二（卦犯反吟姤卦及乾巽互变、爻犯反吟），行兵又忌大煞劫煞交重；劫煞起例；伏吟大煞劫煞犯一不祥，无犯方美

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 犯了伏吟、反吟的，急须回避；大煞、劫煞发动的时候，切莫交战。占交战最忌伏吟、反吟。伏吟有两种：卦犯伏吟（如乾见乾、坎见坎一类的八纯卦），爻犯伏吟（如寅变寅、辰变辰一类，随变夬、需变屯就是）。反吟也有两种：卦犯反吟（下巽上乾的姤卦一类，或者乾变巽、巽变乾），爻犯反吟。用兵的时候又忌大煞、劫煞重叠。劫煞的起例：正月逢亥，二月申，三月随蛇（巳），四月寅，五月循环又到亥，周而复始。伏吟、大煞、劫煞，犯一样就不吉祥，都不犯才好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Those who have hit Hidden Chant or Reversed Chant must withdraw urgently; when the great baleful spirit and Robbery move, on no account join battle. In divining for battle, Hidden Chant and Reversed Chant are most to be avoided. Hidden Chant is of two kinds: the hexagram hitting Hidden Chant (the eight pure hexagrams, such as Qian meeting Qian or Kan meeting Kan) and a line hitting Hidden Chant (such as Yin changing into Yin or Chen into Chen, as Sui changing into Guai or Xu into Zhun). Reversed Chant is also of two kinds: the hexagram hitting Reversed Chant (Gou with Xun below and Qian above, and the like, or Qian changing into Xun and Xun into Qian) and a line hitting Reversed Chant. In moving troops one also fears the great baleful spirit and Robbery doubled. The setting out of Robbery: in the first month at Hai, the second Shen, the third following the snake (Si), the fourth Yin, the fifth going round again to Hai, going round and round. Hidden Chant, the great baleful spirit or Robbery, if any one is hit it is inauspicious; to hit none is best.

> [!note] 按
> 伏吟、反吟的定义见术语表；这里书里把它们分成卦与爻两种，卦伏吟是八纯卦，卦反吟是姤一类（下巽上乾）或乾巽互变，爻伏吟是爻变出同一个地支，这些和术语表里的说法是一致的。「地福诀」是把十二地支配八卦方位的歌诀。第十二句里「出征者以上反吟」文字有残缺，译文只照字面写出，不补意思；「天狱煞」是一种神煞。「随变夬、需变屯」是爻伏吟的例子，书里没有展开。
>
> *Note:* Hidden Chant and Reversed Chant are defined in the glossary; here the book divides each into a hexagram kind and a line kind: a hexagram in Hidden Chant is a pure hexagram, a hexagram in Reversed Chant is the Gou kind (Xun below, Qian above) or Qian and Xun exchanging, and a line in Hidden Chant is a line that changes into the same branch, which agree with what the glossary says. The 'Difu rhyme' is a rhyme matching the twelve branches to the directions of the eight trigrams. In the twelfth sentence 'chuzheng zhe yi shang fanyin' is defective, and the translation records it by the letter without supplying a sense; the 'Heavenly Prison baleful spirit' is a spirit-star. 'Sui changing into Guai, Xu into Zhun' are examples of line Hidden Chant that the book does not spell out.
