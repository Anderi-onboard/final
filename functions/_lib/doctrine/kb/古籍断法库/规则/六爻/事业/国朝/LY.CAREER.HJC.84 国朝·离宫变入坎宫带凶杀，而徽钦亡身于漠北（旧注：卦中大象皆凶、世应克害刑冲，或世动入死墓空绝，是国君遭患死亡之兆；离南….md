# 国朝·离宫变入坎宫带凶杀，而徽钦亡身于漠北（旧注：卦中大象皆凶、世应克害刑冲，或世动入死墓空绝，是国君遭患死亡之兆；离南坎北，离化坎由南入北，如宋徽钦二宗被金所掳老死沙漠）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 整个卦象都凶，世爻、应爻互相克害刑冲，或者世爻发动入死、墓、空、绝，是国君遭灾死亡的征兆；离宫变入坎宫，离是南方、坎是北方，由南入北，就像宋朝的徽宗、钦宗被金人掳走，囚在五国城，老死在北方的沙漠地带。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the whole image is bad, the Self and the Other control, harm, punish and clash with each other, or the Self moves into death, tomb, Void or extinction, it is a sign that the ruler will meet calamity and die; a Li-palace hexagram turning into a Kan-palace hexagram goes from south to north (Li is south, Kan is north), like the Song emperors Huizong and Qinzong, who were carried off by the Jin, held at Wuguo City, and grew old and died in the desert land.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5882 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 离宫变入坎宫带凶杀，而徽钦亡身于漠北； | 离宫变入坎宫，又带着凶煞，就像徽宗、钦宗身亡在北方的沙漠； | If a Li-palace hexagram turns into a Kan-palace hexagram and carries baleful spirits, it is like Huizong and Qinzong who lost their lives in the northern desert; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5883 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦中大象皆凶，世应克害刑冲，或世动入死墓空绝，必是国君遭患，死亡之兆。 | 卦中整体的象都是凶的，世爻、应爻互相克害刑冲，或者世爻发动入死、墓、空、绝，必定是国君遭祸患、死亡的征兆。 | If the whole image in the hexagram is bad, and the Self and the Other control, harm, punish and clash with each other, or the Self moves into death, tomb, Void or extinction, it is surely a sign that the ruler will meet calamity and death. |
> | 离乃南方，坎乃北方。 | 离是南方，坎是北方。 | Li is the south and Kan is the north. |
> | 离化坎，乃南入北。 | 离变化为坎，就是从南入北。 | Li turning into Kan is going from south into north. |
> | 故如宋之徽钦二宗，被金兀术所掳，幽于五国城，卒老死于沙漠之地。 | 所以就像宋朝的徽宗、钦宗二帝，被金兀术掳走，囚禁在五国城，最后老死在沙漠地带。 | So it was with the two Song emperors Huizong and Qinzong, who were taken captive by the Jin general Wuzhu, held at Wuguo City, and in the end grew old and died in the desert land. |

^q2
