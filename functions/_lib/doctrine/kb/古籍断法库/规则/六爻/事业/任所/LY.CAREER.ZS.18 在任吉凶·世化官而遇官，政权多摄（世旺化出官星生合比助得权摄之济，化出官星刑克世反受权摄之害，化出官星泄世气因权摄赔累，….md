# 在任吉凶·世化官而遇官，政权多摄（世旺化出官星生合比助得权摄之济，化出官星刑克世反受权摄之害，化出官星泄世气因权摄赔累，官星持世旺又动出官星拱扶亦主多摄）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻变出官星又遇官星，多代理职务：世爻旺相，变出的官星生合、比助世爻，代理有益；变出的官星刑克世爻，代理反受其害；变出的官星泄世爻之气，代理要赔钱受累；官星持世旺相、爻中又发动出官星来拱扶、比合，也主多次代理政务。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self turns into the star of office and meets the star of office again, one often takes charge of the administration in an acting capacity: if the Self is thriving and the star of office it turns into generates, combines with or helps the Self, acting in a post benefits one; if the star of office it turns into punishes or controls the Self, acting in a post brings harm instead; if it drains the Self's strength, one loses money and is burdened by acting; if the star of office holds the Self and is thriving and another star of office moves in the lines to hold up, help or combine with it, one also often takes charge in an acting capacity.

> [!note] 按
> 「权摄」按这一章的用法，是暂时代理别人的职务；书里没有另外解释。
