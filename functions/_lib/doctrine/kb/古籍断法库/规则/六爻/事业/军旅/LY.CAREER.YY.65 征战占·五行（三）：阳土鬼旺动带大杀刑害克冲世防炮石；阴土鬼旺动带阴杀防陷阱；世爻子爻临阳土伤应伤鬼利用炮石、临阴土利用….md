# 征战占·五行（三）：阳土鬼旺动带大杀刑害克冲世防炮石；阴土鬼旺动带阴杀防陷阱；世爻子爻临阳土伤应伤鬼利用炮石、临阴土利用机井；当于应爻鬼爻死墓绝胎之右设伏

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 土看炮石和陷阱：阳土爻的官鬼旺相发动，带大杀来刑、害、克、冲世爻的，防炮石；阴土爻的官鬼旺相发动，带阴杀来冲、刑、克、害世爻的，防陷阱。世爻、子孙爻临阳土而伤应爻、伤官鬼的，利于使用炮石；临阴土而伤应爻与官鬼的，利于使用机关陷阱。应当在应爻、官鬼爻死、墓、绝、胎的右侧设下伏兵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Earth, catapult stones and pits. If a yang Earth Pressure line is thriving and moving, with a great baleful spirit, and comes to punish, harm, control or clash with the Self, guard against catapult stones; if a yin Earth Pressure line is thriving and moving, with a yin baleful spirit, and comes to clash with, punish, control or harm the Self, guard against pits. If the Self and Output sit on yang Earth and injure the Other and Pressure, it is favourable to use catapult stones; if on yin Earth and injuring the Other and Pressure, favourable to use mechanisms and pitfalls. One should set an ambush to the right of where the Other and Pressure lines are in death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage.

## 《易隐·征战占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2033 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining war", base text

> | 阳土鬼旺动，带大杀来刑害克冲世者，防炮石也。 | 阳土爻的官鬼旺相发动，带大杀来刑、害、克、冲世爻的，防炮石。 | If a yang Earth Pressure line is thriving and moving, with a great baleful spirit, and comes to punish, harm, control or clash with the Self, guard against catapult stones. |

> | 世爻子爻临阳土，伤应伤鬼者，利用炮石也。 | 世爻、子孙爻临阳土，伤应爻、伤官鬼的，利于使用炮石。 | If the Self and Output sit on yang Earth and injure the Other and Pressure, it is favourable to use catapult stones. |
> | 临阴土，伤应与鬼者，利用机井也。 | 临阴土，伤应爻与官鬼的，利于使用机关陷阱。 | If on yin Earth and injuring the Other and Pressure, it is favourable to use mechanisms and pitfalls. |
> | 当于应爻鬼爻死墓绝胎之右设伏也。 | 应当在应爻、官鬼爻死、墓、绝、胎的右侧设下伏兵。 | One should set an ambush to the right of where the Other and Pressure lines are in death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「陷井」即陷阱。「阴杀」书里没有解释，是阴性的凶煞；「右」是五个方位里的哪一边，书里没有说是相对于谁，译文照字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Xian jing' is a pit trap. 'Yin baleful spirit' is not explained in the book and is a yin-natured baleful spirit; the book does not say to whose right 'the right' is, and the translation follows the letter.
