# 君位合咸池，唐玄宗受杨妃之祸：世爻五爻带咸池玄武则上必荒淫；卦有咸池而被君爻克则虽有西施之美无害国政；应爻加咸池飞入五爻旺相兼世爻空亡必有武氏乱唐之患；加吉神天下庶几安定更带凶神四海必乱；君位合咸池带杀旺相必有女祸

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻和五爻如果带咸池、玄武，在上的君主必定荒淫；卦里有咸池却被君爻克住，虽有西施那样的美人，也无害于国政；应爻加咸池、飞入五爻旺相，又加上世爻空亡，必有像武氏乱唐那样的祸患，以后必定见到女主治理天下。再加吉神，天下大概可以安定，再带凶神，四海必乱。君位如果合咸池、带杀、旺相，必有女祸，就像唐皇与杨贵妃的故事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self line and the fifth line carry Xianchi and the Dark Tortoise, the ruler above is sure to be dissolute; if the hexagram has Xianchi but it is controlled by the lord's line, then though there is a beauty like Xishi it does no harm to the government; if the Other line carries Xianchi and flies into the fifth line, strong, and the Self line is also Void, there will surely be the calamity of a Lady Wu throwing Tang into disorder, and later a female ruler will surely rule the world. If an auspicious spirit is added the world will perhaps be at peace, and if a baleful spirit is added the four seas are sure to be in disorder. If the lord's place combines with Xianchi and carries a baleful spirit, strong, there is sure to be calamity through a woman, like the story of the Tang emperor and Yang Guifei.
