# 卜筮元龟·求官总断：抑身扶世求官得，天驿相生允不刻，更逢鬼旺讵相疑（例：二月坤之升卦）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求官，压住身、扶助世就能得官；天驿互相生扶，允许不用拖延；再逢官鬼旺相，哪里还有怀疑——例如二月占得坤之升卦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking office, with the Body held down and the Self supported the office is gained; with the Heavenly Post mutually generating, it is promised there will be no delay; and with Pressure also strong how could there be doubt — as for example in the second month the hexagram Kun changing to Sheng.

## 《断易天机·占仕宦第十一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 2668 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 11: Divining official careers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 抑身扶世求官得， | 压住身、扶助世，求官就能得到； | with the Body held down and the Self supported, the office is gained; |
> | 天驿相生允不刻， | 天驿互相生扶，允许不用拖延； | with the Heavenly Post mutually generating, it is promised there will be no delay; |
> | 更逢鬼旺讵相疑， | 再逢官鬼旺相，哪里还有怀疑； | and meeting a strong Pressure besides, how could there be doubt; |
> | 二月坤之升卦是。 | 二月占得坤之升卦就是这样。 | in the second month the hexagram Kun changing to Sheng is such a case. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「抑身扶世」没说是哪一爻压住身、哪一爻扶世；「天驿」没有解释，可能指天喜、驿马，这里照字面译成 Heavenly Post；「允不刻」的「不刻」也没有解释，这里读作「不拖延」，不确定。「卜筮元龟」是断易天机转引的书名。
>
> *Note:* The text does not say which line holds down the Body or supports the Self; 'tianyi' is not explained and may mean the Joy star and the Post Horse, and it is rendered literally as Heavenly Post; the 'bu ke' of 'it is promised not to be ...' is not explained either and is read here as 'no delay', which is uncertain. 'Bushi Yuangui' is the name of a book quoted by the Duanyi Tianji.
