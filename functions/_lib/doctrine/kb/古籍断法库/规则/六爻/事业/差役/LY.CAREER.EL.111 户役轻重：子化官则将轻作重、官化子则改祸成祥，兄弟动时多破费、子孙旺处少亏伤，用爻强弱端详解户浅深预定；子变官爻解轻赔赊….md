# 户役轻重：子化官则将轻作重、官化子则改祸成祥，兄弟动时多破费、子孙旺处少亏伤，用爻强弱端详解户浅深预定；子变官爻解轻赔赊，鬼之子象役大赔微，兄弟发动或持世上定多赔赊，子孙旺相或值身爻费财稍可

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙化成官，就把轻变成重；官化成子孙，就转祸为祥。兄弟发动时，多破费；子孙旺相处，少亏损。要细看用爻的强弱，解户的深浅就预先定了。具体说：子孙变成官爻，解运看着轻，赔补赊欠却有；官鬼变成子孙的象，役大、赔补却小；兄弟发动或者持世，一定多赔补、多赊欠；子孙旺相或者临在身爻上，花费钱财还算可以。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output changes into the star of office, light is made heavy; if the star of office changes into Output, calamity is turned to fortune. When Peer moves there is much expense; where Output thrives there is little loss. Examine the strength of the Subject line, and the depth or shallowness of the conveyor household's burden is settled in advance. In detail: if Output changes into the Pressure line, the conveyance is light but there are shortfalls and arrears; if Pressure changes into the Output image, the service is great but the shortfall is slight; if Peer moves or rests on the Self, there will surely be many shortfalls and arrears; if Output is thriving or rests on the Body line, the cost in wealth is somewhat tolerable.

> | 子孙旺相或值身爻，费财稍可。 | 子孙旺相或者临在身爻上，花费钱财还算可以。 | If Output is thriving or rests on the Body line, the cost in wealth is somewhat tolerable. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「赔赊」是赔补、赊欠（解户押运钱粮，短少的要自己赔补）。「子变官爻，解轻赔赊」「鬼之子象，役大赔微」与诀里「子化官则将轻作重」「官化子则改祸成祥」放在一起读：子化官是外面看着轻、赔补却多，官化子是役虽大、赔补却小；这是按文意的读法，书里没有再解释。「用爻」是官鬼（这一章以官鬼为主）。
>
> *Note:* 'Peishe' is making up shortfalls and arrears (a conveyor household that carries money and grain must make up what falls short out of its own pocket). 'If Output changes into the Pressure line, the conveyance is light but there are shortfalls and arrears' and 'if Pressure changes into the Output image, the service is great but the shortfall is slight' are read together with the verse's 'if Output changes into the star of office, light is made heavy' and 'if the star of office changes into Output, calamity is turned to fortune': Output into Pressure looks light on the outside but the shortfalls are many, and Pressure into Output is a great service but with a small shortfall; this is a reading by the sense and the book does not explain it further. 'Yongyao', the Subject line, is Pressure (the chief of this chapter).
