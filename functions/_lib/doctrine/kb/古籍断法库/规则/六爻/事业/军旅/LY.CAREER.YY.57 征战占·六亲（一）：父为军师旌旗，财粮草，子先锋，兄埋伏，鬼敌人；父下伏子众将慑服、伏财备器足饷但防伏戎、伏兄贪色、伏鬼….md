# 征战占·六亲（一）：父为军师旌旗，财粮草，子先锋，兄埋伏，鬼敌人；父下伏子众将慑服、伏财备器足饷但防伏戎、伏兄贪色、伏鬼智短；父临衰墓老、生旺少壮、胎养不威、刑害不恤众；父旺外动彼兴兵、内动我出师；世持父动将不惜士

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六亲配军中的人和物，以及军师：父爻是军师，也是旌旗；财爻是粮草，子孙是先锋，兄弟是埋伏，官鬼是敌人。父爻下面伏着子孙，众将慑服，擒纵如意；伏着财爻，器械齐备粮饷充足、谋略周全，但要防左右的伏兵；伏着兄爻，军师贪财好色、狎侮身边的人；伏着官鬼，智谋短、计策拙，一行动就出疏漏。父爻临衰、墓，军师年老；临生旺，军师年轻力壮；临胎、养，不威严持重；临刑、害，不体恤众人。父爻旺相在外卦发动，对方兴兵；在内卦发动，我方出师；带白虎、凶煞在外卦发动，作战失利；在外卦发动克世爻，他来侵犯；克应爻，他自己退去。世爻持父爻发动，主将不爱惜士卒，再加大杀、劫杀发动，不爱惜士卒还要防兵变。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Assigning the six kin to people and things in the army, and the strategist. The Resource line is the military adviser and also the banners; Wealth is provisions, Output the vanguard, Peer the ambush, and Pressure the enemy. If Output lies hidden under Resource, the generals are cowed and capture and release go as wished; if Wealth, arms are ready and rations plentiful and plans complete, but guard against hidden troops on both sides; if Peer, the adviser is greedy for money and women and treats those around him with contempt; if Pressure, his wit is short and his schemes clumsy, and any move shows a flaw. If Resource sits on weak or tomb, the adviser is old; on born or thriving, young and strong; on Tai or Yang, not awe-inspiring; on punishment or harm, he has no care for the many. If Resource is thriving and moves in the outer trigram, the enemy raises troops; in the inner trigram, I send out troops; with the Tiger and baleful spirits moving outside, the battle goes badly; moving outside to control the Self, they come to invade; controlling the Other, they withdraw of themselves. If the Self holds Resource and it moves, the commander does not spare his men, and with great baleful spirits and Robbery added and moving, he spares not his men and one must guard against mutiny.

> [!note] 按
> 「慑服」是畏惧而服从；「擒纵如意」是抓住还是放走都由自己；「伏戎」是埋伏的兵；「老髦」是年老的人；「狎侮」是亲昵而轻慢。第一句的「父为军师，为旌旗」是后面讲旌旗（YY.58）的依据。
>
> *Note:* 'Shefu' is submitting from fear; 'qinzong ruyi' is that catching or letting go is entirely at one's will; 'fuyong' are troops lying in ambush; 'laomao' is an old man; 'xiawu' is being familiar and disrespectful. The first sentence, 'Resource is the adviser and also the banners', is the basis for the section on banners which follows (YY.58).
