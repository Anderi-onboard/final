# 在任防参罚与离任虑后患：在任而防参罚则恶子动而好官兴、世空则离任；离任而虑后患则好子动而恶官兴、世空则无事

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任上防被参劾、处罚的，厌子孙动而喜官旺，世空就是要离任；离任后担心留下后患的，喜子孙动而厌官旺，世空就没有事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For one in office guarding against impeachment and penalty, Output moving is hated and Pressure rising is welcome, and the Self Void means leaving the post; for one who has left office and fears trouble afterwards, Output moving is welcome and Pressure rising is hated, and the Self Void means no trouble.

## 《易冒·功名章第六十二》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1435 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 62: Fame and office", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 夫在任而防参罚，恶子动而好官兴，世空则离任。 | 在任上防被参劾、处罚的，厌子动而喜官旺，世空就是要离任； | For one in office guarding against impeachment and penalty, Output moving is hated and Pressure rising is welcome, and the Self Void means leaving the post; |
> | 离任而虑后患，好子动而恶官兴，世空则无事。 | 离任后担心留下后患的，喜子动而厌官旺，世空就没有事。 | for one who has left office and fears trouble afterwards, Output moving is welcome and Pressure rising is hated, and the Self Void means no trouble. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「参罚」是被上司参劾、处罚。「恶」是厌，「好」是喜：在任上怕被参，所以厌子孙动（子孙克官、伤官）而喜官旺；离任之后怕被追究后患，反过来喜子孙动（子孙克官，官不来追）而厌官旺。「世空则离任」「世空则无事」是同一个世空在两种处境下的不同结果：在任时世空就是要离开，离任后世空就是没有事。这一条是同一组爻神在『在任』和『离任』两个处境下取向相反的例子；黄金策仕宦的子孙交重（HJC.03）只讲了在任的一面。
>
> *Note:* 'Canfa' is being impeached and penalised by a superior. 'E' is dislike and 'hao' is liking: in office one fears impeachment, so Output moving (Output controls Pressure and injures the office) is hated and thriving Pressure welcome; after leaving office one fears being called to account for what is left behind, and the other way round Output moving (Output controls Pressure, so the office does not come after one) is welcome and thriving Pressure is hated. 'The Self Void means leaving the post' and 'the Self Void means no trouble' are different results of the same Void Self in two situations: while in office a Void Self means leaving, and after leaving it means nothing happens. This entry is an example of the same set of lines leaning opposite ways in the two situations of being in office and having left; the Huangjin Ce on Output moving (HJC.03) speaks only of the in-office side.
