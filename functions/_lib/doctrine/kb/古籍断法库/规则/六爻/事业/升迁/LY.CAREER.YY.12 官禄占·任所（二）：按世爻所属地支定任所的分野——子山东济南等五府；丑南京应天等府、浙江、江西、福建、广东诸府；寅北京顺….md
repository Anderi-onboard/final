# 官禄占·任所（二）：按世爻所属地支定任所的分野——子山东济南等五府；丑南京应天等府、浙江、江西、福建、广东诸府；寅北京顺天等府与辽东；卯南京徐州淮安；辰山东兖州；巳湖广、四川夔州、贵州、广东廉州、广西诸府；午河南河南南阳、湖广郧阳襄阳德安；未陕西、甘肃、四川、云南、贵州诸府；申山西太原平阳等；酉北直真定顺德、山西大同；戌南京凤阳庐州与徐州；亥河南开封等与北直大名

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 任所在什么地方，按世爻所属的地支和星宿分野来定：世属子，是坎宫、女宿虚宿危宿三宿的分野，今山东济南等五府；世属丑，是艮宫、牛宿斗宿的分野，今南京的应天、扬州等府，浙江、江西、福建、广东诸府；世属寅，是艮宫、尾宿箕宿的分野，今北京顺天等府和辽东沈阳；世属卯，是震宫、氐房心三宿的分野，今南京徐州、淮安；世属辰，是巽宫、角亢二宿的分野，今山东兖州府；世属巳，是巽宫、翼轸二宿的分野，今湖广诸府、四川夔州府、贵州铜仁黎平、广东廉州、广西诸府；世属午，是离宫、柳星张三宿的分野，今河南河南府、南阳府和湖广郧阳、襄阳、德安三府；世属未，是坤宫、井鬼二宿的分野，今陕西、甘肃、四川、云南、贵州的诸府州；世属申，是坤宫、毕觜参三宿的分野，今山西太原、平阳等地；世属酉，是兑宫、胃昴二宿的分野，今北直真定、顺德二府和山西大同府；世属戌，是乾宫、奎娄二宿的分野，今南京凤阳、庐州二府和徐州；世属亥，是乾宫、室壁二宿的分野，今河南开封、彰德、卫辉、怀庆、汝宁等府和北直大名府。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The region of the post is fixed by the branch the Self belongs to and the allotted lunar mansions: Zi is the Kan palace and the mansions Nü, Xu and Wei, today five prefectures of Shandong; Chou is the Gen palace and the mansions Niu and Dou, today the prefectures of the southern capital region such as Yingtian and Yangzhou and those of Zhejiang, Jiangxi, Fujian and Guangdong; Yin is the Gen palace and the mansions Wei (Tail) and Ji, today the prefectures of Beijing such as Shuntian, with Liaodong and Shenyang; Mao is the Zhen palace and the mansions Di, Fang and Xin, today Xuzhou and Huai'an in the southern capital region; Chen is the Xun palace and the mansions Jiao and Kang, today Yanzhou prefecture in Shandong; Si is the Xun palace and the mansions Yi and Zhen, today the prefectures of Huguang, Kuizhou in Sichuan, Tongren and Liping in Guizhou, Lianzhou in Guangdong and the prefectures of Guangxi; Wu is the Li palace and the mansions Liu, Xing and Zhang, today Henan and Nanyang prefectures in Henan and Yunyang, Xiangyang and De'an in Huguang; Wei is the Kun palace and the mansions Jing and Gui, today the prefectures and departments of Shaanxi, Gansu, Sichuan, Yunnan and Guizhou; Shen is the Kun palace and the mansions Bi, Zi and Shen, today Taiyuan, Pingyang and other places in Shanxi; You is the Dui palace and the mansions Wei (Stomach) and Mao, today Zhending and Shunde in the northern metropolitan region and Datong prefecture in Shanxi; Xu is the Qian palace and the mansions Kui and Lou, today Fengyang and Luzhou in the southern capital region and Xuzhou; Hai is the Qian palace and the mansions Shi and Bi, today Kaifeng, Zhangde, Weihui, Huaiqing and Runing in Henan and Daming prefecture in the northern metropolitan region.

> [!note] 按
> 这是明代的府州名，按分野（把天上二十八宿对应地上区域）排的。原文有几处地名和星宿名像是形近误字，译文照原文拼写，不改：卫州（浙江的一府，应是衢州）、河涧（应是河间）、氏（应是氐宿）、虁州（夔州）、卢州（一处在四川，一处在南京，应分别是泸州和庐州）、卯州、渣州、胃昂（应是胃昴）、室璧（应是室壁）。「北直」是北直隶，「南京」指南直隶。
>
> *Note:* These are Ming prefecture and department names arranged by 'fenye', the matching of the twenty-eight lunar mansions in the sky to regions of the earth. Several place and mansion names in the text look like miscopied look-alike characters, and the translation follows the spelling in the text without emending: Weizhou (a Zhejiang prefecture, presumably Quzhou), Hejian, 'Shi' for the mansion Di, Kuizhou, Luzhou (once in Sichuan and once in the southern capital region, presumably Luzhou and Luzhou with different characters), Maozhou, Zhazhou, 'Ang' for the mansion Mao and 'Bi' for the mansion Bi (Wall). 'Beizhi' is the northern metropolitan region and 'Nanjing' the southern one.
