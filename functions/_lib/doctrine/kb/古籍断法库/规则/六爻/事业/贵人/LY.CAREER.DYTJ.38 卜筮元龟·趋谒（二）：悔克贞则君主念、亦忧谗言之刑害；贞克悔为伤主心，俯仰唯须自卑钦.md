# 卜筮元龟·趋谒（二）：悔克贞则君主念、亦忧谗言之刑害；贞克悔为伤主心，俯仰唯须自卑钦

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 悔（外卦）克贞（内卦），是说主人惦念你，但也要担忧谗言带来的刑害；贞克悔，是伤了主人的心，进退俯仰只须自己谦卑恭敬。注文说：终究不如相生好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The outer trigram controlling the inner means the host is thinking of you, but worry too about punishment and harm through slander; the inner controlling the outer means one has hurt the host's heart, and in every bearing one must humble oneself and show respect. The note says: in the end it is not as good as mutual generation.
