# 求进职·谋干事看世上有无文书：科举功名求进职皆取官多、旺相必得；凡占赴试、谒贵、面君、参官、到部谋干事，看世上有无文书——父母旺相可许、官爻旺相便吉、忌子孙持世；占赴任子孙持世或独发必不满任；看世上文书者，因专用官为主、用爻为辅

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 科举功名，是为了求进职，都以官爻为主，旺相就一定得到。凡是占赴考、谒见贵人、面见君主、参谒官员、到部里谋办事情的，看世爻上有没有文书（父母爻）：父母旺相，可以允许；官爻旺相，就吉；忌子孙持世，不中。占赴任，子孙持世或独发，必定做不满任期。为什么要看世爻上有没有文书？因为这是专以官为主、用爻为辅，所以要父母在世爻上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Examinations and a name in office are for seeking advancement in post, and the Pressure line is taken as the main one in all of them; if it is thriving it is certainly obtained. In divining about going to an examination, calling on a great man, seeing the sovereign, attending an official, or going to a ministry to see to business, look at whether there is the document line, Resource, on the Self: if the Resource line is thriving, it may be granted; if the Pressure line is thriving, it is auspicious; Output holding the Self is feared, and it does not hit the mark. In divining about going to take up a post, if Output holds the Self or moves alone, one will surely not complete the term. Why look at whether there is Resource on the Self? Because this takes the official alone as the master and the Subject line as the aid, and so one wants Resource on the Self.

> | 答曰：此专用官为主，用爻为辅，所以要父母在世上也。 | 答：这是专以官为主、用爻为辅，所以要父母在世爻上。 | The answer: this takes the official alone as the master and the Subject line as the aid, and so one wants Resource on the Self. |

> [!note] 按
> 「求进职」是求升官取职。「赴试、谒贵、面君、参官、到部谋干事」是一串求上进的事：赴试归考试域，其余（谒见贵人、面君、参谒官员、到部门谋办事情）归事业。「文书」是父母爻。「世上」是世爻上。「专用官为主，用爻为辅」是说这类占问官爻是主角，所求的那件事的用爻只是辅助，所以文书（父母）要在世爻上扶持。「赴任」是上任。「不满任」是做不满一个任期。第三段末尾「以文书为主，要文书持世无刑克太岁贴身，必作状元」是专讲考中状元的，另收入考试域，这里不取。这一节同时是考试域的，该域另收。参看易林补遗趋谒贵人（EL.99–102）。
>
> *Note:* 'Qiu jin zhi' is seeking promotion and a post. 'Going to an examination, calling on a great man, seeing the sovereign, attending an official, going to a ministry to see to business' is a string of matters of seeking advancement: going to an examination belongs to Examinations, and the rest (calling on a great man, seeing the sovereign, attending an official, going to a department to see to business) belong to Career. 'Wenshu' is the Resource line. 'On the Self' is on the Self line. 'The official alone as the master and the Subject line as the aid' says that in this kind of divination the Pressure line is the principal and the Subject line of the matter sought is only the aid, so Resource must support from the Self. 'Fu ren' is taking up a post, and 'bu man ren' is not completing a term. The sentence at the end of the third section, 'taking Resource as the master, one wants Resource to hold the Self, with no punishment or control and the Tai Sui attached to the body, and one will surely be the top graduate', speaks only of coming first in the examination, is taken under Examinations and not here. This section is also one for Examinations, which takes it separately. See the Yilin Buyi on calling on the great (EL.99-102).
