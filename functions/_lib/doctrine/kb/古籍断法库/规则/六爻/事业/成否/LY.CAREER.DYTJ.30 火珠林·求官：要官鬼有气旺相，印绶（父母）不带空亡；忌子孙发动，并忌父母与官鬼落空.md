# 火珠林·求官：要官鬼有气旺相，印绶（父母）不带空亡；忌子孙发动，并忌父母与官鬼落空

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求官，官鬼要有气、旺相，印绶（父母爻）不带空亡；忌讳子孙发动，也忌父母和官鬼落空。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking office, Pressure must have qi and be strong and the seal and ribbon (the Resource line) must not carry Void; Output moving is to be avoided, and so are Resource and Pressure falling void.

## 《断易天机·占仕宦第十一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 2731 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 11: Divining official careers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 要官鬼有气旺相， | 官鬼要有气、旺相， | Pressure must have qi and be strong, |
> | 并印绶不带空亡， | 并且印绶不带空亡； | and the seal and ribbon must not carry Void; |
> | 印绶者父母爻也。 | 印绶，就是父母爻。 | the seal and ribbon are the Resource line. |
> | 忌子孙发动， | 忌讳子孙发动， | Output moving is to be avoided, |
> | 并父母与官鬼落空也。 | 以及父母和官鬼落空。 | as well as Resource and Pressure falling void. |
