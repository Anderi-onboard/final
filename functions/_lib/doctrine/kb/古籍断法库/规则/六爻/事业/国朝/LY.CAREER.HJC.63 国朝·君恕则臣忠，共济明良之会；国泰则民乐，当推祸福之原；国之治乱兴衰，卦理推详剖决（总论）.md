# 国朝·君恕则臣忠，共济明良之会；国泰则民乐，当推祸福之原；国之治乱兴衰，卦理推详剖决（总论）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占国家的兴衰，是把整个卦当作国运来读。国家有始有终，必有兴有废；国君宽厚，臣子就忠心；国家太平，百姓就安乐；这些祸福的来由，都可以用卦理推到根上、剖开判决。哪一爻代表什么，见下一条。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A question about the fortunes of a state reads the whole hexagram as the fortune of the realm. A state has a beginning and an end and must rise and fall; when the ruler is forgiving the ministers are loyal, and when the state is at peace the people are happy; the source of such blessings and disasters can be traced and decided by the principles of the hexagram. Which line stands for what is in the next rule.

> | 国泰则民乐，当推祸福之原。 | 国家太平，百姓就安乐，要推究的就是祸福产生的根源。 | when the state is at peace the people are happy, and what is to be traced is the source of disaster and fortune. |
> | 虽天地尚知其始终，况国家岂能无兴废？ | 连天地尚且知道有它的开始和终结，何况一个国家，怎么可能没有兴盛和衰亡？ | Even heaven and earth are known to have a beginning and an end, so how could a state be without rise and fall? |
