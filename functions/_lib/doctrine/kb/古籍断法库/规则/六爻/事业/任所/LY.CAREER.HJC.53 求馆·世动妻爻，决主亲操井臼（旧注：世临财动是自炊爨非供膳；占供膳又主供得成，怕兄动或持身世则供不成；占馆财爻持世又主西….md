# 求馆·世动妻爻，决主亲操井臼（旧注：世临财动是自炊爨非供膳；占供膳又主供得成，怕兄动或持身世则供不成；占馆财爻持世又主西席家眷同至）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻临着财爻发动，是自己做饭，不是东家供给膳食；如果占的是供膳，又主供得成，只怕兄弟发动，或者虽然不动而持身世的，都主供不成；大凡占馆，遇到财爻持世，又主先生的家眷一同到馆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self rests on Wealth and it moves, one cooks for oneself and meals are not provided by the host; if one divines whether meals will be provided, it also means they will be provided, and what is feared is Peer moving, or Peer holding the Self or the Body even if it does not move, in all of which cases they will not be provided; in general in divining a school post, Wealth holding the Self also means the teacher's family comes along.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2785 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 世临财动，乃自炊爨，非供赡也。 | 世爻临着财爻发动，是自己做饭，不是供给膳食。 | If the Self rests on Wealth and it moves, one cooks for oneself, and meals are not provided. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「井臼」是汲水（井）和舂米（臼），代指家务、自己做饭。「供膳」是东家供给先生饭食。同一个「财临世」在这里有两面：不问供膳时是自己做饭、家眷同来；占供膳时是供得成。
>
> *Note:* 'Drawing water and pounding rice' is household chores, meaning cooking for oneself. 'Provide meals' is the host supplying the teacher's food. The same 'Wealth on the Self' has two faces here: when meals are not what is asked, it is cooking for oneself and the family coming along; when meals are asked, it is that they are provided.
