# 出师征伐：福带青龙摇艮丙进生门人获全功，鬼临白虎动乾中纵入开门未能取胜；离家子动宜步景门，巽土官兴杜门莫往

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 福神带青龙在艮宫丙位摇动，如果进生门，人获全功；官鬼临白虎在乾宫中发动，纵然进开门也未能取胜。离宫子孙发动，宜走景门；巽宫土爻官鬼兴起，不要往杜门去。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the fortune spirit carrying the Azure Dragon stirs at Bing in Gen and one enters the Life gate, one wins complete success; if Pressure on the White Tiger moves in Qian, even entering the Open gate one cannot win. If Output moves in Li, it is fit to take the Scene gate; if an Earth Pressure line rises in Xun, do not go by the Block gate.

> [!note] 按
> 「艮丙」是艮卦纳的天干丙（艮纳丙）；「摇」是发动。这两句是 EL.15 那首诗的举例。
>
> *Note:* 'Gen Bing' is the stem Bing that Gen takes in the Najia system (Gen takes Bing); 'yao' is to move. The two sentences are illustrations of the verse in EL.15.
