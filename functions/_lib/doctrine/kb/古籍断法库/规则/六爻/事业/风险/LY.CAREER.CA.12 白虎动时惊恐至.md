# 白虎动时惊恐至

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 白虎爻旺相发动，惊恐的事马上就到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the White Tiger line strong and moving, fright and alarm arrive at once.

## 《阐奥歌章·仕宦章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3759 行 · Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on holding office", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 白虎动时惊恐至， | 白虎发动的时候，惊恐就来了； | When the White Tiger moves, fright and alarm come; |

^q1
