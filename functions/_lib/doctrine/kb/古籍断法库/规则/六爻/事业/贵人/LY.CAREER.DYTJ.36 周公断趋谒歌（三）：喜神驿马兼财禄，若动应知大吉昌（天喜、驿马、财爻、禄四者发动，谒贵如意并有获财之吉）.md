# 周公断趋谒歌（三）：喜神驿马兼财禄，若动应知大吉昌（天喜、驿马、财爻、禄四者发动，谒贵如意并有获财之吉）

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 喜神、驿马兼有财、禄，如果发动，就知道大吉昌：天喜、驿马、财爻和禄这四样发动，谒见贵人如意，并且有得财的吉利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Joy spirit and the Post Horse together with Wealth and the Emolument, if they move, know that it is greatly auspicious: if the Joy star, the Post Horse, the Wealth line and the Emolument, these four, move, the call on the noble goes as one wishes and there is also the good fortune of gaining wealth.
