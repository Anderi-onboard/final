# 求馆·世如变鬼，央人转荐于东家（旧注：鬼爻出现而世又化鬼须再得推荐乃成；卦无官而动爻化出者初无人荐亦待央人荐之；鬼化鬼父化父反复难成）

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻出现而世爻又化出官鬼的，必须再得到推荐才能成；卦里没有官鬼而动爻化出官鬼的，当初没有人推荐，也必须等央求人去推荐才能成；官鬼化官鬼、父母化父母，又主反复难成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure appears and the Self also turns into Pressure, one must obtain a further recommendation before it can come through; if there is no Pressure in the hexagram but a moving line turns into Pressure, no one recommended one at first and one must wait for someone to be begged to recommend one before it can come through; if Pressure turns into Pressure or Resource into Resource, it also means going back and forth and being hard to bring about.

> | 卦无官而动爻有化出者，初无人荐，亦必央人荐之可成也。 | 卦里没有官鬼而动爻有化出官鬼的，当初没有人推荐，也必须央求人推荐才能成。 | if there is no Pressure in the hexagram but a moving line turns into Pressure, no one recommended one at first and one must beg someone to recommend one before it can come through. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「转荐」是荐馆人再向东家转荐一次，等于多一层中间人。旧注最后一句「鬼化鬼、父化父，又主反复难成」（和 LY.CAREER.HJC.35 里「父化父有两处书馆」不同，那里是吉），王洪绪没有。
>
> *Note:* 'Recommend again' means the recommender passes one on to the host a second time, one more level of go-between. The old commentary's last sentence, 'Pressure turning into Pressure or Resource into Resource also means going back and forth and being hard to bring about' (different from 'Resource turning into Resource means two schools' in LY.CAREER.HJC.35, where it is favourable), is not in Wang Hongxu.
