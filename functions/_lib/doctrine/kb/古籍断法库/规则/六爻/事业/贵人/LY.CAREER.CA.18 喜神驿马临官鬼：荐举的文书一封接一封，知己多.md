# 喜神驿马临官鬼：荐举的文书一封接一封，知己多

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 喜神、驿马落在官鬼上，荐举的文书一封接一封，知己很多。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Joy spirit and the Post Horse on Pressure, recommending letters come one after another and those who know you are many.

## 《阐奥歌章·仕宦章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3777 行 · Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on holding office", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 喜神驿马临官鬼， | 喜神、驿马落在官鬼上， | The Joy spirit and the Post Horse on Pressure, |
> | 美削重重有知己。 | 荐举的文书一封又一封，有知己。 | recommending letters follow one another, and there are those who know you. |
