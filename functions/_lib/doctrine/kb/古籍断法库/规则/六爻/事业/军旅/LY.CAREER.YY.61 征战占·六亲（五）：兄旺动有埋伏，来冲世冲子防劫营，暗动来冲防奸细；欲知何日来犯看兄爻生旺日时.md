# 征战占·六亲（五）：兄旺动有埋伏，来冲世冲子防劫营，暗动来冲防奸细；欲知何日来犯看兄爻生旺日时

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟看埋伏：兄爻旺相发动，有埋伏；来冲世爻、冲子孙的，防劫营；兄爻暗动来冲的，防奸细。想知道哪天来进犯，就看兄爻生旺的日子、时辰。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer and ambush. If the Peer line is thriving and moving, there is an ambush; if it comes to clash with the Self or Output, guard against a raid on the camp; if it moves in secret and comes to clash, guard against spies. To know on what day they will attack, look at the day or hour when the Peer line is born or thriving.
