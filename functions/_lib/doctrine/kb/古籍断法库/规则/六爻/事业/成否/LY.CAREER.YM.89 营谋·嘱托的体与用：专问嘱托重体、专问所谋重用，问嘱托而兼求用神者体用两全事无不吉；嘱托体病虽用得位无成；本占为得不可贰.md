# 营谋·嘱托的体与用：专问嘱托重体、专问所谋重用，问嘱托而兼求用神者体用两全事无不吉；嘱托体病虽用得位无成；本占为得不可贰

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是专问嘱托的，重体；专问所谋的事的，重用；问嘱托而又兼求用神的，体用两全，事情没有不吉的。然而嘱托的体有病，即使用神得位也不成。所以以眼前这一占为得，不可有二心。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When one asks only about the entrusting, the body counts for most; when one asks only about the matter schemed for, the use counts for most; when one asks about the entrusting and also seeks the Subject, with body and use both whole, there is no matter that is not auspicious. But if the body of the entrusting is sick, even if the Subject is well placed it does not come off. So the divination at hand is what is to be taken, and one must not be of two minds.

> [!note] 按
> 「体」是受托的这个人所对应的爻（他肯不肯出力），「用」是所谋的这件事的用神（谋文用父、谋利用财等，见 YM.86）。「专问嘱托则重体」是只问托人这件事，看托的那个人；「专问所谋则重用」是只问事情本身，看用神；两样都问，就体用两全才吉。「嘱托体病，虽用得位无成」是托的人有病（空、破、克世等），事情的用神再好也成不了：人靠不住，事就落空。「本占为得，不可贰」是以所占的这一件为准，不要同时拿两件事去凑。
>
> *Note:* The 'body' is the line that corresponds to the person entrusted (whether he is willing to exert himself), and the 'use' is the Subject of the matter schemed for (documents by the father line, profit by Wealth, and so on, see YM.86). 'Asking only about the entrusting counts the body' is asking only the matter of entrusting someone, looking at the person entrusted; 'asking only about the matter schemed for counts the use' is asking only about the matter itself, looking at the Subject; if both are asked, it is auspicious only when body and use are both whole. 'If the body of the entrusting is sick, even if the Subject is well placed it does not come off' is that if the person entrusted is ailing (Void, broken, controlling the Self and so on), however good the Subject of the matter, it cannot come off: if the man cannot be relied on, the matter comes to nothing. 'The divination at hand is what is to be taken, and one must not be of two minds' is to go by the one matter divined and not to piece two matters together at once.
