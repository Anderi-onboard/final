# 援例·财静子飞空用意（子孙持世，财爻动不动皆不可行；财临空破亦不可行；子财官同动又可行，因子动生财、财动生官）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 援例捐官，子孙持世，不管财爻动不动，都不可以去办；财爻落空落破，也不可以去办；但子孙、财、官一起发动又可以去办，因为子孙动生财、财动生官。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In buying office by precedent, if Output holds the Self, whether or not the Wealth line moves it should not be done; if Wealth falls in Void or break, it should not be done either; but if Output, Wealth and the star of office all move together, it may be done, because Output moves to generate Wealth and Wealth moves to generate the star of office.
