# 征战占·五行（四）：金旺兵甲新加龙雀虎光明耀日，金衰兵甲旧加勾蛇武钝敝；金胎墓弓橐戈戢；金动将战斗，金动带刑刃战必伤人，金空动征鼓齐鸣兵刃既接

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金看兵甲：金爻旺相，兵器铠甲新，加青龙、朱雀、白虎的，光亮耀眼；金爻衰弱，兵甲旧，加勾陈、螣蛇、玄武的，钝坏没有光彩；金临胎、墓，弓入囊、戈收起（休兵）；金发动，将要打仗；金发动带刑、刃的，打起来必定伤人；金落空而发动，战鼓齐鸣，兵刃已经相接。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Metal and arms. If Metal is thriving, weapons and armour are new, and with the Azure Dragon, the Bird or the Tiger added they are bright and dazzling; if Metal is weak, arms and armour are old, and with Hook Earth, the Snake or the Tortoise added they are blunt, worn and colourless; Metal on Tai or tomb, bows are put in their cases and spears put away; if Metal moves, a battle is about to be fought; if Metal moves carrying punishment and the blade, the fight will surely wound men; if Metal is Void and moves, war drums sound together and blades have already met.

> [!note] 按
> 「龙雀虎」「勾蛇武」是青龙、朱雀、白虎和勾陈、螣蛇、玄武的省称，前一组好、后一组坏。「弓橐戈戢」出自《诗·时迈》「载戢干戈，载櫜弓矢」，意思是收起兵器、不再用兵。
>
> *Note:* 'Long que hu' and 'gou she wu' are abbreviations for the Azure Dragon, the Vermilion Bird and the White Tiger and for Hook Earth, the Coiling Snake and the Dark Tortoise, the first group good and the second bad. 'Gong tuo ge ji' comes from the Odes, 'Shi mai', 'they put away shield and spear, they sheathe bow and arrow', meaning that the weapons are put away and the army rests.
