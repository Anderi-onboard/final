# 仕宦·兴隆官鬼，定居一品之尊（旧注：鬼为官爵，旺相官高爵大、休囚死绝官小职卑；发动生合世爻、得月日生扶必有升擢；安静被克害无声名）

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼代表官职和爵位：旺相有气，官高爵大；休囚死绝，官小职卑；发动去生合世爻，或者得到月建、日辰的生扶，一定有升迁提拔，名声远播京城；安静不动而被月建、日辰克害，或者被世爻刑冲克害，一定没有声名威望。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure stands for office and rank: thriving and with breath, the office is high and the rank great; resting, trapped, dead or in extinction, the office is small and the post low; moving to generate or combine with the Self, or generated and supported by the Month or the Day, there will surely be promotion and the name spreads far to the capital; still, and controlled or harmed by the Month or the Day, or punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Self, there will surely be no fame or standing.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1926–1928 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼旺相，官高爵大，休囚死绝，官小职卑。 | 官鬼旺相的，官位高、爵位大；休囚死绝的，官位小、职务低。 | When Pressure is thriving the office is high and the rank great; resting, trapped, dead or in extinction, the office is small and the post low. |
> | 若发动生合世爻，得月建日辰生扶，必有荐擢。 | 如果发动生合世爻，得到月建、日辰生扶，一定有人举荐提拔。 | If it moves to generate or combine with the Self and is generated and supported by the Month and the Day, there will surely be recommendation and promotion. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「一品」是官阶里最高的一级，「定居一品之尊」是形容官鬼旺相时最好的情形，不是说一定做到一品。「声名闻望」是名声和威望。旧注说的「升擢，远播都下」，卜筮正宗改成「荐擢」（有人举荐提拔），意思略有不同：旧注是升迁加名声远传，王洪绪这里强调的是被举荐。
>
> *Note:* 'The first rank' is the highest grade of office, and 'one will surely hold the honoured place of the first rank' describes the best case when Pressure is thriving, and does not say that one will certainly reach the first rank. 'Fame and standing' is name and reputation. Where the old commentary says 'promotion, and the name spreads far to the capital', the Bushi Zhengzong changes it to 'recommendation and promotion' (someone recommends and raises one), a slightly different sense: the old commentary has promotion and a far-spreading name, and Wang Hongxu stresses being recommended.
