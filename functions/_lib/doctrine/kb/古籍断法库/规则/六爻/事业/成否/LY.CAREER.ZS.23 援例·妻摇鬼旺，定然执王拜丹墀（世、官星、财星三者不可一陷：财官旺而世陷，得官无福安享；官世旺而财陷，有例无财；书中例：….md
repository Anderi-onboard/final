# 援例·妻摇鬼旺，定然执王拜丹墀（世、官星、财星三者不可一陷：财官旺而世陷，得官无福安享；官世旺而财陷，有例无财；书中例：豫之否，财动生官世临月破，名成而身丧；困之兑，世休囚动变鬼，次年得噎疾而终）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 援例捐官，世爻、官星、财星这三样不能有一样失陷：妻财、官星都旺而世爻失陷，终究得了官也没有福气安享；官星、世爻都旺而财星失陷，是有例可援却没有钱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In buying office by precedent, of the Self, the star of office and the star of Wealth, not one may be lost: if Wealth and the star of office are both thriving but the Self is lost, one may in the end get office but has no fortune to enjoy it in peace; if the star of office and the Self are both thriving but the star of Wealth is lost, there is a precedent to appeal to but no money.

> | 妻摇鬼旺，定然执王拜丹墀。 | 妻财摇动、官鬼旺相，必定能上殿朝拜、得官。 | When Wealth sways and Pressure thrives, one will surely bow at the vermilion steps of the court. |
> | 世与官星、财星，三者不可一陷，若财官两旺，而世爻失陷者，终使得官，无福安享； | 世爻、官星、财星这三样不能有一样失陷；如果财、官都旺，而世爻失陷，终究得了官，也没有福气安享； | Of the Self, the star of office and the star of Wealth, not one may be lost: if Wealth and the star of office are both thriving but the Self is lost, one may in the end get office but has no fortune to enjoy it in peace; |
> | 若官世两旺，财星失陷者，乃有例无财。 | 如果官星、世爻都旺，而财星失陷，就是有例可援而没有钱。 | if the star of office and the Self are both thriving but the star of Wealth is lost, there is a precedent to appeal to but no money. |

> [!note] 按
> 「执王拜丹墀」原文如此，意思是上殿朝拜、得官；「王」字可能是「玉」（手执玉圭）的误字，译文只按意思译。「捐复」是出钱捐纳恢复官职。
>
> *Note:* 'Holding the king and bowing at the vermilion steps' is how the text reads and means bowing at court and gaining office; the character 'king' may be a slip for 'jade' (holding the jade tablet), and the translation follows the sense only. 'Juanfu' is buying reinstatement in office with money.
