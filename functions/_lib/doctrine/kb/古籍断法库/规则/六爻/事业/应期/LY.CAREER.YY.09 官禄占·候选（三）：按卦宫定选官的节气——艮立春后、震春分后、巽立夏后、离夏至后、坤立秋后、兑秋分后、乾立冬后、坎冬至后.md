# 官禄占·候选（三）：按卦宫定选官的节气——艮立春后、震春分后、巽立夏后、离夏至后、坤立秋后、兑秋分后、乾立冬后、坎冬至后

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 再看卦宫来定选官的节气：卦宫是艮的，立春以后选；震，春分以后；巽，立夏以后；离，夏至以后；坤，立秋以后；兑，秋分以后；乾，立冬以后；坎，冬至以后。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Look also at the palace of the hexagram to fix the solar term of the selection: if the palace is Gen, the selection comes after the start of spring; Zhen, after the spring equinox; Xun, after the start of summer; Li, after the summer solstice; Kun, after the start of autumn; Dui, after the autumn equinox; Qian, after the start of winter; Kan, after the winter solstice.
