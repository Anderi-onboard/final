# 出师征伐：福化文书兵必骄而后悔无及，子之兄弟将虽弱而大获全功；父母为子之大忌，子变父则用变忌神，克伐如雪狮向阳初张威势日渐消，大有刑伤噬脐无及；兄弟为子之元神，子化兄则用化元神扶助，如猛虎添翼百战百胜大获全功

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 福神变成文书，兵必定骄傲，后悔也来不及；子孙变成兄弟，将领虽弱，也大获全功。父母是子孙的大忌，如果子孙变父母，就是用爻变成忌神，而克伐就像雪狮向着太阳，起初张扬威势，日渐消融，大有刑伤，噬脐莫及；兄弟是子孙的元神，子孙变兄弟，就是用爻化出元神来扶助，如同猛虎添翼，越发精神，百战百胜，大获全功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the fortune spirit changes into the document, the troops are surely proud and regret will be too late; if Output changes into Peer, though the commander is weak he wins a great and complete success. Resource is the great Adversary of Output, and if Output turns into Resource, the Subject changes into the Adversary, and the control is like a snow lion facing the sun, at first putting on a show of might and day by day melting away, with much punishment and injury and the regret of biting one's own navel too late; Peer is the Support of Output, and if Output turns into Peer, the Subject changes into its Support to assist, like a fierce tiger adding wings, ever more spirited, winning a hundred battles in a hundred and gaining a great and complete success.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「福化文书」的福是福神（子孙），文书是父母；「元神」是生用神的爻（见术语表）。第一句解里的「日渐日苏」疑是「日渐日消」，译作「日渐消融」；「噬脐」是自咬肚脐够不着，比喻后悔也来不及。这里以子孙为兵将的用神，所以子孙变父母（忌神）凶、变兄弟（元神）吉；与易隐征战占「子化兄者将弱而功成，子化父者兵骄而致败」（YY.60）完全一致。
>
> *Note:* In 'fu hua wenshu' the 'fu' is the fortune spirit (Output) and the document is Resource; the 'Support' is the line that generates the Subject (see the glossary). 'Ri jian ri su' in the first explanation is presumably 'ri jian ri xiao', and is translated 'day by day melting away'; 'biting one's navel' is trying to bite one's own navel and not reaching it, an image of regret too late. Here Output is taken as the Subject for the commanders and troops, so Output turning into Resource (the Adversary) is ominous and into Peer (the Support) auspicious, which agrees exactly with 'Output turns into Peer, the commander is weak but succeeds; Output turns into Resource, the troops grow proud and bring defeat' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.60).
