# 趋谒贵人：以世应为主、外卦月卦为凭；外卦原来是用爻，兴隆出现得相交，无身无鬼皆非遇；凡去谒贵最嫌外卦空亡，外卦出现旺相人必相逢，外三爻皆动定有变更，一二爻动亦不如此论、又要取卦身与鬼官之爻，如缺一神又不遇

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 去谒见贵人，以世爻、应爻为主，以外卦、月卦为凭据。外卦本来就是用爻：兴旺并且出现，就能相会；没有卦身、没有官鬼，都见不到。凡是去谒见贵人，最嫌外卦空亡；外卦出现而且旺相，人一定能见到；如果外卦三个爻都动，一定有变更；一、二个爻动，不这样论，还要取卦身和官鬼之爻，缺了其中一个，又见不到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In going to call on a noble, take the Self and the Other as the chief, and the outer trigram and the month hexagram as the ground. The outer trigram is originally the Subject line: if it flourishes and appears, one gets to meet; with no Body and no Pressure, one does not meet. In every call on a noble, what is most disliked is the outer trigram falling on Void; if the outer trigram appears and is thriving, one will surely meet the person; if all three lines of the outer trigram move, there will surely be a change; if one or two lines move, it is not judged so, but one must also take the Body and the Pressure line, and if one of these is lacking, one again does not meet.

> [!note] 按
> 谒见贵人时，外卦代表被拜见的一方（按本章后文「内为主，外为宾」）。「月卦」书里没有解释，本章别处也没有再用，这里按字面译作「月卦」。「无身无鬼皆非遇」的「身」是卦身（见术语表）。「外三爻皆动，定有变更」是说外卦三爻齐动，所见的人或事有变，书里没有展开。「鬼官」是官鬼。这一条与易隐谒贵占「外卦与应爻官爻属阴在家、属阳出外」「动者难见空者不见」（YY.74、YY.75）取同一个象：外卦、应爻是被拜见的人。
>
> *Note:* In calling on a noble, the outer trigram stands for the party who is visited (by the chapter's later 'the inner is the host and the outer is the guest'). The book does not explain 'yuegua', the month hexagram, and the chapter does not use it again, so it is translated literally. The 'body' of 'with no Body and no Pressure one does not meet' is the Body of the hexagram (see the glossary). 'If all three lines of the outer trigram move there will surely be a change' says that when the three lines of the outer trigram all move, the person or the matter seen changes, and the book does not go into it. 'Guiguan' is Pressure. This entry takes the same image as 'the outer trigram and the Pressure line of the Other, if yin he is at home and if yang he is out' and 'if it moves he is hard to see, if Void he is not seen' in the Yi Yin divination on calling on a noble (YY.74, YY.75): the outer trigram and the Other are the person who is visited.
