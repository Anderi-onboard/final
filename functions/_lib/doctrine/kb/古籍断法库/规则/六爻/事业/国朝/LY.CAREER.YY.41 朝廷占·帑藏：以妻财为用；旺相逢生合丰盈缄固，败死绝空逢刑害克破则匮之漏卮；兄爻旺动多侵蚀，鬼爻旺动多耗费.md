# 朝廷占·帑藏：以妻财为用；旺相逢生合丰盈缄固，败死绝空逢刑害克破则匮之漏卮；兄爻旺动多侵蚀，鬼爻旺动多耗费

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国库：以妻财为用神。财爻旺相又遇生合，国库丰盈、封存牢固；衰败、死、绝、落空，又遇刑、害、克、破，国库就空虚，像漏了的酒器一样不断外泄；兄爻旺相发动，多被侵吞蚕食；官鬼爻旺相发动，多有耗费。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The treasury. Take Wealth as the Subject. If it is thriving and meets generation or combination, the treasury is full and securely sealed; if it is spent, dead, extinct or Void and meets punishment, harm, control or breaking, the treasury is empty, like a leaking vessel; if the Peer line is thriving and moving, there is much embezzlement; if the Pressure line is thriving and moving, there is much expenditure.

> [!note] 按
> 「帑藏」是国库。「匮之漏卮」的「卮」是酒器，「漏卮」比喻财富不断外泄。「缄固」是封存牢固。
>
> *Note:* 'Tangcang' is the state treasury. In 'kui zhi lou zhi', 'zhi' is a wine vessel, and a 'leaking vessel' is an image of wealth running out without stop. 'Jiangu' is being sealed up firmly.
