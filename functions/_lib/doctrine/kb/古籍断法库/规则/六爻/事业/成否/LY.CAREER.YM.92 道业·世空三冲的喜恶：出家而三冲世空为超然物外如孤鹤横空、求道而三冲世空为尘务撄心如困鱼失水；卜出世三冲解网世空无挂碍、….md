# 道业·世空三冲的喜恶：出家而三冲世空为超然物外如孤鹤横空、求道而三冲世空为尘务撄心如困鱼失水；卜出世三冲解网世空无挂碍、卜入道则空无成

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 然而世空有喜与恶的分别，三冲有宜与忌的分别：出家而三冲、世空，是超然物外，像孤鹤横空；求道而三冲、世空，是尘世杂务缠心，像困鱼失水。占出世，三冲是解开（俗世的）网，世空是没有牵挂；若占入道，那么空就成不了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Yet the Self Void has a distinction of welcome and unwelcome, and the three clashes a distinction of suitable and unsuitable: for one leaving home, the three clashes and the Self Void are being detached from the world, like a lone crane crossing the sky; for one seeking the Way, the three clashes and the Self Void are worldly business tangling the heart, like a trapped fish out of water. In divining about leaving the world, the three clashes are undoing the net and the Self Void is having nothing hanging on one's mind; if one divines about entering the Way, then Void means it does not come off.

> [!note] 按
> 「三冲」（见 YM.07 注）是冲散、断开；对出家（离开俗世）的人，冲散俗缘是好事，所以宜；对要修行的人（还在俗世里求道），冲散会把修行也断掉，所以忌。「世空」同样：离俗的人世空是无牵无挂，求道的人世空是心里空了、无从落脚。「撄心」是扰乱心神。「出世」是出家离俗，「入道」是进入修行。书里自注把这两种问法分开：卜出世，三冲是解网，世空是无挂碍；卜入道，空就无成。同一个卦象，问的是『出去』还是『进来』，喜恶相反。
>
> *Note:* The 'three clashes' (see the note to YM.07) are scattering and cutting apart; for one leaving the world (leaving the secular), scattering the worldly ties is a good thing and so suitable; for one who is to practise (still seeking the Way in the world), scattering will cut off the practice too and so is unsuitable. The 'Self Void' is the same: for one leaving the secular it is having nothing hanging on one's mind, and for one seeking the Way it is the heart gone empty with nowhere to rest. 'Yingxin' is disturbing the mind. 'Chushi' is leaving home and the secular, and 'rudao' is entering practice. The book's own note keeps the two ways of asking apart: divining about leaving the world, the three clashes are undoing the net and the Self Void is having nothing on one's mind; divining about entering the Way, Void means it does not come off. For the same image, whether one asks about 'going out' or 'coming in', welcome and unwelcome are opposite.
