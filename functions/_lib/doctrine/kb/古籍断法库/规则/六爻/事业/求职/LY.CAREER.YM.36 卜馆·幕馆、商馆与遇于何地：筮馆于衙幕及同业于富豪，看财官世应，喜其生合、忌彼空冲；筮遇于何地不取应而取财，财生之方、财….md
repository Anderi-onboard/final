# 卜馆·幕馆、商馆与遇于何地：筮馆于衙幕及同业于富豪，看财官世应，喜其生合、忌彼空冲；筮遇于何地不取应而取财，财生之方、财动之宫乃可图，财属五行为其象，再参六神诸星

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占在衙门里做幕友，以及和富豪同业共事，看财、官、世、应，喜它们生、合，忌它们空、冲。占在什么地方遇到馆，就不取应爻，而取财：财长生的方位、财动所在的宫，才是可以去图谋的；财属哪一行，就是它的象。再参看六神、诸星。财动按宫来论，如财动在乾宫，宜往西北去；财静按长生来论，如财属金爻（金长生在巳，在东南），利涉东南；妻财临水，遇鱼盐一类的行业、江海之滨；妻财临木，遇花果的行当、山林之处；土是田野，火是城市，金是玉石；玄武主水、青龙主木，白虎主兵，朱雀主市，螣蛇主商，勾陈主医；天医主医使，华盖主僧从，都用财来考察。接受人家的聘请，以世应为先；替人家办事，以财利为务。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about a post as a secretary in a government office, or about working in the same trade as a rich man, look at Wealth, Pressure, the Self and the Other: it is good for them to generate and combine, and bad for them to be Void and clashed. In divining where one will meet a post, one does not take the Other line but takes Wealth: the quarter where Wealth is born and the palace where Wealth moves are where one can make plans, and the phase Wealth belongs to is its image. Then consult the six spirits and the stars. When Wealth moves, judge by the palace: if Wealth moves in the palace of Qian, go to the north-west; when Wealth is still, judge by Birth: if Wealth is a Metal line (Metal is born at Si, in the south-east), the south-east is favourable. Wealth on Water meets trades of fish and salt and the shores of river and sea; Wealth on Wood meets the flower-and-fruit trade and the mountain forest; Earth looks for fields, Fire for towns, Metal for jade and stone; the Dark Tortoise is water, the Azure Dragon is wood, the White Tiger is soldiers, the Vermilion Bird is markets, the Coiling Snake is merchants and Hook Earth is doctors; the Heavenly Doctor is physicians and messengers, the Canopy is monks and followers; all are examined by Wealth. In accepting a person's invitation the Self and the Other come first; in serving a person's affairs, Wealth and profit are the business.

> [!note] 按
> 「衙幕」是衙门里的幕友（师爷），「同业于富豪」是在富商豪门的行当里共事，这两样就是章首十三问里的「幕馆」「商馆」。「遇于何地」是在哪个地方能遇到馆。「则不以应而财」一句，「而」字下原文疑脱一字（当是「取」或「以」），按文意译作「不取应而取财」。「财生之方」按文意是财的长生所在的方位，「财动之宫」是财爻动在哪一宫、那一宫所主的方位：动用宫、静用生，这就是注里「财动论宫、财静论生」。书里的例子：财动乾宫，乾在西北，宜趋西北；财是金爻，金长生在巳，巳在东南，所以利涉东南。「武水龙木，虎兵雀市，蛇商勾医」是六神各配一个行业；「天医医使，华盖僧从」是两个神煞。收尾「纳人之请，世应为先，服人之事，财利为务」是总括：被人聘用看世应，替人办事看财利。
>
> *Note:* 'Yamu' is a secretary in a government office, and 'working in the same trade as a rich man' is working in the line of a rich merchant's or magnate's house; these two are the 'muguan' and 'shangguan' of the thirteen questions at the head of the chapter. 'Yu yu hedi' is where one can meet a post. In the sentence 'ze bu yi ying er cai' a character seems to be missing below 'er' (presumably 'qu' or 'yi'), and by the sense it is translated 'does not take the Other but takes Wealth'. 'The quarter where Wealth is born', by the sense, is the quarter of Wealth's Birth, and 'the palace Wealth moves in' is the direction governed by the palace in which the Wealth line moves: for a moving line the palace is used, for a still one Birth, which is what the note means by 'Wealth moving, judge by the palace; Wealth still, judge by Birth'. The examples in the book: Wealth moving in Qian, which is in the north-west, one should go north-west; Wealth a Metal line, Metal being born at Si, which is in the south-east, so crossing to the south-east is favourable. 'Wu shui long mu, hu bing que shi, she shang gou yi' gives each of the six spirits a trade; 'tian yi yi shi, hua gai seng cong' are two spirit-stars. The closing 'in accepting a person's invitation the Self and the Other come first, in serving a person's affairs Wealth and profit are the business' sums it up: when engaged by someone, look at the Self and the Other; when working for someone, look at Wealth and profit.
