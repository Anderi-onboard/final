# 卜筮元龟·占求官任所有无：父母爻为诰牒、任官之处是其财；财落空亡无任所，空持诰牒意徘徊；父母被危诰身破、财爻被破任官危；岁月之冲须重说、日辰之克可轻挂

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占求官有没有任所：父母爻是诰牒（任命书），任官的地方就是它的财；财落空亡，就没有任所，空握着诰牒、心意徘徊；父母被危，诰身就破；财爻被破，任官就危险；岁月的冲要加重来说，日辰的克可以看轻。注文举例：甲辰旬占得乾宫卦，甲寅为财，甲辰旬里寅、卯空，财落空亡，表示没有俸禄、没有任职之处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On divining whether one seeking office will have a post to go to: the Resource line is the patent of appointment, and the place of the post is its Wealth; with Wealth fallen Void there is no post, and holding the patent in vain one's mind wavers; with Resource endangered the patent is spoiled; with the Wealth line broken, taking office is perilous; a clash by the year or the month is to be weighed heavily and a control by the Day can be taken lightly. The note gives an example: if a hexagram of the Qian palace is cast in the Jiachen decade, Jiayin is Wealth, and in the Jiachen decade Yin and Mao are void, so Wealth falls Void, which shows no salary and nowhere to take office.
