# 朝廷占·万金赋穿断法（二）：五生合二君礼士，三四生合二臣下贤，三四合二生五臣荐贤于上，二生三四上景从，二临父克五书生进牍，四生克三大臣能任百官，三持财动生世下赂上，五生合三奖良吏，五生合四委心执政，四生合五摅诚报主

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《万金赋》穿断法，看君、士、臣：五爻生合二爻，君主礼敬士人；三爻、四爻生合二爻，臣下贤明；三爻、四爻合二爻又生五爻的，臣子向君主举荐贤才；二爻生三爻、四爻的，在上的人如影随形地追随；二爻临父母爻克五爻的，书生上书进奏；四爻生克三爻的，大臣能统率百官，提拔贤才、罢黜奸邪；三爻持财爻发动生世爻的，下面的人贿赂上面；五爻生合三爻，君主奖励、资助良吏；五爻生合四爻，君主把心思托付给执政大臣；四爻生合五爻，大臣竭诚报效君主。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Chuanduan method, reading sovereign, scholars and ministers. If the fifth line generates or combines with the second, the sovereign honours the scholars; if the third and fourth generate or combine with the second, the ministers are worthy; if the third and fourth combine with the second and generate the fifth, the ministers recommend the worthy to the sovereign; if the second generates the third and fourth, those above follow like a shadow; if the second sits on Resource and controls the fifth, a scholar presents a memorial; if the fourth generates or controls the third, the great minister can direct the hundred officials, promoting the worthy and dismissing the wicked; if the third holds Wealth and moves to generate the Self, those below bribe those above; if the fifth generates or combines with the third, the sovereign rewards and supports good officials; if the fifth generates or combines with the fourth, the sovereign entrusts his mind to the minister in charge; if the fourth generates or combines with the fifth, the minister gives his sincerity to repay the sovereign.

> [!note] 按
> 「上景从」按字面是在上的人像影子一样跟从，书里没有说谁追随谁，译文照字面。「进牍」是呈上奏章。「摅诚」是尽情抒发诚意。「四生克三」可以读成四爻生或克三爻，书里没有分开，译文也不分开。
>
> *Note:* 'Shang jing cong' is by the letter those above following like a shadow; the book does not say who follows whom, and the translation follows the letter. 'Jindu' is presenting a memorial. 'Shucheng' is pouring out one's sincerity. 'The fourth generates or controls the third' can be read either as generating or as controlling; the book does not separate them and the translation does not either.
