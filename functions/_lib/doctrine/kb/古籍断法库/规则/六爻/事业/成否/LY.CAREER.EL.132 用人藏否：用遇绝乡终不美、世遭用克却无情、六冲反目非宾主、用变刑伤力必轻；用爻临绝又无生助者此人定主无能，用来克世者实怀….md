# 用人藏否：用遇绝乡终不美、世遭用克却无情、六冲反目非宾主、用变刑伤力必轻；用爻临绝又无生助者此人定主无能，用来克世者实怀欺主之心彼非仗义之人，卜六冲之卦终成反目，用爻虽有气而变克制之乡或墓绝人必先勤后惰事当有始无终

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻遇到绝乡，终究不好；世爻被用爻所克，却是无情；六冲就反目、不成宾主；用爻变出刑伤，力量一定轻。用爻临绝又没有生扶，这个人一定没有能耐；用爻来克世，实在怀着欺骗主人的心，他不是仗义的人；卜得六冲的卦，终究反目，怎么能和谐？卦上用爻虽然有气，但变成克制它的地方、或者变入墓、绝，这个人必定先勤后惰，事情当是有始无终。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject meets a place of extinction it is in the end not good; if the Self is controlled by the Subject, it is without feeling; with a six-clash they fall out and are not host and guest; if the Subject changes into punishment and injury, its strength is surely slight. If the Subject line is in extinction and has no generation or support, the man is surely incapable; if the Subject comes to control the Self, he truly harbours a mind to cheat his master and is not a man of loyalty and justice; if a six-clash hexagram is cast, they end by falling out, and how can there be harmony? Even if the Subject line in the hexagram has qi, if it changes into a place that controls it or into tomb or extinction, the man will surely be diligent at first and idle later, and the affair will have a beginning but no end.

> [!note] 按
> 「绝乡」是绝地。「世遭用克却无情」是世爻被用爻所克。「仗义之人」是讲义气、可靠的人。「之墓绝」的「之」是变入。这一条与前一条（EL.131）合起来，是用人的两面：用爻不被冲破空绝是可用，用爻临绝、克世、六冲、变克都是不可用。
>
> *Note:* 'Juexiang' is a place of extinction. 'Shi zao yong ke que wuqing' is the Self line controlled by the Subject line. 'Zhangyi zhi ren' is a man of loyalty and justice, one who can be relied on. The 'zhi' of 'zhi mu jue' is changing into. This entry and the previous one (EL.131) together are the two sides of employing a man: a Subject line that is not clashed, broken, Void or extinct can be employed, while a Subject line in extinction, controlling the Self, in a six-clash or changing into control cannot.
