# 卜筮元龟·求官不成之象：官鬼空亡及冲破，世应俱值尤难拟；官爻带杀克身凶，纵得官而复返止（例：正月损之临，上九寅爻带杀阴）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼落空亡或被冲破，世、应又都遇上这种情形，尤其难以指望；官爻带杀克身，是凶，纵然得到官也会反过来中止。例子：假定正月占得损之临，上九的寅爻带阴杀，举这一爻作为定例，不必再久久思量。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure Void or clashed and broken, and the Self and the Other both meeting the same, it is all the harder to count on; with the Pressure line carrying a baleful spirit and controlling the self it is bad, and even if the office is gained it will turn back and stop. For example, suppose in the first month the hexagram Sun changing to Lin, with the top line, a nine, Yin carrying a yin baleful spirit: take this one line as the fixed example, and there is no need to ponder longer.
