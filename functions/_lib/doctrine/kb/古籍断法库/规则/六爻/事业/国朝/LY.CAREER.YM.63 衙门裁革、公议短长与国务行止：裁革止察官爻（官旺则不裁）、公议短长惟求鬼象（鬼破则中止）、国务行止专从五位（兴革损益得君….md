# 衙门裁革、公议短长与国务行止：裁革止察官爻（官旺则不裁）、公议短长惟求鬼象（鬼破则中止）、国务行止专从五位（兴革损益得君命为行止、五爻克世则不行）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 衙门裁撤革除，只察看官爻；公议的短长，只求鬼象；国家政务行止，专从五位。官旺就不裁；公议将要实行的事，鬼破就中止；兴办、革除、减少、增加，以得到君命才行，五爻克世，就不能行。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether a government office is abolished, one looks only at the Pressure line; whether public opinion carries or fails, one seeks only the Pressure image; whether a state affair goes ahead or stops, one follows only the fifth place. Thriving Pressure means it is not abolished; of a matter public opinion is about to carry out, broken Pressure stops it; to set up, abolish, reduce or increase anything, it goes ahead only if the sovereign's order is obtained, and if the fifth line controls the Self, it does not go.

> [!note] 按
> 「裁革」是衙门被裁撤革除，「公议」是公众（朝野）的议论，「国务」是国家政务。「五位」是五爻（君位）。书里自注：官旺则不裁；公议要实行的事，鬼破则中止；兴、革、损、益（兴办、革除、减少、增加）要得到君命才行得通，五爻克世就行不通。这一条是功名章的结尾，从个人的官职转到了衙门和国家的事；和本书国事章（YM.01 起）、易林补遗朝廷国事（EL.01 起）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Caige' is a government office being cut and abolished, 'gongyi' is the discussion of the public (court and country), and 'guowu' is the business of the state. 'Wuwei' is the fifth line (the sovereign's place). The book's own gloss: thriving Pressure means it is not cut; of a matter public opinion is about to carry out, broken Pressure stops it; to set up, abolish, reduce or increase anything one must obtain the sovereign's order for it to go through, and the fifth line controlling the Self means it does not. This entry is the end of the chapter on fame and office, turning from a person's own post to the affairs of the offices and the state; read against the chapter on affairs of state in this book (from YM.01) and the Yilin Buyi on the affairs of the court (from EL.01).
