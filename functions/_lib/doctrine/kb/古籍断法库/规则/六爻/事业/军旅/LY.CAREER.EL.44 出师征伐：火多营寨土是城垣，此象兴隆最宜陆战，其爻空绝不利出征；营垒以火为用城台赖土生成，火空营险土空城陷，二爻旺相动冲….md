# 出师征伐：火多营寨土是城垣，此象兴隆最宜陆战，其爻空绝不利出征；营垒以火为用城台赖土生成，火空营险土空城陷，二爻旺相动冲可使西域沙漠之胜，衰休空绝必有华容小道之危

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 火多是营寨，土是城墙，这两个象兴旺，最宜陆战；这两爻空、绝，不利出征。营垒以火为用，城台靠土生成：火空则营寨危险，土空则城池陷落；两爻如果值旺相、动、冲，可以取得西域沙漠（那样的）胜利；（两象）如果遇到衰、休、空、绝，必有华容小道（曹操败走）那样的危险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fire is mostly the camp and Earth is the city wall; if these two images flourish, they are most fit for a land battle; if these two lines are Void or extinct, it is not favourable to set out. Camps and ramparts take Fire as the Subject, and city terraces rely on Earth for their making: if Fire is Void the camp is in danger, if Earth is Void the city falls; if the two lines meet thriving, moving and clashing, one can win a victory such as that in the western deserts; if they meet weak, resting, Void or extinct, there will surely be danger like that of the small road at Huarong.

> [!note] 按
> 「华容小道」是《三国演义》里曹操赤壁战败后败走华容道的故事；「西域沙漠之胜」书里没有说是哪一场战役，译文照字面。「雨象」又是「二象」之误（火、土两象）。这一条与易隐征战占「五行」里的火（营寨）、土（城池）（YY.64、YY.65）取象相同。
>
> *Note:* 'The small road at Huarong' is the story, in the Romance of the Three Kingdoms, of Cao Cao fleeing by the Huarong road after his defeat at Red Cliff; the book does not say which campaign 'the victory in the western deserts' is, and the translation follows the letter. 'Yu xiang' is again a slip for 'er xiang', the two images (Fire and Earth). This entry takes the same images as Fire (camps) and Earth (city walls) in the 'five elements' section of the Yi Yin war divination (YY.64, YY.65).
