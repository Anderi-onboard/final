# 丁产·立户成丁、置业归产：止畏征科之累，官鬼为徭役不可动不可克（动烦而克累）、不可随墓助伤（有飞差突派），惟鬼静福兴而安居乐业

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 立户头成年丁、置办产业入册，说到底只是怕征收赋税的牵累，所以官鬼是徭役：不可动、不可克（动就烦，克就累）；不可随墓、助伤（随墓助伤就有飞来的差役、突然的摊派）；只有鬼静、福兴，才能安居乐业。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Establishing a household and registering adult males, acquiring property and entering it on the rolls are in the end only out of fear of the burden of tax-levying; so Pressure is the corvée, and it must not move or be controlled (moving means vexation, being controlled means burden); it must not follow into the tomb or help the injury (following and helping bring sudden duties and abrupt levies); only with Pressure still and Output rising does one live in peace and enjoy one's work.

> [!note] 按
> 「立户成丁」是立户头、把成年男子（丁）登记入册，「置业归产」是置办产业、把产业登记归户，「征科」是征收赋税，「徭役」是官府摊派的劳役，「飞差突派」是突然飞来的差役和临时的摊派。章首目录里：「寄户」是把户口寄在别人名下，「投图」是投入某个里图（明代基层户籍的册籍单位），「归甲」是归入甲（十户为甲），「清漏」是清查漏报的丁产，「交纳」是缴纳钱粮，「散派」是摊派，「违限」是逾期。这一章讲户籍、赋役这套官府制度里的占问，和下一章户役（YM.77 起）是一对：这里管丁产户籍，下一章管差役。官鬼在这里仍是『官府加给的负担』。
>
> *Note:* 'Lihu chengding' is setting up a household head and entering the adult males (ding) on the register, 'zhiye guichan' is acquiring property and entering it under the household, 'zhengke' is levying taxes, 'yaoyi' is labour service assigned by the government, and 'feichai tupai' is sudden duties that fly in and ad hoc levies. In the index at the head of the chapter: 'jihu' is lodging one's household registration under another's name, 'toutu' is joining a 'tu' (a unit of the rural registers under the Ming), 'guijia' is being returned to a 'jia' (ten households), 'qinglou' is clearing omitted males and holdings, 'jiaona' is paying in grain and money, 'sanpai' is apportioning, and 'weixian' is missing the deadline. This chapter deals with divinations within the system of household registration and levies, and is a pair with the next chapter on service (from YM.77): this one governs the registration of households and holdings, the next governs duties. Pressure here is still 'the burden the government lays on one'.
