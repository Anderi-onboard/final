# 仕宦·子若交重，当虑剥官削职（旧注：子孙是伤官之杀，发动则所谋不遂；未仕不能除选、已仕有夺官褫职之祸，鬼爻无气必至除名落籍）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙是伤官的煞，最喜欢它空亡墓绝，尤其适宜安静受制才吉。它如果在卦里发动，所谋的事必不遂意：没做官的得不到除授选用，已经做官的有被夺官革职的祸患；官鬼再无气，必定被除名、削去官籍，不只是贬责降罚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the killer that harms office. It is best when Void, in a tomb or in extinction, and above all fitting when still and held in check. If it moves in the hexagram, what is planned will surely go against one's wish: one not yet in office cannot be appointed or selected, and one already in office has the calamity of being deprived of office and stripped of post; and if Pressure has no breath as well, one will surely be struck from the roll and removed from the register of office, which is more than being rebuked and demoted.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1930 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙若在卦中发动，所谋必不遂意，已任者，恐有褫职之祸。 | 子孙如果在卦中发动，所谋的事必定不遂意，已经任职的，恐怕有被革职的祸患。 | If Output moves in the hexagram, what is planned will surely go against one's wish, and for one already in office there may be the calamity of being stripped of post. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「除选」是朝廷任命、选用官员；「褫职」是革职；「落籍」是从官籍上除去。这里和占病、占财的说法反过来：占财、占病子孙都是吉神，占官却是「伤官之杀」，因为子孙克官鬼。同样的道理见黄金策求名章「福如发动，难期金榜之题名」（在「考试」一域）。
>
> *Note:* 'Appointment and selection' is the court appointing and choosing officers, 'stripped of post' is dismissal, and 'removed from the register' is being struck from the roll of officers. This is the reverse of divining illness or wealth, where Output is a lucky spirit, whereas in divining an office it is 'the killer that harms office', because Output controls Pressure. The same reasoning is in the chapter on seeking a name, 'if Output moves, do not hope to be listed on the golden roll' (that chapter belongs to the domain of examinations).
