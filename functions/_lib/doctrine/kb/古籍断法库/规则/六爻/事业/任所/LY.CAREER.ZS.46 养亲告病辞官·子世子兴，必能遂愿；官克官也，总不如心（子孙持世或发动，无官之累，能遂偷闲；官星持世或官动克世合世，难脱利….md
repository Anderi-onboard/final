# 养亲告病辞官·子世子兴，必能遂愿；官克官也，总不如心（子孙持世或发动，无官之累，能遂偷闲；官星持世或官动克世合世，难脱利名缰锁）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 养亲、告病、辞官：子孙持世、子孙发动，就没有官职的牵累，必定能如愿偷闲；官星持世，以及官星发动克世、合世的，难以摆脱名利的缰绳和锁链。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To care for a parent, take sick leave or resign: if Output holds the Self or Output moves, there is no burden of office and one will surely get the leisure one wants; if the star of office holds the Self, or the star of office moves to control or combine with the Self, one can hardly break free from the reins and chains of rank and profit.

## 《增删卜易·养亲、告病、辞官章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10900 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Chapter on caring for a parent, sick leave and resignation", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子世子兴，必能遂愿； | 子孙持世、子孙兴旺，必定能如愿； | With Output on the Self and flourishing, one will surely get one's wish; |
> | 官克官也，总不如心。 | 官星克官，总归不称心。 | Pressure controlling the office, it never goes as one wishes. |
> | 子孙持世，子孙发动，乃无官之累，必能遂我偷闲。 | 子孙持世、子孙发动，就是没有官职的牵累，必定能遂我偷闲的心愿。 | If Output holds the Self or Output moves, there is no burden of office, and one will surely get the leisure one wants. |
> | 若官星持世、及官动克世合世者，难脱利名缰锁。 | 如果官星持世，以及官星发动克世、合世的，难以摆脱名利的缰绳和锁链。 | If the star of office holds the Self, or the star of office moves to control or combine with the Self, one can hardly break free from the reins and chains of rank and profit. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「官克官也」原文如此，字面不通；后一句注文把它讲成「官星持世、官动克世合世」，译文照字面译，不另改字。
>
> *Note:* 'Pressure controls Pressure' is how the text reads and it does not make sense literally; the following note explains it as 'the star of office holds the Self, or moves to control or combine with the Self', and the translation follows the letter without emending.
