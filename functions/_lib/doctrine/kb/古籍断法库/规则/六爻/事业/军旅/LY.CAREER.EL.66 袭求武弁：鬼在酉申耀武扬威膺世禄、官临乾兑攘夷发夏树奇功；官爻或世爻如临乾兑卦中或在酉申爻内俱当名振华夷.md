# 袭求武弁：鬼在酉申耀武扬威膺世禄、官临乾兑攘夷发夏树奇功；官爻或世爻如临乾兑卦中或在酉申爻内俱当名振华夷

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼在酉爻、申爻，显耀武功、扬起威风，享受世袭的俸禄；官爻落在乾卦、兑卦，抵御外族、振奋华夏，树立非凡的功劳。官爻或者世爻如果落在乾、兑卦中，或者在酉、申爻上，都会名声震动华夏和外族。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure is on a You or Shen line, one displays martial prowess and raises one's awe, and receives a hereditary emolument; if the star of office is in the Qian or Dui trigram, one drives out the barbarians, raises up the Hua-Xia, and sets up extraordinary merit. If the Pressure line or the Self line falls in the Qian or Dui trigram, or is on a You or Shen line, in every case one's name shakes both the Chinese and the barbarians.

> [!note] 按
> 酉、申属金，乾、兑也属金；金主兵刃，所以取武职要看它们（黄金策征战「金为刀箭」，HJC.111）。「世禄」是世代承袭的俸禄。「攘夷发夏」的「发夏」疑是「发扬华夏」，译作「振奋华夏」；「华夷」是华夏和外族。
>
> *Note:* You and Shen belong to Metal, and Qian and Dui also belong to Metal; Metal governs blades and weapons, which is why they are looked at for military posts (the Huangjin Ce on campaigns says 'Metal is knife and arrow', HJC.111). 'Shi lu' is an emolument handed down from generation to generation. The 'fa xia' of 'rang yi fa xia' is presumably 'fayang Huaxia', to bring glory to the Chinese, and is translated 'raises up the Hua-Xia'; 'hua yi' is the Chinese and the barbarians.
