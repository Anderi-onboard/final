# 出师征伐：密密刀枪为金宫之空动，飘飘旗帜因火父之旺冲；刀剑属金又属官鬼，值空亡又逢发动必振扬，金空则鸣刀枪光耀日金鼓振轰雷营寨重重提防有备；旌旗以父母为用爻又属离火，旺相更遇日冲火发则焰，黄幡豹尾彩帜麒麟飘摇号令严明军机整肃

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 密密的刀枪，是金宫空而发动；飘飘的旗帜，是因为火的父爻旺相逢冲。刀剑本属金，又属官鬼，如果值空亡又逢发动，必定振扬，所以经书说「金空则鸣」，必是刀枪光芒耀日、金鼓声震如雷，营寨密密重重是提防有备的缘故。旌旗以父母爻为用爻，又属离火，如果旺相，更遇日辰冲，所以日冲火发就有焰，必是黄幡悬着豹尾、彩旗上写着麒麟，飘飘摇摇，引导军队、行阵，号令严明，军机整肃。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Dense blades and spears are Metal palace Void and moving; fluttering banners are the Fire Resource line thriving and clashed. Swords and blades belong to Metal, and also to Pressure, and if they meet Void and also move they surely rise up, so the classic says 'Metal Void rings', and surely the blades and spears flash against the sun, the gongs and drums shake like thunder, and the camps are dense and layered out of watchfulness and readiness. The banners take the Resource line as the Subject line and also belong to Li Fire, and if it is thriving and also meets the Day's clash, then when the Day clashes Fire moves there is flame, and surely yellow pennants hang with leopard tails and bright flags are written with the qilin, fluttering and waving, leading the army and the lines, orders strict and clear and military secrets well guarded.

> [!note] 按
> 「金空则鸣」是「金空则鸣，金实则不鸣」的老话：金爻落空，就像空的钟一敲就响。「豹尾」「麒麟」是旌旗上的图案，「黄幡豹尾」是古代军中的旗饰。「营衡寨」疑是「营栅寨」，不影响大意。这一条与 EL.30 的旌旗合着读，与易隐征战占「父旺外动彼兴兵」等（YY.57）不同的是这里只说金、火两爻的外在景象。
>
> *Note:* 'Metal Void rings' is from the old saying 'Metal Void rings, Metal full does not ring': a Metal line that is Void is like an empty bell, which rings when struck. The 'leopard tail' and the 'qilin' are designs on the banners, and 'yellow pennant and leopard tail' is an old flag decoration of armies. 'Ying heng zhai' is presumably 'ying zha zhai', camps and stockades, which does not affect the general sense. This entry is read together with the banners of EL.30, and differs from 'Resource thriving and moving outside, the enemy raises troops' and the like in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.57) in speaking only of the outward scenes of the Metal and Fire lines.
