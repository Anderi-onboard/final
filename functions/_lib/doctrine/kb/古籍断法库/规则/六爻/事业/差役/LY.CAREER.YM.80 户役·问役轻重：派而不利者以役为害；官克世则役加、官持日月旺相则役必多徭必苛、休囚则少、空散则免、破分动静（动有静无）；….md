# 户役·问役轻重：派而不利者以役为害；官克世则役加、官持日月旺相则役必多徭必苛、休囚则少、空散则免、破分动静（动有静无）；随墓助伤亦应大凶、得逢空破反为得免

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 被摊派而不利的，以役为害。所以问役的轻重：官克世，役就加重；官持日辰、月建而旺相，役必多、徭必苛；休囚就少，空、散就免。破要分动静：动就有役，静就无役；随墓助伤，也应当大凶，但遇到空、破，反而得免。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When one is assigned a duty to one's disadvantage, the duty is a harm. So in asking whether the duty is light or heavy: Pressure controlling the Self, the duty is increased; Pressure holding the Day or Month and thriving, the duty is sure to be many and the corvée harsh; resting or imprisoned, it is little; Void or scattered, it is escaped. Broken is divided into moving and still: moving, there is duty; still, there is none; following into the tomb and helping the injury should also be greatly ominous, but meeting Void or broken one escapes instead.

> [!note] 按
> 「派而不利者」是被摊派而没有好处的差役。「役加」是役加重。「徭」是徭役。「官持日月旺相」是官爻临日辰或月建又旺相，力量最大，所以役最多最苛。「破分动静」按文意：官爻破的时候，动的还有役，静的就没有；书里没有再展开。「随墓助伤……得逢空破，反为得免」是说平时的大忌，在躲役的占问里反而变成好事：官入墓、官落空破，差役就摊不到我。这一条里『忌』和『喜』因所问是『求役』还是『躲役』而反过来，总的原则在本章末尾（YM.84）的「用神无执一」。
>
> *Note:* 'Pai er bu li zhe' is a duty assigned with no benefit. 'Yijia' is the duty becoming heavier. 'Yao' is corvée. 'Pressure holding the Day or Month and thriving' is the Pressure line on the Day or Month branch and thriving, its force being greatest, so that the duty is the most and the harshest. 'Broken is divided into moving and still', by the sense: when the Pressure line is broken, if it is moving there is still duty, and if still there is none; the book does not expand on it. 'Following into the tomb and helping the injury ... meeting Void or broken one escapes instead' says that what is a great taboo in ordinary times becomes a good thing in a divination about avoiding a duty: Pressure entering the tomb or falling into Void or broken means the duty is not assigned to me. In this entry what is feared and what is welcomed are reversed according to whether the question is seeking or avoiding service; the general principle is in 'the Subject is not held to one' at the end of this chapter (YM.84).
