# 征战·二福刑冲化绝，钟邓互隙而丧身家（旧注：两个子孙生旺变动相冲相刑、两化入死墓绝空，虽胜敌将必争权夺宠互相残害，如晋钟会邓艾平蜀后嫌隙互生，自相屠戮，身家俱丧）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 两个子孙爻生旺发动，互相冲、刑，又都变入死、墓、绝、空，虽然能战胜敌将，但必定争权夺宠、互相残害，就像晋朝的钟会、邓艾带兵平定蜀国，蜀国平定之后彼此生出嫌隙，以致自相残杀，身家都丧。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If two Output lines are thriving and moving, clash with and punish each other, and both turn into death, tomb, extinction or Void, then though they may overcome the enemy's generals they will surely struggle for power and favour and harm each other, like Zhong Hui and Deng Ai of Jin, who led troops to subdue Shu and, when Shu was settled, fell into mutual suspicion, until they killed each other and lost both their persons and their families.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1536 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 两重子孙旺动皆化入死墓绝空，虽胜敌将，必争权夺宠，两相残害，如晋钟会、邓艾领兵平蜀，蜀平而嫌隙互生，乃至自相屠戮，身家俱丧。 | 两个子孙旺相发动，都变入死、墓、绝、空，虽然能战胜敌将，必定争权夺宠，互相残害，就像晋朝的钟会、邓艾带兵平定蜀国，蜀国平定之后彼此生出嫌隙，以致自相屠杀，身家都丧。 | If two Output lines are thriving and moving and both turn into death, tomb, extinction or Void, then though they may overcome the enemy's generals they will surely struggle for power and favour and harm each other, like Zhong Hui and Deng Ai of Jin, who led troops to subdue Shu and, when Shu was settled, fell into mutual suspicion, until they slaughtered each other and lost both their persons and their families. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 书里写「晋钟会、邓艾」；钟会、邓艾平蜀（公元 263 年）时的国家是魏，晋是三年后才建立的，译文照书写作晋朝。旧注还写了两个子孙「相冲相刑」，卜筮正宗没有这一层。
>
> *Note:* The books write 'Zhong Hui and Deng Ai of Jin'; when they subdued Shu (263 CE) the state was Wei and Jin was founded three years later, but the translation follows the books and says Jin. The old commentary also has the two Output lines clashing with and punishing each other; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong has no such clause.
