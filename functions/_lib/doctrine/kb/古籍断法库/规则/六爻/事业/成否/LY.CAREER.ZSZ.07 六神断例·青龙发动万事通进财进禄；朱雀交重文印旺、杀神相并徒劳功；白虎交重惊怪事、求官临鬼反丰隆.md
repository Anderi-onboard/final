# 六神断例·青龙发动万事通进财进禄；朱雀交重文印旺、杀神相并徒劳功；白虎交重惊怪事、求官临鬼反丰隆

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙发动，万事通达，进财进禄，临在凶上、遇杀都无妨碍，只忌落在金上和落空；朱雀交重，文书印绶旺，和杀神并在一起就白劳功夫，是非口舌都由此而来，持水、临空反而有利于公事；白虎交重，是惊恐怪异之事，持金、世上带杀妨碍人口，求官时临在官鬼上，反而丰盛隆盛。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Azure Dragon moving, all matters go through and wealth and emolument come in, and standing on ill or meeting a baleful spirit it meets no obstruction, only avoid its standing on Metal or falling void; with the Vermilion Bird moving heavily the documents and the seal and ribbon are strong, joined with a baleful spirit the labour is spent in vain, quarrels and gossip all come from this, and holding Water or standing in the void it is on the contrary favourable to public business; with the White Tiger moving heavily it is fright and strange things, holding Metal with a baleful spirit on the Self harms the household, and in seeking office, standing on Pressure, it brings abundance and prosperity on the contrary.

## 《周易尚占·六神断例》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 487 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Judgments by example for the six spirits", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 白虎交重惊怪事， | 白虎交重，是惊恐怪异之事； | With the White Tiger moving heavily, it is fright and strange things; |
> | 求官临鬼反丰隆， | 求官时临在官鬼上，反而丰盛隆盛； | in seeking office, standing on Pressure, it brings abundance and prosperity on the contrary; |
> | 持金世杀妨人口， | 持金、世上带杀，妨碍人口； | holding Metal with a baleful spirit on the Self harms the household; |
> | 遇火加空却不同。 | 遇火再加空，就不一样了。 | meeting Fire and Void, it is otherwise. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这三首和海底眼「六神爻动」里的青龙、朱雀、白虎三首是同一首诗，字句基本相同（海底眼多出每句后面的小注）。白虎一般主凶，这里说求官临鬼反而「丰隆」，照原文录。
>
> *Note:* These three are the same poems as the Azure Dragon, Vermilion Bird and White Tiger poems in the Haidi Yan's 'The six spirits moving', nearly word for word (the Haidi Yan adds a small note after each line). The White Tiger generally stands for ill, yet here it is said that in seeking office, on Pressure, it brings abundance on the contrary, as the text has it.
