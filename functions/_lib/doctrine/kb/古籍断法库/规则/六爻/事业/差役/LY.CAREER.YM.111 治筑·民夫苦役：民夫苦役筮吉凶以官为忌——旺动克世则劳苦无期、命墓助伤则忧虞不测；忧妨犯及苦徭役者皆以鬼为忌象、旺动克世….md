# 治筑·民夫苦役：民夫苦役筮吉凶以官为忌——旺动克世则劳苦无期、命墓助伤则忧虞不测；忧妨犯及苦徭役者皆以鬼为忌象、旺动克世及随助而凶

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果是民夫服苦役占吉凶，就以官为忌：官旺动克世，劳苦没有尽头；命墓、助伤，忧虑难测。如果担忧触犯妨碍，以及受苦于徭役的，都以鬼为忌象：旺动克世，以及随墓助伤，就凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a labourer in hard service divines about good and ill, Pressure is the taboo: Pressure thriving, moving and controlling the Self, the toil has no end; the life in the tomb and helping the injury, there is fear of the unforeseen. If one worries about offending and obstruction, and for those who suffer under labour service, Pressure is the taboo image in all cases: thriving, moving and controlling the Self, and following into the tomb and helping the injury, are ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 「民夫苦役」是被征去服苦役的民夫。「旺动克世」是官鬼旺相、发动、克世；「劳苦无期」是辛苦没有尽头；「命墓助伤」是世爻（命）入墓、并助官鬼伤世；「忧虞不测」是忧虑难以预料的祸事。「忧妨犯」是担忧触犯禁忌或遭妨碍，「徭役」是官府摊派的劳役。这两处都是以官鬼为忌：受役的人希望官鬼静、弱、不克世（与 YM.80 的问役轻重、YM.84 的『役而免害恶鬼旺』同一个意思）。同一章里的修塘修坪、治水兴营等工程则以父为用爻，不属事业域，没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Minfu kuyi' is the labourers conscripted for hard service. 'Thriving, moving and controlling the Self' is Pressure thriving, moving and controlling the Self; 'toil without end' is hardship that does not end; 'the life in the tomb and helping the injury' is the Self line (the life) entering the tomb and also helping Pressure to injure the Self; 'fear of the unforeseen' is anxiety about a calamity that cannot be foreseen. 'Worrying about offending and obstruction' is worrying about offending a taboo or meeting obstruction, and 'yaoyi' is labour service assigned by the government. In both places Pressure is the taboo: one who serves hopes Pressure will be still, weak and not controlling the Self (the same idea as asking how heavy the duty is in YM.80 and 'for one who serves a duty to escape harm, Pressure thriving is hated' in YM.84). The works in the same chapter, such as repairing ponds and embankments, controlling water and building fortifications, take the father line as the Subject, are not Career, and are not taken.
