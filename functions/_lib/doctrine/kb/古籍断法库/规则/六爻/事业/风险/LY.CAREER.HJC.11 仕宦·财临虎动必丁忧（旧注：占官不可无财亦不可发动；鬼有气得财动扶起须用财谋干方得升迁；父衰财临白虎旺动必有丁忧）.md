# 仕宦·财临虎动必丁忧（旧注：占官不可无财亦不可发动；鬼有气得财动扶起须用财谋干方得升迁；父衰财临白虎旺动必有丁忧）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占官，不能没有财爻，也不能让它发动。如果官鬼有气，又得到财爻发动来扶起，就必须用钱财去谋求打点，才能升迁；如果父母衰弱，又遇到这一爻加临白虎旺相发动，必有丁忧（为父母守丧）的事；财化子孙、子孙化财，或财临世发动，或子孙发动而父母无故自己空亡的，都主丁忧。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining an office, Wealth cannot be missing, nor should it move. If Pressure has breath and is raised up by a moving Wealth, one must use money to seek and arrange it before one is promoted; if Resource is weak and this line, settled on the White Tiger, moves in strength, there will surely be a mourning for a parent; and if Wealth turns into Output, or Output into Wealth, or Wealth settles on the Self and moves, or Output moves while Resource falls Void by itself without cause, in all these cases there will be mourning.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1946 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占官不可无财，亦不可发动，若鬼爻无气而得财动扶起，必须用财谋干，方得升迁； | 凡占官不可没有财，也不可发动，如果官鬼无气而得财爻发动扶起，必须用财谋求，才能升迁； | In divining an office, Wealth must not be missing nor may it move, and if Pressure has no breath and is raised up by a moving Wealth, one must seek by means of money before one is promoted; |
> | 若父母衰弱，而遇此爻加临白虎旺动者，必有忧丁之事。 | 如果父母衰弱，而遇到这一爻加临白虎旺相发动的，必有忧丁的事。 | if Resource is weak and this line, settled on the White Tiger, moves in strength, there will surely be a mourning. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「丁忧」是官员遭遇父母丧事，要离职守孝（通常二十七个月）。财克父母（文书），所以财动、父母衰是丁忧的象。「用财谋干」是花钱打点，书里没有说怎么打点。卜筮大全旧注说的是「财临虎动」（财爻临白虎发动），卜筮正宗把经文改成「财临福动」（财爻临子孙发动），可是它的注里仍写「加临白虎旺动」；「福」像是「虎」的误写，或是另有传本，书里没有说明，译文按卜筮大全的「虎」读。王洪绪把「忧丁」和旧注的「丁忧」是同一个词（丁忧）的两种写法。另有「官鬼有气得财动扶起」一句，卜筮正宗改写为「鬼爻无气而得财动扶起」：旧注说鬼有气、财动扶起，王洪绪说鬼无气、财动扶起，一个是锦上添花，一个是雪中送炭，但结论都是要用钱谋干。
>
> *Note:* 'Mourning' is the official's leave from post to observe mourning (usually twenty-seven months) on the death of a parent. Wealth controls Resource (the documents), so Wealth moving while Resource is weak is the sign of mourning. 'Use money to seek and arrange' means spending money to smooth the way, and the book does not say how. The old commentary of the Bushi Daquan says 'Wealth settled on the Tiger moves' (Wealth on the White Tiger moving), while the Bushi Zhengzong changes the verse to 'Wealth settled on Output moves', yet its commentary still says 'settled on the White Tiger and moving in strength'; the character printed 'Output' looks like a miswriting of 'Tiger', or there was another transmitted text, and the book does not say, and the translation follows the 'Tiger' of the Bushi Daquan. In one sentence the old commentary says 'if Pressure has breath and is raised up by a moving Wealth' and Wang Hongxu says 'if Pressure has no breath and is raised up by a moving Wealth': one adds flowers to brocade and the other sends charcoal in the snow, but both conclude that money must be used.
