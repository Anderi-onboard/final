# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（六）：欲知卦内作何官，先向身中仔细看——火为文章喉舌位、土为守令、水为排岸监司职、金代兵刑有重权、木为工部及作院，小大皆随旺相言

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道卦里是做什么官，先仔细看身：火是文章、喉舌的位置；土是守、令；水是排岸、监司一类的职事；金代表兵、刑，握有重权；木是工部和作院（军器作坊）；官职小大，都随爻的旺相来说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know what office the hexagram holds, first look carefully at the Body: Fire is the place of letters and of the throat and tongue of the court; Earth is the prefect and the magistrate; Water is a post such as embankment supervisor or commissioner; Metal stands for soldiers and punishments and holds heavy power; Wood is the Ministry of Works and the arsenal; and whether the post is small or large is said according to strength.

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全本作「水是江滨巡守职，金带兵刑生杀权。木主工科司建创，大小皆随衰旺言」，字句不同，说的是同一类分法；卜筮大全的旧注还对「火」「土」两句作了解释，断易天机本没有。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan text reads 'Water is the post of patrol and guard along the rivers and shores; Metal carries soldiers, punishments and the power of life and death; Wood governs the works section, in charge of building and founding; large or small follows weakness or strength', different in words but the same kind of division; the note in the Bushi Daquan also explains the Fire and Earth lines, which the Duanyi Tianji text does not.
