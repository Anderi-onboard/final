# 出兵交战：子孙宜强，鬼旺则彼赢我亡；财为草粮兄莫克、父为旌帜忌摇扬

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出兵交战，子孙要强；官鬼旺，对方赢、我必败亡；财是粮草，不要被兄弟克；父母是旌旗，忌讳摇动飘扬。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In sending out troops to battle, Output should be strong; if Pressure is strong the other side wins and we are surely lost; Wealth is the fodder and grain and should not be controlled by Peer; Resource is the banner and standard and should not be shaken and flying.
