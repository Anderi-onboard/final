# 征战·世逢绝地，项羽自刎于乌江（旧注：世为一国之君、三军之帅，宜旺相克应为吉，若被应爻刑冲克害而世又休囚，或动入死墓空绝，主将必不利；如项羽百战百胜而一朝兵散，羞见江东父老自刎乌江）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻是一国之君、三军之帅，最好旺相、克住应爻才吉；如果被应爻刑、冲、克、害，世爻又休囚，或者发动变入死、墓、空、绝，主将必然不利，就像项羽百战百胜，一朝兵败星散，羞于去见江东父老，在乌江自刎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self is the ruler of a state and the commander of the three armies; it is best thriving or strong and controlling the Other. If it is punished, clashed with, controlled or harmed by the Other, and the Self is also resting or trapped, or moves into death, tomb, Void or extinction, the chief commander is surely in trouble, like Xiang Yu, who won a hundred battles in a hundred, yet in one morning his troops scattered and, ashamed to face the elders east of the river, he cut his own throat at Wu River.
