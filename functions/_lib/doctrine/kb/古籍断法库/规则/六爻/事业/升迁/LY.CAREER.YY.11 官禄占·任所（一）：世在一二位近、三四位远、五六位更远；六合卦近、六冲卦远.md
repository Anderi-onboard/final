# 官禄占·任所（一）：世在一二位近、三四位远、五六位更远；六合卦近、六冲卦远

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 任所的远近：世爻在一、二位的近，三、四位的远，五、六位的更远；六合卦近，六冲卦远。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The distance of the post: if the Self is in the first or second place it is near, in the third or fourth far, and in the fifth or sixth farther still; a Six-Combine hexagram is near and a Six-Clash hexagram far.
