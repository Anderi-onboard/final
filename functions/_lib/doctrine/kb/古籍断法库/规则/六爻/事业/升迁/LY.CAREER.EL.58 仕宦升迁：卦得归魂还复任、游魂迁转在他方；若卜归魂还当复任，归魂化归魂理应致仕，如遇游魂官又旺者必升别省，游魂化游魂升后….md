# 仕宦升迁：卦得归魂还复任、游魂迁转在他方；若卜归魂还当复任，归魂化归魂理应致仕，如遇游魂官又旺者必升别省，游魂化游魂升后再升远处

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦得归魂，是回来再任原职；卦得游魂，是迁转到别的地方去。具体说：卜得归魂，还会恢复原任；归魂化成归魂，按理应当退休（致仕）；遇到游魂而官又旺相的，必定升到别的省；游魂化成游魂，升了之后再升到远处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Returning Soul hexagram means returning to the former post; a Wandering Soul means being moved and transferred elsewhere. In detail: if a Returning Soul is cast, one will return to the former post; a Returning Soul changing into a Returning Soul means that by right one should retire from office; if one meets a Wandering Soul and the star of office is again thriving, one is surely raised to another province; a Wandering Soul changing into a Wandering Soul means being promoted and then promoted again to a far place.

> [!note] 按
> 「游魂」「归魂」是八宫卦里每宫的第七、第八卦（见术语表）。「复任」是回到原来的任上，「致仕」是退休。归魂变归魂「理应致仕」，与易隐官禄占「游魂化归魂宜致仕」（YY.20）一样落在「归」字上，但前提不同：那里是游魂变归魂，这里是归魂变归魂。
>
> *Note:* 'Wandering Soul' and 'Returning Soul' are the seventh and eighth hexagrams of each of the eight palaces (see the glossary). 'Fu ren' is returning to one's former post and 'zhi shi' is retiring from office. That a Returning Soul changing into a Returning Soul 'by right should retire' falls on the word 'return' just as 'a Wandering Soul changing into a Returning Soul suits retiring' does in the Yi Yin divination for official salary and rank (YY.20), but the starting point differs: there a Wandering Soul changes into a Returning Soul, here a Returning Soul changes into a Returning Soul.
