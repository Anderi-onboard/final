# 世在离宫眼露聪明性急、身居乾象面圆正大仁慈：离属火为目，乾属金为首

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世在离宫，这人眼睛外露、聪明、性急；身在乾象，这人脸圆、正大、仁慈。离属火，为目；乾属金，为首；各取它的形体和德性来说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self in the palace of Li the man's eyes show, he is clever and quick-tempered; with the Body in the Qian image his face is round and he is upright and magnanimous and benevolent. Li belongs to Fire and is the eye; Qian belongs to Metal and is the head; each is taken by its bodily form and its virtue.
