# 六亲断例·持世：官鬼持世必得官（官化官为禄、求官宜疾速、化子必伤官）；子孙持世谒贵求官反不亨；父母持世旺相文书喜信通（化鬼身迁举）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼持世，必定得官，文书、印信两样一起看；官化官是得禄，求官宜赶快；化财，占病凶；化父，文书相随；化子，必伤官；化兄，家里不和。子孙持世是福神，事成忧散，占胎问病重重吉利，谒见贵人、求官反而不亨通。父母持世及身宫，旺相，文书、喜信通达；父母化父母，文书一定应许；化子添人口；化鬼，自身得迁升荐举；化财，住宅长忧；兄弟，要从本身去取。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure holding the Self one is sure to get office, and the documents and the seal are looked at together; Pressure turning into Pressure is gaining emolument, and in seeking office one should be swift; turning into Wealth, in illness it is bad; turning into Resource, the documents follow one after another; turning into Output, it surely injures office; turning into Peer, the household is not harmonious. Output holding the Self is the spirit of blessing: matters succeed and worries scatter, and in pregnancy and illness it is good upon good, but in calling on a noble or seeking office it does not go through. With Resource holding the Self and the palace of the Body, strong, documents and glad news go through; Resource turning into Resource, the documents are sure to be granted; turning into Output, people are added; turning into Pressure, one is promoted and recommended; turning into Wealth, the dwelling long worries; for Peer, take it from oneself.

## 《周易尚占·六亲断例》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 475 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Judgments by example for the six relations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母持世及身宫， | 父母持世以及身宫， | Resource holding the Self and the palace of the Body, |
> | 旺相文书喜信通， | 旺相，文书、喜信通达； | strong, documents and glad news go through; |
> | 田宅禾苗皆遂意， | 田宅、禾苗都遂意； | fields, houses and crops all as one wishes; |
> | 占身问病却成凶。 | 占自身、问疾病，却成凶。 | but for the self and for illness it turns ill. |
> | 父母化父母， | 父母化父母， | Resource turning into Resource, |
> | 文书定相许， | 文书一定应许； | the documents are sure to be granted; |
> | 化子进人丁， | 化成子，添进人口； | turning into Output, people are added; |
> | 化鬼身迁举， | 化成鬼，自身得迁升荐举； | turning into Pressure, one is promoted and recommended; |
> | 化财宅长忧， | 化成财，住宅长久忧愁； | turning into Wealth, the dwelling long worries; |
> | 兄弟本身取。 | 兄弟，要从本身去取。 | Peer: take it from oneself. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「化父文书逐」的「逐」没有解释，这里读作「文书相随而来」，不确定；「兄弟本身取」也没有解释，按字面译。这三行各问题通用，这里按次序把官鬼持世、子孙持世、父母持世三行都收了。
>
> *Note:* 'Turning into Resource, the documents chase' does not explain the 'chase', and it is read here as 'the documents follow one after another', which is uncertain; 'Peer, take it from oneself' is not explained either and is translated literally. These three lines serve every kind of question, and the lines for Pressure, Output and Resource holding the Self are all taken here in order.
