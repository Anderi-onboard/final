# 仕宦·鬼化福冲当代职（旧注：出巡官宜鬼动、牧守官宜鬼静；鬼动化子必有别官代职；子动化鬼先难后易；官福皆动亦主有官替代）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出巡的官，适合官鬼发动；牧守一类的官，适合官鬼安静。如果官鬼发动、化出子孙，必有别的官员来代理职务，不然也会被别人抢在前面；子孙发动化出官鬼，就是先难后易，或者先凶后吉；官鬼和子孙都发动，也主有官员来接替。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For an officer on tour, it is fitting that Pressure moves; for a governor or magistrate, it is fitting that Pressure is still. If Pressure moves and turns into Output, another officer will surely come to take over the post, or one is otherwise forestalled by someone else; if Output moves and turns into Pressure, it is hard first and easy later, or ominous first and fortunate later; if both Pressure and Output move, it likewise means an officer comes to replace one.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文的「福冲」是说官鬼动化出子孙（福）、又被冲；「当代职」是「会有人来替代」。出巡官与牧守官对官鬼动静的要求相反：出巡是外出差使，官鬼动才有事可办；牧守是坐镇一地，官鬼静才稳。同一个判据又见 LY.CAREER.HJC.22（牧守）和 LY.CAREER.HJC.23（出巡）。
>
> *Note:* 'Turns into Output and is clashed' in the verse means Pressure moving and turning into Output (the 'blessing' line) and then being clashed, and 'another takes over the post' means that someone will come to replace one. An officer on tour and a governor or magistrate need opposite things of Pressure: touring is a commission outside, where Pressure must move for there to be business to do, and a magistrate is posted in one place, where Pressure must be still for the post to be steady. The same criteria are met again in LY.CAREER.HJC.22 (governing) and LY.CAREER.HJC.23 (touring).
