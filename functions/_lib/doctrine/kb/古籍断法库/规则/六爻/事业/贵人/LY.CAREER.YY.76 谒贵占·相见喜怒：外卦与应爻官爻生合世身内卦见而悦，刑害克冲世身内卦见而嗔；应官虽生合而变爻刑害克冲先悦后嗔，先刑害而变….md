# 谒贵占·相见喜怒：外卦与应爻官爻生合世身内卦见而悦，刑害克冲世身内卦见而嗔；应官虽生合而变爻刑害克冲先悦后嗔，先刑害而变来生合先怒后善；应官受日辰动爻刑害克破或化死墓绝胎彼有灾病；加龙和蔼多情、雀多言易怒、勾率直无文、蛇多疑寡信、虎狠毒无恩、武狡诈多端

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 见面时他是喜是怒：外卦和应爻、官爻，生合世爻、身爻和内卦的，见面时他高兴；刑、害、克、冲世爻、身爻和内卦的，见面时他恼怒。应爻、官爻虽然生合身爻、世爻，而变爻刑、害、克、冲的，先高兴后恼怒；先刑、害、克、冲身爻、世爻，而变爻来生合的，先恼怒后和善。应爻、官爻受日辰、动爻刑、害、克、破，或变成死、墓、绝、胎的，他有灾病祸事。加青龙发动的，和气多情；加朱雀发动的，话多易怒；加勾陈发动的，直率没有文采；加螣蛇发动的，多疑少信；加白虎发动的，狠毒没有恩情；加玄武发动的，狡诈多端。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether he is pleased or angry at the meeting. If the outer trigram and the Other and Pressure lines generate or combine with the Self, Body and inner trigram, he is pleased at the meeting; if they punish, harm, control or clash with the Self, Body and inner trigram, he is angry. If the Other and Pressure lines generate or combine with the Body and Self but the changed line punishes, harms, controls or clashes with them, he is pleased first and angry after; if they first punish, harm, control or clash with the Body and Self and the changed line comes to generate or combine, he is angry first and kind after. If the Other and Pressure lines are punished, harmed, controlled or broken by the Day or a moving line, or turn into death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, he has illness or disaster. With the Azure Dragon moving, he is genial and warm; with the Bird, talkative and quick to anger; with Hook Earth, blunt and unrefined; with the Snake, suspicious and little trusting; with the Tiger, fierce and without kindness; with the Dark Tortoise, deceitful in many ways.
