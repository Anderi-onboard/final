# 出师征伐：八门与官鬼子孙——鬼值坤宫嫌死门、子临兑卦利惊门，伤门云凶而震有将星征反胜、休门云吉而坎兴大杀战还输，艮发青龙生方大利、乾摇白虎开处非宜，离福交重景上喜悦、巽发杜门战去暌违

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 八门要和官鬼、子孙的位置合着看：官鬼值在坤宫，嫌死门；子孙临在兑卦，只利惊门。伤门说是凶，但震宫发动带将星，出征反而得胜；休门说是吉，但坎宫兴起大杀，作战反而输。艮宫发动青龙，往生门的方向大利；乾宫摇动白虎，走开门的地方不适宜；离宫福神重叠发动，走景门能得喜悦；巽宫发动，走杜门去作战相差很远。将军和兵卒要知道忌讳进哪个门、利于走哪个方向，须推算官鬼值在哪一宫、官星临在哪个卦象，不要进那一门；又看子孙在哪一卦、福神在哪一爻，宜从那个门走。比如坤宫官鬼发动，最忌死门；兑卦子孙摇动，惊门可以进；伤门是祸害，但震宫有将星、福德，就是好的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Eight Gates are read together with the positions of Pressure and Output. Pressure on the Kun palace dislikes the Death gate; Output on the Dui trigram favours only the Fright gate. The Harm gate is said to be ill, yet if Zhen moves with the General star, a campaign wins instead; the Rest gate is said to be auspicious, yet if Kan raises a great baleful spirit, the battle is lost instead. If the Azure Dragon moves in Gen, the direction of the Life gate is greatly favourable; if the White Tiger stirs in Qian, the place of the Open gate is unsuitable; if the fortune spirit doubles in Li, going by the Scene gate brings joy; if Xun moves, going to battle by the Block gate goes much against one. Commanders and soldiers who want to know which gate to avoid and which direction to take must work out in which palace Pressure sits and on which image the star of office lies, and not enter that gate; again, see in which trigram Output lies and on which line the fortune spirit is, and take that gate. For example, if Pressure moves in the Kun palace, the Death gate is most to be avoided; if Output stirs in Dui, the Fright gate may be entered; the Harm gate is a calamity, but if there is the General star and Fortune Virtue (Output) in the Zhen palace it is good.

> [!note] 按
> 「死户」「景上」的「户」「上」都是门。「鬼值坤宫」按八卦的方位对应奇门八门：坎休、艮生、震伤、巽杜、离景、坤死、兑惊、乾开。「将星」「大杀」是神煞（起例见 EL.16）。第二联「伤户云凶……休门曰吉」是反例：凶门里有吉的爻象仍然可行，吉门里有凶的爻象也不可行，和 EL.14 的「鬼煞司权纵往开休生不吉」是同一个意思。「暌违」按字面是背离，译作「相差很远」。
>
> *Note:* In 'si hu' and 'jing shang' the 'hu' and 'shang' stand for gates. 'Pressure on the Kun palace' follows the matching of the eight trigrams with the Eight Gates of Qimen: Kan Rest, Gen Life, Zhen Harm, Xun Block, Li Scene, Kun Death, Dui Fright, Qian Open. The 'General star' and the 'great baleful spirit' are spirit-stars (their rules of setting out are in EL.16). The second couplet, 'the Harm gate is said to be ill ... the Rest gate is said to be good', gives the counter-cases: an ill gate with an auspicious line image can still be taken, and an auspicious gate with an ill line image cannot, which is the same sense as 'if Pressure and baleful spirits hold power, going by Open, Rest and Life is not auspicious' in EL.14. 'Kuiwei' is by the letter going against, and is translated 'goes much against one'.
