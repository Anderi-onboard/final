# 收雇：三冲不久；收仆以财为用神——财神空则身从心不向、破散则轻疾病重丧亡、绝衰则不得其用、旺而生合世身则手足奉腹心、旺而克冲世则干仆加弱主；未收占臧否止忌三冲财病虽克不嫌、已收占去留亦忌三冲财动克世当去

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是占收雇，三冲就不长久。收仆是为了备驱使差遣的人，以财为用神：所以财神空，人虽然跟着我，心却不向着我；财神破、散，轻则疾病、重则丧亡；财神绝、衰，用他不上；财神旺而生合世身的，这是手足来奉养腹心；财神旺而克冲世的，这是干练的仆人欺凌软弱的主人。所以未收时占好坏，只忌三冲、财有病，即使克（世）也不嫌；已经收了占去留，也忌三冲、财动，克世就该让他走。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In all divinations about taking on and hiring, the three clashes do not last. Taking on a servant is to have someone to be driven and sent about, and Wealth is the Subject: so with the Wealth spirit Void, the man follows in person but his heart is not toward me; with the Wealth spirit broken or scattered, there is illness at the lightest and death at the worst; with the Wealth spirit extinct or weak, one cannot use him; with the Wealth spirit thriving and generating or combining with the Self, this is hands and feet serving heart and belly; with the Wealth spirit thriving and controlling or clashing with the Self, this is a capable servant bullying a weak master. So when divining before taking him on whether he is good or bad, one fears only the three clashes and a sick Wealth, and even controlling is not objected to; when divining after taking him on whether he will go or stay, one also fears the three clashes and Wealth moving, and when it controls the Self he should go.

> [!note] 按
> 「收雇」是收用仆人、雇工，「驱役之使」是供驱使差遣的人。「财神」是妻财爻。「手足奉腹心」是手足供养腹心，比喻仆人尽心奉主；「干仆加弱主」是能干的仆人欺凌软弱的主人。「臧否」是好坏。「三冲」见 YM.07 注。「虽克不嫌」是还没收的时候财克世也不算坏；「克世当去」是已经收了的，财克世就该让他走。书里自注：未收之时，财弱生世看着吉却不吉，财强克世看着凶却不凶；已收之后问忠奸去留，克世就是辜负自己，生世才是尽心——不能拿『吉神』的名义去说克世不凶。章首目录列的是雇工、请人、去恶、防夺、防非、防病六问。与易林补遗用人藏否（EL.131–133）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Shougu' is taking on servants and hired hands, and 'quyi zhi shi' is those who are driven and sent about. 'The Wealth spirit' is the Wealth line. 'Hands and feet serving heart and belly' means a servant serving his master with all his heart; 'a capable servant bullying a weak master' is just that. 'Zangpi' is good or bad. For the 'three clashes' see the note to YM.07. 'Even controlling is not objected to' means that before he is taken on, Wealth controlling the Self is not counted as bad; 'when it controls the Self he should go' means that once he is taken on, Wealth controlling the Self means he should be let go. The book's own gloss: before he is taken on, weak Wealth generating the Self looks auspicious but is not, and strong Wealth controlling the Self looks ominous but is not; after he is taken on, in asking about loyalty or treachery and going or staying, controlling the Self is failing oneself and generating the Self is serving with all his heart, and one cannot use the name of an 'auspicious spirit' to say that controlling the Self is not ominous. The index at the head of the chapter lists six questions: hiring labour, engaging a person, getting rid of a bad one, guarding against being robbed of him, against slander, and against illness. Read against the Yilin Buyi on employing people and whether they hide (EL.131-133).
