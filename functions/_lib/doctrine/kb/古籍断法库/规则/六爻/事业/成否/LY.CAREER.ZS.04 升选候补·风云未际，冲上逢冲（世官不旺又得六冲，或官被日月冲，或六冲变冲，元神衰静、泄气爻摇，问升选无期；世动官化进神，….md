# 升选候补·风云未际，冲上逢冲（世官不旺又得六冲，或官被日月冲，或六冲变冲，元神衰静、泄气爻摇，问升选无期；世动官化进神，升迁在即）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 时机未到的征兆：世爻、官星都不旺，又得六冲卦，或者官星被日月相冲，或者六冲变六冲；元神衰弱安静，或者泄元神之气的爻发动，问升选就没有日期。反过来，世爻发动、官星变成进神，升迁就在眼前；世爻发动或变吉，或禄、马、贵人临世，或官星发动变吉、生合世爻，都可以收拾行装等着。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Signs that the moment has not come: the Self and the star of office are not thriving and the hexagram is Six-Clash, or the star of office is clashed by the Day or Month, or a Six-Clash hexagram turns into Six-Clash; or the Support is weak and still, or the line that drains it moves, and then promotion has no date. The reverse: if the Self moves and the star of office turns into an Advancing line, promotion is at hand; if the Self moves, or turns auspicious, or Emolument, the Horse and a Noble sit on the Self, or the star of office moves, turns auspicious and generates or combines with the Self, one may pack one's bags and wait.
