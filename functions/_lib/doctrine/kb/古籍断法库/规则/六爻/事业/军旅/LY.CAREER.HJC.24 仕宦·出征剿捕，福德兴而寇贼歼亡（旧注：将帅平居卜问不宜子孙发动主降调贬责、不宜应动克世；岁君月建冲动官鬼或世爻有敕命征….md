# 仕宦·出征剿捕，福德兴而寇贼歼亡（旧注：将帅平居卜问不宜子孙发动主降调贬责、不宜应动克世；岁君月建冲动官鬼或世爻有敕命征讨；临敌卜问喜子孙发动必成剿捕之功；官鬼当贼寇论，世克应亦吉）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 担任将帅或征讨之官的人，平时占问，不适宜子孙发动，主有降职调任、贬责，也不适宜应爻发动克世爻，主有意想不到的变故；太岁、月建冲动官鬼或世爻，主有敕命征讨的事。如果临阵对敌占问，就喜欢子孙发动，必成剿捕的功绩，再得到太岁、月建生合，还有升赏；这时官鬼不当作爵位，要当作贼寇来论，世克应也吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For one holding the post of commander or an officer of punitive expeditions, in divining in ordinary times it is not fitting that Output moves, which means demotion, transfer and rebuke, nor that the Other moves and controls the Self, which means an unforeseen change; if the Tai Sui or the Month clashes Pressure or the Self into motion, there will be an imperial order for a punitive expedition. If the divination is made facing the enemy, it is welcome that Output moves, and one will surely achieve merit in suppressing the bandits, and if the Tai Sui and the Month also generate or combine, there will be promotion and reward as well; Pressure is then not taken as rank but as the bandits, and the Self controlling the Other is also auspicious.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联要分两种问法：平时问（还没有开战，问自己的仕途）和临阵问（敌人当前，问这一仗）。平时问，子孙是「伤官之杀」，发动就降调贬责；临阵问，子孙是「克鬼」的神（官鬼这时是贼寇），发动就能灭贼。所以同一个子孙发动，平时凶、临阵吉，这和 LY.CAREER.HJC.03（子孙发动剥官削职）不矛盾，是用神换了：官鬼不再是你的官，而是你的敌人。「敕命征讨」是皇帝下令出兵，「剿捕」是围剿捕捉。
>
> *Note:* This couplet must be read for two kinds of question: in ordinary times (no war has begun, and one asks about one's own career) and facing the enemy (the enemy is before one, and one asks about this battle). In ordinary times Output is 'the killer that harms office', and moving brings demotion and rebuke; facing the enemy Output is the spirit that controls Pressure (which is then the bandits), and moving can wipe them out. So the same moving Output is ominous in ordinary times and auspicious facing the enemy, which does not contradict LY.CAREER.HJC.03 (Output moving strips the office): the Subject has changed, and Pressure is no longer your office but your enemy. 'An imperial order for a punitive expedition' is the emperor ordering out the army, and 'suppress' is surrounding and seizing.
