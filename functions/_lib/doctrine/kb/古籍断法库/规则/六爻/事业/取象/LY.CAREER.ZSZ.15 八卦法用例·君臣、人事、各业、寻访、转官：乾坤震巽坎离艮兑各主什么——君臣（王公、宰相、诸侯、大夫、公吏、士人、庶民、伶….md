# 八卦法用例·君臣、人事、各业、寻访、转官：乾坤震巽坎离艮兑各主什么——君臣（王公、宰相、诸侯、大夫、公吏、士人、庶民、伶伦）；转官（朝廷、任所、差遣、宣命、印绶、保明、复任、解田）等

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 八卦法用例里和事业有关的几行，按乾、坤、震、巽、坎、离、艮、兑的次序：君臣——王公、宰相、诸侯、大夫、公吏、士人、庶民、伶伦；人事——官事、家事、身事、公事、忧事、心事、闲事、喜事；各业——士、农、工、商、武、文、医、卜；寻访——贵人、主人、客人、行人、吏人、士人、商人、匠人；转官——朝廷、任所、差遣、宣命、印绶、保明、复任、解田。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The rows of the 'uses of the eight trigrams' bearing on careers, in the order Qian, Kun, Zhen, Xun, Kan, Li, Gen, Dui: lord and ministers: prince and duke, chancellor, feudal lord, grandee, public clerk, scholar, commoner, musician; human affairs: officials, household, oneself, public business, worries, the heart, idle matters, joyful matters; trades: scholar, farmer, artisan, merchant, soldier, literary man, doctor, diviner; seeking and visiting: the noble, the host, the guest, the traveller, the clerk, the scholar, the merchant, the craftsman; transfer of office: the court, the place of the post, the assignment, the proclaimed commission, the seal and ribbon, the certification, reinstatement, discharge.

## 《周易尚占·八卦法用例》原文

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 转官 | 调转官职 | Transfer of office: |
> | 朝廷 | 朝廷上 | the court, |
> | 任所 | 任职的地方 | the place of the post, |
> | 差遣 | 差遣的委派 | the assignment, |
> | 宣命 | 宣读的任命 | the proclaimed commission, |
> | 印绶 | 印信绶带 | the seal and ribbon, |
> | 保明 | 保证查明 | the certification, |
> | 复任 | 重新任职 | reinstatement, |
> | 解田 | 解田（疑是解由） | discharge (the 'jietian' is probably a slip for 'jieyou'). |

^q6

> [!note] 按
> 这是一张表，每一行是「标题 + 八格」，八格依次对应乾、坤、震、巽、坎、离、艮、兑。「转官」一行最后一格「解田」，六位例的「转官」行里相应的是「解由」（离任的文书），「解田」疑是「解由」之误。
>
> *Note:* This is a table, each row being a heading and eight cells corresponding in order to Qian, Kun, Zhen, Xun, Kan, Li, Gen and Dui. The last cell of the 'transfer of office' row, 'jietian', corresponds in the 'transfer of office' row of the table of the six positions to 'jieyou' (the certificate of discharge), and 'jietian' is probably a slip for it.
