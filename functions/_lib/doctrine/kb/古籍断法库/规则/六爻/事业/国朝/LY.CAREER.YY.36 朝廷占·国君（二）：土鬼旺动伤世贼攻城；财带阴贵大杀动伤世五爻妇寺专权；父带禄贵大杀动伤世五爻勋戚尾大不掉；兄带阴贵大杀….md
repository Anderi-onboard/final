# 朝廷占·国君（二）：土鬼旺动伤世贼攻城；财带阴贵大杀动伤世五爻妇寺专权；父带禄贵大杀动伤世五爻勋戚尾大不掉；兄带阴贵大杀动伤世五爻贵戚骄恣；子带华盖大杀动伤世五爻妖僧方士蛊惑君心

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国君身边的祸患：土爻的官鬼旺相发动、伤世爻的，贼寇攻城；财爻带阴贵人、大杀发动，伤世爻、五爻的，后宫妇人和宦官专权，蛀蚀国家；父爻带禄、贵人、大杀发动，伤世爻、五爻的，功臣外戚尾大不掉；兄爻带阴贵人、大杀发动，伤世爻、五爻的，皇亲国戚倚仗恩宠骄横放纵；子孙爻带华盖、大杀发动，伤世爻、五爻的，妖僧和方士蛊惑君主的心。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Dangers about the sovereign. If an Earth Pressure line is thriving and moving and injures the Self, bandits attack the city; if the Wealth line carrying the Yin Noble and a great baleful spirit moves to injure the Self and the fifth line, women of the palace and eunuchs seize power and eat away at the state; if the Resource line carrying Emolument, the Noble and a great baleful spirit does so, the meritorious ministers and imperial in-laws become too big to control; if the Peer line carrying the Yin Noble and a great baleful spirit does so, the imperial kin presume on favour and are arrogant and unbridled; if the Output line carrying Canopy and a great baleful spirit does so, evil monks and alchemists bewitch the sovereign's mind.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「妇寺」是后宫的妇人和寺人（宦官）；「勋戚」是功臣和外戚；「尾大不悼」的「悼」疑是「掉」（尾大不掉），译文按「掉」译；「方士」是炼丹求仙的术士；「阴贵」是阴贵人，「华盖」是一颗主孤高、宗教的神煞，书里没有解释。
>
> *Note:* 'Fusi' is the women of the palace and the 'si', the eunuchs; 'xunqi' is the meritorious ministers and the imperial in-laws; in 'weida budao' the character 'dao', grief, is presumably a miswriting of 'diao', to swing, in the saying 'the tail too big to wag', and the translation follows it; 'fangshi' are the magicians who make elixirs and seek immortality; 'Yin Noble' is the yin form of the Noble, and 'Canopy' is a spirit-star of aloofness and religion that the book does not explain.
