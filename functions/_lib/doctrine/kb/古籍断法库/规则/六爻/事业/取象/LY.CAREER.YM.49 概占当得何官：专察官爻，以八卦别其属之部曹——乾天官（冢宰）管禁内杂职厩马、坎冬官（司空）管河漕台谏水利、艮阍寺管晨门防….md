# 概占当得何官：专察官爻，以八卦别其属之部曹——乾天官（冢宰）管禁内杂职厩马、坎冬官（司空）管河漕台谏水利、艮阍寺管晨门防卫狱禁矿藏宝库钱局、震春官（宗伯）管东宫师傅试务宗庙礼乐文章、巽天使管上林与奉命四方之上差、离夏官（司马）管耳目纠弹修史、坤地官（司徒）管农事疆场社稷母后内臣、兑秋官（司寇）管言语刑罚章疏上膳

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 总的占该得什么官，专门察看官爻，用八卦分它属于哪个部曹：乾是天官（古称冢宰），管朝廷禁内的杂职、厩马；坎是冬官（古称司空），管总河、总漕之务和台谏，属天下水利的官；艮是阍寺，管晨门的官吏、防御守卫的僚属、治狱禁，属矿藏、宝库、钱局一类；震是春官（古称宗伯），是太子东宫的师傅，或总试、总裁，以及管宗庙祭祀、礼乐文章的官；巽是天使，以及管上林苑、奉命向四方宣布的上差的僚佐；离是夏官（古称司马），专管耳目之权、纠弹之事，又一说是端门的辅臣、修史的文明之官；坤是地官（古称司徒），管天下农事，是有土之官，担疆场社稷之任，是皇太后的内臣；兑是秋官（古称司寇），管言语、主刑罚、通章疏、主皇上膳食之事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine in general what office one will get, look only at the Pressure line, and tell by the eight trigrams the department it belongs to: Qian is the Heavenly Office (anciently called the Grand Steward), governing miscellaneous posts within the forbidden palace and the stable horses; Kan is the Winter Office (anciently the Minister of Works), governing the affairs of the river-conservancy and grain-transport directorates and the censors and remonstrators, officials of the waterworks of the realm; Gen is the palace gatekeeper, governing the officers of the dawn gate, the officials of defence and guard, the management of prisons, and things like mines, treasuries and mints; Zhen is the Spring Office (anciently the Elder of the Ancestral Temple), the tutors of the Crown Prince's Eastern Palace, or chief examiners and chief compilers, and the officials who manage the ancestral temples, sacrifices, rites, music and letters; Xun is the Heavenly Envoy, and those who manage the Shanglin park and the assistants of senior envoys sent with orders to the four quarters; Li is the Summer Office (anciently the Marshal), governing the power of ears and eyes and the work of impeachment, or also the supporting ministers of the Duan Gate and the civilised office of compiling histories; Kun is the Earth Office (anciently the Minister of the Multitude), governing the farming of the realm, officials with land, who carry the charge of the borders and the altars of the state, and the inner servants of the Empress Dowager; Dui is the Autumn Office (anciently the Minister of Crime), governing speech, in charge of punishments, passing memorials, and having the charge of the sovereign's meals.

> [!note] 按
> 天官、地官、春官、夏官、秋官、冬官是《周礼》的六官（冢宰、司徒、宗伯、司马、司寇、司空），后世分别相当于吏部、户部、礼部、兵部、刑部、工部。八卦配六官：乾配天官、坤配地官、震配春官、离配夏官、兑配秋官、坎配冬官，艮和巽另配阍寺（守门的）和天使（奉使的）。「木天」是翰林院的别称。「总河」「总漕」是总督河道、总督漕运。「台谏」是御史和谏官。「阍寺」是守宫门的官，「晨门」是清晨守城门的。「钱局」是铸钱的局。「东宫」是太子居处。「上林」是皇家的上林苑。「上差」是上司派出的差官。「端门」是宫殿的正南门。「一」在这类注里是「又一说」的意思（下面 YM.50 同）。「司台谏」的「司」按文意是管。这一条与易隐官禄占的何官（YY.03–YY.06）取同一类：先按官爻定官，再按所在宫、五行、爻位和六神细分。
>
> *Note:* The Heavenly, Earth, Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter Offices are the six offices of the Rites of Zhou (the Grand Steward, the Minister of the Multitude, the Elder of the Ancestral Temple, the Marshal, the Minister of Crime and the Minister of Works), which later correspond to the Boards of Personnel, Revenue, Rites, War, Punishments and Works. The eight trigrams are matched to the six offices: Qian to the Heavenly Office, Kun to the Earth Office, Zhen to the Spring Office, Li to the Summer Office, Dui to the Autumn Office and Kan to the Winter Office, while Gen and Xun are matched separately to the palace gatekeepers (those who keep the gates) and the Heavenly Envoy (those sent on missions). 'Wood heaven' is a name for the Hanlin Academy. 'Zonghe' and 'zongcao' are the governors-general of river conservancy and of grain transport. 'Taijian' are the censors and remonstrators. 'Hunsi' are the officials who guard the palace gates, and 'chenmen' are those who keep the city gate at dawn. 'Qianju' is the mint. 'Donggong' is where the Crown Prince lives. 'Shanglin' is the imperial Shanglin park. 'Shangchai' is an envoy sent out by a superior. 'Duanmen' is the main south gate of a palace. 'Yi', in notes of this kind, means 'another view' (the same in YM.50 below). The 'si' of 'si taijian' is read by the sense as 'govern'. This entry is of a kind with 'which office' in the Yi Yin on official careers (YY.03–YY.06): first fix the office by the Pressure line, then divide it more finely by palace, phase, line position and the six spirits.
