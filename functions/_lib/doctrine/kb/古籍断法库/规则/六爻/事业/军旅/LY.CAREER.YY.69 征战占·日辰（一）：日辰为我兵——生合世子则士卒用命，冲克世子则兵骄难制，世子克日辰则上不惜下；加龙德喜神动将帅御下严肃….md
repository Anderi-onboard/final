# 征战占·日辰（一）：日辰为我兵——生合世子则士卒用命，冲克世子则兵骄难制，世子克日辰则上不惜下；加龙德喜神动将帅御下严肃；日辰克应克鬼我兵胜，应鬼克日辰敌兵强

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰看我方的兵：日辰代表我方的兵。日辰生合世爻、子孙爻的，士卒听命效力；冲克世爻、子孙爻的，兵骄横难以管束；世爻、子孙爻克日辰的，上面的人不爱惜下面的人；日辰加青龙、德神、喜神而发动的，将帅统御部下严肃；日辰克应爻、克官鬼的，我方兵胜；应爻、官鬼克日辰的，敌兵强。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Day and my troops. The Day stands for my soldiers. If the Day generates or combines with the Self or Output, the soldiers obey orders and serve; if it clashes with or controls the Self or Output, the troops are proud and hard to rein in; if the Self or Output controls the Day, those above do not spare those below; if the Day adds the Azure Dragon, Virtue and Joy and moves, the commander governs his men with strictness and dignity; if the Day controls the Other and Pressure, my troops win; if the Other or Pressure controls the Day, the enemy troops are strong.
