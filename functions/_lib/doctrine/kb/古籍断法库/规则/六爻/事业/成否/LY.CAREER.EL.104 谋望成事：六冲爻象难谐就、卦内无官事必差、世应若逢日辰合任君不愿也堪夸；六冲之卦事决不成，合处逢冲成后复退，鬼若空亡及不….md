# 谋望成事：六冲爻象难谐就、卦内无官事必差、世应若逢日辰合任君不愿也堪夸；六冲之卦事决不成，合处逢冲成后复退，鬼若空亡及不上卦皆不就，世应二爻自相会合或得日辰合世合应纵若心中不欲也得允成

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六冲的爻象难以谐成，卦里没有官爻，事一定出差错；世应如果逢日辰来合，任凭你不愿意也值得夸赞。六冲的卦，事一定不成；合的地方遇到冲，成了之后还会退回；官鬼如果空亡或者不上卦，都办不成；世、应二爻自相会合，或者得到日辰来合世、合应，即使心里不想，也能得到允准办成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A six-clash image is hard to bring to harmony, and with no star of office in the hexagram the affair is sure to go wrong; if the Self and the Other meet a combination from the Day, even if you do not wish it, it is worth praising. With a six-clash hexagram the affair will surely not succeed; if where there is a combination it meets a clash, after it succeeds it falls back again; if Pressure is Void or not in the hexagram, in neither case does it succeed; if the Self and the Other lines combine with each other, or the Day combines with the Self or the Other, even if one does not wish it in one's heart, it will be granted and accomplished.

> [!note] 按
> 「合处逢冲，成后复退」与求馆章「如合处逢冲或变出六冲，虽见成就，其年定主不终」（EL.53）取同一个关系。「任君不愿」「心中不欲」书里没有说是谁心里不愿，这里按字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Where there is a combination it meets a clash, after it succeeds it falls back' takes the same relation as 'if where there is a combination it meets a clash, or it changes out a six-clash, then even if it seems settled that year it will not last' in the chapter on seeking a school post (EL.53). The book does not say whose heart it is that is unwilling in 'even if you do not wish it' and 'even if one does not wish it in one's heart', and it is translated literally.
