# 升选候补·雨露承恩，名中逢合（世与官星三合官局、财局，或局生世、世在局中，或官星持世与日月动爻相合，得六合卦及动变六合，皆主承恩之日；书中例：官局生应不生世，此缺不得）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻与官星三合成官局或财局，或者这个局生世爻，或者世爻在局中，或者官星持世、又与日月动爻相合，得到六合卦，以及发动变成六合卦的，都是得到皇恩的日子。但如果官局生的是应爻、不是世爻，这叫「出现无情」，这个缺得不到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self and the star of office form a three-harmony Pressure frame or Wealth frame, or the frame generates the Self, or the Self is within the frame, or the star of office holds the Self and combines with the Day, the Month or a moving line, or one gets a Six-Combine hexagram or a hexagram that moves into Six-Combine, it is the day of receiving the ruler's favour. But if the Pressure frame generates the Other and not the Self, this is called 'appearing but without feeling', and the post cannot be had.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10350 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 断曰：内卦巳酉丑合成官局，不生世而生应，正所谓“出现无情”也，此缺不得。 | 断语说：内卦巳、酉、丑合成官局，不生世爻而生应爻，正是所谓「出现无情」，这个缺得不到。 | The judgment said: the inner trigram Si, You and Chou combines into a Pressure frame, which does not generate the Self but generates the Other, which is just what is meant by 'appearing but without feeling', and this post cannot be had. |
> | 彼问：如何不得？ | 他问：为什么得不到？ | He asked: why can it not be had? |
> | 余曰：官生应，如何能得？ | 我说：官星生的是应爻，怎么能得到？ | I said: the star of office generates the Other, so how could he get it? |
> | 后果另选别人。 | 后来果然另选了别人。 | In the end someone else was indeed chosen. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「出现无情」书里只用在这个例子上，没有另外解释；按断语的意思，是官局出现了，却不生世、去生了应爻。
>
> *Note:* The book uses 'appearing but without feeling' only in this example and does not explain it elsewhere; from the judgment, it means that the Pressure frame appears but does not generate the Self and instead generates the Other.
