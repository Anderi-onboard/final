# 谒贵占·见否（一）：外卦与应爻官爻属阴在家、属阳出外；阳变阴外方回、阴变阳方出外；应爻官爻出现在家、伏藏不在

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 见不见得到，先看他在不在家：外卦和应爻、官爻属阴的，他在家；属阳的，他出外；阳变阴的，刚从外地回来；阴变阳的，刚要出外。应爻、官爻出现的，在家；伏藏的，不在。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether he can be seen, first whether he is at home. If the outer trigram and the Other and Pressure lines are yin, he is at home; if yang, he is out; yang changing into yin, he has just returned from elsewhere; yin changing into yang, he is just going out. If the Other and Pressure lines appear, he is at home; if they lie hidden, he is not in.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段的前四句里「外方回」「方出外」的「方」是刚刚。书里这一行被断行隔开（「方出外」在上一行末、「也」在下一行首），这里连起来读。
>
> *Note:* In the first four sentences the 'fang' of 'fang chu wai' is 'just now'. This passage is split by a line break in the book ('just going out' at the end of one line and 'ye' at the start of the next), and is read here joined up.
