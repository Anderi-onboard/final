# 官禄占·在任（四）：官化财非奖则荐；官贵旺动生合世目下蒙荐；岁月带父旺动朝有宣勅；太岁五爻动生合世征召入朝；官世临五爻或太岁得父动生合名书御榻；三传加财动生合世受赐赍

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任，荐举、宣敕和征召：官星变出财爻，不是受嘉奖就是被荐举；官星、贵人旺相发动、来生合世爻的，眼前就蒙人荐举；太岁、月建带着印星旺相发动的，朝廷有宣读的敕命；太岁与五爻发动、来生合世爻的，被征召入朝；官爻、世爻临在五爻或太岁上，又得印星发动来生合的，名字上了御榻；三传带着财爻发动、来生合世爻的，受赏赐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In office, recommendation, edicts and summons. If the star of office turns into Wealth, he is either commended or recommended; if the star of office and the Noble are thriving and moving and generate or combine with the Self, he is recommended right away; if Tai Sui and the Month carry the seal line, thriving and moving, an edict is proclaimed at court; if Tai Sui and the fifth line move to generate or combine with the Self, he is summoned to court; if the Pressure line and the Self sit on the fifth line or on Tai Sui and a moving seal line generates or combines with them, his name is written on the imperial couch; if the three rulers carrying Wealth move to generate or combine with the Self, he receives gifts.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「名书御榻」按字面译：名字写在御座旁，大概是说皇帝亲自留意、记下了他的名字。「宣勅」是宣读皇帝的敕命，「赐赍」是赏赐财物。三传在这部书里是太岁、月建、日辰（见术语表），不是大六壬的三传。
>
> *Note:* 'Ming shu yu ta' is translated by the letter: the name is written beside the imperial seat, presumably meaning that the emperor himself has noted his name. 'Xuan chi' is the proclamation of an imperial order and 'cixi' is a bestowal of goods. The three rulers in this book are Tai Sui, the Month and the Day (see the glossary), and not the three transmissions of Da Liu Ren.
