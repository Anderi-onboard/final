# 投充兵卒：世去克他宜出战、应来克我莫登场；世为我应为彼，若世克应爻则战必胜彼，应若克世彼必多能

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻去克对方，适宜出战；应爻来克我，不要登场。世爻是我，应爻是对方：如果世克应爻，打仗必定胜过对方；应如果克世，对方一定很有本事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self goes to control the other, it is fitting to go out and fight; if the Other comes to control me, do not enter the field. The Self is me and the Other is the enemy: if the Self controls the Other line, one will surely beat the other in battle; if the Other controls the Self, he is surely very able.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和前面的「官鬼是随军出战的大忌」是两层：官鬼看自己会不会遭难，世应看这一仗能不能胜。与出师征伐章（EL.12 起）、易隐征战占「世克应可战、应克世宜守防贼」（YY.54）和本书前面「世克应则大胜，应克世则难赢」（EL.64）取同一个关系。
>
> *Note:* This entry and the earlier 'Pressure is the great taboo of going out to battle' are two layers: Pressure shows whether one will meet disaster, and the Self and the Other show whether this battle can be won. It takes the same relation as the chapter on sending out troops (from EL.12), 'if the Self controls the Other one can fight, if the Other controls the Self defend and guard against bandits' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.54), and 'if the Self controls the Other it is a great victory, if the Other controls the Self it is hard to win' earlier in this book (EL.64).
