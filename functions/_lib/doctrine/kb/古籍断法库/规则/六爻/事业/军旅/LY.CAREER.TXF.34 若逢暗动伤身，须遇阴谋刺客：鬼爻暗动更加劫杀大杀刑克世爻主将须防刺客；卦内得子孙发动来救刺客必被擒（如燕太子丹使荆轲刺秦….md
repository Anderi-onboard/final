# 若逢暗动伤身，须遇阴谋刺客：鬼爻暗动更加劫杀大杀刑克世爻主将须防刺客；卦内得子孙发动来救刺客必被擒（如燕太子丹使荆轲刺秦王）；日辰冲动伏鬼伤飞爻须防手下有人谋己；卦中无救祸将及身；鬼旺日决见应验

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果遇到暗动伤身，就要遇上阴谋刺客。凡是鬼爻暗动，又加劫杀、大杀，刑克世爻，主将要防刺客；卦内如果有子孙发动来救，刺客一定被擒，好比燕太子丹派荆轲刺秦王这一类事；如果日辰冲动、伏着的鬼伤了飞爻，要防自己手下有人谋害自己；卦中没有救助，祸将及身；在鬼旺的日子，一定应验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If one meets a hidden stirring that harms the self, one will meet a plotting assassin. Whenever the Pressure line moves in secret, with the Robbery Killing and the Great Baleful Spirit added and punishing or controlling the Self line, the commanding general must guard against assassins; if Output in the hexagram moves to rescue, the assassin is surely caught, like the matter of Crown Prince Dan of Yan sending Jing Ke to stab the King of Qin; if the Day clashes and moves and the hidden Pressure injures the flying line, guard against someone among one's own men plotting against one; if there is no rescue in the hexagram, calamity is about to reach one; on a day when Pressure is strong it will surely be confirmed.
