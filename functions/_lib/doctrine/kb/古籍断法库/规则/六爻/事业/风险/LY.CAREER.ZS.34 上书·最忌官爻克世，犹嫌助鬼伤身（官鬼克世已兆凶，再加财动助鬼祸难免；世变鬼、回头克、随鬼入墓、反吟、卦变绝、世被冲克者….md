# 上书·最忌官爻克世，犹嫌助鬼伤身（官鬼克世已兆凶，再加财动助鬼祸难免；世变鬼、回头克、随鬼入墓、反吟、卦变绝、世被冲克者，事非重大急宜止之）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上书最忌官爻克世爻，也嫌弃帮助官鬼伤害自身：官鬼克世已经预兆凶象，再加上财发动来帮助官鬼的，灾祸难免；世爻变出官鬼、变出回头克、随官鬼入墓、卦得反吟、卦变成绝、世爻被冲克的，事情如果不是重大的，要赶紧停止。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a memorial what is most to be avoided is the Pressure line controlling the Self, and also disliked is helping Pressure to injure the Body: when Pressure controls the Self it already foretells ill, and if Wealth also moves to help Pressure the misfortune cannot be avoided; if the Self turns into Pressure, or into a returning control, or follows Pressure into the tomb, or the hexagram gets Reversed Chant or changes into extinction, or the Self is clashed or controlled, unless the matter is grave it should be stopped at once.
