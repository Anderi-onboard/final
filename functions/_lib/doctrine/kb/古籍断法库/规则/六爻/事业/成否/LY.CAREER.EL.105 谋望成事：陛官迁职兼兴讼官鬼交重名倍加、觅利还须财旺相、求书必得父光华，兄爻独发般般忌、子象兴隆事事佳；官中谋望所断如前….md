# 谋望成事：陛官迁职兼兴讼官鬼交重名倍加、觅利还须财旺相、求书必得父光华，兄爻独发般般忌、子象兴隆事事佳；官中谋望所断如前复喜官爻旺相独忌子摇，谋利息财忌空亡、斡文书不宜财动卦如无父终不能成，诸般谋事各嫌兄弟交重惟子动无不为佳功名独忌

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 升官迁职、兴讼，官鬼发动，名声更添；求利，还要财爻旺相；求书信，必须父爻有光彩；兄爻独自发动，样样都忌；子孙兴旺，事事都好。在官府里谋求的，断法同前，又喜官爻旺相，唯独忌讳子孙发动。谋求利息钱财，忌讳财空亡；如果是办理文书，不适宜财爻发动；卦里如果没有父爻，终究不能成。各种谋事，都嫌兄弟发动；只有子孙发动没有不好的，唯独功名一项忌讳。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For promotion and transfer in office, and for starting a lawsuit, if Pressure moves one's name is doubled; for seeking profit, Wealth must also be thriving; for seeking a letter, Resource must be bright; if the Peer line moves alone, everything is taboo; if Output flourishes, everything is good. For scheming in official business the judgment is as before, and one is again glad for the Pressure line to be thriving and alone fears Output moving. In scheming for interest and wealth, Void is to be avoided for Wealth; if one is managing a document, Wealth moving is not fitting; and if the hexagram has no Resource it cannot in the end succeed. In every kind of scheming, Peer moving is disliked; only Output moving is never anything but good, and for fame and office alone is it a taboo.

> [!note] 按
> 「陛官迁职」的「陛」是「升」（见仕宦升迁章）。「斡文书」的「斡」是转、办理。「父光华」是父爻有光彩，即旺相不受伤。子孙发动是各种谋事的吉兆，唯独功名、官职一项忌讳——这与前面仕宦升迁章「官职升迁子莫刚」（EL.55）是一回事：官职功名最忌子孙。
>
> *Note:* The 'bi' of 'bi guan qian zhi' is 'sheng', to rise (see the chapter on official promotion). The 'wo' of 'wo wenshu' is to turn and to manage. 'Fu guanghua' is Resource having lustre, that is thriving and unhurt. Output moving is an auspicious sign for every kind of scheming, and only for fame and office is it a taboo, which is the same thing as 'for promotion in office Output must not be strong' in the earlier chapter on official promotion (EL.55): office and fame most fear Output.
