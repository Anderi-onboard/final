# 贵人持世，九天雨露承恩：贵人有天乙贵人、福星贵人二种，宜旺相怕刑冲；临官鬼及文书主官职高迁必登相位；福贵持世主人有福德、一生见险无虞

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 贵人持世，承受九天雨露般的恩泽。贵人有两种：天乙贵人和福星贵人，宜旺相，怕刑、冲；落在官鬼和文书上，主官职高升、必登相位；福星、贵人持世，这人有福德，一生遇险不用忧虑，临到凶事也无祸咎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Noble holding the Self one receives favour like the rain and dew of the ninth heaven. There are two kinds of Noble, the Tianyi Noble and the Fortune-star Noble; it should be strong and fears punishment and clash; standing on Pressure or on the documents it means a high promotion and surely a seat as chancellor; with the Fortune star or the Noble holding the Self the person has blessing and virtue, meets danger all his life without anxiety and meets calamity without fault.
