# 要决输赢，先察世辰相克：专征伐以世为我、应为彼；世克应我胜、应克世彼胜；世墓宜坚壁自守；世旺应入墓彼必折人丁；世空我有难、应空彼有伤；世在阴宫不宜先动、世在阳象不利后兴；世动克应我必进、应动克世他欲来；鬼爻克世他胜我、克应他欲退；鬼持世须防围困；金火持世应两家流血；世应比和皆旺两强敌手；世应相生合将帅有允和之心

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要决定输赢，先看世和日辰相克的情形。专意出征讨伐，以世为我方、应为对方：世克应，我胜；应克世，对方胜；世入墓，只宜坚壁自守，不可妄动；世旺相、应入墓，对方必折损人丁；世空，我方有难；应空，对方有伤；世在阴宫，不宜先动；世在阳象，不利于后发；世动克应，我方必进；应动克世，对方要来攻；官鬼爻克世，对方胜我；官鬼爻克应，对方要退；鬼持世，要防被围困；金、火持世或应，两家流血交战；世、应比和而都旺相，是两强对手，胜败不能立刻定下，只能凭各自的所长来断；世、应相生相合，将帅有答应议和之心，士卒没有攻战之意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To decide victory or defeat, first examine how the Self and the Day control each other. In an expedition of conquest the Self is us and the Other is them: if the Self controls the Other we win, if the Other controls the Self they win; with the Self in its tomb one should only hold the walls firm and guard oneself and not move rashly; with the Self strong and the Other entering its tomb they are sure to lose men; the Self void, we are in difficulty, the Other void, they are wounded; with the Self in a yin palace one should not move first, with the Self in a yang image it is not favourable to rise later; the Self moving and controlling the Other, we are sure to advance, the Other moving and controlling the Self, they mean to come; the Pressure line controlling the Self, they beat us, controlling the Other, they wish to retreat; with Pressure holding the Self guard against being besieged; with Metal or Fire holding the Self or the Other both sides shed blood in the fight; if the Self and the Other are in accord and both strong they are two strong rivals, and victory and defeat cannot yet be decided, so judge only by what each is best at; if the Self and the Other generate and combine, the commanders are minded to agree to peace and the soldiers have no intention to attack.
