# 国事：内为邦畿、外为人民，卦宫安旺而国祚长、爻象合和而宗社久；冲莫见于正之、合莫被于破害，则天下和平国家奠安；次观旺衰动静合冲

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦是国都和邦畿，外卦是人民；卦宫安稳旺相，国运就长；爻象和合，宗庙社稷就长久。冲不要出现在正卦和之卦（本卦和变卦）里，合不要被破、被害，这样天下就和平，国家就安定了。接着还要再看旺衰、动静、合冲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The inner trigram is the capital and its domain and the outer is the people: if the palace of the hexagram is secure and thriving the dynasty is long, and if the lines combine harmoniously the ancestral temple and altars last long. If clash does not appear in the original or the changed hexagram, and a combination is not broken or harmed, the world is at peace and the state is secure. Next look at strength and weakness, movement and stillness, combination and clash.

> [!note] 按
> 「邦畿」是国都周围的王畿之地。「国祚」是国运，「宗社」是宗庙社稷。「正之」是正卦（本卦）和之卦（变卦）；「冲莫见于正之」是本卦、变卦里都不要出现冲（见术语表）。这一条与易林补遗朝廷国事「亲宫为邦国大忌空亡、本卦作朝堂最宜旺相」（EL.02）取同一个象：卦的整体代表国。
>
> *Note:* 'Bangji' is the royal domain around the capital. 'Guozuo' is the fortune of the dynasty and 'zongshe' is the ancestral temple and altars. 'Zheng zhi' is the original hexagram and the changed hexagram; 'clash must not appear in the original or the changed hexagram' means that neither is to show a clash (see the glossary). This entry takes the same image as 'the home palace is taken as the state, Void most to be avoided; the original hexagram is taken as the court hall, thriving best' in the Yilin Buyi chapter on court and state affairs (EL.02): the hexagram as a whole stands for the state.
