# 朝廷占·国君（一）：世与五爻旺相居阳宫阳爻者明君，旺静福主，带龙喜德合福贵动者仁主，加刑杀威震臣邻，带虎鬼大杀刑刃旺动暴虐，带玄武咸池动荒淫，带兄耗羊刃动多靡，带虎鬼死符病符动倦勤，休囚被太岁冲克随鬼入墓殂落不久

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国君的品性和寿数：世爻与五爻旺相，又在阳宫的阳爻上的，是明君；旺相而安静的，是有福的君主；带青龙、喜神、德神、合神、福、贵人而发动的，是仁君；加上刑杀，威严震动臣下和邻国；带白虎、官鬼、大杀、刑、刃，旺相发动的，暴虐无道，肆意诛杀夷灭；带玄武、咸池发动的，荒淫无度；带兄爻、耗神、羊刃发动的，多奢靡而不节制；带白虎、官鬼、死符、病符发动的，身体常常倦怠，懒于政事；休囚，又被太岁冲克，以及随着官鬼入墓的，不久就驾崩。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The character and span of the sovereign. If the Self and the fifth line are thriving and sit on a yang line in a yang palace, he is a wise ruler; if thriving and still, a fortunate ruler; if they carry the Dragon, Joy, Virtue, Combination, Fortune and the Noble and move, a benevolent ruler; with punishing baleful spirits added, his majesty shakes ministers and neighbours; if they carry the Tiger, Pressure, a great baleful spirit, punishment and the blade, thriving and moving, he is cruel and lawless and gives free rein to executions and exterminations; if they carry the Dark Tortoise and Xianchi and move, he is dissolute without limit; if they carry a Peer line, the Waste spirit and the Goat Blade and move, he is extravagant and without restraint; if they carry the Tiger, Pressure, the Death Tally and the Illness Tally and move, he is often in poor health and tired of government; if they are resting or trapped and are clashed or controlled by Tai Sui, or follow Pressure into the tomb, he will not last long.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「福主」按字面是有福的君主；「倦勤」是厌倦、懒于政事；「殂落」是死亡，用于君主就是驾崩。喜、德、合、福、贵、耗、死符、病符、羊刃、大杀都是神煞，书里没有逐个解释。
>
> *Note:* 'Fuzhu' is by the letter a fortunate ruler; 'juanqin' is being tired of and slack in government; 'culuo' is death, which for a sovereign means demise. Joy, Virtue, Combination, Fortune, the Noble, the Waste spirit, the Death Tally, the Illness Tally, the Goat Blade and the great baleful spirits are all spirit-stars that the book does not explain one by one.
