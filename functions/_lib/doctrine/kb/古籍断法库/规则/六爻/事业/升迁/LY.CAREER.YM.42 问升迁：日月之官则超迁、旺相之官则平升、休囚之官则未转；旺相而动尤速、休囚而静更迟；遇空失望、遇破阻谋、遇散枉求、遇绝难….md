# 问升迁：日月之官则超迁、旺相之官则平升、休囚之官则未转；旺相而动尤速、休囚而静更迟；遇空失望、遇破阻谋、遇散枉求、遇绝难始、遇世空则占不遂；官伏得助亦升、子动得日月亦升、冲合得交重亦升、随助得旺相亦升；专看官爻，代占不忌世空

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问升迁：占得官爻在日、月上，就是越级升迁；得到旺相的官，就是按常规升；得到休囚的官，就是还没转动。旺相又动，尤其快；休囚又静，更迟。遇空就失望，遇破就阻碍图谋，遇散就白求，遇绝就难开始，遇世空，这一占就不会如愿。不要说官伏就不升，得到生助也升；不要说子动就不升，得日月相助也升；不要说被冲被合就不升，有动爻也升；不要说随墓助伤就不升，得旺相也升。只看官爻；替别人占，就不忌世空。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about promotion: if one gets the Pressure line on the Day or the Month, it is promotion over the heads of others; if one gets thriving, strong Pressure, it is promotion in the ordinary course; if one gets resting, imprisoned Pressure, there is not yet a move. Thriving and moving is especially fast; resting and still is slower still. Meeting Void, one is disappointed; meeting broken, the plan is obstructed; meeting scattered, the seeking is in vain; meeting extinct, it is hard to begin; and with the Self Void the divination does not come to pass. Do not say that Pressure hidden means no promotion: with help it is promoted too; do not say that Output moving means none: with the Day and Month it is promoted too; do not say that being clashed or combined means none: with a moving line it is promoted too; do not say that following into the tomb and helping the injury means none: with thriving strength it is promoted too. Look only at the Pressure line; in divining for another, the Self being Void is not feared.

> [!note] 按
> 「日月之官」是官爻的地支正是当日（日辰）或当月（月建）的地支，书里说这样是「超迁」（越级提拔）；旺相的官是「平升」（按资格依次升）；休囚的官是「未转」（还没轮到）。「枉求」是白求。「交重」是动爻。「毋谓……亦升」四句是反着说：官伏、子动、冲合、随助本来都是不利的信号，但只要另有日月、动爻或旺相相救，仍然会升；可见这四样不是一票否决。「随助」是「随墓助伤」的缩写。书里自注：只看官爻；替别人占，世空不忌（因为世不代表占的人自己）。和易林补遗仕宦升迁（EL.55）、易隐升迁（YY.21）、黄金策的官爻隐伏（HJC.06）可对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Pressure on the Day or the Month' is a Pressure line whose branch is just the branch of the day or the month, which the book says gives 'chaoqian', promotion over the heads of others; thriving Pressure is 'pingsheng', promotion in the ordinary course by seniority; resting, imprisoned Pressure is 'weizhuan', not yet turned, its turn not come. 'Wangqiu' is seeking in vain. 'Jiaozhong' is a moving line. The four sentences 'do not say ... it is promoted too' are put the other way round: Pressure hidden, Output moving, clash or combination, and following into the tomb and helping the injury are all unfavourable signs, but so long as the Day and Month, a moving line or thriving strength comes to the rescue, one is still promoted, so these four are not a single-vote veto. 'Suizhu' is short for 'following into the tomb and helping the injury'. The book's own gloss: look only at the Pressure line; in divining for another, the Self being Void is not feared (because the Self does not stand for the diviner himself). Compare the Yilin Buyi on promotion (EL.55), the Yi Yin on promotion (YY.21) and the Huangjin Ce on Pressure hidden (HJC.06).
