# 征战占·六亲（二）：旌旗——父旺则旗新带龙雀则彩鲜，父衰墓则旧加勾蛇虎武则敝垢；卦无父或父空绝则帜不备；父被刑害克破宜改旗帜；父静财动功成拔帜；内财动克外父我夺彼帜、外财动克内父彼夺我帜

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 旌旗：父爻旺相，旗帜就新；带青龙、朱雀，画的色彩鲜艳；父爻衰、墓，旗帜就旧，加勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武，就破旧肮脏没有颜色。卦里没有父爻，或父爻落空、绝的，旗帜不齐备；父爻被刑、害、克、破的，宜改换旗帜。父爻安静、财爻发动的，功成之后拔旗（收兵）；内卦的财爻发动克外卦的父爻，我夺对方的旗；外卦的财爻发动克内卦的父爻，对方夺我的旗。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The banners. If Resource is thriving the banners are new, and with the Azure Dragon and Vermilion Bird the colours are bright and fresh; if Resource is weak or in tomb the banners are old, and with Hook Earth, the Coiling Snake, the White Tiger and the Dark Tortoise they are tattered, dirty and colourless. If the hexagram has no Resource line, or it is Void or extinct, the banners are incomplete; if Resource is punished, harmed, controlled or broken, it is fit to change the banners. If Resource is still and Wealth moves, success is won and the banners are pulled up; if inner Wealth moves to control outer Resource, I seize their banners; if outer Wealth moves to control inner Resource, they seize mine.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「勾蛇虎武」是勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武的省称。「拔帜」按字面是拔掉旗帜，即收兵；书里没有解释，译文按「功成之后收旗」译。
>
> *Note:* 'Gou she hu wu' is an abbreviation for Hook Earth, the Coiling Snake, the White Tiger and the Dark Tortoise. 'Ba zhi' is by the letter pulling up the banners, that is, withdrawing the troops; the book does not explain it, and the translation reads it as taking in the banners after success.
