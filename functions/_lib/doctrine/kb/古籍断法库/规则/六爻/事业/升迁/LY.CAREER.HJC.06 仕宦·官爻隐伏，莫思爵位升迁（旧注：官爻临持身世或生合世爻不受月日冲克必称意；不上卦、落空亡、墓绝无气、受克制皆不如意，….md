# 仕宦·官爻隐伏，莫思爵位升迁（旧注：官爻临持身世或生合世爻不受月日冲克必称意；不上卦、落空亡、墓绝无气、受克制皆不如意，世身冲克亦凶）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官爻是占官的象。它临持身世，或者来生合世爻，又不受月建、日辰冲克，凡有谋求必然称心；如果不上卦，或者落空亡，或者虽然出现却墓绝无气、受克制，都不如意；世爻、身爻被冲克，也凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure is the image in divining an office. If it settles on the Body or the Self, or comes to generate or combine with the Self, and is not clashed or controlled by the Month or the Day, whatever is sought will surely turn out to one's liking; if it is absent from the hexagram or falls Void, or even though it appears is in a tomb or extinction and without breath, or controlled, nothing will be as one wishes; a clash or control on the Self or the Body is also ominous.

> | 官爻临持身世，或动来生合世爻，不受月建日辰冲克者，凡有谋望，必能称意。 | 官爻临持身世，或者动来生合世爻，不受月建、日辰冲克的，凡有谋求，必能称心。 | If Pressure settles on the Body or the Self, or moves to generate or combine with the Self, and is not clashed or controlled by the Month or the Day, whatever is sought will surely turn out to one's liking. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是「占官」的基本判据：官爻（官鬼）是用神，看它在不在、有没有气、被不被冲克，再看它和世身的关系。旧注把不如意的几种情形都列出来（不上卦、落空亡、墓绝无气、受克制），王洪绪只写了吉的一半。
>
> *Note:* This entry is the basic criterion of 'divining an office': the Pressure line is the Subject, and one sees whether it is present, whether it has breath, whether it is clashed or controlled, and then its relation to the Self and the Body. The old commentary lists the ways it can go wrong (absent from the hexagram, Void, in a tomb or extinction without breath, controlled), and Wang Hongxu writes only the auspicious half.
