# 文书章：六爻中只一爻动最忌兄弟动；文书与贵人原无亦难成，出现有气亦怕空亡；求官与文书要旺相持世有成；应爻不克事体分明；乾兑坎宫谋事不一；见官用动其人多出

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻中如果只有一爻动，最忌讳兄弟爻动，事情不实在、难成。如果文书和贵人在卦中原本没有，也难成；出现了、有气，也怕空亡——所说的动爻急得像火攻，急着落入空亡。求官和文书，要旺相、持世才有成。应爻不受克，事体就分明；乾、兑、坎宫，谋事不专一；见官员，用爻发动，那人多半出门，叫也是白叫。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If only one line moves among the six, what is most to be avoided is Peer moving: the matter is not real and is hard to accomplish. If the documents and the Noble are originally absent from the hexagram, it is also hard to accomplish; if they appear and have qi they still fear Void, as is said, a moving line is urgent like an attack by fire, hurrying into Void. For seeking office and documents, they must be strong and hold the Self to succeed. If the Other line is not controlled, the matter is clear; in the palaces Qian, Dui and Kan the planning is not single-minded; in seeing an official, if the Subject line moves, the man has mostly gone out, and calling for him is in vain.
