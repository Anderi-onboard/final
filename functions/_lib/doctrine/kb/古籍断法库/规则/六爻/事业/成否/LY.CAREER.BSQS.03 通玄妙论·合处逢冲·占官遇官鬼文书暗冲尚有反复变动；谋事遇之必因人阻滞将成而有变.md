# 通玄妙论·合处逢冲·占官遇官鬼文书暗冲尚有反复变动；谋事遇之必因人阻滞将成而有变

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 合处逢冲（卦本来相合，却被日辰冲了）：求官时官鬼、文书被暗中冲着，事里还有反复和变动；谋事遇到这样，一定因为别人而受阻，眼看要成又有变。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A clash at a place of combination (a hexagram of combination that is clashed by the Day): in divining for office, if the Pressure line and the documents are clashed in secret, the matter still has reversals and changes; in planning a matter, meeting this means being held up by someone, and just as it is about to succeed there is a change.

## 《卜筮全书·通玄妙论·三、合处逢冲》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2817 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 3: a clash at a place of combination", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且如占得雷地豫卦。 | 比如占到雷地豫卦。 | Suppose one gets the hexagram Thunder over Earth, Yu. |
> | 世应相生，六爻相合，吉无不利，乃事事可成之象也。 | 世应相生，六爻相合，吉利没有不顺的，是事事都能成的卦象。 | The Self and Other give each other life and the six lines combine; all is auspicious and nothing unfavourable, the sign of every matter being achievable. |
> | 却在子日占得，子冲应上午、害世上未，此乃合处逢冲也。 | 却是在子日占到的，子冲应爻上的午、害世爻上的未，这就是合处逢冲。 | But if it is got on a Zi day, Zi clashes the Wu on the Other line and harms the Wei on the Self line: this is a clash at a place of combination. |

## 《卜筮全书·通玄妙论·三、合处逢冲》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2819 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 3: a clash at a place of combination", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 谋事遇之，必因人阻滞，将成而有变。 | 谋事遇到这种情形，一定因为别人而受阻滞，快成的时候会有变化。 | In planning a matter, meeting this means being held up by someone, and just as it is about to succeed there is a change. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 同一段还讲占婚、占财，以及占讼与病「喜遇合处逢冲」（合则事必成，逢冲灾必散），那几件放在别的问题域。「官鬼文书暗冲」是官鬼和文书（父母）这两爻被日辰暗冲，书里没有展开，译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* The same passage goes on to marriage and wealth, and says that lawsuits and illness are glad to meet a clash at a place of combination (combination means the matter surely succeeds, a clash means the calamity disperses); those go to other domains. "The Pressure line and the documents clashed in secret" means the Pressure and Resource lines are clashed by the Day without moving; the book does not expand on it and the translation follows the words.
