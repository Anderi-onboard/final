# 官员荐奖：六合则心怀喜悦、六冲则面染忧愁；如求扎帖又看文书反嫌财动，父鬼财空允能给发，不拘扁贴皆忌六冲

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六合的卦，心里喜悦；六冲的卦，脸上带着忧愁。如果求扎帖（官府的帖文），又要看文书爻，反而嫌财爻发动；父爻、官鬼在而财爻落空，就准许发给；不论匾额还是帖文，都忌讳六冲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With a six-combine the heart is glad; with a six-clash the face is clouded with sorrow. If one seeks an official warrant, look also at the document line, and on the contrary dislike Wealth moving; with Resource and Pressure present and Wealth Void, it is allowed and can be issued; whether plaque or warrant, a six-clash is feared in all.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 827 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如求扎帖，又看文书，反嫌财动。 | 如果求扎帖（官府的帖文），又要看文书爻，反而嫌财爻发动。 | If one seeks an official warrant, look also at the document line, and on the contrary dislike Wealth moving. |
> | 父鬼财空，允能给发。 | 父爻、官鬼在而财爻落空，就准许发给。 | With Resource and Pressure present and Wealth Void, it is allowed and can be issued. |
> | 不拘扁贴皆忌六冲。 | 不论匾额还是帖文，都忌讳六冲。 | Whether plaque or warrant, a six-clash is feared in all. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「扎帖」是官府发给的帖文（札帖），求的是一张批准的文书，所以看文书爻；文书爻怕财克，所以反嫌财动。「父鬼财空」书里连写，按上一句「反嫌财动」读作父、鬼在而财空；若读成三样都空，就与「允能给发」不合。「扁贴」是匾额和帖文。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhatie' is a written warrant issued by the authorities (a 'zhatie' of the yamen); what is sought is an approving document, so the document line is looked at, and the document line fears being controlled by Wealth, which is why Wealth moving is disliked. 'Fu gui cai kong' is written run together in the book and is read, after the previous sentence's 'on the contrary dislike Wealth moving', as Resource and Pressure present and Wealth Void; if it were read as all three Void it would not agree with 'it is allowed and can be issued'. 'Bian tie' is the plaque and the warrant.
