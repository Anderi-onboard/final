# 六亲爻变·官化官见贵求官事尽难（有名无实）、官化文书官未顺

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官化官，病没有安好；拜见贵人、求官，事事都难（有名无实）；官化文书，官运还不顺（官位高过文书，文书就难）；纵然忧愁、疾病，也还算吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure turning into Pressure, the illness is not yet settled; in calling on a noble or seeking office everything is hard (name without substance); Pressure turning into the documents, the office does not yet go smoothly (with Pressure ahead of the documents, the documents are hard); even so, worry and illness are still auspicious.
