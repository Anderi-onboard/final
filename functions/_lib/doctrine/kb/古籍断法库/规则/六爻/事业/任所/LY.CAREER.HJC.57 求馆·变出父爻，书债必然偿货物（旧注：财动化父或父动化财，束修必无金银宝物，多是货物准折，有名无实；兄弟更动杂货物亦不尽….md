# 求馆·变出父爻，书债必然偿货物（旧注：财动化父或父动化财，束修必无金银宝物，多是货物准折，有名无实；兄弟更动杂货物亦不尽得）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻发动化出父母，或者父母发动化出财，束修一定没有金银财宝，多半是用货物折价，是有名无实的象；卦里兄弟再发动，连杂货物也不能全部得到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth moves and turns into Resource, or Resource moves and turns into Wealth, the fee will surely not be in gold and silver but mostly in goods taken at a set value, the image of name without substance; if Peer also moves in the hexagram, even the miscellaneous goods cannot all be had.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2791 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财动化父或父动化财，主束修以货物准折。 | 财动化父母，或者父母动化财，主束修用货物折抵。 | If Wealth moves and turns into Resource, or Resource moves and turns into Wealth, the fee is paid in goods at a set value. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「书债」是欠先生的束修；「准折」是用货物折价抵付。这一联的象：财（钱）和父（文书、物件）互相变化，钱变成了东西。
>
> *Note:* 'School debt' is the fee owed to the teacher, and 'taken at a set value' is paying by goods valued as the sum. The image of this couplet: Wealth (money) and Resource (documents, goods) turn into each other, and money becomes things.
