# 投充兵卒：随官入墓身遭厄、助鬼伤身命受殃；随军出战所忌者官也，临世临身临命而入墓者名曰随鬼入墓必有丧亡之凶难，鬼宜安静若逢财动能助鬼兴又伤身世者名曰助鬼伤身决主身陷命倾难归乡井

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻随着官爻入墓，身遭厄难；助官鬼伤身，性命受殃。随军出战，所忌的是官爻。如果官爻临在世爻、身爻、命爻上而入墓，叫做随鬼入墓，必定有丧亡的凶难；官鬼适宜安静，如果遇到财爻发动、能帮助官鬼兴旺，又伤克身爻、世爻，叫做助鬼伤身，一定主身陷险地、性命倾覆，难以回到家乡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self follows the star of office into the tomb, one's person meets disaster; if one helps Pressure to injure the Body, one's life suffers calamity. In going out to battle with the army, what is feared is the star of office. If it rests on the Self, the Body or the fate line and enters the tomb, it is called following Pressure into the tomb, and there will surely be the misfortune of death; Pressure is best quiet, and if Wealth moves and can help Pressure to flourish, and also injures the Body or the Self, it is called helping Pressure to injure the Body, and it surely means that one's person falls into danger, one's life is overturned, and it is hard to return to one's native village.

> [!note] 按
> 「随官入墓」「助鬼伤身」增删卜易对它们另有说法（ZS.06）：古人当作凶，作者说世旺的反而升官；那是问升选，这里是随军出战，问的事不同，取法也不同，这里一律以官鬼为大忌。「临命」的「命」按文意是本命。「乡井」是家乡。
>
> *Note:* 'Following the star of office into the tomb' and 'helping Pressure to injure the Body' have another account in the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.06): the ancients took them as ominous, and the author said that a thriving Self on the contrary rises; that was asking about promotion and selection, while here it is going out to battle with the army, so the matter asked differs and so does the method, and here Pressure is throughout the great taboo. The 'fate' of 'lin ming' is by the sense the native fate. 'Xiangjing' is one's native place.
