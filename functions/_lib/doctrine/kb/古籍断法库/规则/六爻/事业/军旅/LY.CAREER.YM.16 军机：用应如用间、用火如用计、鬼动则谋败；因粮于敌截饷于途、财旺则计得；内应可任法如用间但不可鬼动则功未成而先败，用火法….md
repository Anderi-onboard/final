# 军机：用应如用间、用火如用计、鬼动则谋败；因粮于敌截饷于途、财旺则计得；内应可任法如用间但不可鬼动则功未成而先败，用火法如用计务在胜负然水火为民害之大者亦忌鬼动或谋泄流祸

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用内应，如同用间；用火攻，如同用计；官鬼一动，谋划就败了。取粮于敌、截敌军粮饷于途中，财爻旺相，计谋就得手。内应可以委任，办法如同用间，只是不可官鬼发动，发动就功劳未成而先败；用火的办法如同用计，务求取胜，但水、火是百姓最大的祸害，也忌官鬼发动，否则谋划泄漏、祸患流布。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Using an inside collaborator is like using a spy, and using fire is like using a stratagem; if Pressure moves, the scheme fails. In taking provisions from the enemy and intercepting the pay and grain on the road, if Wealth is thriving the plan succeeds. An inside collaborator can be appointed, and the method is like that of using spies, but Pressure must not move, for if it does the merit is not yet won and one is first defeated; the method of using fire is like that of a stratagem, and one must aim at victory, but water and fire are the greatest harms to the people, and Pressure moving is also to be avoided, otherwise the scheme leaks out and calamity spreads.

> [!note] 按
> 「应」在这里是内应（敌方内部接应我们的人）。「因粮」是就地取粮，「截饷」是中途截取敌人的粮饷。「用火如用计」是火攻要有周密的计谋。「水火为民害之大者」是水淹、火攻会连累百姓。书里这一条用的是同一个官鬼动忌：谋密则成，鬼动则泄。
>
> *Note:* 'Ying' here is the inside collaborator (a man inside the enemy camp who responds to us). 'Yinliang' is taking grain on the spot, and 'jiexiang' is intercepting the enemy's pay and grain on the way. 'Using fire is like using a stratagem' is that an attack by fire needs a careful stratagem. 'Water and fire are the greatest harms to the people' is that flooding and attack by fire fall on the common people. This entry uses the same taboo of Pressure moving: if the plan is secret it succeeds, and if Pressure moves it leaks.
