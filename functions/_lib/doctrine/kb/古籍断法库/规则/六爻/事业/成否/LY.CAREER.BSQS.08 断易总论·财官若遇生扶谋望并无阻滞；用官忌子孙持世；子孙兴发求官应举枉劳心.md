# 断易总论·财官若遇生扶谋望并无阻滞；用官忌子孙持世；子孙兴发求官应举枉劳心

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求官时，财爻、官鬼如果得到生扶，谋划的事没有阻滞；卦身和世爻没有救助，做的事一定彼此背离。用官鬼做用神的，忌子孙持世；子孙兴旺发动，求官、应举都是白费心。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking office, if Wealth and Pressure are given life and support, plans meet no obstruction; if the Body and the Self have no rescue or help, what one does will surely run to discord. When Pressure is the Subject, Output holding the Self is feared; if Output flourishes and moves, seeking office or taking the examinations is a waste of effort.

> [!note] 按
> 「财官若遇生扶」里的财是妻财、官是官鬼（原文写作「财官」）；「参商」是参星和商星，此出彼没，比喻彼此背离。同一行里「兄弟动摇，买婢求财空费力」是求财，放在求财域。「应举」是考试，这一条同时属考试域。「用官忌子孙持世」是说官鬼做用神时怕子孙持世，因为子孙克官鬼。
>
> *Note:* "Wealth and Pressure" are the Wealth and Pressure lines (the text writes them as "wealth-official"); "Shen and Shang" are two stars that never appear together, a figure for people at odds. In the same line, "if Peer moves, buying a maid or seeking wealth is wasted effort" concerns seeking wealth and is in the wealth domain. "Taking the examinations" is also an examination matter, so this entry belongs to the examination domain as well. "When Pressure is the Subject, Output holding the Self is feared" is because Output controls Pressure.
