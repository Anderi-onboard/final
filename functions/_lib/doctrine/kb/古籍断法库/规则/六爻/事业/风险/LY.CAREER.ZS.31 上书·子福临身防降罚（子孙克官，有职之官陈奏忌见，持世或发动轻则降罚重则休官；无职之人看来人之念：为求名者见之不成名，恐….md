# 上书·子福临身防降罚（子孙克官，有职之官陈奏忌见，持世或发动轻则降罚重则休官；无职之人看来人之念：为求名者见之不成名，恐其有祸者见之必无祸）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上书陈奏时子孙持世或发动：子孙是克官星的神，有官职的人上奏陈事，不宜见到它，轻的降职受罚，重的罢官；没有官职的人要看来占者心里的念头：本来是为了求功名的，见子孙必定不成名；担心会有祸患的，见子孙必定没有祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Output holds the Self or moves in a memorial: Output is the spirit that controls the star of office, and for an official with a post who submits a memorial it is not good to see it: at the lightest there is demotion and punishment, at the worst dismissal; for a man without office one must look at the thought of the one who comes: if he does this in order to seek a name, seeing Output means he will surely not gain a name; if he fears that it may bring him misfortune, seeing Output means there will surely be none.
