# 防参劾·身衰鬼克，贬责凌呵（世衰无生扶，又有岁五日月动爻刑冲克世，或官动克世、世随鬼入墓、世动化鬼化克、虎蛇刑克世，轻则贬罚重则刑狱；轻重看世爻有救无救）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻衰弱、官鬼来克，遭贬责、受凌辱。世爻衰弱又得不到生扶，又有太岁、五爻、日月、动爻刑冲克世，或者官星发动克世，或者世爻随官鬼入墓，世爻发动变鬼、变克，以及白虎、螣蛇刑克世爻的，轻的贬官受罚，重的坐牢。轻重要看世爻有没有救：世爻有救，是轻的；世爻休囚又被刑伤冲克，是重的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self weak and Pressure controlling it, there is demotion, reproof and abuse. If the Self is weak and gets no generation or support, and Tai Sui, the fifth line, the Day, the Month or a moving line punishes, clashes with or controls the Self, or the star of office moves to control the Self, or the Self follows Pressure into the tomb, or the Self moves and turns into Pressure or into a control, or the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake punishes or controls the Self, then at the lightest there is demotion and punishment, at the worst prison. Light or heavy depends on whether the Self has a rescue: it is light when the Self has a rescue, and heavy when the Self is resting or trapped and is also punished, injured, clashed or controlled.
