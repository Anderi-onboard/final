# 袭求武弁总纲：以官爻为主、世象为凭；动业赞皇猷鹰扬渭水、簪缨绳祖武虎拜枫宸；大凡袭爵承官皆沐君恩祖荫，欲识利名显晦远凭卦象推详

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占承袭爵位、谋求武职，以官爻为主，以世爻为凭据。承袭爵位、接任官职，都是沾受君王的恩典和祖先的荫庇；想知道利和名是显达还是暗淡，要靠卦象去推详。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about inheriting a title or seeking a military rank, take the Pressure line as the chief and the Self image as the ground. Inheriting a title and succeeding to an office are all through the sovereign's grace and the shade of one's ancestors; to know whether gain and name will be bright or dim, one must work it out from the images of the hexagram.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 773 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 大凡袭爵承官，皆沐君恩祖阴，欲识利名显晦，远凭卦象推详。 | 凡是承袭爵位、接任官职，都沾受君王的恩典和祖先的荫庇；要知道利和名是显达还是暗淡，须靠卦象从远处推详。 | Whenever one inherits a title or succeeds to an office, it is all through the sovereign's grace and the shade of one's ancestors; to know whether gain and name will be bright or dim, one must rely on the images of the hexagram and reckon it out in detail from afar. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「鹰扬渭水」用的是吕尚（姜太公）在渭水边遇周文王、后来在牧野《诗经》称他「维师尚父，时维鹰扬」的典故；「虎拜」用的是《诗经》「虎拜稽首」，召虎受命后叩拜周王；「枫宸」指宫廷。「绳租武」的「租」是「祖」的误写，「绳祖武」是继承祖先的足迹；「祖阴」是「祖荫」，祖先的庇荫。「弁」是武弁，指武官。这一章讲的是承袭武爵和谋求武职两件事，取法与前面的仕宦升迁章一样：官爻为主、世爻为凭。
>
> *Note:* 'Yingyang Weishui' alludes to Lü Shang (the Grand Duke Jiang), who met King Wen by the Wei river and of whom the Book of Odes says 'the Grand Teacher Shangfu, here the eagle soaring'; 'hu bai' alludes to the Odes' 'the tiger bows and touches his head to the ground', Shao Hu bowing to the Zhou king after receiving his charge; 'fengchen', the Maple Court, is the imperial court. The 'zu' of 'sheng zu wu' is written with the character for 'rent' and is a miswriting of the character for 'ancestor': 'to follow the ancestors' footsteps'; the 'yin' of 'zu yin' is written with the character for 'yin and yang' and is a miswriting of 'yin', shade or protection, the shelter of ancestors. 'Bian' in the chapter title is a military officer. The chapter deals with two things, inheriting a military title and seeking a military post, and takes the same method as the preceding chapter on official promotion: the Pressure line as chief and the Self line as ground.
