# 征战占·彼己（二）：世入墓胎宜退；世应皆旺或比和必不交锋；世应持金火鬼旺动两败俱伤；世持虎福旺相决胜千里，加日辰生扶百战百胜；世临龙福加将星良将、虎福猛将、勾福密擒、雀福号令严明、蛇福变幻、武福善用囊沙背水；世加凶煞动宜坚壁不出

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 主将的才具和进退：世爻入墓、入胎的，宜退兵；世、应都旺相，或相生相合比和的，必不交锋，打起来也胜负难分；世、应持金爻、火爻的官鬼，旺相发动，两败俱伤；世爻持白虎、福神旺相的，决胜千里之外，再加日辰生扶，百战百胜；世爻临青龙、福神，再加将星发动，是良将；临白虎、福神是猛将；临勾陈、福神是秘密派人擒贼；临朱雀、福神是号令严明；临螣蛇、福神是变化莫测；临玄武、福神是善用囊沙、背水的计谋，或者巧于偷袭劫营；世爻加往亡、归忌、受死、大败、四废、月厌、飞廉、大杀、亡神、劫杀而发动的，宜坚壁不出，出则必败。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The commander's talent, and advance or retreat. If the Self enters tomb or womb, it is fit to withdraw; if the Self and Other are both thriving or generate, combine or are in harmony, they will not cross swords and a fight would be hard to decide; if the Self and Other hold Metal or Fire Pressure lines, thriving and moving, both sides are badly hurt; if the Self holds the White Tiger and the fortune spirit, thriving, he wins decisively a thousand miles away, and with the Day generating or supporting, wins a hundred battles out of a hundred; if the Self sits on the Azure Dragon and the fortune spirit with the General star added and moving, he is a good general; on the White Tiger, a fierce general; on Hook Earth, one who secretly sends men to seize the bandits; on the Vermilion Bird, one whose orders are strict and clear; on the Coiling Snake, one whose changes cannot be fathomed; on the Dark Tortoise, one skilled in the stratagems of sandbags and fighting with the water behind, or clever at raiding camps; if the Self adds Wangwang, Guiji, Shousi, Dabai, the Four Wastes, Yueyan, Feilian, great baleful spirits, the Lost Spirit and Robbery and moves, hold the walls and do not go out, for going out means defeat.

> [!note] 按
> 「囊沙背水」指韩信用沙袋堵住水流再放水淹敌、背水列阵的计谋；「决胜千里」出自「运筹帷幄之中，决胜千里之外」。往亡、归忌、受死、大败、四废、月厌、飞廉等是神煞，书里没有逐个解释。「世应持金火鬼」可以读成世、应各持金鬼或火鬼，书里没有分开。
>
> *Note:* 'Nang sha bei shui' refers to Han Xin's stratagems of damming a river with sandbags and then loosing it on the enemy, and of drawing up the army with the water behind; 'deciding victory a thousand miles away' comes from 'planning in the tent and deciding victory a thousand miles off'. Wangwang, Guiji, Shousi, Dabai, the Four Wastes, Yueyan and Feilian are spirit-stars that the book does not explain one by one. 'The Self and Other holding Metal and Fire Pressure' can be read as each holding a Metal or a Fire Pressure line, and the book does not separate them.
