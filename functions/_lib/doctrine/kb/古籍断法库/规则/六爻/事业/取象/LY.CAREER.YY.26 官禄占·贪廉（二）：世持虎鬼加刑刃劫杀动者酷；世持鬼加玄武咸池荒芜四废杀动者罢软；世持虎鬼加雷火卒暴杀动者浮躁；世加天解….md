# 官禄占·贪廉（二）：世持虎鬼加刑刃劫杀动者酷；世持鬼加玄武咸池荒芜四废杀动者罢软；世持虎鬼加雷火卒暴杀动者浮躁；世加天解天赦喝散动者昭雪滞狱

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 为官的品性：世爻持白虎、官鬼，再加刑、刃、劫杀发动的，酷；世爻持官鬼，再加玄武、咸池、荒芜、四废杀发动的，疲软无能；世爻持白虎、官鬼，再加雷火、卒暴杀发动的，浮躁；世爻带天解、天赦、喝散发动的，能为积压的冤狱昭雪。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The character of the official. If the Self holds the Tiger and Pressure and the punishing, blade and Robbery spirits are added and move, he is cruel; if the Self holds Pressure and the Dark Tortoise, Xianchi, Huangwu and the Four Wastes baleful spirits are added and move, he is feeble and soft; if the Self holds the Tiger and Pressure and the Thunder-Fire and Sudden-Violence baleful spirits are added and move, he is rash and restless; if the Self carries Tianjie, Tianshe and Hesan and they move, he clears and redresses cases long held up in prison.

> [!note] 按
> 第一句「刑刃劫杀」分在两行里，中间被断行隔开（「加刑刃」在上一行末，「劫杀动者」在下一行首），这里连起来读。「罢软」的「罢」读作疲。天解、天赦、喝散、荒芜、四废、卒暴、咸池等都是神煞，书里没有逐个解释，译文只音译或按字面译。「滞狱」是拖着没有判决的案子。
>
> *Note:* In the first sentence 'xing ren jie sha' is split across two lines, 'adding the punishment and blade' at the end of the earlier line and 'Robbery moving' at the start of the next, and is read here joined up. In 'ruan' of 'baruan', the character 'ba' is read as 'pi', weary. Tianjie, Tianshe, Hesan, Huangwu, the Four Wastes, Cubao, Xianchi and the others are spirit-stars that the book does not explain one by one, and the translation only gives their sounds or translates them by the letter. 'Zhiyu' is a case dragging on without judgment.
