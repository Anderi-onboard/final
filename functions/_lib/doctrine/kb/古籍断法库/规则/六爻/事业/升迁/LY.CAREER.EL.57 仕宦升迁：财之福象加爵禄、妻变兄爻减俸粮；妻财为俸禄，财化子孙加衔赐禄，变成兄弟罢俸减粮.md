# 仕宦升迁：财之福象加爵禄、妻变兄爻减俸粮；妻财为俸禄，财化子孙加衔赐禄，变成兄弟罢俸减粮

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财是俸禄：财爻化成福象（子孙），就加官衔、赐俸禄；财爻变成兄爻（兄弟），就罢俸、减粮。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth is the salary: if Wealth changes into the fortune image, Output, rank is added and pay is granted; if Wealth changes into a Peer line, salary is stopped and grain is cut.

## 《易林补遗·仕宦升迁章第二十九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 763 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 29: Official career and promotion", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财之福象加爵禄，妻变兄爻减俸粮， | 财爻变出福象，加官爵、加俸禄；妻财变成兄爻，减少俸粮。 | If Wealth turns into the fortune image, rank and pay are increased; if Wealth changes into a Peer line, salary and grain are reduced. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 764 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻财为俸禄，财化子孙加衔赐禄，变成兄弟罢俸减粮。 | 妻财是俸禄：财爻化成子孙，加官衔、赐俸禄；变成兄弟，罢俸、减粮。 | Wealth is the salary: if Wealth changes into Output, rank is added and pay is granted; if it changes into Peer, salary is stopped and grain is cut. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一条与黄金策「兄如发动，须防减俸除粮」（HJC.04）、易隐官禄占「财化福加俸秩；财化兄罚俸粮」（YY.19）同取妻财为俸禄，财爻向子孙变是添、向兄弟变是减。
>
> *Note:* This entry, like 'if Peer moves, beware of reduced salary and cut grain' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.04) and 'Wealth changes into Output, rank and pay are added; Wealth changes into Peer, salary is fined' in the Yi Yin divination for official salary and rank (YY.19), takes Wealth as the salary: a Wealth line changing toward Output adds and changing toward Peer subtracts.
