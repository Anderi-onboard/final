# 营造公务防患·六冲不久，六合坚牢（工程以父爻为用神，用神不旺或受冲克又得六冲卦必不坚久，化冲亦然；世休囚被克将来遭害；父旺得六合卦永远坚牢）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看工程牢不牢：工程都以父爻为用神；用神不旺，或者受冲克，又得六冲卦的，必定不坚固长久，变冲的也一样；还要看世爻旺不旺，世爻如果休囚被克，将来终究要遭它的害；只有父爻旺相、又得六合卦的，才永远坚固牢靠。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To tell whether a work will be firm: in construction the Resource line is always the Subject; if the Subject is not thriving, or is clashed or controlled, and the hexagram is also Six-Clash, the work will surely not be firm and lasting, and so also when it turns into a clash; one must also see whether the Self is thriving, for if the Self is resting or trapped and controlled one will in the end suffer harm from it; only when the Resource line is thriving and the hexagram is also Six-Combine will it be firm and secure for ever.
