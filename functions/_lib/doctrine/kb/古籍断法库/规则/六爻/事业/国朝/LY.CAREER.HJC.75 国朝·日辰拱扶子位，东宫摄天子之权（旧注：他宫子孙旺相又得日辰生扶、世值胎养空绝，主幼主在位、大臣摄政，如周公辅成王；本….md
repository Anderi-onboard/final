# 国朝·日辰拱扶子位，东宫摄天子之权（旧注：他宫子孙旺相又得日辰生扶、世值胎养空绝，主幼主在位、大臣摄政，如周公辅成王；本宫子孙生旺得日辰扶助，国君将传位，太子当国）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 别的宫的子孙旺相，又遇日辰生扶拱合，而世爻落在胎、养、空、绝上，主国君年幼，有大臣摄政，像周公辅佐成王；本宫的子孙生旺，又得日辰扶助，是国君厌倦了大位、想要传位，太子主持国政、代行天子的事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Output of another palace is thriving or strong and is further generated, supported or combined by the Day, while the Self falls on Womb, Nurture, Void or extinction, the ruler is young and a great minister acts as regent, like the Duke of Zhou assisting King Cheng; if the Output of the home palace is thriving and gets support from the Day, the ruler is tired of the great seat and wants to hand it on, and the crown prince takes charge of the state and acts in the Son of Heaven's stead.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5854 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日辰拱扶子位，东宫摄天子之权。 | 日辰拱扶子孙之位，东宫（太子）就代行天子的权力。 | If the Day supports and holds up the place of Output, the Eastern Palace takes over the Son of Heaven's authority. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1486 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若本宫子孙生旺，更得日辰扶助，欲传位太子，当国摄天子事也。 | 如果本宫的子孙生旺，又得到日辰扶助，是想要传位给太子，太子主持国政，代行天子的事务。 | If the Output of the home palace is thriving and gets support from the Day, the ruler wants to hand the throne to the crown prince, who takes charge of the state and acts for the Son of Heaven. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注把子孙分成别的宫的（臣子，幼主在位、大臣摄政）和本宫的（太子）两种；卜筮正宗只收了本宫子孙这一半。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary divides Output into that of another palace (the minister: a young ruler on the throne and a great minister as regent) and that of the home palace (the crown prince); the Bu Shi Zheng Zong keeps only the home palace half.
