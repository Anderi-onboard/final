# 印绶旺则职守弥高、官鬼衰则声名卑小：父母官鬼乃占官之根本缺一则事难成；二者生旺官职高大、休囚职分卑小；父强鬼弱则冷衙门；遇进神声名益大、逢退神减前任政声

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 印绶（父母）旺，职位就高；官鬼衰，名声就卑小。父母、官鬼是占官的根本，缺一个事情就难成；两个都逢生逢旺，官职必然高大，休囚则只是卑小的职分；父母强旺而官鬼无气，职位虽平平，身处的是清静冷淡的衙门，没有威光权柄；遇进神，名声更大；逢退神，必减损前任的政声。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the seal and ribbon (Resource) strong the post held is high; with Pressure weak the name is humble and small. Resource and Pressure are the root of divining for office, and lacking either the matter is hard to accomplish; if both meet birth or strength the post is surely high and great, and if they are at rest or trapped it is only a humble station; if Resource is strong but Pressure has no qi, the rank may be ordinary but one sits in a quiet, cold office without glory or authority; meeting an Advancing spirit the name grows greater, meeting a Retreating spirit it will surely lessen the reputation for government of the previous post.
