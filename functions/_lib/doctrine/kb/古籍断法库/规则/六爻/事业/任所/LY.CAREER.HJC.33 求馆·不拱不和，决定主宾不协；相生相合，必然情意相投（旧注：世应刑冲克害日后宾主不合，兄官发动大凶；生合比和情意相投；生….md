# 求馆·不拱不和，决定主宾不协；相生相合，必然情意相投（旧注：世应刑冲克害日后宾主不合，兄官发动大凶；生合比和情意相投；生而化克始和终不和、冲而化合始疏后密；世与子宜生合则师弟恩义兼尽，冲克则多不睦）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应两爻刑冲克害，日后宾主一定不合，再加上兄弟、官鬼发动，是大凶的兆头；世应生合比和，情意必定相投；生而化出克，开始和睦、最后不和；冲而化出合，开始疏远、后来亲密；世爻和子孙也适宜生合，师生之间就恩义兼尽，见到冲克也多不睦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self and the Other punish, clash, control or harm each other, the guest and host will surely be at odds in time, and if Peer and Pressure also move it is a greatly ominous sign; if the Self and the Other generate, combine or are in harmony, their feelings will surely be congenial; generation turning into control is harmonious at the start and not at the end; a clash turning into a combination is distant at first and close afterwards; it is also fitting that the Self and Output generate or combine, and then the teacher and his pupils fulfil both kindness and duty, while with a clash or control they are mostly not on good terms.

> [!warning] 打架
> 师生的关系看哪一爻和子孙的关系，两家说法不一样：旧注说「世与子亦宜生合」（世爻是先生，子孙是学生，先生和学生要合）；王洪绪却写「应与子孙生合」（应爻和子孙）。「应」是东家，「世」是先生，按这一章的体例师生关系应是世和子孙，王洪绪的「应」像是误写，但书里没有说明。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two commentators differ over which line is paired with Output for the relation of teacher and pupils: the old commentary says 'it is also fitting that the Self and Output generate or combine' (the Self is the teacher and Output the pupils, so teacher and pupils should be in accord), while Wang Hongxu writes 'the Other and Output generate or combine' (the Other and Output). The Other is the host and the Self is the teacher, and by the plan of this chapter the relation of teacher and pupils should be the Self and Output, so Wang Hongxu's 'the Other' looks like a misprint, but the book does not say.

> [!note] 按
> 「拱」是两爻夹住中间一爻（这里泛指相合相助）；「宾主」是先生（宾）和东家（主）。这一联讲先生（世）和东家（应）是否合得来，「生而化克、冲而化合」是说一开始的关系会在变爻上反过来，这是通用的判据，在别的章节里也常见。
>
> *Note:* 'Flank' is two lines holding a middle line between them (here used loosely for combining and helping each other); 'guest and host' are the teacher (guest) and the host family (host). This couplet is about whether the teacher (the Self) and the host (the Other) get on, and 'generation turning into control, a clash turning into a combination' says the relation at the start is reversed in the changed line, a general criterion that is also often met in other chapters.
