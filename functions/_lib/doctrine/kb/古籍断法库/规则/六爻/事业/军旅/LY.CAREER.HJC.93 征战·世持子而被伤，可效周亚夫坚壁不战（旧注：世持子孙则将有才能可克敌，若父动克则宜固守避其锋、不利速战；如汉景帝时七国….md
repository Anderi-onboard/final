# 征战·世持子而被伤，可效周亚夫坚壁不战（旧注：世持子孙则将有才能可克敌，若父动克则宜固守避其锋、不利速战；如汉景帝时七国反，周亚夫屯细柳，中夜军惊坚卧不起，深沟高垒数日乃定，遂破七国之兵）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻持着子孙，将领有才能，可以克敌；但如果被父母发动来克，就应当固守营垒、避开敌人的锋芒，不利于速战，就像汉景帝时七国造反，周亚夫驻军细柳，半夜军营受惊他也卧着不起，深沟高垒，几天后营里安定下来，终于击破了七国的军队。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self holds Output, the general has the ability to defeat the enemy; but if Resource moves to control it, one should hold the fort and avoid the enemy's edge, and a quick battle is not favourable, as when the seven states rebelled under Emperor Jing of Han and Zhou Yafu camped at Xiliu and lay still when the camp was alarmed at midnight, kept his ditches deep and his ramparts high, and after some days the camp settled and in the end he broke the armies of the seven states.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5925 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持子而被伤，可效周亚夫坚壁不战； | 世爻持着子孙却被克伤，可以效法周亚夫坚守壁垒、不出战； | If the Self holds Output but is injured, one may follow Zhou Yafu, who held his ramparts and would not fight; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持子孙，将必才能，可以克敌。 | 世爻持着子孙，将领必定有才能，可以克敌。 | If the Self holds Output, the general surely has ability and can defeat the enemy. |
> | 若父动克，宜且固守，以避其锋，不利速战。 | 如果父爻发动来克，应当暂且固守，避开敌人的锋芒，不利于速战。 | If Resource moves to control it, one should for the time being hold firm to avoid the enemy's edge, and a quick battle is not favourable. |
> | 如汉景帝朝，七国反，帝使周亚夫，屯细柳以攻之。 | 就像汉景帝时，七国造反，景帝派周亚夫驻军细柳去攻打他们。 | It is like the reign of Emperor Jing of Han, when the seven states rebelled and the emperor sent Zhou Yafu to camp at Xiliu and attack them. |
> | 中夜军惊，扰乱至帐下，亚夫坚卧不起，深沟高垒，数日乃定，遂破七国之兵。 | 半夜军营受惊，骚乱一直闹到他的帐下，周亚夫仍然稳卧不起，深挖壕沟、高筑壁垒，几天后军营才安定下来，终于击破了七国的军队。 | At midnight the army was alarmed and the disorder reached his tent, but Yafu lay firmly and did not rise, kept his ditches deep and his ramparts high, and after some days the camp settled, and in the end he broke the armies of the seven states. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1526 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持子孙，将必才能，可以克敌。 | 世爻持着子孙，将领必定有才能，可以克敌。 | If the Self holds Output, the general surely has ability and can defeat the enemy. |
> | 若被动克宜固守，不宜速战，如汉景帝时七国反，帝使周亚夫屯细柳以攻之，中夜军惊，扰乱至帐下，亚夫坚卧不起，深沟高垒，数日乃定，遂破七国之兵。 | 如果被动爻克，应当固守，不宜速战，就像汉景帝时七国造反，景帝派周亚夫驻军细柳去攻打他们，半夜军营受惊，骚乱一直闹到他的帐下，周亚夫稳卧不起，深挖壕沟、高筑壁垒，几天后军营才安定下来，终于击破了七国的军队。 | If it is controlled by a moving line one should hold firm and not fight a quick battle, as when the seven states rebelled under Emperor Jing of Han and the emperor sent Zhou Yafu to camp at Xiliu and attack them: at midnight the army was alarmed and the disorder reached his tent, but Yafu lay firmly and did not rise, kept his ditches deep and his ramparts high, and after some days the camp settled, and in the end he broke the armies of the seven states. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说克它的是「父动克」，卜筮正宗说「被动克」，没有指明是父爻。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says the controller is 'Resource moving to control'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says only 'controlled by a moving line' and does not name Resource.
