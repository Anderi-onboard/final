# 用人·六神推人：青龙生世得弹铗之贤、克世惟藏刀之诈；白虎生世刚而直、克世勇而暴；朱雀生世辩而忠、克世佞而奸；玄武生世智而敏、克世巧而贼；螣蛇生克分损益之虚浮、勾陈克生分善不善之迟滞；若来生世虽凶亦吉、来克世虽吉为凶

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六神推人：带青龙而生世，就得到弹铗那样的贤人；随青龙而克世，就只有藏刀的诈。白虎生世的，刚而直；白虎克世的，勇而暴。朱雀生世的，能辩而忠；朱雀克世的，佞而奸。玄武生世的，智而敏；玄武克世的，巧而贼。螣蛇的生、克，分出他对我的损益是真是虚；勾陈的克、生，分出他善不善是否迟钝滞涩。如果来生世，虽然（神）凶也吉；如果来克世，虽然（神）吉也成凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The six spirits judge a man: with the Azure Dragon and generating the Self, one gets a worthy like the man who strikes his sword-hilt; following the Azure Dragon and controlling the Self, there is only the deceit of the hidden blade. The White Tiger generating the Self is firm and straight, the White Tiger controlling the Self is bold and violent. The Vermilion Bird generating the Self is eloquent and loyal, the Vermilion Bird controlling the Self is glib and treacherous. The Dark Tortoise generating the Self is wise and quick, the Dark Tortoise controlling the Self is cunning and thievish. The Coiling Snake's generating and controlling tell whether the gain or loss to me is real or empty; Hook Earth's controlling and generating tell whether his goodness or lack of it is sluggish and stuck. If one comes to generate the Self, even if ominous it is auspicious; if one comes to control the Self, even if auspicious it becomes ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 「弹铗之贤」用的是冯谖弹剑的典故（战国时孟尝君的门客冯谖，倚柱弹他的剑柄唱歌要求待遇，后来为孟尝君经营「三窟」），指有才、不轻易屈从的贤客。「藏刀之诈」是笑里藏刀。「虎」「雀」「武」是白虎、朱雀、玄武（原文写作「元武」）。「损益之虚浮」「善不善之迟滞」书里没有展开，按文意：螣蛇主虚浮，所以它的生克辨的是他对我的损益是真是虚；勾陈主迟滞，所以它的克生辨的是他的善不善是不是迟钝。书里自注：来生世的，神本身即使凶也吉；来克世的，神本身即使吉也凶——生克对我的关系，比六神本来的吉凶更要紧。六神的本义见术语表。
>
> *Note:* 'The worthy who strikes his sword-hilt' alludes to Feng Xuan striking his sword (in the Warring States period Feng Xuan, a retainer of Lord Mengchang, leant against a pillar, struck the hilt of his sword and sang to ask for better treatment, and later made 'three burrows' for Lord Mengchang), meaning a retainer of talent who does not bow easily. 'The deceit of the hidden blade' is the knife behind the smile. 'Tiger', 'bird' and 'tortoise' are the White Tiger, the Vermilion Bird and the Dark Tortoise (written in the text with another character for 'xuan'). 'The emptiness and flightiness of gain and loss' and 'the sluggishness of the good and the not good' are not expanded in the book; by the sense, the Coiling Snake governs emptiness and flightiness, so its generating and controlling tell whether the gain or loss to me is real or empty, and Hook Earth governs delay and stagnation, so its controlling and generating tell whether his goodness is slow. The book's own gloss: one that comes to generate the Self, even if the spirit itself is ominous, is auspicious; one that comes to control the Self, even if the spirit itself is auspicious, is ominous: the relation of generation and control to me matters more than the spirit's own luck. The original meanings of the six spirits are in the glossary.
