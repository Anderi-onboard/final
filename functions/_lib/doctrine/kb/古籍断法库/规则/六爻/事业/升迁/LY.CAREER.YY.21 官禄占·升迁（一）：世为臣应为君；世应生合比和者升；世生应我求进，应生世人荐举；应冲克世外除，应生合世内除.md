# 官禄占·升迁（一）：世为臣应为君；世应生合比和者升；世生应我求进，应生世人荐举；应冲克世外除，应生合世内除

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 升迁，世应论君臣：世爻是臣子，应爻是君上；世爻、应爻相生、相合、比和的，升迁；世爻生应爻，是我自己谋求进用，应爻生世爻，是别人荐举我；应爻冲克世爻，是外放任命，应爻生合世爻，是在京内任命。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Promotion, the Self and the Other as subject and ruler. The Self is the subject and the Other is the ruler; if the Self and the Other generate, combine or are in harmony, he is promoted; if the Self generates the Other, it is I who seek advancement, and if the Other generates the Self, others recommend me; if the Other clashes with or controls the Self, he is appointed outside, and if the Other generates or combines with the Self, he is appointed within the capital.

## 《易隐·官禄占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2644 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining office and salary", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世为臣，应为君。 | 世爻是臣子，应爻是君上。 | The Self is the subject and the Other is the ruler. |
> | 世应生合比和者，升。 | 世爻、应爻相生、相合、比和的，升迁。 | If the Self and the Other generate, combine or are in harmony, he is promoted. |
> | 世生应我求进，应生世人荐举。 | 世爻生应爻，是我自己谋求进用；应爻生世爻，是别人荐举我。 | If the Self generates the Other, it is I who seek advancement; if the Other generates the Self, others recommend me. |
> | 应冲克世外除，应生合世内除也。 | 应爻冲克世爻，是外放任命（外除）；应爻生合世爻，是在京内任命（内除）。 | If the Other clashes with or controls the Self, he is appointed outside; if the Other generates or combines with the Self, he is appointed within the capital. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「外除」是任命到京城以外的职位，「内除」是任命到京城之内的职位。第三句里的「人」译作别人，指有人在上面推荐我，和应爻是君上不是一回事。
>
> *Note:* 'Waichu' is appointment to a post outside the capital and 'neichu' is appointment to a post within it. 'Ren' in the third sentence is rendered as 'others', meaning that someone above recommends me, which is not the same as the Other line being the ruler.
