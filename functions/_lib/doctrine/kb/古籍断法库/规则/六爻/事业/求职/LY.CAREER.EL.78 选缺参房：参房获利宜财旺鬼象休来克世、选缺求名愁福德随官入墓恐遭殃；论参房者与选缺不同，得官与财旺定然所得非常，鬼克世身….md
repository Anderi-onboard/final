# 选缺参房：参房获利宜财旺鬼象休来克世、选缺求名愁福德随官入墓恐遭殃；论参房者与选缺不同，得官与财旺定然所得非常，鬼克世身难逃罪责；论选缺者亦要官兴最嫌子动，鬼克世身不须畏忌，但遇随官入墓恐罹灾患

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 参房求利，适宜财爻旺相，官鬼之象不要来克世爻；选缺求名，怕的是福德（子孙）发动，以及随着官爻入墓，恐怕遭殃。参房和选缺看法不同：参房如果官和财都旺相，所得一定不寻常，官鬼克世身，难逃罪责；选缺也要官爻兴旺，最嫌子孙发动，官鬼克世身反而不必畏忌；只是遇到随官入墓，恐怕遭灾患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For joining a section and seeking gain, Wealth should be thriving and the Pressure image must not come to control the Self; for selection to a vacancy and seeking a name, what is dreaded is Output, the fortune virtue, moving, and following the star of office into the tomb, for fear of calamity. Judging joining a section differs from judging a selection to a vacancy: for joining a section, if the star of office and Wealth are both thriving, what is gained will surely be extraordinary, and if Pressure controls the Self or the Body one cannot escape blame; for a selection to a vacancy the star of office must also flourish and Output moving is most disliked, whereas Pressure controlling the Self or the Body need not be feared; but if one meets following the star of office into the tomb, calamity is to be feared.

> [!note] 按
> 「罪贵」疑是「罪责」，译作「罪责」。「世身」是世爻和卦身（见术语表）。「福德」是子孙。「随官入墓」是世爻跟着官爻一起入墓，增删卜易对它另有说法（ZS.06：古以随官入墓为凶，作者屡试后改说世旺者反升）。参房与选缺要分开看：参房是吏进衙门做事，看财；选缺是官员得缺，看官，所以官鬼克世身，一个是祸，一个不必畏忌——这是据本条上下文读出来的，书里没有另说缘故。
>
> *Note:* The 'gui' of 'zui gui' is presumably 'ze', blame, and it is translated 'blame'. 'Shi shen' is the Self line and the Body of the hexagram (see the glossary). 'Fude' is Output. 'Following the star of office into the tomb' is the Self line entering the tomb together with the Pressure line, and the Zengshan Buyi has a different account of it (ZS.06: the old view took following the star of office into the tomb as ominous, but the author after many trials said that a thriving Self on the contrary rises). Joining a section and selection to a vacancy are to be looked at separately: joining a section is a clerk entering a yamen to work and looks at Wealth, while selection to a vacancy is an official obtaining a post and looks at the star of office, so Pressure controlling the Self or Body is calamity for one and need not be feared for the other; this is read from the context of this entry, and the book gives no other reason.
