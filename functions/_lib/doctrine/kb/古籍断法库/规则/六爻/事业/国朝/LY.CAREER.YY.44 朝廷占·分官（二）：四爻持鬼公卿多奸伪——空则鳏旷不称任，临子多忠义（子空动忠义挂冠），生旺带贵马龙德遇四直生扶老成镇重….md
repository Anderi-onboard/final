# 朝廷占·分官（二）：四爻持鬼公卿多奸伪——空则鳏旷不称任，临子多忠义（子空动忠义挂冠），生旺带贵马龙德遇四直生扶老成镇重，休囚遇蛇虎鬼凶亡劫被四直刑害克破模棱伴食；六爻旺逢生合宗庙奠安，动衰受伤鬼神震恫王纲顿驰，空则飨祀不举朝觐失废

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 分官（二），四爻公卿和六爻宗庙：四爻持官鬼，公卿多奸诈虚伪；落空，职位空缺，不称职；临在子孙爻上，多忠义之臣；子孙爻落空而发动，忠义之臣挂冠而去；生旺，带贵人、驿马、青龙、德神，又得四直（年月日时）生扶的，老成持重，内外四方都畏服；休囚，遇螣蛇、白虎、官鬼、凶煞、亡神、劫杀，又被四直刑、害、克、破的，模棱两可、尸位素餐，朝野失望。（五爻是天子，见前。）六爻旺相又逢生合，宗庙安定，朝廷肃穆安宁；发动、衰弱、受伤，鬼神震动惊恐，王法纲纪顿时松弛；落空，祭祀不举行，朝觐的礼废弛。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Dividing the offices (2), the ministers of the fourth line and the ancestral temple of the sixth. When the fourth line holds Pressure, the ministers are often crafty and false; if Void, posts stand vacant and the holders are unequal to them; if it sits on Output, there are many loyal and righteous ministers; if Output is Void and moves, the loyal and righteous hang up their caps and leave; if thriving and born, carrying the Noble, the Horse, the Dragon and Virtue and supported by the four rulers, the elders are steady and weighty and the Chinese and foreign are awed; if resting or trapped, meeting the Coiling Snake, the Tiger, Pressure, baleful spirits, the Lost Spirit and Robbery and punished, harmed, controlled or broken by the four rulers, they are noncommittal and fill their seats, and court and country are disappointed. (The fifth line is the Son of Heaven, see above.) When the sixth line is thriving and meets generation or combination, the ancestral temple is secure and the court solemn and calm; if it moves, is weak or injured, the spirits are shaken and afraid and the royal rules and bonds suddenly slacken; if Void, sacrifices are not held and the rites of court audience fall into disuse.

> [!note] 按
> 「鳏旷」是官位空缺；「挂冠」是辞官；「夷夏」是外族和中原；「伴食」是陪着吃饭，指官员不干事、只占位置（尸位素餐）；「饗祀」即飨祀，祭祀；「朝觐」原文作「朝观」。「子空动」按字面是子孙落空而发动。书里「临子」「子空动」的「子」都按子孙爻译。
>
> *Note:* 'Guankuang' is a vacancy in office; 'guaguan' is hanging up one's cap, resigning; 'yixia' is the foreign and the Chinese; 'banshi' is accompanying at the meal, an official who does nothing and only occupies a seat; 'xiangsi' is sacrifice; 'chaoguan' is the text's wording for the court audience. In 'on Zi' and 'Zi Void and moving', the 'Zi' is taken as the Output line.
