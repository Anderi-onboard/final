# 署印谋差·财与官星不可有一而不旺，子孙兄弟不可有一而动摇；世陷财空不如守己，官空世破且让他人；衰世随鬼入墓、世动化克化鬼、子孙兄弟持世，自投罗网

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谋署理印务、谋差事：财星和官星不能有一个不旺相，子孙和兄弟不能有一个发动摇动；世爻失陷、财落空，不如守住自己；官星落空、世爻被破，暂且让给别人；用钱去谋的，和援例一样推断。衰弱的世爻随官鬼入墓，世爻发动变克、变鬼，子孙或兄弟持世，这时去谋署印、营求差事，是自己投进罗网。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On seeking to act in a seal-holding post or to scheme for a commission: of the star of Wealth and the star of office neither may fail to be thriving, and of Output and Peer neither may move or sway; if the Self is lost and Wealth is Void, it is better to keep to oneself; if the star of office is Void and the Self is broken, let others have it for now; for one who seeks it with money, judge as for buying office by precedent. If a weak Self follows Pressure into the tomb, or the Self moves and turns into a control or into Pressure, or Output or Peer holds the Self, then seeking to act in a seal-holding post or to scheme for a commission is walking into the net oneself.

> [!note] 按
> 「署印」是暂时代理掌印的官（署理）；「营差」是营求差遣。书里没有另外解释，这里按字面和上下文读。
>
> *Note:* 'Shuyin' is temporarily acting as the official who holds the seal, and 'yingchai' is scheming for a commission; the book does not explain them, and they are read here from the letter and the context.
