# 一世二世任所非遥、五世八纯仕途远涉：一二三世在内卦故非遥，四五八纯在外卦故远涉

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一世、二世卦，任所不远；五世卦、八纯卦，仕途要远行。一、二、三世的世爻在内卦，所以说不远；四、五、八纯在外卦，所以说要远行。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a First- or Second-Generation hexagram the post is not far; in a Fifth-Generation or Eight-Pure hexagram the career takes one far. The First, Second and Third Generations are in the inner trigram, so it is said not to be far; the Fourth, Fifth and Eight-Pure are in the outer trigram, so it is said one travels far.
