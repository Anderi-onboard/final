# 六亲变化歌·官鬼变化与求官：官化进神是禄、求官应疾速，化财占病凶，化父文书遂，化子必伤官，化兄家不睦；父母化官鬼迁举

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼变出进神是禄，求官应验快；变成妻财，占病凶；变成父母，文书顺遂；变成子孙，必定伤官；变成兄弟，家里不和睦。父母变出官鬼，有升迁荐举。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit is salary, and seeking office is answered quickly; changing into Wealth, an illness divined is ill; changing into Resource, papers go smoothly; changing into Output, it surely injures the office; changing into Peer, the household is not at peace. Resource changing into Pressure brings promotion and recommendation.
