# 谋望成事总纲：以世应为主、内外为凭；成事须将世应查，世为本主应为他，空冲破绝临其世凡去谋为莫起牙，冲破绝空居应位他心不合枉嗟呀；凡成事体一应谋为皆取世应为主，或世或应如临绝地或犯旬空月破日冲皆难成就，次将内卦为谋事之人不宜空破，如临旺相定主亨通，再得内克外卦或内外相生比和俱为美

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谋求成事，以世爻、应爻为主，以内卦、外卦为凭据。要成事，必须查世、应：世是本主（自己），应是他人；空、冲、破、绝临到世爻，凡是去谋求，就不要动手；冲、破、绝、空落在应位，对方的心思不合，白白叹息。凡是成事办事、一切谋求，都取世、应为主：不论世还是应，如果落在绝地，或者犯旬空、月破、日冲，都难成就。其次把内卦当作谋事的人，不适宜空、破；如果旺相，一定亨通；再得到内克外卦，或者内外相生以及比和，都是美好的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In scheming to accomplish an affair, take the Self and the Other as the chief and the inner and outer trigrams as the ground. To accomplish an affair one must examine the Self and the Other: the Self is the principal and the Other is the other party; if Void, clash, break or extinction sits on the Self, do not set about scheming; if clash, break, extinction or Void is in the Other's place, the other party's mind does not agree and one sighs in vain. In every business and every scheme, take the Self and the Other as the chief: if either the Self or the Other falls on a place of extinction, or runs into the ten-day Void, Month-break or the Day's clash, it is hard to accomplish. Next, take the inner trigram as the one who schemes the affair, which should not be Void or broken; if it meets thriving it surely means success; if in addition the inner controls the outer trigram, or the inner and outer generate each other or are in accord, all is fine.

> [!note] 按
> 「谋望成事」讲托人、找门路把一件事办成，不限哪一类。「本主」是自己。「莫起牙」的「牙」疑是「芽」，「起芽」是开头、动手，译作「不要动手」。「嗟呀」是叹息。「旬空」「月破」见术语表。这一条与易隐谋望占「可否成败」（YY.83、YY.84）取同一个法：以世应为主，内卦为谋事之人。
>
> *Note:* 'Mouwang chengshi' is getting a matter done by asking others and finding ways in, and is not limited to one kind of matter. 'Benzhu' is oneself. The 'ya' of 'mo qi ya' is presumably 'ya', a sprout, and 'to put out a sprout' is to begin and set about it, so it is translated 'do not set about scheming'. 'Jieya' is sighing. 'Ten-day Void' and 'Month-break' are in the glossary. This entry takes the same method as 'whether it can be done and whether it succeeds' in the Yi Yin divination on scheming (YY.83, YY.84): the Self and the Other are the chief and the inner trigram is the one who schemes the affair.
