# 军机：兵法虽不一而象理只有一个——主帅（世）空则中军折、贼首（应）空则敌人逃；生我之动爻喜有援兵、克我之动爻恶其助敌；彼虽败而不能胜是因有生、己虽伤而不能害是因有扶；全盛而衰指动冲、将靡而起指绝处逢生

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兵法各家说法不一，但象和理只有一个：主帅（世）空，中军就折；贼首（应）空，敌人就逃。生我的动爻，喜它是来援的兵；克我的动爻，厌它是帮敌人的。还有一种：对方虽败而打不垮他，是因为他有生扶；我方虽伤而伤不到根本，是因为我有扶持。全盛而转衰，将倒而复起，进退反复，全看爻神的动静。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The arts of war differ from school to school, but the image and the principle are one: if the commander (the Self) is Void the centre army is broken, and if the bandit chief (the Other) is Void the enemy flees. A moving line that generates me is welcome, being relief troops; one that controls me is hated, for it helps the enemy. There is also the case where the other side, though beaten, cannot be overcome, because it has generation, and where I, though hurt, am not harmed, because I have support. Fully thriving and then declining, about to fall and then rising, advancing and retreating back and forth: it all depends on the stirring and stilling of the lines.

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 857 行 · Bracketed small note in the Yi Mao (author not named in the book)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 主帅谓世，贼首谓应， | 主帅说的是世，贼首说的是应； | The commander means the Self, the bandit chief means the Other; |
> | 全盛而衰，谓动冲， | 全盛而衰，指的是动冲（爻动而遇冲）； | 'fully thriving yet declining' refers to moving and clashing (a line that moves and meets a clash); |
> | 将靡而起，谓绝生。 | 将靡而起，指的是绝处逢生。 | 'about to fall yet rising' refers to being generated in extinction. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「中军」是主帅所在的主力。「主帅空而中军折」是世空则主帅这一边折损。「全盛而衰」「将靡而起」书里自己注作「动冲」「绝生」，字面是：旺的爻动了遇冲而转衰，将倒的爻在绝处逢生而重起。「存乎神」的「神」指爻神的动静变化。这一条和总纲（YM.11）配套：世是主帅、应是贼首。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhongjun' is the main body where the commander is; 'the commander Void and the centre army broken' means that when the Self is Void, the commander's side suffers loss. 'Fully thriving yet declining' and 'about to fall yet rising' are glossed by the book itself as 'moving and clashing' and 'extinction and generation'; read literally, a thriving line moves, meets a clash and declines, while a line about to fall is generated in extinction and rises again. The 'spirit' in 'it lies in the spirit' is the stirring and stilling of the lines. This unit goes with the general statement (YM.11): the Self is the commander and the Other is the bandit chief.
