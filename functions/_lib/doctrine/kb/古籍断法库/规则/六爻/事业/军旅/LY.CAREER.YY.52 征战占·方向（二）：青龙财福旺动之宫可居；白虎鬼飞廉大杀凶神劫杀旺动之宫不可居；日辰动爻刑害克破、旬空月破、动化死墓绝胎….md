# 征战占·方向（二）：青龙财福旺动之宫可居；白虎鬼飞廉大杀凶神劫杀旺动之宫不可居；日辰动爻刑害克破、旬空月破、动化死墓绝胎之宫不可居；世爻死墓绝胎之宫不可居

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 驻扎的方位：青龙、财、福神旺相发动的那一宫，可以驻扎；白虎、官鬼、飞廉、大杀等凶神和劫杀旺相发动的那一宫，不可驻扎；被日辰和动爻刑、害、克、破，或落旬空、月破，或发动变成死、墓、绝、胎的那一宫，不可驻扎；世爻处在死、墓、绝、胎的那一宫，不可驻扎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where to camp. A palace where the Azure Dragon, Wealth and the fortune spirit are thriving and moving can be occupied; a palace where the White Tiger, Pressure, Feilian, great baleful spirits, other ill spirits and Robbery are thriving and moving cannot be occupied; a palace punished, harmed, controlled or broken by the Day and a moving line, or falling into decade Void or Month-break, or moving and turning into death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, cannot be occupied; a palace in which the Self is in death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage cannot be occupied.

## 《易隐·征战占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2017 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 又青龙财福旺动之宫可居也。 | 又，青龙、财、福神旺相发动的那一宫，可以驻扎。 | Again, a palace where the Azure Dragon, Wealth and the fortune spirit are thriving and moving can be occupied. |

> | 日辰与动爻刑害克破，及旬空月破与动化死墓绝胎之宫，不可居。 | 被日辰和动爻刑、害、克、破，以及落旬空、月破，和发动变成死、墓、绝、胎的那一宫，不可驻扎。 | A palace punished, harmed, controlled or broken by the Day and a moving line, or falling into decade Void or Month-break, or moving and turning into death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, cannot be occupied. |
> | 世爻死墓绝胎之宫，不可居也。 | 世爻处在死、墓、绝、胎的那一宫，不可驻扎。 | A palace in which the Self line is in death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage cannot be occupied. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 第二句「白虎足鬼飞廉」的「足鬼」书里没有解释，疑是「及鬼」（白虎以及官鬼）之误，译文按「白虎、官鬼」译。「宫」在这一节指八个方位的宫。飞廉、劫杀、大杀是神煞。
>
> *Note:* In the second sentence 'baihu zu gui feilian', the 'zu gui' is not explained, and is presumably a miswriting of 'ji gui', 'and Pressure', so the translation reads 'the White Tiger, Pressure'. 'Palace' in this section means the palace of one of the eight directions. Feilian, Robbery and the great baleful spirits are spirit-stars.
