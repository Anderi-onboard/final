# 袭求武弁：世带青龙名驰朝野、官居白虎威振华夷；青龙为大贵之星、白虎乃兵权之煞，此二兽若得临世临官定主威扬海内

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻带青龙，名声传遍朝廷和民间；官爻坐着白虎，威势震动华夏和外族。青龙是大贵的星，白虎是兵权的煞，这两兽如果能临在世爻上、官爻上，一定主威名传扬海内。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self carries the Azure Dragon, one's name runs through court and countryside; if the star of office sits on the White Tiger, one's might shakes the Chinese and the barbarians. The Azure Dragon is the star of great nobility and the White Tiger is the baleful spirit of military power; if these two beasts come to the Self or to the star of office, one's might will surely spread throughout the land.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「二兽」是青龙、白虎。白虎在别的断法里多是凶煞（黄金策、易隐里常见），这里因为问的是武职，把它看作兵权之煞，与青龙并取；这是这一章的取法，不是白虎的通例。「海内」是天下。
>
> *Note:* The 'two beasts' are the Azure Dragon and the White Tiger. In other methods the White Tiger is mostly a baleful spirit (as often seen in the Huangjin Ce and the Yi Yin), but since the question here is about a military post it is taken as the baleful spirit of military power and is taken together with the Azure Dragon; this is the method of this chapter and not the general rule for the White Tiger. 'Hainei' is all under heaven.
