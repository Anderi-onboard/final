# 出师征伐：将星起例（正午二卯三子四酉、五月又到午，周而复始，休门祯祥）、坎卦有大煞官鬼难获福、大煞起例（正犬二蛇三月马四未五寅六卯七辰八亥九鼠十牛十一申，国家遇此岂安邦）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 将星的起例：正月在午，二月在卯，三月在子，四月在酉，五月又到午，只有这四个位置，周而复始；休门于是吉祥。坎卦有大煞，官鬼爻难得福。大煞的起例说：正月在犬（戌），二月在蛇（巳），三月在马（午），四月在未，五月在寅，六月在卯，七月在辰，八月在亥，九月忌鼠（子），十月在牛（丑），十一月在申方，「十一」金鸡（酉）为大煞；国家遇到这个，怎么能安邦？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The setting out of the General star: in the first month at Wu, the second at Mao, the third at Zi, the fourth at You, the fifth again at Wu; only these four places, going round and round; the Rest gate then is auspicious. Kan has the great baleful spirit, and the Pressure line there finds it hard to gain fortune. The setting out of the great baleful spirit says: the first month the dog (Xu), the second the snake (Si), the third month the horse (Wu), the fourth Wei, the fifth Yin, the sixth Mao, the seventh Chen, the eighth Hai, the ninth month beware the rat (Zi), the tenth the ox (Chou), the eleventh in the quarter of Shen, and 'the eleventh' the golden cock (You) is the great baleful spirit; a state meeting this, how can it keep the peace?

> [!note] 按
> 第一句「土只此四位」的「土」疑是「止」，译作「只有这四个位置」。「休户作祯祥」连着将星这一句，按字面是休门吉祥。大煞起例里，书里写了两个「十一」：「十一在申方」和「十一金鸡为大煞」，后一个疑是「十二」（酉为金鸡），译文照字面写出并加了引号；生肖对应：犬戌、蛇巳、马午、鼠子、牛丑、金鸡酉。两个起例都是十二个月里的地支位置，书里没有说明它们的来由。
>
> *Note:* In the first sentence the 'tu' of 'tu zhi ci si wei' is presumably 'zhi', only, and is translated 'only these four places'. 'Xiu hu zuo zhenxiang' runs on from the sentence on the General star, and by the letter the Rest gate is auspicious. In the setting out of the great baleful spirit the book writes two 'elevenths': 'the eleventh in the quarter of Shen' and 'the eleventh the golden cock is the great baleful spirit', the second presumably 'twelfth' (You is the golden cock), and the translation writes it by the letter in quotation marks; the animals correspond as dog Xu, snake Si, horse Wu, rat Zi, ox Chou, golden cock You. Both are positions of the branches through the twelve months, and the book does not explain their origin.
