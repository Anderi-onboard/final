# 官禄占·升迁（三）：内旺外衰宜守旧；内衰外旺宜图新；内外俱旺彼此如意；雀临门户爻动吉报至；世空官空、官爻安静伏藏、身动刑岁月者皆不升

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 升迁，守旧还是图新，以及不升的情形：内卦旺相、外卦衰弱，适宜守着旧职；内卦衰弱、外卦旺相，适宜谋求新职；内外都旺相，两头都称心；朱雀临在门户爻上发动的，喜报到来；世爻落空、官星落空，或者官爻安静伏藏，以及身爻发动、刑到太岁月建的，都不升迁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Promotion, keeping the old post or seeking a new one, and when there is no promotion. If the inside is thriving and the outside weak, it is fitting to keep the old post; if the inside is weak and the outside thriving, it is fitting to seek a new post; if both are thriving, either way goes as wished; if the Bird sits on the door-and-gate line and it moves, good news arrives; if the Self is Void and the star of office is Void, or the Pressure line is still and hidden, or the Body moves to punish Tai Sui and the Month, in every case he is not promoted.

> [!note] 按
> 「内」「外」指内卦和外卦。「门户爻」书里没有说是哪一爻，译文不替它定。

> *Note:* 'Inside' and 'outside' mean the inner and outer trigrams. The book does not say which line is the 'door-and-gate line', and the translation does not fix it.
