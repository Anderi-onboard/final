# 征战占·五行（二）：火动生合世立营得地利，冲克刑害世失地利；火鬼持世动被贼围，贴世贼寨近；火旺营大衰营小，空危险，绝无出路；火动利迁营；火鬼伤世防劫营；加龙贼左来、加虎右来、雀前来、武后来、勾蛇四隅来

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 火看营寨：火爻发动生合世爻，立营得地利；发动冲、克、刑、害世爻，立营失地利。火爻官鬼持世发动的，被贼围困；火爻官鬼贴近世爻的，贼寨离得近。火旺，营大；火衰，营小；火落空，营危险；火绝，没有出路；火发动，利于迁营；火爻官鬼伤世爻，防劫营。加青龙，贼从左边来；加白虎，从右边来；朱雀从前面来；玄武从后面来；勾陈、螣蛇从四个角落来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fire and the camp. If Fire moves to generate or combine with the Self, setting up camp gains the advantage of ground; if it moves to clash with, control, punish or harm the Self, the camp loses the advantage of ground. If a Fire Pressure line holds the Self and moves, one is besieged by bandits; if a Fire Pressure line is next to the Self, the bandits' camp is near. Thriving Fire means a big camp, weak Fire a small camp; Void Fire means a camp in danger; extinct Fire means no way out; if Fire moves, it favours moving camp; if a Fire Pressure line injures the Self, guard against a raid on the camp. With the Azure Dragon added, the bandits come from the left; with the White Tiger, from the right; the Bird, from the front; the Tortoise, from behind; Hook Earth and the Snake, from the four corners.

> [!note] 按
> 最后一句「雀前来，武后来，勾蛇，四隅来也」是承上两句「加龙」「加虎」的写法，所以译文把朱雀、玄武、勾陈、螣蛇也当作「加」在火鬼上的神。「贴世」按字面是紧贴着世爻（相邻）。
>
> *Note:* The last sentence, 'the Bird comes from the front, the Tortoise from behind, Hook and Snake from the four corners', follows the form of the two sentences before, 'with the Dragon' and 'with the Tiger', and the translation therefore takes the Vermilion Bird, the Dark Tortoise, Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake also as spirits added to the Fire Pressure line. 'Tie shi' is by the letter pressed against the Self, adjoining it.
