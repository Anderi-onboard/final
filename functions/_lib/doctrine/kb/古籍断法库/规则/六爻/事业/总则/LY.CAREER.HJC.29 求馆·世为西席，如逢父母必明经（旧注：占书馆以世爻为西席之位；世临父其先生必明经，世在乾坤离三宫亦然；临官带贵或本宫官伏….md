# 求馆·世为西席，如逢父母必明经（旧注：占书馆以世爻为西席之位；世临父其先生必明经，世在乾坤离三宫亦然；临官带贵或本宫官伏世下多是秀才）

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占书馆（去当塾师），以世爻代表西席，也就是教书的先生。世爻临父母，这位先生一定是通晓经书的人；世在乾、坤、离三宫也是这样；世临官爻又带贵人，或者本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，多半是秀才。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a school post, the Self stands for the tutor's seat, that is, the teacher. If the Self rests on Resource, the teacher is surely versed in the classics, and so too if the Self is in the Qian, Kun or Li palace; if it rests on Pressure with a noble, or the home palace's Pressure lies hidden under the Self, he is mostly a licentiate.

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「西席」是塾师的座位（古时宾客坐西，主人坐东，所以请来教书的先生叫「西席」），这一章因此用「世」代表先生，「应」代表聘请他的「东家」。「明经」本是汉代的科目，这里指通晓经书的人；「秀才」是生员。章首三句是引语，不是判断：「程风」指程颢、程颐两兄弟讲学的风气，「贾粟之盈仓」是说像商人囤积粮食那样装满仓库，「笔耕」是靠教书、写文章谋生。卜筮正宗写「秀长」，「长」是「才」的误写。
>
> *Note:* 'The tutor's seat' is the seat of the schoolmaster (in old etiquette the guest sat in the west and the host in the east, so the teacher invited to teach was called 'the west seat'), and this chapter therefore takes the Self for the teacher and the Other for the 'host family' that engages him. 'Versed in the classics' was originally a Han examination subject and here means a man who has mastered the classics; a 'licentiate' is a district-school student. The first three sentences of the chapter are a preface and not a judgement: 'the Cheng school' is the manner of teaching of the brothers Cheng Hao and Cheng Yi, 'stored grain filling the granary like a merchant's' means a granary filled as a merchant fills his, and 'earning one's living by the pen' is making a living by teaching and writing. The Bushi Zhengzong writes 'licentiate' with a misprinted second character.
