# 官禄占·何官（二）：世持官贵禄马又得岁月文书生合是威权风宪之职；世持官印旺相而旁爻带贵人禄马来扶者只是正官不风宪

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻持官星、贵人、禄、驿马，又得太岁、月建、文书来生合，是有威权的监察之职（风宪）；世爻持官星、印星而旺相，但是靠旁边的爻带着贵人、禄、驿马来扶助的，只是正官，不是风宪。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self holds the star of office, the Noble, Emolument and the Horse, and Tai Sui, the Month and the document also generate or combine with it, it is a post of authority in the censorate; if the Self holds the star of office and seal and is thriving, but it is a side line carrying the Noble, Emolument and the Horse that supports it, he is only a regular official and not in the censorate.
