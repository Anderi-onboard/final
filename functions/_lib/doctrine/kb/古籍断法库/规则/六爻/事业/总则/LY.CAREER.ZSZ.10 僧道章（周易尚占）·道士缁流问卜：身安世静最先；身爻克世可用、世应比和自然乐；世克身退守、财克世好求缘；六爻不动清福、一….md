# 僧道章（周易尚占）·道士缁流问卜：身安世静最先；身爻克世可用、世应比和自然乐；世克身退守、财克世好求缘；六爻不动清福、一位交重事变迁；官鬼交好谒官员；世临白虎官司挠、身遇勾陈心事缠；父母克身师接引、青龙持世贵周全；勾陈带杀多魔障；兄弟勾陈同位动防法眷勾连；父母重重身受克被俗家牵；财空注疏徒劳、鬼旺营谋枉费钱

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 道士、僧人来问卜：身安稳、世安静最要紧；身爻克世，可以信用；世、应比和，自然安乐；世去克身，当退守；财来克世，好求缘；六爻不动是清福，一位交重，事就变迁；财扶助世，能够施舍行利；官鬼交动，偏好去谒见官员；世临白虎，官司纠缠扰乱；身遇勾陈，心事牵缠；父母克身，有师父接引；青龙持世，有贵人周全；勾陈带杀，多有魔障；官鬼临在财上，被贼人冤害；兄弟和勾陈同位发动，谨防法眷在外勾连；贵人和父母交动，师父通达圣道；青龙并上子孙，徒弟贤能；父母重重、身受克，断定是被俗家牵累；财空，注解经疏徒劳力；鬼旺，谋求营运白费钱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For Taoist priests and monks asking by divination: the Body at ease and the Self still comes first; the Body line controlling the Self, one may be trusted and used; Self and Other in accord, ease comes of itself; the Self controlling the Body, one should withdraw and hold; Wealth coming to control the Self is good for seeking alms; the six lines still is pure blessing, one line moving means change; with Wealth supporting the Self one can dispense benefit; Pressure moving is especially good for calling on officials; the Self on the White Tiger, a lawsuit disturbs one; the Body meeting Hook Earth, worries entangle the heart; Resource controlling the Body, a master receives and leads one on; the Azure Dragon holding the Self, a patron sees one through; Hook Earth carrying a baleful spirit, there are many demonic obstructions; Pressure on Wealth, one is wronged by thieves; Peer and Hook Earth moving together in the same place, beware of Dharma kin colluding with outsiders; the Noble and Resource moving together, the master is versed in the sacred; the Dragon joined to Output, the disciples are worthy; Resource upon Resource and the Body controlled, it is certain one is dragged back by one's lay family; Wealth void, annotating the scriptures is wasted labour; Pressure strong, scheming is money wasted.

> [!note] 按
> 这一首和卜筮大全阐奥歌章的僧道章是同一首歌诀（后者有「羽士缁流来问占」），只有少数字句不同：「道士缁流来问卜」（「羽士缁流来问占」）；「堪谌用」（「堪谋用」，「谌」疑是「谋」之误）；「财扶世上能施利」（「财旺正宜收布施」，意思有出入：一说财扶世能施舍行利，一说财旺宜收布施）；「官司挠」（「官司绕」）；「多魔障」（「遭魔障」）；「官鬼临财被贼冤」（「玄武临财被贼冤」）；「贵交父母」「龙并子孙」（「贵临父母」「福德龙交」）。
>
> *Note:* This is the same song as the chapter on monks and Daoists in the Chan'ao Ge of the Bushi Daquan (which has 'Yushi zi liu lai wen zhan'), with only a few differences in wording: 'coming to ask by divination' for 'coming to ask by prognostication'; 'may be trusted and used' for 'may plan and act' (the 'chen' probably a slip for 'mou'); 'Wealth supporting the Self one can dispense benefit' for 'Wealth strong is just right for receiving alms', which differs in sense (one says Wealth supports the Self so one may give and benefit, the other that Wealth strong suits receiving alms); 'a lawsuit disturbs' for 'a lawsuit entangles'; 'many demonic obstructions' for 'meets demonic obstruction'; 'Pressure on Wealth, wronged by thieves' for 'the Dark Tortoise on Wealth, wronged by thieves'; and 'the Noble and Resource moving together', 'the Dragon joined to Output' for 'the Noble on Resource', 'Output and the Dragon moving together'.
