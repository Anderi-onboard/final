# 出师征伐：为什么奇门与易卦并用——运筹决胜、一失祸大；奇者三奇乙丙丁、门者八门、九星配合；独用奇门则彼此皆知同从吉门岂得并胜；太乙统宗天之数、奇门遁甲地之数、大六壬神人之数，总归八卦不离五行；易卦上应天时下通人事

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 为什么奇门要和易卦并用：在帷幄中运筹、在千里外决胜，功成名就就在史册上留给后世；如果有勇无谋、见识浅短，一着失误，小则陷身失职，大则国破家亡，祸患就从这里开始。所以说，虽然知道奇门的胜负，还要评断易卦的吉凶。奇是三奇乙、丙、丁；门是八门，各配八卦方位，但并非固定，有超神、接气的起例，每时改换，再与九星配合来定吉凶；九星是天心、天柱属金，天冲、天辅属木，天蓬属水，天英属火，天芮、天禽、天任属土。决断交战胜负，奇门、易卦两样都要精通。只用奇门，如果彼此都知道、双方都从吉门进兵，难道能一起获胜吗？何况奇门以接气、超神为主，前后难评，差之毫厘就谬以千里。此外还有太乙统宗是天之数，奇门遁甲是地之数，大六壬是神人之数，这几种数总归于八卦，各种变化不离五行；军中虽用这些数，哪比得上易卦把衰旺动静生克看得清清楚楚，不但推断军务的一个方面，而且能指点万民的趋避。易卦上应天时、下通人事，彰明往事、察知未来，开物成务、验证休咎存亡的机微，衡量判断的人必须精细详审，才不会有差错。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Why Qimen is used together with the hexagram. Planning within the tent and deciding victory a thousand miles off, one who succeeds and wins fame leaves a record in the annals for later ages; but with courage and no wit, and shallow knowledge, a single slip means at the least personal ruin and loss of post, and at the most the fall of the state and ruin of the house, and calamity begins from there. So it is said: though one may know the victories and defeats of Qimen, one still judges the good and ill of the hexagram. The Wonders are the three Wonders Yi, Bing and Ding; the gates are the Eight Gates, each matched with a trigram direction, but not fixed, for there are the rules of leaping the spirit and joining the qi, and they change with every hour, and are then combined with the nine stars to fix good and ill; the nine stars are Tianxin and Tianzhu in Metal, Tianchong and Tianfu in Wood, Tianpeng in Water, Tianying in Fire, and Tianrui, Tianqin and Tianren in Earth. To decide the victory or defeat of a battle one must be expert in both Qimen and the hexagram. If one uses Qimen alone and both sides know it and both advance by auspicious gates, how can both win? Besides, Qimen depends on joining the qi and leaping the spirit, which is hard to judge forwards and backwards, and a hair's breadth of error becomes a thousand miles. Besides these there are the Taiyi Tongzong, the number of Heaven, Qimen Dunjia, the number of Earth, and the Da Liu Ren, the number of spirits and men; these numbers all return to the eight trigrams, and all changes do not leave the five elements; though the army uses these numbers, how do they compare with the hexagram, which sees plainly strength and weakness, motion and stillness, generation and control, and can not only work out one side of military affairs but also point the way for the myriad people to seek or avoid. The hexagram answers above to the seasons of Heaven and below reaches human affairs, shows the past and discerns the future, opens things and completes affairs, and tests the springs of good and ill and survival and loss; one who weighs and judges must be fine and thorough, and only then will there be no error.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段是《易林补遗》对奇门、太乙、大六壬的背景介绍，放在这一章里是为了说明为什么征战要易卦与奇门并用；奇门的详细规则，留给奇门遁甲那一门的专书。「量编浅」的「编」疑是「褊」（狭小），「谬之千里」前一句断在「差之毫厘？」的问号处，文意是「差之毫厘，谬以千里」，译文接起来；「二数总归八卦」的「二数」疑是「三数」（上面讲了太乙、奇门、大六壬三种），译文写成「这几种数」。「曾如」是「何如」。「超神接气」是奇门遁甲起局时的两种置闰办法，书里没有解释。
>
> *Note:* This passage is the Yilin Buyi's background on Qimen, Taiyi and Da Liu Ren, and it is placed in this chapter to explain why war divination uses the hexagram and Qimen together; the detailed rules of Qimen are left to the books of the Qimen Dunjia art. The 'bian' of 'liang bian qian' is presumably 'bian', narrow; the sentence is cut at the question mark of 'a hair's breadth off?', and the sense is 'a hair's breadth off, a thousand miles astray', which the translation joins; the 'two numbers' of 'the two numbers all return to the eight trigrams' is presumably 'three numbers' (the passage has named Taiyi, Qimen and Da Liu Ren), and the translation writes 'these numbers'. 'Zeng ru' is 'he ru', how do they compare. 'Leaping the spirit and joining the qi' are the two methods of intercalation used in setting up a Qimen Dunjia chart, which the book does not explain.
