# 平生学艺：无鬼必无头无绪、游魂必游去游回，世犯旬空自有更张之意、应遭月破师无传授之心，蹇遇六冲彼我无情难习业、幸逢六合师徒相得却成功；一应徒师习业不宜卦内无官鬼，世为己应为师皆不可值旬空月破又不宜六冲，世应不空又遇六合定主师徒相得事必成功；欲择明师未知曲直当占易卦便见虚真

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 没有官鬼，必定没有头绪；游魂，必定游去游回。世爻犯旬空，自己有改弦更张的意思；应爻遭月破，师父没有传授的心。不幸遇到六冲，彼此无情，难以学业；幸而逢六合，师徒相得，反倒成功。凡是徒弟向师父学业，不适宜卦内没有官鬼；如果逢空，事情没有头绪；卦值游魂，往返不定；又看世爻是自己、应爻是师父，都不可值旬空、月破，又不适宜卦值六冲；世应如果不空亡，又遇到爻逢六合，一定主师徒相得、事必成功。想选择明师，不知道曲直好坏，应当占一个易卦，就能看到真假。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With no Pressure there is surely no clue to the matter, and a Wandering Soul surely means going off and returning; if the Self falls on the ten-day Void one is inclined to change one's course, and if the Other meets Month-break the teacher has no heart to pass on his teaching; meeting a six-clash is unlucky, for the two have no feeling for each other and it is hard to learn the trade; luckily meeting a six-combine, master and pupil suit each other and succeed. For all pupils learning under masters, it is not fitting for the hexagram to have no Pressure line in it; if it meets Void, the matter has no clue; if the hexagram falls on a Wandering Soul, one goes back and forth and nothing is settled; look also at the Self as oneself and the Other as the teacher, neither of which may fall on the ten-day Void or Month-break, and it is also unfitting for the hexagram to be a six-clash; if the Self and the Other are not Void and the lines also meet a six-combine, master and pupil will surely suit each other and the matter will surely succeed. To choose a bright teacher when one does not know the crooked from the straight, one should divine a hexagram, and the false and true will be seen at once.

> [!note] 按
> 「游魂」是八宫卦里每宫的第七卦（见术语表）。「更张」是改变做法。「蹇」在这里是不幸、运气艰难（与下句的「幸」相对），不是蹇卦。「徒师习业」是徒弟跟师父学业；「明师」是高明的师父。这一章后半讲的是学手艺时的师徒关系，与前面几条（EL.140 起）合起来，是学艺从选行业、取用神到选师父的整个过程。
>
> *Note:* 'Wandering Soul' is the seventh hexagram of each of the eight palaces (see the glossary). 'Gengzhang' is changing one's way of doing things. 'Jian' here is ill luck and a hard fortune (set against the 'luckily' of the next line) and is not the Jian hexagram. 'Tushi xiye' is a pupil learning under a master, and 'mingshi' is a brilliant teacher. The second half of this chapter speaks of the relation of master and pupil in learning a trade, and together with the earlier entries (from EL.140) it is the whole course of learning a craft, from choosing a trade and the Subject to choosing a master.
