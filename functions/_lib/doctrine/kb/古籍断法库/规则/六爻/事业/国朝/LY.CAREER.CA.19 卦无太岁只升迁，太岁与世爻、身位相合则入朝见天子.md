# 卦无太岁只升迁，太岁与世爻、身位相合则入朝见天子

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁上卦，主入朝；卦里没有太岁，只能迁调；太岁与世爻合、与身位合，主入朝觐见天子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Tai Sui in the hexagram there is entry to court; without Tai Sui one can only be moved or promoted; Tai Sui combining with the Self line and with the Body place means going into court and seeing the Son of Heaven.
