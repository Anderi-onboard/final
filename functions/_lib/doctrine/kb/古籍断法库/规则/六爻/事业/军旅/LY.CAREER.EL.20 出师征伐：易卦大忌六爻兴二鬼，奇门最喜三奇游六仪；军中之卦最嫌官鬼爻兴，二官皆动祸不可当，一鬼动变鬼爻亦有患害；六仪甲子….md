# 出师征伐：易卦大忌六爻兴二鬼，奇门最喜三奇游六仪；军中之卦最嫌官鬼爻兴，二官皆动祸不可当，一鬼动变鬼爻亦有患害；六仪甲子甲辰甲申甲午；三奇得使；夏至后乾之泰、遁之艮，丙得子孙临日月布阵必有祯祥

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 易卦里最忌六爻里兴起两个官鬼，奇门里最喜三奇游行在六仪上。军中的卦，最嫌官鬼爻兴起，如果遇到两个官都发动，祸不可当；纵然只有一个官鬼发动变出官鬼爻，也有祸害。奇门的办法，却喜欢三奇，如果在六仪之上，好处加倍。六仪是甲子、甲辰、甲申、甲午（原文只列这四个）；又（门）开甲午、甲戌，利于遇见星奇；甲子、甲申，喜欢遇见日奇；甲寅、甲辰，遇月奇更吉。虽说是三奇游六仪，也就是说三奇得使者。经书说：三奇得使确实可取，六甲遇到它不是小补。譬如夏至后，乾变泰、遁变艮就是。这类卦中，丙得到子孙临日、月，凡是出兵布阵必有吉祥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the hexagram the great taboo is two Pressure lines rising among the six lines; in Qimen what is best liked is the three Wonders roaming over the six Instruments. In a hexagram for the army, what is most disliked is a Pressure line rising; if both Pressure lines move the calamity cannot be withstood; even a single Pressure moving and changing into a Pressure line brings harm. The method of Qimen likes the three Wonders, and if they are over the six Instruments the benefit is doubled. The six Instruments are Jiazi, Jiachen, Jiashen and Jiawu (the text lists only these four); again, with Jiawu and Jiaxu open, it favours meeting the Star Wonder; with Jiazi and Jiashen, the Sun Wonder is pleasing to meet; with Jiayin and Jiachen, the Moon Wonder is better still. Though called the three Wonders roaming over the six Instruments, it also means the three Wonders getting the envoy. The classic says: the three Wonders getting the envoy are truly worth taking, and for the six Jia to meet them is no small help. For example, after the Summer Solstice, Qian changing into Tai or Dun changing into Gen. In such hexagrams, if Bing has Output on the Day or Month, any sending out of troops or drawing up of lines is sure to be auspicious.

> [!note] 按
> 六仪在奇门里一般是戊、己、庚、辛、壬、癸，这里书里写成甲子、甲辰、甲申、甲午，只列了四个，译文照字面。「非小裱」的「裱」疑是「补」（非小补），译文按「不是小补」译；「乙逢犬马丙鼠猴，六丁壬女骑龙虎」是三奇得使的口诀，书里没有展开，译文照字面。「官」在「二官皆动」里指官鬼。「遁之艮」是遁变艮，书里的写法；第十二句的「丙得子孙临日月」的「丙」是三奇里的月奇。
>
> *Note:* In Qimen the six Instruments are usually Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren and Gui; here the book writes Jiazi, Jiachen, Jiashen and Jiawu, listing only four, and the translation follows the letter. The 'biao' of 'fei xiao biao' is presumably 'bu', help, and the translation reads 'no small help'; 'Yi meets the dog and horse, Bing the rat and monkey, Liu Ding, Ren and the maiden ride the dragon and tiger' is a rhyme for the three Wonders getting the envoy that the book does not spell out, and the translation follows the letter. 'Official' in 'both officials move' means Pressure. 'Dun zhi Gen' is the book's way of writing Dun changing into Gen; the 'Bing' of 'Bing has Output on the Day or Month' is the Moon Wonder among the three Wonders.
