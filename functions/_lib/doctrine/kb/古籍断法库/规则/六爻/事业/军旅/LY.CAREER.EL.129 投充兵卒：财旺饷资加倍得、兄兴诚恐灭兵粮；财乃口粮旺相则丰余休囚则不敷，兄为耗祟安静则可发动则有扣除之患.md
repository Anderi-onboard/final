# 投充兵卒：财旺饷资加倍得、兄兴诚恐灭兵粮；财乃口粮旺相则丰余休囚则不敷，兄为耗祟安静则可发动则有扣除之患

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财旺相，饷银资助成倍得到；兄爻兴旺，实在怕兵粮被灭掉。财是口粮，旺相就丰足有余，休囚就不够；兄是耗损的祸祟，安静就可以，发动就有被克扣的忧患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth is thriving, pay and supplies are gained doubled; if Peer flourishes, one truly fears the soldier's rations being wiped out. Wealth is the ration, which when thriving is abundant and when resting or imprisoned is not enough; Peer is the baleful drain, and if quiet it is tolerable, and if it moves there is the trouble of deductions.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「饷」是军饷，「口粮」是口粮、兵粮。「耗祟」是耗损钱粮的祸祟。「扣除」是克扣。这一条与易隐征战占「财旺相粮草丰足……内兄动克财」（YY.59）、黄金策征战「财为粮草之本根，兄乃伏兵之形势」（HJC.88）取同一类象：财是粮饷，兄劫财。
>
> *Note:* 'Xiang' is military pay, and 'kouliang' is rations and soldiers' grain. 'Haosui' is the baleful drain that wastes money and grain. 'Kouchu' is deducting and skimming. This entry takes the same kind of image as 'Wealth thriving, provisions are abundant ... the inner Peer moves to control Wealth' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.59) and 'Wealth is the root of provisions and Peer is the shape of an ambush' in the Huangjin Ce on campaigns (HJC.88): Wealth is pay and grain, and Peer robs Wealth.
