# 官鬼临身世、卦爻旺相才是得官的真兆；驿马空亡徒费力，卦遭胎没枉劳神

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼落在卦身或世爻上、卦里各爻旺相，才是真的得官，可喜；驿马落空亡，白费力气；卦遇胎、没，白白劳神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure standing on the Body or the Self, with the lines of the hexagram thriving, is the true sign of getting the office and is cause for joy; the Post Horse Void means effort spent for nothing; a hexagram meeting Embryo or Sunk means wearying the mind in vain.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3746 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若卦遇胎没，定枉劳神。 | 若是卦遇到胎、没，必定白白劳神。 | If the hexagram meets Embryo or Sunk, it surely means wearing the mind in vain. |
> | 立春后巽胎离没，若得家人卦之类是也。 | 立春之后，巽是胎、离是没，如果得到家人卦这一类，就是这种情形。 | After the Beginning of Spring, Xun is Embryo and Li is Sunk; getting a hexagram such as Jiaren is a case of it. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 胎、没是八卦随节气轮转的八个状态（旺、相、胎、没、死、囚、休、废）里的两个，这里的旧注只举了立春后的一例。大易断例卜筮元龟目说「胎没二卦，春巽离，夏坤兑，秋乾坎，冬艮震」，春天是巽、离，和旧注的例子相合。
>
> *Note:* Embryo and Sunk are two of the eight states through which the trigrams turn with the solar terms (thriving, mutual, embryo, sunk, dead, trapped, resting, abandoned); the note here gives only one example, after the Beginning of Spring. The Dayi Duanli says 'the two trigrams of embryo and sunk: in spring Xun and Li, in summer Kun and Dui, in autumn Qian and Kan, in winter Gen and Zhen', which agrees with the note's example for spring.
