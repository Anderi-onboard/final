# 军机：寇有地而用应、寇无地而用鬼；两军对迎法世应之克生、四野剿除度鬼福之强弱；若据城郭成两军之势则考世应生克虚实以为胜负，如流寇无定必四向剿灭则论福鬼强弱

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 敌寇有据点的，用应来看；敌寇没有据点的，用官鬼来看。所以两军对阵，按世应的相克相生来断；四野围剿，则衡量官鬼和福神的强弱。如果敌人占据城郭，形成两军对阵之势，就考察世应的生克虚实来断胜负；如果是流窜无定的流寇，必须四面围剿消灭，就论福神与官鬼的强弱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the bandits hold a base, look at the Other; if they have no base, look at Pressure. So when two armies face each other, follow the generation and control of the Self and the Other; when the countryside is being swept clean on all sides, weigh the strength of Pressure and Output. If the enemy holds a walled city and forms the posture of two armies, examine the generation, control, emptiness and fullness of the Self and the Other to decide victory and defeat; if they are roving bandits with no fixed place and must be wiped out from all four directions, then discuss the strength of Output and Pressure.

> [!note] 按
> 「寇有地」是有城郭、据点的敌人，「流寇」是到处流窜没有固定地盘的敌人。「福鬼」是福神（子孙）和官鬼。这一条与黄金策征战「世持子而被伤」「应临官而遭克」（HJC.93、HJC.94）取不同的看法：那里是看世应，这里按敌人有没有据点，分用世应、福鬼。
>
> *Note:* 'Kou you di' is an enemy that has a walled city or a base, and 'liukou' are roving bandits with no fixed ground. 'Fugui' is the fortune spirit (Output) and Pressure. This entry takes a different view from 'if the Self holds Output but is injured' and 'if the Other rests on the star of office and is controlled' in the Huangjin Ce on campaigns (HJC.93, HJC.94): there the Self and the Other are looked at, and here one uses the Self and the Other or Output and Pressure according to whether the enemy has a base.
