# 收雇·察性情：专于用象——金刚火强水智木仁土信；青龙能文朱雀能言白虎暴戾元武奸勾陈迟钝螣蛇浮；八宫辨之诸星配之（用爻独发：乾志高坤性顺震直巽奸坎暗离明艮讷兑佞，角蛟志尾虎性奎狼心星骥德）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 察他的性情，专看用象：金刚、火强、水智、木仁、土信。青龙能文，朱雀能言，白虎暴戾而玄武奸诈，勾陈迟钝而螣蛇浮躁。用八宫来辨别，用诸星来配合。这是用爻独发时来论的：在乾志向高，在坤性顺，震直、巽奸，坎暗、离明，艮讷、兑佞；角宿有蛟龙之志，尾宿有虎性，奎宿有狼心，星宿有骏马的德性。随生克衰旺来说吉凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To examine his nature, look only at the Subject image: Metal firm, Fire strong, Water wise, Wood kind, Earth faithful. The Azure Dragon is able in letters, the Vermilion Bird is able in speech, the White Tiger is violent and perverse and the Dark Tortoise is treacherous, Hook Earth is slow and dull and the Coiling Snake is flighty. Tell it by the eight palaces and match it with the stars. This is for a Subject line moving alone: in Qian the ambition is high, in Kun the nature is yielding; Zhen is straight, Xun is crafty; Kan is dark, Li is bright; Gen is slow of speech, Dui is flattering. The Horn mansion has the dragon's will, the Tail mansion the tiger's nature, the Legs mansion the wolf's heart, and the Star mansion the virtue of the steed. Good or ill is told by generation, control, strength and weakness.

> [!note] 按
> 「用象」是用神（这里指财爻或应爻，看所问是仆还是人）的象。「元武」是玄武。「角」「尾」「奎」「星」是二十八宿：角木蛟、尾火虎、奎木狼、星日马，这里取它们的禽兽性情（「骥德」是良马的德性）。「艮讷兑佞」是艮主讷（不善言辞）、兑主佞（巧言）。「八宫辨之，诸星配之」是先按八宫、再配神煞二十八宿；书里自注：这是用爻独发时的论法。性情的取象与上一章用人（YM.68）用的是同一套，这里多了二十八宿。
>
> *Note:* 'The Subject image' is the image of the Subject (here the Wealth line or the Other line, according to whether it is a servant or an ordinary person who is asked about). 'Yuanwu' is the Dark Tortoise. 'Horn', 'Tail', 'Legs' and 'Star' are four of the twenty-eight mansions: the Horn mansion Wood, the dragon; the Tail mansion Fire, the tiger; the Legs mansion Wood, the wolf; the Star mansion Sun, the horse; here their animal natures are taken ('the virtue of the steed' is the virtue of a good horse). 'Gen is slow of speech, Dui is flattering' is Gen governing slowness of speech and Dui governing glib talk. 'Tell it by the eight palaces and match it with the stars' is first by the eight palaces, then matching the spirit-stars and the twenty-eight mansions; the book's own gloss: this is the method for a Subject line moving alone. The images of nature are of the same set as in the previous chapter on employing people (YM.68), with the twenty-eight mansions added here.
