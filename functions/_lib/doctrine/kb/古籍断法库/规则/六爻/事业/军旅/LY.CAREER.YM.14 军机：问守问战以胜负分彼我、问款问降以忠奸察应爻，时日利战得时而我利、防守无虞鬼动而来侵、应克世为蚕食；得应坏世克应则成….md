# 军机：问守问战以胜负分彼我、问款问降以忠奸察应爻，时日利战得时而我利、防守无虞鬼动而来侵、应克世为蚕食；得应坏世克应则成百战百胜之功、世坏应克世则忍三战三怯之耻，敌降于己己款于敌皆察应爻真伪忌应克世，卜日时如秋时用兵则利金爻持世、亥日出师则利火爻属应

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问守、问战，要以胜负分清彼此；问款（议和）、问降，要以忠奸来察看应爻。时日利于战，得时就对我有利；防守没有忧虑，官鬼一动就有来侵犯的。应克世是蚕食。得到应坏、世克应，就成百战百胜的功劳；世坏、应克世，就要忍受三战三怯的耻辱。敌人降于我，或我向敌人议和，都要察看应爻的真假，忌讳应克世。如果是卜日时的占：比如秋天用兵，就利于金爻持世；亥日出师，就利于火爻属应。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about defence and about battle, distinguish the two sides by victory and defeat; in asking about truce and surrender, examine the Other line by loyalty or treachery. If the day and hour favour battle, getting the right time benefits me; if defence is free from worry, Pressure moving means an invader is coming; the Other controlling the Self is nibbling away like a silkworm. If the Other is broken and the Self controls the Other, the merit of a hundred battles and a hundred victories is accomplished; if the Self is damaged and the Other controls the Self, one must bear the shame of three battles and three times of cowardice. When an enemy surrenders to me, or I seek a truce with an enemy, in both cases one examines whether the Other is true or false, and the Other controlling the Self is taboo. In divining the day and hour, for example if one uses troops in autumn it is favourable for a Metal line to hold the Self, and if one sends out the army on a Hai day it is favourable for a Fire line to belong to the Other.

> [!note] 按
> 「款」是议和、投诚。「蚕食」是一点一点吞掉。「三战三怯」是屡战屡怯（《史记》里曹沫三战三北的意思）。「秋时用兵利金爻持世」是秋属金，「亥日出师利火爻属应」是亥属水、水克火，火属应就是对方属火而被克，书里没有解释这一层，按五行读出来的。这一条与易隐征战占「方向」（YY.53）、黄金策「观世应之旺衰」（HJC.86）取同一个关系：世克应胜、应克世负。
>
> *Note:* 'Kuan' is seeking peace and offering submission. 'Cansi' is eating away bit by bit like a silkworm. 'Three battles and three times of cowardice' is being timid again and again in battle (in the sense of Cao Mo's three defeats in the Shiji). That 'in autumn a Metal line holding the Self is favourable' is because autumn belongs to Metal; 'on a Hai day a Fire line belonging to the Other is favourable' is because Hai is Water and Water controls Fire, so with Fire on the Other the other side is of Fire and is controlled; the book does not explain this layer, and it is read by the five elements. This entry takes the same relation as the 'direction' section of the Yi Yin war divination (YY.53) and 'watch the strength of the Self and the Other' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.86): the Self controlling the Other wins, the Other controlling the Self loses.
