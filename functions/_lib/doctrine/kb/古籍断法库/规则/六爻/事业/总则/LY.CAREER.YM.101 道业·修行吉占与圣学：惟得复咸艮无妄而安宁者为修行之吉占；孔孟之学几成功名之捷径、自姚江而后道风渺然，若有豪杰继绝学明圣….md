# 道业·修行吉占与圣学：惟得复咸艮无妄而安宁者为修行之吉占；孔孟之学几成功名之捷径、自姚江而后道风渺然，若有豪杰继绝学明圣道亦以此章占；四卦专释心学不拘福鬼冲合直言其吉（安静则然变动复非）；圣学无求道故道业止详二氏三教道同占同

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 只有得到复、咸、艮、无妄而安宁的，是修行的吉占。孔孟之学几乎成了功名的捷径，自姚江（王阳明）以后，道风渺茫；如果有豪杰之士起来继承绝学、阐明圣道，也用这一章来占。复、咸、艮、无妄这几个卦，专门解释心学；大抵儒释道三教必以正心为根，占修行得到这几个卦的，不拘福神、鬼、冲合，直接说吉；但安静时才如此，变动了就不是了。圣学没有『求道』一说，所以道业只详述佛道二家；三教之道相同，占法也相同。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Only getting Fu, Xian, Gen or Wuwang and being at peace is an auspicious divination for practice. The learning of Confucius and Mencius has almost become a short cut to fame and office, and since Yaojiang (Wang Yangming) the manner of the Way has grown faint; if there are heroic men who rise up to continue the lost learning and make clear the sages' Way, they too divine by this chapter. The hexagrams Fu, Xian, Gen and Wuwang are devoted to the learning of the heart; in general the three teachings all take rectifying the heart as the root, and one who divines about practice and gets these hexagrams, whatever the fortune spirit, Pressure, clash and combination, is simply told that it is auspicious; but this holds only when still, and if moving it is not so again. The sages' learning has no 'seeking the Way', so the monastic vocation treats only the two schools in detail; the three teachings are the same in the Way and the same in divining.

> [!note] 按
> 「复」「咸」「艮」「无妄」是四个卦名：复是回返本心，咸是感应，艮是止，无妄是不妄动，都有『返回本心、静止』的意思，所以书里说这四卦『专释心学』（「四易」按文意是这四个卦）。「姚江」是王阳明（余姚人，姚江学派），「绝学」是失传的学问，「圣道」是圣人的道。「正心」是端正心思（《大学》语）。「福鬼冲合」是福神、官鬼、冲、合这些平常用的断法，这四卦不拘这些，直接断吉，但只限卦静的时候，变动了就按平常的断法。「圣学无求道者」是儒家圣学里没有『出家求道』这回事，所以这一章只讲佛、道两家；三教（儒释道）之道相同，占法也相同。这一条是道业章的收尾，和章首（YM.90）的『孔孟之道无须筮』前后呼应。
>
> *Note:* Fu, Xian, Gen and Wuwang are four hexagram names: Fu is returning to the original heart, Xian is response, Gen is stopping, Wuwang is not acting rashly, all with the sense of 'return to the original heart and be still', so the book says these four are 'devoted to the learning of the heart' ('the four changes' by the sense is these four hexagrams). 'Yaojiang' is Wang Yangming (a man of Yuyao, the Yaojiang school), 'juexue' is learning that has been lost, and 'shengdao' is the sages' Way. 'Zhengxin' is rectifying the heart (a phrase of the Great Learning). 'Fortune spirit, Pressure, clash and combination' are the ordinary methods of judgment; these four hexagrams are not tied to them and are directly called auspicious, but only when the hexagram is still, and if moving the ordinary judgment is used. 'The sages' learning has no seeking the Way' is that in the Confucian sages' learning there is no 'leaving home to seek the Way', so this chapter speaks only of the Buddhist and Daoist schools; the three teachings (Confucian, Buddhist, Daoist) are the same in the Way and the same in divining. This entry closes the chapter and answers the head of the chapter (YM.90), 'the Way of Confucius and Mencius needs no divining'.
