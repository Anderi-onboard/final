# 概占何官（六神诸星）：以六神参考其官之政事——青龙清耀文章诗赋清华之府、朱雀剖断谏议言路、勾陈田土劝农治寇一方之牧、螣蛇传宣驰骤差遣、白虎威武掌生杀军旅刑罚之宰、元武水利司捕司谋；文昌翰林、官符有司、天马钦差、天狱禁吏，斗木獬巡方、尾火虎兵权、星日马圣传之司、亢金龙文章之相；大抵五行六爻八宫为经、六神诸星为纬

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用六神和诸星（「诸监」疑是「诸星」之误）来参看这个官的政务：青龙，政务清明显耀，是文章诗赋、清华的官署；朱雀，政务是剖断，谏议善恶，折辨是非，是言路之官；勾陈，政务在田土，兴教劝农，治寇保民，是一方的牧守；螣蛇，政务是传宣，往来奔走，上下体察，是差遣之利；白虎，政务是威武，掌生杀之权，秉军旅之事，是刑罚的主宰；元武，政务是水利，是司捕、司谋之象。文昌是翰林，官符是有司，天马是钦差，天狱是禁吏；斗木獬是巡方之官，尾火虎是兵权之主，星日马是圣传之司，亢金龙是文章之相。大抵以五行、六爻、八宫为经，以六神、诸星为纬来参看。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* By the six spirits and the various stars ('jian' here is presumably a slip for 'xing', stars) one consults the government business of the office: with the Azure Dragon the government is clear and bright, the offices of letters, poetry and distinguished rank; with the Vermilion Bird the government is judging, remonstrating on good and evil, deciding right and wrong, an official of the channels of speech; with Hook Earth the government is in land and soil, promoting teaching and urging farming, quelling bandits and protecting the people, a shepherd of one region; with the Coiling Snake the government is relaying proclamations, going to and fro at speed, looking into things high and low, the advantage of commissions; with the White Tiger the government is awesome force, holding the power of life and death, in charge of military affairs, the master of punishments; with the Dark Tortoise the government is waterworks, the image of arresting and of planning. The Literary Star is the Hanlin, the Official Charge is the competent department, the Heavenly Horse is the imperial envoy, the Heavenly Prison is the prison officer; the Dipper Wood Xie-beast is an official who tours the regions, the Tail Fire Tiger is the master of military power, the Star Sun Horse is the office of transmitting the imperial word, the Neck Metal Dragon is the minister of letters. In general the five phases, the six lines and the eight palaces are the warp, and the six spirits and the various stars the weft that is consulted along with them.

> [!note] 按
> 「清华」是清贵显要（「清花」疑是「清华」之误）。「言路」是进言的途径，指谏官御史。「牧」是州牧、地方长官。「传宣」是传达宣布诏令，「差遣」是奉派出差。「文昌」「官符」「天马」「天狱」是神煞名；「斗木獬」「尾火虎」「星日马」「亢金龙」是二十八宿（斗宿木獬、尾宿火虎、星宿日马、亢宿金龙）。「圣传之司」书里没有展开，下面接「星日为马」，当是管传递诏命的驿传一类（存疑）。「六神诸监」的「监」，按下面注文「六神诸星」当是「星」之误。「经」「纬」是织布的纵线和横线：五行、六爻、八宫是定官的主线，六神、诸星是补充。六神的本来取象见术语表；这里是把它们取到官职的政务上。
>
> *Note:* 'Qinghua' is pure and eminent in rank ('qinghua' written with another character is presumably a slip for it). 'Yanlu' is the channel for presenting counsel, meaning remonstrating officials and censors. 'Mu' is a provincial governor, a local head. 'Chuanxuan' is conveying and proclaiming imperial orders, and 'chaiqian' is being sent out on a commission. 'Wenchang', 'guanfu', 'tianma' and 'tianyu' are names of spirit-stars; 'Dou Mu Xie', 'Wei Huo Hu', 'Xing Ri Ma' and 'Kang Jin Long' are four of the twenty-eight mansions (the Dipper mansion, Wood, the xie-beast; the Tail mansion, Fire, the tiger; the Star mansion, Sun, the horse; the Neck mansion, Metal, the dragon). The book does not expand on 'shengchuan zhi si'; it is followed by 'Star Sun is the horse', and it is presumably the postal relay that carries imperial orders (doubtful). The 'jian' of 'liushen zhu jian' is by the note below, 'six spirits and various stars', presumably a slip for 'xing'. 'Jing' and 'wei' are the warp and weft threads of a weave: the five phases, six lines and eight palaces are the main thread that fixes the office, and the six spirits and stars supplement it. The original images of the six spirits are in the glossary; here they are taken onto the government business of an office.
