# 谒见：谒人须问谒何人，世应作鬼枉劳心，用爻出现不乘旺往见必不在家；世应坐鬼彼此疑心，用爻衰弱我何籍焉

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 拜谒人，要问拜谒的是谁；世、应作鬼，白费心思；用爻出现却不乘旺，去见他必定不在家。世、应坐着官鬼，双方互相猜疑；如果用爻衰弱了，我还能凭靠什么呢？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In calling on someone, ask whom one is calling on; with the Self or the Other as Pressure one wearies the mind in vain; the Subject line appearing but not riding strength, going to see him, he is surely not at home. With the Self or the Other sitting on Pressure, each suspects the other; with the Subject line weak, on what could I rely?

> [!warning] 打架
> 断易天机转引的这首（「海底眼云」），第一二句作「世应坐鬼枉劳心」，第三句作「用爻出现不乘土」；这里作「世应作鬼」「用爻出现不乘旺」。「坐」「作」一字之差关系不大，但「不乘土」和「不乘旺」差得远：前者说用爻没有落在土爻上，后者说用爻没有得旺。两本都没有解释，本库各照原书录，不裁定。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The poem as quoted in the Duanyi Tianji ('the Eye at the Bottom of the Sea says') has 'the Self or the Other sitting on Pressure, the mind wearied in vain' in its first two lines and 'the Subject line appearing but not riding Earth' in the third, while here it reads 'the Self or the Other as Pressure' and 'not riding strength'. The one-word difference between 'sit' and 'be' matters little, but 'not riding Earth' and 'not riding strength' are far apart: the first says the Subject line does not stand on an Earth line, the second that it has not gained strength. Neither book explains it, and each is recorded as its book gives it, without adjudication.
