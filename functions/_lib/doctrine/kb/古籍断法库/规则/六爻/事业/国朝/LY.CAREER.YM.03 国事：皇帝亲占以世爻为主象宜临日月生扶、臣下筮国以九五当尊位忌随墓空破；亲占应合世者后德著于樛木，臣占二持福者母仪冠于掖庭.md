# 国事：皇帝亲占以世爻为主象宜临日月生扶、臣下筮国以九五当尊位忌随墓空破；亲占应合世者后德著于樛木，臣占二持福者母仪冠于掖庭

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 皇帝亲自占卜，以世爻为主象，适宜得到日月的生扶；臣下为国占卜，以九五爻当作尊位，忌讳随墓、空、破。皇帝亲占而应爻合世的，皇后的德行像樛木那样昭著；臣下占卜而二爻持福神的，皇后的母仪在后宫居首。臣下占卜，以五爻为君、二爻为后，所以这样说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the emperor divines in person, the Self line is the chief image and it is fitting for the Day and Month to generate and support it; when a minister divines for the state, the ninth-fifth line is the honoured position and following into the tomb, Void and break are to be avoided. If the emperor divines in person and the Other combines with the Self, the empress's virtue shines like that of the drooping tree; if a minister divines and the second line holds the fortune spirit, the empress's model of motherhood heads the harem. A minister divining takes the fifth line as the sovereign and the second as the empress, hence what is said.

> [!note] 按
> 「皇陛」疑是「皇帝」。「九五」是第五爻，代表君位。「樛木」是《诗经·周南》里称颂后妃德行（能惠及下人）的篇名；「掖庭」是后宫。「世爻为主象」是皇帝自己占，世就是皇帝本人。这一条与易林补遗「五爻为天子、太岁为朝廷」（EL.80）和黄金策国朝「九五逢阳，当遇仁明之主」（HJC.65）同取五爻为君。
>
> *Note:* 'Huangbi' is presumably 'huangdi', emperor. 'Jiuwu', the ninth-fifth, is the fifth line, which stands for the sovereign's position. 'Jiumu', the drooping tree, is the title of an ode in the Book of Odes that praises the virtue of a queen who is gracious to those beneath her; 'yeting' is the inner palace. 'The Self line is the chief image' means that when the emperor divines for himself the Self is the emperor in person. This entry takes the fifth line as the sovereign, as 'the fifth line is the Son of Heaven, Tai Sui is the court' in the Yilin Buyi (EL.80) and 'the ninth-fifth meeting yang, one meets a benevolent and enlightened sovereign' in the Huangjin Ce on the state (HJC.65) do.
