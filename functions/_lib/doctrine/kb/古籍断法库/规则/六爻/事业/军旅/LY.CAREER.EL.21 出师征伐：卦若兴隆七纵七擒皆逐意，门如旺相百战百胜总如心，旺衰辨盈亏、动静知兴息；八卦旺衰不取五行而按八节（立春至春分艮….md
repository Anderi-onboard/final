# 出师征伐：卦若兴隆七纵七擒皆逐意，门如旺相百战百胜总如心，旺衰辨盈亏、动静知兴息；八卦旺衰不取五行而按八节（立春至春分艮旺震相…冬至至立春坎旺艮相）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦如果兴隆，七纵七擒都能如意；门如果旺相，百战百胜都能称心。旺衰能辨出盈亏，动静能知道兴息。八卦的旺衰不取乾兑金、震巽木、坎水、离火、坤艮土（那样的五行），而是由八个节气生出八卦的状态，各按它的时节：立春到春分，艮旺、震相；春分到立夏，震旺、巽相；立夏到夏至，巽旺、离相；夏至到立秋，离旺、坤相；立秋到秋分，坤旺、兑相；秋分到立冬，兑旺、乾相；立冬到冬至，乾旺、坎相；冬至到立春，坎旺、艮相。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram flourishes, seven releases and seven captures go as wished; if the gate is thriving, a hundred battles and a hundred victories are all to one's mind. Strength and weakness can tell waxing from waning, and motion and stillness can tell rising from ceasing. The strength of the eight trigrams is not taken from the elements Qian and Dui Metal, Zhen and Xun Wood, Kan Water, Li Fire, Kun and Gen Earth, but from the eight solar terms which produce the states of the eight trigrams, each by its season: from the Start of Spring to the Spring Equinox, Gen thrives and Zhen assists; from the Spring Equinox to the Start of Summer, Zhen thrives and Xun assists; from the Start of Summer to the Summer Solstice, Xun thrives and Li assists; from the Summer Solstice to the Start of Autumn, Li thrives and Kun assists; from the Start of Autumn to the Autumn Equinox, Kun thrives and Dui assists; from the Autumn Equinox to the Start of Winter, Dui thrives and Qian assists; from the Start of Winter to the Winter Solstice, Qian thrives and Kan assists; from the Winter Solstice to the Start of Spring, Kan thrives and Gen assists.

> [!note] 按
> 这里是「八卦随节气的旺相」的另一种写法：只写旺、相两个状态，八个节气各让一个卦旺、一个卦相，而不是用乾兑属金、震巽属木那样的五行；易隐征战占「方向」（YY.51）把八个状态全部列出来了，旺、相和这里完全一致。「七纵七擒」是诸葛亮七擒孟获的故事，比喻出兵从容如意。
>
> *Note:* This is another way of writing the strength of the eight trigrams by solar term: only the two states, thriving and assisting, are written, with each of the eight solar terms giving one trigram thriving and one assisting, and not by the elements, Qian and Dui Metal, Zhen and Xun Wood and so on; the 'direction' section of the Yi Yin war divination (YY.51) lists all eight states, and its thriving and assisting agree exactly with this. 'Seven releases and seven captures' is the story of Zhuge Liang capturing Meng Huo seven times, an image of going out with troops calmly and as wished.
