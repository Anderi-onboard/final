# 征战·阴象持兄兄克应，李愬雪夜走而元济遭擒（旧注：兄为伏兵，内象旺动克应是我伏兵、克世是他伏兵；兄在阳象宜日间伏、在阴象宜夜间伏，如李愬雪夜衔枚直捣蔡城擒吴元济）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟是伏兵。兄弟在内卦旺相发动、克应爻，是我方设伏；克世爻，是对方设伏。兄弟在阳象适合白天设伏，在阴象适合夜里设伏，就像唐宪宗时李愬雪夜衔枚疾行，直捣蔡州城，擒住了吴元济。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer is the ambush. If Peer in the inner trigram is thriving and moves and controls the Other, it is our ambush; if it controls the Self, it is the enemy's. If Peer is in a yang image the ambush is fit for the day and in a yin image for the night, like Li Su in the reign of Emperor Xianzong of Tang, who marched through a snowy night with his men holding sticks in their mouths for silence, struck straight at the city of Cai and captured Wu Yuanji.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5922 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 阴象持兄兄克应，李愬雪夜走而元济遭擒。 | 兄弟在阴象上、克制应爻，就像李愬雪夜行军而吴元济被擒。 | If Peer sits in a yin image and controls the Other, it is like Li Su's march on a snowy night and the capture of Yuanji. |

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5923 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄为伏兵。 | 兄弟是伏兵。 | Peer is the ambush. |
> | 如在内象旺动，克应，乃我伏兵； | 如果它在内卦旺相发动、克应爻，就是我方的伏兵； | If it is in the inner trigram, thriving and moving, and controls the Other, it is our ambush; |
> | 克世，是他伏兵。 | 克世爻，就是对方的伏兵。 | if it controls the Self, it is the enemy's ambush. |
> | 若兄在阳象，宜日间伏； | 如果兄弟在阳象，适合白天设伏； | If Peer is in a yang image, the ambush is fit for the day; |
> | 兄在阴象，宜夜间伏。 | 兄弟在阴象，适合夜里设伏。 | if Peer is in a yin image, the ambush is fit for the night. |
> | 如唐宪宗朝，李朔雪夜衔枚走，直捣蔡城，以擒吴元济也。 | 就像唐宪宗时，李愬（这里原文作「李朔」）雪夜衔枚疾行，直捣蔡州城，擒住吴元济。 | It is like Li Su (written Li Shuo here) in the reign of Emperor Xianzong of Tang, who marched through a snowy night with his men holding sticks in their mouths for silence, struck straight at the city of Cai and captured Wu Yuanji. |

> *Note:* The old commentary writes Li Su as 'Li Shuo', and the Bu Shi Zheng Zong writes it correctly.
