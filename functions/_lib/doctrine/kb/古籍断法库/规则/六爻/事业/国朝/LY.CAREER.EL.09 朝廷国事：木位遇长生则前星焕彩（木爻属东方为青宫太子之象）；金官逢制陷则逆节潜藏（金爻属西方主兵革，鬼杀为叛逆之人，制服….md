# 朝廷国事：木位遇长生则前星焕彩（木爻属东方为青宫太子之象）；金官逢制陷则逆节潜藏（金爻属西方主兵革，鬼杀为叛逆之人，制服或随空亡墓绝则不久潜消）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木爻遇到长生，前星（太子星）焕发光彩：木爻属东方，是青宫太子之象，前星也是太子之星，「焕彩」是说他得位，如果遇到长生又有气，他的德泽就广被而长久。金爻的官鬼逢到克制、陷落，就知道叛逆之人潜藏着：金爻属西方，主兵革之象，官鬼和凶煞是叛逆的人，如果被制服，或者随着空亡、墓、绝，不久就潜消。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a Wood line meets Birth, the Front Star (the heir's star) shines: Wood belongs to the east and is the image of the Azure Palace and the heir apparent; the Front Star is also the star of the heir; 'shines' says that he has his place, and if it meets Birth and has qi, his virtue spreads widely and lasts. If a Metal Pressure line meets restraint or sinks, then know that rebels lie hidden: Metal belongs to the west and is the image of war; Pressure and baleful spirits are the rebels, and if they are subdued, or follow Void, tomb or extinction, they soon vanish in secret.

> [!note] 按
> 第一联的原文是「木位遇长生，会购买关星焕彩」，「会购买关」是传抄的错字，按解里「前星者，亦太子之星，焕彩」应是「前星焕彩」，译文按「前星焕彩」译。「德被长生」字面不顺，译作「德泽广被而长久」。「青宫」是太子的东宫（见 YY.38）。「逆节」是叛逆的行为。
>
> *Note:* The text of the first couplet reads 'mu wei yu changsheng, hui gou mai guan xing huan cai'; 'hui gou mai guan' is a miscopy, and following the explanation, 'the Front Star is also the star of the heir; shines', it should be 'qian xing huan cai', and the translation reads 'the Front Star shines'. 'De bei changsheng' does not run smoothly by the letter and is rendered 'his virtue spreads widely and lasts'. The 'Azure Palace' is the heir apparent's Eastern Palace (see YY.38). 'Nijie' is rebellious conduct.
