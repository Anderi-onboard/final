# 上书奏对：进拜表章兼上本，父旺官兴御笔标，亦忌六冲财子动，次愁印绶化财爻，更嫌鬼象之为福，父绝官空莫进朝；拜表上本启奏皆同，若得父旺官兴定蒙准奏，若犯六冲、财摇子动、父变妻财、官之福、父绝官空一节者莫奏朝廷，纵奏徒费心机难迎圣意

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 递表章、上奏本，父爻旺相、官爻兴旺，就会得到御笔批准。也忌讳六冲、财爻和子孙发动；其次怕印绶（父母）变成财爻，更嫌官鬼变成福神（子孙）；父爻绝、官爻空，就不要进朝。拜表、上本、启奏都是同一个取法：父旺官兴，一定蒙准奏；如果遇到六冲，或者财摇子动、或者父变妻财、或者官变福神、或者父绝官空，这几条里只要犯了一条，就不要奏朝廷；即使奏了，也白费心机，难以合君王的心意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In presenting a memorial and submitting a petition, if Resource is thriving and the star of office flourishes, the imperial brush will mark it for approval. Also fear a six-clash and Wealth and Output moving; next worry the seal line changing into the Wealth line; still more dislike the Pressure image changing into Output; with Resource extinct and the star of office Void, do not go to court. Presenting a memorial, submitting a petition and memorialising all take the same method: if Resource is thriving and the star of office flourishes, the memorial will surely be approved; if it meets a six-clash, or Wealth sways and Output moves, or Resource changes into Wealth, or the star of office changes into Output, or Resource is extinct and the star of office Void, then if even one of these points is violated, do not memorialise the court; even if one does, it is a waste of effort and hardly meets the sovereign's wish.

> [!note] 按
> 「表章」「奏本」都是呈给君王的文书；「御笔标」是皇帝用笔批示。「印绶」是父母爻（文书、印）。「官之福」是官变成福神（子孙）。这一条与增删卜易上书（ZS.28 起）取同一类象：父母（文书）旺、官旺为吉；财动克文书、子动伤官为凶。
>
> *Note:* 'Biaozhang' and 'zouben' are both documents presented to the sovereign; 'yubi biao' is the emperor marking approval with his brush. 'Yinshou', the seal line, is the Resource line (the document and the seal). 'Guan zhi fu' is the star of office changing into the fortune spirit (Output). This entry takes the same kind of images as the Zengshan Buyi on submitting letters (from ZS.28): Resource (the document) thriving and the star of office thriving are auspicious; Wealth moving to control the document and Output moving to hurt the star of office are ominous.
