# 谋望占总述（游南子）与鬼谷分爻：欲为而不敢遽为则有谋，欲得而不可必得则有望；先占可否、再占成败、三占迟速；分爻国事官事人事家事身事心事

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谋望占总述：想做一件事又不敢马上去做，就有了谋；想得到又不能一定得到，就有了望。所以先占它行不行，既然行了，再占它成不成，既然成了，再占它是快是慢，大致就是这些。分爻是：国事、官事、人事、家事、身事、心事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Overview of divining a scheme. When one wants to do a thing and does not dare to do it at once, there is a scheme; when one wants to get a thing and cannot be sure of getting it, there is a hope. So first divine whether it is feasible; once feasible, whether it will succeed; once successful, whether it will be slow or fast; that is the general outline. The division of the lines: affairs of state, official affairs, affairs of others, family affairs, personal affairs and affairs of the heart.

> [!note] 按
> 表里六个名目没有写各是哪一爻，读法同 YY.02：从上爻写到初爻，即上爻国事、五爻官事、四爻人事、三爻家事、二爻身事、初爻心事。这是读法，不是书里明写的。第三、四句在书里各自成句，但意思连着前一句，译文用分号接起来。
>
> *Note:* The table does not say which line each of the six names belongs to, and it is read as in YY.02, from the top line down to the first: the top line affairs of state, the fifth official affairs, the fourth affairs of others, the third family affairs, the second personal affairs and the first affairs of the heart. This is a way of reading it and not something the book writes out. The third and fourth sentences stand each alone in the book but their sense runs on from the previous one, and the translation joins them with semicolons.
