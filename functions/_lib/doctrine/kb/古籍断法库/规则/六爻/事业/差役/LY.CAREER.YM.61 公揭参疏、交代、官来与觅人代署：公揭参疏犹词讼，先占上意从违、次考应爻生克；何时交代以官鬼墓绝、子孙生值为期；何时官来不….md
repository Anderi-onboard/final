# 公揭参疏、交代、官来与觅人代署：公揭参疏犹词讼，先占上意从违、次考应爻生克；何时交代以官鬼墓绝、子孙生值为期；何时官来不拘士庶僚役以官鬼生旺为实；觅人代署应空则谁代之，或从官或从人，用爻当晰

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是公开揭发、参劾上疏的事，就像词讼一样：先占上面的意思是依从还是违背，再看应爻的生克。问什么时候交代（与继任人交接）：以官鬼入墓、入绝，子孙生旺值日（月）为期。问什么时候新官来：不论是读书人、百姓、官员、衙役，都以官鬼生旺为应验。找人代理署事：应空，谁来代他？或者从官（爵位）看，或者从人（那个人）看，用爻应当分清。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A public denunciation or impeaching memorial is like a lawsuit: first divine whether the higher authority will comply or go against it, then examine the generating and controlling of the Other line. When will the handover be: the date is set by Pressure entering the tomb or extinction and Output being born or on duty. When will the new official come: whatever sort of person, scholar, commoner, official or runner, it is confirmed by Pressure being generated and thriving. In seeking someone to act in the post: if the Other is Void, who will stand in for him? Either look from the office (its rank) or from the man, and the line to use should be made clear.

> [!note] 按
> 「公揭」是公开的揭帖，「参疏」是参劾的奏疏，这一类像打官司（词讼），所以先看上意、再看应爻。「上意从违」是上面的意思是依从还是违背。「交代」是前后任官员交接。「官来」是继任的官什么时候到。「代署」是找人代理（署理）这个官职。「士庶僚役」是读书人、百姓、官员、衙役，不论哪一类人来接任，都以官鬼生旺为应验。书里自注：「官来」问的是那个官位（爵），「人代」问的是那个人，用爻要分得这样细。「交代」的应期取官鬼入墓绝（旧官的运尽）和子孙生旺（新的接替）：这与易隐官禄占的替代（YY.24）用官逢死墓绝日代、子旺动即时可代是同一类。
>
> *Note:* 'Gongjie' is a public placard of accusation, and 'canshu' is a memorial of impeachment; this kind of matter is like a lawsuit, so one first looks at the higher authority's mind and then at the Other line. 'Shangyi congwei' is whether the higher authority will comply or go against it. 'Jiaodai' is the handover between the outgoing and incoming officials. 'Guan lai' is when the successor arrives. 'Daishu' is finding someone to act in the post. 'Shishu liaoyi' are scholars, commoners, officials and runners: whichever sort of man comes to take over, it is confirmed by Pressure being generated and thriving. The book's own gloss: 'the official comes' asks about the rank of that post, and 'someone stands in' asks about that man, and the line to use is distinguished this finely. The timing of the handover takes Pressure entering the tomb or extinction (the old official's luck exhausted) and Output being born or thriving (the successor), the same kind as 'Pressure meeting a day of death, tomb or extinction takes over; Output thriving and moving can take over at once' in the Yi Yin on replacement (YY.24).
