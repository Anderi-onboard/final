# 坎、咸、复三卦，求官主心中狐疑不定

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占得坎、咸、复这三个卦，求官的人心里疑惑、犹豫不决。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the hexagrams Kan, Xian or Fu, the seeker of office is left in doubt and hesitation.

## 《阐奥歌章·仕宦章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3741 行 · Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on holding office", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 坎咸复卦定狐疑。 | 坎、咸、复这三卦，一定是狐疑不决。 | Kan, Xian and Fu: it is settled that there is doubt and hesitation. |
