# 袭求武弁：身世爻当衰弱则力怯机疏、世逢日月克冲戈戟最为下辈；世若休囚卦不旺相当推无力无能，又被日辰月建冲克世爻者提拔全无反遭弹劾，其中又看官爻旺则声名显赫空则劾削兵权

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 身爻、世爻衰弱，就力量怯弱、机会稀少；世爻遇到日、月克制或冲，在拿戈戟的人当中也是最下等的。世爻如果休囚、卦又不旺相，就该断为没有力量、没有才能；又被日辰、月建冲克世爻的，完全没有提拔，反而遭到弹劾，哪里能在军队中出名？这中间还要看官爻：旺相就声名显赫，落空就被弹劾、削去兵权。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Body and Self lines are weak, one's strength is timid and one's chances sparse; if the Self meets control or clash from the Day and the Month, even among bearers of spear and halberd one is of the lowest rank. If the Self is at rest or imprisoned and the hexagram is not thriving, one should be judged as without strength or ability; if the Self line is again clashed or controlled by the Day and the Month, there is no advancement at all and on the contrary impeachment, so how could one win fame in the ranks? Among these, look also at the Pressure line: if it is thriving, fame is eminent; if it is Void, one is impeached and stripped of military power.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 782 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世若休囚，卦不旺相，当推无力无能。 | 世爻如果休囚，卦又不旺相，就该断为没有力量、没有才能。 | If the Self is at rest or imprisoned and the hexagram is not thriving, one should be judged as without strength or ability. |
> | 又被日辰月建冲克世爻者，提拔全无，反遭弹劾，岂得驰名于军伍？ | 又被日辰、月建冲克世爻的，完全没有提拔，反而遭到弹劾，哪里能在军队中出名？ | If the Self line is again clashed or controlled by the Day and the Month, there is no advancement at all and on the contrary impeachment, so how could one win fame in the ranks? |
> | 其中又看官爻，旺则声名显赫，空则劾削兵权。 | 这中间还要看官爻：旺相就声名显赫，落空就被弹劾、削去兵权。 | Among these, look also at the Pressure line: if it is thriving, fame is eminent; if it is Void, one is impeached and stripped of military power. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「休囚」是休、囚两种不得令的状态（见术语表）。「戈戟最为下辈」是拿戈戟的兵卒里也是最低的一等。「劾削兵权」是被弹劾、削去兵权。这一条与增删卜易防参劾「身衰鬼克，贬责凌呵」（ZS.41）、黄金策仕宦「日辰冲克，定然诽谤之多招」（HJC.12）取同一个关系：日月冲克世爻，有祸。
>
> *Note:* 'Xiu qiu' are the two states of 'at rest' and 'imprisoned', both out of season (see the glossary). 'Ge ji zui wei xia bei' is the lowest grade even among the soldiers who carry spears and halberds. 'Hexue bingquan' is being impeached and stripped of military power. This entry takes the same relation as 'a weak Self controlled by Pressure brings demotion and rebuke' in the Zengshan Buyi on guarding against impeachment (ZS.41) and 'if the Day clashes or controls, there will surely be much slander' in the Huangjin Ce on official careers (HJC.12): the Day and Month clashing or controlling the Self line means trouble.
