# 升迁何方·官金而应西土，木官必应东方，水北火南，土升中土（野鹤：此古法也，验过的有出入：木官应山东亦有应广东，金官应山西亦有江西、广西，又有以住处分东西南北）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 按官星的五行断去哪个方位上任：金官应西，木官应东，水应北，火应南，土官升到中土。野鹤说这是古法，他验证过的有出入：木官应在山东，也有应在广东的；金官应在山西，也有应在江西、广西的；还有按所住的地方来分东西南北的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To judge in which direction the post lies from the element of the star of office: a Metal one is due in the west, a Wood one in the east, Water in the north, Fire in the south, and an Earth one is promoted to the central land. The Old Man of Yehe says this is the old method and that what he has confirmed does not quite agree: a Wood star of office is due in Shandong, but sometimes in Guangdong; a Metal one in Shanxi, but sometimes in Jiangxi or Guangxi; and sometimes the directions are divided according to the place where one lives.
