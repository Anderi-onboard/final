# 仕宦升迁：欲知何月官升处、遇值逢生喜报祥；欲决何时迁转，推评官鬼旺衰——旺则高升在近，衰则待生旺之期；若六爻无鬼，查那月临官，便知迁升决矣

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道官职在哪个月升，遇到官爻当值、得生的月份，就有喜报。要断定什么时候迁转，就推断官鬼的旺衰：官鬼旺相，高升就在眼前；衰弱，就等它得生、旺相的时候。如果六爻里没有官鬼，再查哪个月临到官，就能知道升迁的决断了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know in which month one is promoted, when the line meets its own month or the month that generates it, joyful news is reported. To decide when the promotion or transfer will come, weigh the strength of Pressure: if it is thriving, the high promotion is near at hand; if weak, wait for the time when it is generated or thriving. If there is no Pressure among the six lines, then check which month falls on the star of office, and one will know the decision of the promotion.

> [!note] 按
> 「官陛处」的「陛」是「升」。「高承升在尔」按字面不顺，「尔」疑是「迩」（近），译作「高升就在近前」；「承」不译。「那月临官」按文意是官爻（官星）当值的那个月，书里没有细说。这一条与易隐官禄占「候选（二）·何日得官」（YY.08）取同一个法：官旺者看当值或合的年月日，官衰者看生旺的时候，官伏藏者看临值的时候。
>
> *Note:* The 'bi' of 'guan bi chu' is 'sheng', to rise. 'Gao cheng sheng zai er' does not run smoothly as written, and 'er' is presumably 'er', near, so it is translated 'the high promotion is near at hand'; 'cheng' is left untranslated. 'The month falls on the star of office' is, by the sense, the month in which the line of the star of office is on duty, which the book does not go into. This entry takes the same method as 'on what day one gets office' in the Yi Yin divination on awaiting selection (YY.08): for a thriving star of office look at the year, month or day on duty or in combination, for a weak one at the time it is generated or thriving, and for a hidden one at the time it comes on duty.
