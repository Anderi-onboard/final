# 申酉辰戌持世为吉；当官遇禄好施为（申酉持世兼得禄最吉）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 申、酉、辰、戌的爻持世是吉；做官又遇上禄，正好施展；其中申、酉持世又得禄，最吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The branches Shen, You, Chen and Xu holding the Self are auspicious; taking office and meeting the Emolument gives room to act; Shen or You holding the Self and getting the Emolument is the most auspicious.
