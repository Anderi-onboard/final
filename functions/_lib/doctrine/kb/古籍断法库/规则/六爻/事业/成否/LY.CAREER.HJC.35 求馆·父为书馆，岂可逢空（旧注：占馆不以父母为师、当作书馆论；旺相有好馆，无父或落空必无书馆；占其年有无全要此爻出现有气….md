# 求馆·父为书馆，岂可逢空（旧注：占馆不以父母为师、当作书馆论；旺相有好馆，无父或落空必无书馆；占其年有无全要此爻出现有气；应来生合世自有人请，世生合应及父须自去访求；无父而动爻化出有人推荐，父化父有两处书馆，本宫化他宫由近及远）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占馆不把父母当作老师，要当作书馆来看。父母旺相有气，就有好书馆；卦里没有父母或者落空，必定没有书馆，事情也难成。占那一年有没有书馆，全要这一爻出现、有气不空；应爻来生合世爻，自然有人来聘请，世爻生合应爻和父爻，就必须自己去访求；卦里没有父母而动爻化出父母的，意想不到有人推荐；父化父必有两处书馆；本宫化入他宫由近及远，他宫化入本宫从外到内。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a school post, Resource is not taken as the teacher but as the school itself. If Resource is thriving and has breath, there is a good school; if there is no Resource in the hexagram or it falls Void, there will surely be no school and the matter is hard to bring about. To divine whether there will be a school that year everything depends on this line appearing with breath and not Void; if the Other comes to generate or combine with the Self, someone naturally comes to engage one, and if the Self generates or combines with the Other and with Resource, one must go and seek on one's own; if there is no Resource in the hexagram but a moving line turns into it, someone unexpectedly recommends one; Resource turning into Resource means two schools; from the home palace into another palace is from near to far, and from another palace into the home palace is from outside to inside.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2753 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占馆以父母爻为馆，旺相则有好馆。 | 占馆以父母爻为书馆，旺相就有好馆。 | In divining a school post Resource is the school, and if thriving there is a good school. |
> | 卦无父或落空，必无书馆，事亦难成。 | 卦里没有父母或者落空，必定没有书馆，事情也难成。 | If there is no Resource in the hexagram or it falls Void, there will surely be no school, and the matter is hard to bring about. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 在这一章里「父母」不是老师（老师是世爻），而是「书馆」本身（馆舍、学馆）。这和占考试、占文书时父母是文书、功名的用法不同。「由近及远」「由外入内」是用本宫、他宫来看书馆的方位：变出在本宫是近处，变到他宫是远处。旧注比王洪绪多讲了：占那一年有没有书馆要看这一爻出现、有人聘请和自己去访求的区别、父化父有两馆。
>
> *Note:* In this chapter 'Resource' is not the teacher (the teacher is the Self) but the 'school' itself (the school building, the schoolhouse). This differs from the usage in examinations and documents, where Resource is documents and the degree. 'From near to far' and 'from outside to inside' use the home palace and other palaces to see where the school is: changing within the home palace is near, and changing into another palace is far. The old commentary says more than Wang Hongxu: to divine whether there will be a school that year one looks for this line to appear; the difference between being engaged and going to seek oneself; and Resource turning into Resource meaning two schools.
