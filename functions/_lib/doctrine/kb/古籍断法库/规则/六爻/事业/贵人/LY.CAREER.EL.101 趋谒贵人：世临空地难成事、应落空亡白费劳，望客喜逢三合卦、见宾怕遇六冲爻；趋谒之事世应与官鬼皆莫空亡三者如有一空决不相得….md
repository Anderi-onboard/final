# 趋谒贵人：世临空地难成事、应落空亡白费劳，望客喜逢三合卦、见宾怕遇六冲爻；趋谒之事世应与官鬼皆莫空亡三者如有一空决不相得，纵不逢空若值六冲决无美意，若得三合六合之卦自然宾主相谐，应象交重谓人不遇

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻落在空地，事难成；应爻落到空亡，白费辛劳。拜访客人，喜欢遇到三合的卦；会见宾客，怕遇到六冲的爻。趋谒的事，世、应和官鬼都不要落空亡，这三个里如果有一个落空，一定彼此不合；即使不遇空，如果遇到六冲，也一定没有好意；如果得到三合、六合的卦，宾主自然和谐；如果应象又发动，就说是人不遇。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self falls on empty ground the matter is hard to accomplish; if the Other falls on Void the toil is wasted. In calling on a visitor one is glad to meet a three-harmony hexagram; in meeting a guest one fears a six-clash line. In a call on a noble, the Self, the Other and Pressure must none be Void; if one of the three is Void, the two will surely not agree; even if none is Void, if there is a six-clash there will surely be no good will; if the hexagram is a three-harmony or a six-combine, guest and host are naturally in accord; if the Other image moves as well, it is said that the person is not met.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「望客」「见宾」都是去拜访。「三合」是三合局，「六合」「六冲」是卦的六合、六冲（见术语表）。「应象交重」是应爻发动（「交重」是动爻的记号）；「谓人不遇」是说要见的人不在、见不到。这一条与易隐谒贵占「动者难见，空者不见；……六合见，六冲不见」（YY.75）取同一类象。
>
> *Note:* 'Wangke' and 'jianbin' both mean going to call. 'Three-harmony' is the three-harmony formation, and 'six-combine' and 'six-clash' are the hexagram patterns (see the glossary). 'Ying xiang jiaozhong' is the Other line moving ('jiaozhong' is the mark of a moving line); 'wei ren bu yu' says that the person to be seen is not in and cannot be seen. This entry takes the same kind of images as 'if it moves he is hard to see, if Void he is not seen; ... with a six-combine one sees him, with a six-clash one does not' in the Yi Yin divination on calling on a noble (YY.75).
