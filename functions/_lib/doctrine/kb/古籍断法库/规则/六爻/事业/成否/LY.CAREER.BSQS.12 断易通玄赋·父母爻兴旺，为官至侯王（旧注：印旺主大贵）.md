# 断易通玄赋·父母爻兴旺，为官至侯王（旧注：印旺主大贵）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻兴旺，做官能做到侯王；旧注说，印（父母）旺，主有大贵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line flourishes, one who holds office rises to marquis or prince; the old note says that when the Seal (Resource) is strong, great nobility is indicated.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2998 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 印旺主大贵。 | 印旺，主有大贵。 | When the Seal is strong, great nobility is indicated. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「印」是印绶，也就是父母爻（文书、印信），官印旺就是父母旺。同一联前半「占宅青龙旺，豪富冠一乡」是占宅，放在家宅。后面的虎易评注说这两句旧注太简略，那是今人的话，不收。
>
> *Note:* The "Seal" is the seal of office, that is, the Resource line (documents and seals); a strong seal means a strong Resource line. The first half of the couplet, "in divining the house, a strong Azure Dragon makes one rich above all in the village", is about the house and goes to the house domain. The later remark by Hu Yi that these notes are too brief is modern commentary and is not quoted.
