# 道业·择师、受徒传法与云游：择师同延师占——父临日月为名师、世空恐吾不能受教；受徒传法同继嗣——子逢生旺为法器、命墓恐彼不能传持；云游同出行而有访道求利之别

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 所以择师，同（延）师占：父临日辰、月建，是名师；世空，恐怕我不能受教。收徒传法，同继嗣占：子逢生旺，是法器（可造之材）；命墓，恐怕他不能传持（继承）。云游同出行占，但有访道和求利的区别，各自兼用相应的象。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* So choosing a teacher is divined like hiring a teacher: the father line on the Day or Month is a famous teacher; the Self Void, I fear I cannot be taught. Taking disciples and transmitting the teaching is divined like continuing a line of descent: the child line meeting generation and thriving is a vessel of the Dharma (a promising disciple); the life line in the tomb, I fear he cannot carry it on and transmit it. Wandering as a cloud is divined like travelling, but there is a difference between seeking the Way and seeking profit, and each also uses its corresponding image.

> [!note] 按
> 「择师」是选老师（同延师占，见本书延师章），「受徒传法」是收徒弟、传授道法（同继嗣，即过继、立嗣），「云游」是僧道四处游方。「名师」是有名的好老师，「法器」是可以承载佛法、承继衣钵的人才。「命墓」是世爻（命）入墓。「传持」是传承并持守。这一条是把道业的几问分别比到世俗已有的占法上去：择师比延师，收徒比继嗣，云游比出行。比到别的占法，就用那一边的断法。
>
> *Note:* 'Zeshi' is choosing a teacher (divined like hiring a teacher, see the chapter on hiring a teacher in this book), 'shoutu chuanfa' is taking disciples and handing on the teaching (like continuing a line of descent, that is adoption and establishing an heir), and 'yunyou' is a monk or Daoist wandering from place to place. 'Mingshi' is a famous good teacher, and 'faqi' is a person who can carry the Dharma and inherit the robe and bowl. 'The life line in the tomb' is the Self line (the life) entering the tomb. 'Chuanchi' is handing on and keeping. This entry matches each question of the monastic vocation to a divination already known in the worldly sphere: choosing a teacher to hiring a teacher, taking disciples to continuing a line of descent, wandering to travelling. Once matched to another divination, the judgments of that side are used.
