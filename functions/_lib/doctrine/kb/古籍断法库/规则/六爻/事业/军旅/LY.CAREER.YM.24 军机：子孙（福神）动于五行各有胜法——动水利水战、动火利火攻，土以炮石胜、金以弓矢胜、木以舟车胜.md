# 军机：子孙（福神）动于五行各有胜法——动水利水战、动火利火攻，土以炮石胜、金以弓矢胜、木以舟车胜

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙动在水，利于水战；福神动在火，利于火攻；属土的靠炮石取胜，属金的靠弓箭取胜，属木的靠舟车取胜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving in Water favours a battle on the water; Output, the fortune spirit, moving in Fire favours an attack by fire; Earth wins by catapult stones, Metal wins by bow and arrow, Wood wins by boats and carts.

## 《易冒·军机章第四十三》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 864 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 43: Military affairs", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙动水，则利于水战， | 子孙动在水，就利于水战； | Output moving in Water favours a battle on the water; |
> | 福神动火，则利于火攻， | 福神动在火，就利于火攻； | Output, the fortune spirit, moving in Fire favours an attack by fire; |
> | 土以炮石胜，金以弓矢胜，木以舟车胜也。 | 土靠炮石取胜，金靠弓箭取胜，木靠舟车取胜。 | Earth wins by catapult stones, Metal wins by bow and arrow, Wood wins by boats and carts. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 上一条（YM.22–23）讲官鬼（敌）落在哪一行哪一宫、敌在谋什么；这一条反过来讲子孙（我的兵）落在哪一行、我用什么取胜。「炮石」是古代的抛石机。「舟车」是船和车，木行主它们。
>
> *Note:* The previous entries (YM.22–23) speak of which phase and palace Pressure (the enemy) is in and what the enemy is planning; this entry turns it round and speaks of which phase Output (my troops) is in and by what I win. 'Paoshi' is the ancient stone-throwing engine. 'Zhouche' is boats and carts, which the Wood phase governs.
