# 征战·内卦福兴生合应，乐毅所以背燕（旧注：内卦子孙发动反去生合应爻、伤克世身，是我将卒背主降敌之兆，如燕将乐毅背燕投赵）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦的子孙发动，反而去生合应爻、伤克世身，是我方将领士兵背叛主人、投降敌人的征兆，就像燕国的将领乐毅背弃燕国、投奔赵国。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output in the inner trigram moves and, instead, generates or combines with the Other and injures and controls the Self or Body, it is a sign that our generals and soldiers will betray their lord and surrender to the enemy, like Yue Yi, general of Yan, who turned his back on Yan and went over to Zhao.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5964 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 内卦福兴生合应，乐毅所以背燕。 | 内卦的福神兴旺、又生合应爻，这就是乐毅背弃燕国的缘故。 | If the Output line in the inner trigram is thriving and generates or combines with the Other, this is why Yue Yi turned his back on Yan. |

^q1

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙发动反去生合应爻，伤克身世，是我将卒有背主降敌之兆，如燕将乐毅背燕投赵是也。 | 子孙发动，反而去生合应爻，伤克世身，是我方将领士兵背叛主人、投降敌人的征兆，就像燕国的将领乐毅背弃燕国、投奔赵国。 | If Output moves and, instead, generates or combines with the Other and injures and controls the Self or Body, it is a sign that our generals and soldiers will betray their lord and surrender to the enemy, like Yue Yi, general of Yan, who turned his back on Yan and went over to Zhao. |

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说「内卦子孙动」，卜筮正宗只说「子孙发动」，没有限定在内卦。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says 'Output moves in the inner trigram'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says only 'Output moves' and does not limit it to the inner trigram.
