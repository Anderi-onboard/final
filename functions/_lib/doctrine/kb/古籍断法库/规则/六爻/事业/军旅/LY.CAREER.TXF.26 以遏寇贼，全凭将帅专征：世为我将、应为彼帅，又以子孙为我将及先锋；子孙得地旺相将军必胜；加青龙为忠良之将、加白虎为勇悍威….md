# 以遏寇贼，全凭将帅专征：世为我将、应为彼帅，又以子孙为我将及先锋；子孙得地旺相将军必胜；加青龙为忠良之将、加白虎为勇悍威猛之将；持世虽有鬼无伤、旺相发动决胜千里、日辰扶拱百战百胜；所嫌父母发动不可当其锋，入墓无气虽动无伤

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要遏止寇贼，全靠将帅专意出征。世是我方将领，应是对方统帅；又说子孙是我方将领和先锋。子孙得地、旺相，将军必胜；加青龙，是忠良的将领；加白虎，是勇悍威猛的将领；子孙持世，虽有鬼也无伤害；旺相发动，决胜千里之外；再得日辰扶持拱卫，有百战百胜之功。所嫌的是父母发动，即使有子孙也抵挡不了它的锋芒；若入墓、无气，虽动也无伤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To check bandits and raiders one relies wholly on a commander going out on campaign. The Self is our general and the Other their commander; another saying is that Output is our general and our vanguard. If Output is well placed and strong the general is sure to win; with the Azure Dragon added he is a loyal and good general, with the White Tiger a brave, fierce and mighty one; with Output holding the Self, even if Pressure is present there is no harm; strong and moving, victory is decided a thousand li away; and if the Day supports and gathers round it there is the merit of a hundred battles and a hundred victories. What is disliked is Resource moving, whose edge even Output cannot withstand; if it enters its tomb and has no qi, though it moves it does no harm.
