# 通玄妙论·六亲取用·官鬼与父母：官鬼克我、凶多福少，畏子孙恃妻财，宜静不宜动；父母遇财伤本体、逢鬼增光辉

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼是克我的爻，凶的地方多、福的地方少，怕子孙、靠妻财，动起来克兄弟、生扶父母；卦里虽然凶，却不能没有它，宜静不宜动。父母是生我的爻，遇到财爻就伤到自己的根本，碰到官鬼就增添光辉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line is the line that controls me: it is more often ill than a blessing; it fears Output and relies on Wealth; moving, it harms Peer and gives life and support to Resource; though it is ill in a hexagram it must not be missing, and is best still and not moving. The Resource line is the line that gives me life: meeting Wealth it harms its own substance, meeting Pressure it gains in lustre.

> [!note] 按
> 官鬼这一爻在求官里是用神，「官鬼」本来是克我的爻，所以这里讲的是它的一般性质，不是专讲求官；求官时的取用见黄金策仕宦章。官鬼一条后面接着讲占身命、占婚姻为夫，那几件不是事业，没有收。
>
> *Note:* In a divination for office the Pressure line is the Subject, but it is by nature the line that controls me, so this passage gives its general character and is not specifically about seeking office; how it is used in seeking office is in the Huangjin Ce chapter on official posts. After this the passage goes on to the reading of one's person and of marriage (Pressure as husband), which are not career matters and are not included.
