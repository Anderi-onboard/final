# 虎蛇皆动，正当离乱之时；世应俱空，宜见升平之乐：大杀动加白虎螣蛇必大乱卒难休息；大杀当胎养长生位乃丧乱发萌之时、在临官帝旺之地正当大乱过此渐息、当墓绝位将次太平；已经历的离乱，要知何年最甚何年稍息依长生法断；六爻无鬼卦无杀世应俱空即太平

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 白虎、螣蛇都动，正是离乱的时候；世、应都空，适宜见到升平的安乐。大杀如果发动，加白虎、螣蛇，必是大乱，一时难以平息；大杀落在胎、养、长生的位置上，是丧乱刚刚萌发的时候；在临官、帝旺的地方，正当大乱，过了这个阶段才渐渐平息；落在墓、绝的位置上，将要太平。离乱已经过去的，要知道哪一年最厉害、哪一年稍稍平息，依长生法来断。六爻没有鬼、卦里没有杀，世、应都空，就是太平。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Tiger and the Snake both moving it is just the time of dispersal and disorder; with the Self and the Other both void it suits seeing the joy of peaceful times. If the Great Baleful Spirit moves and the White Tiger and the Coiling Snake are added, it is surely great disorder, hard to quiet soon; if the Great Baleful Spirit falls on the Embryo, Nurture or Birth stage it is the time when calamity and disorder are just sprouting; if it is at the Office or Peak stage it is just the height of disorder, which then gradually subsides; if it falls on the Tomb or Extinction stage peace is coming. For a disorder already passed, to know in which year it was at its worst and in which it eased a little, judge by the method of the Birth stages. With no Pressure among the six lines and no baleful spirit in the hexagram, and the Self and the Other both void, there is peace.
