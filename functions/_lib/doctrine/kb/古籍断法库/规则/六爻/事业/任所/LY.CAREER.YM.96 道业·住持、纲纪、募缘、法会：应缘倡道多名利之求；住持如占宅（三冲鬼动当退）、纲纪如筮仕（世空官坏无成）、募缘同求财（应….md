# 道业·住持、纲纪、募缘、法会：应缘倡道多名利之求；住持如占宅（三冲鬼动当退）、纲纪如筮仕（世空官坏无成）、募缘同求财（应空财陷徒往）、法会如设馆（应空鬼动多损）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 此后再往前走，顺应因缘倡导道法，就多有名利的追求了。住持（主持寺观）如占宅：三冲、鬼动，就该退；纲纪（寺观的管事）如筮仕：世空、官坏，就无成；募缘（化缘募捐）同求财：应空、财陷，就白去一趟；法会如设馆：应空、鬼动，就多有损失。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Beyond this, to go along with the occasion and promote the Way brings many pursuits of fame and profit. Being abbot (presiding over a temple) is like divining about a house: the three clashes and Pressure moving mean one should withdraw; holding an administrative post is like divining about taking office: the Self Void and Pressure damaged mean nothing comes of it; soliciting donations is the same as seeking wealth: the Other Void and Wealth fallen mean the trip is in vain; a ritual assembly is like opening a school: the Other Void and Pressure moving mean many losses.

> [!note] 按
> 「应缘倡道」是顺应因缘出来倡导、弘扬道法。「住持」是主持一座寺观，「纲纪」是寺观里管事务和纪律的执事，「募缘」是向信众化缘募捐，「法会」是做法事、办法会。书里把它们分别比到世俗的占法上：住持比占宅（宅卜，见家宅各章），纲纪比筮仕（本章 YM.40 起），募缘比求财，法会比设馆（卜馆章 YM.29 起）。「三冲鬼动而当退」是住持要退位的征兆；「世空官坏而无成」是纲纪的位子谋不成；「应空财陷而徒往」是化缘白跑一趟；「应空鬼动而多损」是法会办起来多有损耗。
>
> *Note:* 'Yingyuan changdao' is going along with the occasion to come forward and promote the Way. 'Zhuchi' is presiding over a temple or monastery, 'gangji' is the executive who looks after affairs and discipline in a temple, 'muyuan' is soliciting alms and donations from believers, and 'fahui' is performing rites and holding assemblies. The book matches each to a worldly divination: being abbot to divining about a house (house divination, see the chapters on dwellings), administrative posts to divining about taking office (from YM.40 of this book), soliciting donations to seeking wealth, and the ritual assembly to opening a school (from YM.29, the chapter on a teaching post). 'The three clashes and Pressure moving, one should withdraw' is the sign that an abbot must step down; 'the Self Void and Pressure damaged, nothing comes of it' is that the administrative seat cannot be got; 'the Other Void and Wealth fallen, the trip is in vain' is the begging trip made for nothing; 'the Other Void and Pressure moving, many losses' is the assembly costing much once it is held.
