# 升迁何方·初爻为内地，五六为边方（野鹤：世与官星都在初爻为应内地；世在五、官在六，或相反，或官星持世同在五六爻为应边方；在二三四爻仍以五行决之）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看是内地还是边远：初爻代表内地，五爻、六爻代表边远地方。野鹤解释：「应在初爻」是世爻和官星都在初爻；「应在五、六爻」是世在五爻官在六爻，或世在六爻官在五爻，或官星持世、一起在五、六爻上；如果在二、三、四爻，仍然按五行来决断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To tell whether the post is inland or on the frontier: the first line is the inner land and the fifth and sixth lines are the frontier. The Old Man of Yehe explains: 'due in the first line' means the Self and the star of office are both in the first line; 'due in the fifth or sixth line' means the Self is in the fifth line and the star of office in the sixth, or the Self in the sixth and the star of office in the fifth, or the star of office holds the Self and both are in the fifth or sixth line; if they are in the second, third or fourth line, one still decides by the five elements.
