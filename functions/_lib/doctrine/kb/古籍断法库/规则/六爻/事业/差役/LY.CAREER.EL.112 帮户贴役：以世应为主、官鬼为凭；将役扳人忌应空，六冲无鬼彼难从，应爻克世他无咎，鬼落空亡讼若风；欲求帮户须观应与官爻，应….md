# 帮户贴役：以世应为主、官鬼为凭；将役扳人忌应空，六冲无鬼彼难从，应爻克世他无咎，鬼落空亡讼若风；欲求帮户须观应与官爻，应若空亡师来帮贴，世遭应克无力扳他，若卜六冲决难遂意、如无官鬼岂得扳人，鬼象临空临绝官心无主无为

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要把自己的役扳到别人身上（求帮户），以世爻、应爻为主，以官鬼为凭据。把役扳给别人，忌讳应爻落空；六冲又没有官鬼，对方难以服从；应爻克世爻，他没有过错；官鬼落空亡，官司就像风一样（落空）。想求得帮户，必须看应爻和官爻：应爻如果空亡，「师来帮贴」；世爻被应爻克制，没有力量去扳他；如果卜得六冲，一定难以遂意；没有官鬼，怎么能扳到人？官鬼的象临空、临绝，官的心里没有主意，不会有作为。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To shift a service onto others and so seek help from another household, take the Self and the Other as the chief and Pressure as the ground. In passing the service on to another, the Other being Void is to be avoided; with a six-clash and no Pressure the other party finds it hard to comply; if the Other controls the Self, he is not at fault; if Pressure falls on Void, the lawsuit is like the wind. To seek a helper household one must look at the Other and the Pressure line: if the Other is Void, 'the master comes to help with a subsidy'; if the Self is controlled by the Other, one has no strength to drag him in; if a six-clash is cast it will surely be hard to achieve one's wish; with no Pressure how can one drag another in? If the Pressure image meets Void or extinction, the official's mind has no direction and he does nothing.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「帮户」是协助承担户役的户，「帮贴」「津贴」是补贴费用；「将役扳人」是把役扳到别人身上，让他一起分担。「扳」是攀扯、扳累。「师来帮贴」文字疑有误，按上下文（前面「应空」是忌）似应是没有人来帮贴；这里按字面译。「讼若风」按文意是官司落空、没有结果，书里没有展开。「官心无主」的「官」是承办的官。
>
> *Note:* 'Banghu' is a household that helps to bear a household's service, and 'bangtie' and 'jintie' are subsidising the expense; 'jiang yi ban ren' is dragging another person into the service so that he shares it. 'Ban' is to pull in and to saddle. 'Shi lai bangtie' looks wrong in its characters, and by the context (the earlier 'the Other Void' is a taboo) it should presumably mean that no one comes to help with a subsidy; it is translated literally here. 'Song ruo feng' is by the sense a lawsuit coming to nothing, and the book does not go into it. The 'guan' of 'guan xin wu zhu' is the official who handles the matter.
