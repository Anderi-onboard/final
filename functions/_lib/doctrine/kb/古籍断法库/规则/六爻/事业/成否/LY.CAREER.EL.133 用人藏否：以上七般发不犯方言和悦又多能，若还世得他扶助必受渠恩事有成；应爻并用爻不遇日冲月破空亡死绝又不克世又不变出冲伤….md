# 用人藏否：以上七般发不犯方言和悦又多能，若还世得他扶助必受渠恩事有成；应爻并用爻不遇日冲月破空亡死绝又不克世又不变出冲伤、本卦之卦皆非六冲，便曰宾主相投用之有力，用爻若来生世合世者赖以维持周旋吾事

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以上七样都不犯，才可说他和悦又多能；如果世爻又得他的扶助，必定受他的恩，事情成功。应爻和用爻，不遇日冲、月破、空亡、死绝，又不克世，又不变出冲伤，本卦和之卦都不是六冲，就叫做宾主相投，用他有力；用爻如果来生世、合世，就靠他维持，替我周旋办事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If none of these seven things is violated, one may say he is amiable and very capable; if the Self also gets his support, one will surely receive his kindness and the affair will succeed. If the Other line and the Subject line do not meet the Day's clash, Month-break, Void, death or extinction, and do not control the Self, and do not change out a clash or injury, and neither the original nor the changed hexagram is a six-clash, it is called host and guest in accord, and the man is strong to employ; if the Subject line comes to generate or combine with the Self, one relies on him to hold things up and he manages my affairs for me.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1635 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应爻并用爻，不遇日冲、月破、空亡、死绝、又不克世、又不变出冲伤，本卦兴之卦皆非六冲便曰宾主相投，用之有力。 | 应爻和用爻，不遇日冲、月破、空亡、死绝，又不克世，又不变出冲伤，本卦和之卦都不是六冲，就叫做宾主相投，用他有力。 | If the Other line and the Subject line do not meet the Day's clash, Month-break, Void, death or extinction, and do not control the Self, and do not change out a clash or injury, and neither the original nor the changed hexagram is a six-clash, it is called host and guest in accord, and the man is strong to employ. |
> | 用爻若来生世合世者，赖以难持，周旋吾事。 | 用爻如果来生世、合世，就靠他维持，替我周旋办事。 | If the Subject line comes to generate or combine with the Self, one relies on him to hold things up and he manages my affairs for me. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「七般」按解里列出的是：日冲、月破、空亡、死绝、克世、变出冲伤、六冲。「本卦兴之卦」疑是「本卦与之卦」，「赖以难持」疑是「赖以维持」，按文意译。「周旋」是替我张罗办事。「宾主」在这里是用人的人和被用的人。
>
> *Note:* The 'seven things' are, by what the explanation lists: the Day's clash, Month-break, Void, death or extinction, controlling the Self, changing out a clash or injury, and a six-clash. The 'xing' of 'ben gua xing zhi gua' is presumably 'yu', and 'ben gua yu zhi gua' is 'the original hexagram and the changed hexagram'; 'lai yi nan chi' is presumably 'lai yi weichi', relying on him to hold up; they are translated by the sense. 'Zhouxuan' is bustling about on my behalf. 'Host and guest' here are the man who employs and the man who is employed.
