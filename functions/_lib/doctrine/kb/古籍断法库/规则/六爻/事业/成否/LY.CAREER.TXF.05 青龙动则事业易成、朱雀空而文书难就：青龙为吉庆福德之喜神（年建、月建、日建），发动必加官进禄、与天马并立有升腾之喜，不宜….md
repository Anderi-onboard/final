# 青龙动则事业易成、朱雀空而文书难就：青龙为吉庆福德之喜神（年建、月建、日建），发动必加官进禄、与天马并立有升腾之喜，不宜落空；朱雀为占官用爻，宜旺不宜衰，空亡则文书难成、被刑克则文书伤坏

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙发动，事业容易成；朱雀落空，文书难成。青龙是吉庆福德的喜神，有年建、月建、日建三种，发动必定加官进禄，与天马并立自有升腾进益之喜，不宜落空，落空只是空欢喜；朱雀是占官的用爻，宜旺不宜衰，见空亡文书必难成，被刑克文书必遭伤坏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Azure Dragon moves the undertaking is easily accomplished; when the Vermilion Bird is void the documents are hard to complete. The Azure Dragon is the spirit of joy of auspicious celebration, blessing and virtue, of three kinds, that of the year, of the Month and of the day; moving, it surely adds rank and emolument, and standing together with the Heavenly Horse there is the joy of rising and gain; it must not fall void, for then it is only empty joy. The Vermilion Bird is the Subject line in divining for office and should be strong and not weak; seen Void the documents are sure to be hard to complete, and punished or controlled they are sure to be spoiled.
