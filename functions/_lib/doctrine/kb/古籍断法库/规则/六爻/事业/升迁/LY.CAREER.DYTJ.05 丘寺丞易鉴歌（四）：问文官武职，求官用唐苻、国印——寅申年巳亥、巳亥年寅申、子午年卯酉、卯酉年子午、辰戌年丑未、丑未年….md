# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（四）：问文官武职，求官用唐苻、国印——寅申年巳亥、巳亥年寅申、子午年卯酉、卯酉年子午、辰戌年丑未、丑未年辰戌；鬼临符印最佳，宜静而动必左迁（卜筮大全作升迁）、宜动而静多掩抑

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 不论文官武职，求官要看唐符、国印：寅、申年看巳、亥，巳、亥年看寅、申，子、午年看卯、酉，卯、酉年看子、午，辰、戌年看丑、未，丑、未年看辰、戌。注文说：寅、申年遇到巳、亥爻发动、旺相，是吉；巳、亥年遇到寅、申爻发动、旺相，是吉。官鬼落在符、印上最好，可以做朝廷的辅佐；「宜静而动必左迁，宜动而静多掩抑」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether it is a civil or a military post, in seeking office look to Tangfu and the State Seal: in the years Yin and Shen look to Si and Hai, in Si and Hai years to Yin and Shen, in Zi and Wu years to Mao and You, in Mao and You years to Zi and Wu, in Chen and Xu years to Chou and Wei, in Chou and Wei years to Chen and Xu. The notes say: in the years Yin and Shen, if the Si or Hai line moves and is strong, it is auspicious; in the years Si and Hai, if the Yin or Shen line moves and is strong, it is auspicious. Pressure standing on the tally and the seal is best, one who will serve as a support at court; 'what should be still but moves will surely be demoted; what should move but stays still is mostly held down'.

## 《断易天机·占仕宦第十一》原文

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 鬼临苻印最为佳， | 官鬼落在唐符、国印上，最好， | Pressure standing on the Tangfu or the State Seal is the best, |

^q6

> [!warning] 打架
> 卜筮大全本作「宜静而动必升迁」，断易天机本作「宜静而动必左迁」：「升迁」是升官，「左迁」是降职，意思正相反。两本都没有交代「宜静」「宜动」指哪一爻，所以无法靠上下文裁定。另外，卜筮大全的旧注把巳、亥读成唐符、国印本身，断易天机的注则说巳、亥爻要发动、旺相才吉，两处的读法也不完全一样。本库各照原书录，不裁定。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The Bushi Daquan text reads 'what should be still but moves will surely rise in post', the Duanyi Tianji text 'will surely be demoted': the first is promotion and the second demotion, exactly opposite. Neither book says which line 'should be still' or 'should move', so the context cannot decide it. In addition the note in the Bushi Daquan reads Si and Hai as the Tangfu and the State Seal themselves, while the note here says that the Si or Hai line must move and be strong to be auspicious, so the two do not read it quite alike. Each is recorded as its book gives it and neither is adjudicated.
