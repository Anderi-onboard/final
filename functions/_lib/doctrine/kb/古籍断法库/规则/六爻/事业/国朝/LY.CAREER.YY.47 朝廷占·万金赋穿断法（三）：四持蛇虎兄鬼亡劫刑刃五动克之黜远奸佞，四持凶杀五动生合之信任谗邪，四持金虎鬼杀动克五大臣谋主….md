# 朝廷占·万金赋穿断法（三）：四持蛇虎兄鬼亡劫刑刃五动克之黜远奸佞，四持凶杀五动生合之信任谗邪，四持金虎鬼杀动克五大臣谋主（得日辰动爻合制庶无大咎）；四带玄咸生合五阿谀取容；五持父动克四忠言逆耳；四加福德将星被五刑害克破良将冤死

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《万金赋》穿断法，看奸佞和忠良：四爻持螣蛇、白虎、兄弟、官鬼、亡神、劫杀、刑、刃，而五爻发动克它的，君主罢黜、远离奸佞；四爻持凶煞，而五爻发动生合它的，君主信任谗佞邪恶之人；四爻持金爻、白虎、官鬼、凶煞，发动克五爻的，大臣谋害君主，得日辰、动爻合住或制住它，大概没有大的祸害；四爻带玄武、咸池，生合五爻的，阿谀逢迎取悦君主；五爻持父母爻发动克四爻的，忠言逆耳；四爻加福德、将星，被五爻刑、害、克、破的，良将冤死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Chuanduan method, reading traitors and the loyal. If the fourth line holds the Coiling Snake, the Tiger, a Peer line, Pressure, the Lost Spirit, Robbery, punishment and the blade, and the fifth moves to control it, the sovereign dismisses and keeps away the flatterers; if the fourth holds baleful spirits and the fifth moves to generate or combine with it, the sovereign trusts slanderers and the wicked; if the fourth holds Metal, the Tiger, Pressure and baleful spirits and moves to control the fifth, the great minister plots against the sovereign, and if the Day or a moving line combines with or restrains it, perhaps there is no great harm; if the fourth carries the Dark Tortoise and Xianchi and generates or combines with the fifth, it is flattery to win favour; if the fifth holds Resource and moves to control the fourth, loyal words grate on the ear; if the fourth adds Output and the General star and is punished, harmed, controlled or broken by the fifth, a good general dies wronged.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「福德」是福德，即子孙（见术语表）；「将星」是主将帅的神煞。「玄咸」是玄武和咸池的省称。第三、四句「四持金虎鬼杀，动克五者，大臣谋主也。得日辰动爻合制之，庶无大咎」是连着的，所以放在一条里。
>
> *Note:* 'Fude' is the fortune virtue, that is Output (see the glossary); the 'General star' is the spirit-star of commanders. 'Xuan Xian' is an abbreviation for the Dark Tortoise and Xianchi. The third and fourth sentences, 'the great minister plots against the sovereign' and 'if the Day or a moving line combines with or restrains it, perhaps there is no great harm', run on from each other and are therefore placed in one entry.
