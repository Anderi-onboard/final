# 国朝·游魂遇空，虞舜南巡不返（旧注：游魂卦主国君迁都游荡巡狩；再加凶杀克世，或世动入死墓空绝，则如虞舜南巡崩于苍梧之野）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国君自占得游魂卦，是迁都、四处巡游的象，像周穆王巡游天下；如果再带凶煞克世，或世爻发动、变入死、墓、空、绝，就像虞舜南巡，死在苍梧的野外，一去不回。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the ruler casts for himself, a Wandering Soul hexagram is the image of moving the capital and touring the realm, like King Mu of Zhou who toured the whole land; if a baleful spirit is added that controls the Self, or the Self moves into death, tomb, Void or extinction, it is like Shun of Yu, who went south on a tour and died in the wilds of Cangwu, never to return.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1476 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦遇游魂不宜迁都巡狩，若加凶煞克世，或世爻动化墓绝，如虞舜南巡崩于苍梧之野。 | 卦遇到游魂，不适合迁都、巡视；如果再加凶煞来克世，或者世爻发动变入墓、绝，就像虞舜南巡，死在苍梧的原野上。 | If the hexagram is a Wandering Soul hexagram it is not fit to move the capital or go on a tour; if a baleful spirit is added that controls the Self, or the Self line moves and turns into tomb or extinction, it is like Shun of Yu who went south on a tour and died in the wilds of Cangwu. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注把游魂卦读作「迁都游荡巡狩之象」，只是一个象；卜筮正宗读作「不宜迁都巡狩」，是一条忌讳。两处说法的轻重不完全一样。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary reads a Wandering Soul hexagram as 'the image of moving the capital, wandering and touring', which is only an image; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong reads it as 'not fit for moving the capital or touring', which is a prohibition. The weight is not quite the same.
