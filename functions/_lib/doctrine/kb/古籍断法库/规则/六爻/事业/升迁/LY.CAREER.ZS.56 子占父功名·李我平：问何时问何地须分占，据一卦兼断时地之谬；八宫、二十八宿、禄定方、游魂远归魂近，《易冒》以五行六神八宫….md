# 子占父功名·李我平：问何时问何地须分占，据一卦兼断时地之谬；八宫、二十八宿、禄定方、游魂远归魂近，《易冒》以五行六神八宫为经诸星为纬，可说不可行

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占升迁，问什么时候和问什么地方，必须分开占；凭一个卦兼着断，假如官星是申金，必定七月升迁，按分野申是西方晋地，难道七月升迁的缺就一定在西方？这是古法的荒谬。又有按八宫来定的（如离南坎北），有用二十八宿定分野的，有用禄来定方位的，有说游魂卦远、归魂卦近的，都靠不住。《易冒》以五行、六神、八宫为经，众星为纬，可以讲但不能行：五行、八宫、二十八宿还算近理，如果用六神再加星煞，丁日占得恒卦，酉金官星持世，必掌兵权；六神得螣蛇，螣蛇是奔走差遣的吏；丁日以酉为文昌，文昌是翰林院；按这个方法断，就成了又掌兵权、又在翰林、又听差遣的人了，岂不是胡说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about promotion, when it comes and where it is must be divined separately; judging both from one hexagram, suppose the star of office is Shen Metal, one would be promoted in the seventh month, and by the division of regions Shen is the land of Jin in the west, but does a promotion in the seventh month have to be a post in the west? This is the absurdity of the old method. Then there is fixing it by the eight palaces (such as Li south and Kan north), or by the twenty-eight lunar mansions, or by the Emolument, or saying a Wandering Soul hexagram is far and a Returning Soul hexagram near, none of which can be relied on. The Yi Mao takes the five elements, the six spirits and the eight palaces as the warp and the stars as the weft, which can be talked about but cannot be practised: the five elements, the eight palaces and the twenty-eight mansions are rather more reasonable, but if the six spirits are joined with the stars and baleful spirits, then on a Ding day with Heng and the You Metal star of office holding the Self he must hold military power; the spirit is the Coiling Snake, an official who runs on commissions; on a Ding day You is the Wenchang star, which is the Hanlin Academy; by this method he would be a man who holds military power, sits in the Hanlin and awaits commissions all at once, and is that not idle talk?

> [!note] 按
> 「分野」是把天上的星宿对应地上的区域；「文昌」是一颗神煞星，主文才。这里的「禄」指神煞里的禄。书里没有解释，这里按通行的意思注。
>
> *Note:* 'Fenye' is matching the lunar mansions in the sky to regions of the earth, and 'Wenchang' is a spirit-star of literary talent; the 'Emolument' here is the Emolument spirit-star. The book does not explain them, and they are glossed here in the usual sense.
