# 国朝·身值动官，唐太宗禁庭蹀血（旧注：世身持官带杀旺动，必至杀克兄弟、兄弟不相容，如唐太宗伏兵玄武门射死建成元吉）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻或卦身持着官鬼，带着凶煞、旺相发动，必定发展到杀害兄弟，是兄弟不能相容的征兆，就像唐太宗在玄武门埋伏士兵，射死建成、元吉，血流满了宫中。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self or the Body holds Pressure with a baleful spirit and is thriving and moving, it ends in killing brothers; it is a sign that brothers cannot tolerate each other, like Taizong of Tang, who set troops in ambush at the Xuanwu Gate and shot Jiancheng and Yuanji dead, so that blood flowed in the palace court.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5870 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身值动官，唐太宗禁庭蹀血； | 卦身遇到发动的官，就像唐太宗在禁宫的庭院里血流遍地； | If the Body meets a moving Pressure line, it is like Taizong of Tang with blood trampled in the palace court; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5871 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世身持官，带杀旺动，必至杀克兄弟，乃兄弟必不相容之兆。 | 世爻或卦身持着官，带着凶煞、旺相发动，必定发展到杀害兄弟，这是兄弟必定不能相容的征兆。 | If the Self or the Body holds Pressure, carries a baleful spirit and is thriving and moving, it surely comes to the killing of brothers, a sign that brothers cannot tolerate each other. |
> | 如唐太宗，伏兵玄武门，将建成，元吉射死，血流禁庭，而被军马蹀践也。 | 就像唐太宗，在玄武门埋伏士兵，把建成、元吉射死，血流满了禁宫的庭院，被军马践踏。 | It is like Taizong of Tang, who set troops in ambush at the Xuanwu Gate, shot Jiancheng and Yuanji dead, and the blood flowed in the palace court and was trampled by the soldiers' horses. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1496 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身世持官带杀旺动，必至杀克兄弟，如唐太宗伏兵玄武门，射死建臣元吉，血流禁庭马蹀践也。 | 世爻、卦身持着官，带着凶煞、旺相发动，必定发展到杀害兄弟，就像唐太宗在玄武门埋伏士兵，射死建成、元吉，血流满了宫廷、被马践踏。 | If the Self and the Body hold Pressure, carry a baleful spirit and are thriving and moving, it surely comes to the killing of brothers, like Taizong of Tang who set troops in ambush at the Xuanwu Gate and shot Jiancheng and Yuanji dead, so that blood flowed in the palace court and was trampled by the horses. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗此处写「射死建臣元吉」，「建臣」应是旧注的「建成」，译文按旧注读。
>
> *Note:* The Bu Shi Zheng Zong writes 'shot dead Jianchen Yuanji' here; 'Jianchen' is read as the 'Jiancheng' of the old commentary, and the translation follows the old commentary.
