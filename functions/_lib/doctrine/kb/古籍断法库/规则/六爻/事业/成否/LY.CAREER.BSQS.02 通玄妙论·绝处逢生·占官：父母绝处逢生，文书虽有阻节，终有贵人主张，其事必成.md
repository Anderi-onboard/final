# 通玄妙论·绝处逢生·占官：父母绝处逢生，文书虽有阻节，终有贵人主张，其事必成

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求官时遇到父母爻绝处逢生（它落在日辰的绝地，又得动爻来生）：文书上虽然有阻滞卡节，终究有贵人替你做主，事情必成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for office, if the Resource line meets life in the place of extinction (it falls where the Day extinguishes it but a moving line gives it life): though the documents meet delays and hold-ups, a noble will in the end take charge and the matter will surely succeed.

## 《卜筮全书·通玄妙论·二、绝处逢生》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2806 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 2: life from the place of extinction", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且如申日占卦，遇用爻属木，木见申则绝，木爻无用矣。 | 比如申日占卦，用爻属木，木到申上就是绝地，这个木爻就没有用了。 | Suppose a divination on a Shen day in which the Subject line belongs to Wood: Wood meets extinction at Shen, and the Wood line is of no use. |
> | 若得水爻发动来相生，木爻仍复有用。 | 如果有水爻发动来生它，木爻又有了用处。 | If a Water line moves to give it life, the Wood line is useful again. |
> | 譬人当困穷之际，得遇贵人扶持，必有寒谷回春之象，此乃是绝处逢生也。 | 就像人在穷困的时候，遇到贵人扶持，一定有寒谷回春的景象，这就叫绝处逢生。 | It is like a person in hardship who meets a noble and is helped: surely there is the look of spring returning to a cold valley; this is what is meant by life from the place of extinction. |

^q1

## 《卜筮全书·通玄妙论·二、绝处逢生》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2810 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 2: life from the place of extinction", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占官： | 占求官： | In divining for office: |
> | 遇父母绝处逢生， | 遇到父母爻绝处逢生， | if the Resource line finds life in extinction, |
> | 文书虽有阻节， | 文书上虽然有阻滞卡节， | though the documents meet delays and hold-ups, |
> | 终有贵人主张， | 终究有贵人替你做主， | a noble will in the end take charge, |
> | 其事必成。 | 这件事一定成。 | the matter will surely succeed. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一段后面接着讲占讼、出行（那两件放在官非、出行域）。「绝处逢生」的做法见第一个引文：用爻落在绝地，有动爻来生才算。
>
> *Note:* The passage goes on to lawsuits and travel, which belong to the lawsuit and travel domains. How "life in extinction" works is in the first quotation: the Subject line falls in a place of extinction and a moving line must give it life.
