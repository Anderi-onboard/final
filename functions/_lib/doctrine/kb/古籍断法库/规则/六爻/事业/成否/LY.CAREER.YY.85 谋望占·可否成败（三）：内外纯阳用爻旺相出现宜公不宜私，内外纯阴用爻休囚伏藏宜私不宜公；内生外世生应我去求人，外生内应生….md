# 谋望占·可否成败（三）：内外纯阳用爻旺相出现宜公不宜私，内外纯阴用爻休囚伏藏宜私不宜公；内生外世生应我去求人，外生内应生世人来就我；主卦冲变卦合、动克世变生世先难后易，主卦合变卦冲、动合世变刑世先易后难

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谋事的几种格局：内外卦纯阳、用爻旺相出现的，适宜公事，不适宜私事；内外卦纯阴、用爻休囚伏藏的，适宜私事，不适宜公事。内卦生外卦、世爻生应爻，是我去求人；外卦生内卦、应爻生世爻，是人来就我。本卦冲而变卦合、发动克世爻而变出来生世爻的，先难后易；本卦合而变卦冲、发动合世爻而变出来刑世爻的，先易后难。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Some patterns in a scheme. If the inner and outer trigrams are pure yang and the Subject line is thriving and appears, it is fit for a public matter and not a private one; if the inner and outer are pure yin and the Subject line is resting or trapped and hidden, it is fit for a private matter and not a public one. If the inner generates the outer and the Self generates the Other, I go to ask others; if the outer generates the inner and the Other generates the Self, others come to me. If the original hexagram clashes and the changed hexagram combines, or a moving line controls the Self and changes into one that generates the Self, it is hard at first and easy after; if the original hexagram combines and the changed hexagram clashes, or a moving line combines with the Self and changes into one that punishes the Self, it is easy at first and hard after.

> [!note] 按
> 「宜公不宜私」「宜私不宜公」是看这件事该走公家的路还是私人的路：纯阳、用爻出现，是公开、光明的事；纯阴、用爻伏藏，是私下、隐秘的事。这一句书里没有解释理由，译文只照字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Fit for the public and not the private' and 'fit for the private and not the public' ask whether the matter should take an official road or a private one: pure yang with the Subject line appearing is an open and bright matter, and pure yin with the Subject line hidden is a private and hidden one. The book gives no reason for this sentence, and the translation follows only the letter.
