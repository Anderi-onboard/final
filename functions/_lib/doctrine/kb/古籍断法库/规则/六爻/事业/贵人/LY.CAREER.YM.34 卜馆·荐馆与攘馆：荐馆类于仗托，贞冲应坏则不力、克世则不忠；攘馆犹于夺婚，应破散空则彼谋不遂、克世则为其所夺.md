# 卜馆·荐馆与攘馆：荐馆类于仗托，贞冲应坏则不力、克世则不忠；攘馆犹于夺婚，应破散空则彼谋不遂、克世则为其所夺

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占荐馆（托人推荐馆席），类似仗托（托人办事）：本卦六冲、应坏，他就不出力；应克世，他就不忠。占攘馆（有人谋夺馆席），如同占夺婚：应破、散、空，他的图谋不成；应克世，就被他夺走。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about recommending someone to a post it is like entrusting a matter to someone: if the original hexagram is a six-clash and the Other is damaged, he does not exert himself; if the Other controls the Self, he is not loyal. In divining about snatching a post it is like snatching a marriage: if the Other is broken, scattered or Void, his scheme does not succeed; if the Other controls the Self, one is robbed of it by him.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「荐馆」是托人把我（或别人）荐进某个馆，「仗托」是仗着别人去办（见 EL.134 起的仗托人力）。「贞冲」是本卦（贞）是六冲卦。「攘馆」按下面「彼谋不遂」读，是有人图谋夺我的馆；书里只用「犹于夺婚」一句交代，夺婚的断法见婚姻域。「克世而为其所夺」是应爻克世，我反而被夺了去。
>
> *Note:* 'Jianguan' is getting someone to recommend me (or another) into a post, and 'zhangtuo' is relying on another to see to it (see the entrustment of human help from EL.134). 'Zhenchong' is the original hexagram (zhen) being a six-clash hexagram. 'Rangguan', read by 'his scheme does not succeed' below, is someone plotting to snatch my post; the book gives it only the sentence 'as with snatching a marriage', and the method of snatching a marriage is in the Marriage domain. 'Controlling the Self and being robbed by him' is the Other line controlling the Self, so that I am the one robbed.
