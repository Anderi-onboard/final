# 八宫章·占功名秘法（在任与出仕）：官鬼持世，已仕未仕、文武皆望升即升候选即选，纳粟开垦问缺立功各得所求；子孙持世目前失望下次再占；在任占升迁官鬼持世一定高迁，子孙持世必然而误；未仕问终能成名官鬼持世食禄有期；已有荫资批文问终能出仕官鬼持世一定飞腾

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占功名的，不论文武，是已经做了官还是没有做官，只要官鬼持世，盼升就升，候选就选上，入场一定中，单试一定取；被误了事的，官职还在；被除名的，前程可以恢复；在乡里闲居很久的，一定蒙起用；考职考艺的，一定取上；纳粟的，名字登进仕籍；开垦的，加等就升；问缺的，这个缺一定得到；立功的，一定建奇勋。只是不宜子孙持世，一切功名，目前就要失望，等到有机会，下次再占。又比如在任的人，没事的时候向神祷告说：「我到这个任上，将来升不升？」如果得官鬼持世，一定高升；子孙持世，必定耽误。又比如还没有出仕的读书人，占我终究能不能成名，官鬼持世，吃俸禄有日期；子孙持世，终究没有指望。又比如有祖荫，或已经援例，或已经立功，凡是已经有部文批文委牌等事的，问我将来终究能不能出仕，官鬼持世，一定飞黄腾达；子孙持世，安心在家待着。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining for office, whether civil or military, whether already in service or not, if Pressure holds the Self, one who hopes for promotion is promoted, one awaiting selection is selected, one who enters the examination hall surely passes and a single test surely takes him; one who was set back keeps his post; one whose name was struck off can recover his prospects; one long idle in the country is surely called back; one sitting for an appointment test or a skills test is surely admitted; one who bought office by grain has his name entered on the roll; one who opened wasteland is promoted a grade; one asking about a vacancy surely gets it; one who has done service surely achieves a great feat. Only Output holding the Self is unfit: for every office he is disappointed for the present, and should divine again next time when a chance comes. Again, for a man in office who in an idle time prays to the spirits, "I have come to this post; will I rise in the future?", Pressure holding the Self means surely a high promotion, and Output holding the Self surely means delay. Again, for a scholar not yet in service who asks whether he will make a name in the end, Pressure holding the Self means he will draw a salary on a set date, and Output holding the Self means no hope in the end. Again, for one who has an ancestral privilege, or has already bought office by precedent, or has done service, whoever already has a ministry document, an approval or a warrant and asks whether he will take office in the end, Pressure holding the Self means he will soar, and Output holding the Self means he should stay quietly at home.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段和考试域里「占功名秘法（士子）」同出一处：那边取了读书人先占今科、再占下科、再占终身的一节；这里取的是在任、出仕、荫监和问缺的一节。
