# 出师征伐：占出战以内卦为我外卦为他，内旺外衰更遇吉神发动东征西讨威镇华夷；奇门亦按八节旺衰——立春后生门旺、春分后伤门当道、立夏后杜门虽旺不祥取景门、夏至后景门大吉、立秋后死门不宜取惊门、秋分后惊门开门、立冬后开门旺休门相、冬至后休生二门

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占出战，以内卦为我，外卦为他。如果得到内卦旺相、外卦衰弱，又遇吉神发动，东征西讨，威镇华夏和夷狄。奇门也有旺衰，也都按八个节气：立春后，生门太旺；春分后，伤门当道，只嫌大象（卦象）少加，反而取木能生火，用景门；立夏后，杜门虽旺也不吉祥，所以取火相的景门，用它得胜；夏至后，正值景门大吉；立秋后，虽然现在死门当令，不宜前进，应当取金相的惊门；秋分后，惊门当令，进兵没有不亨通的，水相的开门也吉；立冬后，开门正旺，休门作相门，两个门都好；冬至后，休门旺、生门相，两个门都能导向吉祥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for going out to fight, take the inner trigram as me and the outer as them. If the inner is thriving and the outer weak, and an auspicious spirit also moves, campaigning east and west one overawes the Chinese and the barbarians. Qimen too has its strength and weakness, also by the eight solar terms: after the Start of Spring the Life gate is very strong; after the Spring Equinox the Harm gate holds the road, but as the great image adds little, one instead takes the fact that Wood can generate Fire and uses the Scene gate; after the Start of Summer the Block gate, though strong, is not auspicious, so one takes the Scene gate that Fire assists and wins by it; after the Summer Solstice the Scene gate is exactly in season and greatly auspicious; after the Start of Autumn, though the Death gate is now in season, it is not fit to advance, and one should take the Fright gate that Metal assists; after the Autumn Equinox the Fright gate commands and none who advance fail to prosper, and the Open gate that Water assists is also auspicious; after the Start of Winter the Open gate is exactly thriving, and the Rest gate acts as the assisting gate, both gates being good; after the Winter Solstice the Rest gate thrives and the Life gate assists, and both gates can lead to good fortune.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条后半是奇门八门随节气的旺相，和六爻的断法没有直接关系，留在这里是因为书里把它排在「内卦为我、外卦为他」之后一起讲。第三句「只嫌大象少加」文字不顺，译文照字面。「华夷」是中原和外族。
>
> *Note:* The second half of this entry is the strength of the Eight Gates of Qimen through the solar terms, which has no direct bearing on the judgments of the six lines, and it is kept here because the book sets it after 'the inner is me, the outer is them' in the same discussion. 'Zhi xian daxiang shao jia' in the third sentence does not run smoothly, and the translation follows the letter. 'Hua yi' is the Chinese and the foreign peoples.
