# 营谋·筮嘱托：随所用神（或官、或应、或六亲）皆忌空（空则不力）、忌破（破则无功）、忌六冲克世（疑阻生衅）、随墓助鬼（不利公廷）；旺衰分力之大小、合冲定情之向背、化进勇于事化退倦于行；五行六神断情性不主吉凶

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占嘱托（托人办事），随所取的用神：或者用官，或者用应，或者用六亲，都忌空，空就不出力；都忌破，破就没有功效；都忌六冲克世，那就生疑、受阻、生出嫌隙；随墓、助鬼，就不利于公堂。旺衰来分他出力的大小，合冲来定他情意的向背，化进就勇于办事，化退就倦于行动；五行、六神用来断他的性情，但不要拿它来主断吉凶。金坚直，木从容，水曲折，火燥烈，土诚信；龙和，虎勇，雀再三，武婉转，勾端实，蛇缠绵——这是五行、六神按生旺说的，空、破就反过来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about entrusting a matter to another, one follows the Subject taken: whether Pressure, or the Other, or one of the six relations, all fear Void, and Void means he does not exert himself; all fear broken, and broken means no achievement; all fear a six-clash controlling the Self, which breeds suspicion, obstruction and quarrel; following into the tomb and helping Pressure is unfavourable at the public court. Strength and weakness tell how much force he has; combination and clash fix whether his feeling is toward or against me; changing into an advance means he is bold in the matter, changing into a retreat means he tires of acting; the five phases and the six spirits are used to judge his temper, but one does not make them the master of good and ill. Metal is firm and straight, Wood is easygoing, Water is roundabout, Fire is fierce and hasty, Earth is sincere and faithful; the Azure Dragon is mild, the White Tiger bold, the Vermilion Bird says it again and again, the Dark Tortoise is indirect, Hook Earth is upright and solid, the Coiling Snake clings; this is said of the phases and the six spirits when they are strong, and Void or broken turns it round.

> [!note] 按
> 「嘱托」是托人办事。「随所用神」是按所托的人取用神：所托的是官，就用官爻；所托的是一般的人，就用应；所托的是亲人，就用相应的六亲。「忌空」「忌破」「忌六冲克世」是对这位受托的人的三个忌；「随墓助鬼，不利公廷」是涉及官司公事时的忌。「化进」「化退」是用神化进、化退（进神、退神，见术语表）。「雀再三」是朱雀反复叮咛、话多；「武婉转」是玄武说话绕弯子；「勾端实」是勾陈端正厚实；「蛇缠绵」是螣蛇缠缠绵绵；「龙和」是青龙温和，「虎勇」是白虎勇悍。书里自注：这些性情是按五行、六神生旺来说的，空、破时反过来。「然勿主吉凶也」是五行六神只断性情，吉凶不由它定，由生克旺衰定。与易林补遗仗托人力（EL.134–137）对着看，那边也是应爻为主、嫌空冲破。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhutuo' is entrusting a matter to another. 'Following the Subject taken' is taking the Subject according to whom the matter is entrusted to: if it is an official, the Pressure line is used; if an ordinary person, the Other; if a relative, the corresponding one of the six relations. 'Fears Void', 'fears broken' and 'fears a six-clash controlling the Self' are the three taboos concerning the person who is entrusted; 'following into the tomb and helping Pressure is unfavourable at the public court' is the taboo when a lawsuit or public business is involved. 'Changing into an advance' and 'changing into a retreat' are the Subject changing into an Advancing or Retreating spirit (see the glossary). 'The Vermilion Bird says it again and again' is the Vermilion Bird urging repeatedly and saying much; 'the Dark Tortoise is indirect' is the Dark Tortoise speaking in roundabout ways; 'Hook Earth is upright and solid' is Hook Earth being straight and substantial; 'the Coiling Snake clings' is the Coiling Snake clinging on; 'the Azure Dragon is mild' is the Azure Dragon gentle, and 'the White Tiger is bold' is the White Tiger brave and fierce. The book's own gloss: these natures are said of the phases and six spirits when strong, and when Void or broken it is the other way round. 'But do not make them master good and ill' is that the phases and the six spirits judge only temper, and good and ill are not fixed by them but by generation, control, strength and weakness. Read against the Yilin Buyi on relying on others' help (EL.134-137), where the Other line is also the master and Void, clash and broken are feared.
