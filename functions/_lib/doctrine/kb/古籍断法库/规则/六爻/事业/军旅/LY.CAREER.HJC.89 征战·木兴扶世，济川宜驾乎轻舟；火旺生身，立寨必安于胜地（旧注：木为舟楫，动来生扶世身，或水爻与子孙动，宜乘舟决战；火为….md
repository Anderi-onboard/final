# 征战·木兴扶世，济川宜驾乎轻舟；火旺生身，立寨必安于胜地（旧注：木为舟楫，动来生扶世身，或水爻与子孙动，宜乘舟决战；火为营寨，旺动生扶世身，结寨必得形胜之地）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木是舟船：木爻发动来生扶世爻，或者水爻和子孙发动，适合乘船决战取胜；火是营寨：火爻旺相发动来生扶世身，扎寨必定得到地形优越的好地方。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wood is boats: if a Wood line moves to generate or support the Self, or a Water line moves together with Output, it is fit to fight a decisive battle in boats and win; Fire is the camp: if a Fire line is thriving and moves to generate or support the Self, the stockade will surely stand in a place of natural advantage.

> [!warning] 打架
> 舟是木还是水，两本不一样：卜筮大全的经文是「木兴扶世」，旧注先说「木为舟楫」、又说「水爻与子孙动」；卜筮正宗正文写成「水兴扶世」，注里只说水。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two books differ on whether the boat is Wood or Water: the verse in the Bu Shi Da Quan reads 'Wood thriving supports the Self', and the old commentary says first 'Wood is boats and oars' and then 'a Water line and Output move'; the main text of the Bu Shi Zheng Zong reads 'Water thriving supports the Self', and its note speaks only of Water.

> [!note] 按
> 旧注在「父母兴持」一联末尾说「以上数节，出《天玄赋》，不细解」，所以这一组几联的注都很简短。
>
> *Note:* At the end of the couplet 'Resource holds the Self' the old commentary says 'the sections above come from the Tianxuan Fu and are not explained in detail', so the notes on this group of couplets are brief.
