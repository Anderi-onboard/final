# 求馆开设：以世应为主、财福为凭；觅馆招贤怕世空，应空岂得遇东翁，父空书馆终非美，才陷修仪甚不丰，世应不空须有望，变成冲散定无终；世为自己、应为东家、父为书馆、子为书生、财为束修、鬼为荐馆之人、兄乃同胞之士，逢空各有所缺

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求馆（谋求教书的馆席），以世爻、应爻为主，以妻财、子孙为凭据。求馆、受聘最怕世爻落空，应爻落空就遇不到东家；父爻落空，这个馆终究不是好馆；财爻陷入空亡，束修（馆金）很不丰厚。世爻、应爻都不落空，就必定有希望；如果变成冲散，就一定做不长久。六亲各取一象：世是自己，应是东家，父是书馆，子是书生，财是束修，鬼是荐馆的人，兄是同胞之士。落空各有所缺：世空遇不到尊重、听从自己的人，应空没有东家接纳，父空难遇好馆，财空束修菲薄，子空学生不多，鬼空无人举荐，兄空无人来争，身空住处不安；其余的空还可以稍微放过，只要世应不空，终究有希望。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking a school post, take the Self and the Other as the chief and Wealth and Output as the ground. To seek a post and be engaged, the Self falling Void is the thing most to be feared, and if the Other is Void one cannot meet the host; if Resource is Void the school is in the end no fine one; if Wealth is sunk into Void the fee is very meagre. If neither the Self nor the Other is Void there must be hope; if it changes into a scattering clash there will surely be no lasting end. The six kin each take one image: the Self is oneself, the Other the host, Resource the school, Output the pupils, Wealth the fee, Pressure the one who recommends a post, Peer the men of one's own kind. A Void in each leaves something lacking: if the Self is Void one meets no one who honours and follows one, if the Other is Void there is no host to take one in, if Resource is Void it is hard to meet a good post, if Wealth is Void the fee is slight, if Output is Void the pupils are few, if Pressure is Void no one recommends one, if Peer is Void no one competes, and if the Body is Void the place one lives is unsettled; the other Voids may be set aside somewhat, and if the Self and the Other are not Void there will in the end be hope.

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡图书馆，世为自己，应为东家，父为书馆，子为书生，财为束修，鬼为荐馆之人，兄乃同胞之士，世空不遇尊从，应空无东纳。 | 凡是谋求书馆的，世爻代表自己，应爻代表东家，父爻代表书馆，子孙代表学生，妻财代表束修，官鬼代表荐馆的人，兄弟是同胞之士；世爻落空，遇不到尊重、听从自己的人；应爻落空，就没有东家接纳。 | Whenever one plans for a school post, the Self is oneself, the Other is the host, Resource is the school, Output is the pupils, Wealth is the fee, Pressure is the one who recommends one to the post, and Peer is the men of one's own kind; if the Self is Void one meets no one who honours and follows one, and if the Other is Void there is no host to take one in. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「图书馆」是谋求书馆（教书的馆席），不是今天说的图书馆；「东翁」「东家」是聘请塾师的主人；「束修」是学生送给老师的酬金，这里指馆金；「荐馆之人」是介绍你去这个馆的人。诀里「才陷修仪」的「才」疑是「财」，解里「财空修体轻微」的「修体」疑是「修仪」，译文按「财」「修仪」译；「诸空稍可推」的「推」按「放过」译。「兄乃同胞之士」从后面「兄空无人抢夺」看，指来争这个馆的同行。这一条的六亲取象与黄金策求馆（HJC.29 起）、易隐觅馆占（YY.79）是同一套：世自己、应东家、父书馆、子学生、财馆金、鬼荐馆人、兄谋占者。
>
> *Note:* 'Tu shuguan' means to plan for a school post (a tutor's place) and not a library as the words mean today; 'dongweng' and 'dongjia' are the master who engages a tutor, 'shuxiu' is the fee that pupils give a teacher, here the pay of the post, and 'the one who recommends one to a post' is the person who introduces one to it. The 'cai' of 'cai xian xiuyi' in the verse is presumably 'cai', Wealth, and the 'xiuti' of 'cai kong xiuti qingwei' in the explanation is presumably 'xiuyi', the fee; the translation reads them so, and the 'tui' of 'zhu kong shao ke tui' is read as 'set aside'. From 'if Peer is Void no one competes for the post' later on, 'the men of one's own kind' means those in the same line who compete for the post. The kin images here are the same set as in the Huangjin Ce on seeking a post (from HJC.29) and in the Yi Yin divination for seeking a post (YY.79): the Self is oneself, the Other the host, Resource the school, Output the pupils, Wealth the pay, Pressure the one who recommends, Peer the one who schemes to take it.
