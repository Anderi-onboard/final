# 上书·兄弟临身，喜父母化吉而拱合（兄弟持世，日月生之，或世、父母动而化吉，或世化父以生世，皆得喜动龙颜、因文见用）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟临身时，喜欢父母爻变吉并来拱合：兄弟持世，有日月生它，或者世爻、父母爻发动变吉，或者世爻变出父母爻来生世，都能使皇上龙颜大悦，因为奏章被采用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Peer sits on the Body, it is good that the Resource line turns auspicious and holds up and combines: if Peer holds the Self and the Day and Month generate it, or the Self or the Resource line moves and turns auspicious, or the Self turns into Resource to generate the Self, all of these please the dragon countenance, and one is employed because of the document.
