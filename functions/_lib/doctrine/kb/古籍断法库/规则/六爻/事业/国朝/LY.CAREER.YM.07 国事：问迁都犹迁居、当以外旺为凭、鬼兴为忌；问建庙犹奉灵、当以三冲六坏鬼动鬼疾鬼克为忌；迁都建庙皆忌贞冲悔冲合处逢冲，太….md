# 国事：问迁都犹迁居、当以外旺为凭、鬼兴为忌；问建庙犹奉灵、当以三冲六坏鬼动鬼疾鬼克为忌；迁都建庙皆忌贞冲悔冲合处逢冲，太庙不可破散、太灵不可鬼动鬼空鬼散及鬼克世

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问迁都，如同迁居：以外卦旺相为凭据，官鬼兴旺是忌。问建庙，如同供奉神灵：以三冲、六坏、鬼动、鬼疾、鬼克为忌。迁都、建庙，都忌贞冲、悔冲、合处逢冲这几种情形。六爻是太庙所属，所以不可破散；官鬼是太灵（祖先神灵），所以不可鬼动、鬼空、鬼散，以及鬼克世。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For moving the capital, which is like moving house, the outer being thriving is the ground and Pressure flourishing is the taboo; for building a temple, which is like enshrining the spirits, the taboos are the three clashes, the six breakages, and Pressure moving, being sick or controlling. In moving the capital and building a temple, the taboos are the clash of the original hexagram, the clash of the changed hexagram, and a combination meeting a clash. The six lines belong to the great temple, so it must not be broken or scattered; Pressure is the great spirit, so Pressure must not move, be Void or scatter, nor control the Self.

> [!note] 按
> 「贞冲」「悔冲」是本卦（贞）、变卦（悔）是六冲卦；「合处逢冲」是本来相合的地方遇到冲；注里列的这三种就是正文的「三冲」（见术语表）。「六坏」书里这一处没有列出。「奉灵」是供奉神灵，「太庙」是帝王的宗庙，「太灵」是祖先的神灵。这一条与易林补遗朝廷国事里的忌六冲（EL.01）取同一类忌：国是一个整体，冲散就是国破。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhenchong' and 'huichong' are the original hexagram (zhen) and the changed hexagram (hui) being a six-clash hexagram; 'hechu fengchong' is a place that was a combination meeting a clash; these three listed in the note are the 'three clashes' of the main text (see the glossary). The 'six breakages' are not listed here in the book. 'Fengling' is enshrining the spirits, 'taimiao' is the emperor's ancestral temple, and 'tailing' is the spirits of the ancestors. This entry takes the same kind of taboo as the six-clash in the Yilin Buyi chapter on court and state affairs (EL.01): the state is a whole, and a clash that scatters it is the state broken.
