# 户役轻重：月建临官应佥魁首、日辰克鬼当审轻微，官爻值世估高名、福德加身登下榜；官临月建至重之差，鬼值世爻次重之役，鬼逢日月克伤轻徭可必，子孙世或发动其户亦轻

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建临在官爻上，应被派作魁首（最重的役）；日辰克官鬼，应审定为轻微。官爻值世，估定是高名（高等户，役重）；福德加身，登下榜（下等户，役轻）。具体说：官临月建，是最重的差；鬼值世爻，是次重的役；官鬼遇到日月克伤，役轻是可以断定的；子孙持世或者发动，这个户也轻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Month rests on the star of office, one should be assigned as the headman; if the Day controls Pressure, one should judge it light and slight. If the Pressure line holds the Self, one is reckoned of high name; if Output, the fortune virtue, is added to the Body, one ascends the lower roll. In detail: the star of office on the Month is the heaviest commission; Pressure on the Self line is the next heaviest service; if Pressure meets the control and injury of the Day and the Month, lightness can be affirmed; if Output holds the Self or moves, the household too is light.

> [!note] 按
> 「魁首」是户役里最重的一名（如里长、粮长之首），「佥」是佥派、派定。「估高名」「登下榜」按文意是被定在高等户、下等户：官爻值世是高等户（役重），福德加身是下等户（役轻）；「榜」是户等的名册。「轻摇」疑是「轻徭」，徭役轻。这一条只取诀的前四句和解的前四句，后面的变化（子化官、官化子、兄弟动等）在下一条（EL.111）。
>
> *Note:* 'Kuishou', the headman, is the heaviest single place in the household services (such as the head of the tithing or the head of the grain captains), and 'qian' is to assign and to appoint. 'Gu gaoming' and 'deng xiabang' are by the sense being placed among households of high and low grade: the Pressure line holding the Self is a household of high grade (heavy service), and Output added to the Body is a household of low grade (light service); 'bang' is the register of household grades. The 'yao' of 'qing yao' is presumably 'yao', corvée, so 'light corvée'. This entry takes only the first four clauses of the verse and the first four sentences of the explanation; the changes that follow (Output into Pressure, Pressure into Output, Peer moving and so on) are in the next entry (EL.111).
