# 朝廷国事总纲：以世爻为主、应象为佐，六合子旺为吉、六冲杀动为凶；国泰民安全资文武匡襄；欲知宗社安危须审易爻而较量

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占朝廷国事，以世爻为主，应爻为辅；六合、子孙旺相的是吉，六冲、凶煞发动的是凶。国家太平、百姓安宁，靠文臣武将的匡正辅佐；想知道宗庙社稷的安危，须要审察易爻来衡量。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining the affairs of the court and state, take the Self as the chief and the Other image as the aid; a six-combine and a thriving Output are auspicious, a six-clash and a moving baleful spirit are ominous. A state at peace and a people secure depend on the correction and aid of civil and military ministers; to know the safety or danger of the ancestral temple and altars, one must examine the lines of the Yi and weigh them.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 284 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 宗社乃宗庙社稷六朝延治则安，乱则危，审析易理爻辞，而可剖决吉凶也。 | 宗社就是宗庙社稷：国家（历朝）延续得治理好就安稳，混乱就危险；审察剖析易理爻辞，就能判断吉凶。 | 'Zongshe' is the ancestral temple and altars of grain and soil: if the dynasty continues well governed it is secure, and if in disorder it is in danger; by examining and analysing the principles and line texts of the Yi, one can decide good and ill. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 小标是这一章的取法：世爻为主、应爻为辅。第二、三句的「冶」应是「治」（第二条解里写作「而致冶也」），译文按「治」译。「宗社乃宗庙社稷六朝延治则安」的「六朝」文意不顺，疑是「历朝」之误，译文按「历朝」译。「六合子旺」「六冲杀动」里的六合、六冲指卦的六合、六冲（见术语表）。
>
> *Note:* The subtitle gives the method of the chapter: the Self as chief, the Other as aid. In the explanation the 'ye' of 'zhi zhi ye' is presumably 'zhi', to govern, and the translation reads 'govern'. 'Liu chao yan zhi ze an' in the explanation of 'zongshe' does not run smoothly, and 'liu chao' is presumably a miswriting of 'li chao', successive dynasties, and the translation reads it so. 'Six-combine' and 'six-clash' in the subtitle are the hexagram patterns (see the glossary).
