# 出行求事于升迁：出行而筮求事者亦察用爻——求事于升迁则考诸官鬼而毋世空（科第考文书、寻访考用爻、货利考财象，盖专于事而不专于出行）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 于是占出去求事的，也察看用爻：求事于科第，就考察文书；求事于升迁，就考察官鬼，而不要世空；求事于寻访，考察用爻；求事于货利，考察财象；这是专就事来占，而不专就出行来占。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* So in divining about going out to seek something, one also examines the Subject line: for seeking something in the examinations, one examines the document line; for seeking something in promotion, one examines Pressure, and the Self must not be Void; for seeking something by searching and visiting, one examines the Subject line; for seeking something in goods and profit, one examines the Wealth image; this goes by the matter and not by the travelling.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「求事」是出门去求一件事。「文书」是父母爻，「财象」是财爻。这一句里四种事分属四个域：科第归考试，升迁归事业，寻访（找人、找物）归失物或行人，货利归求财；这里整句收，以免拆开，而升迁一条是事业的：出门求升迁，要看官鬼，不要世空，与 YM.42 的「专考官爻」「世空则占不遂」一致。「盖耑于事而不耑于出行也」（「耑」同「专」）是说这类占问要按所求的那件事取用爻，不按出行取。与出行章后面的求事各节对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Qiushi' is going out to seek a matter. 'Wenshu' is the document line (the Resource line), and the 'Wealth image' is the Wealth line. The four matters in this sentence belong to four domains: the examinations to Examinations, promotion to Career, searching and visiting (looking for a person or a thing) to Lost Things or Travellers, and goods and profit to Wealth; the whole sentence is taken here so as not to split it, and the promotion clause is the one that belongs to Career: going out to seek promotion, one looks at Pressure and the Self must not be Void, which agrees with 'look only at the Pressure line' and 'with the Self Void the divination does not come to pass' in YM.42. 'Gai zhuan yu shi er bu zhuan yu chuxing ye' ('zhuan' here is the same as the usual 'zhuan') says that such divinations take the line by the matter sought and not by the travelling. Read against the later sections on seeking matters in the chapter on travelling.
