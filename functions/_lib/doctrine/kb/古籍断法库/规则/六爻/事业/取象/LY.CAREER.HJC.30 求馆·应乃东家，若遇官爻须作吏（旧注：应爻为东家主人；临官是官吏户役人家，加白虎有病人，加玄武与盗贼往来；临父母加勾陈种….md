# 求馆·应乃东家，若遇官爻须作吏（旧注：应爻为东家主人；临官是官吏户役人家，加白虎有病人，加玄武与盗贼往来；临父母加勾陈种田、朱雀读书、白虎宰杀、螣蛇工艺；临子属金僧道作主；临财无官妇人作主、有官富贵、财化财财化子做买卖；临兄弟平常人家、加朱雀赌博）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占馆，应爻代表聘请先生的东家，看应爻临什么来断这家人：临官爻，是官吏、户役人家（加白虎有病人，加玄武与盗贼往来）；临父母，加勾陈是种田人家，加朱雀是读书人家，加白虎是宰杀人家，加螣蛇是工艺人家；临子孙属金，是僧道作主；临财爻在阴宫阴爻又没有官鬼的，是妇人作主，有官鬼就是富贵人家，财化财、财化子是做买卖的人家，被月建日辰动爻制伏的是奴仆作主；临兄弟，是平常人家，加朱雀是赌博人家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a school post, the Other stands for the host family that engages the teacher, and one judges the household from what the Other rests on: on Pressure, an official's or clerk's household (with the White Tiger, a sick person; with the Dark Tortoise, dealings with thieves); on Resource, a farming household with Hook Earth, a scholars' household with the Vermilion Bird, a slaughterers' household with the White Tiger, a craftsmen's household with the Coiling Snake; on Output belonging to Metal, a monk or Daoist is the master; on Wealth in a yin palace and on a yin line with no Pressure, a woman is the master, with Pressure a rich and noble household, with Wealth turning into Wealth or Output a trading household, and if subdued by the Month, the Day or a moving line, a servant is the master; on Peer, an ordinary household, with the Vermilion Bird a gambling household.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8509 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应乃东家，若遇官爻须作吏。 | 应爻是东家，如果遇到官爻，是官吏人家。 | The Other is the host family, and if it meets Pressure, it is an official's household. |

> | 若应爻临官又举贵人，则是富贵人家； | 如果应爻临官又见贵人，就是富贵人家； | if the Other rests on Pressure and a noble also appears, it is a rich and noble household; |
> | 财化财、财化子，做买卖人家。 | 财化财、财化子孙，是做买卖的人家。 | Wealth turning into Wealth or into Output is a trading household. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联是用「应爻临什么」来断东家是什么人家：六亲定行当（官是官吏、父是耕读、子孙属金是僧道、财是买卖或妇人、兄弟是平常人），六神再细分（勾陈种田、朱雀读书、白虎宰杀、螣蛇工艺、玄武盗贼或打猎）。「户役」是承担差役的户；「孝服在身」是家里有丧事。旧注比王洪绪多出的部分：玄武的盗贼、子孙加朱雀玄武的打猎、财有官鬼是富贵人家以及被制伏是奴仆作主、兄弟一类（平常人家、赌博、兄弟姊妹家、朋友邻里家）。王洪绪把旧注的「财爻有官是富贵人家」改成「应临官又举贵人，是富贵人家」，条件不同：旧注是应临财、卦里有官鬼，王洪绪是应临官爻又有贵人。
>
> *Note:* This couplet judges what kind of household the host is by 'what the Other rests on': the six relations fix the trade (Pressure an official or clerk, Resource farming and scholarship, Output belonging to Metal a monk or Daoist, Wealth trade or a woman, Peer an ordinary person) and the six spirits subdivide it (Hook Earth farming, the Vermilion Bird scholarship, the White Tiger slaughter, the Coiling Snake crafts, the Dark Tortoise thieves or hunting). 'A household with corvée duty' is a household bearing service duties; 'in mourning dress' means there is a death in the family. What the old commentary has beyond Wang Hongxu: the Dark Tortoise and thieves, Output with the Vermilion Bird and the Dark Tortoise as hunters, Wealth with Pressure as a rich and noble household, being subdued as a servant being the master, and the Peer sort (ordinary household, gambling, the home of brothers and sisters, the home of friends and neighbours). Wang Hongxu changes the old commentary's 'Wealth with Pressure is a rich and noble household' into 'the Other rests on Pressure and a noble also appears is a rich and noble household', with a different condition: in the old commentary the Other rests on Wealth and there is Pressure in the hexagram, and in Wang Hongxu the Other rests on Pressure and there is a noble.
