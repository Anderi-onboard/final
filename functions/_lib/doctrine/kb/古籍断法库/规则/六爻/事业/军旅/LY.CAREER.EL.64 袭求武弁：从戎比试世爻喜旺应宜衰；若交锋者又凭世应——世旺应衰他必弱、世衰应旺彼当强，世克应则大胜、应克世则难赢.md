# 袭求武弁：从戎比试世爻喜旺应宜衰；若交锋者又凭世应——世旺应衰他必弱、世衰应旺彼当强，世克应则大胜、应克世则难赢

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 从军、比试武艺，世爻喜欢旺相，应爻适宜衰弱。如果是临阵交锋，又凭世、应：世旺应衰，对方一定弱；世衰应旺，对方就强；世克应是大胜，应克世就难以取胜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For joining the army or a military trial, the Self line likes to be thriving and the Other should be weak. For those who cross swords in battle, rely again on the Self and the Other: if the Self is thriving and the Other weak, the other side is surely weak; if the Self is weak and the Other thriving, the other side is strong; if the Self controls the Other it is a great victory, and if the Other controls the Self it is hard to win.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 775–776 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若交锋者，又凭世应，世旺应衰他必弱，世衰应旺彼当强。 | 如果是交锋（临阵交战）的，又凭世、应：世旺应衰，对方一定弱；世衰应旺，对方就强。 | For those who cross swords in battle, rely again on the Self and the Other: if the Self is thriving and the Other weak, the other side is surely weak; if the Self is weak and the Other thriving, the other side is strong. |
> | 世克应则大胜，应克世则难赢。 | 世爻克制应爻，就是大胜；应爻克制世爻，就难以取胜。 | If the Self controls the Other it is a great victory; if the Other controls the Self it is hard to win. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「又凭」在上一行末，「世应」在下一行首，这里连起来读。「交锋」是两军交战。这一条与易隐征战占「世为我帅应为彼将；世旺应衰宜攻、世衰应旺宜守；世克应可战」（YY.54）、黄金策征战「观世应之旺衰，以决两家之胜负」（HJC.86）取同一个关系：世为己方，应为对方。
>
> *Note:* 'You ping' is at the end of the previous line and 'shi ying' at the start of the next, and they are read joined up here. 'Jiaofeng' is two armies meeting in battle. This entry takes the same relation as 'the Self is my commander and the Other the enemy general; with the Self thriving and the Other weak, attack; with the Self weak and the Other thriving, defend; if the Self controls the Other, one can fight' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.54) and 'watch the strength of the Self and the Other to decide the victory of the two houses' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.86): the Self is one's own side and the Other the opposing side.
