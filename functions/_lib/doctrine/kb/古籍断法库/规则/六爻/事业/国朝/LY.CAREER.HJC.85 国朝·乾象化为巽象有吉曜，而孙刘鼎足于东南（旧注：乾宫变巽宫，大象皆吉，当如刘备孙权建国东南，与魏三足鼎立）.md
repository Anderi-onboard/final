# 国朝·乾象化为巽象有吉曜，而孙刘鼎足于东南（旧注：乾宫变巽宫，大象皆吉，当如刘备孙权建国东南，与魏三足鼎立）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 乾宫变巽宫，又有吉曜，就像刘备、孙权在东南建国，和魏国三足鼎立。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Qian-palace hexagram turning into a Xun-palace hexagram, with an auspicious star, is like Liu Bei and Sun Quan founding states in the south-east and standing with Wei like the three legs of a tripod.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5885 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 乾象化为巽象有吉曜，而孙刘鼎足于东南。 | 乾的象变成巽的象，又有吉曜，孙权、刘备就在东南鼎足而立。 | If the Qian image turns into the Xun image and has an auspicious star, then Sun and Liu stand like the legs of a tripod in the south-east. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5886 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 乾宫变巽宫，大象皆吉。 | 乾宫变成巽宫，整个卦象都吉。 | If a Qian-palace hexagram turns into a Xun-palace hexagram, the whole image is auspicious. |
> | 当如刘先主，吴孙权，建国东南，与魏三足鼎立也。 | 就像刘先主、吴国的孙权，在东南建国，和魏国三足鼎立。 | It is like the First Ruler Liu and Sun Quan of Wu, who founded states in the south-east and stood with Wei like the three legs of a tripod. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1506 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 乾变巽宫，大象皆凶，若有吉曜，如刘玄德与吴魏三国鼎足而立也。 | 乾变成巽宫，整个卦象都凶，如果有吉曜，就像刘玄德和吴、魏三国鼎足而立。 | If Qian turns into the Xun palace and the whole image is bad but there is an auspicious star, it is like Liu Xuande standing with Wu and Wei as three kingdoms like the legs of a tripod. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 旧注说「乾宫变巽宫，大象皆吉」，卜筮正宗写成「大象皆凶，若有吉曜」。一个说这个象本身吉，一个说象凶、要有吉曜才成。书里没有判哪一个对。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The old commentary says 'when a Qian-palace hexagram turns into a Xun-palace hexagram, the whole image is auspicious'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong writes 'the whole image is bad, but if there is an auspicious star'. One says the image itself is good, the other that the image is bad and only an auspicious star makes it work. The books do not decide which is right.
