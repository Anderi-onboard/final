# 求馆·逢龙则俊秀聪明，遇虎则刚强顽劣（旧注：子临青龙其徒聪明俊秀，更逢月日生合又临金水必有颖悟非常之子；临白虎多顽劣不驯，发动性野难教训；青龙必有礼貌，白虎必不尽礼）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙临青龙，他的学生必然聪明俊秀，再遇月建、日辰生合、又临金、水爻的，必有颖悟非常的孩子；临白虎，就多是顽劣不驯的，发动则性情粗野，难以教训；遇到青龙，必定有礼貌，遇到白虎，必定不尽礼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output rests on the Azure Dragon, his pupils are surely clever and handsome, and if the Month or the Day also generates or combines with it and it rests on a Metal or Water line, there will surely be an exceptionally quick-witted child; if it rests on the White Tiger, they are mostly unruly and untamed, and if it moves their nature is wild and hard to teach; with the Azure Dragon they are surely courteous, and with the White Tiger they surely fall short in manners.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2779 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙临青龙，逢月建生合，而又临金水，必有颖悟非常之徒； | 子孙临青龙，逢月建生合，又临金、水的，必有颖悟非常的学生； | If Output rests on the Azure Dragon, meets generation or combination by the Month, and rests on Metal or Water, there will surely be an exceptionally quick-witted pupil; |
> | 若临白虎，则多顽劣之徒。 | 如果临白虎，就多是顽劣的学生。 | if it rests on the White Tiger, they are mostly unruly pupils. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联用六神看学生的性情：青龙是喜庆、文雅，所以学生聪明有礼；白虎是刚猛，所以学生顽劣、不守礼。「金、水」在这里是聪明灵敏的五行（金白水智）。
>
> *Note:* This couplet reads the pupils' temper from the six spirits: the Azure Dragon is joy and refinement, so the pupils are clever and polite; the White Tiger is fierceness, so the pupils are unruly and ill-mannered. 'Metal and Water' here are the elements of quickness and cleverness.
