# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（七）：喜神驿马兼官鬼，美削重重见知己；就中苻印两爻推，早晚当随诏书起

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 喜神、驿马兼有官鬼，荐举的文书一封又一封，被知己看重；再就这当中用唐符、国印两爻来推，早晚会随着诏书起用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Joy spirit and the Post Horse together with Pressure, recommending letters follow one another and one is valued by those who know one; then reckon from the two lines Tangfu and State Seal, and sooner or later one will be raised on the strength of an imperial edict.

## 《断易天机·占仕宦第十一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 2585 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 11: Divining official careers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 喜神驿马兼官鬼， | 喜神、驿马兼有官鬼， | the Joy spirit and the Post Horse together with Pressure, |
> | 美削重重见知己（9）。 | 荐举的文书一封又一封，被知己看重。 | recommending letters follow one another, and one is valued by those who know one. |
> | 就中苻印两爻推， | 就在这当中用唐符、国印两爻来推， | from among these, reckon from the two lines Tangfu and State Seal, |
> | 早晚当随诏书起， | 早晚会随着诏书起用。 | Sooner or later one will be raised on the strength of an imperial edict. |

^q1

## 《断易天机》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 2596 行 · Duanyi Tianji commentary (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | （9）美削者，举纸版也。 | （9）美削，就是用纸版举荐。 | (9) 'Meixiao' means recommending by paper tablets. |
> | 重重者，言其多也。 | 重重，是说它多。 | 'Layer upon layer' says that they are many. |
> | 知己者，言见重于人也。 | 知己，是说被人看重。 | 'Those who know one' means being valued by others. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「就中苻印两爻推」在卜筮大全本作「就多符印两爻权」；断易天机本的「就中」读得通，正好印证卜筮大全本的「就多」是「就中」之误。美削，卜筮大全的旧注作「举纸笺」，这里作「举纸版」。
>
> *Note:* The line 'jiu zhong fu yin liang yao tui' reads in the Bushi Daquan text 'jiu duo fu yin liang yao quan'; the 'jiu zhong' (among these) of the Duanyi Tianji text makes sense and confirms that the 'jiu duo' of the Bushi Daquan text is a slip for it. For 'meixiao' the note in the Bushi Daquan says 'recommending by paper letters' and this one 'by paper tablets'.
