# 出师征伐：内城本寨外是他营——内卦与本宫皆为本国又为我之营寨，他宫与外卦咸作他邦更作彼之营寨；内卦兴则吾营坚固，外卦弱则彼寨萧条

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦是本城、本寨，外卦是对方的营。内卦和本宫都是本国，也是我的营寨；他宫和外卦都是他国，也是对方的营寨。内卦兴旺，我的营寨就坚固；外卦衰弱，对方的寨子就萧条。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The inner trigram is my own city and camp, and the outer is their camp. The inner trigram and the home palace are both my own state and also my camp; another palace and the outer trigram are both another state and also their camp. If the inner trigram flourishes my camp is firm; if the outer trigram is weak their camp is desolate.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和易隐征战占「方向」第一句「内卦为我寨，外卦为彼营」（YY.51）相同；这里多了「本宫」「他宫」：本宫是占得的卦所属的宫，他宫是别的宫（与 EL.02 的「亲宫」「本卦」取法相接）。
>
> *Note:* This entry is the same as the first sentence of the Yi Yin 'direction' section, 'the inner trigram is my camp and the outer is theirs' (YY.51); here it adds 'home palace' and 'another palace': the home palace is the palace to which the hexagram cast belongs and another palace is any other (continuing the view of 'the home palace' and 'the original hexagram' in EL.02).
