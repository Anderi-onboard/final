# 占谒贵（二）：看财爻旺相出现忌动、用官则看官爻忌世应坐鬼，又须问见何人以用爻为主；谒见用支出现旺相不动在家、空冲散不在

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> （为求财去谒见）看财爻旺相、出现，忌讳它动；（要见的是官员，用官）就看官爻，忌世、应坐鬼；又必须问清要见的是什么人，以那个人的用爻为主。谒见的时候，用爻的地支出现、旺相、不动，他在家；如果空、冲、散，他不在。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* (For one who calls to seek money) look at the Wealth line thriving and showing, and fear it moving; (if the one to be seen is an official, so that Pressure is used) look at the Pressure line, and fear the Self or the Other sitting on Pressure; and one must also ask whom one is to see, and the Subject line of that man is the master. In calling on him, if the branch of the Subject line appears, is thriving and does not move, he is at home; if it is Void, clashed or scattered, he is not in.

> [!note] 按
> 「看财爻旺相出现，忌动用官」这一句书里没有写明主语，按文意：去谒见是为求财（或有求于对方）时，看财爻旺相、出现、不动；若所求的是官员（用官），就改看官爻，忌世、应坐在鬼上（世或应带官鬼，表示被官鬼压着）。「又须问见何人，看用爻为主」是还要问清要见的是谁（父亲、兄弟、官员……），取那个人对应的爻为用爻。「用支出现旺相不动，在家」是用爻的地支在卦里出现、旺相、不动，对方在家；「空、冲、散，不在」是用爻空、被冲、散，对方不在。这一句的括号里的解释是按文意补的，书里原文没有写「为求财」。
>
> *Note:* The sentence 'look at the Wealth line thriving and showing, and fear it moving; ... Pressure' has no subject written out in the book, and by the sense: when one calls to seek money (or to ask something of the other), look at the Wealth line thriving, showing and not moving; if what is sought is an official (so that Pressure is used), look instead at the Pressure line, and fear the Self or the Other sitting on Pressure (the Self or Other carrying Pressure, showing one is pressed by Pressure). 'One must also ask whom one is to see, and the Subject line is the master' is that one must also be clear whom one is to see (father, brother, official ...), and take the line corresponding to that man as the Subject line. 'The branch of the Subject appears, thriving and not moving: at home' is the Subject line's branch appearing in the hexagram, thriving and not moving, so the other side is at home; 'Void, clashed, scattered: not in' is the Subject line Void, clashed or scattered, so the other side is not in. The explanation in brackets is supplied by the sense, and the original does not write 'to seek money'.
