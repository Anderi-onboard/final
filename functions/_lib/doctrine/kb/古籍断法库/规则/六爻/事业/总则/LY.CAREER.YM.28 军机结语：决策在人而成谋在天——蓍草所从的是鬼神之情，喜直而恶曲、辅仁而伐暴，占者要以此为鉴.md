# 军机结语：决策在人而成谋在天——蓍草所从的是鬼神之情，喜直而恶曲、辅仁而伐暴，占者要以此为鉴

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 常说决策在人，成谋在天。蓍草所顺从的，是鬼神的情意：喜欢直而厌恶曲，辅助仁者而讨伐暴者。占的人要以此为鉴。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* It is often said that decisions rest with men and the success of schemes with Heaven. What the stalks follow is the feeling of the spirits: they like the straight and dislike the crooked, they aid the benevolent and strike down the violent. Let the diviner take this as a mirror.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「常问」按文意读作「常说」。这一句是整章的收尾，不是判断规则：兵占问的是胜负，可『人谋』在人、『成谋』在天，占卦跟着鬼神的情意走，而鬼神的情意是偏向直、仁的一边。所以占出『能胜』并不等于可以逞暴，占出不利也不等于必败；这和易冒其他章末的劝诫是一个口气。
>
> *Note:* 'Changwen' is read by the sense as 'often said'. This line closes the whole chapter and is not a rule of judgment: a military divination asks about victory and defeat, yet the planning is with men and the success of the scheme is with Heaven, so the divination follows the feeling of the spirits, and that feeling leans to the straight and the benevolent. So a divination of 'one can win' does not license violence, and an unfavourable one does not mean certain defeat; this is of one tone with the closing admonitions of other chapters of the Yimao.
