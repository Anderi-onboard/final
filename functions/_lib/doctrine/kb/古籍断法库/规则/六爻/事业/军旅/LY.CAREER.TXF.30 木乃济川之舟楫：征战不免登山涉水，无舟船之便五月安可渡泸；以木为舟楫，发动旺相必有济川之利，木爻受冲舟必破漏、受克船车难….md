# 木乃济川之舟楫：征战不免登山涉水，无舟船之便五月安可渡泸；以木为舟楫，发动旺相必有济川之利，木爻受冲舟必破漏、受克船车难行；卦无水爻恐无水、木爻逢空必无船、木爻带鬼乃贼船

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木是渡河的舟船桨楫。征战免不了登山涉水，没有舟船的方便，五月怎么能渡泸水？占这件事以木为舟楫：发动、旺相，必有渡河之利；木爻受冲，船必破漏；木爻受克，船车难行；卦里没有水爻，恐怕没有水；木爻逢空，必定没有船；木爻带鬼，是贼船。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wood is the boat and oar for crossing the river. Campaigns cannot avoid climbing mountains and wading rivers, and without boats how could one cross the Lu River in the fifth month? In this divination Wood is the boat and oar: moving and strong, it surely brings the advantage of crossing the river; if the Wood line is clashed the boat is sure to be broken and leaking; if controlled, boats and carts are hard to move; with no Water line in the hexagram there may be no water; a Wood line meeting the void means surely no boat; a Wood line carrying Pressure is a bandits' boat.

> [!note] 按
> 「五月渡泸」是诸葛亮五月渡泸水南征的典故，注文没有解释；「舟揖」是「舟楫」之误。
>
> *Note:* 'Crossing the Lu in the fifth month' alludes to Zhuge Liang's southern campaign across the Lu River in the fifth month, which the commentary does not explain; the text writes the character for 'to bow' where the character for 'oar' is meant.
