# 官员荐奖：如卜官凭但愿文书逢旺气、若占到日远寻父母遇生方；又附占文凭到日须看父母之爻候临生临旺之期此凭决到，卦无父须待父爻值日方得凭来，父若衰而又空纵然等候而未至

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占官凭（官员的委任凭据），只要文书爻逢上旺相的气；占凭据哪一天到，要远远去找父母爻遇上生它的时候。另外附带占文凭到的日子，要看父母爻，等它临到生、旺的时候，这份凭据一定到；卦里没有父爻，要等到父爻当值的日子，凭据才会来；父爻如果衰弱又落空，即使等候，凭据也还没有到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine about the official credentials, the document line need only meet a thriving qi; to divine on what day they arrive, look far for Resource meeting the time that generates it. Also appended: for the day the credentials arrive, look at the Resource line and wait for the time when it meets generation or thriving, and the credentials will surely arrive; if there is no Resource in the hexagram, one must wait for the day when the Resource line is on duty, and only then will the credentials come; if Resource is weak and also Void, even if one waits the credentials have not arrived.

> [!note] 按
> 「官凭」是官员到任的凭证（委任文凭）。「但原文书」的「原」疑是「愿」，译作「只要文书逢旺气」，不论怎么读，要的都是文书爻旺相。解里「此凭决到卦无父」书里没有断句，这里按文意断作「此凭决到；卦无父，须待父爻值日，方得凭来」。「值日」与求馆章「卦中无父，但逢值日方在」（EL.54）同，书里没有说按什么取。这一条与易隐官禄占「领凭」（YY.10）取同一类法：都以父母爻（文书）为用，看它的生旺和当值之日。
>
> *Note:* 'Guanping' is the credentials with which an official takes up his post (the letter of appointment). The 'yuan' of 'dan yuan wenshu' is presumably 'yuan', to wish, and it is translated 'the document line need only meet a thriving qi'; whichever way it is read, what is wanted is the document line thriving. In the explanation 'ci ping jue dao gua wu fu' has no punctuation in the book, and it is broken here by the sense as 'the credentials will surely arrive; if there is no Resource in the hexagram, one must wait for the day when Resource is on duty, and then the credentials will come'. 'Zhiri', the duty day, is the same as in 'if there is no Resource in the hexagram, it is present only when its duty day comes' in the chapter on seeking a school post (EL.54), and the book does not say by what it is reckoned. This entry takes the same kind of method as the 'receiving the credentials' section of the Yi Yin divination on official salary (YY.10): in both, Resource (the document) is the Subject and its thriving and its duty day are looked at.
