# 卦内作何官：火为文章喉舌（翰苑）、土为守令、水为江滨巡守、金带兵刑生杀权、木主工科建创；官爵大小随动爻衰旺

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道卦里是做什么官，先仔细看身：火是文章、喉舌之位（翰林一类）；土是守、令（修治城垒）；水是江边巡守的职事；金带兵、刑和生杀之权；木主工科，管建造创设；官爵大小都随动爻的休囚旺相来说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know what office the hexagram holds, first look carefully at the Body: Fire is the place of letters and of the throat and tongue of the court (an office of the Hanlin kind); Earth is the prefect or magistrate (repairing city walls); Water is a patrol-and-guard post along rivers and shores; Metal carries soldiers, punishments and the power of life and death; Wood governs the works section, in charge of building and founding; and the rank, high or low, follows whether the moving line is thriving or weak.
