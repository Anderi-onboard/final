# 征战占·方向（一）：内卦为我寨外卦为彼营，内卦旺相吉、胎没次之、死囚休废凶；附八卦旺相胎没死囚休废随八节的排列

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 方向：内卦是我方的营寨，外卦是对方的军营。内卦旺相的，吉；胎、没次一等；死、囚、休、废的，凶。八卦的旺、相、胎、没、死、囚、休、废随八个节气排列：立春后艮旺、震相、巽胎、离没、坤死、兑囚、乾休、坎废；春分后震旺、巽相、离胎、坤没、兑死、乾囚、坎休、艮废；立夏后巽旺、离相、坤胎、兑没、乾死、坎囚、艮休、震废；夏至后离旺、坤相、兑胎、乾没、坎死、艮囚、震休、巽废；立秋后坤旺、兑相、乾胎、坎没、艮死、震囚、巽休、离废；秋分后兑旺、乾相、坎胎、艮没、震死、巽囚、离休、坤废；立冬后乾旺、坎相、艮胎、震没、巽死、离囚、坤休、兑废；冬至后坎旺、艮相、震胎、巽没、离死、坤囚、兑休、乾废。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Direction. The inner trigram is my camp and the outer trigram is the enemy's. If the inner trigram is thriving or assisting it is auspicious; Tai and Mo come next; if dead, imprisoned, resting or abandoned it is ominous. The eight states of the trigrams run through the eight solar terms: after the Start of Spring Gen thrives, Zhen assists, Xun is Tai, Li is Mo, Kun is dead, Dui imprisoned, Qian resting and Kan abandoned; after the Spring Equinox Zhen thrives, Xun assists, Li is Tai, Kun is Mo, Dui is dead, Qian imprisoned, Kan resting and Gen abandoned; after the Start of Summer Xun thrives, Li assists, Kun is Tai, Dui is Mo, Qian is dead, Kan imprisoned, Gen resting and Zhen abandoned; after the Summer Solstice Li thrives, Kun assists, Dui is Tai, Qian is Mo, Kan is dead, Gen imprisoned, Zhen resting and Xun abandoned; after the Start of Autumn Kun thrives, Dui assists, Qian is Tai, Kan is Mo, Gen is dead, Zhen imprisoned, Xun resting and Li abandoned; after the Autumn Equinox Dui thrives, Qian assists, Kan is Tai, Gen is Mo, Zhen is dead, Xun imprisoned, Li resting and Kun abandoned; after the Start of Winter Qian thrives, Kan assists, Gen is Tai, Zhen is Mo, Xun is dead, Li imprisoned, Kun resting and Dui abandoned; after the Winter Solstice Kan thrives, Gen assists, Zhen is Tai, Xun is Mo, Li is dead, Kun imprisoned, Dui resting and Qian abandoned.

> [!note] 按
> 第二句「死囚伏废」的「伏」，对照后面八张表里的「休」，应是「休」字抄错；译文照字面写成 hidden，没有改字。八种状态在朝廷占「大象」里也用到（见 YY.32），这里是书里唯一把它们按节气逐项排出来的地方。表里每一行的八个字，都是那一节气之后八个卦各自的状态，每一行里八个卦各出现一次。
>
> *Note:* In the second sentence the character 'fu', hidden, in 'dead, imprisoned, hidden, abandoned' is compared with 'xiu', resting, in the eight tables after it, and is presumably a miscopy for 'xiu'; the translation writes 'hidden' by the letter and does not change the character. The eight states are also used in the 'great image' of the court divination (see YY.32), and this is the only place in the book that sets them out one by one by solar term. In each row, the eight characters give the state of each of the eight trigrams after that solar term, and each trigram appears once in every row.
