# 朝廷国事：亲宫为邦国大忌空亡、本卦作朝堂最宜旺相；本卦是所占之卦名、亲宫是本宫之卦，皆宜旺相兴隆，大忌空亡墓绝

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 亲宫（本宫）当作邦国，最忌空亡；本卦当作朝堂，最适宜旺相。本卦是所占得的那一卦的卦名，亲宫是本宫之卦，所以都当作邦国、朝堂，都适宜旺相兴隆，最忌空亡、墓、绝。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The home palace is taken as the state, and Void is most to be avoided; the original hexagram is taken as the court hall, and thriving is best. The original hexagram is the name of the hexagram cast, and the home palace is the hexagram of the palace to which it belongs; so both are taken as the state and the court, both should be thriving and flourishing, and Void, tomb and extinction are most to be avoided.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 286 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 本卦者，乃所占之卦名，亲宫者，乃本宫之卦也，故为邦国朝堂，皆宜旺相兴隆； | 本卦是所占得的那一卦的卦名，亲宫是本宫之卦，所以（它们）当作邦国、朝堂，都适宜旺相兴隆； | The original hexagram is the name of the hexagram cast, and the home palace is the hexagram of the palace to which it belongs; so they are taken as the state and the court, and both should be thriving and flourishing; |
> | 大忌空亡墓绝。 | 最忌空亡、墓、绝。 | Void, tomb and extinction are most to be avoided. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「亲宫」书里的解释是「本宫之卦」，即占得的卦所属的那一宫（八宫之一）；这一章里的「亲宫」「本宫」是同一个意思。「旺相」是这一宫（的五行）当令得气。与易隐朝廷占「世爻所居之卦为国」（YY.32）是两种取法：易隐取世爻所在的那一个单卦，这里取整个本宫和整个本卦。
>
> *Note:* 'Qingong' is explained in the book as 'the hexagram of the home palace', that is the palace of the eight to which the hexagram cast belongs; in this chapter 'qingong' and 'home palace' mean the same. 'Thriving' means that the element of this palace is in season and has qi. This is a different way of taking it from the 'trigram in which the Self sits is the state' of the Yi Yin court divination (YY.32): the Yi Yin takes the one trigram holding the Self, and this takes the whole home palace and the whole original hexagram.
