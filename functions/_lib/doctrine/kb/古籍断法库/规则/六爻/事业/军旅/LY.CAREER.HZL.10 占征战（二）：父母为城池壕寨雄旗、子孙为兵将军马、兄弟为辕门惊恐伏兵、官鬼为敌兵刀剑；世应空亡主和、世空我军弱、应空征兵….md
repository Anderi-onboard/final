# 占征战（二）：父母为城池壕寨雄旗、子孙为兵将军马、兄弟为辕门惊恐伏兵、官鬼为敌兵刀剑；世应空亡主和、世空我军弱、应空征兵退、世爻被鬼冲克我军不利、兄弟独发凶；鬼去爻中而兄弟化出官鬼合世爻身主有奸人在军中、世下伏鬼亦然

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母是城池、壕沟营寨、大旗；子孙是兵、将、军、马；兄弟是辕门、惊恐、伏兵；官鬼是敌兵和刀剑。世应空亡，主议和；世空，我军弱；应空，来攻的兵退；世爻被官鬼冲克，我军不利；兄弟独自发动，是凶。鬼不在爻中，兄弟化出官鬼来合世爻或身，主有奸细在军中；世下伏着鬼，也是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource is the city, the moat and stockade, and the great banner; Output is the soldiers, generals, army and horses; Peer is the camp gate, alarms and ambush troops; Pressure is the enemy soldiers and their swords. If the Self and the Other are both Void, it means peace; the Self Void, my army is weak; the Other Void, the troops marching against me withdraw; the Self line clashed or controlled by Pressure, my army is at a disadvantage; Peer moving alone is ominous. If Pressure is absent from the lines, and Peer changes into Pressure that combines with the Self line or the Body, it means a traitor in the army; with Pressure hidden under the Self it is the same.

> [!note] 按
> 「壕寨」是壕沟和营寨，「雄旗」是大旗，「辕门」是军营的大门（军门），「惊恐」是惊慌恐惧，「伏兵」是设伏的兵。六亲配军中的人与物：父母是城池营寨旗号，子孙是兵将，兄弟是军门、惊慌和伏兵，官鬼是敌兵刀剑。「世应空亡，主和」是世应都空，战事议和；「世空我军弱，应空征兵退」是世空我弱，应空则来攻的兵退。「鬼去爻中」按文意是官鬼不在卦里（「去」是不在）；「合世爻身」是合世爻或身；兄弟变出官鬼来合我，等于有奸细（内奸）混在军中。「世下伏鬼亦然」是官鬼伏在世爻下面，也是同样。这一节与易隐征战占的六亲（YY.57–59）、本书军机的六亲分工（YM.26）对着看，三书取象大体相近。
>
> *Note:* 'Haozhai' is the moat and the stockade, 'xiongqi' is the great banner, 'yuanmen' is the great gate of a camp (the army gate), 'jingkong' is alarm and fright, and 'fubing' is troops set in ambush. The six relations are matched to the people and things of an army: Resource is the city, stockade and banners, Output is the soldiers and generals, Peer is the army gate, alarms and ambush troops, and Pressure is the enemy soldiers and their swords. 'The Self and the Other Void, it means peace' is both the Self and the Other Void, and the war moves to peace talks; 'the Self Void, my army is weak; the Other Void, the troops marching against me withdraw' is the Self Void and my side weak, and the Other Void and the troops that come to attack withdraw. 'Gui qu yao zhong' is by the sense Pressure absent from the hexagram ('qu' is not there); 'combines with the Self line or the Body'; Peer changing into Pressure that combines with me is the same as a traitor (an inside agent) mixed into the army. 'With Pressure hidden under the Self it is the same' is Pressure hidden under the Self line, likewise. Read this section against the six relations in the Yi Yin on war (YY.57-59) and the division of the six relations in the military chapter of this book (YM.26); the three books take much the same images.
