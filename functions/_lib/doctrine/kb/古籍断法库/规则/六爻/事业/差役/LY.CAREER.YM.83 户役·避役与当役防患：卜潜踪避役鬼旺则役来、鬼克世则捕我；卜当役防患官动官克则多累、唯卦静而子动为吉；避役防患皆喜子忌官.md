# 户役·避役与当役防患：卜潜踪避役鬼旺则役来、鬼克世则捕我；卜当役防患官动官克则多累、唯卦静而子动为吉；避役防患皆喜子忌官

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占躲起来逃避差役吗？鬼旺，差役就来；鬼克世，就来抓我了。占当差防备出事吗？官动、官克，就多牵累；只有卦静而子动，才是这两种占的吉。避役、防患，都喜子、忌官。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about hiding one's tracks to avoid a duty: with Pressure thriving the duty comes; with Pressure controlling the Self, they come to arrest me. In divining about serving a duty and guarding against trouble: with Pressure moving and Pressure controlling there are many entanglements; only with the hexagram still and Output moving are the two divinations auspicious. Avoiding service and guarding against trouble both welcome Output and fear Pressure.

> [!note] 按
> 「潜踪避役」是躲起来逃避差役，「当役防患」是已经当差、防备出事。「鬼旺则役来」是官鬼旺，差役就找上门；「鬼克世则捕我矣」是鬼克世，官府就来抓我。「卦静而子动」是卦里其他爻都静、只有子孙动：子孙克官鬼，官鬼受制，差役就不来、出事也少。书里自注：避役、防患都喜子孙、忌官鬼。这一条与易隐官禄占的避祸（YY.20）、黄金策仕宦的卦静世空（HJC.16）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Qianzong biyi' is hiding one's tracks to avoid a duty, and 'dangyi fanghuan' is already serving a duty and guarding against trouble. 'With Pressure thriving the duty comes' is that when Pressure is thriving the duty comes looking for one; 'with Pressure controlling the Self, they come to arrest me' is the authorities coming to seize me. 'The hexagram still and Output moving' is every other line being still and only Output moving: Output controls Pressure, Pressure is held in check, and the duty does not come and there is little trouble. The book's own note: avoiding service and guarding against trouble both welcome Output and fear Pressure. Read against the Yi Yin on avoiding trouble in the divination of office (YY.20) and the Huangjin Ce on a still hexagram with a Void Self (HJC.16).
