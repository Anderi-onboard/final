# 求荐奖与谪官开复：官文破坏恐荐贤不力、鬼象克世虑奖饰非真、世值空亡求荣不遂望擢难迁；谪官开复同此推；民间坊匾旌表冠带衣巾乡贤乡饮同类

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求荐举、嘉奖，而遇到官爻、文书破坏，恐怕荐贤的人不出力；鬼象克世，担心嘉奖不是真心实意；世遇空亡，求荣不成，盼升也难迁。被贬谪的官员请求开复（恢复官职），也同此推断。民间的牌坊匾额、旌表、冠带、衣巾、乡贤、乡饮等事，也属同类。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking recommendation or commendation, if Pressure and Resource (the document line) are broken and damaged, one fears that those who recommend the worthy do not exert themselves; if the Pressure image controls the Self, one fears the commendation is not genuine; if the Self meets Void, seeking honour does not succeed and hoping for promotion one cannot move. For a demoted official asking to be reinstated, judge in the same way. Memorial arches and tablets in the villages, commendation, caps and sashes, scholars' robes, the shrine of local worthies, and the village drinking ceremony are of the same kind.

> [!note] 按
> 「荐奖」是被上司荐举、嘉奖；「官文」是官爻和文书（父母爻）；「奖餙」的「餙」是「饰」，奖饰就是褒奖的话；「开复」是被贬谪的官员请求恢复官职。「坊匾」是牌坊匾额，「旌表」是朝廷表彰，「冠带」是赐冠带的荣典，「衣巾」是生员的衣巾，「乡贤」是入乡贤祠，「乡饮」是乡饮酒礼里被推为宾介。这些民间荣典占法同荐奖。与易林补遗官员荐奖（EL.79–88）对着看：那一章有更细的断法。
>
> *Note:* 'Jianjiang' is being recommended and commended by a superior; 'guanwen' is the Pressure line and the document line (the Resource line); the 'shi' of 'jiangshi' is the variant for 'to adorn', so 'jiangshi' is words of praise; 'kaifu' is a demoted official asking to be restored to office. 'Fangbian' is memorial arches and tablets, 'jingbiao' is imperial commendation, 'guandai' is the honour of being granted cap and sash, 'yijin' is the robe and cap of a licentiate, 'xiangxian' is entering the shrine of local worthies, and 'xiangyin' is being named a guest of honour at the village drinking ceremony. These village honours are divined in the same way as recommendation. Read against the Yilin Buyi on recommendation of officials (EL.79-88), which has finer judgments.
