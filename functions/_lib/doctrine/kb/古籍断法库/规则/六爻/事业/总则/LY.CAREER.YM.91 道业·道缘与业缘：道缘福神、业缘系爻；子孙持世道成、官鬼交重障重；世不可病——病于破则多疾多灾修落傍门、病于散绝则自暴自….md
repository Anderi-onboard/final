# 道业·道缘与业缘：道缘福神、业缘系爻；子孙持世道成、官鬼交重障重；世不可病——病于破则多疾多灾修落傍门、病于散绝则自暴自弃心慵半路、随墓助伤则身陷泥犁、世生世旺则心开日月之光；专忌鬼动及世爻破散绝为三戒、世动亦主不宁

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 什么叫道缘？福神就是。什么叫业缘？系爻就是。所以子孙持世，道就成；官鬼交重，障就重。而世不可有病：病在破，多病多灾，修行落入傍门；病在散、绝，自暴自弃，半路上心里懒散；随墓、助伤，则身陷泥犁（地狱）之地；世被生、世旺，则心里打开日月的光明。这是它的大象。专忌鬼动，以及世爻破、散、绝，这是三戒；世动也主不安宁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* What is called the affinity for the Way? The fortune spirit, Output, is it. What is called karmic affinity? The binding line is it. So with Output on the Self the Way is accomplished; with Pressure moving and doubled the obstruction is heavy. And the Self must not be sick: sick by being broken, there are many illnesses and calamities and the practice falls into side paths; sick by being scattered or extinct, one gives oneself up and the heart goes slack halfway; following into the tomb and helping the injury, the body sinks into the pit of hell; with the Self generated and thriving, the heart opens to the light of sun and moon. This is its great image. Moving Pressure is above all feared, and the Self line broken, scattered or extinct, are the three warnings; the Self moving also means unrest.

> [!note] 按
> 「道缘」是修道的缘分，取福神（子孙）；「业缘」是业力的牵缠，取系爻（捆住人的爻，按下文是官鬼）；「障」是业障。「傍门」是旁门左道，「泥犁」是地狱（梵语 niraya），「日月之光」是智慧的光明。「病于破」「病于散绝」是世爻破、散、绝三种伤，各有各的后果：破是身体多灾，散绝是意志消沉。书里自注：专忌鬼动，以及世爻破、散、绝，是三戒；世动也主不宁。这一条是整章的总纲：道成与否看福神和世爻，障碍看官鬼，所以后面各问都先看世、福、鬼。
>
> *Note:* 'Daoyuan' is the affinity for cultivating the Way, taken from the fortune spirit (Output); 'yeyuan' is the entanglement of karmic force, taken from the binding line (the line that binds a man, which by the text below is Pressure); 'zhang' is karmic obstruction. 'Pangmen' is a side door, a heterodox way, 'niri' is hell (Sanskrit niraya), and 'the light of sun and moon' is the light of wisdom. 'Sick by being broken' and 'sick by being scattered or extinct' are three kinds of injury to the Self line, each with its consequence: broken means a body with many calamities, scattered or extinct means a sunken will. The book's own note: moving Pressure above all, and the Self line broken, scattered or extinct, are the three warnings; the Self moving also means unrest. This entry is the general principle of the whole chapter: whether the Way is accomplished is seen by the fortune spirit and the Self line, and obstruction by Pressure, so every later question first looks at the Self, Output and Pressure.
