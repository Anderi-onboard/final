# 鬻身投主：子旺财明多积蓄、兄兴鬼发染灾非；子动生财财因子助乃积蓄丰余津津不竭，鬼同兄发鬼耗官灾乃灾生财散件件不实

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙旺相、财爻分明，积蓄就多；兄爻兴旺、官鬼发动，就染上灾祸是非。子孙发动生财，财得子孙相助，就是积蓄丰余、源源不断；官鬼和兄爻一起发动，官鬼耗损、招官灾，就是灾祸生起、钱财散去，件件都不踏实。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output is thriving and Wealth is clear, there are many savings; if Peer flourishes and Pressure moves, one is stained with calamity and wrangling. If Output moves to generate Wealth, with Wealth aided by Output, it means abundant savings flowing unceasingly; if Pressure and Peer move together, Pressure wastes substance and brings official disaster, so calamity arises and wealth scatters, and nothing proves solid.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2502 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子动生财，财因子助，乃积蓄丰余津津不竭。 | 子孙发动生财，财因子孙相助，就是积蓄丰余、源源不断。 | If Output moves to generate Wealth, with Wealth aided by Output, it means abundant savings flowing unceasingly. |
> | 鬼同兄发鬼耗官灾，乃灾生财散，件件不实耳。 | 官鬼和兄爻一起发动，官鬼耗损、招官灾，就是灾祸生起、钱财散去，件件都不踏实。 | If Pressure and Peer move together, Pressure wastes substance and brings official disaster, so calamity arises and wealth scatters, and nothing proves solid. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 诀里「史兴鬼发」的「史」疑是「兄」（解里写「鬼同兄发」），译作「兄兴鬼发」。「津津不竭」是源源不断。「官灾」是官司、官府之灾。
>
> *Note:* The 'shi' of 'shi xing gui fa' in the verse is presumably 'xiong', Peer (the explanation writes 'Pressure and Peer move together'), and it is translated 'Peer flourishes and Pressure moves'. 'Jinjin bu jie' is flowing without end. 'Guanzai' is official disaster, a lawsuit or a calamity from the authorities.
