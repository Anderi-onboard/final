# 鬼谷辨爻法·占仕宦：初爻吏人、二爻曹官、三爻长官、四爻监司、五爻朝仕、六爻执政

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占仕宦，按鬼谷子的爻位法：初爻是吏人，二爻是曹官，三爻是长官，四爻是监司，五爻是朝仕（在朝的官），六爻是执政。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining official careers, by the Guigu line-position method the first line is the clerk, the second the bureau officer, the third the head of the office, the fourth the supervising commissioner, the fifth the court official and the sixth the minister in power.
