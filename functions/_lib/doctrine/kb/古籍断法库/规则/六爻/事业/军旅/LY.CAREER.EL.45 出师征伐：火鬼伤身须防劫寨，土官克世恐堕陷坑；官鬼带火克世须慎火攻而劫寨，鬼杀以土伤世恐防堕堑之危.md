# 出师征伐：火鬼伤身须防劫寨，土官克世恐堕陷坑；官鬼带火克世须慎火攻而劫寨，鬼杀以土伤世恐防堕堑之危

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 火爻官鬼伤身，须防劫寨；土爻官鬼克世，恐怕掉进陷坑。官鬼带火克世的，须慎防火攻、劫寨；官鬼和煞以土伤世的，恐怕有掉进壕沟的危险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a Fire Pressure line injures the Body, guard against a raid on the stockade; if an Earth Pressure line controls the Self, one may fall into a pit. If Pressure carrying Fire controls the Self, one must take care against attack by fire and a raid on the stockade; if Pressure and a baleful spirit injure the Self by Earth, there may be the danger of falling into a trench.

> [!note] 按
> 「危矶」疑是「危机」，译作「危险」。这一条与易隐征战占「火鬼伤世防劫营」「阴土鬼…防陷阱」（YY.64、YY.65）取同一件事。
>
> *Note:* 'Wei ji' is presumably 'weiji', danger. This entry takes up the same matter as 'a Fire Pressure line injures the Self, guard against a raid on the camp' and 'a yin Earth Pressure line ... guard against pits' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.64, YY.65).
