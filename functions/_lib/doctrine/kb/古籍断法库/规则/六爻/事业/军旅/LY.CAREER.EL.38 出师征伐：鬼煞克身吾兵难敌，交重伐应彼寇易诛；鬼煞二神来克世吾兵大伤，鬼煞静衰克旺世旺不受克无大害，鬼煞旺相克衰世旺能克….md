# 出师征伐：鬼煞克身吾兵难敌，交重伐应彼寇易诛；鬼煞二神来克世吾兵大伤，鬼煞静衰克旺世旺不受克无大害，鬼煞旺相克衰世旺能克衰吾兵大被伐戮，鬼煞旺动克应彼已受制易剿伐

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼和煞克身，我军难以抵敌；重叠发动去伐应爻，对方的贼寇容易诛灭。鬼是官鬼，煞是大煞、劫煞之类。官鬼和煞两个神如果来克世爻，我军大受损伤；如果官鬼和煞安静而又衰弱，来克旺相的世爻，是旺相不受克，料想没有大害；官鬼和煞旺相而克衰弱的世爻，这是旺相能克衰弱，我军大被讨伐杀戮；如果官鬼和煞旺相发动克应爻，对方已经受制，容易剿伐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure and the baleful spirit control the Body, my army can hardly stand against them; if they move in double to attack the Other, the enemy bandits are easily destroyed. 'Gui' is Pressure, and 'sha' is the great baleful spirit, Robbery and the like. If the two spirits Pressure and the baleful spirit come to control the Self, my army is badly injured; if Pressure and the baleful spirit are still and weak and come to control a thriving Self, a thriving line is not controlled and no great harm is expected; if Pressure and the baleful spirit are thriving and control a weak Self, this is the thriving controlling the weak, and my army is greatly attacked and slaughtered; if Pressure and the baleful spirit are thriving and moving and control the Other, the enemy is already restrained and easily destroyed.

> [!note] 按
> 「旺不受克」是「旺相的爻不受衰弱的爻所克」，这是五行生克里的通例。这一条与 EL.36、EL.37 连着读：官鬼与煞合起来看克世还是克应。
>
> *Note:* 'The thriving is not controlled' is the general rule in the generation and control of the elements that a thriving line is not controlled by a weak one. This entry reads on from EL.36 and EL.37: Pressure and the baleful spirit are looked at together for whether they control the Self or the Other.
