# 朝廷占·谏官：以子孙为用；子动克世直臣匡敕，刑冲世直臣撄鳞，合世谀臣将顺；子临岁月动克四爻生合五爻，子生旺日必有直臣纠劾权臣；子动克四五爻与日辰生合可其奏，与日辰刑害克冲疏不允反遭贬责

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 进谏的官员：以子孙为用神。子孙爻发动克世爻的，正直的臣子匡正、告诫君主；子孙爻发动刑、冲世爻的，正直的臣子冒死触犯君主的逆鳞；子孙爻发动合世爻的，谄谀的臣子顺从迎合。子孙爻临太岁、月建而发动，克四爻、生合五爻的，到子孙生旺的日子，必有正直的臣子弹劾权臣。子孙爻发动，克四爻、五爻，又得日辰生合的，君主准其奏章；与日辰刑、害、克、冲的，奏疏既不被允准，反而遭到贬官责罚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The remonstrating officials. Take Output as the Subject. If Output moves and controls the Self, an upright minister corrects and warns the sovereign; if it moves to punish or clash with the Self, an upright minister touches the dragon's reversed scales; if it moves to combine with the Self, a flattering minister goes along. If Output sits on Tai Sui or the Month and moves, controlling the fourth line and generating or combining with the fifth, then on a day when Output is born or thriving there will surely be an upright minister impeaching a powerful one. If Output moves and controls the fourth and fifth lines and the Day generates or combines with it, his memorial is approved; if the Day punishes, harms, controls or clashes with it, the memorial is not allowed and he is instead demoted and rebuked.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「撄鳞」是触犯逆鳞：龙喉下有倒生的鳞，碰到它龙就会杀人，比喻触怒君主。「匡敕」是匡正、整饬。「将顺」是顺从。第六句里的「可其奏」按字面是认可他的奏章。
>
> *Note:* 'Yinglin' is touching the reversed scales: a dragon has a scale growing the wrong way under its throat, and whoever touches it is killed, an image of angering the sovereign. 'Kuangchi' is correcting and straightening out. 'Jiangshun' is going along. In the sixth sentence, 'approve his memorial' is by the letter to allow his memorial.
