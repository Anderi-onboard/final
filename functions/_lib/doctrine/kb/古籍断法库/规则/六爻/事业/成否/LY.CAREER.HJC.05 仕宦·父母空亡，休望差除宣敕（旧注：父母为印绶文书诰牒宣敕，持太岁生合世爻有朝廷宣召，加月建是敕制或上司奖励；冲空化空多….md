# 仕宦·父母空亡，休望差除宣敕（旧注：父母为印绶文书诰牒宣敕，持太岁生合世爻有朝廷宣召，加月建是敕制或上司奖励；冲空化空多不实，衰静空亡无宣敕、卦无父母休望迁选）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻是印绶、文书、诰牒、宣敕、奏疏、表章，卦里不能没有它，宜旺不宜衰，扶助世爻最吉。它持太岁、有气、生合世爻，主有朝廷的宣召；再加月建，多是敕令制命和上司的奖励；最怕冲、空、化空，那就多半不实；衰弱安静又空亡，必定没有宣敕，也没有差遣任命；卦里没有父母，别指望迁升选用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource is the seal and ribbon, documents, patents, imperial edicts, memorials and petitions. It cannot be missing from the hexagram, should be thriving and not weak, and supporting the Self is the most auspicious. If it holds the Tai Sui with breath and generates or combines with the Self, there will be a summons from the court; with the Month added, it is mostly an edict or a commendation from superiors; what is most feared is a clash, Void or turning into Void, and then it is mostly unreal; if weak, still and Void, there will surely be no edict and no appointment; if Resource is not in the hexagram, do not hope for promotion or selection.

> [!note] 按
> 「印缓」是「印绶」（官印和系印的丝带）的误写，卜筮大全的原文就是这样写的，译文按「印绶」读。「宣敕」是皇帝的诏命，「诰牒」是授官的文书，「差除」是差遣、任命。这一条是说做官的「文书」：没有父母就没有任命、没有升迁。
>
> *Note:* 'Seal and ribbon' in the Bushi Daquan is written with a wrong character for 'ribbon' (the original prints it that way), and the translation reads 'seal and ribbon'. 'Edict' is an imperial decree, 'patent' is the document conferring office, and 'posting and appointment' is being sent out on a commission or being appointed. This entry is about the 'documents' of an official career: with no Resource there is no appointment and no promotion.
