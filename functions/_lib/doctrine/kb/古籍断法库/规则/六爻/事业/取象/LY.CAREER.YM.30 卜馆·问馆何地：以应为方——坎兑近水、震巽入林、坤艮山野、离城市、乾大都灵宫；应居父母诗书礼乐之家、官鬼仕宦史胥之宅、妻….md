# 卜馆·问馆何地：以应为方——坎兑近水、震巽入林、坤艮山野、离城市、乾大都灵宫；应居父母诗书礼乐之家、官鬼仕宦史胥之宅、妻财商贾、兄弟同侪、子孙清闲道德之门；阴阳分男女之延、内外分远近之请、游魂归魂分本土他境；六神分景色、诸星为人事

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问馆在什么地方，以应爻为方位：应在坎、兑宫，近水；在震、巽，入林；在坤、艮，是山野；在离，是城市；在乾，是大都会或灵宫（神庙）。应落在父母，是诗书礼乐之家；落在官鬼，是做官的、书吏衙役的住宅；落在妻财是商贾，落在兄弟是同辈同行，落在子孙就是清闲讲道德的门第。用阴阳分是男是女来聘，用内外分是远是近来请，用游魂、归魂分本地还是他乡；用六神看四周的景色，用诸星看人事。应带青龙，馆在长林丰草之间；带玄武，在水边池旁；带朱雀，在闹市或有炊烟的村子；带白虎，在荒僻的乡下；带勾陈，近田野旧坟；带螣蛇，依在来往的工匠、舟车一带。贵人是做官的，驿马是经商的，华盖是出家人，天财是商贩，天猪是屠户，其余照这样类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking where the post is, the Other gives the direction: the Other in Kan or Dui is near water, in Zhen or Xun it goes into woods, in Kun or Gen it is mountain wilds, in Li it is a town, and in Qian it is a great city or a hall of the spirits (a temple). The Other on Resource is a house of poetry, books, rites and music; on Pressure, the residence of officials, clerks and runners; on Wealth, merchants; on Peer, one's fellows of the same trade; on Output, a quiet, leisured house of morals and the Way. Yin and yang tell whether a man or a woman engages me, inner and outer whether the invitation is near or far, Wandering Soul and Returning Soul whether it is home ground or another region; the six spirits give the scenery around and the stars give the human affairs. The Other with the Azure Dragon: the place is among long woods and thick grass; the Dark Tortoise: a house by water, beside a pond; the Vermilion Bird: a busy market or a smoky village; the White Tiger: a barren, remote countryside; Hook Earth: near fields and old graves; the Coiling Snake: by the traffic of craftsmen, boats and carts. The Noble is an official, the Post Horse a merchant, the Canopy a monk, Heavenly Wealth a trader, the Heavenly Boar a butcher, and the rest are sought by analogy.

> [!note] 按
> 「史胥」就是吏胥，书吏和衙役。「灵宫」是供神的宫观。「男女之延」是聘他的是男主人还是女主人；「远近之请」是请他的地方远还是近。游魂主迁徙、归魂主回原处，所以拿来分本乡他乡（和 EL.53「游魂迁改它图吉、归魂乃旧胜西东」是同一个意思）。「景色」是馆舍周围的光景。「天财」「天猪」是神煞名，书里没有列出怎么取，按字面译。这一条是把应爻当作东家来看的一整套取象，与黄金策求馆「应乃东家，若遇官爻须作吏」（HJC.30）同类，易冒比它多了方位和六神、神煞一层。
>
> *Note:* 'Shixu' is 'lixu', clerks and runners. 'Lingong' is a hall for the worship of spirits. 'Engaged by man or woman' is whether it is the master or the mistress of the house who engages him; 'far or near invitation' is whether the place that invites him is far or near. Wandering Soul stands for moving about and Returning Soul for going back to the original place, so they are used to tell home ground from other regions (the same idea as in EL.53, 'Wandering Soul: another scheme is good; Returning Soul: the old post is better than east or west'). 'Scenery' is the view around the schoolroom. 'Heavenly Wealth' and 'Heavenly Boar' are names of spirit-stars, and the book does not list how they are taken, so they are translated literally. This entry is a whole set of images that treats the Other as the master of the house, of a kind with 'the Other is the master of the house, and if it meets the Pressure line he must be an official' in the Huangjin Ce on seeking a post (HJC.30), the Yimao adding a layer of direction, the six spirits and the spirit-stars.
