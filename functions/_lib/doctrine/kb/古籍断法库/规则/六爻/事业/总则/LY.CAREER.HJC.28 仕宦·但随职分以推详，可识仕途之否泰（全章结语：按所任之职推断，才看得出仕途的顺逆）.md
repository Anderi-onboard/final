# 仕宦·但随职分以推详，可识仕途之否泰（全章结语：按所任之职推断，才看得出仕途的顺逆）

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占仕宦，要按所任的职位和职分来推断，才能看出仕途的顺逆。这是仕宦章的结语：前面各联是按官职的种类分的（风宪、牧民、将帅、出巡、谏官、僧道医官），用的时候先判断问的是哪一类职位，再取相应的一联。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining official posts, one must infer according to the post held and its duties, and then the smoothness or blockage of an official career can be known. This is the closing line of the chapter on holding office: the couplets before it are divided by the kind of office (censorate, shepherding the people, commander, touring, remonstrance, monk, Daoist and physician officials), and in use one first judges what kind of post is being asked about and then takes the corresponding couplet.

> [!note] 按
> 「职分」是官职和它的职责；「否泰」是否（不通）和泰（通）两个卦，指不顺和顺利。卜筮大全和卜筮正宗这一句字句相同。这一章里的各联，有的是通用的（财、官、父、子、兄在仕宦里的意思，见 LY.CAREER.HJC.01 至 HJC.06），有的是专讲某一类职位的（HJC.19 至 HJC.27），结语是提醒这一点。
>
> *Note:* 'The duties of the post' are the office and what it is charged with, and 'smoothness or blockage' are the two hexagrams Pi (blocked) and Tai (open), meaning unfavourable and favourable. The Bushi Daquan and the Bushi Zhengzong have this line in identical words. Some couplets of this chapter are general (what Wealth, Pressure, Resource, Output and Peer mean in holding office, see LY.CAREER.HJC.01 to HJC.06) and some speak of one kind of post (HJC.19 to HJC.27), and the closing line reminds one of this.
