# 谋望占·可否成败（五）：托贵人图事——官贵受刑害克破不可告贵人事；官贵刑冲克害身世爻求情难蒙许诺后被搀越有始无终反有所费；毕法赋：书占得夜贵曰脱气凡事被贵人掇赚

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 托贵人办事：只要官星、贵人受刑、害、克、破，就不可以向贵人求事，因为贵人自己受克制，不能成就我。如果官星、贵人刑、冲、克、害身爻、世爻的，求情难得许诺，后来被别人插队抢先，有始无终，反而要花费钱财。《毕法赋》说：凡是书占得夜贵人，叫作脱气，凡事被贵人哄骗；这是谋望可否成败的附带占断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Entrusting a noble with the matter. If the star of office and the Noble are punished, harmed, controlled or broken, one must not ask the noble for the matter, because the noble himself is under restraint and cannot see me through. If the star of office and the Noble punish, clash with, control or harm the Body and Self, a plea is hard to get granted, and later someone else jumps ahead, so it begins and does not end, and one even spends money for it. The Bifa Fu says: when a document divination gets the night Noble, it is called a drained breath, and in everything one is cajoled and cheated by the noble; this is an appended judgment on whether a scheme can and will succeed.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「搀越」是插进来越过；「掇赚」是哄骗、诓骗。「夜贵」是夜间的贵人（贵人有昼夜两位），「脱气」书里没有解释，按字面是被泄掉了气。《毕法赋》这句是引的另一部书，书里没有说它的上下文，译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* 'Chanyue' is cutting in and getting ahead; 'duozhuan' is cajoling and cheating. 'Night Noble' is the night one of the two Nobles (the Noble has a day and a night one), and 'tuoqi' is not explained in the book and means by the letter that the breath is drained away. The sentence from the Bifa Fu is quoted from another book, and the book does not give its context; the translation follows the letter.
