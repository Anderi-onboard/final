# 国事：初为国本、二为士人、三乃万方之守牧、四为九州之屏藩、上为宗庙之爻，俱恶鬼而喜福、喜安旺而恶动摇；鬼为国之患又为巨寇，福为国之储又为良臣，财为国之赋又为阍寺，兄弟为国之聚敛又为宗支，父母为山川城郭又为诏令

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推爻位：初爻是国本，二爻是士人，三爻是各地的守牧（地方长官），四爻是九州的屏藩，上爻是宗庙之爻；都厌恶官鬼、喜欢福神，喜欢安稳旺相、厌恶动摇。推六亲：官鬼是国家的祸患，又是大盗；福神是国家的储君，又是良臣；妻财是国家的赋税，又是宦官；兄弟是国家的聚敛，又是宗室；父母是山川城郭，又是诏令。看它们临在什么位置、属于哪一宫，比如官鬼发动在乾宫，西北有事；福神临在三爻，地方守牧得人，等等。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Reckoning by the line positions: the first line is the root of the state, the second the scholars, the third the governors of all the regions, the fourth the screens and bulwarks of the nine provinces, and the top line the line of the ancestral temple; all dislike Pressure and like the fortune spirit, like quiet and thriving and dislike moving and shaking. Reckoning by the six kin: Pressure is the plague of the state and also a great bandit; Output, the fortune spirit, is the heir of the state and also a good minister; Wealth is the revenue of the state and also the palace gatekeepers; Peer is the state's levying and hoarding and also the imperial clan; Resource is the mountains, rivers and city walls and also the edicts and orders. Look at where they sit and what palace they belong to: for example, if Pressure moves in the Qian palace there is trouble in the north-west, and if the fortune spirit sits on the third line the governors are well chosen, and the like.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「守牧」是地方长官，「屏藩」是拱卫朝廷的藩镇，「阍寺」是守宫门的宦官，「宗支」是皇室宗亲的各支，「聚敛」是征收搜刮，「诏令」是皇帝的诏书命令。这一条的取象与易隐朝廷占的「分官」（YY.43、YY.44）、黄金策国朝的爻位（HJC.65）同一套：初民、二士、三守牧、四公卿、五君、上宗庙。
>
> *Note:* 'Shoumu' are the local governors, 'pingfan' are the border governors who guard the court, 'hunshi' are the eunuchs who keep the palace gate, 'zongzhi' are the branches of the imperial clan, 'juilian' is levying and extorting, and 'zhaoling' are the emperor's edicts and orders. The images here are the same set as the 'division of officials' in the Yi Yin divination on the court (YY.43, YY.44) and the line positions in the Huangjin Ce on the state (HJC.65): the first the common people, the second scholars, the third governors, the fourth ministers, the fifth the sovereign, the top the ancestral temple.
