# 征战·观世应之旺衰，以决两家之胜负；将福官之强弱，以分彼我之军师（旧注：世为我、应为彼，世旺克应则胜、应旺克世则负；子孙为我之将军、官鬼为彼之敌帅）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占征战，世爻是我方，应爻是对方：世爻旺相、克住应爻就胜，应爻旺相、克住世爻就败。子孙是我方的将军，官鬼是对方的主帅，两边军师的强弱，就从这两个爻上分。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a question about a campaign the Self is our side and the Other is the enemy: if the Self is thriving or strong and controls the Other we win, and if the Other is thriving and controls the Self we lose. Output is our general and Pressure is the enemy commander, and the strength of the two sides' leaders is told apart from these two lines.

> | --- | --- | --- |

> | 谋事在人，成事在天，当究先师之妙论。 | 谋划在人，成败在天，要钻研前辈高人的精妙议论。 | planning lies with men and success with heaven, so one should study the fine words of the masters before us. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1512 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世为我，应为彼，世旺克应则胜，应旺克世则负。 | 世是我方，应是对方，世旺相克应就胜，应旺相克世就败。 | The Self is us and the Other is them; if the Self is thriving and controls the Other we win, and if the Other is thriving and controls the Self we lose. |
> | 子为我之将，鬼彼之师。 | 子孙是我方的将，官鬼是对方的军队主帅。 | Output is our general and Pressure is the enemy's commander. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗收的是同一段，只有末一句「鬼彼之师」比旧注的「官鬼为彼之敌帅」简略。
>
> *Note:* The Bu Shi Zheng Zong gives the same passage; only its last phrase, 'Pressure is their army', is shorter than the old commentary's 'Pressure is the enemy's commander'.
