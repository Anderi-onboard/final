# 求馆·福或兴隆，会见青矜之济济（旧注：占馆以福爻为门生，旺相多、休囚少；空亡不上卦未必其家子弟；子化子生徒必多；带杀动来克世日后恐有操戈入室者）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占馆以子孙爻代表门生（学生）：旺相就多，休囚就少；空亡、不上卦，虽是这家人作主，未必是他家的子弟；子孙化出子孙，学生必定多；带煞发动来克世爻，日后恐怕有动手作乱、入室反目的人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a school post, Output stands for the pupils: if thriving there are many, if resting or trapped, few; if it is Void or absent from the hexagram, though this household is the master the pupils are not necessarily the sons of that family; Output turning into Output means many pupils; if it carries a killer and moves to control the Self, there may later be one who takes up arms and turns on his master within the room.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「青矜」（青衿）是读书人穿的青领的衣服，这里指学生。「操戈入室」是动手作乱、反目成仇（「入室操戈」是说反过来用老师教的东西来对付老师）。注意：在仕宦里，子孙是「伤官之杀」（LY.CAREER.HJC.03），在求馆里，子孙是门生，是吉神。这一章里子孙只吉不凶，书里这一章里的子孙是「学生」。
>
> *Note:* 'Blue collar' is the blue-collared dress that students wear, here meaning pupils. 'Take up arms and enter the room' is rising in violence and turning into enemies (it also means using what the teacher taught against the teacher). Note that in holding office Output is 'the killer that harms office' (LY.CAREER.HJC.03), whereas in a school post Output is the pupils, a lucky spirit; in this chapter Output is simply the 'students'.
