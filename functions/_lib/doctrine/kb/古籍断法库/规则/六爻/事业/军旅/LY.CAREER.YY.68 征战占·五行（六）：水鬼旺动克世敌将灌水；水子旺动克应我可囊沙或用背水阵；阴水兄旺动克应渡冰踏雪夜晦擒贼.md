# 征战占·五行（六）：水鬼旺动克世敌将灌水；水子旺动克应我可囊沙或用背水阵；阴水兄旺动克应渡冰踏雪夜晦擒贼

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水用于战：水爻的官鬼旺相发动、克世爻的，敌将会灌水淹我；水爻的子孙旺相发动、克应爻的，我可以用沙袋壅水，或者背水列阵；阴水爻的兄弟旺相发动、克应爻的，可以渡冰踏雪，在夜里晦暗时擒贼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Water in battle. If a Water Pressure line is thriving and moving and controls the Self, the enemy general will flood me; if a Water Output line is thriving and moving and controls the Other, I can use sandbags or draw up with the water behind me; if a yin Water Peer line is thriving and moving and controls the Other, one can cross the ice and tread the snow and seize the bandits on a dark night.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「囊沙」「背水阵」见 YY.55 的注。书里「水鬼旺动克世」没有说克世的是官鬼本身还是水，译文按水爻的官鬼译。
>
> *Note:* 'Nang sha' and the 'water-behind formation' are explained in the note to YY.55. 'Water Pressure thriving and moving controls the Self' does not say whether it is the Pressure line itself or Water that controls, and the translation reads it as the Pressure line of Water.
