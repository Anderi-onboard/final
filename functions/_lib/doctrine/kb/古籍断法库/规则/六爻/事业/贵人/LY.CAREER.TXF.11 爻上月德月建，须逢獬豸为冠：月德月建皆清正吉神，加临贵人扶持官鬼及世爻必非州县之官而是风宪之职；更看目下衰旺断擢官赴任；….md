# 爻上月德月建，须逢獬豸为冠：月德月建皆清正吉神，加临贵人扶持官鬼及世爻必非州县之官而是风宪之职；更看目下衰旺断擢官赴任；不忌凶杀，逢杀则威风凛凛

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 爻上有月德、月建，必定戴上獬豸冠（监察执法之冠）。月德、月建都是清正的吉神，各种杀都不敢抬头看；若加在贵人上、扶持官鬼和世爻，必定不是州县的官，而是风宪（监察）的职位；再看眼下的衰旺，就能断他升官赴任；这种情形不忌凶杀，遇杀反而威风凛凛、一举一动惊人、掌权轰轰烈烈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Month Virtue and the Month on the line one will surely wear the Xiezhi cap (the cap of the censor and judge). The Month Virtue and the Month are both upright auspicious spirits at which no baleful spirit dares look up; added to the Noble and supporting Pressure and the Self line, it is surely not a post in a prefecture or county but a censorate office; look then at the present strength or weakness to judge his promotion and taking up the post; here baleful spirits are not feared, and meeting one makes one awe-inspiring, every move startling and one's wielding of power resounding.

> [!note] 按
> 「目下」断易天机本作「日下」。獬豸是传说中能分辨曲直的异兽，獬豸冠是执法监察官戴的冠。
>
> *Note:* The Duanyi Tianji text has 'ri xia' (the day) for 'mu xia' (at present). The Xiezhi is the legendary beast that tells right from wrong, and the Xiezhi cap is the cap worn by officers of law and censorship.
