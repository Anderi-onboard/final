# 征战·鬼爻暗动伤身，吴王被专诸之刺（旧注：官爻虽静而被日辰冲动旺相，克害身世，必若吴王被专诸之刺；鬼暗动而被世克，或子动来救，则如荆轲刺秦王不中反被诛）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻本来安静，却被日辰冲动、又旺相，克害世爻或世身，必定像吴王被专诸刺杀；如果鬼暗动却被世爻克住，或者子孙发动来救，就像荆轲刺秦王没有刺中，反而自己被杀。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a Pressure line is still but the Day clashes it into motion and it is thriving and injures and controls the Self or Body, it is surely like the King of Wu stabbed by Zhuanzhu; if the Pressure line moves in secret but is controlled by the Self, or Output moves to rescue, it is like Jing Ke, who failed to stab the King of Qin and was himself put to death instead.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5973 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼爻暗动伤身，吴王被专诸之刺。 | 官鬼暗中发动伤克世身，就像吴王被专诸刺杀。 | If a Pressure line moves in secret and injures and controls the Self or Body, it is like the King of Wu stabbed by Zhuanzhu. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5974 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官爻虽静，而被日辰冲动旺相，克害身世，必若吴王被专诸之刺。 | 官爻虽然安静，却被日辰冲动、又旺相，克害世爻或世身，必定像吴王被专诸刺杀。 | If a Pressure line is still but is clashed into motion by the Day and is thriving, and injures and controls the Self or Body, it is surely like the King of Wu stabbed by Zhuanzhu. |
> | 若鬼暗动，而被世克，或子动来救，则如荆何刺秦王不中，而反自被诛也。 | 如果鬼暗中发动却被世爻克住，或者子孙发动来救，就像荆轲（这里原文作「荆何」）刺秦王没有刺中，反而自己被杀。 | If the Pressure line moves in secret but is controlled by the Self, or Output moves to rescue, it is like Jing Ke (written Jing He here), who failed to stab the King of Qin and was himself put to death instead. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1557 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 旺官暗动克害世身，如吴王被专诸之刺； | 旺相的官鬼暗中发动，克害世爻或世身，就像吴王被专诸刺杀； | If a thriving Pressure line moves in secret and injures and controls the Self or Body, it is like the King of Wu stabbed by Zhuanzhu; |
> | 世克暗动之鬼或子动来救，如荆轲刺秦王不中，自反被诛也。 | 世爻克住暗动的鬼，或者子孙发动来救，就像荆轲刺秦王没有刺中，反而自己被杀。 | if the Self controls the secretly moving Pressure line, or Output moves to rescue, it is like Jing Ke, who failed to stab the King of Qin and was himself put to death instead. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注把荆轲写成「荆何」，卜筮正宗写「荆轲」。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary writes Jing Ke as 'Jing He'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong writes 'Jing Ke'.
