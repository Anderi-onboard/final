# 军机：筮城虑破鬼动鬼克则危、筮敌来期应克应动则速、筮军被困世墓世伤则凶；若患破城不拘父母反专官鬼发动克世则危

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占城池，担心被攻破，官鬼动、官鬼克世就危险；占敌人来的日期，应克应、应动就快；占军队被围困，世入墓、世受伤就凶。如果担心城被攻破，不用看父母，反而专看官鬼：官鬼发动克世就危险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about a city, if one fears it will be broken, Pressure moving or Pressure controlling the Self is dangerous; in divining the date the enemy will come, the Other controlling or the Other moving means quickly; in divining about an army that has been besieged, the Self entering the tomb or being injured is ominous. If one fears a city will be broken, one does not look at Resource but looks only at Pressure: Pressure moving and controlling the Self is dangerous.

> [!note] 按
> 「筮城虑破」是占一座城守不守得住。「应克应动则速」按文意是应爻克世、应爻发动，敌人来得快（「应克应」疑为「应克世」之误，书里就是这几个字，这里按「应克世、应动」译）。城池平常以父母为城（见易隐朝廷占城池 YY.40），这里反而专看官鬼，因为是怕城破、官鬼就是攻城的敌人。
>
> *Note:* 'Shi cheng lu po' is divining whether a city can be held. 'Ying ke ying dong ze su' is by the sense the Other controlling the Self and the Other moving, and the enemy comes quickly (the 'ying ke ying' is presumably a miswriting of 'ying ke shi', the Other controlling the Self; the book has just these characters and it is translated here as 'the Other controls the Self, the Other moves'). A city is ordinarily taken as Resource (see the divination on the city in the Yi Yin divination on the court, YY.40), but here Pressure alone is looked at, because one fears the city being broken and Pressure is the enemy attacking it.
