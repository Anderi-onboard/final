# 僧道占：父母克身师接引、青龙持世贵周全；贵临父母师通圣、福德龙交弟子贤；父母重重身受克断然身被俗家牵

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母克身，有师父接引；青龙持世，有贵人周全；贵人落在父母上，师父通达圣道；福德、青龙交动，弟子贤能；父母重重叠叠、身受克，断定是被俗家牵累。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource controlling the Body, a master receives and leads one on; the Azure Dragon holding the Self, a patron sees one through; the Noble on Resource, the master is versed in the sacred; Output and the Dragon moving together, the disciples are worthy; Resource upon Resource and the Body controlled, it is certain one is dragged back by one's lay family.
