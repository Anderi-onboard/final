# 道业总纲：孔孟之道五伦为体、经世为用，困亨夷险各尽其道无须筮；弃家入道、拔俗修真、爱网难除、至人难遇，然道有道缘业有业障亦常于筮而得之（割爱离尘何用筮？所处安危、择师邪正亦赖圣贤告我）；本章七问：择师传法、主持纲纪、募缘法会、斋戒

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 孔孟之道，以五伦为体、以经世为用；困与亨、夷与险，各有各的尽道之法，何必占筮。如果是弃家入道、超拔流俗、修行求真，爱欲的网难除，得道的人难遇，这自然是大丈夫的事，哪里同于世俗人的占筮？然而道有道缘，业有业障，也常常靠占筮得知。割断爱欲、离开尘世，一念之间就是万年，哪里用得着占筮？然而所处的安危、择师的邪正，也仍要靠圣贤告诉我。本章分七问：择师、传法、主持、纲纪、募缘、法会、斋戒。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Way of Confucius and Mencius takes the five human relations as its body and ordering the world as its use; in hardship and success, ease and danger, each has its own way of exhausting what is right, and why should one divine? But for one who leaves home to enter the Way, rises above the common run and practises to find the truth, the net of love is hard to cut, and a perfected man is hard to meet; this is of course the affair of a great man, and how could it be the same as the divining of the ordinary world? Yet the Way has its affinity for the Way, and the vocation has its karmic obstruction, and these too can often be learned by divining. Cutting off love and leaving the dust, one thought is ten thousand years, and what use is divining? Yet the safety or danger of where one lives, and whether the teacher one chooses is straight or crooked, one still relies on the sages to tell me. The chapter takes seven questions: choosing a teacher, transmitting the teaching, being abbot, being in the administration, soliciting donations, ritual assemblies and fasting.

> [!note] 按
> 「五伦」是君臣、父子、夫妇、兄弟、朋友。「经世」是治理世事。「至人」是修养到极处的人，「拔俗修真」是超脱流俗、修行求真。「爱网」是情爱的牵缠。「道缘」是和道的缘分，「业障」是前世所造的业带来的障碍。书里自注：割爱离尘的人，一念就是万年，用不着占；但所处的安危、选师的邪正，还要靠占告诉我。这一章讲的是出家修行的人（佛、道两家）的职业和处境，按职业归入事业；和世俗官职不同的是，它的用神多取世爻和福神。章首列的七问是择师、传法、主持、纲纪、募缘、法会、斋戒。
>
> *Note:* The 'five relations' are ruler and minister, father and son, husband and wife, elder and younger brother, and friend. 'Jingshi' is managing worldly affairs. A 'perfected man' is one cultivated to the utmost, and 'rising above the common run and practising to find the truth' is escaping the vulgar and cultivating the truth. The 'net of love' is the entanglement of affection. 'Affinity for the Way' is the karmic affinity with the Way, and 'karmic obstruction' is the hindrance brought by karma made in earlier lives. The book's own note: for one who has cut off love and left the dust, one thought is ten thousand years and there is no need to divine; but the safety of where one lives and whether the teacher chosen is straight or crooked still have to be told by divination. This chapter deals with the occupation and circumstances of those who have left home to practise (Buddhist and Daoist), and is placed under Career by occupation; unlike the worldly office, its Subject is mostly the Self line and the fortune spirit. The seven questions listed at the head of the chapter are choosing a teacher, transmitting the teaching, being abbot, being in the administration, soliciting donations, ritual assemblies and fasting.
