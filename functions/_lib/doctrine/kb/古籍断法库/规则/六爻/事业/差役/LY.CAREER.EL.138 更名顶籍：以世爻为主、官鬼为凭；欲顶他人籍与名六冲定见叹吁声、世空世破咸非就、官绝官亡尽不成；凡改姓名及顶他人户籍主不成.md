# 更名顶籍：以世爻为主、官鬼为凭；欲顶他人籍与名六冲定见叹吁声、世空世破咸非就、官绝官亡尽不成；凡改姓名及顶他人户籍主不成

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想顶替别人的户籍和姓名，以世爻为主，以官鬼为凭据：遇到六冲，一定听到叹息声；世爻空、世爻破，都办不成；官爻绝、官爻亡，全都不成。凡是改换姓名、顶替别人户籍的，主不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In wanting to take over another's register and name, take the Self line as the chief and Pressure as the ground: with a six-clash one will surely hear sighs; if the Self is Void or broken, in neither case is it accomplished; if the star of office is extinct or lost, it all fails. Changing one's name and taking over another's household register is judged not to succeed.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 章名在书里出现两次，头一次写作「更吊顶籍」，「吊」是「名」的误写。「顶籍」是顶替别人的户籍（名册上的名额）；「叹吁」是叹息。「凡改姓名及顶他人户籍主不成」这一句紧接在讲不成的诀后面，按上下文是对前面那几种情形的总结，书里没有写出条件，这里照字面译。这一章与谋差顶役（EL.106 起）是一类：不是顶一个役位，而是顶别人的名、籍和营业。
>
> *Note:* The chapter title is written twice in the book, the first time as 'geng diao ding ji', where the 'diao' is a miswriting of 'ming'. 'Ding ji' is taking over another's household register (a place on the roll); 'tanxu' is sighing. The sentence 'changing one's name and taking over another's household register is judged not to succeed' follows straight after the verse about failure and is by the context a summary of the cases just given; the book states no condition and it is translated literally. This chapter is of a kind with the chapters on seeking a commission and taking over a service (from EL.106): not taking over a service post but taking over another's name, register and business.
