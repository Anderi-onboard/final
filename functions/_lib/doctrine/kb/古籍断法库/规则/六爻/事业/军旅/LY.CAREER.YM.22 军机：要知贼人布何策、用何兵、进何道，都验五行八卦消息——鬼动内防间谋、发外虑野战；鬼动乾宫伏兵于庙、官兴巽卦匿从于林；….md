# 军机：要知贼人布何策、用何兵、进何道，都验五行八卦消息——鬼动内防间谋、发外虑野战；鬼动乾宫伏兵于庙、官兴巽卦匿从于林；坤鬼戒纳降、震鬼不当却叛、艮忌冈陵之困、离忧市井之攻、兑毋信其说口、坎毋听其诱语（鬼之所现即敌之所谋）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要看贼人布的什么策、用什么兵、走哪条路，我又该怎样应对、怎样克制、怎样夺取，全凭验五行八卦的消息。所以官鬼动在内卦，防间谋；发在外卦，虑野战；动在乾宫，伏兵在庙里；兴在巽卦，藏匿的随从在林中；坤宫的鬼，戒备受降；震宫的鬼，不该当作叛军已退；艮宫忌被困在冈陵；离宫忧市井受攻；兑宫不要信他的说辞；坎宫不要听他的诱语。书里自注：鬼显现之处，就是敌人谋划之处：坤是顺，鬼动则降不是真降；震是振，鬼摇则退下的叛军必变；兑是口、是悦，则说话必诈；坎是耳、是陷，则卑下的言辞必有谋。其余类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To see what plan the bandits lay, what troops they use, what road they advance by, and how I shall answer, control and take them, one tests it all by the news of the five phases and the eight trigrams. So Pressure moving in the inner trigram, guard against spies and schemes; Pressure issuing in the outer trigram, take thought for a battle in the field; Pressure moving in the palace of Qian, troops lie in ambush in the temple; Pressure rising in the trigram Xun, followers lie hidden in the woods; Pressure in Kun, be on guard against accepting surrender; Pressure in Zhen, do not take the rebels as having withdrawn; Gen, beware of being trapped on hills and mounds; Li, fear an attack in the market towns; Dui, do not believe their talk; Kan, do not listen to their enticing words. The book's own gloss: where Pressure appears is what the enemy is planning. Kun is yielding, so when Pressure moves their surrender is not a real surrender; Zhen is shaking, so when Pressure sways the rebels who stood down will surely change; Dui is mouth and delight, so the talk must be deceit; Kan is ear and pit, so humble words must hide a scheme. The rest follows by analogy.

> [!note] 按
> 「间谋」是敌方派来的内线。「伏兵于庙」「匿从于林」的庙、林，是敌人把兵藏在庙里、藏在林里。「纳降」是接受投降。「却叛」书里没有注；按它自己的注（「却叛必变」）读作叛军说已经撤了、停了，靠不住，不当作已退。「冈陵」是山冈土丘，「市井」是街市城镇。八卦的象是本章通用的：乾老、坤顺、震动、巽入、坎陷、离丽、艮止、兑悦。这一条是整章里把『鬼落在哪一宫』读成『敌人在谋什么』的一条，下一条（YM.23）接着讲五行八卦的取义。
>
> *Note:* 'Jianmou' is an inside agent sent by the enemy. The temple and woods of 'ambush in the temple' and 'followers hidden in the woods' are where the enemy hides his troops. 'Naxiang' is accepting surrender. The book does not gloss 'quepan'; following its own note ('the rebels who stood down will surely change') it is read as rebels saying they have withdrawn or stopped, which cannot be relied on, so they are not to be taken as having withdrawn. 'Gangling' is hills and mounds, and 'shijing' is markets and towns. The images of the eight trigrams are those used throughout the chapter: Qian is aged, Kun yielding, Zhen moving, Xun entering, Kan a pit, Li clinging, Gen stopping, Dui delight. This is the entry in the chapter that reads 'which palace the Pressure stands in' as 'what the enemy is planning'; the next entry (YM.23) goes on to the meanings of the five phases and the eight trigrams.
