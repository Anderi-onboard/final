# 国事：占享国几何以岁为纪，有岁则一位一元、再之亦一位一元，然必考其本宫之旺衰、鬼象之动静方可推而定；岁司年历国祚，如己亥年得丁亥太岁有六元之运，又以内卦参之、鬼动权之方可推

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占国运能延续多久，以太岁为纪年：有太岁就是一位一元，再有也是一位一元；但必须考察本宫的旺衰、官鬼之象的动静，才能推定。太岁管年历国运：比如己亥年，得丁亥太岁，有六元的运数，还要用内卦参看，以官鬼发动来权衡，才可以推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine how long the dynasty will last, take the Year as the reckoning: if the Tai Sui is present it is one position, one cycle; with another it is again one position, one cycle; but one must still examine the strength or weakness of the home palace and the movement or stillness of the Pressure image before it can be reckoned and settled. The Year governs the calendar and the dynasty's fortune: for example, in a Jihai year, getting the Dinghai Tai Sui, there is a destiny of six cycles, and one also consults the inner trigram and weighs it by Pressure moving, and only then can it be reckoned.

> [!note] 按
> 「享国」是在位、国运延续的年数。「一位一元」「六元之运」书里没有说明怎么算（「元」像是一个计年的单位），这里照字面译，不替它加解释；注里「己亥年」「丁亥太岁」的对应关系书里也没有展开。这一条与易隐朝廷占「卜世卜年」（YY.49）是同一问，但算法不同，不可混用。
>
> *Note:* 'Xiangguo' is the number of years a dynasty holds the throne and its fortune lasts. How 'one position, one cycle' and 'a destiny of six cycles' are counted is not explained in the book ('yuan' looks like a unit for counting years), so it is translated literally with no explanation added, and the book does not unfold how the 'Jihai year' and 'Dinghai Tai Sui' of the note correspond. This entry asks the same question as 'counting the reigns and the years' in the Yi Yin divination on the court (YY.49), but the method of counting differs and the two must not be mixed.
