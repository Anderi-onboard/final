# 升选候补·野鹤：古以晋、升为吉，屯、蹇为凶，晋升也要世爻得地，屯蹇世旺无妨

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看升选不能只看卦名：古人以晋、升两卦为吉，屯、蹇两卦为凶；野鹤说晋、升两卦确实屡次应验，但也要世爻得地，世爻失陷也不吉；屯、蹇两卦，只要世爻旺相就没有妨碍，他有一次世爻旺相占到屯卦，照样越级升迁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Promotion cannot be read from the hexagram name alone: the ancients took Jin and Sheng as auspicious and Zhun and Jian as ominous; the Old Man of Yehe says Jin and Sheng have indeed proved right many times, but the Self must also be well placed, and a Self that is lost or sunk is not auspicious; for Zhun and Jian there is no harm so long as the Self is thriving, and once he got Zhun with a thriving Self and was promoted beyond rank all the same.
