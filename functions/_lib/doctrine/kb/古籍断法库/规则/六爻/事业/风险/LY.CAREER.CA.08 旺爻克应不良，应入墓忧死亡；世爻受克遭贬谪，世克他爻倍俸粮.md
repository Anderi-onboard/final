# 旺爻克应不良，应入墓忧死亡；世爻受克遭贬谪，世克他爻倍俸粮

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 旺相的爻去克应，不好；应入墓的时候，要担心有人死亡；世爻被克，遭贬谪、责罚；世爻去克别的爻，俸禄加倍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A strong line controlling the Other is not good; when the Other enters its tomb, beware of death; the Self being controlled brings demotion and banishment, and the Self controlling another line doubles one's pay.

> | 世爻受克遭贬谪， | 世爻被克，会遭贬谪； | the Self line being controlled, one is demoted and exiled; |

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3748 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世受克，遭贬罚，世克他爻，主倍得俸粮。 | 世爻受克，遭贬谪责罚；世爻克他爻，主俸禄加倍。 | The Self being controlled brings demotion and punishment; the Self controlling another line means the salary is doubled. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 原文没有说在求官里「应」代表谁（上司、君、还是对方），旧注也只解释了后两句；本库照字面译，不补主语。
>
> *Note:* The text does not say whom the Other stands for in seeking office (a superior, the sovereign, or the other party), and the note explains only the last two lines; it is translated literally and no subject is supplied.
