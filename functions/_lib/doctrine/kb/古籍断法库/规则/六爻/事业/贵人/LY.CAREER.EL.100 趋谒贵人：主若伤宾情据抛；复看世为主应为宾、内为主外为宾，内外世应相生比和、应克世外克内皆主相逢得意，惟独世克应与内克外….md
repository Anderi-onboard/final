# 趋谒贵人：主若伤宾情据抛；复看世为主应为宾、内为主外为宾，内外世应相生比和、应克世外克内皆主相逢得意，惟独世克应与内克外主反触宾纵然相见亦不相投

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 主人如果伤了宾，情意就丢开了。再看世爻是主、应爻是宾，内卦是主、外卦是宾：内外卦、世应相生，或者比和，或者应克世、外克内，都主相逢称心；唯独世克应、内克外，主方反而冒犯宾方，即使见面，也不相投。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the host injures the guest, the feeling is thrown away. Look also at the Self as the host and the Other as the guest, and the inner as the host and the outer as the guest: if the inner and outer trigrams or the Self and the Other generate each other, or are in accord, or the Other controls the Self, or the outer controls the inner, in every case it means meeting to one's satisfaction; only if the Self controls the Other, or the inner controls the outer, does the host on the contrary offend the guest, and even if they meet they will not get on.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 875 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 复看世为主，应为实。 | 再看世爻是主，应爻是宾。 | Look again at the Self as the host and the Other as the guest. |
> | 又以内为主，外为实。 | 又以内卦为主，外卦为宾。 | And take the inner as the host and the outer as the guest. |
> | 如遇内外世应相生、或比和、或应克世、外克内，皆主相逢得意也。 | 如果遇到内外卦、世应相生，或者比和，或者应克世、外克内，都主相逢称意。 | If the inner and outer trigrams or the Self and the Other generate each other, or are in accord, or the Other controls the Self, or the outer controls the inner, in every case it means meeting to one's satisfaction. |
> | 惟独世克应与内克外，主反触宾，纵然相见亦不相投。 | 只有世克应和内克外，主方反而冒犯宾方，即使见面也不相投。 | Only if the Self controls the Other, or the inner controls the outer, does the host on the contrary offend the guest, and even if they meet they will not get on. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「应为实」「外为实」的「实」疑是「宾」（诀里写「主若伤宾」，解末也写「触宾」），译作「宾」。这里的「主」是去拜访的一方（世、内），「宾」是被拜访的一方（应、外）。书里不说「应克世、外克内」为什么反而得意、「世克应、内克外」为什么反而触宾，只列了结果。
>
> *Note:* The 'shi' of 'ying wei shi' and 'wai wei shi' is presumably 'bin', guest (the verse says 'if the host injures the guest' and the end of the explanation says 'offends the guest'), and it is translated 'guest'. The 'host' here is the party who goes to call (the Self, the inner) and the 'guest' is the party who is called on (the Other, the outer). The book does not say why 'the Other controls the Self, the outer controls the inner' should on the contrary be satisfactory and 'the Self controls the Other, the inner controls the outer' should on the contrary offend the guest, and gives only the results.
