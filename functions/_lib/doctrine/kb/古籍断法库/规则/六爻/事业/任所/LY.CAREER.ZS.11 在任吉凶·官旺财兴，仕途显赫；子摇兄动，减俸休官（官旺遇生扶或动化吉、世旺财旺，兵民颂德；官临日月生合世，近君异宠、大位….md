# 在任吉凶·官旺财兴，仕途显赫；子摇兄动，减俸休官（官旺遇生扶或动化吉、世旺财旺，兵民颂德；官临日月生合世，近君异宠、大位超升、外任卓异；兄持世或动，破财罚俸；子孙持世或动，有削职之忧）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任吉凶：官星旺相、妻财兴旺，仕途显赫。官星旺相又得生扶，或发动变吉，世旺财旺，或财发动生世，官民称颂，宦途平稳；官星临日月生合世爻，或三合官局生合世爻，或官星持世又得日月生扶、太岁五爻相生合，在君主身边的特别受宠，居高位的不久越级升迁，外任的被评为卓异。反过来，兄弟持世或发动，不是破财就是罚俸；子孙持世或在动爻中，有被剥官削职的忧虑，只有官星休囚而有扶、子孙发动而被制住时，才不过降级。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* How one fares in office: with the star of office thriving and Wealth flourishing, the official career is brilliant. If the star of office is thriving and gets generation and support, or moves and turns auspicious, with the Self and Wealth thriving or Wealth moving to generate the Self, soldiers and people praise his virtue and the sea of officialdom is calm; if the star of office sits on the Day or Month and generates or combines with the Self, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates or combines with the Self, or the star of office holds the Self and the Day and Month generate and support it with Tai Sui or the fifth line generating and combining, then one near the ruler receives uncommon favour, one in high office is soon promoted beyond rank, and one serving outside is rated outstanding. The reverse: if Peer holds the Self or moves, there is either a loss of goods or a fine of salary; if Output holds the Self or is among the moving lines, there is cause to fear being stripped of office and rank, and only when the star of office is resting or trapped but supported and Output moves but is restrained does it come to no more than a demotion.
