# 征战占·彼己（三）：鬼克世彼胜、鬼克应他欲退、鬼持世防围困；鬼暗动加大杀劫杀刑刃克世防刺客，遇福动必擒；日辰冲克世爻伏鬼刑害飞神防下人谋我；无子救鬼生旺日见祸；世衰鬼杀旺动军机泄露；内外两鬼动克世防内应外合；世被日辰动爻刑害克冲防奇兵；世化空死墓绝胎将不遁即死；世应皆旺看日辰生合刑害克破何爻决胜负

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼看敌情：官鬼克世爻，对方胜；官鬼克应爻，他想退兵；官鬼持世爻，防围困。官鬼暗动，加大杀、劫杀、刑、刃克世爻的，防刺客，遇福神发动，刺客必被擒。日辰冲克世爻下伏的官鬼、刑害它的飞神的，防下面的人谋害我。没有子孙发动来救，官鬼生旺的日子必定见祸。世爻衰弱而官鬼和凶煞旺相发动的，军机泄露。内外两个官鬼发动克世爻的，防内应外合；世爻被日辰、动爻刑、害、克、冲的，防奇兵冲击。世爻变成空、死、墓、绝、胎的，主将不逃走就战死。世、应都旺相的，看日辰生合刑害克破哪一爻，来决定胜负。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Reading the enemy by Pressure. If Pressure controls the Self, they win; if Pressure controls the Other, they want to withdraw; if Pressure holds the Self, guard against encirclement. If Pressure moves in secret, with a great baleful spirit, Robbery, punishment and the blade added, and controls the Self, guard against assassins, and if the fortune spirit moves, the assassin will surely be caught. If the Day clashes with or controls the Pressure line hidden under the Self, or punishes or harms its flying spirit, guard against those below plotting against me. With no Output moving to rescue, on the day Pressure is born or thriving there will surely be disaster. If the Self is weak and Pressure and baleful spirits are thriving and moving, military secrets leak. If both Pressure lines, inside and outside, move to control the Self, guard against collusion between inside and outside; if the Self is punished, harmed, controlled or clashed by the Day or a moving line, guard against a surprise force charging in. If the Self turns into Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, the commander either flees or dies. If the Self and Other are both thriving, see which line the Day generates, combines with, punishes, harms, controls or breaks, to decide victory.

> [!note] 按
> 「刺客」「奇兵」按字面译。「飞神」是伏神上面那一爻（见术语表），这里指世爻。第九句是一句话里的两个分句（内外两鬼；世被刑害克冲），中间只有逗号相连，所以放在一起。
>
> *Note:* 'Assassin' and 'surprise force' are by the letter. The 'flying spirit' is the line above a hidden spirit (see the glossary), and here it is the Self line. The ninth sentence is two clauses joined only by a comma (two Pressure lines; the Self punished and harmed), and they are therefore kept together.
