# 任乱地求身安与任危邦求爵显：任乱地随墓助伤世穷鬼发非祥；任危邦官隆世实则吉；治河防决忌鬼动鬼克、抽税征商忌应空财坏、考卷有瑕用父母、征徭无用妻财，志此用此志彼用彼

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 到乱地去任官而求身平安，随墓、助伤、世穷、鬼发，都不吉祥；到危邦去任官而求爵位显赫，官旺、世实，就吉。例如治河防决口，忌鬼动、鬼克；抽税征商，忌应空、财坏；考卷有瑕疵，用父母；征徭役，不用妻财；占的人志在这件就用这个爻，志在那件就用那个爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Taking office in a disordered region and seeking personal safety, following into the tomb, helping the injury, the Self at its end and Pressure moving are all inauspicious; taking office in a perilous state and seeking a distinguished title, thriving Pressure and a solid Self are auspicious. For example, in managing a river and guarding against a breach, Pressure moving or controlling is to be avoided; in levying taxes on merchants, a Void Other and damaged Wealth are to be avoided; a flaw in the examination papers uses Resource, and levying labour service does not use Wealth; where the diviner's aim lies, that line is used, and if the aim is elsewhere, the other line is used.

> [!note] 按
> 「乱地」是政局混乱的地方，「危邦」是危险的国家。「世穷」按文意是世爻无气、到了尽头。注里的几个例子：治河防决口怕鬼动、鬼克；抽税征商怕应空、财坏；考卷有瑕疵要用父母（父母主文书、考卷）；征徭役不用妻财（徭役出的是人力，不是钱）。总的原则是『占的人志在哪一件，就取哪一个爻』：同一个官职，所问的事不同，用爻也不同。和黄金策仕宦的抚绥百姓（HJC.22）、巡察四方（HJC.23）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Luandi' is a place where public order has broken down, and 'weibang' is a perilous state. 'Shiqiong', by the sense, is the Self line without force, come to its end. The examples in the note: in managing a river against a breach one fears Pressure moving or controlling; in levying taxes on merchants one fears a Void Other and damaged Wealth; a flaw in the examination papers uses Resource (Resource governs documents and papers); levying labour service does not use Wealth (labour service pays in manpower, not money). The general principle is 'where the diviner's aim lies, that line is taken': for the same office, the line used differs with what is asked. Read against the Huangjin Ce on pacifying the people (HJC.22) and on touring and inspecting the regions (HJC.23).
