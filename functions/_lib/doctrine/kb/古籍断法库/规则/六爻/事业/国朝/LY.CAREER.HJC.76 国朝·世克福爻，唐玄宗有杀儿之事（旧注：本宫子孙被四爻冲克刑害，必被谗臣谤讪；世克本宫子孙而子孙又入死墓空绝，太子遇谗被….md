# 国朝·世克福爻，唐玄宗有杀儿之事（旧注：本宫子孙被四爻冲克刑害，必被谗臣谤讪；世克本宫子孙而子孙又入死墓空绝，太子遇谗被害，如唐玄宗信李林甫之谮废杀太子瑛等）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本宫的子孙（太子）被第四爻冲、克、刑、害，必定被进谗的臣子诽谤；世爻克本宫的子孙，子孙又落入死、墓、空、绝，太子遭谗言陷害，就像唐玄宗听信李林甫的谗言，把太子瑛、鄂王瑶、光王琚都废为平民，后来又赐死在城东驿。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Output of the home palace (the crown prince) is clashed, controlled, punished or harmed by the fourth line, he is surely slandered by treacherous ministers; if the Self controls the Output of the home palace and that Output also falls into death, tomb, Void or extinction, the crown prince meets slander and is harmed, like Emperor Xuanzong of Tang, who trusted Li Linfu's slander, demoted Crown Prince Ying, Prince E (Yao) and Prince Guang (Ju) to commoners and later granted them death at the Chengdong post station.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1488 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若世克本宫之墓绝子孙，太子遇谗被害，如唐玄宗信李林甫谱，将太子、英鄂五瑶、光王琚皆废，复赐死。 | 如果世爻克本宫落在墓、绝上的子孙，太子遭到谗言陷害，就像唐玄宗听信李林甫的谗言，把太子、瑛、鄂王瑶、光王琚都废掉，又赐他们死。 | If the Self controls the Output of the home palace that is in tomb or extinction, the crown prince meets slander and is harmed, like Emperor Xuanzong of Tang who trusted Li Linfu's slander, deposed the crown prince, Ying, Prince E (Yao) and Prince Guang (Ju), and later granted them death. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗这一句有错字：「谮」写成了「谱」，「瑛、鄂王瑶」写成了「英鄂五瑶」；译文按旧注读。另外旧注有「四爻冲克刑害子孙」和「世克子孙」两种情形，卜筮正宗只收了后一种。
>
> *Note:* The Bu Shi Zheng Zong has miswritten characters in this sentence: 'slander' is written with a wrong character, and 'Ying and Prince E (Yao)' is written as a garbled string; the translation follows the old commentary. The old commentary also has two cases, the fourth line attacking Output and the Self controlling Output; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong keeps only the second.
