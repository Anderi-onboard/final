# 随墓章·各占之害：求官履仕途而不反、出行登平道而遭屯、行人病于他乡、产妇厄于暗室、公役有桎梏之辱、兴造有妨犯之凶、偷关逾险而蹈网罗、出族谋差而受羁绁（随墓统忌，兴造偷逾空破稍可）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> （随墓的害处）求官的人走上仕途，就回不来了；出行的人走在平路上却遇到屯难；行人病在他乡，产妇厄在暗室；当差的有枷锁之辱，兴造的有触犯妨碍之凶；偷关越险的陷入罗网，离开宗族谋差使的受羁绊。随墓是通忌；如果是兴造、偷关逾险，空、破还稍微可以。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* (The harm of following into the tomb:) one who seeks office walks the official road and does not return; one who travels walks on a level road and meets trouble; the traveller falls ill in a strange land, the woman in childbirth is in distress in a dark room; the runner suffers the disgrace of fetters, the builder meets the ill-omen of offence; one who slips past a pass and crosses a danger falls into the net, and one who leaves his clan to seek a post is bound. Following into the tomb is a taboo in all cases; but for building and for slipping past a pass and crossing a danger, Void or broken is somewhat tolerable.

> [!note] 按
> 「求官履仕途而不反」按文意是求官的人走上仕途就回不来、陷在里面（「反」读作返回），和后面「出行登平道而遭屯」（走平路遇到难）同是『随墓』的害。「公役有桎梏之辱」是当差的受枷锁之辱，「兴造」是兴建、营造，「妨犯」是触犯禁忌，「偷关逾险」是偷越关隘、翻越险阻，「出族」是离开宗族，「羁绁」是羁绊束缚。「随墓」（世爻或用神随着官鬼入墓）是本书里的大忌（见 YM.41），这一条列出了各类占问里它带来的害，前两样和后一样（求官、公役、谋差）属事业，其余分属出行、疾病、官非等域，这里整句收，以免拆开。书里自注：随墓是通忌；如果是兴造、偷关逾险，空、破还稍微可以。
>
> *Note:* 'Qiuguan lü shitu er bu fan' is, by the sense, one who seeks office walking the official road and not being able to come back, caught in it ('fan' is read as return), the same harm of 'following into the tomb' as 'walks on a level road and meets trouble' that follows. 'Gongyi you zhigu zhi ru' is a runner suffering the disgrace of fetters, 'xingzao' is building and construction, 'fangfan' is offending a taboo, 'tou guan yu xian' is slipping past a pass and crossing a danger, 'chu zu' is leaving one's clan, and 'jixie' is being bound by ties. 'Following into the tomb' (the Self line or the Subject following Pressure into the tomb) is a great taboo in this book (see YM.41), and this entry lists the harm it brings in each kind of divination: the first, the fifth and the last (seeking office, runners, seeking a post) belong to Career, and the others belong to Travel, Illness, Lawsuits and other domains; the whole sentence is taken so as not to split it. The book's own gloss: following into the tomb is a taboo in all cases; but for building and for slipping past a pass and crossing a danger, Void or broken is somewhat tolerable.
