# 官禄占·在任（七）：游魂化归魂宜致仕；世空杀动宜避罪；世持官加大杀动、鬼带亡劫动伤衰世、世身命爻俱空死墓绝者皆死于任所

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任，退休、避罪和死在任上：游魂卦变归魂卦的，适宜退休（致仕）；世爻落空、凶煞发动的，适宜避罪；世爻持官星加大杀发动，或者官鬼带亡神、劫杀发动伤到衰弱的世爻，或者世爻、身爻、命爻都遇空、死、墓、绝的，都会死在任所。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In office, retirement, evading a charge and dying at one's post. If a Wandering Soul hexagram turns into a Returning Soul hexagram, it is fitting to retire from office; if the Self is Void and a baleful spirit moves, it is fitting to evade a charge; if the Self holds the star of office and a great baleful spirit is added and moves, or Pressure carrying the Lost Spirit and Robbery moves to injure a weak Self, or the Self line, the Body line and the Life line are all Void, dead, in tomb or extinct, in every case he dies at his post.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「世空杀动」宜避罪，是黄金策仕宦章「身空杀动，避祸之征」（HJC.16）一路的说法；增删卜易（ZS.43）明说这样断不对：忌神发动而世爻落空，等到世爻出空的月份日子，必定遭它的害，怎么能避开。易隐这里没有给理由，也没有提出空的那一天。
>
> *Where they disagree:* 'The Self Void and a baleful spirit moving: evade a charge' is the same line of saying as the Huangjin Ce's 'Body Void and baleful spirit moving, a sign of avoiding misfortune' (HJC.16); the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.43) says plainly that this is wrong to judge so: when the Adversary moves and the Self is Void, the month or day when the Self leaves Void will surely bring its harm, so how could one avoid it. The Yi Yin gives no reason here and does not mention the day of leaving Void.

> [!note] 按
> 「致仕」是官员退休，「避罪」是躲开罪责。「大杀」「亡劫」是神煞的统称和简称（亡神、劫杀）。「命爻」书里没有说是哪一爻。游魂、归魂是八宫卦的两种位置（定义见术语表）。附断例的卦「归妹之泰」，书里说午化入戌、丑化入辰，译文照字面译，没有重排卦核对。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhishi' is an official's retirement and 'bizui' is avoiding blame. 'Great baleful spirit' and 'Wangjie' are a general name and an abbreviation for spirit-stars (the Lost Spirit and Robbery). The book does not say which line is the 'Life line'. The Wandering Soul and Returning Soul are two positions among the hexagrams of the eight palaces (defined in the glossary). For the example hexagram 'Guimei to Tai' the book says that Wu turns into Xu and Chou into Chen; the translation follows the letter and has not recast the hexagram to check.
