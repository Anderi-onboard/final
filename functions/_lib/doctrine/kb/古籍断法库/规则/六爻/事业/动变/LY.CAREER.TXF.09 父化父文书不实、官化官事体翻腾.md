# 父化父文书不实、官化官事体翻腾

六爻 · 事业 · 动变 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母化父母，文书是虚的、不实在；官鬼化官鬼，事情翻腾反复。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource turning into Resource, the documents are hollow and not solid; Pressure turning into Pressure, the matter churns and reverses.

## 《天玄赋·求仕章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4400 行 · Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking office", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父化父，文书不实； | 父化父，文书虚而不实； | Resource turning into Resource, the documents are not real; |
> | 官化官，事体翻腾。 | 官化官，事情翻腾反复。 | Pressure turning into Pressure, the matter is tossed over and over. |
