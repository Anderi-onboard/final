# 问文官武职，求官必须用唐符、国印：按太岁年支取——寅申年巳亥、巳亥年寅申、子午年卯酉、卯酉年子午、辰戌年丑未、丑未年辰戌

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 不论文官武职，求官都要看唐符、国印。按年支取：寅、申年，巳是唐符、亥是国印（旧注）；巳、亥年取寅、申；子、午年取卯、酉；卯、酉年取子、午；辰、戌年取丑、未；丑、未年取辰、戌。旧注说「以下类推」，这里按此读成每一对里前一支是唐符、后一支是国印。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether it is a civil or a military post, in seeking office one must look to Tangfu and the State Seal. By the year branch: in the years Yin and Shen, Si is the Tangfu and Hai the State Seal (the note); in Si and Hai years take Yin and Shen; in Zi and Wu years, Mao and You; in Mao and You years, Zi and Wu; in Chen and Xu years, Chou and Wei; in Chou and Wei years, Chen and Xu. The note says the rest follow by analogy, which is read here as the first branch of each pair being the Tangfu and the second the State Seal.
