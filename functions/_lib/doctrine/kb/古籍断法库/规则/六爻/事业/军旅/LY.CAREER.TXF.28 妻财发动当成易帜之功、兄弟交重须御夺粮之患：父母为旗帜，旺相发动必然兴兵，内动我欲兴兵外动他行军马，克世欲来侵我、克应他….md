# 妻财发动当成易帜之功、兄弟交重须御夺粮之患：父母为旗帜，旺相发动必然兴兵，内动我欲兴兵外动他行军马，克世欲来侵我、克应他必自退；父母不动妻财动当有夺旗易帜之事，财内动克外之父母我夺彼旗、财外动克内之父母彼夺我旗；财为粮草，无财必绝粮、财空粮欲缺、财无气粮不多、财动送粮与贼；兄弟交重须防劫粮夺草

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财发动，该成夺旗易帜之功；兄弟交重，要防夺粮的祸患。父母是旗帜，旺相发动必然兴兵：在内卦动，是我方要兴兵；在外卦动，是对方行军出马；克世，是要来侵犯我；克应，是回头的旌旗，对方必自行退兵。父母不动而妻财动，该有夺旗易帜的事：财在内动克外面的父母，我方必夺对方的旌旗；妻财在外动克里面的父母，对方将夺我方的旗旌。财是粮草：卦里没有财必断粮，财空粮食将要缺乏，财无气粮食不多，财动是把粮食送给贼寇；若兄弟交重，要防备劫粮夺草。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Wealth moving, the merit of seizing the banners and changing the standards is to be achieved; with Peer moving heavily, one must guard against the trouble of grain being seized. Resource is the banner; strong and moving it surely means troops are raised: moving inside, we wish to raise troops; moving outside, they send out their army and horses; controlling the Self, they mean to invade us; controlling the Other, it is a banner turned back, and they will surely withdraw of themselves. If Resource does not move and Wealth moves, there will be the seizing of banners and changing of standards: Wealth moving inside and controlling Resource outside, we shall surely seize their banners; Wealth moving outside and controlling Resource inside, they will seize ours. Wealth is the grain and fodder: with no Wealth in the hexagram the grain is sure to be cut off; with Wealth void grain is about to run short; with Wealth lacking qi there is not much grain; with Wealth moving, grain is sent to the enemy; and if Peer moves heavily, guard against the raiding of grain and seizing of fodder.
