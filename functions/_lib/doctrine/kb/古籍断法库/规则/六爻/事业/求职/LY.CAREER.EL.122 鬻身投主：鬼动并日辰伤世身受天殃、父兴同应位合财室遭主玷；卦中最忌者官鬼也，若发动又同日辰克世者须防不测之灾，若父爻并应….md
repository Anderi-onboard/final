# 鬻身投主：鬼动并日辰伤世身受天殃、父兴同应位合财室遭主玷；卦中最忌者官鬼也，若发动又同日辰克世者须防不测之灾，若父爻并应爻合财者则主婢淫混

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动、并同日辰一起伤克世爻、身爻，会遭天降的灾殃；父爻兴旺、与应位一起合财爻（妻室），会遭主人玷污。卦中最忌的是官鬼：如果它发动、又同日辰一起克世，须防不测的灾祸。财就是妻：如果父爻和应爻一起合财爻，就是主人和婢女淫乱混杂。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure moves and, together with the Day, hurts the Self or the Body, one suffers a calamity sent by heaven; if Resource flourishes and, together with the Other's position, combines with Wealth, the wife line, one suffers dishonour from the master. What is most feared in the hexagram is Pressure: if it moves and, together with the Day, controls the Self, one must guard against an unforeseen disaster. Wealth is the wife: if the Resource line and the Other line together combine with Wealth, it means lewd mixing between the master and the maidservant.

> [!note] 按
> 「遭主玷」「主婢淫混」讲的是主人与家里妇女（财爻代表的妻室、婢女）之间的不端行为；书里只此一句，没有展开。官鬼在这一章是灾祸之象。「同日辰」是跟日辰一道。
>
> *Note:* 'Zao zhu dian' and 'zhu bi yin hun' speak of improper conduct between the master and the women of the household (the wife and maidservants whom the Wealth line stands for); the book says only this and does not go into it. Pressure in this chapter is the image of calamity. 'Tong richen' is together with the Day.
