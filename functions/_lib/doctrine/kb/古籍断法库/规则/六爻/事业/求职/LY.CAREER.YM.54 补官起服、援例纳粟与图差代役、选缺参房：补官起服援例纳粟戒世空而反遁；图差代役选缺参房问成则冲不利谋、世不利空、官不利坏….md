# 补官起服、援例纳粟与图差代役、选缺参房：补官起服援例纳粟戒世空而反遁；图差代役选缺参房问成则冲不利谋、世不利空、官不利坏，问财不利财虚，问辱不利官克世

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 补授官职、服满起复、依例捐纳的，要戒备世空，反而退缩不成。图差使、代役、选缺、参房，问成不成：冲不利图谋，世不利空，官不利坏；问财，就不利财虚；问受辱，就不利官克世。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For being appointed to a vacant post, being recalled after mourning, or buying office by precedent, beware of the Self being Void, for one will on the contrary draw back. For seeking a commission, taking another's place in a duty, selecting a vacancy or entering the clerks' office, in asking whether it comes off: clash is unfavourable to the plan, Void is unfavourable to the Self, damage is unfavourable to Pressure; in asking about money, empty Wealth is unfavourable; in asking about disgrace, it is unfavourable for Pressure to control the Self.

> [!note] 按
> 「补官」是补授官职（补缺），「起服」是守丧期满起用（起复），「援例纳粟」是依成例捐纳、出钱买官。「图差」是谋差使，「代役」是顶替差役，「选缺」是选官补缺，「参房」是吏员入衙门房科当差（这几样见易林补遗选缺参房 EL.76–78、谋差顶役 EL.106–108）。「反遁」的「遁」按文意是动词，退缩落空，不是遁卦。「问辱」是问会不会受辱，也就是受责罚。这一条把几种求职的小占问合在一起，断法都落在常见的几个忌上：冲、世空、官坏、财虚、官克世。
>
> *Note:* 'Bu guan' is being appointed to a post, filling a vacancy; 'qifu' is being recalled to office when the period of mourning is over; 'yuanli nasu' is buying office by contribution under a standing precedent. 'Tuchai' is seeking a commission, 'daiyi' is taking the place of a runner, 'xuanque' is selecting an official to fill a vacancy, and 'canfang' is a clerk entering a department of a yamen to serve (these are in the Yilin Buyi on selecting vacancies, EL.76-78, and on seeking commissions, EL.106-108). The 'dun' of 'fan dun' is by the sense a verb, drawing back and coming to nothing, and is not the hexagram Dun. 'Wen ru' is asking whether one will suffer disgrace, that is, reproof or penalty. This entry puts together several small divinations about seeking a post, and judges them by the familiar taboos: clash, a Void Self, damaged Pressure, empty Wealth, Pressure controlling the Self.
