# 求馆·如索束修，可把妻财推究；若居伏地，还求朋友维持（旧注：索束修以财爻为主；不出现不拘伏何爻下皆费力难索，须浼朋友同行取讨；伏藏须月日动爻提起；王洪绪：财出现旺相不被伤克则不欠缺，被克或绝或空或墓皆不遂意）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要讨取束修，以财爻为主。财爻不出现，不管伏在哪个爻下，都主费力难讨，必须央求朋友一同去讨，也许才能得到，因为伏藏的爻必须有月建、日辰、动爻把它提起来，像飞神一样，然后才能出来起作用；财爻出现旺相，月建日辰动爻不来伤克，就不会欠缺；财爻出现却被克，或者绝、空、入墓，都不如意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To collect the tuition fee, Wealth is the main line. If Wealth does not appear, whatever line it lies hidden under, collecting is hard work, and one must beg friends to go along and ask for it, and then it may perhaps be had, for a hidden line must be drawn up by the Month, the Day or a moving line, like a flying line, before it can come out and act; if Wealth appears and is thriving, and the Month, the Day and the moving lines do not injure or control it, there will be no shortfall; if Wealth appears but is controlled, or in extinction, Void or in a tomb, nothing is as one wishes.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2789 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占取索束修，得财爻出现旺相，月建日辰动爻不来伤克，则不欠缺； | 凡占讨取束修，得到财爻出现旺相，月建、日辰、动爻不来伤克，就不会欠缺； | In divining the collecting of the tuition fee, if Wealth appears and is thriving, and the Month, the Day and the moving lines do not come to injure or control it, there will be no shortfall; |
> | 若财出现被克，或绝或空或墓，主皆不遂意也。 | 如果财爻出现却被克，或者绝、或者空、或者入墓，主都不如意。 | if Wealth appears but is controlled, or in extinction, or Void, or in a tomb, nothing is as one wishes. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「维持」是帮忙撑持。伏神要「提起」才能起作用（月建日辰动爻把它冲、生、合起来），这是通用的规矩，在别的章里也常见。卜筮正宗没有 LY.CAREER.HJC.56 那一联的经文，王洪绪这一段把「出现不伤」那一联的意思也包括了。
>
> *Note:* 'Help' is helping to prop up. A hidden line must be 'drawn up' (clashed, generated or combined up by the Month, the Day or a moving line) before it can act, a general rule that is also often met in other chapters. The Bushi Zhengzong does not carry the verse of the couplet in LY.CAREER.HJC.56, and Wang Hongxu's passage here also covers the sense of the couplet 'appearing and not injured'.
