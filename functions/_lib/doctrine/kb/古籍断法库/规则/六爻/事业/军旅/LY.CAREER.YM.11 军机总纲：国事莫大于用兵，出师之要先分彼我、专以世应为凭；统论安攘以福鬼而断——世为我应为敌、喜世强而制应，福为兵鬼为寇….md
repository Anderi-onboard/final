# 军机总纲：国事莫大于用兵，出师之要先分彼我、专以世应为凭；统论安攘以福鬼而断——世为我应为敌、喜世强而制应，福为兵鬼为寇、宜旺而克官；世克应则胜、应克世则负、比和则胜负相等，世坏虽克应亦曳甲而归、应坏虽克世亦夺旗而返

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国事再大，没有比用兵更大的，古代圣王对它很慎重：是尽人力来应天功，也曾用蓍草来谋划（对外之事）。出师的要点，是先分清彼此，形成主客，专以世应为凭据。统论安内攘外，就用福神和官鬼来断：世是我、应是敌，喜欢世强而制住应；福是兵、鬼是寇，宜福旺而克住官鬼，朝廷谋划的胜负在这里就看得出来了。兵占先看世应：世克应就胜，应克世就负，世应比和就胜负相等；世生应的，我方怯弱；应生世的，对方退让。再看子孙是我的兵，旺相发动就勇往直前，休囚空破就不能奋进；官鬼是对方的贼寇，旺相就强横，休囚就溃散。如果世坏，即使克应、动子孙，也只能抛甲而归；如果应坏，即使克世、动官鬼，也夺旗而返。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Of all affairs of state none is greater than the use of troops, and the sage kings of old were careful about it: one exhausts what men can do to answer Heaven's work, and has also consulted the stalks when planning matters outside. The essential in sending out troops is first to distinguish them from us, to form host and guest, and to rely wholly on the Self and the Other. In judging both pacification and repelling, decide by Output and Pressure: the Self is me and the Other is the enemy, and one is glad for the Self to be strong and to control the Other; Output is the troops and Pressure is the bandits, and it is fitting for Output to be thriving and to control Pressure, and the court's planning of victory and defeat is seen in this. In divining about the army, first look at the Self and the Other: if the Self controls the Other one wins, if the Other controls the Self one loses, and if the Self and the Other are in accord, victory and defeat are even; if the Self generates the Other, my side is timid; if the Other generates the Self, the other side gives way. Then look at Output as my troops: if thriving and moving they press bravely forward, if resting, imprisoned, Void or broken they cannot press on; Pressure is the other side's bandits: if thriving they are violent and overbearing, if resting or imprisoned they are routed. If the Self is damaged, even if one controls the Other and moves Output one can only drag one's armour and come home; if the Other is damaged, even if it controls the Self and one moves Pressure, one still seizes their banner and returns.

> [!note] 按
> 「安攘」是安内攘外。「作外」是处理对外的事。「庙算」是朝廷的谋划。「胜岁等」疑是「胜负等」；「休囚空破则不摒（？）」的「摒」字书里自己标了问号，疑是「不振」，按文意译作「不能奋进」。「曵甲」是拖着甲胄，形容败退。这一条与易林补遗出师征伐「世应」一组（EL.12 起）、易隐征战占「彼己」（YY.54）、黄金策「观世应之旺衰」（HJC.86）取同一套：世是我、应是彼、子孙是兵、官鬼是敌。
>
> *Note:* 'Anrang' is pacifying within and repelling without. 'Zuo wai' is handling external affairs. 'Miaosuan' is the court's planning. 'Sheng sui deng' is presumably 'sheng fu deng', victory and defeat even; the 'bing' of 'xiuqiu kong po ze bu bing(?)' carries a question mark in the book itself and is presumably 'zhen', and by the sense it is translated 'cannot press on'. 'Yejia' is dragging one's armour, describing a retreat in defeat. This entry takes the same set as the Self and Other group in the Yilin Buyi on sending out troops (from EL.12), the 'we and they' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.54), and 'watch the strength of the Self and the Other' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.86): the Self is me, the Other is the enemy, Output is the troops and Pressure is the enemy.
