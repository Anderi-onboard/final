# 仕宦·日辰冲克，定然诽谤之多招（旧注：日辰刑冲克世必招诽谤，依五类推：带兄弟贪贿赂或征科太急、带财无调度、带子孙嗜酒好游、带父母事繁剧、带官鬼酷刑或同僚不和；世临月建则无害）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰刑冲克世爻，必定招来诽谤，按带的是哪一类六亲来推：带兄弟是因为贪贿赂或者征税太急，带财爻是因为调度无方或者赋税收不起来，带子孙是嗜酒好游、疏于政事，带父母是事务繁重料理不过来，带官鬼是酷刑或者同僚不和。以上都招诽谤，声名不能振作；如果世爻临月建，即使有诽谤也不能为害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Day punishes, clashes or controls the Self, slander will surely be invited, and one infers by which relation it carries: with Peer it is because of greed for bribes or collecting taxes too hastily, with Wealth because of poor management or revenue that cannot be raised, with Output because of fondness for wine and wandering and neglect of government, with Resource because affairs are heavy and cannot be managed, with Pressure because of harsh punishments or discord among colleagues. All of these invite slander and one's reputation cannot rise; but if the Self rests on the Month, even though there is slander it cannot do harm.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「带兄弟」那一类，两家说得不一样：旧注说带兄弟是贪图贿赂、征税太急，而把「嗜酒好游，疏于政事」归给子孙；王洪绪却说带兄弟是「贪酒好游，疏于政事」，而且他只列了兄弟、父母、官鬼三类，没有提财爻和子孙。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* On what carrying Peer means, the two commentators differ: the old commentary says that with Peer it is greed for bribes and over-hasty tax collecting and gives 'fondness for wine and wandering and neglect of government' to Output, while Wang Hongxu says that with Peer it is 'greed for drink and wandering and neglect of the business of government', and lists only three kinds, Peer, Resource and Pressure, saying nothing of Wealth or Output. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「依五类推之」的「五类」是五种六亲（兄弟、财、子孙、父母、官鬼）。「征科」是征收赋税，「调度」是筹措调配钱粮。这一联是「日辰克世」的一个具体判据，和 LY.CAREER.HJC.15（太岁加刑）是同一类：都是上面的人（日辰、太岁）压到世爻上，招来的是诽谤、贬责。
>
> *Note:* 'The five kinds' in 'infer by the five kinds' are the five relations (Peer, Wealth, Output, Resource and Pressure). 'Collecting taxes' is levying taxes and 'management' is raising and allotting money and grain. This couplet is one concrete criterion under 'the Day controls the Self', of the same kind as LY.CAREER.HJC.15 (the Tai Sui with punishment added): in both something above (the Day, the Tai Sui) presses on the Self and what it brings is slander and rebuke.
