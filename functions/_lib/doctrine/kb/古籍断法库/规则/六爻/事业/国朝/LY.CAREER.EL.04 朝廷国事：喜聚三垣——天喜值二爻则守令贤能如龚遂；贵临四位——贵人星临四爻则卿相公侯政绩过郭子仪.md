# 朝廷国事：喜聚三垣——天喜值二爻则守令贤能如龚遂；贵临四位——贵人星临四爻则卿相公侯政绩过郭子仪

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 喜神聚在三垣，郡守县令的贤能可比渤海的龚遂：喜，就是天喜，如果值在二爻，郡守、县令的贤能就和汉代的龚遂一样，龚遂做渤海太守时，教化百姓卖掉刀剑去买耕牛。贵人临在四爻，公侯的功绩超过汾阳王：贵，就是贵人星，如果临在四爻，卿相公侯的政绩就超过唐代的郭子仪，他以文武全才辅佐肃宗收复西京，立下中兴的伟绩，受封汾阳王。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Joy gathers at the Three Enclosures, the worth of prefects and magistrates equals Gong Sui of Bohai: 'Joy' is the Heavenly Joy, and if it sits on the second line the ability of prefects and magistrates is like that of Gong Sui of Han, who as prefect of Bohai taught the people to sell their swords and buy oxen. If the Noble sits on the fourth line, the achievements of dukes and marquises surpass the Prince of Fenyang: 'the Noble' is the Noble star, and if it sits on the fourth line the governing record of ministers and marquises exceeds that of Guo Ziyi of the Tang, who with his gifts in both letters and arms helped Emperor Suzong recover the western capital, achieved the great work of the restoration, and was enfeoffed Prince of Fenyang.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 贵者，贵人星也，若临四爻，则卿相公侯之政绩过子唐之郭令公子仪，郭以文武全才辅佐肃宗克复西京，中兴伟绩，赐爵汾阳王故云。 | 贵，就是贵人星；如果临在四爻，卿相公侯的政绩就超过唐代的郭令公子仪；郭子仪以文武全才辅佐肃宗收复西京，立下中兴的伟绩，受封汾阳王，所以这样说。 | 'The Noble' is the Noble star; if it sits on the fourth line, the governing record of ministers and marquises exceeds that of the Tang Duke Guo Ziyi, who with his gifts in both letters and arms helped Emperor Suzong recover the western capital, achieved the great work of the restoration, and was enfeoffed Prince of Fenyang, and that is why it is said so. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「三垣」是紫微垣、太微垣、天市垣三个星区；这一联只说「喜聚三垣」，解里只解释了喜是天喜、值二爻，没有再解释三垣，译文照字面。「固守邑令」疑是「郡守邑令」之误，译文按「郡守、县令」译；「过子唐之郭令公子仪」疑是「过于唐之郭令公子仪」，译文按此译。龚遂是汉宣帝时的渤海太守，郭子仪是唐代名将、封汾阳王，这两个典故书里自己写了出来。
>
> *Note:* 'The Three Enclosures' are the three star regions Ziwei, Taiwei and Tianshi; the couplet says only 'Joy gathers at the Three Enclosures', and the explanation only explains that Joy is Heavenly Joy sitting on the second line, and does not explain the Three Enclosures further; the translation follows the letter. 'Gu shou yi ling' is presumably a miswriting of 'jun shou yi ling', and the translation reads 'prefects and magistrates'; 'guo zi Tang zhi Guo ling gong Ziyi' is presumably 'guo yu Tang zhi Guo ling gong Ziyi', surpassing the Tang Duke Guo Ziyi, and is translated so. Gong Sui was prefect of Bohai under Emperor Xuan of the Han, and Guo Ziyi was a famous Tang general enfeoffed Prince of Fenyang; the book writes out both allusions itself.
