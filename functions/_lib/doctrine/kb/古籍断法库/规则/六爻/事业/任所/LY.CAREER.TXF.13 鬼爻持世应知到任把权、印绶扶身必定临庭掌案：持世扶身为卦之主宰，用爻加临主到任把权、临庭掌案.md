# 鬼爻持世应知到任把权、印绶扶身必定临庭掌案：持世扶身为卦之主宰，用爻加临主到任把权、临庭掌案

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻持世，应当知道到任后要掌权；印绶（父母）扶身，必定临堂掌管案牍。官鬼持世、印绶扶身是卦的主宰，再有用爻加在上面，一定到任把权、临堂掌案。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Pressure line holding the Self, know that on taking up the post one will hold power; with the seal and ribbon (Resource) supporting the self, one will surely sit in court and take charge of the cases. Pressure holding the Self and the seal supporting the self are the masters of the hexagram, and if the Subject line is added to them one surely takes power on taking up the post and presides in court over the cases.

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全本这条注只说「持世扶身，卦之主宰」，没点明是谁持世扶身；断易天机本补成「鬼爻持世，印绶扶身，卦之主宰」，与赋文对上。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan note says only 'holding the Self and supporting the self are the masters of the hexagram' without saying who holds and supports; the Duanyi Tianji text fills it out as 'the Pressure line holding the Self and the seal supporting the self', matching the rhapsody.
