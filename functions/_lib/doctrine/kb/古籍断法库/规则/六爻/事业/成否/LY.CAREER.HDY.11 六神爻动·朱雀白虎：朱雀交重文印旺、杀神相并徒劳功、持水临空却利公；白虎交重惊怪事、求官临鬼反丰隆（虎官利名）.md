# 六神爻动·朱雀白虎：朱雀交重文印旺、杀神相并徒劳功、持水临空却利公；白虎交重惊怪事、求官临鬼反丰隆（虎官利名）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 朱雀交重发动，文书印绶旺（朱雀属火）；和杀神并在一起，就白劳功夫（并上凶神就没有文）；是非口舌都由此而来；持水、临空，反而有利于公事。白虎交重，是惊恐怪异之事（白虎属金）；求官时临在官鬼上，反而丰盛隆盛（虎与官有利于名）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Vermilion Bird moving heavily, the documents and the seal and ribbon are strong (the Vermilion Bird belongs to Fire); joined with a baleful spirit, the labour is spent in vain (joined to an ill spirit there are no documents); quarrels and gossip all come from this; holding Water or standing in the void, it is on the contrary favourable to public business. With the White Tiger moving heavily it is fright and strange things (the White Tiger belongs to Metal); in seeking office, standing on Pressure, it brings abundance and prosperity on the contrary (the Tiger and Pressure favour a name).

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 白虎一般主凶，这里却说白虎交重、临官鬼时求官反而「丰隆」，是原文的说法，本库照录，不改；周易尚占里有同一句（「求官临鬼反丰隆」）。
>
> *Note:* The White Tiger generally stands for ill, yet here the White Tiger moving heavily on Pressure is said on the contrary to bring 'abundance' in seeking office, as the text has it; it is recorded as it stands and not altered, and the same line is in the Zhouyi Shangzhan ('seeking office on Pressure brings abundance on the contrary').
