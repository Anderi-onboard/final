# 官禄占·何官（四）：世持官贵禄而无印绶是佐贰；世持衰官得日辰动财生合或伏财化财是仓场府库驿典之官；世持官印而无财非正选而差委之官；官衰印旺是署印官

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻持官星、贵人、禄而没有印绶（父母），是佐贰官；世爻持衰弱的官星，得到日辰、动爻的财来生合，或者财伏藏、化出财的，是管仓场、府库、驿站这类的官；世爻持官星、印星而没有财的，不是正式选授的官，而是差遣委任的官（筮诀说，卦里若没有财，必定不是正式选授）；官星衰弱、印星旺相的，是代理掌印的官。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self holds the star of office, the Noble and Emolument but has no seal and ribbon (Resource), it is an assistant official; if the Self holds a weak star of office and Wealth from the Day or a moving line generates or combines with it, or Wealth lies hidden or turns into Wealth, it is an official of the granaries, treasuries, post stations and the like; if the Self holds the star of office and seal but has no Wealth, it is not a regularly selected official but one dispatched or commissioned (the Divination Secret says: if the hexagram has no Wealth, it is surely not a regular selection); if the star of office is weak and the seal thriving, he is an acting seal-holder.
