# 谋差顶役：财空鬼弱兄爻动但得虚名利不昌、世不逢冲官不绝差成役就乐欢肠；财落空中官居衰地事虽见就必主无财，卦中纵有衰财或逢兄动亦不为祥，世象不冲不破官爻不绝不空便言谋中得成求财得利

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻落空、官鬼衰弱、兄爻发动，只得到虚名，利益不旺；世爻不遇冲、官爻不绝，差使办成、役位到手，心里欢喜。财爻落在空中、官爻居衰地，事情虽看似成了，一定没有钱财；卦中即使有衰弱的财爻，或者遇到兄动，也不吉祥；世爻不冲不破、官爻不绝不空，就可以说谋求能成、求财得利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Wealth Void, Pressure weak and the Peer line moving, one gets only an empty name and profit does not flourish; if the Self does not meet a clash and the star of office is not extinct, the commission is won and the service is taken up, and the heart is glad. If Wealth falls into Void and the star of office sits in a weak place, then although the matter seems to be accomplished there will surely be no money; even if there is a weak Wealth line in the hexagram, or Peer moves, it is not auspicious; if the Self image is not clashed or broken and the Pressure line is not extinct or Void, one may say that the scheme will succeed and that seeking wealth will bring gain.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是谋差顶役一章的收尾：要有名（官）也要有利（财），财空鬼弱兄动是「有名无利」。「欢肠」是心里欢喜。
>
> *Note:* This entry closes the chapter on seeking a commission: one needs both name (the star of office) and profit (Wealth), and Wealth Void, Pressure weak and Peer moving is 'a name without profit'. 'Huanchang' is a glad heart.
