# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（二）：鬼临身世、卦爻旺相得官真而欢欣；驿马空亡徒费力、卦遭胎没枉劳神；旺爻克应不良、应入墓忧死亡；世爻受克遭贬谪、若克他人倍俸粮

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼落在身、世上，卦里各爻旺相，得官是真，欢喜；驿马落空亡，白费力气；卦遇胎、没，白白劳神。旺相的爻克应，不好；应入墓的时候，要担心死亡；世爻被克，会遭贬谪；如果世爻去克别人，俸禄加倍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure on the Body or the Self with the lines of the hexagram strong and thriving, the office is truly gained and there is joy; the Post Horse Void, effort is spent for nothing; the hexagram meeting Embryo or Sunk, the mind is wearied in vain. A strong line controlling the Other is not good; when the Other enters its tomb, worry about death; the Self line being controlled, one is demoted and exiled; if the Self controls others, the salary is doubled.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全本作「卦爻旺相为欢欣」「若克他爻倍俸粮」，断易天机本作「卦爻旺相甚欢欣」「若克他人倍俸粮」，意思没有差别。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan text reads 'the lines strong, there is joy' and 'if it controls another line, the salary is doubled'; the Duanyi Tianji text reads 'the lines strong, great joy' and 'if it controls others, the salary is doubled'; the sense is the same.
