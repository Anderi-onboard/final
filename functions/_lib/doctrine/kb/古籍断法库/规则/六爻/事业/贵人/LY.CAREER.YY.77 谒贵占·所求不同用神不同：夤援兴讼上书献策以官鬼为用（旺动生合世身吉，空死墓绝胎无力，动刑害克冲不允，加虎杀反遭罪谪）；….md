# 谒贵占·所求不同用神不同：夤援兴讼上书献策以官鬼为用（旺动生合世身吉，空死墓绝胎无力，动刑害克冲不允，加虎杀反遭罪谪）；息讼释罪以子孙为用；求书恳荐以父母为用（旺动生合身世有，空死墓绝胎无，刑害克破有若无）；财持世旺动、财独发求必不得

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 所求的事不同，取的用神也不同：为攀援求助、打官司，以及上书献策而去谒见贵人的，以官鬼为用神——官鬼旺相发动、生合世爻、身爻的，吉；官鬼逢空、死、墓、绝、胎的，没有力量；官鬼发动来刑、害、克、冲身爻、世爻的，不允；加白虎、凶煞来伤的，不但不允，反而遭到罪责贬谪。为平息官司、开脱罪名而谒见贵人的，以子孙为用神——应爻持子孙旺相发动，带喝散、解神生合世爻的，或者世爻持子孙，或者月建、日辰是子孙来生合世爻的，吉；应爻上的子孙逢空、死、墓、绝、胎，或被刑、害、克、冲的，没有力量。为求书信、恳求荐举而谒见贵人的，以父母为用神——官鬼旺相发动、生合父爻，以及父爻旺相发动、生合身爻、世爻的，有；父爻逢空、死、墓、绝、胎的，没有；父爻被刑、害、克、破的，有也像没有一样。财爻持世旺相发动，以及财爻单独发动的，所求必定得不到，得到也没有力量。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* What is asked decides the Subject. For one calling on a noble to scheme for help, to sue, or to present a memorial with proposals, take Pressure as the Subject: if Pressure is thriving and moving and generates or combines with the Self and Body, it is auspicious; if Pressure meets Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, it has no strength; if Pressure moves to punish, harm, control or clash with the Body and Self, it is not granted; with the Tiger and a baleful spirit coming to injure, not only is it not granted, but he is instead blamed and demoted. For one calling on a noble to end a lawsuit and clear a charge, take Output as the Subject: if the Other holds Output, thriving and moving, with Hesan and Jiexie generating or combining with the Self, or the Self holds Output, or the Month or Day is Output coming to generate or combine with the Self, it is auspicious; if Output on the Other meets Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, or is punished, harmed, controlled or clashed, it has no strength. For one calling on a noble to ask for a letter or beg for a recommendation, take Resource as the Subject: if Pressure is thriving and moving and generates or combines with Resource, or Resource is thriving and moving and generates or combines with the Body and Self, there will be one; if Resource meets Void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, there will be none; if Resource is punished, harmed, controlled or broken, it is as if there were none though there is. If Wealth holds the Self and is thriving and moving, or if the Wealth line alone moves, what is asked will surely not be had, and if had it will have no force.

> [!note] 按
> 「寅援」即「夤缘」，攀附权贵、借助关系；「罪谪」是治罪和贬官。「喝散」「解神」是神煞，书里没有解释，译文只音译。「世持子孙」「月日是子孙」按字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Yinyuan' is 'yinyuan', climbing up through connections with the powerful; 'zuizhe' is punishment and demotion. Hesan and Jiexie are spirit-stars that the book does not explain, and the translation only gives their sounds. 'The Self holds Output' and 'the Month or Day is Output' are by the letter.
