# 断易总论·公用以官鬼为尊：父母辅佐官鬼、子孙辅佐妻财；私用妻财为主、公用官鬼为尊，最忌动摇大宜安静；兴讼求名须凭官鬼

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母帮着官鬼，子孙帮着妻财；私事用妻财做主，公事用官鬼为尊，官鬼最怕乱动、最适宜安静。要打官司、求功名，必须凭官鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource assists Pressure, and Output assists Wealth. For private matters Wealth is the chief; for public matters Pressure is the honoured one, which above all fears moving about and is best still. To start a suit or to seek fame one must depend on Pressure.

> [!note] 按
> 「公用」「私用」是公事、私事：求官、官司是公事，取官鬼；求财、买卖是私事，取妻财。「兴讼求名」和上一句「散官治病，全赖子孙」是一对，那一句讲疾病，放在疾病域（LY.ILL.BSQS.22）。
>
> *Note:* "Public use" and "private use" mean public and private matters: seeking office and lawsuits are public and take Pressure; seeking wealth and trade are private and take Wealth. "Start a suit or seek fame" is paired with the previous line, "to disperse official matters and cure illness rely wholly on Output", which is about illness and is in the illness domain (LY.ILL.BSQS.22).
