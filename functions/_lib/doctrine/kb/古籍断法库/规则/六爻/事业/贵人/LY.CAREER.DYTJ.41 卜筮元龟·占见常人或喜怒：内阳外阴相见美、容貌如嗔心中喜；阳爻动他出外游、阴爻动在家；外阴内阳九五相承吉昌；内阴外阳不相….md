# 卜筮元龟·占见常人或喜怒：内阳外阴相见美、容貌如嗔心中喜；阳爻动他出外游、阴爻动在家；外阴内阳九五相承吉昌；内阴外阳不相见；注：内为心外为貌、阴主不见阳主见、阴主喜阳主嗔

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占见平常人是喜是怒：内卦阳、外卦阴，相见美好，容貌像是嗔怒、心里却欢喜；阳爻动，是他出外游走，阴爻动，是他在家里；外卦阴、内卦见阳，九五相承，求见吉昌；内阴外阳，见不到面，即使见到，他嗔怒、在外面有伴。注文说：内卦是心，外卦是貌；阴主见不到、阳主见得到；阴主喜、阳主嗔；外卦阴主近、能相见，外卦阳主远、见不到；外阳内阴主不吉，见了也不相喜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On divining whether an ordinary person will be glad or angry on being seen: inner yang and outer yin, the meeting is fair, the face looks angry but the heart is glad; a yang line moving says he has gone out roaming, a yin line moving says he is at home; the outer trigram yin and the inner yang, with the fifth line yang on top, the call is auspicious; inner yin and outer yang, one does not meet, and even if they meet he is angry and has company outside. The notes say: the inner is the heart and the outer the countenance; yin means not meeting, yang means meeting; yin means gladness, yang means anger; outer yin means near and one can meet, outer yang means far and one cannot; outer yang with inner yin means ill luck, and even if they meet they do not take to each other.
