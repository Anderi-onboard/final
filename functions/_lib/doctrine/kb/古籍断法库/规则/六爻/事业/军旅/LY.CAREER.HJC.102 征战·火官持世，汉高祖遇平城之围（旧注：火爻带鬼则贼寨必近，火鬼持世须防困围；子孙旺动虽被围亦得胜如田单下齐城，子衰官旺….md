# 征战·火官持世，汉高祖遇平城之围（旧注：火爻带鬼则贼寨必近，火鬼持世须防困围；子孙旺动虽被围亦得胜如田单下齐城，子衰官旺必致困围如汉高祖被匈奴围于平城七日乃解）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 火爻带着官鬼，敌人的营寨一定很近；火鬼持世，要防被围困。这时如果子孙旺相发动，即使被围也能得胜，像田单下齐城；如果子孙衰弱、官鬼旺相，必定陷入围困，像汉高祖被匈奴围在平城，七天才解围。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a Fire line carries Pressure, the enemy's stockade is surely near; if a Fire Pressure line holds the Self, guard against being besieged. Then if Output is thriving and moves, one wins even when besieged, like Tian Dan's taking of the cities of Qi; if Output is weak and Pressure is thriving, one is surely besieged, like the founder of Han, whom the Xiongnu surrounded at Pingcheng until the siege was lifted after seven days.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注只写「如田单之下齐城」；田单是被燕军围在即墨、反而打败燕军的齐国将领，正合「虽被围得胜」。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary writes only 'like Tian Dan's taking of the cities of Qi'; Tian Dan was a Qi general besieged at Jimo by the army of Yan who turned round and beat it, which fits 'wins even when besieged'.
