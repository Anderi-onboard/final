# 仗托人力总纲：以应爻为主、不遇绝空冲破为佳；佥解京差包完粮税、代出公庭、央邻里之谋为、浼亲朋之所作，事赖他成，前列人之事情皆附卦之应象；粮从产出差徭之重、山长水远人怯丁单，不无亲属之托须情故旧之央，诸般仗托皆看应爻

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要仗托别人出力，以应爻为主，以应爻不遇绝、空、冲、破为好。被派解送钱粮到京的差使，常有人包办完纳粮税；在县里当差，常有人替自己出公庭；或者央求邻里替自己谋划，或者请亲朋替自己做事，承受他们的庇佑，事情靠他们办成。前面列出的这些人的事情，都附在卦的应象上。粮从产业出，好像有差徭的重担；产业卖了、粮税还在，更多力役的征调；或者山长水远路途遥远，或者人胆小、丁口单薄，不能没有亲属的托付，必须央求旧交的情面；事情有万般祸患，要靠卦来一决；一切仗托别人的事，都看应爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In relying on other people's strength, take the Other line as the chief and it is best if it meets no extinction, Void, clash or break. When one is assigned a commission to convey grain and money to the capital, there is often someone who contracts to complete the grain and taxes; when one is on duty in the county, there is often someone who goes to the public court in one's place; or one asks the neighbours to scheme on one's behalf, or begs relatives and friends to act in one's place, receiving their protection, so that the matter is accomplished through them. The affairs of the people listed above are all attached to the Other image of the hexagram. The grain comes out of the property and there seems to be the weight of service and corvée; if the property has gone but the grain tax remains, there are still more levies of labour; or the mountains are long and the waters far, a distant journey, or the man is timid and the adults of the household few and weak; one cannot but entrust the matter to relatives and must entreat old friends for their kindness; the matter has ten thousand troubles and the hexagram must settle it; in all kinds of relying on others, look at the Other line.

> [!note] 按
> 「佥解京差」是被佥派去押解钱粮到京师的差使（解户之役，见前面户役一条）；「包完」是包办完成；「代出公庭」是替人到官府去应役。「粮从产出似有差徭之重，产去粮存尚多力役之征」讲的是田产已卖、粮税还留在名下，所以差徭反而更重。「仗枉」疑是「仗托」。「情故旧之央」的「情」是情面。「审」（「每有包完粮税审」）字义不顺，书里就是这个字，这里不译。这一章问的是靠别人出力办差役、办事，所以看应爻；和前面户役、帮户各章（EL.109 起）是同一类差役。
>
> *Note:* 'Qian jie jing chai' is the commission, to which one is assigned, of conveying money and grain to the capital (the service of a conveyor household, see the earlier entry on household service); 'baowan' is contracting to complete; 'dai chu gongting' is going to the authorities' court to serve in another's place. 'Grain comes out of the property and there seems to be the weight of service; if the property has gone but the grain tax remains, there are still more levies of labour' speaks of land that has been sold while the grain tax stays in the seller's name, so that the service is on the contrary heavier. 'Zhang wang' is presumably 'zhang tuo', relying on and entrusting. The 'qing' of 'qing gujiu zhi yang' is feeling and favour. The character 'shen' in 'mei you baowan liangshui shen' does not make sense and is exactly what the book has; it is not translated here. This chapter asks about relying on others' strength for service and for affairs, so the Other line is looked at; it is the same kind of service as the earlier chapters on household service and on sharing a household's burden (from EL.109).
