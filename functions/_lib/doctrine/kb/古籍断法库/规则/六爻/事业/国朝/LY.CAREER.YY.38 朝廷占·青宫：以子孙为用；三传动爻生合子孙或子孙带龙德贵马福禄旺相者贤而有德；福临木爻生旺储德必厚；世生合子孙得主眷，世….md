# 朝廷占·青宫：以子孙为用；三传动爻生合子孙或子孙带龙德贵马福禄旺相者贤而有德；福临木爻生旺储德必厚；世生合子孙得主眷，世冲克刑害子孙忤主心；子临劫杀大杀动必更立；子被四爻刑害克破防篡逆；四爻带龙德生合子保傅贤，带武鬼咸池合福保傅邪佞；子带咸池偏宫出

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太子（青宫）：以子孙为用神。三传（太岁、月建、日辰）、动爻生合子孙，或者子孙带青龙、德神、贵人、驿马、福、禄而旺相的，太子贤而有德；福神临在木爻上，生旺的，储君的德行必定深厚（青宫属木）；世爻生合子孙的，太子得到君主的眷顾；世爻冲、克、刑、害子孙的，太子违逆君主的心意；子孙爻临劫杀、大杀而发动的，太子必定被更换；子孙爻被四爻刑、害、克、破的，要提防篡逆；四爻带青龙、德神，生合子孙爻的，太子的师保贤明；四爻带玄武、官鬼、咸池，又合福神的，师保奸邪谄佞；子孙爻带咸池的，太子是偏宫所生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Palace, the heir apparent. Take Output as the Subject. If the three rulers or a moving line generate or combine with Output, or Output carries the Dragon, Virtue, the Noble, the Horse, Fortune and Emolument and is thriving, the heir is worthy and virtuous; if the fortune spirit sits on a Wood line and is born or thriving, the heir's stored virtue is surely deep (the Azure Palace belongs to Wood); if the Self generates or combines with Output, the heir wins the sovereign's regard; if the Self clashes with, controls, punishes or harms Output, the heir goes against the sovereign's mind; if Output sits on Robbery and a great baleful spirit and moves, the heir will surely be replaced; if Output is punished, harmed, controlled or broken by the fourth line, beware of usurpation; if the fourth line carrying the Dragon and Virtue generates or combines with Output, the heir's tutors are worthy; if the fourth line carrying the Dark Tortoise, Pressure and Xianchi combines with the fortune spirit, the tutors are wicked and flattering; if Output carries Xianchi, the heir is born of a secondary palace.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「青宫」是太子的东宫，东方属木，所以说「青宫属木」。「保傅」是辅导太子的师保。「偏宫」是正宫皇后以外的宫，即妃嫔所居，「偏宫出」就是妃嫔所生。「武鬼」是玄武和官鬼的省称，按这样译。
>
> *Note:* The 'Azure Palace' is the Eastern Palace of the heir apparent, and since the east belongs to Wood it is said that 'the Azure Palace belongs to Wood'. 'Baofu' are the tutors who guide the heir. 'Pian gong' is a palace other than that of the empress, where the consorts live, and 'born of the secondary palace' means born of a consort. 'Wugui' is an abbreviation of the Dark Tortoise and Pressure, and is translated so.
