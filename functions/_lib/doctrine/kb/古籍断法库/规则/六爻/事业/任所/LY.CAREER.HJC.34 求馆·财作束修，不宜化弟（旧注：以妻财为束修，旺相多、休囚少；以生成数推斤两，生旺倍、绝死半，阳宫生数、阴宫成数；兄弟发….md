# 求馆·财作束修，不宜化弟（旧注：以妻财为束修，旺相多、休囚少；以生成数推斤两，生旺倍、绝死半，阳宫生数、阴宫成数；兄弟发动或财化兄束修不尽入己；财无气得生扶则束修虽少节礼周备）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占馆以妻财为束修（学费）。财旺相就多，休囚就少；要知道有多少，用生成数来推，生旺加倍，绝死减半，在阳宫用生数，在阴宫用成数；只怕兄弟发动或者财化出兄弟，束修不能都归自己，必有人抽成分用，不然就有名无实；财爻无气而遇月建日辰动爻生扶的，束修虽然不多，四季节礼反而周全。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a school post, Wealth is the tuition fee. If thriving it is much, and if resting or trapped, little; to know how much, infer by the generating and completing numbers, doubled if in birth or thriving and halved if in extinction or death, using the generating number in a yang palace and the completing number in a yin palace; what is feared is Peer moving or Wealth turning into Peer, and then the fee does not all go to oneself, someone takes a share, or it is in name only; if Wealth has no breath but is generated or supported by the Month, the Day or a moving line, the fee, though not large, is made up for by gifts at the four seasons' festivals.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「束修」本是十条干肉，古时拜师的见面礼，后来指学费、塾师的薪金。「生数、成数」是河图的数：一二三四五是生数（水一、火二、木三、金四、土五），六七八九十是成数（水六、火七、木八、金九、土十）；旧注的意思是，按财爻的五行取数，阳宫用生数、阴宫用成数，旺相再加倍、衰绝减半，来估计束修的多少。这个算法书里没有给例子，也没有说单位。旧注最后一句引《易》的「天一地二……」，原文只写到「生也」，像是没写全，译文按字面。「抽分」是从里面抽取一份。
>
> *Note:* 'Tuition fee' was originally ten strips of dried meat, the gift of first meeting a teacher in old times, and later meant tuition and the schoolmaster's pay. 'The generating and completing numbers' are the numbers of the River Diagram: one to five are the generating numbers (Water one, Fire two, Wood three, Metal four, Earth five) and six to ten the completing numbers (Water six, Fire seven, Wood eight, Metal nine, Earth ten); the old commentary means that one takes the number from the element of the Wealth line, the generating number in a yang palace and the completing number in a yin palace, doubled if thriving and halved if in extinction, to estimate the fee, and the book gives no example of this calculation nor the unit. The old commentary's last sentence quotes the Changes, 'Heaven one, Earth two ...', and the original stops at 'generation', as if not written out in full, and the translation follows the letter. 'Take a share' is drawing off a portion.
