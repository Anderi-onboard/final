# 官禄占·在任（一）：官加贵禄临扶身世到任操权；父加贵马临扶身世临庭掌印；官加龙贵在阳宫阳爻旺相政声籍甚

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任，到任之后的权势和声望：官星带贵人、禄，临到并扶助身爻、世爻的，到任就掌实权；印星带贵人、驿马，临到并扶助身爻、世爻的，上堂就掌印；官星带青龙、贵人，落在阳宫的阳爻上又旺相的，政绩的名声很大。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In office, power and repute after taking up the post. If the star of office carrying the Noble and Emolument comes to the Body and Self and supports them, he holds real power on taking up the post; if the seal line carrying the Noble and the Horse does so, he holds the seal when he takes his seat; if the star of office carrying the Dragon and the Noble sits on a yang line in a yang palace and is thriving, his name for government is great.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一节开头的「官」「父」都是单字，按上一章的用法译成官星、印星（印绶，即父母爻）；「贵」是贵人，「禄」「马」是禄神、驿马，「龙」是青龙，都是神煞。「籍甚」是名声很大。
>
> *Note:* The single characters 'official' and 'father' at the head of this passage are rendered, as in the previous chapter, as the star of office and the seal line (the seal and ribbon, that is the Resource line); 'Noble' is the Noble, 'Emolument' and 'Horse' are the Emolument spirit and the Post Horse, and 'Dragon' is the Azure Dragon, all spirit-stars. 'Jijia' means a great name.
