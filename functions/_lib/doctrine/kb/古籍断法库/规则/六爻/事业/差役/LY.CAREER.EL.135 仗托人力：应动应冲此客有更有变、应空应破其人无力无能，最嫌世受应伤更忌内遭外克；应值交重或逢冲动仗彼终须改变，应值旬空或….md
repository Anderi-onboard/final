# 仗托人力：应动应冲此客有更有变、应空应破其人无力无能，最嫌世受应伤更忌内遭外克；应值交重或逢冲动仗彼终须改变，应值旬空或临月破托人决不始终，若应克世或外伤内卦皆主其心不善不得误用

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻动、应爻被冲，这个被托的人有更改、有变化；应爻空、应爻破，这个人无力无能；最嫌世爻受应爻伤，更忌内卦被外卦克。应爻发动、或者逢冲而动，仗他办事终究要改变；应爻落旬空、或临月破，托人办事决不能有始有终；如果应克世，或者外卦伤内卦，都主他心地不善，不可误用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Other moves or is clashed, the man entrusted has changes and alterations; if the Other is Void or broken, the man has no strength or ability; what is most disliked is the Self being hurt by the Other, and still more the inner being controlled by the outer. If the Other moves or meets a clash that sets it moving, relying on him will in the end bring a change; if the Other falls on the ten-day Void or meets Month-break, the man entrusted will surely not see it through from start to finish; if the Other controls the Self, or the outer hurts the inner trigram, in every case his heart is not good and one must not employ him by mistake.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1643 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应值交重或逢冲动，仗彼终须改变。 | 应爻发动、或者逢冲而动，仗他办事终究要改变。 | If the Other moves or meets a clash that sets it moving, relying on him will in the end bring a change. |
> | 应值旬空或临月破，托人决不始终。 | 应爻落在旬空、或临月破，托人办事决不能有始有终。 | If the Other falls on the ten-day Void or meets Month-break, the man entrusted will surely not see it through from start to finish. |
> | 若应克世，或外伤内卦，皆主其心不善，不得误用也。 | 如果应克世，或者外卦伤内卦，都主他心地不善，不可误用。 | If the Other controls the Self, or the outer hurts the inner trigram, in every case his heart is not good and one must not employ him by mistake. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「应值交重」的「交重」是动爻的记号，这里指应爻发动（见前）。「客」是被托的人。「不得误用」是不要错用了这个人。这一条与前面用人藏否（EL.132）「用来克世者，实怀欺主之心」取同一个关系：被托的人克世，就是心不善。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiaozhong' in 'ying zhi jiaozhong' is the mark of a moving line and here means the Other line moving (see earlier). 'Ke', the guest, is the man entrusted. 'Bu de wu yong' is not employing this man by mistake. This entry takes the same relation as 'if the Subject comes to control the Self, he truly harbours a mind to cheat his master' in the earlier chapter on employing a man (EL.132): a man entrusted who controls the Self has a heart that is not good.
