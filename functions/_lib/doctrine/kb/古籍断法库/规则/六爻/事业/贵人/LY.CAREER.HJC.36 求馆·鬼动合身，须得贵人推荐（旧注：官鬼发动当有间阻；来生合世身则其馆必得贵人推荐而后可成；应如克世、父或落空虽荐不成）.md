# 求馆·鬼动合身，须得贵人推荐（旧注：官鬼发动当有间阻；来生合世身则其馆必得贵人推荐而后可成；应如克世、父或落空虽荐不成）

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动，应当有阻隔；但如果它来生合世爻、身爻，又主这个馆必得贵人推荐才能成；应爻如果克世，或者父母落空，即使有人推荐也不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure moves there will be obstruction; but if it comes to generate or combine with the Self or the Body, it also means the school can be had only with a noble's recommendation; if the Other controls the Self, or Resource is Void, then even with a recommendation it does not come through.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联的「官鬼」同时有两面：发动就有阻隔（鬼是阻碍），但生合世身时又是「贵人」（鬼是官，官是推荐人）。书里没有说怎么分清它是阻碍还是贵人，只说要看它和世身的关系：生合是推荐，克世是阻。这和 LY.CAREER.HJC.06（官爻临持身世是称意）是同一个道理。
>
> *Note:* In this couplet Pressure has two faces at once: moving, it obstructs (Pressure is the obstacle), but generating or combining with the Self and the Body it is the 'noble' (Pressure is an official, and an official is the one who recommends). The book does not say how to tell whether it is an obstacle or a noble, only that one looks at its relation to the Self and the Body: generating or combining is recommendation, and controlling the Self is obstruction. This is the same principle as LY.CAREER.HJC.06 (Pressure resting on the Self or the Body is to one's liking).
