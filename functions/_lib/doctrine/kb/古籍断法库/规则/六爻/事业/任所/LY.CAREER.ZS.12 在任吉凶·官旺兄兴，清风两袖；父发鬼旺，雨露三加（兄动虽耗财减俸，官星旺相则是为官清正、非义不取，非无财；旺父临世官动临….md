# 在任吉凶·官旺兄兴，清风两袖；父发鬼旺，雨露三加（兄动虽耗财减俸，官星旺相则是为官清正、非义不取，非无财；旺父临世官动临岁五或日月生扶，外任蒙上官推荐，近君大位三赐荣加）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官星旺相而兄爻发动，不一定是坏事：兄爻发动虽然是耗财、减俸的神，但不必拘泥；官星旺相的话，那是做官清正、不义之财不取，并不是没有财；官星旺相而财落空破，也这样断。旺相的父母爻临世，官星发动临在太岁、五爻上，或有日月生扶，外任的必得上司推荐，在君主身边和居高位的，恩赐荣誉一再加身。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A moving Peer line with a thriving star of office is not necessarily bad: a moving Peer line is indeed a spirit that wastes money and cuts salary, but one need not cling to that; when the star of office is thriving, it means an upright official who takes nothing unrighteous, not one who has no wealth; the same judgment holds when the star of office is thriving and Wealth is Void or broken. If a thriving Resource line holds the Self and the star of office moves on Tai Sui or the fifth line, or the Day and Month generate and support it, an official serving outside will surely be recommended by his superiors, and those near the ruler and in high office receive honours again and again.
