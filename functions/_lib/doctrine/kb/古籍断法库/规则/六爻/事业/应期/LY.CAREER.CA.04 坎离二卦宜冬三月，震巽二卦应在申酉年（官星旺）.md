# 坎离二卦宜冬三月，震巽二卦应在申酉年（官星旺）

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占得坎、离二卦，宜在冬季三个月（坎阳离阴，水火相济）；占得震、巽二卦，应在申、酉年——震、巽属木，得申、酉的金作官，官星旺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the two trigrams Kan and Li, the three winter months suit (Kan is yang and Li is yin, water and fire balancing each other); with Zhen and Xun, look to the years of Shen and You, for Zhen and Xun are Wood and the Metal of Shen and You gives them a strong official.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3738 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 震巽木，得申酉金，官旺也。 | 震、巽属木，得到申、酉的金，官星就旺。 | Zhen and Xun are Wood; getting the Metal of Shen and You, the official is strong. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 原文只说「坎离二卦」「震巽」，没说是整个卦、内卦还是外卦；旧注也只解释了为什么是冬天、为什么是申酉年，没有解释这一点，本库不替它补。
>
> *Note:* The verse says only 'the two trigrams Kan and Li' and 'Zhen and Xun', without saying whether the whole hexagram, the inner trigram or the outer one is meant; the note explains why winter and why the years Shen and You but not this, and it is not supplied here.
