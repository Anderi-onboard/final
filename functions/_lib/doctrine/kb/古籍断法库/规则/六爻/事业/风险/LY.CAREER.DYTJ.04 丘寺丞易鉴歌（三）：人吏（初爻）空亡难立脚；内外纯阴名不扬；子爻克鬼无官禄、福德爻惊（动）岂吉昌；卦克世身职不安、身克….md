# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（三）：人吏（初爻）空亡难立脚；内外纯阴名不扬；子爻克鬼无官禄、福德爻惊（动）岂吉昌；卦克世身职不安、身克人吏百忧攒；白虎动时惊恐至；子孙持世必堕官

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 初爻是吏，落空亡，即使官员清正，吏员没力量，难以立足；内外卦全是阴，名声不扬；子孙爻克官鬼，就没有官禄；福德（子孙）爻惊动，哪里还会吉昌；卦克世、身，职位不安；身去克初爻的吏，吏员都畏惧躲避，百样忧愁聚集；白虎动时惊恐就到；子孙持世，必定掉官。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The first line is the clerk, and if it falls Void, even if the official is upright the clerks have no strength and it is hard to find a footing; with inner and outer trigrams wholly yin, the name is not made known; Output controlling Pressure, there is no official emolument; with the Output line startled into moving, how could there be fortune; the hexagram controlling the Self and the Body, the post is not secure; the Body controlling the first line, the clerk, all the clerks shrink away in fear and a hundred anxieties gather; when the White Tiger moves, fright and alarm come; Output holding the Self surely drops the office.

^q7

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全本作「福德爻交反不昌」「子孙持世必隳官」，断易天机本作「福德爻惊岂吉昌」「子孙持世必堕官」：「惊」，注文说就是「动」；「隳」「堕」都是毁掉的意思。「有成忧攒之兆」的「成」疑是「忧」的衍字，这里按「有忧攒之兆」译。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan text reads 'with the Output line moving there is on the contrary no flourishing' and 'Output holding the Self surely ruins the post'; the Duanyi Tianji text reads 'with the Output line startled, how could there be fortune' and 'Output holding the Self surely drops the office'; the note says 'startled' means 'moves', and the two words for ruin and drop both mean to lose the post. The 'cheng' in 'a sign of anxieties becoming gathered' looks like a stray character and is translated as 'a sign of anxieties gathering'.
