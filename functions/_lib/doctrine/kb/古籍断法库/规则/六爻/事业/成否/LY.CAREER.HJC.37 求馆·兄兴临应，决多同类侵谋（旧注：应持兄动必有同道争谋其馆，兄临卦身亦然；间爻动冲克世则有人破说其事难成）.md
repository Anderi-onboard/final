# 求馆·兄兴临应，决多同类侵谋（旧注：应持兄动必有同道争谋其馆，兄临卦身亦然；间爻动冲克世则有人破说其事难成）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占学馆，应爻持兄弟发动，必有同行的人来争谋这个馆，兄弟临卦身也是这样；如果在间爻上发动、冲克世爻，就主有人从中说坏话，事情难成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a school post, if the Other holds Peer and it moves, there will surely be fellow teachers contending for the post, and likewise if Peer rests on the Body; if it moves on a between line and clashes or controls the Self, it means someone undermines it by talk, and the matter is hard to bring about.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄兴临应，决多同类侵谋。 | 兄弟兴旺临在应上，必定多有同类的人来侵夺谋取。 | When Peer flourishes and rests on the Other, there will surely be many of the same kind coming to encroach and scheme for it. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2757 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡应持兄动，必有同道之人争谋其馆，兄临卦身亦然； | 凡是应持兄弟发动，必有同行的人争谋这个馆，兄弟临卦身也是这样； | Whenever the Other holds Peer and it moves, there will surely be fellow teachers contending for the post, and likewise if Peer rests on the Body; |
> | 若在间爻动来冲克世爻，主有人破说也。 | 如果在间爻上发动来冲克世爻，主有人说破。 | if it moves on a between line and comes to clash or control the Self, someone breaks it up by talk. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「同道之人」是同行，也就是同样想谋这个馆的先生。兄弟在占财时是劫财的竞争者，在占馆时是同行的竞争者，是同一个象。「破说」是从中说坏话、把事说破。「间爻」是世应之间的爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Fellow teachers' are people of the same trade, that is, other teachers who also wish for this post. Peer is the rival that robs wealth in divining wealth and the rival of the same trade in divining a school post, the same image. 'Undermines it by talk' is speaking ill from the side and breaking the matter up. 'A between line' is a line between the Self and the Other.
