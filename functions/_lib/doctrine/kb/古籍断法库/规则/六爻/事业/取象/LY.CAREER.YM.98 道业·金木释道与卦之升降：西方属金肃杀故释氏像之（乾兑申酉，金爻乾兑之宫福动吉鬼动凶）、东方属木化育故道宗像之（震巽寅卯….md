# 道业·金木释道与卦之升降：西方属金肃杀故释氏像之（乾兑申酉，金爻乾兑之宫福动吉鬼动凶）、东方属木化育故道宗像之（震巽寅卯，木爻震巽之宫官动为恶子动为善）；卦有升降之爻——世居升爻可入圣谓之成、世居降爻不能越凡谓之败，无病则吉有病则凶

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金木，是释（佛）、道；升降，是成败。西方属金，是肃杀之地，所以用释氏来象征它；况且佛从西边来，当属乾、兑、申、酉，所以金爻以及乾、兑之宫，福动就吉，鬼动就凶。东方属木，是化育之所，所以用道宗来象征它；况且道行于东土，当属震、巽、寅、卯，所以木爻以及震、巽之宫，官动是恶，子动是善。卦有升降之爻：如八月得升卦，则世是升爻；八月得泰卦，则世是降爻，这是秋分后四阴升而三阳降的缘由。世居升爻，可以入圣，叫做成；世居降爻，不能超越凡俗，叫做败；然而无病就吉，有病就凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Metal and Wood are the Buddhists and the Daoists; ascent and descent are success and failure. The west belongs to Metal, a place of austerity and killing, so the Buddhist is imaged by it; and since the Buddha came from the west, it belongs to Qian, Dui, Shen and You, so for a Metal line and the palaces of Qian and Dui, Output moving is auspicious and Pressure moving is ominous. The east belongs to Wood, a place of transformation and nurture, so the Daoist school is imaged by it; and since the Way goes in the eastern land, it belongs to Zhen, Xun, Yin and Mao, so for a Wood line and the palaces of Zhen and Xun, Pressure moving is bad and Output moving is good. A hexagram has lines of ascent and descent: if one gets Sheng in the eighth month, the Self is a line of ascent; if one gets Tai in the eighth month, the Self is a line of descent, which is why after the autumn equinox four yin rise and three yang fall. The Self on an ascending line may enter sagehood, and this is called success; the Self on a descending line cannot rise above the ordinary, and this is called failure; but without sickness it is auspicious, and with sickness ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 「释氏」是佛家（释迦牟尼），「道宗」是道家。西方属金、肃杀，象佛；东方属木、化育，象道，是从方位、五行立论，不是评价两家高下。「乾兑申酉」是金的卦宫和地支（乾兑属金，申酉属金），「震巽寅卯」是木的卦宫和地支。「福动则吉，鬼动则凶」「官动为恶，子动为善」是说无论释道，福神（子孙）动都是好、官鬼动都是坏。「卦有升降之爻」按文意是卦里有些爻是升、有些是降：书里举例，八月得升卦，世是升爻；八月得泰卦，世是降爻，理由是『秋分后四阴升而三阳降』（秋分之后阴气渐长，阳气渐消）。世居升爻是向上走（可入圣，叫成），世居降爻是向下走（越不过凡俗，叫败）；但世爻无病才吉，有病仍凶。这里的升降论法比较晦，书里没有展开，按字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Shishi' is the Buddhist school (Sakyamuni) and 'daozong' is the Daoist school. The west belongs to Metal and austerity, which images the Buddha; the east belongs to Wood and nurture, which images the Way: the argument is from direction and phase and is not a judgment of the worth of the two schools. 'Qian, Dui, Shen and You' are the palaces and branches of Metal (Qian and Dui belong to Metal, and Shen and You belong to Metal), and 'Zhen, Xun, Yin and Mao' are the palaces and branches of Wood. 'Output moving is auspicious, Pressure moving is ominous' and 'Pressure moving is bad, Output moving is good' say that in either school Output (the fortune spirit) moving is good and Pressure moving is bad. 'A hexagram has lines of ascent and descent', by the sense, is that some lines in a hexagram ascend and some descend: the book's example is that getting Sheng in the eighth month makes the Self a line of ascent, and getting Tai in the eighth month makes the Self a line of descent, the reason given being that 'after the autumn equinox four yin rise and three yang fall' (after the equinox the yin force grows and the yang force wanes). The Self on an ascending line goes upward (may enter sagehood, called success), and on a descending line goes downward (cannot surpass the ordinary, called failure); but the Self line must be without sickness for it to be auspicious, and with sickness it is still ominous. This account of ascent and descent is rather obscure, the book does not expand on it, and it is translated by the letter.
