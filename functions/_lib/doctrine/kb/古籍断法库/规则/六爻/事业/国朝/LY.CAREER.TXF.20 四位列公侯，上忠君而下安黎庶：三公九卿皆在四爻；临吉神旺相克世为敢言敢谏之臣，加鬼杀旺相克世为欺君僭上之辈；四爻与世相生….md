# 四位列公侯，上忠君而下安黎庶：三公九卿皆在四爻；临吉神旺相克世为敢言敢谏之臣，加鬼杀旺相克世为欺君僭上之辈；四爻与世相生为阿谀之臣；四爻动生初爻有忧民之心、初爻生四爻民有仰慕、四爻加凶杀克初爻民受无辜之害、初爻动克四爻民视如仇；四爻生合二爻能招贤纳士，生克三爻能任用百官

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 三公九卿的位置都在四爻。四爻临吉神、旺相而克世，是敢言敢谏的社稷之臣；加鬼、杀、旺相而克世，是欺君僭上、蒙蔽君主把持权柄的人；世值吉神，大概没有大害。四爻与世相生，是阿谀奉承的臣子，纵然带吉神也不能规谏君主的过错，带凶神也害不了君主的正道。四爻动生初爻，必有忧虑百姓之心；初爻生四爻，百姓有仰慕之意；四爻加凶杀克初爻，百姓受无辜之害；初爻发动克四爻，百姓看他如同仇敌。四爻生合二爻，必能招揽贤人、接纳士人；二爻生四爻，士人有景仰羡慕之心；四爻生克三爻，必能任用百官、进用贤人罢退不肖之人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The places of the Three Councillors and the Nine Ministers are all on the fourth line. If it stands on an auspicious spirit, strong, and controls the Self, he is a pillar of the state who dares to speak and to remonstrate; if Pressure and baleful spirits are added, strong, and it controls the Self, he is one who deceives the lord, oversteps, keeps his master in the dark and seizes power; if the Self is on an auspicious spirit there is probably no great harm. If the fourth line and the Self generate each other, he is a flattering minister, who even with an auspicious spirit cannot admonish the lord's faults or set right his errors, and with a baleful spirit cannot damage the lord's uprightness or ruin his virtue. The fourth line moving to generate the first, he surely has a heart that grieves for the people; the first generating the fourth, the people look up to him; the fourth with a baleful spirit controlling the first, the people suffer undeserved harm; the first moving to control the fourth, the people regard him as a bitter enemy. The fourth generating or combining the second, he can surely call in the worthy and receive scholars; the second generating the fourth, the scholars admire him; the fourth generating or controlling the third, he can surely employ the hundred officials, advancing the worthy and dismissing the unworthy.
