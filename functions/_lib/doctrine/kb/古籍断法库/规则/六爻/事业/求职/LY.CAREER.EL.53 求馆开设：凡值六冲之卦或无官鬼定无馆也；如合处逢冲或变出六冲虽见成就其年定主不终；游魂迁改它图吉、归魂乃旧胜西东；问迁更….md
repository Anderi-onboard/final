# 求馆开设：凡值六冲之卦或无官鬼定无馆也；如合处逢冲或变出六冲虽见成就其年定主不终；游魂迁改它图吉、归魂乃旧胜西东；问迁更游魂大利、占守旧反要归魂

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是遇到六冲的卦，或者卦里没有官鬼，一定没有馆席；合的地方遇到冲，或者变出六冲，即使看着已经成了，那一年也一定做不到头。游魂卦，改换去别处另谋是吉；归魂卦，是守着旧馆，胜过东奔西走。所以问迁移更换的，游魂卦大为有利；占守旧馆的，反倒要归魂卦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whenever the hexagram is a six-clash, or there is no Pressure line in it, there will surely be no post; if where there is a combination it meets a clash, or it changes out a six-clash, then even if the post seems to be settled, that year it will surely not last to the end. With a Wandering Soul hexagram, to move and change and plan for another post is auspicious; a Returning Soul hexagram is for the old post, better than going east and west. So for asking about moving to a change, a Wandering Soul is greatly favourable; for divining about keeping the old post, a Returning Soul is on the contrary what is wanted.

> [!note] 按
> 「游魂」「归魂」是八宫卦里每宫的第七卦和第八卦（见术语表）。「迁改它图」是改换去别处另谋，「西东」是东奔西走，这里指换馆奔走。「合处逢冲」按文意是本来成合的地方遇到冲，「变出六冲」是变卦成了六冲卦，书里没有再解释。这一条与易隐觅馆占「游魂宜别图，归魂宜仍旧」「六冲无馆、六合有馆」（YY.80）取同一个关系；「游魂」「归魂」只管换不换馆，不管成不成，成不成要看别的爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Wandering Soul' and 'Returning Soul' are the seventh and eighth hexagrams of each of the eight palaces (see the glossary). 'Qian gai ta tu' is to change and plan for another post elsewhere, and 'xi dong' is running east and west, here the running about of changing posts. 'Where there is a combination it meets a clash' is, by the sense, a place that was a combination meeting a clash, and 'changes out a six-clash' is the changed hexagram being a six-clash hexagram; the book does not explain them further. This entry takes the same relations as 'a Wandering Soul suits planning elsewhere, a Returning Soul suits keeping to the old' and 'a six-clash has no post, a six-combine has a post' in the Yi Yin divination for seeking a post (YY.80); the Wandering Soul and Returning Soul decide only whether one changes posts and not whether one succeeds, which is read from the other lines.
