# 杜氏占求事歌：求事先看内卦，内刚柔克外所求通；内爻无气哪能共、世应空亡身心不同；内外相生俱有气事皆成就荷天公

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谋事，先看内卦：是阳卦还是阴卦；内卦克外卦，所求就通，外卦克内卦，难成；内卦的爻无气，即使外卦旺相，也不能共事、所求终不遂心；世、应空亡，身与心相违，与人谋事也难称意；内外卦相生、又都有气，事情都成就，像得到天公的庇佑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In planning a matter, first look at the inner trigram, whether it is a yang trigram or a yin one; if the inner controls the outer, what is sought goes through, and if the outer controls the inner it is hard to accomplish; if the lines of the inner trigram have no qi, then even if the outer is strong one cannot work together and what is sought is in the end not as one wishes; with the Self and the Other Void, body and heart are at odds, and in planning a matter with others it is hard to get what one wants; with inner and outer generating each other and both with qi, all matters are accomplished, as if blessed by Heaven.

> [!note] 按
> 「求事」指谋求办成一件事，不限于求官。「杜氏」是这首歌的作者，断易天机没有交代是谁。
>
> *Note:* 'Seeking a matter' means working to get a thing done and is not limited to seeking office. 'Master Du' is named as the author of the song, and the Duanyi Tianji does not say who he is.
