# 觅馆占·关约何日来：以父母为用；卦无父死值之日来，父旺合日来，父衰生旺日来，父空墓逢冲之日来；又父生世合世之日亦可成关得关

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想知道聘书（关约）什么时候来：以父母爻为用神。卦里没有父爻的，在（父爻）死、当值的日子来；父母爻旺相的，在合它的日子来；衰弱的，在它生旺的日子来；空、墓的，在冲它的日子来。又，父母爻生世爻、合世爻的日子，也可以成关、得到聘约。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know when the engagement (contract) will come: take Resource as the Subject. If the hexagram has no Resource line, it comes on the day it dies and rules; if Resource is thriving, on the day that combines with it; if weak, on the day it is born or thriving; if Void or in tomb, on the day that clashes with it. Again, on the day Resource generates or combines with the Self, the engagement can also be made and obtained.

> | 父母空墓，逢冲之日来。 | 父母爻空、墓的，在冲它的日子来。 | If Resource is Void or in tomb, it comes on the day that clashes with it. |
> | 又父母生世合世之日，亦可成关得关也。 | 又，父母爻生世爻、合世爻的日子，也可以成关、得到聘约。 | Again, on the day Resource generates or combines with the Self, the engagement can also be made and obtained. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「关约」是聘请教书先生的聘书（关书）。「成关得关」是定下聘约、得到聘约。第二句「死值之日」书里没有解释，按字面是父爻在十二长生里「死」而又当值的日子，疑是「伏值」之误（无父即伏藏，待它当值的日子来，和 YY.72 文书伏藏「待文书临值之月日」同理），译文照字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Guanyue' is the engagement letter (guanshu) hiring a private teacher. 'Cheng guan de guan' is settling and obtaining the engagement. The 'day of death and ruling' in the second sentence is not explained in the book; by the letter it is a day on which the Resource line is in its 'death' stage and also rules, and it is presumably a slip for 'hidden and ruling' (with no Resource line it lies hidden and waits for the day it rules, as for a hidden document in YY.72, 'wait for the month or day when the document rules'); the translation follows the letter.
