# 求馆·值土火空无父母，逢金水绝少儿孙（旧注：卦中六亲即人六亲，两人系于一卦以相生之理定：应属土则火是父母、属金则水是子孙；火旺空冲空动空父母不全、衰空无父母；带官鬼贵、兄弟好赌、财贸易、子孙慈善；在应宫者为彼六亲、在世宫者为我六亲）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一个卦里系着两个人时，两人的六亲要用相生的道理来定：占馆以应为东家，应爻属土，火生土，不论卦里有没有火、也不论是哪一类六亲，都当作他的父母；应爻属金，金生水，水就当作他的子孙。火爻旺而空、冲空、动空，父母不全，衰而空就没有父母；火化火有两重父母；带官鬼旺相是贵，衰是病，带兄弟是好赌、无廉耻，带财是做买卖，带子孙是慈善。不能把六爻总起来看，在应宫的爻是对方的六亲，在世宫的爻是我的六亲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When two persons are tied to one hexagram, their relations are fixed by the principle of generation: in divining a school post the Other is the host, and if the Other belongs to Earth, Fire generates Earth, and whether or not there is Fire in the hexagram and whatever relation it is, it is taken as his parents; if the Other belongs to Metal, Metal generates Water, and Water is taken as his children. If the Fire line is thriving but Void, clashed Void or moved Void, his parents are not both alive, and if weak and Void he has no parents; Fire turning into Fire means two sets of parents; carrying Pressure that is thriving means nobility, weak, illness; carrying Peer means gambling and shamelessness; carrying Wealth means trade; carrying Output means kindness. The six lines are not to be looked at all together: lines in the Other's palace are the other's relations, and lines in the Self's palace are all my relations.

## 《黄金策·求馆(附束修)》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8520 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 值土火空，无父母； | 应是土而火空，没有父母； | With Earth and Fire Void, there are no parents; |
> | 逢金水绝，少儿孙。 | 应是金而水绝，缺少儿孙。 | with Metal and Water in extinction, there are few sons and grandsons. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2747 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦得坤属土，如火爻旺空、动空、冲空，主父母不全，衰空必无父母。 | 卦得坤属土，如果火爻旺相而空、动空、冲空，主父母不全，衰弱而空必定没有父母。 | If the hexagram obtained is Kun, belonging to Earth, and a Fire line is thriving but Void, moved Void or clashed Void, his parents are not both alive, and if weak and Void he surely has no parents. |
> | 若卦得乾兑属金，如无水爻，水又绝于飞爻或绝于日辰，则主无子孙。 | 如果卦得乾、兑属金，如果没有水爻，水又绝在飞爻或者绝在日辰，就主没有子孙。 | If the hexagram obtained is Qian or Dui, belonging to Metal, and there is no Water line, or Water is in extinction at the flying line or at the Day, he has no children. |
> | 余皆仿此。 | 其余都照这样推。 | The rest are inferred in the same way. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 两家用什么五行来定「父母」「子孙」，说法不一样：旧注看应爻的五行（应属土，火是他的父母；应属金，水是他的子孙），不管卦里有没有这个爻，也不论它是哪一类六亲；王洪绪看的是卦的宫（卦得坤宫属土，看火爻；卦得乾、兑宫属金，看水爻），而且要「水又绝于飞爻或绝于日辰」才说无子孙。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two commentators differ on which element fixes the 'parents' and the 'children': the old commentary looks at the element of the Other (if the Other belongs to Earth, Fire is his parents, and if it belongs to Metal, Water is his children), whether or not that line is in the hexagram and whatever relation it is; Wang Hongxu looks at the palace of the hexagram (a hexagram of the Kun palace, belonging to Earth, looks at the Fire line, and one of the Qian or Dui palace, belonging to Metal, looks at the Water line), and says there are no children only if Water is also 'in extinction at the flying line or at the Day'. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联讲的是「人」的六亲和「卦」的六亲不是一回事：卦里的父母、子孙等是对「我」说的；要看对方（东家）的父母子孙，得按对方自己的五行，从生的关系重新定。旧注的例子：应属土，火生土，所以火就是东家的父母；应属金，金生水，所以水是东家的子孙。「飞爻」是卦的爻（显现的爻），「飞神」「伏神」见术语表。这条是整本书里少见的、把「两个人系在一个卦上」讲清楚的地方。
>
> *Note:* This couplet says that the relations of the 'person' are not the same as the relations of the 'hexagram': Resource, Output and the others in the hexagram are said relative to 'me'; to see the host's parents and children one must refix them from the host's own element by the relation of generation. The old commentary's example: if the Other belongs to Earth, Fire generates Earth, so Fire is the host's parents; if the Other belongs to Metal, Metal generates Water, so Water is the host's children. 'The flying line' is the line of the hexagram (the visible line); see the glossary for 'flying line' and 'hidden line'. This is one of the few places in the book that makes clear how two persons can be tied to one hexagram.
