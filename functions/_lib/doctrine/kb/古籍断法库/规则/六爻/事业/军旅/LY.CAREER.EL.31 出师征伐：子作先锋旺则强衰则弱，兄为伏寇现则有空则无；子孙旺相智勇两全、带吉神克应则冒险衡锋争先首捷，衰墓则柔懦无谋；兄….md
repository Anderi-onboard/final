# 出师征伐：子作先锋旺则强衰则弱，兄为伏寇现则有空则无；子孙旺相智勇两全、带吉神克应则冒险衡锋争先首捷，衰墓则柔懦无谋；兄弟好佞为埋伏之贼，冲动须防暗害、推生旺日时为期，绝无则不必虑

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙当先锋，旺相就强，衰弱就弱；兄弟是埋伏的贼，出现就有，落空就没有。子孙是现有的雄兵，这是当先锋的说法；子孙旺相，必定是智勇两全的将领，如果带吉神而能克应爻，就能冒险冲锋、争先首捷，唾手成功；子孙如果遇到衰、墓，就柔弱怯懦无谋，难以胜任。兄弟是奸佞之神，就是埋伏的贼，如果逢冲动，必定有埋伏的兵，须防暗害；想知道什么时候来犯，必推它生旺的日子、时辰为期；如果绝、没有，就不必忧虑了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output acts as the vanguard, strong if thriving and weak if declining; Peer is the hidden bandit, there if it appears and absent if Void. Output is the troops that are present, which is the argument for acting as the vanguard; if it is thriving, it is surely a general complete in both wit and courage, and if it carries an auspicious spirit and can control the Other, it can risk a charge, be first to press ahead and win the first success as easily as spitting on the palm; if Output meets weak or tomb, it is soft, timid and without plans and cannot fill the post. Peer is a crafty and flattering spirit, the bandit lying in ambush, and if it meets a clash into motion there are surely troops in ambush and one must guard against secret harm; to know when they will attack, work out the day or hour it is born or thriving as the term; if it is extinct or absent, there is no need to worry.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条与易隐征战占「六亲」里的子（先锋）、兄（埋伏）（YY.60、YY.61）取法相同。「唾手」是不费力气。「衡锋」是「冲锋」。
>
> *Note:* This entry takes the same view as Output (vanguard) and Peer (ambush) in the 'six kin' section of the Yi Yin war divination (YY.60, YY.61). 'Tuo shou' is without effort. 'Heng feng' is 'chong feng', charging the front.
