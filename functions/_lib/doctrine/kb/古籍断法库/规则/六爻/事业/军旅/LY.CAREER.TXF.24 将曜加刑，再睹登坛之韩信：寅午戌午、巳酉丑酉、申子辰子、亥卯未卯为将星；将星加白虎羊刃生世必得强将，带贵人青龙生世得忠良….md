# 将曜加刑，再睹登坛之韩信：寅午戌午、巳酉丑酉、申子辰子、亥卯未卯为将星；将星加白虎羊刃生世必得强将，带贵人青龙生世得忠良之将，加劫杀动来克世须防弑君之祸（此爻将星名为刺客）；以上三节皆在旺相年月日可见

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 将星加刑，再看到登坛拜将的韩信。将星：寅午戌的将星是午，巳酉丑是酉，申子辰是子，亥卯未是卯。将星加白虎、羊刃而生世，必得强将；带贵人、青龙而生世，会得忠良的将领；加劫杀、发动来克世，要防弑君之祸，这样的将星爻名叫刺客。以上三节，都在旺相的年、月、日可以看到应验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The General star with punishment added: one sees again Han Xin ascending the altar. The General star: for Yin, Wu and Xu it is Wu; for Si, You and Chou, You; for Shen, Zi and Chen, Zi; for Hai, Mao and Wei, Mao. If the General star carries the White Tiger or the Goat Blade and generates the Self, a strong general is sure to be found; if it carries the Noble or the Azure Dragon and generates the Self, a loyal and good general is to be had; if it carries the Robbery Killing and moves to control the Self, guard against the calamity of regicide, and such a General-star line is called an assassin. The three sections above can all be seen in strong years, months and days.
