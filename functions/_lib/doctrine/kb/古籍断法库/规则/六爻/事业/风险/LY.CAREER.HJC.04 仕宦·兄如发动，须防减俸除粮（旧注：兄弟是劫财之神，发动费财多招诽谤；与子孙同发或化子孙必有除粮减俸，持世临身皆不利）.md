# 仕宦·兄如发动，须防减俸除粮（旧注：兄弟是劫财之神，发动费财多招诽谤；与子孙同发或化子孙必有除粮减俸，持世临身皆不利）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟是劫财的神。它空亡、不上卦，或者临死绝而安静，就吉；如果发动，免不了花费钱财、招来诽谤，俸禄也不称心；再和子孙一起发动，或者就化成子孙，必有被除粮、减俸的事；持世、临身都不利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer is the spirit that robs wealth. If it is Void, absent from the hexagram, or in death or extinction and still, it is auspicious; if it moves, there is bound to be spending of money and slander, and the salary will not be to one's liking; if it moves together with Output, or turns directly into Output, there will surely be grain struck off and salary cut; holding the Self or settling on the Body is in every case unfavourable.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1932 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟发动，不免费财多招诽误； | 兄弟发动，免不了费财，多招诽谤和过失； | When Peer moves, there is bound to be spending of money and a good deal of slander and fault; |
> | 如与子孙同发或化子孙，必有除粮减俸之事。 | 如果和子孙一同发动，或者化出子孙，必有除粮减俸的事。 | if it moves together with Output or turns into Output, there will surely be grain struck off and salary cut. |
> | 持身临世皆不吉利。 | 持身、临世都不吉利。 | Holding the Body or settling on the Self is in every case unlucky. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「除粮」是从俸粮里扣除。卜筮正宗把「诽谤」写作「诽误」，意思是诽谤和过失。上一条 LY.CAREER.HJC.01 说财动逢冲会减俸，这一条说兄弟发动会减俸：一个是财被冲，一个是兄弟劫财，两处都说减俸，书里没有说哪一种更重。
>
> *Note:* 'Grain struck off' is grain deducted from the stipend. The Bushi Zhengzong writes 'slander and error' in place of 'slander', in the sense of slander and fault. The preceding entry, LY.CAREER.HJC.01, says that a moving Wealth meeting a clash cuts the salary, and this one says that a moving Peer cuts it: in one Wealth is clashed and in the other Peer robs Wealth, both speak of the salary being cut, and the book does not say which is the heavier.
