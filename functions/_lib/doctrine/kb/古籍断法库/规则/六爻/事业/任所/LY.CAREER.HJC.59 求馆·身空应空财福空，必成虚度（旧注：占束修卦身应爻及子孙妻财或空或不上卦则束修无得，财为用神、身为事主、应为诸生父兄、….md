# 求馆·身空应空财福空，必成虚度（旧注：占束修卦身应爻及子孙妻财或空或不上卦则束修无得，财为用神、身为事主、应为诸生父兄、子孙生财之神今皆空休）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占束修，遇到卦身、应爻，以及子孙、妻财，有的空亡或者不上卦，束修就拿不到，因为财是用神，身是事主，应是学生的父兄，子孙又是生财的神，现在都空、都休囚，怎么能拿到呢，所以白白虚度。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining the fee, if the Body, the Other, Output and Wealth are Void or absent from the hexagram, the fee will not be obtained, for Wealth is the Subject, the Body is the one the matter belongs to, the Other is the pupils' fathers and brothers, and Output is the spirit that generates Wealth, and now all are Void and resting, how can it be obtained, and so the time is spent in vain.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8626 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 身空应空财福空，必成虚度； | 卦身空、应爻空、财爻子孙都空，必定落得白白虚度； | With the Body Void, the Other Void and Wealth and Output Void, one will surely pass the time in vain; |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2792 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占束修，遇卦身应爻及子孙妻财皆空，或不上卦者，主束修无得。 | 凡占束修，遇到卦身、应爻以及子孙、妻财都空，或者不上卦的，主束修拿不到。 | In divining the fee, if the Body, the Other, Output and Wealth are all Void, or absent from the hexagram, the fee will not be obtained. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联列出了束修要有的四样：身（事主）、应（东家，即学生的父兄）、财（束修）、子孙（生财的神），缺哪个都不行。旧注「用神」是财爻；王洪绪只写了结论。
>
> *Note:* This couplet lists the four things the fee needs: the Body (the one the matter belongs to), the Other (the host, that is, the pupils' fathers and brothers), Wealth (the fee) and Output (the spirit that generates Wealth), and without any one it cannot be had. In the old commentary 'the Subject' is the Wealth line; Wang Hongxu writes only the conclusion.
