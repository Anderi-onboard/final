# 户役·盐漕河赋之差与吏胥吏卒：官能荣之故官不可空、官能辱之故官不可克、官能利之故财不可无；随官入墓助鬼伤身为公门之大忌

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是盐、漕、河、赋这些差使，以及吏胥、吏卒一类的人：官能使他荣，所以官不可空；官能使他辱，所以官不可被克；官能使他得利，所以财不可没有。随官入墓、助鬼伤身，是公门的大忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For duties connected with salt, grain transport, river works and taxes, and for clerks, runners and attendants: the official can bring him honour, so Pressure must not be Void; the official can bring him disgrace, so Pressure must not be controlled; the official can bring him profit, so Wealth must not be absent. Following Pressure into the tomb and helping Pressure to injure the Body are the great taboos of the public offices.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「盐漕河赋之差」是盐政、漕运、河工、赋税方面的差使，「吏胥吏卒之辈」是书吏、衙役一类。「官能荣之」是官（官爻）能使他荣耀，所以官不可空；「官能辱之」是官能使他受辱，所以官不可被克；「官能利之」是官能使他得利，所以财不可没有（没有财，有官也没有俸）。「随官入墓，助鬼伤身」是公门的大忌，和本卷军机（YM.13）、功名（YM.41）里的是同一条。
>
> *Note:* 'Yan cao he fu zhi chai' are duties in the salt administration, grain transport, river works and taxation, and 'lixu lizu zhi bei' are clerks, runners and the like. 'The official can bring him honour' is the official (the Pressure line) being able to bring him glory, so Pressure must not be Void; 'can bring him disgrace' is that the official can make him suffer disgrace, so Pressure must not be controlled; 'can bring him profit' is that the official can make him gain, so Wealth must not be absent (without Wealth, even with an office there is no salary). 'Following Pressure into the tomb and helping Pressure to injure the Body' is the great taboo of the public offices, the same one as in the military chapter (YM.13) and the chapter on fame and office (YM.41) of this book.
