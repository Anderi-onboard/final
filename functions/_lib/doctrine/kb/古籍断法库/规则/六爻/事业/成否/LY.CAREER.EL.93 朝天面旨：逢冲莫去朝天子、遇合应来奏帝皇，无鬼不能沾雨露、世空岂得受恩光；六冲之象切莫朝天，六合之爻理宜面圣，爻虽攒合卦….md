# 朝天面旨：逢冲莫去朝天子、遇合应来奏帝皇，无鬼不能沾雨露、世空岂得受恩光；六冲之象切莫朝天，六合之爻理宜面圣，爻虽攒合卦中无鬼或值空乡渥恩莫望，卦纵有官世居陷地还嗟运蹇岂沐天恩

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 逢到冲，不要去朝见天子；遇到合，就该来奏对皇帝；没有官鬼，就沾不到雨露（恩泽）；世爻落空，怎能蒙受恩光？具体说：六冲的卦象，千万不要去朝见天子；六合的爻象，按理适宜面见圣上；爻象虽然聚合，但卦中没有官鬼，或者落在空乡，就不要指望丰厚的恩典；卦里即使有官爻，世爻落在陷地，也只能叹运气艰难，怎么谈得上沐浴天恩？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Meeting a clash, do not go to court the Son of Heaven; meeting a combination, it is fitting to come and memorialise the emperor; with no Pressure one cannot share in the rain and dew of favour; with the Self Void how can one receive the glow of grace? In detail: with a six-clash image, on no account go to court; with a six-combine line, it is proper to meet the sage sovereign; even if the lines gather in combination, if the hexagram has no Pressure or it falls on empty ground, do not hope for rich favour; even if the hexagram has a star of office, if the Self sits in a pit one can still only lament one's hard fortune, and how could one bathe in heaven's favour?

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「六冲」「六合」指卦的六冲、六合（见术语表）。「攒合」是聚合，「空乡」是空亡之地，「渥恩」是优厚的恩典。「世居陷地」的「陷」与前面仕宦升迁「临陷临空」同，是落入空、墓、绝一类凶地，书里没有细说。这一条与黄金策仕宦「奏陈谏诤，那堪太岁刑冲」（HJC.26）取同一类忌：冲克是朝见君王的大忌。
>
> *Note:* 'Six-clash' and 'six-combine' are the hexagram patterns (see the glossary). 'Cuanhe' is gathering in combination, 'kongxiang' is the ground of Void, and 'wo'en' is rich favour. The 'xian' of 'shi ju xian di' is the same as in 'lin xian lin kong' in the earlier chapter on official promotion, a fall into a bad place such as Void, tomb or extinction, and the book does not go into it. This entry takes the same taboo as 'for memorials and remonstrance, how can one bear Tai Sui's punishment and clash' in the Huangjin Ce on official careers (HJC.26): clash and control are the great taboo for seeing the sovereign.
