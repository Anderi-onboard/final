# 国朝·子伤君位，隋杨广有弑父之心（旧注：本宫子孙带杀旺动克害世爻，是太子有篡位之兆，如隋杨广弑父自立）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本宫的子孙带着凶煞、旺相发动、克害世爻，是太子有篡位的征兆，就像隋朝的杨广弑杀父亲、自立为皇帝。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Output of the home palace carries a baleful spirit, is thriving and moves, and controls and harms the Self, the crown prince shows signs of usurping the throne, like Yang Guang of Sui who killed his father and set himself up as emperor.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5861 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子伤君位，隋杨广有弑父之心。 | 子孙伤克君位，就像隋朝的杨广有杀父的心思。 | If Output injures the ruler's place, it is like Yang Guang of Sui who had it in his heart to kill his father. |

^q1
