# 户役·助役、卸役、讼官：求人助役须应生世、应空破散者无助；觅人卸役须世克应、应空官克者无门；讼官得理忌官克世、官空破散者无益；公以官而私以应（私处求应生、公断戒官克）

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果求人帮助服役，须应生世，如应空、破、散的就得不到帮助；如果找人替我卸役，须世克应，如应空、官克的就无门；如果为役告官而求得理，官克世不适宜，如官空、破、散的就无益。因为公事看官，私事看应。口诀说：私下求人要应生世，公断要戒官克世；应空谁来怜惜，应克谁来出力。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If one asks someone to help with a duty, the Other must generate the Self, and if the Other is Void, broken or scattered there is no help; if one looks for someone to unload a duty for me, the Self must control the Other, and if the Other is Void or Pressure controls, there is no door; if one sues the official over the duty and wants to be in the right, Pressure controlling the Self is unsuitable, and if Pressure is Void, broken or scattered it is no use. For public matters are seen by Pressure and private matters by the Other. The verse says: in a private plea, one needs the Other to generate the Self; in a public judgment, beware of Pressure controlling it; if the Other is Void, who will pity one; if the Other controls, who will exert himself.

> [!note] 按
> 「助役」是求别人帮我分担差役，「卸役」是找人替我卸掉差役，「讼官」是为役的事和官府打官司。「应生世」是对方生我，才肯帮；「世克应」是我压得住对方，才卸得掉；「官克世」不利于打官司，因为官是公断的一方。「盖公以官，而私以应」是总括：公事（官府的事）看官爻，私事（求人的事）看应爻。书里自注是一首四句的口诀：私下求人看应生世，公断要戒官克世；应空谁来怜惜我，应克世谁来出力。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhuyi' is asking someone to share a duty with me, 'xieyi' is finding someone to take a duty off me, and 'songguan' is going to law against the officials over a duty. 'The Other generating the Self' is the other side generating me, which is when he is willing to help; 'the Self controlling the Other' is my being able to press on the other side, which is when the duty can be unloaded; 'Pressure controlling the Self' is bad for a lawsuit, because the official is the side that judges. 'Public matters are seen by Pressure and private matters by the Other' is the summing up: public matters (the government's affairs) are seen by the Pressure line, and private matters (asking someone) by the Other line. The book's own note is a four-line verse: in a private plea look for the Other generating the Self, in a public judgment beware of Pressure controlling the Self; if the Other is Void who will pity me, if the Other controls the Self who will exert himself.
