# 出师征伐：父母兴隆机关炯炯，妻财发动兵甲纷纷；子孙为兵之用、父母为子之忌，父母旺而兴则机关迭出诡策多端是为伤兵之兆，子旺父衰则将勇兵强；官鬼为彼之用、妻财为鬼之助，财爻一发鬼逞豪强，惟要财静则官鬼孤立自然太平

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母兴旺，机谋炯炯；妻财发动，兵甲纷纷。子孙是兵的用神，父母是子孙的忌神：如果父母旺相而兴起，就机谋迭出、诡计多端，这是伤兵的征兆；如果子孙旺相、父母衰弱，就是将领勇敢、兵卒强壮，可以有所作为。官鬼是对方的用神，妻财是官鬼的助力：对方本来不兴兵，但财爻一发动，官鬼就逞强，所以士卒纵横、兵戈扰乱；只要财爻安静，官鬼就孤立无助，自然太平了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Resource flourishes, stratagems are bright and clear; if Wealth moves, arms and armour are everywhere in motion. Output is the Subject for the troops and Resource is the Adversary of Output: if Resource is thriving and rises, stratagems pour out and tricks are many, which is a sign of the troops being injured; if Output is thriving and Resource weak, the commander is brave and the troops strong, and one can act. Pressure is the Subject for the enemy and Wealth is the aid of Pressure: the enemy had not meant to raise troops, but as soon as the Wealth line moves Pressure shows its strength, so soldiers swarm and weapons are in disorder; if only Wealth is still, Pressure stands alone without aid and peace comes of itself.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「机关」是机谋、计策。「用神」是占问时被拿来看这件事成败的那个爻，「忌神」是克它的爻（见术语表）。「太乎」疑是「太平」，译作「太平」。这一条和易隐征战占「六亲」里的「财动助鬼」（YY.62）取同一个关系：妻财生官鬼。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiguan' is stratagems and schemes. The 'Subject' is the line taken as the matter asked about, and the 'Adversary' is the line that controls it (see the glossary). 'Tai hu' is presumably 'taiping', peace, and is translated 'peace'. This entry takes the same relation as 'Wealth moves to help Pressure' in the 'six kin' section of the Yi Yin war divination (YY.62): Wealth generates Pressure.
