# 水爻带鬼来克我须防劫寨攻营；有气无气断输赢：世为我将、应为彼军、日辰为我卒、官鬼为彼兵，我克他胜、他克我负；财兴克父斩将夺旌、兄弟交重夺粮劫草

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水爻带着官鬼来克我，要防备劫寨和攻营；还要看有气没气，才能断兵机的输赢。决胜负，以世、应、日辰、官星来论：世是我方将领，应是对方军队，日辰是我方士卒，官鬼是对方兵；我克他，我胜；他克我，我负。财动克父，必定斩将夺旗；兄弟交重发动，必定夺粮劫草。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Water line carrying Pressure and coming to control us: guard against the raiding of the camp and the assault on the encampment; then see whether there is qi or none, and only then judge the winning or losing of the military affair. To decide victory, reckon by the Self, the Other, the Day and the star of office: the Self is our general, the Other is their army, the Day is our soldiers, Pressure is their troops; if we control them, we win; if they control us, we lose. If Wealth rises and controls Resource, the general is surely cut down and the banner seized; if Peer moves, grain is surely seized and fodder raided.
