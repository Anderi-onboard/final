# 占朝国（一）：世应相得则君臣用心、六位无克则万国咸宁；世为帝王、应为功臣、本宫为都；内外比和旺相则天生圣主；金兵戈忌动、土城垒宜安、水泛滥、火炎晴、木风恶、吉神为瑞；震离坎兑四方、艮坤中土

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占朝廷、国家，世应相得，君臣就同心用力；六位没有相克，万国都安宁。世是帝王，应是功臣，本宫是都城。内外卦比和、旺相，是天生圣明的君主，刚柔动静有常规，大地出奇才；最宜吉神，切忌大煞。金是兵戈，忌动；土是城垒，宜安；水是泛滥，火是炎热晴天，木爻是恶风；吉神是祥瑞。震、离、坎、兑是四方，艮、坤两卦是中土。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about the court and the state, if the Self and the Other are in accord, ruler and ministers are of one mind; if none of the six places controls another, all the states are at peace. The Self is the sovereign, the Other is the meritorious minister, and the home palace is the capital. If the inner and outer trigrams are in accord and strong, Heaven has produced a sage ruler, firmness and softness, movement and stillness keep their constancy, and the earth brings forth rare talents; auspicious spirits are most suitable, and great baleful stars are strictly to be avoided. Metal is arms and the spear, and it must not move; Earth is the city wall and rampart, and it should be at peace; Water is flooding, Fire is blazing heat, the Wood line is wicked wind; auspicious spirits are good omens. Zhen, Li, Kan and Dui are the four quarters, and the two trigrams Gen and Kun are the central earth.

> [!note] 按
> 「朝国」是朝廷和国家。「世为帝王，应为功臣，本宫为都」是把卦里的位置配成国家的位置：世是君、应是臣、本宫是京都。「六位无克」是六爻之间没有互相克。「内外比和旺相」是内外卦五行和睦、都旺相。「吉神」「大杀」是神煞。「金为兵戈……木爻风恶」是各行主的国家大事：金主兵、土主城、水主水灾、火主旱热、木主风。「震离坎兑为四方」是震东、离南、坎北、兑西，「艮坤二卦为中土」是艮（东北）、坤（西南）居中。这一节与本书国事（YM.01 起）、易林补遗朝廷国事（EL.01 起）、易隐朝廷占（YY.31–YY.37）取同一类：世应、五爻、各爻位对应君臣百姓，金木水火土对应兵、工、水、旱。
>
> *Note:* 'Chaoguo' is the court and the state. 'The Self is the sovereign, the Other is the meritorious minister, the home palace is the capital' matches the positions in the hexagram to the positions of a state: the Self is the ruler, the Other the minister, and the home palace the capital. 'None of the six places controls another' is no line among the six controlling another. 'Inner and outer in accord and strong' is the phases of the inner and outer trigrams being at peace and both strong. 'Auspicious spirits' and 'great baleful stars' are spirit-stars. 'Metal is arms ... the Wood line is wicked wind' lists the great affairs of state each phase governs: Metal governs the military, Earth city walls, Water floods, Fire heat and drought, Wood wind. 'Zhen, Li, Kan and Dui are the four quarters' is Zhen east, Li south, Kan north, Dui west, and 'Gen and Kun are the central earth' is Gen (north-east) and Kun (south-west) taken as the middle. This section is of a kind with the affairs of state in this book (from YM.01), the Yilin Buyi on the affairs of the court (from EL.01) and the Yi Yin on the court: the Self and the Other, the fifth line and each line position correspond to ruler, ministers and people, and the five phases correspond to the military, public works, floods and drought.
