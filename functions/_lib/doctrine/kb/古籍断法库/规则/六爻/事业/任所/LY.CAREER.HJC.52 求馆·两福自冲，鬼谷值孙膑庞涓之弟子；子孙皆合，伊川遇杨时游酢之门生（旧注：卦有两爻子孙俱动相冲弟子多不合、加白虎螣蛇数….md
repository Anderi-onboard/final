# 求馆·两福自冲，鬼谷值孙膑庞涓之弟子；子孙皆合，伊川遇杨时游酢之门生（旧注：卦有两爻子孙俱动相冲弟子多不合、加白虎螣蛇数有争斗、来伤世必责及先生；二爻俱生合世爻则门生自尽弟子之礼必不轻背其师）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有两个子孙爻，都发动而相冲，弟子之间必定多有不合，再加白虎、螣蛇，时常有争斗，如果来伤世爻，必定连累到先生；如果两个爻都来生合世爻，门生就自己尽弟子的礼，尊师重道，必定不会轻易背弃老师。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram has two Output lines and both move and clash with each other, there will surely be much discord among the disciples, with the White Tiger and the Coiling Snake added there are often fights, and if they come to injure the Self it will surely reach the teacher; if both lines come to generate or combine with the Self, the pupils will fulfil the duty of disciples on their own, respect their teacher and value what is handed down, and will surely not lightly turn their backs on him.

> [!note] 按
> 经文借了两对师生：鬼谷子的弟子孙膑、庞涓后来成了仇敌，程颐（伊川）的弟子杨时、游酢「程门立雪」，以尊师著称。孙膑、庞涓、杨时、游酢书里没有展开，这里只是用来比弟子之间不合和相合。
>
> *Note:* The verse borrows two pairs of teachers and pupils: Guiguzi's disciples Sun Bin and Pang Juan later became enemies, and Cheng Yi's (Yichuan's) disciples Yang Shi and You Zuo, of the story of 'standing in the snow at Cheng's gate', were known for respect to their teacher. The book does not expand on Sun Bin, Pang Juan, Yang Shi or You Zuo, and they are used only as images for discord and accord among disciples.
