# 文书占·何时可得：旺则待入墓之月日，衰则候生旺之月日，旺动则逢合之月日，旺静则冲动之月日，伏藏则文书临值之月日

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问文书什么时候可以拿到：文书爻旺相的，等它入墓的月日；衰弱的，等它生旺的月日；旺相发动的，等逢合的月日；旺相安静的，等冲动它的月日；文书爻伏藏的，等文书当值的月日才能得到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask when a document can be had: if the document line is thriving, wait for the month or day it enters tomb; if weak, wait for the month or day it is born or thriving; if thriving and moving, the month or day it meets a combination; if thriving and still, the month or day that clashes it into motion; if the document line lies hidden, it is had in the month or day in which the document rules.

> | 若出月，则十一月建子，并动应爻，冲脱文书，所以不成也。 | 如果出了本月，到十一月建子，并发动应爻，冲脱文书，所以不成。 | If the month is out, in the eleventh month the Month is Zi, and together with the moving Other line it clashes away and drains the document, so it does not succeed. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 例子是文书占末尾附的断例。「文收」疑是「文书」，译文按文书译。「水胎于午」是水在十二长生里胎在午。第三例的「日占」是白天占，「寅年」没有写干支。
>
> *Note:* The examples are those appended at the end of the divination of documents. 'Wen shou' is presumably 'wenshu', document, and the translation reads it as the document. 'Water is in the womb at Wu' is Water's womb stage at Wu among the twelve stages. In the third example 'ri zhan' is a divination by day, and the 'Yin year' is given without its stem.
