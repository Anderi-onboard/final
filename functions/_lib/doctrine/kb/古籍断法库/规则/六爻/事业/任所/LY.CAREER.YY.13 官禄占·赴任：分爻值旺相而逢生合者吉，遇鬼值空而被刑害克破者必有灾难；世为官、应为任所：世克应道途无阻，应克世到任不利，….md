# 官禄占·赴任：分爻值旺相而逢生合者吉，遇鬼值空而被刑害克破者必有灾难；世为官、应为任所：世克应道途无阻，应克世到任不利，世生应因官耗损，应生世因官进益；世空必有灾难，陆行忧跌扑舟行忧风浪

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赴任：对应的那一爻旺相又逢生合的，吉；遇上官鬼、落空，又被刑、害、克、破的，必有灾难。又，世爻是做官的人，应爻是任所：世克应，路上没有阻碍；应克世，到任不利；世生应，因为做官而耗损；应生世，因为做官而得益。世爻落空必有灾难：走陆路担心跌伤，走水路担心风浪；如果是旺相的空，只是生病而已，或者得到日辰、动爻来冲克，才大概能免于灾咎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Going to the post: the matching line is auspicious when it is thriving and meets generation or combination; if it meets Pressure and is Void, and is punished, harmed, controlled or broken, there will surely be disaster. Again, the Self is the official and the Other is the post: if the Self controls the Other the road is unobstructed; if the Other controls the Self, taking up the post is unfavourable; if the Self generates the Other, one is drained because of the office; if the Other generates the Self, one gains because of the office. A Void Self means disaster: going by land fear a fall, by water fear wind and waves; if it is a thriving Void it is only illness, or if the Day or a moving line comes to clash with or control it one may perhaps escape blame.

> [!note] 按
> 表里的名目是「任所、道路、宾师车马、家眷伴侣行李」，书里没有写各是哪一爻，读法同 YY.02：从上爻写到初爻。「宾师」是随行的幕宾和师爷。

'Binshi' are the guests and secretaries who accompany the official.
