# 卜筮元龟·占何年何月得官：有爻持鬼即为时，如无则取官鬼生旺之时，旺相疾速休囚迟；先看本宫旺为正

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道得官的年、月、日期：有爻持着官鬼，就以那一爻的时间为期；没有这样的爻，就取官鬼得生、得旺的时候；旺相就快，休囚就迟。先看本宫，以旺的为正，比如震取春分，其余照此类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know the year, month and term when the office is gained: if a line carries Pressure, that line's time is the term; if there is none, take the time when Pressure is born or strong; strong, it is quick, and at rest or trapped, slow. First look at the palace of the hexagram, taking the strong as the standard, for Zhen for example the Spring Equinox, and the rest likewise.

> [!note] 按
> 「震则春分余可并」的「并」没有解释，这里读作「照此类推」，不确定。
>
> *Note:* The 'bing' (join) of 'Zhen then the Spring Equinox, the rest can be joined' is not explained and is read here as 'likewise', which is uncertain.
