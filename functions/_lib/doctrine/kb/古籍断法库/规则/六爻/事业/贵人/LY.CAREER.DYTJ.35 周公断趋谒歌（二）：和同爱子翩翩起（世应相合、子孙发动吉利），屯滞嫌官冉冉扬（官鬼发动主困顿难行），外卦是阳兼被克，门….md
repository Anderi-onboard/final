# 周公断趋谒歌（二）：和同爱子翩翩起（世应相合、子孙发动吉利），屯滞嫌官冉冉扬（官鬼发动主困顿难行），外卦是阳兼被克，门前趋谒见凄惶

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应和同、子孙（爱子）发动，轻快地兴起，主吉利；屯滞，最嫌官鬼慢慢扬起（发动），主困顿难行；外卦是阳又被内卦所克（如既济这一类），到门前拜谒，只见凄惶而归。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Self and Other in harmony and the beloved son (Output) moving, one rises lightly and gaily, which is auspicious; stagnation: Pressure slowly rising (moving) is disliked and means being stuck and hard to proceed; with the outer trigram yang and also controlled by the inner (as in a hexagram such as Jiji), at the door where one calls one meets only dismay and goes home in distress.

> [!note] 按
> 「爱子」没有解释，注（5）说的是子孙爻，这里按子孙读。注（7）说「外卦属阳克内卦」，正文却说「外卦是阳兼被克」，一个是克内卦，一个是被克，两处不一致，本库照录，不改。
>
> *Note:* 'Beloved son' is not explained, and note (5) speaks of the Output line, so it is read as Output. Note (7) says 'the outer trigram being yang controls the inner trigram' while the verse says 'the outer trigram is yang and also controlled', one having it control and the other be controlled; the two do not agree and are recorded as they stand.
