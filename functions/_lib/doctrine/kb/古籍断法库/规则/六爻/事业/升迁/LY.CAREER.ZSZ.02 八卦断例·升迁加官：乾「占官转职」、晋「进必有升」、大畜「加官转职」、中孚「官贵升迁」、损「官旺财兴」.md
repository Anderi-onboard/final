# 八卦断例·升迁加官：乾「占官转职」、晋「进必有升」、大畜「加官转职」、中孚「官贵升迁」、损「官旺财兴」

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占官职、升迁，遇到乾卦（占官转职）、晋卦（进必有升）、大畜卦（加官转职）、中孚卦（官贵升迁）、损卦（官旺财兴），都主官职有转迁、升进。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining office and promotion, meeting the hexagram Qian ('asking about office: a change of post'), Jin ('advancing surely brings promotion'), Daxu ('promotion and transfer of post'), Zhongfu ('officials and nobles are promoted') or Sun ('office flourishes and wealth rises') all point to a change or advance of post.

## 《周易尚占·八卦断例章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 882 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Chapter of judgments by example for the eight palaces", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 乾者健也 | 乾，就是刚健。 | Qian (Heaven) means strength. |
> | 事宜专一 | 做事适合专一。 | In affairs, single-mindedness suits. |
> | 人口安康 | 家里人口平安健康。 | The household is at peace and in health. |
> | 田蚕进益 | 田地和养蚕有进益。 | Fields and silkworms bring gain. |
> | 问病获安 | 问病，能得平安。 | Asking about illness: safety is gained. |
> | 占官转职 | 占官职，会转任调职。 | Asking about office: a change of post. |
> | 所谋必成 | 所谋划的事一定成功。 | What is planned will surely succeed. |
> | 所求皆得 | 所求的都能得到。 | All that is sought is obtained. |
