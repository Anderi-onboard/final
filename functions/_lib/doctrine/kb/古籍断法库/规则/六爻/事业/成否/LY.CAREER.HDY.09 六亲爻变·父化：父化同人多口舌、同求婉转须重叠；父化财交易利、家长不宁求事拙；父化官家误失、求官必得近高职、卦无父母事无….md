# 六亲爻变·父化：父化同人多口舌、同求婉转须重叠；父化财交易利、家长不宁求事拙；父化官家误失、求官必得近高职、卦无父母事无头、休囚空费力

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父化同人，多有口舌；一起求谋，要曲折婉转、重重叠叠（纵然成了也要重新更改）；父化财，做买卖有利（先难后易），家里长辈不安宁、求事笨拙（化成财克了父）；父化官，家里有耽误、有失（化成鬼，不适宜），求官必定得到靠近高位的职务（官、父两个月），卦里没有父母，事情没有头绪（父母是总体），再在休囚，白费力气（父母衰，后来劳苦）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource turning into a companion (Peer), there is much quarrelling; seeking together, it winds and turns and must be layered (even if it succeeds it must be changed again); Resource turning into Wealth, trading is profitable (hard first, then easy), but the elders of the house are unsettled and seeking a matter goes clumsily (turning into Wealth controls Resource); Resource turning into Pressure, the household suffers delay and loss (turning into Pressure is unsuitable), and seeking office one is sure to get a post near a high one (the two months of Pressure and Resource); with no Resource in the hexagram the matter has no head (Resource is the whole body), and if it is also at rest or trapped, effort is spent in vain (Resource weak, labour afterwards).

> [!note] 按
> 「官父两月」小注没有解释，这里照字面译作「官、父两个月」，可能指官鬼、父母爻所值的月份，不确定。「父化同人」的「同人」，小注说是「化兄」，按兄弟译。「虎易注：此篇各节明显错乱」，海底眼这一整段原书就有今人标出的错乱，这几句的先后读起来也有跳跃。
>
> *Note:* The small note 'the two months of Pressure and Resource' is not explained and is translated literally; it may mean the months ruled by the Pressure and Resource lines, which is uncertain. For 'Resource turning into a companion', the small note says 'turning into Peer', and it is translated as Peer. A modern editor remarks of this part of the Haidi Yan that its sections are plainly in disorder, and these lines too read with jumps.
