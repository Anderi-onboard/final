# 投充兵卒总纲：以世身为主、财福为凭；投兵须把世爻详，逢旺逢生去必昌，若遇旬空无对敌，如逢月破丧他乡；凡占投充兵者世以为用，得日辰生世或世旺相则功成利厚力加体康，若值旬空则无人对敌，若逢月破则大有刑伤大不吉利

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 投军当兵，以世爻、身爻为主，以财爻、子孙（福）为凭据。投兵必须细看世爻：逢旺、逢生，去了必定兴旺；如果遇到旬空，就没有对手可打；如果逢月破，就死在他乡。凡是占投充当兵的，以世爻为用，只要得到日辰生世、或者世旺相，就功成利厚、力量增加、身体康健；如果值旬空，就没有人对敌；如果逢月破，就大有刑伤，非常不吉利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In enlisting as a soldier, take the Self and the Body as the chief and Wealth and Output as the ground. In joining the army one must examine the Self line in detail: if it meets thriving or generation, going will surely bring prosperity; if it meets the ten-day Void, there is no enemy to face; if it meets Month-break, one dies in a foreign land. In every divination about enlisting as a soldier, the Self is the Subject: if the Day generates the Self or the Self is thriving, one's service is accomplished, the profit is great, one's strength increases and one's body is hale; if it falls on the ten-day Void there is no one to oppose; if it meets Month-break there is much punishment and injury and it is greatly inauspicious.

> [!note] 按
> 「投充」是应募、投身，「投充兵」就是投军当兵。「世身」是世爻和卦身。「德旬空」的「德」疑是「值」，译作「值旬空」。「旬空无对敌」是没有对手可以交战，书里没有说这是好是坏，这里只照字面译。「丧他乡」是死在外乡。这是书中事业各章的最后一章。
>
> *Note:* 'Touchong' is volunteering and committing oneself, and 'touchong bing' is joining the army as a soldier. 'Shishen' is the Self line and the Body of the hexagram. The 'de' of 'de xunkong' is presumably 'zhi', to fall on, and it is translated 'falls on the ten-day Void'. 'Xunkong wu duidi' is having no opponent to fight; the book does not say whether this is good or bad and it is translated literally. 'Sang taxiang' is dying in a foreign land. This is the last of the book's chapters on a career.
