# 治筑·官吏奉差治河：大官奉敕、小官奉命、吏民奉差，官爻不可病并冲克世位；官吏因河工而筮利钝反以官为重——官旺世实则纪录升荐、官伤世空及随墓助伤则罪戾降责

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果是大官奉敕命、小官奉上司之命、吏民奉派出差，官爻不可有病，并且不可冲克世位。如果是官吏因河工（治河工程）占利不利，就反过来以官为重：官旺、世实，就被记功、升荐；官伤、世空，以及随墓、助伤，就有罪过、降责。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a great official acts on an imperial edict, a lesser official on an order, or clerks and commoners on an assignment, the Pressure line must not be sick, nor clash with or control the Self's place. If an official or clerk asks about gain or loss in connection with river works, then on the contrary Pressure counts for most: with Pressure thriving and the Self solid, he is recorded for merit and recommended for promotion; with Pressure injured and the Self Void, and following into the tomb and helping the injury, there is guilt and demotion.

> [!note] 按
> 「治筑」是治河筑堤一类的工程，「河工」是治河工程。治筑章前面讲的是堤岸能不能修成（用父母为用，不属事业域）；这几句讲的是被派去办这件事的人：大官奉勅、小官奉命、吏民奉差，官爻不可病、不可冲克世；官吏因河工占利钝（得失），这时反过来以官爻为重——官旺世实则被记功、升荐，官伤、世空、随墓助伤则获罪降责。「奉勅」是奉皇帝的敕命，「奉差」是奉派差使。「纪录」是记功。「罪戾」是罪过。与易林补遗谋差顶役（EL.106–108）、黄金策仕宦的巡察四方（HJC.23）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhizhu' is works such as managing a river and building embankments, and 'hegong' is river works. The earlier part of the chapter on building and repair speaks of whether the embankments can be completed (taking Resource as the Subject, which is not Career); these sentences speak of the people sent to carry it out: a great official on an imperial edict, a lesser one on an order, clerks and commoners on an assignment, for whom the Pressure line must not be sick and must not clash with or control the Self; and for an official or clerk asking about gain and loss in connection with river works, Pressure then counts for most the other way round: with Pressure thriving and the Self solid he is recorded for merit and recommended, and with Pressure injured, the Self Void, and following into the tomb and helping the injury he is found guilty and demoted. 'Fengchi' is acting on an imperial edict, and 'fengchai' is acting on an assignment. 'Jilu' is recording merit. 'Zuili' is guilt. Read against the Yilin Buyi on seeking a commission and taking a place (EL.106-108) and the Huangjin Ce on touring and inspecting the regions (HJC.23).
