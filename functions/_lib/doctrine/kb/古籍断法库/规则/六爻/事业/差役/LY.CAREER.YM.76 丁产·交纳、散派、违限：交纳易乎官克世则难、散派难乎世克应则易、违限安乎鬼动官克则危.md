# 丁产·交纳、散派、违限：交纳易乎官克世则难、散派难乎世克应则易、违限安乎鬼动官克则危

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 交纳（缴纳钱粮）容易吗？官克世，就难；散派（摊派）难吗？世克应，就容易；违限（逾期）安稳吗？鬼动、官克，就危险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Is paying in easy? With Pressure controlling the Self it is hard. Is apportioning hard? With the Self controlling the Other it is easy. Is missing the deadline safe? With Pressure moving and Pressure controlling, it is dangerous.

## 《易冒·丁产章第六十九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1575 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 69: Households and holdings", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 交纳易乎，官克世而难也， | 交纳容易吗？官克世，就难； | Is paying in easy? With Pressure controlling the Self it is hard; |
> | 散派难乎，世克应而易也， | 散派难吗？世克应，就容易； | is apportioning hard? With the Self controlling the Other it is easy; |
> | 违限安乎，鬼动官克而危也。 | 违限安稳吗？鬼动、官克，就危险。 | is missing the deadline safe? With Pressure moving and Pressure controlling, it is dangerous. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「交纳」是缴纳钱粮，「散派」是官府把赋役分摊给下面的人，「违限」是错过规定的期限。「官克世而难」：官克世是官府压住我，所以缴纳难；「世克应而易」：世克应是我压得住对方，所以摊派容易。按文意，交纳时我是被派的一方，摊派时我是派人的一方（收粮的里长、粮长一类），所以同一个官爻、世应，在这两问里的好坏方向不同，要看清自己站在哪一边。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiaona' is paying in grain and money, 'sanpai' is the government apportioning levies and services among those below, and 'weixian' is missing the prescribed deadline. 'Pressure controlling the Self, so hard': Pressure controlling the Self is the authorities pressing on me, so paying in is hard; 'the Self controlling the Other, so easy': the Self controlling the Other is my being able to press on the other side, so apportioning is easy. By the sense, in paying in I am the one assigned, and in apportioning I am the one assigning (a village head or grain chief who collects the grain), so the same Pressure line and Self and Other have a different direction of good and bad in these two questions, and one must see on which side one stands.
