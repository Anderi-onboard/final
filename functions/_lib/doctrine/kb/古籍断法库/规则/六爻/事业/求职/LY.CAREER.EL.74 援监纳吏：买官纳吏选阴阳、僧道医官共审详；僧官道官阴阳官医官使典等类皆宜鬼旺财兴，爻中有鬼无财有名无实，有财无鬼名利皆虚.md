# 援监纳吏：买官纳吏选阴阳、僧道医官共审详；僧官道官阴阳官医官使典等类皆宜鬼旺财兴，爻中有鬼无财有名无实，有财无鬼名利皆虚

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买官、纳吏、选阴阳官，以及僧官、道官、医官，都要仔细审察；僧官、道官、阴阳官、医官、使典这一类，都适宜官鬼旺相、财爻兴旺；爻里有官鬼没有财，有名无实；有财爻没有官鬼，名和利都落空。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Buying office, buying a clerkship, being selected as a Yin-Yang official, and the monk, Daoist and medical officials, are all to be examined in detail; for monk-officials, Daoist officials, Yin-Yang officials, medical officials, clerks and the like, it is fitting that Pressure be thriving and Wealth flourishing; if there is Pressure among the lines but no Wealth, there is a name without substance; if there is Wealth but no Pressure, both name and profit are empty.

> [!note] 按
> 「阴阳官」是地方上掌管阴阳（天文历数、选择吉日）的小官；「僧官」「道官」是管僧、道的官；「医官」是医学的官；「使典」是衙门里办事的差役、典吏一类。这几类都是靠捐纳或选授得来的小官职，所以要官鬼（名）和财爻（利）两样都在。这一条与黄金策仕宦「僧道医官，岂可文书发动」（HJC.27）、增删卜易「僧官医官·亦喜官文发动」（ZS.53）说的是同一类人，但取法不同：那两处看子孙，这里看官鬼和财。
>
> *Note:* 'Yin-Yang official' is a minor local official in charge of yin and yang (the calendar and astronomy, and the choice of lucky days); 'monk-officials' and 'Daoist officials' are the officials who supervise monks and Daoists; 'medical official' is an official of medicine; 'shidian' are the runners and clerks who do the work in a yamen. These are all minor posts obtained by contribution or selection, so both Pressure (the name) and Wealth (the profit) must be present. This entry speaks of the same kinds of men as 'for monk, Daoist and medical officials, how could the document move' in the Huangjin Ce on official careers (HJC.27) and 'monk-officials and medical officials also like the star of office and the document to move' in the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.53), but takes a different method: those two look at Output, and this one looks at Pressure and Wealth.
