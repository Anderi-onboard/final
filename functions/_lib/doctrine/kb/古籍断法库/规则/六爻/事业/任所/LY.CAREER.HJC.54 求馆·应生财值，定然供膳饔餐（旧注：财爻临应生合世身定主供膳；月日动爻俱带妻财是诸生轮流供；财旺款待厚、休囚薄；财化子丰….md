# 求馆·应生财值，定然供膳饔餐（旧注：财爻临应生合世身定主供膳；月日动爻俱带妻财是诸生轮流供；财旺款待厚、休囚薄；财化子丰洁、化鬼无美味、化父淡薄、化兄常食而已）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻临在应爻上、生合世爻身爻，一定供给膳食，因为财就是饮食；月建、日辰、动爻都带妻财，一定不是一家做东供膳，而是各位学生轮流供给；财旺相款待丰厚，休囚款待菲薄，财化子孙丰盛洁净，化官鬼没有美味，化父母清淡，化兄弟不过是平常饭食。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth rests on the Other and generates or combines with the Self or the Body, meals will surely be provided, because Wealth is food and drink; if the Month, the Day and the moving lines all carry Wealth, it is surely not one family acting as host but the pupils providing in turn; thriving Wealth means generous hospitality and resting or trapped Wealth meagre, Wealth turning into Output means plentiful and clean food, into Pressure no delicacies, into Resource plain fare, and into Peer no more than everyday food.

> | 应生财值，定然供膳饔餐。 | 应爻生财、财爻当值，一定供给饭食。 | If the Other generates Wealth and Wealth is on duty, meals will surely be provided. |

^q1

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「饔餐」是熟食、饭食。财爻化出什么六亲，就看饭食的样子（化子孙丰洁、化官鬼无美味、化父母淡薄、化兄弟平常）：旧注细，王洪绪只写了前两句和旺衰厚薄。
>
> *Note:* 'Cooked meals' is prepared food. What Wealth turns into gives the look of the meals (Output plentiful and clean, Pressure no delicacies, Resource plain, Peer everyday): the old commentary is detailed, and Wang Hongxu writes only the first two points and the generosity by strength.
