# 在任吉凶·世临官而遇马，差遣烦劳（官星持世，明动暗动加临驿马必有差遣；日月动爻作财生扶，差中得利；兄爻发动日月动爻刑克世，辛苦赔财；盼差遣者世动即有差）

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻临官星又遇驿马，会有差遣，烦劳辛苦：官星持世，明动、暗动又加临驿马的，必定有差遣；日月、动爻作财来生扶的，在差遣中能得到利益；卦中兄爻发动，日月、动爻刑克世爻的，辛苦还要赔钱；心里盼着得到差遣的人，不用等驿马加临，只要世爻发动就有差遣了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self sits on the star of office and meets the Post Horse, there will be commissions and toil: if the star of office holds the Self and moves openly or in secret upon the Post Horse, there will surely be a commission; if the Day, Month or a moving line acts as Wealth and generates or supports it, one profits from the commission; if a Peer line moves in the hexagram and the Day, Month or a moving line punishes or controls the Self, one toils and loses money as well; for one who hopes in his heart for a commission, there is no need to wait for the Post Horse, for as soon as the Self moves there is a commission.
