# 谋望占·可否成败（四）：成败于何人以六亲定——本宫内卦至亲、本宫外卦远亲、他宫内卦邻里、他宫外卦远方人，鬼加贵缙绅否则牙人保人媒人，八卦定其方；托人图事外人看应、家亲看用爻，应爻用爻犯日破月破旬空及空死墓绝胎无能赞襄，生旺化空死墓绝胎有始无终，冲克刑害世必怀欺，六冲终反目，虎损害蛇心变勾愚钝武诈欺雀多诳语，惟旺相带龙喜成神生合世身者赖其扶持

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想知道事情成败在哪个人身上，就用六亲来定：本宫的内卦，是至亲；本宫的外卦，是远亲；他宫的内卦，是邻里；他宫的外卦，是远方的人；官鬼加贵人，是缙绅，否则是牙人、保人、媒人；想知道是哪个方向的人，用八卦来定。又有托人办事的，外人看应爻，亲人看用爻。凡应爻、用爻犯日破、月破、旬空的，都主受托的人没有能力帮忙，空、死、墓、绝、胎也一样；生旺而变成空、死、墓、绝、胎的，必有始无终；冲、克、刑、害世爻的，受托的人必定怀着欺骗，难以托付；六冲的卦，终究反目成仇。白虎发动的，损害；螣蛇发动的，变心；勾陈发动的，愚钝；玄武发动的，欺诈；朱雀旺相落空而发动的，多说谎话。只有旺相、带青龙、喜神、成神，发动来生合世爻、身爻的，才靠他扶持把事办成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know through whom the matter succeeds or fails, fix it by the six kin: the inner trigram of the home palace is the nearest kin; the outer trigram of the home palace, distant kin; the inner trigram of another palace, neighbours; the outer trigram of another palace, men from afar; Pressure with the Noble is gentry, otherwise a broker, a guarantor or a go-between; the direction is fixed by the eight trigrams. If one entrusts someone with the matter, outsiders are seen through the Other and kin through the Subject line. Whenever the Other or the Subject line suffers Day-break, Month-break or decade Void, the man entrusted has no ability to help, and so too for Void, death, tomb, extinction and womb; if thriving and turning into Void, death, tomb, extinction or womb, there is a beginning without an end; if the Self is clashed, controlled, punished or harmed, the man entrusted surely harbours deceit and cannot be relied on; a six-clash hexagram ends in a falling out. If the Tiger moves, he does harm; the Snake, his heart changes; Hook Earth, he is dull; the Dark Tortoise, he cheats; the Bird thriving and Void and moving, he tells many lies. Only if he is thriving and carries the Dragon, Joy and the Completion spirit and moves to generate or combine with the Self and Body does one rely on his support to get the matter done.

> [!note] 按
> 「本宫」「他宫」指占得的这一卦所属的宫和别的宫（本宫是用爻或世爻所在的宫，书里没有展开，译文照字面）。「缙绅」是做官的人和乡绅；「牙人」是经纪，「保人」是担保的人，「媒人」是中间说合的人。「赞襄」是帮助。日破是被日辰冲的爻。「成神」书里没有解释，是神煞。「雀旺空动」按字面是朱雀旺相、落空、而发动。
>
> *Note:* 'Home palace' and 'another palace' mean the palace to which the hexagram cast belongs and other palaces (the book does not spell out whose palace the home palace is, and the translation follows the letter). 'Jinshen' are officials and country gentry; 'yaren' are brokers, 'baoren' guarantors, and 'meiren' go-betweens. 'Zanxiang' is helping. Day-break is a line clashed by the Day. 'Chengshen' (the Completion spirit) is a spirit-star that the book does not explain. 'Que wang kong dong' is by the letter the Vermilion Bird thriving, Void, and moving.
