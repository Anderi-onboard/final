# 国朝·凶神生合世神，玄宗信林甫之佞（旧注：鬼杀发动生合世爻，必是佞臣阿谀、人君信任，如唐玄宗信任李林甫十九年，养成天下大乱）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼、凶煞发动来生合世爻，必定是奸佞的臣子阿谀奉承、国君信任他，就像唐玄宗信任李林甫十九年，养成了天下大乱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a Pressure line or a baleful spirit moves to generate or combine with the Self, a treacherous minister is surely flattering the ruler and the ruler trusts him, like Emperor Xuanzong of Tang who trusted Li Linfu for nineteen years and bred great disorder in the realm.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5876 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凶神生合世神，玄宗信林甫之佞； | 凶神生合世神，就像唐玄宗相信李林甫的奸佞； | If a baleful spirit generates or combines with the Self, it is like Xuanzong believing in the treachery of Li Linfu; |
