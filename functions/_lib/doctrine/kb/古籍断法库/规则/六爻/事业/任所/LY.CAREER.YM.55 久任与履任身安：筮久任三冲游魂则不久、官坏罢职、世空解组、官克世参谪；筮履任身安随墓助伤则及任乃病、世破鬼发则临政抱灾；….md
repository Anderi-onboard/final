# 久任与履任身安：筮久任三冲游魂则不久、官坏罢职、世空解组、官克世参谪；筮履任身安随墓助伤则及任乃病、世破鬼发则临政抱灾；久任以官为官、身安以官为病，妙在变

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占能不能久任：三冲、游魂就不长久；官坏就罢职；世空就解印离任；官克世就被参劾、贬谪。占到任后身体平安与否：随墓、助伤，就是一到任就生病；世破、鬼发，就是临政时抱灾祸。占久任，以官爻为官职本身；占身安，以官爻为病；占卜取用爻，妙处在于变通。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining whether one will keep the post long: the three clashes or Wandering Soul mean it will not last; Pressure damaged means dismissal; the Self Void means laying down the seal and leaving the post; Pressure controlling the Self means impeachment and demotion. In divining whether one will be safe in body on taking up the post: following into the tomb and helping the injury means falling ill on arriving at the post; the Self broken and Pressure moving means bearing calamity while in charge of government. For a long tenure the Pressure line is taken as the office itself; for bodily safety it is taken as illness; in divining, the wonder of using a line lies in the change.

> [!note] 按
> 「久任」是长久任职，「履任」是到任，「身安」是到任后自身平安。「三冲」（见 YM.07 注）是冲散，「游魂」是迁动，都主不长久。「罢职」是革职，「解组」是解下印绶、卸任，「参谪」是被参劾、谪贬。「及任乃病」是一到任就生病。注里「久任以官为官，身安以官为病」：同一个官爻，占久任取它作官职（官坏就罢），占身安取它作病（鬼发就是病灾）；「妙在变尔」书里没有再展开，「变」字这里按文意读作变通，取什么要看所占的事。和黄金策仕宦的世动逢空（HJC.08）、卦静世空（HJC.16）、易隐官禄占在任（YY.14–YY.20）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiuren' is holding a post for a long time, 'lüren' is taking up the post, and 'shen an' is one's own safety after taking up the post. The 'three clashes' (see the note to YM.07) are clash and scatter and 'Wandering Soul' is shifting, both meaning it does not last. 'Bazhi' is dismissal, 'jiezu' is untying the seal-ribbon and leaving the post, and 'canzhe' is being impeached and demoted. 'Ji ren nai bing' is falling ill on arriving at the post. In the note 'for long tenure take Pressure as the office, for bodily safety take Pressure as illness': the same Pressure line is taken as the office when divining about tenure (damaged Pressure means dismissal), and as illness when divining about safety (Pressure issuing means a sickness); 'miao zai bian er' is not expanded in the book, and 'bian' is read here by the sense as adapting, what is taken depending on what is asked. Compare the Huangjin Ce on the Self moving into Void (HJC.08) and the still hexagram with a Void Self (HJC.16), and the Yi Yin on being in office (YY.14-YY.20).
