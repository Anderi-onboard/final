# 征战占·六亲（四）：子旺先锋谋勇，衰墓懦弱，空绝有难，化败死墓绝胎必败绩；子带大杀动克应先锋冲突取胜；子旺受刑害克冲勿轻出；子衰得日辰动爻生合用多取胜，子旺动克众官用少取胜；子化兄将弱功成，化父兵骄致败；世下伏子合应先锋降敌，加日辰生子被唆降仇

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 先锋：子孙旺相，先锋有谋有勇；衰、墓，懦弱无能；落空、绝，先锋有难；变成败、死、墓、绝、胎，必定战败。子孙带大杀发动克应爻的，先锋冲突取胜；子孙旺相却受刑、害、克、冲的，不要轻易出战；子孙衰弱而得日辰、动爻生合的，以多取胜；子孙旺相发动克众多官鬼的，以少取胜；子孙变兄爻，将弱而成功；子孙变父爻，兵骄而致败。世爻下伏着子孙并合应爻的，先锋投降敌人；再加日辰生子孙的，必定被人唆使怂恿而投降仇敌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The vanguard. If Output is thriving, the vanguard has both wit and courage; if weak or in tomb, timid and incapable; if Void or extinct, the vanguard is in trouble; if it turns into spent, dead, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, it surely loses the battle. If Output carrying a great baleful spirit moves to control the Other, the vanguard wins by a charge; if Output is thriving but punished, harmed, controlled or clashed, do not go out lightly; if Output is weak but generated or combined by the Day or a moving line, win with numbers; if it is thriving and moves to control many Pressure lines, win with few; if Output turns into Peer, the commander is weak but succeeds; if Output turns into Resource, the troops grow proud and bring defeat. If Output lies hidden under the Self and combines with the Other, the vanguard goes over to the enemy; with the Day generating Output as well, he will surely be egged on to surrender to his enemy.

> [!note] 按
> 第四句「必败续」疑是「必败绩」（败绩是战败），第五句「先锋无突取胜」的「无突」疑是「冲突」（冲锋），译文按「败绩」「冲突」译，没有改原文字。「官」在「克众官」里指官鬼。「唆赞」是唆使怂恿。
>
> *Note:* In the fourth sentence 'bi bai xu' is presumably 'bi bai ji', defeat in battle, and in the fifth 'xianfeng wu tu qu sheng' the 'wu tu' is presumably 'chong tu', to charge; the translation reads 'defeat' and 'charge' without changing the original characters. 'Official' in 'control many officials' means the Pressure lines. 'Suozan' is egging on.
