# 平生学艺：凡习经书父须生旺、若求官职鬼莫空无、祝巫大利官兴僧道惟宜子旺；习儒业者父母为用，求功名者官鬼为用，祝巫迎神召将照马关亡之类亦用官爻，僧道之流子孙为用，凡用爻皆宜旺相各忌空亡；诸般道术一应生涯皆把财为衣禄养命之源兼推子乃福神发家之主，诸般艺业皆宜财神兴隆财若空亡利资必绝望

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 学经书，父爻必须生旺；求官职，官鬼不可空无；祝巫大利官爻兴旺，僧道只适宜子孙旺。学儒业的，父母爻是用爻；求功名的，官鬼是用爻；祝巫一类，迎神召将、照马、关亡之类，也用官爻；只有僧道一流，以子孙为用。凡是用爻都适宜旺相，各自忌空亡：旺就事事有成，空就样样不成。各种道术、一切生计，都把财当作衣食俸禄、养命的源头，并且要推子孙是福神、发家的主；各种艺业，都适宜财神（财爻）兴隆；财如果空亡，利润资财一定没有指望了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In studying the classics, Resource must be thriving; in seeking office, Pressure must not be empty; for diviner-priests and shamans it is greatly favourable for the star of office to flourish, and for monks and Daoists only Output thriving is fitting. For the scholarly calling, Resource is the Subject line; for those seeking fame and office, Pressure is the Subject; for shamans, those who greet gods and summon generals, 'look at the horse' and 'close the gate on the dead' and the like, the Pressure line is also used; only for the monks and Daoists is Output the Subject. Every Subject line should be thriving and each fears Void: if thriving everything succeeds, and if Void nothing is accomplished. For every kind of Daoist art and every livelihood, Wealth is taken as the source of food, clothing, pay and the sustaining of life, and Output must also be reckoned as the fortune spirit and the master of making a household prosper; in every kind of art and trade it is fitting for Wealth to flourish, and if Wealth is Void, profit and funds are surely without hope.

> [!note] 按
> 「祝巫」是祝由、巫术一类的行当；「照马」「关亡」是民间神道里的两种法事，书里没有细说。「儒业」是读书应试的业。这一条是看行业取什么用神：儒业取父母，功名取官鬼，祝巫取官爻，僧道取子孙，其余一切生计取财。僧道取子孙与黄金策「僧道医官，岂可文书发动」（HJC.27）、增删卜易僧官医官（ZS.53）说的是同一个取法。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhuwu' is the callings of invocation and healing by charms and of shamanism; 'zhaoma' and 'guanwang' are two kinds of rite of the folk religion, and the book does not go into them. 'Ruye' is the calling of studying for examinations. This entry looks at what Subject each calling takes: the scholarly calling takes Resource, fame and office take Pressure, shamans take the Pressure line, monks and Daoists take Output, and every other livelihood takes Wealth. That monks and Daoists take Output is the same method as 'for monk, Daoist and medical officials, how could the document move' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.27) and the Zengshan Buyi on monk-officials and medical officials (ZS.53).
