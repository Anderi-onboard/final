# 投麾效用·官宜旺相不宜空破休囚，世与财忌刑冲克坏（世、官、财哪个失陷或动变凶各有其害；官持世财相生、官世临日月、世动化吉，封拜可期；觉子：先识来人之意，未见用而问收用与否，则官星是收用之官）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 投军效用：官星要旺相，不能落空、被破、休囚；妻财和世爻两个爻，怕遇到刑、冲、克、坏。世爻与官星都要旺相又变出吉象，财是俸禄的供养，哪能让日月来刑冲？世爻失陷或发动变凶，自身尤其难保；官星失陷或发动变凶，难许成名；财爻失陷或发动变凶，缺粮没有俸禄，官星再旺也像厨房没有烟火。只喜欢官星持世、有财发动生它、财爻持世、官星发动与它相合，或官星与世爻临日月，以及太岁、月建、日辰相生，或世爻发动变吉，将来封官拜将可以期待。觉子说：要先识得来占的人的用意，为名利而问的这样断；如果问的是还没被任用时能不能被收用，官星就是收用他的那个官，官星来生合世爻的必收，官爻克世爻、或落空落破的不录用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On joining the colours: the star of office should be thriving and must not be Void, broken, or resting or trapped, and the Wealth line and the Self line fear punishment, clash, control and ruin. The Self and the star of office should both be thriving and turn into auspicious lines, and Wealth is the support of the salary, so how could the Day and Month punish or clash with it? If the Self is lost or moves and turns bad, one's own safety is all the harder to keep; if the star of office is lost or moves and turns bad, a name is hard to promise; if the Wealth line is lost or moves and turns bad, there is no grain and no salary, and even if the star of office is thriving no smoke rises from the kitchen stove. What is welcomed is the star of office holding the Self with moving Wealth generating it, the Wealth line holding the Self, the star of office moving to combine with it, the star of office and the Self sitting on the Day or Month, Tai Sui, the Month and the Day generating each other, or the Self moving and turning auspicious: then investiture and the rank of general may be hoped for. Juezi says: first understand the intent of the person who comes; for one asking for rank and profit this is how to judge; if he asks whether, not yet employed, he can be taken on, the star of office is the officer who takes him on: if it comes to generate or combine with the Self he will surely be taken on, and if the Pressure line controls the Self or is Void or broken he will not be enrolled.
