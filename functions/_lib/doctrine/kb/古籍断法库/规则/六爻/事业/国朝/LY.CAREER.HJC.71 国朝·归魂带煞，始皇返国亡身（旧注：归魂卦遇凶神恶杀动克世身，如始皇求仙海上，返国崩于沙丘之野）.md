# 国朝·归魂带煞，始皇返国亡身（旧注：归魂卦遇凶神恶杀动克世身，如始皇求仙海上，返国崩于沙丘之野）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国君自占得归魂卦，又遇凶神恶煞发动来克世爻或卦身，就像秦始皇到海上求仙，回国后死在沙丘。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the ruler casts for himself, a Returning Soul hexagram that meets baleful spirits moving to control the Self or the Body is like the First Emperor of Qin, who sought immortals by the sea and died at Shaqiu after returning to his state.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5840 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 归魂带煞，始皇返国亡身。 | 归魂卦带着凶煞，就像秦始皇回到国都途中丧了命。 | If a Returning Soul hexagram carries a baleful spirit, it is like the First Emperor who returned to his state and lost his life. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5841 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若归魂卦，遇凶神恶杀，动克世身，如始皇求仙海上，返国崩于沙丘之野。 | 如果是归魂卦，遇到凶神恶煞发动来克世爻或卦身，就像秦始皇在海上求仙，回国后死在沙丘的原野上。 | If it is a Returning Soul hexagram and meets baleful spirits that move and control the Self or the Body, it is like the First Emperor who sought immortals by the sea and died in the wilds of Shaqiu after returning to his state. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1478 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若归魂卦遇动爻克世身，如始皇求仙海上，返国崩于沙丘。 | 如果是归魂卦，遇到动爻克世爻或卦身，就像秦始皇在海上求仙，回国后死在沙丘。 | If it is a Returning Soul hexagram and meets a moving line that controls the Self or the Body, it is like the First Emperor who sought immortals by the sea and died at Shaqiu after returning to his state. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注要「凶神恶杀」发动克世身；卜筮正宗只说「遇动爻克世身」，没有说凶神恶煞。「世身」按世爻与卦身两者读。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary requires baleful spirits moving to control the Self and Body; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says only 'meets a moving line that controls the Self and Body', without baleful spirits. 'Self and Body' is read as the Self line and the Body together.
