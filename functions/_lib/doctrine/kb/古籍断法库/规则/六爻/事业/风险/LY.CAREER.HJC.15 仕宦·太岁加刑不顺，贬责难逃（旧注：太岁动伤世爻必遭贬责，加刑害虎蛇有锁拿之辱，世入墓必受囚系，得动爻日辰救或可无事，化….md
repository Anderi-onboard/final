# 仕宦·太岁加刑不顺，贬责难逃（旧注：太岁动伤世爻必遭贬责，加刑害虎蛇有锁拿之辱，世入墓必受囚系，得动爻日辰救或可无事，化出子孙罪终不免，月建同看）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁出现、发动伤克世爻，必定遭到贬责；再加上刑、害、白虎、螣蛇等煞，必有被锁拿擒捕的耻辱；世爻入墓，必受囚禁，得到动爻、日辰来救，或者可以无事；但怕化出子孙，罪终究免不了。月建也照这样看。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Tai Sui appears and moves to injure or control the Self, one will surely be demoted and rebuked; if punishment, harm, the White Tiger, the Coiling Snake and other killers are added, there will surely be the disgrace of being chained and seized; if the Self enters a tomb, one will surely be imprisoned, and if a moving line or the Day comes to the rescue one may perhaps come to no harm; but if it turns into Output, the offence cannot in the end be escaped. The Month is looked at in the same way.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1956 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 太岁动伤世爻，必遭贬谪，更加刑害虎蛇，必有锁拘擒拿之辱。 | 太岁发动伤克世爻，必定遭到贬谪，再加上刑害、白虎、螣蛇，必有被锁拿擒获的耻辱。 | If the Tai Sui moves to injure or control the Self, one will surely be demoted and exiled, and if punishment, harm, the Tiger and the Snake are added, there will surely be the disgrace of being chained and seized. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全经文写「贬责难逃」，卜筮正宗写「贬谪难逃」（「责」是责罚，「谪」是贬官并外放）。「虎蛇」是白虎、螣蛇两个六神；旧注里的「锁扭」，王洪绪写作「锁拘」，是锁拿拘禁。世爻「入墓」的意思是世爻落在墓库里，被困住，所以主囚系。这一条和 LY.CAREER.HJC.12（日辰冲克）一起，讲的是太岁、日辰这样的「上面」压到世爻上。
>
> *Note:* The verse is 'demotion and rebuke are hard to escape' in the Bushi Daquan and 'demotion and exile are hard to escape' in the Bushi Zhengzong (the one is rebuke and punishment, the other is being demoted and sent away). 'The Tiger and the Snake' are the two spirits, the White Tiger and the Coiling Snake; the old commentary's 'chained' is written 'chained and held' by Wang Hongxu, being chained and confined. 'The Self enters a tomb' means the Self falls into a tomb and is shut in, hence imprisonment. With LY.CAREER.HJC.12 (the Day clashes or controls) this entry speaks of 'those above', the Tai Sui and the Day, pressing on the Self.
