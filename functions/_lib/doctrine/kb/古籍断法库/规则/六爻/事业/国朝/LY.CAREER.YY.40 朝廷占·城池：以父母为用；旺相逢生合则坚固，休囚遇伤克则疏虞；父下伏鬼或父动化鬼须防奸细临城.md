# 朝廷占·城池：以父母为用；旺相逢生合则坚固，休囚遇伤克则疏虞；父下伏鬼或父动化鬼须防奸细临城

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 城池：以父母为用神。父母爻旺相又遇生合，城池坚固；休囚又遇伤克，防守疏漏；父爻下面伏着官鬼，或父爻发动变官鬼的，须提防奸细到城下。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* City walls and moats. Take Resource as the Subject. If it is thriving and meets generation or combination, the walls are firm; if it is resting or trapped and meets injury or control, the defence is lax; if Pressure lies hidden under the Resource line, or Resource moves and turns into Pressure, beware of spies at the city.
