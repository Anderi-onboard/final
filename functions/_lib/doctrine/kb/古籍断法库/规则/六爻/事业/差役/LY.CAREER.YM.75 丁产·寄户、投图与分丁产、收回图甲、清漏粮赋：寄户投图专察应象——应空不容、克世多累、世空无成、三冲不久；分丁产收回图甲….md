# 丁产·寄户、投图与分丁产、收回图甲、清漏粮赋：寄户投图专察应象——应空不容、克世多累、世空无成、三冲不久；分丁产收回图甲清漏粮赋皆以助伤随墓为恶、鬼动克世为凶

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占寄户（把户口寄在别人名下）、投图（投入某个里图），专看应象：应空，对方不容；克世，多有牵累；世空，成不了；三冲，不长久。至于分丁产、收回图甲、清漏粮赋，都以助伤、随墓为恶，鬼动克世为凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about lodging a household or joining a registry one looks only at the Other image: the Other Void, he will not take one in; controlling the Self, there are many burdens; the Self Void, it will not come off; the three clashes, it will not last. As for dividing the males and property, taking back the registry and tithing, and clearing omitted grain and levies, all take helping the injury and following into the tomb as bad, and Pressure moving to control the Self as ominous.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「寄户」是把户口寄在别人的户名下，「投图」是投入某个里图（「图」是里甲制里按里编的册籍单位，各地叫法不一）。「不容」是对方不肯接纳。「分丁产」是把人丁和产业分开，「收回图甲」是收回原来的册籍归属，「清漏粮赋」是清查漏报、漏纳的钱粮赋税。「三冲」见 YM.07 注。「助伤随墓」就是「随墓助伤」。这一条的头两种（寄户、投图）是求别人接纳，所以看应；后三种（分丁产、收回图甲、清漏）是和官府的清理，所以怕官鬼。
>
> *Note:* 'Jihu' is lodging one's household under another's household name, and 'toutu' is joining a 'tu' (a unit of the registers compiled by 'li' under the 'lijia' system, called differently in different places). 'Burong' is the other side refusing to receive one. 'Fen ding chan' is separating the males from the property, 'shouhui tujia' is taking back the former attribution in the registers, and 'qinglou liangfu' is auditing omitted or unpaid grain and levies. For the 'three clashes' see the note to YM.07. 'Zhu shang sui mu' is 'following into the tomb and helping the injury'. The first two divinations here (lodging a household and joining a registry) ask another to receive one, so look at the Other; the last three (dividing males and property, taking back the registry, clearing omissions) are settlements with the authorities, so Pressure is feared.
