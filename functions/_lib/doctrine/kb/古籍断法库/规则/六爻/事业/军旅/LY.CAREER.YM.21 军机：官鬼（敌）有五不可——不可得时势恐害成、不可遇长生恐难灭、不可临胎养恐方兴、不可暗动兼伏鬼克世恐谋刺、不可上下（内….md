# 军机：官鬼（敌）有五不可——不可得时势恐害成、不可遇长生恐难灭、不可临胎养恐方兴、不可暗动兼伏鬼克世恐谋刺、不可上下（内外）克世恐内应外通

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼是敌，有五件事不可：不可得时得势，怕它妨害成功；不可遇长生，怕它难以消灭；不可临胎、养，怕它正在兴起；不可暗动，再兼伏着的鬼克世，怕它谋划行刺；不可内外卦都有鬼克世，怕它内应外通。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure is the enemy, and there are five things it must not do: it must not obtain season and momentum, for fear it harms the success; it must not meet Birth, for fear it is hard to destroy; it must not stand at Foetus or Nourishment, for fear it is just rising; it must not move in secret, nor, with a hidden Pressure line, control the Self, for fear of a plot to stab; and it must not control the Self from both the inner and the outer trigram, for fear of collusion between inside and outside.

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 上下鬼克世，即内外鬼也。 | 上下鬼克世，就是内外卦都有鬼。 | Pressure above and below controlling the Self means Pressure in both the inner and the outer trigram. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「得时势」是当令有力。「长生」「胎」「养」是十二长生里的位置：长生是刚生出来，胎和养是还在孕育，都是正在起来的意思，所以鬼落在这些位上，敌人还在长。「暗动」是静爻被冲起（见术语表的冲）。「伏鬼」是卦里没有官鬼、伏在别的爻下。「谋刺」是谋划行刺。书里自注：「上下鬼克世」就是内外卦都有鬼。与易隐征战占的鬼动一组（YY.54）、本章防刺行刺（YM.15）合着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Obtaining season and momentum' is being in season and having force. Birth, Foetus and Nourishment are places in the twelve stages of life: Birth is just being born, and Foetus and Nourishment are still being conceived and nursed, all meaning that something is just beginning to rise, so Pressure standing in these places means the enemy is still growing. 'Moving in secret' is a still line being clashed into motion (see the glossary entry on clash). A 'hidden Pressure line' is one absent from the hexagram and hidden under another line. A 'plot to stab' is planning an assassination. The book's own gloss says 'Pressure above and below controlling the Self' means Pressure in both the inner and outer trigrams. Read together with the group on Pressure moving in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.54) and with guarding against and carrying out assassination in this chapter (YM.15).
