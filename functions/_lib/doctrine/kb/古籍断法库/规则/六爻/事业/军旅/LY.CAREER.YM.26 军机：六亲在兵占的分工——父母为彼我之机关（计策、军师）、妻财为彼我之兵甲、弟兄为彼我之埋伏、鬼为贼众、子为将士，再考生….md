# 军机：六亲在兵占的分工——父母为彼我之机关（计策、军师）、妻财为彼我之兵甲、弟兄为彼我之埋伏、鬼为贼众、子为将士，再考生克衰旺以分美恶

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母是彼此双方的机关（计策，也是军师），妻财是双方的兵器甲胄，弟兄是双方的埋伏，鬼是贼众，子是将士；再考察生克衰旺，来分辨好坏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource is the machinery of both sides (the plans, and also the army's counsellor), Wealth is the weapons and armour of both sides, Peer is the ambush of both sides, Pressure is the bandit host and Output is the officers and men; then one examines generation, control, decline and thriving to tell the fine from the bad.

> [!warning] 打架
> 和易隐征战占的六亲分法（YY.57）比较：父母是军师、兄弟是埋伏、官鬼是敌人，两书一致；分歧在另外两亲——易隐以妻财为粮草、以子孙为先锋，易冒以妻财为兵器甲胄、以子孙为将士。粮草和兵甲都算『物』，先锋和将士都算『人』，所以只是取象的粗细不同；但断粮草缺不缺时，按易隐看妻财，按易冒看的是兵器甲胄，不能把两本书的说法混着用。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Compared with the division of the six kin in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.57): Resource as counsellor, Peer as ambush and Pressure as enemy agree in the two books; the difference is in the other two kin, where the Yi Yin takes Wealth as provisions and Output as the vanguard, while the Yimao takes Wealth as weapons and armour and Output as officers and men. Provisions and arms both count as things, and vanguard and officers both count as people, so it is only a difference in the fineness of the image; but in judging whether provisions run short, the Yi Yin looks at Wealth for grain and the Yimao for weapons and armour, and the two books' sayings should not be mixed.

> [!note] 按
> 「机关」是机要、计谋。「兵甲」是兵器和甲胄，和官鬼（贼众）、子孙（将士）分开：子孙是人，妻财是物。「埋伏」是设伏的兵。「彼我」是双方各有一份，所以同一类爻在我是我的，在敌是敌的，看它落在世这一边还是应这一边。书里自注：父母既是计策也是军师。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiguan' is key machinery and planning. 'Bingjia' is weapons and armour, kept apart from Pressure (the bandit host) and Output (the officers and men): Output is people, Wealth is things. 'Maifu' is troops set in ambush. 'Both sides' means each side has a share, so the same kind of line is mine when it falls on my side and the enemy's when it falls on his, according to whether it stands on the Self side or the Other side. The book's own gloss: Resource is both the plan and the counsellor.
