# 仕宦升迁：世空未得高升任、财动还须佐帝皇；世爻为自身本官，自卜当察世爻、世若空亡未能迁转，他人代卜又不取世爻为主专看官爻、若得财动生官自然显达

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻是自己本人的官职：自己占卜，要察看世爻，世爻落空，就不能升迁；替别人占卜，就不取世爻为主，专看官爻，如果得到财爻发动生官爻，自然显达，能去辅佐帝王。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line is one's own post: in divining for oneself, look at the Self line, and if the Self is Void one cannot be promoted or transferred; in divining on behalf of another, do not take the Self as the chief but look only at the Pressure line, and if Wealth moves to generate Pressure, one naturally rises to eminence and goes on to assist the sovereign.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 766 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世爻为自身本官，自卜当察世爻，世若空亡未能迁转。 | 世爻是自己本人的官职，自己占卜，就该察看世爻；世爻如果空亡，就不能迁转。 | The Self line is one's own post; in divining for oneself one should examine the Self line, and if the Self is Void, one cannot be promoted or transferred. |
> | 他人代卜，又不取世爻为主，专看官爻，若得财动生官，自然显达。 | 别人代为占卜，就不取世爻为主，专看官爻；如果得到财爻发动生官爻，自然显达。 | If another divines on one's behalf, the Self is not taken as the chief; look only at the Pressure line, and if Wealth moves to generate Pressure, one naturally rises to eminence. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「本官」是自己现任的官职。「他人代卜」书里没有说明代的是谁；按文意是替别人占他的升迁，世爻不再是被问的人，所以改看官爻——这是据文意的读法。这一条与黄金策「世动逢空，官居不久」（HJC.08）同看世爻：自占看世爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Benguan' is the post one now holds. The book does not say who is divining 'on behalf of another'; by the sense it is divining another person's promotion, so the Self is no longer the person asked about and the Pressure line is looked at instead; this is a reading from the sense. This entry looks at the Self as the Huangjin Ce does in 'if the Self moves and meets Void, one does not stay long in office' (HJC.08): in divining for oneself, look at the Self.
