# 仕宦升迁：鬼变退神宜致仕、化居墓绝早还乡；鬼爻若化退气或变入墓绝之中，莫望升迁，远归田里

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼变成退神，适宜退休（致仕）；变入墓、绝，就早早回乡。官鬼爻如果化成退气，或者变入墓、绝之中，就不要指望升迁，只能远远地回到乡里。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure changes into a retreating spirit it is fitting to retire from office; if it changes into tomb or extinction, one returns home early. If the Pressure line changes into a retreating qi, or changes into tomb or extinction, do not hope for promotion, but go far away and return to the countryside.

> [!note] 按
> 「退气」就是退神（见术语表）：变爻往后退一步。「致仕」是退休，「田里」是乡里、田园。这一条与易隐官禄占「游魂化归魂宜致仕」（YY.20）和增删卜易「官化退、休致须宜化退」（ZS.47）说的是同一个去向：官爻往后退，人就退。
>
> *Note:* 'Tui qi' is the retreating spirit (see the glossary): the changed line takes a step back. 'Zhi shi' is retiring from office and 'tianli' is the countryside and the fields. This entry points the same way as 'a Wandering Soul changing into a Returning Soul suits retiring' in the Yi Yin divination for official salary and rank (YY.20) and as 'for resigning from office, a changing into a retreating spirit is wanted' in the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.47): when the star of office steps back, the man retires.
