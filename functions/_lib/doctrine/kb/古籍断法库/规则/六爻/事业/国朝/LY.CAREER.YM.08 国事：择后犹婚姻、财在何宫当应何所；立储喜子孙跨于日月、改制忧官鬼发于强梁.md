# 国事：择后犹婚姻、财在何宫当应何所；立储喜子孙跨于日月、改制忧官鬼发于强梁

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 选皇后如同婚姻：财爻在哪一宫，就应在哪个地方的女子。如果财在中间的爻，应得晋地的女子；财在内卦，应在本乡。立储君，喜欢子孙跨在日、月上；改制，忧虑官鬼在强横的位置上发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Choosing an empress is like a marriage: in whichever palace Wealth lies, the match corresponds to that place. If Wealth is in a middle line, the match should be a woman of Jin; if Wealth is in the inner trigram, it should correspond to the home district. In naming an heir one is glad for Output to straddle the Day and Month; in changing the institutions one worries about Pressure moving in the place of the violent and overbearing.

> [!note] 按
> 「择后」是选立皇后，「立储」是确立太子，「改制」是改革制度。「中爻」「晋女」书里没有说明，这里照字面译，「晋」疑指晋地。「跨于日月」「发于强梁」书里没有解释，按字面是子孙同时受日、月扶助、官鬼在强横的位置发动。择后看财与婚姻章同（见本书婚姻部分），立储看子孙与易隐朝廷占「青宫：以子孙为用」（YY.38）、黄金策国朝「日辰拱扶子位」（HJC.75）同。
>
> *Note:* 'Zehou' is choosing and installing an empress, 'lichu' is establishing the crown prince, and 'gaizhi' is reforming the institutions. The book does not explain 'zhong yao' and 'Jin nü', which are translated literally, and 'Jin' is presumably the land of Jin. 'Kua yu riyue' and 'fa yu qiangliang' are not explained in the book and by the letter are Output being supported by both the Day and Month and Pressure moving in a violent and overbearing place. Choosing an empress looks at Wealth as in the chapter on marriage (see the marriage part of this library), and naming an heir looks at Output as in 'the Crown Prince: take Output as the Subject' in the Yi Yin divination on the court (YY.38) and 'the Day generating and supporting Output's place' in the Huangjin Ce on the state (HJC.75).
