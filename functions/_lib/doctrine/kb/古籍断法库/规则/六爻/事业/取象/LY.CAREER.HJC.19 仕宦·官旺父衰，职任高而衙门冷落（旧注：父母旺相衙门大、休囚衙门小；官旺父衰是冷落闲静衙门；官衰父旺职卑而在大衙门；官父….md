# 仕宦·官旺父衰，职任高而衙门冷落（旧注：父母旺相衙门大、休囚衙门小；官旺父衰是冷落闲静衙门；官衰父旺职卑而在大衙门；官父俱衰职卑衙小）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母旺相，衙门一定大；休囚，衙门一定小。官鬼旺而父母衰，不是小去处，而是冷落清闲的衙门，因为官旺所以职位高；官鬼衰而父母旺，职位虽然卑微，却在大衙门里办事；官和父都衰，职位低、衙门小，必定不是风宪之地。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Resource is thriving, the office is surely large; if resting or trapped, the office is surely small. With Pressure thriving and Resource weak, it is not a small place but a cold and quiet office, the post being high because Pressure is thriving; with Pressure weak and Resource thriving, the post, though lowly, is in a large office; with both Pressure and Resource weak, the post is low and the office small, and it is surely not a place in the censorate.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1964 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母旺相衙门必大，休囚则衙门必小。 | 父母旺相，衙门一定大，休囚，衙门一定小。 | If Resource is thriving, the office is surely large; if resting or trapped, the office is surely small. |
> | 若官旺父衰，又非小职，乃闲静冷落衙门； | 如果官旺父衰，又不是小职位，而是清闲冷落的衙门； | If Pressure is thriving and Resource weak, it is not a small post either but a quiet and cold office; |
> | 官父俱衰，职卑衙小。 | 官和父都衰，职位低、衙门小。 | if both Pressure and Resource are weak, the post is low and the office small. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联用「父母」看衙门的大小（父母是文书、衙署），用「官鬼」看职位的高低，两个轴分开看。「衙门」是官署；「冷落」是不热闹、没有多少人办事的衙门。旧注多出「官衰父旺」一句（职卑而在大衙门），王洪绪没有。
>
> *Note:* This couplet reads the size of the office from Resource (Resource is the documents and the yamen) and the height of the post from Pressure, two axes looked at separately. 'Yamen' is a government office, and 'cold' means one that is not busy, with not many people at work. The old commentary has the additional case 'Pressure weak and Resource thriving' (a lowly post in a large office), which Wang Hongxu does not have.
