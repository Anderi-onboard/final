# 求馆·临官兮少壮、休囚则贫乏之家；墓库兮高年、旺相则富豪之主（旧注：应临临官帝旺主人强壮、临墓库是高年；五行无气家贫乏、旺相家富厚、重加财福巨富；德性以五类六神参断）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻临临官、帝旺，主人必定年壮强盛，临墓库必是高龄；应爻的五行没有气，他家贫乏，旺相就富厚，再加上财爻、子孙，必然是巨富；他的品德性情，要按五类和六神一起参断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Other rests on the Official-Post or Peak stage, the master is surely in his prime and strong, and if on a tomb he is surely advanced in years; if the element of the Other has no breath, his family is poor, if thriving, rich, and with Wealth and Output added he is surely enormously rich; his character is to be judged by the five relations and the six spirits together.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2745 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应爻临旺爻，主人必然强壮，如临墓库，必是老年。 | 应爻临旺爻，主人必然强壮，如果临墓库，必是老年。 | If the Other rests on a thriving line, the master is surely strong, and if it rests on a tomb, he is surely old. |
> | 临财福必然富贵。 | 临财、子孙，必然富贵。 | With Wealth and Output, he is surely rich and noble. |
> | 若论其德性，当以五类六神参断。 | 如果要论他的品德性情，应当按五类、六神参断。 | If his character is to be discussed, it should be judged by the five relations and the six spirits. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「临官」「帝旺」是十二长生里的两个阶段（临官是壮年成人，帝旺是最盛），「墓库」是入墓的阶段（衰老）。这一联用十二长生的阶段看东家的年龄，用五行旺衰看家境。王洪绪写「临财福必然富贵」，旧注是「重加财福，必然巨富」：旧注要财福重叠才说巨富，王洪绪只要临财福就说富贵，程度略有不同。
>
> *Note:* 'Official-Post' and 'Peak' are two stages of the twelve life stages (Official-Post is a man grown and in his prime, Peak is the fullest), and 'tomb' is the stage of entering the tomb (old age). This couplet reads the master's age from the life stage and the circumstances of the family from the element's strength. Wang Hongxu writes 'with Wealth and Output he is surely rich and noble' while the old commentary says 'with Wealth and Output piled on, he is surely enormously rich': the old commentary needs Wealth and Output doubled before it says enormously rich, and Wang Hongxu says rich and noble at once for being on Wealth and Output, a slightly different degree.
