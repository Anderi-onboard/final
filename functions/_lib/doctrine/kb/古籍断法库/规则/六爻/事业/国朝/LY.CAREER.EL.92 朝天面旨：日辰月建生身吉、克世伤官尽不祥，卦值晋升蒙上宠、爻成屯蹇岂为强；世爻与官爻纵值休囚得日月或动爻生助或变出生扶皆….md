# 朝天面旨：日辰月建生身吉、克世伤官尽不祥，卦值晋升蒙上宠、爻成屯蹇岂为强；世爻与官爻纵值休囚得日月或动爻生助或变出生扶皆为佳兆，朝王卜得火地晋、地风升皆吉，水雷屯、水山蹇皆为难遇者必凶

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰、月建生助身爻是吉，克世爻、伤官爻就都不祥；卦遇晋、升，蒙受君上的恩宠；爻成屯、蹇，哪里谈得上强？世爻和官爻即使逢休囚，只要得到日月或动爻生助，或者变出生扶它们的爻，都是好兆；如果动爻和日月伤官爻、伤世爻，都按凶来推。朝见君王卜得火地晋，晋是臣子遇见君王的象；又取地风升，升是往前、往上走，所以这两卦都吉；如果卜得水雷屯、水山蹇两卦，都是艰难，遇到的一定凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Day and the Month generate the Body it is auspicious; if they control the Self or hurt the star of office, all is inauspicious. If the hexagram is Jin or Sheng, one receives the sovereign's favour; if the lines make Zhun or Jian, how could it be strong? Even if the Self line and the Pressure line are resting or imprisoned, if they get generation and aid from the Day and the Month or a moving line, or change out a line that generates and supports them, it is all a good omen; if a moving line and the Day and Month hurt the star of office or the Self, all is judged ominous. If in going to see the king one casts Jin, Fire over Earth, Jin is the image of a minister meeting his sovereign; Sheng, Earth over Wind, is also taken, for Sheng is advancing and going up, so these two hexagrams are both auspicious. If one casts Zhun, Water over Thunder, or Jian, Water over Mountain, both mean difficulty, and whoever meets them will surely have ill.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这里说卜得屯、蹇两卦「皆为难也，遇者必凶」；增删卜易引了古人「晋、升为吉，屯、蹇为凶」的说法后，野鹤（ZS.07）说晋、升也要世爻得地，屯、蹇两卦只要世爻旺相就没有妨碍，他有一次世爻旺相占到屯卦，照样越级升迁。也就是说：这里把卦名当作断语，增删卜易把卦名放在世爻旺衰的后面。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This entry says that casting Zhun or Jian 'both mean difficulty, and whoever meets them will surely have ill'; the Zengshan Buyi quotes the ancients' 'Jin and Sheng are auspicious, Zhun and Jian ominous' and then the Old Man of Yehe (ZS.07) says that Jin and Sheng also need the Self to be well placed, that for Zhun and Jian there is no harm so long as the Self is thriving, and that once he got Zhun with a thriving Self and was promoted beyond rank all the same. That is, this entry takes the hexagram name as a verdict, while the Zengshan Buyi puts the hexagram name behind the strength of the Self.

> [!note] 按
> 「火地晋」「地风升」「水雷屯」「水山蹇」是《周易》的卦名，这里按卦名取义：晋是前进，升是上升，屯、蹇是艰难。解里先说世爻、官爻的旺衰生克，最后才说卦名。「休囚」见术语表。「动爻与日月伤官伤世」书里连写，这里按「动爻和日月来伤官爻、伤世爻」读。
>
> *Note:* 'Huo di Jin', 'Di feng Sheng', 'Shui lei Zhun' and 'Shui shan Jian' are hexagram names of the Book of Changes, taken here by the meaning of the name: Jin is advancing, Sheng is rising, Zhun and Jian are difficulty. The explanation first speaks of the strength, generation and control of the Self and Pressure and only afterwards of the hexagram names. 'Resting or imprisoned' is in the glossary. 'Dong yao yu ri yue shang guan shang shi' is run together in the book and is read here as 'a moving line and the Day and Month come to hurt the star of office and the Self'.
