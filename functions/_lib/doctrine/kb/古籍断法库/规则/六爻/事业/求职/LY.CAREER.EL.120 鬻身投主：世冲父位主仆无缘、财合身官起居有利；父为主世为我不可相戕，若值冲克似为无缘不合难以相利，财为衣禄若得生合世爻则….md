# 鬻身投主：世冲父位主仆无缘、财合身官起居有利；父为主世为我不可相戕，若值冲克似为无缘不合难以相利，财为衣禄若得生合世爻则衣禄无亏利资有望

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻冲了父爻的位置，主仆没有缘分；财爻与身爻、官爻相合，日常起居有利。父爻是主人、世爻是我，不可互相伤害；如果遇到冲克，就像没有缘分、合不来，难以互相得利；财爻是衣食俸禄，如果生合世爻，衣禄就没有亏缺，利益资财有指望。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self clashes with Resource's position, master and servant have no affinity; if Wealth combines with the Body and the star of office, daily life is favourable. Resource is the master and the Self is me, and they must not injure each other; if they meet clash or control, it is as if there were no affinity and no accord, and it is hard to profit one another; Wealth is clothing and pay, and if it generates or combines with the Self line, clothing and pay will not be lacking and profit and funds can be hoped for.

> [!note] 按
> 「相戕」是互相伤害。「起居」是日常生活。「衣禄」是衣食和俸给。这一条的取象：父为主人、世为自己、财为衣禄。
>
> *Note:* 'Xiangqiang' is injuring each other. 'Qiju' is daily life. 'Yilu' is clothing and pay. The images taken here: Resource is the master, the Self is oneself, Wealth is clothing and pay.
