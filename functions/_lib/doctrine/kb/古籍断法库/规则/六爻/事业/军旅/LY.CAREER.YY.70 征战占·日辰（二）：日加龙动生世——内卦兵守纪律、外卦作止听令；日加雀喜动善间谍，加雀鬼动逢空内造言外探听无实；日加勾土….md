# 征战占·日辰（二）：日加龙动生世——内卦兵守纪律、外卦作止听令；日加雀喜动善间谍，加雀鬼动逢空内造言外探听无实；日加勾土鬼动内阻令外退缩；日加蛇鬼动内诡谲外兴妖；日加虎鬼动内偏强外轻敌偾事；日加武鬼动内属垣窃听外攫功利己；加天贼天盗劫杀旺动克世内窃符盗印外掠妇劫财

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰加六神，看我方兵的品性（在内卦是营内，在外卦是出征在外）：日辰加青龙发动、生世爻的，在内卦，兵守纪律，在外卦，行动停止都听号令；日辰加朱雀、喜神发动的，善用间谍；加朱雀、官鬼发动又逢空的，在内造谣生谤，在外打探的情报不实；日辰加勾陈、土爻官鬼发动的，在内阻挠号令不遵从，在外退缩不前进；加螣蛇、官鬼发动的，在内诡诈多端，在外兴妖惑众；加白虎、官鬼发动的，在内偏强难驯，在外轻敌坏事；加玄武、官鬼发动的，在内隔墙偷听，在外抢功图利；加玄武、官鬼，带天贼、天盗、劫杀旺相发动、克世爻的，在内偷窃符印，在外掠夺妇女财物。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Day carrying the six spirits, reading the character of my troops (inside means within the camp, outside means on campaign). If the Day carries the Azure Dragon, moves and generates the Self, then inside the troops keep discipline, and outside they stop and go at command; if the Day carries the Bird and Joy and moves, spies are used well; if it carries the Bird and Pressure, moves and meets Void, then inside they spread rumours and slander, and outside the intelligence gathered is untrue; if the Day carries Hook Earth and an Earth Pressure line and moves, inside they obstruct orders and do not comply, and outside they shrink back and do not advance; with the Snake and Pressure, inside they are crafty in many ways and outside they raise evil and delude the crowd; with the Tiger and Pressure, inside they are headstrong and hard to tame, and outside they despise the enemy and spoil the matter; with the Tortoise and Pressure, inside they listen at the wall and eavesdrop, and outside they grab credit for private gain; with the Tortoise and Pressure, carrying Tianzei, Tiandao and Robbery, thriving and moving and controlling the Self, inside they steal the tally and seal, and outside they plunder women and goods.

> [!note] 按
> 「在内」「在外」在前两句里写作内卦、外卦，后面几句省了「卦」字，译文同样按内卦、外卦理解，并在规则里解释成营内、出征在外；这是读法，不是书里明写的。「雀」「勾」「蛇」「虎」「武」是朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武的省称。「瞩垣」疑是「属垣」（「有耳属垣」，墙外有耳），译文按隔墙偷听译。「偾事」是把事情搞坏。「符印」是调兵的凭证和官印。「加日雀鬼」的「日」是承前的日辰。天贼、天盗是神煞。
>
> *Note:* In the first two sentences 'inside' and 'outside' are written as the inner and outer trigrams, and the later sentences drop the word 'trigram'; the translation reads them the same way, and the rule explains them as inside the camp and on campaign, which is a way of reading it and not something the book writes out. 'Que', 'gou', 'she', 'hu' and 'wu' are abbreviations for the Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth, the Coiling Snake, the White Tiger and the Dark Tortoise. 'Zhu yuan' is presumably 'shu yuan', 'ears against the wall', and the translation reads it as eavesdropping through a wall. 'Fenshi' is spoiling a matter. 'Fuyin' is the tally for mobilizing troops and the official seal. The 'Day' in 'adding Day, Bird, Pressure' continues the Day from before. Tianzei and Tiandao are spirit-stars.
