# 海底眼·谒人：须问谒何人；世应坐鬼枉劳心；用爻出现不乘土、往见必不在家；以外卦取，外阳可见外阴不见，阴化阳宜再见阳化阴公已出；忌外卦独发伏藏，应动皆不见；一看财爻旺相出现忌发动，见官看官爻，忌世应坐鬼

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 拜谒人，要问拜谒的是谁；世、应坐着官鬼，白费心思；用爻出现却不乘土，去见他必定不在家。又说：凡拜谒人，以外卦来取：外卦阳，可以见到，外卦阴，见不到；阴化阳，宜再次去见，阳化阴，人已经出去了；外卦要出现，他才在家；忌讳外卦单独发动或伏藏，应爻发动，都见不到；第一看财爻，旺相、出现，忌讳发动；要见官员，看官爻；忌讳世、应坐着鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In calling on someone, ask whom one is calling on; with the Self or the Other sitting on Pressure, one wearies the mind in vain; the Subject line appearing but not riding Earth, going to see him, he is surely not at home. It also says: in calling on anyone, take it from the outer trigram: outer yang, one can see him, outer yin, one cannot; yin turning into yang, it suits seeing him again, yang turning into yin, he has already gone out; the outer trigram must appear for him to be at home; avoid the outer trigram moving alone or hidden, and with the Other moving in all cases one cannot see him; first look at the Wealth line, strong and appearing, and it should not move; to see an official, look at the Pressure line; avoid the Self or the Other sitting on Pressure.
