# 营造公务防患·财兴官旺，利获名荣（旺官持世财动相生，因公得利、远许名荣；旺财持世、世动化财生世同断）

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财兴旺、官星旺相，既得利益又得荣名：旺相的官星持世，财发动来生它，不但因公事获利，还将得到名誉荣耀；旺相的财持世，或者世爻发动变出财来生世，也同样断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Wealth flourishing and the star of office thriving, one wins both gain and honour: if a thriving star of office holds the Self and Wealth moves to generate it, one not only gains from public business but will in due time win honour; the same judgment holds when a thriving Wealth line holds the Self, or the Self moves and turns into Wealth to generate the Self.
