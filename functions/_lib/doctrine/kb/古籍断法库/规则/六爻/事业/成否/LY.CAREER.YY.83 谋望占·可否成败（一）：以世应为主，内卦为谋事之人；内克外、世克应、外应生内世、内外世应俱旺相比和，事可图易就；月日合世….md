# 谋望占·可否成败（一）：以世应为主，内卦为谋事之人；内克外、世克应、外应生内世、内外世应俱旺相比和，事可图易就；月日合世应心虽不欲亦允成；间动鬼动生合世应得人劝成；谋官官旺生合世身而子衰静成、谋财财旺而兄衰静成、谋文书父旺而财衰静成；凡事嫌兄动，并贵禄生世则为吹嘘之人；凡事喜福动，求名忌之

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谋事能不能成，以世爻、应爻为主，内卦是谋事的人。内卦克外卦、世爻克应爻，外卦、应爻生内卦、世爻，或者内外卦、世应都旺相比和的，事情可以谋，谋也容易成；月建、日辰合世、应的，对方心里虽然不愿意，也会答应成全；间爻、官鬼发动生合世、应的，有人劝说成全。谋官，逢官星旺相生合世身、子孙衰弱安静的，成；谋财，逢财爻旺相生合世身、兄弟衰弱安静的，成；谋文书，逢父爻旺相生合世身、财爻衰弱安静的，成。凡事都嫌兄弟发动，但如果兄弟带着贵人、禄一起生世爻，就是替你吹嘘推荐的人；凡事都喜欢福神发动，求名（功名）却忌讳它。这是可谋而成的占。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether a scheme can succeed takes the Self and Other as the chief, and the inner trigram is the man who schemes. If the inner controls the outer, the Self controls the Other, the outer and the Other generate the inner and Self, or the inner and outer and the Self and Other are all thriving and in harmony, the matter can be planned and is easily done; if the Month and Day combine with the Self and Other, though the other side is unwilling at heart he will agree and see it through; if a line between or Pressure moves to generate or combine with the Self and Other, someone persuades him to see it through. Scheming for office, if the star of office is thriving and generates or combines with the Self and Body while Output is weak and still, it succeeds; scheming for wealth, if Wealth is thriving and generates or combines with the Self and Body while Peer is weak and still, it succeeds; scheming for a document, if Resource is thriving and generates or combines with the Self and Body while Wealth is weak and still, it succeeds. In everything a moving Peer line is disliked, but if it carries the Noble and Emolument and generates the Self, it is one who talks you up and recommends you; in everything the fortune spirit moving is liked, but in seeking fame it is avoided. This is the pattern of a scheme that can be planned and will succeed.

> [!note] 按
> 「世身」是世爻和身爻，「内外世应」按字面是内卦、外卦、世爻、应爻。「吹嘘」是替人说好话、推荐。「福动」是子孙（福神）发动；求名忌子孙是因为子孙克官（见 HJC.03）。
>
> *Note:* 'Shishen' is the Self and the Body line; 'inner, outer, Self, Other' is by the letter the inner trigram, the outer trigram, the Self and the Other. 'Chuixu' is speaking well of someone, recommending him. 'The fortune spirit moves' is Output moving; Output is avoided in seeking fame because Output controls the star of office (see HJC.03).
