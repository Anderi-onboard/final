# 军机：鬼动于五行八卦各有所谋——火鬼动非劫寨而谋纵火、水鬼动非水淹而计囊沙，金为流矢之凶、木为胶舟之诈、土（款）为平原之战；起于离从南来、发于坎从北犯、艮坤敌出山阜、震巽寇自丰木；乾以老弱骗军士之心、兑以子女惑兵卒之志；取象取义全凭深心测度官鬼情形

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 火鬼动，不是劫寨，是谋划放火；水鬼动，不是水淹，是计谋用沙袋壅水；金是流矢之凶，木是胶舟之诈，「款」（疑是土）是平原之战；起于离的从南边来，发于坎的从北边犯；艮、坤的敌人出自山冈土阜，震、巽的寇从茂盛的树林里来；乾是用老弱来骗我军士的心，兑是用子女来惑我兵卒的志。取象取义，全凭用心去测度官鬼的情形。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fire Pressure moving is not a raid on the camp but a plan to set fires; Water Pressure moving is not a flood but a scheme of sandbags; Metal is the harm of flying arrows, Wood the deceit of a boat held fast, and 'Kuan' (presumably Earth) a battle on the open plain; what rises in Li comes from the south, what issues from Kan attacks from the north; with Gen and Kun the enemy comes out of hills and mounds, with Zhen and Xun the raiders come out of thick woods; Qian uses the old and weak to deceive my soldiers' hearts, and Dui uses sons and daughters to confuse my troops' will. Taking the image and taking the meaning all depends on gauging, with a deep mind, the condition of Pressure.

> [!note] 按
> 「款为平原之战」的「款」疑是「土」之误：前面是火、水、金、木，到这里正该轮到土，书里下面的注也写「土有坑陷」。「囊沙」是用沙袋壅水（拦水断水，再放水），是水路的计谋。「流矢」是乱飞的箭。「胶舟」字面是船被胶住、搁浅动弹不得，书里没有细说，按字面译，当是用舟船设的诈。「山阜」是土山，「丰木」是丰茂的树木。「子女」字面是儿女；兑是少女，按卦象理解为用年轻女子。「起于离而南来」是鬼起在离宫，敌从南方来；「发于坎而北犯」是鬼发在坎宫，敌从北面来。
>
> *Note:* The 'kuan' of 'kuan is a battle on the open plain' is presumably a slip for Earth: Fire, Water, Metal and Wood have come before, and it is Earth's turn, and the note below the text also says 'Earth has pits and traps'. 'Nangsha' is damming water with sandbags (blocking or cutting off water and then releasing it), a scheme on the water. 'Liuzhi' is a stray arrow. 'Jiaozhou' is literally a boat held fast by glue, stranded and unable to move; the book does not explain it, so it is translated literally, and it presumably means a deceit worked with boats. 'Shanfu' is an earth hill, and 'fengmu' is thick, lush trees. 'Zinü' is literally sons and daughters; Dui is the youngest girl, and by the image it is understood as using young women. 'Rising in Li and coming from the south' is Pressure rising in the palace of Li with the enemy coming from the south; 'issuing in Kan and attacking from the north' is Pressure issuing in the palace of Kan with the enemy coming from the north.
