# 官禄占总述（游南子）：未仕占何官、何月选、何日领凭、任所何方；既仕占赴任吉凶、在任休咎、何日升除、何时替代；官之贫廉、失职、补任、丁制、起复为附占

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官禄占分两种情形：还没做官的，占将做什么官、哪个月选官、哪天领到凭证、任所在哪个方向；已经做官的，占赴任的吉凶、在任上的顺逆、哪天升迁任命、什么时候被人替代。至于做官是贫还是廉，以及失去职位、补缺任职、遭父母之丧、起复，是官禄占附带的占问。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The divination of office and salary has two cases. For one not yet in office: what office he will take, in which month he will be selected, on what day he will receive his credentials, and in which direction the post lies. For one already in office: the good or ill of his journey to the post, how his term goes, on what day he will be promoted or appointed, and when he will be replaced. Whether an official is poor or incorruptible, and losing a post, being given a post to fill, mourning for a parent and being recalled to office, are subsidiary questions of this divination.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「丁制」就是丁忧，遭父母之丧；「起复」是服丧或革职之后重新起用。本章后面的小节正是按这个提纲排的：何官、候选、领凭、任所、赴任、在任、升迁、替代、贪廉、官职退复、丁忧起复。
>
> *Note:* 'Dingzhi' is Dingyou, mourning for a parent's death, and 'qifu' is being called back to office after mourning or dismissal. The sections of this chapter follow just this outline: what office, awaiting selection, collecting credentials, the post, going to the post, in office, promotion, replacement, greed and integrity, loss and recovery of office, mourning and recall.
