# 上书奏对：以太岁五爻为主、文书官鬼为凭；启奏君王看五爻，生身合命宠应叨，岁君克世龙颜怒，月将生官品爵高；五爻为天子、太岁为君王，一爻生合身世所奏如心，冲克身世恐犯天威休陈情因，官不空破又得日月生扶者必然破格超升

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上书、启奏，以太岁和五爻为主，以文书爻、官鬼为凭据。启奏君王要看五爻：五爻是天子，太岁是君王，这两者只要有一个生合身爻、世爻，所奏的就如愿，得到宠遇；五爻和太岁如果来冲克身爻、世爻，恐怕触犯天威，就不要陈述情由；岁君克世爻，君王龙颜震怒；月将生官爻，官品爵位就高。另外，官爻多半是自己的本职：不落旬空、月破，又得日月生扶的，一定破格超升。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For submitting a memorial, take Tai Sui and the fifth line as the chief, and the document and Pressure as the ground. To memorialise the sovereign look at the fifth line: the fifth line is the Son of Heaven and Tai Sui is the sovereign, and if either of the two generates or combines with the Body or the Self, what is memorialised goes as one wishes and one receives favour; if the fifth line and Tai Sui come to clash or control the Body and the Self, one fears offending the majesty of Heaven and should not present the circumstances; if Tai Sui controls the Self the dragon countenance is angry, and if the Month General generates the star of office, rank and title are high. Also, the star of office mostly stands for one's own post: if it does not fall on the ten-day Void or Month-break and is further generated and supported by the Day and the Month, one is surely promoted beyond the regulations.

> [!note] 按
> 章名「上书咎奏」的「咎」疑有误，按本章内容（启奏、上本）译作「上书奏本」。「五爻为天子」「太岁为君王」与前面求恩宠一条同取（EL.80，黄金策国朝 HJC.64、HJC.65）。「龙颜」是君王的面容，「天威」是君王的威严。「月将」书里没有解释，按文意是月令（月建）的力量；六壬里的月将是另一回事，这里不用。「破格超升」是不按常规、越级升官。「生身合命」的「命」按解里「生合身世」读作世爻。末句「五爻为天子之爻」是解里对前面的重申。
>
> *Note:* The 'jiu' of the chapter title 'shang shu jiu zou' looks wrong, and by the content of the chapter (memorialising and submitting a petition) it is translated 'submitting letters and memorials'. 'The fifth line is the Son of Heaven' and 'Tai Sui is the sovereign' are taken as in the earlier entry on seeking favour (EL.80, and HJC.64 and HJC.65 in the Huangjin Ce on the state). 'Longyan', the dragon countenance, is the sovereign's face, and 'tianwei' is the majesty of Heaven. 'Yuejiang' is not explained in the book, and by the sense it is the force of the month (the Month); the month general of the Da Liu Ren is another matter and is not used here. 'Po ge chao sheng' is being promoted out of the usual order and over rank. The 'ming' of 'sheng shen he ming' is read as the Self by the explanation's 'generates or combines with the Body or the Self'. The last sentence, 'the fifth line is the line of the Son of Heaven', is the explanation restating what went before.
