# 国朝·九五逢阳，当遇仁明之主；四爻值福，必多忠义之臣（旧注：五爻为君位，逢阳象又遇青龙财福是仁明之君；四爻为臣位，子孙旺相克世是敢谏之臣，兄鬼生合世爻是阿谀佞臣）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 第五爻是君位：逢到阳象，又遇到青龙、妻财、子孙，是仁慈英明的君主；世爻临阳象、带吉神也一样。第四爻是臣位：子孙旺相、克世爻，是敢于直言进谏的臣子；兄弟、官鬼生合世爻，是阿谀奉承的佞臣。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The fifth line is the ruler's seat: if it meets a yang image and also meets the Azure Dragon, Wealth and Output, the ruler is benevolent and enlightened, and so it is if the Self sits in a yang image with auspicious spirits. The fourth line is the ministers' seat: if Output is thriving or strong and controls the Self, the minister dares to remonstrate plainly; if a Peer or Pressure line generates or combines with the Self, he is a flattering and treacherous minister.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1464–1466 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 五爻为君位，逢阳象遇青龙财福，是仁明之君。 | 第五爻是君主的位置，逢到阳象又遇到青龙、妻财、福神，是仁慈英明的君主。 | The fifth line is the ruler's seat; when it meets a yang image and meets the Azure Dragon, Wealth and Output, he is a benevolent and enlightened ruler. |
> | 四爻为臣位，临旺福乃敢谏直臣，临兄鬼乃阿谀佞臣也。 | 第四爻是臣子的位置，逢到旺相的福神是敢于直言进谏的臣子，逢到兄弟、官鬼就是阿谀奉承的奸佞臣子。 | The fourth line is the ministers' seat; when it meets a thriving Output line he is a minister who dares to remonstrate plainly, and when it meets a Peer or Pressure line he is a flattering and treacherous minister. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 条件有出入：旧注要子孙旺相并且克世爻、兄鬼要生合世爻；卜筮正宗只说第四爻「临旺福」「临兄鬼」，没有说克世或生合世爻。
>
> *Note:* The conditions differ: the old commentary requires Output to be thriving and to control the Self, and the Peer or Pressure line to generate or combine with the Self; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says only that the fourth line 'meets a thriving Output' or 'meets Peer or Pressure', and does not mention controlling or combining with the Self.
