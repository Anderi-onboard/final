# 求馆·空爻持世，岂如心（旧注：父母出现、应来生合而世空，非馆不成乃自不上前或成而不去；应不生合父母重叠世空是有别馆；无故自空必不成、成亦不如意）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里父母出现、应爻来生合，而世爻空亡的，不是馆不成，是自己不肯上前，或者虽然成了却不去；应爻不生合、父母重叠而世爻空亡的，是另有别的馆，所以不成这一个；世爻无缘无故自己空亡，必定不能成，即使成了也终究不如意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Resource appears in the hexagram and the Other comes to generate or combine while the Self is Void, it is not that the school will not come about but that one does not step forward oneself, or that even if it comes about one does not go; if the Other does not generate or combine, Resource is doubled and the Self is Void, there is another school, and so this one does not come about; if the Self falls Void by itself without cause, it will surely not come about, and even if it does it is in the end not as one wishes.

> [!warning] 打架
> 同一个象（父母出现、应来生合、世爻空亡），旧注把原因讲清楚：是自己不肯去，或者另有别的馆；王洪绪只写「求馆不成」，没有讲这是为什么。这不是说两家判断不同，是旧注比王洪绪多了一层理由，按旧注读，「不成」不是外面不给，而是自己不去。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For the same image (Resource appears, the Other comes to generate or combine, the Self is Void), the old commentary makes the reason clear: one does not wish to go oneself, or there is another school; Wang Hongxu writes only 'the school is not obtained' without saying why. The two do not judge differently, but the old commentary gives a further reason, and read by it, 'not coming about' is not that the outside refuses but that one does not go oneself.

> [!note] 按
> 「无故自空」的读法见 LY.CAREER.HJC.09 和 LY.ILL.HJC.151。「应来生合」是东家愿意聘请，「世爻空亡」是自己这一头空，所以不是别人不请，是自己没有去。
>
> *Note:* For how to read 'Void by itself without cause' see LY.CAREER.HJC.09 and LY.ILL.HJC.151. 'The Other comes to generate or combine' means the host is willing to engage one, and 'the Self is Void' means one's own side is empty, so it is not that others do not engage one but that one does not go oneself.
