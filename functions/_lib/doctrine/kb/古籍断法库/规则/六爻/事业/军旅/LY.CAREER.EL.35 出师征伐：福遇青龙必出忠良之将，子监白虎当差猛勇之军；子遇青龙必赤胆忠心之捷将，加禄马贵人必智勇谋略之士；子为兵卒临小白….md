# 出师征伐：福遇青龙必出忠良之将，子监白虎当差猛勇之军；子遇青龙必赤胆忠心之捷将，加禄马贵人必智勇谋略之士；子为兵卒临小白虎虽衰亦壮强之卒，临大白虎必骁勇猛烈之雄兵

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 福神遇到青龙，必定出忠良的将领；子孙监临白虎，应当差遣勇猛的军队。子孙遇到青龙，必是赤胆忠心的胜将，再加禄、驿马、贵人，必是智勇谋略之士；子孙是兵卒，如果临小白虎，虽然衰弱也是强壮的兵卒；如果临大白虎，必是骁勇猛烈的雄兵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the fortune spirit meets the Azure Dragon, loyal and good commanders are sure to appear; if Output sits over the White Tiger, bold and fierce troops should be sent. If Output meets the Azure Dragon, he is a victorious general of true loyalty, and with Emolument, the Horse and the Noble added, he is a man of wit, courage and strategy; Output is the soldiers, and if it sits on the Lesser White Tiger, though weak they are still strong soldiers; if it sits on the Greater White Tiger, they are surely valiant and fierce picked troops.

> [!note] 按
> 「福」是福神（子孙）。小白虎、大白虎的起例见 EL.28。「禄马贵人」是禄神、驿马、贵人三个神煞。「捷将」是能打胜仗的将领。
>
> *Note:* 'Fu' is the fortune spirit (Output). The setting out of the Lesser and Greater White Tiger is in EL.28. 'Emolument, the Horse and the Noble' are three spirit-stars. 'Jiejiang' is a commander who wins battles.
