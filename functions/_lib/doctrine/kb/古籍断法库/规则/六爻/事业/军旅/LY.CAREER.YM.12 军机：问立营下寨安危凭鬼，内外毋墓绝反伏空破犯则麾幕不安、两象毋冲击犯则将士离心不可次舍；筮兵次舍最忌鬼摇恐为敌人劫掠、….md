# 军机：问立营下寨安危凭鬼，内外毋墓绝反伏空破犯则麾幕不安、两象毋冲击犯则将士离心不可次舍；筮兵次舍最忌鬼摇恐为敌人劫掠、最恶内卦犯疾及六冲上下乖离

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问立营下寨，安危凭官鬼来断：内外卦不要入墓、绝、反吟、伏吟、空、破，犯了就军帐不安；内外两象不要冲击，犯了就将士离心，不可驻扎。占兵驻扎，最忌官鬼发动，恐怕被敌人劫掠；最厌内卦犯疾，以及六冲，上下乖离。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about setting up camp, safety or danger is judged by Pressure: the inner and outer must not meet tomb, extinction, Reversed Chant, Hidden Chant, Void or break, for if they do the commander's tent is uneasy; the two images must not clash, for if they do officers and men are at odds and the troops cannot be quartered. In divining about the quartering of troops, Pressure moving is most to be avoided, for fear of raiding by the enemy, and what is most disliked is the inner trigram being sick, and a six-clash, with high and low estranged.

> [!note] 按
> 「麾幕」是主帅的军帐。「墓绝反伏空破」里的「反伏」是反吟、伏吟（见术语表）。「次舍」是军队驻扎。「内卦犯疾」是内卦带病（旺衰上有伤），书里没有细说。
>
> *Note:* 'Huimu' is the commander's tent. The 'fanfu' of 'mu jue fan fu kong po' is Reversed Chant and Hidden Chant (see the glossary). 'Cishe' is the quartering of an army. 'The inner trigram being sick' is the inner trigram being ill (damaged in strength), which the book does not go into.
