# 征战·两子合世扶身，李郭同心而兴唐室（旧注：子孙为将、世为国君，卦有两子旺动生扶世身，主国有二将合谋胜敌，如唐李光弼、郭子仪同心忠义，终能靖乱复兴唐室）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙是将，世爻是国君。卦里有两个子孙旺相发动，生扶世爻或世身，主国家有两位将领合谋战胜敌人，就像唐朝的李光弼、郭子仪，两人同心，以忠义自勉，终于平定叛乱，复兴了唐朝。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the general and the Self is the ruler. If the hexagram has two Output lines that are thriving and moving and generate or support the Self, the state has two generals who plot together to overcome the enemy, like Li Guangbi and Guo Ziyi of Tang, who were of one heart, urged themselves on with loyalty and righteousness, and in the end put down the rebellion and restored the house of Tang.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5937 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 两子合世扶身，李郭同心而兴唐室； | 两个子孙合住世爻、扶助世身，就像李光弼、郭子仪同心协力而复兴唐朝； | If two Output lines combine with the Self and support the Self or Body, it is like Li and Guo working with one heart and restoring the house of Tang; |

^q1
