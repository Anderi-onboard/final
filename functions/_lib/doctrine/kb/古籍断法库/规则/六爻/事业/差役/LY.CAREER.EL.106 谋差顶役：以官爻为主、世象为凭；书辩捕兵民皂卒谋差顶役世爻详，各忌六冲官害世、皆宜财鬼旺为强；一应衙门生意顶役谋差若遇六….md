# 谋差顶役：以官爻为主、世象为凭；书辩捕兵民皂卒谋差顶役世爻详，各忌六冲官害世、皆宜财鬼旺为强；一应衙门生意顶役谋差若遇六冲决难成就纵成不久，官鬼若伤身世终遭罪责难逃，得财官两旺其中定见兴家

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谋差使、顶替衙门的役，以官爻为主，以世爻为凭据。书办、捕快、兵、民壮、皂隶、差役这些人谋差顶役，要细看世爻；都忌六冲、官爻害世爻，都适宜财爻、官鬼旺相才强。凡是衙门里的差事、生意、顶役、谋差，如果遇到六冲，一定难以成就，即使成了也不长久；官鬼如果伤了身爻、世爻，终究遭到罪责，难以逃脱；得到财、官两旺在其中，一定见到兴家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking a commission or taking over a post in a yamen's service, take the Pressure line as the chief and the Self image as the ground. For clerks, scribes, constables, soldiers, militiamen, lictors and runners seeking a commission or taking over a service, examine the Self line in detail; all fear a six-clash and the star of office harming the Self, and all are fitly strong when Wealth and Pressure are thriving. For every job in a yamen, whether a livelihood, taking over a service or seeking a commission, if it meets a six-clash it will surely be hard to accomplish, and even if it is accomplished it will not last; if Pressure injures the Body or the Self, one will in the end be blamed and punished and cannot escape; if Wealth and the star of office are both thriving within it, one will surely see the household prosper.

> [!note] 按
> 这三章（第四十至四十二章）的章名在书里缺失，只留下小标；它们处在第三十九章（谋望成事）和第四十三章（除名脱役）之间，这里推定为第四十、四十一、四十二章，章名按内容拟：谋差顶役、户役、帮户。「书辩」疑是「书办」（衙门里的文书），「皂卒」是皂隶、差役，「顶役」是顶替别人的差役名额，「谋差」是谋求一个差使，「衙门生意」是在衙门里谋生的行当。「兴家」是家道兴旺。
>
> *Note:* The titles of these three chapters (Chapters 40 to 42) are missing in the book and only the subtitles remain; as they lie between Chapter 39 (scheming and accomplishing affairs) and Chapter 43 (removal from the rolls and release from service), they are taken here as Chapters 40, 41 and 42, with titles supplied from the content: seeking a commission or taking over a service post, household service, and sharing a household's service. 'Shu bian' is presumably 'shuban', clerks of a yamen; 'zaozu' are the lictors and runners; 'ding yi' is taking over another man's place in the service; 'mou chai' is seeking a commission; 'yamen shengyi' is a line of livelihood in a yamen. 'Xingjia' is the household prospering.
