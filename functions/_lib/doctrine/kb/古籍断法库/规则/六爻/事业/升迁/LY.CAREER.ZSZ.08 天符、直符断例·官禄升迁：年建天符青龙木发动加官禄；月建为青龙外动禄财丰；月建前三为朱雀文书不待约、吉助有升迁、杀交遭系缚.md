# 天符、直符断例·官禄升迁：年建天符青龙木发动加官禄；月建为青龙外动禄财丰；月建前三为朱雀文书不待约、吉助有升迁、杀交遭系缚

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 年建是天符（子年子爻动才断）：天符是青龙木时，发动，加官进禄，在外卦盗去资财，在内卦摇动，忧虑家眷。月建是直符：月建是青龙，动了就不与众相同，在内卦摇动，人口兴旺，在外卦动，禄和财丰厚；月建向前数第三位是朱雀，文书不用等约定，有吉神帮助，就有升迁，杀神交加，遭到拘系束缚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Year branch is the Year Tally (judge when the line of the year branch moves, as in a Zi year with the Zi line moving): when the Year Tally is the Azure Dragon of Wood and moves, it adds office and emolument, outside wealth is stolen, and shaking inside there is worry for the family. The Month branch is the Month Tally: if the Month is the Azure Dragon, when it moves it is unlike the common run, shaking inside the household prospers, moving outside emolument and wealth are abundant; the third place ahead of the Month is the Vermilion Bird, the documents need not wait to be agreed, helped by auspicious spirits there is promotion, and with baleful spirits crossing one is bound and confined.

> [!note] 按
> 这里的「天符」是年建（年支），「直符」是月建，和卜筮大全阐奥歌章里的「唐符」「国印」（按年支取巳亥、寅申等）不是同一样东西。周易尚占自己说明了取法：子年子爻动、临青龙木神之类。
>
> *Note:* The 'Year Tally' here is the year branch and the 'Month Tally' the month branch, which is not the same thing as the 'Tangfu' and 'State Seal' of the Chan'ao Ge in the Bushi Daquan (taken by the year branch as Si and Hai, Yin and Shen and so on). The Zhouyi Shangzhan itself explains the method: in a Zi year with the Zi line moving and standing on the Wood spirit, the Azure Dragon, and so on.
