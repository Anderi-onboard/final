# 升何方（一世官、一伏官）：一世官以禽宿（演禽二十八宿）定分野、一伏官以飞神定地方，占官而不占世、既仕以官为象不执世；例：亥月戊寅日得同人亥官居世京房降宿危星后擢齐地，酉月乙巳日得颐丙戌世伏酉官后擢西北

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 只有一个官爻、又落在世爻上的，用演禽二十八宿的所属来定分野；只有一个官爻、伏藏着的，用它上面的飞神来定地方。不过这是占官，不是占世；已经做官的，以官爻为象，不拘泥于世。例如亥月戊寅日卜升到哪个方向，得同人卦，亥官居世，京房降宿是危星，后来升到齐地的分野；又如酉月乙巳日得颐卦，丙戌世下伏着酉官，后来升到西北。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When there is a single Pressure line and it sits on the Self, the assignment of the twenty-eight mansions of the Yanqin system is used to fix the field-allocation; when there is a single Pressure line and it is hidden, the flying line above it is used to fix the place. But this is divining the Pressure line, not the Self; for one already in office the Pressure line is the image, and one does not hold to the Self. For example, in a Hai month on a Wu-Yin day, divining which direction one would be promoted, one got Tongren, with a Hai Pressure line on the Self, and the mansion that Jing Fang assigns to it is Wei, and later one was promoted to the field-allocation of Qi; or in a You month on a Yi-Si day one got Yi, with a hidden You Pressure line under the Bing-Xu Self, and later one was promoted to the north-west.

> [!note] 按
> 「演禽」是一种按二十八宿推断吉凶的术数，「禽宿」是二十八宿各配一禽（如危月燕、斗木獬）；「京房降宿」是京房把六十四卦的每一爻配到二十八宿上的那一套，「危星」是危宿；「分野」是天上星宿对应地上的区域，危宿对应的是齐地（山东一带）。「飞神」是伏神上面那一爻（见术语表）。书里的两个例子：同人卦里亥官居世（亥水官落在世上），按京房降宿落在危宿，所以应在齐地；颐卦里卦中没有官，要去看伏官（酉）上面的飞神，即世爻丙戌，戌是西北，所以后来升到西北（注里写的伏官是「己酉」，本卦纳甲里这一爻是酉金，这里按原文译）。「然占官而不占世也」是说这套法子取的是官爻，不是世爻本身的地支。和易隐官禄占的任所（YY.12）取分野的办法可对着看：那边是用世爻的地支定分野。
>
> *Note:* 'Yanqin' is a method of divination that judges fortune by the twenty-eight mansions, and the 'mansion animals' are the animal paired with each of the twenty-eight mansions (as the Wei mansion is the swallow and the Dou mansion the xie-beast); 'the mansion Jing Fang assigns' is the scheme by which Jing Fang paired each line of the sixty-four hexagrams with the twenty-eight mansions, and 'Wei' is the Wei mansion; the 'field-allocation' is the region on earth that corresponds to a star-mansion in heaven, and the region the Wei mansion corresponds to is Qi (the Shandong area). The 'flying line' is the line above a hidden line (see the glossary). The two examples in the book: in Tongren, the Hai Pressure line sits on the Self (a Hai Water Pressure line on the Self), and by Jing Fang's assignment it falls on the Wei mansion, so it corresponds to Qi; in Yi there is no Pressure line in the hexagram, so one looks at the flying line above the hidden Pressure line (You), which is the Self line Bing-Xu, Xu being north-west, so one was later promoted to the north-west (the note writes the hidden Pressure line as 'Ji-You', and in the hexagram's line assignment this line is You Metal, and it is translated here as the text has it). 'But this is divining the Pressure line, not the Self' says that this method takes the Pressure line and not the branch of the Self line itself. Compare the way the Yi Yin on the place of the post (YY.12) takes the field-allocation: there the branch of the Self line fixes it.
