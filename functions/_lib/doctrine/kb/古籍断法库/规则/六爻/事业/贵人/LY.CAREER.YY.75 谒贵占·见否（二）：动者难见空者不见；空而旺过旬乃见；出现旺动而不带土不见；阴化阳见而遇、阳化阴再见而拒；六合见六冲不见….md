# 谒贵占·见否（二）：动者难见空者不见；空而旺过旬乃见；出现旺动而不带土不见；阴化阳见而遇、阳化阴再见而拒；六合见六冲不见；卦无身或无鬼或世应俱空世应俱鬼不必往；世应内外官鬼生合比和待应爻官爻生世合世之日或生旺之日见

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 见得到见不到：官星发动的，难见；落空的，见不到；空而旺相的，过了这一旬才见得到；出现、旺相发动而不带土爻的，见不到；阴爻变阳爻的，见到而且被接待；阳爻变阴爻的，第二次去见时被拒绝。占得六合的，见得到；占得六冲的，见不到。卦里没有身爻，或没有官鬼，或世、应都落空，世、应都是官鬼的，不必去见。凡世、应、内外卦的官鬼相生相合比和的，等应爻、官爻生世爻、合世爻的日子可以见到，或者应爻、官爻生旺的日子见面。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether he is seen. If the star of office is moving, he is hard to see; if Void, he is not seen; if Void but thriving, he is seen only after the decade is past; if it appears, thriving and moving, without carrying Earth, he is not seen; a yin line changing into yang: seen and received; a yang line changing into yin: refused on the second visit. If the divination is a six-combine, he is seen; if a six-clash, not. If the hexagram has no Body, or no Pressure, or the Self and Other are both Void, or the Self and Other are both Pressure, there is no need to go. Whenever the Pressure lines of the Self, the Other, and the inner and outer trigrams generate, combine or are in harmony, wait for a day when the Other or the Pressure line generates or combines with the Self, or a day when the Other or the Pressure line is born or thriving, to meet him.

## 《易隐·谒贵占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2707 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining a call on a noble", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 动者难见，空者不见。 | 官星发动的，难见；落空的，见不到。 | If the star of office is moving, he is hard to see; if Void, he is not seen. |
> | 空而旺者，过旬乃见。 | 空而旺相的，过了这一旬才见得到。 | If Void but thriving, he is seen only after the decade is past. |
> | 出现旺动而不带土者，不见也。 | 出现、旺相发动而不带土爻的，见不到。 | If it appears, thriving and moving, without carrying Earth, he is not seen. |
> | 阴爻化阳者，而见而遇。 | 阴爻变阳爻的，见到而且被接待。 | A yin line changing into yang: he is seen and received. |
> | 阳爻化阴者，再见而拒也。 | 阳爻变阴爻的，第二次去见时被拒绝。 | A yang line changing into yin: he is refused on the second visit. |
> | 占六合者见，六冲者不见也。 | 占得六合的，见得到；占得六冲的，见不到。 | If the divination is a six-combine, he is seen; if a six-clash, he is not. |
> | 卦无身，或无鬼，或世应俱空，世应俱鬼者，不必往见也。 | 卦里没有身爻，或没有官鬼，或世、应都落空，世、应都是官鬼的，不必去见。 | If the hexagram has no Body line, or no Pressure, or the Self and Other are both Void, or the Self and Other are both Pressure, there is no need to go and see him. |
> | 凡世应内外官鬼，生合比和者，待应爻官爻生世合世之日可见，或应爻官爻生旺之日相见也。 | 凡世、应、内外卦的官鬼相生、相合、比和的，等应爻、官爻生世爻、合世爻的日子可以见到，或者应爻、官爻生旺的日子见面。 | Whenever the Pressure lines of the Self, the Other, and the inner and outer trigrams generate, combine or are in harmony, he can be seen on a day when the Other or the Pressure line generates or combines with the Self, or met on a day when the Other or the Pressure line is born or thriving. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 第一句「动者难见，空者不见」的主语省了，承上一段是应爻、官爻，译文写成官星。「而见而遇」按字面是见了并且遇到（被接待）。「六合」「六冲」指卦的六合、六冲（见术语表）；「卦无身」的身指卦身，书里没有说是不是上卦。
>
> *Note:* The subject of the first sentence, 'moving, hard to see; Void, not seen', is left out, following on from the previous passage on the Other and Pressure lines, and the translation writes it as the star of office. 'Er jian er yu' is by the letter 'seen and met', that is received. 'Six-combine' and 'six-clash' are the hexagram patterns (see the glossary); the 'Body' of 'the hexagram has no Body' is the hexagram Body, and the book does not say whether it needs to be on the board.
