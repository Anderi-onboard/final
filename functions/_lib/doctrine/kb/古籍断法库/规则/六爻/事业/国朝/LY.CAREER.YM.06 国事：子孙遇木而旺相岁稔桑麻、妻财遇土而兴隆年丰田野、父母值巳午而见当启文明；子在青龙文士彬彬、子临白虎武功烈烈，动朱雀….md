# 国事：子孙遇木而旺相岁稔桑麻、妻财遇土而兴隆年丰田野、父母值巳午而见当启文明；子在青龙文士彬彬、子临白虎武功烈烈，动朱雀善弹劾之章、摇元武懿宫闱之德，勾陈得田畯之喜、螣蛇籍使臣之力

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙遇木而旺相，当年桑麻丰收；妻财遇土而兴隆，当年田野丰收；父母爻值巳、午而出现，当开启文明。子孙在青龙，文士彬彬有礼；子孙临白虎，武功烈烈；动在朱雀，善于弹劾的奏章；摇在玄武，美在宫闱的德行；勾陈得田官的喜庆，螣蛇靠使臣的力量。凡是福神临在五行、六亲、六神上，都是吉祥的应验，所以福神摇在玄武，也是后宫的美事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output meets Wood and thrives, the year's mulberry and hemp are plentiful; if Wealth meets Earth and flourishes, the fields yield well in the year; if Resource falls on Si or Wu and appears, civilisation is opened up. If Output is on the Azure Dragon, the scholars are refined; if Output is on the White Tiger, military exploits blaze; moving on the Vermilion Bird, good memorials of impeachment; swaying on the Dark Tortoise, fine virtue in the inner palace; on Hook Earth it enjoys the delight of the field officers, and on the Coiling Snake it relies on the strength of envoys. Wherever the fortune spirit rests, on the five elements, the six kin or the six spirits, it is an auspicious response, so the fortune spirit swaying on the Dark Tortoise is also a fine thing for the inner palace.

> [!note] 按
> 「稔」是丰熟。「田畯」是管田事的官（《诗经》里的田官）。「弹劾」是监察官的检举。「宫闱」是后宫。「元武」就是玄武。这一条是福神（子孙）落在五行、六神上的象：木主桑麻，土主田野，青龙文、白虎武、朱雀言官、玄武后宫、勾陈农事、螣蛇使臣。「巳午」是火，「父母值巳午」取的是火为文明。
>
> *Note:* 'Ren' is ripening. 'Tianjun' is the official in charge of the fields (the field officer of the Book of Odes). 'Tanhe' is impeachment by the censors. 'Gongwei' is the inner palace. 'Yuanwu' is the Dark Tortoise. This entry gives the images of the fortune spirit (Output) falling on the five elements and the six spirits: Wood governs mulberry and hemp, Earth the fields, the Azure Dragon the civil, the White Tiger the military, the Vermilion Bird the censors, the Dark Tortoise the inner palace, Hook Earth farming, the Coiling Snake envoys. 'Si and Wu' are Fire, and Resource on Si or Wu takes Fire as civilisation.
