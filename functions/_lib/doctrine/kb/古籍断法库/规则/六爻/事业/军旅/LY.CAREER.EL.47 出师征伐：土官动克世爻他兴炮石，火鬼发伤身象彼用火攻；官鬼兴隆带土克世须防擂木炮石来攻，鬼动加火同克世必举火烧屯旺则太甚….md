# 出师征伐：土官动克世爻他兴炮石，火鬼发伤身象彼用火攻；官鬼兴隆带土克世须防擂木炮石来攻，鬼动加火同克世必举火烧屯旺则太甚衰则稍可

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 土爻官鬼发动克世爻，对方兴用炮石；火爻官鬼发动伤身象，对方用火攻。官鬼兴旺带土来克世的，须防对方制备擂木、炮石来攻；如果官鬼发动、加火一起克世，他必定举火烧营，旺相则太厉害，衰弱则稍微好一些。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If an Earth Pressure line moves and controls the Self, the enemy uses catapult stones; if a Fire Pressure line moves and injures the Body image, they attack by fire. If Pressure is flourishing and carries Earth to control the Self, one must guard against their preparing rolling logs and catapult stones to attack; if Pressure moves and, with Fire added, controls the Self together, they will surely raise fire and burn the camp, and if it is thriving it is very serious and if weak it is somewhat better.

> [!note] 按
> 「擂木」是从高处滚下砸人的原木。这一条与 EL.45 连着读，也和易隐征战占「阳土鬼旺动…防炮石」（YY.65）取同一件事。
>
> *Note:* 'Leimu' are logs rolled down from a height to smash people. This entry reads on from EL.45 and also takes up the same matter as 'a yang Earth Pressure line thriving and moving ... guard against catapult stones' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.65).
