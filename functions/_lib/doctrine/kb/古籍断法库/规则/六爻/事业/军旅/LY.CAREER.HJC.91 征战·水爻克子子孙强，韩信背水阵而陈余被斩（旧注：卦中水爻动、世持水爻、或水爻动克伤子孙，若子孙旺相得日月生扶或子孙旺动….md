# 征战·水爻克子子孙强，韩信背水阵而陈余被斩（旧注：卦中水爻动、世持水爻、或水爻动克伤子孙，若子孙旺相得日月生扶或子孙旺动，可效韩信背水战，死中求生反胜）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中水爻发动，或者世爻持着水爻，或者水爻发动克伤子孙，只要子孙旺相、得到月建日辰生扶，或者子孙旺相发动，就可以效法韩信背水列阵，在绝境里求生，反而取胜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a Water line moves in the hexagram, or the Self holds a Water line, or a Water line moves and controls and injures Output, then so long as Output is thriving or strong and is generated and supported by the Month and Day, or Output is thriving and moves, one may follow Han Xin's battle with the river at his back, seeking life in the midst of death, and win instead.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5919 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 水爻克子子孙强，韩信背水阵而陈余被斩； | 水爻克子孙而子孙强，就像韩信摆背水之阵、陈余被斩； | If Water controls Output and Output is strong, it is like Han Xin drawing up his army with the river at his back and Chen Yu being cut down; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5920 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦中水爻动，及世持水爻，或动出水爻克伤子孙，若子孙旺相，得日辰月建生扶，或子孙旺动，则可效韩信背水战，死中求生而反胜也。 | 卦中水爻发动，以及世爻持着水爻，或者发动变出水爻来克伤子孙，如果子孙旺相，得到日辰月建生扶，或者子孙旺相发动，就可以效法韩信背水一战，在死地里求生而反过来取胜。 | If a Water line moves in the hexagram, or the Self holds a Water line, or a moving line turns into a Water line that controls and injures Output, then if Output is thriving or strong and is generated and supported by the Day and Month, or Output is thriving and moves, one may follow Han Xin's battle with the river at his back, seeking life in the midst of death, and win instead. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1522 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持水动或水爻克子孙，若子孙亦动，得日月生扶，可效韩信背水战而反胜也。 | 世爻持着水爻发动，或者水爻克子孙，如果子孙也发动、得到月建日辰生扶，可以效法韩信背水一战而反过来取胜。 | If the Self holds a Water line that moves, or a Water line controls Output, and Output also moves and is generated and supported by the Month and Day, one may follow Han Xin's battle with the river at his back and win instead. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注列了水爻动、世持水爻、动出水爻克子孙三种情形，并要子孙旺相或旺动；卜筮正宗压缩成两种，要「子孙亦动」。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary lists three cases: a Water line moving, the Self holding Water, and a moving line turning into Water that controls Output, with Output thriving or thriving and moving; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong compresses them into two and requires 'Output also moving'.
