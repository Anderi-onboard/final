# 官禄占·在任（五）：太岁五爻生合父母椿萱受赠；生合妻财室人受诰；生合子孙儿孙得荫；生合官鬼祖妣恩荣

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任，封赠：太岁与五爻生合哪一亲的爻，哪一亲就受恩典：生合父母爻，父母受封赠；生合妻财，妻子受诰命；生合子孙，儿孙得到荫庇；生合官鬼，已故的祖母得恩荣。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In office, honours conferred on kin. Whichever kin line Tai Sui and the fifth line generate or combine with, those kin receive the favour: if Resource, his parents receive honorary titles; if Wealth, his wife receives an imperial patent; if Output, his sons and grandsons gain hereditary privilege; if Pressure, his late grandmother receives the grace of honours.

## 《易隐·官禄占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2640 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining office and salary", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 太岁与五爻生合父母，椿萱受赠。 | 太岁与五爻来生合父母爻，父母受到封赠。 | If Tai Sui and the fifth line generate or combine with Resource, his parents receive honorary titles. |
> | 生合妻财，室人受诰。 | （太岁与五爻）生合妻财，妻子受诰命。 | If they generate or combine with Wealth, his wife receives an imperial patent. |
> | 生合子孙，儿孙得荫。 | （太岁与五爻）生合子孙，儿孙得到荫庇。 | If they generate or combine with Output, his sons and grandsons gain hereditary privilege. |
> | 生合官鬼，祖妣恩荣也。 | （太岁与五爻）生合官鬼，祖妣（已故的祖母）得到恩荣。 | If they generate or combine with Pressure, his late grandmother receives the grace of honours. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「椿萱」是父亲和母亲（椿指父、萱指母）；「诰」是皇帝封赠官员及其亲属的诏令，「荫」是官员的子孙凭父祖的官位得到入仕或入学的资格。后三句省了主语，承上一句，仍是太岁与五爻。「祖妣」字面是已故的祖母。
>
> *Note:* 'Chunxuan' means the father and the mother ('chun' for the father and 'xuan' for the mother); 'gao' is the imperial decree by which an official and his kin are given titles, and 'yin' is the privilege by which an official's descendants enter service or school on the strength of the rank of a father or grandfather. The last three sentences leave out the subject, following on from the previous one, and it is still Tai Sui and the fifth line. 'Zubi' is literally a deceased grandmother.
