# 出师征伐结语：胜败我先知匪伊测度，吉凶神已告据理推详

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 胜负我先知道，不是靠揣测；吉凶神明已经告知，要依据道理推究详察。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Victory or defeat I know beforehand, and it is not by guesswork; good and ill the spirits have already told, and one should work them out by principle.

## 《易林补遗·出师征伐章第五》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 402 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 5: Sending out troops and campaigning", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 胜败我先知，匪伊测度。 | 胜负我先知道，不是靠揣测； | Victory or defeat I know beforehand, and it is not by guesswork; |
> | 吉凶神已告，据理推详。 | 吉凶神明已经告知，要依据道理推究详察。 | good and ill the spirits have already told, and one should work them out by principle. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是出师征伐章的结语，后面没有解说；「匪伊」是「不是」。
>
> *Note:* This is the closing couplet of the chapter on sending out troops and campaigning, and the book gives no explanation after it; 'fei yi' is 'not'.
