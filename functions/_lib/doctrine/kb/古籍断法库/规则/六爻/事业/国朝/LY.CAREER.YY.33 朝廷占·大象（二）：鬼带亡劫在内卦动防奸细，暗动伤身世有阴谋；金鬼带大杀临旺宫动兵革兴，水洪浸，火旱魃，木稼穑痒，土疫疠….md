# 朝廷占·大象（二）：鬼带亡劫在内卦动防奸细，暗动伤身世有阴谋；金鬼带大杀临旺宫动兵革兴，水洪浸，火旱魃，木稼穑痒，土疫疠；鬼空动虽凶无害

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大象，灾变：官鬼带亡神、劫杀，在内卦发动的，要提防奸细；官鬼带亡神、劫杀，暗中发动而伤身爻、世爻的，有阴谋。金爻的官鬼带大杀，临在旺相的宫里发动，兵祸起；水爻的，有洪水浸泡之灾；火爻的，旱魃肆虐；木爻的，庄稼受害；土爻的，瘟疫流行。官鬼落空而发动的，虽然凶也没有大害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The great image, disasters and upheavals. If Pressure carrying the Lost Spirit and Robbery moves in the inner trigram, beware of spies; if Pressure carrying the Lost Spirit and Robbery moves secretly to injure the Body and Self, there is a plot. If a Metal Pressure line carrying a great baleful spirit sits in a thriving palace and moves, war arises; if Water, flood disaster; if Fire, the drought demon rages; if Wood, the crops are blighted; if Earth, pestilence breaks out. If Pressure is Void and moves, though it is ominous it does no harm.

> [!note] 按
> 第十一句「临旺官动」的「官」和后四句的「旺宫」对照，应是「宫」字抄错，译文按「宫」译。「旱魃」是传说中造成旱灾的鬼怪。「稼穑痒」的「痒」字面是发痒，疑是「恙」（病害）之误，译文按庄稼受害译。「亡劫」是亡神和劫杀（见 YY.20）。
>
> *Note:* In the eleventh sentence the character for 'official' in 'thriving official' is, compared with 'thriving palace' in the four sentences after it, presumably a miscopy for 'palace', and the translation follows 'palace'. 'Hanba' is the legendary demon that brings drought. In 'jiase yang', 'yang' literally means itchy and is presumably a miswriting of 'yang', sickness and harm, so the crops being blighted is what is translated. 'Wangjie' is the Lost Spirit and Robbery (see YY.20).
