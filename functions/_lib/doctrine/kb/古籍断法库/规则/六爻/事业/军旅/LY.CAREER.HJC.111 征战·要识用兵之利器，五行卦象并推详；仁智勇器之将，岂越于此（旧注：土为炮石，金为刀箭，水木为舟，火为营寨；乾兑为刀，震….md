# 征战·要识用兵之利器，五行卦象并推详；仁智勇器之将，岂越于此（旧注：土为炮石，金为刀箭，水木为舟，火为营寨；乾兑为刀，震巽为弓马，坤为野战；有克应之神宜用此器克敌，应爻官父在此象克世则反防敌用此器）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道用兵该用什么器械，要把五行和卦象一起推究：土是炮石，金是刀箭，水、木是舟船，火是营寨；乾、兑是刀，震、巽是弓马，坤是野战之类。哪个象里有能克应爻的神，就适合用这种器械去克敌；应爻的官鬼或父母在这个象里克世爻，就要反过来防备敌人用这种器械。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know the sharp tools for war, work out the five elements and the hexagram images together: Earth is cannon-stones, Metal is blades and arrows, Water and Wood are boats, Fire is the camp; Qian and Dui are blades, Zhen and Xun are bows and horses, Kun is open-field fighting and the like. Where an image holds a spirit that controls the Other, it is fit to use that tool against the enemy; where the Other's Pressure or Resource in that image controls the Self, one must instead guard against the enemy using that tool.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5982 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 仁智勇器之将，岂越于此？ | 仁、智、勇和器械，作为一个将领所要的这些，哪里能越出这些象之外？ | Benevolence, wisdom, courage and the tools of war, which a general needs, how could they go beyond these? |
> | 攻守克敌用兵，当审于时。 | 是攻是守、克敌用兵，都应当看准时机。 | Whether to attack or defend, to overcome the enemy and deploy troops, one must judge the moment. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 旧注「震巽为弓马，火枪」，卜筮正宗写成「火为枪」，译文按卜筮正宗读作「火是枪」。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary has 'Zhen and Xun are bows and horses, fire lance'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong writes 'Fire is the lance', and the translation reads it so.
