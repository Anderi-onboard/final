# 卜筮元龟·占现任官得多少时：子动克官官位失，财发印亡忧禄秩；欲知失位多少时，退度在二三月日；注：财爻发动克父母则可能追缴印绶

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占现任官还能做多久：子孙动克官鬼，官位就丢；财发动、印（父母）亡，要担忧俸禄品秩；要知道丢官位要多久，退度在二、三个月或日子。注文说：子孙爻动克官鬼，有失官位的兆头；财爻发动克父母，可能被追缴印绶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On divining how long a serving official can remain: Output moving and controlling Pressure, the post is lost; with Wealth moving and the seal lost, worry for the salary and rank; to know how long until the post is lost, the retreating degree is in two or three months or days. The note says: Output moving and controlling Pressure is a sign of losing the post; Wealth moving and controlling Resource may lead to the seal and ribbon being recalled.
