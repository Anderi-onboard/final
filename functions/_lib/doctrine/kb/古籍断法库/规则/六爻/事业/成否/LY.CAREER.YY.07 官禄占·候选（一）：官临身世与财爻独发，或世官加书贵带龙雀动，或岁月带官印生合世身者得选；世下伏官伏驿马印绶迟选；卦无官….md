# 官禄占·候选（一）：官临身世与财爻独发，或世官加书贵带龙雀动，或岁月带官印生合世身者得选；世下伏官伏驿马印绶迟选；卦无官印、身世空绝、子孙持世独发不得选；鬼化鬼反覆，鬼动财空选司无力；身世随鬼入墓选不成

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 候选（等待选官）——能选上：官星临在身爻、世爻上，财爻单独发动；或者世爻、官星加文书、贵人，带青龙或朱雀发动；或者太岁、月建带着官星、印星来生合世身。选得迟：官星伏在世爻下，或伏着驿马、印绶。选在下等：世爻持官星、贵人遇升爻，但休囚受克。选不上：卦里没有官星、印星，或它们空绝，或身爻世爻空绝，或子孙持世旺动、子孙单独发动；官鬼变官鬼，事情多反复；官鬼发动而财空绝，选官的衙门没有力量；官星入绝但逢生，事虽有阻挠，终得贵人之力而成；身爻世爻随官鬼入墓，选不成，成了也不如意；遇丁未、戊戌二杀之墓，即使选了官、到了任，也要带着亲人回乡；官鬼带亡神劫杀大杀克世身，选了官也不得赴任；有官星而无父母，或父母空死墓绝胎，没有任所。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Awaiting selection. Selected: the star of office sits on the Body or the Self and the Wealth line alone moves; or the Self and the star of office carry the document and the Noble and move with the Azure Dragon or the Vermilion Bird; or Tai Sui and the Month carry the star of office and seal and generate or combine with the Self and Body. Late selection: the star of office or the Post Horse or seal lies hidden under the Self. A low selection: the Self holds the star of office and the Noble on a promotion line but is resting or trapped and controlled. Not selected: the hexagram has no star of office or seal, or they are Void or extinct, or the Body and Self are Void or extinct, or Output holds the Self and is thriving and moving, or Output alone moves. Pressure turning into Pressure: the matter goes back and forth; Pressure moves while Wealth is Void or extinct: the selecting office has no power; the star of office extinct but meeting generation: the matter meets obstruction but is in the end achieved through a patron's help; the Body and Self following Pressure into the tomb: the selection fails, and if it succeeds it is not as wished; meeting the tombs of the two baleful spirits Dingwei and Wuxu: though selected and arrived at the post, he travels home with his kin; Pressure carrying the Lost Spirit, Robbery and great baleful spirits and controlling the Self and Body: though selected he cannot go to his post; the star of office present but no Resource, or Resource Void, dead, in tomb, extinct or in the womb stage: there is no post.

> [!note] 按
> 几处字面可疑，译文只按字面意思译：「旅亲回乡」疑是「旅榇回乡」（扶柩回乡）；「丁未戊戌二杀墓」书里没有解释是哪两个杀；「方催选陪点」按字面译。「选」是明清的铨选，按资格抽签选授官缺。三个附断例都是《易隐》官禄占后面的附断例，这里放在最相关的一条下。
>
> *Note:* A few places are doubtful and are translated only by the letter: 'travelling home with kin' is presumably 'travelling home with the coffin' (escorting a coffin home); the book does not say which two baleful spirits the 'tombs of the two killers Dingwei and Wuxu' belong to; and 'just urging the selection and accompanying the roll-call' is rendered literally. 'Xuan' is the Ming and Qing selection by which vacant posts were allotted by lot according to qualification. The three examples are from the examples appended to the end of this chapter of the Yi Yin and are placed here under the entry they illustrate most.
