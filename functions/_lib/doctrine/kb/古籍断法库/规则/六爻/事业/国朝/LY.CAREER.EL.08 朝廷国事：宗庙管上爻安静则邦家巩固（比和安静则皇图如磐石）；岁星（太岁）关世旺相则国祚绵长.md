# 朝廷国事：宗庙管上爻安静则邦家巩固（比和安静则皇图如磐石）；岁星（太岁）关世旺相则国祚绵长

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 宗庙由上爻掌管：上爻安静，国家就稳固。宗庙专管社稷，得到比和、安静，国家的版图就像磐石一样安稳。岁星（太岁）关系到世爻、光芒旺相的，国祚就绵长：岁星就是太岁，关系到治乱，光芒旺相；如果这样，国祚就久远无疆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ancestral temple is governed by the top line: if it is still, the state is firm. The ancestral temple governs the altars of the state alone, and if it is in harmony and still, the realm is as secure as a rock. If the Year Star (Tai Sui) is linked with the Self and shines thriving, the dynasty's span is long: the Year Star is Tai Sui, it bears on order and disorder and shines thriving; if so, the dynasty lasts long without limit.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 304 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 岁星，即太岁也，关乎治乱光芒旺相也，若然值此，则国祚久远无疆也。 | 岁星就是太岁，关系到治乱，光芒旺相；如果值在这里，国祚就久远无疆。 | The Year Star is Tai Sui; it bears on order and disorder and shines thriving; if it sits here, the dynasty lasts long without limit. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「岁星关世连」字面不太顺，按解里的「关乎治乱」译成「关系到世爻」；「安静」是上爻不发动。「比和安静」见 YY.39 一类的「比和」用法。太岁是当年的地支（见术语表）。
>
> *Note:* 'Sui xing guan shi lian' does not read smoothly by the letter, and following the explanation's 'bears on order and disorder' it is translated as 'is linked with the Self'; 'still' means that the top line does not move. Tai Sui is the branch of the current year (see the glossary).
