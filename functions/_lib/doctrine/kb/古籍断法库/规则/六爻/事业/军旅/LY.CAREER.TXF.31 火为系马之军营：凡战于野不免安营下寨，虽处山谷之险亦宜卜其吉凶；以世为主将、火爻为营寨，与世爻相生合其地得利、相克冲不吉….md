# 火为系马之军营：凡战于野不免安营下寨，虽处山谷之险亦宜卜其吉凶；以世为主将、火爻为营寨，与世爻相生合其地得利、相克冲不吉；火爻带鬼贼寨必相近；火爻旺相宜宽阔无气宜小结构；苍屏云水爻带鬼来克我须防劫寨攻营，有气无气方断输赢，火爻旺相水无气纵克世亦无伤

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 火是系马驻军的营寨。在野外作战免不了安营下寨，即使处在山谷的险要之地，也该占卜它的吉凶。占这件事以世为主将、火爻为营寨：与世爻相生相合，那个地方得利；与世爻相克相冲，不吉；火爻带鬼，贼寇的营寨必定靠近；火爻旺相，宜扎得宽阔；无气，宜小规模结营。苍屏说：水爻带着官鬼来克我，要防劫寨和攻营，再看有气还是无气，才能斟酌说败还是赢；假如火爻旺相、水爻无气，纵然来克世也没有伤害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fire is the camp where horses are tethered and the army stays. In fighting in the open one cannot avoid pitching camp and setting up a stockade, and even in the peril of mountain valleys one should divine its good or ill fortune. In this divination the Self is the commanding general and the Fire line is the camp: if it generates or combines with the Self line the place is advantageous, and if it controls or clashes with the Self line it is not auspicious; if the Fire line carries Pressure, the bandits' stockade is sure to be near; a strong Fire line suits a broad camp, and without qi a small layout. Cangping says: a Water line carrying Pressure and coming to control me, guard against the raiding of the camp and the assault on the encampment; then see whether there is qi or none, and only then can one weigh and speak of defeat or victory; suppose the Fire line is strong and the Water line has no qi: even if it comes to control the Self there is no harm.
