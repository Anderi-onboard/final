# 道业·六亲所主：子官善恶、父母摇得典策之誉、兄弟动犯贪嗔之戒、妻财发有萦家之心；子孙动夙有善根福德徒众、官鬼动原有恶孽灾障调达之扰、父母吉动得名誉凶动法嗣聚散、弟兄吉动遇良朋但清苦凶动贪嗔之累、妻财吉动道供盈余凶动妇女缠绊

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子（孙）、官（鬼），是善与恶；父母动，得典籍策书的名誉；兄弟动，犯贪嗔的戒；妻财发，有牵挂家室的心。子孙动，前世就有善根，或者因缘福德深厚，或者有高贤徒众侍奉；官鬼动，原本有恶业，或者灾障缠身，或者调达（提婆达多那样的）搅扰；父母带吉神动，我的书文以后得名誉，带凶神动，主法嗣多聚多散；弟兄吉动，当遇良朋善友，但道风清苦，凶动，常有贪嗔的牵累；妻财吉动，道场供养有盈余，凶动，有妇女缠绊。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output and Pressure are good and evil; Resource moving gains the name of scripture and learning; Peer moving breaks the precept against greed and anger; Wealth issuing brings a heart that clings to home. Output moving means one had roots of goodness from the past, or great karmic merit, or the service of eminent worthies and disciples; Pressure moving means one had evil karma originally, or entanglement by calamity and hindrance, or the harassment of a Devadatta; Resource moving with an auspicious spirit, my writings later win renown, with an ominous spirit, the heirs of the teaching gather and scatter much; Peer moving auspiciously, one will meet good friends but the way of life is austere, ominously, there is often the entanglement of greed and anger; Wealth moving auspiciously, the offerings to the place of practice have a surplus, ominously, women entangle.

> [!note] 按
> 「子官」是子孙和官鬼，「善恶」是它们代表的善和恶。「典策」是典籍、策书，「父母摇而得典策之誉」是父母爻动、得经书著述的名声。「贪嗔」是佛家说的三毒里的贪和嗔。「萦家」是牵挂家室。注里「夙有善恨」疑是「夙有善根」：前世种下的善根。「因缘福德」是因缘和福报，「高贤徒众」是德高的贤人和众弟子。「恶孽」是恶业，「灾障」是灾难和障碍，「调达」是提婆达多（释迦的堂弟，屡屡破坏僧团的人），泛指搅扰修行的人。「法嗣」是承继道法的弟子，「聚散」是来了又走。「道风」是修道的风气，「清苦」是清贫刻苦。「道供」是道场得到的供养。这一条是把六亲分别配到修道人的处境：子孙是福报，官鬼是业障，父母是名声，兄弟是同道与贪嗔，妻财是供养与家缘。
>
> *Note:* 'Zi guan' is Output and Pressure, and 'good and evil' is the good and the evil they stand for. 'Diance' is scriptures and books, and 'Resource moving gains the name of scripture and learning' is that when the Resource line moves one gets the name of writings on the sutras. 'Tanchen' is greed and anger, two of the three poisons of the Buddhists. 'Yingjia' is clinging to home. The 'shan hen' of the note is presumably a slip for 'shan gen', roots of goodness: good roots planted in an earlier life. 'Yinyuan fude' is karmic affinity and the reward of merit, and 'gaoxian tuzhong' is eminent worthies and the host of disciples. 'E'nie' is evil karma, 'zaizhang' is calamity and obstruction, and 'Tiaoda' is Devadatta (the Buddha's cousin, who repeatedly tried to split the order), standing in general for those who disturb the practice. 'Fasi' is a disciple who inherits the teaching, and 'jusan' is coming and then going. 'Daofeng' is the style of the practice, and 'qingku' is poor and hard. 'Daogong' is the offerings the place of practice receives. This entry assigns the six relations to the circumstances of one who practises: Output is the reward of merit, Pressure is karmic hindrance, Resource is fame, Peer is fellow-seekers and greed and anger, and Wealth is offerings and ties to the household.
