# 选缺参房：世爻若遇空冲绝费尽勤劳怎得昌，不遇六冲官有气稳膺美秩姓名扬；世若变为绝地或被日主来冲或卜六冲之卦纵去谋为决难成就，世象不空官爻不陷、应与兄爻纵动不来克世所求必遂并不更张

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻如果遇到空、冲、绝，费尽辛勤劳苦，又怎么能兴旺？不遇六冲、官有气，就稳稳地得到好的官位，姓名远扬。世爻如果变到绝地，或者被日辰来冲，或者卜得六冲的卦，即使去谋求，也一定难以成功；世爻不空、官爻不陷，应爻和兄爻即使发动，只要不来克世，所求的一定遂意，也不会更改。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self meets Void, clash or extinction, however much toil one spends, how can one prosper? If one does not meet a six-clash and the star of office has qi, one will securely receive a fine rank and one's name will be known. If the Self changes into a place of extinction, or is clashed by the Day, or the hexagram cast is a six-clash, then even if one goes about scheming it will surely be hard to succeed; if the Self image is not Void and the Pressure line is not sunk, then even if the Other and the Peer line move, as long as they do not come to control the Self, what one seeks will surely be attained and there will be no change.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 807 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世若变为绝地、或被日主来冲、或卜六冲之卦，纵去谋为，决难成就。 | 世爻如果变到绝地，或者被日辰来冲，或者卜得六冲的卦，即使去谋求，也一定难以成功。 | If the Self changes into a place of extinction, or is clashed by the Day, or the hexagram cast is a six-clash, then even if one goes about scheming it will surely be hard to succeed. |
> | 世象不空官爻不陷、应与兄爻纵动、不来克世，所求必逐，并不更张。 | 世爻不空、官爻不陷，应爻和兄爻即使发动，只要不来克世，所求的一定遂意，也不会更改。 | If the Self image is not Void and the Pressure line is not sunk, then even if the Other and the Peer line move, as long as they do not come to control the Self, what one seeks will surely be attained and there will be no change. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「美秩」是好的官职；「日主」是日辰；「逐」疑是「遂」，「所求必逐」译作「所求必遂」；「并不更张」是不会再更改。这一条是上一条（EL.76）的另一面：世爻自己空冲绝、卦是六冲，和应动、兄动来克世，是两类不同的阻碍。
>
> *Note:* 'Meizhi' is a fine official rank; 'rizhu' is the Day; the 'zhu' of 'suo qiu bi zhu' is presumably 'sui', to be fulfilled, and it is translated 'what one seeks will surely be attained'; 'bing bu gengzhang' is that there will be no further change. This entry is the other side of the previous one (EL.76): the Self itself being Void, clashed or extinct, and the hexagram being a six-clash, are one kind of obstacle, and the Other or Peer moving to control the Self is another.
