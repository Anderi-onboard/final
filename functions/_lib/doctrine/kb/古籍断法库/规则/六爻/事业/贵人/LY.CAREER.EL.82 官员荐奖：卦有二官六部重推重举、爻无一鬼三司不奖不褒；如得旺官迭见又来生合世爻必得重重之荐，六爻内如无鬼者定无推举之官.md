# 官员荐奖：卦有二官六部重推重举、爻无一鬼三司不奖不褒；如得旺官迭见又来生合世爻必得重重之荐，六爻内如无鬼者定无推举之官

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有两个官爻，六部就一再推荐、一再举用；爻里一个官鬼也没有，三司就不奖励也不褒扬。如果旺相的官爻接连出现，又来生合世爻，一定得到一重又一重的荐举；六爻里如果没有官鬼，一定没有推举的官员。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram has two stars of office, the Six Boards recommend and raise one again and again; if the lines have not even one Pressure, the Three Offices neither commend nor praise. If thriving stars of office appear one after another and come to generate or combine with the Self line, one will surely receive recommendation upon recommendation; if there is no Pressure among the six lines, there is surely no official to recommend one.

> [!note] 按
> 「六部」是朝廷的吏、户、礼、兵、刑、工六部；「三司」是省一级的布政使司、按察使司、都指挥使司。「卦有二官六部」「爻无一鬼三司」书里没有展开，这里按字面对仗译：有两个官，六部再三推荐；一个官鬼也没有，三司都不奖励。
>
> *Note:* 'Liubu', the Six Boards, are the Boards of Personnel, Revenue, Rites, War, Justice and Works at court; 'sansi', the Three Offices, are the provincial commissions for administration, for judicial matters and for military command. The book does not unfold 'the hexagram has two stars of office, the Six Boards' and 'the lines have not one Pressure, the Three Offices', and they are translated here literally as a matched pair: with two stars of office the Six Boards recommend again and again; with not one Pressure the Three Offices do not commend.
