# 求馆·化成兄弟，砚田定主欠收成（旧注：兄弟劫财之神，发动或持世皆难入手；财化兄有名无实或得一半；卦无财遇兄化出则有人抽分）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟是劫财的神，发动或持世，束修都难到手；财爻化出兄弟，有名无实，或者只得一半；卦里没有财而兄弟化出来，就有人抽成分去。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer is the spirit that robs wealth, and when it moves or holds the Self the fee is hard to lay hands on; Wealth turning into Peer is in name only, or only half is had; if there is no Wealth in the hexagram and Peer is changed out, someone takes a share.

## 《黄金策·求馆(附束修)》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8623 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 化成兄弟，砚田定主欠收成。 | 化成兄弟，砚田一定歉收。 | Turning into Peer, the inkstone field will surely yield a poor harvest. |

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 财爻化兄，有名无实。 | 财爻化兄弟，有名无实。 | Wealth turning into Peer is in name only. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「砚田」是靠砚台（笔墨、教书）吃饭的田，指教书的收入。LY.CAREER.HJC.34（财作束修，不宜化弟）是同一个判据，在那里是总说，这里是收尾时再讲一次兄弟化出的后果。
>
> *Note:* 'The inkstone field' is the field that feeds one by the inkstone (brush, ink, teaching), meaning the income of teaching. LY.CAREER.HJC.34 ('Wealth is the tuition fee, not fitting to turn into Peer') is the same criterion, stated there in general, and here the consequence of Peer being changed out is told again at the end.
