# 国朝·将星被害，岳武穆抱吁天之冤（旧注：将星看日支，临财子必忠良智勇之将，值官鬼白虎强悍，临父是老将、遇兄庸常；将星持鬼克世如曹操桓温篡位，被动爻克害如岳飞遇秦桧）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 将星按占卦那天的地支来找，例如寅、午、戌日占，午爻就是将星。将星临妻财、子孙，必是忠良有智勇的将领；值官鬼、白虎，是强悍的将领；临父母爻，是年老的将领；遇兄弟，不过是平庸的将领。将星持官鬼来克害世爻，就像曹操、桓温挟天子以令诸侯，往往篡位自立；将星被动爻或第四爻动来克害，就像岳飞遇到秦桧这样的权奸而被害死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Find the General star from the day branch: on a Yin, Wu or Xu day, the Wu line is the General star, and so on for the others. A General star on Wealth or Output means a loyal, able, wise and brave general; meeting Pressure or the White Tiger, a fierce and stubborn one; on Resource, an old general; meeting Peer, no more than a commonplace one. If the General star holds Pressure and controls and harms the Self, it is like Cao Cao and Huan Wen, who held the emperor and gave orders to the lords and mostly ended by usurping the throne; if the General star is controlled and harmed by a moving line, or by a moving fourth line, it is like Yue Fei of Song, who was killed by the harm of the powerful and treacherous Qin Hui.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5847 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 将星，如寅午戌日卜，午爻为将星是。 | 将星，比如在寅、午、戌日占卜，午爻就是将星。 | As for the General star, if the cast is made on a Yin, Wu or Xu day, the Wu line is the General star. |
> | 余类推。 | 其余的依此类推。 | The rest follow by analogy. |
> | 若将星临财子，必得忠良智勇之将。 | 如果将星临在妻财、子孙上，必定得到忠良而有智有勇的将领。 | If the General star is on Wealth or Output, one will surely get a loyal, good, wise and brave general. |
> | 将星值官鬼白虎，必强悍之将。 | 将星遇到官鬼、白虎，必定是强悍的将领。 | If the General star meets Pressure or the White Tiger, he is surely a fierce and stubborn general. |
> | 若临父，必老将； | 如果临在父爻上，必定是年老的将领； | If it is on Resource, he is surely an old general; |
> | 遇兄，不过庸常之将。 | 遇到兄弟，不过是平庸寻常的将领。 | if it meets Peer, he is no more than a commonplace general. |

> | 若将星被动爻克害，或被四爻动克，必如宋岳飞，遇秦桧权奸之害而死也。 | 如果将星被动爻克害，或者被第四爻发动来克，必定像宋朝的岳飞，遇到秦桧这样的权奸迫害而死。 | If the General star is controlled and harmed by a moving line, or controlled by a moving fourth line, it is surely like Yue Fei of Song, who was killed by the harm of the powerful and treacherous Qin Hui. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1482 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 将星，寅午戌日卜，午爻为将星，其余类推。 | 将星是这样找的：寅、午、戌日占卜，午爻就是将星，其余依此类推。 | As for the General star, on a Yin, Wu or Xu day the Wu line is the General star, and the rest follow by analogy. |
> | 若将星临财子，必得忠职智勇，值官鬼白虎，必强悍之将。 | 如果将星临在妻财、子孙上，必定得到尽忠职守、有智有勇的将领；遇到官鬼、白虎，必定是强悍的将领。 | If the General star is on Wealth or Output, one will surely get a general who is loyal to his duty, wise and brave; if it meets Pressure or the White Tiger, he is surely a fierce and stubborn general. |
> | 若将星持鬼克害世爻，恐有造谋之变； | 如果将星持着官鬼，克害世爻，恐怕会有图谋作乱的变故； | If the General star holds Pressure and controls and harms the Self, there may be an upheaval of plotting and scheming; |
> | 若将星被动爻克害，如岳武穆遇秦桧之害也。 | 如果将星被动爻克害，就像岳武穆遇到秦桧的陷害。 | if the General star is controlled and harmed by a moving line, it is like Yue Wumu meeting the harm of Qin Hui. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 将星持鬼克世的结果，旧注举曹操、桓温挟天子篡位；卜筮正宗收成「恐有造谋之变」，没有举人名。被克害那一句，旧注还多一个「被四爻动克」。
>
> *Note:* For a General star holding Pressure and controlling the Self, the old commentary gives Cao Cao and Huan Wen holding the emperor and usurping; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong boils it down to 'there may be an upheaval of plotting' with no names. For the harmed General star, the old commentary adds 'or controlled by a moving fourth line'.
