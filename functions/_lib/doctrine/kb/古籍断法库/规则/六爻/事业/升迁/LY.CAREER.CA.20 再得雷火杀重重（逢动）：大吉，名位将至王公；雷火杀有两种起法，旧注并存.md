# 再得雷火杀重重（逢动）：大吉，名位将至王公；雷火杀有两种起法，旧注并存

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里再得雷火杀、重重叠叠，又逢发动，是大吉的征兆，眼看名位将到王公。旧注给了雷火杀的起法，但前后有两种说法并存。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram further gets the Thunder-Fire baleful spirit, layer upon layer, and it moves, it is a sign of great fortune, and soon rank and position reach prince and duke. The note gives the method for finding the Thunder-Fire spirit, but two accounts of it stand side by side.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注里同时给了两套雷火杀的起法：第一句按「寅、亥、申、巳」四孟轮转一周；第三句又说「正月起寅，二月丑，逆数十二位」。两套到二月就对不上（一个是亥，一个是丑）。「即时冲破为天后」一句也没有解释。本库照录，不裁定。
>
> *Note:* The note gives two accounts of how to find the Thunder-Fire spirit side by side: the first sentence runs through the four Meng branches Yin, Hai, Shen and Si in one circuit, while the third says 'begin at Yin in the first month, Chou in the second, counting backwards through the twelve places'. The two part company already at the second month (Hai in one, Chou in the other), and the sentence 'once clashed and broken it becomes Tianhou' is not explained. It is recorded as it stands and not adjudicated.
