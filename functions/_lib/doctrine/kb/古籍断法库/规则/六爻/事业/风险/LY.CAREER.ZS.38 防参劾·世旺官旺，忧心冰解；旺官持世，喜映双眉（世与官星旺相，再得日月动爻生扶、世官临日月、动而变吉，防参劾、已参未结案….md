# 防参劾·世旺官旺，忧心冰解；旺官持世，喜映双眉（世与官星旺相，再得日月动爻生扶、世官临日月、动而变吉，防参劾、已参未结案、虑大计者皆无忧，且蒙卓异；书中例：丰之旅，被参而保本随至）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 防备被参劾、担心大计考核、案子还没结的人：世爻与官星都旺相，再有日月、动爻生扶，或者世爻、官爻临在日月上，或者发动变吉，就没有什么可忧虑的：防参劾的无忧，已被参劾而未结案的无忧，担心大计的不但无忧，还一定被评为卓异；凡是旺相的官星持世，也同样推断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For one who guards against impeachment, fears the great evaluation, or has a case not yet closed: if the Self and the star of office are both thriving, and the Day, Month or a moving line also generates and supports them, or the Self or the Pressure line sits on the Day or Month, or they move and turn auspicious, there is nothing to fear: one guarding against impeachment has nothing to fear, one already impeached whose case is not closed has nothing to fear, and one who fears the great evaluation not only has nothing to fear but will surely be rated outstanding; whenever a thriving star of office holds the Self, judge in the same way.

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10794–10795 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Chapter on guarding against impeachment and the great evaluation, and on cases not yet closed", base text

> | 世旺官旺，忧心冰解，旺官持世，喜映双眉。 | 世爻旺相、官星旺相，忧愁的心就像冰一样化解；旺相的官星持世，喜气映在双眉上。 | With the Self thriving and the star of office thriving, the heart's worry melts like ice; a thriving star of office holding the Self brings joy to both brows. |
> | 世与官星旺相，何虑何忧？ | 世爻与官星都旺相，有什么可忧虑的？ | If the Self and the star of office are thriving, what is there to fear or worry about? |
> | 再得日月动爻生扶，或得世爻、官爻临于日月，及动而变吉，防参劾者何虑？ | 再得到日月、动爻生扶，或者世爻、官爻临在日月上，以及发动变吉，防备被参劾的人有什么可虑？ | If the Day, Month or a moving line also generates and supports them, or the Self or the Pressure line sits on the Day or Month, or they move and turn auspicious, what has one to fear who is guarding against impeachment? |
> | 已参而未结案者无忧，虑大计者，不且无忧，定蒙卓异。 | 已经被参劾但还没结案的人没有忧虑，担心大计的人，不但没有忧虑，一定会被评为卓异。 | One already impeached whose case is not yet closed has nothing to fear, and one who fears the great evaluation not only has nothing to fear but will surely be rated outstanding. |
> | 凡得旺官持世，亦同此推。 | 凡是得到旺相的官星持世，也同样推断。 | Whenever a thriving star of office holds the Self, judge in the same way. |

> [!note] 按
> 「参劾」是被言官弹劾；「大计」「计典」是定期考核官员政绩的制度。书里没有解释，这里按通行的意思注。
>
> *Note:* 'Canhe' is being impeached by the censors, and 'daji' and 'jidian' are the periodic evaluation of officials' performance; the book does not explain them, and they are glossed here in the usual sense.
