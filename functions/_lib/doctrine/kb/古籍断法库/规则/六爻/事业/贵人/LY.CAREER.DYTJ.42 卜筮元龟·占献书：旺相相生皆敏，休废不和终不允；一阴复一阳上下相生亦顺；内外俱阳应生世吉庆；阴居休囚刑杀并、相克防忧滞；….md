# 卜筮元龟·占献书：旺相相生皆敏，休废不和终不允；一阴复一阳上下相生亦顺；内外俱阳应生世吉庆；阴居休囚刑杀并、相克防忧滞；外阳听顺阴不从；纯阳相生喜听、纯阴相凌似诈聋

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占进献书信、奏章：旺相、相生，都主顺利敏捷；休废、不和，终究不被应允；一阴又一阳、上下相生，也算相顺；内外都是阳、应生世，吉庆无疑；阴居休囚、刑杀并见、相克，要防忧愁滞碍；外卦阳，他听从，阴，他不从；有气相生就好，无气就凶；纯阳相生，他欢喜就听从；纯阴相凌，他像是装聋。注文逐条解释了阴阳相生为顺、相克为逆，以及应生世、父母爻是书信等。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On divining the presenting of a letter or memorial: strong and generating one another, all go swiftly and smoothly; at rest or abandoned and at odds, in the end it is not granted; a yin and then a yang, upper and lower generating each other, it is also in accord; with inner and outer both yang and the Other generating the Self, good fortune is beyond doubt; yin in a place at rest or trapped with punishment and baleful spirits together, and mutual control, guard against worry and obstruction; the outer trigram yang, he listens and complies, yin, he does not follow; with qi and generating it is good, without qi bad; pure yang generating, he is glad and listens; pure yin bullying, he seems to feign deafness. The notes explain item by item that yin and yang generating each other is accord and controlling each other is opposition, that the Other generating the Self, and that the Resource line is the letter.

> | 占献书云： | 占进献书信、奏章，（卜筮元龟）说： | On divining the presenting of a letter or memorial, it says: |
> | 旺相相生皆为敏， | 旺相、相生，都主敏捷顺利； | strong and generating one another, all go swiftly and smoothly; |
> | 休废不和终不允， | 休废、不和，终究不被应允； | at rest or abandoned and at odds, in the end it is not granted; |
> | 若得一阴复一阳， | 如果得到一阴又一阳， | if one gets a yin and then a yang, |
> | 上下相生亦相顺（10）。 | 上下相生，也算相顺（10）。 | upper and lower generating each other, it is also in accord (10). |
> | 内外俱阳应生世， | 内外都是阳、应生世， | with inner and outer both yang and the Other generating the Self, |
> | 吉庆无疑不须记（11）， | 吉庆无疑，不用再记挂（11）； | good fortune is beyond doubt and needs no worry (11); |
> | 阴居休囚刑杀并， | 阴居于休囚，刑、杀并见， | with yin in a place at rest or trapped and punishment and baleful spirits together, |
> | 相克须防至忧滞（12）。 | 相克，要防备到忧愁滞碍（12）。 | and mutual control, guard against worry and obstruction (12). |
> | 外阳听顺阴不从， | 外卦阳，他听从顺应；阴，他不从； | the outer trigram yang, he listens and complies; yin, he does not follow; |
> | 有气相生无气凶（13）， | 有气相生就好，无气就凶（13）； | with qi and generating it is good, without qi it is bad (13); |
> | 纯阳相生喜即听， | 纯阳相生，他欢喜就听从； | pure yang generating, he is glad and listens; |
> | 纯阴相凌似诈聋（14）。 | 纯阴相凌，好像装聋（14）。 | pure yin bullying, he seems to feign deafness (14). |
