# 鬼谷辨爻法·占求事：初心事、二身事、三家事、四人事、五官事、六国事

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占求事（谋事），按鬼谷子的爻位法：初爻是心里的事，二爻是自身的事，三爻是家里的事，四爻是人事往来，五爻是官府的事，六爻是国家的事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining an undertaking, by the Guigu line-position method the first line is the matter of the heart, the second a personal matter, the third a household matter, the fourth dealings with people, the fifth a matter of the officials and the sixth a matter of the state.
