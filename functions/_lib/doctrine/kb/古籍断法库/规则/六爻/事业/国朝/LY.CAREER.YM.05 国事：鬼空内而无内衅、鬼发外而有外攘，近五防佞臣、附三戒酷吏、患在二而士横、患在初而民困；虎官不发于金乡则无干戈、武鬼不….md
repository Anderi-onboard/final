# 国事：鬼空内而无内衅、鬼发外而有外攘，近五防佞臣、附三戒酷吏、患在二而士横、患在初而民困；虎官不发于金乡则无干戈、武鬼不扬于水地则无泛潦，火鬼螣蛇岁忧火患、土官朱雀年虑蝗灾；凡鬼发内外何宫何位何属皆宜慎之

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼在内卦落空，就没有内乱；官鬼在外卦发动，就有外患要抵御；官鬼靠近五爻，要防佞臣；依附三爻，要戒酷吏；祸患在二爻，是士人横行；祸患在初爻，是百姓困苦。白虎官如果不在金乡发动，就没有战事；玄武官如果不在水地张扬，就没有水涝；火属官鬼带螣蛇，当年忧火灾；土官带朱雀，当年虑蝗灾。凡是官鬼发动，在内还是在外、哪一宫、哪一位、属于哪一类，都应当谨慎；整饬弊端要防奸邪，朝廷的谋划应当预先。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure is Void in the inner trigram there is no disorder within; if Pressure moves in the outer trigram there are outside enemies to repel; if Pressure is near the fifth line guard against treacherous ministers; if it attaches to the third line beware of cruel officials; if the trouble is on the second line the scholars run riot, and if on the first line the people are in distress. If a White Tiger Pressure does not move in a Metal palace there is no war; if a Dark Tortoise Pressure does not show itself on watery ground there is no flooding; a Fire Pressure with the Coiling Snake means anxiety about fire in the year, and an Earth star of office with the Vermilion Bird means worry about a plague of locusts. Wherever Pressure moves, inside or outside, in which palace, in which position and of which element, one should be careful; in setting right abuses guard against treachery, and the court's planning should be done in advance.

> [!note] 按
> 「近五而防佞（？）臣」的「佞」字书里自己标了问号，是传抄者对这个字没有把握；文意是官鬼靠近君位（五爻）要防奸臣，译作「佞臣」。「内衅」是内部的变乱，「外攘」是对外抵御，「干戈」是战事，「泛潦」是水涝，「干蛊」是整饬前人留下的弊端（《易》蛊卦「干父之蛊」）。「金乡」「水地」是金、水所属的宫位。「虎官」「武鬼」是白虎、玄武上的官鬼。「庙算」是朝廷的谋划。
>
> *Note:* The book itself puts a question mark after the character 'ning' in 'jin wu er fang ning(?) chen', showing that the copyist was not sure of it; by the sense, Pressure near the sovereign's position (the fifth line) means guarding against treacherous ministers, and it is translated so. 'Neixin' is disorder within, 'waixiang' is repelling enemies from outside, 'gange' is war, 'fanlao' is flooding, and 'gan gu' is setting right the abuses left by one's predecessors (from the Gu hexagram of the Book of Changes, 'setting right the father's decay'). 'Jinxiang' and 'shuidi' are the palaces that Metal and Water belong to. 'Huguan' and 'wugui' are Pressure on the White Tiger and the Dark Tortoise. 'Miaosuan' is the court's planning.
