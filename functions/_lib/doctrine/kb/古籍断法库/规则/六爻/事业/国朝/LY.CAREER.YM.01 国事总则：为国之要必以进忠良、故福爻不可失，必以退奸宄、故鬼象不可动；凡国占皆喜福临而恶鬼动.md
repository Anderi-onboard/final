# 国事总则：为国之要必以进忠良、故福爻不可失，必以退奸宄、故鬼象不可动；凡国占皆喜福临而恶鬼动

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占国事，要义是进用忠良、为万民造福，所以福爻（子孙）不能失；要退去奸邪、除去四海的疾患，所以鬼象（官鬼）不能动。凡是占国事，都喜欢福神临到、厌恶官鬼发动，这是占国事的大象。本章细讲迁都、建庙、择后、立储、改制、享国各问。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about the state, the essential is to advance the loyal and good and to bring blessing to the myriad people, so the fortune line, Output, must not be lost; one must also remove the treacherous and rid the four seas of their sickness, so the Pressure image must not move. In every divination about the state one is glad to see the fortune spirit arrive and dislikes Pressure moving, and this is the great image of such divinations. The chapter treats in detail the questions of moving the capital, building a temple, choosing an empress, naming an heir, changing the institutions, and how long the dynasty will last.

> [!note] 按
> 「福爻」是子孙（福神），「鬼象」是官鬼之象（见术语表）。「奸宄」是奸邪作乱的人。这一章把国事的好坏归在两件事上：福神在不在、官鬼动不动，后面各条是它的展开。
>
> *Note:* 'Fuyao', the fortune line, is Output (the fortune spirit), and 'guixiang', the Pressure image, is the image of Pressure (see the glossary). 'Jiangui' are the treacherous who stir up disorder. The chapter puts the good or ill of the state on two things, whether the fortune spirit is present and whether Pressure moves, and the entries that follow unfold this.
