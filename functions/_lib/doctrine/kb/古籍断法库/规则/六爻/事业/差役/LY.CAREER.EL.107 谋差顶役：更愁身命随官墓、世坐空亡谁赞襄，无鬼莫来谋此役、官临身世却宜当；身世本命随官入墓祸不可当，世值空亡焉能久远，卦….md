# 谋差顶役：更愁身命随官墓、世坐空亡谁赞襄，无鬼莫来谋此役、官临身世却宜当；身世本命随官入墓祸不可当，世值空亡焉能久远，卦中无鬼所作不成纵成始终无益，若得官临身世本命又遇生扶宜充此役

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 更怕身、命随着官爻入墓，世爻坐空亡，谁来帮助？没有官鬼，就不要来谋这份役；官爻临在身爻、世爻上，却适宜担当。具体说：身爻、世爻、本命随着官爻入墓，祸就挡不住；世爻落在空亡，哪能长久？卦中没有官鬼，所做的不成，即使成了也始终无益；如果官爻临在身爻、世爻上，本命又遇到生扶，就适宜充任这份役。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Still more fear the Body and the fate following the star of office into the tomb, and with the Self sitting on Void who will assist? With no Pressure do not come to seek this service; with the star of office on the Body or the Self it is on the contrary fitting to take it up. In detail: if the Body, the Self and the native fate follow the star of office into the tomb, the calamity cannot be withstood; if the Self falls on Void, how can it last long? If the hexagram has no Pressure, what one does will not succeed, and even if it succeeds it is of no benefit from first to last; if the star of office rests on the Body or the Self and the native fate meets generation and support, it is fitting to fill this service.

> [!note] 按
> 「身命」「本命」按常例是身爻、世爻和问卜人的本命（出生年的地支）；书里没有再解释。「随官入墓」增删卜易另有说法（ZS.06）。「赞襄」是帮助。这一条与黄金策仕宦「官爻隐伏，莫思爵位升迁」（HJC.06）、「身空无救，命尽当危」（HJC.09）取同一类象：官爻不出现、世身落空都不成。
>
> *Note:* 'Shenming' and 'benming' are, by the usual practice, the Body and the Self lines and the native fate of the person asking (the branch of his birth year); the book does not explain them further. 'Following the star of office into the tomb' has another account in the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.06). 'Zanxiang' is helping. This entry takes the same kind of images as 'if the star of office lies hidden, do not think of rank and promotion' (HJC.06) and 'a Void Body without rescue means life is exhausted and in danger' (HJC.09) in the Huangjin Ce on official careers: with the star of office not appearing and the Self or Body Void, nothing comes of it.
