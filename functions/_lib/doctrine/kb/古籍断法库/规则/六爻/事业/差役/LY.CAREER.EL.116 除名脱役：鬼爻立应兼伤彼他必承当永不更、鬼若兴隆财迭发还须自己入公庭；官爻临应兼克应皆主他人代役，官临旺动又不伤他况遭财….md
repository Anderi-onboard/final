# 除名脱役：鬼爻立应兼伤彼他必承当永不更、鬼若兴隆财迭发还须自己入公庭；官爻临应兼克应皆主他人代役，官临旺动又不伤他况遭财动生官定难脱也，官爻纵动如来生助世爻反有益我之情必然得脱

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻立在应爻上、又伤克对方，他必定承担下来，永不更改；官鬼如果兴旺、财爻接连发动，还须自己进公堂。官爻临在应爻上又克应爻，都主他人代役；官爻临旺相发动、又不伤他，何况遇到财爻发动来生官爻，一定难脱；官爻即使发动，如果来生助世爻，反而有益于我，必定得脱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line stands on the Other and also hurts him, he will surely take it on and it will never change; if Pressure flourishes and Wealth moves one after another, one must still go oneself into the public court. If the Pressure line rests on the Other and controls it, in every case another man takes over the service; if the star of office is thriving and moving and does not hurt him, and still more if Wealth moves to generate the star of office, release is surely hard; even if the Pressure line moves, if it comes to generate and aid the Self line, it is on the contrary of benefit to me and I will surely get free.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 922 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官爻临应兼克应，皆主他人代役。 | 官爻临在应爻上、又克应爻，都主他人代役。 | If the Pressure line rests on the Other and controls it, in every case another man takes over the service. |
> | 官临旺动又不伤他，况遭财动生官，定难脱也。 | 官爻临旺相发动、又不伤他，何况又遇到财爻发动来生官爻，一定难脱。 | If the star of office is thriving and moving and does not hurt him, and still more if Wealth moves to generate the star of office, release is surely hard. |
> | 官爻纵动，如来生助世爻，反有益我之情，必然得脱。 | 官爻即使发动，如果来生助世爻，反而有益于我，必定得脱。 | Even if the Pressure line moves, if it comes to generate and aid the Self line, it is on the contrary of benefit to me and I will surely get free. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「立应」是立在应爻上；「公庭」是官府的公堂，「入公庭」是自己到官府去服役。「伤彼」是伤克对方（应）。诀里「鬼爻立应兼伤彼，他必承当永不更」说的是对方把役顶下来，与解里「官爻临应兼克应，皆主他人代役」是一回事；「鬼若兴隆财迭发」与解里「官临旺动……况遭财动生官，定难脱也」也是一回事。「官爻纵动，如来生助世爻」是官爻的力量转过来生世，反而有利，是解里在「官临旺动」之外补的一条例外。这是全章和「谋差顶役」至「脱役」各章的收尾。
>
> *Note:* 'Liying' is standing on the Other line; 'gongting' is the public court of the authorities, and 'ru gongting' is going oneself to the authorities to serve. 'Shangbi' is hurting the other party (the Other). The verse's 'if the Pressure line stands on the Other and also hurts him, he will surely take it on and it will never change' says that the other party takes the service over, which is the same as the explanation's 'if the Pressure line rests on the Other and controls it, in every case another man takes over the service'; and 'if Pressure flourishes and Wealth moves one after another' is likewise the same as the explanation's 'if the star of office is thriving and moving ... and still more if Wealth moves to generate the star of office, release is surely hard'. 'Even if the Pressure line moves, if it comes to generate and aid the Self line' is the force of the Pressure line turning to generate the Self, which on the contrary helps, and is an exception that the explanation adds beside 'the star of office thriving and moving'. This closes the chapter and the run of chapters from seeking a commission to release from service.
