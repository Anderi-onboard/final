# 出师征伐：杀陷鬼摇虽凶不振，官空煞动纵乱勿忧；鬼煞并兴兵马云屯成大战，官凶俱绝干戈顿息庆清平；最忌鬼煞二神，煞空绝鬼动难成大害，官空绝煞兴无重祸，鬼煞俱动鬼随煞势煞趋鬼威大肆鏖战，克世冲世必被刑戮，官煞二神衰静或空绝方是弓藏胄解太平之世

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 煞陷落、官鬼摇动，虽然凶也振作不起来；官鬼落空、煞发动，纵然有乱也不必忧虑。官鬼和煞同时兴起，兵马云集，成为大战；官鬼和凶煞都绝，战事顿时止息，庆贺太平。征战的爻里，最忌官鬼和煞两个神：如果煞神空、绝，官鬼纵然发动，也难成大害；如果官鬼逢空、绝，各煞虽然兴旺，也没有重大的祸害；如果官鬼和煞都发动，官鬼就随着煞的势头，煞趋附官鬼的威势，云集聚拢、大肆苦战；或者克世、冲世的，必被刑杀，不轻；如果官鬼和煞两个神衰弱而又安静，或者空或者绝，才是弓箭收起、盔甲解下的太平之世。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the baleful spirit sinks and Pressure stirs, though ominous it cannot rally; if Pressure is Void and the baleful spirit moves, even if there is disorder there is no need to worry. If Pressure and the baleful spirit rise together, horses and men gather like clouds and it becomes a great battle; if Pressure and the ill spirits are both extinct, arms are suddenly stilled and one celebrates peace. In the lines of war, what is most feared is the two spirits Pressure and the baleful spirit: if the baleful spirit is Void or extinct, even though Pressure moves it can hardly do great harm; if Pressure meets Void or extinction, though the baleful spirits flourish there is no heavy disaster; if Pressure and the baleful spirit both move, Pressure follows the baleful spirit's momentum and the baleful spirit joins Pressure's might, they gather like clouds and fight a bitter battle on a large scale; if they control or clash with the Self, one is surely punished and slain, and not lightly; if both Pressure and the baleful spirit are weak and still, or Void or extinct, only then is it an age of peace with bows put away and helmets removed.

> [!note] 按
> 「曦聚」疑是「蚁聚」（像蚂蚁一样聚拢），「胃解」疑是「胄解」（盔甲解下），「太乎这世」疑是「太平之世」，译文按这样的读法译；原文几处笔误，大意清楚。这一联与易隐征战占「鬼衰静敌胆怯、鬼旺动敌势盛」「鬼空者敌必灭」（YY.62）一类。
>
> *Note:* 'Xi ju' is presumably 'yi ju', swarming like ants, 'wei jie' is presumably 'zhou jie', helmets removed, and 'tai hu zhe shi' is presumably 'taiping zhi shi', an age of peace, and the translation reads them so; the text has several slips of the pen but its general sense is clear. This couplet is of the kind of 'Pressure weak and still, the enemy is timid; Pressure thriving and moving, the enemy's force is strong' and 'Pressure Void, the enemy is destroyed' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.62).
