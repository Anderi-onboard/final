# 官员荐奖结语：上察官情之喜怒皆由四象之兴衰，下推人事之亲疏不出两仪之变化

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 往上察看官府的喜怒，全由四象的兴衰决定；往下推究人事的亲疏，不出两仪的变化。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Observing above the pleasure or anger of the officials all depends on the rise and fall of the four images; reckoning below the closeness or distance of human affairs does not go beyond the changes of the two modes.

## 《易林补遗·官员荐奖章第三十三》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 828 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 33: Recommendation and commendation of officials", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 上察官情之喜怒，皆由四象之兴衰。 | 向上察看官府的喜怒，全由四象的兴衰决定。 | Observing above the pleasure or anger of the officials, it all depends on the rise and fall of the four images. |
> | 下推人事之亲疏，不出两仪之变化。 | 向下推究人事的亲疏，不会超出两仪的变化。 | Reckoning below the closeness or distance in human affairs, it does not go beyond the changes of the two modes. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是官员荐奖章的结语，后面没有解说。「四象」「两仪」书里没有说指的是什么，这里只按字面译，不加解释。
>
> *Note:* This is the closing couplet of the chapter on the recommendation and commendation of officials, and the book gives no explanation after it. The book does not say what the 'four images' and the 'two modes' are, and they are translated literally here without comment.
