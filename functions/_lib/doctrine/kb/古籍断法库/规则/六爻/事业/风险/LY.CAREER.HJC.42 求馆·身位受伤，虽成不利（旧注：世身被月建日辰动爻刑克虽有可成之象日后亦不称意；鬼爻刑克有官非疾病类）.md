# 求馆·身位受伤，虽成不利（旧注：世身被月建日辰动爻刑克虽有可成之象日后亦不称意；鬼爻刑克有官非疾病类）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻、身爻被月建、日辰、动爻刑克，虽然有可成的象，日后也不称心；如果是官鬼爻刑克，会有官非、疾病之类的事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self and the Body are punished or controlled by the Month, the Day or a moving line, then though there is the image of its coming about, it will not be to one's liking later; if it is Pressure that punishes or controls, there will be lawsuits, illness and the like.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8566 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身位受伤，虽成不利； | 世身受到伤害，即使成了也不利； | If the Self and the Body are injured, even if it comes about it is unfavourable; |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2764 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世身被月建、日辰、动爻刑克，虽成而日后有官非疾病。 | 世身被月建、日辰、动爻刑克，虽然成了，日后有官非、疾病。 | If the Self and the Body are punished or controlled by the Month, the Day or a moving line, then though it comes about there will be lawsuits and illness later. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是「成了以后」的风险：事成了，但世身受伤，日后不如意；官鬼刑克世身，还会招来官司和疾病。王洪绪没有区分是什么爻刑克，一概说日后有官非疾病。
>
> *Note:* This entry is a risk 'after it has come about': the matter is obtained but the Self and the Body are injured and things go ill later; and if Pressure punishes or controls the Self and the Body, lawsuits and illness are also drawn in. Wang Hongxu does not distinguish by which line is doing the punishing and controlling, and says in all cases that lawsuits and illness come later.
