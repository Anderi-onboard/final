# 朝天面旨：以太岁五爻为主、官鬼世象为凭；面君官旺世无伤折槛廷诤也不妨，太岁五爻冲克世休趋殿陛惹天殃；面君者先推世象次察官爻，二爻旺相不空又不受君爻冲克则并无玷剥，世爻若被天子之爻冲克朝必遭殃守静为上

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 朝见君王，以太岁和五爻为主，以官鬼、世爻为凭据。面君时官爻旺相、世爻没有受伤，就是折断栏杆、在朝廷上直言力谏也无妨；太岁、五爻如果冲克世爻，就不要趋步到殿前去招来天灾。面君的人，先推世爻，再察官爻：这两爻如果旺相、不落空亡，又不受君王之爻冲克，去见天子就没有污损伤害；世爻如果被天子之爻冲克，上朝一定遭殃，以安静守着为上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For an audience with the sovereign, take Tai Sui and the fifth line as the chief, and Pressure and the Self image as the ground. If the star of office is thriving and the Self is unhurt, even breaking the balustrade in a remonstrance at court is no matter; if Tai Sui or the fifth line clashes with or controls the Self, do not hasten to the palace steps to bring on a heavenly calamity. One who is to see the sovereign should first reckon the Self image and then examine the Pressure line: if these two lines are thriving, not on Void, and not clashed or controlled by the sovereign's line, then going before the Son of Heaven there will be no blemish or harm; if the Self line is clashed or controlled by the Son of Heaven's line, going to court will surely bring calamity, and keeping quiet is best.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> | 世爻若被天子之爻冲克者，朝必遭殃，守静为上。 | 世爻如果被天子之爻冲克，上朝一定遭殃，以安静守着为上。 | If the Self line is clashed or controlled by the Son of Heaven's line, going to court will surely bring calamity, and keeping quiet is best. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「折槛」用的是汉朝朱云在殿上直谏、攀折殿槛（栏杆）的典故；「廷诤」是在朝廷上直言劝谏；「殿陛」是宫殿的台阶。「在去天天并无玷剥」文字有误，疑是「此去天颜并无玷剥」，译作「去见天子就没有污损、伤害」。「君爻」是五爻（天子之爻）。「守静」是不去、不动。这一条与增删卜易上书「子动临身，力能折槛」（ZS.33）同用「折槛」一典，但那一条看的是子孙，这里看官爻和世爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhe jian' alludes to Zhu Yun of the Han, who remonstrated in the hall and clung to the balustrade until it broke; 'tingzheng' is remonstrating plainly at court; 'dianbi' are the steps of the palace. 'Zai qu tian tian bing wu dian bo' is corrupt in its characters and is presumably 'ci qu tianyan bing wu dian bo', translated 'going before the Son of Heaven there will be no blemish or harm'. 'Junyao', the sovereign's line, is the fifth line (the line of the Son of Heaven). 'Shoujing' is not going and not acting. This entry uses the same allusion of 'breaking the balustrade' as 'if Output moves on the Body, one has the strength to break the balustrade' in the Zengshan Buyi on submitting letters (ZS.33), but that entry looks at Output and this one looks at the Pressure line and the Self.
