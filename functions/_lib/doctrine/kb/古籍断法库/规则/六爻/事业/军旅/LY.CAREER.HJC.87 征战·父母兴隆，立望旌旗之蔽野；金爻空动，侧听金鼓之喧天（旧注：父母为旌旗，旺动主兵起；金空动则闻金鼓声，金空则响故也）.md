# 征战·父母兴隆，立望旌旗之蔽野；金爻空动，侧听金鼓之喧天（旧注：父母为旌旗，旺动主兵起；金空动则闻金鼓声，金空则响故也）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻是旌旗。父母旺相发动，主兵马起动，旌旗遮满原野；金爻落空而动，就听到金鼓声喧闹震天，因为金落空了才会响。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line is the banners. If Resource is thriving and moves, an army is rising and banners cover the plain; if a Metal line is Void and moves, one hears the din of gongs and drums shaking the sky, since Metal sounds when it is void.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5906 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母兴隆，立望旌旗之蔽野； | 父母爻兴旺，站着就能望见旌旗遮满原野； | If Resource is thriving, one standing and looking sees banners covering the plain; |
> | 金爻空动，侧听金鼓之喧天。 | 金爻落空而发动，侧耳就能听到锣鼓声喧闹震天。 | if a Metal line is Void and moves, listening aside one hears the din of gongs and drums shaking the sky. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5907 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母为旌旗。 | 父母是旌旗。 | Resource is the banners. |
> | 旺动主兵起。 | 旺相发动，主兵马起动。 | If it is thriving and moves, it means an army rises. |
> | 金空动，则闻金鼓声。 | 金落空而发动，就能听到金鼓之声。 | If Metal is Void and moves, one hears the sound of gongs and drums. |
> | 金空则响，故也。 | 金落空就会响，所以是这样。 | Metal sounds when it is void, which is why. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1514 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母为旌旗； | 父母是旌旗； | Resource is the banners; |
> | 金动则闻金鼓声，金空则响故也。 | 金爻发动就能听到金鼓之声，因为金落空了才会响。 | if Metal moves one hears the sound of gongs and drums, since Metal sounds when it is void. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注在「父母兴持」一联末尾说「以上数节，出《天玄赋》，不细解」，所以这一组几联的注都很简短。
>
> *Note:* At the end of the couplet 'Resource holds the Self' the old commentary says 'the sections above come from the Tianxuan Fu and are not explained in detail', so the notes on this group of couplets are brief.
