# 征战·子虽旺而遭克制，毋急兴师（旧注：子孙虽旺，若被父母日辰动爻克害，彼必有计，不可急攻；攻之必被摧折，虽不大败亦损军威，宜缓图之）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙虽然旺相，但如果被父母、日辰、动爻克害，对方一定有计谋，不可急着进攻；进攻就必定受挫，即使不至于大败，也会损伤军威，应当缓缓图谋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Though Output is thriving, if it is controlled and harmed by Resource, the Day or a moving line, the enemy surely has a stratagem and one must not attack in haste; an attack will be broken, and even if it is not a great defeat it will damage the army's prestige, so one should plan it slowly.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5970 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子虽旺而遭克制，毋急兴师。 | 子孙虽然旺相却遭到克制，不要急着出兵。 | Though Output is thriving, if it is controlled, do not send out troops in haste. |

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说克害子孙的是「父母日辰动爻」，卜筮正宗只说「日辰动爻」，没有父母。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary names the controllers of Output as 'Resource, the Day or a moving line'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says only 'the Day or a moving line', without Resource.
