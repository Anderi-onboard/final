# 出师征伐：世克应爻师勇兵强可剿伐，应伤世象贼多凶炽莫撄锋；世克应则决胜，世旺动带福神克应克鬼势如破竹，世旺克而无吉神助反被日辰动爻克世虽胜未全功，世衰应旺只可御敌；世化子孙应化官鬼宜假途灭敌，子孙伏世下冲应克应暗渡陈仓；应克世彼赢按兵守候，应旺动克世或鬼煞同克彼合谋夹战；应上加兄弟诡谲、玄武偷营、白虎猖獗

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻克应爻，师勇兵强，可以剿伐；应爻伤世象，贼多势凶，不要触其锋。世克应爻，我必定取胜；世爻旺相发动而带福神克应、克官鬼的，势如破竹，一战就奏凯；世爻旺相克应但没有吉神发动帮助，反而被日辰、动爻克世的，虽然能取胜却没有获得全功；世衰应旺，对方虽然受克，也只可以抵御；（这样）没有得到奇谋善策，不能大规模专门征伐。世变子孙、应变官鬼的，宜用借路灭敌的计策，又不可再战邀功；子孙伏在世爻下面，而冲应、克应的，必须暗渡陈仓。应克世，必定对方赢，暂且按兵守候；应旺而克世的，来势必定不可轻视；应旺相发动克世，或者同旺相的官鬼、凶煞一起来克的，对方必定合谋夹击，机关难测。应爻上如果加兄弟，（对方）非常诡诈；加玄武，必定偷营；加白虎，猖獗突击得很厉害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self controls the Other, the troops are brave and strong and one can campaign; if the Other injures the Self image, the bandits are many and fierce and one must not touch their edge. If the Self controls the Other, I surely win; if the Self is thriving and moving and carries the fortune spirit, Output, and controls the Other and controls Pressure, the force is like splitting bamboo and one wins in a single battle; if the Self is thriving and controls but no auspicious spirit moves to help, and instead the Day or a moving line controls the Self, though one can win one does not gain complete success; if the Self is weak and the Other thriving, though they are controlled, one can only fend them off, and without marvellous plans and good strategies one cannot make large expeditions. If the Self turns into Output and the Other into Pressure, it is fit to destroy the enemy by borrowing a road, and one must not fight again to seek credit; if Output lies hidden under the Self and clashes with or controls the Other, one must secretly cross at Chencang. If the Other controls the Self, they surely win, and for the moment one holds the troops and waits; if the Other is thriving and controls the Self, its force is surely not to be made light of; if the Other is thriving and moving and controls the Self, or thriving Pressure and baleful spirits come to control together, they will surely plot together and attack from both sides, and their stratagems are hard to fathom. If a Peer line is added on the Other, they are extremely crafty; with the Dark Tortoise, they will surely raid the camp; with the White Tiger, they are fiercely and violently aggressive.

> [!note] 按
> 「席卷长驱」「势如破竹」是势头猛烈、一路取胜；「假途灭敌」（假道伐虢）是借路灭掉敌人，「暗渡陈仓」是明里修栈道、暗里绕道偷袭，这两个典故书里自己写出来了。「其势决识」文字有误，疑是「其势炽盛」一类，译文按「不可轻视」译并标出原文；「机关匡测」的「匡」是「叵」（不可）。第四、五句「世衰应旺……只可御敌而已，未得奇谋善策大专征伐」连读：世弱应强时只能防御。
>
> *Note:* 'Xi juan chang qu' and 'shi ru po zhu' mean a fierce momentum and winning all the way; 'jia tu mie di' (borrowing the road to destroy Guo) is destroying the enemy by borrowing a passage, and 'an du Chencang' is openly repairing the plank road while secretly going round to raid, both allusions the book writes out itself. 'Qi shi jue shi' is defective, presumably something like 'its force is blazing', and the translation reads 'not to be made light of' and marks the original; the 'kuang' of 'jiguan kuang ce' is 'po', cannot. The fourth and fifth sentences, 'if the Self is weak and the Other thriving ... one can only fend them off, and without marvellous plans and good strategies one cannot make large expeditions', read on together: when the Self is weak and the Other strong one can only defend.
