# 求馆·日克月克动变克，恐受刑伤（旧注：月日动变诸爻皆来刑克世爻占馆必不可成；占束修恐被诸生父兄呵责宜慎；王洪绪：占馆有不测之凶）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建、日辰、动爻、变爻等爻，都来刑克世爻的，占馆一定不能成；占束修，恐怕被学生的父兄呵责，应该谨慎。王洪绪说得更重：占馆有意想不到的凶险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Month, the Day, the moving lines and the changed lines all come to punish or control the Self, the school post cannot come about; in divining the fee, one may be scolded by the pupils' fathers and brothers, and care should be taken. Wang Hongxu puts it more heavily: in divining a school post there is an unforeseen ill.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日克月克动变克，恐受刑伤。 | 日辰克、月建克、动爻变爻也克，恐怕要受到刑罚伤害。 | If the Day controls, the Month controls, and the moving and changed lines control too, one may suffer punishment and injury. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 同一个象（月日动变都来刑克世爻），旧注说的是「占馆必不可成」，再说占束修会被学生父兄呵责；王洪绪说的是「占馆有不测之凶」。旧注是事成不成的问题，王洪绪是祸患大小的问题，他把结果说得更重，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For the same image (the Month, the Day and the moving and changed lines all punish or control the Self) the old commentary says 'the school post cannot come about' and then that in divining the fee one will be scolded by the pupils' fathers and brothers, and Wang Hongxu says 'in divining a school post there is an unforeseen ill'. The old commentary is about whether it comes about, and Wang Hongxu about how great the misfortune is, and he puts the result more heavily; the books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「刑伤」在经文里指受到刑罚、伤害，旧注落实为「被呵责」。
>
> *Note:* 'Punishment and injury' in the verse means suffering punishment and harm, which the old commentary makes concrete as 'being scolded'.
