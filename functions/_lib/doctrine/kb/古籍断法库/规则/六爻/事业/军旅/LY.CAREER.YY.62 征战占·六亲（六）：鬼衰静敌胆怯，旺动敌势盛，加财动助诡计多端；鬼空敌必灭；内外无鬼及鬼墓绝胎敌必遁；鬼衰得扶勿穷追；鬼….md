# 征战占·六亲（六）：鬼衰静敌胆怯，旺动敌势盛，加财动助诡计多端；鬼空敌必灭；内外无鬼及鬼墓绝胎敌必遁；鬼衰得扶勿穷追；鬼带亡神劫杀在内卦动防奸细，世旺动伤克鬼奸细必擒；世鬼动生合应无援而降；卦无财应克世食尽而毙

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼看敌人：官鬼衰弱安静，敌人胆怯；旺相发动，敌势强盛；再加财爻发动帮它，敌人诡计多端、变诈百出。官鬼落空，敌人必定灭亡；内外卦都没有官鬼，以及官鬼临墓、绝、胎的，敌人必定逃走；官鬼衰弱但得到扶助，不要追穷寇。官鬼带亡神、劫杀在内卦发动的，防奸细；如果世爻旺相发动、伤克官鬼，奸细必被擒获。世爻和官鬼发动、生合应爻的，因为没有救援而投降；卦里没有财爻，而应爻克世爻的，因为粮尽而毙命。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure and the enemy. If Pressure is weak and still, the enemy is timid; if thriving and moving, the enemy's force is strong, and with Wealth moving to help it, the enemy has many tricks and a hundred deceits. If Pressure is Void, the enemy is surely destroyed; if there is no Pressure in the inner or outer trigram, or Pressure sits on tomb, extinction or the womb stage, the enemy surely slips away; if Pressure is weak but supported, do not hound a desperate foe. If Pressure carrying the Lost Spirit and Robbery moves in the inner trigram, guard against spies; if the Self is thriving and moving and injures or controls Pressure, the spy is surely caught. If the Self and Pressure move and generate or combine with the Other, he surrenders for lack of relief; if the hexagram has no Wealth and the Other controls the Self, he dies when the food runs out.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「穷寇勿追」是兵法的老话；「世鬼动」按字面是世爻和官鬼发动，书里没有说是谁生合应爻，译文把世爻与官鬼一并译出。末句在书里被断行隔开（「而应」在上一行末，「克世者」在下一行首），这里连起来读。
>
> *Note:* 'Do not pursue a desperate foe' is an old maxim of the art of war; 'shi gui dong' is by the letter the Self and Pressure moving, and the book does not say which of them generates or combines with the Other, so the translation renders both together. The last sentence is split by a line break in the book ('and the Other' at the end of one line and 'controls the Self' at the start of the next) and is read here joined up.
