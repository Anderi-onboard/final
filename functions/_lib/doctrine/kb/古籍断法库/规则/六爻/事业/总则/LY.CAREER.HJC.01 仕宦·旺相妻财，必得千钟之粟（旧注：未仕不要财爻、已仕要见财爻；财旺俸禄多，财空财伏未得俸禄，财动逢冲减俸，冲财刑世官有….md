# 仕宦·旺相妻财，必得千钟之粟（旧注：未仕不要财爻、已仕要见财爻；财旺俸禄多，财空财伏未得俸禄，财动逢冲减俸，冲财刑世官有停俸罢职之患）

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占做官，妻财就是俸禄。还没做官而求功名的人，不要财爻；已经做官的人，要见财爻，因为有爵位就有俸禄，没有没俸禄就能做官的。妻财旺相，俸禄一定多；休囚就微薄；卦里没有财或财落空亡，还没有俸禄；财动遇冲，任职以后会因事减俸；日辰月建冲财、又刑害世爻和官爻，恐怕有停发俸禄、罢职的祸患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining an office, Wealth is the salary. For one not yet in office who is seeking a name, Wealth is not wanted; for one already in office, Wealth is wanted, since where there is rank there is a stipend, and no one gets an office with no pay. When Wealth is thriving the salary is surely large; resting or trapped, it is meagre; if there is no Wealth in the hexagram or it falls Void, no salary has yet been received; if Wealth moves and meets a clash, the salary is cut after taking up the post, on account of some matter; and if the Day or the Month clashes Wealth while also punishing or harming the Self and Pressure, there may be the calamity of salary being stopped and dismissal from office.

> [!note] 按
> 这一首的前两句「为国求贤，治国之本；致身辅相，禄养为先」是章首的引语，不是判断；卜筮正宗写作「治民为本」。「千钟之粟」的「钟」是古时量粮的单位，「千钟」是说俸禄很多。「未仕求名，不要财爻」是说还没做官的人求功名时，财爻反而不要（财克父母，妨文书），这和黄金策求名章「财若交重，休望青钱之中选」是同一个道理（求名章在「考试」一域）；已经做官的就要有财，因为官有俸禄。「禄养」是靠俸禄养身养家。卜筮正宗把「兴隆官鬼」那一联的注和这一联的注写在一起，所以王洪绪这一段的后两句（官鬼旺相、发动生合世爻）分到 LY.CAREER.HJC.02。
>
> *Note:* The two opening lines of this entry, 'seeking worthies for the state is the root of governing it; giving oneself to assist the sovereign and his ministers, with the salary that sustains one coming first', are a preface and not a judgement; the Bushi Zhengzong writes 'the root of governing the people'. A 'zhong' is an ancient measure of grain, and 'a thousand zhong' means a large salary. 'For one not yet in office who is seeking a name, Wealth is not wanted' says that for someone not yet in office seeking a name, Wealth on the contrary is not wanted (Wealth controls Resource and harms the documents), the same reasoning as 'if Wealth moves heavily, do not hope to be chosen for the blue coins' in the chapter on seeking a name of this book (that chapter belongs to the domain of examinations); for one already in office there must be Wealth, since an office carries a stipend. 'The salary that sustains' is living and keeping a household on the stipend. The Bushi Zhengzong runs the comment on the couplet 'with Pressure flourishing' together with the comment on this couplet, so the last two sentences of Wang Hongxu's passage (Pressure thriving; moving to generate or combine with the Self) are placed in LY.CAREER.HJC.02.
