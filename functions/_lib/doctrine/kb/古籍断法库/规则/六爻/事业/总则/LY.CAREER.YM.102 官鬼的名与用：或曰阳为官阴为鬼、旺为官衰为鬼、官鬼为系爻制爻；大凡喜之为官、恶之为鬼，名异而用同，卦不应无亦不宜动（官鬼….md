# 官鬼的名与用：或曰阳为官阴为鬼、旺为官衰为鬼、官鬼为系爻制爻；大凡喜之为官、恶之为鬼，名异而用同，卦不应无亦不宜动（官鬼不必以旺衰阴阳分，用则为官忌则为鬼，官亦可凶鬼亦可吉）

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有人说阳是官、阴是鬼；又说旺是官、衰是鬼；又说官鬼是「系」爻、是「制」爻。大凡喜欢它的叫官，厌恶它的叫鬼；用在阳的一面就是官，用在阴的一面就是鬼：名称不同而用法相同。卦里不该没有它，也不宜发动。官鬼不必按旺衰、阴阳来分：只要取它来用就是官，忌它就是鬼；然而官也可能凶，鬼也可能吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Some say that yang is the official and yin is the demon; some say that thriving is the official and weak is the demon; some say that Pressure is the 'binding' line and the 'controlling' line. In general what one welcomes is called the official and what one dislikes is called the demon; used on the yang side it is the official, used on the yin side it is the demon: the names differ but the use is the same. The hexagram should not lack it, and it should not move. Pressure need not be told apart by strength and weakness or by yin and yang: if it is taken for use it is the official, and if it is feared it is the demon; yet the official may be ominous, and the demon may be auspicious.

> [!note] 按
> 「官」和「鬼」是同一个爻（官鬼，克我的那一类）的两个名字：本书的办法不按旺衰或阴阳去分，只看当下是取它来用、还是忌它：要它出力（求官、求名）时叫官，怕它为祸（疾病、官司）时叫鬼，所以书里有时写官、有时写鬼。「系爻」是拴住人的爻，「制爻」是管制人的爻（书里没有再解释）；原文「官鬼为系（击？）爻」里的「击？」是书里自己标的存疑字。「卦不应无，亦不宜动」是官鬼在卦里既不该没有，也不宜发动。这一条是读本书里「官」「鬼」两字的钥匙，和 YM.40 的「以官为用神」、YM.41 的「随官入墓、助鬼伤身」对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Guan' and 'gui' are two names of one line (Pressure, the kind that controls me): the book's method does not tell them apart by strength or yin and yang, but only by whether it is being taken for use or feared at the moment: when one wants it to exert itself (seeking office or a name) it is called the official, and when one fears it as a source of harm (illness, lawsuits) it is called the demon, so the book sometimes writes 'guan' and sometimes 'gui'. The 'binding line' is the line that ties a man down, and the 'controlling line' is the line that regulates a man (the book does not explain further); the 'ji?' in the original 'guan gui wei xi (ji?) yao' is a doubtful character marked by the book itself. 'The hexagram should not lack it, and it should not move' is that Pressure should be neither absent from the hexagram nor moving. This entry is the key to reading the two words 'guan' and 'gui' in this book, to be read against 'taking Pressure as the Subject' in YM.40 and 'following Pressure into the tomb and helping Pressure to injure the Body' in YM.41.
