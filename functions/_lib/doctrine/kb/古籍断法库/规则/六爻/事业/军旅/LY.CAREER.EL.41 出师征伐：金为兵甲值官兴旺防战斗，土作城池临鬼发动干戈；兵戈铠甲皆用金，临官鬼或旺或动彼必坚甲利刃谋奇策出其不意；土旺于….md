# 出师征伐：金为兵甲值官兴旺防战斗，土作城池临鬼发动干戈；兵戈铠甲皆用金，临官鬼或旺或动彼必坚甲利刃谋奇策出其不意；土旺于四季分四隅又作城垣台堡，鬼带土发则干戈扰扰弥山漫野

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金是兵甲，金值官鬼兴旺，要防战斗；土当城池，土临官鬼发动，就动起干戈。兵器铠甲都用金来造，如果金临官鬼，或者旺相、或者发动，对方必定甲坚刃利（与众不同），谋划奇策、出其不意地猛然冲突，不要小看；土旺在四季，分布四个角落，又作城墙、台堡，如果官鬼带土发动，就干戈扰攘，漫山遍野，无地不有。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Metal is arms and armour, and if Metal meets a flourishing Pressure, guard against fighting; Earth is the city walls, and if Earth sits on Pressure and moves, spears and shields are raised. Weapons and armour are all made of Metal, and if Metal sits on Pressure and is thriving or moving, the enemy surely has strong armour and sharp blades, unlike the common, and plans unusual strategies to charge suddenly and unexpectedly, which must not be made light of; Earth is thriving in the four seasons and spread over the four corners, and also serves as city walls, terraces and forts, and if Pressure carries Earth and moves, spears and shields are in turmoil, covering the hills and spreading over the fields, with no place free of them.

> [!note] 按
> 第二联「临鬼发遁动干戈」的「遁」是衍字或误字，译文按「临官鬼发动、动起干戈」译。「墟然」疑是「忽然」（或「虚然」），译作「猛然」。「干戈」是兵器，代指战争。这一条与易隐征战占「五行」里的金（兵甲）、土（炮石城池）（YY.66、YY.65）取象相同。
>
> *Note:* In the second couplet 'lin gui fa dun dong ganqe' the 'dun' is a superfluous or miswritten character, and the translation reads 'sits on Pressure and moves, spears and shields are raised'. 'Xu ran' is presumably 'hu ran', suddenly (or 'xu ran'), and is translated 'suddenly'. 'Ganqe' are weapons, standing for war. This entry takes the same images as Metal (arms and armour) and Earth (catapult stones and city walls) in the 'five elements' section of the Yi Yin war divination (YY.66, YY.65).
