# 卜馆·选文刻书与卜徒好恶：选文刻书名用父母、利用妻财；卜徒三教用子孙、百工用应象，执贽之后分、束修未行则皆从应象；应克世其人矫罔、应空破散其人下劣、三冲无恒心、财空无礼币

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问选文刻书，用神有两个：求名用父母，求利用妻财。卜徒弟的好坏，用神也是两个：儒、释、道三教的师徒情谊同父子，所以用子孙；百工是为谋利学的，所以凭应象。不过执贽（行了拜师礼）之后，才有用子孙还是用应象的分别；束修还没送，就都从应象，这是三教、百工相同的。应克世，这个人矫饰欺罔；应空、破、散，这个人下劣；三冲，没有恒心；财空，没有礼金礼物。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about choosing texts and cutting blocks, the Subject is of two kinds: for fame Resource is used, for profit Wealth. In divining whether a disciple is good or bad the Subject is also of two kinds: the three teachings, Confucian, Buddhist and Daoist, have a bond of teacher and pupil like father and son, so Output is used; the hundred crafts are learned for gain, so the Other image is relied on. But it is after the formal presenting of gifts that there is the division between Output and the Other image; before the tuition gift is offered, all follow the Other image, and in this the three teachings and the hundred crafts are the same. The Other controlling the Self, and the person is deceitful; the Other Void, broken or scattered, and the person is base; the three clashes, and he has no constancy; Wealth Void, and he has no gift of money or goods.

> [!note] 按
> 「选文刻书」是选编文章、刻版印行，「名用父母」是为名声，父母是文书；「利用妻财」是为卖书得利。「三教」是儒释道，「百工」是各行手艺。「执贽」是学生向老师行拜师礼、送上见面礼；「束修」是一束干肉，后来指学生送给先生的酬金，「未行」是还没送。「应象」是用应爻来看这个人。这一条是『收徒』这一边：前面是我被人聘，这里是我收人做徒弟。「矫罔」是矫饰欺骗。「礼币」是礼金礼物。
>
> *Note:* 'Xuanwen keshu' is compiling essays and cutting blocks to print them, and 'fame uses Resource' is because Resource is documents, for the sake of reputation; 'profit uses Wealth' is for the gain from selling the books. The 'three teachings' are Confucianism, Buddhism and Daoism, and the 'hundred crafts' are the trades of every line. 'Zhizhi' is a pupil's formal ceremony of becoming a disciple and the first gift to the teacher; 'shuxiu' is a bundle of dried meat, later the fee a pupil gives his teacher, and 'not yet offered' is not yet given. 'The Other image' is using the Other line to look at this person. This entry is the side of taking disciples: earlier I am the one engaged, and here I am taking on someone as a disciple. 'Jiaowang' is deceit and false show. 'Libi' is gifts of money and goods.
