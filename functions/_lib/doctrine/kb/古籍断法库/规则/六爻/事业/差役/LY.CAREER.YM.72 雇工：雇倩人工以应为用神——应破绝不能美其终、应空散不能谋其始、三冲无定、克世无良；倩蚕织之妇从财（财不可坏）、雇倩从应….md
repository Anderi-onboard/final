# 雇工：雇倩人工以应为用神——应破绝不能美其终、应空散不能谋其始、三冲无定、克世无良；倩蚕织之妇从财（财不可坏）、雇倩从应（应不可病）；应来克世恐害吾事，合冲变冲不可久留、八纯六冲不可暂用

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 雇请人工（雇工），就以应为用神：应破、绝，就不能善终；应空、散，就不能开好头；三冲，没有定准；克世，没有良善。雇请养蚕织布的妇女，妇女按财取：财不可坏；雇请（别的）按应取：应不可有病。应来克世，恐怕妨害我的事；合处逢冲、变出冲，不可久留；八纯卦、六冲卦，连暂时用也不可。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In hiring labour, the Other is the Subject: the Other broken or extinct, and it cannot end well; the Other Void or scattered, and it cannot get a good start; the three clashes, and there is nothing settled; controlling the Self, and there is no goodness. In hiring a woman who raises silkworms and weaves, a woman is taken by Wealth, and Wealth must not be damaged; hiring is otherwise by the Other, and the Other must not be sick. If the Other comes to control the Self, I fear it harms my affairs; a combination meeting a clash, or a changed clash, means he must not be kept long; a pure hexagram of eight or a six-clash, he must not be used even briefly.

> [!note] 按
> 「雇倩」是雇请，「人工」是雇工、工匠。「美其终」是善终，「谋其始」是开好头。「蚕织之妇」是养蚕织布的妇女，她们靠做工得钱，所以取财，不取应。「合冲变冲」是合处逢冲、变出六冲（三冲里的两种，见 YM.07 注）。「八纯」是八纯卦（乾、坤、震、巽、坎、离、艮、兑各自的本宫卦，上下同卦，多是六冲）。「不可暂用」是连暂时用一下也不行。这一条用应当雇工的用神，和上面收仆用财（YM.69）分开：仆人是家里的人，取财；外面雇来的人工，取应。
>
> *Note:* 'Guqian' is hiring, and 'rengong' is hired labour and craftsmen. 'Mei qi zhong' is a good end, and 'mou qi shi' is a good beginning. 'Can zhi zhi fu' is a woman who raises silkworms and weaves; they earn money by their work, so Wealth is taken and not the Other. 'Hechong bianchong' is a combination meeting a clash and a changed six-clash (two of the three clashes, see the note to YM.07). 'Ba chun' is the pure hexagrams of the eight (the home hexagram of each of Qian, Kun, Zhen, Xun, Kan, Li, Gen and Dui, with the same trigram above and below, mostly a six-clash). 'Not to be used even briefly' is that even using him for a short time will not do. This entry takes the Other as the Subject for hired labour, to be kept apart from taking on a servant by Wealth (YM.69): a servant is one of the household and is taken by Wealth, while labour hired from outside is taken by the Other.
