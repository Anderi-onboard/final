# 出师征伐：子爻旺鬼爻休鬼虽克我我无忧，应爻强世爻弱世纵伤他他难伐，世遇生扶子象旺则将勇兵骁，应遭克破鬼爻空则贼衰寇退；子能制鬼，子旺官休则彼先已被制岂能克世；应旺世衰虽克应彼猖獗难取胜（旺不受克）；日辰月将生世合世旺子扶助则我兵精粮足决胜千里；应衰受克受冲官鬼空亡彼必丧胆倒戈拔寨而逃

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻旺相、官鬼爻休囚，官鬼虽然克我，我也无忧；应爻强、世爻弱，世爻纵然伤他，他也难被讨伐；世爻遇到生扶、子孙象旺相，就是将领勇敢、兵卒骁勇；应爻遭到克破、官鬼爻落空，就是贼衰寇退。子孙能制官鬼：如果子孙旺相、官鬼休囚，对方先已被制住，哪里能克世爻呢？就像大盗正想打劫别人，已经被官府搜捕擒获，哪有工夫行劫，我还忧虑什么呢？如果应爻旺相、世爻衰弱，虽然克应爻，无奈对方猖獗，难以取胜，「旺不受克」说的正是这个。如果日辰、月将生世、合世，旺相的子孙又来扶助，就是我兵精良、粮草充足、机谋深远、声势浩大，决胜千里；如果应衰弱受克受冲，官鬼又落空亡，对方必定丧胆、倒戈、拔寨逃走。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Output line is thriving and the Pressure line resting, though Pressure controls me I have nothing to fear; if the Other is strong and the Self weak, though the Self injures them, they are hard to attack; if the Self meets generation and support and the Output image is thriving, the commander is brave and the troops valiant; if the Other is controlled and broken and the Pressure line Void, the bandits decline and withdraw. Output can control Pressure: if Output is thriving and Pressure resting, the enemy has first been restrained, so how can it control the Self? It is like a great robber who is about to rob someone and has already been seized by the officers, so how has he time to rob, and what have I to fear? If the Other is thriving and the Self weak, though the Self controls the Other, the enemy is rampant and hard to beat, and 'the thriving is not controlled' says exactly this. If the Day and the Month General generate or combine with the Self, and a thriving Output also comes to support, my troops are well trained, grain is plentiful, plans are deep and strength great, and victory is decided a thousand miles away; if the Other is weak and controlled and clashed, and Pressure also falls into Void, the enemy will surely lose heart, turn their spears, pull up their stockades and flee.

> [!note] 按
> 「月将」是太阳所在的月份地支（神煞，不是月建）；「倒戈」是掉转兵器、反过来攻自己人。第一首四联的头两联是「子旺鬼休，鬼克我也无忧」和「应强世弱，世伤他也难伐」，解里正好对应到「子能制鬼」和「旺不受克」两个道理。第二个比喻在书里被断行隔开（「被官捕搜擒」在上一行末，「何暇行劫」在下一行首），这里连起来读。
>
> *Note:* The 'Month General' is the branch of the month in which the sun stands (a spirit-star, not the Month branch); 'dao ge' is turning one's weapons round and attacking one's own side. The first two couplets of the four are 'Output thriving, Pressure resting, though Pressure controls me I have no worry' and 'the Other strong, the Self weak, though the Self injures them they are hard to attack', and the explanation answers them with the two principles 'Output can control Pressure' and 'the thriving is not controlled'. The second simile is split by a line break in the book ('seized by the officers' at the end of one line and 'how has he time to rob' at the start of the next) and is read here joined up.
