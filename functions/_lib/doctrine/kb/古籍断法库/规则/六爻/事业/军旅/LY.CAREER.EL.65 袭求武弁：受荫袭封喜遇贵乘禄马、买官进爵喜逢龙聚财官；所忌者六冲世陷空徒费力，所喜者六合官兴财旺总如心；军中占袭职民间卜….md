# 袭求武弁：受荫袭封喜遇贵乘禄马、买官进爵喜逢龙聚财官；所忌者六冲世陷空徒费力，所喜者六合官兴财旺总如心；军中占袭职民间卜官各忌世空官空六冲之卦，得官与世旺定显威风，官世带青龙贵人及卦逢六合威福更加

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 靠祖荫承袭封爵，喜欢遇到贵人乘着禄、马；捐买官职、进升爵位，喜欢遇到青龙聚着财爻、官爻。所忌讳的是六冲、世爻落陷落空，白费力气；所喜欢的是六合、官鬼兴旺、财爻旺相，事事称心。军中占承袭职位、民间卜官职，各自都忌世爻空、官空和六冲的卦；如果官与世都旺相，一定显出威风；官爻或世爻带青龙、贵人，以及卦遇六合，威势和福分更加增添。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To inherit a title by ancestral privilege, one is glad to meet the Noble riding on the Emolument and the Horse; to buy an office and advance in rank, one is glad to meet the Dragon gathering Wealth and the star of office. What is feared is a six-clash with the Self sunk or Void, which is wasted effort; what is welcomed is a six-combine with Pressure flourishing and Wealth thriving, everything as one wishes. In the army divining for inheriting a post, or among the people divining for office, each fears the Self Void, the star of office Void, and a six-clash hexagram; if the star of office and the Self are both thriving, one will surely show authority; if the Pressure line or the Self line carries the Azure Dragon or the Noble, or the hexagram meets a six-combine, authority and fortune are further increased.

> [!note] 按
> 「受荫」是靠祖先的功劳取得封爵；「买官进爵」是捐纳。「恢逢」疑是「喜逢」（上一联是「喜遇」），译作「喜欢遇到」。「贵乘禄马」是贵人、禄、驿马这几样同时遇上（见术语表）。「官空」的「官」是官鬼。「六合」「六冲」指卦的六合、六冲。这一条与易隐官禄占「何官」一组里的「世持官贵禄马」（YY.03、YY.04）取同一类象。
>
> *Note:* 'Shou yin' is obtaining a title through the merit of one's ancestors; 'mai guan jin jue' is buying office and rank by contribution. The 'hui' of 'hui feng' is presumably 'xi', glad (the couplet before has 'xi yu'), and is translated 'glad to meet'. 'Gui cheng lu ma' is the Noble, the Emolument and the Post Horse met together (see the glossary). The 'guan' of 'guan kong' is Pressure. 'Six-combine' and 'six-clash' are the hexagram patterns. This entry takes the same kind of image as 'the Self holds the Noble, Emolument and Horse' in the 'which office' group of the Yi Yin divination on official salary (YY.03, YY.04).
