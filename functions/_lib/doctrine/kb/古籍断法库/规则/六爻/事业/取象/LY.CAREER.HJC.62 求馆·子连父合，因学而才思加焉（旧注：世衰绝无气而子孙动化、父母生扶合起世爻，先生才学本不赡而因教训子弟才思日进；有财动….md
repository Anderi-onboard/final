# 求馆·子连父合，因学而才思加焉（旧注：世衰绝无气而子孙动化、父母生扶合起世爻，先生才学本不赡而因教训子弟才思日进；有财动则不然；王洪绪：子弟之才日加进益）

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占学馆，世爻如果衰绝无气，而遇到子孙发动变化、父母生扶、合起世爻的，主先生的才学本来不够丰富，因为教导子弟，他的才思日渐进益；卦里有财爻发动，就不是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a school, if the Self is weak and in extinction without breath, but Output moves and changes and Resource generates and supports and combines the Self up, it means the teacher's learning was not at first abundant, but through teaching the youngsters his talent and thought advance day by day; if a Wealth line moves in the hexagram, it is not so.

## 《黄金策·求馆(附束修)》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8635 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子连父合，因学而才思加焉。 | 子孙相连、父母相合，因为教学而才思日益增加。 | With Output linked and Resource combined, one's talent and thought grow through teaching. |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2798 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世若衰绝无气，而遇子孙动化生合世爻者，主子弟之才日加进益也。 | 世爻如果衰绝无气，而遇到子孙发动变化、生合世爻的，主子弟的才学日渐进益。 | If the Self is weak and in extinction without breath, but Output moves and changes and generates or combines with the Self, it means the pupils' talent advances day by day. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 进益的是谁，两家说法不一样：旧注说是先生（「先生才学本不克赡，因教训子弟而其才思日加进益」，教学相长）；王洪绪说是子弟（「主子弟之才日加进益也」）。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* On who is the one who advances, the two commentators differ: the old commentary says it is the teacher ('the teacher's learning was not at first abundant, and through teaching the youngsters his talent and thought advance day by day', teaching and learning growing together), and Wang Hongxu says it is the pupils ('the pupils' talent advances day by day'). The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这是求馆章的最后一联：前面都是讲能不能得到馆、束修多少，最后一联讲的是做了这份差事之后人会怎么样。「教学相长」的意思见旧注。
>
> *Note:* This is the last couplet of the chapter on a school post: the earlier ones speak of whether one gets the post and how much the fee is, and the last speaks of what becomes of the person after doing the work. For 'teaching and learning grow together' see the old commentary.
