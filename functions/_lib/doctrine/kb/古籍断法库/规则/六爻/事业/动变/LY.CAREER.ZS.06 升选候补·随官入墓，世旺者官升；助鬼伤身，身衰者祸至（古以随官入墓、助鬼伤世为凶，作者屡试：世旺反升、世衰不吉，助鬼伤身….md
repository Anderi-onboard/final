# 升选候补·随官入墓，世旺者官升；助鬼伤身，身衰者祸至（古以随官入墓、助鬼伤世为凶，作者屡试：世旺反升、世衰不吉，助鬼伤身而元神同动者官必升，世休囚必有祸）

六爻 · 事业 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 古人把「随官入墓」「助鬼伤世」一概当凶，作者多次试验，结果是：世爻旺相的反而升官；世爻衰弱的不吉；助鬼伤身而元神同时发动的，官职必定升；世爻如果休囚，必有灾祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ancients took 'following the star of office into the tomb' and 'helping Pressure to injure the Self' as ominous without exception; the author tested it many times and found: where the Self is thriving the man is promoted instead; where the Self is weak it is not auspicious; where one helps Pressure to injure the Body but the Support moves at the same time, the office is surely raised; if the Self is resting or trapped, disaster is sure to come.

> [!warning] 打架
> 古人把「随官入墓」「助鬼伤世」都当作凶；增删卜易说自己多次试验，世爻旺的反而升官，元神同时发动的官必升，只有世爻休囚才有祸。这是作者对古说的修正，不是两本书的分歧。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The ancients took 'following the star of office into the tomb' and 'helping Pressure to injure the Self' as ominous; the Zeng Shan Bu Yi says that after many tests the man whose Self was thriving was promoted instead, that where the Support moves at the same time the office is surely raised, and that only a resting or trapped Self brings disaster. This is the author's correction of the old teaching, not a disagreement between two books.

> [!note] 按
> 原文第二个例子的月份写作「戍月」，应是「戌月」，译文按戌月。
>
> *Note:* The second example gives the month as 'Shu' in the text, which should be 'Xu'; the translation reads the Xu month.
