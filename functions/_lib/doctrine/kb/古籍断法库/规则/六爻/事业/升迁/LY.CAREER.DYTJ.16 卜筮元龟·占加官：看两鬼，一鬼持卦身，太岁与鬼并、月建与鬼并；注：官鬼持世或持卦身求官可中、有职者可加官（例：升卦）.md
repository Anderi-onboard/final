# 卜筮元龟·占加官：看两鬼，一鬼持卦身，太岁与鬼并、月建与鬼并；注：官鬼持世或持卦身求官可中、有职者可加官（例：升卦）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占加官（加官进爵），看两个官鬼：一个鬼来持卦身，太岁和鬼并在一起，月建和鬼并在一起（原文「基阁」「外基」难解）。注文说：卦中官鬼爻持世或持卦身，求官可以中选，已有职位的人还可能升官；比如升卦，两个官都是酉金，临月卦身，世爻丑土是金的墓库，是有职位的人可以加官的兆头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining the adding of office, look at two Pressure lines: one Pressure line comes to hold the Body of the hexagram, Tai Sui joins with Pressure, and the Month joins with Pressure (the 'jige' and 'outer ji' of the text are unclear). The note says: if the Pressure line in the hexagram holds the Self or holds the Body, one seeking office can be selected, and one who already has a post may be promoted; for example in the hexagram Sheng the two Pressure lines are You Metal standing on the monthly Body, and the Self line Chou Earth is the tomb of Metal, a sign that one with a post can be given more office.
