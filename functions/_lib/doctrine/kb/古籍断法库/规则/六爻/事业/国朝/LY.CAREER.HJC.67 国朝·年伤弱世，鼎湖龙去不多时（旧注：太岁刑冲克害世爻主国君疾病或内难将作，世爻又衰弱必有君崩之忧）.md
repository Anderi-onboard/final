# 国朝·年伤弱世，鼎湖龙去不多时（旧注：太岁刑冲克害世爻主国君疾病或内难将作，世爻又衰弱必有君崩之忧）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国君自己占卜：太岁来刑、冲、克、害世爻，主国君生病，或者宫廷内部将有祸乱；如果世爻同时又衰弱，就要担心国君驾崩。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the ruler casts for himself: if Tai Sui punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Self, the ruler will be ill or trouble will break out within the court; if the Self is also weak, there is cause to fear the ruler's death.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5825 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 年伤弱世，鼎湖龙去不多时。 | 太岁伤克衰弱的世爻，国君像黄帝在鼎湖乘龙升天一样，离世就在不久之后。 | If the year injures a weak Self, the dragon departs from Ding Lake before long. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5827–5828 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 太岁刑冲克害世爻，主国君疾病，或内难将作。 | 太岁刑、冲、克、害世爻，主国君有疾病，或者内部的祸乱将要发作。 | If Tai Sui punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Self, it means illness of the ruler, or internal troubles about to break out. |
> | 若克世爻，而世爻衰弱，必有君崩之忧。 | 如果克的是世爻，而世爻又衰弱，必定有国君驾崩的忧虑。 | If it controls the Self and the Self is weak, there is surely cause to fear the ruler's death. |
> | 史记曰：黄帝乘龙上天。 | 《史记》说：黄帝乘龙升天。 | The Records of the Grand Historian says: the Yellow Emperor rose to heaven on a dragon. |
> | 故凡帝崩曰升遐，讳言死也。 | 所以凡是皇帝去世都叫「升遐」，是避讳说死。 | So whenever an emperor dies it is called 'ascending far away', which avoids the word death. |
> | 今日鼎湖龙去，言其不久而崩也。 | 现在说「鼎湖龙去」，是说他不久就要驾崩。 | Here 'the dragon leaves Ding Lake' says that he will die before long. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1470 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 以下指言国君自卜。 | 以下是指国君自己占卜。 | What follows speaks of the ruler casting for himself. |
> | 如太岁刑冲克害世爻，主疾病或内难将作。 | 例如太岁刑、冲、克、害世爻，主有疾病，或者内部的祸难将要发作。 | For example, if Tai Sui punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Self, it means illness or internal troubles about to break out. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「以下指言国君自卜」是卜筮正宗加的一句话，旧注里没有；按它的说法，从这一联起到「应带咸池」都是国君自占。
>
> *Note:* The sentence 'what follows speaks of the ruler casting for himself' is the Bu Shi Zheng Zong's own; the old commentary has no such line. On its reading, everything from this couplet through 'the Other carrying Xianchi' is the ruler's own cast.
