# 国朝·子发逢空，张子房起归山之计（旧注：子孙为臣，逢空动或四爻臣位空动，更被世爻克害，是君欲害臣、臣欲避君，如张良托辟谷弃职从赤松子游）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙是臣。子孙逢空发动，或者第四爻（臣位）落空而动、又被世爻克害，是国君想害臣子、臣子想躲开国君，就像张良借口辟谷、辞去官职，跟随赤松子出游。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the minister. If Output meets Void and moves, or the fourth line, the ministers' seat, is Void and moves and is also controlled and harmed by the Self, the ruler wants to harm the minister and the minister wants to avoid the ruler, like Zhang Liang, who pleaded fasting from grain, gave up office and went roaming with Chi Songzi.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5843 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子发逢空，张子房起归山之计； | 子孙发动逢到空，就像张子房起了退隐归山的打算； | If Output moves and meets Void, it is like Zhang Zifang forming the plan to retire to the hills; |

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> | 子孙为臣。 | 子孙是臣子。 | Output is the minister. |

> | 如汉张良，托为辟谷，弃职从赤松子游也。 | 就像汉朝的张良，假托辟谷不食，放弃官职跟随赤松子出游。 | It is like Zhang Liang of Han, who gave fasting from grain as his pretext, abandoned his post and went roaming with Chi Songzi. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1480 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 他宫子孙为臣，若逢空动被世克害，必是君欲害臣，如汉张良弃职从赤松子游也。 | 别的宫的子孙是臣子，如果逢空发动、被世克害，必定是国君想害臣子，就像汉朝张良弃官跟随赤松子出游。 | The Output of another palace is the minister; if it meets Void and moves and is controlled and harmed by the Self, the ruler surely wants to harm the minister, like Zhang Liang of Han who gave up office and went roaming with Chi Songzi. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 子孙有两种：本宫的子孙是太子（见「日辰拱扶子位」一联），别的宫的子孙是臣。卜筮正宗在这一联明说「他宫子孙为臣」；卜筮大全旧注这里只写「子孙为臣」，到「日辰拱扶子位」才分他宫与本宫。
>
> *Note:* Output comes in two kinds: the Output of the home palace is the crown prince (see the couplet 'the Day supports Output's place'), and the Output of another palace is the minister. The Bu Shi Zheng Zong says so outright here, 'the Output of another palace is the minister'; the old commentary in the Bu Shi Da Quan writes only 'Output is the minister' here and draws the line between another palace and the home palace at 'the Day supports Output's place'.
