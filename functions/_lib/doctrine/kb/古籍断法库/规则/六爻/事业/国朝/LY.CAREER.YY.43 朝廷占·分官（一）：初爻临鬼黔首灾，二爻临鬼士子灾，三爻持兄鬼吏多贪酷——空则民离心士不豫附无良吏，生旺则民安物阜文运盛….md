# 朝廷占·分官（一）：初爻临鬼黔首灾，二爻临鬼士子灾，三爻持兄鬼吏多贪酷——空则民离心/士不豫附/无良吏，生旺则民安物阜/文运盛/善政超迁，衰而受伤则民遭涂炭/士屈抑/贬秩褫职

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 分官（一），按爻位看百姓、士人和官吏：初爻临官鬼，百姓有灾；落空，百姓有离心，生旺，百姓安居物产丰盛，衰弱受伤，百姓遭涂炭。二爻临官鬼，读书人有灾；落空，读书人不乐意归附；金爻落空而发动，读书人多乱发议论；生旺又加文昌，文运昌盛；衰弱受伤，读书人多受压抑。三爻持兄爻、官鬼，官吏多贪婪酷虐；落空，没有好官吏；生旺，带青龙、喜神、贵人、驿马、进神而发动的，多有善政传到上面，官员晋级、越级升迁；衰弱受伤，再加白虎、螣蛇、刃、劫杀、退神而发动的，多有贪污失德，被降级革职。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Dividing the offices (1), reading the people, scholars and officials by line. When Pressure sits on the first line, the common people suffer; if it is Void the people lose heart, if thriving and born the people are at peace and goods abundant, if weak and injured the people fall into misery. When Pressure sits on the second line, the scholars suffer; if Void the scholars are unwilling to attach themselves; if a Metal line is Void and moves the scholars voice many wild opinions; if thriving and with Wenchang added, literary fortune flourishes; if weak and injured the scholars are often suppressed. When the third line holds a Peer line or Pressure, the officials are often greedy and cruel; if Void there are no good officials; if thriving and carrying the Dragon, Joy, the Noble, the Horse and an Advancing spirit and moving, there is much good government reported upward and officials are advanced and promoted out of turn; if weak and injured, with the Tiger, the Coiling Snake, the blade, Robbery and a Retreating spirit added and moving, there is much corruption and officials are demoted and dismissed.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「黔首」是百姓；「涂炭」是陷在泥和炭火里，比喻极苦的境地；「豫附」的「豫」是乐意；「横议」是肆意议论；「簠簋不饰」是官员贪污失德（簠簋是祭祀盛黍稷的器具，不修饰指官箴败坏）；「褫职」是革职。文昌是一颗主文运的神煞。「金空动」书里没有说「金」是金爻还是金宫，译文按金爻译。
>
> *Note:* 'Qianshou' is the common people; 'tutan' is being in mud and live coals, an image of utter misery; the 'yu' of 'yufu' means willing; 'hengyi' is voicing opinions recklessly; 'fugui bu shi' is an official's corruption and loss of virtue (the fugui are the vessels holding grain at sacrifices, and not adorning them points to the ruin of official conduct); 'chizhi' is dismissal from office. Wenchang is a spirit-star of literary fortune. The book does not say whether the 'Metal' in 'Metal Void moves' is a Metal line or a Metal palace, and the translation takes it as a Metal line.
