# 收雇·游魂归魂与世间：游魂用空有逃亡之变、归魂用破有回赎之嫌；二父二官事系两头、三刑三破身有四体之灾（三破谓岁月日之破）；世空我疑、间动防阻

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 游魂卦而用爻空，就有逃亡的变故；归魂卦而用爻破，就有被回赎（被人赎回去）的嫌疑。卦里有两个父、两个官，事情可能两头牵系；三刑、三破，自身常有四体的灾。世空，是我自己疑惑；间爻动，要防阻隔。三破，说的是岁破、月破、日破。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With a Wandering Soul hexagram and the Subject line Void, there is a change of flight and absconding; with a Returning Soul hexagram and the Subject line broken, there is the suspicion of being redeemed and taken back. If the hexagram has two Resource lines and two Pressure lines, the matter may be tied at both ends; with three punishments or three breaks the person often has a calamity of the limbs. The Self Void means I am in doubt; a between line moving means guard against obstruction. The 'three breaks' are the break of the year, of the month and of the day.

> [!note] 按
> 「游魂」主迁动、「归魂」主回返：游魂卦而用爻空，这个人容易走掉（逃亡）；归魂卦而用爻破，容易被他家里赎回去。「回赎」是（被他的家人）赎回。「二父二官」是卦里有两个父母、两个官鬼。「两头之系」是事情牵连两头。「三刑」是刑，「三破」书里自注：岁破、月破、日破。「四体之灾」是身体的灾。「间动」是间爻动，主阻隔。这一条是雇工、仆人会不会逃、会不会被赎、会不会牵连的预兆。
>
> *Note:* 'Wandering Soul' governs shifting and 'Returning Soul' going back: with a Wandering Soul hexagram and a Void Subject line the man easily slips away (absconds); with a Returning Soul hexagram and a broken Subject line he is easily redeemed and taken back by his family. 'Huishu' is being redeemed (by his family). 'Two Resource and two Pressure lines' is two Resource lines and two Pressure lines in the hexagram. 'Tied at both ends' is the matter involving both sides. 'Three punishments' is punishment, and for the 'three breaks' the book's own gloss is the break of the year, the month and the day. 'A calamity of the limbs' is a bodily calamity. 'A between line moving' is a between line moving, which means obstruction. This entry is the sign of whether a hired hand or servant will flee, be redeemed or be drawn into trouble.
