# 官禄占·官职退复（二）：官遇三传生扶必起复；世下伏官伏印马必官印临值之年月日起官；官父持身世旺静必冲动官印之年月日起官；旁爻带贵马旺动生官印得贵人提拔；旁爻兄弟带亡劫隔神刑冲官印必有匪人相隔

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 起复，重新起用：官星遇到三传（太岁、月建、日辰）生扶的，必定起复；世爻下伏着官星，或伏着印星、驿马的，必定在官星、印星当值的年月日起复；官星、印星持身爻、世爻，旺相安静的，必定在冲动官星、印星的年月日起复；旁边的爻带贵人、驿马，旺相发动，来生身爻、世爻上的官星、印星的，必得贵人提拔，在相生的年月日起复；旁边的兄弟爻旺相发动，带亡神、劫杀、隔神来刑、冲、克、害身爻、世爻上的官星、印星的，必有小人从中阻隔、坏事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Being recalled to office. If the star of office meets generation and support from the three rulers, he is surely recalled; if the star of office lies hidden under the Self, or the seal and the Horse lie hidden, he is recalled in the year, month or day when the star of office and seal rule; if the star of office and seal hold the Body and Self, thriving and still, he is recalled in the year, month or day that clashes them into motion; if a side line carrying the Noble and the Horse is thriving and moving and generates the star of office and seal on the Body and Self, he gets a patron's promotion and is recalled in the year, month or day of generation; if a side Peer line is thriving and moving, carrying the Lost Spirit, Robbery and the obstructing spirit, and punishes, clashes, controls or harms the star of office and seal on the Body and Self, there will surely be bad men who stand between and spoil the matter.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2677 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 是月是日有占起官者，得剥之晋。 | 这个月这一天有人占起复做官，得剥卦变晋卦。 | In that month and on that day someone divined on being recalled to office and got Bo changing into Jin. |
> | 世临子水，伏申金，金生水为印绶。 | 世爻临子水，伏着申金，金生水，是印绶。 | The Self sits on Zi Water, with Shen Metal hidden, and Metal generates Water, so it is the seal and ribbon. |
> | 又寅年午月戌日，马聚于申，世下伏印马，后必起官。 | 又是寅年、午月、戌日，驿马聚在申，世爻下面伏着印星和驿马，后来必定起复做官。 | It is also a Yin year, Wu month and Xu day, and the Horse gathers at Shen, and the seal and the Horse lie hidden under the Self, so he will surely be recalled to office later. |
> | 但嫌本宫午官伏戌墓下，须待壬辰年冲开戌墓，透出午官，又辰年与世爻飞伏申子三合，故其年上寅月，可用财求谋，官生在寅也。 | 但是嫌本宫的午火官星伏在戌墓下面，必须等到壬辰年冲开戌墓，透出午火官星；而且辰年与世爻的飞神子水、伏神申金三合，所以那一年到寅月，可以用钱财去谋求，官星生在寅。 | But what is disliked is that the Wu Fire star of office of the home palace lies hidden under the Xu tomb; he must wait for the Renchen year, when the Xu tomb is clashed open and the Wu Fire star of office comes out; moreover the Chen year forms a three-harmony with the flying Zi Water and the hidden Shen Metal of the Self line, so in that year by the Yin month he can use money to seek it, since the star of office is born in Yin. |
> | 丙午月得官，官旺于午也。 | 丙午月得到官职，官星在午旺。 | He gets the office in the Bingwu month, since the star of office is thriving in Wu. |
> | 近则未月到任，远则戌月到任也。 | 近的话是未月到任，远的话是戌月到任。 | Nearer, he takes up his post in the Wei month; further off, in the Xu month. |
> | 土为印绶，未与午官六合，戌与午官三合也。 | 土是印绶，未与午官星六合，戌与午官星三合。 | Earth is the seal and ribbon, and Wei forms a six-combination with the Wu star of office, and Xu forms a three-harmony with it. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 附断例是《易隐》官禄占末尾的一例，放在最相关的这一条下；书里没有说占的是哪一年的什么日子，只说「是月是日」，接的是前一例庚寅年壬午月甲戌日。「隔神」是神煞的一种，书里没有解释。第四、五句里「相生之年月日」承上一句，是旁边的贵人、驿马生到官星、印星的年月日。
>
> *Note:* The example is one of those appended at the end of this chapter of the Yi Yin, and is placed under the entry it illustrates most; the book does not give the year or day of the divination, saying only 'that month, that day', following on the previous example of the Gengyin year, Renwu month, Jiaxu day. 'Geshen' is a kind of spirit-star that the book does not explain. In the fourth and fifth sentences 'the year, month or day of generation' follows on from the previous sentence, and is the year, month or day in which the side Noble and Post Horse generate the star of office and seal.
