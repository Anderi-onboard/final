# 六亲章·玄武当官奸与盗、两重官鬼必忧丧；若占官府反为吉，福厚官高名誉扬

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 玄武落在官鬼上，是奸和盗；两重官鬼，必有忧愁丧事；但如果占的是官府（的事），反而是吉：福气厚、官位高、名誉远扬。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Dark Tortoise on Pressure means treachery and theft, and two layers of Pressure bring worry and mourning; but if one divines about officialdom it is on the contrary auspicious: blessing thick, office high and fame spread.

## 《周易尚占·六亲章》原文

> | 玄武当官奸与盗， | 玄武当了官（落在官鬼上），是奸和盗； | the Dark Tortoise on Pressure means treachery and theft; |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句在「六亲章」里，前后讲的是家人、婚姻、讼事；「占官府」在这里指占做官、官场的事，不是占官司。
>
> *Note:* This line stands in the chapter on the six relations among lines about family, marriage and lawsuits; 'divining about officialdom' here means divining a career in office and not a lawsuit.
