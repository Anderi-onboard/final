# 官员荐奖：臣沐君恩必得岁君生世；欲求恩宠须看五爻为天子、太岁为朝廷，此二象内若得一爻生世或生官便沾天泽，如值空亡恩先莫望从，不值空如来克世伤官原不为福

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 臣子蒙受君王的恩典，必须得到岁君（太岁）来生世爻。要求恩宠，要看五爻是天子、太岁是朝廷：这两个象里如果有一个来生世爻或者生官爻，就沾到天子和朝廷所给的恩泽；如果落在空亡，就不要指望得到恩宠；不落空而来克世爻、伤克官爻的，本来就不是福。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A minister who receives the sovereign's favour must have Tai Sui, the sovereign of the year, generating the Self. To seek favour, look at the fifth line as the Son of Heaven and Tai Sui as the court: if either of these two images generates the Self or generates the star of office, one shares in the grace given by the Son of Heaven and the court; if it falls on Void, do not hope for favour; if it is not Void but comes to control the Self or to hurt the star of office, it was never a blessing.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 817 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 欲求恩宠，须看五爻为天子，太岁为朝廷，此二象内若得一爻生世或生官，便沾天泽天子与朝廷之位。 | 想求得恩宠，要看五爻是天子、太岁是朝廷；这两个象里如果有一个来生世爻或者生官爻，就沾到天子、朝廷的位置所给的恩泽。 | To seek favour, one must look at the fifth line as the Son of Heaven and Tai Sui as the court; if either of these two images generates the Self or generates the star of office, one shares in the heavenly grace of the positions of the Son of Heaven and the court. |
> | 如值空亡，恩先莫望从，不值空如来克世伤官，原不为福。 | 如果落在空亡，就不要指望得到恩宠；不落空而来克世爻、伤克官爻的，本来就不是福。 | If it falls on Void, do not hope for favour; if it is not Void but comes to control the Self or hurt the star of office, it was never a blessing. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「五爻为天子」是第五爻代表君王，「太岁为朝廷」是太岁代表朝廷，与黄金策国朝「九五逢阳，当遇仁明之主」（HJC.65）、「太岁为君」（HJC.64）同取。「岁君」就是太岁。「天泽」是上天的恩泽，这里指天子的恩典。「恩先莫望从」文字疑有误，按文意译作「不要指望得到恩宠」。
>
> *Note:* 'The fifth line is the Son of Heaven' is the fifth line standing for the sovereign, and 'Tai Sui is the court' is Tai Sui standing for the court, taken as in 'the ninth-fifth meeting yang, one meets a benevolent and enlightened sovereign' (HJC.65) and 'Tai Sui is the sovereign' (HJC.64) in the Huangjin Ce on the state. 'Suijun' is Tai Sui. 'Tianze' is the bounty of heaven and here means the favour of the Son of Heaven. 'En xian mo wang cong' looks corrupt in its characters and is translated by the sense as 'do not hope for favour'.
