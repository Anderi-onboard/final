# 卜馆·设帐、附学、代席、辞馆：设帐应空则负笈无徒、财虚则馆谷不盛；问附来者同设帐、问代席者同成馆；辞馆以应为主人，应克世则主不从虽六冲不能解、应空散破绝则不遂虽游魂不可辞，惟应实及不克世者可以去

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 设帐（开馆）会不会有人来跟着学：应空，就没有背着书箱来的学徒；财虚，馆谷（教书的酬金）就不丰盛。问学生来附读的，和设帐一样断；问代人坐席的，和成馆一样断。占辞馆，以应为主人：应克世，主人不依从；应空、散、破、绝，事情做不成；主人不依从，即使六冲也解不开；事情做不成，即使游魂又怎么辞得掉？只有应爻实（不空）、又不克世的，才可以离开。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking whether, on opening a school, anyone will come to study: the Other Void, and no pupils come carrying their book-chests; Wealth empty, and the stipend for the post is not rich. In asking about pupils who come to attach themselves, judge as for opening a school; in asking about standing in for another, judge as for a post that comes off. In divining about resigning from a post, the Other is the master: the Other controlling the Self, and the master does not comply; the Other Void, scattered, broken or extinct, and it does not go through; when the master does not comply, even a six-clash cannot dissolve it; when it does not go through, even Wandering Soul, how can one resign? Only when the Other is solid and does not control the Self may one leave.

> [!note] 按
> 「设帐」是开馆教书，「负笈」是背着书箱（来求学），「馆榖」就是馆谷，指教书的酬金口粮。「附来者」是带着孩子来附在这个馆里读书的人。「代席」是替人坐馆。「主不遂」的「遂」是事情顺遂成就，这里指辞馆这件事做不成。辞馆本来是『世想走』，看的是东家肯不肯放：应克世是东家不依，应空散破绝是这件事落不了地，这两种情形即使卦是六冲（该散）、游魂（该迁），也走不成。「六冲」在别处主散，这里是反说：再散的卦，主人不依也解不开。
>
> *Note:* 'Shezhang' is opening a school to teach, 'fuji' is carrying a book-chest (to come to study), and 'guangu' is the stipend of grain for the teacher. 'Those who come to attach' are those who bring a child to read at this school. 'Daixi' is sitting in for another at his post. The 'sui' of 'the master does not sui' is a matter going through to success, here the resignation not getting done. Resigning is the Self wishing to leave, and what is looked at is whether the master will let go: the Other controlling the Self is the master not complying, and the Other Void, scattered, broken or extinct is the matter failing to land, so even in a six-clash (which should break up) or Wandering Soul (which should move on) one cannot get away. 'Six-clash' elsewhere means dispersal, and here it is put the other way: however scattering the hexagram, if the master does not comply it cannot be undone.
