# 诸爻持世诀·事业句：官动财旺宜赴试，子孙持世求名切忌坐当头，鬼爻持世功名最喜世当权

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动财爻旺相，适宜赴考；子孙持世，求名切忌；鬼爻持世，功名最喜欢世爻当权。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure moving and Wealth strong, it is fit to go to the examination; with Output holding the Self, seeking a name is strictly to be avoided; with Pressure holding the Self, for office the Self in power is most welcome.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这三句分属三联：父母持世、子孙持世、鬼爻持世；每联开头的持世爻没有拆进引文里。「官动」的「官」是官鬼爻，「坐当头」的主语是子孙。
>
> *Note:* The three lines belong to three couplets: Resource holding the Self, Output holding the Self, and Pressure holding the Self; the line named at the start of each couplet is not split into the quotation. In "Pressure moving" the first word is the Pressure line, and the subject of "seated at the head" is Output.
