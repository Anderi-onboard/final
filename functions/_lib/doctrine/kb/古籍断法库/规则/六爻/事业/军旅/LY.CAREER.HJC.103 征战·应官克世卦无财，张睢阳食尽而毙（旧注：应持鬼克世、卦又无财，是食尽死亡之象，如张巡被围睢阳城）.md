# 征战·应官克世卦无财，张睢阳食尽而毙（旧注：应持鬼克世、卦又无财，是食尽死亡之象，如张巡被围睢阳城）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻持着官鬼来克世爻，卦里又没有妻财，是粮食吃尽、困死的象，就像张巡被围在睢阳城。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Other holds Pressure and controls the Self, and there is no Wealth in the hexagram, it is the image of running out of food and dying, like Zhang Xun besieged in Suiyang.

## 《黄金策·征战》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5955 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Campaigns and war", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应官克世卦无财，张睢阳食尽而毙。 | 应爻的官鬼克世爻，卦里又没有财，就像张睢阳粮食吃尽而死。 | If the Other's Pressure line controls the Self and the hexagram has no Wealth, it is like Zhang of Suiyang who ran out of food and died. |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1546 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应爻持鬼冲世，卦中无财，乃食尽死亡之象，如张巡被围睢阳城也。 | 应爻持着官鬼冲世爻，卦中没有妻财，是粮食吃尽、困死的象，就像张巡被围在睢阳城。 | If the Other holds Pressure and clashes with the Self, and there is no Wealth in the hexagram, it is the image of running out of food and dying, like Zhang Xun besieged in Suiyang. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说应爻鬼「克世」，卜筮正宗说「冲世」。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says the Other's Pressure line 'controls the Self'; the Bu Shi Zheng Zong says 'clashes with the Self'.
