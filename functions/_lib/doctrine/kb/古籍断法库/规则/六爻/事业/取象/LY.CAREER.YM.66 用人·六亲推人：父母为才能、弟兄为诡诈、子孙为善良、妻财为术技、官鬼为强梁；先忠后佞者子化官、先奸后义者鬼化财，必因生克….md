# 用人·六亲推人：父母为才能、弟兄为诡诈、子孙为善良、妻财为术技、官鬼为强梁；先忠后佞者子化官、先奸后义者鬼化财，必因生克而后言吉凶

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六亲推一个人的品性：父母是才能，弟兄是诡诈，子孙是善良，妻财是术技，官鬼是强梁。先忠后佞的，是子化官；先奸后义的，是鬼化财。必须依据生克，之后才说吉凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The six relations judge a man's character: Resource is talent, Peer is trickery, Output is goodness, Wealth is skill and craft, and Pressure is the bully. One who is first loyal and then flattering is Output turning into Pressure; one who is first treacherous and then righteous is Pressure turning into Wealth. One must go by generation and control before speaking of good or ill.

> [!note] 按
> 「才能」「诡诈」「善良」「术技」「强梁」是六亲配给人的品性：父母主才能，弟兄（兄弟）主诡诈，子孙主善良，妻财主术技（手艺），官鬼主强梁（强横不驯）。「先忠后佞者，子化官」：子孙（善良）变成官鬼（强梁），先忠后佞；「先奸后义者，鬼化财」：官鬼变成妻财，先奸后义。书里自注：必须依据生克才说吉凶——也就是六亲配的品性，还要看它生世还是克世才定好坏。
>
> *Note:* 'Talent', 'trickery', 'goodness', 'skill' and 'the bully' are the characters the six relations are assigned: Resource governs talent, Peer trickery, Output goodness, Wealth skill and craft, and Pressure the bully (forceful and unruly). 'First loyal then flattering is Output turning into Pressure': Output (goodness) turns into Pressure (the bully), first loyal and then flattering; 'first treacherous then righteous is Pressure turning into Wealth': Pressure turns into Wealth, first treacherous and then righteous. The book's own gloss: one must go by generation and control before speaking of good or ill, that is, the character assigned to a relation must also be looked at by whether it generates or controls the Self before good or bad is fixed.
