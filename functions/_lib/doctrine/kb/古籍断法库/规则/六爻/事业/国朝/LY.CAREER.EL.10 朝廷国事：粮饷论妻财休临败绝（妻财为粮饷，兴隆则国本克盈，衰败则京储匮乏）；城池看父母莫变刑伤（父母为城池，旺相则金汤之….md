# 朝廷国事：粮饷论妻财休临败绝（妻财为粮饷，兴隆则国本克盈，衰败则京储匮乏）；城池看父母莫变刑伤（父母为城池，旺相则金汤之固，刑伤则有残缺之虞）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 粮饷看妻财，不要临败、绝：妻财是粮饷，兴旺则国家的根本充盈，衰败则京城的储备匮乏。城池看父母，不要变出刑伤：父母是城池，旺相则城池固若金汤，遇刑伤就有残缺的忧患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Provisions are judged by Wealth, which must not sit on spent or extinct: Wealth is the grain and pay, and if it flourishes the root of the state is full, and if it is spent the capital's stores run short. The city walls are judged by Resource, which must not change into punishment or injury: Resource is the walls and moats, and if it is thriving they are as firm as walls of metal and moats of boiling water, and if punished or injured there is the worry of breaches.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 312 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母为城池旺相则有金汤之固，刑伤宁无残缺之虞。 | 父母是城池，旺相则城池固若金汤，遇到刑伤，岂能没有残缺的忧患。 | Resource is the walls and moats; if it is thriving they are as firm as walls of metal and moats of boiling water, and if it meets punishment or injury, how can there not be the worry of breaches. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「金汤」是金属的城、滚水的护城河，比喻极其坚固。这两联和易隐朝廷占的城池（YY.40）、帑藏（YY.41）取法相同：父母为城池，妻财为国库粮饷。
>
> *Note:* 'Jintang' are walls of metal and a moat of boiling water, an image of extreme firmness. These two couplets take the same view as the walls (YY.40) and treasury (YY.41) in the Yi Yin court divination: Resource is the city walls, and Wealth the state treasury and provisions.
