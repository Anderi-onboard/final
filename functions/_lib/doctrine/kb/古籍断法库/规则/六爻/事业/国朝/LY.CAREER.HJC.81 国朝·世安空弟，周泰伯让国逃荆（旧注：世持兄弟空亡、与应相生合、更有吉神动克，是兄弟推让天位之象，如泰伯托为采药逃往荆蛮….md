# 国朝·世安空弟，周泰伯让国逃荆（旧注：世持兄弟空亡、与应相生合、更有吉神动克，是兄弟推让天位之象，如泰伯托为采药逃往荆蛮、让位季历）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻持着落空亡的兄弟，和应爻相生相合，又有吉神发动来克，是兄弟之间互相推让天子之位的象，就像泰伯假托采药逃到荆蛮，把位置让给季历。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self holds a Void Peer line, generating or combining with the Other, and an auspicious spirit also moves to control, it is the image of brothers yielding the throne to one another, like Taibo, who on the pretext of gathering herbs fled to the Jing barbarians and gave the succession to Jili.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5873 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世安空弟，周泰伯让国逃荆。 | 世爻安在落空的兄弟上，就像周朝的泰伯让国、逃到荆地。 | If the Self sits on a Void brother, it is like Taibo of Zhou who yielded the state and fled to Jing. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5874 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持兄弟空亡，与应相生合，更有吉神动克，必是兄弟推让天位之象。 | 世爻持着兄弟且落空亡，和应爻相生相合，又有吉神发动来克，必定是兄弟互相推让天子之位的象。 | If the Self holds the Peer line and it is Void, generating or combining with the Other, and an auspicious spirit also moves to control, it is surely the image of brothers yielding the throne to one another. |
> | 如周泰伯托为采药，逃之荆蛮，让位季历也。 | 就像周朝的泰伯假托去采药，逃到荆蛮之地，把位置让给季历。 | It is like Taibo of Zhou, who on the pretext of gathering herbs fled to the Jing and Man peoples and yielded the throne to Jili. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1498 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持空弟，与应爻合，有吉神动克，是兄弟推让天位之象，如周泰伯托为采药逃之荆，蛮让位季历也。 | 世爻持着落空的兄弟，和应爻相合，有吉神发动来克，是兄弟互相推让天子之位的象，就像周朝的泰伯假托去采药，逃到荆蛮之地，把位置让给季历。 | If the Self holds a Void brother and combines with the Other, and an auspicious spirit moves to control, it is the image of brothers yielding the throne, like Taibo of Zhou who on the pretext of gathering herbs fled to the Jing and Man peoples and yielded the throne to Jili. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗原文「逃之荆，蛮让位季历」中间的逗号是标点之误，应连读为「荆蛮」，译文按此读。
>
> *Note:* In the Bu Shi Zheng Zong the comma in 'fled to Jing, Man yielded the place to Jili' is a punctuation slip; 'Jing and Man' is to be read together, and the translation reads it so.
