# 出师征伐：安营立寨推八卦之显幽，监阵交锋阐各爻之生克；行兵出战先择地安营（乾宫利西北、巽卦利东南），六爻之内有彼生我我生彼我克彼彼克我，须详彼此吉凶以决输赢

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 安营立寨，要推八卦的显与幽；监阵交锋，要阐明各爻的生克。行兵出战，先要选地安营，易卦中自有九宫八卦：乾宫利于西北之地，巽卦利于东南的方位，其余照此推。各爻，是一卦上下的六爻，却不知道还有变出的六爻、有伏出的六爻、又有互卦起作用的爻；六爻之内，有彼生我、我生彼，有我克彼、彼克我，必须详察彼此的吉凶，来判定输赢的道理。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In pitching camp and setting up stockades, work out the manifest and hidden of the eight trigrams; in supervising the formation and engaging, make clear the generation and control of each line. In moving troops to battle, first choose the ground for the camp, and in the hexagram there are of itself the nine palaces and eight trigrams: the Qian palace favours the northwest and Xun the southeast, and the rest follow this. The lines are the six lines above and below in a hexagram, but one does not know that there are also the six lines of the changed hexagram, the six hidden lines and the lines acting in the nuclear hexagram; among the six lines, there is them generating me and me generating them, me controlling them and them controlling me, and one must examine the good and ill of both sides to decide the principle of winning and losing.

> [!note] 按
> 「显幽」是明显与隐伏（本卦与伏、变等），「互卦」是由第二、三、四爻和第三、四、五爻合成的卦。「九宫八卦」指八个卦各配一个方位（乾西北、巽东南等）。
>
> *Note:* 'Xian you' is manifest and hidden (the original hexagram against the hidden and changed lines and so on); the 'nuclear hexagram' is the hexagram formed from the second, third and fourth lines and the third, fourth and fifth lines. 'The nine palaces and eight trigrams' means that each of the eight trigrams is matched to a direction (Qian northwest, Xun southeast and so on).
