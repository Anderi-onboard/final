# 恩封诰命：鬼发父兴迎诰命、父空财动绝皇恩，官鬼休囚宜嘱托、文书破绝莫劳心，来召来宣同此例、去朝去奏照其因；卦内官父两旺定沐洪恩，父若空亡难迎诰命，妻财独发克制文书难沾雨露，鬼值休囚夤缘为美父临空绝枉使机谋，君来宣召臣去朝天皆喜君爻生世，官旺为佳各忌帝位克身鬼空不吉

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动、父爻兴旺，就迎来诰命；父爻落空、财爻发动，就断绝皇恩；官鬼休囚，适宜托人嘱托；文书（父母）被破、被绝，就不要费心。被召、被宣同这个取法；去朝见、去上奏，按它的缘由来断。卦里官爻、父爻都旺，一定沐浴洪恩；父爻如果空亡，难迎诰命；妻财独自发动、克制文书，难沾雨露；官鬼休囚，托人走门路为好；父爻临空、绝，白费机谋。君王来宣召、臣子去朝见，都喜欢君爻生世爻；官爻旺相为好，各自都忌帝位克制身爻，官鬼落空不吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure moves and Resource flourishes, one welcomes the patent; if Resource is Void and Wealth moves, the sovereign's favour is cut off; if Pressure is resting or imprisoned it is fitting to entrust the matter to someone; if the document is broken or extinct, do not trouble your heart; being summoned or called follows this same rule, and going to court or memorialising follows its causes. If in the hexagram the star of office and Resource are both thriving, one will surely bathe in great favour; if Resource is Void, it is hard to receive the patent; if Wealth moves alone and controls the document, it is hard to share in the rain and dew of favour; if Pressure is resting or imprisoned, going through connections is best; if Resource is Void or extinct, scheming is in vain. Whether the sovereign summons or the minister goes to court, in all cases it is welcome for the sovereign's line to generate the Self; a thriving star of office is good, in each case the throne's position controlling the Body is feared, and Pressure Void is ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 「寅绿」疑是「夤缘」（攀附门路、通过关系），与诀里「官鬼休囚宜嘱托」是同一个意思，译作「托人走门路」。「迎诰命」是迎接诰命，「宣召」是君王召见，「洪恩」是大恩。「帝位克身」是五爻（君位）克制身爻。诀里「来召来宣同此例，去朝去奏照其因」是说召见、朝奏两类事，取法与诰命相同。
>
> *Note:* The 'yin lü' of 'yin lü wei mei' is presumably 'yinyuan', climbing by connections or working through relationships, which is the same sense as the verse's 'if Pressure is resting or imprisoned, it is fitting to entrust the matter to someone', and it is translated 'going through connections'. 'Ying gaoming' is receiving the patent, 'xuanzhao' is the sovereign summoning one, and 'hong en' is great favour. 'Diwei ke shen' is the fifth line (the sovereign's position) controlling the Body. The verse's 'being summoned or called follows this same rule, and going to court or memorialising follows its causes' says that being summoned and going to court or memorialise are two kinds of matter that take the same method as the patent.
