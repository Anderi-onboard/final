# 上书·岁五生身防受制，六爻恍惚且休行（太岁生世必须在卦爻里出现才算；五爻生世为吉也防受克空衰破绝，有生名无生实；书中例：中孚之损，巳火生世而受克，此书宜止，后果不准行；野鹤：有益于国之事不必占，可此可彼之事须世与父爻无伤、岁五日月相生才可行）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁生世必须在卦爻里出现才算数，不在卦爻中就生不了世；五爻生世当作吉的，也要防它受克、落空、衰弱、被破、入绝，有生的名义没有生的实际；六爻恍惚不定就不要上书。野鹤说：真有益于国的事，舍身为国也不辞，不必占卜；事情可做可不做之间的，必须世爻与父爻都没有刑冲克破，而且太岁、五爻、日月相生，才可以做；爻中乱动、用神元神非旺非衰、克多生少的，就应当停止。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For Tai Sui to generate the Self it must appear among the lines, and if it is not among the lines it cannot generate the Self; taking the fifth line generating the Self as auspicious, one must also guard against its being controlled, Void, weak, broken or extinct, which would give the name of generation and not the substance; if the six lines are vague and unsettled, do not submit the memorial. The Old Man of Yehe says: if the matter truly benefits the state one gives oneself for the country and refuses nothing, and there is no need of divination; for a matter that could go either way, it may be done only if the Self and the Resource line are free of punishment, clash, control and break and Tai Sui, the fifth line, the Day and the Month generate them; if the lines move in disorder, the Subject and Support are neither thriving nor weak, or controls outnumber generations, it should be stopped.

> | 断曰：五位巳火生世，吉矣！ | 断语说：五爻的巳火生世爻，是吉的！ | The judgment said: Si Fire in the fifth place generates the Self, which is auspicious! |
> | 而不宜巳火受克，此书宜止。 | 但是巳火不宜受克，这个上书应当停止。 | But Si Fire should not be controlled, and this memorial should be stopped. |
> | 问曰：有害否？ | 问：有没有害处？ | He asked: will there be harm? |
> | 余曰：巳火虽则不能生世，而卦中幸无克世之爻，利与害皆无也。 | 我说：巳火虽然不能生世，但卦中幸好没有克世的爻，利和害都没有。 | I said: though Si Fire cannot generate the Self, fortunately there is no line in the hexagram that controls the Self, so there is neither gain nor harm. |
> | 后此人上书，惟不准行而已。 | 后来这个人上书，只是没有被批准施行罢了。 | Afterwards this man submitted the memorial, and it was merely not approved. |
