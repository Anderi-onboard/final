# 仕宦·鬼杀伤身，因见灾殃之不免（旧注：官鬼发动生合世爻是用神、伤克世爻是鬼杀；以化出六亲断：化子贬谪、化财阴人之祸、化兄失财、化父忧小口；不然自身有灾病，世空避则无妨）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动，生合世爻的是用神，伤克世爻的是鬼煞。用神扶助世爻，必有进取；鬼煞伤到世身，必有凶祸。按它化出的六亲来断：化出子孙有被贬谪的忧虑，化出财有女人带来的祸患，化出兄弟主失财，化出父母要忧虑小孩；如果都不是这样，自己一定有灾病，得世爻空亡躲开才不妨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Pressure moves, if it generates or combines with the Self it is the Subject, and if it harms or controls the Self it is a killer. When the Subject supports the Self there will surely be advancement; when a killer harms the Self and the Body there will surely be misfortune. Judge by the relation it turns into: into Output, the worry of being demoted and exiled; into Wealth, the calamity of a woman; into Peer, loss of wealth; into Resource, worry for the children; and if it is none of these, one will surely have calamity or illness oneself, and it is harmless only if the Self is Void and so avoids it.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1952 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼动来生合世者为用神，如动来克伤世爻者为鬼煞。 | 官鬼动来生合世爻的是用神，如果动来克伤世爻的就是鬼煞。 | If Pressure moves to generate or combine with the Self it is the Subject, and if it moves to injure or control the Self it is a killer. |
> | 生扶合世，必有进取之兆，刑冲克世，必有凶祸。 | 生扶合世，必有进取的预兆，刑冲克世，必有凶祸。 | If it generates, supports or combines with the Self, there will surely be a sign of advancement, and if it punishes, clashes or controls the Self, there will surely be misfortune. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联把官鬼分成两种：生合世爻的是「用神」（帮你的官），伤克世爻的是「鬼煞」（害你的鬼）。「阴人之祸」是女人带来的祸患，「小口」是小孩。旧注多出的「按化出的六亲断」「自身决有灾病」，王洪绪没有写。「得世爻空避不妨」是说世爻空亡时，鬼煞伤不到它，所以无妨；这和 LY.CAREER.HJC.09「身空无救」不矛盾：那里是世爻「无故自空」而且没有救，指的是命数，这里是用空亡躲避鬼煞。
>
> *Note:* This couplet divides Pressure into two kinds: the one that generates or combines with the Self is the 'Subject' (the official who helps you), and the one that injures or controls the Self is the 'killer' (the ghost that harms you). 'The calamity of a woman' is trouble brought by a woman, and 'the children' are the little ones. The old commentary has the additional 'judge by the relation it turns into' and 'one will surely have calamity or illness oneself', which Wang Hongxu does not write. 'Harmless if the Self is Void and so avoids it' says that when the Self is Void the killer cannot injure it, so it is harmless; this does not contradict LY.CAREER.HJC.09 ('the Body Void with no rescue'), where the Self falls Void by itself without cause and without rescue, which is about one's fate, whereas here Void is used to evade a killer.
