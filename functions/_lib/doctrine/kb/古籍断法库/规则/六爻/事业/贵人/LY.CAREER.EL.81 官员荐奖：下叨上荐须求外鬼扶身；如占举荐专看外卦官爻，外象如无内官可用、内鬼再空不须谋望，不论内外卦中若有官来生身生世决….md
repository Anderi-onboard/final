# 官员荐奖：下叨上荐须求外鬼扶身；如占举荐专看外卦官爻，外象如无内官可用、内鬼再空不须谋望，不论内外卦中若有官来生身生世决叨提拔之恩

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 下级要承蒙上级的荐举，要求外卦的官鬼来扶持卦身。占荐举，专看外卦的官爻；外卦没有，内卦的官爻也可以用；内卦的官鬼再落空，就不必谋求指望；不论内卦外卦，卦中如果有官爻来生卦身、生世爻，一定承蒙提拔的恩典。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A subordinate who is to receive a recommendation from above must seek the outer Pressure line to support the Body. In divining for a recommendation, look only at the Pressure line in the outer trigram; if the outer trigram has none, the inner star of office can be used; if the inner Pressure too is Void, there is no need to scheme and hope; whether inner or outer, if the hexagram has a star of office that generates the Body or the Self, one will surely receive the grace of being raised.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「叨」是承受（多用作谦辞）。「外鬼」是外卦的官鬼。这一条与易隐官禄占「官贵旺动生合世目下蒙荐」（YY.17）、黄金策求馆「鬼动合身，须得贵人推荐」（HJC.36）取同一个关系：官鬼来生合世身，有人荐举。
>
> *Note:* 'Tao' is receiving (mostly used as a modest word). 'Waigui' is the Pressure line of the outer trigram. This entry takes the same relation as 'if the star of office and the Noble are thriving and moving and generate or combine with the Self, one is recommended at once' in the Yi Yin divination on official salary (YY.17) and 'if Pressure moves and combines with the Body, one must have a noble recommend one' in the Huangjin Ce on seeking a school post (HJC.36): Pressure generating or combining with the Self or Body means that someone recommends one.
