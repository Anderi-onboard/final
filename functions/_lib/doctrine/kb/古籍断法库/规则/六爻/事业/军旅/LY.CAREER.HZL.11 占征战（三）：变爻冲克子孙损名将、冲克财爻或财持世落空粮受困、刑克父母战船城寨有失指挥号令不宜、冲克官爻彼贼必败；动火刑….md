# 占征战（三）：变爻冲克子孙损名将、冲克财爻或财持世落空粮受困、刑克父母战船城寨有失指挥号令不宜、冲克官爻彼贼必败；动火刑克父母火烧宫室、火冲克财火焚粮草；财有仓库近子近我军近父近壕堑近鬼近贼所；父母塞位方

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是变爻冲克子孙，主损伤名将。冲克财爻，以及财持世又落空，主粮食受困。刑克父母，主战船、城寨有损失，指挥号令大为不宜。冲克官爻，对方的贼寇必败。凡是卦中动火（火爻发动）刑父母、克父母，必然火烧宫室；火冲克财，主火烧粮草。又，财有仓库（财的所在）：靠近子的，就近我军；靠近父的，就近壕堑；靠近鬼的，就近敌人所在。又，父母堵塞的方位，比如在坤宫就是西南方。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whenever a changed line clashes with or controls Output, it means a famous general is lost. Clashing with or controlling the Wealth line, or Wealth holding the Self and falling Void, means the provisions are in trouble. Punishing or controlling Resource means loss of warships, city and stockade, and command and orders are greatly unsuitable. Clashing with or controlling the Pressure line, the enemy bandits are sure to lose. Whenever in the hexagram a moving Fire line punishes or controls Resource, there will surely be fire burning the halls; Fire clashing with or controlling Wealth means fire burns the provisions. Also, Wealth has storehouses: near Output, they are near my army; near Resource, near the trenches; near Pressure, near the enemy's place. Also, the direction in which Resource stands as the barrier, as in the palace of Kun it is the south-west.

> [!note] 按
> 「变爻冲克子孙」是变出来的爻冲克子孙，损名将；「冲克财爻」「财持世落空」是粮草受困；「刑克父母」是战船城寨有失；「冲克官爻」是敌人必败。「动火刑父母、克父母」是火爻发动去刑克父母，烧宫室；「火冲克财」是烧粮草。「财有仓库」是财爻（粮草）所在的方位也有仓库，按它靠近哪一类爻来定在谁那边：近子在我军、近父在壕堑、近鬼在敌处。「父母塞位方，如坤宫西南方也」按文意是父母（城池）所堵塞的位置，取它所在的宫的方位，如在坤宫就是西南。这一节是征战占里的具体征兆，与易隐征战占的各条（YY.57–YY.70）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'A changed line clashes with or controls Output' is the line that is changed out clashing with or controlling Output, losing a famous general; 'clashing with or controlling the Wealth line' and 'Wealth holding the Self and falling Void' are the provisions in trouble; 'punishing or controlling Resource' is loss of warships, city and stockade; 'clashing with or controlling the Pressure line' is the enemy being sure to lose. 'A moving Fire line punishes or controls Resource' is a Fire line moving to punish or control Resource and burn the halls; 'Fire clashing with or controlling Wealth' is burning the provisions. 'Wealth has storehouses' is that the direction where the Wealth line (the provisions) stands also has storehouses, and which side they are on is fixed by which kind of line it is near: near Output they are at my army, near Resource at the trenches, near Pressure at the enemy's place. 'Fu mu sai wei fang, ru Kun gong xi nan fang ye', by the sense, is the position where Resource (the city) blocks, taking the direction of the palace it stands in, as Kun is south-west. This section gives the concrete omens in divining war; read against each entry in the Yi Yin on war (YY.57-YY.70).
