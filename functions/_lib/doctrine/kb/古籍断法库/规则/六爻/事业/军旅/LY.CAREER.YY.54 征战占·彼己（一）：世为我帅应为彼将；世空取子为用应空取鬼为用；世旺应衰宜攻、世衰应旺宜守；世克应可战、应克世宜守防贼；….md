# 征战占·彼己（一）：世为我帅应为彼将；世空取子为用应空取鬼为用；世旺应衰宜攻、世衰应旺宜守；世克应可战、应克世宜守防贼；世阳动宜出师、世阴静宜坚壁；世空我有难、应空彼必伤、世应俱空两将休兵

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 彼此强弱：世爻是我方主帅，应爻是对方将领。世爻落空，取子孙为用神；应爻落空，取官鬼为用神。世旺应衰，我强彼弱，宜进攻；世衰应旺，我弱彼强，宜固守。世克应，可以打；应克世，宜固守，而且要防贼来。世爻是阳爻而发动的，宜出兵；世爻是阴爻而安静的，宜坚壁固守。世爻落空，我方有难；应爻落空，对方必受损伤；世、应都落空，两方将领休兵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Strength of them and us. The Self is my commander and the Other is the enemy's general. If the Self is Void, take Output as the Subject; if the Other is Void, take Pressure as the Subject. If the Self is thriving and the Other weak, I am strong and they weak, and one should attack; if the Self is weak and the Other thriving, hold the line. If the Self controls the Other, one can fight; if the Other controls the Self, hold the line and guard against the bandits' coming. If the Self is yang and moves, march out; if yin and still, hold the walls. If the Self is Void, I am in trouble; if the Other is Void, they will surely be hurt; if both are Void, the two commanders rest their troops.

> | 世空，则取子为用，应空则取鬼为用也。 | 世爻落空，就取子孙为用神；应爻落空，就取官鬼为用神。 | If the Self is Void, take Output as the Subject; if the Other is Void, take Pressure as the Subject. |

> | 世衰应旺者，我弱彼强，宜守也。 | 世爻衰弱、应爻旺相的，我弱彼强，宜固守。 | If the Self is weak and the Other thriving, I am weak and they strong, and one should hold the line. |
> | 世克应者，可战。 | 世爻克应爻的，可以打。 | If the Self controls the Other, one can fight. |
> | 应克世者，宜守，且宜防贼来也。 | 应爻克世爻的，宜固守，而且要防贼来。 | If the Other controls the Self, hold the line, and guard against the bandits' coming. |
> | 世阳而动者，宜出师。 | 世爻是阳爻而发动的，宜出兵。 | If the Self is yang and moves, it is fit to march out. |
> | 世阴而静者，宜坚壁也。 | 世爻是阴爻而安静的，宜坚壁固守。 | If the Self is yin and still, it is fit to hold the walls. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「坚壁」是加固壁垒、固守不出。「贼」在这里指对方的敌军。
>
> *Note:* 'Jianbi' is strengthening the ramparts and holding without going out. 'Bandits' here means the enemy force.
