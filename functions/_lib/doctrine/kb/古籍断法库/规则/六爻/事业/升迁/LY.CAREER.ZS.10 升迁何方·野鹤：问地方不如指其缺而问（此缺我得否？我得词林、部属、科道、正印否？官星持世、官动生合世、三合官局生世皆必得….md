# 升迁何方·野鹤：问地方不如指其缺而问（此缺我得否？我得词林、部属、科道、正印否？官星持世、官动生合世、三合官局生世皆必得；世空世破、官破官空、子孙持世或发动、官临应爻、官局生应皆不得）

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占升选，与其问「升到哪个方向」，不如指明具体的缺来问：这个缺我能得吗？我能进翰林吗？我能做部属吗？我能做科道官吗？我能做正印官吗？官星持世、官星发动生合世爻、三合官局生世，都主必得；世爻落空落破、官星落破落空、子孙持世、子孙发动、官星临在应爻上、三合官局生应爻，都主得不到。指明了缺就已经知道在哪里，不必多此一举。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a question about promotion, rather than asking 'in which direction', it is better to name the post and ask: can I get this post? Can I enter the Hanlin Academy? Can I get a post in a ministry? Can I become a supervising censor? Can I become the chief official holding the seal? If the star of office holds the Self, or the star of office moves to generate or combine with the Self, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates the Self, it will surely be had; if the Self is Void or broken, the star of office is broken or Void, Output holds the Self, Output moves, the star of office sits on the Other, or a three-harmony Pressure frame generates the Other, it will not be had. Once the post is named its place is already known, and the extra step is unnecessary.

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 词林、部属、科道、正印是清朝官的类别：词林是翰林院，部属是各部的属官，科道是六科给事中和各道监察御史，正印是主官（掌印的正职）。书里没有逐个解释，这里是按通行的意思注的。原文这一段最后还有一句「今人测占工作调动，职务升迁何方亦可仿此」，是今人加的话，不收。
>
> *Note:* Cilin, bushu, kedao and zhengyin are categories of official in the Qing: the Hanlin Academy, the subordinate officials of the ministries, the supervising secretaries and investigating censors, and the chief officials who hold the seal. The book does not explain them one by one; this is the usual sense. The paragraph ends in the text with a sentence 'for people today who divine job transfers and the direction of promotion the same method may be followed', which is a modern editor's addition and is not quoted.
