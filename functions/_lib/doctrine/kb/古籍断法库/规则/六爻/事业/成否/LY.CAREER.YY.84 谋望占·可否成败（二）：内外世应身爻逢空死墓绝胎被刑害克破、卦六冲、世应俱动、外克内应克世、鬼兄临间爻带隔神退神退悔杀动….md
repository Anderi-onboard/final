# 谋望占·可否成败（二）：内外世应身爻逢空死墓绝胎被刑害克破、卦六冲、世应俱动、外克内应克世、鬼兄临间爻带隔神退神退悔杀动，俱不可谋；合中犯刑害克破恩中变怨成而后毁；用神不上卦或空绝（求官无官爻、求财求文书无财印爻）谋不成；卦中无官及官空绝诸事不成；卦中无鬼伏谋事父动费力成

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 不可谋、难成的占：内外卦、世爻、应爻、身爻，逢空、死、墓、绝、胎，被刑、害、克、破的，或者卦是六冲，或者世、应都发动，或者外卦克内卦、应爻克世爻，或者官鬼、兄弟临在间爻、带隔神、退神、退悔杀发动的，都不可谋，谋也不成。又，合中犯了刑、害、克、破的，事情多半恩中生怨，成了之后又毁，虽然是自己的事，也被旁人阻隔。又，用神不上卦的（比如求官而卦里没有官爻、官爻空绝；求财、求文书而没有财爻、印爻，或财爻、印爻空绝），都谋不成。又凡是卦里没有官星，以及官星逢空、绝的，诸事不成。所以经书说：卦中没有官鬼、伏着的谋事，父爻发动，终究要费力才成。这是不可谋而难成的占。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The pattern of a scheme that cannot be planned and is hard to complete. If the inner and outer trigrams, the Self, Other and Body meet Void, death, tomb, extinction or womb and are punished, harmed, controlled or broken, or the hexagram is a six-clash, or the Self and Other both move, or the outer controls the inner and the Other controls the Self, or Pressure or Peer sits on a line between carrying the obstructing spirit, the Retreating spirit or the regret baleful spirit and moves, none can be planned, and if planned will not succeed. Again, if within a combination there is punishment, harm, control or breaking, the matter often turns from favour to resentment, and is spoiled after succeeding, and though it is one's own matter it is blocked by others. Again, if the Subject does not appear on the board (as when seeking office there is no Pressure line, or Pressure is Void or extinct; seeking wealth or a document, there is no Wealth or seal line, or they are Void or extinct), the scheme will not succeed. Again, when the hexagram has no star of office, or the star of office is Void or extinct, nothing succeeds. So the classic says: if there is no Pressure in the hexagram and it lies hidden, in a scheme, with the Resource line moving, it is in the end completed only with effort. This is the pattern of a scheme that cannot be planned and is hard to complete.

> [!note] 按
> 「隔神」「退悔杀」是神煞，书里没有解释，译文照字面。「经曰」引的是另一部经书，书里没有说是哪一部，所以这句译文照字面，不指认出处；「卦中无鬼伏谋事」这句字面不太通，译文按「卦中没有官鬼，而官鬼伏藏时谋事」译。「用神」是占问时被拿来看这件事成败的那个爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Geshen' (the obstructing spirit) and 'tuihui sha' (the regret baleful spirit) are spirit-stars that the book does not explain, and the translation follows the letter. 'The classic says' quotes another classic, and the book does not say which, so the translation follows the letter and does not identify the source; the line 'gua zhong wu gui fu mou shi' does not run smoothly by the letter, and the translation reads it as 'when there is no Pressure in the hexagram and it lies hidden, in a scheme'. The 'Subject' is the line taken as the matter asked about.
