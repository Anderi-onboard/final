# 通玄妙论·逢冲暗动·求官遇之，吉则有无心之机会，凶则阴中有人损害

六爻 · 事业 · 动变 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 安静的爻被日辰冲到，叫暗动，有吉有凶。求官遇到暗动：吉的话有意想不到的机会，凶的话暗中有人损害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A still line clashed by the Day is said to move in secret, and it may be auspicious or inauspicious. In seeking office, meeting this: if auspicious there is a chance one did not seek; if inauspicious someone harms one in secret.

## 《卜筮全书·通玄妙论·五、逢冲暗动》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 2840 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Tongxuan Miaolun, no. 5: a clash that stirs a still line", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且如六爻安静，不遇冲则不动。 | 比如六爻都安静，没有遇到冲，就不会动。 | Suppose the six lines are still: unless clashed they do not move. |
> | 若日辰相冲，名曰暗动。 | 如果日辰来冲它，就叫暗动。 | If the Day clashes a line, it is called a secret movement. |
> | 暗动者，有吉有凶，各有所用，不可一概而论。 | 暗动有吉有凶，各有各的用处，不能一概而论。 | A secret movement may be auspicious or inauspicious, each with its own use; it cannot be judged by one rule. |
> | 若遇凶煞暗动，伤身克世，件件皆非所宜。 | 如果是凶煞暗动，伤到卦身、克到世爻，件件都不适宜。 | If a baleful spirit moves in secret, harming the Body and controlling the Self, nothing is fitting. |
> | 吉神暗动，合世生身，事事无不为吉。 | 吉神暗动，合着世爻、生着卦身，事事没有不吉的。 | If an auspicious spirit moves in secret, combining with the Self and giving life to the Body, every matter is auspicious. |
> | 余推其义，暗者必非明也，乃阴私潜伏也。 | 我推究它的意思：暗，一定不是明，是阴暗私下、潜伏着的。 | I infer its meaning: "in secret" is surely not "in the open"; it is hidden and private, lying in wait. |
> | 福来而不知，祸来而未觉。 | 福来了不知道，祸来了也没察觉。 | Fortune comes and one does not know; misfortune comes and one has not noticed. |
> | 爻吉则暗中有补，爻凶则暗中有伤。 | 爻吉，暗中有补益；爻凶，暗中有损伤。 | If the line is auspicious there is help in secret; if it is inauspicious there is harm in secret. |
