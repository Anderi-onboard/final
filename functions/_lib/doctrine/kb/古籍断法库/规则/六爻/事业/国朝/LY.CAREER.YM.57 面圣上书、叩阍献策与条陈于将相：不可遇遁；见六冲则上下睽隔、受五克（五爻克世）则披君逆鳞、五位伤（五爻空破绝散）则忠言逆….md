# 面圣上书、叩阍献策与条陈于将相：不可遇遁；见六冲则上下睽隔、受五克（五爻克世）则披君逆鳞、五位伤（五爻空破绝散）则忠言逆耳；条陈于将相喜官兴而忌克世

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 面见皇上上书、叩阍陈情献策，不可遇到遁卦。见六冲，就上下隔阂；受五爻的克，就触犯君主的逆鳞；五位受伤，就忠言逆耳。如果是向将相逐条陈述意见，喜官旺而忌克世。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In presenting a memorial to the sovereign in person, or knocking at the palace gate to offer a plan, the hexagram Dun must not be met. Meeting a six-clash, high and low are estranged; suffering the control of the fifth line, one touches the reversed scale on the sovereign's throat; the fifth place being injured, loyal words grate on the ear. If one is presenting views item by item to a general or chief minister, thriving Pressure is welcome and controlling the Self is feared.

> [!note] 按
> 「面圣上书」是当面向皇上呈递奏章，「叩阍」是到宫门叩击喊冤、陈情，「献策」是进献谋略，「条陈」是向将相逐条陈述意见。「遁不可遇」按文意是遁卦（天山遁，是退避的象）不可遇，与书里别处「得遁而不进」同类，是按卦名取义的忌。「睽隔」是隔阂。「逆鳞」是传说龙喉下倒生的鳞，触到它龙必怒，比喻触犯君主的忌讳。「忠言逆耳」是忠言不被听从。书里自注：五克是五爻克世，五位伤是五爻空、破、绝、散。和易林补遗朝天面旨（EL.91–93）、黄金策仕宦的奏陈谏诤（HJC.26）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Mian sheng shang shu' is presenting a memorial to the sovereign face to face, 'kouhun' is knocking at the palace gate to complain of a wrong or plead one's case, 'xiance' is offering a plan, and 'tiaochen' is presenting views item by item to a general or minister. 'Dun bu ke yu', by the sense, is that the hexagram Dun (Retreat, the image of withdrawing) must not be met, of a kind with 'getting Dun, no advance' elsewhere in the book, a taboo taken from the name of the hexagram. 'Kuige' is estrangement. 'Nilin' is the scale growing the wrong way beneath a dragon's throat, which makes the dragon furious when touched, a figure for offending the sovereign's taboos. 'Loyal words grate on the ear' is loyal words not being heeded. The book's own gloss: 'five controls' is the fifth line controlling the Self, and 'fifth place injured' is the fifth line Void, broken, extinct or scattered. Read against the Yilin Buyi on the audience at court (EL.91-93) and the Huangjin Ce on memorials and remonstrances (HJC.26).
