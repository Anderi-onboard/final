# 仕宦·身边伏鬼若非空，头上乌纱终不脱（旧注：遇凶兆，鬼临身持世或本宫鬼伏世下虽见责罚官职犹在；不临持身世、不伏世下或伏而遇空亡者必革官为民）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 遇到凶兆的时候，如果官鬼爻临身持世，或者本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，虽然被责罚，官职还在；如果不临身持世，也不伏在世爻下面，或者虽然伏着仍遇到空亡，必定被革去官带贬为平民，不只是责罚而已。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When an ominous sign appears, if Pressure settles on the Body or holds the Self, or the home palace's Pressure lies hidden under the Self, then although one is blamed and punished, the office remains; if it neither settles on the Body nor holds the Self, nor lies hidden under the Self, or lies hidden but still meets Void, one will surely be stripped of the official belt and reduced to a commoner, which is more than blame and punishment.

> [!note] 按
> 「乌纱」是官员戴的乌纱帽，「头上乌纱终不脱」是说官位保得住；「革官带为民」是革去官带、降为平民。这一联是「官鬼」在凶兆里的作用：有官鬼护着世身，就是被责罚也不丢官；官鬼不在世身上，就会丢官。它和 LY.CAREER.HJC.03（子孙发动剥官）、LY.CAREER.HJC.15（太岁加刑）是同一件事的另一面：那两条讲祸怎么来，这一条讲官位保不保得住。
>
> *Note:* 'The black gauze cap' is the cap an official wears, and 'the black gauze cap will in the end not come off' says that the post is kept; 'stripped of the official belt and reduced to a commoner' is being deprived of the belt of office and reduced to a commoner. This couplet is about the part Pressure plays in an ominous sign: with Pressure guarding the Self and the Body, one does not lose office even if blamed and punished, and with Pressure not on the Self or the Body one loses it. It is the other face of LY.CAREER.HJC.03 (Output moving strips the office) and LY.CAREER.HJC.15 (the Tai Sui with punishment added): those two say how the misfortune comes, and this one says whether the post is kept.
