# 大计与告养：大计求官鬼无瑕、世爻有气，官临日月生扶定膺卓异廉能之选、子孙动有降职之忧、兄弟摇有罚俸之议、兼逢官破致仕还乡；养亲告病休官若官克世被恶、官爻破绝空散、世随墓助伤则辞不得去

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问大计（定期考核官员），要官鬼没有瑕疵，要世爻有气；官临日、月又得生扶，必定被选在卓异、廉能的一等；子孙动，就有降职的忧虑；兄弟摇动，就有罚俸的议论；再遇官破，就致仕还乡。为奉养双亲请假、告病辞官：如果官克世而被厌恶，官爻破、绝、空、散，谁能明白你高蹈归隐的心怀；世象随墓、助伤，反而被世俗的尘缘束缚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about the grand evaluation of officials, one wants Pressure without flaw and the Self line with force; if Pressure sits on the Day or Month and is generated and supported, one will surely be chosen among the outstanding, upright and capable; Output moving, there is the worry of demotion; Peer stirring, there is talk of a fine of salary; and if Pressure is also broken, one retires and goes home. In asking leave to support one's parents or retiring on grounds of illness: if Pressure controls the Self and one is disliked, and the Pressure line is broken, extinct, Void or scattered, who will understand a heart set on lofty retreat; if the Self image follows into the tomb and helps the injury, one is instead bound by the dust of the world.

> [!note] 按
> 「大计」是明清对官员的定期考核（三年一次的外官大计，京官则是京察）。「卓意」疑是「卓异」之误：卓异是考绩最优，「廉能」是廉洁能干。「罚傣」的「傣」是「俸」之误，罚俸是扣俸禄。「致仕还乡」是退休回家。「养亲给假」是请假奉养双亲，「告病休官」是以病为由辞官。「高蹈」是远走高飞、归隐不仕，「高蹈之怀」就是想辞官归隐的心；「风尘之缚」是被世俗尘缘束缚，这里指走不脱。书里的断法：大计要官鬼无瑕、世爻有气，子孙动、兄弟摇都不利，官破则退休；辞官的占问反过来，官克世、官爻破绝空散、世随墓助伤，都是辞不掉。和易隐官禄占的官职退复（YY.28）、在任（YY.20）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Daji' is the periodic evaluation of officials under the Ming and Qing (the triennial evaluation of provincial officials, and for officials in the capital the capital review). 'Zhuoyi' written 'zhuoyi' with another character is presumably a slip for 'zhuoyi', outstanding: the best in the evaluation; 'lianneng' is honest and able. The 'dai' of 'fadai' is a slip for 'feng', salary, so 'fafeng' is a fine of salary. 'Zhishi huanxiang' is retiring and going home. 'Yangqin gejia' is asking leave to support one's parents, and 'gaobing xiuguan' is resigning on grounds of illness. 'Gaodao' is flying far away and living in retirement without office, so 'gaodao zhi huai' is the wish to resign and retire; 'fengchen zhi fu' is being bound by worldly ties, here meaning one cannot get away. The book's judgments: the grand evaluation wants Pressure without flaw and the Self line with force; Output moving and Peer stirring are both unfavourable; broken Pressure means retirement; resigning is the other way round, and Pressure controlling the Self, the Pressure line broken, extinct, Void or scattered, and the Self following into the tomb and helping the injury all mean one cannot resign. Read against the Yi Yin on dismissal and reinstatement (YY.28) and on being in office (YY.20).
