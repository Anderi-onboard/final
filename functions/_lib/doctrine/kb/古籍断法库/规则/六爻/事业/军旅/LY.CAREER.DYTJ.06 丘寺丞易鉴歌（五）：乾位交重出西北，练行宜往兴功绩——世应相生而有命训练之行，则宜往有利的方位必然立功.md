# 丘寺丞易鉴歌（五）：乾位交重出西北，练行宜往兴功绩——世应相生而有命训练之行，则宜往有利的方位必然立功

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 乾位上有动爻，出在西北；训练出行适宜前往，可以兴起功绩。遇到卦中世、应相生，有奉命训练兵马的差事，就适宜前往有利的方位，必然立功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With moving lines in the Qian place it goes out to the northwest; for a training mission it is fitting to go, and merit will arise. When Self and Other generate each other in the hexagram and there is an order to drill troops and horses, it is fitting to go toward the favourable direction, and merit will surely be won.

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全本在「乾位交重出西北」前面还有「巽象摇时东与南」一句，在后面还有「君臣世应上下交」一句，断易天机本都没有；注文也多出「则宜往有利的方位」。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan text has before 'moving lines in the Qian place go out to the northwest' the line 'when the Xun image stirs, the east and the south', and after it the line 'lord and minister, Self and Other, upper and lower meeting', neither of which is in the Duanyi Tianji text; the note here also adds 'it is fitting to go toward the favourable direction'.
