# 出师征伐：劫煞凶星怕临鬼动，亡神恶曜忌并官兴；官鬼为贼魁，休衰可战易剿、旺相强梁谋勇、发动不可抵挡，岂可再加凶煞（劫煞太煞亡神）；官鬼发动勾同诸煞克身世彼势猖獗我军大损

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 劫煞是凶星，怕临官鬼发动；亡神是恶曜，忌和官鬼同时兴起。官鬼是贼魁：休衰，就可以战、容易剿灭；旺相，必是强横而有谋勇的贼；如果发动，就无法抵挡，哪里还能再加凶煞一起行动呢？凶煞就是劫煞、太煞、亡神，起例已注在前面。如果官鬼发动、勾结各煞来克身爻、世爻，必定对方势头猖獗，我军大有损伤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Robbery is an ill star, and fears sitting with a moving Pressure; the Lost Spirit is an evil luminary, and must not rise together with Pressure. Pressure is the bandit chief: if resting or weak, one can fight and it is easy to destroy; if thriving, it is surely a fierce, wise and brave bandit; if it moves it cannot be withstood, so how could ill spirits be added to move with it? The ill spirits are Robbery, the Great Baleful Spirit and the Lost Spirit, whose setting out is noted earlier. If Pressure moves and colludes with the baleful spirits to control the Body and Self, the enemy is surely rampant and my army suffers heavy losses.

> [!note] 按
> 「太煞」即大煞（见 EL.16）；「勾同」是勾结。第三联「忌并官兴」的「官」指官鬼。与易隐征战占「鬼带亡神劫杀在内卦动防奸细」（YY.62）和彼己的「鬼暗动加大杀劫杀刑刃克世防刺客」（YY.56）取同一类断语。
>
> *Note:* 'Tai sha' is the great baleful spirit (see EL.16); 'gou tong' is colluding. The 'official' of 'must not rise together with the official' means Pressure. It takes the same kind of judgment as 'Pressure carrying the Lost Spirit and Robbery moves in the inner trigram, guard against spies' (YY.62) and 'Pressure moves in secret with a great baleful spirit, Robbery, punishment and the blade and controls the Self, guard against assassins' (YY.56) of the Yi Yin war divination.
