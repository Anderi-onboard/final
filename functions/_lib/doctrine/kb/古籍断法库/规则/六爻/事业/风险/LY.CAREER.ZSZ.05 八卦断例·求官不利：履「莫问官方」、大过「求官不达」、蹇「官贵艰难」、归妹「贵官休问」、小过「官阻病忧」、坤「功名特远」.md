# 八卦断例·求官不利：履「莫问官方」、大过「求官不达」、蹇「官贵艰难」、归妹「贵官休问」、小过「官阻病忧」、坤「功名特远」

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 遇到履卦（莫问官方）、大过卦（求官不达）、蹇卦（官贵艰难）、归妹卦（贵官休问）、小过卦（官阻病忧）、坤卦（功名特远），求官不利，要安分守常。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Meeting the hexagram Lü ('do not ask about official business'), Daguo ('seeking office does not succeed'), Jian ('officials and nobles are hard to reach'), Guimei ('do not ask about nobles or office'), Xiaoguo ('office is blocked and illness is a worry') or Kun ('fame and rank are especially distant'), seeking office is unfavourable and one should keep to one's lot.

## 《周易尚占·八卦断例章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 1008 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Chapter of judgments by example for the eight palaces", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 履者礼也 | 履，就是礼。 | Lü (Treading) means ritual propriety. |
> | 以柔履刚 | 用柔去踩刚。 | The soft treads on the hard. |
> | 危中有救 | 危险之中有救。 | In danger there is rescue. |
> | 险处无妨 | 险处没有妨碍。 | In a perilous place there is no harm. |
> | 弗占疾病 | 不要占疾病。 | Do not divine illness. |
> | 莫问官方 | 不要问官府的事。 | Do not ask about official business. |
> | 一切谋运 | 一切谋划运作， | In all one plans and works, |
> | 谨始营昌 | 开头谨慎，经营才昌盛。 | be careful at the start and the venture will prosper. |
