# 在任吉凶·爻反吟，入三墓，反复昏庸（卦得反吟，世官旺相必升，世官衰必降罚，世官被冲克有不测之祸；世休囚被克化墓入墓立见灾非，世有气化墓入墓不过昏聩）

六爻 · 事业 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 爻遇反吟或入三墓，主反复不定、昏庸糊涂。卦得反吟：世爻、官爻旺相，一定升迁；世爻、官星衰弱，必遭降职处罚，降后还要焦虑；世爻或官星被冲被克，有想不到的灾祸。世爻休囚被克、变墓或入墓，立刻见到灾祸是非；世爻有气而变墓、入墓，不过是糊里糊涂、昏聩罢了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Lines in Reversed Chant, or entering the three tombs, mean going back and forth, muddle and dullness. If the hexagram gets Reversed Chant: with the Self and the Pressure line thriving one is surely promoted; with the Self and the star of office weak one is demoted and punished and anxious after the demotion; if the Self or the star of office is clashed or controlled, there is unforeseen calamity. If the Self is resting or trapped and controlled, turns into the tomb or enters the tomb, disaster and trouble appear at once; if the Self has strength and turns into the tomb or enters the tomb, it is no more than being hazy and befuddled.
