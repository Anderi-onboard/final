# 升何方：以官爻所属五行为法——金官升任西土、木官治事东方、水官齐民北海、土官秉政中央、火官为化南隅；不拘官爻衰旺及十二支神，大略以五行分之多验（戌月戊申日占得革，丑官东北未官西南，后擢河南中土）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 升到哪个方向，以官爻所属的五行为法：属金，该到西方上任；属木，该在东方治事；属水，该在北海一带治民；属土，该在中央执政；属火，该在南方一隅施行教化。不论官爻的衰旺，也不论十二支神所主的方位，大略按五行来分，多半应验。例如戌月戊申日占升到哪个方向，得革卦；若按地支说，丑官在东北、未官在西南，后来升到了河南中土。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* As to which direction one will be promoted to, the rule is the phase to which the Pressure line belongs: if Metal, one should take up office in the west; if Wood, one should govern in the east; if Water, one should rule the people by the northern sea; if Earth, one should hold the government in the centre; if Fire, one should carry out the transforming teaching in a corner of the south. Whether the Pressure line is strong or weak, and the directions governed by the twelve branch spirits, do not matter; dividing by the five phases in rough outline is mostly confirmed. For example, in a Xu month on a Wu-Shen day, divining in which direction one would be promoted, one got Ge; going by the branches, a Chou Pressure line is north-east and a Wei one south-west, and later one was promoted to the central earth, Henan.

> [!note] 按
> 「升任」「治事」「齐民」「秉政」「为化」是各行官的取向：金主肃杀所以西、木主生发所以东、水在北、土居中、火在南。「北海」是北方，「南隅」是南方一角。「齐民」是治理百姓。书里自注的例子：戌月戊申日得革卦，革卦里官鬼是丑土和未土，按地支丑在东北、未在西南，但应验的却是土行主中央——升到河南（中土），可见按五行比按地支准，这也是书里说「大略以五行分之多验」的依据。独发的情形看下一条（YM.45），一世官、一伏官看 YM.46。
>
> *Note:* 'Shengren', 'zhishi', 'qimin', 'bingzheng' and 'weihua' are the leanings of the officials of each phase: Metal governs the severe and the killing and so is west, Wood governs the growing and so is east, Water is north, Earth is the centre, and Fire is south. 'Beihai' is the north, and 'nanyu' is a corner of the south. 'Qimin' is governing the people. The book's own example: in a Xu month on a Wu-Shen day, getting Ge, whose Pressure lines are Chou Earth and Wei Earth, by the branches Chou is north-east and Wei is south-west, but what was confirmed was the Earth phase governing the centre, a promotion to Henan (the central earth), which shows that the phases are more accurate than the branches, and is the basis of the book's 'dividing roughly by the five phases is mostly confirmed'. The case of a Pressure line moving alone is in the next entry (YM.45), and a Pressure line on the Self or a hidden one in YM.46.
