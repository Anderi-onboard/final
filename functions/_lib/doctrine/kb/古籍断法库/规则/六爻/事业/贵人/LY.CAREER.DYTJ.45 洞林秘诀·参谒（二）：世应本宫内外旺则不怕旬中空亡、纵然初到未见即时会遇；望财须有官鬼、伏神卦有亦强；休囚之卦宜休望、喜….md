# 洞林秘诀·参谒（二）：世应本宫内外旺则不怕旬中空亡、纵然初到未见即时会遇；望财须有官鬼、伏神卦有亦强；休囚之卦宜休望、喜神动助亦寻常；休囚空亡休准拟、旺杀伤身返有伤；生合扶而不旺寻常相见正忠良

六爻 · 事业 · 贵人 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世、应、本宫、内外卦都旺，就不怕旬中碰上空亡，纵然刚到的时候没能见到，不久也会遇上，很相当；指望得财，必须有官鬼，伏神在卦里也算强；休囚的卦就不要指望，有喜神发动相助也不过寻常；休囚、空亡，不要打算；旺的杀伤身，反而受伤；生合扶助的卦如果不旺，寻常相见，见到的正是忠良之人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self, the Other, the palace and the inner and outer trigrams all strong, one need not fear meeting Void within the decade, and even if on first arriving one cannot see him, one will soon meet and it is very fitting; hoping for wealth, there must be Pressure, and a hidden spirit present in the hexagram is also strong; a hexagram at rest or trapped, give up hope, and with the Joy spirit moving to help it is still only ordinary; at rest, trapped or Void, do not plan on it; a strong baleful spirit injuring the self brings injury instead; a hexagram generated, combined and supported but not strong, one meets as usual and the one met is just a loyal and good man.
