# 营谋·成否与吉凶：自力可成莫观其应、因人成事莫观其世；止问成否但忌空冲、兼问吉凶并求用旺；谋文用父、谋利用财、释患用子（占己谋不论应、占彼谋不论世；问成占谋、问吉凶占用）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 所以靠自己的力量能办成的，不用看应；靠别人才能成的事，不用看世。只问成不成，只忌空、冲；兼问吉凶，就要同时求用神旺。谋文书（功名）用父，谋利用财，解除祸患用子。占自己谋的事不论应，占别人谋的事不论世；问成不成看谋（世应、冲空），问吉凶看用（用神）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* So for what can be done by one's own strength, one need not look at the Other; for what is done through others, one need not look at the Self. If one asks only whether it comes off, one fears only Void and clash; if one also asks about good and ill, one must also seek the Subject thriving. For documents (a name) use the father line, for profit use Wealth, and for relieving trouble use Output. In divining a scheme of one's own, the Other is not discussed; in divining another's scheme, the Self is not discussed; asking whether it comes off one looks at the scheme (Self, Other, clash, Void), asking about good and ill one looks at the Subject.

> [!note] 按
> 「自力可成」是靠自己的力量能办成的事，不用看应；「因人成事」是靠别人才办成的事，不用看世。「止问成否」是只问成不成，「兼问吉凶」是还要问好坏。「谋文用父」是谋文书、功名用父母，「谋利用财」是谋利用妻财，「释患用子」是解除祸患用子孙（子孙克官鬼）。「用旺」是用神旺相。书里自注：占自己谋的事不论应、占别人谋的事不论世；问成不成看谋（世应、冲空），问吉凶看用（用神）。这一条把『成不成』和『好不好』分成两件事：前者看世应的空冲，后者看用神的旺衰。与易隐谋望占的可否成败（YY.83–YY.85）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Zili ke cheng' is what can be done by one's own strength, so one need not look at the Other; 'yin ren cheng shi' is what is done only through others, so one need not look at the Self. 'Zhi wen cheng fou' is asking only whether it comes off, and 'jian wen jixiong' is also asking about good and ill. 'For documents use the father line' is using Resource for documents and a name, 'for profit use Wealth' is using the Wealth line, and 'for relieving trouble use Output' is using Output (Output controls Pressure). 'Yong wang' is the Subject thriving. The book's own gloss: in divining a scheme of one's own the Other is not discussed, and in divining another's scheme the Self is not discussed; asking whether it comes off one looks at the scheme (the Self and the Other, clash and Void), and asking about good and ill one looks at the Subject. This entry separates 'whether it comes off' from 'whether it is good' as two things: the first is seen by the Void and clash of the Self and the Other, the second by the strength of the Subject. Read against the question of what can and cannot be done in the Yi Yin on scheming (YY.83-85).
