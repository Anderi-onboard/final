# 营谋·来去、求己求人与谋之主：谋事之来者谋望并求用神、谋事之去者谋望而不求用神；求于己者世、求于人者应；有交忌冲、无交不忌冲；官鬼乃谋之主俱不可失

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是谋事要「来」的（要得到什么），办法是谋望，再同时求用神；谋事要「去」的（要摆脱什么），办法是谋望而不求用神。求于自己的看世，求于别人的看应；有交接的忌冲，没有交接的不忌冲；只有官鬼是谋事的主，都不可失去。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a scheme in which something is to 'come' (something is to be gained), the method is the scheme and hope, and also seeking the Subject; for a scheme in which something is to 'go' (something is to be shaken off), the method is the scheme and hope without seeking the Subject. What is sought from oneself is seen by the Self, what is sought from others by the Other; where there is a meeting, clash is feared, and where there is no meeting, clash is not feared; only Pressure is the master of the scheme, and neither may be lost.

> [!note] 按
> 「谋事之来者」按文意是要得到点什么（来我这边）的谋事，「谋事之去者」是要摆脱、除去点什么（从我这边去）的谋事，后者像「释患」那样，不求用神。「谋望」就是谋求和盼望（见易隐谋望占 YY.82）。「有交」是事情需要和对方交接、会面、合作，冲就把交接冲散，所以忌；「无交」是不需要交接，冲就无关紧要。「官鬼乃谋之主」是官鬼在谋事里是主持的一方（掌权、批准的人），所以不能失去。这一条与 YM.85 的「官鬼失而谋无主」是同一个意思。
>
> *Note:* 'A scheme that comes' is by the sense a scheme to gain something (to come to my side), and 'a scheme that goes' is a scheme to shake off or get rid of something (to go from my side), the latter being like relieving trouble, where the Subject is not sought. 'Mouwang' is scheming and hoping (see the Yi Yin on scheming and hope, YY.82). 'Where there is a meeting' is a matter that needs meeting, joining or co-operating with the other side, where a clash scatters the meeting and so is feared; 'where there is no meeting' is where no meeting is needed, and a clash is of no consequence. 'Pressure is the master of the scheme' is that in a scheme Pressure is the side that presides (the one who holds power and approves), so it must not be lost. This entry says the same as 'with Pressure lost, the scheme has no master' in YM.85.
