# 仕宦升迁：鬼化子字忧调降、官连财位沐恩光；官化子孙非降即调，卦中财子动亦然，若还财子同兴转助官爻又不降调，官若化财平升品级，官化进气官爻又主重超美职

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼化成子孙，就要担忧被调、被降；官鬼连着财位（化成财），就沐浴恩光。具体说：官鬼化成子孙，不是降职就是调走，卦里财爻、子孙发动也是这样；但如果财爻、子孙一起发动，转过来帮助官爻，就又不降不调；官鬼化成财爻，是在原品级上平升；官爻化成进气的官爻，又主连升、超升到好的职位。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure changes into Output, fear being transferred or demoted; if Pressure is joined to the Wealth place, one bathes in the glow of favour. In detail: if Pressure changes into Output it is either demotion or transfer, and the same if Wealth and Output move in the hexagram; but if Wealth and Output move together and turn to aid the Pressure line, there is then no demotion or transfer; if Pressure changes into Wealth, one is raised on a level in grade; if Pressure changes into an advancing-qi Pressure line, it again means a double promotion to a fine post.

> [!note] 按
> 「官连财位」按解里的「官若化财」译，指官鬼变成财爻。「平陛品级」的「平」疑是在原来的品级上升，即不加品级的平升；「重超」是连升、越级升；「进气」是进神（见术语表）。财爻、子孙一起发动转助官爻就不降调：按五行相生，子孙生财、财生官，子孙的力量经财爻传给官鬼，它就不再是克官的忌神——这是据五行相生推的，书里没有明说。这一条与易隐官禄占「官化财平升」（YY.22）、增删卜易「化进神加官晋爵」（ZS.15）是同一类断语。
>
> *Note:* 'Guan lian cai wei' is translated after 'if Pressure changes into Wealth' in the explanation, and means Pressure changing into the Wealth line. The 'ping' of 'ping sheng pinji' is presumably a rise on the same grade, that is a level promotion with no grade added; 'chong chao' is repeated or over-ranked promotion; 'jin qi' is an advancing spirit (see the glossary). That Wealth and Output moving together and turning to aid Pressure means no demotion is read by the generation of the five elements: Output generates Wealth and Wealth generates Pressure, so the force of Output passes through Wealth to Pressure and Output is no longer the Adversary that controls the star of office; this is inferred from five-element generation and the book does not say it. This entry is the same kind of judgment as 'Pressure changes into Wealth, level promotion' in the Yi Yin divination for official salary and rank (YY.22) and 'changing into an advancing spirit adds office and rank' in the Zengshan Buyi (ZS.15).
