# 出师征伐：鬼煞司权纵往开休生不吉，福神当道虽行伤死杜无殃（《烟波钓叟歌》八门遇开休生诸事称情；出兵最嫌官鬼交重，鬼动纵往吉门决难取胜；子孙旺相发动或持世虽践凶门亦无所害）

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼和凶煞当权，纵然走开、休、生三个吉门也不吉；福神（子孙）当道，即使走伤、死、杜三个凶门也没有灾殃。《烟波钓叟歌》说：八门遇到开、休、生，诸事遇到都称心。凡是占出兵的卦，最嫌官鬼重叠发动，官鬼如果发动，纵然往吉门去，一定难以取胜；如果走子孙旺相发动或持世爻的方位，即使踏上凶门，也没有妨害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure and baleful spirits hold power, even going by the three auspicious gates, Open, Rest and Life, is not auspicious; if the fortune spirit, Output, holds the road, even going by the three ill gates, Harm, Death and Block, brings no disaster. The Yanbo Diaosou Ge says: if the Eight Gates meet Open, Rest and Life, all matters met are to one's mind. In a hexagram for sending out troops, what is most disliked is Pressure moving in double; if Pressure moves, even going by an auspicious gate one surely finds it hard to win; if one goes by the quarter where Output is thriving and moving or holds the Self, even stepping on an ill gate does no harm.

> [!note] 按
> 「鬼煞司权」的鬼煞是官鬼和凶煞；「福神」是子孙（见术语表）。《烟波钓叟歌》是奇门遁甲的歌诀，「开休生」是三个吉门，「伤死杜」是凶门。这一条是整个这章的核心判断：选门走哪个方位，最后看的是官鬼与子孙谁当权，不是单看门的吉凶。
>
> *Note:* 'Pressure and baleful spirits' are Pressure and the ill spirits; the 'fortune spirit' is Output (see the glossary). The Yanbo Diaosou Ge is a rhyme of the Qimen Dunjia art, and Open, Rest and Life are the three auspicious gates and Harm, Death and Block the ill gates. This entry is the core judgment of the whole chapter: in choosing which gate and which direction to take, what is finally looked at is which of Pressure and Output holds power, and not the good or ill of the gate alone.
