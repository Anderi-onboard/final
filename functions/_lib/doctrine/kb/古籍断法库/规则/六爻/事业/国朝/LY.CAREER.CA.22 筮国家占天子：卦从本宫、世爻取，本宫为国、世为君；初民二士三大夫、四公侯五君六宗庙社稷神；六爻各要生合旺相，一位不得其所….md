# 筮国家占天子：卦从本宫、世爻取，本宫为国、世为君；初民二士三大夫、四公侯五君六宗庙社稷神；六爻各要生合旺相，一位不得其所则一国不宁

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占国家、占天子，从卦的本宫和世爻上取：本宫是国，世是君；本宫和世旺相比和，得圣明之主。六爻各有所主：初是民、二是士、三是大夫、四是公侯、五是君、六是宗庙社稷神；动静刚柔各要得当；六爻都要生合旺相，只要有一位不得其所，整个国家就不安宁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine for the state or the Son of Heaven, take from the palace of the hexagram and from the Self: the palace is the state and the Self is the lord, and when they are strong and in accord there is a sage ruler. The six lines each stand for a rank: the first the common people, the second the scholars, the third the grandees, the fourth the dukes and marquises, the fifth the lord, the sixth the ancestral temple and the altars of land and grain; moving or still, hard or soft, each should be in its right place; the six lines should all be generated, combined and strong, and if a single place is not where it should be, the whole country is unsettled.
