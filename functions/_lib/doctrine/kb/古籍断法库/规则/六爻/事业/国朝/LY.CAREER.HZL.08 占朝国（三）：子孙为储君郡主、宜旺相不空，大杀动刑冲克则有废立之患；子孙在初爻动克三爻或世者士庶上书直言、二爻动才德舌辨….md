# 占朝国（三）：子孙为储君郡主、宜旺相不空，大杀动刑冲克则有废立之患；子孙在初爻动克三爻或世者士庶上书直言、二爻动才德舌辨之臣入上封事、三爻动贤能诸侯诣门直谏、四爻动近臣尽忠死诤

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙是储君、郡主，宜旺相不空；如果大煞发动、刑冲克它，恐怕有废立的祸患。子孙在初爻动，克三爻或克世的，有士人百姓上书直言利害；在二爻动，必有有才有德、口才好的臣子进呈密封奏事；在三爻动，有贤能的诸侯到门前直言劝谏；在四爻动（「艾」疑是「爻」之误），身边近臣必定尽忠、冒死直谏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the crown prince and the princesses; it should be strong and not Void; if a great baleful star moves to punish, clash or control it, I fear there is the trouble of deposition and installation. If Output on the first line moves to control the third line or the Self, scholars and commoners submit memorials speaking directly on benefit and harm; moving on the second line, there will be ministers of talent and virtue and a ready tongue who present sealed memorials; moving on the third line, a worthy and able lord comes to the gate to remonstrate frankly; moving on the fourth line (the 'ai' is presumably a slip for 'yao', line), the courtiers at his side will surely be wholly loyal and remonstrate at the risk of death.

> [!note] 按
> 「储君」是太子，「郡主」是亲王之女。子孙在这里是君主的后嗣一类，所以宜旺相、不空，怕大煞发动来刑冲克它，有『废立』（废太子、另立）之患。「士庶民」是读书人和百姓，「上书直言利害」是上书说出国事的利弊，「上封事」是进呈密封的奏章，「诸侯」是分封各地的诸侯，「直谏」是直言劝谏，「尽忠死诤」是尽忠并不惜死地劝谏。原文「贤能诸侯谓门直谏」的「谓门」疑是「诣门」（到门前）之误，「在四艾动」的「艾」是「爻」之误，这里按文意译。这一节讲的是子孙在各个爻位发动，各会引出哪一类人向君主进言。
>
> *Note:* 'Chujun' is the crown prince, and 'junzhu' is a prince's daughter. Output here is the sovereign's heir and the like, so it should be strong and not Void, and a great baleful star moving to punish, clash or control it brings the trouble of 'deposition and installation' (deposing the crown prince and setting up another). 'Shi shu min' are scholars and commoners; 'shangshu zhiyan lihai' is submitting a memorial that states plainly the benefits and harms of state affairs; 'shang fengshi' is presenting a sealed memorial; 'zhuhou' are the lords enfeoffed in the regions; 'zhijian' is frank remonstrance; and 'jin zhong si zheng' is being wholly loyal and remonstrating at the risk of death. The 'wei men' of 'xian neng zhuhou wei men zhijian' is presumably a slip for 'yi men' (go to the gate), and the 'ai' of 'zai si ai dong' is a slip for 'yao' (line); both are translated by the sense. This section says which kind of person will speak to the sovereign when Output moves on each line position.
