# 仕宦·兄爻化鬼无情，同僚不协（旧注：兄弟为僚属之官，鬼动化兄冲克世爻或兄化鬼刑冲克害、兄带三刑六害伤世主同僚不和；世克兄爻是我欺他而不和）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟是僚属，也就是同僚、下属一类的官。卦中官鬼发动、化出兄弟，冲克世爻，主同僚不和；或者兄弟化出官鬼而刑冲克害，或者兄弟带三刑六害伤害世爻，也都是这样；世爻克兄弟，是我欺负他，因而不和。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer stands for the officers of one's staff, that is, colleagues and subordinates. If in the hexagram Pressure moves and turns into Peer and clashes or controls the Self, it means discord among colleagues; or if Peer turns into Pressure and punishes, clashes, controls or harms, or if Peer carries the three punishments or the six harms and injures the Self, it is the same; if the Self controls Peer, it is I who bully him, and so there is discord.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1954 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 兄弟为僚属，卦中鬼动化出兄弟冲克世爻，主同僚不和，或兄弟刑害伤世皆然； | 兄弟是僚属，卦中官鬼发动、化出兄弟，冲克世爻，主同僚不和，或者兄弟刑害伤世的都是这样； | Peer stands for the staff; if in the hexagram Pressure moves and turns into Peer and clashes or controls the Self, it means discord among colleagues, and so too if Peer punishes or harms and injures the Self; |
> | 世克兄爻是我欺他也。 | 世爻克兄弟，是我欺负他。 | if the Self controls Peer, it is I who bully him. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文写的是「兄爻化鬼」（兄弟变官鬼），旧注解释时先说「鬼动化出兄弟」（官鬼变兄弟），再说「兄弟化鬼」，两个方向都讲；王洪绪只讲了「鬼动化出兄弟」一个方向。两个方向书里都当成同僚不和的象。「僚属」是同署办事的官员和下属。「三刑六害」是地支之间的刑和害（寅巳申三刑、子卯相刑等），这里只说兄弟带它们伤世爻。
>
> *Note:* The verse reads 'Peer turns into Pressure', but in explaining it the old commentary first says 'Pressure moves and turns into Peer' and then 'Peer turns into Pressure', covering both directions, while Wang Hongxu speaks only of 'Pressure moves and turns into Peer'; both directions are taken in the books as signs of discord among colleagues. 'Staff' means officers and subordinates who work in the same office. 'The three punishments and the six harms' are the punishments and harms among the branches (Yin, Si and Shen, Zi and Mao and so on), and here Peer is only said to carry them and injure the Self.
