# 仗托人力：财福生身而有益、见官克世以亏财，六冲则意乱无恒岂谐岂就、六合则心平有信能始能终；妻财子孙发动生助世爻终须有益，若见兄弟官鬼交重克冲世象岂不遭伤，六冲作事无成又无信实，六合所谋遂意更有始终

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻、福神生身，有益处；见官爻克世，就亏损钱财。六冲就心意混乱、没有恒心，哪里能和谐、哪里能成；六合就心平气和、有信用，能有始有终。妻财、子孙发动，生助世爻，终究有益；如果见兄弟、官鬼发动，冲克世象，怎么不受伤？六冲，作事不成，又没有信实；六合，所谋遂意，更有始有终。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth and Output generate the Body, it is beneficial; if the star of office controls the Self, it costs wealth. With a six-clash the mind is confused and there is no constancy, so how could it be in harmony and how could it be accomplished; with a six-combine the heart is calm and there is good faith, so it can both begin and end. If Wealth and Output move and generate and aid the Self line, in the end it is beneficial; if Peer and Pressure are seen moving and clashing with or controlling the Self image, how could one not be hurt? With a six-clash, what is undertaken does not succeed and there is no good faith; with a six-combine, what is schemed turns out as one wishes and moreover there is a beginning and an end.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「又夫信实」的「夫」疑是「无」（「又无信实」），译作「没有信实」。「福」是福神（子孙）。这是仗托人力一章的主体：六冲六合定能不能有始有终，财子生世则有益，兄鬼克世则受伤。与求馆章「凡值六冲之卦或无官鬼定无馆也」（EL.53）、谋望成事章「六冲之卦，事决不成」（EL.104）同取六冲不成的断法。
>
> *Note:* The 'fu' of 'you fu xinshi' is presumably 'wu', and 'you wu xinshi' is translated 'there is no good faith'. 'Fu' is the fortune spirit (Output). This is the main body of the chapter on relying on others: a six-clash or a six-combine settles whether there is a beginning and an end, Wealth and Output generating the Self is beneficial, and Peer and Pressure controlling the Self is injurious. It takes the same judgment that a six-clash does not succeed as 'with a six-clash hexagram there is surely no post' in the chapter on seeking a school post (EL.53) and 'with a six-clash hexagram the affair will surely not succeed' in the chapter on scheming (EL.104).
