# 官鬼者事之主、祸之端：出入行藏贵静勿动、兴词构难畏其动而即成；凡防害及身喜其无鬼、求事益己利在有官

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 所以官鬼是事情的主，也是祸的起头；出入行藏，贵在静而不动；兴词、构难（打官司、制造灾难），怕它一动就成。大凡防备伤害到自身的，喜欢它没有鬼；求事来对自己有益的，利在有官。要请学的人自己分辨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* So Pressure is the master of a matter and also the beginning of calamity; in going out and in, going abroad and hiding, it is valued still and not moving; in bringing a suit or contriving a difficulty, one fears that once it moves the thing is done. In general, in guarding against harm to oneself, it is welcome for there to be no Pressure; in seeking a matter to one's own advantage, the benefit lies in having the official. Let the student distinguish them.

> [!note] 按
> 「事之主」是官鬼是事情的主持者（批准、裁定的一方），「祸之端」是灾祸的起头。「出入行藏」是出门、藏身这类行动。「兴词构难」是打官司、制造灾难。书里的意思：凡是想防备伤害的占问（出入、藏身、防讼），官鬼静才好，动了就一触即发；凡是想求事对自己有益的占问（求官、求名、求事），利在有官。「学者辩之」是请学的人自己分辨这两头。这与 YM.102 的『喜之为官、恶之为鬼』是同一个意思：同一个官鬼，要看这件事是要它还是怕它。
>
> *Note:* 'Shi zhi zhu' is that Pressure is the one who presides over a matter (the side that approves and decides), and 'huo zhi duan' is the beginning of calamity. 'Chu ru xing cang' is acts such as going out and hiding. 'Xingci gounan' is going to law and contriving a difficulty. The sense of the book: in every divination in which one wants to guard against harm (going out and in, hiding, guarding against a suit), Pressure is good when still, and once it moves the thing is sprung; in every divination in which one wants a matter to one's own advantage (seeking office, a name, a matter), the benefit lies in having the official. 'Xuezhe bian zhi' asks the student to tell the two sides apart himself. This is the same idea as 'what is welcome is called the official, what is disliked the demon' in YM.102: with the same Pressure line, one must see whether in this matter one wants it or fears it.
