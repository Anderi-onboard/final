# 上书·世临空破，难以回天（世临旬空月破、临绝化绝、入三墓，不蒙见准宜早知机；世动变凶或岁五日月动爻冲克世，不惟难回天且防不测）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上书时世爻临空、临破，难以挽回圣意：世爻临旬空、月破，以及临绝、变绝、入三墓的，得不到批准，应当早点察觉时机；世爻发动变凶，或者太岁、五爻、日月、动爻冲克世爻的，不但难以挽回，而且要防备想不到的祸患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a memorial, if the Self falls on Void or break, it is hard to turn heaven round: if the Self falls on Void or Month-break, or on extinction or turns into extinction, or enters the three tombs, the request will not be approved, and one should read the moment early; if the Self moves and turns bad, or Tai Sui, the fifth line, the Day, the Month or a moving line clashes with or controls the Self, it is not only hard to turn heaven round but one must guard against unforeseen misfortune.
