# 卦克世身则职位不安；身克人吏则吏皆退避、百忧聚集

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦（宫）所属的五行去克世爻和身位，职位不得安稳；身、世去克初爻（人吏），吏员都躲避退让，各样事业都有忧患聚集的征兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the element the hexagram belongs to controls the Self line and the Body place, the post cannot be held in peace; if Body and Self control the first line, the clerk, all the clerks draw back, and anxieties gather over every undertaking.
