# 更名顶籍：更此官名宜鬼旺、顶其店业要财兴，兄临雀动伤身世犹恐傍人举斗争，世不空冲官不陷事当圆就永无更；在官顶补姓名尤要官爻旺相又不可鬼克世身，顶人艺业还求财象兴隆不拘公私更改兄爻独发难成，纵然乱动兄带朱雀克世或应带朱雀克世必有傍人举首争夺，世爻兴官爻不空冲破绝事事皆成

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 顶补官名（官职），适宜官鬼旺相；顶替别人的店铺产业，要财爻兴旺。兄爻临朱雀发动，伤克身爻、世爻，还怕旁人出来争斗；世爻不空不冲、官不落陷，事情当可圆成，永不更改。在官府顶补姓名，尤其要官爻旺相，又不可官鬼克世爻、身爻；顶替别人的手艺行业，还要财象兴隆；不论公家还是私人的更改，兄爻独发就难成；即使乱动，如果兄爻带朱雀发动来克世，或者应爻带朱雀来克世，必有旁人出头争夺；世爻兴旺、官爻都不空、不冲、不破、不绝，事事都成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To take over an official name, it is fitting for Pressure to be thriving; to take over another's shop and business, Wealth must flourish. If Peer on the Vermilion Bird moves and hurts the Body or the Self, one still fears bystanders rising to contend; if the Self is neither Void nor clashed and the star of office is not sunk, the matter will be fully accomplished and never changed. In taking over a name in an official post, it is especially necessary for the Pressure line to be thriving, and Pressure must not control the Self or the Body; in taking over another's craft or trade, one still wants the Wealth image to flourish; whether the change is public or private, if the Peer line moves alone it is hard to accomplish; even if lines move in confusion, if Peer carrying the Vermilion Bird moves to control the Self, or the Other carrying the Vermilion Bird comes to control the Self, there will surely be bystanders who come forward to contend; if the Self is thriving and the Pressure line is not Void, clashed, broken or extinct, everything succeeds.

> [!note] 按
> 「顶补」是顶替补缺；「艺业」是手艺行业；「店业」是店铺产业；「举首」是出头；「傍人」是旁人。「更此官名」按解里「在官顶补姓名」读，指顶补官府里的名额。这一条的取法和谋差顶役（EL.106 起）一样：官爻旺相、财爻兴旺是吉，兄爻带朱雀来克世是旁人来争。
>
> *Note:* 'Dingbu' is taking over a place to fill a vacancy; 'yiye' is a craft or trade; 'dianye' is a shop and its business; 'jushou' is coming forward; 'bangren' is bystanders. 'Geng ci guan ming' is read by the explanation's 'taking over a name in an official post' and means taking over a place on the roll in an office. The method here is the same as in the chapters on seeking a commission (from EL.106): the Pressure line thriving and Wealth flourishing are auspicious, and Peer carrying the Vermilion Bird coming to control the Self is bystanders coming to contend.
