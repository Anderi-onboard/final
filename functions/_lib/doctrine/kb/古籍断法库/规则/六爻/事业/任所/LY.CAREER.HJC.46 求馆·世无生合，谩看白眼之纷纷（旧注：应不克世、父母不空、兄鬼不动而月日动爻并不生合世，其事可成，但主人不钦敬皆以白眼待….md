# 求馆·世无生合，谩看白眼之纷纷（旧注：应不克世、父母不空、兄鬼不动而月日动爻并不生合世，其事可成，但主人不钦敬皆以白眼待之）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻不克世爻，父母不空，兄弟、官鬼不发动，而月建、日辰、动爻都不生合世爻的，这个事可以成，只是主人不敬重，都用白眼对待。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Other does not control the Self, Resource is not Void, Peer and Pressure do not move, and the Month, the Day and the moving lines do not generate or combine with the Self, the matter can come about, only the master does not respect one and treats one with disdain.

## 《黄金策·求馆(附束修)》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8578 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a school post (with the tuition fee)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世无生合，谩看白眼之纷纷； | 世爻没有人来生合，只能徒然看着人们翻白眼的纷纷景象； | With no one to generate or combine with the Self, one can only look on at the crowds of white-eyed disdain; |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2771 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应不克世，父母不空，兄鬼不动，而月日动爻并不生合世爻者，其事纵成，但主人不钦敬，故白眼待之。 | 应爻不克世爻，父母不空，兄弟、官鬼不发动，而月建、日辰、动爻都不生合世爻的，这个事即使成了，主人也不敬重，所以用白眼相待。 | If the Other does not control the Self, Resource is not Void, Peer and Pressure do not move, and the Month, the Day and the moving lines do not generate or combine with the Self, then even if the matter comes about the master does not respect one, and so treats one with white eyes. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「白眼」是用白眼看人，表示轻视（阮籍青白眼的典故）。这一条说的是：事能成，但没有人扶（日月动爻不生合世），所以在东家那里不受重视。王洪绪加了「纵成」二字，更强调这是成了之后的待遇。
>
> *Note:* 'White eyes' is looking at someone with the whites of one's eyes, showing contempt (from the story of Ruan Ji and his green and white eyes). This entry says that the matter can come about but, with no one to prop one up (the Day, the Month and the moving lines do not generate or combine with the Self), one is not valued by the host. Wang Hongxu adds 'even if it comes about', stressing that this is the treatment after it has come about.
