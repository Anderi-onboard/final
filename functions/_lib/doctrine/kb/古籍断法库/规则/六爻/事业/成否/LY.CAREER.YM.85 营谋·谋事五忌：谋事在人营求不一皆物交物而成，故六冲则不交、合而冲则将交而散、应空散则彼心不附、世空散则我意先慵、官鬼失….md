# 营谋·谋事五忌：谋事在人营求不一皆物交物而成，故六冲则不交、合而冲则将交而散、应空散则彼心不附、世空散则我意先慵、官鬼失而谋无主；唯世应破绝犹得其半（破绝有形空散无迹）

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谋事在人，营求的办法不一，都是物与物相交才成。所以六冲就交不到一起；合处逢冲，就将要交合却又（此处疑脱一字，当是「散」）；应空、散，对方的心就不依附；世空、散，我自己的意思先懒了；只有世应破、绝，还能得到一半；官鬼失去，谋事就没有主。这是五种忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Scheming rests with men; the ways of seeking are not one, and all of them are accomplished by one thing meeting another. So with a six-clash they do not meet; with a combination clashed, they are about to join and then (a character seems lost here, presumably 'come apart'); with the Other Void or scattered, his heart does not attach to me; with the Self Void or scattered, my own will first turns slack; only with the Self and the Other broken or extinct is half still obtained; with Pressure lost, the scheme has no master. These are the five taboos.

> [!note] 按
> 「谋事在人」是谋事要靠人去做，「营求」是经营求取，「物交物」是事物和事物相交、两边凑到一起才成。「六冲则不交」是六冲（散）就凑不到一起；「合而冲」是合处逢冲（见 YM.07 注）。「则将交而」后面原文疑脱一字，当是「散」，按文意译作「将要交合却又散开」。「应空散则彼心不附」是应是对方，应空、散就是对方没有心；「世空散则我意先慵」是世是自己，世空、散就是自己先懒了。「世应破绝，犹得其半」：破、绝是有形的，力量残存，所以还能成一半；空、散是没影的，所以是忌。「官鬼失而谋无主」是官鬼是谋事的主，没有它事无主持（见 YM.88）。五忌就是：六冲、合冲、应空散、世空散、官鬼失。这一章是营谋（谋求一件事）与嘱托（托人办事），和易隐谋望占（YY.82–88）、易林补遗谋望成事（EL.103 起）取同一类，章首「内附嘱托」就是嘱托也在这里。
>
> *Note:* 'Moushi zai ren' is that scheming has to be done by men, 'yingqiu' is managing and seeking, and 'wu jiao wu' is one thing meeting another, so that the two sides come together and it succeeds. 'With a six-clash they do not meet' is that a six-clash (scattering) cannot bring them together; 'a combination clashed' is a combination meeting a clash (see the note to YM.07). Below 'ze jiang jiao er' the original seems to lack a character, presumably 'san', scatter, and by the sense it is translated 'about to join and then comes apart'. 'The Other Void or scattered, his heart does not attach to me' is that the Other is the other side, and the Other Void or scattered means he has no heart for it; 'the Self Void or scattered, my own will first turns slack' is that the Self is myself, and the Self Void or scattered means I am the first to go slack. 'The Self and the Other broken or extinct, half is still obtained': broken and extinct have a form, some force remains, so half can still be had; Void and scattered leave no trace, so they are taboo. 'Pressure lost, the scheme has no master' is that Pressure is the master of a scheme, and without it the matter has no one to run it (see YM.88). The five taboos are: a six-clash, a combination clashed, the Other Void or scattered, the Self Void or scattered, and Pressure lost. This chapter is about scheming (seeking something) and entrusting (asking someone to act for one), of a kind with the Yi Yin on scheming and hope (YY.82-88) and the Yilin Buyi on accomplishing a scheme (from EL.103); 'attached: entrusting' at the head of the chapter means entrusting is treated here too.
