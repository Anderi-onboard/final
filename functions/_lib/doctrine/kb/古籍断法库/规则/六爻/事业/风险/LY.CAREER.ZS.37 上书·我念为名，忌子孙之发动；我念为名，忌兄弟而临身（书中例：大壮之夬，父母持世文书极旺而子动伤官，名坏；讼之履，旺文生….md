# 上书·我念为名，忌子孙之发动；我念为名，忌兄弟而临身（书中例：大壮之夬，父母持世文书极旺而子动伤官，名坏；讼之履，旺文生世而兄弟持世，奏上而钱财终破）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 为求功名而上书，忌讳子孙发动，忌讳兄弟临身：父母持世、文书再旺，只要子孙一动伤官，为名的人文书旺也没用；旺相的文书生世、但兄弟持世，上司的奏章可以上去，自己的钱财终究要破，销账不准。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When one submits a memorial to seek a name, Output moving is to be avoided and Peer on the Body is to be avoided: if Resource holds the Self and the document is extremely thriving, once Output moves to injure the star of office a thriving document is of no use to one who seeks a name; if a thriving document generates the Self but Peer holds the Self, the superior's memorial goes up but one's own money is bound to be lost and the write-off is not approved.
