# 官禄占·在任（三）：道路爻空巡方使者多惊；子孙旺动剿捕官奏凯；太岁伤官朝仕必黜；月日生世外任可升

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在任，差使和升黜：道路这一爻落空，出外巡视的使者多受惊吓；子孙旺相发动，负责剿捕的官员奏报凯旋；太岁伤官星，在朝为官的必被罢黜；月建、日辰生世爻，外任的官可以升迁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In office, commissions, promotion and dismissal. If the road line is Void, the envoy touring his district is often alarmed; if Output is thriving and moving, the officer in charge of suppressing bandits reports victory; if Tai Sui injures the star of office, an official at court is surely dismissed; if the Month and Day generate the Self, an official in a post outside can be promoted.

> [!note] 按
> 「道路」是上一章鬼谷分爻表里的名目之一，书里没有写是哪一爻（见 YY.13）。「巡方」是出外巡视地方的差使，「奏凯」是奏报得胜。「朝仕」是在朝廷任职的官，「外任」是在京城以外任职。
>
> *Note:* 'The road' is one of the names in the Guigu table of the previous chapter, and the book does not say which line it is (see YY.13). 'Xunfang' is the commission of touring the districts, and 'zoukai' is reporting a victory. 'Chaoshi' is an official serving at court and 'waiten' is serving outside the capital.
