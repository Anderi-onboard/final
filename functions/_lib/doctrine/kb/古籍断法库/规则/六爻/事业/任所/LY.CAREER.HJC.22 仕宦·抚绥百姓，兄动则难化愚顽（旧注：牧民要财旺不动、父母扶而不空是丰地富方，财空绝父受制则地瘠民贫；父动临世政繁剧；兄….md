# 仕宦·抚绥百姓，兄动则难化愚顽（旧注：牧民要财旺不动、父母扶而不空是丰地富方，财空绝父受制则地瘠民贫；父动临世政繁剧；兄持世财赋不起；日带兄冲克世下属弄法；兄旁爻动冲克顽民难治；兄化子子化兄刑克世身有下民讼我；世应冲克与乡宦不睦）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 担任牧民的职位，要财爻旺而不动，父母爻扶助而不空，必是富庶的好地方；财爻空绝、父爻受制，就是地瘠民贫；父母发动临世，政务必定繁重；兄弟持世，赋税征不起来；日辰带兄弟冲克世爻，手下人必定舞文弄法，败坏我的政事；兄弟在旁边的爻上发动来冲克，顽劣的百姓难治；兄弟化子孙、子孙化兄弟而刑克世身，恐怕有百姓告我；世应冲克，和乡绅也多不和睦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a post that shepherds the people, Wealth should be thriving and not moving and Resource should support and not be Void, and then it is surely a rich and prosperous place; if Wealth is Void or in extinction and Resource is controlled, the land is barren and the people poor; if Resource moves and settles on the Self, the business of government will surely be heavy; if Peer holds the Self, taxes cannot be raised; if the Day carries Peer and clashes or controls the Self, the underlings will surely twist the law and ruin my government; if Peer moves on a side line and comes to clash or control, the unruly people are hard to govern; if Peer turns into Output or Output into Peer and punishes or controls the Self or the Body, there may be the sign of the people bringing a suit against me; and if the Self and the Other clash or control each other, there is also much discord with the local gentry.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1970 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡任牧民之官，要财爻旺而不动，父母扶而不空，方是善地。 | 凡是担任牧民的官，要财爻旺而不动，父母扶助而不空，才是好地方。 | In any post that shepherds the people, Wealth should be thriving and not moving and Resource should support and not be Void, and then it is a good place. |
> | 若财爻空绝，父爻受刑，则地脊民贫，父母动临世上，政必繁剧； | 如果财爻空绝，父爻受刑，就是地瘠民贫，父母发动、临世爻，政务必定繁重； | If Wealth is Void or in extinction and Resource is punished, the land is barren and the people poor, and if Resource moves and settles on the Self, the business of government will surely be heavy; |
> | 兄弟持世，财赋不起，或贫民难冶。 | 兄弟持世，赋税征不起来，或者是穷民难治。 | if Peer holds the Self, taxes cannot be raised, or the poor people are hard to govern. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「牧民」是治民（像牧人管牲畜，指州县的地方官），「愚顽」是愚昧顽劣的人，「舞文弄法」是玩弄文字、歪曲法令（衙门里胥吏常做的事），「乡宦」是在乡的退职官员、乡绅。这一联是任所里看地方好坏的一组判据：财、父看地方的肥瘠和政务的繁简，兄弟看赋税、下属和百姓。王洪绪写「地脊」，「脊」是「瘠」的误写；「贫民难冶」的「冶」是「治」的误写。王洪绪没有旧注后面几句（下属弄法、顽民难治、下民讼我、与乡宦不睦）。
>
> *Note:* 'Shepherding the people' is governing the people like a herdsman with livestock, meaning a local officer of a prefecture or county; 'the stubborn' are the ignorant and unruly; 'twist the law' is playing with words and bending the statutes, as clerks in a yamen often did; and 'local gentry' are retired officials living in their home districts and the gentry. This couplet is a group of criteria in 'in office' for judging a place: Wealth and Resource show how rich the land is and how heavy the government, and Peer shows taxes, underlings and the people. Wang Hongxu writes 'the land is thin' with a misprinted character for 'barren', and 'the poor are hard to rule' with a misprinted character for 'govern'. Wang Hongxu does not have the old commentary's last sentences (underlings twisting the law, unruly people hard to govern, the people suing me, discord with the gentry).
