# 道业·持斋戒：专务用神、自占以世为本，用旺鬼静则始终不渝、三冲用坏则半途改辙；若筮开斋破戒当无吉占、世坏鬼动尤凶

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占持斋戒（守斋持戒），专取用神；自己占的以世为本。用旺、鬼静，就始终不变；三冲、用坏，就半路改辙。如果占开斋破戒，不会有吉占；世坏、鬼动尤其凶，只能戒（不要做）罢了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining about keeping the fast and the precepts, one attends only to the Subject; in divining for oneself the Self is the base. With the Subject thriving and Pressure still, one does not change from beginning to end; with the three clashes and the Subject damaged, one changes course halfway. If one divines about breaking the fast and the precepts, there will be no auspicious divination; a damaged Self and Pressure moving are especially ominous, and all one can do is refrain.

> [!note] 按
> 「斋戒」是素食守斋、持守戒律，「开斋破戒」是中途吃荤、破坏戒律。「专务用神」是专看用神；「自占以世为本」是自己占就以世爻为本位。「始终不渝」是始终不变，「半途改辙」是半路改变做法。书里自注：占开斋破戒，不会有吉占，世坏、鬼动更凶，只能劝戒。这一条的「用神」没有说是哪一爻，按全章的思路是福神（子孙，见 YM.91 道缘）和世爻，按文意理解。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhaijie' is keeping a vegetarian fast and keeping the precepts, and 'kaizhai pojie' is breaking the fast and breaking the precepts partway. 'Zhuanwu yongshen' is attending solely to the Subject; 'zizhan yi shi wei ben' is that when divining for oneself the Self line is the base. 'Shizhong buyu' is not changing from beginning to end, and 'bantu gaizhe' is changing one's course halfway. The book's own note: divining about breaking the fast and the precepts there will be no auspicious divination, a damaged Self and Pressure moving are worse, and all one can do is advise refraining. The 'Subject' in this entry is not said to be any particular line; by the thought of the whole chapter it is the fortune spirit (Output, see 'affinity for the Way' in YM.91) and the Self line, taken by the sense.
