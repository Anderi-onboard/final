# 国朝·应旺生合世爻，圣主得椒房之助（旧注：应爻为皇后，旺相生合世爻又临财福，皇后美貌智略、天性慈仁，能规君过、导君以善，如汉马后、宋宣仁）

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻是皇后。应爻旺相、生合世爻，又临妻财或子孙，皇后相貌美、有智谋、天性仁慈，能规劝国君的过失，引导国君向善，像汉朝的马皇后、宋朝的宣仁皇后。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other line is the empress. If the Other line is thriving or strong, generates or combines with the Self, and sits on Wealth or Output, the empress is beautiful, wise and kind by nature, and can correct the ruler's faults and lead him to the good, like Empress Ma of Han and Empress Xuanren of Song.

## 《黄金策·国朝》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5849 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The court and the state", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应旺生合世爻，圣主得椒房之助； | 应爻旺相、生合世爻，圣明的君主就能得到皇后的帮助； | If the Other line is thriving and generates or combines with the Self, the sage ruler gains the help of the Pepper Chamber (the empress's quarters); |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5851–5852 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应爻，皇后之位。 | 应爻是皇后的位置。 | The Other line is the empress's place. |
> | 若应爻旺相，生合世爻，更临财福； | 如果应爻旺相，生合世爻，又临在妻财、福神上； | If the Other line is thriving or strong, generates or combines with the Self, and sits on Wealth or Output; |
> | 主皇后美貌智略，天性慈仁； | 主皇后容貌美丽、有智谋才略，天性慈爱仁厚； | it means the empress is beautiful, wise and capable, and kind and benevolent by nature; |
> | 能规君过，导君以善。 | 能规劝国君的过失，引导国君做善事。 | she can correct the ruler's faults and lead him to the good. |
> | 如汉马后。 | 就像汉朝的马皇后。 | Such was Empress Ma of Han. |
> | 宋宜仁，称女中尧舜也。 | 宋朝的宣仁（这里原文作「宜仁」）皇后，被称作女中的尧舜。 | Empress Xuanren of Song (written Yiren here) was called a Yao and Shun among women. |
> | 后房用椒涂壁，以辟除邪气。 | 皇后的居室用花椒涂在墙壁上，用来驱除邪气。 | The empress's chambers had pepper plastered on the walls, to drive away evil influences. |
> | 故曰椒房。 | 所以叫椒房。 | Hence the name Pepper Chamber. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1484 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应为皇后，若旺相合世爻，更临财福，主后智略仁慈，导君以善，如汉马后、宋宣仁，为女中尧舜是也。 | 应爻是皇后，如果旺相、合世爻，又临妻财、福神，主皇后有智谋才略、仁慈，引导国君向善，像汉朝的马皇后、宋朝的宣仁皇后，就是女中的尧舜。 | The Other line is the empress; if it is thriving or strong and combines with the Self, and further sits on Wealth or Output, the empress is wise, capable and kind and leads the ruler to the good, like Empress Ma of Han and Empress Xuanren of Song, who were the Yao and Shun among women. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 宋朝那位皇后，卜筮大全的旧注写「宜仁」，卜筮正宗写「宣仁」；译文按「宣仁」读（宋朝被称作「女中尧舜」的宣仁太后）。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary in the Bu Shi Da Quan writes the Song empress as 'Yiren' and the Bu Shi Zheng Zong writes 'Xuanren'; the translation reads 'Xuanren' (the Song empress dowager Xuanren who was called 'the Yao and Shun among women').
