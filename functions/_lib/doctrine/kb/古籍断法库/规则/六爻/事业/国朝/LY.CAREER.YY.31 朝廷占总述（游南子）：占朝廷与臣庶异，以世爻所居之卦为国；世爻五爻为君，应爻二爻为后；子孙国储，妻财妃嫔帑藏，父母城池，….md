# 朝廷占总述（游南子）：占朝廷与臣庶异，以世爻所居之卦为国；世爻五爻为君，应爻二爻为后；子孙国储，妻财妃嫔帑藏，父母城池，兄弟劫神，官鬼奸贼；初民二士三大夫四公卿五天子六宗庙；卜世卜年另有一法

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占朝廷的事和占臣子百姓自己的事不同：以世爻所在的那个单卦当作国家；世爻和五爻都是君主，君主自己占取世爻为用神，臣子百姓代他占取五爻；应爻和二爻是皇后；子孙是储君，妻财是妃嫔也是国库，父母是城池，兄弟是劫神，官鬼是奸贼；初爻是百姓，二爻是士人，三爻是大夫，四爻是公卿，五爻是天子，六爻是宗庙朝廷。察看各爻的生克旺衰，国家的利弊兴衰就看得清清楚楚；至于占国家传几世、延续几年，另有一种方法。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining for the court differs from divining for ministers and commoners: the trigram in which the Self line sits stands for the state; the Self and the fifth line are both the ruler, and when the ruler divines for himself the Self is taken as the Subject, while ministers and commoners divining on his behalf take the fifth line; the Other line and the second line are the empress; Output is the heir apparent, Wealth is the consorts and also the treasury, Resource is the city walls and moats, Peer is the robber spirit, and Pressure is the traitors and bandits; the first line is the common people, the second the scholars, the third the great officers, the fourth the ministers of state, the fifth the Son of Heaven, and the sixth the ancestral temple and court. Look at the generation, control, strength and weakness of each line and the gains and losses and rise and fall of the state are as clear as the palm of one's hand; as for divining how many reigns or years the dynasty will last, there is another method.

> [!note] 按
> 「单卦」是三爻卦，指内卦或外卦，世爻在哪个三爻里，哪个就是国（见 YY.32）。「劫神」是主夺取、劫掠的凶神。「用神」是占问时被拿来看这件事成败的那个爻。这一节是整个朝廷占的纲，后面各小节都是按这里的分配来断。
>
> *Note:* A 'single trigram' is a trigram of three lines, the inner or the outer trigram: whichever trigram holds the Self line is the state (see YY.32). The 'robber spirit' is the baleful spirit of seizing and plundering. The 'Subject' is the line taken as the matter asked about. This section is the outline of the whole divination for the court, and the sections after it judge by the assignments made here.
