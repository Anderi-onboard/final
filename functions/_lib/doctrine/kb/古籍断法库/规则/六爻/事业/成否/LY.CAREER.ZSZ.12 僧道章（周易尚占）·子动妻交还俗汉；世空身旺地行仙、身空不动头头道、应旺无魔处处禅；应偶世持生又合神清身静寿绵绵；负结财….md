# 僧道章（周易尚占）·子动妻交还俗汉；世空身旺地行仙、身空不动头头道、应旺无魔处处禅；应偶世持生又合神清身静寿绵绵；负结财身动填还俗债；世应不和身妄动待驴年；本身太岁临身命即日逢神得正传；太岁火神加父母天边恩命定来宣

六爻 · 事业 · 成否 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动、妻财交动，是要还俗的汉子；世空、身旺，是地上的行仙；身空而不动，事事都是道；应旺而没有魔，处处都是禅；应落偶位、世持住，又生又合，精神清明、身体安静，寿命绵长；忽然有负债纠结、财和身一起发动，免不了去偿还俗世的债和冤；世、应不和、身又乱动，想了身达命要等到驴年（永远等不到）；本身、太岁落在身和命上，当天就遇上神人、得到真传；太岁、火神加在父母上，天边的恩命一定会来宣布。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving with Wealth also moving: a man who will return to lay life; the Self void and the Body strong makes an earth-wandering immortal; the Body void and still, every single thing is the Way; the Other strong with no demon, Chan is everywhere; the Other on an even place and the Self holding, generating and combining, the spirit clear and the body at peace, life is long; if suddenly there is a debt-knot and Wealth and the Body move, one cannot avoid paying back worldly debts and grievances; with Self and Other at odds and the Body moving wildly, to finish with the body and reach one's destiny one must wait for the Year of the Donkey; the person himself and Tai Sui on the Body and one's fate, on that very day one meets a divine person and receives the true transmission; Tai Sui and the Fire spirit added to Resource, a favour commanded from the edge of heaven will surely be proclaimed.

## 《周易尚占·僧道章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 683 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Chapter on monks and Daoists", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 太岁火神加父母， | 太岁、火神加在父母上， | Tai Sui and the Fire spirit added to Resource, |
> | 天边恩命定来宣， | 天边的恩命一定会来宣布。 | a favour commanded from the edge of heaven will surely be proclaimed. |
> | 问予末后天机事， | 问我最后这件天机之事， | If you ask me about this last matter of heaven's secret, |
> | 拈起笔来画个圈。 | 拈起笔来，画一个圈。 | I take up my brush and draw a circle. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「应偶世持生又合」卜筮大全作「应偶世奇生又合」（一个「持」一个「奇」）；「本身太岁临身命」卜筮大全作「木神太岁临身命」；末句「拈起笔来画个圈」卜筮大全作「拈笔书将一个缘」。「负结」「驴年」的说明同阐奥歌章（CA.31）。
>
> *Note:* 'The Other on an even place and the Self holding' reads in the Bushi Daquan 'the Other on an even place and the Self on an odd one' (a 'hold' for an 'odd'); 'the person himself and Tai Sui on the Body and one's fate' reads there 'the Wood spirit and Tai Sui on the Body and one's fate'; and the last line 'I take up my brush and draw a circle' reads there 'I take up my brush and write down a single yuan'. The remarks on 'debt-knot' and 'the Year of the Donkey' are as for the Chan'ao Ge (CA.31).
