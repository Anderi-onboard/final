# 选缺参房总纲：以官爻为主、世象为凭；官员选缺及吏典参房皆把官爻及世详，此二象内如有一爻犯月破旬空者官不能得此缺吏不能参此房；应动兄兴冲克世，世纵不空若被应动克世或兄弟动来克世必有同袍抢夺

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官员选缺、吏典参房，都以官爻和世爻为主来细看：这两个象里只要有一个遇到月破或旬空，官员就得不到这个缺，吏就不能参这个房。世爻即使不空，如果被发动的应爻、或发动的兄弟克了世爻，一定有同僚来抢夺，要提防兄弟兴旺、竞争激烈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For an official selected to a vacancy and a clerk joining a section, take the Pressure line and the Self line as the chief and examine them in detail: if either of these two images falls in Month-break or the ten-day Void, the official cannot obtain the vacancy and the clerk cannot join the section. Even if the Self is not Void, if the Other moves to control the Self, or Peer moves to control the Self, there will surely be colleagues scrambling for it, and one must be on guard against Peer flourishing and the competition being strong.

> [!note] 按
> 「选缺」是候选的官员被选派到一个空缺的官职上；「参房」是吏典进入衙门里的某一房（吏、户、礼、兵、刑、工等房）当差。「月破」是被月建冲破的爻，「旬空」是落在旬空的爻（见术语表）。「同胞」「同袍」都指同辈、同僚，也就是来争这个缺的人（兄弟爻）。「应动克世」书里连写，这里按「应爻发动而克世」读。这一条与黄金策求馆「兄兴临应，决多同类侵谋」（HJC.37）取同一个关系：兄弟发动，有同行来争。
>
> *Note:* 'Xuan que' is a candidate official being appointed to a vacant post, and 'can fang' is a clerk entering one of the sections of a yamen (the Personnel, Revenue, Rites, War, Justice or Works section and so on) to work. 'Month-break' is a line broken by the clash of the Month, and 'ten-day Void' is a line falling on the Void of its ten-day week (see the glossary). 'Tongbao' and 'tongpao' both mean fellows and colleagues, that is the people who compete for the vacancy (the Peer line). 'Ying dong ke shi' is written run together in the book and is read here as 'the Other moves and controls the Self'. This entry takes the same relation as 'if Peer flourishes and rests on the Other, many of one's own kind will scheme to take it' in the Huangjin Ce on seeking a school post (HJC.37): when Peer moves, people in the same line come to compete.
