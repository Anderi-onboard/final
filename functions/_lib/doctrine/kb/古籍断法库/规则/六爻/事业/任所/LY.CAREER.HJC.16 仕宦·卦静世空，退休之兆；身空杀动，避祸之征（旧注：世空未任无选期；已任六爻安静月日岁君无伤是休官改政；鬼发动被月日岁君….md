# 仕宦·卦静世空，退休之兆；身空杀动，避祸之征（旧注：世空未任无选期；已任六爻安静月日岁君无伤是休官改政；鬼发动被月日岁君伤克是避祸脱灾；卦静日冲欲归不放、杀动日合欲避不能）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦静而世爻空亡，是退休的兆头；身爻空亡而煞发动，是躲避祸事的征兆。世爻空亡，没做官的没有选用的日期；已经做官的，如果六爻安静、月建日辰太岁没有伤它，就是休官改任的象；如果官鬼发动、被月建日辰太岁伤克，是躲避祸患、脱离灾难的兆头；卦静而日辰冲，想回家却被留住；煞动而日辰合，想躲避却躲不开。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A still hexagram with the Self Void is a sign of retirement; the Body Void and a killer moving is a sign of avoiding misfortune. When the Self is Void, one not yet in office has no date of selection; for one already in office, if the six lines are still and the Month, the Day and the Tai Sui do not injure it, it is the image of retiring from office and changing post; if Pressure moves and the Month, the Day or the Tai Sui injures or controls it, it is a sign of avoiding misfortune and escaping calamity; if the hexagram is still and the Day clashes, one wants to go home but is not let go; if a killer moves and the Day combines, one wants to escape but cannot.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1958 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 已任世爻空亡，若六爻安静，日月岁君未伤，乃是休官之象； | 已经任职的世爻空亡，如果六爻安静，日月岁君没有伤它，就是休官的象； | If one already in office has the Self Void and the six lines are still and the Day, the Month and the Tai Sui do not injure it, it is the image of resigning from office; |
> | 若动鬼同日月岁君伤克世爻者，如世爻旬空，急宜避之，可免祸也。 | 如果官鬼发动，同日月岁君一起伤克世爻，而世爻旬空的，急忙躲避，可以免去祸患。 | if a moving Pressure together with the Day, the Month or the Tai Sui injures or controls the Self, and the Self is Void in the ten-day cycle, one should quickly avoid it, and the misfortune can be escaped. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「休官改政」是辞去官职、改作别的事；「选期」是官员待选、等候授职的日期；「杀动」的「杀」是官鬼发动成了煞。这一联讲的是世爻空亡的两种相反的象：官静无伤，是平安退休；官动被克，是躲开祸事。旧注多讲了两句「卦静日冲，欲归而不放；杀动日合，欲避而不能」，是说想退却被日辰留住、想躲却被日辰合住，王洪绪没有这两句。
>
> *Note:* 'Retire from office and change post' is resigning the office and doing something else; 'date of selection' is the date on which an officer waiting for a post is chosen and given it; the 'killer' of 'a killer moving' is Pressure moving and turning into a killer. This couplet speaks of two opposite images of a Void Self: Pressure still and unharmed is a peaceful retirement, and Pressure moving and controlled is an escape from misfortune. The old commentary has two further sentences, 'if the hexagram is still and the Day clashes, one wants to go home but is not let go; if a killer moves and the Day combines, one wants to escape but cannot', meaning that one wishes to retire but is held by the Day, or wishes to flee but is combined by the Day, and Wang Hongxu does not have these two.
