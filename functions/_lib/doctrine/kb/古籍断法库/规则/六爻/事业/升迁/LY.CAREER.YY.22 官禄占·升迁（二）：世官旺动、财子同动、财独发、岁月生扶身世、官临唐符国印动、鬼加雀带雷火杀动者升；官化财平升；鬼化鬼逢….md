# 官禄占·升迁（二）：世官旺动、财子同动、财独发、岁月生扶身世、官临唐符国印动、鬼加雀带雷火杀动者升；官化财平升；鬼化鬼逢进神超迁；逢退神调简；归魂官旺静复任；游魂官旺动远升；两父两官旺动鸳鸯求仕

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 升迁的格局：世爻、官星旺相发动的，升；财爻、子孙一起发动的，升；财爻单独发动的，升；太岁、月建生扶身爻、世爻的，升；官星临唐符、国印而发动的，升；官鬼带朱雀、带雷火杀发动的，升；官星变财爻的，平稳而升；官鬼变官鬼遇到进神的，越级升迁；遇到退神的，调到事务简单的缺；归魂卦、官爻旺相安静的，必定复任；游魂卦、官爻旺相发动的，必定升到远处；游魂变游魂的，升了再升，而且在远处；卦里有两个父爻、两个官爻旺相发动的，是鸳鸯求仕。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The patterns of promotion. If the Self and the star of office are thriving and moving, he is promoted; if Wealth and Output move together, he is promoted; if Wealth moves alone, he is promoted; if Tai Sui and the Month generate or support the Body and Self, he is promoted; if the star of office sits on Tangfu or the State Seal and moves, he is promoted; if Pressure with the Bird and the Thunder-Fire spirit moves, he is promoted; if the star of office turns into Wealth, he is promoted smoothly; if Pressure turns into Pressure and meets an Advancing spirit, he is promoted beyond his rank; if it meets a Retreating spirit, he is transferred to an easier post; in a Returning Soul hexagram with the Pressure line thriving and still, he returns to his post; in a Wandering Soul hexagram with the Pressure line thriving and moving, he is promoted far away; if a Wandering Soul turns into a Wandering Soul, he is promoted and then promoted again, in a distant place; if the hexagram has two seal lines and two star-of-office lines thriving and moving, it is 'mandarin ducks seeking office'.

> [!note] 按
> 这一句里有几处书里没有解释：「唐符」「国印」「雷火杀」是神煞；进神后面括号里的甲子、甲午、己卯、己酉是进神的干支：《卜筮全书》写「进神者，甲子、甲午、己卯、己酉」，和这里一致；「调简」按字面是调到简缺，即事务少的职位；「鸳鸯求仕」没有解释，按字面是成双地求官，译文不替它加意思。「复任」是回到原来的职位。
>
> *Note:* A few things in this passage are not explained in the book: 'Tangfu', 'the State Seal' and 'Thunder-Fire' are spirit-stars; the Jiazi, Jiawu, Jimao and Jiyou in brackets after the Advancing spirit are the ganzhi of the Advancing spirit: the Bushi Quanshu writes 'the Advancing spirit is Jiazi, Jiawu, Jimao and Jiyou', in agreement with this; 'tiaojian' is by the letter a transfer to a light post, that is one with few duties; 'mandarin ducks seeking office' has no explanation and by the letter means seeking office in a pair, and the translation does not add a sense to it. 'Furen' is returning to one's former post.
