# 杂职（太医、僧纲、道纪）：所喜惟官旺财备，所戒乃鬼克世空及随墓助伤；凡占杂职皆同此

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太医、僧纲（僧官）、道纪（道官），所喜的只是官旺、财备；所戒的是鬼克世、世空，以及随墓助伤。凡是占杂职，都和这个相同。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For court physicians, Buddhist registrars and Daoist registrars, what is welcome is only thriving Pressure and ready Wealth; what is to be guarded against is Pressure controlling the Self, the Self Void, and following into the tomb and helping the injury. All divinations about miscellaneous posts are the same as this.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这一条以官旺财备为喜，和易林补遗援监纳吏（EL.74）同：僧官、道官、阴阳官、医官宜鬼旺财兴。黄金策仕宦（HJC.27）却以子孙为僧官、道官、医官的用神，父母发动反而有灾悔，王洪绪也这样说。两边取的象不同：易冒、易林补遗按『官职』取象（有官有俸）；黄金策按『行业』取象（僧道医药主子孙）。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This entry takes thriving Pressure and ready Wealth as what is welcome, as does the Yilin Buyi on buying office and entering the clerks (EL.74): Buddhist officials, Daoist officials, officials of yin and yang, and medical officials should have thriving Pressure and rising Wealth. The Huangjin Ce on official careers (HJC.27) instead takes Output as the Subject of monastic, Daoist and medical officials, and has Resource moving cause disaster and regret, and Wang Hongxu says the same. The two sides take different images: the Yimao and the Yilin Buyi take it by the image of the office (an office with salary); the Huangjin Ce takes it by the image of the trade (monks, Daoists and medicine belong to Output).

> [!note] 按
> 「太医」是宫廷、官府的医官，「僧纲」是管理僧众的僧官（僧纲司），「道纪」是管理道士的道官（道纪司）。「杂职」是不入流的小官。「官旺财备」是官爻旺相、财爻齐备（有官有俸）。「随助」是「随墓助伤」的缩写。原文这一句末尾有一个多出来的「）」，这里去掉。书里自注：凡占杂职都按这个。
>
> *Note:* 'Taiyi' is a medical officer of the court or a government office, 'senggang' is the Buddhist official who supervises the monks (the Sengguang bureau), and 'daoji' is the Daoist official who supervises the Daoists (the Daoji bureau). 'Zazhi' is a petty post outside the nine ranks. 'Guanwang caibei' is the Pressure line thriving and the Wealth line complete (an office with salary). 'Suizhu' is short for 'following into the tomb and helping the injury'. The original sentence has an extra closing bracket at its end, which is dropped here. The book's own gloss: all divinations about miscellaneous posts follow this.
