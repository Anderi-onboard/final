# 仕宦升迁：以官爻为主、世象为凭；官职升迁子莫刚，唯求官鬼动为良，值生值旺当迁转，临陷临空且守常；子乃忌神不宜发动，官为主象最利交重，若临生旺定主高升，如值空亡未能迁转

六爻 · 事业 · 升迁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占官职升迁，以官爻为主，以世爻为凭据。子孙是官鬼的忌神，不宜刚旺发动；只求官鬼发动才好。官鬼得生、得旺，就该迁转升调；官鬼落陷、落空，就暂且守着原职、照常。官鬼是主象，最有利的是它发动；如果临生旺，一定主高升；如果遇到空亡，就不能迁转。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining promotion in office, take the Pressure line as the chief and the Self image as the ground. Output is the Adversary of Pressure and must not be strong or move; one asks only that Pressure move, which is good. If Pressure is generated or thriving, one should expect to be promoted and transferred; if Pressure is sunk or Void, for the time being one keeps to the usual and holds one's post. Pressure is the chief image and is most favoured when it moves; if it meets generation or thriving, it surely means a high promotion; if it meets Void, there will be no promotion or transfer.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 762 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占升迁，须评官鬼，子乃忌神，不宜发动，官为主象，最利交重，若临生旺，定主高陛。 | 凡是占升迁，必须评断官鬼：子孙是官鬼的忌神，不宜发动；官鬼是主象，最有利的是它发动；如果临生旺，一定主高升。 | In divining promotion one must judge Pressure: Output is the Adversary of Pressure and ought not to move; Pressure is the chief image and is most favoured when it moves; if it meets generation or thriving, it surely means a high promotion. |
> | 如值空亡，未能迁转。 | 如果遇到空亡，就不能迁转。 | If it meets Void, one cannot be promoted or transferred. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一章把「升」写作「陛」（「高陛」「平陛」「陛迁」等），应是「升」的误写，译文一律按「升」译。「子莫刚」的「刚」是强旺、发动有力。「交重」是卦里发动爻的记号（「重」是老阳、「交」是老阴，都是会变的爻），这里泛指爻发动。「迁转」是官员的升迁调转。「临陷」的「陷」是落入空亡、墓绝一类的凶地，书里没有细说。这一条与黄金策「兴隆官鬼，定居一品之尊」「子若交重，当虑剥官削职」（HJC.02、HJC.03）和增删卜易升选候补（ZS.01、ZS.02）取同一个关系：官鬼为主，子孙为忌。
>
> *Note:* In this chapter 'sheng', to rise, is written 'bi' ('gao bi', 'ping bi', 'bi qian' and so on), which is presumably a miswriting of 'sheng', and the translation reads 'promotion' throughout. The 'gang' of 'zi mo gang' is strong and moving with force. 'Jiaozhong' are the marks of moving lines in a hexagram ('zhong' is old yang and 'jiao' old yin, both lines that change), and here mean a line moving in general. 'Qianzhuan' is the promotion and transfer of officials. The 'xian' of 'lin xian' is a fall into a bad place such as Void, tomb or extinction, and the book does not go into it. This entry takes the same relation as 'a flourishing Pressure settles one in the first rank' and 'if Output moves, beware of being stripped of office' in the Huangjin Ce (HJC.02, HJC.03) and as the Zengshan Buyi on promotion and selection (ZS.01, ZS.02): Pressure is the chief and Output the Adversary.
