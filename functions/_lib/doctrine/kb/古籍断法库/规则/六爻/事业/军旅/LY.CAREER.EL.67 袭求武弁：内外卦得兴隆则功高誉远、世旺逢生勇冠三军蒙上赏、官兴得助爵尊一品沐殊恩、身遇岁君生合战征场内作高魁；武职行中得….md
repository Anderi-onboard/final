# 袭求武弁：内外卦得兴隆则功高誉远、世旺逢生勇冠三军蒙上赏、官兴得助爵尊一品沐殊恩、身遇岁君生合战征场内作高魁；武职行中得内外卦兴隆世应旺相势压旁疆，世旺再逢太岁或日辰月建生扶者勇冠三军

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 武职之中，内卦、外卦都兴旺，功劳就高、名誉就远；世爻旺相又逢生，勇冠三军、蒙受上司的赏赐；官爻兴旺又得帮助，爵位尊到一品、沐浴特殊的恩典；卦身遇到太岁生合，在战场上拔得头魁。内外卦兴旺、世应旺相，威势一定压倒邻近的疆界；世旺再遇太岁，或者日辰、月建生扶的，勇冠三军。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Among military posts, if the inner and outer trigrams are both flourishing, merit is high and fame far; if the Self is thriving and is generated, one's courage tops the three armies and one receives the superior's reward; if the star of office flourishes and gains aid, one's rank is of the first grade and one bathes in special favour; if the Body meets generation or combination from Tai Sui, one becomes the top man in the field of war. With the inner and outer trigrams flourishing and the Self and the Other thriving, one's power will surely overawe the neighbouring borders; if the Self is thriving and also meets Tai Sui, or the Day and the Month generate and support it, one's courage tops the three armies.

> [!note] 按
> 这一首诀有五联，这一条取其中好的三联和解里前两句，坏的两联放在下一条（EL.68）。「身」是卦身（见术语表），「岁君」是太岁。「戈戟」「三军」是军队。「魁」是第一名。「内外卦」是内卦（下卦）和外卦（上卦）。这一条与易隐征战占「世持虎福旺相决胜千里，加日辰生扶百战百胜」（YY.55）是同一类断语。
>
> *Note:* This verse has five couplets; this entry takes the three auspicious ones and the first two sentences of the explanation, and the two ominous couplets are in the next entry (EL.68). 'Shen' is the Body of the hexagram (see the glossary), and 'suijun' is Tai Sui. 'Sanjun' is the armies. 'Kui' is the first place. The 'inner and outer trigrams' are the inner (lower) and outer (upper) trigrams. This entry is the same kind of judgment as 'the Self holds the Tiger and Output, thriving, decides victory a thousand miles away; with the Day generating and supporting it, a hundred battles, a hundred victories' in the Yi Yin war divination (YY.55).
