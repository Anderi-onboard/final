# 仕宦·世动逢空，官居不久（旧注：未任世动必无京官牧守、出巡之职反顺利；已任遇之居官不久，日辰动爻相冲必不久任政事）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻发动又遇空亡：没做官的，卦中世爻发动，必定得不到京官、牧守一类的职位，不过如果是出巡的职位，反而顺利；已经做官的遇到这种情形，居官不久，再遇日辰、动爻相冲，必定不能长久任职理政。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self moves and meets Void: for one not yet in office, with the Self moving in the hexagram, one will surely get no post as a capital official or a governor or magistrate, though if it is a post of touring and inspecting it goes smoothly on the contrary; for one already in office, meeting this means one will not stay long in office, and if the Day or a moving line also clashes it, one will surely not hold the post and govern for long.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1940 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若是出巡之职，世动逢空反利己任； | 如果是出巡的职位，世爻发动逢空反而有利于已经任职的； | If it is a post of touring and inspecting, the Self moving into Void is on the contrary favourable to one already in office; |
> | 遇日辰动爻相冲，必不久任政事。 | 遇到日辰、动爻相冲，必定不能长久任职理政。 | if the Day or a moving line clashes it, one will surely not hold the post and govern for long. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「牧守」是牧民之官，即州郡的长官；「京官」是在京城任职的官。「出巡」是出外巡视的差使（见 LY.CAREER.HJC.23）。这里的两种读法：旧注说出巡的职位「反为顺利」（没分已任未任），王洪绪说「反利己任」，「己」按上下文是「已」的误写，译作「有利于已经任职的」；两家差一点：旧注是出巡这类差使世动逢空都顺，王洪绪限在已经任职的人身上。
>
> *Note:* 'Governor or magistrate' is the officer who shepherds the people, that is, the head of a prefecture or commandery, and 'capital official' is one holding office in the capital. 'Touring and inspecting' is a commission to go out on a tour of inspection (see LY.CAREER.HJC.23). The two commentators read the last point a little differently: the old commentary says that a post of touring 'goes smoothly on the contrary' (without distinguishing already in office from not yet), while Wang Hongxu writes 'is on the contrary favourable to the one in office', where the printed character for 'in office' is a misprint of the similar character 'already' and is translated 'one already in office'; the old commentary says that for this kind of commission the Self moving into Void goes smoothly in general, and Wang Hongxu confines it to the person already in office.
