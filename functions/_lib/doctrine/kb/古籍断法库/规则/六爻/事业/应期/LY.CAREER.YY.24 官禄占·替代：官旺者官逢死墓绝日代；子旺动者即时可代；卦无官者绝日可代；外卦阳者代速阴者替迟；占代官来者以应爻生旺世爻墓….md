# 官禄占·替代：官旺者官逢死墓绝日代；子旺动者即时可代；卦无官者绝日可代；外卦阳者代速阴者替迟；占代官来者以应爻生旺世爻墓绝之日或官爻生旺月日为期

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 被人替代：官星旺相的，到官星遇死、墓、绝的日子被人替代；子孙旺相发动的，立刻就可以被替代；卦里没有官星的，到官星绝的日子可以被替代；外卦是阳的，替代得快，是阴的，替代得慢。占接替自己的官员什么时候来，以应爻生旺、世爻入墓入绝的日子为期，或者以官爻生旺的月、日为期。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Replacement. If the star of office is thriving, he is replaced on the day when it meets death, tomb or extinction; if Output is thriving and moving, he can be replaced at once; if the hexagram has no star of office, he can be replaced on the day of its extinction; if the outer trigram is yang the replacement is quick and if yin, slow. To divine when the official who will take over comes, take as the term the day when the Other is born or thriving and the Self goes into tomb or extinction, or the month or day when the Pressure line is born or thriving.

> | 卦无官者，绝日可代。 | 卦里没有官星的，到官星绝的日子可以被替代。 | If the hexagram has no star of office, he can be replaced on the day of its extinction. |
> | 外卦阳者代速，阴者替迟也。 | 外卦是阳的，替代得快；是阴的，替代得慢。 | If the outer trigram is yang the replacement is quick; if it is yin the replacement is slow. |
> | 占代官来者，以应爻生旺世爻墓绝之日为期，或官爻生旺月日为期也。 | 占接替自己的官员什么时候来，以应爻生旺、世爻入墓入绝的日子为期，或者以官爻生旺的月、日为期。 | To divine when the official who will take over comes, take as the term the day when the Other is born or thriving and the Self goes into tomb or extinction, or the month or day when the Pressure line is born or thriving. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 最后一句「以应爻生旺世爻墓绝之日为期」可以读成应爻生旺、世爻墓绝两件事同日，也可以读成两个日子，书里没有断开，译文保留原样。
>
> *Note:* In the last sentence, 'the day when the Other is born or thriving and the Self goes into tomb or extinction' can be read either as two events falling on the same day or as two alternative days; the book does not separate them, and the translation keeps it as written.
