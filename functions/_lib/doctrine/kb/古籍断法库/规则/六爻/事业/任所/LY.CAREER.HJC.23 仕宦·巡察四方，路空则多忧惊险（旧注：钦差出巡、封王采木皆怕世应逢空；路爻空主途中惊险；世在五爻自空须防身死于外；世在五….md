# 仕宦·巡察四方，路空则多忧惊险（旧注：钦差出巡、封王采木皆怕世应逢空；路爻空主途中惊险；世在五爻自空须防身死于外；世在五爻动及游魂卦世动是出巡之职）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 钦差出巡，或者奉命去封王、采木，都怕世爻、应爻遇空；如果是路爻空亡的卦，主途中惊险；世爻在五爻而自己空亡，须提防死在外面，有冲克就不会这样；世在五爻发动，以及游魂卦世爻发动的，都是出巡的职位。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For one sent by imperial commission on a tour, or to invest a prince or to gather timber, a Void Self or Other is much feared; if it is a hexagram with the road line Void, there will be danger and fright on the way; if the Self is on the fifth line and Void by itself, beware of dying abroad, though with a clash or control it is not so; and when the Self on the fifth line moves, or the Self moves in a Wandering-Soul hexagram, it is a post of touring.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1972 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 钦差出巡，怕世爻逢空。 | 钦差出巡，怕世爻遇空。 | For one sent on an imperial commission on a tour, a Void Self is feared. |
> | 若世在五爻空，须防日月刑克，恐途中有患难莫测之祸害。 | 如果世爻在五爻而空亡，要提防日月刑克，恐怕途中有难以预料的祸患。 | If the Self is on the fifth line and Void, beware of punishment or control by the Day or the Month, lest on the way there be a misfortune that cannot be foreseen. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「钦差」是皇帝派出的差使，「封王」是奉命去册封藩王，「采木」是奉命去采办皇家用的木材，这些都是远行的差使。「路爻」书里这里没有解释。「游魂卦」是八宫卦里第七个卦（游魂）。第五爻在这里像是「出外」的位置，所以世在五爻是出巡的象，这和 LY.CAREER.HJC.08（世动逢空，出巡之职反为顺利）说的出巡不同：那里是世动逢空对出巡有利，这里是世应逢空怕出事，两条一起看：官位上世动逢空，出巡一类的差使反而顺；但差使走在路上时，世应逢空、路爻空要防惊险。
>
> *Note:* 'Imperial commission' is a mission sent by the emperor, 'to invest a prince' is going on the emperor's order to confer the title on a feudal prince, and 'to gather timber' is going on order to procure timber for the court, all of them missions to a far place. The book does not explain 'the road line' here. A 'Wandering-Soul hexagram' is the seventh hexagram of the eight palaces (the Wandering Soul). The fifth line here looks like the place of 'going out', so the Self on the fifth line is a sign of touring, which differs from what LY.CAREER.HJC.08 ('the Self moves and meets Void: for a post of touring it goes smoothly on the contrary') says of touring: there the Self moving into Void favours touring, and here the Self and the Other going Void is feared as bringing trouble; the two are to be read together: in a matter of rank, the Self moving into Void is on the contrary smooth for a mission of touring, but when the mission is on the road, a Void Self and Other or a Void road line must be guarded against for danger.
