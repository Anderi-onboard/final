# 金爻带鬼一方有兵革之兴、土位逢凶四海尽干戈之难：大杀临金鬼旺动必兴兵革，土鬼带杀发动旺相四海皆乱；有杀无鬼克世亦凶、有鬼无杀虽凶弗咎；有神制杀鬼动无妨；鬼空杀旺纵乱无成、鬼旺杀空虽凶无害；鬼带劫杀或化兄弟为劫财之贼、加玄武为阴私暗害之贼；蛇虎不动不为急迫；以生旺克害何方断方向

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大杀落在金鬼上、旺而发动，必起兵革，世去克它大概无大事，它克世必凶；土鬼带杀、发动旺相，四海皆乱，克世必有攻城的贼寇。卦中有杀无鬼，克世也凶；有鬼无杀，虽凶无咎；有神制住杀，鬼动也无妨；鬼位休囚，财兴有害；鬼空杀旺，纵乱也成不了；鬼旺杀空，虽凶无害。鬼带劫杀、或化为兄弟，不过是劫财的盗贼；加玄武，是暗中阴私加害的贼；卦中螣蛇、白虎不动，纵有大杀也不急迫；若官鬼和恶杀一起发动，实在是大害；再看生旺克害落在哪个方位，才可决断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Metal Pressure line carrying a baleful spirit: war breaks out in one quarter; an Earth place meeting ill: weapons and spears everywhere under heaven. If the Great Baleful Spirit stands on a Metal Pressure line that is strong and moving, war is sure to break out; if the Self controls it there is probably no great matter, and if it controls the Self it is surely bad. If an Earth Pressure line carries a baleful spirit and is strong and moving, the four seas are all in chaos, and controlling the Self there will surely be bandits assaulting cities. If the hexagram has a baleful spirit but no Pressure, controlling the Self is also bad; with Pressure but no baleful spirit, though bad it brings no fault; if a spirit restrains the baleful spirit, a moving Pressure line does no harm; if the Pressure place is at rest or trapped, a thriving Wealth line does harm; with Pressure void and the baleful spirit strong, even a revolt does not succeed; with Pressure strong and the baleful spirit void, though bad it does no harm. If Pressure carries the Robbery Killing, or turns into Peer, it is no more than robbers who rob for wealth; if the Dark Tortoise is added, they are secret and covert harming bandits; if the Snake and the Tiger in the hexagram do not move, even with the Great Baleful Spirit it is not urgent; if Pressure and a vicious baleful spirit move together it is truly a great harm; then see in which direction the generation, strength, control and harm fall, and only then can one decide.
