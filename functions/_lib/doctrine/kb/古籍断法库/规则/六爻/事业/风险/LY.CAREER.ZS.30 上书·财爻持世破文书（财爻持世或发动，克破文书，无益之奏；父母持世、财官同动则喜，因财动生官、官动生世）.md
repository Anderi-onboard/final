# 上书·财爻持世破文书（财爻持世或发动，克破文书，无益之奏；父母持世、财官同动则喜，因财动生官、官动生世）

六爻 · 事业 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻持世或发动，会克破文书，是没有益处的奏章；但如果父母持世，财和官一起发动，又是好的，因为财动生官、官动生世。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Wealth line holds the Self or moves, it controls and breaks the document, and the memorial is of no use; but if Resource holds the Self and Wealth and the star of office move together, that is good again, because Wealth moves to generate the star of office and the star of office moves to generate the Self.
