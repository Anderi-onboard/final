# 朝廷占·卜世卜年（易学主青）：天子自占以世为主，世爻动变则从世爻数到变爻定世与年，卦变世不动则以正变二世爻数之，六爻安静则从所卜之年数至世爻，臣民代占以五爻为主；附古占验——全寅占英宗北狩复位、陈搏对宋太祖享国长短

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占国家传几世、延续几年（易学主青）：天子自己占，以世爻为主。世爻发动变出的，就从世爻数到变爻，来定他传几世、几年：比如纯乾卦世爻壬戌发动，变成泽天夬卦的丁未爻，就从壬戌数到丁未，是四十六世，年数就是四十六年。卦变了、世爻不动的，就用本卦和变卦这两个世爻来数：比如乾变姤，从壬戌数到辛丑世爻，是四十世，年数就是四十年。六爻安静的，就从占卜那一年数到世爻：比如庚寅年占，从庚寅数到壬戌，是三十三世，年数就是三十三年。臣子百姓代占的，以五爻为主：比如甲子年占得乾卦，五爻是壬申，从甲子数到壬申，占世是九世，占年是九年。其余照这样推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining how many reigns or years the dynasty will last (Yixue Zhuqing). When the Son of Heaven divines for himself, take the Self as the chief. If the Self line moves and changes, count from the Self line to the changed line to fix the number of reigns and years: for example, in pure Qian the Self line Renxu moves and turns into the Dingwei line of Guai (Marsh over Heaven); count from Renxu to Dingwei, and it is forty-six reigns, and the years are forty-six. If the hexagram changes but the Self does not move, count between the two Self lines of the original and the changed hexagram: Qian changing into Gou, from Renxu to the Xinchou Self line, is forty reigns and forty years. If all six lines are still, count from the year of the divination to the Self line: divining in a Gengyin year, from Gengyin to Renxu is thirty-three reigns and thirty-three years. When ministers and commoners divine on his behalf, take the fifth line as the chief: divining in a Jiazi year and getting Qian, the fifth line is Renshen, and from Jiazi to Renshen is nine reigns and nine years. The rest follow this.

> [!note] 按
> 前面十二句是易学主青讲的数法，四个数字例子都含头含尾地数过六十甲子：壬戌到丁未是 46，壬戌到辛丑是 40，庚寅到壬戌是 33，甲子到壬申是 9，和书里的数一致。第三句「则日世爻数至变爻」的「日」疑是「自」之误，译文按「自」译。「世」在这里指一代君主，「年」是延续的年数。后面两段是《易隐》「附古占验」里的两个故事，放在最相关的这一条下。第一例讲明朝正统己巳年（按通行的史实是正统十四年）英宗在土木堡被俘（「北狩」是对被俘出走的委婉说法）、庚午年被放回（景泰元年）、丁丑年夺门复辟（天顺元年），占断与年份对得上；句中的「或跃」「飞龙」出自乾卦爻辞（九四「或跃在渊」、九五「飞龙在天」）。第二例是宋太祖问陈搏的故事：占断一直讲到三百年后的庚申年，书里没有给出处，是传说式的长篇预言，这里只当书中记的例子收，不当作断法的依据。「（太祖讳灵）」这个括号小注与宋太祖的名字对不上，疑是后人加的，译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* The first twelve sentences are the counting method of Yixue Zhuqing; all four numerical examples were counted along the sixty-year cycle including both ends: Renxu to Dingwei is 46, Renxu to Xinchou is 40, Gengyin to Renxu is 33, and Jiazi to Renshen is 9, which agree with the book's numbers. In the third sentence the character 'ri' of 'ze ri shi yao' is presumably a miswriting of 'zi', from, and the translation follows 'from'. 'Reign' here means one sovereign's generation, and 'year' is the number of years of continuance. The two stories after it are two of the 'ancient verified divinations' appended in the Yi Yin, and are placed under the entry they illustrate most. The first concerns the Ming Emperor Yingzong, captured at Tumu in the Jisi year of Zhengtong (by the usual history the fourteenth year of the era) ('northern hunt' is the euphemism for being captured and led away), released in the Gengwu year (the first year of Jingtai), and restored by the seizing of the gate in the Dingchou year (the first year of Tianshun), and the judgment fits the years; 'perhaps leaping' and 'the flying dragon' come from the line texts of Qian (nine in the fourth place, 'perhaps leaping in the abyss', and nine in the fifth place, 'the flying dragon in heaven'). The second is the story of Taizu of the Song questioning Chen Tuan: the judgment runs on until the Gengshen year three hundred years later, the book gives no source, and it is a legend-like long prophecy, taken in here only as an example the book records and not as a basis for judging. The bracketed note 'the name of Taizu is Ling' does not match the name of the Song Taizu and is presumably added by a later hand, and the translation follows the letter.
