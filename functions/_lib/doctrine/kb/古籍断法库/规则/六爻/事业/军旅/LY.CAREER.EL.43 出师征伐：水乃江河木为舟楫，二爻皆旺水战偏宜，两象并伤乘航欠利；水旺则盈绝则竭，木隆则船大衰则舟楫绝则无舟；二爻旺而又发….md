# 出师征伐：水乃江河木为舟楫，二爻皆旺水战偏宜，两象并伤乘航欠利；水旺则盈绝则竭，木隆则船大衰则舟楫绝则无舟；二爻旺而又发万事齐备利用操舟，衰而又绝难以觅渡

六爻 · 事业 · 军旅 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水是江河，木是舟楫；两爻都旺相，水战特别适宜；两象都受伤，乘船渡水不利。水是江海的源流，木是舟船的根本，水战各有各的用处：水旺则满，水绝则竭；木旺则船大，木衰则只有小舟，木绝则没有船；两爻旺相又发动，万事齐备，利于操舟；如果两爻衰弱又绝，一切都不成，难以找到渡口了！
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Water is rivers and Wood is boats and oars; if both lines are thriving, a naval battle is especially fit; if both images are injured, going by boat is unfavourable. Water is the source and flow of rivers and seas, and Wood is the root of boats, each having its use in a naval battle: thriving Water is full, extinct Water dries up; thriving Wood means big boats, weak Wood only small boats, extinct Wood no boat; if both lines are thriving and also move, everything is ready and it is favourable to handle boats; if both lines are weak and also extinct, nothing succeeds and it is hard to find a crossing!

> [!note] 按
> 「雨像并伤」的「雨像」是「二象」（水、木两象）之误。这一条与易隐征战占「五行」里的木（舟）、水（泉）（YY.63、YY.67）取象相同，但这里只说水木旺衰与水战的关系，没有船的各部分。
>
> *Note:* 'Yu xiang bing shang' has 'yu xiang' for 'er xiang', the two images (Water and Wood). This entry takes the same images as Wood (boats) and Water (springs) in the 'five elements' section of the Yi Yin war divination (YY.63, YY.67), but speaks only of the strength of Water and Wood in relation to naval battle, without the parts of the boat.
