# 五爻为天子，近亲贤而远去奸邪：占国家以本宫为国、世为君，又云五爻为天子；带财福青龙贵人为仁君，临太岁月建勾陈为守正之君，加大杀白虎为暴虐之君，带咸池为淫乱之君，加劫杀居位不久；与吉神相生合必亲贤，动克凶神必远小人；与凶杀相生合则奸佞相辅

六爻 · 事业 · 国朝 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占国家，以本宫为国、世为君；又说五爻是天子。五爻带财、福、青龙、贵人的，是仁君；临太岁、月建、勾陈的，威震天下，是守正之君；加大杀、白虎的，是暴虐之君；带咸池的，是淫乱之君；加劫杀的，在位不久。五爻与吉神相生相合，必能亲近贤者；吉神来克它，必有贤臣辅佐；它动而克凶神，必能远小人、去奸邪。若与凶杀相生相合，或凶杀发动来克，辅佐的宰相就不得其人，都是奸佞在辅佐，君爻纵然遇上吉神，也不能去掉奸佞。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for the state, the palace of the hexagram is the state and the Self is the lord; it is also said that the fifth line is the Son of Heaven. A fifth line carrying Wealth, Output, the Azure Dragon or the Noble is a benevolent lord; on Tai Sui, the Month or Hook Earth it shakes the world and is a lord who keeps to the right; with the Great Baleful Spirit or the White Tiger it is a tyrant; with Xianchi, a debauched lord; with the Robbery Killing, a lord who will not hold his seat long. If it is generated or combined by auspicious spirits it will surely draw near the worthy; if an auspicious spirit comes to control it, there will be worthy ministers to assist; if it moves and controls a baleful spirit it will surely keep the petty at a distance and remove the wicked. If it is generated or combined by baleful spirits, or baleful spirits move to control it, the chancellors are the wrong men and all are flatterers assisting, and even if the lord's line meets an auspicious spirit it cannot remove the flatterers.
