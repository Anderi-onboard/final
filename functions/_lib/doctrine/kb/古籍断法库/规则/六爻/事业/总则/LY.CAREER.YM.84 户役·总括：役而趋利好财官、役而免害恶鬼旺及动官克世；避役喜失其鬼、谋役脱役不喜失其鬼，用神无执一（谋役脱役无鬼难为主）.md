# 户役·总括：役而趋利好财官、役而免害恶鬼旺及动官克世；避役喜失其鬼、谋役脱役不喜失其鬼，用神无执一（谋役脱役无鬼难为主）

六爻 · 事业 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大抵当役而求利的，喜财、官；当役而求免害的，厌鬼旺，以及官动、官克世。避役的喜欢失去鬼，谋役、脱役的却不喜欢失去鬼。用神不要死守一个爻。谋役、脱役，没有鬼就难以做主。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Broadly, for one who serves a duty to seek profit, Wealth and Pressure are welcome; for one who serves a duty and seeks to escape harm, Pressure thriving is hated, and so are Pressure moving and Pressure controlling the Self. In avoiding a duty it is welcome for Pressure to be lost; in seeking a duty or being freed from one it is not welcome for Pressure to be lost. The Subject is not held to one line. In seeking a duty or being freed from one, without Pressure it is hard to take charge.

> [!note] 按
> 「趋利好财官」是求利的，喜财、官；「免害恶鬼旺」是躲祸的，厌官鬼旺；所以同样是役，求役和躲役对官鬼的取舍相反。「避役而喜失其鬼」是避役时官鬼不上卦或落空是好事；「谋役脱役而不喜失其鬼」是谋役、脱役时要靠官爻做主，没有官爻就办不成（书里自注：无鬼难为主）。「用神无执一」是用神不能死守一个爻，要按所问的是求还是躲来取：这是整章（户役、丁产）的总纲。与易林补遗户役各节（EL.109–116）对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Qu li hao cai guan' is, for those seeking profit, Wealth and Pressure being welcome; 'mian hai wu gui wang' is, for those escaping trouble, Pressure thriving being hated; so for the same duty, seeking it and avoiding it take opposite views of Pressure. 'In avoiding a duty it is welcome for Pressure to be lost' is that when avoiding a duty, Pressure absent from the hexagram or Void is a good thing; 'in seeking a duty or being freed from one it is not welcome for Pressure to be lost' is that in seeking a duty or being freed from one the Pressure line must take charge, and without it nothing can be done (the book's own gloss: without Pressure it is hard to take charge). 'The Subject is not held to one' is that the Subject cannot be held to one line but is taken according to whether the question is seeking or avoiding: this is the general principle of the whole chapter (service, and households and holdings). Read against the sections of the Yilin Buyi on service (EL.109-116).
