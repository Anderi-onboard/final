# 升何时：值月为法，逢生旺则会推、遇扶助则高擢；再筮是期世实官崇；两官一强一弱以弱者值月为期，一官必求值月，长生临官有气亦可升、生助之月亦然，而以值月为最、长生次之

六爻 · 事业 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 它什么时候升？以官爻自己的月份（值月）为法：逢生旺就会被会推（联名推举），遇扶助就高升。再占确切的日期：世爻实、官爻崇旺，就不是别的日子，就是那一天。如果有两个官爻、一强一弱，那个弱的值月就升，例如亥月壬戌日占调迁得丰卦，到季冬才升。只有一个官爻，一定要求它值月，可以占验；官在长生、临官而有气，也可以升迁，生它、助它的月份也一样，但都不如值月最好，长生次之。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When will the promotion come? The rule is to take the month in which the Pressure line is on duty (its own month): meeting generation and thriving, one is put forward by joint recommendation; meeting support, one is raised high. To divine again for the exact date: when the Self is solid and Pressure exalted, it is no other day, but that very one. If there are two Pressure lines, one strong and one weak, the promotion comes when the weak one meets its month; for example in a Hai month on a Ren-Xu day, divining about a transfer, one got Feng, and the promotion came only in the last month of winter. With a single Pressure line one must seek its own month, and it can then be tested; if it is at Birth or Taking Office with force, one can also be promoted, and the months that generate and support it are the same, but none is as good as its own month, with Birth second.

> [!note] 按
> 「值月」是官爻的地支正是某个月的地支（如官是丑，丑月就是它的值月），一般解作『这个爻当值的月份』。「会推」是会同推举（明清高级官员由大臣联名推举），「高擢」是高升。「是期」是确切的期日，书里把『升何时』定到月、『是期』定到日。「世实官崇」按文意：世爻不空（实）、官爻崇旺，到了这一天就应。书里自注的例子：亥月壬戌日，得丰卦，丰卦里有戌、丑两个官爻；弱的丑官值月（季冬十二月）才升。「长生」「临官」是十二长生的位置。和易林补遗仕宦升迁的「欲知何月官升」（EL.61）、易隐升迁的应期（YY.08）可对着看。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhiyue' is a month whose branch is the very branch of the line (if the Pressure line is Chou, the Chou month is its own month), usually explained as 'the month in which this line is on duty'. 'Huitui' is joint recommendation (under the Ming and Qing, senior officials were put forward by senior ministers acting together), and 'gaozhuo' is being raised high. 'Shiqi' is the exact date: the book fixes 'when the promotion comes' to the month and 'the exact date' to the day. 'The Self solid and Pressure exalted', by the sense: the Self line not Void (solid) and Pressure exalted and thriving, so that on that day it comes about. The book's own example: a Hai month and a Ren-Xu day, getting Feng, which has two Pressure lines, Xu and Chou; the weak Chou line was promoted when it met its month, the last month of winter, the twelfth. Birth and Taking Office are places in the twelve stages of life. Compare 'to know in which month the official is promoted' in the Yilin Buyi on promotion (EL.61) and the timing in the Yi Yin on promotion (YY.08).
