# 求馆·鬼化财生，非讼则学金休矣（旧注：卦中无财而兄鬼文书乱动有化出财爻生合世爻者，必须讼诉公庭然后可得束修；官爻独发生世合世亦然）

六爻 · 事业 · 任所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有财，而兄弟、官鬼、文书乱动，有化出财爻来生合世爻的，必须到公堂上打官司，然后才能得到束修；官爻单独发动、生世合世，也是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If there is no Wealth in the hexagram but Peer, Pressure and Resource move wildly and one of them turns into a Wealth that generates or combines with the Self, one must go to the court and bring a suit before the fee can be had; Pressure moving alone to generate or combine with the Self is likewise so.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼化财生，非讼则学金休矣； | 官鬼化出财爻来生，不是打官司，学金就算休了； | When Pressure turns into Wealth that generates the Self, unless there is a lawsuit the tuition money is finished; |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2796 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦中无财，而遇兄、鬼、文书化出财爻生合世爻者，必须讼诉公庭，束修可有。 | 卦里没有财，而遇到兄弟、官鬼、文书化出财爻生合世爻的，必须到公堂上打官司，束修才有。 | If there is no Wealth in the hexagram but Peer, Pressure or Resource turns into a Wealth that generates or combines with the Self, one must go to the court and bring a suit before there is a fee. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「学金」是学费、束修。官鬼是官司、公门，所以束修要靠打官司才能到手。经文的「非讼则学金休矣」是说不告到公堂，束修就算了。
>
> *Note:* 'Tuition money' is the fee. Pressure is lawsuits and the law-court, so the fee has to be got by a suit. The verse's 'unless there is a lawsuit the tuition money is finished' says that unless one goes to law the fee is lost.
