# 治筑·差委雇工：凡筮差委雇工皆以应为用，应旺实则有成、克世则无良、六冲则难成

六爻 · 事业 · 差役 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是占差委、雇工，都以应为用：应旺实，就有成；克世，就不良善；六冲，就难成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In all divinations about commissioned workmen and hired labour, the Other is the Subject: with the Other thriving and solid it comes off; controlling the Self, it is not good; a six-clash, it is hard to accomplish.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「差委」是被官府差遣、委派的人工，「雇工」是出钱雇来的工。以应为用，是因为这两种都是『别人替我（或替官府）做事』，做事的人是对方。「应旺实」是应爻旺相又不空，「克世则无良」是应克世就不是良善。「六冲」按本书的说法是冲散、不成。这一条和本卷收雇章的雇倩人工（YM.72）是同一个断法：雇工以应为用神，应破绝、空散、三冲、克世都不成。
>
> *Note:* 'Chaiwei' is workmen dispatched and commissioned by the government, and 'gugong' is labour hired for pay. The Other is taken as the Subject because in both cases it is 'another person doing the work for me (or for the government)', and the one who does it is the other side. 'The Other thriving and solid' is the Other line thriving and not Void, and 'controlling the Self, it is not good' is the Other controlling the Self and not being good. A 'six-clash', in the book's sense, is scattering and failure. This entry is the same judgment as hiring labour in the chapter on taking on and hiring in this book (YM.72): hired labour takes the Other as the Subject, and the Other broken or extinct, Void or scattered, the three clashes and controlling the Self all do not come off.
