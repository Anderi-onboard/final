# 官禄占·鬼谷分爻：执政、朝仕、监司、长官、曹官、吏人，看所占是何官职，就在对应的爻上取用、断吉凶

六爻 · 事业 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断官职用鬼谷分爻：六爻各配一类官，依次是执政、朝仕、监司、长官、曹官、吏人；看所占的是哪一类官，就在对应的那一爻上取用，来断吉凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To judge an office use Guigu's division of the lines: the six lines are matched with six kinds of official, in order the ruling minister, the court official, the supervising commissioner, the head of an office, the departmental officer and the clerk; see which kind of official is being asked about, take the matching line, and judge good or ill from it.

> | 但看所占是何官职，各于分爻取用，断其吉凶也。 | 只看所占的是什么官职，就在对应的爻上取用，来断吉凶。 | Just see what office is being asked about, take the matching line for it, and judge good or ill from that. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 表里六个名目没有写各是哪一爻；按这部书别处的鬼谷分爻表（如疾病占、贸易占）都是从上爻写到初爻，这里也照从上到下读，即上爻执政、五爻朝仕、四爻监司、三爻长官、二爻曹官、初爻吏人。这是读法，不是书里明写的。
>
> *Note:* The table does not say which line each of the six names belongs to. The other Guigu tables of this book (for illness and for trade) are written from the top line down to the first, and this one is read the same way: the top line the ruling minister, the fifth the court official, the fourth the supervising commissioner, the third the head of an office, the second the departmental officer and the first the clerk. This is a way of reading it and not something the book states.
