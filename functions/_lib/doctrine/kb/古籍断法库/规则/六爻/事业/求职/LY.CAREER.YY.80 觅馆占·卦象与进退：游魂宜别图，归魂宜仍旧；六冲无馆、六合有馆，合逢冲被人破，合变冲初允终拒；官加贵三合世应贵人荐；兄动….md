# 觅馆占·卦象与进退：游魂宜别图，归魂宜仍旧；六冲无馆、六合有馆，合逢冲被人破，合变冲初允终拒；官加贵三合世应贵人荐；兄动克世有人争夺；财动化兄荐人分修；世动化鬼央人转荐；鬼动化兄先索谢仪；子合逢冲聚而复散；子衰得助徒少后增

六爻 · 事业 · 求职 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 觅馆，卦象和进退：游魂卦，宜另作打算；归魂卦，宜仍留旧馆；六冲卦没有馆，六合卦有馆；六合卦遇冲的，被人破坏；六合变六冲的，起初应承、最后拒绝。官星加贵人三合世爻、应爻的，有贵人推荐；兄弟发动克世爻的，有人争夺；财爻发动变兄弟的，推荐的人要分走束修；世爻发动变官鬼的，要央求别人转荐；官鬼发动变兄弟的，推荐人先索要谢礼；子孙爻合而遇冲的，学生聚了又散；子孙衰弱而得到帮助的，学生起初少、后来增多。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking a teaching post, the hexagram and advance or retreat. In a Wandering Soul hexagram it is fit to look elsewhere; in a Returning Soul hexagram, to stay in the old post; a six-clash hexagram has no post and a six-combine hexagram has one; a six-combine meeting a clash is broken by someone; a six-combine changing into a clash is agreed at first and refused in the end. If the star of office with the Noble forms a three-harmony with the Self and Other, a patron recommends; if Peer moves to control the Self, someone contends for the post; if Wealth moves and turns into Peer, the one who recommends takes a share of the fee; if the Self moves and turns into Pressure, one must ask another to pass it on; if Pressure moves and turns into Peer, the recommender first demands a gift of thanks; if Output combines and meets a clash, the pupils gather and then scatter; if Output is weak but helped, the pupils are few at first and grow later.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 第五句「合变冲者，初尤而终拒也」的「尤」疑是「允」，译文按「起初应承」译。「凂人」是央求别人。「合」在这里指六合卦，「合逢冲」是六合卦被日月或动爻冲，「合变冲」是六合卦变成六冲卦。「官加贵三合世应」按字面是官星加贵人，与世、应形成三合。
>
> *Note:* In the fifth sentence the 'you' of 'chu you er zhong ju' is presumably 'yun', agree, and the translation reads 'agreed at first'. 'Mei ren' is begging another. 'Combine' here means a six-combine hexagram: 'combine meeting a clash' is a six-combine hexagram clashed by the Month, Day or a moving line, and 'combine changing into a clash' is a six-combine turning into a six-clash. 'The star of office with the Noble in a three-harmony with the Self and Other' is by the letter the star of office carrying the Noble and forming a three-harmony with the Self and Other.
