# 鬼是土爻要分类：阴土阳土，震巽宫或木化土是树头土，临应冲世或日辰带土鬼冲克是飞来土，日月动变皆带土是五方土

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻是土的要分清是哪一类：阴爻上是阴土，阳爻上是阳土；在震宫、巽宫，或者从木变出来的，是树头（木头）上的土；落在应爻、冲世爻的位置上，或者日辰带着土鬼来冲来克，是飞来的土；日、月、动爻、变爻都带土，是五方的土。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where the ghost is an Earth line, tell which kind: on a yin line, yin earth, on a yang line, yang earth; in the Zhen or Xun palace, or coming from a change from Wood, earth at the head of a tree (wood-head earth); standing on the Other line and clashing the Self line, or with the Day carrying an Earth ghost that clashes or controls, flying earth; if the Day, Month, moving line and changed line all carry Earth, earth of the five directions.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3381 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 土鬼阴爻是阴土，阳爻是阳土，或从木化是木头土，临应冲世是飞来土，若日月动变者，五方土类也。 | 土鬼在阴爻上是阴土，在阳爻上是阳土；从木变出来的是木头土，落在应爻、冲世爻上是飞来土；如果日、月、动爻、变爻都是土，就是五方土这一类。 | An Earth ghost on a yin line is yin earth and on a yang line yang earth; one coming from a change from Wood is wood-head earth; standing on the Other line and clashing the Self line it is flying earth; if the Day, Month, moving and changed lines are all Earth, it is of the kind of earth of the five directions. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全经文写「当分厂类」，「厂」是「厥」字的误刻，卜筮正宗写作「厥类」，译作「哪一类」。旧注说「树头土」，王洪绪说「木头土」，书里没有解释，按字面译。旧注多出「在震巽二宫」这一条。两家都只把土分成了几种，没有说每一种土对应什么事，所以本库只记分类，不替它添意思。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Daquan verse reads 'chang lei', where 'chang' is a mis-cut for 'jue' (as in the Bushi Zhengzong's 'jue lei'), rendered 'which kind'. The old commentary says 'earth at the head of a tree' and Wang Hongxu 'wood-head earth'; neither is explained and both are rendered literally. The old commentary adds 'in the Zhen and Xun palaces'. Both only divide Earth into kinds and do not say what each kind stands for, so the library records the classification and adds no meaning of its own.
