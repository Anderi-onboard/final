# 总断千金赋·最要者身位，喜扶而不喜伤（旧注：身即月卦身；例：困卦身爻在午，兑宫以午火为官鬼，旺则官非，衰则疾病）

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「最要紧的是卦身的位置，喜欢被扶持，不喜欢被伤害。」旧注：身就是月卦身，取法见第一卷；成卦以后，先看卦身出现不出现，和月建、日辰、动爻有没有牵涉，吉凶就显出来了，所以卜易以身为最要紧；例如占得困卦，卦身在午，兑宫以午火为官鬼，官鬼旺就是官非，衰就是疾病。王洪绪：身是月卦身，占事就是事体，占人就是人身，只喜欢生扶拱合，不适宜克害刑冲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'What matters most is the place of the Body: it welcomes support and does not welcome harm.' The old commentary: the Body is the Body of the month's hexagram, with its method in the first volume; after the hexagram is formed, first see whether the Body appears, and whether it bears on the Month, the Day or the moving lines, and good and ill show, which is why the Body is the most important in divining the Changes; for example, if the Kun (Confinement) hexagram is obtained and the Body is at Wu, then in the Dui palace Wu Fire is Pressure, and when Pressure is strong it means official trouble, when weak, illness. Wang Hongxu: the Body is the Body of the month's hexagram; in divining an affair it is the matter itself, in divining a person the person's body; it only welcomes generation, support, flanking and combination and does not suit control, harm, punishment and clash.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1161 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 身即月卦身也，“阳世则从子月起，阴世还从午月生”，其法见《启蒙节要》篇内。 | 身就是月卦身，「阳世从子月起，阴世还从午月生」，它的取法见《启蒙节要》篇里。 | The Body is the Body of the month's hexagram: 'with a yang Self it starts from the Zi month, with a yin Self it again begins from the Wu month'; its method is in the 'Essentials of the Enlightenment' chapter. |
> | 大抵成卦之后，看卦身现与不现，与月建、日辰、动爻有无干涉，则吉凶便知。 | 大致成卦以后，看卦身出现不出现，和月建、日辰、动爻有没有牵涉，吉凶就知道了。 | In general, after the hexagram is formed, look at whether the Body appears and whether it bears on the Month, the Day or the moving lines, and good and ill are known. |

> | 凡占向以身为占事之主，故曰“最要”也。 | 凡是占卜向来以身作为占事的主，所以说「最要」。 | In all divination the Body has always been taken as the master of the matter, and so it is called 'the most important'. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一条里讲到病的只有最后一句「旺则官非，衰则疾病」：卦身恰好落在官鬼上时，官鬼旺主官非（官事），衰主疾病；旧注用困卦做例子（困卦是兑宫卦，兑属金，午火克金，所以午火是官鬼），没有再展开，也没有说这是通则还是只对身爻落在官鬼上才成立。「月卦身」的取法（「阳世从子月起，阴世从午月生」）书里只引了口诀，没有给细则。
>
> *Note:* Only the last line of this entry speaks of illness, 'if strong it means official trouble, if weak it means illness': when the Body happens to fall on Pressure, a strong Pressure means official trouble (official affairs) and a weak one illness; the old commentary takes the Kun (Confinement) hexagram as its example (Kun is a Dui-palace hexagram, Dui belongs to Metal, and Wu Fire controls Metal, so Wu Fire is Pressure), and goes no further, nor does it say whether this is a general rule or holds only when the Body line falls on Pressure. The method of the 'Body of the month's hexagram' ('with a yang Self it starts from the Zi month, with a yin Self from the Wu month') is only given as a rhyme in the book, with no detailed rules.
