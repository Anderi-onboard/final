# 八卦断例·占病逢坎、噬嗑、蛊、离、困、睽、小过、明夷：病凶、病重、件件遭伤、有惊、有忧

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 周易尚占八卦断例里，这八个卦的断辞说病凶险、沉重或有忧：坎「孕病皆凶」，噬嗑「病重讼凶」，蛊「孕吉病凶」，离「病重财轻」，困「宅身孕病件件遭伤」，睽「问病有惊」，小过「官阻病忧」，明夷「占家父病」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the judgments by example of the Zhouyi Shangzhan the line of each of these eight hexagrams says that illness is ominous, heavy or a worry: Kan, 'pregnancy and illness are both ominous'; Shihe, 'illness is heavy and the lawsuit is ill'; Gu, 'pregnancy is auspicious, illness is ominous'; Li, 'illness is heavy and wealth is light'; Kun (Confinement), 'dwelling, person, pregnancy, illness: each one is hurt'; Kui, 'asking about illness: there is a fright'; Xiaoguo, 'office is blocked and illness is a worry'; Mingyi, 'divining the household: the father is ill'.

> [!warning] 打架
> 别的书的占病凶卦名单里，同人、需、剥、节、蒙在内，周易尚占对这几个卦的断辞里却没有讲病：同人是「婚孕皆成、所求皆至」，需是「不宜轻举、所谋不成」，剥是「退则获安、进则不利」，节是「身命不宁」，蒙是「却宜占病」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.06）。阐奥歌章第三首（LY.ILL.CA.03）、第五首（LY.ILL.CA.06）、卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.15）和增删卜易转述的十卦（LY.ILL.ZS.01）都有同人；需、剥、节见于卜筮元龟一段和阐奥歌章；蒙只见于卜筮全书疾病论（LY.ILL.BSQS.35）。没有说的不等于说了好，这几个卦占病吉凶，周易尚占这一行没有表态。反过来，这里的坎、噬嗑、蛊、离、困、睽、小过，别的凶卦名单里大多没有（只有蛊、困在），是周易尚占独有的断语。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Among the other books' lists of ominous hexagrams for illness are Tongren, Xu, Bo, Jie and Meng, yet the lines of the Zhouyi Shangzhan for these hexagrams do not speak of illness: Tongren is 'marriage and pregnancy both succeed, all that is sought arrives', Xu is 'rash moves do not suit, what is planned does not succeed', Bo is 'to withdraw is to gain safety, to advance is unfavourable', Jie is 'the person and fate are unsettled', and Meng is 'but it suits divining illness' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.06). Tongren is in the third (LY.ILL.CA.03) and fifth (LY.ILL.CA.06) poems of the Chan'ao Gezhang, in the passage of the Bushi Yuangui (LY.ILL.DYTJ.15) and in the ten hexagrams reported in the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.01); Xu, Bo and Jie are in the Bushi Yuangui passage and the Chan'ao Gezhang; Meng is only in the Bushi Quanshu's essay on illness (LY.ILL.BSQS.35). Not saying is not saying good: for the verdict on illness of these hexagrams the line of the Zhouyi Shangzhan takes no position. Conversely, of the hexagrams here, Kan, Shihe, Li, Kui and Xiaoguo are for the most part absent from the other lists of ominous hexagrams (only Gu and Kun (Confinement) are in them), so these verdicts are peculiar to the Zhouyi Shangzhan. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这八行里，坎「孕病皆凶」、蛊「孕吉病凶」把病和怀孕并说，困是「宅身孕病」四件并说，都是一句话管几件事。明夷「占孕母忧，占家父病」两句对着读：占怀孕时母亲有忧，占家宅时父亲有病；「占家父病」只有四个字，这个读法是按上一句的格式推的。噬嗑一行的「求财且且」「惟道有者」两处意思不清，「道有者」读来像「有道者」的倒文，译文都照字面，没有改字。噬嗑的头两个词组「噬者啮也」「嗑者合也」都是在解释卦名。「婚寡」译作婚姻孤寡，「孕双」译作怀孕得双胎，都是字面的读法。
>
> *Note:* In these eight lines, Kan, 'pregnancy and illness are both ominous', and Gu, 'pregnancy is auspicious, illness is ominous', speak of illness and pregnancy together, and Kun (Confinement) names 'dwelling, person, pregnancy, illness' together, each a single phrase covering several matters. For Mingyi the two phrases 'divining pregnancy: the mother is in anxiety' and 'divining the household: the father is ill' are read in parallel: in divining pregnancy the mother is anxious, in divining the household the father is ill; 'divining the household: the father is ill' is only four characters in the book, and this reading is inferred from the pattern of the previous phrase. In the line of Shihe, 'seeking wealth: barely so' and 'only those who have the Way' are unclear in sense, and the second looks like an inverted form of 'those who have the Way'; both are translated literally and no character has been changed. The first two phrases of Shihe, 'Shi means to bite' and 'He means to close together', both explain the name of the hexagram. 'Marriage is lonely' translates the literal 'marriage widowed' and 'twins' of Li translates the literal 'pregnancy double'.
