# 卦动两孙用药须当间服：有两爻子孙发动，用药不必连服（分权），须多服见效，或用两般汤药相间服之；疮疽肿毒内用托里外用敷贴

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有两爻子孙发动，用药不必连着服，因为它们分了权，要多服才见效；或者用两种汤药，轮流间隔着服就有效。如果占的是疮疽肿毒，可以说内服托里的药，外面敷贴。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When two Output lines move in the hexagram, medicine need not be taken continuously, because they divide the power, and it takes much to be effective; or use two kinds of decoction and take them alternately, and it works. If the divination is about sores, carbuncles and swollen toxin, one can say to use medicine to support the inside and a poultice on the outside.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「托里」是内服药把毒往外托出的疗法，「敷贴」是外面贴敷。疮疽肿毒一句，王洪绪没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Supporting the inside' (tuoli) is the treatment of taking medicine internally to push the toxin outward, and a 'poultice' is a dressing applied outside. Wang Hongxu does not take the sentence about sores, carbuncles and swollen toxin.
