> [!note] 按
> 这是一张表，原书竖排，这个文件里改成横排：表头是初爻到六爻，下面每一行是一种占问（天时、家宅、身命、国事、鬼神、求谋、疾病、六畜……），按位置一格对一爻。疾病一行有两格原文模糊、无法辨认，这里没有补，只收读得出的四格：足（初爻）、腿肢（二爻）、心肺（五爻）、头脑（六爻）；「一格对一爻」是按表里的位置读的，书里没有另写说明。表前的说明（今人加的）说这一表大约是从《鬼谷分爻》辑录来的，那段说明不收。这一行和易隐引鬼谷分爻（LY.ILL.YY.02）、断易天机的鬼谷辨爻法（LY.ILL.DYTJ.01）是同一类说法，「初足、二腿、五心、六头」几家一致，三、四爻各有各的说法，见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.11 的「打架」。
>
> *Note:* This is a table, written vertically in the original and turned horizontal in this file: the head of the table is the first to sixth lines, and each row below is one kind of divination (the weather, the house, the person and life, affairs of state, spirits, seeking and planning, illness, livestock, and so on), one cell to a line by position. In the row for illness two cells are blurred in the original and cannot be made out; they are not filled in here and only the four readable cells are taken: the feet (first line), the legs and limbs (second line), the heart and lungs (fifth line) and the head and brain (sixth line); 'one cell to a line' is read by position in the table, and the book does not say so separately. The explanation before the table (added by a modern hand) says that this table seems to have been collected from 'Guigu's division of the lines', and that explanation is not quoted. This row is of the same kind as the Yi Yin's quotation of Guigu's division of the lines (LY.ILL.YY.02) and the Duanyi Tianji's table under Guigu's name (LY.ILL.DYTJ.01); 'first the feet, second the legs, fifth the heart, sixth the head' is agreed by them, and the third and fourth lines are put differently in each, see the dispute in LY.ILL.DYTJ.11.
