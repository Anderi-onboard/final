# 易卦发蒙歌·克我者为鬼：如金宫见巳午火；凡疾病皆视此爻，知鬼神情状

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 易卦发蒙歌说：克我的是鬼（官鬼）。自注说：凡是疾病，都看这一爻，从它知道鬼神的情状；比如金宫的卦，见到巳、午的火爻就是。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Yigua Fameng Ge says: what controls me is the ghost (Pressure). Its own note adds: in all illness look to this line, and from it know the state of the ghosts and spirits; in a Metal-palace hexagram, for instance, the Fire lines of Si and Wu are it.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「金宫」是属金的卦宫（乾宫、兑宫）；火克金，所以金宫卦里的巳、午火爻是克我者，也就是官鬼（「鬼」是六亲里的官鬼）。「知鬼神情状」只有这五个字，书里没有展开，按字面译；黄金策把官鬼说成得病根由、虚实、安危、鬼神情状都决于它（LY.ILL.HJC.49），说法相近。这首「易卦发蒙歌」每一句后面都有括号小注，书里没有标注者，是这部书自己的小注，不是今人的虎易注，所以这里引。
>
> *Note:* 'Metal palace' is a palace of the Metal phase (the Qian palace and the Dui palace); Fire controls Metal, so the Fire lines of Si and Wu in a Metal-palace hexagram are what controls me, that is, Pressure (the 'ghost' being Pressure among the six relations). 'Know the state of the ghosts and spirits' is only five characters in the book and is not expanded, and is translated literally; the Huangjin Ce says of Pressure that the cause, the strength or weakness, the safety or danger and the state of ghosts and spirits of the illness are all decided by it (LY.ILL.HJC.49), which is close. Every line of this 'Yigua Fameng Ge' has a bracketed small note after it, with no author named in the book, which is the book's own note and not the modern Hu Yi notes, and is therefore quoted.
