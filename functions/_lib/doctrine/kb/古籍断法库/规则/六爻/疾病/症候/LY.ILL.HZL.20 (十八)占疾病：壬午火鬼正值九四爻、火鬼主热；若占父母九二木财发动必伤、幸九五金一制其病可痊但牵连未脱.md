# (十八)占疾病：壬午火鬼正值九四爻、火鬼主热；若占父母九二木财发动必伤、幸九五金一制其病可痊但牵连未脱

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> （十八）占疾病：壬午火鬼，正好在九四爻，火鬼主热。若占父母，九二的木财发动必然伤（克父母），幸好九五的金一制，他的病可以痊愈，只是牵连着还没有脱身。其余照这个类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* (18) Divining illness: the Ren Wu Fire ghost falls exactly on the ninth in the fourth place, and a Fire ghost means heat. If a parent is divined for, the Wood Wealth on the ninth in the second place moving will surely injure (control Resource), but luckily the Metal on the ninth in the fifth place checks it, and his illness can be cured, only it is still tangled and has not yet shaken free. The rest follow by analogy.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是火珠林后面「占卦例」一部分里的一条：那一部分用同一个卦逐项演示（十七占蚕、十八占疾病、十九占姓字……），所以「壬午火鬼」「九四爻」「九二木财」「九五金」指的是那一个卦上的爻（卦的排盘在这一节前面，原书用表格排，文件里排乱了，这里不收）。这一条本身能读出来的规矩是：火鬼主热；占父母病时，财爻发动克父母必伤，若有金爻制住木财，病就可以痊愈，但牵连着还没有脱身。「木财」是属木的财爻，「金一制」是金制木。「（十八）」是原文的编号。和「妻财独发，生鬼伤父；问病难瘳」（LY.ILL.HZL.18）是同一个道理，只是这里多了「有金制财」这一救。
>
> *Note:* This section is one entry in the part of the Huozhu Lin near the end on 'worked applications': that part works through item after item on one and the same hexagram (17 divining silkworms, 18 divining illness, 19 divining a name ...), and so 'Ren Wu Fire ghost', 'the ninth in the fourth place', 'the Wood Wealth on the ninth in the second place' and 'the Metal on the ninth in the fifth place' refer to the lines of that one hexagram (its layout is set out before this section, arranged as a table in the original and scrambled in the file, and is not taken here). What can be read out of this entry as a rule is: a Fire ghost means heat; divining a parent's illness, the Wealth line moving controls Resource and will surely injure, but if a Metal line checks the Wood Wealth the illness can be cured, only it is still tangled and has not shaken free. 'Wood Wealth' is a Wealth line belonging to Wood, and 'Metal checks it' is Metal controlling Wood. The '(18)' is the numbering of the original. It is the same principle as 'Wealth moving alone generates the ghost and injures the father; asking about illness it is hard to cure' (LY.ILL.HZL.18), only with the rescue of 'Metal checking Wealth' added here.
