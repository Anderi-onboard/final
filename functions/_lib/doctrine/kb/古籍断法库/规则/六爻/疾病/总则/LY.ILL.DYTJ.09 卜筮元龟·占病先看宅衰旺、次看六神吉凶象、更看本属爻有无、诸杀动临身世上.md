# 卜筮元龟·占病先看宅衰旺、次看六神吉凶象、更看本属爻有无、诸杀动临身世上

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病先看宅的衰旺，再看六神吉凶的象，还要看本属爻有没有，以及各种杀有没有动着临在身爻和世爻上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness first look at the strength or weakness of the house, then at the good or ill images of the six spirits, and look further at whether the line of its own kind is there and at the killings moving and sitting on the Body and the Self.

## 《断易天机·占疾病第二十四·卜筮元龟》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1676 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Section 24, on illness: Bushi Yuangui", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占病先看宅衰旺（1）， | 占病先看宅的衰旺， | In divining illness first look at the strength or weakness of the house, |
> | 次看六神吉凶象， | 再看六神吉凶的象， | then look at the good or ill images of the six spirits, |
> | 更看本属爻有无（2）， | 还要看本属爻有没有， | and look further at whether the line of its own kind is there, |
> | 诸杀动临身世上。 | 以及各种杀动着、临在身和世上。 | and at the killings moving and sitting on the Body and the Self. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句是卜筮元龟占疾病的总纲，后面几块（DYTJ.10 起）是它展开的几个方面。「宅」在卜筮大全里说是「内卦第二爻为宅」，这里没有解释；「本属爻」大概是病人所属的那一爻（占父母病就看父母爻），原文没有解释。「（1）」「（2）」是今人加的注号，那两条注不收。
>
> *Note:* This line is the general outline of the Bushi Yuangui on divining illness, and the blocks after it (from DYTJ.10) develop several sides of it. The Buyi Daquan says 'the second line of the inner trigram is the house'; here it is not explained. The 'line of its own kind' is probably the line that belongs to the sick person (divining a parent's illness, look at the Resource line), and the original does not explain it. The '(1)' and '(2)' are modern annotation marks and those annotations are not quoted.
