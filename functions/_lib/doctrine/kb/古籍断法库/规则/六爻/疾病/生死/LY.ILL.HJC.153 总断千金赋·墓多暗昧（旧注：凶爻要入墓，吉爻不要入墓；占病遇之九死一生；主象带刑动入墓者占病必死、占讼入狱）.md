# 总断千金赋·墓多暗昧（旧注：凶爻要入墓，吉爻不要入墓；占病遇之九死一生；主象带刑动入墓者占病必死、占讼入狱）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「墓多暗昧。」旧注：大致上凶爻要入墓，吉爻不要入墓；《金锁玄关》有人墓、事墓、鬼墓的分别，《天玄赋》有身、世、命随着鬼入墓的说法，都是大凶的兆头；占病遇到，九死一生；捕获遇到，深深躲藏难找；人物遇到，愚昧不振作；失脱遇到，暗暗藏着看不见；因为墓是滞塞的意思，是暗昧不明的象；卦中主象带着刑、发动、入了墓的，占病必死，占官司入狱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'The tomb brings much obscurity.' The old commentary: in general an ominous line should enter the tomb and a lucky line should not; the Jinsuo Xuanguan distinguishes the person's tomb, the matter's tomb and the ghost's tomb, and the Tianxuan Fu has the saying of the Body, Self and natal line entering the tomb following the ghost, all greatly ominous omens; in divining illness, meeting it is nine deaths to one life; in a capture the quarry hides deep and is hard to find; in a person, he is dull and does not rouse himself; in a loss, it lies hidden and unseen; for the tomb means stagnation, the image of obscurity and unclarity; when the principal image in the hexagram carries punishment, moves and enters the tomb, in divining illness the sick will surely die and in a lawsuit one goes to prison.

> [!note] 按
> 这里的「墓」是十二长生里的墓位（入墓）。「人墓、事墓、鬼墓」是《金锁玄关》的分别，书里没有展开；「身、世、命随鬼入墓」是天玄赋的说法（LY.ILL.TXF.06）和断易天机的「随官入墓」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.27）是同一件事：占病遇到，断易天机写「十占九死」，这里写「九死一生」，两处意思相同。这一条只有「占病」「占讼」两句是断语，「捕获」「人物」「失脱」讲的是别的事，为了保持这一整段完整一并收在这里。王洪绪对这一联的评语是十二长生里只有长生、墓、绝要紧（LY.ILL.HJC.129 收过），这里没有专门的王洪绪段。「愚蒙」按字面是愚昧蒙昧。
>
> *Note:* The 'tomb' here is the Tomb stage of the twelve stages of life (entering the tomb). 'The person's tomb, the matter's tomb, the ghost's tomb' is the Jinsuo Xuanguan's distinction, not expanded in the book; 'the Body, Self and natal line entering the tomb following the ghost' is the Tianxuan Fu's saying (LY.ILL.TXF.06) and is the same thing as 'following Pressure into the tomb' in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.27): for illness the Duanyi Tianji writes 'nine of ten divinations die', here it is 'nine deaths to one life', the same sense. Only the two sentences 'in divining illness' and 'in a lawsuit' are verdicts for our domain; 'a capture', 'a person' and 'a loss' are about other matters, and are kept here to keep the passage whole. Wang Hongxu's comment on this couplet is that of the twelve stages of life only Birth, Tomb and Extinction matter (taken in LY.ILL.HJC.129), and there is no separate Wang Hongxu passage here.
