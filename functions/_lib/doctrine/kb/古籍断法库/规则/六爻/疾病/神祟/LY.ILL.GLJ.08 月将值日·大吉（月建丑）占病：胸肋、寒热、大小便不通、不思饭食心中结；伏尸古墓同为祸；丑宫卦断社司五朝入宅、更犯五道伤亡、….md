# 月将值日·大吉（月建丑）占病：胸肋、寒热、大小便不通、不思饭食心中结；伏尸古墓同为祸；丑宫卦断社司五朝入宅、更犯五道伤亡、退北方凶恶杀、禳谢本命星辰

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在丑、叫大吉的时候占病：胸和肋疼，有时发冷有时发热，大小便都不通，不想吃饭，心里郁结。伏尸和古墓一同作祸，只是家先牵着引着。「丑宫卦断」那一段说：社司、五朝进了宅，要设祭给香火和土地；又冲犯了五道和伤亡鬼，要把北方的凶恶煞退送出去，向本命上的星辰禳解酬谢，化解流年的灾病厄运。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on Chou, named Daji, divining illness: the chest and ribs, now chills and now fever, neither urine nor stool passing, no wish for food, a knot in the heart. The Hidden Corpse and the ancient tomb make trouble together, only that the household ancestors lead them through and receive them. The passage 'the verdict for the Chou-palace hexagrams' says: the Altar Official and the Five Courts have entered the house, and offerings are to be set out to the incense-fire and the Earth God; the Five Ways and the dead by injury have also been offended, the fierce killer of the north is to be driven off, rites and thanks are to be made to the stars of his natal destiny, and the calamity, illness and misfortune of the passing year resolved.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。「伏尸古墓」的「伏尸」书里没有解释，按字面是暗藏的尸；「家先透引接」读作家先牵引着、接应着（那些鬼），是读法；「五朝」不知所指，像是和「十朝」（见 GLJ.04）同类的叫法，不下定义；「小便大便已不通」的「已」读作都；「解释」是化解，不是解说。「本命上星辰」是病人本命（出生年）对应的星辰。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. The book does not explain 'the Hidden Corpse', which literally is a corpse lying in hiding; 'the household ancestors lead them through and receive them' reads the book's 'lead through and receive' as the ancestors leading and meeting those ghosts, which is a reading; what 'the Five Courts' are is not known, and they look like a kind of thing with the 'Ten Courts' (see GLJ.04), and are not defined; the 'neither' of 'neither urine nor stool passes' reads the book's 'already' as 'both'; 'resolve' is the book's 'release', in the sense of undoing, not of explaining. 'The stars of his natal destiny' are the stars answering to the patient's natal year.
