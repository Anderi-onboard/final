# 玄武主阴虚黑缩、勾陈应胀闷黄浮：玄武临官爻或忌神及阴虚之症，防缩浆黑陷；勾陈附鬼爻，主胀闷黄浮

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 玄武主阴虚、痘色发黑收缩，勾陈应验为胀闷、面色发黄浮肿。玄武带官爻，或带忌神，以及阴虚的症候，要防浆水收缩、痘色发黑塌陷；勾陈如果附在鬼爻上，主胀闷、发黄、浮肿。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Dark Tortoise means yin deficiency, blackened and shrunken pox; the Hook Earth responds with swelling, oppression and a yellow, puffy look. If the Dark Tortoise sits on the Pressure line, or on the Adversary, or in a condition of yin deficiency, guard against the fluid shrinking and the pox turning black and sunken; if the Hook Earth clings to the Pressure line, it means swelling, oppression, yellowness and puffiness.
