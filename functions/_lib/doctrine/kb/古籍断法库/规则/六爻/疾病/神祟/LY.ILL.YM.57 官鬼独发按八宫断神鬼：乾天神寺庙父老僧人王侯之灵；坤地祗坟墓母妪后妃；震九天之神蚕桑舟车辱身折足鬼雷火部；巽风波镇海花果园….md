# 官鬼独发按八宫断神鬼：乾天神寺庙父老僧人王侯之灵；坤地祗坟墓母妪后妃；震九天之神蚕桑舟车辱身折足鬼雷火部；巽风波镇海花果园林长妇女；坎北斗北极百川四海；离荧惑香火灶中女；艮山神五岳荒塚童儿；兑释之宗咒咀尸祝师尼酒色

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神和鬼虽有分别，考察的办法却没有两样。所以官在乾宫发动，是触犯了天神，是冲犯了寺庙，是年老父辈、僧人的鬼，是王侯宫殿的灵；在坤宫动，是亵渎了地祇，是妨碍了坟墓，是母亲、老妇人的鬼，是皇后妃子的神；震是东方，是九天的神，是道的宗主，是掌管蚕桑舟车的，是受辱折了脚的鬼，是雷火部的威灵；巽是东南，是掌管风浪、镇守大海的神，是花果园林里作祟的，是长女、妇人的魂；坎是北方，在天是北斗，在地是北极，是百川、四海、恒河的灵，它的广大是这样，天下的水神火神，确实不能改易；离是火德，在星是荧惑，对读书人和百姓是香火，对一家是灶神，对骨肉亲人是中女的鬼；艮是山神，是五岳，是荒坟，以及童子的鬼；兑是西方，是佛家的宗主，是咒骂诅咒的孽，是巫祝的欺诳，是和尚尼姑的鬼，是酒色的邪。注说：这是官鬼独自发动、其他卦爻都静时，用八宫的象来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Spirits and ghosts differ, but the way of examining them is not two. So when the Pressure line moves in Qian, a heavenly spirit has been offended, a temple has been struck against, and it is the ghost of an elder or a monk, the spirit of a prince or marquis and his great halls; moving in Kun, the earthly deity has been profaned and the graves obstructed, and it is the ghost of a mother or an old woman, the spirit of an empress or a consort; Zhen is the east: a spirit of the Nine Heavens, the head of the Way, in charge of silkworms and mulberries, boats and carts, the ghost of one who was disgraced or broke a leg, the awesome spirit of the Thunder and Fire department; Xun is the southeast: a spirit in charge of wind and waves and of pacifying the sea, a haunting of flowers, fruit, gardens and groves, the soul of an eldest daughter or a woman; Kan is the north: in heaven the Northern Dipper, on earth the North Pole, the spirit of the hundred rivers, the four seas and the Ganges, so vast is it, and the spirits of water and fire under heaven are firmly beyond change; Li is the virtue of Fire: among the stars Yinghuo, to gentry and commoners the incense fire, in the household the stove, and among kin the ghost of the middle daughter; Gen is the mountain spirit, the Five Peaks, a wild grave, and the ghost of a boy or child; Dui is the west, the head of the Buddhists: the baleful work of curses, the deceit of impersonator and invoker, the ghost of a monk or nun, the evil of wine and lust. The note says: this is judgment by the images of the eight palaces when the Pressure line moves alone and the other lines are all still.

> [!warning] 打架
> 八宫各配什么鬼神，黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.95）和易冒大体同路，细目不同：乾都是天神一路；坤都有坟墓、地祇；坎、离都是水火之神。不同的地方——艮，黄金策是城隍、宅土，易冒是山神、五岳、荒坟、童子之鬼；巽，黄金策是缢死或颠仆的阴人，易冒是掌管风浪镇海的神、花果园林之祟、长女妇人之魄；兑，黄金策是口愿、伤神，易冒是佛家宗主、咒诅、巫祝之诳、僧尼之鬼、酒色之邪；震，黄金策是树神或杖死的男鬼，易冒是九天之神、蚕桑舟车之司、辱身折足之鬼、雷火部的威灵。两本书都没有说谁对谁错。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What each palace stands for among ghosts and spirits: the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.95) and the Yi Mao run roughly the same way, with differences of detail: in Qian both have the heavenly spirits; in Kun both have graves and the earthly deity; in Kan and Li both have the spirits of water and fire. Where they differ: for Gen, the Huangjin Ce has the City God and the earth of the house, the Yi Mao the mountain spirit, the Five Peaks, a wild grave and the ghost of a boy; for Xun, the Huangjin Ce has a woman who hanged herself or fell in a fit, the Yi Mao a spirit in charge of wind and waves and pacifying the sea, the haunting of flowers, fruit, gardens and groves, and the soul of an eldest daughter or a woman; for Dui, the Huangjin Ce has a spoken vow or an injuring spirit, the Yi Mao the head of the Buddhists, curses, the deceit of impersonators and invokers, the ghost of a monk or nun and the evil of wine and lust; for Zhen, the Huangjin Ce has a tree spirit or a male ghost beaten to death with a staff, the Yi Mao a spirit of the Nine Heavens, one in charge of silkworms, boats and carts, the ghost of one disgraced or with a broken leg, and the awesome spirit of the Thunder and Fire department. Neither book says which is right.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条原文里「官」是官鬼的省写；「干天神」的「干」是触犯。「荧惑」是火星，「九天」是九重天。「师尼」是和尚、尼姑一类的出家人。「盖天下水火之神，确乎其不可易也」承接坎（水）、离（火）两条，意思是水神、火神的名号大体是固定的。这一条与黄金策的八宫（LY.ILL.HJC.95）、易隐的鬼神名目（LY.ILL.YY.28）是同一件事。
>
> *Note:* In the original 'guan' (official) is short for the Pressure line. 'Gan' in 'offended a heavenly spirit' is to offend or infringe. 'Yinghuo' is the planet Mars, and the 'Nine Heavens' are the nine layers of heaven. 'Monks and nuns' covers those who have left the household life. 'The spirits of water and fire under heaven are firmly beyond change' follows the entries on Kan (Water) and Li (Fire) and means that the names of water and fire spirits are more or less fixed. This entry treats the same matter as the Huangjin Ce's eight palaces (LY.ILL.HJC.95) and the Yi Yin's names of spirits and ghosts (LY.ILL.YY.28).
