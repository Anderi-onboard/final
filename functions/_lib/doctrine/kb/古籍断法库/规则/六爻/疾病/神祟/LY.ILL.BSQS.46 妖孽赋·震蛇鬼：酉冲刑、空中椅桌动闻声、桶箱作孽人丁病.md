# 妖孽赋·震蛇鬼：酉冲刑、空中椅桌动闻声、桶箱作孽人丁病

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妖孽赋讲震宫螣蛇带鬼、遇酉冲刑时的怪异：空中的椅子桌子动，听得到声音，踢踏的响声像人在走；大蛇常出现，窑里的器具响得惊人；桶、箱作孽，家里的人生病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Fu on monsters on the oddities when a Coiling Snake carrying a ghost in the Zhen palace is clashed and punished by You: chairs and tables move in the air and sounds are heard, a stamping sound like a person walking; a great snake often appears, and kiln wares clatter alarmingly; casks and chests do harm, and the people of the household fall ill.

> [!note] 按
> 妖孽赋按八卦讲螣蛇带鬼时出的怪异（乾、坎、艮、震、巽、离、坤、兑各一段），大多和病无关，这一段结尾的「桶箱作孽人丁病」明说家里人生病，所以收在这里。「蛇鬼」是螣蛇临在鬼爻上，「酉冲刑」是酉来冲刑（按前面「震」的位置：酉冲卯）。这一篇的标点是整理者（虎易）重新点的，译文按现有标点。
>
> *Note:* The Fu on monsters goes through the trigrams (Qian, Kan, Gen, Zhen, Xun, Li, Kun, Dui, one passage each) and tells the oddities when the Coiling Snake carries a ghost; most have nothing to do with illness, and this passage, whose last clause 'casks and chests do harm and the people of the household fall ill' says outright that people fall ill, is taken here. 'The snake ghost' is the Coiling Snake on the ghost line, and 'You clashing and punishing' is You coming to clash and punish (You clashes Mao, which is Zhen's place). The punctuation of this piece was redone by the modern editor (Hu Yi), and the translation follows the punctuation as it stands.
