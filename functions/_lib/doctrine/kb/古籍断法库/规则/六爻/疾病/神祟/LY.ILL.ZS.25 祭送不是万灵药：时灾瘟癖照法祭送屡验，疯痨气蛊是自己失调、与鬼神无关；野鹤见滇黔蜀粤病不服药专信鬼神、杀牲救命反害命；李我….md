# 祭送不是万灵药：时灾瘟癖照法祭送屡验，疯痨气蛊是自己失调、与鬼神无关；野鹤见滇黔蜀粤病不服药专信鬼神、杀牲救命反害命；李我平驳《易冒》《易林补遗》指定关岳、说不祭降灾是邪神，并说此书疾病章言鬼神是从俗

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 祭送鬼神，对时行灾病、瘟疫、癖积一类的病有用（野鹤多次验过）；疯、痨、气、蛊是自己没有调养好、酒色伤了身，和鬼神没有关系，祭送没有益处，该做的是节制饮食、远离色欲、平息怒气、养护精神。有病不吃药、专靠杀牲祭神的，反而害了性命。李我平批评有的书指定哪位神（关帝、岳王、关圣），又说祭了降福、不祭降灾，那是作威作福的邪神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Sacrificing and sending off the ghosts and spirits is of use for seasonal calamities, epidemics and hard lumps (the Old Man of Yehe verified it many times); madness, consumption, qi disorder and drum-belly come from failing to look after oneself and from wine and lust damaging the body, have nothing to do with ghosts and spirits, and gain nothing from sacrifice, what is to be done being to moderate food, keep away from lust, calm anger and nourish the spirit. Those who take no medicine when ill and rely wholly on slaughtering animals to the gods only harm life. Li Woping criticises books that name a particular god (Emperor Guan, King Yue, the Sage Guan) and say that those who sacrifice are blessed and those who do not are sent calamity, which he calls evil gods who lord it and dispense favour.

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 13205 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 野鹤曰：凡得时灾瘟癖，照此法而祭送者，余屡验之。 | 野鹤说：凡是得的是时行灾病、瘟疫、癖积一类的，照这个办法祭送，我多次验过。 | The Old Man of Yehe says: for illnesses that are seasonal calamities, epidemics or hard lumps, sacrifices and sending-off by this method have proved true many times. |
> | 倘若是疯痨气蛊，既失调于前，酒色伤身，又失慎重于后，与鬼神何干？ | 如果是疯病、痨病、气病、蛊胀，先前没有调养好，酒色伤了身，后来又不谨慎，和鬼神有什么关系？ | But if it is madness, consumption, qi disorder or drum-belly, when he first failed to look after himself and wine and lust damaged his body, and then was again not careful, what has this to do with ghosts and spirits? |
> | 祭之送之皆无益矣。 | 祭他、送他都没有用。 | Sacrificing to them and sending them off is of no use. |
> | 惟节饱食、远色欲、息气养神，其病自减。 | 只有节制饮食、远离色欲、平息怒气、养护精神，病自然会减轻。 | Only by moderating food, keeping away from lust, calming anger and nourishing the spirit does the illness ease of itself. |
> | 余游遍江湖，曾至滇黔蜀粤，见彼病不服药，专信鬼神。 | 我走遍江湖，到过云南、贵州、四川、广东，看到那里的人生病不吃药，专门相信鬼神。 | I have travelled all over the rivers and lakes, to Yunnan, Guizhou, Sichuan and Guangdong, and seen how there the sick take no medicine and put their whole trust in ghosts and spirits. |
> | 先用三牲五牲，后至杀牛宰马，一次不愈，甚至二三十次。 | 先用三牲、五牲，后来杀牛宰马，一次不好，甚至做二三十次。 | First they use three or five kinds of sacrificial animals, then they slaughter oxen and horses, and if once does not cure, they go on even twenty or thirty times. |
> | 因病致穷者多矣。 | 因为生病弄穷的人很多。 | Many are made poor by illness. |
> | 殊不知害彼命而救命，其罪愈彰。 | 殊不知伤害别的生命来救自己的命，罪过更加明显。 | They do not realise that to harm other lives in order to save a life makes the sin all the plainer. |
> | 所以屡见害性命之多者，终不能救而死，可不戒与？ | 所以屡次看到伤害的性命越多的人，最后还是救不了而死，难道不该引以为戒吗？ | So I have often seen that those who harmed the most lives could still not be saved and died in the end; should one not take warning? |
> | 今下路读书人亦信此邪神者，余实不解。 | 现在下游一带的读书人也信这种邪神，我实在不明白。 | Now even scholars of the lower reaches believe in such evil gods, which I truly do not understand. |
> | 神农尝百草，遗救生灵。 | 神农尝百草，是留给后世来救活生灵的。 | Shennong tasted the hundred herbs and left them to save living beings. |
> | 未闻有何圣贤，教人宰牲命以救命也。 | 没听说有哪位圣贤，教人杀牲畜的命来救人的命。 | I have never heard of any sage who taught men to slaughter animals' lives in order to save a life. |

> [!warning] 打架
> 要不要指定是哪位神：《易冒》以鬼临金为关帝、岳王，《易林补遗》以青龙为关圣（李我平点了这两部书）；李我平说这些说法不通，还说「祭者降福、不祭者降灾」是作威作福的邪神。野鹤对祭送的态度是：时灾瘟癖照法祭送灵验，疯痨气蛊与鬼神无关。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether to name a particular god: the Yi Mao takes Pressure on Metal as Emperor Guan and King Yue, and the Yilin Buyi takes the Azure Dragon as the Sage Guan (Li Woping names the two books); Li Woping says this does not hold, and that 'blessing to those who sacrifice, calamity to those who do not' would make evil gods who lord it and dispense favour. The Old Man's attitude to sacrifice is that for seasonal calamities and epidemics sacrifice by the method proves true, and that madness, consumption, qi disorder and drum-belly have nothing to do with ghosts and spirits.

> [!note] 按
> 「时灾瘟癖」是当令的灾病、瘟疫和癖积（腹内结块的病）；「疯痨气蛊」是疯病、痨病（肺结核一类）、气病和蛊胀。「三牲」是牛、羊、猪，「五牲」是再加鸡、犬之类。「下路」在这里指长江下游一带。「乾按」是今人加的话，本库不引。
>
> *Note:* 'Seasonal calamity, epidemic and hard lump' are the calamities in season, pestilence and lumps forming in the belly; 'madness, consumption, qi disorder and drum-belly' are madness, consumption (an illness of the lungs) and disorders of qi and of belly distension. 'Three sacrificial animals' are the ox, sheep and pig and 'five' add the fowl, dog and the like. 'Lower reaches' here means the lower Yangtze. The 'Qian note' is a modern addition and is not quoted here.
