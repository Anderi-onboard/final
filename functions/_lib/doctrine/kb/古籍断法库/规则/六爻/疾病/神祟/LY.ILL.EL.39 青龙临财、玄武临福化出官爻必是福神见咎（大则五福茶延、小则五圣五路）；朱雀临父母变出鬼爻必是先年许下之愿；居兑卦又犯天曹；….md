# 青龙临财、玄武临福化出官爻必是福神见咎（大则五福茶延、小则五圣五路）；朱雀临父母变出鬼爻必是先年许下之愿；居兑卦又犯天曹；判鬼神须审病源轻重，重则大神轻则小祟

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙、财爻、子孙变出官爻，是福神来照应；兑宫、朱雀、文书变成鬼象，是先前许的愿在催。注说：青龙如果临财、玄武临福（子孙），变出官爻，必是福神见咎；福神，大的是五福和茶延，小的是五圣和五路；凡是判断鬼神，必须审察病源的轻重，重的就是大神，轻的就是小祟；再看朱雀临父母变出鬼爻，必是先年许下的愿；如果落在兑卦，又是冒犯了天曹。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon, Wealth and Output changing into a Pressure line: the fortune spirit shines on it; Dui, the Vermilion Bird and the writing image becoming a ghost image: the earlier vow presses. The commentary says: if the Azure Dragon sits on Wealth and the Dark Tortoise on Output (the fortune spirit) and it changes into a Pressure line, it is surely Output (the fortune spirit) showing fault; the fortune spirit, at the great end, is the Five Blessings and the Tea Feast, and at the small end the Five Sages and the Five Roads; in judging ghosts and spirits one must examine the lightness or weight of the source of the illness, the heavy being a great god and the light a small haunting; look further at the Vermilion Bird on the Resource line changing into a Pressure line, which is surely a vow made in a former year; if it sits in the Dui hexagram, it is again an offence against the Heavenly Bureau.

> [!note] 按
> 「福神」在这里是子孙（福神就是子孙）。「福神见咎」是本来带福的六亲（青龙财、玄武福）变出官爻，成了作祟的鬼——所以是福神要求还愿，而不是凶鬼。「大则五福及茶延，小则五圣及五路」是说福神的大小：大神（五福、茶延）和小神（五圣、五路）；判断时按病源轻重取大神或小祟。「先年许下之愿」是以前许过没还的愿，朱雀是口舌，父母是文书誓约，所以是口头许过的愿；与 LY.ILL.EL.35 前一节不同，与易隐鬼崇·旧愿（LY.ILL.YY.32）、黄金策「鬼持世是旧愿未酬」（LY.ILL.HJC.110）是同一件事。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* 'Fortune spirit' here is Output (the fortune spirit is Output). 'The fortune spirit showing fault' is when a relation that would bring fortune (Wealth on the Azure Dragon, Output on the Dark Tortoise) changes into a Pressure line and becomes a haunting ghost — so it is the fortune spirit demanding the redemption of a vow, and not an evil ghost. 'At the great end the Five Blessings and the Tea Feast, at the small end the Five Sages and the Five Roads' speaks of the size of the fortune spirit: great gods (the Five Blessings, the Tea Feast) and small gods (the Five Sages, the Five Roads); in judging one takes a great god or a small haunting by the weight of the source of the illness. 'A vow made in a former year' is an earlier vow made and not redeemed: the Vermilion Bird is talk, and the Resource line is documents and oaths, so it is a vow spoken aloud; it is the same matter as the Yi Yin's 'ghosts and hauntings: old vows' (LY.ILL.YY.32) and the Huangjin Ce's 'a ghost holding the Self is an old vow unpaid' (LY.ILL.HJC.110). Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
