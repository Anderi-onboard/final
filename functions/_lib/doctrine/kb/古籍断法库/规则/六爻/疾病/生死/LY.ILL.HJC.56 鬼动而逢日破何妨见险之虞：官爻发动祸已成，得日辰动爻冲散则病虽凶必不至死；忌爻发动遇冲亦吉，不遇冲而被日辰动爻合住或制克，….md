# 鬼动而逢日破何妨见险之虞：官爻发动祸已成，得日辰动爻冲散则病虽凶必不至死；忌爻发动遇冲亦吉，不遇冲而被日辰动爻合住或制克，也是见险无虞

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻发动，灾祸就成了。如果得到日辰或动爻来冲它，叫冲散，病虽然凶险，一定不至于死。忌爻发动，遇到冲也吉；如果没有遇到冲，但得到日辰或动爻来合住它、或者制克它，也都是遇到危险而没有忧患的象。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a Pressure line moves, the misfortune is made. If the Day or a moving line clashes it, this is called scattering it, and though the illness looks bad he will surely not die. A moving Adversary line that meets a clash is also lucky; if it meets no clash but is held by a combination or controlled and restrained by the Day or a moving line, this too is the image of meeting danger without harm.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2410 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官爻发动，或忌神发动，其祸成矣。 | 官爻发动，或者忌神发动，灾祸就成了。 | When a Pressure line moves, or the Adversary moves, the misfortune is made. |
> | 若得日辰动爻冲之，谓已冲散，主其病虽凶而不死。 | 如果得到日辰、动爻来冲它，叫已经冲散了，主这病虽凶却不会死。 | If the Day or a moving line clashes it, it is said to have been scattered, and the illness, though bad, will not lead to death. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「日破」是被日辰冲破，「见险」是碰到危险。旧注多讲了两条：忌爻动遇冲也吉，没遇冲时被合住或制克也无虞；王洪绪把官爻和忌神并成一句，没有收这两条。
>
> *Note:* 'Broken by the Day' is clashed and broken by the Day, and 'meeting danger' is running into danger. The old commentary adds two points: a moving Adversary line meeting a clash is also lucky, and if it meets no clash but is held by a combination or controlled there is also no harm; Wang Hongxu puts the Pressure line and the Adversary in one sentence and does not take these two.
