# 按六神断神鬼：青龙喜庆财利之神桃李之妖胎产之鬼；朱雀离神词讼囹圄冤鬼；勾陈土神禾黍社稷工巧折跌之鬼；螣蛇妖邪怪异飞游砍伐绳结惊狂之鬼；白虎金甲之神丧服之祟；玄武强梁之神盗贼河井妓女之魄

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙的象，是掌管喜庆财利的神，东方的圣贤，文官班列里的师相；是桃李的精怪，是胎产而死的鬼。朱雀也是离的神，掌管词讼、纠察善恶，掌管交易，主管生育，守在端门，权衡中鼎；是言语触犯了天曹，或者是牢里被冤枉的鬼。勾陈是一位土神，掌管禾黍，主管社稷；是工匠巧人的亡魂，是摔断跌伤的鬼。螣蛇属火，是黄帝的左右；是妖邪怪异的骚扰，是飞游砍伐的神，是绳结、惊狂的鬼。白虎是金甲的神，是丧服的祟，是兵灾凶险、老虎横行，被称作元帅的就像它。北方的玄武，是强横的神，是盗贼的鬼，是河里井里死去的，是妓女的魂。注说：卦里有两个鬼，上面的鬼临哪个六神、下面的鬼临哪个六神，用六神的象来断，是这样的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The image of the Azure Dragon: a spirit in charge of festivity and gain, a sage of the east, a teacher-minister among the civil ranks; a demon of peach and plum, a ghost of childbirth. The Vermilion Bird is also a spirit of Li: it presides over suits, oversees good and evil, has charge of trade and governs birth, hunts at the Main Gate and weighs the central cauldron; a case of words offending the Heavenly Bureau, or a ghost wrongly imprisoned. Hook Earth is an earth spirit, in charge of millet and grain and governing the altars of soil and grain; the departed spirit of a craftsman, a ghost of breaks and falls. The Coiling Snake belongs to Fire and attends the Yellow Emperor; it is the disturbance of demons and oddities, a spirit of flying wanderers and felling, a ghost of knotted cords, fright and madness. The White Tiger is a golden-armoured spirit, a haunting of mourning dress, the disaster of weapons and the rampage of tigers; those called marshals are its image. The Dark Tortoise of the north is a violent spirit, a ghost of thieves and bandits, one who died in a river or well, the soul of a prostitute. The note says: where the hexagram has two ghosts, whichever spirit the upper one carries and whichever the lower one carries, judgment by the images of the six spirits is as above.

> [!note] 按
> 「元武」是「玄武」避讳的写法。朱雀那一句「狩端门，权中鼎」原文很简，「端门」是宫殿的正门，「中鼎」是中央的鼎，这里照字面译，没有加解释。与黄金策六神推鬼神（LY.ILL.HJC.96）、易隐鬼崇六神（LY.ILL.YY.17）是同一件事：白虎都是血、丧、兵一路，玄武都是盗贼、暗昧一路，青龙都是喜庆一路，细目不同。
>
> *Note:* 'Yuan wu' is the taboo-avoiding form of 'Xuan wu' (the Dark Tortoise). The Vermilion Bird's 'hunts at the Main Gate and weighs the central cauldron' is very terse in the original; the 'Main Gate' is the front gate of a palace and the 'central cauldron' the cauldron at the centre, and both are translated literally without explanation. It treats the same matter as the Huangjin Ce's reading by the six spirits (LY.ILL.HJC.96) and the Yi Yin's six spirits in haunting (LY.ILL.YY.17): the White Tiger is always of the blood, mourning and weapons kind, the Dark Tortoise of thieves and obscurity, the Azure Dragon of festivity, with details differing.
