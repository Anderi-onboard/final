# 占自己病断之二：先凭世象次凭卦身；怕月破旬空，喜生扶拱合；克世为忌客、生世为元神，随官入墓灾难瘥、助鬼伤身命必沉；世落空辨缓急、休囚被克无救则天年命尽、旺相无生助亦无所害；他人病身世随官入墓不必忌，命随鬼入墓即凶；卦身墓绝于月建或变爻决无救

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 自己占病，先凭世爻的象，再凭卦身，怕遇上月破、旬空，喜欢见到生、扶、拱、合；克世的爻是忌客，来生世的象是元神；随官入墓，灾难痊愈；助鬼伤身，命必沉沦。注说：自己占自己的病，专看世，爻如果临月破，不论新病旧病，命也难保全。世如果落空，要分缓急：急症还能医，旧病就不救；如果空中有气，眼下无妨，后来难保。世象不临旬空月破，再看旺衰：世如果休囚，又被动爻来克、或遭日月来伤、或变成死绝，毫无救助的爻，怎么会不是天年命尽？世即使休囚，如果得到动爻生助、或遇日月扶持、或变为有气，纵然临危也不会死；世如果旺相，虽然没有生助的爻，也没有什么害处。凡是克世的叫忌象，宜静不宜动；生世的叫元神，宜旺不宜空。占他人的病，身、世随官入墓，不必忌讳，命随鬼入墓就凶了。只有自己占身、世，命随鬼入墓的命不会回生，助鬼伤身也是同样的道理；又看卦身，如果墓、绝于月建，或者墓、绝于变爻，一定没有救了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining one's own illness, first rely on the image of the Self, then on the Body; it fears meeting Month-break or Void, and is glad to see generation, support, flanking and combination; the line that controls the Self is the adversary guest, and the image that comes to generate it is the Support; following the Pressure line into a tomb, the illness is hard to cure; helping the ghost to harm the Self, life surely sinks. The commentary says: in divining one's own illness, look at the Self alone; if the Self line sits in Month-break, whether the illness is new or old, life is hard to preserve. If the Self is Void, distinguish slow from urgent: an urgent illness can still be treated, an old illness is not saved; if there is qi in the Void, there is no harm for the moment, but later it is hard to keep. If the Self image does not sit in Void or Month-break, look next at strength and weakness: if the Self is resting or trapped and is controlled by a moving line, or wounded by the Day or Month, or changes into death or Extinction, with no line of rescue at all, how can it be anything but the end of one's allotted years? Though the Self is resting or trapped, if a moving line generates and helps it, or the Day and Month support it, or it changes into qi, even at the brink it does not die; if the Self is strong, even without a generating line there is no harm. Whatever controls the Self is called an adversary image and should be still, not moving; whatever generates the Self is called the Support and should be strong, not Void. In divining another's illness, the Body and Self following the Pressure line into a tomb need not be feared, but the natal branch following the ghost into a tomb is ominous. Only in divining for oneself, with the Body and Self, does a natal branch following the ghost into a tomb mean life will not be restored, and helping the ghost to harm the Self is the same in principle; look also at the Body: if it is in a tomb or Extinction under the Month, or in a tomb or Extinction under the changed line, there is surely no rescue.

> [!note] 按
> 「爻若临月故」的「故」疑是「破」之误（月破），这里按上下文译作月破。「拱、合」是拱夹和六合。「天年命尽」是寿数到了。这一条把占自己病的判据收成一张单：世（不临月破旬空，看旺衰，休囚要有生助）、忌象（克世，宜静）、元神（生世，宜旺）、随官入墓与助鬼伤身、卦身（墓绝于月建或变爻则无救）。「占他人之病，身世随官入墓不必忌之，命随鬼入墓即凶矣」把黄金策里「随官入墓」的用法限定在了自占；与易冒 LY.ILL.YM.20（己疾世为用神，助伤只对世成立）读法一致。
>
> *Note:* The 'gu' in 'if the line sits in month gu' looks like an error for 'break' (Month-break) and is translated so from the context. 'Flanking and combination' are the flank (sandwiching) and the six combinations. 'The end of one's allotted years' is the running-out of the span of life. This entry gathers the criteria for divining one's own illness into one sheet: the Self (not in Month-break or Void; look at strength, and if resting or trapped it needs generation and support), the adversary image (controlling the Self, should be still), the Support (generating the Self, should be strong), following the Pressure line into a tomb and helping the ghost to harm the Self, and the Body (in a tomb or Extinction under the Month or the changed line means no rescue). 'In divining another's illness, the Body and Self following the Pressure line into a tomb need not be feared, and the natal branch following the ghost into a tomb is ominous' confines the Huangjin Ce's 'following the Pressure line into a tomb' to divining for oneself, and agrees with the Yi Mao's LY.ILL.YM.20 (the Self is the Subject in one's own illness, and helping to harm holds only for the Self).
