# 卦遇六冲难起发、爻逢六合好收功：占近病喜六冲（冲散灾殃），占痘症不然，花逢冲则败，如风雨侵花；六合卦或用神逢生合则易起发、好收功

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦遇到六冲，痘难起发；爻逢到六合，痘好收功。占近病，喜欢遇到六冲，叫做冲散灾殃；只有占痘症不是这样，叫做花遇到冲就败，好比风雨侵袭花朵，开始难起发，后来也不能收功。如果遇到六合卦，或者用神逢到生、合，就容易起发，一定能好好收功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hexagram meeting Six-Clash makes the pox hard to erupt; lines meeting Six-Combine bring a good completion. In divining a recent illness one is glad to meet Six-Clash, which is called clashing the calamity apart; only in divining pox is it not so, for it is said that a flower meeting a clash is ruined, like wind and rain attacking flowers: at first it hardly erupts, and later it does not come to completion. If it meets a Six-Combine hexagram, or the Subject meets generation or combination, it erupts easily and will surely come to a good completion.

> [!note] 按
> 注里「犹如妨雨侵花」的「妨雨」，按上下文应是「风雨」的误字，译作风雨；书里没有说明。增删卜易也说见花问吉凶忌六冲（LY.ILL.ZS.19），和这里一致；至于占近病遇六冲的好处，见增删卜易 LY.ILL.ZS.02。
>
> *Note:* In the commentary's 'like fang rain attacking flowers', 'fang rain' is by context a mis-written 'wind and rain' and is rendered so; the book does not say. The Zengshan Buyi also says that in asking the fortune of a pox already out a Six-Clash is to be avoided (LY.ILL.ZS.19), which agrees; for the benefit of a Six-Clash in a recent illness see LY.ILL.ZS.02 in the Zengshan Buyi.
