# 鬼在兑宫病在口：金鬼化火或火化金鬼必患牙疔，不化火只是牙痛，金鬼逢冲牙必松动

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻在兑宫，病在口里。金鬼变出火、或者火爻变成金鬼，必生牙疔；没有变到火爻，只是牙痛。金鬼遇冲，牙一定松动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line in the Dui palace puts the illness in the mouth. A Metal Pressure changing out into Fire, or a Fire line changing into a Metal Pressure, surely brings a tooth carbuncle; with no change into a Fire line it is only toothache. A Metal Pressure that meets a clash surely loosens the teeth.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6825 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若患牙疔，兑鬼金连火杀； | 如果患牙疔，是兑宫的鬼爻，金鬼连着火煞； | If one suffers a tooth carbuncle, it is a Pressure line in the Dui palace, a Metal Pressure joined to a Fire killer; |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2331 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼在兑宫，口中有病，若金鬼化忌神，或忌神化金鬼，必患牙疔，不化忌神则是齿痛。 | 鬼在兑宫，口里有病；如果金鬼变成忌神，或者忌神变成金鬼，一定生牙疔；没有变到忌神，就是牙痛。 | Pressure in the Dui palace: there is illness in the mouth; if a Metal Pressure changes into the Adversary, or the Adversary changes into a Metal Pressure, one surely gets a tooth carbuncle; if it does not change into the Adversary, it is toothache. |
> | 静鬼逢冲齿必动摇。 | 静着的鬼遇上冲，牙一定松动。 | A Pressure line that is still and meets a clash: the teeth surely loosen. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说「火」，王洪绪改说「忌神」：金的忌神是克它的火，说的是同一种变法。旧注的「金鬼逢冲」，王洪绪写成「静鬼逢冲」，限定在不动的鬼爻上。「牙疔」是牙龈上长的疔疮，小而硬、根深、来得急。经文的「火杀」，旧注解成「火」，即火爻。
>
> *Note:* Where the old commentary says 'Fire', Wang Hongxu says 'the Adversary': the Adversary of Metal is the Fire that controls it, so both speak of the same change. The old commentary's 'a Metal Pressure meeting a clash' becomes 'a still Pressure meeting a clash' in Wang Hongxu, which limits it to a Pressure line that does not move. A 'tooth carbuncle' is a boil on the gum, small, hard, deep-rooted and quick to come. The 'Fire killer' of the verse is explained by the old commentary as Fire, that is, a Fire line.
