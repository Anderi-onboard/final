# 财合用神居外动吐之则痊：财爻在外卦动主吐，生合世爻用爻则用药涌吐即愈，在内卦宜疏利；财爻动来刑克世与主象，在外服药不纳、在内药后发泻

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻在外卦发动，主呕吐。如果它生合世爻、用爻，用药催吐就会好；如果在内卦，疏通利下最好。如果财爻发动来刑克世爻和主象，在外卦就是吃药吃不下，在内卦就是吃了药之后拉肚子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Wealth line moving in the outer trigram means vomiting. If it generates or combines with the Self and Subject lines, treating with a medicine that makes him vomit heals it; if it is in the inner trigram, a purge that opens the bowels is best. If the Wealth line moves to punish or control the Self line and the main image, then in the outer trigram the medicine is not kept down, and in the inner trigram there is diarrhoea after the medicine.

> [!note] 按
> 「吐之则痊」是中医的「吐法」：病邪在上，让病人吐出来。这一条和 LY.ILL.HJC.30（财动在上卦主吐、下卦主泻）讲的是同一件事，那里是断症候，这里是定治法。
>
> *Note:* 'Healed by vomiting' is the emetic method of Chinese medicine: when the pathogen is high, the patient is made to vomit it up. The entry speaks of the same thing as LY.ILL.HJC.30 (Wealth moving in the upper trigram means vomiting, in the lower diarrhoea): there it judges the symptom, here it fixes the treatment.
