# 按六亲断神鬼：父母为祖先、妻财为臣妾、兄弟为手足之缠、子孙为儿女之孽，世是本宗、应是异姓；六亲有动以六亲象断，化鬼及独发者尤甚

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻是祖先，妻财爻是臣妾，兄弟爻是手足纠缠，子孙爻是儿女带来的孽；世是本宗，应是异姓。注说：六亲有动的，用六亲的象来断；只有变出鬼的和独自发动的，尤其厉害；世是本家的鬼，应是别姓的亡魂。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line is the ancestors, the Wealth line is servants and concubines, the Peer line is the entanglement of brothers and kin, and the Output line is the affliction of sons and daughters; the Self is the same lineage, and the Other is a different surname. The note says: where a relation line moves, judgment by the images of the six relations is as above; one that changes into a ghost or moves alone is especially severe; the Self is a ghost of one's own house, and the Other is the departed of another surname.

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

> | 世，本家鬼也，应，他姓亡也。 | 世是本家的鬼，应是别姓的亡魂。 | The Self is a ghost of one's own house, and the Other is the departed of another surname. |

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的用法是：官鬼旁边的六亲爻在动，或者官鬼变成了某个六亲，就按那个六亲去认是谁的鬼。和黄金策「鬼动化出六亲即以化出者断」（LY.ILL.HJC.108）、易隐「鬼崇是谁的鬼」（LY.ILL.YY.30）是同一件事；易冒的写法更短，只列六亲各是谁（父母祖先、妻财臣妾、兄弟手足、子孙儿女），没有再分阴阳老少。注里的这一段夹在正文中间，原文的括号在「他姓亡也」处收尾，后面的「犯亥子……」仍是正文（见 LY.ILL.YM.61）。
>
> *Note:* The use is this: when a relation line beside the Pressure line moves, or the Pressure line changes into a certain relation, one identifies whose ghost it is by that relation. It is the same matter as the Huangjin Ce's 'a ghost moving into a relation is judged by what it turns into' (LY.ILL.HJC.108) and the Yi Yin's 'whose ghost it is' (LY.ILL.YY.30); the Yi Mao writes it more briefly, listing only who each relation is (Resource the ancestors, Wealth servants and concubines, Peer brothers and kin, Output sons and daughters), without going on to divide by yin and yang, old and young. The note in this passage sits in the middle of the running text, the original bracket closing at 'the departed of another surname', and the 'if it offends Hai and Zi...' that follows is still the text (see LY.ILL.YM.61).
