# 月将值日·登明（月建亥）占病：颠狂、乱言、寒热头疼、对神见鬼；触犯山司木下、暗昧女伤亡；亥宫卦断半天午酉草野鬼、子时得疾难安好

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在亥、叫登明的时候占病：癫狂，躺在床上乱说话，忽冷忽热、头疼，心里郁结，对着神像看见鬼，怪异得很。冲犯的是山司和木下，还有暗昧的女性伤亡鬼。「亥宫卦断」那一段说：半空的、午酉方位的、野外的鬼，也是家里的二姓郎；子时得的病难以安好，要赶紧保祷，免得惶恐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on Hai, named Dengming, divining illness: madness, babbling in his bed, chills and fever, headache, a knot in the heart, seeing ghosts when facing the spirits, uncanny beyond measure. What has been offended is the Mountain Official and the one under the wood, and there are obscure women dead by injury. The passage 'the verdict for the Hai-palace hexagrams' says: the ghosts of the half-sky, of Wu and You, and of the wild fields, and also the household's 'two-surname lord'; an illness taken at the Zi hour is hard to settle, and he should be quickly prayed for, to spare him distress.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。「乱言论语」是乱说话；「木下」「山司」书里没有解释，译文只照字面；「暗昧女伤亡」是说暗中、昏昧的女性伤亡鬼，也是字面；「半天午酉并草野」「二姓郎」不知所指，译文照字面写，不下定义（第 11 首有「家先三五郎」，「郎」像是民间称某类神鬼的叫法，这是推测）；「子时得疾」是说这个病是在子时得的；「保忧」两字不通，译成「保祷」是读法，像是「保佑」「保安」一类，「栖惶」是惶惑不安。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. 'Babbles confusedly' is the book's 'speaks in disorder'; 'the one under the wood' and 'the Mountain Official' are not explained in the book and are only literal; 'obscure women dead by injury' is the sense of women in the dark and the dim who died by injury, again literal; 'the half-sky, Wu and You, and the wild fields' and 'two-surname lord' are not known and are written literally without defining them (verse 11 has 'the household ancestors and the Third and Fifth Lords', and 'lord' looks like a popular name for a kind of spirit, which is a guess); 'an illness taken at the Zi hour' says that this illness was taken at the Zi hour; the two characters of 'guard against worry' do not make sense as printed, and 'prayed for' is a reading, as if it were 'protect' or 'keep safe', while 'distress' is the book's word for being unsettled and perplexed.
