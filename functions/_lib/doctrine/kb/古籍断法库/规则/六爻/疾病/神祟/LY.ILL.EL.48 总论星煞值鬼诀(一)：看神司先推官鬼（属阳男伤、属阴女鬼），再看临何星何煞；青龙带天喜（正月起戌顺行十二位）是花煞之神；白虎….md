# 总论星煞值鬼诀(一)：看神司先推官鬼（属阳男伤、属阴女鬼），再看临何星何煞；青龙带天喜（正月起戌顺行十二位）是花煞之神；白虎带丧门（正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑循环）是丧煞；朱雀带天火（子卯午酉循环）天烛（正月起巳顺行至辰）是五显灵官、灶神；玄武官爻带天贼（按十二属相）或天盗（亥寅巳申循环）是草野三郎

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看神司，先推官鬼：官鬼属阳，是男人受伤；属阴，是女鬼。再看它临着哪一颗星、哪一个煞，就知道是什么在作祟。青龙上的鬼带天喜，可以说是花煞之神（天喜的排法：正月从戌上起，顺着数十二位）。鬼临白虎又带丧门，就说是丧煞为殃（丧门：正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑、五月又到戌，只在这四个位上循环）。天火、天烛和朱雀同在，是五显灵官和灶皇（天火：正月子、二月卯、三月午、四月酉，循环；天烛：正月起在巳宫，顺着数到辰）；这个煞就是大朱雀，官鬼临上它，就是冒犯了五显灵官、灶神在作祟，而且天烛遇到发动时，做福、祈禳也大凶。玄武上的官爻临着天贼星或天盗煞，就是草野三郎（天贼：正月龙〔辰〕、二月鸡〔酉〕、三月虎〔寅〕、四月羊〔未〕、五月鼠〔子〕、六月蛇〔巳〕、七月犬〔戌〕、八月兔〔卯〕、九月猴〔申〕、十月牛〔丑〕、子月马〔午〕、丑月猪〔亥〕；天盗：正月亥、二月寅、三月巳、四月申，只在这四位上循环）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To look at a god or office, first work out the Pressure line: if it is yang, a man is harmed; if it is yin, it is a woman's ghost. Then look at which star and which killing it sits on, and one knows which haunting is the calamity. A ghost on the Azure Dragon with Heavenly Joy may be called the god of the Flower Killing (Heavenly Joy is set thus: the first month starts from Xu and runs forward through twelve places). A ghost on the White Tiger with Mourning Gate is said to be the Mourning Killing as calamity (Mourning Gate: the first month Xu, the second Wei, the third Chen, the fourth Chou, the fifth again reaching Xu, cycling only through these four positions). Heavenly Fire and Heavenly Candle together with the Vermilion Bird are the Five Manifest Spirit Officials and the Kitchen Sovereign (Heavenly Fire: the first month Zi, the second Mao, the third Wu, the fourth You, cycling; Heavenly Candle: the first month starts at the snake palace and runs forward, counting to the dragon); this killing is the Great Vermilion Bird, and a Pressure line on it is an offence against the Five Manifest Spirit Officials or the Kitchen God as haunting, and when it meets movement, making blessings or praying for exorcism is also very ominous. A Pressure line on the Dark Tortoise that sits on the Heavenly Thief star or the Heavenly Robber killing is the Third Lord of the wilds (Heavenly Thief by the twelve animals: the first month the dragon [Chen], the second the rooster [You], the third the tiger [Yin], the fourth the sheep [Wei], the fifth the rat [Zi], the sixth the snake [Si], the seventh the dog [Xu], the eighth the hare [Mao], the ninth the monkey [Shen], the tenth the ox [Chou], the Zi month the horse [Wu], the Chou month the pig [Hai]; Heavenly Robber: the first month Hai, the second Yin, the third Si, the fourth Shen, cycling only through these four).

> [!warning] 打架
> 丧门、天喜怎么排，各书不同。①丧门：易林补遗（LY.ILL.EL.48）是正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑，四个位置周而复始；断易天机的注释引鬼谷子的杀例，同样是正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑，并说「只此四位，轮十二月」；火珠林（卜筮大全里抄了同一段）的丧门杀例是正月未、二月辰、三月丑、四月戌，也说「只此四位轮十二月」——四个位置一样，起点差一位。②天喜：易林补遗是正月从戌起、顺行十二位（戌、亥、子、丑、寅、卯、辰、巳、午、未、申、酉），周易尚占的逐月吉凶神杀表也是这样；卜筮大全的神煞篇是「春戌夏丑为天喜，秋辰冬未」，按季不按月。这几本书都没有说谁对谁错，按哪一家排，同一个月里的位置会不同。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How Mourning Gate and Heavenly Joy are set differs from book to book. (1) Mourning Gate: the Yilin Buyi (LY.ILL.EL.48) has the first month Xu, the second Wei, the third Chen, the fourth Chou, the four positions cycling; the commentary in the Duanyi Tianji quotes the rule of the killings of Guiguzi, which is likewise the first month Xu, the second Wei, the third Chen, the fourth Chou, and says 'only these four positions, rotating through twelve months'; the rule of the Mourning Gate killing in the Huozhulin (whose same passage is copied in the Buyi Daquan) is the first month Wei, the second Chen, the third Chou, the fourth Xu, and also says 'only these four positions rotating through twelve months': the same four positions, with the starting point one place off. (2) Heavenly Joy: the Yilin Buyi has the first month starting from Xu and running forward through twelve places (Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You), and the Zhouyi Shangzhan's monthly table of lucky and unlucky stars agrees; the chapter on killings in the Buyi Daquan says 'spring Xu, summer Chou, autumn Chen, winter Wei is Heavenly Joy', by season and not by month. None of these books says which is right, and which one is followed changes the position in the same month.

> [!note] 按
> 这是「总论」的第四段（编号「之四」），体例又变了：每条先是一句诗、再是解说，解说里还带着各星煞的「起例」——也就是这颗星每个月落在哪一支。这一节共五条（LY.ILL.EL.48 到 EL.52），讲的是「官鬼临了某颗星、某个煞，就是某位神鬼」，而且要同六兽合看（青龙对天喜，白虎对丧门，朱雀对天火天烛，玄武对天贼天盗……）。这一条的头两句是总则：看神司要先推官鬼，鬼属阳是男人受伤、属阴是女鬼（和黄金策的阳男阴女，LY.ILL.HJC.94 同一路）。「凡居卦下论阴阳」的「卦下」按字面译作「居在卦里」。「灶皇」按字面译作 Kitchen Sovereign，解说里已经说是灶神。「荡荡顺行数至龙」的「龙」是辰，天烛从巳起（蛇宫），数到辰正好十二个月；「采山」在下面 LY.ILL.EL.47 的解说里说就是草野三郎，「水三郎」按字面译。天贼那首口诀把十二属相对着地支读：龙辰、鸡酉、虎寅、羊未、鼠子、蛇巳、犬戌、兔卯、猴申、牛丑、马午、猪亥，每月往后推五位，是一个整齐的数列，易冒也有同一首口诀（易冒讲诸星一章的注），两处一致。 书里没有说这里的「正月、二月」是起卦的月还是别的月，这里照抄；「只此四位周而复始」是说五月回到正月的位置、九月也回到正月的位置，依此类推。 这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This is the fourth section of the 'general discourse' (numbered 'four'), and its form changes again: each item is a line of verse and then a commentary, and the commentary also carries the 'rules of setting' of each star or killing, that is, the branch on which the star falls in each month. This part of five entries (LY.ILL.EL.48 to EL.52) is about 'a Pressure line on such a star or such a killing is such a god or ghost', and it has to be read together with the six beasts (Azure Dragon with Heavenly Joy, White Tiger with Mourning Gate, Vermilion Bird with Heavenly Fire and Candle, Dark Tortoise with Heavenly Thief and Robber, and so on). The first two lines of this entry are the general rule: to look at a god or office one first works out the Pressure line; a yang ghost is a man harmed and a yin one a woman's ghost (on the same path as the Huangjin Ce's yang is male, yin is female, LY.ILL.HJC.94). 'Everything under the hexagram' in the first verse is translated as 'wherever it sits in the hexagram'. 'Kitchen Sovereign' is a literal rendering of 'zao huang', which the commentary already says is the Kitchen God. In 'counting until it reaches the dragon' the dragon is Chen; the Heavenly Candle starts from Si (the snake palace) and counting to Chen makes exactly twelve months; the 'Mountain Gatherer' is said in the commentary of LY.ILL.EL.47 to be the Third Lord of the wilds, and 'water Third Lord' is rendered literally. The Heavenly Thief verse reads the twelve animals against the branches: dragon Chen, rooster You, tiger Yin, sheep Wei, rat Zi, snake Si, dog Xu, hare Mao, monkey Shen, ox Chou, horse Wu, pig Hai, each month moving on five places, which is a regular series, and the Yi Mao has the same verse (in its note in the chapter on the stars), the two agreeing. The book does not say whether the 'first month' and so on are the months of the divination or of something else, and here it is copied as written; 'only these four positions, cycling round and round' means that the fifth month returns to the position of the first and the ninth to that of the first, and so on. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
