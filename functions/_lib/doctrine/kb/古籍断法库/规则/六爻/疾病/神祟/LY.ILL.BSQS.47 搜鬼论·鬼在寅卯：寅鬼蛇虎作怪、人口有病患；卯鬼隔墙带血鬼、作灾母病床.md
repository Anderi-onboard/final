# 搜鬼论·鬼在寅卯：寅鬼蛇虎作怪、人口有病患；卯鬼隔墙带血鬼、作灾母病床

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 搜鬼论按十二地支列出鬼爻发动时所应的怪异：寅爻为鬼，蛇、虎来作怪，六畜见血、钱财亡失，家里人口有病患，要赶紧保平安；卯爻为鬼，是隔墙带着血的鬼，作灾，母亲卧病在床。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The essay on searching out the ghost lists, by the twelve branches, what a moving ghost line answers: with Yin as the ghost, snakes and tigers come making trouble, livestock bleed and wealth is lost, the household has illness and one must quickly secure safety; with Mao as the ghost, it is a ghost with blood from across the wall, it makes calamity, and the mother lies ill in bed.

> [!note] 按
> 搜鬼论开头一句是「观鬼爻发动为实」——看的是发动的鬼爻，然后按子到亥十二支各列一条；这里只收和病有关的寅、卯两条，其余十条讲的是鼠咬屋、火灾、走失、牲畜、神庙等，不收。卯条的「作灾母病床」是作灾，母亲卧病在床；「破伞并橱柜」「及有死人床」是这个鬼附着的东西。
>
> *Note:* The essay begins with 'see the ghost line moving as real' (what is looked at is the moving ghost line) and then gives one entry for each branch from Zi to Hai; only the entries for Yin and Mao, which bear on illness, are taken here, and the other ten, on rats gnawing the house, fire, runaways, livestock and shrines, are not. In the Mao entry 'it makes calamity, the mother lies ill in bed' is calamity with the mother bedridden, and 'a broken umbrella and a cupboard' and 'a bed on which someone has died' are the things this ghost clings to.
