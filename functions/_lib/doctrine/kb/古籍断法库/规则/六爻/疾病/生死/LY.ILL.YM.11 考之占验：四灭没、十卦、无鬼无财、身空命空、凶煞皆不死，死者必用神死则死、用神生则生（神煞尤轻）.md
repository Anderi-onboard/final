# 考之占验：四灭没、十卦、无鬼无财、身空命空、凶煞皆不死，死者必用神死则死、用神生则生（神煞尤轻）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 从占验来看，四灭、四没的卦不死，十卦不死，没有鬼、没有财的不死，身空、命空的不死，遇到凶煞的不死；那么人之所以死，一定是用神死了他就死，用神生旺他就活。灭没：春天需、蒙，夏天观、蛊，秋天剥、节，冬天旅、临。十卦：明夷、观、贲、大畜、丰、同人、蛊、夬、需、临。凶煞：三坵五墓、丧车这一类。比如占父亲的病，六月得观卦，凭什么会死？用爻当日、月，卦里没有财、鬼，有什么凶？因为用爻坏而没有鬼，即使祈祷也得不到保佑；用爻旺而没有鬼，不祈祷也安康；没有财而用爻绝，天禄已尽；没有财而用爻旺，只是吃得少。甲子旬空亥，难道亥年生的人就不能问病？寅月破申，难道申年生的人就不能占病？所以应爻（空）不必作戒；身空的，有世爻、卦身可以凭据；本命不出现的，有变卦、伏爻可以取用。大体上专心看用爻来定生死安危，才真正得到《易》的宗旨，神煞就更轻了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Examining what divinations have proved: with the Four Extinguishing and Submerging hexagrams he does not die; with the ten hexagrams he does not die; with no ghost and no Wealth he does not die; with the Body Void or the destiny Void he does not die; with malign stars he does not die; so what he dies of must be that he dies when the Subject dies and lives when the Subject lives. The Extinguishing and Submerging: in spring Xu and Meng, in summer Guan and Gu, in autumn Bo and Jie, in winter Lü and Lin. The ten hexagrams: Mingyi, Guan, Bi (Adornment), Daxu, Feng, Tongren, Gu, Guai, Xu and Lin. Malign stars are such as Three Mounds and Five Tombs and the Funeral Carriage. For example, in divining a father's illness, if in the sixth month one obtains Guan, from what would he die? The Subject line sits on the Day and Month and the hexagram has neither Wealth nor ghost: what ill omen is there? For if the Subject line is spoiled and there is no ghost, prayer brings no protection; if strong and no ghost, he is well without praying; with no Wealth and the Subject in Extinction the heavenly emolument has ended; with no Wealth and the Subject strong he merely takes little food. If the Jiazi decade leaves Hai Void, may a person of the Hai natal branch not ask about illness? If the Yin month is a Month-break against Shen, may a person of the Shen natal branch not divine illness? So the Other line need not be a warning; if the Body is Void, the Self and Body can be relied on; if the natal branch does not appear, the changed hexagram and hidden lines can be taken. In general, to concentrate on the Subject line to fix life and death, safety and danger, truly grasps the intent of the Yi; the spirit-stars weigh lighter still.

> [!warning] 打架
> 凶卦、凶煞碰上了，死不死：易冒说四灭没、十卦、凶煞（三坵五墓丧车）都不死，死不死只看用神生死，神煞尤轻；易隐则把黄泉杀、四灭卦、四没卦、棺椁杀列为占病逢之必死的卦，又把三坵五墓、丧门吊客、死符丧车等列为动而临用必死的凶煞（LY.ILL.YY.05、LY.ILL.YY.08）。同一批卦名和凶煞，两本书的结论相反；增删卜易的野鹤同样说卦名、卦身、蛇虎星煞都不验、只看用神（LY.ILL.ZS.01），与易冒一边。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether meeting the ill-omened hexagrams and stars means death: the Yi Mao says that the Four Extinguishing and Submerging hexagrams, the ten hexagrams and the malign stars (Three Mounds and Five Tombs, the Funeral Carriage) do not mean death, that death rests only on whether the Subject lives or dies, and that the spirit-stars weigh lighter still; the Yi Yin, on the other hand, lists the Yellow Springs, the Four Extinguishing, the Four Submerging and the Coffin killers as hexagrams that mean death for one who divines illness, and lists Three Mounds and Five Tombs, Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence, Death Tally and Funeral Carriage and the like as malign stars that mean death when they move and sit on or control the Subject (LY.ILL.YY.05 and LY.ILL.YY.08). The same hexagram names and stars, and the two books' conclusions are opposite; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise says hexagram names, the Body and the Snake, Tiger and other stars are not reliable and only the Subject counts (LY.ILL.ZS.01), and stands with the Yi Mao.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的口气是「我们试过、检验过」：开头是「考之占验」，是作者拿实际占验的结果来反驳一批流行的凶象。「灭没」的名单和易隐的四灭卦、四没卦（LY.ILL.YY.08）是同一批，易冒把它们合称「灭没」，两本书对季节的配法一致。「天禄」在这里是财。「甲子旬空亥」是甲子旬里空的是戌亥，亥命被旬空。
>
> *Note:* The tone here is 'we have tried and tested it': it opens 'examining what divinations have proved', with the author using actual results to refute a set of popular ill omens. The list of the 'Extinguishing and Submerging' is the same as the Yi Yin's Four Extinguishing and Four Submerging hexagrams (LY.ILL.YY.08), which the Yi Mao names together, and the two books match the seasons the same way. 'Heavenly emolument' here is Wealth. In 'the Jiazi decade leaves Hai Void' the Void of the Jiazi decade is Xu and Hai, so a Hai natal branch is left Void.
