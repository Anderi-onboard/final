# 御避灾患：防灾避患忌官爻——安静休囚祸不招，遇旺遇生灾速起，无或落空陷则病潜消，不伤身世无魔瘴，日月制之殃咎散，子孙一动灭邪妖；无鬼便无灾

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 这一章讲防灾避患，吉的事用子孙爻为主，凶的事用官鬼爻为凭。防灾避患最忌官爻：官爻安静、休囚，祸招不来；遇到旺、遇到生，灾很快就起；官爻没有或落陷，病就悄悄消了；官爻不伤世和卦身，就没有魔障；如果发动，就难逃；日、月制住它，殃咎就散；子孙一动，邪妖就灭。注说：占自己有没有病，要仔细看官鬼，鬼逢生旺，灾一定来得快；落空亡或不上卦，永远不会成灾；鬼发动怎么会不生灾？不克世和卦身，终究没有疾厄；官爻虽然动，只要子孙同时发动、或者子孙临日月、或者子孙在世爻上，万祸都悄悄消了；没有鬼就没有灾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* This chapter is about guarding against calamity and avoiding trouble: for good outcomes take Output as the main line, for bad ones the Pressure line as the ground. To guard against calamity one fears the Pressure line: still, resting or trapped, it brings no misfortune; meeting strength or generation, calamity rises quickly; absent or fallen, the illness quietly fades; if it does not harm the Self and the Body there is no demonic obstruction; if it moves, it can hardly be escaped; if the Day and Month control it, the calamity disperses; when Output moves, evil and demons are wiped out. The commentary says: in divining whether one has illness, examine the Pressure line closely; if the ghost meets generation or strength, calamity is sure to come quickly; if it falls into Void or is absent from the hexagram, it will never cause trouble; if the ghost moves, how could it not breed calamity? But if it does not control the Self and the Body, in the end there is no illness; even if the Pressure line moves, if Output moves with it, or Output sits on the Day or Month, or Output is on the Self, ten thousand misfortunes quietly fade; no ghost, no calamity.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章防的是还没得的病：占自己会不会遇灾、有没有病，不是占已经在病的人，所以吉凶两头各取一个主爻——吉看子孙、凶看官鬼。「魔瘴」是妖魔邪祟造成的障碍。易冒的初筮防疾一条（LY.ILL.YM.28）是同一件事，也是「用坏病起、鬼发病作」，看的也是鬼。
>
> *Note:* This chapter is about guarding against an illness not yet contracted: whether one will meet calamity or have illness, not about someone already sick, so a main line is taken at each end, Output for good and Pressure for bad. 'Demonic obstruction' is the hindrance caused by demons and evil influences. The Yi Mao's entry on guarding against illness in a first divination (LY.ILL.YM.28) is the same matter, 'the Subject spoiled, the illness rises; the ghost moves, the illness breaks out', and it too looks at the ghost.
