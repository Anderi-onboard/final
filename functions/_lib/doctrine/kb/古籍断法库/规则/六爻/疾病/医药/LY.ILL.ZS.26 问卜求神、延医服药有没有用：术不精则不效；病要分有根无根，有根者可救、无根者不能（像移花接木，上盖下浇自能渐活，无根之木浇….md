# 问卜求神、延医服药有没有用：术不精则不效；病要分有根无根，有根者可救、无根者不能（像移花接木，上盖下浇自能渐活，无根之木浇盖无益）

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问卜求神、请医吃药是有道理的：不灵验有两个原因，一是术不精，精了没有不灵验的；二是病要分有根无根，有根的可以救，无根的救不了，就像移栽花木，有根的上面遮盖、下面浇灌，自然慢慢活过来，无根的树、无蒂的花，浇了盖了也没有用。人不懂这个，一次不灵验就不信神和药，是冤枉了它。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* There is reason in consulting the oracle and praying to the spirits, calling a doctor and taking medicine: when it fails there are two causes, one that the art is not refined, for if refined nothing fails, and the other that an illness must be told as rooted or rootless, the rooted being savable and the rootless not, as with moving a flowering plant, where one that has roots is covered above and watered below and revives gradually, while a tree without root or a flower without stem gains nothing from watering and covering. People who do not understand this lose faith in the spirits and medicine at the first failure, and wrong them.

> | 野鹤曰：有人而问余曰：问卜求神，延医服药，有是理乎？ | 野鹤说：有人问我：问卜求神、请医吃药，有这个道理吗？ | The Old Man of Yehe says: someone asked me: to consult the oracle and pray to spirits, to call a doctor and take medicine, is there any reason in this? |
> | 余曰：若无此理，伏羲神农，开天大圣人也，画卦尝药可为乎？ | 我说：如果没有这个道理，伏羲、神农是开天辟地的大圣人，他们画卦、尝药能这样做吗？ | I said: if there were no reason in it, Fuxi and Shennong were great sages who opened up the world, could they have drawn the trigrams and tasted the herbs? |
> | 彼曰：然则每有不效者，何也？ | 他说：那么常常有不灵验的，是什么缘故？ | He said: then why does it so often fail? |
> | 余曰：有二说焉，一在术之不精，精则无有不效者； | 我说：有两种说法，一是术不精，精了就没有不灵验的； | I said: there are two accounts. One is that the art is not refined, for if refined nothing fails; |
> | 一人之疾，要分有根无根。 | 另一种是，一个人的病，要分有根还是无根。 | the other is that a person's illness must be told as rooted or rootless. |
> | 有根者可以救之，无根者则不能也。 | 有根的可以救，无根的就不能。 | The rooted can be saved; the rootless cannot. |
> | 人之星辰过宫，或是交运脱运，谓之移花接木之年，移其有根之花木者，上则遮盖，下宜浇溉，自能渐活，不浇不盖，见日亦枯，有病而求神服药者，即此意也； | 人的星辰过宫，或者是交运、脱运，叫做移花接木的年份，移栽有根的花木，上面要遮盖，下面要浇灌，自然慢慢活过来，不浇不盖，见了太阳也枯死，有病而去求神、吃药，就是这个意思； | When a person's stars pass into another palace, or he enters or leaves a fortune cycle, it is called a year of grafting flowers: for a flowering plant that has roots and is moved, one covers it above and waters it below, and it revives gradually, while without watering and covering it withers in the sun; one who is ill and prays and takes medicine is in the same case; |
> | 若使无根之木，无蒂之花，虽浇之盖之，亦无益矣，故尝有求神服药而弗效者。 | 如果是无根的树、无蒂的花，虽然浇它、盖它，也没有用，所以常常有求神吃药却不灵验的。 | but a tree without root or a flower without stem gains nothing from watering and covering, and so there are those who pray and take medicine and are not cured. |
> | 此天地之至理也，人不识此，一不效，而不信神药焉，岂不冤哉！ | 这是天地间最根本的道理，人不懂这个，一次不灵验，就不信神和药，难道不冤枉吗！ | This is the supreme principle of heaven and earth; people who do not understand it lose faith in the spirits and medicine at the first failure; is that not unjust? |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「有根、无根」书里只用移花接木打了比方，没有说怎样从卦上看出病有根无根。「星辰过宫」「交运脱运」是命理里说的运势交接。
>
> *Note:* For 'rooted' and 'rootless' the book uses only the figure of grafting flowers and does not say how to see from the hexagram whether an illness is rooted or rootless. 'Stars passing into another palace' and 'entering or leaving a fortune cycle' are the handing over of fortune spoken of in fate reckoning.
