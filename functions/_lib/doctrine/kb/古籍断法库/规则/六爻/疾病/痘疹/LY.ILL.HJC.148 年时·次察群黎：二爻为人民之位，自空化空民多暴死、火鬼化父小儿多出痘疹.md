# 年时·次察群黎：二爻为人民之位，自空化空民多暴死、火鬼化父小儿多出痘疹

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》年时章说：「其次观察百姓，喜欢（二爻）在旺相的地方。」旧注：二爻是人民的位置；遇到子孙，四季安乐；逢到官鬼，太岁多灾，发动克世，百姓多去做盗贼；二爻自己落空又化成空，百姓多有暴死的；空而动、又生合应爻，百姓多迁徙到外郡；火鬼化成父母，小孩多出痘疹。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the year says: 'Next observe the common people; it is good for them to be in a place of strength and birth.' The old commentary: the second line is the place of the people; meeting Output, the four seasons are peaceful and happy; meeting Pressure, with Tai Sui, the year has many calamities, and if it moves and controls the Self, many of the people turn to banditry; if the line is Void and turns into Void, many of the people die suddenly; if Void and moving and it generates or combines with the Other line, many of the people move away to other counties; a Fire ghost turning into Resource, many small children break out in pox and rash.

> [!note] 按
> 年时章是占一年的事的一章，用六爻分管天、地、人：二爻是人民。这里和病有关的是两句：「民多暴死」（自空化空，百姓多有突然死的）和「小儿多出痘疹」（火鬼化成父母，当年小孩多出痘疹）。这是一年的气运，不是占一个人的病。痘疹见本域「痘疹」一类的其他条目（卜筮正宗新增痘疹章，LY.ILL.BSZZ.23 等）。「应爻」是应爻，「外郡」是别的郡县。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on the year is about divining the affairs of a whole year, using the six lines to govern heaven, earth and people: the second line is the people. Two sentences bear on illness: 'many die suddenly' (the line Void and turning into Void, many of the people die suddenly) and 'many small children break out in pox and rash' (a Fire ghost turning into Resource, in that year many small children break out in pox and rash). This is the year's fortune and not one person's illness. For pox and rash see the other entries under the 'pox and rash' topic of this domain (the chapter on pox and rash added in the Bushi Zhengzong, LY.ILL.BSZZ.23 and others). The 'Other line' is the Other line, and 'other counties' are other prefectures.
