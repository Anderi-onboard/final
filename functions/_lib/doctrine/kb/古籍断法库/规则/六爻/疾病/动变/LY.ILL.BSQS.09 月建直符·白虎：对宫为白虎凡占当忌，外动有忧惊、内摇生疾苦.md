# 月建直符·白虎：对宫为白虎凡占当忌，外动有忧惊、内摇生疾苦

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建直符里说：月建对宫的那一位是白虎，凡是占问都要避忌它；它在外卦发动主忧虑惊恐，在内卦发动生出疾病和痛苦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on the Month as duty-star says: the palace opposite the Month is the White Tiger and is to be shunned in any divination; moving outside it means anxiety and fright, moving inside it brings illness and suffering.

## 《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·二十四、月建直符》原文

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 月建直符是另一种排六神的办法：拿当月的月建作青龙，月建往前数三位是朱雀，往后数三位是玄武，月建对冲的那一宫（对宫）是白虎，再往后一位是勾陈，螣蛇从正月的辰起、每月逆行一位。歌里的「内」「外」指内卦、外卦。这里的「对宫」就是月建对冲的那个地支，也就是月破的位置；歌没有说这一点，是从前面几句的排法读出来的。原书在歌后加了一句小注：「正月寅爻动是也，主管一月之事」，说的是月建所在的那一爻发动，管这个月的事。
>
> *Note:* The Month as duty-star is another way of setting the six spirits: the Month branch of the current month is the Azure Dragon, three places ahead of it is the Vermilion Bird, three places behind is the Dark Tortoise, the palace opposite the Month branch (the clash) is the White Tiger, one place further back is Hook Earth, and the Coiling Snake starts from Chen in the first month and moves one place backward each month. 'Inside' and 'outside' in the verse are the inner and outer trigrams. Here 'the opposite palace' is the branch that clashes the Month, which is where the Month-break falls; the verse does not say so, and this is read from the way the earlier lines set things out. The original adds a small note after the verse: 'in the first month it is a moving Yin line, and it governs that month's affairs', meaning the line in the Month's place moves and governs the matters of that month.
