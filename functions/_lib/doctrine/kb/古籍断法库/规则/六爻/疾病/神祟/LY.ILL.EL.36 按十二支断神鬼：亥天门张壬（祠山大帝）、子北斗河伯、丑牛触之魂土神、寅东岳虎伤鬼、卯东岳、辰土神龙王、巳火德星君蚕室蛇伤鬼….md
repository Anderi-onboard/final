# 按十二支断神鬼：亥天门张壬（祠山大帝）、子北斗河伯、丑牛触之魂土神、寅东岳虎伤鬼、卯东岳、辰土神龙王、巳火德星君蚕室蛇伤鬼、午金枪教主（五显灵官）马伤鬼、未伽蓝土地、申元帅将军伤司、酉佛象丧煞少女、戌土神犬伤鬼；外鬼带青龙三官大帝、内鬼带青龙水仙五圣；子鬼带玄武外为驿帝内为水伤

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 亥是天门，和张壬作扰害；带青龙的象，宜叩拜三元；子是北斗，加上河伯作为灾殃；加了玄武的爻，应当酬谢圣帝；丑，是被牛顶撞的魂；寅，是被虎伤的鬼；卯，要禳解东岳；辰，要酬谢龙王；巳，推为火德尊星、被蛇所伤的鬼；午，断为金枪教主、被马踏的魂；未，叫伽蓝；申，说是元帅；酉，叫雌雄二煞；戌，遇上恶犬伤人。注说：亥鬼是张壬，就是祠山大帝；外鬼带青龙，是三官大帝；内鬼带青龙，是水仙五圣；子鬼是北斗、又是河伯水官，在外带玄武，是驿帝，在内带玄武，是水伤；丑鬼是土神，又是土伤之鬼；寅鬼是东岳，又是虎伤之鬼；卯鬼也是东岳；辰鬼是土神，又是龙王；巳鬼是火德星君和蚕室，又是蛇伤之鬼；午鬼是金枪教主，就是五显灵官，以及马伤之鬼；未鬼是土神，又是伽蓝土地；申鬼是元帅将军的职司和伤司，又有寺观中所冒犯的神；酉鬼是佛象和丧煞，又是少女；戌鬼是土神，又是犬伤之鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Hai is the Heavenly Gate and the harm done by Zhang Ren; with the image of the Azure Dragon, it is proper to appeal to the Three Primes; Zi is the Northern Dipper together with the River Count, as calamity; a line with the Dark Tortoise added should make offering to the Holy Emperor; Chou is the soul gored by an ox; Yin is the ghost wounded by a tiger; Mao: exorcise the Eastern Peak; Chen: thank the Dragon King; Si: work out the honoured Fire Virtue star and the ghost wounded by a snake; Wu: judge the Golden Spear Master and the soul trampled by a horse; Wei is called the Sangharama; Shen is said to be the Marshal; You is named the two killings, male and female; Xu meets a fierce dog wounding a person. The commentary says: the Hai ghost is Zhang Ren, that is, the Great Emperor of Ci Mountain; an outer ghost with the Azure Dragon is the Three Officials the Great Emperors; an inner ghost with the Azure Dragon is the Water Immortal and the Five Sages; a Zi ghost is the Northern Dipper and also the River Count and Water Official, and with the Dark Tortoise outside it is the Post Emperor, and inside it is the water injury; a Chou ghost is an earth spirit and also a ghost wounded by earth; a Yin ghost is the Eastern Peak and also a ghost wounded by a tiger; a Mao ghost is also the Eastern Peak; a Chen ghost is an earth spirit and also the Dragon King; a Si ghost is the Fire Virtue Star Lord and the silkworm house, and also a ghost wounded by a snake; a Wu ghost is the Golden Spear Master, that is, the Five Manifest Spirit Officials, and a ghost wounded by a horse; a Wei ghost is an earth spirit and also the Sangharama Earth God; a Shen ghost is the office of marshals and generals and the Injuring Office, and also a god offended in a temple or abbey; a You ghost is a Buddha image and the Mourning Killing, and also a young woman; a Xu ghost is an earth spirit and also a ghost wounded by a dog.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节前半是歌诀（到「戌逢恶犬伤人」），后半是解说（从「亥鬼为张壬」起）；原文两半接在同一行里没有断开，这里按内容分成两段引文。「亥鬼为张壬」一句在原文里紧接「戌逢恶犬伤人」，是解说的第一句。十二支各配一位或几位神鬼，大体的路数和易冒「按十二支断神鬼」（LY.ILL.YM.61）一致：亥子是水神（天门、北斗、河伯），寅卯是东岳，巳午是火德、金枪教主（五显），辰戌丑未是土神，申酉是元帅、佛象；又和黄金策旧注的「刑不善终」按地支分（LY.ILL.HJC.103：辰压死、午汤火烫、酉刀箭、亥落水、寅虎噬、巳蛇伤）相通：寅虎伤、巳蛇伤、午马伤、戌犬伤都是各支所属的动物。「驿帝」「水伤」「元帅将军之职并伤司」按字面译。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* The first half of this section is a verse (down to 'Xu meets a fierce dog wounding a person') and the second half explanation (from 'the Hai ghost is Zhang Ren'); in the original the two halves run on in the same line without a break, and they are made into two quotations here by content. The sentence 'the Hai ghost is Zhang Ren' follows 'Xu meets a fierce dog wounding a person' directly in the original and is the first sentence of the explanation. Each of the twelve branches is paired with one or several gods and ghosts, in the same general way as the Yi Mao's 'reading ghosts and spirits by the twelve branches' (LY.ILL.YM.61): Hai and Zi are water gods (the Heavenly Gate, the Northern Dipper, the River Count), Yin and Mao are the Eastern Peak, Si and Wu are the Fire Virtue and the Golden Spear Master (the Five Manifest), Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei are earth spirits, and Shen and You are the Marshal and the Buddha image; it also links with the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce on 'not dying a good death' by branch (LY.ILL.HJC.103: Chen crushed to death, Wu scalded or burned, You blade and arrow, Hai drowned, Yin bitten by a tiger, Si bitten by a snake): Yin the tiger, Si the snake, Wu the horse and Xu the dog are all the animals belonging to each branch. 'The Post Emperor', 'the water injury' and 'the office of marshals and generals and the Injuring Office' are translated literally. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
