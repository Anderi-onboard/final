# 以动爻当鬼、按数推：阳男阴女、午南子北；一动一鬼、三动三鬼；爻与鬼皆动，则以鬼所属之方为方、鬼所属之数为数（例：鬼动值土断五鬼）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有一种办法是拿动爻当鬼：阳的是男、阴的是女，午来自南方、子来自北方；动一爻就是一个鬼，动三爻就是三个鬼；如果爻和鬼都动，就以鬼所属的方位为方位、鬼所属的数为数。注说：鬼静而别的爻动，考察动的爻；鬼动而别的爻也动，考察鬼爻；动的按爻数算，鬼按数算，比如鬼动而值土，就断成五鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* There is a method of taking the moving lines as the ghosts: yang is male and yin female, Wu comes from the south and Zi from the north; one moving line is one ghost and three moving lines are three ghosts; if the lines and the ghost all move, take the direction the ghost belongs to as the direction and the number the ghost belongs to as the number. The note says: if the ghost is still and other lines move, examine the moving lines; if the ghost moves and other lines move too, examine the Pressure line; the moving ones are counted by lines and the ghost by number, so that if the ghost moves and falls on Earth it is judged five ghosts.

> [!note] 按
> 「一动一鬼，三动三鬼」数的是动爻的条数；「鬼所属之数」是五行的数，例子里土断五鬼，正是通常的生成数里土为五（水一、火二、木三、金四、土五），但这一段里书没有列出这张表。「午南子北」是把午配南、子配北，和 LY.ILL.YM.29 里离在南、坎在北是同一种配法。
>
> *Note:* 'One moving line, one ghost; three moving, three ghosts' counts the number of moving lines; 'the number the ghost belongs to' is the number of its phase, and in the example an Earth ghost is judged five, which matches the usual generating numbers in which Earth is five (Water one, Fire two, Wood three, Metal four, Earth five), though the book does not list these in this passage. 'Wu the south, Zi the north' assigns Wu to the south and Zi to the north, the same assignment as Li in the south and Kan in the north in LY.ILL.YM.29.
