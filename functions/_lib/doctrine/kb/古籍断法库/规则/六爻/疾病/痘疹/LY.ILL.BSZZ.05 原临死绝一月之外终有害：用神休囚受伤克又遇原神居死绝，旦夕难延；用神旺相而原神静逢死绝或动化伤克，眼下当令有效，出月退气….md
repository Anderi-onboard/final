# 原临死绝一月之外终有害：用神休囚受伤克又遇原神居死绝，旦夕难延；用神旺相而原神静逢死绝或动化伤克，眼下当令有效，出月退气仍有不测之害

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 原神落在死、绝的地方，一个月之外终究有害处：用神休囚又受伤克，再遇上原神落在死、绝之处，一朝一夕都难拖延；用神出现又旺相，而原神安静地遇到死、绝，或者发动却变出伤克的，眼下当令虽然看得到收功，出了这个月、退了气，仍然有想不到的祸害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Support that falls on Death or Extinction brings harm in the end after a month: if the Subject is resting or trapped and is also harmed and controlled, and the Support meets a place of Death or Extinction, it can hardly last from morning to evening; if the Subject is present and strong but the Support is still and meets Death or Extinction, or moves and changes into harm or control, then for now, in season, one may see it come to completion, but once the month is out and its force retreats there is still an unforeseen harm.

> | 如用神休囚再受伤克，又遇原神居于死绝之处，旦夕难延； | 如果用神休囚，又受到伤克，再遇上原神落在死、绝的地方，一朝一夕都难以拖延； | If the Subject is resting or trapped and is also harmed and controlled, and the Support meets a place of Death or Extinction, it can hardly last from morning to evening; |

> [!note] 按
> 注里最后有「也也」两个字连着，是原文重复，译文按一个「也」。「得令」是当令、正在旺的月份里。
>
> *Note:* The commentary's last words have two 'ye' in a row, a repetition in the text, rendered as one. 'In season' (deling) means in the month when the line is in force.
