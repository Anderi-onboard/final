# 禄系妻财、寿属父母：财是食禄、父母是寿命，卦中不可无、宜静不宜动；财空或无财不思饮食，父母动占病大忌（克制福神、官杀肆虐、服药不效、反促天年）；主象遇绝，财父入绝为禄绝、寿绝

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病，妻财是食禄，父母是寿命，卦里不能没有，宜静不宜动。卦里没有财爻或者财爻落空，是不想吃东西；父母爻动是占病的大忌，因为它克制子孙（福神），官鬼这个凶煞就能肆意逞凶，吃药不见效、祷告不灵，所以说「反促天年」。旧注还说：主象遇到绝，或变到绝地，财爻遇到叫禄绝，父母爻遇到叫寿绝，整个卦象如果凶，这是死的兆头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness, Wealth is the food-emolument and Resource is the span of life; neither may be missing from the hexagram, and both should be still and not moving. If there is no Wealth line or it is Void, he has no wish to eat; a moving Resource line is a great taboo, because it controls Output (the spirit of fortune) and lets the Pressure killer run wild, so medicine has no effect and prayer is not answered, hence 'it shortens the span of life instead'. The old commentary adds: if the main image meets extinction or changes into a place of extinction, then Wealth meeting it is the emolument cut off and Resource meeting it the life span cut off, and if the whole image is bad this is a sign of death.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「占兄弟病反宜动也」这一句是王洪绪独有的，书里没有说道理。按六亲，父母生兄弟，占兄弟的病时父母爻是原神，它动起来反倒是帮忙，大概是这个缘故。「福神」是子孙爻的另一个叫法，「食禄」是口粮、吃的。旧注比王洪绪多讲了「禄绝」「寿绝」。
>
> *Note:* The sentence 'in divining a brother's illness moving is on the contrary right' is Wang Hongxu's alone, and the book gives no reason. By the six relations Resource generates Peer, so in divining a brother's illness the Resource line is the Support, and its moving is on the contrary a help; that is probably the reason. The 'spirit of fortune' is another name for the Output line, and 'food-emolument' is one's ration, what one eats. The old commentary speaks of the 'emolument cut off' and the 'life span cut off', which Wang Hongxu does not.
