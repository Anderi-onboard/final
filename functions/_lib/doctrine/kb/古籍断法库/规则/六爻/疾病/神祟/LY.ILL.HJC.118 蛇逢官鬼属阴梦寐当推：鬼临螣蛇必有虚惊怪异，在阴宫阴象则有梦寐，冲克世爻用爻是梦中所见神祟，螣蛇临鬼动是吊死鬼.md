# 蛇逢官鬼属阴梦寐当推：鬼临螣蛇必有虚惊怪异，在阴宫阴象则有梦寐，冲克世爻用爻是梦中所见神祟，螣蛇临鬼动是吊死鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 螣蛇遇到官鬼、属阴的，梦里的事该推一推：鬼带螣蛇，一定有虚惊怪异；如果在阴宫、阴象，就有梦寐；冲克世爻、用爻的，一定是梦中见到的神祟；旧注还说螣蛇带着鬼发动，就是吊死鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Snake meeting Pressure, of the yin kind: dreams should be worked out. A ghost on the Coiling Snake surely brings false alarms and strange things; in a yin palace and a yin image there are dreams; one that clashes with or controls the Self line or the Subject line is surely a spirit seen in a dream; and the old commentary adds that if the Coiling Snake moves with the ghost, it is a hanged ghost.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3417 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼临螣蛇，必有虚惊怪异，若在阴宫阴象则有梦寐，冲克世爻用爻，必梦中所见神祟。 | 鬼带螣蛇，一定有虚惊怪异；如果在阴宫、阴象，就有梦寐；冲克世爻、用爻的，一定是梦中见到的神祟。 | A ghost on the Coiling Snake surely brings false alarms and strange things; if it is in a yin palace and a yin image, there are dreams; if it clashes with or controls the Self line or the Subject line, it is surely a spirit seen in a dream. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文最后一个字，卜筮大全作「梦寐当推」，王洪绪引作「梦寐难当」：一个是「该推算」，一个是「难以承受」，字面不同，书里没有说哪个对。旧注比王洪绪多一句「螣蛇临鬼动，则是吊死鬼」。
>
> *Note:* The verse's ending in the Bushi Daquan is 'dreams should be worked out' (dang tui), and Wang Hongxu quotes 'dreams are hard to bear' (nan dang); the wording differs and the books do not say which is right. The old commentary has one more sentence than Wang Hongxu: 'if the Coiling Snake moves on the ghost, it is a hanged ghost'.
