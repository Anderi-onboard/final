# 闷而不发皆缘用伏加伤、发而不浆只为官空增制：用神出现不受伤克大吉；用神伏藏又受伤克难过四五朝；官爻旬空月破又遇日辰动爻克害，七八朝内恐不上浆

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 痘闷着发不出来，都是因为用神伏藏又受了伤；发出来却不灌浆，只是因为官爻落空又加受制。用神出现、没有遇到日月动爻的刑冲克害，是大吉的兆头；用神伏藏、又受日月动爻的刑冲克害，要提防这个痘，很难过四五天，这是屡次应验的；官爻旬空、月破，又遇日辰动爻的克害，痘虽然起发了，七八天之内恐怕不能灌浆，难以收功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The pox stifled and unable to erupt is all because the Subject is hidden and further harmed; erupted but not filling with fluid is only because Pressure is Void and further controlled. If the Subject appears and meets no punishment, clash, control or harm from the Day, Month or moving lines, it is a very auspicious sign; if the Subject is hidden and is struck by punishment, clash, control or harm from the Day, Month or moving lines, one must guard against this pox, and it is hard to get past four or five days, as has been proved again and again; if the Pressure line is Void or in Month-break and further meets control or harm from the Day or a moving line, then although the pox may erupt, within seven or eight days it may not fill with fluid, and it is hard to bring to completion.

> | 须仔细而推详。 | 要仔细推敲。 | It must be worked out carefully. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「朝」是天，「四五朝」是四五天。「浆」「灌浆」是痘里长满浆水，是痘的一个阶段。注说「必是防痘」，意思含糊，按字面译作要提防这个痘。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhao' is a day, so 'four or five zhao' is four or five days. 'Fluid' (jiang) and 'filling with fluid' are the pox becoming full of fluid, a stage of the pox. The commentary's 'surely fang dou' is ambiguous and is rendered literally as guarding against this pox.
