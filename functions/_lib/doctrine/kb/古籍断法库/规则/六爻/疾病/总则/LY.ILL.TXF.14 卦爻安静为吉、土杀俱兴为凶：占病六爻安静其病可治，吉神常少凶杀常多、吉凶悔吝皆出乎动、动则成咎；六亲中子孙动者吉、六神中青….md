# 卦爻安静为吉、土杀俱兴为凶：占病六爻安静其病可治，吉神常少凶杀常多、吉凶悔吝皆出乎动、动则成咎；六亲中子孙动者吉、六神中青龙动者吉，余者未必皆凶也不能为福，不如安静，有吉神当权自能为福；土杀即辰戌丑未，占病逢之多凶少吉；诸煞见后凶煞章，有一件动便成凶咎

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦爻安静，重（zhòng）为济世之人；土杀一齐兴动，一定成了黄泉的客人。注说：占病，六爻安静就吉，病可以治；一卦之中凶杀常常多，而吉神常常少，吉凶悔吝都出自动，动就成咎。比如六亲之中，不过子孙动的吉；六神之中，不过青龙动的吉；其余的未必都是凶，也不能成为福，发动对我有什么益处呢？这是吉少凶多。不如卦爻安静，虽然有凶煞，终究没有遭到伤害；如果有吉神当权，自然能为福。土杀就是辰、戌、丑、未，交相变换，运行在四季之间，占病遇上它，多凶少吉。所有诸煞都见后面的凶煞章；如果有一件发动，就成了凶咎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the hexagram and lines still, weighty is he who saves the world; with Earth and the killings both rising, he is sure to be a guest of the Yellow Springs. The commentary says: in divining illness, six lines still is auspicious and the illness can be treated; in a hexagram the malign killings are often many and the lucky spirits often few; good and ill, regret and shame all come out of movement, and movement makes blame. For example, among the six relations only Output moving is auspicious; among the six spirits only the Azure Dragon moving is auspicious; the rest are not all ominous but cannot make blessing either, and what use to me is their moving? That is little good and much ill. Better that the hexagram and lines be still: though there are malign killings, in the end no harm is done; if a lucky spirit holds power, it can of itself make blessing. The Earth killings are Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei, changing in turn and moving through the four seasons; met in divining illness they are mostly ill and little good. All the killings are given in the later chapter on malign killings; if one of them moves, it makes blame.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是天玄赋疾病章的收尾：占病「安静为吉」——因为「吉少凶多」，一发动多半成咎；吉神里只有子孙动、青龙动才算吉，其余动了未必凶但也不成福，所以不如静。「土杀」指辰戌丑未。「重为济世之人」的「重」按上下文读作重（zhòng，有分量、当得起），意思是六爻安静的病人，是尚能济世（活下去）的人，原文没有解释，这里按字面译作「weighty」；「土煞俱兴，定作黄泉之客」的「土」是丘墓之土（三丘五墓）还是辰戌丑未，注里说是后者（「土杀者，即辰戌丑未」）。这句赋在易冒里也被引过，写作「天元赋」（LY.ILL.YM.12），后面加了「土煞俱兴」的解释。「安静为吉」的说法和野鹤「六爻安静、卦吉可以调理」（LY.ILL.ZS.22）、易冒「卦爻皆静」是同一个判断；和阐奥歌章「疾病章」的「六爻安静尤难瘥」（LY.ILL.CA.03）相反——那里说安静反而难愈，书里没有裁决。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* This section closes the Tianxuan Fu's chapter on illness: in divining illness 'still is auspicious' — because 'there is little good and much ill', movement mostly makes blame; among lucky spirits only Output moving and the Azure Dragon moving count as auspicious, and the rest moving are not necessarily ominous but make no blessing, so it is better to be still. 'The Earth killings' are Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei. The 'zhong' of 'weighty is he who saves the world' is read from the context as zhong (weighty, worthy of the burden), and means that a sick person whose six lines are still is one who can still serve the world (live on), which the original does not explain, and it is rendered literally as 'weighty'; whether the 'Earth' of 'Earth and killings both rising, sure to be a guest of the Yellow Springs' is the earth of the mounds and tombs or Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei, the commentary says the latter ('the Earth killings are Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei'). This verse is also quoted in the Yi Mao as 'the Tianyuan Fu' (LY.ILL.YM.12). 'Still is auspicious' is the same judgment as the Old Man of Yehe's 'with six lines still and the hexagram auspicious, it can be treated by nursing' (LY.ILL.ZS.22) and the Yi Mao's 'the hexagram and lines all still'; it is opposite to 'six lines still is harder still to cure' in the chapter on illness of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.03), which says that stillness on the contrary makes it hard to cure, and the books do not settle it. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
