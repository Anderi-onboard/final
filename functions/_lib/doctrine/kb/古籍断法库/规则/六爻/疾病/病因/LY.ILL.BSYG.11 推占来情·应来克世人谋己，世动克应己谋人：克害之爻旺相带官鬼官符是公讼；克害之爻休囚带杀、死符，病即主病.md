# 推占来情·应来克世人谋己，世动克应己谋人：克害之爻旺相带官鬼官符是公讼；克害之爻休囚带杀、死符，病即主病

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推占来情的歌说：应来克世，是别人谋我；世动克应，是我谋别人；旺相又生着官，会成阻碍；休囚又带着杀、鬼，就是互相侵犯。自注说：彼此相克相害，也要看爻的旺衰；克害的爻旺相、又带官鬼官符，是官司；克害的爻休囚、又带着杀和死符，病就是主病，也就是这件事主的是病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verses on reading the purpose of the asker say: when the Other comes to control the Self, others are scheming against me; when the Self moves to control the Other, I am scheming against others; if strong and generating officials, it makes obstruction; if resting or trapped and carrying a killing and a ghost, they encroach on each other. Its own note says: where each controls and harms the other, one must also look at whether the line is strong or weak; if the line that controls and harms is thriving and strong and carries Pressure or the Official Tally, it is a lawsuit; if the line that controls and harms is resting or trapped and carries killings and the Death Tally, the illness is the main matter, that is, what it signals is illness.

## 《大易断例卜筮元龟目》括号里的自注(没标「虎易注」的)

> [!quote]+ [[大易断例卜筮元龟目]] 第 471 行 · The Dayi Duanli's own bracketed notes (those not marked as the modern editor's)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 所言相克者，即相克相害也。 | 这里说的相克，就是相克、相害。 | What is called control here is control and harm. |
> | 又彼此相克害，亦当视爻休旺。 | 又彼此相克相害，也要看爻是休囚还是旺相。 | Where each controls and harms the other, one must also look at whether the line is resting or strong. |
> | 如克害旺相，而带官鬼官符者，即公讼也。 | 如果克害的是旺相的，又带着官鬼、官符，就是官司。 | If what controls and harms is thriving and strong and carries Pressure or the Official Tally, it is a lawsuit. |
> | 如克害之爻休囚，带杀诸死符，病即主病也。 | 如果克害的爻休囚，又带着杀和死符，病就是主病了。 | If the line that controls and harms is resting or trapped and carries killings and the Death Tally, the illness is the main matter. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「病即主病也」读作：克害的爻休囚、又带杀和死符的时候，这件事主的就是病。「旺相与生官作碍」的「官」书里没有说是官鬼还是官司，按字面译成「officials」。「应来克世人谋己」一联前半讲的是应克世、世克应，也是看「谁在谋谁」，后半才讲旺衰带杀，所以收一联四句，没有只取讲病的一句。
>
> *Note:* 'The illness is the main matter' is read as: when the line that controls and harms is resting or trapped and carries killings and the Death Tally, what the matter signals is illness. The 'official' of 'if strong and generating officials' is not said in the book to be Pressure or a lawsuit, and is rendered literally as 'officials'. The first half of the couplet 'when the Other comes to control the Self, others scheme against me' is about the Other controlling the Self and the Self controlling the Other, that is, who is scheming against whom, and only the second half about strength and killings, so the four lines of the couplet are taken and not only the line on illness.
