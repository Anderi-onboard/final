# 彭城密诀·空亡：病值空亡宜作福；久病空亡身不真(身下亡)；父母空亡忧病生；女值空亡忧病生

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 彭城的密诀把空亡落在各处的后果编成了一首歌诀，其中管病的有这几句：病落了空亡，适合做福事（断易天机明写是「暴病」，卜筮大全和火珠林只写「病」）；久病落了空亡，断易天机写「身不真」，卜筮大全和火珠林写「身下亡」；父母爻落了空亡，要担心生病。断易天机另有一句「女值空亡忧病生」，另外两本没有。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The secret verse of Pengcheng sets out, in a song, what follows when each thing falls Void. The lines that concern illness: when the illness falls Void it is fitting to perform blessing rites (the Duanyi Tianji says outright 'sudden illness'; the Bushi Daquan and the Huozhu Lin say only 'illness'); when a long illness falls Void, the Duanyi Tianji reads 'the body is not true' while the Bushi Daquan and the Huozhu Lin read 'the body goes down and is lost'; when Resource falls Void, worry that illness will arise. The Duanyi Tianji alone adds the line 'if a woman falls Void, worry that illness will arise'.

> [!warning] 打架
> 久病落了空亡，三本的写法不一样：断易天机写「身不真」，火珠林写「身下亡」，卜筮大全写「身【下亡】」（把「下亡」两个字加了方括号）。「不真」是说这个人（身）靠不住，「下亡」字面是人往下去、亡掉，凶险程度不一样，三本都没有解释。另外，易林补遗说「暴病逢空可救、久病逢空必死」（LY.ILL.EL.11）——更接近「下亡」那种读法；野鹤驳「主空无救不分近久」（LY.ILL.ZS.05）。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For a long illness that falls Void the three books write differently: the Duanyi Tianji has 'the body is not true', the Huozhu Lin has 'the body goes down and is lost', and the Bushi Daquan has 'the body [goes down and is lost]' with the two characters for 'down and lost' put in square brackets. 'Not true' says the person cannot be relied on, whereas 'down and lost' literally says the person goes down and perishes; the severity differs, and none of the three explains it. The Yilin Buyi, besides, says 'a sudden illness meeting Void can be saved, a long illness meeting Void must die' (LY.ILL.EL.11), which is closer to the 'down and lost' reading, while the Old Man of Yehe rejects 'when the main line is Void there is no rescue, without distinguishing recent from long illness' (LY.ILL.ZS.05). The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这首「彭城密诀」是一首讲空亡的歌诀，原本每一句管一类事：男（子）落空亡忧远行、财落空亡难把捉、鬼落空亡官事停、妻落空亡、室女空亡外有情、宅落空亡急作福、兄弟空亡不得力、子孙空亡主伶仃；这里只收管病的几句，其余的属于别的问题域。三本的字句有几处出入，一并记在这里：一、第一句，断易天机写「男值空亡忧远行，女值空亡忧病生」，卜筮大全、火珠林写「子落空亡忧远行」，没有「女值」那一句；二、病那一句，断易天机写「暴病空亡宜作福」，另两本写「病值空亡宜作福」，它们下一句就是「久病」，所以这里的「病」多半是和久病对着说的新病、暴病，这是读法，不是书里写的；三、「妻值空亡」后面，卜筮大全写「妻有孕」，断易天机和火珠林写「妻有厄」；四、断易天机多出「被他空亡徒事缕，贼来不至空有声」两句；五、卜筮大全开头写「秘诀」，另两本写「密诀」（断易天机的括号里写成「密决」）。「作福」是做祈福还愿一类的事，「身不真」的「不真」书里没有解释，译文只照字面写成「靠不住」。断易天机里这一首歌诀是排在括号里的小注，卜筮大全里它是正文「诀云」后面的一段，火珠林里也在括号里；火珠林括号里那句「记住彭城关于空亡的密诀」是整理者的话，不收。卜筮大全这一段后面有虎易的评注，不收。
>
> *Note:* This 'secret verse of Pengcheng' is a song about Void, in which each line governs one kind of matter: a man (or Output) falling Void, worry about a far journey; Wealth Void, hard to grasp; Pressure Void, the lawsuit stops; the wife Void; the unmarried girl Void, an affair outside; the house Void, quickly perform blessing rites; Peer Void, no strength; Output Void, loneliness. Only the lines about illness are taken here; the rest belong to other domains. The three books differ in several places, recorded together here: first, the opening line: the Duanyi Tianji has 'a man falling Void, worry about a far journey; a woman falling Void, worry that illness will arise', while the Bushi Daquan and the Huozhu Lin have 'Output falling Void, worry about a far journey' and lack the line on the woman; second, the line on illness: the Duanyi Tianji writes 'a sudden illness falling Void, it is fitting to perform blessing rites' while the other two write 'the illness falling Void', and the very next line is 'a long illness', so the 'illness' here is most likely the new or sudden illness set against the long one, which is a reading and not what the book says; third, after 'the wife falling Void', the Bushi Daquan has 'the wife is with child' while the Duanyi Tianji and the Huozhu Lin have 'the wife has a calamity'; fourth, the Duanyi Tianji has two extra lines, 'when it is the other side that is Void, the affair is tangled for nothing; when thieves are said to come, they do not arrive, an empty rumour'; fifth, the Bushi Daquan opens with 'secret verse' where the other two write 'secret verse' with a variant character (the Duanyi Tianji's bracket even misprints the second word). 'Performing blessing rites' means doing rites for fortune and thanksgiving; the 'not true' of 'the body is not true' is not explained in the book and the translation renders it only literally as 'not to be relied on'. In the Duanyi Tianji this verse is a small note in brackets; in the Bushi Daquan it is the part after 'the verse says' in the main text; in the Huozhu Lin it is again in brackets, and the phrase 'remember the secret verse of Pengcheng on Void' in those brackets is the editor's own words and is not quoted. The modern commentary of Hu Yi that follows in the Bushi Daquan is not quoted.
