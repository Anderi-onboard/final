# 木鬼配六兽：木为杖责之魂、疯疾悬梁之鬼；症缓山神五圣、症急东岳家堂；白虎伤司、青龙枷锁之愿与喜庆之神、勾陈九良星煞、螣蛇树圣山神、朱雀玄武草野三郎

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木鬼，是被杖责的魂，疯病、上吊的鬼：症状缓的，是山神、五圣；症状急的，是东兵（东岳）、家堂。白虎同在，就在门外酬谢伤神，用锣鼓追送；青龙同位，就在堂前酬还原愿，用笙萧；勾陈，是九良星煞；螣蛇，是树圣、山神；朱雀、玄武，是草野三郎，祭祀时要提防，灾非都落在你身上。注说：木鬼，主受刑责、戴枷锁的鬼、疯病鬼、上吊死的鬼；症状缓的，必是冒犯了山神、土地和五圣尊神；症状急的，必是干犯了东岳和家堂的众神；带白虎，赶快酬谢大小伤司；带青龙，宜酬还枷锁的愿，以及祷告喜庆的神；带勾陈，是冒犯了九良星；土带螣蛇，如果不是山神，一定是树上的神，又是作犯土；带朱雀或玄武，都是草野三郎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wood is the soul of one beaten with the rod, the ghost of madness and of hanging from a beam: if mild, the Mountain God and the Five Sages; if severe, the Eastern Soldiers (the Eastern Peak) and the household shrine. With the White Tiger in the same place, thank the Injuring spirit outside the gate and send it off with gongs and drums; with the Azure Dragon in the same place, redeem the vow in front of the hall with pipes and flutes; Hook Earth is the Nine Good Stars and the killing stars; the Coiling Snake is the Tree Sage or Mountain God; the Vermilion Bird and the Dark Tortoise are the Third Lord of the wilds, and in sacrificing be careful, for calamity and blame will all fall on you. The commentary says: a Wood ghost governs ghosts of punishment, the cangue and fetters, ghosts of madness and ghosts of hanging; if the symptoms are mild, it is surely from offending the Mountain God, the Earth God and the honoured Five Sages; if urgent, surely from offending the Eastern Peak and the gods of the household shrine; with the White Tiger, quickly make offering to the greater and lesser Injuring Offices; with the Azure Dragon, redeem the vow of the cangue and pray to the spirits of festivity; with Hook Earth, there is an offence against the Nine Good Stars; Earth with the Coiling Snake, if not the Mountain God, must be the spirit in a tree, and is also the offence-making earth; with the Vermilion Bird or the Dark Tortoise, it is the Third Lord of the wilds.

> [!note] 按
> 歌诀里的「东兵」和解说里的「东岳」应是同一位（东岳），歌诀字形可能有误，这里照字面译作「东兵」并在括号里标出；解说里「刑责加析鬼」的「加析」疑是「枷杻」（枷和手铐脚镣），按上下文译作枷锁。歌诀最后一句「祭虞必灾非统汝」原文很晦涩，「虞」和「统汝」都没有解释，这里按字面译，在英文里标了「意思不清」。「土带螣蛇」的「土」指鬼是土爻时，原文行文在木鬼一节里放了这一句，读法没有把握。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地（苕溪一带）的民间神祇和星煞，书里一个也没有解释（章末自己说别的郡的神司不同），这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* The 'Eastern Soldiers' in the verse and the 'Eastern Peak' in the explanation should be one figure (the Eastern Peak), the form in the verse being perhaps a slip, and it is translated literally as 'Eastern Soldiers' with the identification given in brackets; the 'jia xi' in 'ghosts of punishment, jia xi' in the explanation looks like 'jia niu' (the cangue and handcuffs and fetters) and is translated from the context as cangue and fetters. The last line of the verse, 'in sacrificing to Yu, calamity and blame all on you', is very obscure in the original, neither 'Yu' nor 'tong ru' being explained, and it is translated literally with a mark in the English that the meaning is unclear. 'Earth with the Coiling Snake' means the ghost being an Earth line; the original puts this sentence in the section on Wood ghosts, and the reading is uncertain. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain (the book itself says at the end of the chapter that the gods of other prefectures differ), and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
