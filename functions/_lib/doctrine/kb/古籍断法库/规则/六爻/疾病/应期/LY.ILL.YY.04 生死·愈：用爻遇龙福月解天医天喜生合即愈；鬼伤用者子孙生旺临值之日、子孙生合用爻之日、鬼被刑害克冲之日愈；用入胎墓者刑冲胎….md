# 生死·愈：用爻遇龙福月解天医天喜生合即愈；鬼伤用者子孙生旺临值之日、子孙生合用爻之日、鬼被刑害克冲之日愈；用入胎墓者刑冲胎墓之日愈；死绝者生用之日愈；伏藏而元神旺忌神衰者用爻当值之日愈；可救者待鬼衰身旺世生之日愈

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻遇到青龙、福德、月解、天医、天喜这些吉神，临在它上面或者生它、合它，病就会好。鬼伤到用爻的，子孙生旺、当值的那天好，子孙来生用爻、合用爻的那天好，鬼被刑、害、克、冲的那天好。用爻入胎、入墓的，刑冲那个胎、墓的日子好；用爻死、绝的，生它的那天好；用爻在『病』位的，它生旺的那天好；用爻伏藏而原神旺、忌神衰的，用爻当值的那天好；用爻的墓遇空、绝遇生、克用的鬼遇克，都可以救，等鬼衰、身旺、世被生的那天好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line meets the auspicious spirits Dragon, Fortune, Month Release, Heavenly Doctor or Heavenly Joy, sitting on it or generating or combining with it, he recovers. If the ghost harms the Subject line, he recovers on the day Output is strong and on duty, on the day Output generates or combines with the Subject line, and on the day the ghost is punished, harmed, controlled or clashed. If the Subject line enters Womb or a tomb, he recovers on the day that punishes or clashes the Womb or tomb; if it is in Death or Extinction, on the day that generates it; if it is at Sickness, on the day it is born or strong; if the Subject line is hidden but the Support is strong and the Adversary weak, on the day the Subject line comes on duty; and if its tomb meets Void, its Extinction meets generation, or the ghost that harms it meets control, he can be saved, and recovers on the day the ghost is weak, the Body strong and the Self generated.

> [!note] 按
> 「龙福月解天医天喜」是几个吉神：龙是青龙，福是福德，月解、天医、天喜是神煞，书里没有列表。「胎」是十二长生里的胎位。三个断例都在书末的「附断例」里，这里分别挂在它们验证的判断下：解之困（元神独发又化进神，申酉日愈）、革之困（忌神逢绝、官遇长生，戊申日愈）、履之小畜（财遇长生，甲申日愈）。
>
> *Note:* 'The Dragon, Fortune, Month Release, Heavenly Doctor and Heavenly Joy' are auspicious spirits: the Dragon is the Azure Dragon, Fortune is Fude, and Month Release, Heavenly Doctor and Heavenly Joy are spirit-stars, which the book does not list. 'Womb' is the Womb stage of the twelve life stages. The three cases are from the 'appended cases' at the end of the section and are hung here under the judgments they bear out: Xie into Kun (the Support moving alone and changing into an Advancing spirit, cured on a Shen or You day), Ge into Kun (the Adversary meeting Extinction and Pressure meeting Birth, cured on the Wushen day), and Lu into Xiaoxu (Wealth meeting Birth, cured on the Jiashen day).
