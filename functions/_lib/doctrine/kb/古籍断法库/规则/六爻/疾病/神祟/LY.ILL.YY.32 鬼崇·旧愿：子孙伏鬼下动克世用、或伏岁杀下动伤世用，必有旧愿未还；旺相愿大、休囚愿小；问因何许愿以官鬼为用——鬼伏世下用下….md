# 鬼崇·旧愿：子孙伏鬼下动克世用、或伏岁杀下动伤世用，必有旧愿未还；旺相愿大、休囚愿小；问因何许愿以官鬼为用——鬼伏世下用下是自己许、伏父母财子下是为父母妻子许、伏鬼下是因病讼许、伏兄下是因兄弟朋友争斗赌博许；六亲化出鬼亦然

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 又有旧愿没还的：子孙伏在鬼下面、发动克世爻、用爻，或者子孙伏在岁煞下面、发动伤世爻、用爻的，一定是有旧愿没有还；旺相的愿大，休囚的愿小。问是因为什么许的愿，以官鬼为用：鬼伏在世爻下、用爻下的，是为自己许的愿；鬼伏在父母、财、子孙下的，是为父母、妻子、子女许的愿；鬼伏在鬼下的，是因为病、官司许的愿；鬼伏在兄下的，是为兄弟朋友、争斗赌博许的愿。六亲变出鬼的也是这样（出自《源髓诀》）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* There is also an old vow not yet paid: Output hidden beneath the ghost and moving to control the Self and Subject, or Output hidden beneath the Year-killing star and moving to harm the Self and Subject, means surely an old vow is not yet paid; if strong the vow is large, if resting or trapped small. To ask why the vow was made, the Pressure line is the Subject: a ghost hidden beneath the Self or the Subject line, a vow made for oneself; hidden beneath Resource, Wealth or Output, made for parents, wife or children; hidden beneath a ghost, made over illness or lawsuit; hidden beneath Peer, made over brothers, friends, brawls or gambling. The same holds when a relation changes out into a ghost (from the Yuan Sui Jue).

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「岁杀」按字面是岁上的煞，书里没有再解释。「出源髓诀」的「源髓诀」是易隐引的书名，书里没有再介绍。这一条和黄金策讲的「持世则未酬旧愿，伏为有口无心」（LY.ILL.HJC.110）是同一类判断：都是从鬼爻的位置看是不是有旧愿未还；黄金策看鬼是不是持世、伏世下，这里另看子孙伏在鬼下。
>
> *Note:* 'The Year-killing star' (suisha) is literally a killing star of the year and the book does not explain further. The 'Yuan Sui Jue' is a work the Yi Yin cites and the book does not introduce. This entry is a judgment of the same kind as the Huangjin Ce's 'holding the Self: the old vow is not paid; hidden: a spoken word with no heart in it' (LY.ILL.HJC.110): both tell from the position of the Pressure line whether an old vow is unpaid; the Huangjin Ce looks at whether the ghost holds the Self or is hidden beneath it, and this looks in addition at Output hidden beneath the ghost.
