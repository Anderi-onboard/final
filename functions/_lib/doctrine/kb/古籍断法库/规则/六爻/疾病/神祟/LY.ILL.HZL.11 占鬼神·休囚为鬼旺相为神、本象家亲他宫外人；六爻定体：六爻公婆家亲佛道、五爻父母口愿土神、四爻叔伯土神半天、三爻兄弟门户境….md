# 占鬼神·休囚为鬼旺相为神、本象家亲他宫外人；六爻定体：六爻公婆家亲佛道、五爻父母口愿土神、四爻叔伯土神半天、三爻兄弟门户境神、二爻夫妻土地家神、初爻小口灶君司命

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 休囚的是鬼，旺相的是神；在本象里的是家亲，在他宫里的是外人。六爻各有所主：六爻是公婆、家亲、佛道；五爻是父母、口愿、土神；四爻是叔伯、土神、半天；三爻是兄弟、门户、境神；二爻是夫妻、土地、家神；初爻是小口、灶君、司命。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resting or trapped it is a ghost, strong it is a spirit; in the own image it is the family ancestors, in another palace an outsider. The six lines each govern something: the sixth line the in-laws, the family ancestors and Buddhist and Daoist spirits; the fifth the parents, vows and the Earth spirit; the fourth uncles, the Earth spirit and the half-sky spirit; the third brothers, the doors and the district spirit; the second husband and wife, the Earth God and the household spirit; the first the children, the Hearth Lord and the Life-Governing Spirit.

> [!warning] 打架
> 爻位配鬼神，几本书的配法不同。火珠林这里：初爻小口、灶君、司命；二爻夫妻、土地、家神；三爻兄弟、门户、境神；四爻叔伯、土神、半天；五爻父母、口愿、土神；六爻公婆、家亲、佛道。卜筮全书的六位表（LY.ILL.BSQS.02）：初爻井神、二爻司命、三爻家先、四爻土地、五爻社司、六爻天神。易林补遗（LY.ILL.EL.37）是「六爻上仓至圣坟墓之神、五爻中界东岳、四爻右仪门将军、三爻家堂郡王府城隍、二爻县城隍灶神」这一路。同一位置配的神不同：二爻火珠林是土地、家神，卜筮全书是司命，易林补遗是灶神；初爻火珠林是灶君、司命，卜筮全书是井神；六爻火珠林是佛道，卜筮全书是天神，易林补遗是坟墓之神。各家按各地的习俗立说，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Books match line positions to ghosts and spirits differently. The Huozhu Lin here: first line the children, the Hearth Lord, the Life-Governing Spirit; second husband and wife, the Earth God, the household spirit; third brothers, the doors, the district spirit; fourth uncles, the Earth spirit, the half-sky spirit; fifth parents, vows, the Earth spirit; sixth in-laws, family ancestors, Buddhist and Daoist spirits. The table of the six positions in the Bushi Quanshu (LY.ILL.BSQS.02): first line the well spirit, second the Life-Governing Spirit, third the family ancestors, fourth the Earth God, fifth the village altar spirit, sixth the heavenly spirit. The Yilin Buyi (LY.ILL.EL.37) runs along the lines of 'the sixth line the spirit of the high granary and the sage's tomb, the fifth the Eastern Peak of the middle realm, the fourth the general of the right gate, the third the household shrine, the prince and the city god of the prefecture, the second the county city god and the Kitchen God'. The spirit matched to the same position differs: for the second line the Huozhu Lin has the Earth God and the household spirit, the Bushi Quanshu the Life-Governing Spirit and the Yilin Buyi the Kitchen God; for the first line the Huozhu Lin has the Hearth Lord and the Life-Governing Spirit and the Bushi Quanshu the well spirit; for the sixth line the Huozhu Lin has Buddhist and Daoist spirits, the Bushi Quanshu the heavenly spirit and the Yilin Buyi the spirits of the tombs. Each sets it up by the customs of its own place, and the books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节头两句是总纲：休囚的是鬼（作祟的死者），旺相的是神（正神）；鬼落在本象里是家里的先人（家亲），落在别的宫里是外人。「六爻定体」是一张按爻位配对象的表，原书是一格一格排的：每一爻三样，第一样像是六亲的位置（六爻公婆、五爻父母、四爻叔伯、三爻兄弟、二爻夫妻、初爻小口），后两样是鬼神（家亲、佛道；口愿、土神；土神、半天；门户、境神；土地、家神；灶君、司命），但书里没有说三样各是什么，这里只照原样列出，不分栏。「公婆」是丈夫的父母（或祖父母），「境神」是一方境内的神，「半天」是半天之神（取象于四爻在半空），原文没有解释，只是字面译。表末的「（2）五行鬼：」是下一节的小标题，连在同一行里，所以放在这一条的末尾。这一张表（初灶君司命、二土地家神、三门户境神……）和卜筮全书的六爻诸占定位里的鬼神一行（初井神、二司命、三家先、四土地、五社司、六天神，LY.ILL.BSQS.02）、易林补遗的按爻位断神鬼（LY.ILL.EL.37）是同一类，配法各家不同，见「打架」。
>
> *Note:* The first two sentences of this section are the general outline: resting or trapped it is a ghost (a dead person who brings a curse), strong it is a spirit (a proper god); a ghost falling in the own image is the family's ancestors (family ancestors), and falling in another palace it is an outsider. 'The six lines fix the body' is a table matching line positions to objects, laid out cell by cell in the original: three items to each line, the first seeming to be the position of a relation (the sixth line in-laws, the fifth parents, the fourth uncles, the third brothers, the second husband and wife, the first children), and the latter two being ghosts and spirits (family ancestors, Buddhist and Daoist; vows, the Earth spirit; the Earth spirit, the half-sky spirit; the doors, the district spirit; the Earth God, the household spirit; the Hearth Lord, the Life-Governing Spirit), but the book does not say what each of the three is, and here they are only listed as they stand without separating columns. 'Gong po' are the husband's parents (or grandparents), 'the district spirit' is the spirit of a territory, and 'the half-sky spirit' is the spirit of half-heaven (taking the image of the fourth line as being in mid-air), which the original does not explain, and they are rendered literally. The '(2) the ghosts of the five phases' at the end of the table is the small heading of the next section, run into the same line, and so it is placed at the end of this entry. This table (first Hearth Lord and Life-Governing Spirit, second Earth God and household spirit, third the doors and district spirit ...) is of the same kind as the row for spirits in the table of the positions of the lines in the Bushi Quanshu (first the well spirit, second the Life-Governing Spirit, third the family ancestors, fourth the Earth God, fifth the village altar spirit, sixth the heavenly spirit, LY.ILL.BSQS.02) and the Yilin Buyi's judging spirits by line position (LY.ILL.EL.37), and each puts the matching differently, see the dispute.
