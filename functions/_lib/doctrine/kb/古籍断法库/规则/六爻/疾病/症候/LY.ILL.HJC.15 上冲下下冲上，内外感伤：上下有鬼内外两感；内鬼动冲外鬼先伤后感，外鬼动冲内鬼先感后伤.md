# 上冲下下冲上，内外感伤：上下有鬼内外两感；内鬼动冲外鬼先伤后感，外鬼动冲内鬼先感后伤

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上下卦都有鬼，病一定是里外两处感受。两个鬼都动、或者都静，是一起受病；两个鬼互相冲，刚感受了邪就受了伤。内卦的鬼动来冲外卦的鬼，是先受伤后感邪；外卦的鬼动来冲内卦的鬼，是先感邪后受伤。要分轻重，用刑、合来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure in both the upper and lower trigrams, the illness is surely a double contagion of inside and outside. Both Pressure lines moving, or both still, means falling ill together; two Pressure lines clashing each other means hardly is the evil felt than harm is done. If the inner Pressure moves and clashes the outer, it is first harm and then contagion; if the outer Pressure moves and clashes the inner, first contagion and then harm. To tell heavy from light, judge by punishment and combination.

> | 俱动俱静者，一同受病； | 都动或者都静的，是一起受病； | both moving or both still, they fall ill together; |
> | 二鬼自冲者，适感而适愈也。 | 两个鬼自己互相冲的，刚感受了邪就痊愈。 | two Pressure lines clashing each other of themselves: hardly is the evil felt than it is cured. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 两鬼互冲的结果，旧注和王洪绪相反。旧注说「适感而遂伤也」，刚感受了邪就受了伤；卜筮正宗写「适感而适愈也」，刚感受就好了。书里没有再解释，也没有别家的话可对。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two disagree on what follows when two Pressure lines clash each other. The old commentary says 'hardly is the evil felt than harm is done', while the Bushi Zhengzong writes 'hardly is it felt than it is cured'. The book explains no further and there is no other voice to check against.
