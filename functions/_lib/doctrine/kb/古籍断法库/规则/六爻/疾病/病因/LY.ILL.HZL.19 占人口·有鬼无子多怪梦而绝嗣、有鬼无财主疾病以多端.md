# 占人口·有鬼无子多怪梦而绝嗣、有鬼无财主疾病以多端

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有鬼没有子孙，多怪梦、并且绝嗣；有鬼没有财，主疾病多端。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With a ghost and no Output, many strange dreams and no heir; with a ghost and no Wealth, illness in many forms.

## 《火珠林·占人口》原文

> [!quote]+ [[火珠林]] 第 491 行 · Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Divining household members", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 有鬼无子，多怪梦而绝嗣， | 有鬼没有子孙，多怪梦、并且绝嗣； | With a ghost and no Output, many strange dreams and no heir; |
> | 有鬼无财，主疾病以多端。 | 有鬼没有财，主疾病多端。 | with a ghost and no Wealth, illness in many forms. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句在火珠林「占人口」一节里，那一节讲的是家里几口人的命运（卦无父母占人寿命不延、爻无妻财兄伯贫穷……），这里只收有关疾病的一对句子。「有鬼无财」是卦里有官鬼、没有财爻（禄命）：财是禄命，没有财则鬼无制、病多。这和本文「以财为禄，以鬼为祟」（LY.ILL.HZL.04）是同一个道理。「以多端」按「多端」译。
>
> *Note:* This sentence is in the Huozhu Lin's section on 'divining household members', which is about the fortunes of the several people in a house (with no Resource in the hexagram, the person's lifespan does not extend, with no Wealth line the elder brother is poor ...), and here only the pair of lines about illness is taken. 'A ghost and no Wealth' is a Pressure line in the hexagram and no Wealth line (salary and life): Wealth is the salary and life, and without it the ghost is unchecked and the illness is many. This is the same principle as the text 'take Wealth as the salary and the ghost as the curse' (LY.ILL.HZL.04). 'Yi duo duan' is rendered 'in many forms'.
