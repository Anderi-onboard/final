# 财动卦中宜调脾胃：财爻主饮食，宜旺不宜空（空则不思饮食），宜静不宜动（动则生助官鬼）；恐多食伤脾胃，须调养

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻在卦中发动，适合调理脾胃：财爻代表饮食，适合旺、不适合空，空了就不想吃东西；适合安静、不适合发动，发动了就生助官鬼，恐怕因为吃得太多而伤了脾胃，所以要调养。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth moves in the hexagram, it is right to regulate the spleen and stomach: Wealth stands for food and drink; it should be strong and not Void, for if Void the patient has no wish to eat; it should be at rest and not move, for if it moves it generates and helps Pressure, and for fear that eating too much will harm the spleen and stomach, it must be regulated and nourished.
