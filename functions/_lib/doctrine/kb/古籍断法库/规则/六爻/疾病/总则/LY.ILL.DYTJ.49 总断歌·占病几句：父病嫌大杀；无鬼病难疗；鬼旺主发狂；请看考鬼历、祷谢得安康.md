# 总断歌·占病几句：父病嫌大杀；无鬼病难疗；鬼旺主发狂；请看考鬼历、祷谢得安康

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 总断歌里说到病的几句：父亲有病，忌讳大杀；没有鬼，病难以治疗；鬼旺，主发狂；请看考察鬼历，祷告酬谢，得到安康。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The lines of the song of general judgments that speak of illness: for a father's illness the Great Killing is disliked; with no ghost the illness is hard to treat; with the ghost strong it means madness; look up and examine the ghost calendar, pray and give thanks, and health and peace are gained.

> [!note] 按
> 这是断易天机里一首长的「总断歌」中说到病的几句，歌里每句后面的括号是今人加的注释（不收），所以这里每一句单独收一段。「大杀」是大杀（天哭杀一类的凶神，LY.ILL.DYTJ.33）。「鬼旺主发狂」的「发狂」是神志失常，和天玄赋「朱雀鬼：狂言乱语」（LY.ILL.TXF.03）、卜筮元龟「狂言乱语失精神」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.10）是同一类。「考鬼历」读不确定，字面是「察看鬼的历（历书）」，大概是查是哪一路鬼神、在哪一天作祟，再祷谢；「祷谢」是祷告和酬谢（还愿）。「无鬼病难疗」和「卦中无鬼病难医」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.02）是同一条。歌里「空亡母不长」一句不能确定是不是专说病，不收。
>
> *Note:* These are the lines that speak of illness in a long 'song of general judgments' in the Duanyi Tianji, and the brackets after each line in the song are notes added by a modern hand (not quoted), so here each line is taken as a passage of its own. The 'Great Killing' is a fierce spirit of the Weeping Killing kind (LY.ILL.DYTJ.33). 'With the ghost strong it means madness' has 'madness' as losing one's mind, of the same kind as the Tianxuan Fu's 'the Vermilion Bird ghost: raving and wild speech' (LY.ILL.TXF.03) and the Bushi Yuangui's 'raving and wild speech, losing the wits' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.10). 'Examining the ghost calendar' is not certain and is literally 'looking at and examining the ghost's calendar', probably checking which ghost or spirit brings the curse and on which day, and then praying and giving thanks; 'praying and giving thanks' is praying and thanking (redeeming a vow). 'With no ghost the illness is hard to treat' is the same entry as 'with no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to treat' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02). The line in the song 'Void, the mother is not long-lived' cannot be taken to be specially about illness and is not taken.
