# 世上临鬼不可随官入墓：世持官鬼本非吉兆，遇日辰带墓爻、本命爻随官入墓、世爻用爻化入鬼墓、带鬼动化入墓、持鬼墓发动，皆主大凶

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻持着官鬼，本来就不是吉兆；如果再遇到日辰带着墓爻，就叫「随官入墓」；本命爻随官入墓也凶。世爻、用爻变入鬼的墓，以及世爻、命爻带着鬼动、变入墓的，还有用爻、命爻、世爻持着鬼墓发动的，都主大凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line holding the Self line is in itself no lucky sign; if it also meets a tomb line carried by the Day, this is called 'following Pressure into the tomb'; a natal line following Pressure into the tomb is also unlucky. The Self or Subject line changing into the tomb of Pressure, the Self or natal line carrying Pressure, moving and changing into a tomb, and the Subject, natal or Self line holding the tomb of Pressure and moving, all mean great misfortune.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2380 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占自己病，若世上临鬼入墓于日辰，或化入墓库于爻，固非吉兆，世爻持鬼墓发动亦凶。 | 凡是占自己的病，如果世爻上临着鬼、在日辰上入墓，或者变入爻上的墓库，本来就不是吉兆，世爻持着鬼墓发动也凶。 | In divining one's own illness, if a Pressure line sits on the Self line and enters the tomb at the Day, or changes into the tomb of a line, it is in itself no lucky sign, and the Self line holding the tomb of Pressure and moving is also unlucky. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「命爻」「本命爻」是求占人的本命（出生年的地支）在卦里落的那一爻。「随官入墓」是官鬼入墓时，世爻或命爻跟着它一起入墓。王洪绪只讲占自己的病，旧注没有这个限定，而且列的情形多得多。
>
> *Note:* The 'natal line' is the line in the hexagram where the asker's own fate (the branch of his birth year) falls. 'Following Pressure into the tomb' is the Self or natal line entering the tomb together with the Pressure line when it does. Wang Hongxu speaks only of divining one's own illness, which the old commentary does not limit, and the old commentary lists many more cases.
