# 身命章·遇令星如风摇干、逢绝地似雨倾花（占婴孩：子孙得地健旺凶神克战无事，死绝之地决难生养）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》身命章占婴孩时说：「遇到令星，像风摇树干；逢到绝地，像雨打落花。」旧注：凡遇凶神来克战，子孙爻得地、健旺，一定无事，即使见一点小小的悔，也像微风摇树干，伤不了它；如果在死绝之地，一定难以生养，一有克战，就像暴雨打花，很少不残败的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the person and fate says, on divining an infant: 'Meeting the ruling star is like wind shaking a trunk; meeting a place of extinction is like rain beating down flowers.' The old commentary: whenever an ominous spirit attacks, if the Output line holds its ground and is strong, nothing will happen, and even with a slight regret it is like a light breeze shaking a trunk and cannot harm it; if it is in a place of death or extinction, it can hardly be reared, and once attacked it is like a sudden rain beating down flowers, and few escape ruin.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1777 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡父母动克子，若得子孙值日辰月建，虽见小晦，犹微风摇干无妨； | 凡是父母爻发动克子孙，如果子孙爻值着日辰、月建，即使见到一点小小的晦暗，也像微风摇动树干，没有妨碍； | Whenever the Resource line moves to control Output, if Output holds the Day or the Month branch, even with a slight dimming it is like a light breeze shaking a trunk and does no harm; |
> | 若逢墓绝有克战，如骤雨倾花有损。 | 如果遇到墓绝又有克战，就像暴雨打花，有损伤。 | if it meets tomb and extinction with attack besides, it is like a sudden rain beating down flowers, and there is damage. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「令星」是月建（当令的那个支），所以「遇令星」是子孙爻值月建，得时。「风摇干」「雨倾花」是比喻：前者树干不动，后者花被打落。这一段在身命章占婴孩的几联之间，讲的是孩子养不养得活，所以放在疾病域的生死里。王洪绪的注加了一个条件：是父母发动克子孙的时候。
>
> *Note:* The 'ruling star' is the Month branch (the branch that rules the month), so 'meeting the ruling star' is the Output line standing on the Month branch, in season. 'Wind shaking a trunk' and 'rain beating down flowers' are comparisons: in the first the trunk does not move, in the second the flowers are beaten down. The passage stands among the couplets on divining an infant in the chapter on the person and fate and is about whether the child can be raised, which is why it is placed under life and death in the illness domain. Wang Hongxu's note adds a condition: it is when Resource moves to control Output.
