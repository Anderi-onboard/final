# 六神歌断·白虎：交重丧恶事、官司病患必成凶；持金坐世妨人口，遇火临空便不同

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六神歌断里的白虎一段：白虎爻交重，主丧事、恶事，官司和病患一定凶；白虎属金、坐在世爻上，妨害家里的人口；遇到火，或者落空，情形就不同了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The White Tiger stanza in the verse judging by the six spirits: with the White Tiger line moving, there is mourning and ill fortune, and lawsuits and illness are surely ominous; if it is Metal and sits on the Self line it harms the household members; if it meets Fire or falls Void, things are different.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一段讲白虎爻发动。「持金」指白虎本身属金（白虎五行属金，见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.38）落在金爻上。末一句给了一个例外：遇火（火克金）或者白虎落了空亡，前面的凶就变了。六神歌断一共六段，每段一位神，这里只收白虎。
>
> *Note:* This stanza is about the White Tiger line moving. 'Holding Metal' means the White Tiger, which is itself Metal (see LY.ILL.DYTJ.38), falling on a Metal line. The last line gives an exception: if it meets Fire (Fire controls Metal) or the White Tiger is Void, the ill omen above changes. The verse has six stanzas, one for each spirit; only the White Tiger is taken here.
