# 主空无救须防中道而殂：主象落空占病大忌，要有动爻日辰冲克才有救（冲空则实、克空为用）；王洪绪说不独指空，墓绝空破有救者无妨、无救者必死，救指生扶拱合

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 主象落空（王洪绪：或入墓、绝、被破），占病是大忌；要有救才行，没救就会死在半路。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A main image that is Void (Wang Hongxu: or in a tomb, in extinction, or broken) is a great taboo in divining illness; it must have rescue, and without rescue the patient will die on the way.

## 《黄金策·病体》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6896 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 主空无救，须防中道而殂。 | 主象落空又没有救，要提防半路死掉。 | A main image that is Void with no rescue: beware of death half way. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6897 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 主象若空，占病大忌。 | 主象如果落空，占病是大忌。 | If the main image is Void, it is a great taboo in divining illness. |
> | 必得动爻日辰冲克，谓之有救。 | 一定要有动爻、日辰来冲它、克它，才叫有救。 | It must be clashed or controlled by a moving line or the Day for it to be called rescued. |
> | 盖冲空则实，克空为用，故也。 | 因为冲了落空的爻它就实了，克了落空的爻它就有了用。 | For clashing a Void line makes it real, and controlling a Void line puts it to use. |
> | 否则必死。 | 不然一定死。 | Otherwise he will surely die. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2372 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 非独指空而言也。 | 这一句不光是指落空说的。 | This does not speak of Void alone. |
> | 凡主象墓绝空破，有救者无妨，无救者必死。 | 凡是主象入墓、入绝、落空、被破，有救的没关系，没有救的一定死。 | Whenever the main image is in a tomb, in extinction, Void or broken, if there is rescue it does not matter, and if there is none he will surely die. |
> | 救者，生扶拱合也。 | 所说的救，就是生、扶、拱、合。 | By rescue is meant generating, supporting, embracing and combining. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 怎样才算「有救」：旧注说主象落空，要动爻、日辰来冲它、克它才有救（冲空则实，克空为用），不然必死；王洪绪说不光是落空，入墓、绝、被破也算，救是生、扶、拱、合。一个用冲克把它叫起来，一个用生扶合去帮它。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What counts as 'rescue': the old commentary says a Void main image is rescued only when a moving line or the Day clashes or controls it (clashing a Void line makes it real, controlling a Void line puts it to use), otherwise he surely dies; Wang Hongxu says the line speaks not of Void alone but of tomb, extinction and being broken as well, and rescue is generating, supporting, embracing and combining. One wakes the line by clash and control, the other helps it by generation, support and combination.
