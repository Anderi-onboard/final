# 六爻推鬼神：初爻水鬼犯井神、二爻火鬼干司命（灶神）、三爻犯家堂、五爻求五路神、四爻遇世冲是出门撞见、六爻月合是远地相干

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 按鬼落在哪一爻推：水鬼在初爻，冒犯了井神；火鬼在二爻，触犯了司命（灶神）；鬼在三爻（门），冒犯了家堂的香火；鬼在五爻（路上），要求五路神，金鬼是五道、火鬼是五圣、木鬼是五郎神众、水鬼是五路神司，衰弱就是路边的鬼；鬼在四爻，遇到冲世，一定是出门撞见的；鬼在六爻，逢月建生合，是远方的神来干犯，月建、日辰生合，是天神那里有愿。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Work it out by the line on which the ghost falls. A Water ghost on the first line has offended the well god; a Fire ghost on the second has offended the Director of Fate (the Kitchen God); a ghost on the third (the gate) has offended the incense and fire of the household shrine; on the fifth (the road), seek the Gods of the Five Roads: a Metal ghost is the Five Paths, Fire the Five Sages, Wood the Five Lords and their company, Water the Officers of the Five Roads, and if weak a ghost of the roadside. A ghost on the fourth line meeting a clash with Self was surely met on going out; a ghost on the sixth line meeting a combination with the Month is a spirit from far away, and if the Month and the Day generate or combine with it there is a vow to a heavenly spirit.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3387 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 水鬼临于初爻断井神，火鬼临于二爻断司命，如鬼临三爻断家堂，如临五爻断路头五圣，临四爻断出门撞祟，临六爻断远处染邪。 | 水鬼在初爻，断是井神；火鬼在二爻，断是司命；鬼在三爻，断是家堂；在五爻，断是路头五圣；在四爻，断是出门撞上了邪祟；在六爻，断是在远处沾染了邪气。 | A Water ghost on the first line is judged the well god; a Fire ghost on the second, the Director of Fate; a ghost on the third, the household shrine; on the fifth, the Five Sages of the road-head; on the fourth, running into an evil influence on going out; on the sixth, catching an evil taint in a far place. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文「四遇世冲」，王洪绪引作「四遇世神」；「神祗」他也写作「神祇」的同一个意思。王洪绪比旧注少了几样：旧注按五行分五爻上的五道、五圣、五郎、五路，还有「衰弱则道路边鬼」和「月建日辰生合，乃天神中有愿」；王洪绪把五爻一律断作路头五圣。「司命」是管人命运的灶神一类的神，这里旧注和经文都把它对到二爻的灶神。
>
> *Note:* Where the verse says 'the fourth line meeting a clash with Self' (shi chong), Wang Hongxu quotes 'the fourth line meeting the Self spirit' (shi shen). Wang Hongxu has less than the old commentary: the old commentary divides the fifth line by element into the Five Paths, the Five Sages, the Five Lords and the Five Roads, adds 'if weak, a ghost of the roadside' and 'if the Month and Day generate or combine, there is a vow to a heavenly spirit'; Wang Hongxu judges every ghost on the fifth line as the Five Sages of the road-head. The 'Director of Fate' is a spirit in charge of people's destinies of the Kitchen God kind, and both the verse and the old commentary match it to the Kitchen God of the second line.
