# 避暑养病途中的忌象：动见螣蛇愁惊恐、驿马交逢虑奔驰、玄武值鬼兴失财欠利、朱雀临兄动绕舌不宁；八宫鬼动各有所忌（艮忌山岭、坎忌江湖、震忌船内东行、乾忌庙宇、兑财变官毋贪色、巽忌风、离忌火惊、坤忌墓侧）；咸池华盖；世在内卦宜居迩、外卦利遐方

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动的爻见到螣蛇，还怕惊恐；交遇驿马，更担心奔波劳顿；玄武值鬼爻兴旺，失财欠利；朱雀临兄爻发动，饶舌不宁。艮卦有官，不要登山岭；坎宫见鬼，不要去江湖；在震，正东惹祸；临乾，西北招殃；兑里财变官爻，尤忌色迷；巽里兄弟的鬼象，风患难防。注说：螣蛇动，如果遇上惊惶；驿马临兄爻、鬼爻动，不利于出游；驿马动，临财、福，千里都安；鬼临玄武发动，必遭失脱；朱雀值兄爻发动，是非要谨慎。艮宫鬼动，忌去东北，不要去山林；坎卦鬼动，北方不利，水池不合宜；震宫鬼动，不要待在船里，并且忌向东行；乾宫西北为凶，不要游庙宇；兑宫鬼动，必有吵闹是非，若是财变官爻，不要贪美色；巽卦鬼兴以及兄爻化鬼的，都怕冒风；离宫鬼动，虑见火惊；坤卦鬼兴，不要住在墓旁。主合咸池，不要贪美色；用爻冲华盖、或华盖克用爻、或鬼临华盖动，都怕僧道的门下惹祸，如果去避暑却灾就成凶，只有悟道修行，反而成正果。又看世爻在内卦、兴亲宫，宜居在近处；世临他宫、兴外卦，利在远方；内卦旺，本地如意；外卦旺，他乡遂意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A moving line seeing the Coiling Snake still fears fright; meeting the Post Horse, it further worries about rushing about; the Dark Tortoise on a thriving Pressure line loses wealth and lacks profit; the Vermilion Bird on a moving Peer line brings gossip and unrest. Gen with a Pressure line: do not climb the mountain ridges; Kan with a ghost: do not go to river and lake; in Zhen, misfortune in the due east; on Qian, calamity from the northwest; in Dui, Wealth changing into a Pressure line, beware above all of being lost in lust; in Xun, the ghost image of Peer, wind sickness is hard to guard against. The commentary says: the Coiling Snake moving, one may meet a fright; the Post Horse on a Peer or a moving Pressure line is unfavourable for travel; the Post Horse moving on Wealth or the fortune spirit, all is safe for a thousand li; the ghost on the Dark Tortoise moving, loss is sure; the Vermilion Bird on a moving Peer line, take care of quarrels. A ghost moving in the Gen palace: avoid going northeast, do not go to the mountains and forest; a ghost stirring in Kan: the north is unfavourable and pools are unsuitable; a ghost moving in Zhen: do not stay in a boat, and avoid going east; the northwest of Qian is ominous, do not visit temples; a ghost moving in Dui, there will surely be an uproar and quarrel, and if Wealth changes into a Pressure line, do not covet beauty; a ghost thriving in Xun, or a Peer line turning into a ghost, both fear exposure to wind; a ghost moving in Li, fear a fright from fire; a ghost thriving in Kun, do not live beside graves. If the Subject combines with Xianchi, do not covet beauty; if the Subject clashes Huagai, or Huagai controls the Subject line, or a ghost on Huagai moves, trouble is feared at the doors of monks and Daoists, and if one goes to escape the heat and ward off calamity it turns ominous, whereas realising the Way and cultivating oneself on the contrary bears true fruit. Also look at the Self: in the inner trigram, thriving in a kin palace, it suits living near; the Self in another palace, thriving in the outer trigram, favours a far place; a strong inner trigram means the home region is as one wishes, and a strong outer trigram means a foreign land is as one wishes.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条把避暑、养病路上的忌象分成三层：六神和神煞（螣蛇惊恐、驿马奔波、玄武失财、朱雀口舌）、八宫鬼动的方位和处所（与 LY.ILL.EL.04 的八宫是同一套）、咸池华盖（色、僧道）。最后一段讲去近处还是远方：世在内卦、亲宫，住近处；世在外卦、他宫，去远方。原文「兑中财变官爻，色迷尤忌」是说兑宫里财爻变官爻，要小心迷恋美色。「千里皆安」是说驿马动而临财、福，出远门也平安。
>
> *Note:* This entry sorts the taboos on the road to escape the heat or convalesce into three layers: the six spirits and spirit-stars (the Coiling Snake fright, the Post Horse rushing about, the Dark Tortoise loss of wealth, the Vermilion Bird gossip), the direction and place indicated by a ghost moving in each of the eight palaces (the same set as in LY.ILL.EL.04), and Xianchi and Huagai (lust, monks and Daoists). The last paragraph speaks of near or far: the Self in the inner trigram or a kin palace means living near; the Self in the outer trigram or another palace means going to a far place. 'In Dui, Wealth changing into a Pressure line, beware above all of being lost in lust' means in the Dui palace a Wealth line turning into a Pressure line calls for care against infatuation. 'All is safe for a thousand li' means that with the Post Horse moving on Wealth or the fortune spirit even a long journey is safe.
