# 初筮防疾：用坏病起、鬼发病作；时灾专用于鬼，发动则疫扰其室；何命犯鬼是天祸、何神化鬼是人作、鬼化何人是流灾；随墓助伤大凶可畏

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一开始占筮来预防疾病的：用神坏了，病就起来；鬼发动，病就发作。遇到时行的灾病，专门看鬼，鬼发动，就是瘟疫来扰他的家，只能修身反省、等着它过去。哪个人的本命冲犯了鬼，是天降的祸；哪个六亲变出了鬼，是人自己招的；鬼变成哪个人，是流行的灾。随墓、助伤，大凶，可怕。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a first divination to guard against illness: when the Subject line is spoiled the illness arises, and when the ghost moves the illness breaks out. When a seasonal calamity is going about, look at the ghost alone; if it moves, an epidemic is troubling his household, and there is nothing to do but cultivate oneself, reflect and wait. Whichever natal branch offends the ghost, it is a heavenly misfortune; whichever relation turns into the ghost, it is man-made; into which person the ghost turns, that is a spreading calamity. Following into the tomb and helping to harm are greatly ominous, and to be feared.

> [!note] 按
> 「初筮」在这里是没病之前先占一卦，防着病来，不是说头一次占。「何命犯鬼」是哪个人的本命地支犯了鬼（与 LY.ILL.YM.09「命随鬼墓」里的「命」是同一个意思）。「天祸」「人作」「流灾」是三种来路：天降、自己招、传染。时行的灾病，书里叫「时灾」，与痘疹章说的「传染」「流行」（LY.ILL.YM.04）是同一路。
>
> *Note:* 'First divination' here means divining before any illness, to guard against its coming, not the first divination made. 'Whichever natal branch offends the ghost' means whose natal earthly branch runs into the ghost (the same 'natal branch' as in 'the natal branch follows the ghost into a tomb', LY.ILL.YM.09). 'Heavenly misfortune', 'man-made' and 'spreading calamity' are three roads by which it comes: sent from heaven, brought on by oneself, caught by contagion. What the book calls a 'seasonal calamity' is of the same kind as the 'passing calamity' and 'spreading' of the chapter on pox (LY.ILL.YM.04).
