# 总论星煞值鬼诀(二)：勾陈官鬼或螣蛇官鬼临丧门或吊客（正月辰、二月丑、三月戌、四月未循环）皆主伏尸土；玄武鬼爻带咸池沐浴（正月卯、二月子、三月酉、四月午循环）是杜氏夫人；鬼带勾陈又临折煞（正月酉、二月午、三月卯、四月子循环）并卦内驿马（正月申、二月巳、三月寅、四月亥循环）发动必犯途中跌死伤亡；玄武官爻带天河煞（正月起辰顺行十二位）必是井中或江河溺死之鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 勾陈上的官鬼或螣蛇上的官鬼，又临着丧门或遇上吊客，都主伏尸土（吊客的排法：正月辰、二月丑、三月戌、四月未，只在这四位上轮；丧门的排法见上一条）。玄武上的鬼爻如果带着咸池、沐浴，就是杜氏夫人（沐浴：正月卯、二月子、三月酉、四月午，只在这四位上轮；咸池煞的排法相同，也是正月卯、二月子、三月酉、四月午）。鬼带勾陈，又临折煞，并且卦里的驿马发动，必定犯了途中跌死、伤亡（折煞：正月酉、二月午、三月卯、四月子，周而复始；驿马：正月申、二月巳、三月寅、四月亥，也是这四位轮）。玄武上的官爻又带天河煞，必是井中溺死的鬼，不然就必是江河里淹死的人（天河煞：正月从辰上起，顺行十二位）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Hook Earth Pressure line or a Coiling Snake Pressure line that also sits on Mourning Gate or meets Guest of Condolence in every case governs the earth of a buried corpse (Guest of Condolence is set thus: the first month Chen, the second Chou, the third Xu, the fourth Wei, turning only through these four positions; Mourning Gate is set as in the previous entry). If a Dark Tortoise Pressure line carries Xianchi and Bath, it is Lady Du (Bath: the first month Mao, the second Zi, the third You, the fourth Wu, turning only through these four positions; the Xianchi killing is set in the same way, also the first month Mao, the second Zi, the third You, the fourth Wu). If the ghost carries Hook Earth and also sits on Breaking Killing, and the Post Horse in the hexagram moves, it surely offends a death by a fall or an injury on the road (Breaking Killing: the first month You, the second Wu, the third Mao, the fourth Zi, cycling round and round; Post Horse: the first month Shen, the second Si, the third Yin, the fourth Hai, also turning through these four positions). If a Dark Tortoise Pressure line also carries the Heavenly River killing, it is surely the ghost of one drowned in a well, or else of one drowned in a river (Heavenly River killing: the first month starts from Chen and runs forward through twelve places).

> [!note] 按
> 这一条讲勾陈、螣蛇、玄武三兽各自配的星煞。伏尸土：「陈蛇位」是勾陈、螣蛇这两个土兽的位置；「伏尸土禁」按字面译作「buried corpse and the earth taboos」——「土禁」可能指动土的禁忌，书里没有解释。「街妨」译作「obstruction in the street」。沐浴、咸池在书里是两个星，排法却一样（正月卯、二月子、三月酉、四月午），都是每次往后退三位的四位循环。「瀹死」的「瀹」本义是煮，这里按上下句（「溺死」）读作溺死。折煞、驿马也各有四位循环（折煞每次退三位，驿马每次退三位）。天河煞和官符、刀砧一样，是从正月起顺行十二个月，一个月一位。和易冒「天河为觉溺之屈」（LY.ILL.YM.62）、易隐「风波浴盆浮沉溺死」（LY.ILL.YY.29）是同一件事。 书里没有说这里的「正月、二月」是起卦的月还是别的月，这里照抄；「只此四位周而复始」是说五月回到正月的位置、九月也回到正月的位置，依此类推。 这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This entry is about the stars and killings matched with each of the three beasts Hook Earth, the Coiling Snake and the Dark Tortoise. The earth of a buried corpse: the 'seats of Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake' are the positions of these two Earth beasts; 'the buried corpse and the earth taboos' is translated literally, and the 'earth taboos' may mean the taboos on digging the earth, which the book does not explain. 'Jie fang' is translated as 'obstruction in the street'. Bath and Xianchi are two different stars in this book but are set the same way (the first month Mao, the second Zi, the third You, the fourth Wu), a cycle of four positions each moving back three places. The 'yue' in 'yue si' properly means to boil, and is read here as drowned from the neighbouring line ('drowned'). Breaking Killing and the Post Horse also each have a cycle of four positions (Breaking Killing moving back three places at each step, and the Post Horse likewise). The Heavenly River killing, like the Official Talisman and the Chopping Block, runs forward from the first month through the twelve months, one place a month. It is the same matter as the Yi Mao's 'Heavenly River is the wrong of those who wake to drown' (LY.ILL.YM.62) and the Yi Yin's 'wind and waves, the Bath Basin, floating and sinking, drowned' (LY.ILL.YY.29). The book does not say whether the 'first month' and so on are the months of the divination or of something else, and here it is copied as written; 'only these four positions, cycling round and round' means that the fifth month returns to the position of the first and the ninth to that of the first, and so on. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
