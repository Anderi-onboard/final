# 易卦发蒙歌·六神自注：玄武动克身世应，主女多疾病、盗贼；勾陈动克身世应，主有疾病、官司、争田土屋宅山林；刑世应主坟墓不安

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 易卦发蒙歌说六神的自注里有两条讲到病：玄武这个神，发动、克到身、世、应，主女的多有疾病、还有盗贼的事；勾陈这个神，发动、克到身、世、应，主有疾病、官司、争夺田土房屋山林的事，如果刑到世、应，主坟墓不安宁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Two of the notes on the six spirits in the Yigua Fameng Ge speak of illness: the Dark Tortoise spirit, when it moves and controls the Body, Self or Other, means much illness among women and matters of thieves; the Hook Earth spirit, when it moves and controls the Body, Self or Other, means illness, lawsuits and quarrels over fields, houses, hills and forests, and if it punishes the Self or Other, the graves are unquiet.

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 这两条自注都以「其神动克身世应」为条件：六神动了、又克到卦身、世或应，才断主某事。同一部书在月建六神吉凶一段的末尾（第 415 行）明说「右爻动或克世应断之，不动不克不断」。「主女多疾病」可读作「主女的多生病」，也可读作「主在女人那里多有疾病」，书里没有展开，译文取前一种说法。这一整首歌诀里别的六神（青龙、白虎、朱雀、螣蛇）的自注讲的是喜庆、丧服、口舌、怪梦，与病无关，没有收；白虎的病见 LY.ILL.BSYG.05。
>
> *Note:* Both notes take 'when this spirit moves and controls the Body, Self or Other' as the condition: the spirit must move and also control the Body, Self or Other before it is judged to mean such and such. The same book at the end of the passage on the omens of the spirits of the Month (line 415) says outright 'judge by a line to the right when it moves or controls the Self or Other; if it does not move or control, do not judge'. 'Much illness among women' can be read as 'women fall ill often' or as 'much illness on the women's side'; the book does not expand, and the translation takes the first. The notes on the other spirits in the same song (Azure Dragon, White Tiger, Vermilion Bird, Coiling Snake) speak of joy, mourning dress, quarrels and strange dreams and are not about illness, so are not taken; for the White Tiger's illness see LY.ILL.BSYG.05.
