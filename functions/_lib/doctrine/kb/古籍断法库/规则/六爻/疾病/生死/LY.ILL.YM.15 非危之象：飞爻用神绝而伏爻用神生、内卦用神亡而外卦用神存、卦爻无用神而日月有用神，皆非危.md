# 非危之象：飞爻用神绝而伏爻用神生、内卦用神亡而外卦用神存、卦爻无用神而日月有用神，皆非危

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 飞爻上的用神绝了，但伏爻上的用神生旺，不危险；内卦的用神没了，但外卦的用神还在，不危险；卦爻里没有用神，但日、月里有用神，不危险。注举例：寅月丙寅日，占兄弟，得乾变需，申飞在用爻上，日破月破，却不知道酉伏在戌下，火生土而土生金，所以说不危险；辰月辛卯日，占儿子本人，得未济，外卦的未虽旬空，内卦的辰当月建，后来病好了；午月戊寅日，占妻子本人，得既济，卦里虽然没有用爻，但月是午、日是寅，原神和用神都出现，当月病就好了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject on the flying line is in Extinction but the Subject on the hidden line is born, there is no danger; if the Subject in the inner trigram is lost but the Subject in the outer trigram remains, there is no danger; if there is no Subject among the lines of the hexagram but the Day or Month holds the Subject, there is no danger. The note's examples: in the Yin month on the Bingyin day, a brother was divined and Qian changing into Xu obtained: Shen stood on the Subject line as the flying line, in Day-break and Month-break, but it was not noticed that You lay hidden beneath Xu, and Fire generates Earth and Earth generates Metal, so it is said there is no danger; in the Chen month on the Xinmao day, a son himself was divined and Weiji obtained: Wei in the outer trigram was in Void, but Chen in the inner trigram stood on the Month, and later he recovered; in the Wu month on the Wuyin day, a wife herself was divined and Jiji obtained: though there was no Subject line in the hexagram, the month was Wu and the day Yin, and the Support and the Subject both appeared, and that month she recovered.

> [!note] 按
> 「飞爻」是卦里现出来的那一爻，「伏爻」是伏在它下面的爻。第一个例子里申金飞在用爻上而日破月破，看起来危险，但酉金伏在戌土下面，火生土、土生金，伏着的用神得生，所以不危。「外未」「内辰」是外卦的未、内卦的辰。
>
> *Note:* The 'flying line' is the line that appears in the hexagram and the 'hidden line' the one hidden beneath it. In the first example Shen Metal stands on the Subject line as the flying line and is in Day-break and Month-break, which looks dangerous, but You Metal is hidden beneath Xu Earth, Fire generates Earth and Earth generates Metal, so the hidden Subject is born and there is no danger. 'The Wei of the outer' and 'the Chen of the inner' are the Wei in the outer trigram and the Chen in the inner.
