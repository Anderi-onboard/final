# 家宅秘诀·鬼墓临身常有阴人伏枕；阴鬼主阴司之病症、阳鬼为户役之官非

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 家宅秘诀说：鬼爻的墓临在身上，常有女人卧病在床；阴爻的鬼主阴司作祟的病症，阳爻的鬼是户役方面的官非。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The secret rules on the house say: with the ghost's tomb falling on the Body, there is often a woman lying ill in bed; a yin ghost means illnesses sent by the netherworld office, a yang ghost means official trouble over household duties.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「阴鬼」是阴爻上的官鬼，「阳鬼」是阳爻上的官鬼；「阴司」是阴间的官府，也就是鬼神，所以阴鬼主的病症是鬼神作祟一类。「户役」是户籍和差役。「鬼墓临身」的「身」是卦身还是自身，书里没有说，译文写作「身」。「阴人」通常指女人。
>
> *Note:* A 'yin ghost' is a Pressure line on a yin line and a 'yang ghost' one on a yang line; the 'netherworld office' is the office of the underworld, that is, spirits, so what a yin ghost brings is illness of the kind sent by spirits. 'Household duties' are household registration and corvée. Whether 'the Body' in 'the ghost's tomb falling on the Body' is the hexagram's Body or oneself is not said, and the translation writes 'the Body'. 'A yin person' ordinarily means a woman.
