# 官鬼克到哪一处，那一处也有病：克上六头痛，克间爻或临间爻胸膈不利；被克之处和鬼所临之处都要看

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病不只在鬼爻所坐的地方，被鬼克到的地方也有病痛：克到最上面一爻（上六），主头痛；克到间爻，或者鬼坐在间爻上，主胸膈不舒服；忌神也一样。其余照此类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The illness is not only where the Pressure line sits; the place it wounds also has pain. Controlling the top line brings headache; controlling a between line, or sitting on one, makes the chest and diaphragm uneasy; the Adversary does the same. The rest follows by analogy.

## 《黄金策·疾病》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6857 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官伤上六，斯人当主头疼。 | 官鬼伤到上六爻，这个人会头痛。 | A Pressure line wounding the top line: this person will have a headache. |

^q1

> | 上六。 | 指最上面的第六爻。 | The top, sixth line. |

> | 如克上六，当主头痛； | 比如克到上六，主头痛； | For instance, controlling the top line means headache; |
> | 克间爻，则胸隔不利。 | 克到间爻，胸膈就不舒服。 | controlling a between line, the chest and diaphragm are uneasy. |
> | 余可类推。 | 其余照这个类推。 | The rest can be reasoned by analogy. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2354 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 不唯官鬼克伤上六而头疼，即如官鬼所临之处，亦有病也。 | 不只是官鬼克伤上六爻才头疼，官鬼所坐的地方，也有病。 | It is not only that a Pressure line controlling and wounding the top line brings headache: the place where a Pressure line sits also has illness. |
> | 如官鬼克间爻，或临间爻，皆主胸膈不利，忌神亦然。 | 比如官鬼克到间爻，或者坐在间爻上，都主胸膈不舒服，忌神也是这样。 | For instance, if a Pressure line controls a between line or sits on one, the chest and diaphragm are uneasy, and the Adversary does the same. |
> | 余可类推。 | 其余照这个类推。 | The rest can be reasoned by analogy. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注开头的「上六」两个字是给经文「上六」注的名称（最上面一爻叫上六），下面从「大抵」起才是解释。王洪绪比旧注多两处：鬼「临」间爻也算，忌神也算。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary's opening 'shang liu' is a gloss on the verse's 'shang liu' (the top line is called the sixth at the top), and the explanation begins at 'in general'. Wang Hongxu goes beyond the old commentary in two places: a Pressure line 'sitting on' a between line also counts, and so does the Adversary.
