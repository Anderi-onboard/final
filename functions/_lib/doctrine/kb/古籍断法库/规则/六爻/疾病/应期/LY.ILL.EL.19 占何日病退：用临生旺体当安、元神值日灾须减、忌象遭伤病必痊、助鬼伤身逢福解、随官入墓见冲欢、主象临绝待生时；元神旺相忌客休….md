# 占何日病退：用临生旺体当安、元神值日灾须减、忌象遭伤病必痊、助鬼伤身逢福解、随官入墓见冲欢、主象临绝待生时；元神旺相忌客休囚取用爻值日、用爻衰弱取生旺之期、用爻太旺反喜入墓之时、不太旺取元神值日、忌神动逢冲克忌神之日

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是占病人哪一天痊愈，用爻临在生旺上，身体应当安好；元神值日，灾应减轻；忌象受伤，病必痊愈；助鬼伤身，遇到福解（福神来解）；随官入墓，见冲就欢喜；如果主象落在绝地，就等它逢生的时候，免去祸愆。注说：占病哪一天痊愈，要推主象。卦里元神旺相、忌客休囚而有用爻的，就取用爻值日的那天安好；如果用爻衰弱，才取生旺的时期。倘若用爻重重太旺，反而喜欢它入墓的时候；如果不太旺，又取元神值日；忌神如果动，必须遇上冲克忌神的日子，才能安康。若遇助鬼伤身，又利于子孙值日。随官入墓，还要求冲开墓的日辰。用象如果临在绝地而逢生，一定主平安。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine on what day a patient recovers: with the Subject line on generation or strength, the body should be well; on the day of the Support the calamity should lessen; when the adversary image is wounded the illness surely heals; helping the ghost to harm the Self is resolved by meeting the fortune spirit; following the Pressure line into a tomb is glad to meet a clash; if the chief image sits in Extinction, wait for the time it is born, and misfortune is avoided. The commentary says: to divine on what day the illness is cured, work out the chief image. If in the hexagram the Support is strong and the adversary guest resting or trapped and there is a Subject line, take the day on which the Subject line is on duty for ease; if the Subject line is weak, take a period of generation or strength. If the Subject line is far too strong, on the contrary it is glad of the time it enters a tomb; if it is not too strong, take the day of the Support; if the Adversary moves, one must meet the day that clashes and controls the Adversary before health returns. Meeting helping the ghost to harm the Self, the day of Output on duty is again favourable. Following the Pressure line into a tomb, seek further a day that clashes the tomb open. If the Subject image sits in Extinction and meets generation, it surely means peace.

> [!note] 按
> 「福解」（福神来解）指子孙发动或值日来解开助鬼伤身的凶；「体当安」的「体」是身体。「用爻重重太旺，反喜入墓之时」是说太旺的用爻要靠入墓来泄它的过旺，与黄金策「用壮有扶切恐太刚则折」（LY.ILL.HJC.36）是同一路道理：占病不喜太旺。这一条和黄金策的应期（LY.ILL.HJC.57）、增删卜易的应期（LY.ILL.ZS.16）、易隐愈日（LY.ILL.YY.04）、易冒问安日（LY.ILL.YM.19）是同一件事，几家取日的办法大体一致：取用神（元神）生旺值日、冲克忌神之日、冲开墓库之日。
>
> *Note:* 'Resolved by meeting the fortune spirit' means Output moving or on duty resolves the ill of helping the ghost to harm the Self; the 'body' in 'the body should be well' is the physical body. 'The Subject line far too strong is on the contrary glad of the time it enters a tomb' means that an over-strong Subject line needs to enter a tomb to be drained of its excess, the same reasoning as the Huangjin Ce's 'the Subject strong and supported, fearing it is too rigid and breaks' (LY.ILL.HJC.36): in divining illness one does not welcome too much strength. This entry is the same matter as the Huangjin Ce's timing (LY.ILL.HJC.57), the Zengshan Buyi's timing (LY.ILL.ZS.16), the Yi Yin's days of recovery (LY.ILL.YY.04) and the Yi Mao's days of ease (LY.ILL.YM.19), and the books agree in outline on how to take the day: the day the Subject (or Support) is strong and on duty, the day that clashes and controls the Adversary, the day that clashes the tomb open.
