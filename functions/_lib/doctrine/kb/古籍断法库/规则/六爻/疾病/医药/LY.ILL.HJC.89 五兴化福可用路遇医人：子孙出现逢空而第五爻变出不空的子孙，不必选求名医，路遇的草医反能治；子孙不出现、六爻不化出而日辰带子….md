# 五兴化福可用路遇医人：子孙出现逢空而第五爻变出不空的子孙，不必选求名医，路遇的草医反能治；子孙不出现、六爻不化出而日辰带子孙生合，意外自有医来治

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙出现在卦里却落空，而第五爻动、变出不落空的子孙，不用选求名医服药，路上遇到的乡野医生反而能治好。如果子孙不出现、六爻也没有变出子孙，而日辰带着子孙来生合，想不到的时候自然有医生来治。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output appears in the hexagram but is Void, and the fifth line moves and changes out an Output that is not Void, there is no need to seek a famous doctor and take medicine: a rustic doctor met on the road can cure him instead. If Output does not appear and none of the six lines changes it out, but the Day carries Output and generates or combines with it, a doctor will come to treat him unexpectedly.

> | 若子孙不现，而日辰临子孙生合者，意外自有医生可治也。 | 如果子孙不出现，而日辰临着子孙来生合，想不到的时候自然有医生可以治。 | If Output does not appear but the Day sits on Output and generates or combines with it, a doctor who can treat him will come unexpectedly. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注的条件比王洪绪细：子孙出现而空，第五爻变出的子孙不空。王洪绪只说第五爻变出子孙。「草医」是乡间没有名气的医生。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary's condition is finer than Wang Hongxu's: Output appears and is Void, and the Output changed out at the fifth line is not Void. Wang Hongxu says only that the fifth line changes out Output. A 'rustic doctor' is a country doctor of no repute.
