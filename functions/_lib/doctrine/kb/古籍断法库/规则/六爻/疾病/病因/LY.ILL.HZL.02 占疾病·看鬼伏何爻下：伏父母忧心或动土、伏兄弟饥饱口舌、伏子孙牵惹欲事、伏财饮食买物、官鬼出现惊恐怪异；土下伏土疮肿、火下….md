# 占疾病·看鬼伏何爻下：伏父母忧心或动土、伏兄弟饥饱口舌、伏子孙牵惹欲事、伏财饮食买物、官鬼出现惊恐怪异；土下伏土疮肿、火下火手足、金见金闷乱、木下木寒热、水下水冷疾、金下火喘满；阳宫财动主吐阴宫财动主泻；鬼在内下受病外上受病

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看鬼伏在哪一爻下，再按金木水火土分辨：伏在父母下，是忧心得的病，或动了土、去修造的地方得的；伏兄弟下且动，是饥饱失节得的，或因口舌、生气得的；伏子孙下且动，是牵扯招惹得的，或欲事太过得的；伏财下，是饮食上得的，或买东西得的；官鬼出现，是惊恐怪异，或在寺观庙宇里得的。土下伏土生疮肿，火下伏火病在手足，金见金闷乱，木下木寒热，水下水冷疾，金下伏火喘满；阳宫财动主吐，阴宫财动主泻。鬼爻现在外、金鬼爻伏在里，主心腹病；鬼在内，下部受病；鬼在外而动，上部受病；用爻同。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Look at which line the ghost is hidden under, and tell it apart by Metal, Wood, Water, Fire and Earth. Hidden under Resource: caught through worry, or by disturbing the earth, or by going to a place of building and repair. Hidden under Peer and moving: caught through the lapse of hunger and fullness, or through quarrels and anger. Hidden under Output and moving: caught through entanglement, or through excess of desire. Hidden under Wealth: caught through food and drink, or through buying things. The Pressure line appearing: fright and strange things, or caught in a temple or monastery. Earth hidden under Earth: sores and swelling; Fire under Fire: the hands and feet; Metal meeting Metal: oppression and confusion; Wood under Wood: chills and fever; Water under Water: a cold illness; Metal under Fire: panting and fullness. In a yang palace Wealth moving means vomiting, in a yin palace Wealth moving means diarrhoea. With the Pressure line showing outside and the Metal Pressure line hidden inside, it is an illness of the heart and belly; a ghost inside, the lower part is ill; a ghost outside and moving, the upper part is ill; the Subject line is the same.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段和海底眼「鬼伏何爻下」一节（LY.ILL.HDY.04）、天玄赋「无鬼看伏鬼」（LY.ILL.TXF.10）是同一套，字句和顺序略有不同；这里多了「土下伏土疮肿……金下火喘满」一串五行相叠的症候。原文「寺观宙宇」的「宙」是「庙」之误，按庙宇译。「鬼在内地，下受病」的「内地」按「在内」译。「阳宫财动主吐，阴宫财动主泻」是说阳宫里财爻动主吐、阴宫里财爻动主泻，「阳宫」「阴宫」原文没有解释。「土下伏土」按「土鬼伏在土爻下」读。「用爻同」是说用爻的内外动也照这样看。原文中间隔了一个空行：「鬼伏兄弟」在一行的末尾，「动，失饥伤饱得」在下一行的开头，这里按连着读。
>
> *Note:* This passage is the same set as the Haidi Yan's section on which line the ghost is hidden under (LY.ILL.HDY.04) and the Tianxuan Fu's 'with no ghost look for the hidden ghost' (LY.ILL.TXF.10), with slightly different wording and order; here there is in addition the string of symptoms from phases stacked on each other, 'Earth hidden under Earth: sores and swelling ... Metal under Fire: panting and fullness'. The 'zhou' of 'si guan zhou yu' in the original is a miswriting of 'miao' (temple), rendered as temple or monastery. The 'inside the earth' of 'the ghost inside the earth, the lower part is ill' is rendered 'inside'. 'In a yang palace Wealth moving means vomiting, in a yin palace Wealth moving means diarrhoea' says that Wealth moving in a yang palace means vomiting and in a yin palace diarrhoea, and the original does not explain 'yang palace' and 'yin palace'. 'Earth hidden under Earth' is read as an Earth ghost hidden under an Earth line. 'The Subject line is the same' says that the Subject line's inside and outside moving is also looked at this way. In the original a blank line falls in the middle: 'the ghost hidden under Peer' is at the end of one line and 'moving, caught through the lapse of hunger and fullness' at the beginning of the next, and they are read here as continuous.
