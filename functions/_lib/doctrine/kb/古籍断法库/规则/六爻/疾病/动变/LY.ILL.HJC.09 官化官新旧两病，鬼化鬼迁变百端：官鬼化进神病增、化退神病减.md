# 官化官新旧两病，鬼化鬼迁变百端：官鬼化进神病增、化退神病减

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻化出官鬼爻，是新旧两种病；鬼爻化出鬼爻，病情迁变，花样百出。旧注说：本宫官鬼还伏在别宫官鬼下面，是旧病，或者旧病没有谨慎、变出别的病，或者新旧两种病；鬼化鬼是病有进有退，或者变了症候，或者药石杂乱，或者忽冷忽热。王洪绪补充：官鬼化进神病加重，化退神病减轻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure changing into Pressure means two illnesses, new and old; Pressure changing into Pressure shifts and changes in a hundred ways. The old commentary says: if the home palace's Pressure is still hidden beneath another palace's Pressure, it is an old illness, or through carelessness with the old illness another disease has grown from it, or it is two kinds of illness, new and old; Pressure changing into Pressure means the illness advances and retreats, or the syndrome changes, or the drugs and needles are muddled, or chills and fever are uncertain. Wang Hongxu adds: Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit, the illness grows; into a Retreating spirit, it lessens.
