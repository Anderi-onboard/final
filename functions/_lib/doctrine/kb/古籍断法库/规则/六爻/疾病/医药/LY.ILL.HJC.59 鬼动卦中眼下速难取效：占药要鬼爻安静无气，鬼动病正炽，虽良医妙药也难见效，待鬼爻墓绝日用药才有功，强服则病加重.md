# 鬼动卦中眼下速难取效：占药要鬼爻安静无气，鬼动病正炽，虽良医妙药也难见效，待鬼爻墓绝日用药才有功，强服则病加重

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占药要官鬼爻安静、没有气才吉。鬼一发动，病正在最厉害的时候，就算有好医生和妙药，一时也很难见效；休囚的还稍可，旺的就更凶。要等鬼爻入墓、入绝的时候用药才有功效；勉强吃药，病一定加重。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining medicine the Pressure line should be still and without force. If it moves, the illness is at its fiercest, and even with a good doctor and wonderful medicine it is hard to gain effect for the moment; if resting or trapped it is a little more bearable, and if strong so much the worse. One must wait until the Pressure line meets its tomb or extinction before medicine has any effect; if he forces himself to take medicine, the illness will surely grow heavier.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说「待鬼爻墓绝」，王洪绪说「待鬼爻墓绝日」，落到具体的日子上。这一条和 LY.ILL.HJC.43（鬼变入长生一日重一日）相通：鬼在走旺的路上，药力压不住。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says 'wait until the Pressure line meets its tomb or extinction' and Wang Hongxu 'a day on which' it does, which brings it down to a particular day. The entry is related to LY.ILL.HJC.43 (Pressure changing into Birth grows heavier each day): while Pressure is on the way to strength, medicine cannot hold it down.
