# 乱动皆非吉、伏吟亦是凶：各种卦都怕乱动，占卜都怕伏吟，痘花更不例外

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里乱动都不吉利，伏吟也是凶：各种卦都怕乱动，何况是痘花；凡是占卜都怕伏吟，难道只有痘症才怕吗。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Lines moving in disorder are all inauspicious, and Hidden Chant is also ominous: every hexagram fears lines moving in disorder, let alone the pox flowers; all divining fears Hidden Chant, is it only so for pox?

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2249 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)

> | 诸卦皆怕乱动，何况于痘花？ | 各种卦都怕乱动，何况是痘花？ | Every hexagram fears lines moving in disorder, let alone the pox flowers. |

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的注是两句反问，没有给新的判据，只是说明：乱动和伏吟是各种占都忌的，痘疹并不例外。
>
> *Note:* The commentary here is two rhetorical questions and gives no new criterion; it says only that disorderly movement and Hidden Chant are dreaded in every kind of divination and the pox is no exception.
