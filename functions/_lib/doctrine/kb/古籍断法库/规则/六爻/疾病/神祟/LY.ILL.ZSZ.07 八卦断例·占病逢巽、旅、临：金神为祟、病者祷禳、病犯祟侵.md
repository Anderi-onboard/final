# 八卦断例·占病逢巽、旅、临：金神为祟、病者祷禳、病犯祟侵

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 周易尚占八卦断例里有三个卦的断辞把病和鬼神联在一起：巽卦「惟占病人，金神为崇」，占病人的时候是金神（白虎）在作祟；旅卦「病者祷禳」，病人要祷告、禳解；临卦「病犯崇侵」，病是撞上了鬼神作祟的侵扰。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the judgments by example of the Zhouyi Shangzhan three hexagrams link illness with spirits: Xun, 'only in divining a sick person: a Metal spirit (the White Tiger) is the curse'; Lü (the Traveller), 'the sick should pray and perform exorcism'; Lin, 'illness meets the encroachment of a curse'.

## 《周易尚占·八卦断例章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 1234 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Chapter of Judgments by Example for the Eight Palaces", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 临者大也 | 临，就是大。 | Lin (Approach) means great. |
> | 克己临人 | 克制自己，面对别人。 | Master oneself to face others. |
> | 宜占家宅 | 适合占家宅。 | Suits divining the household. |
> | 利问婚姻 | 利于问婚姻。 | Favourable to asking about marriage. |

> | 谋望同伦 | 谋划指望，与同辈同步。 | Plans and hopes are in step with one's peers. |
> | 孕生男子 | 怀孕，生男孩。 | Pregnancy: a boy is born. |
> | 病犯崇侵 | 病，遇到鬼神作祟的侵扰。 | Illness meets the encroachment of a curse. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 巽、临两处原文写的是「崇」（崇高的崇），按上下文是作祟的「祟」，译文按「祟」（鬼神降灾）译，书里没有标注。「金神」按这一部前面「十一、定六神」的「白虎金神」（第 461 行）是白虎，这里「金神为崇」是白虎作祟；别的书里金神常和七煞连称（LY.ILL.EL.34、EL.47），那是另一个意思，这里没有用。「祷禳」是祈祷、禳解（用仪式把灾禳除）。「病犯崇侵」读作病是触犯了、遇到了鬼神作祟的侵扰。三行里别的词组照录。
>
> *Note:* In Xun and Lin the original writes the character for 'lofty' where the context calls for the character for 'curse'; the translation reads it as 'curse' (harm sent by a spirit), and the book gives no mark. By 'Section 11: Fixing the Six Spirits' earlier in the same part ('the White Tiger is the Metal spirit', line 461) the 'Metal spirit' is the White Tiger, so 'a Metal spirit is the curse' here is the White Tiger afflicting; in other books the Golden Spirit is often named together with the Seven Killings (LY.ILL.EL.34, EL.47), which is another sense and is not used here. 'Pray and perform exorcism' is to pray and to dispel the calamity by rites. 'Illness meets the encroachment of a curse' reads the illness as having offended or run into the invasion of a curse sent by a spirit. The other phrases of the three lines are kept as they stand.
