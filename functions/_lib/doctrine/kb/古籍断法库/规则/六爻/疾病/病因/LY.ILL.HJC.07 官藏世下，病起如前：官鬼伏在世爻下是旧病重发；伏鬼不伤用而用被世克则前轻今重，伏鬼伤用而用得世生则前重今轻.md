# 官藏世下，病起如前：官鬼伏在世爻下是旧病重发；伏鬼不伤用而用被世克则前轻今重，伏鬼伤用而用得世生则前重今轻

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本宫的官鬼伏在世爻下面，一定是旧病重新发作。如果伏着的鬼不伤用爻、用爻反而被世爻克，以前病轻、现在反倒沉重；如果伏着的鬼刑伤用爻、而用爻反而得到世爻的生扶，以前病重、现在轻。用爻要看问的是谁：比如占妻子的病就看财爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the home palace's Pressure is hidden beneath the Self line, it is surely an old illness breaking out again. If the hidden Pressure does not harm the Subject line but the Subject is controlled by Self, the illness was light before and is heavier now; if the hidden Pressure punishes and harms the Subject but the Subject is generated and supported by Self, it was heavy before and is lighter now. The Subject line depends on whom one asks about: for a wife's illness, for example, look at the Wealth line.
