# 孙膑断疾病歌(五)：明夷蛊剥夬丰同六卦占病不可逢；财鬼二爻俱发动、丧门吊客闹匆匆；六重白虎临身恶；鬼爻并爻应发动、化蛇入库身终

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 明夷、蛊、剥、夬、丰、同这六个卦，占病时哪里愿意遇上；财、鬼两爻都发动，丧门、吊客闹哄哄地赶来；六重白虎临在身上凶恶，若在别的爻上也不好；鬼爻和应爻一起发动，化成螣蛇、入了墓库，身命就终了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Mingyi, Gu, Bo, Guai, Feng and Tong: who would wish to meet these six hexagrams in divining illness? With Wealth and the ghost both moving, Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence crowd in noisily and in haste; a six-fold White Tiger sitting on the Body is vicious, and if it is on another line it is also not good; the Pressure line and the Other line moving together, changing into the Coiling Snake and entering the tomb, the body's life ends.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「同」是同人卦的简称。这六个卦和阐奥歌章、天玄赋、卜筮元龟的凶卦名单大同小异，见 LY.ILL.CA.03 的「打架」。「六卦哪须占病逢」的「哪须」，这里按「哪里愿意」译（意思是占病时不想遇上）；字面也可读成「何须」，书里没有说明。「六重白虎」字面译作 six-fold，「六重」是什么意思书里没有说。「鬼爻并爻应发动」的「并爻应」读不通顺，这里按「并应爻」译，即鬼爻和应爻一起动。「（2）」是今人的注号，那条注不收。「化蛇入库」是化成螣蛇、又入了墓库（辰戌丑未），原文没有说是什么爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Tong' is short for the Tongren hexagram. These six hexagrams are much like the lists of ominous hexagrams in the Chan'ao Gezhang, the Tianxuan Fu and the Bushi Yuangui, see the dispute in LY.ILL.CA.03. In 'who would wish to meet them in divining illness' the 'na xu' is rendered 'who would wish' (meaning one does not want to meet them in divining illness); it can also be read literally as 'why need', and the book does not explain it. 'Six-fold White Tiger' is literal, and the book does not say what 'six-fold' means. 'Gui yao bing yao ying fa dong' does not read smoothly in 'bing yao ying'; it is rendered 'bing ying yao', the Pressure line and the Other line moving together. The '(2)' is a modern annotation mark and that annotation is not quoted. 'Changing into the Snake and entering the tomb' is changing into the Coiling Snake and also entering a tomb (Chen, Xu, Chou, Wei), and the original does not say which line.
