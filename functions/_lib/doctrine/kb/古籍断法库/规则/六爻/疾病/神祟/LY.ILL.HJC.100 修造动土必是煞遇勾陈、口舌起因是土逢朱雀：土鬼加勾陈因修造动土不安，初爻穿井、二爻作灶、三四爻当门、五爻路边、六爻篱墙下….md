# 修造动土必是煞遇勾陈、口舌起因是土逢朱雀：土鬼加勾陈因修造动土不安，初爻穿井、二爻作灶、三四爻当门、五爻路边、六爻篱墙下；临朱雀则动土时暗有口舌咒诅

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 因为修造、动土出了事，是土鬼碰上了勾陈：落在初爻是挖井动土，二爻是砌灶动土，三、四爻是门口动土，五爻是路边动土，六爻是篱笆墙下动土；在兑宫是挖池动土，艮宫是筑墙动土，坤宫是造坟动土，坎宫加玄武是挖厕坑动土。以上如果带朱雀，就是动土的时候暗中有口舌和咒诅，或者有人多嘴惹事，因此成了祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Trouble from building or breaking ground is an Earth ghost meeting the Hook Earth: on the first line, digging a well; the second, building a stove; the third and fourth, breaking ground at the door; the fifth, by the road; the sixth, beneath the fence or wall; in the Dui palace, digging a pond; in the Gen palace, building a wall; in the Kun palace, building a grave; in the Kan palace with the Dark Tortoise, digging a privy pit. If any of these carries the Vermilion Bird, then at the time of breaking ground there were hidden quarrels and curses, or someone talked too much and stirred up trouble, and this became the misfortune.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3385 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 此亦土鬼也，如勾陈，必因修造动土以致不安。 | 这也是土鬼；带勾陈的话，一定是因为修造、动土才弄得不安。 | This too is an Earth ghost; if with the Hook Earth, it is surely because of building or breaking ground that there is unrest. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注列了一长串位置，王洪绪只讲了总的一句。旧注写「在初爻」「二爻」「三爻四爻」没有说指的是土鬼落在哪一爻，按上下文译作土鬼所在的爻位。「杀」这里就是「煞」，一种凶的力量。「呪咀」是咒骂诅咒。旧注的位置表里三爻、四爻都是「当门动土」，而上面六爻推鬼神一条里三爻是门、四爻是户，两处不完全对应；书里没有说明。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary gives a long list of positions and Wang Hongxu only the general sentence. The old commentary's 'on the first line', 'the second line' and 'the third and fourth lines' do not say which line is meant, and are rendered by context as the line on which the Earth ghost stands. 'Sha' here is the malign influence. 'Zhouzu' is cursing and imprecation. In the old commentary's list both the third and fourth lines are 'breaking ground at the door', while in the entry on the six lines the third is the gate and the fourth the door of the house, so the two do not correspond exactly; the book does not explain.
