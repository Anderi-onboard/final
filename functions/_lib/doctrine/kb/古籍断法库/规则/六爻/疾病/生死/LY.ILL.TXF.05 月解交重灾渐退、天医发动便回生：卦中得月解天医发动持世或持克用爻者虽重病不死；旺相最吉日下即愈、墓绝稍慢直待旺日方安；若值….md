# 月解交重灾渐退、天医发动便回生：卦中得月解天医发动持世或持克用爻者虽重病不死；旺相最吉日下即愈、墓绝稍慢直待旺日方安；若值空亡其病难疗

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月解交重，灾祸渐渐退去；天医发动，当下就回生。注说：月解、天医见后面的吉神章。卦里如果得到它们发动、持世，或者持着、克着用爻，即使重病也不会死；旺相最吉，当天就好；墓绝的稍慢，要等到旺的日子才安；如果落空亡，病就难治。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Month Release crossing heavily, calamity gradually retreats; Heavenly Doctor moving, one returns to life at once. The commentary says: Month Release and Heavenly Doctor are given in the later chapter on the lucky spirits. If the hexagram has them moving and holding the Self, or holding or controlling the Subject line, then even in a heavy illness the patient does not die; if strong it is most auspicious and the cure comes that very day; if in a tomb or Extinction it is a little slower, and one must wait for a strong day to be at peace; if it meets Void, the illness is hard to treat.

> [!note] 按
> 月解、天医是吉神，天玄赋自己说「见后吉神章」——本库还没有收吉神章。这一条的判语是：月解或天医发动、持世，或持克用爻的，重病也不死；和易隐「生死·愈」（LY.ILL.YY.04）「用爻遇龙福月解天医天喜生合即愈」一致；和易林补遗（LY.ILL.EL.13）里「月解天医同发而用爻已遭伤仍死」的例子不冲突——那是说用爻被伤时，月解天医也救不回。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* Month Release and Heavenly Doctor are lucky spirits, and the Tianxuan Fu itself says 'see the later chapter on the lucky spirits'; this library has not yet quoted that chapter. The verdict of this entry is that with Month Release or Heavenly Doctor moving and holding the Self, or holding or controlling the Subject line, even a heavy illness does not kill, which agrees with the Yi Yin's 'life and death: recovery' (LY.ILL.YY.04), 'the Subject line meeting the Dragon, Fortune, Month Release, Heavenly Doctor or Heavenly Joy and being generated or combined, he recovers at once'; and it does not conflict with the example in the Yilin Buyi (LY.ILL.EL.13) in which 'Month Release and Heavenly Doctor move together but the Subject line is already wounded and he still dies' — that says that when the Subject line is wounded even Month Release and Heavenly Doctor cannot bring it back. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
