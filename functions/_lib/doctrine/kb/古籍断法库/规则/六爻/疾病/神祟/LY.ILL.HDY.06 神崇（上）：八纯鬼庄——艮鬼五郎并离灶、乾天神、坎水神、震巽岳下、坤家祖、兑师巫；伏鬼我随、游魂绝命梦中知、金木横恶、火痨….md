# 神崇（上）：八纯鬼庄——艮鬼五郎并离灶、乾天神、坎水神、震巽岳下、坤家祖、兑师巫；伏鬼我随、游魂绝命梦中知、金木横恶、火痨发血、土瘟；乾坎艮震是男儿；坎象临官求落水；庙动家亲；爻动克日分祖先、阴司、儿女、路死

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 怎么占出是哪路神作祟：八纯卦里鬼的所在可以去找。艮宫鬼是五郎，离宫是灶，乾宫出天神，坎宫出水神；震巽是岳（东岳）下，坤宫主家里的祖先，兑宫鬼是师巫；鬼动占亲人。伏着的鬼跟着我；游魂、绝命卦在梦中能知；金木是横死的恶鬼，火是痨病、发血，土是瘟病。乾坎艮震是男的；要知道是内外的什么人作祟，庙动是家亲。爻动克日：是祖先；财动克日：是阴司；子动克日：是儿女。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* How is it divined which spirit is haunting? The ghost's seat in the eight pure hexagrams can be sought out. The Gen ghost is the Five Lords, Li is the stove, Qian brings out the heavenly gods, Kan the water gods; Zhen and Xun are below the Peak (the Eastern Peak), Kun governs the household ancestors, the Dui ghost is the teacher and shaman; a ghost moving means divining kin. A hidden ghost follows me; the Wandering Soul and Cut-off Life hexagrams are known in dreams; Metal and Wood are violent and malignant ghosts, Fire is consumption with bleeding, Earth is epidemic. Qian, Kan, Gen and Zhen are male; to know who inside or outside is doing it, a moving temple means family kin. A line moving and controlling the Day: ancestors; Wealth moving and controlling the Day: the netherworld office; Output moving and controlling the Day: the children.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节讲怎么从卦上认作祟的是哪一路神鬼，和黄金策的八宫推鬼神（LY.ILL.HJC.95）、易冒（LY.ILL.YM.57）、易林补遗的「总论八宫值鬼诀」（LY.ILL.EL.44、EL.45）是同一类办法，但海底眼只点了几个宫：艮五郎、离灶（井灶）、乾天神、坎水神、震巽在岳下（东岳）、坤家祖、兑师巫。「五郎」「岳下」按字面译。「游魂绝光」的「绝光」和下面歌诀的「绝命」不同，应是「绝命」的另一种写法，眉批这里照字面译并注明；「主村随」「兑有师巫神佛仵」的「仵」不成句，照抄标出；「内外男谋」按字面译。「爻动克日」指动爻克日辰，这里只写了「日」，按字面译作 the Day。「坎象临官求落水」的「求落水」按字面译作「one who fell in the water」，不确定。「庙动家亲」的「庙」书里没有解释。末一条括号里的小字「（儿女兄动，克日路死，官动克日，刑侵日一，分其老少）」是书里句下的小注，和歌诀排在一起，这里跟着一起收，「刑侵日一」不成句，标出。这里神鬼的名字（五郎、灶、岳下等）是民间叫法，书里没有解释，这里按字面译，不作认定。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> *Note:* This section is about how to tell from the hexagram which kind of god or ghost is haunting, the same kind of method as the Huangjin Ce's eight palaces (LY.ILL.HJC.95), the Yi Mao (LY.ILL.YM.57) and the Yilin Buyi's 'general discourse on ghosts by the eight palaces' (LY.ILL.EL.44, EL.45), except that the Haidi Yan touches only a few palaces: Gen the Five Lords, Li the stove (well and stove), Qian the heavenly gods, Kan the water gods, Zhen and Xun below the Peak (the Eastern Peak), Kun the household ancestors, and Dui the teacher and shaman. 'Five Lords' and 'below the Peak' are rendered literally. The 'Cut-off Light' of the marginal comment differs from the 'Cut-off Life' of the verses below and is probably another way of writing 'Cut-off Life', and is translated literally here with a remark; 'cun sui' and the 'wu' of 'Dui has teachers and shamans, gods and Buddhas, wu' are not sentences, and are copied and marked; 'inside and outside, male scheming' is rendered literally. 'A line moving and controlling the Day' means a moving line controlling the Day, and only the word 'Day' is written, so it is rendered literally as the Day. 'One who fell in the water' for 'qiu luo shui' is literal and uncertain. The 'temple' of 'the temple moving is family kin' the book does not explain. The small bracketed note in the last verse, '(a child or Peer moving and controlling the Day, death on the road; the official moving and controlling the Day, punishment invading, day one; divide old from young)', is a note under the line in the book and is set together with the verse, and is quoted with it here, and 'punishment invading, day one' is not a sentence and is marked. The names here (the Five Lords, the stove, below the Peak and so on) are folk names that the book does not explain, and are rendered literally without being identified. The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.
