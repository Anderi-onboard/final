# 病忌父兄·主爻伏鬼或伏兄弟或伏父母旺相大忌；乱动之卦只取主爻、休囚伏兄弟父母官鬼之下克世者死（兄无食、父母无药、官鬼真病）；八纯游魂卦病者沉重占小儿主死

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 主爻伏在鬼下，或伏在兄弟下，或伏在父母下，旺相是大忌。卦乱动时只取主爻：大抵休囚、伏在兄弟、父母、官鬼之下、又克世的，会死；因为兄弟没有食、父母没有药、官鬼是真病。凡得八纯卦、游魂卦，病人一定沉重，占小儿主死。问答：主爻伏鬼、伏兄伏父之下，就是财伏兄、财伏父母、官伏兄的道理，举一隅而三隅反；八纯、游魂、归魂三种卦，世持父母、官鬼、兄弟，或子孙伏在父母下，占大人是病重，占小儿是病死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject line hidden under the ghost, or under Peer, or under Resource: if strong it is a great taboo. When the hexagram moves confusedly take only the Subject line: mostly one that is resting or trapped, hidden under Peer, Resource or the Pressure line and also controlling the Self, will die; for Peer has no food, Resource has no medicine and the Pressure line is a real illness. Whoever gets a pure hexagram or a Wandering Soul hexagram, the sick person is surely heavy, and for a small child it means death. Questions and answers: the Subject line hidden under the ghost, under Peer or under Resource is the principle of Wealth hidden under Peer, Wealth hidden under Resource, the official hidden under Peer, and 'raise one corner and the other three come back'; in the three kinds of hexagram, pure, Wandering Soul and Returning Soul, with the Self holding Resource, the Pressure line or Peer, or Output hidden under Resource, divining an adult it is a heavy illness and divining a small child the illness ends in death.

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「主爻」是这一卦里代表病人的那一爻（用爻）。「兄无食，父母无药，官鬼真病」是这一节的解释：兄弟克财（食），父母克子孙（药），官鬼是病本身。「财伏兄」「财伏父母」「官伏兄」是「伏神」被飞神克的三种情形。「举一隅则三隅反」是《论语》的话，意思是举一个例子，别的照这个类推。「八纯」是八个纯卦（乾坤震巽坎离艮兑）；「游魂」「归魂」是卦名。第二问答里「世持父母、官鬼、兄弟，或子孙伏父母下」是说这三种卦在什么情形下会病重：世爻落在父母、官鬼、兄弟上，或子孙伏在父母下。「八纯游魂卦病重、占小儿主死」和天玄赋、易冒等处的八纯卦说法（LY.ILL.YM.12 一带）是同一类。
>
> *Note:* The 'Subject line' is the line that stands for the sick person in the hexagram (the Subject line). 'Peer has no food, Resource has no medicine and the Pressure line is a real illness' is the explanation of this section: Peer controls Wealth (food), Resource controls Output (medicine), and the Pressure line is the illness itself. 'Wealth hidden under Peer', 'Wealth hidden under Resource' and 'the official hidden under Peer' are three cases of the hidden spirit being controlled by the flying spirit. 'Raise one corner and the other three come back' is a saying of the Analects, meaning that from one example the others follow by analogy. 'Pure' means the eight pure hexagrams (Qian, Kun, Zhen, Xun, Kan, Li, Gen, Dui); 'Wandering Soul' and 'Returning Soul' are names of hexagrams. The second question and answer, 'the Self holding Resource, the Pressure line or Peer, or Output hidden under Resource', says under what conditions these three kinds of hexagram are heavy: the Self line falling on Resource, the Pressure line or Peer, or Output hidden under Resource. 'A pure or Wandering Soul hexagram is a heavy illness, for a small child it means death' is the same kind of saying as the sayings on the pure hexagrams in the Tianxuan Fu, the Yi Mao and elsewhere (around LY.ILL.YM.12).
