# 医药章(一)：子孙出现是医人、生世生身药必灵；两个子孙齐发动换医困厄即时平；子囚医拙、子旺医明；无子病人难服药、子空患者便归程；子孙值土宜丸散、火艾火蒸、木咬咀、金针刺、水汤药；青龙独发最为良、持世生身便离床；子孙同位发不须服药；鬼财安贴无忧虑、身世比和大吉祥；何日瘥：鬼衰身旺世生方

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙出现就是医生，生世、生身，药一定灵验。两个子孙一起发动，换医生，困厄立刻平息。子孙被囚，医生笨拙，没有功效；子孙旺，医生高明，有准绳。没有子孙，病人难服药；子孙空亡，患者就要归去了。子孙值土，宜丸散；值火，要用艾火熏蒸；遇木，用咬咀才对症；逢金，用针刺才保平安；子孙值水，宜汤药；有天喜扶持，妙不可言。青龙独发，最好；持世生身，便可离床；再有子孙同位发动，不必服药，保安康。鬼、财安贴，没有忧虑；身、世比和，大吉祥。想问病人哪一天痊愈？鬼衰、身旺、世生的时候。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output appearing is the doctor; generating the Self and the Body, the medicine is surely effective. Two Output lines moving together: change the doctor and the distress is settled at once. Output trapped: the doctor is clumsy and without effect; Output strong: the doctor is wise and has a standard. With no Output the patient can hardly take medicine; Output Void: the sufferer starts on his homeward road. Output on Earth: suit pills and powders; on Fire: it must be moxa fire steaming; meeting Wood: crushed herbs answer the illness; meeting Metal: the needle is guaranteed to bring health; Output on Water: suit decoctions; with Heavenly Joy helping, the wonder cannot be praised enough. The Azure Dragon moving alone is best; holding the Self and generating the Body, he leaves his bed at once; and if Output moves in the same place, no medicine need be taken and health is assured. With the ghost and Wealth quiet and settled there is no worry; the Body and Self harmonious is greatly auspicious. To ask on what day the sick person recovers: when the ghost is weak, the Body strong and the Self generated.

> [!note] 按
> 这是阐奥歌章「医药章」的第一首，和天玄赋的「医药」（LY.ILL.TXF.12、TXF.13）、易林补遗的「求医疗病」（LY.ILL.EL.26）、易隐的「医药」（LY.ILL.YY.21、YY.25）一样，都是「子孙为医药」的一套：子孙旺相、生世生身，药灵；两个子孙齐发，换医；无子孙、子孙空，难服药或不治。五行用药：土宜丸散、火宜艾灸、木宜咬咀、金宜针刺、水宜汤药——和天玄赋的「火鬼宜凉剂、水鬼宜温剂、木鬼宜针、金鬼宜灸、土鬼宜剉散」（LY.ILL.TXF.13）不同：那里按鬼的五行、这里按子孙的五行；这两个标准给出的用药并不一样（如木：天玄赋鬼属木宜针、这里子孙属木宜咬咀；金：天玄赋鬼属金宜灸、这里子孙属金宜针刺），书里没有说明二者的关系。「咬咀」是把药物切碎、入口嚼碎之类的做法（下面注释：「咀」音 ju，嚼）。「子空患者便归程」的「归程」是回去的路，指死。「鬼财安贴」的「贴」是安稳。周易尚占的疾厄部（第 794–799 行）里这一首有五处字不同：「值火须当艾火蒸」写作「值火须当火艾蒸」（「艾火」「火艾」的次序）；「遇木咬咀方应病」写作「值木吺咀方应病」（「遇」写作「值」，「咬咀」写作「吺咀」，两本各是各的字形，书里都没有解释）；「逢金针刺保亨贞」写作「值金针刺保亨贞」；「身世比和大吉祥」写作「身世比和大吉昌」；「鬼衰身旺世生方」写作「鬼衰生旺世生方」（「身旺」写作「生旺」，两个词意思不一样：一个说身爻旺，一个说鬼衰之后是生旺的状态）。
>
> *Note:* This is the first poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang's chapter on medicine, and like the Tianxuan Fu's 'medicine' (LY.ILL.TXF.12, TXF.13), the Yilin Buyi's 'seeking a doctor to treat illness' (LY.ILL.EL.26) and the Yi Yin's 'medicine' (LY.ILL.YY.21, YY.25) it is one set of 'Output is the medicine and doctor': Output strong and generating the Self and Body, the medicine is effective; two Output lines moving together, change the doctor; with no Output or Output Void, medicine can hardly be taken or there is no cure. The medicine by phase: Earth suits pills and powders, Fire moxa, Wood crushed herbs, Metal the needle, Water decoctions — which differs from the Tianxuan Fu's 'a Fire ghost suits cooling remedies, a Water ghost warming remedies, a Wood ghost the needle, a Metal ghost moxa, an Earth ghost cut-and-ground powders' (LY.ILL.TXF.13): there it goes by the phase of the ghost, here by the phase of Output, and the two criteria do not give the same medicine (for Wood: in the Tianxuan Fu a Wood ghost suits the needle, here Wood Output suits crushed herbs; for Metal: a Metal ghost suits moxa there, Metal Output the needle here), and the book does not say how the two relate. 'Yao ju' (crushed herbs) is cutting medicines fine and crushing them by chewing and the like (the note below has 'ju' as to chew). The 'gui cheng' of 'Output Void: the sufferer starts on his homeward road' is the road home, meaning death. The 'tie' of 'the ghost and Wealth quiet and settled' is settled and steady. In the Zhouyi Shangzhan's section on illness and affliction (lines 794–799) this poem differs in five places: 'on Fire it must be moxa fire steaming' is written with the two words of 'moxa fire' in the other order; 'meeting Wood, crushed herbs answer the illness' is written 'on Wood, [a differently written] crushed herbs answer the illness' (the word for 'meeting' and the first character of 'crushed herbs' are written differently, and the book explains neither); 'meeting Metal, the needle keeps the omen fortunate' is written 'on Metal, ...'; 'the Body and Self harmonious is greatly auspicious' ends with a different word for 'auspicious'; and 'the ghost weak, the Body strong, the Self generating' is written 'the ghost weak, birth and strength, the Self generating' ('Body strong' becomes 'birth and strength', and the two phrases do not mean the same: one says the Body line is strong, the other that after the ghost's weakening the state is one of birth and strength).
