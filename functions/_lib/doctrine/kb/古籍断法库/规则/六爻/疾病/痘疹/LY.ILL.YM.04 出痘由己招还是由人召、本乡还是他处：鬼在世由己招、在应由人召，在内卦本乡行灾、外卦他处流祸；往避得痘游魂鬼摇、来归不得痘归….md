# 出痘由己招还是由人召、本乡还是他处：鬼在世由己招、在应由人召，在内卦本乡行灾、外卦他处流祸；往避得痘游魂鬼摇、来归不得痘归魂鬼静；伏鬼有气亦见

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼落在世爻上，是自己招来的；落在应爻上，是别人招来的；在内卦，是本乡流行的灾，在外卦，是别处流传来的祸。出去躲避反而得了痘，是游魂卦、鬼发动；回来却没得痘，是归魂卦、鬼安静；伏着的鬼有气的，痘也会出现。官鬼一动，天花就开了：在世爻是单独发出来的，在应爻是被人传染缠上的，在六爻是童仆起的根，在三爻是弟兄接着种下的；内卦是本地，外卦是外乡；游魂是出去，归魂是回来。鬼在内卦空，本地没事；鬼在外卦空，到外乡可以躲过；鬼动而卦是归魂，回家有痘；鬼空而卦是游魂，出去就能免。大体上鬼爻旺相，即使伏藏也会萌发，鬼爻休囚，即使现出来也会平息。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On the Self the pox is called down by oneself; on the Other, called by others; in the inner trigram it is a passing calamity of the home district, in the outer trigram a spreading misfortune from elsewhere. Going away to avoid it and yet catching the pox is a Wandering Soul hexagram with the ghost moving; coming home and not catching it is a Returning Soul hexagram with the ghost still; a hidden ghost that has force also shows. When the Pressure line moves the heavenly flowers open: on the Self it is a lone outbreak, on the Other it is caught by contagion, on the sixth line the root is from a servant boy, on the third line brothers sow it in succession; the inner is the home district and the outer another place; Wandering Soul is going away and Returning Soul is coming back. If the ghost is Void in the inner trigram the home district is safe; if Void in the outer trigram one can avoid it by going elsewhere; if the ghost moves in a Returning Soul hexagram, there is pox on returning home; if the ghost is Void in a Wandering Soul hexagram, going out escapes it. In general, if the Pressure line is strong, even hidden it will sprout; if it is resting or trapped, even openly present it will subside.

> [!note] 按
> 「行灾」是流行的灾，「流祸」是流传的祸，都是说痘是传染来的。「童仆起根」「弟兄继种」是注文的说法，按字面译：六爻是痘的根从童仆那里起，三爻是从弟兄那里接着传。游魂、归魂是八宫里每宫的第七、第八卦。
>
> *Note:* 'Passing calamity' and 'spreading misfortune' both say the pox comes by contagion. 'The root is from a servant boy' and 'brothers sow it in succession' are the note's wording, rendered literally: on the sixth line the root of the pox starts from a servant boy, and on the third it is passed on by brothers. Wandering Soul and Returning Soul are the seventh and eighth hexagrams of each of the eight palaces.
