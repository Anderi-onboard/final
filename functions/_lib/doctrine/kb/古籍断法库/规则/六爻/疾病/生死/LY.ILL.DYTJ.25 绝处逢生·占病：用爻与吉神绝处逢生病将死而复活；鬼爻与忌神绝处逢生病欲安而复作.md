# 绝处逢生·占病：用爻与吉神绝处逢生病将死而复活；鬼爻与忌神绝处逢生病欲安而复作

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 绝处逢生：比如申日占卜，卦里的用爻属木，木遇到申就是绝，这木爻就没有用了，和没有一样；如果有水爻发动，水在申长生，动来相生，木爻又重新有用，好比人在困窘的时候遇到贵人扶持，必有寒谷回春的样子。占病：用爻和吉神在绝处逢生，病将死而又活过来；鬼爻和忌神在绝处逢生，病快要好了却又发作。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Meeting life in a place of extinction: for example, on a Shen day the Subject line in the hexagram belongs to Wood; Wood meeting Shen is in Extinction and this Wood line is of no use, the same as absent; if a Water line moves, Water having its Long Life at Shen moves to generate it, and the Wood line is again of use, like a person in hard straits who meets a patron to support him, and there is surely the look of spring returning to a cold valley. Divining illness: if the Subject line and a lucky spirit meet life in a place of extinction, the illness about to end in death revives; if the Pressure line and the Adversary meet life in a place of extinction, the illness about to settle breaks out again.

> [!note] 按
> 「绝处逢生」是断易天机「论」里的一章：一个爻在它的绝地，又逢生它的爻发动来相生，就像人在困境里遇到贵人。这一章占婚、占产、占官、占讼、求财、出行、行人、家宅各有一例（见 LY.MARRY.DYTJ 一带、LY.WEALTH.DYTJ 一带），这一条收占病那一句：用爻（病人）遇吉神绝处逢生，病将死而复活；鬼爻（病）遇忌神（生鬼的神）绝处逢生，病要好了又发作。「木见申则绝」是木在申（金）受克为绝；「水长生动来相生」是水在申长生。这两句和天玄赋「月解交重灾渐退，天医发动便回生」（LY.ILL.TXF.05）的「病将好又复发」是不同的：那里是吉神动，这里是绝地逢生。
>
> *Note:* 'Meeting life in a place of extinction' is a chapter in the 'discussions' of the Duanyi Tianji: a line in its place of extinction, with a line that generates it moving to generate it, like a person in hard straits meeting a patron. This chapter has one case each for marriage, childbirth, office, lawsuit, wealth, travel, a traveller and the house (see LY.MARRY.DYTJ and around, LY.WEALTH.DYTJ and around), and this entry takes the sentence on illness: the Subject line (the sick person) with a lucky spirit meeting life in a place of extinction, the illness about to end in death revives; the Pressure line (the illness) with the Adversary (the spirit that generates the ghost) meeting life in a place of extinction, the illness about to settle breaks out again. 'Wood meeting Shen is in Extinction' is Wood controlled at Shen (Metal) and so in Extinction; 'Water having its Long Life at Shen' is Water born long-lived at Shen. These two lines differ from the Tianxuan Fu's 'when the Month resolves and the Day moves heavily calamity fades, when the Heavenly Physician moves he recovers at once' (LY.ILL.TXF.05): there it is a lucky spirit moving, and here it is meeting life in extinction.
