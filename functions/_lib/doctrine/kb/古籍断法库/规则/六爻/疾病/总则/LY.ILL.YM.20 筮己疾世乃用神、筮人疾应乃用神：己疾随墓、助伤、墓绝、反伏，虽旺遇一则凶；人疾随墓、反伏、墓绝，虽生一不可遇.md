# 筮己疾世乃用神、筮人疾应乃用神：己疾随墓、助伤、墓绝、反伏，虽旺遇一则凶；人疾随墓、反伏、墓绝，虽生一不可遇

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占自己的病，世爻就是用神；随墓、助伤、入墓、入绝、反吟、伏吟，这几样里碰上一样，即使世爻旺相也凶。占别人的病，应爻就是用神；随墓、反吟、伏吟、入墓、入绝，即使应爻得了生，这几样里也一样都不能碰上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining one's own illness, the Self line is the Subject; following into the tomb, helping to harm, the tomb, Extinction, Reversed Chant and Hidden Chant: if any one of these is met, even a strong Self is ominous. In divining another person's illness, the Other line is the Subject; following into the tomb, Reversed Chant, Hidden Chant, the tomb and Extinction: even when the Other is generated, not one of these may be met.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「反伏」是反吟、伏吟（卦或爻变出与自己相冲、或与自己相同的），「墓绝」是入墓、入绝，「随墓」是本命、世爻随着官鬼等入了墓（见 LY.ILL.YM.09），都是易冒前面已经讲过的忌象（见 LY.ILL.YM.10）。这一条多出来的只是两件事：自占取世，代占取应；自占的一串里多一个「助伤」，代占的一串里没有。「助伤」在易冒助伤章里的定义是「以世爻受鬼克，而鬼长生于日」，只对世爻成立，所以只有自占才有。
>
> *Note:* 'Reversed and Hidden' means Reversed Chant and Hidden Chant (a hexagram or line changing into its own clash, or into the same as itself); 'tomb and Extinction' means entering the tomb or Extinction; 'following into the tomb' means the natal branch, the Self line and the like following the Pressure line into a tomb (see LY.ILL.YM.09); all are the ill omens the Yi Mao has already explained (see LY.ILL.YM.10). What this entry adds is only two things: one's own illness takes the Self and another's takes the Other, and the list for one's own illness has one more item, 'helping to harm', which the list for another's does not. In the Yi Mao's chapter on helping to harm this is defined as 'the Self line controlled by a ghost that has Birth on the day', which holds only for the Self line, so it appears only in divining for oneself.
