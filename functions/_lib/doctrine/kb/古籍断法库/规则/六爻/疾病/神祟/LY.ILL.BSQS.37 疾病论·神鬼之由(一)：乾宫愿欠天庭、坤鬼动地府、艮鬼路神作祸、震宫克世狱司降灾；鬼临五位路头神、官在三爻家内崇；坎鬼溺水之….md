# 疾病论·神鬼之由(一)：乾宫愿欠天庭、坤鬼动地府、艮鬼路神作祸、震宫克世狱司降灾；鬼临五位路头神、官在三爻家内崇；坎鬼溺水之魂、巽鬼悬梁之崇；坎宫船车之鬼、艮象路狱之魂

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里按八卦推是哪一路神鬼作祟：乾宫发动，是欠了天庭的愿；坤宫鬼爻兴旺，是冒犯了地府；艮宫鬼爻伤身，是路神作祸；震宫克世，是狱司降灾；鬼临在第五爻，是路头神；官鬼在第三爻，是家里作祟；坎宫鬼动，要求溺水之魂；巽宫鬼兴，要祭上吊之祟；坎宫交重是船车之鬼，艮象土爻发动是路上、狱中之魂。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness works out from the trigrams which kind of spirit or ghost is at work: Qian moving, a vow owed to the Heavenly Court; Kun ghost thriving, it disturbs the Earth Prefecture; Gen ghost harming the person, a road spirit causing harm; Zhen controlling the Self line, a spirit of the prison sends down calamity; the ghost on the fifth place, the Road-Head spirit; Pressure on the third line, a haunting within the house; a Kan ghost moving, seek the soul of one drowned; a Xun ghost rising, sacrifice to the haunting of one who hanged himself from a beam; Kan moving is a ghost of boats and carts, a Gen Earth line moving is a soul of the road or the prison.

> [!warning] 打架
> 鬼爻落在哪一爻是什么神，几处说法不同。这一段是「五位路头神、三爻家内崇」；卜筮全书的六位取象表（LY.ILL.BSQS.02）是初爻井神、二爻司命、三爻家先、四爻土地、五爻社司、六爻天神，三爻家先和这里的「家内崇」相通，五爻则是社司，不是路头神。另两种排法见 LY.ILL.HZL.11 和 LY.ILL.EL.37。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books differ on what spirit a ghost on each line stands for. This passage has 'the fifth place the Road-Head spirit, the third line a haunting within the house'; the table of the positions of the lines in the Bushi Quanshu (LY.ILL.BSQS.02) has the first line the well spirit, the second the Life-Governing Spirit, the third the family ancestors, the fourth the Earth God, the fifth the village altar spirit and the sixth the heavenly spirit; the third line, family ancestors, is akin to 'a haunting within the house' here, but the fifth line is the village altar spirit there and not the Road-Head spirit. Two other arrangements are in LY.ILL.HZL.11 and LY.ILL.EL.37. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段按「哪一宫（八卦）的鬼爻发动」来断是哪一类神鬼作祟：乾是天庭（欠了天上神的愿），坤是地府，艮是路神，震是狱司，坎是溺水的魂（也是船车的鬼），巽是上吊的鬼；按爻位断的只有「鬼临五位」「官在三爻」两处。「求七杀」的七杀是一颗凶星，「三司」是几个官署，书里没有解释，译文照字面；「动干地府」的「干」按冒犯、触犯读。这一段按八卦取神，和按爻位取神的表（LY.ILL.BSQS.02）是两条思路。
>
> *Note:* This passage judges which kind of spirit or ghost is at work from the palace (trigram) whose ghost line moves: Qian is the Heavenly Court (a vow owed to the gods above), Kun the Earth Prefecture, Gen a road spirit, Zhen a spirit of the prison, Kan the soul of one drowned (and a ghost of boats and carts), Xun the ghost of one who hanged himself; only 'the ghost on the fifth place' and 'Pressure on the third line' go by line position. 'Seek the Seven Killings' names a malign star, and the 'Three Offices' are several government offices; the book does not explain them and the translation keeps to the letter; the 'disturbs' in 'disturbs the Earth Prefecture' is read as offending or intruding on. This passage takes the spirit from the trigram, which is a different line of thought from the table that takes it from the line position (LY.ILL.BSQS.02).
