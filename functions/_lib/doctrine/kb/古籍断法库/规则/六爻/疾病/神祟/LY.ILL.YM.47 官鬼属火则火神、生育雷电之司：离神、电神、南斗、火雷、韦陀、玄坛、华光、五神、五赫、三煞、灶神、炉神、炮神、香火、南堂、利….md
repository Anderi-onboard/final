# 官鬼属火则火神、生育雷电之司：离神、电神、南斗、火雷、韦陀、玄坛、华光、五神、五赫、三煞、灶神、炉神、炮神、香火、南堂、利市、生育神之属，及由火成神者

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼属火的，就是火神，是掌管生育、雷电的。像离神、电神、南斗、火雷、韦陀、元坛、华光、五神、五赫、三煞、灶神、旗纛（？）、炉神、炮神、香火、南堂、利市、生育神这一类，以及因火而成神的，都可以类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line is Fire, it is a fire spirit, in charge of childbirth, thunder and lightning, as with the Li spirit, the Lightning God, the Southern Dipper, Fire and Thunder, Weituo, the Dark Altar, Huaguang, the Five Spirits, the Five Blazes, the Three Killings, the Kitchen God, the banner-standard (?), the Furnace God, the Cannon God, the incense fire, the Southern Hall, the Market Luck and the birth-giving spirits, and those who became spirits through fire; all can be reasoned by analogy.
