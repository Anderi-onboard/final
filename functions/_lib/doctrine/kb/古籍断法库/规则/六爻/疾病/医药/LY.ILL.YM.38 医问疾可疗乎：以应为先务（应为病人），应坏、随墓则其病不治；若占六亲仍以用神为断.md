# 医问疾可疗乎：以应为先务（应为病人），应坏、随墓则其病不治；若占六亲仍以用神为断

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 医生问这个病能不能治，先看应爻，因为对医生来说应爻就是病人；应爻坏了、或随墓，这个病就治不好。如果占的是病人的某个亲属，仍然以用神来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a doctor asks whether an illness can be treated, the Other line comes first, for to him the Other is the patient; if the Other is spoiled or follows into the tomb, the illness cannot be cured. If what is divined is one of the patient's relations, judgment still goes by the Subject.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 医生自己占的时候，对方（应）就是病人，所以头一个要看的是应爻；这和病家占病取世（自己）或取用神不同。「若占六亲」是说医生受托问的其实是病人的某个亲属，这时不再取应，回到用神。这是治病问的。
>
> *Note:* When the doctor himself divines, the other party (the Other) is the patient, so the first thing to read is the Other line; this differs from the patient's family, who take the Self (oneself) or the Subject. 'If what is divined is a relation' means the doctor is in fact asked about one of the patient's relations, and then the Other is no longer taken and judgment returns to the Subject. This entry answers the question of treating the illness.
