# 四利三元(十二岁煞)：从年上起顺轮，一太岁二太阳三丧门四太阴五官符六死符七岁破八龙德九白虎十福德十一吊客十二病符

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断易天机在神煞月令表前列了一份十二位的流年神煞：从当年的年支（太岁）起，顺着数下去，依次是太岁、太阳、丧门、太阴、官符、死符、岁破、龙德、白虎、福德、吊客、病符。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Before the monthly table of spirit-stars the Duanyi Tianji lists twelve year-stars: starting from the branch of the current year (Tai Sui) and counting forward, they are Tai Sui, Great Yang, Mourning Gate, Great Yin, Official Tally, Death Tally, Year-break, Dragon Virtue, White Tiger, Fortune Virtue, Guest of Condolence and Sickness Tally.

> [!warning] 打架
> 十二位岁煞各家的名单不一样。断易天机的「四利三元」是：一太岁、二太阳、三丧门、四太阴、五官符、六死符、七岁破、八龙德、九白虎、十福德、十一吊客、十二病符；卜筮全书的岁前神煞（LY.ILL.BSQS.51）是：一太岁剑锋伏尸、二太阳天空、三丧门、四勾绞贯索、五官符五鬼、六死符小耗、七栏干大耗、八暴败天厄、九飞廉白虎、十福德卷舌、十一天狗吊客、十二病符。第一、二、三、五、六、九、十、十一、十二位的主名两本一致；第四位（太阴对勾绞贯索）、第七位（岁破对栏干大耗）、第八位（龙德对暴败天厄）不同，而且卜筮全书每一位后面还并带了一两颗别的星。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books' lists of the twelve year-stars differ. The Duanyi Tianji's 'Four Gains and Three Origins' runs: first Tai Sui, second Great Yang, third Mourning Gate, fourth Great Yin, fifth Official Tally, sixth Death Tally, seventh Year-break, eighth Dragon Virtue, ninth White Tiger, tenth Fortune Virtue, eleventh Guest of Condolence, twelfth Sickness Tally; the stars before the Year in the Bushi Quanshu (LY.ILL.BSQS.51) run: first Tai Sui with Sword Edge and Hidden Corpse, second Great Yang with Heavenly Emptiness, third Mourning Gate, fourth Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through, fifth Official Tally with the Five Ghosts, sixth Death Tally with Small Loss, seventh Railing with Great Loss, eighth Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress, ninth Feilian with the White Tiger, tenth Fortune Virtue with the Curled Tongue, eleventh the Heavenly Dog with the Guest of Condolence, twelfth Sickness Tally. The principal names agree in the first, second, third, fifth, sixth, ninth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth places; the fourth (Great Yin against Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through), the seventh (Year-break against Railing and Great Loss) and the eighth (Dragon Virtue against Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress) differ, and the Bushi Quanshu joins one or two further stars to each place. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这是神煞月令表前面的一行名单，题目「四利三元」书里没有解释；列的十二位是按年支顺数的流年神煞：子年时太岁在子、太阳在丑、丧门在寅，依次往下，病符在亥。书里没有说哪一位管病，丧门、死符、吊客、病符的名字里带着丧和病，卜筮全书的岁前神煞把「病符切莫逢」「丧门主孝服」写明了（LY.ILL.BSQS.51），所以收在这里。「十福德」的福德是一颗神煞，不是子孙爻。「见天玄赋家宅类」是书里的一句小注，指这十二位在天玄赋的家宅部分也讲到。原文「官苻」「死苻」「病苻」的苻是符的异写。
>
> *Note:* This is a line of names before the monthly table of spirit-stars, and the heading 'Four Gains and Three Origins' is not explained in the book; the twelve places are year-stars counted forward from the year branch: in a Zi year Tai Sui is at Zi, Great Yang at Chou, the Mourning Gate at Yin, and so on, with the Sickness Tally at Hai. The book does not say which place governs illness; the Mourning Gate, Death Tally, Guest of Condolence and Sickness Tally carry mourning and illness in their names, and the stars before the Year in the Bushi Quanshu say outright 'the Sickness Tally is not to be met' and 'the Mourning Gate means mourning dress' (LY.ILL.BSQS.51), which is why the list is taken here. The Fortune Virtue of 'the tenth is Fortune Virtue' is a spirit-star and not the Output line. 'See the household section of the Tianxuan Fu' is a small note in the book, pointing out that these twelve are also dealt with in the household part of the Tianxuan Fu. The original writes 'tally' in a variant character in 'Official Tally', 'Death Tally' and 'Sickness Tally'.
