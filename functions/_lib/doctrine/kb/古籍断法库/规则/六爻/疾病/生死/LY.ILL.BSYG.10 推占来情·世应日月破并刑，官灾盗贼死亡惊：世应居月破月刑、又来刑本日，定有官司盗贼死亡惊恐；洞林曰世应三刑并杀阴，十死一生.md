# 推占来情·世应日月破并刑，官灾盗贼死亡惊：世应居月破月刑、又来刑本日，定有官司盗贼死亡惊恐；洞林曰世应三刑并杀阴，十死一生

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推占来情的歌说：世、应碰上日、月的破和刑，有官灾、盗贼、死亡、惊恐；如果不是和人争讼的事，就有死亡、盗贼和哭泣的声音。自注说：凡是世、应落在月破、月刑上、又来刑到本日的，一定有官司、盗贼、死亡、惊恐；再看有没有刑杀。《洞林》说，世应三刑又并着杀阴，是十死一生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verses on reading the purpose of the asker say: when the Self and the Other meet the break and punishment of the Day and Month, there is official calamity, thieves, death and fright; if it is not a matter of quarrelling with others, there are death, thieves and the sound of weeping. Its own note says: whenever the Self and Other sit in the Month-break or Month-punishment and also come to punish the day itself, there are surely lawsuits, thieves, death and fright; then look also for punishment and killing. The Dongling says: when the Self and Other meet the three punishments together with the Yin Killing, it is ten deaths to one life.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联讲的是世、应碰到凶的时候会有什么事，死亡只是其中一项，并不专指病；收在疾病域是因为它给了「十死一生」这个最重的断语，而这个断语在占病里最常被引。「洞林曰」引的是《洞林》（书名，书里没有说是哪个人的《洞林》）；「杀阴」大概就是前面（第 437 行自注）按月排的杀阴（正午、二巳、三辰……逐月下），这里书里没有指明。「月刑」是和月建相刑的地支。「亡贼哭泣声」的「亡贼」读作「死亡、盗贼」。「若不人与相争事」的「人与」是别人，书里字序如此，按字面。
>
> *Note:* This couplet is about what happens when the Self and Other meet ill omens, and death is only one item among them, not particular to illness; it is taken in the illness domain because it gives the heaviest verdict 'ten deaths to one life', which is most often quoted in divining illness. 'The Dongling says' quotes the Dongling (a book title; the book does not say whose Dongling); the 'Yin Killing' is probably the Yin Killing set by month spoken of earlier (the note at line 437: Wu in the first month, Si in the second, Chen in the third, going down by months), which the book does not specify here. 'Month-punishment' is the branch that punishes the Month branch. The 'death and thieves' of 'the sound of death and thieves and weeping' is read as 'death, thieves'. The 'person with' of 'if it is not a matter of quarrelling with others' is 'others', in the character order of the book, and is literal.
