# 五行推鬼神：金是伤司刀兵鬼、火是灶神香愿、木是枷锁、水是河伯江神；旧注细分火鬼二爻灶君、午火衰弱烧死鬼、木鬼带螣蛇吊死、水鬼衰弱落水鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 按鬼所属的五行推：金是被刀兵所伤的鬼，旺的是伤神，衰的是伤司手下的部众；火，一定是灶神或者香愿，落在二爻是灶君，衰弱的午火是被烧死的鬼；木是枷锁，东方的神鬼，变金或从金变出来的是戴枷锁的鬼，带螣蛇是吊死的鬼，木变木再加白虎是被夹死的鬼；水是河伯江神，旺相是江神河伯，衰弱是落水的鬼，在震、巽宫或木变水，是从船上掉下水淹死的鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Work it out by the element the ghost belongs to. Metal is a ghost wounded by blade and weapon: strong, an injuring spirit; weak, the followers of the Injuring Office. Fire is surely the Kitchen God or an incense vow: on the second line the Kitchen God, and a weak Wu Fire is a ghost burned to death. Wood is cangue and lock, a spirit or ghost of the east: changing into Metal or coming from Metal, a ghost in shackles; with the Coiling Snake, a hanged ghost; Wood changing into Wood with the White Tiger added, a ghost pressed to death. Water is the Lord of the River and the river god: strong, a river god; weak, a ghost who fell into the water; in the Zhen or Xun palace or Wood changing into Water, a ghost who fell from a boat and drowned.

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 要不要把五行落实到一位具体的神：旧注给出很具体的名目（火鬼在二爻是灶君，金旺是伤神、衰是伤司部众，水旺是江神河伯），王洪绪只留了金的一例；增删卜易的觉子认为不必把金官指为关公、岳公这一类具体的神，应该问病人心里疑的是哪位神，再占一卦验证（见 LY.ILL.ZS.24）。三处怎样落实到神，说法各不相同，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether to fix an element to a particular named spirit: the old commentary gives very specific names (a Fire ghost on the second line is the Kitchen God; strong Metal is an injuring spirit and weak Metal the followers of the Injuring Office; strong Water is a river god), while Wang Hongxu keeps only the Metal example; the Master Awakened (Jue Zi) in the Zengshan Buyi holds that one should not name Metal Pressure as Guan Gong, Yue Gong or the like, but ask which spirit the patient has in mind and divine again to check (see LY.ILL.ZS.24). The three differ on how far to name the spirit, and the books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「伤司」是主刀兵伤害的司，「部众」是它手下的众鬼；「香信」是香愿。「夹死鬼」旧注没有再解释，按字面译作被夹死的鬼。「河泊」是王洪绪引经文时对「河伯」的写法。「持世」是鬼爻落在世爻的位置，这里指鬼就是本地的，是社会中的香愿。
>
> *Note:* The 'Injuring Office' is the office in charge of wounds by blade and weapon and its 'followers' are the ghosts under it; 'incense pledge' is a vow of incense. 'A ghost pressed to death' is not explained further in the old commentary and is rendered literally. 'Hebo' is Wang Hongxu's way of writing 'Lord of the River' when he quotes the verse. 'Holding the Self position' means the ghost sits on the Self line's place, here meaning the ghost belongs to the locality: a vow made in the village society.
