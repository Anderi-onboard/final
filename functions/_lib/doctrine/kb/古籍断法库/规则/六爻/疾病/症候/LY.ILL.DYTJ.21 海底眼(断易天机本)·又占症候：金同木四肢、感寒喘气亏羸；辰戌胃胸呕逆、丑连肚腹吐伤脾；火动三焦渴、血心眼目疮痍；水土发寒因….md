# 海底眼(断易天机本)·又占症候：金同木四肢、感寒喘气亏羸；辰戌胃胸呕逆、丑连肚腹吐伤脾；火动三焦渴、血心眼目疮痍；水土发寒因冷得、泄泻虚劳耳肾衰

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金和木，病在四肢，感寒、喘、气亏体弱；辰、戌，胃和胸，呕逆；丑连着肚腹，吐而伤脾；火动，势头到极点，三焦干渴，血、心、眼目有疮痍；水、土发寒，是因受冷得的，泄泻、虚劳，耳和肾衰弱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Metal and Wood: the four limbs, catching a chill, quick panting, deficient qi and frailty; Chen and Xu: the stomach and chest, vomiting and retching; Chou links with the belly and abdomen, vomiting injures the spleen; Fire moving: at its extreme, the Triple Burner parched, and blood, heart and eyes have sores; Water and Earth cause chills, caught from cold, diarrhoea, consumption, and weakness of the ears and kidneys.

> [!note] 按
> 这是海底眼「症候」一段的另一个抄本（LY.ILL.HDY.05 是海底眼本书的一份），字句略有不同：海底眼本书是「肺病金同木四肢，感寒痰喘气亏羸」，这里是「候病金同木四肢，感寒疾喘气亏羸」；又，海底眼本书里「水主发寒因冷得，世泻虚劳耳肾衰」，这里是「水土发寒因冷得，泄泻虚劳耳肾衰」。「（2）」是今人加的注号，那条注不收。
>
> *Note:* This is another copy of the passage on symptoms of the Haidi Yan (LY.ILL.HDY.05 is the copy in the Haidi Yan itself), with slight differences in wording: the Haidi Yan itself has 'lung illness: Metal and Wood the four limbs, catching a chill, phlegm and panting, deficient qi and frailty' where this has 'in taking the illness: Metal and Wood the four limbs, catching a chill, quick panting'; and where the Haidi Yan itself has 'Water governs chills caught from cold, the Self: diarrhoea, consumption, weakness of the ears and kidneys', this has 'Water and Earth cause chills caught from cold, diarrhoea, consumption, weakness of the ears and kidneys'. The '(2)' is a modern annotation mark and that annotation is not quoted.
