# 求医疗病：子为药剂应为医，福德交重病必驱，鬼旺福灾病不治，官衰子旺患能除，应空只恐人难遇，子陷还愁药不宜；子发动药奏神功；官鬼太旺子休囚不愈；父发动药力全空，父与兄弟同发子赖兄生药有效

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以子孙为主，应象为凭。子孙是药剂，应爻是医生；福德发动，病必被驱走；鬼旺、福衰，灾病治不好；官鬼衰、子孙旺，病患能除；应爻空，只怕遇不到医生；子孙陷落，还愁药不对症。注说：凡是求医，子孙是用象，子孙如果发动，药就奏神功；其中官鬼太旺，子孙休囚，这个灾不会好；官鬼如果衰弱、福神兴隆，再得到应克世爻、或外卦伤内卦，一定遇到良医，没有灾不好的；如果内克外、以及世克应、子孙旺相，还行；子孙再休囚，药就没有效验，月将伤官铲有余；鬼如果遇上生财、或动，子孙纵然发动也成空虚。父爻如果发动，药力全空；父爻与兄弟同时发动，子孙靠兄弟生，那药有效；如果得到月建、或日辰克制官爻，就像遇到岐伯；倘或鬼临日月、或鬼持世卦，虽然有子孙药也无功；又如子孙、财爻并发，官鬼伏着、财爻扶它，怎么能治病？财爻、子孙纵然发动，卦里没有鬼或鬼空，仍然有效；凡是论医药，若得子孙上卦，官鬼父母两方都安，应爻不空亡，才有效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the medicine and the Other is the doctor. The fortune spirit moving, the illness is surely driven out; the ghost strong and the fortune spirit weak, the calamity cannot be treated; the Pressure line weak and Output strong, the affliction can be removed; the Other Void, one fears only that the man is hard to meet; Output fallen, one worries still that the medicine is unsuitable. The commentary says: in seeking a doctor Output is the Subject image, and if Output moves the medicine works wonders; where the Pressure line is too strong and Output resting or trapped, the calamity is not cured; if the Pressure line is weak and the fortune spirit thriving, and further the Other controls the Self line or the outer trigram wounds the inner trigram, one is sure to meet a Lu Bian-like physician and no calamity is not cured; if the inner controls the outer, or the Self controls the Other, and Output is strong, it is still fine; if Output is again resting or trapped the medicine has no effect, the Month general wounding the official and planing off what is in excess; if the ghost meets generation of Wealth, or moves, even if Output moves it is hollow. If the Resource line moves, the power of the medicine is wholly empty; if the Resource and Peer lines move together, Output depends on Peer to generate it and the medicine is effective; if the Month or the Day controls the Pressure line, it is as if one met Qi Bo; if the ghost sits on the Day or Month, or the ghost holds the Self, then even with Output as medicine there is no effect; again, if Output and Wealth both move, the Pressure line hidden and supported by Wealth, how can the illness be treated? If Wealth and Output move but the hexagram has no ghost or the ghost is Void, it is still effective; in all discussion of medicine, it works only if Output is in the hexagram, the Pressure line and the Resource line are both at peace, and the Other is not Void.

> [!note] 按
> 「卢医」是扁鹊（卢地的医），泛指高明的医生；「岐伯」是传说中黄帝的医官，也是良医的代称。原文这一句「月将伤官铲有余」指子孙休囚时，月将来伤官鬼，把官鬼的多余之势铲掉——这一句前后的连接不很清楚，这里按字面译。这一章和黄金策医药章（LY.ILL.HJC.58 到 HJC.92）是同一路读法：子孙是药，应爻是医，官鬼是病；子旺官衰药灵，官旺子衰不愈；父爻动克子孙则药力空；财爻生官鬼则药误。与易冒医药章（LY.ILL.YM.34 到 YM.36）、增删卜易（LY.ILL.ZS.26 到 ZS.34）也一致。
>
> *Note:* 'A Lu physician' is Bian Que (the physician of Lu), used for any skilled doctor, and 'Qi Bo' is the legendary physician-official of the Yellow Emperor, also a name for an excellent doctor. The line 'the Month general wounding the official and planing off what is in excess' means that when Output is resting or trapped, the Month general comes to wound the Pressure line and plane off its excess strength; how it connects with what surrounds it is not very clear, and it is translated literally. This chapter reads in the same way as the Huangjin Ce's chapter on medicine (LY.ILL.HJC.58 to HJC.92): Output is the medicine, the Other is the doctor, and the Pressure line is the illness; Output strong and Pressure weak means the medicine works, Pressure strong and Output weak means no cure; the Resource line moving and controlling Output empties the power of the medicine; Wealth generating the Pressure line means the medicine goes wrong. It also agrees with the Yi Mao's chapter on medicine (LY.ILL.YM.34 to YM.36) and the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.26 to ZS.34).
