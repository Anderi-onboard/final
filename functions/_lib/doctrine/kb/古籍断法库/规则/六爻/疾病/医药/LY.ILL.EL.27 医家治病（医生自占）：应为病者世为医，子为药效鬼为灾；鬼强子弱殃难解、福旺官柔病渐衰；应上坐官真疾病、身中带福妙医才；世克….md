# 医家治病（医生自占）：应为病者世为医，子为药效鬼为灾；鬼强子弱殃难解、福旺官柔病渐衰；应上坐官真疾病、身中带福妙医才；世克应灾当瘥、应生身宾主谐；子动兄安财静旺得多财；应空他不迎吾救、世陷吾非治彼灾

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以子孙为主，世应为凭。应是病人，世是医生；子孙是药效，鬼是灾；鬼强、子孙弱，殃难解；福旺、官柔，病渐衰；应上坐着官鬼，是真疾病；身上带福，是妙医才。世如果克应，灾当痊愈；应如果生世，确实是宾主相合；子动、兄安、财静旺，仙丹妙剂多得财；应空，他不迎我去救；世陷落，我治不了他的灾。注说：医生自己来占，反而把应当作病人，世是自己，子孙是药效，鬼是灾殃：官旺子衰、或官动子静，病一定难医；子动官静、或子旺官柔，药没有不灵验的；应如果临官，病真的无法治；世如果值福，治病能痊愈。世克应爻、或内克外卦，能治好他的灾；应如果生世以及外卦生内爻、或卦逢六合，是有为而来、宾主相得；妻财旺相，子孙交重发动，鬼静兄弟安，药灵而利益加倍；应值空亡，他必定无心就我；世居空地，自己心里萎靡懒散，医恐怕做不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the main line, and the Self and the Other are the ground. The Other is the patient and the Self is the doctor; Output is the effect of the medicine and the ghost is the calamity; the ghost strong and Output weak, the misfortune is hard to resolve; the fortune spirit strong and the Pressure line soft, the illness gradually wanes; a Pressure line sitting on the Other means a real illness; the fortune spirit on the body means a marvellous physician. If the Self controls the Other, the calamity should heal; if the Other generates the Self, host and guest truly accord; Output moving, Peer at peace and Wealth still and strong, elixir and wonderful remedy earn much wealth; the Other Void, he does not welcome my rescue; the Self fallen, I cannot cure his calamity. The commentary says: when a doctor himself divines, on the contrary the Other is taken as the patient, the Self as himself, Output as the effect of the medicine and the ghost as the calamity: with Pressure strong and Output weak, or Pressure moving and Output still, the illness is surely hard to treat; with Output moving and Pressure still, or Output strong and Pressure soft, the medicine is never without effect; if the Other sits on Pressure, the illness is real and cannot be treated; if the Self holds the fortune spirit, the treatment can heal. The Self controlling the Other line, or the inner trigram controlling the outer, can cure his calamity; if the Other generates the Self, or the outer generates the inner line, or the hexagram meets six-combine, one comes with purpose and host and guest suit each other; Wealth strong, Output moving, the ghost still and Peer at peace, the medicine is effective and the profit doubled; if the Other is Void, he surely has no heart to come to me; if the Self sits in Void, my own heart is dispirited and lazy, and the doctor may not succeed.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章的立场和上一章（LY.ILL.EL.26）相反：上一章是病家占，应是医生、世是病人；这一章是医生自己占，应是病人、世是医生。其余不变：子孙是药，官鬼是病。这和易冒医药章里医生问的四问（LY.ILL.YM.38 到 YM.41）是同一件事：易冒说「医问疾可疗乎，以应为先务，盖应为病人」，与这里「应为病者世为医」相同。这一章讲医生自己占两件事：病能不能治（子孙对官鬼），和这趟医有没有酬谢（财爻、应世关系）。「六合」按这个写法译作 six-combine。
>
> *Note:* This chapter stands opposite to the last (LY.ILL.EL.26): there the patient's family divined, and the Other was the doctor and the Self the patient; here the doctor himself divines, and the Other is the patient and the Self the doctor. The rest is unchanged: Output is the medicine and the Pressure line the illness. It is the same matter as the four questions the doctor asks in the Yi Mao's chapter on medicine (LY.ILL.YM.38 to YM.41): the Yi Mao's 'when a doctor asks whether an illness can be treated, the Other comes first, for to him the Other is the patient' agrees with this chapter's 'the Other is the patient and the Self the doctor'. The chapter treats two matters for a doctor's own divination: whether the illness can be treated (Output against the Pressure line), and whether this visit will bring a fee (the Wealth line, and the relation of Other and Self).
