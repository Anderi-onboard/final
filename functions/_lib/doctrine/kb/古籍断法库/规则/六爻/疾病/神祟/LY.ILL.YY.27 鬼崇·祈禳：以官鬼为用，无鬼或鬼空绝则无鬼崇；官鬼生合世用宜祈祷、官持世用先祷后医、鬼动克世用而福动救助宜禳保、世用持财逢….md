# 鬼崇·祈禳：以官鬼为用，无鬼或鬼空绝则无鬼崇；官鬼生合世用宜祈祷、官持世用先祷后医、鬼动克世用而福动救助宜禳保、世用持财逢火鬼动宜众保；阳鬼为神、阴官为鬼、鬼带书贵为神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以官鬼为用爻：卦里没有鬼，或者鬼遇到空、绝，就没有鬼崇。官鬼来生、合世爻和用爻的，适合祈祷；官爻持着世爻、用爻的，先祈祷后医治；鬼发动刑害克冲世爻、用爻，而有福神发动来救助的，适合禳解保佑；世爻、用爻持着财、又遇火鬼发动的，适合大家一起保祷。问祈禳的是哪一路神鬼：阳鬼是神，阴官是鬼；鬼带文书、贵人的，是神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line is the Subject: if there is no ghost in the hexagram, or the ghost meets Void or Extinction, there are no ghostly influences. If the Pressure line generates or combines with the Self and the Subject, prayer is fitting; if Pressure holds the Self and Subject, pray first and treat afterwards; if the ghost moves to punish, harm, control or clash the Self and Subject and Fortune moves to rescue and help, exorcism and protection suit; if the Self and Subject hold Wealth and a Fire ghost moves, a communal rite of protection suits. To ask which spirit or ghost the prayer is for: a yang ghost is a spirit and a yin Pressure line is a ghost; a ghost carrying the Document and the Noble is a spirit.

> [!warning] 打架
> 怎么分神和鬼：易隐说「阳鬼为神，阴官为鬼」，按鬼爻是阴爻还是阳爻分；这和卜筮正宗的王洪绪「阳为神为男，阴为鬼为女」一致（LY.ILL.HJC.94），而黄金策的旧注和增删卜易的觉子都是按旺衰分——旺相是神、休囚是鬼（LY.ILL.HJC.94、LY.ILL.ZS.23），觉子还说鬼休囚或空破墓绝根本不是鬼神作祟。两种分法不同，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to tell a spirit from a ghost: the Yi Yin says 'a yang ghost is a spirit, a yin Pressure line is a ghost', by whether the Pressure line is a yin or a yang line; this agrees with Wang Hongxu of the Bushi Zhengzong, 'yang is a spirit and male, yin a ghost and female' (LY.ILL.HJC.94), while the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Juezi of the Zengshan Buyi both go by strength — strong is a spirit, resting or trapped a ghost (LY.ILL.HJC.94 and LY.ILL.ZS.23), and Juezi adds that if the ghost is resting, trapped, Void, broken, in a tomb or in Extinction it is not a ghost or spirit making trouble at all. The two divide differently and the books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「祈祷」「禳保」「众保」是三种不同的仪式：祈祷是向神祈求，禳保是禳解并求保佑，众保是众人一起（如社里的会）保祷。「书贵」按字面是文书、贵人两颗星，书里没有再解释。
>
> *Note:* 'Qidao', 'rangbao' and 'zhongbao' are three different rites: prayer is asking a spirit, 'rangbao' is exorcism together with a request for protection, and 'zhongbao' is protection sought by many together (as in a village society). 'Shugui' is literally the Document and the Noble stars and the book does not explain further.
