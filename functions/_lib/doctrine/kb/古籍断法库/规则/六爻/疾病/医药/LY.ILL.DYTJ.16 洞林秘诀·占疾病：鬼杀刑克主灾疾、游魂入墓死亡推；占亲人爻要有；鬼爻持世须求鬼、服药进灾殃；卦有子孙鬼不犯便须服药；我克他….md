# 洞林秘诀·占疾病：鬼杀刑克主灾疾、游魂入墓死亡推；占亲人爻要有；鬼爻持世须求鬼、服药进灾殃；卦有子孙鬼不犯便须服药；我克他先呕逆、他克我泻为伤；忌食鬼爻生肖肉、子孙能解

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼、杀、刑克，主有灾病；游魂卦、入墓，按死亡推。占亲人，那个爻要有，没有就主倾危。虎刑这一个杀最可怕，其余别的凶恶都可以禳解。鬼爻持世，要向鬼求（祈祷），这时若服药反而增加灾殃；卦里有子孙，鬼不来侵犯，就该服药，得安康。我克他，先呕逆；他克我，泻而成伤。忌吃鬼爻生肖的肉，子孙的能解，就无妨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost, a killing, punishment and control mean calamity and illness; a Wandering Soul hexagram or entering a tomb is inferred as death. Divining for a relative, the relative's line must be there, and if it is not it means peril. The killing of Tiger and punishment is the one to fear deeply, and all the other evils can be warded off. With the Pressure line holding the Self one must seek the ghost, and if one then takes medicine it only advances calamity; if the hexagram has Output and the ghost does not offend, then one should take medicine and gain health and peace. If I control him there is retching first; if he controls me purging is the harm. Avoid eating the meat of the animal of the ghost line; that of Output can dissolve it and does no harm.

> [!note] 按
> 「我」「他」这里按世和应读（世克应先呕逆、应克世泻），孙膑断疾病歌的「外克内应克世有药频频泻、内克外世克应多呕吐」是同一对（LY.ILL.DYTJ.04）。「鬼爻持世须求鬼」的「须求鬼」按字面译作「要向鬼求」：上下文是接着说服药会增加灾殃，所以这里大概是说该向鬼神祈求，而不是服药，这是读法，原文没有明说。「虎刑一杀」是白虎加刑的杀，原文没有展开。「（1）」是今人加的注号，那条注不收；忌食生肖肉的做法，火珠林一段（LY.ILL.HZL.04 一带）也有：丑鬼不可食牛肉，未子孙当食羊。
>
> *Note:* 'I' and 'he' are read here as the Self and the Other (the Self controlling the Other means retching first, the Other controlling the Self purging), the same pair as in Sun Bin's song, 'the outer controlling the inner, the Other controlling the Self: purging again and again; the inner controlling the outer, the Self controlling the Other: mostly vomiting' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.04). The 'xu qiu gui' of 'the Pressure line holding the Self, one must seek the ghost' is rendered literally as 'must seek the ghost': the context goes on to say that taking medicine will add calamity, so it probably says one should pray to the spirits rather than take medicine, which is a reading and the original does not say so plainly. 'The killing of Tiger and punishment' is the killing of the White Tiger with punishment, and the original does not expand on it. The '(1)' is a modern annotation mark and that annotation is not quoted; the practice of avoiding the meat of the branch animal is also in the Huozhu Lin (around LY.ILL.HZL.04): a Chou ghost must not eat beef, and Output at Wei should eat mutton.
