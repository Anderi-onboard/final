# 鬼化长生、忌化进神须虑添灾：鬼动病重，长生于日辰或动化长生病势必增；忌神动化进神同此，化退则渐减；觉子：占父母或妻占夫，鬼是元神、用神，其病即愈

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼变入长生、忌神变进神，要担心添灾。鬼爻发动，病势一定重，如果在日辰上长生、或动而变入长生，病势一定加重；克用神的是忌神，它动而变进神，也是这个意思；忌神变退，病就渐渐减轻。觉子补充：鬼是父母的元神，占父母的病，这病马上就好；鬼是妻子占丈夫时的用神，占丈夫的病，也主马上就好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure changing into Birth, or the Adversary changing into an Advancing spirit: beware of added trouble. When the Pressure line moves the illness is surely heavy; if it is at Birth on the Day, or moves and changes into Birth, the illness surely grows; what controls the Subject is the Adversary, and if it moves and changes into an Advancing spirit the meaning is the same; if the Adversary changes into Retreat, the illness gradually eases. Juezi adds: Pressure is the Support of the Resource line, so in divining a parent the illness heals at once; Pressure is the Subject when a wife divines her husband, and in divining a husband the illness also heals at once.

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 觉子曰：鬼乃父母之元神，占父母者，其病即愈； | 觉子说：鬼是父母的元神，占父母的病，这病马上就好； | Juezi says: Pressure is the Support of the Resource line, so in divining a parent the illness heals at once; |
> | 鬼乃妇占夫之用神，占夫者亦主即愈。 | 鬼是妻子占丈夫时的用神，占丈夫的病，也主马上就好。 | Pressure is the Subject when a wife divines her husband, and in divining a husband the illness also heals at once. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是黄金策病体章 LY.ILL.HJC.43（鬼化长生日下沉重）的野鹤版：鬼变入长生病加重，他又把忌神动化进神、化退神放在一起说。觉子的补充和 LY.ILL.HJC.32 里王洪绪的限定一样：占父母、占丈夫时，鬼不是凶煞，是元神或用神，所以鬼动反而是好事。
>
> *Note:* This entry is the Old Man's version of LY.ILL.HJC.43 in the chapter on the patient's condition (Pressure changing into Birth, heavy these days): Pressure changing into Birth makes the illness heavier, and he adds the Adversary moving into an Advancing spirit or changing into Retreat. Juezi's addition is the same limit as Wang Hongxu's in LY.ILL.HJC.32: in divining a parent or a husband Pressure is not a fierce killer but the Support or the Subject, and so its moving is on the contrary good.
