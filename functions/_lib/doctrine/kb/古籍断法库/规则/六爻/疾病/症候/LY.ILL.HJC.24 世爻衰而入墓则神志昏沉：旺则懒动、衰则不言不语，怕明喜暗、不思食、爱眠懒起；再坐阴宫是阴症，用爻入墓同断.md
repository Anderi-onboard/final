# 世爻衰而入墓则神志昏沉：旺则懒动、衰则不言不语，怕明喜暗、不思食、爱眠懒起；再坐阴宫是阴症，用爻入墓同断

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻衰弱又落进墓里，病人昏沉困倦。世爻有气（旺相），不过是懒得动；衰弱就不说话：怕亮喜暗，不想吃东西，爱睡，怕起床，懒得睁眼。再坐在阴宫上，一定是阴症。用爻入墓也这样断；鬼爻持世入墓、鬼的墓临在用爻上，都照这个断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A weak Self line falling into the tomb makes the patient drowsy and clouded. If the Self line is strong and has force, he is only too listless to move; if it is weak, he does not speak: he shrinks from the light and likes the dark, has no wish to eat, loves to sleep, dreads getting up and is too listless even to open his eyes. If it also sits in a yin palace it is surely a yin syndrome. A Subject line entering the tomb is judged the same way; so are a Pressure line on the Self line entering the tomb and the tomb of Pressure falling on the Subject line.

> | 用爻入墓、鬼墓临用、原神入墓，皆依此法断。 | 用爻入墓、鬼的墓库临在用爻上、原神入墓，都照这个办法断。 | A Subject line entering the tomb, the tomb of Pressure falling on the Subject line, and the Support entering the tomb are all judged by this method. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 两家末句列的情形不完全一样：旧注是「用爻入墓」「世鬼入墓」「鬼墓临用」，王洪绪是「用爻入墓」「鬼墓临用」「原神入墓」。旧注的「世鬼入墓」，按上下文读作鬼爻持世、又入墓。「阴症」是虚寒、精神萎靡一类的症候。
>
> *Note:* The two ends differ a little in the cases they list: the old commentary gives 'a Subject line entering the tomb', 'a Pressure line on the Self line entering the tomb' and 'the tomb of Pressure falling on the Subject line', Wang Hongxu 'a Subject line entering the tomb', 'the tomb of Pressure falling on the Subject line' and 'the Support entering the tomb'. The old commentary's 'shi gui ru mu' is read from the context as a Pressure line holding the Self line and entering the tomb. A 'yin syndrome' is one of cold and deficiency, with the spirits low.
