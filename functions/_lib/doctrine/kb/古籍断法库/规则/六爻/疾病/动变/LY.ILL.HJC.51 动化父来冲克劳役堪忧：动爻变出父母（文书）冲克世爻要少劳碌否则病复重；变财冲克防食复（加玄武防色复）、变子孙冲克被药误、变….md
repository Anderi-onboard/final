# 动化父来冲克劳役堪忧：动爻变出父母（文书）冲克世爻要少劳碌否则病复重；变财冲克防食复（加玄武防色复）、变子孙冲克被药误、变兄弟冲克恼气即复；日辰带刑冲则不依此断

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里的动爻变出父母（文书）来冲克世爻，要自己放宽心、少劳碌，不然病马上复发，反而加重。变出财爻来冲克，要提防饮食引起复发；再加玄武，要提防女色引起复发；变出子孙来冲克，是被药耽误了；变出兄弟来冲克，一生气就复发。以上这些，如果日辰带着刑、冲，就不能照这个断，只能说是因为这个而又加重，因为日辰上就是鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a moving line in the hexagram changes out the Resource line (documents) that clashes or controls the Self line, he should keep at ease and toil little, or the illness returns at once and grows heavier. If it changes out Wealth that clashes or controls, beware of a relapse through food; with the Dark Tortoise added, beware of a relapse through lust; if it changes out Output that clashes or controls, he has been misled by medicine; if it changes out Peer that clashes or controls, anger brings the relapse. If in all this the Day carries punishment or clash, one may not judge this way: one can say only that it grows heavier because of this, for the Day is Pressure there.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2400 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦中父母爻动来冲克用爻，或用爻动变父母不冲克者，宜自减少劳碌，不然病即反复，又加沉重矣。 | 卦里父母爻动来冲克用爻，或者用爻发动变成父母而没有冲克的，要自己减少劳碌，不然病马上反复，又加重了。 | If in the hexagram the Resource line moves to clash or control the Subject line, or the Subject line moves and changes into Resource without clashing or controlling, he should reduce his toil himself, or the illness relapses at once and grows heavier again. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说父母冲克的是世爻；王洪绪说是用爻，又添了一种「用爻动变父母、没有冲克」，那四个字书里没有再解释，译文按字面。旧注还有财、子孙、兄弟三种变出的情形和玄武的加成，以及日辰带刑冲时怎么改断，王洪绪都没有收。「文书」是父母爻的另一个名字。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says what the Resource line clashes or controls is the Self line; Wang Hongxu says the Subject line, and adds the case 'the Subject line moves and changes into Resource, without clashing or controlling', on which the book says no more and which the translation renders as written. The old commentary also gives the changes into Wealth, Output and Peer, the addition of the Dark Tortoise, and how to judge otherwise when the Day carries punishment or clash, none of which Wang Hongxu takes. 'Documents' is another name for the Resource line.
