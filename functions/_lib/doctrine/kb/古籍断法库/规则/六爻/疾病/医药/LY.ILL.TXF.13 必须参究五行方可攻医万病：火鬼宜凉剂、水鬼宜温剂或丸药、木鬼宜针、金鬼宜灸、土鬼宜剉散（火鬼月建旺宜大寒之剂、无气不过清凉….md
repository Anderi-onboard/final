# 必须参究五行方可攻医万病：火鬼宜凉剂、水鬼宜温剂或丸药、木鬼宜针、金鬼宜灸、土鬼宜剉散（火鬼月建旺宜大寒之剂、无气不过清凉）；金鬼利南方医人、木鬼利西方、水鬼利四散、火鬼利北方、土鬼利东方；前卦水鬼后卦火鬼先须四散医治、终于北方医人收功

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 必须钻研五行，才可以攻医各种病。注说：火鬼宜凉剂；水鬼宜温剂，或者丸药；木鬼宜针；金鬼宜灸；土鬼宜剉散。火鬼在月建旺的时候，宜大寒之剂；无气，不过用清凉的药。其余照这样推。金鬼利于请南方的医生，木鬼利西方的医生，水鬼利四散的医生，火鬼利北方的医生，土鬼利东方的医生。如果前一卦是水鬼、后一卦是火鬼，先要四散地医治，最后由北方的医生收功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* One must study the five phases before one can attack and treat the myriad illnesses. The commentary says: a Fire ghost suits cooling remedies; a Water ghost suits warming remedies, or pills; a Wood ghost suits the needle; a Metal ghost suits moxa; an Earth ghost suits cut-and-ground powders. When a Fire ghost is strong on the Month, it suits very cold remedies; without qi, only cooling and clearing medicines; the rest follow this. A Metal ghost favours a doctor from the south, a Wood ghost a doctor from the west, a Water ghost doctors from all quarters, a Fire ghost a doctor from the north, an Earth ghost a doctor from the east. If the earlier hexagram is a Water ghost and the later a Fire ghost, one must first treat with doctors from all quarters, and the work is completed at the end by a doctor from the north.

> [!warning] 打架
> 请医生要往哪个方向请，几家不同。天玄赋注（LY.ILL.TXF.13）按鬼的五行取克它的方位：金鬼利南方（火克金）、木鬼利西方（金克木）、火鬼利北方（水克火）、土鬼利东方（木克土），水鬼利四散（土克水，土没有定方）；火珠林（见 LY.ILL.HZL.09）大体同此说，只有水鬼一项作「利土值」；增删卜易（LY.ILL.ZS.34）则是往「子孙」所在的方位请（午火子孙请南方的医生），子孙休囚空破时看应爻所在的方位，再有独发生用神的爻取那个爻的方位——一个按官鬼的克星取方位，一个按子孙或应爻取方位，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* In which direction to call the doctor, the books differ. The commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.13) takes the direction that controls the phase of the ghost: a Metal ghost favours the south (Fire controls Metal), a Wood ghost the west (Metal controls Wood), a Fire ghost the north (Water controls Fire), an Earth ghost the east (Wood controls Earth), and a Water ghost favours all quarters (Earth controls Water, and Earth has no fixed direction); the Huozhu Lin (see LY.ILL.HZL.09) says much the same, only for the Water ghost it has 'favoured by the earth quarter'; the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.34) calls the doctor from the direction of Output instead (Wu Fire Output, a doctor from the south), and when Output is resting, trapped, Void or broken looks at the direction of the Other line, and further takes the direction of a line that moves alone and generates the Subject — one takes the direction from what controls the ghost, the other from Output or the Other line, and the books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是「五行用药」和「五行请医的方位」。用药：火鬼凉剂、水鬼温剂丸药、木鬼针、金鬼灸、土鬼剉散——和易隐「医药·治法与用药」（LY.ILL.YY.25：官鬼属金宜灸、属木宜针、属水宜温药丸药、属火宜凉药汤药、属土宜咬咀）一致，「剉散」和「咬咀」都是把药切碎捣散的剂型。「火鬼月建旺宜大寒之剂」的「月建旺」是火鬼旺在当月；这条和黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.76）「火鬼生旺遇生扶用大寒药、休囚死绝用凉药」是同一句。请医方位：金鬼利南方、木鬼利西方、火鬼利北方、土鬼利东方，正是各自的克星方位；水鬼「利四散」是水的克星土没有固定方位（中央）。「前卦水鬼、后卦火鬼，先须四散医治，终于北方医人收功」是说两次占卜先水鬼（先请四面的医生），后火鬼（最后北方的医生才收功）。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* This section is 'medicine by the five phases' and 'the direction to call a doctor by the five phases'. Medicine: a Fire ghost cooling remedies, a Water ghost warming remedies and pills, a Wood ghost the needle, a Metal ghost moxa, an Earth ghost cut-and-ground powders — the same as the Yi Yin's 'medicine: treatment and the use of medicine' (LY.ILL.YY.25: a Metal Pressure line suits moxa, Wood the needle, Water warming medicine and pills, Fire cooling medicine and decoctions, Earth crushing and grinding); 'cut-and-ground powders' and 'crushing and grinding' are both forms of medicine cut fine and ground to a powder. The 'strong on the Month' of 'a Fire ghost strong on the Month suits very cold remedies' is the Fire ghost strong in the current month; this is the same sentence as the Huangjin Ce's (LY.ILL.HJC.76, 'a Fire ghost in a strong place and helped is treated with very cold medicines, resting, trapped, dead or in Extinction with cooling medicines'). The direction for calling a doctor: a Metal ghost favours the south, Wood the west, Fire the north, Earth the east, which is exactly the direction of what controls each; a Water ghost 'favours all quarters' because Earth, which controls Water, has no fixed direction (it is the centre). 'The earlier hexagram a Water ghost and the later a Fire ghost: first treat with doctors from all quarters, and the work is completed by a doctor from the north' says that of the two divinations the Water ghost comes first (call doctors from all sides first) and the Fire ghost after (a doctor from the north finally completes it). After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
