# 君臣、阍寺、将卒、师弟取用：臣占君用九五、君占臣用应爻、阍寺用财、将占卒用子孙、师弟谊用子；宗人之臣从朝廷长幼取用（太子臣用子、皇弟兄臣用弟兄、太后臣用父母）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 臣子占君主，用九五爻，不论是皇亲贵戚、异姓的臣子还是平民；宗室的官员占他自己的本主，就按本主在朝廷里的长幼辈分来取：太子的臣子占太子，用朝廷的储君当子，取子孙爻；皇帝弟兄的臣子占皇弟皇兄，用朝廷的枝叶当兄弟，取兄弟爻；太后的臣子占太后，取父母爻。君主占臣子，用应爻，因为那是辅佐我的人。对守门的宦官用财爻；将领占士卒用子孙爻，为的是制住鬼；师徒的情谊用子爻。取用神这件事，占卜的人屡屡弄错，不能不审慎；占筮的人取用神常有两种困惑，以致吉凶不灵验，所以在这里说得详细，其他的取用也照这样推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A minister divining his sovereign takes the ninth-five line, whether he is of the imperial kin, of another surname or a commoner. An official of the imperial clan divining his own lord takes by the lord's seniority at court: the Crown Prince's minister divining the Crown Prince takes the court's heir apparent as the Output line; the minister of the emperor's brothers divining an imperial brother takes the branches of the court as brothers, the Peer line; the Empress Dowager's minister divining the Empress Dowager takes the Resource line. A sovereign divining his minister takes the Other line, for he is my aide. For palace gate-keepers and eunuchs the Wealth line is taken; a general divining his soldiers takes Output, in order to control the ghost; and in the bond of teacher and pupil the Output line is taken. In taking the Subject diviners have repeatedly erred, and one cannot but be careful; diviners often fall into two kinds of confusion in taking the Subject, so that fortune and misfortune do not prove true, and therefore it is set out in detail here, and other cases may be reckoned by it.

> [!note] 按
> 「阍寺」是守宫门的人和宫里的宦官。「宗人之臣，代君而用」注里解作：宗室的官员占他自己的本主，不用九五，而是照本主在朝廷里排的辈分取六亲。注里说的「两惑」没有点明是哪两种；从上下文看，一是不知道该取哪一亲，二是把年纪、亲疏当成取用的依据，前面的「主虽少而用父母」等句就是纠正后一种，这是推的，书里没有写。
>
> *Note:* 'Gate-keepers and eunuchs' are those who guard the palace gates and the eunuchs within. Of 'the ministers of the clan take in place of the sovereign', the note explains that an official of the imperial clan divining his own lord does not take the ninth-five line but takes the six relations by the lord's rank in the court's generations. The note does not say which two 'confusions' are meant; from the context they seem to be not knowing which relation to take and taking age or closeness as the ground of choice, the latter being what the earlier sentences such as 'a master, though young, takes the Resource line' correct, but this is inferred and not written in the book.
