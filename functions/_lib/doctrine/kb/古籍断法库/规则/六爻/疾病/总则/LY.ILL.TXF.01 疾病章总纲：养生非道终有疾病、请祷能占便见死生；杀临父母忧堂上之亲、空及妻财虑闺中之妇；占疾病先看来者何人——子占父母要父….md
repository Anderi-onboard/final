# 疾病章总纲：养生非道终有疾病、请祷能占便见死生；杀临父母忧堂上之亲、空及妻财虑闺中之妇；占疾病先看来者何人——子占父母要父母上卦、妻占夫看鬼、夫占妻看财、父占子看子、占兄弟朋友看兄弟、占奴仆看子孙；用爻不上卦或空亡多凶少吉

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国家的治乱，靠的是风俗纪纲；人的寿夭，不在身体强弱。养生不得法，终究会有疾病；请祷能占卜，就能看出死生的决断。凶杀临在父母爻上，要为堂上的双亲担忧；妻财爻落空，要为闺中的妻子担心灾祸。注说：占疾病，先看来占的是什么人：儿子占父母，要父母爻上卦；妻子占丈夫看鬼爻；丈夫占妻子看财爻；父亲占儿子看子孙；占兄弟、占朋友都看兄弟爻；占奴仆也看子孙爻。用爻不上卦或空亡的，多凶少吉；哪一爻空亡、哪一爻受克，吉凶就照这个来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* That a state is in order or disorder is nothing but a matter of customs and discipline; whether a person's life is short or long is not argued from frailty or strength. If one does not nourish life in the proper way, in the end there will be illness; if prayers can be divined, life and death can be seen decided. A killing sitting on Resource: worry for the parents in the hall; Void reaching Wealth: fear calamity for the woman in the inner chamber. The commentary says: in divining illness, first look at who the person is who has come to divine: a son divining about his parents needs the Resource line in the hexagram; a wife divining about her husband looks at the ghost, a husband about his wife at Wealth, a father about his son at Output; in divining about a brother or a friend, look at the Peer line; in divining about a servant, also look at Output. If the Subject line is absent from the hexagram or Void, there is mostly ill and little good; whichever line is Void, whichever is controlled, good and ill are judged by this.

> [!note] 按
> 这是天玄赋「疾病章」的头三段：两句开场议论（治乱靠风俗纪纲、寿夭不看强弱，养生不得法总会生病），两句杀临父母、空及妻财的提醒，再是「占疾病看来者何人」的用爻表。用爻表和易林补遗「疾病用神」（LY.ILL.EL.09）、黄金策「取用神」一带是同一套：子占父母看父母爻、妻占夫看鬼、夫占妻看财、父占子看子孙、兄弟朋友看兄弟、奴仆看子孙。「尫羸」是瘦弱多病。这一章在卜筮大全里赋文和注文排成同样的行，分不出体例，这里按内容分：对偶的句子是赋，「凡……」「若……」起头的解释是注。断易天机和火珠林里同一章把注放在括号里，可以对照。卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* These are the first three passages of the Tianxuan Fu's chapter on illness: two lines of opening argument (order and disorder depend on customs and discipline, long or short life not on strength; if life is not nourished one will fall ill), two lines warning of a killing on Resource and Void reaching Wealth, and then the table 'in divining illness, look at who has come' for the Subject line. The table is the same set as the Yilin Buyi's 'the Subject line in illness' (LY.ILL.EL.09) and the Huangjin Ce's choosing of the Subject: a son divining about his parents looks at the Resource line, a wife about her husband at the ghost, a husband about his wife at Wealth, a father about his son at Output, a brother or friend at Peer, a servant at Output. 'Wang ying' is frail and often ill. In the Buyi Daquan this chapter sets the verse and the commentary in lines of the same kind, so the two cannot be told apart by form, and here they are divided by content: the paired sentences are the verse, and the explanations beginning 'whenever ...' or 'if ...' are the commentary. The Duanyi Tianji and the Huozhulin put the commentary of the same chapter in brackets, and can be compared. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
