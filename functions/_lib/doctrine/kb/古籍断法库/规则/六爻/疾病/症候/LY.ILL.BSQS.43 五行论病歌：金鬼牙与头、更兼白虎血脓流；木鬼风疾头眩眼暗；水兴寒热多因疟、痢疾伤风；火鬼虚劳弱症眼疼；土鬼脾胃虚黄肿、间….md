# 五行论病歌：金鬼牙与头、更兼白虎血脓流；木鬼风疾头眩眼暗；水兴寒热多因疟、痢疾伤风；火鬼虚劳弱症眼疼；土鬼脾胃虚黄肿、间动膈病

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 五行论病歌：金鬼兴隆，病在牙和头，再加白虎，血和脓流；木鬼发动是风病，头晕眼花；水兴是发冷发热，多半是疟疾，痢疾、伤风不容易治；火鬼是虚劳加上虚弱，眼睛疼痛也有来由；土鬼是脾胃虚、黄肿，间爻动要担心膈的病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on illness by the five elements: a Metal ghost thriving, the teeth and the head, with the White Tiger as well, blood and pus flow; a Wood ghost moving, a wind illness, with dizziness and dim eyes; Water rising, chills and fever mostly from malaria, and dysentery and wind-damage are not easily treated; a Fire ghost, consumption and weakness, and eye pain has its cause too; an Earth ghost, weak spleen and stomach and yellow swelling, and if the between lines move, worry about a diaphragm illness.

> [!warning] 打架
> 五行的鬼各主什么病，几家说法不同。这一首是「金鬼牙与头（加白虎血脓）、木鬼风疾头眩眼暗、水寒热疟痢、火鬼虚劳眼痛、土鬼脾胃虚黄肿」；阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.02）是水腰肾、金肺、火心经、木肝、土脾胃；卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.12）是木鬼身体疼痛、水土鬼痈肿、金鬼牙骨痛；同一本书的疾病论（LY.ILL.BSQS.32）是金木动骨骼疼酸、水火兴皮肤寒热、丑未伤脾。土配脾胃、金配牙，几家相近；火配心（阐奥歌章）、配虚劳眼痛（这一首）、配皮肤寒热（疾病论），木配肝（阐奥歌章）、配风疾头眩（这一首）、配身体疼痛（卜筮元龟），各不相同。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books differ on what illness each element's ghost governs. This verse has 'Metal ghost teeth and head (with the White Tiger, blood and pus), Wood ghost wind illness, dizziness and dim eyes, Water chills and fever with malaria and dysentery, Fire ghost consumption and eye pain, Earth ghost weak spleen and stomach with yellow swelling'; the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.02) has Water the waist and kidneys, Metal the lungs, Fire the heart channel, Wood the liver, Earth the spleen and stomach; the Bushi Yuangui passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.12) has a Wood ghost bodily pain, a Water or Earth ghost swollen abscesses, a Metal ghost pain in the teeth and bones; and the chapter on illness in the same book (LY.ILL.BSQS.32) has Metal or Wood moving, aching bones and joints, Water or Fire rising, chills and fever of the skin, Chou or Wei moving, harm to the spleen. Earth matched to the spleen and stomach and Metal to the teeth are close in several; Fire is matched to the heart (Chan'ao Gezhang), to consumption and eye pain (this verse) and to chills and fever of the skin (the chapter on illness), and Wood to the liver (Chan'ao Gezhang), to wind illness and dizziness (this verse) and to bodily pain (Bushi Yuangui), all different. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这是阐幽精要里一首十句的歌，每一句配一种五行的鬼爻和它主的病。「风疾」是风病，头晕、抽搐、中风一类；「虚劳」是体虚而劳损，近于痨病；「膈病」是胸膈的病，噎膈一类；「间动」是间爻（世应之间的爻）发动。书里的病名按字面译，括号里不加现代病名。
>
> *Note:* This is a ten-line verse in the Chanyou Jingyao, each line matching one element's ghost line to the illness it governs. 'Wind illness' is an illness of wind, dizziness, convulsions, strokes and the like; 'consumption' is deficiency and wasting, close to a consumptive illness; 'diaphragm illness' is illness of the chest and diaphragm, of the choking-obstruction kind; 'the between lines move' is the lines between the Self and Other lines moving. The names of illnesses are translated to the letter, without modern disease names added in brackets.
