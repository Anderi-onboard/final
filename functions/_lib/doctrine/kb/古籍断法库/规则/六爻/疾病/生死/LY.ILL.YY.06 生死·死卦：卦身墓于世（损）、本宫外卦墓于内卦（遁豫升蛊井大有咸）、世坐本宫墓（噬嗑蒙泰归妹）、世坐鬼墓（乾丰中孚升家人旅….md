# 生死·死卦：卦身墓于世（损）、本宫外卦墓于内卦（遁豫升蛊井大有咸）、世坐本宫墓（噬嗑蒙泰归妹）、世坐鬼墓（乾丰中孚升家人旅）、世坐财墓（观解随益泰兑）、卦化墓绝、用逢月破者死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 从卦象断死：卦身入墓在世爻的（损卦）；本宫的外卦入墓在内卦的（遁、豫、升、蛊、井、大有、咸）；世爻坐在本宫的墓上的（噬嗑、蒙、泰、归妹）；世爻坐在鬼的墓上的（乾、丰、中孚、升、家人、旅）；世爻坐在财的墓上的（财是禄命，最忌刑、冲、墓、绝；观、解、随、益、泰、兑）；卦变入墓、绝的；用爻遇月破的，都是死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Death judged from the hexagram: the Body's tomb at the Self (the hexagram Sun); the outer trigram of the home palace having its tomb in the inner trigram (Dun, Yu, Sheng, Gu, Jing, Dayou, Xian); the Self sitting on the tomb of the home palace (Shihe, Meng, Tai, Guimei); the Self sitting on the ghost's tomb (Qian, Feng, Zhongfu, Sheng, Jiaren, Lü); the Self sitting on the tomb of Wealth (Wealth is the salary and destiny and fears punishment, clash, tomb and extinction; Guan, Xie, Sui, Yi, Tai, Dui); the hexagram changing into a tomb or Extinction; and the Subject line meeting Month-break: in each case he dies.

## 《易隐·疾病占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2993 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦身墓于世者死也(损卦)。 | 卦身入墓在世爻的，死（损卦）。 | If the Body's tomb lies at the Self, he dies (the hexagram Sun). |
> | 本宫外卦墓于内卦者死(遁豫升蛊井大有咸)。 | 本宫的外卦入墓在内卦的，死（遁、豫、升、蛊、井、大有、咸）。 | If the outer trigram of the home palace has its tomb in the inner trigram, he dies (Dun, Yu, Sheng, Gu, Jing, Dayou, Xian). |
> | 世坐本宫墓者，死也(噬嗑蒙泰归妹)。 | 世爻坐在本宫的墓上的，死（噬嗑、蒙、泰、归妹）。 | If the Self sits on the tomb of the home palace, he dies (Shihe, Meng, Tai, Guimei). |
> | 世坐鬼墓者死也(乾丰中孚升家人旅)。 | 世爻坐在鬼的墓上的，死（乾、丰、中孚、升、家人、旅）。 | If the Self sits on the ghost's tomb, he dies (Qian, Feng, Zhongfu, Sheng, Jiaren, Lü). |
> | 世坐财墓者死也(财为禄命，忌刑冲墓绝，观解随益泰兑卦)。 | 世爻坐在财的墓上的，死（财是禄命，最忌刑、冲、墓、绝；观、解、随、益、泰、兑卦）。 | If the Self sits on the tomb of Wealth, he dies (Wealth is the salary and destiny and fears punishment, clash, tomb and extinction; Guan, Xie, Sui, Yi, Tai, Dui). |
> | 卦化墓绝者死也(乾兑化艮坎，艮坤化巽离，化乾震巽化坤)。 | 卦变入墓、绝的，死（乾、兑变艮、坎；艮、坤变巽、离；变乾；震、巽变坤）。 | If the hexagram changes into a tomb or Extinction, he dies (Qian and Dui changing into Gen and Kan; Gen and Kun changing into Xun and Li; changing into Qian; Zhen and Xun changing into Kun). |
> | 用逢月破者死。 | 用爻遇到月破的，死。 | If the Subject line meets Month-break, he dies. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 括号里的卦名是书里自己注的，指明哪几个卦有这个象。「卦化墓绝」那一句括号里的名单在原文里是「乾兑化艮坎，艮坤化巽离，化乾震巽化坤」：第三个「化乾」没有主语，书里没有说明，译文照写。「旅」是旅卦（Lü），「履」是履卦（Lu），两个卦不要混。「财为禄命」的意思是财爻代表食禄和命，所以怕刑冲墓绝。
>
> *Note:* The hexagram names in brackets are the book's own and show which hexagrams carry the image. In the bracket of the sentence on the hexagram changing into a tomb or extinction, the original list is 'Qian and Dui changing into Gen and Kan, Gen and Kun changing into Xun and Li, changing into Qian, Zhen and Xun changing into Kun': the third 'changing into Qian' has no subject, which the book does not explain, and the translation follows the text. 'Lü' is the hexagram Lü (Travel) and 'Lu' the hexagram Lu (Treading); the two are not to be confused. 'Wealth is salary and destiny' means the Wealth line stands for one's pay and one's life, so it fears punishment, clash, tomb and extinction.
