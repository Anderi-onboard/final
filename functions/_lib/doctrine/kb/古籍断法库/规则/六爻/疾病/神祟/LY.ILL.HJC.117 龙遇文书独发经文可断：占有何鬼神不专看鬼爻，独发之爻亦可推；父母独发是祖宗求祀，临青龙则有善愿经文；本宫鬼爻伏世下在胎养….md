# 龙遇文书独发经文可断：占有何鬼神不专看鬼爻，独发之爻亦可推；父母独发是祖宗求祀，临青龙则有善愿经文；本宫鬼爻伏世下在胎养生旺是新死亡人讨求经忏荐度

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙遇到父母（文书）、一爻单独发动，可以断是经文：占有什么鬼神，不一定只看鬼爻，单独发动的那一爻也可以推。父母爻单独发动，是祖宗要求祭祀，带青龙就有善愿和经文；旧注还说，本宫的鬼爻伏在世爻下、又在胎、养、长生、帝旺的位置上，一定有刚死的人在讨要念经拜忏来超度，伏鬼属水的话棺材还暴露着，落空就不是。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Dragon meeting the document line, with a lone line moving, may be judged to mean scripture: in divining what ghost or spirit is involved, one need not rely only on the Pressure line, since a lone moving line can also be worked out. If Resource alone moves, the ancestors are asking for sacrifice, and with the Azure Dragon there is a good vow and scripture; the old commentary adds that if the Pressure line of the home palace is hidden beneath the Self line and stands at Womb, Nurture, Birth or Peak, a newly dead person is asking for scripture-chanting and rites of repentance to deliver him, and if the hidden ghost is Water the coffin is still exposed, and if it is Void it is not so.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3415 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如父母独发，乃祖宗求祀，临青龙则有善愿经文。 | 如果父母爻单独发动，是祖宗要求祭祀，带青龙就有善愿和经文。 | If Resource alone moves, the ancestors are asking for sacrifice, and with the Azure Dragon there is a good vow and scripture. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文说「龙遇文书」，旧注没有解「文书」，按上下文文书是父母爻。旧注多出两层：一层是「不持鬼爻断，独发之爻亦可推」的总原则，一层是本宫鬼爻伏世下、在胎养生旺的新死亡人，王洪绪只留了父母独发这一句。「荐度」是做法事超度亡灵。
>
> *Note:* The verse says 'the Dragon meeting the document line', and the old commentary does not gloss 'document'; by context it is the Resource line. The old commentary has two layers Wang Hongxu lacks: the general principle 'not relying on the Pressure line, a lone moving line may also be worked out', and the newly dead person when the Pressure line of the home palace is hidden beneath the Self line at Womb, Nurture, Birth or Peak. Wang Hongxu keeps only Resource moving alone. 'Jiandu' is to perform rites to deliver the spirit of the dead.
