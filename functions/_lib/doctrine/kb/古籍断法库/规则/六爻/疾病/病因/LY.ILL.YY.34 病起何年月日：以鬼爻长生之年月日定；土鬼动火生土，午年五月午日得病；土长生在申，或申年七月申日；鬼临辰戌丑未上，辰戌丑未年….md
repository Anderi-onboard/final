# 病起何年月日：以鬼爻长生之年月日定；土鬼动火生土，午年五月午日得病；土长生在申，或申年七月申日；鬼临辰戌丑未上，辰戌丑未年月日得病；金木水火鬼仿此

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病是哪年哪月哪天得的，以鬼爻长生的年、月、日来定：比如土鬼发动、火生土，是午年、五月、午日得的病；土的长生在申，或者是申年、七月、申日得的病；鬼落在辰、戌、丑、未上，或者是辰、戌、丑、未的年、月、日得的病；其他金、木、水、火的鬼，都照这样推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the illness was caught is fixed by the year, month or day in which the Pressure line is born: for example, if an Earth ghost moves and Fire generates Earth, the illness was caught in a Wu year, the fifth month, on a Wu day; Earth is born at Shen, or the illness was caught in a Shen year, the seventh month, on a Shen day; a ghost on Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei, the illness was caught in a year, month or day of Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei; for the Metal, Wood, Water and Fire ghosts, work by the same pattern.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 原文举的例子里，土鬼「火生土」在午年五月午日：午是火的旺位，火生土，所以取午；「土长生在申」是按水土同宫的长生（申）。这一条是「附占」，不是易隐疾病占里的主要事项。
>
> *Note:* In the original's example the Earth ghost 'Fire generates Earth' falls in a Wu year, the fifth month, a Wu day: Wu is Fire's strong place and Fire generates Earth, so Wu is taken; 'Earth is born at Shen' follows the birth (Shen) of Water and Earth together. This entry is a subsidiary matter, not one of the main heads of the Yi Yin's section on illness.
