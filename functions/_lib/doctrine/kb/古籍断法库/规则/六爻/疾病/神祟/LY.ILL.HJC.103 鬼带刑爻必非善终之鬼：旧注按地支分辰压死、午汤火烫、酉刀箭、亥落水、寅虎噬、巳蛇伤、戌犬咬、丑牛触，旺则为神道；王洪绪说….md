# 鬼带刑爻必非善终之鬼：旧注按地支分辰压死、午汤火烫、酉刀箭、亥落水、寅虎噬、巳蛇伤、戌犬咬、丑牛触，旺则为神道；王洪绪说按五行所属推

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼带刑爻，一定是没有善终的鬼。怎么死的：旧注按鬼的地支分，辰是被墙壁压死，午是被热汤烈火烫死烧死，酉是被刀箭所伤，亥是投河落水，寅也许是被老虎咬死，巳是被蛇咬伤，戌是被狗咬，丑是被牛顶；旺相的成了神道，像刘郡王、猛将这样的神。王洪绪说要按鬼所属的五行去推是怎么死的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost carrying a punishing line is surely one who did not die a good death. How he died: the old commentary goes by the ghost's branch — Chen, crushed by a wall; Wu, scalded or burned by hot water or fire; You, wounded by blade or arrow; Hai, threw himself into a river or fell into water; Yin, perhaps devoured by a tiger; Si, bitten by a snake; Xu, bitten by a dog; Chou, gored by an ox — and if strong it becomes a spirit-way, like the god Prince Liu, the Fierce General. Wang Hongxu says to work out the manner of death from the element the ghost belongs to.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3393 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼带刑爻，必非善终之鬼，当以五行所属推其何死鬼； | 鬼带刑爻，一定是没有善终的鬼，要按它所属的五行推是怎么死的鬼； | A ghost carrying a punishing line is surely one who did not die a good death; work out what kind of death from the element it belongs to; |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 怎么推是怎么死的：旧注按鬼爻的地支分（辰压死、午烫死、酉刀箭、亥落水、寅虎、巳蛇、戌犬、丑牛）；王洪绪说「当以五行所属推其何死鬼」，按五行推，不按地支。两种办法给出的名目不完全重合（例如增删卜易 LY.ILL.ZS.23 按五行说金是刀剑亡鬼、木是刑杖悬梁、水是水死、火是汤火焚烧、土是墙倒屋塌，和旧注的辰午酉亥有部分重合）。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to work out how the ghost died: the old commentary goes by the branch of the Pressure line (Chen crushed, Wu scalded, You blade and arrow, Hai water, Yin tiger, Si snake, Xu dog, Chou ox); Wang Hongxu says 'work out the manner of death from the element it belongs to', by element and not by branch. The two methods do not give exactly the same categories (for instance the Zengshan Buyi, LY.ILL.ZS.23, goes by element — Metal a ghost of blade and sword, Wood of the staff or the hanging beam, Water of drowning, Fire of hot water and burning, Earth of a collapsed wall — which overlaps in part with the old commentary's Chen, Wu, You and Hai). The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「刑爻」这里指带刑的爻，书里没有列出哪些是刑。「刘郡王猛将之神」是民间信奉的神，书里没有解释，按字面译作刘郡王、猛将。这一条的经文只有「刑不善终」四字，和下一条「绝则无祀」在王洪绪那里连成一段注。
>
> *Note:* 'A punishing line' means a line carrying punishment (xing); the book does not list which they are. 'Prince Liu, the Fierce General' is a spirit worshipped among the people and is not explained in the book, so is rendered literally. The verse here is only the four characters 'punishment, no good death', and in Wang Hongxu it forms one passage of commentary with the next entry, 'extinction, no one to sacrifice'.
