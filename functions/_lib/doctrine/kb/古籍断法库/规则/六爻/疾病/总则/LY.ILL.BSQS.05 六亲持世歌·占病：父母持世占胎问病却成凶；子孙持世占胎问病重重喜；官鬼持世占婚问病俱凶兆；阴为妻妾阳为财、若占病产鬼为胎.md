# 六亲持世歌·占病：父母持世占胎问病却成凶；子孙持世占胎问病重重喜；官鬼持世占婚问病俱凶兆；阴为妻妾阳为财、若占病产鬼为胎

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六亲持世歌里讲病的几句：父母爻持世，占胎产、问疾病反而凶；子孙爻持世，占胎产、问疾病重重有喜；官鬼爻持世，占婚姻、问疾病都是凶兆；财爻持世，若占病、占产，官鬼爻就是胎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The lines on illness in the verse on the six relations holding the Self line: with Resource holding the Self line, divining a pregnancy or asking about illness is ominous; with Output holding the Self line, divining a pregnancy or asking about illness brings joy upon joy; with Pressure holding the Self line, divining marriage or asking about illness are both ill omens; with Wealth holding the Self line, if divining illness or birth, Pressure is the womb.

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 这首歌分五段（父母、子孙、官鬼、财、兄弟），和病有关的是前四段，兄弟一段不收。「占胎问病」是两件事并提：占怀孕和占病。「遏贵」原文如此，照上下文应是「谒贵」（拜见贵人），译文按「求见贵人」写。「若占病产鬼为胎」只有这一句，歌里没有讲官鬼是胎对占病意味着什么，译文照字面。后两段婚姻域收过（LY.MARRY.BSQS.09）。同一首歌在火珠林（第 948 行）和周易尚占（第 475 至 478 行）里也有，字句略有不同：周易尚占第一段作「占身问病却成凶」（占身，不是占胎），第二段作「占胎问病重重吉」「谒贵求官反不亨」（「谒」字不误，可证「遏」是误字），第三段作「占婚问病俱凶犯」。
>
> *Note:* The poem has five stanzas (Resource, Output, Pressure, Wealth, Peer); the first four bear on illness and the stanza on Peer is not taken. 'Divining a pregnancy or asking about illness' puts two matters together: a divination about conception and a divination about illness. The text reads 'to check the noble' where, from the context, 'to call on the noble' is expected; the translation gives 'seeking an audience with a noble'. The line 'if divining illness or birth, Pressure is the womb' stands alone, and the poem does not say what Pressure being the womb means for an illness; it is translated literally. The last two stanzas were taken in the marriage domain (LY.MARRY.BSQS.09). The same poem is also in the Huozhu Lin (line 948) and the Zhouyi Shangzhan (lines 475 to 478), with small differences: the Zhouyi Shangzhan has the first stanza as 'divining about one's person or asking about illness is ominous' (person, not pregnancy), the second as 'divining pregnancy or asking about illness: joy upon joy' and 'calling on a noble and seeking office goes badly' (with the correct character for 'call on', which bears out that the Bushi Daquan's 'check' is a mistake), and the third as 'divining marriage or asking about illness are both ominous and offending'.
