# 丧车杀：丧车春鸡夏鼠秋马冬羊、人来占病断兴去、讨钱火急买棺材

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丧车：春天是鸡、夏天是鼠，秋天是马、冬天是羊，好安排。有人来占病，断他是起身离去（去世）了，赶紧去讨钱、火速买棺材。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Mourning Cart: in spring it is the rooster, in summer the rat, in autumn the horse and in winter the sheep, which is well arranged. A person who comes to divine illness is judged to be setting off to leave; hurry to ask for money and buy a coffin in all haste.

> [!note] 按
> 丧车杀的四季排法是春酉（鸡）、夏子（鼠）、秋午（马）、冬未（羊）。「断兴去」的「兴去」读不确定，「兴」有起身的意思，这里按「起身离去」译，也就是去世；「讨钱火急买棺材」是说要赶紧去讨钱备棺材，是个凶极的断语。「好安排」按字面译，是说四季的排法好排。丧车这个名目也见天玄赋（「丧车」「丧服」「棺椁」，LY.ILL.HZL.03 的申为丧车）。
>
> *Note:* The four-season layout of the Mourning Cart killing is You (the rooster) in spring, Zi (the rat) in summer, Wu (the horse) in autumn and Wei (the sheep) in winter. The 'xing qu' of 'divined to be xing qu' is not certain; 'xing' has the sense of setting off, and it is rendered 'setting off to leave', that is, dying; 'hurry to ask for money and buy a coffin' says one must hurry to ask for money and prepare a coffin, an extremely ominous judgment. 'Well arranged' is rendered literally and says the layout by seasons is easy to arrange. The name Mourning Cart also appears in the Huozhu Lin (Shen as the mourning cart, LY.ILL.HZL.03).
