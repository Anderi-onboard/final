# 孙膑断疾病歌(六)：卦变墓乡须丧命、不然亦主困蒙蒙；乾坎震巽为生魂在外不死内难存、离坤艮兑人为鬼在内定生在外死；勾陈爻动即开坟、乾兑须臾为孝子

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦变到墓乡，必定丧命，不然也主昏沉困顿。乾、坎、震、巽是生魂，在外不死，在内难活；离、坤、艮、兑是人变成鬼，在内一定活，在外就死。勾陈爻动，就要开坟了；乾、兑，片刻之间就有人做孝子。孙膑这个诀很难领会，只在于善于推算。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram changes into the tomb quarter life will surely be lost, and otherwise it also means a dull, hazy exhaustion. Qian, Kan, Zhen and Xun are the living soul: outside he does not die, inside he can hardly survive; Li, Kun, Gen and Dui are the person becoming a ghost: inside he surely lives, outside he dies. When the Hook Earth line moves the grave is opened; Qian and Dui: in a moment someone is a filial mourner. Sun Bin's secret here is hard to know and lies only in being good at working it out.

> [!note] 按
> 「生魂」「鬼」是这首歌自己对八个卦的分类：乾、坎、震、巽为一类，离、坤、艮、兑为另一类；「在外」「在内」指什么，书里没有说，可能指这个卦是落在外卦还是内卦，也可能指鬼所在的位置，这里按字面译，不作选择。这套分法别的书里没有，这里只有这一首。「乾兑须臾为孝子」的「孝子」是披麻戴孝的人，也就是有人死了。「须臾」是片刻。「困蒙蒙」按「昏沉困顿」译。这一首的尾两句是收束，说孙膑的诀难懂，要靠善于推算。
>
> *Note:* 'Living soul' and 'ghost' are this song's own sorting of the eight trigrams: Qian, Kan, Zhen and Xun in one class, Li, Kun, Gen and Dui in the other. The book does not say what 'outside' and 'inside' refer to; it may be whether the trigram falls in the outer or the inner trigram of the hexagram, or the position of the ghost, and it is rendered literally here without choosing. This way of sorting is not found in the other books, only in this song. The 'filial mourner' of 'Qian and Dui in a moment a filial mourner' is a person in mourning dress, that is, someone has died. 'Xu yu' is a moment. 'Kun meng meng' is rendered 'a dull, hazy exhaustion'. The last two lines close the song, saying that Sun Bin's secret is hard to know and depends on being good at working it out.
