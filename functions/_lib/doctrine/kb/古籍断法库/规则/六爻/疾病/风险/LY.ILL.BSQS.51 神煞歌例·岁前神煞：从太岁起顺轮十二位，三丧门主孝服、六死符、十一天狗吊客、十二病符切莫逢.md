# 神煞歌例·岁前神煞：从太岁起顺轮十二位，三丧门主孝服、六死符、十一天狗吊客、十二病符切莫逢

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 岁前神煞：十二个神煞都从太岁起，顺着一位一位轮下去：太岁、剑锋、伏尸同位，第二位太阳、天空，第三位丧门主孝服，第四位勾绞、贯索，第五位官符、五鬼，第六位死符、小耗，第七位栏干、大耗，第八位暴败、天厄，第九位飞廉、白虎，第十位福德、卷舌，第十一位天狗、吊客，第十二位病符，千万不要遇上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The stars before the Year: all twelve start from Tai Sui and go round forward place by place: Tai Sui, Sword Edge and Hidden Corpse in one place; second Great Yang and Heavenly Emptiness; third the Mourning Gate, meaning mourning dress; fourth Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through; fifth Official Tally and the Five Ghosts; sixth Death Tally and Small Loss; seventh Railing and Great Loss; eighth Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress; ninth Feilian and the White Tiger; tenth Fortune Virtue and the Curled Tongue; eleventh the Heavenly Dog and the Guest of Condolence; twelfth the Sickness Tally, which one must not meet.

> [!warning] 打架
> 十二位岁煞各家的名单不一样。卜筮全书的岁前神煞是：一太岁剑锋伏尸、二太阳天空、三丧门、四勾绞贯索、五官符五鬼、六死符小耗、七栏干大耗、八暴败天厄、九飞廉白虎、十福德卷舌、十一天狗吊客、十二病符；断易天机的「四利三元」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.50）是：一太岁、二太阳、三丧门、四太阴、五官符、六死符、七岁破、八龙德、九白虎、十福德、十一吊客、十二病符。第一、二、三、五、六、九、十、十一、十二位的主名两本一致；第四位（勾绞贯索对太阴）、第七位（栏干大耗对岁破）、第八位（暴败天厄对龙德）不同，而且卜筮全书每一位后面还并带了一两颗别的星。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books' lists of the twelve year-stars differ. The stars before the Year in the Bushi Quanshu are: first Tai Sui with Sword Edge and Hidden Corpse, second Great Yang with Heavenly Emptiness, third Mourning Gate, fourth Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through, fifth Official Tally with the Five Ghosts, sixth Death Tally with Small Loss, seventh Railing with Great Loss, eighth Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress, ninth Feilian with the White Tiger, tenth Fortune Virtue with the Curled Tongue, eleventh the Heavenly Dog with the Guest of Condolence, twelfth Sickness Tally; the Duanyi Tianji's 'Four Gains and Three Origins' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.50) runs: first Tai Sui, second Great Yang, third Mourning Gate, fourth Great Yin, fifth Official Tally, sixth Death Tally, seventh Year-break, eighth Dragon Virtue, ninth White Tiger, tenth Fortune Virtue, eleventh Guest of Condolence, twelfth Sickness Tally. The principal names agree in the first, second, third, fifth, sixth, ninth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth places; the fourth (Hook-Strangle and Cord-Through against Great Yin), the seventh (Railing and Great Loss against Year-break) and the eighth (Sudden Ruin and Heavenly Distress against Dragon Virtue) differ, and the Bushi Quanshu joins one or two further stars to each place. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这十二位是流年神煞：子年时太岁、剑锋、伏尸在子，太阳、天空在丑，丧门在寅，依次往下，病符在亥。占病要看的是名字里带丧和病的几位：丧门（主孝服）、死符、吊客、病符（「切莫逢」），歌里只对这几位给了明白的话，其余几位只点名。原文「褔德」的褔是福的异写，这里的福德是一颗神煞，不是子孙爻。这一组里其余的神煞（天哭、四季小杀等）和本条的区别是：它们按月取，这一条按年取。
>
> *Note:* These twelve are year-stars: in a Zi year Tai Sui, Sword Edge and Hidden Corpse are at Zi, Great Yang and Heavenly Emptiness at Chou, the Mourning Gate at Yin, and so on, with the Sickness Tally at Hai. In divining illness the ones to look at are those with mourning and illness in their names: the Mourning Gate (mourning dress), the Death Tally, the Guest of Condolence and the Sickness Tally ('not to be met'); the verse speaks plainly only of these, and the rest are merely named. The 'Fortune' of 'Fortune Virtue' is written in a variant character in the original, and the Fortune Virtue here is a spirit-star, not the Output line. This entry differs from the other spirit-stars in this group (the Weeping, the small killing of the four seasons and so on) in being placed by the year, while those are placed by the month.
