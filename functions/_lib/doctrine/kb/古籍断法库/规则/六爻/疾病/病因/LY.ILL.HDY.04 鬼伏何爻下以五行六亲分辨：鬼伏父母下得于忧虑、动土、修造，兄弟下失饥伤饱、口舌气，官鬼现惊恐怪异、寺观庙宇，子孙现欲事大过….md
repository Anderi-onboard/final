# 鬼伏何爻下以五行六亲分辨：鬼伏父母下得于忧虑、动土、修造，兄弟下失饥伤饱、口舌气，官鬼现惊恐怪异、寺观庙宇，子孙现欲事大过，妻财饮食、买物；土伏土疮肿、火下火手足、金见金大寒热、水下水冷疾、金下金喘咳；阳宫世动主吐、阴宫财动主泻；鬼现在外表、鬼伏在心腹

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼伏在哪一爻下面，要用五行来分辨。父母爻：因为忧虑得的，或是动了土，或是到修造的地方得的。兄弟爻：因为饥饱失常，或者因口舌气恼得的。官鬼出现：惊恐怪异，或者是在寺观庙宇里得的。子孙出现：或者因为欲事太过。妻财：饮食上得的，或者买东西得的。五行伏着五行：土伏土，生疮肿胀；火下火，手足的病；金遇金，大寒大热（中间「问乱未下」原文含混）；水下水，冷病；金下金，气喘咳嗽。阳宫里世爻发动主吐，阴宫里财爻发动主泻。鬼出现，病在外表；鬼伏着，病在心腹；病在里面，或是动的爻在下面受病，用爻也一样；鬼在外面，或是动土变成病，用爻也一样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where the ghost hides, under which line, discriminate by the five phases. The Resource line: got from worry, or from moving earth, or got at a place of repair and building. The Peer line: from irregular hunger and overeating, or from quarrels and anger. The Pressure line appearing: fright and strangeness, or got in a temple, abbey or shrine. Output appearing: or from excess in matters of desire. Wealth: got through food and drink, or through buying things. Phase under phase: Earth hidden under Earth, sores and swelling; Fire under Fire, the hands and feet; Metal meeting Metal, great chills and fever (the words 'wen luan wei xia' in the middle are unclear); Water under Water, a cold illness; Metal under Metal, wheezing and coughing. In a yang palace the Self moving governs vomiting, in a yin palace Wealth moving governs diarrhoea. The ghost showing: the illness is on the outside surface; the ghost hidden: the illness is in the heart and belly; the illness inside, or the moving line below where the illness is received, the Subject line likewise; the ghost outside, or a moved earth turning into illness, the Subject line likewise.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节没有歌诀，是一段直白的条文。它和易隐「病因·六亲」（见 LY.ILL.YY.17 一带）、易林补遗「按六神论病由何得」（LY.ILL.EL.05）一样，是拿病的来由配给六亲：父母是忧虑、动土、修造，兄弟是饮食失节和口舌，官鬼是惊怪、寺观，子孙是欲事，妻财是饮食买物。「上下伏土，疮肿」的「上下」疑是「土下」之误（下面是「火下火」「水下水」「金下金」），按字面译作「Earth hidden above and below」；「金见金，问乱未下，大寒热」的「问乱未下」不成句，这里照抄标出；「金下金」出现了两次（「金见金」和「金下金」），原文没有解释。「阳宫世动主吐，阴宫财动主泻」和黄金策的「财在外卦动主吐、内卦动主泻」（LY.ILL.HJC.80）大体相反的讲法，这里不作比较，只如实译出。「病在内，或动下受病，用又同」几句文字很简，按字面译，不展开。
>
> *Note:* This section has no verse and is a plain string of clauses. Like the Yi Yin's 'cause of illness by the six relations' (see LY.ILL.YY.17 and around it) and the Yilin Buyi's 'how the illness was contracted by the six spirits' (LY.ILL.EL.05), it matches the origin of an illness to the six relations: Resource is worry, moving earth and repairs, Peer is irregular eating and quarrels, Pressure is fright and strangeness and temples, Output is matters of desire, and Wealth is food and buying. The 'above and below' in 'above and below hidden Earth, sores and swelling' is probably a slip for 'Earth under' (since what follows is 'Fire under Fire', 'Water under Water' and 'Metal under Metal'), and is rendered literally as 'Earth hidden above and below'; the 'wen luan wei xia' in 'Metal meeting Metal, wen luan wei xia, great chills and fever' is not a sentence and is copied and marked; 'Metal under Metal' appears twice ('Metal meeting Metal' and 'Metal under Metal') and the original does not explain. 'In a yang palace the Self moving governs vomiting, in a yin palace Wealth moving governs diarrhoea' is set out truthfully, and is not compared here with the Huangjin Ce's 'Wealth in the outer trigram moving governs vomiting, in the inner trigram diarrhoea' (LY.ILL.HJC.80), which puts it differently. The few clauses 'the illness inside, or the moving line below receives the illness, the Subject likewise' are very terse and are rendered literally and not expanded.
