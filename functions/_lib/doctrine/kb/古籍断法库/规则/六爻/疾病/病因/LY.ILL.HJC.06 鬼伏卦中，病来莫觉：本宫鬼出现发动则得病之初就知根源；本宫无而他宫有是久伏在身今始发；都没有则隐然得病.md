# 鬼伏卦中，病来莫觉：本宫鬼出现发动则得病之初就知根源；本宫无而他宫有是久伏在身今始发；都没有则隐然得病

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病时，本宫的官鬼出现并且发动，得病之初就知道根由；本宫的官鬼不出现、而别的宫有官鬼出现，是病早就伏在身上，这一回因七情四气感冒才发出来；本宫和别的宫都没有官鬼，是不知不觉得的病，起初谁也说不出为什么。旧注又附了两组按卦名的忌讳：明夷、观、贲、需、临最忌世爻和身爻入墓；大畜、丰、同人、蛊、夬不要遇上财爻鬼爻一起兴旺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness: if the Pressure of the home palace appears and moves, the source is known as soon as the illness begins; if the home palace's Pressure does not appear but another palace's Pressure does, the illness has long lain hidden in the body and now breaks out through the seven emotions and the four airs; if neither the home palace nor another palace has Pressure, the illness was contracted unseen and at first no one can say why. The old commentary adds two groups of taboos by hexagram name: for Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin, beware above all of Self and Body entering the tomb; for Daxu, Feng, Tongren, Gu and Guai, do not meet Wealth and Pressure both stirring.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2309 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 遇官鬼不出现必隐藏，得症不知何由； | 遇到官鬼不出现，一定是隐藏着，得了病不知道是什么缘故； | If Pressure does not appear it is surely hidden, and the illness comes with no known reason; |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注最后两句按卦名给忌讳，没有说明理由，王洪绪没有收。「七情」是喜怒忧思悲恐惊，「四气」指寒热温凉（或风寒暑湿），书里没有说是哪一种，译文保留原词。
>
> *Note:* The last two sentences of the old commentary give taboos by hexagram name without reasons, and Wang Hongxu does not take them. The 'seven emotions' are joy, anger, worry, longing, grief, fear and fright, and the 'four airs' are cold, hot, warm and cool (or wind, cold, heat and damp); the book does not say which, and the translation keeps the original words.
