# 占医药·金鬼不宜针、木鬼不宜草木、水鬼不宜汤饮汤洗、火鬼不宜灸熨、土鬼不宜服丸药；金鬼可炙木鬼药方水鬼宜热剂土鬼宜木药；金鬼利南方木鬼利西方水鬼利土值火鬼利北方土鬼利东方求请医者；丑鬼不可食牛肉未子孙当食羊

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金鬼不宜针，木鬼不宜草木，水鬼不宜汤饮汤洗之类，火鬼不宜灸熨，土鬼不宜服丸药；金鬼可以炙，木鬼用药方，火鬼带服鬼剂，水鬼宜服热剂，土鬼宜服木药。金鬼利南方，木鬼利西方，水鬼利土值，火鬼利北方，土鬼利东方，去求请医生。又：丑鬼不可食牛肉，未是子孙就该吃羊肉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Metal ghost must not be needled, a Wood ghost must not have grasses and woods, a Water ghost must not have hot drinks or hot washing and the like, a Fire ghost must not have moxibustion or hot compresses, an Earth ghost must not take pills; a Metal ghost can be burned with moxa, a Wood ghost takes a prescription, a Fire ghost takes ghost medicine along, a Water ghost should take a hot remedy, an Earth ghost should take Wood medicine. A Metal ghost is favoured by the south, a Wood ghost by the west, a Water ghost by the earth quarter, a Fire ghost by the north and an Earth ghost by the east, in seeking and inviting a doctor. Again: a Chou ghost must not eat beef, and where Wei is Output one should eat mutton.

> [!warning] 打架
> 治法和请医的方位，火珠林和天玄赋大体一致，有两处不同。一、土鬼：天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.13）是「土鬼宜锉散」，火珠林这里是「土鬼宜服木药」「土鬼不宜服丸药」；二、水鬼请医的方位：天玄赋是「水鬼利四散医人」，火珠林这里是「水鬼利土值」。增删卜易（LY.ILL.ZS.34）另有一说，请医的方位不看鬼的五行，看子孙和应爻的方位（见 TXF.13 的「打架」）。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The treatment and the direction of the doctor are broadly agreed between the Huozhu Lin and the Tianxuan Fu, with two differences. One, the Earth ghost: the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.13) has 'an Earth ghost is fit for cut powders', and the Huozhu Lin here has 'an Earth ghost should take Wood medicine' and 'an Earth ghost must not take pills'; two, the direction for a doctor for a Water ghost: the Tianxuan Fu has 'a Water ghost is favoured by a doctor from all quarters' and the Huozhu Lin here has 'a Water ghost by the earth quarter'. The Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.34) has yet another view, that the direction of the doctor is looked at not by the phase of the ghost but by the quarter of Output and the Other line (see the dispute in TXF.13). The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段和天玄赋的医药一节（LY.ILL.TXF.13）相近：天玄赋是「火鬼宜凉剂，水鬼宜温剂或丸药，木鬼宜针，金鬼宜灸，土鬼宜锉散……金鬼利南方医人，木鬼利西方医人，水鬼利四散医人，火鬼利北方医人，土鬼利东方医人」。两处大部分一致（金宜灸不宜针、水宜热剂、请医的方位是克这一行的方位），不一致的是土鬼：天玄赋说宜锉散，这里说宜木药、不宜丸药；水鬼的方位，天玄赋是「四散」，这里是「土值」（字面译作 the earth quarter，「值」的意思原文没有解释，可能是「值土」）。「金鬼可炙，木鬼药方，火鬼带服鬼剂」中「带服鬼剂」的「鬼剂」不清楚，字面译作 ghost medicine。「丑鬼不可（食）牛肉」的「（食）」是原文里的圆括号。忌食鬼爻所属生肖的肉、宜食子孙所属生肖的肉，和洞林秘诀（LY.ILL.DYTJ.16）是同一个说法。
>
> *Note:* This passage is close to the Tianxuan Fu's section on medicine (LY.ILL.TXF.13): the Tianxuan Fu has 'a Fire ghost is fit for a cooling remedy, a Water ghost for a warm remedy or pills, a Wood ghost for the needle, a Metal ghost for moxa, an Earth ghost for cut powders ... a Metal ghost is favoured by a doctor from the south, a Wood ghost by one from the west, a Water ghost by one from all quarters, a Fire ghost by one from the north, an Earth ghost by one from the east'. They mostly agree (moxa for Metal and no needle, a hot remedy for Water, and the direction of the doctor being the direction that controls the phase); where they differ is the Earth ghost: the Tianxuan Fu says cut powders, this says Wood medicine and not pills; and for the Water ghost's direction the Tianxuan Fu has 'all quarters' and this has 'the earth quarter' (rendered literally, the original does not explain 'zhi' (rule, hold), which may be 'ruled by Earth'). The 'ghost medicine' of 'a Fire ghost takes ghost medicine along' is not clear and is rendered literally. The '(shi)' of 'a Chou ghost must not (eat) beef' is a round bracket in the original. Avoiding the meat of the animal of the ghost line and eating the meat of the animal of Output is the same saying as the Dongling Mijue (LY.ILL.DYTJ.16).
