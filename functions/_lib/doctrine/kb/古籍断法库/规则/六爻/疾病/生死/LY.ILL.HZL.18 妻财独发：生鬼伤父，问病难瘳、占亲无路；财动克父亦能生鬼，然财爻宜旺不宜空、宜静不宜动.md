# 妻财独发：生鬼伤父，问病难瘳、占亲无路；财动克父亦能生鬼，然财爻宜旺不宜空、宜静不宜动

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财独发，生鬼、伤父；问病，难以痊愈，占亲人，没有出路。大抵财动克父，也能生鬼；但财爻要旺、不要空，要静、不要动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth moving alone generates the ghost and injures the father; asking about illness, it is hard to be cured, and divining a relative, there is no way out. Broadly, Wealth moving controls the father and can also generate the ghost; but the Wealth line should be strong and not Void, still and not moving.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节的标题是「妻财独发」，头两句「妻财独发，生鬼伤父」说的是财爻一个人动：动了就克父母（伤父）、又生官鬼（鬼），所以问病难愈、占亲人无路。「大抵财动克父，亦能生鬼」是给这个道理的解释；「然财爻宜旺，不宜空；宜静、不宜动」一句是说财爻本身宜旺相、宜静，不是专门讲病的，这里连着收下来。下文讲占脱货、占婚姻、占讼的几句（惟占脱货要财爻发动……）不在病的范围，不收。「父」在这里是父母爻（Resource），「占亲」是占亲人。这和天玄赋「妻财独发」的说法，以及易冒等处的「财动克父」（LY.ILL.YM.42 一带）是同一类。
>
> *Note:* The heading of this section is 'Wealth moving alone', and its first two lines 'Wealth moving alone generates the ghost and injures the father' say that when the Wealth line alone moves it controls Resource (injuring the father) and also generates the Pressure line (the ghost), and so asking about illness it is hard to cure and divining a relative there is no way out. 'Broadly, Wealth moving controls the father and can also generate the ghost' is the explanation given for this; 'but the Wealth line should be strong and not Void, still and not moving' says the Wealth line itself should be strong and still, and is not about illness in particular, and is taken along here as it runs on. The following lines about divining the disposal of goods, marriage and lawsuits ('only in divining the disposal of goods must the Wealth line move ...') are outside illness and are not taken. The 'father' here is the Resource line, and 'divining a relative' is divining a relative. This is of the same kind as the Tianxuan Fu's saying on Wealth moving alone, and the 'Wealth moving controls the father' in the Yi Mao and elsewhere (around LY.ILL.YM.42).
