# 既疾必筮其吉凶而调剂之：慎于未病是君子之道；命随鬼墓是不治之疾（例：卦中子孙旺相病不死，亥命随官入墓，半年内溺水亡）

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生死有命，不是祈祷能改的；疾病在于人，不可以忽视；没生病之前就谨慎，是君子的做法。已经生了病，能不占问它的吉凶而加以调理吗？本命跟着鬼入了墓，是治不好的病。比如戌月甲辰日，占儿子的病，他生于己亥年，得同人卦，卦中子孙旺相、六爻安静，病应当不会死，但是亥命随官入了墓，按法大凶；后来他的病没有死，却不到半年溺水而死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Life and death have their appointed lot and cannot be prayed away; illness lies with the person and must not be neglected; to take care before falling ill is the way of the gentleman. Once ill, can one fail to divine its fortune and adjust the treatment? If the natal branch follows the ghost into a tomb, it is an untreatable illness. For example, in the Xu month on the Jiachen day, someone divined a son's illness; he was born in a Jihai year and the hexagram was Tongren, with Output strong and the six lines still, so the illness should not kill; but the Hai natal branch followed Pressure into a tomb, which by the method is a great ill omen; afterwards he did not die of the illness, but within half a year he drowned.

> [!note] 按
> 「命随鬼墓」的「命」是本命（出生年的地支），「随鬼入墓」是本命的地支跟着官鬼入墓。这一条例子里病人没有死于病却死于水，说明这个象断的是「命」不是「病」——和黄金策讲的「命入幽明两墓」（LY.ILL.HJC.54）、「世上临鬼不可随官入墓」（LY.ILL.HJC.41）、易隐的「用爻随鬼入墓者死」（LY.ILL.YY.05）是同一个象。
>
> *Note:* The 'destiny' of 'the destiny following the ghost into a tomb' is the natal branch (the branch of the birth year), and 'following the ghost into a tomb' is the natal branch entering a tomb along with the Pressure line. In the example the patient did not die of the illness but of water, which shows that this image judges the 'destiny' and not the 'illness' — it is the same image as the Huangjin Ce's 'the destiny entering the bright and dark tombs' (LY.ILL.HJC.54) and 'the Self holding Pressure must not follow Pressure into a tomb' (LY.ILL.HJC.41) and the Yi Yin's 'if the Subject line follows the ghost into a tomb, he dies' (LY.ILL.YY.05).
