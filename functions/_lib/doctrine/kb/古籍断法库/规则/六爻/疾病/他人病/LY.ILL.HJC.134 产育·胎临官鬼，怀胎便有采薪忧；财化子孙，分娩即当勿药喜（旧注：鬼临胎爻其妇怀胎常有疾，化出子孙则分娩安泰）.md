# 产育·胎临官鬼，怀胎便有采薪忧；财化子孙，分娩即当勿药喜（旧注：鬼临胎爻其妇怀胎常有疾，化出子孙则分娩安泰）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》产育章说：「胎爻临着官鬼，怀孕时就有生病的忧虑；财化成子孙，分娩时就是不用吃药的喜事。」旧注：鬼临在胎爻上，这个妇人怀孕期间常常有病；胎爻被官鬼冲克也一样；如果胎爻虽然临着官鬼，但化出子孙，或者财化成福爻，怀孕时虽然多病，分娩就安泰。王洪绪：鬼临胎爻，主孕妇有病，或者财合福爻，分娩就安泰。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on childbirth says: 'With the Womb line on Pressure, there is the worry of illness from the moment of pregnancy; Wealth turning into Output, at delivery there is the joy of needing no medicine.' The old commentary: a ghost on the Womb line means the woman is often ill during pregnancy, and so too if the Womb line is clashed or controlled by Pressure; if the Womb line, though on Pressure, turns into Output, or Wealth turns into a fortune line, she is often ill during pregnancy but delivery is safe and sound. Wang Hongxu: a ghost on the Womb line means the pregnant woman is ill, or if Wealth combines with the fortune line, delivery is safe and sound.

> [!note] 按
> 「采薪忧」（采薪之忧）是生病的婉辞，古人说「负薪之忧」「采薪之忧」，意思是病得不能去砍柴。「勿药喜」是病好了不用吃药的喜，出自《周易·无妄》「无妄之疾，勿药有喜」。这一联是占产育的：官鬼临胎爻是孕妇孕中多病，化出子孙则分娩平安；放在「他人病」里。
>
> *Note:* The 'worry of gathering firewood' is a polite word for being ill: the ancients said 'the worry of carrying firewood' or 'the worry of gathering firewood', meaning too ill to go and cut wood. 'The joy of needing no medicine' is the joy of recovering without medicine, from the Yijing's Wuwang, 'illness without cause: no medicine and there is joy'. This couplet is about childbirth: Pressure on the Womb line means the woman is often ill during pregnancy, and if it turns into Output, delivery is safe; it is placed under the illness of others.
