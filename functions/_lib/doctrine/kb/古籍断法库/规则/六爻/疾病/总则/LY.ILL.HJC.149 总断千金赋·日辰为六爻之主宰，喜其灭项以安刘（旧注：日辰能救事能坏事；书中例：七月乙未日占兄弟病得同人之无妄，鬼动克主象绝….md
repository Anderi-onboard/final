# 总断千金赋·日辰为六爻之主宰，喜其灭项以安刘（旧注：日辰能救事能坏事；书中例：七月乙未日占兄弟病得同人之无妄，鬼动克主象绝爻又动，幸日辰未土克坏官爻合起主象，后果无事）

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「日辰是六爻的主宰，喜的是它能灭项以安刘。」旧注：日辰是卜筮的主宰，不看日辰就不知道轻重；日辰能冲动安静的爻、合起安静的爻，发动的它能制住，凶恶的能压抑，强旺的能挫折，衰弱的能扶持；所以日辰能救事、也能坏事，是六爻的主宰。书中例：七月乙未日占兄弟的病，得同人变无妄，卦中鬼爻动克主象、绝爻又动，凶是可以想见的；幸好日辰是未土，克坏了官爻、合起了主象，后来果然无事。王洪绪的说法一样，并说：如果忌神旺动、用神休囚，得日辰克制忌神、生扶用神，凡事转凶为吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'The Day is the master of the six lines, and one welcomes how it destroys Xiang to secure Liu.' The old commentary: the Day is the master of divination, and without looking at the Day one does not know what is light and what is heavy; the Day can clash a still line into motion and combine and raise it, can restrain what moves, suppress what is ominous, break down what is strong and support what is weak; so the Day can save an affair and can spoil an affair, and is the master of the six lines. A case in the book: on a Yiwei day in the seventh month a brother's illness was divined and Tongren changing to Wuwang obtained; in the hexagram the Pressure line moved and controlled the principal image and the Extinction line moved too, so the ill omen could be imagined; fortunately the Day was Wei Earth, which broke the Pressure line and combined the principal image into life, and in the end all was well. Wang Hongxu says the same, and adds: if the Adversary is strong and moving and the Subject is resting or trapped, and the Day controls the Adversary and supports the Subject, in everything ill turns to good.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1155 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日辰乃卜筮之主，不看日辰则不知卦中吉凶轻重了。 | 日辰是卜筮的主宰，不看日辰就不知道卦中吉凶轻重了。 | The Day is the master of divination; without looking at the Day one does not know the good and ill and the light and heavy in the hexagram. |
> | 盖日辰能冲起、冲实、冲散那动、空、静、旺的爻象，能合能填月破之爻，衰弱的能扶助帮比，强旺的能抑挫制伏，发动的能去克得，伏藏的能去提拔，可以成得事，可以坏得事，故为六爻之主宰也。 | 因为日辰能冲起、冲实、冲散那些动的、空的、静的、旺的爻象，能合住、能填实月破的爻，衰弱的它能扶助帮衬，强旺的它能抑制挫败，发动的它能去克，伏藏的它能去提拔，可以成事，也可以坏事，所以是六爻的主宰。 | For the Day can clash into motion, clash into fullness and clash apart the moving, Void, still and strong lines and images, can combine and fill a line broken by the Month-break, can support and help the weak, can restrain and defeat the strong, can control what moves and can raise what is hidden, and can bring an affair to success or spoil it, and therefore it is the master of the six lines. |
> | 如忌神旺动，用神休囚，倘得日辰去克制那忌神，生扶了用神，凡事转凶为吉，故曰：“灭项俱值”。 | 如果忌神旺动，用神休囚，倘若得到日辰去克制那个忌神、生扶用神，凡事转凶为吉，所以说：「灭项俱值」。 | If the Adversary is strong and moving and the Subject is resting or trapped, and the Day happens to control that Adversary and support the Subject, in everything ill turns to good, and so it is said: 'destroy Xiang, both worthy'. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「灭项以安刘」是借汉高祖刘邦灭掉项羽、天下才安定的典故打比方：日辰除掉了害用神的，用神才安稳。王洪绪引作「灭项俱值」，字面不通，照原文译。这一联在总断千金赋里讲的是日辰对整个卦的权力，占病只是旧注举的一个例子（占兄弟的病），所以放在「总则」。例子里的「兄弟」是真的兄弟（不是兄弟爻）；「主象」就是所占的那一爻（兄弟占病，用神是兄弟爻）；「绝爻」书里没有解释，按字面译。书中例是旧注自己举的，结果只写「后果应无事」。旧注讲日辰的前一联「最恶者岁君」「月建乃万卜之提纲」（LY.ILL.HJC.147 是月建那一条）是同一类。
>
> *Note:* 'Destroy Xiang to secure Liu' is an allusion to Liu Bang, founder of the Han, destroying Xiang Yu and so settling the realm: the Day removes what harms the Subject, and only then is the Subject safe. Wang Hongxu quotes it as 'destroy Xiang, both worthy', which does not read literally and is translated as it stands. This couplet in the Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements is about the Day's power over the whole hexagram, and divining illness is only an example the old commentary gives (a brother's illness), which is why it is placed under 'general rules'. The 'brother' of the example is a real brother (not the Peer line); the 'principal image' is the line being divined (for a brother's illness the Subject is the brother line); the 'Extinction line' is not explained in the book and is translated literally. The case is the old commentary's own and the result is only given as 'in the end all was well'. The couplet before this on the Day in the same rhapsody, 'the Month is the guiding thread of every divination' (LY.ILL.HJC.147 is that one on the Month), is of the same kind.
