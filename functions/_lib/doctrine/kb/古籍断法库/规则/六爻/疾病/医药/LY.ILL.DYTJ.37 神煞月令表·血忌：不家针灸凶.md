# 神煞月令表·血忌：不家针灸凶

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 血忌（正月起丑、未、寅、申、卯、酉、辰、戌、巳、亥、午、子）：不可在家里针灸，凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Blood Taboo (from the first month Chou, Wei, Yin, Shen, Mao, You, Chen, Xu, Si, Hai, Wu, Zi): no needling or moxibustion at home, ominous.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 血忌按月排：正月丑、二月未、三月寅、四月申、五月卯、六月酉、七月辰、八月戌、九月巳、十月亥、十一月午、十二月子。「不家针灸凶」的「不家」读不确定，这里按「不可在家里（用针灸）」译；意思大概是血忌落在的月份、日子里，不要在家里做针灸放血这类会见血的治疗。这一行是这一章里唯一说到治疗方式的神煞。针灸的宜忌按鬼爻五行定的说法，见天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.13）和火珠林（LY.ILL.HZL.09）。
>
> *Note:* Blood Taboo is laid out by month: Chou in the first month, Wei in the second, Yin in the third, Shen in the fourth, Mao in the fifth, You in the sixth, Chen in the seventh, Xu in the eighth, Si in the ninth, Hai in the tenth, Wu in the eleventh, Zi in the twelfth. The 'bu jia' of 'bu jia zhen jiu xiong' is not certain, and here it is rendered 'no needling or moxibustion at home'; the sense is probably that in the months and days on which the Blood Taboo falls, one should not do at home the treatments that draw blood, such as needling and moxibustion. This row is the only spirit or killing in this chapter that speaks of a way of treatment. For the rules on what to use and avoid in needling and moxibustion by the phase of the ghost line see the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.13) and the Huozhu Lin (LY.ILL.HZL.09).
