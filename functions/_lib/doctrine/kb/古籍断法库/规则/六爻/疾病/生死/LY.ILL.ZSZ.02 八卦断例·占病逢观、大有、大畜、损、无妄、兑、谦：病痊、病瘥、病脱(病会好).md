# 八卦断例·占病逢观、大有、大畜、损、无妄、兑、谦：病痊、病瘥、病脱(病会好)

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 周易尚占的八卦断例是一卦一行断辞，下面七个卦的断辞明说病会好：观「财聚病痊」，大有「讼散病痊」，大畜「婚成病脱」，损「婚成病瘥」，无妄「病瘥讼和」，兑「病瘥婚成」，谦「身吉病瘥」。痊、瘥是痊愈，病脱是病脱去了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the judgments by example of the Zhouyi Shangzhan each hexagram has one line of verdicts, and in these seven the line says outright that the illness will be well: Guan, 'wealth gathers, the illness is cured'; Dayou, 'the lawsuit disperses, the illness is cured'; Daxu, 'marriage is made, the illness drops away'; Sun, 'marriage is made, the illness is cured'; Wuwang, 'the illness is cured, the lawsuit is settled'; Dui, 'the illness is cured, marriage is made'; Qian (Humility), 'the person is fortunate, the illness is cured'. 'Cured' and 'recovered' both mean full recovery, and 'drops away' means the illness is shed.

> [!warning] 打架
> 按卦名断病，各书不一样，而这七个卦里的观、大畜，别的书说占病凶。观卦：这里说「财聚病痊」，可是阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.03）说「夏观」凶，卜筮全书疾病论（LY.ILL.BSQS.35）说「夏嫌观蛊」，卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.15）和阐奥歌章第五首（LY.ILL.CA.06）都把观列进占病的凶卦，天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.06）说观「切忌世身入墓」（有条件的），增删卜易（LY.ILL.ZS.01）转述的「断之必死」的十个卦里也有观、大畜。大畜卦这里说「婚成病脱」，天玄赋却说大畜「莫逢财鬼俱兴」。另一方面，野鹤（LY.ILL.ZS.01）和易林补遗（LY.ILL.EL.13）根本反对按卦名断病，说断病全凭用神、「休把卦名推祸福」。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Judging illness by hexagram name differs from book to book, and of these seven the other books call Guan and Daxu ominous for illness. Guan: here it says 'wealth gathers, the illness is cured', but the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.03) calls 'Guan in summer' ominous, the Bushi Quanshu's essay on illness (LY.ILL.BSQS.35) says 'summer dislikes Guan and Gu', the passage of the Bushi Yuangui (LY.ILL.DYTJ.15) and the fifth poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.06) both put Guan among the ominous hexagrams for illness, the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.06) says of Guan 'above all avoid the Self or the Body entering the tomb' (a conditional statement), and the ten hexagrams of 'judge death' reported in the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.01) include Guan and Daxu. For Daxu the line here says 'marriage is made, the illness drops away', while the Tianxuan Fu says of Daxu 'do not meet Wealth and Pressure both stirring'. On the other side, the Old Man of Yehe (LY.ILL.ZS.01) and the Yilin Buyi (LY.ILL.EL.13) are against judging illness by hexagram name at all, saying that life and death in illness rest wholly on the Subject, 'do not push fortune and misfortune from the name of the hexagram'. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 八卦断例章一卦一行断辞，每行八个词组：头一个解释卦名的字义，后面几个分别讲事、人口、田蚕、官司、婚姻、出行、病、孕等。所以病只是其中的一个词组，这里把整行录出来，病的那一句在观卦是第七个、大有第五个、大畜第六个、损第三个、无妄第五个、兑第三个、谦第七个词组。这些断辞只按卦名给，没有讲用神、世应、动变，也不分近病久病。大有卦这一行开头没有「X者Y也」的释名，是「盛大丰育」四个字，照原文。第三个卦「畜者聚也」是大畜（艮宫第三卦），不是小畜，小畜另有一行「小畜止也」，那一行没有讲病，这里不用。「病脱」的「脱」是病去掉了。原文里整行没有标点，词组之间只有空格，译文分词组对齐。
>
> *Note:* The chapter of judgments by example gives each hexagram one line of verdicts, in eight phrases: the first explains the sense of the hexagram's name, and the rest speak in turn of affairs, household members, fields and silkworms, lawsuits, marriage, travel, illness, pregnancy and so on. Illness is therefore only one phrase among them, and the whole line is taken here, with the phrase on illness being the seventh for Guan, the fifth for Dayou, the sixth for Daxu, the third for Sun, the fifth for Wuwang, the third for Dui and the seventh for Qian (Humility). These verdicts are given by hexagram name alone, with nothing said of the Subject, Self and Other or moving lines, and no distinction between recent and long illness. The line of Dayou has no explanation of the name of the form 'X means Y' at its head, but the four characters 'flourishing, grand, abundant, nourishing', as in the original. The 'Xu means gathering' of the third hexagram is Daxu (the third hexagram of the Gen palace) and not Xiaoxu, which has a line of its own, 'Xiaoxu means holding back', that does not speak of illness and is not used here. The 'drops away' of 'the illness drops away' is the illness being shed. The whole line has no punctuation in the original, only spaces between the phrases, and the translation is aligned phrase by phrase.
