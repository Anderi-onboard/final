# 占官事疾病、六爻安静则看世作父爻：因何而得——用爻伏是我爻、上飞是彼、有动看动变爻、安静看有气爻

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻伏着的，那伏着的是我的爻，它上面的飞爻是对方；有动爻就看动爻和变爻，安静的就看有气的爻；如果占官事、疾病，六个爻都安静，就看世爻，把它当作父爻来看，问事情是因为什么得来的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line is hidden, the hidden one is my line and the flying line above it is the other party; where there is movement look at the moving and changed lines, and where all is still look at the line that has qi; if divining a lawsuit or illness and the six lines are all still, look at the Self and take it as the Resource line, asking from what the matter was obtained.

## 《海底眼·六亲》原文

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 372 行 · Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "The six relations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 又曰： | 又说道： | It says again: |
> | 用爻伏是我爻， | 用爻伏着的是我的爻， | if the Subject line is hidden, the hidden one is my line; |
> | 上飞是彼， | 上面的飞爻是对方； | the flying line above is the other party; |
> | 有动看动变爻， | 有动爻就看动爻和变爻， | where there is movement, look at the moving and changed lines; |
> | 安静看有气爻， | 安静的就看有气的爻； | where still, look at the line that has qi; |
> | 若占官事疾病， | 如果占官事、疾病， | if divining a lawsuit or an illness, |
> | 如六爻安静， | 六个爻都安静， | and the six lines are all still, |
> | 是看世， | 就看世爻， | look at the Self, |
> | 作父爻， | 把它当作父爻， | and take it as the Resource line, |
> | 因何而得。 | 看是因为什么得来的。 | asking from what it was obtained. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句在海底眼的「六亲」一节里，是一段讲用爻取法的话的最后一句；前面几句（「六亲者，乃八卦占主也……」）讲的是六亲占法的一般规矩，不专说疾病，这里只收最后这一句里带「疾病」的部分及其前提。「作父爻」的意思是：占官事、疾病，六个爻都不动，没有可看的动爻，就看世爻，把它当作父母爻去问「因什么而得」（父母爻是文书、劳心、忧虑，也是来由）——书里没有更多解释。和易林补遗「卦静取外卦」（LY.ILL.EL.42）、易冒「卦爻俱静求之外卦」（LY.ILL.YM.55）相比，这里看的是世爻，不是外卦，是另一个办法。
>
> *Note:* This sentence is the last one of a passage in the Haidi Yan's section on the six relations about how to take the Subject line; the sentences before it ('the six relations are the master of the eight hexagrams in divination ...') set out the general rules of divining by the six relations and are not about illness in particular, so only this last sentence, which mentions illness, and its premise are quoted here. 'Take it as the Resource line' means: in divining a lawsuit or illness, with all six lines still and no moving line to look at, look at the Self line and take it as the Resource line in asking 'from what was it obtained' (the Resource line being documents, worry of the mind, and also the origin) — the book explains no further. Compared with the Yilin Buyi's 'when the hexagram is still, take the outer trigram' (LY.ILL.EL.42) and the Yi Mao's 'with the hexagram and lines all still, seek it in the outer trigram' (LY.ILL.YM.55), what is looked at here is the Self line and not the outer trigram, which is another method.
