# 官鬼属木则文神及倚草附木之妖：东岳、家堂、山神、树神、五圣、园神、九良、茶筵、三茅、九天、鲁班、花神、三郎、道君、文昌、南木神之属，及凭依山木树木器皿成神者

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼属木的，就是文神，以及依托在草木上的精怪。像东岳、家堂、山神、树神、五圣、园神、九良、茶筵、三茅、九天、鲁班、花神、三郎、道君、文昌、南木神这一类，以及各地有依附山木、树木、器皿而成神的，都可以类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line is Wood, it is a civil spirit, and the demons that lean on grass and cling to trees, as with the Eastern Peak, the household shrine, the Mountain God, the Tree God, the Five Sages, the Garden God, the Nine Good Ones, the Tea Feast, the Three Mao, the Nine Heavens, Lu Ban, the Flower God, the Third Lord, the Daoist Lord, Wenchang and the Southern Wood God, and those places where a spirit has arisen from a mountain, a tree or a vessel it clings to; all can be reasoned by analogy.

> [!note] 按
> 神名的处理见 LY.ILL.YM.44 的注。「文神」和金的「武神」相对（LY.ILL.YM.44）：文昌、鲁班是文事、工艺的神，东岳、家堂、山神、树神是草木土地一类的神，都算木。
>
> *Note:* Names are treated as in the note to LY.ILL.YM.44. 'Civil spirit' is the counterpart of the Metal 'martial spirit' (LY.ILL.YM.44): Wenchang and Lu Ban are spirits of letters and crafts, and the Eastern Peak, the household shrine, the Mountain God and the Tree God are spirits of plants and land, all counted as Wood.
