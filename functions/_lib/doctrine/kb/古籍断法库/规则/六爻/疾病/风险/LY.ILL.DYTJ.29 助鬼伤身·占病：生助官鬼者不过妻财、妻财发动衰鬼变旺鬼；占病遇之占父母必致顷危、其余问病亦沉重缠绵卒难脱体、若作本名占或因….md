# 助鬼伤身·占病：生助官鬼者不过妻财、妻财发动衰鬼变旺鬼；占病遇之占父母必致顷危、其余问病亦沉重缠绵卒难脱体、若作本名占或因财致病或房事过频乃大凶之兆；子孙发动解神来救庶可反凶成吉

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生助官鬼的，不过是妻财，不宜发动，动了衰鬼就变成旺鬼；旺鬼遇到它，势头更凶。比如申日占得离卦，鬼临应爻克世，本来不是好兆，再加妻财发动、生助鬼爻，凶更厉害，这就是助鬼伤身；凡是卦里鬼爻克身、世的，没有财，凶有限；若有两个财爻都动，祸就大凶。占病遇到它：若占的是父母，必定顷刻危险；其余人问病，也是沉重缠绵，一时难以脱身；若是替本人占，或是因财致病，或是房事过频，是大凶的兆。以上各种事，若有子孙发动、解神来救，或许可以反凶成吉、变祸为祥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* What generates and helps the Pressure line is only Wealth, which should not move, for moving it turns a weak ghost into a strong ghost; a strong ghost meeting it, its force is fiercer still. For example, divining the hexagram Li on a Shen day, the ghost sits on the Other line and controls the Self, which is not a good sign to begin with, and Wealth moving in addition to generate and help the ghost line makes the ill fiercer still: this is helping the ghost to injure the Body; whenever the ghost line in a hexagram controls the Body or the Self, with no Wealth the ill is limited, and if two Wealth lines both move the calamity is greatly ominous. Divining illness and meeting it: if a parent (the Resource line) is divined for, it will surely bring peril in a moment; for other askers about illness it is also heavy and lingering, and hard to shake off in the end; if it is divined in one's own name, it may be illness brought on by wealth, or excess of sexual activity, and it is a sign of great ill. In all the above matters, if Output moves and a resolving spirit comes to rescue, it may be possible to turn ill into luck and calamity into fortune.

> [!note] 按
> 「助鬼伤身」是鬼爻克世或卦身、又有财爻发动生鬼，三样凑在一起。头一段是总句，和婚姻、求财、占官等一章（LY.MARRY.DYTJ.03 一带）相同；这一条收占病那一段。「占父母必致顷危」是因为财爻克父母、又生鬼；「其余问病亦是沉重缠绵」是别人的病也重；「若作本名占」是替自己占，「本名」是占的人自己；「或因财致病」是因财而得病（钱事劳心或财多招灾），「房事过频」是色欲。最后一句「子孙发动，解神来救」是说有子孙发动能克鬼、有解神来解，可以转祸为福；但子孙发动若同时有财爻发动生鬼，情形又不同，原文没有再展开。财爻发动生鬼、问病难愈，和火珠林「妻财独发，生鬼伤父；问病难瘳」（LY.ILL.HZL.18）、天玄赋的「鬼旺财兴难保命」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.02）是同一个道理。
>
> *Note:* 'Helping the ghost to injure the Body' is the ghost line controlling the Self or the Body, and a Wealth line moving to generate the ghost, the three together. The first passage is the general statement, the same as the chapters on marriage, wealth and office (around LY.MARRY.DYTJ.03); this entry takes the passage on illness. 'If a parent is divined for it will surely bring peril in a moment' is because Wealth controls Resource and also generates the ghost; 'for other askers about illness it is also heavy and lingering' is that another person's illness is heavy too; 'if it is divined in one's own name' is divining for oneself, the 'own name' being the diviner himself; 'illness brought on by wealth' is caught through money worries or through much wealth inviting calamity, and 'excess of sexual activity' is lust. The last sentence, 'Output moves and a resolving spirit comes to rescue', says that with Output moving to control the ghost and a resolving spirit to untie, calamity may be turned into fortune; but if, while Output moves, a Wealth line also moves to generate the ghost, the case is different, and the original does not go on to say so. Wealth moving to generate the ghost, so that an illness is hard to cure, is the same principle as the Huozhu Lin's 'Wealth moving alone generates the ghost and injures the father; asking about illness it is hard to cure' (LY.ILL.HZL.18) and the Duanyi Tianji's 'ghost strong and Wealth rising, life is hard to keep' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02).
