# 看鬼临何卦何爻发动，知犯何神何煞：八宫方位（坎北水口、离南灶前、艮山林东北、坤坟墓西南、乾西北兑西天曹寺观、巽东南、震东伐树起造）；爻位动工（六造墙作墓、五砌路修街、四门栏檐下、三房内桥梁、二修厨作灶、初穿井开沟）；世为本宅兴修、应为对门垦掘

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看鬼落在哪一卦、哪一爻发动，就知道在那里冒犯了什么神、什么煞，因而牵连上身：官鬼在坎位，是北方来侵，或是兴动水口；鬼到离宫，是南向的妨碍，或是在灶前动工；艮主山林，或是遭遇东北；坤成坟墓，或是值西南；乾是西北，兑是西方，或是冒犯天曹、修寺观；巽是东南；震是东方，或是砍伐树林并起造。六爻的鬼，必是造墙作墓；五爻的官，必是砌路修街；四爻，断门栏，或是在檐下动工；三爻，推房内，或是动犯桥梁；二爻，是修厨房、作灶；初爻，是穿井、开沟；鬼在世爻上，是本宅动了工；在应爻上，是对门在挖掘；临门的爻，是开窗不便；变出兄弟，是挖坑、建造不通。注说：以上说的，都是把上面的鬼逐一说明白，不必再注。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Look at which trigram and which line the ghost moves in, to know where a god or a killing was offended and thereby brought on the body: the Pressure line in the Kan place means encroachment from the north, or works at a water mouth; a ghost at the Li palace means obstruction facing south, or works in front of the stove; Gen governs mountain and forest, or meets the northeast; Kun makes graves, or falls southwest; Qian is northwest and Dui is west, or an offence against the Heavenly Bureau in repairing a temple or abbey; Xun is southeast; Zhen is east, or felling woods and building. A ghost on the sixth line surely means building a wall or making a grave; the Pressure line on the fifth surely means paving a road or repairing a street; the fourth, judge gates and railings, or works under the eaves; the third, work out the inside of the house, or works offending bridges and beams; the second is repairing the kitchen or making a stove; the first is digging a well or opening a ditch; on the Self line, works on one's own house; on the Other line, digging opposite the door; on the door line, opening a window is inconvenient; changing into Peer, a pit or structure is blocked. The commentary says: all of the above sets the ghosts out one by one, and there is no need for further notes.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节和黄金策「修造动土必是煞遇勾陈」（LY.ILL.HJC.100）是同一件事：病由动土修造、犯了土神引起，用鬼所在的卦位定方向，用鬼所在的爻位定动的是什么工（初爻穿井开沟、二爻修厨作灶、三爻房内桥梁、四爻门栏檐下、五爻砌路修街、六爻造墙作墓）。「官」在这里是官鬼的省写。「在世则本宅兴修，在应则对门垦掘」是说鬼落在世爻是自己家动工，落在应爻是对门人家动工。「临门爻」的「门」原文没有说是哪一爻，这里照字面译。「化兄弟，坑造不通」的「不通」读法没有把握。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This section is the same matter as the Huangjin Ce's 'works and breaking ground surely mean a killing meeting Hook Earth' (LY.ILL.HJC.100): the illness comes from breaking ground or building and offending an earth spirit, the direction is fixed by the palace the ghost is in, and what work was done is fixed by the line the ghost is on (the first line digging a well or ditch, the second repairing the kitchen or making a stove, the third the inside of the house or bridges, the fourth gates and eaves, the fifth paving a road or repairing a street, the sixth building a wall or making a grave). 'Guan' (official) is here short for the Pressure line. 'On the Self, works on one's own house; on the Other, digging opposite the door' means that with the ghost on the Self line the works are in one's own house, and on the Other line in the house across the door. The 'door' of 'the door line' is not said in the original to be any particular line, and is translated literally. The reading of 'is blocked' in 'changing into Peer, a pit or structure is blocked' is uncertain. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
