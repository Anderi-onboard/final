# 占何日病凶：用爻无气更遭伤、忌爻逢生助之日便主身危；忌神旺动仇人发而无用爻则待用神值日难避；月破用爻不拘衰旺命必归阴；元神被克灾加重、忌客逢生定受殃——书中九例(观之益、涣之垢、剥、萃、师、艮、归妹……)

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病人来占，要细看凶日：用爻无气，又遭伤克，忌爻哪天逢到生助，便主身危，立在孝堂；忌神旺动、仇神发动，用神失位反而无妨；变爻怕日辰临在用爻的位置，如果挨过去就免于凄惶；元神被克灾更重，忌客逢生一定受殃；月破用爻，天命就止，纵然旺相也必死。注说：论病哪一天出现凶险，不过看用爻的生克：用爻如果无气、被日辰克的就凶，忌神动、用爻很衰的时候，遇上再生忌神的日子就死；又说卦里元神不发，忌神兴旺、仇神都动，唯独没有用爻，眼前无事，等到日后用神值日就难以回避，一定入黄泉；倘若忌神、仇神兴旺而元神同时发动，也没有主象，等到用爻值日反而无妨；再说卦里用象既衰，全靠元神相救，担心忌客来伤，日辰克制元神，灾就沉重；忌象如果逢生旺的时候，一定成凶咎；主象如果逢月破，不论衰旺，命必归阴。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a patient who comes to divine, examine the ominous days closely: with the Subject line lacking qi and moreover wounded, on the day the adversary line meets generation and support the body is in danger, standing in the house of mourning; the Adversary strong and moving and the enemy spirit rising, the Subject having lost its place is on the contrary all right; a change fears the Day sitting on the Subject's place, and if it is got past there is freedom from dismay; the Support controlled makes the calamity heavier, and the adversary guest meeting generation surely brings disaster; the Subject line in Month-break ends one's allotted span, and even if strong it will die. The commentary says: to judge on what day the illness turns ominous is nothing but looking at the generation and control of the Subject line: if the Subject line has no qi and is controlled by the Day it is ominous, and when the Adversary moves and the Subject line is very weak, meeting a day that again generates the Adversary means death; again, if in the hexagram the Support does not move, the Adversary thrives and the enemy spirit all move, and there is only no Subject line, nothing happens for now, but when the Subject's day comes it is hard to avoid and he surely enters the Yellow Springs; if the Adversary and enemy spirit thrive and the Support moves with them, and there is also no chief image, then waiting until the Subject line's day is on the contrary harmless; moreover, if the Subject image in the hexagram is already weak, everything depends on the Support to rescue it, and one worries that the adversary guest will come to wound it, and if the Day controls the Support the calamity will be heavy; if the adversary image meets a time of generation or strength it is sure to make misfortune; if the chief image meets Month-break, whether strong or weak, life must return to the shades.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1317–1319 行 · Case from the book

> | 且如子占父病，五月甲子日，占得观之益卦，此卦父爻化居旺地，又带青龙贵人，本为吉象，岂知父临月破，后至乙亥日，元神绝而忌神生，果然父丧。 | 比如儿子占父亲的病，五月甲子日，占得观卦变益卦，这一卦父爻化出旺地，又带青龙、贵人，本来是吉象，哪知道父爻临月破，后来到乙亥日，元神绝而忌神生，果然父亲去世。 | For example, a son divined his father's illness on a Jiazi day in the fifth month and obtained Guan changing into Yi; in this hexagram the Resource line changed into a strong place and carried the Azure Dragon and the Noble, which would have been an auspicious image, but who would have known that the Resource line sat in Month-break, and later on the Yihai day the Support was in Extinction and the Adversary generated, and the father indeed died. |
> | 又如妻占夫病，三月甲子旬丁卯日，卜得涣之垢卦，所嫌仇神忌神皆动，独无用爻，又鬼伏仇爻之下，又值旬空，毫无救。 | 又如妻子占丈夫的病，三月甲子旬丁卯日，占得涣卦变垢卦，所嫌的是仇神、忌神都动，唯独没有用爻，又鬼伏在仇神爻之下，又值旬空，毫无救助。 | Again, a wife divined her husband's illness on a Dingmao day of the Jiazi decade in the third month and obtained Huan changing into Gou; what was disliked was that the enemy spirit and the Adversary both moved, and only the Subject line was missing, the ghost was moreover hidden under the enemy-spirit line and in Void as well, with no rescue at all. |
> | 挨至乙亥日用爻透出，免受忌神来伤，夫果死也。 | 挨到乙亥日用爻透出，免受忌神来伤，丈夫果然死了。 | It was held off until the Yihai day, when the Subject line came out and was spared the Adversary's wounding, and the husband indeed died. |
> | 假令弟占兄病，十月戊辰日，卜得剥初二三爻皆动，却本卦无兄弟，所喜申金兄弟伏在世爻用爻透出果应病痊。 | 假如弟弟占哥哥的病，十月戊辰日，占得剥卦初、二、三爻都动，可是本卦没有兄弟爻，所幸申金兄弟爻伏在世爻之下、用爻透出，果然应验病愈。 | Suppose a younger brother divined his elder brother's illness on a Wuchen day in the tenth month and obtained Bo with the first, second and third lines all moving, but the main hexagram had no Peer line; happily the Shen Metal Peer line lay hidden under the Self and the Subject line came out, and it came true that the illness was cured. |
> | 又如父占子病，五月甲午旬癸卯日，占得萃卦上六爻动，此卦子孙太弱，父母相而又动，虽受卯日相伤，又逢月建扶起，父又化为进气能克用爻，虽有元神暗动又值立时空不能相救，后至丙午日忌象叨生，此男果死。 | 又如父亲占儿子的病，五月甲午旬癸卯日，占得萃卦上六爻动，这一卦子孙太弱，父母爻旺相又动，虽然受卯日相伤，又逢月建扶起，父爻又化为进气，能克用爻，虽然有元神暗动，又值立时空，不能相救，后来到丙午日忌象叨生，这个男孩果然死了。 | Again, a father divined his son's illness on a Guimao day of the Jiawu decade in the fifth month and obtained Cui with the top line moving; in this hexagram Output was too weak, the Resource line was strong and moving, and though wounded by the Mao day it was raised by the Month, and the Resource line also changed into advancing qi and could control the Subject line; though the Support moved in secret it was Void at the moment and could not rescue, and later on the Bingwu day the adversary image drew generation, and the boy indeed died. |
> | 又如夫占妻病，三月甲戌旬庚辰日，卜得师卦九二上六爻动，此卦青龙财爻持世，兄弟又不交重，鬼又化出财来，本为佳兆，岂知土官发动，书云更论土爻临鬼动，多凶少吉祸难让，果应五月庚申日，官遇长生月建又扶土鬼，此鬼太刚死而可验。 | 又如丈夫占妻子的病，三月甲戌旬庚辰日，占得师卦九二、上六爻动，这一卦青龙财爻持世，兄弟爻又不发动，鬼又变出财来，本是佳兆，哪知道土官发动，书上说更论土爻临鬼动，多凶少吉祸难禳，果然应在五月庚申日，官遇长生、月建又扶土鬼，这个鬼太刚，死而验证。 | Again, a husband divined his wife's illness on a Gengchen day of the Jiaxu decade in the third month and obtained Shi with the second line and the top line moving; in this hexagram the Azure Dragon Wealth line held the Self, the Peer line did not move, and the ghost changed into Wealth, which was an auspicious sign, but who would have known that the Earth Pressure line moved, and the book says 'an Earth line on a moving ghost is mostly ominous and seldom auspicious, and the misfortune is hard to exorcise', and it came true on the Gengshen day in the fifth month, the Pressure line meeting Birth and the Month again supporting the Earth ghost; the ghost was too rigid, and her death confirmed it. |
> | 又如父占女久远病，七月甲寅旬癸亥日，卜得艮卦安静，此卦用爻临月建，忌神又不兴似无凶兆，岂知卦犯六冲，经云初灾遇此当瘵瘥，久病逢之命必伤，果应在子月戊午日，用象死于月建，败于日辰，其女死也。 | 又如父亲占女儿的久病，七月甲寅旬癸亥日，占得艮卦安静，这一卦用爻临月建，忌神又不发动，似乎没有凶兆，哪知道卦犯六冲，经上说初灾遇此当全好，久病遇上命必伤，果然应在子月戊午日，用象死于月建、败于日辰，这个女儿死了。 | Again, a father divined his daughter's long illness on a Guihai day of the Jiayin decade in the seventh month and obtained Gen still; in this hexagram the Subject line sat in the Month and the Adversary did not move, so there seemed no ominous sign, but who would have known that the hexagram committed a six-clash, and the classic says 'a fresh calamity meeting it should be wholly cured, a long illness meeting it surely brings harm to life', and it came true on a Wuwu day in the Zi month, the Subject image dying to the Month and defeated by the Day, and the daughter died. |
> | 又母占子病，九月甲子旬辛未日，卜得归妹卦安静，此卦六爻无子，虽伏出亥水子孙在四爻之下，又落空亡，世上父母又被日辰冲动本绝卦也，岂知互出水火既济取互体坎水配成兑卦，子孙此乃无中生有也。 | 又如母亲占儿子的病，九月甲子旬辛未日，占得归妹卦安静，这一卦六爻里没有子孙，虽然伏出亥水子孙在四爻之下，又落空亡，世上的父母爻又被日辰冲动，本来是绝卦，哪知道互出水火既济，取互体坎水配成兑卦，子孙这就是无中生有。 | Again, a mother divined her son's illness on a Xinwei day of the Jiazi decade in the ninth month and obtained Guimei still; in this hexagram there was no Output among the six lines, and although Hai Water Output came out hidden under the fourth line it was in Void, and the Resource line on the Self was clashed into motion by the Day, so it was a hopeless hexagram, but who would have known that Jiji came out of the nuclear trigrams, taking the nuclear Kan Water to join with Dui, and Output was thus born from nothing. |
> | 惟互出之爻，再不受日月并动爻伤克，此子果应亥月戊子日，用值旺乡，病全脱体。 | 只有互卦里出来的爻，再不受日、月和动爻的伤克，这个儿子果然应在亥月戊子日，用爻逢旺，病全部脱体。 | Only the line that comes out of the nuclear trigrams is no longer wounded or controlled by the Day, Month or moving lines, and the son indeed answered on a Wuzi day in the Hai month, the Subject line meeting strength and the illness wholly leaving the body. |

> [!note] 按
> 这一条前半是规则，后半是九个卦例。卦例里最后一个（归妹静卦，互出水火既济，取互卦的坎水配兑）是「卦无用象，查互卦」（LY.ILL.EL.10）那条规则的实例：主卦里没有子孙，伏神空，却从互卦里取出了亥水子孙，而且互出来的爻不受日月动爻的伤克，所以病愈。第五个例（萃卦）里的「立时空」与 LY.ILL.EL.11 里的是同一个词。卦例里的「进气」即化进神。「立孝堂」「归阴」都是死的委婉说法。各例的月日和卦都是原文的，没有替它排过盘。
>
> *Note:* The first half of this entry is rules and the second half nine cases. The last of the cases (Guimei still, with Jiji from the nuclear trigrams, taking the nuclear Kan Water to join Dui) is an instance of the rule 'if the hexagram has no Subject image, examine the nuclear trigrams' (LY.ILL.EL.10): the main hexagram has no Output and the hidden line is Void, yet Hai Water Output is taken from the nuclear trigrams, and the line that comes out of the nuclear trigrams is not wounded by the Day, Month or moving lines, so the illness is cured. 'Void at the moment' in the fifth case is the same word as in LY.ILL.EL.11. 'Advancing qi' in the cases is a change into an Advancing line. 'Standing in the house of mourning' and 'returning to the shades' are euphemisms for death. The months, days and hexagrams of the cases are the original's, and no chart has been cast for them.
