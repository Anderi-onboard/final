# 鬼谷辨爻法·占疾病：初爻足、二爻腿股、三爻腰、四爻脊、五爻心、六爻头

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼谷子辨爻法在占疾病一章的表：初爻管足，二爻管腿和股，三爻管腰，四爻管脊，五爻管心，六爻管头。表的第一行另有丧门、吊客、哭声、棺木、行兆、坟墓六个名目，按六列的次序对着初爻到六爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Guigu's table of the lines for the chapter on illness: the first line governs the feet, the second the legs and thighs, the third the waist, the fourth the spine, the fifth the heart and the sixth the head. The table's first row also gives six names, Mourning Gate, Guest of Condolence, weeping, the coffin, Xingzhao and the grave, which by the order of the six columns stand over the first to the sixth lines.

> [!warning] 打架
> 哪一爻管身体哪一处，几本书差得很远。这张表是「初足、二腿股、三腰、四脊、五心、六头」；同一章孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）是「一五脏、二皮肉、三体骨腰并足、四肺心经、五六头」，和阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.04）同；同一章卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.11）是「初两足、二双腿、三腰背、四心腹肚肠、五肾脏、六头」；易隐引鬼谷分爻（LY.ILL.YY.02）是「初足、二腿股、三肝肾、四脾脏、五心肺、六头脑」。头在六爻、足在初爻，几家一致；中间三爻各说各的。同一本书里，鬼谷辨爻法的表和孙膑歌就对不上。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line governs which part of the body is put very differently in several books. This table has 'first the feet, second the legs and thighs, third the waist, fourth the spine, fifth the heart, sixth the head'; Sun Bin's song in the same chapter (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) has 'the first the five viscera, the second skin and flesh, the third body, bones, waist and feet, the fourth lungs and heart channel, the fifth and sixth the head', as in the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.04); the Bushi Yuangui passage in the same chapter (LY.ILL.DYTJ.11) has 'first the two feet, second both legs, third waist and back, fourth heart, belly and bowels, fifth the kidneys, sixth the head'; and the Yi Yin, quoting Guigu's division of the lines (LY.ILL.YY.02), has 'first the feet, second the legs and thighs, third liver and kidneys, fourth the spleen, fifth heart and lungs, sixth the head and brain'. The head at the sixth line and the feet at the first are agreed; the three lines between are put differently in each. Even within this one book the table under Guigu's name and Sun Bin's song do not agree. The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这是一张三行的表，原书排成六列：第一行六个名目，第二行初爻到六爻，第三行六个部位。第一行在原文里连着写、没有标点，这里按六列的次序把丧门配初爻、吊客配二爻、哭声配三爻、棺木配四爻、行兆配五爻、坟墓配六爻；这是照表的列序读的，书里没有另写解释。「行兆」的意思书里没有说，「兆」有墓地的意思，可能是出殡去墓地的行列，这里不能确定，英文只给拼音。同一书的每一章开头都有一张「鬼谷辨爻法」的表，占六畜章那张是初爻鸡鸭、二爻猫犬、三爻猪、四爻羊、五爻牛、六爻马，所以这一行是把六个爻位配给这一章要看的东西。
>
> *Note:* This is a table of three rows, laid out in the book as six columns: the first row six names, the second the first to sixth lines, the third six parts of the body. The first row is written in the original run together without punctuation; here Mourning Gate is taken with the first line, Guest of Condolence with the second, weeping with the third, the coffin with the fourth, Xingzhao with the fifth and the grave with the sixth, which is reading by the order of the columns, and the book does not explain it separately. The book does not say what 'Xingzhao' means; 'zhao' can mean a burial ground and it may be the procession to the grave, but this cannot be settled here, so the English gives only the pinyin. Every chapter of this book opens with a table headed 'Guigu's method of telling the lines apart'; the one for the chapter on livestock runs first line fowl, second cats and dogs, third pigs, fourth sheep, fifth cattle, sixth horses, so this row assigns the six line positions to what that chapter is about.
