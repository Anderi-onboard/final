# 六神论神鬼心决：青龙观音三官、朱雀家堂灶君鬼为吐血劳怯、勾陈土地城隍鬼为痢疾蛊胀、螣蛇缢死汤火、白虎横死肚饱魂、玄武真武帝鬼作落水伤亡

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六神论神鬼心决是六首歌，每首一位神：青龙是善愿、观音、三官；朱雀是家堂、灶君，鬼为吐血和劳怯，目疾、伤亡也有缘故；勾陈是土地、城隍，鬼为痢疾和蛊胀，遇土爻是咽塞而死；螣蛇逢官缢死，孩童汤火受伤；白虎临太岁是大将军，横死伤亡；玄武是真武帝，鬼爻作祟断为落水伤亡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verses on the six spirits and the heart-secret of spirits and ghosts are six songs, one for each spirit: the Azure Dragon is good vows, Guanyin and the Three Officials; the Vermilion Bird is the household shrine and the Hearth Lord, its ghost means spitting blood and consumptive weakness, and eye disease, injury and death have their cause in it; Hook Earth is the Earth God and the City God, its ghost means dysentery and swelling of the belly, and meeting an Earth line one dies of a blocked throat; the Coiling Snake meeting Pressure means death by hanging, and children are hurt by scalding and fire; the White Tiger on Tai Sui is the great general, with violent death and injury; the Dark Tortoise is the Emperor Zhenwu, and its ghost doing harm is judged a death by drowning.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9819 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Chanyou Jingyao, no. 13: the six spirits and the heart-secret of spirits and ghosts", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 玄武就名真武帝， | 玄武就叫作真武帝， | The Dark Tortoise is called the Emperor Zhenwu, |
> | 水仙五圣佛经祈， | 水仙、五圣，靠佛经祈祷； | the water immortals and the Five Sages are prayed to by scripture; |
> | 鬼作落水伤亡断， | 鬼爻作祟，断为落水伤亡； | a ghost doing harm is judged a death by drowning; |
> | 活法全凭智略推。 | 活的办法全凭智谋来推。 | the living method rests wholly on wit and strategy. |

^q6

> [!note] 按
> 这是六首歌，每首四句：前两句说这位神对应哪些神（青龙配观音、三官；朱雀配家堂、灶君；勾陈配土地、城隍；螣蛇配飞来土；白虎配太岁、大将军、五路神；玄武配真武帝），后两句说鬼爻加在这位神上时的病或死法。各句省得很，好几处字面读不出完整的意思（「断鬼产亡并带血」「遇鬼七杀金作三」「肚饱魂」「活法全凭智略推」），译文照字面，没有补。「蛊胀」是腹胀如鼓一类的病，「劳怯」是虚劳怯弱，「咽塞」是咽喉堵塞。原文「鰧蛇」是「螣蛇」的异写。
>
> *Note:* These are six songs of four lines each: the first two lines say which gods the spirit matches (the Azure Dragon Guanyin and the Three Officials; the Vermilion Bird the household shrine and the Hearth Lord; Hook Earth the Earth God and the City God; the Coiling Snake earth that has flown in; the White Tiger Tai Sui, the great general and the Five Road gods; the Dark Tortoise the Emperor Zhenwu), and the last two lines say what illness or manner of death a ghost line on this spirit gives. The lines are very terse and in several places the letter does not give a complete sense ('to judge the ghost: dead in childbirth, with blood', 'meeting a ghost, the Seven Killings and Metal, making three', 'the soul with a swollen belly', 'the living method rests wholly on wit'); the translation keeps to the letter without filling in. 'Swelling of the belly' is a drum-like bloating of the abdomen, 'consumptive weakness' is deficiency and feebleness, and 'blocked throat' is a blockage in the gullet. The original writes the snake with a variant character.
