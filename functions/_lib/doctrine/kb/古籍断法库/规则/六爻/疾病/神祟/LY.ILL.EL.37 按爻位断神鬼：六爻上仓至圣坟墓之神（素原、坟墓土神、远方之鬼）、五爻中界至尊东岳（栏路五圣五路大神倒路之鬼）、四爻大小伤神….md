# 按爻位断神鬼：六爻上仓至圣坟墓之神（素原、坟墓土神、远方之鬼）、五爻中界至尊东岳（栏路五圣五路大神倒路之鬼）、四爻大小伤神右仪门将军（门前鬼）、三爻家堂郡王府城隍（床婆弟兄鬼房内鬼桥上鬼）、二爻县城隍灶神（夫妻鬼厨下鬼）、初爻土地井泉童子（井前之鬼、无井即土鬼）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻，是上仓的至圣、坟墓的神；五爻，是中界的至尊、路途的鬼；四爻，推为檐外伤、朝门前的魍魉；三爻断，鬼在六爻，是上苍的素原、以及坟墓土神、或远方的鬼；鬼在五爻，是中界至尊，就是东岳，又是栏路五圣、五路大神、或倒在路上的鬼；鬼在四爻，是大小伤神、以及右仪门的将军、又是门前的鬼；鬼在三爻，是家堂和郡王，就是府城隍，又是床婆、弟兄的鬼，以及房内的鬼、桥上的鬼；鬼在二爻，是县城隍和灶神，又是夫妻等的鬼、厨下的鬼；鬼在初爻，是土地和井泉童子、以及井前的鬼，如果没有井，就是土鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The sixth line is the supreme sage of the upper granary and the god of graves; the fifth is the supreme honoured one of the middle realm and the ghost of the road; the fourth is worked out as an injury beyond the eaves and the goblins before the court gate; the third judge: a ghost on the sixth line is the plain vow of the upper heaven, the earth god of graves, or a ghost from a far place; a ghost on the fifth line is the supreme honoured one of the middle realm, that is, the Eastern Peak, and also the Five Sages of the barrier road, the Great God of the Five Roads, or a ghost fallen on the road; a ghost on the fourth line is the greater and lesser Injuring gods, and the general at the right ceremonial gate, and also a ghost before the door; a ghost on the third line is the household shrine and the Prince of the Commandery, that is, the City God of the prefecture, and also the Bed Mother and a ghost of brothers, and a ghost within the room and a ghost on the bridge; a ghost on the second line is the City God of the county and the Kitchen God, and also a ghost of husband and wife and a ghost beneath the kitchen; a ghost on the first line is the Earth God and the Well-and-Spring Boy, and a ghost before the well, and if there is no well it is an earth ghost.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段歌诀只剩三句（六、五、四爻），第四句「三断」之后原文没有接歌诀，而是直接接解说「鬼在六爻」，所以「三断」这两个字看起来是歌诀残留，这里和解说的第一句连在一起引。第一位「上仓至圣」「素原」的「素原」是素食所许的愿（见 LY.ILL.HJC.93 后各条的「素愿」）。这一节的爻位配法和易冒「按爻位断神鬼」（LY.ILL.YM.58）、黄金策「六爻推鬼神」（LY.ILL.HJC.101）大体同路：初爻井、二爻灶、三爻家堂、五爻路、六爻坟墓；差别是易林补遗每爻多列了具体的神名和几种鬼。「床婆」是保护婴孩的床神。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* The verse of this passage is left with only three lines (the sixth, fifth and fourth lines), and after 'the third judge' the original does not go on with verse but goes straight into the explanation 'a ghost on the sixth line', so that 'the third judge' looks like a remnant of the verse and is quoted here joined to the first sentence of the explanation. 'Plain vow' is a vow made with vegetarian offering. The assignment by line position agrees in outline with the Yi Mao's 'reading ghosts and spirits by line position' (LY.ILL.YM.58) and the Huangjin Ce's 'reading ghosts and spirits by the six lines' (LY.ILL.HJC.101): the first line the well, the second the stove, the third the household shrine, the fifth the road, the sixth the graves; the difference is that the Yilin Buyi lists specific gods and several kinds of ghost for each line. The 'Bed Mother' is the bed goddess who protects infants. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
