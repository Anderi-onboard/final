# 坟墓·月卦勾陈之土鬼，瘟疫相侵（旧注：月卦是月将勾陈，土鬼临世身爻主时灾瘟疫相侵）

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》坟墓章说：「月卦的勾陈和土鬼，瘟疫就会侵袭。」旧注：月卦是月将、勾陈，土鬼临在世爻、身爻上，主时灾、瘟疫侵袭。王洪绪的说法一样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on graves says: 'The Hook Earth of the month hexagram and an Earth ghost: epidemic attacks.' The old commentary: the month hexagram is the Month general and Hook Earth; an Earth ghost sitting on the Self line or the Body means a seasonal calamity, with epidemic attacking. Wang Hongxu says the same.

> | 月卦是月将勾陈土鬼临世身爻，主时灾瘟疫相侵； | 月卦是月将勾陈，土鬼临在世爻、身爻上，主时灾、瘟疫侵袭； | The month hexagram is the Month general and Hook Earth; an Earth ghost sitting on the Self line or the Body means a seasonal calamity, with epidemic attacking; |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一联在坟墓章里，讲的是整个时节的瘟疫（「时灾」），不是占一个人的病；和年时章的「更嫌值土，千门疫疠若符同」（LY.ILL.HJC.132）是同一个意思：土鬼发动或临世身，主瘟疫。「月卦」是按月起的卦，「月将」是当月的月将。联的后半「阳宫朱雀之凶神，火灾烦数」讲火灾，不收。
>
> *Note:* This couplet in the chapter on graves is about an epidemic of the whole season ('seasonal calamity') and not one person's illness; it has the same sense as 'still worse if it meets Earth: the epidemics in a thousand households tally as one' in the chapter on the year (LY.ILL.HJC.132): an Earth ghost moving or sitting on the Self line or the Body means epidemic. The 'month hexagram' is the hexagram set up by the month, and the 'Month general' is the month's general. The second half of the couplet, 'the ominous Vermilion Bird of a yang palace: fire calamities come thick', is about fire and is not taken.
