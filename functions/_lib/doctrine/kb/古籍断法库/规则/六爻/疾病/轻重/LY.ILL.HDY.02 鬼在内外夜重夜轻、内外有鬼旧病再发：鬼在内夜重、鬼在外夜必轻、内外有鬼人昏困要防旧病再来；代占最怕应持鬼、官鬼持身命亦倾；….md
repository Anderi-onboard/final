# 鬼在内外夜重夜轻、内外有鬼旧病再发：鬼在内夜重、鬼在外夜必轻、内外有鬼人昏困要防旧病再来；代占最怕应持鬼、官鬼持身命亦倾；病因怕鬼旺相、子孙不宜克世、财忌飞克、父母交重病沉、大怕世空、又嫌月破、更嫌身世入墓

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼在内卦，夜里病重；鬼在外卦，夜里一定较轻；内外都有鬼，人昏沉困顿，要提防旧病再来侵扰。替别人占，最怕应爻持鬼；官鬼持了身，命也会倾倒。眉批说：鬼在内，夜里重；鬼在外，灾厄解除；内外都有鬼，危险；代占时应爻有鬼、又逢官鬼的墓，命一定倾倒。后面的散句又把它重述了一遍：病因怕（鬼）旺相；子孙是药，不宜克世；财是禄命，忌被飞爻克；父母交重病重；最怕世空，又嫌月破，更嫌身世入墓；内外有鬼旧病再发；代占应爻持鬼凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost in the inner trigram makes the illness heavy at night; a ghost in the outer trigram, at night it is surely lighter; with ghosts both inside and outside the person is faint and trapped, and one must beware of the old illness coming again to invade. In divining for another, most to be feared is the Other line holding the ghost; if the Pressure line holds the body, life too topples. The marginal comment says: ghost inside, heavy at night; ghost outside, calamity released; ghosts both inside and outside, dangerous; in divining for another, the Other with the ghost and the official tomb, life surely topples. The prose that follows restates it: for the cause of illness the ghost is feared when strong; Output is the medicine and should not control the Self; Wealth is the salary and life and fears the flying line controlling it; Resource crossing makes the illness heavy; most feared are the Self Void and Month-break, and more disliked still the body and Self entering a tomb; ghosts both inside and outside bring the old illness back; in divining for another, the Other line holding the ghost is ominous.

> [!warning] 打架
> 病在昼夜哪一头重，几家的依据不同。海底眼（LY.ILL.HDY.02）、黄金策的旧注和王洪绪（LY.ILL.HJC.12）都按官鬼在内卦还是外卦来分：鬼在内卦夜里重，鬼在外卦白天重（海底眼说「夜必轻」），内外都有鬼昼夜都不轻；易隐（LY.ILL.YY.15）则按官鬼的阴阳分：鬼属阴白天轻、夜里重，鬼属阳白天重、夜里轻。两个标准可以给出相反的结论（比如阳爻的鬼在内卦：前一种夜里重，后一种白天重），书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether an illness is heavier by day or by night: the books use different criteria. The Haidi Yan (LY.ILL.HDY.02), the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu (LY.ILL.HJC.12) all divide by whether the Pressure line is in the inner or the outer trigram: a ghost in the inner trigram is heavy at night, a ghost in the outer trigram heavy by day (the Haidi Yan says 'at night it is surely lighter'), and with ghosts both inside and outside neither day nor night is light; the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.15) divides by the yin or yang of the ghost: a yin ghost is light by day and heavy at night, a yang ghost heavy by day and light at night. The two criteria can give opposite conclusions (for example, a yang ghost in the inner trigram is heavy at night by the first and heavy by day by the second), and the books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 下面一段散句（「疾病病因，怕逢旺相……」）是对上面两首歌诀（LY.ILL.HDY.01、这一条）的复述，几乎逐句对应。「怕逢旺相」缺主语，按上下文（眉批「鬼怕旺相」）补作「（鬼）」。「更嫌身世入墓内犹重，官外尽凶」文字含混：「内犹重」按「里面的更重」译，「官外尽凶」按字面译作「官在外面就全凶」，和上一首歌诀眉批的「鬼外解厄」看上去不合，书里没有解释，这里不强作调和。「旧病再发」同黄金策「官藏世下，病起如前」（LY.ILL.HJC.07）是同一件事的另一个说法。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> *Note:* The prose that follows ('the cause of illness, fear meeting strength ...') restates the two verses above (LY.ILL.HDY.01 and this entry), almost sentence by sentence. 'Fears meeting strength' has no subject, and from the context (the marginal comment 'the ghost fears strength') it is supplied as 'the ghost'. 'Still more disliked is the body and Self entering a tomb, inside heavier still, with the official outside all ominous' is loose: 'inside heavier still' is rendered 'what is inside is heavier still', and 'with the official outside all ominous' is rendered literally; it looks at odds with the marginal comment on the previous verse, 'ghost outside, calamity released', and the book does not explain, so no reconciliation is forced here. 'The old illness flares again' is another way of saying the Huangjin Ce's 'the Pressure line hidden under the Self, the illness rises as before' (LY.ILL.HJC.07). The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.
