# 占病取用爻：自占以世爻、身爻、命爻为用，代占以应爻为用；占家亲祖妣用官鬼、父兄妻子各以所属；五属不上卦从《黄金策》分爻取用；奴占家主用五爻、占主母用二爻

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病取用爻：自己占，拿世爻、身爻、命爻做用；替别人占，拿应爻做用。占家里的亲人，祖父祖母用官鬼，父亲、兄弟、妻子、子女各用自己所属的那一类爻；这五类亲属如果不上卦，就按《黄金策》分爻的办法取用。奴仆丁口占家主，拿五爻做用；占主母，拿二爻做用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Taking the Subject line in divining illness: in divining for oneself, the Self line, the Body line and the destiny line are the Subject; in divining for another, the Other line is the Subject. In divining for kin of the household, grandfather and grandmother use the Pressure line, and father, brother, wife and child each use the relation to which they belong; if these five kinds of relative do not appear in the hexagram, take the Subject by the Huangjin Ce's division of the lines. A servant or hired hand divining for the master of the house takes the fifth line as the Subject, and for the mistress the second line.

> [!warning] 打架
> 代占取哪一爻：易隐说代占以应爻为用，但占家里的亲人又各用所属的六亲；黄金策的旧注和王洪绪对这一点也不一致，旧注说自占以世为主、代占以应为主，王洪绪说占夫用官、占妻用财、按所占的人取（见 LY.ILL.HJC.33）。增删卜易则按所占的人取用神，不取应爻。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line to take in divining for another: the Yi Yin says the Other line is the Subject in divining for another, yet for kin of the household it takes each relation as its own; the old commentary and Wang Hongxu on the Huangjin Ce also disagree on this, the old commentary saying the Self is the master in divining for oneself and the Other in divining for another, and Wang Hongxu taking Pressure for a husband and Wealth for a wife, by the person divined (see LY.ILL.HJC.33). The Zengshan Buyi takes the Subject by the person divined and does not take the Other line.

> [!note] 按
> 「五属」是父母、兄弟、妻财、子孙、官鬼这五类六亲；这里说的「黄金策分爻」是黄金策疾病章讲的按所占是谁取六亲的办法。「身爻」指卦身，「命爻」指本命所在的爻。
>
> *Note:* The 'five kinds' are the five relations Resource, Peer, Wealth, Output and Pressure; the 'Huangjin Ce division of the lines' is the way the Huangjin Ce's chapter on illness takes the relation according to who is divined. 'The Body line' means the hexagram Body and 'the destiny line' the line where the person's natal branch stands.
