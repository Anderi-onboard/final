# 水鬼的来历：生前谋利于水，或亡于江海风波汤卤，病丧于腰肾茶酒，凡司河泊舟楫，按水象考；出身显圣者亦可推而变化无穷

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生前靠水谋利的，或者死在江海风浪、开水卤水之中，以及病死在腰、肾，因茶、酒而死的，凡是掌管河泊舟船的，就按水象来考察。注说：凡是靠出身、来历显了圣的，也可以这样推，变化就无穷无尽了；大慈在南海显道，三官从龙神那里受生，都仿照这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* One who sought profit by water in life, or died in river and sea, wind and waves, or scalding water and brine, or of illness of the waist and kidneys, or over tea and wine, and whose charge is rivers, moorings, boats and oars, is examined by the Water image. The note says: those who manifested as holy beings from their origins can likewise be worked out, and then the variations are without end; Great Compassion manifested the Way in the Southern Sea, and the Three Officials received their birth from the Dragon Spirit; follow this.

> [!note] 按
> 「凡出身显圣」原文没有再解释，这里译成「凡是靠出身显了圣的」。「大慈」指大慈大悲的观世音；「三官生受龙神」的读法原文没有讲，这里按字面译。这一条和金的那一条（LY.ILL.YM.49）是平行的：性（谋利于水）、成（亡于江海风波）、司（河泊舟楫），最后一句提醒神的来历还有另一条路——靠出身显圣，那就没有一定之规了。
>
> *Note:* The original does not explain 'those who manifested as holy beings from their origins', and it is rendered as it stands. 'Great Compassion' is the Guanyin of great compassion and mercy; how 'the Three Officials received birth from the Dragon Spirit' is to be read is not stated, and it is translated literally. This entry runs parallel to the Metal one (LY.ILL.YM.49): nature (seeking profit by water), origin (dying in river and sea) and charge (rivers, moorings, boats); the last sentence warns that a spirit may also come by another road, manifesting from its origins, for which there is no fixed rule.
