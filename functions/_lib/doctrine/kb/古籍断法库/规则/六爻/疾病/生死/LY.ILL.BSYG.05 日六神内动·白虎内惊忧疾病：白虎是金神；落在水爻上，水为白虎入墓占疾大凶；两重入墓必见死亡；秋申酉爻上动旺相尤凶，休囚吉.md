# 日六神内动·白虎内惊忧疾病：白虎是金神；落在水爻上，水为白虎入墓占疾大凶；两重入墓必见死亡；秋申酉爻上动旺相尤凶，休囚吉

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日六神内动的歌说：白虎在内卦发动，忧惊、疾病。自注说：白虎是金神；白虎也可以落在水爻上，这时水爻就是白虎，入了墓，占病大凶；例如庚、辛日得乾卦，甲子水爻动，变辛丑土，水墓在辰，白虎又是金神，叫两重入墓，必见死亡；白虎在秋天申、酉爻上动，旺相，更凶，休囚则吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The song on the spirits of the Day moving within says: when the White Tiger moves in the inner trigram, there is worry, fright and illness. Its own note says: the White Tiger is the Metal spirit; it can also come on a Water line, and then the Water line is the White Tiger; if it enters the tomb, illness is greatly ominous; for example, on a Geng or Xin day, casting Qian, the Water line Jiazi moves and turns into Xinchou Earth, the Water tomb is at Chen, and since the White Tiger is also the Metal spirit, it is called entering the tomb twice, and death is sure to be seen; when the White Tiger moves on a Shen or You line in autumn it is thriving and strong and still more ominous, and when resting or trapped it is auspicious.

> [!note] 按
> 这是这部书自注里最长的一个病例。「是水为白虎」是说白虎这个神附在水爻上；白虎本来是金神，所以又叫「两重入墓」——按字面，是金神白虎、和它所附的水爻，两个都入了墓（水墓在辰）。例子里「内见巽」的「巽」书里没有解释，译文照字面。「秋申酉爻上动，为旺相尤凶，休囚吉」是说白虎旺的时候凶得更重，衰了反而吉；这是白虎自己的旺衰，不是病人的。断易天机里同一句歌诀（LY.ILL.DYTJ.38）后面只有今人的注号（1），没有这段自注。
>
> *Note:* This is the longest case on illness in this book's own notes. 'Water is the White Tiger' says that the spirit of the White Tiger is attached to a Water line; the White Tiger is by nature the Metal spirit, so it is also called 'entering the tomb twice' — literally, both the Metal spirit White Tiger and the Water line it is attached to enter the tomb (the Water tomb is at Chen). The 'Xun' of 'within is seen Xun' in the example is not explained in the book and is translated literally. 'Moving on a Shen or You line in autumn, thriving and strong and especially ominous; resting or trapped, auspicious' says that the White Tiger is more ominous when it is strong and, when it is weak, on the contrary auspicious; this is the strength of the White Tiger itself and not of the sick person. The same line of the song in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.38) has after it only a modern note number (1) and not this note.
