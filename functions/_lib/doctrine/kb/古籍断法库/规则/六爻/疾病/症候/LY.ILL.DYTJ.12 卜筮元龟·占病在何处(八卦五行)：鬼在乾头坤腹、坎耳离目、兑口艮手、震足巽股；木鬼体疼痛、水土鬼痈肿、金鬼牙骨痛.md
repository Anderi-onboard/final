# 卜筮元龟·占病在何处(八卦五行)：鬼在乾头坤腹、坎耳离目、兑口艮手、震足巽股；木鬼体疼痛、水土鬼痈肿、金鬼牙骨痛

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼在乾是头，在坤病在腹；坎要患耳，离伤目；兑要忧口，艮在手上；震要防足，巽患在股。木爻是鬼，身体疼痛；水、土爻持着，必生痈肿；克象若说是金鬼，以此类推，是牙和骨头痛。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ghost in Qian is the head, in Kun the illness is in the belly; Kan must mean the ears, Li injures the eyes; Dui: worry for the mouth; Gen: the hands; Zhen: guard the feet; Xun: trouble in the thighs. A Wood line as the ghost: pain in the body; Water or Earth holding it: there will be abscess and swelling; if the controlling image is a Metal ghost, by analogy it is pain in the teeth and bones.

> [!warning] 打架
> 八卦各管身体哪一处，几处说法不同。这一块是「乾头、坤腹、坎耳、离目、兑口、艮手、震足、巽股」；孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）是巽肠、艮手，其余同；阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.02）是巽手、艮鼻，其余同；卜筮正宗痘疹章（LY.ILL.BSZZ.23）是巽股、艮手，同这一块。乾头、坤腹、坎耳、离目、兑口、震足几家一致；巽有股、肠、手三说，艮有手、鼻两说。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which part of the body each of the eight trigrams governs is put differently in several places. This block has 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Li the eyes, Dui the mouth, Gen the hands, Zhen the feet, Xun the thighs'; Sun Bin's song (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) has Xun the bowels and Gen the hands, the rest the same; the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.02) has Xun the hands and Gen the nose, the rest the same; and the smallpox chapter of the Bushi Zhengzong (LY.ILL.BSZZ.23) has Xun the thighs and Gen the hands, as here. Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Li the eyes, Dui the mouth and Zhen the feet are agreed; Xun has three readings (thighs, bowels, hands) and Gen two (hands, nose). The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 前四句按八卦配身体：乾头、坤腹、坎耳、离目、兑口、艮手、震足、巽股。后四句是五行配症候：木鬼疼痛、水土鬼痈肿、金鬼牙骨痛。「克象若言是金鬼」的「克象」不确定所指，按字面译；意思大概是说到金鬼时，照这个类推。五行症候这一半和孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.04）、天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.03）是同一类，各家说法大同小异。
>
> *Note:* The first four lines match the trigrams to the body: Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Li the eyes, Dui the mouth, Gen the hands, Zhen the feet, Xun the thighs. The last four lines match the five phases to symptoms: a Wood ghost pain, Water and Earth ghosts abscess and swelling, a Metal ghost pain in teeth and bones. What the 'controlling image' of 'if the controlling image is said to be a Metal ghost' points to is not certain and it is rendered literally; it probably means that when a Metal ghost is spoken of, one reasons by analogy. The half on the five phases and symptoms is of the same kind as Sun Bin's song (LY.ILL.DYTJ.04) and the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.03), and the books are much alike.
