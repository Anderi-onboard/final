# 最吉者官安用旺、最凶者鬼旺忌兴：官鬼为痘花，不宜伤损也不宜动；遇刑冲克害伏藏是险逆之症，用神旺相而官鬼安静得生扶拱合是顺症

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 最吉的是官爻安静、用神旺相，最凶的是鬼爻旺相、忌神兴旺发动。官鬼就是痘花，不适合受到伤损，也不适合发动，动了恐怕会变坏；如果遇到刑、冲、克、害、伏藏这些象，是凶险不顺的症候，即使勉强出得起来，也难以善终；用神旺相、官鬼安静又得到生、扶、拱、合的，是顺症，不用忧虑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Most auspicious is Pressure at rest and the Subject strong; most ominous is Pressure strong and the Adversary rising. The Pressure line is the pox flowers themselves: it should not be harmed and should not move, for if it moves it may turn bad; if it meets punishment, clash, control, harm or hiding, it is a dangerous and adverse condition, and even if it barely erupts it is hard to bring to a good end; if the Subject is strong and the Pressure line is at rest and is generated, supported, embraced or combined, it is a favourable condition and there is nothing to fear.

> [!note] 按
> 经文的「官安用旺」「鬼旺忌兴」里，「官」「鬼」都是官鬼爻，「用」是用神，「忌」是忌神。「起发」是痘起出来、长起来，「收成」「收功」是痘出完、结好了。
>
> *Note:* In the verse's 'Pressure at rest and the Subject strong' and 'Pressure strong and the Adversary rising', 'guan' and 'gui' both mean the Pressure line, 'yong' the Subject and 'ji' the Adversary. 'Erupt' (qifa) is for the pox to come out and grow, and 'completion' (shoucheng, shougong) for the pox to run its course and heal.
