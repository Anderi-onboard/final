# 六十四卦吉凶歌·无鬼七卦：小畜未济家人旅讼益颐七者皆无鬼、问病可喜亦可悲

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 小畜、未济、家人、旅、讼、益、颐这七个卦，卦里都没有鬼；占病的时候，可喜也可悲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Xiaochu, Weiji, Jiaren, Lü, Song, Yi (Increase) and Yi (Nourishment): in all these seven hexagrams there is no ghost; in asking after illness it may be glad and it may be sad.

## 《断易天机·六十四卦吉凶歌》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 11 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The song of the good and ill fortune of the sixty-four hexagrams", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 小畜未济家人卦， | 小畜、未济、家人这几个卦， | Xiaochu, Weiji and Jiaren, |
> | 更同旅讼并益颐， | 再加上旅、讼、益、颐， | together with Lü, Song, Yi (Increase) and Yi (Nourishment), |
> | 七者之中皆无鬼（3）， | 这七个卦里都没有鬼， | in all these seven there is no ghost, |
> | 问病可喜亦可悲。 | 问病的时候，可喜也可悲。 | in asking after illness it may be glad and it may be sad. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是《六十四卦吉凶歌》里的一段：歌里先说哪些卦没有父母、哪些卦没有兄弟、哪些卦没有妻财、哪些卦没有子孙，最后是这七个卦「皆无鬼」。「（3）」是今人加的注号，那条注不收。「问病可喜亦可悲」为什么可喜、为什么可悲，歌里没有说：没有鬼，可以说没有病或没有祟（喜），也可以说没有能祈求的鬼神、没有病的出口（悲）；后一种读法和这一章孙膑断疾病歌「卦中无鬼病难医」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.02）、「占病无鬼，必无叩告之门」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.24）一致，前一种读法和易林补遗「鬼空亡或不上卦决无鬼祟」（LY.ILL.EL.29）一致，这里只照原文译，不作选择。「益」「颐」拼音都是 Yi，英文分别标出。
>
> *Note:* This is a passage of the 'Song of the good and ill fortune of the sixty-four hexagrams': the song first says which hexagrams have no Resource, which no Peer, which no Wealth and which no Output, and last that in these seven hexagrams there is 'no ghost in all'. The '(3)' is a modern annotation mark and that annotation is not quoted. Why 'in asking after illness it may be glad and it may be sad' the song does not say: with no ghost one can say there is no illness or no curse (glad), or that there is no spirit to appeal to and no way out for the illness (sad); the second reading agrees with 'with no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to treat' in Sun Bin's song in this chapter (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02) and 'divining illness with no ghost, there is surely no door to knock and pray at' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.24), and the first agrees with the Yilin Buyi's 'a ghost Void or not in the hexagram surely has no ghostly curse' (LY.ILL.EL.29); here it is only rendered as in the original and no choice is made. The two characters 'yi' and 'yi' have the same pinyin, and the English marks them separately.
