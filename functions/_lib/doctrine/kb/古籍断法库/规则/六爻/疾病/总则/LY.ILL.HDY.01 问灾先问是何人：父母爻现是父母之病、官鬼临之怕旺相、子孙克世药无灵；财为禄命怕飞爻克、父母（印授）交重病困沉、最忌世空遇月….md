# 问灾先问是何人：父母爻现是父母之病、官鬼临之怕旺相、子孙克世药无灵；财为禄命怕飞爻克、父母（印授）交重病困沉、最忌世空遇月破、又嫌墓爻发动损身

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占问灾病，先要问是什么人得病：父母爻出现，说的就是父母的事；官鬼临上去，愁它旺相；子孙克世爻，吃药没有灵验。财是禄命，怕被飞爻克制（原文「忘非克」，含混）；父母（印绶）交重发动，病重而困顿；最忌世爻落空又遇月破，也嫌墓爻发动伤了身体。眉批说：父亲得病要看病（官鬼）；鬼怕旺相；子克世爻，服药不灵；财是禄命，印绶发动命沉，世空、月破、墓绝都凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about an illness or calamity, first ask whose it is: if Resource appears, it is the parents' matter that is set out; if Pressure sits on it, one worries about its being strong; Output controlling the Self line makes medicine ineffective. Wealth is the salary and life and fears being controlled by the flying line (the text reads 'wang fei ke', which is unclear); Resource (the seal ribbon) crossing and moving heavily makes the illness heavy and trapped; most to be avoided is the Self Void together with Month-break, and the tomb line moving to harm the body is also disliked. The marginal comment says: for a father's illness, look at the illness; the ghost fears strength; if Output controls the Self line, taking medicine is ineffective; Wealth is the salary and life, the seal ribbon moving means life sinks, and the Self Void, Month-break, tomb and Extinction are all ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 这是海底眼「疾病」一节的头两首歌诀，每首后面带一条眉批。「父母逢之父母陈」的「陈」按字面译作「set out」，大意是这一爻出现就说明占的是这一层亲人；眉批「父病看病」很简，按字面译。「财为禄命忘非克」的「忘非克」不成话：下面散句（LY.ILL.HDY.02）同一句写作「忌飞克之」，所以这里按「怕被飞爻克」译，并把原字标在译文里。「印授」就是印绶，是父母爻的别名，眉批「印授命沉」按字面译作「the seal ribbon moving means life sinks」。「世空」「墓发」「墓绝」照字面译。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> *Note:* These are the first two verses of the Haidi Yan's section on illness, each followed by a marginal comment. In 'if Resource meets it, the parents are set out' the word 'chen' is rendered literally as 'set out', the sense being that this line's appearing shows which relation the divination is about; the marginal comment 'for a father's illness, look at the illness' is very short and is rendered literally. 'Wang fei ke' in 'Wealth is the salary and life, wang fei ke' does not make sense: the prose sentence below (LY.ILL.HDY.02) writes the same sentence as 'it fears the flying line controlling it', so it is translated here as 'fears being controlled by the flying line', and the original characters are marked in the translation. 'Yin shou' is the seal ribbon, another name for the Resource line, and the marginal comment 'the seal ribbon means life sinks' is rendered literally as 'the seal ribbon moving means life sinks'. 'Self Void', 'the tomb moving' and 'tomb and Extinction' are rendered literally. The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.
