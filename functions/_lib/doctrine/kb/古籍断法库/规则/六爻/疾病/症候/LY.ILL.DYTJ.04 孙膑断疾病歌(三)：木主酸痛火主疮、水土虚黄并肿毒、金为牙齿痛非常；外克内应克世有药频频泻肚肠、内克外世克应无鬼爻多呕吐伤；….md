# 孙膑断疾病歌(三)：木主酸痛火主疮、水土虚黄并肿毒、金为牙齿痛非常；外克内应克世有药频频泻肚肠、内克外世克应无鬼爻多呕吐伤；更看五鬼配五脏病源有短长

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木主酸痛，火主疮，水和土主虚黄和肿毒，金是牙齿痛得厉害，务必仔细推详。外卦克内卦、应克世，有药也频频泻肚肠；内卦克外卦、世克应，没有鬼爻的话多是呕吐所伤。还要看五鬼配五脏，病源是长是短就在这里。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wood governs aching pain and Fire governs sores; Water and Earth govern deficiency with yellowing and swollen toxins; Metal is toothache beyond the ordinary; work it out carefully. The outer trigram controlling the inner, the Other controlling the Self: there is medicine, yet he purges from the bowels again and again; the inner controlling the outer, the Self controlling the Other: with no Pressure line it is mostly injury by vomiting. Look further at the five ghosts matched to the five viscera: there is where the source of the illness, short or long, is found.

> [!note] 按
> 「五鬼」是五行各自的鬼（金鬼、木鬼、水鬼、火鬼、土鬼）；「五鬼配五脏」是鬼属哪一行、就配哪一脏来看病源，歌里没有把配法列出来。「有药频频泻肚肠」可以读成服了药反而频频泻，也可以读成有药也止不住泻，这里按字面译。「无鬼爻多呕吐伤」是说内克外、世克应而卦里没有鬼爻时，多是呕吐；洞林秘诀一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.16）有同样的一对：我克他先呕逆、他克我泻为伤。「虚黄」按「虚弱发黄」译。
>
> *Note:* The 'five ghosts' are the ghost of each of the five phases (the Metal, Wood, Water, Fire and Earth ghost); 'the five ghosts matched to the five viscera' means looking at the source of the illness by which viscus the phase of the ghost matches, and the song does not list the matching. 'There is medicine, yet he purges again and again' can be read as purging the more after taking medicine, or as medicine unable to stop the purging, and it is rendered literally here. 'With no Pressure line, mostly injury by vomiting' says that when the inner controls the outer and the Self controls the Other and the hexagram has no Pressure line it is mostly vomiting; the Dongling Mijue passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.16) has the same pair: when I control him there is vomiting first, when he controls me the purging is the harm. 'Xu huang' is rendered 'deficiency with yellowing'.
