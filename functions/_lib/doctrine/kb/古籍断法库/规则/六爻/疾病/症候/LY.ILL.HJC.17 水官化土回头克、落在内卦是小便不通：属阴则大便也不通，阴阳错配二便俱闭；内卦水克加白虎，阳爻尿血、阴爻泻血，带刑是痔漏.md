# 水官化土回头克、落在内卦是小便不通：属阴则大便也不通，阴阳错配二便俱闭；内卦水克加白虎，阳爻尿血、阴爻泻血，带刑是痔漏

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水行的官鬼动了，变出土来回头克它，又落在本宫的内卦（下三爻）：小便不通；这一爻属阴，大便也不通；阳宫里是阴的象、阴宫里是阳的象，大小便都不通。内卦的水爻起克、又动、又加白虎：阳爻是尿血，阴爻是泻血（白虎是血神）；再带刑是痔漏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Water Pressure line that moves and changes into Earth, which turns back to control it, and falls in the inner trigram of the home palace (the lower three lines): the urine is blocked; if the line is yin, the stool is blocked too; a yang palace with a yin image, or a yin palace with a yang image, blocks both. If the Water line in the inner trigram controls, moves and has the White Tiger added: a yang line means blood in the urine, a yin line bloody stool (the White Tiger is the spirit of blood); with punishment added it is piles and fistula.

> [!warning] 打架
> 水官化土在哪里才是小便不通：旧注说在「本宫内卦」（下三爻），王洪绪说在「本宫初爻」（最下面一爻）。范围一个宽一个窄，书里没有说哪个对。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Where a Water Pressure line changing into Earth means blocked urine: the old commentary says in the 'inner trigram of the home palace' (the lower three lines), Wang Hongxu says on the 'first line of the home palace' (the lowest line). One range is wide and one narrow, and the book does not say which is right.

> [!note] 按
> 「属阴」书里没有说属什么阴；按上下文是这一爻是阴爻（下一句的「阳爻、阴爻」说的也是这一爻）。「阳宫阴象，阴宫阳象」是宫的阴阳和这一爻的阴阳相反。「痔漏」是痔疮和肛瘘。「回头土克」是水鬼动出来的土反过来克它自己。
>
> *Note:* The book does not say what 'belonging to yin' refers to; from the context it is that the line is a yin line (the 'yang line, yin line' of the next sentence also speaks of this line). 'A yang palace with a yin image' means that the yin or yang of the palace is the opposite of the yin or yang of this line. 'Piles and fistula' are haemorrhoids and anal fistula. 'Earth turning back to control' means the Earth that the Water Pressure line moves out into controls that line in return.
