# 何知章·疾病：螣蛇入火瘟气麻痘病、入金怕逢病半死、爻入水病来伤小口退一双；白虎临子孙位子孙今年忌、临财妻妾恐人灾殃、临父母父母切忌讳、临兄弟不久入泉乡；玄武牛猪空亡瘴死为瘟疫；有长病人八纯卦、有妻病身克财、重丧鬼化财、有长不安青龙入水患疮

六爻 · 疾病 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 螣蛇入火，主瘟气、麻疹、痘疹的病；入金，怕遇上病得半死；若爻入水，病来伤人，小孩要退一双。白虎临在子孙位，子孙今年忌；临在财、妻妾位，恐怕有人灾殃；临在父母位，父母切忌；临在兄弟宫，不久入黄泉。玄武在牛、猪空亡处当着，瘴气死人成为瘟疫。有长期生病的人，是八纯卦；有妻子病，是身克财；重丧，是鬼化财；有长辈不安，是青龙入水、患疮；有聋人，是兑卦入乾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Coiling Snake entering Fire: plague, measles and smallpox; entering Metal: one fears meeting an illness half deadly; if the line enters Water the illness comes to injure, and the children retreat by a pair. The White Tiger sitting on the Output place: Output must beware this year; on Wealth and the wife's or concubine's place: fear a calamity to a person; on Resource: the parents must beware; on Peer: before long he enters the Yellow Springs. The Dark Tortoise standing at Chou and Hai Void: miasma deaths become an epidemic. A long-standing sick person: a pure hexagram; a wife's illness: the Body controlling Wealth; a double mourning: the ghost changing into Wealth; an elder unwell: the Azure Dragon entering Water, with sores; a deaf person: Dui changing into Qian.

> [!note] 按
> 《何知章》是一首「怎么从卦里知道家里的事」的歌：每一句是「有某某，是某卦某爻」。这里只收说到病、瘟疫、丧的几处。第一联「鰧蛇入火火烧定，瘟气麻痘病」和「入金怕逢病半死……若爻入水病来伤，小口退一双」是螣蛇落在不同五行爻上的情形（火、金、水），原文里前后隔着说家宅、蛇伤、野兽的几句，不收；「入金怕逢病半死」的「入金」承上文「鰧蛇入」。「小口退一双」是小孩要走（死）一对，字面译。第二联是白虎临六亲位：临子孙位子孙忌、临财妻妾恐人灾殃、临父母父母忌讳、临兄弟不久入泉乡（入黄泉）。第三联「牛猪空亡玄武当，瘴死为瘟疫」是玄武在牛（丑）、猪（亥）空亡的位置上，瘴气死人成瘟疫。第四、五联是「怎么知道」的句子：「有长病人」是八纯卦，「有妻病」是身克财，「重丧」是鬼化财，「有长不安」是青龙入水患疮，「有聋人」是兑卦入乾。「有长病人，八纯卦也」和火珠林「凡得八纯游魂卦，病者决主沉重」（LY.ILL.HZL.05）是同一类。这一章的「有多事不成……」等几句不在病的范围，不收。
>
> *Note:* The 'chapter on how it is known' is a song on how to know from a hexagram the affairs of a family, and each line is 'there is such-and-such: it is such-and-such hexagram or line'. Here only the passages that speak of illness, plague and mourning are taken. The first couplet, 'the Coiling Snake entering Fire, fire is sure, plague, measles and smallpox' and 'entering Metal one fears meeting an illness half deadly ... if the line enters Water the illness comes to injure, the children retreat by a pair', is the Coiling Snake falling on lines of different phases (Fire, Metal, Water), and in the original the lines about the house, snake bites and wild beasts fall in between them and are not taken; the 'entering Metal' of 'entering Metal one fears meeting an illness half deadly' follows the earlier 'the Coiling Snake enters'. 'The children retreat by a pair' means a pair of children go (die), rendered literally. The second couplet is the White Tiger on the places of the six relations: on Output Output must beware, on Wealth and the wife's or concubine's place fear a calamity to a person, on Resource the parents must beware, on Peer before long he enters the Yellow Springs. The third couplet, 'the Dark Tortoise standing at Chou and Hai Void, miasma deaths are an epidemic', is the Dark Tortoise at the places of the ox (Chou) and the pig (Hai) Void, miasma killing people and becoming an epidemic. The fourth and fifth couplets are sentences of 'how it is known': 'a long-standing sick person' is a pure hexagram, 'a wife's illness' is the Body controlling Wealth, 'a double mourning' is the ghost changing into Wealth, 'an elder unwell' is the Azure Dragon entering Water with sores, and 'a deaf person' is Dui changing into Qian. 'A long-standing sick person: a pure hexagram' is of the same kind as the Huozhu Lin's 'whoever gets a pure or Wandering Soul hexagram, the sick person is surely heavy' (LY.ILL.HZL.05). The other lines of this chapter, 'many affairs not accomplished ...' and so on, are outside illness and are not taken.
