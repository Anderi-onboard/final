# 占疾病要慎而详：大端有四——占死生、占病症、占医药、占鬼崇；病何日起、病何处得是疾病的附占（游南子）

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 人不幸有了病来占，不可以不谨慎、不周详。大的方面有四件事：占他是死是活，占他是什么病症，占医和药，占有没有鬼崇；至于病是哪一天起的、在哪里得的，是疾病附带要占的事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a person is unfortunate enough to fall ill and divines about it, one must be careful and thorough. There are four main heads: whether he will live or die, what the illness is, doctors and medicine, and ghostly influences; when the illness began and where it was caught are subsidiary matters of illness.

## 游南子(《易隐》引)

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2976 行 · Master Younan (quoted in the Yi Yin)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 游南子曰，夫人不幸有疾而占之，不可不慎而详也。 | 游南子说：人不幸有了病来占，不可以不谨慎、不周详。 | Master Younan says: when a person is unfortunate enough to fall ill and divines about it, one cannot but be careful and thorough. |
> | 然大端有四，占其死生，占其病症，占其医药，占其鬼崇而已。 | 然而大的方面有四件：占他的死活，占他的病症，占他的医药，占有没有鬼崇，仅此而已。 | But the main heads are four: divine whether he lives or dies, divine the symptoms, divine the doctors and medicine, and divine the ghostly influences, and that is all. |
> | 若乃病何日起，病何处得，亦疾病之附占也。 | 至于病是哪一天起的、在哪里得的，也是疾病的附带占问。 | As for what day the illness began and where it was caught, these too are subsidiary questions of illness. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「病症」在这里是病是什么样的症候，「鬼崇」是鬼神作祟。易隐这一章正是按这四件事排的：二、生死；三、病症；六、医药；七、鬼崇，另有四、病因，五、饮食，八、病起何方，九、病起何年月日。增删卜易的李我平也把来问的事分成吉凶、犯了什么鬼神、病源三类（见 LY.ILL.ZS.39）。
>
> *Note:* 'Bingzheng' here is the pattern of symptoms the illness shows, and 'guisui' is ghosts and spirits making trouble. The chapter of the Yi Yin is arranged by just these heads: 2 life and death, 3 symptoms, 6 doctors and medicine, 7 ghostly influences, with 4 the cause of the illness, 5 food and drink, 8 where the illness arose and 9 when it arose in addition. Li Woping in the Zengshan Buyi likewise sorts the matters asked into fortune, which ghost or spirit was offended, and the source of the illness (see LY.ILL.ZS.39).
