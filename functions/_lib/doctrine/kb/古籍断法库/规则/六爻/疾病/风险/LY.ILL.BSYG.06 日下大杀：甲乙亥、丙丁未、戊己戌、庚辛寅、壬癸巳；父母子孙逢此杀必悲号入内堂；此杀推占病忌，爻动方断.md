# 日下大杀：甲乙亥、丙丁未、戊己戌、庚辛寅、壬癸巳；父母子孙逢此杀必悲号入内堂；此杀推占病忌，爻动方断

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日下大杀按日干排：甲乙日在亥（猪），丙丁日在未（羊），戊己日在戌（狗），庚辛日在寅（虎），壬癸日在巳（蛇）；父母、子孙遇到这个杀，必有悲伤号哭进到内堂。自注说：占病要忌这个杀，爻动了才断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Great Killing under the Day is laid out by the Day stem: Hai (the pig) on Jia and Yi days, Wei (the sheep) on Bing and Ding days, Xu (the dog) on Wu and Ji days, Yin (the tiger) on Geng and Xin days, Si (the snake) on Ren and Gui days; if Resource or Output meets this killing, wailing and grief are sure to enter the inner hall. Its own note says: this killing is to be avoided in an illness divination, and is judged only when the line moves.

> [!note] 按
> 这一本的歌诀里没有「壬癸蛇为大杀方」那一句（卜筮全书、断易天机有），第五个日干（壬癸巳）是在自注里补出来的。五个日干的位置各书一致，但各书说的用处不同：卜筮全书的神煞歌例（第 10073 行起）只写「凡事忌之」；断易天机（第 323 行）写「父母兄弟并妻子，杀临杀动主悲伤」；易隐的日煞表（第 1023 行）写「占家宅凶百事不吉」；只有大易断例这里的自注点明「占病忌」。「兮」「处」是歌诀里的衬字。「悲号入内堂」是丧事的征兆。同一个歌诀前面的「杀阴」「大杀」（按月排的）是另外两个煞，讲的是亡殁、伤亡，没有提病，这里不收。
>
> *Note:* In this copy of the song the line 'Ren and Gui: the snake is the Great Killing' (found in the Bushi Quanshu and the Duanyi Tianji) is absent, and the fifth day stem (Ren and Gui: Si) is supplied in the note. The positions for the five day stems agree among the books, but the books name different uses: the verses of spirit-stars in the Bushi Quanshu (from line 10073) write only 'avoid it in everything'; the Duanyi Tianji (line 323) writes 'Resource, Peer and wife and children: where the killing arrives or moves, it means grief'; the table of day stars of the Yi Yin (line 1023) writes 'in divining the household, ominous, and nothing is auspicious'; only the note here in the Dayi Duanli makes plain 'avoid it in illness'. 'Xi' and 'chu' are filler words of the song. 'Wailing and grief entering the inner hall' is an omen of mourning. The 'Yin Killing' and 'Great Killing' laid out by month earlier in the same song are two other killings that speak of death and injury and make no mention of illness, and are not taken here.
