# 金为肺腑增疼增嗽非宜、火属心经发疮发斑大忌：官鬼属金毒发肺部主身痛咳嗽须防鼻偏；官鬼属火毒发心经，起初防发斑、继防发疮及舌头宿硬；衰静者轻、旺动者重

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金对应肺腑，加重疼痛、加重咳嗽都不相宜；火属心经，发疮、发斑是大忌。具体说，官鬼属金，毒发在肺部，主身体作痛或咳嗽，要防鼻子歪偏；官鬼属火，毒发在心经，是火毒的症候，起初要防发斑，接着要防发疮和舌头长久僵硬。衰而静的轻，旺而动的更重。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Metal is the lungs and viscera, and increased pain or increased cough is unfavourable; Fire belongs to the heart channel, and breaking out in sores or spots is a great taboo. Specifically, if the Pressure line is Metal, the poison breaks out in the lungs, bringing bodily pain or cough, and one must guard against a crooked nose; if the Pressure line is Fire, the poison breaks out in the heart channel and is a fire-poison condition; at first guard against spots, then against sores and a stiffened tongue that stays so. If weak and still it is light; if strong and moving it is heavier still.
