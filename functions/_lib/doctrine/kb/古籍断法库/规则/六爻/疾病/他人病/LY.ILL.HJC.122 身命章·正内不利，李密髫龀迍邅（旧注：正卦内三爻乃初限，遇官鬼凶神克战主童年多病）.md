# 身命章·正内不利，李密髫龀迍邅（旧注：正卦内三爻乃初限，遇官鬼凶神克战主童年多病）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》身命章说：「正卦的内卦不利，李密儿童时期困顿坎坷。」旧注解释：正卦的内卦三爻是初限（早年）；如果遇到官鬼、凶神来克战，主童年多病，像李密九岁才能走路，是因为幼年多病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the person and fate says: 'With the inner trigram of the main hexagram unfavourable, Li Mi's childhood was one of hardship.' The old commentary explains: the inner three lines of the main hexagram are the first period of life; if Pressure or an ominous spirit attacks it, it means many illnesses in childhood, as Li Mi could walk only at nine because he was often ill in his early years.

> [!note] 按
> 身命章把一个人的一生分成三限：正卦内三爻是初限（约十五年），正卦外三爻加支卦内三爻是中限，支卦外三爻是末限（旧注 L6059）。这里讲初限遇官鬼凶神，童年多病。王洪绪对同一句的注是「正卦者，卜卦前之事」，把正卦读成卜卦之前的事，没有提到多病，所以没有收他的。李密是晋代人，字令伯，幼年多病，九岁才能走路，是旧注举的例子。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on the person and fate divides a person's life into three periods: the inner three lines of the main hexagram are the first period (about fifteen years), the outer three lines of the main hexagram with the inner three of the branch hexagram are the middle period, and the outer three lines of the branch hexagram the last period (the old commentary, line 6059). Here, Pressure or an ominous spirit in the first period means many illnesses in childhood. Wang Hongxu's note on the same line reads 'the main hexagram is what came before the divination' and takes the main hexagram as events before the divination, with no mention of illness, so his note is not taken. Li Mi, of the Jin, courtesy name Lingbo, was often ill as a child and could walk only at nine; he is the old commentary's example.
