# 太岁鬼临是祖传旧例、日辰官并是口许初心：太岁、日辰都带鬼，是眼下许下酬谢祖辈老规矩的愿还没办完

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁上带鬼，是祖辈传下来的老规矩；日辰上并着官，是当初口头许下的心愿。如果太岁、日辰都带鬼，就是眼下许下要酬谢祖辈老规矩的愿，还没有办完。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost on the Tai Sui is an old custom handed down from the ancestors; Pressure joined to the Day is the original intention of a spoken vow. If the Tai Sui and the Day both carry the ghost, then a vow has been made for now to repay the ancestral custom, and it is not yet completed.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3403 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若太岁日辰俱官，则目下许酬祖先例未完。 | 如果太岁、日辰都是官，就是眼下许下要酬谢祖先老规矩的愿还没有办完。 | If the Tai Sui and the Day are both Pressure, then a vow made for now to repay the custom of the ancestors has not been completed. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文两句是分开的：太岁带鬼是「祖传旧例」，日辰官并是「口许初心」。旧注和王洪绪都把它们合成一条，只讲太岁、日辰都带鬼的情形，没有分别讲各自的意思。「日下」旧注写作「日下」，王洪绪写作「目下」，是字形相近的两种写法，都译作眼下。
>
> *Note:* The verse's two lines are separate: the Tai Sui carrying the ghost is 'an ancestral custom', and Pressure joined to the Day is 'the original intention of a spoken vow'. The old commentary and Wang Hongxu both merge them into one entry and treat only the case of both the Tai Sui and the Day carrying the ghost, without explaining each separately. The old commentary writes 'ri xia' and Wang Hongxu 'mu xia', two close-looking forms, both rendered 'for now'.
