# 出花吉凶全在子孙爻：所问各事应分占，不可一卦兼断；子孙临日月或被日月动爻生扶、动而化吉、不受刑冲克、爻不乱动，花虽密可全生，痘虽陷仍收功效

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 上面问的几件事应当分开占，不能用一个卦兼着断，大体上全看子孙爻。子孙临着日月、又被日月和动爻生扶，动了变出吉的，不受刑伤冲克，爻又不乱动的，花虽然密，也可以保全性命；痘就算塌陷了，仍然能收到效果。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The matters asked above should be divined separately and cannot be judged from one hexagram together; in general everything depends on the Output line. If Output sits on the Day or Month, is generated and supported by the Day, Month or a moving line, moves and changes into something lucky, suffers no punishment, harm, clash or control, and the lines do not move wildly, then though the flowers are dense the child can survive whole, and even if the pox sink, the treatment still takes effect.

> *Note:* This entry brings the several matters of the coming of the flowers (when, how dense, fortune) back to the Output line: LY.ILL.ZS.19 uses the Pressure line for the time, but the fortune returns to Output. The 'Qian note' at the end of the chapter on smallpox is a modern addition and is not quoted here.
