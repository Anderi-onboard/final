# 疾病论·神鬼之由(二)：日值贵官可告城隍、岁临官贵宜求五道家堂；兑象官兴急还口愿、火爻鬼动速祀灶君；离鬼休囚持世祈斗、乾鬼旺相克身告天神还素愿；衰官外动别宫来是外崇、休鬼内兴本宫动可辨家亲

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里接着按日、岁、五行和宫来断神鬼：日辰值着贵官，可以向当地的城隍祷告；太岁临官贵，宜求五道和家堂；兑象官鬼兴旺，赶紧还口愿；火爻鬼动，赶快祭灶君；离鬼休囚持世，要祈禳北斗；乾鬼旺相克身，宜向天神还素愿；衰官在外卦、从别宫来，是外来作祟；休鬼在内卦、本宫动，可以辨出是家亲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness goes on judging spirits by the Day, the Year, the elements and the palace: with the Day on the noble officer, one may petition the City God of the district; with the Year on the noble officer, it is fitting to beseech the Five Roads and the household shrine; with Pressure in the Dui image rising, hurry to repay a spoken vow; with a Fire ghost moving, quickly sacrifice to the Hearth Lord; a Li ghost resting or confined and holding the Self line, pray to the Dipper; a Qian ghost strong and controlling the Body, petition the heavenly spirits and repay a vegetarian vow; a weak Pressure line moving outside and coming from another palace is an outside haunting; a resting ghost rising inside and moving from the home palace can be identified as the family's own kin.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段里「日值贵官」「岁临官贵」的贵官、官贵是有贵人星的官鬼一类（书里没有展开）。「城隍」是城隍神，「五道」是五道神，「家堂」是家里供的神堂，「灶君」是灶神，「素愿」是许下的吃素、斋戒一类的愿，「口愿」是口头许的愿。「花穷」两字书里没有解释，译文只音译。「家亲」是家里已故的亲人的鬼魂。「衰官外动别宫来」「休鬼内兴本宫动」是用鬼爻的旺衰、内外、本宫来分外来的祟和家里的祟。
>
> *Note:* In this passage the 'noble officer' and 'officer noble' of 'the Day on the noble officer' and 'the Year on the officer noble' are a Pressure line carrying a noble star (the book does not go further). The 'City God' is the god of the city, the 'Five Roads' are the Five Road gods, the 'household shrine' is the shrine of spirits kept at home, the 'Hearth Lord' is the kitchen god, a 'vegetarian vow' is a vow of vegetarian fasting, and a 'spoken vow' is one made aloud. The two characters rendered 'Huaqiong' are not explained in the book and are only transliterated. 'The family's own kin' are the ghosts of deceased relatives of the household. 'A weak Pressure line moving outside from another palace' and 'a resting ghost rising inside and moving from the home palace' use the strength or weakness of the ghost line, inside or outside, and the home palace to tell a haunting from outside from a haunting of the household.
