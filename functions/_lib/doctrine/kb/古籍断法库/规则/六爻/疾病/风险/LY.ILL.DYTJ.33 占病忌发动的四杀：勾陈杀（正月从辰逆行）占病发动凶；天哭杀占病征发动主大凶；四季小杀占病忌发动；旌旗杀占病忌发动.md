# 占病忌发动的四杀：勾陈杀（正月从辰逆行）占病发动凶；天哭杀占病征发动主大凶；四季小杀占病忌发动；旌旗杀占病忌发动

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 勾陈杀：正月从辰起，逆行向卯，轮十二个位置；占病时它发动，凶。天哭杀：正五九月是羊（未）、二六十月是猴（申）、三七十一月是鸡（酉）、四八十二月是犬（戌）；若临在身、世上，就忧愁哭泣；占病时征兆发动，主大凶。四季小杀：春忌羊头（未）、夏是龙（辰）、秋牛（丑）、冬犬（戌）；占病忌它发动。旌旗杀：春兔（卯）、夏鼠（子）、秋鸡（酉）、冬马（午）；占病忌它发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Hook Earth killing: it starts from Chen in the first month and goes backward towards Mao, rotating through the twelve places; in divining illness, if it moves it is ominous. The Weeping killing: in the first, fifth and ninth months the sheep (Wei), in the second, sixth and tenth the monkey (Shen), in the third, seventh and eleventh the rooster (You), in the fourth, eighth and twelfth the dog (Xu); if it sits on the Body or the Self there is worry and weeping; in divining illness, if the sign moves it means great ill. The Small killing of the four seasons: in spring avoid the sheep's head (Wei), in summer the dragon (Chen), in autumn the ox (Chou), in winter the dog (Xu); in divining illness it must not move. The Banner killing: in spring the rabbit (Mao), in summer the rat (Zi), in autumn the rooster (You), in winter the horse (Wu); in divining illness it must not move.

> [!note] 按
> 这四杀的共同点是括号里的那一句「占病发动凶」「占病征发动主大凶」「占病忌发动」：它们平时（不占病）另有断语（天哭杀是哭泣、四季小杀和旌旗杀不专说病），只有占病时忌它们发动。「猴上真」的「上真」意思读不确定，这里按「是猴，很真切」字面译，原文没有解释。「逆行向卯轮十二位」是从正月的辰起每月倒退一位（二月卯、三月寅……），「向卯」是下一月落在卯。天哭杀的四组（正五九羊、二六十猴、三七十一鸡、四八十二犬）是每四个月一循环，在未、申、酉、戌。「若临身世忧哭泣，定知下泪泪沾襟」是说临身或世就要哭；占病时它发动，主大凶。「（占病忌发动）」是原文括号里的注。
>
> *Note:* What these four killings have in common is the sentence in brackets, 'in divining illness, if it moves it is ominous', 'in divining illness, if the sign moves it means great ill' and 'in divining illness it must not move': in ordinary use (when not divining illness) they have other judgments (the Weeping killing is weeping, and the Small killing of the four seasons and the Banner killing are not specially about illness), and only in divining illness is it taboo for them to move. The 'shang zhen' of 'the monkey shang zhen' is not certain in sense, and it is rendered literally as 'the monkey, a true one', and the original does not explain it. 'Goes backward towards Mao, rotating through the twelve places' is going back one place each month from Chen in the first month (Mao in the second, Yin in the third ...), and 'towards Mao' is that the next month falls on Mao. The four groups of the Weeping killing (first, fifth, ninth the sheep; second, sixth, tenth the monkey; third, seventh, eleventh the rooster; fourth, eighth, twelfth the dog) run in a cycle of four months through Wei, Shen, You and Xu. 'If it sits on the Body or the Self there is worry and weeping, surely tears fall and wet the lapel' says that sitting on the Body or the Self means weeping; in divining illness, if it moves it means great ill. The '(in divining illness it must not move)' is a note in brackets in the original.
