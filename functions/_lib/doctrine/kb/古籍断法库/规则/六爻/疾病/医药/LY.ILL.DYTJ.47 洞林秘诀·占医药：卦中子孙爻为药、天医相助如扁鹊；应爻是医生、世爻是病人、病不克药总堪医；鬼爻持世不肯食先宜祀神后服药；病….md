# 洞林秘诀·占医药：卦中子孙爻为药、天医相助如扁鹊；应爻是医生、世爻是病人、病不克药总堪医；鬼爻持世不肯食先宜祀神后服药；病伏为鬼不除根

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里的子孙爻是药，有天医相助，像扁鹊一样；卦爻若旺相、伏神没有鬼，病得以除根，治好、安乐。要看医生，应爻就是；病人求医药，世爻来当；病不克药，总是可以医治；药的颜色按五行自己斟酌。鬼爻持世，不肯吃东西，或者过不了喉咙就吐，即使吃下去，病更加重，应当先祭祀神灵，后服药。病伏着成了鬼，不能除根，一时暂且收功，得个大概；再加上生克、杀神来推；病人所适宜的，全在医生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the hexagram the Output line is the medicine, and with the Heavenly Physician helping it is like Bian Que; if the lines are strong and the hidden spirit has no ghost, the illness is rooted out, cured, and one is at ease. If one looks at the doctor, it is the Other line; the sick person seeking medicine, the Self line stands for him; if the illness does not control the medicine it can always be treated; the colour of the medicine is for oneself to weigh by the five phases. With the Pressure line holding the Self he will not take food, or it does not pass the throat and is vomited, and even if it is eaten the illness grows worse; one should first sacrifice to the spirits and then take medicine. With the illness hidden and turned into a ghost, it is not rooted out, for a time it gains merit briefly, roughly so; further work it out by generation, control and the killing spirits; what suits the illness is chosen entirely by the doctor.

> [!note] 按
> 这首洞林秘诀在占医药一章里，和占疾病一章里那一段洞林秘诀（LY.ILL.DYTJ.16）是同一部书的两首：那一首讲鬼、杀、刑克主灾疾，这一首讲医和药。「鬼爻持世不肯食……先宜祀神后服药」和那一首「鬼爻持世须求鬼，若逢服药进灾殃」是同一个意思：鬼爻持世，先要向神灵祈求，然后才服药。「病伏为鬼不除根」按「病爻下伏着鬼」读（病的爻下面伏着鬼爻，所以断不了根），字面译。最后一句「病撰所宜尽医者」读不确定，「撰」有选的意思，这里按「病人所适宜的（药），全在医生（去选）」字面译。「扁鹊」是战国时的名医。「（1）（2）（3）」是今人注号，那几条注不收。天医（天医相助如扁鹊）见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.30、DYTJ.34。药色按五行取，见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.46 的「打架」。
>
> *Note:* This Dongling Mijue in the chapter on doctors and medicine is a second poem from the same book as the passage of the Dongling Mijue in the chapter on illness (LY.ILL.DYTJ.16): that one speaks of the ghost, the killings, punishment and control bringing calamity and illness, and this one of the doctor and the medicine. 'The Pressure line holding the Self, he will not take food ... one should first sacrifice to the spirits and then take medicine' and the other one's 'the Pressure line holding the Self, one must seek the ghost, and if one then takes medicine it only advances calamity' are the same sense: with the Pressure line holding the Self one must first pray to the spirits, and only then take medicine. 'With the illness hidden and turned into a ghost, it is not rooted out' is read as a ghost line hidden under the line of the illness (so that the root cannot be cut), and is rendered literally. The last line, 'what suits the illness is chosen entirely by the doctor', is not certain, and 'zhuan' has the sense of choosing; it is rendered literally as 'what suits the illness (medicine) is chosen entirely by the doctor'. Bian Que was a famous physician of the Warring States. The '(1)', '(2)' and '(3)' are modern annotation marks and those annotations are not quoted. For the Heavenly Physician (the Heavenly Physician helping) see LY.ILL.DYTJ.30 and DYTJ.34. For the colour of the medicine taken by the five phases see the dispute in LY.ILL.DYTJ.46.
