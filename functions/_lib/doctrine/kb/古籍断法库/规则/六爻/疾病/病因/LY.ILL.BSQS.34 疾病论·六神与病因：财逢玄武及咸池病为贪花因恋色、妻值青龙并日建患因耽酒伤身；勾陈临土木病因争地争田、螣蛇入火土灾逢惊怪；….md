# 疾病论·六神与病因：财逢玄武及咸池病为贪花因恋色、妻值青龙并日建患因耽酒伤身；勾陈临土木病因争地争田、螣蛇入火土灾逢惊怪；虎动临财因丧而得、龙兴遇鬼喜处染来

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里按六神看病因：财逢玄武和咸池，病是因为贪恋女色；妻财值青龙、又逢日建，病因是沉迷喝酒、伤了身体；金爻的兄发动，气喘；木鬼交重，身体疼痛；勾陈临在土爻或木爻上，病因是争地争田；螣蛇入了火爻土爻，灾是遇到惊吓怪异；白虎发动、临在财上，是因为办丧事得的病；青龙兴起、遇到鬼，是在喜庆的地方染上的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness reads the cause of an illness by the six spirits: Wealth meeting the Dark Tortoise and the Xianchi star, the illness comes from craving flowers and loving lust; Wealth on the Azure Dragon and also the Day branch, the illness comes from indulging in wine and harming the body; a Metal brother line moving, panting; a Wood ghost moving, bodily pain; Hook Earth on an Earth or Wood line, the illness comes from quarrelling over land and fields; the Coiling Snake entering a Fire or Earth line, the trouble comes from fright and strange things; the White Tiger moving and on Wealth, it is got from a funeral; the Azure Dragon rising and meeting a ghost, it is caught at a happy occasion.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段按「六神看病因」列了八种：玄武带财爻、咸池是色；青龙带妻财、日建是酒；金爻的兄发动是气喘；木鬼交重是身疼；勾陈临土木是争地争田；螣蛇入火土是惊怪；虎（白虎）动临财是丧事；龙（青龙）兴遇鬼是喜庆场合。各句的条件写得很省，只能照字面译。「日建」是日辰。「咸池」是一个神煞（桃花一类）。「贪花因恋色」里「花」指女色。末两联的「虎」「龙」是白虎、青龙的简称。
>
> *Note:* This passage lists eight causes read by the six spirits: the Dark Tortoise with Wealth and the Xianchi star is lust; the Azure Dragon with Wealth and the Day branch is wine; a moving Metal brother line is panting; a Wood ghost moving is bodily pain; Hook Earth on Earth or Wood is quarrelling over land and fields; the Coiling Snake in Fire or Earth is fright and strange things; the Tiger moving on Wealth is a funeral; the Dragon rising and meeting a ghost is a happy occasion. The conditions of each line are written very sparely, and can only be translated to the letter. 'The Day branch' is the Day. The 'Xianchi star' is a spirit-star (of the peach-blossom kind). In 'craving flowers' the 'flowers' are women. In the last two couplets 'Tiger' and 'Dragon' are short forms of the White Tiger and the Azure Dragon.
