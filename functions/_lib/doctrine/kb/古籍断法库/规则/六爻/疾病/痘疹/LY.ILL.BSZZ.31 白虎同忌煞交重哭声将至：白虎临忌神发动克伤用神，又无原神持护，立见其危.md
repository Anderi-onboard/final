# 白虎同忌煞交重哭声将至：白虎临忌神发动克伤用神，又无原神持护，立见其危

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 白虎和忌神、煞神一起交重（发动），哭声将要到来：白虎带着忌神发动，克伤用神，又没有原神来扶持保护的，马上就出现危险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The White Tiger together with the Adversary and killing spirits all moving means the sound of wailing is coming: if the White Tiger sits on the Adversary and it moves to control and wound the Subject, and there is no Support to hold and protect it, danger appears at once.
