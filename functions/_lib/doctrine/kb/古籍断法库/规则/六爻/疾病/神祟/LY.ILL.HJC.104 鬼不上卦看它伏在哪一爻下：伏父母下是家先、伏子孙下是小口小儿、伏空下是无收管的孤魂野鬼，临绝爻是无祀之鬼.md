# 鬼不上卦看它伏在哪一爻下：伏父母下是家先、伏子孙下是小口小儿、伏空下是无收管的孤魂野鬼，临绝爻是无祀之鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻不上卦，就看它伏在哪一爻下面，便知道是什么鬼祟：伏在父母爻下，是家里已故的祖先；伏在子孙（福神）爻下，是小孩一类；伏在空亡的爻下，是没有人收管的鬼，是孤魂野鬼；如果临在绝爻上，是没有人祭祀的鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line is absent from the hexagram, see under which line it is hidden and you will know what kind of ghost it is: hidden under the Resource line, the family's forebears; under the Output (fortune spirit) line, children and the like; under a Void line, a ghost that no one keeps, a lonely wandering ghost; and if it meets an Extinction line, a ghost to whom no one sacrifices.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 4534 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 断曰：凡卜鬼神以官爻为用，今官鬼伏辰爻之下，与飞神作合，又得日辰合住。 | 断说：凡是占鬼神，以官爻为用；现在官鬼伏在辰爻下面，和飞神合在一起，又被日辰合住。 | Judgment: in all divining of ghosts and spirits the Pressure line is the Subject; now Pressure is hidden beneath the Chen line, combines with the flying line, and is further held by a combination with the Day. |
> | 予想伏合乃藏匿之象，酉金是正气之神，第三爻为房室，即断曰：汝家房中藏有神像作祟。 | 我想，伏着又被合住，是藏起来的象；酉金是正气之神；第三爻是房室；就断说：你家房里藏着一尊神像在作祟。 | I thought that being hidden and combined is the image of concealment; You Metal is a spirit of proper energy; the third line is the bedroom; so I judged: a statue of a spirit is hidden in a room of your house and is making trouble. |
> | 彼曰：有铜达摩祖师在匣中。 | 他说：有一尊铜的达摩祖师像在匣子里。 | He said: there is a bronze figure of Patriarch Damo in a box. |
> | 命其送于寺内，其痛亦愈。 | 叫他把像送到寺里去，他的病痛也好了。 | I told him to send it to a temple, and his pain was cured as well. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 这一条经文只有「绝则无祀」四字；王洪绪把它和上一条「刑不善终」并成一段注，后半是「不上卦看伏何爻下」，和旧注相同。卜筮正宗后面的书中例（小畜卦）用的是伏鬼的看法：官鬼酉金伏在第三爻辰土下面，辰酉相合，又被戊辰日合住；但断「房中藏着神像」用的是「伏合是藏匿之象、酉金是正气之神、第三爻是房室」，不是上面旧注这条里的父母下是家先、子孙下是小儿。
>
> *Note:* The verse here is only the four characters 'extinction, no one to sacrifice'; Wang Hongxu joins it with the previous entry, 'punishment, no good death', into one passage of commentary, whose second half, 'if absent from the hexagram, see under which line it is hidden', is the same as the old commentary. The case that follows in the Bushi Zhengzong (Xiaoxu) uses the reading of a hidden ghost: the You Metal Pressure lies hidden beneath the third line, Chen Earth; Chen and You combine and the Wuchen day also holds it in combination; but the judgment 'a statue is hidden in the room' rests on 'hidden and combined is the image of concealment, You Metal is a spirit of proper energy, the third line is the bedroom', not on the old commentary's 'under Resource the family forebears, under Output children'.
