# 用煞如临白虎且慢栽花：用神或忌神带白虎，用神又受日月动爻刑冲克害，不宜种痘，恐反被害

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神或者煞神如果带白虎，先别种痘。具体说，或者用神带白虎，或者忌神带白虎，用神又受到日、月、动爻的刑冲克害，不适合种痘，恐怕反而被害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject or the killing spirit carries the White Tiger, hold off sowing the pox for now. Specifically, either the Subject carries the White Tiger or the Adversary carries the White Tiger, and the Subject is struck by punishment, clash, control or harm from the Day, Month or moving lines: it is not suitable to inoculate, for fear of being harmed instead.
