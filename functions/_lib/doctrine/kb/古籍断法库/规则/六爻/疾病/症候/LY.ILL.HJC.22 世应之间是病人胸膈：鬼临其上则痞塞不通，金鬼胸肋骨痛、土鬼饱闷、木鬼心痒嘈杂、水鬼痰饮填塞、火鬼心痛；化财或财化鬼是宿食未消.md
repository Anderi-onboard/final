# 世应之间是病人胸膈：鬼临其上则痞塞不通，金鬼胸肋骨痛、土鬼饱闷、木鬼心痒嘈杂、水鬼痰饮填塞、火鬼心痛；化财或财化鬼是宿食未消

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻和应爻中间的位置，是病人胸膈的地方。官鬼临在这里，胸膈一定堵闷不通：金鬼是胸肋骨头痛，土鬼是胀饱憋闷、不舒畅，木鬼是心里发痒、胃里嘈杂，水鬼是痰饮堵塞，火鬼多半是心口痛。如果变出财爻，或财爻变成鬼，一定是隔夜的食物没消化，弄得胸膈不舒服。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The place between the Self and Other lines is where the patient's chest and diaphragm are. With a Pressure line on it the chest is surely stuffed and blocked: a Metal Pressure is pain in the ribs and chest bones, an Earth Pressure a full, oppressive feeling with no relief, a Wood Pressure an itching heart and a gnawing stomach, a Water Pressure phlegm and fluid choking it up, and a Fire Pressure mostly heart pain. If it changes into a Wealth line, or a Wealth line changes into Pressure, it is surely food from before left undigested, so that the chest and diaphragm are not at ease.

> [!note] 按
> 经文的「间」是世爻和应爻之间的两爻；旧注拿它当病人胸膈的位置。王洪绪这一句写成「病入胸膈处」，大概是「病人胸膈处」之误，译文按病人。旧注写「胸肋骨痛」，王洪绪写「胸隔骨痛」；旧注「土鬼」，王洪绪写作「土官」，是同一个意思。「嘈杂」是胃里烧灼、空空乱乱的不适，「痰饮」是体内水液积聚成痰、成饮。
>
> *Note:* The 'between' of the verse means the two lines between the Self and Other lines, and the old commentary takes them as the place of the patient's chest and diaphragm. Wang Hongxu writes 'where the illness enters the chest' here, which is probably a slip for 'where the patient's chest is', and the translation follows 'patient'. The old commentary writes 'pain in the ribs and chest bones' and Wang Hongxu 'pain in the chest bones', and where the old commentary has 'Earth Pressure' Wang Hongxu has 'Earth official'; both mean the same. 'Gnawing' (caoza) is a burning, empty, disordered discomfort in the stomach, and 'phlegm and fluid' (tanyin) is water in the body collecting as phlegm and as fluid.
