# 占鬼神·附六神：青龙善恶经文蘸祀庙香、无气带刑自缢死；朱雀花幡口愿符命灶神、劳死鬼；勾陈天曹勅士、黄病路死鬼；螣蛇夜梦惊恐上许下保福、夜梦见鬼；白虎金刘神作犯白虎刀伤鬼；玄武上真北阴神、落水阴鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 附六神。青龙：善恶、经文、蘸祀、庙香；无气带刑，是自缢死的鬼。朱雀：花幡、口愿、符命、灶神；无气带刑，是劳死的鬼。勾陈：天曹、勅士；无气带刑，是黄病、路死的鬼。螣蛇：夜梦、惊恐、上许下保福；无气带刑，是夜梦见鬼。白虎：金刘神、作犯白虎、刀伤鬼；无气带刑，是刀伤鬼。玄武：上真、北阴神；无气带刑，是落水的阴鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Appended, the six spirits. The Azure Dragon: good and evil, scriptures, sacrificial rites, temple incense; without qi and bearing a punishment, a ghost of hanging. The Vermilion Bird: flower banners, spoken vows, talismans, the Kitchen God; without qi and bearing a punishment, a ghost who died of toil. Hook Earth: the Heavenly Bureau, the commissioned officers; without qi and bearing a punishment, a ghost of jaundice, or of dying on the road. The Coiling Snake: dreams at night, fright, vows made above and thanks given below; without qi and bearing a punishment, seeing ghosts in dreams at night. The White Tiger: the Jin Liu spirit, the offence-making White Tiger, the ghost of a blade wound; without qi and bearing a punishment, a ghost of a blade wound. The Dark Tortoise: the high true one, the North Yin spirit; without qi and bearing a punishment, a yin ghost of drowning.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节把六神各自的神和死鬼列出来：每一神前半是它管的神（青龙善恶、经文、蘸祀、庙香；朱雀花幡、口愿、符命、灶神……），后半「无气带刑」是这一神所临的爻无气又带刑时的鬼（青龙自缢死、朱雀劳死、勾陈黄病路死、螣蛇夜梦见鬼、白虎刀伤、玄武落水阴鬼）。「蘸祀」是醮祀，「上许下保福」是上面许愿、下面还愿保福，「金刘神」「作犯白虎」的意思原文没有解释，字面译。「上真」是高一级的真人，「北阴」是北方阴神。原文「六神」二字连在上一行「附：」的后面，又接在「青龙：」前面，所以「附」「六神」分成两个小句。这一节和易林补遗的「总论六神值诀」（LY.ILL.EL.47）是同一类，神名和鬼名各有出入。
>
> *Note:* This section lists for each of the six spirits its gods and its dead ghost: the first half of each is the gods it governs (the Azure Dragon good and evil, scriptures, sacrificial rites, temple incense; the Vermilion Bird flower banners, spoken vows, talismans, the Kitchen God ...), and the second half 'without qi and bearing a punishment' is the ghost when the line the spirit sits on is without qi and bears a punishment (the Azure Dragon a ghost of hanging, the Vermilion Bird died of toil, Hook Earth jaundice or dying on the road, the Coiling Snake seeing ghosts in dreams, the White Tiger a blade wound, the Dark Tortoise a yin ghost of drowning). 'Sacrificial rites' is a jiao offering, 'vows made above and thanks given below' is a vow made above and redeemed below to keep fortune, and what the 'Jin Liu spirit' and the 'offence-making White Tiger' mean the original does not explain, and they are rendered literally. The 'high true one' is a true one of a higher rank and the 'North Yin' is the yin spirit of the north. In the original the two characters for 'the six spirits' run on from 'appended' on the line before and run into 'the Azure Dragon:' after, so 'appended' and 'the six spirits' are made two small pieces. This section is of the same kind as the Yilin Buyi's 'general discussion of the six spirits on the ghost' (LY.ILL.EL.47), with differences in the names of gods and ghosts.
