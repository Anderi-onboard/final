# 行人章·鬼兄并发多应病，官化为官更不祥

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占行人的歌里说：官鬼爻和兄弟爻一起发动，多半应在行人生病；官鬼化出官鬼，更不吉祥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on travellers says: when the Pressure line and the Peer line move together, it mostly points to the traveller falling ill; Pressure turning into Pressure is still more inauspicious.

## 《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·行人章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3892 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses: the chapter on travellers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼兄并发多应病， | 鬼爻和兄弟爻一起发动，多半应在生病， | When Pressure and Peer move together, it mostly points to illness, |
> | 官化为官更不祥。 | 官鬼化成官鬼，更不吉祥。 | and Pressure turning into Pressure is still more inauspicious. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是占行人（外出的人）的歌里的一联，讲的是出门在外的人生病。「鬼兄」是官鬼和兄弟两爻。「官化为官」是官鬼发动、变出来又是官鬼。同一首歌的其余句子讲行人什么时候回、在路上有什么事，不收。
>
> *Note:* This couplet is in the verse on travellers (people away from home) and is about the traveller falling ill. 'Ghost and brother' means the Pressure and Peer lines. 'Official turning into official' is a Pressure line that moves and changes into Pressure again. The other lines of the same poem are about when the traveller returns and what befalls him on the road, and are not taken.
