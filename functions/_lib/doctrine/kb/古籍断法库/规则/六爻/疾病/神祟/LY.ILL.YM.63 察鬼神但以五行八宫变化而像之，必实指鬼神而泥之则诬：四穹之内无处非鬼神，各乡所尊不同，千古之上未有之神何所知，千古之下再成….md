# 察鬼神但以五行八宫变化而像之，必实指鬼神而泥之则诬：四穹之内无处非鬼神，各乡所尊不同，千古之上未有之神何所知，千古之下再成之神何所拟

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 天地四方之内，没有哪里没有鬼神，但各地方各有风俗所尊奉的，地水火风所掌管的，有的只显灵在一个乡，有的应验在一个国，很难一一细说。俗话说，千古以前还没有的神，怎么知道；千古以后再成的神，又拿什么去比拟。考察鬼神，只要用五行、八宫，变化着去取象，作为后学的规范，古今都能通用。如果一定要落实指定是哪位鬼神、还死抠着不放，那就是诬妄了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Within the four vaults of heaven there is no place without ghosts and spirits, yet each quarter has those its customs revere and those that earth, water, fire and wind govern, some manifest in a single village, some proving true over a whole country, hard to set out one by one. It is said: of spirits that did not exist a thousand ages ago, how can they be known; of spirits that will come to be a thousand ages hence, what can they be compared to. In examining ghosts and spirits, simply take the image by the five phases and the eight palaces with their changes, as a pattern for later learners, and it applies to past and present alike. To point insistently at a particular ghost or spirit and cling to it is to make a false claim.

> [!warning] 打架
> 要不要落实到某一位神：易冒在本章前面（LY.ILL.YM.44 到 YM.62）列了大量神名，这里却说「必实指鬼神而泥之，则诬矣」，前后的关系是：名单是举例，办法是用五行八宫去取象。增删卜易的觉子和李我平批评的是把名单当成定论，即金官就指关公岳公（LY.ILL.ZS.24、ZS.25），而且李我平点名批评了《易冒》指定关岳、并说本书疾病章讲鬼神是「从俗」；从这一段看，易冒自己在这里已经说了不能泥于某一位。黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.93 到 HJC.104）和易隐（LY.ILL.YY.28）则直接给出神名和鬼的死因，没有这样的收束。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether to pin it to a particular god: the Yi Mao lists a large number of names earlier in the chapter (LY.ILL.YM.44 to YM.62), yet here says 'to point insistently at a particular ghost or spirit and cling to it is to make a false claim'; the relation between the two is that the lists are examples and the method is to take the image by the five phases and the eight palaces. What Jue Zi and Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi criticise is taking the list as a conclusion, that is, naming Guan Gong or Yue Gong at once for a Metal Pressure line (LY.ILL.ZS.24, ZS.25), and Li Woping names the Yi Mao for pinning Guan and Yue and says its talk of ghosts and spirits in the illness chapter 'follows the common custom'; from this passage the Yi Mao itself already says here that one must not cling to a particular one. The Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.93 to HJC.104) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.28) give names of gods and the causes of death of ghosts directly, without such a closing qualification.

> [!note] 按
> 「地水火风」是佛家说的四大，这里指各地方由地、水、火、风所司的神。「千古而上，未有之神何所知」这句话的意思是，一个人死后成神，是后来的事；古书里没有的神，不能用古书去对。这一段在 LY.ILL.YM.42 总纲里埋下的「鬼神不是全然虚妄」和这里的「必实指而泥之则诬」合在一起，就是这一章的态度：承认有，但不为一个具体的名字负责。
>
> *Note:* 'Earth, water, fire and wind' are the four elements of the Buddhist teaching, here the spirits of each place that are governed by earth, water, fire and wind. 'Of spirits that did not exist a thousand ages ago, how can they be known' means that a person becoming a spirit after death is a later matter, and a spirit not found in the old books cannot be matched against the old books. What was planted in the outline (LY.ILL.YM.42), 'ghosts and spirits are not wholly false', together with this 'to point insistently and cling to it is to make a false claim', is the attitude of the chapter: to admit that they exist, but not to answer for any particular name.
