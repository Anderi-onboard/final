# 父合变孙莫若闭门修养：福爻空亡、官爻衰静，若有父母动来生合世身主象，不须服药；再变出子孙，不如抛开家事静养，或入寺观闭门修养，不吃药自会好

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里子孙爻空亡、官鬼衰弱安静，如果有父母爻发动来生合世爻、身爻和主象，就不用吃药。再变出子孙，不如丢开家里的事，到深山里静养，或者进寺庙道观闭门修养，不吃药也会自己安好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Output line in the hexagram is Void and the Pressure line weak and still, and a Resource line moves to generate or combine with the Self line, the body and the main image, no medicine need be taken. If it also changes into Output, better to abandon household affairs and recuperate quietly in the deep mountains, or to enter a temple or Daoist abbey and recuperate behind a closed door, and without medicine he will recover of himself.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注比王洪绪多了两层：福爻是空亡，又变出子孙时，连家事也要抛开，到深山或寺观去静养。王洪绪只说宜住僻静处闭门修养。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary has two layers more than Wang Hongxu: with the Output line Void and also changing into Output, one should abandon even household affairs and recuperate in the mountains or in a temple or abbey. Wang Hongxu says only that he should live in a secluded place and recuperate behind a closed door.
