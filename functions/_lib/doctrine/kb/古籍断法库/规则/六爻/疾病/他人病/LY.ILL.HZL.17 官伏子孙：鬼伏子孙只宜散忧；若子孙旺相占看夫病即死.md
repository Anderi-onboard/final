# 官伏子孙：鬼伏子孙只宜散忧；若子孙旺相占看夫病即死

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼伏在子孙下，只宜散忧；若要取用官，须官鬼旺相、透出到值日的日辰才行。若子孙旺相，占看丈夫的病，就死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ghost hidden under Output is only fit for dispelling worries; if the official is to be used, the Pressure line must be strong and come out on the ruling day. If Output is strong, divining a husband's illness, he dies.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节的标题是「官伏子孙」，头一句对句（鬼伏子孙，去路无门；官乘旺相，透出可分）不在这里收。占的是妻子问丈夫：官鬼代表丈夫，鬼伏在子孙下、子孙又旺相（子孙克官鬼），所以占丈夫的病是死。「若用宫」的「宫」是「官」之误，这里按「官」译。「直日辰」是值日的日辰，「透出」是伏藏的鬼在日辰上现出来。和阐奥歌章、断易天机的「妻占夫病」诗（LY.ILL.CA.07、LY.ILL.DYTJ.08）不是同一条，那两首讲的是鬼、财、兄、父的动静。
>
> *Note:* The heading of this section is 'the official hidden under Output', and its opening couplet ('the ghost hidden under Output, the road out has no door; the official riding strength, once it comes out it can be told apart') is not taken here. The case is a wife asking about her husband: the Pressure line stands for the husband, the ghost is hidden under Output and Output is also strong (Output controls the Pressure line), and so divining the husband's illness he dies. The 'gong' of 'ruo yong gong' is a miswriting of 'guan' (official), and is rendered official here. The 'ruling day' is the day that is on duty, and 'come out' is the hidden ghost appearing on the day branch. It is not the same entry as the poems 'a wife divining her husband's illness' in the Chan'ao Gezhang and the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.CA.07, LY.ILL.DYTJ.08), which speak of the movement and stillness of the ghost, Wealth, Peer and Resource.
