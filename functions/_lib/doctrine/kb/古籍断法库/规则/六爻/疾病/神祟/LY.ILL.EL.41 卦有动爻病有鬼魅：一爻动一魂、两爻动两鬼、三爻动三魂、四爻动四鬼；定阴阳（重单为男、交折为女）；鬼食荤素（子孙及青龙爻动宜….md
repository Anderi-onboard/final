# 卦有动爻病有鬼魅：一爻动一魂、两爻动两鬼、三爻动三魂、四爻动四鬼；定阴阳（重单为男、交折为女）；鬼食荤素（子孙及青龙爻动宜素祭、其余爻动荤素皆合）；定方向（子爻动正北方鬼、丑爻动东北方鬼，卯东酉西）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有动爻，病里就有鬼魅：比如三爻动，就是三个魂，四个象兴动就是四个鬼。要定阴阳，重、单是男，交、折是女；要分方向，卯动是东方，酉动是西方。注说：凡是推儿官，专看动爻，一爻动就是一个魂，两爻动就是两个鬼；想分男女，要看阴阳，重的是男，交的是女；想知道鬼吃荤还是吃素，只有子孙和青龙爻动的，都宜用素祭，其余的爻动，一概荤素都合；想知道鬼在哪个方位，就看动在哪一象：比如子爻动，是正北方的鬼；丑爻动，是东北方的鬼；其余照这样推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where the hexagram has moving lines, the illness has ghosts and sprites: for example, if the third line moves, there are three souls, and if four images stir, four ghosts. To fix yin and yang, the double and the single are male, the crossed and the broken are female; to divide the direction, a moving Mao is east and a moving You is west. The commentary says: in working out a child-official, look only at the moving lines: one line moving is one soul, two lines moving are two ghosts; to divide male and female, look at yin and yang, the double being male and the crossed female; to know whether the ghost eats meat or vegetables, only when Output or the Azure Dragon line moves is a vegetarian offering suitable, and when any other line moves, both meat and vegetarian suit; to know in which direction the ghost is, look at which image it moves in: for example, a moving Zi line is a ghost of the due north, and a moving Chou line a ghost of the northeast; the rest follow this.

> [!note] 按
> 「重、单、交、折」是摇卦时四种爻的名称：重（老阳）、单（少阳）是阳，交（老阴）、折（少阴）是阴，所以歌诀说重、单是男，交、折是女；解说只说「重则为男，交则为女」，是说动的爻（重是动的阳、交是动的阴）。「儿官」原文没有解释，疑是「鬼」的别称（「官」是官鬼），这里按字面译作「child-official」并保留；「一爻动则一魂」是数动爻的条数当作魂数，这和易冒「以动爻当鬼、一动一鬼、三动三鬼」（LY.ILL.YM.54）是同一件事。「子孙及青龙爻动者宜素祭」是说这类鬼（子孙、青龙）偏于善神，用素祭。
>
> *Note:* 'Double, single, crossed, broken' are the names of the four kinds of line in casting: the double (old yang) and the single (young yang) are yang, and the crossed (old yin) and the broken (young yin) are yin, so the verse says the double and the single are male and the crossed and the broken female; the explanation says only 'the double is male, the crossed female', speaking of the moving lines (the double being a moving yang and the crossed a moving yin). 'Child-official' (er guan) is not explained in the original and looks like another name for the ghost ('guan' being the Pressure line); it is translated literally and kept. 'One line moving is one soul' counts the number of moving lines as the number of souls, the same matter as the Yi Mao's 'take the moving lines as the ghosts, one moving one ghost, three moving three ghosts' (LY.ILL.YM.54). 'Output or the Azure Dragon line moving suits a vegetarian offering' means that these kinds of ghost (Output and the Azure Dragon) lean toward benevolent spirits, and take a vegetarian offering.
