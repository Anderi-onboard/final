# 卜筮元龟·占何处得病：鬼爻在初堂内病、三二在门庭、四五居道路遇风雨；鬼入坎河海疾；丧门临象死丧家；福德象来因酒食；本宫五鬼象喧哗

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻在初爻，是在堂屋里得的病；在三爻和二爻，是在门庭；在四、五爻，是在道路上，或者遇上风雨，伤身惊心。鬼爻入坎，是河海上得的病；丧门临在象上，是死丧之家；福德的象来到，是因酒食；本宫五鬼的象，是喧哗吵闹。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line on the first line: caught in the hall of the house; on the third and second: at the gate and courtyard; on the fourth and fifth: on the road, or meeting wind and rain it harms the body and startles the heart. The Pressure line entering Kan: illness from rivers and seas; Mourning Gate sitting on the image: a house of death and mourning; when the Output image comes it is through wine and food; the image of the Five Ghosts of the own palace is clamour and uproar.

> [!note] 按
> 「初堂内病」按「在堂屋里得的病」译。这一段是说病在哪里染上的，而不是病在身体哪一处（那一段是 LY.ILL.DYTJ.11、DYTJ.12）。「死丧家」承上文「占何处得病」，大概是说在办丧事的人家染上的病，字面是「死丧之家」，这里按字面译，书里没有说明。「福德象来因酒食」的「福德象来」大概是子孙的象出现，字面译。「本宫五鬼象喧哗」的「喧哗」意思不明，字面译作 clamour and uproar，「五鬼」是什么书里没有说。「（5）」是今人加的注号，那条注不收。这一块的「鬼在初爻…」和天玄赋的「六兽临官」（LY.ILL.TXF.09）、易林补遗的「何处得病」（LY.ILL.EL.03、EL.04）是同一类问题，答案各有各的路数。
>
> *Note:* 'Chu tang nei bing' is rendered 'caught in the hall of the house'. This passage says where the illness was caught, and not which part of the body is ill (that passage is LY.ILL.DYTJ.11 and DYTJ.12). The 'house of death and mourning' follows the heading 'where the illness was caught', and probably says it was caught in a house that is holding a funeral; the literal words are 'a house of death and mourning', and it is rendered literally here, and the book does not explain it. The 'Output image comes' of 'when the Output image comes it is through wine and food' probably means the image of Output appears, and is rendered literally. What 'clamour and uproar' means in 'the image of the Five Ghosts of the own palace' is not clear and is rendered literally, and the book does not say what the 'Five Ghosts' are. The '(5)' is a modern annotation mark and that annotation is not quoted. The 'ghost on the first line...' of this block is of the same kind of question as the Tianxuan Fu's 'the six beasts on the official' (LY.ILL.TXF.09) and the Yilin Buyi's 'where the illness was caught' (LY.ILL.EL.03, EL.04), each with its own way of answering.
