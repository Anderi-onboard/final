# 占药占医取象：水子旺宜汤、土子旺宜丸、火灸金针木末；火鬼喜凉药、木鬼喜风药、土脾金肺水肾；占医考子孙之属，木东金西乾僧坤妇——注：子为药鬼为病，象只用来问何药何人，问吉凶仍看子孙衰旺

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占药：水属的子孙旺，宜用汤剂；土属的子孙旺，宜用丸药；火像艾灸，金像针，木像药末，以这些作象。火鬼喜欢凉药，木鬼喜欢祛风的药；土对应脾，金对应肺，水对应肾，也以这些作象。占医生：要考察子孙属于哪一类，木属东、金属西，乾是僧人、坤是妇人，也以这些作象。注说：子孙是药，鬼是病，占该用什么药、是什么病，就用这个办法；如果占的是这味药吉不吉，就看子孙的衰旺，不要死板地认为子孙属土就该用丸药。占该请哪个方位、哪种人当医生，也是这个办法；单独占医生好不好，还是用子孙的强弱，比如问请僧人当医生，得乾卦而子孙落空，怎么能说吉？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining medicine: a strong Water Output suits a decoction, a strong Earth Output suits pills; Fire is moxibustion, Metal needles, Wood powders, taken as the images. A Fire ghost likes cooling medicine and a Wood ghost likes wind medicine; Earth is the spleen, Metal the lung and Water the kidney, also taken as images. Divining the doctor: examine what kind the Output belongs to; Wood is east and Metal west, Qian a monk and Kun a woman, again taken as images. The note says: Output is the medicine and the ghost is the illness, and to divine which medicine to use or what the illness is, use this method; but if what is divined is whether a medicine is auspicious, use the strength of Output, and do not rigidly hold that an Earth Output must mean pills. To divine which direction and which kind of person to use as doctor the method is the same; but to divine on its own whether the doctor is good, still use the strength of Output: for instance, if one asks about a monk-doctor and obtains Qian with Output Void, how could one call it auspicious?

> [!warning] 打架
> 用药要不要按五行来配：易冒把「水子旺宜汤、土子旺宜丸、火灸金针木末」「火鬼喜凉药、木鬼喜风药」当作象，但注马上限定，象只用来问「用何药、是何病」，问吉凶还是看子孙衰旺，不要认死「子孙属土就用丸药」；黄金策旧注、王洪绪和易隐把五行当作用药的依据（火鬼旺用大寒药、水鬼旺用大热药、金鬼宜灸、木鬼宜针，见 LY.ILL.HJC.76、HJC.81、HJC.84、YY.25）；增删卜易的李我平则批评这类说法拘泥，说子孙不是针灸之药、火鬼旺不一定该用大寒药（LY.ILL.ZS.40）。易冒站在中间：象可以用，但不作凭据。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether medicine should be matched by the phases: the Yi Mao takes 'a strong Water Output suits a decoction, an Earth Output pills, Fire moxibustion, Metal needles, Wood powders' and 'a Fire ghost likes cooling medicine, a Wood ghost wind medicine' as images, but the note at once limits them: images are used only for 'which medicine to use and what the illness is', while for whether it is auspicious one still reads the strength of Output and should not hold rigidly that 'an Earth Output means pills'; the old commentary and Wang Hongxu on the Huangjin Ce and the Yi Yin take the phases as the ground for the choice of medicine (a strong Fire ghost takes a very cold medicine, a strong Water ghost a very hot one, a Metal ghost moxibustion, a Wood ghost needles; see LY.ILL.HJC.76, HJC.81, HJC.84, YY.25); Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi criticises such sayings as rigid, saying Output is not a medicine for needles and moxibustion and that a strong Fire ghost does not necessarily call for a very cold medicine (LY.ILL.ZS.40). The Yi Mao stands in the middle: the images may be used, but not as the ground.

> [!note] 按
> 「四败」是十二长生里的沐浴：金败在午、木败在子、火败在卯、水土败在酉（见卜筮大全，沐浴即四败）；例中辛酉日，土的败正在酉，所以说逢四败。「真空」在易冒旬空章里指被月建克住的那种空（春天的土、夏天的金、秋天的木、冬天的火，以及辰戌丑未月里的水），卯月里的未土正是春土，所以叫真空。「以为像」的「像」就是象。
>
> *Note:* 'The four Defeats' is Bath in the twelve life stages: Metal's Defeat is at Wu, Wood's at Zi, Fire's at Mao, and Water's and Earth's at You (see the Bushi Daquan, where Bath is the four Defeats); in the case the day is Xinyou, and Earth's Defeat is exactly at You, hence 'meets the four Defeats'. 'True Void' in the Yi Mao's chapter on Void means the Void of a line controlled by the Month (Earth in spring, Metal in summer, Wood in autumn, Fire in winter, and Water in the Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei months), and the Wei Earth in a Mao month is spring Earth, hence 'truly Void'. The 'xiang' in 'taken as images' is the image.
