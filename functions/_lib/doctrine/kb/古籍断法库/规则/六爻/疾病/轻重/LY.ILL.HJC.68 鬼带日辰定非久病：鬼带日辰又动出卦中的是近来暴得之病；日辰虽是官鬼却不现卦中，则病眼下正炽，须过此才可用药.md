# 鬼带日辰定非久病：鬼带日辰又动出卦中的是近来暴得之病；日辰虽是官鬼却不现卦中，则病眼下正炽，须过此才可用药

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼带着日辰、又动出在卦里的，一定是近来突然得的病，不是久病。如果日辰虽然是官鬼，却没有出现在卦里，就不是这样；可以说这病眼下正厉害，必须等这一阵过去，才可以用药。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line that carries the Day and moves out in the hexagram is surely an illness got suddenly in these days, not a long one. If the Day is Pressure but does not appear in the hexagram, it is not so; one can say the illness is at its fiercest just now, and only after this has passed can medicine be used.

> [!note] 按
> 「鬼带日辰」按字面是鬼爻的地支和日辰的地支是同一个。这一条和病体章里的「日辰带鬼」（LY.ILL.HJC.50）不是一回事：那里讲的是日辰带鬼来生合世爻，该祈祷。
>
> *Note:* 'Pressure carrying the Day' is in the letter that the branch of the Pressure line is the same as the branch of the Day. This entry is not the same as 'the Day carrying Pressure' in the chapter on the patient's condition (LY.ILL.HJC.50), which speaks of the Day carrying Pressure to generate or combine with the Self line, where one should pray.
