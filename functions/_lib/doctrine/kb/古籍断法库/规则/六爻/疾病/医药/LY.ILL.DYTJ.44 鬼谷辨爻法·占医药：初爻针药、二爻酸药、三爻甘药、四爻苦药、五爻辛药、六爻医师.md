# 鬼谷辨爻法·占医药：初爻针药、二爻酸药、三爻甘药、四爻苦药、五爻辛药、六爻医师

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼谷子辨爻法在占医药一章的表：初爻是针药，二爻是酸药，三爻是甘药，四爻是苦药，五爻是辛药，六爻是医师。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Guigu's table of the lines for the chapter on doctors and medicine: the first line is needle medicine, the second sour medicine, the third sweet medicine, the fourth bitter medicine, the fifth pungent medicine and the sixth the physician.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是占医药一章开头的一张三行的表，原书排成六列：第一行是「鬼谷辨爻法」这个标题，第二行是初爻到六爻，第三行是六个名目。按位置一格对一爻：初爻针药、二爻酸药、三爻甘药、四爻苦药、五爻辛药、六爻医师。「针药」是用针（针灸）的治法，「酸药」「甘药」「苦药」「辛药」是酸、甘、苦、辛四味的药，表里没有咸味；书里没有解释这六格为什么这样配。这张表的性质和占疾病一章开头的鬼谷辨爻法表（LY.ILL.DYTJ.01）一样，是把六个爻位配给这一章要看的东西。
>
> *Note:* This is a three-row table at the head of the chapter on doctors and medicine, laid out in the original as six columns: the first row is the heading 'Guigu's method of telling the lines apart', the second the first to sixth lines, and the third six names. Cell by cell by position: the first line needle medicine, the second sour medicine, the third sweet medicine, the fourth bitter medicine, the fifth pungent medicine, the sixth the physician. 'Needle medicine' is treatment with the needle (acupuncture), and 'sour', 'sweet', 'bitter' and 'pungent medicine' are medicines of the four tastes sour, sweet, bitter and pungent, with no salty taste in the table; the book does not explain why the six cells are matched so. This table is of the same kind as the table under Guigu's name at the head of the chapter on illness (LY.ILL.DYTJ.01), matching the six line positions to what the chapter is about.
