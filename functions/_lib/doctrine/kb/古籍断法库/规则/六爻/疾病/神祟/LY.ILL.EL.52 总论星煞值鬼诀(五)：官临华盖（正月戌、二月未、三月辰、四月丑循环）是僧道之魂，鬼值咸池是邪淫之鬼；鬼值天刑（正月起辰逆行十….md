# 总论星煞值鬼诀(五)：官临华盖（正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑循环）是僧道之魂，鬼值咸池是邪淫之鬼；鬼值天刑（正月起辰逆行十二位）必犯责死之鬼，临天狱煞（正月亥、二月申、三月龙蛇、四月寅循环）是牢中之鬼，临劫煞（与天狱同）或刀砧必自刎而亡

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼临着华盖，就是僧道的魂（华盖：正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑，只在这四位上周而复始；咸池煞已写在前面）。鬼值咸池，就是邪淫的鬼。鬼值天刑煞，必定是犯了被责打死的鬼（天刑：正月从辰上起，逆行十二位）；如果临着天狱煞，就是牢里的鬼（天狱煞：正月遇亥，二月申，三月龙蛇〔即巳〕，四月寅，五月循环又到亥，周而复始定它是哪一路神）；鬼临劫煞，或者刀砧煞，必定是自刎而亡的（劫煞的起法和天狱煞相同，刀砧煞见前）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line sitting on Huagai is the soul of a monk or Daoist (Huagai: the first month Xu, the second Wei, the third Chen, the fourth Chou, cycling round and round through only these four positions; the Xianchi killing has been set out earlier). A ghost on Xianchi is a ghost of lewdness. A ghost on the Heavenly Punishment killing is surely one who offended by dying of flogging (Heavenly Punishment: the first month starts from Chen and runs backward through twelve places); if it sits on the Heavenly Prison killing, it is a ghost in prison (Heavenly Prison killing: the first month meets Hai, the second Shen, the third the dragon or snake [that is, Si], the fourth Yin, the fifth month circles round and reaches Hai again, cycling round and round it fixes which spirit it is); a ghost on the Robbery Killing or the Chopping Block killing is surely one who died by cutting his own throat (the Robbery Killing is set in the same way as the Heavenly Prison killing, the Chopping Block killing as before).

> [!note] 按
> 华盖是每次退三位的四位循环（戌、未、辰、丑），咸池已在 LY.ILL.EL.49 写了。天刑是从正月的辰起、每月退一位，走十二个月（和官符、刀砧、天河相反的方向）；断易天机的注释引鬼谷子的刑煞例（天刑）也是「从正月起辰，逆行」，一致。天狱煞「三月龙蛇」：龙是辰、蛇是巳，原文没有定是哪一个；按前后是每次退三位（亥、申、巳、寅），这里应是巳，易冒讲诸星一章注里引的同一首口诀写的是「三月随蛇」，是巳，所以译文里写作「the dragon or snake」并在这里说明。「劫煞兴天狱煞同」按字面译，不另作展开；歌诀「天刑牢狱中鬼」少了一个字（应是「天刑天狱牢中鬼」），这里按残句直译，「自刎伤」译作「wounded by cutting one's own throat」。「责死」是被责打（杖责）而死。与易隐「鬼带天刑天狱牢狱鬼」「鬼加刀砧羊刃劫杀自刎鬼」「鬼带华盖孤神僧道绝嗣鬼」（LY.ILL.YY.29）、易冒「天狱笞杖徒流之鬼」「华盖僧道咸池娼妓」（LY.ILL.YM.62）同一路。 书里没有说这里的「正月、二月」是起卦的月还是别的月，这里照抄；「只此四位周而复始」是说五月回到正月的位置、九月也回到正月的位置，依此类推。 这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* Huagai is a cycle of four positions each moving back three places (Xu, Wei, Chen, Chou), and Xianchi was already written in LY.ILL.EL.49. Heavenly Punishment starts from Chen in the first month and moves back one place a month through the twelve months (the opposite direction to the Official Talisman, the Chopping Block and the Heavenly River); the commentary in the Duanyi Tianji quotes the rule of Guiguzi for the punishing killing (Heavenly Punishment) as 'starting from Chen in the first month and running backward', which agrees. In the Heavenly Prison killing's 'the third month the dragon or snake', the dragon is Chen and the snake is Si, and the original does not fix which; the series moves back three places each time (Hai, Shen, Si, Yin), so here it should be Si, and the same verse quoted in the note of the Yi Mao's chapter on the stars reads 'the third month follows the snake', which is Si, so the translation writes 'the dragon or snake' and this is explained here. 'The Robbery Killing rises the same as the Heavenly Prison killing' is translated literally and not expanded; the verse 'Heavenly Punishment, the ghost in prison' is short of a character (it should be 'Heavenly Punishment and Heavenly Prison, the ghost in prison'), and is translated as the broken line stands, 'wounded by cutting one's own throat' translating 'zi wen shang'. 'Ze si' is dying by being flogged (beaten with the rod). It is on the same path as the Yi Yin's 'a ghost carrying Heavenly Punishment, Heavenly Prison and Earthly Prison is a jail ghost', 'a ghost with the Chopping Block, Goat Blade and Robbery is a throat-cutting ghost' and 'a ghost carrying Huagai and the Lone Spirit is a ghost of monks and Daoists with no heir' (LY.ILL.YY.29), and the Yi Mao's 'Heavenly Prison is a ghost of flogging, beating, penal servitude and exile' and 'Huagai is monks and Daoists, Xianchi is courtesans' (LY.ILL.YM.62). The book does not say whether the 'first month' and so on are the months of the divination or of something else, and here it is copied as written; 'only these four positions, cycling round and round' means that the fifth month returns to the position of the first and the ninth to that of the first, and so on. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
