# 怪动有不祥之祸：怪爻依月份取位（旧注：季月初六爻、仲月二五爻、孟月三四爻），动了才成怪；临父母必有怪器、加玄武是盗人之物、螣蛇又动临鬼爻然后可言有怪

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 怪动有不吉利的灾祸：怪爻按起卦的月份取，一定要动了才成怪，不动就没有；带父母，一定有怪异的器物；加玄武，是偷来的东西；遇到这一爻发动，即使不是鬼爻，也一定是怪事；螣蛇又发动、落在鬼爻上，然后才可以说有怪异。旧注取位是季月落在初爻和六爻，仲月二爻和五爻，孟月三爻和四爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the strange line moves there is inauspicious misfortune: the strange line is taken by the month of casting, and it becomes strange only when it moves — if still, there is none. With Resource there is a strange object; with the Dark Tortoise added, stolen goods; whenever this line moves, even if it is not the Pressure line, it is a strange matter; and only when the Coiling Snake also moves and sits on the Pressure line can one say there is something strange. The old commentary takes the position as the first and sixth lines in the Ji months, the second and fifth in the Zhong months, and the third and fourth in the Meng months.

> [!warning] 打架
> 怪爻按月份落在哪几爻：旧注的口诀是季月（辰戌丑未）在初、六爻，仲月（子午卯酉）在二、五爻，孟月（寅申巳亥）在三、四爻；王洪绪写「初六爻是仲月，二五爻是孟月，三四爻是季月」，把三对爻位和三种月份的配法错开了一位（初六配仲、二五配孟、三四配季）。两家对不上，书里没有说哪个是抄错的。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which lines the strange line falls on by month: the old commentary's rhyme puts the Ji months (Chen, Xu, Chou, Wei) on the first and sixth lines, the Zhong months (Zi, Wu, Mao, You) on the second and fifth, and the Meng months (Yin, Shen, Si, Hai) on the third and fourth; Wang Hongxu writes 'the first and sixth lines are the Zhong months, the second and fifth the Meng months, the third and fourth the Ji months', which shifts the pairing of the three line-pairs and the three kinds of month by one place (first and sixth with Zhong, second and fifth with Meng, third and fourth with Ji). The two do not agree, and the books do not say which is a copying slip.

> [!note] 按
> 怪爻的月份口诀只见于旧注。另外旧注末句是「然后可言有怪」，王洪绪写作「然后可言妖怪」，前者说「有怪异」，后者说「有妖怪」，轻重略有不同。「季月」是辰戌丑未月，「仲月」是子午卯酉月，「孟月」是寅申巳亥月。
>
> *Note:* The rhyme for the months of the strange line is found only in the old commentary. The old commentary's last sentence is 'only then can one say there is something strange' and Wang Hongxu's 'only then can one speak of a monster'; the first says 'something strange', the second 'a monster', a slight difference of weight. The Ji months are those of Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei, the Zhong months those of Zi, Wu, Mao and You, and the Meng months those of Yin, Shen, Si and Hai.
