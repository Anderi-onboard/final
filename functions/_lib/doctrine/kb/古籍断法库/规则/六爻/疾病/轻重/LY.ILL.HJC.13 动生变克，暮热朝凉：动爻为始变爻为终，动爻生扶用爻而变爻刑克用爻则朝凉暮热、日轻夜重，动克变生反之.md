# 动生变克，暮热朝凉：动爻为始变爻为终，动爻生扶用爻而变爻刑克用爻则朝凉暮热、日轻夜重，动克变生反之

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占卦时动爻是开头、变爻是结尾。卦中动爻生扶用爻、而变爻刑克用爻，病一定早上凉快、晚上发热，或者白天轻夜里重；动爻克用爻、变爻生用爻，反过来断。鬼爻化出所喜的爻、所喜的爻化出鬼爻，也是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In every divination the moving line is the beginning and the changed line the end. If the moving line in the hexagram generates and supports the Subject while the changed line punishes and controls it, the illness is surely cool in the morning and hot in the evening, or light by day and heavy at night; if the moving line controls the Subject and the changed line generates it, judge the reverse. Where a Pressure line changes into a welcome line, or a welcome line changes into Pressure, it is the same.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2321 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡动爻为始，变爻为终，若动爻生扶用爻，而变爻刑克用爻者，必朝凉暮热，日轻夜重。 | 凡是动爻为开头，变爻为结尾，如果动爻生扶用爻，而变爻刑克用爻，一定是早上凉、晚上热，白天轻夜里重。 | The moving line is the beginning and the changed line the end; if the moving line generates and supports the Subject while the changed line punishes and controls it, it is surely cool in the morning and hot in the evening, light by day and heavy at night. |
> | 动生变克反此断。 | 动生变克反着这个断。 | Moving generating and changed controlling: judge the reverse of this. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗最后一句原文是「动生变克反此断」，和上一句同样是「动生变克」，意思接不上，应是「动克变生反此断」之误（旧注作「动克变生，反此断」），译文按旧注的意思译。
>
> *Note:* The last sentence of the Bushi Zhengzong reads 'moving generating and changed controlling, judge the reverse of this', which repeats the case of the sentence before and does not follow; it is probably a slip for 'moving controlling and changed generating, judge the reverse' (the old commentary reads 'moving controlling, changed generating, judge the reverse'), and the translation follows the sense of the old commentary.
