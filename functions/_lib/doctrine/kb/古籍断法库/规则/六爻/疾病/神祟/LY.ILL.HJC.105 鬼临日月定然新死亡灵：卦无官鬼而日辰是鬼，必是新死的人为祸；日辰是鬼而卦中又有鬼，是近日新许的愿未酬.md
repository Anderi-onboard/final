# 鬼临日月定然新死亡灵：卦无官鬼而日辰是鬼，必是新死的人为祸；日辰是鬼而卦中又有鬼，是近日新许的愿未酬

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼落在日辰上，一定是刚死不久的亡灵：卦里没有官鬼、日辰是鬼的，一定是刚死的人在作祸；日辰是鬼、卦里又有鬼的，是因为祈祷，近来新许下的愿还没有还。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost that falls on the Day is surely a newly dead spirit: if there is no Pressure in the hexagram and the Day is the ghost, a newly dead person is surely making trouble; if the Day is the ghost and the hexagram also has a ghost, it is because of a prayer: a vow newly made lately has not been paid.

## 《黄金策·鬼神》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7148 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Ghosts and spirits", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如临日月，定然新死亡灵； | 如果鬼落在日、月上，一定是刚死不久的亡灵； | If the ghost falls on the Day or the Month, it is surely a newly dead spirit; |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3395 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦无官鬼而日辰是鬼者，必然新死亡灵为祸。 | 卦里没有官鬼而日辰是鬼的，一定是刚死的亡灵在作祸。 | If there is no Pressure in the hexagram but the Day is the ghost, a newly dead spirit is surely making trouble. |
> | 若日辰是鬼，而卦中又有鬼，是近日新许之愿未酬也。 | 如果日辰是鬼，而卦里又有鬼，是近来新许下的愿还没有还。 | If the Day is the ghost and the hexagram also has a ghost, it is a vow newly made lately that has not been paid. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文说的是「日月」，旧注和王洪绪都只讲日辰是鬼，没有说月建是鬼怎么办。旧注比王洪绪多一层原因：「必因祈祷」。这一条和病体章里「日辰带鬼，亟为祈祷」（LY.ILL.HJC.49、LY.ILL.HJC.50）是同一个看法，那里讲怎样认是哪一路神。
>
> *Note:* The verse speaks of 'Day and Month', but both the old commentary and Wang Hongxu speak only of the Day being the ghost and do not say what to do when the Month is. The old commentary gives one more reason than Wang Hongxu: 'surely because of a prayer'. This entry is the same view as 'when the Day carries the ghost, pray urgently' in the chapter on the patient's condition (LY.ILL.HJC.49 and LY.ILL.HJC.50), where the question of which spirit it is is treated.
