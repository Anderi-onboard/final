# 病患先须得病因：卦静先寻世下伏神、次看鬼爻藏伏处、再以爻象察真；用是病人宜有气、福德是医师、子孙发动勿服药、卦宫旺相病逡巡、六位无财食不纳、兄弟交重气积深、鬼多不一原曾病、用发休囚损病因

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看病先要弄清病因。六爻安静的时候，先找世爻下面伏着的神；再看官鬼藏伏在什么地方；再用爻象去考察它的真假。用爻是病人，应该有气；福德（子孙）是医师，能保全愈；子孙发动就不必服药（原文作「误」，校作「勿」）；卦宫旺相，病就迟疑退缩；六个位置里没有财，吃不下东西；兄弟交重，气积得深；鬼有好几个而不止一个，是原来就有的病；用爻发动却休囚，受损于病因。眉批把这几句压缩成了词条。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In an illness, first find its cause. When the six lines are still, first look for the spirit under the Self; next see where the Pressure line lies hidden; and further use the images of the lines to examine its truth. The Subject line is the sick person and should have qi; Output (the fortune spirit) is the doctor and can bring full recovery; when Output moves, do not take medicine (the text has 'error', which the editor corrects to 'do not'); if the hexagram's palace is strong, the illness hesitates and shrinks back; with no Wealth in the six positions, food is not taken; with Peer crossing heavily, qi is accumulated deep; with many ghosts, not just one, the illness was there before; if the Subject moves while resting or trapped, it is harmed by the cause of the illness. The marginal comment compresses these sentences into headings.

> [!note] 按
> 「安静先寻世下神」的「世下神」，眉批说是「世下伏神」——世爻下面的伏神，这一条和易林补遗「先看世下伏神」（LY.ILL.EL.03、EL.42）是同一个办法。「子孙发动误（勿之误）服药」：原文夹着整理者的校字，「误」是「勿」的误写，意思是子孙发动时不用吃药；易隐的颗师说「子孙动者误服药」（LY.ILL.YY.26）也是这一句的另一个版本，意思是子孙发动的病不必服药。「卦宫旺相病逡巡」的「逡巡」是徘徊、退缩。「用发休囚损病因」和眉批最后「用率病厄」文字含混，这里按字面译，不作展开；眉批第四句「祸随不吉」也含混。「六位无财食不纳」的「财」是妻财，在病里代表饮食。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> *Note:* 'The spirit under the Self' in 'when still, first look for the spirit under the Self' is said by the marginal comment to be 'the hidden spirit under the Self', the hidden line under the Self line, and this entry uses the same method as the Yilin Buyi's 'first look at the hidden line under the Self' (LY.ILL.EL.03, EL.42). In 'when Output moves, wu (mistaken) medicine' the original carries an editor's correction: the character 'error' is a mistaken writing of 'do not', so that when Output moves no medicine need be taken; the Yi Yin's 'Keshi says: when Output moves, error, take medicine' (LY.ILL.YY.26) is another version of the same sentence, and means that an illness with Output moving needs no medicine. 'Hesitates' in 'the illness hesitates and shrinks back' is to linger and retreat. 'The Subject moving while resting or trapped harms the cause of illness' and the last words of the marginal comment, 'the Subject leads illness and calamity', are loose in wording and are translated literally and not expanded; the marginal comment's fourth clause, 'misfortune follows, not auspicious', is also unclear. The 'Wealth' of 'with no Wealth among the six positions, food is not taken' is the Wealth line, which in illness stands for food and drink. The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.
