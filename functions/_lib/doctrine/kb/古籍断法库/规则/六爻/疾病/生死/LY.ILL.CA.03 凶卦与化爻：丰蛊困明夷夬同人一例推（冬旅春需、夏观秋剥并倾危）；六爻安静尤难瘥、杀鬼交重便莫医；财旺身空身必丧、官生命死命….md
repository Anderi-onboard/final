# 凶卦与化爻：丰蛊困明夷夬同人一例推（冬旅春需、夏观秋剥并倾危）；六爻安静尤难瘥、杀鬼交重便莫医；财旺身空身必丧、官生命死命应衰；两重官鬼因劳复；鬼化兄病不宜、化财凶有准、化子瘥无疑

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丰、蛊、困、明夷，夬和同人，一样推断；冬天的旅、春天的需，都困重；夏天的观、秋天的剥，都倾危。六爻安静，尤其难痊；杀和鬼交重，就不要医。财旺而身空，身一定丧；官生而命死，命应衰。两重官鬼，是因劳累复发；鬼化为兄，病不宜；鬼化为财，凶有准；鬼爻化子，痊愈无疑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Feng together with Gu, Kun (Confinement) and Mingyi, and Guai and Tongren: reason the same for all; in winter Lü, in spring Xu: all are trapped and heavy; in summer Guan, in autumn Bo: all topple and are in danger. With the six lines still it is still harder to recover; with killing and ghost crossing heavily one cannot treat it. Wealth strong and the Body Void: the body will surely be lost; the Pressure line alive and the natal line dead: life is surely weak. Two Pressure lines: a relapse from overwork; a ghost changing into Peer: the illness is not favourable; a ghost changing into Wealth: ill is certain; a Pressure line changing into Output: recovery is beyond doubt.

> [!warning] 打架
> 哪几个卦占病最凶，几本书的名单不同。阐奥歌章这一首（LY.ILL.CA.03）是丰、蛊、困、明夷、夬、同人一例，冬旅、春需、夏观、秋剥；第四首（LY.ILL.CA.06）是明夷、蛊、夬、剥、丰、同，第五首是丰、观、需、节、旅、贲、临、明夷、蛊、夬、同人；天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.06）是明夷、观、贲、需、临（世身入墓）和大畜、丰、同、蛊、夬（财鬼俱兴）；断易天机的卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.15）是丰、观、需、剥、节、旅、贲、明夷、蛊、夬、同人「十死分明」。名单大同小异：丰、蛊、夬、同人、明夷几乎处处都在，观、需、贲、旅、剥、节、临、大畜则有的有、有的没有。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which hexagrams are worst in divining illness is listed differently by several books. This poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.03) has Feng, Gu, Kun, Mingyi, Guai and Tongren alike, with Lü in winter, Xu in spring, Guan in summer and Bo in autumn; its fourth poem (LY.ILL.CA.06) has Mingyi, Gu, Guai, Bo, Feng and Tong, and its fifth Feng, Guan, Xu, Jie, Lü, Bi, Lin, Mingyi, Gu, Guai and Tongren; the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.06) has Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin (the Self and Body entering a tomb) and Daxu, Feng, Tong, Gu and Guai (Wealth and the ghost both rising); the Bushi Yuangui passage in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.15) has Feng, Guan, Xu, Bo, Jie, Lü, Bi, Mingyi, Gu, Guai and Tongren, 'the ten deaths plain'. The lists are much alike with small differences: Feng, Gu, Guai, Tongren and Mingyi are almost everywhere, and Guan, Xu, Bi, Lü, Bo, Jie, Lin and Daxu are in some and not in others. The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这是第一首的末六联。「困」是困卦，按「Kun （Confinement）」译；「旅」「需」「观」「剥」是卦名，前面加季节：冬旅、春需、夏观、秋剥，是说这几个卦在这个季节占病特别凶（按季节各有一个卦）。「官生命死命应衰」文字费解，按字面译：官（鬼）在长生、命在死。「两重官鬼因劳复」的「劳复」是病愈后因劳累而复发（中医词）。「六爻安静尤难瘥」和天玄赋「卦爻安静，重为济世之人」（LY.ILL.TXF.14）、野鹤「六爻安静、卦吉可以调理」（LY.ILL.ZS.22）正好相反：这里说安静更难愈——三家说法不同，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Note:* These are the last six couplets of the first poem. 'Kun' is the hexagram Kun (Confinement); 'Lü', 'Xu', 'Guan' and 'Bo' are hexagram names, each given a season: Lü in winter, Xu in spring, Guan in summer, Bo in autumn, meaning that these hexagrams are especially ominous in divining illness in that season (one hexagram for each season). 'The Pressure line alive and the natal line dead' is hard to understand and is rendered literally: the Pressure line at Birth and the natal line at Death. The 'relapse' of 'two Pressure lines: a relapse from overwork' is illness returning through exertion after recovery (a term of Chinese medicine). 'With the six lines still it is still harder to recover' is exactly the opposite of the Tianxuan Fu's 'the hexagram and lines still, weighty is he who saves the world' (LY.ILL.TXF.14) and the Old Man of Yehe's 'six lines still and the hexagram auspicious, it can be treated by nursing' (LY.ILL.ZS.22): here it says that stillness makes recovery harder — the three differ, and the books do not settle it.
