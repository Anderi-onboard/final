# 动入空中值鬼恐失孝思之礼：官爻动空化空，主先亡中有失祀礼，或逢忌日不设祭；在他宫外卦则是眷属中曾有祀礼今取消不设其位

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼动了又落入空亡，恐怕丢了孝敬追思的礼数：官爻发动落空、或变出来空，都主先人亡故的人里有丢了祭祀礼数的，或者遇到忌日没有摆祭；如果在别的宫、外卦，就是亲属里曾经有过祭祀的礼，现在却取消了，不设他的牌位；或者曾经送过纸钱纸帛，现在没有了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost that moves into Void: it is feared the rites of filial remembrance have been neglected. If the Pressure line moves into Void or changes into Void, it means that among the departed forebears some rites of sacrifice have been lost, or no offering was set out on the anniversary of a death; if it is in another palace or the outer trigram, it is that among the kin there was once a rite of sacrifice which has now been dropped and his place is no longer set, or paper offerings used to be sent and now none is.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「禴」是周礼里的一种春夏之祭。「褚帛」按旧注的用法是烧给亡人的纸钱纸帛，书里没有解释，按字面译。王洪绪没有忌日和纸钱两句；他的「眷中曾会祀礼不设其位」比旧注的「眷属中曾有祀礼，今却除之，不设其位」少了「今却除之」，语意不全，可能是传抄脱字，书里没有说明。
>
> *Note:* 'Yue' is a seasonal sacrifice in the Zhou rites, of spring or summer. 'Chubo' in the old commentary's usage is paper money and cloth burned for the dead; the book does not explain it and it is rendered literally. Wang Hongxu lacks the anniversary and paper-offering sentences; his 'among the kin a rite of sacrifice was once held, and his place is not set' lacks the old commentary's 'now dropped' and is incomplete in sense, perhaps a scribal omission, which the book does not explain.
