# 鬼在死绝其病本轻，逢日辰动爻生扶是绝处逢生，病要好了又重、安而复作；财生鬼则因饮食或女色加重

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻落在死、绝的爻位上，病本来轻。遇到日辰或动爻来生扶，叫绝处逢生，病一定是先轻后重、快好了又犯。如果是财爻生鬼，一定是因为饮食使病加重，或者因为又和女人同房。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line on a death or extinction position means a light illness. When the Day or a moving line generates or supports it, this is called meeting life in a place of extinction, and the illness is surely light and then heavy again, settling and then flaring. If Wealth generates Pressure, the illness is surely made heavier by food and drink, or by being with a woman again.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 官鬼逢绝，其病必轻，如遇生扶，谓之绝处逢生，其病必将复作。 | 官鬼逢绝，这病一定轻，如果再遇上生扶，叫绝处逢生，这病一定还要犯。 | A Pressure line meeting extinction: the illness is surely light; if it then meets generation or support, this is called meeting life in a place of extinction, and the illness will surely flare again. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「妇人重合」的「重合」字面是「再合」。财爻既管饮食，又是男人的妻和女人，旧注这一句按上下文读作又和女人同房；书里没有再解释。「财生鬼」这一句王洪绪没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Chong he' in 'a woman, chong he' literally means 'coming together again'. Wealth governs food and drink and is also the wife and woman for a man, so the old commentary is read from the context as being with a woman again; the book does not explain it further. Wang Hongxu does not take the sentence on 'Wealth generating Pressure'.
