# 总断千金赋·化病兮伤损（旧注：病即长生对冲之神，主象化出病爻则凡事有损，占病未痊；王洪绪：十二长生里只有长生墓绝三件要紧，不可死守「病主伤损」）

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「化成病，就有伤损。」旧注：「病」是和长生相冲的那一位；所占的主象化出病爻，凡事都有损伤：占病，病没有好；占物，不合意；占药，无效；占文书，有破绽；占行人，没有回来；占身命，带着病；占妇人，一定不贞洁；占容貌，一定有破相；所以叫伤损。王洪绪提醒：十二长生里卦中只有长生、墓、绝三件要紧，其余各位都按生克冲合、进退神、伏吟反吟来论，不可死抱着「病主伤损」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'Turning into Illness, there is damage.' The old commentary: 'Illness' is the spirit that clashes the Birth stage; when the principal image being divined turns into the Illness line, all affairs suffer damage: in divining illness, the illness is not cured; a thing, it is not right; medicine, it is ineffective; documents, there is a flaw; a traveller, he has not returned; one's person and fate, one carries illness; a woman, she is surely not chaste; looks, there is surely a blemish; hence it is called damage. Wang Hongxu cautions: of the twelve stages of life only Birth, Tomb and Extinction matter in a hexagram, and the others are all to be judged by generation, control, clash and combination, advancing and retreating spirit, Hidden Chant and Reversed Chant, so one must not cling to 'Illness means damage'.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「化病主伤损」能不能一概而论，两家看法不同。旧注把它当成通则：主象化出病（长生的对冲），凡事有损，占病未痊、占药无效、占行人未回等；王洪绪提醒：十二长生里，卦中只有长生、墓、绝三件要紧，其余各位（病在内）都要按生克冲合、进退神、伏吟反吟来论，不可死抱着「病主伤损」。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two commentaries differ on whether 'turning into Illness means damage' holds in every case. The old commentary takes it as a general rule: the principal image turning into Illness (the clash of Birth) damages everything, with the illness not cured, medicine ineffective, the traveller not returned and so on; Wang Hongxu cautions that of the twelve stages of life only Birth, Tomb and Extinction matter in a hexagram, and the rest (Illness among them) must be judged by generation, control, clash and combination, advancing and retreating spirit, Hidden Chant and Reversed Chant, without clinging to 'Illness means damage'. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这里的「病」是十二长生里的一位（长生、沐浴、冠带、临官、帝旺、衰、病、死、墓、绝、胎、养），不是疾病；旧注说它是「长生对冲之神」，按十二长生的顺序，病正好在长生的对面。「化病」是动爻变出来的那个地支落在所占五行的「病」位上。占病只是旧注一连串应用里的一条（占病未痊），所以放在「动变」里。同一联的下一句「化胎兮勾连」与病无关，没有收。
>
> *Note:* The 'Illness' here is one stage of the twelve stages of life (Birth, Bathing, Capping, Official Post, Peak, Decline, Illness, Death, Tomb, Extinction, Womb, Nurture), not a disease; the old commentary calls it 'the spirit that clashes Birth', and in the order of the twelve stages Illness stands exactly opposite Birth. 'Turning into Illness' is the branch a moving line changes into falling on the Illness stage of the element being divined. Divining illness is only one of a run of applications in the old commentary (the illness is not cured), which is why it is placed under moving lines and what they turn into. The next line of the same couplet, 'turning into Womb: entanglement', has nothing to do with illness and is not taken.
