# 散破无援脱气、忌摇元动仇兴：用神临散、遇破、全无生扶、脱气，四者若无根蒂少吉多凶；忌神动克用神，有元神发动可救，只恐仇神又动则凶危

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神临在散上、遇到破、完全没有生扶、或者被脱气，这四种情形要是没有根基，少吉多凶。忌神动来克用神，如果有元神发动，就许它有救，只怕仇神又动，就主凶险危急。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject sits on dispersal, meets a break, has no generation or support at all, or is drained of qi, and it has no root in any of these four cases, there is little luck and much ill. When the Adversary moves and controls the Subject, if the Support moves, promise rescue; the only fear is that the Enemy spirit moves as well, and then it means danger and misfortune.

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 12647 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 散破无援脱气，忌摇元动仇兴。 | 散、破、没有援助、脱气；忌神摇动、元神发动、仇神兴起。 | Scattered, broken, without help, drained of qi; the Adversary stirs, the Support moves, the Enemy rises. |
> | 用神临散、遇破，及全无生扶、或脱气，此四者若无根蒂，少吉多凶。 | 用神临在散上、遇到破，以及完全没有生扶、或者被脱气，这四种情形要是没有根基，少吉多凶。 | If the Subject sits on dispersal, meets a break, has no generation or support at all, or is drained of qi, and it has no root in any of these four cases, there is little luck and much ill. |
> | 忌神动克用神，若有元神发动，许之有救，只恐仇神又动，当主凶危，宜于元神章内详之。 | 忌神动来克用神，如果有元神发动，就许它有救，只怕仇神又动，就主凶险危急，要在元神章里详看。 | When the Adversary moves and controls the Subject, if the Support moves, promise rescue; the only fear is that the Enemy spirit moves as well, and then it means danger and misfortune; see the chapter on the Support for the details. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「散」「破」是爻被日月冲散、冲破；「脱气」是爻的力量被它生出的爻泄走。「仇神」是和元神作对的那一类爻（生忌神、克元神），详见书里的元神章。
>
> *Note:* 'Dispersed' and 'broken' are a line clashed apart or broken by the Day or Month; 'drained of qi' is a line whose strength is leaked away by the line it generates. The 'Enemy spirit' is the kind of line that works against the Support (it generates the Adversary and controls the Support); see the chapter on the Support in the book.
