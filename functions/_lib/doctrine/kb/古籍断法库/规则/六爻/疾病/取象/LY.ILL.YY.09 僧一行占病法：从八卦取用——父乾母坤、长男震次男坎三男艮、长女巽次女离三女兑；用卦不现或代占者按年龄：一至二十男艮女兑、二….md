# 僧一行占病法：从八卦取用——父乾母坤、长男震次男坎三男艮、长女巽次女离三女兑；用卦不现或代占者按年龄：一至二十男艮女兑、二十一至四十男坎女离、四十一至六十男震女巽、六十以上男乾女坤；正卦无用象取伏卦；主象化死墓绝克破皆死

六爻 · 疾病 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 僧一行占病的办法，从八卦里取用：父亲用乾，母亲用坤，长子用震，次子用坎，三子用艮，长女用巽，次女用离，三女用兑。如果所用的卦不出现，或者是替别人占，就按年龄：一岁到二十岁，男的用艮，女的用兑；二十一岁到四十岁，男的用坎，女的用离；四十一岁到六十岁，男的用震，女的用巽；六十岁以上，男的用乾，女的用坤。正卦里没有用的象，就取伏卦。主象变出死、墓、绝，或者变出克它、破它的，都主死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The monk Yixing's method of divining illness takes the Subject from the eight trigrams: father takes Qian, mother Kun, eldest son Zhen, second son Kan, third son Gen, eldest daughter Xun, second daughter Li, third daughter Dui. If the trigram to be used does not appear, or one is divining for another, go by age: from one to twenty, a male uses Gen and a female Dui; from twenty-one to forty, a male Kan and a female Li; from forty-one to sixty, a male Zhen and a female Xun; over sixty, a male Qian and a female Kun. If the main hexagram has no image to use, take the hidden hexagram. If the main image changes into Death, tomb or Extinction, or into what controls or breaks it, in every case it means death.

> [!note] 按
> 这是另一套办法：不在六爻里找六亲，而是拿八卦对家人（乾父坤母，震坎艮是三个儿子，巽离兑是三个女儿）。按年龄取卦是给「用卦不出现」和代占者用的备用办法。这一条和上面六爻取用（LY.ILL.YY.03）不是一回事，书里没有说两者冲突时怎么办。「僧一行」是唐代的一行和尚，书里没有另作介绍。
>
> *Note:* This is another method: instead of looking for the six relations among the lines, the eight trigrams are matched to the family (Qian father, Kun mother, Zhen, Kan and Gen the three sons, Xun, Li and Dui the three daughters). Taking the trigram by age is a fallback for when the trigram to be used does not appear and for divining for another. It is not the same as taking the Subject among the six lines (LY.ILL.YY.03), and the book does not say what to do when the two conflict. 'The monk Yixing' is the Tang monk Yixing, whom the book does not otherwise introduce.
