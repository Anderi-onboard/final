# 生死·死日：忌神长生之日死（土用亥日、木用巳日、火用申日、水用巳午日、金用寅日）；土鬼动者鬼爻长生之日死；六冲卦用爻败死墓绝之日死；用空无气者元神绝忌神生之日死；用逢空冲者初病即愈久病难痊；土旺动克用者克用爻之日死；卦有三无救（无火无财无子）

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 死的日子：忌神长生的那天死（土做用的，亥日死；木的，巳日死；火的，申日死；水的，巳日、午日死；金的，寅日死）；土鬼发动的，鬼爻长生的那天死；卦是六冲的，用爻败、死、墓、绝的那天死；用爻落空又没有气的，原神绝、忌神生的那天死；土旺盛发动克到用爻的，克用爻的那天死。用爻遇空又被冲，刚起的病马上会好，久病却难好。卦里有三种没有救的：没有火、没有财、没有子孙。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The days of death: he dies on the day the Adversary is born (for Earth as the Subject, a Hai day; Wood, a Si day; Fire, a Shen day; Water, a Si or Wu day; Metal, a Yin day); if an Earth ghost moves, on the day the Pressure line is born; if the hexagram is Six-Clash, on a day when the Subject line is at ruin, death, tomb or Extinction; if the Subject line is Void and without force, on the day the Support is extinct and the Adversary is born; if strong moving Earth controls the Subject line, on the day that controls it. If the Subject line meets Void and is clashed, a recent illness is cured at once while a long illness is hard to cure. A hexagram has three kinds of no rescue: no Fire, no Wealth, no Output.

## 《易隐·疾病占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2993 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining illness", base text

> | 土鬼动者，鬼爻长生之日死。 | 土鬼发动的，鬼爻长生的那天死。 | If an Earth ghost moves, he dies on the day the Pressure line is born. |
> | 卦六冲者，用爻败死墓绝之日死。 | 卦是六冲的，用爻败、死、墓、绝的那天死。 | If the hexagram is Six-Clash, he dies on a day when the Subject line is at ruin, death, tomb or Extinction. |

> | 土旺动持克用者，克用爻之日死也。 | 土旺盛、发动、克到用爻的，克用爻的那天死。 | If Earth is strong and moving and controls the Subject line, he dies on the day that controls the Subject line. |
> | 卦有三无救者，无火无财无子也。 | 卦里有三种没有救的：没有火、没有财、没有子孙。 | A hexagram is beyond rescue in three ways: no Fire, no Wealth, no Output. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「三无救」的「财」「子」书里写得很简，按上下文是财爻和子孙爻；火是官鬼所怕的克，火能克金鬼，财能生官，子孙能制官，这里书里没有解释为什么这三样是救。「败」是十二长生里的沐浴位（又叫败地）。这一条讲死日，和增删卜易讲的「用神死绝生旺日」不同处见 LY.ILL.HJC.57。
>
> *Note:* In the 'three kinds of no rescue', 'Wealth' and 'Output' are written very briefly and by context are the Wealth and Output lines; the book does not explain why these three are what save. 'Ruin' (bai) is the Bath stage of the twelve life stages (also called the ruin ground). This entry gives the days of death; for how it differs from the days given elsewhere see LY.ILL.HJC.57.
