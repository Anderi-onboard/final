# 蚕桑·世爻为养蚕妇，临官鬼蚕姑必有病（旧注：世爻无故自空或化死墓绝空蚕姑当有大难，带自刑身亦病）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》蚕桑章的旧注说：世爻代表养蚕的妇女；世爻临在官鬼上，养蚕的妇人一定有病；世爻无缘无故自己落空，或者化成死、墓、绝、空，养蚕的妇人要有大难，不然就是懒于抬蚕喂养；如果又带自刑，她自己也有病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* An old commentary in the Huangjin Ce's chapter on silkworms says: the Self line stands for the woman who raises the silkworms; if it sits on Pressure, the silkworm woman is sure to be ill; if the Self line falls Void for no reason, or turns into death, tomb, extinction or Void, the silkworm woman is to meet great trouble, or else she is lazy about lifting and feeding the silkworms; if it also carries a self-punishment, she herself is ill too.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7381 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 临官鬼，蚕姑必有病。 | （世爻）临在官鬼上，养蚕的妇人一定有病。 | If it sits on Pressure, the silkworm woman is sure to be ill. |
> | 世爻无故自空，或化死墓绝空，蚕姑当有大难； | 世爻无缘无故自己落空，或者化成死、墓、绝、空，养蚕的妇人要有大难； | If the Self line falls Void for no reason, or turns into death, tomb, extinction or Void, the silkworm woman is to meet great trouble; |
> | 不然，必懒于抬饲。 | 不然，就一定是懒于抬蚕、喂养。 | otherwise she is sure to be lazy about lifting and feeding the silkworms. |
> | 若带自刑，身亦病。 | 如果又带自刑，她自己也有病。 | If it also carries a self-punishment, she herself is ill too. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 蚕桑章的旧注把世爻当成养蚕的妇人，按世爻上临的六亲、旺衰断她是什么样的人：墓库是老年妇，胎养长生是少年女子，临太岁财爻是老手，临子孙精细，临兄懒，临父母辛勤但伤蚕，这里只收讲病的几句。「蚕姑」是养蚕的妇人。「自刑」是辰午酉亥这几个地支自己刑自己。这里讲的病是养蚕妇人自己的病，不是蚕的病（蚕的病不在本域）。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary in the chapter on silkworms takes the Self line as the woman raising the silkworms and judges what sort of woman she is from the relation on the Self line and its strength: a tomb is an old woman, Womb, Nurture or Birth a young girl, Wealth with Tai Sui an old hand, Output fine and careful, Peer lazy, Resource hardworking but harming the silkworms; only the sentences on illness are taken here. The 'silkworm woman' is the woman who raises them. A 'self-punishment' is a branch among Chen, Wu, You and Hai punishing itself. The illness spoken of here is the woman's own illness and not the silkworms' (illness of silkworms is not in this domain).
