# 神煞歌例·解神：月解若值此辰官事散纵然重病也离身；日解戊癸鸡啼官事散病人无药不须忧；解脱神戊癸辰逢官讼消病人不必生忧虑

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神煞歌例里三个解神讲到病：月解神，遇到这个月的解神位，官事消散，纵然重病也会离开身体；日解神，戊、癸日逢酉，官事散，病人没有药也不用忧；解脱神，戊、癸日逢辰，官司消，病人不必忧虑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Three resolving spirits in the verses on spirit-stars bear on illness: the Month Resolution, on meeting the place of the month's Resolution, official business scatters and even a grave illness leaves the body; the Day Resolution, on Wu and Gui days meeting You, official business scatters and a sick person without medicine need not worry; the Release spirit, on Wu and Gui days meeting Chen, lawsuits dissolve and the sick person need not worry.

> [!note] 按
> 月解、日解、解脱神是「解神」一类，本义是解官事（官非、官讼），歌里顺带说到病人。月解神的位置和断易天机的月解表一样（申酉戌亥午未，LY.ILL.DYTJ.30），说法的结尾不同：断易天机是「病人有此解神，天医上卦即病愈」，卜筮全书是「若值此辰官事散，纵然重病也离身」。日解神的歌和断易天机一字不差（LY.ILL.DYTJ.30）。解脱神这一首断易天机里没有。「逢某支」「若值此辰」怎样才算遇上，歌里没有讲。
>
> *Note:* The Month Resolution, the Day Resolution and the Release spirit are 'resolving spirits', whose original use is to resolve official business (official trouble, lawsuits), and the verses mention the sick person in passing. The positions of the Month Resolution are those of the table in the Duanyi Tianji (Shen, You, Xu, Hai, Wu, Wei, LY.ILL.DYTJ.30), and the wording of the ending differs: the Duanyi Tianji has 'a sick person with this resolving spirit, with the Heavenly Physician in the hexagram, is cured at once', the Bushi Quanshu has 'if one meets this branch official business scatters, and even a grave illness leaves the body'. The verse of the Day Resolution is word for word that of the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.30). The verse of the Release spirit is not in the Duanyi Tianji. The verses do not say what counts as 'meeting a certain branch' or 'falling on this branch'.
