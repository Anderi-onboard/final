# 卜筮元龟·占疾病卦例：内戒伤五脏、外戒伤皮肤、骸骨羸瘦、棺椁心惊、血脉疮痍、游魂恍惚、归魂精魄暗扶身

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内戒卦，病伤五脏；外戒卦，病伤皮肤；骸骨卦，羸弱消瘦；棺椁卦，心惊多恐惧；血脉卦，疮痍；游魂卦，恍惚不安宁；归魂卦，精魄暗中扶着身子，气息沉静，懒得说话。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* An Inner Warning hexagram: the illness injures the five viscera; an Outer Warning hexagram: it injures the skin; a Bones hexagram: frail and wasted; a Coffin hexagram: a startled heart and much fear; a Blood Vessels hexagram: sores; a Wandering Soul hexagram: dazed and not at rest; a Returning Soul hexagram: the spirit-souls quietly support the body, the breath is still and the heart too listless to speak.

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1681–1682 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Section 24, on illness: Bushi Yuangui", base text

> [!note] 按
> 内戒、外戒、骸骨、棺椁、血脉、游魂、归魂都是卦的名称：易隐引京房的「十六变卦」，每一宫的十六个变卦里依次有游魂、外戒、内戒、归魂、绝命、血脉、肌肉、骸骨、棺槨、坟墓这些名字（乾宫是晋为游魂、旅为外戒、鼎为内戒、大有为归魂、离为绝命、噬嗑为血脉、颐为肌肉、益为骸骨、无妄为棺槨、同人为坟墓），见 LY.ILL.YY.36。「戒」字按 Warning 译，「棺椁」按 Coffin 译，这是这本库里的译法，原文没有解释。「血疾有无虑」原文作「血脉疮痍有无虑」，「有无虑」意思读不清，按字面译并标出不明。「游魂恍惚」和天玄赋、阐奥歌章说的游魂恍惚是同一个说法（LY.ILL.CA.04、LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）。
>
> *Note:* Inner Warning, Outer Warning, Bones, Coffin, Blood Vessels, Wandering Soul and Returning Soul are all names of hexagrams: the Yi Yin, quoting Jing Fang's 'sixteen changed hexagrams', has among the sixteen changed hexagrams of each palace in order the names Wandering Soul, Outer Warning, Inner Warning, Returning Soul, End of Life, Blood Vessels, Flesh, Bones, Coffin and Grave (in the Qian palace Jin is the Wandering Soul, Lü the Outer Warning, Ding the Inner Warning, Dayou the Returning Soul, Li the End of Life, Shihe the Blood Vessels, Yi (Nourishment) the Flesh, Yi (Increase) the Bones, Wuwang the Coffin and Tongren the Grave), see LY.ILL.YY.36. 'Jie' is rendered 'Warning' and 'guan guo' 'Coffin', which is the rendering used in this library, and the original does not explain them. The original line is 'blood vessels, sores and wounds, whether or not there is worry', and 'you wu lü' does not read clearly, so it is rendered literally and marked as unclear. 'The Wandering Soul dazed' is the same saying as in the Chan'ao Gezhang and Sun Bin's song (LY.ILL.CA.04, LY.ILL.DYTJ.03).
