# 子官强弱定药效：无子药不良、强官病不治；子强官弱如烈日销冰、子弱官强如铅刀攻玉；财动鬼静子动无功；伏鬼根不除、化鬼病传变、空鬼药老；世鬼病曲折、应鬼药跷蹊

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有子孙，药就不好；官鬼强，病就治不好。子孙强、官鬼弱，像烈日融冰；子孙弱、官鬼强，像用铅刀去攻玉。财爻动、鬼爻静，子孙动，都没有功效。鬼伏着，病根拔不掉；变出鬼，病会传变；鬼落空，药就是「老」药。鬼临世爻而兴旺，我的病就曲折反复；鬼临应爻而旺相，那副药就古怪蹊跷。注说：要审察子孙和官鬼的强弱，鬼当值日、月时，即使子孙得到生扶也不算完全好，要比较子孙能不能制住鬼，才是吉占；鬼落空是躲开了子孙，药打不中病，所以叫「老」；鬼在世，病难很快好，鬼在应，医生诡诈难信。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Without Output the medicine is not good, and with a strong Pressure line the illness cannot be cured. Output strong and Pressure weak is a fierce sun melting ice; Output weak and Pressure strong is a lead knife attacking jade. With Wealth moving and the ghost still, or Output moving, there is no effect. A hidden ghost means the root is not removed; a change into a ghost means the illness passes on and changes; a Void ghost makes the medicine 'aged'. A ghost thriving on the Self makes my illness twist and turn, and a ghost strong on the Other makes that medicine dubious. The note says: one must examine the strength of Output and Pressure; when the ghost is on duty in the Day or Month, even if Output meets generation and support it is not wholly good, and one must compare whether Output can subdue the ghost before it is an auspicious divination; a Void ghost evades Output and so the medicine does not hit the illness, hence 'aged'; a ghost on the Self means the illness is hard to cure quickly, and a ghost on the Other means the doctor is deceitful and hard to trust.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 经文里的「老医药」，注说是「鬼空避子，药不中病，故云老也」：「老」是指药不对症，像一味放老了的药。「财动鬼静，子动无功」这一句原文不带说明，注也没解；字面是财爻动、鬼爻静、子孙动的时候没有功效，和上一句「子强官弱」并不矛盾——财动是在生鬼（或冲克父母），子孙即使动，制鬼的力气也被财爻的生助抵掉了，这是推的，书里没有写。「传（转？）变」的「转？」是转录者自己标的疑字，这里照录，没有改。这一条与黄金策医药章「官旺福衰药饵轻而病重」（LY.ILL.HJC.61）、「福化忌爻误服杀身之恶剂」（HJC.66）是同一路读法。
>
> *Note:* For the text's 'aged medicine', the note says a Void ghost evades Output and the medicine does not hit the illness, hence it is called aged: 'aged' means the medicine does not fit the illness, like a herb left to go stale. The sentence 'Wealth moving, the ghost still, Output moving: no effect' comes without comment in the text and the note does not explain it; literally it says there is no effect when Wealth moves, the ghost is still and Output moves, and this does not conflict with 'Output strong and Pressure weak' just before, since Wealth moving feeds the ghost (or clashes the Resource line) and even a moving Output has its strength against the ghost cancelled by the Wealth's support, but this is inferred and not written in the book. The 'transforms?' after 'passes on' is the transcriber's own mark of doubt and is copied as it stands. This entry reads in the same way as the Huangjin Ce's medicine chapter, 'Pressure strong and Output weak, the medicine light and the illness heavy' (LY.ILL.HJC.61) and 'Output changing into an Adversary line, a bad draught that kills' (HJC.66).
