# 疾病论·总起：凡占疾病先祷上下神祇；问生死祸福须详得病之由、次告先贤之验

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论一篇开头说：凡是占疾病，先向天神地祇祷告；去问生死祸福，要把得病的缘由问得详细，再告诉他前贤应验过的例子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness opens: in divining illness, first pray to the spirits above and below; in asking about life and death, calamity and fortune, the cause of the illness must be learned in detail, and then the confirmations of the former sages told.

## 《卜筮全书·阐幽精要·十一、疾病论》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9770 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Chanyou Jingyao, no. 11: on illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 谒卜卦人， | 去找卜卦的人， | In going to the diviner, |
> | 欲问生死祸福， | 想问生死祸福， | wishing to ask about life and death, calamity and fortune, |
> | 须详得病之由， | 要详细问明得病的缘由， | the cause of the illness must be learned in detail, |
> | 次告先贤之验。 | 然后再讲前贤应验过的例子。 | and next the confirmations of the former sages told. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这是疾病论头两句。「先祷上下神祇」是占病之前的礼仪：向天神地祇祷告。「得病之由」是病的来由，占之前要问清，后面讲神鬼、病因的各句都要靠它。「次告先贤之验」的主语书里没有写，这里按「把前人验过的例子告诉他」读。
>
> *Note:* These are the first two sentences of the chapter on illness. 'First pray to the spirits above and below' is the ritual before a divination about illness: a prayer to the spirits of heaven and earth. 'The cause of the illness' is how the illness came about, which must be asked before the divination, since the later lines on spirits and causes depend on it. The subject of 'next tell the confirmations of the former sages' is not written in the book, and it is read here as 'the examples confirmed by the ancients are told to him'.
