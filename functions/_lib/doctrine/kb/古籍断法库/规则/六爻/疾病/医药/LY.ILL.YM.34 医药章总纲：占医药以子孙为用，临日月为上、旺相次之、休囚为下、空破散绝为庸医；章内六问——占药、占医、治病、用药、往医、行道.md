# 医药章总纲：占医药以子孙为用，临日月为上、旺相次之、休囚为下、空破散绝为庸医；章内六问——占药、占医、治病、用药、往医、行道

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占医药的总纲：拿子孙爻当用。子孙临日、月是上等，旺相是次等，休囚是下等，空、破、散、绝就是庸医。这一章开头先说了几句话：得志的时候愿意做良相，不得志的时候愿意做良医；君子借医来施展志向，做儿子的要懂得医理，吉凶取舍能不慎重吗？章里分六问细讲：占药、占医、治病、用药、往医、行道；头两问是病家问，后四问（治病、用药、往医、行道）都是医生自己问。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The outline for divining medicine and doctors: take Output as the one to use. Output sitting on the Day or Month is best, thriving and strong is next, resting and trapped is lowest, and Void, broken, scattered or in Extinction is a mediocre doctor. The chapter opens with a few remarks: when successful one wishes to be a good minister, when not, a good doctor; the gentleman carries out his will through medicine, and a son must know medicine, so how can one not be careful in choosing between fortune and misfortune? The chapter then treats six questions in detail: divining the medicine, divining the doctor, treating the illness, using the medicine, going to treat, and the doctor's practice; the first two are asked by the patient's family and the last four (treating, using, going, practising) are all asked by the doctor himself.

> [!note] 按
> 「用」在这里指取用，和「用神」是一回事：占医药不取病人的用神，取子孙爻。黄金策的医药章同样以子孙为药饵、应爻为医人（见 LY.ILL.HJC.58），下面第二条（LY.ILL.YM.36）也说应爻指医人。目录注里的「往医」「行道」是医生的问法（见后面 LY.ILL.YM.40、YM.41）：「往医」是医生上门去治，「行道」是医生行医的前程。这一条把原文里的目录注放在了最后。
>
> *Note:* 'Use' here means the one to take, the same thing as the Subject: in divining medicine one does not take the patient's Subject but the Output line. The Huangjin Ce's chapter on medicine likewise takes Output as the medicine and the Other as the doctor (see LY.ILL.HJC.58), and the second entry below (LY.ILL.YM.36) also says the Other stands for the doctor. 'Going to treat' and 'practising' in the contents note are the doctor's own questions (see LY.ILL.YM.40 and YM.41 below): 'going to treat' is the doctor visiting a patient, and 'practising' is the course of the doctor's practice. In this entry the contents note from the original is placed last.
