# 鬼者病之源、祟之状：鬼旺则重、衰则轻、动则骤而变、合则染而缠、散则疴将解、空则病未真

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼是病的来源，是祟作怪的样子：所以鬼旺病就重，鬼衰病就轻；鬼动，病来得急而且会变；鬼被合住，灾就染上身、缠着不放；鬼被冲散，病快要解了；鬼落空，病还不是真的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ghost is the source of illness and the shape of the haunting: so when the ghost is strong the illness is heavy, and when the ghost is weak it is light; when the ghost moves the trouble comes on suddenly and changes; when the ghost combines the calamity clings and lingers; when the ghost scatters the ailment is about to be resolved; when the ghost is Void the illness is not yet real.

## 《易冒·疾病章第五十五》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1225 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 55: Illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 夫鬼者，病之源也，祟之状也，是以鬼旺而重，鬼衰而轻，鬼动患骤而变，鬼合灾染而缠，鬼散而疴将解，鬼空而病未真。 | 鬼是病的来源，是祟作怪的样子，所以鬼旺病就重，鬼衰病就轻，鬼动病来得急而且会变，鬼被合住灾就染上身、缠着不放，鬼被冲散病快要解了，鬼落空病还不是真的。 | The ghost is the source of illness and the shape of the haunting; therefore, when the ghost is strong the illness is heavy, when the ghost is weak it is light; when the ghost moves the trouble comes on suddenly and changes; when the ghost combines the calamity clings and lingers; when the ghost scatters the ailment is about to be resolved; when the ghost is Void the illness is not yet real. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这句和痘疹章开头「鬼空、鬼静、鬼散」那一串（LY.ILL.YM.01）是同一套读法，只是把「痘」换成了一般的「病」：痘疹章说鬼空则根芽未萌，这里说鬼空则病未真。「合」在这里是被合住的意思，不是好事：灾缠着不放。
>
> *Note:* This sentence uses the same reading as the run of 'ghost Void, ghost still, ghost scattered' at the head of the chapter on pox (LY.ILL.YM.01), with the general 'illness' put in the place of 'pox': there a Void ghost means the roots and shoots have not sprouted, here a Void ghost means the illness is not yet real. 'Combines' here means the ghost is held by a combination, which is not a good thing: the calamity clings and will not let go.
