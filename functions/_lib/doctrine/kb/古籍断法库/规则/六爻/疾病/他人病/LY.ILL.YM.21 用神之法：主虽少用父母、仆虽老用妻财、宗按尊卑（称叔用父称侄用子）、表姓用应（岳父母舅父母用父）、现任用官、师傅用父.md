# 用神之法：主虽少用父母、仆虽老用妻财、宗按尊卑（称叔用父称侄用子）、表姓用应（岳父母舅父母用父）、现任用官、师傅用父

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 取用神的办法，看的是关系，不看年纪和亲疏：主人即使年纪小，用父母爻；仆人即使年纪老，用妻财爻；同宗的人即使远到五服，称叔的用父爻，称侄的用子孙爻；异姓的表亲即使近到三党之内，也用应爻，只有岳父母、舅父母才用父爻；占现任的官，即使关系亲密，也用官爻而不用应爻；占师傅，即使关系疏远，也用父爻而不用应爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The method of taking the Subject goes by the relation, not by age or closeness: a master, though young, takes the Resource line; a servant, though old, takes the Wealth line; a clansman, even as distant as the five mourning grades, takes the Resource line if addressed as uncle and the Output line if addressed as nephew; kin of another surname, even as near as the three clans, take the Other line, and only a father- or mother-in-law or a maternal uncle or aunt takes the Resource line; a serving official, however close, takes the Pressure line and not the Other; a teacher, however distant, takes the Resource line and not the Other.

> [!warning] 打架
> 家里的主人是用哪一爻：易冒说主人即使年纪小，也用父母爻，占仆人用妻财爻（作者自序里也说「老奴占幼主必用父母，少君占衰仆必用妻财」）；易隐说奴仆丁口占家主用五爻，占主母用二爻（LY.ILL.YY.03）。两本书说的都是仆人和主人这一对关系，取的爻却不同。易冒对君臣另有一条：臣占君用九五（LY.ILL.YM.23），所以在易冒里，九五只给君主、不给家主。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line stands for the master of a household: the Yi Mao says a master, however young, takes the Resource line, and a servant takes the Wealth line (the author's own preface says the same: 'an old servant divining a young master must take the Resource, a young lord divining a feeble servant must take the Wealth'); the Yi Yin says servants and household hands divining the master of the house take the fifth line, and divining the mistress take the second line (LY.ILL.YY.03). Both speak of the same pair, servant and master, but take different lines. The Yi Mao has a separate rule for ruler and subject: a minister divining the sovereign takes the ninth-five line (LY.ILL.YM.23), so in the Yi Mao the ninth-five line is for the sovereign only, not for the master of a household.

> [!note] 按
> 「五服」是丧服的五个等级（斩衰、齐衰、大功、小功、缌麻），「三党」是父党、母党、妻党。注里说同宗的人「称叔用父，称侄用子」，是按对方相对于自己的辈分取，不管血缘多远；异姓的亲戚（表姓）一律取应爻，只有岳父母、舅父母算父辈，才用父爻。
>
> *Note:* The 'five mourning grades' are the five degrees of mourning dress (from the coarsest sackcloth down to the finest hemp), and the 'three clans' are the father's, the mother's and the wife's. The note says that for a clansman one takes 'addressed as uncle, the Resource; addressed as nephew, the Output', that is, by his generation relative to oneself and however remote the blood; relatives of another surname are all taken by the Other line, and only a father- or mother-in-law or a maternal uncle or aunt counts as a senior generation and takes the Resource line.
