# 不必按五行硬指哪路神：金官指为关公岳公等武神不对，武神有封有未封各地不同；须问病人许愿未酬或污秽了哪座庙，把所疑的神占一卦，鬼爻旺或动就是此神，不上卦再占别处；得罪正神用香花纸马祭，招惹邪祟用浆纸钱夜静时送（金鬼向西、水鬼向北）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 古时候按五行分是哪路神，比如金官就说是天将、关公、金刚、伍公、岳公这些武神，觉子认为不对：武神有朝廷敕封的、有没有封的，各地风俗又不同，书里没记的更多，不可能一座庙一座庙地祭。该问病人是不是在哪座武神庙里许了愿没有还、或者在哪座庙里弄脏糟蹋了；凡是心里怀疑哪位神，就指着那位神占一卦，鬼爻旺或发动就是这位神，不上卦再占别处的神。鬼临木火水土的，都这样办。得罪了正神的，用香花纸马祭；招惹了邪祟的鬼，用浆水和纸钱，夜深人静时送走，金鬼向西送，水鬼向北送。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In ancient times the gods were told apart by the five phases, for example a Metal Pressure was said to be a heavenly general, Guan Gong, a Vajra, Wu Gong, Yue Gong and the other martial gods; Juezi thinks this wrong: martial gods include those enfeoffed by imperial decree and those not, local customs differ, and those not recorded in books are still more, so one cannot sacrifice at temple after temple. One should ask the patient whether he vowed something in the temple of some martial god and has not paid it, or defiled and abused some temple; whatever god he suspects in his heart, point to that god and divine, and if the Pressure line is strong or moving it is that god, and if it is not in the hexagram divine the god of some other place. Pressure on Wood, Fire, Water or Earth is treated in the same way. If he has offended an orthodox god, sacrifice with incense, flowers and paper horses; if he has provoked an evil haunting ghost, use rice-water and paper money and send it off in the stillness of the night, a Metal ghost to the west and a Water ghost to the north.

> [!note] 按
> 觉子上一条（LY.ILL.ZS.23）先按五行分了大类，这一条说不必再把大类落实到某位具体的神：古人把金官指为关公岳飞等，他不同意，主张问病人心里疑的是哪位神，再占一卦验证。「金官」按字面是金爻的官鬼。「浆纸钱」是米浆水和纸钱。「香花纸马」是香、花和纸扎的马，祭正神用。
>
> *Note:* In the previous entry (LY.ILL.ZS.23) Juezi first told the great kinds apart by the five phases; this entry says the great kinds need not be pinned down to a particular god: he disagrees with the ancients pointing a Metal Pressure to Guan Gong, Yue Fei and the like, and holds that one should ask which god the patient suspects in his heart and then divine to test it. A 'Metal official' is in the letter a Metal Pressure line. 'Rice-water and paper money' is rice-water and paper money. 'Incense, flowers and paper horses' are incense, flowers and paper-made horses, used to sacrifice to orthodox gods.
