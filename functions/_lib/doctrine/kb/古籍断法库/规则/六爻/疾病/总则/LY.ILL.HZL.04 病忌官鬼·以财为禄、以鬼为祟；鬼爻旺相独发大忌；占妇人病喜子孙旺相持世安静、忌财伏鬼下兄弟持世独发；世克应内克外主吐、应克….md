# 病忌官鬼·以财为禄、以鬼为祟；鬼爻旺相独发大忌；占妇人病喜子孙旺相持世安静、忌财伏鬼下兄弟持世独发；世克应内克外主吐、应克世外克内主泻

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以财为禄，以鬼为祟；鬼爻旺相、独发，大忌。占妇人病，喜子孙旺相、持世、安静；忌财伏在鬼下、兄弟持世、兄弟独发。世克应、内克外，主吐；应克世、外克内，主泻。问答：妇人病喜子孙旺相、世安，是用财而以子孙辅助的道理；忌财伏鬼、兄弟持世，是用财伏在兄弟下的道理。内克外为什么主吐？内是腹、外是口；外克内主泻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Take Wealth as the salary and the ghost as the curse; the Pressure line strong and moving alone is a great taboo. In divining a woman's illness, it is welcome for Output to be strong, holding the Self and still; the taboos are Wealth hidden under the ghost, Peer holding the Self and Peer moving alone. The Self controlling the Other, the inner controlling the outer, means vomiting; the Other controlling the Self, the outer controlling the inner, means diarrhoea. Questions and answers: that in a woman's illness Output strong and the Self at peace is welcome is the principle of taking Wealth as the Subject with Output to help it; that Wealth hidden under the ghost and Peer holding the Self are taboo is the principle of the Subject Wealth hidden under Peer. Why does the inner controlling the outer mean vomiting? The inner is the belly and the outer is the mouth; the outer controlling the inner means diarrhoea.

## 《火珠林·病忌官鬼》原文

> [!quote]+ [[火珠林]] 第 449 行 · Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Illness: the Pressure line as the thing to avoid", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 又问：内克外何故主吐？ | 又问：内克外为什么主吐？ | Asked again: why does the inner controlling the outer mean vomiting? |
> | 曰：内为腹、外为口也，外克内主泻。 | 答：内是腹、外是口，外克内主泻。 | The answer: the inner is the belly and the outer is the mouth; the outer controlling the inner means diarrhoea. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「以财为禄，以鬼为祟」是这一节的主句：财爻是禄命（生机），鬼爻是祟（病）。「用财」是用神取财爻，占妇人病时以财爻为用神，所以要子孙辅助财、忌财伏在鬼或兄弟之下。「世克应、内克外主吐；应克世、外克内主泻」和断易天机孙膑歌的「外克内应克世有药频频泻肚肠、内克外世克应无鬼爻多呕吐伤」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.04）、洞林秘诀「我克他先呕逆、他克我泻为伤」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.16）、天玄赋的医药一节是同一条说法；这里给了理由：「内为腹、外为口」。「问：妇人病占喜子孙旺相、世安何也」里，「世安」是世爻安静。
>
> *Note:* 'Take Wealth as the salary and the ghost as the curse' is the main line of this section: the Wealth line is the salary (the life force) and the Pressure line is the curse (the illness). 'Taking Wealth as the Subject' is taking the Wealth line as the Subject, because in divining a woman's illness the Wealth line is the Subject, and so Output must help Wealth, and Wealth hidden under the ghost or Peer is taboo. 'The Self controlling the Other, the inner controlling the outer means vomiting; the Other controlling the Self, the outer controlling the inner means diarrhoea' is the same saying as Sun Bin's song in the Duanyi Tianji, 'the outer controlling the inner, the Other controlling the Self: purging again and again; the inner controlling the outer, the Self controlling the Other, with no Pressure line: mostly vomiting' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.04), the Dongling Mijue's 'if I control him there is retching first, if he controls me purging is the harm' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.16) and the section on medicine in the Tianxuan Fu; here the reason is given: 'the inner is the belly and the outer is the mouth'. In 'why is Output strong and the Self at peace welcome in a woman's illness', 'the Self at peace' is the Self line still.
