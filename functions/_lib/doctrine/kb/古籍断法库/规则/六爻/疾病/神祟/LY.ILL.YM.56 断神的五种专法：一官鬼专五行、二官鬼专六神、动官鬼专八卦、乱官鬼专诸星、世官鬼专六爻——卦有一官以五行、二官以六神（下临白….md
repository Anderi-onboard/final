# 断神的五种专法：一官鬼专五行、二官鬼专六神、动官鬼专八卦、乱官鬼专诸星、世官鬼专六爻——卦有一官以五行、二官以六神（下临白虎为武神、上临青龙为树神）、动鬼以八宫（鬼动乾天神、鬼动坤地祗）、乱动以何星临官

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里只有一个官鬼，专门用五行来断；有两个官鬼，专门用六神来断；官鬼发动的，专门用八卦；官鬼乱动的，专门用诸星；官鬼在世爻上的，专门用六爻。注说：卦里有一个官，用五行取象；有两个官，用六神取象，比如下面的官临白虎，就说是武神，上面的官临青龙，就说是树神；有动的鬼，用八宫取象，比如鬼在乾宫动，就说是天神，鬼在坤宫动，就说是地祇；卦爻乱动、或者其中有官在动，就看哪颗星临在官上来取象。这一节是专门断神的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With one Pressure line, go by the five phases alone; with two Pressure lines, go by the six spirits alone; with a moving Pressure line, go by the eight trigrams alone; with Pressure lines moving in disorder, go by the various stars alone; with the Pressure line on the Self, go by the six lines alone. The note says: where the hexagram has one Pressure line, take the image by the five phases; where it has two, take the image by the six spirits, so that if the lower one carries the White Tiger it is called a martial spirit, and if the upper one carries the Azure Dragon it is called a tree spirit; where it has a moving ghost, take the image by the eight palaces, so that a ghost moving in Qian is called a heavenly spirit and in Kun an earthly deity; where the lines move in disorder, or a Pressure line among them moves, take the image by whichever star sits on the Pressure line. This section is solely for judging the spirit.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条给出了后面六段（LY.ILL.YM.57 到 YM.62）的分工：五行（前面 LY.ILL.YM.44–YM.48 已讲）、六神（LY.ILL.YM.59）、八宫（LY.ILL.YM.57）、诸星（LY.ILL.YM.62）、六爻（LY.ILL.YM.58）；六亲（LY.ILL.YM.60）和十二支（LY.ILL.YM.61）在这一条的总目里没有列出，但下面紧接着还有。「乱官鬼」的「乱」是乱动、多爻纷动。原文「世官鬼而专六爻」只说官鬼在世爻上，后面讲六爻（LY.ILL.YM.58）时又分了「现在何爻」和「世鬼居何象」两种。
>
> *Note:* This entry sets out the division of labour among the following sections (LY.ILL.YM.57 to YM.62): the five phases (already treated in LY.ILL.YM.44 to YM.48), the six spirits (LY.ILL.YM.59), the eight palaces (LY.ILL.YM.57), the various stars (LY.ILL.YM.62) and the six lines (LY.ILL.YM.58); the six relations (LY.ILL.YM.60) and the twelve branches (LY.ILL.YM.61) are not listed in this summary but follow immediately. 'Disorderly Pressure' means Pressure lines moving in confusion, many lines stirring at once. The text's 'the Pressure line on the Self, go by the six lines alone' says only that the Pressure line is on the Self, and the later treatment of the six lines (LY.ILL.YM.58) distinguishes 'which line it appears on' from 'in what image the ghost on the Self lies'.
