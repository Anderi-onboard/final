# 决轻重存亡专察鬼爻、定金木水火可分症候：鬼爻旺日沉重、库日困迍、绝日轻可；鬼化鬼病进退或有变症或旧病发；鬼持世难除根、鬼带杀持世是瘵疾；日辰旺是暴病、月建旺是经月之疾、鬼无气临身是久病；卦无鬼病难安、鬼动伤身症候急；旺鬼不死则易愈、衰鬼不死则难疗

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要断定病的轻重存亡，专门看鬼爻；要定金木水火各是哪个方位，可以分出症候。注说：鬼爻旺的日子病沉重，入库的日子困顿，绝的日子轻可。鬼化鬼，病时进时退，或者有变症，或者旧病再发，或者症候驳杂。鬼持世，病难除根；鬼爻带杀持世，是瘵疾。卦中鬼爻遇日辰旺，是暴病；月建旺，是过了整月的病；鬼爻无气临身的，是久病，很难痊可。卦里没有鬼，病难安；鬼动伤身，症候急。凡占，鬼旺而不死，病容易好；鬼衰而不死，病难治。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To decide the light or heavy and the life or death, look only at the Pressure line; to fix the quarters of Metal, Wood, Water and Fire, one can distinguish the symptoms. The commentary says: on a day when the Pressure line is strong the illness is heavy, on its store day it is trapped and hard, on its Extinction day it is light and tolerable. The ghost changing into a ghost: the illness advances and retreats, or there is a change of symptoms, or an old illness flares, or the symptoms are mixed. The ghost holding the Self: the illness is hard to root out; the Pressure line carrying a killing and holding the Self is a wasting illness. If the Pressure line in the hexagram is strong on the Day, it is a sudden illness; if strong on the Month, an illness of a full month; a Pressure line without qi sitting on the body is a long illness that can hardly be cured at once. With no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to settle; the ghost moving and wounding the body, the symptoms are urgent. In every divination, a strong ghost that does not kill means an easy cure, a weak ghost that does not kill means an illness hard to treat.

> [!note] 按
> 赋文「专察鬼爻」是本章的总原则：病的轻重存亡看官鬼，症候看鬼的五行。注里分几个层次：①按日辰（鬼旺日重、入库日困、绝日轻）；②按鬼的变化（鬼化鬼是进退或变症，鬼持世难除根，带杀持世是瘵疾）；③按鬼旺在哪一层（日辰旺是暴病、月建旺是经月之病、无气临身是久病）；④卦无鬼病难安、鬼动伤身症候急；⑤旺鬼不死易愈、衰鬼不死难疗。这一段和黄金策的疾病章头几段（LY.ILL.HJC.01 到 HJC.14）是同一路，字句多有相同；卦无鬼「病难安」一处，黄金策旧注也有（见 LY.ILL.HJC.03）。「库日」是鬼爻入墓库的日子。「瘵疾」按拼音带出，是消耗性的病（古人常指肺痨）。「经月之疾」按「过了整月的病」译。「凡占，旺鬼不死则易愈；衰鬼不死其病难疗」的意思是：鬼旺时若没死，病好得快，因为鬼旺却不致命，说明用爻扛得住；鬼衰而没死，病反缠绵——原文没有解释，这里只译字面。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* The verse 'look only at the Pressure line' is this chapter's general principle: whether an illness is light or heavy, whether one lives or dies, is seen from the Pressure line, and the symptoms from the phase of the ghost. The commentary works on several levels: (1) by the day (strong Pressure day heavy, store day trapped, Extinction day light); (2) by what the ghost does (a ghost changing into a ghost is advance and retreat or a change of symptoms, a ghost holding the Self is hard to root out, with a killing and holding the Self it is a wasting illness); (3) by the level at which the ghost is strong (strong on the Day a sudden illness, strong on the Month an illness of a full month, without qi on the body a long illness); (4) with no ghost the illness is hard to settle, the ghost moving and wounding the body the symptoms are urgent; (5) a strong ghost that does not kill heals easily, a weak ghost that does not kill is hard to treat. This passage runs on the same road as the first paragraphs of the Huangjin Ce's chapter on illness (LY.ILL.HJC.01 to HJC.14) and its wording is often the same; 'with no ghost the illness is hard to settle' is also in the Huangjin Ce's old commentary (see LY.ILL.HJC.03). 'Store day' is the day on which the Pressure line enters its tomb or store. 'Zhai illness' is given by its pinyin and is a wasting illness (the ancients often meant consumption by it). 'An illness of a full month' is translated as 'an illness of a full month'. 'A strong ghost that does not kill means an easy cure; a weak ghost that does not kill means an illness hard to treat' means that if a strong ghost has not killed, the cure comes fast, because a ghost strong yet not fatal shows the Subject line can bear it, and if a weak ghost has not killed the illness on the contrary drags on — the original does not explain, and only the literal sense is translated. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
