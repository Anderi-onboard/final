# 火珠林(断易天机引)·占疾病何日愈：爻须旺相子扶身、龙蛇动处当轻利、狼虎过伤是重辰；己身自病何时瘥福德来临愈必真

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病问哪一天痊愈，爻要旺相，子孙扶身；青龙、螣蛇动的地方，当有轻快顺利；狼、虎过来伤，就是病重的时辰。重象不妨身带困顿，一交上恐怕气就离身；自己身上的病，什么时候好？福德来临的时候，一定真的痊愈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask on what day an illness is cured: the line must be strong and Output support the Body; where the Azure Dragon and the Coiling Snake move it should be light and smooth, and where the Wolf and the Tiger pass and injure is the heavy time. In a heavy image there is no harm if the body is worn, but on meeting it one must fear the breath leaves the body; for one's own illness, when will it be cured? When Output arrives the cure is certain.

## 《断易天机·占疾病第二十四·火珠林》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1721–1722 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Section 24, on illness: Huozhu Lin", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占疾病何日愈云： | 占疾病哪一天痊愈，歌诀说： | On which day an illness will be cured it says: |
> | 占病问于何日痊， | 占病问什么时候痊愈， | In divining illness, to ask on what day it is cured: |
> | 爻须旺相子扶身， | 爻要旺相，子孙扶身； | the line must be strong and Output support the Body; |
> | 龙蛇动处当轻利， | 青龙、螣蛇动的地方，当有轻快顺利； | where the Azure Dragon and the Coiling Snake move it should be light and smooth; |
> | 狼虎过伤是重辰。 | 狼、虎过来伤，就是病重的时辰。 | where the Wolf and the Tiger pass and injure is the heavy time. |
> | 重象不妨身带困， | 重象不妨身上带着困顿， | In a heavy image there is no harm if the body is worn, |
> | 交当必恐气离身， | 一交上，恐怕气就离开身体； | but on meeting it one must fear the breath leaves the body; |
> | 己身自病何时瘥， | 自己身上的病，什么时候好？ | for one's own illness, when will it be cured? |
> | 福德来临愈必真。 | 福德来临的时候，一定真的痊愈。 | when Output arrives the cure is certain. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「龙蛇」是青龙、螣蛇；「狼虎」的「狼」是什么，书里没有说，字面译作 Wolf，「虎」按白虎读。「重辰」是病重的时候。「重象不妨身带困，交当必恐气离身」两句读不确定，按字面译。「福德来临愈必真」是说子孙（福德）出现的月日就是病愈的日子；子孙管应期的说法，阐奥歌章和别的书也有（LY.ILL.CA.07 一带）。
>
> *Note:* The 'dragon and snake' are the Azure Dragon and the Coiling Snake; what the 'wolf' of 'wolf and tiger' is the book does not say, and it is rendered literally as Wolf, with 'tiger' read as the White Tiger. 'Chong chen' is the time when the illness is heavy. The two lines 'in a heavy image there is no harm if the body is worn; on meeting it one must fear the breath leaves the body' are not certain and are rendered literally. 'When Output arrives the cure is certain' says that the month or day on which Output (Fortune-and-Virtue) appears is the day of recovery; Output governing the timing is also said in the Chan'ao Gezhang and other books (around LY.ILL.CA.07).
