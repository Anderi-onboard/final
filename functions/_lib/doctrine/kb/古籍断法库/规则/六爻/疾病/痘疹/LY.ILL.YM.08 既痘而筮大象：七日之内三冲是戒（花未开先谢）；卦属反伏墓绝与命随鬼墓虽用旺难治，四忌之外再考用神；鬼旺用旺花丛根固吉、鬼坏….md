# 既痘而筮大象：七日之内三冲是戒（花未开先谢）；卦属反伏墓绝与命随鬼墓虽用旺难治，四忌之外再考用神；鬼旺用旺花丛根固吉、鬼坏用坏花悴根枯凶；生旺用神之日有成、冲鬼象之辰有变；感不感在鬼、害不害在用，鬼化鬼痘后痍、交加动瘄痘相参

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 已经出痘，再占整体的吉凶：七天之内，要戒备三冲，就是花还没开就先谢了。卦是反吟、伏吟、入墓、入绝的，和本命随鬼入墓的，即使用爻旺也难治；这四种忌讳之外，再考察用神。鬼旺而用爻旺，花丛茂盛、根牢固，是吉；鬼坏而用爻坏，花憔悴、根枯，是凶。生旺用神的日子是有成的时候，冲鬼象的时辰是有变的日期。大体上，感染不感染取决于鬼，有害没害取决于用爻；鬼变鬼的，痘后有疮痍；交错发动的，是瘄和痘掺杂。注说：这是专就用爻来说吉凶，不再论鬼的衰旺；如果用爻有生扶救助，鬼旺不过是花密；鬼爻被填实、被冲动，不过是要防变；鬼变鬼和用爻变鬼，不过是痘后余毒；鬼交错发动，或者有别的病夹杂，也没到大害的地步。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Once the pox has come and one divines the great image's fortune, within seven days the three clashes are the thing to guard against, meaning the flower withers before it has opened. A hexagram that is Reversed, Hidden, in a tomb or in Extinction, and one where the natal branch follows the ghost into a tomb, are hard to treat even if the Subject line is strong; apart from these four taboos, then examine the Subject. If the ghost is strong and the Subject line strong, the flowers are luxuriant and the roots firm, which is auspicious; if the ghost is spoiled and the Subject line spoiled, the flowers droop and the roots wither, which is ominous. The day that strengthens the Subject is the time of completion, the hour that clashes the ghost image the time of change. In general whether the child catches it depends on the ghost, whether it does harm on the Subject line; a ghost changing into a ghost means sores after the pox; lines moving in tangles mean measles and pox mixed together. The note says: this speaks of fortune purely by the Subject line and no longer discusses whether the ghost is strong or weak; if the Subject line has generation, support and rescue, a strong ghost only means dense flowers; if the Pressure line is filled or stirred by a clash, it only means guarding against change; a ghost changing into a ghost, or the Subject line changing into a ghost, only means residual poison after the pox; ghosts moving in tangles, or another illness riding along, does not amount to great harm.

> [!note] 按
> 「四忌」是卦反（反吟）、卦伏（伏吟）、卦墓、卦绝，再加「命随鬼墓」这一项，书里合称「反伏墓绝与命随鬼墓」；「三冲」按易冒的用法是卦冲的三种情形。「瘄」是麻疹。注文里「痘后痍」「痘后余毒」都是痘好了以后留下的疮疡和余毒。这一条把吉凶全归到用爻（用神），与增删卜易讲痘的吉凶全在子孙爻（LY.ILL.ZS.21）相近，只是易冒的用神是所占那个人的爻，不专指子孙。
>
> *Note:* The 'four taboos' are the hexagram Reversed (Reversed Chant), Hidden (Hidden Chant), in a tomb and in Extinction, and the book adds 'the natal branch following the ghost into a tomb', naming them together as 'Reversed, Hidden, tomb, Extinction, and the natal branch following the ghost into a tomb'; the 'three clashes' in the Yi Mao's usage are the three cases of a hexagram in clash. 'Cu' is measles. 'Sores after the pox' and 'residual poison after the pox' are the sores and leftover poison after the pox has healed. This entry puts fortune wholly in the Subject line, close to the Zengshan Buyi's putting the fortune of pox wholly in the Output line (LY.ILL.ZS.21), except that the Yi Mao's Subject is the line of the person divined and not specifically Output.
