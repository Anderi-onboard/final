# 水带财兴大忌鱼鲜生冷：财是饮食，动则生助鬼爻反为所害；财属水忌鱼鲜生冷，木忌动风之物，火忌炙煿热物，金忌坚硬咸物，土忌油腻滑物；财不动不可妄言；又忌鬼爻生肖之物

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻管饮食，靠它养生，但它一动就会去生助鬼爻，反而被它所害。财若属水，一定忌鱼鲜和生冷的东西，药才见效；属木，忌动风的东西；属火，忌烤炒的热东西；属金，忌坚硬和咸的东西；属土，忌油腻和滑的东西。财爻不动，不可乱说。又要忌鬼爻所属生肖的东西，比如丑忌牛，酉忌鸡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wealth line governs food and drink, on which life is sustained, but when it moves it generates and helps the Pressure line and one is harmed by it instead. If Wealth is Water, fish, fresh seafood and raw or cold things must be avoided, and only then does the medicine work; if Wood, avoid things that stir wind; if Fire, roasted, fried and hot things; if Metal, hard and salty things; if Earth, greasy and slippery things. If Wealth does not move, one may not speak at random. Also avoid what belongs to the zodiac animal of the Pressure line: for example Chou avoids the ox and You avoids the fowl.

> [!note] 按
> 「动风之物」是中医说的容易引动风病的食物（引起发痒、头晕、抽动一类）。旧注「忌值火，忌炙煿热物」多出一个「忌」字，按上下文是「值火，忌炙煿热物」；王洪绪写「炙炊」，旧注写「炙煿」，都是烤炒。这一条和 LY.ILL.HJC.38 里财管饮食、财动生鬼是一个道理。
>
> *Note:* 'Things that stir wind' are foods that Chinese medicine holds apt to stir up wind illnesses (itching, dizziness, twitching and the like). The old commentary's 'avoid, on Fire, avoid roasted ...' has one 'avoid' too many and from the context means 'on Fire, avoid roasted and fried hot things'; Wang Hongxu writes 'roasted and cooked' where the old commentary writes 'roasted and fried', both being roasting and frying. The entry gives the same reason as LY.ILL.HJC.38: Wealth governs food, and Wealth moving generates Pressure.
