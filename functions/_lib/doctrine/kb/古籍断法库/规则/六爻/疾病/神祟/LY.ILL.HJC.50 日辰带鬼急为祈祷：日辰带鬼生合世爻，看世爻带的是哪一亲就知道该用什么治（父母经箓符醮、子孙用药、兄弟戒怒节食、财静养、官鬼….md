# 日辰带鬼急为祈祷：日辰带鬼生合世爻，看世爻带的是哪一亲就知道该用什么治（父母经箓符醮、子孙用药、兄弟戒怒节食、财静养、官鬼祈祷）；认哪路神鬼，旧注按日辰地支、王洪绪按生合的六神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰带着鬼、来生合世爻，就该祈祷。看世爻带的是哪一亲，就知道该用什么办法治：带父母，要靠经、箓、符、醮来保扶；带子孙，要用药调理；带兄弟，要戒暴怒、节制饮食；带财爻，要好好休养；带官鬼，要祈祷鬼神。要知道该求哪一路神鬼，两家的办法不同，见分歧。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Day carries Pressure and generates or combines with the Self line, one should pray. What the Self line carries shows the cure: Resource, scriptures, registers, talismans and rites; Output, medicine; Peer, curbing temper and moderating food; Wealth, good rest; Pressure, prayer to the ghosts and spirits. Which spirit or ghost to seek is found differently by the two commentaries (see the dispute).

## 《黄金策·病体》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6940 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日辰带鬼，急为祈祷保无虞。 | 日辰上带着鬼，赶紧祈祷，保证没有忧患。 | The Day carrying Pressure: hurry to pray, and there will be nothing to fear. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6941–6942 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日辰带鬼，生合世爻，世爻看带何等神，便知得何者为治。 | 日辰带着鬼，来生、合世爻，看世爻带的是哪一类神，就知道该用什么办法治。 | When the Day carries Pressure and generates or combines with the Self line, look at what sort of spirit the Self line carries, and one knows what is the cure. |
> | 如带父母，必须经箓符醮保扶则愈。 | 如果带父母，一定要靠经、箓、符、醮来保扶才好。 | If it carries Resource, it will heal only with scriptures, registers, talismans and jiao offerings to protect and sustain. |
> | 如带子孙，必须用药调理。 | 如果带子孙，一定要用药调理。 | If it carries Output, medicine is needed to regulate it. |
> | 如带兄弟，必须戒暴怒，节饮食。 | 如果带兄弟，一定要戒暴怒，节制饮食。 | If it carries Peer, he must give up violent anger and moderate his food and drink. |
> | 如带财爻，必须好好将息。 | 如果带财爻，一定要好好休养。 | If it carries Wealth, he must take good rest. |
> | 带官鬼，祈祷鬼神。 | 带官鬼，就祈祷鬼神。 | If it carries Pressure, he should pray to the ghosts and spirits. |
> | 世持鬼，亦宜先祷。 | 世爻持着鬼，也宜先祈祷。 | When the Self line itself holds Pressure, one should also pray first. |
> | 但世鬼是先曾许下，日辰鬼是未曾许下。 | 不过世爻上的鬼是以前许过愿的，日辰上的鬼是没有许过愿的。 | But Pressure on the Self line is a vow made before, and Pressure on the Day is a vow not yet made. |
> | 要知求何神鬼，有福力，以日辰推之。 | 要知道该求哪一路神鬼才有福力，用日辰来推。 | To know which spirit or ghost to beg, so that there is blessing and power, reason it from the Day. |
> | 如子为北斗或北阴； | 比如子是北斗，或者北阴； | For example, Zi is the Northern Dipper or the North Yin; |
> | 亥或溺死鬼，察其为河伯，或划龙船。 | 亥是溺死鬼之类，要察看是河伯，还是划龙船的。 | Hai is a drowned ghost or the like: look into whether it is the River Earl or the rowing of dragon boats. |
> | 盖亥水中有甲木，故也。 | 因为亥水里藏着甲木。 | For within the Hai Water there is Jia Wood. |
> | 余仿此。 | 其余照这个类推。 | The rest follow this. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2398 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如日辰带官鬼生合世爻或用爻者，当为祈祷，看其生合者是何等神。 | 如果日辰带着官鬼、来生合世爻或者用爻，就该去祈祷，看生合它的是哪一路神。 | If the Day carries Pressure and generates or combines with the Self or Subject line, one should pray, and look at what sort of spirit it is that generates or combines with it. |
> | 如生合青龙父母，是花幡香愿，勾陈则土地城隍，朱雀则香灯口愿，螣蛇则百怪惊神，白虎则伤司五道，玄武则玄帝北阴。 | 如果生合它的是青龙、父母，是花幡香愿；勾陈是土地、城隍；朱雀是香灯口愿；螣蛇是百怪惊神；白虎是伤司、五道；玄武是玄帝、北阴。 | If what generates or combines with it is the Azure Dragon and Resource, it is a vow of flower banners and incense; Hook Earth means the Earth God and the City God; the Vermilion Bird a vow of incense and lamps spoken aloud; the Coiling Snake the hundred oddities and startled spirits; the White Tiger the Office of Injury and the Five Ways; the Dark Tortoise the Dark Emperor and the North Yin. |
> | 阳象阳爻是神，阴象阴爻是鬼。 | 阳的象、阳的爻是神，阴的象、阴的爻是鬼。 | A yang image on a yang line is a god; a yin image on a yin line is a ghost. |
> | 今陈大略，后有《鬼神章》尽细，阅之照断方是，切不可妄断！ | 现在只讲个大概，后面有《鬼神章》讲得详尽，读了照着断才对，千万不可乱断！ | Only the outline is set out now; the later Chapter on Ghosts and Spirits is fully detailed, and one should read it and judge by it; on no account judge at random! |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 怎样认该求哪一路神鬼：旧注用日辰的地支推（子是北斗或北阴，亥是溺死鬼，要察看是河伯、还是划龙船的），再按世爻带的六亲定治法；王洪绪看生合世爻或用爻的那一爻带的六神（青龙、勾陈、朱雀、螣蛇、白虎、玄武各是一路），并且说阳象阳爻是神、阴象阴爻是鬼。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to tell which spirit or ghost to seek: the old commentary reasons from the branch of the Day (Zi is the Northern Dipper or the North Yin, Hai a drowned ghost, to be looked into as the River Earl or dragon-boat rowing) and fixes the cure by the relation the Self line carries; Wang Hongxu looks at the six spirits carried by the line that generates or combines with the Self or Subject line (the Azure Dragon, Hook Earth, Vermilion Bird, Coiling Snake, White Tiger and Dark Tortoise each a different sort), and says that a yang image on a yang line is a god and a yin image on a yin line a ghost.

> [!note] 按
> 「箓」是道士画的符箓，「醮」是道士设坛祭神的仪式，「保扶」是保护、扶助。「花幡香愿」「香灯口愿」是向神许过愿、还愿时献的花幡、香灯。「伤司」「五道」是主伤亡、管冥途的神，「玄帝」是玄天上帝，「北阴」是北方的阴神。亥里藏着壬水和甲木，所以旧注说除了河伯（水），还要看有没有划龙船的（木船）。王洪绪说的《鬼神章》是本书后面专讲鬼神的一章。
>
> *Note:* A 'lu' is a register or talisman drawn by a Daoist, a 'jiao' a rite in which a Daoist sets up an altar and offers to the spirits, and 'protect and sustain' is to protect and support. 'A vow of flower banners and incense' and 'a vow of incense and lamps spoken aloud' are vows made to a god and the flower banners, incense and lamps offered in fulfilling them. 'The Office of Injury' and 'the Five Ways' are gods over injury and death and the road to the underworld, the 'Dark Emperor' is the Supreme Emperor of the Dark Heaven, and the 'North Yin' is the yin spirit of the north. Hai holds Ren Water and Jia Wood, so the old commentary says that besides the River Earl (Water) one must see whether there is dragon-boat rowing (a wooden boat). The Chapter on Ghosts and Spirits that Wang Hongxu mentions is the chapter later in the book that treats ghosts and spirits alone.
