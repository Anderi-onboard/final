# 六亲变化歌·疾病：子孙化鬼忧病产；官鬼化财占病凶

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六亲变化歌里讲病的两句：子孙爻动，变出官鬼，担忧疾病和生产；官鬼爻动，变出财爻，占病主凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The two lines on illness in the verse on what each relation turns into: when the Output line moves and turns into Pressure, worry over illness and childbirth; when the Pressure line moves and turns into Wealth, divining illness is ominous.

> | 化父田蚕旺， | 化出父母，田地蚕桑兴旺； | turning into Resource, fields and silkworms flourish; |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 歌里每一句的格式是「甲化乙」：甲爻发动，变出乙爻（动是事情的开头，变是事情的结局）。全诀按「父母化」「子孙化」「官化」「妻财化」「兄弟化」一段一段排，这里只收有病的两段，整段带着。「化鬼忧病产」的「产」是生产。
>
> *Note:* Every line of the verse has the form 'A turns into B': line A moves and changes into line B (the moving line is the start of the matter, the changed line its end). The whole verse runs in stanzas on Resource, Output, Pressure, Wealth and Peer turning; only the two stanzas with illness in them are taken, each complete. In 'turning into Pressure, worry over illness and childbirth' the word rendered 'childbirth' is giving birth.
