# 孙膑断疾病歌(四)：问病须分六亲；父母空亡防父母、财爻无气损妻身、子孙化鬼须遭死、兄弟煞临定不贞

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问病一定要分清六亲，吉凶要弄分明。父母爻空亡，要防父母；财爻无气，损伤妻子的身体；子孙化为鬼，必定遭死；兄弟带煞临上，一定不贞。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking after illness be sure to tell the six relations apart and sort out good and ill clearly. Resource Void: guard the parents; the Wealth line without qi harms the wife's person; Output changing into a ghost will meet death; a Peer with a killing sitting on it is surely not firm.

> [!note] 按
> 四句是四种人的病：占父母病看父母爻、占妻病看财爻、占子女病看子孙爻、占兄弟病看兄弟爻，和天玄赋的用爻表（LY.ILL.TXF.01）一致。「定不贞」的「贞」按字面译，意思大概是不吉，书里没有说明。
>
> *Note:* The four lines are about four kinds of person: divining a parent's illness look at the Resource line, a wife's the Wealth line, a child's the Output line and a brother's the Peer line, in agreement with the Tianxuan Fu's table of the Subject line (LY.ILL.TXF.01). The 'firm' of 'surely not firm' (zhen) is rendered literally; it probably means not auspicious, and the book does not explain it.
