# 医药·推医是何人：子孙为用，五行定方位姓氏——金宜西方金旁姓氏、木宜东方草木旁、水宜北方点水旁、火宜南方日火旁、土宜土旁及丹散医人；子孙带贵人宜有官职人、带华盖宜僧道

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推断请来的医生是什么样的人，拿子孙做用：子孙属金，适合西方的、姓氏带金字旁的人；属木，适合东方的、姓氏带草头或木字旁的人；属水，适合北方的、姓氏带三点水的人；属火，适合南方的、姓氏带日字旁或火字旁的人；属土，适合姓氏带土字旁的，以及用丹药散剂的医生。子孙带贵人，适合有官职的人；子孙带华盖，适合僧人道士。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To work out what kind of person the doctor is, Output is the Subject: if Output is Metal, it suits the west, a person whose surname has the metal radical; if Wood, the east, a surname with the grass or wood radical; if Water, the north, a surname with the water radical; if Fire, the south, a surname with the sun or fire radical; if Earth, a surname with the earth radical, and a doctor who works with cinnabar and powders. If Output carries the Noble, a person holding an official post; if Output carries the Canopy, a monk or Daoist.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和增删卜易讲的「延医于子孙之方，姓名按五行：水点水、火象火、木草头木旁、土有土」（LY.ILL.ZS.34）几乎一样，是同一个说法两本书都有。「华盖」是一颗神煞，主僧道孤高；「贵人」也是神煞，主有人扶助。
>
> *Note:* This entry is almost the same as the Zengshan Buyi's 'calling the doctor in the direction of Output, the surname by the element: Water with the water radical, Fire with the fire image, Wood with the grass or wood radical, Earth with earth' (LY.ILL.ZS.34); the two books have the same saying. The 'Canopy' (Huagai) is a spirit-star for monks and Daoists and the solitary and aloof; the 'Noble' is also a spirit-star, for help from others.
