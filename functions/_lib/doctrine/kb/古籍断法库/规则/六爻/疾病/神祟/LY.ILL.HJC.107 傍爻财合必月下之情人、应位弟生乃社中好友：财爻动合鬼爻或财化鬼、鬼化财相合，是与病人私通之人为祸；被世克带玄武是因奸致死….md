# 傍爻财合必月下之情人、应位弟生乃社中好友：财爻动合鬼爻或财化鬼、鬼化财相合，是与病人私通之人为祸；被世克带玄武是因奸致死之鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 旁边的财爻来合鬼，一定是月下私会的情人；应爻位上的兄弟来生它，是同社的好友。财爻发动来合鬼爻，或者财变鬼、鬼变财，自己互相合在一起的，一定是和病人私通的人在作祸；如果被世爻克，又带玄武，是因为通奸而死的鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Wealth line beside it combining with the ghost is surely a lover met under the moon; a Peer on the Other line generating it is a good friend of the village society. If a Wealth line moves and combines with the Pressure line, or Wealth changes into Pressure or Pressure into Wealth so that they combine with each other, it is surely a person who had an illicit affair with the patient who is making trouble; if it is controlled by the Self line and carries the Dark Tortoise, it is the ghost of one who died because of adultery.

> [!note] 按
> 经文两句，两家注都只讲了前一句（财合）；「应位弟生，乃社中之好友」这一句旧注和王洪绪都没有再解释。旧注比王洪绪多「被世克而带玄武，是因奸致死之鬼」一句。这条判断是古书里说的，写得很肯定（「必」），本库照抄，不替它加验证。
>
> *Note:* Of the verse's two lines, both commentaries explain only the first (Wealth combining); neither explains 'the Peer on the Other line generating it is a good friend of the society'. The old commentary adds 'if controlled by the Self line and carrying the Dark Tortoise, it is the ghost of one who died because of adultery', which Wang Hongxu lacks. The classic states this judgment with certainty ('surely'); the library copies it without adding any verification.
