# 占鬼神·五行鬼：金木横死、土时疫、火劳血、水落水；八纯卦：艮五圣、震天神、巽木神、离火神、乾功德、坤家将、坎落水、兑口愿

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 五行的鬼：金、木是横死，土是时疫，火是劳血，水是落水。八个纯卦：艮是五圣，震是天神，巽是木神，离是火神，乾是功德，坤是家将，坎是落水，兑是口愿。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ghosts of the five phases: Metal and Wood are violent death, Earth an epidemic, Fire consumption with spitting blood, Water drowning. The eight pure hexagrams: Gen is the Five Sages, Zhen the heavenly spirit, Xun the tree spirit, Li the fire spirit, Qian merit, Kun the household generals, Kan drowning, Dui vows.

> [!note] 按
> 「五行鬼」是鬼爻属哪一行，就是哪一种死者：金木是横死（意外而死）、土是时疫（死于瘟疫）、火是劳血（劳伤吐血）、水是落水（淹死）。「八纯卦」是鬼落在八个纯卦里对应的神：艮五圣、震天神、巽木神、离火神、乾功德、坤家将、坎落水、兑口愿。「功德」是为功德而许的斋供，「家将」是家里供的将军神，「口愿」是口头许下的愿，这三处原文没有解释，是字面加常识的译法。五行鬼的分法和易林补遗的「金鬼配六兽」等（LY.ILL.EL.30 到 EL.34）、易冒的五行值鬼是同一类，各家说法大同小异：金鬼易林补遗说是「刀下之魂、喘咳横亡之鬼」，这里说是横死；火鬼易林补遗是「毒疮痨之魂、带血焚烧之鬼」，这里说是劳血；水鬼两处都是落水一类。
>
> *Note:* 'The ghosts of the five phases' says that whichever phase the ghost line belongs to is the kind of dead person: Metal and Wood a violent death (dying by accident), Earth an epidemic (dying of a plague), Fire consumption (wasting and spitting blood), Water drowning. 'The eight pure hexagrams' gives the spirit matched to a ghost falling in each of the eight pure hexagrams: Gen the Five Sages, Zhen the heavenly spirit, Xun the tree spirit, Li the fire spirit, Qian merit, Kun the household generals, Kan drowning, Dui vows. 'Merit' is a fast offering vowed for merit, the 'household generals' are the general spirits worshipped in the house, and 'vows' are vows spoken aloud; the original does not explain these three, and they are rendered literally with common sense. This way of dividing the ghosts by five phases is of the same kind as the Yilin Buyi's 'a Metal ghost matched to the six beasts' and so on (LY.ILL.EL.30 to EL.34) and the Yi Mao's ghost by five phases, and the books are much alike: for the Metal ghost the Yilin Buyi says 'the soul of one killed by a blade, the ghost of one who died of coughing and panting', where this says violent death; for the Fire ghost the Yilin Buyi says 'the soul of a poisonous sore or consumption, the ghost of burning with blood', where this says consumption with blood; for the Water ghost both say drowning.
