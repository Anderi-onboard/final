# 主衰得助重亦何妨：用爻不宜太旺也不宜太弱，弱则体虚力怯难痊；得日辰动爻生合扶助最吉，纵是十分重病亦不至死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻虽然不宜太旺，也不宜太弱。太弱，病人就体虚力怯，一时难以痊愈。如果得到日辰、动爻来生、合、扶助，最吉，就算是十分重的病，也不至于死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Though the Subject line should not be too strong, it should not be too weak either. If weak, the patient is frail and timid in strength and cannot quickly be healed. If it gets generation, combination and support from the Day and moving lines it is most lucky: even a most heavy illness will not lead to death.

> [!note] 按
> 旧注这里明说「虽不宜太旺，亦不宜太弱」，和 LY.ILL.HJC.36 的「太刚则折」是一对：两头都不行，要的是有气而不过头。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says outright here 'though it should not be too strong, it should not be too weak', which is a pair with the 'too rigid, it snaps' of LY.ILL.HJC.36: neither extreme will do, and what is wanted is force without excess.
