# 以卦身推是什么人的鬼：鬼克身是冤家债主、身克鬼是妻妾阴人，我生它是卑幼儿童僧道、它生我是祖宗尊长父母，无生合克刑是兄弟朋友

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 拿卦身来推是什么人的鬼：鬼克卦身，是冤家债主的鬼，一定难以退去，病也不容易好；卦身克鬼，是妻妾一类女人的鬼；卦身生鬼，是晚辈、小孩、僧人道士的鬼（子孙）；鬼生卦身，是祖宗、长辈、父母的鬼（父母）；两者比和，是兄弟朋友的鬼，阴爻上是妯娌姊妹的鬼；没有生、合、克、刑，也一定是兄弟朋友。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Work out whose ghost it is by the Body. A ghost that controls the Body is the ghost of an enemy or creditor, hard to drive off, and the illness will not easily settle; the Body controlling the ghost is the ghost of a wife, concubine or woman; the Body generating the ghost is the ghost of a junior, child, monk or Daoist (Output); a ghost generating the Body is the ghost of an ancestor, elder or parent (Resource); where the two are in harmony it is the ghost of a brother or friend (Peer), and on a yin line of a sister-in-law or sister; with no generating, combining, controlling or punishing, it is surely a brother or friend.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3389–3391 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 此以卦身推之。 | 这是按卦身来推。 | This is worked out by the Body. |
> | 鬼生卦身为父母，卦身生鬼为子孙，鬼克卦身为冤仇，卦身克鬼为妻妾，二者比合为兄弟朋友姊妹之鬼。 | 鬼生卦身的是父母，卦身生鬼的是子孙，鬼克卦身的是冤仇，卦身克鬼的是妻妾，两者比合的是兄弟朋友姊妹的鬼。 | A ghost that generates the Body is a parent (Resource), the Body generating the ghost is a descendant (Output), a ghost controlling the Body is an enemy, the Body controlling the ghost is a wife or concubine, and where the two are in harmony it is the ghost of a brother (Peer), friend or sister. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文只说「身」，旧注和王洪绪都说明这是「卦身」。旧注多出的四句（阴爻是妯娌姊妹、子孙是小口卑幼、父母是尊长祖宗、财爻是妻妾阴人）和最后「惟鬼克身…病亦未易安」一句王洪绪没有。月卦身法本身，增删卜易的野鹤认为不验（见 LY.ILL.ZS.01）；他的鬼神章（LY.ILL.ZS.23–25）也没有用卦身去认是谁的鬼，而是按鬼爻的五行和旺衰认。
>
> *Note:* The verse says only 'the Body', and both the old commentary and Wang Hongxu make clear that it is the hexagram Body. The four sentences the old commentary adds (a yin line is a sister-in-law or sister, Output is the ghost of the young, Resource of elders and ancestors, the Wealth line of a wife, concubine or woman) and its last 'only a ghost that controls the Body... the illness will not easily settle' Wang Hongxu lacks. The month-Body method itself the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi held unreliable (see LY.ILL.ZS.01); his chapter on ghosts and spirits (LY.ILL.ZS.23-25) does not use the Body to tell whose ghost it is either, but goes by the element and strength of the Pressure line.
