# 搜决神鬼章结语：细查何象临官便议何神作祟；请祷则福无不至、祈禳则祸无不消；作此篇为却灾度命，恐人妄断而好杀费财，万不可轻言，须将六兽配五行细论方判无私

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 仔细查看是哪个象临在官鬼上，才议论是哪位神在作祟，这样请祷就没有福不到的，祈禳就没有祸不消的。现在作这一篇，恳切是为了却灾、度人性命，又恐怕人乱判断，常常担心人好杀生而白费钱财，万万不可轻易说，一样一样都要细细论，把六兽端正地配上五行来决断诸神，终究没有一个错误，详审它的确实，才判得没有私心。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Examine carefully which image sits on the Pressure line, and only then discuss which god is haunting; so prayer brings blessing everywhere and exorcism removes calamity everywhere. This chapter is written earnestly to ward off calamity and save lives, and for fear that people judge at random, always worrying that they are fond of killing and waste money, one must by no means speak lightly; each point must be discussed in detail, matching the six beasts correctly with the five phases to decide the spirits, so that in the end there is not one error, and its certainty is examined carefully, and only then is the judgment without partiality.

> [!warning] 打架
> 要不要指到某一位神、祭祀要不要杀牲：易林补遗在本章前面列了大量神名，这里的收束是「恐人妄断，恒忧好杀而费财，万不可轻言」，一样一样细论、把六兽配五行才可判，而它随后的「解禳通用法」（LY.ILL.EL.54）更是劝人不要杀牲、多用斋戒念经；易冒也在结尾说「必实指鬼神而泥之则诬矣」，并且「祭祀莫善于解禳，解禳莫善于修身」（LY.ILL.YM.63、YM.64）；增删卜易的李我平批评指定神名（LY.ILL.ZS.25），野鹤反对杀牲救命（LY.ILL.ZS.25）。这几家在收束上意见接近。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether to pin it to a particular god, and whether sacrifice should involve slaughter: the Yilin Buyi lists a great many names of gods earlier in the chapter, but its closing here is 'fearing people will judge at random, always worrying that they love killing and waste money, one must by no means speak lightly', so that each point must be discussed in detail and the six beasts matched with the five phases before one may judge, and its following 'general methods of exorcism' (LY.ILL.EL.54) go further and urge people not to slaughter but to use fasting and the recitation of scriptures; the Yi Mao also closes with 'to point insistently at a particular ghost or spirit and cling to it is to make a false claim' and 'of sacrifice none is better than exorcism, and of exorcism none is better than cultivating oneself' (LY.ILL.YM.63, YM.64); Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi criticises pinning names (LY.ILL.ZS.25), and the Old Man of Yehe opposes slaughter to save a life (LY.ILL.ZS.25). These books are close in their closing views.

> [!note] 按
> 这一句的文字有些含混：「今作此篇切为却灾而度命」的「切」是恳切；「恒忧好杀而费财」是担心世人因为鬼神之说而宰杀牲畜、花费家财；「将六兽端配五行决诸神」是说要把六神和五行按次序配起来去断哪位神，不可随意。「终无一误详其的确，方判无私」的「无私」是不偏私、不受情面左右。
>
> *Note:* The wording of this sentence is somewhat loose: the 'qie' in 'written earnestly (qie) to ward off calamity and save lives' is earnestness; 'always worrying that they love killing and waste money' is the worry that, because of talk of ghosts and spirits, people will slaughter animals and spend their household wealth; 'matching the six beasts correctly with the five phases to decide the spirits' means matching the six spirits with the five phases in order to decide which god, not at will. 'Without partiality' in 'only then is the judgment without partiality' means impartial and not swayed by favour.
