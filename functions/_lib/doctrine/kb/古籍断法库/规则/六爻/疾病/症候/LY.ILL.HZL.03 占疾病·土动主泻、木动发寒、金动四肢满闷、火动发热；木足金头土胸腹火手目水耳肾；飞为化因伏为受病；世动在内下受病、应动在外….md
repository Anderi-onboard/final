# 占疾病·土动主泻、木动发寒、金动四肢满闷、火动发热；木足金头土胸腹火手目水耳肾；飞为化因伏为受病；世动在内下受病、应动在外上受病、间爻动胸膈病；《易镜》长男受病宜纯震不摇；大忌申酉持世木爻独发(申丧车酉丧服木棺椁)

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 土动主泻，木动发寒，金动主四肢或满闷，火动发热。木主足，金主头，土主胸腹，火主手目，水主耳肾。飞伏都旺相时，飞是化出病的原因，以伏为受病的地方。又：世是动爻，在内则下部受病；应是动爻，在外则上部受病；间爻动，主胸膈的病。《易镜》说：长男受病，宜纯震卦不动；小女染病，宜兑卦不动。大忌申、酉持世，木爻独发，因为申是丧车，酉是丧服，木是棺椁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Earth moving means diarrhoea, Wood moving means chills, Metal moving means the four limbs or oppression, Fire moving means fever. Wood governs the feet, Metal the head, Earth the chest and belly, Fire the hands and eyes, Water the ears and kidneys. When the flying and the hidden are both strong, the flying is the cause the illness turns on and the hidden is where the illness is received. Again, the Self as the moving line, inside, the lower part receives the illness; the Other as the moving line, outside, the upper part receives it; a between line moving means an illness of the chest and diaphragm. The Yijing says: for a person of the eldest son to fall ill it is fitting that the pure Zhen hexagram does not move, and for the youngest daughter to be infected that the Dui hexagram does not move. It is greatly feared that Shen and You hold the Self and a Wood line moves alone, for Shen is the mourning cart, You the mourning dress and Wood the coffin.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段和海底眼的（LY.ILL.HDY.05）几乎一字不差：「土动主吐水动主泻……木主足金主头土主胸腹火主手水主脾肾，飞伏俱旺相飞为起因……」，以及「易镜云：且如长男受病，宜纯震之不摇，小女染病，则兑卦之不动」。两本书里哪一本抄哪一本，书里没有说。这里和海底眼有几处字不同：火珠林是「土动主泻」、海底眼是「土动主吐」；火珠林是「火主手目」、海底眼是「火主手」；火珠林是「水主耳肾」、海底眼是「水主脾肾」；火珠林是「飞为化因，以伏为受病」、海底眼是「飞为起因，以代为受病」。「化因」「起因」都按「病由之而起的原因」译。「《易镜》」是一部书名，这里译作 Yijing，不是《周易》。「长男」是震，「少女」（原文「小女」）是兑。申为丧车、酉为丧服、木为棺椁，是拿字面取象。
>
> *Note:* This passage is almost word for word the same as the Haidi Yan's (LY.ILL.HDY.05): 'Earth moving means vomiting, Water moving means diarrhoea ... Wood governs the feet, Metal the head, Earth the chest and belly, Fire the hands, Water the spleen and kidneys; the flying and the hidden both strong, the flying is the origin ...', and 'the Yijing says: for a person of the eldest son to fall ill it is fitting that the pure Zhen hexagram does not move; for the youngest daughter to be infected that the Dui hexagram does not move'. Which of the two books copied the other, the books do not say. There are a few differences in characters from the Haidi Yan: the Huozhu Lin has 'Earth moving means diarrhoea' where the Haidi Yan has 'Earth moving means vomiting'; 'Fire the hands and eyes' where the Haidi Yan has 'Fire the hands'; 'Water the ears and kidneys' where the Haidi Yan has 'Water the spleen and kidneys'; and 'the flying is the cause, the hidden is where the illness is received' where the Haidi Yan has 'the flying is the origin, the substitute is where the illness is received'. Both 'hua yin' and 'qi yin' are rendered 'the cause the illness arises from'. 'Yijing' is the title of a book, here rendered Yijing, and is not the Zhouyi. The eldest son is Zhen and the youngest daughter (in the original 'the small daughter') is Dui. Shen as the mourning cart, You as the mourning dress and Wood as the coffin are images taken from the wording.
