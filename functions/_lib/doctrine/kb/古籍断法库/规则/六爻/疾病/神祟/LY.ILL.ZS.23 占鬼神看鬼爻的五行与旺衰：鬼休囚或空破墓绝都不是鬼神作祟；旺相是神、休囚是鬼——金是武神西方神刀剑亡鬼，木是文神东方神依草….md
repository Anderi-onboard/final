# 占鬼神看鬼爻的五行与旺衰：鬼休囚或空破墓绝都不是鬼神作祟；旺相是神、休囚是鬼——金是武神西方神/刀剑亡鬼，木是文神东方神/依草附木之妖或刑杖悬梁之鬼，水是河海北方神/水死之鬼，火是火神南方神雷公电母窖灶神/汤火焚烧之鬼，土是土神中央神山川社稷神/墙倒屋塌土死之鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占鬼神，卦里的鬼要是休囚，或者空、破、墓、绝，都不是鬼神在作祟。鬼爻旺相是神，休囚衰弱是鬼：临金，旺相是武神和西方的神，衰弱是死于刀剑的鬼；临木，旺相是文神和东方的神，衰弱是依草附木的妖、或被杖责悬梁而死的鬼；临水，旺相是河海和北方的神，衰弱是死在池井江湖里的鬼；临火，旺相是火神和南方的神、雷公电母、窖灶的神，衰弱是被汤火烧死的鬼；临土，旺相是土神和中央的神、掌管山川社稷的神，衰弱是墙倒屋塌压死的鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining ghosts and spirits, if the Pressure line in the hexagram is resting or trapped, or Void, broken, in a tomb or extinct, it is not the harm of ghosts or spirits. A strong Pressure line is a god and a weak one a ghost: on Metal, if strong a martial god and a god of the west, if weak a ghost that died by sword or blade; on Wood, if strong a civil god and a god of the east, if weak a goblin clinging to grass and trees or a ghost that died by the rod or by hanging from a beam; on Water, if strong a god of river and sea and of the north, if weak a ghost that drowned in a pond, well, river or lake; on Fire, if strong the god of fire and a god of the south, the Duke of Thunder and Lady Lightning, the gods of the cellar and the stove, if weak a ghost that died by scalding or burning; on Earth, if strong the god of the soil and a god of the centre and a god in charge of mountains, rivers and the altars of the land and grain, if weak a ghost that died buried under a fallen wall or collapsed house.

> [!warning] 打架
> 怎样认是哪一路神鬼：黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.50）按日辰的地支推（子是北斗或北阴，亥是溺死鬼），王洪绪按生合世爻或用爻的那一爻带的六神认；觉子这里按鬼爻自己的五行和旺衰认（旺相是神，休囚是鬼），又说鬼休囚或空破墓绝根本不是鬼神作祟。三家的办法各不相同。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to tell which sort of spirit or ghost it is: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.50) reasons from the branch of the Day (Zi is the Northern Dipper or the North Yin, Hai a drowned ghost), and Wang Hongxu from the six spirits carried by the line that generates or combines with the Self or Subject line; Juezi here tells by the phase and strength of the Pressure line itself (strong is a god, resting or trapped a ghost), and adds that Pressure that is resting or trapped, Void, broken, in a tomb or extinct is not the harm of ghosts and spirits at all. The three ways all differ.

> [!note] 按
> 「武神」是关公、岳飞一类的神；「文神」是文昌、孔子一类，书里没有列名单，译文按字面。「刑杖悬梁」是被杖打死、吊死。「雷公电母」是司雷电的神，「窖灶」是地窖和灶，「社稷」是土神和谷神。
>
> *Note:* 'Martial gods' are gods such as Guan Yu and Yue Fei, and 'civil gods' are of the kind of Wenchang and Confucius; the book gives no list, and the translation follows the letter. 'The rod or hanging from a beam' is being beaten to death or hanged. The 'Duke of Thunder and Lady Lightning' are the gods of thunder and lightning, 'cellar and stove' are the underground store and the hearth, and 'the altars of the land and grain' are the gods of soil and grain.
