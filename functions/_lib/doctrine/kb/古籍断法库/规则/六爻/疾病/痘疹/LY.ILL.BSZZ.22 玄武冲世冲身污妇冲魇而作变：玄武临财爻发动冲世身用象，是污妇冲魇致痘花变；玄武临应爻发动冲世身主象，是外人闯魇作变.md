# 玄武冲世冲身污妇冲魇而作变：玄武临财爻发动冲世身用象，是污妇冲魇致痘花变；玄武临应爻发动冲世身主象，是外人闯魇作变

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 玄武冲世爻、冲卦身，是不洁的妇人冲撞魇魅，使痘起了变化。具体说，玄武带着财爻发动，来冲世爻、卦身、用神的象，是因为不洁的妇人冲撞魇魅，使痘花起了变化；玄武带着应爻发动，来冲世爻、卦身、主象，是被外人闯进来冲撞魇魅而起了变化。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Dark Tortoise clashing the Self and the Body means an unclean woman has clashed and cursed and made the pox change. Specifically, if the Dark Tortoise carries the Wealth line and it moves to clash the Self, the Body or the image of the Subject, it is because an unclean woman has clashed and cursed and caused the pox flowers to change; if the Dark Tortoise carries the Other line and it moves to clash the Self, the Body or the main image, an outsider has burst in and clashed and cursed and caused the change.

> [!note] 按
> 「污妇」是不洁的妇人，书里没有说指的是什么情形；「冲魇」是冲撞、魇魅，按字面译。这一条是把痘花的变化归给外来的人事，不是归给病人自己的身体。
>
> *Note:* An 'unclean woman' is not defined in the book; 'chong yan' is to clash and to curse, rendered literally. This entry attributes a change in the pox flowers to a person coming from outside, not to the patient's own body.
