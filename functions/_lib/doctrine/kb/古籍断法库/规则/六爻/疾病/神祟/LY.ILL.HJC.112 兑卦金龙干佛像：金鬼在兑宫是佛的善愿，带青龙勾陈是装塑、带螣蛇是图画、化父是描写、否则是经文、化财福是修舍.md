# 兑卦金龙干佛像：金鬼在兑宫是佛的善愿，带青龙勾陈是装塑、带螣蛇是图画、化父是描写、否则是经文、化财福是修舍

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金在兑卦里发动，涉及佛像：兑是西方的卦，佛又是金身，所以遇到兑宫的金鬼，神佛那里一定有善愿。带青龙、勾陈，是要装銮塑像；带螣蛇，是想画像；变成父母，是描摹；不是这些，就是经文；变成财或福，是想修建房舍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Metal moving in the Dui trigram concerns a Buddha image: Dui is a trigram of the west and a Buddha has a body of gold, so meeting a Metal ghost in the Dui palace, there is surely a good vow to a god or Buddha. With the Azure Dragon and Hook Earth, it is to mount and mould an image; with the Coiling Snake, to paint a picture; changing into Resource, to copy or delineate; otherwise, scripture; changing into Wealth or Output, to build or repair a hall.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 金在兑宫发动，金身佛像； | 金在兑宫发动，是金身的佛像； | Metal moving in the Dui palace: a Buddha image with a body of gold; |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文「金龙」按旧注是兑宫金鬼带青龙，「干」是涉及、冒犯。王洪绪的注只有一句「金身佛像」，旧注按六神和变出的六亲分了装塑、图画、描写、经文、修舍五种。「舍」在这里指房舍、殿堂，旧注没有再解释。
>
> *Note:* By the old commentary, 'gold dragon' in the verse is a Metal ghost in the Dui palace carrying the Azure Dragon, and 'gan' means to concern or to offend. Wang Hongxu's commentary is the one phrase 'a Buddha image with a body of gold', while the old commentary divides by the six spirits and the relation it changes into: mounting and moulding, painting, copying, scripture, and building. 'She' here means a house or hall, and the old commentary does not explain further.
