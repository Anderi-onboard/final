# 生死·凶卦：黄泉杀（春大畜小畜履井复、夏遁睽临、秋明夷蛊旅、冬遁既济明夷归妹）、四灭卦（春蒙夏蛊秋剥冬旅）、四没卦（春需夏观秋节冬临）、棺椁杀（恒与益），占病逢之死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 黄泉杀：春天是大畜、小畜、履、井、复，夏天是遁、睽、临，秋天是明夷、蛊、旅，冬天是遁、既济、明夷、归妹。四灭卦：春天蒙、夏天蛊、秋天剥、冬天旅。四没卦：春天需、夏天观、秋天节、冬天临。棺椁杀：恒和益。以上这些卦，占病的人逢到就会死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Yellow Springs killer: in spring Daxu, Xiaoxu, Lu, Jing and Fu; in summer Dun, Kui and Lin; in autumn Mingyi, Gu and Lü; in winter Dun, Jiji, Mingyi and Guimei. The Four Extinguishing hexagrams: Meng in spring, Gu in summer, Bo in autumn, Lü in winter. The Four Submerging hexagrams: Xu in spring, Guan in summer, Jie in autumn, Lin in winter. The Coffin killer: Heng and Yi. One who divines about illness and meets any of these hexagrams dies.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「遁」在黄泉杀的夏和冬里各出现一次，原文如此。这几组卦名书里只列名单，没有说为什么；「四灭」「四没」是按季节各取一卦的名目。「旅」（Lü）和「履」（Lu）是两个卦。「已上诸卦占病者死」是书里对这一组的总结，是说占病遇到这些卦就是死，写得很绝对，本库照抄，不替它担保。
>
> *Note:* 'Dun' appears once in summer and once in winter in the Yellow Springs killer, as in the original. The book gives only the lists of hexagram names and no reasons; the 'Four Extinguishing' and 'Four Submerging' are names for one hexagram per season. 'Lü' and 'Lu' are two hexagrams. 'For all the hexagrams above, one who divines about illness dies' is the book's own summing up of the group and is stated absolutely; the library copies it and does not stand surety for it.
