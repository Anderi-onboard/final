# 总断千金赋·刑害不宜临用（旧注：刑爻为主象占病病必死、占人人有疾；王洪绪：占病沉重、占人有病）

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「刑和害不适宜临在用爻上。」旧注：凡是刑爻成为主象，一定不利：占事，事不成；占物，物不好；占病，病一定死；占人，这个人有病；占妇人，一定不贞洁；占文书，一定有破绽；占官司，有利也一定有罪责；害爻成为主象，也一定坏事，大致和刑爻相似，化入的也一样，但要按衰旺分它的轻重。王洪绪：用神、身、世遇到日辰相刑，一定不利，占病是病沉重，占人是有病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'Punishment and harm should not fall on the Subject.' The old commentary: whenever a punishing line becomes the principal image it is sure to be unfavourable: an affair does not come to completion, a thing is not good, in illness the illness is sure to be fatal, in divining a person the person is ill, a woman is surely not chaste, documents are sure to have a flaw, a lawsuit brings blame even with a gain; a harming line as the principal image surely spoils things too, much like a punishing line, and so too one that is changed into, but its weight must be told by strength and weakness. Wang Hongxu: when the Subject, Body or Self meets a Day branch that punishes it, it is sure to be unfavourable, in illness the illness is grave, and in divining a person he is ill.

> [!warning] 打架
> 刑爻为主象，占病断到什么程度，两家不同。旧注说「占病病必死」；王洪绪说「占病沉重」（病重，没有说死）。两处都说占人是「人有疾（有病）」，都要再按衰旺分轻重。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two commentaries differ on how far to take illness when a punishing line is the principal image. The old commentary says 'in divining illness the illness is sure to be fatal'; Wang Hongxu says 'in divining illness the illness is grave' (grave, without saying death). Both say that in divining a person he is ill, and both say to tell the weight by strength and weakness. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「主象」是所占的那一爻（占病时是用神）。「刑」是三刑一类的相刑，「害」是六害。王洪绪的这一句后面还有一句「死绝于日辰之爻临持世身用神者，诸占不利，变动化入」，原文到「变动化入」就断了，不收。原文写「刑害不宜临用」的下一句是「死绝岂可持身」，是另一联。
>
> *Note:* The 'principal image' is the line being divined (in divining illness, the Subject). 'Punishment' is the mutual punishment of the three-punishment kind, and 'harm' is the six harms. Wang Hongxu's text goes on with another sentence, 'a line of death or extinction by the Day branch sitting on the Self, Body or Subject: no divination is favourable, in moving and changing into', but the text breaks off at 'in moving and changing into' and it is not taken. The next line after 'punishment and harm should not fall on the Subject' in the text is 'death and extinction, how can they hold the Body', which is another couplet.
