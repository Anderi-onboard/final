# 行人·墓持墓动，必然卧病呻吟（旧注：用爻入墓化墓或持鬼墓或伏鬼墓爻下皆主病卧他家，伏官爻下亦然；带朱雀或化文书必在狱中非病；王洪绪：伏官爻下或临白虎在狱非病）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》行人章说：「墓持着墓、墓发动，一定卧病呻吟。」旧注：用爻入墓又化成墓，或者持着鬼墓，或者卦里有鬼墓爻发动，或者用爻伏在鬼墓爻下面，都主病卧在别人家，所以不回来；如果伏在官爻下面，也是这样；带朱雀，或者化出文书，一定是在狱中，不是病。王洪绪：用爻像墓化成墓，或持鬼墓，或伏在鬼墓爻下，都主卧病不回；如果伏在官爻下，或者临白虎，一定是在狱中，不是病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on travellers says: 'A tomb holding a tomb and moving: surely lying ill and groaning.' The old commentary: if the Subject line enters a tomb and turns into a tomb, or holds the ghost's tomb, or the hexagram has the ghost's tomb line moving, or the Subject line is hidden under the ghost's tomb line, all mean he lies ill in another's house and so does not return; if it is hidden under the Pressure line, the same; if it carries the Vermilion Bird or turns into documents, he is surely in prison and not ill. Wang Hongxu: if the Subject line is a tomb turning into a tomb, or holds the ghost's tomb, or is hidden under the ghost's tomb line, all mean lying ill and not returning; if it is hidden under the Pressure line, or sits on the White Tiger, he is surely in prison and not ill.

> [!warning] 打架
> 用爻伏在官爻下面，是病还是狱，两家说法相反。旧注：伏在官爻下面「亦然」，也是病卧他家；带朱雀或化出文书才是在狱中、不是病。王洪绪：伏在官爻下面，或者临白虎，必在狱中、非病。同一条上，旧注把「伏官爻下」归到病，王洪绪把它归到狱，而临白虎则被王洪绪归到狱。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two commentaries say opposite things on whether a Subject line hidden under the Pressure line is illness or prison. The old commentary says that hidden under the Pressure line it is 'the same', also lying ill in another's house, and only carrying the Vermilion Bird or turning into documents means prison and not illness; Wang Hongxu says hidden under the Pressure line, or sitting on the White Tiger, he is surely in prison and not ill. On this one case the old commentary files 'hidden under the Pressure line' under illness and Wang Hongxu files it under prison, and for sitting on the White Tiger Wang Hongxu files it under prison. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联在行人章里，问的是出门在外的人为什么不回，墓是原因之一：用爻入墓、化墓、持着鬼墓，那个人是病倒在别人家。「鬼墓」是官鬼所入的墓库。这一联讲的是不在场的人的病，放在他人病里；和前面的「如藏官下」（LY.ILL.HJC.144）是同一章里的两条。「呻吟」是病中的呻吟声。
>
> *Note:* This couplet in the chapter on travellers asks why a person away from home does not return, and the tomb is one cause: with the Subject line entering a tomb, turning into a tomb or holding the ghost's tomb, the person has fallen ill in another's house. The 'ghost's tomb' is the tomb that the Pressure line enters. This is about the illness of someone not present and is placed under the illness of others; it is one of two entries from the same chapter, with 'if hidden under the officer' (LY.ILL.HJC.144). 'Groaning' is the sound of groaning in illness.
