# 先看子孙：能克制官鬼，古人叫解神、又名福德，占病里就是医药；卦中无它则鬼无制伏、服药不效祷神不灵；要生旺有气，日辰动爻生扶则吉、冲坏则凶；占父母丈夫病不宜它发动

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断病先看子孙爻。子孙能克制官鬼这个凶煞，古人叫它解神，又叫福德，占病时它又是医药。卦里没有子孙，鬼就没有东西制伏，吃药没有效果，祷告求神也不灵。子孙要得令有力，不要衰弱、被困、死、绝；日辰、动爻来生它、扶它就吉，来冲它、破坏它就凶。替父母或丈夫占病，子孙不宜发动：动了会伤克夫星，又克伤父母爻的原神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In judging illness look first at Output. Output can control and restrain the Pressure killer; the ancients called it the relieving spirit and also Output-virtue, and in divining illness it is also the medicine. If there is no Output in the hexagram, Pressure has nothing to restrain it: medicine has no effect and prayer to the spirits is not answered. Output should be in season and have force, not resting, trapped, dead or extinct; if the Day or a moving line generates and supports it, it is lucky, and if they clash and spoil it, unlucky. In divining the illness of a parent or a husband, Output should not move: moving, it harms and controls the husband star and also harms the Support of the Resource line.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「解神」是能解除灾厄的神，子孙克官鬼，所以叫解神；「福德」是子孙的另一个名字。「生扶拱合」是帮用神的四种办法：生是五行相生，扶和拱是同一五行的两个地支互相帮衬（《卜筮正宗》在别处举的例子：亥扶子、子拱亥），合是二合、三合、六合。旧注的「官杀」「鬼杀」，王洪绪写作「鬼煞」，是同一个意思。王洪绪比旧注多说了一条：替父母或丈夫占病，子孙不宜发动。丈夫用官鬼，父母的原神也是官鬼（官鬼生父母），所以子孙一动就克到病人依靠的那一爻；旧注没有这个限定。「财鬼无制」，旧注只说「鬼无制伏」，王洪绪多了一个财。
>
> *Note:* The 'relieving spirit' is the spirit that can relieve disaster; Output controls Pressure, hence the name. 'Output-virtue' (fude) is another name for Output. 'Generating, supporting, embracing and combining' are four ways of helping the Subject: generating is the generation of the five phases; supporting and embracing are two branches of the same phase helping each other (the Bushi Zhengzong gives elsewhere the examples Hai supports Zi and Zi embraces Hai); combining is the two-combine, the three-combine and the six-combine. The old commentary's 'guan sha' and 'gui sha' are written 'gui sha' by Wang Hongxu, and mean the same. Wang Hongxu adds one thing to the old commentary: in divining the illness of a parent or a husband, Output should not move. A husband takes Pressure, and the Support of the Resource line is also Pressure (Pressure generates Resource), so a moving Output controls the very line the patient depends on; the old commentary has no such limit. Of 'Wealth and Pressure unrestrained' the old commentary says only 'Pressure has nothing to restrain it', and Wang Hongxu adds Wealth.
