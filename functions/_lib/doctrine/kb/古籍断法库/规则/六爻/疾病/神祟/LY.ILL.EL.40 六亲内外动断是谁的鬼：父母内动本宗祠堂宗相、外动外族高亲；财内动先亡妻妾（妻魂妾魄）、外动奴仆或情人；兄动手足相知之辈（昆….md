# 六亲内外动断是谁的鬼：父母内动本宗祠堂宗相、外动外族高亲；财内动先亡妻妾（妻魂妾魄）、外动奴仆或情人；兄动手足相知之辈（昆弟姨妹朋友）；福动儿孙之鬼、僧道之灵（儿女侄婿）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻在内发动，才断祠堂里的宗相；父母爻在外发动，可以说是外族的高亲；财爻在内宫动，必是妻子的魂、妾的魄；妻财在外卦摇动，不是奴仆就是情人；兄弟发动，是手足之亲、相知的人；子孙发动，是儿孙的鬼，僧道的灵。注说：父爻在内卦以及在亲宫动的，就说是本宗的尊长；父爻居外卦以及在他宫动的，就说是外姓的尊亲；财爻临内卦或在亲宫动的，必是先亡的妻妾；财爻在他宫以及外卦动的，如果不是奴仆，必是恩爱的情人；兄弟动的，或是兄弟、或是姨妹、或是朋友的魂；子孙动的，是儿女、是侄、是女婿，又是僧道的魂。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line moving inside is judged the ancestral image of the ancestral hall; the Resource line moving outside may be called a high relative of another clan; Wealth moving in the inner palace is surely a wife's spirit and a concubine's soul; Wealth stirring in the outer trigram is either a servant or a lover; Peer thriving is a kin of hand and foot, an intimate; Output moving is the ghost of sons and grandsons, the spirit of a monk or Daoist. The commentary says: the Resource line moving in the inner trigram or in a kin palace is called an elder of one's own line; the Resource line in the outer trigram or moving in another palace is a revered kinsman of another surname; Wealth in the inner trigram or moving in a kin palace is surely a wife or concubine who died earlier; Wealth in another palace or moving in the outer trigram, if not a servant, is surely a loving sweetheart; when Peer moves, it is the spirit of a brother, or an aunt or sister, or a friend; when Output moves, it is a son or daughter, a nephew, a son-in-law, and also the spirit of a monk or Daoist.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节和易冒「按六亲断神鬼」（LY.ILL.YM.60）、黄金策「以卦身推是什么人的鬼」和「鬼动化出六亲即以化出者断」（LY.ILL.HJC.102、HJC.108）是同一件事。易林补遗比易冒多了一个内外的分法：父母爻在内卦、亲宫是本宗的，在外卦、他宫是外姓的；财爻在内是先亡的妻妾，在外是奴仆或情人。「亲宫」是本宫（与卦同宫的爻所在的宫）。「椿萱」是父母的雅称，原文这里用在歌诀里。
>
> *Note:* This section is the same matter as the Yi Mao's 'reading ghosts and spirits by the six relations' (LY.ILL.YM.60) and the Huangjin Ce's 'reading whose ghost it is from the Body' and 'a ghost moving into a relation is judged by what it turns into' (LY.ILL.HJC.102, HJC.108). The Yilin Buyi adds a division into inner and outer beyond the Yi Mao: the Resource line in the inner trigram or a kin palace is of one's own line, and in the outer trigram or another palace of another surname; Wealth inside is a wife or concubine who died earlier, and outside a servant or a lover. A 'kin palace' is the home palace (the palace to which the hexagram belongs). 'Chun-xuan' is a polite name for parents and is used here in the verse.
