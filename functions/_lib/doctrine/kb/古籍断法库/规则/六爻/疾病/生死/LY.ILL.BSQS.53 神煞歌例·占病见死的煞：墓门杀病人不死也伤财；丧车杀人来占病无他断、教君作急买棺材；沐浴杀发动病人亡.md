# 神煞歌例·占病见死的煞：墓门杀病人不死也伤财；丧车杀人来占病无他断、教君作急买棺材；沐浴杀发动病人亡

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神煞歌例里三个主病人死亡的煞：墓门杀，甲乙见金、丙丁见水、戊己见木、庚辛见火、壬癸见土，病人不死也伤财；丧车杀，春鸡夏鼠秋兔冬马，有人来占病，没有别的可断，劝你赶紧去买棺材；沐浴杀，春辰夏未秋戌冬丑，发动，病人死亡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Three killings in the verses on spirit-stars that mean a sick person's death: the Tomb Gate killing, Jia and Yi meeting Metal, Bing and Ding Water, Wu and Ji Wood, Geng and Xin Fire, Ren and Gui Earth: the sick person, if he does not die, at least loses money; the Mourning Cart killing, the rooster in spring, the rat in summer, the rabbit in autumn and the horse in winter: a person who comes to divine illness has nothing else to be judged, and I advise you to buy a coffin at once; the Bathing killing, Chen in spring, Wei in summer, Xu in autumn and Chou in winter: if it moves, the sick person dies.

> [!warning] 打架
> 丧车杀的四季位置，两本不一样。卜筮全书这一首是「春鸡夏鼠秋兔冬马」；断易天机（LY.ILL.DYTJ.32）是「春鸡夏鼠秋马冬羊」；春、夏两季一致，秋、冬不同。另外断易天机的旌旗杀（LY.ILL.DYTJ.33）是「春兔夏鼠秋鸡冬马」，和卜筮全书这一首的丧车只差春、秋两季的鸡兔互换。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The seasonal positions of the Mourning Cart differ between the two books. This verse in the Bushi Quanshu has 'the rooster in spring, the rat in summer, the rabbit in autumn, the horse in winter'; the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.32) has 'the rooster in spring, the rat in summer, the horse in autumn, the sheep in winter'; spring and summer agree, autumn and winter do not. Moreover the Banner killing of the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.33) has 'the rabbit in spring, the rat in summer, the rooster in autumn, the horse in winter', which differs from the Mourning Cart of this verse only in the rooster and rabbit swapped between spring and autumn. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 墓门杀的五句，每句的前一个字组是日干（甲乙、丙丁、戊己、庚辛、壬癸），后一个字是五行，对着的正好是克那一组日干的五行（木逢金、火逢水、土逢木、金逢火、水逢土），也就是各日干的官鬼；歌里没有这样说明，这是从字面排出来的。「哭哀哀」「孝服」是丧事的征兆。丧车杀的位置，按地支对冲推：春（卯）冲酉（鸡）、夏（午）冲子（鼠）、秋（酉）冲卯（兔）、冬（子）冲午（马），卜筮全书这一首正好是各季旺支的对冲，断易天机的秋冬两处不是；这是按地支推出来的，书里没有这样讲。「教君作急买棺材」是语气很重的一句，原文如此。沐浴杀的位置（春辰夏未秋戌冬丑）和本篇疾病论里催尸杀的位置一样（LY.ILL.BSQS.36），歌里没有说它们是不是同一个煞。
>
> *Note:* In each of the five lines on the Tomb Gate killing, the first group of characters is a pair of day stems (Jia and Yi, Bing and Ding, Wu and Ji, Geng and Xin, Ren and Gui) and the second is an element, and the element is exactly the one that controls that pair of stems (Wood meeting Metal, Fire meeting Water, Earth meeting Wood, Metal meeting Fire, Water meeting Earth), that is, the Pressure of each day stem; the verse does not say so and this is laid out from the letter. 'Mournful weeping' and 'mourning dress' are signs of a funeral. The positions of the Mourning Cart, reckoned by the clashes of the branches: spring (Mao) clashes You (the rooster), summer (Wu) clashes Zi (the rat), autumn (You) clashes Mao (the rabbit), winter (Zi) clashes Wu (the horse); this verse in the Bushi Quanshu is exactly the clash of each season's ruling branch, and the Duanyi Tianji's autumn and winter are not; this is reckoned from the branches and the book does not say it. 'I advise you to buy a coffin at once' is a very heavy sentence, as the original has it. The positions of the Bathing killing (Chen in spring, Wei in summer, Xu in autumn, Chou in winter) are the same as those of the Urging-the-Corpse killing in the chapter on illness in this book (LY.ILL.BSQS.36), and the verses do not say whether they are the same killing.
