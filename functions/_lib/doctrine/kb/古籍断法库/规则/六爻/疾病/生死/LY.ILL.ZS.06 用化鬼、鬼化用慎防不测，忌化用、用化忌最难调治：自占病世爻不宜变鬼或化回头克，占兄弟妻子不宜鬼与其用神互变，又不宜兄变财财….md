# 用化鬼、鬼化用慎防不测，忌化用、用化忌最难调治：自占病世爻不宜变鬼或化回头克，占兄弟妻子不宜鬼与其用神互变，又不宜兄变财财化兄父化子子化父；父动化鬼是化生，轻病即愈，久病鬼化父、父化鬼皆主危亡

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神变成官鬼、官鬼变成用神，要慎防不测；忌神变成用神、用神变成忌神，最难调治。自己占病，世爻不宜变鬼，也不宜变出回头克；占兄弟、妻子的病，鬼与兄弟、妻子不宜互变，兄变财、财变兄、父变子、子变父也不宜。鬼是父母的元神，父母爻动、变成鬼，是化生，病轻的马上好；久病的，鬼变父、父变鬼，都主危亡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject changing into Pressure or Pressure changing into the Subject calls for great care against the unforeseen; the Adversary changing into the Subject or the Subject into the Adversary is hardest to treat. In one's own illness the Self line should not change into Pressure or into a returning control; in a brother's or a wife's illness Pressure and the Peer or Wealth line should not change into each other, and neither should Peer into Wealth, Wealth into Peer, Resource into Output or Output into Resource. Pressure is the Support of Resource, so Resource moving and changing into Pressure is a changing into generation and a light illness heals at once; in a long illness, Pressure changing into Resource or Resource into Pressure always means danger of death.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「用化鬼」是不是必死：黄金策旧注读作用爻发动又变出鬼爻、没有解救必死（LY.ILL.HJC.44），王洪绪反对拿「用神变官鬼必死」来断（同条）；野鹤的说法在两者之间：用化鬼是凶兆，轻病（父动化鬼是化生）和近病落旬空的可以愈，久病则鬼化父、父化鬼都主危亡。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether 'the Subject changing into Pressure' is surely fatal: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce reads it as the Subject line moving and also changing out a Pressure line, and with no rescue he surely dies (LY.ILL.HJC.44), while Wang Hongxu rejects judging 'the Subject changing into Pressure is surely fatal' (same entry); the Old Man of Yehe stands between the two: the Subject changing into Pressure is an ill omen, but a light illness (Resource moving into Pressure is a changing into generation) and a recent illness whose Subject falls into Void can recover, and in a long illness Pressure changing into Resource or Resource into Pressure always means danger of death.

> [!note] 按
> 书中例里，儿子占病，用神是子孙，「鬼变子孙」就是官鬼变成用神。子孙落旬空，所以近病出空之日就好了；但「用化鬼」这个凶兆没有消失，后来在午年死于痘症。
>
> *Note:* In the case, a son's illness is divined, so the Subject is Output, and 'Pressure changing into Output' is Pressure changing into the Subject. Output being in Void, the recent illness healed on the day the Void was out; but the ill omen of 'the Subject changing into Pressure' did not go away, and he later died of smallpox in the Wu year.
