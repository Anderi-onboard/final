# 占鬼神·凡祭赛有三：祀上帝取藏爻中鬼神祗用月建、神堂家庙用日辰；皆要生合卦身不宜刑冲、不要动爻克害刑冲；合生福利而吉、带刑冲反招祸；卦爻中鬼自化入墓必有不了再牵之患

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 祭赛（祭神还愿）有三种：祭上帝，就取伏藏的爻里的鬼神，要用月建；神堂家庙，要用日辰。都要生合卦身，不宜刑冲，也不要动爻来克害刑冲。若合而生，福利而吉；若带刑冲，反而招祸。卦爻中的鬼自己化入墓，必有了不完、再牵连的祸患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Offerings and thanksgiving are of three kinds: for sacrificing to the High God take the ghost or spirit in the hidden line and use the Month; for a shrine hall or a family temple use the Day. All must generate and combine with the Body of the hexagram, must not clash or punish, and a moving line must not attack, harm, punish or clash with it. If they combine and generate it is fortunate and lucky; if it bears punishment or clash it invites calamity instead. If the ghost in the hexagram lines itself changes into a tomb, there will surely be an unfinished matter that drags on again.

> [!note] 按
> 原文「凡祭赛有三」只列出两种（祀上帝、神堂家庙），第三种没有写出来，这里按原文译，不补。「藏爻中鬼神祗」是伏藏的爻里的鬼神，「祗」是地神；「当用月建」「当用日辰」是说祭上帝取月建为主、神堂家庙取日辰为主（月建管大，日辰管近）。「皆要生合卦身」的「卦身」译作 the Body of the hexagram。这一节讲还愿酬神时怎么看祭，和易林补遗的「还赛解厄」（LY.ILL.EL.57）、「解禳通用法」（LY.ILL.EL.54）是同一类；这里的判据是生合卦身则吉、刑冲则祸。
>
> *Note:* The original 'offerings and thanksgiving are of three kinds' lists only two (sacrificing to the High God, and the shrine hall and family temple), and the third is not written, and here it is rendered as the original and not filled in. 'The ghost or spirit in the hidden line' is the ghost or spirit in the concealed line, and 'zhi' is an earth deity; 'use the Month' and 'use the Day' says that sacrifice to the High God takes the Month as the main and the shrine hall and family temple take the Day (the Month rules the great, the Day the near). The 'Body' of 'must generate and combine with the Body of the hexagram' is rendered the Body of the hexagram. This section tells how to look at a sacrifice when paying a vow to the gods, and is of the same kind as the Yilin Buyi's 'giving thanks and resolving misfortune' (LY.ILL.EL.57) and 'the general method of ward-offs' (LY.ILL.EL.54); the criterion here is that generating and combining with the Body is lucky and punishment or clash is calamity.
