# 既论染灾之表里须言得病之因由：先断症候然后议病因；六兽临官分内外、八宫值鬼别阴阳；鬼临六神看在何爻——在内在家得病、在外他处招殃；青龙鬼酒色过度或因财有伤、妇人因喜有疾、鬼空堕胎；朱雀口舌咒咀（二爻灶下三爻当门或家堂神前、金鬼动敲锅铛）；勾陈饮食不节脾胃不调、临木鬼因修造动土；螣蛇事务萦心忧愁思虑虚惊怪异；白虎斗殴跌扑伤筋损骨或寒酸冲肺；玄武色欲过度伤饥冒雨

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 已经讲了染灾的表里，还要说得病的因由。注说：占病，先断定症候，然后议论病因；能把这一章弄明白，才称得上通神的占卜。六兽临在官鬼上，要分内外；八宫值鬼，要分阴阳。注说：但凡鬼临着六神，就看它在哪一爻：在内，是在家里得病；在外，是在别处招来的灾殃。青龙鬼：酒色过度，或者因为财而有伤，妇人是因喜事有病；青龙鬼落空，有堕胎的忧患。朱雀鬼：口舌和咒骂，在二爻是在灶下咒骂，在三爻是当门咒骂，或者是家堂神前咒骂；如果朱雀临着金鬼发动，是敲着锅铛咒骂。勾陈鬼：饮食不节制，饥饱失常，脾胃不调；临着木鬼，是在修造动土的地方得的病。螣蛇鬼：事务缠心，忧愁思虑，或者虚惊怪异，因而得病，这一爻大多是心家受病。白虎鬼：斗殴、跌扑，伤筋损骨，或者是寒酸冲肺。玄武鬼：色欲过度，饥饱失常、冒雨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Having discussed the outside and inside of the infection, one must now speak of the cause of the illness. The commentary says: in divining illness, first settle the symptoms, and then discuss the cause; if one can understand this chapter, one may truly be called one whose divination reaches the gods. When the six beasts sit on the Pressure line one must distinguish inside and outside; when the ghost falls in the eight palaces one should tell yin from yang. The commentary says: whenever a ghost sits on one of the six spirits, look at which line it is on: inside, the illness was got at home; outside, calamity was brought on elsewhere. The Azure Dragon ghost: excess in wine and lust, or injury through wealth, and in a woman illness from a joyful event; the Azure Dragon ghost Void: the danger of miscarriage. The Vermilion Bird ghost: quarrels and cursing; on the second line, cursing beside the stove; on the third line, cursing at the door, or cursing before the household gods; if the Vermilion Bird sits on a Metal ghost that moves, cursing while banging pots and pans. The Hook Earth ghost: irregular eating, hunger and overeating, the spleen and stomach out of order; if it sits on a Wood ghost, the illness was got at a place of building and digging. The Coiling Snake ghost: affairs entangling the heart, worry and brooding, or a false fright and strangeness, and so the illness is contracted; this line is mostly an illness of the heart. The White Tiger ghost: brawls and falls, injury to sinews and bones, or cold and sourness striking the lungs. The Dark Tortoise ghost: excess of lust, hunger and overeating, and exposure to rain.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节接上一条（TXF.08）之后，是「先断症候、再议病因」的分界：赋文「既论染灾之表里，须言得病之因由」是上半章（症候、轻重、生死）到下半章（病因、医药）的转折句。「六兽临官」是官鬼临着哪一神，「八宫值鬼宜别阴阳」是鬼在哪一宫、阴爻还是阳爻，注文只详细写了六兽，没有展开八宫。六神鬼的病因和易隐「病因·六神」（LY.ILL.YY.17）、易林补遗「按六神论病由何得」（LY.ILL.EL.05）、黄金策六神病因是同一类办法，天玄赋这里还多了一层：朱雀鬼在二爻是灶下咒咀、三爻是当门咒咀，金鬼动是「敲锅铛咒咀」，这和易隐同条几乎一字不差（LY.ILL.YY.17「朱雀……二爻灶下、三爻当门、五爻香火道路、金鬼敲锅」），易隐还多了五爻。「寒酸冲肺」按字面译作「cold and sourness striking the lungs」。「妇人因喜有疾」是女人因喜事（生子、宴会）得病。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* This section follows the previous entry (TXF.08) and is the dividing line between 'first settle the symptoms, then discuss the cause': the verse 'having discussed the outside and inside of the infection, one must speak of the cause of the illness' is the turn from the first half of the chapter (symptoms, light and heavy, life and death) to the second (cause, medicine). 'The six beasts sit on the Pressure line' is which spirit the Pressure line sits on, and 'the ghost falls in the eight palaces, tell yin from yang' is which palace the ghost is in and whether the line is yin or yang, but the commentary sets out only the six beasts in detail and does not expand the eight palaces. The causes of illness by the six spirits are the same kind of method as the Yi Yin's 'cause of illness by the six spirits' (LY.ILL.YY.17), the Yilin Buyi's 'how the illness was contracted by the six spirits' (LY.ILL.EL.05) and the Huangjin Ce's causes by the six spirits, and here the Tianxuan Fu adds a further layer: the Vermilion Bird ghost on the second line is cursing beside the stove and on the third line cursing at the door, and a Metal ghost moving is 'cursing while banging pots and pans', which is almost word for word the same as the Yi Yin's same entry (LY.ILL.YY.17, 'the Vermilion Bird ... second line beside the stove, third line at the door, fifth line incense fire and roads, a Metal ghost banging pots'), the Yi Yin adding the fifth line. 'Cold and sourness striking the lungs' is rendered literally. 'In a woman illness from a joyful event' is a woman falling ill from a joyful event (a birth, a feast). After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
