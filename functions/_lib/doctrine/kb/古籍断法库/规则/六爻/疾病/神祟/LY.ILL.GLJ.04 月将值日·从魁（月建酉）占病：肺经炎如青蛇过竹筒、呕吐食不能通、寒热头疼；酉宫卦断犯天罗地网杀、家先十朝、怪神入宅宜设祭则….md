# 月将值日·从魁（月建酉）占病：肺经炎如青蛇过竹筒、呕吐食不能通、寒热头疼；酉宫卦断犯天罗地网杀、家先十朝、怪神入宅宜设祭则沉病脱体

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在酉、叫从魁的时候占病：肺经发炎，像青蛇钻过竹筒，节节生忧，药难用上；呕吐，吃下去的东西又翻出来；忽冷忽热、头疼、心神不定；病退了一下，吃药也没有功效。「酉宫卦断」那一段说：冲犯了天罗地网煞，家先和十朝都在里面；怪神进了宅，适合设祭，设了祭，沉重的病就像风一样脱身而去。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on You, named Congkui, divining illness: the lung channel is inflamed, like a green snake passing through a bamboo tube, with worry at every joint and medicine hard to use; he retches and vomits and the food comes back; chills and fever, headache and a restless heart; even when it recedes for a time, taking medicine has no effect. The passage 'the verdict for the You-palace hexagrams' says: he has offended the Heaven's-Net and Earth's-Net Killer, with the household ancestors and the Ten Courts in it; uncanny spirits have entered the house and it is fitting to set out offerings, and then the deep illness leaves the body like the wind.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。标题括号「月建值如从上卦酉爻主八月事」里的「如从」两字不通，按上下文像是「从魁」写错了，译文照「酉=从魁」读，这是读法。「肺经炎」的「炎」在这本书里别处常是「灾」的误写，但这里和「青蛇过竹筒」「呕」「寒热」放在一起，读作肺经发炎（发热）更顺，仍是读法。「嗤呕」的「嗤」不知本字，按上下文读作呕吐一类；「近退」译成「刚退下去一点」是照字面。「十朝」书里没有解释，下一首说「十朝鬼判宫」，像是阴间十殿一类，译文照写「十朝」，不下定义。「天罗地网煞」是一种凶煞，书里没有解释。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. In the heading's bracket 'when the Month falls on ... the You line governs the matters of the eighth month' the two characters rendered 'on ... Congkui' do not make sense as printed and look like a miswriting of 'Congkui', and the translation reads 'You = Congkui', which is a reading. The 'inflamed' of 'the lung channel is inflamed' is elsewhere in this book often a misprint for 'calamity', but placed beside 'a green snake passing through a bamboo tube', 'retching' and 'chills and fever' it reads better as the lung channel being inflamed (hot), again a reading. The first character of 'retches' is not known as a word and is read from context as retching or vomiting; 'recedes a little' is the literal sense of 'nearly recedes'. The book does not explain 'the Ten Courts', and the next verse says 'the ghost judge's palace of the Ten Courts', which looks like the Ten Courts of the underworld, and the translation writes 'the Ten Courts' without defining them. 'The Heaven's-Net and Earth's-Net Killer' is a kind of fierce killer, not explained in the book.
