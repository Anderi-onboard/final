# 验福德以迎医：子孙在什么爻就知道该请哪个方位的医生（子北方、丑东北方）；寅木子孙其医是木旁草头的姓名或属虎，虽非东方皆能医

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是占服药，看子孙在哪一爻，就知道该请哪个方向的医生：子孙在子爻，宜请北方的医生；在丑爻，宜请东北方的医生，依此类推。又如寅爻是子孙，五行属木，那位医生姓名带木字旁或草字头，或者是属虎的；就算不在东北，也都能治。其余照这个类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining the taking of medicine, look at which line Output is on, and one knows from which direction a doctor can treat him: Output on the Zi line, a doctor from the north; on the Chou line, a doctor from the north-east, and so on. Again, if the Output line is Yin, which belongs to Wood, the doctor has a name with the wood radical or the grass radical, or was born in a tiger year; though not from the north-east, all of these can treat him. The rest follow this.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说寅木子孙「虽非东北」，王洪绪说「虽非东方」：寅在东北偏东，五行属木、木在东方，王洪绪的写法更顺。「木旁草头姓名」指姓名里有木字旁或草字头的字。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says of a Yin Wood Output 'though not from the north-east', Wang Hongxu 'though not from the east'; Yin lies east-north-east and its phase Wood belongs to the east, so Wang Hongxu's wording is the smoother. A 'name with the wood radical or the grass radical' means a name that has a character with the wood radical or the grass radical.
