# 用爻被哪一行克就伤哪一处：金克木肢体酸痛、木克土皮骨伤残、火为仇喘咳、水来害恍惚；卦中无鬼，看谁刑冲克害用爻

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有鬼爻，就看谁来刑、冲、克、害用爻来断。用爻被金伤，四肢一定酸痛；用爻遭木克，皮和骨一定伤残；火是仇敌，主喘咳的灾病；水来相害，主恍惚的症候。动的金克过来，木爻受伤，四肢关节酸痛；动的木克过来，土爻受制，皮肉伤损。其余照此类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With no Pressure line in the hexagram, judge by whatever punishes, clashes, controls or harms the Subject line. A Subject line wounded by Metal: the limbs surely ache and are sore; controlled by Wood: skin and bones are surely injured and maimed; with Fire as the enemy, the trouble of panting and cough; with Water coming to harm, a dazed, wandering state. So when a moving Metal line controls, the Wood line is wounded and the limbs and joints ache; when a moving Wood line controls, the Earth line is held down and flesh and skin are injured. The rest follows by analogy.

> [!note] 按
> 经文只说「用受金伤」「主遭木克」「火为仇」「水来害」，没有写用爻本身是什么五行；旧注只解释了前两句（金克的是木爻，木克的是土爻）。「火为仇」「水来害」对应的是用爻为金被火克、用爻为火被水克，这是按五行相克推的，书里没有写出来。旧注说「皮肉伤损」，王洪绪写「皮骨伤损」；旧注「肢节」，王洪绪写「支节」。
>
> *Note:* The verse only says 'the Subject wounded by Metal', 'the Subject controlled by Wood', 'Fire as the enemy' and 'Water coming to harm', and does not say which element the Subject line itself is; the old commentary explains only the first two (what Metal controls is the Wood line, what Wood controls is the Earth line). That 'Fire as the enemy' and 'Water coming to harm' correspond to a Metal Subject controlled by Fire and a Fire Subject controlled by Water is reasoned from the controlling cycle of the five phases and is not written in the book. The old commentary says 'flesh and skin are injured' and Wang Hongxu 'skin and bones are injured'; the old commentary writes 'limb joints' with one character and Wang Hongxu with another.
