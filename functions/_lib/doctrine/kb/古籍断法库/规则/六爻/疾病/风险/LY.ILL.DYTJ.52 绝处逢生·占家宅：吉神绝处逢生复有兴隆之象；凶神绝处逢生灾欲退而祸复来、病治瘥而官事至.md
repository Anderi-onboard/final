# 绝处逢生·占家宅：吉神绝处逢生复有兴隆之象；凶神绝处逢生灾欲退而祸复来、病治瘥而官事至

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占家宅时：吉神遇上绝处逢生，家宅又有兴隆的样子；凶神遇上绝处逢生，灾祸正要退下去，祸又转回来——病治好了，官事又到了。后面还有一句「占国同此义推」，占国家大事也照这个意思推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining the house: when a lucky spirit meets life in a place of extinction, the house again has the look of prosperity; when an ominous spirit meets life in a place of extinction, the calamity is about to withdraw and then misfortune comes again: the illness is treated and mends, and an official matter arrives. The book adds, 'divining the state is inferred in the same sense'.

> [!note] 按
> 「绝处逢生」的意思和例子见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.25（用爻落在绝地、有生它的爻发动来生，像人在困境里遇到贵人）；断易天机在那一章里给占婚、占产、占官、占讼、求财、出行、行人、家宅各写了一句，占病的一句收在 DYTJ.25，这一条收家宅里的一句，因为它提到了病：凶神绝处逢生，灾祸要退又回来，病治好了官事又到。「瘥」是病好；「官事」书里没有说是哪一类官事。这一句和占病那一句（「鬼爻与忌神绝处逢生，病欲安而复作」）是同一个思路——凶的一方在绝处遇到生它的，退下去的又回头——只是这里讲的是整个宅的事，病和官事是宅里先后出的两件。三本字句有出入：卜筮大全写「家宅：吉神逢绝处逢生，复有兴隆之象」，少了第二个「家宅」；火珠林写「吉神绝处逢生，复有兴隆之象」，少了「逢」，而且「家宅」两个字连在上一句「必遇故人同回家宅」的末尾；两本都没有「占国同此义推」。
>
> *Note:* For what 'meeting life in a place of extinction' means and an example, see LY.ILL.DYTJ.25 (a Subject line fallen in extinction, with a line that generates it moving to generate it, like a person in hard straits who meets a patron); in that chapter the Duanyi Tianji writes one line each for marriage, childbirth, office, lawsuit, wealth, travel, a traveller and the house, the line on illness is taken in DYTJ.25, and this entry takes the line on the house, because it mentions illness: an ominous spirit meeting life in a place of extinction, the calamity about to withdraw comes back, the illness is treated and mends, and an official matter arrives. 'Mends' is the book's word for recovering from illness; the book does not say what kind of official matter. The line is of one mind with the line on illness ('the Pressure line and the Adversary meeting life in a place of extinction, the illness about to settle breaks out again'): the ominous side meets what generates it in a place of extinction and what was withdrawing turns back; only here it is the whole house that is spoken of, and illness and official matters are two things that come one after the other in the house. The three books differ slightly: the Bushi Daquan reads 'the house: a lucky spirit meeting life in a place of extinction, again has the look of prosperity', lacking the second 'the house'; the Huozhu Lin reads 'a lucky spirit in a place of extinction meeting life, again has the look of prosperity', lacking the word for 'meeting', and the two characters for 'the house' are joined to the end of the line before, 'will surely meet an old friend and come home together with him'; neither has 'divining the state is inferred in the same sense'.
