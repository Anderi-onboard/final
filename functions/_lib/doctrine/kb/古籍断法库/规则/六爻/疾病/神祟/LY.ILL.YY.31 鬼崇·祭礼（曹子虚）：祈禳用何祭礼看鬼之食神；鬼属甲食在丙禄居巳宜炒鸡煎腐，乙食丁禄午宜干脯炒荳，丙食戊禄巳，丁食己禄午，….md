# 鬼崇·祭礼（曹子虚）：祈禳用何祭礼看鬼之食神；鬼属甲食在丙禄居巳宜炒鸡煎腐，乙食丁禄午宜干脯炒荳，丙食戊禄巳，丁食己禄午，戊食庚禄申宜三牲馒首果饼，己食辛禄酉宜鸡肉鱼馄饨，庚食壬禄亥宜猪首三牲，辛食癸禄子宜池鱼血羹麦面，壬食甲禄寅宜三牲时果新蔬，癸食乙禄卯宜鸡鹅鸭蔬果鸭蛋；送于何方以鬼爻支神定；子带龙雀素祭、带勾蛇虎武荤盘、伏化水鬼水父佛经、火鬼火父道醮、化财伏财福礼、化兄伏兄演戏众保

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 曹子虚说：祈禳用什么祭品礼数，要看鬼的食神。鬼属甲，食神在丙，禄在巳，用炒鸡、煎豆腐、酒、礼、纸钱纸马送走；鬼属乙，食神在丁，禄在午，用干肉脯、炒豆、酒、礼、纸钱纸马；属丙，食神在戊，禄在巳，同属甲的祭品；属丁，食神在己，禄在午，同属乙的祭品；属戊，食神在庚，禄在申，用三牲、馒头、果饼、酒、礼、纸钱纸马；属己，食神在辛，禄在酉，用鸡肉、鱼、馄饨、酒、礼、纸钱纸马；属庚，食神在壬，禄在亥，用猪头、三牲、酒、礼、纸钱纸马；属辛，食神在癸，禄在子，用池里的鱼、血羹、麦面、酒、礼、纸钱纸马；属壬，食神在甲，禄在寅，用三牲、时鲜的果子、新蔬菜、酒、礼、纸钱纸马；属癸，食神在乙，禄在卯，用鸡、鹅、鸭、蔬果、鸭蛋、酒、礼、纸钱纸马。送到哪个方向，以鬼爻的地支来定。如果是神，祈祷的礼数把子孙当作福神：子孙带青龙、朱雀的，适合素祭；带勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武的，适合荤盘；子孙伏在水鬼或水父母下、或变出水鬼水父母的，适合念佛经；伏在火鬼或火父母下、或变出火鬼火父母的，适合道教的醮；子孙变出财、或伏在财下的，适合福礼；变出兄、或伏在兄下的，适合演戏、大家一起保祷。这是祈禳的占法。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Cao Zixu says: what sacrifice and rite to use for prayer and exorcism depends on the ghost's food-star. If the ghost belongs to Jia, the food-star is Bing and the emolument is at Si, and one sends it off with stir-fried chicken, fried bean curd, wine, ritual gifts and paper money and horses; Yi: food-star Ding, emolument at Wu, dried meat, fried beans, wine, gifts and paper money and horses; Bing: food-star Wu, emolument at Si, as for Jia; Ding: food-star Ji, emolument at Wu, as for Yi; Wu: food-star Geng, emolument at Shen, the three sacrificial animals, steamed buns, fruit cakes, wine and gifts; Ji: food-star Xin, emolument at You, chicken, fish, wonton, wine and gifts; Geng: food-star Ren, emolument at Hai, a pig's head, the three animals, wine and gifts; Xin: food-star Gui, emolument at Zi, pond fish, blood soup, wheat noodles, wine and gifts; Ren: food-star Jia, emolument at Yin, the three animals, seasonal fruit, fresh vegetables, wine and gifts; Gui: food-star Yi, emolument at Mao, chicken, goose, duck, vegetables and fruit, duck eggs, wine and gifts. The direction in which to send it is fixed by the branch of the Pressure line. If it is a spirit, in the rites of prayer Output is taken as the spirit of blessing: Output carrying the Dragon and the Bird, a vegetarian sacrifice; carrying the Hook, Snake, Tiger and Tortoise, a meat platter; hidden beneath a Water ghost or Water Resource, or changing into one, Buddhist scripture; beneath a Fire ghost or Fire Resource, or changing into one, a Daoist ritual; changing into or beneath Wealth, a blessing rite; changing into or beneath Peer, a staged play and communal protection. This is the divination of prayer and exorcism.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条在原书里跨了 3019、3021 两行。「食神」按字面是「鬼所属的天干所生的天干」（甲生丙、乙生丁……），「禄居某」是那个食神的禄位（丙禄在巳、丁禄在午、戊禄在巳、己禄在午、庚禄在申、辛禄在酉、壬禄在亥、癸禄在子、甲禄在寅、乙禄在卯）；书里没有另作解释，这里按上下文理解。「三牲」是牛、羊、猪（或猪、鸡、鱼），「财马」是纸钱纸马，「醮」是道教的法事。书里在这一段没有给出为什么某个天干配某种祭品。
>
> *Note:* In the original this entry runs across lines 3019 and 3021. The 'food-star' (shishen) is literally the stem generated by the stem the ghost belongs to (Jia generates Bing, Yi generates Ding ...), and 'emolument at' is that food-star's emolument seat (Bing at Si, Ding at Wu, Wu at Si, Ji at Wu, Geng at Shen, Xin at You, Ren at Hai, Gui at Zi, Jia at Yin, Yi at Mao); the book does not explain this separately and it is understood here by context. The 'three sacrificial animals' are ox, sheep and pig (or pig, chicken and fish), 'caima' is paper money and paper horses, and 'jiao' is a Daoist rite. The book gives no reason in this passage why a given stem is matched with a given offering.
