# 鬼伤二间立方须用宽胸：官鬼动来冲克间爻，或鬼在间爻发动，胸膈必不利，须用宽胸汤药；间爻逢兄弟发动是气不顺，药宜调气

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动来冲克间爻，或者鬼在两个间爻上发动，胸膈一定不舒服，要用宽胸的汤药。间爻遇到兄弟发动，是气不顺（气逆），药宜调气。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Pressure moves to clash or control a between line, or moves on the two between lines, the chest and diaphragm are surely uneasy, and a decoction that eases the chest must be used. A between line meeting a moving Peer means the qi is not flowing smoothly (counterflow of qi), and the medicine should regulate qi.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说「气不顺」，王洪绪说「气逆」，意思相近。间爻是病人胸膈的位置，见 LY.ILL.HJC.22；官鬼克间爻胸膈不利见 LY.ILL.HJC.29。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says 'the qi not flowing smoothly' and Wang Hongxu 'counterflow of qi', which mean much the same. The between lines are the place of the patient's chest and diaphragm, see LY.ILL.HJC.22; Pressure controlling a between line and the chest being uneasy is in LY.ILL.HJC.29.
