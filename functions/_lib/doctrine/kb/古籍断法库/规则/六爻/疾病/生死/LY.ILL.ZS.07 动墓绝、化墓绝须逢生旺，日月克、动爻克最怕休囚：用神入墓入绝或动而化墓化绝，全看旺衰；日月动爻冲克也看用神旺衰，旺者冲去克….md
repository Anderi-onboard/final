# 动墓绝、化墓绝须逢生旺，日月克、动爻克最怕休囚：用神入墓入绝或动而化墓化绝，全看旺衰；日月动爻冲克也看用神旺衰，旺者冲去克神之日愈，衰者生助克神之日危

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神入墓、入绝，或者动了变入墓、变入绝，全看用神旺衰：旺的不必担心，衰的确实令人担忧。日月、动爻来冲克用神，也要看用神旺衰：旺相的，到冲走克神的那天就好；衰弱的，到生助克神的那天就危险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject that enters a tomb or extinction, or moves and changes into tomb or extinction, is judged wholly by whether it is strong or weak: a strong one need not be feared, a weak one is truly a cause for worry. When the Day, Month or a moving line clashes or controls the Subject, one also sees whether the Subject is strong or weak: if strong, it heals on the day the controlling spirit is clashed away; if weak, it is in danger on the day the controlling spirit is generated or helped.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条讲墓绝不是一见就死，要看用神本身旺不旺。和黄金策病体章的 LY.ILL.HJC.41、HJC.54（世鬼入墓、幽明两墓）相比，野鹤把墓绝的后果全交给旺衰去定。
>
> *Note:* This entry says that tomb and extinction do not mean death at first sight but depend on whether the Subject itself is strong. Compared with LY.ILL.HJC.41 and HJC.54 in the Huangjin Ce's chapter on the patient's condition (Pressure on the Self line entering the tomb, the dark and bright tombs), the Old Man of Yehe leaves the outcome of tomb and extinction wholly to strength and weakness.
