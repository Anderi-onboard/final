# 六神论神崇决：青龙爻动旧年佛愿催讨、朱雀爻兴游方五鬼梦中口愿、勾陈爻动土地家堂灶王时疫五瘟、螣蛇爻发病缠身东厨土地家亲、白虎爻兴神前愿佛前香、玄武爻兴病患凶家中人物不和

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六神论神崇决也是六首歌，讲六神的爻发动时，病是哪一路神鬼、该向谁祭祀还愿：青龙爻动，是往年的佛愿在催讨，赶紧告东厨和祖先；朱雀爻兴，是游方的五鬼、梦中的口愿；勾陈爻动，是土地、家堂、灶王，时疫、五瘟要求祭祀；螣蛇爻发，病缠身，是东厨、土地、家亲和天曹的口愿；白虎爻兴，是神前的愿、佛前的香；玄武爻兴，病患凶，家里人彼此不和，要靠北阴求土地。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verses on the six spirits and the secret of haunting spirits are also six songs, saying, when the line of each spirit moves, which kind of spirit or ghost the illness comes from and to whom to sacrifice or repay a vow: the Azure Dragon line moving, a Buddhist vow of past years is pressing for repayment, hurry to tell the Eastern Kitchen and the ancestors; the Vermilion Bird line rising, wandering Five Ghosts and a vow spoken in a dream; Hook Earth moving, the Earth God, household shrine and Hearth King, with epidemics and the Five Plagues calling for sacrifice; the Coiling Snake line moving, illness clings to the body, from the Eastern Kitchen, the Earth God, family kin and a vow spoken to the Celestial Bureau; the White Tiger line rising, a vow before the gods and incense before the Buddha; the Dark Tortoise line rising, illness is ominous, the people of the household are at odds, and one must rely on the North Yin to beseech the Earth God.

> [!note] 按
> 这是六首歌，每首四句，按六神讲爻发动时的病和该祭祀的对象。青龙一首的第二句「得病先占问祸原」点出了这一组的用处：先占出病因，再决定向谁还愿、向谁祭祀。「黄泉」是阴间；「东厨」是灶神；「五瘟」是五种瘟疫（五瘟使者）；「天曹」是天官的衙门；「北阴」「北殿」是北方阴间的神（北阴是北方玄武之阴，北殿是阎罗殿一类），书里没有解释，译文照字面；「正照傍临本命星」是（鬼）照在本命星上、或靠近本命星，照字面。和前一组六神论神鬼心决（LY.ILL.BSQS.44）是姊妹篇：那一组讲每位神配哪些神鬼，这一组讲每位神爻发动时应向谁祭祀。
>
> *Note:* These are six songs of four lines each that set out, by the six spirits, the illness when that spirit's line moves and the one to whom to sacrifice. The second line of the Azure Dragon song, 'when one is ill, first divine and ask the origin of the harm', states the purpose of this set: first divine the cause of the illness, then decide to whom to repay a vow and to whom to sacrifice. The 'Yellow Springs' is the underworld; the 'Eastern Kitchen' is the kitchen god; the 'Five Plagues' are five kinds of epidemic (the Five Plague Envoys); the 'Celestial Bureau' is the office of the heavenly officials; the 'North Yin' and the 'Northern Hall' are spirits of the north and the underworld (the yin of the northern Dark Tortoise, and a hall of the Yama kind), which the book does not explain, and the translation keeps to the letter; 'directly shining on and close beside the natal star' is the ghost shining on, or near, the natal star, to the letter. This set is a sister to the preceding verses on the heart-secret of spirits and ghosts (LY.ILL.BSQS.44): that set says which gods each spirit matches, and this one to whom to sacrifice when each spirit's line moves.
