# 医药·受病在此所治在彼（周杰）：金虎鬼冲伤用是肝受病然治肺不治肝，木虎鬼脾受病治肝，水虎鬼心受病治肾，火虎鬼肺受病治心，土虎鬼肾受病治脾；颗师：子孙动者误服药

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 周杰说：有的病是在这里受的，该治的却在那里。比如金的虎鬼冲伤用爻，是肝经受了病（金来克木），但应当治肺，不能治肝（这就是所说的去掉害我的那一个，其余照这个办法推）；木的虎鬼冲伤用爻，是脾经受病（木来伤土），该治肝，不能治脾；水的虎鬼冲伤用爻，是心经受病（水来伤火），该治肾，不能治心；火的虎鬼冲伤用爻，是肺经受病（火来伤金），该治心，不能治肺；土的虎鬼冲伤用爻，是肾经受病（土来伤水），该治脾，不能治肾（出自《松径玄谈》）。颗师说：子孙发动的，是吃错了药。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Zhou Jie says: in some cases the illness is received in one place while the treatment lies in another. For example, if a Metal Tiger ghost clashes with and wounds the Subject line, the liver channel has received the illness (Metal comes to control Wood), yet one may treat the lungs and must not treat the liver (this is what is meant by removing what harms me; the rest follow this); a Wood Tiger ghost wounding the Subject line: the spleen channel has received the illness (Wood comes to wound Earth), treat the liver, not the spleen; a Water Tiger ghost: the heart channel (Water wounds Fire), treat the kidneys, not the heart; a Fire Tiger ghost: the lung channel (Fire wounds Metal), treat the heart, not the lungs; an Earth Tiger ghost: the kidney channel (Earth wounds Water), treat the spleen, not the kidneys (from the Songjing Xuantan). Master Ke says: when Output moves, the wrong medicine has been taken.

> [!warning] 打架
> 子孙发动是好是坏：颗师说「子孙动者误服药」，子孙一动就是吃错了药，断例（剥之观）也照这个断，子孙发动冲克官爻、又变出巳绝，药不对症；黄金策、卜筮正宗、增删卜易都是另一个方向——子孙发动制鬼，是药能见效、甚至不药自愈（LY.ILL.HJC.32、LY.ILL.BSZZ.12、LY.ILL.ZS.30）。同一个爻象，两个相反的说法，书里没有裁决。周杰讲的「受病在此所治在彼」则是易隐这一组里独有的说法，别处没有。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether a moving Output line is good or bad: Master Ke says 'when Output moves, the wrong medicine has been taken', and the case (Bo into Guan) is judged the same way — Output moving to clash with and control Pressure and then changing into Si in Extinction, the medicine did not fit; the Huangjin Ce, the Bushi Zhengzong and the Zengshan Buyi go the other way — Output moving to restrain the ghost means the medicine will take effect, even that he recovers without medicine (LY.ILL.HJC.32, LY.ILL.BSZZ.12 and LY.ILL.ZS.30). The same line-image has two opposite sayings and the books do not decide. Zhou Jie's 'the illness is received here and the treatment lies there' is a saying peculiar to this group in the Yi Yin and is found nowhere else.

> [!note] 按
> 「虎鬼」是白虎带鬼。周杰讲的是一种间接的治法：病是某一行的鬼伤了用爻所在的那一脏引起的，治的却不是被伤的那一脏，而是「去掉那个害我的」，也就是鬼那一行自己所属的脏——金鬼伤木（肝），治肺；木鬼伤土（脾），治肝；水鬼伤火（心），治肾；火鬼伤金（肺），治心；土鬼伤水（肾），治脾。书里只给了这张对应表和一句「去其害我者，余仿此」，没有再解释。「颗师」「松径玄谈」都是易隐引的人名书名，书里没有再介绍。
>
> *Note:* The 'Tiger ghost' is the White Tiger carrying the ghost. Zhou Jie gives an indirect way of treatment: the illness is caused by a ghost of one element wounding the viscus that the Subject line stands for, yet what is treated is not the viscus wounded but 'what harms me is removed', that is, the viscus belonging to the ghost's own element — a Metal ghost wounding Wood (the liver), treat the lungs; a Wood ghost wounding Earth (the spleen), treat the liver; a Water ghost wounding Fire (the heart), treat the kidneys; a Fire ghost wounding Metal (the lungs), treat the heart; an Earth ghost wounding Water (the kidneys), treat the spleen. The book gives only this table and the phrase 'remove what harms me; the rest follow this' and no further explanation. 'Master Ke' and the 'Songjing Xuantan' are a person and a work the Yi Yin cites and the book does not introduce.
