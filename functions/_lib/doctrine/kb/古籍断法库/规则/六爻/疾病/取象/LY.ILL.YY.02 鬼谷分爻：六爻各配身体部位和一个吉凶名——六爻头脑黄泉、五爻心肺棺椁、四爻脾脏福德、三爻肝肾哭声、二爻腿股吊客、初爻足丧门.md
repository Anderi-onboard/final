# 鬼谷分爻：六爻各配身体部位和一个吉凶名——六爻头脑黄泉、五爻心肺棺椁、四爻脾脏福德、三爻肝肾哭声、二爻腿股吊客、初爻足丧门

六爻 · 疾病 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼谷把六爻这样分：六爻是头脑，配黄泉；五爻是心肺，配棺椁；四爻是脾脏，配福德；三爻是肝肾，配哭声；二爻是腿和大腿，配吊客；初爻是足，配丧门。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Guigu divides the six lines as follows: the sixth line is the head and brain, matched with the Yellow Springs; the fifth is the heart and lungs, with the Coffin; the fourth is the spleen, with Fortune; the third is the liver and kidneys, with Wailing; the second is the legs and thighs, with the Guest of Condolence; the first is the feet, with the Mourning Gate.

> [!note] 按
> 表是分两栏排的，书里没有说是从哪一爻排起。按后面「又分宫」一段（初爻是足，二爻股膝，三爻腹腰，四爻胸胃乳，五爻面项手，六爻头脑），头脑在六爻、足在初爻，可知表是从六爻往下排到初爻；右栏的黄泉、棺椁、福德、哭声、吊客、丧门书里没有解释，按字面译。「福德」在这里是这一爻配的名目，不是六亲里的子孙。
>
> *Note:* The table is set out in two columns and the book does not say which line it starts from. By the later passage 'again by the division of the lines' (first line the feet, second the thighs and knees, third the belly and waist, fourth the chest, stomach and breasts, fifth the face, neck and hands, sixth the head and brain), the head is on the sixth line and the feet on the first, so the table runs from the sixth line down to the first; the right column — Yellow Springs, Coffin, Fortune, Wailing, Guest of Condolence, Mourning Gate — is not explained in the book and is rendered literally. 'Fortune' (fude) here is the name matched to that line and is not the Output line of the six relations.
