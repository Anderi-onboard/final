# 逢冲暗动·占病：六爻安静不遇冲则不动、日辰相冲名曰暗动；凶煞暗动伤身克世百事不佳、吉神暗动合世生身事事吉；占病遇之吉则病人有默佑、凶则脉阴隙所致、病人不病须防暗有伤

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻安静，不遇到冲就不动；如果日辰来冲它，叫作暗动。暗动有吉有凶，各有各的用处，不可以一概而论：凶煞暗动、伤身克世，百事都不好；吉神暗动、合世生身，事事都吉。我推究它的意思：暗的一定不是明的，是阴私、潜藏的样子，福来了却不知道，祸来了却没有察觉；爻吉，暗中就有补益，爻凶，暗中就有伤害。占病遇到它：吉，就说病人有暗中的保佑；凶，就说是脉络里暗中的空隙造成的；病人若还没有发病，也要防暗中有伤。这一章宜细细玩味，就万无一失。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the six lines are still, they do not move unless they meet a clash; if the Day clashes with one, it is called a hidden movement. A hidden movement has luck and ill, each with its own use, and it cannot be judged all alike: a fierce killing moving hidden, injuring the Body and controlling the Self, is not good in all things; a lucky spirit moving hidden, combining with the Self and generating the Body, is lucky in everything. I work out its sense: what is hidden is surely not open, it is of the private and the concealed, fortune comes and one does not know it, calamity comes and one does not notice it; if the line is lucky there is help in secret, and if ominous there is injury in secret. Divining illness and meeting it: if lucky it is said the sick person has a silent protection; if ominous it is said to be caused by a hidden gap in the pulse; and if the sick person is not yet sick one must guard against a hidden injury. This chapter should be studied closely, and nothing will fail.

> [!note] 按
> 「暗动」是静爻遇日辰冲而动（见增删卜易的「暗动」）。这一章占婚、占产、占官、占讼、失脱、求财、出行、行人、家宅各有一句，这一条收占病那一句。「凶则言脉阴隙所致也」的「脉阴隙」意思读不确定，「阴隙」是暗中的缝隙，这里按「脉络里暗中的空隙」字面译；「病人不病须防暗有伤」的「病人不病」按「病人还没有发病」译，原文没有解释。「吉则言病人有默佑」的「默佑」是暗中的神佑。这一节和「合处逢冲」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.26）是冲在病里的两面。「（暗动）遇凶煞伤身克世」的「身」是卦身。
>
> *Note:* A 'hidden movement' is a still line moving on meeting a clash from the Day (see the Zengshan Buyi on hidden movement). This chapter has one sentence each for marriage, childbirth, office, lawsuit, losses, wealth, travel, a traveller and the house, and this entry takes the sentence on illness. The 'mai yin xi' of 'if ominous it is said to be caused by a hidden gap in the pulse' is not certain in sense; 'yin xi' is a hidden crack, and it is rendered literally as 'a hidden gap in the pulse'; 'the sick person not sick' of 'the sick person not sick must guard against a hidden injury' is rendered 'the sick person is not yet sick', and the original does not explain it. The 'silent protection' of 'if lucky it is said the sick person has a silent protection' is protection by a spirit in secret. This section and 'a clash where there is a combination' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.26) are the two sides of a clash in illness. The 'Body' of 'injuring the Body and controlling the Self' is the hexagram Body.
