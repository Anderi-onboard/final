# 住居宅第章·杀爻旺相官灾起、杀遇休囚疾病生（旧注：旺主官非，衰主疾病）

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占家宅的歌里说：杀爻旺相，官灾就起；杀爻遇到休囚，疾病就生。旧注：旺的杀主官非，衰的杀主疾病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on dwellings says: when the killing line is strong, official calamity arises; when the killing line meets rest or confinement, illness arises. The old note: a strong killing spirit brings official trouble, a weak one brings illness.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3492 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 旺主官非， | 旺的杀主官非， | A strong one brings official trouble, |
> | 衰主疾病。 | 衰的杀主疾病。 | a weak one brings illness. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这是占家宅（住居宅第章第二首）里的一联。歌里的「杀」，歌前面一句的旧注说是勾陈杀（鬼谷起勾陈杀例：正月起辰、二月卯，每月逆行一位；如正月占宅，辰爻动，就是杀旺，主官灾起），歌本身没有明说，译文照字面写「杀爻」。断易天机把勾陈杀列为占病忌发动的四杀之一（LY.ILL.DYTJ.33）。
>
> *Note:* This is a couplet in the verse on dwellings (the second poem of the chapter on dwellings and houses). The 'killing' line here is, according to the old note on the previous line, the Hook Earth killing (the Guigu method: begin at Chen in the first month and Mao in the second, moving back one place each month; if a house is divined in the first month and the Chen line moves, the killing is strong and official calamity arises), but the verse itself does not say so, and the translation keeps the literal 'killing line'. The Duanyi Tianji lists the Hook Earth killing among the four killings to avoid when divining illness (LY.ILL.DYTJ.33).
