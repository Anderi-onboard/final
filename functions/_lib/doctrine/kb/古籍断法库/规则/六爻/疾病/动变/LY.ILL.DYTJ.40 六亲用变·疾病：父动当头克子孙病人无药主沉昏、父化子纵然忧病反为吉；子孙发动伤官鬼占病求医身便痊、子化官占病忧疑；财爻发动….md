# 六亲用变·疾病：父动当头克子孙病人无药主沉昏、父化子纵然忧病反为吉；子孙发动伤官鬼占病求医身便痊、子化官占病忧疑；财爻发动病人伤脾腹胃虚；兄弟交重克财病人难愈、兄化官占病难医须见哭；官化官病未安、官鬼病困门庭祸祟来

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母动而克子孙，病人没有药，主沉重昏迷；父母化子孙，宜退散，纵然忧病，反而是吉。子孙发动、伤克官鬼，占病求医，身体就痊愈；子孙化官鬼，要防祸患，占病的忧疑都不准。财爻发动，病人伤脾胃、腹胃虚。兄弟交重克财，病人难愈、没有离灾；兄化官，不要下状，占病难医、须见哭。官鬼发动：病困在门庭、祸祟来；官化官，病未安。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line moving and controlling Output: the sick person has no medicine, and it means heaviness and stupor; Resource changing into Output: it is fit to retreat and scatter, and though there is worry about illness, it is on the contrary lucky. Output moving and injuring the Pressure line: in divining illness and seeking a doctor, the body is cured at once; Output changing into the Pressure line: guard against calamity, and the worries about illness are all off the mark. The Wealth line moving: the sick person injures the spleen and the belly and stomach are deficient. Peer crossing heavily and controlling Wealth: the sick person is hard to cure and has not left the calamity; Peer changing into the Pressure line: do not bring a suit, and divining illness it is hard to treat and weeping will be seen. The Pressure line moving: the illness is trapped at the gate, curses of calamity come; the Pressure line changing into the Pressure line: the illness is not yet at ease.

> [!note] 按
> 「用变」是每一类六亲（父母、子孙、妻财、兄弟、官鬼）发动、以及它们变成别的六亲时的断语，每一类有两首歌，这里只收说到病的句子。「父动当头克子孙，病人无药主沉昏」是父母爻发动克子孙（药）：没有药，病人昏沉；「子孙发动伤官鬼，占病求医身便痊」是子孙克鬼：治病有效。「财爻发动……病人伤脾腹胃虚」的「腹胃虚」原文如此，「财」（土）克水，所以「伤脾」这里按字面译，不作解释。「兄弟交重克了财，病人难愈未离灾」是兄弟克财（禄命），病人难愈。「兄化官兮休下状，占病难医须见哭」是兄弟化官鬼。「官鬼发动，病困门庭，祸祟来」是官鬼动：病困在门庭里。这些句子多与前面几章（孙膑歌的六亲、家人占病歌 LY.ILL.DYTJ.05、DYTJ.08）是同一个意思。父母、子孙的用变在求财里也有（LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.32 一带）。
>
> *Note:* 'Uses by change' are the judgments when each kind of relation (Resource, Output, Wealth, Peer, Pressure) moves and when it changes into another relation, with two songs for each kind, and here only the sentences that speak of illness are taken. 'The Resource line moving and controlling Output head on: the sick person has no medicine, heaviness and stupor' is Resource moving and controlling Output (the medicine): with no medicine the sick person is in a stupor; 'Output moving and injuring the Pressure line: in divining illness and seeking a doctor the body is cured at once' is Output controlling the ghost: the treatment is effective. In 'the Wealth line moving ... the sick person injures the spleen and the belly and stomach are deficient' the 'belly and stomach are deficient' is as in the original, and it is rendered literally with no explanation. 'Peer crossing heavily and controlling Wealth: the sick person is hard to cure and has not left the calamity' is Peer controlling Wealth (salary and life): the sick person is hard to cure. 'Peer changing into the Pressure line: do not bring a suit, divining illness it is hard to treat and weeping will be seen' is Peer changing into the Pressure line. 'The Pressure line moving, the illness trapped at the gate, curses of calamity come' is the Pressure line moving: the illness is trapped in the gate and courtyard. These sentences mostly agree with the earlier chapters (the six relations in Sun Bin's song and the verses on a family member, LY.ILL.DYTJ.05 and DYTJ.08). The uses by change of Resource and Output are also in the wealth domain (around LY.WEALTH.DYTJ.32).
