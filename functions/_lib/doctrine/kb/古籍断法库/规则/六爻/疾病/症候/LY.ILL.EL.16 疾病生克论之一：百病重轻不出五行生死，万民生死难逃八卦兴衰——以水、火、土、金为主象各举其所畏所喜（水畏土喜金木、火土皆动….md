# 疾病生克论之一：百病重轻不出五行生死，万民生死难逃八卦兴衰——以水、火、土、金为主象各举其所畏所喜（水畏土喜金木、火土皆动见木反凶，土为用神水木火三动当从吉断……），查何日逢金便决何时作殡

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 百病的轻重，不出五行的生死；万民的生死，逃不出八卦的兴衰。注举了用神为水、为金、为土、为火的几种情形，一一说明畏什么、喜什么：水是主象，怕土，也怕金以外的受制；卦里土静火兴，不必畏忌；爻内火安静、土动，却不吉祥；怕遇上巳、午两个时辰，喜遇上木、金；又如火土都动，见木反而凶，得到酉、申来助水，这病才会痊愈。假如金藏在土下，飞神能生伏神就好；木爻安静，日辰忌卯、寅，木象发动，时辰忧亥、子，这就是所说的遇金则吉、遇水则凶。再拿土作用神，卦里见水、木、火三个爻同时发动，不当作凶来推，应当按吉断；遇午、申的时候，土得到生扶就吉，逢卯、巳的日子，土临死绝就被囚。又论火是用爻，只有水发动的日子遇上戌、辰、丑、未，水被土克无妨；一见酉、申就命尽，只求寅、卯就身安。如果用爻居于衰位、木化入金乡，卦里没有亥、子和父爻兴旺，一定难以救度，查哪一天逢金，就断哪个时候办丧事；倘若金空，就不这样断，节气产到亥、子，命也会回生，细究哪个爻动静，就知道哪天存亡。其中有贪生忘克、救处遭伤，盈虚要精细详审，怎么可以寻常一概而论？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The lightness or heaviness of a hundred illnesses does not go beyond the birth and death of the five phases; the life and death of the people of ten thousand houses cannot escape the rise and fall of the eight trigrams. The commentary gives cases with the Subject as Water, Metal, Earth and Fire, and says of each what it fears and what it welcomes: with Water as the chief image, it fears Earth; if in the hexagram Earth is still and Fire thriving there is no need to fear it; if in the lines Fire is still and Earth moves, it is not auspicious; it fears meeting the hours Si and Wu, and is glad to meet Wood and Metal; again, if Fire and Earth both move, meeting Wood is on the contrary ominous, and when You and Shen come to help Water the illness will be cured. If Metal lies hidden under Earth, it is good if the flying line can generate the hidden one; if the Wood line is still, the Mao and Yin days are to be feared, and if the Wood image moves, the Hai and Zi hours are a worry: this is what is meant by 'meeting Metal is auspicious, meeting Water ominous'. Taking Earth again as the Subject, if the hexagram shows Water, Wood and Fire lines all three moving, do not judge it ominous but by the auspicious; at the hours Wu and Shen Earth gains generation and support and it is auspicious, and on Mao and Si days Earth on death or Extinction is imprisoned. Again, with Fire as the Subject line, when only Water moves on a day that meets Xu, Chen, Chou or Wei, Water is controlled by Earth and it does not matter; at the first sight of You or Shen life is at an end, and only if one hopes for Yin or Mao is the body safe. If the Subject line sits in a weak place and Wood changes into Metal's country, and the hexagram has no Hai or Zi or a thriving Resource line, rescue is hard, and to find on what day it meets Metal is to decide at what time the coffin is made; if Metal is Void, it is not judged so, and when the solar term reaches Hai and Zi life is restored, and if one examines which line moves and which is still, one knows on which day one lives or dies. Among these are those who, greedy for generation, forget control, or are harmed at the place of rescue, and fullness and emptiness must be examined finely: how can they be judged by the common general rule?

> [!note] 按
> 这一段是全部五行—地支推算的例子，密度很高，原文有几处文字可疑：头一句「畏土纵金」的「纵」（放过、让金存在）按上下文译作「让金」；「疑此病以方痊」的「疑」按字面译；第五句的「假令金藏土下，飞能生伏为佳」是说金是用神、藏在土爻之下，土（飞）生金（伏）则好；「用居衰木化入金乡」是用神为木，木变入金地（被金克）；「卦无亥子父兴」的「父」是父爻；「查何日逢金，便决何时作殡」是说用神为木时，金克木，遇金的日子就是死期；这段的含意是逐个五行说清「怕什么、喜什么」，不是要背下每个时辰。「节产亥子」的「产」疑是「交」之误（节气交到亥、子），读法没有把握。
>
> *Note:* This passage is a run of cases reckoned by the five phases and the branches, and dense; a few readings in the original are doubtful: in the first line, 'fears Earth and lets Metal (zong jin)' is rendered from the context as 'lets Metal be'; 'suspects this illness will then be cured' is translated literally; in 'if Metal hides under Earth, it is good if the flying line can generate the hidden one' Metal is the Subject hidden under an Earth line, and Earth (flying) generating Metal (hidden) is good; 'the Subject sits in a weak place and Wood changes into Metal's country' means the Subject is Wood and it changes into a Metal place (controlled by Metal); the 'father' in 'no Hai or Zi or a thriving father' is the Resource line; 'to find on what day it meets Metal is to decide at what time the coffin is made' means that when the Subject is Wood, Metal controls Wood and the day it meets Metal is the day of death; the sense of the passage is to say for each phase in turn 'what it fears and what it welcomes', not to memorise every hour. The 'chan' in 'when the solar term reaches (chan) Hai and Zi' looks like an error for 'jiao' (arrives), and the reading is uncertain.
