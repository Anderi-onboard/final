# 总断千金赋·无故勿空（旧注：身世主象无故自落空亡大凶，占病必死；王洪绪：「故」是受伤，无故之空不可当真空，旧注误解）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说「无故勿空」四个字，两家读法相反。旧注：旬里的空亡，有有缘故而空的、有没有缘故而空的；被日、月、动爻伤克而落在空亡，叫有缘故而空，避开它是可以的；如果没有刑冲克害、身世主象自己落了空亡，叫没有缘故而空，是大凶的兆头：占病必死，占事不成，占人有难，因为空了，即使有日辰、动爻也难以扶持救援。王洪绪：「故」是受伤的意思，「勿」要当「不」讲；旬空的爻若安静又被月建日辰克制，是有伤的空，到底是无用的空；若是没有被月建日辰克伤，或者发动、或得月日动爻生扶拱合冲起，这是无故之空，出旬、值日、得合的时候仍然能办事，不可当真空论；旧注把意思弄反了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements has the four characters 'wu gu wu kong', which the two commentators read in opposite ways. The old commentary: of Void within the ten-day cycle some is Void with a cause and some Void without cause; a line hurt or controlled by the Day, the Month or a moving line and in Void is Void with a cause, and avoiding the harm in this way is acceptable; if there is no punishment, clash, control or harm and the Body, Self or principal image falls into Void by itself, it is Void without cause, a greatly ominous omen: in divining illness the sick will surely die, an affair does not come to completion, a person is in trouble, for when it is Void, even the Day and moving lines can hardly support or rescue. Wang Hongxu: 'cause' means being hurt, and 'do not' must be read as 'not'; a line in the ten-day Void that is still and controlled by the Month and Day is Void with injury, in the end a useless Void; one that has not been hurt by the Month or Day, or that moves, or is supported, flanked, combined or clashed into motion by the Month, Day or a moving line, is Void without cause, and when it comes out of the cycle, when the Day falls on it, or when it gets a combination, it can still do its work, and is not to be judged as truly Void; the old commentary has the sense backwards.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「无故勿空」四个字怎么读，旧注和王洪绪相反。旧注：没有被刑冲克害、身世主象自己落空的，是「无故而空」，是大凶，占病必死；被日月动爻伤克而落空的，是「有故而空」，是避凶，可以。王洪绪：「故」是受伤的意思，没有被月建日辰克伤的旬空，才是「无故之空」，它不能当真空论，出旬、值日、得合时仍然有用；被克伤的旬空才是无用的空；他明说旧注把意思弄反了。书里没有裁决，也就是说：一个不受伤、自己落空的主象，旧注断凶（占病必死），王洪绪断不算真空。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The old commentary and Wang Hongxu read the four characters 'wu gu wu kong' in opposite ways. The old commentary: a principal image that is not hurt by punishment, clash, control or harm and falls Void by itself is 'Void without cause', greatly ominous, and in divining illness the sick will surely die; one hurt or controlled by the Day, the Month or a moving line and then falling Void is 'Void with a cause', which is avoiding ill, and acceptable. Wang Hongxu: 'cause' means being hurt; only a ten-day Void not hurt by the Month and Day is 'Void without cause', which is not to be judged as truly Void and is still useful when it comes out of the cycle, when the Day falls on it or when it gets a combination; it is the ten-day Void that is hurt that is the useless Void; and he says outright that the old commentary has the sense backwards. The books do not decide: a principal image not hurt and Void by itself the old commentary judges ominous (in illness, death), and Wang Hongxu judges it not truly Void.

> [!note] 按
> 原文只有「无故勿空」四个字，没有标点以外的说明，所以两家的读法都说得通：旧注读成「没有缘故的空要不得」，王洪绪读成「没有缘故（没受伤）的空不算空」。经文翻成「Without cause， do not be Void」是照字面，保持两种读法都成立。「真空」「避凶而空」是旧注在紧接的「卦遇凶星，避之则吉」里的用语（LY.ILL.HJC.156）。「先天」指易的本来道理。「旬空」是起卦的那一旬里轮空的两个地支；三合局在王洪绪这里是来克的一方（会局克用神）。本库在疾病域收这一条，因为旧注给了「占病必死」这个断语；和天玄赋、易隐等讲「用空」的几条（LY.ILL.EL.11、ZS.04、ZS.05）互相照应。
>
> *Note:* The text has only the four characters 'wu gu wu kong' with no explanation, so both readings are possible: the old commentary reads it as 'Void without cause is not to be had', Wang Hongxu as 'Void without cause (not hurt) does not count as Void'. The line is translated literally as 'Without cause, do not be Void', so that both readings stand. 'True Void' and 'Void to avoid ill' are terms of the old commentary in the immediately following 'a hexagram that meets an ominous star: avoiding it is auspicious' (LY.ILL.HJC.156). 'The Former Heaven' means the original principle of the Changes. 'The ten-day Void' is the two branches left out of the ten-day cycle of the casting; in Wang Hongxu a three-harmony frame is the side that controls (a combined frame controlling the Subject). It is taken in the illness domain because the old commentary gives the verdict 'in divining illness the sick will surely die'; it answers the several entries that speak of the Subject Void (LY.ILL.EL.11, ZS.04, ZS.05).
