# 火珠林(断易天机引)·疾病财爻鬼莫连：二爻俱发命危难；同人丰夬蛊剥明夷是死缘；杀世鬼身三入墓人将魂魄入黄泉；药爻在世难医疗；虎动占身病未痊；卦入墓乡并丧命

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占疾病，财爻和鬼不要连在一起，两爻都发动，命有危难；同人、丰、夬成了凶的一对，蛊、剥、明夷是死的缘由。杀、世、鬼、身，三样入了墓，人的魂魄就要入黄泉；药爻在世，还是难医；虎动、占住身，病没有痊愈；卦入墓乡，并且丧命，卧在床席上，病绵绵不断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In illness the Wealth line and the ghost must not be linked, and if both move life is in danger; Tongren, Feng and Guai make an ominous set, and Gu, Bo and Mingyi are causes of death. The killing, the Self, the ghost and the Body, three of them entering the tomb, the person's spirit-souls are about to enter the Yellow Springs; the medicine line on the Self, hard to treat; the Tiger moving and taking hold of the Body, the illness is not cured; the hexagram entering the tomb quarter, life is lost, and lying on the bed-mat the illness drags on and on.

> [!note] 按
> 这一首在断易天机里题作《火珠林》，和火珠林本书的「占疾病」（LY.ILL.HZL.01 起）是不同的文字。「杀世鬼身三入墓」读不确定：是杀、世、鬼、身三样入墓，还是别的断法，原文没有点明，这里按字面译。「药爻在世难医疗」按字面译，上一句已经说入墓，这一句大概是说即使药爻（子孙）持世也难医，是读法，原文没有「即使」二字。这一首的凶卦名单是同人、丰、夬、蛊、剥、明夷，和别处的名单大同小异，见 LY.ILL.CA.03 的「打架」。
>
> *Note:* This poem is headed 'Huozhu Lin' in the Duanyi Tianji, and it is a different text from the 'divining illness' of the Huozhu Lin itself (from LY.ILL.HZL.01). 'Sha shi gui shen san ru mu' is not certain: whether it is the killing, the Self, the ghost and the Body, three of them entering the tomb, or some other way of judging, the original does not make plain, and it is rendered literally. 'The medicine line on the Self, hard to treat' is rendered literally; the line before has already said entering the tomb, and this line probably says that even with the medicine line (Output) holding the Self it is hard to treat, which is a reading, and the original has no word for 'even'. The list of ominous hexagrams of this poem is Tongren, Feng, Guai, Gu, Bo and Mingyi, much like the lists elsewhere, see the dispute in LY.ILL.CA.03.
