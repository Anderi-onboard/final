# 福化忌爻误服杀身之恶剂：动则有变，变出父母回头来克、变出财爻扶持官鬼、变出兄弟药不精洁、变出官杀药反助病、子变子用药太杂；不如安静，变爻伤世克用必因药伤命

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有动就有变。子孙动了，变出父母就回头来克它，变出财爻就去扶持官鬼，变出兄弟药不精洁，变出官鬼这个凶煞药反而助长病，子孙变子孙是用药太杂，不能见效。所以不如安静；如果变出的爻伤了世爻、克了用爻，一定会招来因为吃药而伤命的祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where there is movement there is change. If Output moves and changes out Resource, it turns back to control it; out Wealth, it supports Pressure; out Peer, the medicine is not refined or clean; out a Pressure killer, the medicine feeds the illness instead; Output changing into Output is medicine too mixed to work. So stillness is better; if the changed line harms the Self line or controls the Subject line, disaster through medicine, harm to life, is sure to follow.

> | 盖有动则有变，变出父母回头来克，难伤官鬼，必致用药伤命。 | 因为有动就有变，变出父母回头来克它，伤不了官鬼，一定会因为用药而伤命。 | For where there is movement there is change; changing out Resource that turns back to control it, it cannot harm Pressure, and harm to life through medicine is sure to follow. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说的《海底眼》「误服药」一句，在本库收的《海底眼》里查不到；《易隐》里有一句意思相近的话（颗师曰：「子孙动者误服药」）。王洪绪只收了变出父母这一种，旧注列了父母、财、兄弟、官杀、子孙五种。
>
> *Note:* The Haidi Yan line on wrongly taking medicine that the old commentary mentions is not found in the Haidi Yan collected in this library; the Yiyin has a saying close in meaning (Master Ke says: 'when Output moves, one takes the wrong medicine'). Wang Hongxu takes only the case of changing out Resource, where the old commentary lists five: Resource, Wealth, Peer, the Pressure killer and Output.
