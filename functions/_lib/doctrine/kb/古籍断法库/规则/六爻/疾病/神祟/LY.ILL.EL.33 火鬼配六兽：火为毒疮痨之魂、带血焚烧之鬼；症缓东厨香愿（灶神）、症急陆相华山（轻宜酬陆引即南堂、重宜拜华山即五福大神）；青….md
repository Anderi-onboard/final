# 火鬼配六兽：火为毒疮痨之魂、带血焚烧之鬼；症缓东厨香愿（灶神）、症急陆相华山（轻宜酬陆引即南堂、重宜拜华山即五福大神）；青龙五福、勾陈螣蛇三煞土、白虎赵玄坛与痴癫鬼、玄武南堂水神、朱雀华光司命（五显灵官、灶神）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 火鬼，是毒疮、痨病的魂，带血、焚烧的鬼：症状缓的，是东厨、香愿；症状急的，是陆相、华山。青龙，冒犯了五福星；勾陈、螣蛇，动了三煞的土；白虎，是玄坛再加上横死的鬼；玄武，是南堂和水神一起；朱雀，是华光、司命，并且酬还口愿才安宁。注说：火鬼，主疮毒鬼、痨病鬼、带血鬼、烧死的鬼、心疼的鬼；症状缓的，宜酬谢灶神和香愿；症状急的，也有轻重之分：轻病宜酬陆引，陆相就是南堂；重病宜拜华山，华山就是五福大神；带青龙也是五福；带勾陈或螣蛇都是三煞土；带白虎是赵玄坛和痴癫鬼；带玄武是南堂和水神；带朱雀是华光，华光就是五显灵官，又是司命，就是灶神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fire is the soul of poisonous sores and consumption, the ghost of bleeding and of burning: if mild, the Eastern Kitchen and the incense vow; if severe, Minister Lu and Huashan. The Azure Dragon offends the Five Blessings star; Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake move the earth of the Three Killings; the White Tiger, the Dark Altar, and a violent-death ghost added; the Dark Tortoise, the Southern Hall and the water spirit together; the Vermilion Bird, Huaguang and the Director of Fate, and the spoken vow redeemed, then there is peace. The commentary says: a Fire ghost governs ghosts of sores and poison, ghosts of consumption, ghosts of bleeding, ghosts of burning to death and ghosts of heart pain; if the symptoms are mild, thank the Kitchen God and the incense vow; if urgent, there are still degrees: a light illness suits an offering to Lu Yin, Minister Lu being the Southern Hall; a heavy illness suits worship at Huashan, Huashan being the Great God of the Five Blessings; with the Azure Dragon it is also the Five Blessings; with Hook Earth or the Coiling Snake it is the Three Killings Earth; with the White Tiger it is Zhao Xuantan and a ghost of madness; with the Dark Tortoise it is the Southern Hall and a water spirit; with the Vermilion Bird it is Huaguang, and Huaguang is the Five Manifest Spirit Officials and also the Director of Fate, who is the Kitchen God.

> [!note] 按
> 歌诀的「陈蛇」是勾陈和螣蛇。「玄坛」「赵玄坛」是同一位（玄坛，财神一类），歌诀里的「元坛」是避讳写法；「陆引」「陆相」「南堂」被解说等同起来，「华山」「五福大神」也被等同，「华光」「五显灵官」「司命」「灶神」被解说连成一串。这几条等同都是书里自己说的，这里照译，没有另外核对。解说里「轻病」与「重病」的分法，是火鬼一节独有的：症急的再分轻重。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地（苕溪一带）的民间神祇和星煞，书里一个也没有解释（章末自己说别的郡的神司不同），这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* 'Chen-she' in the verse is Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake. 'Xuantan' and 'Zhao Xuantan' are one figure (the Dark Altar, of the wealth-god kind), and the 'Yuan Altar' in the verse is the taboo-avoiding form; 'Lu Yin', 'Minister Lu' and 'the Southern Hall' are identified with one another in the explanation, 'Huashan' and 'the Great God of the Five Blessings' are identified, and 'Huaguang', 'the Five Manifest Spirit Officials', 'the Director of Fate' and 'the Kitchen God' are strung together by the explanation. These identifications are the book's own and are translated as they stand, without independent checking. The division of 'light illness' and 'heavy illness' in the explanation is peculiar to the section on Fire ghosts: an urgent illness is divided again by weight. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain (the book itself says at the end of the chapter that the gods of other prefectures differ), and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
