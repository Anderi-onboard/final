# 欲定症候须看鬼临之五行：金鬼肺腑病咳嗽气喘或疮疖血光筋骨疼痛；木鬼四肢不利、风气肝胆、左瘫右痪口眼歪斜、头疼发热三焦口渴，加朱雀狂言乱语阳症伤寒；水鬼沉寒痼冷遗精白浊淋沥吐泻呕逆；火鬼寒热往来脾寒疟疾；土鬼脾胃发瘴水肿虚浮瘟疫时气；前卦火鬼后卦水鬼则服冷药过多变阴症

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要定症候，必须看鬼临着的五行。金鬼：肺腑的病，咳嗽气喘，或者疮疖、血光，或者筋骨疼痛。木鬼：四肢不利，风气、肝胆病，左瘫右痪，口眼歪斜，头疼发热，三焦口渴；加朱雀，说胡话、乱语，是阳症伤寒。水鬼：沉寒痼冷，遗精白浊，小便淋沥，吐泻呕逆。火鬼：寒热往来，脾寒疟疾。土鬼：脾胃发瘴，水肿虚浮，瘟疫时气。如果前一卦是火鬼、后一卦是水鬼，前面的症候一定是阳症，服冷药过多，变成了阴症；如果午时以前占，遇到这两个鬼，应当说不要服药；过了头，以后必有变化。这说的是在火旺的月日占卦的情形。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To fix the symptoms, one must look at the phase the ghost sits on. A Metal ghost: illness of the lungs and viscera, coughing and wheezing, or sores and boils, or bleeding, or aching of the sinews and bones. A Wood ghost: the four limbs not free, wind-qi and illness of the liver and gall, left and right palsy, mouth and eyes awry, headache and fever, thirst in the Triple Burner; with the Vermilion Bird added, wild and confused speech, a yang cold-damage illness. A Water ghost: deep cold and chronic chill, seminal emission and turbid urine, dribbling urination, vomiting, diarrhoea and retching. A Fire ghost: alternate chills and fever, spleen chill and malaria. An Earth ghost: spleen and stomach miasma, water swelling and puffy weakness, epidemic and seasonal qi. If the earlier hexagram is a Fire ghost and the later a Water ghost, the earlier symptoms were surely yang, and taking too much cold medicine has turned them into a yin condition; if the divination is made before noon and these two ghosts are met, one should say do not take medicine; if it is overdone, there will surely be a change afterwards. This speaks of divining in a month or day when Fire is strong.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段和黄金策疾病章里的「五行症候」（LY.ILL.HJC.14 到 HJC.20 一带）、易隐「五行鬼各主之症」（LY.ILL.YY.13）是同一份名单，每种五行的具体病名略有不同：天玄赋的金鬼是「肺腑病、咳嗽气、疮疖、血光、筋骨疼痛」，木鬼里有「三焦口渴」和「加朱雀狂言乱语阳症伤寒」，火鬼只有「寒热往来、脾寒疟疾」（易隐的火鬼阴是心目之疾、阳是疮痍疖毒虐热），土鬼是「脾胃发瘴、水肿虚浮、瘟疫时气」。「遗精白浊」「淋沥」「痼冷」「脾寒疟疾」是医病名，照字面译；「三焦」是中医说的上中下三焦，不作解释。「前卦火鬼、后卦水鬼」说的是同一个人先后占了两卦：前面是火鬼阳症，若已服了太多冷药，后一卦水鬼就是变成了阴症。「若午前占，遇此两鬼，当言勿服药」的「午前」是午时以前；末一句说火鬼那一条指的是在火旺的月日占卦。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* This passage is the same list as the 'symptoms by the five phases' in the Huangjin Ce's chapter on illness (LY.ILL.HJC.14 to HJC.20) and the Yi Yin's 'the symptoms each phase of ghost governs' (LY.ILL.YY.13), with the specific illness names differing a little for each phase: in the Tianxuan Fu the Metal ghost is 'illness of the lungs and viscera, coughing and wheezing, sores and boils, bleeding, aching sinews and bones'; the Wood ghost has 'thirst in the Triple Burner' and 'with the Vermilion Bird added, wild and confused speech, a yang cold-damage illness'; the Fire ghost has only 'alternate chills and fever, spleen chill and malaria' (the Yi Yin's Fire ghost is, if yin, illness of the heart and eyes, and if yang, sores, boils and hot malaria); and the Earth ghost is 'spleen and stomach miasma, water swelling and puffy weakness, epidemic and seasonal qi'. 'Seminal emission and turbid urine', 'dribbling urination', 'chronic chill' and 'spleen chill and malaria' are names of illnesses and are translated literally; the 'Triple Burner' is the upper, middle and lower burners of Chinese medicine and is not explained. 'The earlier hexagram a Fire ghost and the later a Water ghost' speaks of one person divining two hexagrams in succession: the earlier is a yang condition with the Fire ghost, and if too much cold medicine has already been taken, the Water ghost of the later hexagram is the change to a yin condition. 'If divined before noon and these two ghosts are met, say do not take medicine': the 'before noon' is before the Wu double-hour; the last sentence says that the Fire ghost's case refers to divining in a month or day when Fire is strong. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
