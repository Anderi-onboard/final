# 应爻带官鬼刑克合世：是探望亲友的病时缠染上的，鬼属土是时疫，用爻临应则病卧他家

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻上坐着官鬼，来刑、克、合世爻（王洪绪说是用爻），一定是因为探望亲友的病而被缠染上。鬼爻属土的，是流行的时疫。用爻临在应爻上，一定病倒在别人家里。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a Pressure line sits on the Other line and punishes, controls or combines with the Self line (Wang Hongxu says the Subject line), the illness was surely caught by entanglement through visiting a sick relative or friend. If the Pressure line is Earth, it is an epidemic of the season. If the Subject line sits on the Other line, the patient surely lies ill in someone else's house.

## 《黄金策·疾病》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6846 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应鬼合身，缠染他人之症； | 应爻上的鬼合住了本人，是缠染了别人的病； | A Pressure line on the Other line combining with the person: the illness is caught by entanglement from someone else; |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2345 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 应临官鬼刑克合用爻，必因探访亲友病而缠染也。 | 应爻上坐着官鬼，刑、克、合用爻，一定是因为探望亲友的病而缠染上。 | When a Pressure line sits on the Other line and punishes, controls or combines with the Subject line, the illness was surely caught by entanglement through visiting a sick relative or friend. |
> | 鬼爻属土，是时行瘟症； | 鬼爻属土，是流行的瘟病； | if the Pressure line is Earth, it is a pestilence going the rounds; |
> | 用爻临应，必然病卧他家。 | 用爻临在应爻上，一定病倒在别人家里。 | if the Subject line sits on the Other line, the patient surely lies ill in another's house. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 被应鬼刑克合的是哪一爻：旧注说是世爻，而且世爻要在发动的位置上；王洪绪说是用爻，没有发动这个条件。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line the Pressure on the Other line punishes, controls or combines with: the old commentary says the Self line, which must also be in a moving position; Wang Hongxu says the Subject line, without the condition of moving.

> [!note] 按
> 「合身」的「身」是本人，即下面说的世爻或用爻。「缠染」是被缠上、传染上。「时行瘟症」是当令流行的瘟病。
>
> *Note:* The 'body' of 'combining the body' is the person himself, that is, the Self or Subject line spoken of below. 'Entangled and infected' (chanran) means being caught and infected. 'Shixing wenzheng' is a pestilence going the rounds in the season.
