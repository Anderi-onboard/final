# 六亲爻动·疾病：父动克子孙，病人无药主沉昏(子能医药)；子孙发动伤官鬼，占病求医身便痊(鬼制病安)；妻财发动，病者伤脾并胃虚(财为饮食)；兄弟克财，病人占者恐悲哀；官鬼克兄弟，病困门庭祸崇来(病占忌鬼)

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 海底眼的六亲爻动一节，每一句后面带眉批：父母爻动克子孙，病人没有药、主昏沉（眉批：子能医药）；子孙发动伤官鬼，占病求医身体就痊愈（眉批：鬼被制住、病就安）；妻财发动，病人伤脾、胃虚（眉批：财是饮食）；兄弟克财，占病的人恐怕悲哀（眉批：兄动反复）；官鬼克兄弟，病困、门庭里有祸崇（眉批：占病忌鬼）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The section on the six relations moving in the Haidi Yan has a marginal comment after each line: when the Resource line moves and controls Output, the sick have no medicine and are heavily drowsy (comment: Output can doctor and medicate); when Output moves and harms Pressure, in divining illness and seeking a doctor the body is cured at once (comment: the ghost is held down and the illness settles); when Wealth moves, the sick have a harmed spleen and a deficient stomach (comment: Wealth is food and drink); when Peer controls Wealth, the one divining the illness fears grief (comment: when Peer moves there is relapse); when Pressure controls Peer, the illness is stuck and a curse comes to the household (comment: in divining illness, avoid the ghost).

> [!warning] 打架
> 同一首歌诀在卜筮全书的六亲发动诀里也有（LY.ILL.BSQS.06），父动、子动两句几乎一样（「沉昏」「昏沉」只是字序），另外三处说的不一样：妻财发动，海底眼写「病者伤脾并胃虚」（脾和胃），卜筮全书写「病人伤胃更伤脾」，意思相近；兄弟克财，海底眼写「病人占者恐悲哀」（占病的人恐怕悲哀），卜筮全书写「病人难愈未离灾」（病难好、没有离开灾）；官鬼克兄弟，海底眼写「官鬼从来克兄弟」「病困门庭祸崇来」（病困），卜筮全书写「官鬼发动克兄弟」「病因门庭祸祟来」（病因）。「病困」和「病因」是一字之差、意思不同：一个说病重困顿，一个说病的原因是门庭里的祸祟。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The same song is also in the verses on the six relations moving in the Bushi Quanshu (LY.ILL.BSQS.06), and the lines for the Resource and Output moving are almost the same (the 'heavy drowsiness' differs only in the order of the characters); the other three differ: for Wealth moving the Haidi Yan writes 'the sick have a harmed spleen and a deficient stomach' (spleen and stomach) while the Bushi Quanshu writes 'the sick harm the stomach and then the spleen', close in sense; for Peer controlling Wealth the Haidi Yan writes 'the one divining the illness fears grief' while the Bushi Quanshu writes 'the illness is hard to cure and has not left calamity' (the illness is hard to mend, not yet free of calamity); for Pressure controlling Peer the Haidi Yan writes 'Pressure has always controlled Peer' and 'the illness is stuck, and misfortune and a curse come to the household' (stuck) while the Bushi Quanshu writes 'Pressure moves and controls Peer' and 'the cause of the illness is that misfortune and a curse come to the household' (cause). 'Stuck' and 'cause' differ by one character and in sense: one says the illness is heavy and stuck, the other that the cause of the illness is a misfortune and curse in the household. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 每一句后面括号里的眉批，是这部书自己的旁注（「海底眼眉批」），原文里夹在两个分句中间，所以每句经文和它的眉批分开引，各引一段。眉批大多是四个字的提示：「子能医药」是子孙爻管医药，「鬼制病安」是鬼被克制、病就安，「病占忌鬼」是占病忌讳鬼爻动。「兄动反复」的「反复」是病情反复。「同谓同类」的「同」是兄弟同人的同，指同类。「崇」是「祟」的写法。这一节在原书里每一个六亲各有一首，这里只取讲到病的句子；六亲爻变、六亲持世见 LY.ILL.HDY.16、HDY.17。
>
> *Note:* The marginal comments in brackets after each line are the book's own side notes ('Haidi Yan marginal comments'); in the original they sit between two phrases, so each line of the verse and its comment are quoted separately, one quotation each. Most comments are four-character prompts: 'the son line can doctor and medicate' says the Output line governs doctors and medicine, 'the ghost is held down and the illness settles' says the ghost is controlled and so the illness settles, and 'in divining illness, the ghost is avoided' says in divining illness it is feared if the ghost line moves. The 'relapse' of 'when the Peer line moves, there is relapse' is the illness going back and forth. The 'kind' of 'Peer and its kind' is the 'same' of the same kind. The character for 'curse' is written in a variant form. In the original book each of the six relations has a verse of its own, and only the lines that speak of illness are taken here; for the six relations changing and holding the Self see LY.ILL.HDY.16 and HDY.17.
