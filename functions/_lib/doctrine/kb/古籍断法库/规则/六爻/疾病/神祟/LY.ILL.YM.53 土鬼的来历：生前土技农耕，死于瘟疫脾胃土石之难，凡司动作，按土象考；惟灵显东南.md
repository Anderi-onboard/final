# 土鬼的来历：生前土技农耕，死于瘟疫脾胃土石之难，凡司动作，按土象考；惟灵显东南

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生前做土工、务农耕作的，死在瘟疫、脾胃的病、土石之难中的，凡是掌管动土动作的，就按土象来考察。注说：只是灵验多显现在东南。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* One who in life had an earth craft or farmed, or died of epidemic, illness of the spleen and stomach, or the calamity of earth and stone, and whose charge is works and movement, is examined by the Earth image. The note says: only, its power is manifest chiefly in the southeast.

> [!note] 按
> 「惟灵显东南」承接土神那条（LY.ILL.YM.48）：东南土薄，土曹的名号多，土鬼的灵验也多显在东南。这一条和 LY.ILL.YM.49–LY.ILL.YM.52 合起来，是五行各自的「性、成、司」的一张表。
>
> *Note:* 'Only its power is manifest in the southeast' follows the entry on Earth spirits (LY.ILL.YM.48): in the southeast the earth is thin and the bureau titles are many, and the power of an Earth ghost is chiefly manifest there too. Together with LY.ILL.YM.49 to YM.52 this makes a table of the 'nature, origin and charge' of each of the five phases.
