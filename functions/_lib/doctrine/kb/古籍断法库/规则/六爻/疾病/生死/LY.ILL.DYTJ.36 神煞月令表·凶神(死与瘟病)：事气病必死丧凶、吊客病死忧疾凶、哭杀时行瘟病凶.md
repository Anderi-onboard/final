# 神煞月令表·凶神(死与瘟病)：事气病必死丧凶、吊客病死忧疾凶、哭杀时行瘟病凶

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神煞月令表里凶神的三行：事气（正月起午、未、申、酉、戌、亥、子、丑、寅、卯、辰、巳），病必死，丧事凶；吊客（辰、丑、戌、未循环），病人死亡、忧愁、疾病，凶；哭杀（巳、午、未、申、酉、戌、亥、子、丑、寅、卯、辰），流行的瘟病，凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Three fierce rows of the table of spirits and killings by month: Affair Qi (from the first month Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si): in illness surely dead, mourning is ominous; Guest of Condolence (Chen, Chou, Xu, Wei in a cycle): the sick person dies, worry and sickness, ominous; the Weeping Killing (Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen): epidemic plague is ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 这三行的断语都说到死或瘟病：事气「病必死，丧凶」是最重的一句，吊客是病人死亡和忧愁，哭杀是时行的瘟病。「吊客」这一名和天玄赋里「丧门」「吊客」并提的说法（LY.ILL.TXF.04：「若临丧门、吊客、死符、丧车，持克用爻者，决主不起」）是同一个吊客。「哭杀」在表里的排法（巳午未申酉戌亥子丑寅卯辰）和前面「天哭杀」歌里的排法（正五九羊、二六十猴……，LY.ILL.DYTJ.33）不同，是两个杀。表里另有「大杀」「飞廉」「天丧」「大忌」等几行说到死亡，但都没有说病，这里不收。
>
> *Note:* The judgments of these three rows all speak of death or plague: Affair Qi, 'in illness surely dead, mourning is ominous', is the heaviest sentence, Guest of Condolence is the death and worry of the sick person, and the Weeping Killing is the plague of the season. The name 'Guest of Condolence' is the same Guest of Condolence as in the Tianxuan Fu's pairing of 'Mourning Gate' and 'Guest of Condolence' (LY.ILL.TXF.04: 'if it sits on the Mourning Gate, the Guest of Condolence, the Death Talisman or the Mourning Cart and controls the Subject line, it surely means he does not rise'). The layout of the 'Weeping Killing' in the table (Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen) differs from that in the song of the 'Heavenly Weeping killing' (first, fifth, ninth the sheep, second, sixth, tenth the monkey ..., LY.ILL.DYTJ.33), and they are two killings. The table has other rows, 'Great Killing', 'Feilian', 'Heavenly Mourning' and 'Great Taboo', that speak of death but do not say illness, and they are not taken here.
