# 何知章·怪与死：多梦寐（螣蛇带鬼持世）、出鬼怪（螣蛇白虎临门）、人投水（玄武入水煞临鬼）、有吊颈（螣蛇木鬼临世爻）、孝服来（白虎临鬼；大全作丧门吊客临鬼）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 怎么知道这家的人多做噩梦：螣蛇带着鬼来持世；出鬼怪：螣蛇、白虎临在门上；有人投水：玄武入水，煞神临在鬼上；有人上吊：螣蛇、木鬼落在世爻上；要有孝服（丧事）来：卜筮正宗说是白虎交重（发动）、排在鬼上，卜筮大全说是丧门、吊客两颗星排在鬼上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* How to know that a household is troubled by dreams: the Coiling Snake carrying the ghost holds the Self position; that ghosts and strange things appear: the Coiling Snake and White Tiger sit at the door; that someone will throw himself into water: the Dark Tortoise enters Water and a killing spirit sits on the ghost; that someone will hang himself: the Coiling Snake and a Wood ghost sit on the Self line; that mourning dress will come: in the Bushi Zhengzong the White Tiger moves and is set upon the ghost, in the Bushi Daquan the stars Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence are set upon the ghost.

> [!warning] 打架
> 孝服来（有丧事）看什么：卜筮正宗答「交重白虎临鬼排」，白虎发动排在鬼爻上；卜筮大全答「丧门吊客临鬼排」，丧门、吊客两颗神煞排在鬼爻上。一个用六神白虎，一个用神煞丧门吊客，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What tells that mourning is coming: the Bushi Zhengzong answers 'the White Tiger moving, set upon the ghost', the White Tiger of the six spirits moving on the Pressure line; the Bushi Daquan answers 'Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence set upon the ghost', two spirit-stars on the Pressure line. One uses the White Tiger among the six spirits and the other the spirit-stars Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence, and the books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「何知章」一问一答，一共问了三四十件事（父母子孙的病、有没有子孙、讼事、财富、田地、屋宇、坟墓、六畜、口舌、盗贼……），都是一家的事；这里只收与病、死、鬼怪有关的。卜筮大全里它是「十六、何知章」，卜筮正宗里它在《赋文三篇》中；两本字句略有出入。「梦寐」是睡着做梦，这里是不好的梦；「吊颈」是上吊；「孝服」是丧服，指家里有丧事。丧门、吊客是两颗神煞，书里没有再解释。
>
> *Note:* The 'how to know' rhyme asks and answers some thirty or forty things (illnesses of parents and children, whether there are children, lawsuits, wealth, land, houses, graves, livestock, quarrels, thieves and more), all affairs of a household; only those to do with illness, death and ghostly happenings are taken here. In the Bushi Daquan it is chapter 16, 'the how-to-know rhyme'; in the Bushi Zhengzong it is among the three rhapsodies; the two texts differ a little in wording. 'Mengmei' is dreaming in sleep, here bad dreams; 'diaojing' is hanging oneself; 'mourning dress' (xiaofu) means a death in the family. Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence are two spirit-stars that the book does not explain further.
