# 日加福去生扶药医则愈：日辰临子孙、生扶（拱合）用爻，必得药力而愈

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰坐在子孙上，来生、扶用爻（王洪绪：或拱、合），病一定靠药力好起来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Day sits on Output and generates and supports the Subject line (Wang Hongxu: or embraces or combines with it), the patient will surely recover through the power of medicine.

## 《黄金策·病体》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6948 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日加福去生扶，药医则愈。 | 日辰加在子孙上，去生扶用爻，用药医治就会痊愈。 | The Day placed on Output and going to generate and support the Subject: treated with medicine, it heals. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6949 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日辰临子孙，生扶用爻，必得药力而安也。 | 日辰坐在子孙上，来生、扶用爻，一定靠药力平安。 | The Day sitting on Output and generating and supporting the Subject line: he surely recovers through the power of medicine. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2402 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日辰临子孙生扶拱合用爻，必得药力而愈。 | 日辰坐在子孙上，来生、扶、拱、合用爻，一定靠药力痊愈。 | The Day sitting on Output and generating, supporting, embracing or combining with the Subject line: he surely heals through the power of medicine. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「拱」的意思见 LY.ILL.HJC.32 的注。旧注写「生扶」，王洪绪写成「生扶拱合」。
>
> *Note:* For what 'embracing' means see the note of LY.ILL.HJC.32. The old commentary writes 'generating and supporting', Wang Hongxu 'generating, supporting, embracing and combining'.
