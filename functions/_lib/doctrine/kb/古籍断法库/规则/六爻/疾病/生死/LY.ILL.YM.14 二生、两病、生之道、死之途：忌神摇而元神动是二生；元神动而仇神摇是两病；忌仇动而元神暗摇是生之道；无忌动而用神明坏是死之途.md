# 二生、两病、生之道、死之途：忌神摇而元神动是二生；元神动而仇神摇是两病；忌仇动而元神暗摇是生之道；无忌动而用神明坏是死之途

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 忌神发动了，而原神也动，叫做二生；原神动了，而仇神也动，叫做两病；忌神、仇神动了，原神暗中动，是生路；没有忌神动，而用神明明白白地坏了，是死路。注举例：未月丙申日，占母亲的病，得同人变噬嗑，申是忌神本来克卯，亥是原神，发动泄掉忌神的气而生卯木用爻，这是二生；卯月乙卯日，占妻子的病，得同人变咸，子孙发动生财，本来是原神帮我，但卯木仇神来克戌爻，又多一层病；未月壬寅日，占丈夫的病，得同人变既济，兄、子都动，伤官本应病重，但寅申冲财，暗发生亥，后来病好起来；卯月丙寅日，占儿子本人，得坤变损，卦里有父、兄发动，木来救子孙，但酉金明明受日绝、月破，所以救不了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Adversary moves and the Support also moves: this is called the double life; the Support moves and the enemy spirit also moves: the double illness; the Adversary and enemy spirit move and the Support stirs in secret: a road to life; the Adversary does not move but the Subject is plainly spoiled: a road to death. The note's examples: in the Wei month on the Bingshen day, a mother's illness was divined and Tongren changing into Shihe obtained: Shen is the Adversary and by nature controls Mao, while Hai is the Support and moves to drain the Adversary's qi and generate the Mao Wood Subject line — the double life; in the Mao month on the Yimao day, a wife's illness was divined and Tongren changing into Xian obtained: Output moves to generate Wealth, which should have been the Support helping me, but Mao Wood, the enemy spirit, controls the Xu line, adding one more illness; in the Wei month on the Renyin day, a husband's illness was divined and Tongren changing into Jiji obtained: Peer and Output both move and harm Pressure, so it should be grave, but Yin clashes Shen Wealth and secretly stirs it to generate Hai, and later he recovered; in the Mao month on the Bingyin day, a son himself was divined and Kun changing into Sun obtained: Resource and Peer move, and Wood comes to rescue Output, but You Metal is plainly in Extinction by the Day and in Month-break, so it could not be saved.

> [!note] 按
> 「二生」是有两条生路（忌神被原神泄去、又生用神），「两病」是原神之外又有仇神一起动、多一层病；「仇神」是生忌神的爻。这一组和卜筮正宗讲原神忌神仇神的《原忌仇神论》（第 967 行起）是同一套用法。第三例里「伤官应笃」的「官」是丈夫的用神（占夫以官为用）。
>
> *Note:* The 'double life' is two roads to life (the Adversary drained by the Support, which also generates the Subject), and the 'double illness' is the enemy spirit moving together with the Support so that there is one more illness; the 'enemy spirit' (chou shen) is the line that generates the Adversary. This group is the same usage as the Bushi Zhengzong's essay on the Support, Adversary and enemy spirit (from line 967). In the third example the 'Pressure' of 'harming Pressure it should be grave' is the husband's Subject (Pressure is the Subject in divining for a husband).
