# 青龙会恩星发动庆贺齐来：青龙临原神发动，生合用神，痘必收功

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙会合恩星发动，庆贺的人一起到来：青龙带着原神发动，生合用神，痘一定能收功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon meeting the kindly star and moving means congratulations arrive together: if the Azure Dragon sits on the Support and it moves to generate and combine with the Subject, the pox will surely come to completion.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2293 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 经文的「恩星」注里没有解释，从注文看就是原神。这一条和上一条（白虎同忌）是一对：一个是白虎带忌神，一个是青龙带原神。
>
> *Note:* The verse's 'kindly star' is not explained in the commentary and from the commentary it is the Support. This entry and the previous (the White Tiger with the Adversary) are a pair: one is the White Tiger on the Adversary, the other the Azure Dragon on the Support.
