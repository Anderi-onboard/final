# 官鬼属土则土神及山川社稷之司：地祗、太岁、城隍、社稷、土神、庙神、宅神、伽蓝、皮肠、当境、本家土地之属；土部名号非一（飞游作犯斫伐坟墓坑厮金神吟神伏尸螣蛇天曹土），当以八宫六神诸星配辨；西北土厚知地祗、东南土薄多土曹

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼属土的，就是土神，以及掌管山川社稷的。像地祇、太岁、城隍、社稷、土神、庙神、宅神、伽蓝、皮肠、当境、本家土地这一类，以及因为在当地有功绩而成神的，都是。土这一部的神灵，圣号、曹名不止一种，有飞游、作犯、斫伐、坟墓、坑厮、金神、吟神、伏尸、螣蛇、天曹土等称呼，要用八宫、六神、诸星配合起来辨别，比如白虎带土官，就断成金神七煞。大体上西北土地厚，只知道有地祇；东南土地薄，所以有很多土曹的名号。所以要因人、因地、因事，变通的方法很多。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line is Earth, it is an earth spirit, and those in charge of mountains, rivers and the altars of soil and grain, as with the Earth Spirit, the Tai Sui, the City God, the Altars of Soil and Grain, earth gods, temple gods, house gods, the Sangharama, the Skin and Intestines, the Local Boundary God and the family's own Earth God, and those who became spirits through merit in a locality. The spirits of the Earth department have more than one holy title and office name, such as Flying Wanderer, Offence-maker, Felling, Grave, Pit Rascal, Golden Spirit, Chanting Spirit, Buried Corpse, Coiling Snake and Heavenly Bureau Earth; one must tell them apart by combining the eight palaces, the six spirits and the various stars, so that for example an Earth Pressure line with the White Tiger is judged the Golden Spirit or the Seven Killings. In general the earth of the northwest is thick and people know only the Earth Spirit, while the earth of the southeast is thin and so has many bureau titles of the earth. So one goes by person, place and matter, and the ways of adapting are many.

> [!note] 按
> 神名的处理见 LY.ILL.YM.44 的注。土部的称呼里「螣蛇」在这里是土部一个神煞的名字，不是六神里的螣蛇，原文没有再分；「土曹」是掌管土地的曹官。西北土厚、东南土薄的说法，和前面土鬼独发那一条（LY.ILL.YM.12）里东南土薄、犯土忌多、西北土厚不畏土禁，是同一个地理判断。
>
> *Note:* Names are treated as in the note to LY.ILL.YM.44. Among the titles of the Earth department 'Coiling Snake' is the name of one earth star here, not the Coiling Snake of the six spirits, and the original does not distinguish them further; 'bureau of the earth' is the office that governs the land. The saying that the northwest earth is thick and the southeast earth thin is the same geographical judgment as in the entry on an Earth ghost moving alone (LY.ILL.YM.12), where in the southeast the earth is thin and earth taboos are often broken, and in the northwest it is thick and earth prohibitions are not feared.
