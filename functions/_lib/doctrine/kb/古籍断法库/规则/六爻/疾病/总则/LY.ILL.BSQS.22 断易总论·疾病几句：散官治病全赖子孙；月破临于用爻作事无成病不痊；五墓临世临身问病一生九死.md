# 断易总论·疾病几句：散官治病全赖子孙；月破临于用爻作事无成病不痊；五墓临世临身问病一生九死

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断易总论里讲病的几句：要消散官事、医治疾病，全靠子孙；月破落在用爻上，做事没有结果，病不得痊愈；五墓落在世爻或卦身上，问病是一生九死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The lines on illness in the general discussion of Duanyi: to disperse official matters and to treat illness, rely wholly on Output; with the Month-break falling on the Subject line, nothing done comes to completion and the illness is not cured; with the Five Tombs on the Self line or the Body, in asking about illness it is nine deaths to one life.

> [!note] 按
> 「散官治病」按下一句「兴讼求名，须凭官鬼」对着读：兴讼求名要靠官鬼，散官治病要靠子孙（子孙克官鬼），所以「散官」是消散官事，不是指一个官职；这是按对句读出来的，书里没有注。「用爻」就是用神。「五墓」指什么书里没有当场讲：可能是五行的墓库（断易天机论墓一节，LY.ILL.DYTJ.23），也可能是三丘五墓杀里的五墓（LY.ILL.DYTJ.31），译文直写「五墓」。「一生九死」是九分死、一分生。同一段话在火珠林里也有（第 866 行）。
>
> *Note:* 'Disperse official matters and treat illness' is read against the next line, 'to start a suit or seek fame one must depend on Pressure': starting a suit or seeking fame relies on Pressure, and dispersing official matters or treating illness relies on Output (which controls Pressure), so 'disperse officials' means dissolving official trouble and not an official post; this is read from the parallel lines and the book gives no note. The 'Subject line' is the Subject. The book does not say on the spot what 'the Five Tombs' are: they may be the tombs of the five elements (the section on tombs in the Duanyi Tianji, LY.ILL.DYTJ.23) or the Five Tombs of the Three Mounds and Five Tombs killing (LY.ILL.DYTJ.31), and the translation writes 'the Five Tombs' as it stands. 'Nine deaths to one life' is nine parts death and one part life. The same passage is also in the Huozhu Lin (line 866).
