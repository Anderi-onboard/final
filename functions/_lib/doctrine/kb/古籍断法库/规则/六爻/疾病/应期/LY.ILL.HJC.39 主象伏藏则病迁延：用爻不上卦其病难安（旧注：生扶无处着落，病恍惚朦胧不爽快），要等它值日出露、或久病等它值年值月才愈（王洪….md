# 主象伏藏则病迁延：用爻不上卦其病难安（旧注：生扶无处着落，病恍惚朦胧不爽快），要等它值日出露、或久病等它值年值月才愈（王洪绪）

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻不上卦，这病一定拖着难好：出现在卦里的爻，日辰、动爻才能生它、合它，不上卦，就算有生扶也没有地方落脚，这种病也是恍惚朦胧、不爽快的病。要等用爻值日露出来，或者久病等它值年、值月才好，所以说一定拖延日子和月份。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject line that is absent from the hexagram means the illness drags on and cannot settle: a line that appears can be generated and combined by the Day and moving lines, but if it is not in the hexagram, where would generation and support land? Such an illness is vague and hazy, one that never feels clear. One must wait until the Subject line rules the day and comes into the open, or in a long illness wait for the year or month that it rules, before it heals; hence it is said to drag on for days and months.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6904–6905 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用爻不上卦，其病必难安。 | 用爻不上卦，这病一定难以安稳。 | A Subject line absent from the hexagram: the illness is surely hard to settle. |
> | 盖凡出现之爻，日辰动爻能生能合； | 因为凡是出现在卦里的爻，日辰、动爻能生它、能合它； | For a line that appears in the hexagram can be generated and combined by the Day and moving lines; |
> | 若不上卦，虽有生扶，着于何地？ | 如果不上卦，就算有生扶，落在哪里？ | if it is not in the hexagram, then though there is generation and support, where does it land? |
> | 故其占如此，病亦是恍惚朦胧，不爽快之症。 | 所以这样占，病也是恍恍惚惚、朦朦胧胧，不爽快的症候。 | So such a divination gives an illness that is vague and hazy and never feels clear. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2376 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用爻不上卦，纵有提拔扶引者，必待其值日出露，或久病必待其值年值月方愈，故曰定主迁延日月也。 | 用爻不上卦，就算有提拔、扶引它的，一定要等它值日的时候露出来，或者久病一定要等它值年、值月才好，所以说一定拖延日子和月份。 | A Subject line absent from the hexagram: even if something would raise and lead it out, one must wait for the day it rules, when it comes into the open, or in a long illness wait for the year or month it rules before it heals; hence it is said to drag on for days and months. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 两家讲的是同一个卦象的两面：旧注讲这种病是什么样子（恍惚朦胧、不爽快），王洪绪讲它什么时候好（值日、值年、值月）。王洪绪引的经文是「迁延乎岁月」，而他自己的注里说「迁延日月」，和卜筮大全的「迁延乎日月」一致，「岁月」大概是印错了。
>
> *Note:* The two speak of two sides of the same image: the old commentary of what sort of illness this is (vague, hazy, never clear), Wang Hongxu of when it heals (on the day, year or month the line rules). The verse as Wang Hongxu quotes it reads 'dragging on through years and months', while his own note says 'dragging on through days and months', in agreement with the Bushi Daquan's 'days and months'; 'years' is probably a printing slip.
