# 移徒章·朱雀又临官鬼位、克世兼持世：病遭危困、讼遭囚

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占迁居的歌里说：朱雀又临在官鬼爻上，官司口舌就有来由；再克世、又持世，生病会遇到危险困厄，打官司会被囚禁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on moving house says: if the Vermilion Bird sits on a Pressure line, there is cause for lawsuits and quarrels; if it also controls and holds the Self line, illness meets danger and distress, and a lawsuit ends in imprisonment.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是占迁居（移徒章第一首）里的一联，讲朱雀、官鬼、世爻三样凑在一起。「克世兼持世」是同一个神既克世爻又坐在世爻上，照字面写成「控制并占住」在汉语里说不通，译文写作「又克世、又持世」，保留歌里两个动作并列的样子。
>
> *Note:* This couplet is in the verse on moving house (the first poem of the chapter on moving), where the Vermilion Bird, Pressure and the Self line come together. 'Controls and holds the Self line' describes one spirit that both controls the Self line and sits on it; the translation keeps the two actions side by side as in the verse.
