# 近病逢空、化空即愈，若两卦俱值三合必成久病而终：应在填实墓库的年月（例：妻占夫近病，卯官化未土空亡，两卦俱三合，明年未月卒）

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 近病：用神落空、或变成空，到出空的日子就好；可是如果两个卦都值三合，就会拖成久病，最后死掉，死期应在把它的墓填实的年月。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a recent illness, if the Subject falls into Void or changes into Void, it heals on the day the Void is out; but if both hexagrams meet a three-harmony, it turns into a long illness and ends in death, the time of death falling in the year or month that fills its tomb.

> [!note] 按
> 「近病逢空即愈」是野鹤在 LY.ILL.ZS.04 讲的道理；这一条补上例外：近病落空虽然好得快，但两个卦都值三合就不行了，三合把病拖成久病。觉子在 LY.ILL.ZS.05 里说的是同一个经验。「填实其墓」是等到墓的地支值年、值月，把空着的墓填上，病人就在那时死。「存此式者」这一句野鹤是说他为什么把这个卦例留在书里。
>
> *Note:* 'A recent illness meeting Void heals at once' is the principle the Old Man gives in LY.ILL.ZS.04; this entry adds the exception: a recent illness in Void gets well quickly, but if both hexagrams meet a three-harmony it does not, for the three-harmony drags it into a long illness. Juezi says the same experience in LY.ILL.ZS.05. To 'fill the tomb' is to wait until the branch of the tomb rules the year or month and fills the empty tomb, and the patient dies at that time. In 'the one who keeps this form' the Old Man says why he kept this case in the book.
