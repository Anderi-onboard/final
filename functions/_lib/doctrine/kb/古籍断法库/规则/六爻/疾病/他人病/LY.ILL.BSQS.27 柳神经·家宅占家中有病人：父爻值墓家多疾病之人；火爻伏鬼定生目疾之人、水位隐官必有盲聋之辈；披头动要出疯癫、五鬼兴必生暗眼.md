# 柳神经·家宅占家中有病人：父爻值墓家多疾病之人；火爻伏鬼定生目疾之人、水位隐官必有盲聋之辈；披头动要出疯癫、五鬼兴必生暗眼

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 柳神经里讲家里会有什么样的病人：父爻落在墓上，家里多有生病的人；火爻下面伏着鬼，一定生出眼病的人；水位下面藏着官，一定有又瞎又聋的人；披头煞发动，要出疯癫的人；五鬼煞兴起，一定生出眼睛看不见的人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Liushen Jing on what kind of sick person the household will have: with the Resource line on a tomb, the household has many sick people; with a ghost hidden under a Fire line, someone with an eye disease is sure to appear; with an officer hidden in the Water place, there will surely be people blind or deaf; when the Loosened Hair star moves, a mad person will appear; when the Five Ghosts rise, someone with darkened eyes is sure to be born.

> [!note] 按
> 「家多疾病之人」的人是家里的人；「生」是出现、生出来，不是生孩子。「水位隐官」是水爻下面伏着官鬼。「披头」「五鬼」是神煞名，位置见卜筮全书神煞歌例里的「驾后神煞」（披头五鬼，辰上起子逆行）。「暗眼」书里没有解释，可能是看不清，也可能是瞎，译文写作「眼睛看不清」。「父爻值墓，家多疾病之人」同一句在参玄赋里也有（LY.ILL.BSQS.28）。
>
> *Note:* 'The household has many sick people' means people of the household; 'appear' is to come into being, not to give birth. 'An officer hidden in the Water place' is a Pressure line hidden under a Water line. 'Loosened Hair' and 'Five Ghosts' are spirit-stars whose positions are given in the verses on spirit-stars in this book under 'the stars after the carriage' (the Loosened Hair and Five Ghosts start at Chen and count back from Zi). 'Darkened eyes' is not explained in the book and may mean dim sight or blindness; the translation says 'eyes that cannot see clearly'. The same sentence 'the Resource line on a tomb: the household has many sick people' also appears in the Canxuan Fu (LY.ILL.BSQS.28).
