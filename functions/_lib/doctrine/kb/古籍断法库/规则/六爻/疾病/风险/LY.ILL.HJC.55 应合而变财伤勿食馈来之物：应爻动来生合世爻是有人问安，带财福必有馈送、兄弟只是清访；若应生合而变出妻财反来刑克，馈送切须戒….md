# 应合而变财伤勿食馈来之物：应爻动来生合世爻是有人问安，带财福必有馈送、兄弟只是清访；若应生合而变出妻财反来刑克，馈送切须戒食否则反受伤害，占长辈尤宜忌

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻发动来生、合世爻，是有人来问安：带财爻、子孙的，一定有送东西的；带兄弟的，只是来看望。如果应爻虽然生合，却变出妻财反过来刑克世爻（或者应爻生合世爻而刑克主象），要是有人送东西，千万不要吃，不然反而受伤害。占长辈的病，尤其要忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Other line moves to generate or combine with the Self line, someone will come to ask after him: if it carries Wealth or Output, there will surely be gifts; if Peer, only a plain visit. But if the Other line, though generating or combining, changes out Wealth that turns to punish or control the Self line (or the Other line generates and combines with the Self line while punishing or controlling the main image), then any gift sent should on no account be eaten, or it does harm instead; in divining an elder it is even more to be avoided.

> [!warning] 打架
> 变出妻财的是哪一爻：旧注是应爻变出妻财、反过来刑克世爻（另有应爻生合世爻却刑克主象的一种）；王洪绪是用爻自己变出妻财，或者有财爻刑冲克害用神。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line changes into Wealth: in the old commentary it is the Other line changing out Wealth that in turn punishes and controls the Self line (with a second case, the Other line generating and combining with the Self line while punishing or controlling the main image); in Wang Hongxu it is the Subject line itself changing into Wealth, or a Wealth line punishing, clashing with, controlling or harming the Subject.

> [!note] 按
> 「清访」是清淡的探望，不带东西。这一条防的是有人借「送东西」害人，或者因为妻财克伤了病人依靠的那一爻，吃了送来的东西反倒加重；书里没有把机理讲得更细。
>
> *Note:* 'A plain visit' is a bare call, bringing nothing. This entry guards against a gift that does harm, or against eating what is sent when Wealth harms the very line the patient depends on, which makes him worse; the book does not spell the mechanism out further.
