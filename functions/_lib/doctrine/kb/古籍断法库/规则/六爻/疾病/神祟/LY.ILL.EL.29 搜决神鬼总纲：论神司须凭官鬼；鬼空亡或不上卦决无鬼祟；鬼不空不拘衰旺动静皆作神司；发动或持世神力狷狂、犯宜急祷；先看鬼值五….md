# 搜决神鬼总纲：论神司须凭官鬼；鬼空亡或不上卦决无鬼祟；鬼不空不拘衰旺动静皆作神司；发动或持世神力狷狂、犯宜急祷；先看鬼值五行、次察官临何六兽、再查病源缓急，便知何祟为殃

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以官鬼和动爻为主，以五行和六兽（六神）为凭。凡是论神灵的职司，必须凭官鬼，只要不逢旬空，或旺或衰都是作祟，只要出现在卦上，不论动静，一概按神仙来推。先看鬼落在哪一行，再察官鬼临的是哪个六兽，再查病的来源是缓是急，就知道是什么祟作怪。注说：如果占神灵的职司，要推官鬼；鬼如果空亡或不上卦，一定没有鬼祟，不可妄说；鬼如果不空，不论衰旺动静，都作为神灵的职司；如果发动或持世，神力狂暴，冒犯了应当赶快祷告；还要看鬼落在哪一爻、哪个六兽上，才说是什么鬼、什么神，六兽和五行列在后面。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line and the moving lines are the main lines, and the five phases and the six beasts (the six spirits) are the ground. In judging the offices of the spirits one must rely on the Pressure line; so long as it does not fall on Void, strong or weak it makes haunting, and so long as it appears in the hexagram, moving or still, it is in all cases reckoned as an immortal spirit. See first which phase the ghost falls on, then examine which of the six beasts the Pressure line sits on, then check whether the source of the illness is slow or urgent, and one knows what haunting is doing the harm. The commentary says: if divining the office of a spirit, work out the Pressure line; if the ghost is Void or absent from the hexagram, there is surely no haunting by a ghost, and one must not speak at random; if the ghost is not Void, whether weak or strong, moving or still, it stands for a spirit's office; if it moves or holds the Self, the spirit's power is fierce and wild, and if offended one should pray at once; look also at which line and which beast the ghost sits on, and only then say which ghost and which spirit; the six beasts and five phases are set out below.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章接在「还赛解厄」之前：病由鬼神作祟时，怎么认是哪一路。「六兽」就是六神（青龙、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武），这一章都称六兽。第二句「概为神仙推」按字面译：出现在卦上的鬼一概当作神灵的职司来推，不分神、鬼。「神力狷狂，犯宜急祷」是说鬼发动或持世时，那位神的力量强，冒犯了要赶紧祈祷。这一章的办法和易冒的鬼神章（LY.ILL.YM.42 到 YM.64）、黄金策鬼神章（LY.ILL.HJC.93 到 HJC.120）是同一件事：用官鬼的五行、六神、卦宫、爻位去认神鬼；它比它们多出的是逐个五行配六神列出了具体的神名（下面 LY.ILL.EL.30 起）。 这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地（苕溪一带）的民间神祇和星煞，书里一个也没有解释（章末自己说别的郡的神司不同），这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This chapter comes before 'thanksgiving rites to lift calamity' and shows how to tell which kind of ghost or spirit is at work when the illness is a haunting. The 'six beasts' are the six spirits (Azure Dragon, Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth, Coiling Snake, White Tiger, Dark Tortoise), which this chapter calls the six beasts throughout. The second sentence, 'in all cases reckoned as an immortal spirit', is translated literally: a ghost that appears in the hexagram is in all cases reckoned as the office of a spirit, without distinguishing spirit from ghost. 'The spirit's power is fierce and wild; if offended one should pray at once' means that when the ghost moves or holds the Self, that spirit's power is strong, and if offended one must pray quickly. The method is the same as the Yi Mao's chapter on ghosts and spirits (LY.ILL.YM.42 to YM.64) and the Huangjin Ce's (LY.ILL.HJC.93 to HJC.120): recognising ghosts and spirits by the phase, spirit, palace and line position of the Pressure line; what it adds is that it lists specific names of gods for each phase paired with each of the six spirits (from LY.ILL.EL.30). Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain (the book itself says at the end of the chapter that the gods of other prefectures differ), and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
