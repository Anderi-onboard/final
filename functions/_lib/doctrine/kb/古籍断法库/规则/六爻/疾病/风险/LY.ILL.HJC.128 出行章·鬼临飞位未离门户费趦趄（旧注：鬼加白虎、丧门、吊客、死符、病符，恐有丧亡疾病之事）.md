# 出行章·鬼临飞位未离门户费趦趄（旧注：鬼加白虎、丧门、吊客、死符、病符，恐有丧亡疾病之事）

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》出行章说：「鬼临在飞爻的位置，还没离开门户就迟疑不前。」旧注解释：官鬼加上白虎、丧门、吊客、死符、病符这些星，恐怕有丧亡、疾病的事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on travel says: 'With the ghost on the flying place, one has not yet left the door and hesitates.' The old commentary explains: Pressure joined by the White Tiger, Mourning Gate, Guest of Condolence, Death Tally or Sickness Tally may bring a death or an illness.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这是占出行（出远门）一章的旧注，说的是官鬼带着凶星时，出行的人可能遇到丧事或生病。白虎、丧门、吊客、死符、病符都是流年神煞（LY.ILL.DYTJ.50、LY.ILL.BSQS.51 里有名单）；「飞位」是飞神所在的位置。整段旧注的前后讲的是鬼持世、鬼带贵人、鬼加官符朱雀（官事牵连），这里只收讲病的一句。
>
> *Note:* This is an old commentary in the chapter on travel (going far from home), saying that when Pressure carries ominous stars the traveller may meet a death or fall ill. The White Tiger, Mourning Gate, Guest of Condolence, Death Tally and Sickness Tally are year-stars (they are listed in LY.ILL.DYTJ.50 and LY.ILL.BSQS.51); the 'flying place' is where the flying line stands. The passages before and after the old commentary are about the ghost holding the Self line, the ghost carrying a noble star and the ghost with the Official Tally and Vermilion Bird (entanglement in official matters); only the sentence on illness is taken here.
