# 疾病论·家亲与愿：看祖宗叔伯推父母爻、姨母妻奴推财爻；白虎丧门亲座席、青龙天乙贵家亲；龙雀素愿、虎勾晕盟；雀虎入乾离只言拜愿烧香、勾陈临震巽便说愿心枷锁；鬼静生身吉、官兴克世告神凶

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里讲是哪一位亲人、哪一种愿：要看祖宗、叔伯，用父母爻推；要看姨母、妻子、奴仆，用财爻断；阳爻是男子，阴爻是女人；白虎、丧门，是亲人在座；青龙、天乙贵人，是尊贵的家亲；青龙、朱雀叫素愿，白虎、勾陈是晕盟；朱雀、白虎进乾、离动，只说拜愿烧香；勾陈临震、巽交重，就说愿心枷锁；鬼静、生身还是吉，官鬼兴动、克世，告神也凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness on which kin and which vow: to see the ancestors and uncles, work it out from the Resource line; to see the aunt, wife or servants, judge by the Wealth line; a yang line is a man, a yin line a woman; the White Tiger and Mourning Gate are kin in their seats; the Azure Dragon and Heavenly Noble are honoured family kin; the Azure Dragon and Vermilion Bird are called vegetarian vows, the White Tiger and Hook Earth are sure to be yun-meng oaths; the Vermilion Bird and White Tiger in Qian or Li and moving speak only of making vows and burning incense; Hook Earth on Zhen or Xun and moving speaks of the cangue and lock of a vow; a ghost still and generating the Body is still auspicious, Pressure rising and controlling the Self line is ominous even when one petitions the spirits.

> [!note] 按
> 「晕盟」照原文，字面是「晕」（头晕）的盟，从上下句看它和「素愿」相对，可能是「荤盟」（带荤腥的盟誓）的误字，书里没有说，译文只音译为「yun-meng 盟誓」。「天乙」是天乙贵人。「愿心枷锁」是许了愿没有还，被愿心锁住。「亲座席」按字面是亲人坐在席上，书里没有解释。「阳爻男子，阴爻女流」是说被推的亲人是男是女，按爻的阴阳分。
>
> *Note:* 'Yun-meng' is kept as written; literally it is an oath of 'dizziness', and from the lines before and after, where it stands opposite 'vegetarian vow', it may be a miswriting of 'meat oath' (an oath sworn with meat); the book does not say, and the translation only transliterates it as 'yun-meng oaths'. 'Heavenly Noble' is the Heavenly Noble star. 'The cangue and lock of a vow' is a vow made and not repaid, leaving one locked in by it. 'Kin in their seats' is literally relatives seated at the mat, and the book does not explain it. 'A yang line a man, a yin line a woman' says whether the kinsman being worked out is male or female, by the yin or yang of the line.
