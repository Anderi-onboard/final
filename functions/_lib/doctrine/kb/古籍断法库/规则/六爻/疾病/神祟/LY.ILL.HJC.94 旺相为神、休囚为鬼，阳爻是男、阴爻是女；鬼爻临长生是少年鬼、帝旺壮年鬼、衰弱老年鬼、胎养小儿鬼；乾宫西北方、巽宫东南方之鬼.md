# 旺相为神、休囚为鬼，阳爻是男、阴爻是女；鬼爻临长生是少年鬼、帝旺壮年鬼、衰弱老年鬼、胎养小儿鬼；乾宫西北方、巽宫东南方之鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻旺相是神，休囚是鬼；阳爻是男，阴爻是女。鬼爻临长生是少年的鬼，临帝旺是壮年的鬼，临衰弱是老年的鬼，临胎、养是小孩的鬼。乾宫的鬼在西北方，巽宫的鬼在东南方。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line that is strong is a spirit; resting or trapped, it is a ghost. Yang is male and yin female. A Pressure line at Birth is a young person's ghost, at Peak a ghost of the prime of life, at decline an old person's ghost, and at Womb or Nurture a child's ghost. A ghost of the Qian palace lies to the northwest, and a ghost of the Xun palace to the southeast.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3372 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡鬼爻旺相是神，休囚是鬼，阳为神为男，阴为鬼为女。 | 凡是鬼爻旺相就是神，休囚就是鬼；阳爻是神、是男的，阴爻是鬼、是女的。 | Whenever the Pressure line is strong it is a spirit, and resting or trapped it is a ghost; yang is a spirit and male, yin is a ghost and female. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 神和鬼、男和女是一件事还是两件事：旧注分成两条，旺相是神、休囚是鬼看旺衰，阳爻是男、阴爻是女看阴阳；王洪绪把它们连成一条，「阳为神为男，阴为鬼为女」。照旧注，一条旺相的阴爻是一个女的神；照王洪绪，阴爻就是女鬼。另外王洪绪没有旧注按长生、帝旺、衰弱、胎养分年龄的那一段。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether spirit-or-ghost and male-or-female are one matter or two: the old commentary makes two rules, strong or weak deciding spirit or ghost and yang or yin deciding male or female; Wang Hongxu joins them into one, 'yang is a spirit and male, yin is a ghost and female'. On the old commentary a strong yin line is a female spirit; on Wang Hongxu a yin line is a female ghost. Wang Hongxu also lacks the old commentary's passage that gives the age by Birth, Peak, decline and Womb and Nurture. The books do not decide between them.

> [!note] 按
> 「长生」「帝旺」是十二长生里的两位（长生、沐浴、冠带、临官、帝旺、衰、病、死、墓、绝、胎、养）；「衰弱」按字面译作衰。「隅」是角落（卜筮大全篇末生僻字注），「方隅」是方位。
>
> *Note:* Birth and Peak are two of the twelve life stages (Birth, Bath, Cap, Office, Peak, Decline, Sickness, Death, Tomb, Extinction, Womb, Nurture); 'weak' here is rendered as decline. 'Yu' is a corner (the book's glossary), and 'fangyu' means the direction.
