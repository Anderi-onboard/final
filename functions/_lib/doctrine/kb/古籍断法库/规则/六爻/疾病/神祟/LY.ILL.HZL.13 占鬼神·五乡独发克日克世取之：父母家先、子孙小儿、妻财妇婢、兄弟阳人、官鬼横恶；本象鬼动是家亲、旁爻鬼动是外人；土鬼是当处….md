# 占鬼神·五乡独发克日克世取之：父母家先、子孙小儿、妻财妇婢、兄弟阳人、官鬼横恶；本象鬼动是家亲、旁爻鬼动是外人；土鬼是当处灵验之鬼、旺相为神休囚为鬼；《易林》察祸推其鬼处

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 五种六亲独发，克日、克世的取它，各有两层意思：父母是家先，子孙是小儿，妻财是妇人婢女，兄弟是阳人（男人），官鬼是横恶，以上随爻取。本象里的鬼动是家亲，旁爻的鬼动是外人；比如乾卦壬午鬼动是家亲，大有卦己巳鬼动是外人。土鬼是当地灵验的鬼，俗称神：旺相是神，休囚是鬼；动爻克世克日，也可以取来断祟。《易林》说：察祸要推它的鬼在什么地方，再拿身去配六亲，相克招生，就看见祸的端的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When one of the five quarters moves alone, take the one that controls the Day or the Self; each has two senses: Resource is the ancestors of the house, Output the small children, Wealth women and maids, Peer men (yang people), the Pressure line violent evil; the above follow the line. A ghost moving in the own image is the family ancestors, a ghost moving on a side line is an outsider; for example, in Qian a ghost moving on Ren Wu is the family ancestors, and in Dayou a ghost moving on Ji Si is an outsider. An Earth ghost is a ghost efficacious in the place, commonly called a spirit: strong it is a spirit, resting or trapped it is a ghost; a moving line controlling the Self or the Day can also be taken to judge a curse. The Yilin says: to examine a calamity, work out where its ghost is, and then match the Body to the six relations; control brings generation, and the true beginning of the calamity is seen.

> [!note] 按
> 「五乡」指五种六亲（父母、子孙、妻财、兄弟、官鬼）这几个位置，「独发」是只有一爻动，「各有两义」是每一种六亲各有两层意思：一层是它本来的人物，一层是它作为鬼的样子（父母是家先，子孙是小儿，妻财是妇婢，兄弟是阳人，官鬼是横恶）。原文「他官外人」的「官」是「宫」之误，问答里同样，这里按「宫」译。「本象」是本卦本身，「他宫」是别的宫；乾卦的官鬼是午火，「壬午」是乾卦第四爻，鬼在本象里动，所以是家亲；大有卦（离宫）的鬼是亥水，「己巳」是巳火，和大有所属的宫（乾宫）相比是别宫的爻，所以是外人——原文没有把这层推理写出来，这一句我只按字面译。「《易林》」是书名，译作 Yilin。「身」是卦身。「相克招生」是被克的一方带来生机，原文没有解释。这一节和易林补遗的「六亲内外动断是谁的鬼」（LY.ILL.EL.40）是同一类。
>
> *Note:* The 'five quarters' are the five relations (Resource, Output, Wealth, Peer, the Pressure line), 'moving alone' is only one line moving, and 'each has two senses' is that each relation has two layers: the person it originally is, and how it looks as a ghost (Resource the ancestors of the house, Output small children, Wealth women and maids, Peer yang people, the Pressure line violent evil). The 'guan' of 'ta guan wai ren' in the original is a miswriting of 'gong' (palace), and so in the questions, and it is rendered palace here. The 'own image' is the hexagram itself and 'another palace' is a different palace; in the sentence about Qian and Dayou, the original does not spell out the reasoning behind 'Ren Wu' and 'Ji Si' and only the words are rendered here. The 'Yilin' is the title of a book. The 'Body' is the hexagram Body. 'Control brings generation' says that the one controlled brings a chance of life, and the original does not explain it. This section is of the same kind as the Yilin Buyi's 'which of the six relations moving inside or outside tells whose ghost it is' (LY.ILL.EL.40).
