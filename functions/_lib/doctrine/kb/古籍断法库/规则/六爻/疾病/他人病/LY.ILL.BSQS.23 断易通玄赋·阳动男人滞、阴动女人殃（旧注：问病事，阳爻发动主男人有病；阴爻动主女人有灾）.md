# 断易通玄赋·阳动男人滞、阴动女人殃（旧注：问病事，阳爻发动主男人有病；阴爻动主女人有灾）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断易通玄赋里说：阳爻动，男子受阻滞；阴爻动，女子有灾殃。火珠林的旧注讲得明白：问病的事，遇到阳爻发动，主男人有病；阴爻发动，主女人有灾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Duanyi Tongxuan Fu says: when a yang line moves the man is held up; when a yin line moves the woman meets misfortune. The old note in the Huozhu Lin is explicit: in asking about illness, if a yang line moves, the man is ill; if a yin line moves, the woman has calamity.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联在卜筮大全里的旧注只写「阳爻动，主男人唉；阴爻动，主女人灾」，「唉」字照原文，按上下文该是「滞」或「疾」的误字，译文照字面写成「叹息」；火珠林本的旧注则明写「问病事」，所以放在疾病域。虎易的评注认为这两句可能转抄有误，应是「子动男人滞，兄动女人殃」（从夫妻生克上说），那是今人的评注，这里不收。另：这一条讲动爻本身的阴阳，和前面「阴化为阳」「阳化为阴」（LY.ILL.BSQS.15）讲的变爻阴阳不同。断易天机里也有同一联（第 2807 行，无旧注）。
>
> *Note:* In the Bushi Daquan the old note reads only 'a yang line moving means the man sighs; a yin line moving means the woman has calamity'; the character for 'sighs' is kept as in the text, which from the context should be a miswritten 'held up' or 'ill', and the translation renders it literally as 'sighs'; the old note in the Huozhu Lin says outright 'in asking about illness', which is why this is placed in the illness domain. Hu Yi's commentary holds that the two lines may have been miscopied and should read 'Output moves: the man is held up; Peer moves: the woman meets misfortune' (from the control relations of husband and wife); that is a modern commentary and is not quoted here. Note also that this entry is about the yin or yang of the moving line itself, unlike the 'yin turning into yang' and 'yang turning into yin' lines (LY.ILL.BSQS.15) which are about the changed line. The Duanyi Tianji also has the same couplet (line 2807, without an old note).
