# 按六神论病由何得：青龙鬼动因喜处亲友酒席、朱雀因恼怒词讼文章、勾陈因跌磕坟田动作、螣蛇因惊恐思虑妖邪、白虎因哀声丧事兵刀宰杀、玄武因酒色水路冒雨盗失

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼带青龙发动，是因为权势和喜悦，或者是去了亲友办喜事的人家、到了树林芳草的地方、或者叨扰了酒席和簪花——灾由喜处来，或探亲访友、酬酒簪花、游山、居树下、谒贵人，这些地方病由此得。鬼带朱雀，是因为恼怒，是非、词讼和文章，看书、写字、唱歌都是生灾惹祸的缘由——灾由怒气来，或被闲非词讼、或写书修书、或遇喧哗、或逢歌耍、或看火场沾上灾祸。鬼带勾陈发动，病难痊愈，或许到过田边、坟前坟后，或是为别人修造、自己动工，莫非是犯了禁忌牵连上了——灾由跌磕来，或登过坟左墓右、行过地后田前、内外动过工，所以有灾瘴。螣蛇带着鬼爻兴旺，病是惊惶恐吓造成的，或是被七情伤了气血，或是遇上鬼魅作妖精——灾由惊恐、多思虑、忧愁或遇妖邪来。白虎临着官鬼兴旺，或听见邻近有悲声、看见撤席行丧经过、看见兵戈和宰牲——祸起于哀声，邻里的丧事、新朋的撤席、去了孝堂之内、看见刀兵或遇上宰杀，这几样祸都从这里出。玄武是从水路来的，或贪酒色得的灾，或洗浴又遇雨，或受了寒邪病更乘势——玄武临官，灾由酒色、往江河、冒雨、失物、被盗惊起病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost moving with the Azure Dragon comes from power and delight, or from going to the house of a relative or friend holding a happy event, to woods and fragrant grass, or from taking part in wine drinking and putting flowers in the hair; the calamity comes from a place of joy, or from visiting relatives and friends, toasting and wearing flowers, touring the hills, dwelling under trees or calling on a noble, and the illness was contracted in one of these ways. A ghost with the Vermilion Bird comes from vexation and anger, quarrels, lawsuits and writing; reading, writing and singing are all ways of bringing on calamity; the calamity comes from anger, from idle quarrels and lawsuits, from writing or revising books, from meeting uproar, from songs and play, or from watching a fire ground. A ghost moving with Hook Earth makes the illness hard to cure; perhaps he went by fields or in front of or behind a grave, or built for another or did works himself, and surely a taboo is implicated; the calamity comes from a knock or fall, from climbing to the left or right of a grave, walking behind the ground or before the field, or doing works inside or outside, and hence the calamity and miasma. If the Coiling Snake carries a thriving Pressure line, the illness is made by fright and terror, or the seven emotions have harmed the qi and blood, or ghosts and sprites have made demons; the calamity comes from fright, from much brooding, from grief or from meeting demons and evil. The White Tiger sitting on a thriving Pressure line: perhaps he heard weeping nearby, watched a funeral banquet cleared away or a funeral pass, or saw weapons or the slaughter of animals; the misfortune rises from the sound of mourning, a neighbour's funeral, a new friend's cleared table, going into a house of mourning, or seeing weapons or meeting slaughter, and from these several kinds misfortune comes. The Dark Tortoise comes by the water road, or the calamity is got from greed for wine and lust, or he bathed and also met rain, or took in cold evil, and the illness rode further; with the Dark Tortoise on the Pressure line, the calamity comes from wine and lust, going to river or lake, being out in the rain, losing goods or being frightened by theft.

> [!note] 按
> 六神各配一类得病的缘由：青龙是喜、酒席；朱雀是怒气、词讼、文字；勾陈是跌磕、坟田动作；螣蛇是惊恐、思虑；白虎是丧事、兵刃、宰杀；玄武是酒色、水、盗。这和易隐病因·六神（LY.ILL.YY.17）、易冒的六神配病因（LY.ILL.YM.26、YM.30）、黄金策六神临鬼各主一症（LY.ILL.HJC.05）是同一件事，各家细目略有出入；易林补遗把它写成「病是怎么得来的」（去了哪里、遇上了什么事），黄金策写的是症状。「灾瘴」是灾祸和瘴气，「簪花」是把花插在头上。
>
> *Note:* Each of the six spirits is paired with a class of cause of falling ill: the Azure Dragon joy and banquets; the Vermilion Bird anger, lawsuits and writing; Hook Earth knocks, falls and works at graves and fields; the Coiling Snake fright and brooding; the White Tiger mourning, weapons and slaughter; the Dark Tortoise wine, lust, water and theft. This is the same matter as the Yi Yin's six spirits in the causes of illness (LY.ILL.YY.17), the Yi Mao's six spirits in the causes of illness (LY.ILL.YM.26, YM.30) and the Huangjin Ce's 'each of the six spirits on the ghost governs a symptom' (LY.ILL.HJC.05), with details differing among the books; the Yilin Buyi writes it as 'how the illness was contracted' (where he went and what he met), where the Huangjin Ce writes symptoms. 'Calamity and miasma' is misfortune and pestilential air, and 'putting flowers in the hair' is wearing flowers on the head.
