# 子孙空绝必乏调理之肥甘：子孙是药又是酒肉，落空、临死绝或不上卦则病中无肥甘调理；日辰或应爻带子孙生合世爻用爻则有人馈送食物资养

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻既是医药，又是酒肉。子孙落空亡、临在死绝上，或者不上卦，病中一定没有肥甘的东西调养，病也缠缠绵绵难好。如果日辰或者应爻带着子孙，来生、合世爻和用爻，一定有人送礼物、送吃的来补养。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Output line is both medicine and wine and meat. If Output is Void, sits on death or extinction, or is not in the hexagram, the patient surely lacks rich food to build him up during the illness, and the illness also lingers and is hard to cure. If the Day or the Other line carries Output and generates or combines with the Self and Subject lines, someone will surely send gifts or food to nourish him.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6908 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙固为医，又为酒肉。 | 子孙本来是医生，又是酒肉。 | Output is the doctor, and also wine and meat. |
> | 若在空亡，或临死绝，或不上卦，其病必乏肥甘调理，亦缠绵难愈。 | 如果落在空亡，或者临在死绝，或者不上卦，这病一定缺少肥甘的调养，也缠缠绵绵难好。 | If it falls in Void, or sits on death or extinction, or is not in the hexagram, the illness surely lacks the building-up of rich food, and lingers and is hard to cure. |
> | 若日辰应爻上带子孙，生合世爻用爻者，必有馈送礼物资养。 | 如果日辰或者应爻上带着子孙，来生、合世爻和用爻，一定有人送礼物来资助调养。 | If the Day or the Other line carries Output and generates or combines with the Self and Subject lines, there will surely be gifts sent to help nourish him. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2378 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙固为药，又为酒肉，若临死绝，或在空亡，或不上卦，病中必无肥甘调理。 | 子孙本来是药，又是酒肉，如果临在死绝，或者落在空亡，或者不上卦，病中一定没有肥甘的东西调养。 | Output is medicine, and also wine and meat; if it sits on death or extinction, or is Void, or is not in the hexagram, the patient surely has no rich food to build him up during the illness. |
> | 或日辰或应爻带子孙生合用爻者，必有人馈送食物资养。 | 或者日辰、或者应爻带着子孙来生、合用爻的，一定有人送吃的来资助调养。 | If the Day or the Other line carries Output and generates or combines with the Subject line, someone will surely send food to help nourish him. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注说子孙是「医」（医生），王洪绪说是「药」。「肥甘」是油腻甘美的好东西，病中拿来补养。旧注多说了一句「亦缠绵难愈」，王洪绪没有。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary says Output is 'the doctor' and Wang Hongxu says it is 'the medicine'. 'Rich food' (feigan) is fat and sweet good things, used to build a patient up. The old commentary adds 'and it lingers and is hard to cure', which Wang Hongxu does not.
