# 鬼持世是旧愿未酬、鬼伏世下是有口无心、鬼化妻财是斋修不了、变财空亡是有头无尾

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻持世，不是祖传的老规矩，而是先前口头许过愿、没有还；卦里没有鬼、世爻下面却伏着本宫的鬼，也是旧欠的愿，是嘴上答应了却没放在心上，所以招来责罚；鬼爻变出妻财，一定是斋戒修行没有做完，或者欠土煞的愿、破了戒；变成的财落空亡，是许了愿却没有戒口，有头没尾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Pressure line holds the Self position, it is not an old custom but a spoken vow made earlier and not yet paid; if there is no ghost in the hexagram but the ghost of the home palace is hidden beneath the Self line, it too is an old debt, words without the heart in it, and so blame has come; if the Pressure line changes into Wealth, a fast or observance was surely not completed, a debt owed to the earth spirits, or a precept was broken; and if the Wealth it changes into is Void, one made the vow but did not keep the abstinence of the mouth: a head with no tail.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3405 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼爻持世，有旧愿宜酬类。 | 鬼爻持世，是有旧愿应该还的这一类。 | The Pressure line holding the Self position is of the kind where there is an old vow that ought to be paid. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文「持世则未酬旧愿」，王洪绪引作「持世则来酬旧愿」，「未」和「来」形近，按上下文应是「未」；他的注「有旧愿宜酬」也是这个意思。「心斋」按旧注的解释是斋戒修行，「土杀之欠」按字面译作欠土煞的愿。王洪绪的注只有一句，旧注讲了四种情形。
>
> *Note:* Where the verse says 'holding Self, the old vow is not yet paid' (wei chou), Wang Hongxu quotes 'holding Self, the old vow is to be paid' (lai chou); 'wei' and 'lai' look alike and by context 'wei' must be right, and his commentary 'an old vow that ought to be paid' means the same. 'Xinzhai', in the old commentary's explanation, is a fast or observance, and 'a debt owed to the earth-killing' is rendered literally as a debt to the earth spirits. Wang Hongxu's commentary is a single sentence, while the old commentary treats four cases.
