# 卦内无财则饮食不纳：财主饮食，落空吃不下，不上卦不想吃；财动化官鬼病因酒食而得，财临身世或化财则病重也能食

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻管饮食。卦里没有财爻，吃不下东西：财爻落空，吃不下；不上卦，不想吃。财爻动而变成官鬼，这病一定是吃喝来的；财爻临在身爻、世爻上，或者变出财爻，病再重也还能吃。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wealth line governs food and drink. With no Wealth in the hexagram, food and drink are not taken: if the Wealth line falls into Void, nothing goes down; if it is absent from the hexagram, he has no wish to eat. A Wealth line that moves and changes into Pressure means the illness came from drink and food; a Wealth line sitting on the Body or the Self line, or a change into a Wealth line, means that even in a heavy illness he can still eat.

> | 财主饮食，若遇空亡，饮食不纳，若不上卦，不思饮食。 | 财爻管饮食，遇上空亡吃不下，不上卦就不想吃。 | Wealth governs food and drink; if it meets Void, food and drink are not taken, and if it is absent from the hexagram, he has no thought of food or drink. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文的「卦内无财」，旧注分成两种：有财爻但落空（吃不下），和根本不上卦（不想吃）。「身世」是身爻和世爻。旧注的「动化官鬼」和「财临身世」两句，王洪绪没有收。
>
> *Note:* The verse's 'no Wealth in the hexagram' is split by the old commentary into two cases: a Wealth line that is there but Void (food is not taken) and one that is not in the hexagram at all (no wish to eat). 'Shen shi' is the Body line and the Self line. Wang Hongxu does not take the old commentary's two sentences on 'moving and changing into Pressure' and 'Wealth on the Body or Self line'.
