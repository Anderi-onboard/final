# 疾病论·神鬼之由(三)：应鬼克身逢外鬼、官爻生世是连亲；日辰冲发为无主、土位塞兴定有坟；土动死囚牢狱、火兴临没产亡；旺日看四时、休囚寻八节

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里最后几联讲神鬼：应爻的鬼克身，遇到的是外来的鬼；官爻生世爻，是连着亲戚的；被日辰冲动而发出来的，是无主的鬼；土位被堵塞又兴起，一定有坟；土爻动，是死在牢狱里的囚犯；火爻兴旺，是产亡的鬼；只要知道旺的日子，就看四季；细察休囚的日子，就找八节。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The last couplets of the chapter on illness on spirits and ghosts: a ghost on the Other line controlling the Body is an outside ghost; a Pressure line generating the Self line is of kin connections; one stirred out by the Day's clash is a ghost without an owner; with the Earth place stopped up and rising, there is surely a grave; an Earth line moving is one who died as a prisoner in jail; a Fire line rising is one who died in childbirth; know the strong days and look to the four seasons, examine the resting and confined closely and seek the eight festivals.

> [!note] 按
> 「应鬼克身逢外鬼」的「应鬼」是应爻上的鬼，「外鬼」是外来的鬼。「官爻生世是连亲」的「连亲」是连着亲戚的鬼。「日辰冲发为无主」是说被日辰冲动、自己本来不动的鬼，是没有主人的鬼（游魂）。「土位塞兴」「火兴临没产亡」两处文意不全，「塞」按字面是堵塞，「临没」按字面是快要死，译文照字面，没有补。最后两句「旺日看四时，休囚寻八节」是说找神鬼作祟的时间：旺的日子看四季，休囚的日子找八节（立春、春分等八个节气）。
>
> *Note:* 'A ghost on the Other line controlling the Body: an outside ghost' is a ghost on the Other line, and the 'outside ghost' one from elsewhere. 'Pressure generating the Self line is kin connections' is a ghost connected to relatives. 'Stirred out by the Day's clash: without an owner' is a ghost that was not itself moving and is set moving by the Day's clash, a ghost with no master (a wandering soul). In 'the Earth place stopped up and rising' and 'Fire rising and on the point of death: a death in childbirth' the sense is incomplete; 'stopped up' is literally blocked and 'on the point of death' literally about to die, and the translation keeps to the letter without filling in. The last two lines, 'know the strong days and look to the four seasons, examine the resting and confined and seek the eight festivals', are about finding the time of the haunting: strong days by the four seasons, resting days by the eight festivals (the start of spring, the spring equinox and so on).
