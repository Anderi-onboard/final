# 总断千金赋·月建乃万卜之提纲（旧注：月建中有乃是真有；书中例：五月占小儿病得大过，卦中无子孙而月建午火正旺为主象，不断其死亡）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「月建是万卜的总纲，怎么可以帮着凶爻作恶。」旧注：月建是龙德之神，所以占卜以它为总纲；要详细看它有没有刑冲克害、生扶拱合，和世爻、身、主象有没有牵涉，就看出吉凶；月建里有的，才是真有，如果是坏事，就是真正的坏事。书中例：五月里占小孩的病，得到大过卦，卦里没有子孙爻，但月建是午火，正旺相，作为主象，所以不断他死亡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'The Month is the guiding thread of every divination: how could it help the tyrant do harm?' The old commentary: the Month is the spirit of Dragon Virtue, and so a divination takes it as its guiding thread; look closely at whether it has punishment, clash, control or harm, and generation, support, flanking or combination, and whether it bears on the Self, the Body or the principal image, and good and ill show; what the Month has is truly there, and if it is a bad matter it is truly a bad matter. A case in the book: in the fifth month a child's illness was divined and the Daguo hexagram obtained; the hexagram had no Output line, but the Month was Wu Fire, at its peak, serving as the principal image, and so death was not judged.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5140 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且如五月内，占小儿病，得大过卦。 | 比如五月里，占小孩的病，得到大过卦。 | For example, in the fifth month, divining a child's illness, one obtains the Daguo hexagram. |
> | 卦中无子孙爻，而月建午火，正旺为主象，不断其死亡。 | 卦里没有子孙爻，但月建是午火，正旺相，作为主象，不断他死亡。 | The hexagram has no Output line, but the Month is Wu Fire, at its peak, serving as the principal image, and one does not judge death. |
> | 余仿此。 | 其余照这样类推。 | The rest follow this. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这条的意思是：卦里没有用神（占小孩的病，用神是子孙爻），可是当月的月建正是用神那个五行、又正旺，就算数，不能因为卦里没有子孙爻就断死。「月建中有，乃是真有」是这一条的总括。「龙德」是月建的别名（月建是龙德之神）。「助桀为虐」是帮着暴君作恶，这里指月建帮着凶爻，使凶更凶（旧注接着说「忌爻旺动而又生扶之，则是助桀为虐」）。书中例是旧注自己举的，没有写明后来的结果。
>
> *Note:* The sense of this entry: when the hexagram has no Subject (in divining a child's illness, the Subject is the Output line) but the Month branch of that month is exactly the Subject's element and at its peak, it counts, and one cannot judge death merely because the hexagram has no Output line. 'What the Month has is truly there' is the summing-up. 'Dragon Virtue' is another name for the Month (the Month is the spirit of Dragon Virtue). 'Help the tyrant do harm' is to help a tyrant do evil, and here means the Month helping an ominous line to make it more ominous (the old commentary goes on: 'if the Adversary is strong and moving and is also supported, that is helping the tyrant do harm'). The case is the old commentary's own, and the book does not say how it turned out.
