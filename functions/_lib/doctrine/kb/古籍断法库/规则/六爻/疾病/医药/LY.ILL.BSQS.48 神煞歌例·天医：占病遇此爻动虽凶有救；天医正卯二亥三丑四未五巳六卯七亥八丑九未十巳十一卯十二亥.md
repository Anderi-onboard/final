# 神煞歌例·天医：占病遇此爻动虽凶有救；天医正卯二亥三丑四未五巳六卯七亥八丑九未十巳十一卯十二亥

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神煞歌例说：占病遇到天医这一爻，发动虽然凶，也有救。天医的位置按月取：正月卯、二月亥、三月丑、四月未、五月巳、六月卯、七月亥、八月丑、九月未、十月巳、十一月卯、十二月亥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verses on spirit-stars say: in divining illness, meeting the Heavenly Physician line, even if it moves and is ominous there is rescue. The Heavenly Physician is placed by month: Mao in the first, Hai in the second, Chou in the third, Wei in the fourth, Si in the fifth, Mao in the sixth, Hai in the seventh, Chou in the eighth, Wei in the ninth, Si in the tenth, Mao in the eleventh and Hai in the twelfth.

> [!note] 按
> 这一首的十二个月位置和断易天机的天医月表一字不差（LY.ILL.DYTJ.30、LY.ILL.DYTJ.34）。结论的说法两本不同：断易天机说「病人有此爻上卦，必主名医下药有效」，卜筮全书说「占病遇此爻，动虽凶有救」，两处都是吉，只是卜筮全书连「动而为凶」时也说有救。「动虽凶有救」按字面读是这一爻发动虽然凶（或：遇到它发动虽有凶象），仍然有救，歌里没有细讲。
>
> *Note:* The positions for the twelve months are word for word those of the monthly table of the Heavenly Physician in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.30, LY.ILL.DYTJ.34). The conclusion is worded differently in the two books: the Duanyi Tianji says 'a sick person who has this line in the hexagram will surely have a famous doctor whose medicine is effective', the Bushi Quanshu says 'in divining illness, meeting this line, even if it moves and is ominous there is rescue'; both are favourable, only the Bushi Quanshu says there is rescue even when it moves and is ominous. 'Even if it moves and is ominous there is rescue' is read to the letter as: even though this line moves ominously (or: even though meeting it moving has an ominous look), there is still rescue; the verse goes no further.
