# 六亲用钤·疾病：占双亲父母为用爻宜旺不宜空；占子孙父母为恶杀宜静不宜动动则必死；子孙占病为医药当权发动病即安卦无其神服药无效；官鬼占兄弟为克杀旺相发动病必危；官鬼占身命逢凶必丧亡疾病

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻的用处：占双亲的疾病，父母爻是用爻，宜旺不宜空，空就必危；占子孙，父母爻是恶杀，宜静不宜动，动就必死。子孙爻的用处：占病，子孙是医药，当权发动，病就安；卦里没有它，服药无效；占小孩和奴仆，子孙是用爻，不落空亡，终究没有大害。官鬼爻的用处：占兄弟的病，官鬼是克杀，旺相发动，病必危险；占身命，遇凶必定丧亡、疾病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The uses of the Resource line: divining the illness of the parents, the Resource line is the Subject line, and it should be strong and not Void, and Void means peril; divining children, the Resource line is an evil killing, and it should be still and not move, and moving means death. The uses of the Output line: divining illness, Output is the medicine, and if it holds power and moves the illness is at ease; if it is not in the hexagram, medicine has no effect; divining small children and servants, Output is the Subject line, and if it does not fall Void there is in the end no great harm. The uses of the Pressure line: divining a brother's illness, the Pressure line is the controlling killing, and if strong and moving the illness is surely dangerous; divining the person's fortune, meeting ill surely means death and illness.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是《断易天机》「六亲用钤」里的几条，每一条是「占某事：某六亲是什么」的句式。头一条在「父母用」里：占双亲的病，父母爻就是用爻（病人），所以宜旺不宜空；占子孙（小孩）的病，父母爻（生我者）反而成了「恶杀」，动了就死。第二条在「子孙用」里：子孙爻在占病里是医药（和天玄赋的用爻表 LY.ILL.TXF.01 里「父占子看子」不同，那里是占子孙的病看子孙，这里说的是占病时子孙是医药），当权发动病就安，卦里没有子孙，服药无效。第三条在「官鬼用」里：占兄弟的病，官鬼是「克杀」（克兄弟的杀），旺相发动病必危。第四条是「官鬼用」里「占身命」一句后面括号里的注：遇吉进禄加官，逢凶丧亡疾病。原文里每条前面的「占某」是说占什么事，句里的「为」是「是」，我加在括号里的是补出的主语，不是原文有的词。「占兄弟」的「兄弟」是兄弟这个亲人，不是兄弟爻。
>
> *Note:* This section takes several entries from the 'uses of the six relations' of the Duanyi Tianji, each in the pattern 'divining such a matter: what such a relation is'. The first is in the 'uses of Resource': divining the illness of the parents, the Resource line is the Subject line (the sick person), so it should be strong and not Void; divining the illness of children, the Resource line (what generates me) becomes an 'evil killing' instead, and if it moves he dies. The second is in the 'uses of Output': the Output line in divining illness is the medicine (different from the table of the Subject line in the Tianxuan Fu, LY.ILL.TXF.01, 'a parent divining a child looks at Output', which is divining a child's illness by Output, while here it is that in divining illness Output is the medicine), and if it holds power and moves the illness is at ease, and if the hexagram has no Output taking medicine has no effect. The third is in the 'uses of the Pressure line': divining a brother's illness, the Pressure line is the 'controlling killing' (the killing that controls the brother), and if strong and moving the illness is surely dangerous. The fourth is the note in brackets after 'divining the person's fortune' in the 'uses of the Pressure line': meeting luck brings salary and office added, meeting ill means death and illness. In the original the 'divining such-and-such' before each entry says what matter is divined, and the 'wei' in the lines is 'is'; what I put in brackets are subjects supplied, not words in the original. The 'brother' of 'divining a brother' is a brother as a relative, not the Peer line.
