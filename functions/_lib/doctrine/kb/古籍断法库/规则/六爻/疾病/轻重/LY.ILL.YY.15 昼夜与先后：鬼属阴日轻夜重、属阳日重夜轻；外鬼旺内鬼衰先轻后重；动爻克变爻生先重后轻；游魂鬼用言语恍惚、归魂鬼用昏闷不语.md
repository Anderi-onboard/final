# 昼夜与先后：鬼属阴日轻夜重、属阳日重夜轻；外鬼旺内鬼衰先轻后重；动爻克变爻生先重后轻；游魂鬼用言语恍惚、归魂鬼用昏闷不语

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼属阴的，白天轻、夜里重；鬼属阳的，白天重、夜里轻。外卦的鬼旺、内卦的鬼衰，先轻后重；动爻克、变爻生，先重后轻。游魂卦里鬼做用爻，说话恍惚；归魂卦里鬼做用爻，昏闷不说话。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the ghost is yin, the illness is lighter by day and heavier by night; if yang, heavier by day and lighter by night. If the outer ghost is strong and the inner weak: light at first, then heavy; if the moving line controls and the changed line generates: heavy at first, then light. A ghost taken as the Subject line in a Wandering Soul hexagram: speech is confused; in a Returning Soul hexagram: drowsy, oppressed and silent.

> [!note] 按
> 「游魂」「归魂」是八宫里的两个卦（每宫第七、第八卦）。鬼属阴在内卦、属阳在外卦（见 LY.ILL.YY.11），所以「鬼属阴日轻夜重」和黄金策的「鬼在内卦夜重、在外卦日重」（LY.ILL.HJC.12）是同一个意思；「动爻克而变爻生，先重后轻」和黄金策「动生变克，暮热朝凉」（LY.ILL.HJC.13）相对应，方向一致。
>
> *Note:* 'Wandering Soul' and 'Returning Soul' are two hexagrams in each of the eight palaces (the seventh and eighth of each). A ghost is yin in the inner trigram and yang in the outer (see LY.ILL.YY.11), so 'a yin ghost is lighter by day and heavier by night' means the same as the Huangjin Ce's 'a ghost in the inner trigram is heavy at night, in the outer trigram heavy by day' (LY.ILL.HJC.12); and 'the moving line controls and the changed line generates: heavy first, then light' corresponds to the Huangjin Ce's 'the moving line generates and the changed line controls: warm by evening, cool by morning' (LY.ILL.HJC.13), in the same direction.
