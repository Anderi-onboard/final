# 延医于子孙之方、治病以应爻为定：子孙旺则往子孙所在方位请（午火请南方医）；子孙休囚空破则看应爻旺相生合用神，往应爻之方请；有一爻独发生用神，往此方请，姓名按五行——水点水、火象火、木草头木旁、土有土、金有金

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 请医生要往子孙所在的方位去请，治病则以应爻来定。卦里子孙爻旺相的，从子孙所在的方位请医生，比如午火子孙，旺相或发动，就请南方的医生。子孙休囚、空、破的，再看应爻旺不旺，如果应爻旺相、又生合用神，就往应爻所在的方位请，比如应爻临申金，名医一定在西南。卦里有一个爻单独发动、而且生用神的，就从这个爻的方位请，并用五行来定医生的姓名：水爻动，姓名带三点水；火爻动，姓名像火；木爻动，带草字头、木字旁；土爻动，姓名里有土；金爻动，姓名里有金。比如占妻子的病，妻爻属水，得申金发动来生它，就请姓钱的医生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Call the doctor from the direction of Output; for treatment, the Other line decides. If the Output line in the hexagram is strong, call the doctor from the direction of Output; for example, with Wu Fire Output, strong or moving, call a doctor from the south. If Output is resting, trapped, Void or broken, look at whether the Other line is strong; if the Other line is strong and generates or combines with the Subject, call from the direction of the Other line; for example, with the Other line on Shen Metal, a famous doctor is surely in the south-west. If one line in the hexagram moves alone and generates the Subject, call from the direction of that line, and fix the doctor's name by the five phases: a Water line moving, a name with the water radical; Fire, a name resembling fire; Wood, a name with the grass or wood radical; Earth, a name with earth in it; Metal, a name with metal in it. For example, in divining a wife's illness, if the wife's line is Water and Shen Metal moves and generates it, call a doctor with the surname Qian.

> [!note] 按
> 书中例是 LY.ILL.ZS.18 里那个孩子的后几个卦：第二卦（临之师）之后，父母爻巳火动克子孙，说北方的医生可治；痘症变化后再占（解之归妹），子日冲动午火子孙、寅木生它，姓杨的医生名字里有木，野鹤说就是他。「古以子孙爻为医人」一句，按上下文「医人」应是「医药」的意思（下一句讲医药），译文按字面。黄金策的对应条目是 LY.ILL.HJC.77（验福德以迎医）。
>
> *Note:* The case is the later hexagrams of the child of LY.ILL.ZS.18: after the second hexagram (Lin into Shi), the Si Fire Resource line moves and controls Output, and a doctor from the north can treat him; after the pox changed he divined again (Xie into Guimei), the Zi day clashed Wu Fire Output into motion and Yin Wood generated it, and the Yang doctor's name has wood in it, so the Old Man said it was he. In the phrase 'the ancients took the Output line as the doctor', 'doctor' is from the context probably meant as 'medicine' (the next words speak of medicine), and the translation follows the letter. The matching entry of the Huangjin Ce is LY.ILL.HJC.77 (examine Output to welcome a doctor).
