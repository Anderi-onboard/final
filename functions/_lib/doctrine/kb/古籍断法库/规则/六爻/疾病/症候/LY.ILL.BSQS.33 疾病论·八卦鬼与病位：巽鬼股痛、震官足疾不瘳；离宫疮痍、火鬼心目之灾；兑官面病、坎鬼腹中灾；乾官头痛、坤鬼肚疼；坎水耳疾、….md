# 疾病论·八卦鬼与病位：巽鬼股痛、震官足疾不瘳；离宫疮痍、火鬼心目之灾；兑官面病、坎鬼腹中灾；乾官头痛、坤鬼肚疼；坎水耳疾、艮土手灾

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里按八卦配病位：巽宫鬼爻兴旺，大腿痛；震宫官鬼旺动，脚病不愈；离宫旺相、内卦发动，是疮疡一类的病；火鬼休囚、外卦交重，是心和眼睛的灾；兑宫官鬼发动，是面部的病；坎宫鬼爻交重，是腹中的灾；乾宫官鬼旺相，是头痛；坤宫鬼爻兴旺，是肚子疼；坎卦水爻兴旺，只因为耳病；艮宫土爻发动，一定是手的灾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness matches the trigrams to the place of illness: the ghost of Xun thriving, pain in the thigh; the Pressure line of Zhen strong and moving, a disease of the foot that does not heal; Li strong and its inner lines moving, a disease of sores and wounds; a Fire ghost resting or confined with the outer lines moving, trouble of the heart and eyes; the Pressure line of Dui moving, illness of the face; a Kan ghost moving, trouble within the belly; the Pressure line of Qian strong, headache; the Kun ghost thriving, belly pain; the Water line of Kan rising, only an ear disease; the Earth line of Gen moving, sure to be trouble of the hand.

> [!warning] 打架
> 八卦各管身体哪一处，几处说法不同。疾病论这一段是「乾头、坤肚腹、坎耳（另一联又说腹中）、艮手、震足、巽股、离疮痍和心目、兑面」；卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.12）是「乾头、坤腹、坎耳、离目、兑口、艮手、震足、巽股」，和这一段在巽股、艮手上一致；孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）是巽肠、艮手；阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.02）是巽手、艮鼻。不同的地方有三处：兑，这里是面，别处是口；坎，这一段本身就有两说，一联说耳、一联说腹中；离，这里多了疮痍和心，别处只有目。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books differ on which part of the body each trigram governs. This passage of the chapter on illness gives 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ear (and in another couplet the inside of the belly), Gen the hand, Zhen the foot, Xun the thigh, Li sores and heart and eyes, Dui the face'. The Bushi Yuangui passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.12) gives 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ear, Li the eye, Dui the mouth, Gen the hand, Zhen the foot, Xun the thigh', which agrees with this passage on Xun the thigh and Gen the hand; Sun Bin's verse (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) has Xun the intestines and Gen the hand; the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.02) has Xun the hand and Gen the nose. There are three places of difference: Dui is the face here and the mouth elsewhere; Kan has two readings within this one passage, the ear in one couplet and the inside of the belly in another; and Li has sores and the heart added here, where elsewhere there is only the eye. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段里的「官」是官鬼的简称（「兑官」「乾官」「震官」），「鬼」同指鬼爻。「乾官旺相因头痛」等句的主语是某一宫里的官鬼爻。「内爻发动」「外象交重」是内卦、外卦发动。「疮痍」是疮疡创伤。坎在这一段里出现两次，一次说腹中灾，一次说耳疾，书里没有解释哪一种对。
>
> *Note:* In this passage 'official' is a short form of Pressure ('the Dui official', 'the Qian official', 'the Zhen official'), and 'ghost' likewise means the ghost line. The subject of lines such as 'the Qian official strong: from headache' is the Pressure line in that palace. 'Inner lines moving' and 'outer image moving' are the inner and outer trigrams moving. 'Sores and wounds' means ulcers and injuries. Kan appears twice in this passage, once as trouble within the belly and once as an ear disease, and the book does not say which is right.
