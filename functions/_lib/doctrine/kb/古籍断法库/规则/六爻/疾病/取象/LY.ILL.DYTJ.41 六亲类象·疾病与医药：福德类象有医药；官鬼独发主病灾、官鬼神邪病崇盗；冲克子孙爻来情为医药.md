# 六亲类象·疾病与医药：福德类象有医药；官鬼独发主病灾、官鬼神邪病崇盗；冲克子孙爻来情为医药

六爻 · 疾病 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 福德（子孙）所主的类象里，有缉捕、医药、闲人、鱼贩。官鬼是鬼爻，独发主病灾，是损失的兆；官鬼是神、邪、病、祟、盗。来情看冲克哪一爻：冲克子孙爻，是冲福瑞的物件和医药。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Among the images governed by Output (Fortune-and-Virtue) are arrests, medicine, idlers and fishmongers. The Pressure line is the ghost line, and moving alone it means illness and calamity, a sign of loss; the Pressure line is gods, evil, illness, curses and thieves. The reason for the divination is looked at by which line is clashed or controlled: a clash on the Output line is a clash with lucky things and medicine.

> [!note] 按
> 这几处是《断易天机》里六亲各管哪些事物（类象）的歌，这里只收说到病或药的：子孙类里「缉捕医药闲鱼侩」有医药；官鬼类里头一行的括号说「独发主病灾」，歌里说「官鬼神邪病崇盗」；「来情章」里「冲克子孙爻：冲福瑞物及医药」是说占的人来占的缘由是冲克了子孙爻，他问的就是福瑞的东西和医药。「官鬼类」的括号（为鬼爻，独发主病灾，损失之兆）是原文里加在标题后面的说明。「闲鱼侩」的「鱼侩」是鱼贩（牙侩），字面译。这几句的意思和前面「子孙为医药、官鬼为病」的说法（LY.ILL.DYTJ.39、LY.ILL.HZL.06）是一回事。
>
> *Note:* These are songs in the Duanyi Tianji about which things (images) each of the six relations governs, and only the lines that speak of illness or medicine are taken: under Output 'arrests, medicine, idlers and fishmongers' has medicine; the first line of the Pressure line's section has in brackets 'moving alone it means illness and calamity', and the song has 'the Pressure line is gods, evil, illness, curses and thieves'; and in the 'chapter on the reason for the divination' 'a clash on the Output line: a clash with lucky things and medicine' says that the reason the person has come to divine is that the Output line has been clashed and controlled, and what he asks about is lucky things and medicine. The bracket in the Pressure line's section (the ghost line, moving alone it means illness and calamity, a sign of loss) is a note added after the heading in the original. The 'yu kuai' of 'idlers and fishmongers' is a fish broker, rendered literally. The sense of these lines is the same as the earlier sayings 'Output is the medicine and the Pressure line is the illness' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.39, LY.ILL.HZL.06).
