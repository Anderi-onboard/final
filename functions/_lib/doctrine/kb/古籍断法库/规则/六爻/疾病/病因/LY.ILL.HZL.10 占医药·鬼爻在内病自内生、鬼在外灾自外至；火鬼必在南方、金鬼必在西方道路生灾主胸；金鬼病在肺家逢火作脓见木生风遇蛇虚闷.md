# 占医药·鬼爻在内病自内生、鬼在外灾自外至；火鬼必在南方、金鬼必在西方道路生灾主胸；金鬼病在肺家逢火作脓见木生风遇蛇虚闷

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻在内，病从里面生；鬼在外，灾从外面来。火鬼必在南方；金鬼必在西方，路上生灾，又主胸；金鬼则病在肺，逢火成脓，见木生风，遇螣蛇则虚闷。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line inside, the illness arises from within; the ghost outside, the calamity comes from outside. A Fire ghost must be in the south; a Metal ghost must be in the west, calamity arising on the road, and it governs the chest as well; a Metal ghost then has the illness in the lungs, meeting Fire it makes pus, seeing Wood it breeds wind, and meeting the Snake it is a deficient oppression.

> [!note] 按
> 「鬼爻在内，病自内生，鬼又在外，灾自外至」是内外分病的来处，和易林补遗「何处得病」（LY.ILL.EL.03）、天玄赋「六兽临官当分内外」（LY.ILL.TXF.09）是同一类说法。「遇蛇虚闷」的「蛇」是螣蛇；「又为主胸」一句读不确定，按「又主胸」译。「金鬼必在西方，道路生灾」是拿五行配方位（金在西）、又配「路上」取象。
>
> *Note:* 'The Pressure line inside, the illness arises from within; the ghost outside, the calamity comes from outside' is the division of where the illness comes from by inside and outside, of the same kind as the Yilin Buyi's 'where the illness was caught' (LY.ILL.EL.03) and the Tianxuan Fu's 'the six beasts on the official: tell inside from outside' (LY.ILL.TXF.09). The 'snake' of 'meeting the Snake' is the Coiling Snake; the line 'wei zhu xiong' (and it governs the chest) is not certain and is rendered 'and it governs the chest as well'. 'A Metal ghost must be in the west, calamity arising on the road' matches the phase to a quarter (Metal in the west) and takes the image of the road.
