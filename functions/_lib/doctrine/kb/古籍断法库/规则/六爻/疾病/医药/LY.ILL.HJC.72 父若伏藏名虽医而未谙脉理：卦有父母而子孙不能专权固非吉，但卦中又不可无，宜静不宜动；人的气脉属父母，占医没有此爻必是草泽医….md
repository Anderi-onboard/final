# 父若伏藏名虽医而未谙脉理：卦有父母而子孙不能专权固非吉，但卦中又不可无，宜静不宜动；人的气脉属父母，占医没有此爻必是草泽医人，用药凭意度

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里父母爻动，子孙就不能专权，本来不吉；但卦里又不能没有父母爻，要静不要动。因为人的气脉都属父母。所以占医生，如果没有这一爻，这个医生一定是乡野草泽的医人：虽然会用药，脉理却不明白，只能凭猜测。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Resource line moves in the hexagram, Output cannot hold sole power, which is in itself not lucky; yet the hexagram cannot be without the Resource line, and it should be still, not moving. For a person's breath and pulse all belong to Resource. So in divining a doctor, if this line is missing, he is surely a rustic country doctor: though he can use medicine, he does not understand the pulse and goes by guesswork.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文写「父若伏藏」，两家的注讲的都是卦里没有父母爻。王洪绪引的经文作「父基伏藏」，大概是「父若」之误。「气脉」是气血和脉象，「草泽医人」是乡野民间的医生。
>
> *Note:* The verse reads 'if Resource lies hidden' and both commentaries speak of the Resource line being missing from the hexagram. Wang Hongxu quotes the verse as 'Resource base hidden', which is probably a slip for 'if Resource'. 'Breath and pulse' is the qi and blood and the pulse image, and a 'country doctor' is a doctor of the countryside and the common people.
