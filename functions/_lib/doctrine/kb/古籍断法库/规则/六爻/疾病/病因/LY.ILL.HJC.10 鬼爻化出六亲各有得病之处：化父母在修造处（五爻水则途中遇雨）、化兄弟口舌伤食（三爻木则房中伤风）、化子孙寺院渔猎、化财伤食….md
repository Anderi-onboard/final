# 鬼爻化出六亲各有得病之处：化父母在修造处（五爻水则途中遇雨）、化兄弟口舌伤食（三爻木则房中伤风）、化子孙寺院渔猎、化财伤食妻奴买卖

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻化出哪个六亲，就是在哪里、怎么得的病：化出父母，是在修造的地方得病，在五爻又属水，是在路上淋了雨；化出兄弟，是口舌呕气或者伤食，在三爻又属木，是在房里脱衣露体着了风寒；化出子孙，是在僧道寺院，或者打鱼打猎游戏时得的；化出财爻，多是伤食，或者因为妻子奴仆、买卖交易得病。六亲化出鬼爻，也照这样断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Which relative the Pressure line changes into shows where and how the illness was got: changing into Resource, it was got at a place of repair or building, and if it is on the fifth line and belongs to Water, it was got braving rain on the road; changing into Peer, it is from quarrels and vexation or harm by food, and if on the third line and Wood, from undressing and baring the body in the room and catching wind and cold; changing into Output, it was got at a temple of monks and Daoists, or while fishing, hunting and at play; changing into Wealth, it is mostly harm by food, or through wife or servants, or through buying and selling. Where a relative line changes into Pressure, judge in the same way.
