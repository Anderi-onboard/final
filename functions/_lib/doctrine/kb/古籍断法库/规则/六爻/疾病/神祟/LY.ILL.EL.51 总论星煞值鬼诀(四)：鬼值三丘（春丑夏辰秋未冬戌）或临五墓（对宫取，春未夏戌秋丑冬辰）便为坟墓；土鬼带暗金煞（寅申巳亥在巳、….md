# 总论星煞值鬼诀(四)：鬼值三丘（春丑夏辰秋未冬戌）或临五墓（对宫取，春未夏戌秋丑冬辰）便为坟墓；土鬼带暗金煞（寅申巳亥在巳、子午卯酉在酉、辰戌丑未在丑）或值羊刃是产亡带血之鬼；冲对年庚为撞命（天对甲庚乙辛丙壬丁癸、地冲六冲；天对且地冲是真撞命，只地冲是傍撞命，只天对不冲不是）；鬼值年建当言太岁

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼值三丘，或者临着五墓，就是坟墓（三丘：春丑、夏辰、秋未、三冬逢戌；五墓从三丘的对宫取，病人做福也难留）。土鬼带着暗金煞，或者值着羊刃星，是难产而亡、带血的鬼（暗金煞：寅、申、巳、亥的落在巳，子、午、卯、酉的落在酉，辰、戌、丑、未的落在丑；暗金对产妇最难当）。冲到病人的生年（年庚）就叫撞命：撞有天对和地冲的分别，天对是甲庚、乙辛、丙壬、丁癸，地冲是子午、丑未、寅申、卯酉、辰戌、巳亥；天对又地冲是真撞命（比如病人丙子年生，卦里壬午鬼动），只有地冲而天干大不对的（比如庚午鬼）是傍撞命，只有天对而地不冲的不算撞命；鬼值年建，就说是太岁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost on the Three Mounds, or on the Five Tombs, is then a grave (Three Mounds: spring Chou, summer Chen, autumn Wei, the three winter months meeting Xu; the Five Tombs are taken from the opposite palace of the Three Mounds, so that even when the sick person performs blessing rites he can hardly be kept). An Earth ghost carrying the Hidden Metal killing, or sitting on the Goat Blade star, is the bloody ghost of one who died in childbirth (Hidden Metal killing: for Yin, Shen, Si and Hai it falls at Si; for Zi, Wu, Mao and You at You; for Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei at Chou; Hidden Metal is the hardest for a woman in childbirth to bear). A clash on the year of birth (nian geng) of the sick person is called a collision with life: collision has the distinction of heaven-opposition and earth-clash; heaven-opposition is Jia-Geng, Yi-Xin, Bing-Ren and Ding-Gui, and earth-clash is Zi-Wu, Chou-Wei, Yin-Shen, Mao-You, Chen-Xu and Si-Hai; heaven-opposition together with earth-clash is a true collision with life (for example, the sick person was born in the Bingzi year and a Renwu ghost moves in the hexagram); earth-clash with the stems greatly unmatched (for example a Gengwu ghost) is a side collision with life; heaven-opposition without earth-clash is not a collision with life; if the ghost sits on the year's branch, one says it is on Tai Sui.

> [!note] 按
> 三丘、五墓的口诀，卜筮大全的神煞篇里有一模一样的一首（「春丑夏辰秋即未，三冬逢戌是三丘；却与五墓对宫取，病人作福也难留」），断易天机的注释引鬼谷子的五墓杀例是「春未、夏戌、秋丑、冬辰」，正是三丘的对宫，三处一致；周易尚占的四时神杀表也是三丘春丑夏辰秋未冬戌、五墓春未夏戌秋丑冬辰。原文「三冬逢戍」的「戍」是「戌」的误刻，按戌译。暗金煞的口诀没有说前面的寅申巳亥是什么的支（日、月还是年），原文只写「寅申巳亥巳来防」，这里照抄、不补；「羊刃已其在前」按「羊刃已经写在前面」译；「层口是产亡带血之鬼也」的「层口」文字含混，这里译出后面的意思，并在译文里标明这里原文含混。撞命这一段：「年庚」是生年干支，「撞对」是冲或对着它；天对是天干相冲（甲庚、乙辛、丙壬、丁癸），地冲是地支相冲；例子里病人丙子年生、鬼是壬午——壬对丙是天对、午冲子是地冲，两样都有，所以是真撞命；庚午鬼和丙子只有午冲子（庚不对丙），所以是傍撞命；只有天干相对、地支不冲的，不算撞命。「鬼值年建当言太岁」是说鬼落在当年地支上，就当太岁论（不再按撞命论）。与黄金策「太岁鬼临是祖传旧例」（LY.ILL.HJC.109）是同一个落点。 这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* The verse of the Three Mounds and Five Tombs is found word for word in the chapter on killings of the Buyi Daquan ('spring Chou, summer Chen, autumn Wei, the three winter months meet Xu are the Three Mounds; then the Five Tombs are taken from the opposite palace, and even if the sick person performs blessing rites he can hardly be kept'); the commentary in the Duanyi Tianji quotes the rule of the Five Tombs killing from Guiguzi as 'spring Wei, summer Xu, autumn Chou, winter Chen', which is exactly the opposite palace of the Three Mounds, so the three agree; the Zhouyi Shangzhan's table of seasonal killings also has the Three Mounds at spring Chou, summer Chen, autumn Wei, winter Xu and the Five Tombs at spring Wei, summer Xu, autumn Chou, winter Chen. The 'xu' in the original 'the three winter months meet Xu' is a miscut of 'xu' (the branch Xu), and is translated as Xu. The verse of the Hidden Metal killing does not say what branch the preceding Yin, Shen, Si and Hai are (of the day, the month or the year), the original only writing 'for Yin, Shen, Si, Hai, guard against Si', and it is copied as written and not supplied; 'the Goat Blade has already been set out' translates a garbled 'yang ren yi qi zai qian'; the 'ceng kou' in 'ceng kou is the bloody ghost of one who died in childbirth' is garbled, and the meaning of what follows is given and the translation marks that the original is garbled here. The collision with life: 'nian geng' is the stem-branch of the birth year, and 'zhuang dui' is a clash with or opposition to it; heaven-opposition is a clash of stems (Jia-Geng, Yi-Xin, Bing-Ren, Ding-Gui) and earth-clash a clash of branches; in the example the sick person was born in the Bingzi year and the ghost is Renwu: Ren opposes Bing (heaven-opposition) and Wu clashes Zi (earth-clash), so both are present and it is a true collision; the Gengwu ghost and Bingzi have only Wu clashing Zi (Geng does not oppose Bing), so it is a side collision; where the stems are opposed but the branches do not clash, it does not count as a collision. 'A ghost on the year's branch is to be called Tai Sui' means that a ghost falling on the branch of the current year is judged as Tai Sui (and no longer as a collision with life). It comes to the same point as the Huangjin Ce's 'the ghost on Tai Sui is an old family custom' (LY.ILL.HJC.109). Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
