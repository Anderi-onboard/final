# 应衰世旺病家富而医贫：世是病家、应是医家，相合相生非亲即友，相冲相克亲亦不往来；应旺世衰病家贫医必富

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻是病人一家，应爻是医生一家。世应相合相生，不是亲戚就是朋友；相冲相克，亲戚也不往来。应旺世衰，病人一家贫困，医生一定富；应衰世旺，反过来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line is the patient's family and the Other line the doctor's. If they combine and generate each other, the two are kin or friends; if they clash and control each other, even kin do not visit. If the Other line is strong and the Self line weak, the patient's family is poor and the doctor surely rich; if the Other line is weak and the Self line strong, judge the reverse.
