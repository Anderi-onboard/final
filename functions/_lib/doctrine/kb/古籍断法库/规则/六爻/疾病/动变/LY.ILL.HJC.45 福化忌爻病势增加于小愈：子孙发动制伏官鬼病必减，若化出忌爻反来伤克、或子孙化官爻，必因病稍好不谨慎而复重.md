# 福化忌爻病势增加于小愈：子孙发动制伏官鬼病必减，若化出忌爻反来伤克、或子孙化官爻，必因病稍好不谨慎而复重

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动制伏官鬼，病一定减轻；如果子孙变出忌爻反过来伤克，或者子孙变成官爻，一定是病稍微好了一点、不谨慎，结果又加重。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Output moves and restrains Pressure the illness surely lessens; but if Output changes into an Adversary line that turns back to harm, or Output changes into a Pressure line, it is surely because the illness has slightly eased and the patient is not careful, so that it grows heavy again.

## 《黄金策·病体》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6922 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 福化忌爻，病势增加于小愈； | 子孙变成忌爻，病势在稍好之后又增加； | Output changing into an Adversary line: the illness grows worse after a slight improvement; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6923 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙发动，制伏官鬼，其病必减。 | 子孙发动，制伏官鬼，这病一定减轻。 | When Output moves and restrains Pressure, the illness surely lessens. |
> | 若遇化出忌爻，反来伤克用神者，必因病势少愈，不能谨慎，以致复加沉重。 | 如果变出忌爻，反过来伤克用神，一定是因为病势稍微好了一点，不能谨慎，以致又加重。 | If it changes out an Adversary line that turns back to harm and control the Subject, it is surely because the illness has eased a little and he cannot be careful, so that it grows heavy again. |
> | 子孙化官爻，亦然。 | 子孙变成官爻，也是这样。 | Output changing into a Pressure line is the same. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2388 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙发动制伏官鬼，其病必减，若化回头克坏子孙，必因病势小愈不能谨慎，以致复加沉重，子孙化官爻亦然。 | 子孙发动制伏官鬼，这病一定减轻，如果变出回头克、坏了子孙，一定是因为病势稍好、不能谨慎，以致又加重，子孙变成官爻也是这样。 | When Output moves and restrains Pressure the illness surely lessens; if it changes into a returning control that ruins Output, it is surely because the illness has eased a little and he cannot be careful, so that it grows heavy again, and Output changing into a Pressure line is the same. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 变出的忌爻伤到谁，两家写得不同：旧注说反过来伤克用神，王洪绪说是回头克坏了子孙。两家都说这是病人自己一好就不谨慎的缘故。
>
> *Note:* The two write differently whom the changed-out Adversary line harms: the old commentary says it turns back to harm and control the Subject, and Wang Hongxu that a returning control ruins Output. Both say the cause is that the patient stops being careful as soon as he is a little better.
