# 身上虎须向五行言带疾：卦身临福德吉神主无痘毒，临官鬼必有结毒成疾之处——金鬼肺经鼻孔生疮或耳疾、木鬼肝经两目或耳疾、水鬼肾经耳枯唇疾、火鬼心经舌焦目疾、土鬼脾经口如鱼口鼻梁疾；休囚见福神发动可用药愈，无福神克制加白虎是终身之疾

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦身这一爻主出痘的人从头到尾的事：落在福德这个吉神上，没有痘毒；落在官鬼上，一定有毒结成病的地方，按官鬼的五行说会留下什么毛病：金鬼是肺经炎毒未消，鼻孔里生疮或右耳有毛病；木鬼是肝经火毒未消，两眼里出痘或右耳有毛病；水鬼是肾经火毒未消，两耳干枯，嘴唇有毛病；火鬼是心经火毒未消，舌头干焦，眼睛有毛病；土鬼是脾经火毒未清，嘴像鱼嘴，鼻梁有毛病。己土官鬼带着卦身的，如果休囚、又见福神发动，用药就能好；如果没有福神克制，反而加上白虎附着，就有损失，是终身的毛病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Body line governs the whole course of the person with the pox from start to finish: if it sits on Output, the fortunate spirit, there is no pox poison; if it sits on Pressure, there is surely a place where poison lodges and becomes a lingering ailment, and by the element of Pressure it is worked out as follows: a Metal ghost, inflammatory poison of the lung channel not dispersed, with sores in the nostrils or an ailment of the right ear; Wood, fire poison of the liver channel not dispersed, pox within both eyes or an ailment of the right ear; Water, fire poison of the kidney channel not dispersed, both ears dry and withered and an ailment of the lips; Fire, fire poison of the heart channel not dispersed, a dry scorched tongue and an eye ailment; Earth, fire poison of the spleen channel not cleared, a mouth like a fish's mouth and an ailment at the bridge of the nose. If a Ji-Earth Pressure line sits on the Body and is resting or trapped, and Output is seen moving, medicine can cure it; if no Output controls it and the White Tiger clings to it instead, there is loss, and it is a lifelong ailment.

> [!note] 按
> 经文「身上虎须向五行言带疾」的「虎」是白虎；「带疾」是痘好了以后留下的毛病。注里金鬼和木鬼两处都写「右耳带疾」，书里没有解释。「己土」是阴土（丑未），注里单提它，没有说为什么。这一条讲的是病好以后留下什么，不是病重不重，后面 LY.ILL.BSZZ.25 讲同一个题目按八宫来推。
>
> *Note:* The 'tiger' in the verse's 'with the Tiger on the Body' is the White Tiger; 'lingering ailment' is what is left after the pox has healed. The commentary writes 'an ailment of the right ear' for both the Metal and the Wood ghost, which the book does not explain. 'Ji Earth' is yin Earth (Chou and Wei) and the commentary singles it out without saying why. This entry is about what remains after recovery, not how severe the illness is; LY.ILL.BSZZ.25 below treats the same subject by the eight palaces.
