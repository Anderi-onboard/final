# 白虎临官临用火毒甚而未清：白虎是血神，临官鬼或用神又遇日月动爻刑冲克害，不是生痰就是结毒；毒结的部位按宫论（乾头面、坤腹、震足、巽股、艮手、离目、坎耳、兑口）

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 白虎带官爻或带用神，火毒很重而没有清干净。白虎是血神，如果带官鬼或带用神，又遇到日、月、动爻的刑冲克害，不是生痰，一定是毒结在体内；毒结的部位按宫来推：乾宫在头脸，坤宫在腹，震宫在脚，巽宫在大腿，艮宫在手，离宫在眼，坎宫在耳，兑宫在口。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The White Tiger on Pressure or on the Subject means the fire poison is heavy and not yet cleared. The White Tiger is the blood spirit; if it sits on Pressure or on the Subject and meets punishment, clash, control or harm from the Day, Month or moving lines, then if it is not phlegm being produced, it is surely poison lodged within; the place where the poison lodges is worked out by the palace: the Qian palace, the head and face; Kun, the belly; Zhen, the foot; Xun, the thigh; Gen, the hand; Li, the eye; Kan, the ear; Dui, the mouth.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这里兑宫是「口」，而下面 LY.ILL.BSZZ.25 里兑象是「面」，同一章里兑宫的部位有两说，见那一条。增删卜易也有按宫论花长在哪里的说法（LY.ILL.ZS.20：乾多生头、坤多腹、艮多手、兑多口、坎离上身、震巽下身），那里讲的是痘长在哪里，这里讲的是毒结在哪里。
>
> *Note:* Here the Dui palace is the 'mouth', while in LY.ILL.BSZZ.25 below the Dui image is the 'face'; the palace's place has two readings in the same chapter, see that entry. The Zengshan Buyi also places the flowers by palace (LY.ILL.ZS.20: Qian mostly on the head, Kun on the belly, Gen on the hands, Dui on the mouth, Kan and Li on the upper body, Zhen and Xun on the lower), but there it says where the pox will grow, and here where the poison lodges.
