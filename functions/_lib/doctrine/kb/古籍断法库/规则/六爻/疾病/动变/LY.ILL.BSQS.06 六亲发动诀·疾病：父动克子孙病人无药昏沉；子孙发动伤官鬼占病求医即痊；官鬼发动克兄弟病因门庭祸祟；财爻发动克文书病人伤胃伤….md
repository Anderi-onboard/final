# 六亲发动诀·疾病：父动克子孙病人无药昏沉；子孙发动伤官鬼占病求医即痊；官鬼发动克兄弟病因门庭祸祟；财爻发动克文书病人伤胃伤脾；兄弟交重克财病人难愈

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六亲发动诀里讲病的句子：父母爻发动，当头克子孙，病人没有药可用，昏沉；子孙爻发动伤官鬼，占病求医，人就痊愈；官鬼爻发动克兄弟，病因是门庭里有祸祟；财爻发动克文书，病人伤胃，更伤脾；兄弟交重克了财，病人难愈，灾没有离开。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The lines on illness in the verse on the six relations moving: with Resource moving and controlling Output head-on, the sick person has no medicine to use and sinks into stupor; with Output moving and injuring Pressure, in divining illness and seeking a doctor one is cured at once; with Pressure moving and controlling Peer, the cause of the illness is misfortune from the gate and courtyard; with Wealth moving and controlling documents, the sick person has harm to the stomach and still more to the spleen; with Peer moving together and controlling Wealth, the sick person is hard to cure and has not left misfortune.

## 《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·二十一、六亲发动诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3157 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses, no. 21: the six relations moving", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟交重克了财， | 兄弟爻交重，克了财， | With the Peer line moving and controlling Wealth, |
> | 病人难愈未离灾。 | 病人难以痊愈，还没有脱离灾难。 | the sick person is hard to cure and has not left misfortune. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 这首诀每一段讲一种六亲发动时各件事的结果，共五段（父母、子孙、官鬼、财、兄弟），每一段里讲到病的那一句都收在这里。财爻一段里讲病的是最后一句，所以整段八句原样带着。「官鬼发动克兄弟」一句里的兄弟是兄弟爻。父动克子孙、子孙发动伤官鬼两段，断易天机的六亲用变里也有，文字几乎一样（LY.ILL.DYTJ.40）。「病因门庭祸祟来」的「祸祟」是鬼神、邪气作祟。
>
> *Note:* Each stanza of this verse gives the outcome of one relation moving, five stanzas in all (Resource, Output, Pressure, Wealth, Peer), and the line on illness in each is taken here. In the stanza on Wealth the line on illness is the last, so the whole stanza of eight lines is carried. In 'Pressure moving and controlling Peer' the Peer is the Peer line. The stanzas 'Resource moving controls Output' and 'Output moving injures Pressure' are also in the section on the six relations turning in the Duanyi Tianji, in almost the same words (LY.ILL.DYTJ.40). 'Misfortune and haunting' means a harm from spirits or evil influence.
