# 家宅说·父母子孙俱旺老者安少者康；螣蛇白虎安静灾不生祸不作

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占家宅的一篇说：父母、子孙都旺，老人平安，年轻人康健；螣蛇、白虎安静，灾不生，祸不作。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The essay on divining a house says: when Resource and Output are both strong, the old are at peace and the young are healthy; when the Coiling Snake and White Tiger are still, calamity does not arise and misfortune does not happen.

## 《卜筮全书·阐幽精要·四、家宅说》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9633 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Chanyou Jingyao, no. 4: on the house", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鰧蛇白虎安静， | 螣蛇、白虎安静， | with the Coiling Snake and White Tiger still, |
> | 灾不生， | 灾难不会产生， | calamity does not arise, |
> | 祸不作。 | 祸患不会发作。 | and misfortune does not happen. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「老者安，少者康」是按父母代表老人、子孙代表年轻人读的，书里没有另注。螣蛇、白虎是主怪异、丧病的神，它们安静，灾祸就起不来。原文「鰧蛇」是「螣蛇」的异写。
>
> *Note:* 'The old at peace, the young healthy' is read with Resource standing for the old and Output for the young; the book gives no separate note. The Coiling Snake and White Tiger are the spirits of oddities and of mourning and illness, and when they are still calamity cannot arise. The original writes the snake with a variant character.
