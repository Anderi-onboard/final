# 父爻持世妙药难调：自占病父持世药不见功宜静养；兄持世有子孙动可延医，嫌鬼旺鬼兴主误服药；财爻持世勿误食肥甘；旺相遇生扶良医有觅，休囚逢冲克无药可医

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻持世，再好的药也难调治。自己占病，父母持世，药不见功效，宜于静养，远离色欲，平息气恼就可以了；兄弟持世，有子孙在卦中发动的，可以请医生，只嫌鬼旺相、鬼兴起，主吃错药；财爻持世，千万不要误吃油腻甘美的东西。旺相又遇到生扶，能找到良医；休囚又遇到冲克，没有药可以医。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Resource line holding the Self line, even wonderful medicine is hard to prescribe. In divining one's own illness, with Resource holding the Self line the medicine shows no effect, and it is fitting to rest quietly, keep away from lust and calm one's anger, and that will do; with Peer holding the Self line, if Output moves in the hexagram a doctor may be called, and the only fear is that Pressure is strong and rising, which means taking the wrong medicine; with the Wealth line holding the Self line, take care not to eat rich food by mistake. If strong and generated and supported, a good doctor can be found; if resting or trapped and clashed or controlled, there is no medicine that can treat him.

> [!warning] 打架
> 父母持世怎么办：黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.63）说父母持世可以许经醮保禳、祈祷之后再服药；野鹤说药不见功，宜静养、远色欲、息气恼。兄弟持世饮食减省见 LY.ILL.HJC.46。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What to do when Resource holds the Self line: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.63) says one may vow scriptures, offerings and protective rites and take medicine after praying; the Old Man says the medicine shows no effect and one should rest quietly, keep from lust and calm anger. For Peer holding the Self line reducing food, see LY.ILL.HJC.46.
