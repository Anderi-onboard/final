# 痘疹占：以官鬼为用，鬼不上卦或鬼化死墓绝胎空、被刑害克破者不出，鬼衰死而化生旺、无鬼而月建临官、鬼静而日当生旺、火鬼加蛇旺动者皆出；出期以官鬼生旺之年月日断；鬼衰子旺或子持身世必稀朗、鬼旺子衰或鬼持身世必稠密；父动者重、兄动者轻；福临空死墓绝胎或受刑害克冲者命难保

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以官鬼为用爻。鬼不上卦，或者鬼变出死、墓、绝、胎、空，以及被刑、害、克、破的，都不出痘。如果鬼虽然衰、死、受伤而变出生旺，或者没有鬼而月建临官，或者鬼安静而日正当生旺，以及火鬼带螣蛇旺盛发动的，都会出痘。想知道出痘疹的日期，以官鬼生旺的年、月、日来断。想知道痘疹的稀密：鬼衰、子孙旺，以及子孙持身、世的，一定稀疏明朗；鬼旺、子孙衰，以及鬼持身、世的，一定稠密。父母爻动的重，兄爻动的轻。福神临在空、死、墓、绝、胎上，以及受到刑、害、克、冲的，性命一定难保。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line is the Subject. If the ghost does not appear in the hexagram, or changes into Death, tomb, Extinction, Womb or Void, or is punished, harmed, controlled or broken, the pox will not come out. If the ghost is weak, dead or wounded but changes into Birth or strength, or there is no ghost but the Month stands at Office, or the ghost is still and the day is exactly its Birth or strength, or a Fire ghost with the Snake is strong and moving, the pox will come out. To know when the pox will come out, judge by the year, month or day in which the Pressure line is born or strong. To know whether it will be sparse or dense: if the ghost is weak and Output strong, or Output holds the Body or the Self, it will surely be sparse and clear; if the ghost is strong and Output weak, or the ghost holds the Body or the Self, it will surely be dense. If Resource moves it is heavy, if Peer moves it is light. If Fortune falls on Void, Death, tomb, Extinction or Womb, or is punished, harmed, controlled or clashed, his life can hardly be preserved.

> [!warning] 打架
> 痘的稀密看什么：易隐是「鬼衰子旺（或子持身世）则稀朗，鬼旺子衰（或鬼持身世）则稠密」，和卜筮正宗「官强而痘难开朗，福旺则花必稀疏」（LY.ILL.BSZZ.06）、增删卜易「鬼旺相临日月有生扶则花稠密，空破休囚墓绝则痘疏朗」（LY.ILL.ZS.20）是同一个方向，三家一致。取用的办法则不同：易隐以官鬼为用（出不出、何时出、稀密），吉凶另看子孙（福）是否受克；卜筮正宗按谁出花取用（兄弟取兄弟爻、子侄取子孙、妻奴取财），官鬼是痘花本身（LY.ILL.BSZZ.02）；增删卜易问何时出花以鬼为用、问吉凶全看子孙（LY.ILL.ZS.19、LY.ILL.ZS.21）。易隐这一条在取用上和增删卜易相近。「父动者重，兄动者轻」在另两家没有。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What decides whether the pox is sparse or dense: the Yi Yin says 'ghost weak and Output strong (or Output holding the Body or Self), sparse and clear; ghost strong and Output weak (or the ghost holding the Body or Self), dense', in the same direction as the Bushi Zhengzong's 'Pressure strong and the pox hardly clears; Output strong and the flowers are sparse' (LY.ILL.BSZZ.06) and the Zengshan Buyi's 'a strong Pressure line on the Day or Month with support brings dense flowers; Void, broken, resting, trapped, entombed or extinct, sparse pox' (LY.ILL.ZS.20); the three agree. The way of taking the Subject differs: the Yi Yin takes Pressure as the Subject (whether the pox comes out, when, sparse or dense) and looks separately at whether Output (Fortune) is controlled for fortune; the Bushi Zhengzong takes it by who has the pox (Peer for a brother, Output for a child, Wealth for a wife or servant) with Pressure the pox flowers themselves (LY.ILL.BSZZ.02); the Zengshan Buyi takes Pressure as the Subject for when the pox comes out and looks wholly at Output for fortune (LY.ILL.ZS.19 and LY.ILL.ZS.21). In taking the Subject the Yi Yin is close to the Zengshan Buyi. 'Resource moving is heavy, Peer moving light' is found in neither of the others.

> [!note] 按
> 原文「月建临官」的「官」是十二长生里的临官位，不是官鬼；「火鬼加蛇旺动」是火鬼带螣蛇旺盛发动（何知章里的「螣蛇入火」，LY.ILL.BSZZ.35，是同一个看法）。「福」是福神，即子孙。这一条在易隐里单独一节，题作《痘疹占》，在《小儿占》一类的后面。
>
> *Note:* The 'Office' of 'the Month stands at Office' is the Office stage of the twelve life stages, not the Pressure line; 'a Fire ghost with the Snake, strong and moving' is a Fire ghost carrying the Coiling Snake strong and moving (the 'Snake entering Fire' of the how-to-know rhyme, LY.ILL.BSZZ.35, is the same view). 'Fortune' is the spirit of blessing, that is Output. In the Yi Yin this is a section of its own, titled 'Divining pox', placed after the section on children.
