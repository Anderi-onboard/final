# 身命章·父母持世辛勤劳碌、兄弟持世疾病连绵

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 阐幽精要的身命章说：父母爻持世，一生辛勤劳碌；兄弟爻持世，疾病连绵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on the person and life in the Chanyou Jingyao says: with Resource holding the Self line one toils and labours; with Peer holding the Self line, illness drags on.

## 《卜筮全书·阐幽精要·三、身命章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9614 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Chanyou Jingyao, no. 3: on the person and life", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母持世， | 父母爻持世， | With Resource holding the Self line, |
> | 辛勤劳碌； | 辛勤劳碌； | it is toil and labour; |
> | 兄弟持世， | 兄弟爻持世， | with Peer holding the Self line, |
> | 疾病连绵。 | 疾病连绵。 | illness drags on. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是阐幽精要里占身命（问一个人一生）一章里的一联，讲世爻上坐什么六亲，对这个人一生的影响。「疾病连绵」是病一个接一个，不是指某一场病；这一章是讲人的命，不是讲一次占病，所以放在本域的「总则」里。
>
> *Note:* This couplet is in the chapter of the Chanyou Jingyao on divining a person's life, and it says what the relation on the Self line does to a person over his lifetime. 'Illness drags on' means one illness after another, not one particular illness; the chapter is about a person's fate, not a single divination about illness, which is why it sits under 'principles' in this domain.
