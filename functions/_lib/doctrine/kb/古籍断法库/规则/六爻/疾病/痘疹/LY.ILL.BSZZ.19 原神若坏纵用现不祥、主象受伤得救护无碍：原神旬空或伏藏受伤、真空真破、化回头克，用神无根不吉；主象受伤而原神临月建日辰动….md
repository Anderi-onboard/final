# 原神若坏纵用现不祥、主象受伤得救护无碍：原神旬空或伏藏受伤、真空真破、化回头克，用神无根不吉；主象受伤而原神临月建日辰动爻救护，痘虽险可断不妨

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 原神如果坏了，即使用神出现也不吉祥；主象受了伤，得到救护就没有妨碍。原神就是生用神的爻：原神旬空，或者伏藏又受伤克，主症状虚、身体弱，不补就不能起发，起发了也没有力气灌浆；原神遇到真空、真破，或者伏藏又受伤太过，或者变出回头克伤它，叫做原神受伤，用神没有根，怎么能得到生，不是吉祥的兆头。如果主象遇到伤克，而原神带着月建、日辰、动爻来救护，痘症虽然凶险，可以断定没有妨碍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Support is spoiled, then even if the Subject appears it is inauspicious; if the main image is wounded but gains rescue and protection, there is no obstruction. The Support is the line that generates the Subject: if it is Void, or hidden and struck by harm or control, the condition is one of emptiness and the body weak, so that without tonics it cannot erupt and once erupted has no strength to fill with fluid; if the Support meets true Void or true breaking, or is hidden and too badly wounded, or changes into a returning control that wounds it, this is called the Support wounded, the Subject has no root, and how can it be born? It is not an auspicious sign. If the main image meets harm or control but the Support, backed by the Month, the Day or a moving line, comes to rescue and protect it, then although the pox is dangerous, it may be judged that there is no obstruction.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 注的第一句「若伏藏而夫伤克者」里的「夫」按上下文应是「受」或「又」之类的误字，译作「又受伤克」；书里没有说明。「真空、真破」是真正的旬空、月破（不是被冲开、被填实的假空假破），书里没有在这里另作解释。「主象」就是用神所代表的那一象。
>
> *Note:* In the commentary's first sentence, 'hidden and fu struck', the character 'fu' is by context a mis-written 'shou' or 'you' and is rendered 'hidden and further struck'; the book does not say. 'True Void, true breaking' are a real Void and Month-break (as against a false one that has been clashed open or filled), which the book does not explain here. The 'main image' is the image the Subject stands for.
