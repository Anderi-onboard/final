# 鬼化长生日下沉重：鬼动病重，鬼化入长生（或在长生爻上发动）一日重似一日，直过帝旺日才轻减；化衰病则一日轻似一日，福化长生也吉

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻发动，病势一定重。如果鬼变入长生，就是一天比一天重的象，一直要过了帝旺的日子，才会减轻；鬼在长生的爻上发动，也是这样。如果变入衰、病，就是一天比一天轻，和子孙（福）变入长生一样，都吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a Pressure line moves, the illness is surely heavy. If Pressure changes into Birth, it is the image of growing heavier each day, and it is only after the day of its Peak has passed that it lightens; a Pressure line moving while on a Birth line is the same. If it changes into Decline or Sickness, it grows lighter each day, and this, like Output changing into Birth, is all lucky.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 长生是十二长生的第一位（长生、沐浴、冠带、临官、帝旺、衰、病、死、墓、绝、胎、养），帝旺是第五位。鬼变入长生，是鬼在往盛的方向走，病就一天天重下去，一直到它旺到顶才回头。旧注还讲了变入衰、病是一天轻一天的相反情形；王洪绪只收了前半。
>
> *Note:* Birth is the first of the twelve life stages (Birth, Bath, Cap, Office, Peak, Decline, Sickness, Death, Tomb, Extinction, Womb, Nurture) and Peak is the fifth. A Pressure line changing into Birth is Pressure moving toward strength, so the illness grows heavier day by day until Pressure reaches its peak and turns back. The old commentary also gives the opposite case, changing into Decline or Sickness, where it grows lighter each day; Wang Hongxu takes only the first half.
