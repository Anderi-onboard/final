# 问孩童何时见痘：官鬼当值及生旺官鬼的年月日；上下鬼填其虚、变伏鬼实其质；问岁以年计、问时以月计；两鬼一空一实以填补空鬼为期，卦无官而伏变爻有者以变伏之鬼所值为期

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果问孩子什么时候出痘，是官鬼当值的年、月、日，以及生旺官鬼的年、月、日；上下的鬼填补它的空，变出的鬼、伏着的鬼落实它的实体。远近要分开占：问哪一岁就以年计，问什么时候就以月计，先看填实的时间，再看生旺的年月来断；如果卦里有两个鬼，一个空一个实，反而以填补空鬼的时间为期；卦里一个官也没有，而伏爻、变爻上有的，就以变出、伏着的鬼所值的时间为期。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If asked when a child will have the pox, it is the year, month or day on which the Pressure line is on duty, and the year, month or day that strengthens the Pressure line; the ghosts above and below fill its emptiness, and the changed or hidden ghost makes its substance real. Far and near are divined separately: asking which year, count by years; asking when, count by months; first the time of filling the emptiness, then the years and months of strength; if the hexagram has two ghosts, one Void and one full, the time of filling the Void ghost is taken as the term instead; if the hexagram has no Pressure line at all but the hidden or changed lines have one, take the time held by the changed or hidden ghost as the term.

> [!note] 按
> 「填实」是空的爻等到它的地支值年、值月、值日，把空补上；「上下鬼」按注是卦里上、下两个鬼。这一条和增删卜易讲的出花之期（LY.ILL.ZS.19：鬼静逢值逢冲、鬼动逢值逢合、鬼空破在填实之时、鬼伏在出现之岁）是同一路，只是易冒多了两个鬼、无鬼时怎么取。
>
> *Note:* 'Filling' is a Void line waiting for the year, month or day of its own branch to fill it; the 'ghosts above and below' are, by the note, the two ghosts in the hexagram. This entry is on the same road as the Zengshan Buyi's time for the flowers to come out (LY.ILL.ZS.19: a still ghost on the day of duty or clash, a moving ghost on duty or combination, a Void or broken ghost at the time of filling, a hidden ghost in the year it appears); the Yi Mao adds only what to do with two ghosts and with none.
