# 娼家·官居刑地，门招恶病之人（旧注：鬼带刑爻生合世身多招带病之人来往，与世生合与财刑冲须防妓亦沾染）

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》娼家章说：「官鬼落在刑的地方，门上就招来恶病的人。」旧注：鬼爻带着刑爻、生合世和身，多半招来带病的人往来；（鬼）和世爻生合、又和财刑冲，要防妓女也被传染；鬼化成鬼，或者化成忌爻刑克世、身，恐怕因为留宿的客人招来祸事；有制伏就没有妨碍，有救就可以解开。王洪绪：鬼带刑爻生合世、身，多招恶病的人往来；与世生合、与财刑冲，要防妓女也被传染。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on courtesan houses says: 'With the officer in a place of punishment, the door draws people with vile illness.' The old commentary: a ghost carrying a punishing line and generating or combining with the Self and Body draws many people carrying illness; if it generates or combines with the Self line and punishes or clashes Wealth, beware that the courtesan too is infected; a ghost turning into a ghost, or turning into a taboo line that punishes or controls the Self and Body, may bring misfortune from a lodging guest; if it is restrained there is no harm, and if there is rescue it can be resolved. Wang Hongxu: a ghost carrying a punishing line and generating or combining with the Self and Body draws many people with vile illness; if it generates or combines with the Self line and punishes or clashes Wealth, beware that the courtesan too is infected.

> [!note] 按
> 娼家章是占娼家（妓院）事的一章。这一联讲的是妓院里来往的人带病：官鬼带刑、生合世身，招来带病的客人；鬼与世爻生合、与财刑冲，妓女也要被传染。「恶病」「带病」是笼统说法，书里没有指明是哪种病，译文照字面。这不是占一个人的病，所以放在风险里。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on courtesan houses is about divining matters of a courtesan house. This couplet is about the people who come and go carrying illness: Pressure carrying punishment and generating or combining with the Self and Body brings in clients who carry illness; if the ghost generates or combines with the Self line and punishes or clashes Wealth, the courtesan is infected too. 'Vile illness' and 'carrying illness' are general terms and the book does not name the illness; the translation keeps the letter. This is not a divination about one person's illness, which is why it is placed under risks.
