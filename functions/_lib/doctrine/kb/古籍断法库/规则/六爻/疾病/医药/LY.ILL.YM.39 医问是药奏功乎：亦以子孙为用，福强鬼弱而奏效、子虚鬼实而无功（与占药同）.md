# 医问是药奏功乎：亦以子孙为用，福强鬼弱而奏效、子虚鬼实而无功（与占药同）

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 医生问这味药能不能奏效，也是拿子孙爻当用：子孙强、鬼弱，药就见效；子孙虚、鬼实，药就没有功效。注说：和占药一样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a doctor asks whether a medicine will take effect, Output is again the line to use: with Output strong and the ghost weak it takes effect, and with Output empty and the ghost full it has no effect. The note says: it is the same as divining medicine.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是用药问：医生已经开了某味药，问它灵不灵，取子孙的强弱，和病家占药一样（见 LY.ILL.YM.35、YM.37）。「福强鬼弱」的「福」是子孙，「子虚鬼实」的「虚」「实」用的是上面 LY.ILL.YM.31 里的意思：空破散绝叫虚，旺相有生扶叫实。
>
> *Note:* This entry is the question of using the medicine: the doctor has settled on a certain medicine and asks whether it will work, and takes the strength of Output, as the patient's family does in divining medicine (see LY.ILL.YM.35, YM.37). The 'fortune' in 'fortune strong, ghost weak' is Output, and 'empty' and 'full' in 'Output empty, ghost full' are used in the sense given in LY.ILL.YM.31 above: Void, broken, scattered or in Extinction is empty, and thriving and strong with generation and support is full.
