# 种作·世值三刑，农须带疾（旧注：世为治田之人，被日辰动爻刑冲克害最不利；带白虎三刑农夫必带疾，世持官爻亦有疾）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》种作章说：「世爻遇到三刑，种田的人要带着病。」旧注：世爻代表种田的人，被日辰、动爻刑冲克害，最不利；官鬼是疾病、官司、火灾盗贼一类；如果带着白虎、三刑，农夫一定带病；世爻持着官爻，也有病，不然就有差役在身；再加朱雀，恐怕是官司。王洪绪：带白虎三刑，农夫一定带病，世持官爻也是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on farming says: 'With the Self line meeting the three punishments, the farmer carries an illness.' The old commentary: the Self line stands for the man who works the field, and if the Day or a moving line punishes, clashes, controls or harms it, it is the most unfavourable; Pressure is illness, lawsuits, fire and theft; if it carries the White Tiger and the three punishments, the farmer is sure to carry an illness; if the Self line holds the Pressure line there is illness too, or else some duty or service is laid on him; with the Vermilion Bird added, it is probably a lawsuit. Wang Hongxu: carrying the White Tiger and the three punishments, the farmer is sure to carry an illness, and so too if the Self line holds the Pressure line.

> [!note] 按
> 种作章是占种地、农事的一章。旧注把财爻、福爻、兄弟、父母、官鬼各自伤克世爻的后果依次列了一遍（财：女人财物事；福：小孩六畜僧道事；兄弟：口舌争斗；父母：尊长文书屋宅；官鬼：病讼火盗），这里把讲官鬼（病）和带疾的几句连着收，让「官鬼为病讼」有出处。「三刑」是几种地支相刑（寅巳申、丑戌未、子卯等）。「世持官爻亦有疾」后面的「否则必有职役在身」是说，不是病就是被官府差遣。王洪绪的版本更短，没有五类六亲的那一串。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on farming is about divining the working of land and farm affairs. The old commentary lists in turn what follows when each of the Wealth, fortune, Peer, Resource and Pressure lines harms or controls the Self line (Wealth: women and property; fortune: small children, livestock, monks and Daoists; Peer: quarrels and fights; Resource: elders, documents and the house; Pressure: illness, lawsuits, fire and theft), and the sentences on Pressure (illness) and on carrying an illness are taken together here so that 'Pressure is illness and lawsuits' has its source. The 'three punishments' are several mutual punishments of branches (Yin, Si and Shen; Chou, Xu and Wei; Zi and Mao; and so on). In 'if the Self line holds the Pressure line there is illness too' the following 'or else some duty or service is laid on him' means that if it is not illness it is being called up by the authorities. Wang Hongxu's version is shorter and lacks the run through the five relations.
