# 神崇（下）：父动克日求祖先、或因修造；财兴克日阴司恼、或因买物；子孙克日儿女作、或奸邪畜类；兄弟克日神无主、路死伤亡；鬼来克我招邪崇、或神明口头牵；易镜云察祸推其鬼处、配六亲、旺相为神休囚为鬼、动爻克世克日亦可取鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻发动克日：去求祖先，或者是因为修造不安；财爻兴动克日：是阴司恼了，或者是因为买东西，祸事勾连上来；子孙克日：是儿女作出来的，或者是奸邪、畜类传来的；兄弟克日：神没有主，路上死、伤亡是先来的祸；鬼来克我，招来邪崇，或者是神明的口头愿牵着；再拿日子分出老少，仔细推详，不要乱说。《易镜》说：察看灾祸，就推它的鬼在什么地方，再拿自己的身去配六亲，相克相生，就看见祸的开端；旺相的是神，休囚的是鬼；凡是动爻克世爻、克日的，也可以取它作鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Resource line moving and controlling the Day: seek the ancestors, or it is from repairs and building that unrest comes; Wealth rising and controlling the Day: the netherworld office is vexed, or from buying goods misfortune links up; Output controlling the Day: made by children, or handed on by wicked ones or beasts; Peer controlling the Day: a god without a master, and death on the road, injury and death make the misfortune first; a ghost coming to control me invites an evil haunting, or a god's spoken vow draws me; further use the day to divide old from young, consider carefully and do not speak wildly. The Mirror of the Changes says: to examine misfortune, trace where its ghost is, and further match the body with the six relations; by control and generation the beginning of the misfortune appears; strong is a god, resting or trapped is a ghost; any moving line controlling the Self or the Day may also be taken as the ghost.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是「神崇」的后半，四首歌诀讲的是「动爻克日」的几种情形：父（祖先、修造）、财（阴司、买物）、子孙（儿女、畜类）、兄弟（神无主、路死）。它的办法是按动爻的六亲去认作祟的是谁——和易林补遗「六亲内外动断是谁的鬼」（LY.ILL.EL.40）、易隐「鬼崇·是谁的鬼」（LY.ILL.YY.30）、黄金策「以卦身推是什么人的鬼」（LY.ILL.HJC.102）是同一类办法，不同的只是这里看的是动爻克的是不是日辰。「日」只写了一个字，按字面译作 the Day（日辰）。「神明口头牵」按「神明许过口愿，牵着我」译；「路死伤亡作祸先」里的「先」按「先来」译。《易镜》一句：「旺相为神，休囚为鬼」同黄金策「旺相为神、休囚为鬼」（LY.ILL.HJC.94）是同一句；「凡动爻克世克日，亦可取鬼」是说鬼不一定是官鬼爻，动爻克世或克日也可以当鬼来取。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> *Note:* This is the second half of 'spirit hauntings', in which four verses set out the cases of a moving line controlling the Day: Resource (ancestors, repairs and building), Wealth (the netherworld office, buying goods), Output (children, beasts) and Peer (a god without a master, death on the road). Its method is to tell who is haunting from the six relation of the moving line, the same kind of method as the Yilin Buyi's 'whose ghost it is from the six relations moving inside or outside' (LY.ILL.EL.40), the Yi Yin's 'whose ghost it is' (LY.ILL.YY.30) and the Huangjin Ce's 'from the Body, whose ghost it is' (LY.ILL.HJC.102), except that here what is looked at is whether the moving line controls the Day. 'Day' is written only as one character and is rendered literally as the Day. 'A god's spoken vow draws me' is translated as 'a god to whom a spoken vow was made draws me'; 'xian' in 'death on the road, injury and death make the misfortune first' is translated 'first'. The Mirror of the Changes's 'strong is a god, resting or trapped is a ghost' is the same sentence as the Huangjin Ce's 'thriving is a god, resting or trapped is a ghost' (LY.ILL.HJC.94); 'any moving line controlling the Self or the Day may also be taken as the ghost' says that the ghost need not be the Pressure line, and a moving line controlling the Self or the Day can also be taken as the ghost. The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.
