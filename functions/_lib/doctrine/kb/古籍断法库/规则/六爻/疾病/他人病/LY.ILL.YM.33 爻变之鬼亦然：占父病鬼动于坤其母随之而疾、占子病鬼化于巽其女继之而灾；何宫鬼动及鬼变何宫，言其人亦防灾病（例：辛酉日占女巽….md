# 爻变之鬼亦然：占父病鬼动于坤其母随之而疾、占子病鬼化于巽其女继之而灾；何宫鬼动及鬼变何宫，言其人亦防灾病（例：辛酉日占女巽之涣、丙辰日占次女痘革之咸）；疾病之章不离用爻鬼象

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 还有占父亲的病而鬼在坤宫发动，他的母亲跟着也生病；占儿子的病而鬼变到巽宫，他的女儿接着遭灾：这就是说，爻变出来的鬼也是同样。所以疾病这一章离不开用爻和鬼的象。注：鬼在哪一宫发动，以及鬼变到哪一宫，就说那一宫所代表的人也要提防灾病。举了三个例子：辛酉日占女儿的病，巽变涣，子孙遇长生，这个女儿不会死，但鬼动变入坎宫，次子接着生病，后来还死了；丙辰日占二女儿出痘，革变咸，子孙值月建，二女儿无事，但内卦变到艮，官鬼落在少男宫里，后来小儿子也出了痘。总之占疾病，生死靠用爻，趋避、变通、救治的门路，都在于详细考察鬼爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Again, where a father's illness is divined and the ghost moves in Kun, his mother falls ill after him; where a son's illness is divined and the ghost changes into Xun, his daughter suffers calamity in turn: so the ghost that appears by change is also the same. So the chapter on illness never leaves the Subject line and the image of the ghost. The note says: whichever palace the ghost moves in, and whichever palace it changes into, say that the person that palace stands for should also guard against calamity and illness. It gives cases: on a Xinyou day a woman's illness was divined, Xun changing into Huan, Output met Birth and the woman did not die, but the ghost moved into the Kan palace and the middle son fell ill in succession and later died; on a Bingchen day the pox of a second daughter was divined, Ge changing into Xian, Output stood on the Month and the second daughter came to no harm, but the inner trigram changed into Gen, the Pressure line sat in the palace of the youngest son, and afterwards the youngest son also had the pox. In short, in divining illness life and death rest on the Subject line, and the gates of seeking and avoiding, altering and remedying all depend on a careful examination of the Pressure line.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条讲的是牵连：病的鬼落在哪一宫，那一宫代表的家人就会跟着有事——坤是母，巽是长女，坎是中男，艮是少男。与痘疹章「考六亲化鬼及鬼化六亲」（LY.ILL.YM.06）是同一路读法，只是那里是问谁会出痘，这里是问谁会跟着有病。注里第二例「及甲申旬化午子空」一句，原文的次序和断句都不清楚（甲申旬空的是午未，而不是子），这里按字面译，不改。「及甲寅旬……及甲申旬……」两个旬，说的是同一个鬼在两个旬里应验的情形不同：头一旬是有病，后一旬是死。
>
> *Note:* This entry is about knock-on effects: into whichever palace the ghost of the illness falls, the family member that palace stands for will be affected in turn — Kun is the mother, Xun the eldest daughter, Kan the middle son, Gen the youngest son. It reads in the same way as the chapter on pox's 'examine the relations that turn into ghosts and the ghosts that turn into relations' (LY.ILL.YM.06), except that there the question was who would have the pox and here it is who would fall ill in turn. In the note's second case, the sentence 'in the Jiashen decade changing into Wu with Zi Void' is unclear in both order and punctuation in the original (the Void branches of the Jiashen decade are Wu and Wei, not Zi), and is translated literally without correction. The two decades, Jiayin and Jiashen, show the same ghost coming true differently in each: illness in the first, death in the second.
