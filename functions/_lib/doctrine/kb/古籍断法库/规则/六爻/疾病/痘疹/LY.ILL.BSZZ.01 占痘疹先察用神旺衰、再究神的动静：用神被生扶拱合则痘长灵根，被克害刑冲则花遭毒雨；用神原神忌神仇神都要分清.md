# 占痘疹先察用神旺衰、再究神的动静：用神被生扶拱合则痘长灵根，被克害刑冲则花遭毒雨；用神原神忌神仇神都要分清

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 痘、痧、疮、疹是随着六气五行的流转一阵一阵流行的，想问平安还是危险，要凭易卦。先看用神是旺是衰，再考究各爻是动是静：用神被生、扶、拱、合，痘就长得有根；被克、害、刑、冲，花就遭到毒雨。凡占痘痧，一定要先分清用神、原神的衰旺，再考究忌神、仇神的动静。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pox, measles, sores and eruptions come and go with the turning of the six qi and the five phases; to ask whether it will be safe or dangerous one must rely on the hexagram. First look at whether the Subject is strong or weak, then at whether the lines move or are still: if the Subject is generated, supported, embraced or combined, the pox grows a living root; if it is controlled, harmed, punished or clashed, the flowers are struck by poison rain. In all divining of pox and measles one must first tell how strong or weak the Subject and the Support are, then examine how the Adversary and the enemy spirit move or stand still.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章是卜筮正宗新增的，不在《黄金策》原书里（卜筮大全和黄金策文件都没有）；赋文和注文的作者，书里没有分别写明，本库把赋文标作本文、把「——」后面的话标作王洪绪注。「痧」这里指跟痘一起流行的出疹的病，书里没有另外解释。「仇神」书里在《原忌仇神论》（卜筮正宗第 971 行）有解释：制克原神、生扶忌神的爻。
>
> *Note:* This chapter is an addition of the Bushi Zhengzong and is not in the original Huangjin Ce (neither the Bushi Daquan nor the Huangjin Ce file has it); the book does not say separately who wrote the verse and who the commentary, so the library marks the verse as the base text and what follows the dash as Wang Hongxu's commentary. 'Sha' here means the eruptive illness that spreads along with pox and is not otherwise explained. The 'enemy spirit' (chou shen) is explained in the book's essay on the Support, Adversary and enemy spirit (Bushi Zhengzong, line 971): the line that controls the Support and generates or supports the Adversary.
