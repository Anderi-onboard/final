# 占花疏密、部位、颜色：鬼旺日月生扶花稠密、空破休囚死绝痘疏朗；鬼动何宫定花丛何处（艮手臂、兑口颊、坎离上身、震巽下身）、旺在初爻先于足、六爻发于面；火鬼红紫、金鬼虚白、水鬼黑陷、木杂细、土肿大；须论用神生死定吉凶

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占花的稀密，问鬼的旺衰，用八卦分手足，用六爻定身形，用五行定颜色。鬼临日、月，旺相又有生扶，花一定稠密；遇到空、破、休、囚、死、绝，痘一定疏朗。鬼在哪个宫发动，花就集中在哪里；不动的，就看哪一爻旺：发在艮宫的手臂上密，动在兑宫的口和脸颊上多，坎、离在上身，震、巽在下身；旺在初爻，先出在脚上，旺在六爻，开始发在脸上，五爻在胸背，二爻在腿膝，三、四爻在腰、大腿、心口、肚子。火鬼发的，颜色红紫；金鬼动的，虚白；水鬼发的，黑而塌陷；木是杂而细，土是肿而大。但是一定要论用神的生死才能定吉凶，所以水鬼动而主象旺的，痘出现黑钉，后来仍能收功。痘症多变，假如遇到用象破空，或者某个鬼发动，就变成那种病而死，比如未月甲辰日，占孩子的痘，得到屯卦变革卦，到戊申日变成脾病而治不好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine whether the flowers are sparse or dense, ask whether the ghost is strong or weak; the eight trigrams divide hands and feet, the six lines fix the parts of the body, the five elements bring out the colours. If the ghost sits on the Day or Month, is strong and supported, the flowers are surely dense; if it meets Void, breaking, resting, trapped, death or extinction, the pox is surely sparse and clear. Wherever the ghost moves, in which palace, that fixes where the flowers cluster; if it does not move, see which line is strong: from the Gen palace, dense on the arms; moving in the Dui palace, many on the mouth and cheeks; Kan and Li the upper body, Zhen and Xun the lower; strong on the first line, first on the feet; on the sixth line, starting on the face; the fifth the chest and back, the second the legs and knees, the third and fourth the waist, thighs, heart and belly. A Fire ghost gives red-purple, a Metal ghost hollow white, a Water ghost black and sunken; Wood is mixed and fine, Earth swollen and large. But one must judge whether the Subject lives or dies before fixing fortune, so when a Water ghost moves and the main image is strong, the pox shows black nails and yet comes to completion. Pox often changes: if the image of the Subject is broken or Void, or some ghost moves, it changes into that illness and he dies — for example, in the Wei month on the Jiachen day a child's pox was divined and Zhun changing into Ge obtained, and on the Wushen day it turned into a spleen illness that could not be cured.

> [!warning] 打架
> 痘长在哪里、颜色如何：易冒这里按鬼所在的宫（艮手臂、兑口颊、坎离上身、震巽下身）和鬼旺在哪一爻（初爻足、六爻面）来断，增删卜易也是这样（乾多生头、坤多腹、艮多手、兑多口、坎离上身、震巽下身，LY.ILL.ZS.20），两家一致，颜色也相同（火鬼紫红、金鬼虚白、水鬼黑陷、木杂细、土肿大）。卜筮正宗讲的是好了以后留下的毛病在哪里（LY.ILL.BSZZ.23–25），兑宫一说口、一说面，与这里说的口颊又有出入。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Where the pox grows and what colour: the Yi Mao judges here by the palace the ghost stands in (Gen the arms, Dui the mouth and cheeks, Kan and Li the upper body, Zhen and Xun the lower) and by which line the ghost is strong on (first line the feet, sixth line the face), and so does the Zengshan Buyi (Qian mostly on the head, Kun on the belly, Gen on the hands, Dui on the mouth, Kan and Li on the upper body, Zhen and Xun on the lower, LY.ILL.ZS.20); the two agree, and the colours are the same too (Fire ghost purple-red, Metal hollow white, Water black and sunken, Wood mixed and fine, Earth swollen and large). The Bushi Zhengzong speaks of where the ailment left after recovery lies (LY.ILL.BSZZ.23-25), and gives the Dui palace once as the mouth and once as the face, which again differs slightly from the mouth and cheeks here.

> [!note] 按
> 「五脢」是脊柱两旁的肉，「二腓」是两个小腿肚。「黑钉」是黑色的痘，一种险象。注的最后一个例（屯之革，戊申日变脾病）是说痘症会变成别的病而致命。这一条和易隐 LY.ILL.YY.35「鬼衰子旺则稀朗、鬼旺子衰则稠密」和卜筮正宗 LY.ILL.BSZZ.06「福旺则花必稀疏」方向一致。
>
> *Note:* 'The back' (wumei) is the flesh on either side of the spine and 'the two calves' (erfei) the two calf muscles. 'Black nails' are black pox, a dangerous sign. The last example in the note (Zhun into Ge, turning into a spleen illness on the Wushen day) says that pox may change into another illness and prove fatal. This entry is in the same direction as the Yi Yin's LY.ILL.YY.35 ('ghost weak and Output strong, sparse; ghost strong and Output weak, dense') and the Bushi Zhengzong's LY.ILL.BSZZ.06 ('Output strong and the flowers are sparse').
