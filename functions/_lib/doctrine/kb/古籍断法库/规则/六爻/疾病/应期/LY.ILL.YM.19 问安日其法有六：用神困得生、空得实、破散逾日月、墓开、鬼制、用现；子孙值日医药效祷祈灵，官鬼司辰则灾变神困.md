# 问安日其法有六：用神困得生、空得实、破散逾日月、墓开、鬼制、用现；子孙值日医药效祷祈灵，官鬼司辰则灾变神困

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问哪一天转安，有六种办法：用神被困了，得到生扶，病就好转；用神落空了，等到填实，病就痊愈；用神破散了，过了破散的日、月就能保住；用神入墓的，墓被冲开，病就退；鬼被制住，祸患就消；用神伏藏的，用神出现，人就起得来。这六种是病转安的时辰。注里逐种落到日子和时期上；又说子孙值日，医药见效、祷告灵验，官鬼当值的时辰，灾患生变、神志疲困；只有占丈夫、占官的病不这样论。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* There are six ways to ask the day of ease: the Subject beleaguered, gaining generation brings recovery; the Subject Void, becoming full heals the illness; the Subject broken and scattered, it can be preserved once the day or month of the breaking is past; the tomb opened, the illness withdraws; the ghost controlled, the trouble dissolves; the Subject appearing, the body gets up. These six are the times when the illness settles into ease. The note applies each to a day or a period, and adds that when Output is on duty medicine takes effect and prayer is answered, and when the Pressure line rules the hour the trouble changes and the spirit is worn down, except that in divining a husband or an official this is not counted.

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1216 行 · Bracketed small note in the Yi Mao (author not named in the book)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡用休囚，病退于生扶之日，用爻空陷，病退于填补之期，用爻破散，病退于逾是破散之时，用爻墓，病退于开墓，用爻潜，病退于现起，子孙值日，疾患渐安，惟占夫占官不论。 | 凡是用爻休囚的，病在生扶它的日子退；用爻落空陷的，病在被填补的时期退；用爻破散的，病在过了破散的时候退；用爻入墓的，病在墓被冲开时退；用爻伏藏的，病在它现出来时起；子孙值日，病渐渐安稳，只有占丈夫、占官的病不这样论。 | Whenever the Subject line is resting or trapped, the illness withdraws on the day that generates and supports it; when the Subject line is Void and sunk, the illness withdraws in the period that fills it in; when the Subject line is broken and scattered, the illness withdraws once the time of breaking is past; when the Subject line is in a tomb, the illness withdraws when the tomb is opened; when the Subject line is hidden, the illness lifts when it appears and rises; when Output is on duty, the illness gradually settles, except that in divining a husband or an official this is not counted. |
> | 大抵子孙值日，医药效而祷祈灵，官鬼司辰，灾患变而神思困也。 | 大体上，子孙值日，医药见效、祷告灵验；官鬼当值的时辰，灾患生变，神志疲困。 | In general, when Output is on duty, medicine takes effect and prayer is answered; when the Pressure line rules the hour, the trouble changes and the spirit is worn down. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 注里「惟占夫占官不论」没有说缘故。照这门方法本身的道理，占丈夫、占官时用神就是官鬼，而子孙克官鬼，子孙值日对它反而不利，所以不这样论；这是从道理上推的，书里没有写。「用爻潜，病退于现起」，注在这里用「退」，而经文说的是「用现而身起」，是同一件事的两种说法：用神一现出来，病人就起得来。
>
> *Note:* The note does not say why 'divining a husband or an official' is excluded. By the method's own logic, in those cases the Subject is the Pressure line, and Output controls Pressure, so Output on duty works against it; this is inferred from the principle and is not written in the book. Of 'the Subject line hidden, the illness withdraws when it appears', the note says 'withdraws' where the text says 'the Subject appears and the body gets up'; the two are the same thing said two ways: once the Subject comes out, the sick person can rise.
