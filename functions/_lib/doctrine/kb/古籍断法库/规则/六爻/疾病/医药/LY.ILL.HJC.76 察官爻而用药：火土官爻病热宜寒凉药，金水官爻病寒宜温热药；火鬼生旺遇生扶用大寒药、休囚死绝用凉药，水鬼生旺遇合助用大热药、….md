# 察官爻而用药：火土官爻病热宜寒凉药，金水官爻病寒宜温热药；火鬼生旺遇生扶用大寒药、休囚死绝用凉药，水鬼生旺遇合助用大热药、休囚死绝用温剂；火鬼在阴宫阴爻是阴虚火动宜滋阴降火，水鬼在阳宫内卦是血气虚损宜补中益气

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看官鬼爻的五行来用药：火、土官鬼，病一定发热，宜用寒凉的药去攻；金、水官鬼，病多半发寒，一定用温热的药来治。火鬼在生旺的地方又遇到生扶，一定用大寒的药攻它，休囚死绝就用凉药；水鬼在生旺的地方又遇到合助，要用大热的药，休囚死绝就用温药。火鬼在阴宫阴爻，是阴虚火动的症候，可以用滋阴降火的药；水鬼在阳宫内卦，是血气虚损的症候，可以用补中益气的药。要会变通，其余照这个类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Choose the medicine by the phase of the Pressure line. A Fire or Earth Pressure line: the illness is surely hot, and should be attacked with cold and cool medicine; a Metal or Water Pressure line: the illness is mostly cold, and is surely treated with warm and hot medicine. A Fire Pressure in a place of birth or strength that also meets generation and support must be attacked with very cold medicine, and with cool medicine if resting, trapped, dead or extinct; a Water Pressure in a place of birth or strength that also meets combination and help needs very hot medicine, and warm medicine if resting, trapped, dead or extinct. A Fire Pressure in a yin palace on a yin line is the syndrome of yin deficiency with stirring fire, and medicine that nourishes yin and lowers fire may be used; a Water Pressure in the inner trigram of a yang palace is the syndrome of depleted blood and qi, and medicine that supplements the centre and boosts qi may be used. One must be flexible; the rest follow this.

> [!warning] 打架
> 有三处两家对不上：一，旧注说「火用寒药、土用凉药、水用热药、金用温药」，王洪绪写成「水必寒、土必凉、火必热、金必温」，水和火对调了，而且和他前一句「火土官爻宜用凉药攻之」也不通，大概是抄写之误；二，讲血气虚损的一种，旧注是「水鬼在阳宫内卦」，王洪绪写成「火鬼在阳宫内卦」；三，旧注在火鬼、水鬼后面各有一句休囚死绝时改用凉药、温药，王洪绪没有，而水鬼遇的是「合助」，王洪绪写成「合住」。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two disagree in three places. First, the old commentary says 'Fire takes cold medicine, Earth cool, Water hot, Metal warm', and Wang Hongxu writes 'Water cold, Earth cool, Fire hot, Metal warm', with Water and Fire exchanged, which also does not agree with his own sentence before it, 'a Fire or Earth Pressure line should be attacked with cool medicine'; probably a copyist's slip. Second, for the syndrome of depleted blood and qi the old commentary has 'a Water Pressure in the inner trigram of a yang palace' and Wang Hongxu 'a Fire Pressure in the inner trigram of a yang palace'. Third, after the Fire and the Water Pressure the old commentary has a sentence each turning to cool and warm medicine when the line is resting, trapped, dead or extinct, which Wang Hongxu lacks; and where the Water Pressure meets 'combination and help', Wang Hongxu writes 'held by a combination'.

> [!note] 按
> 「阴虚火动」是阴液不足、虚火上炎的病；「补中益气」是补脾胃之气、把下陷的中气提起来；「滋阴降火」是养阴液、降虚火。这些是中医的说法，书里没有解释。
>
> *Note:* 'Yin deficiency with stirring fire' is an illness in which the yin fluids are short and deficient fire flares up; 'supplement the centre and boost qi' is to build up the qi of the Spleen and Stomach and lift the sunken qi of the middle; 'nourish yin and lower fire' is to feed the yin fluids and bring down the deficient fire. These are terms of Chinese medicine, which the book does not explain.
