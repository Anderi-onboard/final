# 子孙制鬼最喜旺相生扶：不受刑冲克害、不逢破墓绝空药必见效；子动化鬼化克药不精、化空实空之日有效、化生化旺化进神更灵；古以子动化子药必杂，野鹤说非也，子动化旺子孙是改换药品即比仙丹

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙制服鬼，最喜欢旺相、有生扶。子孙临在世爻、应爻上，或者在卦中发动，也要旺相，不受刑、冲、克、害，不遇破、墓、绝、空，药就一定见效；子孙动了变成鬼、变出克，那药就不精；动了变空，到填实空的那天有效；动了变出生、变旺、变进神，药更灵。古人认为子孙动变子孙，药一定杂乱，不对！子孙动变成旺相的子孙，是另外改换了药品，好比仙丹。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output restrains Pressure and most wants to be strong and to be generated and supported. If Output sits on the Self or Other line, or moves in the hexagram, it too must be strong, suffer no punishment, clash, control or harm, and meet no break, tomb, extinction or Void, and then the medicine surely takes effect; if Output moves and changes into Pressure or into a control, the medicine is not refined; if it moves and changes into Void, it is effective on the day the Void is filled; if it moves and changes into generation, strength or an Advancing spirit, the medicine is even more effective. The ancients held that Output moving and changing into Output means a mixed medicine, but this is wrong! Output moving and changing into an Output that is strong means that the medicines have been changed, and it is as good as an immortal's elixir.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「子化子」是好是坏：黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.65）说子孙重叠、或子孙又变子孙，是医生不精、用药杂；王洪绪读作子孙化进神药有效、化退神与伏吟卦不可服；野鹤直接说「古以子动化子，其药必杂，非也」，变成旺相的子孙是另换药品，好比仙丹。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether 'Output changing into Output' is good or bad: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.65) says that Output doubled, or Output changing again into Output, means an unskilled doctor and mixed medicines; Wang Hongxu reads it as Output changing into an Advancing spirit making the medicine effective, and a change into a Retreating spirit or a Hidden Chant hexagram meaning it may not be taken; the Old Man says outright that 'the ancients judged Output moving and changing into Output as a surely mixed medicine, and this is wrong', and that a change into a strong Output means the medicine is changed, as good as an elixir.
