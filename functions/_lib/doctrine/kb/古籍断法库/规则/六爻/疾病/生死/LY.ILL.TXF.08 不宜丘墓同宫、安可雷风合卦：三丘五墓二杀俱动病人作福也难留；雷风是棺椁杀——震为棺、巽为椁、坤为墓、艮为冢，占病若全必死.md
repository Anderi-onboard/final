# 不宜丘墓同宫、安可雷风合卦：三丘五墓二杀俱动病人作福也难留；雷风是棺椁杀——震为棺、巽为椁、坤为墓、艮为冢，占病若全必死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 不适宜丘墓在同一宫，怎么能雷风合成一卦？注说：三丘、五墓都见后面。这两个煞一起发动，病人做福也留不住；雷风是棺椁杀，震是棺，巽是椁，坤是墓，艮是冢，占病如果都齐了，必死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* It is not fitting for the Mounds and Tombs to be in the same palace; how can Thunder and Wind combine in one hexagram? The commentary says: the Three Mounds and Five Tombs are all given later. When the two killings move together, even if the sick person performs blessing rites he can hardly be kept; Thunder and Wind are the Coffin-and-Outer-Coffin killing, Zhen being the coffin, Xun the outer coffin, Kun the grave and Gen the tomb mound; if in divining an illness all are present, he surely dies.

> [!note] 按
> 「二杀」是三丘和五墓，书里说「俱见后」——后面的凶煞章。三丘五墓的排法（春丑夏辰秋未冬戌为三丘，对宫取五墓）见易林补遗「总论星煞值鬼诀」（LY.ILL.EL.51），「病人作福也难留」一句同是那首歌诀里的原话。「雷风」指震（雷）、巽（风）两卦：震为棺、巽为椁，和易冒、增删卜易里「风雷合卦、棺椁俱全」（LY.ILL.ZS.15）是同一个说法。「占病若全必死」的「全」是丘、墓、棺、椁几样都齐了。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* The 'two killings' are the Three Mounds and the Five Tombs, of which the book says 'all given later' — in the later chapter on malign killings. How the Three Mounds and Five Tombs are set (Chou in spring, Chen in summer, Wei in autumn and Xu in winter for the Three Mounds, the Five Tombs taken from the opposite palace) is in the Yilin Buyi's 'general discourse on ghosts by the stars and killings' (LY.ILL.EL.51), and 'even if the sick person performs blessing rites he can hardly be kept' is the very line of that verse. 'Thunder and Wind' are the Zhen (thunder) and Xun (wind) trigrams: Zhen is the coffin and Xun the outer coffin, the same saying as 'thunder and wind combining, coffin and outer coffin complete' in the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.15). The 'all' of 'if in divining illness all are present' is that the mound, the tomb, the coffin and the outer coffin are all there. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
