# 卦属反伏墓绝亦不治；病未久三冲而愈、病已久三冲而亡，此三者不论用神衰旺；外此专重用神——动散、月破、克空、日破、受伤无援、脱气者危，日月动变有一能救则危而复安

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦是反吟、伏吟、入墓、入绝的，也治不好，即使能治好也会落下病根。病没多久的，遇到三冲就好；病已经久了的，遇到三冲就死；这三种情形不论用神的衰旺。除此之外，就专门看重用神：遇到动散、月破、克空、日破、受伤没有救援、脱气的，危险；遇到日、月、动爻、变爻里有一个能救的，危险了又能转安。注里给了每一种的例子（七种情形都是大凶，得到日、月、动爻、变爻里有一个能救的，就要反过来看）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hexagram that is Reversed, Hidden, in a tomb or in Extinction is likewise untreatable, and even if it can be treated, it becomes a lingering ailment. If the illness is not old, the three clashes cure it; if it is already long, the three clashes bring death; in these three cases the strength or weakness of the Subject is not considered. Apart from this, the Subject is what matters above all: if it meets moving-and-scattering, Month-break, control-into-Void, Day-break, being wounded without help, or drained of qi, it is in danger; if any one of the Day, Month, moving or changed lines can rescue it, danger turns again to safety. The note gives an example of each (the seven cases are all great ill omens, but if any one of the Day, Month, moving or changed lines can rescue, then look at it the other way round).

> [!note] 按
> 原文说「以上七法」，但列出的只有动散、月破、克空、日破、受伤无援、脱气六项名目；把「受伤」「无援」算两件，就是七种，书里没有说明。「动散」是动爻发动而被冲散；「克空」是被克又落空。三冲与久病新病的分法，易隐讲近病逢六冲则愈、久病逢冲则死的地方见 LY.ILL.YY.07，黄金策与增删卜易见 LY.ILL.ZS.02、LY.ILL.ZS.03。
>
> *Note:* The original says 'the above seven methods' but lists only six names — moving-and-scattering, Month-break, control-into-Void, Day-break, wounded-without-help and drained-of-qi; counting 'wounded' and 'without help' as two makes seven, which the book does not explain. 'Moving and scattering' is a moving line that is clashed apart; 'control into Void' is a line controlled and also Void. For the division of recent and long illness under the three clashes, where the Yi Yin says a recent illness is cured by a Six-Clash and a long illness dies, see LY.ILL.YY.07, and for the Huangjin Ce and Zengshan Buyi see LY.ILL.ZS.02 and LY.ILL.ZS.03.
