# 五行脏腑与八卦部位：水腰肾、金肺、火心经、木肝，土爻脾胃休逢杀；乾首坤腹巽手震足兑口艮鼻坎耳离目；一宫杀旺一般灾、子动龙兴保平复；虎鬼同兴应哭泣、龙孙并旺保平安

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水是腰和肾，金是肺，火主心经，木主肝；土爻是脾胃，不要遇上杀；发动、交重时要仔细看。虎和鬼一起兴动，应该哭泣；龙和子孙一起旺，保平安。乾是头，坤是腹，巽是手，震是足，兑是口，艮是鼻，坎主耳，离主目。哪一宫杀旺，就是那一处的灾；子孙动、青龙兴，保平复。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Water is the waist and kidneys, Metal the lungs; Fire governs the heart channel, Wood the liver; the Earth line is the spleen and stomach and must not meet a killing; if it moves or crosses heavily, look carefully. The Tiger and the ghost rising together: there will surely be weeping; the Dragon and Output both strong: peace is assured. Qian is the head and Kun the belly, Xun the hands and Zhen the feet, Dui the mouth and Gen the nose, Kan governs the ears and Li the eyes. Where a killing is strong in one palace, the calamity is the same there; with Output moving and the Dragon rising, recovery is assured.

> [!warning] 打架
> 八卦各管身体哪一处，几处说法不同。阐奥歌章这一首（LY.ILL.CA.02）是「乾首、坤腹、巽手、震足、兑口、艮鼻、坎耳、离目」，巽是手、艮是鼻；断易天机的孙膑断疾病歌（见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）是「乾首、坤腹、坎耳、震足、巽肠、艮手、兑口、离目」，巽是肠、艮是手；断易天机的卜筮元龟一段（见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.12）是「乾头、坤腹、坎耳、离目、兑口、艮手、震足、巽股」，巽是股、艮是手；卜筮正宗的痘疹章（LY.ILL.BSZZ.23）也是「乾头面、坤腹、震足、巽股、艮手、离目、坎耳、兑口」。乾、坤、坎、离、兑、震六个卦几家一致，不同的只是巽（手、肠或股）和艮（鼻或手）。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which part of the body each of the eight trigrams governs is put differently in several places. This poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.02) has 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Xun the hands, Zhen the feet, Dui the mouth, Gen the nose, Kan the ears, Li the eyes', with Xun the hands and Gen the nose; Sun Bin's song in the Duanyi Tianji (see LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) has 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Zhen the feet, Xun the bowels, Gen the hands, Dui the mouth, Li the eyes', with Xun the bowels and Gen the hands; the Bushi Yuangui passage in the Duanyi Tianji (see LY.ILL.DYTJ.12) has 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Li the eyes, Dui the mouth, Gen the hands, Zhen the feet, Xun the thighs', with Xun the thighs and Gen the hands; and the smallpox chapter of the Bushi Zhengzong (LY.ILL.BSZZ.23) likewise has 'Qian the head and face, Kun the belly, Zhen the feet, Xun the thighs, Gen the hands, Li the eyes, Kan the ears, Dui the mouth'. Six of the trigrams (Qian, Kun, Kan, Li, Dui, Zhen) are agreed; the difference is only in Xun (the hands, bowels or thighs) and Gen (the nose or the hands). The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这是第一首的中间十二句：五行配脏腑（水腰肾、金肺、火心、木肝、土脾胃），八卦配部位，和虎鬼同兴、龙孙并旺的判语。「龙孙」是青龙和子孙。「虎鬼同兴」的「虎」是白虎。「土爻脾胃休逢杀」按字面译作「the Earth line is the spleen and stomach and must not meet a killing」。「一宫杀旺一般灾」按字面译，意思是杀在哪一个卦宫旺，就是那一个卦所管的部位遭灾。前面的「水为腰肾金为肺」和黄金策、易隐的五行脏腑大体同一说（LY.ILL.YY.11、HJC.16 一带），只是这里水是「腰肾」、火是「心经」，没有小肠。
>
> *Note:* These are the middle twelve lines of the first poem: the five phases matched with the viscera (Water the waist and kidneys, Metal the lungs, Fire the heart, Wood the liver, Earth the spleen and stomach), the eight trigrams matched with body parts, and the verdicts 'the Tiger and the ghost rising together' and 'the Dragon and Output both strong'. 'The Dragon and Output' are the Azure Dragon and the Output line. The 'Tiger' of 'the Tiger and the ghost rising together' is the White Tiger. 'The Earth line is the spleen and stomach and must not meet a killing' is rendered literally. 'Where a killing is strong in one palace, the calamity is the same' says that where a killing is strong in a given palace, the part that trigram governs meets calamity. The earlier 'Water the waist and kidneys, Metal the lungs' is broadly the same as the five phases and viscera of the Huangjin Ce and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.11, HJC.16 and around), only that here Water is 'waist and kidneys' and Fire 'the heart channel', without the small intestine.
