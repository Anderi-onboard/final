# 鬼神章总纲：欲知鬼神情状亦以筮而得之；天神地祗以官鬼类其象，不外二仪四象五行八卦及六神诸星；索鬼神之性（生前偏好）、司（上帝命司何事）、成（何因缘而成）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 古时候的祭祀，不是那位神的主祭人就不当「尸」，不是鬼的同类就不享用祭品，所以没有越位的祭祀、没有轻慢的祭祀。后来的人有祟就做禳解、求福就去祈祷，鬼神的名目就多起来了；虽然这样，吉事有神帮忙，凶事有鬼凭依，史书传记里记的，并不全是虚妄。想知道鬼神的情形，也可以用占筮得到。天神地祇，就用官鬼来归类它的象；取象、画出它的样子，离不开两仪、四象、五行、八卦，再加上六神、诸星来给它成形，用有无来衡量，用上下来定位。探求这位鬼神的性情、所掌管的事，以及它怎么成为鬼神的，就可以推断了。注解释这三样：「性」是生前偏爱的性情，「司」是上帝命它掌管什么事，「成」是因为什么缘由成为鬼神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the sacrifices of old, one who was not the rightful master did not stand as the impersonator, and a ghost did not savour what was not of its kind, so there were no sacrifices beyond one's station and no profane offerings. Later ages exorcised when there was a haunting and prayed for blessing, and so ghosts and spirits multiplied; even so, good fortune has spirits to help and misfortune has ghosts to possess, and what the histories and traditions record is not all false. To learn the state of ghosts and spirits, this too can be obtained by divination. For heavenly spirits and earthly deities, the image is classified by the Pressure line; the image is taken and drawn, and it does not go beyond the two modes, the four images, the five phases and the eight trigrams, together with the six spirits and the various stars to give it form, presence or absence to measure it, and above or below to place it. Seek the nature of the ghost or spirit, what it governs, and how it came to be one, and it can be worked out. The note explains the three: 'nature' is the disposition it favoured in life, 'what it governs' is what the Supreme Lord appointed it to govern, and 'how it came to be' is by what cause it became a ghost or spirit.

> [!note] 按
> 「尸」是祭祀时代表神或亡者接受祭品的人。「二仪」指阴阳，「四象」是老阳、少阴、少阳、老阴一类的四象。这一章通篇的办法在这里立好了：鬼神只由官鬼爻来看，看的方法有五行、八卦（宫）、六神、诸星、六爻、六亲、十二支；要探的有三样，性、司、成，后面「金木水火土」各有一段，把这三样分开说。开头几句里作者的态度是，鬼神不是全然虚妄的，但也不主张一味祭祀，这一点到章末（LY.ILL.YM.63、YM.64）才收拢。
>
> *Note:* The 'impersonator' (shi) is the person who stands for the god or the dead in a sacrifice and receives the offerings. The 'two modes' are yin and yang, and the 'four images' are the four of old yang, young yin, young yang and old yin. The method of the whole chapter is set up here: ghosts and spirits are read only from the Pressure line, by the five phases, the eight trigrams (palaces), the six spirits, the stars, the six lines, the six relations and the twelve branches; there are three things to seek, nature, charge and origin, and the later paragraphs on Metal, Wood, Water, Fire and Earth each speak of these three separately. In the opening lines the author's attitude is that ghosts and spirits are not wholly false, but he does not urge sacrifice on every occasion, and this is only drawn together at the end of the chapter (LY.ILL.YM.63, YM.64).
