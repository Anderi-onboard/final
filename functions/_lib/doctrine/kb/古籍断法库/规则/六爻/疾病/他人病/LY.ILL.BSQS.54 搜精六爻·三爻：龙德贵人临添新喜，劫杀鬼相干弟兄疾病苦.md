# 搜精六爻·三爻：龙德贵人临添新喜，劫杀鬼相干弟兄疾病苦

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 搜精六爻讲第三爻（门户）时说：龙德、贵人临在上面，添上新的喜事、生出新的门户；劫杀和鬼爻互相干犯，兄弟们疾病受苦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On the third line (the gate and doors) the essay on searching out the essence of the six lines says: with Dragon Virtue and the Noble on it, new joys are added and a new household arises; with the Robbery killing and the ghost interfering with each other, the brothers suffer from illness.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 搜精六爻里第三爻是门户（「三爻为门户」），这一联是第三爻一段里的几联之一：龙德、贵人临它是添喜，劫杀和鬼爻相干，是家里的兄弟生病。「弟兄」指家里的兄弟（人），不是兄弟爻。「劫杀」是一个神煞。同一篇里初爻、二爻、五爻、六爻各有一条讲病（LY.ILL.BSQS.30）。
>
> *Note:* In the essay on searching out the essence of the six lines the third line is the gate and doors ('the third line is the gate and doors'), and this couplet is one of several in the passage on the third line: Dragon Virtue and the Noble on it add joy, and the Robbery killing interfering with the ghost line means the brothers of the house fall ill. 'The brothers' are the brothers of the household (persons), not the Peer line. The 'Robbery killing' is a spirit-star. The first, second, fifth and sixth lines each have an entry on illness in the same essay (LY.ILL.BSQS.30).
