# 用神表·占医生、药物用子孙作用神：子孙即是福德之神，诸事见之为吉，占功名、词讼忌之

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[文王金钱课]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 文王金钱课的用神表说：占儿女、女婿、外甥、门徒、忠臣、医生、药物、僧道（原文写作「曾道」）、兵将、六畜、禽鸟，凡是占小辈的，都用子孙作用神；子孙就是福德之神，诸事见到它都吉利，只有占功名、词讼忌讳它。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The table of the Subject in the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says: in divining children, a son-in-law, a sister's son, disciples, loyal ministers, doctors, medicines, monks and Daoists (written 'zeng dao' in the original), soldiers and officers, livestock and birds, and in general in divining those of the younger generation, use Output as the Subject; Output is the spirit of fortune and virtue and is auspicious to meet in everything, and is only feared in divining fame and rank and lawsuits.

> [!note] 按
> 这一行只管一件事：占医生、药物，用子孙当用神。「子孙为医药」在别的书里也是通例（阐奥歌章 LY.ILL.CA.08、天玄赋 LY.ILL.TXF.12、易林补遗 LY.ILL.EL.26、易隐 LY.ILL.YY.21），这里是用神表里的一项。「曾道」应是「僧道」的误写（「曾」「僧」同音），原文如此，译文按「僧道」译。这一部书的用神表里没有「占病」一项，占病该用什么爻，这部书没有说。括号里「子孙即是福德之神……」是这部书自己的小注。
>
> *Note:* This line is about one thing only: in divining doctors and medicines, Output is the Subject. 'Output is the medicine' is also a general rule in other books (the Chan'ao Gezhang LY.ILL.CA.08, the Tianxuan Fu LY.ILL.TXF.12, the Yilin Buyi LY.ILL.EL.26, the Yi Yin LY.ILL.YY.21), and here it is one item of the table of the Subject. The 'zeng dao' is probably a miswriting of 'monks and Daoists' (the two characters 'zeng' and 'monk' sound alike), as in the original, and the translation reads 'monks and Daoists'. The table of the Subject in this book has no entry for 'divining illness', and the book does not say which line to use for illness. The bracketed 'Output is the spirit of fortune and virtue ...' is the book's own small note.
