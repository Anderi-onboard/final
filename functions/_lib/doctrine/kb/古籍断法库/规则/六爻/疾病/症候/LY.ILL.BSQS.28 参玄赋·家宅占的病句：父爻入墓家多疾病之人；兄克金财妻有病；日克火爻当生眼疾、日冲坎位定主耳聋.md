# 参玄赋·家宅占的病句：父爻入墓家多疾病之人；兄克金财妻有病；日克火爻当生眼疾、日冲坎位定主耳聋

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 参玄赋里讲家宅时提到病的几句：父爻入了墓，家里多有生病的人；兄爻克金财，妻子有病；日辰克火爻，要生眼病；日辰冲坎位，一定主耳聋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The lines on illness in the Canxuan Fu's discussion of a house: with the Resource line entering a tomb, the household has many sick people; with the brother line controlling a Metal Wealth line, the wife is ill; when the Day controls the Fire line, eye disease will arise; when the Day clashes the Kan place, deafness is sure.

## 《卜筮全书·阐幽精要·六、参玄赋》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9686 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Chanyou Jingyao, no. 6: the Canxuan Fu", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日克火爻， | 日辰克火爻， | When the Day controls the Fire line, |
> | 当生眼疾； | 就要生眼病； | eye disease will arise; |
> | 日冲坎位， | 日辰冲坎位， | when the Day clashes the Kan place, |
> | 定主耳聋。 | 一定主耳聋。 | deafness is sure. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 参玄赋是阐幽精要里的一篇赋，占家宅。「兄克金财妻有病」的「兄」是兄爻（兄弟爻），「金财」是五行属金的妻财爻，妻子看财爻，所以是妻子有病；这一句后面接的「弟临火应友多非」讲朋友，不收。「日克火爻」「日冲坎位」的日是日辰。「宅有产亡之鬼」是宅里有难产而死的人的鬼魂，不是病，但和前半句同在一联，所以带着。
>
> *Note:* The Canxuan Fu is a fu in the Chanyou Jingyao, on divining a house. In 'the brother line controls a Metal Wealth line: the wife is ill' the brother line is the Peer line and the Metal Wealth line is the Wealth line of the Metal element; a wife is read from the Wealth line, so it is the wife who is ill; the next clause, about friends, is not taken. In 'the Day controls the Fire line' and 'the Day clashes the Kan place' the Day is the Day branch. 'The ghost of one who died in childbirth' is not illness, but it is in the same couplet as the first half and is carried with it.
