# 总断千金赋·用爻变动忌遭死墓绝空；用化用有用无用（旧注：占病逢死墓绝空必死无疑；用爻化用爻谓之化去占病尤忌；王洪绪：用化进神有用，化退神、伏吟无用）

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「用爻变动，忌遭死、墓、绝、空」，「用化用，有用无用」。旧注：死、墓、绝、空是陷阱一样的大凶之神，主象发动变入这几位，不管公私大小，都主不成；占病遇到，必死无疑；卦中既然有用爻，就不可再化出来，叫「化去」，别的爻化出（用爻）也不济事，所以和没有用爻一样，占病尤其忌讳。王洪绪：用神化用神有「有用的」「没用的」：化进神的有用，化退神和伏吟卦的没用；空爻安静就不能化空，动了就不算空，出旬有用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'When the Subject line moves and changes, it fears meeting Death, Tomb, Extinction or Void'; 'the Subject changing into the Subject: useful or useless'. The old commentary: Death, Tomb, Extinction and Void are greatly ominous spirits like a pit; if the principal image moves and changes into them, public or private, great or small, nothing succeeds; in divining illness, meeting this, death is certain; when the hexagram already has the Subject line it must not change out again, which is called 'changing away', and another line changing out [a Subject line] does not help either, so it is the same as having no Subject line, and in divining illness this is especially feared. Wang Hongxu: a Subject changing into a Subject may be useful or useless: changing into an Advancing spirit is useful, changing into a Retreating spirit or into a Hidden Chant hexagram is useless; a Void line that is still cannot change into Void, and one that moves is not Void and is useful when it comes out of the cycle.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1231 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用神化用神，有有用之用神，有无用之用神。 | 用神变出用神，有有用的用神，有没有用的用神。 | A Subject changing into a Subject may be a useful Subject or a useless Subject. |
> | 有用者，用神化进神； | 有用的，是用神化进神； | The useful one is the Subject changing into an Advancing spirit; |
> | 无用者，用神化退神并伏吟卦也，故以“有用无用”分别之。 | 没有用的，是用神化退神，和伏吟卦，所以用「有用无用」来分别它。 | The useless one is the Subject changing into a Retreating spirit, and the Hidden Chant hexagram, which is why 'useful or useless' is used to tell them apart. |
> | 空爻安静则不能化空，爻发动则能化，既发动，动不空也，化出之空亦因动而化。 | 空爻安静就不能化出空，爻发动才能化，既然发动，动了就不空，化出的空也是因为动才化出的。 | A Void line that is still cannot change into Void; only when the line moves can it change, and having moved it is not Void, and the Void it changes into comes about only because it moved. |
> | 凡动爻值空，或动爻变空，皆不作真空论，出旬有用矣。 | 凡是动爻落在空，或者动爻变成空，都不能当作真空来论，出了旬就有用了。 | Whenever a moving line is in Void or changes into Void, it is not to be judged as truly Void, and once it is out of the cycle it is useful. |

^q6

> [!warning] 打架
> 「用化用」是好是坏，旧注和王洪绪不同。旧注：卦中已经有用爻，不可以再化出来，叫「化去」，别的爻化出（用爻）也不济事，所以有用爻也同没有一样，占病尤其忌讳——只有「不可」，没有分情形；王洪绪：要分「有用」「无用」：用神化进神的有用，化退神和伏吟卦的没用。用爻变动遇死墓绝空，两家一致认为忌，旧注加了一句「占病逢之必死无疑」。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The old commentary and Wang Hongxu differ on whether 'the Subject changing into the Subject' is good or bad. The old commentary: when the hexagram already has the Subject line it must not change out again, which is called 'changing away', and another line changing out a Subject line does not help either, so having a Subject line is the same as having none, and in divining illness this is especially feared; there is only 'it must not', with no distinction of cases. Wang Hongxu: one must tell 'useful' from 'useless': a Subject changing into an Advancing spirit is useful, changing into a Retreating spirit or the Hidden Chant hexagram is useless. On the Subject line moving into Death, Tomb, Extinction or Void the two agree that it is feared, and the old commentary adds 'in divining illness, meeting it, death is certain'. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「主象」就是所占的那一爻（用神）。「化去」是旧注的用词，意思是用爻变出来的又是用爻（化出同类），没有变出新东西。「进神」「退神」是同一五行往前、往后变（寅化卯是进神，卯化寅是退神）；「伏吟」是变出来的爻地支和原来一样。「空化空，虽空弗空」（旧注紧接着的一联）讲的是空，不涉及病，没有收。这一条里「必死无疑」「尤其忌讳」两句是占病的断语，和下面墓多暗昧（LY.ILL.HJC.153）、凶化长生（LY.ILL.HJC.154）几条合起来，是总断千金赋给占病的几个凶断。
>
> *Note:* The 'principal image' is the line being divined (the Subject). 'Changing away' is a term of the old commentary, meaning that what the Subject line changes into is again a Subject line (a change into its own kind), nothing new coming out of the change. 'Advancing spirit' and 'Retreating spirit' are a change forward or backward within the same element (Yin into Mao is advancing, Mao into Yin retreating); 'Hidden Chant' is when the branch changed into is the same as the original. 'Void into Void: though Void, not Void' (the couplet right after in the old commentary) is about Void and does not touch illness, and is not taken. The sentences 'in divining illness, meeting it, death is certain' and 'in divining illness it is especially feared' are verdicts for illness, and together with the entries on the tomb bringing obscurity (LY.ILL.HJC.153) and an ominous line changing into Birth (LY.ILL.HJC.154) they are the several ominous verdicts the Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements gives for illness.
