# 兄弟持世饮食减省：兄弟持世饮食必减，病也因贪食而得；旧注：兄弟不动又无气、财福旺则不是这样，是气短力弱之症难愈

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻持世，饮食一定比平时少，这病也是因为吃得太多得的。旧注补充：如果兄弟不动、又没有气，而卦里财爻、子孙爻旺，就不是这样，那是气短力弱的症候，不容易痊愈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a Peer line holds the Self line the patient surely eats less than usual, and the illness was also got through eating too much. The old commentary adds: if the Peer line does not move and has no force, while Wealth and Output are strong in the hexagram, it is not so; it is then a syndrome of short breath and weak strength, not easily healed.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6926 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)

> | 兄弟持世，饮食必减。 | 兄弟持着世爻，饮食一定减少。 | A Peer line holding the Self line: eating is surely reduced. |
> | 其病亦是贪食而得。 | 这病也是因为贪吃得来的。 | The illness too was got through gluttony. |
> | 若不动又无气，而卦中财福爻旺者，则不然。 | 如果兄弟不动、又没有气，而卦里财爻、福爻旺的，就不是这样。 | If it does not move and has no force, while the Wealth and Output lines in the hexagram are strong, it is not so. |
> | 乃是气短力弱之症，不易痊可。 | 那是气短力弱的症候，不容易痊愈。 | It is then a syndrome of short breath and weak strength, not easily healed. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2390 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟持世，饮食必减，其病亦因多食而得。 | 兄弟持世，饮食一定减少，这病也是因为吃得多得来的。 | A Peer line holding the Self line: eating is surely reduced, and the illness too was got through eating too much. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文写「世挠兄弟」，王洪绪引的经文作「世持兄弟」；两家的注讲的都是兄弟持世。旧注用「贪食」，王洪绪用「多食」，是同一个意思。
>
> *Note:* The verse reads 'the Self troubled by a Peer' where Wang Hongxu quotes it as 'the Self holding a Peer'; both commentaries speak of a Peer holding the Self line. The old commentary says 'gluttony' and Wang Hongxu 'eating much', which come to the same thing.
