# 日破月破分其半成、在内在外分其表里、居变居伏分其新旧：问有病半成、问无病半解

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用日破、月破来分病是成了一半；用鬼在内、在外来分病在里还是在表；用鬼在变爻上还是伏着来分病是新的还是旧的。注说：问有没有病的，病算成了一半；问是不是没有病的，病算解了一半；内是里、外是表，变出来的是新病，伏着的是旧病，就是这个意思。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Day-break and Month-break are used to tell a half-formed illness; being in the inner or in the outer to tell inside from surface; sitting in a change or in hiding to tell new from old. The note says: asked whether there is illness, it is half formed; asked whether there is none, the illness is half resolved; the inner is inside and the outer is surface, the changed is new and the hidden is old: this is the meaning.

> [!note] 按
> 「在内在外」主语没有写明，注里的「内里外表」说的是内卦、外卦；这里按注译作「鬼在内、在外」。「日破月破」不是说病不成，而是说它只成一半：问有病时这是「半成」，问无病时这是「半解」。变爻与伏爻的新旧，和黄金策「官藏世下，病起如前」（LY.ILL.HJC.07）、「官化官新旧两病」（LY.ILL.HJC.09）是同一路读法。
>
> *Note:* The subject of 'in the inner, in the outer' is not stated; the note's 'inside is the inner, surface is the outer' refers to the inner and outer trigrams, and it is rendered here as the ghost being in the inner or the outer. 'Day-break and Month-break' does not say the illness will not form, only that it forms by half: asked whether there is illness this is 'half formed', asked whether there is none this is 'half resolved'. The new and old of a changed and a hidden line follows the same reading as the Huangjin Ce's 'a Pressure line hidden under the Self, the illness rises as before' (LY.ILL.HJC.07) and 'Pressure turning into Pressure, two illnesses new and old' (LY.ILL.HJC.09).
