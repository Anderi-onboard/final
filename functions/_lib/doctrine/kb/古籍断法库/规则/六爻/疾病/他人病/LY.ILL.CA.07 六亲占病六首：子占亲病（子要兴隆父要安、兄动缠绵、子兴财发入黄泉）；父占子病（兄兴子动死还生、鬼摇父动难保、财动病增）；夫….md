# 六亲占病六首：子占亲病（子要兴隆父要安、兄动缠绵、子兴财发入黄泉）；父占子病（兄兴子动死还生、鬼摇父动难保、财动病增）；夫问妻疾（财明子动便安然、鬼爻发动沉重、父发兄摇定断弦）；妻占夫病（鬼静财兴可放心、兄子交重恩义绝、父摇难愈）；兄弟占病（父兴兄静自安全、鬼摇财动黄泉客、子动留连数日）；奴婢疾病（鬼摇更连绵、财安子发无事、父动兄兴不得痊）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子占父母的病：什么时候好？子孙要兴隆，父母要安。兄动，缠绵难脱体；子孙兴、财发，入黄泉。父占子病：兄兴、子动，死里逃生；鬼摇、父动，终究难保；财动，缠绵，病反增。夫问妻的病：财明、子动，便安然；鬼爻发动，还沉重；父发、兄摇，定断弦。妻占夫的病：鬼静、财兴，可以放心；兄、子交重，恩义断绝；父摇，难愈，病沉沉。兄弟来占病：父兴、兄静，自然安全；鬼摇、财动，黄泉客；子动，留连几日卧床。奴婢的病：鬼摇，病更连绵；财安、子发，没有别的事；父动、兄兴，不得痊愈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A son divining a parent's illness, when will it be cured? Output must thrive and Resource must be at peace; Peer moving, it drags on and is hard to shake off; Output rising and Wealth moving, it enters the Yellow Springs. A parent divining a child's illness: Peer rising and Output moving, one dies and yet lives; the ghost shaking and Resource moving, in the end he cannot be kept; Wealth moving, it drags on and the illness increases. A husband asking of his wife's illness: Wealth clear and Output moving, at once she is at ease; the Pressure line moving, it is still heavy; Resource issuing and Peer shaking, surely the string is cut. A wife divining her husband's illness: the ghost still and Wealth rising, one may set the heart at rest; Peer and Output crossing heavily, love and duty are cut; Resource shaking, hard to cure, the illness deep. Brothers coming to divine an illness: Resource rising and Peer still, of itself safe; the ghost shaking and Wealth moving, a guest of the Yellow Springs; Output moving, it lingers several days in bed. A servant's illness: the ghost shaking, the illness drags on the more; Wealth at peace and Output issuing, no other matter; Resource moving and Peer rising, no recovery.

> [!warning] 打架
> 子占父母病要哪一爻兴隆，两个抄本不同。阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.07）是「子占亲病几时痊，子要兴隆父要安」——要子孙兴隆、父母安静；断易天机（见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.08）同一首作「鬼要兴隆父要安」——要官鬼兴隆、父母安静。「子孙旺」是医药有力、能制鬼，「官鬼兴隆」却是病重，前一种更合病理，后一种文字上可能是「子」字误作「鬼」，书里没有说明，这里只如实并列。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line must thrive when a son divines a parent's illness differs in the two copies. The Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.07) has 'a son divining a parent's illness, when will it be cured: Output must thrive and Resource must be at peace' — Output thriving and Resource at peace; the Duanyi Tianji (see LY.ILL.DYTJ.08) has the same poem as 'the ghost must thrive and Resource must be at peace' — the Pressure line thriving and Resource at peace. Output being strong means the medicine is effective and can control the ghost, while the Pressure line thriving means the illness is heavy: the first agrees better with the logic of illness, and the second may be a miswriting of 'ghost' for 'Output', which the book does not explain, and here the two are only set side by side.

> [!note] 按
> 这六首（第六到第十一首）是按病人和占卜人的关系分的六亲占病歌：子占父母、父占子、夫占妻、妻占夫、兄弟占、奴婢占。每首都是「哪一爻动、哪一爻静、哪一爻兴」几个组合的吉凶。「断弦」是妻子去世的委婉说法（琴弦断了）；「黄泉客」是死亡；「财明」是财爻明显出现；「恩义绝」是夫妻情义断绝（兄弟克财而说兄弟伤妻的说法，原文没有解释）。这六首在断易天机里作「经验论家人占病」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.08），文字略有出入（除了上面「打架」里那一处，还有「妻问夫病」等）。用爻表是：子占父母看父母、父占子看子孙、夫占妻看财、妻占夫看鬼（见 LY.ILL.TXF.01）。第十首「兄弟来占疾病缠」的「兄弟」是兄弟这个亲人，不是兄弟爻。
>
> *Note:* These six poems (the sixth to the eleventh) are poems for divining illness by relations, divided by the relation between the sick person and the diviner: a son about a parent, a parent about a child, a husband about a wife, a wife about a husband, a brother, and a servant. Each is the good or ill of a few combinations of 'which line moves, which stays still, which rises'. 'Duan xian' (the string cut) is a delicate way of saying the wife has died (a broken zither string); 'guest of the Yellow Springs' is death; 'Wealth clear' is the Wealth line clearly appearing; 'love and duty cut' is the bond between the couple cut off (the original does not explain why Peer and Output should cut a husband's love from his wife). In the Duanyi Tianji these six appear as 'verses from experience on divining illness by family members' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.08), with slight differences in wording (besides the one place in the 'dispute' above, also 'the wife asks of her husband's illness' and so on). The table of the Subject line: a son about a parent looks at Resource, a parent about a child at Output, a husband about a wife at Wealth, a wife about a husband at the ghost (see LY.ILL.TXF.01). The 'brothers' of the tenth poem, 'brothers come to divine an illness that clings', are brothers as relatives and not the Peer line.
