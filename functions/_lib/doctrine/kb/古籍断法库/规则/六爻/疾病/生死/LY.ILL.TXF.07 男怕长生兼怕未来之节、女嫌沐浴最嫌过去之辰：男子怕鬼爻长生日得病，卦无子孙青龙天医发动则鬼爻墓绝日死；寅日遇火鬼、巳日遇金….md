# 男怕长生兼怕未来之节、女嫌沐浴最嫌过去之辰：男子怕鬼爻长生日得病，卦无子孙青龙天医发动则鬼爻墓绝日死；寅日遇火鬼、巳日遇金鬼、申日水土鬼、亥日木鬼谓之长生鬼；先看鬼爻衰旺再论节气（男怕未来节、近节必重、过节无伤）；女人怕鬼爻沐浴日得病（卯日火鬼、午日金鬼、酉日水土鬼、子日木鬼），过节后必重

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男人怕长生，还怕未来的节气；女人嫌沐浴，最嫌过去的日子。注说：男子怕在鬼爻长生的日子得病，和鬼一起衰旺；卦里没有子孙、青龙、天医发动的，鬼爻墓绝的日子就死。又说：寅日遇火鬼、巳日遇金鬼、申日遇水土鬼、亥日遇木鬼，叫长生鬼；占的人遇上，大凶在后面，沉重才会来。凡决断安危，先按鬼爻的衰旺推断，再按节气来论：男人怕未来的节，临近节令必定加重，过了节就没有伤害；节前不重的，一定没有妨碍；如果是久病，应当以四季节候来论，比如木鬼忌秋天的节气，遇节不重，过节一定安。女人最忌在鬼爻沐浴的日子得病。又说：卯日遇火鬼、午日遇金鬼、酉日遇水土鬼、子日遇木鬼，叫沐浴鬼；遇上难痊愈，到后来反而加重；再看节后必定加重，不加重就没有伤害；女人极怕过后的节。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A man fears the Birth stage and also fears the coming solar term; a woman dislikes the Bath stage and most dislikes the term that has passed. The commentary says: a man fears falling ill on a day when the Pressure line is at Birth, and it rises and falls with the ghost; if the hexagram has no Output, Azure Dragon or Heavenly Doctor moving, he dies on a day when the Pressure line is in a tomb or at Extinction. It says again: a Fire ghost met on a Yin day, a Metal ghost on a Si day, a Water or Earth ghost on a Shen day, a Wood ghost on a Hai day, is called a Birth ghost; one who meets it will have great ill afterwards, and the heaviness only comes later. To decide danger and safety, first judge by the strength of the Pressure line, and then by the solar term: a man fears the coming term, near the term it is surely heavier, and past the term there is no harm; if it is not heavy before the term, there is surely no obstruction; if it is a long illness, one should reason by the seasonal terms of the four seasons, for example a Wood ghost fears the autumn terms, and if on meeting the term it is not heavy, after the term it will surely be at peace. A woman most fears falling ill on a day when the Pressure line is at Bath. It says again: a Fire ghost met on a Mao day, a Metal ghost on a Wu day, a Water or Earth ghost on a You day, a Wood ghost on a Zi day, is called a Bath ghost; met, it is hard to cure, and it becomes heavier afterwards; look next at the term after, when it is surely heavier, and if it is not heavy there is no harm; a woman greatly fears the term that has passed.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节讲的是「长生鬼」和「沐浴鬼」：鬼爻长生的日子、沐浴的日子得病，加上节气的前后，判轻重。「长生」是十二长生的第一位（生），「沐浴」是第二位；寅日遇火鬼是火长生在寅，巳日遇金鬼是金长生在巳，申日遇水土鬼是水土长生在申，亥日遇木鬼是木长生在亥——所以叫长生鬼；沐浴鬼：卯日火鬼、午日金鬼、酉日水土鬼、子日木鬼，正是沐浴的位置。「未来之节」是还没到的节气，「过去之辰」是已过的日子/节气，男怕节前、女怕节后：男人在近节时必重、过节无伤；女人在节后必重。「四时节候」是二十四节气。这一段和易隐（LY.ILL.YY.10「男忌官鬼长生日得病，女忌官鬼沐浴日得病」）、黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.05 一带）是同一说，只是这里多了节气的讲法。男女判轻重的方向是相反的，这是这本书的规定，没有解释。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* This section is about the 'Birth ghost' and the 'Bath ghost': falling ill on a day when the Pressure line is at Birth or at Bath, together with what comes before or after the solar term, decides light or heavy. 'Birth' is the first place of the twelve stages of growth, and 'Bath' the second; a Fire ghost on a Yin day is Fire's Birth at Yin, a Metal ghost on a Si day is Metal's Birth at Si, a Water or Earth ghost on a Shen day is Water and Earth's Birth at Shen, and a Wood ghost on a Hai day is Wood's Birth at Hai — hence 'Birth ghost'; the Bath ghost: Fire on Mao, Metal on Wu, Water and Earth on You, Wood on Zi, which are exactly the Bath places. 'The coming term' is the solar term not yet arrived, and 'the term that has passed' is the day or term that has gone by: a man fears before the term and a woman after: for a man near the term it is surely heavy and past the term there is no harm; for a woman after the term it is surely heavy. The 'seasonal terms of the four seasons' are the twenty-four solar terms. This passage says the same as the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.10, 'a man should fear falling ill on the day the Pressure line is born, a woman on the day it is at Bath') and the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.05 and around), only with the added account of the solar terms. That the man's and the woman's directions of judging light and heavy are opposite is this book's rule and is not explained. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
