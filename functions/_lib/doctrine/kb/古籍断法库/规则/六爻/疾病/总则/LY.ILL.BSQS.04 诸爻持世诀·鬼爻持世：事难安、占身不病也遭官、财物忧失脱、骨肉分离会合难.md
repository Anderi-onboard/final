# 诸爻持世诀·鬼爻持世：事难安、占身不病也遭官、财物忧失脱、骨肉分离会合难

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 诸爻持世诀说：官鬼爻持世，事情难得安稳；占自身，就算没有病，也会遇上官司；财物时时担心丢失，亲人分离了很难再聚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on each line holding the Self line says: with the Pressure line holding the Self line, matters are hard to settle; if one divines about oneself, even without illness one meets an official case; property is always at risk of loss, and kin who are parted are hard to bring together again.

## 《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·三、诸爻持世诀》原文

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「占身」是占问的人自己，歌里没有说是占身体还是占身命，译文照字面写成「占自身」。「不病也遭官」的意思是官鬼持世时，占自身，不是有病就是有官司，两样里总要占一样。这首诀一共六句，父母、子孙、官鬼、财爻、兄弟各持世一句，这里只收和病有关的官鬼一句；同样的诀在火珠林（第 945 行）里也有。
>
> *Note:* 'Divining about one's person' is the asker himself; the verse does not say whether it means the body or one's fortune, and the translation keeps it literal as 'about oneself'. 'If not ill, then an official case' means that with Pressure on the Self line, in a divination about oneself, if it is not illness it will be a lawsuit, one or the other. The verse has six lines, one each for Resource, Output, Pressure, Wealth and Peer holding the Self line; only the Pressure line, which bears on illness, is taken here. The same verse also appears in the Huozhu Lin (line 945).
