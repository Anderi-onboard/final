# 官鬼持世本有旧病根，再伤用爻是旧病复发，否则难脱体，须保禳；卦身持鬼也是旧病

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻持世，病本来有旧根；再伤到用爻，一定是旧病又犯。不然也难脱身，要靠祈禳保佑才吉。卦身上坐着鬼，也是旧病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a Pressure line holds the Self line, the illness surely has an old root; if it also wounds the Subject line, it is an old illness breaking out again. Otherwise it is hard to shake off, and protective rites are needed to be lucky. A Pressure line on the hexagram Body is also an old illness.

## 《黄金策·疾病》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6849 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世官伤用，重发旧日之灾。 | 世爻上的官鬼伤到用爻，重新发作的是往日的灾病。 | A Pressure line on the Self line that wounds the Subject line: the trouble of former days breaks out again. |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2347 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 大抵官爻持世，必然原有病根，伤用必是旧病复发，否则必难脱体。 | 大体上官爻持世，一定原来有病根，伤到用爻一定是旧病复发，不然一定难以脱身。 | In general, when a Pressure line holds the Self line, there is surely an old root of illness from before; wounding the Subject line, it is surely an old illness breaking out again; otherwise it is surely hard to shake off. |
> | 卦身持鬼，亦是旧病。 | 卦身上坐着鬼，也是旧病。 | A Pressure line on the hexagram Body is also an old illness. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「保禳」是祈祷禳解、求神保佑一类的仪式。旧注末尾「须用保禳则吉」，王洪绪没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Baorang' is the kind of rite in which one prays, averts and asks the spirits for protection. Wang Hongxu does not take the old commentary's closing 'protective rites are needed to be lucky'.
