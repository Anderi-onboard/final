# 月将值日·小吉（月建未）占病：头痛、三焦热闷腹疼、浑身沉重；墓杀最凶；未宫卦断伤亡死鬼土地作祟、请巫师禳让药方通

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在未、叫小吉的时候占病：头痛，三焦发热发闷，腹中疼痛，全身沉重，难以起身；飞来的墓杀最凶；卯方得的病会绕一圈又回来。「未宫卦断」那一段说：作祟的是横死的亡魂、死去的鬼和土地，让病人说胡话；板木没有备齐，就凶，多怪梦；要请巫师禳解让退，药方才通。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on Wei, named Xiaoji, divining illness: headache, heat and oppression in the Triple Burner, pain in the belly, the whole body heavy so that he can hardly rise; the Tomb Killer that flies in is the most ominous; an illness taken in the Mao direction goes around and comes back. The passage 'the verdict for the Wei-palace hexagrams' says: the ones at work are the dead by injury, the dead ghosts and the Earth God, making the patient talk deliriously; if the wooden boards are not complete it is ominous, with strange dreams; a shaman should be asked to perform rites of averting and yielding, and then the medicine will work.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。这一首里读不顺的地方：「月建值末各小吉」的「末」是「未」、「各」是「名」的误写；「浑身沉重难起例」的「例」按上下文读作倒、卧；「飞来墓杀」「卯方得病绕回转」「南北社廊鬼相衡」书里都没有解释，「相衡」译成「互相抵着」是照字面；「作崇令人谵语逢」最后一个「逢」字和下一句「板木末全凶怪梦」连不上，像是脱了字，译文把它留在句末；「板木末全」的「末」读作「未」（没有备齐），这是读法；「禳让」译成「禳解让退」也是照字面。「三焦」是中医里的三焦（上焦、中焦、下焦），书里没有展开。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. Places in this verse that do not read smoothly: in 'when the Month falls on Wei, named Xiaoji' the book prints the wrong characters for 'Wei' and for 'named', plainly misprints; 'the whole body is heavy and he can hardly rise' has a last character read from context as 'lie down' or 'fall'; the 'flying Tomb Killer', 'an illness taken in the Mao direction goes around and turns back' and 'the ghosts of the altar and the corridor in the south and north face each other' are not explained in the book, and 'face each other' is the literal sense; the final character of 'they make trouble and make the patient talk deliriously, and meeting' does not connect with the next line, 'wooden boards not complete, ominous, strange dreams', and looks as if a character has dropped out, so the translation leaves it at the end of the line; the 'not' of 'boards not complete' is printed with a character that is read as 'not yet' (not provided in full), which is a reading; 'rites of averting and yielding' is again the literal sense. The 'Triple Burner' is the Triple Burner of Chinese medicine (upper, middle and lower), which the book does not expand.
