# 用象强弱与变化：用象虽弱逢日月生扶无咎；用静不必细详，用动推变化（变生扶吉、变墓绝凶）；用爻有气元神动忌神纵发不须惊；主象休囚元神静忌神动或被月日伐则后必伤躯；用象临旺遇生扶有起死回生之兆

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用象虽然落在弱地，如果遇上日、月来生扶，一定没有灾咎；用象如果安静，不必细究，如果发动，就必须推它的变化，变出生扶就吉，变成墓、绝就凶。歌诀说：用爻有气、元神发动，忌神纵然发动也不必惊慌；主象休囚，又加上被克被破，身体即使暂时没病也会倾覆；用神旺相又遇扶助，病即使到了危险的地步，反而主生。注说：得到用爻旺相、元神又动，日、月即使来伤它，也永远不受克，忌神虽然发动，也不成灾殃；主象如果休囚、元神又静，忌神又正在发动、或者用爻被月建、日辰所伐，身体虽然只是小病，后来一定伤及身躯；用象如果落在旺地又遇生扶，一定有起死回生的征兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Though the Subject image sits in a weak place, if the Day and Month generate and support it, there is surely no fault; if the Subject is still, there is no need to examine it closely, and if it moves, one must work out its change, a change into generation or support being auspicious and a change into a tomb or Extinction ominous. The verse says: with the Subject line in qi and the Support moving, even if the Adversary moves there is no need for alarm; with the chief image resting or trapped and also controlled and broken, though the body is for the moment unharmed it will still collapse; with the Subject strong and meeting support, though the illness reaches danger it on the contrary means life. The commentary says: if the Subject line is strong and the Support moves too, then even if the Day and Month come to wound it, it is never controlled, and though the Adversary moves, it does no harm; if the chief image is resting or trapped, the Support is still, and the Adversary is moreover moving or the Subject is attacked by the Month or Day, then though the body has only a minor illness, in the end the body will be harmed; if the Subject image is in a strong place and meets generation and support, there is surely a sign of rising from death to life.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和 LY.ILL.EL.08 同出一段，读法是：用象的强弱先看，然后看它动不动；动了看变爻（变生扶吉，变墓绝凶）；再配上元神、忌神。「体虽无恙也遭倾」的「体」是身体。与增删卜易「动墓绝、化墓绝须逢生旺」（LY.ILL.ZS.07）、易冒「用神有一生则生，有一死则死」（LY.ILL.YM.16）是同一件事。
>
> *Note:* This entry comes from the same passage as LY.ILL.EL.08 and reads: first the strength of the Subject image, then whether it moves; if it moves, the changed line (a change into generation or support is auspicious, into a tomb or Extinction ominous); and then the Support and the Adversary are set beside it. The 'body' in 'though the body is unharmed it collapses' is the physical body. It is the same matter as the Zengshan Buyi's 'moving into a tomb or Extinction, or changing into one, needs generation or strength' (LY.ILL.ZS.07) and the Yi Mao's 'if the Subject has one generation it lives, if one death it dies' (LY.ILL.YM.16).
