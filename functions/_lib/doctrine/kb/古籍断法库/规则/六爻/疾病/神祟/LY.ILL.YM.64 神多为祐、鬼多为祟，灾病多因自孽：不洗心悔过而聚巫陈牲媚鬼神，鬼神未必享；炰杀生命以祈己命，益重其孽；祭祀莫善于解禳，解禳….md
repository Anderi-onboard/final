# 神多为祐、鬼多为祟，灾病多因自孽：不洗心悔过而聚巫陈牲媚鬼神，鬼神未必享；炰杀生命以祈己命，益重其孽；祭祀莫善于解禳，解禳莫善于修身

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大体上神多半是保佑的，鬼多半是作祟的；灾病的到来，多半是自己造的孽。如果不洗心悔过、除掉孽的根本，只是聚集巫师、摆上牲畜去讨好鬼神，鬼神未必享用。何况宰杀生灵来为自己的性命祈祷，那是加重自己的罪孽。所以说：祭祀没有比解禳更好的，解禳没有比修身更好的，说的就是这个。我在鬼神这一章里，再三告诫。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In general spirits mostly bless and ghosts mostly haunt; for calamity and illness mostly come from one's own doing, and if one does not cleanse the heart and repent to remove the root of the fault, but merely gathers shamans and lays out animal victims to please ghosts and spirits, the ghosts and spirits will not necessarily partake. How much more so to roast and slaughter living creatures to pray for my own life: that only makes the fault heavier. Hence it is said: of sacrifice none is better than exorcism, and of exorcism none is better than cultivating oneself; this is what it means. In this chapter on ghosts and spirits I have given warning again and again.

> [!warning] 打架
> 祭祀送鬼有没有用：易冒说灾病多因自孽，不修身而只靠巫祝牲牢献媚鬼神，鬼神未必享用，杀生祈命更是加重罪孽，祭祀最好不过解禳、解禳最好不过修身；增删卜易的野鹤同样反对靠杀牲救命（LY.ILL.ZS.25），但承认时灾瘟癖照法祭送屡验；黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.93、HJC.50）和易隐（LY.ILL.YY.27、YY.31）则把祈禳的对象、祭品、方法一项项讲出来，把祭祀当作要认真做的事。三家不是互相否定，重点不同：易冒重修身，野鹤按病分，黄金策和易隐重办法。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether sacrifice and sending away ghosts is of use: the Yi Mao says calamity and illness mostly come from one's own doing, that without cultivating oneself, currying favour with ghosts and spirits by means of shamans and animal victims is not necessarily partaken of, that slaughtering to pray for life makes the fault heavier, and that of sacrifice none is better than exorcism and of exorcism none is better than cultivating oneself; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise opposes slaughter to save a life (LY.ILL.ZS.25), but admits that sacrifice and sending away by the method has been proved many times for seasonal calamities and abdominal masses; the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.93, HJC.50) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.27, YY.31) set out object, offering and method of prayer and exorcism item by item and treat sacrifice as something to be done with care. The three do not deny one another; their emphasis differs: the Yi Mao stresses self-cultivation, the Old Man of Yehe distinguishes by illness, the Huangjin Ce and the Yi Yin stress procedure.

> [!note] 按
> 「炰杀生命」的「炰」是煮烤，「牲」是祭祀用的牲畜，「巫」是巫师。「吾于鬼神章，兽三致戒云」的「兽」字放在这里说不通，像是「再」「屡」一类字的误刻；「三致戒」是再三告诫，这一层意思是清楚的，这里译成「再三告诫」，没有替这个字定读音。「祭祀莫善于解禳，解禳莫善于修身」是作者引的成语（前面有「故曰」），书里没有说出处。这一句正是 LY.ILL.YM.55 里「伏鬼若空则无鬼祟，惟修德」的落点。
>
> *Note:* 'Roast and slaughter' (pao sha) is boiling and roasting, 'victims' are the animals used in sacrifice, and 'shamans' are wu. The 'shou' (beast) in 'in the chapter on ghosts and spirits I have given warning shou-three times' does not make sense here and looks like a miscarved form of a word such as 'again' or 'repeatedly'; that it means 'has given warning three times over', that is, again and again, is clear, and it is rendered 'again and again' without deciding what the character should be. 'Of sacrifice none is better than exorcism, and of exorcism none is better than cultivating oneself' is quoted by the author as a saying (it is introduced by 'hence it is said'), and the book gives no source. This sentence is where 'if the hidden ghost is also Void there is no haunting, and only cultivating virtue remains' in LY.ILL.YM.55 lands.
