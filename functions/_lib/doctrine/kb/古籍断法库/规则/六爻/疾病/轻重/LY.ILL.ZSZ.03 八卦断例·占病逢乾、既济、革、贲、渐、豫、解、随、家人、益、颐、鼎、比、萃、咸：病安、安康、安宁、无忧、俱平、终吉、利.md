# 八卦断例·占病逢乾、既济、革、贲、渐、豫、解、随、家人、益、颐、鼎、比、萃、咸：病安、安康、安宁、无忧、俱平、终吉、利

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 周易尚占八卦断例里，这十五个卦的断辞说病平安、顺利：乾「问病获安」，既济「病者安康」，革「孕病皆吉」，贲「病安婚遂」，渐「病疾渐安」，豫「病者获安」，解「病者安宁」，随「疾病安康」，家人「病讼无忧」，益「词病俱平」，颐「讼吉病安」，鼎「婚成病利」，比「讼病解散」，萃「病讼终吉」，咸「利讼利病」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the judgments by example of the Zhouyi Shangzhan the line of each of these fifteen hexagrams says that illness fares peacefully and well: Qian (Heaven), 'asking about illness: safety is gained'; Jiji, 'the sick are at peace and in health'; Ge, 'pregnancy and illness are both auspicious'; Bi (Adornment), 'the sick are safe, marriage is achieved'; Jian (Gradual Progress), 'illness gradually settles'; Yu, 'the sick gain peace'; Xie, 'the sick are at ease'; Sui, 'illness is eased and health returns'; Jiaren, 'neither illness nor lawsuit brings worry'; Yi (Increase), 'lawsuit and illness are both calm'; Yi (Nourishment), 'the lawsuit is auspicious and the illness safe'; Ding, 'marriage is made, illness fares well'; Bi (Union), 'lawsuit and illness are dissolved'; Cui, 'illness and lawsuit end auspiciously'; Xian, 'favourable to lawsuits and favourable to illness'.

> [!warning] 打架
> 贲卦这里说「病安婚遂」，别的书却把贲列进占病的凶卦：卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.15）和阐奥歌章第五首（LY.ILL.CA.06）都有贲，天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.06）说贲「切忌世身入墓」（有条件的），增删卜易（LY.ILL.ZS.01）转述的「断之必死」的十个卦里也有贲。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Of these, the line of Bi (Adornment) says 'the sick are safe, marriage is achieved', yet other books put Bi among the ominous hexagrams for illness: the passage of the Bushi Yuangui (LY.ILL.DYTJ.15) and the fifth poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.06) both have Bi, the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.06) says of Bi 'above all avoid the Self or the Body entering the tomb' (conditional), and the ten hexagrams of 'judge death' reported in the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.01) include Bi. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这十五行的说法有轻有重：乾、既济、豫、解、随说得最干脆（获安、安康、安宁），渐是「渐安」（慢慢好），萃是「终吉」（最后吉），革、家人、益、颐、比、咸、鼎的病只是和别的事并说（「孕病皆吉」「病讼无忧」），没有单独给病一个断语；「词病俱平」的「词」是词讼、官司。「利讼利病」「婚成病利」的「利」是有利。各行里别的词组（田蚕、官司、婚姻、孕、出行等）对问病没有用处，但一并录在这里，是为了让每一行保持完整、可以对着原书查。书里同一个卦有两个同音的名字（乾和谦，益和颐，贲和比，蹇和渐），英文名后面括号里写的是卦的意思，用来区分。原文「媾」「畜」等写法见各行。上面比较各卦用词轻重的话是编者的归纳，不是书里的话。
>
> *Note:* The statements of these fifteen lines range from light to heavy: Qian (Heaven), Jiji, Yu, Xie and Sui are the most outright (gain safety, at peace and in health, at ease); Jian (Gradual Progress) says 'gradually settles' (getting better slowly), and Cui says 'ends auspiciously' (auspicious in the end); for Ge, Jiaren, Yi (Increase), Yi (Nourishment), Bi (Union), Xian and Ding illness is only named together with other matters ('pregnancy and illness are both auspicious', 'neither illness nor lawsuit brings worry') without a verdict of its own; the 'ci' of 'ci and illness are both calm' is a lawsuit or pleading. The 'favourable' of 'favourable to lawsuits and favourable to illness' and 'marriage is made, illness fares well' is 'to the advantage of'. The other phrases of each line (fields and silkworms, lawsuits, marriage, pregnancy, travel and so on) are not useful to a question on illness but are all kept here so that each line stays whole and can be checked against the original book. Some hexagram names in the book have a homonym (Qian for Heaven and for Humility, Yi for Increase and for Nourishment, Bi for Adornment and for Union, Jian for Obstruction and for Gradual Progress), and the parenthesis after the English name gives the meaning of the hexagram to tell them apart. The variant ways of writing a name in the original, such as the 'union' character for Gou, are seen in the lines themselves. The comparison of the strength of the words used in each hexagram above is the editor's summary and not the book's own words.
