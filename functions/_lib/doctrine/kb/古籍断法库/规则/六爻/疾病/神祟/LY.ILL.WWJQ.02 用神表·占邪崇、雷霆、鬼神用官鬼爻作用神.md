# 用神表·占邪崇、雷霆、鬼神用官鬼爻作用神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[文王金钱课]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 文王金钱课的用神表说：占官、官府、功名、（妻占父）、乱臣、贼盗、邪崇、雷霆、鬼神、词讼，凡是以上这些，都用官鬼爻作用神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The table of the Subject in the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says: in divining officials, the authorities, fame and rank, (a wife divining the father), rebellious ministers, thieves and robbers, evil curses, thunder, ghosts and spirits, and lawsuits, in all of these use the Pressure line as the Subject.

## 《文王金钱课·用神》原文

> [!quote]+ [[文王金钱课]] 第 36 行 · Wenwang Jinqian Ke (King Wen's Coin Readings), "The Table of the Subject", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占官、官府、功名、妻占父、乱臣、贼盗、邪崇、雷霆、鬼神、词讼、凡以上俱官鬼爻为用神。 | 占官、官府、功名、（妻占父）、乱臣、贼盗、邪崇、雷霆、鬼神、词讼，凡是以上这些，都用官鬼爻作用神。 | In divining officials, the authorities, fame and rank, a wife divining the father, rebellious ministers, thieves and robbers, evil curses, thunder, ghosts and spirits, and lawsuits, in all of the above use the Pressure line as the Subject. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一行列了官鬼爻作用神的各件事，「邪崇」「雷霆」「鬼神」就是占病时的「鬼」：疾病由什么鬼神作祟，在别的书里也是看官鬼（LY.ILL.BSYG.01、LY.ILL.EL.29）。「妻占父」按上下文应是「妻占夫」（妻占丈夫以官鬼为用神），原文如此，译文照字面。「崇」是「祟」的写法，按「curse」译。
>
> *Note:* This line lists the matters for which the Pressure line is the Subject, and 'evil curses', 'thunder' and 'ghosts and spirits' are the 'ghost' of divining illness: what ghost or spirit afflicts an illness is also read from Pressure in other books (LY.ILL.BSYG.01, LY.ILL.EL.29). 'A wife divining the father' should from the context be 'a wife divining the husband' (a wife divining her husband takes Pressure as the Subject), as in the original, and the translation is literal. The character for 'curse' is written in its variant form, and is rendered 'curse'.
