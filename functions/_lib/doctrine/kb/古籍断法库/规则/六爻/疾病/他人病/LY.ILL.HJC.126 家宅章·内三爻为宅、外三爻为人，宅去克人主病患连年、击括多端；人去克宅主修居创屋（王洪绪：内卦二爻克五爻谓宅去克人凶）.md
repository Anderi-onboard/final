# 家宅章·内三爻为宅、外三爻为人，宅去克人主病患连年、击括多端；人去克宅主修居创屋（王洪绪：内卦二爻克五爻谓宅去克人凶）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》家宅章说：「要看卦爻的内外，才能知道人和宅的兴衰。」旧注：内三爻是宅，外三爻是人；宅去克人，主病患连年，多有吵闹骚扰；人去克宅，主修缮住房、创建房屋、整旧更新。王洪绪：内卦第二爻克外卦第五爻，叫宅去克人，凶；外卦五爻克二爻，叫人来克宅，吉；内卦二爻生五爻，叫宅去生人，吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the household says: 'Look at the inner and outer of the lines to know the rise and fall of the people and the house.' The old commentary: the inner three lines are the house and the outer three the people; when the house controls the people, it means years of illness and disturbances of many kinds; when the people control the house, it means repairing a dwelling or building a house, mending the old and renewing. Wang Hongxu: the second line of the inner trigram controlling the fifth line of the outer trigram is called the house controlling the people, ominous; the fifth line of the outer trigram controlling the second is called the people controlling the house, auspicious; the second line of the inner trigram generating the fifth is called the house generating the people, auspicious.

> [!warning] 打架
> 宅和人各取哪几爻，两家不一样。《黄金策》旧注是「内三爻为宅，外三爻为人」（整个内卦是宅、整个外卦是人）；王洪绪是「内卦第二爻为宅舍，外卦第五爻为人口」（各取一爻）。宅去克人，旧注点明是「病患连年」，王洪绪只说「凶」，没有点名是病。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two commentaries take different lines for the house and the people. The old commentary of the Huangjin Ce has 'the inner three lines are the house and the outer three lines the people' (the whole inner trigram is the house and the whole outer trigram the people); Wang Hongxu has 'the second line of the inner trigram is the dwelling and the fifth line of the outer trigram is the household members' (one line each). On the house controlling the people, the old commentary names 'years of illness', while Wang Hongxu says only 'ominous' without naming illness. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「击括」二字照原文，指吵闹、骚扰一类的事（柳神经里也有「家门括扰」），译文写作「disturbances」；这里不另作解释。同一章千金赋后面还有一联「丧门加白虎，须忧疾病丧亡」（LY.ILL.HJC.127）。
>
> *Note:* The two characters rendered 'disturbances' are kept as in the text and mean clamour and trouble of that kind (the Liushen Jing also has 'the gate of the house is troubled'); no further explanation is given here. A later couplet of the same rhapsody has 'the Mourning Gate with the White Tiger: beware of illness and death' (LY.ILL.HJC.127).
