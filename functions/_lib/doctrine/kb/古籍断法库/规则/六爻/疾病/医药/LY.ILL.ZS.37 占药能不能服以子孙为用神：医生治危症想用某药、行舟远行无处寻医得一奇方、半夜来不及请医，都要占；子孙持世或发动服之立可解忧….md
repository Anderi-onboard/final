# 占药能不能服以子孙为用神：医生治危症想用某药、行舟远行无处寻医得一奇方、半夜来不及请医，都要占；子孙持世或发动服之立可解忧；子孙不拘五行，旺或动即喜悦之神，不可因金临子孙用针、火属子孙用炙（例：热极用附子肉桂、人参、牛黄）

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占药能不能服，拿子孙当用神。医生治垂危的病、想用某味药，可以起死回生，又怕一有闪失；泛海行船、长途旅行没有地方找医生、有人传来奇方；半夜来不及请医、手边的古方或家里的药散确实对这个病却不敢擅用，都要占。只要子孙持世或发动，服了立刻可以解忧，必定见效。子孙不管是金木水火土哪一行，旺相或发动就是喜悦的神；不可以又因为金临子孙就用针、火属子孙就用灸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine whether a medicine may be taken, take Output as the Subject. It should be divined when a doctor treating a dying patient wants a certain medicine that may bring him back from death, and fears one failure; when one is crossing the sea or on a long journey with nowhere to find a doctor and someone passes on a rare prescription; when in the depth of night there is no time to call a doctor and the old prescriptions or powders kept at home truly fit the illness but one does not dare use them on one's own authority. If Output holds the Self line or moves, taking it at once relieves the worry, and it will surely work. Whatever phase Output is, metal, wood, water, fire or earth, so long as it is strong or moving it is the spirit of joy; one must not take Metal on Output to mean the needle, or Fire as Output to mean moxibustion.

> [!warning] 打架
> 占药看什么：黄金策旧注、王洪绪讲用药（LY.ILL.HJC.76 起）是看官鬼的五行、子孙的五行来定药；李我平、野鹤说占某味药能不能服，拿子孙当用神，子孙持世或发动服了就见效，不拘五行。同样是儿子占父亲的药，一卦看父爻（人参）、一卦看子孙（牛黄），野鹤说要看哪一个动、哪一个持世，在人通变。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What to look at in divining medicine: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu fix the medicine (from LY.ILL.HJC.76 on) by the phase of the Pressure line and of Output; Li Woping and the Old Man say that in divining whether a given medicine may be taken, Output is the Subject, and if Output holds the Self line or moves it works when taken, whatever its phase. Of two hexagrams of a son divining medicine for his father, one looks at the Resource line (ginseng) and the other at Output (bezoar), and the Old Man says one must see which one moves and which holds the Self line, and adapt.

> [!note] 按
> 书中例是 LY.ILL.ZS.36 里那个孩子：在山东受暑回来，卧冰吹风后满床乱滚，医生已经下了凉药。野鹤连占几卦，子孙动、酉金兄弟动来生它，断定要用附子、肉桂这样的大热药，服后果然好了。觉子说，前辈看到酉金兄动，又会把兄爻当成药，这是不对的。「使姻掌扇」的「姻」按上下文大概是「侄女」之误，译文按字面。人参、牛黄两例讲的是同一个道理：占药要看的是喜悦之神。
>
> *Note:* The case is the child of LY.ILL.ZS.36, who had taken the heat in Shandong and after lying on ice in the wind rolled about the bed, the doctor having already given cold medicine. The Old Man divined several hexagrams in a row, found Output moving and You Metal Peer moving to generate it, judged that a very hot medicine such as aconite and cinnamon bark was needed, and he recovered after taking it. Juezi says that the worthies of old would again have taken the moving Peer line as the medicine, which is wrong. The 'in-law' of 'an in-law made to fan him' is from the context probably a slip for 'niece', and the translation follows the letter. The ginseng and bezoar cases speak of the same principle: what one looks at in divining medicine is the spirit of joy.
