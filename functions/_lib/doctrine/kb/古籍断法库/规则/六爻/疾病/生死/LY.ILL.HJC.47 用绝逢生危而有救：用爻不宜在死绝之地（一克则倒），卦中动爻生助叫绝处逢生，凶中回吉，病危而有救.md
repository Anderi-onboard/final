# 用绝逢生危而有救：用爻不宜在死绝之地（一克则倒），卦中动爻生助叫绝处逢生，凶中回吉，病危而有救

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻不宜落在死绝的地方，因为一克它就倒。卦里有动爻来生它、帮它，叫绝处逢生，是凶里转吉的象，病虽危险也有救。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject line should not be in a place of death or extinction, because one control topples it. If a moving line in the hexagram generates and helps it, this is called meeting life in a place of extinction, an image of turning to luck out of misfortune: the illness is in danger, but there is rescue.
