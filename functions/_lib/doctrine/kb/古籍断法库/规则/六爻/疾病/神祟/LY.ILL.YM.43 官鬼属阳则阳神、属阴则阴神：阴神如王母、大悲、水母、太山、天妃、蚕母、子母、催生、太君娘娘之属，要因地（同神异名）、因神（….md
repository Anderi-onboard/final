# 官鬼属阳则阳神、属阴则阴神：阴神如王母、大悲、水母、太山、天妃、蚕母、子母、催生、太君娘娘之属，要因地（同神异名）、因神（男女）、因事（问蚕得阴官乃蚕母）而考

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻属阳的，就是阳神；属阴的，就是阴神。属阴的官鬼，像王母、大悲、水母、太山、天妃、陈周、圣妃、蚕母、子母、催生、太君、娘娘这一类，也应当按地方、按人、按事去考察，这里只是举个大概。因为有的神是同一位而名字不同，这叫因地，比如在北方叫子母娘娘，在南方叫太君娘娘；男女有别叫因神，为什么事叫因事，比如问蚕的事，得到阴的官鬼，就是蚕母。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line that is yang is a yang spirit, and one that is yin is a yin spirit. Where the Pressure line is yin, as with the Queen Mother, the Great Compassionate, the Water Mother, Taishan, the Heavenly Consort, Chen and Zhou, the Holy Consort, the Silkworm Mother, the Child Mother, the Birth Hastener, the Grand Lady and the Ladies, one should also examine by place, by person and by matter; this only gives an outline. For there are spirits who are the same but named differently, which is 'by place': in the north she is the Lady of Child and Mother, in the south the Lady Grand Dame. Difference of man and woman is 'by spirit', and what the matter is about is 'by matter': for instance, when asking about silkworms, a yin Pressure line is the Silkworm Mother.

> [!warning] 打架
> 阴阳是分「神」和「鬼」，还是分男神女神：易冒说官鬼属阳是阳神、属阴是阴神，举的阴神是王母、天妃、蚕母这些女神，阴的仍是神，不是鬼；易隐说「阳鬼为神、阴官为鬼」，卜筮正宗的王洪绪说「阳为神为男，阴为鬼为女」（LY.ILL.YY.27、LY.ILL.HJC.94），阴就是鬼；黄金策旧注和增删卜易的觉子则按旺衰分，旺相是神、休囚是鬼（LY.ILL.HJC.94、LY.ILL.ZS.23）。易冒在这里分的是阳神和阴神，神与鬼另在后面「生前之性」几段里分（LY.ILL.YM.49 起）。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether yin and yang divide 'spirit' from 'ghost' or male deity from female: the Yi Mao says a yang Pressure line is a yang spirit and a yin one a yin spirit, and its yin examples are goddesses such as the Queen Mother, the Heavenly Consort and the Silkworm Mother, so yin is still a spirit and not a ghost; the Yi Yin says 'a yang ghost is a spirit, a yin Pressure is a ghost', and Wang Hongxu of the Bushi Zhengzong says 'yang is a spirit and male, yin is a ghost and female' (LY.ILL.YY.27, LY.ILL.HJC.94), so yin is a ghost; the old commentary on the Huangjin Ce and Jue Zi of the Zengshan Buyi divide by strength, strong being a spirit and resting or trapped a ghost (LY.ILL.HJC.94, LY.ILL.ZS.23). The Yi Mao here divides yang spirits from yin spirits, and divides spirit from ghost later, in the paragraphs on how each was in life (from LY.ILL.YM.49).

> [!note] 按
> 这一份名单里的神名，书里一个也没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定；「大悲」「太山」「陈周」也是照字面写。「因地、因神、因事」三样是考察同一位神的三个角度：同神异名靠地方分，男女靠神分，要问什么事靠事分。
>
> *Note:* Names of gods and goddesses in the lists are given by transliteration or by a plain rendering; the book does not explain any of them, and none is identified here beyond what the book says. 'Great Compassionate', 'Taishan' and 'Chen and Zhou' are likewise written as they stand. 'By place, by spirit, by matter' are three angles from which to examine the same deity: the same deity under different names is told by place, male and female by the spirit, and what is being asked by the matter.
