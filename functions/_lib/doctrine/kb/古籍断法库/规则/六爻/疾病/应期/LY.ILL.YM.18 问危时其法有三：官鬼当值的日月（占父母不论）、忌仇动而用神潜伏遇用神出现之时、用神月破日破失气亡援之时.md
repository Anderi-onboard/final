# 问危时其法有三：官鬼当值的日月（占父母不论）、忌仇动而用神潜伏遇用神出现之时、用神月破日破失气亡援之时

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问什么时候危险，有三种办法。第一，官鬼当值的日、月，大概就是完了的时候（占父母的病不这样论，因为对父母来说官鬼是原神）。第二，忌神、仇神发动，用神伏藏着躲避克制，等到用神当值的日、月它一露出来，就受仇神、忌神的伤。第三，用神月破，危险在那个月；日破，危险在那一天；用神失了气，危险在失气的时候；用神失了援助，危险在失援的那一段时期。注里举了四个例子，又总结说：冲破原神的日子、生助忌神的时候，都凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* There are three ways to ask the time of danger. First, the day or month that the Pressure line rules may be the end (in divining a parent's illness this is not counted, because for a parent the Pressure line is the Support). Second, the Adversary and enemy spirits move while the Subject lies hidden to escape being controlled, and when the day or month that the Subject rules arrives and it comes out, it is wounded by the enemy and the Adversary. Third, with the Subject in Month-break the danger is in that month, in Day-break on that day; with the Subject losing its qi, at that time; with the Subject losing its support, in that period. The note gives four cases and sums up: the day that breaks the Support and the time that helps the Adversary are both ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 经文头一句「当鬼之日月其休乎」里的「休」字，注文把整句解作「当凶」，这里按注的意思译成「完了的时候」，原文没有再解释。注里第一例说「值独发甲寅鬼」，是指夬变革只有二爻一爻在动，动的正是甲寅鬼。注里的四个例子，前两个和后两个说的是同一件事的两面：用神被克绝的月日、原神被冲散的日子。
>
> *Note:* The character 'xiu' in the first sentence of the text, 'the day or month that falls on the ghost, may it be the end (xiu)?', is glossed by the note as 'it should be ominous', and that sense is followed here as 'the end'; the original gives no further explanation. In the note's first case, 'meeting the Jiayin ghost that moves alone' refers to Guai changing into Ge, in which only the second line moves, and the one that moves is the Jiayin ghost. Of the note's cases, the first two and the last two show two sides of the same thing: the month or day when the Subject is controlled and in Extinction, and the day when the Support is clashed apart.
