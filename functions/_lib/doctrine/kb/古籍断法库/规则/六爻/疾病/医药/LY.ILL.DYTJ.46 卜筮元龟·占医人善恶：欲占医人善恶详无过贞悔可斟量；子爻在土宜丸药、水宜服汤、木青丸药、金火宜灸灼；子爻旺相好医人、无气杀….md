# 卜筮元龟·占医人善恶：欲占医人善恶详无过贞悔可斟量；子爻在土宜丸药、水宜服汤、木青丸药、金火宜灸灼；子爻旺相好医人、无气杀冲还恶；卦无子爻何以遂、旺相相生药无崇、内外不和服药凶、更遇杀冲须早惧

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要占医生的好坏，要看得详细，不过是斟酌贞（内卦）和悔（外卦）。子爻在土，宜用丸药；若是水爻，宜服汤；子爻是木，宜青丸药；金、火两爻，宜灸灼。子爻旺相，是好医生；无气、遭杀冲，还是坏的。卦里没有子爻，怎么能遂心？旺相相生，药就没有祟；内外不和，服药凶；再遇到杀、冲，要早早警惕。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine whether a doctor is good or bad, look in detail; it is no more than weighing the inner (zhen) and outer (hui) trigrams. If the Output line is on Earth, pills are fit; if it is Water, a decoction should be taken; if the Output line is Wood, green pills; if Metal or Fire, moxibustion and cautery are fit. If the Output line is strong the doctor is good; if it has no qi or meets a killing or clash he is still a bad one. With no Output line in the hexagram how can it be accomplished? Strong and generating each other, the medicine has no curse; with the inner and outer not in harmony taking medicine is ominous; and if meeting a killing or clash as well, one must be wary early.

> [!warning] 打架
> 用药按谁的五行选，几本书的做法不同。断易天机卜筮元龟这一首按子爻（药）的五行：子爻属土宜丸药、属水宜汤、属木宜青丸药、属金火宜灸灼；洞林秘诀（LY.ILL.DYTJ.47）说「药色五行自斟酌」，注也是按子孙爻的五行取药色；天玄赋注（LY.ILL.TXF.13）、火珠林（LY.ILL.HZL.09）、易隐（LY.ILL.YY.25）按鬼爻的五行：火鬼宜凉剂、水鬼宜温剂或丸药、木鬼宜针、金鬼宜灸、土鬼宜锉散。两个体系都在：一个看药本身（子孙）的五行，一个看病（鬼）的五行，并且结论不同（比如金爻：子爻属金宜灸灼，金鬼也宜灸，一致；水：子爻是水宜服汤，水鬼宜温剂或丸药，不一致）。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whose phase to use in choosing medicine is done differently in different books. The Bushi Yuangui song of the Duanyi Tianji takes the phase of the Output line (the medicine): Output on Earth pills, Water a decoction, Wood green pills, Metal and Fire moxibustion and cautery; the Dongling Mijue (LY.ILL.DYTJ.47) says 'the colour of the medicine is for oneself to weigh by the five phases', taking the colour of the medicine also from the phase of the Output line; the commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.13), the Huozhu Lin (LY.ILL.HZL.09) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.25) take the phase of the ghost line: a Fire ghost a cooling remedy, a Water ghost a warming remedy or pills, a Wood ghost the needle, a Metal ghost moxa, an Earth ghost cut-and-ground powders. Both systems are there: one looks at the phase of the medicine itself (Output), the other at the phase of the illness (the ghost), and their conclusions differ (for Metal, Output on Metal is fit for moxibustion and a Metal ghost is also fit for moxa, which agree; for Water, Output on Water is fit for a decoction and a Water ghost for a warming remedy or pills, which do not agree). The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 「贞悔」是内卦（贞）和外卦（悔），内卦是病人、外卦是医药，拿这两个来比。「子爻」是子孙爻，是药；这里按子爻所属的五行选药：子爻属土宜丸药、属水宜汤药、属木宜青色的丸药、属金或火宜灸灼。「药无崇」的「崇」按「祟」译（药里没有作祟的东西），字面译作 curse。「（1）」「（2）」「（3）」是今人加的注号，那几条注不收。这里的用药是按子孙爻的五行选，天玄赋、火珠林和易隐是按鬼爻的五行选（见「打架」）。
>
> *Note:* The 'zhen and hui' are the inner trigram (zhen) and the outer trigram (hui); the inner trigram is the sick person and the outer the medicine, and the two are compared. The 'Output line' is the Output line, the medicine; here the medicine is chosen by the phase the Output line belongs to: Output on Earth is fit for pills, on Water for a decoction, on Wood for green pills, on Metal or Fire for moxibustion and cautery. The 'chong' of 'the medicine has no chong' is rendered 'curse' as a miswriting of 'sui' (a curse), that there is nothing in the medicine that brings a curse. The '(1)', '(2)' and '(3)' are modern annotation marks and those annotations are not quoted. The use of medicine here is chosen by the phase of the Output line, and the Tianxuan Fu, the Huozhu Lin and the Yi Yin choose it by the phase of the ghost line (see the dispute).
