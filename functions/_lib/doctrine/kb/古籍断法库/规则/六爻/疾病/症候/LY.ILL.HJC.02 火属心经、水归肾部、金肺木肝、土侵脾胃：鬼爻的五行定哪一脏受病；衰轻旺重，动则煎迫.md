# 火属心经、水归肾部、金肺木肝、土侵脾胃：鬼爻的五行定哪一脏受病；衰轻旺重，动则煎迫

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻的五行，定哪一脏受病：火是心经（发热、咽干、口燥），水是肾（怕冷、盗汗、遗精白浊），金是肺（咳嗽、虚怯、气喘），木是肝（感冒风寒、四肢不和），土是脾胃（虚黄、浮肿、瘟疫时气）。鬼爻衰弱病轻、旺相病重；静则安卧，动则煎熬躁急。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The phase of the Pressure line fixes which organ is ill: Fire is the Heart channel (fever, dry throat, dry mouth), Water the Kidney (aversion to cold, night sweats, seminal emission and cloudy urine), Metal the Lung (cough, weak timidity, panting), Wood the Liver (catching wind-cold, limbs out of harmony), Earth the Spleen and Stomach (sallow weakness, swelling, epidemic and seasonal disease). A weak Pressure line means a light illness and a strong one a heavy illness; still, the patient lies quiet, and moving, he is tormented and restless.

## 《黄金策·疾病》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6766 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 火属心经，发热咽干口燥； | 火对应心经，病发热、咽喉干、口燥； | Fire belongs to the Heart channel: fever, dry throat and dry mouth; |
> | 水归肾部，恶寒盗汗遗精。 | 水归肾这一部，怕冷、盗汗、遗精。 | Water belongs to the Kidney: aversion to cold, night sweats and seminal emission. |
> | 金肺木肝，土乃病侵脾胃； | 金对应肺，木对应肝，土就是病侵到脾胃； | Metal is the Lung and Wood the Liver; Earth means the illness invades the Spleen and Stomach; |
> | 衰轻旺重，动则煎迫身躯。 | 鬼爻衰弱病轻，旺相病重，发动就煎熬逼迫身体。 | weak, it is light; strong, it is heavy; moving, it gnaws at and presses on the body. |
