# 死气发时病讼凶、月破忧者散病者死：死气——占病、官司，动爻旺相克其世身；月破——忧者散、病者死、事不成、财无气，是用爻被月建冲

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 死气发动的时候，病和官司凶。死气，是占病、占官司时，动爻旺相，克了世和身。月破：忧愁的事散去，病的人死，事情不成，财没有气，这是用爻被月建冲的缘故。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the death-qi issues, illness and lawsuits are ominous. The death-qi: in divining an illness or a lawsuit, a moving line that is strong controls the Self and the body. Month-break: the one worried is dispersed, the sick person dies, the matter is not achieved and wealth has no qi, because the Subject line is clashed by the Month.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是「生气死气日冲月破」一段里和病有关的三句：歌诀「死气发时病讼凶」、解说「死气者：如占病，官司，动爻旺相克其世身」、解说「月破者：忧者散，病者死，事不成，财无气」。歌诀的其他三句（生气、日冲、日破）和眉批「日破为日虎神……」不专说病，没有收。「月破者：忧者散」的「忧者散」是「忧愁的事散去」还是「忧愁的人散去」，书里没有解释，这里译作前者；「病者死」是「病人死」，和黄金策、增删卜易的「用逢月破，久病难安」（LY.ILL.ZS.04）一致，不过那里说「月破看用神衰旺」，这里说得更绝对。「其世身」的「身」按字面译作 the body。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> *Note:* This section quotes the three sentences about illness from the passage 'life-qi, death-qi, the Day clash and Month-break': the verse 'when the death-qi issues, illness and lawsuits are ominous', the explanation 'the death-qi: in divining an illness or lawsuit, a moving line strong controls its Self and body', and the explanation 'Month-break: the one worried is dispersed, the sick person dies, the matter is not achieved, wealth has no qi'. The other three lines of the verse (the life-qi, the Day clash, the Day break) and the marginal comment 'the Day break is the Day-tiger god ...' are not about illness in particular and are not quoted. Whether 'the one worried is dispersed' means 'the worry is dispersed' or 'the worrier is dispersed' the book does not explain, and it is rendered here as the former; 'the sick person dies' agrees with the Zengshan Buyi's 'the Subject meeting Month-break, a long illness is hard to settle' (LY.ILL.ZS.04), except that there 'Month-break depends on the strength of the Subject', and here it is put more absolutely. The 'body' of 'its Self and body' is rendered literally as the body. The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.
