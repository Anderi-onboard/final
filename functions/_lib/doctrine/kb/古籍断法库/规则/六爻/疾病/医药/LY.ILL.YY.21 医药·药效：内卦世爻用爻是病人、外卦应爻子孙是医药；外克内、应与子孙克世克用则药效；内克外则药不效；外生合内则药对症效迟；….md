# 医药·药效：内卦世爻用爻是病人、外卦应爻子孙是医药；外克内、应与子孙克世克用则药效；内克外则药不效；外生合内则药对症效迟；无子孙不遇良医；子孙两动换医而愈；鬼子俱空不治自愈；鬼子俱动医祷兼用；子财两动鬼得助病难治

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦的世爻、用爻是病人，外卦的应爻、子孙是医生和药。外卦克内卦，应爻和子孙克世、克用的，药有效；内卦克外卦，世爻、用爻克应、克子孙的，药没有效；外卦生合内卦，应爻和子孙生合世的，药对症但见效慢；外卦应爻克内卦世爻而卦里没有子孙的，遇不到好医生。应爻带子孙发动来克世的，药有效；世克应和子孙的，是名医但药没有效；子孙克世而外卦应爻不克内卦世爻的，药没效也没害。应爻带鬼发动、刑害克冲世爻的，是用错药伤了人；子孙落在辰午酉亥自刑之地的，用药有差错；日辰、动爻冲伤子孙的，药没有效；子孙变出父母或鬼的，药收不到功；子孙变出子孙的，药杂不精；子孙带白虎煞发动的，是庸医误人。世下伏官、遇子孙发动的，药虽好而病根除不掉；世持衰鬼的，病轻而药难治。世应比和而卦无子孙、或子孙空绝的，宜换药换医；子孙有两个都动的，换医就好；鬼和子孙都落空的，不治自愈；鬼和子孙都发动的，医药和祈祷一起用；子孙和财爻都动，鬼得到帮助，病难治。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line and Subject line in the inner trigram are the patient, and the Other line and Output in the outer trigram are the doctor and medicine. If the outer controls the inner, and the Other and Output control the Self and the Subject, the medicine is effective; if the inner controls the outer, and the Self and Subject control the Other and Output, it is not effective; if the outer generates and combines with the inner, and the Other and Output generate and combine with the Self, the medicine fits the illness but works slowly; if the Other in the outer trigram controls the Self in the inner and there is no Output in the hexagram, one does not meet a good doctor. If the Other line carries Output and moves to control the Self, the medicine is effective; if the Self controls the Other and Output, the doctor is famous but the medicine does not work; if Output controls the Self but the Other in the outer trigram does not control the Self, the medicine has no effect and does no harm. If the Other line carries the ghost and moves to punish, harm, control or clash the Self, wrong medicine harms the person; if Output stands on Chen, Wu, You or Hai, which punish themselves, the medicine is mistaken; if the Day or a moving line clashes with and wounds Output, it does not work; if Output changes into Resource or a ghost, it does not bring completion; if Output changes into Output, the medicines are mixed and not refined; if Output carries the Tiger and a killing star and moves, a mediocre doctor harms people. If Pressure is hidden beneath the Self and Output moves, the medicine is good but the root of the illness is not removed; if the Self holds a weak ghost, the illness is light but the medicine has difficulty curing it. If the Self and Other are in harmony and there is no Output, or Output is Void or in Extinction, change the medicine and doctor; if two Output lines both move, changing the doctor leads to recovery; if the ghost and Output are both Void, he recovers without treatment; if the ghost and Output both move, use doctors and prayers together; if Output and Wealth both move, the ghost is helped and the illness is hard to treat.

> [!note] 按
> 两个断例挂在这里：坎之蹇（应爻旺相本可服药，却变出申金冲刑寅木子孙，服药不效；子旺官衰，新病不死只是难痊）、震之豫（子孙是药而午自刑，用药差误，巳午日子旺生财病才退）。书里最后一句「若至日病加重」缺了是哪一日，译文按上下文译作「那一日」，不猜是哪天。「自刑」是辰午酉亥四个地支遇到自己（如午见午）。「六冲」的新病与久病的不同，见 LY.ILL.YY.07。「良医」「名医而药不效」这一组和黄金策讲医药（LY.ILL.HJC.58 起）、增删卜易讲延医（LY.ILL.ZS.26 起）是三家各说各的一套：黄金策以应爻为医、子孙为药，易隐同；增删卜易另有以子孙为忧神解药、应爻为医人的说法。
>
> *Note:* Two cases hang here: Kan into Jian (the Other line strong and so medicine should work, but it changes into Shen Metal which clashes with and punishes Yin Wood Output and the medicine fails; Output strong and Pressure weak, so a recent illness does not kill and is only hard to cure) and Zhen into Yu (Output is the medicine but Wu punishes itself, the medicine was mistaken, and the illness retreated only on a Si or Wu day when Output strong generated Wealth). The last sentence of the second case, 'if on reaching the day the illness grows heavier', lacks which day, and the translation renders it 'that day' without guessing. 'Self-punishment' (zixing) is the four branches Chen, Wu, You and Hai meeting themselves (Wu meeting Wu). For the difference between a recent illness and a long one under Six-Clash, see LY.ILL.YY.07. This group on the good doctor and 'famous doctor, ineffective medicine' is a set of its own beside the Huangjin Ce's treatment of medicine (from LY.ILL.HJC.58) and the Zengshan Buyi's on calling a doctor (from LY.ILL.ZS.26): the Huangjin Ce takes the Other line as the doctor and Output as the medicine, as the Yi Yin does; the Zengshan Buyi also has its own account of Output as the spirit that relieves worry and the Other line as the doctor.
