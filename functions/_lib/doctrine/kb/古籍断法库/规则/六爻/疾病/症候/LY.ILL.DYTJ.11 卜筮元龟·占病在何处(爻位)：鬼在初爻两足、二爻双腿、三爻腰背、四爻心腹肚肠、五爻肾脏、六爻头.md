# 卜筮元龟·占病在何处(爻位)：鬼在初爻两足、二爻双腿、三爻腰背、四爻心腹肚肠、五爻肾脏、六爻头

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼在初爻，两足受伤；在二爻，两腿的病不同寻常；在三爻，腰背常发软；在四爻，心、腹和肚肠；在五爻，肾脏，多气胀；在六爻，头上的病是灾殃。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ghost on the first line: both feet are injured; on the second: an unusual trouble in both legs; on the third: the waist and back are often limp; on the fourth: the heart, belly and bowels; on the fifth: the kidneys, often with distension of qi; on the sixth: trouble in the head is the calamity.

> [!warning] 打架
> 哪一爻管身体哪一处，几本书差得很远。这一块是「初两足、二双腿、三腰背、四心腹肚肠、五肾脏、六头」；同一章鬼谷辨爻法的表（LY.ILL.DYTJ.01）是「初足、二腿股、三腰、四脊、五心、六头」；孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）和阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.04）是「一五脏、二皮肉、三体骨腰并足、四肺心经、五六头」；易隐引鬼谷分爻（LY.ILL.YY.02）是「初足、二腿股、三肝肾、四脾脏、五心肺、六头脑」。初爻管足、上爻管头，几家一致；中间几爻各说各的，而孙膑歌和阐奥歌章那一说连初爻也不管足。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line governs which part of the body is put very differently in several books. This block has 'first the two feet, second both legs, third waist and back, fourth heart, belly and bowels, fifth the kidneys, sixth the head'; Guigu's table in the same chapter (LY.ILL.DYTJ.01) has 'first the feet, second the legs and thighs, third the waist, fourth the spine, fifth the heart, sixth the head'; Sun Bin's song (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) and the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.04) have 'the first the five viscera, the second skin and flesh, the third body, bones, waist and feet, the fourth lungs and heart channel, the fifth and sixth the head'; and the Yi Yin, quoting Guigu's division of the lines (LY.ILL.YY.02), has 'first the feet, second the legs and thighs, third liver and kidneys, fourth the spleen, fifth heart and lungs, sixth the head and brain'. The first line for the feet and the top line for the head are agreed by most of them; the lines between are put differently in each, and the reading of Sun Bin's song and the Chan'ao Gezhang does not even put the feet at the first line. The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 「三爻腰背常轻软」的「轻软」按「发软」译。这一块和同一章鬼谷辨爻法的表（LY.ILL.DYTJ.01）、孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）一样，都是把六个爻位配给身体的六个部位，各家的配法见「打架」。
>
> *Note:* The 'light and soft' of 'the waist and back are often light and soft' is rendered 'limp'. This block, like Guigu's table in the same chapter (LY.ILL.DYTJ.01) and Sun Bin's song (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03), matches the six line positions to six parts of the body; how the books match them is in the dispute.
