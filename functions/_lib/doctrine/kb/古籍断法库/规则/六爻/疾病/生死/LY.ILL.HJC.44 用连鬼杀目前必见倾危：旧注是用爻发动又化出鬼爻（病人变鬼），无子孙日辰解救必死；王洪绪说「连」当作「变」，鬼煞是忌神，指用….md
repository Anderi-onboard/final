# 用连鬼杀目前必见倾危：旧注是用爻发动又化出鬼爻（病人变鬼），无子孙日辰解救必死；王洪绪说「连」当作「变」，鬼煞是忌神，指用神化回头克，有日月动爻解救则不危，并驳「用神变官鬼必死」

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神连着鬼煞，眼前一定看到危险：没有子孙、日辰、动爻来解救，危险就在眼前。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject is joined to the Pressure killer, danger is surely in view: with no Output, Day or moving line to relieve it, the danger is immediate.

## 《黄金策·病体》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6919 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用连鬼杀，目前必见倾危。 | 用神连着鬼煞，眼前一定看见危险。 | The Subject joined to the Pressure killer: danger is surely to be seen before one's eyes. |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2386 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | “连”字当作“变”字解。 | 「连」字应当当作「变」字来解。 | The character 'lian' (joined) should be read as 'bian' (change). |
> | 今术家以用神变出官鬼者，断其病必死，是以词害义矣。 | 现在的术士把用神变出官鬼的，断成这病必死，这是拿字面害了本意。 | Today's practitioners judge that when the Subject changes out a Pressure line the illness is surely fatal; this is letting the words hurt the meaning. |
> | 孰知鬼煞者是忌神也，用连鬼煞，即指用化回头克耳。 | 哪里知道鬼煞说的是忌神，用连鬼煞，就是指用神变出回头克罢了。 | Who knows that the 'Pressure killer' is the Adversary, and that 'the Subject joined to the Pressure killer' just means the Subject changing into a returning control. |
> | 用神变回头克，而无日月动爻解救者，目前立见其危也。 | 用神变出回头克，又没有日月、动爻来解救的，眼前立刻就看到危险。 | When the Subject changes into a returning control and there is no Month, Day or moving line to relieve it, the danger is at once before one's eyes. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 「用连鬼杀」是什么意思：旧注读作用爻发动、又变出官鬼，叫病人变成鬼，没有子孙、日辰解救就一定死；王洪绪说「连」字应当读作「变」，「鬼煞」是忌神、不是官鬼，句子是说用神变出回头克，没有日月、动爻解救，眼前才危险，并且明说术家「用神变官鬼必死」的断法是拿字面害了本意。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What 'the Subject joined to the Pressure killer' means: the old commentary reads it as the Subject line moving and also changing out a Pressure line, called the patient turning into a ghost, and with no Output or Day to rescue him he surely dies; Wang Hongxu says the character 'lian' should be read as 'change', that the 'Pressure killer' is the Adversary and not the Pressure line, that the line means the Subject changing into a returning control, and only with no Month, Day or moving line to relieve it is the danger before one's eyes; and he says outright that the practitioners' judgment 'the Subject changing into Pressure is surely fatal' lets the words hurt the meaning.

> [!note] 按
> 王洪绪这一段和他在本章最后一条（LY.ILL.HJC.57）里的话是一个意思：鬼煞的道理在忌神，不在官鬼。「回头克」是动爻变出来的爻反过来克它自己。
>
> *Note:* Wang Hongxu's passage says the same as his words in the last entry of this chapter (LY.ILL.HJC.57): the principle of the 'Pressure killer' lies in the Adversary and not in the Pressure line. A 'returning control' is when the line a moving line changes into controls that line in return.
