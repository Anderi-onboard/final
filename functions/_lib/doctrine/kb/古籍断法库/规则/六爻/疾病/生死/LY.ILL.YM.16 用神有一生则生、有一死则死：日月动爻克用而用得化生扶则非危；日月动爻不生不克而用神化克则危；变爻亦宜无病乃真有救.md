# 用神有一生则生、有一死则死：日月动爻克用而用得化生扶则非危；日月动爻不生不克而用神化克则危；变爻亦宜无病乃真有救

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日、月、动爻克用爻，这叫做不得救，但用爻变出生它、扶它的，不危险；日、月、动爻对用神不生也不克，而用神变出克它的，就危险了。所以说：用神有一个生它的就活，有一个死的就死。注：这是说用神变生、变克是最要紧的办法，但变出的爻也应当没有毛病，才是真有救。比如未月甲辰日，占儿子本人，得咸变蹇，日、月克用爻，大凶，但用爻变出兄弟长生，所以不死；戌月戊申日，占父亲本人，得艮变蛊，用爻独自发动本该活，因为变出去在亥绝，就救不了了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Day, Month and moving lines control the Subject line, this is called no rescue, but if the Subject line changes into what generates or supports it, there is no danger; if the Day, Month and moving lines neither generate nor control the Subject, but the Subject changes into what controls it, it is in danger. So it is said: if the Subject has one source of life it lives, and if it has one death it dies. The note: this says that the Subject changing into generation or control is the most important method, but the changed line must also be without flaw for the rescue to be real: for example, in the Wei month on the Jiachen day, a son himself was divined and Xian changing into Jian (Obstruction) obtained; the Day and Month control the Subject line, a great ill omen, but the Subject changed into Peer at Birth, so he did not die; in the Xu month on the Wushen day, a father himself was divined and Gen changing into Gu obtained: the Subject line moved alone and should have lived, but it changed into Extinction at Hai and so could not be saved.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的核心是「用神有一生则生，有一死则死」：用神（所占那个人的爻）只要有一条生路就能活，只要有一条死路就死；注加了一层，变出去的爻自己也要没毛病。黄金策讲的「用绝逢生」「主衰得助」（LY.ILL.HJC.47、LY.ILL.HJC.48）与此相通。
>
> *Note:* The heart of this entry is 'the Subject with one source of life lives, with one death dies': as long as the Subject (the line of the person divined) has a road to life it lives, and a road to death it dies; the note adds that the line it changes into must itself be without flaw. The Huangjin Ce's 'Extinction meeting generation' and 'weak and yet helped' (LY.ILL.HJC.47 and LY.ILL.HJC.48) go with it.
