# 鬼动化出六亲即以化出者断：化兄是朋友兄弟妯娌、化鬼加玄武是盗贼、化父母是伯叔、加螣蛇是匠人作魇魅；父化官虽非螣蛇亦是匠人作弊

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻发动，变出哪一个六亲，就按变出来的这个断：变成兄爻，是朋友、兄弟、妯娌、公婆这一类；变成鬼再加玄武，是盗贼；变成父母，是伯叔这些亲属，再加螣蛇，是他家屋里有工匠做了魇魅的手脚，使家里的人不得安宁；父母变成官，即使没有螣蛇，也是工匠作了弊。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Pressure line moves and turns into one of the six relations, judge by what it turns into: into Peer, friends, brothers, sisters-in-law and parents-in-law; into Pressure with the Dark Tortoise, a thief; into Resource, uncles and other relatives, and with the Coiling Snake added, a craftsman in his house has worked a nightmare-spell so that the household is uneasy; if Resource changes into Pressure, even without the Coiling Snake, a craftsman has played a trick.

> [!note] 按
> 「穿窬」是穿墙洞、爬墙头去偷，指小偷。「魇魅」是用邪术暗中害人（卜筮大全篇末的生僻字注只分别解释了「魇」「魅」两个字）。「姑嫜」是丈夫的母亲和父亲（嫜，丈夫的父亲，见生僻字注）；王洪绪的名单里没有它。「匠人作魇魅」是古人的一种说法：工匠在造房子时暗中做手脚害主人，这条书里没有再解释。这一条的核心是：鬼动变出什么，就按变出来的那一类亲属去认。
>
> *Note:* 'Chuanyu' is to break through a wall or climb over it to steal, meaning a thief. 'Yanmei' is to harm someone secretly with evil arts (the book's glossary explains only the two characters separately). 'Gujiang' are the husband's mother and father (the glossary defines 'zhang' as the husband's father); Wang Hongxu's list lacks them. 'A craftsman worked a nightmare-spell' is an old way of saying that a craftsman secretly does something against the owner while building the house, which the book does not explain further. The heart of this entry is that whatever the moving ghost changes into decides which kind of relative it is.
