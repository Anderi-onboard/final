# 疾病论·鬼的形貌：乾金之鬼白发老翁、坎鬼休囚耳聋鬼崇、艮鬼手瘫、震官脚疾、巽爻官发折腿腰跎、离象鬼兴眼昏目暗、坤土之鬼花苍母、兑金死鬼缺唇魂

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里按八卦说鬼的模样和它带来的病：乾金之鬼是白发老头；坎鬼休囚，是耳聋的鬼作祟；艮宫的鬼，手瘫；震宫的官鬼，脚有病；巽宫的官鬼发动，折腿、腰驼；离象的鬼兴旺，眼睛昏暗；坤土之鬼是花白头发的老母；兑金的死鬼是缺嘴唇的魂。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness gives, by trigram, the look of the ghost and the illness it brings: the ghost of Qian Metal is a white-haired old man; a Kan ghost resting or confined is a deaf ghost haunting; a Gen ghost, a paralysed hand; the Pressure line of Zhen, disease of the foot; the Pressure line of Xun moving, a broken leg and a bent waist; a ghost rising in the Li image, dim and dark eyes; the ghost of Kun Earth is an old mother with mottled grey hair; the dead ghost of Dui Metal is the soul of one with a split lip.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段讲的是鬼的模样，同时也是它带来的病或伤：白发老翁、花苍母、缺唇魂是鬼的样子，手瘫、脚疾、折腿、眼昏目暗既可以读作鬼的模样，也可以读作病人的病，书里没有分开。「腰跎」的「跎」字读作驼背、腰弯，按字面译成「腰驼了」。这一段里艮手、震足、离目、坎耳和本篇前面的八卦配病位（LY.ILL.BSQS.33）一致，巽这里是腿和腰，和前面的「股」相近，见 LY.ILL.BSQS.33 的「打架」。
>
> *Note:* This passage gives the look of the ghost and, at the same time, the illness or injury it brings: the white-haired old man, the mottled grey mother and the soul with a split lip are how the ghost looks, while the paralysed hand, the foot disease, the broken leg and the dim and dark eyes can be read either as the look of the ghost or as the illness of the sick person, and the book does not separate them. The character rendered 'bent' in 'a bent waist' means stooped or bowed, and it is translated to the letter. In this passage Gen the hand, Zhen the foot, Li the eye and Kan the ear agree with the matching of the trigrams to places of illness earlier in the chapter (LY.ILL.BSQS.33); for Xun it gives the leg and the waist, which is close to the thigh there; see the dispute in LY.ILL.BSQS.33.
