# 三空无香火之堂：占病遇第三爻空亡，象是不供香火的厅堂；动空化空似有若无，旺相空亡供器不全

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 第三爻落空，就是没有供香火的厅堂：占病时遇到第三爻空亡，他家不供奉香火；动了又落空、变出来也是空，像有又像没有；旺相而落空亡，供奉的器物不齐全。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Void third line means a hall where no incense or fire is offered: in divining illness, when the third line is Void, his family does not offer incense and fire; if it moves into Void or changes into Void, it is as if there were something and as if there were nothing; if strong yet Void, the sacrificial vessels are incomplete.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3413 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 三爻空，其家不奉香火。 | 第三爻空，他家不供奉香火。 | If the third line is Void, his family does not offer incense and fire. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文两句「三空无香火之堂，怪动有不祥之祸」，王洪绪的注把两句连成一段，本库按句拆成这一条和下一条。旧注比王洪绪多了动空化空、旺相空亡两种细分。
>
> *Note:* The verse has two lines, 'a void third: no hall of incense; a strange line moving: inauspicious misfortune', and Wang Hongxu's commentary runs them into one passage; the library splits them by line into this entry and the next. The old commentary distinguishes two more cases than Wang Hongxu: moving into or changing into Void, and strong yet Void.
