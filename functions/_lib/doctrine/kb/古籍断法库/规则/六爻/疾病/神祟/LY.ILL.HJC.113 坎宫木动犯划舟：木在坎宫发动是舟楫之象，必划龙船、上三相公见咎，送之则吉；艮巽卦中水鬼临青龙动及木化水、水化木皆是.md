# 坎宫木动犯划舟：木在坎宫发动是舟楫之象，必划龙船、上三相公见咎，送之则吉；艮巽卦中水鬼临青龙动及木化水、水化木皆是

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木在坎宫发动，冒犯了划船（龙船）的神：是船和桨的象，一定是划龙船的事，上三相公怪罪，把他们送走就吉利；在艮卦、巽卦里水鬼带青龙发动，以及木变水、水变木，鬼落在水上，也都是这个象。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wood moving in the Kan palace has offended the spirit of the rowed boat: it is the image of a boat and oars, surely a matter of rowing the dragon boat, and the Three Lords Above are displeased; seeing them off brings good fortune. In the Gen or Xun trigram, a Water ghost carrying the Azure Dragon and moving, or Wood changing into Water or Water into Wood, with the ghost on Water, is the same image.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「上三相公」是旧注提到的神，书里没有解释，按字面译作上面的三位相公。王洪绪把「舟楫」写作「舟辑」，字形相近，意思同。这一条和上一条（兑卦金龙）在王洪绪那里是同一段注的前后两句。
>
> *Note:* The 'Three Lords Above' are spirits the old commentary mentions but does not explain, and are rendered literally. Wang Hongxu writes 'boat and oars' with a variant character; the meaning is the same. This entry and the previous one (Metal and the Dragon in Dui) are the two halves of one passage in Wang Hongxu.
