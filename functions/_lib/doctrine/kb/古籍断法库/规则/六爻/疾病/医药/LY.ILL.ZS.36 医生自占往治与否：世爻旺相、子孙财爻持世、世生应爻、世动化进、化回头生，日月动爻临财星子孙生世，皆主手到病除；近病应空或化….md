# 医生自占往治与否：世爻旺相、子孙财爻持世、世生应爻、世动化进、化回头生，日月动爻临财星子孙生世，皆主手到病除；近病应空或化空、卦逢六冲等宜速往，久病应旺相或生世宜往，此外勿往治

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 医生自己占要不要去治，和替别人占卜差不多：世爻是自己，应爻是病人。世爻旺相，子孙或财爻持世，世爻生应爻，世动变进神、变回头生，日月和动爻临着财星或子孙来生世爻，都主手到病除。近病：应爻值旬空，或动了变空、变回头生、变旺、变退，或日月、动爻生应爻，以及卦逢六冲、卦变六冲的，都该赶快去救，别让别人先到而成了功。久病：应爻旺相，或遇到日月、动爻来生，或动了变生、变旺，应爻临子孙、临财爻，动而生世爻的，虽然是久病，我一定能除根。除了这些，都不要去治，白白损了名声。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A doctor divining for himself whether to go and treat is much like divining for another: the Self line is oneself and the Other line is the patient. If the Self line is strong, Output or Wealth holds the Self line, the Self line generates the Other line, the Self line moves and changes into an Advancing spirit or into a returning generation, or the Day, Month or a moving line sits on the Wealth star or Output and generates the Self line, all mean that the hand arrives and the illness is gone. For a recent illness: if the Other line falls into Void, or moves and changes into Void, a returning generation, strength or retreat, or the Day, Month or a moving line generates the Other line, or the hexagram is Six-Clash or changes into Six-Clash, one should hurry to the rescue and not let others arrive first and succeed. For a long illness: if the Other line is strong, or is generated by the Day, Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into generation or strength, or sits on Output or Wealth, or moves and generates the Self line, then though the illness is long I shall surely remove the root. Apart from these, do not go to treat, for it only harms one's reputation.

>
> *Note:* This entry is the rule for a doctor's divination on whether to go and treat; the Old Man's case is of being called to a child who fell ill on coming back from Shandong, and who, before setting out, divined 'will this trip do good' and got Wealth holding the Self line with Output moving to generate it, and judged that the trip would do good. The child's illness and medicine are carried on in LY.ILL.ZS.37.
