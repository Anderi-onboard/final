# 世人有疾病者亦有贤愚之分：贤者以医药为重、愚者以祷告为先，占者要察其情状；鬼神儒家本不言、为患者之占不得不据理推详以为阴功默佑；木主东岳天齐圣帝等、水是河泊水府等、土是瘟蝗疫疠中央土府之神、金是衔刀五道飞廉白虎金神七杀等、火是南方火部赤睛朱发鹤神等，各连着相应的伤亡之鬼；二爻鬼为灶神、三爻鬼为香火神、子孙动为福神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世上的人有了病，也有贤愚的分别：贤的把医药放在首位，愚的把祷告放在前头；占卜的人不能不察看他的情状，一眼看去就能知道高下，这样贤的愚的都可以和他说得上话，这门学问才能流传。至于鬼神，儒家本来不说这些，现在为病人占卜，不得不按道理推详，当作暗中的阴功和默佑。木鬼：是东岳天齐圣帝、七十二司、三十六案的神，还有自吊而亡的；水鬼：是河泊水府、沿江七十二庙的神，还有溺水而亡的；土鬼：是瘟蝗疫疠、中央土府的神，还有饥饿绝食而亡的；金鬼：是衔刀、五道、飞廉、白虎、金神、七杀、吟呻、三杀、九良星杀、丧殃、丧杀之神，还有自刎、被刑、阵亡，一切伤亡；火鬼：是南方火部、赤睛朱发的鹤神、毛头毒火之神，还有赴汤蹈火而亡的。如果二爻是鬼，就是灶神；三爻是鬼，是香火神；子孙动是福神。照这样推，万无一失。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* People with illness are also divided into the worthy and the foolish: the worthy put medicine first, the foolish put prayer first; the one who divines must not fail to observe his circumstances, and one look at the person shows high or low, so that worthy and foolish can both be spoken to and this way can circulate. As for ghosts and spirits, the Confucians did not speak of them originally; now, for divining for the sick, one cannot but reason it out from principle, taking it as hidden merit and silent protection. A Wood ghost: Tianqi, the Holy Emperor of the Eastern Peak, the seventy-two departments and the spirits of the thirty-six cases, and also those who hanged themselves; a Water ghost: the River Count and the Water Mansion, the spirits of the seventy-two temples along the river, and also the drowned; an Earth ghost: epidemic, locusts and plague, the spirits of the central Earth Mansion, and also those who died of hunger and starvation; a Metal ghost: the knife-bearing, the Five Roads, Feilian and the White Tiger, the Golden Spirit, the Seven Killings, Yinshen, the Three Killings, the Nine Good Star killings, the spirits of Mourning Calamity and the Mourning Killing, and also every wounding and death by cutting the throat, by execution or in battle; a Fire ghost: the southern Fire Department, the crane spirit with red eyes and vermilion hair, the shaggy-headed poison-fire spirit, and also those who died going into boiling water and fire. If the ghost is on the second line it is the Kitchen God; if on the third line it is the incense-fire spirit; Output moving is the fortune god. Reasoning from this, not one error in ten thousand.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是天玄赋对「鬼神」的态度和名单。态度：「鬼神者，儒家本不言此」——本来不说，但因为病人占卜，不得不据理推详，当作「阴功默佑」；这和黄金策、易冒把神鬼只当大类看的态度接近，和易林补遗把神名逐个点出的做法不同。名单按五行给：木（东岳天齐圣帝等）、水（河泊水府）、土（瘟蝗疫疠、中央土府）、金（衔刀五道飞廉白虎金神七杀等）、火（南方火部、鹤神、毒火之神），每类还带着相应的死法。这一套和黄金策「五行推鬼神」（LY.ILL.HJC.97）、易隐「鬼崇·神与鬼的名目」（LY.ILL.YY.28）、易林补遗的五行诀（LY.ILL.EL.46）是同一类名单，野鹤则批评把金官指为关公岳公等具体神（LY.ILL.ZS.24）。「二爻鬼为灶神、三爻鬼为香火神、子孙动为福神」和易林补遗「按爻位断神鬼」（LY.ILL.EL.37）、黄金策「六爻推鬼神」（LY.ILL.HJC.101）一致。「吟呻」「三杀」「九良星杀」「丧殃」照字面译；「赤睛朱发鹤神」「毛头毒火之神」是形容火部神的样子，按字面译。「自吊」是上吊自尽。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。 这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This section is the Tianxuan Fu's attitude to and list of 'ghosts and spirits'. The attitude: 'as for ghosts and spirits, the Confucians did not speak of them' — they did not originally, but for the divining of the sick one cannot but reason it out from principle, taking it as 'hidden merit and silent protection'; this is close to the Huangjin Ce and the Yi Mao, which treat gods and ghosts only as broad kinds, and unlike the Yilin Buyi, which names the gods one by one. The list is given by the five phases: Wood (Tianqi, the Holy Emperor of the Eastern Peak and so on), Water (the River Count and the Water Mansion), Earth (epidemic, locusts and plague, the central Earth Mansion), Metal (the knife-bearing, the Five Roads, Feilian, the White Tiger, the Golden Spirit, the Seven Killings and so on) and Fire (the southern Fire Department, the crane spirit, the poison-fire spirit), each with its matching kind of death. This is the same kind of list as the Huangjin Ce's 'the five phases and the gods and ghosts' (LY.ILL.HJC.97), the Yi Yin's 'the names of gods and ghosts' (LY.ILL.YY.28) and the Yilin Buyi's verse on the five phases (LY.ILL.EL.46), while the Old Man of Yehe criticises pointing a Metal Pressure line at particular gods such as Guan Gong and Yue Gong (LY.ILL.ZS.24). 'A ghost on the second line is the Kitchen God, a ghost on the third line the incense-fire spirit, Output moving the fortune god' agrees with the Yilin Buyi's 'ghosts and spirits by line position' (LY.ILL.EL.37) and the Huangjin Ce's 'the six lines and gods and ghosts' (LY.ILL.HJC.101). 'Yinshen', 'the Three Killings', 'the Nine Good Star killings' and 'Mourning Calamity' are rendered literally; 'the crane spirit with red eyes and vermilion hair' and 'the shaggy-headed poison-fire spirit' describe how the gods of the Fire Department look, and are rendered literally. 'Zi diao' is hanging oneself. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; the book does not explain them, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
