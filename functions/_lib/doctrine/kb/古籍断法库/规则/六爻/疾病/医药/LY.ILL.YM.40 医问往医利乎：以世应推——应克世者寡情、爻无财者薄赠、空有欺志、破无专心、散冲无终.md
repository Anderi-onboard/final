# 医问往医利乎：以世应推——应克世者寡情、爻无财者薄赠、空有欺志、破无专心、散冲无终

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 医生问上门去治病有没有利，就从世、应来推：应克世的，病家没有情义；卦里没有财爻的，酬谢微薄；落空的，有欺骗的心思；冲破的，没有专心；散了、冲了的，不会有个结果。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a doctor asks whether going to treat will be profitable, judge by the Self and the Other: the Other controlling the Self means little feeling; a hexagram with no Wealth line means a meagre gift; Void means an intent to deceive; broken, no wholehearted devotion; scattered or clashed, no end.

## 《易冒·医药章第五十六》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1257 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 56: Medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 医问往医利乎，则以世应而推，应克世者寡情，爻无财者薄赠，空有欺志，破无专心，散、冲无终。 | 医生问上门去治病有没有利，就从世、应来推：应克世的，病家没有情义；卦里没有财爻的，酬谢微薄；落空的，有欺骗的心思；冲破的，没有专心；散了、冲了的，不会有个结果。 | When a doctor asks whether going to treat will be profitable, judge by the Self and the Other: the Other controlling the Self means little feeling; a hexagram with no Wealth line means a meagre gift; Void means an intent to deceive; broken, no wholehearted devotion; scattered or clashed, no end. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 原文里「空有欺志，破无专心，散、冲无终」没有说是谁的空、破、散、冲；按上文「以世应而推」，这里译作世、应落空、冲破、散、冲时的情形，没有替它加主语。这是医生问的第三种（往医）。
>
> *Note:* The original does not say whose Void, breaking, scattering or clash it is in 'Void has intent to deceive, broken has no devotion, scattered or clashed has no end'; following 'judge by the Self and the Other' just before, it is rendered as the case when the Self and Other are Void, broken, scattered or clashed, and no subject has been added. This is the third of the doctor's questions (going to treat).
