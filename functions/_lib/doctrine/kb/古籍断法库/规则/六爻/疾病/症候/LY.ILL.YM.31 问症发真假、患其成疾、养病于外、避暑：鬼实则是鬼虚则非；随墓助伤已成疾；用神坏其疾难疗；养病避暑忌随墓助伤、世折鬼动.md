# 问症发真假、患其成疾、养病于外、避暑：鬼实则是鬼虚则非；随墓助伤已成疾；用神坏其疾难疗；养病避暑忌随墓助伤、世折鬼动

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有人问病症发作是不是真的，鬼爻实就是真的，鬼爻虚就不是。有人占担心会不会成病，遇到随墓、助伤，病就已经成了；用神如果坏了，这个病怎么能治好呢？占在外面养病的，遇到随墓、助伤，世爻受损、鬼动，不行；在这里避暑的，也是这个道理。注：官鬼空、破、散、绝叫做虚，旺相、有生扶叫做实；用神空、破、绝、散就会成病，旺相有生扶就不会成病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If someone asks whether the outbreak of symptoms is real: when the ghost is full it is real, when the ghost is empty it is not. If someone divines whether a worry will become an illness, following into the tomb and helping to harm mean the illness has already formed; if the Subject is spoiled, how can the illness be cured? For one who divines convalescing away from home, following into the tomb, helping to harm, the Self broken and the ghost moving will not do; for one escaping the summer heat here, it is the same. The note says: the Pressure line Void, broken, scattered or in Extinction is called empty, and thriving and strong, generated and supported, is called full; the Subject Void, broken, in Extinction or scattered, the illness forms, and thriving and strong, generated and supported, it does not form.

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1238 行 · Bracketed small note in the Yi Mao (author not named in the book)

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一条问的是病有没有真的发出来、会不会成病、能不能到外面去养，注里只补了一个判据：虚实看鬼，成不成看用神。「虚」是空、破、散、绝，「实」是旺相、有生扶。前面说的是「鬼实则是」，后面注里说的是「用神旺相则不成疾」，两处一个看鬼、一个看用神，并不矛盾：问症发是不是真，问的是鬼；问会不会成病，问的是人的用神。「避暑于此」的「此」字，原文没有交代是哪里，这里照译。
>
> *Note:* This entry asks whether the illness has really broken out, whether it will become an illness, and whether one may go away to convalesce, and the note adds only one criterion: emptiness or fullness is read from the ghost, and whether it forms from the Subject. 'Empty' is Void, broken, scattered or in Extinction; 'full' is thriving, strong, generated or supported. The text says 'the ghost full is real' and the note says 'the Subject thriving and strong, it does not form'; one looks at the ghost and the other at the Subject, and they do not contradict: asking whether the outbreak is real asks about the ghost, and asking whether it will become an illness asks about the person's Subject. The 'here' in 'escaping the heat here' is not identified in the original and is rendered as it stands.
