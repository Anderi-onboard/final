# 身临福德岂宜父动来伤：占病以子孙为解神，身上临着是大吉，父母动来克伤仍为不美（王洪绪：父母有制则无妨）

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病把子孙当解神，身上临着它是大吉的兆头；如果父母爻发动来克伤它，仍然不好（王洪绪补一句：父母爻自己被制住就没关系）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness Output is the relieving spirit, and its sitting on the body is a very lucky sign; if the Resource line moves to control and harm it, it is still not good (Wang Hongxu adds: if the Resource line is itself restrained, there is no harm).

## 《黄金策·病体》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6913 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身临福德，岂宜父动来伤。 | 身上临着福德，怎么能容父母发动来克伤它。 | The body sitting on Output-virtue: how could one allow a moving Resource line to come and harm it? |

^q1

> | 占病以子孙为解神，身临之大吉之兆。 | 占病把子孙当解神，身爻临着它是大吉的兆头。 | In divining illness Output is taken as the relieving spirit, and its sitting on the body is a very lucky sign. |
> | 如父母动来克伤，仍为不美，如父母有制无妨。 | 如果父母发动来克伤它，仍然不好，如果父母爻自己被制住就没关系。 | If the Resource line moves to control and harm it, it is still not good; if the Resource line is itself restrained, there is no harm. |

> [!note] 按
> 「身」指哪一爻，见 LY.ILL.HJC.35。这一条和它是一对：那里讲身临子孙是有救，这里讲父母动来克子孙就把这个好处破掉。王洪绪多了一句「父母有制无妨」。
>
> *Note:* For which line the 'body' is, see LY.ILL.HJC.35. This entry is a pair with it: there the body sitting on Output means rescue, and here a moving Resource line that controls Output spoils that advantage. Wang Hongxu adds 'if the Resource line is restrained there is no harm'.
