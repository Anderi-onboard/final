# 官临世上按爻位断神：鬼持世于初爻家堂作祸、二爻社坛作祟、三爻犯桥道之鬼或园中花木之精、四爻犯五道及师王佛实之灾、五爻无鬼不言、六爻山神为害及星宿降灾；鬼值空中莫断片殃之祟

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼临在世爻上，要注明六象的神；鬼落在空亡中，不要断作哪一份殃祟。注说：鬼在初爻持世，是家堂作祸；鬼在二爻持世，上气为实，并且是社坛作祟；鬼在三爻持世，是冒犯了桥道中的鬼，如果不是桥道，就是园中花木的精怪；鬼在四爻持世，是冒犯了五道，也有师王佛实的灾；鬼在五爻持世，原文说没有（可说的）不言；鬼在六爻持世，是山神为害，以及星宿降灾；六爻里鬼虽然持世，落空了就不说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line on the Self line: note the spirits of the six images; a ghost in Void: do not judge a haunting of any part of calamity. The commentary says: a ghost on the first line holding the Self is the household shrine making trouble; a ghost on the second holding the Self, the upper qi is real, and the altar of the soil haunts; a ghost on the third holding the Self offends the ghost of a bridge or road, and if not a bridge or road, it is the spirit of garden flowers and trees; a ghost on the fourth holding the Self offends the Five Roads, and there is also calamity from teachers, kings and Buddhas (the text says 'shi'); with the fifth, if there is no ghost, nothing is said; a ghost on the sixth holding the Self is the Mountain God doing harm and stars descending calamity; though the ghost in the six lines holds the Self, if it is Void nothing is said.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节和易冒「世于初者，家神也，世于二者，土神也……」（LY.ILL.YM.58）是同一件事：鬼持世时，看世在哪一爻。易林补遗的配法：初家堂、二社坛、三桥道或园中花木之精、四五道及师王佛、五爻不言、六山神及星宿。「上气为实」「师王佛实」两处原文没有解释，读法没有把握，这里按字面译。「六象之神」指上一节（LY.ILL.EL.37）里六个爻位各配的神。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This section is the same matter as the Yi Mao's 'the Self on the first line is a household spirit, on the second an earth spirit...' (LY.ILL.YM.58): when the ghost holds the Self, look at which line the Self is on. The Yilin Buyi's assignments: the first the household shrine, the second the altar of the soil, the third a bridge or road or the spirit of garden flowers and trees, the fourth the Five Roads and teachers, kings and Buddhas, the fifth left unsaid, the sixth the Mountain God and the stars. 'The upper qi is real' and 'teachers, kings and Buddhas (shi)' are not explained in the original, the reading is uncertain, and they are translated literally. 'The spirits of the six images' means the gods paired with the six line positions in the previous section (LY.ILL.EL.37). Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
