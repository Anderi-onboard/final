# 各类所占之人的用神、元神、忌神、仇神、泄气：占父母尊长——父母为用官鬼为元妻财为忌子孙为仇兄弟泄；占兄弟朋友、占子孙卑幼、占妻妾奴婢、占夫主官员各有其表；自己取世、他人取应；此法非惟占病，凡看卦无不用之

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占父母及家主尊长之类，取父母爻为用神，官鬼为元神，妻财为忌神，子孙为仇神，兄弟为泄气；占兄弟朋友之类，兄弟为用神，父母为元神，官鬼为忌神，妻财为仇神，子孙为泄气；占子孙卑幼之类，子孙为用神，兄弟为元神，父母为忌神，官鬼为仇神，妻财为泄气；占妻妾、弟媳子媳奴婢之类，妻财为用神，子孙为元神，兄弟为忌神，父母为仇神，官鬼为泄气；占丈夫、官员之类，官鬼为用神，妻财为元神，子孙为忌神，兄弟为仇神，父母为泄气；占自己取世作用爻，生世的为元神，克世的为忌神；占他人取应作用爻，生应的为元神，克应的为忌神；这个办法不光占病，凡是看卦，没有不用它的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a parent, or the master of the house or an elder, take the Resource line as the Subject, the Pressure line as the Support, the Wealth line as the Adversary, Output as the enemy spirit and Peer as the drain; in divining a brother or friend, Peer is the Subject, Resource the Support, Pressure the Adversary, Wealth the enemy spirit and Output the drain; in divining a son or a junior, Output is the Subject, Peer the Support, Resource the Adversary, Pressure the enemy spirit and Wealth the drain; in divining a wife, a concubine, a sister-in-law, a daughter-in-law or a maid, Wealth is the Subject, Output the Support, Peer the Adversary, Resource the enemy spirit and Pressure the drain; in divining a husband or an official, Pressure is the Subject, Wealth the Support, Output the Adversary, Peer the enemy spirit and Resource the drain; in divining oneself take the Self as the Subject line, what generates the Self as the Support and what controls the Self as the Adversary; in divining another take the Other as the Subject line, what generates the Other as the Support and what controls the Other as the Adversary; this method is not only for divining illness, and in looking at any hexagram there is none that does not use it.

> [!note] 按
> 这是全书通用的取用表，写在占病一章里：五类被占的人，每类五个位置——用神（所占者）、元神（生它的）、忌神（克它的）、仇神（生忌神的、克元神的）、泄气（被它生的）。比如占父母，用神是父母爻，生它的官鬼是元神，克它的妻财是忌神，生妻财的子孙是仇神，被它生的兄弟是泄气。「占自己取世、占他人取应」是通则；上面五类是按所占者的身份再细分。这一表与黄金策的用神取法（LY.ILL.HJC.33）、易冒的用神之法（LY.ILL.YM.21 到 YM.23）、易隐的取用爻（LY.ILL.YY.03）相比，五类的划分与常法一致，但没有易冒「主虽少而用父母」那样单独论辈分和亲疏。第一条「占父母及家主尊长」里，家主也取父母爻，与易冒相同（易冒 LY.ILL.YM.21），而与易隐「奴丁占家主取五爻」（LY.ILL.YY.03）不同。
>
> *Note:* This is the book's general table for taking the Subject, written in the chapter on illness: five kinds of person divined, each with five positions — Subject (the one divined), Support (what generates it), Adversary (what controls it), enemy spirit (what generates the Adversary, or controls the Support) and drain (what it generates). For example, in divining a parent the Subject is the Resource line, the Pressure line that generates it is the Support, the Wealth line that controls it is the Adversary, Output which generates Wealth is the enemy spirit, and Peer which it generates is the drain. 'In divining oneself take the Self, in divining another the Other' is the general rule; the five kinds above subdivide it by the status of the one divined. Compared with the Huangjin Ce's way of taking the Subject (LY.ILL.HJC.33), the Yi Mao's (LY.ILL.YM.21 to YM.23) and the Yi Yin's line for the Subject (LY.ILL.YY.03), its division into five kinds agrees with the usual method, but it does not go on, as the Yi Mao does, to treat seniority and closeness separately, as in 'a master, though young, takes the Resource line'. In its first item, 'a parent, or the master of the house or an elder', the master also takes the Resource line, as in the Yi Mao (LY.ILL.YM.21), and unlike the Yi Yin's 'servants divining the master of the house take the fifth line' (LY.ILL.YY.03).
