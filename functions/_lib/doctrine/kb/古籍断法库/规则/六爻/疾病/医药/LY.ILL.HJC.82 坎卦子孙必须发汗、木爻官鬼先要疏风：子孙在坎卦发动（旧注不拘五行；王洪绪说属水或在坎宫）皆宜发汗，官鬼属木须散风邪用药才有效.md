# 坎卦子孙必须发汗、木爻官鬼先要疏风：子孙在坎卦发动（旧注不拘五行；王洪绪说属水或在坎宫）皆宜发汗，官鬼属木须散风邪用药才有效

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙在坎卦发动，都宜发汗（旧注：不管是什么五行；王洪绪：子孙属水，或者在坎宫）；官鬼属木，要先散风邪，用药才有效。这只是举一个例子，五行八卦都照这个推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving in the Kan trigram is always suited to inducing sweat (the old commentary: whatever its phase; Wang Hongxu: Output that is Water, or in the Kan palace); a Wood Pressure line calls for dispersing the wind pathogens first, and then the medicine is effective. This is only one instance, and the five phases and eight trigrams are all reasoned in this way.

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 什么样的子孙宜发汗：旧注是子孙在坎卦发动，不管什么五行；王洪绪是子孙属水，或者在坎宫发动，多了「属水」这一种。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What sort of Output is suited to inducing sweat: the old commentary says Output moving in the Kan trigram, whatever its phase; Wang Hongxu says Output that is Water, or moves in the Kan palace, which adds the case of 'being Water'.

> [!note] 按
> 「发汗」「表汗」是让病人出汗、把外邪从体表散出去；「疏风」「散风邪」是散去风邪。坎属水、主北方寒冷，书里没有说为什么坎宜发汗。
>
> *Note:* 'Inducing sweat' and 'releasing the exterior' are to make the patient sweat and disperse the external pathogen from the surface of the body; 'dispersing wind' is to scatter the wind pathogens. Kan belongs to Water and to the cold of the north, and the book does not say why Kan is suited to sweating.
