# 子孙发动当勿药而自痊、父母交重纵延医而难治：子孙发动而不遇日月动变伤克，用药立效，喻之不药也能好

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动，应当不用吃药自己就好；父母爻交重（发动），即使请医生也难以治好。具体说，子孙发动、没有遇到日、月、动爻、变爻的伤克，如果占用药，应当许他立刻见效，所以用「不吃药也能好」来比喻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output moves, one recovers of oneself without medicine; if Resource moves, then even calling a doctor it is hard to cure. Specifically, if Output moves and meets no harm or control from the Day, Month, moving or changed lines, then if one divines about taking medicine one may promise that it will take effect at once, so it is put as 'it can be cured without medicine'.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条对痘疹讲的「子孙发动」和增删卜易讲的「子孙持世发动服之立可解忧」（LY.ILL.ZS.37）、「子孙制鬼最喜旺相生扶」（LY.ILL.ZS.30）是同一个看法；「父母交重」难治，和增删卜易的「父爻持世妙药难调」（LY.ILL.ZS.32）也一致。「交重」就是发动。
>
> *Note:* What is said here of a moving Output line agrees with the Zengshan Buyi's 'Output on the Self position or moving, taking it gives immediate relief' (LY.ILL.ZS.37) and 'Output controlling Pressure most wants to be strong and supported' (LY.ILL.ZS.30); that a moving Resource line is hard to cure also agrees with the Zengshan Buyi's 'Resource holding Self: even wonderful medicine is hard to mix' (LY.ILL.ZS.32). 'Jiaozhong' simply means moving.
