# 鬼伏六亲之下各有病因：伏财伤饥失饱、伏子酒色、伏父劳心、伏兄口舌争竞

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼伏在哪个六亲下面，就是怎么得的病：伏在妻财下，是饮食不节、饥饱失度，或者因为钱财、妇人得病；伏在子孙下，是喝酒过度、放纵房事，夏天露宿贪凉、冬天穿得太暖，或者吃多了养生炼丹的药；伏在父母下，是劳心劳力、忧虑伤神，或者因为动土修造、尊长得病；伏在兄弟下，是口舌争竞、停食生气，或者有人诅咒、因为赌钱争财得病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Which relative the Pressure is hidden beneath shows how the illness was got: beneath Wealth, eating without restraint and hunger and surfeit, or illness through money or a woman; beneath Output, too much drinking and unrestrained sexual indulgence, sleeping out in the cool in summer, dressing too warmly in winter, or too many drugs for nourishing and refining; beneath Resource, toil of mind and body and worry that harms the spirit, or breaking ground and building, or illness through an elder; beneath Peer, quarrels and rivalry, food stopped and anger felt, or a curse laid, or gambling and struggling for money.

## 《黄金策·疾病》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6789 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若伏妻财，必是伤饥失饱； | 如果伏在妻财下面，一定是饥一顿饱一顿伤了身； | If hidden beneath Wealth, it is surely from going hungry and then overeating; |
> | 如藏福德，必然酒醉耽淫。 | 如果藏在子孙下面，一定是喝醉了酒、沉迷女色。 | if hidden beneath Output, it is surely from drunkenness and lust. |
> | 父乃劳伤所致，兄为气食相侵。 | 伏在父母下是劳累所致，伏在兄弟下是脾气和饮食互相侵扰。 | Beneath Resource it comes from overwork; beneath Peer, from temper and food attacking one another. |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2311 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼伏财下，必是伤食，或因财物起因，或因妇女得病。 | 鬼伏在财下，一定是伤了食，或者因为钱财起因，或者因为妇女得病。 | Pressure hidden beneath Wealth: surely damage from food, or the cause is money and goods, or the illness comes through a woman. |
> | 鬼伏子下，必是酒醉过渡，或恣行房事，夏或过于风凉，冬或多着裘帛，或过服补药所致。 | 鬼伏在子下，一定是酒醉过度，或者放纵房事，夏天太贪风凉，冬天穿得太多皮衣丝绸，或者补药吃多了。 | Pressure hidden beneath Output: surely too much drunkenness, or unrestrained sexual indulgence, too much cool wind in summer, too many furs and silks in winter, or too many tonic drugs. |
> | 鬼伏父下，必是劳心劳力，忧虑伤神，或因动土所致，或因尊长得病。 | 鬼伏在父下，一定是劳心劳力，忧虑伤神，或者因为动土，或者因为尊长得病。 | Pressure hidden beneath Resource: surely toil of mind and body, worry that harms the spirit, or breaking ground, or the illness comes through an elder. |
> | 鬼伏兄下，必因口舌争竞，停食感气，或有咒诅得病。 | 鬼伏在兄下，一定是口舌争竞，停食生气，或者有人诅咒得病。 | Pressure hidden beneath Peer: surely quarrels and rivalry, food stopped in the stomach and anger felt, or a curse laid. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注里「咒咀」是「咒诅」的另一种写法。「养精等药」「修炼」指道家炼丹养生的药物，书里没有具体说是哪几种。
>
> *Note:* 'Curse' is written in two ways in the old commentary. The 'drugs for refining and nourishing the vital essence' are the medicines of Daoist alchemy and self-cultivation, which the book does not specify.
