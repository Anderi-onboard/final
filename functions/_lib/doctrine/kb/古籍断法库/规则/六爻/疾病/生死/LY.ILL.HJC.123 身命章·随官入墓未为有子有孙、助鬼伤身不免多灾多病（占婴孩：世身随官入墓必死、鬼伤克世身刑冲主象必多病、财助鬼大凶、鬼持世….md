# 身命章·随官入墓未为有子有孙、助鬼伤身不免多灾多病（占婴孩：世身随官入墓必死、鬼伤克世身刑冲主象必多病、财助鬼大凶、鬼持世临身亦多病）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》身命章占婴孩的一联说：「随官入墓，不算有子有孙；助鬼伤身，免不了多灾多病。」旧注：世爻和身随着官鬼入墓，一定死；鬼伤克世、身，刑冲主象，一定多病，财爻再帮它，是大凶；鬼持世、临身，也主多病。王洪绪：子孙入墓或化官入墓、化官鬼，一定死；鬼伤克兄弟爻、使子孙爻没有根，一定多病难养；财动助鬼克兄，或鬼持世临身，也主多病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A couplet in the Huangjin Ce's chapter on the person and fate, on divining an infant, says: 'Following Pressure into the tomb, one cannot be said to have sons and grandsons; helping the ghost to harm the Body, one cannot escape many calamities and illnesses.' The old commentary: if the Self and Body follow Pressure into the tomb, one is sure to die; if a ghost harms and controls the Self or Body, punishing or clashing the principal image, there is sure to be much illness, and still worse if Wealth helps it; a ghost holding the Self line or sitting on the Body also means much illness. Wang Hongxu: Output entering the tomb, or turning into an officer and entering the tomb, or turning into Pressure, is sure death; a ghost harming and controlling Peer so that Output has no root means much illness and a child hard to raise; Wealth moving to help the ghost control Peer, or a ghost holding the Self line or sitting on the Body, also means much illness.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联在身命章里是占婴孩（小孩的造化）的一段，「有子有孙」是说这个孩子立得住、活得成。「随官入墓」「助鬼伤身」和通玄妙论里同名的两节是同一个说法：随官入墓见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.27，助鬼伤身见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.29（那两节讲的是占病）。旧注「刑冲主象」的主象是所占的那一爻，占婴孩时是子孙爻。「世身」是世爻和身。
>
> *Note:* This couplet is in a passage of the chapter on the person and fate that divines an infant (a child's fate), and 'having sons and grandsons' says that the child will stand and live. 'Following Pressure into the tomb' and 'helping the ghost to harm the Body' are the same sayings as the two sections of the same names in the Tongxuan Miaolun: following Pressure into the tomb is in LY.ILL.DYTJ.27 and helping the ghost to harm the Body in LY.ILL.DYTJ.29 (those two sections are about divining illness). The 'principal image' of the old commentary is the line being divined, which in divining an infant is the Output line. 'The Self and the Body' are the Self line and the Body.
