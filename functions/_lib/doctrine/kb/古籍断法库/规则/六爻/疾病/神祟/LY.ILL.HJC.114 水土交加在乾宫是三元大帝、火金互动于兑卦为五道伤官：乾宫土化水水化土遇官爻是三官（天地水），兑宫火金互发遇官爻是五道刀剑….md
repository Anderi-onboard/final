# 水土交加在乾宫是三元大帝、火金互动于兑卦为五道伤官：乾宫土化水水化土遇官爻是三官（天地水），兑宫火金互发遇官爻是五道刀剑之神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水和土交错加在一起，在乾宫就是三元大帝（天官、地官、水官）：乾宫里土变水、水变土，又遇到官爻的，就是这个象；火和金互相发动，在兑卦就是五道伤官（五道是刀剑之神）：在兑宫里互相发动，又遇到官爻的，就是这个象。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Water and Earth mingled together, in the Qian palace, are the Three Primes Great Emperor (the Officials of Heaven, Earth and Water): in the Qian palace, Earth changing into Water or Water into Earth and meeting a Pressure line is this image. Fire and Metal moving upon each other, in the Dui trigram, are the Five Paths and the Injuring Officer (the Five Paths are the spirit of blade and sword): moving upon each other in the Dui palace and meeting a Pressure line is this image.

> [!note] 按
> 经文和旧注在卜筮大全里写在同一行，中间只隔一个空格；本库按内容拆开。「三元大帝」旧注解作天、地、水三官。旧注和王洪绪都只解释了三官和五道，「伤官」没有再解释。王洪绪引经文时把「在乾宫」「于兑卦」的位置调到了句中，意思一样。
>
> *Note:* In the Bushi Daquan the verse and old commentary sit on one line separated by a single space; the library splits them by content. The old commentary explains the 'Three Primes Great Emperor' as the Three Officials of Heaven, Earth and Water. Both commentaries explain only the Three Officials and the Five Paths, and neither explains the 'Injuring Officer'. When Wang Hongxu quotes the verse he moves 'in Qian' and 'in Dui' to the middle of the line; the meaning is the same.
