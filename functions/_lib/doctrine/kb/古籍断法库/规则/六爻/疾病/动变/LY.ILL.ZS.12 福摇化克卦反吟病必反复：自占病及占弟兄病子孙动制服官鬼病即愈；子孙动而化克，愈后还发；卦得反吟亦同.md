# 福摇化克卦反吟病必反复：自占病及占弟兄病子孙动制服官鬼病即愈；子孙动而化克，愈后还发；卦得反吟亦同

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动却变出克、或者卦是反吟，病一定反复。自己占病和占兄弟的病，如果子孙发动、制服官鬼，这病马上就好；如果子孙动而变出克，即使病好了，好了以后还会再发，是反复的象；卦是反吟的，也照这个断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving but changing into a control, or the hexagram being a Reversed Chant: the illness surely relapses. In divining one's own illness or a brother's, if Output moves and subdues Pressure, the illness heals at once; if Output moves but changes into a control, then even if he recovers, the illness breaks out again afterward, an image of relapse; a hexagram that is a Reversed Chant is judged the same way.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是黄金策病体章 LY.ILL.HJC.45（福化忌爻，病势增加于小愈）的另一说法：黄金策讲的是病人病稍好就不谨慎，野鹤讲的是子孙动而变出克，病好了还会再发，并把反吟卦也算进来。「反吟」是卦或爻变出与自己相冲的，主反复。
>
> *Note:* This entry is another way of putting LY.ILL.HJC.45 in the chapter on the patient's condition (Output changing into an Adversary line, the illness worse after a slight improvement): the Huangjin Ce speaks of the patient becoming careless as soon as he is a little better, while the Old Man speaks of Output moving and changing into a control, so that the illness breaks out again after he has recovered, and he also counts the Reversed Chant hexagram. A 'Reversed Chant' is a hexagram or line that changes into a clash with itself, and means going back and forth.
