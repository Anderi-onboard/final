# 孙膑断疾病歌(一)：凡占疾病先从卦中寻本命、仍看来占是何人；福德持世鬼暂退、爻旺之日又加进；卦中无鬼病难医、鬼旺财兴命难保；鬼煞临身五墓加、鬼神休祷药无灵

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占疾病，先在卦里找出病人的本命爻，再看来占的是什么人；福德持世，鬼暂时退去，可是爻旺的日子又进一步；卦里没有鬼，病难医；鬼旺、财兴，命难保；鬼煞临在身上又加了五墓，向鬼神祷告也不必了，药也无灵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness, first find the sick person's natal line in the hexagram and look at who has come to divine. With Output holding the Self the ghost retreats for a time, but on a day when the line is strong it advances again. With no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to treat; with the ghost strong and Wealth rising life is hard to keep. A ghost-killing sitting on the Body with the Five Tombs added: there is no use praying to ghosts and spirits, and medicine has no effect.

> [!note] 按
> 这是孙膑断疾病歌的头十句。前面几句和阐奥歌章的第二首疾病歌（LY.ILL.CA.04）几乎一样，只有几处用字不同：阐奥歌章是「官旺之日又加进」，这里是「爻旺之日又加进」；阐奥歌章是「鬼旺财兴难保命」，这里是「鬼旺财兴命难保」；阐奥歌章是「鬼杀临身五墓侵、鬼神不应药无灵」，这里是「鬼煞临身五墓加、鬼神休祷药无灵」。「爻旺之日」的「爻」，照阐奥歌章同一句读作鬼爻。「本命」是病人的本命爻（生年属相所在的爻）。「鬼煞临身五墓加」后面的「（1）」是今人加的注号，那条注不收；五墓的排法（春未、夏戌、秋丑、冬辰）见 LY.ILL.EL.51。「休祷」按「不必祷告」译。
>
> *Note:* These are the first ten lines of Sun Bin's song on judging illness. The first lines are almost the same as the second illness poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.04), with a few differences in wording: the Chan'ao Gezhang has 'on a day when the Pressure line is strong it advances again' where this has 'on a day when the line is strong'; it has 'ghost strong and Wealth rising, hard to keep life' where this has 'life is hard to keep'; and it has 'a ghost-killing on the Body and the Five Tombs invading, the ghosts and spirits do not respond and medicine has no effect' where this has 'the Five Tombs added, no use praying to ghosts and spirits, medicine has no effect'. The 'line' of 'on a day when the line is strong' is read as the Pressure line, following the same line in the Chan'ao Gezhang. The 'natal line' is the sick person's natal line (the line where the birth animal falls). The '(1)' after 'the Five Tombs added' is a modern annotation mark and that annotation is not quoted; for how the Five Tombs are laid out (Wei in spring, Xu in summer, Chou in autumn, Chen in winter) see LY.ILL.EL.51. 'Xiu dao' is rendered 'no use praying'.
