# 坟墓·叠刃刑鬼破之劫亡，疲癃残疾（旧注：鬼临破碎杀兼三刑六害叠刃亡劫同克用爻，八宫主病；金鬼劳漱、木鬼风邪、火鬼热症、水鬼吐泻、土鬼黄浮肿）

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》坟墓章说：「叠刃、刑、鬼、破、劫、亡（这些凶煞）叠加，就有疲癃残疾。」旧注：鬼爻临着破碎杀，又带三刑六害、叠刃、亡神劫煞，一同克用爻，按八宫主病：乾主头脸、喘急、咳嗽、小肠；坎主手臂脸面、两耳、小便、气血、腰痛、肋和心；艮主鼻疮、手指腿脚；震主骨、脚、肝、腿、三焦、笑言；巽主须鬓膝、血气风邪；离主脾胃痈疽、眼痛、心惊心惧、热症、阳火气；坤主肚腹呕吐、便血泻痢、黄肿；兑主口齿残缺、唇翻、皮肤；金鬼乘旺是痨嗽，木鬼是风邪，火鬼是热症，水鬼是吐泻，土鬼是黄浮肿；其中说不完，要按道理推详。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on graves says: 'Doubled Blade, Punishment, ghost, break, Robbery and Loss together: infirmity and disablement.' The old commentary: a ghost on the Breaking killing, with the three punishments and six harms, the Doubled Blade, Loss and Robbery together controlling the Subject line, means illness by palace: Qian, the head and face, rapid panting, cough and the small intestine; Kan, the arms and face, the two ears, urine, blood and qi, waist pain, the ribs and heart; Gen, nose sores and the fingers, legs and feet; Zhen, the bones, feet, liver, legs, the triple burner and raving laughter and talk; Xun, the beard, temples and knees, blood and qi and wind-evil; Li, abscesses of the spleen and stomach, eye pain, heart fright and fear, heat symptoms and yang-fire qi; Kun, the belly, vomiting, blood in the stool, diarrhoea and dysentery, and yellow swelling; Dui, mouth and missing teeth, a turned-up lip and the skin; a Metal ghost in strength is consumption and cough, a Wood ghost wind-evil, a Fire ghost heat symptoms, a Water ghost vomiting and diarrhoea, an Earth ghost yellow puffy swelling; there is too much in between to set out and it is to be worked out by reason.

> [!warning] 打架
> 八卦各管身体哪一处，几处说法不同。这张表里艮有「鼻疮」又有「手指腿足」，震有「骨足肝腿」和「三焦笑言」（王洪绪作「三焦癫狂」），巽是「须鬓膝、血气风邪」；同一书家宅章的另一张表（LY.ILL.HJC.139）是艮背肋手指、震下部腰足、巽两股两腿。艮有鼻（同阐奥歌章 LY.ILL.CA.02）也有手（同孙膑歌、卜筮元龟 LY.ILL.DYTJ.03、LY.ILL.DYTJ.12）；巽这里是须鬓膝，那一张是股、腿，和卜筮元龟的巽股相近。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books differ on which part of the body each trigram governs. In this table Gen has 'nose sores' and also 'fingers, legs and feet', Zhen has 'bones, feet, liver, legs' and 'the triple burner, laughing and talking' (Wang Hongxu has 'the triple burner and madness'), and Xun has 'beard, temples and knees, blood and qi and wind-evil'; another table in the chapter on the household of the same book (LY.ILL.HJC.139) has Gen the back, ribs and fingers, Zhen the lower body, waist and feet, and Xun the two thighs and two legs. Gen has the nose (as in the Chan'ao Gezhang, LY.ILL.CA.02) and also the hand (as in Sun Bin's verse and the Bushi Yuangui, LY.ILL.DYTJ.03 and LY.ILL.DYTJ.12); Xun here is the beard, temples and knees, while the other table has the thighs and legs, close to the Bushi Yuangui's Xun the thigh. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这是坟墓章里「叠刃刑鬼破之劫亡，疲癃残疾」一句的旧注，讲鬼爻带着一串凶煞（破碎杀、三刑、六害、叠刃、亡神、劫煞）同克用爻时，按八宫和五行看病。「疲癃」是衰弱、驼背之病，「残疾」是肢体残废。「劳漱」是痨病、咳嗽；「黄浮肿」是面目浮肿发黄。王洪绪的版本在「离宫主脾胃、」处断了，后面的几宫和五行没有，所以他只收到巽宫。「用爻」就是用神。
>
> *Note:* This is the old commentary on the line 'Doubled Blade, Punishment, ghost, break, Robbery and Loss together: infirmity and disablement' in the chapter on graves, telling the illness by palace and by element when a ghost line carries a run of ominous stars (the Breaking killing, the three punishments, the six harms, the Doubled Blade, Loss and Robbery) and together controls the Subject line. 'Infirmity' is weakness and a bent back, and 'disablement' is crippled limbs. 'Consumption and cough' is a consumptive illness with cough; 'yellow puffy swelling' is swelling of the face and eyes with a yellow tinge. Wang Hongxu's version breaks off at 'the Li palace governs the spleen and stomach,' and lacks the later palaces and the elements, so his is taken only as far as the Xun palace. The 'Subject line' is the Subject.
