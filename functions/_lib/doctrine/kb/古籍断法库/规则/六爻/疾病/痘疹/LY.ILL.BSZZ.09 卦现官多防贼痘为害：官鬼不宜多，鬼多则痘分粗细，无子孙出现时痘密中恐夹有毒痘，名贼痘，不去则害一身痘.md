# 卦现官多防贼痘为害：官鬼不宜多，鬼多则痘分粗细，无子孙出现时痘密中恐夹有毒痘，名贼痘，不去则害一身痘

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里官爻出现得多，要防备贼痘为害：官鬼不适合多，鬼多的话痘就分粗和细；如果没有子孙出现，恐怕在密密的痘当中夹着有毒的痘，名叫贼痘，如果不把它除去，就会害了满身的痘。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If many Pressure lines appear in the hexagram, guard against the harm of thief pox: there should not be many Pressure lines, and with many the pox divide into coarse and fine; if no Output appears, there may be poisonous pox among the dense ones, called thief pox, and if it is not removed it will spoil the pox over the whole body.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2245 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼不宜多，鬼多则痘分粗细，如无子孙出现，恐痘密之中间有毒痘，其名曰贼痘，如不去之，则害一身痘矣。 | 官鬼不适合多，鬼多的话痘就分粗细；如果没有子孙出现，恐怕在密密的痘当中夹着有毒的痘，名叫贼痘，如果不把它除去，就会害了满身的痘。 | There should not be many Pressure lines; with many, the pox divide into coarse and fine; if no Output appears, there may be poisonous pox among the dense ones, called thief pox, and if it is not removed it will spoil the pox over the whole body. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 注里没有说怎样「去」贼痘，只说不去会害一身痘。「贼痘」是这一章自己的名目，书里其他地方没有再讲。
>
> *Note:* The commentary does not say how to remove a thief pox, only that if it is not removed it spoils the pox over the whole body. 'Thief pox' is this chapter's own term and the book does not treat it elsewhere.
