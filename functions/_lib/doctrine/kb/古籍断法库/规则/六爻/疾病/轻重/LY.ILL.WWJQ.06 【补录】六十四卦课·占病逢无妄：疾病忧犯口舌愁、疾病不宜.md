# 【补录】六十四卦课·占病逢无妄：疾病忧犯口舌愁、疾病不宜

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[文王金钱课]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 文王金钱课文末补录的网上传本里，无妄卦的卦辞和推断都说病不好：卦辞「疾病忧犯口舌愁」（病是一件忧事，又添上口舌之愁），推断「疾病不宜」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the internet copy that the library appended at the end of the Wenwang Jinqian Ke, both the hexagram verse and the judgment of Wuwang say that illness does not fare well: the verse 'illness is a worry, and the grief of quarrels is added', and the judgment 'illness: not fit'.

## 《文王金钱课·【补录】天雷无妄》原文

> [!quote]+ [[文王金钱课]] 第 265–266 行 · Wenwang Jinqian Ke (King Wen's Coin Readings), "[Supplement] Heaven over Thunder, Wuwang", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦辞： | 卦辞说： | The hexagram verse says: |
> | 鸟如笼中难出头， | 鸟像在笼子里，难出头， | like a bird in a cage that cannot put out its head, |
> | 占着此卦不自由。 | 占到这一卦，不自由。 | divining this hexagram, one is not free. |
> | 谋望求财不定准， | 谋划求财没有定准， | plans and the seeking of wealth are not sure, |
> | 疾病忧犯口舌愁。 | 疾病有忧，又犯口舌之愁。 | illness is a worry, and the grief of quarrels is added. |
> | 推断： | 推断说： | The judgment says: |
> | 出门不遇， | 出门遇不到人， | going out, one meets no one, |
> | 求财不利， | 求财不利， | the seeking of wealth is unfavourable, |
> | 婚姻走失， | 婚姻、走失， | marriage, losses: |
> | 疾病不宜。 | 疾病不适宜。 | illness: not fit. |

^q1

> [!warning] 打架
> 无妄卦，周易尚占说「病瘥讼和」（病好了、官司和解）（LY.ILL.ZSZ.02），这里补录的传本却说「疾病不宜」，两本相反；另外，增删卜易（LY.ILL.ZS.01）列的「断之必死」的十个卦里没有无妄。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For Wuwang the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the illness is cured and the lawsuit settled' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.02), but this supplemented copy says 'illness: not fit', and the two are opposed; moreover, the ten hexagrams of 'judge death' reported in the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.01) do not include Wuwang. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这个卦是库里「补录」的网上传本，不是这部书原文里有的（原文只有二十六卦）；库里的说明写：这份传本每卦只有「卦辞」（七言四句）和「推断」（四言四句）两项，没有课名、六亲、地支、世应，拿它和原有二十六卦对，十七卦两项都对得上，三卦卦辞对得上、推断有出入，另有三对卦的内容整个互换（风地观与山地剥、火泽睽与风泽中孚、天泽履与风山渐），所以补录部分个别卦也可能挂错了卦；卦名已按卦画订正；传本里没有风火家人。「婚姻走失」只有四个字，没有断语，译文照字面写成「婚姻、走失」。「出门不遇」按字面译成「出门遇不到人」，书里没有说是遇不到什么人。「忧犯口舌愁」读作「病是忧，又添口舌之愁」。
>
> *Note:* These hexagrams are from the 'supplemented' copy found on the internet that the library appended, and are not in the original text of this book (the original has only twenty-six hexagrams); the library's note says: this copy gives each hexagram only two items, the 'hexagram verse' (four lines of seven characters) and the 'judgment' (four phrases of four characters), with no name of the reading, no six relations, no branches, no Self and Other; compared with the twenty-six original hexagrams, seventeen agree in both items, three agree in the verse but differ in the judgment, and three pairs of hexagrams have their contents swapped wholesale (Guan and Bo, Kui and Zhongfu, Lü and Jian), so some of the supplemented hexagrams may also be hung on the wrong hexagram; the names of the hexagrams have been corrected from the hexagram figures; the copy lacks Jiaren. 'Marriage, losses' is only four characters with no verdict, and is translated literally. 'Going out, one meets no one' is literal, and the book does not say whom one fails to meet. 'A worry, and the grief of quarrels is added' reads the line as 'illness is a worry, and the grief of quarrels is added'.
