# 鬼自入墓刑决是狱中囚犯：未日占得木爻官鬼谓鬼自入墓，是死于囹圄的囚人之鬼，更带刑爻必死于非命；旺相发动则是庙神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼自己入墓、带刑，一定是关在牢里的囚犯的鬼：比如在未日占卦，得到木爻的官鬼，叫做鬼自己入墓，一定是死在牢狱里的人的鬼；再带刑爻，一定是死于非命，或者死在坟墓之间；旺相又发动的，就是庙里的神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost that enters its own tomb or is punished is surely the ghost of a prisoner in jail: if one divines on a Wei day and gets a Wood Pressure line, the ghost is said to enter its own tomb, and it is surely the ghost of one who died in prison; if it also carries a punishing line, he surely died an unnatural death or died among graves; and if strong and moving, it is a temple god.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3397 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 如未日占卦，得木爻官鬼入墓，必是死于囹圄囚狱之鬼； | 比如在未日占卦，得到木爻官鬼入墓，一定是死在牢狱里的囚徒的鬼； | For example, if one divines on a Wei day and gets a Wood Pressure line entering its tomb, it is surely the ghost of one who died in prison; |
> | 旺相发动则是庙神。 | 旺相发动的，就是庙神。 | if strong and moving, it is a temple god. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「囹圄」是监狱（卜筮大全篇末生僻字注）。「自入墓」的例子是：未是木的墓，在未日占卦，得木爻官鬼，被日辰带进了墓。旧注多一句「更带刑爻，必死于非命，或死于坟墓间者」，王洪绪没有。
>
> *Note:* 'Lingyu' is a prison (the book's glossary at the end of the chapter). The example of 'entering its own tomb' is: Wei is the tomb of Wood, so on a Wei day, with a Wood Pressure line, the ghost is taken into its tomb by the Day. The old commentary has one more sentence, 'if it also carries a punishing line, he surely died an unnatural death or died among graves', which Wang Hongxu lacks.
