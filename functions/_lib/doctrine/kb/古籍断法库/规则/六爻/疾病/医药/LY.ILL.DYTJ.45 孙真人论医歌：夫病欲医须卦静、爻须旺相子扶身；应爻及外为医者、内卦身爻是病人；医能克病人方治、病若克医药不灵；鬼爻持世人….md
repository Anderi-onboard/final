# 孙真人论医歌：夫病欲医须卦静、爻须旺相子扶身；应爻及外为医者、内卦身爻是病人；医能克病人方治、病若克医药不灵；鬼爻持世人难保、吉杀加临获万全

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病人的病要医治，卦要安静，爻要旺相，子孙扶身；应爻和外卦是医生，内卦和身爻是病人；医生克病，病人才能治；病克医生，药不灵；鬼爻持世，人难保；吉杀加临，可获万全。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a man's illness is to be treated the hexagram must be still, the lines strong and Output supporting the Body; the Other line and the outer trigram are the doctor, the inner trigram and the Body line are the sick person; if the doctor controls the illness the person can be treated; if the illness controls the doctor the medicine is not effective; the Pressure line holding the Self, the person can hardly be kept; a lucky killing added, complete safety is gained.

> [!note] 按
> 孙真人是唐代的孙思邈。这首医药歌和阐奥歌章医药章的第二首（LY.ILL.CA.09）几乎一样，前四句（夫病欲医须卦静……内卦身爻是病人）和「鬼爻持世人难保」一字不差，只有三处不同：阐奥歌章是「医来克病人方愈」，这里是「医能克病人方治」；阐奥歌章是「吉曜加临大吉大利」，这里是「吉杀加临获万全」；「病若克医药不灵」两处相同。「吉杀」是带吉的神煞（指吉神吉煞），「获万全」是可得万全。原文孙真人这一首后面，断易天机没有再引注（今人有译文，不收）。这一首的医、病分法（应和外卦是医、内卦和世身是病人）和天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.12）是同一套。
>
> *Note:* Master Sun is Sun Simiao of the Tang. This song on medicine is almost the same as the second poem in the chapter on medicine in the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.09): the first four lines ('when a man's illness is to be treated the hexagram must be still ... the inner trigram and the Body line are the sick person') and 'the Pressure line holding the Self, the person can hardly be kept' are word for word the same, and there are only differences in a few lines: the Chan'ao Gezhang has 'if the doctor controls the illness the person is cured' where this has 'if the doctor is able to control the illness the person can be treated', and 'a lucky star added, greatly favourable' where this has 'a lucky killing added, complete safety is gained'; 'if the illness controls the doctor the medicine is not effective' is the same in both. 'A lucky killing' is a spirit or killing that brings luck, and 'complete safety is gained' is that complete safety can be had. After this poem of Master Sun the Duanyi Tianji cites no commentary (modern hands have added a translation, which is not quoted). The division of doctor and illness in this poem (the Other line and the outer trigram are the doctor, the inner trigram and the Self and Body are the sick person) is the same set as the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.12).
