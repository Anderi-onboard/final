# 子孙持世也要不受克：前面各篇都说子孙持世病即愈，野鹤补一条——必要子孙不受克才行（例：杨梅疮占医人，子孙持世应为医人，但初爻巳火动克申金，巳日添灾）

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 前面几篇都说子孙持世，病就马上好，但一定要子孙不受克才行，后来的人不可以不知道。比如有人生了杨梅疮，占这位医生好不好：虽然是子孙持世、应爻是医生，但初爻巳火发动克申金，又有卯木生火，世爻虽旺，巳日一定添灾；他不听，还是请了这位医生，卯日、辰日服药还平安，巳日忽然病症变了，全身疼痛难以忍受。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The earlier passages all say that with Output holding the Self line the illness heals at once, but it is so only when Output is not controlled, which later people must know. For example, a man with syphilitic sores divined whether a certain doctor was a good one: though Output held the Self line and the Other line was the doctor, the first line, Si Fire, moved and controlled Shen Metal, with Mao Wood also generating Fire, so that though the Self line was strong there would be added trouble on the Si day; he did not listen and called the doctor, and on the Mao and Chen days the medicine kept him safe, but on the Si day the symptoms suddenly changed and pain all over the body was unbearable.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是对 LY.ILL.ZS.10（子孙持世不药而痊）的补充限制：子孙持世还要看它有没有被克。书中例是占医生，不是占病：子孙持世、应是医人，但初爻巳火动来克申金，野鹤据此断这位医生的药会在巳日添灾。
>
> *Note:* This entry is a limiting supplement to LY.ILL.ZS.10 (Output holding the Self line heals without medicine): one must also see whether Output is controlled. The case divines a doctor and not an illness: Output holds the Self line and the Other line is the doctor, but the Si Fire at the first line moves to control Shen Metal, and from this the Old Man judged that this doctor's medicine would bring added trouble on the Si day.
