# 经验论家人占病六首：子占父病鬼要兴隆父要安；父占子病兄兴子旺死还生；夫占妻病财明子动便安然；妻占夫病鬼静财兴可放心；占兄弟病父兴兄静病回生；占奴婢病财安子发无他事

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 按病人和占卜人的关系分六首：子占父病，鬼要兴隆、父要安，兄动缠绵难脱体，子孙兴、财发就入黄泉；父占子病，兄兴子旺死里逃生，鬼摇父动终难保，财动病反增；夫占妻病，财明子动便安然，鬼爻发动更沉重，父发兄摇妻亡；妻占夫病，鬼静财兴可放心，兄弟交重恩义绝，父摇难愈；占兄弟病，父兴兄静病回生，鬼摇财动是黄泉客，子动躺几天；占奴婢病，鬼摇更缠绵，财安子发无事，父动兄兴不再生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Six poems divided by the relation between the sick person and the diviner. A son divining a father's illness: the ghost must thrive and Resource must be at peace; Peer moving, it drags on and is hard to shake off; Output rising and Wealth moving, it enters the Yellow Springs. A father divining a son's illness: Peer rising and Output strong, he dies and yet lives; the ghost shaking and Resource moving, in the end he cannot be kept; Wealth moving, the illness grows the more. A husband divining his wife's illness: Wealth clear and Output moving, at once she is at ease; the Pressure line moving, it grows heavier still; Resource issuing and Peer shaking, surely the string is cut. A wife divining her husband's illness: the ghost still and Wealth rising, one may set the heart at rest; Peer crossing heavily, love and duty are cut; Resource shaking, hard to cure. Divining a brother's illness: Resource rising and Peer still, the illness turns and he lives; the ghost shaking and Wealth moving, a guest of the Yellow Springs; Output moving, he lingers several days in bed. Divining a servant's illness: the ghost shaking, it drags on the more; Wealth at peace and Output issuing, no other matter; Resource moving and Peer rising, he does not live again.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这一组和阐奥歌章那一组（LY.ILL.CA.07）有两处是意思不同的句子，其余是同义换字。一、子占父母病：阐奥歌章「子要兴隆父要安」（子孙兴旺、父母安静），这里「鬼要兴隆父要安」（官鬼兴旺、父母安静），两句差一个字、意思相反，书里没有说明；二、妻占夫病：阐奥歌章「兄子交重恩义绝」（兄和子孙都发动），这里「兄弟交重恩义绝」（兄弟发动）。别的只是同义换字：「问卦灵」和「问卦因」、「子动」和「子旺」、「缠绵」和「绵绵」、「增」和「添」、「疾病煎」和「疾病生」、「还沉重」和「加沉重」、「自安全」和「病回生」、「不得痊」和「不再生」。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This set and that of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.07) differ in two places in sense, and the rest are synonyms swapped. One, a son divining a parent's illness: the Chan'ao Gezhang has 'Output must thrive and Resource must be at peace' (Output flourishing and Resource still), this has 'the ghost must thrive and Resource must be at peace' (the Pressure line flourishing and Resource still); the two differ by one character and are opposite in sense, and the book does not explain it. Two, a wife divining her husband's illness: the Chan'ao Gezhang has 'Peer and Output crossing heavily, love and duty are cut' (Peer and Output both moving), this has 'Peer crossing heavily, love and duty are cut' (Peer moving). The others are only synonyms swapped: 'asking the hexagram whether it is efficacious' and 'asking the hexagram for the cause', 'Output moving' and 'Output strong', 'drags on' in two words, 'increases' and 'adds', 'the illness that torments' and 'the illness that has arisen', 'still heavy' and 'heavier', 'of itself safe' and 'the illness turns and he lives', 'no recovery' and 'he does not live again'.

> [!note] 按
> 这六首和阐奥歌章的六亲占病歌（LY.ILL.CA.07）是同一组，字句有几处不同。「断弦」是妻子去世的委婉说法（琴弦断了）；「黄泉客」是死亡；「财明」是财爻明显出现。第一首的第二句是「鬼要兴隆父要安」，阐奥歌章是「子要兴隆父要安」，见「打架」。用爻表是：子占父母看父母、父占子看子孙、夫占妻看财、妻占夫看鬼（见 LY.ILL.TXF.01）。「占兄弟病」的「兄弟」是兄弟这个亲人，不是兄弟爻。
>
> *Note:* These six are the same set as the poems of the Chan'ao Gezhang for divining illness by relations (LY.ILL.CA.07), with several differences in wording. 'Duan xian' (the string cut) is a delicate way of saying the wife has died (a broken zither string); 'guest of the Yellow Springs' is death; 'Wealth clear' is the Wealth line clearly appearing. The second line of the first poem is 'the ghost must thrive and Resource must be at peace', where the Chan'ao Gezhang has 'Output must thrive and Resource must be at peace', see the dispute. The table of the Subject line: a son about a parent looks at Resource, a parent about a child at Output, a husband about a wife at Wealth, a wife about a husband at the ghost (see LY.ILL.TXF.01). The 'brother' of 'divining a brother's illness' is a brother as a relative and not the Peer line.
