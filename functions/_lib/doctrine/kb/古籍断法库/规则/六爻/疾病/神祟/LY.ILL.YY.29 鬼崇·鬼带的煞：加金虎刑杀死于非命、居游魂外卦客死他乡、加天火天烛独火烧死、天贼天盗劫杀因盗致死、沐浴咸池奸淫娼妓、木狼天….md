# 鬼崇·鬼带的煞：加金虎刑杀死于非命、居游魂外卦客死他乡、加天火天烛独火烧死、天贼天盗劫杀因盗致死、沐浴咸池奸淫娼妓、木狼天缢缢死、风波浴盆浮沉溺死、羊刃刀砧屠刽鬼、暗金血刃产亡鬼、天刑天狱地狱牢狱入狱牢狱鬼、刀砧羊刃劫杀自刎鬼、华盖孤神僧道绝嗣鬼、螣蛇天怪精怪妖邪；鬼居太岁是当年岁君、居月日时是游野神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看鬼带的煞：鬼带金、白虎、刑煞的，是死于非命；鬼落在游魂卦的外卦的，是客死他乡；带天火、天烛、独火煞的，是烧死；带天贼、天盗、劫煞的，是因盗致死；带沐浴、咸池煞的，是奸淫、娼妓；带木狼、天缢煞的，是缢死；带风波、浴盆、浮沉煞的，是溺死；带羊刃、刀砧煞的，是屠夫刽子手的鬼；带暗金、血刃煞的，是产亡的鬼；带天刑、天狱、地狱、牢狱、入狱煞的，是牢狱的鬼；带刀砧、羊刃、劫煞的，是自刎的鬼；带华盖、孤神的，是僧道、绝了后的鬼；带螣蛇、天怪煞的，是精怪妖邪。鬼落在太岁上的，是当年的岁君；落在月、日、时上的，是游荡在野外的神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* By the stars the ghost carries: a ghost carrying Metal, the Tiger and punishing stars is one who died an unnatural death; a ghost in the outer trigram of a Wandering Soul hexagram, one who died a stranger in a far land; with Heavenly Fire, Heavenly Candle and Lone Fire, burned to death; with Heavenly Thief, Heavenly Robber and Robbery-Killing, dead through robbers; with Bath and Xianchi, adultery and prostitution; with Wood Wolf and Heavenly Hanging, hanged; with Wind-and-Waves, Bath Basin and Floating-Sinking, drowned; with Goat Blade and Chopping Block, a butcher or executioner ghost; with Hidden Metal and Blood Blade, a ghost who died in childbirth; with Heavenly Punishment, Heavenly Prison, Earthly Prison, Jail and Entering-Prison, a jail ghost; with Chopping Block, Goat Blade and Robbery-Killing, a ghost who cut his own throat; with Canopy and Lone Spirit, a monk or Daoist, one who left no heir; with the Coiling Snake and Heavenly Monster, a sprite, monster or evil. A ghost on the Tai Sui is the Year Lord of the year, and one on the Month, Day or hour a wandering field spirit.

> [!note] 按
> 天火、天烛、独火、天贼、天盗、劫煞、咸池、木狼、天缢、风波、浴盆、浮沉、羊刃、刀砧、暗金、血刃、天刑、天狱、地狱、牢狱、入狱、华盖、孤神、天怪都是神煞，书里只给了名目和它们指向的死因，没有解释每一颗从何而来。易冒有一套神煞起法（见易冒神煞章），但这里没有指明用哪一套。「岁君」就是太岁。「游魂」是八宫里每宫的第七卦。「刽」是刽子手，即行刑的人。
>
> *Note:* Heavenly Fire, Heavenly Candle, Lone Fire, Heavenly Thief, Heavenly Robber, Robbery-Killing, Xianchi, Wood Wolf, Heavenly Hanging, Wind-and-Waves, Bath Basin, Floating-Sinking, Goat Blade, Chopping Block, Hidden Metal, Blood Blade, Heavenly Punishment, Heavenly Prison, Earthly Prison, Jail, Entering-Prison, Canopy, Lone Spirit and Heavenly Monster are all spirit-stars; the book gives only the names and the causes of death they point to and does not explain where each comes from. The Yi Mao has a scheme for setting these stars (see its chapter on spirit-stars) but this passage does not say which scheme is meant. 'Year Lord' is the Tai Sui. 'Wandering Soul' is the seventh hexagram of each palace among the eight. A 'gui' is an executioner, one who carries out the punishment.
