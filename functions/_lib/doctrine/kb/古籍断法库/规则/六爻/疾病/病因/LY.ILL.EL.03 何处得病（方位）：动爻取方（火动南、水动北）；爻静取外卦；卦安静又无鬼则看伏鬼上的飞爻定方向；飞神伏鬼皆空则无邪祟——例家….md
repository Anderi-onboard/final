# 何处得病（方位）：动爻取方（火动南、水动北）；爻静取外卦；卦安静又无鬼则看伏鬼上的飞爻定方向；飞神伏鬼皆空则无邪祟——例家人、讼、遁、小畜

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道灾星从哪里起，要把交（动）、复（变）的方位弄准：爻静就取外卦；没有官鬼，就到本宫去找；要推官鬼伏在哪一爻下面，鬼上面的飞爻才有缘由。比如占得家人卦，鬼伏在三爻辛酉金之下，只看飞神是己亥水，就说西北方冒犯了灵神；飞神、伏鬼都空的，病人终究没有邪祟侵扰。注说：占从哪里得灾、冒犯了哪个方位的祟，不论卦里有鬼没有鬼，只要见动爻，就用动爻来取；火动是南，水动是北；卦如果安静而有官鬼，就取外卦来断，外巽是东南起祸，外乾是西北招殃；卦安静又没有鬼的，不取外卦，看鬼伏在哪一爻之下，由鬼上面的飞爻定方向，比如讼卦安静，鬼伏在三爻午火之下，就说病起南方；又如遁卦三爻、六爻动，有动爻就以动爻为准，动申是西南，戌是西北，病从一处来哪有两个方向，一定要两个并看，就只说西方；又如遁卦安静，卦里虽有鬼却没有动爻，就用外卦来断，祸从西北来；又如小畜卦初爻、二爻动，官鬼虽然没有，动爻可以取，子当正北，寅是东北，两个象一起推，总说在北；又如小畜卦，无官难取外卦，才看鬼伏在三爻之下，祟从西北方来；卦里如果没有鬼，伏鬼又空，就没有神祟。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know from where the star of calamity rose, get the directions of the moving and the returning lines right: if the lines are still, take the outer trigram; if there is no Pressure line, look in the home palace; work out under which line the Pressure line lies hidden, for the flying line above the ghost gives the reason. Suppose Jiaren is obtained, with the ghost hidden under the third line, Xinyou Metal; just look at the flying line, Jihai Water, and say the northwest has offended a spirit; if both the flying line and the hidden ghost are Void, the patient in the end is not invaded by evil influences. The commentary says: to divine from where the calamity was got and in which direction the haunting was offended, whether or not the hexagram has a ghost, if there is a moving line, take it by the moving line; a moving Fire is south and a moving Water is north; if the hexagram is still but has a Pressure line, judge by the outer trigram, so that an outer Xun means misfortune rising in the southeast and an outer Qian means calamity from the northwest; if the hexagram is still and has no ghost, do not take the outer trigram, but see under which line the ghost lies hidden and fix the direction by the flying line above the ghost, so that in a still Song with the ghost hidden under the third line, Wu Fire, one says the illness rose in the south; again in Dun with the third and sixth lines moving, with moving lines go by the moving lines, a moving Shen is southwest and Xu northwest, and since the illness comes from one place and not two directions, if the two must be looked at together, say only west; again in a still Dun, although the hexagram has a ghost there is no moving line, so judge by the outer trigram, and the misfortune comes from the northwest; again in Xiaoxu with the first and second lines moving, although there is no Pressure line the moving lines can be taken, Zi being due north and Yin northeast, and reckoning the two images together one says only north; again in Xiaoxu, when there is no Pressure line and the outer trigram is hard to take, look at the ghost hidden under the third line, and the haunting comes from the northwest; and if the hexagram has no ghost and the hidden ghost is again Void, there is no haunting by a spirit.

> [!note] 按
> 「交复位向」原文如此；「复」疑是「伏」之误（交伏位向：动爻和伏神的方位），因为歌诀后面几句讲的正是动爻取方、伏鬼看飞爻。这里照字面译成「交、复（动与变）」，读法没有把握。三个方位法的先后是：先看动爻（不论有鬼没鬼），没有动爻再看外卦（有官鬼的话），卦安静又没有鬼才看伏鬼上面的飞爻——这和易冒「病从何起」（LY.ILL.YM.29）的次序略有不同：易冒把鬼同动、鬼独发分开，而且六爻俱静才取外卦、卦无官鬼才取伏鬼的飞爻。例里的卦：讼卦鬼伏在三爻午火之下，遁卦动申戌取西方，小畜动子寅取北；家人卦鬼伏辛酉下，飞神己亥水在西北。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiao fu wei xiang' is as in the original; 'fu' (returning) looks like an error for 'fu' (hidden) — the direction of moving and hidden lines — because the following lines of the verse are precisely about taking the direction from the moving line and reading a hidden ghost by the flying line. It is translated literally as 'the moving and the returning (moving and changing) lines', the reading being uncertain. The order of the three methods of finding the direction is: first the moving line (whether or not there is a ghost), if there is no moving line then the outer trigram (if there is a Pressure line), and only if the hexagram is still and has no ghost the flying line above the hidden ghost; this differs slightly from the Yi Mao's 'where the illness rose from' (LY.ILL.YM.29), which separates the ghost moving together with others from the ghost moving alone, and takes the outer trigram only when all six lines are still and the flying line of a hidden ghost only when the hexagram has no Pressure line. The hexagrams in the cases: in Song the ghost lies hidden under the third line, Wu Fire; in Dun a moving Shen and Xu give west; in Xiaoxu a moving Zi and Yin give north; in Jiaren the ghost lies under Xinyou, and the flying line Jihai Water is in the northwest.
