# 行人章·附健否：旺相不入墓爻活、囚死空亡随水沫；卦爻不化入墓中皆平善，反之必死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占行人是否平安的歌里说：行人的爻旺相、没有入墓就活，囚死、落空亡就像水沫一样散去；卦和爻不变化入墓，都平安稳当，反过来一定死，只好去招魂、办殡宫。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on whether a traveller is well says: if the traveller's line is strong and does not enter the tomb it lives; if it is confined, dead or Void it drifts away like foam on water; if the hexagram and lines do not change into a tomb, all is peaceful and secure; the reverse is certain death, and one can only go and call the soul and prepare the mourning hall.

> | 旺相相生行泼泼。 | 旺相又相生，行动就活泼泼的。 | thriving and generating each other, one moves about briskly. |
> | 卦爻不化入墓中， | 卦和爻不变化入墓， | If the hexagram and lines do not change into a tomb, |
> | 皆为平善得安隆。 | 都是平安顺遂，稳当兴隆； | all is peaceful and smooth, secure and prosperous; |
> | 反之必死不须望， | 反过来就一定死，不必指望， | the reverse is certain death, with no hope, |
> | 但去招魂作殡宫。 | 只好去招魂、办殡宫了。 | and one can only go and call the soul and prepare the mourning hall. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一首歌收在占行人一章末尾的「附：健否」，问的是出门在外的人是死是活，和占病的生死是同一类判断：看用爻旺不旺、入没入墓、落没落空。「殡宫」是停放灵柩的地方。这里的行人是一个不在场的人，他的病死判断和本域「他人病」「生死」两类相通。
>
> *Note:* This poem is the 'Appendix: whether he is well' at the end of the chapter on travellers and asks whether a person away from home is alive or dead; it is the same kind of judgement as life and death in an illness: whether the Subject line is strong, whether it enters a tomb, whether it is Void. A 'mourning hall' is where the coffin is kept. The traveller is someone not present, and the judgement of his illness or death is akin to the 'illness of others' and 'life and death' topics in this domain.
