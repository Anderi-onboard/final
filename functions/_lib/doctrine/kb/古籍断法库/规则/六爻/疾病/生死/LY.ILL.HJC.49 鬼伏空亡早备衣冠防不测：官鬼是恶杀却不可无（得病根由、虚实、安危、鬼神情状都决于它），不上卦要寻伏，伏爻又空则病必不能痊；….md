# 鬼伏空亡早备衣冠防不测：官鬼是恶杀却不可无（得病根由、虚实、安危、鬼神情状都决于它），不上卦要寻伏，伏爻又空则病必不能痊；王洪绪说这两句只指占父母、官府、丈夫的病

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼这一爻虽然是恶煞，也不能没有：得病的原因、症候的虚实、疾病的安危、鬼神的情状，都由它来决定。它不上卦，就要去找它伏在哪里才能推测；如果伏着的爻又落空，这病一定好不了：就算有好医生，也找不到病源；就算有鬼神，也没有可以叩告的门路，是天年命尽。病轻的不在此论。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line is a malign killer, yet it cannot be missing: how the illness was got, whether it is deficient or excess, whether it is safe or dangerous, and the state of ghosts and spirits are all decided by it. If it is not in the hexagram one must look for where it lies hidden in order to reason it out; if the hidden line is also Void, the illness surely cannot be cured: even a good doctor cannot reach its source, even the spirits have no door to be petitioned, and the span of life is used up. A light illness is not counted here.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2396 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 此两句唯言父母、官府、丈夫病，如遇官爻伏而又空者，须防不测。 | 这两句只是说父母、官府、丈夫的病，如果遇到官爻伏藏又落空，就要提防不测。 | These two lines speak only of the illness of a parent (the Resource line), an official or a husband: if the Pressure line is hidden and also Void, beware of the unforeseen. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 这两句用在哪些病上：旧注说官鬼是一切病的根由，不能没有，伏而又空则病必不能痊（病轻的除外）；王洪绪说这两句只讲父母、官府、丈夫的病，遇到官爻伏而又空才要提防不测。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which illnesses these two lines apply to: the old commentary says the Pressure line is the root of every illness and cannot be missing, and if it is hidden and also Void the illness surely cannot be cured (a light illness excepted); Wang Hongxu says the two lines speak only of the illness of a parent, an official or a husband, and only when the Pressure line is hidden and Void must one beware of the unforeseen.

> [!note] 按
> 「衣冠」按上下文是入殓的衣帽，「早备衣冠」是叫人早些准备后事。「伏」是官鬼不在卦里，藏在本宫首卦某一爻的下面（见 LY.ILL.HJC.06、HJC.07）。
>
> *Note:* 'Robes and cap' (yiguan) is, from the context, the clothes and cap of the burial, and 'prepare them early' tells one to get affairs ready in good time. 'Hidden' means that the Pressure line is not in the hexagram and lies under a line of the head hexagram of the home palace (see LY.ILL.HJC.06 and HJC.07).
