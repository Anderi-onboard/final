# 家宅章·丧门加白虎，须忧疾病丧亡（旧注：丧门、白虎谓之四利三元，乃凶神，此爻动则病患连绵、丧亡叠至）

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》家宅章说：「丧门星加上白虎星，要担忧疾病和丧亡。」旧注：丧门、白虎叫作「四利三元」，是凶神，见后面凶煞一节；如果见到这一爻发动，就有病患连绵、丧亡接连而来，种种不吉祥，要仔细审察。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the household says: 'The Mourning Gate joined by the White Tiger: beware of illness and death.' The old commentary: the Mourning Gate and the White Tiger are called the 'Four Gains and Three Origins' and are ominous spirits, to be seen below among the ominous killings; if this line is seen moving, there is illness dragging on, deaths following one on another and all kinds of ill omens, to be examined with care.

> [!note] 按
> 「四利三元」这个名目也在断易天机里出现（LY.ILL.DYTJ.50），那里是一份十二位的流年神煞名单，丧门排第三、白虎排第九；旧注这里把丧门、白虎两颗叫作四利三元。这里的白虎是流年神煞里的白虎，不是六神里的白虎；卜筮全书的岁前神煞（LY.ILL.BSQS.51）里也有它（第九位）。经文这一联的前一半「月建会青龙，当主婚姻妊喜」讲喜事，不收。
>
> *Note:* The name 'Four Gains and Three Origins' also appears in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.50), where it heads a list of twelve year-stars with the Mourning Gate third and the White Tiger ninth; the old commentary here calls the Mourning Gate and the White Tiger the Four Gains and Three Origins. The White Tiger here is the White Tiger among the year-stars, not the one among the six spirits; it is also in the stars before the Year in the Bushi Quanshu (LY.ILL.BSQS.51, the ninth place). The first half of this couplet in the text, 'the Month meeting the Azure Dragon: surely marriage and the joy of pregnancy', is about joy and is not taken.
