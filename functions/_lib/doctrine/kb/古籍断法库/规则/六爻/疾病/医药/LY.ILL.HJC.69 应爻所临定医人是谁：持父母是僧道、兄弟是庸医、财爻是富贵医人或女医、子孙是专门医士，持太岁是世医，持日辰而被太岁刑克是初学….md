# 应爻所临定医人是谁：持父母是僧道、兄弟是庸医、财爻是富贵医人或女医、子孙是专门医士，持太岁是世医，持日辰而被太岁刑克是初学，持月建是官医，更临子孙用药神效

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻是医生，看它带的是什么，就知道医生是哪一类人：持父母，一定是僧道人家，或者兼会别的技艺；持兄弟，是庸医，或者先要药钱；持财爻，是富贵人家的医生，或者是女医生；临子孙，是专门行医的人，或者是僧道；持太岁，一定是世代行医的；持日辰而被太岁刑克的，是刚学医的；持月建，是官医，再临子孙，用药有神效。医生年老年少，用生旺墓绝来推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other line is the doctor, and what it carries shows what sort of man he is: holding Resource, a monk or Daoist household, or one who also has other arts; holding Peer, a quack, or one who asks the price of the medicine first; holding Wealth, a doctor of wealth and rank, or a woman doctor; sitting on Output, a specialist physician, or a monk or Daoist; holding Tai Sui, a doctor from a family of doctors; holding the Day but punished or controlled by Tai Sui, a beginner in medicine; holding the Month, an official physician, and if it also sits on Output, his medicine works wonders. Whether the doctor is old or young is reasoned from birth, strength, tomb and extinction.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2440 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应临子孙乃专门医士，可托之。 | 应爻临子孙，是专门行医的人，可以托付。 | The Other line sitting on Output: a specialist physician, one who can be trusted. |

^q4

> [!warning] 打架
> 持日辰的医生是哪一种：旧注说持日辰而被太岁刑克的是刚学医的，持月建的是官医；王洪绪说持月建、日辰的一律是官医，没有「刚学医」这一档。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What sort of doctor one who holds the Day is: the old commentary says one who holds the Day and is punished or controlled by Tai Sui is a beginner, and one who holds the Month is an official physician; Wang Hongxu says those who hold the Month or the Day are all official physicians and has no class of 'beginner'.

> [!note] 按
> 「官医」是官方的医官，「世医」是世代行医的人家，「庸医」是平庸的医生。旧注这一条挂在经文「应临月建，必是官医」下面，把应爻临各种爻的医生类型一次列全；王洪绪把「应临子孙」单列一句。
>
> *Note:* An 'official physician' is a physician holding office, a 'family doctor' (shiyi) one from a family that has practised medicine for generations, and a 'quack' (yongyi) a mediocre doctor. The old commentary hangs this entry under the verse 'the Other line sitting on the Month: surely an official physician' and lists all the types of doctor for what the Other line carries at once; Wang Hongxu sets 'the Other line sitting on Output' apart as its own sentence.
