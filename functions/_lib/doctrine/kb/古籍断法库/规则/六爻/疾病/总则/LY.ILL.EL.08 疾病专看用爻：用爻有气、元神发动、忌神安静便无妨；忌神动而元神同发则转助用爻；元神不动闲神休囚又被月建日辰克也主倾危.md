# 疾病专看用爻：用爻有气、元神发动、忌神安静便无妨；忌神动而元神同发则转助用爻；元神不动闲神休囚又被月建日辰克也主倾危

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以用神为主，元神为凭。占疾病，必须求用象兴旺；元神旺相又发动，一定亨通；忌神千万不要在发动的位置上；日、月到来，要配合它的克与生；动的要看变爻才知道去留；没有（用象）就找伏象，才知道亏盈。注说：凡是占疾病，专看用爻，卦里如果用爻有气、元神发动、忌神安静，就没有妨碍；忌神怕动，但如果元神同时发动，转过来帮助用爻，病反而安稳；卦里元神不动，闲神又休囚，再遇上月建或日辰来克，也主倾危。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject is the main line, and the Support is the ground. In illness one must seek a thriving Subject image; the Support strong and moving surely means success; the Adversary must on no account be in a moving position; when the Day and Month arrive, match them with their control and generation; for a moving line look at the changed line to know whether it goes or stays; if there is none, search for a hidden image to sense its waning and waxing. The commentary says: in divining illness look only at the Subject line; if in the hexagram the Subject line has qi, the Support moves and the Adversary is still, there is no obstruction; the Adversary should not move, but if the Support moves with it, it turns to help the Subject line and the illness on the contrary settles; if the Support in the hexagram does not move, the idle spirits are resting or trapped, and the Month or Day also controls it, danger of collapse is indicated.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段没有章题；小标「以用神为主，元神为凭」说的是整章的取法：占病主看用神，凭据是元神。「用象」「用爻」「主象」在这一段里是用神的别称。「闲神」是与用神既不相生也不相克的爻。「无寻伏象」的「无」是没有用象时才去找伏象。与黄金策「主衰得助重亦何妨」（LY.ILL.HJC.48）、增删卜易「用绝逢生危而有救」（LY.ILL.ZS.13）是同一路读法。
>
> *Note:* This passage has no chapter title; the subtitle 'the Subject is the main line, the Support the ground' gives the method for the whole: in divining illness the Subject is the chief, and the Support is what one relies on. 'Subject image', 'Subject line' and 'chief image' are other names for the Subject in this passage. 'Idle spirits' are lines that neither generate nor control the Subject. 'If there is none, search for a hidden image' means the hidden image is looked for only when there is no Subject image. It reads in the same way as the Huangjin Ce's 'the chief weak but helped, however heavy what matter' (LY.ILL.HJC.48) and the Zengshan Buyi's 'the Subject in Extinction meeting generation, danger yet rescue' (LY.ILL.ZS.13).
