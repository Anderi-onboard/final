# 八卦断例·占病宜不宜：履「弗占疾病」、大壮「休占讼病」、蒙「却宜占病」；恒「病须疾治」

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 周易尚占八卦断例里有四个卦对占病给的是劝告：履卦说不要占疾病，大壮卦说不要占官司和病，蒙卦说不要营谋、倒适合占病，恒卦说病要赶快治。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the judgments by example of the Zhouyi Shangzhan four hexagrams give advice about divining illness rather than a verdict: Lü (Treading) says do not divine illness, Dazhuang says do not divine lawsuits or illness, Meng says one cannot yet scheme but it suits divining illness, and Heng says illness must be treated quickly.

> [!warning] 打架
> 蒙卦这里说「却宜占病」，卜筮全书疾病论（LY.ILL.BSQS.35）却把蒙列进春天的十死卦。「宜占病」说的是适合拿这一卦来问病，没有说病会好，所以两处不一定正面冲突，合起来看蒙卦占病到底吉凶，书里没有定论。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Of Meng the line here says 'but it suits divining illness', whereas the Bushi Quanshu's essay on illness (LY.ILL.BSQS.35) puts Meng among the ten fatal hexagrams of spring. 'Suits divining illness' says that this hexagram is a fit one with which to ask about illness, and does not say the illness will mend, so the two places need not clash head on; taken together, the books do not settle whether Meng is auspicious or ominous for illness.

> [!note] 按
> 「弗占疾病」「休占讼病」是劝人不要占这件事，书里没有说是因为病凶，还是因为这一卦本身不适合问这类事；履卦同一行里还有「危中有救，险处无妨」。「却宜占病」的「却」是转折：上一个词组说「未可营谋」，这一个说倒适合占病。恒卦「病须疾治」里的「疾」是迅速、赶快，不是疾病。
>
> *Note:* 'Do not divine illness' and 'do not divine lawsuits or illness' advise against divining this matter, and the book does not say whether this is because illness is ominous or because the hexagram itself is not a fit one for such questions; the same line of Lü (Treading) also has 'in danger there is rescue, in a perilous place there is no harm'. The 'but' of 'but it suits divining illness' is a turn: the previous phrase says 'one cannot yet scheme', and this says that it nevertheless suits divining illness. The 'quickly' of 'illness must be treated quickly' in Heng is the word for swift, not the word for illness.
