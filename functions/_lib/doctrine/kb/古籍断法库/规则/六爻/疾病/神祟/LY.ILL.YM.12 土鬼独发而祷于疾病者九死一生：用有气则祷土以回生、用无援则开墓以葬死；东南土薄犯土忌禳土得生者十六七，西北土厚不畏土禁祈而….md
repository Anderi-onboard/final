# 土鬼独发而祷于疾病者九死一生：用有气则祷土以回生、用无援则开墓以葬死；东南土薄犯土忌禳土得生者十六七，西北土厚不畏土禁祈而无功

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 常常试到：土鬼独自发动、又为疾病祈祷的，九死一生。有人说，用爻有气，祷告土神就能回生；用爻没有援助，就是开墓来葬死人，这话确实可信。注引《天元赋》说：土和煞一起发动，一定成为黄泉下的客；《补遗》说：夏天论，土爻带鬼发动，凶多吉少，祸难禳解。大体上东南土地薄，犯了土的忌讳、禳土而得生的，十个有六七个；西北土地厚，不怕土的禁忌，所以祈祷也没有功效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* I have often tried it: where an Earth ghost moves alone and one prays over an illness, nine die and one lives. Some say that if the Subject line has force, praying to the earth spirit restores life, and if it has no help it is opening a tomb to bury the dead; this is truly credible. The note quotes the Tianyuan Fu: when Earth and killing stars both rise, he is sure to become a guest of the Yellow Springs; and the Buyi: in summer, an Earth line carrying a moving ghost is mostly ominous and seldom auspicious, and the misfortune is hard to exorcise. In general the earth of the southeast is thin, and of those who have offended earth taboos and exorcised the earth, six or seven in ten live; the earth of the northwest is thick and does not fear earth prohibitions, so prayer there has no effect.

> [!note] 按
> 「天元赋」不知是哪部书，「补遗」按上下文是《易林补遗》，书里没有说明。「土禁」「土忌」是动土等犯土神的忌讳。这一条把「土鬼独发要祷」讲成地域的差别：东南土薄、犯土忌的多，禳土有效；西北土厚，祈祷无功。
>
> *Note:* The 'Tianyuan Fu' is not known as a work, and the 'Buyi' is by context the Yilin Buyi, which the book does not say. 'Earth prohibitions' and 'earth taboos' are the taboos of offending the earth spirit, by breaking ground and the like. This entry turns 'an Earth ghost moving alone calls for prayer' into a matter of region: in the thin-earthed southeast, where earth taboos are often broken, exorcising the earth works; in the thick-earthed northwest, prayer is without effect.
