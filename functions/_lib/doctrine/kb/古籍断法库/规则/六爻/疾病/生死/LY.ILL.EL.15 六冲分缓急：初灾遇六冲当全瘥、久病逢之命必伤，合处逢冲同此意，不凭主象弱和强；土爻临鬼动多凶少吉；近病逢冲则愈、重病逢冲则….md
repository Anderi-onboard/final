# 六冲分缓急：初灾遇六冲当全瘥、久病逢之命必伤，合处逢冲同此意，不凭主象弱和强；土爻临鬼动多凶少吉；近病逢冲则愈、重病逢冲则死；占尊长鬼为元神动则祈让可疗

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 只有六冲这一种，能分缓急，病的来源要自己斟酌衡量：刚起的灾遇上它应当全好，久病遇上它命必伤；合处逢冲也是这个意思，不看主象是弱还是强；再看土爻临鬼发动，凶多吉少，灾祸难以禳解。注说：病得六冲、六冲变冲、合处逢冲，都要审察病是远是近、是轻是重：暴病来占，朝夕之间就应当痊愈；如果久病来占，用象虽然旺相，也主身亡。又说近病逢冲就好，重病逢冲就死；卦纵然不冲，用爻也旺，凡是遇上财爻、鬼爻发动的，都可以断死；只有占尊长，鬼是元神，发动了就祈祷禳解可以治疗。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Only the Six-Clash tells slow from urgent, and the source of the illness must be weighed by oneself: a fresh calamity meeting it should be wholly cured, a long illness meeting it surely brings harm to life; a clash met in a combination has the same meaning, without regard to whether the chief image is weak or strong; and further, an Earth line on a moving ghost is mostly ominous and seldom auspicious, and the misfortune is hard to exorcise. The commentary says: when an illness has a six-clash, or a six-clash changing into a clash, or a clash met in a combination, one must examine whether it is near or far, light or heavy: a sudden illness divined should be cured by morning or evening; if a long illness is divined, though the Subject image is strong it still means death. It says also that a recent illness meeting a clash is cured and a heavy illness meeting a clash dies; even if the hexagram is not clashing and the Subject line is strong, wherever the Wealth or Pressure line moves one may say death; only in divining an elder, the ghost being the Support, moving it can be treated by prayer and exorcism.

> [!note] 按
> 原文「初灾遇此当全瘥」中的「瘥」是病愈；歌诀最后一句「爻凶少吉祸难禳」里的「爻凶」疑是「多凶」之误（注解处写作「多凶少吉」），这里按上下文译作「凶多吉少」。「六冲、六冲变冲、合处逢冲」是三种冲：卦是六冲卦、六冲卦变六冲卦、六合卦（或合爻）遇冲。这一条与增删卜易「六冲变冲久病难于调治」（LY.ILL.ZS.02）、「卦变绝克新疾亦主危亡」（LY.ILL.ZS.03）、易冒「卦属反伏墓绝亦不治；病未久三冲而愈、病已久三冲而亡」（LY.ILL.YM.10）、易隐（LY.ILL.YY.07）是同一件事，各家对久病遇冲的结论一致：久病逢冲必死；对近病遇冲则略有出入（此处近病逢冲则愈）。
>
> *Note:* 'Cured' (jiu) in 'a fresh calamity meeting it should be wholly cured' is recovery from illness; the 'yao xiong' (line ominous) in the last line of the verse looks like an error for 'duo xiong' (mostly ominous), which the commentary writes as 'mostly ominous and seldom auspicious', and it is translated so from the context. 'A six-clash, a six-clash changing into a clash, a clash met in a combination' are three kinds of clash: the hexagram being a Six-Clash hexagram, a Six-Clash hexagram changing into another, and a Six-Combine hexagram (or a combining line) meeting a clash. It is the same matter as the Zengshan Buyi's 'a Six-Clash changing into a clash makes a long illness hard to treat' (LY.ILL.ZS.02) and 'a change into Extinction or control also means danger in a fresh illness' (LY.ILL.ZS.03), the Yi Mao's 'a hexagram that is Reversed, Hidden, in a tomb or in Extinction is untreatable; a recent illness is cured by the three clashes, a long one dies' (LY.ILL.YM.10) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.07); the books agree that a long illness meeting a clash surely dies, and differ slightly on a recent illness meeting a clash (here it is cured).
