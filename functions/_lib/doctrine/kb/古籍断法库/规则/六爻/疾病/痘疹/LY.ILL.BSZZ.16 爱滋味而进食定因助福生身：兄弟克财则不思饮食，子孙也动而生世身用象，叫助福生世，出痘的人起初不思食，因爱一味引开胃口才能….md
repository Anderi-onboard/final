# 爱滋味而进食定因助福生身：兄弟克财则不思饮食，子孙也动而生世身用象，叫助福生世，出痘的人起初不思食，因爱一味引开胃口才能进食

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 爱某种味道才肯吃东西，一定是因为帮了福神、生了世身：兄弟爻本来克财，一发动就不想吃东西；如果子孙也发动，动来生世爻、卦身、用神的象，叫做助福生世，主出痘的人起初不想吃，因为爱吃某一种味道，把胃口引开了，才吃得下东西。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Eating because one loves a certain flavour is surely because it helps Output and generates the Self and the Body: Peer by nature controls Wealth, and when it moves the patient has no wish to eat; if Output also moves and moves to generate the Self, the Body or the image of the Subject, this is called helping Output to generate the Self, and it means the person with the pox at first has no wish to eat, but because he loves one flavour his appetite is drawn open and only then can he take food.
