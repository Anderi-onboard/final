# 父母不宜持世、鬼杀岂可临身：卦身与世爻都不宜临官鬼、父母二爻，遇之药便不中；父母持世可许经醮保禳，鬼爻持世可祷鬼神护佑，然后服药才验

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦身和世爻都不宜临在官鬼、父母两爻上，遇到了，药就不中用。父母爻持世，可以许下经醮保禳的愿；鬼爻持世，可以祷告鬼神护佑，然后吃药才有效验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Neither the hexagram Body nor the Self line should sit on the Pressure or Resource lines; if they do, the medicine does not hit the mark. With Resource holding the Self line one may vow scriptures, offerings and protective rites; with Pressure holding the Self line one may pray to the ghosts and spirits for protection, and only then will the medicine be effective.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2428 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 卦身与世爻皆不宜临官临父，遇之则药不效。 | 卦身和世爻都不宜临官爻、临父母爻，遇到就药不见效。 | The hexagram Body and the Self line should neither of them sit on Pressure or on Resource; if they do, the medicine has no effect. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注多讲了两种补救办法（父母持世许经醮保禳，鬼爻持世祷鬼神），王洪绪只收了前一句。「经醮保禳」是念经、设醮、祈禳这一类祈求平安的仪式。「身」指哪一爻，见 LY.ILL.HJC.35。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary adds two remedies (with Resource holding the Self line vow scriptures, offerings and protective rites; with Pressure holding it pray to the spirits), of which Wang Hongxu takes only the first sentence. 'Scriptures, offerings and protective rites' are the rites of chanting, holding a jiao offering and averting misfortune that ask for safety. For which line the 'body' is, see LY.ILL.HJC.35.
