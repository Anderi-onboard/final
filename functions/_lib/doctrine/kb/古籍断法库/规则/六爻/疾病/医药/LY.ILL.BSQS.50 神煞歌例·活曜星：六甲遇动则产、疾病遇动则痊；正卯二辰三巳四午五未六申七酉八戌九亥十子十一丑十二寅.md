# 神煞歌例·活曜星：六甲遇动则产、疾病遇动则痊；正卯二辰三巳四午五未六申七酉八戌九亥十子十一丑十二寅

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 活曜星：怀孕遇它发动就生产，疾病遇它发动就痊愈；位置按月顺行：正月卯、二月辰、三月巳、四月午、五月未、六月申、七月酉、八月戌、九月亥、十月子、十一月丑、十二月寅。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Living Star: a pregnancy, when it moves, gives birth; an illness, when it moves, is cured; its place goes forward month by month: Mao in the first month, Chen in the second, Si in the third, Wu in the fourth, Wei in the fifth, Shen in the sixth, You in the seventh, Xu in the eighth, Hai in the ninth, Zi in the tenth, Chou in the eleventh and Yin in the twelfth.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 活曜星是一颗吉星，位置从正月的卯起，每月顺行一位。「六甲」是怀孕（「身怀六甲」），「遇动」是这颗星所在的爻发动。这一颗星断易天机里没有。「则痊」的痊愈只是结论，歌里没有说病轻病重。
>
> *Note:* The Living Star is an auspicious star, placed from Mao in the first month and going forward one place each month. 'The Six Jia' is pregnancy ('carrying the six jia'), and 'when it moves' is the line where this star stands moving. This star is not in the Duanyi Tianji. 'Is cured' is only the conclusion, and the verse does not say whether the illness is light or grave.
