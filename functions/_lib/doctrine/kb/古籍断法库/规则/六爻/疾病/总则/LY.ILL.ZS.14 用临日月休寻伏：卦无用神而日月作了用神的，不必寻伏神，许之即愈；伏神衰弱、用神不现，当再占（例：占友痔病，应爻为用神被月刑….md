# 用临日月休寻伏：卦无用神而日月作了用神的，不必寻伏神，许之即愈；伏神衰弱、用神不现，当再占（例：占友痔病，应爻为用神被月刑日冲，令亲人再占）

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有用神，而日、月作了用神的，不必去找伏神，断他马上就好；伏神衰弱的，应当再占一次。凡是用神不出现，都应当再占。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the hexagram has no Subject but the Day or Month acts as the Subject, one need not look for the hidden line; promise recovery at once. If the hidden line is weak, one should divine again. Whenever the Subject does not appear, one should divine again.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 12803 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 前卦应临巳火，月刑日克，此卦又是巳父伏于寅木之下，破木不生无焰之火，应十月必危。 | 前一卦应爻临着巳火，被月刑、日克，这一卦又是巳火父母伏在寅木下面，破了的木生不了没有火焰的火，应验在十月，一定危险。 | In the former hexagram the Other line sits on Si Fire, punished by the Month and controlled by the Day; in this hexagram Si Resource is hidden under Yin Wood, and broken Wood cannot generate Fire that has no flame, so the tenth month will surely be dangerous. |
> | 果卒于亥月，以亥水冲破无焰之巳火也。 | 果然死在亥月，是因为亥水冲破了没有火焰的巳火。 | He did die in the Hai month, because Hai Water clashed and broke the flameless Si Fire. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 这一条讲的是再占：用神不现、只有伏神而且衰弱时，不要硬断，叫问卦的人再占一次。书中例里朋友的痔病，应爻是用神，被月刑日冲，野鹤不敢断，叫亲人再占；第二卦（儿子占父病）的巳火父母又伏在寅木下面，他才断出亥月必危。后面的 LY.ILL.ZS.15 把这种多占的做法专门讲了一遍。疾病章里伏神的另一面见 LY.ILL.HJC.49。
>
> *Note:* This entry is about divining again: when the Subject does not appear and there is only a hidden line, and that one is weak, do not press a judgment but have the asker divine again. In the case of the friend's piles the Other line is the Subject, punished by the Month and clashed by the Day, and the Old Man did not dare to judge but had the relative divine again; in the second hexagram (a son divining his father's illness) Si Resource is again hidden under Yin Wood, and only then did he judge that the Hai month would be fatal. The next entry LY.ILL.ZS.15 speaks of this practice of repeated divination on its own. For the other side of hidden lines in the illness chapter see LY.ILL.HJC.49.
