# 卦若无鬼病难医症候难决：以克杀动者断、不动以克用爻者断、又以伏鬼断；鬼伏父母下忧心得病或穿衣过与不及（冬言衣单夏言衣重）、伏兄弟下赌博争财、伏子孙下外情牵惹、伏财下饮食太过、伏杀下旧病再发难除根；老人卦忌生旺、小儿卦忌休囚；最宜病人本命上卦；阴鬼夜重日轻、阳鬼夜轻日重

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里如果没有鬼，病就难医，症候也难决断。只能用克的杀发动的来断；不动的，就用克用爻的来断，再用伏着的鬼来断。如果鬼伏在父母爻下：忧心得的病，或者穿衣服得的病，穿得过多或不足，冬天就说衣服单薄，夏天就说衣服厚重。鬼伏在兄弟下：赌博争财；伏在子孙下：被外面的情事牵惹；伏在财下：饮食太过；伏在杀下：旧病再发，也难除根。老人的卦忌旺相，小孩的卦忌休囚。最好是病人的本命上卦，大吉。遇到阴鬼，夜里重白天轻；阳鬼，夜里轻白天重；阴阳各自依爻上来看。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If there is no ghost in the hexagram, the illness is hard to treat and the symptoms hard to decide. One can only judge by the killing that controls and moves; if it does not move, judge by what controls the Subject line, and also judge by the hidden ghost. If the ghost is hidden under Resource: illness got from a worried heart, or from clothing, too much or too little: in winter say the clothes were thin, in summer say the clothes were heavy. The ghost hidden under Peer: gambling and quarrels over money; hidden under Output: entangled by an affair outside; hidden under Wealth: excess in food and drink; hidden under a killing: an old illness flares again, and it is also hard to root out. An old person's hexagram fears being strong, a child's hexagram fears being resting or trapped. Best is for the sick person's natal line to be in the hexagram, which is greatly auspicious. A yin ghost is heavy at night and light by day; a yang ghost is light at night and heavy by day; yin and yang are each read from the line.

> [!warning] 打架
> 病在昼夜哪一头重，几家的依据不同。天玄赋注（LY.ILL.TXF.10）是「阴鬼夜重日轻、阳鬼夜轻日重」，和易隐（LY.ILL.YY.15）一致，都按官鬼的阴阳分；海底眼（LY.ILL.HDY.02）、黄金策的旧注和王洪绪（LY.ILL.HJC.12）则按官鬼在内卦还是外卦分（内卦夜重、外卦白天重）。两个标准可以给出相反的结论，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether an illness is heavier by day or by night: the books use different criteria. The commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.10) has 'a yin ghost is heavy at night and light by day, a yang ghost light at night and heavy by day', which agrees with the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.15) in dividing by the yin or yang of the Pressure line; the Haidi Yan (LY.ILL.HDY.02), the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu (LY.ILL.HJC.12) divide instead by whether the Pressure line is in the inner or outer trigram (inner trigram heavy at night, outer trigram heavy by day). The two criteria can give opposite conclusions, and the books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是「卦无鬼」时怎么办：没有官鬼，病难医、症候难决，就退而求其次——用「克杀动者」（克用爻的煞在动）断；它不动，用「克用爻者」断；再用「伏鬼」断，也就是官鬼伏在哪一爻下。伏鬼的六亲断法和海底眼（LY.ILL.HDY.04）、易冒的六亲（LY.ILL.YM.60 一带）是同一个路子：父母下是忧虑或穿衣、兄弟下是赌博争财、子孙下是外情牵惹、财下是饮食太过、杀下是旧病再发。「穿衣服得病，过与不及，冬言衣单，夏言衣重」是说冬天穿得少，夏天穿得多——按季节说得病是穿衣不当。「老人卦忌生旺，小儿卦忌休囚」和易隐（LY.ILL.YY.10）「少年卦忌死囚休废、老人卦忌旺相」同一句。「最宜病人本命上卦」的「本命」是病人的生年属相所在的爻（本命爻）。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* This section is about what to do when the hexagram has no ghost: with no Pressure line the illness is hard to treat and the symptoms hard to decide, and one falls back on the next best — judging by 'the killing that controls and moves' (the killing that controls the Subject line moving); if it does not move, judging by 'what controls the Subject line'; and further judging by the hidden ghost, that is, under which line the Pressure line hides. The six-relation reading of a hidden ghost is on the same road as the Haidi Yan (LY.ILL.HDY.04) and the Yi Mao's six relations (LY.ILL.YM.60 and around): under Resource, worry or clothing; under Peer, gambling and quarrels over money; under Output, entangled by an affair outside; under Wealth, excess in food and drink; under a killing, an old illness flares again. 'Illness from clothing, too much or too little; in winter say the clothes were thin, in summer say the clothes were heavy' says that a person who wore too little in winter or too much in summer fell ill from wearing the wrong clothing for the season. 'An old person's hexagram fears being strong, a child's hexagram fears being resting or trapped' is the same sentence as the Yi Yin's (LY.ILL.YY.10, 'a young person's hexagram fears Death, trapped, resting and abandoned, an old person's hexagram fears being strong'). The 'natal' of 'best is for the sick person's natal line to be in the hexagram' is the line where the sick person's birth animal falls (the natal line). After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
