# 避暑养病往何处：父母生世宜投书馆尊长、子孙生世利僧道卑幼、兄弟生世往弟兄朋友、妻财生世往妻族仆隶、官鬼生世宦宅；一卦皆安方是修行之路、六爻尽破岂为养静之窝；用爻旺遇青龙宜往、忌象兴加白虎莫往；随寓而安、六冲往返不定

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻扶身，宜去书馆；福神生世，利于去僧堂。注说：父母爻生世，宜去文墨的地方和长辈的家；子孙生世，利于住在僧道的门下和晚辈小孩的地方；兄弟生世，宜去兄弟朋友的家；妻财生世，应去妻族亲友、仆隶下人的住处；官鬼生世，偏宜在官宦人家安身。一卦都安，才是修行的路；六爻都破，怎么是养静的窝？用爻旺的遇上青龙，宜去这个地方；忌象兴旺又加白虎，就不要去那一家。注说：各爻都占得随住处而安；一卦六冲，往来不定；用爻有气，又值青龙，利于有所往；忌象交重，又临白虎，这个地方不要去。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line supporting the Self suits going to a schoolroom, and the fortune spirit generating the Self favours the monks' hall. The commentary says: the Resource line generating the Self suits places of letters and the homes of elders; Output generating the Self favours living with monks and Daoists and among the young; Peer generating the Self suits going to the homes of brothers and friends; Wealth generating the Self, go to the dwellings of the wife's kin, friends, servants and underlings; the Pressure line generating the Self suits above all lodging in an official's house. When the whole hexagram is at peace, that is the road of cultivation; when all six lines are broken, how could it be a nest for quiet nurture? When the Subject line is strong and meets the Azure Dragon, it suits going to this place; when the adversary image thrives and adds the White Tiger, do not go to that house. The commentary says: if all lines are divined, one is at peace wherever one lodges; a hexagram of six clashes means going and returning are unsettled; if the Subject line has qi and also holds the Azure Dragon, it is favourable to go; if the adversary image moves and sits on the White Tiger, do not go to this place.

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 原文「诸爻俱卜随寓而安」的「卜」疑是「安」之误（诸爻俱安），这里按字面译作「各爻都占得」，读法没有把握。这一条把去哪里养病、避暑用六亲生世来选：生世的六亲是什么，就去那一类人家。第一句的六亲和第二句（LY.ILL.EL.23）是同一件事的一体两面：前面看世爻和官鬼动不动，这里看谁生世。「六冲，往返不定」与 LY.ILL.EL.15 的六冲是两码事：这里是住处不定，不是病的缓急。
>
> *Note:* The 'bu' (divined) in 'if all lines are divined, one is at peace wherever one lodges' looks like an error for 'an' (all lines at peace), and is translated literally, the reading being uncertain. This entry chooses where to convalesce or escape the heat by which of the six relations generates the Self: whichever relation generates it, go to that kind of household. It is the other face of the previous entry (LY.ILL.EL.23): that looked at the Self line and whether the Pressure line moves, and this looks at what generates the Self. 'A hexagram of six clashes means going and returning are unsettled' is a different matter from the Six-Clash of LY.ILL.EL.15: here it is that the lodging is unsettled, not that the illness is fast or slow.
