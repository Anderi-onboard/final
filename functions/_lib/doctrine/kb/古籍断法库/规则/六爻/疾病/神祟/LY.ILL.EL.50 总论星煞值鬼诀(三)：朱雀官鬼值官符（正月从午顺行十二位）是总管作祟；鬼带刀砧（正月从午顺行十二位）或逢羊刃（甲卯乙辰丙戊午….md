# 总论星煞值鬼诀(三)：朱雀官鬼值官符（正月从午顺行十二位）是总管作祟；鬼带刀砧（正月从午顺行十二位）或逢羊刃（甲卯乙辰丙戊午丁己未庚酉辛戌壬子癸丑）是伤司；鬼值太岁带黄旌（正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑循环）是后土（土皇）；官临贵人（甲戊庚牛羊、乙己鼠猴、丙丁猪鸡、壬癸蛇兔、六辛马虎）或天喜是萧堂五圣

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 朱雀上的官鬼又值着官符，就说是总管在作祟（官符：正月从午上起，顺行十二位）。鬼带刀砧，或遇上羊刃，就是伤司（刀砧煞：正月从午上起，顺行十二位；羊刃煞按日干：甲日在卯、乙日在辰、丙戊日在午、丁己日在未、庚日在酉、辛日在戌、壬日在子、癸日在丑）。太岁就是当年；鬼值太岁若带着黄旌，就是后土，后土就是土皇（黄旌星：正月戌、二月未、三月辰、四月丑，只在这四位上轮）。官鬼临着贵人或者天喜，就是萧堂五圣（天乙贵人：甲、戊、庚日在牛羊〔丑未〕，乙、己日在鼠猴〔子申〕，丙、丁日在猪鸡〔亥酉〕，壬、癸日在蛇兔〔巳卯〕，辛日遇马虎〔午寅〕；天喜的排法见前）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line on the Vermilion Bird that also meets the Official Talisman is said to be the Chief Official haunting (Official Talisman: the first month starts from Wu and runs forward through twelve places). A ghost carrying the Chopping Block, or meeting the Goat Blade, is the Injuring Office (Chopping Block killing: the first month starts from Wu and runs forward through twelve places; Goat Blade killing by day stem: a Jia day at Mao, a Yi day at Chen, a Bing or Wu day at Wu, a Ding or Ji day at Wei, a Geng day at You, a Xin day at Xu, a Ren day at Zi, a Gui day at Chou). Tai Sui is the year itself; a ghost on Tai Sui, if it carries the Yellow Banner, is then the Sovereign Earth, and the Sovereign Earth is the Earth Emperor (Yellow Banner star: the first month Xu, the second Wei, the third Chen, the fourth Chou, turning only through these four). A Pressure line on the Noble or on Heavenly Joy is the Five Sages of the Xiao Hall (Tianyi Noble: Jia, Wu and Geng days at the ox and sheep [Chou, Wei], Yi and Ji days at the rat and monkey [Zi, Shen], Bing and Ding days at the pig and rooster [Hai, You], Ren and Gui days at the snake and hare [Si, Mao], and Xin days meeting the horse and tiger [Wu, Yin]; Heavenly Joy is set as before).

> [!warning] 打架
> 天乙贵人和羊刃怎么定，各书不同。①天乙贵人：易林补遗（LY.ILL.EL.50）是「甲戊庚牛羊，乙己鼠猴乡，丙丁猪鸡位，壬癸蛇兔藏，六辛逢马虎」，庚日的贵人在牛羊（丑未）；卜筮大全的神煞篇是「甲戊兼牛羊，乙己鼠猴乡，丙丁猪鸡位，壬癸兔蛇藏，庚辛逢马虎」，庚日和辛日一样在马虎（午寅）；周易尚占的逐日表里庚日的贵人也是午。②羊刃：易林补遗是每个日干都取「禄前一位」（甲卯、乙辰、丙戊午、丁己未、庚酉、辛戌、壬子、癸丑）；易冒讲诸星一章的注明说阳干顺行、阴干逆行（甲禄寅取卯、乙禄卯取寅、丙戊禄巳取午、丁己禄午取巳，「乙逢寅动当为刃，辛见申摇岂不凶，丁己忌巳癸忧亥」），并批评「近世不分阴阳顺逆，概以禄前一位为刃，至有误认乙禄到卯、辰为羊刃之说」——易林补遗的表正是这种一律取禄前一位的排法（乙日在辰），易冒没有点名。两边差在乙、丁、己、辛、癸五个阴干上，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How the Tianyi Noble and the Goat Blade are fixed differs from book to book. (1) Tianyi Noble: the Yilin Buyi (LY.ILL.EL.50) has 'Jia, Wu and Geng the ox and sheep; Yi and Ji the land of the rat and monkey; Bing and Ding the places of the pig and rooster; Ren and Gui the snake and hare lie hidden; the six Xin meet the horse and tiger', so that the Noble of a Geng day is at the ox and sheep (Chou, Wei); the chapter on killings in the Buyi Daquan has 'Jia and Wu together the ox and sheep; Yi and Ji the land of the rat and monkey; Bing and Ding the places of the pig and rooster; Ren and Gui the hare and snake lie hidden; Geng and Xin meet the horse and tiger', so that a Geng day is, like a Xin day, at the horse and tiger (Wu, Yin); in the table by day in the Zhouyi Shangzhan the Noble of a Geng day is also Wu. (2) Goat Blade: the Yilin Buyi takes, for every day stem, 'the place one step ahead of the Salary' (Jia Mao, Yi Chen, Bing and Wu Wu, Ding and Ji Wei, Geng You, Xin Xu, Ren Zi, Gui Chou); the note of the Yi Mao in its chapter on the stars says plainly that yang stems go forward and yin stems go backward (Jia with its Salary at Yin takes Mao, Yi with its Salary at Mao takes Yin, Bing and Wu with the Salary at Si take Wu, Ding and Ji with the Salary at Wu take Si, 'Yi meeting Yin moving is surely the blade, Xin seeing Shen shake is not ominous, Ding and Ji fear Si and Gui worries about Hai'), and it criticises that 'in recent times people do not tell yang from yin, forward from backward, and take in every case the place one step ahead of the Salary as the blade, even to the mistaken saying that Yi with its Salary at Mao has Chen as the Goat Blade': the Yilin Buyi's table is exactly this way of taking the place one step ahead of the Salary in every case (Yi on Chen), though the Yi Mao does not name it. The two differ on the five yin stems Yi, Ding, Ji, Xin and Gui, and the books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条讲朱雀、白虎（伤司）、太岁、贵人配的星煞。官符和刀砧煞的排法完全一样：正月从午起，顺行十二个月，一月一位；羊刃煞不按月、按日干排，一个日干一个位置。「太岁者，即年如也」的「如」文字费解，按「太岁就是当年」译。天乙贵人那首口诀里的牛羊是丑未、鼠猴是子申、猪鸡是亥酉、蛇兔是巳卯、马虎是午寅，译文里一并写出地支。「六辛」按字面译作「the six Xin days」（辛日）。「后土者，土皇也」——后土（Sovereign Earth）就是土皇（Earth Emperor），和前面的勾陈、土鬼里的「土皇」（LY.ILL.EL.34、EL.47）是同一个称呼。「萧堂五圣」按字面译作 the Five Sages of the Xiao Hall，和前面的「萧公即五圣」（LY.ILL.EL.46）呼应。这里「贵人」的「贵人」不是天将，是天乙贵人星。 书里没有说这里的「正月、二月」是起卦的月还是别的月，这里照抄；「只此四位周而复始」是说五月回到正月的位置、九月也回到正月的位置，依此类推。 这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This entry is about the stars and killings matched with the Vermilion Bird, the White Tiger (the Injuring Office), Tai Sui and the Noble. The Official Talisman and the Chopping Block killing are set in exactly the same way: from Wu in the first month, running forward through the twelve months, one place a month; the Goat Blade killing is set not by month but by day stem, one position for each day stem. The 'ru' in 'Tai Sui is the year itself' is hard to understand, and is translated as 'Tai Sui is the year itself'. In the verse of the Tianyi Noble the ox and sheep are Chou and Wei, the rat and monkey Zi and Shen, the pig and rooster Hai and You, the snake and hare Si and Mao, and the horse and tiger Wu and Yin, and the translation writes the branches out together. 'The six Xin' is translated literally as 'the six Xin days' (Xin days). 'The Sovereign Earth is the Earth Emperor': the Sovereign Earth is the Earth Emperor, the same title as 'Earth Emperor' in the earlier Hook Earth and Earth ghost entries (LY.ILL.EL.34, EL.47). 'The Five Sages of the Xiao Hall' is a literal rendering, and echoes the earlier 'Lord Xiao is the Five Sages' (LY.ILL.EL.46). The 'Noble' here is not a celestial general but the Tianyi Noble star. The book does not say whether the 'first month' and so on are the months of the divination or of something else, and here it is copied as written; 'only these four positions, cycling round and round' means that the fifth month returns to the position of the first and the ninth to that of the first, and so on. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
