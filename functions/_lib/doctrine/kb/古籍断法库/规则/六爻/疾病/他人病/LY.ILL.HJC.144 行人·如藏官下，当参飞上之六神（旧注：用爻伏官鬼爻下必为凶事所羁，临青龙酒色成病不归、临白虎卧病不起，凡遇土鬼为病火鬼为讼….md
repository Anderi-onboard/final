# 行人·如藏官下，当参飞上之六神（旧注：用爻伏官鬼爻下必为凶事所羁，临青龙酒色成病不归、临白虎卧病不起，凡遇土鬼为病火鬼为讼勿论六神）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》行人章说：「用爻如果藏在官鬼爻下面，要参看飞神上的六神。」旧注：用爻伏在官鬼爻下，一定被凶事缠住：临着青龙，是因为酒色生了病回不来；临朱雀，是官非口舌回不来；临勾陈，是跌倒受伤；临螣蛇，是牵连惊恐；临白虎，是卧病不起；临玄武，是被盗失财回不来；凡是遇到土鬼就是病，火鬼就是官司，可以不论六神；卦里没有官鬼，也照上面断。王洪绪：临白虎或者官鬼属土，卧病不回；其余临勾陈、螣蛇、玄武各有所断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on travellers says: 'If the Subject line is hidden under the officer, look at the six spirit on the flying line.' The old commentary: with the Subject line hidden under the Pressure line, one is surely held back by ill events: on the Azure Dragon, wine and lust have brought illness and he does not return; on the Vermilion Bird, official trouble and quarrels; on Hook Earth, a fall and injury; on the Coiling Snake, entanglement and fright; on the White Tiger, bedridden and unable to rise; on the Dark Tortoise, robbed and having lost money; whenever it meets an Earth ghost it is illness and a Fire ghost a lawsuit, and one need not discuss the six spirits; if the hexagram has no Pressure line, judge in the same way. Wang Hongxu: on the White Tiger, or with Pressure of the Earth element, bedridden and not returning; on Hook Earth, the Coiling Snake and the Dark Tortoise, other outcomes.

> | 用爻伏于官爻下，必为凶事所羁，临勾陈跌仆损伤，临螣蛇勾连惊恐，临白虎或官鬼属土卧病不归，临玄武盗贼所阻，或贪色不归。 | 用爻伏在官爻下面，一定被凶事缠住：临着勾陈，是跌倒受伤；临着螣蛇，是牵连惊恐；临着白虎，或者官鬼属土，是卧病不回；临着玄武，是被盗贼阻拦，或者贪恋女色不回。 | If the Subject line is hidden under the Pressure line, one is surely held back by ill events: on Hook Earth, a fall and injury; on the Coiling Snake, entanglement and fright; on the White Tiger, or with Pressure of the Earth element, bedridden and not returning; on the Dark Tortoise, blocked by thieves, or not returning from craving lust. |
> | 其余下文引证类推之。 | 其余的在下面引证，照这样类推。 | The rest are cited below and are worked out by analogy. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 行人章是占出门在外的人的一章，用爻是那个人。用爻藏在官鬼爻下面（伏神），他就被凶事缠住不得回，飞神上临着哪位六神，就是哪一类事：青龙是酒色成病、白虎是卧病不起，这里只有青龙和白虎两处直接讲病，其余几位讲官非、伤、惊、盗，也一并带着，让「土鬼为病」的总括有上下文。「蹼跌」照原文，从意思看是「仆跌」（跌倒）。王洪绪的版本没有青龙、朱雀两条，多了「官鬼属土」一条，并且玄武一条是「盗贼所阻，或贪色不归」。这一条讲的是不在场的人的病，放在他人病里。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on travellers is about people away from home, and the Subject line is that person. With the Subject line hidden under the Pressure line (as a hidden line) he is held back by ill events and cannot return, and whichever of the six spirits sits on the flying line says what kind: the Azure Dragon is illness from wine and lust, the White Tiger lying ill and unable to rise; only the Azure Dragon and the White Tiger speak directly of illness, but the others, on official trouble, injury, fright and theft, are carried along so that the summing-up 'an Earth ghost is illness' has its context. 'Fall' is as the text has it and, from the sense, means falling down. Wang Hongxu's version lacks the Azure Dragon and Vermilion Bird entries, adds 'Pressure of the Earth element', and has the Dark Tortoise as 'blocked by thieves, or not returning from craving lust'. This entry is about the illness of someone not present and is placed under the illness of others.
