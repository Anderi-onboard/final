# 身命章·子孙化鬼，孝殇十月入冥途；禄贵临爻，拜住童年登相位（旧注：子化出鬼乃九死一生之兆，贵人禄马旺相则异日必贵）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》身命章占婴孩时说：「子孙化成鬼，像汉殇帝才十个月就走进了冥途；禄、贵人临在爻上，像元朝的拜住童年就登上相位。」旧注：子孙爻化出鬼爻，是九死一生的征兆；如果贵人、禄马一起临上、子孙又旺相，将来必定显贵；子孙遇凶而化鬼，是说孩子自己化成了鬼，所以凶，遇吉而化官，是化成了官，所以吉。王洪绪：子孙休囚，化鬼或化父母，都是死的征兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the person and fate says, on divining an infant: 'Output turning into a ghost: Emperor Shang of Han went to the underworld at ten months; Salary and the Noble on the line: Baizhu rose to minister in childhood.' The old commentary: the Output line turning into a ghost line is a sign of nine deaths to one life; if the Noble, Salary and Horse all sit on it and Output is strong, he will surely be eminent one day; when Output meets ill and turns into a ghost, the child himself has turned into a ghost, hence ill, and when it meets good and turns into an officer, he has turned into an officer, hence good. Wang Hongxu: Output resting or confined and turning into a ghost or into Resource is a sign of death.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1779 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙休囚化鬼化父，皆死之兆，似汉殇帝生才十月即亡； | 子孙爻休囚，又化成鬼爻或父母爻，都是死的征兆，像汉殇帝才生十个月就死了； | Output resting or confined and turning into a ghost or into Resource, all are signs of death, like Emperor Shang of Han, who died when barely ten months old; |
> | 若临贵人禄马旺相，如元拜住年十四即为相。 | 如果临着贵人、禄、马又旺相，就像元朝的拜住，十四岁就做了宰相。 | if it sits on the Noble, Salary and Horse and is strong, it is like Baizhu of the Yuan, who became minister at fourteen. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 汉殇帝（刘隆）生后一百多天即位，不满一岁就死；拜住是元朝人，十四岁做了宰相。「九死一生」是九分死、一分生，不是必死；旧注接着讲了活的条件（贵人禄马交临、子孙旺相）。王洪绪的版本把「休囚」和「化父」也算进去：子孙休囚、化鬼或化父母，都是死的征兆；旺相又带贵人禄马才像拜住。两处说的是同一件事，条件讲得略有不同。
>
> *Note:* Emperor Shang of Han (Liu Long) took the throne a little over a hundred days after birth and died before he was a year old; Baizhu was a man of the Yuan who became minister at fourteen. 'Nine deaths to one life' is nine parts death and one part life, not certain death; the old commentary goes on to give the conditions for life (the Noble, Salary and Horse all on it, and Output strong). Wang Hongxu's version counts 'resting or confined' and 'turning into Resource' as well: Output resting or confined and turning into a ghost or into Resource are signs of death, and only when it is strong and carries the Noble, Salary and Horse is it like Baizhu. The two say the same thing with slightly different conditions.
