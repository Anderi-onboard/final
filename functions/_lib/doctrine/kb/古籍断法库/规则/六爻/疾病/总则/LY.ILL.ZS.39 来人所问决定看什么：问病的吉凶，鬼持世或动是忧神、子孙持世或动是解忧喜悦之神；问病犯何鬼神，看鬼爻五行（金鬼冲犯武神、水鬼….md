# 来人所问决定看什么：问病的吉凶，鬼持世或动是忧神、子孙持世或动是解忧喜悦之神；问病犯何鬼神，看鬼爻五行（金鬼冲犯武神、水鬼得罪河神）；问病源，看鬼爻五行脏腑（火鬼心经、水鬼肾部）——李我平

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼是忧神、子孙是喜悦解忧之神，虽然是古法，用起来很恰当；但也有不把鬼当忧神的，全看来问的人心里想问什么。他问病的吉凶：鬼爻持世、鬼在卦里发动，是忧神；子孙持世、子孙发动，是解忧喜悦的神。他问病是犯了哪路鬼神：卦里出现金鬼，一定冲犯了武神；水鬼，一定得罪了河神这一类。他问病源：卦里出现火鬼，一定属心经；水鬼，一定在肾部。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* That the Pressure line is the spirit of worry and Output the spirit of joy that relieves worry, though an old method, is very apt in use; but there are cases in which Pressure is not taken as the spirit of worry, and it all depends on what the asker has in mind to ask. If he asks about the fortune of the illness: Pressure holding the Self line or moving in the hexagram is the spirit of worry, and Output holding the Self line or moving is the spirit of joy that relieves worry. If he asks which ghost or spirit the illness offends: a Metal Pressure appearing in the hexagram means he has surely offended a martial god, and a Water Pressure that he has offended the river god and the like. If he asks about the source of the illness: a Fire Pressure appearing in the hexagram surely belongs to the Heart channel, and a Water Pressure surely lies in the Kidney.

> [!note] 按
> 李我平在这里把三种看法各归到它回答的问题上：野鹤的「忧神/喜悦神」回答病的吉凶，觉子的五行鬼神（LY.ILL.ZS.23）回答犯了什么，黄金策疾病章开头的五行脏腑（LY.ILL.HJC.02）回答病在哪里。所以野鹤删掉黄金策的首论（LY.ILL.ZS.22），李我平认为不是不对，是问题不同。
>
> *Note:* Here Li Woping assigns each of three ways of looking to the question it answers: the Old Man's 'spirit of worry and spirit of joy' answers the fortune of the illness, Juezi's ghosts and spirits by phase (LY.ILL.ZS.23) answers what has been offended, and the organs by phase at the opening of the Huangjin Ce's chapter on illness (LY.ILL.HJC.02) answers where the illness lies. So when the Old Man deletes the opening discussion of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.ZS.22), Li Woping holds that it is not wrong but answers a different question.
