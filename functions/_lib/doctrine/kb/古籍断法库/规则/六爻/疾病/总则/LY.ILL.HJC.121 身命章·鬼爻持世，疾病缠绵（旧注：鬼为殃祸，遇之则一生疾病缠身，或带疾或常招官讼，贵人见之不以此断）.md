# 身命章·鬼爻持世，疾病缠绵（旧注：鬼为殃祸，遇之则一生疾病缠身，或带疾或常招官讼，贵人见之不以此断）

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》身命章说：「鬼爻持世，疾病缠绵。」旧注解释：官鬼是殃祸，遇到它，一生疾病缠身，或者带着病，或者常常招来官司；如果是贵人遇到，就不这样断。王洪绪说：官是祸殃，遇到主带疾病、招官讼，贵人并临则贵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the person and fate says: 'With the ghost line holding the Self line, illness lingers.' The old commentary explains: Pressure is calamity, and meeting it means illness clinging to the body for a whole life, either carrying an illness or often drawing a lawsuit; if a person of rank meets it, it is not judged this way. Wang Hongxu says: Pressure is calamity, and meeting it means carrying illness or drawing a lawsuit, but if a noble star is on it as well, it means rank.

> [!warning] 打架
> 一生多病落在哪一爻持世上，两本说法不同。《黄金策》身命章是官鬼爻持世（这一条），卜筮全书的阐幽精要身命章是兄弟爻持世「疾病连绵」（LY.ILL.BSQS.24）；两处都说父母持世辛勤劳碌。诸爻持世诀里鬼爻持世是「占身不病也遭官」（LY.ILL.BSQS.04），和黄金策同在鬼爻上，只是没有说一生多病。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books differ on which line holding the Self line brings a lifetime of illness. The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the person and fate has the Pressure line holding the Self line (this entry); the chapter on the person and life in the Chanyou Jingyao of the Bushi Quanshu has the Peer line holding the Self line, 'illness drags on' (LY.ILL.BSQS.24); both say that Resource holding the Self line means toil and labour. The verse on each line holding the Self line has, for the Pressure line holding the Self line, 'divining about oneself, even if not ill one meets an official case' (LY.ILL.BSQS.04), which agrees with the Huangjin Ce in being on the Pressure line but does not say lifelong illness. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这是《黄金策》身命章（占一个人一生）里「父母持身辛勤劳碌，鬼爻持世疾病缠绵，遇兄则财莫能聚，见子则身不犯刑」一联的第二句；婚姻域收过第三句（LY.MARRY.HJC.105）。「贵人见之不以此断」是说有官位、有贵人命的人遇到官鬼持世，官鬼是他的官，不当作灾病。「带疾」是带着病，指有慢性的、长期的病。
>
> *Note:* This is the second line of the couplet in the Huangjin Ce's chapter on the person and fate (on a person's whole life): 'Resource holding the Self line: toil and labour; Pressure holding the Self line: illness lingers; meeting Peer, wealth cannot gather; meeting Output, one does not run into punishment'; the third line was taken in the marriage domain (LY.MARRY.HJC.105). 'If a person of rank meets it, it is not judged this way' means that for a man with an official post or a noble fate, Pressure holding the Self line is his office and is not read as calamity or illness. 'Carrying an illness' means a long-standing, chronic illness.
