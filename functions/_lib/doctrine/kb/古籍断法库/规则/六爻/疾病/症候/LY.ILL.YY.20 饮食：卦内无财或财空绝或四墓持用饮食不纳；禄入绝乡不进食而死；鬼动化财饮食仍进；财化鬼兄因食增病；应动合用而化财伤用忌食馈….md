# 饮食：卦内无财或财空绝或四墓持用饮食不纳；禄入绝乡不进食而死；鬼动化财饮食仍进；财化鬼兄因食增病；应动合用而化财伤用忌食馈来之物；丑鬼忌牛肉、卯兔、未羊、酉鸡、戌犬、亥猪；水鬼忌鱼腥冷咸、木鬼忌果核酸、金鬼忌葱蒜姜辣、火鬼忌煎炒苦、土鬼忌米面瓜蔬甘

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有财，或者财爻空、绝，或者四个墓库的爻持着用爻的，吃不下东西；禄（财）落入绝地的，吃不下饭而死；鬼发动变出财的，饮食还能吃；财变出鬼、兄的，是因为吃东西使病加重；应爻发动合用爻、又变出财来伤用爻的，忌讳吃人家送来的东西。忌口：丑鬼忌牛肉，卯鬼忌兔肉，未鬼忌羊肉，酉鬼忌鸡肉，戌鬼忌狗肉，亥鬼忌猪肉；水鬼忌鱼腥、冷的、咸的东西，木鬼忌带核的果子、酸的东西，金鬼忌葱、蒜、姜、辣的东西，火鬼忌煎炒烤炙、香味浓的、苦的东西，土鬼忌米面、茄子、芋头、瓜菜、甜的东西。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If there is no Wealth in the hexagram, or Wealth is Void or in Extinction, or the four tombs hold the Subject line, food and drink are not taken in; if the emolument (Wealth) falls into a place of Extinction, he dies without taking food; if the ghost moves and changes into Wealth, food and drink can still be taken; if Wealth changes into a ghost or Peer, the illness worsens because of eating; if the Other line moves to combine with the Subject line and changes into Wealth that harms it, gifts of food sent over must be avoided. Foods to avoid: a Chou ghost must avoid beef, a Mao ghost rabbit, a Wei ghost mutton, a You ghost chicken, a Xu ghost dog, and a Hai ghost pork; a Water ghost must avoid fishy, cold and salty things, a Wood ghost fruit with stones and sour things, a Metal ghost onions, garlic, ginger and hot pungent things, a Fire ghost fried, roasted and strongly scented or bitter things, and an Earth ghost rice and wheat, aubergine, taro, melons and vegetables and sweet things.

> [!note] 按
> 「禄」在这里指财爻（前面讲死卦时说「财为禄命」，LY.ILL.YY.06）。「四墓」是辰戌丑未四个墓库的地支。忌口这一段是把五行、地支和食物对起来，书里没有另讲为什么。黄金策讲饮食的地方见 LY.ILL.HJC.21（卦内无财则饮食不纳）。
>
> *Note:* 'Emolument' (lu) here means the Wealth line (in the hexagram-images that judge death the book says 'Wealth is the salary and destiny', LY.ILL.YY.06). The 'four tombs' are the four branches Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei, the tomb-storehouses. The passage on foods to avoid matches elements, branches and foods and the book does not say why. Where the Huangjin Ce treats food and drink see LY.ILL.HJC.21 ('no Wealth in the hexagram, food is not taken in').
