# 月将值日·河魁（月建戌）占病：难安如行船下急滩、烦闷呕逆、头疼作热加寒、骨节拘急；戌宫卦断日中出路骂神鬼、社司咒咀、退西方五墓煞

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在戌、叫河魁的时候占病：难以安稳，像行船冲下急滩；心里烦闷，又呕吐上逆；头疼发热，有时又加上发冷；四肢骨节更加拘紧。冲犯的是十朝的鬼判宫。「戌宫卦断」那一段说：白天在路上骂过神鬼，社司的咒咀名目很多，要把西方的五墓煞退送出去，免得病人的身体重得像山。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on Xu, named Hekui, divining illness: he cannot be at ease, like a boat running down a rapid; the heart is vexed and oppressed, with retching and counterflow; headache with fever, sometimes with chills added; the joints of the limbs are more cramped still. What has been offended is the ghost judge's palace of the Ten Courts. The passage 'the verdict for the Xu-palace hexagrams' says: he cursed spirits and ghosts on the road at midday, the curses of the Altar Official come in many kinds, and the Five-Tombs Killer of the west is to be driven back, lest the sick body grow heavy as a mountain.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。「日中出路骂神鬼」的「日中」译成白天、中午，「出路」译成在路上，都是照字面。「番退」的「番」读作翻，「番退西方五墓煞」译成「翻转退送」，书里没有给做法。「五墓煞」是一种凶煞，书里没有解释。「十朝鬼判宫」见 LY.ILL.GLJ.04 的说明。这一首在原文里和下一首登明挨在同一行里，没有分行。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. 'Midday' for the book's 'in the middle of the day' and 'on the road' for 'goes out on the road' follow the letter. The 'turned back' of 'the Five-Tombs Killer of the west is to be turned back' reads the book's 'turn' as 'overturn', and the book does not give a method. 'The Five-Tombs Killer' is a kind of fierce killer, not explained in the book. For 'the ghost judge's palace of the Ten Courts' see the note to LY.ILL.GLJ.04. In the original this verse and the next, Dengming, run on in the same line without a break.
