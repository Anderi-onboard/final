# 六位取象表·鬼神：初爻井神、二爻司命、三爻家先、四爻土地、五爻社司、六爻天神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卜筮全书的「六爻诸占定位」表里，鬼神一行按初爻到六爻的次序是：井神、司命、家先、土地、社司、天神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the table of the position of the six lines for the various divinations in the Bushi Quanshu, the row for spirits runs, from the first line to the sixth: the well spirit, the Life-Governing Spirit, the family ancestors, the Earth God, the village altar spirit and the heavenly spirit.

> [!note] 按
> 和上一条是同一张表的另一行，占鬼神（谁在作祟）时按爻位取象：鬼爻落在初爻是井神、二爻司命（灶君管人命的神）、三爻家先（家里的祖先）、四爻土地、五爻社司（社里管事的神）、六爻天神。同一类的爻位配鬼神，火珠林占鬼神一节也有一张（LY.ILL.HZL.05）。「一格对一爻」是按表里的位置读的。
>
> *Note:* This is another row of the same table as the last entry: in divining spirits (which one is at work) the images are taken by line position: the ghost line falling on the first line is the well spirit, on the second the Life-Governing Spirit (the hearth-god who governs a person's life), on the third the family ancestors (the ancestors of the house), on the fourth the Earth God, on the fifth the village altar spirit (the spirit in charge of the village altar) and on the sixth the heavenly spirit. The Huozhu Lin's section on divining spirits also has a table matching line positions to spirits (LY.ILL.HZL.05). 'One cell to a line' is read by position in the table.
