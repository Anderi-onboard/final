# 卦静当寻外象、无官另看伏神：卦无动爻只取外卦（外乾病从西北、外坤祸起西南）；内外皆无官鬼则看鬼伏何爻之下、鬼上飞神定其所向（颐卦伏三爻飞神庚辰为东南、讼卦伏三爻午火为南）；伏鬼再空莫言祸祟

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦安静，就该找外卦的象；没有官鬼，另外看伏神；鬼上面的飞爻，方向就定下来了。注说：卦里没有动爻，只取外卦：比如外卦属乾宫，病就从西北来；外卦临坤，祸就起在西南；六爻内有鬼的，这样来看。内外都没有官鬼，又不取外卦为凭据，另外去找鬼伏在哪一爻之下，由鬼上面的飞神来定它的方向：假如占得颐卦安静，鬼伏在三爻之下，就取飞神庚辰，辰是东南方，断定一定没有差错；又如讼卦安静，鬼也伏在三爻午火之下，就说是南方；伏鬼再落空，就不要说是祸祟。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the hexagram is still, look for the image of the outer trigram; if there is no Pressure line, look separately at the hidden line; the flying line above the ghost fixes the direction again. The commentary says: if the hexagram has no moving line, take only the outer trigram: for example, if the outer belongs to the Qian palace, the illness comes from the northwest; if the outer sits on Kun, misfortune rises in the southwest; where there is a ghost among the six lines, look at it so. If neither inner nor outer has a Pressure line, and one does not rely on the outer trigram, look separately under which line the ghost lies hidden, and fix its direction by the flying line above the ghost: if, for example, a still Yi is obtained with the ghost hidden under the third line, take the flying line Gengchen, Chen being the southeast, and the judgment will surely not err; or in a still Song the ghost is also hidden under the third line, Wu Fire, and one says south; if the hidden ghost is again Void, do not speak of haunting.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条与本章「何处得病」（LY.ILL.EL.03）最后一段几乎是同一段话，只是那里问的是病从哪里起，这里问的是作祟的鬼在哪个方向。颐卦（卦名）的例：鬼（官鬼，金）伏在三爻之下，三爻的飞神是庚辰，辰属东南；讼卦的例与 LY.ILL.EL.03 相同（鬼伏三爻午火之下，南方）。和易冒「考鬼的四种情形」（LY.ILL.YM.55）、「病从何起」（LY.ILL.YM.29）、易隐「病起何方」（LY.ILL.YY.33）是同一套办法。「六爻内有鬼者，如此看之」是说卦里有鬼、也没有动爻时，才用外卦定方向。
>
> *Note:* This entry is almost the same passage as the last paragraph of this chapter's 'where the illness was contracted' (LY.ILL.EL.03), except that there the question was from where the illness rose and here it is in which direction the haunting ghost lies. In the case of Yi (the hexagram): the ghost (the Pressure line, Metal) lies hidden under the third line, the flying line of the third line is Gengchen, and Chen belongs to the southeast; the case of Song is the same as in LY.ILL.EL.03 (the ghost hidden under the third line, Wu Fire, the south). It is the same method as the Yi Mao's 'four cases in examining a ghost' (LY.ILL.YM.55), 'where the illness rose from' (LY.ILL.YM.29) and the Yi Yin's 'the direction from which the illness rose' (LY.ILL.YY.33). 'Where there is a ghost among the six lines, look at it so' means that when the hexagram has a ghost and no moving line, the outer trigram fixes the direction.
