# 易卦发蒙歌·六亲各忧：父母爻动忧子孙；财爻动合杀忧父母病；比和为兄弟，此爻动忌妻妾疾病

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 易卦发蒙歌的自注给了三条六亲发动时要担心的事：父母爻动，担心子孙；财爻动、又合上杀，担心父母生病；兄弟爻动，忌妻妾的疾病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The notes to the Yigua Fameng Ge give three things to worry about when a relation line moves: when the Resource line moves, worry for Output (the children); when the Wealth line moves and joins a killing, worry that the Resource (the parents) will fall ill; when the Peer line moves, the illness of wife and concubine is to be feared.

> [!note] 按
> 三条自注都是从五行相克来的：父母克子孙，财克父母，兄弟克财（也就是克妻妾）。例子都举金宫：土生金所以是父母，木是金所克所以是财，金同金所以是兄弟。「合杀」的杀书里没有指明是哪一种杀，按字面译成「joins a killing」。这几条在书里同一首歌诀里，和 LY.ILL.BSYG.09 讲的那一串（财动忧父母、父母动忧子孙……）是同一个道理，那一条更完整。
>
> *Note:* All three notes come from control among the five phases: Resource controls Output, Wealth controls Resource, Peer controls Wealth (that is, controls wife and concubine). The examples are all given in a Metal palace: Earth generates Metal so it is Resource, Wood is controlled by Metal so it is Wealth, Metal is the same as Metal so it is Peer. The killing of 'joins a killing' is not specified in the book as to which killing, and is rendered literally as 'joins a killing'. These notes are in the same song as the longer chain of LY.ILL.BSYG.09 (Wealth moves, worry for Resource; Resource moves, worry for Output ...), and are the same reasoning, which that entry gives more fully.
