# 【补录】六十四卦课·占病逢解、恒、升、随、巽、小畜、颐、离、坤、临、大壮、夬、兑、萃、咸：疾病大好、有病就好、疾病口舌皆除根、疾病痊愈、灾消病散、疾病即痊、疾病安宁

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[文王金钱课]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 文王金钱课文末补录的网上传本里，这十六个卦的卦辞或推断说病会好：解「疾病大好」，恒「有病就好」，升「疾病口舌皆除根」，随「疾病口舌保平安」，巽「疾病痊愈」，小畜「疾病口舌，交节安宁」，颐「疾病口舌可离身」，离「灾消病散」，坤「疾病见好」，临「疾病即痊」，大壮「疾病皆除」，夬「疾病除根」，兑「疾病痊愈」，萃「口舌疾病永无灾」，咸「疾病安宁」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the internet copy that the library appended at the end of the Wenwang Jinqian Ke, the hexagram verse or judgment of these fifteen hexagrams says that the illness will mend: Xie, 'illness mends greatly'; Heng, 'if there is illness, it mends'; Sheng, 'illness and quarrels are all uprooted'; Sui, 'illness and quarrels are kept safe and peaceful'; Xun, 'illness is cured'; Xiaoxu, 'illness and quarrels: after the turn of the season, peace'; Yi (Nourishment), 'illness and quarrels can leave the body'; Li, 'calamity vanishes and illness disperses'; Kun, 'illness shows improvement'; Lin, 'illness is cured at once'; Dazhuang, 'all illness is removed'; Guai, 'illness is uprooted'; Dui, 'illness is cured'; Cui, 'quarrels and illness bring no calamity ever'; Xian, 'illness is at ease'.

> [!warning] 打架
> 补录的这些卦里，有几个和周易尚占（LY.ILL.ZSZ.03 至 ZSZ.07）说的相反或相左：巽，周易尚占说「金神为崇」，这里说「疾病痊愈」；离，周易尚占说「病重财轻」，这里说「灾消病散」；临，周易尚占说「病犯崇侵」，这里说「疾病即痊」；夬，周易尚占说「病讼迟延」，这里说「疾病除根」；大壮，周易尚占说「休占讼病」，这里说「疾病皆除」；恒，周易尚占说「病须疾治」（要赶快治），这里说「有病就好」。解、随、颐、兑、萃、咸这六个两本一致说病好；升、小畜、坤周易尚占没有讲病。（这几个卦在补录部分里，而补录部分的来历不明，见下面的说明。）书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Several of these supplemented hexagrams are opposed to or at odds with what the Zhouyi Shangzhan says (LY.ILL.ZSZ.03 to ZSZ.07): Xun, the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'a Metal spirit is the curse' while here it says 'illness is cured'; Li, the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'illness is heavy and wealth is light' while here 'calamity vanishes and illness disperses'; Lin, the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'illness meets the encroachment of a curse' while here 'illness is cured at once'; Guai, the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'illness and lawsuit are delayed' while here 'illness is uprooted'; Dazhuang, the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'do not divine lawsuits or illness' while here 'all illness is removed'; Heng, the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'illness must be treated quickly' while here 'if there is illness, it mends'. Xie, Sui, Yi (Nourishment), Dui, Cui and Xian are six in which the two books agree that the illness mends; for Sheng, Xiaoxu and Kun the Zhouyi Shangzhan does not speak of illness. (These hexagrams are in the supplemented part, whose origin is unknown; see the note below.) The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这个卦是库里「补录」的网上传本，不是这部书原文里有的（原文只有二十六卦）；库里的说明写：这份传本每卦只有「卦辞」（七言四句）和「推断」（四言四句）两项，没有课名、六亲、地支、世应，拿它和原有二十六卦对，十七卦两项都对得上，三卦卦辞对得上、推断有出入，另有三对卦的内容整个互换（风地观与山地剥、火泽睽与风泽中孚、天泽履与风山渐），所以补录部分个别卦也可能挂错了卦；卦名已按卦画订正；传本里没有风火家人。「疾病口舌」是疾病和口舌是非并说。「交节」是过了节气。「五关脱难」是过五关、脱了难。补录部分的卦名已按卦画订正，不是原帖的写法。
>
> *Note:* These hexagrams are from the 'supplemented' copy found on the internet that the library appended, and are not in the original text of this book (the original has only twenty-six hexagrams); the library's note says: this copy gives each hexagram only two items, the 'hexagram verse' (four lines of seven characters) and the 'judgment' (four phrases of four characters), with no name of the reading, no six relations, no branches, no Self and Other; compared with the twenty-six original hexagrams, seventeen agree in both items, three agree in the verse but differ in the judgment, and three pairs of hexagrams have their contents swapped wholesale (Guan and Bo, Kui and Zhongfu, Lü and Jian), so some of the supplemented hexagrams may also be hung on the wrong hexagram; the names of the hexagrams have been corrected from the hexagram figures; the copy lacks Jiaren. 'Illness and quarrels' names illness and quarrels together. 'The turn of the season' is the passing of a solar term. 'Passing the five gates, escaping hardship' is getting through the five passes and escaping hardship. The names of the hexagrams in the supplemented part have been corrected from the hexagram figures and are not the original post's spelling.
