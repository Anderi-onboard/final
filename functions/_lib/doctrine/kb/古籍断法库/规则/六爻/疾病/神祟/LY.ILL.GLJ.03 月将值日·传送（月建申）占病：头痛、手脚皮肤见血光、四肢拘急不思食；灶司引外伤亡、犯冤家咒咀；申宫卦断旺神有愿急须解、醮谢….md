# 月将值日·传送（月建申）占病：头痛、手脚皮肤见血光、四肢拘急不思食；灶司引外伤亡、犯冤家咒咀；申宫卦断旺神有愿急须解、醮谢星辰祭土主王

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在申、叫传送的时候占病：头痛，手脚和皮肤上见血光，四肢抽紧，不想吃东西。作祟的是灶司引进来的外面的伤亡鬼、冤家和咒咀他的鬼，牵连到香火和家堂。「申宫卦断」那一段说：旺相的神有愿没还，赶紧要解；再适合许愿保平安，做醮酬谢星辰，在本方位祭土主王。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on Shen, named Chuansong, divining illness: headache, bloodshed on the hands, feet and skin, the limbs cramped and no wish to eat. The ones at work are the dead by injury from outside whom the Kitchen Official has drawn in, enemies and the ghosts of those who cursed him, tied to the incense-fire and the family hall. The passage 'the verdict for the Shen-palace hexagrams' says: a strong spirit has a vow outstanding and it must be released quickly; it is also fitting to make a vow to keep him safe, to perform a thanksgiving offering to the stars, and to make offerings to the Earth Lord, the King, in the quarter concerned.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。标题括号里的「中月名曰傅送」，「中」是「申」、「傅」是「传」的误写。「灶司引透外伤亡」的「引透」按上下文读作引进来；「咒咀」就是咒诅；「勾连」是牵连；「土主王」书里没有解释，译成「土主王」（当地的土主、那位王），只是字面。「旺神有愿」是说某个旺相的神明有过愿没有还，书里没有说是哪个神；GLJ.11 里有几乎一样的一句。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. In the heading's bracket 'the Shen month is called Chuansong' the character printed for 'Shen' and the one for 'Chuan' are misprints. 'The Kitchen Official draws in' reads the book's 'draws through' as 'draws in' from context; 'cursed' is the book's 'curse'; 'tied to' is 'linked with'; 'the Earth Lord, the King' is not explained in the book and is only the literal sense (the local Earth Lord, that King). 'A strong spirit has a vow outstanding' means that some thriving spirit was promised something that has not been paid; the book does not say which spirit, and GLJ.11 has an almost identical line.
