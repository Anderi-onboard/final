# 鬼墓是伏尸为祸、财库是藏神不安：鬼爻属金卦有丑动是鬼墓，妻财属木卦有未动是财库；不伤世则不为祸

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼的墓，是埋着的尸骨在作祸；财的库，是藏着的神不得安宁。看法：鬼爻属金，卦里有丑爻发动，就是鬼的墓；妻财属木，卦里有未爻发动，就是财的库；别的照这样推。但是不伤到世爻，就不会作祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ghost's tomb means a buried corpse is making trouble; Wealth's storehouse means a hidden spirit is unsettled. How to see it: if the Pressure line is Metal and a Chou line in the hexagram moves, that is the ghost's tomb; if the Wealth line is Wood and a Wei line in the hexagram moves, that is Wealth's storehouse; work out the others the same way. But if it does not harm the Self line, it does no harm.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注末了有两句王洪绪没有：一句指向家宅的断法，一句给了条件「不伤世则不为祸」。王洪绪把旧注的「财库」直接写成「木墓」，意思相同（未是木的墓）。「墓」这里指的是五行入墓的那一支：金墓在丑，木墓在未。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary ends with two sentences that Wang Hongxu lacks: one pointing to the household section and one giving the condition 'if it does not harm the Self line, it does no harm'. Wang Hongxu writes the old commentary's 'Wealth's storehouse' directly as 'the tomb of Wood', which means the same (Wei is the tomb of Wood). The 'tomb' here is the branch in which an element enters its tomb: Chou for Metal, Wei for Wood.
