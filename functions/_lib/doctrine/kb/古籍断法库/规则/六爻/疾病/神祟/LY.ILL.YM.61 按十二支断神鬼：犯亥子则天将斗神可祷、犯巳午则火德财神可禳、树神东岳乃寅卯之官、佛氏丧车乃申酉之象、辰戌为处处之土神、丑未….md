# 按十二支断神鬼：犯亥子则天将斗神可祷、犯巳午则火德财神可禳、树神东岳乃寅卯之官、佛氏丧车乃申酉之象、辰戌为处处之土神、丑未为家家之地鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 犯了亥、子，就是天将斗神，可以祷告；犯了巳、午，就是火德财神，可以禳解；树神、东岳，是寅、卯的官；佛家的丧车，是申、酉的象；辰、戌是处处都有的土神，丑、未是家家都有的地鬼。注说：十二支的神值在鬼上，就用十二支的象来断，是这样的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If it offends Hai and Zi, the Heavenly Generals and the spirit of the Dipper may be prayed to; if it offends Si and Wu, the Fire Virtue and the god of wealth may be exorcised; the tree spirit and the Eastern Peak are officials of Yin and Mao; the Buddhist funeral carriage is the image of Shen and You; Chen and Xu are the earth spirits found everywhere, and Chou and Wei the earth ghosts found in every house. The note says: where a spirit of the twelve branches sits on the ghost, judgment by the images of the twelve branches is as above.

> [!note] 按
> 原文里这一段跨了两行：第三句「树神东岳，乃寅卯之官，佛氏丧车，」在上一行结尾，「乃申酉之象；」在下一行开头，这里合起来读。「亥子」是北、水，配天将斗神；「巳午」是南、火，配火德财神；「寅卯」是东、木，配树神东岳；「申酉」是西、金，配佛家丧车。「辰戌」与「丑未」是四土，分成处处的土神和家家的地鬼。「可祷」「可禳」两个词分开用：亥子是神，可以向它祷告；巳午是火德财神，是可以禳解的。
>
> *Note:* In the original this passage runs over two lines: the third sentence, 'the tree spirit and the Eastern Peak are officials of Yin and Mao, the Buddhist funeral carriage', ends the first line and 'is the image of Shen and You' begins the next, and they are read here together. Hai and Zi are north and Water, matched with the Heavenly Generals and the spirit of the Dipper; Si and Wu are south and Fire, matched with the Fire Virtue and the god of wealth; Yin and Mao are east and Wood, matched with the tree spirit and the Eastern Peak; Shen and You are west and Metal, matched with the Buddhist funeral carriage. Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei are the four Earths, divided into the earth spirits found everywhere and the earth ghosts found in every house. 'May be prayed to' and 'may be exorcised' are used separately: with Hai and Zi it is a spirit that may be prayed to, and with Si and Wu it is the Fire Virtue and the god of wealth, which may be exorcised.
