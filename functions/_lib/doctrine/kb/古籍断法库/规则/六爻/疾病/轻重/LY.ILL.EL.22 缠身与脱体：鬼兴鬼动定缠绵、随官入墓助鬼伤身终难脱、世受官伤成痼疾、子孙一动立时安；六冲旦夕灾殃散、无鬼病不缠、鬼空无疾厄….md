# 缠身与脱体：鬼兴鬼动定缠绵、随官入墓助鬼伤身终难脱、世受官伤成痼疾、子孙一动立时安；六冲旦夕灾殃散、无鬼病不缠、鬼空无疾厄、忌神旺发患多年、鬼化福神他日解、用象兴隆祸不干

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以福神为主，用象为凭。身处灾难、相貌残损，鬼兴鬼动，一定缠绵不去；随官入墓，终究难脱；助鬼伤身，永不痊愈；世爻受官鬼所伤，成为痼疾；子孙一动，立刻安好。六冲的卦，灾殃旦夕就散；没有鬼，病终究不缠；鬼如果空亡，没有疾厄；忌神旺相发动，病患延续多年；鬼变出福神，他日就解；用象兴隆，祸事不相干。注说：占带不带疾，最嫌官鬼兴隆，官鬼如果旺相，病一定缠身，纵然不旺相，发动也是这样；若遇上随官入墓、或助鬼伤身，必成痼疾；鬼克世爻也难脱体；如果得到子孙旺相、或发动、或持世、或临日月，病就离身。六冲的卦，这个病容易消，合处逢冲的后来才解；鬼不上卦以及落空亡，永远没有殃疾；纵然有鬼象，如果变出福神，眼下虽然凶，后来当解脱；虽然凭官鬼，也要看用爻，用爻遇旬空月破，病也难痊愈；土象休囚，又被忌神发动，岂不成凶？用爻纵然弱，如果遇上动出元神，虽然有这个灾，决不至损寿；用爻父母若得兴隆，官鬼又居衰地，起初虽然见病，终究不成殃。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The fortune spirit is the main line, and the Subject image is the ground. The body in trouble, the features worn and disfigured; the ghost thriving and moving surely clings and lingers; following the Pressure line into a tomb, it is hard in the end to shake off; helping the ghost to harm the Self, it never heals; the Self wounded by Pressure becomes a chronic illness; when Output moves, ease comes at once. A Six-Clash hexagram disperses calamity by morning or evening; with no ghost the illness in the end does not cling; if the ghost is Void there is no illness; the Adversary strong and moving makes an affliction of many years; a ghost that changes into the fortune spirit is resolved some day later; the Subject image thriving, misfortune does not touch. The commentary says: for whether one carries an illness or not, most feared is a thriving Pressure line; if the Pressure line is strong the illness surely clings to the body, and even if it is not strong the same is true if it moves; meeting following into a tomb or helping the ghost to harm the Self, it will surely become a chronic illness; a ghost controlling the Self line also can hardly be shaken off; if Output is strong, or moves, or holds the Self, or sits on the Day or Month, the illness leaves the body. A Six-Clash hexagram makes this ailment easy to dispel, and a clash met in a combination is resolved only later; if the ghost is absent from the hexagram or falls into Void, there will never be affliction; even if there is a ghost image, if it changes into the fortune spirit, though it is ominous for the moment it will be resolved later; though one relies on the Pressure line, one must also look at the Subject line, and if the Subject meets Void or Month-break the illness too is hard to heal; if an Earth image is resting or trapped and the Adversary moves, how can it fail to be ominous? Though the Subject line is weak, if the Support comes out moving, then although there is this calamity it does not cost life; if the Resource line as Subject is thriving and the Pressure line sits in a weak place, then although at first there is illness, in the end it does no harm.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章问的是「病会不会缠身、能不能脱体」。章首小标是「以福神为主」（福神是子孙），这和上一大段以用神为主不同：这里要看的是病能不能被解掉，而解病的是子孙。「相貌残」是形容病人的身形样貌受损。「土象休囚，更被忌神发动」的「土象」是用神属土（占父母尊长等），读法没有把握。最后一句的「用父」是用爻为父母爻。与黄金策「世持鬼爻病纵轻而难疗」（LY.ILL.HJC.34）、易隐的轻重（LY.ILL.YY.16、YY.19）同一路读法。
>
> *Note:* This chapter asks whether the illness will cling to the body and whether it can leave it. The chapter's subtitle is 'the fortune spirit is the main line' (the fortune spirit being Output), which differs from the previous large passage, where the Subject was the main line: here what is looked at is whether the illness can be dissolved, and it is Output that dissolves it. 'Features worn and disfigured' describes the sick person's frame and appearance being damaged. In 'an Earth image resting or trapped and the Adversary moving' the 'Earth image' is the Subject being Earth (as in divining a parent or elder), the reading being uncertain. The 'Resource as Subject' in the last sentence means the Subject line is the Resource line. It reads in the same way as the Huangjin Ce's 'the Self holding the ghost, though the illness be light it is hard to treat' (LY.ILL.HJC.34) and the Yi Yin's light and heavy (LY.ILL.YY.16, YY.19).
