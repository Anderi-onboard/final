# 土鬼配六兽：土为瘟疫之魂、膨胀虚黄之鬼；症缓庙神土府、症急贤圣城隍；青龙素土、勾陈土皇、白虎金神七煞、玄武坑厕、螣蛇作犯与本境之神、朱雀天曹与飞游之土；乾兑二卦或带天咒地咒负结煞亦为天曹土

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 土鬼，是瘟疫的魂，膨胀、虚黄的鬼：症状缓的，是庙神、土府；症状急的，是贤圣、城隍。青龙，是素土；勾陈，叫土皇；白虎，是金神，忌它；玄武，是坑厕的妨害；螣蛇，应当作犯，并且求本境的神；朱雀，合于天曹，并且谢飞游的土。注说：土鬼，主瘟疫鬼、膨胀鬼、黄病鬼；症状缓的，是庙里的神、大的是土神；症状急的，是五方贤圣、又是城隍；带青龙，是正土，宜念素诰；带勾陈，是土皇，宜中奏；带白虎，是金神，就是七煞；带玄武，是水口上的作犯，如果鬼变出兄弟、或兄弟变出鬼，就是坑厕；土带螣蛇是作犯，土又是螣蛇，也是当地的杀神祠；带朱雀是飞土；如果在乾、兑两卦，就是天曹；即使不在乾、兑卦中，如果带天咒、地咒或负结煞，也是天曹土。天咒煞说：正月二月鼠来，三月四月酉，五月六月马头，七月猴走，八月鸡，九月犬，十月逢猪，子、兔、丑、鼠，是天咒；地咒煞从正月起在卯上顺行十二位；负结煞说：正月二月猪亏，三月四月牛，五月六月其星向兔游，七月八月蛇宫，九月十月未，十一月十二月酉中求。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Earth is the soul of epidemic, the ghost of swelling and pale-yellow emptiness: if mild, the temple gods and the earth mansion; if severe, the sages and the City God. The Azure Dragon is the plain Earth; Hook Earth is called the Earth Emperor; the White Tiger is the Golden Spirit, which is to be avoided; the Dark Tortoise is the hindrance of the pit and privy; the Coiling Snake should be the offence-maker, and one seeks the god of the local region; the Vermilion Bird accords with the Heavenly Bureau, and one thanks the Flying-Wandering Earth. The commentary says: an Earth ghost governs ghosts of epidemic, ghosts of swelling and ghosts of jaundice; if the symptoms are mild, it is a god within a temple, or an earth god of greater rank; if urgent, it is the sages of the five quarters and also the City God; with the Azure Dragon, it is the right Earth, and a plain prayer is suitable; with Hook Earth, it is the Earth Emperor, and a central memorial is suitable; with the White Tiger, it is the Golden Spirit, that is, the Seven Killings; with the Dark Tortoise, it is an offence at a water mouth, and if the ghost changes into Peer or Peer changes into a ghost, it is the pit and privy; Earth with the Coiling Snake is the offence-maker, and Earth is also the Coiling Snake, which is also the local killing-god shrine; with the Vermilion Bird it is the flying earth; if it is in the Qian or Dui hexagram it is the Heavenly Bureau, and even if it is not in Qian or Dui, if it carries the Heavenly Curse, the Earth Curse or the Burden-Knot killing it is also the Heavenly Bureau earth. The Heavenly Curse killing says: 'in the first and second months the Rat comes, third and fourth the You, fifth and sixth the Horse's head, seventh the Monkey runs, eighth the Rooster, ninth the Dog, tenth meets the Pig, Zi, Rabbit, Chou, Rat': this is the Heavenly Curse; the Earth Curse killing runs forward twelve places from Mao in the first month; the Burden-Knot killing says: 'first and second the Pig is deficient, third and fourth the Ox, fifth and sixth its star wanders toward the Rabbit, seventh and eighth the Snake's palace, ninth and tenth Wei, eleventh and twelfth seek in You'.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段里的三个咒煞口诀（天咒、地咒、负结），文字明显有讹脱（如「十逢猪子兔丑鼠」一句不合月份次序），这里照字面译，没有替它排出月份对照表。「素诰」是素食斋戒的诰文，「中奏」是向上奏告的一种仪式，「飞游土」「作犯土」「金神七煞」「天曹土」都是土部的神煞名（见易冒 LY.ILL.YM.48 里土部的称呼）。「土带螣蛇为作犯，土又为螣蛇上亦为当方上杀神祠」这一句文字不顺，读法没有把握。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地（苕溪一带）的民间神祇和星煞，书里一个也没有解释（章末自己说别的郡的神司不同），这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* The three formulas for the curse killings (Heavenly Curse, Earth Curse, Burden-Knot) in this passage are plainly corrupt in places (for instance 'tenth meets the Pig, Zi, Rabbit, Chou, Rat' does not fit the order of the months), and they are translated literally, without drawing up a table of months. 'Plain prayer' is a text of fasting and vegetarian devotion, and 'central memorial' is a kind of rite of reporting upward; 'the Flying-Wandering Earth', 'the offence-making earth', 'the Golden Spirit and Seven Killings' and 'the Heavenly Bureau earth' are all names of earth stars and spirits (see the titles of the Earth department in the Yi Mao, LY.ILL.YM.48). 'Earth with the Coiling Snake is the offence-maker, and Earth is also the Coiling Snake, which is also the local killing-god shrine' does not run smoothly, and the reading is uncertain. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain (the book itself says at the end of the chapter that the gods of other prefectures differ), and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
