# 官旺福衰药饵轻而病重：官爻无气、子孙旺相则药能胜病有效；子孙休囚、官爻旺相是药轻病重，服之无功

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占药，官爻没有气、子孙旺相，药能胜过病，才有效。如果子孙休囚，官爻反而旺，就是药轻病重，就算吃了，也一定不能奏效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining medicine, if the Pressure line has no force and Output is strong, the medicine can overcome the illness, and then it is effective. If Output is resting or trapped and the Pressure line is strong instead, the medicine is light and the illness heavy, and even if he takes it, it surely cannot take effect.

## 《黄金策·医药》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6991 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Doctors and medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官旺福衰，药饵轻而病重； | 官爻旺、福德衰，药轻而病重； | Pressure strong, Output weak: the medicine is light and the illness heavy; |
