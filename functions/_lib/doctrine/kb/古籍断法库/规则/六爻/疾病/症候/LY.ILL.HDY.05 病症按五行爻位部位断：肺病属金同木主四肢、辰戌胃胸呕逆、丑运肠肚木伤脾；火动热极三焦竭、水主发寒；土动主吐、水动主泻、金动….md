# 病症按五行爻位部位断：肺病属金同木主四肢、辰戌胃胸呕逆、丑运肠肚木伤脾；火动热极三焦竭、水主发寒；土动主吐、水动主泻、金动四肢满闷；木足金头土胸腹火手水脾肾；飞为起因伏为受病；世为动爻则内下受病、应为动爻则外上受病、间爻动受阻隔；易镜云长男受病宜纯震不摇、少女染病宜兑不动

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病症要按五行、爻位、身体部位来断：肺病属金，木主四肢；感受寒邪、痰喘、气弱消瘦；辰戌是胃和胸，生出呕吐逆气；丑运转肠肚，木克伤脾。火动热到极点，三焦枯竭，还有血、心、眼目；水主发寒，是受冷得的；世爻泻泄、虚劳、耳肾衰弱。土动主吐，水动主泻，水动发寒，火动发热，金动四肢和胀闷。木主足，金主头，土主胸腹，火主手，水主脾肾。飞爻、伏爻都旺相时，飞爻是起病的原因，伏爻（原文作「代」）是受病的。世爻是动爻，病在里面下面；应爻是动爻，病在外面上面；间爻动，受阻隔。《易镜》说：长男得病，适宜纯震不摇动；少女染病，适宜兑卦不动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Symptoms are judged by the five phases, the line positions and the parts of the body: lung illness is Metal, with Wood governing the four limbs; catching cold, phlegm and wheezing, qi weak and emaciated; Chen and Xu are the stomach and chest, giving rise to vomiting and retching; Chou moves the bowels and belly, and Wood wounds the spleen. Fire moving: heat to the extreme, the Triple Burner exhausted, and also blood, heart and eyes; Water governs chills, got through cold; the Self line: diarrhoea, consumptive weakness, decline of ears and kidneys. Earth moving governs vomiting, Water moving governs diarrhoea, Water moving brings chills, Fire moving brings fever, Metal moving the four limbs and fullness and oppression. Wood governs the feet, Metal the head, Earth the chest and belly, Fire the hands, Water the spleen and kidneys. When the flying and hidden lines are both strong, the flying line is the cause that begins the illness and the hidden line (the text has 'substitute') is what receives it. If the Self is the moving line, the illness is received inside, below; if the Other is the moving line, outside, above; if the between line moves, there is obstruction. The Mirror of the Changes says: when the eldest son falls ill, it is fitting that the pure Zhen does not shake; when the youngest daughter is infected, that the Dui hexagram does not move.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节按五行、爻位、部位断病，和黄金策「五行推病」、易隐「病症以官鬼为凭」（LY.ILL.YY.11、YY.13）是同一个路子，只是这里更简、更碎，而且有几处和它们不一致：「水主脾肾」把脾也归了水；眉批「巳肠木脾」和歌诀「丑运肠肚木伤脾」的地支不同；「世泻虚劳耳肾衰」的「世泻」没有说清是世爻还是「世」上的泄泻。「气[ ]羸」里的方括号是整理本标的缺字，照抄。「痦痰」的「痦」不常见（白痦是一种皮疹），译作「rash」，不确定。「以代为受病」的「代」疑是「伏」之误：飞爻是起病之因、伏爻是受病之处，和「飞为起因」的说法对得上，这里按字面译并标出。「易镜云」是海底眼引的别的书，火珠林也引了这一句（火珠林末尾多一句「大忌申酉持世，木爻独发者，申为丧车，酉为丧服，木为棺椁耳」）；它的意思是病人是哪一卦（长男是震、少女是兑），那一卦就该纯而不动。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> *Note:* This section judges illness by the five phases, the line positions and the body parts, on the same road as the Huangjin Ce's 'the five phases and illness' and the Yi Yin's 'symptoms by the Pressure line' (LY.ILL.YY.11, YY.13), only terser and more fragmentary, and it disagrees with them in a few places: 'Water governs the spleen and kidneys' also gives the spleen to Water; the marginal comment 'Si the bowels, Wood the spleen' has a different branch from the verse 'Chou moves the bowels and belly, Wood wounds the spleen'; and in 'the Self: diarrhoea, consumptive weakness, decline of ears and kidneys' it is not clear whether it is the Self line or a diarrhoea 'of the Self'. The square brackets in 'qi [ ] emaciated' are the edited text's mark for a missing character, and are copied. The 'wu' of 'wu tan' is uncommon (white 'wu' is a kind of rash) and is rendered 'rash', without certainty. The 'dai' in 'the dai receives the illness' is probably a slip for 'fu' (hidden): the flying line is the cause and the hidden line the place that receives the illness, which matches 'the flying line is the cause', and it is translated literally here and marked. 'The Mirror of the Changes says' is another book quoted by the Haidi Yan, and the Huozhulin quotes the same passage (with one more sentence at its end, 'most feared are Shen or You holding the Self and a Wood line moving alone: Shen is the funeral carriage, You is the mourning dress, Wood is the coffin'); its meaning is that the trigram of the sick person (the eldest son being Zhen, the youngest daughter Dui) should be pure and not moving. The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.
