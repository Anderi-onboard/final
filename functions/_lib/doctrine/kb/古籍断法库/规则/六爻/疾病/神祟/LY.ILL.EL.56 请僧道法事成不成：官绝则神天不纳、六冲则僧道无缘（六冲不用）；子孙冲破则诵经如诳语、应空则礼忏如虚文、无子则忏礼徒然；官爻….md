# 请僧道法事成不成：官绝则神天不纳、六冲则僧道无缘（六冲不用）；子孙冲破则诵经如诳语、应空则礼忏如虚文、无子则忏礼徒然；官爻主设醮之场、无鬼无因果、官空无德无功；子旺则斋坛功德无亏、福德掌醮功程有力；福动化鬼、官来克世则作福反凶（求亲反疏、求荣反辱）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 法事成不成，要看几个关口：官鬼绝，神天不接受；卦是六冲，僧道没有缘分，所以六冲的卦不用。子孙用神管着自家的事，诵经宣偈要念得琳琅悦耳，如果遇到冲破，就只是白诵、成了谎话；僧道所在的应位是占卜人的用神，金经实偈要诚敬地宣扬，如果被冲，就白念、成了虚文；应爻落空，穿着袈裟礼拜也没有用，没有子孙，忏悔礼拜也是徒然。官爻主设醮的场地，没有鬼就没有因果，官空就没有德也没有功；子孙乘旺，斋坛的功德没有亏欠；福德掌管醮事，主事功有力。设斋修善的时候，福动却变成了鬼，就是想亲近反而疏远了；作福祈恩的时候，官鬼来克世爻，就是求荣反而得辱——都是要人感应、用心与天神相合，怎么可以亵渎而不加谨慎呢。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether the rite succeeds depends on several points: if the Pressure line is cut off, the gods and heaven do not accept; if the hexagram is a Six-Clash, monks and Daoists have no affinity, so a Six-Clash hexagram is not used. Output as the Subject governs the affairs of my household, and reciting scriptures and proclaiming verses must be delivered in ringing tones; if it meets clash or break, the reciting is in vain and becomes lying words; the Other position of the monk or Daoist is the Subject of the one divining, and the golden scriptures and true verses must be proclaimed with sincerity and reverence; if it is clashed, the reciting is wasted and becomes an empty text; if the Other line is Void, what use is the robe and the bowing, and if there is no Output, penance and prostration are in vain. The Pressure line governs the site of the ritual; with no ghost there is no cause and effect, and with the Pressure line Void there is no virtue and no merit; with Output riding strength, the merit of the fast altar has no deficiency; Output holds the power of the ritual, and the work has strength. In holding a fast and cultivating good, if Output moves and changes into a ghost, then seeking closeness one grows distant; in performing blessing rites and praying for grace, if the Pressure line comes to control the Self, one seeks honour and gets shame instead; all of it asks for response and for the heart to accord with the heavenly gods, so how may one profane it without taking care?

> [!note] 按
> 这一节接上一条（LY.ILL.EL.55）：上一条看请来的僧道是贤是愚，这一条看这场法事能不能成。它的判据基本是熟悉的：官鬼是神天的受纳（官绝则神天不纳）、子孙是诵经念偈的功德（子孙被冲破则徒诵）、应爻是僧道本人（应空则礼忏无益）、六冲是缘分散（六冲不用），福动化鬼、官来克世则作福反凶。「六冲」在这里的意思是「缘散」，和别处的用法（六冲卦病易消，LY.ILL.EL.22）不同——这里是说法事不宜遇六冲。「神天不纳因官绝」的「官绝」是官鬼绝。「鹤憋忏礼」的「鹤憋」不成词，译文里标出；「设旧」疑是「设醮」的误写，译作「holding the ritual」；「修齐」是「修斋」；「鬼煞临斋坛之事」不成句，译文标出；「谄之而不蒙福媚之而反见遭用人感应」一句文字散乱，译文只译出能读的部分。这一节的说理是：要人感应、要用心，所以不能亵渎神明而不谨慎。 这一段里神、经、法事的名字，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This section follows the previous entry (LY.ILL.EL.55): the previous one looks at whether the monk or Daoist invited is worthy or foolish, and this one looks at whether the rite can succeed. Its criteria are mostly familiar: the Pressure line is the acceptance by the gods and heaven (with the Pressure line cut off, the gods and heaven do not accept); Output is the merit of reciting scriptures and verses (with Output clashed or broken the reciting is in vain); the Other line is the monk or Daoist himself (with the Other line Void, penance is of no use); the Six-Clash scatters affinity (the Six-Clash is not used); and with Output moving and changing into a ghost, or the Pressure line controlling the Self, blessing rites bring misfortune instead. 'Six-Clash' here means 'affinity scattered', which differs from its use elsewhere (a Six-Clash hexagram makes an illness easy to dispel, LY.ILL.EL.22): here it says that a rite should not meet the Six-Clash. The 'guan jue' in 'because the Pressure line is cut off' is the Pressure line cut off. 'He bie chan li' contains 'he bie', which is not a word, and the translation marks it; 'she jiu' is probably a slip for 'she jiao' (holding the ritual), and is translated as 'holding the ritual'; 'xiu qi' is 'xiu zhai' (cultivating the fast); 'the ghost killing sits on the affairs of the fast altar' is not a sentence, and the translation marks it; the sentence 'flattering it brings no blessing, fawning on it one instead meets ... response' is scattered in wording, and only the readable part is translated. The reasoning of this section is: one must have response and must have the heart, and so one may not profane the gods without taking care. Names of gods, spirits, scriptures and rites in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; the book does not explain them, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
