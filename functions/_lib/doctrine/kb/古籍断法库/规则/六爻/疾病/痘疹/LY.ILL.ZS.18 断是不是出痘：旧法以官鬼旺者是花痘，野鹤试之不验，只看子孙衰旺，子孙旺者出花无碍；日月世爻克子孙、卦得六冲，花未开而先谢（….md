# 断是不是出痘：旧法以官鬼旺者是花痘，野鹤试之不验，只看子孙衰旺，子孙旺者出花无碍；日月世爻克子孙、卦得六冲，花未开而先谢（例：占子发热是痘否，兑为泽，次日再占大过之涣）

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问发热是不是出痘：旧法说官鬼旺的就是出花痘，野鹤试过不灵验，说那是揣摩出来的说法，只看子孙的衰旺就行，子孙旺的，出花也没有妨碍。子孙被日月、世爻克，卦又是六冲，是花还没开就先谢了，是不祥的兆头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask whether a fever is smallpox: the old method says a strong Pressure line means smallpox is flowering; the Old Man tried it and it did not prove true, saying it was a guess, and one need only look at whether Output is strong or weak: if Output is strong, no harm comes even if the flowers come out. Output controlled by the Day, the Month and the Self line, with the hexagram Six-Clash, means the flowers fade before they open, an ill omen.

> [!warning] 打架
> 怎样断是不是出痘：旧法（书里没有指名）以官鬼旺相的为花痘；野鹤试了不验，说是揣摩之说，只看子孙衰旺。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to judge whether it is smallpox: the old method (which the book does not name) takes a strong Pressure line as smallpox in flower; the Old Man tried it without result, calls it guesswork, and looks only at whether Output is strong or weak.

> [!note] 按
> 「花痘」「出花」都是出天花、痘疹。这个卦例的后半（医人从哪个方位请，请到杨姓医生）在 LY.ILL.ZS.34，那里用的是同一个孩子的后几个卦。痘疹章（LY.ILL.ZS.19 起）里另有以鬼爻为用神断出花的办法。
>
> *Note:* 'Flowering' and 'coming into flower' both mean the coming out of smallpox and pox eruptions. The second half of this case (from which direction to call the doctor, and calling a doctor with the surname Yang) is in LY.ILL.ZS.34, which uses the later hexagrams of the same child. The chapter on smallpox (from LY.ILL.ZS.19) has a separate way of judging the coming of the flowers with the Pressure line as the Subject.
