# 鬼在坎宫中满血虚兼湿毒，在离卦内虚眼赤及尿黄

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻在坎宫，病是中满、血虚，兼有湿毒；在离卦，病是内虚、眼红、尿黄。旧注分得更细：坎宫属水，火鬼主血虚、水鬼主湿毒；离宫属火，火鬼主眼红、水鬼主尿黄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Pressure line in the Kan palace, the illness is fullness in the middle and blood deficiency, together with damp toxin; in the Li hexagram it is inner deficiency, red eyes and yellow urine. The old commentary divides it more finely: the Kan palace belongs to Water, where a Fire Pressure means blood deficiency and a Water Pressure damp toxin; the Li palace belongs to Fire, where a Fire Pressure means red eyes and a Water Pressure yellow urine.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6773 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 坎象中满，故主中满； | 坎的卦象中间是实的，所以主中满； | The Kan image is full in the middle, so it means fullness in the middle; |
> | 离象中虚，故主内虚。 | 离的卦象中间是空的，所以主内虚。 | the Li image is empty in the middle, so it means inner deficiency. |
> | 然坎宫属水，火鬼主血虚，水鬼主湿毒。 | 不过坎宫属水，火鬼主血虚，水鬼主湿毒。 | But the Kan palace belongs to Water: a Fire Pressure means blood deficiency, a Water Pressure means damp toxin. |
> | 离宫属火，或火官主眼赤，水鬼主尿黄。 | 离宫属火，火鬼主眼红，水鬼主尿黄。 | The Li palace belongs to Fire: a Fire Pressure means red eyes, a Water Pressure means yellow urine. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一联卜筮正宗的王洪绪注里没有。旧注里最后一句写作「或火官主眼赤」，「或」应是「火」之误，译文按上文「火鬼」对「水鬼」译。
>
> *Note:* This couplet has no note by Wang Hongxu in the Bushi Zhengzong. The last sentence of the old commentary reads 'or the Fire Pressure means red eyes'; the 'or' is probably a slip, and the translation follows the contrast between the Fire Pressure and the Water Pressure in the sentence before.
