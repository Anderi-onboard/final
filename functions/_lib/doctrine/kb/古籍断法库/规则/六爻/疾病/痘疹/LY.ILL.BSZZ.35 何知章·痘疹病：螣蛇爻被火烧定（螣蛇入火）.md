# 何知章·痘疹病：螣蛇爻被火烧定（螣蛇入火）

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 怎么知道这家有痘疹的病：螣蛇爻遇到火，被火烧着，就断定了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* How to know that a household has the pox: the Coiling Snake line is burned by Fire, and that settles it.

## 《卜筮正宗·赋文三篇·何知章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9845 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Three rhapsodies: the "how to know" rhyme", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 何知人家痘疹病？ | 怎么知道这家有痘疹的病？ | How can one know that a household has the pox? |
> | 鰧蛇爻被火烧定。 | 螣蛇爻被火烧着，就断定了。 | The Coiling Snake line is burned by Fire, and that settles it. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「何知章」一问一答，一共问了三四十件事（父母子孙的病、有没有子孙、讼事、财富、田地、屋宇、坟墓、六畜、口舌、盗贼……），都是一家的事；这里只收与病、死、鬼怪有关的。卜筮大全里它是「十六、何知章」，卜筮正宗里它在《赋文三篇》中；两本字句略有出入。这一句口诀只说螣蛇遇火，没有说是螣蛇落在火爻上还是火爻克螣蛇的爻位；断易天机的《何知章》里有一句「鰧蛇入火火烧定，瘟气麻痘病」，是同一个意思，说得更明白（螣蛇落在火上）。
>
> *Note:* The 'how to know' rhyme asks and answers some thirty or forty things (illnesses of parents and children, whether there are children, lawsuits, wealth, land, houses, graves, livestock, quarrels, thieves and more), all affairs of a household; only those to do with illness, death and ghostly happenings are taken here. In the Bushi Daquan it is chapter 16, 'the how-to-know rhyme'; in the Bushi Zhengzong it is among the three rhapsodies; the two texts differ a little in wording. This line of the rhyme says only that the Coiling Snake meets Fire and does not say whether the Snake falls on a Fire line or a Fire line burns the Snake's place; the Duanyi Tianji's version of the rhyme has 'the Coiling Snake entering Fire, burned by fire: epidemic qi, measles and pox', the same meaning said more plainly (the Snake falls on Fire).
