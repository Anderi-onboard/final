# 六亲爻变·疾病：官化官兮病未安(谓之化去)；官化文书官未顺，纵能忧病还为吉(病凶亦吉)；兄化官占病难医哭泣临(鬼能克子)；子化官兮防祸患，占疾忧疑总不中(医药受克)

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 海底眼的六亲爻变一节（「甲化乙」是甲爻发动、变出乙爻），带眉批的讲病的句子有四处：官鬼化官鬼，病没有好（眉批：叫化去）；官鬼化父母，官还不顺，就是忧着病也还是吉（眉批：病凶也吉）；兄弟化官鬼，占病难医、哭泣临门（眉批：鬼能克子）；子孙化官鬼，要防祸患，占病忧疑总是不中（眉批：医药受克）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the section on the six relations changing in the Haidi Yan ('A changes into B' means line A moves and changes out line B), with its marginal comments there are four places that speak of illness: Pressure changing into Pressure, the illness has not mended (comment: this is called 'turning away'); Pressure changing into Resource, the official matter is not yet smooth, but even worrying about illness it is still auspicious (comment: illness ominous is also auspicious); Peer changing into Pressure, in divining illness it is hard to treat and weeping comes (comment: the ghost can control Output); Output changing into Pressure, guard against misfortune, and in divining illness doubt and worry never hit the mark (comment: medicine is controlled).

> [!note] 按
> 「化去」是眉批在好几处用的词（「财化财兮……（谓之化去）」「子化子兮……（谓之化去）」），指化出同类、变而不变。「官化文书」的「文书」是父母爻（文书属父母）；「纵能忧病还为吉」接在「官化文书官未顺」后面，字面是「纵然担忧着病，也还吉」，眉批「病凶亦吉」只有四个字，没有展开，译文照字面。「兄化官兮休下状」后面的眉批「事元十象」意思不清，这里不引。「占疾忧疑总不中」的「不中」是不应验、不符合，「医药受克」是眉批给的原因：子孙化官鬼，子孙（医药）被官鬼克。卜筮全书的六亲变化歌（LY.ILL.BSQS.07）讲「化鬼忧病产」「化财占病凶」，和这里的几条不是同一批句子。这一节在原书里有校者的话「此篇各节明显错乱」，指节与节的次序，这里没有受影响。
>
> *Note:* 'Turning away' is a word the marginal comments use in several places ('Wealth changing into Wealth ... (it is called turning away)', 'Output changing into Output ... (it is called turning away)'), meaning changing into its own kind, a change that is no change. The 'document' of 'Pressure changing into Resource' is the Resource line (documents belong to Resource); 'even if one worries about illness, it is still auspicious' follows 'the official matter is not yet smooth', its literal sense is 'even though worrying about illness, it is also auspicious', and the comment 'illness ominous is also auspicious' is only four characters and is not expanded, and is translated literally. The comment 'the matter at root ten images' after 'Peer changing into Pressure, do not file a suit' is unclear and is not quoted. The 'does not hit' of 'doubt and worry never hit the mark' is not borne out or not fitting, and 'medicine is controlled' is the reason given by the comment: Output changes into Pressure, and Output (medicine) is controlled by Pressure. The verses on the six relations changing in the Bushi Quanshu (LY.ILL.BSQS.07) say 'changing into the ghost, worry for illness and childbirth' and 'changing into Wealth, divining illness is ominous', which are not the same lines as these. The book has an editor's remark at the head of this section that 'the sections of this part are plainly out of order', which concerns the order of the sections and does not affect this.
