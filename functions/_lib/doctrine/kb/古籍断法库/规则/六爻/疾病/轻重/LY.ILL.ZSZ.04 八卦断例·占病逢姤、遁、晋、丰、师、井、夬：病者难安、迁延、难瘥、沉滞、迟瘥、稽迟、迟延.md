# 八卦断例·占病逢姤、遁、晋、丰、师、井、夬：病者难安、迁延、难瘥、沉滞、迟瘥、稽迟、迟延

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 周易尚占八卦断例里，这七个卦的断辞说病难好、拖得久：姤「病者难安」，遁「病者迁延」，晋「病者难瘥」，丰「病人沉滞」，师「病人迟瘥」，井「问病稽迟」，夬「病讼迟延」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the judgments by example of the Zhouyi Shangzhan the line of each of these seven hexagrams says that illness is hard to mend and drags on: Gou, 'the sick will find it hard to be at peace'; Dun, 'the sick one drags on'; Jin, 'the sick will find it hard to recover'; Feng, 'the sick one is weighed down and lingering'; Shi, 'the sick recover late'; Jing, 'asking about illness: delay and slowness'; Guai, 'illness and lawsuit are delayed'.

> [!warning] 打架
> 丰、夬两卦的轻重，几本书说得不一样。别的书把丰、夬列为占病的凶卦，甚至是「十死」：阐奥歌章第三首（LY.ILL.CA.03）、第五首（LY.ILL.CA.06）、卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.15）、天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.06）、增删卜易转述的十卦（LY.ILL.ZS.01）里都有丰和夬；周易尚占这里丰只是「病人沉滞」，夬只是「病讼迟延」，是病拖得久，没有说死。同样，丰在这里是「利官利贵、婚姻有成」的卦。程度不同，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books do not agree on how heavy Feng and Guai are. Other books list Feng and Guai among the ominous hexagrams for illness, even among the 'ten deaths': the third (LY.ILL.CA.03) and fifth (LY.ILL.CA.06) poems of the Chan'ao Gezhang, the passage of the Bushi Yuangui (LY.ILL.DYTJ.15), the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.06) and the ten hexagrams reported in the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.01) all have Feng and Guai; here in the Zhouyi Shangzhan Feng is only 'the sick one is weighed down and lingering' and Guai only 'illness and lawsuit are delayed', that is, illness that drags on, with no word of death. Likewise Feng is here a hexagram of 'favourable to office and to nobles, marriage is achieved'. The degree differs, and the books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这七行的病句都是「拖」：难安、迁延、难瘥、沉滞、迟瘥、稽迟、迟延，没有一个说死；姤的「病者难安」最重（难得平安），夬是病和官司一起拖。「沉滞」是沉重、拖着不好，「稽迟」是拖延迟缓，「迁延」是一拖再拖。姤卦在原书里写作「媾」（「媾者遇也」），这里照原文；「卜筮行人 端的在路」是说占行人，他确实还在路上，和病无关。「求官谒贵」（井）这个词组只有事项、没有断语，原文如此。「财平稍平」（晋）意思不清，译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* The illness phrases of these seven lines all speak of dragging on: hard to be at peace, protracted, hard to recover, weighed down, late to recover, delay and slowness, delayed; none speaks of death. The heaviest is 'the sick will find it hard to be at peace' of Gou (hard to gain peace), and for Guai illness and the lawsuit drag on together. 'Weighed down and lingering' is heavy and not getting better, 'delay and slowness' is dragging and slow, and 'drags on' is delay upon delay. In the original book Gou is written with the 'union' character ('that character means meeting'), and the line is quoted as it stands; 'as for divining about a traveller, he is indeed on the road' is about a traveller and not about illness. The phrase 'seeking office, calling on nobles' (Jing) has an affair but no verdict, as in the original. 'Wealth is level, only slightly so' (Jin) is unclear in sense and is translated literally.
