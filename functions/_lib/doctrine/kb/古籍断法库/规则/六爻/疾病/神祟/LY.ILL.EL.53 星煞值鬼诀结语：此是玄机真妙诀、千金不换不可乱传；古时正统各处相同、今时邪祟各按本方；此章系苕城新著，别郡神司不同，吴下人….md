# 星煞值鬼诀结语：此是玄机真妙诀、千金不换不可乱传；古时正统各处相同、今时邪祟各按本方；此章系苕城新著，别郡神司不同，吴下人可依此断，在他方问卜须另行细查

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 这套用星煞认鬼神的办法，是玄机里真正妙的诀窍，千金也不换，不要乱传。古时候正的（正统的）大概全天下都一样，现在的邪祟却各按本地不同；这一章是苕城新写的，别的郡的神司不同，凡是吴下的人，可以照这个来断，如果在别的地方问卜，还应该另外详细查。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* This way of recognising ghosts and spirits by the stars and killings is the truly marvellous secret of the mysterious mechanism, not to be traded for a thousand pieces of gold, and not to be spread about. In antiquity the orthodox ones were, on the whole, the same all under heaven, but now evil hauntings each follow their own locality; this chapter was newly written at Tiaocheng, and the gods and offices of other prefectures differ, so that for all people of Wu one may judge by this, and if divining in another place, one should look into it separately in detail.

> [!note] 按
> 这是整个「搜决神鬼」一章和它的四段「总论」的适用范围声明：这套神名和星煞，书里自己说是苕城新著、只适用吴下（别的郡的神司不同，别的地方问卜要另外详查）。所以把它们当作通用规则用，是超出了书自己划定的范围。「古之正人盖同天下」的「正人」文字含混，很可能是「正神」（和下句「邪祟」相对），这里按「正统的」译；「苕城」按字面译作 Tiaocheng（苕溪一带的城）。「玄机真妙诀，千金不换乱传扬」是歌诀的结尾，收的是本节的星煞诀。 这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This is the book's statement of the scope of the whole 'searching out ghosts and spirits' chapter and its four sections of 'general discourse': the book says itself that these names of gods and these stars and killings are newly written at Tiaocheng and apply only to Wu (the gods and offices of other prefectures differ, and in other places one must look into it separately in detail). To use them as general rules therefore goes beyond the scope the book itself draws. The 'zheng ren' in 'the orthodox ones of old' is loose in wording and is most likely 'zheng shen' (orthodox gods, against the 'evil hauntings' of the next line), and is translated as 'the orthodox ones'; 'Tiaocheng' is rendered literally as the town on the Tiao River. 'The true marvellous secret of the mysterious mechanism, not to be traded for a thousand pieces of gold' is the verse that closes, and it closes this section of the stars and killings. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
