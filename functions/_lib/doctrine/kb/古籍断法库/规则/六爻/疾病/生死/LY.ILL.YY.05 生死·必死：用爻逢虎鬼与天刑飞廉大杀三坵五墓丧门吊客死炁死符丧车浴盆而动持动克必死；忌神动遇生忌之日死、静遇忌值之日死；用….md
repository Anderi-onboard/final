# 生死·必死：用爻逢虎鬼与天刑飞廉大杀三坵五墓丧门吊客死炁死符丧车浴盆而动持动克必死；忌神动遇生忌之日死、静遇忌值之日死；用随鬼入墓、财动助鬼伤用、世应双空、财旺用空、鬼生命绝、鬼动化墓、月卦化墓、用化墓绝、用逢月绝皆死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻遇到白虎、鬼，以及天刑、飞廉、大杀、三坵五墓、丧门、吊客、死炁、死符、丧车、浴盆这些凶煞，它们发动而临在用爻上或发动来克用爻，一定死。忌神发动的，遇到生忌神的那天死；忌神安静的，遇到忌神当值的那天死。此外用爻随鬼入墓、财爻发动帮鬼伤用、世应都落空、财旺而用爻落空、鬼旺而命爻绝、鬼动变入墓、月卦变入墓、用爻变入墓绝、用爻遇月绝，都是死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line meets the Tiger, the ghost, and the malign stars Heavenly Punishment, Feilian, Great Killing, Three Mounds and Five Tombs, Mourning Gate, Guest of Condolence, Death Qi, Death Tally, Funeral Carriage and Bath Basin, and they move to sit on it or move to control it, he will surely die. If the Adversary moves, he dies on the day that generates the Adversary; if the Adversary is still, he dies on the day the Adversary is on duty. Besides, he dies if the Subject line follows the ghost into a tomb, Wealth moves to help the ghost and harm the Subject line, the Self and the Other are both Void, Wealth is strong and the Subject line Void, the ghost is in Birth and the destiny in Extinction, the ghost moves and changes into a tomb, the month-hexagram changes into a tomb, the Subject line changes into a tomb or Extinction, or the Subject line meets Extinction in the Month.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是「生死」段里断死的爻象；下一条断死的卦象、再下一条断死日。「虎」是白虎，「鬼」是官鬼；天刑、飞廉、大杀、三坵五墓、丧门、吊客、死炁、死符、丧车、浴盆都是凶的神煞，书里没有再解释。「月卦」书里没有解释，按字面译作月卦（应是按月起的卦身一类的说法）。两个断例挂在这里：临之损（用爻化绝又岁月绝再遭日冲，木日死）、涣之姤（用爻伏藏值旬空，到乙亥日透出才受忌伤而死）。
>
> *Note:* This entry gives the line-images that judge death in the section on life and death; the next gives the hexagram-images and the one after the days of death. 'Tiger' is the White Tiger and 'ghost' the Pressure line; Heavenly Punishment, Feilian, Great Killing, Three Mounds and Five Tombs, Mourning Gate, Guest of Condolence, Death Qi, Death Tally, Funeral Carriage and Bath Basin are all malign spirit-stars which the book does not explain further. 'Month-hexagram' is not explained in the book and is rendered literally (probably something of the kind of the Body lines taken by the month). Two cases hang here: Lin into Sun (the Subject line changing into Extinction, in Extinction in the Year and Month as well, and clashed by the day, dying on a Wood day) and Huan into Gou (the Subject line hidden and in Void, coming out on the Yihai day, harmed by the Adversary and dying).
