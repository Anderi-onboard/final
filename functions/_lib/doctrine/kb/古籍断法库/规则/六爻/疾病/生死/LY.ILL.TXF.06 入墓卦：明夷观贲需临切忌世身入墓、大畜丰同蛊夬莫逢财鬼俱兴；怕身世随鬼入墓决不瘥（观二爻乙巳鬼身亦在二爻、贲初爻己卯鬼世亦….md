# 入墓卦：明夷观贲需临切忌世身入墓、大畜丰同蛊夬莫逢财鬼俱兴；怕身世随鬼入墓决不瘥（观二爻乙巳鬼身亦在二爻、贲初爻己卯鬼世亦在初爻）；苍屏云八宫化入墓——乾化艮父丧、坤化巽母亡、震化坤长男厄、巽化坤长女当、坎化巽中男厄、离化乾中女卒、艮化巽少男倾、兑化艮少女卒，十人得病九人故；乾化艮子占父谓之应题大凶；财鬼不动未必丧亡、财鬼空亡虽病不死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 明夷、观、贲、需、临，切忌世和身入墓；大畜、丰、同（人）、蛊、夬，不要遇到财和鬼一起兴动。注说：占病怕身、世跟着鬼入墓，一定治不好。比如风地观的二爻乙巳，鬼和身也在二爻，如果入了墓，就是身随鬼入墓；山火贲卦初爻己卯，鬼和世也在初爻，如果入了墓，就是世随鬼入墓；上面两卦举作例子，其余卦照这样仿。苍屏说：乾化入艮，父亲遭丧；坤化入巽，母亲忧亡；震化入坤，长男有厄；巽化入坤，长女当灾；坎化入巽，中男遭厄；离化入乾，中女暴卒；艮化入风（巽），少男倾倒；兑化入山（艮），少女暴卒；这是八宫化来入墓，十个人得病九个亡故。上面说的卦墓，不能单说乾化入艮墓只忧父亲的亡，而要看占病都不吉；假如儿子占父亲，遇到乾化艮，叫做应题，是大凶。大畜等五个卦，占病大忌；财和鬼不动，不一定死；财和鬼空亡，虽病也不死；必须把它的条理理清了，才可以断为全凶。上文所说的财鬼发动，也有吉凶的分别：比如雷火丰卦，鬼动忌占兄弟，财动忌占父母和自己本人；妻子占丈夫，终究没有凶；其余卦仿此。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Mingyi, Guan, Bi (Adornment), Xu and Lin: most to be avoided is the Self and the Body entering a tomb; Daxu, Feng, Tong(ren), Gu and Guai: do not meet Wealth and the ghost both rising. The commentary says: in divining illness one fears the Body and the Self following the ghost into a tomb, and then there is surely no recovery. For example, in Feng-Di Guan the second line Yisi has the ghost and the Body also on the second line; if it enters a tomb, that is the Body following the ghost into a tomb; in the hexagram Bi (Adornment), Mountain over Fire, the first line Jimao has the ghost and the Self also on the first line, and if it enters a tomb, that is the Self following the ghost into a tomb; these two hexagrams are given as examples and the others follow. Cangping says: Qian changing into Gen, the father is in mourning; Kun changing into Xun, the mother worried to death; Zhen changing into Kun, calamity for the eldest son; Xun changing into Kun, the eldest daughter takes it; Kan changing into Xun, calamity for the middle son; Li changing into Qian, the middle daughter dies suddenly; Gen changing into the wind (Xun), the youngest son topples; Dui changing into the mountain (Gen), the youngest daughter dies suddenly; these are the eight palaces changing into a tomb, and of ten who fall ill nine die. As for the tomb hexagrams above, one may not take Qian changing into the Gen tomb as only a worry for the father's death, but must look at illness in all of them as inauspicious; suppose a son divines about his father and meets Qian changing into Gen: it is called 'answering the topic' and is greatly ominous. The five hexagrams such as Daxu are greatly to be avoided in divining illness; if Wealth and the ghost do not move, death is not certain; if Wealth and the ghost are Void, though ill he will not die; one must grasp its threads before judging it wholly ominous. The Wealth and ghost moving mentioned above also have a division of good and ill: for example in the hexagram Feng, Thunder over Fire, the ghost moving is to be avoided in divining about brothers, Wealth moving in divining about parents and oneself; a wife divining about her husband has in the end no ill; the other hexagrams follow this.

> [!warning] 打架
> 周易尚占和文王金钱课按卦名断病，对这里点名的几个卦说得相反或轻重不同：观，周易尚占说「财聚病痊」，文王金钱课说「占病即愈」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.02、WWJQ.03）；贲，周易尚占说「病安」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.03）；大畜，周易尚占说「病脱」，文王金钱课说「疾病口舌渐渐磨」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.02、WWJQ.03）；丰，文王金钱课说「疾病渐好」，周易尚占说「病人沉滞」（LY.ILL.WWJQ.03、ZSZ.04）；夬，周易尚占说「病讼迟延」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.04）；临，周易尚占说「病犯崇侵」，文王金钱课补录部分说「疾病即痊」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.07、WWJQ.05）。不过天玄赋这里说的是有条件的：观、贲、需、临是世身入墓才忌，大畜、丰、同、蛊、夬是财鬼俱兴才忌，不是见到卦名就凶。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The Zhouyi Shangzhan and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke judge illness by the name of the hexagram, and for several of the hexagrams named here they say the opposite, or a lighter thing: for Guan the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'wealth gathers and the illness is cured' and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says 'divining illness, it is cured at once' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.02, WWJQ.03); for Bi the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the illness is settled' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.03); for Daxu (Great Accumulation) the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the illness is shed' and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says 'illness and quarrels are gradually worn away' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.02, WWJQ.03); for Feng the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says 'the illness gradually gets better' and the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the sick are held fast' (LY.ILL.WWJQ.03, ZSZ.04); for Guai the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'illness and lawsuit are delayed' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.04); for Lin the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the illness offends a curse that attacks' and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke, in its supplementary part, says 'the illness is cured at once' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.07, WWJQ.05). The Tianxuan Fu here, however, speaks with a condition: Guan, Bi, Xu (Waiting) and Lin are to be feared only when the Self and the Body enter a tomb, and Daxu, Feng, Tongren, Gu and Guai only when Wealth and Pressure both rise, so that the name of the hexagram alone does not make it ominous. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节说的是「入墓卦」：两句赋点出十个卦（明夷、观、贲、需、临是「世身入墓」，大畜、丰、同、蛊、夬是「财鬼俱兴」），注里先举观、贲两例讲什么叫「身随鬼入墓」「世随鬼入墓」，再引苍屏的一首歌诀，讲八宫里各卦化入墓（乾化艮、坤化巽、震化坤、巽化坤、坎化巽、离化乾、艮化风、兑化山）对应家里的哪一位亲人有灾——「艮化风内」「兑化山中」的「风」是巽、「山」是艮。「应题」这里译作「answering the topic」，按字面，意思大概是卦象应了所问的人。「同」按上下文是同人。「条贯」是条理、头绪，「必须把其条贯」是说要把这些条件都对上了才可以断为全凶。这里用的是卦名代表一类：文字里提到的卦（观、贲、丰）是举例，「余卦仿此」。注末原文还有一段「虎易评注」，是今人加的，本库不引。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* This section is about the 'tomb hexagrams': two lines of verse name ten hexagrams (Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin as 'the Self and Body entering a tomb', and Daxu, Feng, Tong, Gu and Guai as 'Wealth and the ghost both rising'); the commentary first gives the examples of Guan and Bi to say what 'the Body following the ghost into a tomb' and 'the Self following the ghost into a tomb' mean, and then quotes a verse of Cangping's on which relative in the family is in calamity when each hexagram of the eight palaces changes into a tomb (Qian into Gen, Kun into Xun, Zhen into Kun, Xun into Kun, Kan into Xun, Li into Qian, Gen into the wind, Dui into the mountain) — the 'wind' of 'Gen changing into the wind' is Xun and the 'mountain' of 'Dui changing into the mountain' is Gen. 'Answering the topic' is translated literally, and probably means that the hexagram answers to the person asked about. 'Tong' is Tongren from the context. 'Tiao guan' is orderliness or thread, and 'one must grasp its threads' says that all these conditions must fit before it may be judged wholly ominous. The hexagram names stand for a kind here: the hexagrams mentioned in the text (Guan, Bi, Feng) are examples, and 'the other hexagrams follow this'. At the end of the commentary the original has a further passage, 'Huyi's commentary', added by a modern hand, which this library does not quote. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
