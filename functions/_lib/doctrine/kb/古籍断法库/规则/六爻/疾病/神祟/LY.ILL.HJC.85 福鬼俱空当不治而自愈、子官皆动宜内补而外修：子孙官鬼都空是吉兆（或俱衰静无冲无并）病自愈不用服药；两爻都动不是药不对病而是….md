# 福鬼俱空当不治而自愈、子官皆动宜内补而外修：子孙官鬼都空是吉兆（或俱衰静无冲无并）病自愈不用服药；两爻都动不是药不对病而是神祟作祸，须服汤药又祈祷鬼神才愈，俗谓外修内补

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病，子孙、官鬼两爻都空，是吉兆；或者两爻都衰弱安静、没有冲也没有并，这病自己会好，不用吃药。如果两爻都动，不是药不对病，而是有神祟隔在中间（王洪绪：神祟在作祸），所以没有功效，必须又吃汤药、又祈祷鬼神才会痊愈，俗话叫「外修内补」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness, when the Output and Pressure lines are both Void it is a lucky sign; or if both are weak and still, with neither clash nor joining, the illness heals of itself and no medicine need be taken. If both lines move, it is not that the medicine does not fit the illness but that spirits or a haunting stand between (Wang Hongxu: are working mischief), and so it does no good; he must take decoctions and also pray to the ghosts and spirits to be cured, which the common saying calls 'cultivate without and nourish within'.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2470 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占病子官二爻俱空，乃吉兆也，或俱衰静无冲无并者，其病自愈，不用服药。 | 占病，子孙、官鬼两爻都空，是吉兆，或者两爻都衰弱安静、没有冲也没有并的，这病自己会好，不用吃药。 | In divining illness, when the Output and Pressure lines are both Void it is a lucky sign; or if both are weak and still, with neither clash nor joining, the illness heals of itself and no medicine need be taken. |
> | 若二爻俱动，此非药不对病，乃是神祟作祸，故曰无功，必须祈祷服药方得病痊，俗所谓外修内补也。 | 如果两爻都动，这不是药不对病，而是神祟在作祸，所以说没有功效，必须又祈祷又吃药才能痊愈，就是俗话说的外修内补。 | If both lines move, this is not that the medicine does not fit the illness but that spirits or a haunting are working mischief, hence it is said to do no good; he must pray and take medicine to be cured, which is the common 'cultivate without and nourish within'. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「并」大概是日辰、月建或别的爻和它同支、并起来帮它。旧注说神祟「间隔」（隔在药和病之间），王洪绪说神祟「作祸」。「外修内补」是外面修功德祈祷、里面吃药调养。
>
> *Note:* 'Joining' (bing) is probably the Day, the Month or another line of the same branch joining up to help it. The old commentary says the haunting 'stands between' (between the medicine and the illness), Wang Hongxu that it 'works mischief'. 'Cultivate without and nourish within' is to cultivate merit and pray outside while taking medicine and recuperating inside.
