# 月将值日·天罡（月建辰）占病：心腹膨胀卧高床、寒热往来须作痛；天罗地网煞山司木下廊边鬼；辰宫卦断半天午酉降祸、家先三五郎、旺神有愿急解谢、退送墓煞

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在辰、叫天罡的时候占病：这天得的病，心腹胀满，卧在床上，寒热交替，必定疼痛。作祟的是天罗地网煞，山司、木下、廊边的鬼，还有正通和伤亡鬼。「辰宫卦断」那一段说：半空的、午酉方位的鬼一同降祸，还有家先和三郎、五郎；旺相的神有愿没还，赶紧解愿酬谢，把墓煞退送出去，免得病人惶恐不安。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on Chen, named Tiangang, divining illness: an illness taken on this day, the heart and belly swollen, lying in his bed, chills and fever alternating, bound to be painful. The ones at work are the Heaven's-Net and Earth's-Net Killer, the Mountain Official, the one under the wood and the ghosts by the corridor, and also the Zhengtong and the dead by injury. The passage 'the verdict for the Chen-palace hexagrams' says: the ghosts of the half-sky and of Wu and You send down misfortune together, and there are also the household ancestors and the Third and Fifth Lords; a strong spirit has a vow outstanding, and it is to be released quickly with thanks, and the Tomb Killer sent away, to spare the sick person distress.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。「此日得病值天」这一句只有六个字，像是脱了「罡」字（天罡），译文把这一点写在括号里，是读法。「正通」书里没有解释，有可能是「五通」（民间的一种神）之误，但原文没有说，译文只照字面写「正通」。「廊边鬼」「三五郎」（三郎、五郎）「半天午酉」书里都没有解释；「墓煞」是一种凶煞。「旺神有愿」同 GLJ.03。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. The line 'an illness taken on this day falls upon Heaven' has only six characters and looks as if the character for 'Gang' (Tiangang) has dropped out, and the translation puts this in brackets, which is a reading. The book does not explain 'the Zhengtong', and it may be a miswriting of 'the Wutong' (a kind of popular spirit), but the original does not say so, and the translation writes 'the Zhengtong' literally. 'The ghosts by the corridor', 'the Third and Fifth Lords' and 'the half-sky, Wu and You' are not explained in the book; 'the Tomb Killer' is a kind of fierce killer. For 'a strong spirit has a vow outstanding' see GLJ.03.
