# 身临福德势虽险而堪医有两说：自占病子孙持世不药而痊，临空破者实空实破之日愈；代占六亲之病不必看用神衰旺即知其安，因子孙是喜悦之神（觉子：占父母病、妻占夫病子孙动反不是喜悦之神）

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 身上临着福德，病势虽凶险也可以医治，有两种情形。自己占病：子孙持世，不吃药也会痊愈；临空亡、月破的，到填实空、填实破的那天就好。替别人占六亲的病：不必看用神旺衰，就知道他平安，因为子孙是喜悦的神，只要旺相，如果在卦中发动，也是吉庆。觉子补充：占父母的病，子孙持世又发动，会克掉父母的元神；妻子占丈夫的病，子孙动会伤丈夫，这些情形它都不是喜悦的神，要通变来看。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the body sitting on Output-virtue, though the danger looks great the illness can be treated, and there are two cases. Divining one's own illness: with Output holding the Self line, one recovers without medicine; if it sits on Void or Month-break, he recovers on the day the Void or the break is filled. Divining a relation's illness for another: one need not look at whether the Subject is strong or weak to know he will be safe, because Output is the spirit of joy, and so long as it is strong, if it moves in the hexagram it is also a sign of good fortune. Juezi adds: in divining a parent's illness, an Output line that holds the Self line and moves controls away the Support of the Resource line; when a wife divines her husband's illness, a moving Output harms the husband; in these cases it is not the spirit of joy, and one must look at it flexibly.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和上一条（LY.ILL.ZS.09）是一对：那里讲鬼持世，这里讲子孙持世，都拆成自占和代占。黄金策的对应句是 LY.ILL.HJC.35；那里把「身」解作用爻或月卦身，野鹤这里讲的是子孙持世，看的是世爻。「填实」是落空的爻等到自己的地支值日，把空填上。
>
> *Note:* This entry is a pair with the previous one (LY.ILL.ZS.09): there Pressure holds the Self line, here Output does, and both are split into divining oneself and divining another. The matching line of the Huangjin Ce is LY.ILL.HJC.35, which explains the 'body' as the Subject line or the month's hexagram Body; what the Old Man treats here is Output holding the Self line, and so he looks at the Self line. To 'fill' is for a Void line to wait until its own branch rules the day and fills the void.
