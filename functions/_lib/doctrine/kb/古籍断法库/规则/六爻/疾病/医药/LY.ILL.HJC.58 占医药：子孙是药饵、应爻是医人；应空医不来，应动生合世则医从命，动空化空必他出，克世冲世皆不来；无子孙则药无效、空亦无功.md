# 占医药：子孙是药饵、应爻是医人；应空医不来，应动生合世则医从命，动空化空必他出，克世冲世皆不来；无子孙则药无效、空亦无功

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病了不去求医，能保全性命的很少；药不对症，白白死掉的很多。占医药时，应爻是医生，子孙是药。请医生：应爻落空，医生一定不来；应爻发动来生合世爻，医生一定听请；动了又落空、变出来也是空，一定去了别处；克世、冲世，都是不来。卦里没有子孙，吃药没有效果，子孙落空也没有功效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* One who falls ill and does not seek a doctor seldom keeps his life whole, and one who takes medicine that does not fit the illness dies in vain more often than not. In divining medicine the Other line is the doctor and Output the medicine. In calling a doctor: if the Other line is Void, the doctor surely will not come; if it moves and generates or combines with the Self line, he surely obeys the call; if it moves, is Void and changes into Void, he has surely gone elsewhere; controlling or clashing the Self line, he will not come. With no Output in the hexagram medicine has no effect, and if Output is Void it does no good either.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2416–2418 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡卜医药，以子孙爻为药饵，以应爻为医生。 | 凡是卜医药，拿子孙爻当药，拿应爻当医生。 | In all divining of medicine, the Output line is the medicine and the Other line is the doctor. |
> | 如子孙受伤或墓绝，或官爻生旺，是药不对症，必不能去病。 | 如果子孙受伤或者入墓、入绝，或者官爻生旺，就是药不对症，一定不能去病。 | If Output is harmed or in a tomb or extinction, or the Pressure line is born and strong, the medicine does not fit the illness and surely cannot drive it out. |
> | 如应爻旬空，医生非它出不来，定用药无效。 | 如果应爻落了旬空，医生要么出门了，要么不会来，用他的药一定无效。 | If the Other line falls into Void, the doctor either has gone elsewhere or will not come, and his medicine surely has no effect. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「蓍人」是用蓍草占卜的人，也就是占卜家。「药饵」是药和药食。「闲亡」是闲散在外、找不到人；王洪绪引经文时写作「瞷亡」，是不同的写法，书里没有解释。王洪绪比旧注多讲了一条：子孙受伤、入墓入绝，或官爻生旺，是药不对症。
>
> *Note:* A 'yarrow man' is one who divines with yarrow stalks, that is, a diviner. 'Medicine' (yao'er) is medicine and medicinal food. 'Idle and away' (xianwang) means loitering away and not to be found; where Wang Hongxu quotes the verse he writes it with a different character (jianwang), which the book does not explain. Wang Hongxu adds one thing to the old commentary: Output harmed, or in a tomb or extinction, or the Pressure line born and strong, means the medicine does not fit the illness.
