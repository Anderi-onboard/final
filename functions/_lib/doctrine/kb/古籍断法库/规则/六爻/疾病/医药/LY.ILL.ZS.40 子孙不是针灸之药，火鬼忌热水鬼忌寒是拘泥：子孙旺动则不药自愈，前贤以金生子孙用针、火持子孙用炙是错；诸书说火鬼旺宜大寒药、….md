# 子孙不是针灸之药，火鬼忌热水鬼忌寒是拘泥：子孙旺动则不药自愈，前贤以金生子孙用针、火持子孙用炙是错；诸书说火鬼旺宜大寒药、水鬼旺宜大热药，李我平举土用神、妻占夫二例驳之，说胶柱决之坑陷人命

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙旺相又发动的，不吃药就好，并不是教人拿这个子孙当治病的药；前辈贤人常常看到子孙旺动，占病病好、占医医良，就把子孙爻当成治病的药，金生子孙就断用针，火持子孙就断用灸，不知道子孙旺相病自己就好了，哪里用得着针灸，子孙落空被破，针灸又有什么益处。各种书又说火鬼忌热、水鬼忌寒，水鬼在生旺之地要用大热的药，火鬼旺相宜用大寒的药去攻；李我平不知道他们从哪里看出来的：假使用神属土、儿子占父亲的病，父爻临土，火鬼发动是生用爻的元神，怎么能用大寒的药去治元神？妻子占丈夫的病，鬼是用神，丈夫临水鬼，又怎么能用大热的药去攻用神？死板地照大寒大热去断，坑害了不知多少性命。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where Output is strong and moving, one recovers without medicine, and this does not teach us to take that Output as the medicine that treats the illness; the worthies of old often saw Output strong and moving, found that in divining illness the illness healed and in divining a doctor the doctor was good, and so took the Output line as the medicine, judging the needle where Metal generates Output and moxibustion where Fire holds Output, without realising that if Output is strong the illness heals of itself, so what need is there of needle and moxa, and if Output is Void or broken what good would they do. The books also say that a Fire Pressure fears heat and a Water Pressure fears cold, that a Water Pressure in a place of birth or strength must be treated with very hot medicine and a strong Fire Pressure attacked with very cold; Li Woping does not know where they saw this: suppose the Subject is Earth, as when a son divines his father's illness and the Resource line sits on Earth, a moving Fire Pressure is the Support that generates the Subject line, and how could one treat the Support with very cold medicine? When a wife divines her husband's illness, Pressure is the Subject, and if the husband sits on a Water Pressure, how could one attack the Subject with very hot medicine? To judge rigidly by very cold and very hot has sunk who knows how many lives in the pit.

> [!warning] 打架
> 用药看什么：黄金策旧注、王洪绪（LY.ILL.HJC.76、HJC.81、HJC.84）按官鬼的五行定寒热（火鬼旺用大寒、水鬼旺用大热），按子孙的五行定针灸（子孙属金用刀针、属火用灸）；李我平、野鹤说这是拘泥：子孙旺动本来就不药而愈，不是针灸之药；火鬼可能是用神的元神，不能拿大寒药去攻；死板照大寒大热去断，是坑人性命。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What to look at in fixing the medicine: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu (LY.ILL.HJC.76, HJC.81, HJC.84) fix cold or hot by the phase of the Pressure line (a strong Fire Pressure very cold, a strong Water Pressure very hot) and needle or moxa by the phase of Output (Metal Output knife and needle, Fire Output moxa); Li Woping and the Old Man call this rigid: Output strong and moving means recovery without medicine and is not the medicine of needle and moxa, a Fire Pressure may be the Support of the Subject and cannot be attacked with very cold medicine, and to judge rigidly by very cold and very hot sinks lives.

> [!note] 按
> 这是整个疾病域里两派分歧最尖锐的一条。李我平末尾说「此书一出，而寒热之药不敢轻用」，是说《增删卜易》出来以后，占卜的人不该再拿五行定寒热。「炙」「灸」在书里写法不一，都指艾灸。「元神」的用法见 LY.ILL.ZS.08。
>
> *Note:* This is the sharpest split between the two schools in the whole illness domain. Li Woping's closing 'once this book is out, no one will dare to use cold or hot medicines lightly' means that after the Zengshan Buyi diviners should no longer fix cold and hot by the five phases. 'Zhi' and 'jiu' are written differently in the book and both mean moxibustion. For the use of the 'Support' see LY.ILL.ZS.08. The 'Qian note' at the end of the chapter is a modern addition and is not quoted here.
