# 搜精六爻·初爻克五爻宅长病、克二爻宅母危；六爻白虎带鬼旺灾病及公婆

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 搜精六爻说：（初爻）克第五爻，是一家之长有病；克第二爻，是宅母有危险。第六爻，白虎带着鬼爻又旺，灾病牵连到祖父母。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The essay on searching out the essence of the six lines says: [the first line] controlling the fifth line, the head of the household is ill; controlling the second line, the mistress of the house is in danger. On the sixth line, with the White Tiger carrying a strong ghost, calamity and illness reach the grandparents.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 搜精六爻是阐幽精要里逐爻讲家宅的一篇：初爻是根基，二爻是宅母，三爻是门户，四爻是外户，五爻是家长，六爻是虚位、祖父母。「克五宅长病」没有写谁克，按上文（讲初爻）读是初爻克五爻；「公婆」按上一联「六爻为虚位，永为祖父婆」读作祖父母。这两处书里都没有另注。
>
> *Note:* The essay on searching out the essence of the six lines goes line by line through a house: the first line is the foundation, the second the mistress of the house, the third the gate and doors, the fourth the outer door, the fifth the head of the household, and the sixth the empty place and the grandparents. 'Controls the fifth: the head of the house is ill' does not say who controls; it is read from the context (the passage on the first line) as the first line controlling the fifth. 'The old man and old woman' is read as the grandparents from the earlier couplet 'the sixth line is the empty place and is always the grandparents'. The book gives no note on either point.
