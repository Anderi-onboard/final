# 却灾避暑、养病悟道、修真：四者皆宜子动、各忌官兴；占己世爻休墓绝、问他用体怕空亡；子孙旺相或发动参禅避暑必如愿、官鬼交重旺相心求安反遭不测；用神遇月破旬空或临墓绝不惟无福反惹非殃

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以用爻为主，福德为凭。天行酷暑，难道没有避暑的办法？人染了患灾，也有躲灾的地方。悟道要选清闲的地方，修真要访幽僻的住处。这四样都宜子孙发动，各样都忌官鬼兴旺；占自己，怕世爻休囚墓绝，问别人，用爻和体怕空亡。注说：凡是占避暑、养病、悟道、修真这些事，都宜福德发动，各自忌讳官爻发动；占自己，以世爻为主，卜别人，以应象为凭；如果问亲人，要推用象，若临旺相、或遇生扶，却灾能脱身、悟道能成功；用神如果遇月破、旬空、或临墓绝，不光没有福，反而招来祸殃。子孙旺相或发动的，参禅打坐没有不成功的，避暑却灾必定如愿；官鬼交重或旺相的，心里想求安，反而遭遇不测。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject line is the main line, and the fortune spirit is the ground. When the heat of the season rages, is there not a way to escape it? When a person is infected with calamity, there is a place to ward it off. To realise the Way, choose a quiet and leisured place; to cultivate the truth, visit a secluded dwelling. For all four Output moving is favourable, and in each a thriving Pressure line is feared; in divining oneself, the Self line resting, in a tomb or in Extinction is feared, and in asking about another, the Subject and the body fearing Void. The commentary says: in all matters of escaping the heat, convalescing, realising the Way and cultivating the truth, the fortune spirit moving is favourable, and in each the Pressure line moving is feared; in divining oneself the Self line is the main line, and in divining another the Other image is the ground; if asking about a relative, work out the Subject image, and if it sits on strength or meets generation and support, one escapes calamity and the Way is achieved; if the Subject meets Month-break or Void, or sits in a tomb or Extinction, there is not only no blessing but a misfortune is brought on. With Output strong or moving, sitting in meditation is never without success, and escaping the heat and warding off calamity are sure to be as wished; with the Pressure line moving or strong, the heart seeks peace and on the contrary meets the unforeseen.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章讲的不是已经生了病，而是躲灾避暑、养病、悟道、修真这类要去一处安静地方的事，所以它和疾病放在一起（「人染患灾，亦有却灾之所」）。「福德」是子孙。「用体」的「体」疑是身体；「用爻和体怕空亡」按字面译。「参禅」是坐禅修行，「修真」是道家修行。占自己以世为主，占别人以应为凭，和 LY.ILL.EL.18 是同一条。
>
> *Note:* This chapter is not about someone already ill, but about the matters that call for going to a quiet place — warding off calamity, escaping the heat, convalescing, realising the Way, cultivating the truth — and so it is put with illness ('when a person is infected with calamity, there is a place to ward it off'). 'Fortune spirit' is Output. The 'body' in 'the Subject and the body' looks like the physical body, and 'the Subject line and the body fearing Void' is translated literally. 'Meditation' is Chan sitting practice and 'cultivating the truth' is Daoist practice. That the Self is the main line in divining oneself and the Other the ground in divining another is the same rule as in LY.ILL.EL.18.
