# 父兴得地扁鹊无功：父母发动、子孙受制，药必不效；子孙有气、日辰动爻来冲合（旧注）或克（王洪绪）父母而救得有用，才可活，仍须多服；父化子孙也是医人太慢

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻发动，子孙受制，药一定没有效。如果子孙有气，又有日辰、动爻来救（冲、合父母爻，或者克父母爻），救得有用，才可以活，但也必须多服才好。父母变成子孙，也是医生太慢。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Resource line moves and Output is restrained, the medicine surely has no effect. If Output has force, and the Day or a moving line comes to rescue it (clashing or combining with the Resource line, or controlling it) so that the rescue works, he may live, but even so it is best to take much of it. Resource changing into Output is also the doctor being too slow.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2452 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母发动子孙受伤，药必不效。 | 父母爻发动，子孙受了伤，药一定没有效。 | When the Resource line moves and Output is harmed, the medicine surely has no effect. |
> | 若得子孙有气，日辰动爻克父母，必须多服有功。 | 如果子孙有气，日辰、动爻来克父母爻，必须多服才有功效。 | If Output has force and the Day or a moving line controls the Resource line, then taking much of it is effective. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 怎样解救发动的父母爻：旧注说日辰、动爻「冲合」父母爻；王洪绪说日辰、动爻「克」父母爻。一个是把它冲开、合住，一个是直接克制。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to relieve a moving Resource line: the old commentary says the Day or a moving line 'clashes or combines with' the Resource line; Wang Hongxu says the Day or a moving line 'controls' it. One clashes it apart or holds it, the other restrains it directly.
