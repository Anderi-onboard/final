# 麻面官乘四土、破碎遇三冲：官鬼临辰戌丑未四土，土属脾经，痘粗扁多密故云麻面，防口如鱼口；官爻遇年月日冲，灌浆后防破碎泄气

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 麻面是官爻乘着辰、戌、丑、未这四个土，破碎是遇到三处冲。官鬼就是痘花，如果落在辰、戌、丑、未四个土上，土属脾经，主痘又粗又扁又多又密，所以叫麻面，要防嘴像鱼嘴；如果官爻遇到年、月、日（注里写「二建」）来冲，灌浆以后要防破碎、泄气。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A pockmarked face is Pressure riding the four Earths, and breaking and shattering is meeting three clashes. The Pressure line is the pox flowers; if it sits on the four Earths, Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei, Earth belongs to the spleen channel, and the pox are coarse, flat, many and dense, so it is called a pockmarked face, and one must guard against a mouth like a fish's mouth; if the Pressure line meets a clash from the Year, Month or Day (the commentary writes 'two rulers'), then after the fluid has filled one must guard against breaking apart and the leaking of qi.

> [!note] 按
> 经文说「遇三冲」，注写「年月日二建冲者」：年、月、日是三处，「二建」的「二」按上下文可能是「三」的误字，也可能指月建、日建两处，书里没有说明，译文照写。
>
> *Note:* The verse says 'meeting three clashes' and the commentary 'clashed by the two rulers of Year, Month and Day': the Year, Month and Day make three, so the 'two' of 'two rulers' may by context be a mis-write of 'three', or may mean the Month and Day rulers, which the book does not explain; the translation follows the text.
