# 生死·男女老少之忌：男忌官鬼长生日得病、女忌官鬼沐浴日得病；无龙福月解天医来救则鬼爻墓绝日必死；晁以道：用爻命爻不上卦者死；男忌鬼空、女忌财空，少年卦忌死囚休废、老人卦忌旺相

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男的忌讳在官鬼长生的那天得病，女的忌讳在官鬼沐浴的那天得病。如果没有青龙、福德、月解、天医发动来生合用爻，鬼爻入墓、绝的那天一定死。晁以道说：用爻、命爻不上卦的，死。还有，男的忌讳鬼落空，女的忌讳财落空；少年占的卦忌讳死、囚、休、废，老人占的卦忌讳旺相。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A man should fear falling ill on the day the Pressure line is born, and a woman on the day the Pressure line is at Bath. If none of the Dragon, Fortune, Month Release or Heavenly Doctor moves to generate or combine with the Subject line, he will surely die on the day the Pressure line is in its tomb or Extinction. Chao Yidao says: if the Subject line and the destiny line do not appear in the hexagram, he dies. Further, a man should fear the ghost being Void and a woman Wealth being Void; a young person's hexagram fears Death, trapped, resting and abandoned, and an old person's hexagram fears being strong.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和黄金策讲的「主空无救」「用绝逢生」（LY.ILL.HJC.37、LY.ILL.HJC.47）相近，不同的是这里按男女、老少分了忌讳：男忌鬼空，女忌财空；少年怕死囚休废，老人怕旺相——老人怕旺相与黄金策「占病不喜旺相，太刚则折」（LY.ILL.HJC.36）同一个意思。「晁以道」是宋代人晁说之，书里只写了字。「死囚休废」是五行在季节里的四种无气状态。
>
> *Note:* This entry is close to the Huangjin Ce's 'the main image Void, no rescue' and 'Extinction meeting generation' (LY.ILL.HJC.37 and LY.ILL.HJC.47); the difference is that it divides the fears by sex and age: a man fears the ghost Void and a woman Wealth Void; the young fear Death, trapped, resting and abandoned and the old fear being strong — the old fearing strength agrees with the Huangjin Ce's 'in illness one does not like strength; too rigid, it snaps' (LY.ILL.HJC.36). 'Chao Yidao' is the Song man Chao Shuozhi, of whom the book gives only the style-name. 'Death, trapped, resting, abandoned' are the four forceless states of an element in a season.
