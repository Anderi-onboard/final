# 静居宅上临木家停暴露之棺：木爻官鬼安静地落在二爻，或木鬼伏在父母爻下，其家必停灵柩，举之则安；衰弱的是座席，撤了家宅安泰

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼安静地落在宅位（二爻）上又是木，家里停着裸露的棺材：木爻的官鬼安静地落在二爻，或者木鬼伏在父母爻下面，这家一定停着灵柩，把它抬走就安宁了；衰弱的话是座席，撤掉它，家宅安泰；旧注说照这样断，万无一失。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A still ghost on the house position (the second line), being Wood, means the family has a coffin lying exposed: if a Wood Pressure line sits still on the second line, or a Wood ghost is hidden beneath the Resource line, the family surely has a coffin lying in the hall, and once it is carried away there will be peace; if weak, it is a seat or mat, and once it is taken away the household is at peace. The old commentary says that judged this way nothing can go wrong.

> [!note] 按
> 「座席」旧注没有解释，按字面译作座位或席子。旧注末句「依此断，万无一失」是注者自己的保证，王洪绪没有，本库照抄注者的话，不替它担保。这一条是鬼神章的最后一条。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary does not explain 'zuoxi' (seat and mat), rendered literally as a seat or mat. The old commentary's last sentence, 'judge by this and nothing will go wrong', is the commentator's own guarantee, which Wang Hongxu lacks; the library copies the commentator's words and does not stand surety for them. This is the last entry of the chapter on ghosts and spirits.
