# 身临福德势虽险而堪医：「身」是一卦之体（旧注说是用爻，王洪绪说是月卦身），子孙临之决然无虞，纵然凶险用药必愈

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一卦的「身」是整个卦的主体：身上坐着子孙（福德），一定没有忧患，病势再凶险也有救，用药医治一定痊愈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The 'body' is the substance of the whole hexagram: if Output (Output-virtue) sits on it, there is surely nothing to fear; even if the illness looks dangerous it can be saved, and treated with medicine it will surely be cured.

## 《黄金策·病体》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6889 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身临福德，势虽险而堪医。 | 身上坐着福德，病势虽然凶险还是可以医治。 | The body sitting on Output-virtue: though the danger looks great, it can be treated. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6890 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用爻为身，乃一卦之体。 | 用爻就是身，是整个卦的主体。 | The Subject line is the body, the substance of the whole hexagram. |
> | 若得子孙临之，决然无虞。 | 如果有子孙坐在它上面，一定没有忧患。 | If Output sits on it, there is surely nothing to fear. |
> | 纵然病势凶险，自可有救，以药治之，必定痊愈。 | 就算病势凶险，自然有救，用药医治，一定痊愈。 | Even if the illness looks dangerous, it can be saved, and treated with medicine it will surely be cured. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2368 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 月卦身乃一卦之体，子孙临之，决然无虞，纵然病势凶险，用药可以痊愈。 | 月卦身是整个卦的主体，子孙坐在它上面，一定没有忧患，就算病势凶险，用药也可以痊愈。 | The month's hexagram Body is the substance of the whole hexagram; if Output sits on it there is surely nothing to fear, and even if the illness looks dangerous it can be cured with medicine. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 「身」指哪一爻：旧注这里说用爻就是身；王洪绪说是月卦身（按月起的卦身）。这一章的旧注在 LY.ILL.HJC.26 里另外讲「卦身持鬼」，把卦身当成另一样东西，所以旧注自己在这个字上也不一致。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line the 'body' is: the old commentary here says the Subject line is the body; Wang Hongxu says it is the month's hexagram Body (the Body set by the month). In LY.ILL.HJC.26 the old commentary of this chapter speaks separately of 'a Pressure line on the hexagram Body' and treats the hexagram Body as something else, so the old commentary is itself not consistent about this word.
