# 占居官有疾病否：卦变为囚死乡居官身病至衰亡、无气带杀终有祸更值空亡必有丧；鬼动若逢休废地此身退位终无位、胎没并之世自微更加刑杀为衰

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占在任的官有没有疾病：凡是卦变到囚、死的地方，在任的官身上有病，以至衰亡；无气又带杀，终究有祸，再遇上空亡，必定有丧。鬼动，若逢到休、废的地方，这个人退位，终究没有官位；胎没并起来，世自己就衰微，再加刑、杀，就是衰败了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On whether an official in office has illness: whenever the hexagram changes into a place of trapped or dead, the official in office falls ill, even to decline and death; without qi and bearing a killing, in the end there is calamity, and meeting Void as well there is surely a death. The ghost moving, if it meets a place of resting or ruin, this person leaves office and in the end has no post; with Womb and Submerged joined, the Self itself is faint, and with punishment and killings added it is decline.

> [!note] 按
> 这首在占官一章的「占居官有疾病否」一题下，问的是在任的官会不会生病。「囚死乡」是囚、死两种状态所在的地方（旺相休囚死里的囚和死）；「卦变为囚死乡」是卦（或世爻）变到囚死的地方。「胎没」是什么书里没有说，「胎」和「没」是两个名目，这里字面译作 Womb and Submerged；「并之」是并在一起。这首歌前半是说身病，后半转到「退位无位」的官运上，所以放在一起收。这一首在同一书里出现两次（一次是歌诀，一次是今人译文里重复的标题），译文不收。前半的意思（状态衰败、又带杀、再遇空亡则死）和孙膑歌「卦变墓乡须丧命」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.07）、天玄赋的「入墓卦」（LY.ILL.TXF.06）是同一类。
>
> *Note:* This poem is under the heading 'divining whether an official in office has illness' in the chapter on divining office, and asks whether an official in office will fall ill. The 'place of trapped or dead' is the place where the two states of trapped and dead (of strong, helped, resting, trapped, dead) lie; 'the hexagram changing into a place of trapped or dead' is the hexagram (or the Self line) changing into a place of trapped or dead. What 'Womb and Submerged' is the book does not say, and 'tai' and 'mo' are two names, rendered literally as Womb and Submerged; 'bing zhi' is joined together. The first half of the poem speaks of illness of the body, and the second half turns to fortune in office, 'leaves office and has no post', and so they are taken together. This poem appears twice in the same book (once as the verse and once as a repeated heading in a modern translation), and the translation is not quoted. The sense of the first half (a state of decline, bearing a killing, and meeting Void as well means death) is of the same kind as Sun Bin's song, 'if the hexagram changes into the tomb quarter life will surely be lost' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.07), and the Tianxuan Fu's 'hexagrams that enter tombs' (LY.ILL.TXF.06).
