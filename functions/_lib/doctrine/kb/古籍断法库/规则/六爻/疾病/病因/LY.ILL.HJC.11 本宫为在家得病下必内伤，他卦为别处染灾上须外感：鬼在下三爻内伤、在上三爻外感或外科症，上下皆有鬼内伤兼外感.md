# 本宫为在家得病下必内伤，他卦为别处染灾上须外感：鬼在下三爻内伤、在上三爻外感或外科症，上下皆有鬼内伤兼外感

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼在本宫，是在家里得的病；鬼在别的宫（别的卦），是在外面染上的。鬼在下面三爻，一定是内伤的症候，不然病也在里面；鬼在外宫、又在上三爻，一定是外感风邪，不然也是外科的症候；上下都有鬼，是内伤兼外感，症候不止一种。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure in the home palace means the illness was got at home; Pressure in another palace or hexagram means it was caught elsewhere. Pressure in the lower three lines is surely a syndrome of inner damage, or else the illness is in the inside; Pressure in the outer palace and in the upper three lines is surely outer wind evil from without, or else a surgical syndrome; with Pressure both above and below it is inner damage together with outer contagion, and the syndrome is mixed.
