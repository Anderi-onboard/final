# 鬼入六神断决·占病：鬼入青龙病渐轻；鬼入朱雀身命危宜求谢；鬼入白虎占病急去买棺材；鬼入玄武占病昏睡耳聋

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼落在六神上，各有各的断法，占病时：鬼入青龙，病渐渐轻了，是堂前讨吃的亡人，或死在外面回来作怪的鬼；鬼入朱雀，身家性命危险，是伤亡鬼、五道，适合祈求酬谢，不谢会损害病人；鬼入白虎，哭声哀哀，占病的话赶紧去买棺材，秋冬多精怪，要提防老人和小孩；鬼入玄武，必定凶，占病昏睡耳聋，是落水死的、伤亡的鬼来讨吃的，赶紧保祷酬谢。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure falling on each of the six spirits has its own judgment, and in divining illness: Pressure in Azure Dragon, the illness gradually lightens, and it is a dead person asking for food in front of the hall, or one who died outside and has come back to make trouble; Pressure in Vermilion Bird, his person and life are in danger, it is the dead by injury and the Five Ways, and it is fitting to pray and give thanks, for without thanks the sick body is harmed; Pressure in White Tiger, mournful weeping, and in divining illness go quickly and buy the coffin, in autumn and winter there are many sprites, and one should guard the old and the young; Pressure in Dark Tortoise, surely ominous, in divining illness he sleeps heavily and is deaf, the drowned and the dead by injury come asking for food, and he should quickly pray and give thanks.

> [!note] 按
> 这一组在书里有六首（青龙、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武），这里只收四首，因为勾陈那首（「弟兄求」「公婆也容只见愁」）和螣蛇那首没有讲到病，螣蛇那首的字句又残缺不通（「鬼入蛇你莫夸 外面更有神 冤家 不时有愿前时穴 更有宅居有些些」），译不出来，不收。书里的「鬼」就是官鬼那一爻，同时也指它代表的鬼；译文第一次出现写「Pressure」，说「鬼里有人」「亡人」时指鬼魂。「求食」是亡魂来讨祭祀的饭食（要给它祭）；「梦魂精」不知所指，按字面写；「保谢」是保祷酬谢；「须防老幼免心怀」的「心怀」读作挂心，这句不很通，是读法。这四首里直接讲病轻重生死的是：青龙「病渐轻」，朱雀「身命危」，白虎「占病急去买棺材」，玄武「占病昏睡耳又聋」。
>
> *Note:* This group has six verses in the book (Azure Dragon, Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth, Coiling Snake, White Tiger, Dark Tortoise), and only four are taken here, because the Hook Earth verse ('seeking brothers', 'the in-laws too can only see sorrow') and the Coiling Snake verse do not speak of illness, and the Coiling Snake verse is also broken and does not make sense (it reads, roughly, 'Pressure enters the snake, you must not boast, outside there is also a spirit, enemies, at times a vow, the earlier grave, and also something about the dwelling'), so it could not be translated and is not taken. The book's 'ghost' is the Pressure line and also the ghosts it stands for; the translation writes 'Pressure' at the first mention and 'ghost' where it says 'among the ghosts there is one' or 'a dead person'. 'Asking for food' means the dead soul has come for the sacrificial food (that is, it is to be given offerings); the book does not explain 'the soul-sprites of dreams' and the translation keeps to the letter; 'pray and give thanks' is the book's 'protect and thank'; 'to spare anxiety' reads the book's 'heart' as being anxious about it, and the line is not smooth, which is a reading. The four verses that speak directly of how light or heavy the illness is, and of life and death, are: Azure Dragon 'the illness gradually lightens', Vermilion Bird 'his person and life are in danger', White Tiger 'divining illness, go quickly and buy the coffin' and Dark Tortoise 'in divining illness he sleeps heavily and is deaf in the ears'.
