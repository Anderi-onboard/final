# 六亲属类·子孙：缉捕医药闲渔僧；师巫捕抗，苻药医卜(子孙是医药)

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 海底眼的六亲属类里，子孙管的物类里有医药：「缉捕医药闲渔僧」，散句里还有「师巫捕抗，苻药医卜」——子孙是医生和药物（还有符药、卜算）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the classes of the six relations in the Haidi Yan, among what Output governs are doctors and medicines: 'arrest, doctors and medicines, idle fishermen and monks', and in the prose 'shamans, catching and resisting, charms, medicines, doctors and diviners' — Output is the doctor and the medicine (and also charm-medicines and divining).

> | 苻药医卜。 | 符、药、医、卜。 | charms, medicines, doctors and diviners. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 子孙管医药，是整个六爻通例（阐奥歌章 LY.ILL.CA.08、天玄赋 LY.ILL.TXF.12、文王金钱课 LY.ILL.WWJQ.01 等），这里是用类象的歌诀说出来的：子孙这一类里有「医药」「医卜」。「缉捕医药闲渔僧」是一连串的名词，没有动词，译文逐项列出；「苻」是「符」的写法。「捕抗」意思不清，按字面译。
>
> *Note:* That Output governs doctors and medicines is a general rule of the whole of the six lines (the Chan'ao Gezhang LY.ILL.CA.08, the Tianxuan Fu LY.ILL.TXF.12, the Wenwang Jinqian Ke LY.ILL.WWJQ.01 and others), and here it is said in a verse of images: in this class of Output there are 'doctors and medicines' and 'doctors and diviners'. 'Arrest, doctors and medicines, idlers, fishermen and monks' is a string of nouns with no verb, and the translation lists them one by one; the character for 'charm' is written in a variant form. 'Catching and resisting' is unclear in sense and is translated literally.
