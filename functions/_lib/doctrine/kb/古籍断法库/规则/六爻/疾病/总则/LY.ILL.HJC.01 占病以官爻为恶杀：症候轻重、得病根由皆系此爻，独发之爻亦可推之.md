# 占病以官爻为恶杀：症候轻重、得病根由皆系此爻，独发之爻亦可推之

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病时把官鬼爻当作凶恶的煞：病的症状轻重、得病的根由，都看这一爻；卦里只有一爻独自发动的，也可以照它来推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness the Pressure line is taken as the malign killer: how heavy or light the symptoms are, and how the illness was contracted, are both read from this line; a line that moves alone in the hexagram can also be reasoned from in the same way.

## 《黄金策·疾病》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6763 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 人孰无常，疾病无常； | 人谁没有无常的时候？疾病就是无常的； | Who among men is without impermanence? Illness is impermanent; |
> | 事孰为大，死生为大。 | 什么事最大？生死最大。 | what matter is greatest? Life and death are the greatest. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6764 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占病以官爻为恶杀。 | 占病，把官鬼爻当作凶恶的煞。 | In divining illness, the Pressure line is taken as the malign killer. |
> | 凡病：症候轻重，得病根由，皆系此爻。 | 凡是病，症状的轻重、得病的根由，都系在这一爻上。 | In every illness, how heavy or light the symptoms are and how the illness was contracted both hang on this line. |
> | 独发之爻，亦可以此推之。 | 只有一爻独自发动的，也可以照这个办法推。 | A line that moves alone can also be reasoned out in the same way. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2301 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占疾症，以官鬼爻为轻重； | 凡是占疾病，以官鬼爻定轻重； | In divining any illness, the Pressure line fixes how heavy or light it is; |
> | 得病根由，独发之爻亦可推之。 | 得病的根由，独自发动的爻也可以推。 | for the cause of the illness, a line moving alone can also be reasoned from. |
