# 六冲变冲久病难于调治：久病卦逢六冲、卦变六冲，不论用神衰旺，是不治之症；近病逢之不药而愈；久病逢冲必死，六冲变六冲更甚

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 久病遇到卦是六冲、又变六冲，不管用神旺衰，是治不好的病；近病遇到它，不吃药也会好。久病遇冲必死，六冲变六冲更是如此，就算用神临着月建、又有生它的爻，也活不了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a long illness, a hexagram that is Six-Clash and changes into Six-Clash is an incurable illness whatever the strength of the Subject; a recent illness that meets it recovers without medicine. A long illness meeting a clash surely means death, and Six-Clash changing into Six-Clash all the more, so that even if the Subject sits on the Month and a line generates it, the patient cannot live.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 13042 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 久病逢冲必死，矧六冲变六冲乎？ | 久病遇冲必死，何况是六冲变六冲呢？ | A long illness meeting a clash surely means death, let alone Six-Clash changing into Six-Clash. |
> | 三月必危。 | 三月一定危险。 | The third month will be dangerous. |
> | 果卒于丑月。 | 果然死在丑月。 | He did die in the Chou month. |
> | 应三月者，酉子为用神，动而逢合之月也，又是冲去戌土，酉金无以为生也。 | 应验在三月，是因为酉金子孙是用神，动了遇到合的月份；又是冲走了戌土，酉金没有东西生它。 | It came true in the third month because the You Metal Output line is the Subject, and the month is one in which it moves and meets a combination; besides, Xu Earth is clashed away, and You Metal has nothing to generate it. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 书里没有交代断语里的「三月」和实际死的「丑月」是怎么算的，译文按字面。「酉子」按上下文是坤宫里酉金的子孙爻（坤属土，土生金）。野鹤的注留这一卦，是要说明：久病逢冲，用神就算临月建、又有生它的爻，也活不了；同一条注的后半（趋避之法）对应下面的 LY.ILL.ZS.17。
>
> *Note:* The book does not say how the 'third month' of the judgment relates to the 'Chou month' in which he in fact died, and the translation follows the letter. 'You Output' is, from the context, the You Metal Output line in the Kun palace (Kun belongs to Earth and Earth generates Metal). The Old Man's note keeps this hexagram to show that in a long illness meeting a clash, the Subject cannot live even if it sits on the Month and a line generates it; the second half of the same note (the method of avoidance) matches LY.ILL.ZS.17 below.
