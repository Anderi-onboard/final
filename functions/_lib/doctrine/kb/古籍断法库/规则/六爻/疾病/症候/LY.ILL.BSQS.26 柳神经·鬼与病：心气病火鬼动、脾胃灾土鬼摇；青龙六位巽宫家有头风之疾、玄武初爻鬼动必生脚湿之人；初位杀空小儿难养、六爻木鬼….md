# 柳神经·鬼与病：心气病火鬼动、脾胃灾土鬼摇；青龙六位巽宫家有头风之疾、玄武初爻鬼动必生脚湿之人；初位杀空小儿难养、六爻木鬼老者中风、上透金爻终年眼暗、五爻火鬼痨瘵缠身

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 柳神经里把鬼爻的五行、所在的位置和病挂在一起：火鬼动是心气病，土鬼摇是脾胃病；青龙在六位、巽宫，家里有头风；玄武在初爻、鬼动，生脚湿的人；初爻的杀落空，小孩难养；六爻木鬼，老人中风；上面透出金爻，终年眼暗；五爻火鬼，痨瘵缠身。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Liushen Jing ties the element of a ghost line, its place and an illness together: a Fire ghost moving is illness of the heart qi, an Earth ghost stirring is trouble of the spleen and stomach; the Azure Dragon in the sixth place and the Xun palace gives the household head-wind; the Dark Tortoise on the first line with the ghost moving gives someone with damp feet; a Void killing on the first place makes a small child hard to raise; a Wood ghost on the sixth line, an old person has a stroke; a Metal line showing through above, dim eyes all the year; a Fire ghost on the fifth line, consumption clings to the body.

> [!note] 按
> 柳神经是阐幽精要里占家宅的一篇，前后各联讲房屋、牲畜、神鬼，这里收的几联把鬼爻和病挂上：心气病配火鬼、脾胃灾配土鬼（本篇疾病论里也是这样，见 LY.ILL.BSQS.32、LY.ILL.BSQS.43）。「头风」是长年头痛；「脚湿」是脚上湿病（脚气一类）；「痨瘵」是肺痨；「中风」「眼暗」照字面。「初位杀空」「上透金爻」两处文意不全（杀是哪一种杀、金爻透在哪里），书里没有注，译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* The Liushen Jing is an essay on divining a house in the Chanyou Jingyao; its couplets run through buildings, livestock and spirits, and the ones taken here tie a ghost line to an illness: a heart-qi illness goes with a Fire ghost and trouble of the spleen and stomach with an Earth ghost (so also in this book's chapter on illness, see LY.ILL.BSQS.32 and LY.ILL.BSQS.43). 'Head-wind' is a long-standing headache; 'damp feet' is a damp disease of the feet (of the beriberi kind); 'consumption' is pulmonary consumption; 'stroke' and 'dim eyes' are literal. In 'a killing spirit Void in the first place' and 'a Metal line showing through above' the sense is incomplete (which killing spirit, and where the Metal line shows through); the book gives no note and the translation keeps to the letter.
