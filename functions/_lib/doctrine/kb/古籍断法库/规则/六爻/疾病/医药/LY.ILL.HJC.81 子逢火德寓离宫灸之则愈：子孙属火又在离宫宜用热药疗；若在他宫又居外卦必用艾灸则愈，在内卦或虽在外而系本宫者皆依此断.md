# 子逢火德寓离宫灸之则愈：子孙属火又在离宫宜用热药疗；若在他宫又居外卦必用艾灸则愈，在内卦或虽在外而系本宫者皆依此断

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙属火，又在离宫，宜用热药来治；如果在别的宫，又居外卦，一定用艾灸才会好；如果在内卦，或者虽然在外卦却属本宫的，都照这个断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output is Fire and also in the Li palace, hot medicine should be used to treat it; if it is in another palace and in the outer trigram, it is healed only by moxibustion; if it is in the inner trigram, or, though in the outer, belongs to the home palace, judge in each case by this.

## 《黄金策·医药》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7057 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Doctors and medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子逢火德寓离宫，灸之则愈。 | 子孙遇到火德、又住在离宫，用灸法就会痊愈。 | Output meeting the virtue of Fire and dwelling in the Li palace: moxibustion heals it. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2464 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙属火，又在离宫，宜用热药疗之，或用艾炙则愈。 | 子孙属火，又在离宫，宜用热药来治，或者用艾灸才会好。 | If Output is Fire and also in the Li palace, hot medicine should be used to treat it, or moxibustion, and it heals. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「灸」是用艾绒烧灼穴位的疗法，王洪绪写作「艾炙」，是同一个意思。旧注末句的「依此断」指依哪一句，书里没有说。
>
> *Note:* Moxibustion is the treatment of burning mugwort floss on the acupuncture points; Wang Hongxu writes it with a different character, meaning the same. Which sentence the old commentary's closing 'judge by this' refers to the book does not say.
