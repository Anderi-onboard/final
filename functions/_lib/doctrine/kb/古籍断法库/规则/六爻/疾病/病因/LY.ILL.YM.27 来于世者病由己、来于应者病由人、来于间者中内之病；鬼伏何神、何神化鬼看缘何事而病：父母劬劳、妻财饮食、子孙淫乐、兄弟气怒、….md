# 来于世者病由己、来于应者病由人、来于间者中内之病；鬼伏何神、何神化鬼看缘何事而病：父母劬劳、妻财饮食、子孙淫乐、兄弟气怒、官鬼邪妖

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼从世爻来的，病由自己而起；从应爻来的，病由别人而来；从间爻来的，是体内中间的病。鬼伏在哪个六亲之下，就看因为什么事生了病；哪个六亲变出了鬼，就看因为什么事出现了病兆。因为父母爻管的是劳累辛苦，妻财爻管的是饮食，子孙爻管的是淫乐，兄弟爻管的是生气发怒，官鬼爻管的是邪祟妖异。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Coming from the Self, the illness is of one's own doing; coming from the Other, it is from another person; coming from the between line, it is an illness of the interior. Under which relation the ghost hides shows from what matter the illness sprouted, and which relation turns into the ghost shows by what matter the illness was foreshadowed. For the Resource line is toil, the Wealth line is food and drink, the Output line is debauchery and pleasure, the Peer line is temper and anger, and the Pressure line is evil and monstrous things.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「何神」在这一条里是指六亲（下一句列的正是六亲各管什么）。「中内之病」原文没有再解释，这里译成「体内中间的病」。这一条与黄金策「鬼伏六亲之下各有病因」（LY.ILL.HJC.08）、「鬼爻化出六亲各有得病之处」（LY.ILL.HJC.10）和易隐「鬼伏六亲下或六亲化鬼」（LY.ILL.YY.18）是同一件事，但易冒把六亲的所管压成了一句话，没有再分五行、六神。
>
> *Note:* 'He shen' (which spirit) in this entry means the relations, since the next sentence lists what each relation governs. 'An illness of the interior' is not explained further in the original and is rendered as an illness of the inside. This entry is the same matter as the Huangjin Ce's 'each relation the ghost hides under gives its own cause' (LY.ILL.HJC.08) and 'each relation the Pressure line turns into gives its own place of contracting the illness' (LY.ILL.HJC.10), and the Yi Yin's 'the ghost hidden under a relation, or a relation turning into the ghost' (LY.ILL.YY.18), but the Yi Mao compresses what each relation governs into one sentence and does not go on to divide by the phases and spirits.
