# 官鬼属水则河海江神：普陀、真武、云台、北斗、海神、龙王、河神、大王、杨将、施相、天妃、晏公、祠山、周王、汪公、水母、井神池神之属；各方水神随占人之民俗土风，漕河尊大王、东海尊天妃、南河尊周王

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼属水的，就是河、海、江的神。像普陀、真武、云台、北斗、海神、龙王、河神、大王、杨将、施相、天妃、晏公、祠山、周王、汪公、水母、水圣、井神、池神、水伤神这一类，以及因水而成神的，都可以类推。九河百川，各个地方各有掌管的神，百姓的尊称各不相同，要随着占卜人所在的民俗和风土，用五行八卦、六神诸星去取象，才算射中靶心。大体上水神、火神灵验显赫的多，没法一一细说，各地不一样：漕河尊大王，东海尊天妃，南河尊周王，洞庭尊湘妃，鄱阳是小姑，长江是杨四将军，各有各的分管。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line is Water, it is the spirits of river, sea and great river, as with Putuo, Zhenwu, Yuntai, the Dipper, the Sea God, the Dragon King, the River God, the Great King, General Yang, Lord Shi, the Heavenly Consort, Lord Yan, Cishan, Prince Zhou, Lord Wang, the Water Mother, the Water Sage, the Well God, the Pond God and the water-wound spirits, and those who became spirits through water. For the nine rivers and the hundred streams each place has its own governing spirit, and the honorifics the common people give differ from place to place; following the customs and local ways of the one who divines, take the image by the five phases, the eight trigrams, the six spirits and the various stars, and one hits the mark. In general the spirits of water and fire are often powerfully manifest and cannot be set out in detail, differing from place to place: the grain-transport canal honours the Great King, the Eastern Sea the Heavenly Consort, the southern river Prince Zhou, Dongting the Xiang Consort, Poyang has the Little Aunt, and the Yangtze General Yang the Fourth, each with its own domain.

> [!note] 按
> 神名的处理见 LY.ILL.YM.44 的注。这一条比别的四行多讲了一点道理：各地的水神名号不同，要随占卜人所在的地方风俗去取象，不是拿一张名单去对。「射的」是射中靶心。
>
> *Note:* Names are treated as in the note to LY.ILL.YM.44. This entry gives more of a reason than the other four phases: the names of water spirits differ from region to region, so the image is to be taken according to the customs of the place of the one who divines, and not by matching against a list. 'Hits the mark' is hitting the bull's-eye.
