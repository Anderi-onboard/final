# 医家占酬谢与阻碍：兄动倘遭同辈阻、财空休望谢金来、六冲岂得终其事、官化官忧病复乖、应克世身兄雀动反遭非讼莫开怀

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟发动，倘若遭遇同辈阻挠；财空，不要指望谢金来；六冲，怎么能把事做完；官化官，担心病又变糟；应克世身、兄弟和朱雀发动，反而遭到是非和官司，不要开怀。注说：兄弟发动或克世爻，一定被同行霸占；木如果遇空，药金不要指望；六冲的卦，医治不能有始有终；鬼变出鬼爻、或卦里有两个官鬼都动，病重又变病，怎么能治？应如果克世，兄动、财空，又加上朱雀吵嚷，不光求不到谢礼，反而惹来闲是非。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer moving, he may meet obstruction from his fellows; Wealth Void, do not look for the fee; a Six-Clash hexagram, how can the matter be finished; Pressure changing into Pressure, one worries the illness turns bad again; the Other controlling the Self, Peer and the Vermilion Bird moving, one meets quarrels and lawsuits instead, and should not be light-hearted. The commentary says: Peer moving or controlling the Self line, one is surely muscled out by fellow physicians; if Wood is Void, do not look for medicine money; in a Six-Clash hexagram the treatment cannot have a beginning and an end; if the ghost changes into a Pressure line, or the hexagram has two Pressure lines both moving, the illness changes again into another, and how can it be treated? If the Other controls the Self, and Peer moves, Wealth is Void and the Vermilion Bird adds its noise, one not only fails to get the fee but brings on idle quarrels.

> [!note] 按
> 「木若逢空，药金莫望」的「木」是财爻（金在药上，财属木，木是金所克）——这里是说医生的酬谢（药金）靠财爻，财爻落空就没有酬金；原文没有明说「木」指财，这里按上下文理解。「非讼」是是非和词讼。这一条与易冒的往医（LY.ILL.YM.40：应克世者寡情、爻无财者薄赠）是同一件事，只是这里多讲了兄弟发动（同行争夺）和官化官（病又变）。
>
> *Note:* In 'if Wood is Void, do not look for medicine money' the 'Wood' is the Wealth line (Metal governs the medicine, Wealth belongs to Wood, and Wood is what Metal controls) — it means the doctor's fee (medicine money) depends on the Wealth line, and a Void Wealth line brings no fee; the original does not say outright that 'Wood' means Wealth, and it is taken so from the context. 'Quarrels and lawsuits' is disputes and legal action. It is the same matter as the Yi Mao's on going to treat (LY.ILL.YM.40: the Other controlling the Self means little feeling, no Wealth line means a meagre gift), except that here it adds Peer moving (fellows competing) and Pressure changing into Pressure (the illness changing again).
