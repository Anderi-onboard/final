# 病症以官鬼为凭：鬼持身世命用、鬼动来刑害克冲身世命用、官鬼伏其下、或身世命用化官鬼，各随五行断；鬼属阴在内卦金肺木肝胆水肾火心小肠土脾胃大肠，鬼属阳在外卦金四肢骨节牙齿右耳、木筋骨左耳、水口皮痰血汗、土鼻腹背肌肉、火目胸手心脚底；八卦乾首坤腹震足巽股坎耳离目艮背指兑口舌

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断病症以官鬼为凭据：鬼爻持在身、世、命爻、用爻上，或者鬼发动来刑、害、克、冲它们，或者官鬼伏在它们下面，或者它们发动变出官鬼，都各按官鬼的五行来断。鬼属阴爻、在内卦的：金是肺，木是肝胆，水是肾，子（水）是膀胱，火是心和小肠，土是脾、胃、大肠。鬼属阳爻、在外卦的：金是四肢、骨节、牙齿、右耳、小便，木是筋骨、左耳，水是口、嘴、皮、尿、痰涎、血、汗，土是鼻尖、腹背、肌肉，火是眼睛、胸、手心、脚底；另外辰、戌是头顶，丑、未是肩背。再按八卦：乾是头，坤是腹，震是脚，巽是大腿，坎是耳朵，离是眼睛，艮是背和手指，兑是口和舌头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The symptoms are judged by the Pressure line: if the Pressure line holds the Body, the Self, the destiny line or the Subject line; or the ghost moves to punish, harm, control or clash them; or the Pressure line is hidden beneath them; or they move and change into Pressure: in every case judge by the element of Pressure. If the ghost is yin and in the inner trigram: Metal is the lungs, Wood the liver and gallbladder, Water the kidneys and Zi the bladder, Fire the heart and small intestine, Earth the spleen, stomach and large intestine. If the ghost is yang and in the outer trigram: Metal is the limbs, bone joints, teeth, right ear and urine; Wood the sinews and bones and the left ear; Water the mouth, lips, skin, urine, phlegm and spittle, blood and sweat; Earth the tip of the nose, belly and back, and flesh; Fire the eyes, chest, palms and soles; Chen and Xu are the crown of the head and Chou and Wei the shoulders and back. And by the eight trigrams: Qian is the head, Kun the belly, Zhen the feet, Xun the thighs, Kan the ears, Li the eyes, Gen the back and fingers, and Dui the mouth and tongue.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是易隐病症段的总纲，把五行、八卦、内外卦阴阳三套办法叠在一起：先看鬼在哪个五行，再看它属阴属阳、在内在外，得出是哪个脏腑或身体部位。黄金策讲同一件事的地方见 LY.ILL.HJC.02（五行定脏腑）、LY.ILL.HJC.04（八宫定病位）。「水为口嘴皮溺痰涎血汗」里的「溺」是尿，金那一栏的「小便」也是尿，原文两处都写。
>
> *Note:* This is the general outline of the Yi Yin's section on symptoms and lays three systems over one another — the five elements, the eight trigrams, and yin-yang with inner and outer trigrams: first see which element the ghost is, then whether it is yin or yang and inner or outer, to arrive at which viscus or part of the body. Where the Huangjin Ce treats the same thing see LY.ILL.HJC.02 (the element fixes the viscus) and LY.ILL.HJC.04 (the palace fixes the site of the illness). In 'Water is the mouth, lips, skin, urine ...' the 'ni' is urine, and 'xiaobian' in the Metal column is also urine; the original writes both.
