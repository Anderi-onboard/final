# 问何时出花以鬼爻为用（鬼静逢值逢冲、鬼动逢值逢合、鬼衰逢生旺之年、鬼旺鬼多逢墓库之岁、鬼空鬼破在填实之时、鬼伏在出现之岁）；见花问吉凶忌六冲、子孙化鬼、鬼化子、父化子、子化父、子孙伏而空破

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 平常问什么时候出花，拿鬼爻当用神：鬼爻静的，逢到值日、逢到冲的时候；鬼爻动的，逢到值日、逢到合；鬼爻衰的，在生旺的年份；鬼旺、鬼多的，在墓库的年岁；鬼空、鬼破的，在填实的时候；鬼伏藏的，在它出现的年岁。已经见花来问吉凶的，卦忌六冲，还忌子孙变鬼、鬼变子、父变子、子变父、子孙伏藏又空又破，都不是吉兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In ordinary times, when asking when the flowers will come out, the Pressure line is the Subject: if it is still, at a day it rules or a clash; if moving, when it rules the day or meets a combination; if weak, in a year of birth or strength; if strong or many, in the year of its tomb; if Void or broken, at the time it is filled; if hidden, in the year it appears. For one who asks about fortune when the flowers have already come out, the hexagram must avoid Six-Clash, and also Output changing into Pressure, Pressure into Output, Resource into Output, Output into Resource, and Output hidden and also Void and broken: none of these is a lucky sign.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「花」是痘花，出花就是出痘。「墓库」是鬼爻五行的墓；「填实」是落空或被破的爻等到它的地支值年、值月、值日，把空破填上。这一条问的是出花的时间，和 LY.ILL.ZS.18 里问「发热是不是出痘」不同。
>
> *Note:* The 'flowers' are the flowers of the pox, and to 'flower' is to have the pox come out. The 'tomb' is the tomb of the phase of the Pressure line, and 'filling' is for a Void or broken line to wait until its branch rules the year, month or day and fills the void or break. This entry asks when the flowers come out, which is different from asking in LY.ILL.ZS.18 whether a fever is smallpox.
