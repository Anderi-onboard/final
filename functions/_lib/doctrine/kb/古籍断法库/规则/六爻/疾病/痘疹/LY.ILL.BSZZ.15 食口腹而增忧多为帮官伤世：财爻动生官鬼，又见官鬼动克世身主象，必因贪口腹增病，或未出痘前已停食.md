# 食口腹而增忧多为帮官伤世：财爻动生官鬼，又见官鬼动克世身主象，必因贪口腹增病，或未出痘前已停食

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 贪吃口腹反而增加忧患，多半是帮了官爻、伤了世爻：财爻发动就生官鬼；如果卦中又见官鬼发动而克世爻、卦身、主象的，一定是因为贪吃而使病加重，或者没出痘之前就已经停食了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Indulging the mouth and belly adds to the trouble, mostly because it helps Pressure and harms the Self: when the Wealth line moves it generates Pressure; if in the hexagram Pressure also moves and controls the Self, the Body or the main image, it is surely because of indulging the mouth and belly that the illness has grown worse, or the patient had already stopped eating before the pox came out.
