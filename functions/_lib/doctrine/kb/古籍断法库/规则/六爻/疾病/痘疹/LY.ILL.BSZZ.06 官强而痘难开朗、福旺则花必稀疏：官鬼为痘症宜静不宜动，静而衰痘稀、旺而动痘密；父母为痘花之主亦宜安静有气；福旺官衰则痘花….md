# 官强而痘难开朗、福旺则花必稀疏：官鬼为痘症宜静不宜动，静而衰痘稀、旺而动痘密；父母为痘花之主亦宜安静有气；福旺官衰则痘花稀朗好收功

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官爻强，痘就难以开朗；福神旺，花一定稀疏。官鬼爻是痘症本身，适合安静、不适合发动，动了就有变化；安静而衰的痘稀，旺而发动的痘密。父母爻是痘花的主，也适合安静有气，最忌讳发动后变出伤克。如果福神旺、官鬼衰，痘花一定稀疏明朗，一定能好好收功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure is strong the pox will hardly open up clear; if Output is strong the flowers will surely be sparse. The Pressure line is the pox itself; it should be at rest and not move, for if it moves there is change: at rest and weak the pox is sparse, strong and moving the pox is dense. The Resource line is the master of the pox flowers and should likewise be at rest and have force, and most dreaded is moving and changing into harm or control. If Output is strong and Pressure weak, the pox flowers are sure to be sparse and clear, and will surely come to a good completion.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2239 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼爻为痘症，宜静不宜动，动则有变。 | 官鬼爻是痘症，适合安静、不适合发动，动了就有变化。 | The Pressure line is the pox itself; it should be at rest and not move, and if it moves there is change. |
> | 静而衰者痘稀，旺而动者痘密。 | 安静而衰的痘稀，旺而发动的痘密。 | If at rest and weak, the pox is sparse; if strong and moving, the pox is dense. |
> | 父神为痘花之主，亦宜安静有气，最忌动化伤克； | 父母爻是痘花的主，也适合安静有气，最忌讳发动后变出伤克； | The Resource line is the master of the pox flowers and should also be at rest with force, and most dreaded is moving and changing into harm or control; |
> | 若得福旺官衰，痘花定然稀朗，必好收功也。 | 如果福神旺、官爻衰，痘花一定稀疏明朗，一定能好好收功。 | if Output is strong and Pressure weak, the pox flowers are sure to be sparse and clear, and will surely come to a good completion. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「父神」注里没有解释，按上下文是父母爻（它生官鬼，所以说是痘花之主）。增删卜易的野鹤也说鬼爻旺相、临日月又有生扶花必稠密，空破休囚墓绝痘疏朗（LY.ILL.ZS.20），和这里「静而衰者痘稀，旺而动者痘密」一致；这里多了福神旺则花稀的一层。
>
> *Note:* 'Fu shen' is not explained in the commentary and by context is the Resource line (it generates Pressure, hence the master of the pox flowers). The Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi also says that a strong Pressure line on the Day or Month with support brings dense flowers, and Void, broken, resting, trapped, entombed or extinct brings sparse pox (LY.ILL.ZS.20), which agrees with 'at rest and weak, sparse; strong and moving, dense' here; this adds the further point that strong Output makes the flowers sparse.
