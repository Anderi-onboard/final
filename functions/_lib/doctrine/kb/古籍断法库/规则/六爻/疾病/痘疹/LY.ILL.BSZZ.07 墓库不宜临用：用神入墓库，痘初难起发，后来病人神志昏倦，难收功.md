# 墓库不宜临用：用神入墓库，痘初难起发，后来病人神志昏倦，难收功

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神不适合落在墓库上：痘喜欢起发，发出来以后又喜欢人神志清楚；用神入了墓库，一开始难以起发，后来病人一定神志昏沉疲倦，主难以收功。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject should not sit in a tomb or storehouse: the pox likes to erupt, and once erupted likes the patient to be clear in spirit; if the Subject enters a tomb, at first it is hard for it to erupt, and later the patient is surely dull and drowsy in spirit, which means it is hard to bring to completion.
