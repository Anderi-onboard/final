# 六神临鬼各主一症：螣蛇心惊、青龙酒色过度、勾陈肿胀、朱雀言语颠狂、白虎损伤血崩血晕、玄武忧郁阴虚

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻临着哪个六神，就是哪一种病：螣蛇鬼坐卧不安、心神不定；青龙鬼酒色过度、瘦弱无力；勾陈鬼胸腹胀满、脾胃不和；朱雀鬼狂言乱语、身热面赤；白虎鬼跌打损伤，女人则血崩血晕、产后各症（白虎是血神）；玄武鬼色欲过度、忧闷在心，在本宫主阴虚。最后一句叫人变通着断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Which of the six spirits the Pressure line sits on fixes the kind of illness: Coiling Snake Pressure, unable to sit or lie at ease and the mind unsettled; Azure Dragon Pressure, excess of wine and lust, thin and without strength; Hook Earth Pressure, fullness and swelling, Spleen and Stomach out of harmony; Vermilion Bird Pressure, wild talk, hot body and red face; White Tiger Pressure, falls and blows and injury, and in a woman flooding and blood-faintness and the ailments after childbirth (the White Tiger is the spirit of blood); Dark Tortoise Pressure, excess of lust and gloom pent in the heart, and in the home palace yin deficiency. The last sentence tells one to judge flexibly.

> [!note] 按
> 玄武鬼化子孙：旧注说男子必阳痿，王洪绪写成男子阴症阴虚，两家只差一个病名，意思都是男子的房室虚损。卜筮正宗白虎鬼一句的「跌打气防」四个字不通，大概是「跌扑打斗」之误，译文按跌打译。「阴症」是中医说的阴证（虚寒一类的病），不是阴性病。
>
> *Note:* For a Dark Tortoise Pressure changing into Output, the old commentary says a man surely suffers impotence and Wang Hongxu writes yin syndrome and yin deficiency; the two differ by one disease name and both mean a man's depletion in bedchamber matters. The four words 'die da qi fang' in the Bushi Zhengzong's White Tiger clause do not read and are probably a slip for 'falls, blows and fights'; the translation takes it as falls and blows. A 'yin syndrome' is a yin-type disorder in Chinese medicine (a cold, deficient kind of illness) and not an illness of a yin nature.
