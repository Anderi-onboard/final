# 按八宫论病起何处：坤坟野、艮山林、离窑炉、乾兑寺庙、震巽树林花草路、坎江河池沼；鬼值本宫非出境；独发看地支所属之方；乱动照八卦推，鬼在本宫或内卦为当地之灾、在外卦或他宫为外方之灾

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 坤宫鬼旺，是遭了坟地野外；艮宫官动，是起于山林；离宫，或是到了窑炉的地方；乾、兑，是曾冲犯寺庙的门；震、巽，是树林花草和道路；坎卦，是江河池沼村落。鬼在本宫，不是出了境；官鬼在别的宫惹祸，又另外评断。注说：官鬼发动时，不像前面那样断：鬼在坤宫，一定是往西南犯了祟、或者登了坟墓、或者在田野之间；鬼在艮宫，曾经去过东北，不然就是往山林；鬼在离宫，南方染的祸、或者去了火场、或者到了窑炉的地方；鬼在兑宫，正西有所冒犯，或者去了庵堂、或者住在水口；鬼在震宫，正东惹祸，或者去了树林下、或者上了船轿；巽宫鬼发，曾经走过东南、或者到了竹园旁边、或者住在柴草旁；鬼在坎宫，病从北方来，或者踏过江湖之口、或者遇上骤雨淋身。鬼如果独发，再看地支所属的方位，比如贲卦初爻独发，病起东方，其余都照这样；鬼不是独发的，就不必说；卦爻乱动，照前面的八卦推，再看鬼在本宫或在内卦，就说当地的灾；鬼在外卦或者在他宫动的，要推病起于外地，学的人自己要变通。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost thriving in Kun means graves and open country; Pressure moving in Gen rises from mountain and forest; in Li, perhaps a kiln or furnace; in Qian and Dui, having struck against the gate of a temple; in Zhen and Xun, woods, flowers and paths; in Kan, rivers, pools and villages. A ghost in its home palace has not left the region; trouble incurred in another quarter is judged otherwise. The commentary says: when the Pressure line moves, judgment is not as above: a ghost in Kun surely means offending a haunting in the southwest, or climbing a grave, or being in the fields; in Gen he has gone northeast, or else to the mountains and forest; in Li, misfortune caught in the south, or a trip to a fire ground or a kiln; in Dui, an offence in the due west, or a trip to a hermitage, or living by a water mouth; in Zhen, misfortune in the due east, or being under the trees or in a boat or sedan; a ghost moving in Xun, he has walked the southeast, or been by a bamboo garden, or by firewood and grass; a ghost in Kan, the illness came from the north, or he trod on the mouth of a river or lake, or was soaked in a sudden rain. If the ghost moves alone, look again at the direction the earthly branch belongs to, so that in Bi with the first line moving alone the illness rose in the east, and the rest follow this; if the ghost does not move alone there is no need to say more; if the lines move in disorder, reckon by the eight trigrams as above, and see whether the ghost is in the home palace or the inner trigram, which is a local calamity; a ghost in the outer trigram, or moving in another palace, calls for the illness to have risen in a place outside, and the learner must adapt.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是上一条（LY.ILL.EL.03）的「之一」，专讲鬼在哪个宫里动。原文注里「鬼在坤宫」出现了两次，第二次配的是「寺观、高楼」，而寺观、高楼在别处配的是乾宫（见 LY.ILL.EL.04 歌诀「乾兑曾冲寺庙门」和易冒 LY.ILL.YM.57），所以第二个「坤宫」疑是「乾宫」之误，这里照字面译，只在括号里标出。「水口」是水流出入的地方。「本宫」「内卦」是当地，「外卦」「他宫」是外地。八宫配的处所与易冒鬼神章的八宫配法（LY.ILL.YM.57）、黄金策的八宫推鬼神（LY.ILL.HJC.95）是同一套八卦象，细目不同。
>
> *Note:* This is the 'first' of the sequel to the last entry (LY.ILL.EL.03), and speaks only of the palace in which the ghost moves. In the note 'a ghost in Kun' occurs twice, and the second is paired with 'temples and abbeys, a high tower', which are elsewhere paired with Qian (see the verse of this entry, 'in Qian and Dui, having struck against the gate of a temple', and the Yi Mao's LY.ILL.YM.57), so the second 'Kun' looks like an error for 'Qian'; it is translated literally, with the doubt marked in brackets. A 'water mouth' is a place where water flows in or out. The 'home palace' and the 'inner trigram' are the local place, and the 'outer trigram' and 'another palace' are a place elsewhere. The places assigned to the eight palaces are the same images of the trigrams as in the Yi Mao's assignments in the chapter on ghosts and spirits (LY.ILL.YM.57) and the Huangjin Ce's reading by the eight palaces (LY.ILL.HJC.95), with differences of detail.
