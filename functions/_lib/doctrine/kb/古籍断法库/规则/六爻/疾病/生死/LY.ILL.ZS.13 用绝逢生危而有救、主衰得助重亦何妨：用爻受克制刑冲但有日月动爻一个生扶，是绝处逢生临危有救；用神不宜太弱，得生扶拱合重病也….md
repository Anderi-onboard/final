# 用绝逢生危而有救、主衰得助重亦何妨：用爻受克制刑冲但有日月动爻一个生扶，是绝处逢生临危有救；用神不宜太弱，得生扶拱合重病也不至死；觉子：用神也不宜太旺，过犹不及（旺极的老人卦全胜，壬戌日死）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻在绝地遇到生，危险却有救；主象衰弱得到帮助，病重又有什么妨碍。用爻受到克制、刑冲，只要日月、动爻有一个来生扶它，就是绝处逢生，临危有救。用神不宜太弱，太弱就体虚，一时难以痊愈，但只要得到生扶拱合，就算得重病，也不至于死。觉子补充：用神也不宜太旺，易理贵在中庸，过头和不及一样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject meeting life in extinction is in danger yet rescued; a weak main image that is helped, however heavy the illness, what does it matter? If the Subject line is controlled, punished or clashed, but the Day, Month or a moving line has one that generates and supports it, this is meeting life in a place of extinction, and in danger it is rescued. The Subject should not be too weak; if too weak the body is frail and cannot quickly heal, but if it gets generation, support, embracing or combining, then even in a heavy illness he does not die. Juezi adds: the Subject should not be too strong either; the principle of the Changes prizes the mean, and going too far is like falling short.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是黄金策病体章 LY.ILL.HJC.47（用绝逢生）和 LY.ILL.HJC.48（主衰得助）的合并重写，觉子那一句「用神也不宜太旺」对应 LY.ILL.HJC.36（太刚则折）。书中例是自占病的一卦：卦旺到极点，老人反而在壬戌日死了，用来说明旺过头也不好。案语「过十八壬戌日」按字面译。
>
> *Note:* This entry is a combined rewriting of LY.ILL.HJC.47 (the Subject meeting life in extinction) and LY.ILL.HJC.48 (a weak main image that is helped) in the chapter on the patient's condition, and Juezi's line 'the Subject should not be too strong either' matches LY.ILL.HJC.36 (too rigid, it snaps). The case is a divination on one's own illness: the hexagram is at the extreme of strength and the old man on the contrary dies on the Renxu day, which shows that strength carried too far is not good either. The phrase 'once the eighteenth, the Renxu day, is past' is translated as it stands.
