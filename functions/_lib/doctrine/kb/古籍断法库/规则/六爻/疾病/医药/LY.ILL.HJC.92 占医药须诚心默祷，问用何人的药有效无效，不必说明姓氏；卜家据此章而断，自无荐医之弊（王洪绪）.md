# 占医药须诚心默祷，问用何人的药有效无效，不必说明姓氏；卜家据此章而断，自无荐医之弊（王洪绪）

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是占医药，要诚心默默祷告，问用哪个人的药有效还是无效，不必说出医生的姓氏。卜家依照这一章来断，自然没有替医生推荐的毛病，心诚没有不感通的，卦没有不灵验的，这不是彼此都安心吗？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whoever divines about medicine must pray silently and sincerely, asking whether the medicine of a certain person will or will not be effective, and need not state the doctor's surname. A diviner who judges by this chapter will have no abuse of touting doctors; sincerity never fails to reach, and the hexagram never fails to prove true; is that not peace of mind for both sides?

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一段是王洪绪自己加在医药章末尾的话，卜筮大全没有对应的文字。「荐医之弊」是占卜的人借断卦替某位医生说好话、推荐他的毛病。
>
> *Note:* This passage is Wang Hongxu's own addition at the end of the chapter on medicine, and the Bushi Daquan has no matching text. The 'abuse of touting doctors' is the fault of a diviner who uses his judgment of the hexagram to speak well of a certain doctor and recommend him.
