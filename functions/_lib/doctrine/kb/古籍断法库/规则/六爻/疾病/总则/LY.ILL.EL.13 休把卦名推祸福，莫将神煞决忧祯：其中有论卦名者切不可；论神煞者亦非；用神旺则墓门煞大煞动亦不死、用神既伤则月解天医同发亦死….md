# 休把卦名推祸福，莫将神煞决忧祯：其中有论卦名者切不可；论神煞者亦非；用神旺则墓门煞大煞动亦不死、用神既伤则月解天医同发亦死；五行为重，神煞难凭，星家专忌本命空亡非正道

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 不要用卦名去推祸福，不要拿神煞去断忧祯。注说：其中有拿卦名来论的，切不可；经里说，易卦的渊源是论五行阴阳的道理，本来生生不息，可怜愚昧无知的人颠倒阴阳去论卦名，如今有人拿神煞来论的也不对；经里又说，易卦阴阳在变通，五行生克妙无穷，现在的人不明白阴阳的道理，拿神煞来定吉凶。比如儿子占父亲的病，父爻旺相，墓门煞或大煞又动，用爻有气，怎么会死？又如丈夫占妻子的病，财爻无气，父爻兄弟爻都动，纵然月解、天医同时发动，用爻既然受了伤，怎么能不推断为死？所以以五行为重，神煞难以凭信，星家专门忌讳本命空亡，这不是正道。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Do not push fortune and misfortune by the names of hexagrams, and do not decide grief and omen by spirit-stars. The commentary says: those who judge by hexagram names must on no account be followed; the classic says the source of the Yi hexagrams is to judge by the principles of the five phases and yin and yang, which by nature give birth without end, and pitiable ignorant people turn yin and yang upside down and judge by hexagram names, and those who now judge by spirit-stars are also wrong; the classic says again that in the hexagrams yin and yang lie in change, and the generation and control of the five phases are marvellous without end, but people now do not understand the principle of yin and yang and use the spirit-stars to fix fortune. For example, if a son divines a father's illness and the Resource line is strong, and the Tomb Gate star or the Great Killing also moves, the Subject line having qi, how could he die? Again, if a husband divines a wife's illness and the Wealth line has no qi, and the Resource and Peer lines both move, even if Month Release and Heavenly Doctor move together, the Subject being already wounded, how can one judge otherwise than death? Therefore the five phases are weighty and the spirit-stars hard to rely on, and that astrologers particularly fear the Void of the natal branch is not the right way.

> [!warning] 打架
> 卦名、神煞能不能断病的生死：易林补遗说五行为重、神煞难凭，卦名不可论，星家专忌本命空亡不是正道；增删卜易的野鹤同样说卦名、卦身世身兼用、本命丧门、蛇虎星煞都不验，只看用神，只有卦变六冲例外（LY.ILL.ZS.01）；易冒也说四灭没、十卦、凶煞皆不死，神煞尤轻（LY.ILL.YM.11）；易隐则把黄泉杀、四灭卦、四没卦、棺椁杀列为占病逢之必死，又把三坵五墓、丧门吊客、死符丧车等列为动而临用必死的凶煞（LY.ILL.YY.05、YY.08）。前三家一边，易隐一边。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether hexagram names and spirit-stars can judge life and death in illness: the Yilin Buyi says the five phases weigh most and the spirit-stars are hard to rely on, that hexagram names cannot be used, and that astrologers' special fear of the Void of the natal branch is not the right way; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi likewise says that hexagram names, using the Body and Self together, the mourning stars on the natal branch, and the Snake and Tiger stars are all unproved, that only the Subject is looked at, with the Six-Clash changing into Six-Clash the one exception (LY.ILL.ZS.01); the Yi Mao also says the Four Extinguishing and Submerging, the ten hexagrams and the malign stars do not mean death, and that spirit-stars weigh lighter still (LY.ILL.YM.11); the Yi Yin, on the other hand, lists the Yellow Springs, the Four Extinguishing, the Four Submerging and the Coffin killers as hexagrams that mean death for one who divines illness, and lists Three Mounds and Five Tombs, Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence, Death Tally and Funeral Carriage and the like as malign stars that mean death when they move and sit on the Subject (LY.ILL.YY.05, YY.08). The first three stand on one side and the Yi Yin on the other.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段的两个「经云」是评注者引用的，原文没有说明出自哪部经，这里照录、没有指认出处。「墓门煞」「大煞」「月解」「天医」都是神煞名（月解、天医是吉神，墓门煞、大煞是凶煞）。评注者用两个反例把「神煞能救」「神煞能杀」都打掉：用爻有气，凶煞动也不死；用爻受伤，吉神动也死。「星家」指按命理星命来断的人。
>
> *Note:* The two 'the classic says' in this passage are quoted by the commentator, and the original does not say from which classic; they are copied as they stand and no source is asserted. 'Tomb Gate', 'Great Killing', 'Month Release' and 'Heavenly Doctor' are names of spirit-stars (Month Release and Heavenly Doctor being auspicious, Tomb Gate and Great Killing malign). The commentator uses two counter-examples to knock down both 'a spirit-star can save' and 'a spirit-star can kill': with the Subject line in qi a malign star moving does not kill, and with the Subject wounded an auspicious star moving does not save. 'Astrologers' are those who judge by the astrology of fate.
