# 主绝受伤卢医难救：主象在休囚死墓绝之地又被日辰动爻乘旺刑克，或变入死墓空绝，病必死，虽良医无效

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 主象处在休囚死墓绝的地方，又被日辰、动爻趁着旺势刑克，这病一定死；主象变成死、墓、空、绝的，也是这样。就算有好医生，也没有效果。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A main image in a place of rest, trap, death, tomb or extinction that is also punished or controlled by the Day or a moving line riding its strength: the patient will surely die; a main image that changes into death, tomb, Void or extinction is the same. Even with a good doctor there is no effect.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7034 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Doctors and medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 主绝受伤，虚医难救； | 主象在绝地又受了伤，虚医也难以救； | The main image in extinction and wounded: a physician of no substance can hardly save him; |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2450 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 主象若遇休囚墓绝，或变入墓绝，再有克伤者，虽良医不能救也。 | 主象如果遇到休囚墓绝，或者变入墓绝，又有克伤它的，就算好医生也救不了。 | If the main image meets rest, trap, tomb or extinction, or changes into tomb or extinction, and something also controls and harms it, even a good doctor cannot save him. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文写「虚医难救」，王洪绪引作「卢医难救」。卢医就是和扁鹊齐名的古代名医，和下一条的「扁鹊无功」相对；「虚医」大概是「卢医」之误，译文按经文字面译。
>
> *Note:* The verse reads 'an empty physician can hardly save him' where Wang Hongxu quotes it as 'Lu the physician can hardly save him'. Lu Yi is the ancient healer named together with Bian Que, and matches the 'Bian Que has no success' of the next entry; 'empty physician' is probably a slip for 'Lu Yi', and the translation follows the letter of the verse.
