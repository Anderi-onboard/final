# 鬼在宅中住居不稳、官临应上朝向不通：内卦第二爻是宅，动鬼临之宅不安常有病；应爻临鬼宅朝向不利宜改

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼在住宅的位置上，住得不安稳；官落在应爻上，房子的朝向不顺。内卦的第二爻是宅：发动的鬼落在这里，住宅不安，常常有病；应爻带着鬼，这座宅子的朝向不利，应该改；旧注还说冲克二爻的爻也是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost in the house position means the dwelling is unsettled; Pressure on the Other line means the orientation of the house is blocked. The second line of the inner trigram is the house: if a moving ghost sits there, the dwelling is unsettled and there is often illness; if the Other line carries the ghost, the orientation of the house is unfavourable and should be changed; the old commentary adds that a line that clashes with or controls the second line does likewise.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7167 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 内卦第二爻为宅。 | 内卦的第二爻是宅。 | The second line of the inner trigram is the house. |
> | 若动鬼临之，宅不安，常有病。 | 如果发动的鬼落在这里，住宅不安，常常有病。 | If a moving ghost sits there, the house is unsettled and there is often illness. |
> | 若冲克之爻，或应爻临鬼，其宅朝向不利，宜改。 | 如果是冲克它的爻，或者应爻带着鬼，这座宅子的朝向不利，应该改。 | If a line clashes with or controls it, or the Other line carries the ghost, the orientation of the house is unfavourable and should be changed. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3407 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 内卦第二爻为宅若动鬼临之，住宅不安，常有疾病； | 内卦第二爻是宅，如果发动的鬼落在这里，住宅不安，常常有病； | The second line of the inner trigram is the house; if a moving ghost sits there, the dwelling is unsettled and there is often illness; |
> | 若应爻临鬼，其宅朝向不利，宜改作为喜。 | 如果应爻带着鬼，这座宅子的朝向不利，改动一下才好。 | if the Other line carries the ghost, the orientation of the house is unfavourable, and it is well to make changes. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注多出「冲克二爻的爻」一种情形，王洪绪没有。家宅本身怎么断，旧注说另有家宅章，这里只讲鬼和宅的关系。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary adds the case of a line that clashes with or controls the second line, which Wang Hongxu lacks. How to judge the household itself the old commentary leaves to the chapter on the household; here it treats only the relation of the ghost to the house.
