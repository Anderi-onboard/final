# 鬼是忧神、子孙是喜悦解忧之神：鬼持世或动，得子孙动而制之，此医可请手到成功；子孙临应爻是明医，因为子孙克去的是忧不是邪魔（例：三十余人泪眼相对，子水兄爻旬空，半夜子时退灾）

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼这一爻，不是鬼也不是病，是忧神：替儿子占病的，父母爱子的心，没病时都怕他生病担忧，何况已经病了；自己占病的，性命在一呼一吸之间，一个人起不来，全家惊慌，谁不担忧？所以官鬼就是一家人的忧神，能解这份忧的，只有子孙爻。鬼持世或在卦中发动，得到子孙发动来制服它，这个医生可以请，一下手就成功；子孙临在应爻上，是高明的医生，因为子孙克掉的是忧神，我就没有忧愁了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line is neither ghost nor illness but the spirit of worry: in divining a son's illness, the parents' love for their son is such that even before he is ill they fear illness and worry, and how much more when he is already ill; in divining one's own illness, life hangs on a breath, and when one person cannot rise the whole household is alarmed, and who would not worry? So the Pressure line is the spirit of worry of the whole household, and what can relieve that worry is only the Output line. If Pressure holds the Self line or moves in the hexagram and Output moves to restrain it, this doctor can be called and his hand will succeed; if Output sits on the Other line he is a brilliant doctor, because what Output controls away is the spirit of worry, and then I have no worry.

> [!warning] 打架
> 子孙是什么：黄金策旧注、王洪绪都说子孙是医药（LY.ILL.HJC.32）；野鹤说官鬼不是鬼也不是病，是一家人的忧神，子孙不是药，是解忧的喜悦之神，所以「子孙旺动，不药而愈」。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What Output is: the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce and Wang Hongxu both say Output is the medicine (LY.ILL.HJC.32); the Old Man says the Pressure line is neither ghost nor illness but the spirit of worry of the whole household, and that Output is not medicine but the spirit of joy that relieves worry, so that 'with Output strong and moving, one recovers without medicine'.

> [!note] 按
> 这是野鹤对「子孙为医药」的新解释，后面几条（LY.ILL.ZS.28、ZS.37、ZS.39、ZS.40）都建立在它上面。书中例里近病、子水兄弟爻落旬空，野鹤断半夜子时（空被填实）退灾，次日卯日（子孙日）起床。子孙（福）一词在译文里保留为 Output。
>
> *Note:* This is the Old Man's new explanation of 'Output is the medicine', and several later entries (LY.ILL.ZS.28, ZS.37, ZS.39 and ZS.40) rest on it. In the case the illness is recent and the Zi Water Peer line falls into Void; the Old Man fixed the Zi hour at midnight (when the Void is filled) for the trouble to recede, and the next day, the Mao day (a day of Output), for him to rise. The word for Output as 'fortune' is kept as Output in the translation.
