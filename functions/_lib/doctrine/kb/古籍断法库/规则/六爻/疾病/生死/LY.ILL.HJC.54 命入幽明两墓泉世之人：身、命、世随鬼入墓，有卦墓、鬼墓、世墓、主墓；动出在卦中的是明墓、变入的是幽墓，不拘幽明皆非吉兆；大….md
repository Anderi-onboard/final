# 命入幽明两墓泉世之人：身、命、世随鬼入墓，有卦墓、鬼墓、世墓、主墓；动出在卦中的是明墓、变入的是幽墓，不拘幽明皆非吉兆；大象凶又逢墓动、或世爻主象俱入墓者必死，动爻日辰冲破墓爻则无事

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 身、命、世随着鬼入墓，墓有卦墓、鬼墓、世墓、主墓几种。凡是动出来在卦里的，人人都看得见，那个墓叫明墓；变进墓里去的，人看不见，那个墓叫幽墓。不管幽的明的，都不是吉兆：大局就算不死，病也危险困顿；如果大局本来就凶，又遇到墓发动，或者世爻和主象都入了墓，一定死。得到动爻、日辰把墓爻冲破，才可能没事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Body, natal and Self lines follow Pressure into a tomb: there are the tomb of the hexagram, the tomb of Pressure, the tomb of the Self and the tomb of the main image. A tomb that moves out in the hexagram, which everyone can see, is a bright tomb; one that a line changes into, which people cannot see, is a dark tomb. Whether dark or bright, neither is a lucky sign: even if the whole image does not show death, the illness is dangerous and hard; if the whole image is already bad and a tomb also moves, or the Self line and the main image both enter a tomb, he will surely die. If a moving line or the Day clashes and breaks the tomb line, there may be no harm.

> | 得日辰动爻冲破墓爻，庶几无事。 | 得到日辰、动爻把墓爻冲破，才差不多没事。 | If the Day or a moving line clashes and breaks the tomb line, there may be no harm. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 墓有几种、什么情形必死：旧注列四种墓（卦墓、鬼墓、世墓、主墓，「以日时看」），必死的条件是大象凶又逢墓动、或世爻主象俱入墓；王洪绪列三种（鬼墓、世墓、主墓，分别从卦、世爻、用神看），必死的条件是世爻用爻被官鬼两头夹着、或有两重鬼墓夹身。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How many kinds of tomb there are and what makes death certain: the old commentary lists four (the tomb of the hexagram, of Pressure, of the Self and of the main image, 'viewing by the day and hour') and makes death certain when the whole image is bad and a tomb moves, or when the Self line and the main image both enter a tomb; Wang Hongxu lists three (the tombs of Pressure, of the Self and of the main image, seen from the hexagram, the Self line and the Subject) and makes death certain when the Self or Subject line is clamped by Pressure lines on both sides or two layers of Pressure tombs clamp the body.

> [!note] 按
> 「明墓」是墓爻自己动出来、摆在卦面上的；「幽墓」是动爻变进墓里去、卦面上看不见的。经文「泉世之人」四个字字面是「泉下和在世的人」，两家的注都没有解释。
>
> *Note:* A 'bright tomb' is one whose tomb line has itself moved out and lies on the face of the hexagram; a 'dark tomb' is one that a moving line changes into, which cannot be seen on the face of the hexagram. The four characters 'quan shi zhi ren' of the verse literally mean 'a person of the springs below and of the world', and neither commentary explains them.
