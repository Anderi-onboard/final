# 火鬼冲财上临则呕逆多吐：火鬼动克外卦财爻必呕吐，重则反胃不食；财带螣蛇吐虫，财在间爻消渴易饥

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 火的性子向上，财爻代表饮食。占病遇到火鬼动来克外卦的财爻，一定是呕吐，重的是反胃、吃不下饭。财爻带着螣蛇，吐出来的还带虫；财爻在间爻，是消渴、容易饿的症候。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fire's nature is to flare upward and Wealth stands for food and drink. In divining illness, a Fire Pressure moving to control the outer Wealth line is surely vomiting, and when heavy, a stomach that turns and cannot take food. If Wealth carries the Coiling Snake, worms are brought up as well; if Wealth is on the between line, it is the syndrome of thirst and quick hunger.
