# 疾病歌(二)：凡占疾病先从卦中寻本命、仍看来者是何人（克者是何加世应）；福德持世鬼暂退、官旺之日又加进；卦中无鬼病难医、鬼旺财兴难保命；鬼杀临身五墓侵鬼神不应药无灵；一为五脏二皮肉三为体骨腰并足四肺心经五六头；游魂恍惚如神触、归魂渐向安

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是有人占疾病，先从卦里找本命，还要看来占的是什么人，克它的又是什么、加在世应上。福德持世，鬼暂时退去；官鬼旺相的日子又进一步。卦里没有鬼，病难医；鬼旺、财兴，难保命。鬼杀临在身上、又有五墓侵犯，鬼神不应验，药也无灵。第一爻是五脏，第二爻是皮肉，第三爻是体骨、腰和足；第四爻是肺和心经，第五、第六爻是头。游魂卦恍惚，像被神鬼触到；只有归魂卦，渐渐向安；吉凶我细细地为你嘱咐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If someone divines an illness, first look in the hexagram for the natal line, and still look at who has come, and what it is that controls, added to the Self and Other. Output holding the Self: the ghost retreats for a time; on a day when the Pressure line is strong it advances again. With no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to treat; with the ghost strong and Wealth rising life is hard to keep. A ghost-killing sitting on the Body with the Five Tombs invading: the ghosts and spirits do not respond and medicine has no effect. The first line is the five viscera, the second skin and flesh, the third body, bones, waist and feet; the fourth is the lungs and heart channel, the fifth and sixth the head. The Wandering Soul hexagram: dazed, as if touched by spirits; only the Returning Soul gradually turns to peace; good and ill I tell you in detail.

> [!warning] 打架
> 哪一爻管身体哪一处，几本书的说法差得很远。阐奥歌章这一首（LY.ILL.CA.04）是「一爻五脏、二爻皮肉、三爻体骨腰并足、四爻肺心经、五六爻头」，断易天机的孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）同；断易天机卜筮元龟一段（见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.11）是「初爻两足、二爻双腿、三爻腰背、四爻心腹及肚肠、五爻肾脏、六爻头」；断易天机鬼谷辨爻法的表（LY.ILL.DYTJ.01）是「初足、二腿股、三腰、四脊、五心、六头」；易隐引鬼谷分爻（LY.ILL.YY.02）是「初爻足、二爻腿股、三爻肝肾、四爻脾脏、五爻心肺、六爻头脑」；卜筮大全卜筮全书歌诀里的六位取象表（见 LY.ILL.BSQS.01）疾病一行是「足、腿肢、（缺）、（缺）、心肺、头脑」。卜筮元龟、鬼谷辨爻法的表、易隐、六位表都是「初足、二腿、上头」，只有阐奥歌章和孙膑歌把初爻配成五脏、二爻配成皮肉。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line governs which part of the body is put very differently in several books. This poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.04) has 'the first line the five viscera, the second skin and flesh, the third body, bones, waist and feet, the fourth lungs and heart channel, the fifth and sixth the head', and Sun Bin's song in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) is the same; the Bushi Yuangui passage in the Duanyi Tianji (see LY.ILL.DYTJ.11) has 'the first line the two feet, the second both legs, the third waist and back, the fourth heart, belly and bowels, the fifth the kidneys, the sixth the head'; the table headed Guigu's method of telling the lines apart in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.01) has 'first the feet, second the legs and thighs, third the waist, fourth the spine, fifth the heart, sixth the head'; the Yi Yin, quoting Guigu's division of the lines (LY.ILL.YY.02), has 'first the feet, second the legs and thighs, third liver and kidneys, fourth the spleen, fifth heart and lungs, sixth the head and brain'; and the table of the six positions in the verses of the Bushi Quanshu in the Buyi Daquan (see LY.ILL.BSQS.01) has for illness 'feet, legs and limbs, (missing), (missing), heart and lungs, head and brain'. The Bushi Yuangui, Guigu's table, the Yi Yin and the table of the six positions all run 'first line the feet, second the legs, top the head', and only the Chan'ao Gezhang and Sun Bin's song match the first line with the viscera and the second with skin and flesh. The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 这是第二首疾病歌，它在断易天机里作「孙膑断疾病歌」（从 LY.ILL.DYTJ.02 起到 LY.ILL.DYTJ.07，共六条），字句略有出入。「先从卦中寻本命」的「本命」是病人的本命爻（生年属相所在的爻）；「克者是何加世应」文字含混，按字面译。「福德持世鬼暂退，官旺之日又加进」是说子孙持世时鬼暂时退去，但官鬼旺相的日子又会进一步。「五墓」是五墓煞（春未、夏戌、秋丑、冬辰，见 LY.ILL.EL.51）。「游魂恍惚如神触」的「游魂」「归魂」是卦的名称（游魂卦、归魂卦），「恍惚」是神志不清。整首和易林补遗的「卦无用象」（LY.ILL.EL.10）、海底眼的「疾病」一节（LY.ILL.HDY.01）是同一类判据：先找本命、看来者何人、看鬼有无。
>
> *Note:* This is the second illness poem, which appears in the Duanyi Tianji as 'Sun Bin's song on judging illness' (six entries, LY.ILL.DYTJ.02 to LY.ILL.DYTJ.07), with slight differences in wording. The 'natal line' of 'first look in the hexagram for the natal line' is the sick person's natal line (the line where the birth animal falls); 'what it is that controls, added to the Self and Other' is loose in wording and is rendered literally. 'Output holding the Self, the ghost retreats for a time; on a day when the Pressure line is strong it advances again' says that when Output holds the Self the ghost retreats for a time, but on a day when the Pressure line is strong it advances again. The 'Five Tombs' is the Five Tombs killing (Wei in spring, Xu in summer, Chou in autumn, Chen in winter; see LY.ILL.EL.51). The 'Wandering Soul' and 'Returning Soul' of 'the Wandering Soul hexagram, dazed as if touched by spirits' are names of hexagrams (the Wandering Soul hexagram and the Returning Soul hexagram), and 'dazed' is clouded consciousness. The whole poem is on the same kind of criteria as the Yilin Buyi's 'when the hexagram has no Subject image' (LY.ILL.EL.10) and the Haidi Yan's section on illness (LY.ILL.HDY.01): first find the natal line, look at who has come, and look at whether there is a ghost.
