# 占疾病·应药世身、若坐墓鬼病主昏沉；卦有三墓（宫墓、鬼墓、财墓），世爻逢墓鬼主自身合灾（暴病未可、久病必死）；应为药忌坐鬼、本宫墓鬼得之主无药；最怕申酉持世、木爻独发

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占疾病，应是药，世是身；世若坐在墓、鬼上，病主昏沉。卦有三墓（宫墓、鬼墓、财墓），以世为身，忌讳生鬼爻；本宫的墓、鬼落在世爻上，主自身该有灾：暴病还不能好，久病必死。以应为药，忌讳坐着鬼爻，旺相就凶；本宫的墓、鬼落在应上，主没有药，服药不见效。最怕申、酉爻持世，还有木爻独发、鬼爻旺相伏在世下、旺又动来克世。财墓是吉兆，占的是妇人时遇到它却要大忌；鬼旺伏在世下，是病跟着我，所以忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness the Other is the medicine and the Self is the Body; if the Self sits on a tomb or a ghost the illness means stupor. A hexagram has three tombs (the palace tomb, the ghost's tomb and the Wealth tomb); taking the Self as the Body it is taboo to generate the Pressure line; when the own palace's tomb or ghost falls on the Self line the person is due a calamity: a sudden illness cannot yet recover and a long illness surely ends in death. Taking the Other as the medicine, it is taboo for it to sit on a Pressure line and if strong it is ominous; when the own palace's tomb or ghost falls on the Other there is no medicine and taking it has no effect. Most feared are a Shen or You line holding the Self, a Wood line moving alone, and the Pressure line strong and hidden under the Self, strong and also moving to control the Self. The Wealth tomb is a lucky sign, but when a woman is being divined for, meeting it is a great taboo; a strong ghost hidden under the Self is the illness following me, and so it is a taboo.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是火珠林「占疾病」的头一部分：先是一副对句（凡占疾病，应药世身；若坐墓鬼，病主昏沉），然后一段一段地展开，末了是「或问……答曰」的问答。原文「封有三墓」的「封」是「卦」之误，「中孚封」同；「艮官属土」的「官」是「宫」之误；这里都按卦、宫译。「旁爻丑持世」的「旁爻」字面译作 the side line。三墓是宫墓（卦所属宫的五行的墓）、鬼墓（鬼爻五行的墓）、财墓（财爻五行的墓）：姤卦世爻丑，乾宫属金，金墓在丑，是宫墓；中孚世爻未，艮宫属土，以寅木为鬼，木墓在未，是鬼墓；泰卦世爻辰，坤宫属土，以亥水为财，水墓在辰，是财墓。「得之」是在世爻上遇到。对句里说「三墓」却只列宫基（宫墓）和鬼墓两个，第三个财墓在问答里补出；「本宫墓鬼得之」读作本宫的墓或鬼，原文没有拆开。这里说的世爻遇本宫墓、鬼，和天玄赋「不宜丘墓同宫」（LY.ILL.TXF.08）、断易天机「卦变墓乡」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.07）是同一类。
>
> *Note:* This section is the first part of the Huozhu Lin's 'divining illness': first a couplet ('In divining illness the Other is the medicine and the Self is the Body; if it sits on a tomb or a ghost the illness means stupor'), then a passage at a time, and at the end questions and answers ('someone asks... the answer'). The 'feng' of the original 'feng you san mu' is a miswriting of 'gua' (hexagram), and so is that of 'Zhongfu feng'; the 'guan' of 'Gen guan shu tu' is a miswriting of 'gong' (palace); all are rendered as hexagram and palace here. The 'pang yao' of 'the side line Chou holding the Self' is rendered literally as 'the side line'. The three tombs are the palace tomb (the tomb of the phase of the palace the hexagram belongs to), the ghost's tomb (the tomb of the phase of the ghost line) and the Wealth tomb (the tomb of the phase of the Wealth line): in Gou the Self line is Chou, the Qian palace belongs to Metal and the tomb of Metal is at Chou, the palace tomb; in Zhongfu the Self line is Wei, the Gen palace belongs to Earth, taking Yin Wood as the ghost, and the tomb of Wood is at Wei, the ghost's tomb; in Tai the Self line is Chen, the Kun palace belongs to Earth, taking Hai Water as Wealth, and the tomb of Water is at Chen, the Wealth tomb. 'Obtaining it' is meeting it on the Self line. The couplet says 'three tombs' but lists only the palace tomb and the ghost's tomb, and the third, the Wealth tomb, is supplied in the questions and answers; 'the own palace's tomb or ghost obtains it' is read as the own palace's tomb or ghost, and the original does not separate them. The Self line meeting the own palace's tomb or ghost here is of the same kind as the Tianxuan Fu's 'the mound and the tomb must not share a palace' (LY.ILL.TXF.08) and the Duanyi Tianji's 'the hexagram changing into the tomb quarter' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.07).
