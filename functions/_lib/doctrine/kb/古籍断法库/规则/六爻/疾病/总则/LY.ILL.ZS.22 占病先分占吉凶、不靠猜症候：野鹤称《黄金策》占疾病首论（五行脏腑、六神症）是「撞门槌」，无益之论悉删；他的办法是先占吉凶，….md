# 占病先分占吉凶、不靠猜症候：野鹤称《黄金策》占疾病首论（五行脏腑、六神症）是「撞门槌」，无益之论悉删；他的办法是先占吉凶，卦吉许以调理即愈，卦凶命延医，鬼动克用神再占鬼神、家宅、坟茔

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病时不靠猜症候，先单占一卦问吉凶：卦吉就说调理调理就好，不必吃药求神；卦凶就叫他请医生；卦里鬼动克用神的，再占鬼神，或者占家宅、占坟墓，一定要弄清楚是犯了什么、用什么办法治。拿五行脏腑、六神去猜发冷发热这类症候，病人本来就知道，对他没有用处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness do not rely on guessing the symptoms, but first divine a hexagram on good or ill fortune alone: if it is lucky, say that with care he will recover and there is no need for medicine or prayer; if ill, tell him to call a doctor; if the hexagram shows Pressure moving to control the Subject, divine the ghosts and spirits again, or the house or the tomb, and be sure to find out what he has offended and how to treat it. To guess symptoms such as chills and fever from the five phases, the organs and the six spirits tells the patient nothing he does not already know, and is of no use to him.

> [!warning] 打架
> 占病开头该讲什么：黄金策的疾病章开头（LY.ILL.HJC.02 到 HJC.05）拿鬼爻的五行、宫和六神去断症候，并且用同一卦兼断死生；野鹤说这是撞门槌，只是猜发冷发热，对病人没有用，全部删掉，改成先占吉凶、再占治法。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What the divination of illness should begin with: the opening of the Huangjin Ce's chapter on illness (LY.ILL.HJC.02 to HJC.05) judges the symptoms from the phase of the Pressure line, the palace and the six spirits, and judges life and death from the same hexagram; the Old Man of Yehe says this is a battering ram, only a guess at chills and fever, of no use to the patient, and deletes it all, replacing it with first divining good or ill fortune and then divining the treatment.

> [!note] 按
> 「撞门槌」是拿来撞门的粗木槌，比喻只会硬撞一下、没有下文。第一段引的就是他要删的黄金策原文（见 LY.ILL.HJC.02 和 HJC.05）。「祈凶吉」的「祈」字按上下文是「占」，即占一卦问吉凶。
>
> *Note:* A 'battering ram' is a rough wooden beam used to batter a door, and is a figure for one who can only knock once and has nothing to follow. The first passage quotes the very text of the Huangjin Ce that he deletes (see LY.ILL.HJC.02 and HJC.05). The 'qi' of 'qi xiong ji' is from the context 'divine', that is, divine a hexagram on good or ill fortune.
