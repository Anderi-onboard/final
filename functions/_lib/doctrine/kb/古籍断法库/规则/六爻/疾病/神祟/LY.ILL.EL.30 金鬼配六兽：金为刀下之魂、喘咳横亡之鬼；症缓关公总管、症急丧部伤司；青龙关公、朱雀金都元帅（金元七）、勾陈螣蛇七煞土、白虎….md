# 金鬼配六兽：金为刀下之魂、喘咳横亡之鬼；症缓关公总管、症急丧部伤司；青龙关公、朱雀金都元帅（金元七）、勾陈螣蛇七煞土、白虎丧煞伤司、玄武曹堂水伤水道；退送则病体安康

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 金鬼，是死在刀下的魂，喘咳、横死的鬼：症状缓的，是关公、总管；症状急的，是丧部、伤司。带青龙，是汉寿亭侯（关公）；朱雀，是金都元帅；勾陈、螣蛇，是七煞；白虎，是丧殃；玄武，是曹堂、西府。把它送走，病体就安康。注说：金鬼，主刀枪伤死的鬼、喘咳的鬼、横死的鬼；症状缓的，宜向关爷和总管祷告；病急的，必须酬谢丧煞和伤神；带青龙，是云长公（关公）有所妨碍；临朱雀，是金元七作祟为灾；带勾陈、螣蛇，是七煞土；带白虎，是丧煞和伤司；带玄武，是水伤、水道一类。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Metal is the soul beneath the blade and the ghost of asthma-cough and violent death: if mild, Guan Gong and the Chief Official; if severe, the Mourning Department and the Injuring Office. With the Azure Dragon, the Marquis of Hanshou; the Vermilion Bird is the Golden Capital Marshal; Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake are the Seven Killings; the White Tiger is Mourning Calamity; the Dark Tortoise is the Cao Hall and the Western Bureau. Send it away, and the sick body is well. The commentary says: a Metal ghost governs ghosts of death by blade and spear, ghosts of asthma-cough and ghosts of violent death; if the symptoms are mild, pray to Lord Guan and the Chief Official; if the illness is urgent, one must make offering to the Mourning Killing and the Injuring spirit; with the Azure Dragon, it is an obstruction from Lord Yunchang; on the Vermilion Bird, Lord Jin Yuanqi is making the calamity; with Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake, it is the Seven Killings Earth; with the White Tiger, it is the Mourning Killing and the Injuring Office; with the Dark Tortoise, it is the water injury and water-way sort.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节起，五行各一段：先是歌诀，再是解说。「喘敕」的「敕」不成词，像是「嗽」（咳）之误，这里译作喘咳，在英文里保留了「chi」的标记；解说第一句「土刀枪伤死鬼」的「土」按前后文应是「主」（下面木鬼、水鬼、火鬼、土鬼都是「主……鬼」），这里按「主」译。「汉寿亭侯」是关羽的封号（汉寿亭侯），「云长」是关羽的字，所以青龙带金鬼说的是关公；「关公」「关爷」「云长公」是同一位。「总管」是民间对某类地方神的统称，「丧部」「伤司」「曹堂」「西府」按字面译，书里没有解释。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地（苕溪一带）的民间神祇和星煞，书里一个也没有解释（章末自己说别的郡的神司不同），这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* From this section there is one passage for each of the five phases: first a verse, then explanation. The 'chi' in 'asthma-cough (chi)' is not a word and looks like an error for 'cough' (sou), and it is rendered as asthma-cough with the 'chi' marked in the English; the 'tu' at the head of the first sentence of the explanation, 'tu blade-and-spear death ghosts', should by the context be 'zhu' (governs), as in the Wood, Water, Fire and Earth ghosts below, and it is translated as 'governs'. The Marquis of Hanshou is Guan Yu's title of enfeoffment and Yunchang is his style name, so the Azure Dragon on a Metal ghost speaks of Guan Gong; 'Guan Gong', 'Lord Guan' and 'Lord Yunchang' are one figure. 'Chief Official' is the popular collective name for a certain class of local god, and 'the Mourning Department', 'the Injuring Office', 'the Cao Hall' and 'the Western Bureau' are translated literally, the book not explaining them. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain (the book itself says at the end of the chapter that the gods of other prefectures differ), and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
