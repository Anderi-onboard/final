# 青龙得位终见安康、白虎伤身必成凶咎：青龙临子孙克世期日痊安、临应克世服药有效、临用爻发动纵凶险不伤、带鬼爻非凶恶发狂之病、三爻动香火为福；苍屏云青龙空亡并福德平日无阴骘、白虎加临主哭丧；白虎交重乃占病大咎——无气犹可旺相大凶、临丧门吊客死符丧车持克用爻决不起、得子孙旺动月解兴隆可回生

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙得位，终究见到安康；白虎伤身，必成凶咎。注说：青龙如果临着子孙又克世，到日期就痊愈安康；临在应爻上克世，服药有效；临在用爻上发动，纵有凶险也不受伤；带着鬼爻，也不是凶恶发狂的病；在三爻发动，香火是福。苍屏说：青龙空亡，和福德一起，病人平日没有阴德；遇土动、虎伤身，那一年一定有悲哭；你来占病断存亡，白虎加临主哭丧；临着、克着父爻，就有咎，妻财的位置一定受伤；子孙遇上它，终究不成，兄弟遇上更不昌；再逢官爻持世，自身要恐怕有灾殃。凡遇白虎交重，是占病的大咎，无气还可以，旺相大凶；如果临着丧门、吊客、死符、丧车、又持着或克着用爻，决定是不起的征兆；如果得到子孙旺相发动，月解兴隆，才可以回生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Azure Dragon in its place one sees peace and health at last; with the White Tiger wounding the body it becomes ill and blame. The commentary says: if the Azure Dragon sits on Output controlling the Self, recovery and peace come on the appointed day; if it sits on the Other line controlling the Self, taking medicine is effective; if it sits on the Subject line and moves, even if there is danger no harm is done; if it carries a Pressure line, it is still not a fierce and frenzied illness; if it moves on the third line, the incense fire is the blessing. Cangping says: the Azure Dragon Void, together with Output, the sick person had no hidden merit in ordinary life; meeting Earth moving, the Tiger wounds the body, and that year there will surely be weeping; you who come to divine an illness to decide life or death, the White Tiger added governs wailing and mourning; sitting on and controlling the Resource line there will be blame, and the Wealth and wife positions will surely be wounded; Output meeting it ends in failure, Peer meeting it is still less prosperous; and if the Pressure line holds the Self, one's own person should fear calamity. When the White Tiger crosses heavily it is the great misfortune of divining illness: without qi it is still tolerable, strong it is greatly ominous; if it sits on Mourning Gate, Guest of Condolence, Death Tally or Funeral Carriage and holds or controls the Subject line, it surely is a sign that he will not rise; if Output is strong and moves and Month Release thrives, there may be a return to life.

> [!note] 按
> 「苍屏」是天玄赋注里引的一位作者（或一部书），原文只写「苍屏云」，这里按字面译作 Cangping，不作认定；它的歌诀用韵，后四句「临克父爻当有咎，并财妻位必遭伤；子孙际会终成否，兄弟相逢更不昌」是说白虎临克父爻（父母）、财爻（妻财）、子孙、兄弟时各自的后果。「阴骘」是暗中积的德。「香火为福」按字面译，意思大概是三爻动时，祭祀香火是福神所在，原文没有说清。「丧门、吊客、死符、丧车」都是凶煞名，见易林补遗「总论星煞值鬼诀」（LY.ILL.EL.48 到 EL.52）、易隐（LY.ILL.YY.05）。这一段的判语和黄金策的青龙、白虎（LY.ILL.HJC.24、HJC.25 一带）一致。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* 'Cangping' is an author (or a book) quoted in the commentary on the Tianxuan Fu, of whom the original writes only 'Cangping says', and it is translated literally as Cangping, without identification; his rhyme's last four lines, 'sitting on and controlling the Resource line there will be blame, and the Wealth and wife positions will surely be wounded; Output meeting it ends in failure, Peer meeting it is still less prosperous', give the consequence when the White Tiger sits on and controls the Resource line, the Wealth line, Output and Peer in turn. 'Yin zhi' is merit stored up in secret. 'The incense fire is the blessing' is translated literally, and probably means that when the third line moves, the incense fire of the sacrifices is where the fortune spirit lies, which the original does not make clear. 'Mourning Gate, Guest of Condolence, Death Tally and Funeral Carriage' are names of malign stars, for which see the Yilin Buyi's 'general discourse on ghosts by the stars and killings' (LY.ILL.EL.48 to EL.52) and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.05). The verdicts of this passage agree with the Huangjin Ce's on the Azure Dragon and the White Tiger (LY.ILL.HJC.24, HJC.25 and around). After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
