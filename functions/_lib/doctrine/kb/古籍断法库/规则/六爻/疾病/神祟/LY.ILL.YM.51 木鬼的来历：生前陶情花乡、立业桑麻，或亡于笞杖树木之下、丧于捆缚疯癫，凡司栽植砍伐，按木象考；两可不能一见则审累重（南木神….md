# 木鬼的来历：生前陶情花乡、立业桑麻，或亡于笞杖树木之下、丧于捆缚疯癫，凡司栽植砍伐，按木象考；两可不能一见则审累重（南木神应水官者体木司水）

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生前在花乡里陶冶性情、靠桑麻立业的，或者死在笞杖、树木之下，死于捆绑、疯癫的，凡是掌管栽种砍伐的，就按木象来考察。注说：凡是两种都说得通、不能只取一种的，要审察哪种分量重，比如南木神对应水官的，是体属木、掌管水。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* One who in life took his pleasure in the land of flowers and made his living from mulberry and hemp, or died under the rod or beneath a tree, or by binding or madness, and whose charge is planting and felling, is examined by the Wood image. The note says: where either reading is possible and one cannot be settled on by a single look, one must weigh which carries more; for example, the Southern Wood God answering a Water Pressure line has a body of Wood and a charge of Water.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的注比正文重要：它给了一条判别规则，当一位神的「体」（本身属哪一行）和它的「所司」（掌管的属哪一行）不是同一行，不能只取一个，要看哪个分量重；例是南木神，本身是木神，可是掌管水，所以官鬼是水时要按「体木司水」去读。这是对 LY.ILL.YM.49 里「性、司、成」三样可以各属一行的补充。
>
> *Note:* The note of this entry matters more than the text: it gives a rule of discrimination, that when a spirit's 'body' (which phase it is itself) and its 'charge' (which phase what it governs is) are not the same phase, one cannot take only one, and must weigh which carries more; the example is the Southern Wood God, a Wood spirit by nature yet governing water, so that when the Pressure line is Water it is read as 'body Wood, charge Water'. It supplements what LY.ILL.YM.49 says, that nature, charge and origin may each belong to a different phase.
