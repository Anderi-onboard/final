# 病因·鬼伏六亲下或六亲化鬼：伏父或父动化鬼忧心得病、衣服寒暖失时；伏兄或兄动化鬼赌博争财；伏财或财动化鬼饮食不节，财在阳宫动主吐、阴宫动主泻、加土动吐、加水动泻；伏子或子动化鬼外情牵引酒色（加龙嗜酒、加武好色）；子爻空绝调理无资；间爻鬼动胸膈不宽

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼伏在父母爻下、或父母爻发动变出鬼的，是忧心得病，或者衣服冷暖穿脱不及时得病；鬼伏在兄爻下、或兄爻发动变出鬼的，是因为赌博争财得病；鬼伏在财爻下、或财爻发动变出鬼的，是饮食没有节制而起：财爻在阳宫发动主呕吐，在阴宫发动主泄泻，财带土发动的吐，财带水发动的泻；鬼伏在子孙爻下、或子孙发动变出鬼的，是被外面的情事牵引，或者酒色致病（带青龙是嗜酒，带玄武是好色）；子孙爻空亡、绝的，没有钱调理；间爻上的鬼发动，胸膈不舒畅。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost hidden beneath the Resource line, or Resource moving and changing into a ghost: the illness is caught from anxiety of heart, or from clothing not changed in time for cold and warmth. Beneath the Peer line, or Peer moving and changing into a ghost: from gambling and quarrels over money. Beneath Wealth, or Wealth moving and changing into a ghost: from intemperate eating and drinking — Wealth moving in a yang palace means vomiting, in a yin palace diarrhea, Wealth with Earth and moving vomiting, Wealth with Water and moving diarrhea. Beneath Output, or Output moving and changing into a ghost: from being drawn away by outside affairs, or wine and women (with the Dragon a taste for drink, with the Dark Tortoise a love of women); if the Output line is Void or in Extinction there are no means for treatment and care; a ghost moving on a between line makes the chest and diaphragm tight.

> [!warning] 打架
> 财爻动，吐还是泻看什么：易隐说财在阳宫动主吐、在阴宫动主泻，又说财带土动吐、财带水动泻；黄金策说财爻动在上卦（外卦）主吐、动在下卦（内卦）主泻（LY.ILL.HJC.30）。一个看阴阳宫，一个看上下卦，两个分法不一样；书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What decides vomiting or diarrhea when Wealth moves: the Yi Yin says Wealth moving in a yang palace means vomiting and in a yin palace diarrhea, and also Wealth with Earth vomiting and with Water diarrhea; the Huangjin Ce says Wealth moving in the upper (outer) trigram means vomiting and in the lower (inner) trigram diarrhea (LY.ILL.HJC.30). One looks at yang and yin palaces and the other at the upper and lower trigrams, so the two divide differently; the books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「间爻」是世、应之间的两爻。黄金策讲伏鬼在六亲下的病因（LY.ILL.HJC.08：伏财伤饥失饱、伏子酒色、伏父劳心、伏兄口舌争竞）和这里大体一致；这里多了「六亲发动化出鬼」的情形，和 LY.ILL.HJC.10（鬼爻化出六亲各有得病之处）是反过来的一组。
>
> *Note:* The 'between lines' are the two lines between the Self and the Other. The Huangjin Ce's causes when the ghost is hidden beneath a relation (LY.ILL.HJC.08: beneath Wealth hurt by hunger and overeating, beneath Output wine and women, beneath Resource worry of mind, beneath Peer quarrels) are broadly the same; here there is in addition the case of a relation moving and changing into a ghost, the reverse of LY.ILL.HJC.10 (the Pressure line changing into a relation, each with its own place where the illness was caught).
