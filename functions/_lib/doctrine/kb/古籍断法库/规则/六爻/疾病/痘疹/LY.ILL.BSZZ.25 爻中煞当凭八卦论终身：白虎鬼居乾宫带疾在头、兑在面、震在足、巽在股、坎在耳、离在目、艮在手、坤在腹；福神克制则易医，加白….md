# 爻中煞当凭八卦论终身：白虎鬼居乾宫带疾在头、兑在面、震在足、巽在股、坎在耳、离在目、艮在手、坤在腹；福神克制则易医，加白虎持临是终身之疾

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 爻里的煞，要凭八卦来论一生留下什么毛病：白虎、鬼在乾宫，毛病在头；在兑卦，在脸；在震卦，在脚；在巽卦，在大腿；在坎卦，在耳朵；在离卦，在眼睛；在艮卦，在手；在坤卦，在肚子。以上八个宫里所遇到的鬼爻，如果遇到福神克制，就容易医好；如果再加上白虎带着，就是终身的毛病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The killing spirit within the lines should be judged by the eight trigrams for the lifelong ailment: if the Tiger ghost sits in the Qian palace, the ailment lingers in the head; in the Dui image, in the face; in Zhen, in the foot; in Xun, in the thigh; in Kan, in the ear; in Li, in the eye; in Gen, in the hand; in Kun, in the belly. For the Pressure line in any of the eight palaces above, if Output meets and controls it, treatment easily cures it; if the White Tiger is added and sits on it, it is a lifelong ailment.

> [!warning] 打架
> 兑宫的部位，同一章里有两说：LY.ILL.BSZZ.23 说兑宫毒结在口，这一条说兑象带疾在面。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The place of the Dui palace has two readings in this one chapter: LY.ILL.BSZZ.23 says the poison lodges in the mouth in the Dui palace, and this entry says the ailment lingers in the face in the Dui image. The book does not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这里的分布是乾头、兑面、震足、巽股、坎耳、离目、艮手、坤腹。增删卜易讲花长在哪里，说乾多生头、坤多腹、艮多手、兑多口、坎离上身、震巽下身（LY.ILL.ZS.20），大体和这里相近，只是那里讲的是痘长在哪里，这里讲的是好了以后留下什么。
>
> *Note:* The distribution here is Qian the head, Dui the face, Zhen the foot, Xun the thigh, Kan the ear, Li the eye, Gen the hand, Kun the belly. The Zengshan Buyi, on where the flowers grow, says Qian mostly the head, Kun the belly, Gen the hands, Dui the mouth, Kan and Li the upper body, Zhen and Xun the lower body (LY.ILL.ZS.20), which is broadly close, except that there it says where the pox grows and here what is left after recovery.
