# 定死活于五行生克之中、决轻重于六神临持之上：生死全凭五行生克的轻重，兼看六神，此节乃一章之大旨

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生死取决于五行生克之中，轻重取决于六神落在哪里、带在谁身上。生死全靠生克的轻重，再兼看六神，这一节是全章的大旨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Life and death are settled within the generating and controlling of the five phases, and light or heavy is decided by where the six spirits sit and what they hold. Life and death rest wholly on the weight of generating and controlling, together with a look at the six spirits; this passage is the main purpose of the whole chapter.

## 《卜筮正宗·新增痘疹》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2297 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Added chapter on smallpox", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 儿孙满目未出花，耽许多忧虑； | 儿孙满眼，都还没有出花，耽误了多少忧虑； | Children and grandchildren fill the eye and none has yet had the pox: much anxiety is spent; |
> | 金玉满堂失教训，枉费尽心机。 | 金玉满堂却缺了教导，白白费尽心机。 | a hall full of gold and jade but no instruction: all one's ingenuity wasted. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 最后这一对句是全章的收尾，讲做父母的心事（儿孙还没出花时的忧虑，以及家里再富也要教导），不是断卦的规则。注文说的「六神」是螣蛇、朱雀、勾陈、白虎、青龙、玄武。
>
> *Note:* The last couplet closes the chapter and speaks of a parent's cares (the worry before the children have had the pox, and that however rich a household is it must still instruct); it is not a rule of judgment. The 'six spirits' of the commentary are the Coiling Snake, Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth, White Tiger, Azure Dragon and Dark Tortoise.
