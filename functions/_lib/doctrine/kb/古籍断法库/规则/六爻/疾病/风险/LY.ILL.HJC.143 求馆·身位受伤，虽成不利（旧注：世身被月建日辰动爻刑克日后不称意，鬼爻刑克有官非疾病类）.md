# 求馆·身位受伤，虽成不利（旧注：世身被月建日辰动爻刑克日后不称意，鬼爻刑克有官非疾病类）

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》求馆章说：「身的位置受了伤，即使成了也不利。」旧注：世爻和身被月建、日辰、动爻刑克，即使有能成的样子，日后也不称心；如果是鬼爻来刑克，就有官非、疾病一类的事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on seeking a tutoring post says: 'With the Body's place harmed, even if it succeeds it is unfavourable.' The old commentary: if the Self and the Body are punished or controlled by the Month, the Day or a moving line, though it looks as if it can succeed, afterwards it is still not as wished; if it is the ghost line that punishes or controls it, there are official troubles or illness and the like.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8567 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世身被月建日辰动爻刑克，虽有可成之象，日后亦不称意。 | 世爻和身被月建、日辰、动爻刑克，即使有能成的样子，日后也不称心。 | If the Self and the Body are punished or controlled by the Month, the Day or a moving line, though it looks as if it can succeed, afterwards it is still not as wished. |
> | 如鬼爻刑克，有官非疾病类。 | 如果是鬼爻来刑克，就有官非、疾病一类的事。 | If it is the Pressure line that punishes or controls it, there are official troubles or illness and the like. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 求馆章是占谋一个教书的馆（受聘当老师）的一章，「身位」是身爻所在的位置。这里的疾病是求馆的人自己将来会碰到的事，不是占病，所以放在风险里。「官非疾病类」没有分开说哪一种鬼爻带哪一样，照字面。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on seeking a tutoring post is about divining whether one will be engaged as a teacher, and the 'Body's place' is where the Body line stands. The illness here is something the man seeking the post will meet later and is not a divination about illness, which is why it is placed under risks. 'Official troubles or illness and the like' does not say which kind of ghost line brings which, and is kept to the letter.
