# 推占来情·身在财乡欲觅财，亦忧父母及妻灾：财动忧父母、父母动忧子孙、子孙动忧官事、官鬼动忧兄弟、兄弟动忧失财及妻妾；空亡带杀主丧亡

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推占来情的歌说：卦身在财的位置上、想求财，也要担心父母和妻子的灾，变进空亡奴婢会逃，要知道生死，占的是新来的事。自注说：财爻持世，或在别的爻上动，主妻妾有灾、要担心父母；落了空亡又带杀，主有丧亡的事；一串担忧是：财爻动担心父母，父母爻动担心子孙，子孙爻动担心官事，官鬼爻动担心兄弟，兄弟爻动担心失财和妻妾，这是取五行相克的道理。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verses on reading the purpose of the asker say: with the Body at the place of Wealth, wishing to seek wealth, one must also worry for the parents and for the wife's calamity; if it changes into Void, the servants run off; to know life and death, it is a divination of what has newly come. Its own note says: when the Wealth line holds the Self, or moves on another line, it means calamity for wife and concubine and worry for the parents; if it falls Void and carries a killing, it means a death; and the chain of worries is: when the Wealth line moves, worry for the Resource; when the Resource line moves, worry for Output; when the Output line moves, worry for official affairs; when the Pressure line moves, worry for Peer; when the Peer line moves, worry for loss of wealth and for wife and concubine; this takes the sense of control among the five phases.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条讲的是六亲发动时要担心谁，没有一个字说「病」，只说「忧」和「灾」；收在疾病域，是因为占病时代人占亲人（儿占父母、夫占妻）要用这套相克去看谁受累，而且歌里有「定知生死」一句。「父母爻动忧子孙」一类的串和 LY.ILL.BSYG.02 的几条自注一致，这里更完整。「忧官事」的「官事」是官司公事，不是官鬼。「奴婢走」是奴婢逃走。「占新来」的「新来」是新近来的事，按字面译。
>
> *Note:* This entry is about whom to worry for when a relation line moves, and does not say 'illness' in a single word, only 'worry' and 'calamity'; it is taken in the illness domain because in divining for a relative (a son for a parent, a husband for a wife) this chain of control is used to see who is burdened, and because the song has the phrase 'to know life and death for certain'. The chain 'when the Resource line moves, worry for Output' agrees with the notes of LY.ILL.BSYG.02, and is fuller here. The 'official affairs' of 'worry for official affairs' are lawsuits and public business and not the Pressure line. 'The servants run off' is servants fleeing. The 'newly come' of 'divining what has newly come' is what has recently arrived, and is literal.
