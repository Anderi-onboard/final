# 疾病论·五行与病位：金木动骨骼疼酸、水火兴皮肤寒热；丑未伤脾、戌辰破腹；土伏土爻生肿毒、金藏金位动心胞；妻在土兴呕吐；木下木多寒燠、水伏水多冷淋

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里按五行和地支配病：金、木动，骨骼疼酸；水、火兴，皮肤寒热；丑、未交重必伤脾；戌、辰发动是因为腹部的病；土伏在土爻下，生肿毒；金藏在金位，动心包；妻财在土上兴旺，呕吐；木下伏木、木多，忽冷忽热；水伏在水下、水多，冷淋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness matches illnesses to the five elements and branches: Metal or Wood moving, bones and joints ache; Water or Fire rising, the skin has chills and fever; Chou or Wei moving is sure to harm the spleen; Xu or Chen moving comes from trouble of the belly; Earth hidden under an Earth line gives swollen toxin; Metal hidden in a Metal place stirs the pericardium; Wealth on Earth and rising, vomiting; Wood under Wood, with much Wood, blowing hot and cold; Water hidden under Water, with much Water, cold strangury.

## 《卜筮全书·阐幽精要·十一、疾病论》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9773 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Chanyou Jingyao, no. 11: on illness", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 土伏土爻生肿毒， | 土伏在土爻下，生肿毒； | Earth hidden under an Earth line gives swollen toxin; |
> | 金藏金位动心胞。 | 金藏在金位，动心包。 | Metal hidden in a Metal place stirs the pericardium. |
> | 妻在土兴呕吐， | 妻财在土上兴旺，呕吐； | Wealth on Earth and rising, vomiting; |
> | 官临土动迟疑； | 官鬼临在土上发动，拖延迟疑； | a Pressure line on Earth and moving, delay and doubt; |
> | 木下木多寒燠， | 木下伏木、木多，忽冷忽热； | Wood hidden under Wood, with much Wood, blowing hot and cold; |
> | 水伏水多冷淋。 | 水伏水下、水多，冷淋。 | Water hidden under Water, with much Water, cold strangury. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这几联把五行（金木水火土）或地支（丑未、戌辰）配上身体部位和症状。「破腹」字面是腹破，这里按腹泻、腹痛一类的肚腹病读，书里没有另注。「迟疑」讲的是官鬼临土发动的结果，字面不像症状，可能是病拖着不好，译文照字面写作「拖延迟疑」。「寒燠」是忽冷忽热，「冷淋」是小便涩痛而冷的淋病，「心胞」是心包。「土伏土爻」「木下木多」按字面是伏神五行同于飞神（或卦中某五行多）的意思。这一组和本书的五行论病歌（LY.ILL.BSQS.43）、柳神经的火鬼动心气病（LY.ILL.BSQS.26）配法大体相通。
>
> *Note:* These couplets match the five elements (Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth) or branches (Chou and Wei, Xu and Chen) to parts of the body and to symptoms. 'Broken belly' is literally a belly that has burst, and is read here as an upset belly or pain of the abdomen; the book gives no note. 'Hesitation' is the result of a Pressure line on Earth moving; as a symptom it reads oddly and may mean the illness drags on, so the translation keeps it as 'delay and doubt'. 'Blowing hot and cold' is alternating cold and heat; 'cold strangury' is a strangury, a painful and dribbling urination, with cold; the 'pericardium' is the heart's envelope. 'Earth hidden under an Earth line' and 'Wood under Wood' mean, literally, a hidden line of the same element as the flying line (or much of one element in the hexagram). This group is in general akin to the verse on illness by the five elements in this book (LY.ILL.BSQS.43) and to the Liushen Jing's 'heart-qi illness: a Fire ghost moves' (LY.ILL.BSQS.26).
