# 神煞月令表·凶神(病)：岁杀病有官灾凶、飞杀卒病、阴杀阴谋病患、小杀小儿疾患、天杀疾病困厄同丧杀断、地杀疾病凶、天阴女人疾病凶、大月时行疾病凶

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神煞月令表里凶神的八行，断语都说到病：岁杀，病而有官灾，凶，月杀也这样断；飞杀，突然发病又有飞鸟，凶；阴杀，阴谋和病患，凶；小杀，小孩的疾病，凶；天杀，疾病困厄，凶，和丧杀一样断；地杀，疾病凶、恶而凶；天阴，女人的疾病，凶；大月，流行的疾病，凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Eight fierce rows of the table of spirits and killings by month whose judgments speak of illness: the Year Killing, illness with official calamity, ominous, and the Month Killing is judged the same way; the Flying Killing, sudden illness and flying birds, ominous; the Yin Killing, secret schemes and illness, ominous; the Small Killing, illness of small children, ominous; the Heaven Killing, illness and distress, ominous, judged like the Mourning Killing; the Earth Killing, illness is ominous, vicious and ominous; Heaven Yin, illness of a woman, ominous; the Great Month, epidemic illness, ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 这八行的排法都是每月一个地支，一年十二月，有的三个月一循环（岁杀、天杀：丑戌未辰，四个一组），有的按顺序或逆序排下去（天阴子丑寅卯……、大月子丑寅卯……），有的隔位（飞杀酉子卯午、地杀辰戌丑未）。断语里「岁杀：病有官灾凶，月杀同断用」的「月杀」表里没有列出，原文没有解释，字面译。「天杀：疾病困厄，凶。同丧杀断」的「丧杀」是另一个杀（丧门一类），这里说天杀照丧杀断。「飞杀：卒病飞鸟凶」的「飞鸟」是飞鸟的凶象（飞鸟入宅一类），按字面译。这些行是「卦里这一爻上有这个杀，占病凶」的意思；占病时怎么用，断易天机在「占疾病」一章里没有再展开。这几个杀名（天杀、地杀、天阴……）和易林补遗的星煞值鬼一节（LY.ILL.EL.48 到 EL.52）里讲的不是同一批。
>
> *Note:* In these eight rows each month has one branch, over the twelve months of the year: some run in cycles of three months (the Year Killing and the Heaven Killing: Chou, Xu, Wei, Chen, in groups of four), some go on in order or in reverse (Heaven Yin: Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao ...; the Great Month: Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao ...), and some skip places (the Flying Killing: You, Zi, Mao, Wu; the Earth Killing: Chen, Xu, Chou, Wei). The 'Month Killing' of 'the Year Killing: illness with official calamity, ominous; the Month Killing is judged the same way' is not listed in the table, and the original does not explain it, and it is rendered literally. The 'Mourning Killing' of 'the Heaven Killing: illness and distress, ominous; judged like the Mourning Killing' is another killing (of the Mourning Gate kind), and here the Heaven Killing is judged like the Mourning Killing. The 'flying birds' of 'the Flying Killing: sudden illness and flying birds are ominous' is the ominous sign of flying birds (of the flying birds entering the house kind), rendered literally. These rows mean 'this killing on a line of the hexagram makes divining illness ominous'; how to use them in divining illness the Duanyi Tianji does not go on to say in its chapter on illness. These killing names (Heaven Killing, Earth Killing, Heaven Yin ...) are not the same batch as those in the Yilin Buyi's section on stars and killings on the ghost (LY.ILL.EL.48 to EL.52).
