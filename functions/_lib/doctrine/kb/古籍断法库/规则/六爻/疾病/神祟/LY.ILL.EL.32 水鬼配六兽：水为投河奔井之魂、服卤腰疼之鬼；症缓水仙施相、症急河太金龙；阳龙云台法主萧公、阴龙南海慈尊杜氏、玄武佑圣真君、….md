# 水鬼配六兽：水为投河奔井之魂、服卤腰疼之鬼；症缓水仙施相、症急河太金龙；阳龙云台法主萧公、阴龙南海慈尊杜氏、玄武佑圣真君、朱雀江河许愿、陈蛇坑厕、白虎水伤

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水鬼，是投河、奔井的魂，服卤、腰疼的鬼：症状缓的，是水仙、施相；症状急的，是河太、金龙。阳龙（阳的青龙），断作云台法主，也是冒犯了萧公；阴龙，推作南海慈尊，又冲犯了杜氏；玄武，是佑圣真君；朱雀，恐怕是对江河许过愿；遇到勾陈、螣蛇，断作坑厕；逢白虎，论作水伤。注说：水鬼，主淹死的鬼、腰疼的鬼、服卤死的鬼；症状缓的，是水仙五圣和镇海施相公；症状急的，是冒犯了水中的河太、或曹三和金龙四大王；阳鬼值青龙，是三官大帝和五圣的神；阴鬼值青龙，是观世音和杜氏夫人；鬼带玄武，是北极真君；带朱雀，是在水池上许的愿心；带勾陈或螣蛇，就说是水口上冒犯的；如果水鬼变出兄弟、或水的兄弟变出鬼、又带勾陈螣蛇的，就是坑厕的土神；鬼临白虎，必须向水部的伤司求。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Water is the soul of one who threw himself into a river or ran to a well, the ghost of one who drank brine ('you' in the text, for 'lu') or of back pain: if mild, the Water Immortal and Minister Shi; if severe, He Tai and the Golden Dragon. A yang Dragon is judged the Master of the Yuntai Rite, and also offends Lord Xiao; a yin Dragon is worked out as the Compassionate Honoured One of the Southern Sea, and also clashes with Lady Du; the Dark Tortoise is the True Lord Yousheng; the Vermilion Bird: beware a vow made to river or lake; meeting Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake, judge it the pit and privy; meeting the White Tiger, judge it the water injury. The commentary says: a Water ghost governs ghosts of drowning, ghosts of back pain and ghosts of death by drinking brine; if the symptoms are mild, it is the Water (the text says Fire) Immortal and the Five Sages, and the Sea-Pacifying Lord Shi; if urgent, it is an offence against He Tai in the water, or Cao San and the Golden Dragon Fourth Great King; a yang ghost on the Azure Dragon is the Three Officials the Great Emperors and the spirits of the Five Sages; a yin ghost on the Azure Dragon is Guanyin and Lady Du; a ghost with the Dark Tortoise is the True Lord of the North Pole; with the Vermilion Bird, a vow made by a pool; with Hook Earth or the Coiling Snake, say an offence at a water mouth; if the Water ghost changes into Peer, or a Water Peer changes into a ghost and carries Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake, it is the earth spirit of the pit and privy; a ghost on the White Tiger must seek the Injuring Office of the Water Department.

> [!note] 按
> 歌诀里「服卣」的「卣」应是「卤」（盐卤，自尽用的）之误，和易隐的「服卤」（LY.ILL.YY.28）一致；解说里「火仙五圣」的「火仙」疑是「水仙」之误（歌诀写的是水仙），这里按字面译并在括号里标出。「阳龙」「阴龙」是青龙临在阳爻或阴爻上；「陈蛇」是勾陈和螣蛇的合称。「河太」「曹三」「金龙四大王」是民间的河神，书里没有解释。三官大帝是天官、地官、水官。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地（苕溪一带）的民间神祇和星煞，书里一个也没有解释（章末自己说别的郡的神司不同），这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* The 'you' in 'drank you' in the verse should be 'lu' (salt brine, used to end one's life), agreeing with the Yi Yin's 'drank brine' (LY.ILL.YY.28); the 'Fire Immortal' in 'Fire Immortal and the Five Sages' in the explanation looks like an error for 'Water Immortal' (the verse writes Water Immortal), and it is translated literally with the doubt marked in brackets. A 'yang Dragon' and a 'yin Dragon' are the Azure Dragon on a yang or a yin line; 'Chen-she' is the joint name for Hook Earth and the Coiling Snake. 'He Tai', 'Cao San' and the 'Golden Dragon Fourth Great King' are popular river gods, and the book does not explain them. The Three Officials are the Officials of Heaven, Earth and Water. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain (the book itself says at the end of the chapter that the gods of other prefectures differ), and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
