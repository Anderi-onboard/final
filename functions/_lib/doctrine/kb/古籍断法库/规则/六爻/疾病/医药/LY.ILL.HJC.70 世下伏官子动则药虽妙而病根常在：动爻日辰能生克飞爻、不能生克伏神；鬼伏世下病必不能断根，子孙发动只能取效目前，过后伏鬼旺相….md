# 世下伏官子动则药虽妙而病根常在：动爻日辰能生克飞爻、不能生克伏神；鬼伏世下病必不能断根，子孙发动只能取效目前，过后伏鬼旺相月日必再发

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动爻、日辰能生飞爻、克飞爻，却不能生克伏神，因为伏神是隐藏潜伏的意思。占病遇到鬼伏在世爻下面，这病一定不能断根：就算子孙发动，也只能眼前见效，过后到伏鬼旺相的月份日子，这病一定再犯。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The moving lines and the Day can generate and control a flying line but cannot generate or control a hidden line, for a hidden line lies out of view. In divining illness, if Pressure lies hidden under the Self line, the illness surely cannot be cut off at the root: even if Output moves, it can take effect only for the present, and after this, in the month or day when the hidden Pressure is strong, the illness will surely break out again.

## 《黄金策·医药》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7021 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Doctors and medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世下伏官，子动则药虽妙，而病根常在； | 世爻下面伏着官鬼，子孙发动，药虽然好，病根还是常在； | A Pressure line hidden under the Self line: though Output moves and the medicine is good, the root of the illness stays; |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2442 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 大抵自占病遇鬼伏世下，或占他人病遇鬼伏用爻下，其病不能断根，日后恐再发也。 | 大体上自己占病遇到鬼伏在世爻下面，或者占别人的病遇到鬼伏在用爻下面，这病不能断根，以后恐怕会再发。 | In general, if in divining one's own illness Pressure lies hidden under the Self line, or in divining another's illness Pressure lies hidden under the Subject line, the illness cannot be cut off at the root, and it may well break out again later. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 王洪绪多了「占别人的病鬼伏用爻下」一种，没有收旧注里「子孙发动只取效目前」的话。疾病章里讲官藏世下的一条是 LY.ILL.HJC.07。
>
> *Note:* Wang Hongxu adds the case 'in divining another's illness, Pressure hidden under the Subject line' and does not take the old commentary's remark that Output moving only takes effect for the present. The entry in the illness chapter on Pressure hidden under the Self line is LY.ILL.HJC.07.
