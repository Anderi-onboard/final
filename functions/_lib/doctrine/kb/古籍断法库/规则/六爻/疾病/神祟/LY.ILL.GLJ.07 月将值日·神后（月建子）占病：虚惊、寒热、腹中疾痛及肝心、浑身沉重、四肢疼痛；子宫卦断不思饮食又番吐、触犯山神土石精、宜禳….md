# 月将值日·神后（月建子）占病：虚惊、寒热、腹中疾痛及肝心、浑身沉重、四肢疼痛；子宫卦断不思饮食又番吐、触犯山神土石精、宜禳土杀神及伤亡五道

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在子、叫神后的时候占病：虚惊，白天得病是鬼来侵犯；寒热时来，很蹊跷；腹中疼痛，牵连肝和心；全身沉重，转不了身；四肢疼痛，不能安宁。「子宫卦断」那一段说：不想吃喝，又翻着吐；冲犯了山神和土石精；手脚疼痛，一动就气喘，适合禳解土杀神；还有伤亡鬼和五道，要祈求、做福事，保平安。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on Zi, named Shenhou, divining illness: a false alarm; an illness taken in the daytime is a ghost's invasion; chills and fever come at times and are very odd; pain in the belly, reaching the liver and the heart; the whole body heavy and unable to turn; the limbs ache and cannot settle. The passage 'the verdict for the Zi-palace hexagrams' says: no wish for food or drink, and vomiting again and again; he has offended the Mountain Spirit and the earth-and-stone sprites; the hands and feet ache and movement leaves him short of breath, and it is fitting to perform rites against the Earth-Killer spirits; there are also the dead by injury and the Five Ways, and he should pray and perform blessing rites to keep himself in peace.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。「日中得病」的「日中」译成白天；「远跷蹊」的「远」不知本字，按上下文读作很；「四肢疼痛末能安」的「末」读作「未」（不能）；「手足疾疼举动」和「气急宜禳土杀神」在原文里隔着一个空格，像是脱了字，译文把「举动气急」连着读（一动就气喘），这是读法；「土杀神」「五道」「土石精」书里都没有解释，译文只照字面。「神后值卦主虚惊」的「虚惊」是说受惊吓而无事，书里没有展开。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. 'In the daytime' is the book's 'in the middle of the day'; the first character of 'very odd' is not known as a word and is read from context as 'very'; 'cannot settle' reads the book's 'has not yet been able to settle' with the character for 'not yet' standing for 'cannot'; 'the hands and feet ache and movement' and 'short of breath, it is fitting to perform rites against the Earth-Killer spirits' are separated by a space in the original, as if a character had dropped out, and the translation reads 'movement ... short of breath' straight on (a movement leaves him gasping), which is a reading; 'the Earth-Killer spirits', 'the Five Ways' and 'the earth-and-stone sprites' are not explained in the book and are only literal. The 'false alarm' of 'it means a false alarm' says being frightened with no harm to follow, and the book does not expand it.
