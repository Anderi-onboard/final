# 应作医人不宜破空墓绝：自占或代六亲求医都以应爻为医人；应爻空破墓绝休囚衰弱，或旺相被日月动爻冲克，或动而化鬼、化绝、化回头克，药不见效

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻当作医生，不宜落在破、空、墓、绝上。自己求医、替六亲求医，都拿应爻当医生。应爻如果空、破、入墓、入绝、休囚衰弱，或者旺相却被日月和动爻冲克，或者动了变成鬼、变成绝、变出回头克，药就不见效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other line is taken as the doctor and should not be broken, Void, in a tomb or extinct. Whether one seeks a doctor for oneself or for a relation, the Other line is the doctor. If the Other line is Void, broken, in a tomb or extinct, resting, trapped or weak, or is strong but clashed or controlled by the Day, Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into Pressure, into extinction or into a returning control, the medicine has no effect.
