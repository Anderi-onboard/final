# 海底眼(断易天机本)·问病宜先问卜人：父母逢之父母陈、官鬼临之忌旺相、子孙克世药无灵；财为禄命忌飞克、印绶交重病困沉；大忌世官乘月建、又嫌墓发克其身；鬼在内夜重、官在外夜轻、内外有鬼人昏困；代占最怕应持鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问病应先问占卜的人是谁：父母爻现，说的是父母；官鬼临它，忌讳旺相；子孙克世，药不灵。财是禄命，忌被飞爻克；印绶交重，病困顿沉重；最忌世和官乘着月建，又嫌墓爻发动克它的身。鬼在内，夜里重；官在外，夜里轻；内外都有鬼，人昏沉困顿，不然就是旧病再来侵。代占最怕应爻持鬼，官的墓持在身上，命也倾覆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about illness first ask who the person divining is: the Resource line appearing means the parents; the Pressure line sitting on it must not be strong; Output controlling the Self means the medicine has no effect. Wealth is the salary and life and must not be attacked by a flying line; with the seal ribbon (Resource) crossing heavily the illness is deep and heavy; it is worst if the Self and the ghost ride the Month, and it also dislikes a tomb line moving to attack the Body. With the ghost inside the night is heavy, with the ghost outside the night is light; with ghosts both inside and outside the person is dazed and worn, or else an old illness comes again to invade. In divining for another the worst is the Other line holding a ghost, and with the tomb of the ghost line holding the Body life too is overturned.

> [!note] 按
> 这是海底眼疾病歌的后半（LY.ILL.HDY.01、HDY.02 的另一份）。「官在外兮夜心轻」的「夜心轻」，海底眼本书里同一句是「鬼在外兮夜必轻」（LY.ILL.HDY.02），这里按原字译作「夜里心里轻松」；是「必」写成「心」，还是本来就这么写，书里没有说明。昼夜轻重的说法，几本书不同，见 LY.ILL.HJC.12 的「打架」。「印绶」是父母（印授）。
>
> *Note:* This is the second half of the illness poem of the Haidi Yan (another copy of LY.ILL.HDY.01 and HDY.02). The 'ye xin qing' of 'the ghost outside, at night the heart is light' reads in the Haidi Yan itself 'the ghost outside, at night surely light' (LY.ILL.HDY.02); here it is rendered as written, 'at night the heart is light', and whether 'bi' (surely) was written as 'xin' (heart) or the line was always so, the book does not say. The saying on the heaviness of the illness by day and night differs between several books, see the dispute in LY.ILL.HJC.12. The 'seal ribbon' is Resource.
