# 身上飞伏双官膏肓之疾：卦身持鬼、本宫官鬼又伏世爻或用爻之下、世爻飞伏上下皆官鬼，或鬼夹在用爻卦身前后，叫双官夹身，大象不死也是沉困考终之疾

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦身上坐着鬼，而本宫的官鬼又伏在世爻下面；或者世爻持着官鬼，而本宫的官鬼伏在用爻下面；或者自己占，遇到世爻的飞爻、伏爻上下都是官鬼：总叫双官夹身，最不利。就算大局不到死，也是沉重缠困、拖到寿终的病。王洪绪补充：身指卦身和用神；鬼夹在身爻或用爻的前后，或者世爻上有鬼、身爻上又有鬼，都叫双官夹用夹身。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the hexagram Body holds Pressure while the Pressure of the home palace lies hidden under the Self line; or the Self line holds Pressure while the Pressure of the home palace lies hidden under the Subject line; or in a divination for oneself the Self line, flying and hidden, has Pressure both above and below: all this is called double Pressure clamping the body, and is most unfavorable. Even if the whole image does not show death, it is a heavy, lingering illness that lasts to the end of his days. Wang Hongxu adds: the 'body' means the hexagram Body and the Subject; Pressure lines flanking the body line or the Subject line before and after, or Pressure on the Self line and also on the body line, are all called double Pressure clamping the Subject and the body.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「身」指什么、哪些情形算双官夹身：旧注讲三种（卦身持鬼而官鬼伏世下，世持鬼而官鬼伏用爻下，自占世爻飞伏上下皆鬼）；王洪绪先说「身」是卦身和用神，又把鬼夹在身或用爻前后、世爻和身爻都有鬼也算进来，比旧注宽。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What the 'body' is and which cases count as double Pressure clamping the body: the old commentary gives three (the hexagram Body holds Pressure while Pressure is hidden under the Self line; the Self line holds Pressure while Pressure is hidden under the Subject line; in a divination for oneself the Self line flying and hidden has Pressure above and below); Wang Hongxu first says the 'body' is the hexagram Body and the Subject, and also counts Pressure flanking the body or the Subject before and after, and Pressure on both the Self line and the body line, which is wider than the old commentary.

> [!note] 按
> 王洪绪的例子里，恒卦属震宫（木）：三爻辛酉、五爻庚申是金，金克木，是官鬼；四爻庚午是火，是子孙。子孙夹在两个官鬼中间。这是按纳甲排出来的，书里没有写。「膏肓」是心尖和横膈之间的部位，古人说药力到不了那里；「考终」是活到寿终。
>
> *Note:* In Wang Hongxu's example Heng belongs to the Zhen palace (Wood): the third line Xin You and the fifth line Geng Shen are Metal, and Metal controls Wood, so they are Pressure; the fourth line Geng Wu is Fire, which is Output. Output is flanked by two Pressure lines. This is worked out from the Najia assignment and the book does not write it. 'Gaohuang' is the place between the tip of the heart and the diaphragm, which the ancients said medicine cannot reach; 'kaozhong' is to live out one's span to its end.
