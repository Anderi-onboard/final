# 月解、日解、天医：病人有月解神天医上卦即病愈；日解戊癸鸡啼官事散、病人无药不须忧；天医正卯二亥…病人有此爻上卦必主名医下药有效

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月解：正月二月起在申，三月四月在酉，五月六月在戌，七月八月在亥，九月十月在午，子丑两月（十一月、十二月）在未。病人有这个解神，天医又上了卦，就病愈了。日解：甲己日逢巳（蛇），乙庚日逢申（猴），丙辛日逢寅（虎），丁壬日逢丑（牛），戊癸日逢酉（鸡），官事就散，病人没有药也不用忧。天医：正月在卯，二月亥，三月丑，四月未，五月巳，六月卯，七月亥，八月丑，九月未，十月巳，十一月卯，十二月亥。病人有这一爻上卦，必定有名医下药有效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Month Resolution: in the first and second months it is at Shen, the third and fourth at You, the fifth and sixth at Xu, the seventh and eighth at Hai, the ninth and tenth at Wu, and in the two months of Zi and Chou (the eleventh and twelfth) at Wei. A sick person who has this resolving spirit, with the Heavenly Physician also in the hexagram, is cured at once. The Day Resolution: on Jia and Ji days it is Si (the snake), on Yi and Geng days Shen (the monkey), on Bing and Xin days Yin (the tiger), on Ding and Ren days Chou (the ox), on Wu and Gui days You (the rooster), and official business scatters, and a sick person without medicine need not worry. The Heavenly Physician: first month Mao, second Hai, third Chou, fourth Wei, fifth Si, sixth Mao, seventh Hai, eighth Chou, ninth Wei, tenth Si, eleventh Mao, twelfth Hai. A sick person who has this line in the hexagram will surely have a famous doctor whose medicine is effective.

> [!note] 按
> 月解的排法（正二申、三四酉、五六戌、七八亥、九十午、子丑未）和神煞月令表里的「地解」一行完全一样（LY.ILL.DYTJ.34），天医的排法和表里的「天医」一行也一样。两处的断语略有不同：这里说「病人有此解神，天医上卦即病愈」，表里说地解是「病者有此发动即安」。日解「甲己逢蛇乙庚猴……」是按日干取解神所在的地支（甲己取巳、乙庚取申、丙辛取寅、丁壬取丑、戊癸取酉），「官事散」是它对官事的作用，「病人无药不须忧」是对病人的作用。「天医上卦」是天医这个地支落在卦里的爻上。「病人有此爻上卦，必主名医下药有效也」和天玄赋的「月解交重灾渐退，天医发动便回生」（LY.ILL.TXF.05）是同一类说法。
>
> *Note:* The way the Month Resolution is laid out (first and second months Shen, third and fourth You, fifth and sixth Xu, seventh and eighth Hai, ninth and tenth Wu, Zi and Chou Wei) is exactly the same as the row 'Earth Resolution' in the table of spirits and killings by month (LY.ILL.DYTJ.34), and the way the Heavenly Physician is laid out is also the same as the row 'Heavenly Physician' in the table. The judgments differ slightly: here it says 'a sick person who has this resolving spirit, with the Heavenly Physician in the hexagram, is cured at once', and the table says of the Earth Resolution 'a sick person who has this moving is at once at ease'. The Day Resolution 'Jia and Ji meet the snake, Yi and Geng the monkey ...' takes the branch where the resolving spirit lies by the day stem (Jia and Ji take Si, Yi and Geng Shen, Bing and Xin Yin, Ding and Ren Chou, Wu and Gui You), and 'official business scatters' is its effect on official business and 'a sick person without medicine need not worry' its effect on a sick person. 'The Heavenly Physician in the hexagram' is the branch of the Heavenly Physician falling on a line of the hexagram. 'A sick person who has this line in the hexagram will surely have a famous doctor whose medicine is effective' is the same kind of saying as the Tianxuan Fu's 'when the Month resolves and the Day moves heavily calamity fades, when the Heavenly Physician moves he recovers at once' (LY.ILL.TXF.05).
