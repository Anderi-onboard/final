# 总断千金赋·疾病大宜天喜，若临凶杀必生悲（旧注：天喜吉星，占病遇之虽大象凶恶不以死断，临所忌之爻动则以为悲）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「疾病最适宜见天喜，如果天喜临在凶杀上，一定生出悲伤。」旧注：天喜是吉星，占病遇到它，即使整个卦象凶恶，也不按死来断，因为它是喜神；如果它临在所忌的爻上发动，就认为是悲，不认为是喜。王洪绪的说法一样，把「所忌的爻」写成「忌神」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'For illness the Tianxi star is greatly helpful, but if it sits on an ominous killing there is sure to be grief.' The old commentary: the Tianxi star is a lucky star, and in divining illness, meeting it, even if the whole image is ominous one does not judge death, because it is a spirit of joy; if it sits on a taboo line and moves, I take it as grief and not as joy. Wang Hongxu says the same, writing the 'taboo line' as the Adversary.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1282 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 又如天喜吉星也，占病遇之虽大象凶恶，竟不以死断，因天喜故也，若临忌神，我必以为悲而不以为喜。 | 再如天喜是吉星，占病遇到它，即使整个卦象凶恶，竟然不按死来断，是因为有天喜；如果它临在忌神上，我一定认为是悲，不认为是喜。 | Again, the Tianxi star is a lucky star: in divining illness, meeting it, even if the whole image is ominous one does not judge death, because of the Tianxi star; but if it sits on the Adversary, I will surely take it as grief and not as joy. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一句是总断千金赋里一连串「神煞要看它临在哪一爻」的例子之一：同一联接着讲往亡（出行最怕往亡，如系吉神终获利）。旧注、王洪绪借它说明「神煞之权轻而五行之权重」：神煞吉凶要看它临的是喜爻还是忌爻。所以天喜对占病是好东西，但天喜临在忌神（克用神的那一类）上发动，反而是悲。原文「凶杀」「凶煞」是同一个字的两种写法。「天喜」这颗星婚姻域也出现过（LY.MARRY.HJC.107 里的天喜星）。
>
> *Note:* This sentence is one of a run of examples in the Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements of 'a spirit-star must be looked at for the line it sits on': the same couplet goes on to the Fleeing Death star (for travel the Fleeing Death star is feared most, but if it is a lucky spirit one finally gains). The old commentary and Wang Hongxu use it to show that 'the power of spirit-stars is light and that of the five elements heavy': whether a spirit-star is good or ill depends on whether it sits on a line one welcomes or one that is a taboo. So the Tianxi star is good for illness, but when it sits on the Adversary (the kind of line that controls the Subject) and moves, it is grief instead. 'Ominous killing' and 'ominous killing' are two writings of the same word in the text. The Tianxi star also appeared in the marriage domain (the Tianxi star in LY.MARRY.HJC.107).
