# 产育·官空官伏，定然遗腹之儿（旧注：若有鬼伏在白虎爻下或龙伏鬼墓爻下，则主夫病卧在床非死也；旺空则是有病，鬼爻入墓若非有病只在狱）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》产育章说：「官鬼落空、伏藏，一定是遗腹子。」旧注：卦里没有官鬼或者官鬼落空亡，主产妇的丈夫已经死了，这必是遗腹子；但是没有财、福、白虎就不要这样断；如果有鬼伏在白虎爻下，或者青龙伏在鬼墓爻下，就主丈夫病卧在床，不是死；旺相而落空，是有病；鬼爻入墓，或者身临鬼墓，如果不是有病，就只是在狱里。王洪绪只讲了遗腹子一层：卦没有官鬼，或者官鬼在真空墓绝的地方，主产妇的丈夫已死；官爻伏着而旺相、有提拔的，是父亲远出，背生儿。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on childbirth says: 'With the officer Void or hidden, surely a posthumous child.' The old commentary: if the hexagram has no Pressure line or it is Void, the husband of the woman in childbirth is already dead and it is surely a posthumous child; but without Wealth, a fortune line or the White Tiger one does not judge so; if a ghost is hidden under the White Tiger line, or the Azure Dragon is hidden under the ghost's tomb line, it means the husband is bedridden with illness and not dead; strong but Void, he is ill; a ghost line in the tomb, or the Body on the ghost's tomb, if not illness it is only imprisonment. Wang Hongxu gives only the posthumous child: a hexagram without Pressure, or Pressure in a place of true Void, tomb or extinction, means the woman's husband is already dead; a Pressure line hidden but strong and with help means the father has gone far away, a child born behind his back.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2122 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如旁人及孕妇来占，遇卦无官鬼，或在真空墓绝之处，主产妇之丈夫已死，是遗腹子也。 | 如果是旁人或孕妇来占，遇到卦里没有官鬼，或者官鬼在真空、墓、绝的地方，主产妇的丈夫已经死了，是遗腹子。 | If another person or the pregnant woman comes to divine and the hexagram has no Pressure line, or Pressure is in a place of true Void, tomb or extinction, it means the woman's husband is already dead and it is a posthumous child. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一段在占产育的章里，官鬼代表产妇的丈夫（女占看官鬼为夫）。旧注在「官鬼空伏是遗腹」之外，又说了一层：官鬼伏在白虎或青龙下、旺而空的，是丈夫有病卧床、不是死；入墓的，不是病就是入狱。这是旧注自己加的，王洪绪没有这一层，所以只收他讲遗腹子的第一句。「青龙伏鬼墓爻下」「身临鬼墓」的取法书里没有展开。
>
> *Note:* This passage is in the chapter on divining childbirth, where Pressure stands for the husband of the woman (for a woman, Pressure is the husband). Beyond 'Pressure Void or hidden means a posthumous child', the old commentary adds a layer: Pressure hidden under the White Tiger or Azure Dragon, or strong and Void, means the husband is bedridden and not dead; Pressure in the tomb means illness or prison. This is the old commentary's own addition, and Wang Hongxu does not have it, so only his first sentence, on the posthumous child, is taken. The book does not spell out how to find 'the Azure Dragon hidden under the ghost's tomb line' or 'the Body on the ghost's tomb'.
