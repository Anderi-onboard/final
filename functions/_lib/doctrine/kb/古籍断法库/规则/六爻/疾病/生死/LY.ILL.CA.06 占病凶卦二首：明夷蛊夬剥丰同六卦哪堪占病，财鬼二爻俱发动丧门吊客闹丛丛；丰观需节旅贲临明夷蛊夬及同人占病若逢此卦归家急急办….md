# 占病凶卦二首：明夷蛊夬剥丰同六卦哪堪占病，财鬼二爻俱发动丧门吊客闹丛丛；丰观需节旅贲临明夷蛊夬及同人占病若逢此卦归家急急办前程

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 明夷、蛊、夬、剥、丰、同（人）这六个卦，怎么经得起占病的凶；财、鬼两爻都发动，丧门、吊客热闹地聚在一起。丰、观、需、节、旅、贲、临，明夷、蛊、夬和同人，占病如果遇到这些卦，赶紧回家去办后事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Mingyi, Gu, Guai, Bo, Feng and Tong(ren): how can these six hexagrams bear the ill of divining illness; with Wealth and the ghost both moving, Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence crowd noisily together. Feng, Guan, Xu, Jie, Lü, Bi and Lin, Mingyi, Gu, Guai and Tongren: if in divining illness one meets these hexagrams, hurry home and make ready for what lies ahead.

> [!warning] 打架
> 周易尚占和文王金钱课按卦名断病，和第五首这个名单有出入：观，周易尚占说「财聚病痊」，文王金钱课说「占病即愈」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.02、WWJQ.03）；贲，周易尚占说「病安」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.03）；节，文王金钱课说「疾病口舌消除安」（LY.ILL.WWJQ.03）；丰，文王金钱课说「疾病渐好」，周易尚占说「病人沉滞」（LY.ILL.WWJQ.03、ZSZ.04）；夬，周易尚占说「病讼迟延」，文王金钱课补录部分说「疾病除根」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.04、WWJQ.05）；临，周易尚占说「病犯崇侵」，文王金钱课补录部分说「疾病即痊」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.07、WWJQ.05）；蛊、明夷，周易尚占不说吉，和名单一致（LY.ILL.ZSZ.05）；文王金钱课对这两卦没有讲病。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The Zhouyi Shangzhan and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke judge illness by the name of the hexagram and differ from the list of the fifth poem: for Guan the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'wealth gathers and the illness is cured' and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says 'divining illness, it is cured at once' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.02, WWJQ.03); for Bi (Adornment) the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the illness is settled' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.03); for Jie (Restraint) the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says 'illness and quarrels are removed and all is at peace' (LY.ILL.WWJQ.03); for Feng the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says 'the illness gradually gets better' and the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the sick are held fast' (LY.ILL.WWJQ.03, ZSZ.04); for Guai the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'illness and lawsuit are delayed' and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke, in its supplementary part, says 'the illness is rooted out' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.04, WWJQ.05); for Lin (Approach) the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the illness offends a curse that attacks' and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke, in its supplementary part, says 'the illness is cured at once' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.07, WWJQ.05); for Gu and Mingyi the Zhouyi Shangzhan speaks of no good fortune, which agrees with the list (LY.ILL.ZSZ.05), and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says nothing of illness for these two.

> [!note] 按
> 这是第四、五首。两首列的凶卦名单和第一首（LY.ILL.CA.03）、天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.06）不完全一样，名单的比较见 LY.ILL.CA.03 的「打架」一栏。「同」「节」「旅」「贲」「丰」等是卦名，「同」按上下文是同人。「丧门吊客闹丛丛」的「丛丛」是热闹聚在一起的样子。「归家急急办前程」的「办前程」按字面译作「make ready for what lies ahead」，意思是赶紧回家料理后事。
>
> *Note:* These are the fourth and fifth poems. The lists of ominous hexagrams in the two poems are not exactly the same as in the first poem (LY.ILL.CA.03) and the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.06); the comparison of the lists is in the 'dispute' field of LY.ILL.CA.03. 'Tong', 'Jie', 'Lü', 'Bi', 'Feng' and so on are hexagram names, and 'Tong' is Tongren from the context. The 'cong cong' of 'Mourning Gate and Guest of Condolence crowd noisily' is a crowd gathering noisily. 'Ban qian cheng' in 'go home in haste and make ready for what lies ahead' is rendered literally as 'make ready for what lies ahead', meaning to hurry home and see to the funeral.
