# 六神空亡诀·白虎空亡病可痊

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卜筮全书的六神空亡诀说：白虎落了空亡，病可以痊愈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Bushi Quanshu's verse on the six spirits falling Void says: if the White Tiger is Void, the illness can be cured.

## 《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·十九、六神空亡诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3139 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses, no. 19: the six spirits falling Void", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 白虎空亡病可痊， | 白虎落了空亡，病可以痊愈， | With the White Tiger Void, the illness can be cured, |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句是六神空亡诀里的一句。全诀六句，每一句讲一位神落空亡的结果：青龙、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武各一句，讲的分别是家里的喜事、官司、牵连、怪异、病和盗贼，这里只收和病有关的白虎一句。道理是白虎主丧病，它落了空亡，它的凶力暂时不作数。断易天机的六神章里也有「白虎空亡病无事」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.38）。
>
> *Note:* This is one line of the verse on the six spirits falling Void. The whole verse has six lines, one for each of the Azure Dragon, Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth, Coiling Snake, White Tiger and Dark Tortoise, dealing in turn with joy in the house, lawsuits, entanglements, strange happenings, illness and thieves; only the White Tiger line, which bears on illness, is taken here. The reason is that the White Tiger means mourning and illness, and once it is Void its force does not count for the time being. The chapter on the six spirits in the Duanyi Tianji also has 'the White Tiger Void: the illness comes to nothing' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.38).
