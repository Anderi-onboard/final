# 卦无用神：元神动则不死（趋生）；忌神动而用神伏反吉（避克）；亦必得日月之救

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有用神，说是凶兆，但得到原神发动就不会死，因为它是在趋向生路；忌神发动而用神伏藏，反而吉，因为它避开了克；虽然如此，也必须得到日、月的救助。注举例：酉月戊申日，占妻子本人，得睽变损，卦里没有用爻，本来不是吉兆，喜的是子孙发动，到冬天病好了，这叫趋生；亥月甲申日，也是占妻子本人，得姤变升，忌神发动，本来是凶兆，喜的是用神不出现，伏在月建下面，后来到子月壬辰日，病势逐渐缓解，因为申子辰会成水局，忌神合成了原神，这叫避克。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hexagram without the Subject is said to be an ill omen, but if the Support moves he does not die, because he is heading toward life; if the Adversary moves and the Subject is hidden it is instead auspicious, because it avoids the control; even so, it must also gain the rescue of the Day and Month. The note's examples: in the You month on the Wushen day, a wife herself was divined and Kui changing into Sun obtained: the hexagram had no Subject line and so was not auspicious, but happily Output moved, and by winter she recovered — this is 'heading toward life'; in the Hai month on the Jiashen day, again a wife herself was divined and Gou changing into Sheng obtained: the Adversary moved, which was an ill omen, but happily the Subject did not appear and was hidden beneath the Month, and later on the Renchen day of the Zi month the illness gradually eased, for Shen, Zi and Chen meet to form a Water frame and the Adversary is combined into the Support — this is 'avoiding control'.

> [!note] 按
> 「趋生」是用神不在，但原神发动，气往生的方向走；「避克」是忌神发动，用神却伏藏起来，躲开了被克。第二例里申子辰三合水局，把本来的忌神（水，克火用神？）合成了原神，所以化险。这两条和卜筮正宗《原忌仇神论》里的「避空、避凶」（第 970 行）是同一套讲法。
>
> *Note:* 'Heading toward life' is when the Subject is absent but the Support moves and the qi goes toward life; 'avoiding control' is when the Adversary moves and the Subject lies hidden and escapes being controlled. In the second example Shen, Zi and Chen form a Water frame in three-harmony, combining what had been the Adversary into the Support, and so the danger is turned. These two are the same manner of speaking as the 'avoiding Void' and 'avoiding ill' in the Bushi Zhengzong's essay on the Support, Adversary and enemy spirit (line 970).
