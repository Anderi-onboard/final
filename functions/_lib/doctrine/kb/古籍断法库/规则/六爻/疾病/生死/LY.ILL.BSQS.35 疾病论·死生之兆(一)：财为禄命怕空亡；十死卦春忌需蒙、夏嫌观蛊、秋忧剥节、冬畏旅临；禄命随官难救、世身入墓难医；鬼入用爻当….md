# 疾病论·死生之兆(一)：财为禄命怕空亡；十死卦春忌需蒙、夏嫌观蛊、秋忧剥节、冬畏旅临；禄命随官难救、世身入墓难医；鬼入用爻当主死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里看死生的一段：财爻是禄命，怕落空亡，伏着的是命爻，要防克害；先拿十死卦当凶，最凶的是用爻不上卦、又落空亡，有救的是福位（子孙）扶持得力；春天忌需、蒙，夏天嫌观、蛊，秋天忧剥、节，冬天怕旅、临；禄命随着官鬼，难救，世爻和身入了墓，难医；官鬼遇到大杀，怕伤身；鬼爻入了用爻，就主死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A passage of the chapter on illness on reading life and death: Wealth is the salary and fate and fears falling Void, and if it lies hidden the life line must be guarded from control and harm; first take the ten fatal hexagrams as ill-omened, the worst being a Subject line that is absent or Void, while what can be saved is when the Output place supports it with force; in spring avoid Xu and Meng, in summer dislike Guan and Gu, in autumn fear Bo and Jie, in winter dread Lu and Lin; with the fate line following the Pressure line, rescue is hard, and with the Self and the Body entering the tomb, treatment is hard; Pressure meeting a great killing, beware harm to the Body; a ghost entering the Subject line means death.

> [!warning] 打架
> 哪几个卦占病最凶，几本书的名单不同。这一段按季节给：春需、蒙，夏观、蛊，秋剥、节，冬旅、临；阐奥歌章第三首（LY.ILL.CA.03）是丰、蛊、困、明夷、夬、同人一例，加冬旅、春需、夏观、秋剥，和这一段在旅、需、观、剥四卦的季节上一致；卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.15）是丰、观、需、剥、节、旅、贲、明夷、蛊、夬、同人「十死分明」；天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.06）是明夷、观、贲、需、临（世身入墓）和大畜、丰、同、蛊、夬（财鬼俱兴）。蒙只有这一段有，临只有这一段和天玄赋有；另外这一段点名的只有八个卦，却称「十死卦」，名目和数目对不上。书里没有裁决。周易尚占和文王金钱课按卦名断病，和这一段点名的几个卦有出入：蒙，周易尚占说「却宜占病」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.06）；观，周易尚占说「财聚病痊」，文王金钱课说「占病即愈」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.02、WWJQ.03）；节，文王金钱课说「疾病口舌消除安」（LY.ILL.WWJQ.03）；临，周易尚占说「病犯崇侵」，文王金钱课补录部分说「疾病即痊」（LY.ILL.ZSZ.07、WWJQ.05）；蛊，周易尚占说病凶，和这一段一致（LY.ILL.ZSZ.05）。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books differ on which hexagrams are the worst for illness. This passage gives them by season: Xu and Meng in spring, Guan and Gu in summer, Bo and Jie in autumn, Lu and Lin in winter. The Chan'ao Gezhang's third poem (LY.ILL.CA.03) gives Feng, Gu, Kun, Mingyi, Guai and Tongren together, plus Lu in winter, Xu in spring, Guan in summer and Bo in autumn, agreeing with this passage on the seasons of Lu, Xu, Guan and Bo; the Bushi Yuangui passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.15) gives Feng, Guan, Xu, Bo, Jie, Lu, Bi, Mingyi, Gu, Guai and Tongren, 'the ten deaths are clear'; the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.06) gives Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin (the Self and Body entering the tomb) and Daxu, Feng, Tong, Gu and Guai (Wealth and Pressure both rising). Meng is found only in this passage, and Lin only in this passage and the Tianxuan Fu; moreover this passage names only eight hexagrams yet calls them 'the ten fatal hexagrams', so the name and the number do not match. The books do not decide. The Zhouyi Shangzhan and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke judge illness by the name of the hexagram, and differ from several hexagrams named in this passage: for Meng (Youthful Folly) the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'on the contrary fit for divining illness' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.06); for Guan the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'wealth gathers and the illness is cured' and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says 'divining illness, it is cured at once' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.02, WWJQ.03); for Jie the Wenwang Jinqian Ke says 'illness and quarrels are removed and all is at peace' (LY.ILL.WWJQ.03); for Lin the Zhouyi Shangzhan says 'the illness offends a curse that attacks' and the Wenwang Jinqian Ke, in its supplementary part, says 'the illness is cured at once' (LY.ILL.ZSZ.07, WWJQ.05); for Gu the Zhouyi Shangzhan says the illness is ominous, which agrees with this passage (LY.ILL.ZSZ.05).

> [!note] 按
> 这一段连着讲了三个层次：先是用神的安危（财为禄命、用爻不见与空亡、福位扶持），然后是十死卦按季节的忌，最后是墓和大杀（世身入墓、官逢大杀、鬼入用爻）。「禄命随官」是禄命爻随着官鬼（入墓）的意思，断易天机里有「随官入墓」一节可对照（LY.ILL.DYTJ.27）。「用爻」就是用神。
>
> *Note:* This passage runs through three layers: first the safety of the Subject (Wealth as salary and fate, the Subject line absent or Void, the Output place supporting it), then the seasonal taboos of the ten fatal hexagrams, and last the tomb and the great killing (the Self and Body entering the tomb, Pressure meeting a great killing, a ghost entering the Subject line). 'The salary-fate following the Pressure line' means the salary-fate line following the Pressure line (into the tomb), and the section on 'following Pressure into the tomb' in the Duanyi Tianji can be compared (LY.ILL.DYTJ.27). The 'Subject line' is the Subject.
