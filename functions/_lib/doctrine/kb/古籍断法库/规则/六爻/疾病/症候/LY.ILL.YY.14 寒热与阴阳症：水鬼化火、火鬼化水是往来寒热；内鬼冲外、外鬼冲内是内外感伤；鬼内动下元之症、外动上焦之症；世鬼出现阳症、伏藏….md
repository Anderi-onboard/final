# 寒热与阴阳症：水鬼化火、火鬼化水是往来寒热；内鬼冲外、外鬼冲内是内外感伤；鬼内动下元之症、外动上焦之症；世鬼出现阳症、伏藏阴症；阴鬼化阳阴症变阳、阳鬼化阴阳症变阴

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 水鬼变火、火鬼变水的，是忽冷忽热；内卦的鬼冲外卦、外卦的鬼冲内卦的，是内外都受了伤。鬼在内卦发动，是下元的病症；鬼在外卦发动，是上焦的病症。世爻上的鬼出现，是阳症；世爻上的鬼伏藏，是阴症。阴鬼变阳，阴症变成阳症；阳鬼变阴，阳症变成阴症。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Water ghost changing into Fire, or a Fire ghost into Water, means alternating chills and fever; an inner ghost clashing the outer, or an outer ghost the inner, means injury both within and without. A ghost moving in the inner trigram is a disorder of the lower origin; a ghost moving in the outer trigram is a disorder of the upper burner. A ghost at the Self appearing openly is a yang condition, and one at the Self hidden is a yin condition. A yin ghost changing into yang: a yin condition turns yang; a yang ghost changing into yin: a yang condition turns yin.

> [!note] 按
> 「下元」按字面是下部的根本，指下焦、肾一带；「上焦」是胸膈以上。这一条和黄金策讲的「水化火火化水往来寒热」（LY.ILL.HJC.14）、「上冲下下冲上内外感伤」（LY.ILL.HJC.15）是同一个说法，易隐再添了世鬼出现与伏藏分阳症阴症、阴阳互变两条。
>
> *Note:* 'Lower origin' is literally the root of the lower part and means the lower burner and the kidneys; the 'upper burner' is the region above the chest and diaphragm. This entry is the same saying as the Huangjin Ce's 'Water into Fire and Fire into Water: alternating chills and fever' (LY.ILL.HJC.14) and 'upper clashing lower and lower clashing upper: injury within and without' (LY.ILL.HJC.15); the Yi Yin adds two things: a ghost at the Self, open or hidden, marks a yang or yin condition, and yin and yang turning into each other.
