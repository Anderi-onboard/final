# 论墓字如何取用：辰戌丑未五行之库、凶爻要入吉爻不可墓；人墓、事墓、鬼墓；占病得鬼墓为庙神、占病墓旺鬼难脱病难安多死、鬼是庙神墓旺乃太庙之神；墓要冲克破即解神

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 辰、戌、丑、未是五行的库（墓）：凶爻要入库，吉爻不可入墓，入墓就成了休囚。墓有三种：人墓、事墓、鬼墓。人墓：比如占兄弟的病，墓在辰，当日是戌，虽然合着入了墓，也不会死；人旺墓衰，不会死；兄弟旺、墓爻衰，不会死。鬼墓：占病得到鬼墓，鬼是庙里的神；占病而墓旺，鬼难脱身、病难安、多半死；占病时鬼是庙神，若墓旺，就是大庙的神，不是小小的神祠。墓这个字，要冲克破它，这是按墓爻的活法来论，也就是解神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei are the storehouses (tombs) of the five phases: a fierce line should enter one, a lucky line must not be in a tomb, and in a tomb it is resting or trapped. There are three tombs: the person's tomb, the matter's tomb and the ghost's tomb. The person's tomb: for example divining a brother's illness, the tomb is Chen and the day is Xu; though it combines and enters the tomb he does not die; if the person is strong and the tomb weak he does not die; if the brother is strong and the tomb line weak he does not die. The ghost's tomb: divining illness and obtaining the ghost's tomb, the ghost is a temple god; divining illness with the tomb strong, the ghost is hard to shake off, the illness hard to settle and mostly ends in death; where the ghost is a temple god in divining illness, if the tomb is strong it is a god of a great temple and not a small shrine. As for the word tomb, it must be clashed, controlled or broken; this is judged by the living method of the tomb line, and it is the resolving spirit.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段是断易天机「问曰：墓字如何取用」的答话，原来的问答一问一答是旺、墓、空三字各一段，这里只收墓字。原文说墓有三种（人墓、事墓、鬼墓），但只把人墓和鬼墓举了例，事墓只说了「所卜之事为难成费力」（占病用不到，不收）。「人墓」是人（用爻）入墓：占兄弟病，兄弟属土（或以辰为墓），当日是戌，虽戌辰相冲、合入墓，也不死；人旺墓衰、兄弟旺墓爻衰也不死。「鬼墓」里「占病得鬼墓，为庙神」是说这一病的鬼入了墓，这个鬼是庙里的神灵（鬼是神灵作祟）；「墓旺」是墓爻旺，鬼在墓里出不来（脱不了），所以病难安。这一节的「鬼墓」和火珠林的三墓（LY.ILL.HZL.01：宫墓、鬼墓、财墓，是世爻遇到某一墓）不是同一个分类：火珠林的鬼墓是鬼爻五行的墓，这里的鬼墓是鬼入墓；两处都说墓与鬼合在一起病重。「占贼」「占讼」「占失物」「占行人」「占财」的几句不在病的范围，不收。「解神」是把凶事解开的神，墓爻被冲克破，也就是解神。
>
> *Note:* This passage is the answer to 'someone asks: how is the word tomb to be used' in the Duanyi Tianji; the questions and answers originally take the three words strong, tomb and Void a passage each, and only the word tomb is taken here. The original says there are three tombs (the person's, the matter's and the ghost's) but gives examples only for the person's and the ghost's, and for the matter's tomb says only 'the thing divined is hard to accomplish and costs effort' (not used in illness, and not taken). The 'person's tomb' is the person (the Subject line) entering a tomb: divining a brother's illness, the tomb is Chen and the day is Xu, and though it combines and enters the tomb he does not die; and if the person is strong and the tomb weak, or the brother strong and the tomb line weak, he does not die. In the 'ghost's tomb', 'divining illness and obtaining the ghost's tomb, the ghost is a temple god' says that the ghost of this illness has entered a tomb, and this ghost is a spirit of a temple (a spirit is bringing the curse); 'the tomb strong' is the tomb line strong, and the ghost cannot get out of the tomb (cannot shake off), and so the illness is hard to settle. The 'ghost's tomb' of this passage and the three tombs of the Huozhu Lin (LY.ILL.HZL.01: the palace tomb, the ghost's tomb and the Wealth tomb, where the Self line meets one of the tombs) are not the same classification: the Huozhu Lin's ghost's tomb is the tomb of the phase of the ghost line, and this passage's ghost's tomb is the ghost entering a tomb; both say that a tomb joined with the ghost makes the illness heavy. The sentences about divining a thief, a lawsuit, lost things, a traveller and wealth are outside illness and are not taken. The 'resolving spirit' is a spirit that unties an ill matter, and a tomb line that is clashed, controlled or broken is the resolving spirit.
