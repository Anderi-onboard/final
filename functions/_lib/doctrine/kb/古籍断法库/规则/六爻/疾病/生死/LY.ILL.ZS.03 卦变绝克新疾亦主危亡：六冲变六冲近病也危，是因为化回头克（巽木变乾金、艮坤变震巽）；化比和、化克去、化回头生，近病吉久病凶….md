# 卦变绝克新疾亦主危亡：六冲变六冲近病也危，是因为化回头克（巽木变乾金、艮坤变震巽）；化比和、化克去、化回头生，近病吉久病凶（六冲之故）；此外必看用神

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六冲卦变成六冲卦，新得的病也主危亡，是因为变出了回头克：比如巽木变乾金、艮坤变震巽，都叫回头相克，虽然没有入墓入绝，也主危亡。如果变出比和、变出把克去掉的、或者变出回头生，近病吉、久病凶，这是六冲的缘故。除了这种冲克以外，一定要看用神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a Six-Clash hexagram changes into Six-Clash, even a recent illness means danger of death, because of a returning control: Xun Wood changing into Qian Metal, or Gen or Kun changing into Zhen or Xun, are all called a returning control, and though there is no tomb or extinction, they still mean danger of death. If it changes into peace, into a control that removes the danger, or into a returning generation, a recent illness is lucky and a long one unlucky, because of the Six-Clash. Apart from this clash and control, one must look at the Subject.

> [!note] 按
> 「回头相克」是动爻变出来的爻反过来克它自己。「巽木变乾金、艮坤变震巽」是八经卦之间的变化：乾金克巽木，震巽木克艮坤土。「化克去」书里没有解释，大概是变出的爻把克用神的那一爻克掉了。
>
> *Note:* A 'returning control' is when the line that a moving line changes into controls that line in return. 'Xun Wood changing into Qian Metal, Gen and Kun into Zhen and Xun' are changes between the eight trigrams: Qian Metal controls Xun Wood, and Zhen and Xun Wood control Gen and Kun Earth. The book does not explain 'changing into a control that removes'; it probably means that the line changed into controls away the line that was controlling the Subject.
