# 住居宅第章·子兴有喜没官非、更无灾病与盗贼；兄动耗财妻妾病

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占家宅的歌里说：子孙发动，有喜事，没有官非，也没有灾病和盗贼；兄弟发动，耗财，妻妾有病，是非和谋望又多阻隔。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on dwellings says: when Output moves, there is joy, no official trouble, and no calamity, illness or thieves either; when Peer moves, money is spent, the wife and concubines fall ill, and quarrels and plans meet many obstacles.

> [!note] 按
> 这是占家宅（住居宅第章第三首）里的两联。全歌先说「五事俱全不可克」（父母、兄弟、子孙、妻财、官鬼五类都要齐全、不被克），再按子、财、兄、父、鬼各一联讲各自发动的结果，这里只收有病的两联。
>
> *Note:* These are two couplets in the verse on dwellings (the third poem of the chapter on dwellings and houses). The whole poem first says 'all five kinds must be present and not controlled' (Resource, Peer, Output, Wealth and Pressure), then gives one couplet each for Output, Wealth, Peer, Resource and Pressure moving; only the two couplets with illness in them are taken.
