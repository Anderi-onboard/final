# 月将值日·太冲（月建卯）占病：在路头充着游瘟、寒热心头闷、脚手酸疼难动；四野伤亡作祸；卯宫卦断土主社司河神水鬼、急宜禳谢身康吉不须服药

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[断易鬼灵经]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建落在卯、叫太冲的时候占病：病是在路口上得的，撞上了游瘟，作祸生忧；有时冷有时热，心口闷，手脚酸疼，难以走动；是野外的伤亡鬼来作祸，要化解咒咀免得结成冤仇。「卯宫卦断」那一段说：土主、社司要设祭，河神、水鬼使病缠着不走；赶紧禳解酬谢，身体就康健吉利，不用吃药，自然没有忧愁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Month falls on Mao, named Taichong, divining illness: it was taken at the head of the road, having run into the Wandering Plague, which makes trouble and worry; now cold, now hot, oppression at the heart, the hands and feet aching and sore so that he can hardly move about; the dead by injury of the open fields have come to make trouble, and the curses are to be resolved so that they do not turn to enmity. The passage 'the verdict for the Mao-palace hexagrams' says: the Earth Lord and the Altar Official should be given offerings, and the River Spirit and the water ghosts make the illness linger; quickly perform rites and thanks and the body will be healthy and fortunate, with no need of medicine, and there will be no worry.

> [!note] 按
> 格式和名词见 LY.ILL.GLJ.01。标题括号里「太衡」是「太冲」的误写。「充着」读作冲着、撞上；「游瘟」是四处游走传播瘟疫的神鬼，书里没有展开；「作淹留」是使人久病、拖着不走。这一首最后两句「不须服药自无愁」是十二首里唯一直接说不用吃药的（第 2 首说「药方通」，要请巫师做了法事药才有用），两首的态度不一样，书里没有说明。
>
> *Note:* For the form and the terms see LY.ILL.GLJ.01. In the heading's bracket the printed 'Taiheng' is a misprint for 'Taichong'. 'Ran into' reads the book's 'charge against' as running up against; 'the Wandering Plague' is a spirit that wanders about spreading plague, which the book does not expand; 'make the illness linger' is the book's 'make him detained', that is, keep the illness on and not let it go. The last line of this verse, 'no medicine is needed, and there will be no worry', is the only one among the twelve that says outright that medicine is not needed (verse 2 says 'the medicine will work' once a shaman has performed the rites, so that medicine works only after the rites), and the two take different attitudes, which the book does not discuss.
