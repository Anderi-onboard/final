# 年时·更嫌值土，千门疫疠若符同（旧注：土鬼发动其年多瘟疫，生旺为甚，在艮宫动瘴气亦重，克世人多疫死，鬼带白虎亦是瘟疫之兆）

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》年时章（占一年的事）说：「更嫌遇到土（鬼），千家万户的疫病就像符契一样相同。」旧注：土鬼发动，这一年多瘟疫，生旺的更厉害，休囚的稍轻；在艮宫发动，瘴气也重；克世爻，人多死于疫病，世爻克它就不妨；鬼带白虎，也是瘟疫的征兆。王洪绪：土鬼发动，或者临白虎，都主瘟疫，如果克世，人多病死，有制伏就不妨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the year (divining a whole year) says: 'Still worse if it meets Earth: the epidemics in a thousand households tally as one.' The old commentary: an Earth ghost moving, the year has many epidemics, worse if strong and thriving, lighter if resting or confined; moving in the Gen palace, the miasma is heavy too; controlling the Self line, many people die of the epidemic, but if the Self line controls it, it does no harm; a ghost carrying the White Tiger is also a sign of epidemic. Wang Hongxu: an Earth ghost moving, or sitting on the White Tiger, both mean epidemic; if it controls the Self line many people fall ill and die, but if it is restrained, no harm.

> [!note] 按
> 这是占流年（一年的吉凶）一章里讲时疫的一联，看的是这一年的气运，不是占一个人的病。「千门疫疠若符同」是千家万户的疫病像合符契一样一样。「瘴气」是南方山林湿热之地的恶气，艮宫（山）发动特别重。同一章里讲鬼的其他联（旱蝗、火灾、雷）不收。
>
> *Note:* This couplet in the chapter on divining the year speaks of epidemics, and what is read is the year's fortune, not a single person's illness. 'The epidemics in a thousand households tally as one' says the epidemics in thousands of households match like the two halves of a tally. 'Miasma' is the bad air of the hot, damp mountains and forests of the south, and it is particularly heavy when Gen (the mountain) moves. The other couplets on the ghost in the same chapter (drought and locusts, fire, thunder) are not taken.
