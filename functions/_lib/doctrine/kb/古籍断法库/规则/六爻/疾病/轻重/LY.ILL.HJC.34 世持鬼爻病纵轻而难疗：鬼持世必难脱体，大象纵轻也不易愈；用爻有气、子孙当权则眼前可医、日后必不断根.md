# 世持鬼爻病纵轻而难疗：鬼持世必难脱体，大象纵轻也不易愈；用爻有气、子孙当权则眼前可医、日后必不断根

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻持世，病就是轻，也难医治：一定难以脱身。整个卦象再轻，也不容易痊愈。就算用爻有气、子孙当权，眼前能医好，以后也一定断不了根。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a Pressure line holds the Self line, even a light illness is hard to treat: it is surely hard to shake off. However light the whole image, it does not easily heal. Even if the Subject line has force and Output holds power, so that it can be cured for now, the root will surely not be cut later.

## 《黄金策·病体》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6886 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The patient's condition", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持鬼爻，病纵轻而难疗； | 鬼爻持世，病就是轻，也难医治； | A Pressure line holding the Self line: even if the illness is light, it is hard to treat; |

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> | 占病，怕鬼持世，必难脱体。 | 占病最怕鬼持世，一定难以脱身。 | In divining illness, Pressure holding the Self line is feared: it is surely hard to shake off. |
> | 大象纵轻，亦不易愈。 | 整个卦象就算轻，也不容易痊愈。 | Even if the whole image is light, it does not easily heal. |
> | 若用爻有气，子孙当权，目下虽可医愈，日后必不断根也。 | 如果用爻有气，子孙当权，眼前虽然能医好，以后一定断不了根。 | If the Subject line has force and Output holds power, it can be cured for the present, but in the days to come the root will surely not be cut. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2366 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占自病怕鬼持世，必难脱体。 | 占自己的病，怕鬼持世，一定难以脱身。 | In divining one's own illness, Pressure holding the Self line is feared: it is surely hard to shake off. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 是「占病」还是「占自病」：旧注说占病怕鬼持世，泛指所有的病；王洪绪限定成占自己的病（自己占自己的病）。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether it is 'illness' or 'one's own illness': the old commentary says that in divining illness Pressure holding the Self line is feared, taking in every illness; Wang Hongxu limits it to divining one's own illness.
