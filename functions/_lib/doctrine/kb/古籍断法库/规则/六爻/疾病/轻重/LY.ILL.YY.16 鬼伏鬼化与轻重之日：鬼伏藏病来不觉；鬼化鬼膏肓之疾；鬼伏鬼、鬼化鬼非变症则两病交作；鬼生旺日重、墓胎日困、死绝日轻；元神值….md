# 鬼伏鬼化与轻重之日：鬼伏藏病来不觉；鬼化鬼膏肓之疾；鬼伏鬼、鬼化鬼非变症则两病交作；鬼生旺日重、墓胎日困、死绝日轻；元神值日轻、忌神值日重；六冲乱动者重；用持龙福被动爻伤虽重不死；鬼动伤用而月日冲鬼者免

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼伏藏的，病来了不觉得；鬼变鬼的，是膏肓的重病；鬼伏在鬼下、鬼变出鬼的，不是病症有变，就是两种病同时发作；用爻下面伏着鬼的，鬼旺时复发。世爻持着子孙、下面伏着鬼的，子孙旺的那天病退，鬼旺的那天病重。鬼生旺的日子病重，鬼入墓、胎的日子病困，鬼死、绝的日子病轻；原神当值的日子一定轻，忌神当值的日子一定重；财爻入墓、绝的日子也重。卦是六冲又乱动的，重。用爻带着青龙、福德而被动爻伤到的，虽然重也不会死；鬼发动伤到用爻，而月、日冲鬼的，临到凶险也能免。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the ghost is hidden, the illness comes unnoticed; a ghost changing into a ghost is a disease lodged in the vitals; a ghost hidden beneath a ghost, or a ghost changing into a ghost, is either a change of symptoms or two illnesses at once; a ghost hidden beneath the Subject line makes it recur when the ghost is strong. If the Self holds Output with a ghost hidden beneath it, the illness retreats on the day Output is strong and worsens on the day the ghost is strong. It is heavy on days the ghost is born or strong, wearied on days the ghost is in a tomb or Womb, and light on days it is in Death or Extinction; on days when the Support is on duty it is surely light and when the Adversary is on duty surely heavy; it is also heavy on days when the Wealth line is in a tomb or Extinction. A Six-Clash hexagram with lines moving in disorder is heavy. If the Subject line carries the Dragon and Fortune and is harmed by a moving line, it is heavy but he does not die; if the ghost moves to harm the Subject line but the Month and Day clash the ghost, he escapes even at the brink of danger.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「膏肓」是心下膈上的深处，古人说病到膏肓无药可治，书里只说是「膏肓之疾」，没有再解释。「世持子伏鬼」的「子」是子孙爻。这一条的「元神值日必轻，忌神值日必重」和黄金策讲欲决病痊的日子（LY.ILL.HJC.57）是一路。
>
> *Note:* 'Gaohuang' is the deep region below the heart and above the diaphragm, of which the ancients said that an illness that reaches it cannot be treated; the book says only 'a disease of the gaohuang' and does not explain further. The 'zi' of 'the Self holds zi with a ghost hidden beneath' is the Output line. 'On days the Support is on duty it is light, on days the Adversary is on duty heavy' is on the same road as the Huangjin Ce's days for deciding recovery (LY.ILL.HJC.57).
