# 疾病先将身命看：逢龙见子放心宽；妻财入命多沉重、官鬼临身更不堪；身犯勾陈凶有准、命加玄武瘥应难；螣蛇白虎忧丧事、身命空亡定入棺；金鬼值身伤骨节、水鬼血浓攒、火鬼疮痍恼、木鬼百骨酸、土鬼肿胀；杀刑加命命摧残

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病先要看身和命：遇到青龙、见到子孙，可以放心。妻财入了命，多半沉重；官鬼临在身上，更加不堪。身犯勾陈，凶是有准的；命上加了玄武，痊愈就难。螣蛇、白虎，忧的是丧事；身命落空亡，一定入棺。金鬼值身，伤骨节；身临水鬼，血液浓稠聚积；贴着身的火鬼，疮痍烦恼；木鬼在身宫，百骨酸痛；土鬼在身，身体肿胀；杀和刑加在命上，命被摧残。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In illness first look at the Body and the natal line: meeting the Dragon and seeing Output, set the heart at ease. Wealth entering the natal line: mostly heavy; the Pressure line sitting on the Body: still worse. The Body offending Hook Earth: ill is certain; the natal line with the Dark Tortoise added: recovery is surely hard. The Coiling Snake and White Tiger: worry of a funeral; the Body and natal line Void: he will surely enter the coffin. A Metal ghost on the Body: injury to the bone joints; the Body on a Water ghost: blood thick and gathered; a Fire ghost close to the Body: the vexation of sores; a Wood ghost in the Body palace: all the bones ache; an Earth ghost on the Body: the body swells; a killing and punishment added to the natal line: life is ruined.

> [!note] 按
> 这一首（阐奥歌章疾病章第一首）很长，共十九联，这里分成三条：头七联（身命）在这一条，中间五联（五行脏腑、八卦部位）在 LY.ILL.CA.02，末六联（凶卦、化爻）在 LY.ILL.CA.03。「逢龙见子放心宽」的「龙」是青龙，「子」是子孙；「身」「命」是卦身和本命（病人的本命爻），本歌诀这样并称。「贴身火鬼」按字面译作「close to the Body」。「血浓攒」按「血液浓稠聚积」译，字面不确定。这一首在断易天机里也有，题作「孙膑断疾病歌」（见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.02 一带）。周易尚占的疾厄部（第 781–784 行）把这一首几乎原样收了一遍，字句有三处不同：「螣蛇」写作「腾蛇」（同一个神，字形不同）；「身临水鬼血浓攒」写作「身中水鬼血脓钻」（「脓」是脓，「浓」是浓稠；「钻」是往里钻，「攒」是聚积）；「木鬼身宫百骨酸」写作「木鬼身宫有骨酸」。
>
> *Note:* This first poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang's chapter on illness is very long, nineteen couplets, and is divided here into three entries: the first seven couplets (the Body and the natal line) in this entry, the middle five (the five phases and viscera, the trigrams and body parts) in LY.ILL.CA.02, and the last six (the ominous hexagrams, changing lines) in LY.ILL.CA.03. The 'Dragon' of 'meeting the Dragon and seeing Output' is the Azure Dragon, and 'Output' is the Output line; the 'Body' and 'natal line' are the hexagram's Body and the sick person's natal line, which the verse names together. 'A Fire ghost close to the Body' is rendered literally as 'close to the Body'. 'Xue nong zan' is translated as 'blood thick and gathered', the literal sense being uncertain. This poem is also in the Duanyi Tianji, titled 'Sun Bin's song on judging illness' (see LY.ILL.DYTJ.02 and around). The Zhouyi Shangzhan's section on illness and affliction (lines 781–784) takes this poem over almost word for word; the wording differs in three places: 'Coiling Snake' is written with a variant character for 'Coiling' (the same spirit); 'a Water ghost close to the Body, blood thick and gathered' is written 'a Water ghost within the Body, blood and pus boring in' ('pus' where the Bushi Daquan has 'thick', and 'boring in' where it has 'gathered'); and 'a Wood ghost in the Body palace, a hundred bones aching' is written 'a Wood ghost in the Body palace, the bones ache'.
