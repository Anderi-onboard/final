# 何知章·亲人的病灾：父母疾（白虎临爻兼刑克）、父母殃（财爻发动煞神伤）、子孙疾（父母爻动相克）、子孙灾、小儿死（子孙空亡加白虎）、兄弟亡（用落空亡白虎伤）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 「何知」口诀里关于亲人病灾的六问：父母有病，是白虎临着（父母）这一爻，再加刑克；父母有灾殃，是财爻发动、煞神来伤；子孙有病，是父母爻发动来克制子孙；子孙有灾，卜筮大全说是白虎落到福德（子孙）上，卜筮正宗说是父母落到福德上；小孩要死，是子孙落空亡再加白虎；兄弟去世，是用神落空亡又被白虎所伤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Six questions of the 'how to know' rhyme about illness and calamity among kin. A parent is ill when the White Tiger sits on the (Resource) line together with punishment and control; a parent meets calamity when the Wealth line moves and a killing spirit wounds; a child is ill when the Resource line moves and controls Output; a child meets misfortune when, in the Bushi Daquan, the White Tiger arrives upon Output, or, in the Bushi Zhengzong, Resource arrives upon Output; a small child will die when Output is Void and the White Tiger is added; a brother dies when the Subject falls into Void and the White Tiger wounds it.

> [!warning] 打架
> 子孙有灾看什么：卜筮大全答「白虎当临福德来」，白虎落到子孙爻上；卜筮正宗答「父母当临福德来」，父母爻落到子孙爻上。两本一个说白虎、一个说父母，书里没有说哪个对（白虎是六神，父母是六亲，两条不是一回事）。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What to look at for a child's misfortune: the Bushi Daquan answers 'the White Tiger arrives upon Output', the White Tiger falling on the Output line; the Bushi Zhengzong answers 'Resource arrives upon Output', the Resource line falling on the Output line. One says the White Tiger and the other Resource, and the books do not say which is right (the White Tiger is one of the six spirits and Resource one of the six relations, so the two are not the same thing).

> [!note] 按
> 「何知章」一问一答，一共问了三四十件事（父母子孙的病、有没有子孙、讼事、财富、田地、屋宇、坟墓、六畜、口舌、盗贼……），都是一家的事；这里只收与病、死、鬼怪有关的。卜筮大全里它是「十六、何知章」，卜筮正宗里它在《赋文三篇》中；两本字句略有出入。「杀神」「煞神」是同一个字的两种写法。「用落空亡」的「用」是用神。
>
> *Note:* The 'how to know' rhyme asks and answers some thirty or forty things (illnesses of parents and children, whether there are children, lawsuits, wealth, land, houses, graves, livestock, quarrels, thieves and more), all affairs of a household; only those to do with illness, death and ghostly happenings are taken here. In the Bushi Daquan it is chapter 16, 'the how-to-know rhyme'; in the Bushi Zhengzong it is among the three rhapsodies; the two texts differ a little in wording. 'Sha' in the two forms of 'killing spirit' is one word written two ways. The 'yong' of 'yong falls into Void' is the Subject.
