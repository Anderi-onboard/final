# 断病生死全凭用神：卦名、卦身世身兼用、本命丧门、蛇虎星煞都不验，只有卦变六冲例外

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断病的生死，全凭用神，别的都不验：只看卦名的（得明夷、大过、观、贲、蛊、夬、丰、同人、大畜、需就断必死），说本命临死气丧门、寿衣棺木要早备的，说螣蛇动主死、白虎动主丧的，都不灵。恶煞凶神就算临了身命，只要用神、世爻和身爻旺相，你说他是生是死？不看用神也能断生死的，只有卦变六冲一种。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Life and death in an illness are judged from the Subject alone, and nothing else proves true: judging from the hexagram name alone (Mingyi, Daguo, Guan, Bi, Gu, Guai, Feng, Tongren, Daxu and Xu being called surely fatal), saying that a natal line on the death spirit or the mourning gate means shroud and coffin must be ready early, saying that a moving Coiling Snake means death and a moving White Tiger mourning: none of these is reliable. When fierce spirits and ill stars already sit on the body and the natal line, yet the Subject and the Self and body lines are strong, who is to say he lives or dies? The one case in which life and death may be judged without looking at the Subject is a hexagram changing into a Six-Clash hexagram.

> [!warning] 打架
> 断生死看什么：黄金策的病体章除了用神，还讲卦身、世爻、本命爻（LY.ILL.HJC.35、HJC.41、HJC.53、HJC.54）；野鹤说这些并用是舛错，断生死全凭用神，只有卦变六冲例外。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What to look at in judging life and death: besides the Subject the Huangjin Ce's chapter on the patient's condition also speaks of the hexagram Body, the Self line and the natal line (LY.ILL.HJC.35, HJC.41, HJC.53, HJC.54); the Old Man of Yehe says that using them together is error, that life and death rest wholly on the Subject, and that a hexagram changing into Six-Clash is the only exception.

> [!note] 按
> 野鹤这一段是对整个占病断法的总批评。他驳的是靠卦名、本命丧门、蛇虎星煞断生死的旧法；黄金策病体章断生死靠的是各爻的生克旺衰，不靠星煞。「衾棺」是入殓用的被和棺材。「明夷、大过、观、贲、蛊、夬、丰、同人、大畜、需」是那些书里断必死的十个卦名。
>
> *Note:* This passage is the Old Man's general criticism of the whole way of judging illness. What he rejects is the old practice of judging life and death by the hexagram name, by the natal line on the death spirit or mourning gate, and by the stars and the Snake and Tiger; the Huangjin Ce's chapter on the patient's condition judges by the generation, control and strength of the lines and not by stars. 'Quilt and coffin' is the shroud and coffin for the laying-out. Mingyi, Daguo, Guan, Bi, Gu, Guai, Feng, Tongren, Daxu and Xu are the ten hexagram names that those books judge surely fatal.
