# 休囚岂可持身：出花的人要体旺才易收功，用神休囚必是体弱，再无原神日月生扶，后来也有变

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出花的人要身体强旺才容易收功；用神休囚，一定是身体虚弱，再没有原神、日、月来生扶的，后来也会有变化。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A person having the pox should be of strong body, and then it is easy to bring to completion; if the Subject is resting or trapped, the body is surely weak, and if in addition no Support, Day or Month generates or supports it, there will be change later on.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2243 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 出花之人宜于体旺，则易收功，如用神休囚，必是体弱，再无原神日月生扶者，后亦有变。 | 出花的人适合身体强旺，这样就容易收功；如果用神休囚，一定是身体虚弱，再没有原神、日、月来生扶的，后来也会有变化。 | A person having the pox should be of strong body, and then it is easy to bring to completion; if the Subject is resting or trapped, the body is surely weak, and if in addition no Support, Day or Month generates or supports it, there will be change later on. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 经文只有「休囚岂可持身」六个字，没有说是谁休囚；注把它解成用神休囚。
>
> *Note:* The verse is only the six characters 'how can resting or trapped hold the body' and does not say what is resting or trapped; the commentary takes it as the Subject.
