# 医克用爻近病即愈：自占病应爻克世、应爻克用神是克制我的病；不宜久病和体弱的人，克病也伤身；应爻临兄鬼而克用神或克世爻，不拘久近必遭其害

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 医生克用爻，近病就好。自己占病，应爻克世爻，是克制我的病；应爻克用神，也这样断。但是不适宜久病和体弱的人，因为它不光克掉了病，身体也受到伤害。应爻临着兄弟、官鬼而去克用神，以及克世爻的，不管久病近病，一定受它的害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The doctor controlling the Subject line: a recent illness heals at once. In divining one's own illness, if the Other line controls the Self line, it restrains my illness; if the Other line controls the Subject, judge the same. It is unsuitable only for one long ill or of weak body, for it controls not only the illness but harms the body too. If the Other line sits on Peer or Pressure and controls the Subject, or controls the Self line, whether the illness is long or recent, harm from it is certain.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条讲的是「应爻」克世爻反而是好事：病是克我的，应（医生）去克它。和黄金策病体章里应克世的一般讲法（LY.ILL.HJC.25 的应临鬼刑克世）不同，那里是别人的病缠过来。
>
> *Note:* This entry treats the Other line controlling the Self line as, on the contrary, good: the illness is what controls me, and the Other line (the doctor) comes to control it. It differs from the usual reading of the Other line against the Self line in the chapter on the patient's condition of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.25, Pressure on the Other line punishing the Self line), where someone else's illness comes to entangle the patient.
