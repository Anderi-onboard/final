# 鬼持世病虽轻而难疗有两说：自占病鬼持世难痊，子孙克鬼目下愈而终不断根，鬼临帝旺长生必久病；代占六亲之病，鬼持世是忧郁之状，子孙发动克去忧郁则平安（觉子：占父母、妻占夫病不宜子孙动）

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼持世，病虽轻也难治，有两种情形。自己占病：官鬼持世，病难痊愈；就算子孙发动、克掉身边的鬼，眼前好了，终究断不了根，不然就又生别的病，叫「病不离身，身不离药」；官鬼持世又临帝旺、长生的，一定得长久的病。替别人占六亲的病：官鬼持世是忧郁的样子，只喜欢子孙发动、克掉这份忧郁，就算用神衰弱也管保平安。觉子补充：这个办法用于占自己和兄弟、儿子、妻子；占父母的病，官鬼是父母的元神；妻子占丈夫的病，鬼是用神，子孙一动反而伤它。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line holding the Self line means that even a light illness is hard to treat, and there are two cases. In divining one's own illness: with Pressure holding the Self line the illness is hard to heal; even if Output moves and controls away the Pressure by one's side, so that he recovers for the present, the root is never cut, or else another illness grows, which is called 'the illness does not leave the body, the body does not leave medicine'; if Pressure holds the Self line and sits on Peak or Birth, he surely gets a long-lasting illness. In divining a relation's illness for another: Pressure holding the Self line is the picture of worry and gloom, and all that is wanted is Output moving to control away this gloom, and then even if the Subject is weak, peace is assured. Juezi adds: this method serves for divining oneself and a brother, son or wife; in divining a parent's illness Pressure is the Support of the Resource line, and when a wife divines her husband's illness Pressure is the Subject, so a moving Output harms it instead.

> [!note] 按
> 野鹤先重写黄金策的「世持鬼爻，病纵轻而难疗」，再拆成两种说法：自占和代占。黄金策旧注（LY.ILL.HJC.34）泛说占病怕鬼持世，王洪绪只限自占；野鹤的第一种和王洪绪一致，第二种是他另加的。觉子的补充和 LY.ILL.HJC.32 里王洪绪说的「占父母、丈夫病不宜子孙发动」是一个意思。
>
> *Note:* The Old Man first rewrites the Huangjin Ce's line 'Pressure holding the Self line: though light, hard to treat' and then splits it into two views, one for divining oneself and one for divining another. The old commentary of the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.34) says in general that in divining illness Pressure holding the Self line is feared, and Wang Hongxu limits it to divining one's own; the Old Man's first view agrees with Wang Hongxu and the second is his own addition. Juezi's addition says the same as Wang Hongxu's remark in LY.ILL.HJC.32 that in divining a parent's or a husband's illness Output should not move.
