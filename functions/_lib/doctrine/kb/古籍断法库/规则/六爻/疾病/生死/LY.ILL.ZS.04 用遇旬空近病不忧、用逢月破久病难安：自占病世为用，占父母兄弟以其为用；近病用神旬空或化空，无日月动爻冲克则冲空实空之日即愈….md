# 用遇旬空近病不忧、用逢月破久病难安：自占病世为用，占父母兄弟以其为用；近病用神旬空或化空，无日月动爻冲克则冲空实空之日即愈，逢冲克病虽重不死；月破看用神衰旺；久病用神空破，旺相也无法医治

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 自己占病，世爻是用神；占父母、兄弟的病，拿父母、兄弟做用神。近病：用神落旬空或变空，如果没有日月和动爻来冲克，冲空、实空的日子病就好；如果遇到冲克，病再重也不会死。用神逢月破，要看用神旺衰：旺的，到实破那天或出了这个月就好；衰又受克的，一定危险。久病：用神落旬空或月破的，就算用神旺相，也没法医治。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining one's own illness the Self line is the Subject; in divining a parent or brother, the Resource or Peer line is the Subject. A recent illness whose Subject falls into Void or changes into Void heals on the day the Void is clashed or filled if no Day, Month or moving line clashes or controls it; if it meets clash or control, even a heavy illness does not lead to death. If the Subject meets Month-break, look at whether it is strong or weak: if strong, the patient recovers on the day the break is filled or once the month has passed; if weak and controlled, he is surely in danger. In a long illness, if the Subject falls into Void or Month-break, then even if it is strong nothing can be done to treat it.

> [!note] 按
> 「寻空」原文如此，按上下文是「旬空」。「冲空」是用冲把落空的爻冲开；「实空」是落空的那个地支值日，把空填实，「实破」同理。这一条讲的是近病和久病要分开断，是下一条（LY.ILL.ZS.05）驳「主空无救」的根据。
>
> *Note:* 'Xunkong' is written 'searching for the void' in the original, and from the context it is Void. To 'clash Void' is to clash open a line that has fallen into Void; to 'fill Void' is for the branch of the Void line to rule the day and fill the void, and 'fill a break' is the same. This entry judges recent and long illness separately, and is the ground of the next entry (LY.ILL.ZS.05), which rejects 'a Void main image cannot be saved'.
