# 家宅·四鬼贴身，防生灾咎（旧注：贴身鬼带破碎杀主有破相之疾；乾头面小肠喘急咳嗽、坎两耳肾、艮背肋手指、震下部腰足、巽两股两腿头发血气、离眼目心经、坤肠脾胃、兑口齿唇）

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》家宅章说：「四个鬼爻贴着身，要防生出灾祸。」旧注：贴身的鬼爻带着破碎杀，主有伤了相貌的病；按八卦推：乾主头、脸、小肠，喘急咳嗽的病；坎主两耳，肾脏之间的病；艮主背和肋，手指的病；震主下半身，腰脚疼痛的病；巽主两条大腿和两条腿，头发、血气的病；离主眼睛，心经的病；坤主肠子之间，脾胃的病；兑主口和牙，以及嘴唇的病；其余照这样推。王洪绪只解释「四鬼」：是年、月、日、时都值着官鬼，持世、临身、临命，主有灾祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the household says: 'Four ghosts clinging to the Body: beware of calamity.' The old commentary: a ghost clinging to the Body and carrying the Breaking killing means an illness that spoils the looks; by trigram: Qian governs the head, face and small intestine, illnesses of rapid panting and cough; Kan the two ears, illnesses of the region of the kidneys; Gen the back and ribs, illnesses of the fingers; Zhen the lower body, illnesses of pain in the waist and feet; Xun the two thighs and two legs, illnesses of the hair and of blood and qi; Li the eyes, illnesses of the heart channel; Kun the intestines, illnesses of the spleen and stomach; Dui the mouth and teeth and the lips; the rest by analogy. Wang Hongxu explains only the 'four ghosts': the year, month, day and hour all fall on Pressure, holding the Self line, sitting on the Body or on the natal line, which brings calamity.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3165 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 四鬼者，亦谓年月日时，值官鬼持世临身临命，主有灾咎。 | 「四鬼」，也就是说年、月、日、时四个（支）都值着官鬼，持世、临身、临命，主有灾祸。 | The 'four ghosts' means that the year, month, day and hour all fall on Pressure, holding the Self line, sitting on the Body or on the natal line, which brings calamity. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 八卦各管身体哪一处，几处说法不同。这一条的旧注是「乾头面小肠、坎两耳肾、艮背肋手指、震下部腰足、巽两股两腿、离眼目心经、坤肠脾胃、兑口齿唇」，和卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.12：乾头、坤腹、坎耳、离目、兑口、艮手、震足、巽股）大体一致，巽也是股；阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.02）是巽手、艮鼻，孙膑歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.03）是巽肠。同一书坟墓章的另一张表（LY.ILL.HJC.140）艮多了鼻疮，震多了「笑言」，和这一张略有出入。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The books differ on which part of the body each trigram governs. The old commentary on this entry has 'Qian head, face and small intestine; Kan two ears and kidneys; Gen back, ribs and fingers; Zhen lower body, waist and feet; Xun two thighs and two legs; Li eyes and heart channel; Kun intestines, spleen and stomach; Dui mouth, teeth and lips', which is in general consistent with the Bushi Yuangui passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.12: Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ear, Li the eye, Dui the mouth, Gen the hand, Zhen the foot, Xun the thigh), including Xun the thigh; the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.02) has Xun the hand and Gen the nose, and Sun Bin's verse (LY.ILL.DYTJ.03) Xun the intestines. Another table in the chapter on graves of the same book (LY.ILL.HJC.140) adds nose sores for Gen and 'laughing and talking' for Zhen, a little different from this one. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 这是占家宅一章里「四鬼贴身，防生灾咎」一联的旧注，讲贴身的鬼（持世、临身、临命的官鬼）带着破碎杀的时候，按八卦看病在身体哪里。「破碎」是一个神煞（破碎杀）。「破相之疾」是伤了相貌的病（破相是容貌残缺）。「四鬼」，王洪绪解作年月日时四个支都值着官鬼。「小口」「肠间」等按字面。
>
> *Note:* This is the old commentary on the couplet 'four ghosts clinging to the Body: beware of calamity' in the chapter on the household, telling where in the body the illness lies, by trigram, when the ghost clinging to the Body (a Pressure line holding the Self line, sitting on the Body or on the natal line) carries the Breaking killing. 'Breaking' is a spirit-star (the Breaking killing). An 'illness that spoils the looks' is an illness that disfigures. As for the 'four ghosts', Wang Hongxu takes it as the year, month, day and hour branches all falling on Pressure. 'The intestines' and the like are to the letter.
