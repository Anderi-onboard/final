# 空临世上心中强欲求医：世爻空亡病人不会专心求医，无故自空请医不成，勉强为之药亦不效；占服药而世空是药不对症

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻落空，病人不会专心去求医；世爻无缘无故落空，请医生决请不成；勉强去做，药也没有效果。占服药时世爻落空，是药不对症。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Self line is Void, the patient will not set his mind on seeking a doctor; if it is Void for no reason, the call will surely not succeed; if he forces it, the medicine also has no effect. In divining the taking of medicine, a Void Self line means that the medicine does not fit the illness.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6988 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Doctors and medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 空临世上，心中强欲求医。 | 空亡落在世爻上，心里勉强想要求医。 | Void on the Self line: in the heart there is only a forced wish to seek a doctor. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2422 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世爻空亡，必得专心求医，或自不相信他，虽请彼看，亦不用其药石。 | 世爻空亡，一定不会专心求医，或者自己不相信那个医生，就算请他来看，也不用他的药。 | The Self line Void: he surely will not set his mind on seeking a doctor, or does not trust that doctor himself; even if he invites him to look, he will not use his medicines. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 王洪绪原文写「必得专心求医」，和下文「或自不相信他」接不上，大概是旧注「必不专心求医」的笔误；译文按「必不」译。「强欲求医」按字面是「勉强想要求医」。
>
> *Note:* Wang Hongxu's text reads 'he must set his mind on seeking a doctor', which does not join up with the following 'or does not trust him himself'; it is probably a slip for the old commentary's 'he surely does not set his mind on seeking a doctor', and the translation follows 'does not'. 'Strongly wants to seek a doctor' (qiang yu qiu yi) is in the letter 'forces himself to want a doctor'.
