# 坤腹乾头、兑喉风咳嗽、艮足震手、巽瘫痪肠风：鬼在八宫的病位；以鬼在坤宫为例，五行之鬼各主一病

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻落在哪一宫，病就在哪个部位：坤是腹、乾是头，兑是喉风咳嗽，艮和震是手足，巽是瘫痪、肠风。以鬼在坤宫为例：火鬼腹痈，水鬼腹痛，动化财爻或财化水鬼是泻痢，土鬼是癖块食积或沙胀蛊症，木鬼是绞肠痧痛或大肠有病，金鬼是胁肋痛（在上胸痛、在下腰痛）。别的卦照此推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where the Pressure line falls in the palaces fixes where the illness is: Kun is the belly and Qian the head; Dui is throat wind and cough; Gen and Zhen are the hand and foot; Xun is paralysis and intestinal wind. Taking Pressure in the Kun palace as the example: a Fire Pressure is an abdominal abscess, a Water Pressure pain in the belly; moving and changing into a Wealth line, or Wealth changing into a Water Pressure, is diarrhea and dysentery; an Earth Pressure is abdominal masses and food accumulation, or sha bloating and gu-swelling; a Wood Pressure is twisting colic or trouble in the large intestine; a Metal Pressure is pain in the flanks (chest if high, loins if low). The other palaces follow by analogy.

> [!note] 按
> 经文和旧注写「艮足震手」，卜筮正宗写成「艮手、震足」；本章下面讲震宫的一条（LY.ILL.HJC.19）是「鬼在震宫，病在足」，和卜筮正宗的写法相合。卜筮大全旧注这一条的前半（「鬼在坤宫，腹中有病……土鬼：」）在前面重复抄了一次，这里只收完整的那一次。「沙胀蛊症」「绞肠痧」是旧医书里的病名，书里没有解释，译文保留拼音。
>
> *Note:* The verse and the old commentary write 'Gen the foot, Zhen the hand', while the Bushi Zhengzong writes 'Gen the hand, Zhen the foot'; the entry below in this chapter on the Zhen palace (LY.ILL.HJC.19) says 'Pressure in the Zhen palace, the illness is in the foot', which agrees with the Bushi Zhengzong. In the Bushi Daquan the first half of this commentary ('Pressure in the Kun palace, illness in the belly ... Earth Pressure:') is copied twice, and only the complete copy is taken here. 'Sha bloating and gu-swelling' and 'twisting colic (jiaochang sha)' are disease names from old medical books which the book does not explain, and the translation keeps the sounds.
