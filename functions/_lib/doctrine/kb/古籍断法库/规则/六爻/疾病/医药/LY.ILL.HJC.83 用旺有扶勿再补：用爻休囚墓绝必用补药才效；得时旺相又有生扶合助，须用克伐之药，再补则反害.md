# 用旺有扶勿再补：用爻休囚墓绝必用补药才效；得时旺相又有生扶合助，须用克伐之药，再补则反害

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻休囚墓绝，一定要用补药才有效。如果用爻得时旺相，又有生扶合助，就要用克伐的药来治，再补反而有害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line is resting, trapped, in a tomb or extinct, only a tonic is effective. If the Subject line is in season and strong and also has generation, support and combination to help it, it must be treated with medicine that attacks and subdues, and to tonify further does harm instead.

> [!note] 按
> 用爻的旺衰决定用补还是用泻，这和病体章 LY.ILL.HJC.36（用壮有扶，太刚则折）相通：病中的人，用爻太旺反而不好。王洪绪这一段把「用旺有扶」和「鬼衰属水」两条经文合在一起注，后半在下一条（LY.ILL.HJC.84）。
>
> *Note:* That the strength or weakness of the Subject line decides whether to tonify or to purge is related to LY.ILL.HJC.36 in the chapter on the patient's condition (strong and supported, too rigid, it snaps): for a person in illness, a Subject line that is too strong is on the contrary not good. Wang Hongxu's paragraph comments on the two verses 'strong and supported, no more tonics' and 'a weak Pressure line belonging to Water, no needle' together, and the second half is in the next entry (LY.ILL.HJC.84).
