# 出花的是谁就按谁取用神：兄弟出花以兄弟爻为用神、父母爻为原神；子侄以子孙爻为用神、父动克子是凶；妻奴婢妾以财爻为用神、兄动克财不宜

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看是谁出花来取用神：兄弟出花，拿兄弟爻做用神、父母爻做原神，父母爻发动来生它就吉；子侄出花，拿子孙爻做用神，父母爻发动就克制子孙，是凶，而兄弟爻发动对子侄反而是好的；妻子、奴婢、妾出花，拿财爻做用神，兄弟爻发动克制财爻，也不合适。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject is taken by who is having the pox. For a brother, the Peer line is the Subject and the Resource line the Support, and if the Support moves and generates it, it is lucky. For a child or nephew, the Output line is the Subject, and if Resource moves it controls Output, which is unlucky, while a moving Peer is welcome; for a wife, servant, maid or concubine, the Wealth line is the Subject, and a moving Peer controls Wealth, which is likewise unsuitable.

## 《卜筮正宗·新增痘疹》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2233 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Added chapter on smallpox", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父动则护持乎兄弟，儿孙安得云宜； | 父母爻发动，就护着兄弟，那么儿孙怎么能说合适呢； | If Resource moves, it protects and sustains the Peer, so how can it be called suitable for children and grandchildren; |
> | 兄兴则为难于妻奴，子侄喜其相遇。 | 兄弟爻兴旺，就为难妻子、奴婢，而子侄却喜欢碰到它。 | if Peer rises, it makes trouble for wives and servants, while children and nephews are glad to meet it. |

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如卜兄弟出花，以兄弟爻为用神，父母爻为原神，动而生之则吉。 | 比如占兄弟出花，拿兄弟爻做用神，父母爻做原神，父母爻发动来生它，就吉。 | For example, in divining a brother's pox, the Peer line is the Subject and the Resource line is the Support; if it moves and generates it, it is lucky. |
> | 倘卜子侄，以子孙爻为用神，父动则克子是凶。 | 如果占子侄，拿子孙爻做用神，父母爻发动就克制子孙，是凶。 | If divining a child or nephew, the Output line is the Subject, and if Resource moves it controls Output, which is unlucky. |

> | 凡卜子侄喜遇兄弟动也。 | 凡是占子侄，喜欢遇到兄弟爻发动。 | In all divining of a child or nephew, a moving Peer is welcome. |

> [!warning] 打架
> 用神取哪一爻：王洪绪按出花的是谁分开取，兄弟取兄弟爻、子侄取子孙爻、妻奴婢妾取财爻，官鬼爻则是痘花本身；增删卜易的野鹤把出花的几件事分开占，问什么时候出花拿鬼爻当用神（LY.ILL.ZS.19），问吉凶大体上全看子孙爻，不管来问的是谁（LY.ILL.ZS.21）。两家取用神的办法不一样，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which line is the Subject: Wang Hongxu takes it by who has the pox — the Peer line for a brother, the Output line for a child or nephew, the Wealth line for a wife, servant, maid or concubine — and the Pressure line is the pox flowers themselves; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi divides the matters of the pox into separate divinations, taking the Pressure line as the Subject for when the pox will come out (LY.ILL.ZS.19) and looking, on the whole, only at the Output line for fortune, whoever is asking (LY.ILL.ZS.21). The two differ on how the Subject is taken, and the books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 经文说的是「父动则护持乎兄弟」：父母爻发动，护着兄弟（生兄弟），这对占兄弟出花是好事，但父母爻克子孙，所以对儿孙不合适；兄弟发动克财，所以妻奴受难，而子孙不怕兄弟。
>
> *Note:* The verse says 'if Resource moves it protects the Peer': a moving Resource line generates Peer, which is good for a divination about a brother, but Resource controls Output, so it is unsuitable for children and grandchildren; a moving Peer controls Wealth, so wives and servants suffer, while Output has nothing to fear from Peer.
