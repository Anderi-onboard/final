# 医药·治法与用药：官鬼属金宜灸不宜丸药、属木宜针不宜汤药、属水宜温药丸药不宜针、属火宜凉药汤药不宜草头药饮片、属土宜咬咀不宜灸（取克鬼者、忌生鬼者）；鬼伤二间宜宽胸膈；财外动合用宜吐、内动宜泻；鬼在外卦宜外表、伏内卦宜内表

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用药不要轻易乱投。官鬼属金的，适合灸，不适合丸药（火能克金，土生金）；鬼属木的，适合针，不适合汤药（金能克木，水生木）；鬼属水的，适合温药、丸药，不适合针（土克水，金生水）；鬼属火的，适合凉药、汤药，不适合草头药和饮片（水克火，木生火）；鬼属土的，适合咬咀，不适合灸（木克土，火生土）。此外，鬼伤到两个间爻的，适合宽胸膈；世爻旺又有扶助的，不妨再进补；财爻在外卦发动合用爻的，适合吐法，在内卦发动合用爻的，适合泻法；用爻、鬼爻出现或在外卦的，适合治外表，伏藏在内卦的，适合治内。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Medicine is not to be given lightly. If the Pressure line is Metal, moxibustion suits and pills do not (Fire can control Metal, while Earth generates Metal); if the ghost is Wood, acupuncture suits and decoctions do not (Metal can control Wood, while Water generates Wood); if Water, warm medicines and pills suit and acupuncture does not (Earth controls Water, while Metal generates Water); if Fire, cool medicines and decoctions suit and herbal medicines and sliced drugs do not (Water controls Fire, while Wood generates Fire); if Earth, crushed herbs suit and moxibustion does not (Wood controls Earth, while Fire generates Earth). Besides: if the ghost harms the two between lines, ease the chest and diaphragm; if the Self is strong and supported, there is no harm in tonics again; if Wealth moves in the outer trigram and combines with the Subject line, an emetic suits, and in the inner trigram, a purgative; if the Subject line and the Pressure line appear openly or are in the outer trigram, treat the exterior, and if hidden in the inner trigram, treat the interior.

> [!warning] 打架
> 治法与药性要不要按鬼的五行来选：易隐说得很具体——鬼属金宜灸、属木宜针、属水宜温药丸药、属火宜凉药汤药、属土宜咬咀，原则是用克鬼的、避开生鬼的；黄金策也有火鬼旺宜大寒药、水鬼旺宜大热药的说法（LY.ILL.HJC.82、LY.ILL.HJC.84），方向一致。增删卜易的李我平驳这些说法拘泥，说子孙不是针灸之药，胶柱鼓瑟会坑陷人命（LY.ILL.ZS.40）。三家意见不同，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether the way of treatment and the nature of the drug is chosen by the element of the ghost: the Yi Yin is very specific — Metal, moxibustion; Wood, acupuncture; Water, warm medicines and pills; Fire, cool medicines and decoctions; Earth, crushed herbs — the principle being to use what controls the ghost and avoid what generates it; the Huangjin Ce also has the sayings that a strong Fire ghost suits very cold medicine and a strong Water ghost very hot medicine (LY.ILL.HJC.82 and LY.ILL.HJC.84), in the same direction. Li Woping of the Zengshan Buyi rejects these as rigid, saying Output is not the medicine of acupuncture and moxibustion and that sticking to the letter pits and drowns lives (LY.ILL.ZS.40). The three differ and the books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 「君臣佐使」是配药时药的四种分工。「咬咀」（也写作㕮咀）是把药切碎或捣成粗末，用来煎汤。「表」是身体的表层，「外表」「内表」书里没有再解释，译文按字面译作治外表、治内。「二间」是世应之间的两爻（间爻）。括号里的话是书里自己的理由。
>
> *Note:* 'Sovereign, minister, assistant and envoy' are the four divisions of labour among the drugs in compounding. 'Yaozu' (also written fu zu) is cutting medicine up or pounding it into coarse powder for decoction. 'Biao' is the surface layer of the body, and 'outer biao' and 'inner biao' are not explained further and are rendered literally as treating the exterior and the interior. The 'two between' are the two lines between the Self and Other (the between lines). The words in brackets are the book's own reasons.
