# 不起之症卦中不见其医、立愈之灾爻中不报用药：用神化鬼、鬼化用神、忌神化六亲、六亲化忌神、用神化绝化克化墓、久病逢冲逢空、随鬼入墓，都是无药可医；近病六冲或用神旬空或化回头生、自占子孙持世，不药而愈，也不现医

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占医生的卦里看不到医生，有两种情形。一种是治不好的病，神不让医生出现：用神变鬼、鬼变用神，忌神变六亲、六亲变忌神，用神变绝、变克、变墓，久病遇冲遇空，随着鬼入墓，这些都是没有药可医的病。另一种是马上就好的病，神也不让医生出现：近病如果不是卦得六冲，就是用神值旬空、或用神变回头生；自己占病，子孙持世，这些都是不吃药就好的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a hexagram that divines a doctor, the doctor may fail to appear, in two cases. One is an illness that cannot be cured, where the spirit does not let the doctor appear: the Subject changing into Pressure or Pressure into the Subject, the Adversary into a relation or a relation into the Adversary, the Subject changing into extinction, into a control or into a tomb, a long illness meeting a clash or Void, following Pressure into the tomb; all these are illnesses that no medicine can treat. The other is an illness that heals at once, where the spirit also does not let the doctor appear: a recent illness is either in a Six-Clash hexagram, or has its Subject in Void or changing into a returning generation; in divining one's own illness, Output holds the Self line; in all these the patient recovers without medicine.
