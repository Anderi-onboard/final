# 占失物鬼祟(占祟)：阳鬼为男阴鬼为女、伏藏返对而取；占贼占祟以鬼阴阳为用；占祟鬼无正形但以支干取之、鬼动单为少阳拆为少阴重为太阳交为太阴、分老少何人看应爻最切

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 阳鬼是男，阴鬼是女；若是伏藏，就反过来取。占贼、占祟，以鬼的阴阳为用，占定是男是女，要看得的是哪一个官（鬼）。占祟，鬼没有正形，只用地支天干来取：鬼动，单（阳）是少阳，拆（阴）是少阴，重是太阳，交是太阴；要分老少是什么人，只看应爻最切。书中举了一个占祟的例子：遁之姤卦，是子孙鬼，一男一女，和首饰之类的东西有牵连，坟墓在西北。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A yang ghost is male, a yin ghost is female; if it is hidden, take it the other way round. In divining a thief or a curse take the yin or yang of the ghost as the Subject, and fix male or female by which official (ghost) is obtained. In divining a curse the ghost has no true form, and it is taken only by the branch and stem: a moving ghost, single (yang) is lesser yang, split (yin) lesser yin, double is greater yang and crossed is greater yin; to tell what person is old or young look only at the Other line, which is the closest fit. The book gives an example of divining a curse: the hexagram Dun changing to Gou is an Output ghost, one man and one woman, bound up with things such as hairpins and bracelets, and the grave in the northwest.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条前两段（L364—L365）是火珠林「占失物鬼祟」里占祟的那一半；前面「占贼」的那几句（阳宫鬼出现主男鬼、伏藏主女……）是占失物的，归失物那个问题域，这里不收。「以鬼阴阳为用，占主女男看得何官」的后半句意思读不确定，「得何官」是得到的是哪个官（鬼），字面译。「单」「拆」「重」「交」是四种爻的写法：单是阳爻不动（少阳）、拆是阴爻不动（少阴）、重是阳爻动（太阳）、交是阴爻动（太阴）。第三段是书里举的占祟的例子，出在全书前面「易中明义」一节里，按书中例收，原文里两行之间隔了一个空行，连着读。例里「一男丙午，一女辛亥」、「钗钏金」、「火绝在亥」等按原文译。「如二乾宫子孙」读不确定，字面译。
>
> *Note:* The first two passages of this entry (L364-L365) are the half of the Huozhu Lin's 'divining lost things and ghostly curses' that is about divining a curse; the earlier sentences about divining a thief ('a yang-palace ghost appearing means a male ghost, hidden means female ...') are about lost things and belong to that question domain, and are not taken here. The second half of 'take the yin or yang of the ghost as the Subject, fix male or female by which official is obtained' is not certain in sense, and 'which official is obtained' is rendered literally. 'Single', 'split', 'double' and 'crossed' are four ways of writing a line: single is a yang line still (lesser yang), split is a yin line still (lesser yin), double is a yang line moving (greater yang) and crossed is a yin line moving (greater yin). The third passage is an example of divining a curse given in the book, in the section 'What is made plain in the Changes' near the front of the whole book, and it is taken as a case from the book; in the original a blank line falls between two of its lines, and it is read as continuous. In the example 'the one man is Bing Wu and the one woman is Xin Hai', 'hairpin-and-bracelet Metal' and 'Fire is in extinction at Hai' are rendered as in the original. 'Like two Output lines of the Qian palace' is not certain and is rendered literally.
