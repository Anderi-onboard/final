# 神煞月令表·吉神(天医、地解)：天医有此爻者疾病易愈；地解病者有此发动即安

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神煞月令表的吉神两行：天医（正月起卯、亥、丑、未、巳、卯、亥、丑、未、巳、卯、亥），卦里有这一爻的，疾病容易痊愈；地解（申、申、酉、酉、戌、戌、亥、亥、午、午、未、未），病人有这一爻发动，就安稳了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Two lucky rows of the table of spirits and killings by month: the Heavenly Physician (from the first month Mao, Hai, Chou, Wei, Si, Mao, Hai, Chou, Wei, Si, Mao, Hai): if this line is in the hexagram, an illness is easily cured; the Earth Resolution (Shen, Shen, You, You, Xu, Xu, Hai, Hai, Wu, Wu, Wei, Wei): for a sick person, if this moves, he is at once at ease.

> [!note] 按
> 这是《断易天机》「神煞月令表」（表头：神煞、月令一到十二、说明）里和病有关的两行吉神。每一行：第一格是名字，接着十二格是它在正月到十二月各落的地支，最后一格是断语。原表分吉神（天德、月德……）和凶神（岁杀、三丘……）两半，病的几行分散在其中。天医这一行的排法和前面「十六、天医」那首歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.30）一样；地解这一行的排法和「十四、月解」的排法一样（正二申、三四酉、五六戌……），但断语不同：月解说「病人有此解神，天医上卦即病愈」，地解说「病者有此发动即安」。表里别的解神（天解「卦遇此爻恶事解散」、外解「遇此爻灾患消除」、天喜「官事消散」）没有专说病，不收。
>
> *Note:* These are the two lucky rows about illness in the 'table of spirits and killings by month' of the Duanyi Tianji (the head of the table: spirit or killing, month one to twelve, explanation). Each row has as its first cell the name, then twelve cells for the branch it falls on from the first to the twelfth month, and last the judgment. The original table is in two halves, lucky spirits (Heavenly Virtue, Month Virtue ...) and fierce spirits (Year Killing, Three Mounds ...), and the rows about illness are scattered through it. The layout of the Heavenly Physician row is the same as the song '16 Heavenly Physician' before it (LY.ILL.DYTJ.30); the layout of the Earth Resolution row is the same as that of '14 Month Resolution' (first and second Shen, third and fourth You, fifth and sixth Xu ...), but the judgment differs: the Month Resolution says 'a sick person who has this resolving spirit, with the Heavenly Physician in the hexagram, is cured at once', and the Earth Resolution says 'a sick person who has this moving is at once at ease'. The other resolving spirits in the table (Heavenly Resolution 'a hexagram meeting this line, ill matters are resolved and scatter', Outer Resolution 'meeting this line calamity is removed', Heavenly Joy 'official business dissolves') do not speak specially of illness and are not taken.
