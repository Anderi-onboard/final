# 占出痘：卦无官痘无芽、鬼空鬼绝无斑疹、官动官兴有痘；鬼动而休囚、冲散、绝乡、化克绝皆非出，官发动不临死绝刑伤便出；年上临官年出痘、月值鬼月栽花；鬼休子旺稀朗、鬼旺福衰稠密；螣蛇临火发必麻痘

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以官鬼为主，五行六象为凭。还没种花的时候来问种花，卦里没有官鬼的，痘就没有芽；鬼空、鬼绝，没有斑疹；官动、官兴，有痘瘀。占出痘，要看官爻：鬼如果空亡、或不上卦，一定不出；卦里纵有鬼动，也遇上休囚、或遇冲散、或落绝乡、或化出克绝，都不是出痘；官鬼发动而不临死绝刑伤，鬼即使休囚，如果变为有气，就种痘花；鬼虽不动，遇上旺相的时期，也应当起发；卦里虽无鬼，若本月临官，反倒生出痘痧。年上临官，这年出痘；月中值鬼，这个月栽花；鬼休囚、子孙旺，痘应稀朗；鬼旺、福（子孙）衰，痘更稠密；若见螣蛇临火发动，一定是麻疹和痘，断无差错。问哪一年种痘，就看鬼临生旺的年，卦里没有官，再看哪一年值鬼；占哪一个月栽花，就推鬼逢生旺的月；要知道哪一天，也看鬼旺的日子，大同小异，一个道理推；卦里官鬼休囚、子孙旺相，或子孙在世爻上，纵然出痘，也稀疏；官鬼旺相、子孙休囚，痘出来时稠密非常；还要论螣蛇临火发动，鬼虽安静，也必发痘疹。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line is the main line, and the five phases and six images are the ground. When asked, before the flowers are sown, about sowing the flowers, if the hexagram has no Pressure line the pox has no sprout; with the ghost Void or in Extinction there are no spots or rash; with Pressure moving and thriving there is pox with dark stasis. In divining the pox coming out, look at the Pressure line: if the ghost is Void or absent from the hexagram, it will surely not come out; even if the hexagram has a moving ghost, if it meets resting or trapped, or clash and scattering, or falls in Extinction, or changes into control or Extinction, none of it is the pox coming out; if the Pressure line moves and is not on death, Extinction, punishment or wounding, then even if the ghost is resting or trapped, if it changes into strength the flowers are sown; though the ghost does not move, meeting the period when it is strong, the pox should still rise; and though the hexagram has no ghost, if the month itself sits on Pressure, pox and rash are born instead. Pressure on the year: the pox comes out that year; the ghost on duty in the month: the flowers are planted that month; ghost resting and Output strong, the pox is sparse and clear; ghost strong and Output weak, denser still; if the Coiling Snake on Fire moves, it is sure to be measles and pox, and the judgment will not be wrong. To ask in what year to sow the pox, look at the year in which the ghost is on strength or generation, and where the hexagram has no Pressure line, look for the year on which a ghost sits; to divine in what month to plant the flowers, work out the month in which the ghost meets strength; to know the day, look also at the days when the ghost is strong, the same reasoning with small differences; if in the hexagram the Pressure line is resting or trapped and Output strong, or Output is on the Self, then even if the pox comes out it is sparse; if the Pressure line is strong and Output resting or trapped, the pox comes out extremely dense; also, if the Coiling Snake on Fire moves, though the ghost is still, pox and rash will surely come out.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段没有章题（整理本这一处缺章名），从小标「以官鬼为主，五行六象为凭」看，是讲种痘、出痘的。「种痘」「栽花」「种豆」都是指出痘、种痘这件事（「豆」是「痘」的借字）。「痘瘀」的「瘀」原意是血瘀，这里按字面译作「痘瘀」。年、月、日都用「鬼临生旺」来定：官鬼旺相的年月日就是出痘的时候。稀朗、稠密的判据和易冒（LY.ILL.YM.05）、增删卜易（LY.ILL.ZS.20）、易隐（LY.ILL.YY.35）、卜筮正宗（LY.ILL.BSZZ.06）一致：鬼休子旺则稀，鬼旺子衰则稠。
>
> *Note:* This passage has no chapter title (the edition lacks the chapter name here); from the subtitle 'the Pressure line is the main line, the five phases and six images the ground' it is about sowing and bringing out the pox. 'Sowing the pox', 'planting flowers' and 'sowing beans' all mean the pox and its inoculation ('dou', bean, is borrowed for 'dou', pox). The 'stasis' in 'pox with dark stasis' originally means blood stasis and is translated literally. The year, month and day are all fixed by 'the ghost on strength or generation': the year, month or day when the Pressure line is strong is when the pox comes out. The criteria for sparse and dense agree with the Yi Mao (LY.ILL.YM.05), the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.20), the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.35) and the Bushi Zhengzong (LY.ILL.BSZZ.06): ghost resting and Output strong is sparse, ghost strong and Output weak is dense.
