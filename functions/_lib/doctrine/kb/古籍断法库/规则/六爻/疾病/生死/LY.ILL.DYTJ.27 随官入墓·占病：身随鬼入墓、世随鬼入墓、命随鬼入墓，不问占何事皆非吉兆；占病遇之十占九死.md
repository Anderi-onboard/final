# 随官入墓·占病：身随鬼入墓、世随鬼入墓、命随鬼入墓，不问占何事皆非吉兆；占病遇之十占九死

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 随官入墓有三种：身随鬼入墓、世随鬼入墓、命随鬼入墓。以上三种墓，不问占什么事，都不是吉兆。占病遇到它，十占里有九个死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Following the ghost into a tomb has three kinds: the Body following the ghost into a tomb, the Self following the ghost into a tomb, and the natal line following the ghost into a tomb. These three tombs, whatever is divined, are none of them a lucky sign. Divining illness and meeting one, nine out of ten die.

> [!note] 按
> 「随官入墓」的三种（身、世、命随鬼入墓）在这本书里各有一个卦例（丑日占得雷水解卦、未日占得山天大畜、未日寅日生人占得地雷复……），例不收。这一条只收总句和占病的一句：「占病遇之，十占九死」。天玄赋的「入墓卦」（LY.ILL.TXF.06）说的是「世身随鬼入墓决不瘥」，这里多了「命随鬼入墓」（病人的本命爻随鬼入墓）。「命」是病人的本命爻，这一点和孙膑断疾病歌里「先从卦中寻本命」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.02）是同一个「命」。其余各事（占身、占婚、占产、求官、占讼、求财、出行、行人、家宅、移居、风水）的一句不在病的范围，不收。
>
> *Note:* Each of the three kinds of 'following the ghost into a tomb' (the Body, the Self and the natal line following the ghost into a tomb) has an example hexagram in this book (a Chou day divining Thunder over Water, Jie; a Wei day divining Mountain over Heaven, Daxu; a Wei day and a person born on a Yin day divining Earth over Thunder, Fu ...), and the examples are not taken. This entry takes only the general statement and the sentence on illness: 'divining illness and meeting it, nine out of ten die'. The Tianxuan Fu's 'hexagrams that enter tombs' (LY.ILL.TXF.06) says 'the Self and the Body following the ghost into a tomb surely does not recover', and here there is in addition 'the natal line following the ghost into a tomb' (the sick person's natal line following the ghost into a tomb). The 'natal line' is the sick person's natal line, the same 'natal line' as in 'first look in the hexagram for the natal line' in Sun Bin's song (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02). The sentences for the other matters (the person, marriage, childbirth, seeking office, lawsuit, wealth, travel, a traveller, the house, moving house, geomancy) are outside illness and are not taken.
