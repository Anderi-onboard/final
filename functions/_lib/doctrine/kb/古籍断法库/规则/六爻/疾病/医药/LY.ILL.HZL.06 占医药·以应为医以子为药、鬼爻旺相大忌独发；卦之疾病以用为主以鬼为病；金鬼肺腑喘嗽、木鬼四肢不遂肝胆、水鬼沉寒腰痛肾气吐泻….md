# 占医药·以应为医以子为药、鬼爻旺相大忌独发；卦之疾病以用为主以鬼为病；金鬼肺腑喘嗽、木鬼四肢不遂肝胆、水鬼沉寒腰痛肾气吐泻、火鬼头痛发热加朱雀狂言、土鬼脾胃发胀黄肿瘟疫

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以应为医生，以子孙为药；鬼爻旺相，最忌独发。卦里的疾病，以用爻为主，以鬼为病。金鬼：肺腑病、喘嗽、气急、虚怯、瘦瘠，或疮疥、血光，或筋骨病；木鬼：四肢不遂，肝胆主病，右痪左瘫，口眼歪斜；水鬼：沉塞、痼冷、腰痛、肾气淋沥、遗精、白浊、吐泻；火鬼：头痛发热、心胸焦渴，加朱雀则狂言谵语，阳症、伤寒、嗝逆；土鬼：脾胃发胀，黄肿虚浮，瘟疫时气。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Take the Other as the doctor and Output as the medicine; the Pressure line strong is most feared moving alone. The illness of the hexagram takes the Subject line as the main and the ghost as the illness. A Metal ghost: illness of the lungs and viscera, panting and coughing, shortness of breath, deficiency and timidity, emaciation, or sores and scabies, bleeding, or an illness of the sinews and bones; a Wood ghost: the four limbs do not obey, the liver and gallbladder govern the illness, paralysis right and left, mouth and eyes awry; a Water ghost: stoppage, obstinate cold, pain in the waist, dribbling of the kidney qi, seminal emission, turbid urine, vomiting and diarrhoea; a Fire ghost: headache and fever, parched heart and chest, with the Vermilion Bird raving and delirium, a yang pattern, cold damage, hiccups; an Earth ghost: distension of the spleen and stomach, yellow swelling and puffiness, epidemics and seasonal qi.

> [!note] 按
> 「以应为医，以子为药」的分工和天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.12）、阐奥歌章医药章（LY.ILL.CA.09：应爻及外为医者、内卦身爻是病人）是同一套，这里说医是应、药是子孙。五行鬼的症候是天玄赋疾病章那一段（LY.ILL.TXF.03）的另一份，字句略有出入：天玄赋是「金鬼：肺腑病，咳嗽气，或疮疖、血光，或筋骨疼痛」，这里是「金鬼，肺腑疾、喘嗽、气急、虚怯、瘦瘠、或疮疥、血光，或筋骨病」，多了气急、虚怯、瘦瘠几项；水鬼这里有「沉塞、痼冷、腰痛、肾气淋沥、遗精、白浊、吐泻」，天玄赋是「沉寒痼冷，遗精白浊，小便淋沥，吐泻呕逆」。「嗝逆」按「打嗝、气逆」译。「阳症」是中医的阳证。这是中医术语，按字面译。
>
> *Note:* The division of 'the Other as the doctor, Output as the medicine' is the same set as the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.12) and the Chan'ao Gezhang's chapter on medicine (LY.ILL.CA.09: the Other line and the outer trigram are the doctor, the inner trigram and the Body line are the sick person), except that here the doctor is the Other and the medicine is Output. The symptoms of the ghost of each phase are another copy of that passage of the Tianxuan Fu's chapter on illness (LY.ILL.TXF.03), with small differences in wording: the Tianxuan Fu has 'a Metal ghost: illness of the lungs and viscera, coughing and qi, or sores and boils, bleeding, or aching of the sinews and bones', and this has 'a Metal ghost, illness of the lungs and viscera, panting and coughing, shortness of breath, deficiency and timidity, emaciation, or sores and scabies, bleeding, or an illness of the sinews and bones', with shortness of breath, deficiency and timidity and emaciation added; for the Water ghost this has 'stoppage, obstinate cold, pain in the waist, dribbling of the kidney qi, seminal emission, turbid urine, vomiting and diarrhoea', where the Tianxuan Fu has 'deep cold and obstinate chill, seminal emission and turbid urine, dribbling urine, vomiting, diarrhoea and retching'. 'Ge ni' is rendered 'hiccups'. A 'yang pattern' is the traditional medical yang pattern. These are terms of Chinese medicine and are rendered literally.
