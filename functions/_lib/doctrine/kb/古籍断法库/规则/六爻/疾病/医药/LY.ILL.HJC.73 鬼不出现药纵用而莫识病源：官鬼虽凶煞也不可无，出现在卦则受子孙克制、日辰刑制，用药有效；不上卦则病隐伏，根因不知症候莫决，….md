# 鬼不出现药纵用而莫识病源：官鬼虽凶煞也不可无，出现在卦则受子孙克制、日辰刑制，用药有效；不上卦则病隐伏，根因不知症候莫决，率意用药难取效

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼虽然是凶煞，也不能没有。它出现在卦里，就有子孙克制它、日辰刑制它，用药有效。它不上卦，病就隐伏着，病根不知道，症候没法判定，随意用药，也难以见效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line is a malign killer, yet it cannot be missing. If it appears in the hexagram, Output controls it and the Day punishes it, and medicine is effective. If it is not in the hexagram, the illness lies hidden, its root cause is unknown, its symptoms cannot be decided, and medicine used at random also hardly takes effect.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和病体章的 LY.ILL.HJC.49（鬼伏空亡）是一个道理：官鬼是病的根由，卦里没有它就没有地方下手。那里讲的是病能不能好，这里讲的是药能不能对症。
>
> *Note:* This entry gives the same reason as LY.ILL.HJC.49 (Pressure hidden in Void) in the chapter on the patient's condition: the Pressure line is the root of the illness, and without it in the hexagram there is nowhere to begin. That entry speaks of whether the patient can recover, this one of whether the medicine can fit the illness.
