# 总断千金赋·卦遇凶星避之则吉（旧注：凶星是刑冲克害，避之是落空；书中例：六月壬申日占子病得姤之大过，父母旺动用爻无气，喜用爻在空避之，至丙子日愈；王洪绪：凶星即忌神，用爻被月建日辰伤克则无处可避）

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「卦里遇到凶星，避开它就吉。」旧注：凶星，就是刑、冲、克、害；避开它，就是六甲空亡；用爻无缘无故自己落空是真空，万事不成；如果被日辰、动爻刑冲克害到用爻而用爻在空，这是避凶而空，就是《金锁玄关》说的「克空为用」，不会坏，只是眼下略有阻碍，过了旬就成。书中例：六月壬申日占儿子的病，得姤变大过，父母旺动、用爻无气，本是凶兆，喜得用爻落在空亡避开了它，果然到丙子日病好了。王洪绪：凶星就是忌神；用爻被月建日辰伤克，不论空伏，无处可避；如果没有月、日伤克、只是遇到忌神发动来伤，用爻值旬空伏藏而不受克，叫做避，等到冲克忌神的日子，凶就自己散了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'A hexagram that meets an ominous star: avoiding it is auspicious.' The old commentary: an ominous star is punishment, clash, control or harm; avoiding it is the Void of the six jia; a Subject line that falls Void by itself without cause is true Void and nothing succeeds; if the Subject line is punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Day or a moving line and is in Void, this is Void to avoid ill, which is what the Jinsuo Xuanguan calls 'Void under control is used', it will not spoil, only for the moment there is some obstruction, and once the cycle has passed it succeeds. A case: on a Renshen day in the sixth month a son's illness was divined and Gou changing to Daguo obtained; the Resource line was strong and moving and the Subject line had no qi, which would be an ominous omen, but fortunately the Subject line was in Void and so avoided it, and indeed on the Bingzi day the illness was cured. Wang Hongxu: an ominous star is the Adversary; when the Subject line is hurt or controlled by the Month or the Day, whether Void or hidden, it cannot be avoided; if there is no hurt from the Month or Day and it is only an Adversary that moves to harm it, then if the Subject line is in the ten-day Void or hidden and does not suffer the control, this is called avoiding, and when the day comes that clashes and controls the Adversary, the ill disperses of itself.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1225 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凶星即是忌神。 | 凶星就是忌神。 | An ominous star is the Adversary. |
> | 凡用爻被月建日辰伤克，不论空伏，始终受制，无处可避。 | 凡是用爻被月建、日辰伤克，不论空还是伏，始终受制，无处可避。 | Whenever the Subject line is hurt or controlled by the Month or the Day, whether it is Void or hidden it is always held, and there is nowhere to avoid it. |
> | 如无月日伤克，独遇卦爻中忌神发动来伤，若用爻值旬空伏藏不受其克，谓之避，待冲克忌神之日，其凶自散也。 | 如果没有月、日伤克，只是遇到卦爻中的忌神发动来伤，如果用爻值旬空、伏藏而不受它的克，叫做避，等到冲克忌神的日子，它的凶就自己散了。 | If there is no hurt or control from the Month or Day and it is only that an Adversary in the hexagram moves to harm it, then if the Subject line is in the ten-day Void or hidden and does not suffer its control, this is called avoiding, and when the day comes that clashes and controls the Adversary, its ill disperses of itself. |
> | 如用爻出现不空便受其毒，难免其伤也，故曰：“避之则吉”。 | 如果用爻出现、不空，就受到它的毒害，难免被伤，所以说「避之则吉」。 | If the Subject line appears and is not Void, it suffers its poison and cannot escape harm, and so it is said 'avoiding it is auspicious'. |

^q4

> [!warning] 打架
> 「凶星」是什么、怎么才算「避」，旧注和王洪绪的说法不同。旧注：凶星是刑冲克害，避就是落空亡；用爻被日辰、动爻刑冲克害而在空，就是避凶（「克空为用」）。王洪绪：凶星就是忌神；用爻被月建日辰伤克的，不论空伏，始终受制，无处可避；只有没有月日伤克、单被卦中忌神发动来伤的，用爻值旬空或伏藏，才叫避。旧注认为被日辰刑冲克害的用爻落空也是避，王洪绪认为被月建日辰克伤的就避不了。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The old commentary and Wang Hongxu differ on what the 'ominous star' is and what counts as 'avoiding'. The old commentary: the ominous star is punishment, clash, control and harm, and avoiding is falling into Void; a Subject line punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Day or a moving line and in Void is avoiding ill ('Void under control is used'). Wang Hongxu: the ominous star is the Adversary; a Subject line hurt or controlled by the Month or the Day, whether Void or hidden, is always held and cannot avoid it; only when there is no hurt from the Month and Day and it is solely an Adversary in the hexagram moving to harm it, and the Subject line is in the ten-day Void or hidden, is it called avoiding. The old commentary takes a Subject line punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Day to avoid it by falling Void, while Wang Hongxu holds that one hurt or controlled by the Month and Day cannot avoid it. The books do not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 书中例是旧注举的占病例子：壬申日在甲午旬（甲午、乙未、丙申、丁酉、戊戌、己亥、庚子、辛丑、壬寅……），原文没有写这个旬；「用爻无气」又落在空亡里避开了父母的克，所以「至丙子日愈」，旧注解释说丙子日时前面的旬已过、又遇用爻帝旺（帝旺是十二长生里最旺的一位）。「六甲」是六个甲（甲子、甲戌……）管的六个旬，「六甲空亡」就是旬空。「金锁玄关」是旧注引的另一部书。「先天」指易的本来道理。这条的主旨是「空」可以是避凶，所以和 LY.ILL.HJC.151（无故勿空）互相对照：同一部总断千金赋里，自己落空的用爻旧注断凶，被克伤而落空的又断为避。
>
> *Note:* The case is an illness example the old commentary gives: the Renshen day is in a ten-day cycle that begins at Jiawu (Jiawu, Yiwei, Bingshen, Dingyou, Wuxu, Jihai, Gengzi, Xinchou, Renyin ...), which the text does not state; the Subject line 'has no qi' and falls Void and so avoids the control of the Resource line, hence 'on the Bingzi day it was cured', and the old commentary explains that by the Bingzi day the earlier cycle had passed and the Subject line met its Peak (Peak is the strongest of the twelve stages of life). 'The six jia' are the six ten-day cycles governed by the six jia (Jiazi, Jiaxu and so on), and 'the Void of the six jia' is the ten-day Void. The 'Jinsuo Xuanguan' is another book that the old commentary cites. 'The Former Heaven' means the original principle of the Changes. The point of the entry is that Void can be avoiding ill, and so it is set against LY.ILL.HJC.151 (wu gu wu kong): in the same rhapsody the old commentary judges a Subject line that falls Void by itself ominous, and one hurt and then falling Void to be avoiding.
