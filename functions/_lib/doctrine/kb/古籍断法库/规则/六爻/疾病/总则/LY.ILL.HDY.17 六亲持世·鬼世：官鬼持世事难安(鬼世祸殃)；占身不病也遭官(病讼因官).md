# 六亲持世·鬼世：官鬼持世事难安(鬼世祸殃)；占身不病也遭官(病讼因官)

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 海底眼的六亲持世一节，鬼世一首：官鬼持世事难安（眉批：鬼持世是祸殃）；占身不病也要遭官司（眉批：病、官司都因官鬼）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the section on the six relations holding the Self in the Haidi Yan, the verse for the ghost holding the Self: with Pressure holding the Self, affairs are hard to settle (comment: the ghost holding the Self is misfortune); in divining oneself, even if not ill one meets official trouble (comment: illness and lawsuits both come from Pressure).

> [!note] 按
> 这一首的经文和卜筮全书诸爻持世诀里的「鬼爻持世事难安，占身不病也遭官」（LY.ILL.BSQS.04）是同一联，文王金钱课的诸爻持事诀（第 9 行）也有；这里的字句是「官鬼持世」（卜筮全书写「鬼爻持世」），眉批「病讼因官」是说：病和官司都是官鬼带来的。「占身」是占自己；「不病也遭官」的「官」是官司。这一首后面两句「财物时时忧失脱，骨肉分离会合难」讲的是财物和亲人，不收。
>
> *Note:* The verse text of this one is the same couplet as 'with the ghost line holding the Self, affairs are hard to settle; in divining oneself, even if not ill one meets official trouble' (LY.ILL.BSQS.04) in the verses of the lines holding the Self of the Bushi Quanshu, and it is also in the verses on lines holding the affair of the Wenwang Jinqian Ke (line 9); the wording here is 'Pressure holding the Self' (the Bushi Quanshu writes 'the ghost line holding the Self'), and the comment 'illness and lawsuits both come from Pressure' says that illness and lawsuits are both brought by Pressure. 'Divining oneself' is divining for oneself; the 'official' of 'meets official trouble' is a lawsuit. The two lines after it, 'one is always worried about losing goods; kin separate and meeting again is hard', are about goods and relatives and are not taken.
