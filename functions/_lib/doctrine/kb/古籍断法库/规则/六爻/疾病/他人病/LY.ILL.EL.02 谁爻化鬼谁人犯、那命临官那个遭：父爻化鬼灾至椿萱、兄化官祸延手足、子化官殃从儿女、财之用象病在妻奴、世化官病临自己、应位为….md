# 谁爻化鬼谁人犯、那命临官那个遭：父爻化鬼灾至椿萱、兄化官祸延手足、子化官殃从儿女、财之用象病在妻奴、世化官病临自己、应位为鬼妻妾不宁；本命临官必为灾；问病有无，忌神不动、鬼不交重、用神不空破便言平安

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 哪个爻变出鬼，是哪个人犯的；哪个人的本命落在官鬼上，是哪个人遭殃；再看六亲里谁受克，轻重就能判断得分毫不差。再说全家有没有人生病，要去找用象：父爻变出鬼，灾落到父母身上；兄弟变出官，祸延及兄弟姐妹；子孙变出官爻，灾殃从儿女来；财爻的用象，病在妻子和仆人；世爻变出官爻，病落在自己身上；应位是鬼，妻妾不安宁。再查每个人的本命，落在官鬼上的，一定成灾，比如占得某卦，午火临官，就说属马的人受患；鬼如果空亡，不这样断，要再看六亲的象，不能受伤。财爻动，祸事忧及长辈；鬼兴旺，灾殃波及兄弟；兄弟摇动，妻子生病；父爻动，儿女有灾；如果妻子来占，子孙动，丈夫受患；其余占问，子孙兴旺都是平安。问有没有病，卦里忌神不动、鬼不发动、用神不遇旬空月破，就说平安。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whichever line changes into a ghost, that person has offended; whichever natal branch sits on the Pressure line, that person suffers; moreover see which of the six relations is controlled, and severity can be judged to the finest degree. Again, to judge whether anyone in the whole household falls ill, one must find the Subject image: if the Resource line changes into a ghost, calamity reaches the father and mother; if Peer changes into Pressure, misfortune spreads to brothers and sisters; if Output changes into a Pressure line, misfortune comes through the children; for the Wealth image, the illness lies with the wife and servants; if the Self changes into a Pressure line, the illness falls on oneself; if the Other's place is the ghost, wife and concubines are unsettled. Then check each person's natal branch: whoever's falls on the Pressure line will surely suffer calamity, so that if a certain hexagram is obtained and Wu Fire sits on the Pressure line, one says the person born in the year of the Horse suffers; if the ghost is Void, do not judge this way, but look further at the image of the six relations, which must not be harmed. Wealth moving, misfortune worries the elders; the ghost thriving, calamity reaches the brothers; the Peer stirring, the wife falls ill; the Resource moving, the children have calamity; if the wife divines and Output moves, the husband suffers; in all other questions Output thriving means peace. To ask whether there is illness: if the Adversary is still, the ghost does not move, and the Subject does not meet Void or Month-break, say all is well.

> [!note] 按
> 「椿萱」是父母（椿指父，萱指母）。「宫化宫爻，令门受患」的「宫」疑是「官」之误（官变官爻，牵连别人家的门户），原文写的是「宫」，这里照字面译，读法没有把握。「本命临官」是按每个人生年的地支，看落在哪一爻的官鬼上——午火官鬼对属马的人，说的是这个意思。这一条和易冒「爻变之鬼亦然」（LY.ILL.YM.33）、「问痘见何人」（LY.ILL.YM.06）、黄金策的「鬼动化出六亲即以化出者断」（LY.ILL.HJC.108）是同一路读法。
>
> *Note:* 'Chun-xuan' (the Chun tree and the day-lily) is father and mother (the Chun tree for the father, the day-lily for the mother). In 'palace changing into a palace line, the honoured household suffers' the 'palace' looks like an error for 'official' (Pressure changing into a Pressure line, involving the household of another), but the original writes 'palace', and it is translated literally, the reading being uncertain. 'The natal branch sitting on the Pressure line' goes by the earthly branch of each person's birth year and sees which Pressure line it falls on: a Wu Fire Pressure line for someone born in the year of the Horse is what is meant. This entry reads in the same way as the Yi Mao's 'the ghost that appears by change is also the same' (LY.ILL.YM.33), 'who will have the pox' (LY.ILL.YM.06) and the Huangjin Ce's 'a ghost moving into a relation is judged by what it turns into' (LY.ILL.HJC.108).
