# 病起何方：以动爻断——火南水北木东金西，不论鬼之有无；有鬼不动取外卦；卦安静无鬼看鬼伏何爻之下、以飞神定（伏子北、丑寅东北、卯东、辰巳东南、午南、未申西南、酉西、戌亥西北）；乱动而鬼动以鬼所居之卦断；官鬼独发以鬼爻所值之支断；鬼在本宫内卦是本地得病、他宫外卦是他方得病

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病是从哪个方向来的，以卦里的动爻来断：火是南，水是北，木是东，金是西，不管有没有鬼。卦里有鬼但不动的，就取外卦来断（乾西北、坤西南、巽东南、艮东北、离南、坎北、震东、兑西）。卦是安静的又没有鬼的，就看鬼伏在哪一爻下面，拿鬼上面的飞神来定灾祸的地方：伏在子下是北方，丑、寅下是东北，卯下是东，辰、巳下是东南，午下是南，未、申下是西南，酉下是西，戌、亥下是西北。卦乱动而鬼也动的，也拿鬼所在的卦来断；官鬼独自发动的，又拿鬼爻所值的地支来断。另外，鬼在本宫的内卦，就说是在本地得病；在别的宫、外卦，就说是在别处得病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where the illness came from is judged by the moving lines in the hexagram: Fire is south, Water north, Wood east, Metal west, whether or not there is a ghost. If the hexagram has a ghost that does not move, judge by the outer trigram (Qian northwest, Kun southwest, Xun southeast, Gen northeast, Li south, Kan north, Zhen east, Dui west). If the hexagram is still and has no ghost, see under which line the ghost is hidden and use the flying line above the ghost to fix the place of the affliction: hidden beneath Zi, the north; beneath Chou or Yin, the northeast; beneath Mao, the east; beneath Chen or Si, the southeast; beneath Wu, the south; beneath Wei or Shen, the southwest; beneath You, the west; beneath Xu or Hai, the northwest. If the hexagram has lines moving in disorder and the ghost moves, judge by the trigram in which the ghost stands; if the Pressure line moves alone, judge by the branch that the Pressure line holds. Also a ghost in the inner trigram of the home palace: the illness was caught at home; in the outer trigram of another palace: caught elsewhere.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的方位依据是五行方位（火南水北木东金西）和后天八卦方位（乾西北……兑西），「飞神」是鬼所伏那一爻上面的那一爻的地支。易林补遗的「何处得病章」（第 1240 行起）讲同一件事，有更细的分法，见易林补遗一批。
>
> *Note:* The directions rest on the element directions (Fire south, Water north, Wood east, Metal west) and the later-heaven trigram directions (Qian northwest ... Dui west), and the 'flying line' is the branch of the line above the one under which the ghost is hidden. The Yilin Buyi's chapter on where the illness was caught (from line 1240) treats the same matter with a finer division, and is taken with the Yilin Buyi batch.
