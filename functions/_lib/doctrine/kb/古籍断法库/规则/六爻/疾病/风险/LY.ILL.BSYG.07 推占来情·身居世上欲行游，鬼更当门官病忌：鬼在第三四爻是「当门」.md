# 推占来情·身居世上欲行游，鬼更当门官病忌：鬼在第三四爻是「当门」

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推占来情的歌里有一联：卦身在世爻上、想出门远行的时候，鬼又正好当门，官司、疾病都要忌。自注说，「当门」指鬼在第三、四爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the verses on reading the purpose of the asker there is a couplet: when the Body is on the Self line and one wants to go travelling, and the ghost stands at the door as well, official trouble and illness are to be feared. Its own note says that 'at the door' means the ghost is on the third or fourth line.

## 《大易断例卜筮元龟目》括号里的自注(没标「虎易注」的)

> [!quote]+ [[大易断例卜筮元龟目]] 第 463 行 · The Dayi Duanli's own bracketed notes (those not marked as the modern editor's)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼在第三四爻是也。 | 鬼在第三、四爻就是。 | The ghost is on the third or fourth line. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「官病忌」的「官」和「病」分开读：官司（或官事）和疾病，都是要忌的。这一联在断易天机里也有（第 502 行），那里的括号是今人的注。自注后面还讲了怎么找「身」（子午世身在初、丑未在二、寅申在三、卯酉在四、辰戌在五、巳亥在六）和另一种只取驿马的断法，那是找卦身的办法，不管病，这里不收。
>
> *Note:* The 'official' and 'illness' of 'official trouble and illness are to be feared' are read separately: lawsuits (or official affairs) and illness are both to be feared. This couplet is also in the Duanyi Tianji (line 502), where the brackets are the modern commentator's notes. After this the note goes on to explain how to find the 'Body' (Zi and Wu: Body on the first line, Chou and Wei the second, Yin and Shen the third, Mao and You the fourth, Chen and Xu the fifth, Si and Hai the sixth) and another way of judging by the Post Horse alone; those are ways of finding the Body, have nothing to do with illness, and are not taken here.
