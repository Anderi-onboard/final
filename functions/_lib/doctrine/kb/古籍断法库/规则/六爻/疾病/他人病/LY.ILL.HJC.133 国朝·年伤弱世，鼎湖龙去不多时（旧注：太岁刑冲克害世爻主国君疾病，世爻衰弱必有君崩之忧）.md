# 国朝·年伤弱世，鼎湖龙去不多时（旧注：太岁刑冲克害世爻主国君疾病，世爻衰弱必有君崩之忧）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》国朝章（占国家的事）说：「年克伤衰弱的世爻，国君驾崩就不会多久了。」旧注：太岁刑、冲、克、害世爻，主国君生病，或者内乱将要发作；如果克世爻、而世爻又衰弱，一定有国君驾崩的忧虑。王洪绪：以下是国君自己占卜的情形，太岁刑冲克害世爻，主疾病或内乱将作。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on the state (divining matters of the state) says: 'The Year harming a weak Self line: the dragon leaves Dinghu before long.' The old commentary: Tai Sui punishing, clashing, controlling or harming the Self line means the ruler falls ill, or domestic trouble is about to break out; if it controls the Self line and the Self line is weak, there is sure to be fear for the ruler's death. Wang Hongxu: what follows is the case of the ruler divining for himself; Tai Sui punishing, clashing, controlling or harming the Self line means illness or domestic trouble about to break out.

> [!note] 按
> 「鼎湖龙去」用黄帝在鼎湖乘龙升天的典故，指帝王去世（旧注后面引《史记》说明，这里不收）。国朝章里世爻是国君，所以世爻受太岁克害，是国君生病、将崩。王洪绪点明这一联是「国君自卜」的情形。这条是占他人（国君）的病，放在「他人病」里。
>
> *Note:* 'The dragon leaves Dinghu' alludes to the Yellow Emperor riding a dragon up to heaven at Dinghu, and means a ruler's death (the old commentary goes on to cite the Shiji to explain it, which is not taken here). In the chapter on the state the Self line is the ruler, so the Self line harmed by Tai Sui means the ruler falls ill and is about to die. Wang Hongxu makes clear that this couplet is the case of 'the ruler divining for himself'. This is a divination about another's (the ruler's) illness, and is placed under the illness of others.
