# 按爻位分身体：初爻足、二爻股膝、三爻腹小腹腰臀肛门小便、四爻胸胃乳、五爻面项手（水口火目土鼻水左耳金右耳）、六爻头脑；遇鬼伏、鬼化、鬼动来伤之处必带疾

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 再按爻位来分：初爻是脚，二爻是大腿和膝，三爻是腹、小腹、腰、臀、肛门、小便，四爻是胸、胃、乳，五爻是脸、颈项、手（水是口，火是眼睛，土是鼻，水是左耳，金是右耳），六爻是头和脑。凡是哪一爻遇到鬼伏在下面、变出鬼、或者鬼发动来伤到它，那个部位一定留下毛病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Again by the position of the lines: the first line is the feet, the second the thighs and knees, the third the belly, lower belly, waist, buttocks, anus and urination, the fourth the chest, stomach and breasts, the fifth the face, neck and hands (Water the mouth, Fire the eyes, Earth the nose, Water the left ear, Metal the right ear), and the sixth the head and brain. Wherever a line has a ghost hidden beneath it, or changes into a ghost, or a ghost moves to harm it, that place will surely be left with an ailment.

> [!warning] 打架
> 左耳属哪一行：同一段里，上一条（LY.ILL.YY.11）说木是筋骨、左耳，这里说「水左耳，金右耳」，两处对左耳的五行不一致；书里没有说明，应是传抄的差异。易隐别处（第 1447 行讲相貌处）是「木聋左耳，金虎右耳」，与这里说水的一处不同。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which element the left ear belongs to: within the same passage, the previous entry (LY.ILL.YY.11) says Wood is the sinews and bones and the left ear, and here it says 'Water the left ear, Metal the right ear', so the two disagree on the left ear's element; the book gives no explanation and it is likely a difference in transmission. Elsewhere the Yi Yin (line 1447, on appearance) has 'Wood deaf in the left ear, Metal and Tiger the right ear', which differs from this one place that says Water.

> [!note] 按
> 这里的分法是从初爻（脚）一路往上到六爻（头脑），和上面鬼谷分爻（LY.ILL.YY.02）的左栏一致。「水为口，火为目，土为鼻，水左耳，金右耳」是接在五爻（脸）后面的，讲脸上的五官各归哪一行。
>
> *Note:* The division here runs from the first line (feet) up to the sixth (head and brain) and agrees with the left column of Guigu's division of the lines (LY.ILL.YY.02). 'Water the mouth, Fire the eyes, Earth the nose, Water the left ear, Metal the right ear' follows the fifth line (the face) and assigns the features of the face to the elements.
