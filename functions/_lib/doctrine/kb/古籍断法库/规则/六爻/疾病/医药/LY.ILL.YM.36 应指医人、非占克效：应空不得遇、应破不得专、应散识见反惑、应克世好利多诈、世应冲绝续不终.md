# 应指医人、非占克效：应空不得遇、应破不得专、应散识见反惑、应克世好利多诈、世应冲绝续不终

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻指的是医生，不是拿来占能不能克住病的：所以应落空，遇不上这个医生；应被冲破，医生不专心；应散，医生的见识反倒糊涂；应克世，医生爱财、多诈；世应相冲，治疗断断续续，做不到底。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other stands for the doctor, and is not for divining whether the illness will be overcome: so with the Other Void, one cannot meet this doctor; broken, the doctor is not devoted; scattered, his insight is confused instead; the Other controlling the Self, the doctor loves profit and is full of deceit; Self and Other clashing, the treatment breaks off and is not carried to the end.

## 《易冒·医药章第五十六》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1252 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 56: Medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 夫应指医人，非占克效，是故应空不得遇，应破不得专，应散则识见反惑，应克世则好利多诈，世应冲则绝续不终也。 | 应爻指的是医生，不是拿来占克住病的效验的，所以应落空，遇不上；应被冲破，医生不专心；应散，识见反倒糊涂；应克世，爱财、多诈；世应相冲，治疗断断续续，做不到底。 | The Other stands for the doctor, and is not for divining the efficacy of overcoming the illness; therefore, with the Other Void, one cannot meet him; broken, he cannot be devoted; scattered, his insight is instead confused; the Other controlling the Self, he loves profit and is full of deceit; Self and Other clashing, the treatment breaks off and does not last to the end. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一条把医生和药分开：药看子孙（LY.ILL.YM.34、YM.35），医生看应爻；问药灵不灵不取应，问医生可不可信不取子孙。黄金策同样以应爻为医人（LY.ILL.HJC.58、HJC.62），增删卜易也以应爻为医人，应爻不宜空破墓绝（LY.ILL.ZS.29）。「绝续不终」的「绝续」指治疗断了又续，始终不能了结。
>
> *Note:* This entry separates the doctor from the medicine: the medicine is read from Output (LY.ILL.YM.34, YM.35) and the doctor from the Other line; to ask whether a medicine works one does not take the Other, and to ask whether the doctor can be trusted one does not take Output. The Huangjin Ce likewise takes the Other as the doctor (LY.ILL.HJC.58, HJC.62), and the Zengshan Buyi also takes the Other as the doctor, who should not be Void, broken, in a tomb or in Extinction (LY.ILL.ZS.29). 'Breaks off and does not last to the end' means the treatment stops and resumes but is never concluded.
