# 总断千金赋·凶化长生炽而未散（旧注：占病福化长生日渐减可，鬼化长生日加沉重，直至墓绝日始杀其势；王洪绪：凶神化入长生祸根日渐增长，必待墓绝日始锄其势）

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「凶爻变成长生，祸势炽盛而没有散。」旧注：主事的爻化入长生都吉，只是成得迟；只有坏事的爻化入长生，祸根才刚萌发，一天天增长，凶恶的势头一定要到盛了才停；比如占病，福神（子孙）化成长生，病势一天天减轻就可以；鬼（官鬼）化成长生，病势一天比一天沉重，一直到墓、绝的日子才杀掉它的势头。王洪绪：用爻化入长生的吉；凶神化入长生，祸根日渐增长，一定等到墓、绝日才锄掉它的势头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'An ominous line changing into Birth blazes and does not disperse.' The old commentary: when the line that governs the matter changes into Birth, all is auspicious, only the completion is late; only when the line of a bad matter changes into Birth does the root of its calamity just begin to sprout and grow day by day, and the ominous force does not stop until it has become full; for example in divining illness, if the Fortune line (Output) changes into Birth, the illness lessening day by day will do; if the ghost (Pressure) changes into Birth, the illness grows heavier day by day, until the Tomb or Extinction day, when its force is killed. Wang Hongxu: a Subject line changing into Birth is auspicious; an ominous spirit changing into Birth, the root of calamity grows day by day, and one must wait for the Tomb or Extinction day before its force is hoed out.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用爻化入长生者吉； | 用爻化入长生的吉； | A Subject line changing into Birth is auspicious; |
> | 如凶神化入长生者，则其祸根始萌，日渐增长也，必待墓绝日始锄其势。 | 如果是凶神化入长生，它的祸根刚开始萌发，一天天增长，一定要等到墓、绝的日子，才锄掉它的势头。 | but if an ominous spirit changes into Birth, the root of its calamity has only begun to sprout and grows day by day, and one must wait for the Tomb or Extinction day before its force is hoed out. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「长生」是十二长生的头一位。「福化长生」的「福」是福神（子孙），「鬼化长生」的「鬼」是官鬼；旧注的意思是：占病时，子孙（医药）化入长生，是医药一天天见效，病势一天天减；官鬼（病）化入长生，病就一天一天重下去，要等到官鬼的墓、绝日才压得下来。这是占病「看病势走向」的几条之一，和 LY.ILL.EL.17、ZS.35 等不同，它不看用神旺衰，只看动爻变入哪一位。旧注和王洪绪在这一联上一致，旧注多出占病的这一例。
>
> *Note:* 'Birth' is the first of the twelve stages of life. The 'Fortune' of 'the Fortune line changes into Birth' is the Fortune line (Output), and the 'ghost' of 'the ghost changes into Birth' is Pressure; the old commentary means: in divining illness, when Output (the medicine) changes into Birth, the medicine works more day by day and the illness lessens day by day; when Pressure (the illness) changes into Birth, the illness grows heavier day by day and is held down only on the Tomb or Extinction day of Pressure. This is one of the entries on 'reading the course of the illness', and unlike LY.ILL.EL.17 and ZS.35 and the like it does not look at the strength of the Subject but only at which stage the moving line changes into. The old commentary and Wang Hongxu agree on this couplet, and the old commentary adds this example on illness.
