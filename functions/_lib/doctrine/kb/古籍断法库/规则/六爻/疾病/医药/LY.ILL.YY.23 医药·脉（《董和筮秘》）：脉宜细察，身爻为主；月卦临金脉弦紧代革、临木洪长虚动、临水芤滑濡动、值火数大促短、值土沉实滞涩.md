# 医药·脉（《董和筮秘》）：脉宜细察，身爻为主；月卦临金脉弦紧代革、临木洪长虚动、临水芤滑濡动、值火数大促短、值土沉实滞涩

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《董和筮秘》说：脉要仔细察，以身爻为主。月卦临在金上，脉一定是弦、紧、代、革；临在木上，脉一定是洪、长、虚、动；临在水上，脉一定是芤、滑、濡、动；值火，脉一定是数、大、促、短；值土，脉一定是沉、实、滞、涩。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Dong He Shimi says: the pulse should be examined carefully, with the Body line as the master. If the month-hexagram falls on Metal, the pulse is surely wiry, tight, intermittent and leathery; on Wood, surging, long, vacuous and stirring; on Water, hollow, slippery, soft and stirring; on Fire, rapid, large, hurried and short; on Earth, deep, full, sluggish and rough.

> [!note] 按
> 弦、紧、代、革、洪、长、虚、动、芤、滑、濡、数、大、促、短、沉、实、滞、涩都是中医脉名，译文用通行的英文脉名的字面。「身爻」「月卦」书里没有在这里解释（月卦身法，见 LY.ILL.HJC.35）。这一段是易隐引的《董和筮秘》，不是易隐自己的话。
>
> *Note:* Xian, jin, dai, ge, hong, chang, xu, dong, kou, hua, ru, shu, da, cu, duan, chen, shi, zhi and se are all names of pulses in Chinese medicine, rendered by the usual literal English pulse names. The 'Body line' and 'month-hexagram' are not explained here (for the month-body method see LY.ILL.HJC.35). This passage is the Dong He Shimi as quoted in the Yi Yin, not the Yi Yin's own words.
