# 痘疹亦天灾，未花而问以鬼为信：鬼空根芽未萌、鬼静气机未动、鬼散虽流行而不及、破静而痘甲不拆、破动而病或殊、鬼绝则未生花

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 痘疹也是天降的灾，叫做天花，也叫圣疮，一出就关系到孩子的性命。还没出花就来问的，拿鬼爻作凭据：鬼空，痘的根芽还没有萌发；鬼静，气机还没有动；鬼散，痘虽在流行也轮不到他；鬼破而安静，痘的壳不裂开；鬼破而发动，得的可能是别的病；鬼绝，还没有生花。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pox is also a calamity from heaven, called heavenly flowers and also the sacred sore, and once it stirs it bears on a child's life. If the question is asked before the flowers have come, take the ghost as the token: if the ghost is Void, the roots and shoots of the pox have not yet sprouted; if still, the working of qi has not yet stirred; if scattered, although the pox is going about it does not reach him; if broken and still, the husk does not split; if broken and moving, the illness may be a different one; if in Extinction, the flowers have not yet been born.

> [!note] 按
> 「鬼」在这一章里就是官鬼爻，本章通篇以它为痘的凭据。「癍麻疮毒」是斑疹、麻疹、疮毒一类的病，不是「正痘」。「甲」是痘的外壳或种子的壳，「甲不拆」是壳裂不开，意思是还没出。这一条和易隐的痘疹占（LY.ILL.YY.35：鬼不上卦、鬼化死墓绝胎空、被刑害克破者皆不出）是同一个判断。
>
> *Note:* In this chapter the 'ghost' is the Pressure line throughout, and it is the token for the pox. 'Spots, measles, sores and poison' are illnesses of the spotted, measly and sore kinds and not the 'true pox'. The 'husk' (jia) is the outer shell of the pox or the husk of a seed, and 'the husk does not split' means it has not yet come out. This entry is the same judgment as the Yi Yin's on divining pox (LY.ILL.YY.35: the ghost absent from the hexagram, changing into Death, tomb, Extinction, Womb or Void, or punished, harmed, controlled or broken, means it will not come out).
