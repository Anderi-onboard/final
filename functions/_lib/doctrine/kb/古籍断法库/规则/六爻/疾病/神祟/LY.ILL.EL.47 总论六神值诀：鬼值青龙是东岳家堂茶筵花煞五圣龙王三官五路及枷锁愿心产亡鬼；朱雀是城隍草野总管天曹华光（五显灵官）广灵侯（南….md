# 总论六神值诀：鬼值青龙是东岳家堂茶筵花煞五圣龙王三官五路及枷锁愿心产亡鬼；朱雀是城隍草野总管天曹华光（五显灵官）广灵侯（南堂陆太君）司令（灶神）及旧欠愿心牢中鬼；勾陈是土皇五方贤圣跌死鬼；螣蛇是妖怪土地里社之神产亡缢死二鬼；白虎是伤司五道丧煞赵玄坛金神（七煞）叶神（九卓）刀伤虎伤鬼；玄武是圣帝曹三河太伏尸坑厕井泉童子杜氏夫人采山（草野三郎）溺死窃盗二鬼；定六神诸位仍须八卦五行排、看清何爻临鬼，莫累人虚费财

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼值青龙：是东岳、家堂、茶筵、花煞、五圣、龙王、三官大帝、五路尊神，以及枷锁愿心、产亡的鬼。鬼值朱雀：是城隍、草野总管、天曹、华光（就是五显灵官）、广灵侯（就是南堂陆太君）、司令（就是灶神），以及旧欠愿心、牢里的鬼。鬼值勾陈：是土皇和五方贤圣，以及跌死的鬼。鬼值螣蛇：是妖怪，并且作犯，又是土、当方的土地、里社之神，以及产亡、缢死两种鬼。鬼值白虎：是伤司、五道、丧煞，赵玄坛和金神就是七煞，叶神就是九卓，以及刀伤、虎伤的鬼。鬼值玄武：是圣帝、曹三、河太、伏尸、坑厕、土井泉童子、杜氏夫人，采山就是草野三郎，以及溺死、偷盗两种鬼。要定下六神各是哪一位圣，还必须靠八卦和五行来排，哪一爻临在鬼上要看清楚，不要连累人白白花钱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line on the Azure Dragon: the Eastern Peak, the household shrine, the Tea Feast, the Flower Killing, the Five Sages, the Dragon King, the Three Officials the Great Emperors and the honoured god of the Five Roads, together with ghosts of the cangue-and-fetters vow and of death in childbirth. On the Vermilion Bird: the City God, the Chief Official of the wilds, the Heavenly Bureau, Huaguang (the Five Manifest Spirit Officials), the Marquis Guangling (Lady Lu of the Southern Hall), the Director (the Kitchen God), together with ghosts of old owed vows and of the prison. On Hook Earth: the Earth Emperor and the sages of the five quarters, and the ghost of one who died by a fall. On the Coiling Snake: monsters, and it makes an offence; also the earth, the Earth God of the local quarter, the god of the village altar, and the two ghosts of death in childbirth and of hanging. On the White Tiger: the Injuring Office, the Five Roads, the Mourning Killing; Zhao Xuantan and the Golden Spirit are the Seven Killings; the Leaf God is Jiuzhuo; together with ghosts of knife wounds and tiger wounds. On the Dark Tortoise: the Holy Emperor, Cao San, He Tai, the buried corpse, the pit and privy, the earth, the youth of the well and spring, Lady Du; the Mountain Gatherer is the Third Lord of the wilds; together with the two ghosts of drowning and of theft. To fix which sage each of the six spirits is, one must still arrange them by the eight trigrams and the five phases, look closely to see which line sits on the ghost, and not burden people with empty spending of money.

> 这是「总论」的第三段（编号「之三」）：官鬼所临的六神各配一串神名，体例是歌诀加解说。它和易冒「按六神断神鬼」（LY.ILL.YM.59）、黄金策「六神推鬼神」（LY.ILL.HJC.96）是同一类办法，只是名目更细；和本章前面按五行配六兽（LY.ILL.EL.30 到 EL.34）也可以对读，六神的神名与上一条（LY.ILL.EL.46）几乎全是同一批。最后一首歌诀说得最重：六神各是哪一位，还要靠八卦和五行来排、看清哪一爻临鬼，不能只凭六神一样就点名，也不要让人白白花钱——这和本章结语（LY.ILL.EL.43）、黄金策和易冒把神鬼只看作大类的做法一致。名字上的几处存疑：「花枷原」按「花枷愿」译；「止苍」疑是「上苍」（High Heaven），这里按原字音译；「承天后土皇」按字面译作「承天的后土皇」；「皇社神」按字面译作皇社（皇家的社坛）之神；「西台」没有解释，按字面译；「穿斋」按下面解说「窃盗」读作「穿窬」（挖墙洞偷东西）；「司令」解说里说就是灶神（通常写作「司命」，本书前文也写「司命」），按原字译作 Director；「螣蛇」一条「并作犯上」文字很简，「作犯」按「作出冒犯」译，「上」没有着落，这里不强译。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。

> *Note:* This is the third section of the 'general discourse' (numbered 'three'): each of the six spirits the Pressure line sits on is matched with a string of divine names, in the form of a verse plus a commentary. It is the same kind of method as the Yi Mao's 'judging gods and ghosts by the six spirits' (LY.ILL.YM.59) and the Huangjin Ce's 'the six spirits and gods and ghosts' (LY.ILL.HJC.96), with finer names, and can also be read against this chapter's earlier matching of the phases with the six beasts (LY.ILL.EL.30 to EL.34); the names of gods here are almost all the same set as in the previous entry (LY.ILL.EL.46). The last verse is the weightiest: which sage each of the six spirits is must still be arranged by the eight trigrams and the five phases, looking closely at which line sits on the ghost; one must not name a god from the six spirits alone, and one must not make people spend money for nothing, which agrees with this chapter's closing passage (LY.ILL.EL.43) and with the Huangjin Ce and the Yi Mao, who treat gods and ghosts only in broad kinds. Doubts about a few names: 'hua jia yuan' is translated as the vow of flowers and the cangue, the character for 'source' standing for 'vow'; 'Zhicang' is probably 'Shangcang' (High Heaven), and is transliterated as written; 'cheng tian hou tu huang' is rendered literally as 'the Sovereign Earth Emperor who receives Heaven'; 'huang she shen' is rendered literally as the spirit of the imperial altar; 'Western Terrace' has no explanation and is rendered literally; 'chuan zhai' is read as 'chuan yu' (boring through a wall to steal) from the commentary 'theft'; 'Director' (si ling) is said by the commentary to be the Kitchen God (usually written 'si ming', and this book earlier also writes 'Director of Fate'), and is translated as written; the Coiling Snake's 'and it makes an offence' is very terse: 'zuo fan' is translated as 'makes an offence', and the 'shang' is left unforced. Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
