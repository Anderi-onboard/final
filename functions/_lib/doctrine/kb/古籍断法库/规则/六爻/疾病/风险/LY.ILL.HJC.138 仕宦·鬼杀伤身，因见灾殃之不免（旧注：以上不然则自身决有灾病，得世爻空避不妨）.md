# 仕宦·鬼杀伤身，因见灾殃之不免（旧注：以上不然则自身决有灾病，得世爻空避不妨）

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》仕宦章说：「鬼杀伤了身，灾殃就免不了。」旧注：官鬼发动，生合世爻的是用神，伤克世爻的是鬼杀；用神扶世，一定有进取；鬼杀伤身，一定有凶祸，按化出来的六亲来断；如果不是上面这些，就是自己一定有灾病，世爻落空避开就没有妨碍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on official careers says: 'A ghost killing harming the Body: calamity cannot be avoided.' The old commentary: when the Pressure line moves, one that generates or combines with the Self line is the Subject, and one that harms or controls the Self line is a ghost killing; the Subject supporting the Self line brings advancement, a ghost killing harming the Body brings ill fortune, judged by the relation it turns into; if it is not the above, then he himself is sure to have calamity or illness, and no harm if the Self line is Void and escapes.

> | 以上不然，则自身决有灾病，得世爻空避不妨。 | 如果不是上面这些，就是自己一定有灾病，世爻落空避开就没有妨碍。 | If it is not the above, then he himself is sure to have calamity or illness, and there is no harm if the Self line is Void and escapes. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 仕宦章是占做官的一章。这里的「灾病」是做官的人自己的灾祸或疾病，旧注没有分开说是灾还是病，译文照字面写作「calamity or illness」。「用神」「鬼杀」在这一段里是按官鬼发动后对世爻的生克来分的：生合世爻是用神（官是他要的），伤克世爻是鬼杀。「世爻空避不妨」是世爻落了空亡，躲过了克害。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on official careers is about divining holding office. The 'calamity or illness' here is the official's own calamity or illness, and the old commentary does not separate calamity from illness; the translation keeps the letter, 'calamity or illness'. The 'Subject' and 'ghost killing' are told apart in this passage by what the moving Pressure line does to the Self line: generating or combining with it is the Subject (the office is what he wants), harming or controlling it is a ghost killing. 'No harm if the Self line is Void and escapes' means that the Self line, falling Void, slips past the harm.
