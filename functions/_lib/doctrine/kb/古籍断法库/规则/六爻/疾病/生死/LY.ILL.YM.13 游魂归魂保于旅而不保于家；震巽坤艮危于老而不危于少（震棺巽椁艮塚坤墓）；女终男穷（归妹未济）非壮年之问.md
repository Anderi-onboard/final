# 游魂归魂保于旅而不保于家；震巽坤艮危于老而不危于少（震棺巽椁艮塚坤墓）；女终男穷（归妹未济）非壮年之问

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 游魂、归魂的卦，在外做客时保得住，在家里却保不住；震、巽、坤、艮的卦，对老人危险，对年轻人不危险；女终、男穷（归妹、未济）不适合壮年人来问。注：游魂是去、归魂是回来，如果病在旅途中，路上就没有忌讳；震象棺，巽象椁，艮象坟冢，坤象坟墓，对上了年纪的人是大忌，有所准备反而更凶；未济、归妹，壮年人不适合；但也要兼用神参看。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In Wandering Soul and Returning Soul hexagrams he is safe abroad but not at home; in Zhen, Xun, Kun and Gen he is in danger if old but not if young; 'the woman's end' and 'the man's exhaustion' (Guimei and Weiji) are not fitting for a divination by a man in his prime. The note: Wandering is going out and Returning is coming back, and if the illness falls on a journey the road holds no taboo; Zhen is the coffin, Xun the outer coffin, Gen the mound and Kun the grave, a great taboo for the old, and to be prepared is worse; Weiji and Guimei are not fitting for the prime of life; but one must also consult the Subject.

> [!note] 按
> 「女终男穷」按《序卦传》，归妹是女之终，未济是男之穷；书里没有再解释，注里用卦名点了出来。「有备更凶」是说预先准备后事反而更凶，书里没有解释。这一条和易隐「黄泉杀、棺椁杀」一类按卦名断死（LY.ILL.YY.08）相近，但易冒这里要「兼用神而参」，不单凭卦名。
>
> *Note:* 'The woman's end and the man's exhaustion' are, after the Xu Gua Zhuan, Guimei as the woman's end and Weiji as the man's exhaustion; the book does not explain further and the note names them by hexagram. 'To be prepared is worse' means that to prepare for the funeral in advance is worse, which the book does not explain. This entry is close to the Yi Yin's judging death by hexagram names, the 'Yellow Springs' and 'Coffin' killers (LY.ILL.YY.08), but here the Yi Mao says 'also consult the Subject' and does not rely on the hexagram name alone.
