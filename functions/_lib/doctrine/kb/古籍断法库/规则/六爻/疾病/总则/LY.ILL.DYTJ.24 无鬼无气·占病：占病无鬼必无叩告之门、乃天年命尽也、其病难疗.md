# 无鬼无气·占病：占病无鬼必无叩告之门、乃天年命尽也、其病难疗

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼这一爻没有形体却有用处，卦里不能没有，宜静不宜动；带着吉神动也能有福，加上凶煞动没有不成祸的。占病而卦里没有鬼，一定没有可以叩告（祷告）的门路，这是天年命数到头了，病难疗。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure has no form but has its use; it must not be absent from the hexagram, and should be still and not move; moving with a lucky spirit it can also bring fortune, moving with a fierce star it always brings calamity. In divining illness with no ghost in the hexagram, there is surely no door to knock at and pray, for the heavenly years and the fate are used up, and the illness is hard to treat.

> [!note] 按
> 头一段是「无鬼无气」一章的总句，占婚、占官、占讼、求财各有各的例（见 LY.MARRY.DYTJ.01 一带），这一条只收占病那一句。「叩告之门」是叩首祷告的门路，大概是没有鬼就没有能祈求的鬼神（病的祟没有着落），所以没有办法祷解；「天年命尽」是天年（寿数）到了头。这一句和「卦中无鬼病难医」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.02）、天玄赋「卦若无鬼，其病难医」（LY.ILL.TXF.10）、阐奥歌章「卦中无鬼病难医」（LY.ILL.CA.04）是同一条。下面一句「惟有占产、出行、行人、田蚕，无鬼方为大吉」不在病的范围，不收。
>
> *Note:* The first passage is the general statement of the chapter 'no ghost and no qi', and marriage, office, lawsuit and wealth each have their own cases (see LY.MARRY.DYTJ.01 and around); this entry takes only the sentence on illness. The 'door of knocking and praying' is the way of bowing and praying, and it probably means that with no ghost there is no spirit to appeal to (the curse of the illness has nowhere to rest), and so there is no way to pray it away; 'the heavenly years and the fate are used up' is that the lifespan has come to its end. This sentence is the same as 'with no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to treat' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02), the Tianxuan Fu's 'if the hexagram has no ghost, the illness is hard to treat' (LY.ILL.TXF.10) and the Chan'ao Gezhang's 'with no ghost in the hexagram the illness is hard to treat' (LY.ILL.CA.04). The next sentence, 'only in divining childbirth, travel, a traveller and fields and silkworms is no ghost greatly lucky', is outside illness and is not taken.
