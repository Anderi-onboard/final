# 兄兴象内须理胸怀：兄弟爻发动克财爻，主饮食少进或乏调理；在间爻宜宽胸理气，临朱雀必因生气而不思饮食

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻在卦里兴起，要调理胸怀：财是用来调理的东西，兄是防气的神；如果遇到兄弟爻发动，就伤克财爻，主吃得少，或者缺少调理；如果落在间爻，适合宽胸理气；如果带朱雀，一定是生了气而不想吃东西。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Peer rises in the hexagram, the chest and mind must be regulated: Wealth is the thing for regulation and Peer is the spirit that guards against breath and temper; if the Peer line moves, it harms and controls the Wealth line, which means little food is taken or regulation is lacking; if it is on a between line, ease the chest and regulate the qi; if it carries the Vermilion Bird, the patient is surely angry and does not want to eat.

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2255 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)

> | 财为调理之物，兄为气防之神，如遇兄弟爻发动，则伤克财爻，主饮食少进或乏于调理。 | 财是用来调理的东西，兄是防气的神，如果遇到兄弟爻发动，就伤克财爻，主吃得少，或者缺少调理。 | Wealth is the thing for regulation and Peer is the spirit that guards against breath and temper; if the Peer line moves, it harms and controls the Wealth line, which means little food is taken or regulation is lacking. |
> | 如在间爻，宜宽胸理气； | 如果落在间爻，适合宽胸理气； | If it is on a between line, ease the chest and regulate the qi; |
> | 如临朱雀，必感怒而不思饮食。 | 如果带朱雀，一定是生了气而不想吃东西。 | if it carries the Vermilion Bird, the patient is surely angry and does not want to eat. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「兄为气防之神」注里没有再解释，按字面译作防气的神（兄弟克财，所以由它管吃与不吃的火气）。
>
> *Note:* 'Peer is the spirit that guards against breath' is not explained further in the commentary and is rendered literally (Peer controls Wealth, so it governs the temper that bears on eating and not eating).
