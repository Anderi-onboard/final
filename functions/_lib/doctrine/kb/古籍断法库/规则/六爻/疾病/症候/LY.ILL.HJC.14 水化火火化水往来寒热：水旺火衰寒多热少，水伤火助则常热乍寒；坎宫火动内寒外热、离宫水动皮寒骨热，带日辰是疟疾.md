# 水化火火化水往来寒热：水旺火衰寒多热少，水伤火助则常热乍寒；坎宫火动内寒外热、离宫水动皮寒骨热，带日辰是疟疾

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里水爻动化火、火爻动化水，不论是不是鬼爻，只要冲犯世爻或主象的，都是忽冷忽热的症候；水、火两爻都动，也是这样。水旺火衰是冷多热少；水受伤、火得助，是常热而偶尔发冷。坎宫火动是里冷外热，离宫水动是皮冷骨热。再带上日辰，一定是疟疾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the hexagram, a Water line moving and changing into Fire, or a Fire line changing into Water, whether or not it is the Pressure line, if it offends the Self line or the main image, is a syndrome of alternating chills and fever; if a Water line and a Fire line both move, it is the same. Water strong and Fire weak means more chill and less fever; Water harmed and Fire helped means constant heat with an occasional chill. Fire moving in the Kan palace is cold inside and hot outside; Water moving in the Li palace is cold skin and hot bones. If it also carries the Day, it is surely malaria.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2323 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 水化火，火化水，不拘鬼爻，但有干犯主象者，皆是寒热往来之症，卦有水火二爻俱动亦然。 | 水化火、火化水，不论是不是鬼爻，只要冲犯了主象的，都是寒热往来的症候，卦里水、火两爻都动也是这样。 | Water changing into Fire or Fire into Water, whether or not it is the Pressure line, if it offends the main image, is a syndrome of alternating chills and fever, and so it is if both a Water line and a Fire line move in the hexagram. |
> | 水旺火衰，寒多热少，倘水受伤，火得助，则常热乍寒也。 | 水旺火衰，冷多热少，如果水受伤、火得帮助，就是常常发热而偶尔发冷。 | Water strong and Fire weak means more chill and less fever; if Water is harmed and Fire helped, it is constantly hot and now and then cold. |
> | 坎宫火动，内寒外热，离宫水动，皮寒骨热； | 坎宫火动是里冷外热，离宫水动是皮冷骨热； | Fire moving in the Kan palace is cold inside and hot outside, and Water moving in the Li palace is cold skin and hot bones; |
> | 若带日辰，必是虐疾。 | 如果带着日辰，一定是疟疾。 | if it carries the Day, it is surely malaria. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗把「疟疾」写成「虐疾」，是同一个字的写法不同，译作疟疾（malaria）是按后世通行的说法；书里没有说明。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Zhengzong writes 'ni ji' (cruel illness) for 'nue ji' (malaria); they are one word written two ways, and 'malaria' follows later usage, which the book does not explain.
