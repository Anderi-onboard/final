# 五行鬼各主之症：金鬼阴肺腑之疾阳骨节疼痛脓血淋漓；木鬼阴肝胆之疾阳四肢风气酸疼口眼歪斜；水鬼阴遗精盗汗肾竭血枯经闭阳呕吐泄泻；火鬼阴心目之疾阳疮痍疖毒虐热；土鬼阴虚浮气喘水蛊脾泄阳瘟疫时气，辰咽喉肿塞戌腹痛胃伤丑气促痿痺未翻胃噎膈痨嗽

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻遇到金鬼：属阴是肺腑的病，属阳是骨节疼痛、脓血淋漓；金鬼旺，是泻肚子再带一样生僻的病；金鬼衰，是血里生脓。遇到木鬼：属阴是肝胆的病，属阳是四肢风气酸疼、口眼歪斜、头疼口干；木动的，阳的痒、阴的疼；木鬼旺，是风病带热；木鬼衰，是在同一个字的病上再生出风来。遇到水鬼：属阴是遗精、盗汗、肾气枯竭，女的是血枯闭经，属阳是呕吐泄泻；水鬼旺，是呕吐带咳嗽；水鬼衰，是受寒腹泻兼虚弱。遇到火鬼：属阴是心和眼睛的病，属阳是疮痍疖毒、疟热呻吟；火鬼旺，先冷后热；火鬼衰，先热后冷。遇到土鬼：属阴是虚浮气喘、水蛊、脾泄，属阳是瘟疫时气；辰鬼是咽喉肿塞，戌鬼是腹痛胃伤，丑鬼是气促痿痹，未鬼是翻胃、噎膈、痨病咳嗽。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line meets a Metal ghost: yin means a disease of the lungs and viscera, yang means aching bone joints and dripping pus and blood; a strong Metal ghost brings diarrhea together with a rare ailment, and a weak Metal ghost brings pus forming within the blood. Meeting a Wood ghost: yin means a disease of the liver and gallbladder, yang means wind-qi with aching limbs, a crooked mouth and eyes, headache and a dry mouth; when Wood moves, yang itches and yin aches; a strong Wood ghost brings wind sickness with heat, a weak one wind growing upon the same rare ailment. Meeting a Water ghost: yin means seminal emission, night sweats and exhausted kidneys, and in a woman dried-up blood and stopped menses; yang means vomiting and diarrhea; a strong Water ghost brings vomiting with cough, a weak one cold diarrhea with weakness. Meeting a Fire ghost: yin means a disease of the heart and eyes, yang means sores, boils and poison, malarial fever and groaning; a strong Fire ghost brings cold first and then heat, a weak one heat first and then cold. Meeting an Earth ghost: yin means puffy weakness and wheezing, water-swelling and spleen diarrhea, yang means epidemic and seasonal qi; a Chen ghost is a swollen blocked throat, a Xu ghost belly pain and a damaged stomach, a Chou ghost shortness of breath and limp numbness, and a Wei ghost regurgitation, choking in the gullet, consumption and cough.

> [!note] 按
> 原书里有两处写着「（此处之字上疒下去）」，是整理者的注：那个字上面是病字头、下面是「去」，一个生僻的病字，整理者没有打出来，病名不明，译文照写不猜。「虐热」应是疟热（疟疾发热），原书写作「虐」。「水蛊」是腹部胀水的病，「膈」「噎膈」是吞咽梗阻，「痿痺」是肢体软弱、麻木疼痛，都按字面。这一条和黄金策的五行症候（LY.ILL.HJC.02、LY.ILL.HJC.04）是同一个路子，但易隐更细：每一行分阴阳，又分旺衰，土另分辰戌丑未四种。
>
> *Note:* The original has two places marked '(the character here: sickness radical above, go below)', the transcriber's note: a rare character for an ailment which the transcriber could not enter, so the name of the ailment is unknown and the translation writes it as it stands and does not guess. 'Malarial fever' (nuere) is a writing of 'nue' with the wrong character in the original. 'Water-swelling' (shuigu) is a disease with a belly swollen with fluid, 'yege' is obstruction in swallowing, and 'weibi' is limpness of the limbs with numbness and pain, all rendered literally. This entry is on the same road as the Huangjin Ce's element symptoms (LY.ILL.HJC.02 and LY.ILL.HJC.04) but the Yi Yin is finer: each element is divided into yin and yang and into strong and weak, and Earth further into Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei.
