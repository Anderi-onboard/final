# 鬼崇·是谁的鬼：父母伏鬼化鬼阳父阴母、兄弟阳兄弟阴姐妹、妻财阳妻奴阴妾婢、子孙阳男阴女；鬼在本宫内卦同姓、他宫外卦异姓；交重大鬼、单拆小鬼；衰墓老年旧鬼、生旺壮年新鬼、胎养孩童鬼；乾宫祖宗与父、坤宫祖妣与母、震坎艮男、巽离兑女；几个鬼按一水二火三木四金五土之数，旺相加倍、休如数、囚死减半

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看是谁的鬼：父母爻下伏着鬼、或变出鬼的，阳爻是父亲，阴爻是母亲；兄弟爻下伏鬼、变鬼的，阳爻是兄弟，阴爻是姐妹；妻财爻下伏鬼、变鬼的，阳爻是妻子和奴仆，阴爻是妾和婢女；子孙爻下伏鬼、变鬼的，阳爻是男的，阴爻是女的。鬼在本宫内卦的，是同姓的鬼；在别的宫、外卦的，是异姓的鬼。发动的是大鬼，不动的是小鬼；衰弱、入墓的是年老的旧鬼，生旺的是壮年的新鬼，胎、养是孩童的鬼（阳男阴女）。按八卦：乾宫的鬼是祖宗和父亲，坤宫的鬼是祖母和母亲，震、坎、艮宫的鬼是男的，巽、离、兑宫的鬼是女的。问有几个鬼，按一水、二火、三木、四金、五土的数来推，旺相的加倍，休的照原数，囚、死的减半。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whose ghost it is: Resource with a ghost hidden beneath it or changing into a ghost — yang is the father, yin the mother; Peer with a ghost hidden beneath or changing into one — yang brothers, yin sisters; Wealth with a ghost — yang a wife or manservant, yin a concubine or maid; Output with a ghost — yang male, yin female. A ghost in the inner trigram of the home palace is a ghost of the same surname, and one in the outer trigram of another palace a ghost of another surname. A moving ghost is a great ghost and a still one a small ghost; weak or in a tomb, an old ghost of long standing; born or strong, a ghost of the prime of life, newly dead; at Womb or Nurture, a child's ghost (yang a boy, yin a girl). By the eight trigrams: a ghost in the Qian palace is an ancestor or the father, in Kun a grandmother or the mother, in Zhen, Kan or Gen male, in Xun, Li or Dui female. To ask how many ghosts there are, work by the numbers Water one, Fire two, Wood three, Metal four, Earth five: if strong, double it; if resting, as given; if trapped or dead, halve it.

> [!note] 按
> 「交重」是发动的爻，「单拆」是不动的爻（单是阳爻、拆是阴爻）。「祖妣」是祖母。「坎」是中男，「艮」是少男，「巽」是长女，「离」是中女，「兑」是少女，这几宫的鬼按男女分。黄金策的旧注和王洪绪也有按长生、帝旺、衰弱、胎养分年龄、按乾巽分方位的说法（LY.ILL.HJC.94），这里多了按六亲、按本宫他宫分同姓异姓、按卦宫分男女和按五行之数推几个鬼几条；卦身推是谁的鬼见 LY.ILL.HJC.102。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiaozhong' is a moving line and 'danchai' a still line (dan a yang line, chai a yin line). 'Zubi' is the grandmother. Kan is the middle son, Gen the youngest son, Xun the eldest daughter, Li the middle daughter and Dui the youngest daughter, and the ghosts of these palaces are divided by sex. The old commentary and Wang Hongxu on the Huangjin Ce also have the age by Birth, Peak, decline, Womb and Nurture and the direction by Qian and Xun (LY.ILL.HJC.94); this adds telling by relation which ghost it is, the same or a different surname by home palace or another, sex by trigram, and the number of ghosts by the number of the element; for telling whose ghost it is by the Body see LY.ILL.HJC.102.
