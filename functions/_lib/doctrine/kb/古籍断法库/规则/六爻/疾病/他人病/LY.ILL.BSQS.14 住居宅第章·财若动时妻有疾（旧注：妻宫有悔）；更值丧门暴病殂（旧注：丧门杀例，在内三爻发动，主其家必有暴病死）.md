# 住居宅第章·财若动时妻有疾（旧注：妻宫有悔）；更值丧门暴病殂（旧注：丧门杀例，在内三爻发动，主其家必有暴病死）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占家宅的歌里说：财爻发动，妻子有病；再遇丧门，暴病而死。旧注：丧门的取法是正月未、二月辰、三月丑、四月戌，四个位置轮着用到十二个月；丧门在内卦三爻发动，主这家一定有人暴病而死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on dwellings says: when Wealth moves, the wife is ill; if the Mourning Gate is met as well, sudden illness and death. The old note: the Mourning Gate is placed at Wei in the first month, Chen in the second, Chou in the third and Xu in the fourth, the four places turning through the twelve months; if the Mourning Gate is in the inner three lines and moves, someone in the household is sure to die of a sudden illness.

> [!note] 按
> 这里讲病的两处都在占家宅（住居宅第章第二首）的歌里：「财若动时妻有疾」「更值丧门暴病殂」，后面各跟一条旧注。「悔」是易经里的小凶、小麻烦。丧门的位置按旧注，正月未、二月辰、三月丑、四月戌，每月退三位，四月之后又回到未（五月），所以「只此四位，轮十二月」是每四个月一轮；旧注只写了前四个月，五月起的轮换是按「轮十二月」读出来的。断易天机的神煞表里也有丧门（LY.ILL.DYTJ.35 的邻近各条），位置是另一种排法，见那边。
>
> *Note:* Both lines on illness here come from the verse on dwellings (the second poem of the chapter on dwellings and houses): 'when Wealth moves, the wife is ill' and 'the Mourning Gate as well: sudden illness and death', each followed by an old note. 'Regret' in the Yijing is a minor trouble. The old note places the Mourning Gate at Wei in the first month, Chen in the second, Chou in the third and Xu in the fourth, three places back each month, with the fifth month returning to Wei, so 'only these four places, turning through twelve months' means one turn every four months; the note writes out only the first four months, and the turning from the fifth month on is read from 'turning through twelve months'. The table of spirit-stars in the Duanyi Tianji also has the Mourning Gate, placed in another way, see there.
