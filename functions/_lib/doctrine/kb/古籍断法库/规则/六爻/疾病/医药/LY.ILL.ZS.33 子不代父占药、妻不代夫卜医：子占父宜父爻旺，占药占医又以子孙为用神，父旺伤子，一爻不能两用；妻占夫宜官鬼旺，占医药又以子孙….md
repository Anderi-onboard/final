# 子不代父占药、妻不代夫卜医：子占父宜父爻旺，占药占医又以子孙为用神，父旺伤子，一爻不能两用；妻占夫宜官鬼旺，占医药又以子孙为用神，子旺伤夫，所以不可代占医药

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 儿子不替父亲占药，妻子不替丈夫卜医。儿子占父亲的病，宜父爻旺相；可是占药、占医，又要拿子孙当用神，父爻旺就会伤子孙，一个爻不能同时当两样用。妻子占丈夫的病，宜官鬼旺相；占医占药，也拿子孙当用神，子孙旺又会伤丈夫，所以不可以替他占医药。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A son does not divine medicine for his father, nor a wife a doctor for her husband. A son divining his father's illness wants the Resource line strong; but in divining medicine or a doctor Output is taken as the Subject, and a strong Resource line harms Output, and one line cannot serve two uses. A wife divining her husband's illness wants the Pressure line strong; in divining a doctor or medicine Output is also taken as the Subject, and a strong Output harms the husband, so one may not divine medicine or a doctor on his behalf.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和 LY.ILL.HJC.32 里王洪绪的限定（占父母、丈夫病不宜子孙发动）是一个道理。但野鹤在医占往治章里自己给儿子占父病的药占过两卦（LY.ILL.ZS.37 的人参、牛黄），用的是子孙，并说「卦无不灵，在人通变」；所以这条是通则，他自己的实占是按情形变通的。
>
> *Note:* This entry gives the same reason as Wang Hongxu's limit in LY.ILL.HJC.32 (in divining a parent's or a husband's illness Output should not move). But in the chapter on the doctor's divination the Old Man himself divined medicine twice for a father's illness on the son's behalf (the ginseng and the bezoar of LY.ILL.ZS.37), used Output, and said 'no hexagram fails to work, it depends on whether one adapts'; so this entry is the general rule and his own practice adapted to the case.
