# 问花的疏密、部位、颜色：鬼爻旺临日月又有生扶花必稠密，空破休囚墓绝痘疏朗；鬼在乾多生头、坤多腹、艮多手、兑多口、坎离上身、震巽下身；火鬼紫红、金鬼虚白、水鬼防黑陷、木鬼杂细、土鬼肿大

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问花出得稀还是密：鬼爻旺相、临着日月、又有动爻生扶的，花一定稠密；如果遇到空、破、休囚、入墓入绝，痘就稀疏明朗。鬼在乾宫发动，多长在头上；鬼在坤卦摇动，肚子上成丛；艮宫多在手上，兑宫多在口上，坎、离在上半身，震、巽在下半身。颜色：火鬼紫红，金鬼虚白，水鬼要防发黑塌陷，木鬼又杂又细，土鬼肿大。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask whether the flowers will be sparse or dense: if the Pressure line is strong, sits on the Day or Month and is generated and supported by a moving line, the flowers are surely dense; if it meets Void or a break, or is resting, trapped, in a tomb or extinct, the pox are sparse and clear. Pressure moving in the Qian palace: mostly on the head; stirring in Kun: clusters on the belly; Gen, more on the hands; Dui, more on the mouth; Kan and Li, the upper body; Zhen and Xun, the lower body. As to colour: a Fire Pressure, purple-red; a Metal Pressure, pale white; a Water Pressure, beware of black and sunken pox; a Wood Pressure, mixed and fine; an Earth Pressure, swollen and large.
