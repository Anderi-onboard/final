# 木鬼乃风邪未表、水宫而寒食尚停：官爻属木是风寒未表、肝经受毒，防呛喘发痒两目直视；官爻属水毒发肾经，防腰疼两耳焦干，尚有寒食停积发热宿浆

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木鬼是风邪还没有发散到表面，水宫则是寒凉的食物还停积着。官爻属木，一定是风寒没有发散出去，肝经受了毒，要防呛咳、气喘、发痒和两眼直瞪；官爻属水，毒发在肾经，要防腰疼和两耳焦干，还有寒凉的食物停积在里面，发热、浆水留着不化。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Wood ghost is a wind evil not yet expelled to the surface; in the Water palace cold food is still stagnant. If the Pressure line is Wood, it is surely because wind-cold has not been expelled and the liver channel has taken the poison, and one must guard against choking cough, wheezing, itching and a fixed stare of the eyes; if the Pressure line is Water, the poison breaks out in the kidney channel, and one must guard against lower-back pain and both ears becoming scorched and dry, and there is also cold food stagnant within, with fever and lingering fluid.
