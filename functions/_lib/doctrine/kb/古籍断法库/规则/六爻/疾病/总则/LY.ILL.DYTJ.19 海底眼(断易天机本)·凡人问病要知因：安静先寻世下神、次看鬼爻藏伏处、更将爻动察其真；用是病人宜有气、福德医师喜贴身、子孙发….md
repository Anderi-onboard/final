# 海底眼(断易天机本)·凡人问病要知因：安静先寻世下神、次看鬼爻藏伏处、更将爻动察其真；用是病人宜有气、福德医师喜贴身、子孙发动勿服药；六位无财食不纳、兄弟交重气积累、鬼多不一原曾病、用发休囚损病人

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问病要知道病因：卦静的话先找世下伏的神，再看鬼爻藏伏在哪里，再拿发动的爻去察它的真相。用爻是病人，宜有气；福德是医师，喜欢贴着身；子孙发动，不要服药；卦宫旺相，病就迟迟拖延。六位没有财，吃不进东西；兄弟交重，气积在里面；鬼多、不止一个，原来就有病；用爻发动却休囚，损伤病人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about illness one must know its cause: with the hexagram still first look for the spirit hidden under the Self, then look at where the ghost line lies hidden, and further examine the truth by the moving lines. The Subject is the sick person and should have qi; Output is the physician and likes to be close to the Body; with Output moving do not take medicine, and with the hexagram's palace strong the illness lingers. With no Wealth in the six places he cannot take food; with Peer crossing heavily the qi piles up; with many ghosts, not just one, there was an illness from before; with the Subject moving while resting or trapped, it harms the sick person.

> [!note] 按
> 这是海底眼的另一个抄本，和海底眼本书里的「疾病」一节（LY.ILL.HDY.03、HDY.04）是同一首，断易天机引的这一份字句不同，有几句海底眼那份没有（福德医师喜贴身、子孙发动勿服药、兄弟交重气积累、用发休囚损病人）。「用发休囚」的「用」是用神，「休囚」译作 resting or trapped。「（1）」是今人加的注号，那条注不收。
>
> *Note:* This is another copy of the Haidi Yan and is the same poem as the section on illness in the Haidi Yan itself (LY.ILL.HDY.03, HDY.04), but the copy quoted in the Duanyi Tianji differs in wording, and has several lines that the Haidi Yan copy does not (Output the physician likes to be close to the Body, with Output moving do not take medicine, with Peer crossing heavily the qi piles up, with the Subject moving while resting or trapped it harms the sick person). The 'Subject' of 'Subject moving, resting or trapped' is the Subject line, and 'resting or trapped' is rendered 'resting or trapped'. The '(1)' is a modern annotation mark and that annotation is not quoted.
