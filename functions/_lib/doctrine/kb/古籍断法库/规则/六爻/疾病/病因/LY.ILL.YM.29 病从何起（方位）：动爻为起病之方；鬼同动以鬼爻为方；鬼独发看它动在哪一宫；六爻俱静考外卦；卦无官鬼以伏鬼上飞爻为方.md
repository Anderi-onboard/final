# 病从何起（方位）：动爻为起病之方；鬼同动以鬼爻为方；鬼独发看它动在哪一宫；六爻俱静考外卦；卦无官鬼以伏鬼上飞爻为方

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病是从哪个方向起的，吉凶都生在动上：鬼跟着别的爻一起动的，就去察看那个鬼的方位，因为鬼是祸；鬼爻自己独发的，就去考察它所在的宫；卦象安静的，就去察看外卦，因为祟孽是从外面进来的；卦里没有鬼的，就去考察伏鬼上面压着的飞爻。注举例：动爻所在的方位就是病起的方位，离卦里卯动，病从东边来；鬼和别的爻一起动，就取鬼爻的方位，离卦里亥、卯都动，病从西北来；鬼独发，就查它在哪一宫动，离卦里亥独发，病从南边开始；六爻都静，考外卦，离卦静着，病从南方的地方缠染上；卦里没有官鬼，就取伏鬼上面飞爻的方位，卦里有官鬼而动爻多的，先找忌爻的方位，没有忌爻，才考外卦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* From what direction did the illness rise? Fortune and misfortune are born of movement: where the ghost moves along with the moving lines, examine the direction of that ghost, for the ghost is the calamity; where the Pressure line moves alone, examine its palace; where the hexagram is still, look at the outer trigram, for haunting and calamity enter from outside; where the hexagram has no ghost, examine the flying line above the hidden ghost. The note gives examples: the direction of the moving line is the direction from which the illness arose, so if in Li Mao moves the illness comes from the east; if the ghost moves along with others, take the direction of the Pressure line, so if in Li Hai and Mao both move the illness comes from the northwest; if the ghost moves alone, seek in which palace it moves, so if in Li Hai alone moves the illness starts from the south; if all six lines are still, examine the outer trigram, so if Li is still the illness was caught from the south; if the hexagram has no Pressure line, take the direction of the flying line above the hidden ghost, and where it has Pressure but many moving lines, first seek the direction of the Adversary line, and if there is none, examine the outer trigram.

> [!note] 按
> 经文里「乃考其官」的「官」字，注文改述成「索其动于何宫」，可知这里说的是宫（八卦的宫），原文「官」字疑是「宫」之误，这里按注译作「它所在的宫」。注里说的方位是把八卦和五行配到方位上得来的：离在南，卯在东，亥在西北。「补遗」按上下文是《易林补遗》，注里「家人西北蒙灾之由」这一句和后面「或卦有官」之间没有标点，这里断成两截译。与易隐「病起何方」（LY.ILL.YY.33）取法相近，但易隐以火南水北木东金西论，不论鬼之有无。
>
> *Note:* In the text's 'examine its guan (official)', the note re-words it as 'seek in which palace it moves', which shows that a palace (gong, of the eight trigrams) is meant, and the original 'guan' looks like an error for 'gong'; it is rendered here as 'the palace it is in'. The directions in the note come from assigning the trigrams and phases to directions: Li is south, Mao is east, Hai is northwest. The 'Buyi' is by context the Yilin Buyi; in the note there is no punctuation between 'Jiaren, the northwest, is the cause of the calamity' and the following 'or if the hexagram has Pressure', and it is translated here as two parts. The method is close to the Yi Yin's 'the direction from which the illness rose' (LY.ILL.YY.33), but the Yi Yin reckons by Fire south, Water north, Wood east, Metal west, whether or not there is a ghost.
