# 鬼崇·神与鬼的名目：阳金鬼庵堂关圣（在虎为七煞）、阴金鬼刀伤自刎痨瘵喘嗽虎伤之魂；阳木鬼东岳五圣家先、阴木鬼自缢疯疾跌扑刑杖死；阳水鬼观音真武三官水神河泊、阴水鬼投河服卤呕血；阳火鬼玄坛五福火神灶司、阴火鬼疮毒痨疫带血心疼烧死；阳土鬼城隍土谷太岁皮场大王、阴土鬼瘟疫咽喉臌胀黄疸压死

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 按五行和阴阳分神和鬼：阳金鬼是庵堂、关圣，带白虎的是七煞；阴金鬼是死于刀伤、自刎、痨病、喘嗽、虎伤的魂。阳木鬼是东岳、五圣、家先香火；阴木鬼是自缢、疯病、跌扑、被杖刑打死的魂。阳水鬼是观音、真武、三官、水神、张神、河泊、水官；阴水鬼是投河、喝卤水、呕血而死的魂。阳火鬼是玄坛、五福、火神、灶司的香火；阴火鬼是死于疮毒、痨病、瘟疫、带血、心痛、烧死的魂。阳土鬼是城隍、土谷、太岁、皮场大王和土神；阴土鬼是死于瘟疫、咽喉病、臌胀、黄疸、被压死的魂。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Spirits and ghosts by element and by yin and yang: a yang Metal ghost is the temples and halls and Guan Sheng, and with the Tiger the Seven Killings; a yin Metal ghost is the souls of those who died of blade wounds, cutting their own throats, consumption, wheezing cough or a tiger's bite. A yang Wood ghost is the Eastern Peak, the Five Sages and the incense of family forebears; a yin Wood ghost the souls of those who hanged themselves or died of madness, falls and blows, or a beating with the rod. A yang Water ghost is Guanyin, Zhenwu, the Three Officials, the water spirits, the Zhang spirit, the Lord of the River and the Water Official; a yin Water ghost the souls of those who threw themselves into a river, drank brine or vomited blood. A yang Fire ghost is the incense of the Dark Altar, the Five Blessings, the Fire God and the Kitchen Official; a yin Fire ghost the souls of those who died of sores and poison, consumption, epidemic, bleeding, heart pain or burning. A yang Earth ghost is the City God, the Earth and Grain gods, the Tai Sui, the Great King of the Hide Market and earth spirits; a yin Earth ghost the souls of those who died of epidemic, throat disease, swelling, jaundice or being crushed.

> [!warning] 打架
> 要不要把五行落实到具体的神：易隐按阳鬼、阴鬼列了很具体的名目（阳金鬼是庵堂关圣，阳木鬼是东岳五圣，阳水鬼是观音真武三官……），黄金策的旧注也列过（灶君、伤司、河伯江神，LY.ILL.HJC.97）；增删卜易的觉子认为不必把金官指为关公岳公这样的神，要问病人心里疑的是哪位再占一卦（LY.ILL.ZS.24），李我平更批评《易冒》《易林补遗》指定关岳（LY.ILL.ZS.25）。指名派和不指名派，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether to fix the elements to particular named spirits: the Yi Yin gives very specific names by yang and yin ghost (a yang Metal ghost the temples and Guan Sheng, a yang Wood ghost the Eastern Peak and the Five Sages, a yang Water ghost Guanyin, Zhenwu and the Three Officials...), and the old commentary of the Huangjin Ce also lists names (the Kitchen God, the Injuring Office, the Lord of the River and river god, LY.ILL.HJC.97); Juezi of the Zengshan Buyi holds that one should not name Metal Pressure as Guan Gong, Yue Gong and the like but ask which spirit the patient has in mind and divine again (LY.ILL.ZS.24), and Li Woping criticises the Yi Mao and the Yilin Buyi for naming Guan and Yue (LY.ILL.ZS.25). The naming school and the non-naming school are not settled by the books.

> [!note] 按
> 阳鬼是神的一列（庙里的神），阴鬼是鬼的一列（死于什么的魂）。「关圣」是关帝（关羽），「玄坛」是赵公明（财神），「真武」是北方之神，「三官」是天、地、水三官，「五福」「五圣」「张神」「皮场大王」是民间信奉的神，书里没有一一解释，按字面译。「七煞」「在虎七煞」书里没有再解释。「滷」是卤水（盐卤，自尽用）。整理本在「阴火鬼」一句里也有那个生僻的病字的说明。
>
> *Note:* The yang ghosts are the column of spirits (the gods of the temples) and the yin ghosts the column of ghosts (the souls of those who died of one cause or another). 'Guan Sheng' is Emperor Guan (Guan Yu), 'Xuantan' is Zhao Gongming (god of wealth), 'Zhenwu' is the god of the north, the 'Three Officials' are the Officials of Heaven, Earth and Water, and the 'Five Blessings', 'Five Sages', 'Zhang spirit' and 'Great King of the Hide Market' are gods worshipped among the people whom the book does not explain one by one, rendered literally. The 'Seven Killings' and 'with the Tiger the Seven Killings' are not explained further. 'Lu' is brine (salt brine, used to end one's life). In the sentence on the yin Fire ghost the transcriber again notes the rare ailment character.
