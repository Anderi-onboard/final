# 卜筮元龟·占疾病凶卦：丰观需剥节旅贲明夷蛊夬及同人，不问四时及生旺，十死分明

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丰、观、需、剥、节、旅、贲、明夷、蛊、夬，还有同人，不论四季、也不论生旺，「十死」是分明的，不必多说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Feng, Guan, Xu, Bo, Jie, Lü, Bi, Mingyi, Gu, Guai and Tongren: regardless of the four seasons or of strength, the 'ten deaths' are plain and there is no need to say more.

> | 占疾病凶卦云： | 占疾病的凶卦，歌诀说： | On the ominous hexagrams in divining illness it says: |
> | 丰观需剥节旅贲， | 丰、观、需、剥、节、旅、贲， | Feng, Guan, Xu, Bo, Jie, Lü and Bi, |

^q1

> [!warning] 打架
> 哪几个卦占病最凶，几本书的名单不同。这一块是丰、观、需、剥、节、旅、贲、明夷、蛊、夬、同人；阐奥歌章第五首（LY.ILL.CA.06）也是这十一个；孙膑断疾病歌（LY.ILL.DYTJ.06）只有明夷、蛊、剥、夬、丰、同六个；天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.06）是明夷、观、贲、需、临（世身入墓）和大畜、丰、同、蛊、夬（财鬼俱兴）；火珠林一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.17）是同人、丰、夬、蛊、剥、明夷。丰、蛊、夬、同人、明夷几乎处处都在，观、需、贲、旅、剥、节、临、大畜则有的有、有的没有。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which hexagrams are worst in divining illness is listed differently by several books. This block has Feng, Guan, Xu, Bo, Jie, Lü, Bi, Mingyi, Gu, Guai and Tongren; the fifth poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.06) has the same eleven; Sun Bin's song (LY.ILL.DYTJ.06) has only the six Mingyi, Gu, Bo, Guai, Feng and Tong; the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.06) has Mingyi, Guan, Bi, Xu and Lin (the Self and Body entering a tomb) and Daxu, Feng, Tong, Gu and Guai (Wealth and the ghost both rising); the Huozhu Lin passage (LY.ILL.DYTJ.17) has Tongren, Feng, Guai, Gu, Bo and Mingyi. Feng, Gu, Guai, Tongren and Mingyi are almost everywhere, and Guan, Xu, Bi, Lü, Bo, Jie, Lin and Daxu are in some and not in others. The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 名单里有十一个卦（丰、观、需、剥、节、旅、贲、明夷、蛊、夬、同人），而说的是「十死」，「十死」按字面译作 ten deaths，可能是「十有九死」一类的说法，书里没有解释。「不问四时及生旺」是说不看季节、也不看这个卦的旺衰，遇上就是凶。这个名单和别处的凶卦名单大同小异，见 LY.ILL.CA.03 的「打架」。
>
> *Note:* The list has eleven hexagrams (Feng, Guan, Xu, Bo, Jie, Lü, Bi, Mingyi, Gu, Guai, Tongren) while it speaks of 'ten deaths'; 'ten deaths' is rendered literally and may be a saying like 'nine out of ten die', and the book does not explain it. 'Regardless of the four seasons or of strength' says that neither the season nor the strength or weakness of the hexagram is looked at, and meeting one is ominous. This list is much like the lists of ominous hexagrams elsewhere, see the dispute in LY.ILL.CA.03.
