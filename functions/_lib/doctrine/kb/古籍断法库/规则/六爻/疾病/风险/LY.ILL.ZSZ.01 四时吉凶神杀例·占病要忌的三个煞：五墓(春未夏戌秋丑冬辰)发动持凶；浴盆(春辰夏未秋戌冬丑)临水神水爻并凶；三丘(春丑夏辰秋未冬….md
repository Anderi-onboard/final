# 四时吉凶神杀例·占病要忌的三个煞：五墓(春未夏戌秋丑冬辰)发动持凶；浴盆(春辰夏未秋戌冬丑)临水神水爻并凶；三丘(春丑夏辰秋未冬戌)发动兄鬼主死

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 周易尚占旁通部的「四时吉凶神杀例」表列了三个占病要忌的煞，位置按春、夏、秋、冬的次序排：五墓是未、戌、丑、辰，发动、持（世）都凶；浴盆是辰、未、戌、丑，落在水神（玄武）或水爻上都凶；三丘是丑、辰、未、戌，发动、又有兄弟或官鬼，主死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The table of auspicious and ominous spirit-stars of the four seasons in the Parallel Tables of the Zhouyi Shangzhan lists three stars to avoid in asking about illness, set out in the order spring, summer, autumn, winter: the Five Tombs at Wei, Xu, Chou, Chen, ominous when they move or hold the Self; the Bathing Basin at Chen, Wei, Xu, Chou, ominous when it falls on a Water spirit (the Dark Tortoise) or a Water line; the Three Mounds at Chou, Chen, Wei, Xu, which mean death when they move together with Peer or Pressure.

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 394–397 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Parallel Tables, Section 10: Auspicious and Ominous Spirit-Stars of the Four Seasons", base text

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这三行是周易尚占旁通部「十、四时吉凶神杀例」表的末三行，表头是春夏秋冬，每个煞的四个地支按这个次序排。三丘、五墓的位置和断易天机（LY.ILL.DYTJ.31）、易林补遗（LY.ILL.EL.51）一致：三丘春丑夏辰秋未冬戌，五墓取三丘的对宫（春未夏戌秋丑冬辰）。浴盆的位置（春辰夏未秋戌冬丑）和卜筮全书神煞歌例里的沐浴杀（LY.ILL.BSQS.53）、疾病论里的催尸杀（LY.ILL.BSQS.36）一样，书里没有说它们是不是同一个煞；卜筮全书「四般休问病」里的浴盆只点了名、没有给位置（LY.ILL.BSQS.11）。「发动持凶」的「持」书里没有写「世」，译文照一般的用法读作持世，放在方括号里；「临水神水爻并凶」「发动兄鬼主死」书里都没有写主语，译文补成「它」。「水神」按这一部前面「十一、定六神」里的「青龙木神，朱雀火神，勾陈土神，白虎金神，玄武水神」（第 461 行），是玄武；「兄鬼」是兄弟、官鬼。同一部分前面的合刑杀例（六合、三合、三刑、驿马、劫杀、亡神、咸池）不管病，这里不收。
>
> *Note:* These are the last three rows of the table 'Section 10: Auspicious and Ominous Spirit-Stars of the Four Seasons' in the Parallel Tables of the Zhouyi Shangzhan; the header row is spring, summer, autumn, winter, and the four branches of each star follow that order. The positions of the Three Mounds and the Five Tombs agree with the Duanyi Tianji (LY.ILL.DYTJ.31) and the Yilin Buyi (LY.ILL.EL.51): the Three Mounds are Chou in spring, Chen in summer, Wei in autumn, Xu in winter, and the Five Tombs are taken from the opposite palace of the Three Mounds (Wei in spring, Xu in summer, Chou in autumn, Chen in winter). The positions of the Bathing Basin (Chen in spring, Wei in summer, Xu in autumn, Chou in winter) are the same as those of the Bathing killing in the verses of spirit-stars of the Bushi Quanshu (LY.ILL.BSQS.53) and of the Corpse-Urging killing in its essay on illness (LY.ILL.BSQS.36); the books do not say whether they are the same star, and the Bathing Basin in the Bushi Quanshu's 'four kinds not to ask about illness' is only named, with no positions given (LY.ILL.BSQS.11). The 'hold' of 'if it moves or holds, it is ominous' is not followed by 'the Self' in the book; the translation reads it in the usual way as holding the Self, and puts that in square brackets; neither 'falling on a Water spirit or a Water line, both ominous' nor 'if it moves, with Peer or Pressure, it means death' has a subject in the book, and the translation supplies 'it'. 'Water spirit' is, by the earlier section 'Section 11: Fixing the Six Spirits' in the same part, with its 'Azure Dragon is the Wood spirit, Vermilion Bird the Fire spirit, Hook Earth the Earth spirit, White Tiger the Metal spirit, Dark Tortoise the Water spirit' (line 461), the Dark Tortoise; 'Peer or Pressure' stands for the two characters meaning 'brother' and 'ghost'. The table of combining, punishing and killing stars before it in the same part (six combinations, three harmonies, three punishments, post horse, robbery, loss, bathing pool) is not about illness and is not taken here.
