# 推占来情·胎没怀胎犯鬼神：值胎、没二卦多是占孕病，鬼神为祟，或逃窜、失财；囚死之卦必占官司囚系、并犯疾厄及亲戚丧亡

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推占来情的歌说：胎、没两个卦（春天巽、离，夏天坤、兑，秋天乾、坎，冬天艮、震）出现，多半是占怀孕和病，是鬼神作祟，或者逃走、或者失财；凡是囚、死的卦，一定是占官司囚禁的事，并且犯到疾病灾厄和亲戚的丧亡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verses on reading the purpose of the asker say: when the two hexagrams Embryo and Sunk appear (in spring Xun and Li, in summer Kun and Dui, in autumn Qian and Kan, in winter Gen and Zhen), it is mostly a divination of pregnancy or illness, with ghosts and spirits afflicting, or flight, or loss of wealth; a hexagram in the trapped or dead state is surely a divination of lawsuits and imprisonment, and runs into illness, calamity and the death of relatives.

> [!note] 按
> 「胎」「没」是八卦按季节排的旺相休囚的几个状态（这部书前面的八卦旺废表里有「旺相胎没死休囚废」），这里的「胎没二卦」是各季处在胎、没状态的两个卦：春是巽、离，夏是坤、兑，秋是乾、坎，冬是艮、震。「囚死之卦」是处在囚、死状态的卦。「疾厄」是疾病灾厄。「怀胎」译作「the pregnant」，书里没有说是谁的怀胎，按字面。
>
> *Note:* 'Embryo' and 'Sunk' are two of the states of the strength cycle of the eight trigrams by season (the table of the strength and weakness of the eight trigrams earlier in this book has 'thriving, strong, embryo, sunk, dead, resting, trapped, abandoned'); here 'the two hexagrams Embryo and Sunk' are the two trigrams that are in the embryo or sunk state in each season: Xun and Li in spring, Kun and Dui in summer, Qian and Kan in autumn, Gen and Zhen in winter. 'A hexagram in the trapped or dead state' is a trigram in those states. 'Illness and calamity' is the pair illness and misfortune. 'The pregnant' translates the word 'carrying an embryo', and the book does not say whose pregnancy, so it is literal.
