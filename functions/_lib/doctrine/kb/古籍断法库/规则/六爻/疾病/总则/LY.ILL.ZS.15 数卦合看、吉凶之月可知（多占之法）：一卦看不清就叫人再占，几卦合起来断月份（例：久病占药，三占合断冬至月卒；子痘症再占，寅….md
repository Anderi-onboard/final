# 数卦合看、吉凶之月可知（多占之法）：一卦看不清就叫人再占，几卦合起来断月份（例：久病占药，三占合断冬至月卒；子痘症再占，寅日卒；近病逢冲再占问应期，乙酉日全愈）

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一个卦看不清、或者问到应期的时候，不要硬断，叫问卜的人再占几个卦，把几个卦合起来详细看，吉凶发生在哪个月就能看出来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When one hexagram is unclear, or when the time of fulfilment is asked, do not press a judgment: have the asker divine several more hexagrams and read them together in detail, and the month in which good or ill fortune falls can be known.

^q13

> [!note] 按
> 野鹤这一句放在几个卦例之后，说的是「以上数卦」，指从 LY.ILL.ZS.14 里朋友痔病那一例起的几组卦：每一组都是第一卦不敢定，叫亲人或本人再占，合几个卦断月份、日子。「东至月」原文如此，按后一句是「冬至月」。「棺椁」是内棺和外棺；风雷益卦上巽下震，野鹤取震象为棺、巽象为椁。
>
> *Note:* The Old Man puts this sentence after a run of cases, and 'the hexagrams above' means the groups of hexagrams from the case of the friend's piles in LY.ILL.ZS.14 onward: in each group the first hexagram is not decided, the relative or the man himself is told to divine again, and the several hexagrams are put together to judge the month or day. 'Dongzhi month' is written with a wrong character in the original, and from the following sentence it is the month of the winter solstice. A 'coffin and outer coffin' is the inner coffin and the outer one; the Old Man takes the image of Zhen as the coffin and that of Xun as the outer coffin.
