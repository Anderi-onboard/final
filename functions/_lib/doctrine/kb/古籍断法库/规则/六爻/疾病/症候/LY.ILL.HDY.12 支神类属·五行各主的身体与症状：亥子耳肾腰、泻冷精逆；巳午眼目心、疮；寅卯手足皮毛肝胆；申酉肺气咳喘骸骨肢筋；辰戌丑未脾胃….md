# 支神类属·五行各主的身体与症状：亥子耳肾腰、泻冷精逆；巳午眼目心、疮；寅卯手足皮毛肝胆；申酉肺气咳喘骸骨肢筋；辰戌丑未脾胃皮肤、肿腹泻肚、时疫呕吐

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 海底眼的支神类属，每一组地支（五行）有一首七言诗和一段散句，说它在世间管的事物，其中带着身体和症状：亥子（水）是耳、肾、腰，泻冷精逆；巳午（火）是眼目、心、疮；寅卯（木）是手足、皮毛、肝胆；申酉（金）是肺气咳喘、骸骨肢筋、眉头；辰戌丑未（土）是脾胃、皮肤、肿腹泻肚、时疫呕吐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the classes of the branch spirits in the Haidi Yan each group of branches (each phase) has a seven-character poem and a prose passage on what it governs in the world, and these carry the body and its symptoms: Hai and Zi (Water): the ears, kidneys and waist, purging, chill and the essence in reverse; Si and Wu (Fire): the eyes, the heart, sores; Yin and Mao (Wood): hands and feet, skin and hair, liver and gall bladder; Shen and You (Metal): lung qi, coughing and wheezing, bones, limbs and sinews, the brow; Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei (Earth): spleen and stomach, the skin, swelling of the belly and diarrhoea, seasonal epidemics and vomiting.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节的顺序是水（亥子）、火（巳午）、木（寅卯）、金（申酉）、土（辰戌丑未）；每一组先有一首七言诗（诗后带眉批），再有一段散句，这里取的是诗和散句里带身体、症状的几句，别的（山川、人物、器物）不收。身体和脏腑的对应大体和别处一致：水是肾、耳、腰，火是心、眼，木是肝胆、手足，金是肺，土是脾胃（见 LY.ILL.CA.02、YY.11、HJC.16），这里多出的是症状：水「泻冷精逆」，火「疮」，金「咳喘」，土「肿腹泻肚、时疫呕吐」。几处意思不清的：「膏末疮汉眼目心」的「汉」、「师巫卜药负元子」的「元子」、「恒听分论」，书里没有解释，译文照字面。「虡」之类字的注、「眼木（目之误）」这类括号里的校注是整理者加的，不引。「相貌长文」「狩药负子」两处意思不清，这里不收。
>
> *Note:* The order in this section is Water (Hai, Zi), Fire (Si, Wu), Wood (Yin, Mao), Metal (Shen, You), Earth (Chen, Xu, Chou, Wei); each group has first a seven-character poem (with a marginal comment after the poem) and then a prose passage, and what is taken here is the lines of the poem and the prose that carry the body and symptoms; the rest (mountains and rivers, people, objects) is not taken. The correspondence of body and viscera is broadly the same as elsewhere: Water the kidneys, ears and waist, Fire the heart and eyes, Wood liver and gall bladder, hands and feet, Metal the lungs, Earth spleen and stomach (see LY.ILL.CA.02, YY.11, HJC.16); what is added here is symptoms: Water 'purging, chill, the essence in reverse', Fire 'sores', Metal 'coughing and wheezing', Earth 'a swollen belly, diarrhoea, seasonal epidemics and vomiting'. Some places are unclear in sense: the 'han' of 'salve and powder, sores, han, eyes, the eye, the heart', the 'yuan zi' of 'carrying the yuan zi', and 'always listening and sorting out the talk', and the book does not explain them, so the translation is literal. The notes on characters such as 'ju' and the bracketed proofreading notes such as 'eye wood (a miswriting of eye)' were added by the editor and are not quoted. 'Looks, long writings' and 'hunting medicine, carrying a child' are unclear in sense and are not taken here.
