# 问痘见何人：鬼当本命、鬼化卦宫者应之；考六亲化鬼及鬼化六亲，必鬼旺动；官鬼值何人本命则是人有痘，鬼动艮宫少男、化离象中女、财爻化鬼奴仆、鬼化子孩提

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果问是谁出痘，鬼爻正当他的本命的，或者鬼变成（某人所属的）卦宫的，就应在这个人身上。考察哪个六亲变出鬼、鬼变成哪个六亲，就知道出痘的是这个人；但也必须鬼旺又动才会发。一家之内，官鬼正当哪个人的本命，就说这个人有痘；又，鬼动在艮宫，是少男；鬼变成离象，是中女；财爻变鬼，是奴仆；鬼变成子孙，是幼儿；总之要在鬼爻旺又动的前提下才这样说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If asked who will have the pox, it answers on the one whose natal branch the ghost stands on, or on the person whose palace the ghost changes into. Examine which relation changes into the ghost and which relation the ghost changes into, and that is the person who has the pox; but the ghost must also be strong and moving before it breaks out. Within one household, whoever's natal branch the Pressure line stands on, say that person has the pox; again, a ghost moving in the Gen palace is the youngest son; a ghost changing into the Li image, the middle daughter; a Wealth line changing into a ghost, a servant; a ghost changing into Output, an infant; in general one says so only where the Pressure line is strong and moving.

> [!note] 按
> 「本命」是人生年的地支。易冒疾病章后面还有一条同样的说法，「占父病而鬼动于坤，其母随之而疾」（LY.ILL.YM.33），那里也是用鬼动在哪个宫来推还有谁会病。
>
> *Note:* The 'natal branch' is the earthly branch of a person's birth year. Later in the chapter on illness the Yi Mao has a similar saying, 'divining a father's illness with the ghost moving in Kun, his mother follows with the illness' (LY.ILL.YM.33), which likewise uses the palace in which the ghost moves to work out who else will fall ill.
