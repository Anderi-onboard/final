# 按爻位断神鬼：初爻井祟、二爻灶祟、三爻家堂之祟、四爻门户之祟、五爻道路之祟、六爻坟墓之祟；世在初为家神、二为土神、三为园神、四为五神、上为山神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 初爻，是井祟，是井里的鬼，是掌管居住的神；二爻，是灶祟，是灶下的亡魂，是掌管境内城邑的神；三爻，是家堂的祟，是闺房里的鬼，是掌管都郡桥埠的神；四爻，是门户的祟，是关隘的神；五爻，是道路的祟，是王侯的神；六爻，是坟墓的祟，是慈悲的尊神，是奴仆的亡魂。世在初爻的，是家神；世在二爻的，是土神；世在三爻，是园神；世在四爻，是五神；世在上爻，是山神。注说：这是卦里得一个鬼，现在哪一爻，以及世爻带鬼落在什么象上，用六爻的象来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The first line: a haunting by the well, a ghost in the well, a spirit in charge of dwellings; the second: a haunting by the stove, one who died beside the stove, a spirit in charge of the district and its town; the third: a haunting of the household shrine, a ghost of the women's quarters, a spirit in charge of cities, prefectures, bridges and wharves; the fourth: a haunting of the doors, a spirit of passes and defiles; the fifth: a haunting of the road, a spirit of princes and marquises; the sixth: a haunting of the graves, a compassionate honoured one, one who died a servant. With the Self on the first line, a household spirit; on the second, an earth spirit; on the third, a garden spirit; on the fourth, the Five Spirits; on the top, a mountain spirit. The note says: where the hexagram has one ghost, on whichever line it appears, and in whatever image the ghost on the Self lies, judgment by the images of the six lines is as above.

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一条给两张表：一张是鬼落在哪一爻（六条），一张是世爻带鬼时世在哪一爻（五条，三个爻位之外的第五爻没有列）。第一张里「初井、二灶、三家堂、四门户、五道路、六坟墓」是一处住宅由内到外、由近到远的次序，和黄金策的六爻推鬼神（LY.ILL.HJC.101）前三条（井神、灶神/司命、家堂）相同，第五爻的道路也相同；第四爻黄金策讲的是出门撞见，这里是门户与关隘，着眼点不同。「五神」在第二张表里是第四爻的世，书里没有解释是哪五位神。
>
> *Note:* This entry gives two tables: one for the line the ghost falls on (six items), and one for which line the Self is on when the Self carries the ghost (five items, the fifth line not being listed). In the first, 'first the well, second the stove, third the household shrine, fourth the doors, fifth the road, sixth the graves' runs in order through a house from inside out and near to far, and agrees with the Huangjin Ce's reading by the six lines (LY.ILL.HJC.101) in its first three items (the well god, the stove god or Director of Fate, the household shrine) and in the road at the fifth line; for the fourth line the Huangjin Ce speaks of a meeting on going out, and here it is doors and passes, a different angle. The 'Five Spirits' for the Self on the fourth line in the second table are not explained in the book.
