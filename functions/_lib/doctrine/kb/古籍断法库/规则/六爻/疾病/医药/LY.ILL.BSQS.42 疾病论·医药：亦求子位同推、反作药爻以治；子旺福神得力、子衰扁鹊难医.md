# 疾病论·医药：亦求子位同推、反作药爻以治；子旺福神得力、子衰扁鹊难医

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论最后说医药：也要去求子孙的位置一起推，反过来把它当作药爻来治；子孙旺，福神就得力；子孙衰，连扁鹊也难医；还要深入详察整个卦象怎么样，察看吉凶休咎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness ends on medicine: also seek the Output place and work it out together, and in turn take it as the medicine line for treatment; if Output is strong the spirit of fortune is effective, if Output is weak even Bian Que finds it hard to cure; and examine deeply how the whole image stands, observing good and ill fortune.

> [!note] 按
> 子孙爻在占病里有两重身份：克官鬼（病），所以是「福神」；又是药，所以是「药爻」。这一段和断易天机的孙真人论医歌、洞林秘诀里「卦中子孙爻为药」是同一个说法（LY.ILL.DYTJ.45、LY.ILL.DYTJ.47）。扁鹊是战国时的名医。
>
> *Note:* In divining illness the Output line has two roles: it controls Pressure (the illness), which is why it is the 'spirit of fortune'; and it is the medicine, which is why it is the 'medicine line'. This passage says the same as Master Sun's song on doctors and the Dongling Mijue in the Duanyi Tianji, 'the Output line in the hexagram is the medicine' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.45, LY.ILL.DYTJ.47). Bian Que was a famous physician of the Warring States.
