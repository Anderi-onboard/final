# 神杀断例·病遇天医瘥

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神杀断例说：占病遇到天医，病会痊愈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse of examples on judging by spirit-stars says: in divining illness, meeting the Heavenly Physician, the illness is cured.

## 《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·二十二、神杀断例》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3163 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses, no. 22: examples of judging by spirit-stars", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 病遇天医瘥， | 占病遇到天医，病就痊愈， | In divining illness, meeting the Heavenly Physician, the illness is cured, |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「瘥」是病愈。天医是一个神煞，按月定位（正月卯、二月亥……）；断易天机有完整的月表（LY.ILL.DYTJ.30、LY.ILL.DYTJ.34），卜筮全书的神煞歌例里也有（LY.ILL.BSQS.48）。这里只有一句结论，没有位置。同一句的下一句「囚因血忌刑」讲的是囚犯，不收。
>
> *Note:* 'Chai' means recovery from illness. The Heavenly Physician is a spirit-star set by month (Mao in the first month, Hai in the second, and so on); the Duanyi Tianji gives the full monthly table (LY.ILL.DYTJ.30, LY.ILL.DYTJ.34), and the verses on spirit-stars in the Bushi Quanshu have it too (LY.ILL.BSQS.48). Here there is only the conclusion, without the positions. The next line, 'a prisoner, because of the Blood Taboo, is punished', is about prisoners and is not taken.
