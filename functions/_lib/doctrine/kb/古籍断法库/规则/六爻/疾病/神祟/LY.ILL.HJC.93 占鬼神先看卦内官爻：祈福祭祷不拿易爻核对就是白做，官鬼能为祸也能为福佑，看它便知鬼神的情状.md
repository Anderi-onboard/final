# 占鬼神先看卦内官爻：祈福祭祷不拿易爻核对就是白做，官鬼能为祸也能为福佑，看它便知鬼神的情状

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 向鬼神求福，是现在人们崇尚的事，古时候也是这样。可是不拿易的爻来核对，祭祀也是白办。占的时候，先看卦里的官爻，鬼神是什么情形就知道了。官鬼既能带来实实在在的灾祸，也能带来福气和保佑，所以看它就能知道鬼神的情形。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking blessing from ghosts and spirits is what people of the present day esteem, and it has been so since ancient times. But if it is not checked against the lines of the Yi, even the sacrificial rites are performed in vain. In divining, look first at the Pressure line in the hexagram and you will know the condition of the ghost or spirit. The Pressure line can bring real disaster and can bring blessing and protection, so looking at it tells you the condition.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「徼」是求（卜筮大全篇末的生僻字注）。旧注写「实祸」「福佑」，王洪绪写「祸福」，意思一样。《黄金策》有一个本子在这一章标着「存赋不注」，只有赋文；旧注见卜筮大全，王洪绪的注见卜筮正宗。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiao' means to seek (the book's own glossary of rare characters at the end of the chapter). The old commentary writes 'real disaster' and 'blessing and protection' where Wang Hongxu writes 'harm and blessing'; the meaning is the same. One edition of the Huangjin Ce marks this chapter 'the verse kept, no commentary', giving only the verse; the old commentary is found in the Bushi Daquan and Wang Hongxu's commentary in the Bushi Zhengzong.
