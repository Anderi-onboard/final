# 医问道行：财官强而利名遂；医药不精不足以利物、求医不慎不足以疗生，己弗误人亦弗自误

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 医生问自己行医的前程：财爻、官爻强，名和利就能遂愿。所以医术不精，不足以造福万物；求医不谨慎，不足以救人性命；自己不耽误别人，也不耽误自己。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a doctor asks about the course of his practice, if the Wealth and Pressure lines are strong, profit and fame are achieved. So medicine that is not refined cannot benefit things, and seeking a doctor without care cannot heal life; if one does not mislead others, one does not mislead oneself.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「财官强而利名遂」是本条唯一的断法：财主利，官主名。后半句「故医药不精……」是章末的劝诫，对医生也对病家说：医生的技术不精，不能救人；病家选医生不小心，也救不了命。「财官」这里是财爻和官爻，原文没有写「妻财」「官鬼」，但意思相同。
>
> *Note:* 'Wealth and Pressure strong, profit and fame are achieved' is the only judgment in this entry: Wealth governs profit and Pressure governs fame. The second half, 'so medicine that is not refined...', is the chapter's closing admonition, addressed to the doctor and to the patient's family alike: a doctor whose skill is poor cannot save a life, and a family that chooses a doctor carelessly cannot save it either. 'Wealth and Pressure' here are the Wealth line and the Pressure line, written without 'wife-wealth' or 'official-ghost' but meaning the same.
