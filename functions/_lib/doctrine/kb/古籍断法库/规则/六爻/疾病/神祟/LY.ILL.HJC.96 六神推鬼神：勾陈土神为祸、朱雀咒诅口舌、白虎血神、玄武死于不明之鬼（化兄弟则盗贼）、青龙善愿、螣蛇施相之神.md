# 六神推鬼神：勾陈土神为祸、朱雀咒诅口舌、白虎血神、玄武死于不明之鬼（化兄弟则盗贼）、青龙善愿、螣蛇施相之神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 按鬼爻带的六神推：带勾陈，是土神在作祸；带朱雀，是有人咒骂诅咒；带白虎，是血神，有带血的女伤，在阳爻上是凶暴恶死的鬼；带玄武，是死得不明不白的鬼，变成兄弟或从兄弟变出来的，是盗贼；带青龙，是善愿；带螣蛇，是施相之神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Work it out by the six spirits the Pressure line carries. With the Hook Earth, an earth spirit is making trouble; with the Vermilion Bird, curses and imprecations; with the White Tiger, the blood spirit, a woman's injury with blood, and on a yang line a violent ghost who died an ugly death; with the Dark Tortoise, a ghost who died in an unclear way, and if it changes into Peer or has come out of Peer, a thief or bandit; with the Azure Dragon, a good vow; with the Coiling Snake, the spirit of Shixiang.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3377 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 此以六神推之。 | 这是按六神来推。 | This is worked out by the six spirits. |
> | 勾陈职专田土，鬼爻临之，乃是土神为祸类。 | 勾陈专管田地土壤，鬼爻带着它，就是土神作祸这一类。 | The Hook Earth is charged with fields and soil; when the Pressure line carries it, it is of the kind where an earth spirit makes trouble. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注把六神一个一个讲了，王洪绪只讲了勾陈一例。「带血女伤」书里没有再解释，按字面译。「施相」书里没有解释；旧注说现在民间酬谢螣蛇这一路神，用面捏一条小蛇献上，所以译成人名式的「施相之神」。王洪绪引经文时，「螣蛇则犯乎施相之神」写作「螣蛇则犯乎施相之人」，「白虎血亡」写作「白虎血神」；这几处字面不同，书里没有说哪个对。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary takes the six spirits one by one; Wang Hongxu treats only the Hook Earth. 'A woman's injury with blood' is not explained further in the book and is rendered literally. 'Shixiang' is not explained; the old commentary says that in present custom thanks to this spirit are given with a small snake moulded of dough, so it is rendered as a name. When Wang Hongxu quotes the verse he writes 'the Coiling Snake offends the person of Shixiang' for 'the spirit of Shixiang', and 'the White Tiger the blood spirit' for 'the White Tiger death by blood'; the books do not say which is right.
