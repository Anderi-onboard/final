# 金鬼的来历：生前好勇（性属金），或亡于干戈锋镝、病丧于喉肺、为义为利，是成鬼之因；成鬼能善则上帝命其所司，位西方、权秋令、主兵事，按金象考

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生前好勇的鬼，是就它的性情说，所以属金；或者死在兵器锋镝之下，以及病死在喉、肺，为义、为利而死，这是就它怎样成鬼说，所以也归金类。成了鬼又能行善的，上帝命它掌管的事，如果位在西方、权在秋令、主管兵事，就按金象来考察。注说：这是察看鬼神的来历。金鬼一定生前好勇，就性情说，它死在刀兵之下；就它成为鬼神说，这位神掌管什么事，可能属水、属火、属土、属金、属木；就所掌管的属性说，比如关帝、岳王的神勇和义属金，七煞、丧煞，体属金、掌管杀伐，也属金。遁甲里的七煞是庚，这就叫体属金。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A ghost who loved courage in life is Metal, speaking of its nature; or one who died under weapons and arrowheads, or of illness of the throat or lungs, or over righteousness or profit, is spoken of as how it became a ghost, and so is also classed as Metal. One who, having become a ghost, can do good, and whom the Supreme Lord has appointed to a charge, if its seat is in the west, its authority in the autumn season and its charge in military affairs, is examined by the Metal image. The note says: this is to examine where the ghost or spirit came from. A Metal ghost must have loved courage in life; by nature, that ghost died by blade and weapon; as to its becoming a ghost or spirit, what this spirit governs may be Water, Fire, Earth, Metal or Wood; as to what its charge belongs to, for example, the valour and righteousness of Guan and Yue belong to Metal, and the Seven Killings and the Mourning Killing, whose body is Metal and whose charge is killing, belong to Metal. The Seven Killings of the Dunjia is Geng, which is what is meant by a body of Metal.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一条讲的是 LY.ILL.YM.42 里「性、司、成」三样怎么用。「性」是生前的性情（好勇），「成」是怎么成鬼的（死于刀兵），「司」是成神以后上帝派它管什么（西方、秋令、兵事）；三样各自可以属金，也可以三样属的不一样（注里说「所司」可以属水火土金木任何一行）。「体金」是本体属金：七煞在遁甲里是庚，庚是金，所以七煞体金；关、岳靠神勇与义归金，是靠所司的性质。
>
> *Note:* This entry shows how the three of 'nature, charge and origin' in LY.ILL.YM.42 are used. 'Nature' is the disposition in life (love of courage), 'origin' is how it became a ghost (died by blade), and 'charge' is what the Supreme Lord set it to govern after it became a spirit (the west, autumn, military affairs); each of the three can be Metal, or the three can belong to different phases (the note says the 'charge' may belong to any of Water, Fire, Earth, Metal or Wood). 'Body of Metal' means the substance is Metal: the Seven Killings in the Dunjia is Geng, and Geng is Metal, so the Seven Killings has a body of Metal, while Guan and Yue go under Metal through the nature of their charge, valour and righteousness.
