# 六亲章·杀神朱雀同时并重病如何得起床；妻犯勾陈妻病殃

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六亲章里讲病的两处：父母爻带着杀神，又和朱雀同时并在一起，重病怎么还能起得了床；妻财犯了勾陈，妻子有病灾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Two places on illness in the chapter on the six relations: when the Resource line carries a killing spirit and is joined by the Vermilion Bird, how can a grave illness ever rise from bed; when Wealth offends Hook Earth, the wife has illness and misfortune.

## 《卜筮全书·卜筮歌诀·六亲章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3641 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Bushi Quanshu verses: the chapter on the six relations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财临朱雀妻贤慧， | 财爻临朱雀，妻子贤惠聪明； | Wealth on the Vermilion Bird: the wife is worthy and clever; |
> | 妻犯勾陈妻病殃。 | 妻财犯了勾陈，妻子有病灾。 | Wealth offending Hook Earth: the wife has illness and misfortune. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这首六亲章是一段很长的歌，从父母一直讲到兄弟，这里只收和病有关的两处。「杀神朱雀同时并」里的杀神泛指凶煞，歌里没有点名；这一联接在讲父母爻的几句后面，所以译文按「父母爻带着杀神」读，歌没有明写主语。婚姻域收过第二处（LY.MARRY.BSQS.10）。
>
> *Note:* This poem on the six relations is long, running from Resource to Peer, and only the two places bearing on illness are taken here. The 'killing spirit' in 'a killing spirit and the Vermilion Bird together' is a general word for ill-omened stars and the verse names none; the couplet follows the lines on the Resource line, so the translation reads it as 'the Resource line carrying a killing spirit', though the verse does not write the subject. The second place was taken in the marriage domain (LY.MARRY.BSQS.10).
