# 孙膑断疾病歌(二)：一爻五脏、二皮肉、三体骨腰并足、四肺心经、五六头；游魂恍惚如神触、归魂渐向安；乾首坤腹、坎耳震足巽肠、艮手兑口离目，鬼在其中即患伤

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一爻管五脏，二爻管皮肉，三爻管体骨、腰和足，四爻管肺和心经，五、六爻管头。游魂卦人恍惚，像被神鬼触到；只有归魂卦渐渐向安。再看八卦：乾是头，坤是腹，坎是耳，震是足，巽是肠，艮是手，兑是口，离是目；鬼在哪一卦里，那一处就生病受伤，时下的术士要仔细推它落在哪里。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The first line governs the five viscera, the second skin and flesh, the third body, bones, waist and feet, the fourth the lungs and heart channel, the fifth and sixth the head. In a Wandering Soul hexagram the person is dazed, as if touched by spirits; only a Returning Soul hexagram gradually turns to peace. Then the trigrams: Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Zhen the feet, Xun the bowels, Gen the hands, Dui the mouth and Li the eyes; whichever of them the ghost is in, that part is ill and injured, and the diviners of the day must carefully work out where it comes to rest.

> [!warning] 打架
> 八卦各管身体哪一处，几处说法不同。孙膑歌这里是「乾首、坤腹、坎耳、震足、巽肠、艮手、兑口、离目」；阐奥歌章（LY.ILL.CA.02）是巽手、艮鼻，其余同；同一章卜筮元龟一段（LY.ILL.DYTJ.12）是巽股、艮手；卜筮正宗痘疹章（LY.ILL.BSZZ.23）也是巽股、艮手。乾首、坤腹、坎耳、离目、兑口、震足几家一致；巽有手、肠、股三说，艮有鼻、手两说。书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which part of the body each of the eight trigrams governs is put differently in several places. Here Sun Bin's song has 'Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Zhen the feet, Xun the bowels, Gen the hands, Dui the mouth, Li the eyes'; the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.02) has Xun the hands and Gen the nose, the rest the same; the Bushi Yuangui passage in the same chapter (LY.ILL.DYTJ.12) has Xun the thighs and Gen the hands; and the smallpox chapter of the Bushi Zhengzong (LY.ILL.BSZZ.23) likewise has Xun the thighs and Gen the hands. Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Li the eyes, Dui the mouth and Zhen the feet are agreed; Xun has three readings (hands, bowels, thighs) and Gen two (nose, hands). The books do not settle it.

> [!note] 按
> 头三句和阐奥歌章的第二首疾病歌（LY.ILL.CA.04）一字不差。「游魂」「归魂」是卦的名称（游魂卦、归魂卦），「恍惚」是神志不清。「时师」按「时下的术士」译。这里的八卦部位是乾头、坤腹、坎耳、震足、巽肠、艮手、兑口、离目，巽配的是肠；别处巽配手或配股，见「打架」。
>
> *Note:* The first three lines are word for word the same as the second illness poem of the Chan'ao Gezhang (LY.ILL.CA.04). 'Wandering Soul' and 'Returning Soul' are names of hexagrams (the Wandering Soul hexagram and the Returning Soul hexagram), and 'dazed' is clouded consciousness. 'Shi shi' is rendered 'the diviners of the day'. The trigram parts here are Qian the head, Kun the belly, Kan the ears, Zhen the feet, Xun the bowels, Gen the hands, Dui the mouth and Li the eyes, with Xun matched to the bowels; elsewhere Xun is matched to the hands or the thighs, see the dispute.
