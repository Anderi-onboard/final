# 鬼在震宫病在脚：加勾陈足肿、加白虎折伤，土鬼化木是脚气，木鬼酸麻、水鬼湿气、火鬼疮毒、金鬼骨节痛或刀伤

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼在震宫，病在脚上：加勾陈，脚一定肿；加白虎，一定摔断打伤、破损。土鬼变成木，得脚气；木鬼是酸痛麻木，水鬼是湿气，火鬼主疮毒，金鬼是踝、膝疼痛和骨头痛，或者被刀刃所伤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line in the Zhen palace puts the illness in the foot: with Hook Earth added the foot surely swells, with the White Tiger it is surely broken, wounded or torn. An Earth Pressure changing into Wood is beriberi; a Wood Pressure is aching, soreness and numbness, a Water Pressure is damp, a Fire Pressure means sores and toxin, and a Metal Pressure is pain in the ankle joints, the knees and the bones, or a wound from a blade.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这里的「脚气」是旧医书说的脚气病，多指腿脚又肿又麻、软弱无力，不是今天说的脚癣。「脚骱」是踝关节（骱是骨节相接的地方）。震宫主脚，和 LY.ILL.HJC.04 里王洪绪的写法一致。
>
> *Note:* The 'jiaoqi' here is the old medical disease of that name, which mostly means legs and feet that are swollen and numb, weak and without strength, not what is now called athlete's foot. 'Jiaojie' is the ankle joint (a 'jie' is the place where bones meet). The Zhen palace governing the foot agrees with the way Wang Hongxu writes it in LY.ILL.HJC.04.
