# 官化官病变不一：医药卦中官鬼化官鬼病必有更变，症候不一、病势不定，用药不见效；王洪绪说是官爻化进神，化退神反此断

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占医药，遇到官鬼变官鬼，病一定有变化：症候不一样，或者病势不稳定，用药也不见效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a medicine hexagram, when Pressure changes into Pressure, the illness surely changes: the symptoms are not uniform, or the course of the illness is unsettled, and medicine also has no effect.

## 《黄金策·医药》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7000 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Doctors and medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官化官，病变不一； | 官鬼变官鬼，病的变化不止一种； | Pressure changing into Pressure: the illness changes in more than one way; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2430 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 此言官爻化进神症候不一，或病势不定，化退神反此断。 | 这是说官爻变进神，症候不一样，或者病势不稳定，变退神就反过来断。 | This says that when the Pressure line changes into an Advancing spirit the symptoms are not uniform or the course is unsettled, and with a Retreating spirit judge the reverse. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 「官化官」指什么变化：旧注泛指官鬼变官鬼，病必有更变；王洪绪限定成官爻化进神（症候不一、病势不定），化退神则反过来断。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What change 'Pressure changing into Pressure' is: the old commentary means Pressure changing into Pressure in general, and the illness must change; Wang Hongxu limits it to the Pressure line changing into an Advancing spirit (symptoms not uniform, course unsettled), and a change into a Retreating spirit is judged the reverse.

> [!note] 按
> 王洪绪这一段同时注了「官化官」和「子化子」两句经文，前半在这一条，后半在下一条（LY.ILL.HJC.65）。疾病章里另有一条讲官化官、鬼化鬼（LY.ILL.HJC.09）。
>
> *Note:* Wang Hongxu's paragraph comments on both the verse 'Pressure changing into Pressure' and the verse 'Output changing into Output'; the first half is in this entry and the second in the next (LY.ILL.HJC.65). The illness chapter has a separate entry on Pressure changing into Pressure (LY.ILL.HJC.09).
