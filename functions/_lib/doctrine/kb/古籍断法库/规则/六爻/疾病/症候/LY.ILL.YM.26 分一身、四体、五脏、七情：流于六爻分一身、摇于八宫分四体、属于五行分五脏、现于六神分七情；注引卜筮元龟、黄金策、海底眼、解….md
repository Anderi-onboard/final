# 分一身、四体、五脏、七情：流于六爻分一身、摇于八宫分四体、属于五行分五脏、现于六神分七情；注引卜筮元龟、黄金策、海底眼、解云为证

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼落在哪一爻，分的是一身的哪一处；鬼动在哪一宫（哪一卦），分的是四肢百体的哪一部分；鬼属哪一行，分的是哪个脏；鬼上的是哪个六神，分的是哪种情绪（七情）。注引了四家为证：卜筮元龟按爻位，从初爻的脚到六爻的头；黄金策按八卦，乾头坤腹、巽腰震足、兑口艮背、坎耳离目；海底眼按五行和地支；解云按六神：白虎是兵祸丧痛、青龙是酒色风流、玄武是忧愁思虑、勾陈是夹带怀胎、朱雀是恼怒焦烦、螣蛇是惊惶恐怖。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* By which of the six lines the ghost flows through, the part of the whole body is told; by which of the eight palaces it stirs in, the four limbs and trunk; by which of the five phases it belongs to, which of the viscera; by which of the six spirits it appears in, which of the seven emotions. The note quotes four authorities: the Bushi Yuangui by line position, from the foot on the first line to the head on the sixth; the Huangjin Ce by trigram, Qian the head and Kun the belly, Xun the waist and Zhen the foot, Dui the mouth and Gen the back, Kan the ear and Li the eye; the Haidi Yan by the five phases and branches; and the explanation by the six spirits: the White Tiger is war, mourning and grief, the Azure Dragon wine, lust and dissipation, the Dark Tortoise worry and anxious thought, Hook Earth complication and pregnancy, the Vermilion Bird anger and vexation, the Coiling Snake fright and terror.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条经文只有一句总纲，分身体的实际办法全在注里，注引了四家：《卜筮元龟》按爻位，《黄金策》按八卦（与 LY.ILL.HJC.04 相同），《海底眼》按五行地支，「解云」按六神。「六爻头上患为算戗（？）」的「算戗」二字，原文自己在后面标了问号，是转录者对这两个字没有把握，这里照录，不改。「有为火鬼病于心」的「有为」疑是「又为」之误，译作「又有」。「元武」是「玄武」避讳的写法。与易隐（LY.ILL.YY.02、YY.11–YY.13）、黄金策（LY.ILL.HJC.02–HJC.05）讲的是同一件事，各家配法略有出入，以各条为准。
>
> *Note:* In this entry the text gives only a single sentence of outline, and the actual ways of dividing the body are all in the note, which quotes four authorities: the Bushi Yuangui by line position, the Huangjin Ce by trigram (the same as LY.ILL.HJC.04), the Haidi Yan by the five phases and branches, and the 'explanation' by the six spirits. In 'the sixth line, trouble in the head, counted as qiang', the two characters 'suan qiang' are followed in the original by a question mark of the transcriber's own, showing doubt about them, and are copied as they stand. In the sentence about a Fire ghost's illness in the heart, the opening 'yǒu wéi' looks like an error for 'yòu wéi' (also), and is rendered 'there is also'. 'Yuan wu' is the taboo-avoiding form of 'Xuan wu' (Dark Tortoise). The same matter is treated by the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.02, YY.11–YY.13) and the Huangjin Ce (LY.ILL.HJC.02–HJC.05), with slightly different assignments in each; go by each entry.
