# 病因·六神：鬼加青龙动男酒色过度或探贵问亲因财而得、女因喜得疾、逢空堕胎；加朱雀动怒气口舌咒咀（二爻灶下、三爻当门、五爻香火道路、金鬼敲锅）；加勾陈动跌打伤失饥伤饱脾胃病（加木动因修造）；加螣蛇动忧愁惊怖思虑伤神或遇妖邪；加白虎动斗殴损伤或丧葬刀兵宰杀之场；加玄武动色欲过度忍饥冒雨或失物被盗

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看病是怎么得的，按鬼爻带的六神：带青龙发动，男的是酒色过度，或者去探望贵人、问候亲戚，因为钱财而得病，女的是因为喜事得病，遇空是堕胎；带朱雀发动，是生气、口舌，或者被人咒骂诅咒得病，在二爻的是灶边咒骂，在三爻的是当门咒骂，在五爻的是香火下、道路上咒骂，金鬼带朱雀发动的是敲着锅咒骂；带勾陈发动，是跌打损伤、饥饱失常，伤了脾胃，勾陈的鬼再加木发动，是因为修造得病；带螣蛇发动，是忧愁惊吓、思虑伤神，或者遇到妖邪得病；带白虎发动，是斗殴受伤，或者在丧葬、刀兵、宰杀的场合得病；带玄武发动，是色欲过度、忍饥冒雨，或者丢了东西被盗而得病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* How the illness was caught, by the six spirits the ghost carries. With the Dragon and moving: in a man from excess of wine and women, or from visiting a noble or calling on kin, caught over money; in a woman from a joyful event, and if Void a miscarriage. With the Vermilion Bird and moving: from anger and quarrels, or from being cursed — on the second line, cursing at the stove; on the third, at the door; on the fifth, beneath the incense-fire or on the road; a Metal ghost with the Vermilion Bird, cursing while banging a pot. With the Hook Earth and moving: injury from falls and blows, hunger and overeating out of turn, an ailment of the spleen and stomach, and a Hook Earth ghost with Wood added and moving is caught from building work. With the Coiling Snake and moving: worry and fright, thought that harms the spirit, or meeting a demon or evil. With the Tiger and moving: injury from brawling, or caught at a funeral, in a scene of blades and weapons, or of slaughter. With the Dark Tortoise and moving: excess of lust, enduring hunger, braving rain, or losing things to theft.

> [!note] 按
> 经文的「加龙」「加雀」「加勾」「加蛇」「加虎」「加武」是加青龙、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武。黄金策讲六神临鬼各主一症（LY.ILL.HJC.05：螣蛇心惊、青龙酒色过度、勾陈肿胀、朱雀言语颠狂、白虎损伤血崩血晕、玄武忧郁阴虚），那里讲的是症状，这里讲的是怎么得病的起因；青龙都是酒色，螣蛇都是惊怖，白虎都是损伤，是相通的。
>
> *Note:* The verse's 'adding the Dragon, the Bird, the Hook, the Snake, the Tiger and the Tortoise' is adding the Azure Dragon, Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth, Coiling Snake, White Tiger and Dark Tortoise. The Huangjin Ce treats the six spirits on the ghost as each governing a symptom (LY.ILL.HJC.05: Coiling Snake fright of heart, Azure Dragon excess of wine and women, Hook Earth swelling, Vermilion Bird raving speech, White Tiger injury and hemorrhage, Dark Tortoise gloom and yin deficiency); there it speaks of symptoms and here of the cause of catching the illness; the Dragon is wine and women in both, the Snake fright in both and the Tiger injury in both, so they are in accord.
