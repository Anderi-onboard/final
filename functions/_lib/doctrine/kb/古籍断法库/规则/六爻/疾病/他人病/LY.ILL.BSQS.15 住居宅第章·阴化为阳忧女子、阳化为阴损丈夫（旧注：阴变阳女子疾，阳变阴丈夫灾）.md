# 住居宅第章·阴化为阳忧女子、阳化为阴损丈夫（旧注：阴变阳女子疾，阳变阴丈夫灾）

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占家宅的歌里说：动爻从阴变阳，女子有忧（旧注：女子有病）；从阳变阴，损伤男子（旧注：男子有灾）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verse on dwellings says: when a moving line turns from yin into yang, there is worry for the woman (the old note: the woman is ill); when it turns from yang into yin, the man is harmed (the old note: the man has calamity).

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3580 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 阳变阴，丈夫灾。 | 阳变阴，男子有灾。 | yang changing to yin, the man has calamity. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 歌只说「忧」「损」，是笼统的担忧和损伤；夹在歌句中间的两条旧注把它写成「女子疾」「丈夫灾」，所以放在疾病域。「阴化为阳」是动爻本来是阴爻、变出来是阳爻，这里的「阴」「阳」指爻，不是卦。旧注「丈夫灾」里的灾不一定是病，译文照字面。断易通玄赋里有相近的一联（阳动男人滞、阴动女人殃，LY.ILL.BSQS.23），那一联讲的是动爻本身的阴阳，这一条讲的是变爻的阴阳，不要混。
>
> *Note:* The verse says only 'worry' and 'harm', a general worry and harm; the two old notes set between its lines put it as 'the woman is ill' and 'the man has calamity', which is why it is placed in the illness domain. 'A yin line turning into yang' means the moving line was a yin line and what it changes into is a yang line; 'yin' and 'yang' here refer to lines, not to hexagrams. The 'calamity' in the note 'the man has calamity' is not necessarily illness, and the translation keeps it literal. The Duanyi Tongxuan Fu has a related couplet (a yang line moving: the man is held up; a yin line moving: the woman has misfortune, LY.ILL.BSQS.23); that couplet is about the yin or yang of the moving line itself and this one about the yin or yang of the changed line, so they should not be mixed.
