# 占医药·青龙临用爻或福德爻病重终可疗、青龙空亡卦无吉解病凶；白虎临父母当损、值财妻遭伤、子孙际遇终成否、兄弟逢之亦不昌、更并官爻临世上自身须忌灾殃

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙临在用爻或福德爻上，病虽重，终究可治；青龙空亡、卦里又没有吉的化解，病凶。白虎临父母，父母当受损；若落在财上，妻子遭伤；遇上子孙，终究成否难定；兄弟遇上它，也不昌盛。再加官爻临在世上，自己就要忌灾殃。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Azure Dragon sitting on the Subject line or the Output line, the illness, though heavy, can in the end be treated; with the Azure Dragon Void and the hexagram having no lucky resolution, the illness is ominous. The White Tiger sitting on Resource harms it; if it falls on Wealth the wife is injured; meeting Output, success in the end is uncertain; and Peer meeting it does not flourish either. And with the Pressure line also sitting on the Self, one must guard against calamity for oneself.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段是天玄赋的注里引的「苍屏云」那首诗（LY.ILL.TXF.04）的另一份：「青龙空亡并福德，病人平日无阴骘……白虎加临主哭丧，临克父爻当有咎，并财妻位必遭伤。子孙际会终成否，兄弟相逢更不昌，更值官爻持世上，己身须恐有灾殃」——这里「白虎临父母当损，若值财上妻遭伤，子孙际遇终成否，兄弟逢之亦不昌，更并官爻临世上，自身须忌有灾殃」几句几乎一样，只是没有「苍屏云」三个字，也没有那一首前面的「青龙」几句。「终成否」按「终究成不成（难定）」译，原文没有说结果。「福德爻」译作 Output line。
>
> *Note:* This passage is another copy of the poem quoted in the Tianxuan Fu's note as 'Cangping says' (LY.ILL.TXF.04): 'the Azure Dragon Void together with Output, the sick person has no hidden merit in ordinary days ... the White Tiger added means mourning, controlling the Resource line there will be harm, joining the Wealth position the wife will surely be injured; Output meeting it, success in the end is uncertain, Peer meeting it flourishes even less, and if in addition the Pressure line holds the Self, one must fear calamity for oneself'. Here 'the White Tiger sitting on Resource harms it, if it falls on Wealth the wife is injured, Output meeting it, success in the end is uncertain, Peer meeting it does not flourish either, and with the Pressure line also sitting on the Self, one must guard against calamity for oneself' is almost the same, only without the words 'Cangping says' and without the Azure Dragon lines before it in that poem. 'Zhong cheng fou' is rendered 'success in the end is uncertain', and the original does not say the outcome. 'Fu de yao' is rendered the Output line.
