# 还赛神祗求保佑：大要生身旺子孙；五世游魂还未尽、伤身鬼旺祸难分；福德动吉、以亲爻取

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 还愿、酬谢神祇来求保佑，最要紧的是生扶自己、子孙旺相；五世卦、游魂卦里（事）还没有了结，鬼旺伤了身，祸就难分。眉批：五世、游魂没有完尽，鬼旺伤身，祸难分。福德发动是吉，要用（所问）亲人的爻来取。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In redeeming vows and giving thanks to the gods to ask for protection, what matters most is generating the body and Output being strong; in the fifth-generation and Wandering Soul hexagrams the matter is still not finished, and a strong ghost wounding the body makes the misfortune hard to divide. The marginal comment: in the fifth-generation and Wandering Soul hexagrams it is not yet finished, and a strong ghost wounding the body makes the misfortune hard to divide. Output moving is auspicious, and it is taken from the line of the relative (asked about).

> [!note] 按
> 这一节只有一首歌诀和一句话，讲还愿、还赛神明：要紧的是「生身旺子孙」——子孙是福神、能压鬼，和易林补遗「还赛解厄」（LY.ILL.EL.57）「世旺子强官受制」是同一个判据。「大要生身旺子孙」的「生身」按字面译作「generating the body」，主语没有交代（可能是生扶世爻）；「五世游魂还未尽」的「五世」「游魂」是卦的名称（五世卦、游魂卦），书里没有解释为什么这两种卦「未尽」。「以亲爻取」按「用所问的亲人的爻来取」译，书里没有解释。 括号里「眉批：……」是书眉上的旁注，整理本排在正文里；这里分开收，说话人标为「海底眼眉批」。
>
> *Note:* This section has one verse and one sentence, about redeeming vows and giving thanks to the gods: what matters is 'generating the body and Output strong' — Output is the fortune spirit and can press down the ghost, the same criterion as the Yilin Buyi's 'redeeming vows and lifting calamity' (LY.ILL.EL.57), 'the Self strong, Output strong, the Pressure line held in check'. In 'generating the body and Output strong' the 'generating the body' is rendered literally, its subject not being given (it may be supporting the Self line); the 'fifth generation' and 'Wandering Soul' are names of hexagrams (the fifth-generation and Wandering Soul hexagrams), and the book does not explain why these two kinds are 'not finished'. 'Take it from the relative's line' is translated as 'take it from the line of the relative asked about', which the book does not explain. The bracketed 'marginal comment' is a note from the top margin that the edited text sets in the main text; it is quoted separately here, with the speaker marked as the marginal comment.
