# 财爻动在上卦主吐、动在下卦主泻：被合住则欲吐不吐、欲泻不泻，是呕逆恶心、里急后重

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻在卦中动：动在上卦，主呕吐；动在下卦，主腹泻。如果被合住，就想吐吐不出、想泻泻不出，像呕逆恶心、里急后重一类。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Wealth line moves in the hexagram: moving in the upper trigram it means vomiting, and in the lower trigram diarrhoea. If it is held by a combination, he wants to vomit and cannot, wants to purge and cannot, as in retching and nausea, or urgency with straining at stool.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「里急后重」是肚子里急着要排、排的时候又觉得下坠不尽。旧注用它和呕逆恶心一起，说明被合住时那种「想而不出」的样子。这一条的上卦、下卦，按字面是外卦、内卦。
>
> *Note:* 'Urgency with straining' (liji houzhong) is a pressing need in the belly to go, with a feeling of sinking and incomplete passing when it does. The old commentary uses it along with retching and nausea to show the state of 'wanting and not managing' when the line is held by a combination. The upper and lower trigram of this entry are, in the letter, the outer and inner trigram.
