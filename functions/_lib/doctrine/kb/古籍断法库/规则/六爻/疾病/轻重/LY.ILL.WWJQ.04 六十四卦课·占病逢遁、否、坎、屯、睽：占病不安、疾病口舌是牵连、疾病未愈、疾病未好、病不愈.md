# 六十四卦课·占病逢遁、否、坎、屯、睽：占病不安、疾病口舌是牵连、疾病未愈、疾病未好、病不愈

六爻 · 疾病 · 轻重 · 出处 [[文王金钱课]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 文王金钱课的六十四卦课（原有的二十六卦）里，这五个卦的断辞说病不好：遁「占病不安」，否「疾病口舌是牵连」，坎「疾病未愈」，屯「疾病未好」，睽「病不愈」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the sixty-four hexagram readings of the Wenwang Jinqian Ke (the twenty-six that the book originally has), the verdicts of these five hexagrams say that the illness does not mend: Dun, 'divining illness: unsettled'; Pi, 'illness and quarrels are entangled with it'; Kan, 'illness is not yet cured'; Zhun, 'illness has not mended'; Kui, 'illness is not cured'.

> [!note] 按
> 这五个卦的说法都是「没好」「不安」，没有一个说死。否卦的病话在「象曰」里（「疾病口舌是牵连」），别的四卦在「断曰」里；「是牵连」是和病有牵扯的意思，书里没有说是病拖着还是病又牵出别的事，译文照字面。遁、坎、睽三卦周易尚占也说病不好（「病者迁延」「孕病皆凶」「问病有惊」，LY.ILL.ZSZ.04、ZSZ.05），两本一致。关于「和课」「垢」等写法，见 LY.ILL.WWJQ.03 的说明。
>
> *Note:* The wording for all five hexagrams is 'not mended' or 'unsettled', and none speaks of death. The words on illness in Pi are in the image verse ('illness and quarrels are entangled with it'), and in the other four in the verdict; 'entangled with it' means that illness is involved, and the book does not say whether the illness drags on or draws other matters after it, and the translation is literal. For Dun, Kan and Kui the Zhouyi Shangzhan also says that the illness does not mend ('the sick one drags on', 'pregnancy and illness are both ominous', 'asking about illness: there is a fright', LY.ILL.ZSZ.04, ZSZ.05), so the two books agree. For the spellings 'he ke', 'gou' and so on, see the notes of LY.ILL.WWJQ.03.
