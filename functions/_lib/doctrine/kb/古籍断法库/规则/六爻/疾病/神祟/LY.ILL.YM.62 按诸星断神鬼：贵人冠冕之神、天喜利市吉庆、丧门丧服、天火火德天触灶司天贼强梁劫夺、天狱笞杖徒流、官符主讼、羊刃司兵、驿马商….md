# 按诸星断神鬼：贵人冠冕之神、天喜利市吉庆、丧门丧服、天火火德天触灶司天贼强梁劫夺、天狱笞杖徒流、官符主讼、羊刃司兵、驿马商旅之冤、天河觉溺之屈、华盖僧道、咸池娼妓、暗金产妇、太岁土德、刀针斩绞、坵墓坟茔、丧车棺椁、浴盆婴童之溺、孤神绝嗣之鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 还有官鬼出现在贵人上的，是戴冠冕的神；天喜，是带来生意兴隆、吉庆的神；丧门，是丧服的神；天火、火德、天触、灶司、天贼，是强横劫夺而死的亡魂；天狱，是受笞刑、杖刑、徒刑、流放的鬼；官符主管诉讼，羊刃掌管兵刃，驿马是行商旅客的冤魂，天河是溺水者的冤屈；华盖是僧人道士，咸池是娼妓，暗金是产妇，血衂是血鬼；太岁是土德；刀针是被斩杀、绞死、受伤，坵墓是坟墓的咎，丧车是棺椁的殃，折煞是摔断跌伤的鬼；飞廉是游神，将星是武神，天医是医神，天马是被马踏的；浴盆是婴孩溺死，摧尸是伏尸的祸，孤神是断了后嗣的鬼；太阴、月德从王母那里受福，太阳、天德靠上帝保佑。注说：鬼临哪颗星宿，就用诸星的象来断；天河是水库，是天上浩大的府库，所以从辰起逐月顺行，刀针是金宿，正月应当从乾起，所以从亥起顺行；其余详见诸星章。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Further, a Pressure line appearing on the Noble is a spirit in cap and robes of office; Heavenly Joy is a spirit of good trade and celebration; Mourning Gate is a spirit of mourning dress; Heavenly Fire, Fire Virtue, Heavenly Touch, Stove Official and Heavenly Thief are the departed of violence and robbery; Heavenly Prison is a ghost of flogging, beating, penal servitude and exile; Official Talisman presides over lawsuits, Goat Blade governs weapons, the Post Horse is the grievance of merchants and travellers, and Heavenly River is the wrong of the drowned; Huagai is monks and Daoists, Xianchi is courtesans, Hidden Metal is women in childbirth, and Bleeding is a blood ghost; the Tai Sui is the Earth Virtue; Blade and Needle is beheading, strangling and wounds, Mounds and Tombs is the fault of the graves, Funeral Carriage is the misfortune of coffins, and Breaking Killing is a ghost of breaks and falls; Feilian is the wandering spirit, the General Star is the martial spirit, Heavenly Doctor is the medicine spirit, and Heavenly Horse is trampling by a horse; Bath Basin is the drowning of infants, Corpse Breaker is the calamity of the buried corpse, and Lone Spirit is a ghost with no heir; Great Yin and Month Virtue receive blessing from the Queen Mother, the Sun and Heavenly Virtue depend on the protection of the Supreme Lord. The note says: whichever star the ghost falls on, judgment by the images of the various stars is as above; Heavenly River is a water store, the vast treasury of heaven, and so it runs forward month by month starting from Chen; Blade and Needle is a Metal lodge, and the first month should start from Qian, so it begins at Hai and runs forward; the rest is detailed in the chapter on the stars.

> [!note] 按
> 「天火、火德，天触、灶司、天贼，强梁劫夺之亡」这一句，谓语「强横劫夺而死的亡魂」管到哪几个星，原文的逗号并不清楚，这里照字面把它放在这一串的末尾。「天河为觉溺之屈」的「觉溺」原文没有解释，这里按字面译作「觉后溺水」，读法没有把握。星名的译名在本仓库各处保持一致：咸池 Xianchi、驿马 Post Horse、浴盆 Bath Basin、丧车 Funeral Carriage。这些星怎么查，详见易冒诸星章。
>
> *Note:* In 'Heavenly Fire, Fire Virtue, Heavenly Touch, Stove Official, Heavenly Thief: the departed of violence and robbery', it is not clear from the commas how many of the stars the predicate 'the departed of violence and robbery' extends to, and it is placed literally at the end of the run. The 'awake-drown' in 'Heavenly River is the wrong of those who wake to drown' is not explained in the original, and is translated literally as 'wake to drown', the reading being uncertain. Star names (Noble, Heavenly Joy, Mourning Gate, Heavenly Fire, Heavenly Prison, Official Talisman, Goat Blade, Post Horse, Heavenly River, Huagai, Xianchi and so on) keep the same renderings used elsewhere in this repository: Xianchi, Post Horse, Bath Basin, Funeral Carriage. How the stars and spirit-stars are found is set out in the Yi Mao's chapters on the stars.
