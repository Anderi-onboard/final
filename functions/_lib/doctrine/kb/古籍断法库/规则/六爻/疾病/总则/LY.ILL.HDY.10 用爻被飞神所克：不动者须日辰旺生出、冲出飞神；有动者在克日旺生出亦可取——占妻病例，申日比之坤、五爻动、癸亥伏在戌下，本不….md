# 用爻被飞神所克：不动者须日辰旺生出、冲出飞神；有动者在克日旺生出亦可取——占妻病例，申日比之坤、五爻动、癸亥伏在戌下，本不好却得日辰生扶，死中得生

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻伏着、被上面的飞神克制，如果用爻不动，就要日辰旺，把它生出来或者冲出来，飞神无气的日子是生不出来的；用爻有动的，在克它的那一日旺相、生出它，也可以取。书里的例子：占妻的病，申日得比之坤，五爻发动，癸亥伏在戌的下面，本来不好，却得到日辰能生扶它出来，死中得生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line is hidden and controlled by the flying line above it, then if it is still it needs the Day to be strong to generate it out or clash it out, and on a day when the flying line has no qi it cannot be generated; if it moves, being strong on the day that controls it and generated out can also be taken. The book's example: divining a wife's illness, on a Shen day the hexagram Bi changing to Kun, the fifth line moves, and Guihai lies hidden under Xu; it was originally not good, but the Day was able to generate and support it out, and life was found in death.

> [!note] 按
> 上一段是规矩（「凡用爻为飞神所克……」），下一段是书里的例。这条规矩不专属疾病，是伏神被飞神克时怎么取——和易林补遗「卦无用象：先察伏神」（LY.ILL.EL.10）的思路相通；书里的例子是占妻病，所以放在疾病里。例里的「（之）」「（生）」是整理本补的字。卦例的拆解：比卦五爻是戊戌土，坤宫的妻财是癸亥水，所以癸亥伏在五爻的戌下——飞神戌土克伏神亥水，「本不好」；申日的金能生水，「日辰能生扶出」；所以「死中得生」。「癸亥伏在戌上下动」的「上下动」文字含混，按「伏在戌下、动」译。「有动者在克日旺生出」也简，按上下文译。
>
> *Note:* The first passage is the rule ('when the Subject line is controlled by the flying line ...') and the second is the book's example. The rule does not belong to illness alone and is about how to take a hidden line controlled by the flying line — on a line of thought with the Yilin Buyi's 'when the hexagram has no Subject image: first examine the hidden line' (LY.ILL.EL.10); the book's example is a divining of a wife's illness, and so it is placed under illness. The '(zhi)' and '(sheng)' in the example are characters supplied by the editor. Working the case out: the fifth line of Bi is Wuxu Earth, the Wealth of the Kun palace is Guihai Water, so Guihai lies hidden under the Xu of the fifth line — the flying line Xu Earth controls the hidden line Hai Water, 'originally not good'; Metal on a Shen day can generate Water, 'the Day was able to generate and support it out'; hence 'life was found in death'. 'Moving, above and below Xu' in 'Guihai lying hidden above and below Xu, moving' is loose in wording and is translated as 'lies hidden under Xu, moving'. 'If it moves, being strong on the day that controls it and generated out' is also terse, and is translated from the context.
