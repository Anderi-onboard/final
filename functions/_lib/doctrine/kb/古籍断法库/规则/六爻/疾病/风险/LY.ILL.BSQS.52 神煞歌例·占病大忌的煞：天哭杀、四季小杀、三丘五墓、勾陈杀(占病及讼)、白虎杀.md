# 神煞歌例·占病大忌的煞：天哭杀、四季小杀、三丘五墓、勾陈杀(占病及讼)、白虎杀

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神煞歌例点明占病大忌的五个煞：天哭杀（正五九月羊、二六十月猴、三七十一月鸡、四八十二月犬）；四季小杀（春羊夏龙秋牛冬犬，临身命，轻灾也凶）；三丘五墓（春丑夏辰秋未冬戌为三丘，五墓取对宫，病人作福也难留）；勾陈杀（从辰逆行，占病及讼大忌）；白虎杀（从申顺行，占病大忌）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The verses on spirit-stars name five killings that are great taboos in divining illness: the Weeping killing (the sheep in the first, fifth and ninth months, the monkey in the second, sixth and tenth, the rooster in the third, seventh and eleventh, the dog in the fourth, eighth and twelfth); the small killing of the four seasons (the sheep in spring, the dragon in summer, the ox in autumn, the dog in winter; on the Body or the natal line even a light misfortune is ominous); the Three Mounds and Five Tombs (Chou in spring, Chen in summer, Wei in autumn, Xu in winter are the Three Mounds, the Five Tombs are taken from the opposite palace, and a sick person can hardly be kept even by making offerings); the Hook Earth killing (backward from Chen, a great taboo for illness and lawsuits); the White Tiger killing (forward from Shen, a great taboo for illness).

> [!note] 按
> 这五个煞各自的月位，和断易天机的一致：天哭杀和四季小杀（LY.ILL.DYTJ.33）、三丘五墓（LY.ILL.DYTJ.31）的位置一字不差（只差几个字的写法），勾陈杀从辰逆行同 LY.ILL.DYTJ.33 的一条，白虎杀从申顺行同占家宅一段的白虎杀例（正月起申、二月酉，见本书第 3577 行的旧注，火珠林第 1129 行也有）。卜筮全书对这五个煞都标了「占病大忌」或「占病忌之」（勾陈杀是「占病及讼大忌」），没有限定发动；断易天机则在勾陈杀、天哭杀、四季小杀下写「占病发动凶」或「占病忌发动」，三丘五墓下写「此占病忌」，勾陈杀、天哭杀、四季小杀这几条限定了要发动才凶。各煞的月位写成十二支是按歌里的生肖（羊=未、猴=申、鸡=酉、犬=戌、牛=丑、龙=辰、马=午、蛇=巳）换的。
>
> *Note:* The monthly positions of these five killings agree with the Duanyi Tianji: the Weeping and the small killing of the four seasons (LY.ILL.DYTJ.33) and the Three Mounds and Five Tombs (LY.ILL.DYTJ.31) are in the same positions (with a few characters written differently), the Hook Earth killing going backward from Chen is the same as the one in LY.ILL.DYTJ.33, and the White Tiger killing going forward from Shen is the same as the White Tiger killing in the passage on divining a house (Shen in the first month, You in the second, see the old note at line 3577 of this book; the Huozhu Lin, line 1129, has it too). The Bushi Quanshu marks all five killings 'a great taboo for illness' or 'to be avoided in illness' (the Hook Earth killing 'a great taboo for illness and lawsuits'), without limiting it to moving; the Duanyi Tianji writes 'in divining illness it is ominous if it moves' or 'in divining illness it must not move' under the Hook Earth killing, the Weeping and the small killing of the four seasons, and 'this is a taboo in divining illness' under the Three Mounds and Five Tombs, so in those three it limits the ill omen to the killing moving. The monthly positions are written as the twelve branches by turning the animals of the verses into branches (sheep Wei, monkey Shen, rooster You, dog Xu, ox Chou, dragon Chen, horse Wu, snake Si).
