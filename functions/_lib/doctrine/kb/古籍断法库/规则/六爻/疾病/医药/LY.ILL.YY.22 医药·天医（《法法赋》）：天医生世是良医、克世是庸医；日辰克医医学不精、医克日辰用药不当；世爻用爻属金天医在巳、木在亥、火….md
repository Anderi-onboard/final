# 医药·天医（《法法赋》）：天医生世是良医、克世是庸医；日辰克医医学不精、医克日辰用药不当；世爻用爻属金天医在巳、木在亥、火在寅、水土在申则效

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《法法赋》说：天医生世爻的，是好医生；天医克世爻的，是庸医；日辰克医生的，医术不精；医生克日辰的，用药不恰当。世爻、用爻属金的，天医在巳有效；属木的，天医在亥有效；属火的，天医在寅有效；属水、土的，天医在申有效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Fafa Fu says: if the Heavenly Doctor generates the Self, he is a good doctor; if it controls the Self, a mediocre one; if the Day controls the doctor, his medical learning is not refined; if the doctor controls the Day, the medicine used is not fitting. If the Self line and Subject line are Metal, the Heavenly Doctor at Si is effective; if Wood, at Hai; if Fire, at Yin; if Water or Earth, at Shen.

> [!note] 按
> 「天医」是一颗神煞。这里的天医按世爻用爻的五行取，取的是那一行长生的地支（金长生在巳、木在亥、火在寅、水土在申）；易冒讲的天医是按月取（丁神），不是这一个（见易冒的天医，书里另有说明）。「法法赋」是易隐引的一部书，书里没有再介绍。
>
> *Note:* The 'Heavenly Doctor' is a spirit-star. Here it is taken by the element of the Self and Subject lines, using the branch where that element is born (Metal is born at Si, Wood at Hai, Fire at Yin, Water and Earth at Shen); the Heavenly Doctor the Yi Mao speaks of is taken by the month (the Ding spirit) and is not this one (the Yi Mao explains its own). The 'Fafa Fu' is a work the Yi Yin quotes and the book does not introduce.
