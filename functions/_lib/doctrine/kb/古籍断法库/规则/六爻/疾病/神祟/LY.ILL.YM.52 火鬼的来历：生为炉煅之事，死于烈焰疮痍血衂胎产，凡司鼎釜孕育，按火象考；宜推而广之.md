# 火鬼的来历：生为炉煅之事，死于烈焰疮痍血衂胎产，凡司鼎釜孕育，按火象考；宜推而广之

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生前做炉锻的事，死在烈火、疮痍、流血、胎产中的，凡是掌管鼎釜和孕育的，就按火象来考察。注说：应当推广开来，用五行、六神、诸星去配。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* One who in life worked at furnace and forge, or died in raging flame, sores and wounds, bleeding, or childbirth, and whose charge is cauldrons and pots and pregnancy, is examined by the Fire image. The note says: one should extend it further, matching it with the five phases, the six spirits and the various stars.

> [!note] 按
> 「血衂」的「衂」同「衄」，流血、鼻出血；这里译作流血。这一条和 LY.ILL.YM.49–LY.ILL.YM.51、YM.53 是平行的，只是火的注只有一句「宜推而广之」，没有像木那条一样给出判别规则。
>
> *Note:* 'Xue nu' (bleeding) uses a variant of the character for nosebleed and is rendered as bleeding. This entry runs parallel to LY.ILL.YM.49 to YM.51 and YM.53, except that the note on Fire has only the one sentence 'one should extend it further', and gives no rule of discrimination as the one on Wood does.
