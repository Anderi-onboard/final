# 鬼动将见、鬼冲将开、鬼旺将起，当月当日则是月是日而出：官鬼值日月当见，卯鬼发动应速、卯鬼安静应迟

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼动了，痘将要出现；鬼被冲了，痘将要开；鬼旺了，痘将要起；鬼当月、当日，就在这个月、这一天出痘。官鬼当值的日、月，痘就该出现：比如丑月乙卯日，占有没有花，得到履卦变同人卦，当天就见了标；另有一人也占，得履卦变睽卦，到第二年二月才出痘，因为卯鬼发动所以应得快，卯鬼安静所以应得迟。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the ghost moves the pox is about to show; when the ghost is clashed it is about to open; when the ghost is strong it is about to rise; when the ghost is on duty in the month or day, it comes out in that month or on that day. The day or month on which the Pressure line is on duty, the pox should appear: for example, in a Chou month on a Yimao day, someone divined whether the flowers would come and obtained Lu changing into Tongren, and the marks showed that same day; another person also divined and obtained Lu changing into Kui, and the pox began only in the second month of the following year, because the Mao ghost moved and so it answered quickly, and the Mao ghost was still and so it answered slowly.

> [!note] 按
> 「标」是痘刚出来的点。注里两个卦例，一个当天见标、一个拖到第二年二月，解释都是同一个卯鬼——发动就快，安静就迟；这和增删卜易「鬼动逢值逢合、鬼静逢值逢冲」（LY.ILL.ZS.19）的思路相近。
>
> *Note:* A 'mark' (biao) is the spot when the pox has just come out. Of the two cases in the note, one showed marks the same day and the other was put off to the second month of the next year, and the explanation for both is the same Mao ghost — moving, it is quick, and still, it is slow; this is close in thinking to the Zengshan Buyi's 'a still ghost on the day of duty or clash, a moving ghost on the day of duty or combination' (LY.ILL.ZS.19).
