# 鬼衰属水莫行针：子孙属金利用刀针，鬼爻属水而用刀针则金生水反助病势；土鬼忌热药、水鬼忌寒药（王洪绪：木鬼忌寒药）、火鬼忌风药、金鬼忌丸药

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙属金，适合用刀针。鬼爻属水，如果用刀针，金能生水，反而助长病势，病一定加重。土鬼不宜用热药，水鬼不宜用寒药，火鬼不宜用风药，金鬼不宜用丸药。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Output is Metal, knife and needle are of use. If the Pressure line is Water and knife and needle are used, Metal can generate Water and helps the force of the illness instead, and the illness surely grows heavier. An Earth Pressure avoids hot medicine, a Water Pressure cold medicine, a Fire Pressure wind medicine, and a Metal Pressure pills.

> [!warning] 打架
> 哪一种鬼忌用寒药：旧注是水鬼，王洪绪是木鬼；土鬼忌热药、火鬼忌风药、金鬼忌丸药，两家相同。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Which Pressure avoids cold medicine: the old commentary says the Water Pressure, Wang Hongxu the Wood Pressure; that an Earth Pressure avoids hot medicine, a Fire Pressure wind medicine and a Metal Pressure pills is the same in both.

> [!note] 按
> 经文说「鬼衰属水」，两家的注讲的都只是「鬼爻属水」，没有说「衰」字起什么作用。「风药」是发散风邪的药，「丸药」是成丸的药。
>
> *Note:* The verse says 'a weak Pressure line belonging to Water', and both commentaries speak only of 'a Pressure line belonging to Water' and say nothing of what the word 'weak' does. 'Wind medicine' is medicine that disperses wind pathogens, and 'pills' medicine made up into pills.
