# 第三爻主腰：无故自空是腰软，旺相而空是腰痛，不空而遇动爻日辰官鬼冲克是闪腰，鬼在此爻也主腰痛

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 第三爻管腰。第三爻无缘无故落空，是腰软；有缘故而空，或者旺相而空，是腰痛；不空，但遇到动爻、日辰、官鬼来克或冲它，是闪了腰的痛；动而空的也是这样。鬼爻就在第三爻上，也主腰痛。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The third line is the waist. If the third line is Void of itself without cause, the waist is weak; if it is Void with a cause, or Void while strong, the waist hurts; if it is not Void but a moving line, the Day or a Pressure line controls or clashes it, it is a strained waist; a moving Void line is the same. A Pressure line on the third line also means waist pain.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> | 第三爻如值旬空，为腰软； | 第三爻如果碰上旬空，是腰软； | If the third line meets Void, the waist is weak; |
> | 或旺相而空，为腰痛。 | 或者旺相而空，是腰痛。 | or if it is Void while strong, the waist hurts. |
> | 不空而遇动爻日辰官鬼冲克者，乃闪腰痛也，动爻亦然。 | 不空，但遇到动爻、日辰、官鬼来冲克的，是闪了腰的痛，动爻也是这样。 | If it is not Void but meets a moving line, the Day or a Pressure line that clashes or controls it, it is a strained waist, and a moving line is the same. |
> | 鬼在此爻者，亦主腰痛。 | 鬼爻就在这一爻上的，也主腰痛。 | A Pressure line on this line also means waist pain. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注的「无故自空」「有故而空」书里没有说「故」指什么。王洪绪把前者写成「值旬空」，后者只留「旺相而空」。旧注末尾的「动空亦然」，王洪绪写成「动爻亦然」，一个说动而空，一个说动爻本身，大概是传抄中「空」「爻」两个字的差别。
>
> *Note:* The book does not say what the 'cause' of the old commentary's 'Void without cause' and 'Void with cause' is. Wang Hongxu writes the former as 'meets Void' and keeps only 'Void while strong' of the latter. The old commentary's closing 'a moving Void line is the same' is written by Wang Hongxu as 'a moving line is the same': one means moving and Void, the other the moving line itself, probably a difference of the two characters for 'Void' and 'line' in copying.
