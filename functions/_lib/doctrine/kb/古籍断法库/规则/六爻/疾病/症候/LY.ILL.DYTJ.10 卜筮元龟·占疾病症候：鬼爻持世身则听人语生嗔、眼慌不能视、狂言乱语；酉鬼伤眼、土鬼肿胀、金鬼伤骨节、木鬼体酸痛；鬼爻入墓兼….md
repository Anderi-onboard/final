# 卜筮元龟·占疾病症候：鬼爻持世身则听人语生嗔、眼慌不能视、狂言乱语；酉鬼伤眼、土鬼肿胀、金鬼伤骨节、木鬼体酸痛；鬼爻入墓兼休囚杀气、本宫白虎入墓凶、子不动难救

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻持世临身，一听见人说话就生气，眼睛发慌看不见，狂言乱语失了精神，吃喝也难；四墓持世也一样。鬼是酉爻，眼睛遭殃；土鬼肿胀气虚；金鬼持世，伤骨节；木鬼，身体酸痛，头闷心郁结。卦里鬼爻入了墓，又带休囚和杀气，或本宫的白虎入墓，就凶；子孙不动，难救。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Pressure line holding the Self and the Body, at the first sound of a voice he grows angry, his eyes are flurried and cannot see, he raves and loses his wits, and food and drink are hard; the Four Tombs holding the Self are the same. A ghost on the You line: the eyes suffer; an Earth ghost: swelling and deficient qi; a Metal ghost holding the Self injures the bone joints; a Wood ghost: aching in the body, a heavy head and a knotted heart. With the Pressure line entering a tomb in the hexagram, together with resting or trapped and a killing aura, or the White Tiger of the hexagram's own palace entering a tomb, it is ominous; if Output does not move he can hardly be saved.

> [!note] 按
> 「四墓」是辰戌丑未四个墓爻。「其字两丁穿目穴」说的是「酉」字的字形（像两个丁字穿透眼窝），用来解释为什么酉鬼伤眼，是拆字的取象。「饮食何能加进啜」是反问，意思是饮食进不去。「休囚」是休、囚两种衰弱的状态，英文按「resting or trapped」译。「本宫白虎」的「本宫」是这一卦所属的宫，白虎是六神里的白虎。文中的「（3）」「（4）」是今人加的注号，那两条注不收。这一块和天玄赋的「五行鬼」症候（LY.ILL.TXF.03）、海底眼的症候（LY.ILL.HDY.05）是同一类，但这里多了一个酉鬼伤眼、四墓持世的说法。
>
> *Note:* The 'Four Tombs' are the four tomb lines Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei. 'The character looks like two ding piercing the eye socket' speaks of the shape of the character You (a rooster), which looks like two 'ding' piercing the eye socket, and is used to explain why a You ghost injures the eyes; it is taking the image from the shape of a character. 'How could he take more food and drink' is a rhetorical question and means food and drink will not go down. 'Resting or trapped' translates the two weak states of resting and being trapped. The 'own palace' of 'the White Tiger of the own palace' is the palace the hexagram belongs to, and the White Tiger is the one among the six spirits. The '(3)' and '(4)' in the text are modern annotation marks and those annotations are not quoted. This block is of the same kind as the symptoms by the five phases in the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.03) and in the Haidi Yan (LY.ILL.HDY.05), but adds a You ghost injuring the eyes and the Four Tombs holding the Self.
