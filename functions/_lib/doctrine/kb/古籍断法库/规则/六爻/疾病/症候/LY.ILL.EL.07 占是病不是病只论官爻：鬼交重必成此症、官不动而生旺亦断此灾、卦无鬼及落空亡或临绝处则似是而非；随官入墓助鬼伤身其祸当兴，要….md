# 占是病不是病只论官爻：鬼交重必成此症、官不动而生旺亦断此灾、卦无鬼及落空亡或临绝处则似是而非；随官入墓助鬼伤身其祸当兴，要知病散须看子孙

六爻 · 疾病 · 症候 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以官鬼为主，旺衰为凭。占疾厄是假是真，官鬼交重，病一定兴起；旺相的也会遭这个患；落空、遇绝，就是虚名。随官入墓、助鬼伤身，灾就不是假的，祸一定兴起；日、月制住官鬼，病是实还是不实要看；子孙旺动，病就难成立。注说：占是不是病，只论官爻，再没有别的说法；鬼如果交重，一定成这个症；官虽不动，如果逢生旺，也断有这个灾；卦如果没有鬼、或落空亡、或临绝处，似是而非，病不真；凡是遇上随官入墓、助鬼伤身，其祸应当兴起，灾难回避；要知道病什么时候散，要看子孙，如果子旺官衰、日月和福德，卦内子爻或动而克制，病就散。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Pressure line is the main line, and strength or weakness is the ground. To divine whether an ailment is false or real: if the Pressure line moves, the illness will surely rise; strong, it too suffers this trouble; Void or meeting Extinction, it is an empty name. Following into a tomb and helping the ghost to harm the Self mean the calamity is no false one, and misfortune will surely rise; if the Day and Month control the Pressure line, it remains to be seen whether it is real; Output strong and moving, and the illness is hard to make out. The commentary says: in judging whether it is an illness or not, go by the Pressure line alone, with no other opinion; if the ghost moves, it will surely make this illness; though the Pressure line does not move, if it meets generation or strength, it too means this calamity; if the hexagram has no ghost, or it is Void, or sits at Extinction, it seems so but is not, and the illness is not real; when one meets following into a tomb or helping the ghost to harm the Self, the misfortune should rise and the calamity is hard to avoid; to know when the illness will disperse, look at Output: if Output is strong and Pressure weak, or the Day and Month and the fortune spirit are on it, or Output in the hexagram moves and controls the ghost, the illness disperses.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段也没有章题。它问的是：这算不算病，是真病还是虚惊。判断只有一个：官鬼——交重必成、生旺也成、空绝就不真。第二组歌诀是把随官入墓、助鬼伤身作为「灾非假」的两个实证，而解病的方法是子孙。「灾难，回避」原文如此，大意是灾难以躲过；最后一句「子爻或动克制」没有写完（缺后半句），按上下文译作「子爻动来克制，病就散」。与易冒的「问症发真假」（LY.ILL.YM.31）相同：鬼实则是、鬼虚则非。
>
> *Note:* This passage too has no chapter title. It asks whether this counts as illness, a real illness or a false alarm. There is only one criterion: the Pressure line — moving, it surely forms; strong, it forms; Void or in Extinction, it is not real. The second verse gives following into a tomb and helping the ghost to harm the Self as two proofs that the calamity is 'no false one', and the way to dissolve the illness is Output. 'The calamity is hard to avoid' is as in the original, meaning the calamity can hardly be escaped; the last sentence, 'Output in the hexagram moves and controls', breaks off without the second half and is translated from the context as 'Output moves and controls, and the illness disperses'. It agrees with the Yi Mao on whether the outbreak of symptoms is real (LY.ILL.YM.31): when the ghost is full it is real, and when the ghost is empty it is not.
