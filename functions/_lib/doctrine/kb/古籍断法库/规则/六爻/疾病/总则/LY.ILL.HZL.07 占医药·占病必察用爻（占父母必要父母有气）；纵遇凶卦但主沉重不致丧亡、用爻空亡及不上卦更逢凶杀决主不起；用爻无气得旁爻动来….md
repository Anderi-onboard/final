# 占医药·占病必察用爻（占父母必要父母有气）；纵遇凶卦但主沉重不致丧亡、用爻空亡及不上卦更逢凶杀决主不起；用爻无气得旁爻动来生扶同生旺；凶杀临父母或父母空可言双亲有病；鬼爻持世沉重绝日轻可；鬼化鬼病进退；鬼带杀持世为瘵病

六爻 · 疾病 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占病一定要察看用爻，占父母就一定要父母有气。纵然遇到凶卦，也只主沉重，不致丧亡；若用爻空亡或不上卦，又遇凶杀，一定不起。用爻无气，若有旁爻动来生扶，就和生旺一样，决没有灾。若凶杀临在父母上，或父母空，就可以说双亲有病，别的爻也一样。鬼爻持世，沉重，绝日轻可。鬼化鬼，病忽进忽退，或有变病，或旧病再发，或症候驳杂，一卦有两个鬼也一样。鬼爻持世，病难除根；鬼带杀持世，是瘵病，难脱体，是养老的病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining illness one must look at the Subject line; divining a parent the Resource line must have qi. Even meeting an ominous hexagram it only means heaviness and does not lead to death; but if the Subject line is Void or not in the hexagram and meets a killing as well, he surely does not rise. A Subject line without qi, if another line moves to generate and support it, is the same as strong and there is surely no harm. If a killing sits on Resource, or Resource is Void, one can say a parent is ill, and the other lines are the same. The Pressure line holding the Self: heavy, and light on a day of Extinction. A ghost changing into a ghost: the illness advances and retreats, or there is a changed illness, or an old illness breaks out again, or the symptoms are mixed, and two ghosts in one hexagram is the same. The Pressure line holding the Self: the illness is hard to root out; a ghost bringing a killing and holding the Self is a wasting illness that is hard to shake off, the illness of old age.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段和天玄赋「决轻重存亡之兆专察鬼爻」后面的注（LY.ILL.TXF.02）、「杀临父母，当忧堂上之亲」的用爻表（LY.ILL.TXF.01）是同一套；「鬼化鬼其病进退……鬼爻持世病难除根，鬼带杀持世乃瘵疾」两句在天玄赋的注里几乎一字不差。「绝日轻可」的「绝日」是鬼爻绝的日子。「瘵病」是痨病、消耗性的病，「养老病」按字面译作 the illness of old age，原文没有解释。「用爻」译作 Subject line。
>
> *Note:* This passage is the same set as the note after the Tianxuan Fu's 'to decide light or heavy, life or death, look only at the Pressure line' (LY.ILL.TXF.02) and the table of the Subject line, 'a killing sitting on Resource: worry for the elder on the hall' (LY.ILL.TXF.01); the two sentences 'a ghost changing into a ghost, the illness advances and retreats ... the Pressure line holding the Self, the illness is hard to root out, a ghost bringing a killing and holding the Self is a wasting illness' are almost word for word the same in the Tianxuan Fu's note. The 'day of Extinction' of 'light on a day of Extinction' is a day on which the Pressure line is in Extinction. A 'wasting illness' is consumption, an illness that wears the body down, and 'the illness of old age' is rendered literally, and the original does not explain it. 'Yong yao' is rendered Subject line.
