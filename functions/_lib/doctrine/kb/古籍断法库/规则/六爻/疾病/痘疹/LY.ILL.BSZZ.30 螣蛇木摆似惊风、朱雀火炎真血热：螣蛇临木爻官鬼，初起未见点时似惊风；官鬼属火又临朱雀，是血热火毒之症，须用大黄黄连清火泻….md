# 螣蛇木摆似惊风、朱雀火炎真血热：螣蛇临木爻官鬼，初起未见点时似惊风；官鬼属火又临朱雀，是血热火毒之症，须用大黄黄连清火泻毒，迟服则痘隐焦黑不能挽回

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 螣蛇带木摆动像惊风，朱雀带火炽盛是真的血热。螣蛇带着木爻的官鬼，主刚开始还没见到痘点的时候，像是惊风；如果卦中官鬼属火，又带朱雀，是血热火毒的症候，必须用大黄、黄连这类药，一定要清火泻毒才能得救；如果服得迟，病势更猛，痘隐没下去、焦黑，没法挽回。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Coiling Snake with Wood swaying is like infantile convulsions; the Vermilion Bird with blazing Fire is truly blood heat. If the Coiling Snake sits on a Wood Pressure line, before the spots are yet seen at the beginning it seems like infantile convulsions; if in the hexagram the Pressure line is Fire and sits on the Vermilion Bird, it is a condition of blood heat and fire poison, and one must use rhubarb, coptis and the like: only by clearing fire and draining poison can there be rescue; if it is taken late the enemy is fiercer, the pox sink out of sight and turn scorched and black, and it cannot be retrieved.

> [!note] 按
> 「黄莲」是「黄连」的误字。「惊风」是小儿抽搐一类的急症。这一条说火鬼带朱雀要用大黄、黄连清火泻毒，而前面医药章有「火鬼旺宜大寒药」的说法（见 LY.ILL.HJC.82、LY.ILL.HJC.84），增删卜易的李我平驳「火鬼忌热水鬼忌寒」是拘泥（LY.ILL.ZS.40）；这一条是痘疹专门的用药，不是一般病的通则。
>
> *Note:* 'Huang lian' written with the lotus character is a mis-write of coptis. 'Jingfeng' is infantile convulsions and the like, an acute illness. This entry says a Fire Pressure line on the Vermilion Bird needs rhubarb and coptis to clear fire and drain poison, while the earlier chapter on medicine has the saying that a strong Fire ghost suits very cold medicines (see LY.ILL.HJC.82 and LY.ILL.HJC.84), and Li Woping in the Zengshan Buyi rejects 'Fire Pressure fears heat, Water Pressure fears cold' as rigid (LY.ILL.ZS.40); this entry is medicine specific to pox, not a general rule for illness.
