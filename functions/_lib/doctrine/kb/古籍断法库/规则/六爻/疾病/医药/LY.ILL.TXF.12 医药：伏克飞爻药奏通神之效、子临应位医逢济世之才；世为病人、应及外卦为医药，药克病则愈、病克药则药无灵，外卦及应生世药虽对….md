# 医药：伏克飞爻药奏通神之效、子临应位医逢济世之才；世为病人、应及外卦为医药，药克病则愈、病克药则药无灵，外卦及应生世药虽对症作效迟；世动克药按五行（水鬼服药不纳、金鬼久病无效、木鬼犯条贯亦无益、火鬼不能止热、土鬼不对症），应加鬼克世误药损人；子孙为医药临应动克世必遇良医、卦无子孙服药无效、世应比和无子宜换医、子孙临应被世克虽遇良医病未疗、外卦及应克世无福德难安、虎杀临子孙动防医不精

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 伏爻克飞爻，药奏神效；子孙临在应位，医生遇上济世之才。注说：世是病人，应和外卦是医药；药如果克病，病就好；病人克药，药就没有灵验；外卦和应生世，药虽然对症，见效迟。世动克药：如果是水鬼，服药不纳；金鬼无气、久病犯真（此处含混），服药无效；木鬼，病一定犯了条贯，即使有药也没有益处；火鬼旺相，症一定刚烈，药也止不住它的热；土鬼，病沉重，药不对症，不能治病；应爻加鬼克世，那就是误了药、损了人。占病以子孙为医药：临应发动克世，必遇良医，病马上痊愈；卦里没有子孙，服药无效；世应比和、卦里又没有子孙，宜换医生；子孙临应爻被世克，虽遇良医病也没有治；外卦和应虽克世，卦里没有福德也难安，可以说有药应当治，只恐怕良医没有缘；子孙虽克世，外爻和应却不克，虽有良医，药饵不中，终究没有大害；虎杀如果临着子孙发动，要防医生不精细。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The hidden line controlling the flying line: the medicine works with wondrous effect; Output sitting on the Other's place: the doctor meets one of world-saving talent. The commentary says: the Self is the sick person, the Other line and the outer trigram are the doctor and the medicine; if the medicine controls the illness, the illness is cured at once; if the sick person controls the medicine, the medicine has no effect; if the outer trigram and the Other generate the Self, though the medicine suits the symptoms it takes effect slowly. The Self moving and controlling the medicine: if it is a Water ghost, the medicine taken is not kept down; a Metal ghost without qi, in a long illness that has offended 'the true' (the words are unclear), taking medicine is ineffective; a Wood ghost: the illness surely offends the regulations, and even medicine is of no benefit; a strong Fire ghost: the symptoms are surely fierce and even medicine cannot stop the heat; an Earth ghost: the illness is heavy, the medicine does not suit the symptoms and cannot treat the illness; the Other line with a ghost added controlling the Self is the wrong medicine harming the person. In divining illness Output is the medicine and doctor: sitting on the Other and moving to control the Self, one meets a good doctor and the illness is cured at once; with no Output in the hexagram, taking medicine is ineffective; with the Self and Other harmonious and no Output in the hexagram, one should change the doctor; Output on the Other line controlled by the Self, though one meets a good doctor the illness is not treated; the outer trigram and the Other though controlling the Self, if the hexagram has no Output (the fortune spirit) it is still hard to be at peace, and one may say there is medicine that should treat it but fear the good doctor has no affinity; Output though controlling the Self, if the outer line and the Other do not control it, though there is a good doctor and the medicine does not hit, in the end there is no great harm; if a Tiger killing sits on Output and moves, beware the doctor not being careful.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节是医药：「世为病人，应及外卦为医药」——世是病人、应和外卦（应爻和外卦）是医和药；药克病则愈、病克药则无灵、外卦及应生世则药对症但效迟。这个「应为医、世为病人、子孙为药」的三角和易隐「医药·药效」（LY.ILL.YY.21）、易林补遗「求医疗病」（LY.ILL.EL.26）、增删卜易（LY.ILL.ZS.29）一致；「子孙为医药」和易冒「占医药以子孙为用」（LY.ILL.YM.34）相同。五行鬼的一段（水鬼服药不纳、金鬼无效、木鬼犯条贯、火鬼刚症、土鬼沉重）和黄金策的医药（LY.ILL.HJC.7x 一带）对得上。「久病犯真」文字含混，按字面译并标出；「条贯」是条规，「病必犯条贯」按字面译作「the illness surely offends the regulations」（可能指病是犯了什么规矩得的，书里没有解释）；「虎杀」是白虎之类的凶煞，这里写「虎杀」而不写「白虎」，译作 Tiger killing。「误药损人」是药用错了，伤了人。 卜筮大全的这一章后面还有「虎易评注」（今人加的），本库不引。
>
> *Note:* This section is about medicine: 'the Self is the sick person, the Other and the outer trigram are the doctor and the medicine'; the medicine controlling the illness cures, the sick person controlling the medicine leaves it without effect, and the outer trigram and the Other generating the Self make the medicine suit the symptoms but act slowly. This triangle 'the Other is the doctor, the Self the sick person, Output the medicine' agrees with the Yi Yin's 'medicine: the effect of the medicine' (LY.ILL.YY.21), the Yilin Buyi's 'seeking a doctor to treat illness' (LY.ILL.EL.26) and the Zengshan Buyi (LY.ILL.ZS.29); and 'Output is the medicine and doctor' is the same as the Yi Mao's 'divining medicine takes Output as the Subject' (LY.ILL.YM.34). The passage on the ghosts of the five phases (Water ghost the medicine not kept down, Metal ghost without effect, Wood ghost offending the regulations, Fire ghost a fierce condition, Earth ghost heavy) matches the Huangjin Ce's medicine passages (LY.ILL.HJC.7x and around). 'A long illness that has offended the true' is unclear, and is translated literally and marked; 'tiao guan' is regulation, and 'the illness surely offends the regulations' is rendered literally (it may mean that the illness was got by breaking some rule, which the book does not explain); 'Tiger killing' is a malign killing such as the White Tiger, and the text writes 'Tiger killing' and not 'White Tiger', so it is translated as Tiger killing. 'The wrong medicine harming the person' is medicine wrongly used and injuring the person. After this chapter the Buyi Daquan also has 'Huyi's commentary' (added by a modern hand), which this library does not quote.
