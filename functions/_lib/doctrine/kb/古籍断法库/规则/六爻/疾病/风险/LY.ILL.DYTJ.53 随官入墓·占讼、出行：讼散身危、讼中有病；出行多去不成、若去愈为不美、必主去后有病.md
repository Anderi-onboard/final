# 随官入墓·占讼、出行：讼散身危、讼中有病；出行多去不成、若去愈为不美、必主去后有病

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 随官入墓有三种（身、世、命随鬼入墓），不问占什么事都不是吉兆。占官司遇到它，恐怕有牢狱禁系的忧患，或者官司散了而人有危险，或者官司进行中出现疾病；出行遇到它，多半去不成，硬要去更不好，一定是去了以后生病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Following the ghost into a tomb has three kinds (the Body, the Self or the natal line following the ghost into a tomb), and whatever is divined it is no lucky sign. Divining a lawsuit and meeting it, there may be the worry of prison and confinement, or the lawsuit breaks up and the person is in danger, or illness appears in the course of the lawsuit; divining a journey and meeting it, one most likely cannot go, and if one goes it is the worse, and surely there will be illness after leaving.

> [!note] 按
> 「随官入墓」的三种、占病的一句（「占病遇之，十占九死」）和三个卦例的说明见 LY.ILL.DYTJ.27；这一条收那一章里另外两处顺带提到病的话：占讼的「或讼中有病」和出行的「必主去后有病」。「讼散身危」是官司了结或撤了、人却有危险；「讼中有病」书里没有说是谁病、是原告被告还是涉案的人，译文只照字面写「官司进行中出现疾病」；「去后有病」是动身以后生病，也没说在路上还是到了地方。卜筮大全和火珠林都有这两句：出行那一句三本一字不差，占讼那一句只差一个字，这两本写「占讼遇之，有牢狱禁系之忧」，没有断易天机的「恐」字。这一章里占身、占婚、占产、求官、求财、行人、家宅、移居、风水各有一句，不在病的范围，不收；占婚一句火珠林写「女财不备」、断易天机写「男财不备」，是另一个问题域的出入。
>
> *Note:* For the three kinds of 'following the ghost into a tomb', the line on illness ('divining illness and meeting it, nine out of ten die') and a note on the three example hexagrams, see LY.ILL.DYTJ.27; this entry takes the two other places in that chapter that touch on illness in passing: 'illness appears in the course of the lawsuit' in the lawsuit line and 'there will surely be illness after leaving' in the journey line. 'The lawsuit breaks up and the person is in danger' is the lawsuit ending or being withdrawn while the person is in danger; the book does not say who is ill in 'illness in the course of the lawsuit', whether plaintiff, defendant or someone in the case, and the translation keeps to the letter, 'illness appears in the course of the lawsuit'; 'illness after leaving' is falling ill after setting out, and it does not say whether on the road or on arrival. The Bushi Daquan and the Huozhu Lin both have these two lines: the line on the journey is word for word the same in all three, and the line on the lawsuit differs by a single word, for they write 'divining a lawsuit and meeting it, there is the worry of prison and confinement', lacking the Duanyi Tianji's word for 'there may be'. The lines in this chapter on the person, marriage, childbirth, seeking office, wealth, a traveller, the house, moving house and geomancy are outside illness and are not taken; on marriage the Huozhu Lin reads 'the woman's wealth is not complete' where the Duanyi Tianji reads 'the man's wealth is not complete', a difference that belongs to another domain.
