# 总论八宫值鬼诀(上)：鬼发乾宫是西北寺观庙内神司、旧许天灯香愿经卷斗斋、已故父辈老魂、头风鬼；坎宫是北方水路神司、家内中男、耳聋溺死之魂，坎在外三爻又为北斗、加玄武作玄天上帝；艮宫是东北土神五圣山神土地、少男、臂疮手折指烂鼻鬼；震宫是东方九天（雷祀大帝）、三茅东岳树神、长男、杖伤折足驾舟鬼

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动，看它落在哪一宫，就知道是哪一路的神鬼。乾宫：西北方来，是误犯了寺观或庙里的神司，或者旧时许下的天灯、香愿、经卷、斗斋这一类愿，是已经去世的父辈和年老过世的人的魂，是头风的鬼。坎宫：祸在北方，是水路的神司，还有家里的中男在作耗，也有耳聋的鬼、溺死的魂；坎在外三爻时又是北斗，再加玄武也当作玄天上帝。艮宫：东北方来，是冒犯了土神、五圣、山神、土地，也有家里的少男，以及臂上生疮、手折、指烂、烂鼻的鬼。震宫：祸在东方，宜向九天（雷祀大帝）叩祷，又犯三茅真君、东岳尊神和树头的神灵，也有家里的长男作祟，或是天嗔，或是被打死兼折足、驾舟的鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Pressure line moves, look at which palace it falls in, to know which kind of god or ghost it is. Qian: it comes from the northwest, and one has wrongly offended a temple or abbey or the gods and offices within a temple, or an old vow of a heaven lamp, incense, scriptures or a Dipper fast; it is the soul of a departed father or of an aged person who has died, the ghost of head-wind (chronic headache). Kan: the misfortune lies in the north; it is the gods and offices of the waterways, with the middle son of the household making trouble, and also ghosts of the deaf and souls of the drowned; when Kan is in the outer three lines it is also the Northern Dipper, and with the Dark Tortoise added it is also counted as the Supreme Emperor of the Mysterious Heaven. Gen: it comes from the northeast, offending the Earth spirit, the Five Sages, the Mountain God and the Earth God, and there is also the youngest son of the household and ghosts of arm sores, broken hands, rotten fingers and rotten noses. Zhen: the misfortune lies in the east, and it is fitting to pray to the Nine Heavens (the Great Emperor of the Thunder Rites); it also offends the True Lord of the Three Mao, the honoured god of the Eastern Peak and the sacred spirits of the treetops, and there is the eldest son as haunting, or heaven's wrath, or ghosts beaten to death, with broken legs, or who piloted boats.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段是书里「总论」的头一段（编号「之一」），接在搜决神鬼章的结语（LY.ILL.EL.43）之后：按官鬼所在的宫来认神鬼，和易冒的八宫（LY.ILL.YM.57）、黄金策的八宫（LY.ILL.HJC.95）、本章前面「鬼临何卦何爻」（LY.ILL.EL.35）是同一类办法，只是这里每宫先给一首歌诀、再给一段解说，并且直接列出民间的具体神名。乾宫这里只有解说、没有歌诀。细目上，易林补遗大体把黄金策和易冒两套并列写了进去：乾用黄金策的天灯斗愿，震既有易冒的九天、折足，又有黄金策的树神、杖死，艮和易冒一样是山神、少男。「椿庭」指父亲（「萱堂」指母亲，见下一条）；「头风」是久治不愈的头痛；「斗齐」按上下文读作「斗斋」，「素原」读作「素愿」——这本书里「愿」常写作「原」（「香原」「香愿」并见）；「三茅」是道教茅山的三位茅君；「王枢经」疑是「玉枢经」（本章后面「解禳通用法」里写的就是「玉枢经」）；「瀹死」的「瀹」本义是煮，这里按上下句（「溺死」）读作溺死；「地氏」没有解释，按字面译；「手指皆为疮烂鼻亡」文字含混，对着下面解说「臂疮手折指烂鼻等鬼」译。「坎在外三爻」按字面译，指坎在外面三爻（外卦）。这一段里神、鬼、星煞的名字，是吴地的民间神祇和星煞，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。
>
> *Note:* This is the first section of the book's appended 'general discourse' (numbered 'one'), coming after the closing passage of the 'searching out ghosts and spirits' chapter (LY.ILL.EL.43): it recognises the god or ghost by the palace the Pressure line is in. It is the same kind of method as the Yi Mao's eight palaces (LY.ILL.YM.57), the Huangjin Ce's eight palaces (LY.ILL.HJC.95) and this chapter's earlier 'which trigram and which line the ghost is in' (LY.ILL.EL.35), except that here each palace gets a verse first and then a commentary, and popular names of particular gods are listed outright. For Qian there is only the commentary and no verse. In detail, the Yilin Buyi in the main sets the two systems, the Huangjin Ce's and the Yi Mao's, side by side: Qian uses the Huangjin Ce's heaven lamp and Dipper vow, Zhen has both the Yi Mao's Nine Heavens and broken leg and the Huangjin Ce's tree spirit and death by the rod, and Gen agrees with the Yi Mao in the Mountain God and the youngest son. 'Chun ting' (the toon-tree courtyard) means the father (the 'Xuan hall' means the mother, see the next entry); 'head-wind' is a headache that does not go away; 'dou qi' is read as 'dou zhai' (a fast of the Dipper) from the context, and the 'yuan' of 'su yuan' (a vegetarian vow) is the character for 'source', which this book often writes for the character for 'vow' (both forms of 'incense vow' occur); the 'Three Mao' are the three Mao lords of the Daoist Mount Mao; 'Wangshu Scripture' is probably the 'Yushu Scripture' (the Jade Pivot Scripture), which is the name written in this chapter's later 'general methods of exorcism'; the 'yue' in 'yue si' properly means to boil, and is read here as drowned from the neighbouring line ('drowned'); 'Earth clans' has no explanation and is rendered literally; the verse 'hands and fingers all turn to sores, and the dead with rotten noses' is loose in wording and is translated against the commentary below, 'ghosts of arm sores, broken hands, rotten fingers and rotten noses'. 'Kan in the outer three lines' is rendered literally and means Kan in the outer three lines (the outer trigram). Names of gods, spirits and stars in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; they are cults and stars of the Wu region that the book does not explain, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
