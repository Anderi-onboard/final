# 疾病论·死生之兆(二)：催尸之杀怕临病者之爻、春辰夏未秋戌冬丑兴还死；天转杀地转杀遇日辰旺相便言死处；春忌卯夏嫌午秋酉冬子；不论少年老年一命见阎君

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 疾病论里接着讲死的征兆：要忌催尸杀，怕它落在病人的爻上；春天的辰、夏天的未最难当，秋天的戌、冬天的丑发动还是死；又嫌天转杀、地转杀，是最凶的神，再遇到日辰旺相，就可以说出死的地方；春天忌卯爻卯日，夏天嫌午位午爻，秋天的金爻、酉日最忌，冬天的子爻遇上子日；不论是白面少年，还是雪白鬓发的老人，家产再多，也总要拿一条命去见阎王。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on illness goes on with the signs of death: one must avoid the Urging-the-Corpse killing and fear it falling on the sick person's line; Chen in spring and Wei in summer are the hardest to bear, and Xu in autumn and Chou in winter, rising, still mean death; also dislike the Heaven-Turning and Earth-Turning killings, the most ill-omened spirits, and if they meet a strong Day one may name the place of death; in spring avoid a Mao line and a Mao day, in summer a Wu place and Wu line, in autumn the Metal line and the You day, in winter a Zi line meeting a Zi day; whether a fair-faced youth or an old person with snow-white temples, however much wealth a family has, one must still go with one's life to see Lord Yama.

> [!note] 按
> 「催尸杀」「天转杀」「地转杀」是神煞名：催尸杀按春辰夏未秋戌冬丑取（和下面的句子对得上），天转杀、地转杀的位置这里没有给。「便言死处」的「死处」按字面是死的地方，也可能是死的时间，书里没有说。「秋金酉日」是秋天的金爻和酉日，两者都算。卜筮全书神煞歌例里另有「丧车杀」（LY.ILL.BSQS.53）和「沐浴杀」，位置也是春辰夏未秋戌冬丑一类的取法，可对照。
>
> *Note:* The 'Urging-the-Corpse', 'Heaven-Turning' and 'Earth-Turning' killings are spirit-stars: the Urging-the-Corpse killing is taken as Chen in spring, Wei in summer, Xu in autumn and Chou in winter (which matches the lines that follow), and the positions of the Heaven-Turning and Earth-Turning killings are not given here. 'The place of death' is literally where death happens, and may mean when; the book does not say. 'Autumn Metal You day' means the Metal line in autumn and the You day, and both count. The spirit-star verses of the Bushi Quanshu also have a 'Funeral Carriage killing' (LY.ILL.BSQS.53) and a 'Bathing killing', placed in the same way (Chen in spring, Wei in summer, Xu in autumn, Chou in winter), and can be compared.
