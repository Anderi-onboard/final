# 求师·卦无父母而鬼化出：带贵人多是生员、不带贵人或临衰绝必是吏人、加白虎或带刑害则是有病之人

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》求师章的旧注说：卦里没有父母爻，看鬼爻化出什么来断老师是什么人：鬼带贵人化出，多半是生员；不带贵人，或者临在衰、绝上，一定是吏人；再加白虎，或者带着刑害，就是有病的人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* An old commentary in the Huangjin Ce's chapter on seeking a teacher says: if the hexagram has no Resource line, what the teacher is is judged from what the ghost line turns out as: a ghost carrying the Noble is mostly a licentiate; one not carrying the Noble, or sitting on decline or extinction, is surely a clerk; with the White Tiger added, or carrying punishment and harm, he is a man who is ill.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8428 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦无父母，而鬼带贵人化出，多是生员。 | 卦里没有父母爻，而鬼带着贵人化出来，多半是生员（秀才）。 | If the hexagram has no Resource line and a ghost carrying the Noble turns out, he is mostly a licentiate. |
> | 不带贵人，或临衰绝，必是吏人。 | 不带贵人，或者临在衰、绝上，一定是吏人。 | If it does not carry the Noble, or sits on decline or extinction, he is surely a clerk. |
> | 加白虎或带刑害，则是有病之人。 | 再加白虎，或者带着刑害，就是有病的人。 | If the White Tiger is added, or it carries punishment and harm, he is a man who is ill. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 求师章是占请老师（教书先生）的一章。这里卦里没有父母爻（父母代表老师），就看鬼爻化出来的是什么来断老师是个什么人；只有最后一句讲到「有病之人」，所以收在他人病里。「生员」是秀才，「吏人」是衙门里的小吏。这一章其余讲老师才学、亲疏的句子不收。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on seeking a teacher is about divining a schoolmaster to engage. Here the hexagram has no Resource line (Resource stands for the teacher), so what kind of man the teacher is is judged from what the ghost line turns out as; only the last sentence speaks of 'a man who is ill', which is why it is taken under the illness of others. A 'licentiate' is a holder of the lowest degree, and a 'clerk' a petty official in a government office. The other lines of the chapter, on the teacher's learning and on kinship, are not taken.
