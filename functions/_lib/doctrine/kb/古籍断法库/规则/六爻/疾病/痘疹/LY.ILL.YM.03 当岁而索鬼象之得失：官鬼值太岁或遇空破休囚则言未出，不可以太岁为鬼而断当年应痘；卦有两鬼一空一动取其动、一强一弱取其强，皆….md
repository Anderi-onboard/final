# 当岁而索鬼象之得失：官鬼值太岁或遇空破休囚则言未出，不可以太岁为鬼而断当年应痘；卦有两鬼一空一动取其动、一强一弱取其强，皆应当出

六爻 · 疾病 · 痘疹 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问哪一年出痘，要探究鬼象的得失；卦里有两个鬼时，要考察其中一爻的虚实。官鬼如果值太岁，或者遇到空、破、休、囚，就说还没出，后来即使日、月会合，也不能说就有：比如己亥年辰月辛未日，占有没有痘，得同人卦，亥鬼旬空，整年平安，可见不能因为太岁就是鬼就断当年应痘。如果卦里有两个鬼，一个空一个动，就取动的；一个强一个弱，就取强的，都应当出痘；这就是所说的考察一爻的虚实。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For the year, seek the gain or loss of the ghost's image; where there are two ghosts, examine the emptiness or fullness of one line. If the Pressure line falls on the Tai Sui, or meets Void, breaking, resting or trapped, say it has not yet come out; even if later the Day and Month meet, one cannot say there is pox: for example, in the Jihai year, the Chen month, on the Xinwei day, someone divined whether there would be pox and obtained Tongren; the Hai ghost was in Void and all year long he was well and at peace, so one cannot take the Tai Sui being the ghost as meaning the pox answers that year. If the hexagram has two ghosts, one Void and one moving, take the moving one; if one strong and one weak, take the strong one; in each case the pox should come out; this is what 'examine the emptiness or fullness of one line' means.
