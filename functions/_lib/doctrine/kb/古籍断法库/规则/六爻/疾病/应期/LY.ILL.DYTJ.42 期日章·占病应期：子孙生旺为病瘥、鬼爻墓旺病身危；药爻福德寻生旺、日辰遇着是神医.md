# 期日章·占病应期：子孙生旺为病瘥、鬼爻墓旺病身危；药爻福德寻生旺、日辰遇着是神医

六爻 · 疾病 · 应期 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看病愈的日子：子孙生旺的时候，病好；鬼爻入墓、旺的时候，病人危险。药爻（福德）要找它生旺的日子，遇到那个日辰，就是遇到神医。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For the day of recovery from illness: when Output is born strong, the illness is cured; when the Pressure line enters a tomb or is strong, the sick person is in danger. For the medicine line (Output) look for the day on which it is born strong, and meeting that day is meeting a divine physician.

## 《断易天机·期日章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 962 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The chapter on the day of the outcome", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 药爻福德寻生旺， | 药爻（福德）要找它生旺的日子， | For the medicine line (Output) look for the day on which it is born strong, |
> | 日辰遇着是神医（6）。 | 遇到那个日辰，就是遇到神医。 | and meeting that day is meeting a divine physician. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这是《期日章》这首歌里说到病的两联：歌里一联管一件事的应期，求财、六甲（怀孕生产）、行人、病、失物、婚姻、求官、公事、阴宅、医药、天气各一联。「子孙生旺为病瘥」是子孙爻长生或帝旺的日子病好；「鬼爻墓旺病身危」是鬼爻入墓或旺的日子病重。「药爻福德寻生旺，日辰遇着是神医」是药爻（子孙福德）生旺的日辰，医药有效，「神医」按字面译。歌后有今人写的「（3）」「（6）」注号，那几条注不收。这两联和孙膑歌的「福德持世鬼暂退」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.02）、火珠林「占疾病何日愈」（LY.ILL.DYTJ.18）里子孙福德临月日愈病是同一个道理。
>
> *Note:* These are the two couplets on illness in the song of the 'chapter on the day of the outcome': in the song each couplet governs the timing of one matter: wealth, the six jia (pregnancy and birth), a traveller, illness, lost things, marriage, seeking office, official business, a yin house (a grave), medicine and the weather one couplet each. 'When Output is born strong the illness is cured' is that the illness gets better on the day when the Output line is at Long Life or Emperor's Prime; 'when the Pressure line enters a tomb or is strong the sick person is in danger' is that the illness is heavy on the day when the Pressure line enters a tomb or is strong. 'For the medicine line (Output) look for the day on which it is born strong; meeting that day is meeting a divine physician' is the day branch on which the medicine line (Output) is born strong, when the medicine is effective, and 'divine physician' is rendered literally. After the song there are annotation marks such as '(3)' and '(6)' written by a modern hand, and those annotations are not quoted. These two couplets are the same principle as Sun Bin's song, 'Output holding the Self, the ghost retreats for a time' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.02), and the Huozhu Lin's 'on which day an illness will be cured' (LY.ILL.DYTJ.18), in which Output arriving on the month or day cures the illness.
