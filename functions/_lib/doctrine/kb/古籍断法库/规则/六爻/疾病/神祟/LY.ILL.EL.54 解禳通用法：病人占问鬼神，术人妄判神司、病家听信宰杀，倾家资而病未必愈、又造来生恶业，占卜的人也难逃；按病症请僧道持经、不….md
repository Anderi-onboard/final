# 解禳通用法：病人占问鬼神，术人妄判神司、病家听信宰杀，倾家资而病未必愈、又造来生恶业，占卜的人也难逃；按病症请僧道持经、不宰杀——瘟疫合门斋素请戒僧、疟疾设灶神、胎产诵玉枢经、怪病念观音经、冤业梁皇职、亡魂地藏经、火殃诵火德经、精魅请天师或诵真武经、膈噎施食、屠宰索命戒杀放生、妄罚誓愿斋祭勾消或叩城隍回缴、动土告土设坛、覆宗绝嗣改修、侵占祠宇修复

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 病人占问是哪路鬼神的时候，术士随口乱判是哪位神司，病家听信了就宰杀牲畜，把有限的家财倾尽，病未必能好，还给来生造下恶业，连占卜的人也难免。所以解禳按病症来，请僧道念经、斋戒，不用宰杀：瘟疫是上帝降下的，全家一起吃素，虔诚地请戒行严的僧人持经，自然消解，或者请童男礼拜也能得吉；疟疾或有鬼邪，依通书的样式设灶神就能送走；胎产是九天圣母管的，请道士诵玉枢经；怪病、倚草附木的妖，请僧人念观音经；冤业相寻，用梁皇忏（原文作「梁皇职」）解；亡魂出现，用地藏经超度；火殃降下，选火闭日或火收日，请道士诵火德经；精魅所临，大的告天师请法，小的诵真武经驱走；得膈噎症的人，布施食物、吃淡口，能超度饿鬼；屠宰索命的，只有戒杀放生才能免；妄罚誓愿、冒犯天曹的，要设斋祭奠，勾消旧账，穷得还不起愿的可以向天曹递疏、叩城隍退回，富家许过猪羊牛的愿，如今穷了可以做粉牲、纸钱纸马，到天库地库勾消也行；动土兴工出了事要告土，按那个方位设坛，没力量的用粉团斋殷太岁；经商、江湖上的诵三官经，也保读书人的功名；持玉皇经、虔诚祝告药师佛求今世的延年；金刚经作来生的福利；求嗣立梓童清坛，婴幼要念大洞尊经；毁平坟墓、社坛的，不是求神能除，只有改修才能解；侮慢神明的要皈依三宝或许能散；侵占祠宇的只有修复，旧庙才得安全。易卦分类的是万物之情，不能全部备写，卜筮通神明之德，可以自己参详。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a sick person divines about which ghost or spirit it is, the diviners wrongly judge the gods and offices at random, the sick household believes them and slaughters animals, exhausting its limited household means; the illness is not necessarily cured, and evil karma is made for the next life, which even the diviner can hardly escape. So exorcism follows the illness, inviting monks and Daoists to recite scriptures and keep fasts, without slaughter: epidemic is sent down by the Supreme Emperor, and the whole household eats vegetarian together and reverently invites a monk who keeps the precepts to recite the scripture, and it dissolves of itself, or boys performing worship can also bring good fortune; malaria, or where there are ghosts and evil, can be sent away by setting up the Kitchen God in the form of the almanac; childbirth is presided over by the Holy Mother of the Nine Heavens, and a Daoist is invited to recite the Yushu Scripture; for strange illnesses and demons that lean on grass and cling to trees, a monk chants the Guanyin Scripture; where grievances and karma pursue each other, the Liang Emperor rite (the text has 'Liang Emperor office') resolves it; where a dead soul appears, the Earth Store Scripture delivers it; if a fire calamity descends, choose a Fire Close day or a Fire Receive day and invite a Daoist to recite the Fire Virtue Scripture; where sprites have come, if great, appeal to the Heavenly Master to request rites, and if small, recite the Zhenwu Scripture to drive them away; one with a choking obstruction of the gullet gives food and keeps a bland mouth, which can deliver the hungry ghosts; where slaughtered animals demand a life, only abstaining from killing and releasing life can avoid it; those who rashly swore oaths and vows and offended the Heavenly Bureau must set a fast and offering to cancel the old debt, and one too poor to redeem the vow can present a memorial to the Heavenly Bureau and kowtow to the City God to return it, and a rich family that vowed pigs, sheep and oxen and is now poor can make flour animals, paper money and paper horses, and cancelling at the Heavenly Treasury and Earthly Treasury will do; when digging and building bring trouble, report to the Earth and set up an altar according to that quarter, and those without means offer flour balls as a fast to Tai Sui Yin; merchants and those on rivers and lakes recite the Three Officials Scripture, which also protects scholars' careers; reciting the Jade Emperor Scripture and reverently praying to the Medicine Buddha seeks long life in this world; the Diamond Sutra makes blessings for the next life; to seek an heir set up a pure altar to Zitong, and for infants recite the Great Cavern Honoured Scripture; where tombs and earth altars have been levelled, praying to the gods cannot remove it, and only rebuilding can resolve it; those who have insulted the gods must take refuge in the Three Jewels and may perhaps be dispersed; for encroaching on a shrine, only restoration, and then the old temple is safe. The hexagrams of the Yi classify the natures of all things and cannot all be written out in full; divination communicates with the virtue of the spirits, and one may examine it for oneself.

> 这一节是「总论」的第五段（编号「之五」），也是整个搜决神鬼章的收尾：前面教人怎么认是哪路鬼神，这里教人认了以后怎么办——不杀生，按病症请僧道念经斋戒。开头一句话点明立场：术人乱判神司、病家听信宰杀，既花光家产、病未必好、又造来生恶业，连占卜的人也难免。和本章结语「恐人妄断，恒忧好杀而费财」（LY.ILL.EL.43）、易冒「祭祀莫善于解禳，解禳莫善于修身」（LY.ILL.YM.63、YM.64）是同一个态度。文字多处含混，译文里都标了：「了剌降」不成词；「持通连经」的「通连经」不知是哪部经；「虐疾」是「疟疾」（疟疾写作虐疾）；「可致临盆之废」的「废」字意思不明，译文按「可以除去临盆的难处」的大意；「梁皇职」应是「梁皇忏」（梁皇宝忏，超度冤业的忏法），原文写作「职」；「火乍汝矣」不成句，译文只标出；「之途屠宰索命者」的「之途」疑是「六道」之类，前面文字缺失，这里译作「屠宰索命的这一路」；「旁生之趣」是佛家说的「旁生趣」（畜生道）；「殷太岁」按字面译作 Tai Sui Yin；「梓童」「大洞尊经」按字面音译；「至如水火不通…而生灾」一句列了几种招祸的行为，书里没有接着写办法，「起灭」「艺侮」也不成词，译文里都直接标明。「火闭日、火收日」按字面译作「Fire Close day」「Fire Receive day」，应是建除十二神里的闭日、收日，书里没有解释。「易卦类万物之情，因不悉备」是说本书没有把万物的解禳全部写出，读的人可以自己参详。 这一段里神、经、法事的名字，书里没有解释，这里按字面译或音译，不作认定。

> *Note:* This is the fifth section of the 'general discourse' (numbered 'five'), and it closes the whole 'searching out ghosts and spirits' chapter: the earlier parts teach how to tell which ghost or spirit it is, and this teaches what to do once one knows: not to kill, but to invite monks and Daoists to recite scriptures and keep fasts according to the illness. The opening sentence states the position: diviners wrongly judging the gods and offices and sick households believing them and slaughtering animals both use up the household's means, may not cure the illness, and make evil karma for the next life, which even the diviner can hardly escape. It is the same attitude as this chapter's closing passage 'fearing people will judge at random, always worrying that they love killing and waste money' (LY.ILL.EL.43) and the Yi Mao's 'of sacrifice none is better than exorcism, and of exorcism none is better than cultivating oneself' (LY.ILL.YM.63, YM.64). The wording is loose in many places, and the translation marks them: 'liao la jiang' is not a word; the 'Tonglian Scripture' is not known to be any particular scripture; 'nue ji' is the sickness 'nue ji' (malaria), written with a variant character; the character 'fei' in 'can bring about the fei of labour' has an unclear sense, and the translation gives the general sense 'can remove the trouble of labour'; 'Liang Emperor office' should be 'Liang Emperor repentance' (the Liang Emperor precious repentance, a rite to deliver karmic grievances), the original writing 'office' for 'repentance'; 'huo zha ru yi' is not a sentence and is only marked; the 'zhi tu' in 'zhi tu of slaughter, demanding life' is probably 'the six paths' or the like with earlier words missing, and is translated as 'the path of slaughter where the victims demand a life'; 'the animal realm' is the Buddhist 'pang sheng qu' (the realm of animals); 'Tai Sui Yin' is rendered literally; 'Zitong' and 'Great Cavern Honoured Scripture' are transliterated literally; the sentence 'as for those who block water and fire ... and bring on calamity' lists several kinds of conduct that call down misfortune but the book does not go on to give a remedy, and 'starting and destroying' and 'yi wu' are not words either, and the translation marks all of these directly. 'Fire Close day' and 'Fire Receive day' are literal, and are probably the Close and Receive days of the twelve day-spirits, which the book does not explain. 'The hexagrams of the Yi classify the natures of all things, and so cannot all be written out' says that this book has not written out the exorcism of all things in full, and the reader may examine it for himself. Names of gods, spirits, scriptures and rites in this passage are given by transliteration or plain rendering; the book does not explain them, and none is identified here beyond what the book says.
