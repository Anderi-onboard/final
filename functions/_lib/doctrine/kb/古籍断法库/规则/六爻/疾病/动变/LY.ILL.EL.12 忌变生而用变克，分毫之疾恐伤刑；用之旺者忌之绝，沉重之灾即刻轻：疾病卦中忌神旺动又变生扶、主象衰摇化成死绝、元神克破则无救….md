# 忌变生而用变克，分毫之疾恐伤刑；用之旺者忌之绝，沉重之灾即刻轻：疾病卦中忌神旺动又变生扶、主象衰摇化成死绝、元神克破则无救；用神居衰位而化出帝旺长生、忌神动化克伤死绝之乡则旦夕可安

六爻 · 疾病 · 动变 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 忌神变生、用神变克，一丝一毫的病也恐怕要伤刑；用神旺、忌神绝，沉重的灾也立刻转轻。像这样断定，可以留传万古，何须再到海外去求明白。注说：疾病卦里，忌神旺又发动、又变出生扶，主象衰弱摇动、变成死绝，元神又被克破，怎么还有救？用神即使居于衰位，如果化出帝旺、长生，又遇上扶助，忌神虽然发动而变成克伤死绝之乡，不能制用神，病虽然沉重，旦夕可以安好，这样推没有差错。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Adversary changes into generation and the Subject changes into control, even the slightest ailment may bring harm; if the Subject is strong and the Adversary in Extinction, a heavy calamity lightens at once. What is decided thus can be handed down for ten thousand ages; why then seek clarity overseas? The commentary says: in an illness hexagram, if the Adversary is strong and moving and also changes into generation and support, the chief image is weak and swaying and changes into death or Extinction, and the Support is controlled and broken, how could there be rescue? Though the Subject sits in a weak place, if it changes into Peak or Birth and meets support, then although the Adversary moves and changes into a place of control, wounding, death or Extinction and cannot restrain the Subject, the illness, however heavy, may be at ease by morning or night, and there is no error in reckoning so.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的歌诀是把上两条（LY.ILL.EL.08、EL.09）里「变」的部分单拿出来说：忌神和用神各自的变化方向，比它们当下的强弱更要紧——忌神变生、用神变克是凶，用神旺忌神绝是吉。「刑」是刑伤。「何须海外再求明」指不必到别处去求更高明的说法，这一套已经够了。歌诀的后四句（卦名、神煞、空身空命、无鬼无财）放在下面 LY.ILL.EL.13、EL.14。「帝旺、长生」是十二长生里的旺位和生位。
>
> *Note:* The verse takes up the 'change' part of the last two entries (LY.ILL.EL.08, EL.09): the direction of change of the Adversary and the Subject matters more than their present strength — the Adversary changing into generation and the Subject into control is ominous, the Subject strong and the Adversary in Extinction auspicious. 'Harm' is punishment or injury. 'Why then seek clarity overseas' means there is no need to look elsewhere for a higher teaching, since this suffices. The last four lines of the verse (hexagram names, spirit-stars, Void of body and natal branch, no ghost and no Wealth) are placed below in LY.ILL.EL.13 and EL.14. 'Peak' and 'Birth' are the strong and generating stages of the twelve life stages.
