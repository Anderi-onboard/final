# 总断千金赋·死绝岂可持身（旧注：死绝二爻临持世身主象必不利，占人有难、占病无救、占医无效、占事不济，变动化入亦然）

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》总断千金赋说：「死、绝，怎么可以持身？」旧注：死、绝两爻，临在、持着世、身、主象上，一定不利：占人有灾难，占病没有救，占医没有效，占事不成；变动化入的也是这样。王洪绪的这一条评语只有半句，「死、绝于日辰的爻，临持世、身、用神的，各种占都不利，变动化入」，原文到这里就断了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements says: 'Death and Extinction: how can they hold the Body?' The old commentary: when the Death and Extinction lines sit on or hold the Self, the Body or the principal image, it is sure to be unfavourable: in divining a person he is in difficulty, in divining illness there is no rescue, in divining a doctor he is ineffective, an affair does not succeed; so too when they are moved or changed into. Wang Hongxu's comment on this has only half a sentence, 'a line in Death or Extinction by the Day branch that sits on or holds the Self, the Body or the Subject: no divination is favourable, in moving and changing into', where the text breaks off.

> | 死绝于日辰之爻临持世身用神者，诸占不利，变动化入 | 死、绝于日辰的爻，临持世、身、用神的，各种占都不利，变动化入 | A line in Death or Extinction by the Day branch that sits on or holds the Self, the Body or the Subject: no divination is favourable, in moving and changing into |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这是「刑害不宜临用」那一联（LY.ILL.HJC.130）的下半句。「死绝」是十二长生里的死、绝两位，不是「死亡」。「占医无效」的「医」是请医生。王洪绪的评语在原书里印到「变动化入」就断了（是原书如此，不是本库截的），所以这里只有半句。「持身」的「身」书里连说「世、身、主象」，「身」应是卦身。
>
> *Note:* This is the second half of the couplet 'punishment and harm should not fall on the Subject' (LY.ILL.HJC.130). 'Death and Extinction' are two of the twelve stages of life and not 'death'. The 'doctor' of 'in divining a doctor' is calling in a physician. Wang Hongxu's comment is printed in the book up to 'in moving and changing into' and breaks off (this is how the book is, not cut by this library), so there is only half a sentence here. The 'Body' of 'hold the Body' is, as the text says 'the Self, the Body and the principal image' in a run, the hexagram's Body.
