# 卜妇人者姑姨姊妹之外皆用妻财，卜同辈者宗弟兄之外皆用应爻：姑姨亦看父母，姊妹同推弟兄，称弟兄而非宗服者不同本用应

六爻 · 疾病 · 他人病 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占女人的病，除了姑、姨、姐妹以外，其余都推作同类，用妻财爻：姑姨这样的亲人也看父母爻，姐妹这样的同辈同样按兄弟爻推，除此以外都用财爻。占同辈的人，除了同宗的兄弟以外，都属于不同本源，用应爻；称作兄弟、但不在宗服之内的，一律用应爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a woman's illness, apart from paternal aunts, maternal aunts and sisters, all are reckoned as the same kind and take the Wealth line: aunts are looked at through the Resource line, sisters are reckoned as brothers, and all others take the Wealth line. In divining someone of one's own generation, apart from brothers of the same clan, all are of a different root and take the Other line; those addressed as brothers who are outside the clan's mourning grades all take the Other line.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和前一条（LY.ILL.YM.21）是连着的：前一条按关系取，这一条把女人和同辈分开说。女人里，姑姨算父母一辈、姐妹算兄弟一辈，其余（妻、女、妾、婢）一概取妻财；同辈里，只有同宗的兄弟取兄弟爻，别的人不同本源，取应爻。
>
> *Note:* This entry runs on from the last (LY.ILL.YM.21): that took each relation by its own line, and this speaks of women and of one's own generation separately. Among women, aunts count as the parents' generation and sisters as the brothers' generation, and all the rest (wife, daughter, concubine, maid) take the Wealth line; among one's own generation, only brothers of the same clan take the Peer line, and others, not of the same root, take the Other line.
