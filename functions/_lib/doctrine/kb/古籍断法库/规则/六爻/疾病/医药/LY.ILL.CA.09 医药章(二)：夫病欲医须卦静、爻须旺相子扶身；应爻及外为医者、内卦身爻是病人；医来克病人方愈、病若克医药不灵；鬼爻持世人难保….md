# 医药章(二)：夫病欲医须卦静、爻须旺相子扶身；应爻及外为医者、内卦身爻是病人；医来克病人方愈、病若克医药不灵；鬼爻持世人难保、吉曜加临大利贞

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丈夫的病要医，要卦静，爻旺相，子孙扶身。应爻和外卦是医生，内卦和身爻是病人。医克病，病人才好；病克医，药不灵。鬼爻持世，人难保；吉曜加临，大吉大利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a man's illness is to be treated the hexagram must be still, the lines strong and Output supporting the body. The Other line and the outer trigram are the doctor; the inner trigram and the Body line are the sick person. If the doctor controls the illness the person is cured; if the illness controls the doctor the medicine is not effective. The Pressure line holding the Self, the person can hardly be kept; a lucky star added, greatly favourable.

> [!note] 按
> 这是医药章的第二首。「应爻及外为医者，内卦身爻是病人」的划分（应和外卦是医、内卦和身爻是病人）和天玄赋（LY.ILL.TXF.12「世为病人，应及外卦为医药」）、易隐（LY.ILL.YY.21「内卦世爻用爻是病人、外卦应爻子孙是医药」）一致，只是这里说「身爻」而不说「世爻」。「医来克病人方愈」的「病人」原文如此，意思是「医克病」。「吉曜」是吉星，书里没有列名。
>
> *Note:* This is the second poem of the chapter on medicine. Its division 'the Other line and the outer trigram are the doctor, the inner trigram and the Body line are the sick person' agrees with the Tianxuan Fu (LY.ILL.TXF.12, 'the Self is the sick person, the Other and the outer trigram are the doctor and the medicine') and the Yi Yin (LY.ILL.YY.21, 'the inner trigram, the Self line and the Subject are the sick person, the outer trigram, the Other line and Output the doctor and medicine'), except that here it says 'the Body line' and not 'the Self line'. The 'sick person' of 'if the doctor controls the sick person he is cured' is as the original reads, and means 'the doctor controls the illness'. 'Lucky star' is a lucky star, of which the book gives no name.
