# 空身空命皆非忌，无鬼无财岂足凭：甲子旬空戌亥不等于属猪属犬人皆命绝；病人无鬼必死之说不准，无鬼不过无神祟；无财只是眼下不思饮食，惟夫占妻、主占仆忌财空

六爻 · 疾病 · 生死 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 空身、空命都不必忌讳，无鬼、无财哪里够得上凭据。注说：比如甲子旬占，空的是戌、亥，天下万万属猪、属狗的人，难道都命绝了？前人又说病人无鬼必死，难道没有验证过吗？《天玄赋》里虽然说占病无鬼，就没有叩告的门路，是天年命尽，这病不能好。可是比如哥哥占弟弟的病，鬼是忌神，怎么该在卦里？又如父亲占儿子的病，鬼是仇人，怎么可以用它？如果按道理论病，只取用爻的旺衰生克，就能断定存亡。凡是卦里无鬼，不过是没有神祟罢了，难道就是天年命尽？何况现在屡次试验没有官鬼的卦，也未必死。又辨别没有财的说法更加差错：财爻虽然是饮食，没有财，不过是眼下不吃东西，怎么能丧命？只有丈夫占妻子、主人占仆人来看，才忌讳财空，其余占的都不忌讳。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Void of the Body and the Void of the natal branch need not be feared, and no ghost or no Wealth is hardly enough to rely on. The commentary says: for example, when a divination is made in the Jiazi decade the Void branches are Xu and Hai, and of the innumerable people under heaven born in the year of the pig or the dog, are all to have their lives cut off? The men of old also said that a patient with no ghost must die, and was there no proof? Although the Tianxuan Fu says that in divining illness with no ghost there is no door to knock on and appeal, it is the end of one's allotted years, and the illness will not be cured. But for instance, if an elder brother divines a younger brother's illness, the ghost is the Adversary, and how should it be in the hexagram? Or if a father divines a son's illness, the ghost is an enemy, and how can it be used? If the illness is discussed on principle, only the strength and the generation and control of the Subject line are taken, and life or death is decided. A hexagram with no ghost only lacks a haunting, and is it therefore the end of one's allotted years? Moreover, no-Official hexagrams have been tried many times and the person did not necessarily die. Again the claim about lacking Wealth is even more mistaken: though the Wealth line is food and drink, lacking it only means that for the moment he does not eat, how can that cost his life? Only in a husband divining a wife or a master a servant is a Void Wealth line feared, and in all other divinations it is not feared.

> [!warning] 打架
> 病人卦里无鬼、无财是不是必死：易林补遗说无鬼不过是没有神祟，不等于天年命尽；无财不过眼下不思饮食，只有夫占妻、主占仆才忌财空；它批评的是《天玄赋》的「占病无鬼必无叩告之门，乃天年命尽」；易冒同样说无鬼无财、身空命空皆不死（LY.ILL.YM.11）；黄金策却说官鬼虽是恶煞不可无，不上卦要寻伏，伏又空则病必不能痊（LY.ILL.HJC.49），黄金策旧注、王洪绪对这句的范围也不一致；易隐里晁以道说用爻、命爻不上卦者死，男忌鬼空、女忌财空（LY.ILL.YY.10）；黄金策又说禄系妻财、寿属父母，财空无财则不思饮食（LY.ILL.HJC.38）。几家说法不同，书里没有裁决。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether a patient's hexagram with no ghost or no Wealth means certain death: the Yilin Buyi says no ghost only lacks a haunting and does not mean the end of one's allotted years, and no Wealth only means he does not want to eat for the moment, a Void Wealth line being feared only in a husband divining a wife or a master a servant; what it criticises is the Tianxuan Fu's 'in divining illness with no ghost there is no door to knock on, it is the end of one's allotted years'; the Yi Mao likewise says that no ghost and no Wealth, Void of the Body or of the natal branch, do not mean death (LY.ILL.YM.11); the Huangjin Ce, however, says that although the Pressure line is a malign killer it cannot be absent, that when it is absent from the hexagram one must look for it hidden, and that if the hidden one is also Void the illness cannot be cured (LY.ILL.HJC.49), and the old commentary and Wang Hongxu differ on the scope of that saying; in the Yi Yin, Chao Yidao says that one whose Subject line or natal-branch line is absent from the hexagram dies, and that men fear Void ghosts and women Void Wealth (LY.ILL.YY.10); the Huangjin Ce also says that emolument belongs to Wealth and long life to the Resource line, and that a Void or absent Wealth means no appetite (LY.ILL.HJC.38). The books differ, and none gives a ruling.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条把上一条歌诀的最后两句「空身空命皆非忌，无鬼无财岂足凭」拿出来单讲：空命（本命落旬空）、无鬼（卦无官鬼）、无财（卦无妻财）三种说法，评注者一个一个驳。「叩告之门」是可以向鬼神叩告的门路，出自《天玄赋》所说的「占病无鬼必无叩告之门」（黄金策也用了这句，见 LY.ILL.HJC.49）。「兄占弟病，鬼乃忌神」是说占兄弟的病，用神是兄弟，官鬼克兄弟，所以是忌神；「父占子病，鬼作仇人」是说占子孙的病，子孙的仇神是官鬼——所以这些卦里官鬼不该出现，何必因为无鬼就断死。
>
> *Note:* This entry takes the last two lines of the verse in the last entry, 'the Void of the Body and of the natal branch need not be feared, no ghost or no Wealth is hardly enough to rely on', and discusses them separately: three sayings, Void natal branch (the natal branch falling on the ten-day Void), no ghost (the hexagram lacks a Pressure line) and no Wealth (it lacks a Wealth line), and the commentator refutes them one by one. The 'door to knock on and appeal' is the way of appealing to ghosts and spirits, from the Tianxuan Fu's 'in divining illness with no ghost there is no door to knock on' (the Huangjin Ce uses this sentence too; see LY.ILL.HJC.49). 'An elder brother divining a younger brother's illness, the ghost is the Adversary' means that in divining a brother's illness the Subject is the Peer line, and the Pressure line controls the Peer, so it is the Adversary; 'a father divining a son's illness, the ghost is an enemy' means that for a son's illness the enemy of Output is the Pressure line; so in such hexagrams the Pressure line ought not to appear, and there is no reason to judge death because the ghost is absent.
