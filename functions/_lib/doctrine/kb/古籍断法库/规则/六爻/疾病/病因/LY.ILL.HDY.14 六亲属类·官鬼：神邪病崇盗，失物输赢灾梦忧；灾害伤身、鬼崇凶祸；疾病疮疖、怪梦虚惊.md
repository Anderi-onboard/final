# 六亲属类·官鬼：神邪病崇盗，失物输赢灾梦忧；灾害伤身、鬼崇凶祸；疾病疮疖、怪梦虚惊

六爻 · 疾病 · 病因 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 海底眼的六亲属类里，官鬼管的物类有：神、邪、病、崇、盗，失物、输赢、灾、梦、忧；散句里还有「灾害伤身，鬼崇凶祸」「疾病疮疖，怪梦虚惊」——官鬼是病和鬼神作祟这一类事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the classes of the six relations in the Haidi Yan, among what Pressure governs are gods, evil, illness, curses and thieves, lost things, winning and losing, calamity, dreams and worry; the prose adds 'calamity harms the body, ghosts and curses, ill-fortune and misfortune' and 'illness, sores and boils, strange dreams and false alarms' — Pressure is illness and the matters of ghosts and spirits afflicting.

> [!note] 按
> 这是用类象的歌诀说出来的官鬼：占病时官鬼是「病」「崇」，和用神表里「占邪崇、雷霆、鬼神用官鬼」（文王金钱课 LY.ILL.WWJQ.02）、易卦发蒙歌「凡疾病皆视此爻，知鬼神情状」（LY.ILL.BSYG.01）是同一个意思。「崇」是「祟」的写法。「尻」本义是臀部，这里是一连串物象里的一项，按字面译。散句后面还有一行「此尾五句，应在官鬼上，未知是否，存考」，是整理者的话，不引。
>
> *Note:* This is Pressure said in a verse of images: in divining illness Pressure is 'illness' and 'curses', which is the same sense as the table of the Subject 'divining evil curses, thunder, ghosts and spirits, use Pressure' (the Wenwang Jinqian Ke LY.ILL.WWJQ.02) and the 'Yigua Fameng Ge' 'in all illness look to this line, and know the state of ghosts and spirits' (LY.ILL.BSYG.01). The character for 'curse' is written in a variant form. 'The buttocks' is the original sense of the word, here one item in a string of images, and is translated literally. After the prose there is a line 'these last five lines should be under Pressure, it is not known; kept for study', which is the editor's words and is not quoted.
