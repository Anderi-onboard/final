# 子化子药杂不精：子孙是占药的用神，只喜一位出现、有气不空、日辰动爻扶持；卦中重叠太过或子孙重化子孙是医不精、用药杂而无功；王洪绪读作子孙化进神有效、化退神与伏吟卦不可服

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙是占药用的用神，只喜欢出现一位：有气、不落空，又有日辰、动爻扶持，就是最吉的兆头。如果卦里重叠太多，或者子孙又变成子孙，就是医生不精，用药杂乱而没有功效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the Subject in divining medicine and is glad to appear as one only: with force, not Void, and supported by the Day and moving lines, it is the best sign. If there are too many of them in the hexagram, or Output changes again into Output, the doctor is not skilled and his medicines are mixed and do no good.

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 「子化子」是好是坏：旧注说子孙重叠、或子孙又变子孙，是医生不精、用药杂；王洪绪说子孙变进神药有效，变退神和伏吟卦不可服，没有把子化子当成坏事。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether 'Output changing into Output' is good or bad: the old commentary says that Output doubled, or Output changing again into Output, means an unskilled doctor and mixed medicines; Wang Hongxu says that with Output changing into an Advancing spirit the medicine is effective, and with a Retreating spirit or in a Hidden Chant hexagram it may not be taken, and he does not treat Output changing into Output as a bad thing.
