# 考鬼的四种情形：鬼爻发动考其宫、卦爻俱静求之外卦、无鬼索伏考其飞爻、伏鬼若空则无鬼祟惟修德；此专考鬼、非断神

六爻 · 疾病 · 神祟 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻发动，就考察它所在的宫；卦爻都静，就从外卦去找；卦里没有鬼，就去找伏着的鬼，考察它上面的飞爻；伏鬼如果又落空，就致力于修德。注说：鬼爻独发，用八宫来考察这个鬼；爻和鬼都静，用外卦来考察；鬼爻隐伏，用鬼上面的飞爻来考察；鬼伏着又落空，就是没有鬼祟，只要修德就行了。这一节是专门考察鬼，不是判断神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line moves, examine its palace; if the hexagram and lines are all still, seek it in the outer trigram; if there is no ghost, look for the hidden one and examine the flying line above it; if the hidden ghost is also Void, devote oneself to cultivating virtue. The note says: if the Pressure line moves alone, examine the ghost by the eight palaces; if the lines and the ghost are all still, examine it by the outer trigram; if the Pressure line is hidden, examine it by the flying line above the ghost; if the hidden ghost is again Void, there is no haunting by a ghost, and one need only cultivate virtue. This section is solely to examine the ghost, not to judge the spirit.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条和 LY.ILL.YM.29「病从何起」是同一套四步（动看宫、静看外卦、无鬼看伏鬼的飞爻），只是那里问的是病从哪个方向来，这里问的是作祟的是什么。第四步「伏鬼若空」得出的结论是没有鬼祟，只有修德，这是全章「祭祀莫善于解禳，解禳莫善于修身」（LY.ILL.YM.64）那个结论的一个具体出口。注里最后一句「此专考鬼，非断神」把这一节和下一节（LY.ILL.YM.56 讲专断神）分开：前者是找到那个鬼，后者是给它定神名。
>
> *Note:* This entry is the same four steps as LY.ILL.YM.29, 'where the illness rose from' (moving, look at the palace; still, look at the outer trigram; no ghost, look at the flying line above a hidden ghost), except that there the question was from what direction the illness came, and here it is what is doing the haunting. The conclusion of the fourth step, 'if the hidden ghost is also Void', is that there is no haunting and only virtue remains, which is one concrete outlet of the chapter's final conclusion, 'of sacrifice none is better than exorcism, and of exorcism none is better than cultivating oneself' (LY.ILL.YM.64). The note's last sentence, 'solely to examine the ghost, not to judge the spirit', separates this section from the next (LY.ILL.YM.56, which is solely for judging the spirit): the first finds the ghost and the second names the spirit.
