# 衰中坐鬼身临则病虽轻而药力难扶：卦身临鬼病不脱体，衰弱无气缠绵难愈；世旺相出现久卧床褥虽良医亦无效；世持及主象身临持官墓者皆然

六爻 · 疾病 · 医药 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦身上坐着鬼，病就不能脱身。鬼衰弱没有气，是缠缠绵绵难好、拖延不愈的象。世爻旺相出现在卦里，却长久卧床，就算有好医生也没有效果。世爻持着鬼，以及主象、身爻临在官墓上的，也都是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure on the hexagram Body, the illness does not leave the body. If Pressure is weak and without force, it is an image of lingering, hard to heal, dragging on. If the Self line appears strong in the hexagram and yet he lies long in bed, even a good doctor has no effect. The same holds when the Self line holds Pressure, and when the main image or the body sits on the tomb of Pressure.

## 《黄金策·医药》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7024 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Doctors and medicine", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 衰中坐鬼，身临则病虽轻，而药力难扶。 | 在衰弱的地方坐着鬼，身爻临着它，病虽然轻，药力也很难扶起来。 | Pressure sitting in weakness, with the body on it: though the illness is light, the force of medicine can hardly lift it. |

^q1

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦身虽临衰弱之鬼，缠绵难愈之象，或主象身临官墓者亦然。 | 卦身即使临着衰弱的鬼，也是缠缠绵绵难好的象，或者主象、身爻临在官墓上的也是这样。 | Even when the hexagram Body sits on a weak Pressure line, it is an image of lingering and hard to heal, and the same holds when the main image or the body sits on the tomb of Pressure. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文「衰中坐鬼」和「身临」连读：鬼在衰处，身又临上去。旧注「世旺相出现，久卧床缛，虽有良医亦无效」的意思书里没有再解释，译文按字面。
>
> *Note:* The verse's 'Pressure sitting in weakness' and 'the body on it' are read together: Pressure in a weak place, with the body sitting on it. What the old commentary means by 'the Self line appearing strong yet lying long in bed, and even a good doctor having no effect' the book does not explain further, and the translation follows the letter.
