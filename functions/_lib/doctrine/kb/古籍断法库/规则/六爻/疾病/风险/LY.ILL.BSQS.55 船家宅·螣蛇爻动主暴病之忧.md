# 船家宅·螣蛇爻动主暴病之忧

六爻 · 疾病 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占船家（以船为家的人家）的一章里说：螣蛇爻发动，主有暴病的忧虑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on divining a boat household says: with the Coiling Snake line moving, there is worry of a sudden illness.

## 《卜筮全书·阐幽精要·十、船家宅》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9763 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Chanyou Jingyao, no. 10: houses on boats", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 螣蛇爻动， | 螣蛇爻发动， | With the Coiling Snake line moving, |
> | 主暴病之忧； | 主有暴病的忧虑； | there is worry of a sudden illness; |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 船家宅是占以船为家的人家的一章，按船的各个部位（船头、舵、桅杆、毛缆、橹蓬）和六神断吉凶。这一句只讲螣蛇发动主暴病之忧（「暴病」是来得急的病）；同一联的下半句讲朱雀、断桅之祸，不收。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on boat households is about divining the households that live on boats, and judges good and ill by the parts of the boat (the bow, the rudder, the mast, the cables, the sculling oar and awning) and the six spirits. This sentence says only that the Coiling Snake moving means worry of a sudden illness (a 'sudden illness' is one that comes on fast); the second half of the same couplet, on the Vermilion Bird and a broken mast, is not taken.
