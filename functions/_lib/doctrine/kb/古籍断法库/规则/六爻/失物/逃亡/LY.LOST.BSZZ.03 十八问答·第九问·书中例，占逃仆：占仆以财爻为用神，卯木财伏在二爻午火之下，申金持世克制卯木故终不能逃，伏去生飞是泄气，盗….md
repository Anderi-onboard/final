# 十八问答·第九问·书中例，占逃仆：占仆以财爻为用神，卯木财伏在二爻午火之下，申金持世克制卯木故终不能逃，伏去生飞是泄气，盗去的财物都窝在炉火人家，断甲子日拿获，果然子日在窝赌的铁匠家申时拿获——应子日是冲克午火飞神、生起卯木伏神

六爻 · 失物 · 逃亡 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神不上卦，看伏神在哪一爻之下，能出不能出，各有四种情形：日月生它、日月持它是一；飞神生伏神、动爻生它是二；日月动爻冲克飞神是三；飞神空破休囚、墓绝在日是四；这四种是有用的伏神。书中例：辰月丁巳日占逃走的仆人，得蹇卦：占仆人以财爻为用神，兑宫二爻卯木伏在本卦二爻午火之下，午火是飞神，卯木是伏神；现在申金持世克制卯木，终究不能逃走；但因为伏神去生飞神，叫做泄气，偷去的财物必定全窝在炉火人家，许他甲子日拿获；果然子日得到消息，在窝赌的铁匠家，申时拿到。应在子日，是冲克午火飞神、生起卯木伏神的缘故。《黄金策》说「伏无提挈终徒尔，飞不推开亦枉然」，这一卦的应验就是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject does not appear, look at which line the hidden spirit lies under; whether it can come out comes in four ways: the Month or the Day gives it life or holds it, which is one; the flying spirit gives life to the hidden one or a moving line gives it life, two; the Month, Day or a moving line clashes or controls the flying spirit, three; the flying spirit is Void, broken, resting, trapped, or in the tomb or extinct on the Day, four; these four are useful hidden spirits. Worked example: in the Chen month, on a Dingsi day, a man divined about a runaway servant and got Jian: in divining for a servant the Wealth line is the Subject; Mao-wood in the second line of the Dui palace is hidden under Wu-fire in the second line of the present hexagram, Wu-fire being the flying spirit and Mao-wood the hidden spirit; now Shen-metal holds the Self and controls Mao-wood, so he cannot in the end get away; but because the hidden spirit gives life to the flying one, which is called draining, the stolen goods are all harboured in a household with a furnace fire, and it was promised that he would be caught on the Jiazi day; indeed on the Zi day word came that he was at the house of a blacksmith who harboured gamblers, and he was taken in the Shen hour. It answered on the Zi day because Zi clashes and controls Wu-fire, the flying spirit, and raises up Mao-wood, the hidden spirit. The Huangjin Ce says "a hidden spirit without a lift is in vain in the end, and a flying spirit not pushed aside is futile all the same", and this hexagram bore it out.

> [!note] 按
> 「兑宫二爻卯木」指本卦所属宫（兑宫）的二爻，那一爻在本卦里伏在二爻午火之下；「窝」是窝藏，「炉火人家」按判词后文指铁匠。
>
> *Note:* "Mao-wood in the second line of the Dui palace" is the second line of the palace the hexagram belongs to, which in this hexagram lies hidden under the second-line Wu-fire; "harbour" is to shelter a criminal, and the household "with a furnace fire" is, as the later words of the judgement say, a blacksmith.
