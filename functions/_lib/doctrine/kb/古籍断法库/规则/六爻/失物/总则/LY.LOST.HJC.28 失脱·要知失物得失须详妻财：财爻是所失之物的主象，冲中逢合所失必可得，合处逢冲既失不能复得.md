# 失脱·要知失物得失须详妻财：财爻是所失之物的主象，冲中逢合所失必可得，合处逢冲既失不能复得

六爻 · 失物 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 百姓饥寒，常有翻墙钻洞的人，不要忘了检点约束，也多有遗失的忧虑。要知道其中的得失，必须详察卦上的妻财。财爻是所失之物的主象：如果得到冲中逢合，所失一定可以得回；如果是合处逢冲，既然失去就不能再得回了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Common people suffer hunger and cold, and there are often those who climb walls and dig through them; never forget to check and restrain, for there is also a worry of loss. To know the gain or loss in it, one must examine the Wealth line on the hexagram. The Wealth line is the principal image of the thing lost: if a combination is met within a clash, what is lost can surely be got back; if a clash is met at a combination, once lost it cannot be got back.
