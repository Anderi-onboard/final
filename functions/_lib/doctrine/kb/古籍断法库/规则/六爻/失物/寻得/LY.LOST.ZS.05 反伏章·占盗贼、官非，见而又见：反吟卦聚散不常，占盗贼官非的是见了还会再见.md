# 反伏章·占盗贼、官非，见而又见：反吟卦聚散不常，占盗贼官非的是见了还会再见

六爻 · 失物 · 寻得 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 反吟卦聚散没有常规。占盗贼、官非，是见了又见。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Reversed Chant hexagram gathers and scatters without constancy. In divining for thieves and lawsuits, it is seen and seen again.

## 《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 2403 行 · Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占盗贼、官非，见而又见。 | 占盗贼、官非，是见了又见。 | In divining for thieves and lawsuits, it is seen and seen again. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句是反吟卦各种占断的一连串里的一句，前面是占天时、婚姻、疾病，后面是占出行、行人；「见而又见」指盗贼、官非这类事，了了又起。
>
> *Note:* This line is one of a run of readings for a Reversed Chant hexagram, preceded by the weather, marriage and illness and followed by setting out and travellers; "seen and seen again" says that matters like thieves and lawsuits end and then arise again.
