# 逃亡·父动变官必得公人捕捉：父母化官鬼、官鬼化父母，或官父都发动，必须写状子告官，差捕快才能捕获

六爻 · 失物 · 捕获 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母发动变出官鬼，一定要靠公差来捕捉。父母化官鬼、官鬼化父母，或者官鬼父母都发动，必须写状告官，差遣捕快才能捕获。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line moves and changes into Pressure, he must be caught by officers of the law. Resource changing into Pressure, Pressure changing into Resource, or Pressure and Resource both moving, require a complaint to be filed with the officials and constables sent, and then he can be caught.
