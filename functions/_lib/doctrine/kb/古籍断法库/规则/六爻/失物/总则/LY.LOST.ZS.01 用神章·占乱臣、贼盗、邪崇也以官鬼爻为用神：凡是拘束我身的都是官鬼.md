# 用神章·占乱臣、贼盗、邪崇也以官鬼爻为用神：凡是拘束我身的都是官鬼

六爻 · 失物 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占乱臣、贼盗、邪祟，也以官鬼爻为用神；官鬼这一类事物很多，一切拘束我身的都是。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for rebellious ministers, thieves and robbers, and evil spirits, the Pressure line is also the Subject; Pressure covers many kinds of things, and everything that constrains my person is of it.

## 《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)

> | 物类亦多，一切拘束我身者是也。 | 官鬼这一类事物很多，一切拘束我身的都是。 | Pressure covers many kinds of things, and everything that constrains my person is of it. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是从「谁是贼」的一头取用神：官鬼代表贼。被偷的东西则取财爻（失脱章里分别说），所以占失物要分清问的是人还是物。
>
> *Note:* This takes the Subject from the side of who the thief is: Pressure stands for the thief. The thing stolen is read from the Wealth line (given separately in the chapter on lost goods), so in divining for lost goods one must distinguish whether one asks about the person or the thing.
