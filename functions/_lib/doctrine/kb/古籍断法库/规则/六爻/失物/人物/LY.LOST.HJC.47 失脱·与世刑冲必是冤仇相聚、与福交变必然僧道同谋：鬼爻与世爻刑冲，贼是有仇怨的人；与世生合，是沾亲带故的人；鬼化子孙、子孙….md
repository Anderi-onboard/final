# 失脱·与世刑冲必是冤仇相聚、与福交变必然僧道同谋：鬼爻与世爻刑冲，贼是有仇怨的人；与世生合，是沾亲带故的人；鬼化子孙、子孙化鬼，必有僧道夹在其中

六爻 · 失物 · 人物 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻和世爻刑冲，必定是有冤仇的人聚在一起；和子孙互相交变，必定有僧人道士同谋。鬼爻和世爻刑冲，贼是有仇怨的人；和世爻生合，是有亲戚关系的人；鬼化子孙、子孙化鬼，必有僧人道士夹杂在里面。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line punishes or clashes with the Self, it is surely people with a grudge gathered together; if it changes with Output and back, monks and Daoists have surely plotted together. Pressure punishing or clashing with the Self means the thief is someone with a grudge; giving life to or combining with the Self, someone related or acquainted; Pressure changing into Output or Output into Pressure, a monk or Daoist is surely mixed in.
