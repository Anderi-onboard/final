# 逃亡·内近外远生世则终有归期：用爻在本宫内卦人在本地，本宫外卦在别府别县，他宫内卦在外县交界，他宫外卦在他府外县，在六爻则去远方；最喜生合持世，人虽去，日后自己回来，寻也容易见到

六爻 · 失物 · 寻得 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦近、外卦远；用爻生世爻，终究有回来的日子。用爻在本宫内卦，人在本地；在本宫外卦，在别的府县；在他宫内卦，在外县交界处；在他宫外卦，在他府外县；如果在上爻，已经去了远方。最喜欢用爻生合持世，那人虽然走了，日后会自己回来，去找也容易找到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The inner trigram is near and the outer far; if the Subject gives life to the Self, there is in the end a day of return. A Subject in the inner trigram of its own palace means he is still in the locality; in the outer trigram of its own palace, in another prefecture or county; in the inner trigram of another palace, at the border of another county; in the outer trigram of another palace, in another prefecture and county; if on the top line, he has gone far away. Best of all is the Subject giving life to or combining with the Self or holding the Self: though he has gone, he will come back of himself later, and a search finds him easily.
