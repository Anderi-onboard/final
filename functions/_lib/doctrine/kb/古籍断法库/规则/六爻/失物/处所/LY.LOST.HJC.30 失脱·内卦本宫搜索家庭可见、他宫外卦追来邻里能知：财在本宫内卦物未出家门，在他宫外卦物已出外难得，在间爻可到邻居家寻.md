# 失脱·内卦本宫搜索家庭可见、他宫外卦追来邻里能知：财在本宫内卦物未出家门，在他宫外卦物已出外难得，在间爻可到邻居家寻

六爻 · 失物 · 处所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财在本宫内卦，在家里搜索可以找到；财在他宫外卦，到邻居那里追问能知道下落。财在本宫内卦，东西没有出家门，可以找到；财在他宫外卦，东西已经出外，难得回；财在间爻，邻居人家可以去找。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Wealth in the inner trigram of its own palace, a search of the household can find it; with Wealth in the outer trigram of another palace, inquiring among the neighbours can learn where it went. Wealth in the inner trigram of its own palace means the thing has not left the house and can be found; Wealth in the outer trigram of another palace means the thing has gone out and is hard to get back; Wealth on a between line means the neighbours' houses can be searched.
