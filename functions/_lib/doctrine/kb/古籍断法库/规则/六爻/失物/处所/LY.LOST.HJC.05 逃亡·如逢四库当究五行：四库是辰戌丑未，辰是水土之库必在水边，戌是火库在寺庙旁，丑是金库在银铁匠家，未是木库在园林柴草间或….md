# 逃亡·如逢四库当究五行：四库是辰戌丑未，辰是水土之库必在水边，戌是火库在寺庙旁，丑是金库在银铁匠家，未是木库在园林柴草间或木工竹匠家；遇墓爻要等冲破墓爻的日月才见得到

六爻 · 失物 · 处所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果遇到四库，要追究它的五行。四库就是辰戌丑未四个地支，用爻属木，卦里有未动一类。辰是水和土的库，人必在水边；戌是火库，在寺庙旁边；丑是金库，在银铁匠家；未是木库，在园林柴草之间，或木工竹匠家。凡是占逃亡盗贼，如果遇到墓爻，一定很难找到，要等冲破墓爻的日月才能见到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the four storehouses are met, examine their element. The four storehouses are the branches Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei; the Subject line is of Wood, and the hexagram has something like an unmoved Wei. Chen is the storehouse of Water and Earth, and he must be by the water; Xu is the storehouse of Fire, by a temple; Chou is the storehouse of Metal, at the house of a silver or iron smith; Wei is the storehouse of Wood, among gardens, woods and brushwood, or at the house of a carpenter or bamboo worker. In divining for a runaway or a thief, if a tomb line is met he is surely hard to find, and he can be seen only in the month or day that clashes the tomb line open.
