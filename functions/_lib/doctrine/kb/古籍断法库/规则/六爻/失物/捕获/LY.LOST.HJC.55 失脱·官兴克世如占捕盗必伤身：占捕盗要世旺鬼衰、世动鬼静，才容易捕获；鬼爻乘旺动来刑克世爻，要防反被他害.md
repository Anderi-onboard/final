# 失脱·官兴克世如占捕盗必伤身：占捕盗要世旺鬼衰、世动鬼静，才容易捕获；鬼爻乘旺动来刑克世爻，要防反被他害

六爻 · 失物 · 捕获 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动克世，如果是占捕盗，一定会伤身。凡是占捕盗，要世爻旺、鬼爻衰，世爻动、鬼爻静，就容易捕获；如果鬼爻乘旺，动来刑克世爻，要防反被他所害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure moves and controls the Self, in divining for catching a thief you will surely be hurt. In divining for catching a thief, the Self should be strong and Pressure weak, the Self moving and Pressure still, and then he is easily caught; if Pressure is strong and moves to punish or control the Self, beware of being harmed by him instead.
