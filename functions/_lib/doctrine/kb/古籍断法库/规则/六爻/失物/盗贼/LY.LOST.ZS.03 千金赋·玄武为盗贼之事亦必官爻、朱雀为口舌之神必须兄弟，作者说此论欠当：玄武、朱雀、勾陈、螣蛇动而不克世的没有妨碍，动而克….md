# 千金赋·玄武为盗贼之事亦必官爻、朱雀为口舌之神必须兄弟，作者说此论欠当：玄武、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇动而不克世的没有妨碍，动而克世的都是凶，不必再分兄弟官鬼

六爻 · 失物 · 盗贼 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 千金赋说：玄武主盗贼的事，也一定要有官鬼爻；朱雀是口舌之神，一定要有兄弟爻。作者说：这个说法不妥当。玄武、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇发动而不克世爻的，没有妨碍；发动而克世爻的，都算凶，何必再分什么兄弟、官鬼呢？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Qianjin Fu says: the Dark Tortoise governs matters of thieves, but it too must have the Pressure line; the Vermilion Bird is the spirit of quarrels but it must have the Peer line. The author says: this view is not proper. When the Dark Tortoise, Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth or Coiling Snake move without controlling the Self, there is no harm; when they move and control the Self, all are taken as ill; why distinguish Peer from Pressure at all?

> [!warning] 打架
> 千金赋（黄金策的总断）说玄武主盗贼要有官鬼爻同临才成，朱雀主口舌要兄弟爻同临；增删卜易的作者认为这个说法不当，只要六神发动克世就凶，不分兄弟官鬼。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The Qianjin Fu (the general judgement of the Huangjin Ce) says that the Dark Tortoise governs thieves only with Pressure sitting together, and the Vermilion Bird governs quarrels only with Peer; the author of the Zengshan Buyi holds this improper: a six-spirit that moves and controls the Self is ill whether or not Peer or Pressure is present.
