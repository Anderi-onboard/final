# 逃亡·若临午地必往南方、或化寅爻转移东北：用爻安静以所临之地定方向，用爻发动以变爻定方向

六爻 · 失物 · 处所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻若落在午，逃的人必往南方；或者化出寅爻，就转移到东北。用爻安静，以它所临的地支作逃去的方向，所以说临午是南方；用爻发动，以变出的爻定方向；转移的意思是转往另一方，比如午变寅爻，一定先往正南，后往东北。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line sits on Wu, the runaway has gone south; or if it changes into a Yin line, he moves on to the northeast. A still Subject takes the branch it sits on as the direction of flight, hence Wu means south; a moving Subject takes the changed line to fix the direction; moving on means turning toward another quarter, so that Wu changing to Yin means he surely goes due south first and then to the northeast.
