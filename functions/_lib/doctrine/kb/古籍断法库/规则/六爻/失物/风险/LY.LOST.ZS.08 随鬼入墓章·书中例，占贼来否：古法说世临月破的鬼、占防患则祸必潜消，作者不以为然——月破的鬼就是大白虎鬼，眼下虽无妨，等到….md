# 随鬼入墓章·书中例，占贼来否：古法说世临月破的鬼、占防患则祸必潜消，作者不以为然——月破的鬼就是大白虎鬼，眼下虽无妨，等到填实之年月就不吉；当时土贼横行，这人带着妻子去避，因小女没跟上回头去抱，一起被贼害死：自己死是世临破鬼入日墓，女儿死是上爻鬼变子孙

六爻 · 失物 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 古人说世爻临着月破的鬼，如果占防患，祸必定悄悄消去；我认为不对，月破的鬼就是大白虎鬼，眼下虽然没有妨碍，到填实月破的年月就不吉。书中例：申月己未日占贼会不会来，得大畜卦变泰卦：当时土匪横行，常去常来，乡里人没有安宁的日子；一天忽然传来贼到了，这个人带着妻子避开，因为小女儿没有跟着，又回去抱女儿，一同被贼害死。自己的死，是世爻临着月破的鬼又入日墓；女儿的死，是上爻鬼变子孙的缘故。怎能说世临破鬼就祸患潜消呢？鬼临月破而不动的就不灵验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ancients said that when the Self sits on a Pressure line struck by the Month-break, in divining for guarding against harm the calamity surely melts away in secret; I think this is wrong: a Month-break Pressure is a Great White Tiger Pressure, and though it does no harm for now, the year or month that fills the break is not lucky. Worked example: in the Shen month, on a Jiwei day, a man divined whether bandits would come and got Daxu changing to Tai: at that time local bandits were rampant, coming and going, and the villagers had no peace; one day word came suddenly that the bandits had arrived, and this man took his wife and fled, but since his little daughter had not come along he went back to carry her, and they were killed together by the bandits. His own death was because the Self sat on a Month-break Pressure and entered the tomb of the Day; the daughter's death was because the Pressure of the top line changed into Output. How can it be said that the Self on a broken Pressure melts the calamity away? A Pressure struck by the Month-break and not moving is not borne out.

> [!note] 按
> 这一例本是讲「世随鬼入墓」不一定吉，借来说防贼：占贼来不来，世爻临月破的鬼并不能保平安。题目是「贼来否」，所以放在失物域「风险」下。
>
> *Note:* The example was given to show that the Self following Pressure into the tomb is not always lucky; here it serves for guarding against bandits: when divining whether bandits will come, the Self on a Month-break Pressure does not guarantee safety. The question was whether the bandits would come, so it is filed under risk in the domain of lost goods.
