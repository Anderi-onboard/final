# 飞伏神章·书中例，占逃仆：卯木财爻伏在二爻午火之下，申金持世克制卯木终不能逃，伏去生飞是泄气，盗去财物都废在炉火人家，断甲子日拿获，果然子日在铁匠家窝赌处擒获，连铁匠一起送官；子水冲克午火飞神、生起卯木伏神

六爻 · 失物 · 逃亡 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 伏神得不到提挈终究是徒劳，飞神不被推开也是枉然。书中例：辰月丁巳日占逃走的仆人，得蹇卦：占仆人以财爻为用神，卯木财爻伏在二爻午火之下，午火是飞神，卯木是伏神；申金持世克制卯木，终究不能逃走；但因为伏在午火之下，伏神去生飞神，叫做泄气，偷去的财物都耗费在炉火人家，许他甲子日拿获；果然子日得到消息，窝赌在铁匠家，申时擒获，连铁匠一起送官。应在子日，是因为子水冲克午火飞神、生起卯木伏神。作者怀疑飞神午火就如铁匠，伏神卯木就是逃仆，到子日冲午、刑卯，所以两个人都被杖责。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hidden spirit not lifted up is in the end in vain, and a flying spirit not pushed aside is futile all the same. Worked example: in the Chen month, on a Dingsi day, a man divined about a runaway servant and got Jian: in divining for a servant the Wealth line is the Subject; Mao-wood Wealth is hidden under Wu-fire in the second line, Wu-fire being the flying spirit and Mao-wood the hidden spirit; Shen-metal holds the Self and controls Mao-wood, so he cannot in the end get away; but because it is hidden under Wu-fire, the hidden spirit gives life to the flying one, which is called draining, and the stolen goods are all spent in a household with a furnace fire, and it was promised that he would be caught on the Jiazi day; indeed on the Zi day word came that he was harboured and gambling at a blacksmith's, and he was seized in the Shen hour and sent to the officials together with the blacksmith. It answered on the Zi day because Zi-water clashes and controls Wu-fire, the flying spirit, and raises up Mao-wood, the hidden spirit. The author suspects that the flying spirit Wu-fire is the blacksmith and the hidden spirit Mao-wood the runaway servant, and that on the Zi day Zi clashes Wu and punishes Mao, so both men were beaten with the rod.
