# 失脱·自空化空皆当置而不问、日旺月旺总未散而可得：用爻自己落空或动而化空都难寻见；财爻值月令或在日辰生旺之地，物未散可寻

六爻 · 失物 · 寻得 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻自己空、或变成空，都应当搁下不问；财爻日旺月旺，东西总是没有散失，可以得回。用爻自己落空，或发动而化空，都难找到；如果财爻值月令，或在日辰生旺之地，这件东西没有散失，可以找。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject that is Void itself or changes into the Void should be set aside and not pursued; a Wealth line strong on the Day and the Month means the thing has not been scattered and can be got back. A Subject that is Void itself, or moves and changes into the Void, is hard to find; if the Wealth line is on the Month command, or on a place of life and strength on the Day, the thing has not been scattered and can be sought.
