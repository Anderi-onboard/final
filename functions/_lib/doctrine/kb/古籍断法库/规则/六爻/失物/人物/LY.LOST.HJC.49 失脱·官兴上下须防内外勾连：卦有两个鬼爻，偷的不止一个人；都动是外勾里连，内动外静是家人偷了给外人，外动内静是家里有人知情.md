# 失脱·官兴上下须防内外勾连：卦有两个鬼爻，偷的不止一个人；都动是外勾里连，内动外静是家人偷了给外人，外动内静是家里有人知情

六爻 · 失物 · 人物 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼在上下卦都发动，要提防内外勾结。卦里有两个鬼爻，偷东西的不止一个人；都发动，是外边勾结里面；内卦动外卦静，是家里人偷给外人；外卦动内卦静，是家里有人知情。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure moving in both the upper and the lower trigram, beware of collusion between inside and outside. A hexagram with two Pressure lines means more than one person stole; both moving means the outside is in league with the inside; the inner moving and the outer still means a person of the household stole it for an outsider; the outer moving and the inner still means someone in the household knows of it.
