# 六亲发动诀、诸爻持世诀·失物句：父动失物逃亡要诉论，官鬼克兄弟失脱难寻多暗昧，财爻发动失物静安家未出，鬼爻持世财物时时忧失脱

六爻 · 失物 · 寻得 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻发动，丢了东西和逃走的人要靠告状追论；官鬼克兄弟，失脱的东西难找、多半说不清；财爻发动，失物安静不动，没有出家门；鬼爻持世，财物时常担忧失脱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Resource line moves, lost things and runaways must be pursued by lodging a complaint; when Pressure controls the Peer line, lost goods are hard to find and mostly murky; when the Wealth line moves and the lost thing is still and safe, it has not left the house; when Pressure holds the Self, one is forever worried about losing goods.

> [!note] 按
> 这四句分属两首诗：前三句出在「六亲发动诀」（父动、官鬼克兄弟、财爻发动三联），最后一句出在「诸爻持世诀」（鬼爻持世一联）。每句前面的六亲发动或持世是同一联的开头，没有拆进引文里。
>
> *Note:* These four lines come from two verses: the first three from the verse on the moving of the six relations (the couplets for Resource moving, Pressure controlling Peer, and Wealth moving) and the last from the verse on lines holding the Self (the couplet for Pressure holding the Self). The opening phrase naming the moving or holding relation in each couplet is not split into the quotation.
