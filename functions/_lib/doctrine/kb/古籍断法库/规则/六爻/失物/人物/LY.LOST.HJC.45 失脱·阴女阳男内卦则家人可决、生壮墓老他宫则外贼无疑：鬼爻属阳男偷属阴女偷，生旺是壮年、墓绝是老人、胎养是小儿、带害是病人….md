# 失脱·阴女阳男内卦则家人可决、生壮墓老他宫则外贼无疑：鬼爻属阳男偷属阴女偷，生旺是壮年、墓绝是老人、胎养是小儿、带害是病人；本宫内卦是家人，他宫内卦是借住的人或异姓人，他宫是外贼

六爻 · 失物 · 人物 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 阴是女、阳是男，在内卦，家里的人可以断定；生壮、墓老，在他宫，外贼无疑。鬼爻属阳，是男子偷；属阴，是女人偷；阴化阳，是女的偷给男的；阳化阴，是男的偷了寄给女的。生旺是壮年人，墓绝是老年人，胎养是小孩偷，带害是有病的人偷。本宫内卦是家中人偷，他宫内卦是住在宅上的借居人偷，或是家中异姓的人偷。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Yin is female and Yang is male; in the inner trigram the person of the household can be decided; Life and strength, Tomb and age, in another palace, there is no doubt it is an outside thief. A Pressure line of Yang means a man stole it; of Yin, a woman; Yin changing to Yang, a woman stole it for a man; Yang changing to Yin, a man stole it and sent it to a woman. In life and strength it is a man in his prime, in tomb or extinction an old person, in the womb or nurture a child, and with harm a sick person. In the inner trigram of its own palace it is a person of the household; in the inner trigram of another palace, a lodger on the premises or a person of another surname in the household.
