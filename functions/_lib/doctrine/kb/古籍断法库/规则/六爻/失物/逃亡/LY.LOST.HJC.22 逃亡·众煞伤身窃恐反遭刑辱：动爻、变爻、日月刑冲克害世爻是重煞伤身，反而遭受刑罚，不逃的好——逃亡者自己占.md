# 逃亡·众煞伤身窃恐反遭刑辱：动爻、变爻、日月刑冲克害世爻是重煞伤身，反而遭受刑罚，不逃的好——逃亡者自己占

六爻 · 失物 · 逃亡 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 许多凶煞伤害身，恐怕反而遭受刑罚和羞辱。动爻、变爻、日月刑冲克害世爻，叫做重煞伤身，反而遭受刑罚，不逃为吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With many baleful influences injuring the Body, one fears he will instead suffer punishment and disgrace. When moving lines, changed lines, the Month and the Day punish, clash, control or harm the Self, it is called heavy baleful influences injuring the Body, and he will instead suffer punishment; it is lucky not to flee.
