# 何知章·失脱与盗贼的几句：遭贼是玄武临财而鬼旺有扶，见失脱是玄武带鬼而应爻发动，失衣裳是勾陈玄武入财乡，损六畜是白虎带鬼临所属，失牛是五爻丑鬼落空，失鸡是初爻带鬼玄武欺

六爻 · 失物 · 盗贼 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 怎么知道人家遇上贼：玄武临在财爻，鬼爻又旺又有扶助。怎么知道人家丢了东西：玄武带着鬼爻，应爻发动。怎么知道人家丢了衣服：勾陈、玄武落在财的位置。怎么知道人家损了牲畜：白虎带着鬼爻，临在所属的位置。怎么知道人家丢了牛：五爻丑土鬼爻落空，令人发愁。怎么知道人家丢了鸡：初爻带鬼，又有玄武欺它。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* How do we know a household has met thieves? The Dark Tortoise sits on Wealth and Pressure is strong and supported. How do we know the household has lost things? The Dark Tortoise carries Pressure and the Other line moves. How do we know they have lost clothes? The Hook Earth and the Dark Tortoise enter the Wealth position. How do we know livestock will be lost? The White Tiger carries Pressure and sits on the line it belongs to. How do we know an ox is lost? The Pressure of the fifth line at Chou falls into the Void, and there is cause for worry. How do we know chickens are lost? The first line carries Pressure and the Dark Tortoise preys on it.
