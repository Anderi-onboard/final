# 逃亡·间爻作合原中必定知情：间爻是原来的保人，没有保人就看邻里，间爻与用爻相合必知内情，再与世爻冲克，必是这个人诱他走的

六爻 · 失物 · 人物 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻与用爻相合，当初的中人必定知道内情。间爻是原来的保人，没有保人，就按邻居来断。间爻如果和用爻相合，必定知道内情；再和世爻冲克，一定是这个人把他诱走的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a line between the Self and the Other combines with the Subject, the original go-between surely knows what happened. The between lines are the original guarantor; where there was no guarantor, judge by the neighbours. If a between line combines with the Subject, it surely knows the inside story; and if it also clashes or controls the Self, this person must be the one who lured him away.
