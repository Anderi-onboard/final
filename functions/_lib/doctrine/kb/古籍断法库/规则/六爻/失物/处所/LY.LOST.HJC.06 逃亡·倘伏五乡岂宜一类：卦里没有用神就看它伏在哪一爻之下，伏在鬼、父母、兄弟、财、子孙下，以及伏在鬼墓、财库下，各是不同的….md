# 逃亡·倘伏五乡岂宜一类：卦里没有用神就看它伏在哪一爻之下，伏在鬼、父母、兄弟、财、子孙下，以及伏在鬼墓、财库下，各是不同的去处

六爻 · 失物 · 处所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果用神伏在五种六亲之下，怎么能一概而论。卦里没有用神，就要看它伏在哪一爻之下，才知道那个人在什么地方。伏在鬼爻下，在官仓官库之中，旺相又逢月建，在官户家，休囚无气，在公差吏役家；伏在父母下，在叔伯父母家，不然在手艺人家；伏在兄弟下，在兄弟姊妹和熟识朋友家；伏在财爻下，在奴婢妻妾家；伏在子孙下，在寺观和年幼辈那里。又比如伏在鬼墓之下，不在庙宇中就在寺庵内；伏在财库之下，不在仓库中，就在富豪家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject is hidden under one of the five relations, how can they all be judged alike? When the hexagram has no Subject, look at which line it is hidden under, and one learns where the person is. Hidden under Pressure, he is in a government granary or storehouse; if strong and also on the Month, in the house of an official family, if resting or without breath, in the house of a clerk or runner. Hidden under Resource, he is at the house of an uncle or parent, otherwise at a craftsman's. Hidden under Peer, he is at the house of brothers and sisters or of acquaintances and friends. Hidden under Wealth, he is at the house of servants, wives or concubines. Hidden under Output, he is at a temple or among the young. Again, hidden under the tomb of Pressure, he is either in a temple or in a nunnery; hidden under the storehouse of Wealth, he is either in a warehouse or at a wealthy family's.
