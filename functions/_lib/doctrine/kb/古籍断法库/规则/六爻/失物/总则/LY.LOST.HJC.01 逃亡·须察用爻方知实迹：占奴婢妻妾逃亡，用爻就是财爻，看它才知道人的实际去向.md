# 逃亡·须察用爻方知实迹：占奴婢妻妾逃亡，用爻就是财爻，看它才知道人的实际去向

六爻 · 失物 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 宽容待人，众人会生出侮慢；严厉管人，逃亡就会发生，所以即使大圣人有容人的度量，也说小人难养。要知道逃走的人的实际去向，必须察看用爻。用爻，比如占奴仆、妻妾逃亡，就看财爻这一类。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Treat people with leniency and the crowd turns insolent; manage them with severity and flight begins; hence even the great sage, for all his tolerance, said that petty people are hard to keep. To learn where the runaway has really gone, one must examine the Subject line; for example, in divining for the flight of a servant or a wife or concubine, one looks at the Wealth line and the like.
