# 逃亡·世应空亡独发徒费乎心：世空则去找的不紧，用空则找也找不到，世应都空必定白跑一趟；兄弟独发是虚假不实，也找不到

六爻 · 失物 · 寻得 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应都空亡、独自发动，是白费心思。世爻空，去找的人不上心；用爻空，找也找不到；世应都空，必定没有可找的地方，空回一趟。兄弟独自发动，是虚假不实，也找不到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self and the Other both Void and one moving alone, it is a waste of effort. If the Self is Void, the searcher does not press the search; if the Subject is Void, he is not found however one searches; with both Void there is surely no place to search and he returns empty-handed. A Peer line moving alone means falsehood and nothing real, and he is not found either.
