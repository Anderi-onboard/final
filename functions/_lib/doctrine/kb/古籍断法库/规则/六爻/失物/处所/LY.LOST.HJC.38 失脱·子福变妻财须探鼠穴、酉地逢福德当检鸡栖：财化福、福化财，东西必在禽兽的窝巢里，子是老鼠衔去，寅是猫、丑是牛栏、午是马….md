# 失脱·子福变妻财须探鼠穴、酉地逢福德当检鸡栖：财化福、福化财，东西必在禽兽的窝巢里，子是老鼠衔去，寅是猫、丑是牛栏、午是马厩、未是羊圈、酉是鸡栖、亥是猪圈；有合在里面，没有合在旁边

六爻 · 失物 · 处所 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙变成妻财，要去探老鼠洞；酉地遇到福德，应当查看鸡窝。财化福、福化财，东西必定在禽兽的窝巢里：值子爻，是老鼠衔去，更在初爻，在地洞里；寅是猫衔走，丑在牛栏，午在马厩，未在羊圈，酉在鸡窝，亥在猪圈。有合，在里面；没有合，在旁边。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output changing into Wealth, one must probe the rat holes; Output meeting You ground, one should check the chicken roost. Wealth changing into Output, or Output changing into Wealth, means the thing is surely in the den or nest of some bird or beast: on Zi, a rat has carried it off, and still more on the first line, in a hole in the ground; Yin is a cat carrying it off, Chou the cattle pen, Wu the horse stall, Wei the sheep pen, You the chicken roost, Hai the pigsty. With a combination it is inside; without a combination it is nearby.
