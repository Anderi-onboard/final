# 失脱·日来克鬼贼心亦自惊疑：鬼爻被日辰或动爻刑克，这时贼心惊疑，贼必能捕获

六爻 · 失物 · 捕获 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰来克鬼爻，贼的心里也自己惊疑。鬼爻被日辰、动爻刑克，这时贼心惊疑，贼一定能捕获。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Day comes to control Pressure, the thief's own heart is startled and uneasy. A Pressure line punished or controlled by the Day or a moving line means the thief's heart is then startled and uneasy, and he can surely be caught.
