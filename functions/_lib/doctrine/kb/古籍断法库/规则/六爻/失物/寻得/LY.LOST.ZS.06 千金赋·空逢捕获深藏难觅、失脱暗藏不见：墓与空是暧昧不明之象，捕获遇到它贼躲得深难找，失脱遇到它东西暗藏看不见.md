# 千金赋·空逢捕获深藏难觅、失脱暗藏不见：墓与空是暧昧不明之象，捕获遇到它贼躲得深难找，失脱遇到它东西暗藏看不见

六爻 · 失物 · 寻得 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 捕获遇到它，贼深藏难找；失脱遇到它，东西暗藏看不见。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In catching a thief, meeting it means the thief is deeply hidden and hard to find; in lost goods, meeting it means the thing is hidden away and not seen.

## 《增删卜易·千金赋》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 5118 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The Qianjin Fu, as edited in this book", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 养主狐疑，墓多暧昧。 | 长养之位叫人犹豫猜疑，墓位多半暧昧不明。 | Nurture brings doubt, and the tomb brings obscurity. |
> | 此亦长生沐浴十二位之星是也，余得验者，只用生、旺、墓、绝，其余不验，今之化出者，亦验生、旺、墓、绝。 | 这也是长生、沐浴十二位里的星，我验证过的，只用生、旺、墓、绝，其余的不灵验，现在变出来的，也只验证生、旺、墓、绝。 | These too are stars of the twelve positions beginning with Life and Bathing; what I have found borne out uses only Life, Strength, Tomb and Extinction, the others not being borne out, and what is changed into is likewise borne out only for Life, Strength, Tomb and Extinction. |

## 《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 5118 行 · Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 失脱遇之，暗藏不见； | 失脱遇到它，东西暗藏看不见； | in lost goods, meeting it means the thing is hidden away and not seen; |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一段先说墓（滞），再按用神动化墓、旺、休囚、空分别讲病，再按各占事说墓或空的后果；这里只取捕获和失脱两句。
>
> *Note:* The passage first calls the tomb stagnation, then runs through illness by whether the Subject moves into the tomb, is strong, resting or Void, then gives the result for each kind of matter; only the lines on catching and on lost goods are taken here.
