# 逃亡·归魂卦用遇生合不捕而自回、游魂卦应又交重能潜而会遁：得归魂卦他回家心切，生合世爻他自己会回来，寻也容易见；得游魂卦他没有回来的意思，能躲会逃，寻必难见

六爻 · 失物 · 寻得 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 归魂卦、用神遇到生合，不用捕他自己会回来；游魂卦、应爻又交重发动，他能潜伏，会逃遁。得归魂卦，他回来的心意急切，如果生合世爻，他一定自己回来，找他容易见到；遇到游魂卦，那人必定没有回来的意思，能潜伏会逃遁，找他必定难见。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a Returning Soul hexagram, if the Subject meets life or combination, he comes back by himself without being caught; in a Wandering Soul hexagram, with the Other also crowded with movement, he can lie low and slip away. In a Returning Soul hexagram his mind is eager to return, and if it gives life to or combines with the Self he surely comes back himself and is easily found; in a Wandering Soul hexagram the man surely has no mind to return, he can lie low and slip away, and a search for him is surely in vain.
