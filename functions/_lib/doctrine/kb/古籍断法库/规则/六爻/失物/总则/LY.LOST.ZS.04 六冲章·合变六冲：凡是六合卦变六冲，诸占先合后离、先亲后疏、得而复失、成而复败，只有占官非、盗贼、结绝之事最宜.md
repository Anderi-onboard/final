# 六冲章·合变六冲：凡是六合卦变六冲，诸占先合后离、先亲后疏、得而复失、成而复败，只有占官非、盗贼、结绝之事最宜

六爻 · 失物 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是六合卦变成六冲卦，各种占都是先合后离，先亲后疏，先浓后淡，先荣后悴，先好后迟，先通后塞，得到又失去，成功又败坏，事情做成又变。只有占官非、盗贼、了结断绝的事最适宜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whenever a six-combine hexagram changes into a six-clash hexagram, every divination first combines and then parts, first close and then distant, first warm and then cool, first flourishing and then withered, first good and then slow, first open and then blocked, gained and then lost, achieved and then ruined, the matter done and then changed again. Only in divining for lawsuits, thieves, and matters of settling or breaking off is it best.
