# 失脱·木克六爻逾墙而入、金伤三位穿壁而来：看鬼爻五行克哪一处就知从哪里进来——木鬼克土逾墙掘洞、金鬼克木割壁穿篱、火鬼克金劈环开锁、水鬼克火灌水灭灯、土鬼克水涉渎跳涧、木火交化明火执仗；木鬼克上爻翻墙而入，克初爻后门掘洞

六爻 · 失物 · 盗贼 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 木克上爻，是翻墙而入；金伤三爻，是穿壁而来。木鬼克土，翻墙挖洞；金鬼克木，割开板壁穿过篱笆；火鬼克金，劈开环扣、撬开锁；水鬼克火，泼水灭灯；土鬼克水，涉过水渠跳过山涧；木火交相变化，是明火执仗。要知道从哪里能进来，用鬼爻克到的地方来定：木鬼克上爻，翻墙而入；克初爻，从后门挖洞而进。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wood controlling the top line means he came in over the wall; Metal injuring the third place means he came through the partition. Wood Pressure controlling Earth: over the wall or through a dug hole; Metal Pressure controlling Wood: cutting through the planking or the hedge; Fire Pressure controlling Metal: splitting the ring and opening the lock; Water Pressure controlling Fire: dousing the lamp with water; Earth Pressure controlling Water: wading the channel or leaping the gully; Wood and Fire changing into each other: robbers with open torches. To know where he came in, fix it by where the Pressure controls: Wood Pressure controlling the top line, over the wall; controlling the first line, through a hole dug at the back door.
