# 失脱·子动丑宫问牧童定知消息、福兴酉地是酒客可探情由：子孙发动，必有人撞见，问那人可知消息，看子孙所临的地支，分别问哪一种人

六爻 · 失物 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动在丑宫，问牧童一定知道消息；子孙兴旺在酉地，是酒客可以打探到情由。子孙发动，必定有人撞见，问他可知消息。如果在子爻，可以问不戴帽子的男子或捕鱼人；在丑爻，问牧童、筑墙等人；在寅爻是木客、木匠、挑竹木器的人；在卯爻，问织席卖鞋的、挑柴砍草的人；在辰爻，问凿井的、傍河的、锄地的人；在巳爻，问穿红衣的女子，或耍蛇的乞丐；在午爻，问烧窑的、骑马的、讨火的、提灯的人；在未爻，问挑灰的、耕种的、放羊的人；在申爻，问铜铁匠或耍猴的人；在酉爻，问针线工、酒客、捉鸡的人；在戌爻，问挑泥的、锄地的、牵狗的人；在亥爻，问挑水的、踏水车的、洗衣的、洗澡的人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving in the Chou palace: ask the herd boy and you will surely learn the news; Output flourishing on You ground: a drinker can be sounded out for the facts. When Output moves, someone surely saw it, and asking him will give news. On Zi, one can ask a bareheaded man or a fisherman; on Chou, herd boys, wall builders and the like; on Yin, woodsmen, carpenters, and people carrying bamboo and wooden things; on Mao, mat weavers, shoe sellers, those carrying firewood or cutting grass; on Chen, well diggers, people by the river, and those hoeing the ground; on Si, a woman in red or a snake-charming beggar; on Wu, kiln burners, riders, those begging a light or carrying a lamp; on Wei, those carrying ash, ploughing or herding sheep; on Shen, copper and iron smiths or monkey handlers; on You, needleworkers, drinkers and chicken catchers; on Xu, those carrying mud, hoeing, or leading a dog; on Hai, water carriers, those treading a water wheel, washing clothes or bathing.
