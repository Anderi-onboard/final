# 十八问答·第十一问·书中例，占失银物可得复否：卦得六冲，未土财爻化午火回头生合，是失而复得之象；旁人说应爻持白虎金鬼、玄武临财难言复得，作者说应是他人、被午火回头克制，财为用神冲中逢合，日主合它，必得；应在巳火青龙原神旬空出空值日的乙巳日，果然得回

六爻 · 失物 · 寻得 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 合是聚、冲是散：冲中逢合，先散后聚，先失后得，先淡后浓；合处逢冲则相反。书中例：寅月戊戌日占丢失的银子和东西能不能失而复得，得巽卦变讼卦：卦得六冲，未土财爻化出午火，回头生合，是失而复得的象；旁人说，应爻持着白虎金鬼，玄武临在财爻，难说能得回；我说，应爻是他人，被午火回头克制，财爻是用神，冲中逢合，日辰又合它，管保一定得到；他问应在哪一天，我说，巳火青龙原神旬空，毛病在巳，必须等乙巳日原神出空值日，会得回；果然应验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A combination gathers and a clash scatters: a combination met within a clash first scatters and then gathers, first loses and then gains, first cool and then warm; a clash met at a combination is the reverse. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined whether lost silver and goods could be recovered and got Xun changing to Song: the hexagram has six-clash, the Wealth Wei-earth changes into Wu-fire which gives life and combines in return, the image of lost and then regained; onlookers said that the Other holds the White Tiger and Metal Pressure and the Dark Tortoise sits on Wealth, so it is hard to say it will be recovered; I said that the Other is another person, controlled by Wu-fire in return, that Wealth is the Subject, met with a combination within a clash, with the Day also combining it, so it is bound to be had. He asked on what day, and I said: the Support, the Azure Dragon Si-fire, is Void, so the trouble lies in Si, and one must wait for the Yisi day when the Support comes out of the Void and rules; and indeed it was borne out.
