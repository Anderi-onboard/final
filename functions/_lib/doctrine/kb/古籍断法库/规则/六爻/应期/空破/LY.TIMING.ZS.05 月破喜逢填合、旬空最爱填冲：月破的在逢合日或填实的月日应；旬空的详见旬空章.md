# 月破喜逢填合、旬空最爱填冲：月破的在逢合日或填实的月日应；旬空的详见旬空章

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月破的，喜欢遇到填实或合：比如子月占卦，主事爻落在午火，是月破，后来遇到未日应验，叫做破而逢合；又有遇到午月应验，填实了就不破了；其余照这个类推。旬空的最喜欢被填实或冲：详见旬空章。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A line struck by the Month-break is glad to meet a filling or a combination: for example, if the divination falls in the Zi month and the line ruling the matter sits on Wu-fire, it is broken by the Month, and it answers on a later Wei day, which is called a break that meets a combination; it may also answer in the Wu month, when it is filled and no longer broken; the rest follow this. A Void line most loves to be filled or clashed: see the chapter on the Void.
