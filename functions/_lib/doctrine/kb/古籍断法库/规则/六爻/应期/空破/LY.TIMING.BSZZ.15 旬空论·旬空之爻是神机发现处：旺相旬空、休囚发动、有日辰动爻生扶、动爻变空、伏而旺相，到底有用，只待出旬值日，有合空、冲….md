# 旬空论·旬空之爻是神机发现处：旺相旬空、休囚发动、有日辰动爻生扶、动爻变空、伏而旺相，到底有用，只待出旬值日，有合空、冲起、冲实、填补之法；休囚安静、被日辰动爻克、伏而被克、静逢月破的是真空到底空

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中的爻遇到旬空，是神机在这里显现。如果旺相而旬空，或休囚而发动，有日辰生扶、动爻生扶，动爻变空，伏而旺相，这些旬空到底有用，不过等它出旬值日：有合空、冲起、冲实、填补的办法，后面各卷的占验里注明。如果休囚安静，或被日辰克、动爻克，伏而被克，静而逢月破，遇到这样的旬空，叫做真空，到底是空了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A line in the Void in the hexagram is where the working of the spirits shows. A line that is strong and Void, or resting and moving, with life and support from the Day and from moving lines, or moving to change into the Void, or hidden but strong, has a Void that is in the end useful, and only waits to come out of the period and meet its ruling day: there are the ways of combining the Void, clashing it up, clashing it solid and filling it, set out in the verified examples in the later volumes. A line that is resting and still, or controlled by the Day or a moving line, or hidden and controlled, or still and meeting the Month-break, with such a Void is called a true Void and is empty in the end.
