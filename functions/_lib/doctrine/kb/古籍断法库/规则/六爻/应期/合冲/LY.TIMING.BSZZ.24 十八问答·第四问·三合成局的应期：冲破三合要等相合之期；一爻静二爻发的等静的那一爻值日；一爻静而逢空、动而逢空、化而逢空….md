# 十八问答·第四问·三合成局的应期：冲破三合要等相合之期；一爻静二爻发的等静的那一爻值日；一爻静而逢空、动而逢空、化而逢空的，等出空之期；空而逢合、静而逢合、动而合的，等冲期；自化合或与日合、自化墓或墓于日的，等期冲；自化绝或绝于日的，等期生

六爻 · 应期 · 合冲 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 三合成局，遇到日冲叫暗动（指静爻被日冲），叫填实（指动爻落空被日辰冲），叫破（指月建冲）。它被冲破，必须等到相合的日期到，才应事的吉凶。如果一爻静、两爻发的，必须等那一爻静的值日应事。如果一爻静而逢空，或动而逢空，或变化而逢空的，等到它出空的日期应事的吉凶。如果空而逢合，静而逢合，动而合的，必须等冲的日期到，才应事的吉凶。如果自己化合或与日辰合，或自己化墓或墓在日辰的，必须等到冲的日期（这是说三爻齐发、其中两爻没有病，指自化的那一爻说）。如果自己化绝或绝在日辰的，必须等到生的日期（也是指有一爻有病的说）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a three-harmony frame, meeting a clash from the Day is called secret motion (a still line clashed by the Day), being filled solid (a moving Void line clashed by the Day), or breaking (clashed by the Month). When it is clashed or broken, the luck or ill of the matter answers only when the time of combination comes. If one line is still and two move, one must wait for the still line to meet its ruling day. If one line is still and Void, or moves and is Void, or changes into the Void, wait for the time it comes out of the Void. If it is Void and meets a combination, or still and meets a combination, or moves and combines, one must wait for the time of the clash. If it changes into a combination of itself or combines with the Day, or changes into the tomb of itself or is in the tomb of the Day, one must wait for the time of the clash (this is said of three lines all moving where two have no ailment, and means the one that changes of itself). If it changes into extinction of itself or is extinct on the Day, one must wait for the time of life (also said of the case where one line has an ailment).
