# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占何日有大雨：辰土父母发动月建帮之土旺，得雨必大，但化卯木旬空回头克，卯木出空值日（乙卯日）才下，至期竟无，到立秋辛酉申时才雨——卯木出空到底被克不尽，交申月克之、酉日冲之方得雨

六爻 · 应期 · 天时 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神被空爻回头克，要等那个空爻出空，还要看克它的是谁。书中例：未月甲辰日占哪天有大雨，得小过卦变革卦：日辰辰土父母爻是用神，发动，月建帮它，又值土旺用事，辰土旺得没法说，下雨一定不小；但不宜化出卯木旬空、卯木回头之克；虽然有申金化出酉金进神克木救土，可是卯木旬空，空叫做畏避，躲开金的克，以致辰土父爻终究被卯木所病；必定到甲寅旬乙卯日卯木出空值日，叫出头难避，戌土暗动助金克它；而卯木已经受金克，不能为害辰土，必定等甲寅旬乙卯日下雨。到期竟然没有，到立秋辛酉日申时才下雨。应在立秋后的酉日，为什么？明显是申化酉，就是申月酉日。卯木出空值日到底被克不尽，交申月克它，酉日又冲它，才得下雨。我因为这一卦，学问又进了一层！
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject struck by the turning-back control of a Void line must wait for that Void line to come out, and one must also look at who controls it. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Jiachen day, a man divined on what day there would be heavy rain and got Xiaoguo changing to Ge: the Resource line Chen-earth of the Day is the Subject, moving, helped by the Month, and Earth is also ruling, so Chen-earth is strong beyond words and the rain will surely not be small; but it is unfit that it changes into Mao-wood, which is Void, with the turning-back control of Mao-wood; though Shen-metal changes into You-metal as an Advancing spirit to control Wood and rescue Earth, Mao-wood is Void, and being Void is called taking shelter, avoiding the control of Metal, so that Chen-earth, the Resource line, is in the end made ill by Mao-wood; it must come to the Yimao day of the Jiayin decade, when Mao-wood comes out of the Void and rules, which is called coming out into the open and hard to avoid; Xu-earth secretly moves to help Metal control it; and since Mao-wood is already controlled by Metal it cannot harm Chen-earth, so it must wait for the Yimao day of the Jiayin decade for rain. At the appointed time there was none, and only on the Xinyou day of the beginning of autumn, in the Shen hour, did rain fall. Why does it answer on a You day after the beginning of autumn? Plainly Shen changes into You, which is the Shen month and a You day. Mao-wood coming out of the Void and ruling was in the end not wholly controlled; the Shen month controls it and the You day clashes it, and only then did the rain fall. Through this hexagram my learning went up another level!
