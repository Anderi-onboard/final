# 原忌仇神论·用神旬空月破衰弱或伏藏而得原神动来生，必待用神出旬出破得令值日才所求必遂；用神旺相而原神休囚不动、变墓变绝变克变破变退，或被日辰月建克制，都不能生用

六爻 · 应期 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道原神。先看用神是哪个爻，生用神的爻就是原神。如果用神旬空、月破、衰弱，或者伏藏不现，得到原神发动来生它，或者日辰月建作原神生它，必须等用神出旬、出破、得令、值日，所求的才一定顺遂。如果用神旺相，原神休囚不动，或者动而变墓、变绝、变克、变破、变退，或者被日辰月建克制，都不能生用神，是用神的根基被伤了，不但没有益处，反而有损。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* One must know the Support. First see which line is the Subject; the line that gives life to the Subject is the Support. If the Subject is Void, struck by the Month-break, weak, or hidden and not appearing, and the Support moves to give it life, or the Day or the Month acts as the Support to give it life, one must wait until the Subject comes out of the period, out of the break, gets its season or meets its ruling day, and then what is sought will surely come. If the Subject is strong, but the Support is resting and still, or moves and changes into the tomb, extinction, control, break or retreat, or is controlled by the Day or the Month, none of these can give life to the Subject: the root of the Subject is injured, and it is not only no help but a loss.
