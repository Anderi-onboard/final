# 大象吉而受克须待克神受克、大象凶而受克须防克者逢生：用神吉象被克，等克它的爻受克时才吉；凶象被克，要防克它的爻逢生时更凶

六爻 · 应期 · 受克 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大局象吉而用神受克，必须等到克它的神受克：比如用神落在辰土，得到日月动爻生扶，是大局吉象；如果被寅卯克害，后来遇到申酉日，冲克克神就吉。大局象凶而用神受克，须防克它的神逢生：同前面说的，用神落在辰土，既没有日月动爻生扶，是大局凶象；再遇到寅卯克制它，后来遇到寅卯亥日就凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the overall image is lucky but the Subject is controlled, one must wait until the controlling spirit is itself controlled: for example, if the Subject sits on Chen-earth and is given life and support by the Month, the Day and moving lines, the overall image is lucky; if it is harmed by Yin and Mao, then on a later Shen or You day, when the controlling spirit is clashed and controlled, it is lucky. When the overall image is ill and the Subject is controlled, beware of the controller meeting life: as before, if the Subject sits on Chen-earth and has no life or support from the Month, the Day or moving lines, the overall image is ill; if Yin and Mao also control it, then on a later Yin, Mao or Hai day it is ill.
