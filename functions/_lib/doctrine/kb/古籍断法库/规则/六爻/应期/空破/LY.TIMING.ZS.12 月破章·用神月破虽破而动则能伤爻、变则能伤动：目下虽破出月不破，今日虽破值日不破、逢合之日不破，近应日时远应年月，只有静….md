# 月破章·用神月破虽破而动则能伤爻、变则能伤动：目下虽破出月不破，今日虽破值日不破、逢合之日不破，近应日时远应年月，只有静而不动又无生助的才到底破了

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 诸书都把用神临月破叫做悖时，像枯根朽木，逢生生不起，逢伤伤得更重；虽然现在卦里，有也像没有；伏在卦中，终究难透出来；就算有日辰生它，也不能生；动而作忌神的，也不能为害，作变爻的，也不能伤克动爻。野鹤说：我验证过，动就能伤爻，变就能伤动，为什么？神灵的兆示机缘在动，事情没有吉凶就不会动；既然动了，就有祸福的根基；目下虽然破，出了这个月就不破；今天虽然破，到实破的日子就不破，逢合的日子不破，近的应在日时，远的应在年月；只有静而不动，又没有日辰动爻生助的，才到底破了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* All the books call a Subject on the Month-break "out of season", like a withered root or rotten wood, which if given life is not raised and if injured is injured the more; though it is on the hexagram it is as if absent; hidden in the hexagram, it is hard to bring out in the end; even given life by the Day, it cannot be raised; moving as the Adversary, it cannot do harm; as a changed line, it cannot injure the moving line. Yehe says: I have found it borne out that it can injure a line if it moves and can injure the moving line if it changes into it. Why? The spirits give their sign in moving, and if a matter had neither luck nor ill it would not move; once it moves, there is a basis of fortune or misfortune. Though it is broken now, it is not broken once the month is out; though it is broken today, it is not broken on the day that fills the break, nor on the day it is combined; near answers by day and hour, far by year and month. Only one that is still, does not move, and has no life or support from the Day or a moving line is broken to the end.
