# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占弟死太湖尸首可见否：占尸首以鬼爻为用神，寅木鬼旬空、亥水得令暗动合它，尸首在大水之中，被合须待出旬逢冲，断庚申日可见；没见到，到丑月甲子旬壬申日才浮起——寅鬼出旬而亥水大旺，丑土制水，亥水旬空如水退，木在水中非冲不起

六爻 · 应期 · 求取 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神旬空被合，要等出旬逢冲。书中例：子月乙巳日有人占弟弟死在太湖，尸首能不能见到，得复卦：占尸首以鬼爻为用神，现在寅木爻落旬空，而亥水是得令之水，暗动去合它，分明尸首在大水之中；但寅木旬空被合，必须等出旬逢冲，庚申日可以见到；后来到庚申日没有见到，直到丑月甲子旬内的壬申日，尸首浮起才见到，为什么？总之寅鬼出旬后亥水大旺，到了丑月丑土制水，甲子旬里亥水旬空，水空就像水退了；木在水中不冲就浮不起来，所以应验在甲子旬壬申日，岂不是神灵的奇报？学的人不能不深究。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject in the Void and combined shut must wait for the ten-day period to be out and then be clashed. Worked example: in the Zi month, on a Yisi day, a man divined whether the body of his brother, who had died in Lake Tai, could be seen, and got Fu: in divining for a body the Pressure line is the Subject; now the Yin-wood line falls into the Void, while Hai-water is water in command and secretly moves to combine it, so the body is plainly in deep water; but Yin-wood is Void and combined shut, and must wait for the period to be out and then be clashed, so it can be seen on the Gengshen day; afterwards it was not seen on the Gengshen day, and only on the Renshen day within the Jiazi decade in the Chou month did the body float up and was found; why? In short, after Yin Pressure came out of the Void Hai-water was very strong, and when the Chou month came Chou-earth held the water in check, and in the Jiazi decade Hai-water is Void, and Void water is like water that has receded; wood in water does not float without a clash, so it was borne out on the Renshen day of the Jiazi decade; is this not a wondrous report from the spirits? The student must study it deeply.
