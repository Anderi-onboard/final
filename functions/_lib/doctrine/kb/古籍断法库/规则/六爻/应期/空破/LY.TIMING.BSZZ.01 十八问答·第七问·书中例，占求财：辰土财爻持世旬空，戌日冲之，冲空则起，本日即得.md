# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占求财：辰土财爻持世旬空，戌日冲之，冲空则起，本日即得

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 旬空的爻，没有生扶又被克，到底是空；有生扶没有克，等到时候就有用。书中例：巳月戊戌日占求财，得益卦：辰土财爻持世，因为落旬空，戌日冲它，叫冲空则起，当天就得到；果然应验。应在当天，是因为戌日也是财星来冲我。这是冲空有用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Void line with no life and under control is empty in the end; one with life and no control is useful when its time comes. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined for wealth and got Yi: the Wealth Chen-earth holds the Self and falls into the Void, and the Xu day clashes it, which is "a clash raises up the Void", so he gets it that very day; and it was borne out. It answered on the same day because the Xu day is also the Wealth star clashing me. This is a Void that is useful when clashed.
