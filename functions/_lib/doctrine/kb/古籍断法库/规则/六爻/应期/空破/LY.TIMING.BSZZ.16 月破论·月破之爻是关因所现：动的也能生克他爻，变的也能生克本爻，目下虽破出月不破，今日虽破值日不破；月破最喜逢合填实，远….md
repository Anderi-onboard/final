# 月破论·月破之爻是关因所现：动的也能生克他爻，变的也能生克本爻，目下虽破出月不破，今日虽破值日不破；月破最喜逢合填实，远应年月近应日时；破而安静又旬空衰弱遇克害的是真破到底破

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中月破的爻，是关键因由所现。动的也能生克别的爻，变出的也能生克本爻——目下虽然破，出了这个月就不破；今天虽然破，到值日就不破。月破最喜欢逢合、填实，远的应在年月，近的应在日时。如果破而安静，又碰上旬空衰弱，遇到动爻、月建、日辰克害，这样的月破，叫做真破，到底是破了！
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A line struck by the Month-break is where the pivot of the matter shows. A moving one can also give life to or control other lines, and a changed one can give life to or control its own line: though it is broken now, it is not broken once the month is out; though it is broken today, it is not broken on its ruling day. The Month-break most loves to meet a combination or be filled; far answers by year and month, near by day and hour. If it is broken and still, and also meets the Void and weakness, and is harmed by moving lines, the Month or the Day, such a Month-break is called a true break and is broken in the end!
