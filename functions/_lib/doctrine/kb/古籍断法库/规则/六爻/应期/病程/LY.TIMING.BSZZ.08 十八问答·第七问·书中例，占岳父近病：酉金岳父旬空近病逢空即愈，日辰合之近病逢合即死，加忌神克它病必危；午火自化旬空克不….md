# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占岳父近病：酉金岳父旬空近病逢空即愈，日辰合之近病逢合即死，加忌神克它病必危；午火自化旬空克不了它，旬内不死，防乙酉日，果于乙酉日卯时死

六爻 · 应期 · 病程 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 近病遇空就好，遇合就死。书中例：酉月庚辰日占岳父的近病，得师卦变升卦：酉金岳父爻落旬空，近病遇空就好；日辰合它，近病遇合就死；又不宜忌神来克它，这个病必定危险；问危险在哪天，我说：午火自己化旬空，旬空克不了它，近病遇空不死，旬内不死，要防乙酉日；果然在乙酉日卯时死了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A near illness meeting the Void is cured, and meeting a combination means death. Worked example: in the You month, on a Gengchen day, a man divined about his father-in-law's recent illness and got Shi changing to Sheng: the father-in-law line You-metal falls into the Void, and a near illness meeting the Void is cured; but the Day combines it, and a near illness meeting a combination means death; moreover it is unfit that the Adversary holds and controls it, so this illness is surely dangerous. Asked on what day the danger falls, I said: Wu-fire itself changes into the Void, and a Void cannot control it; a near illness meeting the Void does not die, and he will not die within the ten-day period, so guard against the Yiyou day; indeed he died on the Yiyou day in the Mao hour.
