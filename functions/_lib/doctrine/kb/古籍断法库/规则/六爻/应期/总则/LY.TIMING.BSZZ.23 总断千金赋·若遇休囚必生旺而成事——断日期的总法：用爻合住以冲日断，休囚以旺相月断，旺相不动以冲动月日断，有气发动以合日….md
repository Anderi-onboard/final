# 总断千金赋·若遇休囚必生旺而成事——断日期的总法：用爻合住以冲日断，休囚以旺相月断，旺相不动以冲动月日断，有气发动以合日断，受制以制煞日月断，太旺以墓库日月断，无气发动遇生扶以生扶月日断，入墓以冲墓冲用月日断，旬空安静以出旬逢冲日断，旬空发动以出旬值日断，发动旬空被合以出旬冲日断，旬空安静被冲以出旬合日断，旬空发动逢冲谓冲实以本日断

六爻 · 应期 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果遇到休囚，必待生旺才成事。断日期的方法不能执着于一种，应当用活法推断，才不会有差错。如果用爻被合住，就以冲它的日期来断。或者用爻休囚，必在生旺的时期才能成事，所以无气的当以旺相月来断。如果用爻旺相不动，就以冲动它的月日来断。如果用爻有气发动，就以合它的日子来断。或者有气发动合日辰，或日辰临它而动，或日辰临它而动来生合世爻身爻，就以本日来断。如果用爻受制，就以制煞它的日月来断。如果用爻多得时旺、发动，又遇生扶，这是太旺，应当以墓库的日月来断。如果用爻无气发动而遇生扶，就以生扶它的月日来断。如果用爻入墓，应当以冲墓、冲用的月日来断。如果用爻旬空安静，就以出旬逢冲的日子来断。如果用爻旬空发动，就以出旬值日来断。如果用爻发动旬空被合，就以出旬冲日来断。如果用爻旬空安静被冲，就以出旬合日来断。如果用爻旬空发动逢冲，叫冲实，就以本日来断。以上的断法只是撮其大要，其中最微妙的道理，学的人自己应当融会贯通、灵活变化，分清轻重，辨别用神和忌神，就一定没有差错了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If it meets resting or trapped, it succeeds only in a strong season. The method of judging the day cannot be held to one rule, and should be worked out flexibly, so that there is no mistake. If the Subject line is combined shut, judge by the day that clashes it. If the Subject is resting or trapped, it can succeed only in a period of life and strength, so one without breath is judged by a strong month. If the Subject is strong and still, judge by the month or day that clashes it into motion. If the Subject has breath and moves, judge by the day that combines it. If it has breath and moves to combine with the Day, or the Day sits on it and it moves, or the Day sits on it and it moves to give life to or combine with the Self or Body line, judge by that very day. If the Subject is controlled, judge by the month or day of the star that restrains the controller. If the Subject is in season, strong and moving, and also meets life and support, it is too strong, and should be judged by the month or day of the tomb or storehouse. If the Subject has no breath, moves and meets life and support, judge by the month or day that gives it life and support. If the Subject enters the tomb, judge by the month or day that clashes the tomb or the Subject. If the Subject is Void and still, judge by the day it meets a clash after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and moving, judge by its ruling day after coming out of the period. If the Subject moves and is Void and combined, judge by the clashing day after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and still and is clashed, judge by the combining day after coming out of the period. If the Subject is Void and moving and meets a clash, which is called clashed solid, judge by that very day. The above only picks out the main points; as for the most subtle principle in it, the student should digest it and turn it flexibly, tell heavy from light and distinguish the Subject from the Adversary, and then there will surely be no mistake.
