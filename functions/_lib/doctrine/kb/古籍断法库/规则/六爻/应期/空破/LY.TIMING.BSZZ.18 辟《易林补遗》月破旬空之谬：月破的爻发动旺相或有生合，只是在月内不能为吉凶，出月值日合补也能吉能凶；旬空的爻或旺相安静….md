# 辟《易林补遗》月破旬空之谬：月破的爻发动旺相或有生合，只是在月内不能为吉凶，出月值日合补也能吉能凶；旬空的爻或旺相安静、休囚发动、被日辰生合冲、变出、伏而有提拔，屡试屡验应在出旬

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中月破的爻，发动旺相，或者遇到动爻生合、日辰生合，或者化出回头生合，不过是在月内不能成吉凶，出了月、值日、合补，也能吉能凶。张星元说月破没法解救，一概当凶推。卦中旬空的爻，或者旺相安静，休囚发动，日辰生合或冲它，或是变出的，或者伏着而有提拔的，屡试屡验，应在出旬。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A line struck by the Month-break that moves and is strong, or meets life or combination from a moving line or the Day, or changes into a turning-back life or combination, is merely unable to make luck or ill within the month; once the month is out, or on its ruling day, or when filled and combined, it can bring luck or ill. Zhang Xingyuan says the Month-break cannot be rescued and judges it ill across the board. A line in the Void that is strong and still, or resting and moving, or given life, combined or clashed by the Day, or one that is changed out, or hidden with someone to lift it, has been borne out again and again to answer when the period is out.
