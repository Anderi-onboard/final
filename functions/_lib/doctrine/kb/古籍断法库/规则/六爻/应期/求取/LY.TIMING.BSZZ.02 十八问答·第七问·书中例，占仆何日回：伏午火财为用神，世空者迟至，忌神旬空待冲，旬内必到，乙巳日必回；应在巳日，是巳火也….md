# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占仆何日回：伏午火财为用神，世空者迟至，忌神旬空待冲，旬内必到，乙巳日必回；应在巳日，是巳火也是财星，冲其飞露其伏

六爻 · 应期 · 求取 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 空下的伏神容易提拔出来。书中例：亥月甲子日占仆人哪天回，得革卦：伏在下面的午火财是用神，被日月克，问吉凶就是凶；现在问哪天回来，世爻空的到得慢，忌神旬空，等到冲它，旬内一定到，乙巳日必回；后来果然应验。应在巳日，是因为巳火也是财星，冲掉飞神、显露伏神。《黄金策》说「空下伏神易于引拔」，说的就是这个。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hidden spirit under a Void line is easily drawn out. Worked example: in the Hai month, on a Jiazi day, a man divined on what day his servant would come back and got Ge: the hidden Wu-fire Wealth is the Subject and is controlled by the Month and the Day, so asked about luck it is ill; but asked on what day he returns, a Void Self arrives late, the Adversary is Void and waits to be clashed, so he surely comes within the ten-day period, and surely returns on the Yisi day; and afterwards it was borne out. It answered on the Si day because Si-fire is also Wealth, clashing away the flying spirit and exposing the hidden one. The Huangjin Ce says "a hidden spirit under a Void line is easily drawn out", and this is it.
