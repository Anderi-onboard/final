# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占子病何时愈：亥水子孙化寅木旬空，近病逢空即愈，但亥水化出的寅木旬空受不到酉金之生，必等寅日愈，果验——化空出空

六爻 · 应期 · 病程 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 近病遇空就好，但用神化出的又是空，要等出空的日子。书中例：寅月庚戌日占儿子的病什么时候好，得姤卦变无妄卦：亥水子孙化出寅木旬空，近病遇空就好，但嫌亥水化出的寅木落旬空，受不到酉金的生，必须等寅日才好；果然应验，这是化空出空。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A near illness meets the Void and is cured, but when what the Subject changes into is also Void, wait for the day it comes out. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Gengxu day, a man divined when his son's illness would be cured and got Gou changing to Wuwang: the Output Hai-water changes into Yin-wood, which is Void; a near illness meeting the Void is cured, but the trouble is that Yin-wood, into which Hai-water changes, is Void and cannot receive life from You-metal, so he must wait for the Yin day; and it was borne out, a case of changing into the Void and then coming out of it.
