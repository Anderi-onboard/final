# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占见贵求财：亥水官星持世旬空，出旬亥日必见，月建财爻生世财利如心，应验——出空待用

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 旬空的爻有生扶无克，等到时候就有用。书中例：申月丁卯日占谒见贵人求财，得同人卦：亥水官星持世落旬空，出了这旬到亥日一定见到；月建财爻生世，财利如意；果然应验。这是出空待用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Void line with life and no control is useful when its time comes. Worked example: in the Shen month, on a Dingmao day, a man divined about seeing a noble to seek wealth and got Tongren: the Pressure Hai-water holds the Self and falls into the Void, so once the ten-day period is out he will surely see him on the Hai day; the Month and the Wealth line give life to the Self, so gain comes as hoped; and it was borne out. This is waiting for the Void to be out and then being useful.
