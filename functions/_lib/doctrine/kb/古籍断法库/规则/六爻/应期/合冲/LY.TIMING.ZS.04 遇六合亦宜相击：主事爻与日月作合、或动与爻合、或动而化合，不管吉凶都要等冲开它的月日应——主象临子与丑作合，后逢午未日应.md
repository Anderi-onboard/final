# 遇六合亦宜相击：主事爻与日月作合、或动与爻合、或动而化合，不管吉凶都要等冲开它的月日应——主象临子与丑作合，后逢午未日应

六爻 · 应期 · 合冲 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 遇到六合，也适宜相互冲击：主事爻与日月作合，或发动与别的爻合，或发动而化出合，不管是凶是吉，必定等到冲开它的月日才应验。就像主象落在子，与丑作合，后来遇到午日、未日应验。其余照这个类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Meeting a six-combine, it is likewise fit to be struck by a clash: if the line ruling the matter forms a combination with the Month or the Day, or moves and combines with another line, or moves and changes into a combination, whether it is ill or lucky it answers only in the month or day that clashes it open. For example, if the principal image sits on Zi and forms a combination with Chou, it answers on a later Wu or Wei day. The rest follow this.
