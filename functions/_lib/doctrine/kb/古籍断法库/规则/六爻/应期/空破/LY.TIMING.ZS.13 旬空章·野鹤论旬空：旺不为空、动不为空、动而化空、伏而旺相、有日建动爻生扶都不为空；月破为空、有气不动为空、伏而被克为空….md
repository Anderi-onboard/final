# 旬空章·野鹤论旬空：旺不为空、动不为空、动而化空、伏而旺相、有日建动爻生扶都不为空；月破为空、有气不动为空、伏而被克为空、真空为空；遇旬空命再占，卦吉的许出旬不空，卦凶的许空

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 野鹤说：旺的不算空，动的不算空，动而化空、伏而旺相，都不算空，有日建动爻生扶的，也不算空；月破算空，有气但不动也算空，伏而被克也算空，真空是空。（真空的意思：春天的土、夏天的金、秋天的木、整个冬天遇火，是真空。）我刚学占卜时，凡是遇到旬空，茫然分不清，想断成到底全空，却又应在填实的日子而不空，当作不空，却又到底全空；后来得到多占的办法，凡是遇到旬空，叫他再占一卦，卦吉的，许他出旬就不空；卦凶的，许他是空了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Yehe says: a strong line is not Void, a moving line is not Void, a line that moves and changes into the Void or is hidden and strong is not Void, and one given life and support by the Day Branch or a moving line is not Void; a line struck by the Month-break counts as Void, a line with breath that does not move also counts as Void, a hidden line that is controlled counts as Void, and a true Void is Void. (A true Void: Earth in spring, Metal in summer, Wood in autumn, and Fire in all three winter months.) When I first learned divination I was at a loss whenever I met the Void: I wanted to judge it wholly empty, yet it answered on the day of filling and was not empty; I took it as not empty, yet it was wholly empty in the end. Later I got the method of divining several times: whenever the Void is met, have the man divine again; if the hexagram is lucky, promise that it is not Void once the period is out; if the hexagram is ill, promise that it is Void.
