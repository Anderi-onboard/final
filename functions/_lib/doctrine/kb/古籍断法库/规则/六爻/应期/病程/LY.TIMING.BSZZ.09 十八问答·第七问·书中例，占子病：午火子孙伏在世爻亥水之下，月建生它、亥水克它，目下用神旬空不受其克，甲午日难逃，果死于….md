# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占子病：午火子孙伏在世爻亥水之下，月建生它、亥水克它，目下用神旬空不受其克，甲午日难逃，果死于午日午时——伏无提拔

六爻 · 应期 · 病程 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 伏神得不到提拔，终究是徒劳。书中例：酉月壬辰日占儿子的病，得大过卦：午火子孙伏在世爻亥水之下，月建生助它，亥水克它，眼下用神落旬空不受克，到甲午日难逃了；果然死在午日午时，这叫伏无提拔。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hidden spirit not lifted up is in vain in the end. Worked example: in the You month, on a Renchen day, a man divined about his son's illness and got Daguo: the Output Wu-fire is hidden under the Self line Hai-water; the Month gives it life and Hai-water controls it; for now the Subject is Void and is not hurt by the control, but on the Jiawu day he cannot escape; and indeed he died on the Wu day in the Wu hour, which is what is meant by a hidden spirit with no lift.
