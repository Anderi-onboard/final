# 总断千金赋·月破要出破填合，旬空要出旬值日，动待合、静待冲，克处逢生、绝处逢生、冲中逢合、合处逢冲：乱动就搜独静的爻，安静就看逢冲的一日

六爻 · 应期 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦理只论得中和之道：乱动就要搜寻独静的爻，安静就要看逢冲的一日，月破要出破、填合，旬空要出旬、值日，动的等合，静的等冲，克处逢生、绝处逢生、冲中逢合、合处逢冲，这些法则就是「太过的损掉它才成，不及的补上它才利」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The principle of the hexagram speaks only of the way of balance: if lines move wildly, search for the sole still line; if all are still, look to the one day that brings a clash; the Month-break must leave the break or be filled and combined; the Void must leave the period or meet its ruling day; a moving line waits for a combination and a still one waits for a clash; life met in a control, life met in extinction, a combination met in a clash, and a clash met in a combination: these rules are what "what is excessive succeeds by being reduced, what is lacking profits by being supplied" means.
