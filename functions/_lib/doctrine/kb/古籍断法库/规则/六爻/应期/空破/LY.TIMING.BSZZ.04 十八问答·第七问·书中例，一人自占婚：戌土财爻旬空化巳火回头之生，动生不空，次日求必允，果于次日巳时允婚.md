# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，一人自占婚：戌土财爻旬空化巳火回头之生，动生不空，次日求必允，果于次日巳时允婚

六爻 · 应期 · 空破 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 空的爻发动变出生它的爻，就不算空。书中例：子月癸酉日有人自己占婚事，得恒卦变鼎卦：世爻官、应爻财，算得地；现在戌土财爻落旬空，化出巳火回头生它，动而被生就不空，第二天去求一定答应；果然第二天巳时答应了婚事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Void line that moves and changes into a line that gives it life is not counted as Void. Worked example: in the Zi month, on a Guiyou day, a man divined for his own marriage and got Heng changing to Ding: the Self is Pressure and the Other is Wealth, which counts as well placed; now the Wealth Xu-earth falls into the Void but changes into Si-fire which gives it life in return, and a line that moves and is given life is not Void, so if he asks the next day it will surely be granted; indeed the next day, in the Si hour, the marriage was agreed.
