# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占父近病：巳火父母为用神旬空，日辰拱它，近病逢空不死，但不利六合卦、六合即死；世上忌神暗动、外卦巳酉丑合成金局助水克它，此病必凶，危于出旬辛亥日，果至其日死——出旬又被冲克

六爻 · 应期 · 病程 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 近病遇空不死，遇六合就死。书中例：丑月甲午日有人占父亲的近病，得复卦变噬嗑卦：巳火父母是用神，落旬空，日辰拱它，近病遇空不死，但不利六合卦，六合就死；我想用神空和六合可以相抵，偏偏不宜世上忌神暗动，外卦巳酉丑合成金局去助水克它，此病必凶；他问：凶在哪天？我说：巳日亥日克冲巳火，还在旬空里没有妨碍，恐怕危险在出旬的辛亥日；果然到那天死了，这是应在出旬又被冲克。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A near illness meeting the Void does not die, and meeting a six-combine dies. Worked example: in the Chou month, on a Jiawu day, a man divined about his father's recent illness and got Fu changing to Shihe: the Resource Si-fire is the Subject and falls into the Void, with the Day embracing it; a near illness meeting the Void does not die, but a six-combine hexagram is unfavourable, and a six-combine means death. I thought the Void Subject and the six-combine could offset each other, but what is unfit is the Adversary on the Self line moving secretly, and the outer trigram Si, You and Chou combining into a Metal frame that helps the water to control it, so this illness is surely ill. He asked: on what day does the ill fall? I said: on a Si or a Hai day it is controlled or clashed, but while it is still in the Void there is no harm; I fear the danger lies on the Xinhai day when it comes out of the ten-day period; and indeed he died that day. This is answering at coming out of the period and being clashed and controlled.
