# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占妻病何日愈：卯木财爻为用神旬空，亥水原神生它，次日必愈；有人说空爻应断卯日，答甲寅日卯木已出空、寅木也是用星，果然先好一日

六爻 · 应期 · 病程 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神旬空，有原神相生，等到出空的时候。书中例：午月癸丑日占妻子的病哪天好，得萃卦变比卦：卯木财爻是用神，落旬空，有亥水原神生它，第二天一定好；旁边有人问：卯爻旬空该断卯日，为什么说寅日？我说：你不懂这个办法，到了甲寅日卯木已经出空了，寅木也是用星；果然先好了一天，应在甲寅日。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject in the Void with the Support giving it life waits until the Void is out. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Guichou day, a man divined on what day his wife's illness would be cured and got Cui changing to Bi: the Wealth Mao-wood is the Subject and falls into the Void, with the Support Hai-water giving it life, so she will surely be better the next day. Someone beside him asked: a Void Mao line should be judged on the Mao day, so why the Yin day? I said: you do not know the method; when the Jiayin day comes Mao-wood is already out of the Void, and Yin-wood is also a star of the Subject; indeed she got better a day early, answering on the Jiayin day.
