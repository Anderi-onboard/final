# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，大旱占何日有雨：月建未土父母爻为用神，日辰帮比，有雨必大；巳火官爻原神旬空安静，静必冲、空必待出旬，断辛亥日大雨，果至其日申酉时得雨五寸

六爻 · 应期 · 天时 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 原神旬空安静，要等出旬逢冲。书中例：未月戊戌日因大旱占哪天有雨，得观卦：月建未土父母爻是用神，日辰帮扶，有雨一定大；但巳火官爻是原神，落旬空安静，静了必须冲它，空了必须等出旬；断辛亥日得大雨；果然到那天申酉时得雨五寸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Support that is Void and still must wait for the period to be out and then be clashed. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined on what day there would be rain in a great drought and got Guan: the Resource line Wei-earth of the Month is the Subject, with the Day helping it, so the rain will surely be heavy; but the Pressure Si-fire is the Support, Void and still, and being still it must be clashed and being Void it must wait for the period to be out; it was judged that heavy rain would come on the Xinhai day; and on that day, in the Shen and You hours, five inches of rain fell.
