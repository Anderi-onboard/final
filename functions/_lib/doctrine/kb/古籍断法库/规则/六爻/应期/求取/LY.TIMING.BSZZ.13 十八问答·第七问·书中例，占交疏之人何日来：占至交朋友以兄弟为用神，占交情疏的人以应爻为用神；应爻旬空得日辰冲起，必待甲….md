# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占交疏之人何日来：占至交朋友以兄弟为用神，占交情疏的人以应爻为用神；应爻旬空得日辰冲起，必待甲辰日到，果验

六爻 · 应期 · 求取 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 旬空的爻被日辰冲起，要等到相应的日子。书中例：未月戊戌日占交情疏远的人哪天来，得蹇卦：凡是占至交朋友以兄弟爻为用神，现在占交情疏远的人，应当以应爻为用神；现在应爻落旬空，得日辰冲起，必须等甲辰日到；果然应验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Void line clashed into motion by the Day waits for the corresponding day. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined on what day a casual acquaintance would come and got Jian: in divining for an intimate friend the Peer line is the Subject, but now divining for a casual acquaintance the Other line should be the Subject; now the Other line falls into the Void and is clashed into motion by the Day, so he must wait for the Jiachen day; and it was borne out.
