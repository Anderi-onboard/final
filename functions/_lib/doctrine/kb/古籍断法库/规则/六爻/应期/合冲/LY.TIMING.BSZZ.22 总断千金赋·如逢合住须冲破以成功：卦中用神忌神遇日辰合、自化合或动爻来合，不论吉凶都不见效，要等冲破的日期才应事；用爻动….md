# 总断千金赋·如逢合住须冲破以成功：卦中用神忌神遇日辰合、自化合或动爻来合，不论吉凶都不见效，要等冲破的日期才应事；用爻动来生世本来易成，遇合住又阻滞，须待冲之日才有成

六爻 · 应期 · 合冲 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果逢合住，必须冲破才成功。卦中用神、忌神遇到日辰合，或自己化出合，或有动爻来合它，不论吉凶，都不见效，必须等冲破的日期才应事的吉凶。假如用爻动来生世，凡事容易成，如果遇到合住，又变得阻滞，必须等冲它的日子，事情才有成。以下都是断日期的办法。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If it meets a combination that holds it shut, it must be clashed open before it succeeds. When the Subject or the Adversary in the hexagram meets a combination with the Day, or changes into a combination of itself, or a moving line comes to combine it, whether luck or ill, it has no effect, and the luck or ill of the matter answers only on the day it is clashed open. For example, if the Subject line moves to give life to the Self, everything is easily accomplished, but if it meets a combination that holds it shut it becomes blocked, and one must wait for the day that clashes it before the matter succeeds. What follows are all methods of judging the day.
