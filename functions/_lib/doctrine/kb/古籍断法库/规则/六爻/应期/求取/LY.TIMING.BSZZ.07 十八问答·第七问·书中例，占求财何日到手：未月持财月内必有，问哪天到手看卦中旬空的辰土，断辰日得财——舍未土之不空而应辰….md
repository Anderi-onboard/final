# 十八问答·第七问·书中例，占求财何日到手：未月持财月内必有，问哪天到手看卦中旬空的辰土，断辰日得财——舍未土之不空而应辰土之空

六爻 · 应期 · 求取 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 旬空的爻是事情的关键所现。书中例：未月庚子日占求财哪天到手，得小畜卦：未月财爻持世，月内必定有；现在问哪天到手，卦中辰土落旬空，必定是关键所现，断辰日得财；果然应验。这是舍弃未土的不空，而应在辰土的空上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Void line is where the pivot of the matter shows. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Gengzi day, a man divined on what day wealth would come into his hands and got Xiaoxu: Wealth holds the Self in the Wei month, so he will surely have it within the month; but asked on what day it comes into his hands, the Chen-earth in the hexagram falls into the Void and is surely where the pivot shows, so it was judged that he would get the wealth on the Chen day; and it was borne out. This is passing over Wei-earth that is not Void and answering on the Void Chen-earth.
