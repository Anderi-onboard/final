# 总断千金赋·动值合而绊住：动爻有合就被绊住而不能动，不能生物克物；日辰合它要等冲它的本爻之日应事，旁爻来合要等冲那个旁爻之日；用丑土财子日合之待未日应，子孙动被日辰合住不能生财要等冲动子孙之期

六爻 · 应期 · 合冲 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动而遇到合就被绊住。凡是动爻没遇到合才算动，如果有合就被绊住不能动了；既然不能动，就不能生物克物了。如果日辰合它，必须等冲它本爻的日子到，才能应事的吉凶；如果旁边的爻发动来合它，必须等冲那个旁爻的日子到，才能应事的吉凶。假如用丑土财被子日合住，等到未日应事；子爻合它的，等午日应事。又比如子孙爻发动而被日辰合住，就不能生财，要等冲动子孙的时候到，才有财；其余照这个类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A line that moves and meets a combination is held fast. A moving line counts as moving only if it meets no combination; if there is a combination it is held fast and cannot move, and being unable to move it cannot give life to or control anything. If the Day combines it, one must wait for the day that clashes its own line before the luck or ill of the matter answers; if a neighbouring line moves to combine it, one must wait for the day that clashes that neighbouring line. For example, if the Wealth is Chou-earth and the Zi day combines it, wait for the Wei day to answer; if a Zi line combines it, wait for the Wu day to answer. Again, if the Output line moves and is combined shut by the Day, it cannot give life to Wealth, and one must wait for the time that clashes the Output into motion before there is wealth; the rest follow this.
