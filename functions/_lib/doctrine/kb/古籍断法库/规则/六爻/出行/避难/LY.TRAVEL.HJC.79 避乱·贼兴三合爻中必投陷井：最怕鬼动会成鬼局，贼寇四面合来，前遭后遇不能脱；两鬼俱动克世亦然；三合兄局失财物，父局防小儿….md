# 避乱·贼兴三合爻中必投陷井：最怕鬼动会成鬼局，贼寇四面合来，前遭后遇不能脱；两鬼俱动克世亦然；三合兄局失财物，父局防小儿，财局生合世则父母失散，子孙局克鬼最吉

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 最怕发动的爻会成鬼局，贼寇从四面合围而来，想躲避也前遭后遇，不能脱身；卦里有两个鬼爻都发动克世也是这样。三合成兄弟局，人没事但财物失散；三合成父母局，要仔细照看小孩；三合成财局生合世爻，主父母失散；三合成子孙局而克制鬼爻，最吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Most to be feared is a moving line meeting into a Pressure frame, so that the bandits close in from all sides and, however one wants to hide, one is met in front and caught behind and cannot get free; two Pressure lines both moving to control the Self is the same. A three-harmony Peer frame leaves the person unharmed but his goods scattered; a three-harmony Resource frame calls for care of the small children; a three-harmony Wealth frame giving life to or combining with the Self means the parents are scattered; a three-harmony Output frame that controls the Pressure line is the most auspicious.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1593 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 最怕动会鬼局，必主贼寇四边合来，虽欲避之，前遭后遇不能脱离。 | 最怕发动会成鬼局，必定主贼寇从四面合拢而来，虽然想躲避，前面遭遇后面碰上，不能脱离。 | Most to be feared is a moving line meeting into a Pressure frame, which means the bandits close in from all four sides: though one wishes to avoid them, one is met in front and caught behind and cannot get free. |
> | 卦有两鬼俱动克世，亦然三合兄局，身虽无事，财物失散； | 卦里有两个鬼爻都发动克世，也是这样；三合成兄弟局，人虽然没事，财物会失散； | If the hexagram has two Pressure lines both moving to control the Self, it is the same; a three-harmony Peer frame leaves the person unharmed but his goods scattered and lost; |
> | 三合父局小儿仔细； | 三合成父母局，要仔细照看小孩； | a three-harmony Resource frame calls for care of the small children; |
> | 三合财局生合世爻，则主父母失散； | 三合成财局，生合世爻，就主父母失散； | a three-harmony Wealth frame giving life to or combining with the Self means the parents are scattered; |
> | 三合子孙局克制鬼爻，为最吉也。 | 三合成子孙局，克制鬼爻，最吉。 | a three-harmony Output frame that controls the Pressure line is the most auspicious. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 注文里的「兄局」「父局」是兄弟局、父母局的简称；「身虽无事，财物失散」说的是人没事、东西丢了。
>
> *Note:* In the note "Peer frame" and "Resource frame" are short for the frames of Peer and Resource lines; "the person is unharmed but his goods scattered" means the man comes through but his things are lost.
