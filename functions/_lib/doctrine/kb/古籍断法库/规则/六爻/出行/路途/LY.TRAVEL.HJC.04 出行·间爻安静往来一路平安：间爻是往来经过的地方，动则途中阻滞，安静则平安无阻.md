# 出行·间爻安静往来一路平安：间爻是往来经过的地方，动则途中阻滞，安静则平安无阻

六爻 · 出行 · 路途 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻代表往来经过的地方：动了，途中有阻滞；安静，往来平安没有阻碍；间爻临着财爻或福神发动，途中谋求的反比到了地头还好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The between lines are the places passed through going and coming: if they move there is obstruction on the way; if they are still, the going and coming is safe and unobstructed; if a between line carrying Wealth or Output moves, what is sought on the road is better than at the destination.
