# 总断千金赋·路上有官休出外、宅中有鬼勿居家：五爻为道路，官鬼在五爻发动，途中惊险，不宜出外，灾咎按所临六神断；二爻为住宅，鬼动二爻不利，得子孙持世发动才无害，不然宜迁店铺

六爻 · 出行 · 路途 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 五爻是道路，官鬼落在五爻发动，路上一定多有惊险，不适宜出外，要知道是什么灾咎，按它所临的六神来断，白虎是风波，玄武是盗贼。二爻是住宅，在家求财，鬼爻动在二爻必然不利，按它所临的五行断，火鬼忌火烛之类；得子孙持世发动，差不多就没有害，如果没有子孙发动，宜迁换店铺来化解。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The fifth line is the road: when Pressure sits on it and moves, there will surely be many frights and dangers on the way, and it is unfit to go out; to know what calamity, judge by the six spirits it carries, the White Tiger meaning storms and the Dark Tortoise thieves. The second line is the dwelling: for seeking wealth at home, Pressure moving on this line is surely unfavourable, judged by the element it carries, a Fire Pressure fearing fire and candles and the like; with Output holding the Self and moving there is hardly any harm, and with no Output moving it is resolved by moving the shop.
