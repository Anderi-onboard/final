# 同舟共行章·同行共处应为尊、同路同舟一样论：应伤世位遭他害，只宜生合相扶助；三合同途皆遂意、六冲半路便灰心；占他人以应为用，占亲戚看所用用神，不宜兄鬼动克世

六爻 · 出行 · 同行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 同行、同住，以应爻为尊，同路、同船一样论：应爻伤了世位，会遭他的害；应爻入墓、空、绝、破，是辜负我的恩情；只适宜生合、互相扶助，永远靠他维持，像至亲一样。三合同路，事事遂意；六冲，走到半路就灰心。日常起居为害，看兄弟、官鬼发动；水路和睦清平，看子孙发动。占别人，以应为用；占亲戚，看所属的用神；不宜兄、鬼发动克世，最宜相合相生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For those who travel and stay together, the Other is the honoured one, and those on the same road or in the same boat are judged alike: if the Other injures the Self position, one suffers harm from him; if the Other is in the tomb, Void, extinct or broken, he is ungrateful for my kindness; it is fit only that they give life, combine and support each other, so that one can always rely on his help as on one's own kin. Three-harmony on the same road, everything goes as wished; six-clash, one loses heart halfway. For harm in daily life and lodging, look to a moving Peer or Pressure; for peace and harmony on the water, look to a moving Output. In divining for another person, the Other is the Subject; for a relative, look at that relative's own Subject; it is unfit for Peer or Pressure to move and control the Self, and best when they combine and generate.
