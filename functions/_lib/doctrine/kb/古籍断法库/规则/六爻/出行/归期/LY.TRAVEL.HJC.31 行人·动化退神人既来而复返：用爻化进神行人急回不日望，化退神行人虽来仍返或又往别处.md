# 行人·动化退神人既来而复返：用爻化进神行人急回不日望，化退神行人虽来仍返或又往别处

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻如果化出进神，行人很快就回，不久可望；化出退神，行人虽然来了仍会返回，或者又去别处；「既来而复返」总的是说回不来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line changes into an Advancing spirit, the traveller hurries home and may be looked for before long; if it changes into a Retreating spirit, though the traveller comes he goes back again, or goes elsewhere; "comes and goes back again" says in sum that he cannot come home.
