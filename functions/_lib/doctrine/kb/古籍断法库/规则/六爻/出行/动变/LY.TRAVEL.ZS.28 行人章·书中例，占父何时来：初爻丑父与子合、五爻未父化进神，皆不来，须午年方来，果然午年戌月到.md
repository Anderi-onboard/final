# 行人章·书中例，占父何时来：初爻丑父与子合、五爻未父化进神，皆不来，须午年方来，果然午年戌月到

六爻 · 出行 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神发动化进神，不回来；发动的遇合则被事绊住，逢合之年才来。书中例：辰年未月丁丑日占父亲什么时候来，得大有卦变井卦：初爻丑土父母与子水相合，不来；五爻未土父母化出进神，不来；必须到午年才来；果然午年戌月到。应在午年，是因为未爻的父母发动，午年合它，正是发动的爻逢合之年；又因为初爻子水与丑土相合，合住要冲开，午年冲开了它。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject that moves and changes into an Advancing spirit does not return; a moving one that meets a combination is held up, and comes in the year it meets the combination. Worked example: in a Chen year, in the Wei month, on a Dingchou day, a man divined when his father would come and got Dayou changing to Jing: the Resource Chou-earth in the first line is combined with Zi-water, so he does not come; the Resource Wei-earth in the fifth line changes into an Advancing spirit, so he does not come; he must come only in a Wu year; and he arrived in a Wu year, in the Xu month. It answered in the Wu year because the Wei-line Resource moves and the Wu year combines it, the year in which a moving line meets a combination; and also because Zi in the first line combines with Chou, and a combination must be clashed open, which the Wu year does.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这一例的断法是「化进神不来、等逢合之年才来」，与《黄金策》行人章「用爻化进神行人急回」相反；两书各有书中例，没有交代谁错。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This example judges that a change into an Advancing spirit means he does not come and arrives only in the year of the combination, which is the opposite of the Huangjin Ce on travellers, where a Subject changing into an Advancing spirit means a quick return; each book has worked examples, and neither says which is wrong.
