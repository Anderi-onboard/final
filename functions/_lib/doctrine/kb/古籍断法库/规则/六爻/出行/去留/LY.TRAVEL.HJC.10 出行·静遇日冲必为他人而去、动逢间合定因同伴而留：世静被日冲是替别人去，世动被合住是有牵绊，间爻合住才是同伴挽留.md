# 出行·静遇日冲必为他人而去、动逢间合定因同伴而留：世静被日冲是替别人去，世动被合住是有牵绊，间爻合住才是同伴挽留

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻安静而被日辰、动爻暗冲，是别人托他去的，不是为自己谋事；世爻发动而被日辰、动爻合住，是将走而有牵绊，不能起程，只有间爻合住才是同伴把他留住。要断起程的日期，要逢到冲开的日子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self is still and is stirred in secret by the clash of the Day or a moving line, someone else has asked him to go and it is not for his own business; if the Self moves and is held by a combination from the Day or a moving line, he is about to go but is tied down and cannot set out, and only when a between line holds it is a companion keeping him back. To fix the day of departure one must wait for the day that clashes it open.
