# 行人章·书中例，舟中占风、次日再占：酉金子孙持世安静，应在静而逢冲之日，卯日必有顺风；次日再占仍是酉金子孙持世，果然卯日风顺

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙安静，在逢冲的日子有顺风。书中例：戌月戊子日在船上占风，得坤卦变震卦：酉金子孙持世，必应在安静而逢冲的日子，卯日必有顺风；第二天己丑日又占，得夬卦，仍是酉金子孙持世，果然到卯日风顺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A still Output brings a fair wind on the day that clashes it. Worked example: in the Xu month, on a Wuzi day, a man divined for the wind on a boat and got Kun changing to Zhen: Output You-metal holds the Self, and it must answer on the day when a still line meets a clash, so a fair wind will surely come on the Mao day; the next day, a Jichou day, he divined again and got Guai, again with Output You-metal holding the Self, and indeed on the Mao day the wind was fair.
