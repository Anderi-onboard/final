# 行人章·书中例，占主人往楚平安否、迁往他省否：连占三卦，前两卦用神真空是凶亡之兆，报的是未年之死，第三卦申父生世才报眼前所问（四月必归）；野鹤：占远应近、占近应远，不能一卦竟断

六爻 · 出行 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神入墓、绝、空、破，回来的消息杳无音信。书中例：寅月庚寅日仆人占主人往楚地平安与否、是否迁往别省，得观卦：父母遇真空，是不祥之兆，不敢直接断，再叫亲人占；同日占兄长，得中孚卦变临卦：前一卦仆人占主人，父母为用神，父母真空；这一卦弟弟占兄长，兄弟为用神，又落真空，是大凶之兆；再叫亲人占；次日辛卯日家人又占，得坎卦变困卦，作者说：得这一卦，现在可以替你放心了——前两卦用神真空是凶亡的兆头，这一卦申金父母生世爻，四月必归；问前面父、兄真空是为什么，是因为此公寿数无多，神灵报的是未年的死，没有说眼下的事；未年死的根据是占主人时父母临未土、占兄长时兄临未土，都被日月克害，因为在旬空所以暂时没有灾祸，未年出空，被日月伤，岂能不危险。辰年占的，果然未年死。野鹤说：连占两卦都报的是没有问的事，第三卦才报眼前所问，所以感叹前人用一卦竟断吉凶，不知道把「占远应近、占近应远」的卦错断了这么多。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject in the tomb, extinct, Void or broken gives no word of a return. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Gengyin day, a servant divined whether his master, gone to Chu, was safe and whether he would move to another province, and got Guan: the Resource met true Void, an ominous sign, and I did not dare judge outright, so the household was told to divine again; the same day, divining for the elder brother, they got Zhongfu changing to Lin: in the former hexagram the servant divined for the master with the Resource as the Subject, and the Resource was truly Void; in this one the younger brother divined for the elder with the Peer as the Subject, and it too fell into true Void, a sign of great ill; so they were told to divine again; the next day, a Xinmao day, the family divined once more and got Kan changing to Kun. I said: with this hexagram I can set your mind at ease. The Subject in the first two was truly Void, a sign of death; in this one the Resource Shen-metal gives life to the Self, and he will surely return in the fourth month. When asked why the Resource and the Peer were truly Void before, I said: because this gentleman has not long to live, the spirits report his death in a Wei year and do not speak of what is before us. When asked how I knew it was a Wei year, I said: in divining for the master the Resource sits on Wei-earth, and in divining for the brother the Peer sits on Wei-earth, both controlled and harmed by the Month and the Day; being in the Void they cause no harm for now, but in the Wei year they come out of Void and are injured by the Month and Day, and how could that not be dangerous? Divined in a Chen year, he did die in the Wei year. Yehe says: two hexagrams in a row reported what had not been asked, and only the third reported the present question; hence I sigh that the former worthies judged luck and ill-luck from a single hexagram, not knowing how many hexagrams of "far matter answered near, near matter answered far" they have misjudged.

^q7

> [!note] 按
> 「真空」指用神落在旬空、又没有被冲被填的空，是凶象；连着三卦的前两卦用神真空报的是远应（未年的死），第三卦才答眼前的归期，这一例是野鹤「占远应近、占近应远」的出处。
>
> *Note:* "True Void" means a Subject in the ten-day Void that is neither clashed nor filled, an ill sign. The first two hexagrams, with the Subject truly Void, reported a far event (a death in a Wei year); only the third answered the present question of return. This example is the source of Yehe's remark that a far matter may be answered near, and a near matter answered far.
