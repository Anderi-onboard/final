# 行人章·世爻空者行人即至、用神静逢休囚空破者且不思回：动空、旺空的在冲实、冲空的月日回来，卦变克绝、反吟、用神被冲克的难望其回

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻空的，行人马上就到；用神安静，又遇到休囚空破的，还没有回来的念头。发动而空、旺而空的，在填实或冲开空亡的月日回来；就怕卦变到克、绝，或变成反吟，或用神被冲被克，这些都很难盼到他回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Self is Void, the traveller is arriving at once; when the Subject is still and meets resting, trapped, Void or broken, he does not yet think of returning. A Void line that moves, or one that is strong yet Void, brings him back in a month or day that fills it or clashes the Void open; but if the hexagram changes into control or extinction, or into Reversed Chant, or the Subject is clashed or controlled, there is little hope of his return.
