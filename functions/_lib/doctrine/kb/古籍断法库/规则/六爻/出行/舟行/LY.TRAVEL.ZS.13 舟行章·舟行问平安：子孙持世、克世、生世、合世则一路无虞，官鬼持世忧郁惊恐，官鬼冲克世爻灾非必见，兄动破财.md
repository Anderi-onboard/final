# 舟行章·舟行问平安：子孙持世、克世、生世、合世则一路无虞，官鬼持世忧郁惊恐，官鬼冲克世爻灾非必见，兄动破财

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 乘船问平安与否：子孙持世、克世、生世、合世，一路没有忧患；官鬼持世，忧愁惊恐；官鬼冲克世爻，必定见灾非；兄爻发动，破财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On a boat, asking whether it will be safe: if Output holds, controls, gives life to or combines with the Self, the whole road is free of trouble; if Pressure holds the Self, worry and fright; if Pressure clashes with or controls the Self, calamity and trouble will surely be seen; a moving Peer line costs money.
