# 三合章·占出行用神在三合之内则被合而留，占行人用神在三合之内则被合不返

六爻 · 出行 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占出行，用神在三合局之内，会被合住而留下来；占行人，用神在三合局之内，被合住不回来。同一章里，占功名三合官局生世利于我、生应利于他，占财财局生世利于我、生应利于他，也是同一个看法。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for setting out, a Subject inside a three-harmony frame is combined shut and stays; in divining for a traveller, a Subject inside a three-harmony frame is combined shut and does not return. In the same chapter, in divining for office a Pressure frame giving life to the Self is good for me and giving life to the Other is good for him, and a Wealth frame in divining for wealth is read the same way.
