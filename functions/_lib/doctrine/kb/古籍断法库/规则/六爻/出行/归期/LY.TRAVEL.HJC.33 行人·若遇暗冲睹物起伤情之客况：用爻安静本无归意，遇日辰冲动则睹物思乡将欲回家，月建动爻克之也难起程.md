# 行人·若遇暗冲睹物起伤情之客况：用爻安静本无归意，遇日辰冲动则睹物思乡将欲回家，月建动爻克之也难起程

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻安静，本来没有回家的意思；如果遇到日辰冲动，必定看见东西就想起家乡，将要回家；如果月建、动爻来克它，也难以起程。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line is still he has no thought of returning; if the Day clashes it into movement, he is sure to be reminded of home by what he sees and is about to go back; but if the Month or a moving line comes to control it, he can hardly set out either.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联的注文在原书里分在两行，「将」字后面断开，「欲回家」接在下一行，这里合起来引。
>
> *Note:* In the book the note to this couplet is split over two lines, breaking after one character, with "about to go back" on the next line; the two are quoted together here.
