# 行人·五爻有鬼皆因途路之不通：用爻伏在五爻官鬼之下，必因关津不通而被阻

六爻 · 出行 · 平安 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻伏在第五爻的官鬼下面，必定是因为关口、渡口不通而被阻住。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line is hidden under the Pressure line in the fifth place, he is surely stopped because the passes and ferries are closed.

## 《黄金策·行人》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3523 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Absent travellers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 五爻有鬼， | 第五爻上有官鬼， | If there is Pressure on the fifth line, |
> | 皆因途路之不通 | 都是因为路途不通 | it is all because the road is blocked |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3523 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用爻伏于五爻官鬼之下，必因关津不通而阻也。 | 用爻伏在五爻的官鬼下面，必定因关口渡口不通而被阻。 | If the Subject line is hidden under the Pressure line in the fifth place, he is surely stopped because the passes and ferries are closed. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 原书这一行把两联写在了一起（「五爻有鬼……」「一卦无财……」），这里按联拆成两条，各取各的注。
>
> *Note:* The book writes two couplets together on this line ("Pressure in the fifth place ..." and "no Wealth in the hexagram ..."); they are split here into two entries, each with its own note.
