# 出行·两间齐空独行则吉：间爻代表同伴，两间都落旬空，宜自己单独走

六爻 · 出行 · 同行 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻如果空，主途中没有阻滞；间爻又代表同伴，两个间爻都落旬空，宜自己单独走，免得被同伴拖累。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a between line is Void there is no obstruction on the way; the between lines also stand for companions, and if both are in the Void of their ten-day period it is fit to travel alone, so as to escape being burdened by companions.
