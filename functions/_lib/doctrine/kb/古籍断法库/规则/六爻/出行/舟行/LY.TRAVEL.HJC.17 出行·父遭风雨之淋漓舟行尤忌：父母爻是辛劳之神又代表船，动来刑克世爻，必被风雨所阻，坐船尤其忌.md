# 出行·父遭风雨之淋漓舟行尤忌：父母爻是辛劳之神又代表船，动来刑克世爻，必被风雨所阻，坐船尤其忌

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻是辛勤劳苦的神，动了就要跋涉路程、不能安稳；刑克世爻，必定被风雨阻住；父母爻又代表船，克世，行船不利，所以坐船出行尤其忌讳。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line is the spirit of toil and hardship; if it moves one must trudge the road and cannot be at ease; if it punishes or controls the Self, one will surely be stopped by wind and rain; the Resource line is also the boat, and controlling the Self makes travel by boat unfavourable, so it is especially to be avoided when travelling by boat.
