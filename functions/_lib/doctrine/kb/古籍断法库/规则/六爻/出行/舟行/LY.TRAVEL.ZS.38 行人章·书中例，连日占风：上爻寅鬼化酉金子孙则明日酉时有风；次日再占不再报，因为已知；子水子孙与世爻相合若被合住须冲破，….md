# 行人章·书中例，连日占风：上爻寅鬼化酉金子孙则明日酉时有风；次日再占不再报，因为已知；子水子孙与世爻相合若被合住须冲破，明日午时顺风必大，舟行三百余里

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动化出子孙，这一天有风；子孙与世爻相合，被合住要冲开才成。书中例：戌月壬辰日占风，得大畜卦变泰卦：上爻寅木官鬼化出酉金子孙，明天酉时有风；第二天癸巳日又占风，得姤卦变小畜卦。有人问：昨天说今天酉时有风，今天的卦为什么不显现？我说：神灵以为我已经知道了，不再报；然而今天酉时固然有风，但一定不大，神灵报的是明天的大风，因为子水子孙与世爻相合，如果被合住，必须冲破才成功，明天午时，顺风必大；果然第二天风顺帆饱，船行三百多里。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Output moves, that day has wind; when Output combines with the Self and is held fast, it needs to be clashed open to succeed. Worked example: in the Xu month, on a Renchen day, a man divined for the wind and got Daxu changing to Tai: the Pressure Yin-wood in the top line changes into Output You-metal, so tomorrow at the You hour there will be wind; the next day, a Guisi day, he divined for the wind again and got Gou changing to Xiaoxu. Someone asked: you said yesterday there would be wind at the You hour today, so why does today's hexagram not show it? I said: the spirits take it that I already know, and do not report it again. There will indeed be wind at the You hour today, but it will certainly not be strong; what the spirits report is tomorrow's great wind. Output Zi-water combines with the Self, and when it is held fast by a combination it must be clashed open before it succeeds; tomorrow at the Wu hour the fair wind will certainly be strong. Indeed the next day the wind was fair and the sail full, and the boat made over three hundred li.
