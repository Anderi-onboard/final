# 行人章·书中例，仆占主人何日回：仆占主人以父母为用神，子水财爻化出巳火父爻，动爻不克变爻，巳日必到

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占亲人，看所属的用神；发动的在逢合的日子回来，伏藏的在出现的日子回来。书中例：寅月癸亥日仆人占主人哪天回，得大畜卦变小畜卦：仆人占主人，以父母爻为用神，这一卦子水财爻发动，化出巳火父母爻，动爻不克变爻，断巳日必到；果然在巳日到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for a kinsman, look at that kinsman's own Subject; a moving one returns on the day it meets a combination, and a hidden one on the day it appears. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Guihai day, a servant divined on what day his master would return and got Daxu changing to Xiaoxu: a servant divining for his master takes the Resource line as the Subject; here the Wealth Zi-water moves and changes into the Resource Si-fire, and the moving line does not control the changed line, so he will surely arrive on the Si day; he did arrive on the Si day.
