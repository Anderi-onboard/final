# 行人章·书中例，由淮回扬附载登舟占风：二爻辰土子孙是得顺风之喜，四爻戌土子孙是到家之喜，断明日辰时顺风、戌时抵扬

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动是顺风；子孙落在哪一爻、临哪个时辰，就应在哪一个时辰有风。书中例：亥月癸亥日占顺风，得未济卦变艮卦：有人从淮安回扬州，我搭他的船，上船后他就占风，我说：明天辰时得顺风，戌时就能到扬州。他问：怎么这么快？我说：二爻辰土子孙，是得顺风的喜悦；四爻戌土子孙，是到家的喜悦。结果和所占的一样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving is the fair wind; the hour answers on whichever line Output sits and whichever hour it rules. Worked example: in the Hai month, on a Guihai day, a man divined for a fair wind and got Weiji changing to Gen: someone was going back from Huai to Yang and I took passage with him; on boarding he divined for the wind at once, and I said: tomorrow at the Chen hour a fair wind will come, and at the Xu hour we will reach Yang. He asked: why so fast? I said: the Output Chen-earth in the second line is the joy of getting a fair wind; the Output Xu-earth in the fourth line is the joy of getting home. It came out as divined.

## 书中卦例

> | 有由淮回杨者，余附载之，登州，彼即占风，余曰：明日辰时，得顺风，戌时即抵杨矣。 | 有人从淮安回扬州，我搭他的船，上船后他就占风，我说：明天辰时得顺风，戌时就到扬州了。 | Someone was going back from Huai to Yang and I took passage with him; on boarding he divined for the wind at once, and I said: tomorrow at the Chen hour a fair wind will come, and at the Xu hour we will reach Yang. |

> | 四爻戌土子孙，到家之喜悦也。 | 四爻戌土子孙，是到家的喜悦。 | the Output Xu-earth in the fourth line is the joy of getting home. |
> | 果如所占。 | 结果和所占的一样。 | It came out as divined. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 原文「余附载之，登州」中的「登州」疑是「登舟」之误（上船）；这里按「上船」译。「杨」即扬州。
>
> *Note:* The original has the place-name "Dengzhou" after "I took passage with him"; it is probably a slip for the phrase meaning "boarding the boat", which sounds the same, and it is translated as "boarding". "Yang" is Yangzhou.
