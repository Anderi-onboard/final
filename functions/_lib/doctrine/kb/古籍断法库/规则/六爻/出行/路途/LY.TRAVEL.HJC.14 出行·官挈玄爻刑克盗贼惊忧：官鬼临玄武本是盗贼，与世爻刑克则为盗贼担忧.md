# 出行·官挈玄爻刑克盗贼惊忧：官鬼临玄武本是盗贼，与世爻刑克则为盗贼担忧

六爻 · 出行 · 路途 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼临着玄武，本来就是盗贼；如果和世爻相刑克，免不了为盗贼担惊受忧。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure on the Dark Tortoise is by nature thieves; if it punishes or controls the Self, one cannot escape fright and worry over thieves.

## 《黄金策·出行》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3451 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官挈玄爻刑克， | 官鬼带着玄武，又刑克（世爻）， | If Pressure carries the Dark Tortoise and punishes or controls the Self, |
> | 盗贼惊忧 | 要为盗贼担惊受忧 | one will be frightened and troubled by thieves |
