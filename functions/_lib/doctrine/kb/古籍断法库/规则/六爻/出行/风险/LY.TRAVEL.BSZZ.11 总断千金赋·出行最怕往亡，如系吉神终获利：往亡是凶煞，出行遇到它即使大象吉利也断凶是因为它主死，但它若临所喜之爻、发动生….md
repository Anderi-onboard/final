# 总断千金赋·出行最怕往亡，如系吉神终获利：往亡是凶煞，出行遇到它即使大象吉利也断凶是因为它主死，但它若临所喜之爻、发动生扶拱合世身用爻，就当利不当害——神煞的分量轻，五行的分量重

六爻 · 出行 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出行最怕往亡，但如果往亡落在吉神上，终究获利。往亡是凶煞，出行遇到它，即使整个卦象吉利，也断为凶，因为它主死；但如果它落在所喜的爻上、发动来生扶拱合世身和用爻，我一定认为有利而不认为有害。因为神煞的权重轻、五行的权重重。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Setting out most fears Wangwang, but if it sits on an auspicious spirit one gains in the end. Wangwang is a baleful star: when setting out meets it, even if the whole hexagram looks lucky it is judged ill, because it speaks of death; but if it sits on a welcome line and moves to give life to, support, embrace or combine with the Self, the Body and the Subject, I must take it as profit and not as harm, because the weight of the stars is light and the weight of the five elements is heavy.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1282 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 往亡，凶煞也，出行遇之，虽大象吉利，竟断其凶，因死之故也，若临所喜之爻动来生扶拱合世身用爻者，吾必以为利而不以为害也。 | 往亡是凶煞，出行遇到它，即使整个卦象吉利，也断为凶，因为它主死；但如果它落在所喜的爻上、发动来生扶拱合世身和用爻，我一定认为有利而不认为有害。 | Wangwang is a baleful star: when setting out meets it, even if the whole hexagram looks lucky it is judged ill, because it speaks of death; but if it sits on a welcome line and moves to give life to, support, embrace or combine with the Self, the Body and the Subject, I must take it as profit and not as harm. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一联的前半是「虎兴而遇吉神……疾病大宜天喜，若临凶煞必生悲」，这里只取出行一句；「往亡」是旧时选日用的凶煞，主出行不利。
>
> *Note:* The first half of the couplet speaks of the White Tiger, the Dragon, the Dark Tortoise, the Vermilion Bird and the Tianxi star for illness; only the line on setting out is taken here. Wangwang is a baleful star of the old day-selection lore, held to make setting out unfavourable.
