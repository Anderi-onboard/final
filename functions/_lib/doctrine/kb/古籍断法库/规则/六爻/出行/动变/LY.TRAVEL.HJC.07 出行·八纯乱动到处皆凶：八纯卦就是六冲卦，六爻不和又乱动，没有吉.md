# 出行·八纯乱动到处皆凶：八纯卦就是六冲卦，六爻不和又乱动，没有吉

六爻 · 出行 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 八纯卦就是六冲卦，六爻不和，再碰上乱动，到处都凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A pure-palace hexagram is a six-clash hexagram, its six lines at odds, and with disorderly movement besides, everywhere is ill.

## 《黄金策·出行》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3437 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 八纯乱动到处皆凶 | 八纯卦六爻乱动，到处都凶 | In a pure-palace hexagram with the lines moving in disorder, everywhere is ill |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3437 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 八纯乃六冲之卦，六爻不和又遇乱动，何吉之有？ | 八纯卦就是六冲卦，六爻不和，又碰上乱动，哪里还有吉？ | A pure-palace hexagram is a six-clash hexagram, its six lines at odds, and meeting disorderly movement besides, where is the luck? |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「八纯」是八个纯卦（乾为天、坤为地、震为雷、巽为风、坎为水、离为火、艮为山、兑为泽），上下卦相同，都是六冲卦。
>
> *Note:* The "eight pure" hexagrams are the eight hexagrams doubled from one trigram (Qian, Kun, Zhen, Xun, Kan, Li, Gen, Dui), whose upper and lower trigrams are the same; all are six-clash hexagrams.
