# 趋避章·书中例，同乡占避乱往哪个方向：众人以子孙属水、金动生之，主往北；作者断兄动化进神是破财之象，午火得令生世，往南避为吉；众人从南避，后来贼从北来放火烧村，所以生方为吉，子孙之方亦吉，要以旺衰分别

六爻 · 出行 · 方位 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 避乱要避到生世的方向和子孙的方向，还要按旺衰来分辨。书中例：巳月丙戌日同乡的人占避乱，得乾卦变大有卦：众人认为子孙属水，有金动来生它，北方为吉。我说：不对。兄爻动化出进神，是破财之象；午火当令，生助世爻，往南避才吉。众人信了我的话，都往南避。后来贼果然从北边来，放火把村子烧光，房屋稻谷都成了灰烬，这是申金兄爻发动破财的缘故。所以生世的方向吉，子孙的方向也吉；又要用旺衰来分别，火虽然是鬼的方向，生我又有什么妨碍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In avoiding disorder, go to the direction that gives life to the Self and the direction of Output, and tell them apart by strength and weakness. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Bingxu day, men of the same village divined which way to flee the disorder and got Qian changing to Dayou. The company held that Output belongs to Water, and Metal moves to give it life, so the north is lucky. I said: no. The Peer line moves and changes into an Advancing spirit, which is a sign of losing wealth; Wu-fire is in command and gives life to the Self, so it is lucky to flee south. They believed me and all fled south. Afterwards the bandits did come from the north, set fire to the village and left, and the houses and the rice were all ashes, which was the loss of wealth from the moving Shen-metal Peer. So the direction that gives life to the Self is lucky, and the direction of Output is also lucky; and one must also distinguish by strength and weakness: though Fire is the direction of Pressure, what harm is there in its giving life to me.
