# 避乱·天来大事也无妨、海样深仇何足虑：总括上面四节，卦中只要有一个吉神，决然无事

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 这两句总括上面四节：卦中只要有一个吉神，决然无事，天大的事也无妨，海一样深的仇怨也不必忧虑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* These two lines sum up the four sections above: so long as the hexagram has one auspicious spirit, there will certainly be no trouble; even a matter as great as heaven does no harm, and a grudge as deep as the sea is nothing to worry over.

## 《黄金策·避乱》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1645 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Fleeing disorder", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 天来大事也无妨， | 天大的事也无妨， | Even a matter as great as heaven does no harm, |
> | 海样深仇何足虑 | 海一样深的仇怨又何必忧虑 | and a grudge as deep as the sea is nothing to worry over |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1645 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 此二句总上文四节，总结而言，卦中有一吉神，决然无事也。 | 这两句总括上面四节，总结起来说，卦中只要有一个吉神，就决然无事。 | These two lines sum up the four sections above: in sum, so long as the hexagram has one auspicious spirit, there will certainly be no trouble. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一章最后的一段（第 1647 行）是对读者的话——「事有百端，理无二致」，要融会贯通，不是判断规则，没有收。
>
> *Note:* The last paragraph of the chapter (line 1647) is advice to the reader — "affairs are hundredfold but the principle is one" — to master it all and reason from principle; it is not a rule of judgement and is not included.
