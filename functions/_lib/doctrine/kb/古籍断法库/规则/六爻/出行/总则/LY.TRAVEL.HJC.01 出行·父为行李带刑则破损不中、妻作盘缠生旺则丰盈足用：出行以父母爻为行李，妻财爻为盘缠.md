# 出行·父为行李带刑则破损不中、妻作盘缠生旺则丰盈足用：出行以父母爻为行李，妻财爻为盘缠

六爻 · 出行 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占出行，父母爻是行李：旺相就多，休囚就少，带着刑害就是破损的旧东西；妻财爻是盘缠（路费、本钱）：旺相就丰足，休囚就微少，从兄弟爻变出来的是合伙的本钱或借来的。为名为利出门免不了奔波，路上的休咎，神灵能给准信。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for a journey the Resource line is the baggage — much if strong, little if resting or trapped, worn and damaged things if it carries punishment or harm — and the Wealth line is the travelling money and capital — full if strong, slight if resting or trapped, and a joint stake or borrowed money if it comes out of the Peer line by change. One who goes out for fame or gain cannot avoid the roads, and the spirits give a sure report of good and ill on the way.
