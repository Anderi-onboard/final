# 十八问答·第十八问·书中例，占伯父何日回：用神伏藏受克而不来，若问平安则父母卯木被日月动爻冲克必不安，问来不来不能这样断；后来果然不来，在外平安（与《增删卜易》同一卦，被占的人是母亲）

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 书中例：酉月戊申日占伯父哪天回来，得旅卦变艮卦：这一卦如果问伯父平安与否，卯木父母伏而不现，被日月动爻冲克，必定不平安；现在问的是回不回来，不这样断，只能断用神伏藏受克所以不来；后来果然没有来，他在外面平安。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Worked example: in the You month, on a Wushen day, a man divined on what day his uncle would come back and got Lü (the Traveller) changing to Gen: had this hexagram been asked about the uncle's safety, the Resource Mao-wood is hidden and does not appear, clashed and controlled by the Month, the Day and the moving lines, and he would surely be unwell; but the question is whether he comes back, so it is not judged that way, and one can only judge that the Subject is hidden and controlled and so does not come; in the end he did not come, and he was safe abroad.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 同一个卦、同一个日子、同样的判词，《增删卜易》行人章记的是占「母」在外何时来，这里记的是占「伯父」何日回——两本书对被占的人说法不同，判词一字不差地相同。
>
> *Note:* The same hexagram, the same date and the same judgement are recorded in the chapter on travellers in the Zengshan Buyi as divining when a mother abroad would come; here it is an uncle. The two books name a different person, with the judgement word for word the same.
