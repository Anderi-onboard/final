# 出行章·静遇日冲必去、动逢合住而留：世爻暗动必去，世旺静逢冲之日必去，世爻动而化合或被日月动爻合住必有事故阻滞（觉子：也有应在冲开之日才走的）

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻安静而遇到日冲，一定去；发动而被合住，就留下。世爻暗中发动的，一定去；世爻旺相而安静的，逢到冲的日子一定去；世爻发动而化出合，或者被日、月、动爻合住的，必有事故，被阻滞不能走。觉子说：我曾试验过，也有应验在冲开的那天才走的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self is still and meets the clash of the Day, one surely goes; if it moves and is held by a combination, one stays. If the Self moves in secret, one surely goes; if the Self is strong and still, one surely goes on the day that clashes it; if the Self moves and changes into a combination, or is held by a combination from the Day, the Month or a moving line, there is surely some affair, and one is obstructed and does not go. Juezi says: I have tried it and found that it sometimes answers by going on the day that clashes it open.
