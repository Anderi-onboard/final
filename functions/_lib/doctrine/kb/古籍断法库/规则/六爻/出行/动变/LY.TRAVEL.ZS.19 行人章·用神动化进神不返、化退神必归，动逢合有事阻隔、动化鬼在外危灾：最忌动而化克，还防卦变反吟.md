# 行人章·用神动化进神不返、化退神必归，动逢合有事阻隔、动化鬼在外危灾：最忌动而化克，还防卦变反吟

六爻 · 出行 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神发动化出进神，不回来；用神发动化出退神，一定回来。发动遇到合，有事阻隔在外；发动化出官鬼，在外有危险灾祸。最怕发动而化出克它的爻，还要提防卦变成反吟。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject that moves and changes into an Advancing spirit does not return; one that changes into a Retreating spirit surely returns. A moving Subject that meets a combination is held up by some affair; one that changes into Pressure meets danger and calamity abroad. Most to be feared is a movement that changes into a controlling line, and beware too of the hexagram changing into Reversed Chant.

> [!warning] 打架
> 《黄金策》说用爻化进神，行人很快就回，化退神则来了又返回或又去别处（见黄金策·行人）；这里相反：化进神不回，化退神必回。两书对进退神的读法正好相反，没有哪一本交代理由。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The Huangjin Ce says that a Subject changing into an Advancing spirit means the traveller hurries home, and changing into a Retreating spirit means he comes and then goes back or goes elsewhere (see the Huangjin Ce on travellers); this chapter says the opposite, that an Advancing change does not return and a Retreating change surely returns. The two books read Advancing and Retreating in opposite ways, and neither gives its reason.
