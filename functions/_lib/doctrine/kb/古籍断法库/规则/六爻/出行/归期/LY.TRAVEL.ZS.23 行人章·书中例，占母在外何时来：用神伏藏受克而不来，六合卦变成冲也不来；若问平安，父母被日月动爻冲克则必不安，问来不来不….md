# 行人章·书中例，占母在外何时来：用神伏藏受克而不来，六合卦变成冲也不来；若问平安，父母被日月动爻冲克则必不安，问来不来不能这样断

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问行人在外平安与否，要看用神有没有病；问回来的日期，只看卦象断来不来。用神被冲被克、卦变克绝的，难盼其回。书中例：酉月戊申日占母亲在外面什么时候来，得旅卦变艮卦：这一卦如果问母亲平安与否，父母卯木被日月和动爻冲克，必定不平安；现在问的是来不来，不这样断，只能断用神伏藏又受克所以不来，六合卦变成六冲也不来，后来果然没有来，她在外面平安。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking whether a traveller is well abroad, look at whether the Subject is ill; in asking the day of return, judge from the hexagram alone whether he comes or not. A Subject that is clashed or controlled, or a hexagram that changes into control or extinction, gives little hope of return. Worked example: in the You month, on a Wushen day, a man divined when his mother abroad would come and got Lü (the Traveller) changing to Gen. Had the question been her safety, the Resource Mao-wood, clashed and controlled by the Month, the Day and the moving lines, would surely have meant she was unwell; but the question was whether she would come, so it is not judged that way: the Subject is hidden and controlled, so she does not come, and a six-combine hexagram changing into a clash also does not come; in the end she did not come, and she was safe abroad.
