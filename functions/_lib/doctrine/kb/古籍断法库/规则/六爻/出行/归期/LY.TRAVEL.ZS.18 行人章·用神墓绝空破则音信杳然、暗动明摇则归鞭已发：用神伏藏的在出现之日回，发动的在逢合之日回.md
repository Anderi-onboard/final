# 行人章·用神墓绝空破则音信杳然、暗动明摇则归鞭已发：用神伏藏的在出现之日回，发动的在逢合之日回

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神入墓、绝、空、破，回来的消息杳无音信；用神暗动（安静而日辰冲动它）或明动（自己发动），就是出发回家了。用神伏藏的，在它出现的日子回来；用神发动的，在逢合的日子回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject is in the tomb, extinct, Void or broken, no word of a return comes; when it is stirred in secret (still but moved by the clashing day) or moves in the open, the whip is already raised for home. A hidden Subject returns on the day it appears; a moving one returns on the day it meets a combination.

## 《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11998 行 · Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 墓绝空破，归信杳然； | 用神入墓、绝、空、破，回来的消息杳无音信； | When the Subject is in the tomb, extinct, Void or broken, no word of a return comes; |
> | 暗动明摇，归鞭发矣。 | 用神暗动、明动，就是出发回家了。 | when the Subject is stirred in secret or moves in the open, the whip is already raised for home. |
> | 伏藏者，出现之日； | 伏藏的，在它出现的日子回来； | A hidden one returns on the day it appears; |
> | 动者，逢合之日； | 发动的，在逢合的日子回来。 | a moving one returns on the day it meets a combination. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「暗动」是用神安静、被日辰冲起来；「明摇」是用神自己发动。这里把二者并说，都当作要动身的信号。
>
> *Note:* "Stirred in secret" means the Subject is still and moved by the clashing day; "moving in the open" means the Subject moves on its own. Both are taken here as signs that he is about to set out.
