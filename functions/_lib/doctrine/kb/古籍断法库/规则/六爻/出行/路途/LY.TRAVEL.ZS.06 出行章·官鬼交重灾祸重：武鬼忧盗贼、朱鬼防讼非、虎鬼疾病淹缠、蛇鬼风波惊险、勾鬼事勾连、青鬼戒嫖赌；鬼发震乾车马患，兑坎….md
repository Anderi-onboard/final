# 出行章·官鬼交重灾祸重：武鬼忧盗贼、朱鬼防讼非、虎鬼疾病淹缠、蛇鬼风波惊险、勾鬼事勾连、青鬼戒嫖赌；鬼发震乾车马患，兑坎虑风波，坤艮山岭逢殃，离巽炎厂林窖被难

六爻 · 出行 · 路途 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动重叠，灾祸就重：带玄武动，担忧盗贼；带朱雀发动，提防官司口舌；带白虎，疾病缠身；带螣蛇，风波惊险；带勾陈发动，事情牵连纠缠；带青龙发动，要戒嫖赌。鬼爻发在震、乾，有车马之患；发在兑、坎，要虑风波；坤、艮的鬼爻，在山间岭野遇灾殃；离、巽的官爻，在炉场、树林、窑窖里遭难。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure moving in force means heavy calamity: with the Dark Tortoise moving, worry about thieves; with the Vermilion Bird, guard against lawsuits and quarrels; with the White Tiger, illness that lingers; with the Coiling Snake, storms and frightening danger; with Hook Earth moving, entanglement in others' affairs; with the Azure Dragon moving, beware of whoring and gambling. Pressure moving in Zhen or Qian brings the danger of carts and horses, moving in Dui or Kan, think of storms; Pressure in Kun or Gen means calamity on mountain and ridge; Pressure in Li or Xun means disaster in forge-yards, woods and kilns.

> [!note] 按
> 「趋避章」是增删卜易讲怎样趋吉避凶的一章，里面讲避难、避贼、避大罪的几节在本库里分别收进「出行」「失物」「官非」。「炎厂」按文意是炉火作坊一类的场所。
>
> *Note:* The "chapter on avoiding" is the Zengshan Buyi's chapter on how to seek the lucky and avoid the ill, whose sections on escaping disorder, avoiding thieves and avoiding a heavy sentence are taken into this library under Travel, Lost goods and Lawsuits respectively. The "flame yard" is by the sense a place of furnaces and workshops.
