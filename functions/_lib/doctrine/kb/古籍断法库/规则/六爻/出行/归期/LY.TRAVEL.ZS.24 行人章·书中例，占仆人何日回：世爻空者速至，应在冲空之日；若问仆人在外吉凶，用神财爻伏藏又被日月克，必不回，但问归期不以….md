# 行人章·书中例，占仆人何日回：世爻空者速至，应在冲空之日；若问仆人在外吉凶，用神财爻伏藏又被日月克，必不回，但问归期不以此断

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻空的，行人马上就到，应在冲开空亡的日子。书中例：亥月甲子日占仆人哪天回来，得革卦变夬卦：如果问仆人在外面吉凶，午火财爻伏藏又被日月克，一定不回来；现在问的是回来的日期，不这样断，断为世爻空，速到，这个人来了，己巳日必到；应在巳日，是冲开空亡的日子，巳火又正是财爻，果然在巳日到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Self is Void the traveller arrives at once, on the day that clashes the Void open. Worked example: in the Hai month, on a Jiazi day, a man divined on what day his servant would return and got Ge changing to Guai. Had the question been the servant's fortune abroad, the Wealth Wu-fire is hidden and controlled by the Month and the Day, so he would surely not return; but the question was the day of return, so it was not judged so: the Self is Void, so he comes quickly and will surely arrive on the Jisi day, which is the day that clashes the Void open, and Si-fire is also Wealth; he did arrive on the Si day.
