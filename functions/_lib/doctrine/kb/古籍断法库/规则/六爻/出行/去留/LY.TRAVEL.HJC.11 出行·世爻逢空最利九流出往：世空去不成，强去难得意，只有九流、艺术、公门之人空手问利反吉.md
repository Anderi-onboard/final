# 出行·世爻逢空最利九流出往：世空去不成，强去难得意，只有九流、艺术、公门之人空手问利反吉

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻逢空，出行去不成，勉强去了也终究难以得意，白白奔波；只有九流、技艺和公门里的人，空着手去求利，反而吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self meets Void the journey cannot be made, and going by force one still finds it hard to succeed and runs about in vain; only for people of the nine trades, arts and crafts, and the yamen, going empty-handed to seek profit, is it on the contrary lucky.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3445 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世空去不成，强去终难得意，徒劳奔走。 | 世爻空，去不成；勉强去了也终究难以得意，白白奔波。 | If the Self is Void the journey cannot be made; going by force one still finds it hard to succeed and runs about in vain. |
> | 若九流艺术及公门等人，是空拳问利，反吉。 | 如果是九流、艺术和公门里的人，是空手去求利，反而吉。 | But for people of the nine trades, arts and crafts, and the yamen, it is going empty-handed to seek profit, and so is lucky. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「也右」两字在这个本子里是「世爻」的误写（注文接着说「世空去不成」），译文按「世爻」翻。「九流」本指儒、道、阴阳、法、名、墨、纵横、杂、农九家，旧时也泛指各行各业靠本事、手艺谋生的人；这是通行的说法，不是本书的定义。
>
> *Note:* The two characters "also right" in this edition are a miswriting of "the Self line" (the note goes on "if the Self is Void the journey cannot be made"), and the translation follows that. The "nine trades" originally meant the nine schools (Confucian, Daoist, yin-yang, legalist, logician, Mohist, diplomatic, eclectic, agrarian) and in old usage also loosely all those who live by skill or craft; this is the usual sense, not a definition given in this book.
