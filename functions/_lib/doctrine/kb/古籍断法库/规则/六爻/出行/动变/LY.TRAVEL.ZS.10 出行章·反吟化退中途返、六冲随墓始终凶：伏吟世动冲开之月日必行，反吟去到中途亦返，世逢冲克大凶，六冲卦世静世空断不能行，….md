# 出行章·反吟化退中途返、六冲随墓始终凶：伏吟世动冲开之月日必行，反吟去到中途亦返，世逢冲克大凶，六冲卦世静世空断不能行，随鬼入墓世休囚是不返之兆

六爻 · 出行 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 反吟、化退神的，中途返回；六冲、随鬼入墓的，始终凶。卦得伏吟，世爻发动的，到冲开的月日一定出行；卦得反吟，走到半路也要返回；世遇冲克，大凶；六冲卦，世爻安静或空，断定不能出行；随鬼入墓，世爻休囚，是不回来的征兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Reversed Chant, or changing into a Retreating spirit, means turning back on the road; six-clash, or entering the tomb with Pressure, is ill from start to finish. If the hexagram is Hidden Chant and the Self moves, one surely sets out in the month or on the day that clashes it open; if it is Reversed Chant, even on getting halfway one turns back; the Self meeting clash and control is greatly ill; in a six-clash hexagram with the Self still or Void it is judged that one cannot go; entering the tomb with Pressure, if the Self is resting or trapped, is the sign of not returning.
