# 行人章·书中例，占伯何日来：父母为用神、克世者速至，断七月必到，实际亥月才到，应在父母化出之爻

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神克世爻，他一定回来。书中例：未月戊戌日占伯父哪天来，得屯卦变随卦：父母为用神，克世爻的，到得快，断为七月必到；后来在亥月才到，应在亥月，是因为父母化出来的爻在亥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject controls the Self he will surely return. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Wuxu day, a man divined on what day his uncle would come and got Zhun changing to Sui: the Resource is the Subject and controls the Self, which means a quick arrival, and it was judged that he would surely arrive in the seventh month; he came only in the Hai month, which answered because the line the Resource changed into is in Hai.
