# 归魂游魂章·游魂卦占出行行止无定、占行人游遍他乡，归魂卦不出疆、诸事拘泥不行：野鹤——要先以用神为主，舍用神而执此断是错的

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 游魂卦是各宫的第七卦，归魂卦是各宫的第八卦。古人认为游魂可以走千里，我做这件事想要久的，游魂却不能久，心里没有定向，迁改不常：占身命，一生没有安家乐业的地方；占行人，游遍他乡；占出行，行止没有定准；占家宅，搬迁变动不常；占坟茔，亡灵不能安妥。野鹤说：必须以用神为主，然后拿这个来参考；如果舍掉用神、执着这个来断，就错了。归魂卦不出疆界，诸事拘泥不行，与游魂卦相反来断就可以。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Wandering Soul hexagram is the seventh hexagram of each palace and a Returning Soul hexagram the eighth. The ancients held that a Wandering Soul can travel a thousand li, but if I am undertaking something that I want to last, the Wandering Soul cannot last; the heart has no settled direction and changes are frequent. In divining for one's life, there is no place to settle and thrive all one's life; for a traveller, he roams other lands; for setting out, going and stopping are unfixed; for the house, moves and changes are frequent; for a tomb, the spirits of the dead cannot be at rest. Yehe says: take the Subject as the main thing first and then use this as a reference; to set the Subject aside and judge by this alone is a mistake. A Returning Soul hexagram does not leave the border and things are stuck and do not go; judge it as the opposite of a Wandering Soul hexagram.
