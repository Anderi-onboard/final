# 十八问答·第二问·书中例，占仆何日回：亥水财爻为用神，不谓月破，却怕重重土动伤它，「双拳不敌四手」，不仅难盼归期，还防途中不测，后来午月火旺合未助土，中途遇害

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 回头克的道理是：凡遇回头克，彻底克尽。书中例：巳月丁亥日有人占仆人哪天回来，得夬卦变履卦：亥水财爻是用神，亥水虽然是月建，不叫月破，但是不宜重重土爻发动来伤它。谚语说「双拳不敌四手」，不但难盼他回来，还要提防途中不测；后来到午月火旺、合未助土的时候，仆人在中途遇害了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On the turning-back control: whatever meets a turning-back control is controlled through and through. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Dinghai day, a man divined on what day his servant would return and got Guai changing to Lü (Treading): the Wealth Hai-water is the Subject; though Hai-water is the Month it is not called Month-break, but it must not be injured by crowded movement of Earth lines. As the saying goes, two fists are no match for four hands; there is not only little hope for his return but beware of mishap on the road. Later, in the Wu month, when Fire was strong, combined Wei and helped Earth, the servant was killed on the way.

> [!note] 按
> 这一例收在第二问「回头克」下面，但判词用的是「重重土动伤财」，没有提回头克；引的那句「回头克」的总说是这一问的开头，本库照原位置放。
>
> *Note:* The example is placed under the second question on the turning-back control, but its judgement rests on crowded Earth lines injuring Wealth and does not mention a turning-back control; the general statement quoted is the opening of that question, kept here in its original place.
