# 诸爻持世诀·子孙持世避乱许安：子孙持世事无忧，占避乱可以安稳

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙持世，事事无忧；占避乱，许他可以平安。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Output holds the Self, there is nothing to worry about; in divining for escaping disorder, safety is promised.

## 《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·三十三、诸爻持世诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 198 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 33: the verse on lines holding the Self", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 避乱许安失可得， | 占避乱，许他可以平安， | in divining for escaping disorder, safety is promised, |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一联的上一句是「子孙持世事无忧」，避乱一句只是这一联里和出行相关的一小句。
>
> *Note:* The preceding line of the couplet is "Output holding the Self means nothing to worry about"; the line on escaping disorder is only the small part of the couplet that bears on this domain.
