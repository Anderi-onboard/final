# 十八问答·第十五问·书中例，占自去寻父回：寅木独发克父爻持世，自身不能动、父也见不到，要等冲克寅木之年月；再占得革之既济，世化申金回头生，果于申年八月寻父回家

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 独静独发只能看事情成败的快慢，至于吉凶要推用神，舍掉用神去决断的，迂腐而且错误。书中例：午月丙午日占亲自去寻找父亲能不能回来，得大有卦变离卦：一位懂易的朋友拿着这一卦问我，寅木一爻独发，正月能见到吗？我说不是的，卦中父爻持世，被寅木克制，自己不能动，父亲也见不到，想让自己动身见到父亲，必须等到冲克寅木的年月；让他再占一卦合起来决断，得革卦变既济卦：这一卦正合前一卦，前一卦应在冲开寅木的申，这一卦世爻化出申金回头生，也应在申；果然在申年八月寻父回了家，应在申年，是前卦冲去忌神、后卦化出申金父母用神生世的缘故。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Sole still and sole moving only show how fast or slow the matter succeeds or fails; luck and ill must be reckoned from the Subject, and to decide while setting the Subject aside is pedantic and wrong. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Bingwu day, a man divined about going himself to seek his father and bringing him back, and got Dayou changing to Li: a friend who knew the Changes held up this hexagram and asked me whether Yin-wood moving alone meant he would see his father in the first month. I said no; the Resource line holds the Self and is controlled by Yin-wood, so he cannot move himself and cannot see his father; to set out and see his father, he must wait for a year or month that clashes or controls Yin-wood. I told him to divine again to settle it together, and he got Ge changing to Jiji: this hexagram agrees exactly with the former; the former answered at Shen, which clashes Yin open, and this one has the Self changing into Shen-metal that turns back to give life, which also answers at Shen. Indeed in the eighth month of a Shen year he went to seek his father and came home with him. It answered in the Shen year because the former hexagram clashed away the Adversary and the latter changed into Shen-metal, the Resource and Subject, to give life to the Self.
