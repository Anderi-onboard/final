# 出行·鬼地墓乡岂宜践履、财方父向动可登临：官鬼所在方位和它的墓方、世的墓方、克世的方位是凶方不可去，宜去财福所在的方位

六爻 · 出行 · 方位 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 自己占出行，方位都从世爻所在的位置说：官鬼所在的方位，鬼爻的墓方、世爻的墓方，以及克世的方位，都是凶方，不可去踏足；应当去财爻、福神所在的方位，大吉利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In one's own divination for a journey the quarters are all spoken of from the position of the Self: the quarter of Pressure, the quarter of Pressure's tomb, the quarter of the Self's tomb and the quarter that controls the Self are ill quarters where one must not tread; it is fit to go to the quarter of Wealth and Output, which is greatly auspicious.

## 《黄金策·出行》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3449 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼地墓乡岂宜践履， | 官鬼所在的地方和它的墓乡，怎么适宜去踩踏？ | How is it fit to tread on the ground of Pressure and the home of its tomb? |
> | 财方父向动可登临 | 财爻所在的方向、父母所向的方位，可以动身去登临 | The direction of Wealth and the quarter faced by Resource: one may set out and climb there |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3449 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼地木乡，财方父向，如自占卜，皆以世位而言。 | 鬼地、墓乡（原文作「木乡」）、财方、父向，如果是自己占，都是就世爻所在的位置说的。 | The ground of Pressure, the tomb quarter (the text has "Wood quarter"), the quarter of Wealth and the facing of Resource are all, in one's own divination, spoken of from the position of the Self. |
> | 官鬼之方及鬼之墓方、世之墓方并克世之方，此等凶方不可践履； | 官鬼所在的方位，以及鬼的墓方、世的墓方，还有克世的方位，这些凶方不可去踏足； | The quarter of Pressure, the quarter of Pressure's tomb, the quarter of the Self's tomb and the quarter that controls the Self are ill quarters where one must not tread; |
> | 宜往财福之方，为大吉利也。 | 宜去财爻、福神所在的方位，最吉利。 | it is fit to go to the quarter of Wealth and Output, which is greatly auspicious. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 注文第一句里「木乡」应是「墓乡」（正文是「鬼地墓乡」），译文按「墓乡」翻。这里的方位是按八卦、五行、地支定的方向，书里没有再列方位表。
>
> *Note:* In the first sentence of the note "Wood quarter" should be "tomb quarter" (the couplet reads "the ground of Pressure and the home of its tomb"), and the translation follows that. The quarters are those fixed by trigram, element and branch, and the book gives no table of them here.
