# 通玄赋·出行宜世动、归魂不出疆，用神发动值三合则行人立回：出行要世爻动；归魂卦不出边疆；用爻发动逢三合，行人马上回来

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出行适宜世爻发动；归魂卦不出疆界；用爻发动又遇三合，行人立刻回到家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Setting out is fit when the Self moves; a Returning Soul hexagram does not leave the border; when the Subject moves and meets a three-harmony, the traveller is back home at once.

## 《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·三十一、通玄赋》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 187 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 31: the Tongxuan rhapsody", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 出行宜世动， | 出行适宜世爻发动， | Setting out is fit when the Self moves, |
> | 归魂不出疆； | 归魂卦不出疆界； | a Returning Soul hexagram does not leave the border; |
> | 用动值三合， | 用爻发动又遇三合， | when the Subject moves and meets a three-harmony, |
> | 行人立回庄。 | 行人立刻回到家。 | the traveller is back home at once. |

^q1

> [!warning] 打架
> 《增删卜易》三合章说：占出行，用神在三合之内，被合而留；占行人，用神在三合之内，被合不返。这里却说用爻发动值三合，行人立回。两处都有「三合」，但一说被合住不回、一说立回，没有一本说明区别。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The chapter on three-harmony in the Zengshan Buyi says that in setting out a Subject inside a three-harmony is combined shut and stays, and for a traveller it is combined shut and does not return; this verse says that a Subject that moves and meets a three-harmony means the traveller returns at once. Both name three-harmony, one saying the traveller is held and the other that he is back, and neither book explains the difference.
