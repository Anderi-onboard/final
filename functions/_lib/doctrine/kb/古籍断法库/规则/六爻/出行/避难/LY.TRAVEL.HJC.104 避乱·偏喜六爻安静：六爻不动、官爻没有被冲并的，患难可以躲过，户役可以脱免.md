# 避乱·偏喜六爻安静：六爻不动、官爻没有被冲并的，患难可以躲过，户役可以脱免

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻不动，官爻没有被冲、并的，患难可以躲过，户役可以脱免。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the six lines do not move and the Pressure line is neither clashed nor joined, trouble can be avoided and household duties escaped.

> [!note] 按
> 原书这一行也把两联写在一起（「偏喜六爻安静……」「又宜一卦无官……」），这里按联拆成两条。
>
> *Note:* The book also writes two couplets together on this line ("it is especially welcome that the six lines are still ..." and "it is also fitting that the hexagram has no Pressure ..."); they are split here into two entries.
