# 行人·克速生迟、我若制他难见面：用爻克世或世落空亡人很快到，生合世爻回得迟，最忌世爻动克用爻

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻发动克世爻，或者世爻落空亡，人一定很快就到；用爻生合世爻，人回得迟；最忌世爻发动去克用爻，那是回不来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject moves and controls the Self, or the Self falls into Void, the man will surely arrive soon; if the Subject gives life to or combines with the Self, he returns late; most to be feared is the Self moving to control the Subject line, which means he cannot come back.

## 《黄金策·行人》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3485 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Absent travellers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 克速生迟， | （用爻）克世则快，生世则迟， | Controlling means quick, generating means late; |
> | 我若制他难见面 | 我如果制住他，就难以见面 | if I control him, it is hard to meet him |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3485 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用动克世或世落空亡，人必速至； | 用爻发动克世，或者世爻落空亡，人一定很快就到； | If the Subject moves and controls the Self, or the Self falls into Void, the man will surely arrive soon; |
> | 生合世爻人必归迟； | 生合世爻，人一定回得迟； | if it gives life to or combines with the Self, the man will return late; |
> | 最忌世爻动克用爻，乃未能归也。 | 最忌世爻发动克用爻，那是不能回来。 | most to be feared is the Self moving to control the Subject line, which means he cannot come back. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一联和十八问答「第一问」前后的行人卦例是配套的；增删卜易行人章开头也说「世克用兮人未动，用爻克世必然归」，是同一个意思（见 LY.TRAVEL 增删卜易行人章）。
>
> *Note:* This couplet goes with the travellers' cases around the first of the eighteen questions; the Zengshan Buyi's chapter on absent travellers opens with "when the Self controls the Subject the man has not moved, when the Subject controls the Self he surely comes home", which says the same thing (see its chapter on absent travellers in LY.TRAVEL).
