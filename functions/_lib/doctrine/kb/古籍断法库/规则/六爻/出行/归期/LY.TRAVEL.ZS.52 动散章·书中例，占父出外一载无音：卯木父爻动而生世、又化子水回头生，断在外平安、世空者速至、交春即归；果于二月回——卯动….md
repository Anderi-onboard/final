# 动散章·书中例，占父出外一载无音：卯木父爻动而生世、又化子水回头生，断在外平安、世空者速至、交春即归；果于二月回——卯动被酉日冲也没有冲散

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 古人以日辰冲动爻叫做冲散，又以动爻冲爻也能冲散。作者屡次试验，旺相的、有气的，冲它不散；只有休囚的间或冲散，也是千百中的一二。原因是神灵的兆示机缘在动，动必有因，即使今天受制，后来逢到值日就不散。书中例：丑月丁酉日占父亲出外一年没有音信，得涣卦变坎卦：卯木父爻发动生世，又化出子水回头生，答应他在外平安，世爻空的行人速到，交春就回；果然二月，称心而回——这不是卯木动、被酉日冲吗，哪里散了？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ancients called a moving line clashed by the Day "scattered", and also said a moving line clashing another line can scatter it. The author has tried this repeatedly: a strong line or one with breath is not scattered by a clash; only a resting or trapped one is now and then scattered, and that is one or two in a thousand. Why? Because the spirits give their sign in the moving, and every movement has a cause: even if the line is checked today, when the ruling day comes it is not scattered. Worked example: in the Chou month, on a Dingyou day, a man divined about his father who had gone abroad and sent no word for a year, and got Huan changing to Kan: the Resource Mao-wood moves and gives life to the Self, and changes into Zi-water that turns back to give life, so he was promised that his father was safe abroad, that a Void Self means a quick arrival, and that he would return at the turn of spring. Indeed in the second month he came back content. Is this not Mao moving and being clashed by the You day? Where was the scattering?
