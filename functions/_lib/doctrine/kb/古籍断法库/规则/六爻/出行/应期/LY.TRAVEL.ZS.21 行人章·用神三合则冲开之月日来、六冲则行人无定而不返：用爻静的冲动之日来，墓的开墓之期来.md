# 行人章·用神三合则冲开之月日来、六冲则行人无定而不返：用爻静的冲动之日来，墓的开墓之期来

六爻 · 出行 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神三合，在冲开三合的月日回来；卦遇六冲，行人去向不定，不会回来。用爻安静的，在冲动它的日子回来；用爻入墓的，在开墓的时候回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject is in a three-harmony, he returns in a month or day that clashes the harmony open; when the hexagram meets six-clash, the traveller's course is unsettled and he does not return. A still Subject returns on the day that clashes it into motion; a Subject in the tomb returns at the time the tomb is opened.
