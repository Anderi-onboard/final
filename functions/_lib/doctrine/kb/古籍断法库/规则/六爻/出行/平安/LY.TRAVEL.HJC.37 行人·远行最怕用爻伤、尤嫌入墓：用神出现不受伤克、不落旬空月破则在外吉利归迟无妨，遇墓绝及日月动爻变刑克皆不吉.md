# 行人·远行最怕用爻伤、尤嫌入墓：用神出现不受伤克、不落旬空月破则在外吉利归迟无妨，遇墓绝及日月动爻变刑克皆不吉

六爻 · 出行 · 平安 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占远行，用神出现、不受伤克、不落旬空月破，主他在外吉利，回来迟也无妨；如果遇到墓、绝，或者日月、动爻、变爻的刑克，都主不吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a distant journey, if the Subject appears, is not injured or controlled, and is not in the Void of its ten-day period or under a Month-break, he does well abroad and there is no harm if his return is late; if it meets the tomb or extinction, or the punishment or control of the Day, the Month, a moving line or a changed line, all are inauspicious.
