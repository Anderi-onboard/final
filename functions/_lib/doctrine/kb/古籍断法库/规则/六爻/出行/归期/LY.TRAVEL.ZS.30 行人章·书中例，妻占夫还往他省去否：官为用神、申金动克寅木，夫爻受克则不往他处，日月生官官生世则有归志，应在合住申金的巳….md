# 行人章·书中例，妻占夫还往他省去否：官为用神、申金动克寅木，夫爻受克则不往他处，日月生官官生世则有归志，应在合住申金的巳日；或曰用爻有病莫问归期，答：问平安则断不利，问去不去断法不同

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻有病，在外不安；问归期，只看卦象断来不来。书中例：亥月甲子日妻子占丈夫在外还会不会去别省，得大壮卦变大过卦：妻子占丈夫，官鬼为用神，申金发动克它，寅木夫爻受克，不会往别处去了，眼下一定回来；日月生官鬼，官鬼生世爻，必有归意；巳日必到，应在巳日，是合住申金的日子，果然在巳日到。有人说用爻有病就不要问归期，这一卦申金化进神克寅木，怎么还说回来？答：他若问平安就断不利，现在问的是往不往别处去，夫爻受克就不往别处去，断卦的人各有各的取用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line is ill, he is uneasy abroad; in asking the day of return, look only at the hexagram to judge whether he comes. Worked example: in the Hai month, on a Jiazi day, a wife divined whether her husband abroad would go on to another province and got Dazhuang changing to Daguo: a wife divining for her husband takes Pressure as the Subject; Shen-metal moves and controls it, so the husband's Yin-wood is controlled and he will not go elsewhere, and he will surely come back soon; the Month and the Day give life to Pressure and Pressure gives life to the Self, so he must have a mind to return; he will surely arrive on the Si day, which answers because it is the day that combines Shen-metal shut, and he arrived on the Si day. Someone objected: if the Subject line is ill, do not ask the day of return, and here Shen-metal changes into an Advancing spirit to control Yin-wood, so how can you allow his return? I said: had they asked about his safety I would have judged it unfavourable, but now they ask whether he goes elsewhere, and when the husband's line is controlled he does not go elsewhere; those who judge hexagrams each take what they need.
