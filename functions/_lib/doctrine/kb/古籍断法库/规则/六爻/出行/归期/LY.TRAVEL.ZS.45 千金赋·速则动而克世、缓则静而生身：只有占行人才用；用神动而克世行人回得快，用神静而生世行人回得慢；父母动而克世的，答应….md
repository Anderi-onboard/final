# 千金赋·速则动而克世、缓则静而生身：只有占行人才用；用神动而克世行人回得快，用神静而生世行人回得慢；父母动而克世的，答应他立刻就回

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神发动来克世爻，回得快；用神安静而生世爻，回得慢。这一条只有占行人才用，别的占不用。用神动而克世的，行人回得快；用神静而生世的，行人回得慢。比如占父母什么时候回，以父母爻为用神，父母爻动而克世的，许他立刻就回；其余照此推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject that moves and controls the Self returns quickly; a Subject that is still and gives life to the Self returns slowly. This is used only in divining for a traveller and not in other divinations. A Subject that moves and controls the Self means a quick return; one that is still and gives life to the Self means a slow return. For example, in divining when a parent will return, the Resource line is the Subject, and if the Resource moves and controls the Self he is promised to return at once; the rest follow this.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 速则动而克世，缓则静而生身。 | 快的是发动而克世爻，慢的是安静而生身。 | The quick is that which moves and controls the Self, the slow that which is still and gives life to the Body. |
