# 六亲发动诀·行人句：父动则行人书信动，子孙发动行人买卖身康泰，官鬼克兄弟出外逃亡定见灾，财爻发动行人在外身将动，兄弟交重克财若带吉神有助则出路行人便未来

六爻 · 出行 · 平安 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六亲发动的诀里，和行人、出外有关的几句：父母爻发动，盼望的行人有书信的动静；子孙发动，行人、做买卖的人身体康泰；官鬼克兄弟，出外或逃亡的人必定见灾；财爻发动，在外的行人将要动身；兄弟交重去克财，如果带着吉神有助，出路上的行人还没有来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The lines of the verse on the six relations that bear on travellers and going out: when the Resource line moves, the letters of the traveller one is watching for begin to stir; when Output moves, travellers and traders are well and at ease; when Pressure controls the Peer, those who go out or flee will surely meet disaster; when the Wealth line moves, the traveller abroad is about to set out; when the Peer lines are crowded with movement and control Wealth, if they carry an auspicious spirit and are helped, the traveller on the road has not yet come.

> [!note] 按
> 这一首诗每一联先说哪个六亲发动、再说各种占的结果；这里只取其中说行人和出外的那一句，前面的「父动」「子孙发动」等是同一联的开头，没有拆进引文里。
>
> *Note:* In this verse each couplet begins by naming the relation that moves and then lists the results for various divinations; only the line on travellers and going out is taken here, and the opening phrases naming the moving relation (the Resource moves, Output moves and so on) belong to the same couplet and are not split into the quotations.
