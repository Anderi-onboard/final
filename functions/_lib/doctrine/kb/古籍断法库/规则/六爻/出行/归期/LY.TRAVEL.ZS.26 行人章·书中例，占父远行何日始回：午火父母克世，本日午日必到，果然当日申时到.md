# 行人章·书中例，占父远行何日始回：午火父母克世，本日午日必到，果然当日申时到

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神克世爻，一定回来。书中例：丑月庚午日占父亲远行哪天才回来，得履卦：今天正是午日，午火父母克世爻，今天一定到；果然当天申时到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject controls the Self he will surely return. Worked example: in the Chou month, on a Gengwu day, a man divined on what day his father, gone on a long journey, would first come back and got Lü (Treading): today is a Wu day, and the Resource Wu-fire controls the Self, so he will surely arrive today; he did arrive that day, in the Shen hour.
