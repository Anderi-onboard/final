# 行人章·问归期有远有近：近则应日、远则应月，去久而眼下当归的只用日断；问平安与否要另占一卦，不可一卦兼断

六爻 · 出行 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问行人什么时候回来，有远有近：近的应在日，远的应在月；也有离开很久、眼下就该回来的，只用日来断。问行人平安与否，要另外占一卦，不可以一卦兼断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking when a traveller will return, some returns are far and some near: a near one falls on a day, a far one on a month; there are also those who have long been away and are due back very soon, and these too are judged by the day alone. In asking whether the traveller is faring well or ill, divine separately; do not judge both from a single hexagram.
