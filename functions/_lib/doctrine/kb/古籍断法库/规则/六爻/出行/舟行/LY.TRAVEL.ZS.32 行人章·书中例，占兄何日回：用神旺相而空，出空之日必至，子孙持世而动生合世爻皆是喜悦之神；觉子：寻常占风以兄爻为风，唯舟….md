# 行人章·书中例，占兄何日回：用神旺相而空，出空之日必至，子孙持世而动生合世爻皆是喜悦之神；觉子：寻常占风以兄爻为风，唯舟行占风以子孙为风，子孙发动之日就是顺风之日

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 发动而空、旺而空的，在填实或冲开空亡的月日回来。书中例：酉月癸酉日占兄长哪天回，得师卦变临卦：亥水兄爻旺相而空，出空的日子必到；来问的人说，后天就是卯日，前面有信约好今天起身，可是两千多里，三天怎么能到？作者说：不只是亥日出空，凡是卦中子孙持世、以及发动而生合世爻的，都是喜悦之神；答应亥日到的，是因为寅与亥相合，又是合起子孙的日子，亥日必归无疑；哪知道走水路遇到顺风，日夜兼程，竟然在亥日到了。觉子说：一般占风，兄爻发动为风，木发动生风；只有行船占风，以子孙为风——行船风逆我就忧愁，子孙发动的日子就得顺风，风顺我就无忧了，这也是没有传下来的秘诀。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Void line that moves, or one that is strong yet Void, returns in a month or day that fills it or clashes the Void open. Worked example: in the You month, on a Guiyou day, a man divined on what day his elder brother would return and got Shi changing to Lin: the Subject, the Hai-water Peer line, is strong yet Void, and on the day it comes out of the Void he will surely arrive. The questioner said: the day after tomorrow is a Mao day, and a letter before had said he would set out today, but it is over two thousand li; how can he arrive in three days? I said: it is not only that Hai comes out of the Void; whenever Output holds the Self in a hexagram, or moves and gives life to or combines with the Self, that is a spirit of joy; I promised the Hai day because Yin combines with Hai, which is also the day that combines Output awake, so he would surely return on the Hai day. Who would have thought that he came by water, met a fair wind, and travelled day and night, arriving on the Hai day after all. Juezi says: in ordinary divination of wind, a moving Peer line is wind, and moving Wood gives rise to wind; but for a boat in motion, Output is the wind. When the wind is foul for a boat, I am worried; on the day Output moves one gets a fair wind, and with a fair wind I have no worry. This too is a secret that was never handed down.

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「寅与亥合」指地支六合，寅亥合木；这里的意思是卦里有子孙在寅，寅日出现、亥日合起它，所以亥日既是出空之日，又是合起子孙（顺风之神）的日子，走水路得顺风就能三天赶两千里。
>
> *Note:* The "combination of Yin and Hai" is the earthly-branch six combination, Yin and Hai together making Wood. The point made is that the Hai day is both the day the Void line comes out of the Void and the day that combines the Output (the fair-wind spirit), so travelling by water with a fair wind he covers two thousand li in three days.
