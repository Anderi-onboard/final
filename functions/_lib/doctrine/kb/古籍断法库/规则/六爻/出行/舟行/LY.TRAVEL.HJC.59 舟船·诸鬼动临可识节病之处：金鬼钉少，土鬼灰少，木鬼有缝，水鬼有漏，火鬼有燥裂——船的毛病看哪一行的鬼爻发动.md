# 舟船·诸鬼动临可识节病之处：金鬼钉少，土鬼灰少，木鬼有缝，水鬼有漏，火鬼有燥裂——船的毛病看哪一行的鬼爻发动

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 船的毛病看是哪一行的鬼爻发动：金鬼是钉子少，土鬼是灰（油灰）少，木鬼是板缝，水鬼是漏水，火鬼是木头干燥裂开。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The boat's faults are read from the element of the moving Pressure line: Metal Pressure means too few nails, Earth Pressure too little putty, Wood Pressure seams, Water Pressure leaks, and Fire Pressure dry cracks.

> [!note] 按
> 「节病」是船体各节、各处的毛病；「灰」指填船缝用的油灰。这一句按鬼爻所属五行断船的毛病，和疾病章按鬼爻五行断病位是同一种取法。
>
> *Note:* "Faults" here are the defects in the parts and joints of the hull, and "putty" is the caulking used to fill the seams. Reading a boat's faults by the element of Pressure is the same kind of reading as judging the seat of an illness by the element of Pressure in the chapters on illness.
