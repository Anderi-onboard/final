# 行人章·世克用则人未动、用克世必然归：占亲人看所属用神，疏远的以应爻为用神；世克用且无归志，用克世指日回家（作者说试过果验）

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻克用神，人还没有动身；用神克世爻，一定会回来。占亲人，看所属的用神；关系疏远的，拿应爻当用神。世爻克用神，他还没有回来的心思；用神克世爻，不久就回家。作者说：我试过，果然应验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Self controls the Subject, the man has not yet moved; when the Subject controls the Self, he will surely return. In divining for a kinsman, look at that kinsman's own Subject; for a distant person, take the Other line as the Subject. If the Self controls the Subject, he has no mind to return; if the Subject controls the Self, he will be home within days. The author says: I have tried it, and it was indeed borne out.
