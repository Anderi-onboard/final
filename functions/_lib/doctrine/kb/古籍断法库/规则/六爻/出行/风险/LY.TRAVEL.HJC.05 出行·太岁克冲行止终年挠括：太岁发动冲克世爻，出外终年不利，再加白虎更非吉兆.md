# 出行·太岁克冲行止终年挠括：太岁发动冲克世爻，出外终年不利，再加白虎更非吉兆

六爻 · 出行 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁发动，冲克世爻，这个人出外一年到头不顺利；再加上白虎这样的凶煞，更不是吉兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Tai Sui moves and clashes with or controls the Self, the man abroad has ill fortune the whole year; with the White Tiger, a baleful star, added, it is the more inauspicious.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「挠括」是受阻碍、被束缚的意思。《增删卜易》的千金赋一节讲太岁时说，太岁只在占官事、年运、乡会试、仕宦等几类事里才要紧，出行不在其内（LY.EXAM.ZS.59）；这里却把太岁克冲世爻当作出外终年不利的信号。
>
> *Note:* "Hampered" translates a word meaning being obstructed and bound. The Zengshan Buyi, in its section on the Thousand-Gold Rhapsody, says the Tai Sui matters only in a few kinds of divination — official affairs, the fortune of the year, the examinations, taking office — and travel is not among them (LY.EXAM.ZS.59); here the Tai Sui clashing with or controlling the Self is taken as a sign of ill fortune abroad all year.
