# 出行·兄乘虎煞交重风波险阻：兄弟加白虎或忌神发动，或鬼在巽宫发动克世，都有风波险阻

六爻 · 出行 · 路途 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻加上白虎、或忌神发动，或者鬼爻在巽宫发动来克世，都有风波险阻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Peer with the White Tiger, or an Adversary, moves, or if Pressure in the Xun palace moves and controls the Self, there are storms and perilous obstruction.

## 《黄金策·出行》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3453 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Setting out on a journey", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄乘虎煞交重， | 兄弟爻乘着白虎凶煞、发动， | If Peer rides the White Tiger, a baleful star, and moves, |
> | 风波险阻 | 会有风波险阻 | there will be storms and perilous obstruction |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3453 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄加白虎及忌神动，或鬼在巽宫，动来克世，皆有风波险阻。 | 兄弟爻加上白虎以及忌神发动，或者鬼爻在巽宫、发动来克世，都有风波险阻。 | If Peer with the White Tiger, or an Adversary, moves, or if Pressure in the Xun palace moves and controls the Self, there are storms and perilous obstruction. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 巽为风，所以鬼爻在巽宫发动被当作风波的信号。
>
> *Note:* Xun is wind, which is why Pressure moving in the Xun palace is taken as a sign of storm.
