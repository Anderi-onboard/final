# 出行章·兄冲世位花月耗破灾非：财持世而兄爻冲克，有的应在月夕花朝无端浪费，有的应在奸徒小辈暗骗明欺，酿成灾非，破耗百出

六爻 · 出行 · 路途 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻冲克世位，主在花前月下耗费钱财、引来灾非：财爻持世而被兄爻冲克的，有的应在月夜花朝无缘无故地浪费，有的应在奸诈之徒、小人暗骗明欺，以致酿成灾非，破耗百出。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Peer line clashing with or controlling the Self position means money wasted amid flowers and moonlight and trouble brought on: when Wealth holds the Self and a Peer line clashes with or controls it, it sometimes answers in pointless waste on moonlit nights and flowery mornings, and sometimes in treacherous rogues and petty men cheating in secret and deceiving openly, so that trouble is brewed and losses of every kind appear.
