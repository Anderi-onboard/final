# 行人章·书中例，行舟占顺风：午火子孙回头生世，今日午时必得顺风，嫌当日是戌日、午火子孙入墓之日，风必不久，午时扬帆，未时风止

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 行船以子孙为顺风：子孙发动回头生世，当日就得顺风；子孙入墓则风不久。书中例：辰月甲戌日行船占顺风，得升卦变恒卦：午火子孙回头生世爻，今天午时必得顺风，只嫌今天是戌日，是午火子孙入墓的日子，风必定不长久；果然午时子孙当令，顺风扬帆，到未时风就停了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a boat, Output is the fair wind: when Output moves and turns back to give life to the Self, a fair wind comes that day, and if Output enters the tomb the wind does not last. Worked example: in the Chen month, on a Jiaxu day, a man divined for a fair wind on a boat and got Sheng changing to Heng: the Output Wu-fire turns back to give life to the Self, so a fair wind will surely come at the Wu hour today, only that today is a Xu day, the day when Wu-fire Output enters the tomb, so the wind will not last; indeed at the Wu hour Output was in command and the sail was hoisted in a fair wind, and by the Wei hour the wind had dropped.
