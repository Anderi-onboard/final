# 十八问答·第十五问·书中例，占父远出何日回：外卦伏吟在外有忧愁之象，内卦辰土父母化巳火回头之生、世爻午火化卯木助火生之，并无害；第四爻午火独静，五月必归；后三四月父在湖广生理，省城兵乱，五月方归

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 书中例：寅月甲辰日占父亲远出哪天回，得遁卦变归妹卦：外卦伏吟，在外有忧愁之象；他问有没有害，我说内卦辰土父母化出巳火回头之生，世爻午火化出卯木助火生它，并无害处；第四爻午火独静，五月必定回来；后来三、四月父亲在湖广做生意，不料省城兵乱，五月才回。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Jiachen day, a man divined on what day his father, gone on a long journey, would return, and got Dun changing to Guimei: the outer trigram is in Hidden Chant, a sign of worry abroad. He asked whether there was harm, and I said: the Resource Chen-earth of the inner trigram changes into Si-fire, a turning-back life, and the Self Wu-fire changes into Mao-wood which helps the fire and gives it life, so there is no harm; the fourth line Wu-fire is alone still, so he will surely come back in the fifth month. Afterwards, in the third and fourth months the father was trading in Huguang, and unexpectedly the provincial capital fell into mutiny, and he came back only in the fifth month.
