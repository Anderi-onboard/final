# 舟行章·舟行风阻：不拿兄弟爻当风云，要拿子孙爻当顺风；动的逢合、逢值之日，静的逢冲之日，空的逢冲实之日（顺风），都与天时章阴晴同断

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 乘船遇到风阻的，不要拿兄弟爻当风云，而要拿子孙爻当顺风：发动的遇到合、值的日子，安静的遇到冲的日子，空的遇到冲实的日子（顺风），都和天时章里断阴晴的办法相同。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a boat held up by wind, do not take the Peer line as wind and cloud but take Output as the fair wind: it comes on the day when a moving one meets a combination or the ruling day, on the day when a still one meets a clash, and on the day when a Void one meets the clash that fills it, all judged in the same way as fine and cloudy weather in the chapter on the weather.

> [!note] 按
> 天时章（占晴雨风云）不属于「出行」，本库没有收；这里只引它说的办法：动的逢合逢值、静的逢冲、空的逢冲实。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on the weather (fine, rain, wind and cloud) is outside Travel and is not taken into this library; only its method is cited here: a moving line by the combining or ruling day, a still line by the clashing day, a Void line by the day that clashes it solid.
