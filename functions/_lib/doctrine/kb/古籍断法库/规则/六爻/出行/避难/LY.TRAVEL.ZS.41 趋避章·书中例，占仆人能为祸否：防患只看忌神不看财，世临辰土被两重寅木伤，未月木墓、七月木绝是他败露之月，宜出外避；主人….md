# 趋避章·书中例，占仆人能为祸否：防患只看忌神不看财，世临辰土被两重寅木伤，未月木墓、七月木绝是他败露之月，宜出外避；主人往坟庄住半月，两仆果然逃走

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 防患的占卜只看忌神，不看财；克世的神在你这一方就宜出外避。书中例：未月丙子日占仆人能不能为祸，得解卦变震卦：这位主人知道仆人变了心，想处置他，还没有实迹，所以占；这一卦如果把仆人当财就错了，因为他既是防患，只看忌神；现在世爻临辰土，被两重寅木伤克。我说：不止这一个人，还有跟随的人。他问：怎么处置？我说：他确实没有实迹，怎么处置？只是未月是木的墓、七月是木的绝，这两个月是他们败露的月份，你宜出外去避，自然能免祸。他果然照做，到坟庄上住了半个月，家中两个仆人逃走了，一个妇人跟着去，一个没有去；没去的妇人说：那两个人本来想害主人才逃，因为主人出外，不能下手，计谋泄露才逃的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining to guard against harm, look only at the Adversary and not at Wealth; where the controlling spirit presses on one's own side, it is fit to go out and avoid it. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Bingzi day, a man divined whether his servant could do him harm and got Jie changing to Zhen: the master knew the servants had changed their hearts and wished to deal with them but had no proof, so he divined; if one took the servant as Wealth in this hexagram one would be mistaken, since he is guarding against harm and only looks at the Adversary; here the Self sits on Chen-earth and is injured by two Yin-wood lines. I said: it is not only this man, there are followers too. He asked how to deal with them. I said: he has really left no trace, how can you deal with him? But the Wei month is the tomb of Wood and the seventh month is its extinction, and these two months are when they will be exposed; you should go out and avoid them, and you will be free of harm. He did as told, stayed half a month at the tomb farm, and two servants at home ran away, one woman following them and one not; the woman who stayed said: those two had meant to harm the master and ran; because the master went out they could not harm him, and the plot leaked, so they fled.
