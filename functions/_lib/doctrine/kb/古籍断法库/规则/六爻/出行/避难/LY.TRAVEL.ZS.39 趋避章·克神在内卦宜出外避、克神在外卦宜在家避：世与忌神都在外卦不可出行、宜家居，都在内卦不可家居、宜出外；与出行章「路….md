# 趋避章·克神在内卦宜出外避、克神在外卦宜在家避：世与忌神都在外卦不可出行、宜家居，都在内卦不可家居、宜出外；与出行章「路上有官休出外，宅中有鬼莫家居」同意

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 克世的神在内卦，动了来克世爻，宜出外去避；克世的神在外卦，动了来克世爻，宜待在家里避。世爻和忌神都在外卦的，不可出行，宜在家居住来避；世爻和忌神都在内卦的，不可在家居住，宜出外去避。出行章说「路上有官休出外，宅中有鬼莫家居」，是同一个意思。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the controlling spirit is in the inner trigram and moves to control the Self, it is fit to go out and avoid it; if the controlling spirit is in the outer trigram and moves to control the Self, it is fit to stay at home and avoid it. When the Self and the Adversary are both in the outer trigram one must not set out and should stay at home to avoid harm; when both are in the inner trigram one must not stay at home and should go out to avoid harm. The chapter on setting out says "if there is an official on the road, do not go out; if there is Pressure in the house, do not stay at home", which is the same idea.
