# 出行章·八纯乱动在处皆凶、两间齐空独行则吉；六冲卦及动变冲、世爻休囚被克者终不能去，有始无终，去亦诸般不就

六爻 · 出行 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 八纯卦乱动，到处都凶；两个间爻同时空，一个人走才吉。六冲卦，以及发动变成冲的、世爻休囚被克的，终究去不成，有始无终，去了也诸事不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A pure-palace hexagram moving in disorder is ill everywhere; if the two between lines are both Void, travelling alone is lucky. A six-clash hexagram, or one that moves and changes into a clash, or one whose Self is resting or trapped and controlled, in the end cannot go; it begins and does not finish, and even if one goes nothing comes to pass.
