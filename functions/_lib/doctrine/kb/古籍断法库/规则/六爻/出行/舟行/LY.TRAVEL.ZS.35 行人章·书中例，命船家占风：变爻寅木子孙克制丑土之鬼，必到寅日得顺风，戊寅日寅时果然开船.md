# 行人章·书中例，命船家占风：变爻寅木子孙克制丑土之鬼，必到寅日得顺风，戊寅日寅时果然开船

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动变出子孙，是顺风之神：变爻寅木子孙克制丑土官鬼，必到寅日得顺风；果然在戊寅日寅时得到顺风开船。书中例：次日乙亥日叫船家占风，得丰卦变大壮卦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Output moves and changes into Output, it is the fair-wind spirit: the changed line, Yin-wood Output, controls the Chou-earth Pressure, so a fair wind will surely come on the Yin day; indeed on the Wuyin day, in the Yin hour, a fair wind came and the boat set out. Worked example: the next day, a Yihai day, the boatman was told to divine for the wind and got Feng changing to Dazhuang.
