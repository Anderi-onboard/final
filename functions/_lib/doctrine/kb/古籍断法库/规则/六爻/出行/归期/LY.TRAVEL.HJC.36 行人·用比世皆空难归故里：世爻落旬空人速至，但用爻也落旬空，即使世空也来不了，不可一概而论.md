# 行人·用比世皆空难归故里：世爻落旬空人速至，但用爻也落旬空，即使世空也来不了，不可一概而论

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻落旬空，人很快就到；但如果用爻也落旬空，即使世爻空，他也来不了；不能一概而论，所以说「用爻和世爻都空，难回故乡」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self is Void in its ten-day period the man arrives soon; but if the Subject line is also Void in its ten-day period, then even though the Self is Void he cannot come; it cannot be said as a blanket rule, hence "if the Subject and the Self are both Void, it is hard to return to his native place".
