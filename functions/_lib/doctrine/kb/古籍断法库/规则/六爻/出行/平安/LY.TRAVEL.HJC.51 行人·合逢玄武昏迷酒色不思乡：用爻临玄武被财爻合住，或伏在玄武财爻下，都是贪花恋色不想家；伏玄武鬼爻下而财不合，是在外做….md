# 行人·合逢玄武昏迷酒色不思乡：用爻临玄武被财爻合住，或伏在玄武财爻下，都是贪花恋色不想家；伏玄武鬼爻下而财不合，是在外做了贼

六爻 · 出行 · 平安 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻临玄武发动而被财爻合住，或者用爻伏在玄武临的财爻下面，都是贪花恋色、不想家乡，要等冲破合住它的爻才可能回来；如果用爻伏在玄武临的鬼爻下面，而财爻不来合，这个人在外面做了贼，没有回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line stands on the Dark Tortoise and moves and is held by a combination from a Wealth line, or is hidden under a Wealth line on the Dark Tortoise, it means indulging in dissipation and not thinking of home, and he can return only when the combining line is clashed or broken; if the Subject line is hidden under a Pressure line on the Dark Tortoise and the Wealth line does not combine with it, the man has become a thief abroad and has not returned.
