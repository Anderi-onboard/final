# 行人·卦得游魂漂泊他乡无定迹：游魂卦用爻发动行人东奔西走，游魂化游魂行踪不定，游魂化归魂游遍才回

六爻 · 出行 · 平安 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 游魂卦里用爻发动，行人东奔西走，不在一个地方；游魂卦变游魂卦，行踪不定；游魂卦变归魂卦，走遍各处才回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a Wandering Soul hexagram, if the Subject line moves the traveller hurries east and west and is not in one place; a Wandering Soul hexagram changing into a Wandering Soul hexagram means his whereabouts are not fixed; a Wandering Soul hexagram changing into a Returning Soul hexagram means he returns only after wandering everywhere.
