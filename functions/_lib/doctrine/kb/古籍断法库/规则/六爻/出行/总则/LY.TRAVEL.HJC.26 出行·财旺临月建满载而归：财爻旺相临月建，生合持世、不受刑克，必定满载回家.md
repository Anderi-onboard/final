# 出行·财旺临月建满载而归：财爻旺相临月建，生合持世、不受刑克，必定满载回家

六爻 · 出行 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出行如果财爻旺相、临在月建上，生合持世爻，又不受刑克，一定满载而归；只要能趋吉避凶，就不必担心登高涉险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In travelling, if the Wealth line is strong and stands on the Month, gives life to or combines with the Self, and is not punished or controlled, one will surely return home with a full load; if one can seek the lucky and avoid the ill, there is no need to fear climbing high or crossing danger.
