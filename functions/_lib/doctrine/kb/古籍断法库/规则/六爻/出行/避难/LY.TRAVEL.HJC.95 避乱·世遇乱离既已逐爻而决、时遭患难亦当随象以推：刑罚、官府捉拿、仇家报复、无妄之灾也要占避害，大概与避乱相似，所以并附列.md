# 避乱·世遇乱离既已逐爻而决、时遭患难亦当随象以推：刑罚、官府捉拿、仇家报复、无妄之灾也要占避害，大概与避乱相似，所以并附列

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 除了避乱，还有避害的占：有刑罚加身、户役牵累，或官府捉拿、仇家报复，或祸从无辜的事上起、灾殃从意外中生，不躲避终究要被害；这类占大概与避乱相似，所以附在这里，按同样的办法逐爻、随象推断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Besides fleeing disorder there is divination for avoiding harm: when punishment is laid on one, or household duties drag one in, or the authorities come to seize one, or an enemy takes revenge, or calamity springs from nothing one has done and misfortune from the unforeseen, one is harmed in the end if one does not avoid it; such divinations are much like fleeing disorder, so they are appended here and judged by the same method, line by line and following the images.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1625 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 平居无事，何暇占卜？ | 平常安居没事，哪有空占卜？ | Living at ease with nothing amiss, who has time to divine? |
> | 或刑罚所加，户投所累，或官府捉拿，仇家报复，或祸起于无辜，殃生于不测，苟不避之，终为所害，是以不能无避害之占也。 | 或者是刑罚加身、户役牵累，或者官府来捉拿、仇家来报复，或者祸从无辜的事上起、灾殃从意外中生，如果不躲避它，终究要被它害，所以不能没有避害的占卜。 | Or punishment is laid on one, or household duties drag one in, or the authorities come to seize one, or an enemy takes revenge, or calamity springs from nothing one has done and misfortune from the unforeseen: if one does not avoid it one is harmed in the end, so one cannot do without divination for avoiding harm. |
> | 然大概与避乱相似，故并附列之。 | 不过大概和避乱相似，所以一并附在这里。 | But in general it is much like fleeing disorder, so it is appended together here. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「户役」是按户摊派的差役、徭役。从这里往下的几联，说的多半是避祸（躲官府、仇家、差役），不限于兵乱。
>
> *Note:* "Household duties" are the corvée and services levied on each household. From here on the couplets mostly concern avoiding harm (hiding from the authorities, enemies and runners) and are not limited to war and banditry.
