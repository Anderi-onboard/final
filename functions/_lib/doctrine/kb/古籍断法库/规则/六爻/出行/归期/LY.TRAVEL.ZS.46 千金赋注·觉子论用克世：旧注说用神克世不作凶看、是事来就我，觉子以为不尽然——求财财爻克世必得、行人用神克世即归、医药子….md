# 千金赋注·觉子论用克世：旧注说用神克世不作凶看、是事来就我，觉子以为不尽然——求财财爻克世必得、行人用神克世即归、医药子孙克世即愈，其余各占都不宜用神克世，占功名官鬼克世更是非祸即灾

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用克世就是用神来克世爻。旧注说不要当凶看，因为主事的爻动来克世，是这件事来就我，事必易成，我虽被克又有什么妨碍，所以说克世的不算凶。觉子认为这个说法不完全对。他验证过的：占求财，财爻克世一定得到；占行人，用神克世马上回来；占医药，子孙克世即愈。除了这几种占，其他都不宜用神克世。如果占功名，官鬼克世，不是祸就是灾，不能一概说不要当凶看。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* "The Subject controls the Self" means the Subject comes to control the Self. The old commentary says not to take it as ill: the line that rules the matter moves to control the Self, which means the matter comes to me and will easily be done, so what harm is there in my being controlled? Hence it is said that a controller of the Self is not taken as ill. Juezi thinks this is not entirely right. What he has found borne out is this: in divining for wealth, a Wealth line that controls the Self is surely gained; in divining for a traveller, a Subject that controls the Self returns at once; in divining for medicine, Output controlling the Self means a cure. Apart from these few divinations, the Subject controlling the Self is unfit in all others. If one divines for office, Pressure controlling the Self is not misfortune but calamity, and it cannot be said across the board not to take it as ill.

> [!warning] 打架
> 旧注：用神来克世是「事来就我」，不当凶看；觉子：只有求财、行人、医药这几种占才是好的，其余都不宜，功名占官鬼克世非祸即灾。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The old commentary holds that the Subject coming to control the Self is the matter coming to me and is not ill; Juezi holds that this is good only in divining for wealth, a traveller and medicine, that it is unfit in the rest, and that in divining for office Pressure controlling the Self is calamity.
