# 避乱·应若遭伤当累众：官鬼伤克应爻必然连累别人，月建日辰伤克应爻也一样

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼伤克应爻，必定连累别人；月建、日辰伤克应爻也是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Pressure injures or controls the Other line, others will surely be dragged in; the same holds when the Month or the Day does it.

## 《黄金策·避乱》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1639 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Fleeing disorder", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应若遭伤当累众 | 应爻如果遭到伤害就会连累众人 | If the Other is injured, others will be dragged in |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1639 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼伤克应爻，必然累及他人，月建日辰亦然。 | 官鬼伤克应爻，必定连累别人；月建、日辰伤克应爻也是这样。 | If Pressure injures or controls the Other line, others will surely be dragged in; the same holds when the Month or the Day does it. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 原书这一行把两联写在一起（「应若遭伤当累众……」「妻如受克定伤财……」），这里按联拆成两条。
>
> *Note:* The book writes two couplets together on this line ("if the Other is injured, others are dragged in ..." and "if Wealth is controlled, money is surely lost ..."); they are split here into two entries.
