# 趋避章·书中例，占流年：寅木动爻临月建化进神克世，六七月木衰墓时防土木之厄，宜出外避；目下去宜往西，六七月去反宜往东；此人七月初八往东，二十八地震房塌，独他得免

六爻 · 出行 · 方位 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 克世的神动了，宜出外避；去的方向要看克世的神旺衰：它旺的时候去西，它衰的时候反而宜往东。书中例：寅月丁卯日占流年，得噬嗑卦变睽卦：寅木动爻临月建，化出进神克世爻，这时木盛、贪着自己的旺，不来克世；六、七月是木衰墓的时候，防土木之灾；问可不可以避，宜出外才能避；问往哪个方向吉，目下去的宜往西，六、七月去的宜往东，因为六七月木衰，反而宜东。这个人竟没有出门，拖到七月初七又做了凶梦，初八就往东去了，二十八日地震房塌，人口受伤，只有他幸免。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the controlling spirit moves, it is fit to go out to avoid it; the direction depends on that spirit's strength: while it is strong go west, while it is weak go east instead. Worked example: in the Yin month, on a Dingmao day, a man divined the year ahead and got Shihe changing to Kui: the moving Yin-wood line sits on the Month and changes into an Advancing spirit that controls the Self; at this time the Wood is thriving and greedy for its own flourishing, so it does not come to control the Self, but in the sixth and seventh months, when Wood is weak and in the tomb, beware calamity from earth and timber. Asked whether it could be avoided, I said it could, only by going out; asked which direction was lucky, I said: if you go now, go west; if you go in the sixth or seventh month, go east, because in those months Wood is weak and east becomes the fit direction. The man did not leave and put it off until the seventh day of the seventh month, when he had an ill dream again; on the eighth he went east, and on the twenty-eighth there was an earthquake, the house fell and people were hurt, and he alone escaped.
