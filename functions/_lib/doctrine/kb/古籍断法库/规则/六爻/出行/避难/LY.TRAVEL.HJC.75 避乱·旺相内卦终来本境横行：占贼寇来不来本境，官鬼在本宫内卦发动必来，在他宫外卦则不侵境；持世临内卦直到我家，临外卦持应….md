# 避乱·旺相内卦终来本境横行：占贼寇来不来本境，官鬼在本宫内卦发动必来，在他宫外卦则不侵境；持世临内卦直到我家，临外卦持应虽来不入我室；卦身也忌临

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占贼寇会不会来我的地界：官鬼在本宫、在内卦发动，必定来；在别的宫、在外卦，就不侵入地界。持世、临在内卦，直接到我家；临在外卦、持应，虽然来也不进我的屋室。卦身也忌讳官鬼临在上面。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining whether bandits will come into my district: if Pressure moves in its own palace and in the inner trigram, they will surely come; in another palace or in the outer trigram, they do not enter the district. If it holds the Self and stands in the inner trigram, they come straight to my house; standing in the outer trigram and holding the Other, though they come they do not enter my rooms. The Body also fears Pressure standing on it.
