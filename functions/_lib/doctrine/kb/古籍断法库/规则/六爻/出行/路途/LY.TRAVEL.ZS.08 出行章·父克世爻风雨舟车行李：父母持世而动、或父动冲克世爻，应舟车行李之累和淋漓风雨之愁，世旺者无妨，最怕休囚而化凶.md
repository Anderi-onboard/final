# 出行章·父克世爻风雨舟车行李：父母持世而动、或父动冲克世爻，应舟车行李之累和淋漓风雨之愁，世旺者无妨，最怕休囚而化凶

六爻 · 出行 · 路途 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻持世而发动，或者发动的父爻冲克世爻，验证过的，有的应在舟车、行李的劳累，有的应在淋漓风雨的忧愁；世爻旺相的无妨，最怕世爻休囚又化出凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line holds the Self and moves, or a moving Resource line clashes with or controls the Self, as I have tested it, it answers sometimes in the burden of boats, carts and baggage and sometimes in the misery of being drenched by wind and rain; if the Self is strong there is no harm, and what is feared most is the Self resting or trapped and changing into something ill.
