# 行人章·书中例，由江右登舟占一路平安否：酉金官鬼持世本是忧疑之象，实应风阻；子水父母发动是子日的风雨，化出巳火子孙则巳日方晴，从此悟到子孙乃行舟之顺风

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 乘船遇到风阻的，不要拿兄弟爻当风云，要拿子孙爻当顺风。书中例：戌月丙戌日作者从江右上船，占一路平安与否，得蛊卦变巽卦：我怀疑酉金官鬼持世，是一路忧虑猜疑之象，起初不知道是应在风阻上；等到戊子日，风雨大作，船停在南康，才明白这一卦子水父母发动，是子日的风雨；看到它化出巳火子孙，就知道巳日才放晴；结果船停了五天，巳日天开，得到顺风；从此才领悟子孙就是行船的顺风。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a boat held up by wind, do not take the Peer line as wind and cloud but take Output as the fair wind. Worked example: in the Xu month, on a Bingxu day, the author boarded a boat at Jiangyou and divined whether the whole road would be safe, and got Gu changing to Xun: I suspected that Pressure You-metal holding the Self was a sign of worry and doubt on the road, not knowing at first that it answered in the wind holding us up; when the Wuzi day came, wind and rain broke out and the boat moored at Nankang, and only then did I understand that in this hexagram the Resource Zi-water moves, which is the wind and rain of the Zi day; and seeing that it changes into Output Si-fire, I knew the sky would clear on the Si day. The boat lay for five days, the sky opened on the Si day, and a fair wind came. From then on I understood that Output is the fair wind for a boat.
