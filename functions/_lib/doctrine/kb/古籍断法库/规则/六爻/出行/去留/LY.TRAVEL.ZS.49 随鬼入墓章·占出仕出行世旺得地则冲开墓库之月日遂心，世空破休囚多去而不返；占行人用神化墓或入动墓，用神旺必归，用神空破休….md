# 随鬼入墓章·占出仕出行世旺得地则冲开墓库之月日遂心，世空破休囚多去而不返；占行人用神化墓或入动墓，用神旺必归，用神空破休囚非病于他乡就是流落异境

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占出仕、出行，世爻旺相得地，冲开墓库的月日遂心；世爻如果空破休囚，多半是去了不回来。占行人，用神化墓，或入动墓的，用神旺相的一定回来；用神空破休囚的，不是病在他乡，就是流落在异地。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for taking office or setting out, if the Self is strong and well placed, matters go as wished in the month or day that clashes the tomb open; if the Self is Void, broken, resting or trapped, he mostly goes and does not return. In divining for a traveller, a Subject that changes into a tomb or enters a moving tomb returns for certain if it is strong; if it is Void, broken, resting or trapped, he is either ill in a strange land or adrift in a foreign place.
