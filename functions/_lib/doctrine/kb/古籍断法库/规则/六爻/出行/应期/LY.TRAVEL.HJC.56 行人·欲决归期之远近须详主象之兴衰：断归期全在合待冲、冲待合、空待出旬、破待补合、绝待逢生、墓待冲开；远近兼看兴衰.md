# 行人·欲决归期之远近须详主象之兴衰：断归期全在合待冲、冲待合、空待出旬、破待补合、绝待逢生、墓待冲开；远近兼看兴衰

六爻 · 出行 · 应期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 断归期，要用这些方法推详：被合住的等冲、被冲的等合、空的等出旬、破的等补合、绝的等逢生、入墓的等冲开；远近还要兼看主象的兴衰。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To judge the return, work it out by these methods: a combined line waits for a clash, a clashed line waits for a combination, a Void line waits for the end of the ten-day period, a broken line waits for repair or combination, an extinct line waits for life, and a tombed line waits to be clashed open; whether it is far or near is also judged by the rise and fall of the principal image.
