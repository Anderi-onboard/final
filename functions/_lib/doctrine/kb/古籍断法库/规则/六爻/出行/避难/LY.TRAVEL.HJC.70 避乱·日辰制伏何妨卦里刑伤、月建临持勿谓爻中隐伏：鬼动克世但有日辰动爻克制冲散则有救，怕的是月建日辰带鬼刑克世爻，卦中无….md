# 避乱·日辰制伏何妨卦里刑伤、月建临持勿谓爻中隐伏：鬼动克世但有日辰动爻克制冲散则有救，怕的是月建日辰带鬼刑克世爻，卦中无鬼也难免

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动来刑克世爻，固然是凶兆，但如果有日辰、动爻克制、冲散它，都叫有救，必定不至于受害；只怕月建、日辰带着鬼来刑克世爻，即使卦中没有鬼，也免不了遭害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure moving to punish or control the Self is of course an ill omen, but if the Day or a moving line restrains, controls or clashes it apart, all that is called rescue, and one will surely not be harmed; what is to be feared is the Month or the Day carrying Pressure and punishing or controlling the Self: even if the hexagram has no Pressure, one cannot escape harm.

> | 日辰制伏， | 有日辰制伏（鬼爻）， | If the Day restrains it, |
> | 何妨卦里刑伤； | 哪怕卦里有刑伤也无妨； | what matter if the hexagram has punishment and injury; |
> | 月建临持， | 月建临在它上、持着（鬼爻）， | if the Month stands on it and holds it, |
> | 勿谓爻中隐伏 | 就别说它在爻中隐伏 | do not say it lies hidden in the lines |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1575 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼动来刑克世爻，固是凶兆，若得日辰动爻克制冲散之，皆谓有救，必不为守。 | 官鬼发动来刑克世爻，固然是凶兆，但如果有日辰、动爻克制、冲散它，都叫有救，一定不至于受害。 | Pressure moving to punish or control the Self is of course an ill omen, but if the Day or a moving line restrains, controls or clashes it apart, all that is called rescue, and one will surely not be harmed. |
> | 唯怕月建日辰带鬼刑克世爻，虽卦中无鬼，不免遭害。 | 只怕月建、日辰带着鬼来刑克世爻，即使卦中没有鬼，也免不了遭害。 | What is to be feared is the Month or the Day carrying Pressure and punishing or controlling the Self: even if the hexagram has no Pressure, one cannot escape harm. |

> [!note] 按
> 注文第一句末尾的「必不为守」，按文意应是「必不为害」，译文按「必不至于受害」翻。
>
> *Note:* The words at the end of the note's first sentence, "surely not be keeping", should by the sense be "surely not be harmful", and the translation follows that.
