# 进神退神章·书中例，占兄归期：兄爻动化退神已有归志，但戌逢月破在外诸事未遂，断六月可望，后来戌月才回——应在实破之月，是动破而变日月，不能说退不及

六爻 · 出行 · 归期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动爻化退神已有归意；动爻遇月破的，要等到填实破的月份才应。书中例：辰月己未日占兄长什么时候归，得履卦变兑卦：兄爻动化退神，已经有了回来的心，但是戌土逢月破，在外面诸事没有如意，六月可以盼回；后来到戌月才回来，是应在实破之月——填实月破的月份。这是动爻破而又变到日月，怎么能说是退神不及呢？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A moving line changing into a Retreating spirit already has the mind to return; a moving line that meets the Month-break answers only when the break is filled. Worked example: in the Chen month, on a Jiwei day, a man divined when his elder brother would return and got Lü (Treading) changing to Dui: the Peer line moves and changes into a Retreating spirit, so he already has a mind to return, but Xu-earth meets the Month-break and nothing abroad has gone as he wished, so he can be hoped for in the sixth month; in the end he came back in the Xu month, answering in the month that fills the break. This is a moving line that is broken and changes into the Month and the Day; how can one say a Retreating spirit falls short?
