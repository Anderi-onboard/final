# 避乱·子爻福德北宜行、午象官爻南勿往：官鬼所临的方位是寇贼出入之处，宜避；子孙所临的地方是贼不到之处，宜往

六爻 · 出行 · 方位 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼所临的方位，是寇贼出入的地方，应当躲开；子孙所临的地方，是贼不到的地方，适宜去。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The quarter where Pressure stands is where the bandits come and go, and should be avoided; the place where Output stands is where the bandits do not come, and it is fit to go there.

## 《黄金策·避乱》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1581 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Fleeing disorder", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子爻福德北宜行， | 子爻是福德，北方适宜去； | The Zi line is Output, and the north is fit to go to; |
> | 午象官爻南勿往 | 午象官爻在南，南方不要去 | the Wu image is Pressure, and one must not go south |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1581 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼所临之方，乃寇出入之墟，宜避之； | 官鬼所临的方位，是寇贼出入的地方，应当躲开； | The quarter where Pressure stands is where the bandits come and go, and should be avoided; |
> | 子孙所临之地，乃贼不到之处，宜往之。 | 子孙所临的地方，是贼不到的地方，适宜去。 | the place where Output stands is where the bandits do not come, and it is fit to go there. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一联是举例：子在北方、午在南方，子爻是福德就往北，午象是官鬼就别往南；换了别的卦，就换成别的方位。
>
> *Note:* This couplet is an example: Zi lies in the north and Wu in the south, so if the Zi line is Output go north, and if the Wu image is Pressure do not go south; in another hexagram the quarters change.
