# 进神退神章·书中例，占出行：世临寅木动化进神，合当就行，因月破目下未必能行，出月才可，后来亥月（破而逢合之月）方行，由燕至粤，次年八月归；子孙化进神知其平安

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动爻化进神是好事：世爻临寅木发动化出进神，按理应当就走，但因为月破，眼下未必能走，要出了这个月才行。书中例：申月乙卯日占出行，得屯卦变节卦：后来到亥月才动身，由燕地到粤地，第二年八月回来。我问：路上平安吗？他说：来去都托庇平安。我是因为子孙化进神，知道他平安，所以才问。应在亥月出行，是月破逢合的月份，这是动爻被月破、又化出日辰，怎么能说它不能进呢？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A moving line changing into an Advancing spirit is a good thing: the Self sits on Yin-wood and moves and changes into an Advancing spirit, so by rights he should go at once, but because of the Month-break he may not be able to go now and can go only after the month is out. Worked example: in the Shen month, on a Yimao day, a man divined about setting out and got Zhun changing to Jie: he set out only in the Hai month, going from Yan to Yue, and came back in the eighth month of the next year. I asked: was the road safe? He said: coming and going I was sheltered and safe. I asked only because Output changes into an Advancing spirit, and I knew he would be safe. That it answered in the Hai month is the month in which a broken line meets a combination; this is a moving line broken by the Month and changing into the Day, so how can one say it cannot advance?
