# 趋避章·书中例，占防流兵能为我害否：巳酉丑合金局克世、世临蛇鬼，看似在劫难逃；作者断子孙合成金局克去身边之鬼，反而无忧，酉金得丑辰二土生，不能论克；后来屡遇兵变皆有惊无险

六爻 · 出行 · 避难 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 避祸要避到生世的方向、子孙的方向；子孙如果发动，不拘克世、生世、持世都吉。书中例：巳月戊辰日占防流兵能不能害我，得临卦变睽卦：当时流兵为害，到处遭殃，有个懂易的人占得这一卦，整天焦虑；我问他这一卦为什么发愁，他说巳酉丑合成金局克世爻，世爻又临螣蛇官鬼，命数在劫难中，万万逃不掉。我笑着说：你和兵贼同住，保你没事。他问为什么，我说：子孙合成金局，克去身边的鬼，还担心什么？他说：酉金子孙在巳月旺，又化出巳火，论克，怎么论生？我说：酉金得丑、辰两个土来生它，怎么论克？结果屡次遇到兵变，这个人有时避、有时不避，都是有惊无险。从癸未年占得这一卦，直到庚寅年地方大定，他对我说：子孙是福神，确实灵验；世上看书不到、论理不透，把吉当凶、把凶当吉的，不只我一个人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Avoid to the direction that gives life to the Self and the direction of Output; if Output moves, it is lucky whether it controls, gives life to or holds the Self. Worked example: in the Si month, on a Wuchen day, a man divined whether roving soldiers could harm him and got Lin changing to Kui. At that time roving soldiers were doing harm and bringing calamity everywhere, and a man who knew the Changes, having got this hexagram, was anxious all day. I asked what worried him in it. He said: Si, You and Chou combine into a Metal frame that controls the Self, and the Self sits on a Coiling Snake and Pressure; my fate is in the calamity and I can never escape. I laughed and said: you live with the soldiers and thieves, and I guarantee you will be fine. He asked why. I said: Output combines into the Metal frame and controls away the Pressure beside you; what is there to fear? He said: You-metal Output is strong in the Si month and also changes into Si-fire; if it is control, how can it be life? I said: You-metal is given life by the two earths Chou and Chen; how can it be called control? In the end he met mutinies of soldiers again and again; sometimes he avoided and sometimes not, but it was always a fright with no harm. From the Guiwei year when he got this hexagram until the Gengyin year when the region was at peace, he said to me: Output is the spirit of fortune and it is truly borne out; those in the world who do not read deeply or reason thoroughly and take the lucky for ill and the ill for lucky are not only myself.
