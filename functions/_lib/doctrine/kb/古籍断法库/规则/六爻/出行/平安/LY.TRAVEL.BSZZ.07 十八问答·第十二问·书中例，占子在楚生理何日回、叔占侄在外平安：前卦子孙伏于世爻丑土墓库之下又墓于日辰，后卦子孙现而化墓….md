# 十八问答·第十二问·书中例，占子在楚生理何日回、叔占侄在外平安：前卦子孙伏于世爻丑土墓库之下又墓于日辰，后卦子孙现而化墓、寅木原神真破真空，申金月建官鬼临道路发动，两卦并看是不祥之兆；后来果然传来五月长江履舟而死的口信

六爻 · 出行 · 平安 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 四生墓绝有三种：生墓绝于日辰是一，生墓绝于飞爻是二，动而变出来的是三。用神墓绝，有救就无凶。书中例：申月癸丑日有人占儿子在楚地做生意哪天回，得损卦：申金子孙伏在世爻丑土的墓库之下，本来就不宜，怎能又墓在日辰？令郎恐怕有大患；他说，近来有信，里面说八月起身，所以才占他来不来，我说这一卦难以断他的归期；他的叔叔说，我来占侄子在外平安与否，又得无妄卦变颐卦：前一卦子孙不现且入墓，后一卦现身却化墓，何况原神寅木是真破真空、没有生助，又有申金月建官鬼临在道路上发动，两卦并看，是不祥之兆；他说，前些日子有口信传来说五月在长江翻船死了，这个信息我已经听到，卦理很明白，所以才开玩笑去占。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Life, Tomb and Extinction come in three kinds: in the Day is one, in the flying line is two, and what comes out by moving and changing is three. A Subject in the tomb or extinct is not ill if there is rescue. Worked example: in the Shen month, on a Guichou day, a man divined on what day his son, trading in Chu, would return and got Sun: Output Shen-metal is hidden under the tomb store Chou-earth of the Self line, which is already unfit, and how can it be in the tomb of the Day as well? Your son may meet a great misfortune. He said: a letter came recently saying he would set out in the eighth month, so I asked whether he would come; I said this hexagram could not fix his day of return. His uncle said: I came to divine whether my nephew is safe abroad, and he got Wuwang changing to Yi: in the former hexagram Output is hidden and in the tomb; in the latter it appears but changes into a tomb, and moreover the Support Yin-wood is truly broken and Void with no help, and Pressure Shen-metal, the Month, sits on the road and moves; read together, the two hexagrams are an ominous sign. He said: a few days ago word came that he died in the fifth month when his boat capsized on the Yangtze; I had already heard it, and the hexagram principle is very clear, so I divined as a jest.
