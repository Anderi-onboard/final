# 出行·世动订期变鬼则自投罗网、官临畏宿化福则终脱樊笼：世动则行期已定，变鬼爻去后遭祸，鬼持世是想走不走，鬼化子孙虽有灾不足畏

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻发动，行期就定了；世爻、应爻都动宜赶快走；世爻动而变出鬼爻，去后必遭祸患，等于自投罗网；鬼爻持世，是犹豫畏缩、想走又不走的样子；鬼爻化出子孙，即使有灾患也不足畏惧，最终能脱身。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Self moves the date of departure is fixed; if the Self and the Other both move it is fit to set out quickly; if the Self moves and changes into a Pressure line, one meets misfortune after going and walks into the net oneself; Pressure holding the Self is the look of hesitation and shrinking, wanting to go and not going; and if Pressure changes into Output, though there is calamity it is not to be feared, and one at last escapes.

> | 世爻不动行期不定，动则期已定矣。 | 世爻不动，行期不定；动了，日期就已经定了。 | If the Self does not move the date of departure is not fixed; if it moves the date is already fixed. |
