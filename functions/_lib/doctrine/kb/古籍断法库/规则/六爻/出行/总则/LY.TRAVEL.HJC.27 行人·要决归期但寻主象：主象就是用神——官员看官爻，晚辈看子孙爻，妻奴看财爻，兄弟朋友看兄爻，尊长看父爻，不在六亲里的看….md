# 行人·要决归期但寻主象：主象就是用神——官员看官爻，晚辈看子孙爻，妻奴看财爻，兄弟朋友看兄爻，尊长看父爻，不在六亲里的看应爻

六爻 · 出行 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占行人（在外的人），先找主象，也就是用神：占官员看官爻，占晚辈看子孙爻，占妻子、奴仆看财爻，占兄弟朋友看兄爻，占尊长看父爻，不在六亲之中的人看应爻；要决断他的归期，就看这个主象。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for an absent traveller one first finds the principal image, which is the Subject: for an official look at the Pressure line, for the young at the Output line, for a wife or servant at the Wealth line, for brothers and friends at the Peer line, for elders at the Resource line, and for anyone outside the six relations at the Other line; to decide his day of return one looks at this principal image.
