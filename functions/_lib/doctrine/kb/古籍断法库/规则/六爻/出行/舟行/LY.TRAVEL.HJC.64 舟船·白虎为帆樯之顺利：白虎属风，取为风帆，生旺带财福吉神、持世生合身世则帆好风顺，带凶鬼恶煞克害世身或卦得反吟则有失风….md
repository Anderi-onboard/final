# 舟船·白虎为帆樯之顺利：白虎属风，取为风帆，生旺带财福吉神、持世生合身世则帆好风顺，带凶鬼恶煞克害世身或卦得反吟则有失风倾覆之患

六爻 · 出行 · 舟行 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 白虎属风，所以取它代表风帆：生旺、带财爻福神等吉神、发动持世或生合身世，船就有好帆，风顺而快；白虎带着凶鬼恶煞、旺而发动克害世爻和身，或者卦是反吟，会遭遇失风、倾覆的祸患。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The White Tiger belongs to wind and so stands for the sail: if it is strong and carries Wealth, Output or other auspicious spirits, and moves to hold or give life to or combine with the Self and Body, the boat has a good sail and the wind is fair and quick; if the White Tiger carries baleful Pressure and evil stars, strong and moving to harm or control the Self and Body, or the hexagram is Reversed Chant, there is the danger of losing the wind and capsizing.
