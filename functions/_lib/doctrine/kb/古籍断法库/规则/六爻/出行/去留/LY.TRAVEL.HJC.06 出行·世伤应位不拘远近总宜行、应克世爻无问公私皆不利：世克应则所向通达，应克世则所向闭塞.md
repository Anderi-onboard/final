# 出行·世伤应位不拘远近总宜行、应克世爻无问公私皆不利：世克应则所向通达，应克世则所向闭塞

六爻 · 出行 · 去留 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻克应位，是我制住对方，所去的方向通达，去了没有阻隔，不论远近都适宜出行；应爻克世爻，所去的方向闭塞，不论公事私事都不利，再遇到动爻、日辰刑克，更不吉利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self controlling the Other position is my controlling the other side: the way I go is open and I go without hindrance, and it is fit to set out however far or near; the Other controlling the Self closes the way I go and is unfavourable for public and private business alike, and with a moving line or the Day punishing or controlling as well it is the more inauspicious.
