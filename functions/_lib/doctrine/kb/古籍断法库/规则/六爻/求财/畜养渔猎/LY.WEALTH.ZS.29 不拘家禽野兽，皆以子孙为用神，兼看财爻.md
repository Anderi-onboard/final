# 不拘家禽野兽，皆以子孙为用神，兼看财爻

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买卖、饲养六畜（家畜野兽）以子孙为用神，兼看财爻：财持世而子孙动来生、日月生助，或财化子孙，大利；子孙持世、财合世也有利。防鬼化子、子化鬼、父化子、子化父、父兄持世、财临空破（书中例：子孙动变鬼、又临月破，贩马多死，幸财暗动生世，未大亏本）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Livestock, bought, sold or raised, domestic or wild: Output is the Subject, with Wealth considered too. Wealth on the Self line fed by a moving Output or by the Day and Month, or Wealth turning into Output, great profit; Output on the Self line with Wealth combining, profit too. Beware of Pressure turning into Output or the reverse, Resource turning into Output or the reverse, Resource or Peer on the Self line, Wealth void or broken (case: Output moving into Pressure and under a Month-break; most of the horses died on the road, but a hidden-stirred Wealth generating the Self line saved the capital).
