# 日伤妻位，财虽旺而当日应无

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻旺相、生合世爻或持世，本是必得；若日辰克财，当天得不到，要过了这一天才得。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A thriving Wealth line that generates, combines with or holds the Self line means certain gain; if the Day controls it, nothing comes that day; it comes after the day has passed.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7782 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日伤妻位， | 日辰克伤财爻， | When the Day harms the Wealth line, |
> | 财虽旺，而当日应无。 | 财即使旺，当天也得不到。 | even a thriving Wealth brings nothing that day. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7783 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财爻旺相，生合持世，乃是必得之象。 | 财爻旺相，又生合世爻或持世，本是一定能得的象。 | A thriving Wealth line that generates, combines with or sits on the Self line is a sign of certain gain. |
> | 若被日辰克制其财，纵现，必然无有。 | 如果日辰克制财爻，财即使出现，也一定拿不到。 | But if the Day controls it, the money will not come, even though it shows. |
> | 须过此日，然后有得。 | 要过了这一天，才会得到。 | Only after that day passes will it be had. |

^q2
