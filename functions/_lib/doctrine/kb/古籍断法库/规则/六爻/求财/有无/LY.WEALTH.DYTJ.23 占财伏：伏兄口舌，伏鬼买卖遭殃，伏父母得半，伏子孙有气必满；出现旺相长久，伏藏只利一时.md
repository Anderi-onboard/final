# 占财伏：伏兄口舌，伏鬼买卖遭殃，伏父母得半，伏子孙有气必满；出现旺相长久，伏藏只利一时

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神出现在卦上而旺相的，主长久；伏藏而有气的，只有一时的好处。财伏在兄弟下有口舌，伏在世下旺相可成；财伏在鬼下买卖遭殃，除非日辰是福德；财伏在父母下旺相只得一半；财伏在子孙下有气就一定圆满。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Subject showing in the hexagram and thriving lasts long and far; one that is hidden but has qi profits only for a while. Wealth hidden under Peer meets quarrels, but under the Self line, if thriving, it can succeed; hidden under Pressure, trade meets disaster unless the Day is Output; hidden under Resource, thriving, it gets half; hidden under Output, with qi, it is surely full.

> [!note] 按
> 「日辰福德」可读作「日辰是福德」，也可读作「日辰、福德（有一个来）」；书里没有断开，这里取前一种。这四句都是歌诀，书里没有讲用法。
>
> *Note:* 'The Day, Output' can be read as 'the Day is Output' or as 'the Day or Output (one of them comes)'; the book does not punctuate it, and this entry takes the first. All four passages are verse and the book does not explain how to apply them.
