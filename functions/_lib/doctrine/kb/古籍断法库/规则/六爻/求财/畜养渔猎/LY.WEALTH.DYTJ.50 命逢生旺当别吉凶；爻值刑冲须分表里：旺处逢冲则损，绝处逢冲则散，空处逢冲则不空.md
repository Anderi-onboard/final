# 命逢生旺当别吉凶；爻值刑冲须分表里：旺处逢冲则损，绝处逢冲则散，空处逢冲则不空

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本命逢生旺，要分吉凶：临财福、旺相则吉，临鬼煞、大小耗则凶，反不如不旺；分宫爻遇刑冲，也要分表里：会财福、青龙逢刑冲则凶，临鬼煞、白虎逢刑冲反吉；旺处逢冲则损，绝处逢冲则散，空处逢冲则不空。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the natal line meets a thriving state one must tell auspicious from ominous: on Wealth or Output and thriving it is auspicious, on Pressure, baleful stars or the Great and Small Waste stars it is ominous and worse than not thriving; when the palace line meets punishment or clash one must tell the outside from the inside: joined with Wealth, Output or the Azure Dragon it is ominous, on Pressure, baleful stars or the White Tiger it is on the contrary auspicious; thriving and clashed it is damaged, severed and clashed it is scattered, Void and clashed it is no longer Void.

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1761 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | （生旺若逢本命，本为吉兆， | （生旺遇到本命，本来是吉兆， | (A thriving state meeting the natal line is originally an auspicious sign, |
> | 却看临财福旺相则吉，临鬼杀、大小耗则凶，反不如不旺。 | 要看它是不是临财福：旺相就吉，临鬼煞、大小耗就凶，反而不如不旺。 | but look at whether it sits on Wealth or Output: thriving it is auspicious, on Pressure, baleful stars or the Great and Small Waste stars it is ominous, worse than not thriving. |
> | 凡吉神扶命，旺处六畜必盛， | 凡是吉神扶持本命，旺的地方六畜必兴盛； | Whenever an auspicious spirit supports the natal line, where it thrives the livestock surely flourish; |
> | 若加鬼杀，得是休囚，庶几损折亦轻。 | 如果加了鬼煞，倒是休囚了好，损伤也轻些。 | if Pressure or baleful stars are added, it is better that it be weak, and the loss is lighter. |
> | 凡本命旺临财福，却加大小杀于其上， | 凡本命旺临财福，却加了大小杀在上面， | When the natal line is thriving on Wealth or Output but the Great and Small baleful stars are added upon it, |
> | 此主孳生虽盛，损失亦多，得失相半之兆。 | 这主繁殖虽然兴盛，损失也多，是得失各半的兆头。 | it means that breeding flourishes but the losses are many, a sign of gain and loss in equal halves. |
> | 不可概论，而断其吉凶。 | 不能一概而论，要分别断它的吉凶。 | It cannot be judged all alike, and its good and ill must be told apart. |

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1764 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | （分宫若日辰刑冲，或动爻刑冲，当有吉凶之辨，表里之分。 | （分宫的爻如果被日辰刑冲，或者被动爻刑冲，就有吉凶之辨、表里之分。 | (If the palace line is punished or clashed by the Day, or by a moving line, there is a distinction of good and ill and of outside and inside. |
> | 凡分宫会财福、青龙，若逢刑冲则凶。 | 凡分宫会上财福、青龙，如果逢刑冲就凶； | When the palace line joins Wealth, Output or the Azure Dragon, meeting punishment or clash is ominous; |
> | 若临鬼杀、白虎，逢刑冲反吉， | 如果临鬼煞、白虎，逢刑冲反而吉， | when it sits on Pressure, baleful stars or the White Tiger, meeting punishment or clash is on the contrary auspicious, |
> | 必主前虽损失，以后必然旺盛。 | 必定是先前虽有损失，以后一定旺盛。 | and surely, though there was loss before, later it will surely flourish. |
> | 大凡旺处逢冲则损，绝处逢冲则散，空处逢冲则不空。 | 大凡旺处逢冲就损，绝处逢冲就散，空处逢冲就不空。 | In general, a thriving line meeting a clash is damaged, a severed one meeting a clash is scattered, and a Void one meeting a clash is no longer Void. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 第一、三段是《天玄赋》的歌诀，第二、四段是紧接着的括号小注。「表里」书里没有解释，注里的意思是：会财福、青龙的遇刑冲看似吉而实凶，临鬼煞、白虎的遇刑冲看似凶而实吉。
>
> *Note:* The first and third passages are verses of the Tianxuan Fu, the second and fourth the bracketed small notes that follow. 'Outside and inside' is not glossed; in the note it means that what joins Wealth, Output or the Azure Dragon looks auspicious but is really ominous when punished or clashed, while what sits on Pressure, baleful stars or the White Tiger looks ominous but is really auspicious.
