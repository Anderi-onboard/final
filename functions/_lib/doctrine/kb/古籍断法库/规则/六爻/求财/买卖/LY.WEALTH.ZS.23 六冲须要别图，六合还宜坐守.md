# 六冲须要别图，六合还宜坐守

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卖货守还是动：旧说六冲宜另图、六合宜坐守；野鹤屡验：六冲而财旺不必改、财衰改也无益，应再占「更改如何」，财世两旺许改，衰则耐守；六合也看财爻：财持世而动不可坐守，衰而静则守待旺时。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Selling, stay or move: the old rule says Six-Clash, change plans; Six-Combine, stay put. Yehe tested it: with a Six-Clash and Wealth strong there is no need to change, with Wealth weak changing does not help; cast again asking 'what if I change', and allow it only if Wealth and the Self line are both strong, otherwise wait. With a Six-Combine, look at Wealth too: on the Self line and moving, do not sit still; weak and still, wait for its season.
