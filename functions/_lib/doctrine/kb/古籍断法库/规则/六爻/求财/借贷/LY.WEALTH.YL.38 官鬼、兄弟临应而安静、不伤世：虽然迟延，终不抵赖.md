# 官鬼、兄弟临应而安静、不伤世：虽然迟延，终不抵赖

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 即使官鬼、兄弟临在应上，只要安静、不伤卦身和世，就不用生气：虽然拖延，终究不会抵赖。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Even with Pressure or Peer on the Other line, as long as it is still and does not hurt the Body and the Self, there is no need to be angry: he delays, but in the end he does not deny the debt.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 纵若鬼兄临此应， | 即使官鬼、兄弟临在这个应上， | Even if Pressure or Peer sits on this Other line, |
> | 不伤身世静非嗔。 | 只要不伤卦身和世、又安静，就不用生气。 | as long as it does not hurt the Body and the Self and stays still, there is no need to be angry. |
