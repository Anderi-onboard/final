# 玄武临财：贼来到、口舌烦恼，退失是寻常；玄武入火终失资财；值金有财须外得

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 玄武临在财上，盗贼要来、口舌烦恼，须要防备，损失是常事；玄武落在火爻上，终究失掉资财；落在金爻上有财，要在外面得，否则是人送来、盗贼拿走。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Dark Tortoise on Wealth: thieves arrive and quarrels and trouble come, and one must guard, since loss is common; the Dark Tortoise entering Fire ends in losing one's property; entering Metal, if there is money it must be gained outside, otherwise it is delivered by people and taken by thieves.

> [!note] 按
> 「牛猪空亡玄武当」等句是说六畜，本库不收。「人送盗将来」的确切意思不明，这里译作「人送来、盗贼拿走」，不确定。
>
> *Note:* The lines about oxen and pigs falling Void under the Dark Tortoise concern livestock and are not included here. The exact sense of 'people deliver, thieves bring it away' is unclear and is rendered loosely.
