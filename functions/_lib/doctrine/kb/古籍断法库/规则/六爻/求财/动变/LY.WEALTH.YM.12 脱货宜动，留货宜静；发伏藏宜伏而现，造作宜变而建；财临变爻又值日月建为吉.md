# 脱货宜动，留货宜静；发伏藏宜伏而现，造作宜变而建；财临变爻又值日月建为吉

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 脱货宜动，留货宜静；发掘伏藏的宜伏着的财出现，造作的宜财变出来而得建旺。注：发伏，如开矿淘沙取宝藏；造作，如木匠、土工、针线、织机；财临变爻，再遇日建或月建，就吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To sell off goods, movement suits; to hold goods, stillness suits; for unearthing hidden things, a hidden Wealth should come to show, and for fabrication, a Wealth that changes should be raised by the month or day. The note adds: unearthing means things like mining, panning sand and recovering buried treasure, and fabrication things like carpentry, earthwork, needlework and weaving; Wealth on a changed line meeting the Day or Month ruler is auspicious.
