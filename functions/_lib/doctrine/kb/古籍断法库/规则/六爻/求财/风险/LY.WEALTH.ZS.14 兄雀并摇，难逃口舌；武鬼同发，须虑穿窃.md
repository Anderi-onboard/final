# 兄雀并摇，难逃口舌；武鬼同发，须虑穿窃

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 投行交易：朱雀临兄弟而官鬼动来克世，有口舌官灾；玄武临兄弟而官鬼动来克世，防盗窃。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Trading through a broker: the Vermilion Bird on Peer with Pressure moving to control the Self line, quarrels and trouble with officials; the Dark Tortoise on Peer with Pressure moving to control the Self line, beware of theft.
