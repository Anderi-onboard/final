# 父兄有气财还绝，莫若安贫

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母、兄弟有气（或发动刑冲克害）而财爻弱、临绝：利少，恐怕折本，不如不做。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource and Peer strong (or moving to punish, clash, control or harm) while Wealth is weak or extinguished: little profit and a risk to the capital; better not to go ahead.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7904 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父兄有气财还绝， | 父母、兄弟有气，财爻却临绝， | Resource and Peer strong while Wealth is extinguished: |
> | 莫若安贫。 | 不如安于贫困。 | better to accept being poor. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7905 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父兄二爻，占财大忌。 | 父母、兄弟两爻，是占财的大忌。 | Resource and Peer are the two great enemies in a money question. |
> | 有发动刑冲克害，利必不多。 | 它们发动刑冲克害，利一定不多。 | If they move to punish, clash, control or harm, the profit will be small. |
> | 财爻更弱，恐防折本。 | 财爻再弱，恐怕要折本。 | If the Wealth line is weak as well, beware of losing the capital. |
> | 故不若安贫守分之为高也。 | 所以不如安贫守分为好。 | So it is wiser to accept being poor and keep to one's lot. |
