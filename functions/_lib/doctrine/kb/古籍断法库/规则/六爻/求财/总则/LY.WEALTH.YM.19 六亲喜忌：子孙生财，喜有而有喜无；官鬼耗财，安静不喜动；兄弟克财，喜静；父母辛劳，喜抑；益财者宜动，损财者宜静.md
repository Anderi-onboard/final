# 六亲喜忌：子孙生财，喜有而有喜无；官鬼耗财，安静不喜动；兄弟克财，喜静；父母辛劳，喜抑；益财者宜动，损财者宜静

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙生我的财，喜欢有，有了就喜、无了也喜；官鬼耗我的财，吉在安静，不喜摇动；兄弟克我的财，喜静不喜动；父母是辛劳，喜抑不喜扬；凡是有益于财的，宜其动，损害财的，宜其静。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output generates my Wealth: it is welcome and glad to be there, and glad also if it is absent; Pressure drains my Wealth: it is auspicious when quiet and it is not glad to be moved; Peer controls my Wealth: it is glad to be still and not glad to move; Resource is toil: it is glad to be held down and not raised; whatever benefits Wealth should move, and whatever harms Wealth should be still.
