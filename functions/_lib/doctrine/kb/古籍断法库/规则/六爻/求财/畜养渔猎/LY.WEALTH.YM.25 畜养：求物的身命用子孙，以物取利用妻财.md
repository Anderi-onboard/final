# 畜养：求物的身命用子孙，以物取利用妻财

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 禽兽这类东西，以子孙为其所属，因为它是积蓄利的来源；有时用财，有时用子，办法有两种：求这只动物自己的身命，用子孙；靠这只动物取利，用财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Creatures are assigned to Output because it is the source that stores up profit; sometimes Wealth is used and sometimes Output, and there are two methods: to seek the animal's own body and life, use Output; to take profit through the animal, use Wealth.
