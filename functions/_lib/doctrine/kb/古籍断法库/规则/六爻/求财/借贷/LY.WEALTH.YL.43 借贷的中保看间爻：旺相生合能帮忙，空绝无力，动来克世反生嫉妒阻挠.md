# 借贷的中保看间爻：旺相生合能帮忙，空绝无力，动来克世反生嫉妒阻挠

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借贷靠中保：一卦里的两个间爻就是居中作保的人；旺相、生合世，一定帮忙；落空落绝，没有力量帮不上；发动来克世，反而生嫉妒、阻挠。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a loan, the guarantor is the two between lines of the hexagram, the go-between who stands surety: strong, generating or combining, he will surely help; void or extinct, he has no strength and cannot assist; moving to control the Self, he breeds jealousy and obstruction.

## 《易林补遗·借贷财物章第一百九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2016 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 109 - Borrowing goods and money", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡若间爻空与绝， | 凡是间爻落空或绝， | If the between lines are void or extinct, |
> | 其间中保定无能。 | 那中间的保人一定没有能力。 | the guarantor between is surely powerless. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2017 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 一卦之中两间爻，系为中作保之人， | 一卦当中的两个间爻，是居中作保的人； | The two between lines in a hexagram stand for the go-between who stands surety; |
> | 但得旺相生合，决然扶助， | 只要旺相、生合，一定会帮忙； | if strong, generating or combining, he will surely help; |
> | 若遇空遇绝，乃无力无能不能赞囊耳。 | 如果遇空遇绝，就是没有力量、没有能力，帮不上忙； | if void or extinct, he has neither strength nor ability and cannot assist; |
> | 若动来克世者，反生嫉妒阻挠。 | 如果发动来克世，反而生出嫉妒、从中阻挠。 | if he moves to control the Self, he breeds jealousy and obstruction instead. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「不能赞囊」的「囊」疑是「襄」（赞襄，帮助）之误。
>
> *Note:* 'Cannot assist' translates a phrase whose last character is probably a slip for 'assist'.
