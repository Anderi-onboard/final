# 岁君逢劫杀，一年生意无聊

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占长期买卖看太岁：太岁临兄弟（劫杀），一年无利，兄弟上卦发动必有损耗；临官鬼一年惊忧，临父母一年艰辛，临财福一年顺利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For long-term trade, look at the Tai Sui: on Peer (the robber), no profit all year, and losses if that Peer line shows and moves; on Pressure, a year of worry; on Resource, a year of hardship; on Wealth or Output, a smooth year.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7820 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 岁君逢劫杀， | 太岁落在兄弟（劫杀）上， | If the Tai Sui falls on the robber (Peer), |
> | 一年生意无聊。 | 这一年生意都没意思。 | the whole year's business is dull. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7821 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占久远买卖，最怕太岁临持兄弟，主一年无利。 | 凡是占长期的买卖，最怕太岁临兄弟，主一年没有利。 | In any question about long-term trade, the thing most feared is the Tai Sui falling on Peer: no profit for the year. |
> | 出现变动，必然损耗。 | （这个兄弟）出现在卦上又发动，一定有损耗。 | If that line appears in the hexagram and moves, there will certainly be losses. |

> | 岁持财福，生意滔滔，一年顺利也。 | 太岁临妻财、子孙，生意兴隆，一年顺利。 | On Wealth or Output: business flows and the year goes smoothly. |

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11255 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 岁君逢劫煞，一年生意无聊。 | 太岁逢劫煞，一年生意没意思。 | If the Tai Sui meets the robber, the whole year's business is dull. |
> | 劫煞者，乃兄弟之爻也，兄临太岁，一年破耗。 | 劫煞就是兄弟爻；兄弟临太岁，一年破耗。 | The robber here is the Peer line; Peer on the Tai Sui means a year of losses. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 这里的「劫杀」「劫煞」指兄弟爻（野鹤明说）。《天玄赋》求财章的「劫杀加临兄位」里，劫杀是另一个神煞，不是兄弟，不要混。
>
> *Note:* Here 'the robber' (jiesha) means the Peer line, as Yehe says outright. In the Tianxuan Fu's chapter on wealth, 'the robber star on the Peer line' names a separate spirit-star, not Peer; do not confuse the two.
