# 卜筮元龟：财持世克世得利；财旺买的货贵、休囚买的货贱；卖看财与世应，见三金得宝，无财鬼克身防偷

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻持世、克世都得利；财旺，买来的货贵如金玉；财休囚，买来的货贱如尘土；卖货看财、世应旺相；买货遇上休囚，货多有益；卦里三个金得宝多，见离象没有不吉的；卦里没有财、鬼克身，丢东西多半是遇上偷的人；内外卦相生有气、财与龙德并，金银加倍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth holding or controlling the Self brings profit; with Wealth thriving what is bought is as precious as gold and jade, with it weak what is bought is cheap as dust; in selling, Wealth and the Self and Other thriving are solid; in buying, meeting Wealth weak, goods are plentiful and profitable; three Metal lines in the hexagram bring many treasures and the Li image is always auspicious; a hexagram without Wealth and Pressure controlling the Body: what is lost is probably taken by a thief; the trigrams generating each other and having qi, Wealth joined with the Dragon Virtue, gold and silver double.

## 《断易天机·占买卖第二十》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1323 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 20: Divining for buying and selling", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财爻持世兼克世， | 财爻持世，又克世， | Wealth holding the Self and also controlling it: |
> | 此皆得利自然真， | 这都是得利之象，一定是真的； | these are all signs of profit, sure and true; |
> | 财旺买物金玉贵， | 财旺，买来的东西贵如金玉； | Wealth thriving, what is bought is precious as gold and jade; |
> | 休囚买必贱如尘。 | 财休囚，买来的东西必定贱如尘土。 | Wealth weak, what is bought will be cheap as dust. |
> | 卖财世应旺相实， | 卖货，财和世应旺相就实在； | In selling, Wealth and the Self and Other thriving is solid; |
> | 买遇休囚货盈益， | 买货，遇上休囚，货物多、有益； | in buying, meeting them weak, goods are plentiful and profitable; |
> | 卦有三金得宝多， | 卦里有三个金，得的宝物多， | three Metal lines in the hexagram, the treasures gained are many, |
> | 离象见之无不吉。 | 见到离象，没有不吉的。 | and where the Li image is seen there is nothing that is not auspicious. |
> | 凡卦无财鬼克身， | 凡是卦里没有财、鬼克着身， | Whenever a hexagram has no Wealth and Pressure controls the Body, |
> | 遗忘应是遇偷人， | 丢了东西，多半是遇上了偷东西的人； | what is lost is probably taken by a thief; |
> | 贞悔相生都有气， | 贞悔相生，都有气， | Zhen and Hui generating each other and both having qi, |
> | 财并龙德倍金银。 | 财再和龙德并在一起，金银加倍。 | with Wealth joined to the Dragon Virtue, gold and silver are doubled. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「龙德」是一颗神煞，书里没有在这一条里解释。「卜筮元龟」是《断易天机》转引的书名，原书这里没有另收。
>
> *Note:* 'The Dragon Virtue' is a spirit-star that this passage does not explain. 'Bushi Yuangui' is a book the Duanyi Tianji quotes; this library has no separate copy of it.
