# 公门何虑官爻

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一般占财忌官鬼发动（阻隔）；求公门（官府）之财反要官鬼：无鬼或鬼空，财旺也难到手；官鬼旺相、生合世爻卦身则吉；刑克世爻主责罚、祸害立至；官鬼死绝也不济事，须财、官两全才大吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Money questions usually fear a moving Pressure line (obstruction), but money from official quarters needs Pressure: with no Pressure line, or a void one, even a strong Wealth is hard to get; Pressure thriving and generating or combining with the Self and Body lines is good; Pressure punishing or controlling the Self line means punishment and immediate trouble; a dead or extinguished Pressure line is no use either; only with both Wealth and Pressure sound is it very good.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7856 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占财皆忌官动，主有阻隔。 | 占财一般都忌官鬼发动，主有阻隔。 | Money questions generally fear a moving Pressure line: it means obstruction. |
> | 惟求公门之财，必然倚托官府。 | 只有求官府的财，一定要依靠官府。 | Only money from official quarters depends on the officials. |
> | 若无鬼爻，或落空亡，则虽财爻有气，亦难到手。 | 如果没有官鬼，或官鬼落空亡，那么财爻即使有气，也难到手。 | If there is no Pressure line, or it is void, then even a Wealth line with force is hard to get into hand. |
> | 必得旺相，生合世身则吉。 | 官鬼一定要旺相、生合世爻卦身才吉。 | The Pressure line must be thriving and must generate or combine with the Self and Body lines; then it is good. |
> | 刑克世爻，主有杖责。 | 官鬼刑克世爻，主受杖刑责罚。 | If it punishes or controls the Self line, expect a beating or punishment. |
> | 死绝亦不济事，须得财鬼两全，方为大吉。 | 官鬼死绝也不管用，要财、官两全，才是大吉。 | A dead or extinguished Pressure line is no use either; only with both Wealth and Pressure sound is it very good. |
