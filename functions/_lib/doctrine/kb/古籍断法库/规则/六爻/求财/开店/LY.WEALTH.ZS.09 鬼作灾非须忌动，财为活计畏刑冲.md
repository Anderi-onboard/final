# 鬼作灾非须忌动，财为活计畏刑冲

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开店：官鬼发动招是非，克世最忌（鬼现爻中是非日有，鬼摇克世灾祸连绵）；财为资本，忌衰墓空破、动而变凶、日月刑冲克害；世爻与财爻落空，开不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Opening a shop: a moving Pressure invites disputes, and controlling the Self line is worst (Pressure present, disputes daily; Pressure moving against the Self, one disaster after another); Wealth is the capital and fears being weak, entombed, void or broken, turning harmful, or being punished, clashed or controlled by the Day or Month; the Self line and Wealth void, the shop will not open.
