# 鬼化为财克世大吉，财爻化鬼逢空全凶

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼化财（鬼送财）而来克世：大吉；若世爻去克官鬼则无财。财化官鬼：从有到无，再逢空为全凶。《断易天机》本：得日辰扶合、动爻生扶为「财入鬼手」，可无伤；旺财化衰鬼，财不全；衰财化旺鬼，防加倍折财。财化财：本卦财旺而变出的财无气，宜速求；反之宜待。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure turning into Wealth ('the ghost bringing money') that controls the Self line: very lucky; if the Self line controls the Pressure line instead, no money. Wealth turning into Pressure: something becomes nothing, and void as well it is wholly unlucky. The Duanyi Tianji text: with the Day supporting or combining, or a moving line generating it, it is 'money in the ghost's hand' and may do no harm; strong Wealth into weak Pressure, the money is not whole; weak Wealth into strong Pressure, beware of double loss. Wealth into Wealth: a strong original turning into a forceless one, seek quickly; the reverse, wait.

## 《天玄赋·求财章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4566 行 · Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼化为财，克世须云大吉； | 官鬼化出财来，又来克世，应当说大吉； | Pressure turning into Wealth that controls the Self line: call it very lucky; |
> | 财爻化鬼，逢空可谓全凶。 | 财爻化出官鬼，再逢空亡，可说全凶。 | the Wealth line turning into Pressure and falling void: call it wholly unlucky. |

^q1

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4567 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼化财，从空而至。 | 官鬼化财，财是从无中来的。 | Pressure turning into Wealth: the money comes out of nothing. |
> | 且来克世，是利追我而走，故云大吉； | 又来克世，是利追着我跑，所以说大吉； | If it also controls the Self line, the profit is chasing after me; hence very lucky; |
> | 财化鬼，自有化无。 | 财化官鬼，是从有变成无。 | Wealth turning into Pressure: what exists turns into nothing. |
> | 又落空亡，是消磨殆尽，故曰全凶。 | 又落空亡，是消耗殆尽，所以说全凶。 | Falling void as well, it wears away to nothing; hence wholly unlucky. |

^q2

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1224 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼化财，是财从空而至。 | 官鬼化财，是财从无中来。 | Pressure turning into Wealth: the money comes out of nothing. |
> | 财本是生鬼的，而鬼反而化财，谓之鬼送财。 | 本来是财生官鬼，现在官鬼反而化出财来，叫作「鬼送财」。 | Normally Wealth feeds Pressure; when Pressure turns into Wealth instead, it is called 'the ghost bringing money'. |
> | 且来克世，是利追我而走，故云大吉。 | 又来克世，是利追着我跑，所以说大吉。 | If it also controls the Self line, the profit is chasing me; hence very lucky. |
> | 若世爻克鬼，则无财。 | 如果是世爻去克官鬼，就没有财。 | If instead the Self line controls the Pressure line, there is no money. |
> | 财化鬼，是财自有化无。 | 财化官鬼，是财从有变无。 | Wealth turning into Pressure: the money turns from something into nothing. |
> | 午前占，谓之鬼偷财。 | 午前占得，叫作「鬼偷财」。 | Cast before noon, it is called 'the ghost stealing money'. |
> | 若得日辰扶合，或动爻生扶，谓之财入鬼手，庶无伤也。 | 如果有日辰扶合，或动爻生扶，叫作「财入鬼手」，大概没有伤害。 | If the Day supports or combines with it, or a moving line generates and supports it, it is called 'money in the ghost's hand', and there may be no harm. |
> | 若旺财化衰鬼，其财不全。 | 旺的财化成衰的官鬼，财不能全得。 | A strong Wealth turning into a weak Pressure: the money does not come whole. |
> | 若衰财化旺鬼，须防两倍折财。 | 衰的财化成旺的官鬼，要防加倍折财。 | A weak Wealth turning into a strong Pressure: beware of losing twice as much. |
> | 又落空亡，是消磨殆尽，故曰全凶。 | 又落空亡，是消耗殆尽，所以说全凶。 | Falling void as well, it wears away to nothing; hence wholly unlucky. |
> | 凡遇财化财，本来是吉，但其中也有分别。 | 凡是财化财，本来是吉，但其中也有分别。 | Wealth turning into Wealth is normally lucky, but there are distinctions. |
> | 若前卦财旺化财无气，午前占，宜速求，向后财必薄。 | 如果本卦的财旺、化出的财无气：午前占得，宜赶快求，往后财一定薄； | If the original Wealth is strong and the Wealth it turns into has no force: cast before noon, seek it quickly, for later the money will be thin; |
> | 午后占，宜早求，财利丰盈，今财稍薄不如前。 | 午后占得，宜早点求，财利丰厚，现在的财比先前稍薄。 | cast after noon, seek it early; the profit is ample, though the money now is a little thinner than before. |
> | 若前卦财衰而化财旺，反此断之必无差。 | 如果本卦的财衰而化出的财旺，就反过来断，一定不会错。 | If the original Wealth is weak and the Wealth it turns into is strong, judge the other way round and you will not err. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 《断易天机》本的注比《卜筮大全》本详细，而且是穿插着多出来的，所以两段都全文引出，方便对照。
>
> *Note:* The Duanyi Tianji commentary is fuller than the Bushi Daquan one, with its extra sentences woven through, so both are quoted in full for comparison.
