# 内财衰而外财旺，宜往他乡

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦财衰、外卦财旺，或外卦财旺而生合世爻、持世：宜到外地卖。财不生合世爻或世爻动而化凶：外地再好，我去也遇不上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth weak in the inner trigram and strong in the outer, or strong in the outer and generating, combining with or sitting on the Self line: sell elsewhere. If Wealth does not generate or combine with the Self line, or the Self line moves into harm, however good the other place, you will not find your spring there.
