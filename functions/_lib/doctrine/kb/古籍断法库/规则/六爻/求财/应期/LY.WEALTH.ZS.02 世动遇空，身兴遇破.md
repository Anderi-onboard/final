# 世动遇空，身兴遇破

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谒贵：世爻动而化空、化破，不作凶论，是应期——应在到达那里的月日（书中例：官星持世而空，出空之日见到，财利如意）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Calling on the great: the Self line moving into the void or into a Month-break is not bad; it marks the month or day you arrive (case in the book: Pressure on the Self line was void, and the meeting came on the day it left the void, with the money as wished).

## 《增删卜易·谒贵求财章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11263 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Calling on the great for money", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世动遇空，身兴遇破。 | 世爻动而逢空，世身动而逢破。 | The Self line moving into the void; the Self stirring into a Month-break. |
> | 世动化空破者，勿曰不吉，乃应到彼之月日也。 | 世爻动而化空、化破，不要说不吉，那是应在到达那里的月日。 | If the Self line moves and turns void or broken, do not call it unlucky: it marks the month or day you arrive there. |
