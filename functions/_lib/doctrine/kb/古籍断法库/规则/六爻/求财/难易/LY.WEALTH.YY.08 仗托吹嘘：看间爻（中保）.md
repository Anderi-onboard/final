# 仗托吹嘘：看间爻（中保）

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 靠人说合（仗托吹嘘）看间爻（中人、保人）：死绝受伤则中保无能；生合世爻善成全，刑克世爻多欺骗；带兄鬼玄武冲伤为奸人阻隔，带贵人生合为贵人做主；应克间，对方起疑；世伤间，我心怀怨；两间兄动，争做中人；朱雀兄弟旁动伤世克间，有人破坏；间爻空，没有中保。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When it depends on someone putting in a word, read the between lines (go-between and guarantor): dead or hurt, they are useless; generating or combining with the Self, they see it through; punishing or controlling it, they cheat; with Peer, Pressure or the Dark Tortoise clashing, villains block it; with the Noble star generating, a person of rank takes charge; the Other controlling them, the other side grows suspicious; the Self hurting them, you bear a grudge; both between lines Peer and moving, people compete to be the go-between; the Vermilion Bird on a moving Peer elsewhere hurting the Self and controlling the between line, someone spoils it; a void between line, no go-between.
