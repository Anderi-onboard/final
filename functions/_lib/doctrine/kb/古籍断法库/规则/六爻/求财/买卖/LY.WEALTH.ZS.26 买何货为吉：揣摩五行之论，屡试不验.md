# 买何货为吉：揣摩五行之论，屡试不验

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买什么货：古法以财爻五行、六神定货类（金珠宝、水鱼盐、火陶冶果实、木竹木、土五谷；青龙喜庆、白虎丧葬屠宰等），野鹤认为是揣摩之论、屡试不验；要指定某一货物单独占（书中例：旅卦六合、世应相生，只断此行顺遂，不断买何物，后买檀香翠羽都获大利）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Which goods to buy: the old method reads the goods from the element and spirit of the Wealth line (Metal jewels, Water fish and salt, Fire pottery and fruit, Wood timber, Earth grain; Azure Dragon festive goods, White Tiger funeral and butchery goods, and so on); Yehe holds this speculation that fails when tested; name a particular kind of goods and cast for it (case: the hexagram Lü in Six-Combine with Self and Other generating, read only as 'the journey will go well'; he bought sandalwood and kingfisher feathers and profited on both).

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 野鹤曰：古以金财而像珠宝玉石，水财以类鱼盐，火财陶冶果实，木财竹木，土财五谷。 | 野鹤说：古法以金财象珠宝玉石，水财象鱼盐，火财象陶冶和果实，木财象竹木，土财象五谷。 | Yehe says: the old method took Metal Wealth for jewels, pearls and jade, Water Wealth for fish and salt, Fire Wealth for pottery, smelting and fruit, Wood Wealth for bamboo and timber, Earth Wealth for grain. |
> | 青龙木利，又兼喜庆之用；白虎金财，丧仪屠宰之物；玄武水利；朱雀火宜；螣蛇利于出入；勾陈利于农工。 | 青龙利木，又兼喜庆用品；白虎金财，是丧葬、屠宰用物；玄武利水；朱雀宜火；螣蛇利于进出买卖；勾陈利于农工。 | The Azure Dragon favours wood and festive goods; the White Tiger with Metal Wealth, funeral and butchery goods; the Dark Tortoise favours water; the Vermilion Bird, fire; the Coiling Snake, buying and selling; the Hook Earth, farming and crafts. |
> | 余以为此乃揣摩五行之论，非经验者也，屡试不验，间有验者，亦偶尔凑合耳，不可为法， | 我认为这是揣摩五行的推论，不是验证过的，屡试不灵，偶尔灵了也只是碰巧，不能当法则； | I hold this to be speculation from the five elements, not experience; tested again and again it fails, and when it works it is chance; it cannot be a rule; |
> | 必须意属何类，指其事而占之，方能有效。 | 一定要心里想好是哪一类货，指明这件事来占，才会灵验。 | you must decide which kind of goods you mean and divine for that specific thing; only then does it work. |
