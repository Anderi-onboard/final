# 囤积：审看脱手时的衰旺；春脱宜木象乘财，冬售恶火爻为用；占时财神要有生扶、无破坏

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 囤积货物，要看脱手那个时节财的衰旺，养殖种植也同这样看；春天出手的，宜木象的财旺；冬天卖出的，忌火爻作用神。注：要考察占卦当时，财神也要有生扶、无破坏，日后遇上那个时节才得厚利；如果占的时候本来破散、没有救援，即使日后遇上时节，利也薄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In stockpiling, examine the thriving or weakness of Wealth at the time the goods will be sold, and raising and planting are read the same way; for what is sold in spring a Wood image riding on Wealth is fitting, and for what is sold in winter a Fire line as the Subject is disliked. The note adds: the Wealth spirit at the time of the divination must also have support and no breakage, and only then, meeting that season later, is the profit rich; if at the time of the divination it was already scattered and without rescue, then even meeting the season later the profit is thin.
