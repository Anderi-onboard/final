# 卦得反吟多反复，冲中变合再重兴

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开店：卦得反吟，反复不定，已开久的店主迁移变更；六冲变六合、爻逢六合，或世与财、子成三合，关后重兴；六合变六冲，眼前热闹，终久萧条。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Opening a shop: a Reversed Chant hexagram means back and forth, and a long-established shop will move or change hands; Six-Clash changing into Six-Combine, combining lines, or the Self line in a three-harmony frame with Wealth and Output, it will close and flourish again; Six-Combine changing into Six-Clash, busy now and deserted in the end.
