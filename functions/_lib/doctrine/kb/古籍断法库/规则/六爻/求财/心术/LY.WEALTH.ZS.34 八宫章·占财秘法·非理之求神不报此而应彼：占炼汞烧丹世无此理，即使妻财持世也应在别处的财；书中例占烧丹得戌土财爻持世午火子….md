# 八宫章·占财秘法·非理之求神不报此而应彼：占炼汞烧丹世无此理，即使妻财持世也应在别处的财；书中例占烧丹得戌土财爻持世午火子孙动，只说戌月成丹，却是九月妻妾子女从数千里外来团聚；占地下忽见异物，妻财持世必有金银，兄弟持世不独无财且有破财，官鬼持世必是妖邪

六爻 · 求财 · 心术 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占炼汞烧丹，世上没有这个道理，卦不必占，念头也不要起，即使卦得妻财持世，也应在另外地方的财。曾经有人占烧丹，得戌土财爻持世，午火子孙发动相生，只以为戌月一定成丹，哪知道九月妻子、妾、子女从几千里外来，异乡团聚。戌土妻财，应妻妾；午火子孙，应子女。这是占这件应那件，《易》是为君子谋的，难道不可信吗？所以不合道理的求取，神灵不报这一件而应到那一件上。占地下忽然见到有光彩的异物，或者现出黄白奇形，怀疑有财的，如果妻财持世，必有金银；兄弟持世，不但没有财，而且有破财的事；如果官鬼持世，必是妖邪。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining for refining mercury and burning cinnabar: there is no such principle in the world, so the hexagram need not be divined and the thought should not even be raised; even if the hexagram gives Wealth holding the Self, it answers on wealth elsewhere. Someone once divined about burning cinnabar and got Wealth Xu-earth holding the Self with Output Wu-fire moving to give life to it, and thought it meant that the cinnabar would surely be done in the Xu month; he did not know that in the ninth month his wife, his concubine and his children would come from thousands of li away and be reunited with him in a strange land. Xu-earth Wealth answers to wife and concubine; Wu-fire Output answers to children. This is divining one matter and having another answer; the Changes are for the benefit of the noble man, and can it be disbelieved? So for a seeking that is against reason the spirits do not report that matter but answer on another. In divining when one suddenly sees a strange shining object in the ground, or a yellow or white form, and suspects there is treasure, if Wealth holds the Self there is surely gold or silver; if Peer holds the Self, there is not only no wealth but a matter of losing money; if Pressure holds the Self, it is surely an evil spirit.
