# 博戏求财：子孙出现、世临它，再旺相无刑克，称心而归；眉批：子孙持世财旺得遂，鬼持世福星无益

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博求财，子孙出现在卦上、世临着它，再加旺相、没有刑克，一定高高兴兴称心而归。眉批：子孙持世，财旺才能如愿；鬼爻持世，福星也没有用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In gambling for wealth, with Output showing in the hexagram and the Self sitting on it, thriving and free of punishment or control, you will surely come home glad and satisfied. The marginal comment: with Output on the Self, Wealth thriving is fulfilled; with a Pressure line on the Self, even the Fortune star is of no use.

## 《海底眼·博戏》原文

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 666 行 · Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Gambling", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 博戏求财皆是木， | 赌博求财，全是木（疑是「本」，本钱）； | For gambling for wealth it is all Wood [probably a miscopy of 'stake']; |
> | 子孙出现世临之， | 子孙出现，世临着它； | Output showing and the Self sitting on it; |
> | 更兼旺相无刑克， | 再加旺相、没有刑克， | thriving as well, with no punishment or control: |
> | 管取欢欣称意归。 | 保管欢欢喜喜、称心而回。 | you will come home glad and satisfied. |

^q1

## 《海底眼》眉批(书眉上的旁注,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 666 行 · Marginal comment in the Haidi Yan (author unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙持世， | 子孙持世， | With Output on the Self, |
> | 财旺得遂， | 财旺才能如愿； | Wealth thriving is fulfilled; |
> | 鬼爻持世， | 鬼爻持世， | with a Pressure line on the Self, |
> | 福星无益。 | 福星也没有用。 | even the Fortune star is of no use. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 第一句「皆是木」，《断易天机》同一首作「财是本」（见 DYTJ.09），「木」疑是「本」之误；这里照原文，译文里注明。「福星」指福德，眉批里的「福星」不一定就是子孙，书里没有说。
>
> *Note:* The first line's 'all Wood' reads 'Wealth is the stake' in the Duanyi Tianji's version of the same verse (see DYTJ.09), so 'Wood' is probably a miscopy of 'stake'; the original is kept, and the translation says so. 'The Fortune star' is Output; the book does not say more.
