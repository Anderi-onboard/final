# 日辰冲应加朱雀动，被谗言破阻；日月生用神加青龙福德，财满钱袋

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰冲应，或再加朱雀交重（发动），一定被谗言破坏阻挠；日月生用神，或再加青龙、福德同在一处，一定财满钱袋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Day clashing the Other, or the Vermilion Bird added and moving: it is surely wrecked and blocked by slander; the Day and Month generating the Subject, or the Azure Dragon and Output added on the same line: the purse is surely full of money.

> | 日辰冲应遭人破， | 日辰冲应，会被人破坏； | The Day clashing the Other, someone wrecks it; |
> | 月建扶财囊可盈。 | 月建扶助财，钱袋可以装满。 | the Month supporting Wealth, the purse can be filled. |
