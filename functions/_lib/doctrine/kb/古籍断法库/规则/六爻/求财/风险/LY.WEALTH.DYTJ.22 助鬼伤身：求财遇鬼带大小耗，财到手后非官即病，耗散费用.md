# 助鬼伤身：求财遇鬼带大小耗，财到手后非官即病，耗散费用

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 能生助官鬼的只有妻财；它不宜发动，一动，衰鬼变成旺鬼，旺鬼更凶。求财遇到鬼带大耗、小耗，这笔财到手以后，不是惹官司就是生病，钱都花耗了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The only line that can generate and help Pressure is Wealth; it should not move, for once it moves a weak Pressure becomes strong and a strong Pressure becomes more fierce. In seeking wealth, meeting Pressure carrying the Great or Small Waste stars, after this money is gained there is either a lawsuit or an illness and the money is spent away.

> [!note] 按
> 书里这一节的末了说：若有子孙发动、解神来救，可以反凶成吉。这一条只取求财。
>
> *Note:* At the end of this section the book adds that if Output moves and a relieving spirit comes to the rescue, the ominous can turn auspicious. This entry takes only wealth.
