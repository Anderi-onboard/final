# 寄放财物：应伤衰世，他非善；世随官鬼入墓，我不祥；以财爻为主象，忌空亡，静久无损、动有变

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寄放：世是我、应是对方，世不可空；旺相的应伤克衰弱的世，他一定不是善良之辈；世跟着官鬼入墓，我不吉祥。货物一概以财爻为主象，忌落空亡、没有气；财明现而沉静，就长久没有损失，一兴发就有变动的兆头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving goods with someone: the Self is me and the Other is him, and the Self must not be void; a strong Other hurting and controlling a weak Self, he is surely no good sort; the Self following Pressure into the tomb, it is not auspicious for me. Goods are all judged with the Wealth line as the main image, which must not fall void or lack breath; Wealth showing and still, there is no loss for a long time, and once it stirs there is a sign of change.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> | 若被旺应伤克衰世，他必非是善良之辈。 | 如果旺相的应伤克衰弱的世，他一定不是善良之辈； | if a strong Other hurts and controls a weak Self, he is surely no good sort; |

> | 静无后患之辞，动有变爻之论。 | 静就没有后患可说，动就有变爻的说法； | still, there is no aftertrouble to speak of; moving, there is the matter of the changed line; |
