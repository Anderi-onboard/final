# 冲克妻财：即是系占财，铺店金银买卖来，或有阴人成合事，金银钱铜器和钗

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 冲克妻财，就是关系到占财的事：开铺店、金银、买卖，或者有女人参与的合作事，以及金银钱铜器和钗的交易。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A clash or control upon Wealth means a matter tied to divining for wealth: shops, gold and silver, buying and selling, or a joint matter involving a woman, and dealings in gold, silver, coin, copper ware and hairpins.

> [!note] 按
> 《断易天机》来情章里同一句作「冲克即是非占财」（DYTJ.36）；这里作「冲妻即是系占财」，「系」是关系到的意思，读起来更顺。两处只差一个字，「非」和「系」哪一个是原字，书里没有别的证据，本库不裁。
>
> *Note:* The Duanyi Tianji's chapter on what the visit is about has the same line as 'a clash or control is disputes, and divining wealth' (DYTJ.36); here it reads 'a clash on Wealth is tied to divining wealth', in which 'tied to' reads more smoothly. The two differ in one character, and the books give no further evidence about which is original; this library does not decide.
