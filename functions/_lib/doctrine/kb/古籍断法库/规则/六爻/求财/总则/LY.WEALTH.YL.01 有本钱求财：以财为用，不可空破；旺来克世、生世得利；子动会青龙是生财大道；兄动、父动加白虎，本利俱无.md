# 有本钱求财：以财为用，不可空破；旺来克世、生世得利；子动会青龙是生财大道；兄动、父动加白虎，本利俱无

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有本钱去求财，以财爻为用神：不能空无，逢旬空月破，不但没有利，连本也亏；财旺来克世爻，利息滚滚，生世，财源滚滚。子孙发动是生财之神，会青龙是生财的大道；兄弟动而子孙不动，本利都没有；父母动、兄弟不动再加白虎，克伤子孙这个元神，财得不到生助，是断利的根源。福德发动财源增，兄弟发动资本亏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking money with capital, Wealth is the Subject: it must not be void or absent; with Void or Month-break there is no profit and the capital is lost too. Wealth strong and controlling the Self brings interest in a flood, and generating the Self, a flowing source of wealth. Output moving is the spirit that gives birth to Wealth, and meeting the Azure Dragon it is the great road to wealth; Peer moving with Output still leaves neither capital nor profit; Resource moving with Peer still and the White Tiger added controls Output, the Support of Wealth, so Wealth has nothing to feed it and the root of profit is cut. Output moving increases the source of wealth; Peer moving wounds the capital.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1933 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 将财求利，财为用， | 有本钱去求利，以财爻为用神， | Seeking profit with capital, Wealth is the Subject; |
> | 凡值旬空月破，凶 | 凡是碰上旬空、月破，凶； | whenever it meets Void or Month-break, ill; |
> | 旺相生身兼死世。 | 旺相，生扶卦身，又……（「死世」读不通，下面的解说的是「旺来克世」，疑是「克世」之误）； | strong, generating the Body, and also 'dead Self' (probably a slip for 'controlling the Self', as the commentary below reads it); |
> | 金银倍得利无穷， | 金银加倍到手，利润无穷； | gold and silver come in double and the profit has no end; |
> | 财源增益。摇福德 | 财源不断增加，是福德发动的时候； | the source of wealth grows when Output moves; |
> | 资本亏伤，动弟兄 | 资本亏伤，是兄弟发动的时候； | the capital is wounded when Peer moves; |
> | 父发助兄能克子， | 父母发动，帮着兄弟去克子孙； | Resource moving helps Peer to control Output; |
> | 生涯有始定无终。 | 生意有开头，一定没有结果。 | the business will have a beginning and surely no end. |

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1934 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 将本求财，以财为用，不可空无， | 有本钱求财，以财爻为用神，不能落空或没有； | Seeking money with capital, Wealth is the Subject and must not be void or absent; |
> | 如遇旬空月破，不但无利，本亦亏折。 | 碰上旬空、月破，不但没有利润，本钱也要亏掉。 | if it meets Void or Month-break, there is no profit and the capital is lost too. |
> | 如若旺来克世者，利息津津。 | 如果财旺来克世，利息滚滚而来； | If a strong Wealth comes to control the Self, the interest flows in; |
> | 生世者，财源滚滚。 | 生世的，财源滚滚。 | if it generates the Self, the source of wealth rolls on. |
> | 若得子孙发动，乃生财之神，财如源水流而不竭， | 如果子孙发动，那是生财的神，财就像源头的水流个不停； | If Output moves, it is the spirit that gives birth to Wealth, and money is like water from a spring that never runs dry; |
> | 故经云：子动会青龙，乃生财之大道， | 所以经上说：子孙动而会青龙，是生财的大道； | so the classic says: 'Output moving and meeting the Azure Dragon is the great road to wealth'; |
> | 若兄动而子不动者，本利俱无， | 如果兄弟动而子孙不动，本钱和利润都没有； | if Peer moves and Output does not, neither capital nor profit remains; |
> | 若父动而兄不动更加白虎，乃在克子孙元神，财无生助， | 如果父母动、兄弟不动，再加白虎，那是克伤子孙这个元神，财得不到生助， | if Resource moves and Peer does not, and the White Tiger is added, it controls Output, the Support of Wealth, and Wealth has nothing to feed it, |
> | 故经云：父兴临白虎，为绝利之根源也。 | 所以经上说：父母兴旺而临白虎，是断绝利润的根源。 | so the classic says: 'Resource rising on the White Tiger cuts the root of profit.' |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这首诗在原书里标点错位：按七字一句，应是八句——「将财求利财为用，凡值旬空月破凶，旺相生身兼死世，金银倍得利无穷，财源增益摇福德，资本亏伤动弟兄，父发助兄能克子，生涯有始定无终」。这里原文照抄原来的标点，译文按七字句读。第三句「死世」读不通，解里说的是「旺来克世」，疑是「克世」之误。
>
> *Note:* In the source the punctuation of this poem is off: read seven characters to a line it is eight lines. The original text is kept with its own punctuation and translated by the seven-character lines. In the third line 'dead Self' cannot be read, and the commentary speaks of a strong Wealth 'coming to control the Self', so a slip for 'control' is likely.
