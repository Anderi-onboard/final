# 财爻死绝要等日辰生它，太旺太多要等墓日

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻落在衰、绝之地，要日辰来生扶，那一天才是能得到的日期；财爻太旺太多，必定要到墓日才有。经上说，财逢墓库便可归还，多财反复，必须墓库收藏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth in decline or extinction needs the Day to generate or support it, and that day is when it can be had; Wealth too strong and too many comes on a tomb day. The classic says: Wealth meeting the tomb-store can be brought home; abundant Wealth is unsteady and needs the tomb-store to hold it.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1948 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 死绝财爻生日得， | 死绝的财爻，逢生它的日子才得； | A dead or extinct Wealth line gets its money on the day that generates it; |
> | 太旺财爻死墓来。 | 太旺的财爻，要到死墓的日子才来。 | an over-strong Wealth line comes on the day of death or tomb. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1949 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财爻逢衰绝之地，必得日辰生扶，乃可得之期。 | 财爻落在衰、绝之地，一定要日辰来生扶，才是能得到的日期； | When Wealth is in decline or extinction, the Day must generate or support it, and that is the day it can be had; |
> | 若财爻太旺太多，决定墓日免有。 | 如果财爻太旺太多，一定要到墓日才有。 | if Wealth is too strong and too many, it will be on a tomb day that there is any. |
> | 故经云：财逢墓库便可归还， | 所以经上说：财逢墓库，便可以归还（到手）； | So the classic says: Wealth meeting the tomb-store can be brought home; |
> | 又云多财反覆，必须墓库以收藏。 | 又说：多财反复，必须用墓库收藏。 | and again: abundant Wealth is unsteady and needs the tomb-store to hold it. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「决定墓日免有」的「免」疑是「方」（然后）之误，译成「一定要到墓日才有」。
>
> *Note:* 'Exempt from having' in 'it is decided that on a tomb day there is exemption' is probably a slip for 'then there is', and is translated as 'it will be on a tomb day that there is any'.
