# 赌博求财：内克外、世克应胜，外克内、应克世输

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博：内克外、世克应，或财子旺持世生合世，胜（旺世克衰应小胜，旺应生衰世大胜）；外克内、应克世、财官俱无、世空破墓绝，输；世空动缺庄家，应空无对手；世应比和不分胜负，俱空两无所得；世上子孙化父因骄而败，化兄得助而赢；世居阴卦阴爻宜守，阳爻阳卦宜争先。应爻所临六神与六亲定对方手段：内外两鬼动伤世合局骗，间爻兄动抽头多，应带龙喜生世得人提携，虎刃克世斗殴，玄兄伤世设局偷筹，勾鬼伤世牵连不清，蛇火鬼伤世药骰，雀兄伤世因财争斗，雀兄化鬼伤世有人告发；世持雀鬼化父或雀父化鬼，因赌成讼，加太岁讼经年。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Gambling: inner controlling outer, Self controlling Other, or a strong Wealth or Output on or generating the Self, a win (strong Self over weak Other a small win, strong Other generating weak Self a big one); outer controlling inner, Other controlling Self, no Wealth or Pressure, or the Self void, broken, entombed or extinguished, a loss; Self void and moving, no banker; Other void, no opponent; in harmony, no winner; both void, nothing gained; Output on the Self turning into Resource, pride loses; into Peer, help wins; the Self yin in a yin trigram, hold back; yang in yang, press on. The Other's spirit and relation show how they play you: two Pressure lines against the Self, a set-up; a moving between Peer, a heavy house cut; the Other with Dragon and Joy generating the Self, someone helps you; Tiger and Blade controlling it, a brawl; Tortoise and Peer, a rigged game or swapped counters; Hook and Pressure, dragged into something murky; Snake and Fire Pressure, loaded dice; Bird and Peer, a fight over money; Bird and Peer turning into Pressure, someone informs; the Self on Bird and Pressure turning into Resource, or Bird and Resource into Pressure, gambling ends in a lawsuit, a year long with the Tai Sui.

> | 外克内，应克世，或财鬼两无，或世逢空破墓绝者，输也。 | 外克内、应克世，或财、官都没有，或世逢空破墓绝，输。 | Outer controlling inner, the Other controlling the Self, no Wealth and no Pressure, or the Self void, broken, entombed or extinguished: a loss. |
> | 世空动者，缺管头。应空者，无对手。 | 世空而动，缺少做庄的；应空，没有对手。 | Self void and moving: no one to run the game; Other void: no opponent. |
> | 世应比和，两无胜负。世应俱空，两无所得。 | 世应比和，不分胜负；世应俱空，双方都得不到什么。 | Self and Other in harmony: no winner; both void: neither gains. |
> | 世子化父，以骄取败。世子化兄，得助而赢。 | 世上的子孙化父母，因骄傲而败；世上的子孙化兄弟，得人相助而赢。 | Output on the Self line turning into Resource: pride brings defeat; turning into Peer: help brings victory. |
> | 世居阴卦阴爻，宜退守也。世入阳爻阳卦，宜争先也。 | 世在阴卦阴爻，宜退守；世在阳爻阳卦，宜抢先。 | The Self on a yin line in a yin trigram: hold back; on a yang line in a yang trigram: press forward. |
> | 内外两鬼动伤世者，彼此合局来骗也。 | 内外两个官鬼都动来伤世，是对方合伙设局来骗。 | Two Pressure lines, inner and outer, moving to hurt the Self: they are in league to cheat you. |
> | 间爻动者，多撞来。间兄动者，抽头多。 | 间爻动，多有人闯进来；间爻兄弟动，抽头（抽成）多。 | A between line moving: people barge in; a between Peer moving: a heavy house cut. |
> | 应带龙喜生世，得人提拔。应加虎刃克世，与人斗殴。 | 应爻带青龙、天喜生世，得人提携；应爻带白虎、羊刃克世，和人打架。 | The Other with the Dragon and Joy stars generating the Self: someone gives you a leg up; with the Tiger and the Blade controlling the Self: a fistfight. |
> | 应加玄兄伤世，被人局骗，或轮筹窃马。 | 应爻带玄武、兄弟伤世，被人设局骗，或偷换筹码。 | With the Tortoise and Peer hurting the Self: you are set up, or the counters are switched. |
> | 应加勾鬼伤世，主牵连不明白也。。 | 应爻带勾陈官鬼伤世，主被牵连、说不清楚。 | With Hook Earth and Pressure hurting the Self: you are dragged into something murky. |
> | 应加蛇火鬼伤世，被药骰骗也。 | 应爻带螣蛇、火鬼伤世，被灌了药的骰子骗。 | With the Snake and a Fire Pressure hurting the Self: loaded dice. |
> | 应加雀兄伤世，因财争斗也。 | 应爻带朱雀兄弟伤世，因钱争斗。 | With the Bird and Peer hurting the Self: a fight over money. |
> | 应加雀兄动化鬼伤世，同伴出首。旁爻雀兄动化鬼伤世，他人出首。 | 应爻带朱雀兄弟动化官鬼伤世，同伴去告发；旁爻朱雀兄弟动化官鬼伤世，别人去告发。 | The Other with the Bird and Peer moving into Pressure against the Self: your companion informs on you; another line doing so: an outsider informs. |
> | 世持雀鬼化父，或雀父化鬼者，皆因赌成讼。若加太岁，讼必经年也。 | 世爻持朱雀官鬼化父母，或朱雀父母化官鬼，都是因赌成讼；再加太岁，官司一定拖一整年。 | The Self on the Bird and Pressure turning into Resource, or the Bird and Resource turning into Pressure: gambling ends in a lawsuit; with the Tai Sui as well, the case drags on a year. |
