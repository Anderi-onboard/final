# 世空我心变，应陷他意不真；应克世爻、外伤内卦，对方狠心欺赖、忘恩负义

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世空，我自己不情愿；应空，对方灰心；世应两空，彼此都算了。应克世爻，他必定狠着心欺赖；外卦伤内卦，也是对方不好，必然忘恩负义。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Self void: I am unwilling; Other void: he loses heart; both void: both are done with it. The Other controlling the Self line: he will harden his heart and cheat and default; the outer trigram hurting the inner is also bad on his part, and he will forget kindness and betray your trust.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世空我必心多变， | 世空，我必定心里多变； | Self void: my own mind must be changeable; |
> | 应陷他须意不真， | 应陷空，他的心意必然不真； | Other sunk: his intention must be insincere; |
> | 应克世爻遭虎噬， | 应克世爻，会被老虎咬噬（吃大亏）； | the Other controlling the Self line: bitten by a tiger; |
> | 外伤内卦被鲸吞。 | 外卦伤内卦，被鲸鱼吞掉。 | the outer trigram hurting the inner is swallowed by a whale. |
