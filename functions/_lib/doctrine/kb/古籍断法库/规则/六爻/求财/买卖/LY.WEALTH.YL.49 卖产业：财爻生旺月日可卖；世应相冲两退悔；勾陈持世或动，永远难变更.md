# 卖产业：财爻生旺月日可卖；世应相冲两退悔；勾陈持世或动，永远难变更

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 变卖产业：只看财爻生旺的月份、日子，就可以卖出去，财爻衰弱终究难成交；世应相冲，双方都反悔，成交不了；勾陈是管田地的，又是迟滞之神，持世或发动，很难变更，永远搬不动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Selling property: look only at the months and days when the Wealth line is born or strong, and it can be sold; with a weak Wealth line the deal is hard to close; Self and Other clashing, both sides regret and the deal does not close; Hook Earth rules fields and is the spirit of delay, and sitting on the Self or moving it is very hard to change and stays put for good.
