# 猫犬彘豕羊鹿：鬼依二三四爻、寅午未戌亥位为凶，发于坎兑艮宫不利；子孙喜旺，白虎喜宁

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 猫、犬、猪、羊、鹿这类，没有不以鬼依在二、三、四爻和寅、午、未、戌、亥位为凶的，鬼发在坎、兑、艮宫也不利，而子孙喜欢旺，白虎喜欢宁静。注：二爻是猫犬，三爻是猪，四爻是羊鹿，寅是猫、午是鹿之类。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For cats, dogs, pigs, sheep and deer, Pressure resting on the second, third or fourth line, or on the Yin, Wu, Wei, Xu or Hai positions, is always ominous, and Pressure moving in the Kan, Dui or Gen palace is unfavourable, while Output likes to be thriving and the White Tiger likes to be quiet. The note adds: the second line is cats and dogs, the third pigs, the fourth sheep and deer, and Yin is the cat and Wu the deer, and so on.
