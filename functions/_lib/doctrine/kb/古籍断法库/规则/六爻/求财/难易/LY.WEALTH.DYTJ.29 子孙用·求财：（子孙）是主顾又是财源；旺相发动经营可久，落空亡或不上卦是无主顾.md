# 子孙用·求财：（子孙）是主顾又是财源；旺相发动经营可久，落空亡或不上卦是无主顾

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求财里，子孙爻是主顾，又是财源：旺相发动，经营可以长久，绵绵不断；落空亡或者不上卦，就是没有主顾；妻财再旺也只许一次，再难成就，好比水没有源头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking wealth the Output line is the customer and also the source of wealth: thriving and moving, the business can last, endlessly; falling Void or not appearing in the hexagram, there are no customers; even a thriving Wealth allows only one time, after which it is hard to bring about, like water with no source.
