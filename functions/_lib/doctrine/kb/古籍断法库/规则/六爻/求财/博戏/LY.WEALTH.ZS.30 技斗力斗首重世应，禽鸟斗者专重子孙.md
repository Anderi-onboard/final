# 技斗力斗首重世应，禽鸟斗者专重子孙

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 博戏：比技、比力看世应，赌钱兼看财爻；世临日月、财得日月生扶、世动化吉、应带财生世，我胜，反之他胜；兄动破财，鬼克世有灾。斗禽专看子孙：旺者胜；休囚空破、受克害刑冲，或卦中父母动，多败（书中例：父母当令的巳午时斗，大败）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Contests: skill or strength, read Self and Other; with money staked, the Wealth line too. The Self on the Day or Month branch, Wealth supported by them, the Self moving into good, the Other carrying Wealth and generating the Self: I win; the reverse, he wins. Peer moving, money lost; Pressure controlling the Self, disaster. Bird fights: Output alone; strong wins; resting, trapped, void, broken, controlled, clashed, or Resource moving in the hexagram, mostly loses (case: fighting at the hours when Resource was in season, a heavy defeat).

> | 世喜临于日月，财宜日月生扶， | 世爻喜临日月，财爻宜得日月生扶； | The Self line is best on the Day or Month branch, Wealth best generated or supported by them; |
> | 世爻动而化吉，应带财而生世，皆我胜也；反此者，必系他赢。 | 世爻动而化吉，应爻带财来生世，都是我赢；相反的，一定是对方赢。 | the Self line moving into something good, or the Other line carrying Wealth and generating the Self: I win; the reverse, he wins. |
> | 兄动卦中，破财之兆；鬼爻克世，灾祸相侵。 | 兄弟在卦中发动，是破财之兆；官鬼克世，灾祸临身。 | Peer moving in the hexagram: a sign of losing money; the Pressure line controlling the Self line: disaster. |
> | 有禽鸟斗者，专重子孙，子孙旺者，必胜； | 斗鸟斗禽的，专看子孙，子孙旺的一定赢； | For fights between birds, look only at Output: strong Output wins; |
> | 休囚空破，或被克害刑冲，及卦中父动者，多被其伤。 | 子孙休囚空破，或受克害刑冲，以及卦中父母发动的，多半被对方所伤。 | resting, trapped, void or broken, or controlled, harmed, punished or clashed, or with Resource moving in the hexagram, it will mostly be hurt. |
