# 路上有官，休出外

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 第五爻为道路：临官鬼发动，途中多惊险，不宜出外求财，灾祸看所临六神（白虎风波、玄武盗贼）；道路爻空亡也不宜出外；临财、福则吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The fifth line is the road: Pressure on it and moving means many dangers on the way, so do not go abroad for money; the kind of trouble shows in its spirit (White Tiger, storms; Dark Tortoise, thieves); a void road line is also against travel; Wealth or Output on it is good.
