# 月带财神，卦虽无而月中必有

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中无财而月建是财：这个月内必有财，月建旺财纵遇兄弟也不怕；财再出现在卦上更好。王洪绪：月建扶起伏藏的财，到那一爻值日可得。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram has no Wealth but the Month is Wealth, money will come within the month, and a Wealth this strong fears no Peer line; better still if Wealth also shows in the hexagram. Wang Hongxu: the Month lifts the hidden Wealth line, and the money arrives on the day that branch comes round.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7779 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 月带财神， | 月建带着财， | When the Month carries Wealth, |
> | 卦虽无，而月中必有； | 卦里虽然没有财，这个月里一定会有； | even if the hexagram has none, it will come within the month; |

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 月建为提纲。 | 月建是总纲。 | The Month is the master key. |
> | 若带财爻得时有气，纵遇兄弟，衰不敌旺，必主财利厚。 | 月建若是财爻，当令有力，即使碰上兄弟，弱的敌不过旺的，一定财利丰厚。 | If the Month carries the Wealth line, in season and full of force, then even against a Peer line the weak cannot beat the strong, and the profit will be rich. |
> | 虽卦中无财，月中必然有。 | 即使卦里没有财，这个月里也一定会有。 | Even with no Wealth in the hexagram, it will come within the month. |

^q2
