# 多财反复，必须墓库以收藏

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻太多（三五重，或临日月、动变俱是财）为太过，反复难求；要有财的墓库来收：见财的墓库爻发动或持世（旧注、王洪绪），或等财爻入墓之日（野鹤）。财化财也主反复。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Too much Wealth (three to five lines, or coinciding with Day and Month, or moving and changed lines all Wealth) is excess and goes back and forth; it needs Wealth's tomb to gather it in: Wealth's tomb line moving or on the Self line (old commentary, Wang Hongxu), or the day Wealth enters its tomb (Yehe). Wealth turning into Wealth also means back and forth.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7786 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦中财只一位，有气不空，生合持世皆美。 | 卦里财爻只有一个，有气、不空，生合世爻或持世，都好。 | One Wealth line in the hexagram, with force and not void, generating, combining with or sitting on the Self line, is good in every way. |
> | 若三五重太过，其财反复难求。 | 如果有三五个，太多了，这财就反复不定，难求。 | Three to five of them is too much: the money goes back and forth and is hard to get. |
> | 必须卦中有财库爻发动，谓财有库藏，必得厚利。 | 一定要卦里有财的墓库爻发动，叫作财有库藏，才会得厚利。 | It needs Wealth's tomb line moving in the hexagram; that is called Wealth having a storehouse, and the profit will be rich. |
> | 财化财，亦主反复不定。 | 财化财，也主反复不定。 | Wealth turning into Wealth also means back-and-forth uncertainty. |

^q2

> | --- | --- | --- |

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11096–11098 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 多财反覆，必须墓库以收藏。 | 财太多就反复，一定要等墓库把它收起来。 | Too much Wealth goes back and forth; it must be gathered in by its tomb. |
> | 卦中财临日月，谓之太旺， | 卦里财爻临日辰或月建，叫作太旺； | When the Wealth line in the hexagram coincides with the Day or Month, it is called over-strong; |
> | 或动变俱是财爻，日月又作财星，谓之重叠， | 或者动爻、变爻都是财爻，日月又是财，叫作重叠； | or when moving and changed lines are all Wealth lines and the Day and Month are Wealth as well, it is called piled up; |
> | 若要求谋遂心，须待财爻入墓。 | 要想求得如愿，要等到财爻入墓的时候。 | to get what you seek, wait until the Wealth line enters its tomb. |
> | 且如水作财神，辰日而得，余仿此。 | 比如财属水，就在辰日得（辰是水的墓），其余照此类推。 | For instance, if Wealth is Water, the money comes on a Chen day (Water's tomb); the rest by analogy. |

^q4

> [!warning] 打架
> 「墓库收藏」三家读法不同：旧注要卦里有财的墓库爻发动；王洪绪要财的库爻持世或临卦身；野鹤不看卦里有没有库爻，改成应期——等财爻入墓的那一天。
>
> *Where they disagree:* They read 'gathered in by the tomb' three ways: the old commentary wants Wealth's tomb line moving in the hexagram; Wang Hongxu wants that tomb line on the Self or Body line; Yehe ignores whether the hexagram has one and turns it into timing: the money comes on the day Wealth enters its tomb.
