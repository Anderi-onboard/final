# 渔猎：世值旬空月破自无技能；兄弟发动，猎物灵异远遁难捕

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 渔猎：世落旬空、月破，是自己没有好本事；兄弟发动，那一定是灵禽异兽、怪鱼智龟一类，终究高飞远逃，难以捕获。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fishing and hunting: the Self in Void or Month-break means one has no good skill; Peer moving means a spirit bird, a strange beast, a weird fish or a clever turtle, which in the end flies high and flees far and is hard to take.

## 《易林补遗·捕猎畋渔章第一百十三》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2063 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 113 - Fishing and hunting", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世落旬空兄弟动， | 世落旬空、兄弟发动， | The Self falls into Void and Peer moves, |
> | 纵然有物不收藏。 | 即使有猎物也收不到。 | even if there is quarry, none is gathered in. |

^q1
