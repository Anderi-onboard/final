# 鬼兄发动，有制何妨；随墓助伤，多凶少吉

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟、官鬼发动，有制则无妨（子孙制鬼，或日月冲克鬼爻）；兄、鬼同动时，官鬼反能制兄、护财（觉子）。忌世随鬼入墓、世动变凶；做买卖虽宜财动，官鬼克世时财动反成「助鬼伤身」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer and Pressure moving do no harm if restrained (Output holding Pressure down, or the Day or Month clashing or controlling it); when Peer and Pressure move together, Pressure actually restrains Peer and shields the money (Juezi). Beware of the Self line following the ghost into the tomb or moving into harm; in trade a moving Wealth is welcome, but with Pressure controlling the Self line it only 'feeds the ghost that harms you'.

> | 觉子曰：凡遇兄鬼同动者，不可概曰鬼动招非，盖鬼动能制兄爻不劫财也。 | 觉子说：凡是兄弟、官鬼同时发动，不能一概说官鬼动招是非，因为官鬼动能制住兄弟，使它不劫财。 | Juezi says: when Peer and Pressure move together, do not simply say the moving Pressure invites disputes, for it holds the Peer line down so that it cannot rob the money. |
