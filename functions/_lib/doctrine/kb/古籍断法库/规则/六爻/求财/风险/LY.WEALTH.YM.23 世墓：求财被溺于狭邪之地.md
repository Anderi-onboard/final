# 世墓：求财被溺于狭邪之地

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻入墓，求财会被沉溺在声色场所；这一条和占穴的一条，都只说世墓。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self line in the tomb, one seeking wealth is drowned in the districts of vice; this line and the one about graves speak only of the Self's tomb.

## 《易冒·随墓章第三十五》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 606 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 35: Following into the tomb", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 求财被溺于狭邪之地， | 求财，被沉溺在狭邪（声色）之地； | In seeking wealth, one is drowned in the districts of vice; |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 下一句是占穴，「或犯夫古塚之尸」；后面的小注说：这两句只说世墓。
>
> *Note:* The next line, on divining a grave site, adds 'or one may offend the corpse in an old tomb'; the small note after it says that these two lines speak only of the Self's tomb.
