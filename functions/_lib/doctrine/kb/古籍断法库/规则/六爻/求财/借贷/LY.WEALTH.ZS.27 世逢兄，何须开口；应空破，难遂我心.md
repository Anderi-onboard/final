# 世逢兄，何须开口；应空破，难遂我心

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借贷：世临兄弟不必开口；应爻空破难遂意；财空破休指望；子孙兴、财发动可求；世应都不宜月破旬空；兄弟不可临世或发动。书中例：兄动化退，而明日辰日合住兄爻，借款于辰日到手。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Borrowing: Peer on the Self line, do not bother asking; the Other line void or broken, you will not get your wish; Wealth void or broken, no hope; Output stirring and Wealth moving, ask; neither Self nor Other should be under a Month-break or void; Peer must not sit on the Self line or move. Case: the moving Peer turned Retreating, and the next day, a Chen day, combined with and held it; the loan came that day.

> | 世逢兄，何须开口；应空破，难遂我心。 | 世爻临兄弟，何必开口；应爻空破，难以如愿。 | Peer on the Self line: why even ask? The Other line void or broken: you will not get what you want. |
> | 财如空破休指望，子兴财发可相求。 | 财如果空破，别指望；子孙兴、财爻发动，可以去求。 | Wealth void or broken: do not hope; Output stirring and Wealth moving: go and ask. |
> | 世应两爻，皆不宜月破旬空； | 世、应两爻，都不宜月破、旬空； | Neither the Self nor the Other line should be under a Month-break or void; |
> | 兄弟一爻，断不可临身及动。 | 兄弟这一爻，绝不能临世（身）或发动。 | and the Peer line must on no account sit on the Self line or move. |
