# 世应同人，放债必然连本失

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 放债：世、应都是兄弟（同人），讨不回来；财爻再绝，连本俱失；世应落空亦然。应爻生合世爻、妻财有气、子孙发动则吉；间爻兄鬼发动防经手人抽分；卦无财而官鬼化出财，要打官司才讨得回。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Lending: Peer on both the Self and Other lines means the money cannot be recovered; with Wealth extinguished as well, the principal is lost; the same if Self and Other are void. Good if the Other line generates or combines with the Self line, Wealth has force and Output moves. A moving Peer or Pressure on the between lines: the middleman skims a share. No Wealth, but Pressure turning into it: recoverable only through a lawsuit.

> | 凡放私债，最忌世应值兄弟，必无讨处。 | 凡是放私人债，最忌世、应都是兄弟，一定讨不回来。 | In private lending the thing most feared is Peer on both the Self and Other lines: there will be no one to collect from. |
