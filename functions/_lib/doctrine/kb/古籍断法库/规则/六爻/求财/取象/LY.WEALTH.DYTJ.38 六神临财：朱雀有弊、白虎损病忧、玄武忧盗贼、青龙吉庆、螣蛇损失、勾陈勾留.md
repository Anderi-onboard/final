# 六神临财：朱雀有弊、白虎损病忧、玄武忧盗贼、青龙吉庆、螣蛇损失、勾陈勾留

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六神临在财爻上：朱雀有弊，白虎主损失、生病、忧愁，玄武要忧盗贼，青龙就是吉庆，螣蛇主损失，勾陈遇上羁留、不是争财就是互相嫉妒。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The six spirits on the Wealth line: the Vermilion Bird is a defect, the White Tiger brings loss, illness and worry, the Dark Tortoise means fear of thieves, the Azure Dragon is auspicious and joyful, the Coiling Snake means loss, and Hook Earth meets detention, being either a fight over money or mutual jealousy.

## 《断易天机·六神有气吉凶》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 473 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The six spirits when they hold qi", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 非论朱雀临财弊， | 不用多说，朱雀临在财上有弊， | No need to argue: the Vermilion Bird on Wealth is a defect; |
> | 白虎临财损病忧， | 白虎临在财上，主损失、生病、忧愁， | the White Tiger on Wealth brings loss, illness and worry; |
> | 玄武临财忧盗贼， | 玄武临在财上，忧盗贼， | the Dark Tortoise on Wealth, worry over thieves; |
> | 青龙临财即吉庆， | 青龙临在财上就是吉庆， | the Azure Dragon on Wealth is at once auspicious and joyful; |
> | 鰧蛇临财主损失， | 螣蛇临在财上，主损失， | the Coiling Snake on Wealth means loss; |
> | 勾陈临财遇勾留， | 勾陈临在财上，遇上羁留， | Hook Earth on Wealth meets detention; |
> | 若不争财相嫉妒。 | 如果不是争财，就是互相嫉妒。 | if it is not a fight over money, it is mutual jealousy. |
> | 但能如此与人占， | 只要能这样给人占卦， | One who can divine for people this way |
> | 万事吉凶无一失。 | 万事吉凶都不会有一点差错。 | will make no mistake about the fortune of any matter. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「非论」两字含义不明，可能是「不必说」，也可能是「不只是」；这里译作「不用多说」，不确定。「鰧」是「螣」的异体。
>
> *Note:* The two words 'fei lun' are unclear: they may mean 'need not be argued' or 'not only'; they are rendered as 'no need to argue', without certainty. The character in 'Coiling Snake' is a variant form.
