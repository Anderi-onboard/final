# 世为己，应为人，大宜相合；财为本，福为基，最喜同兴

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开行开店：世为自己、应为对方，宜相合；财为本、福为基，宜同旺。觉子：应爻代表谁，看问者本意——问伙计，应为伙计（世应生合同心；应生世他益我，世生应我益他；冲克生变，应克世我被瞒，世克应他从我；应临玄武兄爻暗中盗骗，应临官鬼克世明降灾非；世财应兄受累，世财应子得力；世应空合彼此虚诈）；问买卖，应为顾客（应生合世，生客成熟客；世应冲克，熟人成仇）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Opening a shop: the Self line is oneself, the Other the other party, and they should combine; Wealth is the capital, Output the foundation, and both should flourish. Juezi: who the Other line stands for depends on what is asked. Asking about a partner, it is the partner (generating or combining, of one mind; Other generating Self, he benefits me; Self generating Other, I benefit him; clashing or controlling, goodwill changes; Other controlling Self, he deceives me; Self controlling Other, he goes along; Other on the Dark Tortoise and Peer, secret fraud; Other on Pressure controlling Self, open trouble; Wealth on Self and Peer on Other, he drags me down; Wealth on Self and Output on Other, he helps; both void yet combining, both insincere). Asking about the trade, it is the customer (generating or combining with the Self line, strangers become regulars; clashing or controlling, old friends become enemies).

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11298–11299 行 · Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 觉子曰：古法，应为伙计，又为下顾之客。 | 觉子说：古法把应爻当作伙计，又当作上门的顾客。 | Juezi says: the old method takes the Other line as the partner, and also as the customer who calls. |
> | 《黄金策》曰：开行定主有人投，余以为须在占者之念。 | 《黄金策》说「开行定主有人投」，我认为要看占的人心里问的是什么。 | The Golden Strategy says 'a brokerage that opens will draw clients'; I hold that it depends on what the asker has in mind. |
> | 彼若一意而问伙计，则应为伙计。 | 他如果一心问的是伙计，应爻就是伙计。 | If he is asking only about a partner, the Other line is the partner. |
> | 若得世应相生相合，彼此同心；应生世，他益于我；世生应，我益于他。 | 世应相生相合，彼此同心；应生世，他对我有益；世生应，我对他有益。 | Self and Other generating or combining: of one mind; Other generating Self: he benefits me; Self generating Other: I benefit him. |
> | 相克相冲，两情有变，应克世，我被他瞒；世克应，他从我愿。 | 相克相冲，两人的情分会变；应克世，我被他瞒；世克应，他顺我的意。 | Controlling or clashing: their goodwill will change; Other controlling Self: he deceives me; Self controlling Other: he does as I wish. |
> | 应临玄武兄爻，暗中盗骗；应临官鬼克世，明降灾非。 | 应爻临玄武、兄弟，暗中偷骗；应爻临官鬼来克世，公开带来灾祸是非。 | The Other line on the Dark Tortoise and the Peer line: secret theft and fraud; the Other line on Pressure controlling the Self line: open trouble and disputes. |
> | 世财应兄，受他之累；世财应子，得他之力；世应空合，彼此有虚诈之心。 | 世临财、应临兄弟，受他连累；世临财、应临子孙，得他的力；世应空而相合，彼此都有虚假欺诈之心。 | Wealth on Self and Peer on Other: he drags me down; Wealth on Self and Output on Other: he helps me; Self and Other void yet combining: both sides are insincere. |
> | 如一意而重在买卖者，则以应爻为下顾之客。 | 如果一心问的是买卖，就以应爻为上门的顾客。 | If his mind is on the trade itself, the Other line is the customer. |
> | 应爻生世、合世，生客来交成故旧； | 应爻生世、合世，生客也会成为老主顾； | The Other line generating or combining with the Self line: strangers become regular customers; |
> | 世应相冲相克，熟人终久变成仇。 | 世应相冲相克，熟人最终也会变成仇人。 | Self and Other clashing or controlling: even old acquaintances become enemies in the end. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「世为已」的「已」当是「己」，两个本子都作「已」，原文照录。
>
> *Note:* The text writes yi (already) where ji (oneself) is meant; both editions have it, and we quote it as it stands.
