# 寄物：卦六合、财旺静可寄；财衰动、空破、应动带凶神则有失

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 把东西寄放在别人那里：先要托给可托的人，因为人心难测，有见物起欺心的，有收藏不严招盗的，有安放不当弄坏东西的。卦六合可寄，六冲不可寄；月日和动爻合财、合世应可寄，冲财、冲世应不可寄。财旺静，或财动化子孙、化长生、化旺，无失；财衰动，或动化兄弟、官鬼、空破绝，有失。财化土朽腐，化木微黷，化火遭火灾，化水受潮漏，化金封识被拆开。世应空破、随官鬼入墓、被兄弟官鬼动伤，应爻动化兄鬼空破绝，都有失。应爻动，他心变；应动助鬼伤身世财，他与人合谋劫骗。应临玄武动偷窃，临朱雀动口舌，临勾陈动牵连，临螣蛇动惊恐，临白虎动吞没，临青龙动则善加保护。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving goods with someone: first choose a person who can be trusted, for hearts are hard to fathom: some see the goods and turn deceitful, some store them carelessly and invite thieves, some put them in the wrong place and let them spoil. A Six-Combine hexagram: you may leave things; a Six-Clash: you may not. The Month, the Day and moving lines combining with Wealth, the Self or the Other: you may; clashing with them: you may not. Wealth strong and still, or Wealth moving into Output, into Birth or into strength: nothing is lost; Wealth weak and moving, or moving into Peer, Pressure, void, break or extinction: loss. Wealth turning into Earth: rot and decay; into Wood: slightly soiled; into Fire: fire; into Water: damp and leaks; into Metal: the seals broken open. Self and Other void or broken, following Pressure into the tomb, hurt by moving Peer or Pressure, or the Other moving into Peer, Pressure, void, break or extinction: loss. The Other moving: his mind has changed; the Other moving to help Pressure hurt the Body, the Self and the Wealth line: he plots with others to rob and cheat you. The Other moving on the Dark Tortoise: theft; on the Vermilion Bird: quarrels; on Hook Earth: entanglement; on the Coiling Snake: fright; on the White Tiger: devouring; on the Azure Dragon: good safekeeping.

> | 吾之寄物，必托其人之可托， | 我把东西寄放出去，一定要托给可以托付的人， | when I leave my goods with someone, I must choose a person who can be trusted, |
> | 而后以物付之也， | 然后才把东西交给他。 | and only then hand the goods over. |
> | 然而人情叵测， | 可是人心难测： | But human hearts are hard to fathom: |
> | 睹物怀欺者有之， | 有看见东西就起欺心的， | some see the goods and turn deceitful; |
> | 谩藏诲盗者有之， | 有收藏不严、招来盗贼的， | some store them carelessly and so invite thieves; |
> | 安置失所，致物败坏者有之， | 有安放不当、把东西弄坏的， | some put them in the wrong place and let them spoil; |
> | 是不可以不慎厥始也。 | 所以一开始就不能不谨慎。 | so one cannot but be careful from the very start. |
