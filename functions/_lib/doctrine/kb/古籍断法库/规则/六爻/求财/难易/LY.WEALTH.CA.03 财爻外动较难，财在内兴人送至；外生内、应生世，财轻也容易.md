# 财爻外动较难，财在内兴人送至；外生内、应生世，财轻也容易

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻在外卦发动，比较艰难；财在内卦兴旺，人会把钱送上门；外卦生内卦、应生世，即使财轻也容易。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Wealth line moving in the outer trigram is harder; Wealth thriving in the inner trigram, people bring the money to you; the outer generating the inner and the Other generating the Self, even if Wealth is light it is easy.
