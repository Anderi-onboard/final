# 前卦有财后卦无，前卦无财后卦有

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本卦有财、变卦无财：宜速求，迟则难得；本卦无财、变卦有财：先难后得，宜等时机，或等财爻旺相的年月。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth in the original hexagram but not in the changed one: seek quickly, for later it is hard to get. No Wealth in the original but Wealth in the changed one: hardship first, gain later; wait for the moment, or for the year and month in which Wealth is strong.
