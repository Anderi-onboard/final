# 三四爻猪羊怕官杀兄弟；五六爻牛马喜福德妻财；各爻带什么看盈亏

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 三爻猪、四爻羊，怕见官杀、兄弟；五爻牛、六爻马，喜遇福德、妻财。注：猪带子孙妻财必旺，带兄弟不长，带官逢空有损；羊带财福旺，带鬼杀损失，逢空无羊；牛带子孙有子母牛，遇青龙财福吉，遇鬼杀白虎凶；驴马一爻照牛爻断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The third line is the pig and the fourth the sheep, which fear Pressure, baleful stars and Peer; the fifth is the ox and the sixth the horse, which like Output and Wealth. The commentary adds: a pig with Output or Wealth thrives, with Peer does not grow, with Pressure and Void suffers loss; a sheep with Wealth or Output thrives, with Pressure or baleful stars suffers loss, falling Void there is no sheep; an ox with Output has cow and calf, is auspicious with the Azure Dragon, Wealth or Output, and ominous with Pressure, baleful stars or the White Tiger; the last line for donkey and horse is judged like the ox line.

> [!note] 按
> 第一段是《天玄赋》的歌诀，第二段是紧接着的括号小注。「胎养临爻」的「胎」「养」是长生十二宫里的胎、养两位。「子日冲午爻」是子冲午。
>
> *Note:* The first passage is the verse of the Tianxuan Fu and the second the bracketed small note that follows it. 'Womb' and 'Nurture' are two of the twelve stages of the life cycle. 'A Zi day clashing the Wu line' is Zi clashing Wu.
