# 官若兴隆，行主可千金之托；应如空破，牙人无毫忽之能

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 委托牙行（投行）：官鬼（行主）、应爻（牙人）旺而生合世爻，可托千金；官、应静衰空破，是奸险之人，交财必被骗。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Placing goods with a brokerage: Pressure (the house) and the Other line (the broker) strong and generating or combining with the Self line, you can trust them with a fortune; still, weak, void or broken, they are crooks and your money will be taken.
