# 趋利之占皆以妻财为用；干谒宜官，商旅忌鬼动

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是谋利的占卜，都以妻财为用神；因事而求，偶尔兼用别的象；所以求见权贵宜看官，财官都不能没有气，商旅忌鬼，官鬼哪里宜于发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* All divinations that pursue profit take Wealth as the Subject; they are sought for a particular matter and occasionally take in other images; so to petition a superior look to Pressure, neither Wealth nor Pressure may lose its qi, merchants and travellers avoid Pressure, and Pressure is in no way fit to move.
