# 置脱货物：子孙发动最宜，子动生财，独忌兄弟交重

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 囤货、脱货：要子孙发动，从头到尾嫌兄弟发动；贵贱要赶时机，盈亏凭这一卦。子孙动就生财，兄弟旺就克财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Stocking and selling goods: Output should move, and Peer moving is unwelcome from start to finish; catch the moment, dear or cheap, for profit and loss rest on the cast. Output moving generates Wealth, and Peer rising controls it.

## 《易林补遗·收顿货物章第一百六》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1978 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 106 - Stocking goods", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 置脱用子孙发动， | 囤货、脱货，用子孙发动； | For stocking and selling off, Output should move; |
> | 始终嫌兄弟交重。 | 自始至终都嫌兄弟交重。 | from start to finish Peer moving is unwelcome. |
> | 贵贱赶时，盈亏据卜 | 价贵价贱都要赶上时机，盈亏就凭这一卦。 | Dear or cheap, catch the moment; profit or loss rests on this cast. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1978 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 大凡脱货制货，最宜子动， | 大凡脱货、置货，最适宜子孙动， | In selling off goods and buying them in, the best is Output moving; |
> | 子动生财，独忌兄兴， | 子孙动就生财，只忌兄弟兴旺， | Output moving generates Wealth, and the only taboo is Peer rising; |
> | 兴则克财，大概如此据理推详。 | 兴旺了就克财；大概就是这样，按道理去推详。 | risen, it controls Wealth; that is the general rule, to be worked out by reason. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「盈亏据卜大凡脱货制货」是一句连着的原文，前半属诗，后半「大凡脱货制货……」起是解，这里在那里切开。
>
> *Note:* The source runs the end of the verse into the start of the commentary without a break ('profit and loss rests on the cast in selling and buying goods ...'); it is cut there.
