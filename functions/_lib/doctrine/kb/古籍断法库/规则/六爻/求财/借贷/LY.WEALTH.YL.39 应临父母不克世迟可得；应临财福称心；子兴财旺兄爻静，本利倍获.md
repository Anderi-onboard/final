# 应临父母不克世迟可得；应临财福称心；子兴财旺兄爻静，本利倍获

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应临父母（文书）而不克世，或再加勾陈，不过迟一点，终究有钱可得；应值财爻或临福德，即使不生世，钱也称心；子孙兴旺、财旺，兄弟休囚或安静，本钱不亏、利息加倍，是上等大吉的卦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other on Resource without controlling the Self, or with Hook Earth added, is merely late and the money can be had in the end; the Other on the Wealth line or on Output, even if it does not generate the Self, gives money as one wishes; Output rising and Wealth strong with Peer resting or still, the capital is not lost and the interest comes doubled - the best and most auspicious kind of hexagram.

## 《易林补遗·索取债利章第一百八》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2004 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 108 - Collecting debts and interest", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如带文书迟可得， | 如果带文书（父母），迟一些可以得到； | If it carries documents (Resource), it can be had late; |
> | 应居财福遂吾心， | 应居财或福德，称我的心； | the Other in Wealth or Output suits my heart; |
> | 子兴财旺兄爻静， | 子孙兴旺、财旺、兄爻安静， | Output rising, Wealth strong and the Peer line still, |
> | 本利无亏倍获金。 | 本利都不亏，还能加倍得到银子。 | capital and interest are unharmed and the silver comes back doubled. |
