# 行险求财，此非营生之本：世墓随墓、应克世、官克世、鬼爻发动，都有害；加玄武盗、朱雀斗、勾陈拦、螣蛇惊、白虎疾，惟青龙可凶而吉

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 行险求财不是谋生的根本，书里不谈它的利，只把害讲明：动伤随墓，害大；应克世，路上遇强盗；官克世，路上遭罗网；鬼爻发动，如虎卧当道。要去哪里呢？加玄武是盗寇之凶，加朱雀是争斗之患，勾陈是拦阻的人，螣蛇是风波之惊，白虎是灾病之危，只有青龙能把凶变吉。注：非分的求取，凶多吉少，常常身家性命一起搭上，所以有告诫的话。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking wealth by risk is not the root of livelihood, and the book does not speak of its profit but makes its harm plain: a moving line that harms and follows into the tomb brings great harm; the Other controlling the Self means meeting bandits on the road; Pressure controlling the Self means meeting nets on the road; a moving Pressure line is a tiger lying across the path. Where would one go? With the Dark Tortoise it is the ill of robbers, with the Vermilion Bird the trouble of quarrels, Hook Earth means people who block the way, the Coiling Snake the fright of wind and waves, the White Tiger the danger of disaster and illness, and only the Azure Dragon can turn ill into good. The note adds: a demand beyond one's lot brings more ill than good and often costs the whole household and the person, so the book gives a warning.
