# 财是根本不可变：化鬼化兄则耗散无结果，化子孙则利禄津津

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财是根本，不能变动：化成官鬼、变成兄弟，财就被耗散，两条路都没有好处，终究难有结果；财化成子孙，则利禄滚滚，永远没有边际。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth is the root and must not change: turning into Pressure or Peer, Wealth is consumed and scattered, neither way does any good, and in the end there is no result; Wealth turning into Output, profit and emolument flow on without limit.

## 《易林补遗·开张店肆章第一百五》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1963 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 105 - Opening a shop", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财化鬼兄无结果， | 财化成官鬼或兄弟，没有结果； | Wealth turning into Pressure or Peer comes to no result; |
> | 财之子象有收成。 | 财化成子孙的象，有收成。 | Wealth turning into an image of Output brings a harvest. |
