# 祝巫：鬼旺有神通；官旺合真人之应，鬼空则道不通彻

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 圆光召将、画符念咒的太保、师巫这类人求财，一定靠官鬼兴旺，灵验才像回声一样快；官旺合真人之应，法术高明；官鬼落空，就没有野祟那样的灵验，道法不通透。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For crystal-gazers who summon spirit generals and for the Taibao and shamans who draw talismans and utter spells, everything rests on Pressure being strong, and then the response is as prompt as an echo: a strong Pressure fits the response of a true adept and the art is lofty; with Pressure void there is none of the efficacy of the wild spirits and the art is not fully effective.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 祝巫鬼旺有神通。 | 祝巫一类，官鬼旺相就有神通。 | For priests and shamans, a strong Pressure means spirit power. |

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1939 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 惟有圆光召将、施符设咒太保师巫等类，必仗官兴鬼旺，其应如音。 | 只有圆光召将、画符念咒的太保、师巫这一类人，一定要靠官鬼兴旺，灵验才像回声一样快。 | Only for the likes of crystal-gazers who summon spirit generals, and Taibao and shamans who draw talismans and utter spells, everything rests on Pressure being strong, and then the response is as prompt as an echo. |
> | 经云：请师来巫水祈延术、蓍龟之卜，官旺合真人之应，法必高明。 | 经上说：请师、请巫、水中祈祷、延请法术、蓍龟占卜这一类，官爻旺相，才合真人的灵验，法术一定高明； | The classic says: for inviting masters, shamans, water prayers, extension arts and milfoil-and-tortoise divination, a strong Pressure fits the response of a true adept, and the art is surely lofty; |
> | 鬼空，无野祟之灵道非通撤。 | 官鬼落空，就没有野祟那样的灵验，道法不通透。 | with Pressure void, there is none of the efficacy of the wild spirits, and the art is not fully effective. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「请师来巫水祈延术、蓍龟之卜」一句列举的行当，原文断句不明，这里是照字面拆开译的；「通撤」疑是「通彻」之误。
>
> *Note:* The list 'inviting masters, shamans, water prayers, extension arts, milfoil and tortoise divination' is punctuated unclearly in the source and is split literally here; 'not thoroughly effective' translates a phrase whose last character is probably a slip.
