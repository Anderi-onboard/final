# 寄放财物：财化水，防上漏下湿（亥子、父母动同）

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寄放：财化成水，要防水神玄冥，上面漏、下面潮，以致腐烂；亥子、父母发动也是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving goods: Wealth turning into Water, guard against the water god Xuanming, leaks above and damp below, and the goods rot; Hai, Zi and Resource moving are the same.

## 《易林补遗·安寄财物章第一百十四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2080 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 114 - Leaving goods for safekeeping", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 化水防上下之玄冥， | 化成水，要防上下的玄冥（水神）； | Turning into Water, guard against Xuanming above and below; |
> | 亥子父动皆然。 | 亥子、父母动，也是这样。 | Hai, Zi and Resource moving are the same. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2081 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 玄冥，水神也。 | 玄冥是水神； | Xuanming is the god of water; |
> | 财化水爻，或卦申水火发动，恐遭上漏下湿而致腐烂也。 | 财化成水爻，或者卦里水爻发动（原文「卦申水火发动」有误），恐怕遭上漏下湿而腐烂。 | Wealth turning into a Water line, or a Water line in the hexagram moving (the source's 'hexagram Shen Water Fire moving' is garbled), it is feared the goods suffer leaks above and damp below and rot. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「或卦申水火发动」字有讹，读不通，这里按上下文读成卦里的水爻发动。
>
> *Note:* 'Or the hexagram's Shen Water Fire moves' is garbled and cannot be read; from the context it is taken as a Water line in the hexagram moving.
