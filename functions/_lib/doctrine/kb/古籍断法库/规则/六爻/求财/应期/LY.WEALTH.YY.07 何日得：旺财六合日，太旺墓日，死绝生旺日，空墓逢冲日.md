# 何日得：旺财六合日，太旺墓日，死绝生旺日，空墓逢冲日

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 何日得财：旺相而生合世身的财→六合之日；财多太旺→墓日；死绝→生旺之日；空墓→逢冲之日；子孙旺而卦无财→财值之日。书中五例：伏财靠月建扶起而得；伏财因飞神旬空透出、逢长生而得；财化绝待绝处逢生之日；用神不透待财值之日；伏克飞神「出暴」、次日兄空而得。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The day the money comes: a strong Wealth generating or combining with the Self or Body, the day that combines with it; too much or over-strong, its tomb day; dead or extinguished, a day of birth or strength; void or entombed, the clashing day; Output strong and no Wealth, the day of Wealth's branch. Five cases in the book: hidden Wealth lifted by the Month; hidden Wealth showing through when its flying line is void, and coming on its Birth day; Wealth turning into extinction, coming on the day it is reborn; a Subject that does not show, coming on the day of its branch; a hidden line controlling its flying line ('breaking out') and coming the next day when Peer is void.

> | 财多太旺者，墓日得也。 | 财多、太旺的，在墓日得。 | Too much or over-strong Wealth: on its tomb day. |
> | 死绝之财，生旺日得。 | 死、绝的财，在生旺之日得。 | Dead or extinguished Wealth: on a day of birth or strength. |
> | 空墓之财，逢冲日得。 | 空、墓的财，在逢冲之日得。 | Void or entombed Wealth: on the clashing day. |
> | 福旺无财者，财值(此处缺一字)日得也。 | 子孙旺而卦无财的，在财爻值日（原文这里缺一个字）得。 | Output strong but no Wealth: on the day Wealth's branch comes round (a character is missing in the text). |
