# 巫医、优娼以官鬼为专；工技忌应克世；僧道缘募恶应爻空；行次迎客、脱产求交，应空则无人来、无人与

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 巫医的利在名誉，专看官鬼；优娼的利在鼓舞，也以官鬼为务；工技之人重情好，忌应克世；僧道的缘募重悦从，恶应爻空；出门迎客，对方空了谁来；卖产业求成交，应空了谁来买；成交有指定的对象，就和谋事一样。注：有的兼用官，有的兼用应，而财神不可脱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For shamans and doctors the profit lies in reputation and the Pressure line is the specialist; for actors and courtesans the profit lies in drumming and dancing, and Pressure is again the business; for craftsmen good feeling counts and the Other controlling the Self is avoided; for monks and priests raising alms, willing agreement counts and the Other line falling Void is disliked; in going out to meet guests, if the other side is Void who will come; in selling property to close a deal, if the Other is Void who will take it; if the deal has a named counterpart it is the same as scheming a matter. The note adds: some also use Pressure, some also use the Other, but the Wealth spirit must not be lost.
