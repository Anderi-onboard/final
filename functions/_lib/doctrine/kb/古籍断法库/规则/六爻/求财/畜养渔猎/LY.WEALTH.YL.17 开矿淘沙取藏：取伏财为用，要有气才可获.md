# 开矿淘沙取藏：取伏财为用，要有气才可获

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开矿、淘沙、掘藏：地下的宝物藏而不露，只取伏着的财为用神，伏财要有气才能得到，福神成双跟着来；劈山开矿、破石寻珍，是同一个道理。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Mining, panning sand and digging for buried treasure: treasure in the ground lies hidden, so take the hidden Wealth as the Subject; it must have breath to be had, and Output follows alongside; splitting mountains, opening mines and breaking rock to find gems are judged by the same principle.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1944 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 开矿淘沙兼取藏，伏财有气福骈从。 | 开矿、淘沙，还有掘藏，伏着的财有气，福神成双跟着来。 | Mining, panning sand and digging for buried treasure: hidden Wealth with breath, and Output follows alongside. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1945 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 地中宝乃藏而不露，但只取伏财为用，须要有气可获。 | 地下的宝物是藏着不露的，只取伏财为用神，伏财要有气才能得到。 | Treasure in the ground lies hidden and does not show, so take the hidden Wealth as the Subject; it must have breath to be had. |
> | 沙内之金乃理而禾吐必得。 | 沙里的金子，（「理而禾吐」读不通，这句有讹）一定能得。 | The gold in the sand (the words 'put in order and grain spits out' cannot be read; the sentence is corrupt) is surely obtained. |
> | 财隐旺土动而变化淘之方， | 财隐伏在旺相的土下面，土动而变化，是淘取的办法； | Wealth hidden under a strong Earth, the Earth moving and changing: that is the method of washing it out; |
> | 有劈山开旷破石寻珍以此无异，一理同推。 | 劈山开矿、破石寻珍，和这个没有两样，同一个道理去推。 | splitting mountains, opening mines and breaking rock to find gems are no different, all judged by the same principle. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「沙内之金乃理而禾吐必得」一句字有讹误，读不通，译文只译出能读的部分并标出。「财隐旺土动而变化淘之方」也很省，这里照字面译。
>
> *Note:* The sentence about gold in the sand is corrupt and cannot be read; only the readable part is translated and marked. 'Wealth hidden under a strong Earth ... the method of washing' is also terse and is translated literally.
