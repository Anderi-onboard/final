# 店面货物：父母为店，妻财为货；父旺店大，财旺货流；书纸店以父为用，宝珠古董店以财为用

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看店和货：父母代表店，妻财代表货。父旺而静，店里热闹平安；财旺而动，货物畅销。父旺是大店，父衰是小店；父逢冲开不长，父遇生合能长久。有财无父是有货无店，有父无财是有店无货；父旺财衰，店大利薄；父衰财旺，店小利厚。两个财：内卦被冲、外卦旺，本少利多；内卦旺、外卦被冲，本多利少。卦身和世爻跟官鬼入墓，白费力，利归别人。财爻遇冲暗动，带龙喜暗中得利，带玄武劫煞被骗失财。官鬼临财库发动，不是官司就是灾祸。父母带杀刃亡劫发动，或日雀会月雀发动，招争讼。珠宝古董杂货店以财为用，书纸巾帽衣服店以父为用，酒肴牲畜店以子为用；用神旺、财衰，货多利薄。另有郭雍说：开书籍纸货古董店，财旺而父母被克，吉；开当铺，父母旺而财被克，吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Reading the shop and its goods: Resource stands for the shop, Wealth for the goods. Resource strong and still: the shop is busy and peaceful; Wealth strong and moving: the goods circulate. Resource strong is a big shop, weak a small one; Resource clashed will not stay open long, generated or combined it stays open for good. Wealth without Resource is goods without a shop; Resource without Wealth is a shop without goods; Resource strong and Wealth weak: a big shop, thin profit; the reverse: a small shop, thick profit. Two Wealth lines, the inner clashed and the outer strong: little capital, much profit; the inner strong and the outer clashed: much capital, little profit. The Body and Self lines following Pressure into the tomb: labor in vain, the profit goes to others. A Wealth line clashed into hidden motion profits in secret with the Dragon and Joy stars, and is cheated of money with the Dark Tortoise or the Robbery star. Pressure moving on the tomb-store of Wealth: a lawsuit or a disaster. Resource moving with the killing stars, the Goat Blade, the Loss or the Robbery star, or the Day's Vermilion Bird meeting the Month's Vermilion Bird, moving: it invites lawsuits. Jewel, antique and sundry shops take Wealth as the Subject; shops of books, paper, scarves, hats and clothes take Resource; wine, food and livestock shops take Output; the Subject strong but Wealth weak: many goods, thin profit. Guo Yong adds: for a shop of books, paper goods and antiques, Wealth strong while Resource is controlled is auspicious; for a pawnshop, Resource strong while Wealth is controlled is auspicious.

> [!warning] 打架
> 书籍纸张店该看谁，同一段里有两个说法：前面说书、纸、巾、帽、衣服店以父母为用神，父母旺才好；郭雍却说开书籍纸货古董店，要财旺、父母被克才吉；当铺则反过来，父母旺、财被克才吉。原文并列，没有分出高下。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For a bookshop the same paragraph gives two readings: first, shops of books, paper, scarves, hats and clothes take Resource as the Subject, so Resource should be strong; Guo Yong says a shop of books, paper goods and antiques does well when Wealth is strong and Resource controlled, while a pawnshop is the other way round, Resource strong and Wealth controlled. The source sets them side by side and does not rank them.

> [!note] 按
> 「日雀会月雀」只见于《易隐》这一处，按字面译成日的朱雀会月的朱雀。「纸贴」译成纸货。
>
> *Note:* 'The Day's Bird meeting the Month's Bird' occurs only here in the Yi Yin and is translated literally. 'Paper and sheets' is rendered as 'paper goods'.
