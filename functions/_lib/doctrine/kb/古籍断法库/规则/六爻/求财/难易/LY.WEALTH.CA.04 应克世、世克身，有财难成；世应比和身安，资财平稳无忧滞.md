# 应克世、世克身，有财难成；世应比和身安，资财平稳无忧滞

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应克世、世克身，就算有财，终究难以成就；世应比和、卦身又安稳，资财平稳，没有忧愁阻滞。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other controlling the Self and the Self controlling the Body: even with Wealth it is hard to bring about in the end; Self and Other in harmony and the Body at ease: the funds are steady, with no worry or delay.
