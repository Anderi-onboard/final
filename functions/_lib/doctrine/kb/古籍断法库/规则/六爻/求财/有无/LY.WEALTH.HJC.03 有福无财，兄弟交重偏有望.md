# 有福无财，兄弟交重偏有望

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财不上卦（或不动）而子孙在，兄弟发动反而有望：兄弟生子孙，子孙生财，只是来得慢。财爻空而兄动叫「避空」，过旬、到财爻生旺之日可得。财爻不空不伏却被兄弟劫，是下等卦。王洪绪、野鹤都要求子孙也动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Wealth absent (or still) but Output present, a moving Peer line actually gives hope: Peer feeds Output and Output feeds Wealth, only slowly. Wealth void while Peer moves is called 'hiding in the void': the money comes after the ten-day cycle, on a day that births or empowers Wealth. Wealth neither void nor hidden yet robbed by Peer is the lowest grade. Wang Hongxu and Yehe both require Output to be moving too.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7767 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 有福无财， | 子孙在、妻财不在， | With Output present but no Wealth, |
> | 兄弟交重偏有望； | 兄弟发动反而有希望； | a moving Peer line actually gives hope; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7768 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财为用神，而不上卦，必无可望。 | 妻财是用神，它不在卦上，本来没有指望。 | Wealth is the Subject; if it is absent from the hexagram there should be nothing to hope for. |
> | 若得兄弟发动，无财可劫，则反生扶福德，财源有气，仍旧有望，但主迟滞耳。 | 但如果兄弟发动，卦里没有财可劫，它反而去生扶子孙，财源有了力量，仍然有希望，只是来得慢。 | But if the Peer line moves and finds no Wealth to rob, it generates and supports Output instead; the source of money gains force and there is still hope, only slow. |
> | 兄动而财空，谓之避空。 | 兄弟动而财爻落空，叫作「避空」（财在空里，兄弟劫不着）。 | When Peer moves and Wealth is void, this is called 'hiding in the void' (the robber cannot reach it). |
> | 过旬生旺日，财亦可得。 | 过了这一旬，到财爻生旺的日子，财也能得到。 | After the ten-day cycle passes, on a day that births or empowers Wealth, the money can still be had. |
> | 财若不空不伏，而遇兄劫，则为下卦。 | 财爻如果既不空也不伏，却被兄弟劫，就是下等卦。 | If Wealth is neither void nor hidden and yet Peer robs it, the hexagram is of the lowest grade. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2659–2661 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 有者言其发动之意，无者言其伏藏之意。 | 「有」是说它发动，「无」是说它伏藏。 | 'Present' here means moving; 'absent' means hidden. |
> | 凡卜求财，卦中子孙爻动而无伤，则财源丰厚固吉。 | 凡是卜求财，卦里子孙爻发动而不受伤，财源丰厚，本来就吉。 | In any money question, if the Output line moves and is unharmed, the source of money is rich and the outlook is good. |
> | 如再见兄弟爻发动，生扶子孙，则财愈加根深蒂固， | 如果再见到兄弟爻发动，去生扶子孙，财就更加根深蒂固， | If a Peer line also moves and feeds Output, the money is rooted all the deeper; |
> | 故曰“兄弟爻交重偏有望”，皆为子孙亦动也。 | 所以说「兄弟交重偏有望」，都是因为子孙也在动。 | that is why it says 'a moving Peer line gives hope': it is always because Output is moving too. |

^q3

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11084 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 有福无财，兄弟交重偏有望。 | 有福无财，兄弟发动反而有望。 | With Output present but no Wealth, a moving Peer line gives hope. |
> | 兄弟乃劫财之神，占财之最忌也。 | 兄弟是劫财之神，是占财最忌的。 | Peer is the robber of wealth, the thing most feared in a money question. |
> | 财兄同动，必主阻隔破耗。 | 财和兄弟一起动，一定有阻隔、破耗。 | If Wealth and Peer move together there will be obstruction and loss. |
> | 假设财静及不现者，倘得子孙与兄弟同动，又为可喜，以兄动生子孙也，故曰有望。 | 假如财爻安静或不在卦上，而子孙和兄弟一起动，又是可喜的，因为兄弟动去生子孙，所以说有望。 | But if Wealth is still or absent and Output moves together with Peer, that is welcome, because the moving Peer feeds Output; hence 'gives hope'. |
> | 若财爻、动爻、子孙爻俱动于卦中者，更为可喜， | 如果财爻、兄爻（原文作「动爻」）、子孙爻都在卦中发动，更可喜， | If the Wealth line, the Peer line (the text says 'the moving line') and the Output line all move in the hexagram, better still, |
> | 盖兄动生子、子动生财，其利甚厚，且主财有根源，利能久远也。 | 因为兄弟动生子孙、子孙动生财，利益很厚，而且财有根源，利能长久。 | for Peer feeds Output and Output feeds Wealth: the profit is rich, the money has a root, and the gain lasts. |

^q4

> [!warning] 打架
> 「有福无财」怎么读：旧注读作「财不上卦」，只要兄弟发动去生子孙就有望；王洪绪把「有、无」读成「动、伏」，而且认为有望全靠子孙也在动；野鹤也要子孙和兄弟同动，并说财、兄同动主阻隔。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to read 'Output present, no Wealth': the old commentary takes it as Wealth absent from the hexagram, with hope as long as the moving Peer feeds Output; Wang Hongxu reads 'present/absent' as 'moving/hidden' and says the hope depends on Output moving as well; Yehe also wants Output and Peer moving together, and says Wealth and Peer moving together means obstruction.

> [!note] 按
> 野鹤那句「若财爻、动爻、子孙爻俱动」，按下文「兄动生子、子动生财」，「动爻」应是「兄爻」；两个本子都作「动爻」，这里不改原文，只在译文里注明。
>
> *Note:* In Yehe's 'if the Wealth line, the moving line and the Output line all move', the next clause ('Peer feeds Output, Output feeds Wealth') shows 'the moving line' should be 'the Peer line'. Both editions read 'moving line'; we leave the text as it is and flag it in the translation.
