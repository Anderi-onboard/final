# 身或兄临，必难求望

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟临卦身或持世，求财难有指望（卦身临财福吉、临父辛劳、临官利公门之财）。野鹤只看世爻，并认为兄弟持世而世逢月破、旬空、化墓，或日月为财冲克世爻，或世上兄弟化出财爻，仍可得财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer on the Body line or the Self line makes the money hard to get (Wealth or Output on the Body line is good, Resource means toil, Pressure favours official money). Yehe reads only the Self line, and holds that Peer on the Self line still gets the money if the Self line is under a Month-break, void, or turns into its tomb, if the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line, or if the Peer on the Self line turns into Wealth.

> [!warning] 打架
> 看卦身还是看世爻：旧注、王洪绪看卦身（兄临卦身难求，持世亦然）；野鹤认为卦身不验，只看世爻，并列出兄弟持世仍能得财的几种情形。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Body line or Self line? The old commentary and Wang Hongxu read the Body line (Peer on it means hardship, and the same on the Self line); Yehe holds the Body line unreliable, goes by the Self line alone, and lists the cases in which Peer on the Self line still gets the money.
