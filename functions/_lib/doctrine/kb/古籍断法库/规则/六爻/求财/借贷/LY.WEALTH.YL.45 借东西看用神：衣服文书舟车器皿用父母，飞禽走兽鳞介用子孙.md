# 借东西看用神：衣服文书舟车器皿用父母，飞禽走兽鳞介用子孙

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借东西各取各自的用神：帽子衣服、文书、船车、器皿这一类，以父母为用；飞禽走兽、鱼鳞介甲这些有生气的东西，以子孙为用。旺相兴隆大吉，空绝大凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For borrowed goods each takes its own Subject: hats and clothes, documents, boats and carts and utensils take Resource; flying birds, running beasts, scaled and shelled creatures and other living things take Output. Thriving is greatly auspicious; void or extinct is greatly ill.

## 《易林补遗·借贷财物章第一百九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2020 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 109 - Borrowing goods and money", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 借贷衣舟寻父母， | 借贷衣服、船，找父母； | For borrowing clothes and boats, look to Resource; |
> | 那移禽兽子孙凭。 | 挪借禽兽，凭子孙。 | for shifting birds and beasts, rely on Output. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> | 凡借贷之物，各取其用， | 凡是借贷的东西，各取各自的用神； | For any borrowed goods, each takes its own Subject; |
> | 冠服文书舟车器血之类，父母为用。 | 帽子衣服、文书、船车、器皿这一类，以父母为用神； | hats and clothes, documents, boats and carts, utensils and the like take Resource as the Subject; |
> | 飞禽走兽鳞介生气之物，子孙为用。 | 飞禽走兽、鱼鳞介甲这些有生气的东西，以子孙为用神； | flying birds, running beasts, scaled and shelled creatures and other living things take Output as the Subject; |

> [!note] 按
> 「器血」的「血」疑是「皿」之误，译作器皿。
>
> *Note:* 'Utensils' translates a two-character word whose second character is probably a slip for 'dishes'.
