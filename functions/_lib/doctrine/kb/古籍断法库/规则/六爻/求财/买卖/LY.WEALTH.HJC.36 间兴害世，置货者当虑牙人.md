# 间兴害世，置货者当虑牙人

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买货：应爻生合世爻易成，刑克世爻难买。代表货物的爻（六畜看子孙、五谷看妻财、布帛看父母）太过货多、不及货少、空伏无货。兄鬼发动防骗；兄鬼在间爻是牙人虚诈、口舌；间爻伤克世爻防牙人谋财；出门买货而应爻空，多不顺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Buying goods: the Other line generating or combining with the Self line makes it easy; punishing or controlling it makes it hard. The line for the goods (Output for livestock, Wealth for grain, Resource for cloth) in excess means plenty, falling short means few, void or hidden means none. Peer and Pressure moving: beware of swindlers; on the between lines, a false broker or quarrels; a between line harming or controlling the Self line: the broker is after your goods; travelling to buy with the Other line void, it mostly goes badly.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7884–7885 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 买货者，应要生合世爻，必然易成。刑克世，必难置买。 | 买货的，要应爻生合世爻，一定容易成交；应爻刑克世爻，一定难买到。 | For a buyer, the Other line should generate or combine with the Self line; then the deal is easy. If it punishes or controls the Self line, it will be hard to buy. |
> | 物爻太过，其货必多；物爻不及，其货必少；物爻空伏，其货必无。 | 代表货物的那一爻太旺太多，货一定多；不及，货一定少；空或伏，一定没有货。 | If the line for the goods is in excess, there will be plenty of goods; if it falls short, few; if it is void or hidden, none. |
> | 物爻者，六畜看子孙，五谷看财爻，丝绵布帛看父母类。 | 货物那一爻：六畜看子孙，五谷看财爻，丝绵布匹看父母，诸如此类。 | The line for the goods: Output for livestock, the Wealth line for grain, Resource for silk, cotton and cloth, and so on. |
> | 最怕兄鬼交重，须防光棍诓骗。 | 最怕兄弟、官鬼发动，要防骗子诓骗。 | The worst is Peer and Pressure moving: beware of swindlers. |
> | 在间爻，则是牙人虚诈不实，或有口舌争竞。 | （兄鬼）在间爻上，就是经纪人虚假不实，或有口舌争执。 | On the between lines, it is the broker being false and dishonest, or quarrels and disputes. |
> | 伤克世爻，当虑牙人谋劫财物。 | 来伤克世爻，要提防经纪人图谋侵吞财物。 | If they harm or control the Self line, beware of the broker plotting to seize the goods. |
> | 出路买货，应空多不顺利。 | 出门去买货，应爻落空，多半不顺利。 | If you travel to buy and the Other line is void, it will mostly go badly. |
