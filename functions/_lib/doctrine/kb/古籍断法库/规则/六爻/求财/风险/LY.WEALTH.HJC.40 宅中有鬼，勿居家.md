# 宅中有鬼，勿居家

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 第二爻为宅：在家求财而官鬼在宅爻发动，必不利，按官鬼五行断灾（火鬼火灾、水鬼盗贼）；子孙持世发动可解，否则宜迁店铺（王洪绪）；宅爻空亡，只宜在外开铺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The second line is the house: seeking money at home with Pressure moving on the house line goes badly; judge the trouble by its element (Fire, fire; Water, thieves). Output on the Self line and moving resolves it, otherwise move the shop (Wang Hongxu); a void house line means trade only from a shop outside.
