# 兄动财空本利绝望；鬼化为财，须经官追讨才得

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟发动或财落空，本钱和利息都没指望；官鬼化成财，一定要经官府追讨才能得到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer moving or Wealth void leaves no hope of capital or interest; Pressure turning into Wealth, the debt must go through the officials before it can be had.

## 《易林补遗·索取债利章第一百八》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2006 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 108 - Collecting debts and interest", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄动财空连本失， | 兄弟动、财落空，连本钱都亏； | Peer moving and Wealth void, even the capital is lost; |
> | 鬼化为财须诉论。 | 官鬼化成财，必须打官司争论。 | Pressure turning into Wealth, you must go to law to argue it. |
