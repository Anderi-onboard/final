# 期日章·求财：财从长生到帝旺，再加墓库同归；空手求财看鬼爻同断；博戏看生旺

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求财的应期：财爻从长生到帝旺的时候，再加上墓库、同归的时候；空手求财，鬼爻也照这样断；博戏，看生旺也是这样推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The date for wealth: from Long Life to Emperor's Prime, and in addition the times of the tomb and of returning together; for seeking wealth with empty hands, judge the Pressure line the same way; for gambling, the thriving state is reckoned in the same way.

## 《断易天机·期日章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 962 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The chapter on dates", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 求财长生至帝旺， | 求财，（财爻）从长生到帝旺， | For wealth, [Wealth] from Long Life through to Emperor's Prime, |
> | 更加墓库及同归， | 再加上墓库和同归的时候； | and in addition the times of the tomb and of returning together; |
> | 空手鬼爻同此断， | 空手求财，鬼爻也照这样断； | for seeking wealth with empty hands, the Pressure line is judged the same way; |
> | 博戏生旺亦同推（1）。 | 博戏，看生旺也这样推。 | for gambling, the thriving state is reckoned in the same way. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「同归」书里没有解释，这里照字译。这首歌诀后面还有行人、病、失物、婚姻、求官、官司、阴宅、药、天时各条，属于别的问题域。原文里的（1）是整理本给今人注文编的号。
>
> *Note:* 'Returning together' is not explained in the book and is translated literally. The verse goes on with travellers, illness, lost things, marriage, seeking office, lawsuits, graves, medicine and weather, which belong to other domains. The (1) in the Chinese is a number the transcription gave to a modern note.
