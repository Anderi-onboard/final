# 买卖：财爻持世克身得利，发动克身亦利；外克内、应克世易得财，内克外、世克应难得利

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买卖：财爻持世、克卦身，得利；发动去克卦身，也得利。外卦克内卦、应克世，容易得财；内卦克外卦、世克应，难得利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a trade: Wealth sitting on the Self and controlling the Body brings profit, and moving to control the Body also brings profit. The outer trigram controlling the inner and the Other controlling the Self: money comes easily; the inner controlling the outer and the Self controlling the Other: profit is hard to get.
