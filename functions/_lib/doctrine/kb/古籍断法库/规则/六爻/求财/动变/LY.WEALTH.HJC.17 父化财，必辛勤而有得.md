# 父化财，必辛勤而有得

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父化财：辛劳后得。兄化财：先散后聚（后利前不利）。官化财：利公门、谒贵、九流手艺求财；私财先惊后喜、先阻后得；若官鬼克世，为「助鬼伤身」，不吉（王洪绪）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource turning into Wealth: gain after toil. Peer turning into Wealth: first scattered, then gathered (good later, not at first). Pressure turning into Wealth: favours official money, patrons and people who live by a trade; for private money, fright then joy, obstruction then gain; if Pressure controls the Self line it is 'feeding the ghost that harms you', unlucky (Wang Hongxu).

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7812 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父化财，不自然而得，必勤劳后得。 | 父母化财，财不会自然到手，一定要勤劳之后才得。 | Resource turning into Wealth: the money does not come of itself; it comes after hard work. |
> | 兄化财，先散后聚；或利于后，不利于前。 | 兄弟化财，先散后聚；或者后面有利，前面不利。 | Peer turning into Wealth: first scattered, then gathered; or favourable later, not at first. |
> | 官化财，最利公门谒贵，及九流艺术之人，求财十分有望。 | 官鬼化财，最利于在官府、拜见权贵求财，以及九流、手艺之人，求财很有希望。 | Pressure turning into Wealth is best for money sought in official quarters or from patrons, and for people who live by a trade or an art; the money is very likely. |
> | 私财，则先惊后喜，或先阻后得。 | 如果是私人的财，就先惊后喜，或先有阻碍后得到。 | For private money, first a fright and then joy, or first obstruction and then gain. |
