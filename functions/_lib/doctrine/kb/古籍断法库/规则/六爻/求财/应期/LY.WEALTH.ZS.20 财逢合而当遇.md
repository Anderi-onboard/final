# 财逢合而当遇

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财逢合正当时：应爻发动而在逢合之月得价，或财衰静而占时得日月生合，价眼下正好（书中例：贲卦，财逢月合，速去卖，得价一倍多）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth meeting a combination: the moment has come; either the moving Other line gets its price in the month that combines with it, or a weak still Wealth is generated or combined by the Day and Month at the time of casting and the price is right now (case: the hexagram Bi, Wealth combined by the Month; he went at once and sold at more than double).

## 《增删卜易·囤货卖货章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11404 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Stockpiling and selling goods", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财逢合而当遇。 | 财爻逢合，正当其时。 | Wealth meeting a combination: the moment has come. |
> | 合者，有应动而逢合之月而得价者， | 所谓合，有应爻发动、在逢合之月得价的， | Combination here means either the Other line moving and the price coming in the month that combines with it, |
> | 亦有财爻衰静，占时得遇日月生合，其价目下正及时者。 | 也有财爻衰弱安静，占时正好遇日月生合，价格眼下正是时候的。 | or a weak, still Wealth line that at the time of casting is generated or combined by the Day and Month, so the price is right now. |

## 书中卦例

> | 但因世爻暗动，身动方可脱卸。 | 只因为世爻暗动，人要动身才能卖掉。 | But since the Self line was stirred by a hidden clash, he would have to set out to sell. |
