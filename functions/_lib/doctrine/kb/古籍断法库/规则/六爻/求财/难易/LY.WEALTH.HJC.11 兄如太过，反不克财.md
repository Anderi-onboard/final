# 兄如太过，反不克财

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一个兄弟旺动最不利。兄弟重叠太过（月日动变俱兄）时，经文说反不克财：旧注说到财爻生旺日可得；王洪绪说须见子孙发动；觉子不同意，认为到兄弟入墓或受损克之日仍会劫财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A single strong moving Peer line is the worst case. When Peer is in excess (Month, Day, moving and changed lines all Peer) the classic says it does not rob after all: the old commentary says the money comes on a day that births or empowers Wealth; Wang Hongxu says only if Output moves; Juezi disagrees: excessive Peer still robs on the day it enters its tomb or is cut down.

^q7

> [!warning] 打架
> 兄弟太过劫不劫财，三家说法不同：旧注说太过反而不劫，到财生旺日可得；王洪绪说要见子孙发动才不劫、利无穷，子孙静则不吉；觉子说太过的兄弟到入墓或受损克之日照样劫财，并举了一个九月兄弟入墓破财的例子。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Does excessive Peer rob or not? The old commentary: excess means it does not rob, and the money comes on a day that births or empowers Wealth. Wang Hongxu: it does not rob only if Output moves, and then the profit is endless; with Output still it is unlucky. Juezi: excessive Peer still robs on the day it enters its tomb or is cut down, and gives a case where the loss came in the ninth month when Peer entered its tomb.
