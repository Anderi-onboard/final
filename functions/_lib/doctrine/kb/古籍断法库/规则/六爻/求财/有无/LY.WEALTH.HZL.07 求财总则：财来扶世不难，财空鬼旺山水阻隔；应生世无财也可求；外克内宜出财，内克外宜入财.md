# 求财总则：财来扶世不难，财空鬼旺山水阻隔；应生世无财也可求；外克内宜出财，内克外宜入财

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求财：财来扶世，求起来不难；财空、官鬼旺，隔着千山万水。与吉神合、并与世应相合，六爻上有财就可以得到。白虎临财、临应，先生气；比和无关；官鬼动就要费力经营。应生世，没有财也可以求；外生内、应生世，或比和不落空，现在没有，将来还有钱回来。死气的财在长生日得到。外卦克内卦宜出钱，内卦克外卦宜进钱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking wealth: Wealth coming to support the Self, seeking is not hard; Wealth void and Pressure strong, a thousand rivers and ten thousand hills lie in the way. Combining with an auspicious spirit and with the Self and the Other, and with Wealth among the six lines, it can be had. The White Tiger on Wealth or on the Other is angry first; in harmony it does not matter; Pressure moving, you must work at it. The Other generating the Self, even with no Wealth it can be sought; the outer generating the inner and the Other generating the Self, or harmony without void: none yet, but money still comes back. Wealth in the dead state is got on a Birth day. The outer trigram controlling the inner suits paying out; the inner controlling the outer suits bringing in.
