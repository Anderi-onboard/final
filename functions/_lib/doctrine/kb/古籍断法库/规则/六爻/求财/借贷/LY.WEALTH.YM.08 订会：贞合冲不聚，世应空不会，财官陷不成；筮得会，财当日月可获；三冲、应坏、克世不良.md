# 订会：贞合冲不聚，世应空不会，财官陷不成；筮得会，财当日月可获；三冲、应坏、克世不良

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 订会（合会、摇会）和谋事相类：贞卦合了又变冲，就聚不成；世应空，就不成会；财官陷落，就不成；替人说合也是这样。占是否得会，财值日、值月就可以得到；占能否有始有终，三冲、应坏、克世都不好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Forming a savings club is like scheming a matter: a Zhen hexagram that combines and then turns to a clash does not gather; the Self and Other Void means no club; Wealth and Pressure sunk means it does not come about; brokering for another is the same. Divining whether the club is obtained: it can be had when Wealth rules the day or the month; divining whether it lasts to the end: three clashes, the Other spoiled, or the Other controlling the Self are all bad.

> [!note] 按
> 「贞合冲」读法不定：可读作贞卦由合变冲，也可读作贞卦逢合、逢冲；这里取前一种。第二段的两个卦例是书里附的小注。
>
> *Note:* How to read 'Zhen combining and clashing' is not settled: it can mean the Zhen hexagram turning from a combination to a clash, or the Zhen hexagram meeting a combination or a clash; this entry takes the first. The two cases in the second passage are the book's own small note.
