# 开张贩卖、营运抽拈、停塌赌博、索欠觅借，都要财爻旺相、日辰生扶、官静；福德旺相也可有财

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开张、贩卖、营运、抽拈、囤货、赌博、讨债、借钱这些，都要财爻旺相，有日辰生扶，官爻安静才吉祥；福德旺相，也可以有财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Opening a business, hawking goods, running a trade, drawing lots, storing goods, gambling, collecting debts and seeking loans all need the Wealth line thriving and supported by the Day, with Pressure quiet for it to be auspicious; if Output is thriving there can be wealth as well.

## 《海底眼·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 654 行 · Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 至如开张贩卖， | 至于开张、贩卖， | As for opening a business and hawking goods, |
> | 营运抽拈， | 营运、抽拈， | running a trade and drawing lots, |
> | 停塌赌博， | 囤货存放、赌博， | storing goods and gambling, |
> | 索欠觅借， | 讨债、借钱， | collecting debts and seeking loans, |
> | 并要财爻旺相， | 都要财爻旺相， | all need the Wealth line thriving, |
> | 日辰生扶， | 有日辰生扶， | with the support of the Day, |
> | 官静言祥。 | 官爻安静，才说吉祥。 | and Pressure quiet, only then is it called auspicious. |
> | 如福德旺相， | 如果福德旺相， | If Output is thriving, |
> | 亦可有财。 | 也可以有财。 | there can be wealth as well. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「停塌」指存放货物的塌房（货栈），这里按此译作囤货；「抽拈」原文没有解释，照字译作「抽签」一类的事。
>
> *Note:* 'Ting ta' refers to a goods-shed or warehouse and is rendered as storing goods; 'drawing lots' is translated literally as the original does not explain it.
