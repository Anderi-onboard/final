# 公私用事：占买卖、占求财看财爻；占财必用子孙；兄弟是破财之人，不主不辅

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 公事用官爻，私事用财爻：占买卖钱财、占家事、占婚姻、占求财，都看财爻。天下的事总起来不出财、官两个字；占官必用父母，占财必用子孙；兄弟是破财的人，既不是主也不是辅，不必去看。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Public business takes the Pressure line and private business the Wealth line: trade and money, household affairs, marriage and money-seeking all look to the Wealth line. The affairs of the world come down to the two words Wealth and Pressure; seeking office always uses Resource and seeking wealth always uses Output; Peer is the one who breaks Wealth, neither chief nor helper, and need not be looked at.

## 《火珠林·公私用事》原文

> [!quote]+ [[火珠林]] 第 77 行 · Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Public and private business", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 或问：言公私用事，只言财官，而不及父子兄弟，何也？ | 有人问：说公事私事，只说财和官，而不说父母、子孙、兄弟，为什么？ | Someone asks: in speaking of public and private business you speak only of Wealth and Pressure and not of Resource, Output and Peer - why? |
> | 答曰： | 回答说： | The answer: |
> | 天下之事，散而言之，纷若物色； | 天下的事，分开来说，纷纷杂杂像各种颜色； | the affairs of the world, spoken of one by one, are as varied as colours; |
> | 总而言之，不出财官二字。 | 总起来说，逃不出财、官两个字。 | taken together, they come down to the two words Wealth and Pressure. |
> | 占官必用父母，占财必用子孙。 | 占官一定用到父母，占财一定用到子孙； | seeking office always uses Resource, seeking wealth always uses Output; |
> | 兄弟是破财之人，不为主、不为辅，何必看也？ | 兄弟是破财的人，既不是主，也不是辅，何必去看它？ | Peer is the one who breaks Wealth, neither chief nor helper; why look at it? |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 兄弟要不要看：《火珠林》说兄弟是破财的人、不主不辅，不必去看；《黄金策》《易隐》《增删卜易》里却有大量条目专看兄弟动不动、旺不旺、持世还是克世（如兄弟交重、兄弟持世、兄弟克世）。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whether to look at Peer: the Huozhu Lin says Peer is only the one who breaks Wealth, neither chief nor helper, and need not be looked at; the Huangjin Ce, the Yi Yin and the Zengshan Buyi devote a great many entries to whether Peer moves, is strong, sits on the Self or controls it.
