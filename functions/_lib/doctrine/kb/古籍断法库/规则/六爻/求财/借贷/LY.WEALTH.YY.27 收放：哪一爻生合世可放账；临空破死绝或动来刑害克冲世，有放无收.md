# 收放：哪一爻生合世可放账；临空破死绝或动来刑害克冲世，有放无收

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 放账收账：《管公口诀》说，六爻是钱财往来的路；哪一爻生合世爻，可以放账；如果它落在空、破、死、绝上，或者发动来刑、害、克、冲世爻，放出去收不回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Extending and collecting credit: Guan Gong's rhymes say the six lines are the roads by which money comes and goes; a line that generates or combines with the Self, you may lend on credit; if it falls in void, break, death or extinction, or moves to punish, harm, control or clash the Self, you lend and never collect.

## 《管公口诀》(《易隐》引)
