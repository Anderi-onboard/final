# 子孙为财之源：加青龙发动，不问财衰旺可求；父母动、财源绝再遇白虎凶；财旺生世也只一度

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙是财的源头，再加青龙发动，不管财爻旺衰，一定可以谋求，是大吉之兆；父母动则子孙受伤，不能生财；财源已断再遇白虎同上，凶；即使财旺、生合世爻，也只能得一次，不能再谋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the source of Wealth; with the Azure Dragon added and moving, then whether the Wealth line is strong or weak it can surely be sought, a sign of great good fortune; Resource moving hurts Output, which then cannot generate Wealth; the source already cut and the White Tiger joining is ill; even if Wealth is strong and generates or combines with the Self line it allows one time only and cannot be sought again.
