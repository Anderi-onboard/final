# 得何财：看财入何宫、属何支

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问得什么财：看财爻落在哪个卦宫（乾金玉珠宝骡马、坤田土布帛五谷牛、震竹木茶、巽山林园圃、坎池沼鱼盐酒醋猪、离窗灶炉冶书吏、艮坟石山路、兑姬妾缸瓷羊五金），属哪个地支（子池荡石灰布帛……亥楼台仓库绸绢鱼介猪）。野鹤认为这类以五行定物的说法屡试不验（见 LY.WEALTH.ZS.26）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* What kind of money: see which palace the Wealth line lies in (Qian gold, jade, jewels, mules and horses; Kun land, cloth, grain, cattle; Zhen bamboo, timber, tea; Xun forests and gardens; Kan ponds, fish and salt, wine and vinegar, pigs; Li stoves, furnaces, clerks' work; Gen graves, stone, mountain paths; Dui concubines, jars, sheep, metals) and which branch it falls on (Zi ponds, lime, cloth ... Hai towers, granaries, silk, fish, pigs). Yehe held such element-based readings of goods unreliable (see LY.WEALTH.ZS.26).
