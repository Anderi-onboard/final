# 用财六看：世下财有无、日辰生扶、伏在哪一爻下、忌兄弟持世、独发要生世

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用财（以财为用神）的几个要点：财伏在世下，忌伏官；要妻财或生财的子孙值日；财喜伏在子、父之下，忌伏在鬼、兄之下；忌兄弟持世，须日辰旺相、用爻透出才不怕；用财旺相，伏动都要生世。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Points for using Wealth as the Subject: Wealth hidden under the Self should not be hidden under Pressure; the Wealth line or Output that gives it life should rule the day; Wealth likes to be hidden under Output or Resource and dislikes being hidden under Pressure or Peer; Peer holding the Self is to be avoided unless the Day is thriving and the Subject shows; a thriving Wealth, hidden or moving, must generate the Self.

> [!note] 按
> 这一节原来官、财并说，本库只取用财的句子。「为子孙生财吉」的例子是子水为财、申金值日，因为金生水。「亦费力中得」后面紧接着「六看一爻独发」，两行在整理本里连在一起，这里在「亦费力中得」处断开。「用财伏世下，忌伏官」一句里的「官」指官鬼。
>
> *Note:* The section originally speaks of Pressure and Wealth together; this library takes only the sentences about Wealth. In the example, Zi Water is Wealth and Shen Metal rules the day because Metal generates Water. The line ending 'and even then it is gained by effort' runs straight into 'Sixth, look at a single line moving' in the transcription, and this entry breaks it there. The 'official' in 'Wealth hidden under the Self should not be hidden under the official' means Pressure.
