# 世遭刑克，赌博者必输

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博：世旺应衰、世动应静为好；世克应我胜，应克世他胜。兄鬼动来刑克世爻、世临兄弟或世空：不胜；世应静空：赌不成；世坐官鬼：防合谋骗局；间爻动出兄鬼：争斗；内外无财：难胜；世应空动：赊账赌；兄鬼化财先败后胜，财化兄鬼先胜后败；卦身临兄：终是输兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Gambling: best with the Self line strong and the Other weak, the Self moving and the Other still; Self controlling Other, I win; Other controlling Self, they win. Peer or Pressure moving to punish or control the Self line, Peer on the Self line, or a void Self line: no win; Self and Other still and void: no game; Pressure on the Self line: beware of a conspiracy; Peer or Pressure moving on the between lines: a fight; no Wealth in either trigram: hard to win; Self and Other void yet moving: gambling on credit; Peer or Pressure turning into Wealth, lose then win; Wealth turning into Peer or Pressure, win then lose; Peer on the Body line: a losing sign whatever you do.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7878 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占赌博，要世旺应衰，世动应静。 | 凡是占赌博，要世爻旺、应爻衰，世爻动、应爻静。 | In any gambling question, the Self line should be strong and the Other weak, the Self moving and the Other still. |
> | 世克应我胜，应克世他胜。 | 世克应我赢，应克世对方赢。 | Self controlling Other: I win; Other controlling Self: they win. |
> | 兄鬼动来刑克世爻，或临兄弟，或临自空，皆主不胜。 | 兄弟、官鬼动来刑克世爻，或世爻临兄弟，或世爻自己落空，都主不赢。 | Peer or Pressure moving to punish or control the Self line, or the Self line carrying Peer, or the Self line void: all mean no win. |
> | 世应静空，赌博不成。 | 世应安静而落空，赌局开不成。 | Self and Other still and void: the game does not happen. |
> | 世坐官爻，防他合谋骗我。 | 世爻坐官鬼，防对方合伙骗我。 | Pressure on the Self line: beware of the others conspiring to cheat me. |
> | 间爻动出兄弟官鬼，多主争斗。 | 间爻动出兄弟、官鬼，多半有争斗。 | Peer or Pressure moving on the between lines: most likely a fight. |
> | 内外俱无财，亦不能胜。 | 内外卦都没有财，也赢不了。 | No Wealth in either trigram: no win either. |
> | 更遇世应空动，必是赌赊。 | 再遇世应空而发动，一定是赊账的赌。 | If Self and Other are void yet moving, it is gambling on credit. |
> | 兄鬼化财，先败后胜；财化兄鬼，先胜后败。 | 兄弟、官鬼化财，先输后赢；财化兄弟、官鬼，先赢后输。 | Peer or Pressure turning into Wealth: lose first, win later; Wealth turning into Peer or Pressure: win first, lose later. |
> | 坐方宜财福之地，世变财福，更宜易换赌色。 | 坐的方位宜在财、福所临之方；世爻变出财、福，更宜换换赌具。 | Sit facing the direction of Wealth or Output; if the Self line turns into Wealth or Output, it is a good time to change the dice or the game. |
> | 大怕卦身临兄弟，任换赌色，终是输兆。 | 最怕卦身临兄弟，怎么换赌具都是输的兆头。 | Worst of all is the Body line carrying Peer: whatever you change, it is a losing sign. |
