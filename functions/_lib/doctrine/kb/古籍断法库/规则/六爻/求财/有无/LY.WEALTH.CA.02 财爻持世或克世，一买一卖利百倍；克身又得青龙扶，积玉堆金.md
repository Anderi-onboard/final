# 财爻持世或克世，一买一卖利百倍；克身又得青龙扶，积玉堆金

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻持世，或克世，一买一卖，利润百倍；克身又有青龙扶助，金玉堆积得算不清。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth on the Self or controlling it brings a hundredfold profit on one purchase and one sale; controlling the Body as well and supported by the Azure Dragon, gold and jade pile up beyond counting.
