# 子孙发动伤官鬼，行人买卖身康泰；子化财宜守旧，托人办事人心不一

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动，伤克官鬼，出门的人和做买卖的人都平安康泰；子孙化成财，宜于守旧，托人办事，对方心不在一处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output moving and harming Pressure leaves the traveller and the trader safe and well; Output changing into Wealth suits keeping to the old, and if one entrusts a favour, the other is not of one mind.

> *Note:* This seven-character verse covers illness, marriage, exams, travellers, lawsuits and lost things together; this library takes the whole verse, of which only a line or two concerns wealth, and does not cite the modern translation printed after it.
