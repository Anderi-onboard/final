# 财生库旺聚钱财；财休囚货贱如尘宜措置，财旺相货贵如金宜乘早弃

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财得生、库又旺，钱财聚拢；子孙动、青龙交，正称心意。财爻落在废、休、囚，货贱如尘土，宜于买进；财爻旺相，货贵如金，有货必须趁早出手。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth generated and its store strong, money gathers; Output moving and the Dragon joining, all as one wishes. The Wealth line abandoned, resting or trapped, the goods are as cheap as dust and right to buy in; the Wealth line strong, the goods are as dear as gold and whoever has goods must sell early.

> [!note] 按
> 「宜措置」按字面读作适合置办（买进），《火珠林》此句作「应措置」。
>
> *Note:* 'Fit to arrange' is read as fit to stock up (buy in); the Huozhu Lin has 'should arrange' here.
