# 求财难易：财来生我、克我易得；我克财为克退，财动追不上；世静财动来生克我，财来逐我易求

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世是我：财来生我、克我，都吉，是容易得到的象；我克财爻叫克退，财静还可以，财动就像下面追高处，追不上；世安静、财爻发动来生我、克我，是财追着我的象，一定容易求。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self is me: Wealth coming to generate or control me is auspicious, the image of easy gain; my controlling the Wealth line is called driving it back, and if Wealth is still it is tolerable but if it moves it is like chasing something above from below and you cannot reach it; the Self still and the Wealth line moving to generate or control me is the image of wealth coming after me, and it is sure to be easy to seek.

## 《火珠林·占求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[火珠林]] 第 304 行 · Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Divining for wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世为我，若财来生我，克我皆吉，乃易得之象； | 世是我，如果财来生我、克我，都吉，是容易得到的象； | The Self is me; if Wealth comes to generate or control me, both are auspicious, the image of easy gain; |
> | 若我克财爻，谓之克退，财静犹可， | 如果是我克财爻，叫做克退，财安静还可以； | if I control the Wealth line, that is called driving it back, and if Wealth is still it is tolerable; |
> | 若动，如入下逐高，不能及也。 | 如果财发动，像是在下面追高处的东西，追不上。 | if it moves, it is like chasing from below something above, and you cannot reach it. |
> | 若世安静，财爻发动，生我、克我， | 如果世安静，财爻发动，来生我、克我， | If the Self is still and the Wealth line moves to generate or control me, |
> | 此财来逐我之象，决主易求。 | 这是财追着我来的象，一定容易求得。 | this is the image of wealth coming after me, and it is sure to be easy to seek. |
