# 开矿探珠淘金取藏：但取伏下金财

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开矿、采珠、淘金、挖宝：只看伏下的金财——生合世而得飞神动生为吉；伏财旺相多、休囚少、空绝无；入胎墓难采；逢冲动不止一处。世为己、应为工匠：世生旺持财福吉，空死墓绝胎受刑克凶；应生合世得力，刑克世被欺；应临财子有能，临兄鬼无才而诈，临父母为工头；应带六神看工匠性情（雀多嘴、勾笨拙、虎凶狠、龙灵巧、蛇虚浮、武防偷）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Mining, pearl-diving, panning gold or digging treasure: read the hidden Metal Wealth; generating or combining with the Self and fed by its moving flying line is good; thriving means plenty, resting little, void or extinguished none; at the Embryo stage or entombed, hard to extract; clashed into motion, in several places. The Self is you, the Other the workmen: the Self strong on Wealth or Output is good, void or hurt is bad; the Other generating the Self, they work well; attacking it, they cheat; on Wealth or Output, able; on Peer or Pressure, incompetent and crooked; on Resource, the foreman; its spirit gives their temper (Bird talkative, Hook clumsy, Tiger fierce, Dragon deft, Snake flighty, Tortoise light-fingered).
