# 六爻都静：将本求财须用神静，脱货占来动才正；放债抽拈、买卖停场、赌斗撼钱，用爻宜静，有气为先

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻都安静的卦：带本钱去求人，用神要静；卖货来占，用神动才正；放债、抽拈、买卖歇了场、赌斗博钱这几样，用爻都宜静，有气最要紧。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a hexagram with all six lines quiet: to go to someone with capital, the Subject should be still; in divining on selling goods, movement is the proper sign; for lending, drawing lots, trade gone quiet, and gambling or contests for money, the Subject line should be still, and having qi comes first.

> [!note] 按
> 这一行接着还列了赴试、见官、投谒、娶妾、屋宅坟田、失物等，本库只取求财这几样。「抽拈」「停场」「撼钱」的确切含义书里没有解释，这里照字面译；「撼钱」和「赌斗」连在一起，可能指赌来的钱。原文里的（2）是整理本给今人注文编的号。
>
> *Note:* The line goes on to list sitting an exam, seeing an official, visiting a patron, taking a wife or concubine, houses, graves and lost things; this library takes only the wealth items. The exact senses of 'drawing lots', 'stopping the market' and 'shaking money' are not explained and are translated literally; 'shaking money' stands beside 'gambling and contests' and may mean money won by gambling. The (2) in the Chinese is a number the transcription gave to a modern note.
