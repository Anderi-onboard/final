# 养鱼养鸟斗虫畜牝：以财考、以利求、以息问，还要计其时

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼守在亥，鱼必消亡；鬼困在申，猴要死；所以养鱼要拿财来考察，养黄鸟、斗虫要拿利来求，畜养母畜要拿息（生息）来问，还要计算它的时节。注：不只以财为用，还要考察得利的时候，如夏天养、秋天斗，就喜金财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure guarding Hai, the fish must dwindle; with it trapped at Shen, the monkey dies; so in raising fish look by Wealth, in keeping orioles and fighting insects seek by profit, in keeping breeding stock ask by its increase, and reckon the season besides. The note adds: not only Wealth is the Subject; one must also examine the time the profit comes, so that raising in summer for fighting in autumn favours Metal Wealth.
