# 将本求利：财爻持世、应旺相有气大吉；无气不多，空亡必无，生旺倍加、休囚减半，逢冲有阻

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有本钱求利：要财爻持世，应旺相有气，才大吉；财爻没气，有也不多；财爻空亡，那财一定没有，还要防破失；财爻生旺可以加倍，休囚减半；遇冲，快到手时有阻碍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking profit with capital: the Wealth line must sit on the Self and the Other be strong and with breath for great good fortune; a Wealth line without breath gives little even if there is some; a Wealth line in Void means there is surely none, and beware of breakage and loss; born or strong, it can double, resting or trapped, it halves; clashed, there is an obstruction just as it reaches the hand.
