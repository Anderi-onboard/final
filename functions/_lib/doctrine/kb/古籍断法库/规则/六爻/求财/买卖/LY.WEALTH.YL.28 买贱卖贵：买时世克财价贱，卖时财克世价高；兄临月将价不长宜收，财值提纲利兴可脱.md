# 买贱卖贵：买时世克财价贱，卖时财克世价高；兄临月将价不长宜收，财值提纲利兴可脱

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买的时候世克财，价便宜；卖的时候财克世，价高。兄弟临月将，价钱涨不起来，适合收进；财值月令，利润正旺，可以脱手。凡世克财爻、兄临月将、财休囚，价贱货宜买；财爻克世或财值月建，货价高、利润加倍，各要赶那个时候，不可错过。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When buying, the Self controlling Wealth means cheap; when selling, Wealth controlling the Self means high. Peer on the month general: the price will not rise, a good time to buy; Wealth on the ruling line of the month: the profit is rising and it can be sold. Whenever the Self controls the Wealth line, Peer sits on the month general, or Wealth is resting or trapped, the price is cheap and the goods should be bought; when the Wealth line controls the Self, or Wealth is on the Month, the price is high and the profit doubled - catch each in its time and do not miss it.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1974 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 但世克财爻、或兄临月将、或财值休囚，皆系价值贱而货宜买。 | 凡是世克财爻，或者兄弟临月将，或者财值休囚，都是价钱便宜、货宜买进； | Whenever the Self controls the Wealth line, or Peer is on the month general, or Wealth is resting or trapped, the price is cheap and the goods should be bought; |
> | 若财爻克世或财值月建，皆属货价高而利倍增，各赶其时不可挫也。 | 如果财爻克世，或者财值月建，都是货价高、利润加倍，各要赶那个时候，不可错过。 | if the Wealth line controls the Self, or Wealth is on the Month, the price is high and the profit doubled; catch each in its time and do not miss it. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「月将」在这里原文没有解释，按字面译成「月将」（当月的将）；下一句「财值月建」里的月建则译作 Month。
>
> *Note:* 'Month general' is not explained in the source and is translated literally; the Month in the next sentence, 'Wealth on the Month', is the usual month branch.
