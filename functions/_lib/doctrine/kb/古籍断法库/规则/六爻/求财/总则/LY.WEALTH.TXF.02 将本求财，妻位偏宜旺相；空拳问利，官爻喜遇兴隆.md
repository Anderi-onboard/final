# 将本求财，妻位偏宜旺相；空拳问利，官爻喜遇兴隆

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 将本求财看妻财：财爻旺相、克世或生世吉，世克财凶；卦有两财，外旺内冲本少利多，内旺外冲只能保本；内外财死绝、财不上卦或空亡则折本；财绝逢生仍有利。空手求财（百工技艺）看官鬼：鬼旺财厚；《断易天机》本说官鬼所临六神指财的来路（青龙馈送、朱雀文书口舌、勾陈稳当、螣蛇靠人带挈、白虎诈取、玄武暗昧）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Trading with capital: read the Wealth line; thriving and controlling or generating the Self line is good, the Self line controlling Wealth is bad; with two Wealth lines, outer strong and inner clashed means small capital and large profit, inner strong and outer clashed means only the capital is kept; both dead, absent or void means losing the capital; Wealth at extinction meeting birth still profits. Earning without capital (crafts and trades): read the Pressure line; strong Pressure, ample money. The Duanyi Tianji text adds that the spirit on the Pressure line shows where the money comes from (Azure Dragon gifts, Vermilion Bird documents or talk, Hook Earth steady, Coiling Snake through others, White Tiger fraud, Dark Tortoise hidden dealings).

## 《天玄赋·求财章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4561 行 · Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 将本求财，妻位偏宜旺相； | 拿本钱求财，妻财尤其宜旺相； | Trading with capital: the Wealth line above all should be thriving; |
> | 空拳问利，官爻喜遇兴隆。 | 空手求利，喜欢官鬼兴旺。 | earning without capital: the Pressure line is welcome to flourish. |

^q1

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4562–4564 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占求财，先达人事，然后决断。 | 凡是占求财，先要了解他实际在做什么事，然后再下断。 | In every money question, first understand the person's actual affairs, then judge. |
> | 若将本求财，必要财爻旺相，克世生世则吉，世克财则凶。 | 如果是拿本钱求财，一定要财爻旺相；财克世、生世就吉，世克财就凶。 | When trading with capital, the Wealth line must be thriving; Wealth controlling or generating the Self line is good, the Self line controlling Wealth is bad. |
> | 若卦有两财，外财旺而内财冲，本虽少而利则多； | 如果卦里有两个财爻，外卦的财旺而内卦的财被冲，本钱虽少利却多； | If there are two Wealth lines, the outer one strong and the inner one clashed, the capital is small but the profit large; |
> | 若内财旺而外财冲，利息淡薄，但可正本。 | 如果内卦的财旺而外卦的财被冲，利息微薄，只能保本。 | if the inner one is strong and the outer one clashed, the profit is thin, and only the capital is kept. |
> | 若：内外财爻俱死绝，将本生涯敛手折。 | （有诗说：）内外卦的财爻都死绝，拿本钱的生意只能收手认赔； | (As the verse says:) if inner and outer Wealth lines are both dead or extinguished, a capital venture ends with hands folded and money lost; |
> | 财不上卦及空亡，买卖经商连本灭。 | 财不在卦上或落空亡，做买卖经商连本钱都没了。 | Wealth absent from the hexagram or void: trade and commerce lose even the capital; |
> | 财爻绝处又逢生，利息依然动欢悦。 | 财爻在绝地又遇到生扶，利息照样让人高兴。 | a Wealth line at extinction that meets birth again: the profit still brings joy; |
> | 妻财虽旺不克世，却向爻中看亡劫。 | 妻财虽旺却不克世，就要到爻中去看亡神、劫杀。 | if Wealth is strong but does not control the Self line, look among the lines for the Loss and Robbery stars. |
> | 先生若也问求财，请看天仙玄妙诀。 | 先生如果也来问求财，请看这天仙玄妙的口诀。 | Sir, if you too ask about money, see this marvellous formula of the immortals. |
> | 空手求财者，不过是百工技艺之人，以鬼爻为用，鬼旺则财丰厚。 | 空手求财的，不过是各行手艺之人，以官鬼为用神，官鬼旺财就丰厚。 | Those who earn without capital are craftsmen and artisans; the Pressure line is what they read, and when it is strong the money is plentiful. |

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1221 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 旺相财空，闹处得财。 | （官鬼）旺相而财爻落空，在热闹的地方得财。 | Pressure strong while Wealth is void: the money comes from crowded places. |
> | 青龙鬼，馈送得财。 | 官鬼临青龙，因馈赠得财。 | Pressure on the Azure Dragon: money from gifts. |
> | 朱雀鬼，文书口舌之财。 | 官鬼临朱雀，是文书、口舌（说话）得来的财。 | On the Vermilion Bird: money from documents or from talking. |
> | 勾陈鬼，财稳无反复。 | 官鬼临勾陈，财稳当，不反复。 | On the Hook Earth: steady money, no reversals. |
> | 蛇鬼，事未伶俐，是于人之财，或是人带挈之财。 | 官鬼临螣蛇，事情不太利索，是从别人那里来的财，或是别人提携带来的财。 | On the Coiling Snake: the matter is not clean-cut; the money comes from others, or through someone taking you along. |
> | 白虎鬼，必是诈取。 | 官鬼临白虎，一定是诈取来的。 | On the White Tiger: it must be taken by fraud. |
> | 玄武鬼，是骗人，阴私不明之财，阴鬼加咸池，必得女人财。 | 官鬼临玄武，是骗来的、见不得光的财；阴爻官鬼加咸池，一定得女人的财。 | On the Dark Tortoise: money got by deceit, hidden and unclear; a yin Pressure line with the Salt Pool star: money from a woman. |

^q3
