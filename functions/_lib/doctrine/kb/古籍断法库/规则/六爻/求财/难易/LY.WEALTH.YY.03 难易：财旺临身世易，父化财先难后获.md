# 难易：财旺临身世易，父化财先难后获

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 易：旺财临世身，或旺动生合克世身，子化财生合世身。鬼化财生合世：从无中得；鬼化子生合身：儿子送财；父化财：先难后得（父旺财衰力多财少，父衰财旺力少财多）；兄化财：费口舌而得；子化子：两处得财。财动又化财：化去难得；财生世又化兄：得而复失或与人分；财合逢冲：成而后变；财旺而被月日动变冲刑克害：求难得薄；财冲破而世得生扶：人安财散；世逢虎鬼大杀或持墓化墓而财旺被合住：财存人亡；世遇虎鬼刃劫克伤而财空绝破：人财两失。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Easy: a strong Wealth on the Self or Body line, or moving to generate, combine with or control them; Output turning into Wealth that generates or combines with them. Pressure into Wealth generating the Self: money from nothing; Pressure into Output generating the Body: a son brings it; Resource into Wealth: hard first, gain later (Resource strong and Wealth weak, much effort and little money; the reverse, the reverse); Peer into Wealth: gained after much talk; Output into Output: money from two places. Wealth moving into Wealth: it changes away, hard to get; Wealth generating the Self but turning into Peer: gained and lost, or shared; Wealth combined then clashed: settled, then changed; a strong Wealth hit by Month, Day, moving or changed lines: hard to get and little of it; Wealth clashed and broken while the Self is supported: you are safe, the money scatters; the Self with the White Tiger on Pressure or the Great Killing star, or entombed, while a strong Wealth is held by combination: the money survives, the person does not; the Self attacked by Tiger, Pressure, Blade or Robbery while Wealth is void, extinguished or broken: both lost.

> [!warning] 打架
> 财化财怎么断，三家不同：《易隐》说「化去而难得」；《黄金策》旧注说「反复不定」；《天玄赋》注（《断易天机》本）说「本来是吉」，只是要分本卦、变卦的财孰旺孰衰。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Wealth turning into Wealth is read three ways: the Yi Yin says it 'changes away and is hard to get'; the Golden Strategy's old commentary says 'back and forth, uncertain'; the Tianxuan Fu commentary (Duanyi Tianji text) says 'normally lucky', judged by which of the two Wealths is stronger.
