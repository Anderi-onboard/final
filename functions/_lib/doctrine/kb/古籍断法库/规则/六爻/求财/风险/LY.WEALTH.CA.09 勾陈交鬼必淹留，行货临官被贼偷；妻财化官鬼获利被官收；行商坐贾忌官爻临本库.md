# 勾陈交鬼必淹留，行货临官被贼偷；妻财化官鬼获利被官收；行商坐贾忌官爻临本库

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 勾陈、官鬼发动，必被耽搁滞留；运货时官鬼临身，货被贼偷；妻财忽然化成官鬼，虽然获利，却被官府收去；行商坐贾经营钱财，切忌官爻落在本库上，不是官司送来祸事，就一定被贼偷走。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Hook Earth and Pressure moving, you are surely held up; carrying goods with Pressure on it, thieves steal them; Wealth suddenly turning into Pressure, though profit is made the officials take it; for travelling merchants and shopkeepers, the Pressure line falling on its own store is above all to be avoided: it is either a lawsuit bringing trouble or thieves who surely steal it away.
