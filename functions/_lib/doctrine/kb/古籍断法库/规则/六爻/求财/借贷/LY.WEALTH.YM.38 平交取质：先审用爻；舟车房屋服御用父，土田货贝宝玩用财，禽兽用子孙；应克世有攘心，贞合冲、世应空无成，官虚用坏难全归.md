# 平交取质：先审用爻；舟车房屋服御用父，土田货贝宝玩用财，禽兽用子孙；应克世有攘心，贞合冲、世应空无成，官虚用坏难全归

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有东西押在别人那里去取回，何必起卦？因为世道到了末世，人情物情难测，所以卜吉再去，先审用爻：人看六亲，物随用象；舟车、房屋、服御这类多用父，土田、货贝、宝玩多用财，禽兽这类有生气的多用子孙；平常交往的取质，应克世，对方有侵占的心；贞卦合了又冲、世应空，就办不成；官虚、用神坏，怎能全数取回。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When there is a pledge to be redeemed, why bother to cast? Because in a late age the feelings of people and things are hard to fathom, so one divines the auspicious time before going, and first examines the Subject line: for people look to the six relations, for things follow the image of the Subject; for boats, carts, houses and daily articles mostly use Resource, for land, goods, cowries, treasures and curios mostly use Wealth, and for living creatures mostly use Output; in an ordinary redemption, the Other controlling the Self means he has a mind to seize it, a Zhen hexagram that combines and then clashes, or the Self and Other Void, means it cannot be done, and with Pressure empty and the Subject spoiled how could it be fully returned.
