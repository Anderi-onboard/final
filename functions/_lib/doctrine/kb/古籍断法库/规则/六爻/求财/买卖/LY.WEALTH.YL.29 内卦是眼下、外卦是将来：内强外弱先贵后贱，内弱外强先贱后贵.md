# 内卦是眼下、外卦是将来：内强外弱先贵后贱，内弱外强先贱后贵

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦代表眼下、近期，外卦代表将来、远期。内卦旺相、外卦衰，一定是先贵后贱；外卦强盛、内卦没有气，一定是后贵先贱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The inner trigram stands for the present and near term, the outer trigram for the season to come and the far term. Inner strong and outer weak: dear first and cheap later; outer strong and inner without breath: cheap first and dear later.

## 《易林补遗·收顿货物章第一百六》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1976 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 106 - Stocking goods", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 内宫为目下之时，外卦乃未来之节。 | 内卦是眼下的时候，外卦是将来的节令； | The inner trigram (the source says 'inner palace') is the present time and the outer trigram the season to come; |
> | 内强外弱，必前重而后轻。 | 内强外弱，必定先重后轻； | inner strong and outer weak: heavy first and light later; |
> | 内弱外强，必前轻而后重。 | 内弱外强，必定先轻后重。 | inner weak and outer strong: light first and heavy later. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1976–1977 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡内卦为近时，外卦为远期， | 内卦代表近期，外卦代表远期； | The inner trigram stands for the near term, the outer trigram for the far term; |
> | 内卦旺相外卦衰，彼决见前贵后贱， | 内卦旺相、外卦衰，那就一定是先贵后贱； | inner strong and outer weak, it will surely be dear first and cheap later; |
> | 外象强盛内象无气，必然后重前轻。 | 外卦强盛、内卦没有气，必然是后贵先贱。 | outer strong and inner without breath, it will surely be dear later and cheap first. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 经里说「前重后轻」，解里说「前贵后贱」，可见「重」指价钱高、「轻」指价钱低。
>
> *Note:* The verse says 'heavy first, light later' and the commentary 'dear first, cheap later', so 'heavy' means a high price and 'light' a low one.
