# 空门：出家修道要安稳，子孙要带青龙

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出家修道求安稳：子孙要骑青龙。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For those who take up the religious life and need peace: Output must ride the Azure Dragon.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1942 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 空门子要带青龙。 | 出家人，子孙要带青龙。 | For those who have left the world, Output must carry the Azure Dragon. |
