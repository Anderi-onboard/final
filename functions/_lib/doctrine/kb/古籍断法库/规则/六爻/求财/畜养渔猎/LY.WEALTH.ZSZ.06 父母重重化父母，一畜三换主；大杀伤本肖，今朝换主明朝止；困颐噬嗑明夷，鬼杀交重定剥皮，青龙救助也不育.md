# 父母重重化父母，一畜三换主；大杀伤本肖，今朝换主明朝止；困颐噬嗑明夷，鬼杀交重定剥皮，青龙救助也不育

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻重重叠叠又化成父母，一头牲畜一年要三换主人；大杀来伤本肖爻，今天换主，明天就到头了；困、颐、噬嗑和明夷这几卦，鬼、杀交重，一定被剥皮；忽然见到青龙来救助，也一定没有生育，只是瘦弱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource doubled and changing into Resource means one animal changes owners three times in a year; a great baleful star harming its own sign means today it changes owners and tomorrow it is over; in the hexagrams Distress, Nourishment, Biting Through and the Darkening of the Light, with Pressure and baleful stars doubled, it is surely skinned; if the Azure Dragon suddenly comes to the rescue, there is still no breeding and it is merely thin and weak.
