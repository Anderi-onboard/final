# 财爻发动，买卖交官财吉利；财化子好望财，财化兄财少成

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻发动，克文书，应试求官都落空，做买卖、结交官府对财有利；财化成子孙有望得财，化成兄弟钱财少成、熟人之间要避嫌；财化成官鬼要防走失，化成文书办事和谐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wealth line moving and controlling documents: exams and office come to nothing, but trading and dealing with officials favour the money; Wealth changing into Output gives hope of gaining money, into Peer little money is made and with acquaintances one should avoid suspicion; into Pressure guard against loss, into Resource matters go smoothly.

## 《断易天机·妻财用变》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 837 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "What the Wealth line does when it moves or changes", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财化子兮好望财， | 财化成子孙，有望得财； | Wealth changing into Output, there is hope of gaining money; |
> | 财化财兮妇主灾； | 财化成财，妇人有灾； | Wealth changing into Wealth, the woman meets calamity; |
> | 财化官兮防走失， | 财化成官，要防走失； | Wealth changing into Pressure, guard against loss and straying; |
> | 财化文书用可谐。 | 财化成文书，办事可以和谐； | Wealth changing into Resource, what is needed goes smoothly; |
> | 财化兄兮财少成， | 财化成兄弟，钱财少成； | Wealth changing into Peer, little money is made; |
> | 相知脱嫌勿交亲； | 熟识的人之间，要避嫌，不要走得太近； | among acquaintances, avoid suspicion and do not grow too close; |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这首七言歌诀把病、婚姻、考试、行人、官司、失物等一并说完，本库整首收下，求财只在其中一两句；书里的今人[译文]不引。
>
> *Note:* This seven-character verse covers illness, marriage, exams, travellers, lawsuits and lost things together; this library takes the whole verse, of which only a line or two concerns wealth, and does not cite the modern translation printed after it.
