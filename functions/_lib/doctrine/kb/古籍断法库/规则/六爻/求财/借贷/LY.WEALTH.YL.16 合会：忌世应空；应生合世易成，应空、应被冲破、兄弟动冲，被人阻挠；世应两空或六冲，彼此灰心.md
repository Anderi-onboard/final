# 合会：忌世应空；应生合世易成，应空、应被冲破、兄弟动冲，被人阻挠；世应两空或六冲，彼此灰心

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 合会（亲友凑钱互助）：怕世应落空。应爻生世、合世，容易成、容易得；应空则对方推却；应被日冲月破或兄弟动来冲，被人搅扰办不成；世空，自己起不来；世应两空或六冲，双方灰心，事情难成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pooling money with kin and friends (a savings society): what is feared is the Self or the Other void. The Other generating or combining with the Self makes it easy to form and to receive; the Other void, and he declines; the Other clashed by the Day or broken by the Month, or Peer moving to clash it, and someone interferes and blocks it; the Self void, and I cannot get it going; both void or a Six-Clash, and both sides lose heart and the affair fails.
