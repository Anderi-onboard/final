# 按买卖的季节看财爻：冬收夏发用巳午火，秋置春卖用寅卯木，夏养冬鱼用亥子水，春种秋收用申酉金

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 按买进、卖出的季节，看那个季节里旺的五行：冬天收进、夏天卖出，看巳午的财爻，四五月有财；秋天置办、春天卖，看寅卯的财，正二月大吉；夏天养、冬天出鱼，见水，十月十一月亥子得利；春天种、秋天收，逢金，七八月申酉财可得。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Follow the season of buying and of selling, and the element that is strong in that season: buying in winter and selling in summer, look for a Wealth line of Si or Wu, with money in the fourth and fifth months; buying in autumn and selling in spring, a Wealth of Yin or Mao, greatly auspicious in the first and second months; raising in summer and selling fish in winter, Water, with profit in the tenth and eleventh months, Hai and Zi; planting in spring and reaping in autumn, Metal, with money in the seventh and eighth months, Shen and You.

## 《易林补遗·收顿货物章第一百六》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1971 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 106 - Stocking goods", base text

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且如冬藏夏货，宜巳午之财爻。 | 比如冬天收藏、夏天出货，宜用巳午的财爻； | For example, goods stored in winter and sold in summer suit a Wealth line of Si or Wu; |
> | 秋放春收，喜卯寅之妻位。 | 秋天放出、春天收回，喜欢卯寅的妻位（财）； | let out in autumn and taken in during spring, they like the Wealth position of Mao or Yin; |
> | 夏育冬鱼利见水， | 夏天养、冬天出鱼的，利在见水； | fish raised in summer for winter find profit in Water; |
> | 春培秋果要逢金。 | 春天培、秋天出果的，要逢金。 | fruit tended in spring for autumn needs Metal. |

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1972 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 假令冬收夏发，乃夏旺于火，在四五巳午之期有财。 | 比如冬天收进、夏天发出，是因为夏天火旺，在四、五月巳午的时候有财； | For instance, buying in winter and selling in summer: Fire is strong in summer, so there is money in the fourth and fifth months, Si and Wu; |
> | 秋置春卖，乃春旺于木，故正二月卯寅之期大吉。 | 秋天置办、春天卖出，是因为春天木旺，所以正、二月卯寅的时候大吉； | buying in autumn and selling in spring: Wood is strong in spring, so the first and second months, Yin and Mao, are greatly auspicious; |
> | 夏养冬鱼，乃冬旺于水，是十月十一月亥子节而得利。 | 夏天养、冬天出鱼，是因为冬天水旺，在十月、十一月亥子的节令得利； | raising in summer for winter fish: Water is strong in winter, so profit comes in the tenth and eleventh months, Hai and Zi; |
> | 春种秋收，乃秋旺于金，在七八月申酉之令而财可得。 | 春天种、秋天收，是因为秋天金旺，在七、八月申酉的节令财可以到手。 | planting in spring and reaping in autumn: Metal is strong in autumn, so money can be had in the seventh and eighth months, Shen and You. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一节和《易隐》「冬收夏化货，火财者吉……」是同一套配法（冬收夏卖看火、秋收春卖看木、夏收冬卖看水、春收秋卖看金）。
>
> *Note:* This pairing is the same scheme as the Yi Yin's 'bought in winter, sold in summer: a Fire Wealth is auspicious ...' (winter to summer, Fire; autumn to spring, Wood; summer to winter, Water; spring to autumn, Metal).
