# 八宫章·占财秘法：占钱粮、买卖经营、开张店面、远处求财、见贵求财，妻财持世宜行、兄弟持世速止；已经营久的妻财持世愈久愈丰、兄弟持世渐渐衰败；囤货妻财持世可买；放债妻财持世始终两好、兄弟持世有借无还；取债妻财持世终归偿还、屡占仍兄弟持世终成画饼、得官鬼持世必要经官；开矿买山伐木园林盐池鱼沼妻财持世物阜财丰、兄弟持世破财折本

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占财的，问钱粮能不能征收足额？领钱粮能不能全额？妻财持世就丰足，兄弟持世要包赔。问买卖经营、开张店面，以及远处求财、见贵人求财，妻财持世适宜去，兄弟持世赶快停止。占经商贸易、开张店面，如果已经做了很久，问将来兴废？妻财持世，愈久愈丰；兄弟持世，慢慢衰败。占囤货的，妻财持世可以买，兄弟持世不要收。占放债的，妻财持世，始终两好；兄弟持世，有借无还。占讨债的，妻财持世，即使目下得不到，最终会偿还；兄弟持世，另日再占；如果屡占仍是兄弟持世，终成画饼；屡占得官鬼持世，必要告官。占开矿以及煤窑祇山，以及买山、伐木、园林、盐池、鱼沼，凡是靠山岗江海取利的，妻财持世，物阜财丰；兄弟持世，破财折本。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for wealth: asked whether the grain and money levies can be collected in full, or whether the stipend can be drawn in full, Wealth holding the Self means full and abundant, and Peer holding the Self means making up the loss oneself. Asked about trade and management, opening a shop, seeking wealth far away or seeking wealth by seeing a noble, Wealth holding the Self is fit to go, and Peer holding the Self means stop quickly. In divining for trade and commerce or a shop already long in business, asking whether it will prosper or fail in the future: Wealth holding the Self, the longer the richer; Peer holding the Self, it gradually decays. In divining for hoarding goods, Wealth holding the Self means one may buy, Peer holding the Self means do not take in. In divining for lending money, Wealth holding the Self is good for both throughout, and Peer holding the Self means loans without repayment. In divining for calling in a debt, Wealth holding the Self means that even if not got now it is repaid in the end, and Peer holding the Self means divine again another day; if on repeated divinations it is still Peer holding the Self, it ends as a painted cake, and if repeated divinations give Pressure holding the Self, one must go to law. In divining for mining, coal pits and mountain tenures, buying hills, felling timber, orchards, salt pans and fish ponds, anything that profits from hills, rivers or seas, Wealth holding the Self means goods in plenty and wealth abundant, and Peer holding the Self means loss of money and ruin.
