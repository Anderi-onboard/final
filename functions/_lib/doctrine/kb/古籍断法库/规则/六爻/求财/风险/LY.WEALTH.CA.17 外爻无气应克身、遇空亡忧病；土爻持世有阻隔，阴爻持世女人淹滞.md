# 外爻无气应克身、遇空亡忧病；土爻持世有阻隔，阴爻持世女人淹滞

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 外卦的爻没有气、应又克身，若再遇空亡，要担心生病；土爻持世，怎么离得开；阴爻持世，主是阴人（女人）。小注：土爻持世主有阻隔，阴爻持世主被女人耽搁滞留。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The outer lines without breath and the Other controlling the Body, and if void besides, fear illness; an Earth line on the Self, how can you get away; a yin line on the Self points to a woman. The note: an Earth line on the Self means obstruction, a yin line on the Self means being held up by a woman.
