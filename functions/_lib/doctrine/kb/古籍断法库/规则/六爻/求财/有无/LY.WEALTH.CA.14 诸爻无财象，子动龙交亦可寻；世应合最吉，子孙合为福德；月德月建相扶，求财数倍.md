# 诸爻无财象，子动龙交亦可寻；世应合最吉，子孙合为福德；月德月建相扶，求财数倍

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 各爻都没有财的象，子孙动、青龙交，也能求到；世应相合最吉，子孙来合就是福德；月德、月建来相扶，求财终究能得数倍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If none of the lines shows the image of Wealth, Output moving and the Dragon joining still lets you seek it; Self and Other combining is most auspicious, and Output combining is fortune and virtue; the Month's Virtue and the Month supporting it, seeking wealth surely brings several times the sum.
