# 货价贵贱：进货宜财衰易买，出货宜财旺得价；财生旺价贵，衰病价贱；财逢合而当遇，财逢冲而当起

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财逢合，那时能遇上；财逢冲，那时会起来；买进货物，财宜衰，容易买；卖出货物，财宜旺，才得价；财逢生旺，应价贵；财逢衰病，应价贱。这是说货价贵贱、收进和发出的合宜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth meeting a combination is met at that time; Wealth meeting a clash rises at that time; for goods coming in, Wealth is best weak, and they are easy to buy; for goods going out, Wealth is best thriving, and the price is had; Wealth meeting life or a thriving state foretells a high price; Wealth meeting weakness or sickness foretells a low price. This speaks of the price of goods and of what is fit in taking in and sending out.
