# 博戏又云：世上出现鬼、被应爻克，纵有财神也输；福德休囚陷伏，白费功夫

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博的另一首：世爻上见鬼、又被应爻克，纵然有财也是输；福德休囚、又都陷伏，来回白下功夫。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Another verse on gambling: the Self showing Pressure and controlled by the Other loses even with Wealth present; Output weak and hidden everywhere, one goes back and forth and works in vain.

## 《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1298 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 又云： | 书里又有一首说： | The book also has another verse, which says: |
> | 世出现鬼应爻克， | 世上出现鬼，被应爻克， | with Pressure showing on the Self and controlled by the Other, |
> | 纵有财神也是输， | 纵然有财神也是输； | even with Wealth present you lose; |
> | 福德休囚皆陷伏， | 福德休囚，又都陷伏， | Output weak and all of it sunk or hidden: |
> | 往来空去下功夫。 | 来来往往白下功夫。 | coming and going, you work in vain. |
