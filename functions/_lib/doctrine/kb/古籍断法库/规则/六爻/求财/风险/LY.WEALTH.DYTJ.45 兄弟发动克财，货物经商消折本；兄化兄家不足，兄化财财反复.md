# 兄弟发动克财，货物经商消折本；兄化兄家不足，兄化财财反复

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟发动，克了财，货物、经商亏本，官司和暗中的盗贼耗损钱财；兄弟化成兄弟，家里钱不足；兄弟化成财，钱财反复。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer moving and controlling Wealth means that goods and trade lose capital and lawsuits and secret thieves waste money; Peer changing into Peer, the household is short; Peer changing into Wealth, the money comes and goes.

## 《断易天机·兄弟用变》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 845 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "What the Peer line does when it moves or changes", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟交重克了财， | 兄弟发动，克了财， | Peer moving and controlling Wealth: |
> | 病人难愈未离灾； | 病人难好，灾还没有走； | the sick are hard to cure and the calamity has not left; |
> | 应举雷同为大忌， | 应考遇到雷同，是大忌； | in an exam, meeting the same rivals is a great taboo; |
> | 官非阴贼耗钱财。 | 官司和暗中的盗贼，耗损钱财。 | lawsuits and secret thieves waste money. |
> | 若带吉神为有助， | 如果带着吉神，就有帮助； | if it carries an auspicious spirit, there is help; |
> | 出路行人尚未来； | 出门在外的人还没有回来； | the traveller on the road has not yet come home; |
> | 货物经商消折本， | 货物、经商，亏了本； | goods and trade lose capital; |
> | 买婢求妻事不谐。 | 买婢女、求妻，事不和谐。 | buying a maid or seeking a wife does not go smoothly. |

## 《断易天机·兄弟用变》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 846 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "What the Peer line does when it moves or changes", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄化兄兮家不足， | 兄弟化成兄弟，家里钱不足； | Peer changing into Peer, the household is short; |
> | 兄化财兮财反复； | 兄弟化成财，钱财反复。 | Peer changing into Wealth, the money comes and goes. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这首七言歌诀把病、婚姻、考试、行人、官司、失物等一并说完，本库整首收下，求财只在其中一两句；书里的今人[译文]不引。
>
> *Note:* This seven-character verse covers illness, marriage, exams, travellers, lawsuits and lost things together; this library takes the whole verse, of which only a line or two concerns wealth, and does not cite the modern translation printed after it.
