# 生计多端，占法不一

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》求财章的结语：谋生的门路多，占法要随所问之事变；以诚敬之心求卦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The closing words of the Golden Strategy's chapter on wealth: there are many ways to make a living, and the reading must fit the particular matter; ask with sincerity and reverence.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7907 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 生计多端，占法不一。 | 谋生的门路很多，占法也不一样。 | There are many ways to make a living and many ways to read them. |
> | 但宜诚敬以祈求，自可预知其得失。 | 只要诚心恭敬地去求卦，自然能预先知道得失。 | Ask with sincerity and reverence, and you can know in advance what you will gain or lose. |
