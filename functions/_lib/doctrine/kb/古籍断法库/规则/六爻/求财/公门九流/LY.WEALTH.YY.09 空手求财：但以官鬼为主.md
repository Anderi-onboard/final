# 空手求财：但以官鬼为主

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 空手求利（百工九流、到官员任所打秋风）以官鬼为主：官鬼旺动生合身世吉，财爻旺动助鬼生合身世更吉；子孙旺动伤官、兄持世动或兄弟独发、官鬼刑克世身、官财空破死墓绝胎，少利。求财方向：世爻所克之方为财方，世爻生旺之方利，死墓绝胎或受刑克之方凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Earning without capital (craftsmen, the trades, visitors soliciting gifts at an official's post) reads the Pressure line: a strong moving Pressure generating or combining with the Self and Body is good, better still with a strong moving Wealth feeding it; Output strong and moving against Pressure, Peer on the Self line moving or Peer moving alone, Pressure punishing or controlling the Self or Body, Pressure or Wealth void, broken, dead, entombed, extinguished or embryonic: little profit. Direction: the element the Self line controls is the direction of money; where the Self is born or strong is good; where it dies or is hurt is bad.

## 《易隐·空手求财占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2900–2902 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Earning without capital", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡百工九流之人，与任所抽丰之客，皆空拳求利者，但以官鬼为主。 | 凡是百工九流的人，以及到官员任所去打秋风（求馈赠）的客人，都是空手求利的，只以官鬼为主。 | Craftsmen and people of the trades, and visitors who go to an official's post to solicit gifts, all earn without capital; for them Pressure is the main line. |
> | 官爻旺动生合身世者吉， | 官鬼旺而发动、生合身世，吉； | A strong moving Pressure line generating or combining with the Body and Self lines is good; |
> | 则爻旺动助鬼生合身世者，尤吉也。 | 财爻（原文作「则爻」）旺而发动去助官鬼、生合身世，更吉。 | a strong moving Wealth (the text reads ze, 'then') that feeds Pressure and generates or combines with the Body and Self lines is better still. |
> | 子旺动伤官，或兄持世动，与兄弟独发者， | 子孙旺动伤官鬼，或兄弟持世发动，或只有兄弟发动， | Output strong and moving to hurt Pressure, or Peer on the Self line and moving, or Peer moving alone; |
> | 或官动刑害克破世身者， | 或官鬼发动刑害克破世爻、卦身， | or Pressure moving to punish, harm, control or break the Self and Body lines; |
> | 或官爻，财爻，逢空破死墓绝胎，与化空破死墓绝胎者，少利也。 | 或官鬼、财爻逢空、破、死、墓、绝、胎，或化出空破死墓绝胎，利就少。 | or the Pressure and Wealth lines void, broken, dead, entombed, extinguished or embryonic, or turning into these: little profit. |
> | 问往何方可求，则世爻所克是财方也。 | 问往哪个方向去求，世爻所克的方位就是财方。 | Which direction to seek in: the direction of the element the Self line controls is the direction of money. |
> | 世爻生旺之方利，死墓绝胎，刑害克破之方凶也。 | 世爻生旺的方位有利；世爻死、墓、绝、胎，或受刑害克破的方位凶。 | Directions where the Self line is born or strong are good; those where it dies, is entombed, extinguished or embryonic, or is punished, harmed, controlled or broken, are bad. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「则爻旺动助鬼」的「则」，两个本子都这样写，按文义（助鬼的是财）当是「财」。
>
> *Note:* 'Ze-line strong and moving, feeding the ghost': both editions read ze; since it is Wealth that feeds Pressure, cai (Wealth) is meant.
