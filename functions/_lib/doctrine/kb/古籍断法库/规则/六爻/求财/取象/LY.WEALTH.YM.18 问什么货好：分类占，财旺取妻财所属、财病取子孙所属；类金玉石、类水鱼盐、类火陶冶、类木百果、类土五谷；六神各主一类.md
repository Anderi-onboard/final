# 问什么货好：分类占，财旺取妻财所属、财病取子孙所属；类金玉石、类水鱼盐、类火陶冶、类木百果、类土五谷；六神各主一类

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问哪种货好，没有一定的办法，要分类占；财旺，就取妻财所属的东西，财有病，就取子孙所属的东西，用五行六神来考察：属金的像玉石，属水的像鱼盐，属火的像陶冶，属木的像百果，属土的像五谷；青龙木利，用于喜庆；白虎金利，用于丧屠；玄武水利，用于酒食；朱雀火利，用于市肆；螣蛇土利，用于出入；勾陈人事之利，用于农田。有所指才去求，不要自己妄断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Asked which goods are good, there is no fixed method, and one divines by kinds: with Wealth thriving take what belongs to Wealth, with Wealth ill take what belongs to Output, and examine by the five phases and six spirits; the kind of Metal is jade and stone, of Water fish and salt, of Fire pottery and smelting, of Wood the hundred fruits, of Earth the five grains; the Azure Dragon and Wood profit in joyous occasions, the White Tiger and Metal in funerals and butchery, the Dark Tortoise and Water in wine and food, the Vermilion Bird and Fire in markets and shops, the Coiling Snake and Earth in coming and going, Hook Earth in human affairs and farmland. Seek only what you have in mind and do not judge rashly.
