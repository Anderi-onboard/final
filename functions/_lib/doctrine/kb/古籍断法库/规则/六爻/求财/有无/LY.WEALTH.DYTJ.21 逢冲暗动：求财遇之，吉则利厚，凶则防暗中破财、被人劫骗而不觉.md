# 逢冲暗动：求财遇之，吉则利厚，凶则防暗中破财、被人劫骗而不觉

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 静爻被日辰冲到，叫暗动，有吉有凶；求财遇到暗动，吉的，所求的利益很厚；凶的，要防暗中破财，或者暗地里被人劫骗，自己毫无察觉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A still line clashed by the Day is called a hidden movement, and it can be auspicious or ominous; in seeking wealth, if it is auspicious the profit sought is very rich, and if ominous one must guard against hidden waste of money or being robbed and cheated in secret without being able to notice.
