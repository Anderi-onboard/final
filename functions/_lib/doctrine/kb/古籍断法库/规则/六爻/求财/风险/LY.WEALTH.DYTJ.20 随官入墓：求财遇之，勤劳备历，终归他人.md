# 随官入墓：求财遇之，勤劳备历，终归他人

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 身随鬼入墓、世随鬼入墓、命随鬼入墓，这三种不管占什么事都不是吉兆；求财遇到，辛辛苦苦什么劳碌都尝遍了，钱最后归了别人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Body, the Self or the natal line following Pressure into the tomb: whatever is divined, none of the three is an auspicious sign; in seeking wealth one goes through every kind of toil and the money in the end goes to someone else.

## 《断易天机·随官入墓》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 88 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Following Pressure into the tomb", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 求财遇之， | 求财遇到这种情形， | Seeking wealth and meeting it: |
> | 勤劳备历， | 辛苦劳碌样样尝遍， | one goes through every kind of toil, |
> | 终归他人。 | 钱最后归了别人。 | and in the end the money goes to someone else. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 原书给这三种各举了一个卦例（丑日得解卦、未日得大畜、未日得复等），那是卦例，不是判据，本库不收。「命」指问卜人出生地支所在的爻。
>
> *Note:* For each of the three kinds the book gives an example hexagram; those are cases, not rules, and this library does not include them. 'The natal line' is the line at the asker's birth branch.
