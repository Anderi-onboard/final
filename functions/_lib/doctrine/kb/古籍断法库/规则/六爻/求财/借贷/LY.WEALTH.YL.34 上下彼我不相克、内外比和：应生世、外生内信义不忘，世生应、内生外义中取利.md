# 上下彼我不相克、内外比和：应生世、外生内信义不忘，世生应、内生外义中取利

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借贷双方上下、彼我没有相克，内外比和、世应相合，是两个「我」和顺，和同胞兄弟没有两样；应生世、外生内，对方不忘信义，利钱不少；世生应、内生外，或者比和，也是情义中取利，两边心意缠绵长久。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With no control between above and below, between him and me, inner and outer in harmony and Self and Other combined, the two sides are like two selves in accord, no different from brothers. The Other generating the Self and the outer generating the inner: he keeps faith and the interest is not small; the Self generating the Other and the inner generating the outer, or harmony: profit taken within good faith, the two minds entwined and lasting.

## 《易林补遗·托本求利章第一百七》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1989 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 107 - Lending capital for interest", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 上下彼我无相克， | 上下、彼我之间没有相克， | No control between above and below, between him and me, |
> | 异姓相交胜嫡亲， | 异姓相交也胜过亲骨肉； | and dealings between people of different surnames beat those of blood kin; |
> | 应生身世外生内， | 应生卦身和世，外卦生内卦， | the Other generates the Body and the Self, the outer generates the inner, |
> | 借主常思了债根， | 借钱的人常想着还清债的根由； | and the borrower keeps thinking of clearing the debt at its root; |
> | 贞悔比和世生应， | 贞悔（内外卦）比和，世生应， | inner and outer trigrams in harmony and the Self generating the Other, |
> | 也主和谐无异心。 | 也主和谐，没有二心。 | also mean harmony and no double heart. |

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1990 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 内外比和，世应相合，乃两我和顺，同胞无异。 | 内外比和，世应相合，是两个「我」和顺，和同胞兄弟没有两样； | Inner and outer in harmony and Self and Other combined: two selves in accord, no different from brothers; |
> | 若应生世、外生内，是信义不忘，利资不少。 | 如果应生世、外生内，是不忘信义，利钱不少； | if the Other generates the Self and outer generates inner, he keeps faith and the interest is not small; |
> | 世生应、内生外，或比和， | 世生应、内生外，或者比和， | the Self generating the Other and inner generating outer, or harmony, |
> | 亦主义中取利，两意绸缪永久耳。 | 也主在情义中取利，两边心意缠绵长久。 | also means profit taken within good faith, the two minds entwined and lasting. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「贞悔」是内卦、外卦的旧称（内为贞，外为悔）。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhen and hui' is the old name for the inner and outer trigrams (by the usual account inner is zhen, outer hui).
