# 官用取官，私用取财：占买卖、家事、奴婢、求财、婚姻，都看财爻

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 公家的事用官爻，私人的事用财爻；占买卖钱财、家里的事、奴婢的事、求财的事、婚姻的事，这些都看财爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For public matters take the Pressure line and for private matters take the Wealth line; for buying and selling, household affairs, servants, seeking wealth and marriage, look in every case at the Wealth line.

## 《断易天机·占公私用事》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 887 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Divining public and private matters", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 阴阳男女，次第推排； | 阴阳男女，依次推排； | Yin and yang, man and woman, are laid out in order; |
> | 官用取官， | 公家的事，用官爻； | for public matters, take the Pressure line; |
> | 私用取财。 | 私人的事，用财。 | for private matters, take Wealth. |

## 《断易天机·占公私用事》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 888 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Divining public and private matters", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占买卖财，占家事，占奴婢事，占求财事，占婚姻事， | 占买卖钱财，占家里的事，占奴婢的事，占求财的事，占婚姻的事， | Divining buying and selling, household affairs, servants, seeking wealth, and marriage: |
> | 以上皆看财爻。 | 以上这些都看财爻。 | in all of these look at the Wealth line. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一行前半说的是占病、占失物、求官、官司、占婚问夫，都看官爻；婚姻在两边都出现，问夫看官爻、问妻看财爻。
>
> *Note:* The first half of the line says that for illness, lost things, seeking office, lawsuits and asking about a husband one looks at the Pressure line; marriage appears on both sides, the Pressure line for a husband and the Wealth line for a wife.
