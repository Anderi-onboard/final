# 求财何日得：死财等财生的日月，生财不要拿财合当日期；空亡加退度，说得再多也要迟疑

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求财哪天到手：财爻衰死的，等财逢长生的日月才得；财爻得令的，不要拿合日当日期。上下相生的，可以取相克的时候；内克外的，白说合日；再遇空亡、退度，怎么说能得，也要迟疑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On which day the money arrives: for a dead Wealth, the day or month that gives it life; for a living Wealth, do not take the combining day as the date. When above and below generate each other one may take a time of control; when the inner controls the outer it is idle to speak of a combining day; with Void and a retreating degree added, however much one says it will come, hesitate.

## 《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1264 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占求财何日得， | 凡是占求财哪天能得， | Whenever you divine on which day the money will come, |
> | 死财财生日月得（5）， | 死财，等财逢生的日月能得； | a dead Wealth comes on the day or month that gives it life; |
> | 生财财合莫为期， | 生财，财合的日子不要当日期， | a living Wealth: do not take the combining day as the date; |
> | 以此推之必无惑（6）。 | 照这样推，一定没有疑惑。 | reasoned this way, there will be no doubt. |
> | 上下相生可克时， | 上下相生的，可以取相克的时候； | When above and below generate each other, the time of control may be taken; |
> | 克外徒言相合期， | 克外的，白白说相合的日期； | when the inner controls the outer, it is idle to name the day of combining; |
> | 更想空亡兼退度， | 再想到空亡，加上退度， | and think further of Void together with a retreating degree: |
> | 百般云得亦迟疑。 | 怎么说能得，也要迟疑。 | however one says it will come, still hesitate. |

^q1

> [!warning] 打架
> 《天玄赋》说「财合日辰，方能入手」，合日是入手的条件；这里说生财「财合莫为期」，合日不能当日期。两处不好并成一说：一处讲财与日辰合，一处讲财自己得令时的合日。整理本后面今人注把「财合」读成「入墓」，原文写的是「合」，本库照原文，不改。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The Tianxuan Fu says that when Wealth combines with the Day the money can be taken in hand, making the combining day a condition; this verse says that for a living Wealth 'Wealth combining' is not to be taken as the date. The two do not merge into one statement. A modern note in the transcription reads 'combining' as 'entering the tomb'; the original says 'combining', and this library follows the original.

> [!note] 按
> 「上下相生可克时」一句，字面很难落实；这里照字译，不加解释。原文里的（5）（6）是整理本给今人注文编的号。
>
> *Note:* The line about 'above and below generate each other, a time of control is possible' is hard to pin down from its words; it is translated literally here without explanation. The (5) and (6) in the Chinese are numbers the transcription gave to modern notes.
