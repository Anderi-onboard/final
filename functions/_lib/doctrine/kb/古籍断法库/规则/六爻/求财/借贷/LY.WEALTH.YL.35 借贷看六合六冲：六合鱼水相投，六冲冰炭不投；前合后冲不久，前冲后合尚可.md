# 借贷看六合六冲：六合鱼水相投，六冲冰炭不投；前合后冲不久，前冲后合尚可

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借贷往来看六合、六冲：六合的卦像鱼得水，六冲的卦像冰和炭合不到一起；先合后冲，聚了不久就分开；先冲后合，分了又合，往来还可以。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For lending and borrowing, look at Six-Combine and Six-Clash: a Six-Combine hexagram is like fish in water, a Six-Clash like ice and coals that cannot unite; combining first and clashing after, they gather but not for long and then part; clashing first and combining after, they part and join again and dealings can go on.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 六合百年颜带悦， | 六合，一辈子面带喜色； | Six-Combine: a lifetime of a pleased face; |
> | 六冲旬日面生嗔， | 六冲，十天就翻脸生气； | Six-Clash: within ten days the face turns angry; |
> | 前合后冲交不久， | 先合后冲，交情不久； | combining first and clashing after: the friendship does not last; |
> | 今人却要细沉吟。 | 现在的人却要仔细斟酌。 | people today should think it over carefully. |
