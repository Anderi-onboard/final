# 渔猎：财官两备才有收获，缺一或遇空，鱼飞鸟走

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 渔翁猎户占，只取财和官为用神：卦里缺一个就不好，要财旺官兴同时出现，才能打到大鱼、擒到大兽；财、鬼两爻有一个落空亡，就像鹅飞上天、鱼跳进深渊，白白张弓设网也捉不住。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When fishermen and hunters ask, take only Wealth and Pressure as the Subject: if either is missing from the hexagram it is not good; a strong Wealth and a thriving Pressure must both appear, and then the fisherman lands great fish and the hunter seizes big game; if either the Wealth or the Pressure line falls void, the goose flies to the sky and the fish leaps into the deep, and drawing bow and spear is in vain.
