# 财爻衰者宜停脱，妻位当时可脱之

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 囤货卖货看财爻四时旺衰：财衰宜囤，财旺宜卖（如春天木财旺宜速卖，金水土财宜囤，火财为相也不宜囤）；忌财空破、动而变回头克。觉子：财衰可买，但要查它有没有根——有根将来再涨，无根终难卖出。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Stockpiling or selling goes by the seasonal strength of the Wealth line: weak Wealth, stockpile; strong Wealth, sell (in spring Wood Wealth is thriving, sell fast; Metal, Water or Earth Wealth, stockpile; Fire is strong in spring, do not stockpile). Void or broken Wealth, or Wealth turning into its own control, is bad. Juezi: weak Wealth can be bought, but check its root; rooted, it will rise again; rootless, it will never sell.

## 《增删卜易·囤货卖货章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11319 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Stockpiling and selling goods", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财爻衰者宜停脱，妻位当时可脱之。 | 财爻衰弱的，宜停下不卖；财爻当令的，可以卖出。 | A weak Wealth line: hold off selling; a Wealth in season: sell. |
> | 积货卖货，皆忌财逢空破，及动而变克。 | 囤货、卖货，都忌财爻逢空、破，以及动而变出回头克。 | Stockpiling or selling, both fear Wealth being void or broken, or moving into something that controls it. |
> | 财衰宜囤，财旺宜脱。 | 财衰宜囤积，财旺宜卖出。 | Weak Wealth: stockpile; strong Wealth: sell. |
> | 如春时占得财爻属木，谓之旺，宜速发卖； | 比如春天占得财爻属木，叫作旺，宜赶快卖； | For instance, in spring a Wood Wealth line is called thriving: sell quickly; |
> | 若临金水土者，宜堆积也；临火为相，亦不宜堆积。 | 如果财属金、水、土，宜囤积；属火，春天是相，也不宜囤积。 | if it is Metal, Water or Earth, stockpile; Fire is strong (xiang) in spring, so do not stockpile that either. |
> | 此论四时之衰旺也。 | 这是按四季的旺衰来论。 | This goes by the strength of the four seasons. |
