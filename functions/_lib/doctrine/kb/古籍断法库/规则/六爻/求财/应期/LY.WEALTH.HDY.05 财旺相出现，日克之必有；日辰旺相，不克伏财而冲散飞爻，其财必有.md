# 财旺相出现，日克之必有；日辰旺相，不克伏财而冲散飞爻，其财必有

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻旺相、又出现在卦上，日辰克它，钱一定有；日辰旺相，却不要克财，要看财伏在哪一爻下，日辰冲散那个飞爻，这笔钱一定有。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Wealth line is thriving and showing in the hexagram, the Day controlling it means the money is sure to come; when the Day is thriving it should not control the Wealth, and one looks at which line the Wealth is hidden under: if the Day clashes and scatters that flying line, the money is sure to come.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这里说「财旺相出现，日克之必有」；《黄金策》说「日伤妻位，财虽旺，而当日应无」（见 HJC.08）。两句都讲日辰克旺财，结论相反。一种可能是两处说的应期不同（这里似是日克之时财才发，那里是克日当天无财），书里没有明说，本库不裁。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This says 'a thriving Wealth that shows, the Day controlling it, surely has money'; the Huangjin Ce says 'the Day harming the Wealth place, though Wealth is thriving, has none on that day' (see HJC.08). Both speak of the Day controlling a thriving Wealth and reach opposite results. The two may be speaking of different dates, but the books do not say so and this library does not decide.

> [!note] 按
> 第二段里的「他」我读作财；这一段说的是财伏藏的情形：日辰不要去克伏着的财，而要冲散压着它的飞爻。
>
> *Note:* In the second passage 'it' is read as the Wealth; the passage is about a hidden Wealth: the Day should not control the hidden Wealth but should clash and scatter the flying line that presses on it.
