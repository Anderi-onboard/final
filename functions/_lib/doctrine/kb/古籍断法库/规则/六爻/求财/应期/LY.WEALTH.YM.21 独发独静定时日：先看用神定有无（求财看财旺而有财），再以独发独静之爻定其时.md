# 独发独静定时日：先看用神定有无（求财看财旺而有财），再以独发独静之爻定其时

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有人说，独发独静只是给吉凶的兆头：求财要遇财旺才有财，求官遇官旺才有官，行人看用神的生克定迟速，病人看用神的衰旺定死生，然后再用独发、独静的那一爻定日期，就常有应验，这才是持平的说法。注：独发独静可以定日期、察事情的应验，若拿它定吉凶，就要审用爻，不可拘执。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Someone says that a single moving or single still line only gives an omen of good or ill: in seeking wealth it takes a thriving Wealth to have wealth, in seeking office a thriving Pressure to have office; for a traveller the Subject's generation or control fixes how slow or quick, for a sick person the Subject's strength or weakness decides life or death; then use the single moving or still line to fix the date, and it is often verified; this is the balanced account. The note adds: a single moving or still line can fix the date and check how the matter answers, but to use it to fix good or ill one must examine the Subject line and not cling to it.

## 书中卦例

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如寅建己亥日占求财， | 比如寅月己亥日占求财， | For example, in a Yin month on a Ji Hai day, divining for wealth, |
> | 得否之临， | 得否卦变临卦， | getting Pi changing to Lin, |
> | 卯财长生， | 卯上的财长生， | the Wealth at Mao being born, |
> | 兄破不克妻爻， | 兄弟破了也不克妻爻， | and Peer broken and not controlling the Wealth line, |
> | 主应有财， | 主应该有财； | it means there should be wealth; |
> | 不得于癸卯而得于乙卯者， | 不在癸卯日得而在乙卯日得的， | that it came not on a Gui Mao day but on a Yi Mao day |
> | 日值干支财也； | 是因为乙卯日的日干和日支都值财。 | was because the stem and branch of the Yi Mao day both ruled Wealth. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 第三、四段是书里的卦例，只收判断和应验的日子。「占八」是说独静之爻是八（少阴）。两个例子前后出入：第一个只说「乙卯日而得财」，第二个的小注说明为什么不在癸卯日而在乙卯日。
>
> *Note:* The third and fourth passages are the book's cases, taken only for the judgement and the day it came true. 'Divined an eight' means the single still line was an eight (lesser yin). The two cases are related: the first says only that wealth came on a Yi Mao day, and the second explains why it came on a Yi Mao day rather than a Gui Mao day.
