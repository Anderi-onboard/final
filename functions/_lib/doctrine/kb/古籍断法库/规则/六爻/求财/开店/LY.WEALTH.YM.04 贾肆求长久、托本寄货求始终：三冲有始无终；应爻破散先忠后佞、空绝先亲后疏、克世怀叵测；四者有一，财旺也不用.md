# 贾肆求长久、托本寄货求始终：三冲有始无终；应爻破散先忠后佞、空绝先亲后疏、克世怀叵测；四者有一，财旺也不用

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开店铺求长久，托本钱、寄货求始终如一；三冲的卦有始无终；应爻破散，先忠后佞；应爻空绝，先亲后疏；应爻克世，心怀叵测、侵吞；这四种有一种，即使财没有被克破，也不要用。注：占开业得财神旺相，如果没有伙伴，即使遇三冲，不久也还有利可做；只有托寄遇冲，一定没有始终，财再旺也不能做。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A shop seeks to last, and entrusting capital or consigning goods seeks to be constant from start to finish; a Six-Clash pattern has a beginning without an end; the Other broken or scattered is first loyal and later crafty; the Other Void or cut off is first close and later distant; the Other controlling the Self bears unfathomable designs and embezzles; if any one of these four is present, even with Wealth not controlled or broken, do not use it. The note adds: in divining a business, if the Wealth spirit is thriving and there is no partner, then even meeting the three clashes there is still profit to be made for a while; only in entrusting or consigning, meeting a clash surely has no start and finish, and however thriving Wealth is it must not be done.
