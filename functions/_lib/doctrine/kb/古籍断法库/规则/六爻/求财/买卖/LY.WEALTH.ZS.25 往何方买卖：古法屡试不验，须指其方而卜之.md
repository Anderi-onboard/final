# 往何方买卖：古法屡试不验，须指其方而卜之

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 往何方买卖：古法有推方位的说法，野鹤屡试不验；要指明去哪个地方单独起卦（书中例：往楚买豆，财休囚在外卦主那里货贱，日建生世主厚利，间爻兄弟旺主耗费；后果如此）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Which way to trade: the old methods have rules for finding the direction, but Yehe found they fail; name the place and cast for it (case: going to Chu to buy beans, Wealth resting in the outer trigram meant cheap goods there, the Day generating the Self line meant rich profit, a strong between-line Peer meant heavy expenses; all came true).

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11427 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 野鹤曰：古法有之，屡试不验，须指其方而卜之。 | 野鹤说：古法里有（推往何方有利的）说法，屡试不灵，一定要指明哪个地方来卜。 | Yehe says: the old methods have rules for this, but they fail when tested; you must name the place and divine for it. |

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11432 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 断曰：亥水财爻，休囚于外，楚豆必贱， | 断语：亥水财爻，在外卦而休囚，楚地的豆子一定便宜； | Judgment: the Hai Water Wealth line was resting or trapped in the outer trigram, so beans in Chu must be cheap; |
> | 子水日建而生世爻，必得厚利，宜速行之， | 子水是日建，又生世爻，一定得厚利，宜赶快去； | the Zi Water Day generated the Self line, so a rich profit was certain; go quickly; |
> | 但嫌间爻兄弟月生日合，耗费者多。 | 只嫌间爻兄弟被月建生、日辰合，花费会多。 | but the between-line Peer was generated by the Month and combined by the Day, so expenses would be heavy. |
> | 后果牙人作弊，鸣之于官，迟误一月，花费些须。 | 后来果然牙人作弊，告到官府，耽误了一个月，花了些钱。 | In the event the broker cheated, the matter went to the officials, a month was lost, and some money spent. |
> | 幸到地头，其价大涨，得厚利而归。此乃指地而占，无有不验者如此。 | 好在到了地头，价格大涨，得了厚利回来。这就是指明地方来占，没有不灵验的。 | Luckily, when he reached his destination the price had soared and he came back with a rich profit. That is divining for a named place: it never fails. |
