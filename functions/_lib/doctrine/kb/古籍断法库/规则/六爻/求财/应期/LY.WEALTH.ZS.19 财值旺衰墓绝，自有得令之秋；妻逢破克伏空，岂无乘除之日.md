# 财值旺衰墓绝，自有得令之秋；妻逢破克伏空，岂无乘除之日

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 货物何时得价：财太旺→墓库之月；休囚→生旺之时；入墓→冲开；逢绝→生旺；月破→填实；被克→冲去克神之时；伏藏→出现之时；旬空→冲空、实空之月；被合→冲开。觉子：财受克要分有根无根——有根者冲去克神即得价，无根者财命两伤（书中例：归妹之兑、复之颐、家人之小畜）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When goods fetch a price: over-strong Wealth, its tomb month; resting or trapped, its time of birth and strength; entombed, when clashed open; extinguished, at birth and strength; under a Month-break, when filled in; controlled, when the controller is clashed away; hidden, when it appears; void, in the month that clashes or fills the void; combined, when clashed open. Juezi: a controlled Wealth must be judged by its root; rooted, it gains once the controller is clashed away; rootless, both money and life may be lost (cases: Guimei to Dui, Fu to Yi, Jiaren to Xiaoxu).

> | 财值旺衰墓绝，自有得令之秋；妻逢破克伏空，岂无乘除之日。 | 财爻无论旺、衰、墓、绝，自有它得时的时候；妻财逢破、受克、伏藏、落空，哪会没有消长转机的日子。 | Whether Wealth is strong, weak, entombed or extinguished, it has its season; whether it is broken, controlled, hidden or void, it has its day of reckoning. |
> | 财太旺者，得利于墓库之月；休囚者，出脱于生旺之时。 | 财太旺的，在墓库之月得利；休囚的，在生旺之时卖出。 | Over-strong Wealth profits in its tomb month; resting or trapped Wealth sells in its time of birth and strength. |
> | 入墓必待冲开，遇绝宜逢生旺。 | 入墓的一定要等冲开，逢绝的宜等生旺。 | Entombed, wait for the clash that opens it; extinguished, wait for birth and strength. |
> | 月破者，填实之候；被克者，冲去克神之秋； | 月破的，等填实之时；受克的，等冲去克神的时候； | Under a Month-break: when it is filled in; controlled: when the controlling line is clashed away; |
> | 伏藏者，得价于出现之时；旬空者，冲空、实空之月。 | 伏藏的，在出现之时得价；旬空的，在冲空、实空的月份。 | hidden: it fetches its price when it appears; void: in the month that clashes or fills the void. |
> | 财逢合住，亦待冲开。 | 财被合住，也要等冲开。 | Wealth held by a combination also waits to be clashed open. |

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11403 行 · Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 觉子曰：归妹与小畜两卦皆是财爻受克， | 觉子说：归妹、小畜两卦都是财爻受克， | Juezi says: in both the Guimei and the Xiaoxu hexagrams the Wealth line was controlled; |
> | 前卯木之财有子月之生，谓之有根，所以逢寅月冲去克神而得财； | 前一卦卯木财有子月生它，叫有根，所以逢寅月冲去克神就得财； | in the first, the Mao Wood Wealth was generated by the Zi month, which is called having a root, so it gained when the Yin month clashed its controller away; |
> | 后卦丑财并无生扶，谓之无根，所以财命两伤， | 后一卦丑财没有任何生扶，叫无根，所以钱和命都伤了； | in the second, the Chou Wealth had no generation or support at all, which is called rootless, so both money and life were lost; |
> | 岂可亦许冲去克神之月而旺耶？所以断卦贵乎周详通变也。 | 怎么能也断它到冲去克神之月就旺呢？所以断卦贵在周详变通。 | how could one also promise it a revival when its controller is clashed away? So a reading must be thorough and flexible. |
