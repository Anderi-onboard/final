# 应落空亡，索借者失望

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 向人讨要、借贷：应爻空（见不到人、不实）或动（对方变卦）都不宜；应爻生合世爻则对方慷慨。代表所借之物的爻也要不空：衣服书籍看父母，六畜酒器看子孙，财物（食物）看妻财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Asking for something or borrowing it: the Other line should be neither void (you will not find them, nothing is real) nor moving (they change their mind); if it generates or combines with the Self line they give generously. The line for the thing itself must also not be void: Resource for clothes and books, Output for livestock and wine vessels, Wealth for money, goods and food.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7875 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 求索假借，不宜应空，应动。 | 向人讨要、借东西，不宜应爻落空，也不宜应爻发动。 | In asking for something or borrowing it, the Other line should be neither void nor moving. |
> | 动则更变，空则不遇。 | 动就会变卦，空就见不到人。 | Moving, the other party changes their mind; void, you will not find them. |
> | 生合世爻，慨然不吝。 | 应爻生合世爻，对方慷慨不吝啬。 | If it generates or combines with the Self line, they give generously. |
> | 必得物爻不空为妙。 | 还要代表那样东西的爻不空才好。 | It is best if the line for the thing itself is not void. |
> | 如衣服经史看父爻，六畜酒器看福爻，其余财物看财爻。 | 比如衣服、书籍看父母，六畜、酒器看子孙，其余财物看妻财。 | For clothes and books, look at the Resource line; for livestock and wine vessels, the Output line; for other goods and money, the Wealth line. |
> | 又如花果看木爻，砖瓦看土爻类，亦是。 | 又比如花果看木爻，砖瓦看土爻，诸如此类，也可以。 | Flowers and fruit can also be read from the Wood line, bricks and tiles from the Earth line, and so on. |

^q2
