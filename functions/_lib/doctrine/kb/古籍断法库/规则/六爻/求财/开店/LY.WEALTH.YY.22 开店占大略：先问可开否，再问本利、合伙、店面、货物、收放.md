# 开店占大略：先问可开否，再问本利、合伙、店面、货物、收放

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开店要依次问六件事：能不能开成；本钱多少、利润厚薄；合伙人好不好；店面大小；店里的货物；能不能放账收账。管辂分爻（六爻各管一件事）：上爻财路，五爻主人，四爻店屋，三爻基业，二爻伴侣，初爻心事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Opening a shop asks six things in order: can it be opened; how much capital and how thick the profit; is the partner worthy; how big is the shopfront; what of the goods in the shop; and may credit be given and collected. Guan Lu's assignment of the lines: top, the money road; fifth, the master; fourth, the shop building; third, the foundation of the business; second, companions; first, the mind's intent.
