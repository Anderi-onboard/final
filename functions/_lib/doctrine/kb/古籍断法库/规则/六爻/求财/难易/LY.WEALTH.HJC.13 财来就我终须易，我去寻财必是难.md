# 财来就我终须易，我去寻财必是难

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻生、合、克世爻或持世，为「财来就我」，容易得；财爻虽动而与世爻无关，为「我去寻财」，难得。野鹤：不可拘泥，兄弟持世、财不生合世爻而照样得财的卦也有。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wealth line generating, combining with, controlling or sitting on the Self line is 'the money coming to me': easy to get. A moving Wealth line with no tie to the Self line is 'me going after the money': hard. Yehe: do not cling to this; there are hexagrams with Peer on the Self line and no Wealth reaching it where the money came all the same.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7797 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财来就我，终须易； | 财来找我，终究容易； | When the money comes to me, it is easy in the end; |
> | 我去寻财，必是难。 | 我去找财，一定困难。 | when I go looking for the money, it is sure to be hard. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7798 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡遇财爻生合世爻，克世持世，皆谓财来就我，必然易得。 | 凡是财爻生合世爻，或克世、持世，都叫财来就我，一定容易得。 | Whenever the Wealth line generates or combines with the Self line, controls it or sits on it, that is called the money coming to me, and it is easy to get. |
> | 若虽动出财爻，而与世爻不相干者，谓我去寻财，必难望也。 | 如果虽然动出了财爻，却和世爻没有关系，叫作我去找财，一定难有指望。 | If a Wealth line moves but has nothing to do with the Self line, that is called me going after the money, and there is little hope. |

^q2

## 《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11202 行 · Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财来就我终须易，我去求财定是难。 | 财来就我终究容易，我去求财一定困难。 | When the money comes to me it is easy in the end; when I go seeking it, it is sure to be hard. |
> | 旧注，凡遇财爻生世、合世、克世、持世，皆谓之财来就我。 | 旧注说：凡是财爻生世、合世、克世、持世，都叫财来就我。 | The old commentary: whenever the Wealth line generates, combines with, controls or sits on the Self line, it is called the money coming to me. |
> | 若卦中虽有财动，不来生合世爻，亦非我之财也。 | 卦中即使有财爻发动，不来生合世爻，也不是我的财。 | Even if Wealth moves in the hexagram, if it does not generate or combine with the Self line, the money is not mine. |
