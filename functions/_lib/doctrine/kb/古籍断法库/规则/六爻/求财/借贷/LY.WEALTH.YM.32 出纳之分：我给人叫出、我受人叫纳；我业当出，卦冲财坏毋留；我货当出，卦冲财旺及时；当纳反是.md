# 出纳之分：我给人叫出、我受人叫纳；我业当出，卦冲财坏毋留；我货当出，卦冲财旺及时；当纳反是

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 出和纳的交易，兼看人和己：我给人叫出，我接受人叫纳，问对方弃也算出，问对方受也算纳。所以我的产业当出，卦冲财坏就不必留；我的房子当出，卦冲、鬼动、内坏就不是我住的；我的货当出，卦冲财旺就及时；该纳的则相反。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In dealings of paying out and taking in, consider both the other person and oneself: what I give to another is 'out', what I receive from another is 'in', and asking whether the other gives up counts as out, asking whether the other receives counts as in. So when my estate is to go out, a clash in the hexagram and Wealth spoiled means there is no need to keep it; when my house is to go out, a clash, Pressure moving and the inner spoiled means it is not mine to live in; when my goods are to go out, a clash and Wealth thriving means it is timely; for what is to come in it is the reverse.
