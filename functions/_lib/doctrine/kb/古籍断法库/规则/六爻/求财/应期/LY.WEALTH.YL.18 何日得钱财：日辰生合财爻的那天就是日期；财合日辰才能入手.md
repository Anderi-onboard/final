# 何日得钱财：日辰生合财爻的那天就是日期；财合日辰才能入手

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求财想知道哪一天到手：看日辰生合财爻的那一天。经上说，财爻合日辰，才能入手。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know on what day money arrives in any money question: it is the day on which the Day generates or combines with the Wealth line. The classic says: only when Wealth combines with the Day can it come into the hand.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1946 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 又云欲知何日得钱财， | 又说：想知道哪一天得到钱财， | It also says: to know on what day money is obtained, |
> | 但逢合处定欢谐。 | 只要碰上合的地方，一定欢喜顺遂。 | just meet the place of combining and all will be joyful and smooth. |

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1947 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡一应求财，要知何日到手，但以日辰生合财爻是期矣！ | 凡是各种求财，想知道哪天到手，只看日辰生合财爻的那一天就是日期！ | For every kind of money question, to know the day it arrives, it is the day on which the Day generates or combines with the Wealth line! |
> | 经云：财合日辰，方能入手。 | 经上说：财合日辰，才能入手。 | The classic says: only when Wealth combines with the Day can it come into the hand. |
