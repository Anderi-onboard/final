# 占畜养：财福上卦，虎动鬼兴防损失；鬼持初爻鸡鸭不吉，官坐五爻牛马难安

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占畜养：财旺、福神相，牲畜有益；虎动、鬼兴，要防损失。占畜养先定体：关键要财、福上卦，没有就不利；官鬼在初爻，鸡鸭不吉；官鬼在五爻，牛马难安；还要配合六神来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Livestock: Wealth strong and Output thriving, the animals do well; the Tiger moving and Pressure rising, guard against loss. First fix the body of the question: the essential is Wealth and Output in the hexagram, and without them it is unfavourable; Pressure on the first line, poultry do badly; Pressure on the fifth line, cattle and horses are unsettled; and the six spirits are consulted together.
