# 兄临世，放则无还；应临兄，索则不获

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 放债、索债：兄弟持世，放出去收不回；应临兄弟，去讨讨不到（「世应同人」两头都忌）；应临官鬼克世，防负义，好意成恶意；应临财生世，对方守信。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Lending and collecting: Peer on the Self line, what you lend does not come back; Peer on the Other line, what you ask for you do not get ('both Self and Other Peer' is feared at either end); the Other on Pressure controlling the Self line, beware of ingratitude, goodwill turning to ill will; the Other on Wealth generating the Self line, he will keep faith.
