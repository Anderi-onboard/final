# 财爻驿马木神扶，成交遇贵；兄弟螣蛇是悔神；忌朱雀当头

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻带驿马、又有木神扶助，买卖成交，还能遇到贵人；兄弟和螣蛇是招悔的神，财怕散失，身怕不顺；不要再让朱雀当头，官司口舌就会牵连上来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth on the Post Horse and helped by a Wood spirit: the deal is made and a noble is met besides; Peer and the Coiling Snake are spirits of regret, Wealth fearing to be scattered and lost and the Body fearing distress; do not let the Vermilion Bird head it, or lawsuits and quarrels follow.
