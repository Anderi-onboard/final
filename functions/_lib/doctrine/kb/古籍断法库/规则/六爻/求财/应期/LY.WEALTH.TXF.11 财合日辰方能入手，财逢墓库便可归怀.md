# 财合日辰方能入手，财逢墓库便可归怀

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 得财之日：财与日辰相合之日（或财爻值日）、财爻入墓之日；《断易天机》本细分：财旺逢入墓之日得，财衰逢生旺之日得。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The day the money comes: the day that combines with Wealth (or Wealth's own day), or the day Wealth enters its tomb. The Duanyi Tianji text: a strong Wealth on its tomb day, a weak one on a day that births or empowers it.

## 《天玄赋·求财章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4593 行 · Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财合日辰，方能入手； | 财爻逢日辰相合，才能到手； | Only when Wealth combines with the Day does it come into hand; |
> | 财逢墓库，便可归怀。 | 财爻逢墓库，就能收进怀里。 | when Wealth meets its tomb, you can take it home. |

^q1

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4594 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财与日辰合，财爻入墓日，方可断其上手。 | 财和日辰相合的日子、财爻入墓的日子，才可以断它到手。 | The day that combines with Wealth, or the day the Wealth line enters its tomb: only then can you say it is in hand. |
