# 九流术士，偏宜鬼动生身

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 空手求财（百工九流）以官鬼为主顾：无官鬼最不利；官鬼出现发动、生合世爻则如意，化财更有大利；官鬼刑克世爻或化兄主是非；应爻空更不吉。但仍须财爻旺相、兄弟不动，才有实利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Earning without capital (craftsmen and the trades) takes the Pressure line as the client: no Pressure is the worst; Pressure appearing, moving and generating or combining with the Self line goes as wished, and turning into Wealth brings a large profit; Pressure punishing or controlling the Self line, or turning into Peer, means trouble; a void Other line is worse still. Yet there is real profit only if Wealth is also thriving and Peer still.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7859–7861 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 百工九流求财，以鬼爻为主顾，无鬼最不利。 | 百工九流求财，以官鬼为主顾（出钱的客人），没有官鬼最不利。 | Craftsmen and people of the trades seek money with the Pressure line as their client; no Pressure line is the worst. |
> | 出现发动，生合世爻，必然称意。 | 官鬼出现、发动，生合世爻，一定如意。 | If it appears, moves and generates or combines with the Self line, it will go as wished. |
> | 更化财爻，决得大利。 | 再化出财爻，一定得大利。 | If it also turns into a Wealth line, a large profit is certain. |
> | 须忌刑克世爻，或化兄，皆主惹是招非，不能遂意。 | 要忌它刑克世爻，或化出兄弟，都主招惹是非，不能如意。 | Beware of it punishing or controlling the Self line, or turning into Peer: both mean trouble and disappointment. |
> | 应空尤不吉。 | 应爻落空更不吉。 | A void Other line is worse still. |
> | 大抵空手求财，虽要官爻，然须财爻旺相，兄弟不动，方主有财。 | 总之空手求财，虽然要官鬼，但还必须财爻旺相、兄弟不动，才有财。 | In short, earning without capital needs the Pressure line, but it also needs a thriving Wealth line and a still Peer before there is money. |
> | 否则空好看，终无财利。 | 否则只是好看，终究没有财利。 | Otherwise it only looks good, and in the end there is no profit. |

^q2
