# 捕鱼求财：内卦世爻为人，外卦应爻为物

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 捕鱼：内卦世爻为人、外卦应爻为鱼。内世克外应、财官旺动生合世、鬼化财克世、水财旺持世、官鬼临天罡煞动，多获；外应克内世、财官空无破克、财化鬼或空破绝，无获；世持兄动或兄独发难得，兄动克世同伴欺；世为船、应为人，世应相合顺利得利；日克世、世克财空返，日合世、世合财满载。五行定鱼类（火鳖蟹龟蚌赤鲤、土鳗鳝鳅黄鲿、水鳢江豚、木青鱼、金白鲦白鲢石首）；白浪、风波、覆舟、沐盆浮沉诸杀防风浪翻船落水；管公分爻：初船主、二伴侣、三行李雨具、四网、五道路、六渔所，逢空破不吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fishing: the inner trigram and Self are the fisherman, the outer and Other the catch. Inner Self over outer Other, strong moving Wealth and Pressure generating the Self, Pressure into Wealth controlling the Self, a strong Water Wealth on the Self, Pressure moving with the Tiangang star: a big catch; the reverse, or Wealth and Pressure void or absent, or Wealth into Pressure, void or extinction: nothing; Peer moving on the Self or alone, little; Peer moving against the Self, companions cheat. The Self is the boat, the Other the crew: combining, all goes well; the Day controlling the Self and the Self controlling Wealth, home empty; the Day combining with the Self and the Self with Wealth, home laden. The element gives the fish (Fire turtles and crabs, Earth eels, Water snakehead and river dolphin, Wood black carp, Metal silver carp and croaker); the White Waves, Storm, Capsize and Floating Basin stars warn of alarms, storms, capsizing and falling in; Master Guan's lines: owner, companions, baggage, net, route, fishing ground, any void or broken bodes ill.
