# 走水：官衰无险厄，风浪不险全靠官爻衰

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 走江湖水路求利：官爻衰弱就没有险难，风浪不凶险，全靠官爻衰。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking profit on rivers and lakes: a weak Pressure means no danger; that the wind and waves are not dangerous depends wholly on Pressure being weak.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1938 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 走水官衰无险厄， | 走水路的，官爻衰弱就没有险难； | For travel by water, a weak Pressure means no danger; |

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若涉江湖而觅利，风波不险全赖官衰。 | 如果渡江湖去求利，风浪不凶险，全靠官爻衰弱。 | If you cross rivers and lakes to seek profit, that the waves are not dangerous depends wholly on Pressure being weak. |
