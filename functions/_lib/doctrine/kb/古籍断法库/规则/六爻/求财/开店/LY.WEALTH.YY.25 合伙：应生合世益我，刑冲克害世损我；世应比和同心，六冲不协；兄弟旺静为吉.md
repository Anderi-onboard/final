# 合伙：应生合世益我，刑冲克害世损我；世应比和同心，六冲不协；兄弟旺静为吉

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 合伙：应生合世，对我有益；应刑冲克害世，对我有损。世生应、内生外，是成全别人；世克应、内克外，是借别人的力。世应比和，两人同心；世应六冲，两情不协；世应六合，两意相投；应静而逢冲，心意多变；世空，自己心疏懒；应空，对方心松弛；世应俱空，两家都要退悔；应遭刑害克破，对方多灾病。应带龙喜，交易和气；应临白虎兄弟刻傲，螣蛇兄弟琐碎，玄武兄弟欺诈，勾陈兄弟拙钝，朱雀兄弟多是非。应上的财伏在官鬼下、或应上的财发动化兄弟，必被侵占。游魂卦应临驿马发动，人不守店；归魂卦应临驿马发动，常在家。福神带朱雀加咸池发动，爱吹爱喝；应合财加咸池，有私情。离宫火应带月盲杀克金财，分不出银子成色。应旺，勤俭多能；应衰，疲弱无才；应先合后化冲，有始无终。卦无兄弟，店里没人掌管；兄弟以旺相安静为吉。与内外亲属合伙，以用爻为凭。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Partnership: the Other generating or combining with the Self helps me; the Other punishing, clashing with, controlling or harming the Self costs me. The Self generating the Other, inner generating outer: helping another succeed; the Self controlling the Other, inner controlling outer: drawing on another's strength. Self and Other in harmony: two of one mind; in a Six-Clash: feelings do not agree; in a Six-Combine: minds meet; the Other still but clashed: his mind keeps changing; the Self void: my heart is slack; the Other void: his intent is loose; both void: both sides withdraw and regret; the Other punished, harmed, controlled or broken: he has many mishaps and illnesses. The Other with the Dragon and Joy stars: dealings are gracious; on the White Tiger as Peer: sharp and arrogant; Coiling Snake as Peer: petty; Dark Tortoise as Peer: deceitful; Hook Earth as Peer: clumsy and dull; Vermilion Bird as Peer: many quarrels. The Other's Wealth hidden under Pressure, or moving into Peer: he will surely take your share. A Wandering-Soul hexagram with the Other on the Post Horse, moving: he does not stay in the shop; a Returning-Soul hexagram: he is often at home. Output on the Vermilion Bird with the Salt Pool star, moving: fond of boasting and drinking; the Other combining with Wealth and carrying the Salt Pool star: a private affair. In the Li palace, the Other on Fire with the Moon-Blind star, controlling Metal Wealth: cannot tell the fineness of silver. The Other strong: diligent, thrifty and capable; weak: feeble and without talent; combining then turning into a clash: a start with no finish. No Peer in the hexagram: the shop has no one in charge; Peer is auspicious when strong and still. For a partnership with relatives, inside or outside the family, take the Subject line as your guide.

> [!note] 按
> 兄弟在这一段里出现两回，说的不是同一个位置：卦里有旺静的兄弟是好事（店里有人掌管）；应爻自己是兄弟并带某个六神时，是对方的毛病。「应衰则□弱无才」里那个字，原书没有印出来，只写了「此处之字上艹下爾」，这里照原文保留，译文里注明是「薾」。
>
> *Note:* Peer appears twice in this passage and not in the same place: a strong, still Peer in the hexagram is good (someone runs the shop), while the Other itself being Peer under a given spirit is a fault of the other party. The character in 'the Other weak ... feeble' is not printed in the source; only its description 'grass on top, er below' is, and it is kept as written.
