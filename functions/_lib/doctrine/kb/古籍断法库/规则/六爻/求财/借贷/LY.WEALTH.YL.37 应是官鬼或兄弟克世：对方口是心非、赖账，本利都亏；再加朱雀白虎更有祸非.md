# 应是官鬼或兄弟克世：对方口是心非、赖账，本利都亏；再加朱雀白虎更有祸非

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应是官鬼克世，或应临兄弟克世，都是对方不良善：嘴里说脏话，心里存奸险，不但没有利息，本钱也亏；再加朱雀、白虎，反而有祸事是非。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other on Pressure controlling the Self, or on Peer controlling the Self, means he is not good: foul words in his mouth, a treacherous intent in his heart, no interest and the capital lost too; and with the Vermilion Bird or the White Tiger added, disaster and quarrel besides.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应值鬼爻如克世， | 应是官鬼又克世， | The Other on Pressure and controlling the Self, |
> | 彼生恶计赖其银， | 他会生出恶计赖掉银子； | he will hatch a wicked plan to default on the silver; |
> | 应逢兄弟伤身命， | 应逢兄弟，伤害卦身， | the Other meeting Peer and hurting the Body, |
> | 口是心非定不仁。 | 口是心非，一定不仁义。 | says one thing and means another, and is surely unkind. |
