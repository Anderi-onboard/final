# 请会须宜财旺，更宜世应相生

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 请会：宜财旺、世应相生；六冲则财旺也不久，六冲变合（即使世应相克）可成，六合变冲有始无终，财破绝宜速止。摇会：旺财持世必得；何时得：破空→填实之时，伏藏→出现之时，太旺→逢墓之时，衰绝→逢生旺之时；财化回头克、衰静破空又六冲，会散，终无得会之期。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Starting a savings society: Wealth strong and Self and Other generating; a Six-Clash means it will not last even with strong Wealth; clash changing into combination forms (even with Self and Other controlling); combination changing into clash, a start with no finish; Wealth broken or extinguished, stop at once. Winning the draw: a strong Wealth on the Self line wins; when: broken or void, when filled in; hidden, when it appears; over-strong, at its tomb; weak or extinguished, at its birth or strength; Wealth turning into its own control, or weak, still, broken or void in a Six-Clash hexagram, the society breaks up and your turn never comes.
