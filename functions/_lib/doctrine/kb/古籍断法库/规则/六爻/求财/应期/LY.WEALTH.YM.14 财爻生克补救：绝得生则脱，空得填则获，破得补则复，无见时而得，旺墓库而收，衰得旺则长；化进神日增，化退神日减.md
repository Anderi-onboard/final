# 财爻生克补救：绝得生则脱，空得填则获，破得补则复，无见时而得，旺墓库而收，衰得旺则长；化进神日增，化退神日减

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻绝，得生就能出手；财爻空，得到填实就获得；财爻破，得到补救就恢复；财爻无（不上卦），到了那个时候得到；财爻旺，逢墓库收进；财爻衰，得旺就增长；化进神的日日增，化退神的日日减；利厚的是财化生，利薄的是妻化克。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth cut off gives up its goods when given life; Wealth Void is gained when filled; Wealth broken is restored when repaired; Wealth absent is gained when its time appears; Wealth thriving is gathered at the tomb; Wealth weak grows when it gains strength; changing into an Advancing spirit it grows daily, into a Retreating spirit it shrinks daily; a rich profit is Wealth changing into life, a thin profit is Wealth changing into control.
