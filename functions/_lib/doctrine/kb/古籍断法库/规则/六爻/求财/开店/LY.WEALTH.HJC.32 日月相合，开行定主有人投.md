# 日月相合，开行定主有人投

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开行（牙行、铺面）：世应不空，财福齐全，官鬼有气，父兄衰静，为上吉；再得月建、日辰、动爻生合世爻，客人远近来投。卦无财而世爻只得生合：门口热闹、无实利（鬼旺财空同）；动出兄官多是非；世应空，开不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Opening a brokerage or shop: Self and Other not void, Wealth and Output both present, Pressure with force, Resource and Peer weak and still is first-class; if the Month, the Day and moving lines also generate or combine with the Self line, clients come from near and far. With no Wealth, support for the Self line is only a busy doorway without profit (the same for strong Pressure and void Wealth); Peer or Pressure moving means quarrels; Self and Other void, it will not open.
