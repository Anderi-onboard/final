# 本利：卦旺财旺本多，卦衰财衰本少；财旺生扶得利；财不上卦、空破死绝，或兄劫动克世，折本

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本钱和利润：卦旺财旺，本钱多；卦衰财衰，本钱少；财落在胎、墓，本钱少；财落空、绝，缺本钱。得利：世带旺财，财得生而库旺，财在绝地逢生，外卦的财生合内卦的世，财落在三爻或四爻（门户）旺而发动，三传临财发动生合世，财伏在世爻或子孙下得生得旺，财发动化成子孙、化长生、化旺，子孙发动化成财、化长生、化旺。折本：财不上卦，财空破死绝，三传临兄弟或劫煞发动，兄弟旺动克世，父母发动帮兄弟克世，应爻刑害克破世。财伏在父母下，失一半；伏在官鬼下，耗损多；伏在兄弟下，谨防口舌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Capital and profit: hexagram and Wealth both strong, much capital; both weak, little; Wealth in the womb or tomb stage, little; Wealth void or extinct, no capital. Profit comes when: the Self carries a strong Wealth; Wealth is generated and its store strong; Wealth in extinction meets Birth; outer Wealth generates or combines with the inner Self; Wealth on the Gate or Door line (the third and fourth), strong and moving; the three rulers on Wealth, moving to generate or combine with the Self; Wealth hidden under the Self or under Output and generated or strong; Wealth moving into Output, into Birth or into strength; Output moving into Wealth, into Birth or into strength. Capital is lost when: Wealth is absent from the hexagram, or void, broken, dead or extinct; the three rulers on Peer or the Robbery star, moving; Peer strong and moving to control the Self; Resource moving to help Peer control the Self; or the Other line punishes, harms, controls or breaks the Self. Wealth hidden under Resource: half is lost; under Pressure: heavy losses; under Peer: beware of quarrels.

> [!note] 按
> 「财持旺雀」在别的书里没有这一句，按字面译成财带着旺相的朱雀；朱雀通常主口舌，这里却算得利的一项，可能是字有误，没有别本可对，不改。「财生库旺」《阐奥歌章》也有（「财生库旺聚钱财」），这里读作「财得生、库又旺」。「化生」译成化出长生，「化旺」译成化出旺相，是照字面取的读法。
>
> *Note:* 'Wealth carrying a strong Vermilion Bird' appears in no other book here and is translated literally; the Vermilion Bird usually means quarrels yet is counted as a gain, so a wrong character is possible, but there is no other copy to check against and it is left as it is. 'Wealth generated and its store strong' also appears in the Chan'ao Gezhang verse 'Wealth generated and its store strong gathers money' and is read as Wealth being generated while its store is strong. 'Turning into Birth' and 'turning into strength' follow the literal words.
