# 世应二爻空合，虚约难凭

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻空：有财难得；应爻空：别人靠不住；世应俱空：谋事无准。空而动又相合（或化空），为「虚约」，口头答应而不实。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Self line void: even existing money is hard to get. Other line void: others cannot be relied on. Both void: nothing certain. Void and moving yet combining (or turning void) is an 'empty promise': agreed in words, not in fact.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7823 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世应二爻空合， | 世、应两爻落空又相合， | If the Self and Other lines are void yet combining, |
> | 虚约难凭； | 是空口的约定，靠不住； | it is an empty promise that cannot be relied on; |

^q1

> | 世空，有财难得；应空，难靠他人； | 世爻空，有财也难得；应爻空，靠不住别人； | Self void: even existing money is hard to get; Other void: others cannot be relied on; |

> | 化入空亡，亦是心口不相应之象。 | 动而化入空亡，也是心口不一之象。 | Turning into the void is also a sign that words and intentions do not match. |
