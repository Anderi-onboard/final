# 寄放财物：财爻安静、官衰子旺，货放别处无妨；兄兴世破应空有失

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 把财货寄放在别人那里：要财爻安静，官鬼不动、子孙旺相最好，货放在他那里无妨；兄弟发动不好；世被冲、应落空亡，财物终究被消耗，这样寄托不好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving money and goods at another's house: the Wealth line must be still, best of all with Pressure not moving and Output strong, and there is no harm in storing the goods there; Peer moving is not good; the Self clashed and the Other void, the goods will in the end be consumed and such entrusting is not good.
