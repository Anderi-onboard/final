# 可开否：本宫、世爻、财爻带福禄龙喜旺相可开；死墓绝胎空破刑害不可开；六冲开不成

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 能不能开：本宫、世爻、财爻带福、禄、龙、喜而旺相，财或子孙持世旺相且不犯刑害克破，应上的财旺而发动生合世，或四直临财生合世，可开。本宫、世爻、财爻逢死墓绝胎、空破刑害，或四直和应爻带兄弟官鬼、旺而发动克世，不可开；四直全是兄弟而世爻旺相，兄弟受制，开张安稳。六冲卦开不成，开成也不久；太岁生合世而有动爻冲太岁，开不满一年；月生合世而有动爻冲月，开不满一月。财化子孙，宜守老本行。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether it can be opened: if the home palace, the Self line and the Wealth line carry Output, Emolument, the Dragon or Joy and are strong, or Wealth or Output on the Self is strong and free of punishment, harm, control and breaking, or the Other's Wealth is strong and moving to generate or combine with the Self, or the four rulers on Wealth generate or combine with the Self, it can be opened. If the home palace, the Self and the Wealth line meet death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, void, broken, punished or harmed, or the four rulers and the Other carry Peer or Pressure, strong and moving to control the Self, it cannot; but if the four rulers are all Peer while the Self is strong, Peer is held in check and the opening is safe. A Six-Clash hexagram cannot be opened, or if opened will not last; the Tai Sui generating or combining with the Self but a moving line clashing the Tai Sui: it will not last a year; the Month generating or combining with the Self but a moving line clashing the Month: not a month. Wealth turning into Output: keep to the old business.
