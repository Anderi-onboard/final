# 姜太公论渔猎：外卦为物内为人；内克外得千斤；物克人无所得；财爻旺相最亨

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占渔猎，外卦是猎物，内卦是人；内卦能克外卦，能得千斤；猎物克人，一无所得；财爻旺相最亨通；打猎宜金，捕鱼宜水；财爻、鬼爻兴旺一定称心；相克哪里还会有；杀鬼交重收获丰厚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining fishing and hunting the outer trigram is the quarry and the inner is the person; the inner controlling the outer gains a thousand catties; the quarry controlling the person gains nothing; a thriving Wealth line is most favourable; Metal suits hunting and Water suits fishing; with Wealth and Pressure lines thriving one is sure to be satisfied; if they control each other, how could there be a catch; with the killing and Pressure stars doubled the gain is rich.

> | 金宜获兽鱼宜水（5）， | 打猎宜金，捕鱼宜水； | Metal suits hunting beasts and Water suits fishing; |

> | 杀鬼交重厚获金（6）。 | 杀与鬼交重发动，收获丰厚。 | with the killing star and Pressure doubled and moving, the gain is rich. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 原文里的（1）到（6）是整理本给今人注文编的号，那些注文本库不引。「财鬼爻」在原文里连写，读作财爻、鬼爻。「杀鬼交重厚获金」的「杀」指后面说的天罡杀，书里在今人注里才解释，这里只照字译。
>
> *Note:* The (1) to (6) in the Chinese are numbers the transcription gave to modern notes, which this library does not cite. 'Wealth and Pressure lines' run together in the original and are read as two lines. The 'killing star' in the last line is explained only in the modern note (the Tiangang star), so it is translated by its words only.
