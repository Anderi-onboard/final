# 日辰克身货出手，财克地头贼相守；青龙发动最佳，阴阳得位财必有

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰克身，货可以出手；财克地头，有贼守着；青龙发动最好，阴阳爻得位，财一定有。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Day controlling the Body, the goods go out; Wealth controlling the place, thieves lie in wait; the Azure Dragon moving is best, and with yin and yang lines in their right places Wealth is sure to be had.
