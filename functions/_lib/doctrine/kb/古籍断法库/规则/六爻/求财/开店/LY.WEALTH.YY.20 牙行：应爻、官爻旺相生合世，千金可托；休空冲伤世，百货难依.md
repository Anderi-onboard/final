# 牙行：应爻、官爻旺相生合世，千金可托；休空冲伤世，百货难依

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 找牙行（中间人）：应爻、官爻旺相，又生合世爻，千金也可托付；休囚落空、或冲伤世，各样货物都难托付。应爻发动克世，或应上的财化成兄弟，难免被侵吞；应上的财发动生合世、或化成子孙，或应上的官鬼化成财，办事肯替你周旋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Choosing a broker: the Other and Pressure lines strong and generating or combining with the Self, and a thousand gold can be entrusted to him; resting or void, or clashing with and hurting the Self, and no goods can be relied upon to him. The Other moving to control the Self, or the Other's Wealth turning into Peer: you cannot escape being fleeced. The Other's Wealth moving to generate or combine with the Self, or turning into Output, or the Other's Pressure turning into Wealth: he handles matters with care and works on your behalf.
