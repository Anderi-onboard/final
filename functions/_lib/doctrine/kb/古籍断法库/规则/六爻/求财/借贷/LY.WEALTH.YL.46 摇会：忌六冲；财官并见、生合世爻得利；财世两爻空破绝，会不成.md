# 摇会：忌六冲；财官并见、生合世爻得利；财世两爻空破绝，会不成

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 摇会（凑钱互助）：最忌六冲，冲就是散；只要财和官同时出现、生合世爻，一定得到自然的好处；财爻、世爻遇空、破、绝，是用爻被害没有用了，会不成，钱也周转不来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A rotating savings society: a Six-Clash is the great taboo, for clashing means scattering; all that is needed is Wealth and Pressure appearing together and generating or combining with the Self line, and the natural profit is surely had; with the Wealth and Self lines void, broken or extinct, the Subject line is harmed and useless, the society does not come off, and the silver cannot be raised.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2027 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 摇会者乃实银赴会，祈而得之， | 摇会的人是拿着实在的银子去赴会，求着把会银摇到手； | Those who join a rotating society go with real silver and hope to win the pot; |
> | 最忌六冲，冲者散也，岂能成会而得财乎？ | 最忌六冲，冲就是散，怎么能成会、得到钱呢？ | a Six-Clash is the great taboo: clashing means scattering, so how could the society hold and money be had? |
> | 惟要财官并见，生合世爻者必得自然之利。 | 只要财和官同时出现，生合世爻的，一定得到自然的好处； | all that is needed is Wealth and Pressure appearing together and generating or combining with the Self line, and the natural profit is surely had; |
> | 财世两爻值空值破值绝，此乃用爻被害乃无用也， | 财爻、世爻遇空、遇破、遇绝，这是用爻被害，没有用了， | if the Wealth and Self lines meet void, break or extinction, the Subject line is harmed and useless; |
> | 会岂得成，而银岂得济哉？ | 会怎么能成？银子怎么能周济到手呢？ | how could the society come off, and how could the silver be raised? |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 摇会是民间的互助会：会员按期出银，用摇骰子的办法决定谁先得会银（这是通常的说法；本章原文只说「实银赴会，祈而得之」）。
>
> *Note:* A rotating savings society is a folk mutual-aid club: members put in silver at set times and dice decide who takes the pot first (this is the usual account; this chapter says only 'they go with real silver and pray to win it').
