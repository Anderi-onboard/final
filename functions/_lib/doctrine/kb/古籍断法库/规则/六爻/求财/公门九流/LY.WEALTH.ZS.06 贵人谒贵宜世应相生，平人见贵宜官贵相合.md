# 贵人谒贵宜世应相生，平人见贵宜官贵相合

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谒贵分身份：贵人见贵人看世应相生相合；平民见贵人看官鬼与世爻相生相合，或官鬼持世。问能否得见，官鬼生合世即见；问功名，官鬼持世、生世才得名（觉子：勿把所见贵人之官当功名）。世应冲克、财伏而空，去无益；官鬼克世、世化鬼化克，凶，平人勿去；财官生世、持世吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* It depends on who is calling: rank calling on rank reads Self and Other generating or combining; a commoner calling on the great reads Pressure generating or combining with the Self line, or sitting on it. If the question is only whether you will be received, Pressure generating or combining with the Self line says yes; if it is office, Pressure must sit on or generate the Self line (Juezi: do not take the great man's Pressure line for office). Self and Other clashing or controlling with Wealth hidden and void, no use going; Pressure controlling the Self line, or the Self turning into Pressure or into its own control, bad, and a commoner should not go; Wealth or Pressure generating or sitting on the Self line is good.

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 觉子曰：有心而问见贵求名者，官星持世、生世，必得功名。 | 觉子说：如果心里问的是见贵人求功名，官鬼持世、生世，一定得功名。 | Juezi says: if the question in your mind is seeking office through a great man, Pressure on or generating the Self line means office will come. |
