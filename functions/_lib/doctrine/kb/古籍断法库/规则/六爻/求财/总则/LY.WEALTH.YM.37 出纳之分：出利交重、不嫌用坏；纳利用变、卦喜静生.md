# 出纳之分：出利交重、不嫌用坏；纳利用变、卦喜静生

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大抵出的时候，利在爻交重，不嫌用神坏；纳的时候，利在用神变，卦喜欢静而生；这就是出与纳的区别。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In general, when paying out the gain lies in the lines being doubled and there is no objection to a spoiled Subject; when taking in the gain lies in the Subject changing, and the hexagram likes to be still and generating; this is the difference between out and in.
