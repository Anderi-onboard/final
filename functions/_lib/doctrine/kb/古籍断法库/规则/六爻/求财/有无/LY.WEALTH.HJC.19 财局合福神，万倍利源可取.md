# 财局合福神，万倍利源可取

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 三合成财局而子孙在局中动，或成子孙局而财在局中动，并来生合世爻：财利不绝，财旺可许厚利（经文说万倍，野鹤说百倍）；野鹤要世爻在局中或局来生世。合成父局辛劳、兄局破耗、鬼局阻隔口舌，克世更凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A three-harmony Wealth frame with Output moving inside it, or an Output frame with Wealth moving inside it, generating or combining with the Self line: profit without end, and with a strong Wealth a great profit (the classic says ten-thousandfold, Yehe a hundredfold); Yehe requires the Self line to be in the frame or to be generated by it. A Resource frame means toil, a Peer frame losses, a Pressure frame obstruction and quarrels, worse if it controls the Self line.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦有三合，会成财局，而子孙亦在合中动者，上吉之卦。 | 卦里有三合，会成财局，而子孙也在合局中发动，是上吉之卦。 | If three lines combine into a three-harmony frame of the Wealth element and Output moves within it, it is a first-class hexagram. |
> | 主财利绵绵不竭。更得财旺，可许万倍财利。 | 主财利绵绵不绝。再加上财旺，可以断万倍之利。 | The profit never runs dry; if Wealth is also strong, a ten-thousandfold profit may be granted. |

> | 若会鬼局，则多阻隔。会成兄局，则主分散。会成父局，艰辛难得。 | 如果会成官鬼局，多阻隔；会成兄弟局，主分散；会成父母局，艰辛难得。 | A Pressure frame means much obstruction; a Peer frame means dispersal; a Resource frame means hardship and little gain. |
