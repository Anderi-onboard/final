# 绝处逢生：求财遇妻财绝处逢生，财易求，利厚，不比旺相之财有限

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻被日辰绝了，又有生它的爻发动来相生，叫绝处逢生。求财遇到财爻绝处逢生，钱容易求、利也厚；不像旺相的财有个限度，新生出来的财没有穷尽。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject line is cut off by the Day but a line that gives it life moves to generate it, that is 'meeting life where there was severance'. In seeking wealth, Wealth meeting life where it was cut off is easy to seek and its profit is rich; unlike a thriving Wealth, which has a limit, newly born Wealth has none.

## 《断易天机·绝处逢生》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 56 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Meeting life where there was severance", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且如申日占，卦遇用爻属木， | 比如在申日占卦，卦里遇到的用爻属木， | Suppose the divination is on a Shen day and the Subject line is Wood: |
> | 木见申则绝，木爻无用矣。 | 木遇到申就绝了，木爻没有用了； | Wood meets Shen and is cut off, and the Wood line is of no use; |
> | 此同无也。 | 这等于没有。 | this is the same as having none. |
> | 若得水爻发动，水长生动来相生，木爻仍复有用。 | 如果有水爻发动，水在申长生，动来相生，木爻又重新有用了。 | But if a Water line moves, Water being born at Shen and coming to generate it, the Wood line is useful again. |
> | 譬如人当困穷之际，得遇贵人扶持， | 就像人在穷困的时候，遇上贵人扶持， | It is like a person at a time of poverty and hardship who meets a noble patron's help: |
> | 必有寒谷回春之象，此乃是绝处逢生也。 | 一定有寒谷回春的样子，这就是绝处逢生。 | there is surely the look of spring returning to a cold valley; this is 'meeting life where there was severance'. |

^q1

## 《断易天机·绝处逢生》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 61 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Meeting life where there was severance", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 求财： | 求财这件事上： | Seeking wealth: |
> | 遇妻财绝处逢生， | 遇到妻财绝处逢生， | when Wealth meets life where it was cut off, |
> | 其财易求， | 这笔财容易求， | that money is easy to seek, |
> | 利殊厚， | 得利很丰厚， | and its profit is rich; |
> | 非比旺相之财有限， | 不像旺相的财有个限度， | unlike a thriving Wealth, which has a limit, |
> | 生乃之财无穷也。 | 新生出来的财没有穷尽。 | newly born Wealth is without end. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「生乃之财」的「乃」字疑为「迺」或别的字，意思是刚生出来的财；这里照这个意思译。
>
> *Note:* The word 'nai' in 'sheng nai zhi cai' may be a miscopy; the sense is newly born Wealth, and it is translated that way.
