# 寄放财物：六合、日辰合世应宜寄；六冲、日冲用神不可托

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寄放：六合的爻，或日辰合世、合应，最适合寄存财物，因为彼此心意相投；六冲的爻，或日辰冲用神，不能把财货安放托付给他，以后会有变卦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving goods: with Six-Combine lines, or the Day combining with the Self and the Other, it is best to leave money and goods, because the two hearts agree; with Six-Clash lines, or the Day clashing the Subject, goods and money cannot be safely entrusted to him, for later there will be a change.

## 《易林补遗·安寄财物章第一百十四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2086 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 114 - Leaving goods for safekeeping", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 六合最宜寄顿， | 六合最宜寄存； | A Six-Combine is best for leaving things; |
> | 六冲岂可安藏。 | 六冲怎么能安放收藏？ | how could a Six-Clash keep them safe? |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2087 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 六合之爻或日辰合世应，大官寄财顿物因其彼此心口投机。 | 六合的爻，或日辰合世、合应，最适合寄存财物，因为彼此心口相投； | With Six-Combine lines, or the Day combining with the Self and the Other, it is best to leave money and goods, because the two hearts and mouths agree; |
> | 六冲之爻，或日冲主用，不得安货藏财为其付托，后有更变也。 | 六冲的爻，或日辰冲主要的用神，不能把货、财安放托付给他，以后会有变卦。 | with Six-Clash lines, or the Day clashing the chief Subject, goods and money cannot be safely left and entrusted to him, for later there will be a change. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「大官寄财顿物」的「官」疑是「宜」之误，译作最适合。
>
> *Note:* 'Greatly official to leave money and goods' has a character that is probably a slip for 'suitable', and is translated as 'best'.
