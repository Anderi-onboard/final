# 借贷求财：财持世生合世而不空破绝

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借贷：财持世或生合世而不空破绝，财逢生合之日得；应生合世而兄静财伏，或财伏而兄子同动，财值日得；父空无借据，父化父改契；世应空合，虚约；应受刑伤，求而不遇；应生合世而财空绝，想借无钱；财生合世而应冲克世，借而不情愿；财动生世而日合财，有人把持拖延；应动合世而日冲应，有人说破不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Borrowing: Wealth on or generating or combining with the Self line and not void, broken or extinguished, the money comes on a day that generates or combines with it; the Other generating the Self with Peer still and Wealth hidden, or Wealth hidden with Peer and Output both moving, it comes on Wealth's day; Resource void, no written note; Resource into Resource, the contract rewritten; Self and Other void yet combining, an empty promise; the Other hurt, you will not find him; the Other generating the Self but Wealth void, willing but empty-pursed; Wealth generating the Self but the Other clashing it, lent grudgingly; Wealth moving to the Self but held by the Day, someone delays it; the Other combining with the Self but clashed by the Day, someone talks him out of it.
