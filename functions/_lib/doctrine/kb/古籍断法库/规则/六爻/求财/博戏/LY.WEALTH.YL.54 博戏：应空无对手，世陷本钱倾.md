# 博戏：应空无对手，世陷本钱倾

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博：应值空绝，赌场里冷冷清清没有对手，遇不到输钱的人；世遇冲、陷，袖子里空空，只剩一双空拳，本钱倾尽。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Gambling: the Other void or extinct, the gaming table is deserted, there is no opponent and no loser to meet; the Self clashed and sunk, the sleeves are empty, only bare fists remain and the capital is gone.

## 《易林补遗·博戏求财章第一百十二》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2051 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 112 - Gambling for money", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应空难遇输钱客， | 应空，难遇到输钱的客人； | The Other void, it is hard to meet a loser; |
> | 世陷还遭资本倾。 | 世陷，还会倾尽本钱。 | the Self sunk, the capital is overturned besides. |
