# 出入财：内阴、外阴，财也阴的吉；纯阳的多散失

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看钱进钱出：内卦阳、财空、又变阳，不宜进财，多散失；内卦阴、财爻也变阴，如期变吉；外卦阴、财阴，出钱吉；外卦阳、财阳，宜藏好；总之逢阴为好，纯阳多散失。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Money coming in and going out: an inner yang with Wealth void and changing to yang is no good for taking money in and mostly scatters; an inner yin whose Wealth line changes to yin turns auspicious on time; an outer yin with yin Wealth is good for spending; an outer yang with yang Wealth should be stored away; yin is always the good sign, pure yang mostly loses.

## 《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1266 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 内阳财空还变阳， | 内卦是阳，财空，又变成阳， | An inner trigram of yang, its Wealth void and changing to yang: |
> | 不宜入财多散亡， | 不宜进财，多半散失； | not fit for taking money in, it mostly scatters; |
> | 内阴财爻还变阴， | 内卦是阴，财爻也变成阴， | an inner trigram of yin, its Wealth line also changing to yin: |
> | 如期变吉不须吟（7）。 | 如期变吉，不必犹豫。 | it turns auspicious on time, and there is no need to hesitate. |
> | 外阴财阴出财吉， | 外卦阴、财也阴，出钱吉； | An outer yin with yin Wealth: spending is good; |
> | 外阳财阳宜贮密， | 外卦阳、财也阳，宜藏好； | an outer yang with yang Wealth: store it away; |
> | 但阴为好吉无疑， | 只要是阴就好，吉无疑； | as long as it is yin it is good, and auspicious without doubt; |
> | 若是纯阳多散失。 | 若是纯阳，多半散失。 | if it is pure yang, most of it is lost. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「财空还变阳」的断句，有的读作「财空、还变阳」，有的读作「财空还，变阳」；这里取前一种。原文里的（7）是整理本给今人注文编的号。
>
> *Note:* The phrase about Wealth void 'and again changing to yang' can be punctuated in more than one way; this entry takes the first. The (7) in the Chinese is a number the transcription gave to a modern note.
