# 洞林秘诀·渔猎：内卦世爻旺相克外，世克应，得青龙勾陈，吉神临动财爻，无空亡，多得渔猎之财

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦的世爻旺相，克外卦，外卦休囚，世爻克应爻，六神又得青龙、勾陈，吉神临在动的财爻上，又没有空亡，主多得财，得渔猎的财物。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self line in the inner trigram thriving and controlling the outer, the outer trigram weak, the Self controlling the Other, the six spirits also giving the Azure Dragon and Hook Earth, an auspicious spirit on a moving Wealth line, and no Void, much wealth is gained, the fruits of fishing and hunting.
