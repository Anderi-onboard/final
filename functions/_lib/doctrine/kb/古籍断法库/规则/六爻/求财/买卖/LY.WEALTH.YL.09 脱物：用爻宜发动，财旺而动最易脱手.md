# 脱物：用爻宜发动，财旺而动最易脱手

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 脱手货物：用爻宜发动，最适宜财旺而发动，一动就容易把货泄出去（卖掉）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Selling off goods: the Subject line should move; best is a strong Wealth that moves, for moving it lets the goods flow out easily.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1940 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 脱物用爻宜发动， | 脱手货物，用爻宜发动； | For selling off goods, the Subject line should move; |
