# 财福俱无，何异守株而待兔

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财、子孙都不上卦，或上卦而休囚、空破、墓绝、受克冲：求财无望，不如按兵不动。例外（觉子）：日辰或月建本身是财，或财爻伏藏而旺，仍可求。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If neither Wealth nor Output is in the hexagram, or they are there but weak, void, broken, entombed, extinguished, controlled or clashed, there is no hope in pursuing money; better to hold still. Exception (Juezi): the Day or Month is Wealth itself, or the hidden Wealth line is strong.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7773 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财福俱无， | 妻财、子孙都没有， | With neither Wealth nor Output, |
> | 何异守株而待兔； | 和守着树桩等兔子撞上来有什么两样； | it is no different from waiting by a tree stump for a hare to run into it; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财福二爻，占财主象。 | 妻财、子孙两爻，是占财的主要象。 | Wealth and Output are the two main signs in a money question. |
> | 有财无福，财必有限；有福无财，财必不实； | 有财无子孙，财一定有限；有子孙无财，财一定不实在； | Wealth without Output: the money will be limited; Output without Wealth: the money will not be solid; |
> | 财福俱无，焉能有望？ | 两样都没有，哪来的指望？ | with neither, what hope can there be? |
> | 守株待兔，喻妄想也，空亡亦然。 | 守株待兔比喻空想；两爻落空亡也一样。 | Waiting by the stump for a hare is a figure for wishful thinking; the same holds if both are void. |

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11085–11087 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财福俱无，莫若守株而待兔。 | 妻财、子孙都没有，不如守着树桩等兔子（安守不动）。 | With neither Wealth nor Output, better to sit by the stump and wait for the hare (that is, stay put). |
> | 财与子孙爻俱不现，或现而又值休囚、空破、墓绝，或被克害刑冲， | 财爻和子孙爻都不在卦上，或者在卦上却休囚、空亡、月破、入墓、临绝，或者受克、害、刑、冲， | If neither the Wealth line nor the Output line appears, or they appear but are resting or trapped, void or broken, in tomb or extinction, or are controlled, harmed, punished or clashed, |
> | 须宜株守，图则无益。 | 就该守着不动，去谋求也没有用。 | one should hold still; pursuing it brings nothing. |

^q4

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11087 行 · Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 觉子曰：倘得日月为财，或伏而旺者，亦可求也。 | 觉子说：如果日辰或月建就是财，或者财爻伏藏而旺，也还可以求。 | Juezi says: if the Day or the Month is itself Wealth, or the Wealth line is hidden but strong, it can still be pursued. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 野鹤把「何异守株而待兔」改成「莫若守株而待兔」：经文说这是空想，野鹤借同一个典故劝人按兵不动。觉子另补了两种还能求的情形。
>
> *Note:* Yehe changed 'no different from waiting by the stump' to 'better to wait by the stump': the classic calls it wishful thinking, while Yehe uses the same image to advise staying put. Juezi adds two cases in which it can still be pursued.
