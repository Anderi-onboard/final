# 助鬼伤身：世爻受鬼克而鬼长生于日；求财有妄求之害

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 助鬼伤身，是指世爻被鬼克，而鬼又在日辰长生，就是引祸来害自己；这样的卦，求财有妄求的害处，出行有行险的忧虑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* 'Helping Pressure to harm the Body' means the Self line is controlled by Pressure while Pressure is born on the Day, which is drawing calamity to harm oneself; in such a hexagram, seeking wealth harms through an unwarranted demand, and travelling brings the danger of a risky course.
