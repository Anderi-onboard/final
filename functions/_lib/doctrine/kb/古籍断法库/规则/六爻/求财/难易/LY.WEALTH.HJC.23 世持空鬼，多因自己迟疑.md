# 世持空鬼，多因自己迟疑

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼持世，财来生鬼，本来求财容易；若世上的官鬼（或妻财）落空，是自己迟疑退缩，所以不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure on the Self line, Wealth feeds it and money should come easily; if that Pressure (or a Wealth) on the Self line is void, it is your own hesitation that makes it fail.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7830 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持空鬼， | 世爻持官鬼而落空， | If the Self line holds a void Pressure line, |
> | 多因自己迟疑； | 多半是因为自己迟疑； | it is mostly because you yourself hesitate; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7831 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼爻持世，财必相生，凡求必易。 | 官鬼持世，财一定来生它，凡是求财一定容易。 | With the Pressure line on the Self line, Wealth is bound to feed it, and anything sought comes easily. |
> | 若遇空亡，乃是自不上前，迟疑退懒，故无成也。 | 如果逢空亡，是自己不上前，迟疑退缩、懒散，所以不成。 | If it is void, it is you who hold back, hesitating and slacking, so nothing comes of it. |
> | 世持空财亦然。 | 世爻持财而落空，也一样。 | A void Wealth on the Self line is the same. |

^q2
