# 得于何人：看化财生合世身的六亲，财临何类、属何支、入何宫

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问从谁那里得财：看化出财来生合世身的是哪一类六亲（内卦本宫为本家，外卦本宫为外亲，内卦他宫为邻里，外卦他宫为远方；阳为男、阴为女）；财临哪一类（父尊长、鬼官员牙人兵卒、兄朋友、子僧道医士、财女人）；财属哪个地支（子妇人姬妾、丑贵人尊长……亥小儿牙人盗贼）；财落哪个卦宫（乾君父名宦、坤母后老妇农夫……兑少妇歌妓巫师）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* From whom the money comes: see which relation turns into Wealth and generates or combines with the Self or Body (the home palace in the inner trigram is one's own family, in the outer the in-laws and maternal side; another palace inside is neighbours, outside is people far away; yang is male, yin female); which relation the Wealth sits on (Resource elders, Pressure officials, brokers and soldiers, Peer friends, Output monks, doctors and constables, Wealth women); which branch it falls on (Zi women and concubines, Chou nobles and elders ... Hai children, brokers and thieves); and which palace it lies in (Qian rulers, fathers and famous officials, Kun mothers, old women and farmers ... Dui young wives, singers and shamans).
