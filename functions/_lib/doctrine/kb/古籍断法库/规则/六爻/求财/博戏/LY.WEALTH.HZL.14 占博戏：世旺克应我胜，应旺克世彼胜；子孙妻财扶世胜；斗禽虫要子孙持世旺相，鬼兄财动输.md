# 占博戏：世旺克应我胜，应旺克世彼胜；子孙妻财扶世胜；斗禽虫要子孙持世旺相，鬼兄财动输

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博斗禽：福神旺，才有真物（能赢）；财是利息，官鬼动就不赢。世应上见鬼爻都败；世旺克应，我胜；应旺克世，对方胜；子孙、妻财扶世，我胜；子孙旺相喜动。斗禽虫要子孙持世旺相，或子孙独发才赢，鬼、兄、财爻动就输。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Gambling and setting birds to fight: Output strong makes the creature true (able to win); Wealth is the winnings, and Pressure moving means no win. Pressure showing on the Self or the Other means defeat; a strong Self controlling the Other, I win; a strong Other controlling the Self, he wins; Output and Wealth supporting the Self, I win; Output strong is glad to move. For fighting birds and insects Output must sit on the Self and be strong, or move alone, and you win; if Pressure, Peer or the Wealth line moves, you lose.

## 《火珠林·占博戏》原文

> [!quote]+ [[火珠林]] 第 313–314 行 · Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Divining for gambling", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 或问：妻为我物，鬼为彼虫，如何取用？ | 有人问：妻财是我的东西，官鬼是对方的虫，怎么取用神？ | Someone asks: Wealth is my creature and Pressure his insect - how is the Subject chosen? |
> | 答曰：此言斗禽虫也。 | 回答说：这说的是斗禽虫（斗鸡、斗蟋蟀之类）。 | The answer: this speaks of pitting birds and insects. |
> | 若转变之事，则不一同专， | 如果是别的转变之事，就不专一同论； | For other kinds of matter it is not judged as one; |
> | 要子孙持世旺相，或独发便赢，若鬼兄财爻动便输。 | 要子孙持世旺相，或子孙独发就赢；如果鬼、兄、财爻动就输。 | Output must sit on the Self strong, or move alone, and you win; if Pressure, Peer or the Wealth line moves, you lose. |
> | 要知当日，俱以时辰取福德言之。 | 想知道当天（结果），都用时辰取福德来说。 | To know the day's result, take Output by the hour. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「妻为我物，鬼为彼虫」是斗禽虫时的取法，财是我的、鬼是对方的；「若转变之事」一句很省，这里照字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Wealth is my creature, Pressure his insect' is how the roles are taken when birds and insects are pitted against each other; 'if a matter of change' is terse and translated literally.
