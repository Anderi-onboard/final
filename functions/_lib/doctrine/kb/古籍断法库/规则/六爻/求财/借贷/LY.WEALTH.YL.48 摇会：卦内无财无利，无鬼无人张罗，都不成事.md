# 摇会：卦内无财无利，无鬼无人张罗，都不成事

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 摇会：卦里没有财，就没有钱财利益；卦里没有官鬼，就没有人张罗做主；都办不成事，只是白费心机。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A rotating savings society: with no Wealth in the hexagram there is no profit; with no Pressure in the hexagram there is no one to run it; neither gets done, and it is only wasted effort.

## 《易林补遗·摇会得失章第一百十》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2030 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 110 - Gains and losses in a rotating savings society", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 无鬼无财皆不实， | 没有官鬼、没有财，都不落实； | With no Pressure and no Wealth, nothing is solid; |
> | 定然空去费心功。 | 一定白白费心费力。 | you will surely spend effort for nothing. |
