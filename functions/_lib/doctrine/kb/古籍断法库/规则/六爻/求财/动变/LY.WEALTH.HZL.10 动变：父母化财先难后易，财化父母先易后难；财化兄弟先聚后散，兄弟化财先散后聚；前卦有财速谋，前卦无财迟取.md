# 动变：父母化财先难后易，财化父母先易后难；财化兄弟先聚后散，兄弟化财先散后聚；前卦有财速谋，前卦无财迟取

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母化成财，先难后易；财化成父母，先易后难。财化成兄弟，先聚后散；兄弟化成财，先散后聚。本卦有财、变卦无财，赶快谋求有得，迟了就没有；本卦无财、变卦有财，迟一些取才有，眼下还不当值。钱财多少，要凭爻的衰旺来定。书中例：九二的财动，求就一定有；九五的比肩爻动，被阻拦还没得到；买卖同样推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource turning into Wealth: hard first, easy later; Wealth turning into Resource: easy first, hard later. Wealth turning into Peer: gathered first, scattered later; Peer turning into Wealth: scattered first, gathered later. Wealth in the first hexagram and none in the later one: act quickly and you get it, late and you do not; no Wealth in the first and Wealth in the later one: take it late and you have it, it is not on duty now. How much money there is must be decided by the strength or weakness of the line. Case in the book: the Wealth on the second line moves, so seeking will surely bring it; the Peer on the fifth line moves, so it is blocked and not yet obtained; trade is worked out the same way.

> | 九二财动、求之必有； | 九二爻的财发动，求就一定有； | the Wealth on the second line moves: seek and you will surely have it; |
> | 九五比肩爻动， | 九五爻的比肩爻发动， | the Peer on the fifth line moves, |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 最后一段是书里的占例：那一卦是乾变离，九二、九五两爻动（前面「占来情」等各项说的是同一卦）。「前卦」「后卦」指本卦和变卦。「比肩」是和世爻同五行的爻，这里就是兄弟。
>
> *Note:* The last passage is a case from the book: the hexagram is Qian changing to Li with the second and fifth lines moving (the earlier items on the arrival of the question and so on speak of the same hexagram). 'Former' and 'later' hexagram mean the main and the changed hexagram. 'Bijian' is a line of the same element as the Self line, here Peer.
