# 克害刑冲迫兄鬼，则灾殃消散

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟、官鬼是求财的忌神，它们受克、害、刑、冲就灾消、财有望。《断易天机》本细分：吉神克我吉、凶神克我凶，我克兄鬼愁散、我克财福无财；害财则财有妨，害鬼则鬼无作，还要看衰旺；刑鬼煞吉、刑财福凶，刑世伤我、刑应伤彼，应合世而被刑难成；日辰冲世应或冲财难成，冲散财则无财；吉神逢冲凶，凶神逢冲吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer and Pressure are the adversaries of money; when they are controlled, harmed, punished or clashed, trouble disperses and money becomes possible. The Duanyi Tianji text details it: a good spirit controlling me is good, a baleful one bad; my controlling Peer or Pressure dispels worry, my controlling Wealth or Output means no money; harming Wealth hinders it, harming Pressure disarms it, strength decides; punishing Pressure or a killing star is good, punishing Wealth or Output bad, punishing the Self line hurts me, the Other line the other side, and an Other line combining with the Self but punished will not close; the Day clashing Self, Other or Wealth makes it hard, clashing Wealth apart leaves no money; a good spirit clashed is bad, a baleful one clashed is good.

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1239 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡遇害妻财，则财有妨。害鬼杀，则鬼无作。 | 凡是（某爻）害妻财，财就受妨碍；害官鬼凶煞，鬼就作不了祸。 | When something harms Wealth, the money is hindered; when it harms the Pressure line or a killing star, that ghost can do nothing. |
> | 害彼则吉，害我则凶。 | 害别人就吉，害自己就凶。 | Harm done to the other side is good; harm done to me is bad. |
> | 占者当分衰旺，若我世旺，彼虽欲害而无伤，若害爻旺，则凶。 | 占的人要分清衰旺：如果我的世爻旺，对方虽想害也伤不着；如果害我的那一爻旺，就凶。 | Distinguish strength: if my Self line is strong, the other cannot hurt it though it tries; if the harming line is strong, it is bad. |
> | 刑者：阴阳反德也。若加羊刃，可谓逞凶刑伤也。刃者，煞也。有刑而无刃，则不杀。 | 刑，是阴阳违背了本德。若再加羊刃，就是逞凶的刑伤。刃就是煞。有刑而没有刃，就不至于杀伤。 | Punishment is yin and yang turned against their virtue. With the Goat Blade star added, it is a punishment that wounds. The blade is the killing star; punishment without the blade does not kill. |
> | 凡占刑，刑鬼煞凶神则吉，刑财福吉神则凶。 | 凡是看刑：刑官鬼、凶煞就吉，刑妻财、子孙这些吉神就凶。 | As for punishment: punishing Pressure or a baleful star is good; punishing Wealth, Output or a good spirit is bad. |
> | 世应皆怕逢刑，刑世伤我，刑应则对方遭伤。 | 世、应都怕逢刑：刑世伤我，刑应就是对方受伤。 | Both Self and Other fear punishment: punishing the Self line hurts me, punishing the Other hurts the other side. |
> | 求财必应合世，若被刑伤，安可就也。 | 求财一定要应爻合世爻，如果被刑伤，怎么能成呢。 | Seeking money needs the Other line to combine with the Self line; if that is punished, how can it succeed? |
> | 冲者：十二支战击之象。 | 冲，是十二地支相互攻击的象。 | A clash is the twelve branches striking at each other. |
> | 有日辰冲世应，冲财，则难就，冲散财则无财。 | 日辰冲世、应，或冲财，事情难成；把财冲散，就没有财。 | If the Day clashes with Self, Other or Wealth, the matter is hard to close; if it clashes the Wealth apart, there is no money. |
> | 吉神逢冲凶，凶神恶煞逢冲则吉。 | 吉神逢冲凶，凶神恶煞逢冲反而吉。 | A good spirit clashed is bad; a baleful spirit or killing star clashed is good. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 《断易天机》本在这一句下有一大段讲克、害、刑、冲的通论（包括哪几对地支相害、各家说法），这里只引和求财有关的几句。
>
> *Note:* Under this line the Duanyi Tianji text has a long general essay on control, harm, punishment and clash (including which branch pairs harm each other and what various authorities say); only the sentences bearing on money are quoted here.
