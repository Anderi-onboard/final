# 最重财官两旺，不宜世应皆空

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 替贵人奔走效力求财：财、官两旺为要，世应俱空不宜；世临财、应临福，我得益；应临官、世逢破，他得益；兄弟持世，不如不去；应克世，防对方反目。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Earning by serving a great man: Wealth and Pressure both strong is what matters; Self and Other both void is bad. Wealth on the Self line and Output on the Other, I gain; Pressure on the Other and the Self line broken, he gains; Peer on the Self line, better not to go; the Other controlling the Self, beware of him turning on you.
