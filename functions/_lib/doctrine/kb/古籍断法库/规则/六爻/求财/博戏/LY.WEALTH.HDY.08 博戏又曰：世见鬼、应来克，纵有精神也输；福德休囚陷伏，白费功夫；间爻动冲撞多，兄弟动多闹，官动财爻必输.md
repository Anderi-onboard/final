# 博戏又曰：世见鬼、应来克，纵有精神也输；福德休囚陷伏，白费功夫；间爻动冲撞多，兄弟动多闹，官动财爻必输

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博：世爻见鬼，被应爻克，纵然有精神也是输；福德休囚、又陷伏，来回白下功夫；间爻动，冲撞多；兄弟动，吵闹多；官动财爻，一定输。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Gambling: the Self showing Pressure and controlled by the Other loses even with spirit; Output weak and sunk or hidden, one goes back and forth and works in vain; the between lines moving means many clashes; Peer moving means much noise; Pressure moving on the Wealth line surely loses.

> | 纵有精神也是输， | 纵然有精神，也是输； | even with spirit, you lose; |
> | 福德休囚皆陷伏， | 福德休囚，又都陷伏， | Output weak and all of it sunk or hidden: |

## 《海底眼·博戏》原文

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 间爻动， | 间爻发动， | The between lines moving, |
> | 冲撞多， | 冲撞的事就多； | there are many clashes; |
> | 兄弟动多闹， | 兄弟动，吵闹多； | Peer moving, there is much noise; |
> | 官动财爻必输。 | 官动财爻，一定输。 | Pressure moving on the Wealth line, you surely lose. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 《断易天机》同一首（DYTJ.10）第二句作「纵有财神也是输」，这里作「纵有精神也是输」。「间爻」在博戏里指旁人，书里没有明说，这里只照字译。「官动财爻必输」也可读作「官动、财爻必输」。
>
> *Note:* The Duanyi Tianji's version of the same verse (DYTJ.10) reads 'even with the Wealth spirit you lose' in the second line, and this one reads 'even with spirit'. The book does not say who the between lines stand for in gambling, and they are translated by their words. 'Pressure moving on the Wealth line' can also be read 'Pressure moving, the Wealth line surely loses'.
