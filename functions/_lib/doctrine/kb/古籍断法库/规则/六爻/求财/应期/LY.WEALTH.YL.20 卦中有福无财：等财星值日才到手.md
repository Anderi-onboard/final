# 卦中有福无财：等财星值日才到手

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有福神（子孙）而没有财：六爻里没有财出现，即使有子孙也还得不到，一定要遇到财爻当值的日子，钱才进口袋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hexagram with Output but no Wealth: with no Wealth appearing among the six lines, even Output gives nothing yet; only on the day the Wealth line is on duty does the money go into the purse.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1950 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦中有福无财者， | 卦里有福神而没有财的， | In a hexagram with Output but no Wealth, |
> | 财星值日也开怀。 | 财星当值的日子，也能开怀。 | the day the Wealth star is on duty still brings joy. |
