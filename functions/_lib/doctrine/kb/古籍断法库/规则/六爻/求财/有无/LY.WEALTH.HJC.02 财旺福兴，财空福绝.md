# 财旺福兴，财空福绝

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻旺相、子孙发动：公事私事求财都如意。财、福落空、临墓绝（或受伤克而无救）：做什么营生都不如意。野鹤另加两条：宜动而化吉、宜生合世爻；忌动而变凶、忌日月冲破。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth thriving and Output moving: money comes as wished, public or private. Wealth and Output void, in tomb or extinction (or harmed with no rescue): no business goes as wished. Yehe adds: they should turn into something favourable and generate or combine with the Self line; bad if they turn into something harmful or are clashed and broken by the Day or Month.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财旺福兴，不问公私皆称意； | 妻财旺相、子孙兴旺，不论公事私事都能如意； | When Wealth is thriving and Output flourishing, things go as wished, public or private; |
> | 财空福绝，不拘营运总违心。 | 妻财落空、子孙衰绝，不论做什么营生都不如意。 | when Wealth is void and Output extinguished, whatever the business, it goes against your wishes. |

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11083 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 俱宜旺而化吉，又宜生合世爻，公私皆得如意。 | 财、福都宜旺相而且化吉，又宜生合世爻，这样公事私事都能如意。 | Both should be strong and turn into something favourable, and should generate or combine with the Self line; then public and private matters go as wished. |
> | 若逢衰墓绝空、刑冲克害、动而变凶、日月冲破者，上下皆连心矣。 | 如果遇到衰、墓、绝、空，受刑冲克害，动而变凶，或被日月冲破，上上下下都要揪心了。 | If they are weak, entombed, extinguished or void, punished, clashed, controlled or harmed, turn into something harmful when they move, or are clashed and broken by the Day or Month, high and low alike are sick at heart. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 野鹤把经文「总违心」改成了「上下连心」。两个本子（合集本、殆知阁本）都作「连心」；这里按上下文读作「揪心、忧心」，是整理者的读法。
>
> *Note:* Yehe replaced the classic's 'goes against your wishes' with 'high and low alike lian xin'. Both editions (the owner's collection and the Daizhige text) read lian xin; taking it as 'sick at heart' from the context is our reading.
