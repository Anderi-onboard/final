# 妻财发用克文书，买卖交易财利吉（用神出现）

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财发动克文书，考试求官总是落空；买卖交易，财利吉祥（因为用神出现）；婚姻如意（财是妻的主）；出门的人身想动（财动，人就要回）；产妇求神容易分娩；失物静着，在家里找；病人伤脾和胃（财是饮食）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wealth line moving and controlling the document line: sitting exams and seeking office always come to nothing; in trade and dealing, profit is auspicious (because the Subject shows); marriage goes as wished (Wealth is the master of the wife); the traveller feels like moving (Wealth moving brings him back); the woman in labour finds childbirth easy; a lost thing is quiet, look for it at home; the sick have trouble in spleen and stomach (Wealth is food and drink).
