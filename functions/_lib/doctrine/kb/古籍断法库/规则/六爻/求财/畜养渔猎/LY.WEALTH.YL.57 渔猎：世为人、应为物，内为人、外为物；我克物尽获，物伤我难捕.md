# 渔猎：世为人、应为物，内为人、外为物；我克物尽获，物伤我难捕

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 渔猎：世代表人、应代表物，内卦代表人、外卦代表物。我克物，打鱼打猎都尽善，大获其利；物伤我，即使有强弓硬弩、密网张戈，也决难捕到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fishing and hunting: the Self stands for the person and the Other for the quarry, the inner for the person and the outer for the quarry. If I control the quarry, fishing and hunting both go perfectly and win great profit; if the quarry hurts me, even with strong bows, crossbows, close nets and spears it is very hard to catch anything.
