# 放债借出：有子孙、妻财旺相生世合世，有财有利；官鬼、兄弟发动冲世克世，多耗多是非

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借出本钱：卦里有子孙，遇到善良的客人；有官鬼，遇到强横的对手。妻财最喜欢落在生旺之地；兄弟只怕克世和卦身。爻上有子孙、妻财，旺相，或生世、合世、克世，有财有利；官鬼、兄弟发动，冲世、克世，多耗损多是非，即使只是发动也不是吉象，要忌讳。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Lending out capital: Output in the hexagram means meeting a good customer; Pressure means meeting a domineering guest. Wealth is most glad to sit in a place of Birth or strength; Peer only worries about controlling the Self and the Body. Output or Wealth on the lines, strong, or generating, combining with or controlling the Self, means money and profit; Pressure or Peer moving to clash with or control the Self brings many losses and quarrels, and even merely moving is not auspicious and must be avoided.
