# 索债求财：外生内、应生世者还

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 讨债：外生内、应生世、内外世应比和，还；外克内、应克冲刑害世，不还；应生合世而无财，想还没钱；应冲伤世而财旺，能还不肯；应持兄鬼动伤世，嘴甜心狠；应持兄动而财空破绝，被骗；应空，穷或逃；应入墓胎，见不到人；财化空破绝，全无；财动助应而鬼伤世，借势耍赖；鬼动化财，告状才还；世持财子旺而兄衰静，本利俱全；应持兄鬼安静不伤世，或持勾陈父母不伤世，只是拖延。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Collecting a debt: outer generating inner, the Other generating the Self, inner and outer, Self and Other in harmony, repaid; outer controlling inner, the Other attacking the Self, not repaid; the Other generating the Self but no Wealth, willing but unable; the Other hurting the Self with Wealth strong, able but unwilling; the Other on Peer or Pressure moving against the Self, sweet words, bad heart; the Other on a moving Peer with Wealth void, swindled; Other void, too poor or hiding; entombed or embryonic, not to be found; Wealth turning void or extinguished, nothing; Wealth feeding the Other while Pressure hurts the Self, he leans on power to welsh; Pressure into Wealth, repaid only after a lawsuit; a strong Wealth or Output on the Self with Peer weak and still, principal and interest; the Other on a still Peer or Pressure, or on Hook Earth and Resource, not hurting the Self, only delay.

> | 外克内，应克冲刑害世者，不还。 | 外克内，应爻克、冲、刑、害世爻，不还。 | Outer controlling inner, the Other controlling, clashing, punishing or harming the Self: not repaid. |
> | 应生合世而无财，欲还而力不逮。 | 应生合世而卦中无财，想还却没能力。 | The Other generating or combining with the Self but no Wealth: willing but unable. |
> | 应冲伤世而财旺，能还而心不肯。 | 应冲伤世而财旺，能还却不肯还。 | The Other clashing and hurting the Self with Wealth strong: able but unwilling. |
> | 应持兄鬼动伤世，口甜而心歹。 | 应持兄弟、官鬼动来伤世，嘴甜心狠。 | The Other on Peer or Pressure moving to hurt the Self: sweet words, bad heart. |
> | 应持兄动，而财逢空破绝者，被其骗也。 | 应持兄弟发动而财空破绝，被他骗了。 | The Other on a moving Peer with Wealth void, broken or extinguished: you have been swindled. |
> | 应空者，非贫乏，则逃躲也。应入墓胎者，人不见也。 | 应空，不是穷得还不起，就是躲起来了；应入墓、胎，见不到人。 | Other void: either too poor or hiding; Other entombed or embryonic: you cannot find him. |

> [!note] 按
> 「外甥内」两个本子都这样写，按前后文（「外克内」）当是「外生内」。
>
> *Note:* Both editions write 'outer nephew inner'; by contrast with 'outer controlling inner' it means 'outer generating inner'.
