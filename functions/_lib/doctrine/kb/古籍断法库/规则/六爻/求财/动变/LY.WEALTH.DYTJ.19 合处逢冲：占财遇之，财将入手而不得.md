# 合处逢冲：占财遇之，财将入手而不得

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里本来世应相生、六爻相合，事事可成，却在冲它的日子占到，叫合处逢冲；占财遇到，钱将要到手却拿不到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a hexagram in which Self and Other generate each other and the six lines combine, so that everything can succeed, is divined on a day that clashes it, that is 'meeting a clash where there was combination'; in divining for wealth, the money is about to come to hand and is not gained.

## 《断易天机·合处逢冲》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 68 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Meeting a clash where there was combination", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且如占得雷地豫卦。 | 比如占到雷地豫卦。 | Suppose the divination gets the Yu hexagram, Thunder over Earth. |
> | 世应相生，六爻相合，吉无不利，乃事事可成之象也。 | 世应相生，六爻相合，吉利没有不顺的，是事事可成的样子。 | Self and Other generate each other and the six lines combine; all is auspicious and nothing goes against it; it is the look of everything succeeding. |
> | 却在子日占得， | 却是在子日占到的， | But it is got on a Zi day, |
> | 子冲应上午、害世上未， | 子冲应爻上的午、又害世爻上的未， | and Zi clashes the Wu on the Other line and harms the Wei on the Self line; |
> | 此乃合处逢冲也。 | 这就是合处逢冲。 | this is 'meeting a clash where there was combination'. |

## 《断易天机·合处逢冲》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 71 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Meeting a clash where there was combination", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占财遇之， | 占财遇到这种情形， | In divining for wealth, meeting this: |
> | 财将入手而不得。 | 钱将要到手却拿不到。 | the money is about to come to hand and is not gained. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 书里紧接着说：占讼和占病遇到合处逢冲反而好，因为合则事成、冲则灾散。这一条只取求财。
>
> *Note:* The book goes on to say that in divining a lawsuit or an illness, this same pattern is welcome, since combination brings the matter about and a clash scatters the calamity. This entry takes only wealth.
