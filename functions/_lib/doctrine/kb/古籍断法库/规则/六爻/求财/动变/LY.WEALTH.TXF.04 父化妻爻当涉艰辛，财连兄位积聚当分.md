# 父化妻爻当涉艰辛，财连兄位积聚当分

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母化财：先难后得；父旺财衰，辛苦多财少；父衰财旺，用力少财多。财化兄弟：有侵夺，积蓄也要分散（本就与人分利则无妨）。兄弟化财：先散后聚。《断易天机》本：父母、兄弟化财，虽辛苦亦吉；父爻带贵人有依靠，带太岁凶煞防因财染病。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource turning into Wealth: difficulty first, gain later; Resource strong and Wealth weak, much toil and little money; the reverse, little effort and much money. Wealth turning into Peer: encroachment, savings scattered (no harm if you were sharing the profit anyway). Peer turning into Wealth: scattered first, gathered later. The Duanyi Tianji text: Resource or Peer turning into Wealth is lucky however hard the way; the Resource line with the Noble star means someone to lean on, with the Tai Sui's killing star beware of falling ill over the money.

> | 父化妻爻，当涉艰辛始得； | 父母化出妻财，要经历艰辛才得到； | Resource turning into Wealth: gain comes only after hardship; |
> | 财连兄位，纵然积聚当分。 | 财连着兄弟（财化兄弟），即使积聚了也要分掉。 | Wealth linked with Peer: whatever is gathered will be shared out. |

> | 父爻带贵，可言倚靠，若加太岁杀，因财染病归。 | 父母爻带贵人，可以说有依靠；如果带太岁凶煞，会因为求财染病而归。 | If the Resource line carries the Noble star, there is someone to lean on; if it carries the Tai Sui's killing star, you will come home ill because of the money. |
> | 财化弟兄，纵来克世，亦无全得，将有侵夺之患，虽有积蓄，亦当分散也。 | 财化兄弟，即使来克世，也不能全得，会有被侵夺的祸患，即使有积蓄，也要分散。 | Wealth turning into Peer: even if it controls the Self line, you will not get it all; there will be encroachment, and even savings will be scattered. |
> | 若持世，难取谋望之心。 | 如果（化出的兄弟）持世，所谋所望难以达成。 | If that Peer sits on the Self line, what you hope for is hard to reach. |
> | 财爻旺化兄弟，当见聚而后分，若果与人分利则无妨。 | 财爻旺而化兄弟，会先聚后分；如果本来就是和人分利，就不要紧。 | A strong Wealth line turning into Peer: gathered first, then divided; if you were sharing the profit with others anyway, no harm. |
> | 若兄弟化财，则主先散后聚，或谋出于众，利归一源，或始艰辛，然后称心。 | 如果兄弟化财，主先散后聚；或者是大家出主意、利归一处；或者开头艰辛，后来称心。 | Peer turning into Wealth: first scattered, then gathered; or many plan together and the profit flows to one place; or hardship first, then satisfaction. |
> | 凡父兄化财，纵有艰辛，亦吉。 | 凡是父母、兄弟化财，即使有艰辛，也是吉。 | Whenever Resource or Peer turns into Wealth, it is lucky, however hard the way. |
