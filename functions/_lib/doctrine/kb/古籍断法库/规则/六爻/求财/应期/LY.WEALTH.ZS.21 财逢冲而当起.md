# 财逢冲而当起

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财旺而静或旺而空，被占日的日辰冲：价渐涨；财静而休囚被日冲是日破，不作起价论。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A strong Wealth that is still, or strong but void, clashed by the Day of casting: the price will gradually rise. A still, resting or trapped Wealth clashed by the Day is broken, and must not be read this way.
