# 兄弟同人克了财，货物经商消折本（以财为主）；见官虚词应累众；求妻买婢事难谐

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟发动，克了财（同是同类），病人有悲哀，考试雷同、文章不一，平常占问也还要破财；见官打官司，虚词牵累多人（兄动虚诈）；出门的人身未来（兄动有阻）；货物经商亏本（以财为主）；求妻买婢事难成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer moving and controlling Wealth (the 'same' means the same kind): the sick have sorrow, in exams the candidates are alike and the essays inconsistent, and even an ordinary divination still loses money; going to an official, false pleas involve many people (Peer moving is false and deceitful); the traveller has not yet come (Peer moving obstructs); goods and trade lose capital (Wealth is the main thing); seeking a wife or buying a maid does not go smoothly.
