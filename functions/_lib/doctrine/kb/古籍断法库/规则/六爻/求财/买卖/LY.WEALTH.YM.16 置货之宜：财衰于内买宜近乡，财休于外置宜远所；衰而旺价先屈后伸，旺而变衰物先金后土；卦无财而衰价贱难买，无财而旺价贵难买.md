# 置货之宜：财衰于内买宜近乡，财休于外置宜远所；衰而旺价先屈后伸，旺而变衰物先金后土；卦无财而衰价贱难买，无财而旺价贵难买

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财在内卦衰，买货宜在近乡；财在外卦休，置货宜到远处；财衰而变旺，价钱先屈后伸；财旺而变衰，货物先金后土；卦里无财而财又衰，价贱却买不到；卦里无财而财又旺，价贵也买不起；只有财出现而衰，才满心买得到；财叠现而旺，才如意脱得了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Wealth weak in the inner trigram, it is fit to buy in a nearby village; with Wealth resting in the outer trigram, it is fit to lay in goods at a distant place; weak and then thriving, the price is first bent and then stretched; thriving and then changing to weak, the goods are first gold and then earth; a hexagram without Wealth whose Wealth is also weak has a cheap price and yet cannot be bought; one without Wealth whose Wealth is thriving has a dear price and cannot be bought; only Wealth showing and weak lets one buy to one's heart's content, and doubled and thriving lets one sell as one wishes.
