# 推本位、所属身宫：本身旺相保无虞，死囚灾立至；本身发动按子孙推，生财长育，得青龙相助吉，杀神并则倾危

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 还要旁通，推它的本位，再推它所属的身宫：本身旺相，保证没有忧患，如果逢死、囚，灾祸立刻到；本身发动，要按子孙来推，正是生财、长养的时候，再得青龙相助就吉，杀神并在一起，就有倾覆的危险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Then go by the side route and judge its own place, and next the palace of the Body it belongs to: the Body thriving, it is sure to be free of worry, and meeting Death or Imprisonment, calamity comes at once; the Body moving, judge by Output, for it is just the time of breeding wealth and growing; with the Azure Dragon's help it is auspicious, and with baleful stars joined there is danger of ruin.
