# 畜养：最忌兄弟官鬼；官鬼五行各主一样病；本命爻临财福无伤则吉

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 畜养最忌兄弟、官鬼。官鬼落在上爻（或说以五爻为主）：金鬼，牛极瘦；木鬼，脚疼或肚子受风；水鬼，牲畜散失；火鬼，中热；土鬼，发痒、瘟疫、发黄。所属牲畜的本命爻临在财、福上、没有受伤，就吉；比如兄弟、官鬼临三爻本来不好，但有亥爻这个本命临财福，就吉，不为害，其余照此类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Livestock most fear Peer and Pressure. With Pressure on the top line (or, some say, the fifth line as the chief): a Metal Pressure, the ox grows very thin; Wood, sore feet or wind in the belly; Water, the animals scatter; Fire, heat stroke; Earth, itching, plague and jaundice. The animal's own natal line on Wealth or Output and unharmed is auspicious; for instance Peer or Pressure on the third line is bad in itself, but if the Hai line, the natal one, sits on Wealth or Output it is auspicious and does no harm; the rest follow from this.

> [!note] 按
> 「本命爻」是牲畜属相所对的地支爻，例子里的亥就是猪。
>
> *Note:* The natal line is the branch line matching the animal's zodiac sign; in the example Hai is the pig.
