# 博戏：官鬼静、兄弟衰绝、财爻旺相、子孙兴隆，大赢而归

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博大赢：官鬼安静，兄弟衰绝，财爻旺相，子孙兴隆，就能得大利，称心如意，说笑玩乐着回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A big win at gambling: Pressure still, Peer weak and extinct, the Wealth line strong and Output thriving, and you win a great profit, content and pleased, and come home laughing and merry.

## 《易林补遗·博戏求财章第一百十二》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2053 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 112 - Gambling for money", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼静兄衰财福旺， | 官鬼安静、兄弟衰弱、财和福神旺， | Pressure still, Peer weak, Wealth and Output strong, |
> | 君回定唱凯歌声。 | 你回来时一定唱着凯歌。 | you will surely come home singing a song of triumph. |
