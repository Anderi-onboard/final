# 寄放财物：财爻化绝化空终亏损；化兄化鬼被剥被侵

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寄放：财爻化成绝、化成空，终究亏损；化成兄弟，财被剥削砍伐，化成官鬼，财被侵占谋算，哪能不受伤害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving goods: Wealth turning into extinction or void ends in loss; turning into Peer, Wealth is stripped and felled, turning into Pressure, Wealth is encroached upon and schemed against - how could there be no harm.

## 《易林补遗·安寄财物章第一百十四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2076 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 114 - Leaving goods for safekeeping", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 化绝化空，终遭亏损。 | 化成绝、化成空，终究亏损； | Turning into extinction or void, in the end there is loss; |
> | 化兄化鬼，岂不成伤。 | 化成兄弟、化成官鬼，哪能不受伤害？ | turning into Peer or Pressure, how could there not be harm? |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2077 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 动者化机括，化者动之变迁， | 动是化的机关，化是动的变迁； | Moving is the mechanism of changing, and changing is the transformation of moving; |
> | 如财爻一至于化绝则绝，无可生之理； | 比如财爻一到化绝就绝了，没有可以再生的道理； | when the Wealth line turns into extinction it is extinct and there is no reason for it to be born again; |
> | 或财爻一至于化空则空，宁有盈实之肘， | 或者财爻一到化空就空了，哪里还有盈实的余地， | or when the Wealth line turns into void it is void, and where is there room to fill it; |
> | 故日终遭亏损。 | 所以说终究遭到亏损。 | hence 'in the end there is loss.' |
> | 兄弟乃克财之神，化兄则财遭剥伐， | 兄弟是克财的神，化成兄弟，财就遭到剥削砍伐； | Peer is the spirit that controls Wealth; turning into Peer, Wealth is stripped and felled; |
> | 鬼爻为泄气之辈，化鬼则财被侵谋， | 官鬼是泄气的东西，化成官鬼，财就被侵占谋算； | Pressure drains the breath; turning into Pressure, Wealth is encroached upon and schemed against; |
> | 故日岂不侵伤者也。 | 所以说怎么能不受侵害。 | hence 'how could it not be encroached upon and harmed.' |
