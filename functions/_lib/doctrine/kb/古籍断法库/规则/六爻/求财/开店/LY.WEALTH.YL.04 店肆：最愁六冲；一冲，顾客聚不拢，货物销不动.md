# 店肆：最愁六冲；一冲，顾客聚不拢，货物销不动

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 开店做买卖最怕六冲卦：一冲，主顾聚不拢，货物销不动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A shop and its business fear a Six-Clash hexagram above all: with a clash, customers are hard to gather and the goods do not move.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1938 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 店肆惟愁卦六冲， | 开店的，最愁卦是六冲； | For shops, the only worry is a Six-Clash hexagram; |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1939 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 店肆肄业，切忌六冲， | 开店经营，切忌六冲， | A shop and its business must above all avoid a Six-Clash; |
> | 冲则主雇难聚，货物不岂。 | 一冲，主顾就难聚拢，货物销不动。 | with a clash, customers are hard to gather and the goods do not move. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「主雇」在这本书里是主顾、来雇你或买你东西的人。「货物不岂」的「岂」疑是「起」字之误，译作销不动。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhu gu' in this book means the patrons who hire you or buy from you. 'The goods do not rise' is written with a character that is probably a slip for 'rise', and is translated as 'do not move'.
