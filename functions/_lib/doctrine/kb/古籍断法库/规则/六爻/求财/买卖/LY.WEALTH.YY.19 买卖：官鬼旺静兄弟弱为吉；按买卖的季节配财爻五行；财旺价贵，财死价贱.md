# 买卖：官鬼旺静兄弟弱为吉；按买卖的季节配财爻五行；财旺价贵，财死价贱

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买卖看价：官鬼旺相安静、兄弟无气，吉。财爻旺相有生助，价贵；死败，价贱；财属火价一天天涨，属水一天天落。按季节看财爻的五行：冬收夏卖看火财，春收秋卖看金财，秋收春卖看木财，夏收冬卖看水财。财变兄弟或官鬼、子孙变父母，先贵后贱；财化子孙、官鬼化财，先贱后贵。内卦财旺外卦财衰，别处贱；外卦财旺内卦财衰，以后贵。本卦有财而变卦无，晚买便宜；本卦财旺而变卦财衰，先卖的卖得贵。内卦财空可卖，外卦财空难买；外卦的财生合世易买，应爻合内卦的财易卖，世爻落在财的墓上难卖。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Reading the price in a trade: Pressure strong and still, with Peer lacking strength, is auspicious. Wealth strong and fed means dear; Wealth dead or spent means cheap; Wealth on Fire, the price climbs day by day; on Water, it falls. By season, look at the element of Wealth: goods bought in winter and sold in summer want a Fire Wealth, spring to autumn Metal, autumn to spring Wood, summer to winter Water. Wealth turning into Peer or Pressure, or Output turning into Resource: dear first, cheap later; Wealth turning into Output, or Pressure into Wealth: cheap first, dear later. Wealth strong inside and weak outside: cheap elsewhere; strong outside and weak inside: dear later. Wealth in the main hexagram but not in the changed one: buy late and it is cheaper; strong in the main and weak in the change: whoever sells first gets the dear price. Inner Wealth void: it can be sold; outer Wealth void: hard to buy; outer Wealth generating or combining with the Self: easy to buy; the Other combining with the inner Wealth: easy to sell; the Self standing on the tomb of Wealth: hard to sell.

> [!note] 按
> 「收四季所卖之货」一句很省，「四季」「时」都还有别的读法；这里按四季月就是辰戌丑未这四个季末月读。另外，这一节用买进卖出的季节去配财爻的五行；《增删卜易》野鹤的取法不同：财旺宜卖、财衰宜囤，有固定旺季的货凭时令断，没有固定时节的货要单独占，并说古人按五行推货类屡试不验。
>
> *Note:* The sentence 'goods bought to be sold in the four seasons' is terse; both 'four seasons' and 'times' have other readings, and here the four seasons are read as the four season-end months Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei. Also, this passage pairs the season of buying and selling with the element of Wealth; the Old Man of Yehe in the Zengshan Buyi works differently: sell when Wealth is strong and stock up when it is weak, judge goods with a fixed season by the season, cast separately for goods without one, and he says the old element-by-goods method never held up in his trials.
