# 身遇旺财，似取囊中之物；世持动弟，如捞水底之针

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻持财：即使无气也容易得，旺相更好；世爻持兄弟：安静也难得，发动更难。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth on the Self line: easy to get even without force, better still if thriving. Peer on the Self line: hard even when still, harder when moving.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7800 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身遇旺财，似取囊中之物； | 世爻临旺相的财，像从自己口袋里拿东西； | If the Self line carries a strong Wealth, it is like taking something out of your own pocket; |
> | 世持动弟，如捞水底之针。 | 世爻上是发动的兄弟，像在水底捞针。 | if the Self line carries a moving Peer, it is like fishing a needle from the bottom of the water. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7801 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世为求财之人。 | 世爻是求财的人。 | The Self line is the person seeking the money. |
> | 若临财爻，虽或无气，亦主易得；旺相更美。 | 世爻如果临财爻，即使无气，也主容易得；旺相更好。 | If it carries the Wealth line, the money is easy to get even without seasonal force; thriving is better still. |
> | 若临兄弟，虽或安静，亦主难得；发动尤甚。 | 如果临兄弟，即使安静，也主难得；发动就更难。 | If it carries Peer, the money is hard to get even when the line is still; moving, harder still. |

^q2
