# 世应是财求易得，卦无财少准凭；财化鬼凶、鬼化财吉；财落空亡难把捉，遇二杀贼侵

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应是财，求就容易得到；卦里没有财，就少有把握。财爻化成官鬼是凶象，官鬼化成财一定是吉神。财落在空亡，难以把握；再遇二杀（劫杀、天贼杀），贼来相侵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self or the Other being Wealth, seeking is easily rewarded; with no Wealth in the hexagram there is little to go on. Wealth turning into Pressure is an ill sign, and Pressure turning into Wealth is surely an auspicious spirit. Wealth fallen into Void is hard to seize, and if the two killers (the Robbery star and the Heavenly Thief star) are also met, thieves encroach.
