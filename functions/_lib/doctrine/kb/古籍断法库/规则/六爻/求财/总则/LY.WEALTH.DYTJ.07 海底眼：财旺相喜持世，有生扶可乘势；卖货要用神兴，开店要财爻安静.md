# 海底眼：财旺相喜持世，有生扶可乘势；卖货要用神兴，开店要财爻安静

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 海底眼说：占财，财旺相，喜欢它持世；财出现在卦上又有生扶，可以乘势而为；卖货求财要用神兴旺；开店要财爻安静才有利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea says: when divining for wealth, Wealth thriving is best when it holds the Self; Wealth showing and supported may be ridden while it lasts; for selling goods the Subject must be thriving; for opening a shop the Wealth line should be quiet.

## 《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1294 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占财旺相喜持世， | 占财，财旺相，喜欢它持世； | Divining for wealth, Wealth thriving, it is glad to hold the Self; |
> | 出现生扶可乘势， | 财出现在卦上又有生扶，可以乘势； | Wealth showing and supported may be ridden while it lasts; |
> | 脱货求财要用兴， | 卖货求财，要用神兴旺； | to sell goods for money the Subject must thrive; |
> | 开店财爻安者利。 | 开店，财爻安静的有利。 | for a shop, the one whose Wealth line is quiet profits. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这几句在《海底眼》原书里也有（见 HDY.01），第二句字面不同：这里作「出现生扶可乘势」，原书作「出现生扶司秉劳」。
>
> *Note:* These lines also stand in the Haidi Yan itself (see HDY.01), where the second line reads differently: here 'showing and supported one may ride the moment', there 'showing and supported it takes charge and does the work'.
