# 传言得萃卦，经营折本，占者当戒；求财、兴贩尤忌

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 相传得到萃卦，经营就要折本，占卜的人应当引以为戒。注：京房《易传》里萃卦的数缺而无补，因此戒占求财，兴贩尤其忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* It is handed down that getting the Cui hexagram means trade loses its capital, and the one who divines should take warning. The note adds: in Jing Fang's Commentary the count for Cui is missing and unmade up, so seeking wealth is warned against, and hawking goods most of all.

> [!note] 按
> 原文写作「京子传」，这里读作京房《易传》；「数缺无补」的意思是萃卦在京房《易传》的那套数里缺了，书里没有更多解释。
>
> *Note:* The original reads 'Jing zi zhuan', taken here as Jing Fang's Commentary on the Changes; 'the count is missing and unmade up' means Cui is lacking in that work's numbering, and the book explains no further.
