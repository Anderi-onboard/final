# 地头克身，事未周全，阴爻主女人滞留；身爻克地头，满载荣归

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 地头（所到之地）克身，事情没办周全，阴爻则是女人把你留住；身爻克地头，就能满船载宝、荣耀而归。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The place you go to controlling the Body, matters are not fully done, and a yin line means a woman keeps you; the Body line controlling the place brings you home in glory with a boat full of treasure.

## 《阐奥歌章·求财买卖章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3857 行 · Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on seeking wealth and trade", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 满船载宝荣归者， | 满船载着宝物荣耀而归的， | Those who come home in glory with a boat full of treasure |
> | 盖为身爻克地头。 | 是因为身爻克了地头。 | do so because the Body line controls the place. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「世未周」的「世」，《周易尚占》作「事未周」，读作事情没办周全。
>
> *Note:* The Zhouyi Shangzhan has 'matters not fully done' where this text has 'the Self not fully done'; it is read as matters not fully done.
