# 寄放财物：遇玄武常忧偷盗，逢朱雀常恐是非

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寄放：遇到玄武，总担心盗贼来偷钱财；碰上朱雀，常怕引出口舌是非。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving goods: meeting the Dark Tortoise, always fear thieves coming to steal; meeting the Vermilion Bird, often fear quarrels.

## 《易林补遗·安寄财物章第一百十四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2084 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 114 - Leaving goods for safekeeping", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 遇玄武，恒忧偷盗。 | 遇到玄武，总担心偷盗； | Meeting the Dark Tortoise, always worry about theft; |
> | 逢朱雀，每恐生非。 | 碰上朱雀，常怕生出是非。 | meeting the Vermilion Bird, often fear quarrels. |

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2085 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 玄武是贼人，恒者常也，常常忧盗贼侵偷子财。 | 玄武是贼人；「恒」就是常，常常担心盗贼来偷钱财。 | The Dark Tortoise is the thief; 'always' means often, and you often fear thieves coming to steal your money and goods. |
> | 朱雀唇吻也，每亦常也，每每恐怕激生口舌。 | 朱雀是嘴唇；「每」也是常，常常怕引出口舌。 | The Vermilion Bird is the lips; 'every time' also means often, and you often fear provoking quarrels. |
