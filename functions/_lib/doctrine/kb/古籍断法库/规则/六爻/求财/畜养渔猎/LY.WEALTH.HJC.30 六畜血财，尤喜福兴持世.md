# 六畜血财，尤喜福兴持世

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占六畜（贩卖或饲养）以子孙为主：子孙旺相不空、持世临身则吉；父母发动有伤损；化出土鬼防瘟死；子孙旺而财空，畜好无利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For livestock, bought and sold or raised, Output is the main line: Output thriving, not void, on the Self or Body line is good; a moving Resource line means losses; turning into an Earth Pressure line, beware of plague; Output strong but Wealth void, the animals do well but there is no profit.

> | 尤喜福兴持世。 | 尤其喜欢子孙兴旺并持世。 | an Output line flourishing on the Self line is especially welcome. |

> | 凡占贩卖牲口，蓄养六畜，皆要子孙旺相不空，则吉。持世临身尤好。 | 凡是占贩卖牲口、饲养六畜，都要子孙旺相不空才吉；持世、临卦身更好。 | Whether dealing in animals or raising livestock, Output must be thriving and not void; then it is good, and better still on the Self or Body line. |
