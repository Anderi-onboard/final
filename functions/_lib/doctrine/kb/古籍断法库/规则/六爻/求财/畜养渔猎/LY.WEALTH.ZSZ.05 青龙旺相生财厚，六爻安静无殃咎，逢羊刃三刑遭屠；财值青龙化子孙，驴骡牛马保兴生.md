# 青龙旺相生财厚，六爻安静无殃咎，逢羊刃三刑遭屠；财值青龙化子孙，驴骡牛马保兴生

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙旺相，生财丰厚，六爻安静，没有灾殃；如果遇上羊刃和三刑，一定落到屠夫手里。财爻值青龙又化成子孙，驴、骡、牛、马保证兴旺滋生，牛强马壮，没有别的事；外卦旺是乾的刚，内卦旺是坤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon thriving breeds wealth richly, and with the six lines still there is no calamity; meeting the Goat Blade and the three punishments, the animal surely falls into the butcher's hands. Wealth ruling with the Azure Dragon and changing into Output guarantees that donkeys, mules, oxen and horses prosper and breed, the ox strong and the horse sturdy, with nothing else amiss; a thriving outer trigram is the strength of Qian, a thriving inner one is Kun.
