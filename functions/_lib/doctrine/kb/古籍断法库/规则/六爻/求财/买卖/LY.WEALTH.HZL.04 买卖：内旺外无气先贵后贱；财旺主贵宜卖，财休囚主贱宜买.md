# 买卖：内旺外无气先贵后贱；财旺主贵宜卖，财休囚主贱宜买

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦旺相、外卦没有气，货先贵后贱；财旺相，价贵，宜卖；财休囚，价贱，宜买。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Inner strong and outer without breath: the goods are dear first and cheap later; Wealth strong means dear and the time to sell; Wealth resting or trapped means cheap and the time to buy.

## 《火珠林·占买卖》原文

> [!quote]+ [[火珠林]] 第 290 行 · Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Divining for trade", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 内旺相，外无气，其物先贵后贱。 | 内卦旺相、外卦没有气，货先贵后贱； | Inner strong and outer without breath: the goods are dear first and cheap later; |

## 《火珠林·占买卖》原文

> [!quote]+ [[火珠林]] 第 291 行 · Huozhu Lin (The Fire-Pearl Forest), "Divining for trade", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财旺相，主贵、宜卖； | 财旺相，主价贵，宜卖； | Wealth strong means dear and time to sell; |
> | 财休囚主贱，宜买。 | 财休囚，主价贱，宜买。 | Wealth resting and trapped means cheap and time to buy. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 原文这两句的「内」「外」没有写「卦」字，按内卦、外卦读，与《易林补遗》「内卦为近时，外卦为远期」同一套。
>
> *Note:* The source writes just 'inner' and 'outer' without 'trigram'; they are read as the inner and outer trigrams, the same scheme as the Yilin Buyi's 'the inner is the near term, the outer the far term'.
