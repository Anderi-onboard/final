# 妻财持世益财荣，问卜求财定称心；再得子孙临应上，官鬼断不成

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财持世，财运增益（财持世容易得）；占求财一定称心；再有子孙临在应爻上（应爻上的子孙克鬼），官鬼就成不了事（有鬼就没有官司）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth holding the Self increases wealth and fortune (Wealth on the Self is easy to get); divining for wealth, you will surely be satisfied; and with Output sitting on the Other (Output on the Other controls Pressure), Pressure cannot bring anything about (with Pressure there is no lawsuit).
