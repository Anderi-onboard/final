# 卦内无所肖者取八卦象：乾马坤牛艮狗，巽鸡离雉兑羊；毛色：巽兑白、离赤、乾玄、震翠、艮坤黄

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里如果没有它所肖的（生肖），就用八卦的象来轮着看：乾是马，坤是牛，艮是狗，巽是鸡，离是雉，兑是羊，震是龙，艮又作马，坎是豕（又作乌）；颜色：巽、兑白，离赤，乾玄（黑），震翠，艮、坤黄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram holds none of the sign it resembles, go round by the images of the eight trigrams: Qian is the horse, Kun the ox, Gen the dog, Xun the chicken, Li the pheasant, Dui the sheep, Zhen the dragon, Gen also the horse, and Kan the pig, also the crow; for colour, Xun and Dui are white, Li red, Qian black, Zhen green, and Gen and Kun yellow.

## 《周易尚占·六畜血财章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[周易尚占]] 第 736 行 · Zhouyi Shangzhan (Divination First in the Zhou Changes), "Livestock and living wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 震龙艮马一般断， | 震是龙，艮又作马，也照这样断； | Zhen is the dragon and Gen also the horse, judged in the same way; |
> | 坎豕为乌细审详， | 坎是豕，又作乌，要仔细审察； | Kan is the pig, also the crow, to be examined closely; |
> | 巽兑白兮离又赤， | 巽、兑是白色，离又是赤色； | Xun and Dui are white, and Li is red; |
> | 乾玄震翠艮坤黄。 | 乾是玄（黑），震是翠（青绿），艮、坤是黄。 | Qian is black, Zhen green, and Gen and Kun yellow. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「艮又作马」和《断易天机》八卦诗歌里的「艮为手狗」不同，这里照原文译；「坎豕为乌」的「乌」字义不明，这里译作「乌」（黑色的鸟），不确定。
>
> *Note:* 'Gen also the horse' differs from the Duanyi Tianji's verse on the eight trigrams, where Gen is 'the hand and the dog'; it is translated as it stands. The word 'crow' after Kan the pig is unclear and is rendered literally, without certainty.
