# 脱货吉方在财生之处，置货吉方在世生之所

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 脱货往哪个方向吉：财生的那个方位，独发看它的宫，重复出现看它的所属；置货往哪个方向吉：世生的那个地方就行了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Which direction is auspicious for selling off goods: the place where Wealth is born; for a single moving line look to its palace, for one appearing twice look to its affiliation; which is auspicious for laying in goods: only the place where the Self is born.
