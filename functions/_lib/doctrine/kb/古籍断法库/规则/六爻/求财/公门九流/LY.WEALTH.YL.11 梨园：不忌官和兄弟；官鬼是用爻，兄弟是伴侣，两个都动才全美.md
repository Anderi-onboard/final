# 梨园：不忌官和兄弟；官鬼是用爻，兄弟是伴侣，两个都动才全美

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 戏班、走戏、傀儡这类行当：不是一两个人做得了的事，又要有主顾来找；兄弟是他们的伙伴，官鬼是用爻，两个都发动才十全十美，所以说不怕官兴、兄发。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For theatre troupes, touring players and puppet shows: it is not work for one or two people and patrons must come to hire them; Peer is their companion and Pressure the Subject line, and only if both move is it perfect, hence the saying that there is no fear of Pressure rising and Peer moving.
