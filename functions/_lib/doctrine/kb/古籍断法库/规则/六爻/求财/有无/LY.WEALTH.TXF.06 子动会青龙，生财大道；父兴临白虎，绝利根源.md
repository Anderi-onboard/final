# 子动会青龙，生财大道；父兴临白虎，绝利根源

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙是财源：卦无财而子孙旺动仍可求，临青龙等同有财；鬼化财或化子孙最利；子孙化子孙，财从两处来，宜两处求或与人同求；财逢长生、子孙临官，绵绵不绝；财旺而子孙死绝，只得一次；子孙旺而被父母克（父母发动临白虎），断绝财源，日辰合子孙可免。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the source of money: with no Wealth in the hexagram, a strong moving Output still allows the venture, and with the Azure Dragon it counts as having money; Pressure turning into Wealth or into Output is the best of luck; Output turning into Output, money from two places, so seek in two places or with a partner; Wealth at Birth and Output at its Official stage, the money keeps coming; Wealth strong but Output dead or extinguished, only once; Output strong but controlled by Resource (Resource moving with the White Tiger), the source is cut, unless the Day combines with Output.

> | 子动会青龙，乃生财之大道； | 子孙发动又临青龙，是生财的大道； | Output moving with the Azure Dragon: the great road to making money; |
> | 父兴临白虎，为绝利之根源。 | 父母发动又临白虎，是断绝财利的根源。 | Resource moving with the White Tiger: the root that cuts off profit. |

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4576–4578 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若卦内无财，而子孙旺动，亦可求谋。 | 如果卦里没有财，而子孙旺相发动，也可以去求。 | If there is no Wealth in the hexagram but Output is strong and moving, the venture can still be pursued. |
> | 更临青龙，比同有财之卦；若值休囚，更宜斟酌； | 再临青龙，就等同有财的卦；如果子孙休囚，就要再斟酌； | With the Azure Dragon as well, it counts as a hexagram with money; if Output is resting or trapped, weigh it further; |
> | 若鬼化子孙，谓之鬼运财来付好人。 | 如果官鬼化子孙，叫作「鬼把财运来交给好人」。 | Pressure turning into Output is called 'the ghost carrying money to a good person'. |
> | 苍屏有歌曰：鬼化为财及子孙，求财最利称心情。更逢福德来生世，安坐高堂也获珍。 | 苍屏有歌说：官鬼化财或化子孙，求财最有利、最称心；再有子孙来生世，安坐家中也能得宝。 | Cangping's song says: Pressure turning into Wealth or into Output is the best of luck for money; if Output also generates the Self line, you gain treasure sitting at home. |
> | 若子孙化子孙，财从两处生。财既是两处生，宜两处求，或与人同求，大吉。 | 如果子孙化子孙，财从两处来。既然从两处来，宜在两处求，或和别人一起求，大吉。 | Output turning into Output: money arises from two places; so seek it in two places, or together with someone; very lucky. |
> | 子孙乃生财之神，比同水之有源，源头受克则泉竭，泉水旺盛则流长。 | 子孙是生财之神，就像水有源头：源头受克泉水就干，泉水旺盛水流就长。 | Output is the spirit that generates money, like the spring of a stream: controlled at the source, the spring dries; strong at the source, the stream runs long. |
> | 若财位逢长生，福德临官，此等求财，绵绵不绝。 | 如果财爻逢长生，子孙临官，这样求财绵绵不绝。 | If Wealth is at its Birth stage and Output at its Official stage, the money keeps coming. |
> | 或财虽旺，子孙死绝，只许一度，后再难求。 | 如果财虽旺而子孙死绝，只能得一次，以后难再求。 | If Wealth is strong but Output dead or extinguished, you get it once, and not again. |
> | 子孙虽旺，父母来克，谓之绝生财之源，虽有钱财，亦无接济。 | 子孙虽旺，父母来克它，叫作断绝生财之源，即使有钱也没有后续。 | If Output is strong but Resource controls it, it is called cutting off the source; even with money now, nothing follows. |
> | 若遇日辰相合，庶几不被克伤。 | 如果子孙遇日辰相合，大概不会被克伤。 | If the Day combines with Output, it may escape the harm. |

^q2
