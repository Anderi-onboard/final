# 鬼克身爻，商贩者必遭盗贼

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 外出贩货：世应生合、官鬼空伏、动爻日辰不伤世爻则平安，财旺福兴大吉。兄鬼发动又玄武发动，遇劫窃；再克世，大祸；世空可避；官鬼动在第五爻（路），途中小心。王洪绪：官临玄武动来克世，必遭盗贼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Travelling to trade: Self and Other generating or combining, Pressure void or hidden, and no moving line or Day harming the Self line means safety; with Wealth strong and Output flourishing, very good. Peer and Pressure moving with the Dark Tortoise moving: robbers or thieves; if they also control the Self line, disaster; a void Self line may escape; Pressure moving on the fifth line (the road): take care on the way. Wang Hongxu: Pressure on the Dark Tortoise moving to control the Self line means certain robbery.
