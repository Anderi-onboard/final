# 占渔猎：以世为主、以财为物，财子旺相大吉；财在四爻遇兔豕，鬼在六爻防虎豹；内克外、世克应、青龙财动不空可得

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占渔猎：以世为主，以财为猎物；福神兴旺、财旺，前程可望；财、官鬼落空，一无所获。财和子孙都旺相大吉；财在四爻，可遇兔和猪；官鬼在六爻，要防虎豹。内克外、内旺相、世克应，有青龙、财爻发动又不空亡，猎物可得；恶杀临在旺相的财上，发动克世，主有野兽伤人，凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Fishing and hunting: the Self is the chief and Wealth the quarry; Output rising and Wealth strong, the prospect is bright; Wealth and Pressure falling void, nothing is caught. Wealth and Output both strong is greatly auspicious; Wealth on the fourth line, hares and boars may be met; Pressure on the sixth line, beware of tigers and leopards. The inner controlling the outer, the inner strong and the Self controlling the Other, with the Azure Dragon and the Wealth line moving and not void, the quarry can be had; the ill stars on a strong Wealth, moving and controlling the Self, mean a wound from a beast: ill.
