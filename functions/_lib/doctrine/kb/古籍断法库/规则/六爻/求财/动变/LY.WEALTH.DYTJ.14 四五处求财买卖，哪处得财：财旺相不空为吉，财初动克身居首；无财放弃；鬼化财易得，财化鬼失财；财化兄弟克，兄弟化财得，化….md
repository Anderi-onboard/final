# 四五处求财买卖，哪处得财：财旺相不空为吉，财初动克身居首；无财放弃；鬼化财易得，财化鬼失财；财化兄弟克，兄弟化财得，化子孙无忧

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 几处都想求财做买卖，看哪处得财：财爻旺相、不落空亡就吉；财爻刚发动又克身，这处最好；卦里没有财（如遁、蹇一类），说白了就是放弃；鬼化为财，财容易得；财化为鬼，有失财之忧；财化兄弟，终究被克；兄弟化财，终究能得；化为子孙，也没有忧。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With four or five places in mind for seeking wealth and trade, which one gets the money: Wealth thriving and not falling Void is auspicious; a Wealth line just moving and controlling the Body makes that place the first; a hexagram without Wealth, such as Dun or Jian, says plainly to give it up; Pressure changing into Wealth, the money is easy to get; Wealth changing into Pressure, worry over losing it; Wealth changing to Peer is finally controlled, Peer changing to Wealth is finally gained, and changing to Output is free of worry.

> [!note] 按
> 题目的「四五处」就是四五个候选的地方；「财爻初动」可读作「财爻刚发动」，也可读作「初爻上的财发动」，原文没有分明，这里取前一种。
>
> *Note:* The heading's 'four or five places' means four or five candidate places. 'The Wealth line first moving' can be read as 'just beginning to move' or as 'the Wealth on the first line moving'; the text does not settle it, and this entry takes the first.
