# 洞林秘诀·六畜：以生肖和财鬼推；有财之卦畜财旺，无财六畜衰，忌刀砧

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六畜牛马看生肖，以财爻、鬼爻推；生肖看哪一爻动：初爻鸡鸭，二爻犬猫，三爻猪，四爻羊，五爻牛，六爻马；从卦上看，乾震是马，巽是鸡，离是雉，兑是羊，艮是狗，坤是牛，离也兼牛，坎是猪；有财的卦畜财旺，无财六畜衰；占畜忌刀砧卦，离是屠杀，乾坤也主破散。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For cattle and horses look at the animal's sign and judge by the Wealth and Pressure lines; the sign is found by which line moves: first line chickens and ducks, second dogs and cats, third pigs, fourth sheep, fifth oxen, sixth horses; by trigram, Qian and Zhen are horses, Xun is the chicken, Li the pheasant, Dui the sheep, Gen the dog, Kun the ox with Li also sharing it, Kan the pig; a hexagram with Wealth means the herd prospers, without Wealth the livestock decline; in divining livestock avoid the Knife-and-Block hexagram, since Li means slaughter and Qian and Kun also mean breaking up.

## 《断易天机·占六畜·洞林秘诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1774 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Divining livestock: the Dongling secret", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 六畜牛马生肖奇，为财为鬼便推之， | 六畜牛马看生肖，为财为鬼便可推断； | For the six kinds of livestock, cattle and horses, look at the animal's sign, and judge by whether it is Wealth or Pressure; |
> | 生肖要看哪爻动，初爻鸡鸭二犬猫。 | 生肖要看哪一爻动，初爻鸡鸭，二爻犬猫， | the sign is found by which line moves: the first line chickens and ducks, the second dogs and cats, |
> | 三猪四羊五牛地，六爻为马可推之， | 三爻猪，四爻羊，五爻牛，六爻马，都可以这样推； | the third pigs, the fourth sheep, the fifth oxen, the sixth horses, all judged this way; |
> | 以远先推乾震马，鸡居巽卦雉居离。 | 从远处说，先推乾、震是马，巽卦是鸡，离卦是雉； | by trigram, first Qian and Zhen are horses, Xun is the chicken, Li is the pheasant; |
> | 兑卦为羊艮为狗，坤卦为牛离兼之， | 兑卦是羊，艮卦是狗，坤卦是牛，离也兼着牛； | Dui is the sheep, Gen the dog, Kun the ox, and Li shares in the ox; |
> | 坎为豚豕看财鬼，若值无财绝不宜。 | 坎是猪，看财、鬼，如果没有财，就绝不相宜； | Kan is the pig, look at Wealth and Pressure, and if there is no Wealth it is in no way fitting; |
> | 有财之卦畜财旺，卦若无财六畜衰， | 有财的卦，畜类的财旺，卦里无财，六畜就衰； | in a hexagram with Wealth the livestock's wealth thrives, in one without Wealth the livestock decline; |
> | 占畜忌逢刀砧卦，刀居砧上不通医。 | 占畜忌遇刀砧卦，刀在砧上，医治不了； | in divining livestock avoid the Knife-and-Block hexagram: with the knife above the block it cannot be cured; |
> | 离为屠杀归铛镬，乾坤亦主破分离， | 离是屠杀，归到铛镬，乾、坤也主破散分离； | Li is slaughter, ending in the pot and cauldron, and Qian and Kun also mean breaking up and parting; |
> | 更加六神时下看，占畜余皆仿此推（1）。 | 再加上六神、时下来看，占畜其余的都照这样推。 | add to that the six spirits and the present moment, and for the rest divine livestock the same way. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 原文最后的（1）是整理本给今人注文编的号，那段注（刀砧：春亥子，夏寅卯……）本库不引。「以远先推乾震马」的「以远」含义不明，这里译作「从卦上说」，不确定；「刀砧」在书里指金在木上（刀在砧上）等，见断易天机今人注，本库不引。
>
> *Note:* The (1) at the end of the Chinese is a number the transcription gave to a modern note (on the Knife-and-Block hexagram: spring Hai and Zi, summer Yin and Mao, and so on), which this library does not cite. The words 'yi yuan' in the fourth couplet are unclear and are rendered loosely as 'by trigram', without certainty.
