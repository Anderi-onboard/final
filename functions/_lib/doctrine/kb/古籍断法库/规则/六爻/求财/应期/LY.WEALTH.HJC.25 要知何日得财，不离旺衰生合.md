# 要知何日得财，不离旺衰生合

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 定得财之日看财爻状态：有气→合日或本日；太旺、太过→墓库日；休囚、逢绝→生旺日；被合、入墓→冲开之日；逢冲→合日；动逢月破→填实或合日；静→冲日；旬空→出旬；伏藏→出现（提起）之日。财死绝而有子孙扶，按子孙断；卦无财而兄弟生子孙，按兄弟断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Time the money by the state of the Wealth line: with force, the day that combines with it or its own day; over-strong or in excess, its tomb day; resting, trapped or extinguished, a day that births or empowers it; combined or entombed, the day that clashes it open; clashed, the combining day; moving under a Month-break, the day that fills it in or combines with it; still, the clashing day; void, once the cycle passes; hidden, the day it appears. If Wealth is dead but propped up by Output, time it by Output; if there is no Wealth and Peer feeds Output, time it by Peer.

> | 太旺，墓库日得； | 太旺的，在它入墓库的日子得； | Over-strong: on the day of its tomb; |

> | 旺空，过旬日得； | 旺而落空的，过了这一旬得； | strong but void: once the ten-day cycle has passed; |
