# 交易：子动财明大吉；兄鬼爻摇合不成；父兴费尽力气

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买卖和交易：子孙动、财明现，最吉；兄弟、官鬼爻动摇，合不成；父母兴旺，费尽千钧之力也办不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For trade and dealings: Output moving and Wealth showing is greatly auspicious; Peer or Pressure lines stirring, the deal cannot be joined; Resource rising, all one's strength is spent for nothing.
