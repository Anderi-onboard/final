# 脱货：以财爻生旺的月日为脱手之期；财化五行各防一样货损；应动合财易脱

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 脱货：财爻生旺的月份、日子是脱手的日期。应发动合财、或财发动合应，易脱；应落空落破，一定没有买主；财临勾陈土，货难脱。财化水防漏和潮，化火防枯干和火灾，化木防微烂，化土防朽腐，化金又临螣蛇防生虫；财落在辰午酉亥自刑的地支上，货差难脱。世应相冲又带退悔杀发动，或应动合财却化成退神，多半中途作罢；应动合财而被日辰或动爻冲破，有人破坏；日辰合住动财，旁人把持；官鬼动伤世，牙人阻挠；卦里没有官鬼，买卖不成；财动生世生应而官鬼入墓，要等冲开墓的日子成交。书中例：需卦安静，申金子孙持世，人人说吉，作者断四月金空生不了财、又有四个兄弟互相冲脱，买主看了货就散了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Selling off goods: the month or day when the Wealth line is born or strong is the time to unload. The Other moving to combine with Wealth, or Wealth moving to combine with the Other: easy to sell; the Other void or broken: surely no buyer; Wealth on Hook Earth: hard to sell. Wealth turning into Water: beware leaks and damp; into Fire: drying out and fire; into Wood: slight rot; into Earth: decay; into Metal with the Coiling Snake: worms; Wealth on Chen, Wu, You or Hai, the self-punishing branches: poor goods, hard to sell. Self and Other clashing with the Regret star moving, or the Other moving to combine with Wealth but turning into a Retreating line: often broken off midway; the Other moving to combine with Wealth but clashed and broken by the Day or a moving line: someone wrecks it; the Day combining with a moving Wealth: a bystander holds it back; Pressure moving to hurt the Self: the broker obstructs; no Pressure in the hexagram: the trade does not close; Wealth moving to generate both Self and Other while Pressure has gone into its tomb: it closes on the day the tomb is clashed open. In the case from the book, hexagram Xu with no moving line looked auspicious because Output on Shen Metal held the Self line; the author judged that Metal was void in the fourth month and could not generate Wealth, and that four Peer lines clashed each other away, so the buyers left when they saw the goods.

## 《易隐·贸易占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2932–2934 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining for trade", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 以财爻生旺月日为脱卸之期。 | 以财爻生旺的月份、日子为脱手的日期。 | The month or day when the Wealth line is born or strong is the time to unload. |
> | 应动合财，财动合应者，易脱。 | 应发动去合财，或财发动去合应，容易脱手。 | The Other moving to combine with Wealth, or Wealth moving to combine with the Other: easy to sell. |
> | 应居空破，必无买主。 | 应落在空、破上，一定没有买主。 | The Other in void or broken: there will surely be no buyer. |
> | 财持勾土，货必难脱。 | 财临勾陈土，货一定难脱手。 | Wealth on Hook Earth: the goods will surely be hard to sell. |
> | 财化水，防上漏下湿。 | 财化成水，要防上面漏、下面潮。 | Wealth turning into Water: guard against leaks above and damp below. |
> | 财化火，防枯干回禄， | 财化成火，要防枯干和火灾； | Wealth turning into Fire: guard against drying out and fire; |
> | 财化木，防微烂。 | 财化成木，要防微烂； | turning into Wood: guard against slight rot; |
> | 财化土，防朽腐。 | 财化成土，要防朽腐。 | Wealth turning into Earth: guard against decay. |
> | 财化金加蛇，防生虫。 | 财化成金又临螣蛇，要防生虫。 | Wealth turning into Metal with the Coiling Snake: guard against worms. |
> | 财持辰午酉亥自刑，货恶难脱也。 | 财落在辰、午、酉、亥这几个自刑的地支上，货色差，难脱手。 | Wealth on Chen, Wu, You or Hai, the self-punishing branches: the goods are poor and hard to sell. |
> | 世应相冲，加退悔杀动，或应动合财，化退神者，多中止也。 | 世应相冲，又带退悔杀发动，或者应发动去合财、却化成退神，多半中途作罢。 | Self and Other clashing with the Regret star moving, or the Other moving to combine with Wealth but turning into a Retreating line: it is often broken off midway. |
> | 应动合财，被日辰动爻冲破者，有人破也。 | 应发动去合财，却被日辰或动爻冲破，有人从中破坏。 | The Other moving to combine with Wealth but clashed and broken by the Day or a moving line: someone wrecks it. |
> | 日合动财者，旁人把持也。 | 日合住发动的财，旁人把持着不放。 | The Day combining with a moving Wealth: a bystander holds it back. |
> | 鬼动伤世者，牙人挠阻也。 | 官鬼发动伤世，牙人从中阻挠。 | Pressure moving to hurt the Self: the broker obstructs. |
> | 卦无官鬼者，买卖不成也。 | 卦里没有官鬼，买卖成不了。 | No Pressure in the hexagram: the trade will not go through. |
> | 财动生世生应，而官鬼入墓者，待冲开墓日成交也。 | 财发动，既生世又生应，而官鬼入了墓，要等冲开墓的那天才成交。 | Wealth moving to generate both the Self and the Other, while Pressure has gone into its tomb: the deal closes on the day the tomb is clashed open. |

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2938 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 庚寅年辛巳月甲辰日，占脱货，得需卦安静。 | 庚寅年辛巳月甲辰日，占脱货，得需卦，没有动爻。 | In a Gengyin year, Xinsi month, Jiachen day, he divined about selling off goods and got Xu (Waiting) with no moving line. |
> | 申金子孙持世，人皆云吉。 | 申金子孙持世，人人都说吉。 | Output on Shen Metal held the Self line, and everyone called it auspicious. |
> | 岂知四月金空，不能生财。 | 哪知道四月里金落空，生不了财。 | But in the fourth month Metal is void, and cannot generate Wealth. |
> | 经云，用爻最怕立时空也。 | 经上说，用爻最怕起卦那一刻就落空。 | The classic says the Subject line most fears being void at the moment of the cast. |
> | 本宫亥财，伏辰土兄下， | 本宫的亥水财，伏在辰土兄弟下面， | The home palace's Hai Wealth is hidden under the Chen-Earth Peer, |
> | 本宫世应俱持兄弟，日辰又是兄弟，必有对主承买。 | 本宫里世爻、应爻都带兄弟，日辰又是兄弟，一定有买主来接货。 | and in the home palace both Self and Other carry Peer, and the Day is also Peer, so a buyer will surely come to take the goods. |
> | 但因亥财自刑，其货必背时不佳，买主见货散去也。 | 只是亥水的财自刑，那批货一定不合时、不好，买主看了货就散了。 | But because the Hai Wealth punishes itself, the goods are surely out of season and poor, and the buyers left when they saw the goods. |
> | 盖兄弟绝于月建巳中，墓于日建辰中， | 因为兄弟在月建巳里是绝、在日建辰里是墓， | For Peer is in extinction in the Month's Si and in its tomb in the Day's Chen, |
> | 又飞伏辰戌丑未，四位兄弟， | 又飞神伏神都在辰戌丑未上，四个兄弟， | and the flying and hidden lines are all Chen, Xu, Chou and Wei, four Peers, |
> | 自相冲脱，故不买而去也。 | 自己互相冲开、把货脱散了，所以不买就走了。 | clashing among themselves and scattering the goods, so they left without buying. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 需卦是第五卦，原文只说「安静」，没写哪一爻动，这里译成「没有动爻」。「用爻最怕立时空」一句，例里引作「经云」，《易隐》别处没有单独的出处。
>
> *Note:* Xu is the fifth hexagram; the source says only that it is 'still', which is rendered as 'no moving line'. The line 'the Subject fears being void at the moment of the cast' is quoted in the case as 'the classic says'; nothing else in the Yi Yin gives it a separate source.
