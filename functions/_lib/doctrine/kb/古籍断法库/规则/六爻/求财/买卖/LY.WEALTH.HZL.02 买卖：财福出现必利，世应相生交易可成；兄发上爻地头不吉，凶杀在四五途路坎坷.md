# 买卖：财福出现必利，世应相生交易可成；兄发上爻地头不吉，凶杀在四五途路坎坷

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占买卖只要财、福（子孙）出现，没有就不利；世应相生，交易能成。兄弟宫在上爻发动，那个地方不吉；凶杀落在四爻、五爻，路上多坎坷。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a trade hexagram all that is needed is Wealth and Output showing, and without them it is unfavourable; Self and Other generating each other, the deal can be made. Peer moving on the top line, the place is inauspicious; the ill stars on the fourth or fifth line, the road is full of hardships.

## 《火珠林·占买卖》原文

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财福出现，买卖必利； | 财和福神出现，买卖一定有利； | Wealth and Output showing, buying and selling surely profits; |
> | 世应相生，交易可成。 | 世应相生，交易可以成功。 | Self and Other generating each other, the deal can be made. |

> | 若是兄宫发动于上爻，必知地头不吉。 | 如果兄弟宫在上爻发动，一定知道那个地方不吉； | If the Peer palace moves on the top line, know that the place is inauspicious; |

> 「兄宫」按字面译成兄弟宫（兄弟所在的爻）。
>
> *Note:* 'Peer palace' is translated literally as the position where Peer stands.
