# 福变财生，滚滚财源不竭

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动（生财），利长久；子孙化财、财化子孙，而且生合世爻、卦身，财源不绝。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A moving Output line (which feeds Wealth) means lasting profit; Output turning into Wealth, or Wealth into Output, especially generating or combining with the Self and Body lines, means money that keeps coming.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7803 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 福变财生， | 子孙动而变出财来， | When Output turns into Wealth, |
> | 滚滚财源不竭； | 财源滚滚，用之不竭； | the money rolls in and never runs dry; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7804 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占财，得子孙发动，利必久远。 | 占财时，子孙发动，利一定长久。 | In a money question, a moving Output line means a lasting profit. |
> | 更化财爻，生合身世，乃绵绵不绝之象，尽求则尽有也。 | 再变出财爻，生合卦身、世爻，是绵绵不绝之象，要多少有多少。 | If it also turns into a Wealth line that generates or combines with the Body and Self lines, the money never stops: whatever you ask for, you get. |
> | 财化子亦妙。 | 财化子孙也好。 | Wealth turning into Output is also good. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> | 占财得子孙发动，利必久远， | 占财时子孙发动，利一定长久； | In a money question a moving Output line means a lasting profit; |

^q3
