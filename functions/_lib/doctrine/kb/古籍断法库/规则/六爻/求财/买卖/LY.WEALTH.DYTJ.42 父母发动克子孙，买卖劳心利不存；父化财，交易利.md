# 父母发动克子孙，买卖劳心利不存；父化财，交易利

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻发动克子孙，做买卖劳心费力、利钱留不住；父母化成财，交易有利，只是家里长辈不安宁、求人办事不顺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Resource moving and controlling Output means that in trade one wears out the mind and no profit remains; Resource changing into Wealth, dealing profits, though the elders of the house are uneasy and seeking help goes badly.

> *Note:* This seven-character verse covers illness, marriage, exams, travellers, lawsuits and lost things together; this library takes the whole verse, of which only a line or two concerns wealth, and does not cite the modern translation printed after it.
