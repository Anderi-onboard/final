# 亩猎求财：金财旺动生合世，世临天罡虎杀动者获

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 打猎：金财旺动生合世，或世临天罡煞、虎杀发动去克旺财，有获；世临虎杀动而财空破绝，无获；财旺而虎杀不动，鹰犬倦；刑刃虎杀冲伤身世，防野兽伤人。财的地支定猎物（子黄鼠、卯兔、未山羊、申猴、戌獾狗、亥野猪），演禽法以干支定鸟兽；其余内外世应与捕鱼同断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Hunting: a strong moving Metal Wealth generating or combining with the Self, or the Self moving with the Tiangang or Tiger star to control a strong Wealth, a kill; the Self moving with the Tiger but Wealth void, broken or extinguished, nothing; Wealth strong but the Tiger still, the hawks and hounds are tired; punishment, Blade or Tiger clashing the Self and Body, beware of wild beasts. The Wealth branch names the game (Zi weasel, Mao hare, Wei wild goat, Shen monkey, Xu badger-dog, Hai boar), the animal-sign method names birds and beasts by stem and branch; the rest is judged as for fishing.
