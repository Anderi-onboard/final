# 青龙发动万物通，进财旺禄福无穷；临凶遇杀无碍，惟忌临金与落空

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙发动，什么事都通顺（青龙属木）；进财、旺禄，福气无穷；临了凶星、遇了煞也没有妨碍（能化解凶煞）；只忌讳临金和落空（龙被金克）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon moving, all things go through (the Azure Dragon belongs to Wood); wealth comes in and salary thrives, and blessings are without end; sitting on an ominous star or meeting a baleful one, there is no obstruction (it can resolve them); only to be avoided is sitting on Metal or falling Void (the Dragon is controlled by Metal).
