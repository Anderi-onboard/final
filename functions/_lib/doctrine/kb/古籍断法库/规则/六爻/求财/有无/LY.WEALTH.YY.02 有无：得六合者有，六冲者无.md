# 有无：得六合者有，六冲者无

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有财：六合卦；外生内、应生世；内外世应比和旺相不空破；卦无财而月日为财生合世；世下伏财得日辰扶起；应动化财生合世；子孙旺而兄动生之；日辰与世应三合财局；应财虽衰而临日辰长生。无财：六冲卦；内克外、世克应；应、财空破；财化鬼、化空破绝；财动去合应、兄或旁爻；卦身克动财。财旺而子孙空绝，得一次难再。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* There is money with: a Six-Combine hexagram; outer generating inner, Other generating Self; inner and outer, Self and Other in harmony, strong, not void or broken; no Wealth in the hexagram but a Wealth Month or Day generating or combining with the Self; hidden Wealth under the Self lifted by the Day; the Other moving into Wealth that generates or combines with the Self; Output strong and fed by a moving Peer; the Day forming a Wealth frame with Self and Other; the Other's weak Wealth at its Birth stage under the Day. No money with: a Six-Clash hexagram; inner controlling outer, Self controlling Other; Other and Wealth void or broken; Wealth turning into Pressure, void, a break or extinction; Wealth moving off to combine with the Other, Peer or another line; the Body controlling a moving Wealth. Wealth strong but Output void or extinguished: once, and not again.

## 《易隐·求财占》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2860 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining for wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 得六合者有，六冲者无。 | 得六合卦的有财，得六冲卦的没有。 | A Six-Combine hexagram: there is money; a Six-Clash one: there is none. |
> | 外生内，应生世者有。内克外，世克应者无。 | 外卦生内卦、应爻生世爻的有；内卦克外卦、世爻克应爻的没有。 | The outer trigram generating the inner, or the Other generating the Self: there is money; the inner controlling the outer, or the Self controlling the Other: none. |
> | 内外世应比和旺相不空破者有， | 内外卦、世应比和，旺相而不空不破的，有； | Inner and outer, Self and Other, in harmony, strong and neither void nor broken: there is money; |
> | 卦本无财，而月日是财，来生合世， | 卦里本来没有财，而月建、日辰是财，来生合世爻， | no Wealth in the hexagram but the Month or Day is Wealth and generates or combines with the Self line; |
> | 或世不伏财，得日辰并起， | 或者世爻下伏财（原文作「世不伏财」，「不」疑为「下」），得日辰把它扶起， | or Wealth hidden under the Self line (the text reads 'not hidden'; 'under' is probably meant) and lifted up by the Day; |
> | 或应动化财生合世， | 或者应爻发动化出财来生合世爻， | or the Other moving into Wealth that generates or combines with the Self line; |
> | 或子孙旺相，兄动生之者， | 或者子孙旺相，又有兄弟动来生它， | or Output thriving with a moving Peer generating it; |
> | 或日辰世应三合财局者， | 或者日辰和世、应三合成财局， | or the Day, the Self and the Other forming a three-harmony Wealth frame; |
> | 或应财衰而临日建长生者，皆有财也。 | 或者应爻的财虽衰而临日辰的长生，都有财。 | or the Other's Wealth, though weak, at its Birth stage under the Day: in all these there is money. |
> | 应爻与财爻值空破， | 应爻和财爻逢空、破， | The Other line and the Wealth line void or broken; |
> | 或财动化鬼，化空破绝， | 或者财动化官鬼，化空、化破、化绝， | or Wealth moving into Pressure, or into the void, a break or extinction; |
> | 或财动去合应爻兄爻旁爻， | 或者财动去合应爻、兄爻或别的爻， | or Wealth moving to combine with the Other line, the Peer line or some other line; |
> | 或身克动财者，皆无财也。 | 或者卦身去克发动的财，都没有财。 | or the Body line controlling a moving Wealth: in all these there is no money. |
> | 财旺子空绝者，一得难再也。 | 财旺而子孙空、绝的，得一次就难再得。 | Wealth strong but Output void or extinguished: you get it once and not again. |

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2894 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 庚寅年辛巳月丙戌日，占求财，得随之屯。 | 庚寅年辛巳月丙戌日，占求财，得随卦变屯卦。 | Gengyin year, Xinsi month, Bingxu day; a money question; the hexagram Sui changing to Zhun. |
> | 此卦辰财持世，戊日冲之，未财持应，化丑冲之， | 这一卦辰土财持世，被戌日（原文作「戊日」）冲；未土财持应，又化出丑来冲它； | Here the Chen Wealth sat on the Self line and was clashed by the Xu day (the text says 'Wu day'); the Wei Wealth sat on the Other line and turned into Chou, which clashes it; |
> | 此为四财，自相冲脱，又财绝于月建巳中， | 这是四个财自相冲散，财又在月建巳中临绝， | four Wealth branches clashing one another apart, and Wealth extinguished under the Si Month, |
> | 不可云世应是财求易得，日建世应，比和吉也，后果求之不得。 | 不能说世应都是财就容易得、日辰和世应比和就吉；后来果然没求到。 | so one cannot say that with Wealth on Self and Other the money is easy, or that the Day in harmony with them is good; in the event it was not obtained. |

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2892 行 · Case from the book

> | 庚寅年庚辰月壬申日，卜求财，得屯卦安静。 | 庚寅年庚辰月壬申日，卜求财，得屯卦，六爻安静。 | Gengyin year, Gengchen month, Renshen day; a money question; the hexagram Zhun, all lines still. |
> | 六爻无财，惟本宫午火财，伏辰宫下，飞来耗财， | 六爻里没有财，只有本宫的午火财，伏在辰爻下面，飞神来耗泄财； | No Wealth among the six lines; only the home palace's Wu Fire Wealth, hidden under the Chen line, whose flying line drains the Wealth; |
> | 又辰午皆为匿刑，此必不义之财，为小人欺骗而不得也。 | 辰、午又都是自刑，这一定是不义之财，会被小人欺骗而得不到。 | moreover Chen and Wu are both self-punishing branches: this must be ill-gotten money, and he would be cheated by petty people and not get it. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「或世不伏财」两个本子都这样写，按「得日辰并起」的上下文当是「世下伏财」；卦例里「戊日冲之」按起卦日丙戌当是「戌日」。原文照录。
>
> *Note:* Both editions read 'or the Self line not hidden Wealth'; the following 'lifted up by the Day' implies 'Wealth hidden under the Self line'. In the case, 'the Wu day clashes it' should be 'the Xu day' given the casting day Bingxu. We quote the text as it stands.
