# 顿货先访脱期：近买近卖看本月财旺，置后再脱看以后财逢生旺之日

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 囤货先推脱手的日期，有快有慢：近期买近期卖，要本月财旺相的日子才有利；现在买、以后再脱手，要等年里财逢生旺的日子才入袋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For stocking goods, first work out the time of sale, which may be quick or slow: buying and selling soon, profit comes this month when Wealth is strong; buying now for a later sale, wait for a day later in the year when Wealth meets Birth or strength and it can go into the purse.

## 《易林补遗·收顿货物章第一百六》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1969 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 106 - Stocking goods", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占顿货，先访脱期。 | 凡是占囤货，先要访问脱手的日期。 | In any question about stocking goods, first look into the time of sale. |
> | 有迟速之不同，取旺衰之名异， | 有迟有快的不同，取旺衰的名目也不一样； | There is a difference between slow and quick, and the names taken from strength and weakness differ; |
> | 近买近卖，于本月当旺相而得财。 | 近期买、近期卖的，在本月正当旺相的时候得财； | for buying and selling soon, money comes this month when Wealth is strong; |
> | 今置后脱，於经年值休囚而有利。 | 现在买进、以后再脱手的，隔了一年，在休囚的时候反而有利。 | for buying now and selling later, over a full year, there is profit even in the resting and trapped time. |

^q2

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1970 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 置买货物欲求利息，专看迟速之期，可觅厚薄子财。 | 买进货物想求利息，专看快慢的时间，就能求得厚薄不同的利钱。 | To seek interest from buying goods, look only at the time, fast or slow, and you can find a thicker or thinner return. |
> | 近买即卖，求目下财旺之日而利入手。 | 近期买了就卖，求的是眼下财旺的日子，利润才到手； | Buying and selling at once, look for a day when Wealth is strong now, and the profit comes to hand; |
> | 今制后脱，待年馀财逢生旺之日而可入囊。 | 现在置办、以后脱手，要等到年里财逢生旺的日子，才能入袋。 | buying now for a later sale, wait for a day later in the year when Wealth meets Birth or strength, and it can go into the purse. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「於经年值休囚而有利」一句，字面是说隔了整年、在休囚的时候反而有利，这里照字面译。「年馀」译成年里以后。
>
> *Note:* 'Over a full year, in the resting and trapped time, there is profit' is translated literally; 'the rest of the year' is rendered as 'later in the year'.
