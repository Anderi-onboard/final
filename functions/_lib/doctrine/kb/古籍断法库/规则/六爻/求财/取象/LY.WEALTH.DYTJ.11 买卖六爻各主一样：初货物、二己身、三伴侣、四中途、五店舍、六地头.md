# 买卖六爻各主一样：初货物、二己身、三伴侣、四中途、五店舍、六地头

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买卖占里六个爻各代表一样：初爻是货物，二爻是自己，三爻是同伴，四爻是半路，五爻是店舍，上爻是买卖的地方。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a divination on buying and selling the six lines each stand for one thing: the first for goods, the second for oneself, the third for a companion, the fourth for the road midway, the fifth for the lodging, the top for the place of the trade.

## 《断易天机·占买卖第二十》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1311–1312 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 20: Divining for buying and selling", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 初爻二爻三爻四爻五爻六爻 | 第一爻、第二爻、第三爻、第四爻、第五爻、第六爻，依次是： | The first line, the second, the third, the fourth, the fifth, the sixth, in order, are: |
> | 货物己身伴侣中途店舍地头 | 货物、自己、同伴、半路、店舍、地头。 | goods, oneself, a companion, the road midway, the lodging, the local ground. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「地头」是买卖发生的那个地方。《阐奥歌章》求财买卖章里，外卦是「地头」，内卦是「人」（见 CA.15）。
>
> *Note:* 'The local ground' is the place where the trade happens. In the Chan'ao Gezhang's verse on seeking wealth and trade the outer trigram is 'the local ground' and the inner is 'the person' (see CA.15).
