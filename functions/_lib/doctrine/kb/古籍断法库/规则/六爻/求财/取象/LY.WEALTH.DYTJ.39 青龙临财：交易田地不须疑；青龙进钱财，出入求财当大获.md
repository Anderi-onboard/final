# 青龙临财：交易田地不须疑；青龙进钱财，出入求财当大获

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙与卦身、应爻相合，临在财爻上，交易田地不用怀疑；青龙主进钱财，出门入门求财该有大收获。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Azure Dragon combining with the Body and the Other and sitting on the Wealth line, buying and selling land need not be doubted; the Azure Dragon brings in money, and going out and coming in to seek wealth there should be a great haul.

## 《断易天机·六神彖象歌·何知章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 476 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The verse on the six spirits and the chapter on how to know", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 青龙入水身应合， | 青龙落在水爻上，和卦身、应相合， | The Azure Dragon entering a Water line, combining with the Body and the Other: |
> | 必定求官事事宜， | 求官必定事事相宜； | in seeking office everything is sure to be fitting; |
> | 若临财爻妻有孕， | 如果临在财爻上，妻子有孕， | if it sits on the Wealth line, the wife is with child, |
> | 交易田地不须疑。 | 交易田地不必怀疑。 | and buying and selling land need not be doubted. |
