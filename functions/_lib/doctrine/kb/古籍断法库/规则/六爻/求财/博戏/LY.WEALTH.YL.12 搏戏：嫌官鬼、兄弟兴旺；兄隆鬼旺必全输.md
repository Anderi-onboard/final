# 搏戏：嫌官鬼、兄弟兴旺；兄隆鬼旺必全输

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 下棋赌钱、争采取胜，是钱财往来来决输赢的事：兄弟耗财，官鬼克削，兄弟隆盛、官鬼旺相，必然输光。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Board games, betting and contests for prizes are matters where money passes back and forth and decides who wins: Peer wastes money and Pressure strips it, and with Peer thriving and Pressure strong you will lose everything.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1942 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 搏戏犹嫌鬼兴兄， | 赌博还是嫌官鬼和兄弟兴旺； | In gambling, Pressure and Peer rising are still unwelcome; |
