# 纠会、摇会求财

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 纠会（发起互助会）：财、应生合世易成；财应空破绝或应刑冲克世不成；应空对方推辞，世空自己倦怠，应空合世虚诺；应生合世带退悔杀或化退神，先允后却；带破碎杀，零星交财；应财化财，一人两脚；应兄化财，两人合脚；六合变六冲不成。摇会：财旺动助官生合世得；兄持世动、兄弟独发、无财鬼、世财空破死绝受刑克则失；六合得六冲失；财合世而兄克财，得而复失；财合应而应生世，彼得让我；财生世又生应，谁衰伤谁失；世应俱旺，两人分得。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Gathering a savings society: Wealth and the Other generating or combining with the Self, it forms; Wealth or the Other void, broken or extinguished, or the Other attacking the Self, it does not; Other void, they refuse; Self void, you tire of it; Other void yet combining, an empty promise; the Other generating the Self but moving with the Regret star or turning Retreating, agreed then withdrawn; with the Breaking star, paid in dribs and drabs; the Other's Wealth turning into Wealth, one person taking two shares; the Other's Peer turning into Wealth, two sharing one; Six-Combine changing into Six-Clash, it does not form. Drawing: a strong moving Wealth feeding Pressure and generating the Self wins; Peer moving on the Self or alone, no Wealth or Pressure, or Self and Wealth void or hurt, loses; Six-Combine wins, Six-Clash loses; Wealth combining with the Self but controlled by Peer, won then lost; Wealth combining with the Other and the Other generating the Self, he wins and hands it to you; Wealth generating both, whichever is weaker loses; both strong, they share.
