# 财化进神，其价正涨；兄爻持世，破败之端

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财化进神：价正在涨，不可收购囤积；财化退：价将落，有货速卖。兄弟持世或兄弟发动：破财之兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth turning Advancing: the price is rising, do not buy to store; turning Retreating: the price will fall, sell what you hold at once. Peer on the Self line or Peer moving: a sign of losing money.
