# 凶神冲散财爻，劫杀加临兄位

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凶神（如白虎、螣蛇）冲克财爻：出外行船防风波，世爻临吉神则人无事而财散，虎蛇克世则财命难保；在家求财则主恶人搅扰或因财惹官非，有解神无事。劫杀（神煞）临财，财不可得；临发动的兄弟，得财后防失，再加玄武有盗劫；贵人临官鬼持世，或官鬼动而制住兄弟，可免；官鬼无气则兄弟仍为祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A baleful spirit (such as the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake) clashing or controlling the Wealth line: abroad, beware of storms on the water; with a good spirit on the Self line you are safe but the money drifts off; the Tiger or Snake controlling the Self line endangers both money and life. At home it means troublemakers or the money dragging you into an official matter; a relieving spirit ends it. The Robbery star on Wealth: the money cannot be had; on a moving Peer: beware of losing it afterwards, and with the Dark Tortoise, of robbery; the Noble star on a Pressure line holding the Self line, or a moving Pressure restraining Peer, averts it; a forceless Pressure line cannot.

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4573 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡凶神来冲克财爻，行船便见风波之患。 | 凡是凶神来冲克财爻，行船就有风浪之患。 | Whenever a baleful spirit clashes with or controls the Wealth line, a voyage will meet storms. |
> | 世爻若值吉神，身虽无咎，财必飘零。 | 世爻如果临吉神，人虽然没事，财一定散失。 | If the Self line carries a good spirit, you yourself are unharmed but the money drifts away. |
> | 凶神若遇虎蛇克世，财命不可保全。 | 凶神如果是白虎、螣蛇来克世，钱财和性命都难保。 | If the baleful spirit is the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake controlling the Self line, neither money nor life is safe. |
> | 若就家中求，却不如此断。 | 如果是在家里求财，就不这样断。 | If the money is sought at home, do not judge it this way. |
> | 必是凶人搅乱，或因财引惹公事。 | 那一定是有恶人捣乱，或因为财惹上官司。 | It will be bad people making trouble, or the money dragging you into an official matter. |
> | 若值解神，却得无事。 | 如果遇到解神，就能没事。 | If a relieving spirit is present, all will be well. |
> | 劫杀乃求财之大忌，若临财位，必不可得。 | 劫杀是求财的大忌，如果临在财爻上，一定得不到。 | The Robbery star is the great enemy of seeking money; if it falls on the Wealth line, the money cannot be had. |
> | 若临兄弟发动，得财之后，防有失脱； | 如果临在发动的兄弟上，得财之后，要防失落； | If it falls on a moving Peer line, after you get the money, beware of losing it; |
> | 若更玄武加临，必有盗贼劫掠之祸； | 如果再加玄武，一定有盗贼抢劫之祸； | if the Dark Tortoise is there as well, robbery is certain; |
> | 若得贵人临鬼持世，虽有贼来，无伤于我； | 如果贵人临官鬼持世，即使有贼来，也伤不到我； | if the Noble star falls on a Pressure line sitting on the Self line, thieves may come but cannot hurt me; |
> | 如逢鬼动，兄弟受制，纵有凶神，亦无大患。 | 如果官鬼发动，兄弟受制，即使有凶神，也没有大患。 | if Pressure moves and Peer is held down, there is no great harm even with baleful spirits about. |
> | 鬼爻无气则无权，兄弟依然来作祸。 | 官鬼无气就没有力量，兄弟照样来作祸。 | But a Pressure line without force has no power, and Peer will still do its damage. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这里的「劫杀」是神煞，和《黄金策》「岁君逢劫杀」里指兄弟爻的「劫杀」不是一回事。
>
> *Note:* The Robbery star here is a spirit-star; it is not the 'robber' of the Golden Strategy's 'Tai Sui meets the robber', which means the Peer line.
