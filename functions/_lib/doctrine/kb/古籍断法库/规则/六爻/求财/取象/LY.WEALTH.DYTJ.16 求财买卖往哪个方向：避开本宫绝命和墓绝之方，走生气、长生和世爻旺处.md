# 求财买卖往哪个方向：避开本宫绝命和墓绝之方，走生气、长生和世爻旺处

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求财买卖往哪个方向走吉：本宫的绝命方、入墓的方位、加上鬼的墓在同一边，墓绝的方位最凶，想经营就避开这条路；生气、长生的方位吉，世爻旺的地方好，八卦的方位都照这个定，终身没有病也没有灾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Which direction is auspicious for seeking wealth and trade: the Life-severing direction of the home palace, the tomb direction, and the tomb of Pressure on the same side, the directions of tomb and severance are the worst, and one who means to run a business avoids that road; the directions of Vital Qi and Long Life are auspicious, a place where the Self is thriving is good, and the directions of the eight trigrams are all fixed this way; then one has no illness and no calamity for life.

## 《断易天机·占买卖第二十》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1330 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 20: Divining for buying and selling", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 本宫绝命及入墓， | 本宫的绝命方，以及入墓的方位， | The Life-severing direction of the home palace, and the tomb direction, |
> | 兼之鬼墓同厢顾， | 再加上鬼的墓也在同一边， | together with the tomb of Pressure on the same side: |
> | 墓绝之方最为凶， | 墓、绝的方位最凶， | the directions of tomb and severance are the worst; |
> | 若欲经营避此路。 | 想经营就要避开这条路。 | if you mean to run a business, avoid that road. |
> | 生气长生是吉方， | 生气、长生的方位是吉方， | The directions of Vital Qi and Long Life are the auspicious ones; |
> | 世爻旺处即为良， | 世爻旺的地方就是好的； | where the Self is thriving is good; |
> | 八卦之方同此定， | 八卦的方位都照这个来定， | the directions of the eight trigrams are all fixed this way, |
> | 终身无病亦无秧。 | 终身没有病，也没有灾。 | and for life there is no illness and no calamity. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「绝命」「生气」是八宅游年里的方位名；本书没有在这一条里讲怎么排，本库不补。「无秧」的「秧」可能是「殃」的误字，原文如此。
>
> *Note:* 'Life-severing' and 'Vital Qi' are direction names from the house-facing system; this passage does not explain how they are laid out and this library does not add it. 'Yang' in the last line may be a miscopy of 'calamity'; the original reads as given.
