# 借贷戒应空（虚诺）；觅物忌世空（难得）；畋猎同；买货世空，财在卦也难买

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借贷要戒应空，那是空头许诺；觅物忌世空，那是难得；打猎也一样。注：买货如果遇世空，妻财即使在卦上，也主难买。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In borrowing and lending beware the Other falling Void, which is an empty promise; in seeking a thing beware the Self falling Void, which means it is hard to get; hunting is the same. The note adds: in buying goods, if the Self falls Void, even with Wealth in the hexagram it is hard to buy.
