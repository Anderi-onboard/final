# 买卖：月建临财则吉；官鬼临库，公财吉私财凶

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买卖：兄弟和财不利，官鬼动则价贱、货要出手；月建临财就吉；官鬼临库，公家的财吉，私人的财凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a trade: Peer and Wealth are unfavourable, and Pressure moving means cheap and the goods should go out; the Month on Wealth is auspicious; Pressure on the tomb-store: public money is auspicious and private money ill.

## 《火珠林·占买卖》原文

> | 月建临财则吉， | 月建临财就吉； | the Month on Wealth is auspicious; |
> | 官鬼临库，公财吉，私财凶。 | 官鬼临库，公家的财吉，私人的财凶。 | Pressure on the tomb-store: public money is auspicious and private money ill. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「兄财不利，鬼动贱发」两句很省，读法不止一种，这里照字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Peer and Wealth unfavourable, Pressure moving, cheap, send out' is terse and has more than one reading; it is translated literally.
