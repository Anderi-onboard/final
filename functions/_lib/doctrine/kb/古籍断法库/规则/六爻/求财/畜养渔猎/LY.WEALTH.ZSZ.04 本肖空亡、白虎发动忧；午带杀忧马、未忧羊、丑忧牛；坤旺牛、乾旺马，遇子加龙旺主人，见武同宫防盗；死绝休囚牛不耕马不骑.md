# 本肖空亡、白虎发动忧；午带杀忧马、未忧羊、丑忧牛；坤旺牛、乾旺马，遇子加龙旺主人，见武同宫防盗；死绝休囚牛不耕马不骑

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[周易尚占]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 它自己的生肖落空亡，格外发愁；白虎发动，牲畜（血财）有忧；午爻带杀，要忧马，未要忧羊，丑要忧牛。还要推本卦、追究它的宫卦：坤旺是牛，乾旺是马；遇子加青龙，旺，利于主人；见玄武同宫，防盗贼。死、绝、休、囚的爻持在本位，牛不能耕、马不能骑；再遇煞来，应主病死，十占八九无疑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If its own sign falls Void, worry is doubled; the White Tiger moving, the livestock have trouble; Wu carrying a baleful star means worry for the horse, Wei for the sheep, Chou for the ox. Then look at the hexagram and its palace: Kun thriving is the ox, Qian thriving the horse; meeting Zi with the Dragon added and thriving favours the owner; the Dark Tortoise in the same palace, guard against thieves. Dead, cut-off, resting or imprisoned lines holding their own place mean the ox cannot plough and the horse cannot be ridden; meeting a baleful star as well, sickness and death answer, nine in ten without doubt.

> [!note] 按
> 「见武同宫」的「武」是玄武的简写。「血财」指牲畜这类有血气的财。
>
> *Note:* 'Wu' in 'seeing Wu in the same palace' abbreviates the Dark Tortoise. 'Living wealth' means wealth in creatures that have blood and breath.
