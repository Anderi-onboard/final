# 世克官，官生世，须宜谒见

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谒贵求财：世爻克官鬼或官鬼生世，宜去拜见；财临月破、官鬼克世，白跑。野鹤：求名以官鬼为用，求利以妻财为用，用神持世或生合世爻为吉；逢空破墓绝、动而变凶，徒劳；世爻化鬼、化回头克，或随鬼入墓、助鬼伤身，不宜去，去则到彼或途中遇祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Calling on the great for money: the Self line controlling Pressure, or Pressure generating the Self line, go; Wealth under a Month-break or Pressure controlling the Self line, a wasted journey. Yehe: for a post take Pressure, for gain take Wealth; the Subject on or generating or combining with the Self line is good; void, broken, entombed, extinguished, or turning harmful, the trip is for nothing; the Self line turning into Pressure or into its own control, following the ghost into the tomb or feeding the ghost that harms it, do not go, or trouble meets you there or on the way.
