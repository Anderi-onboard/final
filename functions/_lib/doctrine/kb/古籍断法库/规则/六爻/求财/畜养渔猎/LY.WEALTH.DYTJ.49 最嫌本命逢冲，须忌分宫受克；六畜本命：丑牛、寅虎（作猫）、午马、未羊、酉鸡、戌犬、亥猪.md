# 最嫌本命逢冲，须忌分宫受克；六畜本命：丑牛、寅虎（作猫）、午马、未羊、酉鸡、戌犬、亥猪

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 最怕的是六畜的本命爻逢冲，也忌分宫爻受克。注：丑爻是牛，寅爻是虎（也当猫爻断），午马、未羊、酉鸡、戌犬、亥猪；本命怕遇鬼煞、大小耗相刑相克，也嫌空亡，都主损伤；另一说，初鸡鸭、二猫犬、三猪、四羊、五牛、六马和驴骡，是分宫；本命上卦以分宫为主，吉神来生则吉，凶煞来克则凶，日辰扶持亦吉，日辰刑冲则凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* What is most disliked is the livestock's natal line meeting a clash, and the line of its own palace being controlled. The commentary says: Chou is the ox, Yin the tiger (also judged as the cat line), Wu the horse, Wei the sheep, You the chicken, Xu the dog, Hai the pig; the natal line fears Pressure, baleful stars and the Great and Small Waste stars punishing and controlling it, and dislikes Void, all of which mean damage; another saying gives first chicken and duck, second cat and dog, third pig, fourth sheep, fifth ox, sixth horse, donkey and mule as the palace division; when the natal line is in the hexagram, the palace division is the main thing, an auspicious spirit coming to generate it is auspicious, a baleful one coming to control it is ominous, the Day's support is auspicious and the Day's punishment or clash is ominous.

> [!note] 按
> 第一段是《天玄赋》的歌诀，第二段是紧接着的括号小注。「分宫」指六爻各主一种牲畜（初鸡鸭……），「本命」指这种牲畜的生肖地支所在的爻，两者在这段注里并用。
>
> *Note:* The first passage is the verse of the Tianxuan Fu and the second the bracketed small note that follows it. 'The palace division' assigns each line to one animal (first line chickens and ducks, and so on) and 'the natal line' is the line at the animal's own branch; the note uses the two side by side.
