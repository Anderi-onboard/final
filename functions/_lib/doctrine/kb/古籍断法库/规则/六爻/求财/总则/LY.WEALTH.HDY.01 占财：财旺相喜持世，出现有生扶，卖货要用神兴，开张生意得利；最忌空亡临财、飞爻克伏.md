# 占财：财旺相喜持世，出现有生扶，卖货要用神兴，开张生意得利；最忌空亡临财、飞爻克伏

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占求财：财旺相，喜欢它持世；出现在卦上又有生扶，就掌了权、出了力；卖货求财，用神要兴旺；开张做生意才得利。眉批说：最忌空亡临在财上、伏着的财被飞爻克制，那样只是白费力。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining for wealth: Wealth thriving is glad to hold the Self; showing in the hexagram and supported, it takes charge and does the work; to sell off goods for money the Subject must thrive; in opening a business there is profit. The marginal comment says: most to be avoided are Void on Wealth and a hidden Wealth controlled by its flying line, where the effort is only wasted.

## 《海底眼》眉批(书眉上的旁注,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 648 行 · Marginal comment in the Haidi Yan (author unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 最忌空亡临财， | 最忌空亡临在财上， | Most to be avoided is Void on Wealth, |
> | 飞爻克伏， | 伏着的财被飞爻克制， | or a hidden Wealth controlled by its flying line, |
> | 徒为费力。 | 只是白费力。 | where the effort is only wasted. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 《断易天机》的求财章里也有这一首，引作「海底眼云」，第二句作「出现生扶可乘势」（见 DYTJ.07）；这里作「司秉劳」，字义比较费解，「司秉劳」照字面译作「掌权担劳」，不确定。
>
> *Note:* The Duanyi Tianji's chapter on wealth also has this verse, quoted as 'the Eye at the Bottom of the Sea says', with the second line reading 'showing and supported one may ride the moment' (see DYTJ.07); here it reads 'si bing lao', which is hard to make out and is translated literally as 'takes charge and does the work', without certainty.
