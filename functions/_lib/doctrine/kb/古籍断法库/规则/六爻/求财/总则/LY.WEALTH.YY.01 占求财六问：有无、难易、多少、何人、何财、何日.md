# 占求财六问：有无、难易、多少、何人、何财、何日

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占求财要分六问：有无、难易、多少、得于何人、得何财、何日得；靠人说合的也要占。鬼谷分爻：初爻己身、二爻伴侣、三爻行李、四爻车马、五爻道路、六爻店舍。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A money question breaks into six: whether there is money, easy or hard, how much, from whom, what kind, and on what day; where it depends on someone putting in a word, ask about that too. Guigu's assignment of the lines: first, oneself; second, companions; third, baggage; fourth, carts and horses; fifth, the road; sixth, the lodging.

## 游南子(《易隐》引)

> | 游南子曰，占求财者，求其求之有无， | 游南子说：占求财，要问的是求得到求不到， | Master Younan says: in a money question, ask whether there is anything to get, |

> | 若乃吹嘘成就，全藉乎人，亦不可以不占也。 | 至于靠别人说好话促成的，全凭人力，也不能不占。 | And where it depends on someone putting in a word for you, it rests entirely on people, so that too must be asked. |
