# 又曰：月破空亡未可凭，飞爻克伏要留神；要事事如意，只看财生世

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财逢月破、空亡，不可凭信；伏着的财被飞爻克制，要留神；如果想事事如意，只看财有没有生世。眉批举了一个例子：寅日占得益卦，讨益（求利）当天就有益，因为日辰与财库比和。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth meeting Month-break or Void cannot be relied on; a hidden Wealth controlled by its flying line needs care; if you want everything to go as wished, look only at whether Wealth generates the Self. The marginal comment gives an example: on a Yin day the Yi hexagram is got, and the benefit sought comes the same day, because the Day matches the store of Wealth.
