# 无鬼分争，又怕交重而阻滞

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼能制兄弟：卦中无官鬼则兄弟专权、有人分争；官鬼发动又主阻隔。兄弟动时喜官鬼动来制；兄弟静时官鬼不宜动，动则泄财气、有口舌（觉子）。官鬼被克、冲散或合住，阻而无妨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure keeps Peer in check: without Pressure, Peer takes over and there is fighting over shares; yet a moving Pressure line means obstruction. When Peer moves, a moving Pressure line is welcome to restrain it; when Peer is still, Pressure should not move, since it drains Wealth and brings quarrels (Juezi). If the Pressure line is controlled, clashed apart or combined, its obstruction does no harm.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7789 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 无鬼之卦，兄必专权，财虽有气，亦多虚耗。 | 没有官鬼的卦，兄弟一定专权，财即使有气，也多虚耗。 | In a hexagram without Pressure, Peer is sure to take control; even a Wealth with force is largely wasted. |
> | 兄更发动，必有争夺买卖，分散财物之患。 | 兄弟再发动，一定有人争夺买卖、分走财物。 | If Peer also moves, there will be rivals fighting over the trade and splitting up the goods. |
> | 然虽不可无，官又不宜动，动则必有阻隔。 | 官鬼虽然不能没有，却又不宜发动，动了一定有阻隔。 | So although Pressure must not be absent, it should not move either; if it moves there will be obstruction. |
> | 若有克制，或被冲散，或被合住，虽阻不妨。 | 如果官鬼被克制，或被冲散、被合住，即使有阻也不要紧。 | If the Pressure line is controlled, clashed apart or held by a combination, the obstruction does no harm. |

^q2
