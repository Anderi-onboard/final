# 辰合动财，却被他人把住

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻动来生合世爻本易得；若被日辰或动爻合住，财被人把持：合它的那一爻（卦无财则看财伏在哪一爻下）就是把持的人，如父母为长辈、官鬼为贵人；到冲开之日才到手（王洪绪）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A moving Wealth line that generates or combines with the Self line is easy money; but if the Day or a moving line combines with and holds it, someone is keeping it back. The holding line (or, if there is no Wealth, the line the hidden Wealth lies under) shows who: Resource an elder, Pressure a person of rank. It arrives on the day that clashes the combination open (Wang Hongxu).

> | 要财动来生合，固是易得之兆； | 财爻动来生合世爻，本来是容易得的征兆； | Wealth moving to generate or combine with you is certainly a sign of easy gain; |

> | 卦若无财，看伏何爻下，亦可推之。如伏官下，为贵人把持之类。 | 卦里如果没有财，看财伏在哪一爻下面，也能推出来：比如伏在官鬼下，就是贵人把持，诸如此类。 | If the hexagram has no Wealth, see which line the hidden Wealth lies under: under Pressure, a person of rank is holding it, and so on. |
