# 寄放财物：财化子孙、化财、化生、化旺，吉庆平安

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寄放：财化成子孙、化成财，都吉庆；化生、化旺，永远平安。子孙是福神，是生财的母亲；财爻是主宰，管着安全寄放。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving goods: Wealth turning into Output or Wealth is all auspicious; turning into Birth or strength, lasting peace. Output is the spirit of fortune, the mother that gives birth to Wealth; the Wealth line is the master in charge of safekeeping.

## 《易林补遗·安寄财物章第一百十四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2078 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 114 - Leaving goods for safekeeping", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 化子化财咸吉庆， | 化成子孙、化成财，都吉庆； | Turning into Output or Wealth, all is auspicious; |
> | 化生化旺永平宁。 | 化生、化旺，永远平安。 | turning into Birth or strength, peace for ever. |
