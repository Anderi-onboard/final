# 六亲变用：妻财宜旺相出现；兄弟为隔神；子孙为散神，旺相生世可求财，休囚发动只宜散财

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财宜旺相、出现在卦上，伏在世下的要生世；动而不当，宜脱货；兄弟叫隔神，忌动而不当；子孙叫散神，旺相生世可以求财，休囚发动就只宜散财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth should be thriving and showing in the hexagram, and if hidden under the Self it should generate the Self; if it moves out of place, sell off goods; Peer is called the Separating spirit and should not move out of place; Output is called the Scattering spirit, and thriving and generating the Self one may seek wealth, but weak and moving it suits only scattering money.

> [!note] 按
> 「动不中」的确切含义书里没有解释，这里译作「动而不当」。
>
