# 托本求利：放债求息、出租房船车轿，同一个断法

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 把本钱借给亲戚邻居、放债求息、把房屋车船轿子租给别人取利，都以妻财为主，看世应和内外卦来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Lending capital to kin and neighbours, lending at interest, and letting houses, boats, carts and sedan chairs are all judged the same way: Wealth is the chief, and the Self and the Other, inner and outer, are the evidence.
