# 欲纳其物而无祸，类防讼；欲纳而无争，戒应克

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想接受他的东西而不遭祸，要像防打官司那样防；想接受而不起争执，要戒应爻克世。注：随墓、助伤、鬼动、官克世，是有祸；应克世，是有争。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To take in his goods without misfortune, guard as one guards against a lawsuit; to take them in without dispute, beware of the Other controlling the Self. The note adds: following into the tomb, helping to harm, Pressure moving or Pressure controlling the Self means misfortune; the Other controlling the Self means dispute.
