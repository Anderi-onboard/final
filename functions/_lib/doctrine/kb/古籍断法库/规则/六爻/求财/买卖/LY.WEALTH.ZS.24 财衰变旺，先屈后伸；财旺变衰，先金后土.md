# 财衰变旺，先屈后伸；财旺变衰，先金后土

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财衰动变旺：眼下贱、将来涨；财旺动变衰：卖要急、买不可收。财爻旺时卖，衰时买。内卦财衰外卖，世临旺财近卖；财化进神往外发卖，世爻化退回近处出脱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A weak Wealth moving into strength: cheap now, dearer later; a strong Wealth moving into weakness: sell fast, do not buy. Sell when Wealth is strong, buy when it is weak. Wealth weak in the inner trigram, sell elsewhere; a strong Wealth on the Self line, sell near home; Wealth turning Advancing, go further afield; the Self line turning Retreating, come back and sell nearby.

## 《增删卜易·卖货宜守宜动章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11421 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Selling goods: stay or move", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 财衰变旺，先屈后体；财旺变衰，先金后土。 | 财由衰变旺，先屈后伸；财由旺变衰，先贵后贱（先是金，后是土）。 | Wealth changing from weak to strong: bent first, then straightened; from strong to weak: gold first, then dirt. |
> | 谓财爻动而变也。 | 说的是财爻动而变。 | This refers to the Wealth line moving and changing. |
> | 财衰动变旺相，目下虽贱，而将来必长； | 财衰而动变旺相，眼下虽然贱，将来一定涨； | A weak Wealth moving into strength: cheap now, bound to rise later; |
> | 财旺动变衰绝，如若卖货，宜于急卖；如果收货，切不可收。 | 财旺而动变衰绝，如果卖货，宜赶紧卖；如果收货，千万不可收。 | a strong Wealth moving into weakness or extinction: if selling, sell quickly; if buying, do not buy. |
> | 买货与卖货相反，财爻旺而可卖，财爻衰而宜买。 | 买货和卖货相反：财爻旺时可以卖，财爻衰时宜买。 | Buying is the reverse of selling: sell when the Wealth line is strong, buy when it is weak. |
> | 内卦财衰，宜于外卖；世爻财旺，近地相宜。 | 内卦财衰，宜到外地卖；世爻临财而旺，就近卖合适。 | Wealth weak in the inner trigram: sell elsewhere; strong Wealth on the Self line: sell near home. |
> | 财化进神，宜往外方发卖；世爻化退，宜回近地出脱。 | 财化进神，宜往外地发卖；世爻化退，宜回到近处卖掉。 | Wealth turning Advancing: go further afield to sell; the Self line turning Retreating: come back and sell nearby. |

> [!note] 按
> 「先屈后体」的「体」，按上下文当是「伸」（先屈后伸）；原文照录，标题按意思写作「先屈后伸」。
>
> *Note:* The text reads 'bent first, then ti'; from the context ti stands for shen (straightened). We quote it as written and give the sense in the title.
