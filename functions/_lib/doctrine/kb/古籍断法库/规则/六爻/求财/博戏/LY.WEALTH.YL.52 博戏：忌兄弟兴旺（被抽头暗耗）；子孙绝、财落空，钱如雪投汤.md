# 博戏：忌兄弟兴旺（被抽头暗耗）；子孙绝、财落空，钱如雪投汤

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌钱博弈：兄弟兴旺，会被人抽头、暗中耗去，手气不顺、心里不快，钱财该输；子孙和财不空不绝，只是一般；一遇空，即使有万贯钱财，也像雪投进热汤。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Gambling for money: Peer thriving means being skimmed in the open and drained in secret, luck running against you and an unhappy heart, and the money is lost; with Output and Wealth neither void nor extinct it is only slight; once void, even ten thousand strings of cash is like snow dropped into hot soup.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2047 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 赌钱博奕之事，若兄弟兴隆， | 赌钱博弈的事，如果兄弟兴旺， | In gambling for money, if Peer thrives, |
> | 必被抽筹窍码，明取暗耗色不顺而心不快，财物当输。 | 一定会被人抽筹、在计数上做手脚，明里被取、暗里被耗，手气不顺、心里不快，钱财该输； | you will surely be skimmed of chips and cheated in the counting, taken from in the open and drained in secret, the dice run against you and you are unhappy, and the money is lost; |
> | 若子财不空不绝稍稍而已。 | 如果子孙、财不空不绝，也只是稍稍而已。 | if Output and Wealth are neither void nor extinct, it is only slight. |
> | 一逢空，纵有万贯囊资，浑如片雪投汤。 | 一遇空，即使有万贯钱财，也像一片雪投进热汤。 | Once void, even ten thousand strings of cash in the purse is like a snowflake dropped into hot soup. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「抽筹窍码」两词都不常见，这里译作被抽筹码、在计数上做手脚，是照字面取的读法。
>
> *Note:* 'Chou chou qiao ma' are two rare terms; they are read literally as being skimmed of chips and cheated in the counting.
