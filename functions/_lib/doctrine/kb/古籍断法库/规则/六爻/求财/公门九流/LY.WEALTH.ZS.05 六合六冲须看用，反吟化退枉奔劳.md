# 六合六冲须看用，反吟化退枉奔劳

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六合、六冲不能单独定吉凶：六合要用神旺相，或世与财、官相合，才吉；六冲要用神受克失陷，才凶。反吟、世动化退：难以成行，勉强去也空手回。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Six-Combine and Six-Clash do not decide the matter by themselves: a Six-Combine is good only with the Subject strong or the Self line combining with Wealth or Pressure; a Six-Clash is bad only if the Subject is controlled and falls. Reversed Chant, or the Self line moving into Retreating: hard to set out, and back empty-handed if you force it.
