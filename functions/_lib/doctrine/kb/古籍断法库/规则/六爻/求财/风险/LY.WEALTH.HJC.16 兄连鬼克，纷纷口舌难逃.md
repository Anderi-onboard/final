# 兄连鬼克，纷纷口舌难逃

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟、官鬼同时发动（王洪绪），或兄弟动而官鬼克世：主口舌是非，父母再动则成官讼。兄弟动变官鬼是回头克，兄弟被制，不能劫财克世（王洪绪、觉子驳旧注）；但野鹤举例，问财而见兄化鬼，可能应在兄弟（亲人）身上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer and Pressure moving together (Wang Hongxu), or Peer moving while Pressure controls the Self line: quarrels and trouble, and a lawsuit if Resource moves too. Peer moving into Pressure is a turn-back control that disables Peer, so it cannot rob or attack the Self line (Wang Hongxu and Juezi against the old commentary); but Yehe gives a case where Peer turning into Pressure in a money question showed up as a brother's death.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「兄连鬼克」怎么读：旧注读作兄弟变出官鬼来克世，主口舌、官讼；王洪绪和觉子都驳它——兄弟变官鬼是回头克，兄弟自身难保，不能再去克世；王洪绪改读为兄弟、官鬼同时发动才有口舌。野鹤把经文改成「兄伤鬼克」，并举例说兄化鬼虽然不劫财，却应在问卜人的弟弟身亡。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to read 'Peer together with Pressure': the old commentary takes it as Peer turning into Pressure and attacking the Self line, meaning quarrels and lawsuits. Wang Hongxu and Juezi both reject this: Peer turning into Pressure is a turn-back control, Peer cannot save itself, let alone attack the Self line; Wang reads it instead as Peer and Pressure moving at the same time. Yehe rewrites the line as 'Peer harming, Pressure attacking', and gives a case where Peer turning into Pressure did not rob the money but showed up as the death of the asker's younger brother.
