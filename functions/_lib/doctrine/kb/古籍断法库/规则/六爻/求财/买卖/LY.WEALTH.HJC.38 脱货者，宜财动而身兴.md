# 脱货者，宜财动而身兴

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卖货：财动易脱、世动易卖；应空无买主，世空自己卖不成；动变日辰来生合，有人争买；遇刑冲多阻；财在内卦动（生世）本地卖，在外卦动外地卖（财持世而子孙在外卦动，也宜外卖）；动而遇合将成不成，动而逢空欲卖不卖；卦无官鬼难成交。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Selling goods: a moving Wealth means they go easily, a moving Self line that they sell easily; a void Other line, no buyer; a void Self line, you fail to sell; moving and changed lines and the Day generating or combining, buyers compete; punishment or clash, obstacles; Wealth moving in the inner trigram (generating the Self line), sell locally; in the outer, sell elsewhere (also when Wealth is on the Self line and Output moves in the outer trigram); moving but combined, almost closed yet not; moving but void, wanting to sell yet not; no Pressure in the hexagram, hard to close.

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 脱货财动，则主易脱。 | 卖货时财爻发动，主容易卖掉。 | When selling off goods, a moving Wealth line means they sell easily. |
> | 应空，无人置买；世空，自卖不成； | 应爻空，没人来买；世爻空，自己卖不成； | Other void: no buyer; Self void: you fail to sell; |
> | 动变日辰，俱来生合，有人争买。 | 动爻、变爻、日辰都来生合，有人争着买。 | moving and changed lines and the Day all generating or combining: buyers compete. |
> | 若遇刑冲，则主多破阻难成。 | 如果遇刑冲，主多阻碍，难以成交。 | If met by punishment or clash, many obstacles; hard to close. |
> | 财在内动，宜在本处卖；在外动，宜向他处脱之。 | 财在内卦发动，宜在本地卖；在外卦发动，宜到外地卖。 | Wealth moving in the inner trigram: sell locally; in the outer: sell elsewhere. |
> | 动而遇合，将成不成；动而逢空，欲卖不卖。 | 财动而遇合，快成交却成不了；动而逢空，想卖又卖不掉。 | Moving but combined: almost closed, yet not; moving but void: wanting to sell, yet not selling. |
> | 卦无官鬼，亦是难成之象。 | 卦里没有官鬼，也是难以成交之象。 | No Pressure in the hexagram is also a sign that it will be hard to close. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2731 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财动则主易脱，世动主易卖也。 | 财动主容易脱手，世动主容易卖出。 | A moving Wealth means the goods go easily; a moving Self line means they sell easily. |
> | 如财在外生世，宜往他处卖；如在内动生世，就本地脱之可也。 | 如果财在外卦生世爻，宜到外地去卖；如果在内卦动来生世，就在本地卖掉。 | If Wealth in the outer trigram generates the Self line, sell elsewhere; if it moves in the inner trigram and generates the Self line, sell locally. |
> | 倘财爻持世，有子孙爻在外动，亦宜往他处脱，学者宜通变。 | 如果财爻持世，有子孙在外卦发动，也宜到外地卖，学的人要会变通。 | If the Wealth line is on the Self line and an Output line moves in the outer trigram, also sell elsewhere; students should judge flexibly. |
