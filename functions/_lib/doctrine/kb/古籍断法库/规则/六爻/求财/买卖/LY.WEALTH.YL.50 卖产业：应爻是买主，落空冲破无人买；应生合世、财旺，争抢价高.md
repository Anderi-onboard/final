# 卖产业：应爻是买主，落空冲破无人买；应生合世、财旺，争抢价高

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卖产业：应爻是买主，落空亡就没有力量成交，被日辰冲破就被人阻挠；卦里没有官，也难成事；应爻生世、合世，或者财旺来生合，买主争抢，价钱立刻上涨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Selling property: the Other line is the buyer; fallen into void he has not the means to close, clashed and broken by the Day someone obstructs it; with no Pressure in the hexagram it is also hard to complete; the Other line generating or combining with the Self, or a strong Wealth coming to generate or combine, buyers scramble and the price shoots up.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2039 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应爻为承买之人，若落空亡乃无力成交。 | 应爻是承买的人，如果落空亡，就是没有力量成交； | The Other line is the buyer; if it falls into void, he has not the means to close; |
> | 日辰冲破，被人挠阻。 | 被日辰冲破，会被人阻挠； | clashed and broken by the Day, someone obstructs it; |
> | 用户内外无官，亦难成事。 | 内外卦里都没有官，也难成事； | with no Pressure in the inner or outer trigram it is also hard to complete; |
> | 应爻生世合世，又或财旺来生合，纷纷争夺产价顿增。 | 应爻生世、合世，或者财旺来生合，买主纷纷争抢，产业价钱立刻上涨。 | the Other line generating or combining with the Self, or a strong Wealth coming to generate or combine, buyers scramble one after another and the price of the property shoots up. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「用户内外无官」的「用户」在这里读作占问的这一家（用神所在的内外），原文语焉不详。
>
> *Note:* 'The user's household, inner and outer, has no Pressure' is terse in the source; here it is read as no Pressure anywhere in the inner or outer trigram.
