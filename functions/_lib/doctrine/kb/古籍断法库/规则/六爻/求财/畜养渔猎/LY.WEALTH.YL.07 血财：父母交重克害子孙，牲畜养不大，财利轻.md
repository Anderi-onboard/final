# 血财：父母交重克害子孙，牲畜养不大，财利轻

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 养六畜这类血财：最怕父母交重去克害子孙，那样牲畜养不大，财利也薄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For living-goods money such as livestock: the great fear is Resource moving to harm Output, and then the animals do not thrive and the profit is small.

> | 血财更虑文书发， | 血财更担心文书（父母）发动； | For living-goods money, the further worry is the document line, Resource, stirring; |
