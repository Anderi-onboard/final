# 趋利公门要把握官的喜怒；博戏须详应象克生；应克世，财旺也先赢后输

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在公门谋利，要把握官的喜怒；求财、博戏，要仔细看应的生克。注：官鬼空破，是官不重；如果官克世，主有刑责，所以财虽旺也不吉。应爻克世，是对方胜我，财再旺也一定先赢后输。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To seek profit through official channels, master the official's likes and dislikes; in seeking wealth or gambling, examine closely how the Other generates or controls. The note adds: Pressure Void or broken means the official is not weighty; if Pressure controls the Self there is punishment, so even a thriving Wealth is not auspicious. The Other controlling the Self means the other side beats me, and however thriving Wealth is, one first wins and then loses.
