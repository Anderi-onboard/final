# 顿货：财爻衰弱才可买进囤置，利反而厚

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 囤货：偏偏要财爻衰弱才可以买进，衰的时候买，利润反而更厚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Stocking goods: it is precisely a weak Wealth line that allows buying, and buying when it is weak makes the profit richer.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1940 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 顿货财衰利转浓， | 囤货，财衰，利反而更厚； | For stocking goods, a weak Wealth makes the profit richer, not poorer; |
