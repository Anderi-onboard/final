# 脱货之利：游魂财旺宜行、归魂财衰宜止；六冲改图得利、六合守而生财；财坐世本地脱、居应他乡脱、现间爻中途生息

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 游魂卦财旺宜行动，归魂卦财衰宜停止；六冲卦宜改变谋划而得利，六合卦宜守着而生财；财坐世爻，遂在本地脱货的心愿，财居应爻，在他乡脱货称心，财现在间爻，在中途反而生息。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a Wandering Soul hexagram with thriving Wealth it is fit to move, in a Returning Soul hexagram with weak Wealth it is fit to stop; a Six-Clash hexagram is fit for changing plans to gain, and a Six-Combine hexagram for holding fast and breeding wealth; Wealth sitting on the Self fulfils selling at home, on the Other it suits selling in another place, and showing on a between line it breeds interest on the way.
