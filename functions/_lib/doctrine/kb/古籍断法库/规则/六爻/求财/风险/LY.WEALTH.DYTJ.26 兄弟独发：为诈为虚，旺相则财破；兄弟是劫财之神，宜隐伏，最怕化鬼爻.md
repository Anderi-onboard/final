# 兄弟独发：为诈为虚，旺相则财破；兄弟是劫财之神，宜隐伏，最怕化鬼爻

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟一爻独自发动，是诈、是虚，旺相时钱财破耗；兄弟是劫财的神，最好隐伏，发动主虚假诈骗不实之事，旺相主口舌忧疑破财，出现发动还要看变成什么，最怕化成鬼爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer moving alone means fraud and emptiness, and when thriving the money is broken; Peer is the spirit that robs Wealth and had best lie hidden; moving, it stands for false, deceitful and untrue matters; thriving, for quarrels, worry, doubt and loss of money; showing and moving, look at what it changes into, and most of all fear a change into a Pressure line.
