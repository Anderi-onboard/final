# 财爻不动平安，遇杀逢空破费；外鬼牵玄武，官灾盗贼；劫杀临身克身，忌暗昧

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻不动，钱财平安；遇凶杀、逢空，终究破费。外卦的官鬼把玄武牵来，官灾、盗贼，重重后悔。劫杀临身又克身，暗昧私下的事必须忌讳。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth still, the money is safe; meeting an ill star or void, in the end it is spent. A Pressure outside bringing in the Dark Tortoise: official trouble and thieves, regrets on regrets. The Robbery star on the Body and controlling it: secret and shady dealings must be avoided.
