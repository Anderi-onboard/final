# 求财无鬼，须分主客：同本而权落他人，空拳而我无遭遇

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求财而卦里没有鬼，要分主客：合本经营的，权落到别人手里；空手求财的，我没有遭遇。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking wealth with no Pressure in the hexagram, tell host from guest: with jointly held capital the power falls to another, and with empty hands I meet nothing.

## 《易冒·无鬼章第三十二》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 558 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 32: No Pressure", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 求财无鬼， | 求财而卦里没有鬼， | Seeking wealth with no Pressure in the hexagram, |
> | 须分主客， | 要分主客： | tell host from guest: |
> | 同本而权落他人， | 合本经营的，权落到别人手里； | with jointly held capital, the power falls to another; |
> | 空拳而我无遭遇也； | 空手求财的，我没有遭遇。 | with empty hands, I meet nothing. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「主客」在这里指出本钱的一方和出力的一方，书里没有明说，这里取此意，不确定。
>
> *Note:* 'Host and guest' here probably means the party who puts up capital and the party who does the work; the book does not say so plainly and this is a reading, without certainty.
