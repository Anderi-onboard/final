# 螣蛇临外财：在外州做买卖后回来

六爻 · 求财 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 螣蛇临在外面的财上，是到外州去做买卖再回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Coiling Snake sitting on an outer Wealth means going to another prefecture to trade and then returning.

## 《断易天机·何知章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 975 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "The chapter on how to know", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若见鰧蛇临外财， | 如果见到螣蛇临在外面的财上， | If the Coiling Snake is seen on an outer Wealth, |
> | 经纪外州回。 | 是到外州做买卖后回来。 | it means going to another prefecture to trade, and returning. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「外财」可指外卦上的财，也可指外地的财；书里没有分开，这里译作「外面的财」。
>
> *Note:* 'Outer Wealth' can mean the Wealth in the outer trigram or money from outside; the book does not distinguish, and it is rendered 'outer Wealth'.
