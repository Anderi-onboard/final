# 官鬼发动克兄弟，买卖财轻赌博输；官化财，财自得，赌博抽拈却输

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼发动克兄弟，做买卖财利微薄，赌博输；官鬼化成财，钱财自己到手，但赌博、抽拈反而输。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure moving and controlling Peer means that trade brings slight profit and gambling loses; Pressure changing into Wealth, the money comes of itself, but gambling and drawing lots lose all the same.

## 《断易天机·官鬼用变》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 854 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "What the Pressure line does when it moves or changes", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼发动克兄弟， | 官鬼发动，克兄弟， | Pressure moving and controlling Peer: |
> | 婚姻未就生滞疑； | 婚姻还没成，生出停滞和疑虑； | a marriage not yet made meets delay and doubt; |
> | 病困门庭祸祟来， | 家里病困，灾祸作祟； | illness besets the household and misfortune haunts it; |
> | 更改动身皆不吉。 | 改动、动身，都不吉； | changing plans or setting out are both inauspicious; |
> | 出外逃亡定见灾， | 出外、逃亡，一定遇灾； | going abroad or fleeing surely meets calamity; |
> | 词讼官非有囚系； | 打官司，有被拘禁的事； | a lawsuit brings confinement; |
> | 买卖财轻赌博输， | 做买卖财利微薄，赌博输； | in trade the profit is slight, and in gambling one loses; |
> | 失物难寻多暗昧。 | 失物难找，多是暗昧不明。 | lost things are hard to find and mostly obscure. |

## 《断易天机·官鬼用变》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 855 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "What the Pressure line does when it moves or changes", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官化财兮财自得， | 官鬼化成财，钱财自然到手； | Pressure changing into Wealth, the money comes of itself; |
> | 赌博抽拈却主输； | 赌博、抽拈，却主输。 | gambling and drawing lots, however, lose. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这首七言歌诀把病、婚姻、考试、行人、官司、失物等一并说完，本库整首收下，求财只在其中一两句；书里的今人[译文]不引。
>
> *Note:* This seven-character verse covers illness, marriage, exams, travellers, lawsuits and lost things together; this library takes the whole verse, of which only a line or two concerns wealth, and does not cite the modern translation printed after it.
