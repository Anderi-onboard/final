# 寄放财物：财化火，防内外失火（巳午、火性官鬼动同）

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寄放：财化成火，要防火神祝融，里面走火、外面延烧；巳午、火性的官鬼发动也是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Leaving goods: Wealth turning into Fire, guard against the fire god Zhurong, fire breaking out within and spreading without; Si, Wu and a Fire Pressure moving mean the same.

## 《易林补遗·安寄财物章第一百十四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2082 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 114 - Leaving goods for safekeeping", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 化火防内外之祝融， | 化成火，要防内外的祝融（火神）； | Turning into Fire, guard against Zhurong within and without; |
> | 巳午鬼兴同意。 | 巳午、官鬼兴旺，也是同一个意思。 | Si, Wu and a thriving Pressure mean the same. |

^q1
