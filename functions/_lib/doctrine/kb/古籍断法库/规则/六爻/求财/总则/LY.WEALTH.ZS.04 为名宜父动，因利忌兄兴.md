# 为名宜父动，因利忌兄兴

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问的是什么就看什么：谒贵能否见到，看世爻与官鬼相生相合；能否得名、得利要另起一卦（名看父母、官鬼两旺，利看妻财、子孙两旺）。为求名而起的卦里财爻持世，是破文书的忌神，不能读作「不成名也得利」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Read what was asked: whether you will be received depends on the Self line and Pressure generating or combining; whether it brings a post or gain needs a separate casting (a post wants Resource and Pressure strong, gain wants Wealth and Output). In a hexagram cast for a post, Wealth on the Self line is the Adversary that breaks the documents; do not read it as 'no post, but money'.

## 《增删卜易·谒贵求财章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11274 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "Calling on the great for money", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 为名宜父动，因利忌兄兴。 | 为求名，宜父母发动；为求利，忌兄弟兴动。 | For a post, a moving Resource is welcome; for gain, a stirring Peer is feared. |
> | 凡占谒贵，既得世与官星相生相合，必然得见。 | 凡是占拜见贵人，只要世爻和官鬼相生相合，一定能见到。 | When asking about calling on the great, if the Self line and Pressure generate or combine with each other, you will be received. |
> | 若为名利，须宜另占，不可即以此卦断之。 | 至于能否得名得利，要另外起卦，不能就用这一卦来断。 | Whether it brings a post or gain must be asked in a separate casting; do not judge it from this one. |
> | 盖为名者，宜父官两旺；为利者，宜财福两旺也。 | 因为求名要父母、官鬼两旺，求利要妻财、子孙两旺。 | For a post, Resource and Pressure should both be strong; for gain, Wealth and Output. |

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11280 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 求题荐全仗文书，卦中财爻持世，父母受克，难许如心。 | 求推荐全靠文书（父母），卦里财爻持世，父母受克，难以如愿。 | A recommendation depends wholly on documents (Resource); here the Wealth line sat on the Self line and Resource was controlled, so success could not be promised. |
> | 彼曰：如果得财，亦不负此一行。 | 他说：如果能得到钱财，也不枉走这一趟。 | He said: if I get money out of it, the trip will still be worth it. |
> | 余曰：非此论也，汝来意原为求名，此持世之财，乃破文书之忌神也。非许汝以求财也。 | 我说：不是这样论的。你来的本意是求名，这个持世的财，是破文书的忌神，并不是许你求财。 | I said: that is not how it reads. You came to seek a post; this Wealth on the Self line is the Adversary that breaks the documents. It does not promise you money. |
