# 妻财用·求财：财是用爻、一卦的主宰，宜生世克世，大吉；无气必难求；诸事逢之皆吉，遇兄弟有损，遇福德更佳

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财是我克的那个六亲，诸事遇到它没有不吉的，只有占父母、文书不宜见它；遇兄弟有损，遇福德更好，遇官鬼泄我的气；动静都吉。占求财，它是用爻，一卦的主宰，宜生世、克世，大吉，没有气就一定难求。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth is the relation that I control; in all matters meeting it is auspicious, and only in divining about parents or documents is it unwelcome; meeting Peer it suffers loss, meeting Output it is better still, meeting Pressure it drains my qi; moving or still, it is auspicious. In divining for wealth it is the Subject line and the master of the hexagram, best when it generates or controls the Self, greatly auspicious; with no qi the money is sure to be hard to seek.
