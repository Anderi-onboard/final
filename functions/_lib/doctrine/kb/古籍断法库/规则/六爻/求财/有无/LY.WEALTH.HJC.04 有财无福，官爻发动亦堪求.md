# 有财无福，官爻发动亦堪求

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦有财而子孙不现，本来一见兄弟就被劫；此时官鬼发动（或日辰为官鬼）克住兄弟，仍可求财，在官鬼生旺日得。卦中若有子孙，官鬼动反成阻滞。野鹤把条件改成「兄、财同动」时官鬼动可制兄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth is present but Output is not, any Peer line would rob it; a moving Pressure line (or a Pressure Day branch) that holds Peer down still makes the money obtainable, arriving on a day that births or empowers Pressure. If Output is present, a moving Pressure line becomes an obstruction instead. Yehe restates the condition as Peer and Wealth moving together, with a moving Pressure line restraining Peer.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7770 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 有财无福， | 妻财在、子孙不在， | With Wealth present but no Output, |
> | 官爻发动亦堪求。 | 官鬼发动也还可以求。 | a moving Pressure line still makes it worth pursuing. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7771 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙藏伏，财无生气，一遇兄弟，便被劫尽。 | 子孙伏藏不现，财就没有生气，一碰上兄弟，就被劫光。 | With Output hidden, Wealth has no living source, and the first Peer line it meets robs it bare. |
> | 须得卦有官动，或日辰是鬼克制兄爻，则用神无损，亦可求谋。 | 必须卦里有官鬼发动，或者日辰就是官鬼、克住兄弟，用神才不受损，也可以去求。 | It needs a moving Pressure line in the hexagram, or a Day branch of the Pressure element holding the Peer line down; then the Subject is unharmed and the venture can be tried. |
> | 卦有子孙而官鬼动，则有阻节，反不易矣。 | 如果卦里有子孙，官鬼再动，反而会有阻滞，不容易了。 | If Output is present and Pressure moves, there will be obstruction instead, and it becomes harder. |
> | 此象鬼爻生旺日得财。 | 这种卦象，在官鬼生旺的日子得财。 | In this pattern the money comes on a day that births or empowers the Pressure line. |

^q2

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11085 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄兴财振，官爻发动亦堪求。 | 兄弟和妻财同时发动，官鬼也动，也还可以求。 | When Peer and Wealth both stir, a moving Pressure line still makes it worth pursuing. |
> | 兄财同动，而官鬼亦动者，亦许得财，以官鬼克制兄爻之故耳。 | 兄弟、妻财一起动，官鬼也动，也可以断得财，因为官鬼克住了兄弟。 | If Peer and Wealth move together and Pressure moves too, money may still be granted, because Pressure holds the Peer line down. |

^q4

> [!warning] 打架
> 野鹤把经文前半句「有财无福」改成「兄兴财振」：适用的卦从「子孙不现」换成了「兄、财同动」。两种说法的理由一样——官鬼克兄弟、护住财——但管的是不同的卦。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Yehe rewrote the first half: 'Wealth present, no Output' became 'Peer and Wealth both stirring'. The reason is the same in both (Pressure controls Peer and so shields the money), but they apply to different hexagrams.
