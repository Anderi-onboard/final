# 世动则己心多变，应动则伙伴怀反背，应空无能不久；应克世更要防欺害分离

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世代表自己，不宜发动，一动心里变化多端；应代表伙伴，也宜安静，一动就怀了背叛的心，空就没有能力也不长久，动而克世，更要防被欺害、分离；应空，合伙的人还是换掉为好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self stands for oneself and should not move: moving, the mind changes in many ways. The Other stands for the partner and should also be still: moving, he harbors a heart of betrayal; void, he has no ability and will not last; moving and controlling the Self, expect to be cheated and harmed and to part. With the Other void, the partner should be replaced.
