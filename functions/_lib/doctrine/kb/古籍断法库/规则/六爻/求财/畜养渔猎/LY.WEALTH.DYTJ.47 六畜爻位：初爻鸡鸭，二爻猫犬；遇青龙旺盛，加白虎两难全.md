# 六畜爻位：初爻鸡鸭，二爻猫犬；遇青龙旺盛，加白虎两难全

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六畜里，鸡鸭在初爻，猫犬在二爻；遇到青龙就四季旺盛，加上白虎就鸡鸭猫犬两样难保全。注：初爻带鬼或落空亡，难养鸡鸭，加大小耗有狐狸黄鼠伤害，临青龙大吉；二爻的猫犬，寅动克戌是虎伤犬，戌克二爻是犬伤猫，猫爻带吉煞善捕鼠，带鬼煞家有恶犬，逢空不利，加白虎必有伤，带青龙必旺相。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Among the six kinds of livestock, chickens and ducks are on the first line and cats and dogs on the second; meeting the Azure Dragon they thrive through the four seasons, and with the White Tiger added the two cannot both be kept. The commentary adds: the first line with Pressure or Void makes chickens and ducks hard to keep, with the Great and Small Waste stars foxes and weasels hurt them, and on the Azure Dragon it is greatly auspicious; for cats and dogs on the second line, Yin moving against Xu is the tiger hurting the dog, Xu controlling the second line is the dog hurting the cat, a cat line with an auspicious star catches rats well, with Pressure and a baleful star the house has a fierce dog, falling Void is bad, with the White Tiger added there is surely harm, and with the Azure Dragon added it surely thrives.
