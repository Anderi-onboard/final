# 畜养忌官鬼（物之灾）、白虎（物之屠）

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦属、爻属、生属，都载在五行里，要考察；官鬼是物的灾，白虎是物的屠，占畜养都忌。注：官鬼是疾病，白虎是刀砧，都忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The hexagram's kind, the line's kind and the birth kind are all recorded in the five phases and are to be examined; Pressure is the animal's calamity and the White Tiger its slaughter, and both are avoided in divining raising animals. The note adds: Pressure is illness and the White Tiger is knife and block; both are to be avoided.
