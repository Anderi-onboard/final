# 空拳问利：以官为主，官旺生合世为大吉；财是元神不可空；子孙动伤官，父母动空拳无害

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 空手（没本钱）问利，是九流、工匠、靠手艺吃饭一类人的事：以官爻为主，要旺相并生世、合世，又喜财爻兴旺来扶助，最吉；财爻是官的元神，不能空；子孙发动伤官，用神就没用了；父母发动，空手求财没有妨碍，有本钱的却遭殃。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking profit empty-handed is for the trades, craftsmen and people who work for their food: Pressure is the chief; it must be strong and generate or combine with the Self, and it is best of all if a strong Wealth comes to support it; Wealth is the Support of Pressure and must not be void; Output moving hurts Pressure and leaves the Subject useless; Resource moving does the empty-handed no harm but is a curse to those with capital.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1935 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 空拳问利宜官旺， | 空手问利，宜官爻旺相； | Seeking profit empty-handed, Pressure should be strong; |
> | 财作元神莫犯空， | 财爻是它的元神，不要落空； | Wealth is its Support and must not fall void; |
> | 反怪子兴来制鬼， | 反倒要怪子孙兴旺来制住官鬼； | on the contrary, blame Output rising to hold Pressure in check; |
> | 不嫌父母值兴隆。 | 不嫌父母兴旺。 | Resource being strong is no objection. |

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1936 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 空拳问利者，九流之辈。 | 空手问利的，是九流一类的人。 | Those who seek profit empty-handed are people of the trades. |
> | 工匠餐食之流，皆名白手求财， | 工匠、靠做饭卖吃食过日子的这类人，都叫白手求财， | Craftsmen and people who make their living from food are all said to make money with bare hands; |
> | 以官为主，须要兴隆生世合世，又喜财爻兴旺而来扶助，此为大吉。 | 以官爻为主，要兴旺并且生世、合世，又喜财爻兴旺来扶助，这样最吉。 | Pressure is the chief; it must be strong and generate or combine with the Self, and it is a joy if Wealth is strong and comes to support it: this is greatly auspicious. |
> | 犯忌子孙动，动则伤官，而用无用也。 | 忌讳子孙发动，一动就伤官，用神就没用了。 | Output moving is taboo: moving, it hurts Pressure and the Subject becomes useless. |
> | 父母发动，空拳无害，将本为殃。 | 父母发动，空手求财没有妨碍，有本钱的却成了祸害。 | Resource moving does no harm to the empty-handed but is a curse to those with capital. |
> | 将本为殃，有资本者遭殃。 | 说「将本为殃」，就是有本钱的人遭殃。 | 'A curse to those with capital' means that those who have capital suffer. |
