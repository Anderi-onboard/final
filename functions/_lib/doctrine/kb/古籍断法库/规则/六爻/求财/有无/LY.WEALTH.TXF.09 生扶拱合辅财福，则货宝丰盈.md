# 生扶拱合辅财福，则货宝丰盈

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财、子孙得生扶拱合，财货丰盈。《断易天机》本细分：日辰生财福吉、生鬼杀凶；财生世易求、世生财难求；应生世是人来就我，世生应是我赶他人；世应相生逢冲仍有阻；财福拱我得人相助，我拱兄弟是他人把权；合财福吉、合鬼杀凶，合处逢生十拿九稳。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth and Output helped by generation, support, propping and combination: goods and money abound. The Duanyi Tianji text details it: the Day generating Wealth or Output is good, generating Pressure or a killing star bad; Wealth generating the Self line is easy money, the Self generating Wealth hard; the Other generating the Self means they come to me, the reverse means I run after them; Self and Other generating each other can still be blocked by a clash; Wealth or Output propping me means help from others, my propping Peer means others hold the power; combining with Wealth or Output is good, with Pressure or a killing star bad; a combination that also meets generation is a sure thing.
