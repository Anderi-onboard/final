# 主人一位刑伤，往求不遇

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 去求某人得财，以代表他的那一爻为「主人」（求贵人看官鬼，求妇人看妻财，不知是谁看应爻）。这一爻被日辰、动爻刑克，或空、化空：见不到，见了也无益；生合世爻、化出财爻生合世爻：最吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the money depends on someone you go to, the line standing for that person is the 'patron' (Pressure for a person of rank, Wealth for a woman, the Other line if you cannot tell). If that line is punished or controlled by the Day or a moving line, or is void or turns void, you will not find them and meeting would not help; if it generates or combines with the Self line, or turns into Wealth that does, it is best.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7826 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 主人一位刑伤， | 所求之人那一爻受刑伤， | If the line of the person you go to is punished or harmed, |
> | 往求不遇。 | 去求也见不到。 | you will not find them when you go. |

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7827–7828 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 主人：如求贵人，财鬼为主；求妇人财，财为主类。 | 主人（所求的人）：比如求贵人，以财、官鬼为主；求妇女的财，以财为主，诸如此类。 | The patron (the person you go to): when seeking a person of rank, Wealth and Pressure stand for them; when seeking money from a woman, Wealth does, and so on. |
> | 若主人遇动爻日辰刑克，或自空化空，皆主不遇，遇亦不利。 | 如果主人那一爻被动爻、日辰刑克，或自身落空、化空，都主见不到，见到也不利。 | If that line is punished or controlled by a moving line or the Day, or is void or turns void, you will not find them, and meeting them would not help. |
> | 须得生合世爻，财为契爱，求必易得。 | 要它生合世爻，财来得情愿，求财一定容易。 | It should generate or combine with the Self line; then the money is given willingly and is easy to get. |
> | 主人化出财爻，生合世爻，最吉。 | 主人那一爻化出财爻、生合世爻，最吉。 | If that line turns into a Wealth line that generates or combines with the Self line, it is best. |
> | 不知主人，以应爻论之。 | 不知道主人该看哪一爻，就用应爻来论。 | If you cannot tell which line is the patron, judge by the Other line. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2699 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 主人，如求贵人则鬼为主，求妇人财为主类。 | 主人：比如求贵人，就以官鬼为主；求妇女，就以财为主，诸如此类。 | The patron: when seeking a person of rank, Pressure stands for them; when seeking a woman, Wealth, and so on. |
> | 若主爻遇日辰动爻刑伤，或自空、或化空，皆主不遇，遇亦不利。 | 如果主人那一爻被日辰、动爻刑伤，或者自身落空、化空，都主见不到，见到也不利。 | If that line is punished or harmed by the Day or a moving line, or is void or turns void, you will not find them, and meeting would not help. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 求贵人时，旧注写「财鬼为主」，王洪绪写「鬼为主」；王的写法和「求妇人财为主」一句对得更齐，但两个本子各自如此，不改原文。
>
> *Note:* For a person of rank the old commentary says 'Wealth and Pressure stand for them', Wang Hongxu says 'Pressure'. Wang's wording pairs more neatly with 'Wealth for a woman', but each edition reads as it does and we leave both as they are.
