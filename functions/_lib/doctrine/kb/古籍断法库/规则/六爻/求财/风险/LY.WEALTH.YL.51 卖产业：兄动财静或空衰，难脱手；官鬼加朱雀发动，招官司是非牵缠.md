# 卖产业：兄动财静或空衰，难脱手；官鬼加朱雀发动，招官司是非牵缠

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卖产业：兄弟兴旺、财爻安静，或又空又衰，一定难脱手，不必多疑；官鬼鼓动、朱雀噪闹，必有官司拖累、是非招来，牵缠不安宁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Selling property: Peer rising with the Wealth line still, or void and weak besides, is surely hard to unload, no need to doubt it; Pressure stirring and the Vermilion Bird clamouring bring lawsuits, burdens and quarrels, and entanglement with no peace.

## 《易林补遗·变产求财章第一百十一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2040 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 111 - Selling property for money", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄动财安难脱卸， | 兄弟动、财安静，难脱手； | Peer moving and Wealth still, it is hard to unload; |
> | 鬼加雀动讼非招。 | 官鬼加朱雀发动，招来官司是非。 | Pressure with the Vermilion Bird moving, and lawsuits and quarrels are invited. |
