# 借本钱经营：财爻旺相不空，待日辰生合财爻的日子到手；六冲一场空

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 借本钱经营：要靠财爻旺相、不落空，或者生世、克世，一定能借到、没有阻隔，但要等日辰生合财爻的那一天才到手；六冲的卦万事不成，不要指望。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Borrowing capital to run a business: Wealth must be strong and not void, or generating or controlling the Self; then it is sure to be had without obstruction, but it comes to hand only on the day the Day generates or combines with the Wealth line; in a Six-Clash hexagram nothing succeeds, so do not hope.
