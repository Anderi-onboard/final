# 世被应伤，遭他陷害；财逢兄克，虑彼相瞒

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 投行交易：应伤世遭陷害，应克世账目不清；兄克财已入圈套、防隐瞒；兄动货难脱卸；子孙兴、财旺，交易顺利、利息丰厚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Trading through a broker: the Other hurting the Self line, you will be framed; the Other controlling the Self, the accounts will never be clear; Peer controlling Wealth, you are already in the trap and should beware of concealment; Peer moving, the goods are hard to sell; Output stirring and Wealth strong, the deal goes well and the interest is rich.
