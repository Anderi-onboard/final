# 兄弟化财须防圈套；妻财化兄弟折本伤财；子孙化财、财化子孙利多；青龙水上来登山涉海饶利

六爻 · 求财 · 动变 · 出处 [[阐奥歌章]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟化成财，虽有财，要忌落入别人的圈套；折本伤财，只因妻财化成兄弟；子孙化成财，利润宜多，财化成子孙更不是小利；青龙落在水上，登山涉海多得利润。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer turning into Wealth, though there is money, take care not to fall into another's trap; capital and money are lost only because Wealth turned into Peer; Output turning into Wealth brings large profit, and Wealth turning into Output is better still; with the Azure Dragon on Water, climbing mountains and crossing seas brings much profit.

## 《阐奥歌章·求财买卖章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3853 行 · Chan'ao Gezhang (Songs Expounding the Mysteries), "Verse on seeking wealth and trade", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟化财虽有财， | 兄弟化成财，虽然有财， | Peer turning into Wealth, though there is money, |
> | 切须忌落人圈圆。 | 千万要忌讳落入别人的圈套； | take great care not to fall into another's trap; |
> | 折本伤财为甚么？ | 折本伤财是为什么？ | why are capital and money lost? |
> | 只为妻财化兄弟。 | 只因为妻财化成了兄弟； | only because Wealth turned into Peer; |
> | 子孙化财宜利多， | 子孙化成财，利润宜多； | Output turning into Wealth, profit should be large; |
> | 财化子孙更非细。 | 财化成子孙，更不是小利； | Wealth turning into Output is better still; |
> | 却又青龙水上来， | 又有青龙在水上来， | and if the Azure Dragon comes upon Water, |
> | 登山涉海多饶利。 | 登山渡海多得利润。 | climbing mountains and crossing seas brings much profit. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「圈圆」是「圈圈」（圈套）的另一种写法，周易尚占作「圈圚」。「水上来」译作落在水爻上，是照字面取的读法。
>
> *Note:* 'Quan yuan' is another way of writing the word for a trap; the Zhouyi Shangzhan has yet another variant. 'Comes upon Water' is read literally as falling on a Water line.
