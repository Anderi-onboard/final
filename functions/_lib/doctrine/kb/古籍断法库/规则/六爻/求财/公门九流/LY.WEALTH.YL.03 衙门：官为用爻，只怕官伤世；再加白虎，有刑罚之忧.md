# 衙门：官为用爻，只怕官伤世；再加白虎，有刑罚之忧

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 在衙门当差求财：官爻是用爻，旺相为好，只怕官爻伤世；若再加白虎，一定有刑罚之忧。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Earning money from a post in a government office: Pressure is the Subject line and should be strong; the only fear is Pressure hurting the Self, and with the White Tiger added there is surely the worry of punishment.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1938 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 衙门但怕官伤世， | 衙门里当差，只怕官爻伤世； | In a government office, the only fear is Pressure hurting the Self; |
