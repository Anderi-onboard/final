# 世克动财若赶沙场之马，财生静世如逢涸泽之鱼

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财来生、克、合世爻或持世：财来就我，伸手可得；世爻去克发动的财：我去追财，难得（即使日辰合财也不得）；世生动财而日辰合住，或许可得。《断易天机》本：财在内卦动易，在外卦动难。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth generating, controlling or combining with the Self line, or sitting on it: the money comes to me, reach out and take it. The Self line controlling a moving Wealth: I chase the money and do not catch it (even if the Day combines with it). The Self line generating a moving Wealth that the Day holds by combination: it may be had. The Duanyi Tianji text: Wealth moving in the inner trigram is easy, in the outer hard.

## 《天玄赋·求财章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4580 行 · Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "Seeking wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世克动财，若赶沙场之马； | 世爻去克发动的财，像在沙场上追马； | The Self line controlling a moving Wealth: like chasing a horse across the sands; |
> | 财生静世，如逢涸泽之鱼。 | 财来生安静的世爻，像在干涸的水塘里捉鱼。 | Wealth generating a still Self line: like catching fish in a dried-up pond. |

^q1

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4581 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 大凡求财，财来生世克世则易。 | 大凡求财，财来生世、克世就容易。 | In seeking money, Wealth generating or controlling the Self line makes it easy. |
> | 财来逐我，如涸泽取鱼，伸手便得。 | 财来追我，像在干塘里取鱼，伸手就得。 | The money chases me, like taking fish from a dry pond: reach out and it is yours. |
> | 若世克动财，是我去逐利，如沙丘劣马，愈赶愈奔，终难上手。 | 如果世爻克发动的财，是我去追利，像沙丘上的劣马，越赶跑得越远，终究抓不到手。 | If the Self line controls a moving Wealth, I am the one chasing profit, like an unruly horse on the dunes: the more you chase, the farther it runs, and you never catch it. |

^q2

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1236 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 大凡求财，财在内动易求，外动难求。 | 大凡求财，财在内卦动容易求，在外卦动难求。 | In seeking money, Wealth moving in the inner trigram is easy to get, in the outer hard. |
> | 财来生世、克世、合世、持世则易，是财来逐我，如涸泽取鱼，伸手便得。 | 财来生世、克世、合世、持世就容易，是财来追我，像在干塘里取鱼，伸手就得。 | Wealth generating, controlling, combining with or sitting on the Self line is easy: the money chases me, like taking fish from a dry pond. |
> | 若世生动财，得日辰合住，财庶几可以得。 | 如果世爻去生发动的财，有日辰把财合住，财大概还能得到。 | If the Self line generates a moving Wealth and the Day combines with and holds it, the money may still be had. |
> | 若世克动财，是我去逐利，纵与日辰相合，亦不可得。 | 如果世爻克发动的财，是我去追利，即使（财）和日辰相合，也得不到。 | If the Self line controls a moving Wealth, I am chasing profit; even if the Day combines with it, it cannot be had. |
> | 倘世爻无杀，财在内动稍可，若在外动，则如沙丘劣马，愈赶愈奔，终难上手。 | 如果世爻上没有凶煞，财在内卦动还稍可；如果在外卦动，就像沙丘上的劣马，越赶越跑，终究抓不到手。 | If the Self line carries no killing star, Wealth moving in the inner trigram is just possible; moving in the outer, it is the unruly horse on the dunes that runs the harder you chase. |

^q3
