# 抽丰：喜财官旺；官旺、官生、官合，对方慷慨厚赠，再有财动助鬼，称心如意

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 向人求资助（抽丰）：以官爻为用神，只喜官旺相、被生、被合，对方一定慷慨厚赠；再有财发动来帮官鬼，就称心如意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Soliciting gifts (choufeng): Pressure is the Subject; all that is wanted is Pressure strong, generated and combined, and then he will give generously; if Wealth also moves to help Pressure, the heart is content.

## 《易林补遗·求财觅利章第一百四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1944 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 104 - Seeking wealth and profit", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 抽丰却喜财官旺， | 抽丰，却喜财和官都旺； | For soliciting gifts, it is a joy if Wealth and Pressure are both strong; |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1945 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 抽丰者，晋眉侯门餐食伊利， | 抽丰的人，去到侯门大户那里，靠他们的好处吃饭（「晋眉」二字不明）， | Those who solicit gifts go to the gates of the great and eat off their bounty ('jin mei' here is unclear), |
> | 以官为用， | 以官为用神， | with Pressure as the Subject; |
> | 惟喜官旺官生官合，彼必慨然厚赠， | 只喜官旺、官生、官合，那人一定慷慨厚赠， | all that is wanted is Pressure strong, Pressure generated, Pressure combined, and he will surely give generously; |
> | 又得财动助鬼，则积心遂意。 | 又有财发动来帮官鬼，就称心如意了。 | and if Wealth moves to help Pressure as well, the heart is content. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「官旺官生官合」读作官爻旺相、又被生、被合（或者是官去生世、合世，原文没有说明宾语），这里照字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Pressure strong, generated, combined' is translated literally; the source does not say whether Pressure is being generated and combined or is generating and combining with the Self.
