# 有意还财否：世应相生、外生内卦、子孙财爻动生世，他有还意；相克又无财，再去也白去

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 对方有没有还钱的意思：世应相生、外卦生内卦、子孙和财爻发动生世，他心里是想还的；世应相克又没有财，再去讨也白讨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether the other side means to repay: Self and Other generating each other, the outer trigram generating the inner, or Output and the Wealth line moving to generate the Self, all show that he means to pay; Self and Other controlling each other with no Wealth, to go again is in vain.

## 《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1262 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 世应相生有意还， | 世应相生，对方有还钱的意思， | Self and Other generating each other: he has the will to repay, |
> | 虽道无财常准拟， | 嘴上说没有钱，其实常在筹划； | and though he says there is no money, he is often making ready; |
> | 外生内卦有还心， | 外卦生内卦，有还钱的心； | the outer trigram generating the inner trigram: a heart to repay; |
> | 内外比和终有益（2）。 | 内外卦比和，终究有好处。 | inner and outer trigrams in harmony: in the end there is benefit. |
> | 子与财爻动生世， | 子孙和财爻发动生世， | Output and the Wealth line moving to generate the Self: |
> | 他心自来还有意（3）， | 他自己心里本来就有还的意思； | in his own heart he already has the will to repay; |
> | 相生无财意欲还， | 世应相生而卦里没有财，仍是想还； | Self and Other generating each other with no Wealth, he still wants to repay; |
> | 相克无财徒再诣（4）。 | 世应相克而卦里没有财，再去也是白去。 | Self and Other controlling each other with no Wealth, to go again is in vain. |
