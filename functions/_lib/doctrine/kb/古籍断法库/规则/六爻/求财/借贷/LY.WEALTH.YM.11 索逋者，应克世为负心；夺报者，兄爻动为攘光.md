# 索逋者，应克世为负心；夺报者，兄爻动为攘光

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 追债的，应爻克世是对方负心；夺取报酬的，兄爻发动是被抢夺一空。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For collecting debts, the Other controlling the Self means the debtor is faithless; for seizing a reward, the Peer line moving means being stripped bare.

> [!note] 按
> 「夺报」「攘光」的确切含义书里没有解释，这里照字面译作夺取报酬、抢夺一空，不确定。
>
> *Note:* The exact senses of 'seizing a reward' and 'stripped bare' are not explained in the book and are translated by their words, without certainty.
