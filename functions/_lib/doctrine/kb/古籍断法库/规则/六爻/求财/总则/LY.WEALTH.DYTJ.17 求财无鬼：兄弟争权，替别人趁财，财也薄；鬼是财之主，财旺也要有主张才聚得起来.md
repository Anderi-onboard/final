# 求财无鬼：兄弟争权，替别人趁财，财也薄；鬼是财之主，财旺也要有主张才聚得起来

六爻 · 求财 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求财而卦里没有鬼：兄弟一定来争权，等于在别人手下赚钱，钱也少；鬼是财的主人，财再旺也要有人作主才聚得起来，没有鬼就没有主，一定破耗多端、钱聚不起来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking wealth with no Pressure in the hexagram: the Peer lines will surely contend for control, which means earning under another's hand, and the money is thin; Pressure is the master of Wealth, and however thriving Wealth is it needs someone in charge before it can gather, so with no Pressure there is no master, and there will surely be many kinds of waste and the funds will not gather.
