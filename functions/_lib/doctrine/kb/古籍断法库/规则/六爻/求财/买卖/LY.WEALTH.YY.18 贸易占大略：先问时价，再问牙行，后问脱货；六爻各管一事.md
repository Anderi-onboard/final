# 贸易占大略：先问时价，再问牙行，后问脱货；六爻各管一事

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买卖要问三件事：现在的价钱是贵是贱、以后是涨是落；牙行（中间人）是好是坏；货好不好脱手。六爻各管一事（鬼谷分爻）：上爻店舍，五爻道路，四爻舟车马，三爻行李，二爻伴侣，初爻己身。哪一爻旺相、带财福龙德贵禄、又扶着卦身和世爻，那件事就顺；哪一爻带鬼、伏鬼、化鬼来刑冲克害卦身和世爻，或落在空死墓绝胎、被四直和动爻冲伤，疏漏就出在那件事上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A trade question asks three things: is the price now dear or cheap, and will it later rise or fall; is the broker honest or crooked; and will the goods sell easily. The six lines each govern one matter (Guigu's assignment): top, the lodging; fifth, the road; fourth, carts, boats and horses; third, baggage; second, companions; first, oneself. A line that is strong, carries Wealth, Output, the Azure Dragon, Virtue, Noble or Emolument, and supports the Body and Self lines means that matter goes well; a line that holds, hides or turns into Pressure and punishes, clashes with, controls or harms the Body and Self lines, or falls in void, death, tomb, extinction or the womb stage, or is clashed and hurt by the four rulers and moving lines, is where things go wrong.

> [!note] 按
> 鬼谷分爻的表原文只列了名目，没写各是哪一爻；从上到下的读法，本书自己有旁证：捕鱼占里《管公口诀》写明「初爻船主，二爻伴侣，三爻行李雨具，四爻网，五爻道路，六爻渔所」。「舟车马行李」在原书里连着排、中间没有断开，这里读作四爻舟车马、三爻行李。
>
> *Note:* The source lists the items without naming their lines. Reading them from the top down is supported by the same book's fishing chapter, where Guan Gong's rhymes say: first line the boat's master, second companions, third baggage and rain gear, fourth the net, fifth the road, sixth the fishing ground. Here the source runs 'carts, boats and horses' and 'baggage' together with no break; they are read as the fourth and third lines.
