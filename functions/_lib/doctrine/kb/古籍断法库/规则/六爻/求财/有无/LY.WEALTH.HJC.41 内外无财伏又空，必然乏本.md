# 内外无财伏又空，必然乏本

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻（内外卦）无财，本宫财爻又伏在空亡之地：缺本钱，勉强经营也无利；若动爻化出财来，有小利。野鹤说屡试屡验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* No Wealth among the six lines, and the home palace's Wealth line hidden in a void place: no capital, and forcing a venture brings no profit; if a moving line turns into Wealth, a small profit. Yehe says this has proved true again and again.

## 《黄金策·求财》原文

> | 内外无财伏又空， | 内外卦都没有财，伏着的财又落空， | No Wealth in either trigram, and the hidden Wealth void as well: |
