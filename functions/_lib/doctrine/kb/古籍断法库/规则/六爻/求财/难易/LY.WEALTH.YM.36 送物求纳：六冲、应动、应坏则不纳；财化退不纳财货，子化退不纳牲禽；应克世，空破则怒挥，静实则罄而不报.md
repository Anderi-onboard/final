# 送物求纳：六冲、应动、应坏则不纳；财化退不纳财货，子化退不纳牲禽；应克世，空破则怒挥，静实则罄而不报

六爻 · 求财 · 难易 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 送东西求人接受：六冲、应动、应坏，就不接受；财化退，就不收钱财货物；子孙化退，就不收牲畜禽鸟；如果应克世，应空破，对方发怒挥手赶走，应安静充实，则收下一空而不回报。注：父母化退不受书信文书，官鬼化退不受图像，兄弟化退不受成双成对的东西。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Sending a gift and asking it be accepted: a Six-Clash hexagram, the Other moving or the Other spoiled means it will not be accepted; Wealth changing into a retreating spirit means money and goods are not taken; Output changing into a retreating spirit means livestock and birds are not taken; if the Other controls the Self, with the Other Void or broken he angrily waves it away, and if quiet and solid he takes it all and gives nothing back. The note adds: Resource changing into a retreating spirit does not accept letters and documents, Pressure does not accept pictures, and Peer does not accept things in pairs.
