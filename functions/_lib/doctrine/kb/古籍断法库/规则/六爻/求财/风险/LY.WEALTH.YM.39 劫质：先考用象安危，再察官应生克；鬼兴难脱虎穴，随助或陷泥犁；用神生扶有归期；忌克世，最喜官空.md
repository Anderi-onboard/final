# 劫质：先考用象安危，再察官应生克；鬼兴难脱虎穴，随助或陷泥犁；用神生扶有归期；忌克世，最喜官空

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 被劫持押住的质：又和平常交往不同，先考用象的安危，后察官和应的生克；鬼兴旺就难脱虎穴，随墓、助伤就可能陷入地狱；所以人和物的存亡看用神就能知道，回来的日子，用神有生扶就有期。大抵质押的情理兼有交与的意思，所以忌讳克世；被劫持的形势像遭难，最喜官空。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a pledge held by force, it differs from an ordinary redemption: first examine the image of the Subject for safety or danger, then examine how Pressure and the Other generate or control; Pressure thriving makes it hard to escape the tiger's den, and following into the tomb or helping to harm may drop one into hell; so whether people and things survive can be seen from the Subject, and the day of return is fixed when the Subject is generated and supported. In general a pledge involves both handing over and receiving, so the Self being controlled is avoided; being held by force is like meeting misfortune, and Pressure Void is most welcome.
