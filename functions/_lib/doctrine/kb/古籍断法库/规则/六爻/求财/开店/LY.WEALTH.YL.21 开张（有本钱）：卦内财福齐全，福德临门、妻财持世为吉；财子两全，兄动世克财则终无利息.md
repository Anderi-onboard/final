# 开张（有本钱）：卦内财福齐全，福德临门、妻财持世为吉；财子两全，兄动世克财则终无利息

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有本钱开张：卦里要财和福都齐全，福德临门则顾客盈门，妻财持世则利润绵绵；以财为用神，财子两全，财旺则财源无穷，子孙动则顾客不断；不宜兄弟动又世克财，那样即使勤谨也终无利息。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Opening a shop with capital: the hexagram needs Wealth and Output both complete; Output at the gate fills the shop with customers and Wealth on the Self brings unbroken profit. Wealth is the Subject and Wealth and Output must both be there: Wealth thriving, its source is endless; Output moving, customers keep coming; but Peer moving while the Self controls Wealth is bad, and even a diligent person ends with no interest. With capital, Wealth and Pressure each want to be still.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1958 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 开张店业亦有二端： | 开张店铺也有两种： | Opening a shop has two cases: |
> | 将本求财者，以财为用， | 有本钱求财的，以财为用神， | with capital, Wealth is the Subject; |
> | 须要财子两全，财兴则财源炽盛无穷。 | 要财和子孙两样齐全，财兴旺，财源就旺盛无穷； | Wealth and Output must both be present; when Wealth thrives its source flows without end; |
> | 子动则主雇络纭不绝， | 子孙动，顾客络绎不绝； | when Output moves, customers keep coming; |
> | 但不宜兄动又值世克财者，纵是勤谨之人，终无利息。 | 只是不宜兄弟动，又碰上世克财，即使是勤勤恳恳的人，最终也没有利息。 | but it is bad if Peer moves and the Self also controls Wealth: even a diligent person ends with no interest; |
> | 故经云：世克动财若越沙场之马。 | 所以经上说：世克发动的财，好比要越过沙场的马。 | so the classic says: 'the Self controlling a moving Wealth is like a horse crossing a battlefield of sand.' |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 《天玄赋》里同一句作「世克动财若赶沙场之马」，这里是「越」，一个字之差。
>
> *Note:* The Tianxuan Fu has the same line as 'like chasing a horse across a battlefield of sand'; here the verb is 'cross' - one character apart.
