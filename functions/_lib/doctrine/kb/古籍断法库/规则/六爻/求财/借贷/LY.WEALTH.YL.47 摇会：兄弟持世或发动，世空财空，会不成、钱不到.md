# 摇会：兄弟持世或发动，世空财空，会不成、钱不到

六爻 · 求财 · 借贷 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 摇会：兄弟是阻挡耗财的神，不宜持世，发动也是卦中大忌；世是主、财是用爻，不可落空，遇到这样，会成不了、钱得不到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A rotating savings society: Peer is the spirit that obstructs and consumes and should not sit on the Self, and moving is also a great taboo; the Self is the master and Wealth the Subject line and neither may be void; with these, the society does not form and no money is had.

## 《易林补遗·摇会得失章第一百十》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2028 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 110 - Gains and losses in a rotating savings society", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄爻持世兄爻动， | 兄爻持世，或兄爻发动， | Peer on the Self, or Peer moving, |
> | 世或空亡财或空。 | 世落空亡，或财落空。 | the Self void, or Wealth void. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 2029 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟者，乃阻耗之神，岂宜持世？ | 兄弟是阻挡耗财的神，怎么宜于持世？ | Peer is the spirit that obstructs and consumes; how could it suit sitting on the Self? |
> | 又或发动卦中大忌。 | 又或者发动，是卦中大忌； | and moving is a great taboo in the hexagram; |
> | 世为主者，财为用爻，安可落空？ | 世是主，财是用爻，怎么可以落空？ | the Self is the master and Wealth the Subject line; how could they be void? |
> | 爻中值此定是会不财不得。 | 爻中遇到这样，一定是会成不了、钱得不到。 | if the lines show this, the society is sure not to form and the money not to be had. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 最后一句「定是会不财不得」字序不顺，应是「会不（成）、财不得」，这里照此读。
>
> *Note:* The last sentence is garbled in word order and is read as 'the society does not (form) and the money is not had'.
