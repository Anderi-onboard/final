# 周公断牛马歌：占牛马怕泰卦倒装，夬强，益怕刀，离遭屠，坎入镬，噬嗑颐需咸入鼎，坤艮分散；刀砧分上下，无杀始昌

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占牛马怕得泰卦，坤在上乾在下是倒装；夬卦力强不畏热；益卦怕刀伤；离卦遭屠宰；坎卦重爻入镬汤；噬嗑、颐、需、咸入鼎；坤、艮怕被分散；再看刀砧上下，没有各种煞才昌盛。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining oxen and horses, fear getting Tai, with Kun above and Qian below, an upside-down placing; Guai is strong and does not fear heat; Yi fears the knife; Li means slaughter; Kan with a doubled line goes into the cauldron; Shi He, Yi, Xu and Xian go into the tripod; fear meeting Kun and Gen and being split up; then tell the Knife and Block above and below, and only with none of the baleful stars does it prosper.

> [!note] 按
> 原文里的（1）（2）（3）和一处误排的「（（2）」是整理本给今人注文编的号，那些注文本库不引。「坤腹乾头」是说泰卦是坤上乾下。「离为虚市」的「虚市」字义不明，这里译作「空市」。
>
> *Note:* The (1), (2) and (3), and a stray '((2)', in the Chinese are numbers the transcription gave to modern notes, which this library does not cite. 'Kun belly, Qian head' says that in Tai Kun is above and Qian below. The words 'xu shi' are unclear and are rendered 'an empty market'.
