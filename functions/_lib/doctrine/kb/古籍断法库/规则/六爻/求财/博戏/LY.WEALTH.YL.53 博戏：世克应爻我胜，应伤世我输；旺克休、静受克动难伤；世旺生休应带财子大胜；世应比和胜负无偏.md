# 博戏：世克应爻我胜，应伤世我输；旺克休、静受克动难伤；世旺生休应带财子大胜；世应比和胜负无偏

六爻 · 求财 · 博戏 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 赌博输赢看世应：世克应爻，我全胜而归；应伤世，我大败，钱袋倒空。克的规矩：旺相能克休囚，休囚不能克旺相；安静的受克，发动的难伤，所以经上说静而休囚的要受克、兴旺的决难伤。世旺生休囚的应，再带财或子孙，大胜；不带，只得几文；应旺生衰弱的世，对方来追随我，没有不吉利的；世应比和相生，彼此谦让，胜负没有偏向。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Win or lose in gambling by the Self and the Other: the Self controlling the Other line, I win outright and come home content; the Other hurting the Self, I lose badly and my purse is emptied. The rules of control: the strong can control the resting and trapped, and the resting and trapped cannot control the strong; one that is still takes the control and one that moves is hard to hurt, so the classic says that still and resting takes the control and thriving cannot be hurt. A strong Self generating a resting Other and carrying Wealth or Output is a great win, without them only a few coins; a strong Other generating a weak Self, the other side comes to follow me and all is auspicious; Self and Other in harmony and generating each other, both yield and win and loss lean neither way.

> | 克有数端；旺相能克休囚，休囚不能克旺相。 | 克有几种情形：旺相的能克休囚的，休囚的不能克旺相的。 | Control comes in several kinds: the strong can control the resting and trapped, and the resting and trapped cannot control the strong. |
