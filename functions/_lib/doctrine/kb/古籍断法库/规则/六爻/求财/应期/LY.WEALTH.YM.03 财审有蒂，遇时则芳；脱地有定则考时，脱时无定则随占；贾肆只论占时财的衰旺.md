# 财审有蒂，遇时则芳；脱地有定则考时，脱时无定则随占；贾肆只论占时财的衰旺

六爻 · 求财 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财要看有没有根蒂，有蒂遇到时节就旺，财神没有根，遇到时节也平常；出手的地方定了，就考察时节；出手的时间不定，就按占卦当时的状态。注：比如开店铺这一行，只论占卦当时财的衰旺，不能拿日后的日子时辰作应验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth must be examined for whether it has a stem: with a stem it blooms when its season meets it, without a root it is ordinary even then; where the place of sale is fixed, examine the season; where the time of sale is not fixed, follow the state at the time of the divination. The note adds: for a business such as a shop, judge only the thriving or weakness of Wealth at the time of the divination and do not take a later day or hour as the date it comes true.
