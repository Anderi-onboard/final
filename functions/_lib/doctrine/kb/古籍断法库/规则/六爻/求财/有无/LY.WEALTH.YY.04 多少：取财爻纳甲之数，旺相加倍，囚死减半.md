# 多少：取财爻纳甲之数，旺相加倍，囚死减半

六爻 · 求财 · 有无 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 估数目：数大的用财爻纳甲数（甲己子午九、乙庚丑未八、丙辛寅申七、丁壬卯酉六、戊癸辰戌五、巳亥四），把卦中所有的财（两财、伏财、变财、四直之财、卦宫之财）加总，旺相加倍、休照数、囚死减半；数小的用财爻地支五行数（水一、火二、木三、金四、土五）随旺衰增减。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Estimating the sum: for a large one, use the stem-and-branch numbers of the Wealth line (Jia Ji Zi Wu nine, Yi Geng Chou Wei eight, Bing Xin Yin Shen seven, Ding Ren Mao You six, Wu Gui Chen Xu five, Si Hai four), add up every Wealth in the reading (two Wealth lines, hidden Wealth, changed Wealth, Wealth among the four rulers, the palace's own Wealth), doubling the strong, keeping the resting as they are, halving the trapped and dead; for a small one, use the element number of the Wealth branch (Water one, Fire two, Wood three, Metal four, Earth five), raised or lowered by strength.
