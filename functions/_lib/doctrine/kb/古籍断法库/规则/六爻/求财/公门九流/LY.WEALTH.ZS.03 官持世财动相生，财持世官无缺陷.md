# 官持世财动相生，财持世官无缺陷

六爻 · 求财 · 公门九流 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 谒贵求财：官鬼持世而财爻动来生它，或财持世而官鬼完好无损，再得日月生世生财（或世、财临日月），双方投缘，吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Calling on the great for money: Pressure on the Self line with a moving Wealth generating it, or Wealth on the Self line with Pressure sound, and the Day and Month generating the Self or Wealth (or the Self or Wealth line being the Day or Month branch): the two sides take to each other; good.

## 《增删卜易·谒贵求财章》原文
