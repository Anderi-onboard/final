# 合伙不嫌兄弟

六爻 · 求财 · 开店 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 合伙：世、应都是财爻最好；兄弟安静（或临卦身而世爻静）不嫌，因为合伙本要分财；兄弟发动则不宜。世应两动或落空：合不长久；相冲克：日后不和；化出兄官：多私心；世应生合而被刑冲克害：有人挑拨。野鹤：世、应临兄弟仍不吉，只有日月为财冲克世时反吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Partnership: best if both the Self and Other lines are Wealth; a still Peer (or Peer on the Body line with the Self line still) is no objection, since partners share the money anyway; a moving Peer is bad. Self and Other both moving or void: it will not last; clashing or controlling each other: they will fall out; turning into Peer or Pressure: self-interest; combining but punished, clashed or controlled by others: someone will sow discord. Yehe: Peer on the Self or Other line is still unlucky, except when the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 7851–7853 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占合伙买卖，若世应俱财爻，必然称意。 | 凡是占合伙买卖，如果世、应都是财爻，一定如意。 | In a partnership question, if both the Self and the Other lines are Wealth lines, it will go as wished. |
> | 兄临卦身，世爻安静不妨。 | 兄弟临卦身，只要世爻安静，也不要紧。 | Peer on the Body line does no harm as long as the Self line is still. |
> | 盖合必至分财，故也。 | 因为合伙本来就一定要分财。 | For a partnership means sharing the money anyway. |
> | 动则不宜。 | （兄弟）发动就不好。 | But a moving Peer is not good. |
> | 世应两动，必合不久，空亡亦然。 | 世、应都动，合伙一定不长久；（世应）落空亡也一样。 | If the Self and Other lines both move, the partnership will not last; the same if they are void. |
> | 相冲相克，后必不睦。 | 世应相冲相克，日后一定不和。 | If they clash or control each other, the partners will fall out later. |
> | 化出兄官，必多私心。 | （世应）化出兄弟、官鬼，一定多私心。 | If they turn into Peer or Pressure, there will be much self-interest. |
> | 世应生合，而被动爻日辰刑冲克害者，必有挑唆破说也。 | 世应相生相合，却被动爻或日辰刑冲克害，一定有人挑拨离间。 | If Self and Other generate or combine with each other but a moving line or the Day punishes, clashes, controls or harms them, someone will sow discord between them. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2709 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占合伙买卖，若世应俱财必然称意。 | 凡占合伙买卖，世、应都是财，一定如意。 | In a partnership question, Wealth on both Self and Other means it goes as wished. |
> | 兄临卦身必主分财故也。 | 兄弟临卦身，本来就主分财，所以不嫌。 | Peer on the Body line simply means sharing the money, which is why it is no objection. |
> | 静者无嫌，动则不宜。 | 兄弟安静不嫌，发动就不好。 | Still, it is no objection; moving, it is not good. |

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11307 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 合伙不嫌兄弟，乏本内外无财。 | 合伙不嫌有兄弟；缺本钱的，卦里内外都没有财。 | A partnership does not mind Peer; lacking capital shows as no Wealth inside or out. |
> | 世应临兄不吉，兄爻发动亦不吉。 | 世、应临兄弟不吉，兄弟发动也不吉。 | Yet Peer on the Self or Other line is unlucky, and so is a moving Peer line. |
> | 倘世临兄弟，日月作财星冲克世者，反为吉也。 | 如果世爻临兄弟，而日辰月建是财、来冲克世爻，反而是吉。 | But if the Self line carries Peer and the Day or Month is Wealth and clashes with or controls the Self line, it becomes lucky after all. |

^q4

> [!warning] 打架
> 兄弟到底嫌不嫌：经文说合伙不嫌兄弟；旧注、王洪绪说兄弟静可以、动不宜；野鹤引了经文，却接着说世、应临兄弟不吉、兄弟动也不吉，只有日月为财冲克世爻时反吉。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Is Peer really no objection? The classic says so for partnerships; the old commentary and Wang Hongxu allow a still Peer but not a moving one; Yehe quotes the classic but then says Peer on the Self or Other line is unlucky and a moving Peer too, the only exception being a Wealth Day or Month clashing with or controlling the Self line.
