# 停榻者，喜财安而鬼静

六爻 · 求财 · 买卖 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 囤货：财爻不宜动、不宜空，不宜被动爻日辰克劫；财坐胎、养、长生，日后得利。兄官交变或同动防盗；兄弟独发多耗折；水爻父母（王洪绪：父化官鬼）刑克世爻，货被雨水淹腐；财化死墓空绝，日后价贱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Holding goods in store: Wealth should neither move nor be void, nor be attacked by moving lines or the Day; Wealth at the Embryo, Nurture or Birth stage means profit later. Peer and Pressure turning into each other or moving together: beware of theft; Peer moving alone: waste and loss; a Water Resource line (Wang Hongxu: Resource turning into Pressure) punishing or controlling the Self line: the goods are spoiled by rain; Wealth turning into death, tomb, void or extinction: the price will fall.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 停榻者， | 囤货存放的人， | For goods held in store, |
> | 喜财安而鬼静； | 喜欢财爻安静、官鬼也安静； | it is best that Wealth rests and Pressure is still; |

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 积货不宜财动，亦不宜空亡，又不宜动爻日辰克劫。 | 囤积货物不宜财爻发动，也不宜财爻落空，又不宜被动爻、日辰克劫。 | Stored goods do not want the Wealth line moving, nor void, nor attacked by moving lines or the Day. |
> | 更得坐于胎养长生爻上，后必得利。 | 再加上财爻坐在胎、养、长生的位置，日后一定得利。 | If the Wealth line sits at the Embryo, Nurture or Birth stage, profit will come later. |

> | 水爻父母刑克世爻，主被雨水淹腐。 | 属水的父母爻刑克世爻，主货被雨水淹坏。 | A Water Resource line punishing or controlling the Self line: the goods will be spoiled by rain or flood. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2729 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 积货不宜财动，动恐有变；亦不宜空，空恐有更。 | 囤货不宜财动，动了怕有变故；也不宜空，空了怕有更改。 | Stored goods do not want Wealth moving, for fear of a change; nor void, for fear of an upset. |
> | 官鬼若动，兴灾作祸莫测； | 官鬼若发动，会兴起难以预料的灾祸； | If Pressure moves, it brings unforeseeable trouble; |
> | 如父母化官鬼刑克世爻，货被雨水污腐。 | 如果父母化官鬼来刑克世爻，货被雨水弄脏腐坏。 | if Resource turns into Pressure and punishes or controls the Self line, the goods will be soiled and rot in the rain. |
> | 故脱货者宜六爻安静，唯子孙喜动。 | 所以脱货的人宜六爻安静，只喜子孙发动。 | So for goods to be disposed of, all six lines should be still; only Output is welcome to move. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 王洪绪本经文作「停脱者」，注末又说「故脱货者宜六爻安静」，和下一句经文「脱货者宜财动而身兴」正相反；旧注本作「停榻者」（囤货）。按上下文，这一条讲的是囤货，王本的「脱」字疑有讹误。原文照录。
>
> *Note:* Wang Hongxu's edition reads 'holding and disposing' in the base text, and his note ends 'so for goods to be disposed of, all six lines should be still', which contradicts the very next line ('for goods to be sold off, Wealth should move'). The old-commentary edition reads 'goods held in store'. From the context this rule is about stockpiling, and 'dispose' in Wang's edition is probably a corruption. We quote it as it stands.
