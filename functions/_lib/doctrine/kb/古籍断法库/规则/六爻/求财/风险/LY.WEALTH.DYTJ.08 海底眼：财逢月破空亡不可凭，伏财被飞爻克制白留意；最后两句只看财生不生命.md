# 海底眼：财逢月破空亡不可凭，伏财被飞爻克制白留意；最后两句只看财生不生命

六爻 · 求财 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财遇月破、空亡，不可凭信；伏着的财被飞爻克制，白白留意；最后「争困撼钱财」几个字含义不明，只能读出：看财有没有生到命上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth meeting Month-break or Void cannot be relied on; a hidden Wealth controlled by its flying line is attended to in vain; the last two lines, with their unclear words about contending, being pressed and shaking money, can only be read as: look at whether Wealth generates the natal line.

## 《断易天机·占求财第十九》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 1294 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Chapter 19: Divining for wealth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 月破空亡未可凭， | 财逢月破、空亡，不能凭信； | Wealth meeting Month-break or Void cannot be relied on; |
> | 飞爻克制徒留意， | 伏着的财被飞爻克制，白白留意； | a hidden Wealth controlled by the flying line is attended to in vain; |
> | 若求争困撼钱财， | 如果是求争、困、撼一类的钱财， | if the money sought is of the sort called contending, pressed or shaken loose, |
> | 但看财生命者是。 | 只看财有没有生到命上就是了。 | look only at whether Wealth generates the natal line. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「争困撼钱财」四个字，「撼钱」在本书别处（六爻俱静类）和「赌斗」放在一起，可能指赌斗得来的钱；「争」「困」两字含义不明。这里照字译，不解释。「命」按本书「随官入墓」一节里「命随鬼入墓」的用法，指问卜人生年地支所在的爻。
>
> *Note:* The words about contending, being pressed and shaking loose money are unclear. 'Shaking money' stands beside 'gambling and contests' elsewhere in this book (the section on six quiet lines) and may mean money won in contests; the other two words are unclear. They are translated literally without explanation. 'The natal line' follows the sense of 'the natal line following Pressure into the tomb' elsewhere in this book: the line of the asker's birth-year branch.
