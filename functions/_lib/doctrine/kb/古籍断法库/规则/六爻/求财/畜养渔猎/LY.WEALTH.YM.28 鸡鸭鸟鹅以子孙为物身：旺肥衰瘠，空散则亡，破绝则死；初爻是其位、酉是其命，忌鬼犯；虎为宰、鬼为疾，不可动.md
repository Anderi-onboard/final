# 鸡鸭鸟鹅以子孙为物身：旺肥衰瘠，空散则亡，破绝则死；初爻是其位、酉是其命，忌鬼犯；虎为宰、鬼为疾，不可动

六爻 · 求财 · 畜养渔猎 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鸡、鸭、鸟、鹅这类，以子孙为它们的身：旺则肥，衰则瘦，空散则亡，破绝则死；初爻是它们的位，酉是它们的命，都忌鬼来犯；虎是它们的屠宰，鬼是它们的疾病，都不可发动。注：这是说畜禽的消长肥瘦；如果是贩鸡养鸭，求财就要以财神为用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Chickens, ducks, birds and geese take Output as their body: thriving they grow fat, weak they grow thin, Void or scattered they die out, broken or cut off they die; the first line is their place and You is their natal branch, and both dislike being offended by Pressure; the Tiger is their slaughterer and Pressure their illness, and neither may move. The note adds: this speaks of how creatures grow or shrink, fat or lean; if the business is hawking chickens and raising ducks for money, take the Wealth spirit as the Subject.
