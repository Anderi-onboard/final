# 乡试会试章·出现无情难遂青云之志：父与官星虽出现，若不生合世、不持世或世空破亦无成；生合世而又被日月动爻冲坏亦作无情

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻和官星虽然出现在卦中，如果不生合世爻、不持世，或者世爻空破，也不成；生合世爻而又被日月、动爻冲坏，也算无情。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Even if the Resource line and the official star appear in the hexagram, if they do not give life to or combine with the Self, or do not hold the Self, or the Self is Void or broken, it comes to nothing; if they give life to or combine with the Self but are then clashed and spoiled by the Day, the Month or a moving line, that too counts as being without feeling.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10141 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 出现无情，难遂青云之志。 | 出现却无情，难以实现青云直上的志向。 | Present but without feeling: hard to fulfil the ambition to rise to the clouds. |
> | 父与官星虽则出现，若不生合世爻，或不持世，或世爻空破，亦属无成。 | 父爻和官星虽然出现在卦中，如果不生合世爻，或者不持世，或者世爻空破，也属于不成。 | Even if the Resource line and the official star appear in the hexagram, if they do not give life to or combine with the Self, or do not hold the Self, or the Self is Void or broken, it still comes to nothing. |
> | 父与官星生合世爻，若又被日月动爻冲坏者，亦作无情之喻。 | 父爻和官星生合世爻，如果又被日月、动爻冲坏的，也算是无情的比喻。 | If the Resource line and the official star give life to and combine with the Self but are then clashed and spoiled by the Day, the Month or a moving line, that too counts as being without feeling. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「出现无情」是黄金策求名章的句子（「出现无情，难遂青云之志」），这里是野鹤自己的解释：爻在卦里出现，却和世爻没有生合的关系，就和你没有关系。
>
> *Note:* "Present but without feeling" is a line of the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a name; what is given here is the Old Man of Yehe's own explanation: a line that appears in the hexagram but has no relation of life or combination with the Self has nothing to do with you.
