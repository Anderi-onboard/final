# 乡试会试章·三合无冲聘登甲第：官、父、世爻合成父局或官局而不遇日月冲破定许联登；合成兄局得日月生扶官星亦旺亦许得意；忌合成子孙局；合成财局虽不美亦有验吉者（附例：艮之益占会试）

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官、父、世三爻合成父局或官局，没有日月冲破，一定能连登；合成兄局而得日月生扶、官星也旺，也能得意；最忌合成子孙局；合成财局虽然不好，也有应验为吉的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure, Resource and Self lines form a three-harmony frame of Resource or of Pressure and are not clashed or broken by the Day or the Month, one will surely rise in succession; a frame of Peer given life and support by the Day or the Month with the official star also strong may also succeed; a frame of Output is most to be feared; a frame of Wealth, though not good, has also proved auspicious in some cases.

> [!note] 按
> 「甲第」是榜上头等；「联登」是接连登科（乡试、会试连中）。「三合」指申子辰、亥卯未等三合成局，父局、官局、兄局、子孙局、财局是看合成的五行是哪一类六亲。例子里申日同卦里的子、辰会成申子辰水局，水是财，所以是财局。
>
> *Note:* The "first class" is the top tier on the list; "rise in succession" is passing one examination after another (provincial then metropolitan). A "three-harmony" is a group such as Shen-Zi-Chen or Hai-Mao-Wei, and a frame of Resource, Pressure, Peer, Output or Wealth is named by which relation the phase of the group is. In the example the Shen day joins the Zi and Chen lines of the hexagram into a Shen-Zi-Chen Water frame, and Water is Wealth, so it is a Wealth frame.
