# 求师·彼迁此请持世应而兴隆（世应俱动主有家延请，两爻俱空俱不成）；母值应而世生须知假馆（父临应上世动来生合，必馆他家而欲附学）；父在外而福合必是担囊（自占以世为徒、不看福爻，父兄占以子弟为徒、不看世爻；父在外卦他宫安静而子孙动去相会则游学他方）

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世、应都动，有人家来聘请；两爻都空，都不成。父爻临在应爻上、世爻动来生合它，老师在别人家设馆，自己想去附学。自己占以世爻为徒、不看福爻，父兄代占以子弟为徒、不看世爻；父爻在外卦又是别宫、安静，而子孙动去相会，必是到别处游学。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self and Other both move, some household will engage him; if both lines are Void, neither comes about. If the Resource line stands on the Other and the Self moves to give life to or combine with it, the teacher keeps his school at another's house and one wishes to attach oneself to it. When one divines for oneself the Self is the pupil and the Output line is not looked at; when a father or elder brother divines for him the son or younger brother is the pupil and the Self line is not looked at; if the Resource line is in the outer trigram, of another palace and still, and Output moves to meet it, he will surely go to study in another place.

> | 父兄来占，以子弟为徒，不看世爻。 | 如果是父兄来占，以子弟为徒弟，不看世爻。 | if a father or elder brother divines, the son or younger brother is the pupil, and the Self line is not looked at. |

^q6

> [!note] 按
> 「假馆」是借别人的馆舍；「担囊」是挑着行李出门游学。「福爻」是子孙爻（福神）。
>
> *Note:* "Borrowing a school" is using another man's school premises; "carrying one's bag" is setting out with one's luggage to study abroad. The "Output line" here is the line of blessing, Output.
