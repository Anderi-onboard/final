# 乡试会试章·日月为制煞之将军：官星、父母、世爻有一被动爻克制或化回头克，得日月冲制克神亦许得意；官父有一伏藏，得日月冲制飞神提出伏神亦许高发；有一不旺得日月生扶亦同

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官星、父爻、世爻里有一个被动爻克制，或变成回头克，只要日月冲制了那个克它的神，也许能得意，所以日月叫制服恶煞的将军；世爻旺相而官爻、父爻有一个伏藏不现，日月冲制飞神、提出伏神，也许高中；三者有一个不旺，得日月生扶，同此。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If one of the official star, the Resource line and the Self is controlled by a moving line or changes into a line that turns back to control it, one may still succeed provided the Day or the Month clashes and restrains the controlling spirit, which is why the Day and Month are called the generals who subdue baleful spirits; if the Self is thriving but one of the Pressure and Resource lines lies hidden, and the Day or the Month clashes and restrains the flying line and brings the hidden line out, one may pass high; if one of the three is not strong but the Day or the Month gives it life and support, the same applies.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10109 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日月为制煞之将军。 | 日月是制服凶煞的将军。 | The Day and the Month are the generals who subdue baleful spirits. |
> | 官星、父母、世爻，有一而被动爻克制，或化回头之克，若得日月冲制克神，亦许得意，故谓之制恶煞之将军。 | 官星、父母、世爻，有一个被动爻克制，或者变成回头克，如果得日月来冲制那个克它的神，也许能得意，所以叫做制服恶煞的将军。 | If one of the official star, the Resource line and the Self is controlled by a moving line or changes into a line that turns back to control it, but the Day or the Month clashes and restrains the controlling spirit, one may still succeed, which is why they are called the generals who subdue baleful spirits. |
> | 又如世爻旺相，而官父两爻若有一而伏藏不现，倘得日月冲制飞神，提出伏神者，亦许高发。 | 又如世爻旺相，而官爻、父爻两个里有一个伏藏不现，如果日月冲制飞神、把伏神提出来，也许能高中。 | Again, if the Self is thriving but one of the Pressure and Resource lines lies hidden and does not appear, and the Day or the Month clashes and restrains the flying line and brings the hidden line out, one may pass high. |
> | 又如官、父、世爻，有一而不旺者，若得日月生扶，亦同此例。 | 又如官爻、父爻、世爻有一个不旺的，如果得日月生扶，也是同样的道理。 | Again, if one of the Pressure, Resource and Self lines is not strong but the Day or the Month gives it life and support, the same applies. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「制煞」的「煞」是克用神的爻（这里是克官星、父爻、世爻的那个爻）；「提出伏神」是日月冲开飞神，让伏神露出来。
>
> *Note:* The "baleful spirit" is the line that controls the Subject (here the one that controls the official star, the Resource line or the Self); "bringing the hidden line out" is the Day or Month clashing the flying line open so the hidden line shows.
