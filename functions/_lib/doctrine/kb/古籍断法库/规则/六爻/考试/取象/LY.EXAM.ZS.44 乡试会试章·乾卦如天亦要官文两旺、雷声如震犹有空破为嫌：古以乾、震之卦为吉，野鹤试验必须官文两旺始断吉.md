# 乡试会试章·乾卦如天亦要官文两旺、雷声如震犹有空破为嫌：古以乾、震之卦为吉，野鹤试验必须官文两旺始断吉

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 古人把乾卦、震卦当作吉卦，野鹤试验：乾卦也要官星、文章两旺，震卦怕有空破，一定要官文两旺才断吉，否则不算吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ancients took the hexagrams Qian and Zhen as auspicious by themselves; the Old Man of Yehe found by trial that even Qian needs the official star and the writing both strong, and Zhen fears Void or break, and that only if the official star and the writing are both strong may one judge it auspicious, otherwise it is not.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10289 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 乾卦如天，亦要官文两旺； | 乾卦象天，也要官星、文章两旺； | Even the hexagram Qian, like Heaven, still needs the official star and the writing both to be strong; |
> | 雷声如震，犹有空破为嫌。 | 雷声像震卦，还是怕有空、破。 | like the thunder of Zhen, it still has the dislike of Void or break. |
> | 古以乾、震之卦，即以为吉，余试务必官文两旺，始以吉断，否则未为吉也。 | 古人把乾卦、震卦就当作吉，我试过，一定要官星、文章两旺，才断为吉，否则不算吉。 | The ancients took the hexagrams Qian and Zhen as auspicious by themselves; in my trials the official star and the writing must both be strong before I judge it auspicious, otherwise it is not. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「乾卦如天」「雷声如震」是黄金策求名章一类的旧说（乾为天，震为雷，以卦象论吉）；野鹤的意思是卦象不能代替官、父两爻的旺衰。
>
> *Note:* "Qian like Heaven" and "thunder like Zhen" are old sayings of the kind found in the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a name (Qian is Heaven and Zhen is Thunder, judged auspicious by the image of the hexagram); the Old Man of Yehe's point is that the image of the hexagram cannot take the place of the strength of the official star and Resource lines.
