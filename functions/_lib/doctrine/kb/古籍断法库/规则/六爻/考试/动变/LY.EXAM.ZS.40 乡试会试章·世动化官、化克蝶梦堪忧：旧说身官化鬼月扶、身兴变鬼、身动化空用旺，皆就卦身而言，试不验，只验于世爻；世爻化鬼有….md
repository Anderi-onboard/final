# 乡试会试章·世动化官、化克蝶梦堪忧：旧说身官化鬼月扶、身兴变鬼、身动化空用旺，皆就卦身而言，试不验，只验于世爻；世爻化鬼有两验——父母旺相、世化官星不回头克世为变官今科即中，世休囚被克化官星为化鬼不中且见危灾，化出鬼爻克世更凶（觉子举例）

六爻 · 考试 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻动变官星要分两种：卦中父母爻旺相，世爻化出官星而不回头克世，是变官，这一科就中；世爻休囚被克，动而化出官星，是化鬼，不但不中还有危灾，化出的鬼爻克世的更凶。旧说「身官化鬼月扶」「身兴变鬼」「身动化空用旺」都是就卦身说的，野鹤试过不灵，只在世爻上灵验。觉子的例子：世爻变出子水官星得月建生合却没连捷，因为变出的鬼回头克世，后来客死途中；世爻变空应在填实空的那年。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Self line that moves into the official star is of two kinds: if the Resource line is thriving in the hexagram and the Self changes into the official star without turning back to control the Self, it is a change into office and one passes in this very examination; if the Self is resting and trapped and controlled and moves to change into the official star, it is a change into a ghost, and not only does one fail but danger and calamity appear, worse still if the Pressure line it changes into controls the Self. The old sayings, "the Body or official star changes into Pressure with the Month's support", "the Body flourishes and changes into Pressure" and "the Body moves into Void while the Subject is strong", all speak of the hexagram Body, and when the Old Man of Yehe tried them they were not accurate, holding true only of the Self line. Juezi's examples: a Self that changed into the Zi Water official star with the Month giving life and combining did not pass in succession, because the ghost it changed into turned back to control the Self, and he died on the road; a Self that changes into Void is answered in the year that fills the Void.

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10279 行 · Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 觉子曰：旧以世动化空用旺，豹变翻成蝶梦。 | 觉子说：旧说认为世爻动而变空、用神旺，豹变（的美事）反成一场梦。 | Juezi says: the old saying holds that if the Self moves and changes into Void while the Subject is strong, the leopard's change turns into a butterfly dream. |
> | 此卦世爻午火官星，化出丑土文章，官文两旺，因丑父旬空，午岁乡试入，丑年甲榜先登，此宁非世动化空耶。 | 这一卦世爻是午火官星，变出丑土文章，官星、文章两旺，因为丑土父爻旬空，午年入场乡试，丑年中了甲榜，这难道不是世动化空吗？ | In this hexagram the Self is Wu Fire the official star, and it changes into the Chou Earth writing, so the official star and the writing are both strong; because the Chou Earth Resource line is in the Void, he entered the provincial examination in a Wu year and topped the list in a Chou year; is this not the Self moving and changing into Void? |
> | 止见其占鳌头，而未见其成蝶梦，所以化空者，必应实空之年也。 | 只见他占了鳌头，没有见他成蝴蝶梦，所以变空的，必定应在填实空的那一年。 | I saw only that he took first place, not that it turned into a butterfly dream; so a change into Void is surely answered in the year that fills the Void. |

^q7

> [!note] 按
> 「身」在旧说里指卦身，野鹤试验的结论是不灵，只对世爻灵验（所以世爻化官星要分「变官」和「化鬼」）。「变官」是化出官星而回头生合世，是好事；「化鬼」是化出官鬼回头克世，是坏事。觉子的第二个例子里「午岁乡试入」一句原文末尾的「入」字没有宾语（整理本如此），译文按「入场」译。「蝴蝶梦」是白做一场梦，「蟾宫折桂」是登科。
>
> *Note:* In the old sayings the "Body" means the hexagram Body; the Old Man of Yehe found them inaccurate and true only of the Self line (so the Self changing into the official star is divided into a "change into office" and a "change into a ghost"). A "change into office" is a change into the official star that turns back to give life to or combine with the Self, which is good; a "change into a ghost" is a change into the Pressure line that turns back to control the Self, which is bad. In Juezi's second example the word "entered" at the end of "entered the provincial examination in a Wu year" has no object in the edited text, and the translation reads it as "entered the hall". A "butterfly dream" is a dream that comes to nothing, and "breaking a laurel in the Toad Palace" is passing the examination.
