# 延师章·月扶世爻日助生世则青出于蓝；动衰变旺先惰后勤、动旺变衰后怠；父化父殊经、化财多病、化鬼显贵之交、化兄贪财好利

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻旺相、父母持世或官星持世又得日月动爻生扶，学生必有长进、青出于蓝；父爻由衰变旺老师先惰后勤，由旺变衰老师后来懈怠；父化父教的经不同，化财多病，化官鬼是结交显贵的人，化兄弟是贪财好利的人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self is thriving, or Resource or the official star holds the Self with the Day, the Month and moving lines giving life and support, the pupil will make progress and surpass the master; a Resource line moving from weak to strong means the teacher is lazy first then diligent, and from strong to weak, slack later; Resource turning into Resource, he teaches a different classic; into Wealth, he is often ill; into Pressure, he keeps company with the eminent; into Peer, he is greedy for money and profit.

## 《增删卜易·延师章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9898 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on engaging a teacher", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父爻衰，而化生、化旺、化日月、化进神，皆主后勤； | 父爻衰，但变成长生、变旺、变成日月、变进神，都主老师后来勤勉； | If the Resource line is weak but changes into Birth, into strength, into the Day or the Month, or into an Advancing line, all indicate that the teacher becomes diligent later; |
> | 父爻旺，而化空破墓绝、化退神，皆主后怠。 | 父爻旺，却变空、变破、变墓、变绝、变退神，都主老师后来懈怠。 | if the Resource line is strong but changes into Void, break, tomb, extinction or a Retreating line, all indicate that he becomes slack later. |
> | 父化父而殊经，父化财而多病。 | 父爻变父爻，老师教的经和别人不同；父爻变财，老师多病。 | Resource turning into Resource: he teaches a different classic; Resource turning into Wealth: he is often ill. |
> | 父化鬼显贵之交； | 父爻变官鬼，是和显贵交往的人； | Resource turning into Pressure: he is a man who keeps company with the eminent; |
> | 父化兄贪财好利。 | 父爻变兄弟，是贪财好利的人。 | Resource turning into Peer: he is greedy for money and profit. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 原文「动旺变衰，后勤后怠」的「后勤」应是「先勤」之误（与前一句「先惰后勤」对仗，下面一条也说「父爻旺而化空破墓绝、化退神，皆主后怠」），译文按「先勤后怠」译。「殊经」是教的经和一般的不同，书里没有说是什么经。
>
> *Note:* In the line "moving from strong to weak, diligent later, slack later" the first "later" is a slip for "first" (it balances the line before, "lazy first, diligent later", and the next entry says that a strong Resource line changing into Void, break, tomb, extinction or a Retreating line indicates slackness later); the translation reads it as "first diligent, then slack". "A different classic" means the classic he teaches is not the usual one; the book does not say which.
