# 治经章·占习何经：金作春秋、木作毛诗、水作书经、火为礼记、土为易经，须指定一经祷告「我习此经何如」，再看卦象（觉子）

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占自己该学哪一部经，不能笼统地问：要把五行对五经（金春秋、木毛诗、水书经、火礼记、土易经），指定其中一部，向神祷告说「我学这部经怎么样」，再看得到的卦象决定可不可以。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining which classic one should take up, one must not ask in general: match the five phases to the five classics (Metal the Spring and Autumn Annals, Wood the Songs, Water the Documents, Fire the Rites, Earth the Changes), name one of them and pray to the spirits, saying "How will it go if I study this classic?", and then decide from the hexagram obtained whether it can be done.

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9820 行 · Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 觉子曰：占以金作春秋、木作毛诗、水作书经、火为礼记、土为易经，占者不可概问，须指定一经祈于神曰：“我习此经何如？” | 觉子说：占的时候，把金当作春秋，木当作毛诗，水当作书经，火当作礼记，土当作易经；占的人不能笼统地问，要指定一部经，向神祷告说：「我学这部经怎么样？」 | Juezi says: in divining, take Metal as the Spring and Autumn Annals, Wood as the Songs, Water as the Documents, Fire as the Rites and Earth as the Changes; the one divining must not ask in general, but name one classic and pray to the spirits, saying, "How will it go if I study this classic?" |
> | 后视所得之卦象而决其可否？ | 然后看所得的卦象，来决定可不可以？ | Then look at the hexagram obtained and decide whether it can be done. |
> | 始无错误。 | 这样才不会出错。 | Only so will there be no mistake. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 五经在科举里要选一部「本经」专治。这一条是说：占卦时就要点名问哪一部，不要问「我学经怎么样」。原文第二句句末是问号，照录；意思是「看卦象来决定可不可以」。后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* Under the examination system one chose one of the five classics as one's own to specialise in. This says that in divining one should name the classic in the question and not ask "how will studying a classic go". The second sentence ends in a question mark in the text, which is kept; it means "decide from the hexagram whether it can be done". The [Qian's note] after the chapter is not quoted.
