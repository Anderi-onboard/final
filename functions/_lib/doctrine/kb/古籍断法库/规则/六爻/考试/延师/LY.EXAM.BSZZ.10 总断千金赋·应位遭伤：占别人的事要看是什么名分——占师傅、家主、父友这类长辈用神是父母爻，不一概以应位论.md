# 总断千金赋·应位遭伤：占别人的事要看是什么名分——占师傅、家主、父友这类长辈用神是父母爻，不一概以应位论

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占别人的事，不能一概拿应爻当用神：交往疏远的人、没有尊卑名分的人，才以应爻代表他人；占师傅、一家之主、父亲的朋友这类长辈，用神是父母爻；占子孙的朋友，用神是子孙爻；占妻妾奴婢，用神是妻财爻。父亲的朋友、自己的朋友和子孙的朋友，虽然都是别人，要按老幼、称呼、名分来取用神，不能一概用应位断错。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining another person's matter one must not always take the Other line as the Subject: it stands for the other person only when he is a distant acquaintance or has no rank of seniority; for a teacher, the head of a household or an older friend of one's father, the Subject is the Resource line; for the friend of one's descendants, the Output line; for wives, concubines, maids and servants, the Wealth line. A father's friend, one's own friend and the friend of one's descendants are all other people, but the Subject must be taken according to age, form of address and status, and one must not always misjudge by the Other position.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1167 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应位者，该当一个用神解说。 | 应位这一爻，应当按一个用神来解说。 | The Other position should be explained by taking it as a Subject. |
> | 如占他人亦各有用神分别，或占交疏之人及无尊卑之人，是应为他人也； | 比如占别人的事，也各有各的用神，占交往疏远的人和没有尊卑名分的人，是以应爻代表他人； | For example, in divining about other people each has his own Subject: for a distant acquaintance or a person with no rank of seniority, the Other line stands for the other person; |
> | 倘占父友、家主、师傅辈，这是父母爻为用神了； | 如果占父亲的朋友、一家之主、师傅这类长辈，这是以父母爻为用神； | if one divines for a father's friend, the head of a household or a teacher, the Subject is the Resource line; |
> | 子孙之友，这是子孙爻为用神了，妻妾奴婢，这是妻财爻为用神了。 | 子孙的朋友，是以子孙爻为用神；妻妾奴婢，是以妻财爻为用神。 | for the friend of one's descendants the Subject is the Output line, and for wives, concubines, maids and servants the Subject is the Wealth line. |
> | 那父友、自友及子孙之友，虽是他人，当分别老幼称呼名份取用，不可一概以应位误断。 | 父亲的朋友、自己的朋友和子孙一辈的朋友，虽然是别人，要分别老幼、称呼、名分来取用神，不能一概用应位断错。 | A father's friend, one's own friend and the friend of the Output generation (descendants), though other people, must be taken by distinguishing age, form of address and status, and one must not always misjudge by the Other position. |
> | 如卜损益自己之事，以世爻为自己也，世若受制，岂宜自己之谋乎？ | 如果占的是对自己有损有益的事，世爻就是自己，世爻如果受制，哪里适宜自己谋划呢？ | If one divines a matter that harms or benefits oneself, the Self line is oneself, and if the Self is controlled, how can one's own scheme be fit? |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一条取的是《总断千金赋》里「应位遭伤，不利他人之事」一句和王洪绪的注，和黄金策求师章（LY.EXAM.HJC.01）里「有尊卑名分者不看应爻，门人投师以父母爻为师长」说的是同一件事。注文最后一句讲的是占自己的事，放在一起没有拆开。
>
> *Note:* This takes the line "the Other position is injured, unfavourable to other people's matters" from the Rhapsody of a Thousand Golden Judgements together with Wang Hongxu's note; it says the same thing as the Huangjin Ce's seeking-a-teacher chapter (LY.EXAM.HJC.01), "where there is rank of seniority the Other line is not consulted, and a pupil going to a teacher takes the Resource line for the teacher". The last sentence of the note concerns one's own matters and is kept together without being split off.
