# 求师·财化父爻妻族荐之于不日（有父母而本宫财爻化出一重则妻家荐师，兄弟化出则朋友荐；动爻是重已荐过、是交将荐来）；母藏福德僧家设帐于先年（父爻伏子孙下师前年设帐于僧房道观，伏世下乃旧师）

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻化出父爻，妻家的亲族不久会推荐老师：卦有父母、本宫财爻化出一「重」，妻家就再荐一位老师；兄弟爻化出，是朋友来荐；动爻是「重」的已经荐过，是「交」的将要荐来。父爻伏在子孙爻下，老师前年在僧房道观设馆；伏在世爻下，是旧时的老师。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth changes into the Resource line the wife's kin will soon recommend a teacher: if the hexagram has a Resource line and a Wealth line of the home palace changes out one "chong", the wife's family will recommend another teacher, and if Peer changes out, a friend recommends one; if the moving line is a "chong" the recommendation has already been made, and if a "jiao" it is about to be made. If the Resource line lies hidden under Output, the teacher kept his school the year before last in a monks' cell or Taoist temple; if it lies hidden under the Self, he is a former teacher.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1857 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如父爻伏在子孙爻下，其师必前年设帐于僧房道观； | 如果父爻伏在子孙爻下，这位老师一定前年在僧房道观里设馆； | If the Resource line lies hidden under the Output line, the teacher surely kept his school the year before last in a monks' cell or Taoist temple; |
> | 父伏世下，乃是旧师。 | 父爻伏在世爻下，是旧时的老师。 | if the Resource line lies hidden under the Self, he is a former teacher. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「重」「交」是起卦里的两种动爻：重是老阳（三个正面），交是老阴（三个反面）；「设帐」是开设私塾；「本宫财爻」是和卦同宫的财爻。书里没有说「化出一重」具体怎么看，这里按字面译。
>
> *Note:* "Chong" and "jiao" are the two kinds of moving line in casting: chong is the old yang (three heads) and jiao the old yin (three tails); "setting up the curtain" is opening a private school; the "Wealth line of the home palace" is the Wealth line of the same palace as the hexagram. The book does not say exactly how "changes out one chong" is read, and it is translated as written.
