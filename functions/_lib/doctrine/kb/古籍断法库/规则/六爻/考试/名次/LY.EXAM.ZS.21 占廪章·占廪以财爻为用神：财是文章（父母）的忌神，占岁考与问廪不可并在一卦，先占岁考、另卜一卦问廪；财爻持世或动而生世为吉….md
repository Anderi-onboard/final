# 占廪章·占廪以财爻为用神：财是文章（父母）的忌神，占岁考与问廪不可并在一卦，先占岁考、另卜一卦问廪；财爻持世或动而生世为吉，断同求财章（觉子）

六爻 · 考试 · 名次 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占廪（廪生的名额和官给口粮）以财爻为用神，而财是文章（父母爻）的忌神，所以不能把岁考和占廪放在一卦里问：先占岁考的吉凶，另外再卜一卦问廪；财爻持世，或动而生世为吉，断法同求财章。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for the stipend (a stipend place and the government ration) the Wealth line is the Subject, but Wealth is the adversary of the writing (the Resource line), so the annual examination and the stipend must not be asked in one hexagram: first divine the fortune of the annual examination, then divine another hexagram for the stipend; Wealth holding the Self, or moving to give life to the Self, is auspicious, and it is judged as in the chapter on seeking wealth.

## 《增删卜易·占廪章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10081 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the stipend", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 廪以财爻为用神，财爻持世，或动而生世为吉，与求财章同断。 | 廪以财爻为用神，财爻持世，或者发动来生世为吉，按求财章的断法同断。 | For the stipend the Wealth line is the Subject; Wealth holding the Self, or moving to give life to the Self, is auspicious, and it is judged as in the chapter on seeking wealth. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「廪」是廪膳生，考岁考得一等才补；「占廪」是占能不能补上廪生的名额。财是文章的忌神（财克父），所以占考试时财是坏的，占廪时财却是用神，正反要分开占，书里叫「秘」。后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* A "stipend student" was a licentiate who drew a government grain ration, appointed on a first-grade result in the annual examination; "divining for the stipend" is asking whether one will be given a place. Wealth is the adversary of the writing (Wealth controls Resource), so in an examination Wealth is bad, while in the stipend it is the Subject; the two must be divined separately, which the book calls the "secret". The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
