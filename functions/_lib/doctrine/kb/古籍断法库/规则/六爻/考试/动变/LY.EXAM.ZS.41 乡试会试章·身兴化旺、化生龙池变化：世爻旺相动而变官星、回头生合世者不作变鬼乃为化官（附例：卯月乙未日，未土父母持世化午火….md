# 乡试会试章·身兴化旺、化生龙池变化：世爻旺相动而变官星、回头生合世者不作变鬼乃为化官（附例：卯月乙未日，未土父母持世化午火官星生世，果中会魁）

六爻 · 考试 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻旺相，发动变出官星，回头来生合世爻的，不算变鬼，是化官；例如未土父母临日建持世，变出午火官星回头生世，又得二爻巳火、初爻未土生扶，断为高中，果然中了会魁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self is thriving and moves to change into the official star, and that star turns back to give life to or combine with the Self, it is not a change into a ghost but a change into office; for example, the Wei Earth Resource line standing on the Day and holding the Self changes into the Wu Fire official star, which turns back to give life to the Self, with the Si Fire of the second line and the Wei Earth of the first line supporting it; the verdict was a high pass, and he did pass as a metropolitan head.

> [!note] 按
> 「龙池变化」是龙在池里变化，比喻身位兴旺而变得更好。「会魁」是会试的前几名。例子的卦名在原书里只画了图（风地观变天泽履），这里不收图。
>
> *Note:* "A dragon transforming in the pool" is a figure for a flourishing line changing for the better. A "metropolitan head" is one of the first in the metropolitan examination. The hexagram of the example (Wind over Earth, Guan, changing to Heaven over Marsh, Lü) is only drawn in the book and the drawing is not included.
