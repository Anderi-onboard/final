# 千金赋·岁君：太岁只在占官事、年运、乡会试、仕宦、面圣上书等事里才要紧，宜生合世爻、最忌刑冲克世，其余按他爻看；例：巳月壬子日占乡试，官星持世旬空，许之卯科，子年占果于卯年登科（太岁填实）

六爻 · 考试 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁平常和别的爻一样看，只有占官事、占年运、占乡试会试、做官、面圣、上书、叩阍、诤谏、请封请荫这几类事才要紧：宜太岁生合世爻，动而生世更吉，最忌刑冲克世。原因是太岁虽管一年的令，但尊而不亲，吉凶都比不上日月。书中例：巳月壬子日占乡试，得水地比卦，巳火父爻当月建、文星旺，只嫌官星持世落旬空，许他卯科才能得意；子年占，果然卯年登科，应在卯年，是太岁把空填实了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Tai Sui is read like any other line, and is important only in a few kinds of divination: official affairs, the fortune of the year, the provincial and metropolitan examinations, taking office, an audience with the emperor, submitting a memorial, beating the drum at the palace gate, remonstrating, and petitioning for a title; there it is good for the Tai Sui to give life to or combine with the Self line, better still if it moves to give life to the Self, and most of all one fears it punishing, clashing with or controlling the Self. The reason is that though the Tai Sui rules the command of a year it is honoured but not close, and its luck and ill never match those of the Day and Month. The case in the book: on a Ren-Zi day in the Si month a man divined the provincial examination and got Bi; the Si Fire Resource line stood on the Month and the star of writing was strong, and only the Pressure star held the Self while Void in its ten-day period, so I promised him that the Mao-year examination would bring success; he divined in the Zi year and indeed passed in the Mao year, answering in the Mao year because the Tai Sui filled the void.

> [!note] 按
> 「叩阍」是到宫门前击鼓喊冤，「诤谏」是直言规劝君主，「请封请荫」是替自己或子孙请求封赠、荫补官职；这是旧时通行的说法，不是本书的定义。「文星」指父母爻（文章）。卦图不收。这一段后面接着讲岁破、岁合、衰旺等，不属于考试，没有收。
>
> *Note:* To "beat the drum at the palace gate" is to go to the palace gate and beat the drum to cry out a grievance, to "remonstrate" is to admonish the ruler frankly, and to "petition for a title" is to ask for a title or an inherited post for oneself or one's descendants; these are the usual old meanings, not definitions given in this book. The "star of writing" is the Resource line (the essay). The hexagram diagram is not included. What follows in the book (the Tai Sui's break and combination, strength and weakness) is outside examinations and is not included.
