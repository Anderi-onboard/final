# 童试章·父衰变旺愈出愈奇、文化退神日长日短：父衰动化旺、回头生、进神则后胜于前，皆不宜化退神；旺化退神前篇稍遂后劲不力，衰化退神窗下千日之长临场一日之短（附例）

六爻 · 考试 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻衰而发动变旺、变回头生、变进神，文章越写越好，后胜于前；最不宜变退神：旺的变退神，前面顺利后劲不足；衰的变退神，以及变克、变空破墓绝、变回头克，是窗下千日用功、临场一日不济。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line is weak and moves to change into strength, into a line that turns back to give it life, or into an Advancing line, the writing grows better as it goes and the later is better than the earlier; a change into a Retreating line is what is most unwelcome: a strong line changing into a Retreating line goes well at first but lacks force later; a weak line changing into a Retreating line, or into control, or into Void, break, tomb or extinction, or into a line that turns back to control it, means a thousand days of work at the window and one day that falls short in the hall.

> [!note] 按
> 「进神」「退神」是同一五行往前、往后变（寅化卯是进，卯化寅是退）；「日长日短」书里自己解释为「窗下千日之长，临场一日之短」。「续案」是第一榜之后补取的名单。第二个例子在上一条（LY.EXAM.ZS.16）里。
>
> *Note:* An "Advancing" or "Retreating" line is the same phase changing forward or back (Yin turning into Mao is advancing, Mao into Yin retreating); the book itself explains "the days long, the day short" as "a thousand days at the window are long, one day in the hall falls short". The "supplementary list" is the list of those taken after the first posting. The second example is under the previous entry (LY.EXAM.ZS.16).
