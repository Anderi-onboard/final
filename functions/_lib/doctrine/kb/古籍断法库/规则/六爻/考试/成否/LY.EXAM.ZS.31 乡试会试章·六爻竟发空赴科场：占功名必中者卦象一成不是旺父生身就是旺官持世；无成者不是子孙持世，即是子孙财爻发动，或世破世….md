# 乡试会试章·六爻竟发空赴科场：占功名必中者卦象一成不是旺父生身就是旺官持世；无成者不是子孙持世，即是子孙财爻发动，或世破世空，或六爻乱动

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻都发动，白赶考场。占功名一定能中的卦，不是旺父生身，就是旺官持世；功名没成的，不是子孙持世，就是子孙、财爻发动，或者世破世空，或者六爻乱动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With all six lines moving one goes to the examination hall for nothing. A hexagram in which one is sure to pass is either a strong Resource line giving life to the Body or a strong Pressure line holding the Self; where one does not succeed it is either Output holding the Self, or Output and Wealth moving, or the Self broken or Void, or the six lines moving in disorder.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10138–10140 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 六爻竟发，空赴科场。 | 六爻都发动，白白赶考。 | With all six lines moving, one goes to the examination hall for nothing. |
> | 旧系：六爻竞发，功名恍惚以难成，此论是理。 | 旧本写的是：「六爻竞发，功名恍惚以难成」，这个说法是有道理的。 | The old text reads, "with all six lines contending to move, fame and rank are dim and hard to achieve"; this view is reasonable. |
> | 余见占功名必中者，卦象一成，若非旺父生身，定是旺官持世。 | 我见到占功名必中的，卦象一成，不是旺父生身，就一定是旺官持世。 | In the cases I have seen of divining for fame where one was sure to pass, once the hexagram is formed, it is either a strong Resource line giving life to the Body or a strong Pressure line holding the Self. |
> | 功名若无成者，不是子孙持世，即是子孙财爻发动，或是世破世空，或是六爻乱动，功名必无成。 | 功名如果没有成的，不是子孙持世，就是子孙、财爻发动，或者是世爻破、世爻空，或者是六爻乱动，功名必定没有成。 | Where fame and rank are not achieved, it is either Output holding the Self, or Output and Wealth moving, or the Self broken or Void, or the six lines moving in disorder; then fame will surely not be achieved. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「旧系」指前人的旧说，这里引的是《黄金策》求名章里的「六爻竞发，功名恍惚以难成」（见 LY.EXAM.HJC 求名章）。「科场」是考场。
>
> *Note:* "The old text" is an earlier saying; what is quoted is the line from the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a name, "with all six lines contending to move, fame and rank are dim and hard to achieve" (see the Huangjin Ce entries, LY.EXAM.HJC). The "examination hall" is the place where the examination is held.
