# 延师章·三合连年受教，六冲半载难留：世、父爻加日月成三合则情同父子受连年之教，卦得六冲或世克父、父克世则难收一岁之功

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻、父爻再加日月成三合，师生情同父子，会受连年教导；卦成六冲，或世爻克父爻、父爻克世爻，彼此不投缘，连一年的成效也难收到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self and Resource lines together with the Day or the Month form a three-harmony, teacher and pupil are as close as father and son and he is taught year after year; if the hexagram is a Six-Clash, or the Self controls the Resource line or the Resource line controls the Self, they do not get on and even a year's result is hard to gain.

## 《增删卜易·延师章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9899 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on engaging a teacher", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 三合连年受教，六冲半载难留。 | 三合，连年受教；六冲，半年也难留住。 | With a three-harmony one is taught year after year; with a Six-Clash not even half a year can be kept. |
