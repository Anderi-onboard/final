# 治经章·文旺官兴王家栋梁，世空子动蓬荜寒儒；吉象各举，凶象宜另习一经再诚心卜（附例：酉月丙子日占本经，改经再占，果于子科中魁）

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占所学的经：文章（父母）旺、官星兴旺是国家栋梁；世爻落空、子孙动是寒士。官持世而父动相生，或父母持世、官动相生、日月生世、世动化吉，都是飞腾之兆；世空世破、动变凶、子孙财爻持世、父母官鬼空破，宜另学一部经，诚心再占一次。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining the classic one studies: with the writing (Resource) strong and the official star thriving one is a pillar of the realm; with the Self Void and Output moving one is a poor scholar. Pressure holding the Self with Resource moving to give it life, or Resource holding the Self with Pressure moving to give life, or the Day or Month giving life to the Self, or the Self moving into an auspicious line, are all signs of soaring; if the Self is Void or broken, or moves into ill, or Output or Wealth holds the Self, or Resource and Pressure are Void or broken, one should study another classic and divine again with sincerity.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的例子是三个卦连着：第一卦「益之观」断为官旺合世但文章被回头克，宜改经；第二卦占易经「天火同人」，旺官持世却父爻临月破，还要再改；第三卦占书经「丰之离」，旺父持世、官动生之，只因子日冲午火，等下科壬子年冲去午火就应。「官持世、父动相生」后面的括注「疑为父持世、官动相生」是整理本里的校语（书里没有标是谁写的），照录。后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* The example is three hexagrams in a row: the first, "Yi changing to Guan", is judged as a strong Pressure line combining with the Self but with the writing controlled by a line turning back on it, so one should change classic; the second, for the Changes, "Heaven over Fire, Tongren", has a strong Pressure line holding the Self but the Resource line on the Month-break, so one must change again; the third, for the Documents, "Feng changing to Li", has a strong Resource line holding the Self with Pressure moving to give it life, and the only fault is that the Zi day clashes Wu Fire, which is cleared in the next examination in the Ren-Zi year. The bracketed remark "probably Resource holding the Self and Pressure moving to give life" after "Pressure holding the Self, Resource moving to give life" is a correction in the edited text (the book does not say who wrote it) and is kept. The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
