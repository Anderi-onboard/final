# 廷试章·官星为用父母次之：持世爻得日月岁生合定蒙首拔，官父世爻三合必受殊恩；世与官爻有一遇空破墓绝克害刑冲或动变凶为不祥，岁五官鬼冲克世为不测之灾（与会场兼断）

六爻 · 考试 · 乡会试 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 廷试（殿试）以官星为用神，父母爻次之：持世爻得日、月、太岁生合的，一定被选拔为首；官、父、世三爻合成三合，必受特别恩宠；世爻和官爻有一个遇空破、墓绝、克害、刑冲或发动变凶，是不吉之兆；太岁作官鬼来冲克世爻，是意想不到的灾祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the palace examination the official star is the Subject and the Resource line comes second: the line holding the Self given life or combined by the Day, the Month and the Year means one will surely be raised first; the Pressure, Resource and Self lines forming a three-harmony means special favour; if either the Self or the Pressure line meets Void or break, tomb or extinction, control or harm, punishment or clash, or moves into ill, it is an ill omen; if the Year acts as Pressure and clashes and controls the Self, it is an unforeseen disaster.

## 《增删卜易·廷试章》原文

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「廷试」是皇帝在殿廷亲自主持的考试（殿试），章名下注「与会场兼断」，是说和会试一起看。原文「日月岁五」「岁五官鬼」的「岁五」不见于别处，从上下文看是太岁（「岁君」「岁王」之类），可能是「岁王」的误字；译文按太岁译，并保留原文字面在括注里。「首拔」是被首先选拔。后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* The "palace examination" is the one the emperor himself presided over in the palace hall; the title notes that it is judged together with the metropolitan examination. The "sui wu" in "the Day, the Month and the sui wu" and "sui wu as Pressure" is not found elsewhere; from the context it is the Tai Sui (the Year Lord) and may be a slip for "sui wang"; the translation reads it as Tai Sui and keeps the literal reading in brackets. "Raised first" means selected first. The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
