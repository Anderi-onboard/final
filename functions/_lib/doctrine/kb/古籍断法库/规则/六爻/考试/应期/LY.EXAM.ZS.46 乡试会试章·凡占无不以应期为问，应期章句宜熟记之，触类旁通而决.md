# 乡试会试章·凡占无不以应期为问，应期章句宜熟记之，触类旁通而决

六爻 · 考试 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占考试没有不问应期的，所以讲应期的句子要熟记，自然能触类旁通。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* No divination for an examination fails to ask about the time of fulfilment, so the lines on timing should be learned by heart, and one will understand by analogy and decide.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10293 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占，无不以应期为问，故应期章句宜熟记之，自能触类旁通而决矣。 | 凡是占卦，没有不问应期的，所以应期章的句子要熟记，自然能触类旁通而断。 | In every divination one asks about the time of fulfilment, so the lines of the chapter on timing should be memorised, and one will understand by analogy and decide. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 增删卜易有专讲应期的章节；这一条只是提醒要熟记，本身没有具体规则。放在这里，是因为发案挂榜的应期已经在 LY.EXAM.ZS.24、25 里。
>
> *Note:* The Zengshan Buyi has a chapter devoted to timing; this entry only urges one to memorise it and gives no specific rule itself. It is placed here because the timing for issuing the list and posting the board is already in LY.EXAM.ZS.24 and 25.
