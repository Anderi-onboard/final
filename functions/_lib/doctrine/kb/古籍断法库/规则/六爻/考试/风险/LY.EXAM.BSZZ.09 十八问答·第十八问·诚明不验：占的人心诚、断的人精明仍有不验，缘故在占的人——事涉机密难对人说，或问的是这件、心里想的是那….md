# 十八问答·第十八问·诚明不验：占的人心诚、断的人精明仍有不验，缘故在占的人——事涉机密难对人说，或问的是这件、心里想的是那件；例：问家宅实为乡试功名（自己头场落榜、业师中第四名），十二岁的孩子被父命占功名实为父占子（父动克子而亡）

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占的人心诚、断的人精明，有时仍然不验，缘故在占的人，不在断的人：占的人的心意虽然诚，但有的事是机密，难对人说，或者问的是这件事，心里真正想的却是另一件事，所以才有不验。书中例一：申月乙亥日「占家宅」得井卦变节卦，断为应爻临父母、必有外姓长者同住，内卦合成鬼局、宅内不安，兄弟月破伏藏、兄弟家不利，对方一句句说没有，最后才说，占家宅其实是想和家里的老师一同去赴乡试；王洪绪说功名和家宅天远地隔，功名以官鬼为官星，家宅以鬼为祸害，既然占功名，你的功名不许，你的老师必定高中——官局生应、不来生世，叫出现无情，与你无干；后来到八月酉金实空之月，他自己头场就被贴出，他的老师中了第四名。例二：午月辛酉日占功名，得萃卦变遁卦，是一个十二岁的孩子，父亲命他占功名；若按孩子自占，官爻持世、夏火当令、未土父母爻是文章又化进神，功名有望，谁知是父亲叫孩子占，这个心意发自父亲的诚，所以是父占子，父母爻发动来克子孙，未月戌日孩子就去世了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the one who divines is sincere and the one who judges is shrewd, the result is sometimes still not borne out, and the cause lies in the one who divines, not the one who judges: however sincere his intent, the matter may be a secret that is hard to tell anyone, or he asks about one thing while his mind is really on another, and so it fails to come true. First case: in the Shen month, on a Yi-Hai day, a man divined "the household" and got Jing changing to Jie; the judgement was that the Other line sat in the second line carrying Resource, so an elder of another surname must be living with them, that the inner trigram combined into a Pressure frame, so the house was uneasy, and that Peer was Month-broken and hidden, so the brothers' family was unlucky; he denied each point in turn and at last said that he had divined the household but really wanted to go to the provincial examination together with the tutor of his family; Wang Hongxu replied that examination success and the household are as far apart as heaven and earth, that for examination success Pressure is the official star while for the household Pressure is calamity, and that since it was examination success that was asked, his own was not promised and his tutor would surely pass high: the Pressure frame gave life to the Other and not to the Self, which is called appearing without feeling and has nothing to do with him; later, in the eighth month, when the Void You Metal was filled, he himself was struck off in the first session and his tutor passed as number four. Second case: in the Wu month, on a Xin-You day, a twelve-year-old boy, ordered by his father to divine his examination prospects, got Cui changing to Dun; judging it as the boy's own divination, Pressure held the Self, Fire was in command in summer and Wei Earth, the Resource line, was the essay and changed into an Advancing spirit, so success looked hopeful; but the father had told the son to divine, so the intent arose from the father's sincerity and it was the father divining for the son; Resource moved to control Output, and in the Wei month, on a Xu day, the boy died.

> [!note] 按
> 第十八问的问句原文作「亦有验」，依答语讲的是不验的缘故、以及标题「诚明不验」，应是「亦有不验」，译文按这个意思翻。例一、例二的卦图都不收；例二的原文开头连着卦图最后一行（「兄弟申金」），只取「此子十二岁」起的正文。例一最后一句「其业师中」后面接着的「式第四名」在下一行。
>
> *Note:* The question in question 18 reads "yet it is also borne out" in the text; since the answer speaks of why it is not borne out, and the heading is "sincere and shrewd, yet not borne out", it should read "also not borne out", and the translation follows that sense. The hexagram diagrams of both cases are not included; the text of the second case begins glued to the last line of its diagram ("Peer, Shen Metal"), and only the text from "this boy was twelve" is taken. In the first case the words "and passed as number four" follow on the next line after "his tutor passed".
