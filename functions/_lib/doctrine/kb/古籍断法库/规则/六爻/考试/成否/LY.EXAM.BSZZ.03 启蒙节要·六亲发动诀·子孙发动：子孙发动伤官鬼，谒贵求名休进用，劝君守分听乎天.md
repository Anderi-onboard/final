# 启蒙节要·六亲发动诀·子孙发动：子孙发动伤官鬼，谒贵求名休进用，劝君守分听乎天

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻发动，会伤官鬼；这时拜见贵人、求功名都不宜往前进取，诀劝人守住本分、听凭天意。同一诀里，子孙发动对别的事是吉的：病人求医就痊愈，出门人和做买卖的平安，婚姻美满，产妇生子好养，官司私下和解不到官府。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Output line moves it wounds Pressure; then calling on a patron or seeking a title should not press forward, and the verse urges one to keep to one's lot and leave it to Heaven. The same verse treats a moving Output as good for other matters: the sick who seek a physician recover, travellers and traders are safe, a marriage is a happy one, a woman in labour bears a child easy to rear, and a lawsuit is settled privately without reaching the court.
