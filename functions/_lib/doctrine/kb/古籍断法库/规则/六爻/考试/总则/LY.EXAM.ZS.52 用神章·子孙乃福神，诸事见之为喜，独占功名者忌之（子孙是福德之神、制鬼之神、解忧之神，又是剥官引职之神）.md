# 用神章·子孙乃福神，诸事见之为喜，独占功名者忌之（子孙是福德之神、制鬼之神、解忧之神，又是剥官引职之神）

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻是福神：占别的事见到它是喜，只有占功名要忌它，因为子孙制住官鬼、剥去官位、引走职位。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Output line is the spirit of blessing: for every other matter seeing it is welcome, but for a title it is the one to avoid, because Output controls Pressure, strips office and leads away a post.

## 《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 443–444 行 · Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙为福德之神、为制鬼之神、为解忧之神、又为剥官、引职之神，故谓之子孙乃是福神，诸事见之为喜，独占功名者忌之。 | 子孙是福德之神、制住官鬼之神、解忧之神，又是剥去官位、引走职位之神，所以说子孙是福神，各种事占到它都是喜，只有占功名的人忌讳它。 | Output is the spirit of fortune and virtue, the spirit that controls Pressure, the spirit that dispels worry, and also the spirit that strips office and leads away a post; therefore Output is called the spirit of blessing, and for every matter seeing it is welcome, except for one who divines for a title, to whom it is a taboo. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「剥官引职」是说子孙克官鬼，把官位和职位剥走、引开。
>
> *Note:* "Stripping office and leading away a post" means that Output controls Pressure and takes the office and the post away.
