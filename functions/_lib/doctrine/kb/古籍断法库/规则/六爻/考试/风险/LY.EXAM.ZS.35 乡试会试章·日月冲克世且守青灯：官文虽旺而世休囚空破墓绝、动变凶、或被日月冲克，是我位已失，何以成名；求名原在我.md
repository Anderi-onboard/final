# 乡试会试章·日月冲克世且守青灯：官文虽旺而世休囚空破墓绝、动变凶、或被日月冲克，是我位已失，何以成名；求名原在我

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中官星、文章虽然旺，但世爻休囚、空破墓绝、发动变凶，或者被日月冲克，是自己的位子已经失了，怎么能成名？因为求名本来就取决于自己。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Even if the official star and the writing are strong in the hexagram, if the Self is resting and trapped, Void or broken, in the tomb or extinct, moves into ill, or is clashed and controlled by the Day or the Month, one's own place is already lost and one cannot win a name, for seeking a name rests in the end on oneself.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10145 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日月冲克世，且守青灯。 | 日月冲克世，只好继续守着青灯（再读）。 | If the Day or the Month clashes and controls the Self, one must keep to the green lamp (and study on). |
> | 卦中官文虽旺，而世爻休囚、空破墓绝，及动而变凶，或日月冲克世爻者，是我位既失，何以成名？ | 卦中官星、文章虽然旺，但世爻休囚、空破墓绝，以及发动变凶，或者被日月冲克世爻，是我的位子已经失了，凭什么成名？ | Even if the official star and the writing are strong in the hexagram, if the Self is resting and trapped, Void or broken, in the tomb or extinct, or moves and changes into ill, or is clashed and controlled by the Day or the Month, my own place is already lost; how can I win a name? |
> | 盖求名原在我也。 | 因为求名本来就取决于自己。 | For seeking a name rests in the end on oneself. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一条回应黄金策求名章「世伤官鬼，仕途窒而不通」一类的说法，强调世爻是求名的人自己，世爻坏了，官文再旺也没有用。
>
> *Note:* This answers lines of the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a name such as "if the Self harms Pressure the path of office is blocked": the Self is the man himself who seeks the name, and if the Self is spoiled, a strong official star and writing are of no use.
