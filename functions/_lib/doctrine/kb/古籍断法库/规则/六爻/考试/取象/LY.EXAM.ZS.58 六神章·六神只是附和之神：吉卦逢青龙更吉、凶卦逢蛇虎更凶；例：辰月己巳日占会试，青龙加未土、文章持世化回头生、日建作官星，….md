# 六神章·六神只是附和之神：吉卦逢青龙更吉、凶卦逢蛇虎更凶；例：辰月己巳日占会试，青龙加未土、文章持世化回头生、日建作官星，首唱无疑

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六神（青龙、朱雀、勾陈、螣蛇、白虎、玄武）不是不灵，而是「附和之神」：本来吉的遇到青龙更吉，卦本来凶的遇到螣蛇白虎更凶，玄武主盗贼、朱雀主是非，没有不灵验的。书中例：辰月己巳日占会试，得观卦变否卦，青龙加在未土上，文章持世、动而变出回头之生，日辰在第五爻作官星，一起来生世，第一名没有疑问。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The six spirits (Azure Dragon, Vermilion Bird, Hook Chain, Entwining Snake, White Tiger, Dark Tortoise) are not without effect; they are "supporting spirits" that go along with the rest: what was lucky is luckier still when it meets the Azure Dragon, what was ill in the hexagram is worse still when it meets the Snake and the Tiger, the Dark Tortoise governs thieves and the Vermilion Bird governs disputes, and none of this ever fails. The case in the book: on a Ji-Si day in the Chen month a man divined the metropolitan examination and got Guan changing to Pi; the Azure Dragon was added to Wei Earth, the writing held the Self and, moving, changed into a returning generation, and the Day at the fifth line acted as the Pressure star, all coming to give life to the Self, so first place was beyond doubt.

> [!note] 按
> 「首唱无疑」大意是名列第一没有疑问；卦图不收，这一段结尾的「又如」以后是下一个例子，不收。《卜筮正宗》里也有一个观卦变否卦的会试例（LY.EXAM.BSZZ.08），日期和断语都不一样。
>
> *Note:* The phrase "first place is beyond doubt" means in sense that coming first is not in question; the hexagram diagram is not included, and what follows "another" at the end of this passage belongs to the next case and is not included. The Bushi Zhengzong also has a metropolitan-examination case of Guan changing to Pi (LY.EXAM.BSZZ.08), with different dates and a different judgement.
