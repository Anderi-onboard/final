# 用神章：占功名以官鬼爻为用神（忌空破，宜生怕克）；师长、章奏、文书、文章、书馆以父母爻为用神；门徒以子孙爻为用神

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 考试里要看的东西各有各的用神：问功名，用神是官鬼爻，最忌落空落破，宜有相生、怕被相克，月日动变四处的生克和别的用神同样看；问师长、奏章、文书、文章、书馆，用神是父母爻；问门徒（学生），用神是子孙爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In an examination reading each thing to be watched has its own Subject: for a title the Subject is the Pressure line, which most fears Void and break, is glad of generation and fears control, and whose generation and control from the four places (Month, Day, moving line, changed line) are read as for any other Subject; for teachers, memorials, documents, essays and the study hall the Subject is the Resource line; for pupils the Subject is the Output line.

## 《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙爻：占子孙、占女、女婿、侄、甥、门徒，凡在我子孙辈中，皆以子孙爻为用神。 | 子孙爻：占子孙、女儿、女婿、侄、外甥、门徒，凡是在我子孙辈里的，都以子孙爻为用神。 | The Output line: for children and grandchildren, a daughter, a son-in-law, nephews, a sister's son and pupils, anyone in the generation of my own descendants, the Output line is in every case the Subject. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「三父八母」是旧时服丧礼制里对各种名分的父亲、母亲的合称，这里只是说和父母同类的人都归父母爻。卜筮正宗十八论里有同一节的另一种写法，见 LY.EXAM.BSZZ.01。
>
> *Note:* The "three fathers and eight mothers" is the old mourning-rule term for the various kinds of father and mother by status; here it only says that all in the class of parents belong to the Resource line. The Bushi Zhengzong gives the same assignments in its Eighteen Treatises: see LY.EXAM.BSZZ.01.
