# 乡试会试章·世退、官星化退窗下囊萤；世与官父俱旺内有一化进神者名姓高悬，占乡试定然联捷，独不喜化退神：世化退无勇往之心，父化退学问日颓，官化退仕途却步

六爻 · 考试 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻、官星、父爻都旺，其中有一个变进神的，名姓高悬，占乡试必定连捷；最不喜欢变退神：世爻变退，不是临场受阻就是没有勇往之心；父爻变退，学问一天天衰退；官星变退，在仕途上止步，白头无成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self, the official star and the Resource line are all strong and one of them changes into an Advancing line, one's name is hung high and in divining for the provincial examination one will surely pass in succession; a change into a Retreating line is what is unwelcome: the Self changing into retreat means being blocked on the day or having no heart to press forward; the Resource line changing into retreat means learning declines day by day; the official star changing into retreat means halting on the path of office and achieving nothing even in white hair.

> [!note] 按
> 「囊萤」是晋人车胤用袋装萤火虫读书的故事，指苦读；「进退章」指增删卜易里讲进神退神的一章。
>
> *Note:* "Fireflies in a bag" is the story of Che Yin of Jin who read by the light of fireflies in a bag, meaning hard study; "the chapter on advancing and retreating" is the chapter of the Zengshan Buyi on Advancing and Retreating lines.
