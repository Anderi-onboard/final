# 求师·本象同乡在内则离家不远、他宫异地在外则隔属须遥（用象在本宫居外卦本处人而住远，在他宫居内卦外郡人而住近）；与世相生非亲则友（与世爻生合有亲道，不同宫者相识朋友）

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 老师是哪里的人、住得远近：用神在本宫，是同乡，在内卦离家不远；在别宫，是异地人，在外卦就隔得很远；注补充：本宫而居外卦是本地人但住得远，他宫而居内卦是外郡人却住得近。用神和世相生，不是亲戚就是朋友：与世爻生合有亲谊，不同宫的是相识的朋友。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Where the teacher is from and how far he lives: the Subject in its own palace means a fellow villager, and in the inner trigram he lives not far from home; in another palace he is from another place, and in the outer trigram he lives across a jurisdiction and far away; the commentary adds that in its own palace but in the outer trigram he is a local man who lives far, and in another palace but in the inner trigram he is from another prefecture but lives near. If the Subject and the Self give each other life he is a relative or a friend: giving life to or combining with the Self means kinship, and of a different palace an acquaintance and friend.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1826 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 与世爻生合，必有亲道； | 和世爻生合，一定有亲戚关系； | If it gives life to or combines with the Self, there is surely kinship; |
> | 若与世爻不同宫者，是相识朋友。 | 如果和世爻不同宫，是相识的朋友。 | if it is not of the same palace as the Self, he is an acquaintance and friend. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「隔属」是隔着一个行政辖区（府、县）。注讲的是经文的两种交叉情形，和经文一起看就是四种：本宫内卦（同乡近）、他宫外卦（异地远）、本宫外卦（同乡但住远）、他宫内卦（异乡但住近）。
>
> *Note:* A "jurisdiction" is an administrative district (a prefecture or county). The commentary gives the two crossed cases of the verse, which together make four: own palace and inner trigram (fellow villager, near), other palace and outer trigram (from elsewhere, far), own palace and outer trigram (fellow villager but lives far), other palace and inner trigram (from elsewhere but lives near).
