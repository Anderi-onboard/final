# 求名·应合日生必资鹗荐、动伤日克还守鸡窗：父官化绝名必不成；应爻、动爻或月建日辰扶起官鬼，必须浼人推荐或用财资求才可成

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻来合、日辰来生，必靠推荐；动爻受伤、被日辰克，还得守着鸡窗读书。父爻、官星变绝，名必不成；如果应爻、动爻或月建、日辰扶起官鬼，必须托人推荐，或者用钱财去求才可成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Other combines and the Day gives life, one will surely rely on a recommendation; if the moving line is harmed and the Day controls it, one must go on keeping to the chicken window. If the Resource line or the official star changes into extinction, the name will surely not be made; if the Other line, a moving line, the Month or the Day raises the Pressure line up, one must ask someone to recommend one or use money to seek it, and then it can succeed.

> [!note] 按
> 这一联增删卜易（乡试会试章）引过并各自讲了喜忌（LY.EXAM.ZS.38、39）。「浼人」是请托别人；「用财资求」是花钱去谋求。
>
> *Note:* The Zengshan Buyi (chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations) quotes this couplet and discusses each half (LY.EXAM.ZS.38 and 39). "Asking a man" is entrusting the matter to someone; "using money to seek it" is spending money to try for it.
