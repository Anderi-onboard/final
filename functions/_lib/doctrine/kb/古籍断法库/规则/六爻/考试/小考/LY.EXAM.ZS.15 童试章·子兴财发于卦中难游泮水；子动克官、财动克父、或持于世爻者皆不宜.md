# 童试章·子兴财发于卦中难游泮水；子动克官、财动克父、或持于世爻者皆不宜

六爻 · 考试 · 小考 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 童试时子孙兴旺、财爻发动在卦中，难以入县学；子孙动克官星、财爻动克父爻，或者子孙、财爻持在世爻上，都不适宜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the youth examinations, if Output flourishes and Wealth moves in the hexagram it is hard to enter the county school; Output moving and controlling Pressure, or Wealth moving and controlling Resource, or either of them holding the Self line, is unfavourable in every case.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「游泮水」是入县学成生员（旧时县学前有泮池，新生入学要行礼，叫「游泮」）。子孙克官星、财爻克父爻是考试里最忌的两种克：官星管功名，父爻管文章。
>
> *Note:* "Swimming in the Pan pond" is entering the county school and becoming a student (the county school had a ceremonial pond, and new students performed a rite there). Output controlling the official star and Wealth controlling the Resource line are the two most feared controls in examinations: the official star governs rank and the Resource line governs the writing.
