# 延师章·鬼化文书克世则讼由乎学：鬼动化父母冲克世，日后必有争讼；纵有日月动爻制服也不宜，鬼动克世、世动变凶皆不宜从

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼变成父母来冲克世爻，日后一定因学业上的事引出官司；就算日月、动爻能制住官鬼也不适宜，因为眼下制住了，日后官鬼逢生旺还是灾祸；凡官鬼动克世、世动变凶，都不宜跟这位老师学。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line changes into Resource and clashes and controls the Self, a lawsuit will arise from the schooling in future; even if the Day, the Month or a moving line subdues Pressure it is unsuitable, for though it is held down for now, when Pressure later meets life and strength there will be calamity and trouble; whenever Pressure moves and controls the Self, or the Self moves into ill, it is unsuitable to follow this teacher.

## 《增删卜易·延师章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9895 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on engaging a teacher", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼化文书克世，讼由乎学。 | 官鬼变成文书来克世，会因为学业上的事引起官司。 | If Pressure turns into the document line and controls the Self, a lawsuit will arise from the schooling. |
> | 鬼动化父母，冲克世爻，异日必主争讼。 | 官鬼发动变成父母，冲克世爻，将来一定有争讼。 | If the Pressure line moves and changes into Resource and clashes and controls the Self, there will surely be a quarrel and lawsuit in future. |
> | 能使日月动爻制服鬼爻，也不相宜，此何故也？ | 即使有日月、动爻能制服鬼爻，也不相宜，这是什么缘故呢？ | Even if the Day, the Month or a moving line can subdue the Pressure line, it is still unfavourable; why is that? |
> | 神兆机于动。 | 因为神在动处显出先兆。 | Because the spirits show their omen in what moves. |
> | 目前虽可制服，他日鬼逢生旺，必见灾非。 | 眼前虽然可以制服，日后官鬼遇到生旺，一定出现灾祸是非。 | Though it can be subdued for now, when Pressure meets life and strength later, calamity and trouble will surely appear. |
> | 不唯鬼动化父，但系鬼动克世，或世动变凶，皆不宜从。 | 不只是官鬼动变父母，凡是官鬼发动克世，或者世爻发动变凶，都不适宜跟这位老师学。 | Not only when Pressure moves and turns into Resource: whenever Pressure moves and controls the Self, or the Self moves and changes into an ill line, it is unsuitable to follow this teacher. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「神兆机于动」是这一段的理由：神的先兆从动处显出来，所以被制住的鬼只是暂时按下，等它逢生旺会再起。官非的断法另见官非域。
>
> *Note:* "The spirits show their omen in what moves" is the reason given: the omen appears in what moves, so a Pressure line that is held down is only suppressed for the moment and will rise again when it meets life and strength. Rules on lawsuits are in the lawsuit domain.
