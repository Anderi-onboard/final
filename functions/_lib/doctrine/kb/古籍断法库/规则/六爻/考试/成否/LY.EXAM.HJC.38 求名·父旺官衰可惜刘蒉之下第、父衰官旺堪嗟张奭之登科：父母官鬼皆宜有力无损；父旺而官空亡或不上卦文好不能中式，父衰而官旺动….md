# 求名·父旺官衰可惜刘蒉之下第、父衰官旺堪嗟张奭之登科：父母官鬼皆宜有力无损；父旺而官空亡或不上卦文好不能中式，父衰而官旺动扶起文书文平亦可成名

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻、官鬼都宜有力而没有损伤才可成功名：父爻旺相而官鬼空亡或不上卦，文字再好也不能中式；父爻衰弱而官爻旺动、扶起文书，文字平常也可许他成名。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource and Pressure lines should both be strong and unharmed for fame and rank to be achieved: with the Resource line thriving but Pressure Void or absent from the hexagram, however fine the writing one cannot pass; with the Resource line weak but the Pressure line strong and moving and raising the document line up, one may be granted a name even if the writing is ordinary.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1912 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母官鬼皆宜有力无损，功名可成。 | 父母爻、官鬼都宜有力量而没有损伤，功名才可成。 | The Resource and Pressure lines should both be strong and unharmed, and then fame and rank can be achieved. |
> | 若父母爻旺相，官鬼空亡，或不上卦，文字虽好不能中式，如刘蒉之锦绣文章，竟不登第； | 如果父母爻旺相，官鬼空亡或不上卦，文字虽然好也不能中式，就像刘蒉的锦绣文章，竟然没有登第； | If the Resource line is thriving but Pressure is Void or absent from the hexagram, however fine the writing one cannot pass, like Liu Fen's brocade essays, which failed to win him the degree; |
> | 若父爻衰弱，得官爻旺动扶起文书，文字虽平常，可许成名，如张奭之文章，虽欠精美，反登高第也。 | 如果父爻衰弱，得官爻旺而动，扶起文书，文字虽然平常，可以许他成名，就像张奭的文章，虽然欠精美，反而登了高第。 | If the Resource line is weak but the Pressure line is strong and moving and raises the document line up, though the writing is ordinary one may be granted a name, like Zhang Shi's essay, which lacked polish yet won a high place. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 刘蒉、张奭是唐代的两位应试者（书里只提名字、没有讲故事）：一个文章好却落第，一个文章平常却登科，正对着「父旺官衰」「父衰官旺」。增删卜易乡试会试章有同一对意思（「父旺官衰，终须下第」「父衰官旺，堪许登科」，LY.EXAM.ZS.37、38）。
>
> *Note:* Liu Fen and Zhang Shi were two Tang examination candidates (the book gives only the names and tells no stories): one failed with fine writing and the other passed with ordinary writing, matching "Resource strong, Pressure weak" and "Resource weak, Pressure strong". The Zengshan Buyi chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations has the same pair ("with Resource strong and the official star weak one fails in the end", "with Resource weak and the official star strong one may be granted success", LY.EXAM.ZS.37 and 38).
