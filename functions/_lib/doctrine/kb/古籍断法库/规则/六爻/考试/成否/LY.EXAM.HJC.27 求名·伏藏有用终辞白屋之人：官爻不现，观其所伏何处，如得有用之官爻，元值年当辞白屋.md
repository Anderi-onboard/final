# 求名·伏藏有用终辞白屋之人：官爻不现，观其所伏何处，如得有用之官爻，元值年当辞白屋

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官爻不出现，看它伏在哪里；如果是有用的官爻，到它自己地支值年的那一年，就会告别平民的白屋。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line does not appear, look where it lies hidden; if it is a useful Pressure line, in the year of its own branch one will leave the white cottage.

## 《黄金策·求名》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1888 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a name", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 伏藏有用， | 伏藏的爻有用， | A hidden line that is of use |
> | 终辞白屋之人 | 终究是能告别白屋的人 | makes a man who in the end leaves the white cottage |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1888 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官爻不现，但观其所伏何处，如得有用之官爻，元值年当辞白屋矣。 | 官爻不出现，只要看它伏在哪里，如果得到有用的官爻，元值年就会告别白屋。 | If the Pressure line does not appear, look only where it lies hidden; if one gets a useful Pressure line, in the year of its own branch one will leave the white cottage. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「元值年」书里没有解释，从上下文看是这个官爻自己的地支轮到当年（流年）的那一年，译文按此译。
>
> *Note:* The book does not explain the "year of its own branch"; from the context it is the year in which the branch of that Pressure line comes round as the year, and the translation reads it so.
