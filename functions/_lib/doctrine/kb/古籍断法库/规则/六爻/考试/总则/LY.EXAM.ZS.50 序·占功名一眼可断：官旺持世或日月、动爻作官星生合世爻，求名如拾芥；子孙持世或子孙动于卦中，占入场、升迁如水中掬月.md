# 序·占功名一眼可断：官旺持世或日月、动爻作官星生合世爻，求名如拾芥；子孙持世或子孙动于卦中，占入场、升迁如水中掬月

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占功名有两种一眼就能断的卦：官爻旺相持世、或者日月动爻作官星来生合世爻，求名像弯腰拾起一根草那样容易；子孙持世、或者子孙在卦中发动，不管问入场（考试）还是升迁，都像水中捞月，捞不着。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for a title there are two hexagrams that can be read at a glance: a strong Pressure line holding the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acting as the Pressure star and giving life to or combining with the Self, and seeking a name is as easy as picking up a blade of grass; Output holding the Self, or Output moving in the hexagram, and whether one asks about entering the examination hall or about promotion it is like scooping the moon from the water.

## 野鹤老人(《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 24 行 · The Old Man of Yehe (Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 假令占功名，若得旺官持世，或日月、动爻作官星生合世爻，求名如拾芥耳； | 譬如占功名，如果得到旺相的官爻持世，或者日月、动爻作官星来生合世爻，求名就像弯腰拾起一根草那样容易； | Suppose one divines for a title: if a strong Pressure line holds the Self, or the Day, the Month or a moving line acts as the Pressure star and gives life to or combines with the Self, seeking a name is as easy as picking up a blade of grass; |
> | 倘遇子孙持世，或子孙动于卦中，不拘占入场、占升迁，悉如水中掬月。 | 如果遇到子孙持世，或者子孙在卦中发动，不管占入场还是占升迁，全都像水中捞月。 | if Output holds the Self, or Output moves in the hexagram, whether one divines for entering the examination hall or for promotion, all is like scooping the moon from the water. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「拾芥」是弯腰拾起一根草，比喻毫不费力；「掬月」是用手去捧水里的月亮，比喻白费力气。这一句后面接着讲占求财，不属于考试，没有收。
>
> *Note:* Picking up a blade of grass means doing something without effort, and scooping the moon means trying to cup the moon in the water with one's hands, that is, labouring in vain. The next sentences go on to divining for wealth and are outside examinations, so they are not included.
