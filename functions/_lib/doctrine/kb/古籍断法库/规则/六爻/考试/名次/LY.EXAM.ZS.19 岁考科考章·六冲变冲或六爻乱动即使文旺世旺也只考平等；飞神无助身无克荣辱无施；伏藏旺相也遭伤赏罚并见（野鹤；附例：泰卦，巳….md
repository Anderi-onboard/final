# 岁考科考章·六冲变冲或六爻乱动即使文旺世旺也只考平等；飞神无助身无克荣辱无施；伏藏旺相也遭伤赏罚并见（野鹤；附例：泰卦，巳午之火非文旺乃字佳）

六爻 · 考试 · 名次 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦成六冲、变冲或六爻乱动，即使文章旺、世爻旺，也只考平等；文章衰受刑克、世爻休囚被克、变凶的，必定劣等；飞神得不到帮助、卦身不受克，无荣无辱；父爻出现、不旺不衰、没有生扶也没有克制、世爻不被冲克的，保得住平等；伏藏的爻即使旺相也遭伤，赏罚一起来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram is a Six-Clash or changes into a clash, or the six lines move in disorder, then even with the writing strong and the Self strong one takes only the middle grade; with the writing weak and punished or controlled, or the Self resting and trapped and controlled, or changing into ill, it is surely the lowest grade; if the flying line gets no help and the Body is not controlled there is neither honour nor disgrace; if the Resource line appears, neither strong nor weak, with neither support nor control and the Self not clashed or controlled, the middle grade is assured; a hidden line, even though thriving, is harmed, and reward and punishment appear together.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10072 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 午火月建为父母，而卦中之巳火伏藏于寅木之下，旺而又遇飞神之生，爻逢六合，堪称吉卦，独嫌日辰克世，未为全美。 | 午火是月建、做父母爻，而卦里的巳火伏藏在寅木之下，旺相，又得飞神来生，爻遇六合，可以称作吉卦，只嫌日辰克世，不算完美。 | Wu Fire is the Month and acts as the Resource line, and the Si Fire in the hexagram lies hidden under Yin Wood, thriving and given life by its flying line, the lines meeting a Six-Combine; it may be called an auspicious hexagram, only that the Day controls the Self, so it is not perfect. |
> | 发落之日，果宗师面叱，念字仿古帖，姑置三等。 | 放榜发落那天，果然被学官当面训斥，又念及他的字是临仿古帖，姑且列在三等。 | On the day the results were announced, the examiner indeed reproved him to his face but, taking into account that his writing followed an old copy-book model, placed him in the third grade for the time being. |
> | 后悟此巳午之火，非文旺也，乃字佳也，但此卦虽多逢，事确少见，存之为法。 | 后来才悟到这巳午之火，不是说文章旺，而是说字写得好；不过这样的卦虽然常遇到，这样的事确实少见，留下来作个参考。 | Later I realized that this Si-Wu Fire did not mean strong writing but fine handwriting; though such a hexagram is often met, such a matter is truly rare, and I keep it as a model. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 「飞神无助身无克，荣辱无施」是说父爻没有得到生扶、也没有受克，不上不下，所以无赏无罚；「伏藏旺相也遭伤」是伏神旺相但飞神克它，所以赏罚并见。例子里巳火伏在寅木下、午火是月建，断语说「旺」本来该是文章好，结果是字好，所以野鹤说这样的卦存之为法而已。「宗师」是学官（提学）。
>
> *Note:* "If the flying line gets no help and the Body is not controlled, there is neither honour nor disgrace" means the Resource line is neither supported nor controlled, in the middle, so there is neither reward nor punishment; "a hidden line, though thriving, is harmed" is a hidden line that is strong but controlled by its flying line, so reward and punishment appear together. In the example Si Fire lies hidden under Yin Wood and Wu Fire is the Month; the verdict first took the strength to mean good writing, and it turned out to mean good handwriting, so the author keeps such a hexagram only as a model. The "examiner" is the school official (the education commissioner).
