# 考遗才章·官爻持世生世则必蒙收，父母空破墓绝则不见用；世受克、空破墓绝、动化凶、子孙持世或动则不录；父母持世生世吉，休囚空破凶（凡告考同例）

六爻 · 考试 · 小考 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 考遗才（补考漏掉的人）和告考：官爻持世或生世，一定被收录；父母爻空破墓绝，不会被用；世爻受克或空破墓绝、动变凶、子孙持世或子孙发动，都主不被录取；父母持世生世吉，休囚空破凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For the supplementary examination (taking those who were missed) and for petitioning to sit an examination: the Pressure line holding or giving life to the Self means one will surely be admitted; the Resource line Void, broken, in the tomb or extinct means one will not be used; the Self controlled, or Void, broken, in the tomb or extinct, or moving into ill, or Output holding the Self or moving, all mean one will not be taken; the Resource line holding or giving life to the Self is auspicious, and resting and trapped, Void or broken is inauspicious.

> [!note] 按
> 「考遗才」是学官为没赶上考试的人再开的补考；「告考」是自己具呈请求准许应考，章名下注「凡告考同例」。「他不见用」的「他」是主考的人（书里没有明说）。后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* The "supplementary examination" is a later examination the school official holds for those who missed the main one; "petitioning to sit" is submitting a petition to be allowed to sit, and the chapter title notes that the same rules apply. The "he" in "he will not be taken into use" is the examiner (the book does not say so). The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
