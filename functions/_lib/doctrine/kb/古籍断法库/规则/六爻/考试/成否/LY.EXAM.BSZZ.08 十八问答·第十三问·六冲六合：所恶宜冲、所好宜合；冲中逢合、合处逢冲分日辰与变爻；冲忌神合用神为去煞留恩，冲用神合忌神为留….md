# 十八问答·第十三问·六冲六合：所恶宜冲、所好宜合；冲中逢合、合处逢冲分日辰与变爻；冲忌神合用神为去煞留恩，冲用神合忌神为留煞害命；例：会试未土持世，化出日辰午火官星生合，果中探花

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六冲、六合怎么断：人所厌恶的事宜冲，所喜欢的事宜合，只有占病要分近病、久病：近病遇冲就好、久病遇冲就死，六合则相反。六冲卦里，有日辰相合、变爻相合的，叫冲中逢合；六合卦里，有日辰相冲、变爻相冲的，叫合处逢冲。冲掉忌神、合住用神，叫去煞留恩，事事说吉；冲掉用神、合住忌神，叫留煞害命，件件皆凶。书中例：辰月庚午日占会试，得观卦变否卦，未土持世，变出的午火官星正是日辰，生着合着世爻，断为「鼎甲在掌」，结果中了探花。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* How the Six-Clash and Six-Combine hexagrams are judged: what a person hates is better clashed and what he likes is better combined, and only in divining illness is there the distinction between a recent and a long illness: a recent illness that meets a clash is cured, a long one dies, and with a combination it is the reverse. In a Six-Clash hexagram, if the Day or the changed line combines with it, it is called a clash meeting a combination; in a Six-Combine hexagram, if the Day or the changed line clashes with it, it is called a combination meeting a clash. If the Adversary is clashed and the Subject combined, it is called removing the baleful and keeping the gracious and every matter is called lucky; if the Subject is clashed and the Adversary combined, it is called keeping the baleful to harm life and every matter is ill. The case in the book: on a Geng-Wu day in the Chen month a man divined the metropolitan examination and got Guan changing to Pi; Wei Earth held the Self and changed out into the Wu Fire Pressure star, which is the Day itself, giving it life and combining with it, so the judgement was that the top three places lay in the palm of his hand, and he indeed passed as Tanhua.

> [!note] 按
> 卦例原文作「辰日庚午日占会试」，第一个「日」疑为「月」之误（辰月庚午日），译文按「辰月」翻；卦图不收。按通行说法，「鼎甲」是殿试一甲的前三名（状元、榜眼、探花），「探花」是第三名。功名里六冲、六合的取法，《黄金策》说六冲难成、六合易得（LY.EXAM.HJC.37），《增删卜易》补上了官、父、世要旺才行（LY.EXAM.ZS.36）。合处逢冲、冲中逢合三种分法，婚姻里的同一条见 LY.MARRY.BSZZ.02。
>
> *Note:* The case text reads "Chen day, Geng-Wu day"; the first "day" is probably a slip for "month" (a Geng-Wu day in the Chen month), and the translation follows that; the hexagram diagram is not included. By the usual reckoning the "top three" are the first three places of the palace examination (Zhuangyuan, Bangyan, Tanhua), and Tanhua is third. For examinations, the Huangjin Ce says a Six-Clash hexagram is hard to succeed and a Six-Combine one easy (LY.EXAM.HJC.37), and the Zengshan Buyi adds that Pressure, Resource and the Self must also be strong (LY.EXAM.ZS.36). The same threefold division of a combination meeting a clash is given for marriage at LY.MARRY.BSZZ.02.
