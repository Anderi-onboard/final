# 求名·病阻试期无故空临于世位：动爻日辰来伤世爻而世爻落空大凶，试前占去不成，强去终不利，轻则病重则死

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 无缘无故病倒耽误考期，是空亡临在世位：动爻、日辰来伤世爻而世爻又落空，是大凶的象；考试前占，说去不成，勉强去终归不利，轻的生病，重的死亡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Being blocked from the examination by illness for no reason is Void standing on the Self's position: a moving line or the Day harming the Self while the Self falls Void is a sign of great ill; if divined before the examination that going will not come about, going by force is in the end unfavourable, lightly it is illness and heavily death.
