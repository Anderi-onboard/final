# 求名·身兴变鬼来试方成：卦遇不成之兆，而身、世爻变出官鬼有气、父母不坏，下帷可中（来年再考才成）

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有不成的兆头，但卦身、世爻变出有气的官鬼、父母爻又没有坏，闭门苦读（下帷）可以考中，要等下一次考试才成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram shows a sign of failure but the Body or Self line changes into a Pressure line that has breath and the Resource line is not spoiled, one may pass by shutting oneself away to study, and success comes only on the next sitting.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 卦遇不成之兆，而得身世爻变官鬼有气，而父母不坏者，下帷可中也。 | 卦遇到不成的兆头，而得到身爻、世爻变出官鬼有气，父母爻又没有坏的，闭门读书（下帷）就可以考中。 | If the hexagram shows a sign of failure but the Body or Self line changes into a Pressure line that has breath, and the Resource line is not spoiled, one may pass by shutting oneself away to study. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「下帷」是放下帷幕闭门读书（汉代董仲舒下帷讲诵）。「来试方成」是这一科不中，下一次才成。增删卜易也引过「身兴变鬼，来试方成」（LY.EXAM.ZS.40），说这类讲卦身的旧说它试过不灵，只在世爻上灵验。
>
> *Note:* "Lowering the curtain" is shutting oneself away to study (from Dong Zhongshu of the Han, who taught behind a lowered curtain). "Success on the next sitting" means failing this time and succeeding the next. The Zengshan Buyi also quotes "the Body flourishes and changes into Pressure, success comes only on the next sitting" (LY.EXAM.ZS.40) and says that of these old sayings about the Body it found them inaccurate and true only of the Self line.
