# 求名·六爻竞发功名恍惚以难成：六爻皆喜安静，官鬼有气不空、月建日辰不伤克则吉；动则有变，变出之爻又有死墓绝空刑克为破败；乱动卦大概不吉

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻都争着发动，功名恍惚难成：六爻都喜安静，只要官鬼有气不空、月建日辰不来伤克就吉；凡动就有变，变出的爻又有死、墓、绝、空、刑、克的说法，都是破败，所以乱动的卦大概不吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If all six lines contend to move, fame and rank are dim and hard to achieve: the six lines all like stillness, and as long as the Pressure line has breath and is not Void and the Month and Day do not come to harm or control it, it is auspicious; whatever moves changes, and the line it changes into is again judged by death, tomb, extinction, Void, punishment and control, all of which are ruin, so a hexagram of disordered movement is on the whole inauspicious.

> [!note] 按
> 增删卜易引过这一联（LY.EXAM.ZS.31），并补充自己的验证：占功名必中的，不是旺父生身就是旺官持世；没成的，不是子孙持世就是子孙财爻发动、世破世空或六爻乱动。
>
> *Note:* The Zengshan Buyi quotes this couplet (LY.EXAM.ZS.31) and adds its own findings: where one is sure to pass it is either a strong Resource line giving life to the Body or a strong Pressure line holding the Self; where one does not succeed it is Output holding the Self, or Output and Wealth moving, or the Self broken or Void, or the six lines moving in disorder.
