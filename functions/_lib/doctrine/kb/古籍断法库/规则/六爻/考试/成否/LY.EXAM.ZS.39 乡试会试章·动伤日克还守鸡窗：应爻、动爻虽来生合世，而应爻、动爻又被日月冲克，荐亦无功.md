# 乡试会试章·动伤日克还守鸡窗：应爻、动爻虽来生合世，而应爻、动爻又被日月冲克，荐亦无功

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动爻受伤、被日辰克制，还得守着鸡窗读书；应爻、动爻虽然来生合世爻，但应爻、动爻又被日月冲克，推荐也没有功效。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the moving line is harmed and the Day controls it, one must go on keeping to the chicken window (studying); even if the Other line and a moving line come to give life to or combine with the Self, if the Other line and the moving line are in turn clashed and controlled by the Day or the Month, the recommendation is of no avail.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10186 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 动伤日克，还守鸡窗。 | 动爻受伤、又被日辰克制，还得继续守着鸡窗读书。 | If the moving line is harmed and the Day controls it, one must go on keeping to the chicken window (studying). |
> | 应爻、动爻虽来生合世爻，而应爻、动爻又被日月冲克者，荐亦无功。 | 应爻、动爻虽然来生合世爻，但应爻、动爻又被日月冲克的，推荐也没有功效。 | Even if the Other line and a moving line come to give life to or combine with the Self, if the Other line and the moving line are in turn clashed and controlled by the Day or the Month, the recommendation is of no avail. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「鸡窗」是书房的雅称（晋人宋处宗养了一只会说话的鸡，放在窗边，和他谈论，后来称书窗为鸡窗）；「守鸡窗」就是继续读书、等下一科。这一条和上一条「应合日生，必资鹗荐」相反。
>
> *Note:* The "chicken window" is a literary name for a study (from the story of Song Chuzong of Jin, whose talking cock sat by his window and discussed learning with him, after which a study window was called a chicken window); "keeping to the chicken window" is going on studying to wait for the next examination. It is the reverse of the previous line, "if the Other combines and the Day gives life one will surely rely on a recommendation".
