# 十八问答·第十问·进神喜忌：吉神宜化进、忌神宜化退；进有三种（旺相乘势、休囚待时、逢空破冲合待期），退也有三种；例：乡试官星持世，第五爻官化进神，今秋中式、次年辰年联捷

六爻 · 考试 · 动变 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动爻变出来的神有进神、退神，吉凶要分喜忌：吉神喜欢化进神，忌神喜欢化退神。进有三种：旺相的趁势就进；休囚的等到得时才进；动爻或变爻有一个遇到空、破、冲、合的，等到填补、合住、冲开的时候才进。退也有三种：旺相的有日月或动爻生扶，占近期的事暂时不退；休囚的当时就退；动变爻有一个遇空破冲合的，等到那个时候填补、合冲了才退。书中例：申月癸卯日占乡试，得恒之大过卦，酉金官星持世，日辰冲而暗动，又得第五爻官化进神拱扶，断为不但今秋中举，来春也定占鳌头；结果登了乡榜，第二年辰年连中——「化进神」应在今秋，「联捷」应在辰年冲而逢合。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The spirits that come out of a moving line when it changes are Advancing and Retreating spirits, and their luck divides into the welcome and the feared: an auspicious spirit is glad to change into an Advancing spirit, and an Adversary is glad to change into a Retreating spirit. There are three ways of advancing: a strong spirit advances by riding its momentum; a resting or trapped one advances by waiting for its season; and if the moving line or the changed line meets Void, break, clash or combination, it advances by waiting for the period that fills, combines or clashes it. There are also three ways of retreating: a strong spirit given life and support by the Day, the Month or a moving line does not retreat for the time being when a near matter is divined; a resting or trapped one retreats at once; and if the moving line or the changed line meets Void, break, clash or combination, it retreats when the period comes that fills, combines or clashes it. The case in the book: on a Gui-Mao day in the Shen month a man divined the provincial examination and got Heng changing to Daguo; the Pressure star in You Metal held the Self and was stirred in secret by the clash of the Day, and the fifth line's Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit supported it, so the judgement was that not only would he pass in the autumn but next spring he would surely take first place; he made the provincial list and succeeded again in the Chen year after: the change into an Advancing spirit answered in this autumn, and the consecutive success answered in the Chen year, a clash that meets a combination.

> [!note] 按
> 卦例的卦图（恒卦变大过卦的六爻排列）不收，只收卦图前后的文字。「折桂」指乡试中举（乡试在秋天，榜叫桂榜），「占鳌头」指在更高一级的考试里名列第一，「联捷」指一场接一场连续考中；这是科举里通行的说法，不是本书给的定义。
>
> *Note:* The case's hexagram diagram (the arrangement of the six lines of Heng changing to Daguo) is not included, only the text before and after it. "Breaking the laurel" means passing the provincial examination (held in autumn, with its list called the laurel list), "taking the head of the sea-turtle" means coming first in a higher round, and "consecutive success" means passing round after round in a row; these are common examination idioms, not definitions given in this book.
