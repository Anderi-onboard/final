# 求名·卦值六冲此去难题雁塔、爻逢六合这回必占鳌头：占功名得六冲卦必难成，得六合卦必易得

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占功名，得六冲卦一定难成，得六合卦一定容易得到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for fame and rank, a Six-Clash hexagram is surely hard to achieve and a Six-Combine hexagram surely easy to gain.

## 《黄金策·求名》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1910 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a name", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦值六冲， | 卦遇上六冲， | If the hexagram is a Six-Clash, |
> | 此去难题雁塔； | 这一去难以在雁塔题名； | this time it is hard to inscribe one's name at the Wild Goose Pagoda; |
> | 爻逢六合， | 爻遇上六合， | if the lines meet a Six-Combine, |
> | 这回必占鳌头 | 这一回一定夺得鳌头 | this time one will surely take the head of the turtle |

> [!warning] 打架
> 这里（王洪绪注）断得很干脆：得六冲卦必难成，得六合卦必易得。增删卜易（野鹤）不赞成一刀切：六冲卦只要官、父、世爻都旺，不必再占，竟断必发；欠旺的另占一卦再断；六合卦也要官、父、世都得地，有一个失陷又无生扶，六合也没有益处（LY.EXAM.ZS.36）。野鹤还记了旧注「卦逢六冲，冲之即散」，说他见过六冲而世、父皆旺的卦应了高中。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Here (Wang Hongxu's commentary) judges flatly: a Six-Clash is surely hard to achieve and a Six-Combine surely easy to gain. The Zengshan Buyi (the Old Man of Yehe) does not accept a blanket rule: a Six-Clash hexagram in which the official star, Resource and Self are all strong needs no second divination and one judges outright that one will succeed; one lacking in strength should be divined again; a Six-Combine also needs the official star, Resource and Self all well placed, and if one is lost with no life or support even a Six-Combine is of no use (LY.EXAM.ZS.36). The Old Man of Yehe also records the old note, "a hexagram meeting a Six-Clash is scattered by the clash", and says he has seen a Six-Clash with the Self and Resource both strong come out in a high pass.
