# 启蒙节要·诸爻持世诀·考试三句：父母持世，官动财旺宜赴试；子孙持世，求名切忌；鬼爻持世，功名最喜世当权

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 诸爻持世诀里和考试有关的三句：父母爻持世，官爻发动、财爻旺相，适合去赴考；子孙爻持世，求名最忌（子孙坐在世位上）；官鬼爻持世，别的事难安，只有求功名最喜欢世爻当权。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Three lines of the verse on lines holding the Self bear on examinations: with Resource holding the Self, if Pressure moves and Wealth is strong it is a good time to go and sit the examination; with Output holding the Self, seeking a title above all should avoid it; with Pressure holding the Self, other matters are uneasy, but for success in the examinations the best thing is that the Self holds power.

## 《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·三十三、诸爻持世诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 197 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 33: the verse on lines holding the Self", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母持世主身劳， | 父母爻持世，主身体劳碌， | Resource holding the Self means toil of the body; |
> | 求嗣妾众也难招， | 求子嗣的，妾再多也难招来； | one seeking an heir will find it hard to draw one, however many concubines there are; |
> | 官动财旺宜赴试， | 官爻发动、财爻旺相，宜去赴考； | when Pressure moves and Wealth is strong, it is fit to go and sit the examination; |
> | 财摇谋利莫心焦， | 财爻发动，谋利的不要心焦； | when Wealth moves, one seeking profit need not be anxious; |

## 《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·三十三、诸爻持世诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 199 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 33: the verse on lines holding the Self", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 鬼爻持世事难安， | 官鬼爻持世，事情难安， | Pressure holding the Self, matters are uneasy; |
> | 占身不病也遭官， | 占自身，即使不生病也要遭官非； | in divining for oneself, even without illness one meets an official suit; |
> | 财物时时忧失脱， | 财物时时担忧失脱； | goods and money are always at risk of loss; |
> | 功名最喜世当权， | 求功名，最喜世爻当权。 | but in seeking success in the examinations, the best thing is that the Self holds power. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 诀文没有解释「官动财旺」为什么宜赴试；读的时候要和「财爻发动克文书，应举求名总是虚」（LY.EXAM.BSZZ.04）分开看：这里的前提是父母爻持世、官爻发动，那一句只说财动克文书。引文里夹着的求嗣、谋利、占身、失脱几句说的是别的事，因为引文要连着引、中间不能断，所以留在了里面。
>
> *Note:* The verse does not explain why Pressure moving and Wealth strong should be fit for sitting the examination. Read it apart from "Wealth moves and controls the document line, so sitting exams and seeking a title all come to nothing" (LY.EXAM.BSZZ.04): the premise here is Resource holding the Self with Pressure moving, whereas that line speaks only of Wealth moving against the document line. The clauses about heirs, profit, the body and loss that sit inside the quotations are other matters; they are kept because each quotation must run on without a gap.
