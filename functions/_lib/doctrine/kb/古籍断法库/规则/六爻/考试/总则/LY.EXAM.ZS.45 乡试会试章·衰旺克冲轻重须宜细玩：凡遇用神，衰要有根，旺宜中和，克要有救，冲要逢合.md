# 乡试会试章·衰旺克冲轻重须宜细玩：凡遇用神，衰要有根，旺宜中和，克要有救，冲要逢合

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 衰、旺、克、冲的轻重要仔细玩味：用神衰的要有根，旺的宜适中，被克的要有救，被冲的要遇合。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The weight of weakness and strength, control and clash must be weighed with care: if the Subject is weak it must have roots, if strong it should be moderate, if controlled it must have rescue, if clashed it must meet a combination.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10290–10291 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 衰旺克冲，轻重须宜细玩。 | 衰、旺、克、冲的轻重，要仔细玩味。 | The weight of weak and strong, control and clash must be savoured with care. |
> | 凡遇用神，衰要有根，旺宜中和，克要有救，冲要逢合。 | 凡遇到用神，衰的要有根，旺的宜适中，被克要有救，被冲要遇合。 | Whenever one meets the Subject: if weak it must have roots, if strong it should be moderate, if controlled it must have rescue, if clashed it must meet a combination. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是乡试会试章的结尾总则，适用于所有取用神的断法，不限考试。「有根」是衰的爻有生扶、或在长生、禄旺之地，不会被一冲就断。
>
> *Note:* This closes the chapter with a general rule that applies to every Subject reading and is not limited to examinations. "Having roots" means a weak line has life and support or stands on a Birth or Emolument stage, so that a single clash does not cut it off.
