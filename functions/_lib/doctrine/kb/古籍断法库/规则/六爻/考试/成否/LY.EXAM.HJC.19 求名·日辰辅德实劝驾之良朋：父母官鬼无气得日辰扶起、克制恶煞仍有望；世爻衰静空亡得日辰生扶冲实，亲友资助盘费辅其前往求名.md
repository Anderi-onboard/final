# 求名·日辰辅德实劝驾之良朋：父母官鬼无气得日辰扶起、克制恶煞仍有望；世爻衰静空亡得日辰生扶冲实，亲友资助盘费辅其前往求名

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻、官鬼无气，得日辰扶起、克制恶煞，仍然有望，叫辅德；世爻衰、静、空亡，得日辰生扶或冲实，有亲友资助路费，帮他去应考求名。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource and Pressure lines have no breath but the Day raises them up and restrains the baleful spirit, there is still hope, which is called supporting virtue; if the Self is weak, still and Void but the Day gives it life and support or clashes it solid, relatives and friends will help with the travelling expenses and aid his going to seek a name.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1872 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如父母官鬼无气，若得日辰扶起，克制恶煞，仍旧有望，故曰辅德。 | 如果父母爻、官鬼无气，若得日辰扶起、克制恶煞，仍然有望，所以叫辅德。 | If the Resource and Pressure lines have no breath but the Day raises them up and restrains the baleful spirit, there is still hope, which is why it is called supporting virtue. |
> | 或世爻衰静空亡，得日辰生扶冲实，主有亲友资助盘费，辅其前往求名也。 | 或者世爻衰而静、落空亡，得日辰生扶、冲实，主有亲友资助路费，帮助他前去求名。 | Or if the Self is weak and still and Void but the Day gives it life and support or clashes it solid, relatives and friends will help with the travelling expenses and aid his going to seek a name. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「冲实」是冲开落空的爻，让它实在起来（空遇冲则实）。「劝驾」是劝人出来应考、做官，这里指资助盘费的人。
>
> *Note:* "Clashed solid" is clashing a Void line so that it becomes real (a Void line that meets a clash is made real). "Urging one to set out" is urging a man to come forward for the examination or for office, here meaning the people who give money for the journey.
