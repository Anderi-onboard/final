# 占此应彼·举意先后：念头发自谁、卦就应谁，父叫子占功名仍作父占子（例：十五岁孩子，父动克子，妻死申月、子卒未年）；叫人代占须先告知、待他忘怀后自己想起再占；孝廉不明说占会试而混问功名是昧己

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦应的是动念的那个人的念头：别人的心事，他没有动念去占，是你提醒他、叫他占的，那就是你的念头，卦应你不应他；所以父亲叫孩子占功名，仍按父亲占孩子来断。想让别人为自己的事占，可以先告诉他，等他忘怀了、自己想起来再另起念头去占。占的时候要照直说出来，不要含混：孝廉不明说占会试而混问功名，就是欺瞒自己。书中例：午月辛酉日占功名，得萃卦变遁卦，是一个年仅十五的孩子，父亲叫他占将来有没有功名，若按官星持世、父爻是文章又化进神，功名有望，但因为是父亲叫儿子占，念头是父亲的，仍作父占子：父爻发动克子、兄弟爻发动伤妻，妻子死在申月，儿子死在未年。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hexagram answers the thought of the person from whom the thought arose. If a matter is on another's mind but he has not thought of divining it, and I remind him and tell him to divine, then the thought is mine, and the hexagram answers me and not him; so when a father tells his son to divine for a title, it is still judged as the father divining for the son. If one wants someone to divine his own matter, one may first tell him and let him divine later with a fresh thought of his own after he has forgotten it. And one must state the question plainly: a provincial graduate who does not say he is divining the metropolitan examination but asks vaguely about a title is deceiving himself. The case in the book: on a Xin-You day in the Wu month a boy of fifteen, told by his father to divine whether he would have a title in future, got Cui changing to Dun; by the Pressure star holding the Self, with the Resource line as the essay changing into an Advancing spirit, success looked hopeful, but since it was the father who told the son to divine, the thought was the father's and it was read as the father divining for the son: Resource moved and controlled the son, Peer moved and wounded the wife, and the wife died in the Shen month and the son in the Wei year.

> [!warning] 打架
> 同一个卦例，《卜筮正宗》（LY.EXAM.BSZZ.09）说孩子十二岁、未月戌日就去世；这里说孩子年仅十五，妻子死在申月、孩子死在未年。年岁和应期两书对不上，这里还多了妻子去世的一节。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The same case appears in the Bushi Zhengzong (LY.EXAM.BSZZ.09), where the boy is twelve and dies in the Wei month on a Xu day; here he is only fifteen, the wife dies in the Shen month and the son in the Wei year. The two books disagree on the age and on the timing, and this one adds the wife's death.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段在书里没有单独的章名，上接一连几个「占此应彼」的卦例（先占功名后占子病等），本条只取了与考试有关的部分；卦例的卦图不收。「孝廉」是举人的别称，这是通行的说法。
>
> *Note:* This passage has no chapter name of its own in the book and follows a run of "divining this and being answered that" cases (a title divined first and a child's illness after, and so on); only the part about examinations is taken here, and the case's hexagram diagram is not included. "Provincial graduate" is the usual name for the holder of the second degree.
