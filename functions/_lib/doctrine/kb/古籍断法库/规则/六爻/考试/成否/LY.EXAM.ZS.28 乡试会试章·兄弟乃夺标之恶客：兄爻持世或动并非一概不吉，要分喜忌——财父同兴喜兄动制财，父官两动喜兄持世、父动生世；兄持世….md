# 乡试会试章·兄弟乃夺标之恶客：兄爻持世或动并非一概不吉，要分喜忌——财父同兴喜兄动制财，父官两动喜兄持世、父动生世；兄持世临日月旺相又官文两旺必能中式但不能夺魁（觉子：官鬼子孙同动…最怕兄兴）

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻在考试里被叫做夺标的恶客，俗传兄爻持世或发动都不吉，其实有喜有忌：财爻、父爻同兴时喜兄动制财；父、官两动时喜兄弟持世、父动生世；兄弟持世临日月或旺相、官文两旺，必能中式，只是不能夺魁。觉子补充：官鬼、子孙同动，官旺得生扶、子孙衰弱被克，再有父动最好，最怕兄兴。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Peer line is called the unwelcome guest who snatches the prize; common lore says Peer holding the Self or moving is ill, but there are welcome cases and feared cases: when Wealth and Resource both flourish it is a joy that Peer moves to control Wealth; when Resource and Pressure both move it is welcome that Peer holds the Self and the moving Resource gives it life; with Peer holding the Self and standing on the Day or the Month or thriving, and the official star and the writing both strong, one will surely pass, but cannot take first place. Juezi adds: when Pressure and Output both move, with Pressure strong and given life and Output weak and controlled, a moving Resource line is best, and Peer flourishing is most to be feared.

> | 兄弟乃夺标之恶客。 | 兄弟爻是来夺魁首的恶客。 | Peer is the unwelcome guest who comes to snatch the prize. |
> | 俗传兄弟爻兴难上榜，故凡兄爻持世，及动者，皆以不吉断，殊不知有喜有忌。 | 民间传说兄弟爻兴旺就难上榜，所以凡是兄爻持世以及发动的，都断为不吉，却不知道有的情形喜、有的情形忌。 | Common lore says that if the Peer line flourishes it is hard to get on the list, so whenever the Peer line holds the Self or moves it is judged inauspicious, without knowing that there are cases for welcome and cases for dread. |
> | 所喜者，卦中财父同兴，喜得兄动以制财也； | 所喜的情形是：卦中财爻和父爻同时兴旺，喜得兄弟发动来制住财爻； | What is welcome is this: when Wealth and Resource are both flourishing in the hexagram, it is a joy that Peer moves to control Wealth; |
> | 卦中父官两动者，又喜兄弟持世，父动以生世也； | 卦中父爻、官爻两个都动的，又喜兄弟持世，因为父爻动可以生世； | when the Resource and Pressure lines both move in the hexagram, it is again welcome that Peer holds the Self, since the moving Resource line gives life to the Self; |
> | 兄弟持世，若临日月，或旺相，再得官文两旺； | 兄弟持世，如果临日月，或者旺相，再得官星、文章（父母）两旺； | with Peer holding the Self, if it stands on the Day or the Month, or is thriving, and moreover the official star and the writing are both strong; |
> | 或官动生父、父动生世，必能中式，但不能抡元而夺魁也，故以兄弟乃夺标之客。 | 或者官爻动生父爻、父爻动生世爻，一定能中式，但是不能得第一名，所以说兄弟是夺标的客人。 | or the Pressure line moves to give life to the Resource line and the Resource line moves to give life to the Self, one can surely pass, but cannot take first place and the prize, which is why Peer is called the guest who snatches the prize. |

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10108 行 · Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 觉子曰：卦中官鬼子孙同动，官临旺相遇生扶，子孙衰弱而被克，再宜父动，最怕兄兴。 | 觉子说：卦中官鬼和子孙同时发动，官鬼临旺相又得生扶，子孙衰弱又被克，再有父爻动就好，最怕兄弟兴旺。 | Juezi says: when Pressure and Output both move in the hexagram, with Pressure thriving and given life and support and Output weak and controlled, it is further good if the Resource line moves, and Peer flourishing is most to be feared. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「夺标」是夺取标的（夺魁）。这一条是说兄弟爻在求名里不是一概坏：兄弟克财，在财克父（财伤文章）的卦里，兄动恰好制住财，所以喜；父官两动生世时，兄弟持世也不碍。兄弟不能让人夺魁，因为它是同经之人（LY.EXAM.HJC 求名章「兄弟同经，乃夺标之恶客」）。
>
> *Note:* "Snatching the prize" is taking first place. The point is that Peer is not simply bad in seeking a name: Peer controls Wealth, so in a hexagram where Wealth is controlling Resource (Wealth harming the writing) a moving Peer is welcome because it holds Wealth down; when Resource and Pressure both move and give life to the Self, Peer holding the Self does no harm. Peer cannot make one take first place because it stands for rivals studying the same classic (see the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a name, LY.EXAM.HJC, "Peer of the same classic is the unwelcome guest who snatches the prize").
