# 岁考科考章·文宜旺相最忌休囚、官要生身不宜克世：父、世旺相又得生扶全无破绽定考超等，旺而逢生上等，有一遇刑冲又次之，世父同受克劣等无疑（野鹤、觉子；附例）

六爻 · 考试 · 名次 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 岁考、科考按父爻和世爻的旺衰分等第：两爻旺相又得日月动爻生扶、全无破绽的考超等；旺而逢生上等；旺而无生、或相而遇扶、没有刑冲克破的次之；两爻有一个遇刑冲的又次之；世爻受克要受责罚，世爻父爻同受克必定劣等。野鹤说要变通，以六冲变冲、反吟伏吟、变克化绝为破绽；觉子说《易冒》以进神为上等、退神为下等，没有进退之神时就按旺衰定。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the annual and qualifying examinations the grade is set by how strong the Resource and Self lines are: both thriving and given life and support by the Day, the Month and moving lines, with no flaw at all, is the top grade; strong and meeting life is the upper grade; strong without life, or thriving and given support, with no punishment, clash, control or breaking, is next; one of the two meeting punishment or clash is lower still; if the Self is controlled one will be flogged, and if the Self and Resource are both controlled it is surely the lowest grade. The Old Man of Yehe says to be flexible, and takes a Six-Clash, a clash by change, a Reversed or Hidden Chant, or a change into control or extinction as flaws; Juezi says the Yi Mao takes an Advancing line as the upper grade and a Retreating line as the lower, and that when there is neither one decides by strength and weakness.

## 《增删卜易·岁考科考章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10026 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the annual and qualifying examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 文宜旺相，最忌休囚； | 文章（父母爻）宜旺相，最怕休囚； | The writing (the Resource line) should be thriving and most fears being resting and trapped; |
> | 官要生身，不宜克世。 | 官星要来生身，不适宜克世。 | the official star should give life to the Body and must not control the Self. |
> | 父与世爻旺相，又得日月动爻生扶，及动而化吉，全无破绽者，定考超等。 | 父爻和世爻旺相，又得到日月、动爻生扶，以及发动变吉，全无破绽的，一定考超等。 | If the Resource and Self lines are thriving, given life and support by the Day, the Month and moving lines, or move and change into auspicious lines, with no flaw at all, one will surely take the top grade. |
> | 若父世旺而逢生，上等； | 如果父爻、世爻旺而又逢生，是上等； | If Resource and Self are strong and meet life, it is the upper grade; |
> | 旺而无生，相而遇扶，无刑冲克破者，次之； | 旺而没有生、相而得到扶助，没有刑、冲、克、破的，是次一等； | strong but without life, or thriving and given support, with no punishment, clash, control or breaking, is the next grade; |
> | 父母有一而遇刑冲者，又次之； | 两爻（父母、世爻）里有一个遇到刑、冲的，再次一等； | if one of the two (Resource and Self) meets punishment or clash, the grade is lower still; |
> | 世爻受克，教刑不免，世父同受克者，劣等无疑。 | 世爻受克，会受到教官的责罚；世爻、父爻同时受克的，必定是劣等。 | if the Self is controlled, a flogging by the teacher cannot be avoided; if the Self and Resource are both controlled, it is surely the lowest grade. |

> [!note] 按
> 岁考是生员每年一次的考试，科考是应乡试前的资格考，按成绩分一二三…等；「超等」「上等」「平等」「劣等」是等第的叫法。「廪」是官给口粮的廪膳生，「帮粮」是优等另给的补贴口粮。「教刑」是教官的责罚。「父世」就是父爻和世爻。这一章后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* The annual examination was held each year for licentiates, and the qualifying examination was the one that qualified a man to sit the provincial examination; results were graded first, second and so on, and "top", "upper", "middle" and "lowest" are names of grades. A "stipend student" received a government grain ration, and the "grain allowance" was an extra ration for the excellent. The "flogging" is the teacher's punishment. The "Resource and Self" is the Resource line and the Self line. The [Qian's note] at the end of the chapter is not quoted.
