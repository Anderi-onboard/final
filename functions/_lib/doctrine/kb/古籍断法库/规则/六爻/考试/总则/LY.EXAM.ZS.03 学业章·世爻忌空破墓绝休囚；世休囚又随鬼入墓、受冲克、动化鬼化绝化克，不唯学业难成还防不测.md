# 学业章·世爻忌空破墓绝休囚；世休囚又随鬼入墓、受冲克、动化鬼化绝化克，不唯学业难成还防不测

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 学业上世爻最怕落空、受破、入墓、逢绝或休囚，否则诸事无成；世爻休囚，又跟着官鬼入墓，或被日月、动爻冲克，或发动变官鬼、变绝、变克，不但学业难成，还要提防意外。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In study the Self line most fears being Void, broken, in the tomb, extinct, or resting and trapped, or nothing will succeed; if the Self is resting and trapped and moreover follows Pressure into the tomb, or is clashed and controlled by the Day, the Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into Pressure, extinction or control, then not only will study be hard to complete but one must guard against the unforeseen.

## 《增删卜易·学业章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9815 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on study", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 以上皆不宜世爻空破墓绝休囚，诸事无成。 | 以上各种，都不适宜世爻落空、受破、入墓、逢绝或休囚，否则诸事无成。 | In all the above, the Self line must not be Void, broken, in the tomb, extinct, or resting and trapped, or nothing will succeed. |
> | 世若休囚，而又随鬼入墓，及受日月动爻冲克，或动而化鬼、化绝、化克者，不唯学业难成，还防不测。 | 世爻如果休囚，而且又跟着官鬼入了墓，以及被日月、动爻冲克，或者发动变成官鬼、变绝、变克的，不但学业难成，还要提防意外。 | If the Self is resting and trapped, and moreover follows Pressure into the tomb, or is clashed and controlled by the Day, the Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into Pressure, extinction or control, then not only will study be hard to complete but one must guard against the unforeseen. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「随鬼入墓」指世爻跟着官鬼一起入墓，通玄妙论讲过它的三种（LY.CAREER.BSQS.04）。「不测」是意外、不测之祸，书里没有说具体是什么。
>
> *Note:* "Following Pressure into the tomb" means the Self follows the Pressure line into the tomb, the three kinds of which are given in the Tongxuan Miaolun (LY.CAREER.BSQS.04). "The unforeseen" is an unexpected misfortune; the book does not say what.
