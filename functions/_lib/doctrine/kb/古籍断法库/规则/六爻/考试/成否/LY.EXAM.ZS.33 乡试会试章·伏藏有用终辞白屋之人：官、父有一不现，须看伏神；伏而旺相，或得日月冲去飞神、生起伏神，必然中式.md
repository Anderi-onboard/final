# 乡试会试章·伏藏有用终辞白屋之人：官、父有一不现，须看伏神；伏而旺相，或得日月冲去飞神、生起伏神，必然中式

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官爻、父爻有一个不出现，要看它伏在哪里；伏神旺相，或者日月冲去飞神、生起伏神，必定中式。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If one of the Pressure and Resource lines does not appear, look at where it lies hidden; if the hidden line is thriving, or the Day or the Month clashes away the flying line and gives life to the hidden line, one will surely pass.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10142 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 伏藏有用，终辞白屋之人。 | 伏藏的爻有用，终究能告别平民的白屋。 | A hidden line that is of use will in the end take leave of the common man's cottage. |
> | 官父有一而不现，须看伏神。 | 官爻、父爻有一个不出现，要看它伏在哪里。 | If one of the Pressure and Resource lines does not appear, one must look to the hidden line. |
> | 若伏而旺相，或得日月冲去飞神，生起伏神，必然中式。 | 如果伏神旺相，或者得日月冲去飞神、生起伏神，必定中式。 | If the hidden line is thriving, or the Day or the Month clashes away the flying line and gives life to the hidden line, one will surely pass. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「白屋」是平民住的房子，「辞白屋」是脱离平民身份、做官。伏神是卦里没有出现、藏在某一爻（飞神）下面的六亲。
>
> *Note:* A "white cottage" is a commoner's house, and "leaving the white cottage" is leaving the commoner's status to become an official. A hidden line is a relation that does not appear in the hexagram but lies under some line (the flying line).
