# 求名·财若交重休望青钱之中选、福如发动难期金榜之题名：唯卜功名财福反为恶煞（财克父、子克鬼）；官爻持世得财动来生则财无忌，子孙固为忌客

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻发动，别指望被选中；福神（子孙）发动，难指望金榜题名。只有占功名时，财、福反而是恶煞，因为财克父、子孙克官鬼；官爻持世又得财爻动来生它，财就没有忌讳，子孙则本来就是忌客。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth moves, do not hope to be chosen; if Output moves, it is hard to hope for one's name on the Golden List. Only in divining for fame and rank are Wealth and Output on the contrary baleful spirits, for Wealth controls Resource and Output controls Pressure; if the Pressure line holds the Self and Wealth moves to give it life, Wealth is no taboo, but Output is by nature the hostile guest.

> [!note] 按
> 「青钱中选」是说像「青钱学士」那样每试必中：唐代张鷟应试屡次得第一，人称「青钱学士」，这里只借来比喻考中。财克父（伤文章）、子孙克官鬼（伤功名），所以在求名里这两样都是忌；和占财时财、子孙为吉正好相反。官爻持世又有财来生，是财生官，所以财不忌。
>
> *Note:* "Chosen like the green coin" is a figure for passing every time: Zhang Zhuo of the Tang took first place over and over and was called the "green-coin scholar", and the book borrows it only as a figure for passing. Wealth controls Resource (harming the writing) and Output controls Pressure (harming rank), so in seeking a name both are taboos, which is the opposite of divining for wealth, where Wealth and Output are auspicious. With the Pressure line holding the Self and Wealth giving it life, Wealth gives life to Pressure, so Wealth is not taboo.
