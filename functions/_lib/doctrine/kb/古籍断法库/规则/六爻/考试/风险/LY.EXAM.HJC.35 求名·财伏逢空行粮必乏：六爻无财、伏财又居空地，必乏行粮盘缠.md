# 求名·财伏逢空行粮必乏：六爻无财、伏财又居空地，必乏行粮盘缠

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻伏藏又逢空，路上的口粮必定不足：六爻里没有财爻，伏着的财又落在空地，必定缺少行粮、盘缠。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth lies hidden and meets Void, the travelling provisions will surely run short: with no Wealth among the six lines and the hidden Wealth in a Void place, provisions and travelling expenses will surely fall short.
