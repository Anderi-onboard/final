# 求名·妻财助鬼父爻空可图侥幸：父母空亡而财爻发动生扶官鬼侥幸可成；财官两动而父爻旬空反不宜用，父爻不空可望

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻空亡，如果财爻发动来生扶官鬼，侥幸可以成；如果财、官两爻都动而父爻在旬空里，反而不宜用这个说法，父爻不空才有希望。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line is Void but the Wealth line moves to give life and support to Pressure, one may succeed by luck; if Wealth and Pressure both move and the Resource line is in the Void, this saying must not be used, and there is hope only if the Resource line is not Void.

## 《黄金策·求名》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1882 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a name", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻财助鬼父爻空， | 妻财帮助官鬼、而父爻落空， | If Wealth helps Pressure while the Resource line is Void, |
> | 可图侥幸 | 可以图个侥幸 | one may try for luck |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1882 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父母空亡，若得财爻发动生扶官鬼，侥幸可成； | 父母爻空亡，如果财爻发动来生扶官鬼，侥幸可成； | If the Resource line is Void but the Wealth line moves to give life and support to Pressure, luck may bring success; |
> | 若财官两动而父爻旬空，反不宜用，父爻不空可望。 | 如果财、官两爻都动而父爻旬空，反而不宜用这个说法，父爻不空才有希望。 | If Wealth and Pressure both move and the Resource line is in the Void, this saying must not be used; there is hope only if the Resource line is not Void. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一联是上一联「财若交重」的例外：一般财克父，但父爻已经空了、文章靠不住时，反要借财去生官，所以说「可图侥幸」；注里又把这个例外收紧（财官两动而父旬空，又不宜）。
>
> *Note:* This couplet is an exception to the earlier "if Wealth moves": ordinarily Wealth controls Resource, but when the Resource line is already Void and the writing cannot be relied on, one borrows Wealth to give life to Pressure, hence "one may try for luck"; the commentary then tightens the exception (when Wealth and Pressure both move and the Resource line is in the Void it is again unsuitable).
