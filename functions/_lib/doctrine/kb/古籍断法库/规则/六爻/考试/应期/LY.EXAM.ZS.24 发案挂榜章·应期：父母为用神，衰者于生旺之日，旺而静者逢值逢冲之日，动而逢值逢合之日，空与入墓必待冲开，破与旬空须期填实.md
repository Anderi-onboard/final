# 发案挂榜章·应期：父母为用神，衰者于生旺之日，旺而静者逢值逢冲之日，动而逢值逢合之日，空与入墓必待冲开，破与旬空须期填实

六爻 · 考试 · 应期 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占考试结果几时公布（发案、挂榜），以父母爻为用神：衰的，应在它得生、得旺的日子；旺而静的，应在值日或冲日；动的，应在值日或合日；落空和入墓的，要等冲开；被破和旬空的，要等填实。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine when the result will be issued and the board posted, the Resource line is the Subject: if weak, it answers on the day that gives it life or strength; if strong and still, on the day of its own branch or the day that clashes it; if moving, on the day of its own branch or the day that combines with it; if Void or in the tomb, one must wait until it is clashed open; if broken or in the Void, one must wait until it is filled.

## 《增删卜易·发案挂榜章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10092 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on issuing the list and posting the board", base text

> | 旺而静者，逢值逢冲之日； | 旺而静的，在逢值、逢冲的日子； | if strong and still, on the day when it is met by its own branch or clashed; |
> | 动而逢值逢合之日； | 动的，在逢值、逢合的日子； | if moving, on the day when it is met by its own branch or combined; |
> | 空与入墓，必待冲开； | 落空和入墓的，一定要等冲开； | if Void or in the tomb, one must wait until it is clashed open; |
> | 破与旬空，须期填实。 | 被破和旬空的，要等到填实。 | if broken or in the Void, one must wait until it is filled. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「发案」是考官公布取中的名单（案），「挂榜」是把榜张挂出来。「值」是爻的地支恰好是当日的地支；「填实」是等到落空的地支那一日。这一章后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* "Issuing the list" is the examiner publishing the list of those taken, and "posting the board" is hanging the board up. "Meeting its own branch" is the day whose branch is the same as the line's branch; "filled" means waiting for the day whose branch is the one that is Void. The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
