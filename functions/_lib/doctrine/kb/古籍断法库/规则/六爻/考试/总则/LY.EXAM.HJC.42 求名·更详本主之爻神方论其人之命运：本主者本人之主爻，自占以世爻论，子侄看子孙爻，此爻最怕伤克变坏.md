# 求名·更详本主之爻神方论其人之命运：本主者本人之主爻，自占以世爻论，子侄看子孙爻，此爻最怕伤克变坏

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 再要看本主的爻神，才能论这个人的命运：本主是本人的主爻，自己占以世爻论，子侄占看子孙爻等，这一爻最怕被伤克、变坏。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Study further the line that is the principal of the person, and then one may judge his fortune: the principal is the person's own chief line, which for oneself is the Self, and for a son or nephew the Output line, and so on; this line most fears being harmed, controlled or changed for the worse.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1920 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 本主者，本人之主爻也，自占以世爻论，如子侄看子孙爻类，此爻最怕伤克变坏。 | 本主，就是本人的主爻：自己占以世爻来论，比如子侄看子孙爻等，这一爻最怕被伤克、变坏。 | The principal is the person's own chief line: for oneself it is taken as the Self, and for a son or nephew one looks at the Output line, and so on; this line most fears being harmed, controlled or changed for the worse. |
> | 如此搜索吉凶自应。 | 这样搜寻，吉凶自然就会应验。 | Searching in this way, the auspicious and inauspicious will answer of themselves. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一联是整章的收束：前面讲官、父，这里提醒：占的是谁（自己、子侄），就要看谁的主爻，不能只看官父。「本主」就是本人。
>
> *Note:* This couplet closes the chapter: the earlier lines speak of Pressure and Resource, and this reminds one that whoever it is that is divined for (oneself, a son or nephew), that person's own chief line must be looked at, and one cannot look only at Pressure and Resource. The "principal" is the person himself.
