# 学业章·九流、百工：财兴官旺则道重人钦，兄动世衰则有毁无誉；百工以财为用神、子孙为元神，最忌兄爻持世；天师驱邪治鬼之家反以子孙持世为吉（觉子）

六爻 · 考试 · 学业 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 学九流技艺要财爻兴旺、官爻旺相；学百工手艺以财爻为用神、子孙爻为元神，最忌兄弟持世或旺而发动；觉子补充：天师、驱邪治鬼的、师巫和医家，反而要子孙持世或发动才吉，因为靠子孙制鬼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For learning the nine schools of skill, Wealth should be thriving and Pressure strong; for learning a craft, Wealth is the Subject and Output the Support, and what is most feared is Peer holding the Self or being strong and moving; Juezi adds that for Heavenly Masters, exorcists, shamans and physicians, it is the other way round: Output holding the Self or moving is auspicious, since they control ghosts by Output.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 九流者，财兴官旺，道重人钦； | 学九流技艺的，财爻兴旺、官爻旺相，所学的道就受人看重、人人佩服； | For the nine schools, with Wealth thriving and Pressure strong, one's way is valued and people admire one; |
> | 兄动世衰，有毁无誉。 | 兄弟动而世爻衰，只有诋毁、没有赞誉。 | if Peer moves and the Self is weak, there is censure and no praise. |

> | 兄动卦中，兄爻持世，只可糊口，难许成家。 | 兄弟在卦中发动，或者兄弟爻持世，只能勉强糊口，难以成家立业。 | if Peer moves in the hexagram, or the Peer line holds the Self, one can only just make a living and can hardly set up a household. |

## 《增删卜易·学业章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9815 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on study", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 百工皆以财为用神，子孙为元神，最忌兄爻持世，及兄爻旺动者，劳碌终身。 | 各种工匠都以财爻为用神、子孙爻为元神，最忌兄弟爻持世，以及兄弟爻旺而发动，会劳碌一生。 | For all the crafts, Wealth is the Subject and Output is the Support; what is most feared is the Peer line holding the Self, or the Peer line strong and moving, and one toils all one's life. |
> | 余以求财章同断。 | 我用求财章的断法一并来断。 | I judge them together by the rules of the chapter on seeking wealth. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 九流是各种技艺、行当（书里没有列名单），百工是各行手艺人。觉子这一条是纠正「九流皆以官鬼为吉」：靠制服鬼神吃饭的人，官鬼是他要制的东西，所以反以子孙为吉。「余以求财章同断」的「余」是野鹤老人。
>
> *Note:* The "nine schools" are the various skills and trades (the book gives no list) and the "hundred crafts" are the manual trades. Juezi's remark corrects the claim that Pressure is auspicious for all the nine schools: for those who live by subduing ghosts and spirits, Pressure is what they are to subdue, so Output is auspicious for them instead. The "I" in "I judge them together by the chapter on seeking wealth" is the Old Man of Yehe.
