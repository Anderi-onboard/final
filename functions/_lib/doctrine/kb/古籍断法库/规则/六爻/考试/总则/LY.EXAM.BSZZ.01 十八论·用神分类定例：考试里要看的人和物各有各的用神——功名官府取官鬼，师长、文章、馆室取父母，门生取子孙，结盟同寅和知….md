# 十八论·用神分类定例：考试里要看的人和物各有各的用神——功名官府取官鬼，师长、文章、馆室取父母，门生取子孙，结盟同寅和知交朋友取兄弟

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 考试这件事里，先要定下来看哪一爻：问功名（考中、做官），用神是官鬼爻；问老师、文章、奏章、读书的馆舍，用神是父母爻；问学生（门生），用神是子孙爻；问结拜兄弟、同僚和知交朋友，用神是兄弟爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In an examination reading one first settles which line to watch: for success in the examinations and in office the Subject is the Pressure line; for a teacher, an essay, a memorial or the study hall the Subject is the Resource line; for pupils the Subject is the Output line; for sworn brothers, colleagues and close friends the Subject is the Peer line.

## 《卜筮正宗·十八论·一、用神分类定例》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 949 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Eighteen treatises, no. 1: the standard assignment of the Subject", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占功名、官府、雷电、鬼神、丈夫、夫之兄弟同辈及夫之相与朋友，乱臣、盗贼、邪祟、忧疑、病症、尸首、逆风，俱以官鬼爻为用神。 | 凡是占问功名、官府、雷电、鬼神、丈夫、丈夫的同辈兄弟和丈夫相交的朋友，以及乱臣、盗贼、邪祟、忧虑疑难、病症、尸首、逆风，全都以官鬼爻为用神。 | Whenever one asks about success in the examinations and in office, government offices, thunder and lightning, ghosts and spirits, a husband, the brothers of the husband's own generation and the friends he keeps, or about rebellious ministers, thieves and bandits, evil hauntings, worry and doubt, illness, a corpse or an adverse wind, the Pressure line is in every case the Subject. |

## 《卜筮正宗·十八论·一、用神分类定例》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 950 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Eighteen treatises, no. 1: the standard assignment of the Subject", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占兄弟姊妹、姊妹丈夫、妻之兄弟、世兄弟、结盟同寅及知交朋友，俱以兄弟爻为用神。 | 凡是占问兄弟姊妹、姊妹的丈夫、妻子的兄弟、世交的兄弟、结盟的同僚和知交朋友，全都以兄弟爻为用神。 | Whenever one asks about brothers and sisters, a sister's husband, a wife's brothers, brothers by old family ties, sworn colleagues and close friends, the Peer line is in every case the Subject. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 同一节原文还把嫂子、弟媳、妻妾、朋友的妻妾和仆婢、物价、钱财、珠宝、金银、仓库、钱粮、杂物、器皿和天晴都归给妻财爻，考试里用不上，这里没有收；妻财爻在考试里是父母（文章）的克神，见 LY.EXAM.HJC.17。
>
> *Note:* The same section also gives sisters-in-law, wives and concubines, the wives, concubines and servants of friends, prices, money, jewels, gold and silver, treasuries, stores, miscellaneous goods, utensils and clear weather to the Wealth line; none of this is used in examinations and it is not included. In an examination the Wealth line is the controller of the Resource line (the essay): see LY.EXAM.HJC.17.
