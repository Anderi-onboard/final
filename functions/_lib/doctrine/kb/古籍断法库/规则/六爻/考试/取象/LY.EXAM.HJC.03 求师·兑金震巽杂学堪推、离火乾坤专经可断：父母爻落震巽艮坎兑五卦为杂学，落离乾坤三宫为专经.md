# 求师·兑金震巽杂学堪推、离火乾坤专经可断：父母爻落震巽艮坎兑五卦为杂学，落离乾坤三宫为专经

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 推断老师是专治一经还是学问杂：父母爻落在震、巽、艮、坎、兑五卦里的，是杂学；落在离、乾、坤三宫里的，是专经。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To judge whether a teacher specialises in one classic or has a miscellaneous learning: a Resource line in the five trigrams Zhen, Xun, Gen, Kan and Dui means miscellaneous learning; in the three palaces Li, Qian and Kun it means a single classic.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1821–1822 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡推师之专经杂学者，当以父母在震、巽、艮、坎、兑五卦为杂学，离、乾、坤三宫为专经。 | 凡是推断老师是专治一经还是杂学的，应当以父母爻落在震、巽、艮、坎、兑五卦的为杂学，落在离、乾、坤三宫的为专经。 | In judging whether a teacher is a specialist in one classic or of miscellaneous learning, take the Resource line in the five trigrams Zhen, Xun, Gen, Kan and Dui as miscellaneous learning, and in the three palaces Li, Qian and Kun as a single classic. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 经文只点了兑、震巽、离、乾坤，注把它补全成五卦（震巽艮坎兑）对三宫（离乾坤）。「专经」是一生专治一部经，「杂学」是各样学问都涉及。
>
> *Note:* The verse names only Dui, Zhen-Xun, Li and Qian-Kun, and the commentary fills it out into five trigrams (Zhen, Xun, Gen, Kan, Dui) against three palaces (Li, Qian, Kun). A "single classic" is a lifelong specialisation in one classic and "miscellaneous learning" is a spread across many kinds of learning.
