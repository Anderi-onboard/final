# 求名·父官三合相逢连科及第：卦有三合会成官局必连科及第，会成父局亦吉

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻、官星三合相遇，接连考中：卦中三合会成官局，必定接连及第，会成父局也吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line and the official star meet in a three-harmony one passes in successive examinations: a three-harmony forming a Pressure frame in the hexagram means one will surely pass in successive examinations, and forming a Resource frame is also auspicious.
