# 求名·月克文书程式背而不中：父旺得动爻日辰生合文字字锦绣，妻财伤克必多破绽，月建冲克文必不中试官之程式

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建克文书（父爻），文章违背程式，考不中：父爻旺又得动爻、日辰生合，文章字字锦绣；妻财伤克，必多破绽；月建冲克，文章必不合考官的程式。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Month controls the document line, the essay goes against the model form and is not chosen: with the Resource line strong and given life and combination by a moving line or the Day, the essay is brocade in every word; if Wealth harms and controls it, there are many flaws; if the Month clashes and controls it, the essay will surely not fit the examiner's model form.

> [!note] 按
> 「程式」是科举文章的规定格式（八股）；增删卜易岁考科考章里讲文章好坏也是看父爻（LY.EXAM.ZS.18）。
>
> *Note:* The "model form" is the prescribed form of an examination essay (the eight-legged essay); the Zengshan Buyi chapter on the annual and qualifying examinations also judges the quality of the writing by the Resource line (LY.EXAM.ZS.18).
