# 求师·母化子孙必主能诗能赋（父化福善作杂文，带刑害病败虽能作文多破绽，子带月建加青龙出口成章）；鬼连兄煞定然多诈多奸（兄动化鬼、鬼动化兄皆奸诈）；口是心非临空亡而发动（用爻宜静不宜动、宜旺不宜空）

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻化子孙，老师能诗能赋、善作杂文，带刑害病败的文章多破绽，子孙带月建加青龙则出口成章；兄动化鬼、鬼动化兄，老师多诈多奸，刑克世爻有是非口舌；用爻临空亡而发动，口是心非：动而空不诚实，静而空懒教训，化空也一样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line changes into Output the teacher can write poems and rhapsodies and is good at miscellaneous prose, though if it carries punishment, harm, sickness or decay his writing is full of flaws, and if Output carries the Month and the Azure Dragon he speaks in finished prose; if Peer moves and changes into Pressure, or Pressure moves and changes into Peer, the teacher is deceitful and cunning, and punishing and controlling the Self means quarrels and gossip; if the Subject line stands on Void and moves, he says one thing and means another: moving and Void is insincere, still and Void is lazy at teaching, and the same if it changes into Void.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1840 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用爻宜静不宜动，宜旺不宜空，动空不诚实，静空懒教训，化空亦然。 | 用爻宜静不宜动，宜旺不宜空；动而空的不诚实，静而空的懒于教训，变空也是这样。 | The Subject line should be still and not moving, strong and not Void; moving and Void means he is not sincere, still and Void means he is lazy at instruction, and the same if it changes into Void. |

> [!note] 按
> 「福」是福神（子孙爻）。「小儿带来」是老师带着自己的小孩一起来。
>
> *Note:* The "spirit of blessing" is the Output line. "Bring a small child along" means the teacher brings his own young child with him.
