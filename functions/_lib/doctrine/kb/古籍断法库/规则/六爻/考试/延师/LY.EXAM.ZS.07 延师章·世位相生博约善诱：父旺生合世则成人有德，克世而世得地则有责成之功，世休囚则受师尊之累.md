# 延师章·世位相生博约善诱：父旺生合世则成人有德，克世而世得地则有责成之功，世休囚则受师尊之累

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻和父爻相生，老师博学守约、善于诱导；父旺生合世，能造就人又有德行；父克世但世爻得地，严教也能成才；世爻休囚，会受老师的拖累。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Self and the Resource line give each other life, the teacher is learned, restrained and good at drawing one on; if the strong Resource line gives life to or combines with the Self he makes men of his pupils and is virtuous; if it controls the Self but the Self is well placed, his stern demands still bring results; if the Self is resting and trapped, one will be burdened by the teacher.

## 《增删卜易·延师章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9892 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on engaging a teacher", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世位相生，博约善诱。 | 世爻和父爻相生，老师博学守约，善于诱导。 | When the Self and the teacher's line give each other life, the teacher is learned and restrained and skilled at drawing one on. |
> | 父旺生合世爻，成人有德； | 父旺生合世爻，能造就人、有德行； | If the Resource line is strong and gives life to or combines with the Self, he makes men of his pupils and is virtuous; |
> | 若克世爻，而世爻得地者，有责成人之功。 | 如果克世爻，而世爻得地的，有严格责成而成就人的功劳。 | if it controls the Self but the Self is well placed, he has the merit of making men through stern demands; |
> | 世若休囚，必受师尊之累。 | 世爻如果休囚，必定受老师的拖累。 | if the Self is resting and trapped, one will surely be burdened by the teacher. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「博约」出自《论语》的「博我以文，约我以礼」，意思是学问广博又守规矩；「世位相生」指世爻和父爻（老师）互相生。
>
> *Note:* "Learned and restrained" comes from the Analects, "he broadened me with letters and restrained me with ritual", meaning broad learning together with discipline; "the Self and the teacher's line give each other life" means the Self and the Resource line (the teacher) give life to each other.
