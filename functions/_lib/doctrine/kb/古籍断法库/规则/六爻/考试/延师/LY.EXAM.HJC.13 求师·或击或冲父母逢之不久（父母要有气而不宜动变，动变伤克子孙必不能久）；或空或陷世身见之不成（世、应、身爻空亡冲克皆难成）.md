# 求师·或击或冲父母逢之不久（父母要有气而不宜动变，动变伤克子孙必不能久）；或空或陷世身见之不成（世、应、身爻空亡冲克皆难成）

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻被击被冲，老师做不长久：父母爻要有气，但不宜动变，动变就伤克子孙，必不能久；世爻、卦身遇空或陷，事不成：世、应、身爻空亡、被冲克，都是难成的象。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line is struck or clashed the teacher does not last: the Resource line must have breath but should not move or change, for moving or changing it harms and controls Output and it cannot last; if the Self or the Body meets Void or sinks, it does not come about: the Self, the Other and the Body line being Void, clashed or controlled are all signs of difficulty.
