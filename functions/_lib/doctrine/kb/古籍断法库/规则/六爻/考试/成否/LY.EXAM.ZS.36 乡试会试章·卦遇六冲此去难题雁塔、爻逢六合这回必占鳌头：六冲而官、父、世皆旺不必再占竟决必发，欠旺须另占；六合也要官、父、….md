# 乡试会试章·卦遇六冲此去难题雁塔、爻逢六合这回必占鳌头：六冲而官、父、世皆旺不必再占竟决必发，欠旺须另占；六合也要官、父、世得地（野鹤；附例：兑卦再占临之师，果中式）

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占得六冲卦，难以题名；占得六合卦，必占鳌头。但要看实际：六冲而官、父、世都旺，不必再占，断必发；欠旺的，另占一卦，再得吉按吉断、得凶按凶断；六合卦也要官、父、世都得地，有一个失陷又没有生扶，六合也没有益处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Six-Clash hexagram means it is hard to inscribe one's name; a Six-Combine hexagram means one will surely take first place. But it depends: if a Six-Clash has the official star, Resource and Self all strong, there is no need to divine again and one judges that one will succeed; if one is lacking in strength, divine again, and judge by the second hexagram, auspicious as auspicious and ill as ill; a Six-Combine hexagram also needs the official star, Resource and Self all well placed, and if one of them is lost with no life or support, even a Six-Combine is of no use.

> | 如欠旺者，须宜另占，再得吉者，以吉断； | 如果欠旺，就要另外再占，再得吉的，按吉断； | If one is lacking in strength, one should divine again, and if the next is auspicious, judge it auspicious; |
> | 再得凶者，以凶推。 | 再得凶的，按凶推。 | if the next is ill, judge it ill. |
> | 凡得六合卦，亦要官、父、世爻得地，有一失陷，不遇生扶者，即六合亦无益矣。 | 凡是得六合卦，也要官星、父爻、世爻都得地，有一个失陷、又得不到生扶的，就是六合也没有益处。 | When a Six-Combine hexagram is obtained, the official star, the Resource line and the Self must also all be well placed; if one of them is lost and gets no life or support, even a Six-Combine is of no use. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 这两句「卦遇六冲……爻逢六合……」本是黄金策求名章的句子。「雁塔题名」是唐代进士在大雁塔上题名，「占鳌头」是殿试第一名站在殿前石阶上鳌头的位置。旧注说六冲「冲之即散」，野鹤的看法是要看官、父、世旺不旺。这一条和 LY.EXAM.HJC 求名章同一联对看。
>
> *Note:* These two lines, "if the hexagram meets a Six-Clash ... if the lines meet a Six-Combine ...", come from the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a name. "Inscribing one's name at the Wild Goose Pagoda" is the Tang custom of successful candidates inscribing their names on the Big Wild Goose Pagoda, and "taking the turtle's head" is the first-place man standing on the carved turtle at the head of the palace steps. The old note says a Six-Clash "scatters at the clash"; the Old Man of Yehe's view is to look at whether the official star, Resource and Self are strong. Read together with the same couplet in the Huangjin Ce chapter on seeking a name (LY.EXAM.HJC).
