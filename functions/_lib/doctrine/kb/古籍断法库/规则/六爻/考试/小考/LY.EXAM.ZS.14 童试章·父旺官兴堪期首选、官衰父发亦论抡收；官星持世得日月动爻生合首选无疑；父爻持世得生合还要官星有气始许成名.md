# 童试章·父旺官兴堪期首选、官衰父发亦论抡收；官星持世得日月动爻生合首选无疑；父爻持世得生合还要官星有气始许成名

六爻 · 考试 · 小考 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 童试：父爻旺、官星兴，可期名列榜首；官衰而父爻发动，也算能被选中；官星持世，得日月动爻相生相合，首选没有疑问；父爻持世得生合，一定要官星有气，才许成名。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the youth examinations: with the Resource line strong and the official star thriving one may hope to be first; with the official star weak but the Resource line moving one is still counted as selected; with the official star holding the Self and the Day, the Month and moving lines giving it life and combining with it, being first is beyond doubt; with the Resource line holding the Self and given life and combination, the official star must also have breath before one may win a name.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「童试」是考秀才之前的各级考试（县试、府试、院试）；「首选」「抡收」是被选中、取中。官星就是官鬼。
>
> *Note:* The "youth examinations" are the examinations before the one that makes a licentiate; "chosen first" and "selected" mean being picked and admitted. The official star is the Pressure line.
