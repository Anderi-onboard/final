# 进神退神章：喜者宜化进神、忌者宜化退神，进神八对、退神八对；例：乡试官星持世、五爻官化进神，今秋折桂、明春联捷；乡试世爻未土化进神、巳火官动生世，揭榜中第八名

六爻 · 考试 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动爻变出来的爻是进神或退神，吉凶要分喜忌：所喜的宜化进神，所忌的宜化退神。进神有八对：亥化子、寅化卯、巳化午、申化酉、丑化辰、辰化未、未化戌、戌化丑，退神是它们的反方向；进神像春天的树木越长越旺、像有源头的水，有长久的样子，退神像秋天的花木渐渐凋零。书中例一：申月癸卯日占乡试，得恒卦变大过卦，酉金官星持世、旺相当令，卯日冲动它，又有第五爻官爻化进神扶助，断为今秋折桂、明春占鳌头，后来果然联捷。例二：酉月乙丑日占乡试中不中，得兑卦变讼卦，世爻未土化进神、巳火官爻发动生世，吉兆分明，断为坐等报喜，果然戊寅日揭榜中第八名；书上说古法认为动爻遇破、遇散、变到日月上还不能进，这个卦动爻散了又化空，也照样考中。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A line that comes out of a moving line when it changes is an Advancing or Retreating spirit, and its luck divides into the welcome and the feared: what is welcome is better changing into an Advancing spirit, and what is feared is better changing into a Retreating spirit. There are eight pairs of Advancing spirits (Hai into Zi, Yin into Mao, Si into Wu, Shen into You, Chou into Chen, Chen into Wei, Wei into Xu, Xu into Chou), and the Retreating spirits are their reverse; an Advancing spirit is like spring trees coming into leaf or water that has a source, the look of what lasts long, and a Retreating spirit is like flowers and trees in autumn withering little by little. First case: on a Gui-Mao day in the Shen month a man divined the provincial examination and got Heng changing to Daguo; the Pressure star in You Metal held the Self, strong and in command, and was stirred by the clash of the Mao day, and the fifth line's Pressure changing into an Advancing spirit supported it, so the judgement was that he would break the laurel that autumn and take first place the next spring, and he indeed passed in consecutive rounds. Second case: on a Yi-Chou day in the You month a man divined whether he would pass the provincial examination and got Dui changing to Song; Wei Earth at the Self changed into an Advancing spirit and the Si Fire Pressure line moved to give life to the Self, an omen plainly good, so the judgement was to sit and wait for the news, and the list was indeed posted on a Wu-Yin day with him at number eight; the book says that by the old rule a moving line that meets a break or a scattering, or changes onto the Day or Month, still cannot advance, yet here the moving line was scattered and changed into Void and he passed all the same.

> [!note] 按
> 「折桂」「占鳌头」「联捷」的意思见 LY.EXAM.BSZZ.07 的注；两个卦例的卦图都不收，第二个例子的开头一句在卦图之前。第二个例子里「动破散化日月，尚不能进」文字简略，大意是：动爻遇到破、被冲散，或变出的爻落在日月的空破上，古法说还不能算进神；译文按这个大意翻，是按上下文的理解，书里没有再解释。进神八对里的「戍化丑」「丑化戍」，书里把「戌」写成了「戍」，原文照录。「乡试」之例与《卜筮正宗》第十问是同一个卦例（LY.EXAM.BSZZ.07），两书的断语文字不同。
>
> *Note:* For "breaking the laurel", "taking first place" and "consecutive success" see the note at LY.EXAM.BSZZ.07; the hexagram diagrams of the two cases are not included, and the second case's opening sentence comes before its diagram. The terse phrase in the second case, "moving, broken, scattered, changing into Day or Month, still cannot advance", means in sense that a moving line which meets a break or is scattered by a clash, or whose changed line falls on a Void or break of the Day or Month, was held by the old rule not yet to count as Advancing; the translation follows this reading, taken from the context, and the book does not explain it further. In the list of eight pairs the text writes "Xu" with a wrong character in two places and is reproduced as it stands. The first case is the same hexagram as the one in the Bushi Zhengzong's tenth question (LY.EXAM.BSZZ.07), though the wording of the judgement differs.
