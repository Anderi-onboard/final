# 求名·父爻旺相其文掷地金声、鬼位兴隆家报泥金捷喜：凡占功名以父爻为文章、鬼为官职，二者一卦之主，伤一则不成

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占功名，父爻是文章，官鬼是官职，这两样是一卦的主体，伤了一样就不成；父爻旺相文章必好，官鬼得地功名有望，泥金报喜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for fame and rank, the Resource line is the writing and Pressure the post; these two are the masters of the hexagram, and if either is harmed it will not succeed; with the Resource line thriving the writing is surely fine, and with Pressure well placed fame and rank are within hope and the gilt-ink news of joy comes.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1864–1866 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡占功名，以父爻为文章，鬼为官职，二者一卦之主，伤一则不成。 | 凡是占功名，以父爻为文章，官鬼为官职，这两个是一卦的主体，伤了一个就不成。 | In divining for fame and rank, the Resource line is the writing and Pressure is the post; these two are the masters of the hexagram, and if either is harmed it will not succeed. |
> | 若父爻旺相，文章必佳，官鬼得地，功名有望，泥金报喜。 | 如果父爻旺相，文章必定好；官鬼得地，功名有望，泥金报喜。 | If the Resource line is thriving, the writing is surely fine; if Pressure is well placed, fame and rank are within hope and the gilt-ink news of joy comes. |
> | 总言金榜题名，功成名就之意，非以鬼为音信也。 | 这是总的说金榜题名、功成名就的意思，不是拿官鬼当作音信。 | This speaks in general of one's name on the Golden List and success in fame and rank, and does not take Pressure as news. |
> | 学者志之。 | 学的人要记住。 | Students should remember it. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「泥金」是用金粉写的捷报（旧时中举后把泥金帖子寄回家）；注特别说「不是拿鬼当音信」，是怕读的人把「鬼」当作报信的人。这一章是求名（应考成名）的总纲，下面各条逐联细讲。增删卜易的乡试会试章（LY.EXAM.ZS.27）是同一个意思的另一种写法。
>
> *Note:* "Gilt ink" is gold-powder ink used for the victory notice (after passing, a gilt-ink card was sent home); the commentary stresses that Pressure is not taken as a bearer of news, lest the reader think the word "ghost" stands for the messenger. This chapter opens the whole of seeking a name (taking examinations to win fame); the couplets below take it up one by one. The chapter of the Zengshan Buyi on the provincial and metropolitan examinations (LY.EXAM.ZS.27) says the same thing in other words.
