# 延师章·父入墓中懒于教训，财临世上懦弱不严

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻入墓或临绝，不是老师偷安懒惰，就是老师不肯教；财爻持世，是我克了父爻，不是老师教得不严，就是老师怕被我欺凌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line enters the tomb or rests on extinction, then unless the teacher is idly seeking ease he will not teach; if Wealth holds the Self, I control the Resource line, and if the teacher is not strict it is because he fears being bullied by me.

## 《增删卜易·延师章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9894 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on engaging a teacher", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父入墓中，懒于教训。 | 父爻入墓，老师懒于教导训诫。 | If the Resource line enters the tomb, the teacher is lazy about teaching and admonishing. |
> | 父爻入三墓，及临绝者，若非偷安懒惰，定是师不授教。 | 父爻入了三墓，以及临在绝地的，如果不是老师偷安懒惰，就一定是老师不肯传授教导。 | If the Resource line enters one of the three tombs, or rests on the extinction stage, then unless the teacher is idly seeking ease he surely will not teach. |
> | 财临世上，懦弱不严。 | 财爻临在世爻上，老师软弱不严厉。 | If Wealth stands on the Self, the teacher is weak and not strict. |
> | 财爻持世乃我克父母，若非师教不严，定是我师畏凌。 | 财爻持世就是我克父母爻；如果不是老师教得不严，就一定是老师怕被我欺凌。 | If the Wealth line holds the Self, it is I who control the Resource line; if the teacher is not strict, he is surely afraid of being bullied by me. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「三墓」指五行入墓的三种（火墓戌、金墓丑、木墓未，水土墓辰）；书里没有在这里列出。
>
> *Note:* The "three tombs" are the tombs of the five phases (Fire at Xu, Metal at Chou, Wood at Wei, Water and Earth at Chen); the book does not list them here.
