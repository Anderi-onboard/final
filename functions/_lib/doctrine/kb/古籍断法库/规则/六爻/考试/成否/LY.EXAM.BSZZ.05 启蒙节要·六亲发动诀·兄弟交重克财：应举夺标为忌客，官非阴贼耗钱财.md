# 启蒙节要·六亲发动诀·兄弟交重克财：应举夺标为忌客，官非阴贼耗钱财

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻发动、克住财爻时：病人难好，灾还没有走；应举时，同来夺魁首的人是最要提防的客人；官司和暗中的盗贼会耗损钱财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Peer line moves and controls Wealth: the sick are hard to cure and the calamity has not left; in sitting the examinations, those who contend for the top place are the guests most to be avoided; and lawsuits and secret thieves waste money.

> [!warning] 打架
> 诀文把兄弟发动克财看成应举的大忌。《增删卜易》（LY.EXAM.ZS.28）却说兄爻持世或发动不是一概不吉：财、父两爻都动时，兄动制住财反而是喜；父、官两动时，喜兄弟持世。《黄金策》（LY.EXAM.HJC.18）和这句诀一致，把兄弟看成同经的竞争者，必夺魁首。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The verse makes a moving Peer line that controls Wealth a great taboo for sitting the examinations. The Zengshan Buyi (LY.EXAM.ZS.28) says that Peer holding the Self or moving is not ill throughout: when Wealth and Resource both move, Peer moving to control Wealth is on the contrary welcome, and when Resource and Pressure both move, Peer holding the Self is welcome. The Huangjin Ce (LY.EXAM.HJC.18) agrees with this verse in treating Peer as rivals who share one's classic and will snatch first place.
