# 求名·福德变官身位合亦忝科名：正卦无官而子孙变出官鬼、与世身生合、文书有气，功名有望但不能高中

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本卦没有官鬼，如果子孙变出官鬼，和世爻、卦身生合，文书有气，功名有望，但不能高中。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the original hexagram has no Pressure but Output changes into Pressure and gives life to or combines with the Self and Body, and the document line has breath, fame and rank are within hope, but one cannot pass high.

## 《黄金策·求名》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1884 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a name", base text

> | 福德变官身位合， | 福德（子孙）变出官爻，和世身相合， | If Output changes into the official star and combines with the Self and Body, |
> | 亦忝科名 | 也能勉强玷列科名 | one may also unworthily share the roll of fame |
