# 乡试会试章·父旺官兴姓标虎榜、父官持世旺相得生扶名登金榜；子摇财动名落孙山、子孙财爻持世或动则下第

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 乡试、会试：父爻旺、官星兴，名上虎榜；父爻或官爻持世而旺相，或临日月，或得日月动爻生扶，或动而变吉，名登金榜。子孙动、财爻动，或子孙、财爻持世、在卦中发动，一定落第。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the provincial and metropolitan examinations: with the Resource line strong and the official star thriving one's name goes on the Tiger List; if the Resource or Pressure line holds the Self and is thriving, or stands on the Day or the Month, or is given life and support by the Day, the Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into an auspicious line, one's name appears on the Golden List. If Output stirs and Wealth moves, or Output or Wealth holds the Self or moves in the hexagram, one will surely fail.

> | 子孙财爻持世，及动于卦中，定然下第。 | 子孙、财爻持世，以及在卦中发动，一定落第。 | If Output or Wealth holds the Self, or moves in the hexagram, one will surely fail. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「虎榜」「金榜」都是登科的榜；「名落孙山」是考不上（孙山是榜上最后一名，名字落在他后面就是没考上）。官星就是官鬼。这一章后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* The "Tiger List" and "Golden List" are both lists of those who passed; "one's name falls below Sun Shan" means failing (Sun Shan was the last name on a list, and to fall below him is to miss it). The official star is the Pressure line. The [Qian's note] after the chapter is not quoted.
