# 启蒙节要·六亲发动诀·父母发动：父动当头克子孙，士人科举登金榜

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻（文书）发动，当头克制子孙时，读书人应科举能登上金榜。同一诀里还说，父母发动时，等远行的人、盼书信会有动静；打官司下状子，先把是非分清；失物、逃亡要靠告状评理才能了结。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Resource line (the document line) moves and squarely controls Output, a scholar sitting the civil examinations is placed on the golden list. The same verse adds that with Resource moving, a traveller or a letter being waited for shows movement, a plaint filed in a lawsuit first sorts out right from wrong, and lost goods or a runaway have to be pleaded for in court.

## 《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·四十六、六亲发动诀》原文

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 观望行人书信动， | 等候远行的人、盼书信，都有动静； | for a traveller or a letter being waited for, there is movement; |
> | 论官下状理先分， | 说到官司，下状子，先把是非分清； | in a lawsuit, when the plaint is filed, right and wrong are sorted out first; |

> [!note] 按
> 这一诀中间三句（病人无药、婚姻子息、买卖劳心）说的是别的事，这里没有收。
>
> *Note:* The three lines in the middle of this verse (the sick without medicine, marriage and offspring, trade that wears out the mind) speak of other matters and are not included here.
