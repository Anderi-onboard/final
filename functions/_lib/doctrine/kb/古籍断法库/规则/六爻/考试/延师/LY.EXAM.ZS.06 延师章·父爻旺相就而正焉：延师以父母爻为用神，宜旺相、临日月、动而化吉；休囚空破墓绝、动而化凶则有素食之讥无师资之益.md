# 延师章·父爻旺相就而正焉：延师以父母爻为用神，宜旺相、临日月、动而化吉；休囚空破墓绝、动而化凶则有素食之讥无师资之益

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 请老师教学，以父母爻为用神：父母爻旺相、临日月、动而变吉，老师可做楷模；休囚、落空被破、入墓绝、动而变凶，老师徒有虚名，得不到益处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In engaging a teacher, the Resource line is the Subject: if it is thriving, stands on the Day or the Month, or moves and changes into an auspicious line, the teacher can be a model; if it is resting and trapped, Void or broken, in the tomb or extinct, or moves and changes into an ill line, he is a teacher in name only and there is no benefit.

## 《增删卜易·延师章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9891 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on engaging a teacher", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父爻旺相，就而正焉。 | 父爻旺相，就可以向他求教、得到匡正。 | If the Resource line is thriving, one may go to him and be set right. |
> | 父母爻，既为师长，又为诗书。 | 父母爻既代表老师，又代表诗书。 | The Resource line stands both for the teacher and for the books of poetry and history. |
> | 延师受教，即以此爻为用神。 | 请老师来教，就以这一爻为用神。 | In engaging a teacher for instruction, take this line as the Subject. |
> | 宜旺相，或临日月，及动而化吉，可式可矜。 | 宜旺相，或临日月，以及发动变出吉象，就可以做楷模、值得敬重。 | It should be thriving, or stand on the Day or the Month, or move and change into an auspicious line; then he can be a model and be respected. |
> | 如若休囚、空破、墓绝、及动而化凶，有素食之讥，无师资之益。 | 如果休囚、落空被破、入墓或绝、以及发动变凶，就会被讥为白吃饭，得不到老师的益处。 | If it is resting and trapped, Void or broken, in a tomb or extinct, or moves and changes into an ill line, he is mocked as eating the bread of idleness, and there is no benefit from the teacher. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「素食之讥」是「尸位素餐」的意思，白吃饭不做事；「师资之益」是从老师那里得到的帮助。
>
> *Note:* "Mocked as eating the bread of idleness" means drawing a salary and doing nothing; "the benefit of a teacher" is the help one gets from the teacher.
