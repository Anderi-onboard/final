# 求名·喜添场屋有情龙合于身爻：大象既吉更得青龙动生合世身，不但名成，必然别有喜事；空动则出空之月日见喜

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 大局已吉，再得青龙动来生合世爻和卦身，不但成名，必定另有喜事；如果是空而动，出空的月、日有喜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the overall figure is already auspicious and the Azure Dragon moves to give life to and combine with the Self and Body, not only will one win a name but there will surely be other joy besides; if it is Void and moving, the joy comes in the month or day it leaves the Void.

> | 龙合于身爻 | 青龙合在身爻上 | if the Azure Dragon combines with the Body line |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1902 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若大象既吉，更得龙动生合世身，不但名成，必然别有喜事； | 如果大的格局已经吉，再得青龙动来生合世爻和卦身，不但成名，必定另有喜事； | If the overall figure is already auspicious and the Azure Dragon moves to give life to and combine with the Self and Body, not only will one win a name but there will surely be other joy besides; |
> | 空动，出空之月日见喜。 | 如果是空而动，出空的月、日见喜。 | if it is Void and moving, the joy comes in the month or day it leaves the Void. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「场屋」是考场里一间间的号舍；「空动」是落空而又发动，应期在出空（出旬）的月日。
>
> *Note:* "Examination cells" are the numbered cubicles in the examination hall; "Void and moving" is a line that is Void and also moving, whose time is the month or day it leaves the Void (leaves the ten-day period).
