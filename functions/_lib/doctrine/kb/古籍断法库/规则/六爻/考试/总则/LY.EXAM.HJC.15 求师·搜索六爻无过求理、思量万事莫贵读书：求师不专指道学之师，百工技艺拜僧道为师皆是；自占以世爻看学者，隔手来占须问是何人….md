# 求师·搜索六爻无过求理、思量万事莫贵读书：求师不专指道学之师，百工技艺拜僧道为师皆是；自占以世爻看学者，隔手来占须问是何人；师弟相生相合则吉相冲相克则凶

六爻 · 考试 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求师不只指讲道学的老师，学百工技艺、拜僧人道士为师也都是；自己占，以世爻看学习的人；别人代占，要问是什么人，朋友兄弟就以兄弟爻为主；总要师弟相生相合才吉，相冲相克就凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeking a teacher does not mean only a teacher of moral learning: learning a craft or skill, or taking a monk or Taoist as teacher, all count; when one divines for oneself, the learner is read from the Self line; when another divines on one's behalf one must ask who he is, and for a friend or brother Peer is the main line; in every case teacher and pupil giving life to and combining with each other is auspicious, and clashing and controlling each other inauspicious.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1859 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡求师，不可专指道学之师，如欲投学百工技艺及拜僧道为师类皆是。 | 凡是求师，不能只指讲道学的老师，想拜师学百工技艺以及拜僧人道士为师的，也都属于求师。 | In seeking a teacher, one must not mean only a teacher of moral philosophy; wanting to learn a craft or skill, or taking a monk or Taoist as teacher, all count. |
> | 但师之主象，自占不以父母； | 但是老师的主象，自己占时不用父母爻； | But the main image of the teacher, when one divines for oneself, is not the Resource line; |
> | 而学者主象，自占当以世爻看之。 | 而学习者的主象，自己占时要用世爻来看。 | and the main image of the learner, when one divines for oneself, is to be read from the Self line. |
> | 如隔手来占，须问是何人，如朋友兄弟则以兄弟为主之类。 | 如果是别人代占，必须问是什么人，比如朋友、兄弟就以兄弟爻为主，等等。 | If another person divines on one's behalf, one must ask who it is; for a friend or brother, Peer is the main line, and so on. |
> | 皆要师弟相生相合则吉，相冲相克则凶。 | 都要老师和弟子相生相合才吉，相冲相克就凶。 | In every case, teacher and pupil giving life to and combining with each other is auspicious, and clashing and controlling each other is inauspicious. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一段说「师之主象，自占不以父母」，和第一条注里「门人卜投师，不论老幼，皆以父母爻为师长」看上去不一致，书里没有解释；这里照字面译，不替它调和。可能的读法是：拜僧道百工为师时，老师不以父母爻取，而以应爻或别的爻取，但原文没有明说。
>
> *Note:* This passage says "the main image of the teacher, when one divines for oneself, is not the Resource line", which seems at odds with the first commentary, "when a pupil divines about taking a teacher, whether old or young, the Resource line is the teacher"; the book does not explain, and it is translated as written without reconciling them. One possible reading is that for teachers of crafts, monks and Taoists the teacher is not taken from the Resource line but from the Other or another line, but the text does not say so.
