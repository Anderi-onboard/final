# 八宫章·功名只可自己占：自占功名只有一点功名之念才灵验，有官爵者心怀二念，代人占功名难取用神；例：代卜长辈功名，巽木化乾金回头克，午月削职、七月而终

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占功名和防灾虑患一样，只能自己占、不能替别人占：自己占功名，心里只有一点功名的念头，才自然灵验；若既有怕出事的心、又有求名的念，就难以决断。有官爵的人「心怀二念」（既怕失官又怕获罪），得子孙持世时，说他无事又怕神灵报的是失名，说功名有碍又怕神灵报的是无忧，所以说「自占必验，代卜不灵，不是卦不灵，是别人的心不专于一」。书中例：有人替长辈卜功名，野鹤说功名必须亲占、代占难取用神，从不敢断，幸而这个卦显而易见——巽木化出乾金，是变出来的爻回头来克，为绝卦，不必问功名，寿数也不长；果然午月被削职，七月去世。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining for a title, like divining against calamity, should be done only for oneself and not by proxy: when one divines one's own title with only a single thought of the title in mind, the divination is naturally borne out; if one has both a fear of mishap and a wish for fame, it is hard to decide. A man holding rank is "of two minds" (afraid of losing office and afraid of blame): if Output holds the Self, to promise him safety risks the spirit reporting the loss of his name, and to call his title obstructed risks the spirit reporting no worry; hence the saying "one's own divination is always borne out, a proxy's is not; it is not that the hexagram fails, but that the other person's mind is not set on one thing". The case in the book: someone divined an elder's title by proxy; Yehe said a title must be divined in person, since by proxy the Subject is hard to choose, and he never dares to judge, but luckily this hexagram was plain: Xun Wood changing into Qian Metal is a changed line coming back to control, a hexagram of cutting-off, so there was no need to ask about the title and life would not be long; indeed the man was stripped of rank in the Wu month and died in the seventh month.

> [!note] 按
> 最后一个卦例不在这一章里，是书中别处（第 2091–2096 行）的卦例，放在这里是因为它正说明「功名必须亲自占」；卦图不收，开头一句「如：卯月辛巳日……」连在上一个例子的结尾那一行里。「化来」是变出来的爻反过来克原来的爻（这里是金克木），也就是回头克。
>
> *Note:* The last case is not in this chapter but elsewhere in the book (lines 2091 to 2096); it is placed here because it illustrates "a title must be divined in person". Its hexagram diagram is not included, and its opening sentence "For example: in the Mao month, on a Xin-Si day" sits on the last line of the preceding case. "Coming by transformation" means that the changed line comes back to control the original line (here Metal controlling Wood), that is, the returning control.
