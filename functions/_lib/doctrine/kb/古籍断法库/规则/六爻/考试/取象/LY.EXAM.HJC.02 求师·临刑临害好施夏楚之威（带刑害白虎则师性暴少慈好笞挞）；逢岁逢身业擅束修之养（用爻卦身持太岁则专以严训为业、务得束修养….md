# 求师·临刑临害好施夏楚之威（带刑害白虎则师性暴少慈好笞挞）；逢岁逢身业擅束修之养（用爻卦身持太岁则专以严训为业、务得束修养家）

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻临刑、临害，老师爱用戒尺显威，带着刑害白虎的，性情暴躁少慈，必定爱打人，旺而动更甚；用爻逢太岁、逢卦身，老师以严格训导为业，一定要拿到束修来养家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line stands on punishment or harm, the teacher loves to show his severity with the rod, and one carrying punishment, harm and the White Tiger is violent and short of kindness and surely loves to flog, the more so if strong and moving; if the Subject line meets Tai Sui or the Body, the teacher makes strict instruction his trade and must have the tuition fee to keep his family.

> | 用爻卦身或持太岁，其师专以严训为业，务得束修以养家者。 | 用爻、卦身或持太岁，这位老师专以严格训导为业，一定要拿到束修来养家。 | If the Subject line, the Body or Tai Sui is held, the teacher makes strict instruction his trade and must have the tuition fee to keep his family. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「夏楚」是「夏」和「楚」两种惩治学生的木条（楚是荆条）；「束修」是学生交给老师的学费（古时十条干肉为一束）。「逢岁逢身」的「岁」是太岁，「身」是卦身。
>
> *Note:* The "xia and chu" are the two kinds of rod used to punish pupils (chu is the thorn switch); the "tuition fee" is what pupils pay the teacher (anciently a bundle of ten strips of dried meat). The "year" in "meeting the year or the Body" is Tai Sui and the "body" is the hexagram Body.
