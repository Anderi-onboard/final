# 求名·杀化生身之鬼恐发青衣：以子孙为煞，乘旺发动必遭斥退；得化鬼爻生世终不脱白，无过降青衣；卦有财动合住子孙可用资财谋旧职

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙是求名的煞，趁旺发动必被斥退；如果变出官鬼来生世，终究不至于被削成白身，最多降为青衣；卦中有财动来合住子孙，可以用钱财去谋求保住旧职。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output is the baleful spirit in seeking a name, and if it moves while strong one is surely dismissed; if it changes into a Pressure line that gives life to the Self, one is in the end not stripped to a commoner and at worst demoted to the blue gown; if Wealth moves in the hexagram and combines with Output and holds it, one may use money to try to keep one's former post.

> | 以子孙为煞，乘旺发动必遭斥退，若得化鬼爻生世，终不脱白，无过降青衣而已。 | 以子孙为煞，趁旺发动必定被斥退；如果得变出官鬼爻来生世，终究不会被削去身份，不过降为青衣罢了。 | Taking Output as the baleful spirit, if it moves while strong he will surely be expelled; if a Pressure line it changes into gives life to the Self, he will in the end not be stripped to a commoner, and at worst is only demoted to the blue gown. |
> | 卦有财动合住子孙，可用资财谋能干旧职。 | 如果卦中有财爻动来合住子孙，可以用钱财去谋求保住旧职。 | If Wealth moves in the hexagram and combines with Output and holds it, one may use money to try to keep one's former post. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「脱白」「青衣」书里没有解释；这一联讲的是已经有功名（生员）的人遇到子孙发动被斥退的情形，「脱白」译作「被削成白身」、「青衣」译作「降为青衣（较低的身份）」是按上下文（斥退—不脱白—降青衣）读出来的，只供参考。
>
> *Note:* The book does not explain "stripped to white" or "the blue gown"; this couplet concerns one who already has a degree (a licentiate) and is dismissed when Output moves, and "stripped to a commoner" and "demoted to the blue gown (a lower standing)" are read from the context (dismissed, not stripped, demoted), for reference only.
