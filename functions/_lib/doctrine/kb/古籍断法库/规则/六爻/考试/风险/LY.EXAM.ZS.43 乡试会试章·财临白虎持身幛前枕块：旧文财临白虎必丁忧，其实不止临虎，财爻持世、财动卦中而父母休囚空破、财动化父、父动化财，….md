# 乡试会试章·财临白虎持身幛前枕块：旧文财临白虎必丁忧，其实不止临虎，财爻持世、财动卦中而父母休囚空破、财动化父、父动化财，皆守丧之兆

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻临白虎持身，要守丧；旧文说得不全，凡是财爻持世，或财爻在卦中发动而父母休囚空破，或财动化父、父动化财，都是守丧的兆头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth standing on the White Tiger and holding the Body means mourning; the old text does not say it all: whenever Wealth holds the Self, or Wealth moves in the hexagram while Resource is resting and trapped, Void or broken, or Wealth moves and changes into Resource or Resource moves and changes into Wealth, these are all signs of mourning.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10288 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财临白虎持身，幛前枕块。 | 财爻临白虎持身，会在灵幛前枕土块守丧。 | If Wealth stands on the White Tiger and holds the Body, one will lie on clods of earth before the mourning screen. |
> | 旧文：财临白虎必丁忧，然亦不惟临虎，凡遇财爻持世，及财动卦中，父母又值休囚空破，及财动化父、父动化财，皆枕块之兆。 | 旧文说：财临白虎必定丁忧；其实也不只是临白虎，凡是遇到财爻持世，以及财爻在卦中发动、父母又休囚空破，以及财动变父、父动变财，都是守丧的兆头。 | The old text says: Wealth standing on the White Tiger means mourning for a parent; but it is not only when it stands on the Tiger; whenever Wealth holds the Self, or Wealth moves in the hexagram while Resource is resting and trapped, Void or broken, or Wealth moves and changes into Resource or Resource moves and changes into Wealth, these are all signs of lying on clods. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「丁忧」是遭父母丧、要守孝（官员须离职回家），「枕块」是守孝时睡在草席上、以土块为枕。财克父母爻，所以财爻太旺或和父爻互变，都是父母有事的象。
>
> *Note:* "Mourning" here is the mourning for a parent (an official had to leave his post and go home), and "lying on clods" is sleeping on a straw mat with a clod of earth for a pillow during the mourning. Wealth controls the Resource line, so Wealth too strong, or changing into and from the Resource line, is a sign of trouble for a parent.
