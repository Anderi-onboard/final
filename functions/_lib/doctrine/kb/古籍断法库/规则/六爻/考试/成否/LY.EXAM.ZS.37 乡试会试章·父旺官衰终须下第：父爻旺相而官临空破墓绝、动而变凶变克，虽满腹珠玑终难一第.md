# 乡试会试章·父旺官衰终须下第：父爻旺相而官临空破墓绝、动而变凶变克，虽满腹珠玑终难一第

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻旺相而官星临空、破、墓、绝，或者发动变凶、变克，即使满腹文章，终究很难得中。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line is thriving but the official star falls on Void, break, tomb or extinction, or moves and changes into ill or control, then even a man whose belly is full of pearls will hardly pass.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10183 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父旺官衰，终须下第。 | 父爻旺、官星衰，终究还是落第。 | With the Resource line strong and the official star weak, one fails in the end. |
> | 父爻旺相，官临空破、墓绝，及动而变凶、变克者，虽满腹珠玑，终难一第。 | 父爻旺相，而官星临在空、破、墓、绝上，以及发动变凶、变克的，即使满腹珠玑，终究难得一第。 | If the Resource line is thriving but the official star falls on Void, break, tomb or extinction, or moves and changes into ill or control, then even a man whose belly is full of pearls will hardly pass. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「满腹珠玑」是文章极好。文章（父爻）旺而官星衰，是文章好、命里没有「官」，这是黄金策求名章「父旺官衰，可惜刘蒉之下第」的另一种说法。
>
> *Note:* "A belly full of pearls" means extremely fine writing. A strong Resource line with a weak official star is fine writing without the "official" in the fate, which is another way of putting the Huangjin Ce line "with Resource strong and Pressure weak, it is a pity, as with Liu Fen who failed".
