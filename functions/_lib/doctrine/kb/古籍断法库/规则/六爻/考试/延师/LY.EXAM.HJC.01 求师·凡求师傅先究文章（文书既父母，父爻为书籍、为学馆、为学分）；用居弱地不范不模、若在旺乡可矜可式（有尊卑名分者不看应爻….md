# 求师·凡求师傅先究文章（文书既父母，父爻为书籍、为学馆、为学分）；用居弱地不范不模、若在旺乡可矜可式（有尊卑名分者不看应爻，门人投师以父母爻为师长）

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求老师先看文章（父母爻）：父母爻代表书籍、学馆和老师的学问。老师没有尊卑称呼的，以应爻为用神；有尊卑名分的，不看应爻，按名分论；学生占投师，不论老少，都以父母爻为师长。用神休囚，老师畏缩拘束，不能做楷模；旺相有气，魁梧雄伟，可敬可学。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking a teacher look first to the writing (the Resource line): the Resource line stands for the books, the school and the teacher's learning. If the teacher has no title of seniority, the Other line is the Subject; if he has a rank of seniority, the Other line is not looked at and one judges by the rank; when a pupil divines about taking a teacher, whether he is old or young the Resource line is the teacher. If the Subject is resting and trapped the teacher is timid and cramped and cannot be a model; if thriving and having breath he is tall and imposing and worthy of respect and imitation.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1817 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 所请之师，无尊卑称呼者，以应爻为用神也，如有尊卑名分，不可看应爻，当以名分论之。 | 所请的老师，没有尊卑称呼的，以应爻为用神；如果有尊卑名分，不可以看应爻，要按名分来论。 | For the teacher engaged, if there is no title of seniority, the Other line is taken as the Subject; if there is a rank of seniority, the Other line is not to be looked at, and one judges by the rank. |
> | 如门人卜投师，不论老幼，皆以父母爻为师长。 | 如果是学生占投拜老师，不论老少，都以父母爻为师长。 | If a pupil divines about taking a teacher, whether old or young, the Resource line is taken as the teacher. |
> | 如用神休囚，其师必然畏惧局促，不能为人之模范； | 如果用神休囚，那位老师必定畏缩拘束，不能做别人的模范； | If the Subject is resting and trapped, the teacher is surely timid and cramped, and cannot be a model for others; |
> | 旺相有气，则魁梧雄伟，堪为学者矜式仪矣。 | 旺相有气，就魁梧雄伟，足以做学者敬重效法的仪范。 | if it is thriving and has breath, he is tall and imposing and fit to be a pattern respected and followed by scholars. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「应爻为用神」和「父母爻为师长」看起来是两种取法，书里的注把分界讲了：老师和你之间没有尊卑名分（比如请来的先生），看应爻；已经有师生名分、你去拜师，看父母爻。「学分」书里没有解释，从后面「学分欠通」（LY.EXAM.HJC.07）看是学问、学识的意思。
>
> *Note:* The two ways of fixing the Subject, the Other line and the Resource line, are separated by the commentary: where there is no rank of seniority between you and the man (such as a teacher you hire), look at the Other; where the teacher-pupil standing already exists and you go to take him as teacher, look at the Resource line. "The weight of learning" is not explained, but from "his learning is deficient" later (LY.EXAM.HJC.07) it means learning and scholarship.
