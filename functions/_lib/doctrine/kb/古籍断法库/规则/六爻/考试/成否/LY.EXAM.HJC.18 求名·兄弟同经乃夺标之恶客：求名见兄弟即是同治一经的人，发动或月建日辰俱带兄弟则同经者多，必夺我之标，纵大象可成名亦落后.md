# 求名·兄弟同经乃夺标之恶客：求名见兄弟即是同治一经的人，发动或月建日辰俱带兄弟则同经者多，必夺我之标，纵大象可成名亦落后

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求名时见到兄弟爻，就是和自己同治一经的人；兄弟发动，或月建、日辰都带兄弟，同经的人多，必定夺走自己的魁首，纵然大局可成，名次也落后。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Seeing the Peer line in seeking a name means men who study the same classic as oneself; if it moves, or the Month and the Day both carry Peer, there are many who share one's classic and they will surely snatch one's prize, and even if the overall picture can succeed one's rank falls behind.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 同类者为兄弟，求名见之，乃是与我同经之人，如遇发动，或月建日辰俱带兄弟，则同经者多，必能夺我之标，纵大象可成，名亦落后。 | 同类的是兄弟爻；求名时见到它，就是和我同治一经的人；如果它发动，或者月建、日辰都带兄弟，那么同经的人多，必定能夺我的魁首，纵然大局可成，名次也落在后面。 | Those of the same kind are Peer; seen in seeking a name, they are men who study the same classic as I; if it moves, or the Month and the Day both carry Peer, then there are many who share my classic and they will surely snatch my prize, and even if the overall picture can succeed, my rank falls behind. |

^q2

> [!warning] 打架
> 这里（王洪绪注）说兄弟发动、或月建日辰带兄弟，同经者多，必夺其标。增删卜易（野鹤）却说：俗传兄弟爻兴难上榜、凡兄爻持世或动都断不吉，「殊不知有喜有忌」——财父同兴时喜兄动制财，父官两动时喜兄弟持世，兄弟持世临日月旺相再得官文两旺也能中式，只是不能夺魁（LY.EXAM.ZS.28）。两说在「兄弟夺魁」上一致，分歧在「兄弟是不是一概不吉」。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Here (Wang Hongxu's commentary) says that if Peer moves, or the Month and the Day carry Peer, there are many who share the classic and they will snatch the prize. The Zengshan Buyi (the Old Man of Yehe) says that common lore holds that if the Peer line flourishes it is hard to get on the list and that Peer holding the Self or moving is always ill, "not knowing that there are welcome cases and feared ones": when Wealth and Resource flourish together it is welcome that Peer moves to control Wealth, when Resource and Pressure both move it is welcome that Peer holds the Self, and with Peer holding the Self and standing on the Day or Month or thriving, and the official star and writing both strong, one can pass but cannot take first place (LY.EXAM.ZS.28). The two agree that Peer costs one first place; they differ on whether Peer is always ill.
