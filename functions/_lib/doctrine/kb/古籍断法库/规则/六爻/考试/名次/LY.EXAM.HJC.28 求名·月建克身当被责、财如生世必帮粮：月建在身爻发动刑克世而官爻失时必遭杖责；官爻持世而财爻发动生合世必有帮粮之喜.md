# 求名·月建克身当被责、财如生世必帮粮：月建在身爻发动刑克世而官爻失时必遭杖责；官爻持世而财爻发动生合世必有帮粮之喜

六爻 · 考试 · 名次 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建在身爻上、发动刑克世爻，而官爻失时，必遭杖责；卦中官爻持世、财爻发动生合世爻，必有得帮粮的喜事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Month is on the Body line and moves to punish and control the Self while the Pressure line is out of season, one will surely be caned; if the Pressure line holds the Self in the hexagram and the Wealth line moves to give life to and combine with the Self, there will surely be the joy of a grain allowance.

## 《黄金策·求名》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1890 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Seeking a name", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 月建克身当被责， | 月建克卦身，就会被责罚； | If the Month controls the Body, one will be reproved; |
> | 财如生世必帮粮 | 财爻如果生世，必有帮粮（的好处） | if Wealth gives life to the Self, one will surely get the grain allowance |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1890 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 月建若在身爻，发动刑克世爻，而官爻失时者，必遭杖责； | 月建如果在身爻上，发动刑克世爻，而官爻又失时（无气）的，必定遭杖责； | If the Month is on the Body line and moves to punish and control the Self while the Pressure line is out of season, one will surely be caned; |
> | 卦中官爻持世，而财爻发动生合世爻者，必有帮粮之喜。 | 卦中官爻持世，而财爻发动生合世爻的，必有得帮粮的喜事。 | if the Pressure line holds the Self in the hexagram and the Wealth line moves to give life to and combine with the Self, there will surely be the joy of a grain allowance. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「帮粮」是廪生之外官府另给的补助口粮（增删卜易岁考章里「优等帮粮」是同一件事，LY.EXAM.ZS.18）。「失时」是爻没有得月令（休囚）。
>
> *Note:* A "grain allowance" is the supplementary ration the government gave in addition to the stipend (the "excellent grade with a grain allowance" in the Zengshan Buyi chapter on the annual examination is the same thing, LY.EXAM.ZS.18). "Out of season" means the line has not got the month's command (it is resting or trapped).
