# 童试章·世破身空兼墓绝再奋芸窗；文旺遭伤收而复弃，冲中逢合弃而仍收；父母旺相化休囚空破墓绝、回头克或被冲克则始取终弃（附例：官星月破伏藏、飞神合破，择三月考之必取）

六爻 · 考试 · 小考 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻被破、卦身落空又入墓逢绝，不能遂愿，只好再苦读；文章旺却遭伤害，先收取后舍弃；父母爻旺相却变休囚、空破、墓绝、回头克或被冲克，是开始被取、最后被弃；世爻、父爻动而遇冲，若得日月动爻相合，叫冲中逢合，一定续取。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self broken, the Body Void and moreover in the tomb or extinct, one cannot fulfil one's aim and must work hard again at the study window; with the writing strong but harmed, one is taken and then cast aside; if the Resource line is thriving but changes into resting and trapped, Void or broken, tomb, extinction or a line that turns back to control it, or is clashed and controlled, one is taken at first and cast aside in the end; if the Self and Resource lines move and meet a clash but the Day, the Month or a moving line combines with them, this is a combination within a clash and one will surely be taken afterwards.

## 书中卦例

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 9990 行 · Case from the book
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世子水父母临日建，旺文持世，佳作定然得意，但不宜酉金官星临月破，伏藏不露，幸得辰土作飞神，破而逢合，若在三月考之，必取。 | 世爻是子水，父母爻临日建，旺相的文章持世，佳作一定得意；只是不宜酉金官星临月破、又伏藏不现；幸好有辰土做飞神，破了又逢合，如果在三月考，必定录取。 | The Self is Zi Water and the Resource line stands on the Day; a strong writing holds the Self and a fine essay will surely be admired; but it is unfortunate that the Pressure line of You Metal stands on the Month-break and lies hidden and unseen; luckily Chen Earth is its flying line, so what is broken meets a combination, and if the examination is held in the third month he will surely be taken. |
> | 果考于三月，取之。 | 果然考试在三月，被录取了。 | The examination was indeed held in the third month and he was taken. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「冲中逢合」是被冲的爻又遇到来合它的日月或动爻，冲的力量被合化解。例子里说官星酉金月破又伏藏，辰土（飞神）和酉合（辰酉合），所以破而逢合，断为「在三月考之必取」。第 2 句里「再奋芸窗」「始取终弃」是榜上被取、后又被除名的情形。
>
> *Note:* A "combination within a clash" is a clashed line meeting a Day, Month or moving line that combines with it, the combination resolving the clash. In the example the official star You Metal is on the Month-break and hidden, and Chen Earth, its flying line, combines with You (Chen and You combine), so what is broken meets a combination, and the verdict is that if the examination is in the third month he will be taken. "Taken at first and cast aside in the end" is the case of being admitted and later struck from the list.
