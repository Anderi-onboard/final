# 求名·龙虎二爻俱动一举成名：青龙白虎俱在卦中动来生合世爻必中魁选，持官父或持身世尤妙

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙、白虎两爻都动，一考就成名：两样都在卦中、动来生合世爻，必定中魁选；若又持官爻、父爻或持身、世就更好。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Azure Dragon and White Tiger lines both move, one makes a name at a single stroke: if both are in the hexagram and move to give life to or combine with the Self, one will surely be chosen at the head, and it is better still if they hold the official star or Resource, or hold the Body or Self.

> [!note] 按
> 通常白虎是凶神，这里青龙白虎「俱动生合世爻」却是吉：因为它们是六神，吉凶要看它们生合的是什么爻。
>
> *Note:* The White Tiger is ordinarily a baleful spirit, but here the Azure Dragon and White Tiger moving "to give life to and combine with the Self" are auspicious: as six-spirit lines, their good or ill depends on which line they give life to and combine with.
