# 乡试会试章·太岁作官星终登黄甲：官临太岁动而生合世，是人臣面君之象，君臣契合联捷无疑；太岁必要入爻、发动、生合或持世才能这样说

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 太岁是君主的爻，官星临太岁、发动来生合世爻，是臣子面见君主的象，主君臣契合，连捷无疑；但太岁一定要入卦成爻、也要发动、生合或持世爻，才能这样说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Tai Sui is the ruler's line; if the official star stands on Tai Sui and moves to give life to or combine with the Self, it is the image of a minister before his sovereign, which means ruler and minister are in accord and passing in succession is beyond doubt; but Tai Sui must enter the hexagram as a line, must also move, and must give life to, combine with or hold the Self before it can be spoken of in this way.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10144 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 余按：太岁必要入爻，入爻亦要发动，生合世爻，或持世爻，始能如此说也。 | 我的按语：太岁一定要入卦中成爻，入了卦也要发动，生合世爻，或者持世爻，才能这样说。 | My note: Tai Sui must enter the hexagram as a line, and having entered it must also move and give life to or combine with the Self line, or hold the Self, before it can be spoken of in this way. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「黄甲」是进士榜（黄纸写的榜，榜上进士称甲）；太岁是当年的地支，平常不在卦里成爻，只有当年的地支恰好和卦中爻同支时才「入爻」。野鹤自己的按语把条件收得很紧，免得一见太岁就断吉。
>
> *Note:* The "Yellow List" is the list of successful candidates for the highest degree (written on yellow paper); Tai Sui is the branch of the current year and ordinarily is not a line of the hexagram, and it "enters a line" only when a line of the hexagram has the same branch as the year. The Old Man of Yehe's own note keeps the conditions tight so that one does not judge it auspicious at the mere sight of Tai Sui.
