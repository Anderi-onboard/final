# 启蒙节要·六亲发动诀·财爻发动：财爻发动克文书，应举求名总是虚

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻发动，克住父母文书：应考、求名总是落空。同一诀里，财动对别的事是吉的：拿本钱经营大吉，亲戚婚姻如意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Wealth line moves and controls the document line, sitting the examinations and seeking a title all come to nothing. The same verse says a moving Wealth line is good elsewhere: trading with capital is greatly auspicious and marriages and family ties go as one wishes.

## 《卜筮正宗·启蒙节要·四十六、六亲发动诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 257 行 · Bushi Zhengzong (The Orthodox Line of Divination), "Qimeng Jieyao, no. 46: the verse on the moving of the six relations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财爻发动克文书， | 财爻发动，克住文书， | The Wealth line moves and controls the document line: |
> | 应举求名总是虚， | 应考、求名，总是落空； | sitting the examinations and seeking a title always come to nothing; |
> | 将本经营为大吉， | 拿本钱去经营，大吉； | trading with capital is greatly auspicious; |
> | 亲姻如意乐无虞， | 亲戚婚姻如意，快乐无忧。 | marriages and family ties go as one wishes, glad and free of worry. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 诀文说的是财克父母文书的一般情形。《黄金策》另讲了一种例外：父母爻空亡，而财爻发动生扶官鬼，侥幸可以成（LY.EXAM.HJC.24）。
>
> *Note:* The verse speaks of the usual case of Wealth controlling the Resource line (the document line). The Huangjin Ce gives one exception: when the Resource line is Void and the Wealth line moves to give life and support to Pressure, one may succeed by luck (LY.EXAM.HJC.24).
