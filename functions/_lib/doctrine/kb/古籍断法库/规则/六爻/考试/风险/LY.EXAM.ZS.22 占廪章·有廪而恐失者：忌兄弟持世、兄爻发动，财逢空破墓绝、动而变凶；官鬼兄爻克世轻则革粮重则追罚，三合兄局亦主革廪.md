# 占廪章·有廪而恐失者：忌兄弟持世、兄爻发动，财逢空破墓绝、动而变凶；官鬼兄爻克世轻则革粮重则追罚，三合兄局亦主革廪

六爻 · 考试 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 已有廪生名额而怕失去的，忌兄弟持世、兄弟爻发动，以及财爻逢空破墓绝、发动变凶，都会失去；官鬼或兄弟爻克世，轻的革去口粮，重的追罚；卦里三合成兄弟局，也主革去廪生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For one who already holds the stipend and fears losing it: Peer holding the Self, the Peer line moving, or Wealth meeting Void, break, tomb or extinction or moving into ill, all mean it will be lost; if Pressure or the Peer line controls the Self the lighter result is that the grain allowance is stopped and the heavier is a penalty with recovery; a three-harmony frame of Peer in the hexagram also means the stipend is revoked.

## 《增删卜易·占廪章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10082 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the stipend", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 有廪而恐失者，忌兄弟持世，兄爻发动，及财逢空破墓绝，动而变凶，皆有失也。 | 已有廪生名额而担心失去的，忌兄弟持世、兄弟爻发动，以及财爻逢空破墓绝、发动变凶，都会失去。 | For one who holds the stipend and fears losing it, Peer holding the Self, the Peer line moving, or Wealth meeting Void, break, tomb or extinction, or moving and changing into ill, all mean it will be lost. |
> | 如官鬼兄爻克世者，轻则革粮，重则追罚，倘卦中三合兄局者，亦主革廪。 | 如果官鬼、兄弟爻克世，轻的革去口粮，重的追罚；如果卦中三合成兄弟局，也主革去廪生。 | If Pressure or the Peer line controls the Self, the lighter result is that the grain allowance is stopped and the heavier is a penalty with recovery; if the hexagram forms a three-harmony frame of Peer, the stipend is also revoked. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「革粮」是停发口粮，「追罚」是追回已领的粮并罚；「三合兄局」是卦中的爻合成兄弟五行的三合局。
>
> *Note:* "Stopped grain allowance" means the ration is cut off, and "penalty with recovery" means the grain already drawn is recovered and a fine imposed; a "three-harmony frame of Peer" is a three-harmony group in the hexagram of the Peer relation's phase.
