# 岁考科考章·总括：父、世旺相遇生扶为上，略欠次之，刑伤有救又次之，刑伤无救而轻者又次之，世父被刑冲克破而重者劣等；善卜者通变而断（觉子）

六爻 · 考试 · 名次 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 岁考科考的等第次序：父爻世爻旺相又得生扶最上；略有欠缺的次之；受刑伤但有救的又次之；受刑伤无救但程度轻的又次之；世、父被刑冲克破而且重的，必定劣等。会占的人要把吉凶和用神衰旺的轻重合起来，灵活变通地断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The order of grades in the annual and qualifying examinations: the Resource and Self lines thriving and given life and support is the top; one with a slight lack is next; one punished or harmed but with rescue is lower again; one punished or harmed with no rescue but lightly is lower again; if the Self and Resource are punished, clashed, controlled or broken, and badly, it is surely the lowest grade. One skilled at divining must weigh the signs together with how heavy or light the strength or weakness of the Subject is, and judge flexibly.

> | 世父被刑冲克破而重者，劣等必矣。 | 世爻、父爻被刑冲克破而且严重的，必定是劣等。 | one where the Self and Resource are punished, clashed, controlled or broken, and badly, is surely the lowest grade. |
> | 善卜者，须以向之吉凶兼用神衰旺之轻重，通变而断之。 | 善于占卜的人，要把先前所说的吉凶，连同用神衰旺的轻重一起考虑，灵活变通地来断。 | One skilled at divining must take the auspicious and inauspicious signs spoken of above together with how heavy or light the strength or weakness of the Subject is, and judge flexibly. |
