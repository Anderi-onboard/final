# 八宫章·占功名秘法（士子）：先占今科，再占下科，再占终身；官鬼持世金榜标名、子孙持世今科失望；孝廉占会试、童生占入学同此法；要节节问去，先问眼前再问久远

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 读书人占功名，要一步一步问：先占今科中不中，官鬼持世就金榜标名，子孙持世就知道今科没有指望；改天再占下一科，再另选一天占终身能不能中，官鬼持世终能奋发，仍是子孙持世就改行另谋出路。举人占会试、童生占入学，照这个办法。占事要节节问下去，先问眼前的疑问，不要先问一生久远的事，因为神灵回报的多半是近事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A scholar divining for a title should ask step by step: first whether he will pass this year's examination, where Pressure holding the Self means his name is on the golden list and Output holding the Self means this year is hopeless; on another day whether he will pass the next, and on yet another day whether he will ever pass in his life, where Pressure holding the Self means he will in the end rise to success and Output holding the Self again means he should change his occupation. A provincial graduate divining for the metropolitan examination and a boy candidate divining for entry into the school follow the same method. One must ask step by step, the doubt at hand first and the distant future afterwards, because what the spirit reports is mostly the near matter.

## 《增删卜易》正文(未标说话人)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 355 行 · Zengshan Buyi, main text (speaker not marked)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 以上皆秘法也，占事者，全在节节问去，卦无不灵。 | 以上这些都是秘法，占事的人，全在于一步一步问下去，这样卦没有不灵的。 | All of the above are secret methods; for one who divines, everything lies in asking step by step, and then no hexagram fails to be borne out. |
> | 若未卜目下，先问终身久远之事，殊不知神报近事者多，世人不识此理，胸中如许未决之事，先问终身，而神止报眼下之疑，断卦者亦不能知此神意，竟以终身决之，岂非天坏耶？ | 如果还没占眼前的事，就先问一生久远的事，殊不知神灵回报的多半是近事；世人不懂这个道理，心里有这么多没有决定的事，先去问终身，而神灵只回报眼前的疑问，断卦的人也不知道神的这个用意，竟拿终身去断，这岂不是坏得太厉害了吗？ | If one has not yet divined the matter at hand and first asks about the distant future, one does not realise that what the spirit reports is mostly the near matter; people do not understand this, and with so many matters undecided in their hearts they ask first about their whole life, while the spirit answers only the doubt of the moment, and the one who judges the hexagram, not knowing the spirit's intent, judges it as a whole life; is that not a grievous spoiling? |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「孝廉」是举人的别称，「童生」是还没考中秀才的读书人，「入学」是考中秀才、进县学；这是科举里通行的说法，不是本书的定义。原文末句作「岂非天坏耶」，「天坏」疑为「大坏」之误，译文按「大坏」翻。
>
> *Note:* A "provincial graduate" is a holder of the second degree, a "boy candidate" is a student who has not yet won the first degree, and "entry into the school" means passing the first degree and joining the county school; these are the usual examination terms, not definitions given in this book. The last sentence of the original reads "is that not Heaven-spoiled", where "Heaven-spoiled" is probably a slip for "greatly spoiled", and the translation follows that.
