# 求师·父入墓中边孝先爱眠懒读（父爻入墓则师爱安逸、赖于教训、学分欠通，逢空化墓皆然；日辰冲破墓爻又主聪察）；文临身上李老聃博古通今（六爻无父必欠学问，静临卦身或居生旺之地才学非常）

六爻 · 考试 · 取象 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻入墓，老师爱安逸、懒于教训、学问欠通达，逢空、化墓也一样，日辰冲破墓爻则又聪明明察；文章（父爻）临在身上，老师博古通今；六爻里没有父爻，一定欠缺学问，静而临卦身或居生旺之地，才学非常。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line enters the tomb, the teacher loves ease, is lazy at teaching and admonishing and has deficient learning, and the same if it meets Void or changes into the tomb, though if the Day clashes and breaks the tomb line he is again sharp and observant; if the writing (the Resource line) stands on the Body, the teacher is learned in antiquity and versed in the present; if there is no Resource line among the six lines he surely lacks learning, and if it is still and stands on the Body or in a place of life or strength his talent and learning are extraordinary.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡求师，以父为师之才学，六爻无父必欠学问，若得静临卦身或居生旺之地，其师才学非常。 | 凡是求师，以父爻代表老师的才学，六爻里没有父爻，一定欠缺学问；如果静而临在卦身上，或者处在生旺之地，这位老师才学非同寻常。 | In seeking a teacher, the Resource line stands for the teacher's talent and learning; if there is no Resource line among the six lines, he surely lacks learning; if it is still and stands on the Body, or is in a place of life or strength, the teacher's talent and learning are extraordinary. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 边孝先是汉代的边韶，据说白天睡觉，弟子笑他「边孝先，腹便便，懒读书，但欲眠」，他答「腹便便，五经笥，但欲眠，思经事」；李老聃是老子。书里只提名字作比喻，没有讲故事。
>
> *Note:* Bian Xiaoxian is Bian Shao of the Han, said to have napped by day, so that his disciples mocked him, "Bian Xiaoxian, big belly, lazy at reading, only wants to sleep"; Lao Dan is Laozi. The book uses the names only as comparisons and does not tell the stories.
