# 乡试会试章·父衰官旺堪许登科：官爻旺而生世、持世，父爻不旺，是文不齐而福齐，反能遂志；应合日生必资鹗荐，官文两旺世不旺得日月扶助又得应动生合世必得推荐之力

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父爻衰而官星旺，可以许他登科：官爻旺而生世或持世、父爻不旺，叫文不齐而福齐，反能遂愿；应爻来合、日辰来生，必靠推荐；官文两旺而世爻不旺，得日月扶助，又有应爻发动生合世爻，必得推荐的力量。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Resource line weak and the official star strong, one may be granted success: if the Pressure line is strong and gives life to or holds the Self while the Resource line is not strong, it is called "the writing uneven but the fortune even" and one fulfils one's aim; if the Other combines and the Day gives life, one will surely rely on a recommendation; if the official star and the writing are both strong but the Self is not, with the Day or the Month helping and the Other line moving to give life to or combine with the Self, one will gain the force of a recommendation.

## 《增删卜易·乡试会试章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 10184–10185 行 · Zengshan Buyi (Divination, Pruned and Supplemented), "The chapter on the provincial and metropolitan examinations", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父衰官旺，堪许登科。 | 父爻衰、官星旺，可以许他登科。 | With the Resource line weak and the official star strong, one may be granted success in the examination. |
> | 官爻旺而生世、持世，而父爻不旺者，谓之文不齐而福齐，反能遂志。 | 官爻旺而生世、持世，而父爻不旺的，叫做文章不齐而福分齐，反而能遂愿。 | If the Pressure line is strong and gives life to or holds the Self while the Resource line is not strong, it is called "the writing is uneven but the fortune is even", and one can fulfil one's aim instead. |
> | 应合日生，必资鹗荐。 | 应爻来合、日辰来生，必定靠（有人）举荐。 | If the Other combines and the Day gives life, one will surely rely on a recommendation. |
> | 官文两旺，而世爻不旺，或得日月扶助，又得应爻动而生合世爻者，必得推荐之力。 | 官星、文章两旺，而世爻不旺，或者得日月扶助，又得应爻发动来生合世爻的，必定得到推荐的力量。 | If the official star and the writing are both strong but the Self is not strong, or is helped by the Day or the Month, and the Other line moves to give life to or combine with the Self, one will surely gain the force of a recommendation. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「鹗荐」是荐举人才（鹗是鱼鹰，旧时称荐举贤才为「鹗荐」）；「福齐」是官运、福分齐全。这一条和上一条相反：文章好而官衰落第，文章差而官旺能中，因为旺官能生衰父（见后面[乾按]，不收）。
>
> *Note:* A "recommendation" is the recommending of talent (the fish-hawk stands for the one who recommends); "even fortune" is a full measure of office and fortune. This is the reverse of the previous entry: good writing with a weak official star fails, weak writing with a strong official star passes, since a strong official star can give life to a weak Resource line (the [Qian's note] saying so is not quoted).
