# 求师·月扶福德日生则青出于蓝：子孙有气不空又得月日动爻生合则学有进益，用爻反衰则弟子胜于师

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建扶着子孙、日辰生它，学生青出于蓝：子孙爻要有气不空，又得月、日、动爻生合，学业才有进益；用爻反而衰，弟子反而胜过老师。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Month supports Output and the Day gives it life, the pupil surpasses the master: the Output line must have breath and not be Void and be given life and combination by the Month, the Day or a moving line before study makes progress; if the Subject line is on the contrary weak, the pupil surpasses the teacher.
