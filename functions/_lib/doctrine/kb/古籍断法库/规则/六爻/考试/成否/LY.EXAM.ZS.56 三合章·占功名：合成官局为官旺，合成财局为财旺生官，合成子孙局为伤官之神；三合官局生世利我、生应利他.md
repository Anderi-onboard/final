# 三合章·占功名：合成官局为官旺，合成财局为财旺生官，合成子孙局为伤官之神；三合官局生世利我、生应利他

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占功名，三合成官局叫官旺；合成财局叫财旺生官；合成子孙局是伤官的神，不利。三合官局生世爻，对我有利；生应爻，对他人有利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for a title, a Pressure frame formed by the three-harmony is called a strong official star; a Wealth frame is Wealth strong and giving life to Pressure; an Output frame is the spirit that wounds Pressure and is unfavourable. A three-harmony Pressure frame that gives life to the Self favours me, and one that gives life to the Other favours someone else.
