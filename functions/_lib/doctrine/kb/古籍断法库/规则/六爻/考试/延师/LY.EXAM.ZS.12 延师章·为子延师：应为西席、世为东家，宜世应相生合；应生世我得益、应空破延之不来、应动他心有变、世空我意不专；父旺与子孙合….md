# 延师章·为子延师：应为西席、世为东家，宜世应相生合；应生世我得益、应空破延之不来、应动他心有变、世空我意不专；父旺与子孙合名实兼收，父动伤子孙、子动化克不宜延、子动化鬼速谢辞（觉子；附例）

六爻 · 考试 · 延师 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 为儿子请老师：应爻是老师、世爻是东家，宜世应相生相合，不宜相克相冲；应生世我得益，应空破请不来，应动他有变心，世空我自己心意不专。觉子补充：父爻旺又和子孙相合，名实都有；父动伤克子孙，有损无益；子孙动变克，不宜聘请；子孙动变官鬼，应赶快辞退。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In engaging a teacher for one's son: the Other is the teacher and the Self is the host; the Self and Other should give life to and combine with each other and not control or clash; if the Other gives life to the Self I gain, if the Other is Void or broken he will not come, if the Other moves he has a change of mind, if the Self is Void my own mind is not set. Juezi adds: a strong Resource line combining with Output gains both name and substance; if Resource moves and harms Output there is harm and no gain; if Output moves and changes into control the man should not be engaged; if Output moves and changes into Pressure he should be thanked and dismissed quickly.

> [!note] 按
> 「西席」「东家」是旧时请私塾先生的说法：老师坐西席，主人是东家。这里主人是父亲，所以世爻是父亲（东家），儿子拜师时看子孙爻。前面「延师受教，以父母爻为用神」是自己拜师的取法；这一条是父亲为儿子请老师，多看世应，再加子孙爻。后面的[乾按]不收。
>
> *Note:* "The west seat" and "the host" are the old way of speaking of hiring a private-school teacher: the teacher sits in the west seat and the master of the house is the host. Here the master is the father, so the Self is the father; when the son takes a teacher, the Output line is also looked at. The earlier line, "in engaging a teacher for instruction take the Resource line as the Subject", is for a man taking a teacher himself; this one is for a father engaging a teacher for his son, so the Self and Other are looked at first, with the Output line added. The [Qian's note] after it is not quoted.
