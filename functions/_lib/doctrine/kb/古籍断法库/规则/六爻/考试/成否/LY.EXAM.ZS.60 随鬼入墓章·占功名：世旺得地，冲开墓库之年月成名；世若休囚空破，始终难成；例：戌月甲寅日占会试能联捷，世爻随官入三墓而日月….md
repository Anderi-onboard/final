# 随鬼入墓章·占功名：世旺得地，冲开墓库之年月成名；世若休囚空破，始终难成；例：戌月甲寅日占会试能联捷，世爻随官入三墓而日月合成官局，辰年辰月冲开三墓，联捷鼎甲

六爻 · 考试 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻入墓只验三种（世爻随鬼入日墓、入动墓、动而化墓），自占看世爻、代占看用神，旺相的不是真入墓，只有休囚被克又入墓才是，墓神被日月动爻冲破的也不算。占功名：世爻旺相得地，冲开墓库的年月成名；世爻休囚空破，始终难成。书中例：戌月甲寅日占会试能不能联捷，得小过卦变艮卦，世爻随官爻入三个墓（动墓、化墓、入月建之墓），断为明年辰年、发榜之时又在辰月，冲开三个墓，不但联捷，必定名列鼎甲，因为日月合成官局、旺相当令，卦象十全；结果果然传胪三唱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self entering the tomb proves true in only three ways (the Self entering the tomb of the Day with Pressure, entering a moving tomb, and moving and changing into a tomb); in one's own divination watch the Self and in a proxy's watch the Subject; a strong one is not truly entering the tomb, and only a resting or controlled one that also enters the tomb is the real thing, and a tomb spirit broken by the Day, the Month or a moving line does not count either. In divining for a title: if the Self is strong and in place, the name is made in the year or month that clashes open the tomb; if the Self is resting, trapped, Void or broken, success is hard from start to finish. The case in the book: on a Jia-Yin day in the Xu month a man divined whether he would pass the metropolitan examination in consecutive rounds and got Xiaoguo changing to Gen; the Self followed Pressure into three tombs (a moving tomb, a changing tomb and the Month's tomb), and the judgement was that next year is the Chen year and the posting of the list falls again in the Chen month, clashing open all three tombs, so he would not only pass in consecutive rounds but be among the top three, since the Day and Month combine into a Pressure frame, strong and in command, the hexagram perfect; and indeed his name was called out at the palace announcement.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章的墓库规则是按问题分行列的，功名只是其中一行（占身命、出仕出行、求财、婚姻、疾病、狱讼、行人、入公门、家宅、讼事等各有一行），别的行属于别的问题域，婚姻一行见 LY.MARRY.ZS.33。「鼎甲」「传胪」按通行说法，指殿试放榜时名列前三、被唱名公布；卦图不收，例子的开头一句在卦图之前。
>
> *Note:* The tomb rules in this chapter are laid out line by line by kind of matter, and a title is only one line of them (one each for the body and fate, taking office and travelling, wealth, marriage, illness, lawsuits, travellers, entering a government office, the household, and legal suits); the other lines belong to other domains, and the marriage line is at LY.MARRY.ZS.33. By the usual usage "the top three" and "the announcement of names" mean standing among the first three when the palace-examination list is posted and the names are called out; the hexagram diagram is not included, and the case's opening sentence comes before its diagram.
