# 词讼·官父两强词讼表章皆准理、妻财一动申呈诉告总徒劳：凡是要上表、申奏、递状、告诉，官鬼和父母都要有气不空才准理；最怕财爻动伤父母，必不成

六爻 · 官非 · 起讼 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼和父母两爻都强，词讼和表章都能被受理；妻财一发动，申呈和诉告全是白费。凡是要上表、申奏、递呈子、告诉这类事，都要官鬼和父母两样齐全，有气不空，就能受理；最怕财爻动伤父母，一定不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Pressure and Resource both strong, lawsuits and memorials alike are accepted; once Wealth moves, petitions and complaints are all in vain. For anything such as presenting a memorial, submitting a report, lodging a petition or making a complaint, Pressure and Resource must both be present, with breath and not Void, and then it is accepted; most to be feared is Wealth moving to injure Resource, in which case it surely fails.
