# 随鬼入墓章·书中例，占已定重罪可蒙赦免否：世爻随鬼入动墓又动而化墓，古法断凶，作者断吉——日月生世、丑墓月破，破罗破网容易出来，断明年酉年蒙赦，果然次年辰月蒙赦而出；世爻入墓只验三种，旺相的不真，墓被日月动爻冲破的也不真

六爻 · 官非 · 狱囚 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 我屡次验证而得验的，只有世爻入墓三种：一是世爻随鬼入日墓，一是世爻入动墓，一是世爻动而化墓。这三种墓，自占看世爻，世爻旺相的不是真；代占看用神，用神旺相的不是真；只有世爻、用爻休囚被克，又入墓的才是；墓神被日月动爻冲破的，也不是真。因为墓破就像破网，容易出来。书中例：未月戊辰日占已经判定的重罪能不能蒙赦免，得蛊卦变损卦：世爻随鬼入动墓，又动而化墓，古法认为凶，我断为吉：日月生世，丑墓被月破，破罗破网，容易出来，断明年酉年一定蒙赦；果然次年辰月蒙赦出来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* What I have found borne out by repeated trial is that the Self entering the tomb is borne out in only three ways: the Self following Pressure into the tomb of the Day, the Self entering a moving tomb, and the Self moving and changing into the tomb. For these three tombs, in divining for oneself look at the Self, and a strong Self is not the real thing; in divining for someone else look at the Subject, and a strong Subject is not the real thing; it is real only when the Self or the Subject is resting, trapped or controlled and also enters the tomb; a tomb spirit clashed and broken by the Month or a moving line is not real either. For a broken tomb is like a broken net, and it is easy to get out. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Wuchen day, a man divined whether a grave sentence already fixed could be pardoned, and got Gu changing to Sun: the Self follows Pressure into a moving tomb and also moves and changes into the tomb, which the old method calls ill, but I judged lucky: the Month and the Day give life to the Self, the Chou tomb is broken by the Month, a net broken is easy to leave, and I judged that next year, a You year, he would surely be pardoned; indeed in the Chen month of the next year he was pardoned and let out.
