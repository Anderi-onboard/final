# 五行相生章·书中例，弟占兄已得重罪母问能救否：兄弟爻为用神丑土兄动被日月克，明显大罪难脱，幸好丑土化出午火父母回头相生，化父母生兄爻相生为吉，断快快去办，后来蒙恩免死

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神、元神，适宜逢生：有月建生、日建生、动爻生、动变回头生。书中例：卯月己卯日，弟弟占哥哥已经被判重罪，母亲来问能不能救，得复卦变震卦：兄弟爻为用神，丑土兄动，被日月克，明显大罪难脱，幸好兄爻丑土化出午火父母，回头相生；断说：赶快去办！化出父母去生兄爻，相生为吉，神灵告知得很明显，后来果然蒙恩免死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Subject and the Support should meet life: life from the Month, from the Day, from a moving line, or from a turning-back life by change. Worked example: in the Mao month, on a Jimao day, a younger brother divined for his elder brother, who had already been given a grave sentence, with the mother asking whether he could be saved, and got Fu changing to Zhen: the Peer line is the Subject, Chou-earth moves and is controlled by the Month and the Day, so it is plain that he cannot escape the great charge, but fortunately the Chou-earth Peer line changes into Wu-fire as Resource that turns back to give life; I judged: go and do it quickly! Resource changing out to give life to the Peer line, mutual life, is lucky and the spirits spoke clearly; afterwards he was indeed spared death by grace.
