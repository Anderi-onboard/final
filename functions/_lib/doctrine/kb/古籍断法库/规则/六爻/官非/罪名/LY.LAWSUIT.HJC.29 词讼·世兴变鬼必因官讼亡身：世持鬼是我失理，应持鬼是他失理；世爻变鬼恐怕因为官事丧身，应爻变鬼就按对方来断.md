# 词讼·世兴变鬼必因官讼亡身：世持鬼是我失理，应持鬼是他失理；世爻变鬼恐怕因为官事丧身，应爻变鬼就按对方来断

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻发动变成官鬼，必定因为官司丢了性命。世爻持鬼，是我失理；应爻持鬼，是他失理；世爻变鬼恐怕因官事丧身，应爻变鬼就按对方来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self moving and changing into Pressure surely means losing one's life over the lawsuit. The Self holding Pressure means I am in the wrong; the Other holding Pressure means he is in the wrong; the Self changing into Pressure, I fear one may die from the official matter, and the Other changing into Pressure is judged for the opposite party.
