# 五行相克章·书中例，妹占兄官事同前一案也拟重罪：申金兄弟爻为用神，巳火鬼动刑克申金，重罪定了；幸好辰日冲动戌土父母暗动生申，克处逢生，若有父母可以救他；后来因为父亲八十岁，援例告留免死——克处逢生

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 同一天妹妹占哥哥的官事，和前一案一样，也拟了重罪，得否卦变讼卦：申金兄弟爻为用神，巳火官鬼发动，刑克申金，重罪是定了；幸好辰日冲动戌土父母，暗动去生申金，克处逢生，如果有父母，可以救他；后来因为他父亲八十岁了，援例告留，免了死罪。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On the same day a younger sister divined about her elder brother's official matter, the same case as before, and a grave sentence had likewise been drafted; she got Pi changing to Song: the Peer line Shen-metal is the Subject, Pressure Si-fire moves and punishes or controls Shen-metal, so the grave charge is fixed; fortunately the Chen day clashes the Resource Xu-earth into secret motion, which gives life to Shen, life met within a control, and if there is a parent he can be saved; afterwards, because his father was eighty years old, an appeal for retention under the precedent saved him from death.
