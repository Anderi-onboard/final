# 趋避章·书中例，占定何罪：木鬼临白虎绞罪已定，但世临月建、木鬼虽动又被酉金回头之克、外卦反吟，事必反复，虽定绞罪终有改移；后来议绞罪，蒙驳审减轻

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 蛇雀木鬼犯官刑，临火须防焚害；蛇木又言缢绞，勾陈土鬼牢狱。书中例：未月癸亥日占判定什么罪，得中孚卦变临卦：木鬼临白虎，绞罪是定了；但幸好世爻临月建，木鬼虽然发动，又被酉金回头之克，外卦反吟，事情一定反复，虽然定了绞罪，终究会有改动；果然议的是绞罪，后来蒙驳回重审减轻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Coiling Snake or Vermilion Bird with Wood Pressure offends against the official penalty, and with Fire beware of burning; the Coiling Snake and Wood also speak of hanging or strangling, and the Hook Earth with Earth Pressure of jail. Worked example: in the Wei month, on a Guihai day, a man divined what charge would be fixed and got Zhongfu changing to Lin: Wood Pressure on the White Tiger means a sentence of strangulation is fixed; but fortunately the Self sits on the Month, and though the Wood Pressure moves it is controlled by a turning-back control from You-metal, and the outer trigram is Reversed Chant, so the matter will surely go back and forth; though strangulation is fixed there will in the end be a change; indeed strangulation was recommended, and later the case was sent back for retrial and the sentence reduced.

> [!note] 按
> 原文的小注「驳番拨活罪」是说驳回重审后拨作可以活命的罪名；这里译作驳回重审后减轻。
>
> *Note:* The original's small note says the case was sent back and reassigned to a charge that lets the man live; it is rendered as sent back for retrial and reduced.
