# 词讼·间伤世位须防硬证同谋、鬼克间爻且喜有司明见：间爻是中证人，生世合世向着我，生应合应向着他；与世冲克的与我有仇；若冲克我的爻反去生合应爻，要防证人同谋陷害；有鬼爻克制或日辰冲克，官府不听他的话，我无事；间爻受刑克中证必遭杖责

六爻 · 官非 · 人物 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻伤害世位，要防备硬证人同谋；鬼爻克间爻，还喜得官府看得明白。间爻是作中证的人：生世合世，必定向着我；生应合应，必定向着他；与世爻冲克，与我有仇；与应爻冲克，与他有隙。如果旺爻生应、衰爻合世，是帮他的有力、帮我的没有功效；或者应爻发动克世，是向着他的人，即使不上前怪我，偏偏会出面。如果冲克我的爻反去生应合应，须防证人同谋陷害；如果得到鬼爻克制或被日辰冲克，是官府不听他的话，我无事。间爻如果受刑克，作证的人必遭杖责；靠近世爻的必是我的干证，靠近应爻的是他的干证。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a between line injures the Self position, beware of hard witnesses in a plot; if Pressure controls the between line, one is glad that the court sees clearly. The between lines are the witnesses: one giving life to or combining with the Self surely favours me; one giving life to or combining with the Other surely favours him; one clashing or controlling the Self has a feud with me; one clashing or controlling the Other has a rift with him. If a strong line gives life to the Other and a weak line combines with the Self, those helping him are powerful and those helping me are ineffective; or if the Other moves to control the Self, those on his side, though they do not step forward to blame me, will come out in the open all the same. If a line that clashes or controls me instead gives life to or combines with the Other, beware of witnesses plotting together to frame me; if it is controlled by the Pressure line or clashed and controlled by the Day, the officials will not listen to its words and I come to no harm. If a between line is punished or controlled, the witness is surely beaten with the rod; the one near the Self is surely my witness, and the one near the Other is his witness.
