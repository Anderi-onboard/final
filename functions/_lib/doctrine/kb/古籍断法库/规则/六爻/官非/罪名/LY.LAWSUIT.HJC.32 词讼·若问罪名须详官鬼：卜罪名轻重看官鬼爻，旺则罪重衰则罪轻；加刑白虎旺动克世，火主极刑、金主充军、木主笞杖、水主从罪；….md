# 词讼·若问罪名须详官鬼：卜罪名轻重看官鬼爻，旺则罪重衰则罪轻；加刑白虎旺动克世，火主极刑、金主充军、木主笞杖、水主从罪；要按衰旺和有制无制来断，不可拘泥

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 如果问罪名，必须详察官鬼。凡是占罪名轻重，用官鬼爻来定：旺，罪重；衰，罪轻。再加刑、白虎、旺而发动克世爻，火主受极刑，金主充军，木主笞杖，水主从犯罪。要按衰旺、有制无制来断，不可拘泥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If asking about the charge, one must examine Pressure. In judging how heavy or light the charge is, fix it by the Pressure line: strong, the charge is heavy; weak, light. With punishment and the White Tiger added, strong and moving to control the Self, Fire means the extreme penalty, Metal means military exile, Wood means the cane or rod, Water means being an accessory. Judge by strength and weakness and whether it is held in check or not, and do not be rigid.
