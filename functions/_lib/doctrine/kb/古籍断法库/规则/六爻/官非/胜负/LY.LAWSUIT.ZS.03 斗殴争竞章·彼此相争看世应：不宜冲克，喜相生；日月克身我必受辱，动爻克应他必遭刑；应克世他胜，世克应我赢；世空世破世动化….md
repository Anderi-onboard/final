# 斗殴争竞章·彼此相争看世应：不宜冲克，喜相生；日月克身我必受辱，动爻克应他必遭刑；应克世他胜，世克应我赢；世空世破世动化退我胆怯，应动应旺应化进神他得势；六冲必散，六合必成

六爻 · 官非 · 胜负 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 彼此相争，要看世爻应爻：不适宜冲克，喜欢相生；日月克身，我必定受辱；动爻克应爻，他必定遭刑；应爻克世爻他胜，世爻克应爻我赢。世爻空、世爻破、世爻动而化退，我心里胆怯；应爻动、应爻旺、应爻化进神，他得势了。卦遇六冲一定散，卦逢六合一定成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the two sides contest, look to the Self and the Other: clash and control are not fit and giving life is welcome; if the Month or the Day controls the Body, I surely suffer insult; if a moving line controls the Other, he surely suffers punishment; the Other controlling the Self means he wins, the Self controlling the Other means I win. The Self Void, the Self broken, or the Self moving and changing into a Retreating spirit means I am timid at heart; the Other moving, the Other strong, or the Other changing into an Advancing spirit means he has the upper hand. A hexagram meeting six-clash surely breaks up; a hexagram meeting six-combine surely comes to terms.
