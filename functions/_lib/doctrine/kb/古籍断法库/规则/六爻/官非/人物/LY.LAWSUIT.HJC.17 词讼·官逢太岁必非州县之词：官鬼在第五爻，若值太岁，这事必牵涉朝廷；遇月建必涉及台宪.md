# 词讼·官逢太岁必非州县之词：官鬼在第五爻，若值太岁，这事必牵涉朝廷；遇月建必涉及台宪

六爻 · 官非 · 人物 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼逢太岁，必定不是州县的案子。官鬼在第五爻，如果值太岁，这件事必定牵涉朝廷；遇月建，必定涉及台宪。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure meeting Tai Sui surely means this is not a case for a prefecture or county. If Pressure is on the fifth line and sits on Tai Sui, the matter surely concerns the court; meeting the Month, it surely involves the censorate and the provincial authorities.
