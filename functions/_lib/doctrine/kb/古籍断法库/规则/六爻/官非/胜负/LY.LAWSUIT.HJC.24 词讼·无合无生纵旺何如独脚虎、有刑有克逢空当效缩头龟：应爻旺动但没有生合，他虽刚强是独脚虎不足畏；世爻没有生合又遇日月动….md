# 词讼·无合无生纵旺何如独脚虎、有刑有克逢空当效缩头龟：应爻旺动但没有生合，他虽刚强是独脚虎不足畏；世爻没有生合又遇日月动爻刑克，应当学缩头龟，暂且别和他对理

六爻 · 官非 · 胜负 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 没有合没有生，即使旺又怎么样，不过是独脚的老虎；有刑有克又逢空，应当学缩头的乌龟。应爻旺而发动、没有生合的，他虽然刚强，是独脚虎不值得怕；世爻没有生合，又遇到日月动爻刑克，应当学缩头龟，暂且不要和他对理。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With no combination and no life, however strong, what is it but a one-legged tiger? With punishment and control, and also in the Void, one should act like a tortoise drawing in its head. An Other that is strong and moving but has no life or combination is, for all its hardness, a one-legged tiger not worth fearing; a Self with no life or combination that also meets punishment or control from the Month, the Day or a moving line should act like a tortoise drawing in its head and for the time being not argue the case with him.
