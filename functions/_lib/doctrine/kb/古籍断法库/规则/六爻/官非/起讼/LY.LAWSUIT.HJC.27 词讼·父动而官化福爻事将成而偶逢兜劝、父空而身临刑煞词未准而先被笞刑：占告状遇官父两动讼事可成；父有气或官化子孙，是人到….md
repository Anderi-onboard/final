# 词讼·父动而官化福爻事将成而偶逢兜劝、父空而身临刑煞词未准而先被笞刑：占告状遇官父两动讼事可成；父有气或官化子孙，是人到公门将投状时有人劝止；父化空亡墓绝、官鬼刑克世爻或被日辰刑冲克害，状子不准先挨板子

六爻 · 官非 · 起讼 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母发动而官鬼化出福神，事情将成时偶然遇到有人拉着劝；父母落空而身爻临刑煞，状词还没批准就先挨笞刑。凡是占告状，遇到官鬼、父母两爻都动，官司可以成；如果父母有气，或官鬼化出子孙，就是人到公门将要投状，有人在旁边劝；如果父母化出空亡墓绝，官鬼刑克世爻，或者被日辰刑冲克害，状告不准，先遭杖责。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Resource moving and Pressure changing into Output, just as the matter is about to be done someone happens to pull one back and urge restraint; with Resource Void and the Body on a punishing star, the plaint is not yet accepted and one is beaten first. In divining for lodging a complaint, if Pressure and Resource both move, the suit can go ahead; if Resource has breath, or Pressure changes into Output, then when one reaches the court door about to submit the plaint someone persuades one against it; if Resource changes into the Void, tomb or extinction, Pressure punishes or controls the Self, or is punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Day, the complaint is not accepted and one is first beaten with the rod.
