# 兴词举讼章·官父同兴公庭有理、父官克世受屈含冤：世化退神、世空世破、卦变六冲、世被刑冲，终不成讼；财与子孙同动克破文书官鬼，也不成

六爻 · 官非 · 起讼 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼和父母同时发动，在公堂上有理；父母、官鬼克世爻，受屈含冤。世爻化退神、世爻空或破、卦变成六冲、世爻被刑冲，终究不成讼；财爻和子孙同时发动，克破文书和官鬼，也主不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure and Resource moving together, one is in the right in the public hall; Resource and Pressure controlling the Self, one suffers wrong and swallows the grievance. The Self changing into a Retreating spirit, the Self Void or broken, the hexagram changing into six-clash, or the Self punished or clashed, in the end no suit is brought; Wealth and Output moving together to control and break the papers and Pressure also means it is not brought.
