# 十八问答·第二问·书中例，占弟被讼：酉金兄弟为用神，午月克它、未日生它似可相敌，但动出午火月建相克是大凶象；问凶在何时，说今年辰年太岁相合无妨，化退神于申，恐危在午年申月，果然午年申月被重刑

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡遇回头克的，彻底克尽。书中例：午月丁未日占弟弟被人告了吉凶如何，得困卦变恒卦：酉金兄弟爻为用神，午月克它，未日生它，似乎可以相抵；但是动出午火，和月建相克，是大凶之象；他问凶在什么时候，我说今年是辰年，太岁相合没有妨碍，化出退神于申，恐怕危险在午年申月；果然到午年申月被判重刑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whatever meets a turning-back control is controlled through and through. Worked example: in the Wu month, on a Dingwei day, a man divined how things stood for his younger brother, who had been sued, and got Kun changing to Heng: the Peer line You-metal is the Subject, controlled by the Wu month and given life by the Wei day, which seems to set them even; but it moves out Wu-fire, which, controlled by the Month, is a sign of great ill. He asked when the ill would fall. I said: this year, a Chen year, Tai Sui combines with it and there is no harm, but it changes into a Retreating spirit at Shen, and I fear danger in the Shen month of a Wu year; indeed in the Shen month of the Wu year he was given a heavy punishment.
