# 五行相克章·书中例，占父官事已拟重罪：外卦未土父母被卯月克，内卦亥卯未合成木局又克它，月克日刑全无救助，果然被重刑——有克无生

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 忌神、仇神，适宜逢克：有月克、日克、动爻克、用神动化回头克。这四种，用神、元神只要逢一克，别处又见不到生扶的，就是凶兆；占吉事，乐极生悲；占凶事，须赶紧回避。书中例：卯月戊辰日占父亲的官事已经拟了重罪，得萃卦变同人卦：外卦未土父母，被卯月克它，内卦亥卯未合成木局又相克制，月克日刑，全无救助，果然被重刑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Adversary and the enemy spirit should meet control: control by the Month, by the Day, by a moving line, or a turning-back control from the Subject's own change. In these four, if the Subject or the Support meets even one control and sees no life or support elsewhere, it is an ill sign; in divining for a lucky matter, joy brings sorrow; in divining for an ill matter, hurry to avoid it. Worked example: in the Mao month, on a Wuchen day, a man divined about his father's official matter, for which a grave sentence had already been drafted, and got Cui changing to Tongren: the Resource Wei-earth of the outer trigram is controlled by the Mao month, and the inner trigram Hai, Mao and Wei combine into a Wood frame that controls it again; controlled by the Month and punished by the Day, with no rescue at all, he was indeed severely punished.
