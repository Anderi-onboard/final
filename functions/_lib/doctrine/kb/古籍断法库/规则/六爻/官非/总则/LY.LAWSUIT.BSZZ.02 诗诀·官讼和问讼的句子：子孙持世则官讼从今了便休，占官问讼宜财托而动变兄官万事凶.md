# 诗诀·官讼和问讼的句子：子孙持世则官讼从今了便休，占官问讼宜财托而动变兄官万事凶

六爻 · 官非 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙持世，官司从今了结就罢休；财爻持世，求官问讼适宜用财去托人，发动变出兄弟、官鬼，事事皆凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With Output holding the Self, the lawsuit ends and rests from now on; with Wealth holding the Self, in seeking office or asking about a suit it is fit to entrust the matter with money, and if Peer or Pressure moves or changes out, everything is ill.

> [!note] 按
> 这两句出在诸爻持世诀的两联：官讼一句在「子孙持世」那一联，问讼一句在「财爻持世」那一联；每联开头的持世爻没有拆进引文里。
>
> *Note:* The two lines come from two couplets of the verse on lines holding the Self: the line on lawsuits is in the couplet for Output holding the Self, and the line on asking about a suit is in the couplet for Wealth holding the Self; the line named at the start of each couplet is not split into the quotation.
