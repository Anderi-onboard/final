# 已定重罪章·福宜旺相最忌官兴：子孙持世、子孙发动，已赴法场终必赦免；官鬼克世或持世又被日月动爻冲克，或世动化凶，虽定轻罪也拘改重

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙适宜旺相，最忌官鬼兴盛。子孙持世、子孙发动，已经送到法场，终究会赦免；官鬼克世爻、或官鬼持世，又被日月动爻冲克，或者世爻发动化出凶的，虽然判的是轻罪也会拘押改判为重罪。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output should be strong, and Pressure flourishing is most feared. Output holding the Self or moving, even one already taken to the execution ground is in the end pardoned; Pressure controlling the Self or holding the Self and also clashed or controlled by the Month, the Day or a moving line, or the Self moving and changing into something ill, means that though a light sentence is fixed he is held and it is changed to a heavy one.
