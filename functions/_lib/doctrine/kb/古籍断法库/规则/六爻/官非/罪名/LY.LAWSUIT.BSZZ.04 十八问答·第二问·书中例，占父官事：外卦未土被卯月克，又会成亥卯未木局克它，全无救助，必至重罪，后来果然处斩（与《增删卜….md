# 十八问答·第二问·书中例，占父官事：外卦未土被卯月克，又会成亥卯未木局克它，全无救助，必至重罪，后来果然处斩（与《增删卜易》同一卦同一日，那边记为拟重罪被重刑）

六爻 · 官非 · 罪名 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡遇回头克的，彻底克尽。书中例：卯月戊辰日有人占父亲的官事，得萃卦变同人卦：断语说，外卦未土被卯月克它，何况土在春令气败，又会成亥卯未木局克它，全无救助，必定到重罪；后来果然被斩。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whatever meets a turning-back control is controlled through and through. Worked example: in the Mao month, on a Wuchen day, a man divined about his father's official matter and got Cui changing to Tongren: the Judgement said that the Wei-earth of the outer trigram is controlled by the Mao month, and moreover Earth is spent in the spring command and Hai, Mao and Wei combine into a Wood frame that controls it, with no rescue at all, so it must come to a grave charge; afterwards he was indeed beheaded.

> [!note] 按
> 《增删卜易》五行相克章收了同一个卦（卯月戊辰日，萃之同人），判词几乎一样，结局记为「果被重刑」；这里记为「后果斩」。
>
> *Note:* The chapter on the controlling cycle in the Zengshan Buyi records the same hexagram (the Mao month, a Wuchen day, Cui changing to Tongren) with almost the same judgement, but the outcome is given as "severely punished"; here it is "beheaded".
