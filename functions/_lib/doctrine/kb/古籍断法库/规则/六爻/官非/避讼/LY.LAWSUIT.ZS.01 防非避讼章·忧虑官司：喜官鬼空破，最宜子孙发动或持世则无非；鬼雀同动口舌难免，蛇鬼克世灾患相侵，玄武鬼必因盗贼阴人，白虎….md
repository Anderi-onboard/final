# 防非避讼章·忧虑官司：喜官鬼空破，最宜子孙发动或持世则无非；鬼雀同动口舌难免，蛇鬼克世灾患相侵，玄武鬼必因盗贼阴人，白虎鬼必有伤痕见血；世动克应是我兴词，应克世爻是他有讼

六爻 · 官非 · 避讼 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 担心官司的，喜欢官鬼落在空亡或月破，最适宜子孙发动、子孙持世，就没有是非；官鬼和朱雀一起发动，口舌免不了；螣蛇官鬼克世，灾祸忧患相侵；玄武官鬼，必定因盗贼和阴人；白虎官鬼，必定有伤痕见血；世爻动而克应爻，是我兴词告人；应爻克世爻，是他有讼要告我。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In worrying about a lawsuit, one is glad that Pressure is Void or broken, and most fit is Output moving or holding the Self, which means no trouble; Pressure and the Vermilion Bird moving together mean quarrels cannot be avoided; the Coiling Snake and Pressure controlling the Self mean calamity and distress press in; the Dark Tortoise and Pressure surely come from thieves and women; the White Tiger and Pressure surely mean wounds and blood; the Self moving to control the Other means I file a plaint; the Other controlling the Self means he has a suit against me.
