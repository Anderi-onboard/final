# 十八问答·第八问·书中例，占讼事：爻逢六合官事必审，但戌月冲世是月破、卯日克世必输无疑，果然被杖责——应在日克月破

六爻 · 官非 · 胜负 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月破的爻：神机显现在破；祸福的机缘根源在动，动而有生无克的破爻，有出破、填实、合破的办法；安静而有克无生的破爻，就是破了。书中例：戌月丁卯日占官司，得泰卦：卦里爻逢六合，官事必定要审；不适宜的是戌月冲世爻，这是月破，卯日又克世爻，必输无疑；果然被杖责。这是应在日克月破的缘故。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* On a line struck by the Month-break: the working of the spirits shows in the break; the root of luck and misfortune lies in movement, and a broken line that moves and has life without control has the ways of leaving the break, being filled, or being combined out of the break, while a still broken line with control and no life is broken. Worked example: in the Xu month, on a Dingmao day, a man divined about a lawsuit and got Tai: the lines meet six-combine, so the official matter must come to trial; what is unfit is the Xu month clashing the Self line, which is the Month-break, and the Mao day also controlling the Self, so he surely loses; indeed he was beaten with the rod. This answers because the Day controls the Month-break.
