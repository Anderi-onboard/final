# 何知章·讼事与争竞的几句：讼事休是空亡官鬼又休囚，讼事多是朱雀白虎持世而鬼来扶，多争竞是朱雀兄弟推世应，小人生是玄武官鬼动临身，灾祸至是鬼临应爻来克世

六爻 · 官非 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 怎么知道人家的官司了结：官鬼落空亡又休囚。怎么知道人家官司多：朱雀、白虎持世，官鬼来扶助。怎么知道人家多争执：朱雀、兄弟推动世爻应爻。怎么知道人家有小人：玄武、官鬼发动临在身爻。怎么知道人家灾祸到：官鬼临在应爻来克世爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* How do we know a household's suit is over? Pressure is Void and also resting or trapped. How do we know a household has many suits? The Vermilion Bird or the White Tiger holds the Self and Pressure comes to support it. How do we know a household has many quarrels? The Vermilion Bird and the Peer line push on the Self and the Other. How do we know a petty person has appeared? The Dark Tortoise and Pressure move and sit on the Body. How do we know calamity is coming? Pressure sits on the Other line and comes to control the Self.
