# 词讼·墓逢日德刑冲目下即当出狱、岁挈福神生合狱中必遇天恩：世墓、鬼墓动是入狱之象，得日辰刑冲克破眼下就出狱；狱中占卦最喜太岁生合世爻有天恩赦宥，月建生合上司审出，日辰生合有司饶恕，父母生合申诉得免

六爻 · 官非 · 狱囚 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 墓逢日德和刑冲，眼下就该出狱；太岁带着福神来生合，狱中必遇天恩。世墓、鬼墓发动，都是入狱的象，如果得到日辰刑冲克破，眼下就该出狱。在狱中占卦，最喜太岁生合世爻，主有天恩赦免；月建生合，上司审出来；日辰生合，有关官员饶恕；父母爻生合，必须申诉才能免。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The tomb meeting the Day's virtue and punishment or clash, he should leave prison at once; Tai Sui bringing Output to give life and combine, in prison he surely meets imperial grace. The tomb of the Self or of Pressure moving is the image of entering prison, and if the Day punishes, clashes, controls or breaks it, he should be out at once. Divining in prison, best of all is Tai Sui giving life to or combining with the Self, which means imperial pardon; the Month giving life or combining means the higher office trials him out; the Day giving life or combining, the officials spare him; Resource giving life or combining, he must plead to be let off.
