# 娶妻买婢，用爻宜静，有气为先

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 娶妻、买婢女这一类事（同一排里还有买房宅坟田、住庵、拜见人、寻失物、申冤），用爻宜静，以有气（旺相有力）为先。这一排之后的「取索借债、读书请客、生产求归、脱货赎解、官员待次、散事解灾」眉批也标「宜静」；再后面「改易叙职、告欲干求、脱诈失约、行人远信、出行迁移」则标「宜动」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For taking a wife or buying a maid (in the same row: houses, tombs and fields, staying in a hermitage, calling on someone, looking for lost goods, righting a wrong) the Subject line should be still, and having breath comes first. The marginal comment marks the row after it too ('claims and loans, reading, inviting guests, childbirth, releasing goods, an official awaiting a post, dispersing trouble') as 'should be still'; the row after that ('changing posts, petitioning, cheating and broken promises, letters from travellers, journeys and moves') is marked 'should move'.

> [!warning] 打架
> 同一本书前面说占妻要看财爻「宜动」（LY.MARRY.HDY.02），这里又说娶妻用爻「宜静」，两处相反，书里没有解释。《火珠林》（LY.MARRY.HZL.04）和《易林补遗》（LY.MARRY.YL.19）都说占妻看财要静。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Earlier the same book says that for divining a wife the Wealth line 'should move' (LY.MARRY.HDY.02), and here that the Subject line for taking a wife 'should be still'; the two are opposite and the book does not explain. The Huozhulin (LY.MARRY.HZL.04) and the Yilin Buyi (LY.MARRY.YL.19) both say that in divining a wife the Wealth line should be still.

> [!note] 按
> 这一排的头一句「娶妻买婢」是婚姻在这份「用爻宜静」名单里的位置。原文这一行紧接着还有两排：「取索借债……散事解灾」后面括着眉批「宜静」，「改易叙职……出行迁移」后面括着眉批「宜动」，婚姻不在第二排里。眉批「宜静」在原文里放在第二排的后面，读法是它管着前面两排，这里照此读；书里没有再多说。《断易天机》（LY.MARRY.DYTJ.18）有同一句，作「娶妻买妾，用爻宜静，有气为先」，只差「婢」和「妾」一个字。
>
> *Note:* The first phrase of this row, 'taking a wife and buying a maid', is where marriage stands in this list of matters where the Subject line should be still. In the original two more rows follow: 'claims and loans ... dispersing trouble' is followed by the marginal comment 'should be still', and 'changing posts ... journeys and moves' by 'should move'; marriage is not in the second. The comment 'should be still' comes after the second row in the original, and it is read here as governing the two rows before it; the book says nothing more. The Duanyi Tianji (LY.MARRY.DYTJ.18) has the same line as 'taking a wife or a concubine, the Subject line should be still, and having breath comes first', differing only in 'concubine' for 'maid'.
