# 归藏易「妻临克位号重财」：财爻所带六神克它所在的宫（震宫带白虎、坤艮带青龙、乾兑带雀、离带武、坎带勾蛇）叫重财；四直贵马德合聚一爻旺相居二五爻得太岁扶无刑破必为驸马仪宾；出嫁后妇人贵贱贫富从夫同世爻断

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《归藏易》说：妻爻临在克位，叫重财——像金爻进入震宫；贵人、煞会合又正旺相，因妻子而受禄，可比三公。比如震宫的财爻带白虎，白虎属金又克震木，所以叫重财；坤、艮宫的财爻带青龙，青龙属木又克坤艮土，也是重财；乾、兑宫的财爻带朱雀，离宫的财爻带玄武，坎宫的财爻带勾陈、螣蛇，都是。如果四直、贵人、驿马、德神、合聚在同一个爻上，又旺相居二爻或五爻，再得太岁扶持，没有刑破，一定是驸马、仪宾（公主、郡主的丈夫）。出嫁以后，妇人的贵贱贫富跟从丈夫，都同世爻一样来断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Guicang Yi says: the wife's line on a controlling position is called doubled wealth, like a Metal line entering Zhen; with the Nobleman and baleful stars meeting and in strength, one receives emolument through the wife, comparable to the Three Ministers. For example, the Wealth line in the Zhen palace carrying the White Tiger: the White Tiger is Metal and controls Zhen's Wood, so it is called doubled wealth; the Wealth line in the Kun or Gen palace carrying the Azure Dragon: the Azure Dragon is Wood and controls the Earth of Kun and Gen, so this too is doubled wealth; in the Qian or Dui palace with the Vermilion Bird, in the Li palace with the Dark Tortoise, in the Kan palace with Hook Earth or the Coiling Snake: all are so. If the four rulers, the Nobleman, the Post Horse, Virtue and combination gather on one line, strong and on the second or fifth line, and helped by Tai Sui, without punishment or break, he is surely an imperial son-in-law or a consort of a princess. After she marries, the woman's rank and wealth follow her husband and are all judged like the Self line.

> [!note] 按
> 《归藏易》在这里是引来的一句口诀，不是传世的《归藏》。「重财」的道理是财爻带的六神克它所在的宫，原文接着用震、坤艮等例子说明。
>
> *Note:* The Guicang Yi here is a quoted rhyme, not the transmitted Guicang. The idea of 'doubled wealth' is that the spirit the Wealth line carries controls the palace it sits in, and the original goes on to illustrate this with Zhen, Kun and Gen and the rest.
