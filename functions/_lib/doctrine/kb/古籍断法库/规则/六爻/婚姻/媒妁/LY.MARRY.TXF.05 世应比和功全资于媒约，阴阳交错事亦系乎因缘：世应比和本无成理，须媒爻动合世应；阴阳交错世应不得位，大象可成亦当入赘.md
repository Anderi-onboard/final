# 世应比和功全资于媒约，阴阳交错事亦系乎因缘：世应比和本无成理，须媒爻动合世应；阴阳交错世应不得位，大象可成亦当入赘

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应比和（不生不克），本来没有成就的道理，要靠媒爻发动、合住世应才能成。阴阳交错，是世应没有得位；如果大势能成，也应当是入赘，否则夫妻互相欺凌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Self and Other are in harmony as equals, with neither generating nor controlling, there is by itself no reason for success, and it needs the go-between line to move and combine with Self and Other. Yin and yang crossed means that Self and Other are not in their proper places; if the overall image allows it to be made, it should be a match in which the man enters the wife's household, or husband and wife will overbear each other.
