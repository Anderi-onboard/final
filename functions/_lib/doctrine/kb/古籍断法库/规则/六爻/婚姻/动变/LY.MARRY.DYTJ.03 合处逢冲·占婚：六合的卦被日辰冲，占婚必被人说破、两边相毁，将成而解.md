# 合处逢冲·占婚：六合的卦被日辰冲，占婚必被人说破、两边相毁，将成而解

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻相合的卦，遇到日辰冲世应，叫合处逢冲。占婚姻遇到：一定被人说破，两边互相诋毁，快成的时候又散了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hexagram whose lines all combine but whose Self or Other line is clashed by the Day is a combination meeting a clash. In a marriage divination, meeting this, the match is sure to be talked to pieces by others, both sides slander each other, and what is about to be made falls apart.

> | 且如占得雷地豫卦。 | 比如占到雷地豫卦。 | Suppose the divination gets the Yu hexagram, Thunder over Earth. |
> | 世应相生，六爻相合，吉无不利，乃事事可成之象也。 | 世应相生，六爻相合，吉利没有不顺的，是事事可成的样子。 | Self and Other generate each other and the six lines combine; all is auspicious and nothing goes against it; it is the look of everything succeeding. |
> | 却在子日占得， | 却是在子日占到的， | But it is got on a Zi day, |
> | 子冲应上午、害世上未， | 子冲应爻上的午、又害世爻上的未， | and Zi clashes the Wu on the Other line and harms the Wei on the Self line; |
> | 此乃合处逢冲也。 | 这就是合处逢冲。 | this is 'meeting a clash where there was combination'. |
