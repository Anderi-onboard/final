# 婚姻遇命墓，中途定见刑伤：女命入墓戒娶，男命入墓戒嫁；不论世鬼、不拘空破

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 自身和本命遇到入墓，一辈子多灾多病；婚姻遇到这种情形，中途一定会有刑伤，占时要分男的命墓和女的命墓。小注说：不必管世爻和官鬼，也不管落空、破；女子的命入墓，男方要戒娶；男子的命入墓，女方要戒嫁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If one's own person and Life meet this, a whole lifetime brings many calamities and illnesses; if a marriage meets this, there will surely be injury midway, and the divination tells apart the man's Life-tomb from the woman's. The note says: set aside the Self line and Pressure, and do not be bound by Void or broken; if the woman's Life is in the tomb, beware of taking her as a wife; if the man's Life is in the tomb, beware of marrying him.

> [!note] 按
> 「逢之」「遇此」指随墓章前面说的入墓：命随官鬼入墓、世爻入墓、化爻入墓等（见 LY.MARRY.YM.02）。「戒娶」是男方不宜娶她，「戒嫁」是女方不宜嫁他。
>
> *Note:* 'Meet it' and 'meets this' refer to the entering of the tomb described earlier in the chapter: the Life following Pressure into the tomb, the Self line in the tomb, a changed line in the tomb, and so on (see LY.MARRY.YM.02). 'Beware of taking' means the man should not take her as a wife, and 'beware of marrying' means the woman should not marry him.
