# 何知章·青龙：青龙进钱财，住宅婚姻进口来

六爻 · 婚姻 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙进钱财，住宅和婚姻都会进人口来；出入求财大有所获，造作添修也没有灾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon brings in money; for a house and for a marriage, people are added to the household; going in and out seeking wealth, one will gain greatly, and building or repairs bring no calamity.

## 《断易天机·散见各章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 979 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 青龙进钱财， | 青龙主进钱财， | The Azure Dragon brings in money; |
> | 住宅婚姻进口来， | 住宅、婚姻，人口物件都进来； | for house and marriage, people and goods come in; |
> | 出入求财当大获， | 出门入门求财，该有大收获； | going out and coming in to seek wealth, there should be a great haul; |
> | 造作添修不入灾。 | 建造修缮不遇灾。 | building and repairs meet no calamity. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「进口」是添人口：住宅添人口，婚姻娶进新人。
>
> *Note:* 'Bringing in mouths' means adding people to the household: for a house, more members; for a marriage, a new member brought in.
