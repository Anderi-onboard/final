# 论贫富，当究身命；决美恶，可验性情：问妇看财爻、问夫看鬼爻、问男家看世爻、问女家看应爻；应旺财衰，女家富而女貌不扬

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要看男女的贫富美恶：问妇看财爻，问夫的身看鬼爻，问男家看世爻，问女家看应爻；应旺而财衰，是女家虽富，女子相貌不出众；维持和拆台的旁人，用刑冲生合的爻来推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To judge the wealth, poverty, looks and character of the man or woman: to ask about the bride look at the Wealth line, about the bridegroom look at the Pressure line, about the man's family the Self line, about the woman's family the Other line; the Other thriving and Wealth weak means the woman's family is rich but her looks are unremarkable; the bystanders who support or wreck it are judged by the lines that punish, clash, generate or combine.

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 男问妇看财爻，女问夫看鬼爻，女问男家、男问女家皆看应爻。 | 男子问妻子看财爻，女子问丈夫看鬼爻，女子问男家、男子问女家都看应爻。 | A man asking about a wife looks at the Wealth line, a woman asking about a husband the Pressure line, and a woman asking about the man's family or a man about the woman's, the Other line. |
> | 若应旺财衰，女家虽富，女貌不扬。 | 如果应旺财衰，女家虽然富有，女子相貌不出众。 | If the Other thrives and Wealth is weak, the woman's family though rich has a daughter of unremarkable looks. |
> | 余类推。 | 其余类推。 | The rest by analogy. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 看男家女家的爻，旧注和王洪绪不同：旧注是问男家看世、问女家看应；王洪绪是问对方的家一律看应。
>
> *Note:* For looking at the families the old commentary and Wang Hongxu differ: the old commentary looks at the Self for the man's family and the Other for the woman's; Wang Hongxu looks at the Other for whichever is the other party's family.
