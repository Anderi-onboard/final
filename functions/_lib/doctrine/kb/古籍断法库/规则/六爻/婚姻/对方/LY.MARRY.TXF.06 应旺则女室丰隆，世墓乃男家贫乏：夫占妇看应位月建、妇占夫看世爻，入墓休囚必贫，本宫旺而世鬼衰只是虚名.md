# 应旺则女室丰隆，世墓乃男家贫乏：夫占妇看应位月建、妇占夫看世爻，入墓休囚必贫，本宫旺而世鬼衰只是虚名

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻旺，女方家里富足；世爻入墓，男方家里贫乏。男占女，应爻在月建上旺，是富户，但日下休囚，就是近年略有衰退；女占男，世爻要旺相，男家才豪富，入墓、休囚必然贫乏；本宫虽然旺，但世爻和官鬼都衰，只是虚名。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other line strong means the woman's household is abundant; Self in the tomb means the man's family is poor. When a husband divines a wife, if the Other line is strong in the Month, it is a wealthy family without office, but if it is resting and trapped under the Day, its wealth has slipped somewhat in recent years; when a wife divines a husband, Self must be strong for the man's family to be rich, and in the tomb or resting and trapped it means poverty; if the home palace is strong but Self and Pressure are both weak, it is only an empty name.
