# 兄临玄武防劫骗，鬼临白虎遇凶丧：兄鬼动临玄武防局中奸诈骗财，世应相生财官无害也要大破费；鬼临白虎动，未过门或过门不久必见丧事

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻带玄武，要防被劫、被骗；鬼爻带白虎，会遇到凶丧之事。兄弟爻、鬼爻动而带玄武，要防局中有人奸诈骗钱，即使世应相生、财官无害，也要大破费。鬼爻带白虎而动，或是还没过门，或是过门不久，一定见丧事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Peer line with the Dark Tortoise: beware of robbery and cheating; a Pressure line with the White Tiger: you will meet a death. When a Peer or Pressure line moves with the Dark Tortoise, beware of a treacherous scheme to cheat you of money; even if the Self and Other lines generate each other and Wealth and Pressure are unharmed, there will still be great expense. A moving Pressure line with the White Tiger means that, whether before she has entered the house or not long after, a funeral will surely be seen.
