# 配仆：财爻为用；财爻持世或动而生合世、化进化生、财化财，得忠诚出力之人；散破绝空非懒即病；克世冲世忘恩负义；化鬼化绝化墓化克及鬼化财，贫穷夭折

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 配仆（安排仆人）：以财爻为用神。财爻持世，或者动了来生合世爻，或者动而化进神、化生，或者财化财，都会得到忠诚肯出力的人；财爻散、破、绝、空，不是懒就是病；财爻克世、冲世，是忘恩负义的人；财爻化成鬼、绝、墓、克，以及鬼爻化成财爻，是贫穷短命之类的人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Taking on a servant: the Wealth line is the Subject. With the Wealth line on the Self line, or moving to generate or combine with the Self line, or moving and changing into an Advancing spirit or a generator, or Wealth changing into Wealth, you gain someone loyal who serves with effort; if the Wealth line is scattered, broken, extinguished or Void, the person is either lazy or ill; if the Wealth line controls or clashes with the Self line, the person betrays kindness and forgets favors; if Wealth changes into Pressure, extinction, tomb or a controller, or Pressure changes into Wealth, the person is of the poor and short-lived sort.

> [!note] 按
