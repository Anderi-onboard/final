# 世应三合咸池拟定先通后娶，财鬼一临玄武定知眼去眉来：应与财带咸池玄武合世与鬼必先通后娶，合旁爻必与外人有情；咸池被冲淫而不滥，遇进神生合淫心荡漾，遇退神有欲无实

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应三合再带咸池，一定是先私通、后娶亲；财、官一同临玄武，一定是眉来眼去。应爻和财爻带咸池、临玄武，又和世爻、官鬼相合，必定先通后娶；和旁边的爻相合，必与外人有情。应爻带咸池玄武，是妻淫；世爻带，是夫淫。咸池被日辰冲，淫而不滥；贵人动来克它，不淫，只是容貌美丽、多情好欢；咸池遇进神动来生合，淫心荡漾；遇退神或被退神克，虽有欲心而没有实事，不过眼去眉来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Self and Other in a three-harmony frame with the Xianchi star, it is settled that intimacy comes first and marriage after; Wealth and Pressure both on the Dark Tortoise, you will know they are exchanging glances. If the Other line and Wealth carry the Xianchi star and the Dark Tortoise and combine with the Self line and Pressure, there is surely intimacy first and marriage after; if they combine with another line, she is surely attached to an outsider. The Other line with Xianchi and the Dark Tortoise means the wife is licentious; the Self line with them, the husband. If the Day clashes Xianchi, she is licentious without excess; if the Noble moves to control it she is not licentious but fair in bearing, affectionate and fond of pleasure, more so when strong and less when dead or cut off; if Xianchi meets an Advancing spirit that moves to generate or combine with it, the licentious heart wanders and acts as it pleases; if it meets a Retreating spirit, or a Retreating spirit controls it, there is desire but no real deed, only glances and looks.

> [!note] 按
> 「拟定」是「断定、一定」。「贵人动来克」一句原书文字不易读，译文照字面译。
>
> *Note:* 'Ni ding' means it is settled. The sentence on the Noble moving to control it is hard to read in the book and is translated literally.
