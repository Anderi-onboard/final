# 箕踞鼓盆歌，世伤应位：世持白虎螣蛇、乘旺发动，刑害应爻，应又临无气之地，必主克妻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻带着白虎或螣蛇，乘旺相发动，去刑害应爻，应爻又落在没有气的地方，一定克妻，就像庄周的妻子死了，他还箕踞着敲盆唱歌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line carrying the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake, moving on strength and punishing and harming the Other line, while the Other line sits in a place without qi, means the wife is surely harmed, like Zhuang Zhou, who sat sprawled drumming on a basin and singing when his wife died.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1700 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世持虎蛇临兄弟，乘旺发动，刑害应爻，应爻临无气之地，必主克妻，如春秋时庄子，妻死鼓盆而歌。 | 世爻带白虎、螣蛇，又是兄弟爻，乘旺发动，刑害应爻，应爻落在没有气的地方，一定克妻，就像春秋时的庄子，妻子死了敲着盆唱歌。 | The Self line carrying the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake and being a Peer line, moving on strength, punishing and harming the Other line, while the Other line sits in a place without qi: the wife is surely harmed, like Zhuangzi in the Spring and Autumn period, who drummed on a basin and sang when his wife died. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗把「箕踞」写成「僦居」，是同一句的异文。庄子妻死鼓盆而歌，是《庄子·至乐》里的故事，这里只借它指「妻死」。卜筮正宗的注比卜筮全书多了一个条件：世爻还得是兄弟爻（「临兄弟」）。
>
> *Note:* The Zhengzong writes 'jiu ju' where the Quanshu has 'ji ju' (sitting sprawled), a variant of the same line. Zhuangzi drumming on a basin and singing at his wife's death is the story in the Zhuangzi, 'Perfect Joy', and is used here only to stand for the wife's death. The Zhengzong's note adds one condition the Quanshu lacks: the Self line must also be a Peer line.
