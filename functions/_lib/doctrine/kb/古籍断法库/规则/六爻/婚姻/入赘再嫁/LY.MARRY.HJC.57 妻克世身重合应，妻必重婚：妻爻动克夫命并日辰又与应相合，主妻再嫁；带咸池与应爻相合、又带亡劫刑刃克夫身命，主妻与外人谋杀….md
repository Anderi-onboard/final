# 妻克世身重合应，妻必重婚：妻爻动克夫命并日辰又与应相合，主妻再嫁；带咸池与应爻相合、又带亡劫刑刃克夫身命，主妻与外人谋杀夫主

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻子的爻发动克丈夫的命，又遇日辰与应爻相合，主妻子再嫁；如果带咸池与应爻相合，再加日辰、动爻带亡神、劫杀、刑、刃等煞，克丈夫的身命爻，主妻子与外人谋杀丈夫。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the wife's line moves to control the husband's Life and the Day also combines with the Other line, the wife marries again; if it carries the Xianchi star and combines with the Other line, and the Day and a moving line add the Loss star, Robbery star, punishment or Goat Blade stars and control the husband's Body or Life line, it means the wife plots with an outsider to kill her husband.

> | 财爻动克夫命，或妻命动克夫爻，并日辰又与应相合，主妻再嫁。 | 财爻发动克丈夫的命，或者妻子的命发动克丈夫的爻，又遇日辰与应爻相合，主妻子再嫁。 | If the Wealth line moves to control the husband's Life, or the wife's Life moves to control the husband's line, and the Day also combines with the Other line, the wife marries again. |
> | 若带咸池与应爻相合，克夫命身爻，主妻与外人谋杀夫主。 | 如果带咸池与应爻相合，克丈夫的命和身爻，主妻子与外人谋杀丈夫。 | If it carries the Xianchi star and combines with the Other line and controls the husband's Life and Body lines, it means the wife plots with an outsider to kill her husband. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注末一句「临交爻」，王洪绪作「临父爻」，一字不同；两家都只说「是将来的事」，没有说明为什么。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary's last sentence has 'sitting on a moving line' and Wang Hongxu 'sitting on the Resource line', one character different; both say only that it is a matter still to come, without saying why.
