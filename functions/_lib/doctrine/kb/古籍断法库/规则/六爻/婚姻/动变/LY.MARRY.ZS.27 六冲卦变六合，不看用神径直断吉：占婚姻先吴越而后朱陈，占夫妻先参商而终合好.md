# 六冲卦变六合，不看用神径直断吉：占婚姻先吴越而后朱陈，占夫妻先参商而终合好

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六冲卦变成六合卦（比如乾为天，外卦三爻都动，乾变成泰），别的合都要用神旺才算吉，只有这一种不看用神，直接断吉。占婚姻，起初像吴国和越国那样互为仇敌，后来才结成朱陈两家那样的亲家；占夫妻，起初像参星和商星那样见不到面，最终和好；占妻儿，离散了的终会团聚。书里的例子：未月丁巳日占一门已经悔婚的亲事还能不能成，得离之旅；书里说这一卦从来难以断吉，但屡次验过，六冲变合是散了又聚、离了必合，这门婚事一定还成，次年三月果然成婚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Six-clash hexagram changing into a Six-combine one (for example Qian wei Tian with all three outer lines moving, so that Qian changes into Tai): every other kind of combination is auspicious only when the Subject is strong, but this one is judged auspicious without looking at the Subject at all. For a marriage it means first foes like the states of Wu and Yue, then in-laws like the families of Zhu and Chen; for husband and wife, first as far apart as the stars Shen and Shang, in the end reconciled; for wife and children, those scattered will in the end come together. The book's case: in the Wei month on a Dingsi day someone asked whether a marriage already called off could still be made and got Li changing into Lü; the book says this hexagram has never been easy to judge auspicious, but it has proved true many times: a Six-clash turning to combine is scattering and then gathering, parting and then surely joining, so the marriage would surely still be made, and it was, in the third month of the next year.

> [!note] 按
> 「吴越」是春秋时互相为敌的两个国家；「朱陈」是徐州一个村子里的两个姓，世代通婚，后来「朱陈」成了结亲的代称；「参商」是天上两颗星，一出一没，永远不同时出现，比喻亲人见不到面。例中的排盘图这里不收。
>
> *Note:* Wu and Yue were two states that were enemies in the Spring and Autumn period; Zhu and Chen were the two clans of a village in Xuzhou who intermarried generation after generation, so 'Zhu-Chen' came to mean a marriage alliance; Shen and Shang are two stars, one rising as the other sets, never seen together, and stand for kin who cannot meet. The chart of the case is not included here.
