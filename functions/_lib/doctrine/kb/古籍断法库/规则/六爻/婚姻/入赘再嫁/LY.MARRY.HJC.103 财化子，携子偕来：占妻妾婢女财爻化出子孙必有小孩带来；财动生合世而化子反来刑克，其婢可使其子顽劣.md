# 财化子，携子偕来：占妻妾婢女财爻化出子孙必有小孩带来；财动生合世而化子反来刑克，其婢可使其子顽劣

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占妻妾、婢女，妻财爻化出子孙爻，一定有小孩带来。如果妻财动来生合世爻，而化出的子孙反来刑克它，那个婢女还可以使唤，她的孩子一定顽劣不听话。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining for a wife, concubine or maid, when the Wealth line changes out an Output line there will surely be a small child brought along. If Wealth moves to generate or combine with the Self line but the Output it changes into turns to punish and control it, the maid can still be used, but her child will surely be unruly and disobedient.
