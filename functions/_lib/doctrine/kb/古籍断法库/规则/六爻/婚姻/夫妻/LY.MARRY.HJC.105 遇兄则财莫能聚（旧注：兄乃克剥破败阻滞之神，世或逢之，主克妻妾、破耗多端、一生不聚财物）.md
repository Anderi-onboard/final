# 遇兄则财莫能聚（旧注：兄乃克剥破败阻滞之神，世或逢之，主克妻妾、破耗多端、一生不聚财物）

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》身命章说：「遇到兄弟爻，钱财就聚不起来。」旧注解释：兄弟爻是克剥、破败、阻滞的神，世爻如果碰上它，主克妻妾，破耗多端，一辈子聚不起钱财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on one's person and fate says: 'Meeting a Peer line, wealth cannot gather.' The old commentary explains: the Peer line is the spirit of stripping, ruin, breaking and blocking; if Self meets it, the wife and concubines are controlled, losses come in many ways, and wealth never gathers in a whole life.

^q2

> [!warning] 打架
> 这句旧注正是《增删卜易》批评的对象。野鹤（LY.MARRY.ZS.35）说：「既以为克妻，又以为破耗，照这样说，世上伤了妻子的人都是贫困的人。假使穷人白头偕老、富人断弦，又怎么解释？」所以主张分开占：占妻子占一卦、占钱财另占一卦，不能一卦断到底。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This commentary line is exactly what the Zengshan Buyi attacks. The Old Man of Yehe (LY.MARRY.ZS.35) says: 'Since it is taken both as harming the wife and as wasting wealth, by that reasoning all the people in the world who have lost a wife are poor. Suppose the poor grow old together while the rich lose their wives: how would that be explained?' So he argues for divining separately, one hexagram for the wife and another for wealth, and not judging everything from a single hexagram.
