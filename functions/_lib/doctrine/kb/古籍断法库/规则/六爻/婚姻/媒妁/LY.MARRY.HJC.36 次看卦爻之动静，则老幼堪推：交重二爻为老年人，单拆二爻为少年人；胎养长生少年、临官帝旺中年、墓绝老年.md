# 次看卦爻之动静，则老幼堪推：交重二爻为老年人，单拆二爻为少年人；胎养长生少年、临官帝旺中年、墓绝老年

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 交、重（老阴老阳）为老年人，单、拆（少阳少阴）为少年人；胎、养、长生是少年，临官、帝旺是中年，墓、绝是老年；墓化旺，是老年人先来说；阴化阳，是女人先来说；这爻动空化空而自冲另一爻，是原来的媒人有事推辞，别人来做媒。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The doubled and crossed marks (old yin and old yang) mean an old person and the single and broken marks (young yang and young yin) a young one; Womb, Nurture and Long Life mean youth, Official and Emperor's Prime middle age, Tomb and Cut-off old age; Tomb changing to thriving means an old person spoke first; yin changing to yang means a woman spoke first; if this line moves, is Void and turns Void and itself clashes another line, the original go-between has an excuse to decline and someone else acts as go-between.
