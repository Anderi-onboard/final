# 占雇取仆从亦用财爻：财不可太过也不可无财或空亡；子化财纯善、鬼化财带疾、兄化财不诚实、父化财稳重、财化兄淫荡；身爻是鬼有疾、是父识字、是兄说谎、是子慈善、是财最好

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 雇用、聘取仆从，也以财爻为主要的象：财爻不能太过，也不能没有财爻或者落空亡，那样的仆人慵懒不上进。子孙化财，为人纯朴善良；鬼爻化财，带病；兄弟化财，不诚实，爱说谎、瞒骗别人；父母化财，性情稳重，做事稳当；财化兄弟，多淫荡，难以托付钱财。还要看身爻：身爻是鬼，主有病；是父母，主识字；是兄弟，多说谎；是子孙，主慈善；是财爻最好。如果化出鬼爻，主生病、招口舌；化出兄弟爻，主口舌、不稳当。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For hiring or taking on a servant, too, take the Wealth line as the chief sign: it must be neither excessive nor absent or Void, or the servant is idle and unambitious. Output changing into Wealth: simple and kind; Pressure changing into Wealth: carries an illness; a Peer line changing into Wealth: dishonest, lies and cheats people; Resource changing into Wealth: steady in nature and reliable in work; Wealth changing into a Peer line: mostly licentious and cannot be trusted with money. Look also at the Body line: Pressure means illness, Resource means literacy, a Peer line means lying, Output means kindness and charity, and Wealth is best. If it changes out into Pressure, illness and quarrels; into a Peer line, quarrels and unreliability.
