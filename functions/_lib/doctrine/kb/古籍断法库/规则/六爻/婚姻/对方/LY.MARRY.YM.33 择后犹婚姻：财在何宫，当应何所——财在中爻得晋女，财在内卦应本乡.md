# 择后犹婚姻：财在何宫，当应何所——财在中爻得晋女，财在内卦应本乡

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 国事章里说选皇后就像婚姻：妻财爻落在哪一宫，对方就出在哪个地方。小注举例：妻财在中爻，会得到晋地的女子；妻财在内卦，应在本乡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on affairs of state says that choosing an empress is like a marriage: in whichever palace the Wealth line stands, there the bride comes from. The small note gives examples: with Wealth in the middle line, she will be a woman of Jin; with Wealth in the inner trigram, she answers to the home district.

> | 财在何宫， | 妻财爻落在哪一宫， | in whichever palace the Wealth line stands, |
> | 当应何所。 | 就应在哪个地方。 | there is the place it answers to. |

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 828 行 · Bracketed small note in the Yi Mao (author not named in the book)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如财在中爻， | 比如妻财在中爻， | If the Wealth line is in the middle line, |
> | 当得晋女， | 会得到晋地的女子； | she will be a woman of Jin; |
> | 财在内卦， | 妻财在内卦， | if the Wealth line is in the inner trigram, |
> | 当应本乡。 | 就应在本乡。 | it answers to her home district. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「中爻」和「晋女」书里没有再解释：「中爻」大概是卦中间的爻，「晋」可能是晋国，也可能是晋卦，无从核对。这两句和婚姻章里「何地」一条（LY.MARRY.YM.19）是同一个办法：看妻财（或官鬼）落在哪一宫、在内在外，断对方从哪里来。
>
> *Note:* The book does not explain 'middle line' or 'woman of Jin': 'middle line' is probably a line in the middle of the hexagram, and 'Jin' may be the state of Jin or the hexagram Jin; there is nothing to check against. These two lines use the same method as 'where' in the marriage chapter (LY.MARRY.YM.19): look at which palace the Wealth line (or Pressure line) stands in, and whether inside or outside, to judge where the other party comes from.
