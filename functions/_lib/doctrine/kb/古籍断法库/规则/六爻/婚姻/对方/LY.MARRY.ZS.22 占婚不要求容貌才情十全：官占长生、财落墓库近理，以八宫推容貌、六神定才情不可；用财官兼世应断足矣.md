# 占婚不要求容貌才情十全：官占长生、财落墓库近理，以八宫推容貌、六神定才情不可；用财官兼世应断足矣

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 李我平批评古法占婚多浮辞：《黄金策》说的「丈夫有才能，官鬼占长生的位置；妻子丑拙，财爻落在墓库」还算近理；《易冒》用八宫推容貌、用六神定才情就不行——同一卦，按八宫读是端正聪明的淑女，按六神读财爻临白虎又成了悖逆之星，两种读法彼此打架。婚姻有一定的缘分，财旺官兴，就会夫唱妇随；相生相合，自然有讨人喜欢的容貌；吉的就求，凶的就弃，用财、官兼看世、应来断就够了。要是事事求全——命不入墓、六亲不相妨、父母不沉、子孙不溺、财不动、官不没、夫妇不二、阴阳不悖——世上没有这样的女子，六十四卦里也没有这样的卦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Li Woping criticizes the old methods of divining marriage as full of showy words: what the Huangjin Ce says, that a husband with ability has the Pressure line in the position of Birth and an ugly, clumsy wife has the Wealth line in the tomb or storehouse, is still close to reason; but the Yi Mao inferring looks from the eight palaces and settling talent by the six spirits will not do, since the same hexagram read by palace gives an upright and clever virtuous woman, and read by spirit gives Wealth with the White Tiger, a star of perversity, so the two readings contradict each other. Marriage has its appointed bond: with Wealth strong and Pressure flourishing, husband leads and wife follows; where they generate and combine she will naturally have looks that please; seek what is auspicious and abandon what is ill, and judging by Wealth and Pressure together with the Self and Other lines is enough. If one demands perfection in everything (fates not entering a tomb, six kin not harming each other, parents not sinking, children not lost, Wealth not moving, Pressure not lost, no second spouse, yin and yang not at odds), there is no such woman in the world and no such hexagram among the sixty-four.

## 李我平(楚江李坦,《增删卜易》的鉴定者)

> | 《易冒》以八宫而推容貌，六神而定才情，则不可也。 | 《易冒》用八宫来推容貌，用六神来定才情，那就不行了。 | But the Yi Mao infers looks from the eight palaces and settles talent and temperament by the six spirits, which will not do. |
> | 假令卯月戊日，得“否”卦，旺财持世，推容貌以八宫者，此卦财在乾宫，乾为天，谓仪容端正，性质聪明，配天德之淑女也。 | 假如卯月戊日，得否卦，旺相的财爻持世，用八宫推容貌的，这一卦财在乾宫，乾为天，就说仪容端正、性情聪明，配得上天德的贤淑女子。 | Suppose in a Mao month on a Wu day one obtains Pi, with a strong Wealth line on the Self line; for those who infer looks from the eight palaces, this hexagram has Wealth in the Qian palace, Qian is Heaven, so they say her bearing is upright, her nature clever, a virtuous woman matched to Heaven's virtue. |
> | 然财临白虎，若兼六神而断，白虎乃悖逆之星，成婚之后，配天德之良女而悖逆者，未之有也，是以容貌才情，无事卜之，岂不闻在德不在色。 | 可是财爻临白虎，如果再兼顾六神来断，白虎是悖逆之星，成婚以后，配得上天德的好女子而悖逆的，从来没有的事；所以容貌才情，不必去卜它，难道没听说过重德不重色吗。 | But the Wealth line is with the White Tiger, and if one also judges by the six spirits, the White Tiger is the star of perversity; after the wedding, a fine woman matched to Heaven's virtue who turns perverse has never been; so looks and talent need no divination: have you not heard that what counts is virtue, not beauty? |
> | 婚姻有一定之缘，财旺官兴，定遂唱随之好； | 婚姻有一定的缘分，财旺官兴，一定遂夫唱妇随的美好； | Marriage has its appointed bond: with Wealth strong and Pressure flourishing, the good of husband leading and wife following will surely be fulfilled; |
> | 相生相合，自有悦己之容，吉者求之，凶者弃之，用财官兼世应而断足矣。 | 相生相合，自然有悦己的容貌，吉的就求，凶的就弃，用财、官兼看世应来断就够了。 | where they generate and combine, she will naturally have looks that please; seek what is auspicious and abandon what is ill; judging by Wealth and Pressure together with the Self and Other lines is enough. |
