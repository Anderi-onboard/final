# 何地：男看官爻、女看财爻，以卦宫定远近；用神在内则近、在外则远，坎北离南震东兑西，亲宫为本处、他卦为别乡

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问对方在哪个地方：是男子就看官爻，是女子就看财爻，以卦宫定远近。小注说：用神在内就近，在外就远；坎是北、离是南，震是东、兑是西；亲宫是本地，别的卦是别的乡，诸如此类。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask which place the person is from: for a man look at Pressure, for a woman at Wealth, and the trigram palace gives near or far. The note says: the Subject within is near, outside is far; Kan is north and Li south, Zhen east and Dui west; the kin palace is the home place, another hexagram another district, and so on.

> [!note] 按
> 「亲宫」书里没有解释，可能指本宫（卦所属的宫）；「在内」「在外」没有说是内外卦还是别的，译文照字面写。
>
> *Note:* The book does not explain 'kin palace', which may mean the hexagram's own palace; nor does it say whether 'within' and 'outside' mean the inner and outer trigram, and they are translated literally.
