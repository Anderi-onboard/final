# 间爻在婚姻里是媒妁：旧注说间爻动则多阻隔，野鹤说动而生合世应反得其力、只忌克世克应，觉子说婚姻以间爻为媒妁，日月冲克可解；书中例：间爻鬼化退神虽阻无力

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻和应爻中间的两爻叫间爻。《千金赋》说「事阻隔兮间发」，旧注解为：间爻一动，事多阻隔，因为它们站在世应之间，动了就拦住两家的事。觉子试验后说，把这两爻当间爻用，果然应验；要看所占何事，只有牵涉彼此两家的事才可用，婚姻里它就是媒妁（词讼里是居中说合的人，买卖里是牙行，借贷里是中保，舟车里是同载的人，交游里是帮闲，生产里是接生婆和保姆）。野鹤补充：想相亲的两方，间爻发动不能一概断作阻隔——间爻动而去生合世爻、应爻，反得这些人的力；靠近世爻的是帮我的人，靠近应爻的是扶对方的人；生我的我该亲近，克我的我该远离；最忌间爻克世、克应，事必难成：克我的，坏我的事，我被他愚弄；克应的，坏对方的事，对方被他引诱；间爻是兄弟动，害处还小，不过阻事耗财；是官鬼动，一定受连累，祸不浅。觉子又说：间爻动了，如果得到日辰月建的冲克，可以化解。书中一例：求婚，财爻持世化进神，巳火子孙动而生世，回头受克，要等午日冲去子水、午火又合世爻，婚事成，果然午日答应；有人问间爻酉金官鬼动，难道没有阻碍？答：官鬼化成退神，虽有阻碍，但没有力量。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The two lines between the Self and Other lines are called the between lines. The Qianjin Fu says 'obstruction to the matter: the between lines move', and the old commentary explains: when the between lines move the matter meets many obstructions, since they sit between Self and Other and block the affair of the two sides when they move. Juezi tried it and says that taking these two lines as the between lines does prove true; one must look at what is divined, and only a matter between two sides can use them; in marriage they are the go-between (in a lawsuit the one who speaks between, in a sale the broker, in a loan the guarantor, on a boat or carriage the one who rides along, in friendship the hangers-on, in childbirth the midwife and nurse). The Old Man of Yehe adds: where two sides want to become close, if the between lines move do not judge it all as obstruction; if they move to generate or combine with the Self and Other lines, one gains instead from the help of such people; the one near the Self line helps me, the one near the Other line supports the other side; one that generates me I should draw near to and one that controls me I should keep away from; what is to be feared above all is a between line that controls the Self or Other line, and then the matter is sure to be hard to make: one that controls me spoils my affair and I am fooled by it, one that controls the Other line spoils the other party's affair and he is led astray; if a between line carrying a Peer moves the harm is slight, only obstruction and a loss of money, but if a between line carrying Pressure moves one is sure to be burdened and the harm is not slight. Juezi adds: when the between lines move, if the Day or Month clashes or controls them, the trouble can be resolved. The book's case: a proposal of marriage, the Wealth line on the Self line changing into Advancing, the Si Fire Output line moving to generate the Self line but turning back to be controlled, so one must wait for the Wu day to clash away the Zi Water while Wu Fire also combines with the Self line; the match is made, and it was agreed on the Wu day; someone asked whether the moving You Metal Pressure between line would not obstruct, and the answer was that Pressure turning into Retreating may obstruct but has no strength.

> [!warning] 打架
> 旧注说间爻一动就多阻隔；野鹤明说不可一概这样断，间爻动而生合世应反得其力，只有克世、克应才是阻碍。觉子只说婚姻里间爻就是媒妁，没有说动了是吉是凶，又说日月冲克可以化解阻隔。书中例里间爻官鬼动，答的是鬼化退神，有阻而无力，和野鹤「看它生合还是克」是一路。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The old commentary says that when the between lines move there are many obstructions; the Old Man of Yehe says plainly that this is not to be judged across the board: between lines that move to generate or combine with the Self and Other lines help, and only those that control the Self or Other line obstruct. Juezi says only that in marriage the between lines are the go-betweens, without saying whether their moving is lucky or not, and adds that the Day or Month clashing or controlling them resolves the obstruction. In the book's case a moving Pressure between line is answered by 'Pressure turning into Retreating, obstructing but without strength', which is on the same road as the Old Man's 'look at whether it generates and combines or controls'.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条前半是《千金赋》「事阻隔兮间发」这一句以及注它的话，收在这里是因为觉子在同一处直接说到婚姻。野鹤那段里的「史」应是「兄」字之误（与下一句「持鬼」相对，兄弟爻动主耗财），译文按兄弟译，并在译文里注明。求婚的书中例出在进神退神章，不在千金赋一节，排盘图不收。
>
> *Note:* The first part of this entry is the Qianjin Fu line 'obstruction to the matter: the between lines move' and the comments on it; it is included because Juezi speaks directly of marriage at the same place. In the Old Man of Yehe's passage 'shi' is presumably a slip for 'xiong' (Peer), matching 'carrying Pressure' in the next sentence and the fact that a moving Peer means loss of money, and is translated as Peer, with a note in the translation. The proposal-of-marriage case comes from the chapter on Advancing and Retreating, not from the Qianjin Fu section, and its chart is not included.
