# 间曰良媒，逢空少力：两间之爻皆为媒妁，近世为男家月老、近应为女舍冰人；临空犯绝媒必无能，遇旺逢生中须有力

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻叫作好媒人，逢空就少了力量。两个间爻都是媒人：靠近世爻的是男家的月老，靠近应爻的是女方的冰人。临空、犯绝，媒人必定无能；遇旺、逢生，中间人必定有力。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The between line is called the good go-between, but meeting Void it lacks strength. The two between lines are both go-betweens: the one next to the Self line is the man's family's Old Man under the Moon, and the one next to the Other line is the woman's house's ice-man. Meeting Void or extinction, the go-between is surely incompetent; meeting strength or generation, he is surely effective.
