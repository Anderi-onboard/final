# 妇僭姑嫜之爻，家声可见：二爻为儿媳的命爻，与婆婆的命爻相刑相冲则凌上悖逆不孝，相生相合则顺妇道

六爻 · 婚姻 · 家人 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 第二爻是儿媳妇的命爻，与婆婆的命爻相刑相冲，主儿媳欺凌尊长、悖逆不孝；相生相合，主儿媳顺从妇道、恭敬孝顺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The second line is the daughter-in-law's Life line; if it punishes or clashes with the mother-in-law's Life line, the daughter-in-law overrides her elders and is unruly and unfilial; if it generates or combines with it, she follows a wife's way and is respectful and dutiful.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3108 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 二为媳妇之爻，与姑之命爻相刑相克，主凌上悖逆不孝； | 第二爻是儿媳妇的爻，与婆婆的命爻相刑相克，主欺凌尊长、悖逆不孝； | The second line is the daughter-in-law's line; if it punishes or controls the mother-in-law's Life line, it means overriding her elders and being unruly and unfilial; |
> | 相生相合，主能敬孝尽妇道也。 | 相生相合，主能恭敬孝顺、尽儿媳的本分。 | if they generate and combine, it means she can be respectful and dutiful and do her part as a daughter-in-law. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 经文旧注本作「妇僭姑嫜之爻」（僭，越过），卜筮正宗作「妇谐姑嫜之爻」（谐，和顺），一字之差，意思相反的读法都通。注文两家都同时讲了刑克和生合两种情形。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary text of the couplet reads 'the daughter-in-law oversteps the mother-in-law's line' and the Bushi Zhengzong 'the daughter-in-law is in harmony with the mother-in-law's line', a one-character difference; both readings make sense, and the notes of both speak of the punishing and controlling case as well as the generating and combining one.
