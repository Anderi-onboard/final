# 占女婿之续：飞爻入父寿高通文墨、入兄好赌争讼、入福好善、值龙临三爻持三官斋临五爻持观音斋、入财性温和能出纳、入马酷毒好杀、旺相加贵马为官衰下流；其余贫富贵贱带疾祸福同世爻断；男女临世爻、应鬼居二爻或应鬼合内财俱主入养

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 飞爻入父母：长寿，通文墨，旺相的家宅华盛。入兄弟：好赌博、争讼；衰的稍轻，耗财、少奴婢。入福德（子孙）：好善，能成事，遇朱雀好念经；遇青龙，临三爻持三官斋，临五爻持观音斋。入财：性情温和，能管家里出纳，有钱财，旺相的多才识。入驿马：残酷狠毒好杀，自己带病；旺相又加贵人驿马，能做官，衰弱就是下流之辈。其余贫、富、贵、贱、带病、祸福，都同世爻一样来断。又凡是占女婿，遇到「男女」临世爻，和应爻的鬼爻居在二爻（二爻是宅爻），或者应爻的鬼爻合内卦的财爻的，都主入赘养。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The flying line entering the Resource: long-lived, literate, and if strong the household is splendid and prosperous; entering a Peer line: fond of gambling and lawsuits, milder if weak but wasting money and having few servants; entering Output: fond of good deeds and able to accomplish things, and with the Vermilion Bird fond of chanting scripture; with the Azure Dragon on the third line he keeps the Three Officials' fast, on the fifth line the Guanyin fast; entering Wealth: mild in nature and able to manage the household's income and expenditure, with money, and if strong much talent and knowledge; entering the Horse: cruel, venomous, fond of killing, and ill himself; if strong with the Nobleman and Horse added he becomes an official, and if weak he is of the lowest sort. For the rest, poverty, wealth, rank, lowliness, illness, calamity and fortune, judge as with the Self line. Again, in divining a son-in-law, if a 'man-woman' sits on the Self line, and the Other line's Pressure sits on the second line (the house line), or the Other line's Pressure combines with the inner Wealth: all mean he is taken in and supported by the family.

> [!note] 按
> 「入父」「入兄」「入福」「入财」「入马」都是「飞爻入……」的省写；「持三官齐」「持观音齐」的「齐」是「斋」的转录之误（斋戒）；「男女临世爻」的意思原文没有解释，照字面译。飞爻入什么，是指飞爻所在的地支进入哪一类六亲。
>
> *Note:* 'Entering the Resource', 'entering a Peer line' and so on are shortened from 'the flying line enters ...'; 'qi' in 'keeps the ... fast' is a transcription slip for 'zhai' (fast); what 'man-woman on the Self line' means is not explained and it is translated literally. What the flying line enters means which kin the flying line's branch enters.
