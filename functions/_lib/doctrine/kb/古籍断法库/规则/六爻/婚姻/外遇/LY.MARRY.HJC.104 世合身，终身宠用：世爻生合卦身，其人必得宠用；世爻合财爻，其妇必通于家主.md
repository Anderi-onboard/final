# 世合身，终身宠用：世爻生合卦身，其人必得宠用；世爻合财爻，其妇必通于家主

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻生合卦身，这个人一生受宠爱、被重用。不管是借房子、来投靠还是使唤婢女，只要世爻合了财爻，那个妇人一定和家主有私情。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self line generates or combines with the Body, the person is favoured and used for life. Whether borrowing a house, taking refuge or employing a maid, if Self combines with the Wealth line, the woman surely has an affair with the master of the house.

## 《黄金策·进人口》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6678 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Taking in persons", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 世合身，终身宠用。 | 世爻合卦身，一生受宠爱、被重用。 | Self combining with the Body: favoured and used for life. |

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6679 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦身一爻，占事为事之体，古人为人之身。 | 卦身这一爻，占事是事情的本体，占人是人的身体。 | The Body line: in divining a matter it is the substance of the matter, in divining a person it is the person's body. |
> | 若遇世爻生合，其人必得宠用。 | 如果遇到世爻生合它，这个人一定得到宠爱重用。 | If the Self line generates or combines with it, the person will surely be favoured and used. |
> | 不拘借房投靠使婢，若遇世合财爻，其妇必通于家主。 | 不论是借房子、来投靠还是使唤婢女，只要世爻合了财爻，那个妇人一定和家主有私情。 | Whether borrowing a house, taking refuge or employing a maid, if Self combines with the Wealth line, the woman surely has an affair with the master of the house. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 旧注里「古人为人之身」的「古人」大概是「占人」之误（占人才和「占事为事之体」对得上），译文按「占人」译。
>
> *Note:* 'Gu ren' in 'gu ren wei ren zhi shen' in the commentary is probably a slip for 'zhan ren' (divining a person), which matches the preceding 'zhan shi wei shi zhi ti' (divining a matter); it is translated as divining a person.
