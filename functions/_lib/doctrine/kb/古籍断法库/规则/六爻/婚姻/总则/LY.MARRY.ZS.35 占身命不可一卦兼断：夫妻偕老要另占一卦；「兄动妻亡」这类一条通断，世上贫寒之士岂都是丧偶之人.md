# 占身命不可一卦兼断：夫妻偕老要另占一卦；「兄动妻亡」这类一条通断，世上贫寒之士岂都是丧偶之人

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 各书占身命，说妻子、钱财、儿女、官禄一个卦都能包，寿命长短、穷困通达六爻一起断。野鹤不同意：五个六亲各有互相忌讳互相伤害的地方，一个卦兼着断，就会出笑话——父母爻旺相说是「双庆」，又说「父旺伤子」，难道世上有父母的人都没有子嗣？兄弟爻旺持世，是克妻，是耗财，还是手足无伤？《易林补遗》说「兄动妻亡财耗散」，照这样断，世上的穷人和寒士岂不都是丧偶的人？觉子的办法是分开占：占父母、占兄弟各占一卦；终身的财福、终身的功名、夫妻能不能偕老、终身的子嗣和寿数，都应分开占。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The books on divining one's person and fate say that wife, wealth, children and salary can all be covered by one hexagram, and length of life and poverty or success judged together from the six lines. The Old Man of Yehe disagrees: the five relations each have things that fear and harm one another, and judging them all together from one hexagram produces absurdities: a strong Resource line is called 'double celebration' and it is also said that 'a strong Resource harms Output', but is everyone in the world who has parents childless? A strong Peer line on the Self line: does it harm the wife, waste money, or leave the brothers unharmed? The Yilin Buyi says 'a moving Peer line: the wife dies and wealth is scattered'; holding to this, are all the poor and humble scholars of the world people who have lost their spouses? Juezi's method is to divine separately: one hexagram for parents and one for brothers; and lifelong wealth and fortune, lifelong office and fame, whether husband and wife will grow old together, and lifelong children and length of life should each be divined separately.

> [!warning] 打架
> 野鹤批评的是「诸书」的一卦兼断，其中特别点了《易林补遗》一句「兄动妻亡财耗散」。这句在本库的《易林补遗》里没有原样出现，意思相近的是「兄动，妻灾奴仆患，资财耗散事无成」（该书第 109 行）和「兄动损妻，财无积聚」（第 441 行），应是转述。
>
> *Where they disagree:* What the Old Man of Yehe criticizes is judging everything from one hexagram as 'the books' do, and he singles out the Yilin Buyi line 'a moving Peer line, the wife dies and wealth is scattered'. That sentence does not appear word for word in the Yilin Buyi in this library; the close ones are 'Peer moving: trouble for wife and servants, wealth scattered and nothing accomplished' (line 109 of that book) and 'Peer moving harms the wife, wealth does not gather' (line 441), so it is presumably a paraphrase.

> [!note] 按
> 原文「父子财官兄弟」列的是五个六亲，译文写作 Resource、Output、Wealth、Pressure、Peer；「紫荆并茂」用的是三兄弟分家、紫荆枯死又复荣的典故，意思是兄弟和睦兴旺。
>
> *Note:* The text's 'father, sons, Wealth, Pressure, brothers' lists the five relations, translated as Resource, Output, Wealth, Pressure and Peer; 'bauhinia flourishing together' alludes to the story of three brothers who divided the estate, whereupon the bauhinia tree withered and revived when they reunited, and means brothers in concord and prosperity.
