# 月建青龙：青龙在内象，月内有婚姻进人口之喜；在外象，求官进职吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 取象 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建上的青龙：在内象，这个月里有婚姻、添人进口的喜事；在外象，求官、升职吉利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon of the Month: in the inner image, that month brings the joy of a marriage and of people added to the household; in the outer image, seeking office and promotion is lucky.

## 《卜筮元龟·总例门》原文

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句前面有一张表，按月排六神（青龙从正月的寅起顺排到十二月的丑），本库没有收表。「内象」「外象」书里没有解释，按字面译。
>
> *Note:* Before this line the book has a table laying the six spirits out by month (the Azure Dragon runs from Yin in the first month to Chou in the twelfth), which is not included here. 'Inner image' and 'outer image' are not explained and are translated literally.
