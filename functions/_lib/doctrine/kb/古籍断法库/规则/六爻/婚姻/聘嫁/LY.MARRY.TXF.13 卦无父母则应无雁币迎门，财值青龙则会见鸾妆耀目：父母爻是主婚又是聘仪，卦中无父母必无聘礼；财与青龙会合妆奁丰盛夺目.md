# 卦无父母则应无雁币迎门，财值青龙则会见鸾妆耀目：父母爻是主婚又是聘仪，卦中无父母必无聘礼；财与青龙会合妆奁丰盛夺目

六爻 · 婚姻 · 聘嫁 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有父母爻，就没有雁币迎门的聘礼：父母爻是主婚人，又是聘礼。财爻遇青龙，嫁妆丰盛，光彩夺目。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram has no Resource line there are no wild-goose-and-silk gifts to greet at the door: the Resource line is the presider at the wedding and also the betrothal gifts. Wealth meeting the Azure Dragon means a rich trousseau, dazzling to the eye.

> [!note] 按
> 「雁币」是古时聘礼：雁和币帛。两句注文末尾原书没有句号。
>
> *Note:* 'Wild-goose-and-silk' are the traditional betrothal gifts, a goose and bolts of silk. The book's note has no full stop at the end of the two sentences on the gifts.
