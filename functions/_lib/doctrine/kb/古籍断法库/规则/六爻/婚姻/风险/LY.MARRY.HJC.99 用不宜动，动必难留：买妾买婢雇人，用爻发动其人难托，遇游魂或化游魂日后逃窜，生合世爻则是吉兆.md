# 用不宜动，动必难留：买妾买婢雇人，用爻发动其人难托，遇游魂或化游魂日后逃窜，生合世爻则是吉兆

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 买宠妾、买婢女、雇人，用爻（就是被买被雇的那个人）不宜发动，发动了就难留住。用爻发动，这个人靠不住；遇到游魂卦，或者化入游魂卦，日后会逃走；雇工、借住的人也待不长；但如果发动来生合世爻，那是吉兆，不是逃走或留不住。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In buying a favoured concubine or a maid or hiring a person, the Subject line, the person bought or hired, should not move; moving, he or she is hard to keep. A moving Subject line means the person cannot be relied on; meeting a Wandering Soul hexagram or changing into one, he or she will run away in future; a hired hand or a lodger will not stay long; but if it moves to generate or combine with the Self line, that is a lucky sign, not fleeing or being hard to keep.
