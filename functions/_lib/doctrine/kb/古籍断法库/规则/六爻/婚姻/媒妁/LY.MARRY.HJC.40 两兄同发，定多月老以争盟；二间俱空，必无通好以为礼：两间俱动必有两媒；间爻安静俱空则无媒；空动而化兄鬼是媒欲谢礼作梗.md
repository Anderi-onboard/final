# 两兄同发，定多月老以争盟；二间俱空，必无通好以为礼：两间俱动必有两媒；间爻安静俱空则无媒；空动而化兄鬼是媒欲谢礼作梗

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 两个间爻都动，一定有两个媒人，临兄弟或兄化兄、动出两个鬼，主有争着做媒的，要看空亡衰旺、有制无制，才知道谁掌权；间爻安静又都空亡，一定没有媒人来通好；空动而化出兄鬼，是媒人想要谢礼而作梗不来，不是没有媒人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Two between lines both moving means two go-betweens, and if they sit on Peer, or Peer changes into Peer, or two Pressure lines move out, they compete to be go-between, and one must see Void, thriving or weakness, and whether there is restraint to know which holds the power; between lines that are quiet and both Void mean no go-between comes to bring the two sides together; between lines that are Void and moving and change into Peer or Pressure mean the go-between wants his thank-you gift and is holding back, not that there is no go-between.
