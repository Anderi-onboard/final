# 助鬼伤身·占婚：鬼克世而妻财动来生助，纵成其妇也不贤不孝公姑、搬唆夫主、割户分门、不和兄弟

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 生助官鬼的只有妻财，不宜发动，动了就把弱的鬼变成旺鬼。卦中鬼爻克身世，妻财再动来生助，凶更重；没有财，凶有限；两个财都动，大凶。占婚姻遇到：世爻是男家，官鬼是丈夫，鬼克世是自己克自家，本来就不好；再遇财爻发动、生助鬼爻，即使成了，那个妻子也一定不贤、不孝敬公婆、挑唆丈夫、分家另过、不和兄弟。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The only line that generates and supports Pressure is Wealth, and it should not move, for moving turns a weak ghost into a strong one. When the Pressure line in a hexagram controls the Body or Self and Wealth also moves to generate and support it, the ill is all the worse; with no Wealth the ill is limited; with two Wealth lines both moving the calamity is very great. In a marriage divination: Self is the man's family and Pressure is the husband, so Pressure controlling Self is a house controlling itself, which is not fine to begin with; if Wealth then moves and generates and supports Pressure, even if the match is made the wife will surely be unworthy, unfilial to her parents-in-law, egg her husband on, split the household and be at odds with his brothers.
