# 咸恒节泰为吉、睽革解离为凶，惟合冲变冲则不宜；婚姻忌三冲则不索用神之衰旺，用神未胜于卦验

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《易冒》主张占婚姻先看卦、后看爻：得咸、恒、节、泰四卦为吉，只是遇到合冲、变冲就不适宜；得睽、革、解、离四卦为凶，即使用神齐备、又化出合，也不吉。小注说：得咸恒节泰而没有遇到合冲、变冲的，即使财、官衰弱陷落，或者随官入墓、助鬼伤身，都不算凶，这就是「用神比不上卦验」。婚姻忌三冲，遇到了就不必再去查用神的旺衰。这些都是凭卦断、不用爻的，前人往往有应验。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Yi Mao judges marriage by the hexagram first and the lines after: Xian, Heng, Jie and Tai are lucky, unless a combination meets a clash or the hexagram changes into a clash, when they are unsuitable; Kui, Ge, Xie and Li are unlucky, and even if the Subject is complete and turns into a combination they are not lucky. The note says: for Xian, Heng, Jie and Tai that meet neither a combination-clash nor a change-clash, even if Wealth and Pressure are weak and sunk, or Pressure is followed into the tomb and helped to harm, none of it counts as unlucky; this is what is meant by 'the Subject does not outweigh the hexagram omen'. Marriage fears the three clashes, and once they appear there is no need to look for the strength of the Subject. These verdicts rest on the hexagram alone and do not use the lines, and the worthies of old often had confirmed results.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这一条和《增删卜易》直接相反。觉子引了《易冒》这一段（LY.MARRY.ZS.14），自己占了一卦，得泰卦，但兄弟持世、财爻月破旬空，他不敢拿别人的婚姻大事去试，让人再占一卦，得坤卦六冲大凶，才知道泰卦不可信，结论是仍以财、官、世、应来断，有疑再占一卦。《易隐》（LY.MARRY.YY.09）、《火珠林》（LY.MARRY.HZL.11）里有按卦名断婚的口诀，和《易冒》站在同一边。本书文件末尾附的提要也批评这一章「不根五行生克，不究用神衰旺，惟据卦名之美恶而论」。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This is the direct opposite of the Zengshan Buyi. Juezi quotes this passage of the Yi Mao (LY.MARRY.ZS.14) and tried it himself: he got Tai, yet with a Peer line on Self and the Wealth line Month-broken and Void; he dared not stake another person's marriage on it, had a second divination cast and got Kun, a six-clash hexagram and very unlucky, and so learned that Tai was not to be trusted; his conclusion is to keep judging by Wealth, Pressure, Self and Other, and to divine again if in doubt. The Yi Yin (LY.MARRY.YY.09) and the Huozhulin (LY.MARRY.HZL.11) have rhymes that judge marriage by hexagram names and stand on the same side as the Yi Mao. The summary printed at the end of this book's file also faults this chapter for 'not rooting in the generating and controlling of the five phases, not examining the strength of the Subject, and going only by whether the hexagram names are fair or foul'.

> [!note] 按
> 「合冲」「变冲」在这本书自己的合冲章里有定义：六合的卦被日辰冲害在世应上叫合冲（LY.MARRY.YM.25），卦化成六冲叫变冲（LY.MARRY.YM.24）。LY.MARRY.ZS.14 转引这一句时标点不明，读法可以照这里定下来。
>
> *Note:* 'Combination-clash' and 'change-clash' are defined in this book's own chapter on combination and clash: a six-combine hexagram whose Self or Other is clashed or harmed by the Day is a combination-clash (LY.MARRY.YM.25), and a hexagram that changes into a six-clash hexagram is a change-clash (LY.MARRY.YM.24). LY.MARRY.ZS.14 quotes this sentence with unclear punctuation, and its reading can be settled by these definitions.
