# 财鬼同居一卦必然亲上成亲，前后皆值六冲当见退后还退：财鬼共一卦易成，前卦六冲曾许聘复退、后卦又六冲成后又退，被日辰合住想退不得，前冲后合退后还就

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财、官同在一卦，是容易成的兆头；世应财官相生相合，一定是亲上加亲。前卦六冲，曾经许过聘又退了；后卦又六冲，成了以后还会退；被日辰合住的，想退悔也退不成；前卦冲而后卦合，退了以后又会回头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth and Pressure together in one hexagram is a sign that it is easily made, and if Self, Other, Wealth and Pressure generate and combine with each other it is surely a marriage of relative to relative. If the earlier hexagram is a six-clash hexagram, a betrothal was once promised and then withdrawn; if the later hexagram is a six-clash again, after it is made it will again be withdrawn; if it is held by a Day combination, though one wants to withdraw it cannot be done; if the earlier hexagram clashes and the later one combines, there is a withdrawal and then a return to the match.
