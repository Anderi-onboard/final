# 文书若动当权，子嗣必然萧索：父母旺动克子孙；子孙旬空反可得子，不空受伤则无子息

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻当权发动，子孙躲在空亡里，是事情还没定；如果子孙不空，而是死绝无气，就被父母克，日后一定克子，没有子息。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Resource line holds power and moves while Output hides in the Void, the matter is not yet settled; if Output is not Void but dead, cut off and without qi, it is controlled by Resource, and later the children will surely be harmed, leaving no offspring.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6361 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 文书若动当权，子嗣必然萧索。 | 文书爻如果发动当权，子嗣一定稀少冷落。 | If the document line moves and holds power, offspring will surely be few and lonely. |
