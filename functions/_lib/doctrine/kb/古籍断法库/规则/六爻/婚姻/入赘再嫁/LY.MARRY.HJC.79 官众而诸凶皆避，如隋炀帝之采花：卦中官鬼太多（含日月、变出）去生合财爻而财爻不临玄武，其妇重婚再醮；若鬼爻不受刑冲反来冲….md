# 官众而诸凶皆避，如隋炀帝之采花：卦中官鬼太多（含日月、变出）去生合财爻而财爻不临玄武，其妇重婚再醮；若鬼爻不受刑冲反来冲克财爻，是生离活别、不是夫死再嫁

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中官鬼太多（日辰月建是官鬼、或变出官鬼太多），都去生合财爻，而财爻不临玄武这类凶神，这个妇人一定重婚再嫁，就像隋炀帝在西苑剪彩绸做花，颜色旧了就换新的一样。如果卦里的官鬼不受刑冲克害，反过来冲克财爻，那是夫妻生离，不是丈夫死了再嫁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram has too many Pressure lines (including the Day or Month being Pressure, or too many changing out), all generating and combining with the Wealth line, and the Wealth line does not sit on the Dark Tortoise or another ill spirit, the woman surely marries again, like Emperor Yang of Sui, who cut silk into flowers in the West Park and replaced them with fresher colors when the colors faded. If the hexagram's Pressure line is not punished or clashed but itself clashes and controls the Wealth line, it is parting alive, not a widow marrying again.

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「重婚再醮」的醮是古代嫁女时用酒祭神的礼，「再醮」就是再嫁。这一条讲的是妇人卜自己的婚姻状况；男子问妻子的事，可以对照 LY.MARRY.HJC.27（财化财、鬼化鬼）和 LY.MARRY.YY.08（鬼动化财、财动化鬼）。
>
> *Note:* 'Jiao' in 'marries again' is the wine offering to the spirits made when a daughter married in old times, and 'a second jiao' means marrying again. This entry is about a woman's marital state as she reads it herself; for a man asking about his wife compare LY.MARRY.HJC.27 (Wealth turning into Wealth, Pressure into Pressure) and LY.MARRY.YY.08 (Pressure moving into Wealth, Wealth moving into Pressure).
