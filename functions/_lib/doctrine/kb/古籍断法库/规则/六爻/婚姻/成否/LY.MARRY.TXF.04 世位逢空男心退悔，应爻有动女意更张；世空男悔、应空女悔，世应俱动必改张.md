# 世位逢空男心退悔，应爻有动女意更张；世空男悔、应空女悔，世应俱动必改张

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻落空，男方心意退缩后悔；应爻发动，女方心意就会更改。世空男悔，应空女悔，即使有吉神也难成。世应都发动，事情一定有更改；发动的爻若被日辰合住，不管愿不愿意也要成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Self meeting Void, the man's heart draws back in regret; the Other line moving, the woman's intent is sure to change. Self Void means the man regrets, Other Void means the woman regrets, and even with a lucky spirit it is hard to make. If Self and Other both move, the matter is sure to be altered; if the moving line is combined by the Day, whether or not you wish it, it will be made.
