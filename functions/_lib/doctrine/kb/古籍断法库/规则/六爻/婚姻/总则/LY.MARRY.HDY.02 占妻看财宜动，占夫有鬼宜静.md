# 占妻看财宜动，占夫有鬼宜静

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占妻子看财爻，宜动；占丈夫要有鬼爻，宜静。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To divine a wife look at the Wealth line, which should move; to divine a husband the Pressure line must be present and should be still.

> [!warning] 打架
> 占妻看财爻：《海底眼》说宜动；《火珠林》说「占妻看财爻，宜静」（LY.MARRY.HZL.04）；《易林补遗》也说要静。占夫看鬼爻，两家都说宜静。这一处是《海底眼》独异，也可能是转录之误，书里无从核对。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For divining a wife by the Wealth line, the Haidi Yan says it should move; the Huozhulin says 'for a wife look at the Wealth line, which should be still' (LY.MARRY.HZL.04); the Yilin Buyi also asks for stillness. For a husband by the Pressure line both say it should be still. Here the Haidi Yan stands alone; it may be a transcription slip, and there is nothing in the books to check it against.
