# 以应爻为男家是死法：财官为重，世应为轻，女家占男也要财爻不失陷

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 女家占男，用官鬼，把应爻当男家——这是古法，也是死法，断卦要活。官鬼旺相持世（婚该成）而应爻受克（男家像是不肯）时怎么断？觉子的判法：财、官为重，世、应为轻；就算是女家占男，也要财爻不失陷。男女两个人的事，财爻被回头克、又被丑土克，就生不了卯木官鬼，男女不能相合相生，婚姻即使成了也会有变。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the woman's family divines about the man, Pressure is the Subject and the Other line stands for the man's family; that is the old method, and a dead one, and a hexagram must be judged with flexibility. What if a strong Pressure line holds the Self line (the marriage should be made) while the Other line is controlled (the man's family seems unwilling)? Juezi's judgment: Wealth and Pressure carry the weight, the Self and Other lines matter less; even when the woman's family divines about the man, the Wealth line must not fall into ruin. The matter concerns both the man and the woman: if Wealth is controlled by what it turns into and again by Chou Earth, it cannot generate the Mao Wood Pressure, the two cannot combine or generate each other, and even if the marriage is made it will meet change.

> | 夫所得之卦，岂皆显而易见者哉？ | 所得到的卦，难道都是明摆着一看就懂的吗？ | Are the hexagrams one obtains all plain and easy to see? |
> | 即如此卦，女家占男，卯木旺官持世，其婚必成，而应爻受克，又为男家不允，似此，何以决之？ | 就拿这一卦来说，女家占男方，卯木旺官持世，这门婚事该成，可是应爻受克，又表示男家不答应，像这样，怎么来判定？ | Take this very hexagram: the woman's family divines about the man; the Mao Wood Pressure line, strong, holds the Self line, so the marriage should be made, yet the Other line is controlled, which would mean the man's family refuses; in such a case, how is it decided? |
> | 要知财官为重，世应为轻，虽是女家占男，亦要财爻不致于失陷。 | 要知道财、官为重，世、应为轻，虽然是女家占男方，也要财爻不至于失陷。 | Know that Wealth and Pressure carry the weight and the Self and Other lines matter less; even though it is the woman's family divining about the man, the Wealth line must not fall into ruin. |
> | 此—卦也，关乎男女二人，财被回头克，又逢丑土之克，如何能生卯木之官？ | 这一卦，关系到男女两个人，财爻被回头克，又碰上丑土来克，怎么能去生卯木的官鬼？ | This hexagram concerns both the man and the woman: the Wealth line is controlled by what it turns into and is again controlled by the Chou Earth, so how can it generate the Mao Wood Pressure? |
> | 男女不能相合相生，婚姻虽成，亦当有变。 | 男女不能相合相生，婚事即使成了，也该有变故。 | The man and woman cannot combine or generate each other; even if the marriage is made, there will be change. |
> | 后果聘定于四月，未及成婚，被贼兵于四月内劫去，应巳月者，亥水逢月破也。 | 后来果然四月订了聘，还没来得及成婚，人在四月里被贼兵劫走了，应在巳月，是因为亥水逢月破。 | Afterwards the betrothal was indeed settled in the fourth month, and before the wedding could take place the person was carried off by bandit soldiers within that month; it answered in the Si month because the Hai Water met a Month-break. |

> [!note] 按
