# 孤辰值鬼夫必无儿，寡宿临财妻当少子；父旺少兄妨儿女，妻强无鬼损公姑；子摇刑夫财兴有解，兄动克妇子发无妨

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 孤辰星落在鬼爻上，丈夫必定没有儿子；寡宿星临在财爻上，妻子该少子息。男子怕孤辰临官鬼，女子嫌寡宿落妻财，遇到其中一样，以后没有子息，不仅儿女受伤，更主夫妻相克。父母爻旺、兄弟爻少，妨碍儿女；妻财强、没有鬼爻，损害公婆。子孙爻动固然刑克丈夫，但财爻兴旺就有化解；兄弟爻动虽说克妻，但子孙发动就无妨。父母爻动而兄弟爻不动，一定伤儿女；财爻动而鬼爻不动，一定损公婆。子孙动伤夫，财动去生官鬼反而吉；兄弟兴旺害妻，子孙一同发动就无妨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Solitary Star on the Pressure line: the husband surely has no son; the Widowhood Star on the Wealth line: the wife should have few children. A man fears the Solitary Star on Pressure and a woman dislikes the Widowhood Star on Wealth; meeting either, there will be no offspring later, and not only are the children harmed, husband and wife also harm each other. A strong Resource line with few Peer lines hinders the children; a strong Wealth line without Pressure harms the parents-in-law. When the Output line moves it indeed punishes the husband, but if Wealth flourishes there is a remedy; when a Peer line moves it is said to harm the wife, yet if Output moves there is no harm. When the Resource line moves and the Peer line does not, the children are surely harmed; when Wealth moves and Pressure does not, the parents-in-law are surely hurt. When Output moves it harms the husband, but if Wealth moves to generate Pressure it is on the contrary auspicious; when a Peer line flourishes it harms the wife, but if the Output line moves together with it, there is no harm.

> [!note] 按
> 孤辰、寡宿是神煞（孤独之星、寡居之星）。「父旺少兄妨儿女」是把「父旺」「少兄」连读，原文标点没有分开；译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* The Solitary Star and the Widowhood Star are spirit-stars. 'Strong Resource, few Peer lines' is read together as the original does not punctuate between them; the translation is literal.
