# 家宅千金赋·天喜星临必然嫁娶添丁；咸池入酉花酒生涯；财入咸池化鬼女多独守、子临寡宿化官男主孤虚

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 天喜星临着，一定有嫁娶、添丁的事。咸池星落在酉上，一定过着花酒的生涯。妻财爻临着咸池而化成官鬼，女子多半独守空房；子孙爻临着寡宿而化成官鬼，男子多是孤独空虚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Tianxi star arrives there will surely be weddings and new births. The Xianchi star entering You means a life of flowers and wine. Wealth on the Xianchi star changing into Pressure: the woman is mostly left to keep alone; Output on the Widow star changing into Pressure: the man is lonely and empty.

## 《黄金策·家宅千金赋》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9654 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "The household rhapsody", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 咸池入酉，必然花酒生涯。 | 咸池星落在酉上，一定过着花酒的生涯。 | The Xianchi star entering You means a life of flowers and wine, surely. |
> | 财入咸池化鬼，女多独守； | 妻财爻临着咸池而化成官鬼，女子多半独守空房； | Wealth on the Xianchi star changing into Pressure: the woman is mostly left to keep alone; |
> | 子临寡宿化官，男主孤虚。 | 子孙爻临着寡宿而化成官鬼，男子多是孤独空虚。 | Output on the Widow star changing into Pressure: the man is lonely and empty. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「寡宿」是与「孤辰」相对的神煞，主孤独；「孤虚」是孤独空虚。原文在这里没有解释，也没有说「财」「子」是哪一爻（译文按上下文译作妻财爻、子孙爻）。同一行里讲丧门、披头、五鬼、羊刃的几联与婚姻无关，没有收。
>
> *Note:* The 'Widow star' is the star that pairs with the 'Lonely star' and rules solitude; 'lonely and empty' is solitary and hollow. The original does not explain it here or say which lines 'Wealth' and 'Output' are (the translation follows the context and takes them as the Wealth and Output lines). The couplets in the same line on the Mourning Gate, Dishevelled Hair, the Five Ghosts and the Sheep Blade have nothing to do with marriage and are not taken.
