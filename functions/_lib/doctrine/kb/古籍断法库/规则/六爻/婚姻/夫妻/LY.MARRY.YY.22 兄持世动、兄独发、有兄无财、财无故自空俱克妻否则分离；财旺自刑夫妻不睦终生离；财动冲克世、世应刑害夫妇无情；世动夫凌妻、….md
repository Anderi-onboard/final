# 兄持世动、兄独发、有兄无财、财无故自空俱克妻否则分离；财旺自刑夫妻不睦终生离；财动冲克世、世应刑害夫妇无情；世动夫凌妻、应动妻欺夫；财临死墓绝胎加刑刃兼兄克刑克三妻；旺相无伤亦妻不贤

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻持世而动，或兄弟爻独自发动，或卦里有兄弟爻没有财爻，或财爻无缘无故自己落空，都主克妻，否则也分离。财爻旺相又自刑（财持辰、午、酉、亥），主夫妻不和，终究生离——因为旺的不会死，只会离。兄弟爻在旁边动，或持世不动而财爻不空，也主不和睦。财动冲克世，或世应互相刑害，主夫妇无情。世爻动，丈夫欺凌妻子；应爻动，妻子欺压丈夫；世应都动，必常争斗。财爻临死、墓、绝、胎，又加刑、刃，再有兄弟爻动来克，主刑克三个妻子；得月日生合，或许带病延年。飞爻财入兄乡，或应爻持兄弟爻动，遇月日刑害克破，死。即使旺相没有受伤，也是妻子不贤：爱生是非，和妯娌不和，和丈夫不一条心，多偷财物、私藏归己、漏给别家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Peer line holding the Self line and moving, or a Peer line moving alone, or the hexagram having a Peer line but no Wealth, or the Wealth line going Void for no reason: all mean the wife is harmed, or else they part. A Wealth line strong yet self-punishing (Wealth on Chen, Wu, You or Hai): husband and wife are at odds and in the end part alive, since a strong line does not die but leaves. A Peer line moving elsewhere, or holding the Self line without moving while Wealth is not Void: this too means discord. Wealth moving to clash with and control the Self line, or the Self and Other punishing and harming each other: the couple have no affection. When the Self line moves the husband bullies the wife; when the Other line moves the wife overrides the husband; when both move they quarrel often. Wealth on death, tomb, extinction or womb stages with the punishment and blade stars added and a Peer line moving to control it: three wives are punished and harmed; with generation and combination from the Month and Day she may live on with illness. Wealth on the flying line entering the Peer's branch, or the Other line holding a Peer line and moving, meeting punishment, harm, control or break from the Month and Day: she dies. Even strong and unharmed, the wife is not virtuous: she stirs up quarrels, is at odds with her sisters-in-law and not of one mind with her husband, often steals goods, hides them for herself and leaks them to another house.
