# 妻为闺女为再醮：卦中一财不见鬼是闺女；财鬼相合或财下伏鬼已见一夫，一财二鬼相合或财下伏鬼又化鬼已见二夫；二鬼一空一旺必再醮，二鬼旺而财被刑冲克害生离改嫁；《黄金策》妻克世身重合应妻必重婚；床帐香闺法

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问妻子是闺女还是再嫁：卦中只有一个财爻、不见鬼爻的，是闺女。财鬼相合，或财爻下伏着鬼爻的，已经有过一个丈夫；一个财爻和两个鬼爻相合，或财爻下伏鬼又化出鬼的，已经有过两个丈夫。卦里有两个鬼爻，一个空一个旺的，一定是再嫁；两个鬼爻都旺，而日月、动爻刑冲克害财爻的，主生离改嫁。《黄金策》说：妻爻克世身、又重复合应爻，妻子必定重婚；世、应、妻爻三合，该招偏正两种丈夫；官爻多而没有凶煞冲犯，妇人该再嫁。《明睿抄本》说：男子取卦身所生的爻为床帐（卦身所生之爻），女子取卦身所克的爻为香闺（卦身所克之爻）。香闺入墓、绝，还没配成；床帐空亡，还没娶妻。这个办法灵验，不要传给不该传的人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask whether the wife is a maiden or has married before: a hexagram with a single Wealth line and no Pressure means a maiden. If Wealth and Pressure combine, or Pressure is hidden beneath Wealth, she has already had one husband; one Wealth line combining with two Pressure lines, or Pressure hidden beneath Wealth and again changing into Pressure, means two husbands. A hexagram with two Pressure lines, one Void and one strong, means she has surely married again; two strong Pressure lines while the Day, Month and moving lines punish, clash, control or harm Wealth mean parting alive and remarriage. The Huangjin Ce says: the wife's line controlling the Self (Body) and doubly combining with the Other line, the wife will surely marry twice; the Self, Other and wife's lines forming a three-harmony, she takes both a secondary and a principal husband; many Pressure lines with no ill omens touching them, the woman should remarry. The Mingrui manuscript says: a man takes the line the hexagram Body generates as the bed-curtains, a woman takes the line the Body controls as the fragrant chamber. If the fragrant chamber is in a tomb or extinct, the match is not yet made; if the bed-curtains are Void, he has not yet taken a wife. This method is wonderfully accurate; do not pass it to the unworthy.
