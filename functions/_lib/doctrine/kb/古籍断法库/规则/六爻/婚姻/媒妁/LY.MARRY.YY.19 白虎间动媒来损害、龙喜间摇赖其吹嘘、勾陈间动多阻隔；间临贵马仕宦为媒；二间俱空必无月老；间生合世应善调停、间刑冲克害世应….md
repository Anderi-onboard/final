# 白虎间动媒来损害、龙喜间摇赖其吹嘘、勾陈间动多阻隔；间临贵马仕宦为媒；二间俱空必无月老；间生合世应善调停、间刑冲克害世应心怀欺骗；世应冲克间两家相怨；日辰动爻带虎雀刑冲克害间者群口交谪

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 白虎带的间爻动，媒人带来损害；青龙、喜神带的间爻动，靠他撮合；勾陈带的间爻动，必多阻隔。间爻临贵人、驿马，是做官的人做媒。两个间爻都空，一定没有媒人。间爻生合世应，善于调停；间爻刑冲克害世应，心里怀着欺骗。世应冲克间爻，两家互相埋怨。日辰、动爻带白虎、朱雀，刑冲克害间爻的，众口交相指责。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The White Tiger with a moving between line: the go-between brings harm; the Azure Dragon and Joy star with a moving between line: one depends on his boosting; Hook Earth with a moving between line: many obstructions. The between line on the Nobleman and Post Horse: an official is the go-between. Both between lines Void: there is surely no go-between. The between line generating or combining with the Self and Other lines: skilled at mediating; punishing, clashing, controlling or harming them: he harbors deceit. The Self and Other lines clashing with and controlling the between line: the two families resent each other. The Day or a moving line carrying the White Tiger and Vermilion Bird and punishing, clashing, controlling or harming the between line: everyone blames him in chorus.
