# 财爻变鬼变冲妻遭久疾，鬼象化官化破夫染陈灾：财忌冲或化鬼、鬼忌刑冲再化官爻；病在何方看鬼在何卦何爻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻是妻子本人，变成鬼爻或被冲，妻子遭受久病；鬼爻是丈夫，忌刑冲又变成官爻或破，丈夫染上旧病。要知道病在哪个方位，看鬼爻在哪个卦、哪一爻，可以断出带病的部位。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wealth line is the wife's own body: changing into Pressure or clashed, the wife suffers a long illness; the Pressure line is the husband: if it is punished or clashed and changes into Pressure again or into a break, the husband contracts an old affliction. To know where the illness lies, look at which trigram and which line the Pressure is in; the diseased part can be judged from it.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 530 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财为妻体，切不宜冲或化鬼爻，便生残疾。 | 财爻是妻子本人，千万不宜被冲，或者变成鬼爻，那样就生残疾。 | The Wealth line is the wife's own body, and it must not be clashed or change into a Pressure line, or a disability arises. |
> | 鬼为夫主，忌犯刑冲再化官爻，夫当有疾。 | 鬼爻是丈夫，忌犯刑冲，又变成官爻，丈夫就有病。 | The Pressure line is the husband; if it suffers punishment or clash and changes into a Pressure line again, the husband has illness. |
> | 要知疾在何方，看鬼在谁卦谁爻，可断带疾之处，详见身命章中。 | 要知道病在哪个方位，看鬼爻在哪个卦、哪一爻，可以断出带病的部位，详见身命章中。 | To know where the illness lies, look at which trigram and which line the Pressure is in; the diseased part can be judged from it, as detailed in the chapter on one's person and fate. |
