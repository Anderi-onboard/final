# 财官不失方谐合，世应临空岂得成，三合其人能赞美，六冲此客欠调停：卦得财鬼俱全方成，无鬼不谐；世应临空纵有财官也难合；六合定成、六冲未就、合处逢冲将成有变

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求媒人、同时占成不成：卦里财、鬼两样齐全，才能成；没有官鬼，事情一定不谐。世应落在空地，即使有财官也难相合。卦逢六合，一定成婚；见六冲，姻缘还没成；合处又逢冲，快成时有变。三合，媒人能说好话促成；六冲，媒人欠调停的本事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In seeking a go-between and also divining whether the match will be made: the hexagram must have both Wealth and Pressure before it can be accomplished, and with no Pressure the matter does not go well. If the Self and Other lines fall in Void, even with Wealth and Pressure present it is hard for them to combine. A Six-combine hexagram surely makes the marriage; a Six-clash means the match is not yet made; a combination that meets a clash is about to succeed and then changes. With a three-harmony the go-between can speak well and bring it about; with a Six-clash he lacks the skill to mediate.
