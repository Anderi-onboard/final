# 媒约：以间爻为用；世生合间男家之亲、应生合间女家之亲、世应俱生合间两家皆亲；间阳男媒间阴女媒；衰墓老媒生旺少媒；间阴化阳女人先说；间空化空更人为媒；间兄两动二人争媒；蛇武兄动冰人诡诈；朱雀旺空媒多诳语

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以间爻为用神。世爻生合间爻，是男家的亲戚；应爻生合间爻，是女家的亲戚；世应都生合间爻，两家都是亲戚。间爻属阳是男媒人，属阴是女媒人；衰、入墓是老媒人，生旺是年轻的媒人。间爻属阴变阳，是女人先开口；间爻空又化空，换人做媒；间爻和兄弟爻都动，两个人争着做媒。螣蛇、玄武、兄弟爻动，媒人诡诈；朱雀旺相或空，媒人多说谎话。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Take the between lines as the Subject. The Self line generating or combining with a between line: a relative of the man's family; the Other line doing so: a relative of the woman's family; the Self and Other both doing so: a relative of both. A yang between line is a male go-between, a yin one a female go-between; weak or in a tomb, an old go-between; in birth or strength, a young one. A yin between line changing into yang: a woman speaks first; a between line Void and changing into Void: another person becomes the go-between; the between line and a Peer line both moving: two people compete to be the go-between. The Coiling Snake and Dark Tortoise with a moving Peer line: the go-between is crafty and deceitful; the Vermilion Bird strong or Void: he tells many lies.
