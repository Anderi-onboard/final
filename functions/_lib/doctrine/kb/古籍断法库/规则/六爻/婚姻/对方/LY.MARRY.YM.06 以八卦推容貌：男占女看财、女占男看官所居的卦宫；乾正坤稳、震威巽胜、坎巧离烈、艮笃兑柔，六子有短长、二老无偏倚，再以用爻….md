# 以八卦推容貌：男占女看财、女占男看官所居的卦宫；乾正坤稳、震威巽胜、坎巧离烈、艮笃兑柔，六子有短长、二老无偏倚，再以用爻衰旺参之

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用八卦推对方的容貌性情。男方占女方，看财爻所在的卦宫；女方占男方，看官爻所在的卦宫。财在乾：仪容端正、禀性聪明，有男子的风度，叫配天德；财在坤：体貌稳重、气度宽宏，有母后的贤德，叫符地德；震是雷的性子，动而有威；巽是风的体质，多变好胜；坎多志向和巧思，离多激烈；艮端正笃实、擅长针线雕镂，兑喜悦柔和、擅长言语。女占男：官在乾，从容敬慎、性直貌壮；用神在坤，和悦谦卑、言实行朴；震好激昂、常怀英雄之志；巽好贪求、屡屡专心于货利；坎的智识藏在里面，官爻有病的短于忠信；离的聪明显露在外，官爻充实的长于文章；艮有技艺才能，行为要戒防「转石」；兑有奇巧，言语像悬河。乾坤以外的六卦性情有长有短，乾坤两个老卦的德行没有偏，再用用爻的旺衰参看：旺就取它的长处，衰就显出它的短处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Use the eight trigrams to infer the other person's looks and temperament. When a man divines a woman, look at the trigram palace where the Wealth line sits; when a woman divines a man, at the palace where the Pressure line sits. Wealth in Qian: upright bearing, intelligent nature, a man's air, called matching the virtue of Heaven; Wealth in Kun: steady build, a broad and embracing bearing, the worth of an empress mother, called according with the virtue of Earth; Zhen, thunder, moves with authority; Xun, wind, changes and loves to win; Kan has ambition and ingenuity, Li is often fierce; Gen is upright and sincere and skilled at needlework and carving, Dui is pleasing and gentle and good with words. A woman divining a man: Pressure in Qian, composed and reverent-careful, straight in nature and sturdy in looks; the Subject in Kun, gentle and humble, honest in speech and plain in conduct; Zhen loves to stir and rouse and holds a hero's ambition; Xun likes covetous longing and devotes itself to profit; Kan's intelligence is hidden within, and where Pressure is sick it is short of loyalty and good faith; Li's cleverness shows outside, and where Pressure is solid it excels in writing; Gen has skill and talent but its conduct must guard against the rolling stone; Dui has uncommon cleverness and its speech runs on like a hanging river. The six children have qualities with strengths and shortcomings, while the two elders have no bias; then weigh the strength of the Subject line: strong, follow its strengths; weak, its shortcomings show.

> [!warning] 打架
> 李我平批评过这一读法（LY.MARRY.ZS.22）：同一个卦，按八宫读是端正聪明的淑女，按六神读财爻临白虎又成了悖逆之星，两种读法彼此打架；他主张用财、官兼看世、应来断就够了，不要求容貌才情十全。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Li Woping objected to this way of reading (LY.MARRY.ZS.22): the same hexagram read by the eight palaces gives an upright, intelligent lady, and read by the six spirits a Wealth line with the White Tiger becomes a star of perversity, so the two readings quarrel with each other; he holds that judging by Wealth and Pressure together with Self and Other is enough, and that one should not demand a perfect face and talent.

> [!note] 按
> 「卦宫」在这里没有解释：是指财（或官）所在的那一个经卦，还是整个卦所属的八宫，书里没说，译文照原文写作 trigram palace。「其行戒于转石」的意思书里没有讲，照字面译。
>
> *Note:* The book does not say what 'trigram palace' is here: the trigram in which the Wealth (or Pressure) line sits, or the palace of the eight to which the whole hexagram belongs. It is translated as written. It also does not explain 'its conduct must guard against the rolling stone', which is translated literally.
