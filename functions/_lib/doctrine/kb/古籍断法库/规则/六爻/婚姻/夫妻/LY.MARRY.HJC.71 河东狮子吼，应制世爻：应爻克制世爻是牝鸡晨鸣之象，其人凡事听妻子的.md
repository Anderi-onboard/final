# 河东狮子吼，应制世爻：应爻克制世爻是牝鸡晨鸣之象，其人凡事听妻子的

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻克制世爻，是母鸡打鸣的样子，这个人凡事听妻子的，就像宋朝陈季常怕老婆的「河东狮子吼」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other line controlling and holding down the Self line is the image of the hen crowing at dawn: the man goes by his wife's word in everything, like Chen Jichang of Song and the 'lion of Hedong roars'.

## 《黄金策·身命》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6094 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Person and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 河东狮子吼，应制世爻。 | 河东狮子吼叫，是应爻制住了世爻。 | The lion of Hedong roars: the Other line holds the Self line down. |

^q1

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1702 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应爻克冲世爻，其人凭妻言语，如宋陈季常河东狮子吼之事也。 | 应爻克冲世爻，这个人凭着妻子的话行事，就像宋朝陈季常「河东狮子吼」那件事。 | The Other line controlling and clashing the Self line: the man goes by his wife's word, like the affair of Chen Jichang of Song and the 'lion of Hedong roars'. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「牝鸡晨鸣」出自《尚书·牧誓》「牝鸡无晨」，指妻子当家作主。「河东狮吼」是苏东坡取笑朋友陈季常怕老婆的诗句，后来成了怕老婆的代称。卜筮大全「狮子吼」，卜筮正宗「狮吼」，字数不同，意思一样。
>
> *Note:* 'The hen crowing at dawn' comes from the Book of Documents, 'Oath at Mu', 'the hen does not herald the dawn', and means a wife who rules the house. 'The lion of Hedong roars' is Su Dongpo's teasing of his friend Chen Jichang for fearing his wife, and became a byword for a henpecked husband. The Quanshu has 'lion roars' and the Zhengzong 'lion roar', which differ in length but not in meaning.
