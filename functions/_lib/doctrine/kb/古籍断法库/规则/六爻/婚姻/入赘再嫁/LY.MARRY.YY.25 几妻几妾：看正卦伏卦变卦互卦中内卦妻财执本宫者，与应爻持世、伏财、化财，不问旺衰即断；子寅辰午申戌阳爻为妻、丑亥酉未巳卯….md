# 几妻几妾：看正卦伏卦变卦互卦中内卦妻财执本宫者，与应爻持世、伏财、化财，不问旺衰即断；子寅辰午申戌阳爻为妻、丑亥酉未巳卯阴爻为妾，一财一位；二财并旺两妻营活；一空一旺续一弦；二财俱旺一带咸池一偏一正；看何爻得日月世生合知谁得宠

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问一个人有几个妻、几个妾：只看正卦、伏卦、变卦、互卦里，内卦上属本宫的妻财爻，以及应爻持世、伏着的财、化出的财，不问旺衰、有没有德合，就断有几个妻、几个妾。子、寅、辰、午、申、戌属阳爻的是妻，丑、亥、酉、未、巳、卯属阴爻的是妾；一个财爻算一个位。内卦本宫一个财爻、应爻又一个财爻，两个财爻都旺的，一定是两个妻子一起操持生计。一个空一个旺的，会续一次弦；两个空一个旺的，续两次；三个空一个旺的，续三次。两个财爻都旺而其中一个带咸池煞的，一定一偏一正。看哪个爻和日月、世爻生合，就知道谁得宠掌权。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask how many wives and how many concubines a man has, look only at the main, hidden, changed and nuclear hexagrams for Wealth lines in the inner trigram that belong to the home palace, an Other line holding the Self line, hidden Wealth and changed-out Wealth; regardless of strength, weakness, virtue or combination, count them as so many wives and concubines. Those on the yang branches Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen and Xu are wives, those on the yin branches Chou, Hai, You, Wei, Si and Mao are concubines; one Wealth line is one place. If the inner trigram's home palace has one Wealth line and the Other line has another, and both are strong, two wives surely make a living together. One Void and one strong: he will take another wife once; two Void and one strong: twice; three Void and one strong: three times. If both Wealth lines are strong and one carries the Xianchi star, one is surely a secondary wife and one the principal wife. See which line is generated or combined by the Day, Month and Self line, and you know who is favored and holds power.
