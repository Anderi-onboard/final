# 世应俱空，难遂百年之连理：世空自己不想成，应空对方不想成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世空，是自己不想成；强行成了，终究不如意；应空，是对方不想成；成了以后两家一定不来往。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self Void means one does not wish it oneself, and if forced through it never turns out as wished; the Other Void means the other side does not wish it, and if it comes about the two families will surely not visit each other afterwards.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6385 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世应俱空，难遂百年之连理； | 世应都空，难以如愿成就百年的姻缘。 | With the Self and Other both Void, it is hard to achieve a lifelong union as wished. |
