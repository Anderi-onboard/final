# 聘礼：卦无父者无雁币或无婚书；财旺币厚财衰币薄，无财或财化空绝无币礼；金财聘金厚、木财果核盛、火财缎疋多、水财珠玉鱼酒丰、土财以田产代；未财旺羊肥、亥财旺猪硕

六爻 · 婚姻 · 聘嫁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里没有父母爻，没有雁币（聘礼），或者没有婚书。财爻旺聘礼厚，财爻衰聘礼薄；没有财爻，或财爻化成空、绝，就没有聘礼。金的财爻生合应爻，聘金丰厚；木的，果品多；火的，缎匹多；水的，珠玉鱼酒丰盛；土的，用田产代替聘礼。未的财爻旺，羊肥；亥的财爻旺，猪大。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hexagram without a Resource line has no goose-and-silk betrothal gift, or no marriage contract. Strong Wealth means rich gifts, weak Wealth slight ones; with no Wealth line, or Wealth changing into Void or extinction, there are no gifts. A Metal Wealth line generating or combining with the Other line: the betrothal money is generous; Wood: fruits and nuts in plenty; Fire: many bolts of satin; Water: pearls, jade, fish and wine in abundance; Earth: land and property take the place of gifts. A strong Wei Wealth line: fat sheep; a strong Hai Wealth line: big pigs.
