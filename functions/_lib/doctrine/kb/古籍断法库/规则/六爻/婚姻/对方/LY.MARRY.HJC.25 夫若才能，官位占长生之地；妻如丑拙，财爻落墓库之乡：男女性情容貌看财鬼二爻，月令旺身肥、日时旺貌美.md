# 夫若才能，官位占长生之地；妻如丑拙，财爻落墓库之乡：男女性情容貌看财鬼二爻，月令旺身肥、日时旺貌美

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想知男女的性情容貌，看财、鬼两爻：月令旺身体胖，日时旺相貌美；月令衰身体瘦，日时衰相貌丑；月令旺而日时衰，身胖貌不扬；月令衰而日时旺，身瘦貌美；衰而有扶，丑却有才能；旺而入墓，虽美而愚拙；只看性情，男看鬼，女看财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know the temperament and looks of the man or woman, look at the Wealth and Pressure lines: thriving in the month means a stout body, thriving on the day and hour means good looks; weak in the month means a thin body, weak on the day and hour means plain looks; thriving in the month but weak on the day and hour, a stout body and unremarkable looks; weak in the month but thriving on the day and hour, a thin body and handsome looks; weak but supported, plain but capable; thriving but entering the tomb, handsome but dull; for temperament alone, look at Pressure for a man and Wealth for a woman.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2036 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 要知男妇情性容貌，财鬼二爻取之，旺者身肥，衰者瘦弱。 | 想知道男女的性情容貌，取财、鬼两爻，旺的身体胖，衰的身体瘦弱。 | To know the temperament and looks of the man or woman, take the Wealth and Pressure lines: a thriving line means a stout body, a weak one a thin and frail body. |
> | 如虎蛇勾陈玄武属土火，貌丑； | 如果是虎、蛇、勾陈、玄武属土火的，相貌丑； | If it is the Tiger, the Snake, Hook Earth or the Dark Tortoise and belongs to Earth or Fire, the looks are plain; |
> | 如青龙属木金，貌美。 | 如果是青龙属木金的，相貌美。 | if it is the Azure Dragon and belongs to Wood or Metal, the looks are handsome. |
> | 衰而有扶，丑有才能； | 衰而有扶，丑却有才能； | Weak but supported: plain but capable; |
> | 旺而入墓，美偏愚拙。 | 旺而入墓，美却愚拙。 | thriving but entering the tomb: handsome but dull. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 王洪绪在下一条（HJC.26）里说旺衰不能按四季论。本条「月令旺、日时旺」是旧注的说法；王洪绪另加了六神与五行的取象。
>
> *Note:* Wang Hongxu says in the next entry (HJC.26) that thriving and weakness must not be judged by the four seasons. The 'month and day-hour' scheme here is the old commentary's; Wang Hongxu adds his own images from the six spirits and five phases.
