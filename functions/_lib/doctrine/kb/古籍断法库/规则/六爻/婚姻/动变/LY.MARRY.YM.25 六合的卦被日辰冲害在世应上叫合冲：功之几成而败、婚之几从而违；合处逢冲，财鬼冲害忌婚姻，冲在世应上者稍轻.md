# 六合的卦被日辰冲害在世应上叫合冲：功之几成而败、婚之几从而违；合处逢冲，财鬼冲害忌婚姻，冲在世应上者稍轻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六合卦而世爻或应爻被日辰冲害的，叫合冲，好比功业快成却败了，财已到手却失了，婚事快要答应却违了，谋划快要成功却变了，不可居守。不叫「冲合」的缘故是：已经败了就不能再成，已经解开了就不能再交合，已经失去了就不能再得到，即使得到也是应在别处了。另外爻的合处遇到冲：财爻、官鬼被冲害，忌婚姻；父母、官爻被冲害，忌功名；子孙、妻财被冲害，忌谋生；但比起冲害落在世应上的就稍轻，所以合处遇冲，前人专门以世应为主。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A six-combine hexagram whose Self or Other line is clashed or harmed by the Day is called a combination-clash: like a feat nearly done that fails, wealth once gained that is lost, a marriage nearly agreed that goes against, a scheme nearly achieved that changes; one cannot rest on it. It is not called a 'clash-combination' because what has been spoiled cannot then be made, what has come apart cannot then be joined, and what has been lost cannot then be regained, and even if regained it answers elsewhere. Besides this there is a line's combination meeting a clash: Wealth and Pressure clashed or harmed is bad for marriage, Resource and Pressure clashed or harmed for office and fame, Output and Wealth clashed or harmed for making a living; but these are lighter than the clash falling on Self and Other, so for a combination meeting a clash the worthies of old looked chiefly at Self and Other.

## 《易冒·合冲章第三十四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 585–587 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 34: Combination and clash", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且有爻之合处逢冲者，财鬼冲害忌婚姻，父官冲害忌功名，子妻冲害忌谋生，然而视世应冲害者则稍轻矣，故合处逢冲，前贤专主世应也。 | 还有爻的合处遇到冲的情形：财爻、官鬼被冲害，忌婚姻；父母、官爻被冲害，忌功名；子孙、妻财被冲害，忌谋生；但比起冲害落在世应上的，就稍轻了，所以合处遇冲，前人专门以世应为主。 | There is also the case of a line's combination meeting a clash: Wealth and Pressure clashed or harmed is bad for marriage, Resource and Pressure clashed or harmed is bad for office and fame, Output and Wealth clashed or harmed is bad for making a living; but compared with clash or harm falling on Self and Other these are somewhat lighter, so for a combination meeting a clash the worthies of old looked chiefly at Self and Other. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一章的小注还有一个卦例（午月丙子日占婚，得兑之节），文字读不通（先说两家后来都得相偕，又说这一占不成），本库没有收。「财鬼」「父官」「子妻」是两个六亲的简写。
>
> *Note:* The note to this passage also gives a case (a marriage divination on a Bing-Zi day in the Wu month that gave Dui changing to Jie) whose wording does not read through (it says both families came together in the end, and then that the divination did not hold); it is not included here. 'Wealth-Pressure', 'Resource-Pressure' and 'Output-Wealth' are the book's abbreviations for pairs of relations.
