# 交重兄弟克妻身，再理丝弦：兄弟爻发动克伤妻的身命，或丈夫的命临兄弟爻发动克妻身命，主妻亡而续弦

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻发动，克伤妻子的身命，或者丈夫的命临在兄弟爻上发动、克妻子的身命，主妻子亡故、丈夫续弦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a Peer line moves and injures the wife's Body or Life, or the husband's Life sits on a moving Peer line and controls the wife's Body or Life, it means the wife is lost and the husband takes a second wife.

## 《黄金策·家宅》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8047 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Chapter 19: The household", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 交重兄弟克妻身，再理丝弦； | 兄弟爻发动、克了妻子的身，又要重新整理琴弦（续弦）； | A moving Peer line controlling the wife's Body line: the strings are put in order again (he takes a second wife); |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8048 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻身爻起临兄弟之爻，发动伤身命，或夫临兄弟爻，克妻之身命，主琴弦再续也。 | 妻子的身爻起来临在兄弟爻上，发动伤了身命，或者丈夫临在兄弟爻上，克妻子的身命，主琴弦再续。 | If the wife's Body line rises onto the Peer line and moves to injure her Body or Life, or the husband sits on the Peer line and controls the wife's Body or Life, it means the strings are joined again. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3114 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟之爻发动克伤妻命，或夫命临兄弟发动，主琴瑟再续也。 | 兄弟爻发动克伤妻子的命，或者丈夫的命临在兄弟爻上发动，主琴瑟再续。 | If a Peer line moves and controls and injures the wife's Life, or the husband's Life sits on a moving Peer line, it means the lute and zither are joined again. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「再理丝弦」「琴弦再续」是续弦，即丧妻后再娶。
>
> *Note:* 'Putting the strings in order again' and 'joining the lute strings again' mean taking a second wife after losing the first.
