# 前冲后合初更张而卒和调：六冲卦而日月合世应、变成六合，占婚始悖而终就（午月丙子日兑之节）

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦本身叫「前」，变出来的卦叫「后」。前合后冲，起初繁华最后衰败；前冲后合，起初更张动荡，最后和顺；前合后合，伙伴投契而官司缠身，从头到尾结着解不开；前冲后冲，谋事难而官非散，亲疏都背离而不肯跟从。书里举的例子是：午月丙子日占婚，得兑之节，卦虽是六冲，但日辰、月建合了世爻和应爻，又变成六合，断为起初违逆、最终成就，后来两家果然走到了一起。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The original hexagram is 'before' and the hexagram it changes into is 'after'. Combination then clash: flourishing at first, withered at the end. Clash then combination: upheaval at first, harmony at the end. Combination then combination: partners agree but lawsuits entangle, tied together from start to end and not loosened. Clash then clash: schemes are hard and lawsuits scatter, and kin and strangers turn their backs and do not follow. The book's example: in the Wu month on a Bingzi day a marriage was divined and Dui changing into Jie was obtained; the hexagram was a Six-clash, but the Day and Month combined Self and Other and it changed into a Six-combine; it was judged contrary at the start and fulfilled at the end, and afterwards the two families did come together.

> [!note] 按
> 「前」「后」是这一章第 578 行定的：卦本身冲叫前冲，变出来的卦冲叫后冲（LY.MARRY.YM.24）。例子后面还有两句「此占不成，故不贵也」，意思不明（可能是说这一卦不是「合冲」那一类，所以不作为那一类的例子看重；也可能是说这次判断没有应验），而前一句已经说了两家后来都成了，所以最后两句没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Before' and 'after' are fixed at line 578 of this chapter: a clash in the hexagram itself is the front clash, and a clash in the hexagram it changes into is the back clash (LY.MARRY.YM.24). After the example come two more clauses, 'this divination does not form, and so is not valued', whose meaning is unclear (either that this hexagram is not of the 'combination-clash' kind and so is not prized as an example of that kind, or that the judgment was not confirmed); as the clause before already says the two families did come together, these last two clauses are left out.
