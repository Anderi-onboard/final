# 月建会青龙，当主婚姻妊喜：月建与青龙会合一处发动，主重重喜庆；士夫加官进禄，庶民增进钱财，成婚姻产育无不吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 取象 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 月建和青龙会合在一处发动，主一重又一重的喜庆：读书做官的人加官进禄，平民增加钱财，成就婚姻、生育，没有不吉的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Month meeting the Azure Dragon in one place and moving means joy upon joy: a gentleman gains office and salary, a commoner gains money, and marriages and births are made, all auspicious.

## 《天玄赋·家宅章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4697 行 · Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "The chapter on the household", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 月建会青龙， | 月建会青龙， | The Month meeting the Azure Dragon, |
> | 当主婚姻妊喜； | 当主婚姻妊喜； | means the joy of a marriage or of a pregnancy; |

^q1

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4699–4700 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 月建：即正月建寅之类。 | 月建，就是正月建寅这一类。 | The Month: it is the likes of the Yin of the first month. |
> | 与青龙会合一处发动，主重重喜庆。 | 和青龙会合在一处发动，主一重又一重的喜庆。 | Meeting the Azure Dragon in one place and moving, it means joy upon joy. |
> | 士夫当加官进禄，庶民则增进钱财，成婚姻产育，无不吉也。 | 士大夫会加官进禄，平民则增加钱财，成就婚姻、生育，没有不吉的。 | A gentleman will gain office and salary, a commoner will gain money, and marriages and births are made; all is auspicious. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一句在《火珠林》附的天玄赋里也有（第 1730 行），注文相同。它接在家宅章一长串六爻定位的注文后面，是那一章的收束语，前半「搜索六爻，总是家庭小事」不是判断。
>
> *Note:* The line is also in the Tianxuan Fu appended to the Huozhulin (line 1730), with the same note. It follows a long run of notes on the positions of the six lines in the household chapter and closes that chapter; its first half, 'searching the six lines is always about small household matters', is not a judgment.
