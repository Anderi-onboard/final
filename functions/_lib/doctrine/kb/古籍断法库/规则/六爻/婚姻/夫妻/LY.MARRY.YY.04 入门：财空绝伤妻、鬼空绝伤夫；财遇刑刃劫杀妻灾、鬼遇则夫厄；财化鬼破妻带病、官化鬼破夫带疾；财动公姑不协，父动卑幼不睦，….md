# 入门：财空绝伤妻、鬼空绝伤夫；财遇刑刃劫杀妻灾、鬼遇则夫厄；财化鬼破妻带病、官化鬼破夫带疾；财动公姑不协，父动卑幼不睦，兄动妻灾，子动夫灾，鬼动妯娌姑嫂不和

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 入门以后的吉凶：财爻空、绝，伤妻子；鬼爻空、绝，伤丈夫。财爻遇刑、刃、劫杀，妻子有灾；鬼爻遇刑、刃、劫杀，丈夫有厄。财爻变鬼或破，妻子带病；官爻变鬼或破，丈夫带病。财爻动，公婆不和；父母爻动，晚辈幼辈不睦；兄弟爻动妻子有灾，子孙爻动丈夫有灾；鬼爻动，妯娌姑嫂不和（丑未相冲也是这样）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The good or ill after she enters the house: Wealth Void or extinguished harms the wife; Pressure Void or extinguished harms the husband. Wealth meeting the punishment, blade and robbery stars brings the wife calamity; Pressure meeting them brings the husband distress. Wealth changing into Pressure or a break: the wife carries an illness; Pressure changing into Pressure or a break: the husband carries an illness. When Wealth moves the parents-in-law are at odds; when Resource moves the juniors do not get along; when a Peer line moves the wife has calamity, when Output moves the husband has calamity; when Pressure moves the sisters-in-law are at odds (a Chou-Wei clash is the same).
