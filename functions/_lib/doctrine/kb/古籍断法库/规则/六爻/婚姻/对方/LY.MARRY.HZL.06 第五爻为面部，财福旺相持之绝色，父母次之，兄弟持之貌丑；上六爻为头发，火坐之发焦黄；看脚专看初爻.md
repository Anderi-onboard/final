# 第五爻为面部，财福旺相持之绝色，父母次之，兄弟持之貌丑；上六爻为头发，火坐之发焦黄；看脚专看初爻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看女子的相貌：第五爻是面部，财爻、福德（子孙）爻旺相而持在这里，是绝色；父母爻次之；兄弟爻持在这里，相貌丑陋不好看。上六爻是头发，如果火爻坐在这里，主鬓发焦黄。看脚大小专看初爻：初爻是阳，主大脚；初爻是阴，主小脚（缠足）；重爻变化的拆开一半缠；交爻变化的先缠后放。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Reading the woman's looks: the fifth line is the face; if a strong Wealth or Output line holds it, she is a rare beauty; the Resource line comes next; a Peer line holding it means an ugly, unlovely face. The top line is the hair; if a Fire line sits there, the hair is scorched yellow. To see whether the feet are large or small look only at the first line: a yang first line means large feet, a yin first line small feet (bound feet); a doubled line that changes is half-bound, and a crossed line that changes is bound first and released later.

> [!note] 按
> 「扎脚」「缠」指旧时女子缠足，「重」「交」是掷钱起卦时老阳、老阴的旧称。原文把「重」和「交」写得很省，译文照字面，没有多解释。
>
> *Note:* 'Binding' refers to the old practice of foot-binding, and 'doubled' and 'crossed' are old names for the moving yang and moving yin lines in coin-casting. The text is terse; it is translated literally without further explanation.
