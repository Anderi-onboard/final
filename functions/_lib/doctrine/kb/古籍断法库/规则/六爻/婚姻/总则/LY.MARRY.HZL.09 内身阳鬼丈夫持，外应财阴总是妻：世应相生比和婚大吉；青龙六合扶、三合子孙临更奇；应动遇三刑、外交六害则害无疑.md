# 内身阳鬼丈夫持，外应财阴总是妻：世应相生比和婚大吉；青龙六合扶、三合子孙临更奇；应动遇三刑、外交六害则害无疑

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内边（内卦、身）是阳、鬼爻是阳，丈夫就在这一边；外边（外卦、应）是阴、财爻是阴，总是妻子。世爻、应爻相生，婚姻大吉；世应比和，配成夫妻相宜。青龙、六合扶持就美，三合再加子孙爻临上更妙。应爻动而遇三刑，就是刑，不用再问；外卦交来六害，害是无疑的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the inner side, the Body, yang and Pressure yang, the husband holds there; with the outer side, the Other line, yin and Wealth yin, that is always the wife. The Self and Other lines generating each other: the marriage is greatly auspicious; the Self and Other in accord, the pairing is fitting. Helped by the Azure Dragon and a Six-combine it is fair; a three-harmony with the Output line present is more wondrous still. If the Other line moves and meets a three-punishment, there is punishment, ask no further; if the outer side meets a six-harm, harm is beyond doubt.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「内身」「外应」的确切所指原文没有交代：可以读作内卦与身一边、外卦与应一边（译文取这个读法），也可以读作别的分法。同样的阴阳配法见《黄金策》第一条。
>
> *Note:* The text does not say exactly what 'inner Body' and 'outer Other' mean: they can be read as the inner trigram with the Body side and the outer trigram with the Other side (the reading taken here), or divided some other way. The same yin-yang pairing is in the first unit of the Huangjin Ce.
