# 问在外的人娶了没有：财现则娶，用病未婚

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 行人章里问出门在外的人娶了亲没有：妻财爻在卦里出现，就是娶了；用神有病，就是还没有结婚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In the chapter on travellers, on whether one away from home has married: if the Wealth line appears in the hexagram, he has married; if the Subject is sick, he is not yet married.

## 《易冒·行人章第七十七》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1695 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 77: Travellers", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 行者娶乎， | 问出门在外的人娶了亲没有？ | Has the traveller married? |
> | 财现则娶， | 妻财爻在卦里出现，就是娶了； | if the Wealth line appears in the hexagram, he has married; |
> | 用病未婚也。 | 用神有病，就是还没有结婚。 | if the Subject is sick, he is not yet married. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句和上一句「行者仕乎，官旺则仕，官空未仕也」同一个句式：问在外的人有没有做到官，看官鬼爻旺不旺、空不空；问有没有娶妻，看妻财现不现。「用病」的「病」这一段没有定义。
>
> *Note:* This sentence has the same form as the one before it, 'has the traveller taken office? if Pressure is strong he has, if Pressure is Void he has not': to ask whether one away from home has become an official, look at whether the Pressure line is strong or Void; to ask whether he has taken a wife, look at whether the Wealth line appears. The 'sickness' of 'the Subject is sick' is not defined in this passage.
