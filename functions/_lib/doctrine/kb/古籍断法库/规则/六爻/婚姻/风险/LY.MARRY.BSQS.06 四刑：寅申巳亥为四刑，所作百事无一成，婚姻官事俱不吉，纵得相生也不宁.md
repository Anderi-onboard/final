# 四刑：寅申巳亥为四刑，所作百事无一成，婚姻官事俱不吉，纵得相生也不宁

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寅、申、巳、亥是四刑：做什么事都没有一件成，婚姻和官事都不吉，即使相生也不安宁（比如寅世、巳应这一类）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Yin, Shen, Si and Hai are the four punishments: in doing anything at all nothing is accomplished, marriage and official matters are both unlucky, and even if they generate each other there is no peace (for instance, Self at Yin and Other at Si).

## 《卜筮全书·神煞歌例》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 10165 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Rhymes on the spirit stars", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 寅申巳亥为四刑， | 寅、申、巳、亥是四刑， | Yin, Shen, Si and Hai are the four punishments; |
> | 所作百事无一成； | 所做的百事没有一件成； | in what one does, of a hundred things not one is accomplished; |
> | 婚姻官事俱不吉， | 婚姻和官事都不吉， | marriage and official matters are both unlucky; |
> | 纵得相生也不宁。 | 即使得到相生，也不安宁。 | even if they generate each other there is no peace. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这条和《断易天机》里的四刑是同一首（LY.MARRY.DYTJ.26），用字略有不同：那里是「凡作百事一无成」，这里是「所作百事无一成」；举例也不同，那里是世寅应申，这里是寅世巳应。
>
> *Note:* This is the same verse as the four punishments in the Duanyi Tianji (LY.MARRY.DYTJ.26), with a small difference of wording in the second line and a different example: Self at Yin and Other at Shen there, Self at Yin and Other at Si here.
