# 将女招夫继子祥，必须兼看结婚章，内阴外阳称大吉，鬼旺财生夫妇昌：赘婿同继子，与婚姻章同看，须一阴一阳，纯阴纯阳不可，财鬼不全也不宜

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 招女婿入赘和过继儿子一样吉祥，要和婚姻章一起看：内阴外阳是大吉，鬼爻旺、财爻生，夫妇昌盛。卦中的内外卦或者世应，必须一阴一阳才是好配偶；遇到纯阴或纯阳，决不可成；财、鬼不齐全，也不合适。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Taking a son-in-law into the household is as auspicious as adopting a son, and it is to be read together with the marriage chapter: inner yin with outer yang is greatly auspicious, and with a strong Pressure line and Wealth generating, husband and wife prosper. In the hexagram the inner and outer trigrams, or the Self and Other lines, must have one yin and one yang to make a good match; if it is pure yin or pure yang it must never be made; if Wealth and Pressure are not both complete, that too is unsuitable.
