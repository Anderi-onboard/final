# 以五行观德行、诸宿验行藏：金刚木慈水智勇火文土信，用神实备五常、损则反是；驿马、羊刃、破军、文昌等星皆以用神虚实为言；嫁娶吉凶主财官，妍娇主卦象神宿

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用五行观察德行，用各个星宿检验为人。金偏向刚硬肃杀，木成全慈祥，水致力于智勇，火喜好文章，土持守诚信；用神充实，五常俱备，用神受损，就与五德相反，星宿也是这样。用神登驿马：旺相勤于奔走，休囚就游荡；用神随羊刃：得日月扶助是英雄，落空、破就暴虐蛮横。破军主疾，文昌主美，廉贞主乖戾，贪狼主机巧，武曲主丑，巨门、禄存平庸，左辅、右弼纯正，咸池主淫，天触主斑疤，天喜主贤，陀罗主凶悍——都是按用神的虚实来说。大体上，嫁娶的吉凶主要看财、官；男女的美与娇媚主要看卦象和神宿。九曜星只用在婚姻的才貌、坟茔和屋宅的占卜里。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Use the five phases to observe virtue and conduct, and the stars to check the person. Metal leans to hardness and severity, Wood fulfils kindness, Water applies itself to wisdom and courage, Fire loves literary writing, Earth holds to sincerity and good faith; where the Subject is solid the five constant virtues are complete, where the Subject is damaged it is the reverse of them, and the stars are likewise. The Subject mounting the Post Horse: strong, it is diligent and hurries on, resting or trapped, it wanders idly; the Subject following the Goat Blade: with the Day and Month behind it, a hero, Void or broken, violent and overbearing. Pojun means illness, Wenchang beauty, Lianzhen perversity, Tanlang cleverness, Wuqu ugliness, Jumen and Lucun mediocrity, Zuofu and Youbi purity, Xianchi lewdness, Tianchu blemishes, Tianxi worth, Tuoluo fierceness, all said according to whether the Subject is empty or solid. In general, the luck of a marriage rests on Wealth and Pressure, while the beauty and charm of the man and woman rest on the hexagram image and the spirit stars. The nine luminaries are used only in divinations about marriage, talent and looks, graves and houses.

> [!note] 按
> 星名照汉语拼音，不硬译：破军 Pojun、文昌 Wenchang、廉贞 Lianzhen、贪狼 Tanlang、武曲 Wuqu、巨门 Jumen、禄存 Lucun、左辅 Zuofu、右弼 Youbi（第一到第九个是九曜星），另有咸池 Xianchi、天触 Tianchu、天喜 Tianxi、陀罗 Tuoluo。「登马」「随羊」的「马」「羊」指驿马、羊刃；「癍」是斑痕、疤痕。「五常」指仁义礼智信。第三段来自诸星章。
>
> *Note:* Star names are given in pinyin and not translated: Pojun, Wenchang, Lianzhen, Tanlang, Wuqu, Jumen, Lucun, Zuofu and Youbi are the nine luminaries, and there are also Xianchi, Tianchu, Tianxi and Tuoluo. In 'mounting the Horse' and 'following the Goat' the horse and goat are the Post Horse and the Goat Blade; the character rendered 'blemishes' means spots and scars. The five constant virtues are benevolence, righteousness, propriety, wisdom and good faith. The third quotation comes from the book's chapter on the stars.
