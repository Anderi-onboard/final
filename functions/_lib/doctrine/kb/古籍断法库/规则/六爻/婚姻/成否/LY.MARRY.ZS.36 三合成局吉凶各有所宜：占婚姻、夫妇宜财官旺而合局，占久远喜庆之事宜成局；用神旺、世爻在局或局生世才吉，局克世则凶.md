# 三合成局吉凶各有所宜：占婚姻、夫妇宜财官旺而合局，占久远喜庆之事宜成局；用神旺、世爻在局或局生世才吉，局克世则凶

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 申子辰合水局、巳酉丑合金局、寅午戌合火局、亥卯未合木局；三合有四种成局的方式，有吉有凶。占祖坟、家宅，宜父母爻合成局；占婚姻、夫妇，宜财官旺而合成局；占长久的喜庆事，宜成局，永远牢固；占官司、忧疑的事而成局，一辈子心里结着解不开的结。用三合局要看条件：用神旺就无不吉，世爻在局里最好；世爻不在局里，局也要生世爻才断吉；局克世爻，按凶推。日建、月建有一个在局里，叫局旺，更吉。三爻只有两爻动不算成局，要等以后的月日补上，叫虚一待用；一爻明动一爻暗动也算两爻动；局中有一爻逢空、破的，等填实的日月才成；有一爻入墓的，等冲开墓的日子才成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Shen-Zi-Chen combine into a Water frame, Si-You-Chou into Metal, Yin-Wu-Xu into Fire and Hai-Mao-Wei into Wood; a three-harmony frame can form in four ways, and includes ill ones and auspicious ones. For ancestral graves and the household, it suits the Resource line forming a frame; for marriage and husband and wife, it suits Wealth and Pressure being strong and forming a frame; for a long-lasting celebration, forming a frame suits and is firm forever; for a lawsuit or a matter of worry and doubt, a frame that forms leaves a knot in the heart for life. How to use a three-harmony frame depends on conditions: with a strong Subject none fails to be auspicious, and best is the Self line inside the frame; if the Self line is outside, the frame must generate the Self line before it is judged auspicious; if the frame controls the Self line, reckon it ill. If the Day or Month is inside the frame it is called a strong frame, better still. If only two of the three lines move it is not a formed frame, and one waits for a later month or day to supply the third, which is called 'leaving one empty, awaiting use'; one line moving openly and one moving in secret counts as two moving; if one line of the frame is Void or broken, it forms on the day or month that fills it; if one has entered a tomb, it forms on the day that clashes the tomb open.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 申子辰合成水局。 | 申、子、辰三支合成水局。 | Shen, Zi and Chen combine into a Water frame. |
> | 巳酉丑合成金局。 | 巳、酉、丑三支合成金局。 | Si, You and Chou combine into a Metal frame. |
> | 寅午戌合成火局。 | 寅、午、戌三支合成火局。 | Yin, Wu and Xu combine into a Fire frame. |
> | 亥卯未合成木局。 | 亥、卯、未三支合成木局。 | Hai, Mao and Wei combine into a Wood frame. |
> | 此三合者有四：一卦之内，有三爻动而合局者，一也。 | 三合有四种：一个卦里，有三爻都动而合成局的，是第一种。 | There are four kinds of three-harmony: within one hexagram, three lines moving and combining into a frame is the first. |
> | 两爻动，一爻不动，亦成合局者，二也。 | 两爻动、一爻不动，也能合成局的，是第二种。 | Two lines moving and one still, which also form a frame, is the second. |
> | 有内卦初爻、三爻动，动而变出之爻成三合者，三也。 | 内卦初爻、三爻动，动了变出来的爻成三合的，是第三种。 | Where the first and third lines of the inner trigram move and the lines they change into form a three-harmony, is the third. |
> | 有外卦四爻、六爻动，动而变出之爻成三合者，四也。 | 外卦四爻、六爻动，动了变出来的爻成三合的，是第四种。 | Where the fourth and sixth lines of the outer trigram move and the lines they change into form a three-harmony, is the fourth. |
> | 余按：此三合局者，有凶有吉。 | 我按：这三合局，有凶的也有吉的。 | My note: these three-harmony frames include ill ones and auspicious ones. |

> | 但三合其局者，用神旺，则无不为吉，尤要世爻在局为美。 | 不过三合成局的，用神旺，就没有不吉的，尤其要世爻在局里才好。 | But where a three-harmony frame forms and the Subject is strong, none fails to be auspicious, and it is best that the Self line be inside the frame. |
> | 若世爻不在局内，亦须局生世爻，始以吉断。 | 如果世爻不在局里，也必须局去生世爻，才断作吉。 | If the Self line is not inside the frame, the frame must still generate the Self line before it is judged auspicious. |
> | 倘局克世爻，则以凶推。 | 如果局克世爻，就按凶来推。 | If the frame controls the Self line, reckon it ill. |
> | 至于日建月建，但有一在局中，谓之局旺，更吉。 | 至于日建、月建，只要有一个在局里，叫做局旺，更吉。 | As for the Day and the Month, if either one is within the frame it is called a strong frame, and better still. |
> | 三爻若只两爻动，不为成局。 | 三个爻如果只有两爻动，不算成局。 | If of the three lines only two move, it does not count as a formed frame. |
> | 须待后来之月日补凑，合成其局，谓之虚一待用。 | 必须等以后的月、日把缺的补上，合成这个局，叫做「虚一待用」。 | One must wait for a later month or day to supply what is missing and complete the frame; this is called 'leaving one empty, awaiting use'. |
> | 一爻明动，一爻暗动，亦作两爻动。 | 一爻明动、一爻暗动，也算作两爻动。 | One line moving openly and one moving in secret also counts as two lines moving. |
> | 三合局中，有一值空破者，待填实之日月成之。 | 三合局中，有一个爻逢空、破的，等填实的日、月才成。 | If one of the lines within a three-harmony frame is Void or broken, the frame forms on the day or month that fills it. |
> | 有一爻入墓者，待冲开之日成之。 | 有一个爻入墓的，等冲开墓的日子才成。 | If one line has entered a tomb, it forms on the day that clashes the tomb open. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 同一段里还有占功名（官局）和占求财（财局、子孙局生财）的断语，那是别的问题域的内容，这里不收。「虚一待用」是说三爻里缺一个，先空着，等月日来补。
>
> *Note:* The same paragraph also has entries on fame (the Pressure frame) and on seeking wealth (a Wealth frame, an Output frame generating Wealth); those belong to other domains and are not included. 'Leaving one empty, awaiting use' means one of the three is missing and left open until a month or day supplies it.
