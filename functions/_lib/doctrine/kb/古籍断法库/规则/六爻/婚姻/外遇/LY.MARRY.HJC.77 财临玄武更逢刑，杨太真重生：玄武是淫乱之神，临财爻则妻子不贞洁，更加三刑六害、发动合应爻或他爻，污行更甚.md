# 财临玄武更逢刑，杨太真重生：玄武是淫乱之神，临财爻则妻子不贞洁，更加三刑六害、发动合应爻或他爻，污行更甚

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 玄武是淫乱的神。它临在财爻上，妻子就不贞洁；再加上三刑、六害，又发动去合应爻或者别的爻，就像杨贵妃，行为更污秽。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Dark Tortoise is the spirit of lewdness. Sitting on the Wealth line it means the wife is not chaste; with three punishments and six harms added, and moving to combine with the Other line or another line, she is like Yang Guifei, and worse.

## 《黄金策·身命》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6155 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Person and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财临玄武更逢刑，杨太真重生于今日。 | 财爻临着玄武，又碰上刑，杨太真（杨贵妃）在今天又生了一回。 | Wealth on the Dark Tortoise and meeting punishment as well: Yang Taizhen is born again today. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6156 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 玄武乃淫乱之神，若临财爻，必不贞洁。 | 玄武是淫乱的神，如果临在财爻上，一定不贞洁。 | The Dark Tortoise is the spirit of lewdness; if it sits on the Wealth line, she is surely not chaste. |
> | 更加三刑六害，如杨贵妃污秽尤甚。 | 再加三刑、六害，就像杨贵妃，污秽更甚。 | Add three punishments and six harms, and she is filthy like Yang Guifei, and worse. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1739 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 玄武乃淫乱之神，若临财爻，妻子不贞洁，发动与应爻相合，或与他爻相合，如杨贵妃污行尤甚。 | 玄武是淫乱的神，如果临在财爻上，妻子不贞洁，发动去和应爻相合，或者和别的爻相合，就像杨贵妃，行为污秽更甚。 | The Dark Tortoise is the spirit of lewdness; if it sits on the Wealth line, the wife is not chaste, and if it moves to combine with the Other line or with another line, she is like Yang Guifei, her conduct filthy in the extreme. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗把经文写作「财逢玄武更逢刑」。「杨太真」是杨贵妃入宫前的道号。
>
> *Note:* The Zhengzong writes the verse as 'Wealth meeting the Dark Tortoise and meeting punishment'. 'Yang Taizhen' is the Taoist name Yang Guifei took before entering the palace.
