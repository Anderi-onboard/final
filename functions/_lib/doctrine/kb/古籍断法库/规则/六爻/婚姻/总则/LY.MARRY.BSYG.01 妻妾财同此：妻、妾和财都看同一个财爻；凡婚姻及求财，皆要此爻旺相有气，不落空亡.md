# 妻妾财同此：妻、妾和财都看同一个财爻；凡婚姻及求财，皆要此爻旺相有气，不落空亡

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻、妾和财是同一类，都看财爻。占婚姻和求财，这一爻都要旺相有气，不落空亡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wife, concubine and wealth are the same kind, all read from the Wealth line. In divining a marriage or seeking wealth alike, this line must be strong and have qi, and must not fall Void.

## 《卜筮元龟·总例门》原文

> [!quote]+ [[大易断例卜筮元龟目]] 第 363 行 · Bushi Yuangui (The Great Tortoise of Divination), "The general rules", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻妾财同此。 | 妻、妾和财都是这一爻。 | Wife, concubine and wealth are all this one line. |

## 《大易断例卜筮元龟目》括号里的自注(没标「虎易注」的)

> [!quote]+ [[大易断例卜筮元龟目]] 第 363 行 · The Dayi Duanli's own bracketed notes (those not marked as the modern editor's)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 凡婚姻及求财， | 凡是婚姻和求财， | In marriage and in seeking wealth alike, |
> | 皆要此卦爻旺相有气， | 都要这一卦的这一爻旺相有气， | this line of the hexagram must be strong and have qi, |

> [!note] 按
> 前一句是「我克者为财」，这一句接着说：妻、妾都跟着财爻。
>
> *Note:* The line before it is 'what I control is Wealth', and this one goes on: wife and concubine both follow the Wealth line.
