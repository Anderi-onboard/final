# 何年：生合年月定迟速；用神长生帝旺之时、空填破实之期为应期；吉凶大事仍看财官

六爻 · 婚姻 · 应期 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问在哪一年：生合它的年月定快慢。小注说：用神长生、帝旺的时候，落空被填、破被补实的时候，就是日期；至于吉凶这样的大事，仍然看财爻和官爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask which year: the years and months that generate or combine with it set slow or fast. The note says: the times of the Subject's Birth and Peak, and of a Void being filled or a break being made solid, give the dates; as for the great question of luck or misfortune, still look at Wealth and Pressure.

> [!note] 按
> 小注这一句没有动词：「用神长生帝旺，空填破实之期」是在回答「何年」，译文按「这些时候就是应期」来读。
>
> *Note:* The note's sentence has no verb: 'the Subject's Birth and Peak, the times when a Void is filled or a break made solid' answers the question 'which year', and is read as saying that these are the times of fulfilment.
