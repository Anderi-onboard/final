# 婚姻是人道之始，占之不可不慎：先占门第、德行、性情容貌、子嗣，再占入门吉凶、聘奁厚薄，然后占成否；无媒不成，媒也要占

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 婚姻是做人的头一件大事，占它不能不慎重。求婚总是男方先开口，所以挑妻子的这一占尤其要慎重。占的次序：先占门第、德行、性情容貌、有没有子嗣，再占入门以后的吉凶、聘礼和嫁妆的厚薄，然后才占成不成；没有媒人就得不到，所以媒人（月老）也要占。鬼谷的分爻法把爻对到：祖宗、父母、外氏、婿、妇、媒妁、自身。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Marriage is the first business of human life, and its divination must not be undertaken carelessly. It is always the man's side that seeks first, so the divination for choosing a wife calls for still more care. The order of divining: first her family standing, virtue, nature and looks, and whether there will be children; then the good or ill of her entering the house and the richness of the betrothal gifts and dowry; only then whether the marriage will be made. Without a go-between it cannot be had, so the go-between (the Old Man under the Moon) must be divined too. Guiguzi's division of the lines assigns them to: ancestors, parents, the mother's family, the son-in-law, the wife, the go-between and oneself.

> [!note] 按
> 「鬼谷分爻」下面只列了七个名目（祖宗、父母、外氏、婿、妇、媒妁、自身），没有说各对哪一爻，七个名目也多于六个爻；译文照录名单，没有替它排位。
>
> *Note:* Under 'Guiguzi's division of the lines' the text lists only seven names, without saying which line each belongs to, and seven names outnumber the six lines; the list is translated as it stands without assigning positions.
