# 以间分其媒妁：近世的间爻是我方传信的媒、近应的是对方传信的妁，空散不得力、破绝不为情，发动则有变更；间爻作用神宜生助不宜空破

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用间爻分辨媒人。靠近世爻的间爻，是替我这边传话的媒；靠近应爻的间爻，是替对方传话的妁。间爻落空、散，就不得力；破、绝，就不肯出情；帮助还是欺克，两边都是这样；只有发动，才会出现或易或难的变更。间爻作用神，要有生助，不宜落空、破：有生助就有力，空、破就无功，靠近哪一边，就断它偏向哪一边。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Use the between lines to tell the go-betweens apart. The between line near Self is the go-between who carries my side's word; the one near Other is the matchmaker who carries the other side's. Void or scattered, it has no strength; broken or cut off, it does not act from feeling; whether it supports or cheats, it is so for both sides; only when it moves does a change come, easy or hard. When a between line serves as the Subject it should be supported and not Void or broken: supported, it is strong; Void or broken, it achieves nothing; judge by which side it is near.

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 218 行 · Bracketed small note in the Yi Mao (author not named in the book)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 生助有力， | 有生助就有力， | Supported, it is strong; |
> | 空破无功， | 落空、破就无功， | Void or broken, it achieves nothing; |
> | 近彼近我， | 靠近对方还是靠近我， | whether it is near them or near me, |
> | 占其所向。 | 就断它偏向哪一边。 | judge by which side it leans to. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「生助克欺」是对间爻说的：它是在帮哪一边、还是在欺哪一边。「近彼近我」指间爻靠近应爻还是靠近世爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Support, control or cheating' is said of the between line: which side it is helping and which it is cheating. 'Near them or near me' means whether the between line is next to the Other line or the Self line.
