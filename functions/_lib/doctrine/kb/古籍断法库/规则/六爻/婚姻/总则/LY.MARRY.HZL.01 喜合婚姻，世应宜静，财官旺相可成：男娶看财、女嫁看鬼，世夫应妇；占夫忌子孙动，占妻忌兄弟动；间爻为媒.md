# 喜合婚姻，世应宜静，财官旺相可成：男娶看财、女嫁看鬼，世夫应妇；占夫忌子孙动，占妻忌兄弟动；间爻为媒

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 婚姻喜欢卦相合，世爻、应爻宜静，财爻、官爻旺相，婚姻就可以成。世应有动就不成。男家娶妻看财爻（代人占也一样），女家嫁丈夫用官鬼爻；用神忌讳动，出现时怕被冲；旺相可成，世应互相克则不长久。世爻代表丈夫，应爻代表妻子。还要看是谁占：占丈夫忌子孙爻发动，子孙持世就不成；占妻子忌兄弟爻发动，兄弟持世就不成。间爻是媒人，父母爻是三堂，子孙爻是子嗣，都宜静。卦里没有子孙爻，不欢喜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A marriage likes a hexagram that combines; the Self and Other lines should be still, and with Wealth and Pressure strong the marriage can be made. If the Self or Other line moves, it will not succeed. When the man's family takes a wife look at the Wealth line (the same for a divination on someone's behalf); when the woman's family marries her to a husband use the Pressure line. The Subject fears moving, and when it appears it fears being clashed; if strong it can be made, but if the Self and Other control each other it will not last. The Self line is the husband and the Other line the wife. Look also at who is divining: for a husband, fear a moving Output line, and an Output line on the Self means it will not succeed; for a wife, fear a moving Peer line, and a Peer line on the Self means it will not succeed. The between lines are the go-between, the Resource line is the 'three halls', the Output line is the heir; all should be still. A hexagram without an Output line is not a happy one.
