# 咸池杀：占婚大忌，主妇人淫乱；寅午戌在卯、巳酉丑在午、申子辰在酉、亥卯未在子

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 咸池杀：占婚姻是大忌，主女子淫乱。歌诀按三合局排：寅午戌的兔从卯位出来，巳酉丑的马向南方跑，申子辰的鸡叫乱了人伦，亥卯未的鼠子当头要忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Xianchi star: in a marriage divination it is a great taboo and means the woman is licentious. The rhyme goes by the three-harmony frames: for Yin, Wu and Xu the rabbit comes out of Mao; for Si, You and Chou the horse runs south; for Shen, Zi and Chen the rooster crows and disorders human relations; for Hai, Mao and Wei the rat is what must be avoided head-on.

## 《卜筮全书·神煞歌例》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 10115 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Rhymes on the spirit stars", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 寅午戌兔从卯里出， | 寅、午、戌的兔子，从卯位出来， | For Yin, Wu and Xu, the rabbit comes out of Mao; |
> | 巳酉丑跃马南方走， | 巳、酉、丑的马，向南方跑， | for Si, You and Chou, the horse leaps and runs to the south; |
> | 申子辰鸡叫乱人伦， | 申、子、辰的鸡叫，乱了人伦， | for Shen, Zi and Chen, the rooster crows and disorders human relations; |
> | 亥卯未鼠子当头忌。 | 亥、卯、未的鼠子，当头是要忌的。 | for Hai, Mao and Wei, the rat is what is to be avoided head-on. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 歌诀里的兔、马、鸡、鼠是生肖，依次对着卯、午、酉、子，就是咸池所在的位置；书里没有把地支写出来，这里是照生肖对应的。
>
> *Note:* The rabbit, horse, rooster and rat in the rhyme are zodiac animals standing for Mao, Wu, You and Zi in turn, the places of the Xianchi star; the book does not spell out the branches, and they are given by the zodiac.
