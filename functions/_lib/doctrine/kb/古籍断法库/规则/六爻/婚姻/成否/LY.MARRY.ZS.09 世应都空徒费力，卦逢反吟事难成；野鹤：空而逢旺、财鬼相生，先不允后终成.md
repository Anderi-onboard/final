# 世应都空徒费力，卦逢反吟事难成；野鹤：空而逢旺、财鬼相生，先不允后终成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻、应爻都空，是白费力气；卦变成反吟，翻来覆去难成。野鹤补充：如果是空而逢旺，财爻和官鬼互相生，起先虽然不答应，后来终究还是成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self and Other lines both Void, effort is wasted; a hexagram that turns into a Reversed Chant flips back and forth and is hard to accomplish. Yehe adds: if the Void meets strength and Wealth and Pressure generate each other, although at first it is refused, in the end it still succeeds.
