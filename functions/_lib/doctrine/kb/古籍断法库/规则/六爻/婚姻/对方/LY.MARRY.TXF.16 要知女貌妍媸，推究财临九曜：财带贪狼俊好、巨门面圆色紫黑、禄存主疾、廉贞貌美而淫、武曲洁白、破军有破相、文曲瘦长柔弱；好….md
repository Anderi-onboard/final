# 要知女貌妍媸，推究财临九曜：财带贪狼俊好、巨门面圆色紫黑、禄存主疾、廉贞貌美而淫、武曲洁白、破军有破相、文曲瘦长柔弱；好者忌冲，恶者怕扶

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道女子相貌的美丑，推究财爻带的九曜星。财带贪狼（子）：俊好；带巨门（丑、亥）：脸圆，颜色紫黑；禄存（寅、戌）：主有疾；廉贞（辰、申）：主淫，貌美；武曲（巳、未）：洁白，相貌中等；破军（午）：必有破相；文曲（卯、酉）：形貌瘦长，为人柔弱；左辅（亥）、右弼（巳）同巳位。好的怕冲，坏的怕扶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know whether the woman is fair or foul, examine the nine luminaries that Wealth carries. Wealth with Tanlang (Zi): handsome; with Jumen (Chou, Hai): a round face, a purplish-black complexion; Lucun (Yin, Xu): illness; Lianzhen (Chen, Shen): licentious, beautiful; Wuqu (Si, Wei): fair, of middling looks; Pojun (Wu): surely a flaw in appearance; Wenqu (Mao, You): a thin, tall figure and a soft, weak character; Zuofu (Hai) and Youbi (Si): as for the Si entry. The good ones fear a clash, the bad ones fear support.

> [!note] 按
> 地支括号是每个星对应的地支。这里的「文曲」在《易冒》的九曜名单里写作「文昌」，是同一个位置的两种叫法。「同巳上」按字面译为和巳位相同。
>
> *Note:* The branch names in brackets are the branches each star goes with. 'Wenqu' here is written 'Wenchang' in the Yi Mao's list of the nine luminaries, two names for the same place in the list. 'Same as Si above' is translated literally as the same as the Si entry.
