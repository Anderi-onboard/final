# 筮室家用妻财、仆妾同焉；丈夫用官，良朋用应，不可错审

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 身命章里列各种人用什么爻：占家室（妻子）用妻财爻，仆人、妾也是一样；丈夫用官鬼爻，好朋友用应爻，不可以看错了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on life and fate lists which line stands for whom: divining the household (the wife) uses the Wealth line, and servants and concubines are the same; a husband uses the Pressure line, a good friend the Other line, and one must not judge it wrongly.

> | 而不可错审也。 | 不可以看错了。 | and one must not judge it wrongly. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 中间隔着的一句「筮手足而用于弟兄，宗娣同焉」讲兄弟姐妹，与婚姻无关，没有收。这里的「丈夫用官」和婚姻章开头的规矩（LY.MARRY.YM.01）是一回事，只是在这里多了一句「仆妾同焉」：仆人和妾也看妻财爻。
>
> *Note:* The sentence in between, 'divining brothers and sisters uses the Peer line, clan kin the same', concerns siblings and not marriage, and is not taken. 'A husband uses Pressure' here is the same rule as at the head of the marriage chapter (LY.MARRY.YM.01); what this adds is 'servants and concubines the same': servants and concubines are also read from the Wealth line.
