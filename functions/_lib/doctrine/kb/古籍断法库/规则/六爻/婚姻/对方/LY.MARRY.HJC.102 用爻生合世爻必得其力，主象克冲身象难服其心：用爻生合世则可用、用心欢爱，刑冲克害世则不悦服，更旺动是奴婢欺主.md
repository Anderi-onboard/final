# 用爻生合世爻必得其力，主象克冲身象难服其心：用爻生合世则可用、用心欢爱，刑冲克害世则不悦服，更旺动是奴婢欺主

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻生合世爻，这个人可以用，办事一定用心，即使是过继的小孩也一定对我欢喜爱戴，是大吉的兆头；最怕被合住、冲散、克坏。如果用爻刑冲克害世爻，这个人心里不服，难以使唤；再加旺相发动，就是奴婢欺主的象；再加应爻也刑克，日后必遭其祸。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Subject line generates or combines with the Self line, the person can be used and will surely put his heart into the work; even an adopted child will be fond of me: a greatly lucky sign; what is most feared is being held by a combination, clashed apart or controlled and spoiled. If the Subject line punishes, clashes, controls or harms the Self line, the person is not content at heart and hard to command; if it is also strong and moving, that is the image of a servant cheating the master; if the Other line also punishes and controls, one will surely suffer from him in future.
