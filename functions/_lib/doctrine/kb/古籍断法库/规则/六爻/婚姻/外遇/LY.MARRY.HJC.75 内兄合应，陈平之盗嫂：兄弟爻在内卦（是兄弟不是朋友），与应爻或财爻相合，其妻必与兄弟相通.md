# 内兄合应，陈平之盗嫂：兄弟爻在内卦（是兄弟不是朋友），与应爻或财爻相合，其妻必与兄弟相通

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻在内卦，指的是兄弟，不是朋友。它和应爻或者财爻相合，他的妻子一定和兄弟有私情，就像陈平和嫂子的旧事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Peer line in the inner trigram means brothers, not friends. If it combines with the Other line or the Wealth line, his wife surely has an affair with his brothers, like the old story of Chen Ping and his sister-in-law.

## 《黄金策·身命》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6148 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Person and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 内兄合应，陈伯常有孺子之兄。 | 内卦的兄弟爻合应爻，就像陈伯常（陈平）有个「孺子」之兄的旧事。 | A Peer line in the inner trigram combining with the Other line: like the old story of Chen Bochang (Chen Ping) and his brother, the 'young man'. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 陈平年轻时被人说成「盗嫂」（和嫂子有私情），是史书里的旧案。经文「陈伯常有孺子之兄」的语意不清，各书都不解释，译文只照字面写，注文把意思说明白了：妻子和兄弟有私情。
>
> *Note:* As a young man Chen Ping was accused of 'stealing his sister-in-law', an old case in the histories. The verse 'Chen Bochang had a brother, the young man' is unclear and no book explains it; it is translated only as written, and the commentary makes the meaning plain: the wife has an affair with the brothers.
