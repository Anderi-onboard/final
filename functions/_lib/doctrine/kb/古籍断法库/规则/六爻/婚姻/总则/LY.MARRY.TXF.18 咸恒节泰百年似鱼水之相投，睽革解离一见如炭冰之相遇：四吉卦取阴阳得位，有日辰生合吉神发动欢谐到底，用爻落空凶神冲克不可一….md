# 咸恒节泰百年似鱼水之相投，睽革解离一见如炭冰之相遇：四吉卦取阴阳得位，有日辰生合吉神发动欢谐到底，用爻落空凶神冲克不可一概断吉；睽乖革变解散离六冲，占婚皆不利

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 咸、恒、节、泰四卦，取它们阴阳得位，如果又有日辰生合、吉神发动，就一直欢喜和谐，像鱼得水；但用爻落空、有凶神冲克时，不能一概断吉。睽、革、解、离一见如炭遇冰：睽是乖，火上泽下、金火同居，必有伤克；革是变，占婚遇到，有争婚改嫁；解是散，占婚宜合不宜分；离是六冲卦，纯阴之象，不吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Xian, Heng, Jie and Tai are taken for their yin and yang being in place, and if the Day also generates or combines and lucky spirits move, they are harmonious to the end, like fish in water; but if the Subject line falls Void or is clashed and controlled by fierce spirits, one cannot judge them all as lucky. Kui, Ge, Xie and Li at first sight are like charcoal meeting ice: Kui is at odds, with fire above and marsh below and Metal and Fire dwelling together, so there is injury and control; Ge is change, and meeting it in a marriage divination there will be a contested marriage or remarriage; Xie is dispersal, and in a marriage divination it should be together and not apart; Li is a six-clash hexagram, an image of pure yin, and unlucky.

> [!warning] 打架
> 按卦名断婚，《增删卜易》的觉子不同意：他自己占到泰卦却是兄弟持世、财爻月破旬空，让人再占得六冲的坤卦，结论是仍以财、官、世、应断，不要执古说（LY.MARRY.ZS.14）。《易冒》站在这一边（LY.MARRY.YM.03）；《易隐》《火珠林》的口诀也是按卦名（LY.MARRY.YY.09、LY.MARRY.HZL.11）。这一条的注文自己也留了余地：四吉卦要有日辰生合才「鱼水」，用爻落空、凶神冲克就「不可一概断为吉」。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Juezi of the Zengshan Buyi does not accept judging a marriage by hexagram names: he himself got Tai with a Peer line on Self and the Wealth line Month-broken and Void, had a second divination cast and got the six-clash hexagram Kun, and concluded that one should keep judging by Wealth, Pressure, Self and Other and not cling to the old sayings (LY.MARRY.ZS.14). The Yi Mao stands on this side of the Tianxuan Fu (LY.MARRY.YM.03), and the rhymes of the Yi Yin and the Huozhulin also go by hexagram names (LY.MARRY.YY.09, LY.MARRY.HZL.11). The note here leaves itself some room: the four lucky hexagrams are 'fish and water' only with the Day generating or combining, and if the Subject line falls Void or fierce spirits clash and control it, 'one cannot judge them all as lucky'.
