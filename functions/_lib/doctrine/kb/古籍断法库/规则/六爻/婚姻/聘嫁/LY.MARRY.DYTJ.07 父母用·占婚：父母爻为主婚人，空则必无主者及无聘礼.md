# 父母用·占婚：父母爻为主婚人，空则必无主者及无聘礼

六爻 · 婚姻 · 聘嫁 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻是生我的，能吉能凶，各有各的用处：遇财就伤自身，遇鬼反而增光；发动就克伤子孙、生扶兄弟。占婚姻，父母爻是主婚人：空亡了，就一定没有人主婚，也没有聘礼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Resource line is what generates me and can bring good or ill, each with its own use: meeting Wealth it harms its own body, meeting Pressure it grows in radiance; moving, it controls and harms Output and supports Peer. In a marriage divination the Resource line is the presider at the wedding; when it is Void there is surely no presider and no betrothal gifts.

> [!note] 按
> 「为主婚人空」原书没有标点，这里读作「为主婚人，空则……」。
>
> *Note:* The book has no punctuation in 'is the presider, Void', and it is read here as 'the presider; when Void, ...'.
