# 阴阳交错，难期琴瑟之和鸣：世阴应阳、鬼阴财阳、内阴外阳，成婚后夫妻反目

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 阴阳交错是指世阴应阳、鬼阴财阳、内阴外阳，这是反象；成婚后，丈夫欺凌妻子、妻子欺侮丈夫，整天吵架，不得和顺；如果变出的财、鬼不空，主象又安静，也还可用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Yin and yang crossed means the Self yin and the Other yang, Pressure yin and Wealth yang, the inner trigram yin and the outer yang; this is an inverted image, and after the marriage the husband bullies the wife and the wife slights the husband, quarrelling all day and never in harmony; if the changed Wealth and Pressure are not Void and the principal image is quiet, it can still be used.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6309 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 阴阳交错，难期琴瑟之和鸣； | 阴阳交错，难以指望夫妻琴瑟和鸣； | With yin and yang crossed, one can hardly hope for lute and zither to sound in concert; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6310 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)

> | 若变出财鬼不空，主象安静，亦可用也。 | 如果变出的财、鬼不空，主象又安静，也可以用。 | If the changed Wealth and Pressure are not Void and the principal image is quiet, it can still be used. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1988 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如男卜女，遇世阴应阳，世阴财阳者，是阴阳交错，后主夫妻欺凌，终朝反目。 | 比如男子占女子，遇到世阴应阳、世阴财阳的，就是阴阳交错，以后夫妻互相欺凌，整天吵架。 | For instance, when a man divines about a woman and gets the Self yin with the Other yang, or the Self yin with Wealth yang, yin and yang are crossed, and later the couple bully each other and quarrel all day. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「主象」书里没有解释，这里译作「主象」（最主要的那一个象）。
>
> *Note:* The book does not gloss 'the principal image'; it is translated as the main image of the hexagram.
