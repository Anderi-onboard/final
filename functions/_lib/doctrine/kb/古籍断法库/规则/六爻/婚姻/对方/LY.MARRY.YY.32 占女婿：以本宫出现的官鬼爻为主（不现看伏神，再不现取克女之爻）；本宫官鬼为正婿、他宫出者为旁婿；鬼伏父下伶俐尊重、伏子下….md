# 占女婿：以本宫出现的官鬼爻为主（不现看伏神，再不现取克女之爻）；本宫官鬼为正婿、他宫出者为旁婿；鬼伏父下伶俐尊重、伏子下性善、伏兄下贪淫好赌、伏财下能掌财帛；鬼化鬼男家未定、兄化鬼斗狠贪淫、财化鬼克妻损财、子化鬼夫妇和谐、鬼化子与妻相益、鬼化兄伤妻嫖赌、鬼化财能设施

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占女婿：以本宫里出现的官鬼爻为主，不论内卦外卦（因为女婿是半个儿子，有家里人的意思）；不出现，就看本宫的伏神；不现又无伏，就取克女儿的那个爻当女婿。本宫的官鬼是正婿，年月日时上别的宫出来的是旁婿；正宫有两个鬼爻，阳爻得位的是正婿，阴爻失位的是旁婿。正婿的鬼爻在巳、午，火命的吉，女儿命属土的吉（火能生土）。鬼爻伏在父母爻下，伶俐庄重；伏在子孙爻下，性善不损物，能容纳；伏在兄弟爻下，贪淫好赌，不诚实；伏在财爻下，能管钱财，做事分明，夫妻好合（财鬼带辰午酉亥自刑的，主先私通后娶）。鬼化鬼，男家没定准，或停妻再娶；兄化鬼，好斗凶狠贪淫（鬼带刑刃也是）；财化鬼，克妻损财；子化鬼，带青龙、喜神、德神、合，夫妇和谐，带华盖、刑、刃，是僧道还俗；鬼化子，与妻互相有益；鬼化兄，伤妻、嫖赌；鬼化财，能有设施。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining a son-in-law: take the Pressure line that appears in the home palace as the main one, whether in the inner or outer trigram (since a son-in-law is a half-son, with the sense of a member of the household); if it does not appear, look at the home palace's hidden spirit; if it neither appears nor is hidden, take the line that controls the daughter as the son-in-law. The home palace's Pressure is the principal son-in-law, and one that comes from another palace through the year, month, day or hour is a collateral one; if the home palace has two Pressure lines, the yang line in its place is the principal son-in-law and the yin line out of place the collateral one. If the principal Pressure line is on Si or Wu, a man of the Fire fate is auspicious and a daughter whose fate is Earth is auspicious (Fire can generate Earth). Pressure hidden beneath the Resource line: clever and dignified; beneath Output: good-natured, does not waste goods, tolerant; beneath a Peer line: lustful, gambling and dishonest; beneath Wealth: able to manage money, clear in affairs, the couple well matched (if Wealth and Pressure carry the self-punishing branches Chen, Wu, You or Hai: illicit relations first, marriage after). Pressure changing into Pressure: the man's family is unsettled, or he sets aside his wife and marries again; a Peer line changing into Pressure: quarrelsome, fierce and lustful (Pressure carrying punishment and blade is the same); Wealth changing into Pressure: harms the wife and loses money; Output changing into Pressure, with the Azure Dragon, Joy, Virtue and combination: the couple is harmonious, with the Canopy star, punishment and blade: a monk or priest returned to lay life; Pressure changing into Output: mutually beneficial with his wife; into a Peer line: harms his wife, whores and gambles; into Wealth: able to make arrangements.
