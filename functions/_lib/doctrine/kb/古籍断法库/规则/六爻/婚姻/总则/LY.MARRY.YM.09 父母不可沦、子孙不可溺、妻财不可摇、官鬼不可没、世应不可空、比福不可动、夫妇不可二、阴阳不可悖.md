# 父母不可沦、子孙不可溺、妻财不可摇、官鬼不可没、世应不可空、比福不可动、夫妇不可二、阴阳不可悖

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 婚姻卦里有八件事不可出岔子：父母爻不可沉沦，子孙爻不可陷溺，妻财不可动摇，官鬼不可埋没，世应不可落空，兄弟和子孙不可发动，夫妇不可有二，阴阳不可相背。小注说明：父母爻是主婚人，男方占女方而女方没有父母，就由叔伯主婚；女方占男方而男方没有父母，礼仪就淡薄。子孙陷落就子嗣艰难，官鬼落空就难成，妻财发动就公婆意见不一，男占女占都一样。世爻落空是我有退缩的心，应爻落空是对方没有依从的意愿，破、散、动、冲也都主变化。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a marriage divination eight things must not go wrong: the Resource line must not sink, the Output line must not be drowned, Wealth must not be shaken, Pressure must not be lost, Self and Other must not be Void, Peer and Output must not move, the husband and wife must not be two, and yin and yang must not be at odds. The note explains: the Resource line is the presider at the wedding; if a man divines a woman who has no parents, her uncles preside; if a woman divines a man who has no parents, the ceremony is thin. If Output is sunk, heirs are hard to come by; if Pressure is Void, the match is hard to make; if Wealth moves, the parents-in-law disagree; man or woman divining, it is the same. Self Void means I have thoughts of withdrawing, Other Void means the other side has no will to comply, and broken, scattered, moving or clashed also mean change.

> [!warning] 打架
> 李我平不认这八条（LY.MARRY.ZS.22）：要事事求全——命不入墓、六亲不相妨、父母不沉、子孙不溺、财不动、官不没、夫妇不二、阴阳不悖——世上没有这样的女子，六十四卦里也没有这样的卦。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Li Woping does not accept these eight (LY.MARRY.ZS.22): to demand everything at once, with the Life not in the tomb, the six relations not harming one another, Resource not sunk, Output not drowned, Wealth not moving, Pressure not lost, the couple not two and yin and yang not at odds, no such woman exists in the world and no such hexagram among the sixty-four.

> [!note] 按
> 「比福」是兄弟（比肩）和子孙（福德）。「公姑」是公公和婆婆。这一节的后半（二官二财、阴阳相背）放在 LY.MARRY.YM.10。
>
> *Note:* 'Peer and Output' renders the book's abbreviation for the Peer line and the Output (fortune) line. 'Parents-in-law' renders the husband's father and mother. The second half of this passage, on two Pressure or two Wealth lines and on yin and yang at odds, is in LY.MARRY.YM.10.
