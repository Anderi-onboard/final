# 世空本体多柔弱，应空室命少儿郎：世为自己空则精阳不足须医药，应为妻室空则妻命无儿理宜娶妾；世应皆不空而子独陷是天命无儿

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻空，本人多柔弱；应爻空，妻子命里少儿子。世爻是自己，落了空亡就精阳不足，还要靠医药调理；应爻多半代表妻室，临空就推断妻命无儿，理应娶妾。世应都不空，只有子孙爻陷落，是天命无儿，不是人力能定的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self line Void the man himself is often frail; with the Other line Void the wife's fate has few sons. The Self line is oneself: if it falls into Void, vital yang is insufficient and medicine is needed to restore balance; the Other line mostly stands for the wife: if it is on Void ground, infer that her fate has no son and that a concubine should be taken. If neither the Self nor the Other line is Void and only the Output line has fallen, this is fate without a son and not something human effort settles.
