# 鳏寡杀：占姻事忌之；春牛不下田、夏龙飞上天、秋羊草枯死、冬犬厌残年

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鳏寡杀：占婚姻的事忌它。歌诀：春天的牛不下田，夏天的龙飞上天，秋天的羊草枯死，冬天的狗厌恶残年。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The star of widowers and widows: in marriage matters it is to be avoided. The rhyme: in spring the ox does not go down to the field, in summer the dragon flies up to heaven, in autumn the goat's grass withers and dies, in winter the dog is weary of the dying year.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 四句里的牛、龙、羊、犬是生肖，依次对着丑、辰、未、戌，分别是春、夏、秋、冬的鳏寡杀所在；书里没有写出地支。同一首歌诀在《断易天机》里也有，那里注明「婚姻遇发动不久」（LY.MARRY.DYTJ.27）。
>
> *Note:* The ox, dragon, goat and dog in the four lines are zodiac animals standing for Chou, Chen, Wei and Xu, the places of this star in spring, summer, autumn and winter; the book does not write out the branches. The same rhyme is in the Duanyi Tianji, where it is glossed 'in marriage, if it moves it does not last' (LY.MARRY.DYTJ.27).
