# 间爻旺相须知月老英豪，兄位交重定是冰人诡谲，与世应相生相合必然沾亲，如雀虎相克相冲拟定两家相怨；欲知退步须察空亡

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应中间的爻像媒人：旺相，是豪侠有力的媒人；兄弟发动，媒人从中诈伪；间爻和世应生合，是亲朋结契；朱雀、白虎临世应冲克，联了姻也终究两边怨恶。以上这些如果遇到空亡，就要退一步，不能照上面断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The lines between Self and Other are like the go-between: strong, the matchmaker is heroic, generous and effective; if Peer moves, the matchmaker is deceitful in the middle; if a between line generates or combines with Self and Other, relatives and friends form a bond; if the Vermilion Bird and the White Tiger sit on Self and Other and clash or control, though the families are joined they end in resentment on both sides. If any of this meets Void, step back and do not judge as above.
