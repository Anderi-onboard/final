# 财官叠见，重为一度之新人：男占女卦有两财、女占男卦有两鬼，是再娶再嫁

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男占女，卦里有两个财；女占男，卦里有两个鬼；一定是再娶再嫁。两个财都没有损伤，一正一偏；一个旺一个空，前妻已死；两鬼发动，有两家争娶；一旺一空，前夫已死；鬼伏财下，男方家里有妻；财伏鬼下，女方有夫；鬼不空而被动爻日辰冲克妻财，是生离改嫁；世克鬼而动爻日辰生合妻财，是逐婿嫁女。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A man divining about a woman with two Wealth lines in the hexagram, or a woman divining about a man with two Pressure lines, means a remarriage; two Wealth lines both unharmed are one principal and one secondary; one thriving and one Void means the former wife has died; two Pressure lines moving means two families contending to marry her; one thriving and one Void, the former husband has died; Pressure hidden under Wealth means the man has a wife at home; Wealth hidden under Pressure means the woman has a husband; Pressure not Void while a moving line or the Day clashes with or controls Wealth means a living separation and remarriage; the Self controlling Pressure while a moving line or the Day generates or combines with Wealth means driving out the son-in-law and marrying off the daughter.

> [!note] 按
> 这一条的前提是「男占女看财、女占男看鬼」；另一方向的伏（鬼伏财下、财伏鬼下），旧注和王洪绪的说法正好相反：旧注是鬼伏财下男有妻、财伏鬼下女有夫，王洪绪是财伏鬼下男有妻、鬼伏财下女有夫。
>
> *Note:* The premise is 'a man divining about a woman looks to Wealth, a woman divining about a man looks to Pressure'. On the direction of hiding, the old commentary and Wang Hongxu are opposite: the old commentary says Pressure hidden under Wealth means the man has a wife and Wealth hidden under Pressure means the woman has a husband, while Wang Hongxu says Wealth hidden under Pressure means the man has a wife and Pressure hidden under Wealth means the woman has a husband.
