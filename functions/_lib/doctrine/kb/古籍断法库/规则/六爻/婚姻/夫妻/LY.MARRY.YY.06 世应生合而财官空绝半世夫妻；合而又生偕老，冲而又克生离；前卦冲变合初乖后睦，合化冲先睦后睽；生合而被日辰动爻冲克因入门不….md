# 世应生合而财官空绝半世夫妻；合而又生偕老，冲而又克生离；前卦冲变合初乖后睦，合化冲先睦后睽；生合而被日辰动爻冲克因入门不睦；冲克而日辰动爻类合局得人劝复和；阳鬼阴财一卦旺静亲上成亲

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应相生相合，但财爻、官爻空、绝，只做半辈子夫妻。合中又有生的，白头偕老；冲中又有克的，生离。本卦冲而变卦合，起初不合、后来和睦；本卦合而变冲，先和睦、后乖离。世应财鬼相生相合，但被日辰、动爻冲克，因入了门反而不和睦；世应财鬼冲克，而日辰和动爻合成局面的，得人劝解又和好。阳的鬼爻、阴的财爻，其中一个卦旺而静的，一定是亲上加亲，举案齐眉、白头偕老。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self and Other lines generating and combining, but Wealth and Pressure Void or extinguished: husband and wife for half a lifetime. A combination with a generation as well: they grow old together; a clash with a control as well: parting alive. A hexagram of clash changing into combination: at odds at first, in harmony later; a hexagram of combination changing into a clash: harmony first, estrangement later. The Self, Other, Wealth and Pressure lines generating and combining but clashed and controlled by the Day and moving lines: discord arises because of her entering the house; the same lines clashing and controlling, but the Day and moving lines forming something like a combination frame: with someone's mediation they are reconciled. A yang Pressure line and a yin Wealth line, with one trigram strong and still: surely a match within the family, with mutual respect to the end of their days.

> [!note] 按
