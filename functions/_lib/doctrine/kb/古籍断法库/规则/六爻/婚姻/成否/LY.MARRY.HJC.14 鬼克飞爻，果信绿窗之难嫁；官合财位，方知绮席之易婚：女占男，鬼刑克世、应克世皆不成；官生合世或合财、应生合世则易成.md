# 鬼克飞爻，果信绿窗之难嫁；官合财位，方知绮席之易婚：女占男，鬼刑克世、应克世皆不成；官生合世或合财、应生合世则易成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 女方占男方，最不宜鬼爻刑克世爻，也不宜应爻克世，都主不成，男家不愿结婚；如果他有制约、我有扶助、大象又吉，也可成，但他家终究无意；若得官爻生合世爻，或合财爻，或应爻生合世爻，才主容易成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the woman's side divines about the man, the most unfavourable thing is a Pressure line punishing or controlling the Self line, and also the Other line controlling the Self; both mean it will not succeed and the man's family is unwilling; if he is restrained, I am supported and the overall image is auspicious, it can still succeed, though his family in the end has no intention; if Pressure generates or combines with the Self line, or combines with the Wealth line, or the Other generates or combines with the Self, then it is easy to bring about.

> | 如用神用合世位，不但易成，后必恩爱。 | 如果用神生合世位，不但容易成，以后一定恩爱。 | If the Subject generates or combines with the Self place, it is not only easy to bring about but later there will surely be affection. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 王洪绪引的经文与《卜筮全书》不同：他写作「鬼克世爻」「用合身位」，全书作「鬼克飞爻」「官合财位」；王洪绪的注按用神（谁占看谁）讲，旧注按女占男讲。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Wang Hongxu's text of the verse differs from the Bushi Quanshu's: he writes 'Pressure controlling the Self line' and 'the Subject combining with the Body place', where the Quanshu has 'the flying line' and 'the Wealth place'; his note speaks by the Subject (whoever is the object of the question), and the old commentary by a woman divining about a man.
