# 世临阴位女为政，财爻持世赘为姻；二爻冲克破，当家夫妻少恩情（王洪绪注：财爻持世居五爻阴位是入赘成婚，二爻冲克是夫妇各主张，不是赘婚）

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卜筮正宗论家宅六爻的位置时，有两联和夫妻有关。世爻落在阴位（比如五爻是阴爻），家里是妇人当家；但如果是财爻持世在这个位置，是那个男人入赘去成婚，就不是女人主事。二爻如果被冲、被克、被破，当家的夫妻之间少恩爱，王洪绪注：那是夫妇各有各的主张，不是赘婚的说法。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In its treatment of the positions of the six lines in a household reading, the Bushi Zhengzong has two couplets touching husband and wife. Self on a yin position (for example the fifth line being a yin line): the woman rules the household; but if it is the Wealth line that holds Self in that place, the man has gone in to marry as a son-in-law, and a woman is not in charge. If the second line is clashed, controlled or broken, the couple who run the household have little affection; Wang Hongxu's note says that then husband and wife each hold their own views, and it is not the reading of a marriage taken in.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3245 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 二爻如逢冲克，主夫妇各主张，又非赘婚之论也。 | 二爻如果遇上冲克，主夫妻各有各的主张，那就不是入赘婚的说法了。 | If the second line meets clash or control, husband and wife each hold their own views, and it is then not a judgment of marrying in. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 这两联是家宅里的六爻分位诗（五爻是一家之主、二爻是宅和主妇）中的两句；这一组的其余各联讲屋宇、田、畜、坟、水等，与婚姻无关，没有收。注里的「主其家内降为政」的「降」字在这里说不通，大概是「妇」的误字，译文按「妇人当家」译。
>
> *Note:* These are two lines of the rhyme on the positions of the six lines in a household reading (the fifth line as the head of the house, the second as the dwelling and the mistress); the other couplets in the group, on buildings, land, livestock, graves and water, have nothing to do with marriage and are not taken. The word 'jiang' in the note's 'zhu qi jia nei jiang wei zheng' does not read here and is probably a slip for 'fu' (woman); it is translated as the woman ruling the household.
