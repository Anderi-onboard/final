# 神煞表·天月德、青龙：喜庆婚姻，求财合吉；公私吉庆，婚姻财吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 神煞表按月排：天月德按月依次是丁、坤、壬、辛、乾、甲、癸、艮、丙、乙、巽、庚，主喜庆婚姻，求财也合吉；青龙按月依次是寅、卯、辰、巳、午、未、申、酉、戌、亥、子、丑，主公私吉庆，婚姻和财都吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The table of spirit stars is laid out by month. The Heavenly-Month Virtue runs month by month as Ding, Kun, Ren, Xin, Qian, Jia, Gui, Gen, Bing, Yi, Xun, Geng, and means joyful events and marriage, and suits seeking wealth; the Azure Dragon runs month by month as Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou, and means good fortune in public and private matters, with marriage and wealth both lucky.
