# 男家占女以财为用，女家占男以官为用，代占同断；官有生扶、财爻旺相，即许成婚

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚姻：男家占女家，用妻财爻；女家占男家，用官鬼爻；父母、亲戚朋友代占也一样。官鬼有生有扶，或者妻财旺相，就可以答应成婚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a marriage divination, when the man's side asks about the woman take the Wealth line as the Subject, and when the woman's side asks about the man take the Pressure line; parents, relatives and friends divining on their behalf are the same. If the Pressure line is supported, or the Wealth line is strong, the marriage may be granted.
