# 六合而动象刑伤，必多破阻；世冲而日辰扶助，当有吹嘘：动爻日辰冲克则有人破阻、生合则有人吹嘘

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应相生相合本是吉兆，但遇动爻、日辰冲克，两边必有人说坏话，事难成；世应相冲相克本不吉，但遇动爻、日辰生合，两边必有人说好话，也可成；想知道是谁，按五类推，比如父母爻是叔伯尊长一类，外宫、他卦上的是外人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self and Other generating and combining is by nature an auspicious sign, but if a moving line or the Day clashes or controls it, there will be people on both sides speaking against it and the matter is hard to bring about; the Self and Other clashing or controlling each other is by nature not auspicious, but if a moving line or the Day generates or combines with them, there will be people on both sides speaking well of it and it can succeed; to know who they are, judge by the five kinds: the Resource line is the uncles and elders, and lines in the outer palace or another trigram are outsiders.
