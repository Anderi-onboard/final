# 门第：内卦世爻旺男家富，外卦应爻旺女家富；应临贵马德合不空破克伤者贵；应财衰而外卦旺富止虚名，应财旺而外卦衰家窘而女有德色

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦的世爻旺，男方家里富；外卦的应爻旺，女方家里富。应爻带贵人、驿马、德神和合，又不空、不破、不受克伤的，显贵；月建旺、日辰衰的，近几年在走下坡。应爻、财爻衰而外卦旺，富只是虚名；应爻、财爻旺而外卦衰，家里窘迫，但女儿有德有容色。富贵贫贱另见身命占，德行性情容貌另见妻妾一类，这里不重复。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Self line in the inner trigram strong, the man's family is rich; with the Other line in the outer trigram strong, the woman's family is rich. If the Other line carries the Nobleman, the Post Horse, Virtue and a combination, and is not Void, broken, controlled or harmed, the family is eminent; strong in the Month but weak in the Day, it has declined in recent years. With the Other and Wealth lines weak but the outer trigram strong, the wealth is only a name; with the Other and Wealth lines strong but the outer trigram weak, the family is straitened but the daughter has virtue and beauty. Wealth, rank, poverty and lowliness are treated in the divination of one's person and fate, and virtue, nature and looks in the section on wives and concubines, so they are not repeated here.
