# 问妻而忌财陷，不能拿世空去断

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 为自己问「道」，怕的是六冲；问妻子，怕的是妻财陷落；这两种问法，都不能拿世爻落空去断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about the Way the taboo is the six-clash; in asking about a wife the taboo is a sunken Wealth line; in neither is a Void Self line to be made the ground of judgment.

## 《易冒·身命章第四十六》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 927 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 46: Life and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 且问道而忌六冲， | 而且问「道」，忌讳六冲， | Moreover, in asking about the Way the taboo is the six-clash, |
> | 问妻而忌财陷， | 问妻子，忌讳妻财陷落， | in asking about a wife the taboo is a sunken Wealth line, |
> | 皆不可以世空也。 | 这些都不能拿世爻落空去断。 | and in none of these is a Void Self line to be made the ground of judgment. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句紧跟在「问富贵先看世象生旺，世空无成」之后，是转折：富贵要世爻旺，问道和问妻却不能因为世空就断坏。「财陷」书里在这里没有定义。
>
> *Note:* This sentence follows straight after 'in asking about wealth and rank look first at whether the Self line is strong, and a Void Self means nothing is achieved', and turns from it: wealth and rank need a strong Self, but in asking about the Way or a wife one cannot judge it bad merely because Self is Void. The book does not define 'sunken' here.
