# 以应定其门闾：应爻是对方的家，坐青龙积善、坐贵人官宦，旺相富豪、休囚贫乏，空散无主张、破绝少殷勤；阴爻动女人做主、阳爻动男子守信；生世相悦、克世相索

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用应爻定对方的门第。男方占，应爻是女方家；女方占，应爻是男方家。应爻坐青龙是积善之家，坐贵人是做官的人家；旺相是富豪，休囚是贫乏；落空、散，有疑惑而拿不定主意，破、绝，缺少殷勤而多冷落。阴爻发动，拿主意的是女人；阳爻发动，守信用的是男子。应爻生世，两边亲近相悦；克世，彼此互相索取。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Use the Other line to fix the standing of the household. When a man divines, the Other line is the woman's family; when a woman divines, it is the man's. With the Azure Dragon it is a house of stored-up goodness, with the Noble a family of officials; strong, it is rich and powerful, resting or trapped, poor; Void or scattered, it is doubtful and has no settled view, broken or cut off, it shows little attention and much coldness. When a yin line moves, the decisions come from the women; when a yang line moves, good faith comes from the men. If it generates Self the two sides are close and pleased with each other; if it controls Self, each presses the other with demands.
