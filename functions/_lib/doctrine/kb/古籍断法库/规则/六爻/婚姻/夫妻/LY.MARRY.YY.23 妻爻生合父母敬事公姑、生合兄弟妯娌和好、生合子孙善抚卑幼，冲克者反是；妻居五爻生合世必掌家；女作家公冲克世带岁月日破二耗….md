# 妻爻生合父母敬事公姑、生合兄弟妯娌和好、生合子孙善抚卑幼，冲克者反是；妻居五爻生合世必掌家；女作家公冲克世带岁月日破二耗暴败破碎杀必凌夫破家

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻爻生合父母爻，恭敬侍奉公婆；生合兄弟爻，和妯娌和好；生合子孙爻，善于抚育晚辈幼小；冲克父母、兄弟、子孙爻的，相反。妻爻在五爻尊位、生合世爻的，一定掌管家事。妻子当家作主时，如果冲克世爻，又带岁破、月破、日破和二耗、暴败、破碎煞，一定欺凌丈夫、败家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The wife's line generating or combining with the Resource line: she serves her parents-in-law respectfully; with a Peer line: she gets along with her sisters-in-law; with Output: she cares well for the juniors and the young; clashing with or controlling the Resource, Peer or Output lines: the opposite. If the wife's line sits on the fifth line, the place of honor, and generates or combines with the Self line, she surely manages the household. A wife who acts as head of the household, if she clashes with and controls the Self line, or carries a Year-break, Month-break or Day-break and the Two Depletions, Sudden Ruin and Shattering stars, will surely bully her husband and ruin the house.
