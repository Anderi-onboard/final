# 空鬼伏财，必是望门之寡妇；动财值虎，定然带服之婆娘：财伏空鬼之下，其女先曾受聘、未婚夫死；加白虎动，是已嫁夫亡带孝

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里有官、财两爻，而财爻伏在空亡的鬼爻下，那个女子先前受过聘，未婚夫死了，俗称望门寡；再加白虎发动来克，就是嫁了以后丈夫死、带着孝服的；如果伏在不空的鬼下，是有丈夫的妇人，被日辰、动爻提起而刑克世爻，日后要防争讼。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram has both Pressure and Wealth lines and the Wealth line is hidden under a Void Pressure line, the woman was once betrothed and her fiancé died, commonly called a widow at the door; with the White Tiger moving and controlling as well, she married and her husband died and she wears mourning; if she is hidden under a Pressure line that is not Void, she is a woman with a husband, and if the Day or a moving line raises her and it punishes or controls the Self line, guard later against a lawsuit.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2030 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦中财爻伏于空鬼之下，其女先曾受聘，未婚夫死，俗谓之望门寡。 | 卦中财爻伏在空亡的鬼下，那个女子先前受过聘，未婚夫死了，俗称望门寡。 | If the Wealth line is hidden under a Void Pressure line, the woman was once betrothed and her fiancé died, commonly called a widow at the door. |
> | 若加白虎发动，则已嫁而夫死带孝。 | 如果再加白虎发动，就是已经嫁了、丈夫死了，带着孝服。 | If the White Tiger is added and moves, she married and her husband died and she wears mourning. |
> | 若鬼伏财下不空者，必是有夫妇女。 | 如果鬼伏在财下而鬼不空，一定是有丈夫的妇女。 | If Pressure is hidden under Wealth and is not Void, she is surely a woman with a husband. |
> | 如被日辰动爻提起刑克世爻者，后防争讼。 | 如果被日辰、动爻提起，刑克世爻，日后要防争讼。 | If the Day or a moving line raises her and it punishes or controls the Self line, guard later against a lawsuit. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 第三个「伏」的方向，旧注写「若伏鬼不空」（财伏在鬼下，鬼不空），王洪绪写「鬼伏财下不空」（鬼伏在财下），字面相反；两处都是说有夫之妇，本库照原文。
>
> *Note:* On the third case, the old commentary writes 'if hidden under Pressure that is not Void' (Wealth under Pressure) and Wang Hongxu 'Pressure hidden under Wealth, not Void' (Pressure under Wealth); the wording is opposite, though both mean a married woman, and the original is kept.
