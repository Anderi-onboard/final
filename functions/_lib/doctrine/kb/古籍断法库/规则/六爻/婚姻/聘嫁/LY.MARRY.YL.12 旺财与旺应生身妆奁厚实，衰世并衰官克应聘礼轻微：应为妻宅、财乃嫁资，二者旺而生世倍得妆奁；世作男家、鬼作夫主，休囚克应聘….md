# 旺财与旺应生身妆奁厚实，衰世并衰官克应聘礼轻微：应为妻宅、财乃嫁资，二者旺而生世倍得妆奁；世作男家、鬼作夫主，休囚克应聘礼轻微

六爻 · 婚姻 · 聘嫁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 旺相的财爻和旺相的应爻来生扶自身，嫁妆丰厚；世爻衰、官爻也衰又克应爻，聘礼轻薄。应爻是妻子家，财爻是嫁资，两样旺相而生世，加倍得到嫁妆；世爻是男家，鬼爻是丈夫，如果休囚而克应爻，自然聘礼轻薄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A strong Wealth line and a strong Other line generating the Self bring a rich and solid dowry; a weak Self line and a weak Pressure line controlling the Other line bring slight betrothal gifts. The Other line is the wife's house and Wealth the marriage portion: if both are strong and generate the Self, the dowry is doubled; the Self line is the man's family and Pressure the husband: if they are resting and trapped and control the Other line, the betrothal gifts are naturally slight.
