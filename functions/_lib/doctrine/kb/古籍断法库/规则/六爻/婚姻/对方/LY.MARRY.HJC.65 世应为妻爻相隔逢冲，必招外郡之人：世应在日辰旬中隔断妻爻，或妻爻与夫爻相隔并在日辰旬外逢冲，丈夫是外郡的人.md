# 世应为妻爻相隔逢冲，必招外郡之人：世应在日辰旬中隔断妻爻，或妻爻与夫爻相隔并在日辰旬外逢冲，丈夫是外郡的人

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻、应爻在日辰旬中把妻爻隔断，或者妻爻与夫爻相隔，又在日辰旬外遇冲，主丈夫是外郡的人；夫爻隔着妻爻，妻爻在游魂卦的世应之外发动，主是外州的人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Self and Other cut off the wife's line within the Day's ten-day period, or the wife's line is separated from the husband's line and meets a clash outside the Day's period, the husband is surely a man from another prefecture; if the husband's line is separated from the wife's and the wife's line moves outside Self and Other in a Wandering Soul hexagram, he is from another province.

> [!note] 按
> 「日辰旬中」「日辰旬外」指起卦那一天所在的一旬之内、之外；书里没有再解释。
>
> *Note:* 'Within the Day's period' and 'outside the Day's period' mean inside or outside the ten-day period in which the casting day falls; the book does not explain further.
