# 兄兴六位张瞻丧偶于中年：兄弟爻动克妻财，动在六爻主中年丧妻；财动初爻则早年克父母

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻动就克妻财，如果动在六爻上，中年一定像张瞻那样丧妻。同一对句的上半句说财爻动伤父母，动在初爻就是早年受克（像李密生下六个月父亲就去世）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Peer line moving controls Wealth, the wife; if it moves on the sixth line, in middle age a wife is surely lost, as with Zhang Zhan. The first half of the couplet says that Wealth moving harms the parents, and on the first line the harm comes in early years (as when Li Mi's father died when he was six months old).

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1723 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财动伤父母，兄动则克妻财。 | 财爻动伤父母，兄弟爻动就克妻财。 | Wealth moving harms the parents, the Resource line; a Peer line moving controls Wealth, the wife. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 初爻代表早年，六爻代表中年以后，这是三限里「一爻管五年」的排法（见黄金策同章）。卜筮正宗把经文写成「财动克亲于早岁，兄衰丧偶于中年」（「兄兴」作「兄衰」），注文与卜筮大全一致。
>
> *Note:* The first line stands for early years and the sixth for what follows middle age, following the scheme of the three periods in which each line governs five years (see the same chapter of the Huangjin Ce). The Zhengzong gives the verse as 'Wealth moving harms the parents in early years, a Peer line weak loses a spouse in middle age' ('flourishing' becomes 'weak'), with a note matching the Quanshu.
