# 兄动而爻临玄武，须防劫骗之谋：鬼爻不动而兄弟临玄武、螣蛇刑冲世身，防奸诈骗财；世应生合、阴阳得位则大费财而可成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻不动，而兄弟又临玄武、螣蛇，来刑冲世身的，要防其中奸诈，设计骗财；如果世应生合、阴阳得位、财鬼无伤，也一定要大费钱财才能成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Pressure line quiet and Peer sitting on the Dark Tortoise or the Coiling Snake and punishing or clashing with the Self or the Body, guard against deceit and a scheme to swindle money; if the Self and Other generate and combine, yin and yang are in their places and Wealth and Pressure are undamaged, it too must cost a great deal of money to bring about.
