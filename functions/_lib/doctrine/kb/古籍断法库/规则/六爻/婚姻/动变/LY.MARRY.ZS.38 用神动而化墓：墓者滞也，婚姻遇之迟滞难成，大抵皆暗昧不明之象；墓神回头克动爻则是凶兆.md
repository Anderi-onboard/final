# 用神动而化墓：墓者滞也，婚姻遇之迟滞难成，大抵皆暗昧不明之象；墓神回头克动爻则是凶兆

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神动了变出墓，墓就是滞：病、捕获、身命、失物、婚姻遇到都是暗昧不明的象。落在婚姻上，是拖延停滞、难以成就。但如果动爻化墓，而墓神又回头来克动爻，就不要当作暗昧昏滞来断，那是凶兆；要是墓神本身是官鬼，回头克用神，更凶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject moves and changes into tomb, tomb means stagnation: illness, capture, life and fate, lost goods and marriage all become obscure and unclear. For a marriage it means delay and stagnation, and it is hard to accomplish. But if a moving line changes into tomb and the tomb spirit turns back to control the moving line, it should not be judged as mere obscurity and stagnation, for that is an ill omen; and if the tomb spirit is Pressure itself, turning back to control the Subject, it is worse.

> | 如若墓神为鬼，回头克用，更凶。 | 如果墓神是官鬼，回头克用神，更凶。 | If the tomb spirit is Pressure and turns back to control the Subject, it is worse. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 「养主狐疑，墓多暧昧」是十二长生里养、墓两位的老话；作者说他只验生、旺、墓、绝四位，养、病、胎、沐浴在动变里都删了，这里只取墓。这一段是总说，病、捕获、身命、失物、婚姻并列，只有「婚姻遇之，迟滞难成」一句专讲婚姻。「用」这里是用神。
>
> *Note:* 'Nurturing means doubt, tomb means much obscurity' is an old saying about two of the twelve growth stages, nurturing and tomb; the author says he has found only the four states of birth, prosperity, tomb and extinction to hold true, and has dropped nurturing, sickness, womb and bathing from changed lines, so only tomb is taken here. This passage is a general statement, listing illness, capture, life and fate, lost goods and marriage side by side, and only 'in a marriage it means delay and stagnation' speaks of marriage in particular. 'The Subject' translates 'yong' here.
