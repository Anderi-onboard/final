# 嫁聚当求媒妁人，应为月老忌冲刑：卜媒人以应爻为主象，忌空冲破绝、喜扶合旺生；应克世媒心刁诈，应生世是良媒

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚以间爻为主，单问媒人以应爻的象为凭。这时应爻是主要的象：不能落在空、冲、破、绝的地方，最喜逢扶、合、旺、生。应爻克世爻，媒人心怀刁滑欺诈；应爻来生世爻，就是好媒人。应爻空而陷是没有功效，旺而有生扶才有力量。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a marriage divination the between lines come first, and when the go-between alone is asked about, rely on the sign of the Other line. Then the Other line is the chief sign: it must not fall into Void, clash, break or extinction, and is most glad to meet support, combination, strength and generation. If the Other line controls the Self line the go-between is cunning and deceitful; if it comes to generate the Self line, the go-between is a good one. If the Other line is Void and fallen it achieves nothing; strong and supported, it has power.
