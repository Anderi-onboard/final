# 夫择妇兮阴应美，妇择夫兮阳应欢；阴阳得位方和合，纵然交错乐长年

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男子择妻，应爻属阴才好；女子择夫，应爻属阳才欢喜。阴阳得位才和合，即使阴阳交错，也长久快乐。男子娶妻，世爻属阳、应爻属阴算得位；女子嫁男子，世爻属阴、应爻属阳算得位；内阴外阳，也一样。得位则欢喜无穷，即使交错，也主欢乐和谐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a man chooses a wife, a yin Other line is fair; when a woman chooses a husband, a yang Other line brings joy. Yin and yang in their places make harmony; even if they are crossed, there is long joy. For a man taking a wife, the Self line yang and the Other yin count as in their places; for a woman marrying a man, the Self yin and the Other yang count as in their places; inner yin with outer yang is the same. In their places, joy is endless; even crossed, there is still gladness and harmony.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 555 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse

> | 夫聚妇，世阳应动为得位。 | 男子娶妻，世爻属阳、应爻属阴，算得位； | For a man taking a wife, the Self line yang and the Other yin count as in their places; |
> | 女嫁男，世阴应阳为得位。 | 女子嫁男子，世爻属阴、应爻属阳，算得位； | for a woman marrying a man, the Self line yin and the Other yang count as in their places; |

> | 若得阴阳得位，喜悦无穷，纵然阴阳交错，也主欢谐。 | 如果阴阳得位，欢喜无穷；即使阴阳交错，也主欢乐和谐。 | If yin and yang are in their places, joy is endless; even if yin and yang are crossed, there is still gladness and harmony. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 解说第一句原文作「世阳应动为得位」，按下一句（「世阴应阳」）对着看，「动」应是「阴」之误；译文按「应阴」译。
>
> *Note:* The first sentence of the explanation reads 'the Self line yang and the Other moving counts as in place'; set against the next sentence ('the Self yin, the Other yang'), 'moving' should be 'yin', and it is translated as 'the Other yin'.
