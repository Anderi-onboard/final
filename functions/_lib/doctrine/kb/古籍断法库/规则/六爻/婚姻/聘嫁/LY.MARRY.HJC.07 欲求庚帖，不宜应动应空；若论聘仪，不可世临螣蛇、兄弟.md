# 欲求庚帖，不宜应动应空；若论聘仪，不可世临螣蛇、兄弟

六爻 · 婚姻 · 聘嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 求庚帖（女方的八字帖），要应爻安静不空、又生合世爻，才一定应允；应爻发动、空亡或冲克世爻，都主不答应。论聘礼，世爻见螣蛇、兄弟，主男家吝啬、礼不多；应爻见它们，主女家妆奁淡薄；旺相发动，也是克妻的兆头。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To obtain the horoscope card (the woman's family's eight-character card), the Other line must be quiet, not Void, and must generate or combine with the Self, and then consent is sure; the Other moving, falling Void, or clashing with or controlling the Self all mean refusal. As to betrothal gifts, the Coiling Snake or Peer on the Self line means the man's family is stingy and the gifts few; on the Other line, the woman's dowry is meagre; thriving and moving, it is also a sign of harm to the wife.
