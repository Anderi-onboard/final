# 配仆同于取奴，用妻财：拱合生扶则忠诚效力，散空破绝则偷惰难亲，命墓三冲皆不我助

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 收用仆人和收用奴仆是一样的，用妻财爻作用神：有拱合、生扶，就忠诚出力；散、空、破、绝，就偷懒、难以亲近；命入墓、遇三冲，都不能帮我。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Taking on a servant is the same as acquiring a slave, so use the Wealth line: with an arching combination or support he is loyal and works hard; scattered, Void, broken or cut off, he is thievish and lazy and hard to get close to; with the Life in the tomb or the three clashes, none of it helps me.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「配仆」书里没有解释：原文说它「同于取奴」、用妻财、看的是忠诚还是偷懒，所以译作「收用仆人」，和 LY.MARRY.ZS.20 一致。「拱合」是两个地支夹着第三个而合。
>
> *Note:* The book does not explain 'matching a servant': since it says it is 'the same as acquiring a slave', takes Wealth and turns on whether the servant is loyal or lazy, it is rendered as taking on a servant, in line with LY.MARRY.ZS.20. An arching combination is two branches that flank a third and combine with it.
