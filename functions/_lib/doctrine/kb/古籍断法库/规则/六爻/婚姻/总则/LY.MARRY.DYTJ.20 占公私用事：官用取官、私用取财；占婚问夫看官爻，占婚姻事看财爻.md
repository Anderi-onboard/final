# 占公私用事：官用取官、私用取财；占婚问夫看官爻，占婚姻事看财爻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官用取官，私用取财。占病（鬼祟）、占失（看贼）、求官事、官司词讼、占婚姻问丈夫，这些都看官爻；占买卖、家事、奴婢事、求财事、婚姻事，这些都看财爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For an official matter take Pressure, for a private matter take Wealth. Illness (haunting), loss (look for the thief), seeking office, lawsuits, and marriage when asking after the husband: all of these look at the Pressure line; trade, household affairs, servant matters, seeking wealth and marriage matters: all of these look at the Wealth line.

> [!note] 按
> 「占婚问夫」是女方占丈夫；「占婚姻事」没有写明男女，按上下文是一般的婚事，看财爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Marriage when asking after the husband' is a woman divining a husband; 'marriage matters' does not say man or woman and, from the context, is marriage in general, judged by the Wealth line.
