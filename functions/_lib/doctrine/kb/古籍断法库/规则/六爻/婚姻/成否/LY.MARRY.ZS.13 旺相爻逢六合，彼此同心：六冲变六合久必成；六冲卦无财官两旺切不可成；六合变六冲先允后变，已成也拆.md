# 旺相爻逢六合，彼此同心：六冲变六合久必成；六冲卦无财官两旺切不可成；六合变六冲先允后变，已成也拆

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 爻逢六合，占婚最合适，还要财、官旺相才吉；男子最忌财爻逢破、墓，女子要防官星被克、逢绝。六冲变成六合的更吉：求婚的眼下不答应，时间久了一定成；已经成了却不和睦的，最终会和美。只忌六冲卦：如果没有财、官两旺，千万不可成婚，即使勉强成了，不是死别就是生离。六合变成六冲，先答应后改变，已经成的到头来也会拆散。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the lines meet a Six-combine, it is most suitable for a marriage divination, and it is good only if Wealth and Pressure are strong too; a man fears Wealth meeting a break or tomb, a woman guards against the Pressure position being controlled or extinguished. A Six-clash changing into a Six-combine is better still: for one seeking a marriage, although refused for now, it will be made in time; for a couple already wed but at odds, they will in the end be at peace. Only a Six-clash hexagram is to be feared: unless both Wealth and Pressure are strong, on no account make the match, and even if it is forced through it ends either in death or in parting alive. A Six-combine changing into a Six-clash is first agreed and then changed, and one already wed will in the end be split up.
