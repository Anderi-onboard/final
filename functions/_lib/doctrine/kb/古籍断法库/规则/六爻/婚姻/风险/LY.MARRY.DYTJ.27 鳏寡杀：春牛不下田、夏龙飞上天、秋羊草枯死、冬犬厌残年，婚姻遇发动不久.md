# 鳏寡杀：春牛不下田、夏龙飞上天、秋羊草枯死、冬犬厌残年，婚姻遇发动不久

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鳏寡杀的口诀：春天的牛不下田，夏天的龙飞上天，秋天的羊草枯死，冬天的狗厌恶残年。婚姻遇到它发动，长久不了。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The rhyme of the widower-and-widow star: in spring the ox does not go down to the field, in summer the dragon flies up to heaven, in autumn the goat's grass withers and dies, in winter the dog is weary of the dying year. In marriage, if it meets this star moving, it does not last.

## 《断易天机·散见各章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 285 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 春牛不下田， | 春天牛不下田， | In spring the ox does not go down to the field, |
> | 夏龙飞上天， | 夏天龙飞上天， | in summer the dragon flies up to heaven, |
> | 秋羊草枯死， | 秋天羊的草枯死， | in autumn the goat's grass withers and dies, |
> | 冬犬厌残年。 | 冬天狗厌恶残年。 | in winter the dog is weary of the dying year. |
> | 婚姻遇发动不久也。 | 婚姻遇到它发动，长久不了。 | In marriage, if it meets this star moving, it does not last. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 四句口诀里的牛、龙、羊、犬是生肖，依次对着丑、辰、未、戌四个地支，分别是春、夏、秋、冬的鳏寡杀所在；地支对应是照生肖说的，书里这里没有写出来。
>
> *Note:* The ox, dragon, goat and dog in the four-line rhyme are zodiac animals standing for the branches Chou, Chen, Wei and Xu, the places of the widower-and-widow star in spring, summer, autumn and winter respectively; the book does not spell out the branches here, and they are given by the zodiac.
