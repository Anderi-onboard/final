# 妻爻带玄武咸池红艳杀加驿马动必贪淫；合应爻旁爻必与外人私通，进神动来生合淫无度，退神动来合制眼去眉来无实，合逢冲被人撞见；兄合财兄私弟妇、父合财翁妇相通、子合财义男共枕；福（子）动化鬼合财亦然；此断须带咸池红艳杀

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻爻带玄武、咸池、红艳煞，又加驿马而动，一定放纵贪淫。妻爻去合应爻旁边的爻，一定和外人私通：遇进神动来生合，淫乱没有节度；遇退神动来合住，只是眉目传情、没有实事（合又逢空也是这样）；合又逢冲，被人撞见，虽然淫乱但不泛滥。妻爻暗动、去合应爻旁的爻，要防私奔。兄弟爻合财爻，是哥哥私通弟媳；父母爻合财爻，是公公与儿媳相通；子孙爻合财爻，是义子与她同枕；福德爻动化鬼合财、财动化鬼合福德，也是这样——但这些都要带咸池、红艳煞才可以断。应爻与妻爻临兄弟爻，动化财爻来合世的，是姨妹一同的事。《黄金策》说合多而刑煞临身，女子必为娼；《涯泉摘锦》说贵人多是舞裙歌扇，合多是暗约偷期。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The wife's line carrying the Dark Tortoise, the Xianchi star and the Red Allure star, with the Post Horse added and moving: she is surely willful and lustful. If the wife's line combines with a line beside the Other line, she surely has an affair with an outsider: if an Advancing spirit moves to generate or combine with it, her licentiousness is without measure; if a Retreating spirit moves to combine with and restrain it, there are only glances and looks with no actual act (a combination meeting Void is the same); if the combination meets a clash and she is caught by someone, although licentious she is not indiscriminate. If the wife's line stirs in secret and combines with a line beside the Other line, beware of an elopement. A Peer line combining with Wealth: an elder brother has an affair with his younger brother's wife; the Resource line combining with Wealth: a father-in-law and daughter-in-law are involved; Output combining with Wealth: an adopted son shares a pillow with her; Output moving, changing into Pressure and combining with Wealth, or Wealth moving, changing into Pressure and combining with Output, is the same — but all this may be judged only where the Xianchi and Red Allure stars are carried. The Other line and the wife's line on a Peer line, moving and changing into Wealth to combine with the Self line: the wife's younger sister is involved too. The Huangjin Ce says that many combinations with punishment and baleful stars on the Body make the woman a prostitute; the Yaquan Zhaijin says that many Noblemen mean entertainers and many combinations mean secret trysts.

> [!note] 按
> 「姨妹同腔」的意思原文没有解释，按上下文译作「妻子的妹妹也牵涉在内」。「中媾」是「中冓」（闺房之内）的写法。
>
> *Note:* 'Yi mei tong qiang' (literally something like 'the wife's younger sister and she share one cavity') is not explained and is rendered from the context as the wife's younger sister being involved too. 'Zhong gou' in the closing note is a variant writing of 'zhong gou', the inner chambers.
