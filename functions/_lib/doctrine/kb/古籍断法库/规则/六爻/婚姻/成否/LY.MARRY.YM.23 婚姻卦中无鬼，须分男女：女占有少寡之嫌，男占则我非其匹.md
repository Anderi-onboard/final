# 婚姻卦中无鬼，须分男女：女占有少寡之嫌，男占则我非其匹

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 婚姻卦里没有官鬼，要分开男占和女占：女子占，有少年守寡的嫌疑；男子占，则是我不是她的配偶。小注说，这是在讲没有官鬼的情形，各有各的吉凶分辨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a marriage divination without Pressure, tell man from woman: for a woman divining there is the suspicion of being widowed young; for a man divining, then I am not her match. The note says this speaks of having no Pressure, and each case has its own distinction between lucky and unlucky.

> [!note] 按
> 「少寡」按字面是年轻守寡；「我非其匹」的「我」是占卜的男子。这两句出在无鬼章里一串各种事的「无鬼」情形中，本库只取说到婚姻的这一句。
>
> *Note:* 'Widowed young' is taken literally, and the 'I' in 'I am not her match' is the man who divines. The two sentences come from a run of 'no Pressure' cases for many kinds of matter in the chapter on having no Pressure, and only the one about marriage is taken here.
