# 应带勾陈兼值福，妻如孟光：应爻旺相临勾陈（黑丑诚实）与子孙（贤淑）而无伤损，貌虽不扬而德美

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 勾陈主人肤色黑、相貌丑而诚实，子孙爻主贤惠淑德。应爻旺相，又临着勾陈和子孙，没有受伤损的，他的妻子一定像孟光：相貌虽然不出众，品德却很美。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Hook Earth stands for dark, plain and honest looks, and Output for virtue and gentleness. If the Other line is strong and sits on Hook Earth and Output with no injury, the wife is surely like Meng Guang: her looks may be plain, but her virtue is fine.

## 《黄金策·身命》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6152 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Person and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应带勾陈兼值福，孟德耀复产于斯时； | 应爻带着勾陈，又逢子孙，像孟德耀（孟光）这样的人又生在这个时候； | The Other line carrying Hook Earth and also on Output: Meng Deyao (Meng Guang) is born again in this age; |

> | 勾陈主黑丑诚实，子孙主贤淑，应爻为妻，旺相临之无伤损者，妻如孟光，貌虽不扬而德甚美也。 | 勾陈主黑丑而诚实，子孙爻主贤惠淑德，应爻是妻子，旺相临着它们而没有伤损的，妻子像孟光，相貌虽然不出众而品德很美。 | Hook Earth stands for dark, plain and honest looks and Output for virtue and gentleness; the Other line is the wife, and if it is strong and sits on them with no injury, the wife is like Meng Guang, plain in looks but fine in virtue. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 孟光是东汉梁鸿的妻子，相貌丑而德行好，「举案齐眉」的故事就是她；孟德耀是她的字。这是黄金策断「妻的相貌品德」的一组对句里最好的一句，其后各句都是妇人的坏处或好处。
>
> *Note:* Meng Guang was the wife of Liang Hong of the Later Han, plain in looks and excellent in conduct, the wife of the story of raising the tray level with her brows; Deyao was her courtesy name. This is the best of a run of couplets in the Huangjin Ce judging a wife's looks and character, the rest of which set out women's faults and merits.
