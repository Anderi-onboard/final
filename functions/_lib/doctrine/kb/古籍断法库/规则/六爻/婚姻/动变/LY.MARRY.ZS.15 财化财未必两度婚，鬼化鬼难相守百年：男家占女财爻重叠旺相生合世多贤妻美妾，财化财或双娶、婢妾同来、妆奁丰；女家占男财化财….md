# 财化财未必两度婚，鬼化鬼难相守百年：男家占女财爻重叠旺相生合世多贤妻美妾，财化财或双娶、婢妾同来、妆奁丰；女家占男财化财生世聘礼丰厚，鬼化鬼必夫亡或反复灾非

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财化财，不一定是结两次婚；鬼化鬼，很难说能相守百年。《黄金策》说「财爻叠叠，重作新人」，作者屡次试过：男家占女家，财爻重叠、旺相而生合世爻的，多主有贤妻美妾；财化财的，或者应在娶两个，或者应在婢女妾室一起来，或者妻妾众多，或者嫁妆丰厚；只怕爻中兄弟动，或者日月冲克财爻，那样免不了再办喜事。女家占男家，遇到财化财、财爻重叠而生世的，聘礼一定丰厚；不宜鬼动化鬼，那样必主丈夫亡故；鬼爻重叠克世，不是争婚夺娶，就一定反复不定、有灾祸是非。男方占遇到这种情况也主有变更，克世的就该停手，世爻化鬼的更该停手。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth changing into Wealth does not necessarily mean two marriages; Pressure changing into Pressure can hardly be said to mean keeping company for a hundred years. The Huangjin Ce says 'with Wealth lines upon Wealth lines, one becomes a bridegroom anew', but the author has tried it many times: when the man's family divines about the woman and the Wealth line is doubled, strong, and generates or combines with the Self line, it mostly means a virtuous wife and a fair concubine; where Wealth changes into Wealth, it may answer as taking two brides, or a maid or concubine coming along, or many wives and concubines, or a rich dowry; only beware a moving Peer line, or the Day and Month clashing and controlling Wealth, for then the wedding candles cannot be avoided again. When the woman's family divines about the man and gets Wealth changing into Wealth, with doubled Wealth lines generating the Self, the betrothal gifts will surely be rich; it is not good if Pressure moves and changes into Pressure, which surely means the husband's death, and doubled Pressure lines controlling the Self mean, if not a fight over the marriage or a snatched bride, then surely repeated disaster and dispute. A man who meets this also faces change; where it controls the Self one should stop, and where the Self line changes into Pressure one should stop all the more.

> [!warning] 打架
> 《黄金策》：财爻叠叠，重作新人（再娶，见 LY.MARRY.HJC.24）。野鹤说不一定是两次婚姻：他屡次试过，财爻重叠、财化财多是贤妻美妾、双娶、婢妾同来或妆奁丰厚；只有兄弟爻动、或日月冲克财爻的时候，再办喜事才免不了。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The Huangjin Ce: with Wealth lines upon Wealth lines, one becomes a bridegroom anew (remarriage; see LY.MARRY.HJC.24). The author says it is not necessarily two marriages: in his many trials, doubled Wealth or Wealth changing into Wealth mostly meant a virtuous wife and fair concubine, two brides, a maid or concubine coming along, or a rich dowry; only when a Peer line moves, or the Day and Month clash and control Wealth, can the repeated wedding not be avoided.
