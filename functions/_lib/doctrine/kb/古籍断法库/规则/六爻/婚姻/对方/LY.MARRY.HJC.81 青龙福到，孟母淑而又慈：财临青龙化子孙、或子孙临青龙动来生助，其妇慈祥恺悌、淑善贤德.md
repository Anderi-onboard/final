# 青龙福到，孟母淑而又慈：财临青龙化子孙、或子孙临青龙动来生助，其妇慈祥恺悌、淑善贤德

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙是喜悦慈善的神，子孙又是清吉的神。财爻临青龙化出子孙，或者子孙临青龙、动来生助财爻，这个妇人一定慈祥和乐、贤淑善良，像孟子的母亲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon is a spirit of joy and kindness, and Output is a clean and lucky spirit. If the Wealth line sits on the Azure Dragon and changes into Output, or Output sits on the Azure Dragon and moves to generate and support it, the woman is surely gentle, kind and virtuous, like Mencius' mother.

## 《黄金策·身命》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6168 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Person and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 青龙福到，孟母淑而又慈。 | 青龙带着福神到来，孟母那样贤淑而又慈爱。 | The Azure Dragon arriving with Output: virtuous and kind like Mencius' mother. |
