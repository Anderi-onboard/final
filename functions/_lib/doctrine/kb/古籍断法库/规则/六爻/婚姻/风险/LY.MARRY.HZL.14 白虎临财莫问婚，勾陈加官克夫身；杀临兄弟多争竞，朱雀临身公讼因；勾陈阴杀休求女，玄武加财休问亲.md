# 白虎临财莫问婚，勾陈加官克夫身；杀临兄弟多争竞，朱雀临身公讼因；勾陈阴杀休求女，玄武加财休问亲

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 白虎临在财爻上，不要问婚事；勾陈加在官鬼位上，克夫身。凶煞临在兄弟爻上，多有争执；朱雀临身（爻），因官司而起。勾陈带阴煞，不要求女方；玄武加在财爻上，不要问亲事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The White Tiger on the Wealth line: do not ask about the marriage; Hook Earth added to the Pressure position controls the husband's person. A baleful star on a Peer line: many quarrels; the Vermilion Bird on the Body: a cause of public lawsuits. Hook Earth with a yin baleful star: do not seek the woman; the Dark Tortoise added to Wealth: do not ask about the match.
