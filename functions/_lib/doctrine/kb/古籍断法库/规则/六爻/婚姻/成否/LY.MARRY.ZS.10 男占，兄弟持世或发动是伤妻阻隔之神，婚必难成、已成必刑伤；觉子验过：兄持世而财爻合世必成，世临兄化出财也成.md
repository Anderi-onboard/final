# 男占，兄弟持世或发动是伤妻阻隔之神，婚必难成、已成必刑伤；觉子验过：兄持世而财爻合世必成，世临兄化出财也成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男方占，兄弟爻持世或者发动，是伤妻、阻隔的神，婚事一定难成；已经成了的婚姻遇到这种情况，免不了刑伤。觉子验证过的：兄弟爻持世但财爻合世的，一定成；世爻临兄弟爻而变出财爻的，也成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a man's divination, a Peer line on the Self line or a moving Peer line is the spirit that harms the wife and obstructs, and the marriage will be hard to make; a marriage already made that meets this cannot escape injury. What Juezi has verified: with a Peer line on the Self line but the Wealth line combining with the Self, it surely succeeds; if the Self line is on a Peer and changes into Wealth, it also succeeds.
