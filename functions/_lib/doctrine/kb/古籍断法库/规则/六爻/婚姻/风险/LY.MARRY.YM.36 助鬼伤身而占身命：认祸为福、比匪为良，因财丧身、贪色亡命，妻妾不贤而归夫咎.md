# 助鬼伤身而占身命：认祸为福、比匪为良，因财丧身、贪色亡命，妻妾不贤而归夫咎

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 助鬼伤身（世爻被鬼克，而鬼又在日辰上长生，是自己引来祸害）的格局，占身命时：把祸当成福，把匪类当成好人；为钱财送了性命，贪恋女色丢了命；妻妾不贤，过错却归到丈夫身上；奴仆放肆忤逆，连累主人遭殃。注文的理由是：财是利，鬼是害，天下的害都藏在利里面。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the pattern of 'helping Pressure to harm Self' (the Self line is controlled by Pressure while Pressure is in its birth state on the Day, so the person draws the harm on himself), in a reading of life and fate: he takes harm for fortune and wrong company for good; he loses his body for wealth and his life through lust; his wife and concubines are unworthy and yet the blame comes back to the husband; his servants are unruly and bring disaster on the master. The note gives the reason: Wealth is profit, Pressure is harm, and all the harms under heaven lie hidden in profit.

> [!note] 按
> 「助鬼伤身」的定义在这一章开头（LY.MARRY.YM.02 引过第 614 行）。这一句是替问自己命的人断的，其中讲夫妻的只有「妻妾不贤而归夫咎」一句；同一章第 620 行讲「娶妇则主妇不贤」，也在 LY.MARRY.YM.02 里。「比匪」出自《易·比》「比之匪人」，是亲近了不该亲近的人。
>
> *Note:* The definition of 'helping Pressure to harm Self' is at the head of this chapter (line 614, quoted in LY.MARRY.YM.02). This sentence judges for a person asking about his own fate, and only 'his wife and concubines are unworthy and the blame comes back to the husband' concerns husband and wife; line 620 of the same chapter, 'for taking a wife it means the wife is unworthy', is also in LY.MARRY.YM.02. 'Wrong company' is from the Book of Changes, Bi, 'befriending the wrong people', getting close to people one should not.
