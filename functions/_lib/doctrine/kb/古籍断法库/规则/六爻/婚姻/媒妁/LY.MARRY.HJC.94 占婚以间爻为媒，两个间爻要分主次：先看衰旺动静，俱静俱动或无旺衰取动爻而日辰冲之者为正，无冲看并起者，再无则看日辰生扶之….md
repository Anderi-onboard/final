# 占婚以间爻为媒，两个间爻要分主次：先看衰旺动静，俱静俱动或无旺衰取动爻而日辰冲之者为正，无冲看并起者，再无则看日辰生扶之爻，使事归于一

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚姻，以间爻为媒人。间爻有两个的时候，也照这个办法定主次：先分衰旺、辨动静——两爻都静，旺的在先；有动，动的为急。如果两个都静、都动，或者既不旺也不衰，就取动的爻，被日辰冲的那一个为正；没有被冲的，就看并起的爻为媒；又没有合并的，然后看日辰生扶的爻。这样事情就归于一个人，没有两头的疑惑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining a marriage, the between line is the go-betweens. When there are two between lines, decide which is primary in the same way: first tell strong from weak and moving from still; with both still, the strong one comes first, and with one moving, the moving one is urgent. If both are still, or both moving, or neither strong nor weak, take the moving line, the one clashed by the Day being the primary; if none is clashed, look at the line that arises together with the Day as the go-between; if there is no such combination or arising either, only then look at the line generated or supported by the Day. In this way the matter comes to one person and there is no doubt between two ends.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 5237 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如占婚，以间爻为媒。 | 比如占婚姻，以间爻为媒人。 | For example, in divining a marriage, the between line is the go-between. |
> | 间有两爻，亦当以此定之。 | 间爻有两个爻，也应当照这个办法来定。 | When there are two between lines, decide in the same way. |
> | 若俱静俱动，或无旺无衰，则当取动爻，日辰冲之者为正，无冲，则看并起者为媒也。 | 如果两个都静、都动，或者没有旺没有衰，就应当取动的爻，被日辰冲的那个为正；没有冲的，就看并起的爻当媒人。 | If both are still, or both moving, or neither strong nor weak, take the moving line, the one clashed by the Day being the primary; if none is clashed, look at the line that arises together with the Day as the go-between. |
> | 又无合并，然后看日辰生扶之爻。 | 又没有合并的，然后看日辰生扶的爻。 | If there is no such combination or arising either, only then look at the line generated or supported by the Day. |
> | 如此，则事归于一，而无两端之疑也。 | 这样，事情就归到一个人身上，没有两头的疑惑了。 | In this way the matter comes to one person and there is no doubt between two ends. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「并」是爻和日辰同一个地支；「并起」是爻本身发动、又和日辰同支。这一段是旧注对「别衰旺以明克合，辨动静以定刑冲」两句的发挥，只有第 5237 行谈到婚姻，前面的问答是它用的原则。
>
> *Note:* 'Arising together' means a line that has the same branch as the Day; 'arising' here is a line that moves and also shares the Day's branch. The passage is the old commentary's elaboration of the two lines 'tell strong from weak to make clear control and combination, tell moving from still to fix punishment and clash'; only line 5237 speaks of marriage, and the question and answer before it give the principle it uses.
