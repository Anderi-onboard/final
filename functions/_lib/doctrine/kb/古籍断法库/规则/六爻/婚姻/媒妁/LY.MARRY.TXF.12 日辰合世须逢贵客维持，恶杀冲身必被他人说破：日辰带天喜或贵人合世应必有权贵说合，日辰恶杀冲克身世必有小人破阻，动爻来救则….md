# 日辰合世须逢贵客维持，恶杀冲身必被他人说破：日辰带天喜或贵人合世应必有权贵说合，日辰恶杀冲克身世必有小人破阻，动爻来救则无害

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰合世爻，会有贵人来维持；恶煞冲身，必被别人说破。日辰带天喜或贵人，合世爻、应爻，一定有权贵的人前来维持说合；日辰带恶煞，冲克身世，一定有小人破坏阻拦；有动爻来救，就没有害处。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Day combining with Self, a person of standing will come to support it; a fierce star clashing with the Body, it is sure to be broken up by others. If the Day carries the Heavenly Joy star or the Noble and combines with Self and Other, some person of standing will surely come to support and arrange the match; if the Day carries a fierce star and clashes or controls the Body and Self, some mean person will surely break it up and block it; if a moving line comes to the rescue, there is no harm.
