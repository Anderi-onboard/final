# 贞卦逢冲初已难成，悔冲合冲后终分拆；三冲之象离散悖逆，虽财官备而非吉占；随官入墓谓之不寿，助鬼伤身谓之不贤

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本卦遇冲，一开始就难成；变卦遇冲，或六合处遇冲，后来终归分开。这三种冲的卦象主离散、悖逆，财、官都齐备也不算吉占。人随官鬼入墓，叫不长寿；助官鬼来伤世爻（自身），叫不贤。小注说明：男方占女方，是女子的命随官入墓；女方占男方，是男子的命随官入墓，其余情形不论。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Zhen hexagram meets a clash, the match is hard to make from the start; if the Hui hexagram clashes, or a combination meets a clash, it ends in separation later. The image of the three clashes means scattering and perversity, and even with Wealth and Pressure both present it is not a lucky divination. Following Pressure into the tomb is called short-lived; helping Pressure to harm the person is called unworthy. The note explains: when a man's family divines a woman, it is the woman's Life that follows Pressure into the tomb; when a woman's family divines a man, it is the man's Life; other cases are not counted.

> [!note] 按
> 「三冲」按本书合冲章的小注，是本卦冲、变卦冲、合处逢冲三种（第三段引文；本卦叫贞，变卦叫悔）。「助鬼伤身」在书里另有专章，第四段引它的界说；第五、第六段是同一章里说到婚姻的两句。
>
> *Note:* 'The three clashes' are, by the note in this book's chapter on combination and clash, the clash of the original hexagram, the clash of the changed hexagram, and a combination meeting a clash (third quotation; the original hexagram is called Zhen and the changed one Hui). 'Helping Pressure to harm the person' has a chapter of its own; the fourth quotation gives its definition, and the fifth and sixth are the two sentences in that chapter that speak of marriage.
