# 六亲章·妻财：财发动有青龙助因妻致富，临朱雀妻贤慧，犯勾陈妻病，共螣蛇妻拙，同白虎妻性刚，临玄武妻阴暗；两位妻财一妻一妾；妻财合世夫妻顺

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财爻发动、有青龙帮助，靠妻子发家致富；财爻临朱雀，妻子贤惠聪明；犯勾陈，妻子有病灾；和螣蛇同位，妻子笨拙；和白虎同行，妻子性子刚烈；临玄武，多有阴暗的事，心思瞒着人，一定不好。两个妻财爻都旺相，是一妻一妾都容貌光彩。卦里如果没有妻财，常独自一人；妻财安静，平安康健。妻财合世爻，夫妻和顺；妻位生身，家道兴旺。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Wealth line moving and the Azure Dragon helping it, one grows rich through the wife; Wealth on the Vermilion Bird, the wife is worthy and clever; offending Hook Earth, the wife has illness and misfortune; together with the Coiling Snake, the wife is clumsy; going with the White Tiger, the wife's temper is hard; on the Dark Tortoise, much is hidden and dark, and she hides her thoughts from others, surely not good. Two Wealth lines both strong mean one wife and one concubine, both radiant. If the hexagram has no Wealth line one is often alone; Wealth quiet keeps peace and health. Wealth combining with the Self line: husband and wife are in accord; the wife's place generating the Body: the household prospers.

> [!note] 按
> 这首歌里「妻财」是财爻，不专指某一个妻子；「两位妻财俱旺相」指卦里有两个财爻。歌的前半（父母、子孙）和后半（官鬼、兄弟）讲别的六亲，没有收。同一首歌在《火珠林》阐奥歌章的六亲章里也有（第 1167 行），个别字不同。
>
> *Note:* In this rhyme 'Wealth' is the Wealth line and does not point to a particular wife; 'two Wealth lines' means the hexagram has two Wealth lines. The first part of the rhyme (Resource and Output) and the last (Pressure and Peer) are about other relations and are not included. The same rhyme is in the six-relations chapter of the Chan Ao Ge Zhang in the Huozhulin (line 1167), with a few characters differing.
