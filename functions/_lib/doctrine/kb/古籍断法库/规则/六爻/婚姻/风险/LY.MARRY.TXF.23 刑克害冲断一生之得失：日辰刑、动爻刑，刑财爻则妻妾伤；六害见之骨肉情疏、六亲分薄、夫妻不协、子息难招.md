# 刑克害冲断一生之得失：日辰刑、动爻刑，刑财爻则妻妾伤；六害见之骨肉情疏、六亲分薄、夫妻不协、子息难招

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 有日辰的刑、动爻的刑：刑到父母爻，双亲受损；刑到财爻，妻妾受伤；骨肉逢刑，没有不受伤残的。六害（子未、寅巳、卯辰这几对）见到了，骨肉之间感情疏远，六亲缘分淡薄，夫妻不和，子女难得。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* There are punishments by the Day and punishments by moving lines: punishing the Resource line harms the parents; punishing the Wealth line injures the wife and concubine; flesh and blood meeting punishment are never without injury. Where the six harms (Zi-Wei, Yin-Si, Mao-Chen and the like) are seen, feeling among kin is cool, the bonds of the six relations are thin, husband and wife are not in accord, and children are hard to have.

> [!note] 按
> 六害是子未、丑午、寅巳、卯辰、申亥、酉戌六对；注里举的是前三对（子未、寅巳、卯辰），「余皆仿此」。刑的三种（三刑、自刑等）在这一章前面的注里，这里只收刑到父母、财爻的后果。
>
> *Note:* The six harms are the six pairs Zi-Wei, Chou-Wu, Yin-Si, Mao-Chen, Shen-Hai and You-Xu; the note gives the first three and says 'the rest follow this'. The kinds of punishment (three punishments, self-punishment and so on) are set out in the earlier notes of this chapter, and only the consequences of punishing the Resource and Wealth lines are included here.
