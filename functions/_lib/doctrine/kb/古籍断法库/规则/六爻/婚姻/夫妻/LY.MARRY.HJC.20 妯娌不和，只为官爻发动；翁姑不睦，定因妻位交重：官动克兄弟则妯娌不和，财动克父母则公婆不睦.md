# 妯娌不和，只为官爻发动；翁姑不睦，定因妻位交重：官动克兄弟则妯娌不和，财动克父母则公婆不睦

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚以兄弟为妯娌、父母为公婆；官动克兄弟，妯娌之间不利；财动克父母，公婆不和睦；如果旺相而没有制约，或父爻衰弱抵挡不住，双亲就有刑克。觉子反驳：财旺时同样克父母，不必等到动才说。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining marriage Peer stands for the brothers' wives and Resource for the parents-in-law; Pressure moving controls Peer and is unfavourable among the brothers' wives; Wealth moving controls Resource and the parents-in-law are at odds; if it is thriving with no restraint, or Resource is weak and cannot resist, the parents suffer punishment and control. Juezi objects that a thriving Wealth harms Resource just the same, and one need not wait for it to move.

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妯娌不和，只为官爻发动； | 妯娌不和，只是因为官爻发动； | The brothers' wives are at odds only because the Pressure line moves; |
> | 翁姑不睦，定因妻位交重。 | 公婆不睦，一定是因为妻位（财爻）交重。 | the parents-in-law are at odds surely because the wife's place, the Wealth line, is doubled. |

> | 余曰：动则能伤父母，旺则不能伤乎？ | 我说：动就能伤父母，旺就不能伤吗？ | I say: if moving can harm Resource, the parents, can thriving not harm them? |
> | 假令春天占卦，财爻属木，谓之女命旺而可取，但父母爻定然属土，如此当权得令之财爻，有不克休囚之父母爻者乎？ | 假如春天占卦，财爻属木，叫作女命旺而可取，但父母爻一定属土，这样当权得令的财爻，有不克衰弱的父母爻的吗？ | Suppose the divination is in spring and the Wealth line is Wood, called a woman whose fate is strong and worth taking; but the Resource line is surely Earth, and can a Wealth line thus holding power and in season fail to control a weak Resource line? |
> | 何必曰动？ | 何必说动？ | Why say only moving? |
> | 殊不知昔人言动而不言旺者，正虑后人驳议，故讳言其旺，不得已而言动也，岂知亦被看破耶？ | 殊不知前人说动而不说旺，正是担心后人驳议，所以不肯说它旺，不得已才说动，哪知也被看破了呢？ | Little do people know that the ancients said moving and not thriving precisely for fear of later objections, and avoided saying thriving and said moving because they had to; who would have thought this too would be seen through? |

^q4

> [!warning] 打架
> 《黄金策》旧注和王洪绪都说「财动克父母」；觉子（《增删卜易》）认为伤父母的是财旺，不必等到动，前人只说动是为了避免被驳；他的办法是先占财官旺不旺，得吉卦后再单占妨不妨克父母。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The old commentary and Wang Hongxu both say 'Wealth moving controls Resource'; Juezi (Zengshan Buyi) holds that what harms the parents is Wealth being thriving, with no need to wait for it to move, and that the ancients spoke of moving only to avoid being challenged; his method is to divine first whether Wealth and Pressure are thriving and, having got a good hexagram, to divine separately whether the parents will be harmed.
