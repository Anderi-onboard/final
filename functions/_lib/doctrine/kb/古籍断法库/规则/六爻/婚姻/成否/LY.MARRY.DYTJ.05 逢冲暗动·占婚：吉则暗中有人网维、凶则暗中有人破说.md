# 逢冲暗动·占婚：吉则暗中有人网维、凶则暗中有人破说

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻安静的卦，被日辰冲而动，叫暗动；暗动有吉有凶，各有各的用处，不可一概而论。占婚姻遇到暗动：吉的话暗中有人维系撮合；凶的话暗中有人说破。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a hexagram whose six lines are still, a line stirred by the Day's clash is a hidden movement; hidden movement brings good or ill, each with its own use, and cannot be judged all the same. In a marriage divination, meeting hidden movement, if lucky someone quietly holds the match together; if unlucky someone quietly talks it apart.
