# 刑：婚姻遇之惧门第之相压；男娶遇刑在财、女嫁遇刑在官

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 婚姻遇到刑，怕的是两家门第互相压制。小注说：男子娶亲，是财爻遇到刑；女子出嫁，是官爻遇到刑。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If a marriage meets punishment, one fears that the two families' standing will press on each other. The note says: for a man marrying it is the Wealth line that meets it, and for a woman marrying it is the Pressure line.

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 646 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 38: Punishment and harm", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 婚姻遇之，惧门第之相压； | 婚姻遇到刑，怕的是两家门第互相压制； | If a marriage meets punishment, one fears that the two families' standing will press on each other; |

^q1

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 647 行 · Bracketed small note in the Yi Mao (author not named in the book)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身命，谓世及用爻遇之，男娶谓财、女嫁谓官遇之。 | 「身命」是说世爻和用爻遇到刑；男子娶亲是说财爻遇到刑，女子出嫁是说官爻遇到刑。 | 'The person and Life' means the Self line and the Subject line meeting it; for a man marrying it means Wealth meeting it, for a woman marrying, Pressure meeting it. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这里的「刑」指相刑、三刑一类；刑害章开头讲刑的立法，本库只取说到婚姻的这两句。
>
> *Note:* 'Punishment' here is the mutual punishment of branches, the three punishments and the like; the chapter opens with how punishment is set up, and only the two sentences that speak of marriage are taken here.
