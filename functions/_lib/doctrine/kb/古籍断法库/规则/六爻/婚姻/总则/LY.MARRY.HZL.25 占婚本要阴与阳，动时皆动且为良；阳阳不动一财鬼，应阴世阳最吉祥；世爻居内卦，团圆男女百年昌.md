# 占婚本要阴与阳，动时皆动且为良；阳阳不动一财鬼，应阴世阳最吉祥；世爻居内卦，团圆男女百年昌

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚原本要阴阳相配；动的时候一起动也算好。阳爻都不动，只有一个财爻、一个鬼爻，应爻是阴、世爻是阳，最吉祥。再得世爻在内卦，男女团圆，百年昌盛。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A marriage divination needs yin with yang; when they move, moving together is also good. If the yang lines do not move and there is just one Wealth and one Pressure, with the Other line yin and the Self line yang, it is the most auspicious. Better still if the Self line sits in the inner trigram: man and woman are reunited and prosper for a hundred years.

> [!note] 按
> 「阳阳不动一财鬼」一句语法不清，可读作「阳爻都不动，只有一财一鬼」；译文取这个读法。
>
> *Note:* The phrase 'yang yang do not move, one Wealth and Pressure' is unclear in grammar; it is read as 'the yang lines do not move, and there is only one Wealth and one Pressure'.
