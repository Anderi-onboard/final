# 两父齐兴，必有争盟之象；双官俱动，斯为竞娶之端：两个父爻动主有两人主婚或两家庚帖，两个官爻动主两家争娶；官化父、父化官恐有争讼

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里两个父爻动，或父化父，有两个人主婚，不然就是两家的庚帖；两个鬼都动，有两家争着求婚，不然事体多变；一动一静、一旺一空则无此说；官化父、父化官，或官父都动，恐怕有争讼；文书、兄弟动，一定有口舌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Two Resource lines moving in the hexagram, or Resource changing into Resource, means two people presiding or two families' horoscope cards; two Pressure lines both moving means two families contending for the match, or else the affair changes often; one moving and one still, or one thriving and one Void, does not carry this reading; Pressure changing into Resource, Resource into Pressure, or both moving, may bring a lawsuit; Resource or Peer moving surely brings quarrels.
