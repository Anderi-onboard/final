# 应静旬空财化进，是早有的良缘：应空则对方不实，动而化退必有反悔；财动化进生世，虽退悔终是良缘

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻静而旬空、财爻化成进神，是早就有的好姻缘。应爻空，是对方不踏实；如果应爻动了又化成退神，对方必有反悔的心。如果财爻动、化成进神而来生世，虽然会有退悔，终究是好姻缘。世爻、应爻动了又逢破，也应在破被填实的时候。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A still Other line in the Void of its ten-day cycle with Wealth changing into an Advancing spirit is a good match long fated. If the Other line is Void, the other party is not sincere; if it moves and changes into a Retreating spirit, they surely have thoughts of going back on it. If Wealth moves, changes into an Advancing spirit and generates the Self line, then although there is withdrawal and regret, it is in the end a good match. If the Self and Other lines move and are broken, it too answers at the time the break becomes real.
