# 子孙用·占婚：子孙为子息，卦中无子必无儿，但不宜动，动则异日伤夫

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 我所生的是子孙爻，是卦中最吉的神：遇到它没有不好的，背离它就没法带来福；卦里没有父母爻它就不受克，有兄弟爻它就有依靠；发动就生财、克伤官鬼。占婚姻，子孙爻代表子息：卦中没有子孙，一定没有儿子；但不宜发动，动了日后要伤夫。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* What I generate is the Output line, the most auspicious spirit in a hexagram: meeting it, all is fair, and whatever turns its back on it cannot bring fortune; with no Resource line in the hexagram it is not controlled, and with Peer among the lines it has support; moving, it generates Wealth and controls and harms Pressure. In a marriage divination the Output line stands for children: if there is no Output in the hexagram there will surely be no son; but it should not move, for moving it will harm the husband some day.
