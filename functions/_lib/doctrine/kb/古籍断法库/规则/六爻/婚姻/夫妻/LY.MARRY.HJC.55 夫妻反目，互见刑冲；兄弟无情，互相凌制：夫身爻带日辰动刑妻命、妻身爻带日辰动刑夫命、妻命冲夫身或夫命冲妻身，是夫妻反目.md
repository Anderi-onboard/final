# 夫妻反目，互见刑冲；兄弟无情，互相凌制：夫身爻带日辰动刑妻命、妻身爻带日辰动刑夫命、妻命冲夫身或夫命冲妻身，是夫妻反目

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丈夫的身爻带日辰发动刑妻子的命，是丈夫和妻子不和；妻子的身爻带日辰发动刑丈夫的命，是妻子和丈夫不和；或者妻子的命冲丈夫的身、丈夫的命冲妻子的身，是夫妻反目（比如风天小畜九三爻）。兄长的爻带日辰克弟弟的身命爻，或弟弟的爻克兄长的身命爻，是兄弟不和、互相欺凌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the husband's Body line, with the Day, moves to punish the wife's Life, the husband is not at peace with the wife; if the wife's Body line, with the Day, moves to punish the husband's Life, the wife is not at peace with the husband; or if the wife's Life clashes with the husband's Body, or the husband's Life with the wife's Body, husband and wife turn against each other (the third line of Xiaoxu, Wind over Heaven, is an example). If the elder brother's line with the Day controls the younger brother's Body or Life line, or the younger's controls the elder's, the brothers are at odds and abuse each other.

> | 或妻命冲夫身，或夫命冲妻身，主夫妻反目。 | 或者妻子的命冲丈夫的身，或者丈夫的命冲妻子的身，主夫妻反目。 | Or if the wife's Life clashes with the husband's Body, or the husband's Life clashes with the wife's Body, husband and wife turn against each other. |
> | 兄带日辰克弟身命爻，或弟带日辰克兄身命爻，主兄弟不和，互相凌虐。 | 兄长的爻带日辰克弟弟的身命爻，或者弟弟的爻带日辰克兄长的身命爻，主兄弟不和，互相欺凌。 | If the elder brother's line with the Day controls the younger brother's Body or Life line, or the younger brother's line with the Day controls the elder's, the brothers are at odds and abuse each other. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这里的「兄」「弟」是家里的兄长和弟弟，不是六亲里的兄弟爻，所以英文写 brother。旧注多一句「风天小畜九三爻」的例子。
>
> *Note:* The 'elder brother' and 'younger brother' here are the brothers of the family and not the Peer relation, hence the English 'brother'. The old commentary adds the example of the third line of Xiaoxu.
