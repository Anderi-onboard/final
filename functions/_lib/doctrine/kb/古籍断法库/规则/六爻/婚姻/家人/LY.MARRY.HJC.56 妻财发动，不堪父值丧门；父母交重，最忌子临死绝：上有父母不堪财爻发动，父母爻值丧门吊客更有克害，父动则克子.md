# 妻财发动，不堪父值丧门；父母交重，最忌子临死绝：上有父母不堪财爻发动，父母爻值丧门吊客更有克害，父动则克子

六爻 · 婚姻 · 家人 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财发动，父母爻受不了（特别是父母爻再遇丧门、吊客）；父母爻发动，最怕子孙落在死绝上。上有父母的，受不了财爻发动，会有克害的祸患；父母爻动会克子孙。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Wealth moves, the Resource line cannot bear it (especially if the Resource line also meets the Mourning Gate or Funeral Guest stars); when the Resource line moves, most feared is Output on death or extinction. If there are parents above, they cannot bear the Wealth line moving and there will be the trouble of injury and harm; a moving Resource line controls Output.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3141 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |

> | 上有父母，不堪财爻发动，主有克害之患； | 上有父母的，受不了财爻发动，主有克害的祸患； | If there are parents above, they cannot bear the Wealth line moving, and there will be the trouble of injury and harm; |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮正宗把第一句作「不堪父值休囚」（父母爻遇休囚），旧注本作「不堪父值丧门」。「上有父母」是家里真有父母。
>
> *Note:* The Bushi Zhengzong reads the first line as 'the Resource line cannot bear meeting rest and confinement', where the old commentary text has 'meeting the Mourning Gate'. 'Parents above' means the diviner's actual parents.
