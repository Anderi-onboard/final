# 占妻看财爻、占夫看官爻，都宜静；阳宫端正阴宫丑陋；男占得震巽主再婚，女占得坎离主再嫁

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占妻子看财爻，占丈夫看官鬼爻，都宜安静。阳宫的人端正，阴宫的人丑陋：落在飞神上，应在头面四肢；落在伏神下，应在笨拙、不稳当。男子占得震卦、巽卦，主再婚；女子占得坎卦、离卦，主再嫁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a wife look at the Wealth line and for a husband at the Pressure line; both should be still. A yang palace means upright looks and a yin palace ugly ones: on the flying spirit it answers in the head, face and limbs, and under it, in clumsiness and unsteadiness. A man who gets Zhen or Xun will marry again; a woman who gets Kan or Li will marry again.

> [!note] 按
> 「飞上」「飞下」里的「飞」指飞神（卦上现出的爻），「下」指它下面伏着的伏神；译文按这个读法，原文只有这三个字。
>
> *Note:* In 'on the flying' and 'under the flying', 'flying' means the flying spirit (the line shown in the hexagram), and 'under' its hidden spirit; the translation follows this reading, and the original has nothing more to check it against.
