# 用爻值三合，见贵谋事、嫁娶、求财、科甲、田蚕诸般逐意

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻遇到三合成局，求见贵人、谋事、嫁娶、求财、科举、田蚕这些事，件件都称心如意。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject line meets a three-harmony frame, seeing a great man, undertakings, marriage, seeking wealth, examinations, fields and silkworms all go as one wishes.

## 《易林补遗·易林总断章第一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 186 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 1: general judgments", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡用爻值三合者，见贵谋事、嫁娶、求财、科甲、田蚕诸般逐意也。 | 凡是用爻遇到三合成局，求见贵人谋事、嫁娶、求财、科举、田蚕这些事，件件都称心如意。 | Whenever the Subject line meets a three-harmony frame, seeing a great man to plan, marriage, seeking wealth, examinations, fields and silkworms all go as one wishes. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句前面讲的是三合怎样才算成局：三个字全叫真三合，缺一字断为半吉，缺了中间的字就不成（申子辰缺子不成，亥卯未缺卯不成）。「缺一字为半吉」这条和《增删卜易》「两爻动一爻不动也成局」不同（LY.MARRY.ZS.36），后者要求等月日把缺的补上。
>
> *Note:* The lines before it say when a three-harmony counts as formed: all three characters make a true one, one missing is judged half lucky, and if the middle one is missing it does not form (Shen-Zi-Chen without Zi does not, nor Hai-Mao-Wei without Mao). 'One missing is half lucky' differs from the Zengshan Buyi's 'two lines moving and one still also forms a frame' (LY.MARRY.ZS.36), which requires a later month or day to supply what is missing.
