# 财化财，一举两得；鬼化鬼，四复三番：财化财有赠嫁，财化子有带来的幼子；鬼化鬼凡事反复

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚遇财化财，一定有仆人陪嫁（赠嫁）；财化子，有小孩带来（带幼聘）；化子逢空虽来不长寿；化财遇冲以后会走失；退神逢冲，以后背夫改嫁或回娘家。鬼化鬼，不论进退神，凡事反复不定，占婚一定不容易，即使可成也迟滞，女家多有变化，缓急全听对方。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a marriage divination, Wealth changing into Wealth surely means servants come with the bride as a gift; Wealth changing into Output means a small child is brought along, called a betrothal with a child; changing into Output and meeting Void, the child comes but will not live long; changing into Wealth and meeting a clash, she will later run away; changing into a retreating spirit and meeting a clash, she will later leave her husband and remarry, or go back to her family. Pressure changing into Pressure, whether advancing or retreating, means everything is reversed and unsettled, and the marriage surely will not be easy, and even if it can succeed it is delayed; the woman's family will change its mind often, and easy or hard, slow or quick, all is at its pleasure.

> [!note] 按
> 经文「鬼化鬼，四复三番」在旧注本写作「四复三番」，这里译作「四次反复三次重来」。「带幼聘」「有他名」都是旧时的说法，指新娘带着幼子改嫁。
>
> *Note:* The verse's 'four repeats, three reversals' is translated as repeated reversals. 'A betrothal with a child' and 'having another's name' are old expressions for a bride who brings a young child from a previous marriage.
