# 就婚是家：卦冲应坏不可相依，克世游魂岂为久处；择是地结姻同婚姻占，问安宁则忌鬼发

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 去对方家里成婚居住（就婚），看的是那一家：卦冲、应爻坏，就不能依靠他们；克世又是游魂卦，怎么能长久相处。选定那个地方结婚，就和占婚姻一样断；如果问的是安宁，就忌官鬼发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Going to live at the in-laws' home is a matter of that household: if the hexagram clashes or the Other line is broken, one cannot depend on them; if it controls Self and is a Wandering Soul hexagram, how could one stay long? Choosing that place to hold the marriage is judged like a marriage divination; if the question is peace and safety, then Pressure moving is to be feared.

> [!note] 按
> 「就婚」是到对方家里去住着成婚，和入赘相近。「就婚是家」的意思书里没有解释，译文按「就婚说的是那一家」译。
>
> *Note:* 'Moving in for the marriage' means going to live at the other family's house to be married, close to taking a son-in-law into the household. The book does not explain 'moving in is that household', which is translated as 'a matter of that household'.
