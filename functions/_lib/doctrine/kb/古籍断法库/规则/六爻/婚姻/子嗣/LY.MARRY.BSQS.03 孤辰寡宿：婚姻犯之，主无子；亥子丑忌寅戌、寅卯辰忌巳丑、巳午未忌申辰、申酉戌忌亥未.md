# 孤辰寡宿：婚姻犯之，主无子；亥子丑忌寅戌、寅卯辰忌巳丑、巳午未忌申辰、申酉戌忌亥未

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 孤辰寡宿：婚姻犯了它，主没有子女。歌诀：亥子丑忌寅、戌，寅卯辰找巳、丑，巳午未忌申、辰，申酉戌是亥、未来临。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Solitary Star and the Widowhood Star: if a marriage offends them, it means no children. The rhyme: for Hai, Zi and Chou beware Yin and Xu; for Yin, Mao and Chen look to Si and Chou; for Si, Wu and Wei beware Shen and Chen; for Shen, You and Xu, Hai and Wei arrive.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 每一句前一个地支按通行的排法是孤辰、后一个是寡宿（亥子丑年孤辰在寅、寡宿在戌，余类推），书里没有写明。「无子」在这里是没有子女的意思，书里没有区分有儿有女。
>
> *Note:* In each line, by the usual layout, the first branch is the Solitary Star and the second the Widowhood Star (for Hai, Zi and Chou years the Solitary Star is at Yin and the Widowhood Star at Xu, and so on); the book does not say so. 'No son' here means no offspring, and the book does not tell sons from daughters.
