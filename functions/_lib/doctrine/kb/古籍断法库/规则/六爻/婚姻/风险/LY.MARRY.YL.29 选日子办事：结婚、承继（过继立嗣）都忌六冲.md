# 选日子办事：结婚、承继（过继立嗣）都忌六冲

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 选日子办各种事，忌讳各不相同：结婚和承继（过继立嗣）这两件事，占到的卦都忌六冲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In choosing a day for undertakings the taboos differ from matter to matter: for marrying and for adopting an heir, a Six-clash in the hexagram is to be avoided in both.

## 《易林补遗·涓选日时章第四十八》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 1028 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 48: choosing days and hours", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 结婚承继，皆忌六冲。 | 结婚、承继（过继立嗣），都忌讳六冲。 | For marrying and for adopting an heir, a Six-clash is to be avoided in each case. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 同一段里还有别的事的忌讳：各样吉日以官鬼为先忌，接寿停忌白虎，做生意不宜兄弟动；上任的官员不怕鬼发、反忌子孙动而官爻空。这些与婚姻无关，没有收。六冲对婚姻的坏处在别的书里说得更细（LY.MARRY.ZS.13、LY.MARRY.YM.24、LY.MARRY.TXF.11）。
>
> *Note:* The same passage gives the taboos of other matters: for all lucky days Pressure is the first thing to avoid, for receiving longevity or a coffin the White Tiger, for business a moving Peer line; an official going to his post need not fear Pressure moving, and fears instead Output moving with Pressure Void. These have nothing to do with marriage and are not included. Other books say more exactly what a Six-clash does to a marriage (LY.MARRY.ZS.13, LY.MARRY.YM.24, LY.MARRY.TXF.11).
