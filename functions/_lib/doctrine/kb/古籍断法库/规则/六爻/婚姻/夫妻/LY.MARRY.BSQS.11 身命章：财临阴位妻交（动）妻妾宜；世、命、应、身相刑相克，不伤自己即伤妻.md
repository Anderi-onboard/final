# 身命章：财临阴位妻交（动）妻妾宜；世、命、应、身相刑相克，不伤自己即伤妻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻落在阴位、又动，妻妾相宜；世、命、应、身彼此又刑又克，不伤到自己就伤到妻子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth on a yin position and moving suits wife and concubine; if the Self, Life, Other and Body punish and control one another, either oneself or one's wife is harmed.

> | 阴位妻交妻妾宜。 | 阴位，妻交，妻妾宜。 | on a yin position, with the wife line moving, wife and concubine are fitting. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「世为身、应为命」和「内卦为身、外卦为命」是这首歌前面的两种取法，「世命应身」指这几样都算进去。同一首歌在《火珠林》阐奥歌章的身命章里也有（第 1162 行）。
>
> *Note:* 'The Self as the Body and the Other as the Life' and 'the inner trigram as the Body and the outer as the Life' are two ways of taking these earlier in the rhyme, and 'Self, Life, Other, Body' means all of them counted. The same rhyme is in the person-and-fate chapter of the Chan Ao Ge Zhang in the Huozhulin (line 1162).
