# 推占来情：旺相则婚姻官职、休囚则争财退位；内外卦俱旺相者大则求官、次则嫁娶；卦与世爻同克日则必是六畜及婚姻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 取象 · 出处 [[卜筮元龟]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道占卜的人为什么事来：先看旺相休囚的气，旺相是婚姻、官职的事，休囚是争财、丢官位的事。注说：春天是艮、震，夏天是巽、离，秋天是坤、兑，冬天是乾、坎；占卜一定要先看八卦的休旺之气，内外卦都旺相的，大的是求官，其次是嫁娶；内外都休囚的，则相反。卦和世爻同克日辰，来问的一定是六畜和婚姻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know what the diviner has come about, first look at whether the qi is strong, resting or trapped: strong, it is marriage and office; resting or trapped, it is quarrels over money and an official losing his place. The note says: spring is Gen and Zhen, summer Xun and Li, autumn Kun and Dui, winter Qian and Kan; a divination must first look at the resting or strong qi of the eight trigrams, and when inner and outer are both strong, the great matter is seeking office and next comes marriage; when inner and outer are both resting or trapped, it is the reverse. If the hexagram and the Self line both control the Day, it must be livestock and marriage.

> [!warning] 打架
> 同一首歌诀，《断易天机》作「卦与世爻同与日」（LY.MARRY.DYTJ.30），这里作「卦与世爻同克日」，一字之差：「同与日」是和日辰相同，「同克日」是一起克日辰。
>
> *Where they disagree:* In the same rhyme the Duanyi Tianji reads 'the hexagram and the Self line are the same as the Day' (LY.MARRY.DYTJ.30), and this text 'the hexagram and the Self line both control the Day', a one-character difference: 'the same as the Day' means matching the Day and 'both control the Day' means together controlling it.

> [!note] 按
> 「休生之卦」的「生」疑是「旺」之误（上下文说的是休旺之气），译文照字面写；「经常有隧」书里没有解释，译作「有常规可循」。
>
> *Note:* 'Rest and birth' looks like a slip for 'rest and strength', as the context speaks of resting and strong qi, and is translated as written; 'has a regular tunnel' is not explained and is rendered as having a regular course.
