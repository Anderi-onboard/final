# 占婢妾专以财爻为主象：财旺吉；动出官主病讼、动出兄主口舌；兄爻合财主外情；财化子善、化官带疾、化兄淫荡、化父老成；定妇人女子看财福二爻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婢妾：专以财爻为主要的象。财爻旺相就吉；动出官鬼爻，主生病、招官司；动出兄弟爻，主口舌。兄弟爻和财爻相合而合住，主有不正当的外情。有别的爻和财爻三刑六害，一定因此打官司。财化子孙，性善；财化官鬼，带病；财化兄弟，淫荡不良；财化父母，老成；财化子孙，性情迟缓不管事。分辨是妇人还是女子：看财、福两个爻，生扶身、世而没有冲克的是女子；财福去生官鬼、兄弟，或者官鬼、兄弟旺而动的是妇人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining a maid or concubine: take the Wealth line alone as the chief sign. Strong Wealth is auspicious; a Pressure line moving out means illness and a lawsuit; a Peer line moving out means quarrels. If a Peer line and the Wealth line combine and hold each other, there is an improper outside attachment. If any line forms a three-punishment or six-harm with the Wealth line, a lawsuit will surely arise from it. Wealth changing into Output: good-natured; into Pressure: carries an illness; into a Peer line: licentious and of bad conduct; into Resource: mature and steady; into Output: slow by nature and takes no responsibility. To tell a married woman from a maiden, look at the Wealth and Output lines: if they generate the Body and Self lines with no clash or control, she is a maiden; if Wealth and Output generate Pressure or a Peer line, or Pressure or a Peer line is strong and moving, she is a married woman.

> [!note] 按
> 「财化子」出现了两次，一次断「性善」，一次断「性迟缓不管事」，原文如此，没有替它改；第二处也许是别的爻之误，书里无从核对。小标题「附：占婢妾」和正文连在一起，这里从「专以财爻」起引。「妇人」「女子」在这里指已婚的和未嫁的，是按上下文取的。
>
> *Note:* 'Wealth changing into Output' appears twice, once judged 'good-natured' and once 'slow and takes no responsibility'. The text is left as it stands; the second may be a slip for another line, and there is nothing in the book to check it against. The heading 'Appendix: divining maids and concubines' runs into the first sentence, so the quotation starts at 'take the Wealth line alone'. 'Married woman' and 'maiden' are taken from the context.
