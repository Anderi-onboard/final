# 龙并子孙全吉兆，喜重父母福相邻；坤变坎宫男破体，乾来离位女非真

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙并上子孙爻，全是吉兆；喜上加喜的是父母爻也重叠，福气相邻。坤卦变到坎宫，男子身体受损（破体）；乾卦来到离位，女子不真（不是所表现的那样）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon joined to the Output line is an entirely auspicious omen; joy doubled with the Resource line, fortune stands adjacent. Kun changing into the Kan palace: the man's body is broken; Qian coming to the Li position: the woman is not genuine.

> [!note] 按
> 「男破体」「女非真」两句原文只有这几个字：「破体」可以是身体受损，也可以另有所指；「非真」是「不是所表现的那样」。译文按字面，没有替书补充。
>
> *Note:* The two phrases 'the man's body is broken' and 'the woman is not genuine' have only these words in the original: 'body broken' may mean bodily harm or something else, and 'not genuine' means 'not what she seems'. They are translated literally without adding to the book.
