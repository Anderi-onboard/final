# 世生应乃男求女，应生世乃女贪男；应生世逢墓绝尚在踌躇，世生应被日辰冲克男意被间阻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻生应爻，是男方求女方；应爻生世爻，是女方贪男方。应虽然生世，但遇到墓、绝，女方还在犹豫；有动爻来生，要靠媒人出力。世爻生应，但世爻被日辰冲克，男方心意再浓，也会被人间阻，事情又难成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line generating the Other line means the man seeks the woman; the Other line generating Self means the woman is keen on the man. Though the Other line generates Self, if it meets tomb or extinction she is still hesitating; if a moving line generates it, it must rely on the go-between's efforts. If Self generates Other but Self is clashed or controlled by the Day, then though the man's intent is strong he is blocked by someone, and the matter is again hard to make.
