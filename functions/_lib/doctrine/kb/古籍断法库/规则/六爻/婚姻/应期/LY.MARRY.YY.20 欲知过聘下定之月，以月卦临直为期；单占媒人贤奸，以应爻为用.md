# 欲知过聘下定之月，以月卦临直为期；单占媒人贤奸，以应爻为用

六爻 · 婚姻 · 应期 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想知道过聘、下定在哪个月，就以「月卦」临值的时候为期。只占媒人是贤是奸，就以应爻为用神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know the month of the betrothal exchange and the fixing of the engagement, take the time when the 'month hexagram' is in charge. To divine alone whether the go-between is worthy or crafty, take the Other line as the Subject.

## 《易隐·婚姻占·媒约》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易隐]] 第 2438 行 · Yi Yin (What Lies Hidden in the Changes), "Divining marriage: the go-between", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 欲知过聘下定之月，则以月卦临直为期也。 | 想知道过聘、下定的月份，就以月卦临值为期。 | To know the month of the betrothal exchange and the fixing of the engagement, take the time when the month hexagram is in charge as the date. |
> | 又单占媒人贤奸者，则以应爻为用也。 | 又，单独占媒人是贤是奸的，就以应爻为用神。 | Again, to divine alone whether the go-between is worthy or crafty, take the Other line as the Subject. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「月卦临直为期」里的「月卦」是什么，原文没有解释（可能是按月排的卦或月建），译文照字面，没有替书选一种读法。
>
> *Note:* What the 'month hexagram' in 'take the time when the month hexagram is in charge' is, is not explained (perhaps a hexagram assigned to each month, or the Month branch); it is translated literally without choosing a reading for the book.
