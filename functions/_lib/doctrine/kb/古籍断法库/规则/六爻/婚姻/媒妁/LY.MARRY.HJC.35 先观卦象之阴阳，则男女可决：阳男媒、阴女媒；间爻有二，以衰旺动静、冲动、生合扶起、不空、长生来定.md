# 先观卦象之阴阳，则男女可决：阳男媒、阴女媒；间爻有二，以衰旺动静、冲动、生合扶起、不空、长生来定

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻阳是男媒，阴是女媒；间爻有两个时，按衰旺、动静来取；衰爻动、旺爻静，取动的；两爻都旺或都衰，取被冲而动的；没有冲，取被生合扶起的；一旺一空，取不空的；都不空，用长生的办法；务必归到一个，就不杂乱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A yang between line is a male go-between and a yin one a female; when there are two between lines, choose by strength and movement; if the weak one moves and the thriving one is still, take the moving one; if both thrive or both are weak, take the one clashed into movement; with no clash, the one raised by generation or combination; if one thrives and one is Void, the one that is not Void; if neither is Void, use the Long Life method; always come down to one, and then it is not confused.

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6427–6428 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 阳男媒，阴女媒。 | 阳是男媒，阴是女媒。 | Yang is a male go-between and yin a female one. |
> | 或问：间爻有二，当以何爻为主？ | 有人问：间爻有两个，应当以哪一爻为主？ | Someone asks: there are two between lines, which should be taken as the main one? |
> | 以衰旺动静取之。 | 用衰旺、动静来取。 | Take it by strength and movement. |
> | 又问：衰爻动，旺爻静，则如何？ | 又问：衰的爻动、旺的爻静，怎么办？ | Again he asks: what if the weak line moves and the thriving one is still? |
> | 则动者是也。 | 那么动的就是。 | Then the moving one is it. |
> | 又问：两爻皆旺或皆衰，又将何以定之？ | 又问：两个爻都旺或者都衰，又用什么来定？ | Again: if both lines thrive or both are weak, what decides? |
> | 以冲动者定之。 | 用被冲而动的来定。 | Decide by the one clashed into movement. |
> | 若又无冲，以生合扶起者定之。 | 如果又没有冲，用被生合扶起的来定。 | If there is no clash either, decide by the one raised by generation or combination. |
> | 或一旺一空，以不空者定之。 | 或者一旺一空，用不空的来定。 | Or if one thrives and one is Void, decide by the one that is not Void. |
> | 又不遇空，则以长生决法定之。 | 又不遇空，就用长生的办法来定。 | If neither is Void, decide by the Long Life method. |
> | 务归于一，则不杂乱。 | 务必归到一个，就不杂乱。 | Always come down to one, and then it is not confused. |

> | 阳男媒，阴女媒，以衰动旺静取之是也。 | 阳是男媒，阴是女媒，按衰的动、旺的静来取。 | Yang is a male go-between and yin a female one, chosen by taking the weak that moves and the thriving that is still. |

> [!note] 按
> 「长生决法」的「决」疑是「诀」，书里没有说这个办法怎么用，本库不补。
>
> *Note:* 'Chang sheng jue fa' may be a miscopy for 'the Long Life rule'; the book does not say how it is applied, and this library does not add it.
