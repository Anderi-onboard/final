# 断妻妾：以本宫内卦出现的妻财为主，不现看伏神、再不现取飞宫；世克之爻为妻、妻克之爻为妾（自占以应爻为正妻）；阳宫阳爻旺相带吉神者貌丽行洁善主中馈，阴宫阴爻衰墓带刑刃亡劫者丑陋无能夭折

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 认妻妾：以本宫内卦上出现的妻财爻为主；不出现，看内卦的伏神；再不出现、又没有伏神，就取飞宫来论。世爻所克的爻是妻，妻爻所克的爻是妾；自己占的，也可以把应爻当正妻，应爻所克的爻当妾。财爻临阳宫阳爻、旺相又带吉神的，一定相貌秀丽、品行贞洁，善于持家；临阴宫阴爻、衰弱入墓又带刑、刃、亡神、劫煞的，一定丑陋无能而且短命。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Identifying wives and concubines: take the Wealth line that appears in the inner trigram of the home palace as the main one; if it does not appear, look at the hidden spirit of the inner trigram; if it neither appears nor is hidden, reckon from the flying palace. The line controlled by the Self line is the wife, and the line controlled by the wife's line is the concubine; in a divination for oneself one may also take the Other line as the principal wife and the line it controls as the concubine. Wealth on a yang palace and yang line, strong and with auspicious spirits: she is surely beautiful and chaste in conduct and good at running the house; on a yin palace and yin line, weak, in a tomb and with the punishment, blade, Loss and Robbery stars: she is surely ugly and incapable, and dies young.

> [!note] 按
> 「中馈」指家中的饮食，「善主中馈」译作「善于持家」。「亡劫」是亡神、劫煞的合称。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhong kui' means the household meals; 'good at running the household kitchen' stands for good housekeeping. 'Loss and Robbery' are the Wangshen and Jiesha stars.
