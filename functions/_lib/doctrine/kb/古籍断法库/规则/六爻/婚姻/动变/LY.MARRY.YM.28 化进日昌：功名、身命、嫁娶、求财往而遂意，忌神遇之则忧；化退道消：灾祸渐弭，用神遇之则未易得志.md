# 化进日昌：功名、身命、嫁娶、求财往而遂意，忌神遇之则忧；化退道消：灾祸渐弭，用神遇之则未易得志

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 动爻变出进神叫「化进」，势头一天比一天旺：占功名得志、身命荣昌，占嫁娶、求财，去做就称心如意；但如果化进的是忌神，忧患就大了。变出退神叫「化退」，势头在消退：灾祸渐渐平息，官司衰弱、病退、盗贼不来侵犯；但如果化退的是用神，就不容易得志。作者小注：用神、元神要它进，忌神、仇神要它退。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A moving line that changes into an advancing spirit is 'changing forward' and grows stronger day by day: in office and fame one gets one's wish, life and fate flourish, and in marriage and in seeking wealth one goes and it turns out as wished; but if it is the Adversary that changes forward, trouble is great. A line that changes into a retreating spirit is 'changing backward' and is on the wane: calamities gradually ease, lawsuits weaken, illness retreats and robbers do not come near; but if it is the Subject that changes backward, it is not easy to get one's wish. The author's small note: the Subject and its Support want to advance, the Adversary and its enemy spirit want to retreat.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「进神」「退神」在这一章开头定义：顺着天行往前的是进神，逆着往后的是退神，亥变子是前进，未变辰是退后；只有同一五行之内才论进退（申变未是生、丑变卯是死，不论进退）。这两句里的「嫁娶」和「财求」是两件事：婚嫁，和求财；不是「为了婚嫁去求财」。
>
> *Note:* 'Advancing spirit' and 'retreating spirit' are defined at the head of this chapter: going forward with the heavens is advancing, going backward against them is retreating; Hai changing into Zi is forward, Wei changing into Chen is backward; advance and retreat are judged only within the same phase (Shen into Wei is 'birth' and Chou into Mao is 'death', and neither is judged as advance or retreat). 'Marriage' and 'seeking wealth' in these lines are two matters, marrying and seeking money, and not 'seeking money for the sake of a marriage'.
