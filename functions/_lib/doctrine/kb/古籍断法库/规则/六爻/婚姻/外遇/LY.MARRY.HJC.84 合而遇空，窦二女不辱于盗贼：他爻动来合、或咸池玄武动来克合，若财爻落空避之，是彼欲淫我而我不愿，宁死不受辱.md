# 合而遇空，窦二女不辱于盗贼：他爻动来合、或咸池玄武动来克合，若财爻落空避之，是彼欲淫我而我不愿，宁死不受辱

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中如果有别的爻动来相合，或者咸池、玄武动来克合，本不是好兆头；要是财爻落在空亡里避开了，就是对方想污辱我而我不愿意的样子，像唐朝奉天的窦氏两个女儿，被强盗劫住，跳崖也不肯受辱。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If another line moves to combine with it in the hexagram, or the Xianchi star or the Dark Tortoise moves to control the combination, it is not a good sign in itself; but if the Wealth line falls into Void and avoids it, it is the picture of the other wanting to defile me and my being unwilling, like the two daughters of the Dou family at Fengtian in Tang, seized by bandits, who leapt off a cliff rather than be shamed.
