# 子孙发动中途必见伤夫，兄弟交重异日终须失妇：子孙宜静不宜动，动而带贵妻夺夫权，鬼旺不受克加天寡凶杀必伤夫；兄弟克财发动必丧偶

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙发动，中途会伤夫；兄弟交重，日后要丧妻。子孙是福德，卦里不能没有，宜静不宜动：动而带贵人、临吉神，是妻夺夫权；官鬼旺而不受克，再加天寡和凶煞，一定伤夫；旺相来得急，休囚来得缓。兄弟克财，发动必丧偶；有官鬼动，或者日辰冲破它，才能化解。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Output moves, the husband will surely be harmed midway; when Peer moves, the wife will be lost in the end. Output is fortune and virtue and cannot be absent from the hexagram, but it should be still and not move: moving with the Noble and a lucky spirit, it is the wife seizing the husband's authority; if Pressure is strong and uncontrolled and adds the Heavenly Widow star and fierce stars, it surely harms the husband; strong, it comes quickly, resting or trapped, slowly. Peer controls Wealth, and if it moves the spouse is surely lost; only if Pressure moves, or the Day clashes and breaks it, can it be resolved.
