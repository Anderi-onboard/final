# 官鬼不宜持世，求名婚娶两相宜：官鬼持世一般不吉，独有求名和婚娶两件合宜

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼一般不宜持世，只有求功名和婚娶这两件事，官鬼持世反而合宜。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure should not generally hold the Self line, but for seeking office and for marrying alike it is fitting.

## 《天玄赋·总论提纲》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4104 行 · Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "The opening summary", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼不宜持世， | 官鬼不宜持世， | Pressure should not hold the Self line, |
> | 求名婚娶两相宜； | 求名婚娶两相宜； | but for seeking office and for marrying both are fitting; |

^q1

> [!warning] 打架
> 同样是官鬼持世，《卜筮全书》的六亲持世歌说「占婚问病俱凶兆」（LY.MARRY.BSQS.09），这里说婚娶「两相宜」。两处都是口诀，没有注，书里没有裁断。《增删卜易》分男女：女占男，官星持世、合世、生世且旺相是良缘（LY.MARRY.ZS.11）；男占女，官鬼是克我的，不是妻，不看它。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For the same Pressure line holding Self, the rhyme on the six relations holding the Self in the Bushi Quanshu says 'for marriage or illness both are ill omens' (LY.MARRY.BSQS.09), while this verse says marrying is 'fitting'. Both are bare rhymes with no note, and the books do not decide between them. The Zengshan Buyi divides by sex: when a woman divines about a man, a Pressure line that holds, combines with or generates the Self line and is strong makes a good match (LY.MARRY.ZS.11); when a man divines about a woman, Pressure is what controls him and is not the wife, and is not looked at.

> [!note] 按
> 这一句夹在总论提纲的一长串六亲、六神口诀里（白虎动、子孙兴、官鬼持世、妻财扶身、父母文书、玄武、螣蛇、朱雀、勾陈），前后各句与婚姻无关，没有收。
>
> *Note:* The line sits in a long run of rhymes on the six relations and six spirits in the opening summary (White Tiger moving, Output rising, Pressure holding Self, Wealth supporting the Body, Resource and documents, the Dark Tortoise, the Coiling Snake, the Vermilion Bird, Hook Earth); the neighbouring lines have nothing to do with marriage and are not included.
