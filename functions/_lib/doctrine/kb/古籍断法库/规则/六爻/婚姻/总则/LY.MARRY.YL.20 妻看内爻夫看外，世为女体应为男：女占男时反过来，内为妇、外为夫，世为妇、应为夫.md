# 妻看内爻夫看外，世为女体应为男：女占男时反过来，内为妇、外为夫，世为妇、应为夫

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看妻子看内爻，看丈夫看外爻；世爻是女方本人，应爻是男方。女子占男方，反过来取内卦为妇、外卦为夫，又以世爻为妇、应爻为夫。也就是说，占卦的人这一边总是内卦和世爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For the wife look at the inner lines, for the husband at the outer; the Self line is the woman herself and the Other line the man. When a woman divines about a man, take the inner as the wife and the outer as the husband, the reverse of the usual, and the Self line as the wife and the Other line as the husband. That is, the divining party's own side is always the inner trigram and the Self line.

> [!note] 按
> 最后一句「占卦的人这一边总是内卦和世爻」是把上一章（男占：内为夫、世为夫）和这一段（女占：内为妇、世为妇）放在一起得出的概括，原文没有这句话；它是这条规则的译述，不是引文。
>
> *Note:* The last sentence, that the divining party's own side is always the inner trigram and the Self line, is a summary drawn by putting the previous chapter (a man divining: inner is husband, Self is husband) beside this passage (a woman divining: inner is wife, Self is wife); it is not in the original, and is our paraphrase rather than a quotation.
