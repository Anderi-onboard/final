# 子卯夫妻，日下当遭反目：子卯相刑，夫妻当天就反目；辰戌相冲，兄弟当月就阋墙

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> （夫妻两爻是）子和卯，互相刑，当天就会夫妻反目；（兄弟两爻是）辰和戌，互相冲，当月就一定兄弟争吵。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the husband and wife lines are Zi and Mao, punishing each other, husband and wife fall out that very day; if the brother lines are Chen and Xu, clashing each other, brothers surely quarrel within the month.

## 《黄金策·家宅秘诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 9709 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Secrets of the household", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子卯夫妻， | 子和卯做夫妻， | Zi and Mao as husband and wife: |
> | 日下当遭反目； | 当天就会夫妻反目； | on that very day they fall out; |
> | 辰戌兄弟， | 辰和戌做兄弟， | Chen and Xu as brothers (the Peer lines): |
> | 月中定见阋墙。 | 这个月里一定兄弟争吵。 | within the month they surely quarrel. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 子卯相刑（无礼之刑）、辰戌相冲，是用地支之间的刑冲来断夫妻、兄弟不和；原文没有解释，也没有说「夫妻」「兄弟」在卦里是哪两爻，只能照字面译。「日下」是当日，「月中」是当月。
>
> *Note:* Zi and Mao punish each other (the punishment of rudeness) and Chen and Xu clash, so the branches' punishment and clash are used to judge discord between husband and wife and between brothers; the text does not explain this or say which two lines stand for husband and wife or for brothers in the hexagram, and is translated as written. 'Under the day' is that day and 'in the month' is that month.
