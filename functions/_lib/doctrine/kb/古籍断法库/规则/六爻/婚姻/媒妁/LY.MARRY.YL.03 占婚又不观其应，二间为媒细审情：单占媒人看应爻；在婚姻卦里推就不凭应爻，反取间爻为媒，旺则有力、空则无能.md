# 占婚又不观其应，二间为媒细审情：单占媒人看应爻；在婚姻卦里推就不凭应爻，反取间爻为媒，旺则有力、空则无能

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚时不看应爻，把两个间爻当作媒人，仔细审察实情。只占媒人时看应爻；要是在婚姻卦里推，就不凭应爻，反过来取间爻当媒人：旺就有力，空就无能。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a marriage divination one does not look at the Other line; take the two between lines as the go-betweens and examine the facts closely. When only the go-between is divined, look at the Other line; but when reckoning within a marriage hexagram, do not rely on the Other line and take the between lines for the go-between instead: strong means capable, Void means incapable.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 512 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 单占有月老，当看应爻，如在婚姻卦内推之，又不凭其应象，反取间爻为婚，旺须有力，空则无能。 | 单独占媒人，要看应爻；如果是在婚姻卦里推，就不凭应爻的象，反过来取间爻来看婚事，旺就有力，空就无能。 | When divining the go-between alone, look at the Other line; but when reckoning within a marriage hexagram, do not rely on the sign of the Other line and take the between lines instead: strong means capable, Void means incapable. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 原文最后一句「反取间爻为婚」，按上下文「婚」应是「媒」之误（同一段前面说「单占月老看应爻」）；译文按「媒」的意思译。
>
> *Note:* The last sentence reads 'take the between lines for the match', but from the context the word 'match' should be 'go-between' (the same passage says a go-between alone is read from the Other line); it is translated in the sense of go-between.
