# 阳宫端正、阴宫丑陋，落在飞神上应头面，卦无子孙不喜悦

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 阳宫的人端正，阴宫的人丑陋；落在飞神上，只应在头面；落在飞神……不应在四肢。卦里没有子孙爻，不喜悦。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A yang palace means upright looks, a yin palace ugly ones: on the flying spirit it answers only in the head and face; on the flying spirit... it does not answer in the limbs. A hexagram without an Output line is not a happy one.

## 《海底眼·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 599 行 · Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 阳宫端正，阴宫丑陋，在飞止应头面，在飞不应四肢，卦无子孙不喜悦。 | 阳宫端正，阴宫丑陋，落在飞神上只应在头面，落在飞神（下）不应在四肢，卦里没有子孙爻，不喜悦。 | A yang palace is upright, a yin palace ugly; on the flying spirit it answers only in the head and face, on the flying spirit (below) it does not answer in the limbs; a hexagram without an Output line is not a happy one. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「在飞止应头面，在飞不应四肢」两个「在飞」不同义，读不通。《火珠林》同一句作「在飞上应头面四肢，在飞下应拙不稳」（LY.MARRY.HZL.04），这里的第二个「在飞」像是漏了「下」字；译文照原文译，括号里注明。
>
> *Note:* The two 'on the flying spirit' in 'on the flying spirit it answers only in the head and face; on the flying spirit it does not answer in the limbs' cannot both mean the same, and the sentence does not read through. The Huozhulin has the same sentence as 'on the flying spirit it answers in the head, face and limbs, and under it, in clumsiness' (LY.MARRY.HZL.04), so the second 'on the flying spirit' looks as if the word 'below' has dropped out; it is translated as written, with the doubt marked in brackets.
