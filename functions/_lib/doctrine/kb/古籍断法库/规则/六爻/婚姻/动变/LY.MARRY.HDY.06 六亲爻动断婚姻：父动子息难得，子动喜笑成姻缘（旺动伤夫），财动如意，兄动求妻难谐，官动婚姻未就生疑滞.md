# 六亲爻动断婚姻：父动子息难得，子动喜笑成姻缘（旺动伤夫），财动如意，兄动求妻难谐，官动婚姻未就生疑滞

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《海底眼》的「六亲爻动」一节，给每一种六亲爻发动列了一首诗，每首诗里有一句是断婚姻的：父母爻动，婚姻和子息都难得（子孙变成父母，被克）；子孙爻动，婚姻喜悦是一段好姻缘（但小注说：旺而动，伤丈夫）；妻财爻动，婚姻称心如意；兄弟爻动，求娶妻子难以和谐；官鬼爻动，婚姻还没有成，生出疑虑阻滞。每首诗的其余各句讲病、官司、买卖、行人，这里不收。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Haidi Yan's section on 'the relatives moving' gives each relative a poem, and each poem has one line for marriage: Resource moving, marriage and children are hard to get (Output changes into Resource and is controlled); Output moving, marriage is glad and a fated match (but the small note says: strong and moving, it harms the husband); Wealth moving, marriage is as wished; Peer moving, seeking a wife is hard to bring to harmony; Pressure moving, the marriage is not yet made and doubt and hindrance arise. The other lines of each poem speak of illness, lawsuits, trade and travellers and are not included here.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这五句和同一本书别处的说法有出入。第一，子孙爻动：这里说「喜笑是姻缘」，而本书婚姻章（LY.MARRY.HDY.01）说「子旺伤夫夫有疑」，小注「旺动伤夫」把两边并在一起，没有再往下分；第二，妻财爻动：这里说「如意」，本书别处说占妻看财要静（LY.MARRY.HDY.07），《火珠林》也说妻财独发「必克翁姑」（LY.MARRY.HZL.27）。书里没有把这几处说圆。
>
> *Where they disagree:* These five lines do not all agree with the rest of the same book. First, for Output moving, this says 'glad and a fated match', while the marriage chapter of this book (LY.MARRY.HDY.01) says 'a strong Output line harms the husband and he is in doubt'; the small note 'strong and moving harms the husband' sets the two side by side and goes no further. Second, for Wealth moving, this says 'as wished', while elsewhere in the book the Subject line for taking a wife should be still (LY.MARRY.HDY.07), and the Huozhulin says a Wealth line moving alone 'must harm the parents-in-law' (LY.MARRY.HZL.27). The book does not reconcile them.

> [!note] 按
> 每一首里括号里的小注是书里自己的夹注，和正文写在一起，所以引文里带着它们。兄弟爻动那一首的头一句是「兄弟同人克了财」（兄动克财，小注说「同」是同类），它是「求妻买婢事难谐」的缘由，但中间隔着病、应举两句，没有一起收。这一组诗在别的书里也有，字句略有出入：《断易天机》（LY.MARRY.DYTJ.12 至 DYTJ.16）、《卜筮大全》第 3147 行起、《卜筮正宗》第 254 行起、《易隐》第 50 行起、《火珠林》第 1008 行起。《海底眼》这一本的独异之处是给每一句都加了小注，并且兄弟爻动那一首里有「求妻买婢事难谐」一句，《火珠林》的同一首里没有。
>
> *Note:* The bracketed small notes in each poem are the book's own interlinear notes, written together with the text, so they are carried in the quotations. The first line of the Peer poem is 'a Peer moving controls Wealth' (the note says 'same' means the same kind), which is the reason for 'seeking a wife or buying a maid is hard to harmonize'; but two lines on illness and examinations lie between them, and they are not taken together. The same set of poems appears in other books with small differences: the Duanyi Tianji (LY.MARRY.DYTJ.12 to DYTJ.16), the Bushi Daquan from line 3147, the Bushi Zhengzong from line 254, the Yi Yin from line 50 and the Huozhulin from line 1008. What is peculiar to the Haidi Yan is that it adds a small note to every line, and that its Peer poem has the line 'seeking a wife or buying a maid is hard to bring to harmony', which the Huozhulin's poem does not have.
