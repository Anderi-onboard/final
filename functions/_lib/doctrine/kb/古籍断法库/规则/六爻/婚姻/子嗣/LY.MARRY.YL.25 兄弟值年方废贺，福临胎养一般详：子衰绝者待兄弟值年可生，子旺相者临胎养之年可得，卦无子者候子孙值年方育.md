# 兄弟值年方废贺，福临胎养一般详：子衰绝者待兄弟值年可生，子旺相者临胎养之年可得，卦无子者候子孙值年方育

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻衰、绝的，要等兄弟爻值年才可能生；子孙爻旺相的，到胎、养的年份可以得子；卦里没有子孙爻的，要等子孙爻值年才能生育。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Output line is weak or extinguished, wait for the year ruled by a Peer line for a birth; if it is strong, a child can be had in the year of its Womb or Nurture stage; if the hexagram has no Output line, wait for the year ruled by Output for a birth.

## 《易林补遗·婚姻嫁娶章第十·后嗣》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 567 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 10: marriage and giving in marriage (heirs)", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟值年方废贺，福临胎养一般详。 | 兄弟爻值年，才有喜可贺；福德爻临胎、养的年份，道理一样。 | When the Peer line rules the year, congratulations are due; when the Output line reaches the Womb or Nurture stage, examine it the same way. |

^q1

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 568 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子如衰绝者，待兄弟值年可生。 | 子孙爻如果衰、绝，要等兄弟爻值年才可能生。 | If the Output line is weak or extinguished, wait for the year ruled by a Peer line for a birth. |
> | 子若旺相者，临胎养之年可得。 | 子孙爻如果旺相，到胎、养的年份可以得子。 | If the Output line is strong, a child can be had in the year of its Womb or Nurture stage. |
> | 卦若无子者，候子孙值年方育。 | 卦里如果没有子孙爻，要等子孙爻值年才能生育。 | If the hexagram has no Output line, wait for the year ruled by Output for a birth. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 诀里「废贺」两字与下面的解说对不上（解说说的是兄弟值年才可能生），多半是「发贺」之误；译文按解说的意思译。解说后面还有三个卦例（辛丑年问何年得子等），这里没有收。
>
> *Note:* The two words 'set aside congratulations' in the verse do not fit the explanation below (which says a birth can come in the year ruled by a Peer line), and are probably a slip for 'give congratulations'; it is translated in the sense of the explanation. Three cases follow the explanation (a divination in the Xinchou year on when a son would come, and so on), and are not included here.
