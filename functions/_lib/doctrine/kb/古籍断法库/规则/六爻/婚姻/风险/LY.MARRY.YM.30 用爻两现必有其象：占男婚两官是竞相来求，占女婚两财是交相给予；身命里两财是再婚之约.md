# 用爻两现必有其象：占男婚两官是竞相来求，占女婚两财是交相给予；身命里两财是再婚之约

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 同一种用爻在卦里现出两个，推到人事上一定有对应的象。占男子的婚事，卦里有两个官鬼，是有人竞相来求；占女子的婚事，卦里有两个妻财，是彼此交相给予。占身命时，两个妻财是再订一门婚约。作者的理由是：鬼神对蓍草不乱动、不白白现出来，现出来就一定有话要告诉人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the same Subject line appears twice in a hexagram, extended to human affairs it must have a matching image. Divining a man's marriage, two Pressure lines mean people are vying to seek her; divining a woman's marriage, two Wealth lines mean they give to one another in turn. In divining life and fate, two Wealth lines are a second marriage contract. The author's reason: spirits and gods do not move idly through the yarrow or appear for nothing, and when they appear they always have something to tell.

> [!note] 按
> 「两官」「两财」是卦里同时有两个官鬼爻、两个妻财爻。「交相与」书里没有说是谁给谁（可能是两家互送聘礼、也可能是两边各给一份），译文只照字面译。第二条引的是身命章里的一句：那里一连列了两官、两父、两身、两财、两子孙、两兄弟各主什么，只有「两财者再婚之约」这一句和婚姻有关，别的没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Two Pressure' and 'two Wealth' mean that the hexagram has two Pressure lines or two Wealth lines at once. The book does not say who gives to whom in 'give to one another' (two families exchanging betrothal gifts, or each side giving a share); the translation follows the words. The second quotation is one sentence of the chapter on life and fate, which lists in a row what two Pressure, two Resource, two Self, two Wealth, two Output and two Peer lines each mean; only 'two Wealth lines are a second marriage contract' concerns marriage, and the rest is not taken.
