# 财官冲克，反招就里愆尤：间爻被日辰动爻刑冲克害，媒人被两家埋怨；世爻冲克男家怨、应爻冲克女家怨

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻如果被日辰、动爻刑冲克害，那个媒人一定被两家怨恨：世爻冲克，男家有怨；应爻冲克，女家有怨；或者世来生合而财爻冲克，男家虽吉，日后女家一定怨；财、官都冲克，夫妻双方都有怨。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the between line is punished, clashed, controlled or harmed by the Day or a moving line, the go-between is surely resented by both families: the Self line clashing or controlling, the man's family resents; the Other line clashing or controlling, the woman's family resents; or if the Self generates or combines while the Wealth line clashes or controls, the man's family is fine but later the woman's family will resent; if Wealth and Pressure both clash or control, both husband and wife resent.
