# 反吟有冲有克重于伏吟，两者都要看用神旺衰：伏吟用神旺相则冲开之年月志伸，休囚则不过忧郁；不可一概以反伏为畏途

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 伏吟和反吟比，反吟有冲有克，用神再受克，祸不轻。伏吟的卦，用神旺相，在冲开的年月，心志得以伸展；用神休囚，在冲开的年月，不过是忧郁罢了。李我平说：《易林补遗》讲「爻有伏吟不吉」「卦有反吟最凶」，是不看用神的旺衰，竟把反吟、伏吟当成要躲开的路；野鹤的办法是看用神，教人多么透彻明快。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Compared with the Reversed Chant, the Hidden Chant is the lighter, since the Reversed Chant has clash and control, and when the Subject is controlled the misfortune is not slight. In a Hidden Chant hexagram, if the Subject is strong, then in the year or month when it is clashed open one's aims are carried out; if the Subject is resting or trapped, then in that year or month there is only gloom. Li Woping says the Yilin Buyi's 'a line with a Hidden Chant is not auspicious' and 'a hexagram with a Reversed Chant is the worst' do not look at whether the Subject is strong or weak and simply treat Reversed and Hidden Chant as roads to be dreaded; the Old Man of Yehe's way, judging by the Subject, is thoroughly clear and incisive.

> [!warning] 打架
> 《易林补遗》把反吟、伏吟一概当成凶：「爻有伏吟不吉」「卦有反吟最凶」，别处还说「伏吟反吟犯者，急须回避」（见该书第 54、332 行）；增删卜易看用神旺衰：反吟重在有冲有克、用神受克，伏吟用神旺相时冲开之年月反而志伸。李我平明确站在野鹤一边。他引的《易林补遗》原文是「术者未开」「星家谁觉」，和这里的「术者未闻」「卜家谁觉」各差一个字。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The Yilin Buyi takes Reversed and Hidden Chant as ill without condition: 'a line with a Hidden Chant is not auspicious', 'a hexagram with a Reversed Chant is the worst', and elsewhere 'those who meet Hidden or Reversed Chant must hurry to avoid it' (lines 54 and 332 of that book); the Zengshan Buyi looks at whether the Subject is strong: a Reversed Chant is heavy through clash and control and a controlled Subject, and a Hidden Chant with a strong Subject actually carries out its aims in the year or month it is clashed open. Li Woping sides plainly with the Old Man of Yehe. The Yilin Buyi's own words are 'no practitioner has opened it' and 'which astrologer has noticed', each differing by one character from the 'has heard' and 'which diviner' quoted here.

> [!note] 按
> 李我平提到的两个例子在同一章里：比之井是占跟着做官的人上任（书中第 2405 行起），临之中孚是占升迁（第 2450 行起）。这两卦都不是婚姻，放在这里是因为它们是本章讲「反伏不可一概畏」的依据。
>
> *Note:* The two cases Li Woping mentions are in the same chapter: Bi into Jing is a divination on going with an official to his post, and Lin into Zhongfu is a divination on promotion. Neither is about marriage; they are given here because they are this chapter's grounds for saying Reversed and Hidden Chant are not to be feared without condition.
