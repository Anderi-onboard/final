# 世为男家应为女宅，世旺应衰女贫男富、世衰应旺女富男贫；卦旺官衰男富而夫貌不充、卦衰官旺夫宅贫而男容秀；卦旺财衰女舍丰而姿欠美、卦衰财旺女家寒而色精妍；官喜生不喜绝、财宜旺不宜空

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻代表男方的家，应爻代表女方的家；世旺应衰是男方富、女方贫，世衰应旺是女方富、男方贫。鬼爻所在的卦是男家，财爻所在的宫是女宅：卦旺而官衰，男家富而丈夫相貌不足；卦衰而官旺，夫家穷而男子秀气；卦旺而财衰，女方家里丰足而姿容欠美；卦衰而财旺，女方家里寒素而肤色如雪、精致。官鬼爻是丈夫，喜生不喜绝，绝了丈夫亡故；财爻是妻子，宜旺不宜空，空了妻子亡故。鬼爻空，丈夫无远寿；财爻陷，妻子不高寿。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line stands for the man's household and the Other line for the woman's: with the Self strong and the Other weak the man's side is rich and the woman's poor; with the Self weak and the Other strong the woman's side is rich and the man's poor. The trigram where Pressure sits is the man's family and the palace where Wealth sits is the woman's house: with the trigram strong and Pressure weak, the man's family is rich but the husband's looks are lacking; with the trigram weak and Pressure strong, the husband's house is poor but the man is handsome; with the trigram strong and Wealth weak, the woman's home is plentiful but her looks are not fair; with the trigram weak and Wealth strong, the woman's family is poor but her complexion is snow-white and refined. The Pressure line is the husband: it likes generation and not extinction, and if extinguished the husband dies; the Wealth line is the wife: it should be strong and not Void, and if Void the wife dies. If Pressure is Void the husband will not live long; if Wealth has fallen the wife will not reach a great age.

> [!note] 按
> 有两处字看着是转录之误：「世作男安」的「安」多半是「家」，「雪色精姘」的「姘」多半是「妍」。译文按这个意思译。
>
> *Note:* Two characters look like transcription slips: 'an' in 'the Self line stands for the man's peace' is most likely 'jia' (household), and 'pin' in 'snow-white and refined' is most likely 'yan' (fair). They are translated in that sense.
