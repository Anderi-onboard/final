# 子云后代，遇绝无儿：子孙宜静不宜动（动则刑夫）、宜旺不宜空（空则无子）；临绝又主无儿，绝中得生扶反主儿孙多

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻代表后代，遇到绝就没有儿子。子孙爻宜静、不宜动，动了刑克丈夫；宜旺、不宜空，空了没有儿子；落在绝地又没有儿子；绝中如果得到生扶，反而儿孙多育。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Output line is the descendants, and meeting extinction there is no son. The Output line should be still and not moving, since moving it punishes the husband; it should be strong and not Void, since Void means no son; in a place of extinction, again no son; but if in extinction it receives generation and support, on the contrary there are many sons and grandsons.
