# 官强而又连龙福，吴孟子夫主贤明：鬼爻旺相更逢青龙福德、禄马贵人，主有贵显贤明之夫；衰而逢助亦有贤明之夫

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻旺相，又碰上青龙、福德、禄马、贵人这些吉神，一定有显贵贤明的丈夫，像吴孟子嫁给鲁昭公；官鬼衰弱但得到生扶，也有贤明的丈夫。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Pressure line is strong and also meets lucky spirits such as the Azure Dragon, Output, the Emolument and Horse stars and the Noble, she surely has an eminent, wise husband, like Wu Mengzi who married Duke Zhao of Lu; if Pressure is weak but supported, she also has a wise husband.

## 《黄金策·身命》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6199 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Person and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官强而又连龙福，吴孟子夫主贤明。 | 官鬼强而又连着青龙、福德，吴孟子的丈夫贤明。 | Pressure strong and joined to the Azure Dragon and Output: Wu Mengzi's husband was wise. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 吴孟子是鲁昭公的夫人，吴国人，姓姬，因同姓不婚才不称「吴姬」而称「孟子」。卜筮正宗的「临量」疑是「临旺」之误，译文按「强」并照旺相解。
>
> *Note:* Wu Mengzi was the wife of Duke Zhao of Lu, from the state of Wu; she was of the Ji surname, and because marriage within a surname was avoided she was called 'Mengzi' and not 'Wu Ji'. The Zhengzong's 'lin liang' looks like a slip for 'lin wang' (sitting on strength), and is taken as 'strong'.
