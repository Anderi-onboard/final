# 六爻俱静类：卦遇六爻安静须看用与日辰，日辰克用及冲刑宜谨慎，世应临用神及原神则昌盛；娶妻买妾用爻宜静、有气为先

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六爻全都安静的卦，要看用神和日辰：日辰克用神或者冲刑它，事情要谨慎；再推究世应，忌神千万不要加临，世应临用神和原神，做事一定昌盛。娶妻买妾、屋宅坟田、住庵谒人、失物理冤这些事，用爻宜静，有气为先。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When all six lines of a hexagram are still, look at the Subject and the Day: if the Day controls, clashes with or punishes the Subject, the matter calls for caution; then examine Self and Other: the Adversary must on no account come upon them, and if Self and Other sit on the Subject and the Support spirit, the undertaking will certainly flourish. For taking a wife or buying a concubine, houses and graves, living in a hermitage or calling on people, lost property and righting a wrong, the Subject line should be still and having qi comes first.
