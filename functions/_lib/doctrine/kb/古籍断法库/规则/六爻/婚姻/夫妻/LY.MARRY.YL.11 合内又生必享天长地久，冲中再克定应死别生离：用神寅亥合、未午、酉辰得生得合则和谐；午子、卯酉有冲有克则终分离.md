# 合内又生必享天长地久，冲中再克定应死别生离：用神寅亥合、未午、酉辰得生得合则和谐；午子、卯酉有冲有克则终分离

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 合中又有生，一定享到天长地久；冲中又加克，一定应在死别生离。比如用神寅与亥合，用神未逢午，用神酉见辰，都是得生得合，自然极其和谐；又如用神午见子，卯酉相冲，就是有冲有克，终究分离。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A combination with a generation inside it surely enjoys heaven-long, earth-lasting happiness; a clash with a control inside it surely answers in death or parting alive. For example, if the Subject is Yin and combines with Hai, or the Subject is Wei and Wu comes, or the Subject is You and meets Chen, all are cases of gaining generation and combination, and naturally there is harmony a million-fold; again, if the Subject is Wu and meets Zi, or Mao and You clash, there is clash and control, and this means separation in the end.
