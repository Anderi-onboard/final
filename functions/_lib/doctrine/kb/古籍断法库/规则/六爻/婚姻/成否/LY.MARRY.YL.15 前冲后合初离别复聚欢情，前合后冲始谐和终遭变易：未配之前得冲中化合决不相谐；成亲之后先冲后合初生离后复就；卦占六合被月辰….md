# 前冲后合初离别复聚欢情，前合后冲始谐和终遭变易：未配之前得冲中化合决不相谐；成亲之后先冲后合初生离后复就；卦占六合被月辰冲开或化六冲，始欢悦后不和

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 先冲后合，起初离别，后来又聚欢情；先合后冲，起初和谐，最终遭变易。还没婚配之前，如果卜得冲中化合，一定不能相谐；成亲之后，如果得到先冲后合，起初见生离，后来还会重新合好。卦占得六合，被月建日辰冲开，或世应财官变成六冲，都主起初欢悦，后来一定不和谐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* First a clash and then a combination: at first parting, then joy gathered again; first a combination and then a clash: harmony at the start and change in the end. Before the match is made, if the divination gives a clash turning into a combination, it surely will not be harmonious; after the wedding, if it gives first a clash and then a combination, there is at first parting alive and afterwards they come together again. If the hexagram is a Six-combine that is clashed open by the Month or Day, or the Self, Other, Wealth and Pressure change into a Six-clash, all mean joy at the start and surely disharmony afterwards.

> [!warning] 打架
> 还没成亲时得到冲变合：这里说「决不相谐」；《增删卜易》（觉子）说「六冲变六合者尤吉，求婚者目下不允，久必成之」（见 LY.MARRY.ZS.13）——两家在这一种卦上判得相反。成亲以后先冲后合、起初生离后来重聚，两家看法一致。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For a clash turning into a combination obtained before the wedding this text says 'it surely will not be harmonious', while the Zengshan Buyi (Juezi) says 'a Six-clash changing into a Six-combine is better still: for one seeking a marriage, although refused for now, it will be made in time' (see LY.MARRY.ZS.13): the two books judge this kind of hexagram in opposite ways. For a couple already wed, first a clash and then a combination, parting at first and reunion after, the two agree.

> [!note] 按
> 第一句原文作「前冲后台」，「台」是「合」的转录之误；引用原字，译文按「合」译。
>
> *Note:* The first line reads 'first clash, then platform', where 'platform' is a transcription slip for 'combination'; the character is quoted as it stands and translated as 'combination'.
