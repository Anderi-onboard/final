# 如日合而世应比和，因人成事：世生应男求女、应生世女求男；日辰合爻定谁赞成；间爻动合是媒人

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世生应，是男方求女方；应生世，是女方求男方；相合也一样。世应比和，是两家互相成全。想知是谁赞成，看日辰合到哪一爻；间爻发动、与日辰相合，就是媒人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self generating the Other means the man is seeking the woman; the Other generating the Self means the woman is seeking the man; combining is the same. The Self and Other in harmony means the two families are helping each other. To know who approves, see which line the Day combines with; a between line that moves and combines with the Day is the go-between.
