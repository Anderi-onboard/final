# 玄武桃花犯命中，荒淫酒色：身命带玄武、桃花（咸池），主贪酒色，男则粘红缀绿、女则被人牵惹，男女同论

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 身命带玄武、桃花（咸池），主贪图酒色：男子是在花丛里打转，女子是被人牵引招惹；男女一样论。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Body or Life carries the Dark Tortoise and the Peach Blossom star (the Xianchi star), it means greed for wine and women: a man is one who dallies among the reds and greens, a woman one who is drawn in and led on by others; man and woman are judged alike.

## 《黄金策·家宅》原文

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注写「桃花」，王洪绪写「咸池」，是同一个神煞的两个名字。「粘红缀绿」「叶人牵惹」是形容男女沾染风月的说法，照字面译。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary writes 'Peach Blossom' and Wang Hongxu 'Xianchi', two names for the same star. The phrases about dallying among the reds and greens and being drawn in by others are idioms for getting entangled in romance, translated literally.
