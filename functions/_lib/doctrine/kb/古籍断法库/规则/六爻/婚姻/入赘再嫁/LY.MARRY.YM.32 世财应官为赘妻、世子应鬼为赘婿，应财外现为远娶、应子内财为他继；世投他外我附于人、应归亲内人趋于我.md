# 世财应官为赘妻、世子应鬼为赘婿，应财外现为远娶、应子内财为他继；世投他外我附于人、应归亲内人趋于我

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 家宅章末尾，讲一家人的构成时列了几种和婚姻有关的组合：世爻投到外面，是我依附别人；应爻回到亲族里面，是别人来投靠我。世爻是妻财、应爻是官鬼，叫赘妻；世爻是子孙、应爻是官鬼，叫赘婿；应爻是妻财而现在外卦，是从远处娶来；应爻是子孙而妻财在内，是外姓人来继承。另外白虎在应爻上发吼，妻子那一边有丧事；青龙在旁边的爻上跃动，偏房那一支有喜事。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* At the end of the chapter on the household, in speaking of how a family is made up, the book lists several combinations that touch marriage: when Self is cast outward, I attach myself to another; when Other returns inward among kin, others come to me. Wealth on Self with Pressure on Other is a 'zhui' wife; Output on Self with Pressure on Other is a 'zhui' son-in-law; Wealth on Other showing in the outer trigram is a wife taken from afar; Other as Output with Wealth inside is a successor from another family. Besides, the White Tiger roaring on Other means a death on the wife's side, and the Azure Dragon leaping on a side line means joy in the secondary branch.

> [!note] 按
> 原文极简，「赘妻」「赘婿」没有解释。「赘」是男子住到妻家、依附妻家（入赘），「赘婿」是这样住进来的女婿；「赘妻」在这里的确切意思书里没有说，译文保留原词。「虎」「龙」是白虎、青龙，「应象」是应爻的位置，「傍爻」是世应之外的爻。这一段是家宅章里讲家里人口的话，里面「两官一家二宰、两父一宅二居、两财有异业」等讲家宅的句子没有收。
>
> *Note:* The original is very terse and does not explain 'zhui wife' or 'zhui son-in-law'. 'Zhui' is a man who goes to live in his wife's family and depends on it (uxorilocal marriage), and a 'zhui son-in-law' is a son-in-law taken in that way; what exactly a 'zhui wife' is here the book does not say, and the translation keeps the word. 'Tiger' and 'Dragon' are the White Tiger and the Azure Dragon, 'the Other's image' is the place of the Other line, and 'a side line' is a line other than Self and Other. The passage is part of what the household chapter says about the people of a house; its lines on the household itself ('two Pressure lines, one family with two masters; two Resource lines, one house with two dwellings; two Wealth lines, different trades') are not taken.
