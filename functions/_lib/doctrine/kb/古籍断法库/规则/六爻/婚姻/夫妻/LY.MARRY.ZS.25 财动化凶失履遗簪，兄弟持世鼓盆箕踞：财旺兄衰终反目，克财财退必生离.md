# 财动化凶失履遗簪，兄弟持世鼓盆箕踞：财旺兄衰终反目，克财财退必生离

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻动而化成凶象（化鬼、化回头克、化退神、化绝、化墓、化破、化空这一类），不管问什么，都是玉碎珠沉，有丧妻之叹。兄弟爻持世，或者兄弟爻在卦中发动，或者日月临兄弟爻来伤财，或者兄弟爻化成财爻，不管问妻问妾，都不长久。财旺兄衰，最终要反目；克财而财爻退，必定生离。兄弟爻持世而财爻旺的，克不了妻，但夫妻不和，见面如仇人；兄弟爻持世、财爻化成退神的，也是这样，不是反目就是生离。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Wealth moves and changes into ill (into Pressure, into a returning control, a Retreating spirit, extinction, tomb, break, Void and the like), whatever is asked, the jade is shattered and the pearl sunk, with the sigh of losing a wife. A Peer line on the Self line, or a moving Peer line in the hexagram, or the Day or Month being a Peer and harming Wealth, or a Peer changing into Wealth: whether one asks about a wife or a concubine, she will not live long. Wealth strong and the Peer weak, in the end the couple fall out; Wealth controlled and retreating surely means parting alive. A Peer line on the Self line with Wealth strong cannot kill the wife, but the couple are at odds and meet like enemies; a Peer line on the Self line with Wealth changing into a Retreating spirit means the same: either falling out or parting alive.

> [!note] 按
> 「终乘琴瑟」的「乘」疑是「乖」（不合）之误；原文照录，译文按「乖」译。「失履遗簪」「鼓盆箕踞」都是丧妻的典故。
>
> *Note:* In the phrase 'zhong cheng qin se' the word 'cheng' (ride) is probably a slip for 'guai' (at odds); it is kept as written and translated as 'at odds'. 'The lost shoe and dropped hairpin' and 'drumming on a basin and sitting sprawled' are both allusions to the loss of a wife.
