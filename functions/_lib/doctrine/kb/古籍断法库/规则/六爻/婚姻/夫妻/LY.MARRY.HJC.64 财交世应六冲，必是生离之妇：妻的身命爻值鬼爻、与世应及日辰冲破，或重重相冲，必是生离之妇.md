# 财交世应六冲，必是生离之妇：妻的身命爻值鬼爻、与世应及日辰冲破，或重重相冲，必是生离之妇

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻和世应形成六冲，一定是要生离的妻子：妻子的身命爻遇鬼爻，与世应和日辰发动而冲破、相合；重重相冲，又和财爻两合；或者妻子的身命爻与世应、动爻相冲，或者与日辰相冲，都主是生离的妻子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth, Self and Other in a six-clash mean she is surely a wife who will part from you in life: the wife's Body or Life line meets a Pressure line, and with Self, Other and the Day moving it is broken and combined; clashes upon clashes, with two combinations on the Wealth line; or the wife's Body or Life line clashes with Self and Other or with a moving line, or with the Day, it is a wife who will part in life.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3154 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻命值鬼爻，与世应并日辰破合，重重相冲，与财两合，或妻命爻与世应动爻相冲，或日辰相冲，必主生离之妇。 | 妻子的命遇到鬼爻，与世应和日辰破合，重重相冲，与财爻两合，或者妻子的命爻与世应、动爻相冲，或者与日辰相冲，必定主是生离的妻子。 | If the wife's Life meets a Pressure line and is broken and combined with Self, Other and the Day, clashes upon clashes, with two combinations on the Wealth line, or the wife's Life line clashes with Self and Other or with a moving line, or with the Day, it surely means a wife who will part in life. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注比王洪绪多「值鬼爻，与世应并日辰动破合」里的「动」字；两家的意思相同。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary has the word 'moving' in 'meets a Pressure line and with Self, Other and the Day moving is broken and combined', which Wang Hongxu lacks; the meaning is the same.
