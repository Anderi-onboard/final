# 化子合财，唐明皇有禄山之子：子孙从变爻里化出是螟蛉子，与财爻或应爻相合（带咸池玄武），必与妻妾有私情

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻是从变爻（他宫）化出来的，是螟蛉子（义子）。它和财爻相合，或者和应爻相合（再带咸池、玄武），一定和妻妾有私情，就像安禄山和杨贵妃。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* An Output line that comes out by change (from another palace) is an adopted son. If it combines with the Wealth line or with the Other line (and carries the Xianchi star or the Dark Tortoise besides), it surely has an affair with the wife or concubine, like An Lushan and Yang Guifei.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1733 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子从他宫化出，乃螟蛉子也，若与财爻相合，带咸池玄武，必与妻妾有情，如安禄山与杨贵妃之通也。 | 子孙爻是从他宫化出来的，是螟蛉子，如果它和财爻相合，又带咸池、玄武，一定和妻妾有私情，就像安禄山和杨贵妃的私通。 | An Output line that comes out of another palace by change is an adopted son; if it combines with the Wealth line and carries the Xianchi star and the Dark Tortoise, it surely has an affair with the wife or concubine, like the liaison of An Lushan and Yang Guifei. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 安禄山认杨贵妃为养母，后来与她有私情的传说，是这一句的典故。卜筮正宗把经文写作「子化合财」，意思相同。注里说「从他宫化出」才算螟蛉子；同一章里另有一句「子孙化出而系外宫则是，系于本宫则非」，本宫化出的不算。
>
> *Note:* The allusion is the tale that An Lushan took Yang Guifei as his foster mother and later had an affair with her. The Zhengzong writes the verse as 'Output changing into and combining with Wealth', which means the same. The note says an Output line counts as an adopted son only if it comes 'from another palace'; the same chapter has a line saying that one from the home palace does not count.
