# 鬼动化财者女不妨夫必再嫁，财动化鬼者男不妨妻必再婚；卜娶遇两财者停妻再娶，卜嫁逢两鬼者抛夫改嫁；官鬼动者夫朝东暮西

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻动而变成财爻的，女子不妨害丈夫，但必定再嫁；财爻动而变成鬼爻的，男子不妨害妻子，但必定再婚。占娶妻遇到两个财爻的，停了妻再娶；占出嫁逢到两个鬼爻的，抛开丈夫改嫁。官鬼爻动的，丈夫一定朝东暮西，不定。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure moving and changing into Wealth: the woman does not harm her husband but will surely marry again; Wealth moving and changing into Pressure: the man does not harm his wife but will surely marry again. Divining a marriage and meeting two Wealth lines: he sets aside his wife and marries again; divining a bride's marriage and meeting two Pressure lines: she leaves her husband and remarries. When Pressure moves, the husband is surely changeable, east in the morning and west at night.
