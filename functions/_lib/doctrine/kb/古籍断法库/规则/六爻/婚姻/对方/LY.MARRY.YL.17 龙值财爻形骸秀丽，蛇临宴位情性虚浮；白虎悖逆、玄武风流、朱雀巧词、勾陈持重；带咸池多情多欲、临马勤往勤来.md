# 龙值财爻形骸秀丽，蛇临宴位情性虚浮；白虎悖逆、玄武风流、朱雀巧词、勾陈持重；带咸池多情多欲、临马勤往勤来

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻代表妻子的性情，看它带的是什么神，就知道好坏。带青龙，容貌像西施，性情慈祥、有礼、友悌、柔顺、贞洁廉正；带螣蛇，性情虚浮，心机多而少信实，言语能打动人；带白虎，心里怀着妒忌凶悍，性情也刚强；带玄武，吝啬、无缘无故地放荡；带朱雀，急躁多话；带勾陈，做事迟钝；带咸池，在家里扰乱人伦、在外面寻花问柳；再加驿马，早晚在外奔走。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wealth line stands for the wife's nature; see what spirit it carries and you know whether it is good or bad. With the Azure Dragon, her looks are like Xi Shi's and her nature kind, courteous, brotherly, gentle, compliant, chaste and upright; with the Coiling Snake, a light and unstable nature, many schemes and little trustworthiness, though her words move people; with the White Tiger, she harbors jealousy and fierceness, and her nature is also hard; with the Dark Tortoise, she is stingy and dissolute without reason; with the Vermilion Bird, hasty and talkative; with Hook Earth, slow and dull in action; with the Xianchi star, she disorders the human relations at home and seeks flowers and willows outside; adding the Post Horse, she is out and about morning and evening.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「蛇临宴位」的「宴位」原文没有解释，像是转录之误（可能是「妻位」一类）；译文照字面译成「宴位」。解说里说「看值何爻」，下面讲的却是六神，所以译作「看它带的是什么神」。
>
> *Note:* The 'banquet position' in 'the Coiling Snake in the banquet position' is not explained and looks like a slip (perhaps 'the wife's position'); it is translated literally. The explanation says 'see which line it is with' but goes on to talk of the six spirits, so it is rendered as 'what spirit it carries'.
