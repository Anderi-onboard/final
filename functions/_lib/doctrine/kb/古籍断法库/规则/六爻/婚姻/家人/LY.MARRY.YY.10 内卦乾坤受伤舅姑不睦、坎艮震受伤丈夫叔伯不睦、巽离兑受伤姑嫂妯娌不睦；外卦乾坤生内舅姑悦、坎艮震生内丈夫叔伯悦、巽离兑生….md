# 内卦乾坤受伤舅姑不睦、坎艮震受伤丈夫叔伯不睦、巽离兑受伤姑嫂妯娌不睦；外卦乾坤生内舅姑悦、坎艮震生内丈夫叔伯悦、巽离兑生内姑嫂妯娌悦

六爻 · 婚姻 · 家人 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 内卦的乾、坤受伤，公婆不和睦；坎、艮、震受伤，丈夫和叔伯不和睦；巽、离、兑受伤，姑嫂、妯娌不和睦。外卦的乾、坤来生内卦，公婆喜悦；坎、艮、震生内卦，丈夫和叔伯喜悦；巽、离、兑生内卦，姑嫂、妯娌喜悦。（挑选女婿的占法见身命占。）
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Qian or Kun in the inner trigram is harmed, the parents-in-law are at odds; if Kan, Gen or Zhen is harmed, the husband and his uncles and brothers are at odds; if Xun, Li or Dui is harmed, the sisters-in-law are at odds. If Qian or Kun in the outer trigram generates the inner, the parents-in-law are pleased; if Kan, Gen or Zhen generates it, the husband and his uncles and brothers are pleased; if Xun, Li or Dui generates it, the sisters-in-law are pleased. (Choosing a son-in-law is found in the divination of one's person and fate.)
