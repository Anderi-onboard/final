# 占行人·咸池临玄武恐逢觅水之欢：用爻加咸池玄武则行人有外情牵惹不归，二星动合应爻或用爻是觅水之欢，青龙动合世应必娶妾

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占在外的人（行人）：咸池星临在玄武上，恐怕他会遇上寻欢的事。小注展开说：用爻如果加上咸池、玄武，行人在外有别的情爱牵住，不回来；咸池、玄武发动，去合应爻或用爻，是在外寻欢；青龙发动去合世爻和应爻，他一定会娶妾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Divining a traveller: if the Xianchi star sits on the Dark Tortoise, he may meet a dalliance. The note goes on: if the Subject line has the Xianchi star and the Dark Tortoise added, the traveller has an outside affair holding him and does not return; if the Xianchi star and the Dark Tortoise move and combine with the Other line or the Subject line, it is a dalliance; if the Azure Dragon moves and combines with Self and Other, he will surely take a concubine.

> [!note] 按
> 「觅水之欢」的「水」指咸池：咸池又叫桃花，是水星，主男女私情，「觅水之欢」是在外面找私情的欢会。这一段是占出门在外的人（行人），不是占本地的婚姻；这里收的是它里面和男女私情、娶妾有关的三句，前面讲梦的话和别的行人断法没有收。「用爻」这里是行人本人所在的爻。
>
> *Note:* 'Seeking the pleasure of water' refers to the Xianchi star: Xianchi, also called the peach blossom, is a water star and rules illicit love between man and woman, and 'seeking the pleasure of water' is going outside for a liaison. This passage divines a traveller away from home and not a marriage at home; what is taken here are the three sentences in it that concern illicit love and taking a concubine, and the words before them about dreams and the other rules for judging travellers are not taken. The 'Subject line' here is the line that stands for the traveller himself.
