# 兄弟用变·婚姻：兄弟交重克了财，买婢求妻事不谐

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻交重克了财：病人难愈、灾未离；考试雷同是大忌；官非、暗贼耗钱财；若带吉神有助，出路的行人还没来，做买卖折本；买婢女、求妻，事情不和谐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Peer line moves and controls Wealth: a sick person is hard to cure and the calamity has not left; in exams sameness with others is a great taboo; lawsuits and hidden thieves drain money. If it carries a lucky spirit and helps, a traveler on the road has not yet come, trade loses its capital, and buying a maid or seeking a wife does not go smoothly.
