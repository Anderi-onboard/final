# 妻妾德色：妻临金色白净身瘦小性刚烈，木色青身长妖娆性宽慢，火赤颜身矮性躁急，土色黄身肥矮性温和迟慢，水色紫黑身活动性和宽；妻持父寿高伶俐、持兄损物耗财、持子性善多识见、持财貌美安和、持官貌丑狠毒

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问妻妾的品德容色：妻爻临金，肤色白净，身体瘦小，性情刚烈；临木，肤色青，身材长，妖娆多姿，性情宽缓；临火，红脸，身矮，性急躁；临土，肤色黄，身体又胖又矮，性温和，做事迟慢；临水，肤色紫黑，身体灵活敏捷，性情温和宽厚，衰就急躁，动就多机变，冲就没有主见，合就没有知觉。妻爻持父母爻，长寿伶俐，能当家，会读书算账；持兄弟爻，性情损物耗财，留不住奴婢，旺相的败家好赌，和妯娌不和；持子孙爻，性善、见识多，旺相的能当家、生贵子，带朱雀的常念经；持财爻，貌美、安和，能当家开店，旺相的旺夫有财；持官鬼爻，貌丑、性狠毒，旺相的好杀，带贵人的有封赠荫庇。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Asking the virtue and looks of a wife or concubine: the wife's line on Metal, her complexion is fair and clean, her body slight, her nature fierce; on Wood, greenish, tall, alluring and graceful, easy and slow in nature; on Fire, a red face, short, hasty; on Earth, yellow, stout and short, mild and slow in her affairs; on Water, a dark purplish complexion, a nimble body, a gentle and generous nature, hasty if weak, full of shifts if moving, without a mind of her own if clashed, without awareness if combined. The wife's line holding the Resource line: long-lived, clever, able to run the house and to write and reckon; holding a Peer line: she wastes goods and squanders money and keeps no servants, and if strong ruins the house, gambles and is at odds with her sisters-in-law; holding Output: good-natured with much insight, and if strong able to run the house and bear a noble son, with the Vermilion Bird often chanting scripture; holding Wealth: fair, calm and gentle, able to run the house and a shop, and if strong benefits her husband and has money; holding Pressure: ugly and cruel, if strong given to killing, and with the Nobleman having honors and protection.
