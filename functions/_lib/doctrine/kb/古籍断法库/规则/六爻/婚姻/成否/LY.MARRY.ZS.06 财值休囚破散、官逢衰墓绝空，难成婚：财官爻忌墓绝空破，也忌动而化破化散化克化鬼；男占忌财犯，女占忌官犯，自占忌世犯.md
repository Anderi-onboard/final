# 财值休囚破散、官逢衰墓绝空，难成婚：财官爻忌墓绝空破，也忌动而化破化散化克化鬼；男占忌财犯，女占忌官犯，自占忌世犯

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财休囚、破、散，或官鬼衰、入墓、绝、空，都难成婚。财爻、官爻不该落墓、绝、空、破，也不该动了以后化成破、散、克、鬼——那是短命贫寒的命。男方占，忌财爻犯这些；女方占，忌官爻犯；男女自己占，忌世爻犯，勉强成婚，很快就会不和。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If Wealth is resting, trapped, broken or scattered, or Pressure is weak, entombed, extinguished or Void, the marriage can hardly be made. Neither the Wealth line nor the Pressure line should fall into tomb, extinction, Void or break, nor move and change into a break, a scattering, a controller or Pressure: that is a fate of early death and poverty. For a man's divination, fear that the Wealth line commits these faults; for a woman's, the Pressure line; when a man or woman divines for themselves, the Self line; if the marriage is forced through, discord shows at once.
