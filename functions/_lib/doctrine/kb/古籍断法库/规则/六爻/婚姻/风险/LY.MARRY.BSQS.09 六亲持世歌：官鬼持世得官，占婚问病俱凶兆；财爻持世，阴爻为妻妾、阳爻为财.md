# 六亲持世歌：官鬼持世得官，占婚问病俱凶兆；财爻持世，阴爻为妻妾、阳爻为财

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻持世，一定得官，文书印信两样都看得见；但占婚姻、问疾病都是凶兆，还会破宅伤财、身不安宁。财爻持世：阴爻是妻妾，阳爻是财，持世持身都合心意；经商种田养蚕收成百倍；占病占产，官鬼是胎。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Pressure line holding the Self line one is sure to get office, and documents and seals both show; but a divination on marriage or illness is an ill omen, and there will be a broken house, lost money and no ease. With the Wealth line holding the Self line: a yin line is the wife or concubine and a yang line is wealth, and holding the Self or the Body both please the heart; in trade, farming and silkworms the yield is a hundredfold; in divining illness or birth, Pressure is the womb.

> [!warning] 打架
> 同样是官鬼持世，这首歌说「占婚问病俱凶兆」，《天玄赋》总论提纲说「官鬼不宜持世，求名婚娶两相宜」（LY.MARRY.TXF.24）。两处都是口诀，没有注，书里不裁断。《增删卜易》按男女分：女占男，官星持世、合世、生世且旺相是良缘（LY.MARRY.ZS.11）。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For the same Pressure line holding Self, this rhyme says 'a divination on marriage or illness is an ill omen', while the opening summary of the Tianxuan Fu says 'Pressure should not hold the Self line, but for seeking office and for marrying both are fitting' (LY.MARRY.TXF.24). Both are bare rhymes and the books do not decide between them. The Zengshan Buyi divides by sex: when a woman divines about a man, a Pressure line that holds, combines with or generates the Self line and is strong makes a good match (LY.MARRY.ZS.11).

> [!note] 按
> 这首歌其余几句（父母持世、子孙持世、兄弟持世）讲的是文书、谷财、胎病，与婚姻无关，没有收。同一首歌在《火珠林》（第 948 行）和《周易尚占》（第 477 行）里也有。
>
> *Note:* The other lines of the rhyme (on the Resource, Output and Peer lines holding the Self) concern documents, grain and money, and pregnancy and illness, and have nothing to do with marriage and are not included. The same rhyme is in the Huozhulin (line 948) and the Zhouyi Shangzhan (line 477).
