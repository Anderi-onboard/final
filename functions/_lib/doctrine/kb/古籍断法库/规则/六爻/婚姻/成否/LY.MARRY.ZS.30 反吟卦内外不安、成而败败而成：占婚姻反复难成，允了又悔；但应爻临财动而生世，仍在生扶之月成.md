# 反吟卦内外不安、成而败败而成：占婚姻反复难成，允了又悔；但应爻临财动而生世，仍在生扶之月成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 反吟（内卦或外卦动变之后，每一爻的地支都变成和自己相冲的地支，例如丑亥酉变成未巳卯）主里外不安，事情成了又败、败了又成。占婚姻，就是反反复复，难以成功：先答应又反悔。书中一例：巳月戊子日占婚事对方答不答应，得恒之晋，内卦反吟，断为答应了又后悔；问将来能不能成，断为应爻是财爻、动而生世，八九月间一定成，果然酉月成婚。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Reversed Chant (after moving and changing, the branch of every line of the inner or outer trigram becomes the one that clashes with it, for example Chou-Hai-You becoming Wei-Si-Mao) means unease within and without and things that succeed and then fail, and fail and then succeed. For a marriage it means going back and forth and being hard to make: agreed and then regretted. The book's case: in the Si month on a Wuzi day, asked whether a marriage would be agreed to, the hexagram was Heng changing into Jin; the inner trigram was a Reversed Chant, and the judgment was agreeing and then regretting; asked whether it would be made in the end, the judgment was that the Other line is Wealth and moves to generate the Self line, so it would surely be made in the eighth or ninth month, and it was made in the You month.

> [!note] 按
> 「反吟」怎么算，书里在这一章前面用卦例讲（乾变坤、升变观、观变坤、巽变观等），排盘图这里不收。恒之晋的例子里，下三爻的丑亥酉变成未巳卯，每一爻都变成与自己相冲的地支，所以是「内卦反吟」。这个例子和 LY.MARRY.ZS.09 的「反吟卦变，翻覆难成」是同一个意思，例子多告诉我们一件事：反吟不等于不成，只是先反复，应爻临财又动而生世的话，还是成了。
>
> *Note:* How a Reversed Chant is counted is taught earlier in this chapter by cases (Qian into Kun, Sheng into Guan, Guan into Kun, Xun into Guan and others), whose charts are not included here. In the Heng-into-Jin case the lower three lines Chou, Hai and You turn into Wei, Si and Mao, each clashing with itself, so it is an 'inner trigram Reversed Chant'. The case says the same as LY.MARRY.ZS.09 ('a Reversed Chant change flips back and forth and is hard to accomplish') and adds one thing: a Reversed Chant does not mean it never happens, only that it first goes back and forth, and if the Other line is Wealth and moves to generate the Self line the match is still made.
