# 夫妻与福德相逢带合，必近亲邻之女：夫妻的命爻都在本宫，就中合见子孙，主因亲致亲，或旧亲做媒

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 夫妻的命爻都在本宫，就中又合见子孙，一定是近亲近邻家的女子：男子与财爻相近，都在本宫或卦中，主婚姻在近处；合见子孙，是亲上加亲，或者旧亲戚做媒。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When husband and wife with Output meet and combine, she is surely a girl from near kin or near neighbors: if the man's line is near the Wealth line and both are in the home palace or in the hexagram, the marriage is close by; and if Output is also seen in combination, it is a match of kin to kin, or an old relative acts as go-between.

> 旧注「亲邻」，卜筮正宗写作「亲临」，是同一句话里的一字之差。
>
> *Note:* Where the old commentary has 'kin and neighbors', the Bushi Zhengzong has a different second character, a one-character difference in the same line.
