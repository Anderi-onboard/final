# 家宅千金赋·父入子宫后娘来就、子临父位随母嫁人；阴兄化阳兄嫂赘晚夫、阴子变阳子女招婿；父化父外娘晚位、财化财当娶双妻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 讲一家人构成的几联里，和婚嫁有关的有：父母爻进入子孙的宫位，一定有继母来；子孙爻落在父母爻的位置，一定是随着母亲改嫁。阴爻兄弟化成阳爻兄弟，嫂子招赘晚夫、同住一处；阴爻子孙变成阳爻子孙，女儿招婿、同在一家。父母爻化成父母爻，是外来的继母；妻财爻化成妻财爻，当娶两位妻子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Among the couplets on how a household is made up, these concern marriage: the Resource line entering the Output's palace, a stepmother will surely come; the Output line sitting in the Resource's place, he or she will surely follow the mother when she remarries. A yin Peer line changing into a yang Peer line: the sister-in-law takes a later husband in, living under the same roof; a yin Output line changing into a yang Output line: the daughter takes a son-in-law and they live in the same house. Resource changing into Resource: a stepmother from outside; Wealth changing into Wealth: he will marry two wives.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这几联在《黄金策》家宅章的千金赋里，没有旧注。「晚夫」是后来的丈夫，「外娘晚位」是外面来的、位次在后的继母。原文夹在一大段讲家宅人口的话中间，和婚嫁无关的联（如「二姓同居必有两重兄弟」「兄化鬼以空亡」）没有收。《增删卜易》婚姻章里也说「财化财」未必两度婚（LY.MARRY.ZS.15），与这里的「当娶双妻」不同。
>
> *Note:* These couplets are in the household rhapsody of the Huangjin Ce and have no old commentary. 'A later husband' is a husband taken afterwards, and 'a second mother from outside, in a later place' is a stepmother who comes from outside and ranks later. In the original they are set in a long run of lines on the people of a household, and the couplets unrelated to marriage (such as 'two surnames living together must have two sets of brothers' and 'Peer turning to Pressure and Void') are not taken. The marriage chapter of the Zengshan Buyi also says that Wealth turning to Wealth is not necessarily two marriages (LY.MARRY.ZS.15), which differs from 'he will marry two wives' here.
