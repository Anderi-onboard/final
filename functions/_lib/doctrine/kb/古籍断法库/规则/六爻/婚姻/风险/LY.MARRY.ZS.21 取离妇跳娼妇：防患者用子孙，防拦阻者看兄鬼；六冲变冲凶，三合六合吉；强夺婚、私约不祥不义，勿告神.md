# 取离妇跳娼妇：防患者用子孙，防拦阻者看兄鬼；六冲变冲凶，三合六合吉；强夺婚、私约不祥不义，勿告神

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 娶离了婚的妇人、或从娼门出来的妇人：要防祸患，用子孙爻——子孙持世、发动，没有忧患；财爻持世、子孙动来相生更吉；鬼爻或兄弟爻克世、随墓助伤、鬼爻持世，婚事虽成，祸患跟着生；财爻克世、兄弟持世，是落进别人设的局，一定人财两空。要防有人拦阻：兄弟爻动就不成，鬼爻动就有祸。兄弟持世，到底难成；财爻动来相生，不用谋划就成。六冲变冲是凶，三合、六合的吉。六合变冲，成了不长久；六冲变合，最终一定成。强抢的婚事、私下的约定，既不吉祥也不合义，不要去告问神明。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Marrying a woman who has left her husband, or one who has left the brothel: to guard against harm, use the Output line; with the Output line on the Self line or moving there is nothing to worry about; with the Wealth line on the Self line and a moving Output line generating it, better still; if Pressure or a Peer line controls the Self line, or there is following into a tomb or aiding of harm, or Pressure holds the Self line, although the marriage is made calamity follows; if Wealth controls the Self line and a Peer line holds it, you have fallen into someone's trap and will lose both the person and the money. To guard against obstruction: if a Peer line moves it will not succeed, and if Pressure moves there will be disaster. With a Peer line on the Self line it is in the end hard to accomplish; with Wealth moving to generate, it comes about without scheming. A Six-clash changing into a clash is ill, a three-harmony or Six-combine is auspicious; a Six-combine changing into a clash is made but does not last, a Six-clash changing into a combine will surely succeed in the end. Forcing a marriage or fulfilling a secret pact is neither auspicious nor righteous; do not put it to the spirits.

> [!note] 按
> 章名「取离妇跳娼妇」里的「跳」字含义不确定（可能是脱籍、赎身，也可能是别的），别的书里没有同名的章可对；译文按「从娼门出来的妇人」取，不敢说得更死。这一节末尾还有一句「出妾休妻，原非所欲，有不得已而出者，必去难留」，像是下一节的开头，没有收。
>
> *Note:* The word 'tiao' ('to leap') in the section title is uncertain (it may mean leaving the trade, being bought out, or something else) and no other book has a section of the same name; it is translated as a woman who has left prostitution without claiming more. The section ends with the sentence 'Sending away a concubine or wife is not what one wants...', which looks like the start of the next section and is not included.
