# 卦爻相合必男妇成欢，贞悔相冲定夫妻不睦：卦得六爻相合绵远和谐，遇六冲定然反目

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦爻相合，男女必定成欢；贞卦、悔卦相冲，夫妻一定不和睦。卦得六爻相合，长远和谐；遇到六冲，一定反目。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the hexagram and its lines combine, man and woman surely find joy together; when the Zhen and Hui trigrams clash, husband and wife are surely at odds. A hexagram whose six lines combine has long-lasting harmony; meeting a Six-clash, they surely fall out.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 538 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 卦得六爻相合，绵远和谐。 | 卦得六爻相合，长远和谐。 | If the hexagram has all six lines combining, the harmony is long-lasting. |
> | 若遇六冲，定然反目。 | 如果遇到六冲，一定反目。 | If it meets a Six-clash, they surely fall out. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「贞悔」在这一联里，下面的解说读成「六爻相合」「六冲」（整卦的合与冲），没有读成上下两卦相冲；译文保留「贞、悔」两字，读法交给解说。
>
> *Note:* The explanation under the couplet reads 'Zhen and Hui' as the whole hexagram combining or clashing (six-combine and six-clash), not as the two trigrams clashing; the translation keeps the words Zhen and Hui and leaves the reading to the explanation.
