# 暴败杀：婚姻大忌，男克妇、女伤夫；春猪羊犬、夏蛇鼠龙、秋申酉丑、冬虎马兔

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 暴败杀：婚姻是大忌，男的克妻，女的伤夫。歌诀：猪、羊、狗在春天的三个月，蛇、鼠、龙在夏天的月份，申、酉、丑到了秋天一定败，虎、马、兔在冬天要防。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Violent-Ruin star: in marriage it is a great taboo, the man controlling the wife and the woman harming the husband. The rhyme: the pig, goat and dog in the three months of spring; the snake, rat and dragon in the summer months; Shen, You and Chou meeting autumn are sure to fail; the tiger, horse and rabbit are to be guarded against in winter.

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 歌诀里的生肖依次对着：春三月亥、未、戌，夏三月巳、子、辰，冬三月寅、午、卯；秋三月直接写了申、酉、丑。书里没有把春夏冬的地支写出来。
>
> *Note:* The zodiac animals in the rhyme stand for: in the three spring months Hai, Wei and Xu; in the summer months Si, Zi and Chen; in the winter months Yin, Wu and Mao; for the autumn months the branches Shen, You and Chou are written out. The book does not write out the branches for spring, summer and winter.
