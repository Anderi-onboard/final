# 青龙动处无不为祥：管嫁娶喜庆善事生育，问喜即成、问财即得；但不宜冲（冲则失半）、不宜空（空则永无嘉兆）；月建青龙与戊辰青龙同断

六爻 · 婚姻 · 取象 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙这个神，管僧道、嫁娶喜庆、善事、生育等。它如果遇到发动的地方，没有不吉祥的，即使带着凶神恶煞也不会因此止歇；问喜事就成，问钱财就得。但是不宜被冲，被冲就失去一半；也不宜落空，落空就永远没有好兆头。月建上的青龙和戊辰的青龙，断法相同。书上说：卦中吉利的神将叫青龙，做事求财喜气浓，求名和嫁娶都遂意，即使有忧愁的事也没有凶险。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon governs monks and Taoists, marriages and celebrations, good deeds and childbirth, and the like. If it meets a place where it moves, nothing fails to be auspicious, and even carrying ill spirits and fierce stars it is not stopped by them; ask about joy and it is made, ask about wealth and it is gained. But it must not be clashed, for clashed it loses half, and it must not be Void, for Void there is never a good omen. The Azure Dragon of the Month and that of a Wuchen day are judged alike. The books say: the lucky general in a hexagram is called the Azure Dragon; in undertakings and seeking wealth joy is thick, for fame and marrying all goes as wished, and even with a worrying matter there is no ill.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 第一句没有主语：它接在上一行「龙为良善清高喜气利名之兆」和一段讲青龙属木、应甲乙寅卯日的解说后面（上一行文字有讹脱，没有收），主语是青龙。「竹木青缘」的意思书里没有解释，译文照字面。
>
> *Note:* The first sentence has no subject: it follows the line 'the Dragon is a sign of goodness, purity, joy and fame' and a note that the Azure Dragon belongs to Wood and answers to Jia, Yi, Yin and Mao days (that line is corrupt and is not included); the subject is the Azure Dragon. The meaning of 'bamboo and wood, green fringes' is not explained and is translated literally.
