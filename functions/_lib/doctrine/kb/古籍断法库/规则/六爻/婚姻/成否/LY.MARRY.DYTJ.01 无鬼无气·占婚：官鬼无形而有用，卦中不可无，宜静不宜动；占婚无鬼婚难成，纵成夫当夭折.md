# 无鬼无气·占婚：官鬼无形而有用，卦中不可无，宜静不宜动；占婚无鬼婚难成，纵成夫当夭折

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼这一爻没有形体却有用处，卦里不能没有，宜静不宜动；带吉神动也能有福，加凶煞动没有不成祸的。占婚姻而卦中没有官鬼，婚事难成，即使成了，丈夫也会早死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure has no form but has its use; it must not be absent from the hexagram, and should be still and not move; moving with a lucky spirit it can also bring fortune, moving with a fierce star it always brings calamity. In a marriage divination with no Pressure in the hexagram, the marriage is hard to make, and even if it is made the husband will die young.
