# 得六冲叫卦冲无往而不散；卦化六冲叫变冲，占事无后，约婚而不终、结义而不信、营求而不遂

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占得六冲卦，叫卦冲，无论到哪里都散，话多乱、事多变、行多违、谋多阻。卦化成六冲，叫变冲：占事没有下文，约婚不能到底，结义不守信，谋求不能如愿。卦冲叫前冲，变冲叫后冲；如果前面是六合、后面是六冲，更加厉害。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A divination that gives a six-clash hexagram is called a hexagram clash: wherever it goes it scatters; words are muddled, matters change often, actions go against, plans meet obstruction. A hexagram that changes into a six-clash hexagram is called a change-clash: a matter divined has no lasting result, a betrothal is not carried through, sworn brotherhood lacks good faith, a pursuit is not fulfilled. The hexagram clash is the front clash and the change-clash the rear clash; if the front is six-combine and the rear six-clash it is worse still.

## 《易冒·合冲章第三十四》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 578 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 34: Combination and clash", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 筮得六冲，其名卦冲，无往而不散也，言多乱，事多变，行多违，谋多阻也。 | 占得六冲卦，名叫卦冲，无论去哪里都散，话多乱，事多变，行多违，谋多阻。 | If the divination gives a six-clash hexagram, it is called a hexagram clash: wherever it goes it scatters; words are muddled, matters change often, actions go against, plans meet obstruction. |
> | 筮化六冲，其名变冲，占事主无后也，约婚而不终，结义而不信，营求而不遂也。 | 卦化成六冲，名叫变冲：占事没有下文，约婚不能到底，结义不守信，谋求不能如愿。 | If the hexagram changes into a six-clash hexagram, it is called a change-clash: for a matter divined there is no lasting result, a betrothal is not carried through, sworn brotherhood lacks good faith, and a pursuit is not fulfilled. |
> | 卦冲谓之前冲，变冲谓之后冲，倘前六合而后六冲，又其甚已。 | 卦冲叫做前冲，变冲叫做后冲，如果前面六合而后面六冲，就更加厉害了。 | The hexagram clash is called the front clash and the change-clash the rear clash; if the front is six-combine and the rear six-clash, it is worse still. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「占事主无后也」的「无后」，可以读作事情没有后续，也可以读作没有后代；书里这一句没有说明，译文取前一种。这一段讲各种事，婚姻只是其中「约婚而不终」一句。
>
> *Note:* 'No after' in 'a matter divined means no after' may be read as the matter having no follow-up or as having no descendants; the book does not say, and the translation takes the first. The passage speaks of all kinds of matters, and marriage is only the clause about a betrothal not carried through.
