# 用伏应财之下，身赘他家：出门在外的人，用爻伏在应爻的妻财下面，是入赘在别人家不想回来；财动生合世爻能带妻回家；财与伏神不相生合只是替人掌财，不是女婿

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问出门在外的人为什么不回来：他的用爻伏在应爻的妻财下面，就是入赘在别人家，不想回来了。如果妻财动来生合世爻，他会带着妻子回家；妻财如果和伏神不相生合，那只是替别人掌管钱财，或者依靠别人家，不是入赘的女婿。王洪绪补充：要伏在应位的阴爻财下面才是入赘；落在阳爻上、生合世爻或身爻的，是替别人掌财不回来。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Asking why a traveller does not come home: if his Subject line is hidden beneath the Wealth line on the Other, he has married into another's house and does not think of returning. If Wealth moves and generates or combines with the Self line, he will bring his wife home; if Wealth does not generate or combine with the hidden spirit, he is only managing another's money or depending on another family, and is not a son-in-law by marriage. Wang Hongxu adds: it is marrying in only when he is hidden beneath the yin Wealth in the Other's place; if it falls on a yang line and generates or combines with Self or the Body, he is managing another's money and does not return.

> | 用入应爻财库者，踪羁富室也。 | 用爻进入应爻财爻的库里的，行踪被拘留在富户人家。 | One whose Subject enters the tomb of the Wealth on the Other line has his whereabouts held in a rich house. |

^q4

> [!note] 按
> 这是《黄金策》行人章里的一联，问的是出门在外的人（用爻）在哪里、为什么不回来。旧注只要求用爻伏在应爻的妻财下面；王洪绪限定为伏在「应位阴财」之下才是入赘，落在阳爻上是替人掌财，两家说法有一点出入。《易隐》有一句相近的话。这一联收进婚姻域，是因为它讲「赘」；行人章的其他各联不收。
>
> *Note:* This couplet is in the Huangjin Ce's chapter on travellers and asks where the traveller (the Subject line) is and why he does not come home. The old commentary requires only that the Subject be hidden beneath the Wealth line on the Other; Wang Hongxu restricts it to 'beneath the yin Wealth in the Other's place' for marrying in, and says that on a yang line it means managing another's money, so the two differ a little. The Yi Yin has a similar sentence. The couplet is included in the marriage domain because it speaks of marrying into a house; the other couplets of the travellers chapter are not taken.
