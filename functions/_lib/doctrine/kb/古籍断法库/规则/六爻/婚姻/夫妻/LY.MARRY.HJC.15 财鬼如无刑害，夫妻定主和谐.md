# 财鬼如无刑害，夫妻定主和谐

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财、鬼受刑冲克害，夫妻必然不和睦；没有这种象，才能如鱼得水，到老和谐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth and Pressure punished, clashed, controlled or harmed means the couple will surely not be at peace; without this image they are like fish in water and are in harmony into old age.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6358 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财鬼如无刑害，夫妻定主和谐； | 财、鬼如果没有刑害，夫妻一定和谐； | If Wealth and Pressure suffer no punishment or harm, the couple will surely be in harmony; |
