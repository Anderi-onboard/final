# 男子两财逢二姓丝萝之好，阴人两鬼再一番桃李之鲜：一旺一空是再婚，两财不空带桃花一正一偏，两鬼旺不空遇刑冲克财是生离改嫁

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男占女见到两个财，是先后有过两姓的婚姻；女占男见到两个官鬼，是再有一次婚事。男占女见两财一旺一空，已经做过两次新郎；两财不空、只带桃花，一定是一正一偏。女占男见两鬼一旺一空，必定重婚再嫁；两鬼旺而不空，日辰刑冲财爻，或动爻克财，一定是生离改嫁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A man with two Wealth lines meets a marriage of two surnames; a woman with two Pressure lines has another fresh bloom of peach and plum. A man divining a woman who sees two Wealth lines, one strong and one Void, has been a bridegroom twice; if the two Wealth lines are not Void and merely carry the Peach Blossom star, there is surely one principal wife and one secondary. A woman divining a man who sees two Pressure lines, one strong and one Void, will surely marry again; if both Pressure lines are strong and not Void, and the Day punishes or clashes with Wealth, or a moving line controls Wealth, then there will surely be separation in life and remarriage.
