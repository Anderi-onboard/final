# 世应当中两间爻，发动则所求多阻隔：间为中人不可动，兄动脱诈口舌，鬼动见祸，占婚姻为某人诡谲

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻和应爻中间的两个间爻，代表中间人（占婚姻就是媒人），不可发动，动了就有阻隔：兄弟爻动，主诈骗、口舌；官鬼爻动，主人事上受阻隔、见祸；占婚姻，就是某个人在使诈。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The two between lines between Self and Other stand for the go-betweens (in a marriage, the matchmakers) and must not move; moving, they bring hindrance to what is sought: a Peer line moving means cheating and quarrels, a Pressure line moving means people and affairs are cut off and misfortune shows; in a marriage divination it is some person acting deceitfully.

> [!note] 按
> 第 482 行的诗里，「假饶有无事分明，又见切切方始得」两句意思不清（大概是说事情看着有没有都分明了，还要再切切实实地看到才算得），没有收，只收头两句和小注。第 484 行接着往下列「占生产为老娘，占事为阻隔……」，是别的事里间爻各指什么人，不属婚姻，没有收。
>
> *Note:* In the poem at line 482 the two lines 'even if there is or is not, and the matter is clear, it is only obtained when it is seen again earnestly' are unclear (perhaps: even if the matter seems clear either way, it counts as got only when seen again in earnest) and are left out; only the first two lines and the small note are taken. Line 484 goes on to say who the between lines are in other matters ('in childbirth the midwife, in affairs a hinderer ...'), which is not about marriage and is not taken.
