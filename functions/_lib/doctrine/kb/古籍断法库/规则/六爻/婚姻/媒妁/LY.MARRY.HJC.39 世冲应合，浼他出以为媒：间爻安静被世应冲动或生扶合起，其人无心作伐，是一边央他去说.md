# 世冲应合，浼他出以为媒：间爻安静被世应冲动或生扶合起，其人无心作伐，是一边央他去说

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻安静，被应、世两爻冲动，或者被生扶合起，或被日辰冲并起来的，那个人无心做媒，一定是一边请他去说；间爻自己发动的，不要这样断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A quiet between line clashed into motion by the Other and the Self, or raised by generation, support or combination, or by a clash from the Day, belongs to a person who has no mind to act as go-between and has certainly been asked by one side to speak; a between line that moves of itself is not to be judged this way.
