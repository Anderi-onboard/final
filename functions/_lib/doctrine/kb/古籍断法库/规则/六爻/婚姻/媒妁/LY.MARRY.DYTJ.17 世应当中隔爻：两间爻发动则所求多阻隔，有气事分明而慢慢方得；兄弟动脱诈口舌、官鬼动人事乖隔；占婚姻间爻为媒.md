# 世应当中隔爻：两间爻发动则所求多阻隔，有气事分明而慢慢方得；兄弟动脱诈口舌、官鬼动人事乖隔；占婚姻间爻为媒

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应当中的两个间爻发动，所求的事多有阻隔；间爻有气，事情就分明，但要慢慢才得。兄弟动是脱诈口舌，官鬼动是人事乖隔。占婚姻，间爻是媒人；占生产，是接生婆；占事，是阻隔；占种田，是农人；占宅，是邻里。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the two between lines in the middle of Self and Other move, what is sought meets many obstructions; if they have qi the matter is clear, but it is obtained only slowly. Peer moving means evasion, deceit and quarrels; Pressure moving means people and affairs at odds and cut off. In marriage a between line is the go-between; in childbirth the midwife; in an affair, obstruction; in farming, the farmer; for a house, the neighbors.
