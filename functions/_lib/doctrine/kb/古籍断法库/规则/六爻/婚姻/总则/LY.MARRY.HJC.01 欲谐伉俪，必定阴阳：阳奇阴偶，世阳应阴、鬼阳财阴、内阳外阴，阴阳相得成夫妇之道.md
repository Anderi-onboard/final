# 欲谐伉俪，必定阴阳：阳奇阴偶，世阳应阴、鬼阳财阴、内阳外阴，阴阳相得成夫妇之道

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 夫妻要和谐，先看阴阳配不配：世属阳、应属阴，鬼（官）属阳、财属阴，内卦属阳、外卦属阴；各得其位，才是夫妇之道，日后有利。男家占，宜世阳应阴；女家占，宜世阴应阳。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a couple to be harmonious, first see whether yin and yang match: the Self is yang and the Other yin, Pressure is yang and Wealth yin, the inner trigram is yang and the outer yin; each in its place is the way of husband and wife, and later days will be favourable. When the man's family divines, the Self should be yang and the Other yin; when the woman's family divines, the Self should be yin and the Other yang.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 男女合婚，契于前定； | 男女配成婚姻，是前世就定好的； | A man and woman joined in marriage: it was fixed beforehand; |
> | 朱陈缔结，分在夙成。 | 朱陈两家结亲，缘分早就注定。 | the Zhu and Chen families were bound as in-laws, their lot made long before. |
> | 然非月老，焉知夫妇于当时； | 但如果不是月下老人，怎么会在当时就知道谁是夫妻； | But without the Old Man under the Moon, how could one know at the time who would be husband and wife; |
> | 不有宓羲，岂识吉凶于今日！ | 没有伏羲，怎么会在今天知道吉凶！ | and without Fu Xi, how could one know good or ill today! |
> | 欲谐伉俪，必定阴阳。 | 想要夫妻和谐，必须先定阴阳。 | To harmonise a couple, first settle yin and yang. |

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6307 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 阳奇阴偶，配合成婚。 | 阳是奇数，阴是偶数，配合起来成了婚姻。 | Yang is odd and yin is even; matched together they make a marriage. |
> | 世属阳，应属阴； | 世属阳，应属阴； | The Self belongs to yang and the Other to yin; |
> | 鬼属阳，财属阴； | 鬼属阳，财属阴； | Pressure belongs to yang and Wealth to yin; |
> | 内属阳，外属阴。 | 内卦属阳，外卦属阴。 | the inner trigram belongs to yang and the outer to yin. |
> | 阴阳相得，乃成夫妇之道。 | 阴阳相得，才成夫妇之道。 | When yin and yang suit each other, that is the way of husband and wife. |
> | 异日必然大利。 | 日后必然大利。 | In the days to come it will surely be greatly favourable. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1986 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 阳奇阴偶，配合成婚。 | 阳是奇数，阴是偶数，配合起来成了婚姻。 | Yang is odd and yin is even; matched together they make a marriage. |
> | 如男家卜，宜世属阳应属阴，用神阴阳得位； | 如果男家占，宜于世属阳、应属阴，用神的阴阳各得其位； | If the man's family divines, it is fitting that the Self be yang and the Other yin, with the Subject's yin and yang each in its place; |
> | 女家卜，宜世阴应阳。 | 女家占，宜于世阴、应阳。 | if the woman's family divines, the Self should be yin and the Other yang. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「朱陈」指朱陈村，旧时两姓世代联姻的说法；「宓羲」即伏羲；「月老」即月下老人。这几句是章首的引语，不是判断。
>
> *Note:* 'Zhu and Chen' refers to the village of Zhu-Chen, proverbial for two families who intermarried through generations; 'Mi Xi' is Fu Xi; 'Yue Lao' is the Old Man under the Moon. These opening lines are a preface, not a judgement.
