# 都说财动伤克父母，财旺一样能克：先要财官旺相得吉卦，再另占一卦看妨不妨父母

六爻 · 婚姻 · 家人 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 这门婚事妨不妨父母：古书只说妻财发动才伤克父母，觉子驳它——春天占，财爻是木、父母爻是土，当令的木一定克得住休囚的土，旺和动一样能伤。他的办法是分占：先要财、官旺相，得到吉卦，再单独占一卦，问这门婚事会不会妨克父母。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether the marriage harms the parents: the old books say only a moving Wealth line harms and controls the parents, and Juezi rejects this; in a spring divination Wealth is Wood and the Resource line is Earth, and Wood in season surely controls resting Earth, so strength harms just as movement does. His method is to divine separately: first Wealth and Pressure must be strong and the hexagram auspicious, then cast a separate hexagram on whether the marriage harms or controls the parents.

## 觉子(李文辉,《增删卜易》)

> [!quote]+ [[增删卜易]] 第 11572–11576 行 · Juezi (Li Wenhui, Zengshan Buyi)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 今按古今诸书，又以财旺伤克父母，余不知宜财之旺而为吉乎？ | 现在按古今各书，又说财旺会伤克父母，我不知道财爻到底该旺才吉利呢？ | Now, going by the books old and new, it is also said that a strong Wealth line harms and controls the parents; I do not know whether it is good for Wealth to be strong, |
> | 抑宜财不旺而为吉乎？ | 还是财爻不旺才吉利呢？ | or good for Wealth not to be strong? |
> | 或曰：《黄金策》云：翁姑不睦，皆因妻位交重； | 有人说：《黄金策》讲：公婆不和，都是因为妻财爻重叠发动； | Someone says: the Huangjin Ce states: when the parents-in-law are at odds it is all because the Wealth line is doubled and moving; |
> | 《海底眼》云：财动丧双亲； | 《海底眼》讲：财爻发动，会失去双亲； | the Haidi Yan states: when Wealth moves, both parents are lost; |
> | 《易冒》亦云：妻财动，父母参商。 | 《易冒》也讲：妻财爻动，父母就成了参星和商星，彼此见不到面（不和睦、要分离）。 | the Yi Mao also says: when the Wealth line moves, the parents become like the stars Shen and Shang, never to meet (estranged and parted). |
> | 俱以财动则伤克父母，未说旺也。 | 这几本都是说财爻动了就伤克父母，没有说旺。 | All of them say that a moving Wealth line harms and controls the parents; none says a strong one does. |
> | 余曰：动则能伤父母，旺则不能伤乎？ | 我说：动了能伤父母，旺了就不能伤吗？ | I say: if it moves it can harm the parents, but if it is strong it cannot? |
> | 假令春天占卦，财爻属木，谓之女命旺而可取，但父母爻定然属土，如此当权得令之财爻，有不克休囚之父母爻者乎？ | 假如春天占卦，财爻属木，说这是女方命旺、可以娶，但父母爻必定属土，这样当权得令的财爻，有不克那休囚的父母爻的吗？ | Suppose the divination is made in spring and the Wealth line is Wood; we call it a strong lot for the woman and worth taking, yet the Resource line, the parents, must be Earth: a Wealth line holding power and in season like this, is there any that does not control a resting, trapped Resource line? |
> | 何必曰动？ | 何必说「动」呢？ | Why must it be said to be 'moving'? |
> | 殊不知昔人言动而不言旺者，正虑后人驳议，故讳言其旺，不得已而言动也，岂知亦被看破耶？ | 殊不知前人只说动而不说旺，正是担心后人驳斥，所以避讳说旺，不得已才说动，哪知道也被看破了呢？ | It is not realized that the men of old spoke of moving and not of strength precisely because they feared later objections, so they avoided mentioning strength and said 'moving' only under compulsion; who would have thought this too would be seen through? |
> | 或曰：据尔之论，则何以为法？ | 有人问：照你的说法，那用什么办法呢？ | Someone says: by your argument, then what is the method? |
> | 余曰：今分占之法也，先要财官旺相，既得吉卦，再占妨克父母否，是其法也。 | 我说：现在用分占的办法：先要财、官旺相，得到吉卦以后，再另占一卦问妨不妨克父母，这就是办法。 | I say: the method now is to divine separately: first Wealth and Pressure must be strong and the hexagram auspicious, and then divine again whether it harms or controls the parents; that is the method. |
