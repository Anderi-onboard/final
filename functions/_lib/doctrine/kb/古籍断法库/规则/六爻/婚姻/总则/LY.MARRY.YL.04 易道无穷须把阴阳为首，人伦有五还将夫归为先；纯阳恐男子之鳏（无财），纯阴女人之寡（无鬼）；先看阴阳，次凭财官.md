# 易道无穷须把阴阳为首，人伦有五还将夫归为先；纯阳恐男子之鳏（无财），纯阴女人之寡（无鬼）；先看阴阳，次凭财官

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 论婚姻，先看阴阳，再凭财爻、官爻。卦如果纯阳或者没有财爻，一定伤妻；纯阴的卦或者缺少官爻，丈夫要早死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In judging marriage, first look at yin and yang, then rely on Wealth and Pressure. If the hexagram is pure yang or has no Wealth line, the wife will surely be harmed; a pure yin hexagram, or one lacking a Pressure line, means the husband dies early.

## 《易林补遗·婚姻嫁娶章第十》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 517 行 · Yilin Buyi (Supplement to the Forest of Changes), "Chapter 10: marriage and giving in marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 易道无穷，须把阴阳为首； | 易道没有穷尽，须以阴阳为首； | The way of the Changes is without end, and yin and yang must come first; |
> | 人伦有五，还将夫归为先。 | 人伦有五种，还要把夫妇之归排在前面。 | there are five human relations, and the joining of husband and wife comes first among them. |
> | 纯阳，恐男子之鳏，无财可比； | 纯阳（全阳）的卦，怕男子成了鳏夫，因为没有财爻可以比配； | A pure yang hexagram: fear that the man becomes a widower, since there is no Wealth line to match him; |
> | 纯阴，女人之寡，无鬼亦然。 | 纯阴（全阴）的卦，女人成了寡妇，没有官鬼爻也是这样。 | a pure yin hexagram: the woman becomes a widow, and it is the same when there is no Pressure line. |

^q2

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

> [!quote]+ [[易林补遗]] 第 518 行 · Yilin Buyi, prose explanation under the verse
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 凡论婚姻之事，先看阴阳，次凭财官，卦若纯阳或无财者，定见伤妻。 | 凡是论婚姻的事，先看阴阳，再凭财、官；卦如果纯阳或者没有财爻，一定伤妻。 | In judging any matter of marriage, first look at yin and yang, then rely on Wealth and Pressure; if the hexagram is pure yang or has no Wealth line, the wife will surely be harmed. |
> | 纯阴之卦、或缺官爻，夫当早丧。 | 纯阴的卦，或者缺少官爻，丈夫要早死。 | A pure yin hexagram, or one lacking a Pressure line, means the husband dies early. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「人伦有五，还将夫归为先」的「夫归」，原文没有解释；译作「夫妇之归」是取上下文。前面括号里的一行是章的提纲，这里去掉括号引。
>
> *Note:* The phrase 'the return of the husband' in 'there are five human relations, and the return of the husband comes first' is not explained in the original; it is rendered as the joining of husband and wife from the context. The bracketed line before it is the chapter's outline, quoted here without the brackets.
