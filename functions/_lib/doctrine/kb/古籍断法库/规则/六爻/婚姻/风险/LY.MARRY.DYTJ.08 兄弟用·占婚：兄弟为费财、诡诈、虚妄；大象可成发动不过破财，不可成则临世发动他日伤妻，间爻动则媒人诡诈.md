# 兄弟用·占婚：兄弟为费财、诡诈、虚妄；大象可成发动不过破财，不可成则临世发动他日伤妻，间爻动则媒人诡诈

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 兄弟爻是与我五行相同的，不能带来福，也不至于大凶，不过是破败、克剥、阻滞之神；怕官鬼发动来制它，喜欢父母兴旺让它有依靠；发动就伤克妻财、扶持子孙。占婚姻，兄弟爻是耗费钱财、诡诈、虚妄：大象能成的，发动不过是破财；不能成的，兄弟临世发动，日后必伤妻；在间爻上发动，一定是媒人诡诈虚妄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Peer is what shares my element; it cannot bring fortune and cannot bring great ill, being nothing but the spirit of ruin, plunder and obstruction; it fears Pressure moving to hold it in check and is glad when Resource thrives and gives it support; moving, it injures and controls Wealth and supports Output. In a marriage divination Peer means expense, trickery and falsehood: if the overall image allows the match, its moving is no more than a loss of money; if it cannot be made, Peer moving on Self surely means the wife will be harmed some day, and moving on a between line means the go-between is tricky and false.
