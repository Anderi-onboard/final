# 妻犯夫家之杀，妻破夫家：夫家破耗二杀所临之位被妻身命爻犯之而克夫身命爻，或妻命临月破、兄弟加白虎发动，主破夫家

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻子犯了夫家的煞，会败坏夫家：夫家的破、耗二煞所临的位置，被妻子的身命爻犯到，并克了丈夫的身命爻；王洪绪说，妻子的命临月破、兄弟带白虎发动，也是破夫家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the wife offends the stars of her husband's house, she ruins the husband's house: where the two stars of ruin and waste of the husband's house sit, if the wife's Body or Life line offends them and controls the husband's Body or Life line, she ruins his house. Wang Hongxu says the same when the wife's Life line falls on a Month-break and a Peer line carrying the White Tiger moves.
