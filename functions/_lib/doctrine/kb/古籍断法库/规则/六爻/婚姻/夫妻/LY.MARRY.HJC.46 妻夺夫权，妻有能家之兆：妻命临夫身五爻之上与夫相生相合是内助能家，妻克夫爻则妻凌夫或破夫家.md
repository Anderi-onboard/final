# 妻夺夫权，妻有能家之兆：妻命临夫身五爻之上与夫相生相合是内助能家，妻克夫爻则妻凌夫或破夫家

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻子的命爻临在丈夫身爻的五爻之上，与丈夫相生相合，是得到贤内助、能持家的兆头；如果妻爻克夫爻，是妻子欺凌丈夫，或者败坏夫家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the wife's Life line sits above the husband's Body line on the fifth line and generates and combines with the husband, it is a sign of a helpmate who can run the household; if the wife's line controls the husband's line, the wife overbears her husband or ruins his family.

## 《黄金策·家宅》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8030 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Chapter 19: The household", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻夺夫权，妻有能家之兆。 | 妻子夺了丈夫的权，妻子有能持家的兆头。 | The wife takes the husband's authority: the wife shows a sign of being able to run the household. |

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻命临夫五爻之上，与夫相生相合，得内助能家之兆； | 妻子的命爻临在丈夫五爻之上，与丈夫相生相合，是得到贤内助、能持家的兆头； | If the wife's Life line sits above the husband's fifth line and generates and combines with the husband, it is a sign of a helpmate who can run the household; |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「临夫身五爻之上」的意思书里没有解释，译文照字面写作「sits above the husband's Body line on the fifth line」。卜筮正宗此句作「临夫五爻之上」，少一个「身」字。
>
> *Note:* The book does not explain 'sitting above the husband's Body on the fifth line', which is translated literally. The Bushi Zhengzong has 'above the husband's fifth line', without the word for 'Body'.
