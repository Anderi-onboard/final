# 克我为官像夫、我克为财像妇：男占女以财为要、女占男以官为先，代占也一样；官旺相女家吉、财兴隆男得良配，官空绝妇多寡、财破散夫终鳏

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 克我的是官鬼，代表丈夫、男子；我克的是妻财，代表妻子、女人：官是男、财是女，这是定理。男方占女方的婚事，以妻财为要；女方占男方的婚事，以官鬼为先；就算是父母、兄弟、朋友替他们占，也是把财当作妻、把官当作夫。所以官爻旺相，是女方家的吉占；妻财兴旺，是男子的好配偶；官爻落空、断绝，女子择夫多半落得孤身一人；妻财破散，男子择妻会终身无妻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* What controls me is Pressure, which stands for the husband, the man; what I control is Wealth, which stands for the wife, the woman: Pressure is male and Wealth is female, a fixed principle. A man divining a woman's marriage takes Wealth as the key; a woman divining a man's puts Pressure first; even when parents, brothers or friends divine for them, Wealth is still taken as the wife and Pressure as the husband. So a strong Pressure line is a lucky divination for the woman's family and a thriving Wealth line a good match for the man; if Pressure is Void or cut off, a woman choosing a husband is often left alone, and if Wealth is broken or scattered, a man choosing a wife stays wifeless to the end.
