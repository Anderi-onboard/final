# 妻之富贵贫贱：未娶从父断、出嫁从夫断；看外卦本宫父母爻（不现看伏，再不现以生妻之爻为妻母、克妻母之爻为岳父）；带禄马贵人宦家女，带财禄德福生气富家女，带咸池沐浴玄武休囚死气贫贱家女；虎贵武职女、虎刃劫杀军匠女、勾土田家女、雀火旺财牙人女、玄武红艳淫家女、刑害亡劫无气贱人女；蛇分九流艺术之女

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问妻子的富贵贫贱：还没娶，按她父亲那边断；出嫁了，按丈夫这边断。没娶的时候，只看外卦本宫的父母爻；外卦的父母爻不出现，就看伏爻；不现又不伏，就把生妻爻的那个爻当妻母，克妻母的爻当岳父，和真的父母一样分阴阳、真假来断。妻父妻母爻带禄、马、贵人，是官宦人家的女儿；带财、禄、德、福、生气，是富家女；带咸池、沐浴、玄武和休囚死气，是贫贱人家的女儿；带白虎和贵人，武职的女儿；带白虎、刃、劫杀，军人工匠的女儿；带勾陈、土旺相，农家女；带朱雀、火、财旺，牙人的女儿；带玄武、红艳，行为放荡人家的女儿；带刑害亡劫又无气，低贱人的女儿；再加金、白虎、刑、刃，是不务农而追逐末业者的女儿。螣蛇逢生旺，是九流人的女儿；螣蛇逢冲并、墓、合，是有艺术技能的人的女儿，按五行定是哪一种技艺。衰败就贫，空绝就断绝，生合世爻能得她家的荫庇，刑害冲克世爻就被她家欺凌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask the wealth, rank, poverty or lowliness of the wife: before the marriage, judge from her father's side; once married, from the husband's. Before the marriage, look only at the Resource line of the home palace in the outer trigram; if it does not appear, look at the hidden line; if it neither appears nor is hidden, take the line that generates the wife's line as the wife's mother and the line that controls the wife's mother as the father-in-law, and judge them like real parents by dividing yin and yang, true and false. The wife's father and mother lines carrying Emolument, the Horse and the Nobleman: a daughter of an official family; carrying Wealth, Emolument, Virtue, Fortune and the Birth-breath: a rich family; carrying the Xianchi star, the Bathing stage, the Dark Tortoise and the breaths of rest, trap and death: a poor and lowly family; the White Tiger and the Nobleman: a military officer's daughter; the White Tiger, the blade and the Robbery star: a soldier's or artisan's; Hook Earth with strong Earth: a farmer's; the Vermilion Bird and Fire with strong Wealth: a broker's; the Dark Tortoise and the Red Allure star: a licentious household's; punishment, harm, the Loss and Robbery stars without breath: a low person's; with Metal, the White Tiger, punishment and blade added: one who does not farm but pursues the lesser trades. The Coiling Snake in birth or strength: a daughter of the nine schools; the Coiling Snake meeting clash, merging, tomb or combination: a craftsman's or artist's, the Five Phases deciding which craft. Weak and ruined means poor; Void or extinct means cut off; generating or combining with the Self line, one receives their protection; punishing, harming, clashing with or controlling the Self line, one is bullied by them.

> [!note] 按
> 「岳父」在这里指「克妻母之爻」这一个爻代表的人，译作 father-in-law；「九流人」指各行各业的人；「妻母」「岳父」都是用爻来推的。
>
> *Note:* 'Father-in-law' here means the person represented by the line that controls the wife's mother; the 'nine schools' means people of all trades; the wife's mother and father-in-law are both reckoned by lines.
