# 男占女逢二官恐已另许、女占男遇二财恐再另求，财化财官化官夫妇有二；男占女宜世阳、女占男宜世阴，世应升降相配，反之则阴阳相背

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男方占女方而卦中有两个官鬼，可能怀疑女方又许了别家；女方占男方而有两个妻财，可能担心男方另外再求娶。财化财、官化官，都是夫妇有二。男占女，宜世阳、应阳，世升应降；女占男，宜世阴、应阳，世降应升；反过来就是阴阳相背。以上这些，虽然不是婚姻吉凶的主要依据，但见到这样的象，也会应在人事上。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A man divining a woman who meets two Pressure lines may suspect she has been promised again; a woman divining a man who meets two Wealth lines may fear he will seek another. Wealth changing into Wealth or Pressure changing into Pressure both mean the husband or wife is two. For a man divining a woman it suits Self yang and Other yang, Self rising and Other falling; for a woman divining a man it suits Self yin and Other yang, Self falling and Other rising; the reverse is yin and yang turned against each other. These points, though not the chief basis of luck in a marriage, still answer in human affairs when the images appear.

> [!note] 按
> 男占女那一句写作「世阳应阳」，而女占男是「世阴应阳」；若说「反之」就是阴阳相背，男占女那句读起来像是「世阳应阴」的笔误，但书里没有别的文字可以核对，译文照原文译。「升」「降」书里没有解释，也照字面译。
>
> *Note:* The man-divining-woman sentence reads 'Self yang, Other yang', while the woman-divining-man sentence reads 'Self yin, Other yang'; since 'the reverse' is called yin and yang at odds, the first looks like a slip for 'Self yang, Other yin', but nothing else in the book can be checked against it, so it is translated as written. 'Rising' and 'falling' are not explained and are translated literally.
