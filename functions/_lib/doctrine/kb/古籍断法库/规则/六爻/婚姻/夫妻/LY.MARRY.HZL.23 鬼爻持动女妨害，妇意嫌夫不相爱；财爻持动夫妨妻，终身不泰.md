# 鬼爻持动女妨害，妇意嫌夫不相爱；财爻持动夫妨妻，终身不泰

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻持世而动，对女方有妨害，妻子心里嫌丈夫，夫妻不相爱；财爻持世而动，丈夫妨碍妻子，这样一辈子都不会安泰。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Pressure line that holds the Self and moves harms the woman: the wife dislikes her husband and they do not love each other; a Wealth line that holds the Self and moves means the husband hinders the wife, and all their life will not be at peace.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「鬼爻持动」「财爻持动」的「持」，原文没有写持什么；这里按婚姻章一路的用法读作「持世」。
>
> *Note:* The text does not say what the line 'holds' in 'a Pressure line that holds and moves'; it is read as 'holds the Self line', as elsewhere in the marriage chapters.
