# 若父动而子孙墓绝，为嗣求婚：父旺动、子孙墓绝，子息少；大人占，是无子而娶

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚遇到父爻旺相发动，子孙墓绝，日后子息一定少；如果占的是长辈（大人）的婚姻，有这种象，是因为没有儿子而再娶；父爻持身、持世的也一样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining marriage, if the Resource line is thriving and moving and Output is in the tomb or cut off, children later will surely be few; if it is an elder's marriage that is divined, this image means he is marrying because he has no son; Resource holding the Body or the Self is the same.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6340 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 若父动而子孙墓绝，为嗣求婚。 | 如果父母爻发动而子孙墓绝，是为了子嗣而求婚。 | If Resource moves and Output falls into the tomb or is cut off, the marriage is sought for the sake of an heir. |
