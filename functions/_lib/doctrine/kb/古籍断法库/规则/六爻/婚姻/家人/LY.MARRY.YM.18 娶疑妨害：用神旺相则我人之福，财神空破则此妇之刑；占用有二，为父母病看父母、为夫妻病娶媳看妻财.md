# 娶疑妨害：用神旺相则我人之福，财神空破则此妇之刑；占用有二，为父母病看父母、为夫妻病娶媳看妻财

六爻 · 婚姻 · 家人 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 娶亲而怀疑她会带来妨害：用神旺相，就是我家里人的福气；财神落空、破，就是这个女人带来的刑克。小注说，这类占用神有两种：为父母生病而娶妻，怀疑妻子妨害，如果父母爻旺，就是父母的福气，不是她的妨害；为夫妻生病而娶儿媳，怀疑儿媳妨害，如果妻财旺，就是儿媳的贤良，不是她的刑克。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Marrying while doubting that she will bring harm: if the Subject is strong, she is a blessing to my people; if the Wealth spirit is Void or broken, the scourge comes from this woman. The note says there are two Subjects to use in such a divination: when marrying a wife because a parent is ill, and doubting that she will do harm, if the Resource line is strong it is the parents' blessing and not harm from her; when marrying a daughter-in-law because husband and wife are ill, and doubting that she will do harm, if Wealth is strong it is the daughter-in-law's goodness and not her scourge.

> [!note] 按
> 「娶媳为夫妻病」的「夫妻」书里没有说是谁，可能指家里的长辈夫妇；译文照字面写作 husband and wife。「我人」译作「我家里人」。
>
> *Note:* The book does not say whose 'husband and wife' are ill in 'marrying a daughter-in-law because husband and wife are ill'; it may mean the elder couple of the house, and it is translated literally. 'My people' is rendered as the people of my household.
