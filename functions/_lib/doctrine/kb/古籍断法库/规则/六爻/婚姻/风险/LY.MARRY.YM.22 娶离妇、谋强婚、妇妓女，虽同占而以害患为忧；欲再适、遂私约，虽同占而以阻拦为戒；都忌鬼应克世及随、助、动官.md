# 娶离妇、谋强婚、妇妓女，虽同占而以害患为忧；欲再适、遂私约，虽同占而以阻拦为戒；都忌鬼应克世及随、助、动官

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 娶离过婚的女子、谋求强行成婚、娶妓女为妻，虽然算同一类占，忧虑的是祸害后患；想再嫁、想实现私下的约定，虽然也算同一类占，要戒的是遭到阻拦；这几种都忌官鬼、应爻克世，以及随官入墓、助鬼伤身、官爻发动。小注说：这和躲避祸患是同一类，以官鬼为忌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Marrying a divorced woman, arranging a forced marriage, making a courtesan one's wife: though they are all called the same kind of divination, the worry is harm and trouble; wishing to marry again, or carrying out a private pledge: though also called the same kind, the caution is obstruction; all of them fear Pressure or the Other line controlling Self, and Pressure that is followed into the tomb, helped, or moving. The note says: this is the same as avoiding trouble, and Pressure is the taboo.

> [!note] 按
> 「随、助、动官」是「随官入墓」「助鬼伤身」和官爻发动的简写（见 LY.MARRY.YM.02）。「离妇」译作离过婚的女子，「妇妓女」是把妓女娶作妻子。
>
> *Note:* 'Following, helping, moving Pressure' abbreviates Pressure followed into the tomb, helped to harm the person, and Pressure moving (see LY.MARRY.YM.02). 'Divorced woman' renders the book's 'separated wife', and 'making a courtesan one's wife' is taking a courtesan as a bride.
