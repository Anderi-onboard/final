# 明六神诀·青龙：属木属阳，持世及交重者为财喜、为婚姻、为孕育、为姻亲，附于六爻无有不吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 青龙属木、属阳，春天旺，亥月长生，卯月最旺，在未、申墓绝。它持世或发动，主财喜、婚姻、孕育、姻亲；附在哪一爻上都吉而亨通。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Azure Dragon belongs to Wood and is yang; it thrives in spring, is born at Hai, peaks at Mao, and is in the tomb and extinct at Wei and Shen. If it holds Self or moves, it means joy over wealth, marriage, pregnancy and in-law relations; attached to any of the six lines it is lucky and smooth.
