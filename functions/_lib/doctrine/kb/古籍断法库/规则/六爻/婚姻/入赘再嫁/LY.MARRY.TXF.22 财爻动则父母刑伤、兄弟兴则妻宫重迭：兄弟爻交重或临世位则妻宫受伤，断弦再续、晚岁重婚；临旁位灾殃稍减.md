# 财爻动则父母刑伤、兄弟兴则妻宫重迭：兄弟爻交重或临世位则妻宫受伤，断弦再续、晚岁重婚；临旁位灾殃稍减

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻动，父母（父母爻）就被刑伤；兄弟爻兴旺，妻子这一宫就重叠。兄弟爻交重，或者临在世爻的位置上，这个人的妻宫受伤，一定是断了弦再续，晚年重新结婚。如果不临世位而临在别的爻上，灾殃就稍轻一些。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Wealth line moves, the parents (the Resource line) are punished and injured; if the Peer line flourishes, the wife's place is doubled. If the Peer line is doubled by moving, or sits on the Self position, the man's wife place is injured, and it surely means the string is snapped and mended: a second marriage in later years. If it sits on another position, the calamity is somewhat lighter.

> | 以是推之，学者自当通变。 | 照这个道理推下去，学的人自然应当灵活变通。 | Reasoning from this, the student should adapt flexibly. |

> [!note] 按
> 「断弦再续」是妻子去世后再娶；这里的「妻宫重叠」指妻子这一宫出现两次，即前后两位妻子。同一段注里后面几句（父母动难为子息、官鬼兴损害弟兄、子孙持世财爻有气一生衣禄）讲的是子嗣、兄弟和衣禄，没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'The string snapped and mended' means marrying again after a wife's death; 'the wife's place doubled' means the wife's place appears twice, that is, two wives one after the other. The later sentences of the same note (a moving Resource line hinders children, a rising Pressure line harms brothers, Output on the Self line with a Wealth line that has qi brings a lifetime of clothing and salary) are about children, brothers and livelihood and are not included.
