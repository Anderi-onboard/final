# 占来意：旺相则来问婚姻官职，休囚则是争财官退位；卦与世爻同与日则必是六畜及婚姻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 取象 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 要知道占卜的人为什么事来：先看旺相休囚，旺相是婚姻、官职的事，休囚是争财、丢官的事。卦、世爻和日辰相同的话，来问的一定是六畜和婚姻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know what the diviner has come about, first look at whether the qi is strong, resting or trapped: strong, it is marriage and office; resting or trapped, it is quarrels over money and an official losing his place. If the hexagram and the Self line are the same as the Day, it must be livestock and marriage.
