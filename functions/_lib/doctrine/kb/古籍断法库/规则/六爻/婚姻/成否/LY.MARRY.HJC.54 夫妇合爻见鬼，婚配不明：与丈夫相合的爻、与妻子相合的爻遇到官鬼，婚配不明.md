# 夫妇合爻见鬼，婚配不明：与丈夫相合的爻、与妻子相合的爻遇到官鬼，婚配不明

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 与丈夫相合的爻、与妻子相合的爻，如果见到官鬼，婚配不明；凡是合爻见鬼都是这样。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the line that combines with the husband and the line that combines with the wife meet Pressure, the match is unclear; so it is whenever a combining line meets Pressure.

## 《黄金策·家宅》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8083 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Chapter 19: The household", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 夫妇合爻见鬼，婚配不明； | 与夫妇相合的爻遇到鬼，婚配不明； | Where the lines that combine with husband and wife meet Pressure, the match is unclear; |

^q1
