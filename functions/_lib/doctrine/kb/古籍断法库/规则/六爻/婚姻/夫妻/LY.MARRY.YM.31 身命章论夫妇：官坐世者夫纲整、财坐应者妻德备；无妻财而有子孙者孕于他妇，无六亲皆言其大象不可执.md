# 身命章论夫妇：官坐世者夫纲整、财坐应者妻德备；无妻财而有子孙者孕于他妇，无六亲皆言其大象不可执

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占身命时看夫妇：官鬼坐在世爻上，丈夫的家法整肃；妻财坐在应爻上，妻子的德行完备。卦里没有妻财、却有子孙，孩子是别的女人怀的；没有父母、却有兄弟，是异母所生。小注提醒：卦里缺某个六亲，说的只是大概的象，也许是以前受了伤、也许是以后要受损，不可以拘泥。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Judging husband and wife in a reading of life and fate: with Pressure sitting on Self the husband's rule is orderly; with Wealth sitting on Other the wife's virtue is complete. A hexagram with no Wealth but an Output line means the child was conceived by another woman; with no Resource but a Peer line, the child is born of a different mother. The small note warns: when a hexagram lacks a relative, it speaks only of the large image, harm that came before or loss to come after, and is not to be held to rigidly.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章是替问自己一生的人断的，不是专为婚姻问的；夫妻这两句是同一段里讲六亲配置的一部分。原文接着还有「卦空破而出寒微、宫旺相而产豪富」等，讲出身，与夫妻无关，没有收。「夫纲」指丈夫在家里的主持和法度，「妻德」指妻子的德行；书里没有再解释。
>
> *Note:* This chapter judges for a person asking about a whole life, not specially about marriage; the two lines on husband and wife are part of a passage on how the relatives are placed. The original goes on with 'a Void or broken hexagram means a humble birth, a strong palace a wealthy birth' and so on, about birth and rank and not husband and wife, which are not taken. 'Husband's rule' means the husband's management and discipline in the house, and 'wife's virtue' the wife's good conduct; the book does not explain them further.
