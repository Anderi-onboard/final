# 妻在间爻由女方亲人主婚，夫在间爻由男方亲人主婚，得时旺相皆许成；出现忌日冲，世动男不肯，应动女生疑；用神动成也分离；间动有阻隔

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻子（财爻）在间爻上，女方有亲人做主婚人；丈夫（官鬼爻）在间爻上，男方有亲人做主婚人；只要得时旺相，都许可成。用神出现时怕被日辰冲；世爻动，男方不肯；应爻动，女方起疑。用神如果发动，即使成了也会分离。间爻动有阻隔，或者是媒人化作鬼爻；如果占的是女子的美丑，另看下一条。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the wife (Wealth) is on a between line, a relative of the woman's side presides at the marriage; if the husband (Pressure) is on a between line, a relative of the man's side presides; so long as it is in season and strong, all may be made. The Subject, when it appears, fears being clashed by the Day; if the Self line moves the man will not agree, if the Other line moves the woman grows doubtful. If the Subject moves, even if made it ends in separation. A moving between line means obstruction, or the go-between turning into Pressure; if the question is the woman's looks, see the next unit.
