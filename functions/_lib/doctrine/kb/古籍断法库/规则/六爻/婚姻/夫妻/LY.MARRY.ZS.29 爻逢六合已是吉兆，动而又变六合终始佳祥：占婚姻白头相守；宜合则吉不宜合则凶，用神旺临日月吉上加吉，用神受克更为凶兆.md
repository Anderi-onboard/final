# 爻逢六合已是吉兆，动而又变六合终始佳祥：占婚姻白头相守；宜合则吉不宜合则凶，用神旺临日月吉上加吉，用神受克更为凶兆

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 爻逢六合，已经是吉兆；动了又变成六合，从头到尾都好。占婚姻，就是白头相守。合是吉是凶，要看这件事适不适合聚合：婚姻、合伙、功名、钱财、兄弟同居这类事遇合是吉，诉讼、冤仇、犹疑的怪事、生产遇合反而不利（怀孕倒是安稳）。以上各种占问，用神旺又临日月的，吉上加吉；用神被克，更是凶兆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A line meeting Six-combine is already an auspicious sign, and if it moves and changes into a Six-combine again it is fortunate from start to finish. For a marriage that is keeping company to white hair. Whether a combination is good depends on whether the matter suits gathering: marriage, partnership, office, money and brothers living together are helped by a combination, whereas a lawsuit, a grudge, a doubtful or strange matter and childbirth are hindered by it (pregnancy, however, is secure). In all of these, where the Subject is strong and is the Day or Month itself it is good and better still; where the Subject is controlled it is an even worse omen.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段紧接在「六冲卦变六合」那一条的后面（LY.MARRY.ZS.27），第一句「爻逢六合」承的是那里最后一句。「管鲍雷陈」是四个人：管仲、鲍叔牙，雷义、陈重，都是古书里交情极深的朋友。「占兄弟」的兄弟指同胞兄弟，不是六亲里的兄弟爻。
>
> *Note:* This passage follows directly on the Six-clash-changing-into-Six-combine entry (LY.MARRY.ZS.27), and its first sentence takes up the last sentence there. 'Guan, Bao, Lei, Chen' are four men: Guan Zhong and Bao Shuya, Lei Yi and Chen Zhong, friends famous in old books for their devotion. 'Divining brothers' means brothers by blood, not the Peer line among the six relations.
