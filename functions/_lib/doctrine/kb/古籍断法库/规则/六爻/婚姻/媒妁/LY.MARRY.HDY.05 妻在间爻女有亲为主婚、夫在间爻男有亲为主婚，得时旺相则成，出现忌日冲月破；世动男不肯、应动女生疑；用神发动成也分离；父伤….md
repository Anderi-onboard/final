# 妻在间爻女有亲为主婚、夫在间爻男有亲为主婚，得时旺相则成，出现忌日冲月破；世动男不肯、应动女生疑；用神发动成也分离；父伤损长亲，间动阻隔或媒人作鬼

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻子（财爻）在间爻上，女方有亲人做主婚人；丈夫（鬼爻）在间爻上，男方有亲人做主婚人。得时旺相就能成；用神出现时，忌日冲、月破。世爻动，男方不肯；应爻动，女方起疑。用神发动，即使成了也见分离。父母爻受伤，损害长辈；间爻动，有阻隔；或者媒人又化作鬼爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the wife (the Wealth line) is on a between line, a relative of the woman's side presides at the marriage; if the husband (the Pressure line) is on a between line, a relative of the man's side presides. If it is in season and strong it can be made; when the Subject appears, it fears a clash from the Day and a Month-break. If the Self line moves the man will not agree; if the Other line moves the woman grows doubtful. If the Subject moves, even if made it ends in separation. If the Resource line is harmed the elders are injured; a moving between line means obstruction; or the go-between turns into Pressure again.

> [!note] 按
> 「妻在同爻」「夫在同爻」的「同爻」多半是「间爻」之误（《火珠林》同一句作「妻在间爻」，LY.MARRY.HZL.05），译文按「间爻」译。这一节和《火珠林》「占婚姻」里的几句几乎相同，词句略有出入。
>
> *Note:* 'Same line' in 'the wife on the same line' and 'the husband on the same line' is most likely a slip for 'between line' (the Huozhulin has 'the wife on a between line', LY.MARRY.HZL.05) and is translated so. This passage is almost the same as several sentences in the Huozhulin's 'divining marriage', with small differences of wording.
