# 二鬼争权水父冲，钱玉莲逢汝权于江浒：二鬼发动都来生合财爻，又遇水父来冲，财爻落空避之，主两夫争权、父母逼勒，而己有守节之操

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里如果有两个官鬼都发动，都来生合财爻，又遇到属水的父母爻来刑冲克害，财爻落在空亡里避开的，一定是两个男人争夺她、父母逼迫她的样子；而她自己有守节的节操，所以躲进空亡。像孙汝权对钱玉莲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If two Pressure lines in the hexagram both move and both generate and combine with the Wealth line, and a Resource line of Water comes to punish, clash and control it, with the Wealth line falling into Void to avoid it, it is the picture of two men contending for her and her parents forcing her; she herself holds to her chastity and so hides in Void. Like Sun Ruquan and Qian Yulian.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 钱玉莲、孙汝权出自《荆钗记》：钱玉莲嫁王十朋，孙汝权是想夺她的富家子，后来她被逼改嫁、投江。这里只用它说两男争一女、女子守节的情形。
>
> *Note:* Qian Yulian and Sun Ruquan are from the play Jingchai Ji: Qian Yulian married Wang Shipeng, and Sun Ruquan was the rich young man who wanted to take her; she was later pressed to marry again and threw herself into the river. It is used here only for two men contending over one woman and the woman keeping faith.
