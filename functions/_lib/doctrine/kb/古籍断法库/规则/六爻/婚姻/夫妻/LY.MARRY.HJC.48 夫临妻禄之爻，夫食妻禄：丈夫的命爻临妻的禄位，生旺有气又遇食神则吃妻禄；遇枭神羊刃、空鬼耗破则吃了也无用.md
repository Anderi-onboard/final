# 夫临妻禄之爻，夫食妻禄：丈夫的命爻临妻的禄位，生旺有气又遇食神则吃妻禄；遇枭神羊刃、空鬼耗破则吃了也无用

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丈夫的身命爻落在妻子的禄位上（比如妻子甲子年生，禄在寅），生旺有气，又遇食神，丈夫就吃妻子的禄，靠妻子过日子；如果遇到枭神、羊刃，或空、鬼、耗、破等煞，虽然吃妻子的禄也没有用。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the husband's Body or Life line sits on the wife's Emolument place (for a wife born in a Jia-Zi year, her Emolument is at Yin), strong and in season and also meeting the Food spirit, the husband eats off the wife's emolument, living on his wife; if it meets the Owl spirit, the Goat Blade, or Void, ghost, waste and ruin stars, then though he eats the wife's emolument it is of no use.

> | 禄乃甲禄在寅，食乃甲食丙之类。 | 禄是说甲的禄在寅，食是说甲的食神是丙这一类。 | Emolument means Jia's emolument is at Yin; the Food spirit means Jia's food is Bing, and the like. |
> | 如甲子生，妻禄在寅，夫身命爻临之，遇食神，乃食禄顺。 | 比如甲子年生的妻子，禄在寅，丈夫的身命爻落在那里，遇到食神，就是吃禄顺当。 | For example, for a wife born in a Jia-Zi year, her Emolument is at Yin; if the husband's Body or Life line sits there and meets the Food spirit, eating the emolument goes smoothly. |
> | 食不逢空鬼破耗等杀，更值生旺有气者，主夫食妻禄。 | 食神没有遇到空、鬼、破、耗等煞，又生旺有气的话，主丈夫吃妻子的禄。 | If the Food spirit meets no Void, ghost, ruin or waste stars and is also strong and in season, it means the husband eats off the wife's emolument. |
> | 若逢枭神羊刃，空鬼耗破杀，虽食妻禄，亦无用矣。 | 如果遇到枭神、羊刃，或空、鬼、耗、破的煞，虽然吃妻子的禄，也没有用了。 | If it meets the Owl spirit or the Goat Blade, or the Void, ghost, waste and ruin stars, then though he eats the wife's emolument it is of no use. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如妻年甲子生。 | 比如妻子是甲子年生。 | For example, a wife born in a Jia-Zi year. |
> | 禄在寅，夫命临之生旺有气者，主夫食妻禄； | 禄在寅，丈夫的命落在那里，生旺有气的，主丈夫吃妻子的禄； | Her Emolument is at Yin; if the husband's Life sits there, strong and in season, it means the husband eats off the wife's emolument; |
> | 若逢羊刃空鬼耗破，虽食妻禄亦无用矣。 | 如果遇到羊刃或空、鬼、耗、破，虽然吃妻子的禄也没有用了。 | if it meets the Goat Blade or the Void, ghost, waste and ruin stars, then though he eats the wife's emolument it is of no use. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「禄」「食神」「枭神」是子平命理里的说法，书里没有另外解释；这里是把夫妻的生年干支放进卦里来论。
>
> *Note:* 'Emolument', the 'Food spirit' and the 'Owl spirit' are terms of the Ziping birth-chart art, which the book does not explain; here the birth stems and branches of husband and wife are set into the hexagram.
