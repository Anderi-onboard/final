# 妻财用·占婚：财为妇，旺相临青龙吉星必女貌端正，不宜动，动则伤克公姑

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财是我所克的，诸事遇到都吉，只有占父母和文书时不宜见到它；遇兄弟受损，遇子孙更好，遇官鬼会泄我的气；动静都吉。占婚姻，妻财代表妻子：旺相、临青龙等吉星，女子相貌一定端正；不宜发动，动了就伤克公婆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth is what I control, and in every matter meeting it is lucky, except that it is not welcome when divining for parents or documents; meeting Peer it suffers, meeting Output it is better still, meeting Pressure it drains my qi; still or moving, it is lucky. In a marriage divination Wealth stands for the wife: strong and on the Azure Dragon or another lucky star, the woman's looks are surely upright; it should not move, for moving it injures and controls the parents-in-law.
