# 身遭化鬼克刑，班婕妤感伤乎秋扇：卦中官与身世相生合，又化官来克制身世及财爻，先亲爱后弃绝

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦里本来有官鬼，和世爻、卦身相生相合，又化出官鬼来克制世爻、卦身和财爻。断法是动爻为开始，变爻为结局，主先亲爱、后疏绝，像汉朝的班婕妤对汉武帝，起初宠爱，后来疏远；所以看到秋天的扇子而感伤作词，寄托她凄楚的心情。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the hexagram has Pressure from the start, generating and combining with the Self line and the Body, and then changes into Pressure that controls the Self, the Body and the Wealth line, then, since the moving line is the start and the changed line the end, it shows close affection first and rejection after, like Ban Jieyu of Han and the emperor, favored at first and later cast off; hence she wrote a poem in sorrow at seeing the autumn fan, giving voice to her bitter feelings.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1759 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 如卦象六合而世爻化官鬼刑克，以动爻为始，以变爻为终，如汉班姬于成帝始亲爱后疏远，所以见秋扇而感伤，作词以寓其凄楚之意。 | 比如卦象六合，而世爻化出官鬼刑克，以动爻为开始，以变爻为结局，像汉朝班婕妤对汉成帝，起初亲爱、后来疏远，所以看见秋天的扇子感伤，作词寄托她凄楚的心意。 | For example a hexagram of Six-combine in which the Self line changes into a punishing, controlling Pressure line: taking the moving line as the start and the changed line as the end, it is like Ban Jieyu of Han with the emperor Cheng, close at first and later estranged, so that seeing the autumn fan she grieved and wrote a poem giving voice to her bitter feelings. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 班婕妤是汉成帝的妃子，失宠后作《怨歌行》，以秋天被收起的团扇自比。卜筮大全说「汉武帝」，卜筮正宗说「汉成帝」，史实是成帝；译文各照原书。
>
> *Note:* Ban Jieyu was a consort of Emperor Cheng of Han; after losing favor she wrote the 'Song of Resentment', likening herself to a round fan put away in autumn. The Quanshu says 'Emperor Wu of Han' and the Zhengzong 'Emperor Cheng of Han'; the emperor in history is Cheng, and each translation follows its own book.
