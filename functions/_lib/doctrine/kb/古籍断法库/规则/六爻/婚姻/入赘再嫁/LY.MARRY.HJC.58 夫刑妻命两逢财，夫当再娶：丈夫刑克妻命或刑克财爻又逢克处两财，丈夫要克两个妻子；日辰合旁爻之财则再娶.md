# 夫刑妻命两逢财，夫当再娶：丈夫刑克妻命或刑克财爻又逢克处两财，丈夫要克两个妻子；日辰合旁爻之财则再娶

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丈夫刑克妻子的命，或者刑克财爻，又遇到克处的两个财，主丈夫克两个妻子；日辰合旁边爻上的财，主再娶；丈夫带日辰、动爻，带劫、刃、刑等煞，伤妻子的命爻，主妻子遭丈夫毒手。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the husband punishes and controls the wife's Life, or punishes and controls the Wealth line, and also meets two Wealth lines at the place of control, the husband loses two wives; if the Day combines with a Wealth line on another line, he marries again; if the husband, with the Day and a moving line carrying the Robbery, Blade and punishment stars, injures the wife's Life line, she meets her husband's poisonous hand.
