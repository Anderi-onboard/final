# 期日章·婚姻：婚姻本宫内外旺，或然月卦用为仪

六爻 · 婚姻 · 应期 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 期日章说婚姻的应期：本宫内外卦旺的时候，或者以月卦为准。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on days of fulfilment says of the timing of a marriage: when the home palace is strong, inner and outer, or else take the month hexagram as the standard.

## 《断易天机·散见各章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 962 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 婚姻本宫内外旺， | 婚姻的应期，本宫的内卦外卦旺相的时候， | For marriage, when the home palace is strong, inner and outer, |
> | 或然月卦用为仪， | 或者用月卦来做准则。 | or else take the month hexagram as the standard. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句原书后面另有今人的「注释」逐句解释，本库不引。「仪」按上下文是准则。
>
> *Note:* In the book this line is followed by a modern gloss, which this library does not quote. 'Yi' is read from the context as a standard or measure.
