# 诸爻持世诀：父母持世夫妻相克不和同、到老要求他姓之子；兄弟持世克妻财

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻持世，事情忧虑阻塞，身上带着文书和官鬼，夫妻互相克、不和睦，到老要去求别姓的儿子（过继）。兄弟爻持世，克妻财，官司之忧没了结又来，鬼旺要防口舌，自身强反而损失钱财。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With the Resource line holding the Self line, matters are worrying and blocked, one carries documents and Pressure, husband and wife control each other and are not in accord, and in old age one must seek a son of another surname (by adoption). With the Peer line holding the Self line, it controls Wealth, the wife; worry over official matters is not over before more comes; with strong Pressure guard against quarrels; with the Self strong one loses money instead.

## 《卜筮全书·三、诸爻持世诀》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 3048 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "No. 3, rhyme on each line holding the Self", base text

> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 兄弟持世克妻财， | 兄弟爻持世，克妻财， | With the Peer line holding the Self line, it controls Wealth, the wife; |
> | 忧官未了事还来； | 官司的忧虑没了结，事情又来； | worry over official matters is not over before more comes; |
> | 鬼旺正当防口舌， | 鬼旺相，正要防口舌； | with strong Pressure, guard against quarrels; |
> | 身强必定损其财。 | 自身强，一定损失钱财。 | with the Self strong, one will surely lose money. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这首歌诀有六句，这里只收和夫妻有关的父母持世、兄弟持世两句；同样的诀在《火珠林》（第 945 行）和《周易尚占》（第 477 行）里也有。「用求他姓子」是过继别姓的儿子，即养子。
>
> *Note:* The rhyme has more lines; only the two on the Resource and Peer lines holding the Self line, which bear on husband and wife, are included. The same rhyme is in the Huozhulin (line 945) and the Zhouyi Shangzhan (line 477). 'Seeking a son of another surname' means adopting a son of another family.
