# 日逢父合，已期合卺于三星：日辰与父爻相合，或日辰自带文书，成婚的日期已经定下

六爻 · 婚姻 · 应期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰与父爻相合，或者日辰自己带着文书（父母），主成婚的日期已经预先定下。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Day combining with the Resource line, or the Day itself carrying the document (Resource), means the wedding date has already been fixed in advance.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6413 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 日逢父合，已期合卺于三星； | 日辰与父爻相合，已经在三星（出现）的时候约好了成婚。 | The Day combining with the Resource line: the wedding is already set for the time of the Three Stars. |

^q1

> | 日辰与父爻作合，或日辰自带文书，主成婚日期已选定。 | 日辰与父爻相合，或者日辰自己带着文书，主成婚的日期已经选定。 | The Day combining with the Resource line, or the Day itself carrying the document, means the wedding date has been chosen. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「三星」出自《诗经·唐风·绸缪》的「三星在天」「三星在隅」「三星在户」，是婚期的意象；「合卺」是新婚夫妇共饮交杯酒，指成婚。
>
> *Note:* 'The Three Stars' is from the Book of Odes (Tang Feng, 'Chou Miu'), 'the Three Stars are in the sky', 'in the corner', 'at the door', an image for the wedding season; 'sharing the nuptial cup' means the wedding itself.
