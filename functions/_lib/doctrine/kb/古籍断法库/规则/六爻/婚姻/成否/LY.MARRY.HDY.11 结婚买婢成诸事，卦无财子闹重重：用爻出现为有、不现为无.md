# 结婚买婢成诸事，卦无财子闹重重：用爻出现为有、不现为无

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 结婚、买婢女这一类求成的事，卦里没有妻财和子孙，就是困难重重。眉批说明什么叫「无」：用爻在卦里出现叫「有」，用爻不现叫「无」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For marriage, buying a maid and other matters one wants to bring about, if the hexagram has no Wealth or Output line, trouble comes on trouble. The marginal comment explains 'no': the Subject line appearing in the hexagram is 'present', not appearing is 'absent'.

## 《海底眼·有无》原文

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 512 行 · Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Present and absent", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 结婚买婢成诸事， | 结婚、买婢女，成办这些事， | For marriage and buying a maid, bringing such matters about, |
> | 卦无财子闹重重。 | 卦里没有妻财、子孙，就闹得重重叠叠。 | if the hexagram has no Wealth or Output, trouble comes on trouble. |

^q1

## 《海底眼》眉批(书眉上的旁注,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 512 行 · Marginal comment in the Haidi Yan (author unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 用爻出现谓之有， | 用爻在卦里出现，叫做「有」； | The Subject line appearing is called 'present'; |
> | 用爻不现谓之无。 | 用爻不出现，叫做「无」。 | the Subject line not appearing is called 'absent'. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一首诗前两句是「五行忧者先忧否，无鬼忧官喜落空」，讲的是忧事和官讼，没有收。「财子」是妻财和子孙：妻财是妻，子孙是福神，结婚时两个都要有。「无」按眉批的定义是不出现在卦里（比如伏在别爻之下），不是旺衰的衰。
>
> *Note:* The first two lines of this poem, 'in the five elements the worried one worries first about denial; without Pressure he worries about the officials, and is glad if it is Void', speak of worries and lawsuits and are not taken. 'Wealth and Output' are the Wealth line, the wife, and the Output line, the line of fortune; a marriage needs both. 'Absent' by the marginal comment's definition means not appearing in the hexagram (for example hidden beneath another line), and is not the 'weak' of strong and weak.
