# 卦六合者成、六冲者不成；前卦合变冲成后复退、冲化合退后复成；六冲或世应动被日辰合住欲退不得；世应财鬼冲克而日辰动爻生合不成而得人赞成、生合而日辰动爻冲克成而被破；虽生合而临死墓绝胎被刑冲克害，在世鬼则男家不允、在应财则女家不允

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦是六合的成，卦是六冲的不成。本卦合而变冲，成了以后又退；本卦冲而化合，退了以后又成。卦是六冲，或世应动，而被日辰合住的，想退也退不成。世应财鬼互相冲克，逢日辰、动爻来生合的，不成，但得到别人赞助；世应财鬼相生相合，逢日辰、动爻冲克的，成了又被破坏。世应财鬼虽然相生相合，但临着死、墓、绝、胎，被刑冲克害，或者动而化成死、墓、绝、胎而遭刑冲克害的：落在世爻、鬼爻上，男家不答应；落在应爻、财爻上，女家不答应。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A hexagram of Six-combine is made; a hexagram of Six-clash is not. The original hexagram combining and changing into a clash: made, then withdrawn again; clashing and changing into a combination: withdrawn, then made again. A Six-clash hexagram, or the Self and Other moving, held by a combination with the Day: one wants to withdraw and cannot. The Self, Other, Wealth and Pressure lines clashing and controlling, meeting generation or combination from the Day or a moving line: it is not made, yet someone approves and helps it along; generating and combining, meeting a clash or control from the Day or a moving line: made, then broken up. Though the Self, Other, Wealth and Pressure lines generate and combine, if they sit on the death, tomb, extinction or womb stages and are punished, clashed, controlled or harmed, or move and change into those stages and are so harmed: if it is on the Self line or the Pressure line, the man's family does not agree; if it is on the Other line or the Wealth line, the woman's family does not agree.

> [!warning] 打架
> 「冲变合」：这里说「前卦冲而化合，退后复成」（先退后成）；《易林补遗》说未配之前得冲中化合「决不相谐」（LY.MARRY.YL.15），《增删卜易》说「六冲变六合者尤吉，求婚者目下不允，久必成之」（LY.MARRY.ZS.13）。《易隐》和《增删卜易》一致：不是不成，是先退后成。
>
> *Where they disagree:* For a clash changing into a combination this text says 'withdrawn, then made again'; the Yilin Buyi says that before the match is made it 'surely will not be harmonious' (LY.MARRY.YL.15), and the Zengshan Buyi that 'a Six-clash changing into a Six-combine is better still: for one seeking a marriage, although refused for now, it will be made in time' (LY.MARRY.ZS.13). The Yi Yin agrees with the Zengshan Buyi: not that it fails, but that it is withdrawn first and made afterwards.
