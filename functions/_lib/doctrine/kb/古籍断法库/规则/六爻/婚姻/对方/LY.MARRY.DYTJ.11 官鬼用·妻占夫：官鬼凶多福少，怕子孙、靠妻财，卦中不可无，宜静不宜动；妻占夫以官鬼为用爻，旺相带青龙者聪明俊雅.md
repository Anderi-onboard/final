# 官鬼用·妻占夫：官鬼凶多福少，怕子孙、靠妻财，卦中不可无，宜静不宜动；妻占夫以官鬼为用爻，旺相带青龙者聪明俊雅

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼是克我的，大体上凶多福少，怕子孙、靠妻财，发动就伤克兄弟、生扶父母；卦中虽凶却不能没有，宜静不宜动。妻子占丈夫，官鬼是用爻：旺相带青龙的，一定是聪明俊雅的人；加贵人一定有职事，不然也是官宦人家的子弟；在胎、养、沐浴的爻上，如今虽是小官，日后必贵；最宜持世，或者临阳象，都叫得地。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure is what controls me; in general it brings more ill than fortune, fears Output and relies on Wealth; moving, it injures and controls Peer and supports Resource; though ill in a hexagram it must not be missing, and should be still and not move. When a wife divines a husband, Pressure is the Subject line: strong and carrying the Azure Dragon, he is surely an intelligent, handsome and refined man; with the Noble added he surely has a position, and otherwise he is at least a son of an official family; on a line at the Womb, Nurture or Bathing stage, though now a minor official he will surely be eminent some day; it is best holding Self or on a yang image, and either is called being in place.
