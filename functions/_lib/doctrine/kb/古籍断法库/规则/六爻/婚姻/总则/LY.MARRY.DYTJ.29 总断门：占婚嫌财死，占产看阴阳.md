# 总断门：占婚嫌财死，占产看阴阳

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占婚姻，忌财爻死绝；占生产，看阴阳。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In a marriage divination, Wealth dead is disliked; in childbirth, look at yin and yang.

## 《断易天机·散见各章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 2846 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占婚嫌财死， | 占婚姻，嫌财爻死； | For a marriage divination, Wealth dead is disliked; |
> | 占产看阴阳。 | 占生产，看阴阳。 | for childbirth, look at yin and yang. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句后面原书有今人的注释，本库不引；「死」指财爻在十二长生里死、墓、绝一类无力的位置。
>
> *Note:* In the book this verse is followed by a modern gloss, which this library does not quote. 'Dead' means the Wealth line in the death, tomb or extinction stage of the twelve life stages, or a similar powerless place.
