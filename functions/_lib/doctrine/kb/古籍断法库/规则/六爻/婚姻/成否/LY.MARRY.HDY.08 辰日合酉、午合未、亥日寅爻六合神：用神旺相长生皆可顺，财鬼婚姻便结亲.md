# 辰日合酉、午合未、亥日寅爻六合神：用神旺相长生皆可顺，财鬼婚姻便结亲

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日辰和用神爻六合（辰日合酉、午合未、亥日合寅），用神又旺相或长生，事情都顺；占婚姻时，妻财和官鬼爻遇上这样的六合，婚事就结成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Day six-combines the Subject line (a Chen day combines You, Wu combines Wei, a Hai day combines Yin), and the Subject is strong or in its birth state, things all go smoothly; in a marriage divination, when the Wealth and Pressure lines meet such a six-combine, the match is made.

## 《海底眼·六合》原文

> [!quote]+ [[海底眼]] 第 436 行 · Haidi Yan (The Eye at the Bottom of the Sea), "Six-combines", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 辰日合酉午合未， | 辰日合酉，午合未， | A Chen day combines You, Wu combines Wei, |
> | 亥日寅爻六合神， | 亥日和寅爻是六合的神； | and a Hai day and the Yin line are the six-combine spirits; |
> | 旺相长生皆可顺， | 旺相、长生都可以顺， | strong and in season, or in the birth state, all can go smoothly, |
> | 财鬼婚姻便结亲 | 妻财、官鬼（占）婚姻，就结成亲。 | and for Wealth and Pressure in a marriage, the match is made. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「辰日合酉、午合未、亥日寅爻」是举例：辰酉合、午未合、亥寅合。「财鬼」是妻财和官鬼，占婚姻时的两个用神，男占看财、女占看鬼。原文后面括着一条眉批「凶神即凶陈」，前后读不通，没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'A Chen day combines You, Wu combines Wei, a Hai day the Yin line' are examples: Chen-You, Wu-Wei and Hai-Yin are the branch combinations. 'Wealth and Pressure' are the two Subject lines for a marriage, Wealth for a man's divination and Pressure for a woman's. A marginal comment follows in brackets, 'the malign spirit is the malign array', which does not read through with what precedes it and is left out.
