# 世临外卦财爻动、身世命爻合应财，或命爻鬼爻伏墓应财下，俱主入赘；鬼居应财居世，或世鬼属阴应财属阳，非入赘则夫妇相凌

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻临在外卦、财爻动，身爻、世爻、命爻去合应爻上的财，或者命爻、鬼爻伏在墓中、藏在应爻财之下的，都主入赘。鬼爻在应爻、财爻在世爻，或者世爻的鬼属阴、应爻的财属阳的，不是入赘，一定夫妻互相欺凌。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line in the outer trigram with Wealth moving, and the Body, Self or fate line going to combine with the Wealth on the Other line, or the fate or Pressure line hidden in a tomb beneath the Other line's Wealth: all mean marrying into the wife's family. Pressure on the Other line and Wealth on the Self line, or the Self line's Pressure yin and the Other line's Wealth yang: if it is not a marriage into the wife's family, husband and wife will surely bully each other.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 第一句读起来很绕（身、世、命三个爻与应财、伏墓的几种关系），译文照字面，没有替它理顺；最后一句的「蜚入赘」，「蜚」是「非」的转录之误，译作「不是入赘」。
>
> *Note:* The first sentence is convoluted (several relations of the Body, Self and fate lines to the Other line's Wealth and to a hidden tomb) and is translated literally without straightening it out; 'fei ru zhui' in the last sentence has 'fei' (a kind of insect) for 'fei' (not), and is translated as 'if it is not marrying in'.
