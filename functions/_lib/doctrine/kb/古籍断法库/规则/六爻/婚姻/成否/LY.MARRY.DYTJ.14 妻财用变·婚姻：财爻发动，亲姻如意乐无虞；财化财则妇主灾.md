# 妻财用变·婚姻：财爻发动，亲姻如意乐无虞；财化财则妇主灾

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻财爻发动克文书：考试求官都是虚的；买卖、结交官府的财吉利；婚姻亲事如意，快乐无忧。财化财，妇人有灾。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Wealth line moves it controls documents: exams and seeking office come to nothing; trade and dealing with officials bring luck in money; marriage and family ties go as wished, glad and free of worry. Wealth changing into Wealth, the woman meets calamity.
