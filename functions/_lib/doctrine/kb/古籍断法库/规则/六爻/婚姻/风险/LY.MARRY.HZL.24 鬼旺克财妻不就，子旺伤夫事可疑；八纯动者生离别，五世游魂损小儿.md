# 鬼旺克财妻不就，子旺伤夫事可疑；八纯动者生离别，五世游魂损小儿

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 鬼爻旺而克财爻，妻子娶不成；子孙爻旺而伤丈夫，这事可疑。八纯卦动的，主生离别；五世卦、游魂卦，损伤小孩。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A strong Pressure line controlling Wealth: the wife cannot be got; a strong Output line harming the husband: the matter is doubtful. A pure eight-palace hexagram that moves means parting alive; a fifth-generation or Wandering Soul hexagram harms the children.
