# 婚姻从违犹谋望，不可失财官：男占女无财不成、女占男无官不就，贞合冲、世应空是谋事所忌

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 婚姻成不成，就像谋求一件事，不能丢了财、官。小注说：男方占女方，没有财爻就不成；女方占男方，没有官爻就不能成就；贞卦的合冲、世应落空，是谋事所忌讳的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whether a marriage goes forward or falls through is like seeking something: one must not lose Wealth or Pressure. The note says: if a man divines a woman, without Wealth it is not made; if a woman divines a man, without Pressure it is not achieved; the Zhen hexagram's combination-clash and Self and Other Void are what seeking a matter fears.
