# 世获财生，终得妆奁于百两：财爻生合世爻又得日月动爻扶助必有妆奁；临勾陈有妆奁田、临青龙美丽、临螣蛇白虎败病是旧什物

六爻 · 婚姻 · 聘嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看妆奁要看财爻：财爻生合世爻，又有日月、动爻扶助，一定有妆奁；临勾陈，有妆奁田；临青龙，妆奁漂亮；临螣蛇、白虎、败、病等爻，是旧东西；财没有生助，不可乱说；想推多少，看生合妻财那一爻的衰旺；财的衰旺只能推女子的相貌美丑，不能推妆奁厚薄。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To judge the dowry look at the Wealth line: Wealth generating or combining with the Self and supported by the Month, Day or a moving line means there will be a dowry; on Hook Earth, dowry land; on the Azure Dragon, a handsome dowry; on the Coiling Snake, the White Tiger, or the ruined or sick lines, old household goods; if Wealth has no support, do not speak rashly; to reckon how much, judge by the strength of the line that generates or combines with Wealth; the strength of Wealth can only tell the woman's looks, not the richness of the dowry.
