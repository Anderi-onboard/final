# 此婚子嗣有无：子孙旺相或休囚而动、动而化吉皆主有子，化进神回头生有子必多；子孙墓绝、动变鬼、鬼变子孙、父化子子化父父动克子，是我命无儿，成此婚与否也无子，惟多积阴德以培之

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问这门婚事将来有没有子嗣：子孙爻旺相，或者休囚了但动，以及动而化吉的，都主有子；子孙化出进神、化出回头生的，有子一定多。如果子孙爻墓、绝，或者动而变成官鬼，官鬼变成子孙，父化子孙、子孙化父，父动克子孙，都是因为我命里没有儿子，成这门婚也好、不成也好，都没有子嗣；只有多积阴德来培补。书中例：申月丁丑日，因为妾多，占谁的命里有儿子，得革之夬，断为卯木子孙绝在申月、鬼变子孙又逢月破，是这位先生自己的命里没有儿子，与女子的命无关；从八字看也是无子。劝他多积阴德，此后他施舍济贫、成全人的夫妇、修桥铺路、舍药施棺，接连生了两个儿子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Asking whether this marriage will have children: if the Output line is strong, or resting but moving, or moves and changes into good, there will be a son; if Output changes into an Advancing spirit or into a returning generation, there will surely be many. If the Output line meets tomb or extinction, or moves and changes into Pressure, or Pressure changes into Output, or Resource and Output change into each other, or a moving Resource line controls Output, it is because my own fate has no son, and whether or not this marriage is made there will be no child; the only remedy is to pile up hidden merit. The book's case: in a Shen month on a Dingchou day, because he had many concubines, a man divined whose fate would bear a son and got Ge changing into Guai; it was judged that the Mao-wood Output line is in extinction in the Shen month and Pressure changes into Output while meeting the Month-break, so the gentleman's own fate has no son and it has nothing to do with the women's fates; his birth chart also showed no son. He was urged to pile up hidden merit; after that he gave alms to the poor, helped couples in their marriages, mended bridges and roads and gave out medicine and coffins, and had two sons in a row.

> [!note] 按
> 「公」是来占的先生；「公命无子，与女命无干」是说没有儿子出在他自己的命上，不是哪个妾的问题。「平头煞」「魁罡」是八字里的凶煞，书里没有解释；「全人夫妇」大概是成全别人夫妻的事（如劝和、周济），这里译得宽一些。这一章还有一条「［乾按］」是今人的话，不收。
>
> *Note:* 'Gong' is the gentleman who came to divine; 'the gentleman's own fate has no son, and it has nothing to do with the women's fates' says the lack of a son lies in his own fate and not in any one concubine. 'Flat-head killing star' and 'Kuigang' are ill stars in the birth chart, which the book does not explain; 'helping couples' probably means helping other people's marriages, such as reconciling or relieving them, and is translated broadly here. One further note in this chapter, marked 'Qian an', is a modern comment and is not included.
