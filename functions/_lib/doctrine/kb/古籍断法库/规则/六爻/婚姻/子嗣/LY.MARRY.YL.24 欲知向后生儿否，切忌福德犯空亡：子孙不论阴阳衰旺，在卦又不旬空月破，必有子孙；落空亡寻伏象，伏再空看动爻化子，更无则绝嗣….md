# 欲知向后生儿否，切忌福德犯空亡：子孙不论阴阳衰旺，在卦又不旬空月破，必有子孙；落空亡寻伏象，伏再空看动爻化子，更无则绝嗣；出见早生、伏藏迟育；旺相或动来速、休囚或静来迟

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想知道以后生不生儿子，最忌子孙（福德）爻落空亡。子孙爻不论阴阳、衰旺，只要在卦中，又不逢旬空、月破，一定有儿有孙。落了空亡，再找伏神；伏着的子孙再空，就看动爻有没有化出子孙；都没有，一定有绝嗣的叹息。想知道得子的早晚：子孙旺相或者动，来得快；休囚或者静，来得迟；出现的早生，伏藏的迟育。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know whether a son will be born later, above all fear the Output line, the fortune-and-virtue line, falling into Void. Whatever its yin or yang or strength, if the Output line is in the hexagram and is not Void in its cycle or under a Month-break, there will surely be sons and grandsons. If it falls into Void, look for a hidden spirit; if the hidden Output is Void too, look at whether a moving line changes into Output; if none is produced either, there will surely be the sigh of a line without heirs. As to how soon: strong or moving, it comes quickly; resting, trapped or still, it comes late; if it appears, the birth is early; if it is hidden, the child comes late.
