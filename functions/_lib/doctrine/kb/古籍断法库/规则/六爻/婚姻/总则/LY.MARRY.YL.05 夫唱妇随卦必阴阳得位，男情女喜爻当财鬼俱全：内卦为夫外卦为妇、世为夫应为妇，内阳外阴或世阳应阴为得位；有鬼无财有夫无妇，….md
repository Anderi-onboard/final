# 夫唱妇随卦必阴阳得位，男情女喜爻当财鬼俱全：内卦为夫外卦为妇、世为夫应为妇，内阳外阴或世阳应阴为得位；有鬼无财有夫无妇，有财无鬼有妇无夫

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 夫唱妇随，卦里阴阳要得位；男女情投意合，爻里财、鬼要齐全。内卦是夫、外卦是妇，又以世爻为夫、应爻为妇：内阳外阴，或世阳应阴，都算得位，婚姻遇到百年和合。内阴外阳，或世阴应阳，是阴阳交错，夫妻只有一半称心。世应得位而内外卦纯阴纯阳，或内外卦得位而世应纯阴纯阳，都只是半吉。内外卦和世应全是纯阳或纯阴，不成夫妇——自古纯阴不生、纯阳不化，要一阴一阳才成配偶。再看：鬼爻是夫、财爻是妇；有鬼无财是有夫无妇，有财无鬼是有妇无夫；财、鬼齐全，夫妇才能和谐到老。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For the husband to lead and the wife to follow the hexagram must have yin and yang in their places; for man and woman to love and delight in each other, the lines must have Wealth and Pressure complete. The inner trigram is the husband and the outer the wife, and likewise the Self line is the husband and the Other line the wife: inner yang with outer yin, or the Self line yang with the Other yin, count as in their places, and a marriage meeting this is in harmony for a hundred years. Inner yin with outer yang, or the Self yin with the Other yang, is yin and yang crossed, and husband and wife have their wish only by half. If the Self and Other lines are in their places but the inner and outer trigrams are pure yin or pure yang, or the reverse, it is only half auspicious. If the inner and outer trigrams and the Self and Other lines are all pure yang or pure yin, they do not become husband and wife: since antiquity pure yin does not give birth and pure yang does not transform, and only one yin with one yang makes a pair. Then: the Pressure line is the husband and the Wealth line the wife; Pressure without Wealth is a husband without a wife, Wealth without Pressure a wife without a husband; only when both are complete can husband and wife grow old in harmony.
