# 欲知女子如何性：坎聪艮静、兑柔巽恭、坤宽乾刚、离文明、震智慧；容貌：乾面大坤魁肥、艮小坎团圆、兑莹白、震端、巽如雪、离和润

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 想知道女子是什么性情：坎卦主心思聪明，艮卦主沉静，兑卦温和柔顺，巽卦恭顺，坤爻宽厚，乾爻刚正；文雅明白的女子是因为遇到离卦，有智慧的男子是因为见到震卦。看容貌：乾宫面部又大又宽，坤主魁梧肥胖，艮卦身体小，坎爻脸团圆，兑家的女子晶莹白净，震位的妻子儿女奇特而端正，巽体的容颜像瑞雪，离宫温和润泽、好看。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know a woman's nature: Kan means a clever mind, Gen calm and stillness, Dui gentleness, Xun respect, a Kun line generosity and kindness, a Qian line firmness and uprightness; a cultured and bright woman is so because she meets Li, and a wise man because he sees Zhen. For looks: the Qian palace has a face large and broad, Kun a big, stout build, Gen a small body, a Kan line a round face; a woman of the Dui house is lustrous and fair, a wife or child of the Zhen position striking and upright, Xun's complexion is like auspicious snow, and the Li palace is mild, moist and pleasing to look at.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段是按八卦断性情容貌的口诀。《周易尚占》「八卦断例章」、《文王金钱课》里按卦名断事的句子，库里没有收（那是「某卦主某事」的口诀）；这里收，是因为它属于《火珠林》婚姻章本身，而且讲的是「对方是什么人」这一个问题。
>
> *Note:* This passage judges nature and looks by the eight trigrams. The lines that judge matters by hexagram name in the Zhouyi Shangzhan's chapter on the eight trigrams and in the Wenwang Jinqian Ke are not included in this library (they are 'this hexagram means that' rhymes); this one is included because it belongs to the Huozhulin's marriage chapter itself and answers one question, what the other party is like.
