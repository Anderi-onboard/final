# 男婚须财象兴隆、女嫁得官爻旺相；男占妇以财为主决不可无，女占夫用鬼为先

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男子的婚事，财爻的象要兴旺，才能早晚一同欢悦；女子出嫁，官鬼爻要旺相，才能朝夕同欢。男占妻子，以财爻为主，决不可没有；女占丈夫，以鬼爻为先，道理相仿。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a man's marriage the sign of Wealth must flourish, so that they delight together morning and evening; for a woman's marriage the Pressure line must be strong, so that they share joy morning and evening. A man divining a wife takes the Wealth line as the main thing, and it must never be absent; a woman divining a husband puts the Pressure line first; the principle is the same.

## 《易林补遗》诀下的解说

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「须教授象兴隆，晨错共悦」两处像是转录之误：「教授象」多半是「财爻象」（下一句「女嫁，得官爻旺相」与它对着说），「晨错」多半是「晨昏」。译文按这个读法译，并在括号里注明。
>
> *Note:* Two phrases look like transcription slips: 'the professor sign' is most likely 'the Wealth line sign' (the next clause, 'for a woman's marriage, the Pressure line strong', is its counterpart), and 'morning error' is most likely 'morning and dusk'. They are translated in that sense, with the doubt marked in brackets.
