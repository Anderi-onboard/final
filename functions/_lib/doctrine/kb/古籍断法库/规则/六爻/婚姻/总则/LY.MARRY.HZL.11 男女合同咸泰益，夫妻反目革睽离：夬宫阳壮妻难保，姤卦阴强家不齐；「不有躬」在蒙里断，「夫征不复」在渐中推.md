# 男女合同咸泰益，夫妻反目革睽离：夬宫阳壮妻难保，姤卦阴强家不齐；「不有躬」在蒙里断，「夫征不复」在渐中推

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[火珠林]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男女同心合意，是咸、泰、益这些卦；夫妻反目，是革、睽、离这些卦。夬卦一宫阳气壮盛，妻子难保；姤卦阴气强，家里不齐整。「不有躬」到蒙卦里去判断，「夫征不复」到渐卦里去推算。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Man and woman in agreement: hexagrams like Xian, Tai and Yi; husband and wife falling out: Ge, Kui and Li. In the palace of Guai, yang is strong and the wife is hard to keep; in the hexagram Gou, yin is strong and the household is not whole. 'Not to have a body' is judged in Meng; 'the husband goes on the expedition and does not return' is reckoned in Jian.

> [!note] 按
> 最后一联是《周易》爻辞：蒙卦六三「勿用取女，见金夫，不有躬」，渐卦九三「夫征不复，妇孕不育」。这是按卦名断的口诀，和《增删卜易》觉子反对拿卦名断婚的那一条（LY.MARRY.ZS.14）正好相反的一边。
>
> *Note:* The last couplet quotes the line texts of the Book of Changes: Meng, third line, 'do not take this woman; she sees a man of gold and does not keep herself', and Jian, third line, 'the husband goes on the expedition and does not return; the wife conceives but does not bear'. This is judging by the names of hexagrams, the opposite side from Juezi's argument against it in LY.MARRY.ZS.14.
