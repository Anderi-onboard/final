# 子孙用变·婚姻：子孙发动伤官鬼，婚姻喜美是姻缘

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻发动伤克官鬼：占病求医身体就痊愈；行人、买卖身体安康；婚姻喜庆美满，是好姻缘。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Output line moves it injures and controls Pressure: in illness and seeking a doctor the body recovers; travelers and trade are in good health; a marriage is happy and fair, a good match.

## 《断易天机·散见各章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 827 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子孙发动伤官鬼， | 子孙发动，伤克官鬼， | Output moving and harming Pressure: |
> | 占病求医身便痊； | 占病求医，身体就痊愈； | in divining illness and seeking a doctor, the body is cured; |
> | 行人买卖身康泰， | 出门的人和做买卖的人，平安康泰； | the traveller and the trader are safe and well; |
> | 婚姻喜美是姻缘。 | 婚姻喜庆美满，是好姻缘。 | a marriage is happy and fair, a good match. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 子孙发动克官鬼，对占婚的女方是伤夫（见 LY.MARRY.TXF.07、LY.MARRY.DYTJ.09），这里说的却是「婚姻喜美」，是这首歌诀的口径。
>
> *Note:* Output moving to control Pressure is, for a woman divining a marriage, harm to the husband (see LY.MARRY.TXF.07 and LY.MARRY.DYTJ.09), yet this rhyme says 'a marriage is happy and fair'; that is the rhyme's own line.
