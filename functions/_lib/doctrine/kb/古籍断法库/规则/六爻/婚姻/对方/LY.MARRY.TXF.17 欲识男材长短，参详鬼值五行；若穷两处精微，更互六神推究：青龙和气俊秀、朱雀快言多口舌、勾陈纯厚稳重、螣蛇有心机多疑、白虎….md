# 欲识男材长短，参详鬼值五行；若穷两处精微，更互六神推究：青龙和气俊秀、朱雀快言多口舌、勾陈纯厚稳重、螣蛇有心机多疑、白虎性急狠毒、玄武私曲奸邪

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看男子的才质长短，参看官鬼所值的五行（人都是禀五行而生，形象也不离五行）；再要看透两方面的精微，就用六神来互相推究：青龙，和气俊秀、能干持家；朱雀，快言语、多口舌；勾陈，纯厚稳重、有规矩；螣蛇，有心机、多疑虑；白虎，性急、狠毒不仁；玄武，私曲奸邪，但若有吉神制住，反主聪明伶俐。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To know a man's measure, examine the five phase that Pressure sits on (everyone is born endowed with the five phases and the form does not depart from them); and to go further into both sides, use the six spirits in turn. Azure Dragon: gentle, handsome, skilled, able to run a household; Vermilion Bird: quick of speech, many quarrels; Hook Earth: honest, steady and well-mannered; Coiling Snake: scheming and given to doubt; White Tiger: hasty, cruel and unkind; Dark Tortoise: crooked and treacherous, but if a lucky spirit restrains it, it means clever and quick instead.
