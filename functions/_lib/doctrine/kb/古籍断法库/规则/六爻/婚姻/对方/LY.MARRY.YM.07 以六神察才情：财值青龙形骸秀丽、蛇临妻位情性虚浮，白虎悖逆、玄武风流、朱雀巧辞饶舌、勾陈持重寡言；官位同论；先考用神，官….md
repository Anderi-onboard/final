# 以六神察才情：财值青龙形骸秀丽、蛇临妻位情性虚浮，白虎悖逆、玄武风流、朱雀巧辞饶舌、勾陈持重寡言；官位同论；先考用神，官旺临玄武为杰士、官绝临玄武为奸人

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用六神察对方的才情。引《补遗》的话：男占女，财爻值青龙，身形秀丽；螣蛇临妻的位置，性情虚浮；白虎是悖逆之星，玄武是风流之宿，朱雀言辞巧、爱多嘴，勾陈稳重寡言。女占男，官爻的位置有青龙，性情文雅机巧；螣蛇临官鬼，言行虚假；白虎是武勇的人，玄武是奸雄的性子，勾陈敦厚多技艺，朱雀善辩能书。但一定要先考察用神，然后才把这些附上去：官爻旺、临玄武，是杰出之士；官爻绝、临玄武，是奸人；其余照此推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Use the six spirits to judge the other person's talent and temperament. Quoting the Buyi: when a man divines a woman, Wealth with the Azure Dragon means a fine and lovely figure; the Coiling Snake over the wife's place means a vain and shallow temperament; the White Tiger is the star of perversity, the Dark Tortoise the lodge of the philanderer, the Vermilion Bird is clever with words and talkative, the Hook Earth is weighty and sparing of speech. When a woman divines a man, the Azure Dragon at Pressure's place means a cultured, adroit temperament; the Coiling Snake over Pressure means false and deceptive words and deeds; the White Tiger is a martial, valiant man, the Dark Tortoise has the nature of a cunning strongman, the Hook Earth is honest, plain and skilled, the Vermilion Bird argues well and can write. But the Subject must be examined first and these attached only afterward: a strong Pressure line with the Dark Tortoise is an outstanding man, a Pressure line cut off with the Dark Tortoise is a treacherous man; the rest follow this.

> [!warning] 打架
> 李我平批评过这一读法（LY.MARRY.ZS.22）：同一卦，用八宫读是端正聪明，用六神读却可能是悖逆之星，两种读法互相打架。《易冒》自己也加了一道限制：一定先看用神，然后才把六神附上去。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Li Woping objected to this way of reading (LY.MARRY.ZS.22): the same hexagram read by the eight palaces is upright and intelligent, and read by the six spirits may be a star of perversity, so the two readings quarrel. The Yi Mao itself adds a limit: the Subject must be examined first, and only then are the six spirits attached.

> [!note] 按
> 第二段是《易冒》小注引的《易林补遗》，和《易林补遗》本文（LY.MARRY.YL.17）相比有几处用字不同：「财值青龙」对「龙值财爻」、「蛇临妻位」对「蛇临宴位」、「巧辞饶舌」对「巧词绕舌」；书里的「元武」就是「玄武」。
>
> *Note:* The second quotation is the Yilin Buyi as quoted in the Yi Mao's note, and it differs from the Yilin Buyi's own text (LY.MARRY.YL.17) in a few characters: 'Wealth with the Azure Dragon' against 'the Dragon over the Wealth line', 'the Snake over the wife's place' against 'the Snake over the feast place', and a variant spelling of 'clever with words'; the book's 'Yuan Wu' is the Dark Tortoise.
