# 女占男，官爻持世是良缘：官星持世、合世、生世且旺相，姻缘必成；子孙持世或发动克夫；官星重叠主两度新人

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 女方占男方，官鬼爻持世是好姻缘。不宜子孙持世，也不宜子孙发动：子孙是克夫的神，没成的成不了，已成的会伤夫再嫁。只有官星持世、合世、生世、旺相的，姻缘一定成，还能举案齐眉。卦中官星重叠的，也主嫁两次。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When a woman divines about a man, a Pressure line on the Self line is a good match. An Output line should not hold the Self line or move, for Output is the spirit that controls the husband: a match not yet made will not be made, and one already made will harm the husband and she will marry again. Only where the Pressure star holds, combines with or generates the Self, and is strong, is the match certain and even one of mutual respect. If the Pressure star is doubled in the hexagram, that too means two marriages.
