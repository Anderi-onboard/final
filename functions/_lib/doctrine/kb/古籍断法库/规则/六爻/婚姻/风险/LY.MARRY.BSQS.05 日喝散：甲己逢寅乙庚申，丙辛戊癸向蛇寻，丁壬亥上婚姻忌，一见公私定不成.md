# 日喝散：甲己逢寅乙庚申，丙辛戊癸向蛇寻，丁壬亥上婚姻忌，一见公私定不成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[卜筮全书]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 日喝散按日干排：甲日、己日在寅，乙日、庚日在申，丙日、辛日、戊日、癸日在巳，丁日、壬日在亥；丁壬日的亥位，婚姻要忌；一见到它，公事私事都一定不成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Daily Dispersal star goes by the day stem: Jia and Ji days at Yin, Yi and Geng days at Shen, Bing, Xin, Wu and Gui days at Si, Ding and Ren days at Hai; at Hai on Ding and Ren days, marriage is a taboo; once it is seen, public and private matters are sure to fail.

## 《卜筮全书·神煞歌例》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 10026 行 · Bushi Quanshu (Complete Book of Divination), "Rhymes on the spirit stars", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 甲己逢寅乙庚申， | 甲日、己日逢寅，乙日、庚日逢申， | Jia and Ji meet Yin, Yi and Geng meet Shen; |
> | 丙辛戊癸向蛇寻， | 丙日、辛日、戊日、癸日向巳位找， | Bing, Xin, Wu and Gui look to the snake, Si; |
> | 丁壬亥上婚姻忌， | 丁日、壬日在亥位，婚姻要忌， | Ding and Ren, at Hai, are a taboo for marriage; |
> | 一见公私定不成。 | 一见到它，公事私事一定不成。 | once it is seen, public or private matters surely do not succeed. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「向蛇寻」的蛇是巳。这一条前面另有「喝散神」，按季节排（春巳夏申秋亥冬寅），讲官事，不讲婚姻，本库没有收。
>
> *Note:* The snake in 'look to the snake' is Si. Just before it the book has the seasonal 'Dispersal spirit' (spring Si, summer Shen, autumn Hai, winter Yin), which speaks of official matters and not of marriage and is not included here.
