# 化子而生身世，鲁伯姬贤德无疵：财动化出子孙、生合世身，必有懿德；占妇人身命以财爻为主，不以应世作其身

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻动、化出子孙，生合世爻和卦身的，这个妇人一定有美德，言行都好，没有可挑剔的，像鲁国的伯姬。凡是占（妇人）的身命，要以财爻为主，不能拿应爻、世爻当作她本人。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Wealth line moves, changes out into Output and generates or combines with the Self line and the Body, the woman surely has fine virtue, good in word and deed and beyond reproach, like Bo Ji of Lu. In divining a (woman's) person, take the Wealth line as the main sign; the Other and Self lines are not to be taken as the person.

> [!note] 按
> 鲁国的伯姬是鲁宣公的女儿、嫁给宋共公，《春秋》记她宋国宫中失火时守礼不肯夜出，被烧死；书里说她是「鲁庄公夫人」，与史书不合，译文照书。
>
> *Note:* Bo Ji of Lu was a daughter of Duke Xuan of Lu, married to Duke Gong of Song; the Spring and Autumn Annals record that when fire broke out in the Song palace she kept to propriety and would not leave in the night, and was burned to death. The book calls her the wife of Duke Zhuang of Lu, which does not agree with the histories, and the translation follows the book.
