# 福引刑爻发动，卫共姜作誓于柏舟：子孙带刑旺动必克夫；但子孙是贞洁之神，财不值凶者主守节

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙带刑、旺相发动，一定克丈夫；但子孙是贞洁的神，遇到这样的爻而财爻不逢凶的，主守节（守寡不改嫁），像卫国的共姜作《柏舟》诗，以死自誓。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Output carrying punishment and moving on strength surely harms the husband; but Output is a spirit of chastity, and if with such a line the Wealth line does not meet ill, it means keeping faith (a widow not remarrying), like Gong Jiang of Wei, who wrote the poem 'Cypress Boat' and swore by death.

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 《诗经·鄘风·柏舟》据《毛诗序》是卫世子共伯的妻子共姜在丈夫早死后、拒绝父母要她改嫁而作。「福」指子孙爻。
>
> *Note:* According to the Mao Preface, the 'Cypress Boat' of the Odes (Yong Feng) was written by Gong Jiang, wife of the Wei crown prince Gong Bo, after her husband's early death, when she refused her parents' wish that she marry again. 'Fortune' here means the Output line.
