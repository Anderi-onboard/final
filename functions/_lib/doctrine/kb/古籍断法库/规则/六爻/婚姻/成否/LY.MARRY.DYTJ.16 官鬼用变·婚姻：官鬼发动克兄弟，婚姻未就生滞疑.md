# 官鬼用变·婚姻：官鬼发动克兄弟，婚姻未就生滞疑

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼爻发动克兄弟：婚姻还没成，生出停滞和疑虑；病困门庭，祸祟来到。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Pressure line moves it controls Peer: a marriage not yet made meets delay and doubt; illness troubles the household and haunting misfortune comes.

## 《断易天机·散见各章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 854 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 官鬼发动克兄弟， | 官鬼发动，克兄弟， | Pressure moving and controlling Peer: |
> | 婚姻未就生滞疑； | 婚姻还没成，生出停滞和疑虑； | a marriage not yet made meets delay and doubt; |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 《易冒》《海底眼》等书里官鬼是婚姻的用爻，这首诗却说官鬼发动主婚姻停滞，要和 LY.MARRY.HZL.22 一起看。
>
> *Note:* In the Yi Mao and other books Pressure is the Subject for marriage, whereas this rhyme says Pressure moving means marriage delay; read it together with LY.MARRY.HZL.22.
