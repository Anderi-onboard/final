# 六亲持世断夫妻：父母持世夫妻不同床、老来终求他姓之子；兄弟持世克妻财

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《海底眼》的「六亲持世」诗里，和夫妻有关的两句：父母爻持世，夫妻不合、不同床（书里写的是「相合」，疑是「相克」之误），到老终究要过继别姓的儿子（小注：父母必克子孙）；兄弟爻持世，克妻财（小注：兄弟必克财）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Two lines of the Haidi Yan's poem on 'the relatives on Self' touch husband and wife: with the Resource line on Self, husband and wife do not share a bed (the book writes 'combine', probably a slip for 'control'), and in the end one must seek a son of another surname (annotation: Resource must control Output); with the Peer line on Self, Wealth is controlled (annotation: Peer must control Wealth).

> [!note] 按
> 「相合不同床」读不通：相合而不同床，自相矛盾。《卜筮全书》的「诸爻持世诀」同一句作「夫妻相克不和同、到老要求他姓之子」（LY.MARRY.BSQS.08），这里的「相合」「到者」像是「相克」「到老」抄错了，《火珠林》所附歌诀第 945 行也作「夫妻相克不和同，到老用求他姓子」。译文照原文译，括号里注出。第 336 行后面三句讲官司、口舌、损财，不属婚姻，没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Combine yet do not share a bed' is self-contradictory. The Bushi Quanshu's poem on the lines holding Self has the same line as 'husband and wife control each other and do not agree, in old age one must seek a son of another surname' (LY.MARRY.BSQS.08); 'combine' and 'at the last' here look like miscopies of 'control' and 'in old age'. The verse appended to the Huozhulin, at line 945, also reads 'husband and wife control each other and do not agree, in old age one must seek a son of another surname'. The translation follows the text as written, with the doubt marked in brackets. The three lines after line 336 speak of lawsuits, quarrels and loss of money, not marriage, and are not taken.
