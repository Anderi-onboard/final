# 相生相合偕老齐眉、相克相冲终朝反目：世应相生一生如鱼得水，相克相冲百岁似冰投炭；生合逢冲始谐后被搬弄；应克世而动爻克应或日辰冲破，先不和后得人劝而好合

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻应爻相生相合，一定白头偕老、相敬如宾，一辈子如鱼得水；相克相冲，注定整天反目，一百年都像冰和炭放在一起。生合的地方碰上冲，起初和谐，后来被人挑拨。应爻来克世爻本来不好，但如果有动爻去克应爻，或者应爻被日辰冲破，起初虽然不和，以后有人劝解，终究和好。世应比和，双方不分高下。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self and Other lines generating and combining means they surely grow old together in mutual respect, like a fish in water all their lives; controlling and clashing means they are sure to quarrel all day long, like ice thrown on coals for a hundred years. Where generation and combination meet a clash, it is harmonious at first and later someone stirs up trouble. The Other line coming to control the Self line is not good in itself, but if a moving line controls the Other line, or the Other line is clashed and broken by the Day, then although they are at odds at first, someone later mediates and they end up reconciled. With Self and Other in harmony as equals, neither side is above the other.

> [!note] 按
> 「齐眉」出自梁鸿、孟光夫妻「举案齐眉」的故事，指夫妻相敬如宾。这一段和伉俪章里的同类句（LY.MARRY.TXF.01、TXF.03）一起读：那里讲男求女、女求男的成与不成，这里讲成了以后的日子。
>
> *Note:* 'Level with the brows' is from the story of Liang Hong and Meng Guang raising the tray to the level of the brows, meaning a couple in mutual respect. Read it with the like passages of the chapter on husband and wife (LY.MARRY.TXF.01, TXF.03): those speak of whether a match is made from either side, this speaks of the life after it is made.
