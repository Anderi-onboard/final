# 应财世鬼夫唱妇随，应鬼世财夫权妻夺：世持鬼应持财是阴阳得位，鬼临应财持世是牝鸡司晨，惟赘婿反吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻是财、世爻是鬼，夫唱妇随；应爻是鬼、世爻是财，丈夫的权被妻子夺走。世爻持鬼、应爻持财，叫阴阳得位，夫妻必定和谐；鬼在应爻、财在世爻，一定是母鸡司晨，妻子夺了丈夫的权。只有入赘的女婿得到这样的卦反而吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other line as Wealth and the Self line as Pressure: the husband leads and the wife follows; the Other line as Pressure and the Self line as Wealth: the wife takes the husband's authority. The Self line holding Pressure and the Other line holding Wealth is called yin and yang in their places, and the couple will surely be in harmony; Pressure on the Other line and Wealth on the Self line is surely the hen crowing at dawn, the wife seizing the husband's authority. Only for a son-in-law who marries into the wife's family is this, on the contrary, auspicious.
