# 子息：卦无子孙、子孙空绝破、财带孤寡天狗杀动、卦纯阳纯阴俱主无子；子孙胎爻两备有子；子化鬼头胎难招（水一火二木三金四土五）；子之多少以一水二火三木四金五土推

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 没有子孙爻，或子孙爻逢空、绝、破，或财爻带孤寡、天狗煞而动，或卦是纯阳、纯阴，都主不生育、没有儿子。子孙爻和胎爻两样齐备的，有儿子；子孙变鬼，或胎爻临在父母爻上，有儿子也难留住；有胎爻没有子孙爻，怀了孕养不成；有子孙爻没有胎爻，是庶出或过继来的。子孙在阳爻多男，在阴爻多女。水的子孙变鬼，头胎难留；火的，二胎；木的，三胎；金的，四胎；土的，五胎。孩子的多少，按一水、二火、三木、四金、五土的数推：旺相加倍，休囚照数，囚死减半。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* With no Output line, or the Output line meeting Void, extinction or break, or the Wealth line carrying the Solitary and Widowhood stars and the Heavenly Hound star and moving, or a hexagram of pure yang or pure yin: all mean no childbearing and no son. Where both the Output line and the Womb line are present there is a son; if Output changes into Pressure, or the Womb line is on the Resource line, there is a son but he is hard to keep; with a Womb line but no Output line there is conception but no child is raised; with an Output line but no Womb line the son is a concubine's child or adopted. Output on a yang line means mostly boys, on a yin line mostly girls. A Water Output line changing into Pressure: the first child is hard to keep; Fire, the second; Wood, the third; Metal, the fourth; Earth, the fifth. The number of children is reckoned by one for Water, two for Fire, three for Wood, four for Metal, five for Earth: doubled if strong, as it stands if resting, halved if trapped or dead.
