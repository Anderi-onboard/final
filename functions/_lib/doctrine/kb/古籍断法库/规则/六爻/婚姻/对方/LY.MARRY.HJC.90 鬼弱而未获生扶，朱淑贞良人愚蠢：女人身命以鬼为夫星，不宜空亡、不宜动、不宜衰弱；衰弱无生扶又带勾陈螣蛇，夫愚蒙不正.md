# 鬼弱而未获生扶，朱淑贞良人愚蠢：女人身命以鬼为夫星，不宜空亡、不宜动、不宜衰弱；衰弱无生扶又带勾陈螣蛇，夫愚蒙不正

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 看女人的身命，以官鬼为夫星。夫星不宜空亡，空亡就难做丈夫；又不宜发动，发动就为难兄弟（克伤兄弟爻）；也不宜衰弱，弱就招来不成器的丈夫、发不了福。要是衰弱又得不到一点生扶合助，还带勾陈、螣蛇这些凶神，一定像朱淑真的丈夫，愚昧不正、人又矮小，所以她才有断肠的诗。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In reading a woman's person, Pressure is the husband star. It should not be Void, for Void makes it hard for him to be a husband; it should not move, for moving is hard on the brothers (Pressure controls the Peer line); and it should not be weak, for weak brings a worthless husband who cannot bring fortune. If it is weak with no support or help at all and carries Hook Earth, the Coiling Snake or another ill spirit, the husband is surely like Zhu Shuzhen's: stupid, improper and stunted, and that is why she wrote her heartbroken poems.

> [!note] 按
> 朱淑真是南宋女词人，嫁给不相称的丈夫，郁郁而终，诗集叫《断肠集》。卜筮大全作「朱淑贞」「侏儒」。「动则难为兄弟」按官鬼克兄弟爻来读（官鬼发动伤克兄弟，见 LY.MARRY.DYTJ.11）。这一条是「女占夫」的一般规则，和婚姻章里女占男的说法可以合着读（LY.MARRY.HJC.22）。
>
> *Note:* Zhu Shuzhen was a Southern Song woman poet married to an ill-matched husband who died unhappy; her collection is called 'Heartbroken Poems'. The Quanshu writes 'Zhu Shuzhen' and 'dwarf'. 'Moving makes it hard on the brothers' is read as Pressure controlling the Peer line (a moving Pressure line injures the Peer line, LY.MARRY.DYTJ.11). This is the general rule for a woman divining about a husband and can be read together with what the marriage chapter says of a woman divining about a man (LY.MARRY.HJC.22).
