# 六爻竞合阴财动，秦弱兰遇陶谷于邮亭：男带合聪明俊秀，女带合浇浮淫佚；六合卦而财爻属阴，不动犹可，动则淫滥无耻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男子卦带合，聪明俊秀；女子卦带合，轻浮淫乱。要是六合卦而财爻又属阴，不动还算可以，一动就淫滥无耻，像秦弱兰在驿亭遇到陶学士。（卜筮正宗补充：财爻和世爻相合的，不能这样断；女人自己占卦，世爻就是她自己，发动去合别的爻也这样断。）
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A man's hexagram with combination is clever and handsome; a woman's with combination is frivolous and lewd. If the hexagram is a Six-combine and the Wealth line is yin, then still it is passable, but moving it is dissolute and shameless, like Qin Ruolan meeting Scholar Tao at the post pavilion. (The Zhengzong adds: where the Wealth line combines with the Self line one must not judge so; when a woman divines for herself the Self line is herself, and moving to combine with another line is judged the same.)

> | 如女人自卜，以世为自己，发动合旁爻亦此断。 | 如果是女人自己占卦，以世爻为自己，发动去合别的爻，也这样断。 | If a woman divines for herself, the Self line is herself, and moving to combine with another line is judged the same way. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 陶谷是宋初出使南唐的使臣，自命端方，南唐派官妓秦弱兰扮作驿卒之女，在驿亭里勾住了他。「邮亭」就是驿亭。
>
> *Note:* Tao Gu was an envoy of early Song to the Southern Tang who prided himself on his uprightness; the Southern Tang sent the courtesan Qin Ruolan, disguised as a courier's daughter, and she caught him at the post pavilion. 'Youting' is the post pavilion.
