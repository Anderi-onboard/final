# 世为婚、应为姻，须要相生相合；鬼为夫、财为妇，最嫌相害相刑

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻代表男家，应爻代表女家，要相生相合；官鬼是丈夫，妻财是妻子，最怕互相害、互相刑。生合的地方要防冲克，恐怕快成了又被破坏；冲克的地方若得生扶，反而有人从中撮合。财、官都要旺相才吉，最怕三刑六害发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line stands for the man's family and the Other line for the woman's family, and they should generate and combine with each other; Pressure is the husband and Wealth is the wife, and what is most disliked is their harming and punishing each other. Where there is generation and combination guard against a clash, for fear that what is about to be made is broken up; where there is clash and control, if support comes, someone steps in to help the match along. Wealth and Pressure should both be strong to be lucky, and most disliked are the three punishments and six harms when they move.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章开头两句（「天命禀有生之初，非今可易」「夫妇乃人伦之大，自古为先」）是感慨，不是断法，本库没有收。「婚」「姻」按注文是男家、女家。
>
> *Note:* The two opening lines of the chapter ('fate is received at the start of life and cannot now be changed' and 'husband and wife are the greatest of human bonds, first since antiquity') are reflections and not rules, and are not included. By the note, 'hun' and 'yin' are the man's family and the woman's family.
