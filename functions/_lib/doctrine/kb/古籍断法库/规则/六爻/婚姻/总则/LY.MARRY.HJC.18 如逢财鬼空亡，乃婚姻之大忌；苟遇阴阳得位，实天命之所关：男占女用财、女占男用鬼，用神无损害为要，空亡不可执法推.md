# 如逢财鬼空亡，乃婚姻之大忌；苟遇阴阳得位，实天命之所关：男占女用财、女占男用鬼，用神无损害为要，空亡不可执法推

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财、鬼两爻是占婚姻的用神，逢空亡本不吉，但不可死守：男占女以财为主，鬼空不妨；女占男以鬼为主，财空不妨；要紧的是用神没有损害。财鬼空亡而干支相合、世应相生、阴阳得位，是真姻缘，只是半辈子同床、不能白头到老，那是天命，不是刑克；逢冲遇克叫避空，反而不用担心。长辈占子侄、兄占弟，就看子孙爻、兄弟爻，不能一律用财官。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Wealth and Pressure lines are the Subject in a marriage divination and Void on them is by nature unlucky, but it must not be followed rigidly: when a man divines about a woman Wealth is the main thing and Pressure Void does no harm; when a woman divines about a man Pressure is the main thing and Wealth Void does no harm; what matters is that the Subject is undamaged. If Wealth and Pressure are Void but the stems and branches combine, the Self and Other generate each other and yin and yang are in their places, it is a real marriage bond, only that they share a pillow for half a lifetime and do not grow old together, which is decreed by Heaven and not the work of punishment or control; meeting a clash or control is called escaping the Void and there is on the contrary nothing to fear. When elders divine for a son or nephew, or an elder brother for a younger, look at the Output line or the Peer line, and do not always use Wealth and Pressure.

> [!note] 按
> 「衾枕虚设」是名义夫妻、聚少离多的意思；「避空」的说法见 HJC.03（求财）。「财空妻失，鬼空夫亡」是《天玄赋》的话，旧注和王洪绪都说不可死守。
>
> *Note:* 'An empty bed' means a couple in name only who are often apart. 'Escaping the Void' is discussed at the wealth entry HJC.03. 'Wealth Void, the wife is lost; Pressure Void, the husband dies' is from the Tianxuan Fu, and both the old commentary and Wang Hongxu say it must not be followed rigidly.
