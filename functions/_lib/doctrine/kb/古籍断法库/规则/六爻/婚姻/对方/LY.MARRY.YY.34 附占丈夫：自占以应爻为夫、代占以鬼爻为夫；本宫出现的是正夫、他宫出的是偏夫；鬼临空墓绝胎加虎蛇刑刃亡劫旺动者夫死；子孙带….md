# 附占丈夫：自占以应爻为夫、代占以鬼爻为夫；本宫出现的是正夫、他宫出的是偏夫；鬼临空墓绝胎加虎蛇刑刃亡劫旺动者夫死；子孙带煞旺动克鬼则鬼旺有灾病、衰则死；鬼下伏鬼或伏兄是两姓贴夫营活

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 女方自己占，把应爻当丈夫；替别人占，把官鬼爻当丈夫。在本宫里出现的是正夫，在年月日时别的宫里出现的是偏夫；卦里有两个官鬼，阳爻得位的是正夫，阴爻失位的是偏夫（已经成婚的叫正，只是口头说说没成的叫偏）。官鬼临在空、墓、绝、胎上，又加白虎、螣蛇、刑、刃、亡神、劫煞，又旺又动，丈夫会死；子孙爻旺动带着这些煞来克官鬼，官鬼旺就有灾病，衰就会死；丈夫本来有病，倒要官鬼衰、不要官鬼旺。官鬼下面伏着官鬼，或者伏着兄弟爻，一定是两姓人家依附着丈夫谋生。丈夫的贫富、贵贱、带病、祸福，都和世爻一样判断。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a woman divining for herself the Other line is the husband; for another, the Pressure line is the husband. One who appears in the home palace is the principal husband, and one who appears in another palace through the year, month, day or hour is a collateral husband; if the hexagram has two Pressure lines, the yang line in its place is the principal and the yin line out of place the collateral (a marriage that has been made is called principal; one only spoken of and not made is called collateral). If Pressure sits on Void, Tomb, Extinction or Womb, with the White Tiger, Coiling Snake, punishment, blade, Loss and Robbery stars added, and is strong and moving, the husband dies; if the Output line is strong and moving, carries these stars and comes to control Pressure, then strong Pressure means illness or misfortune and weak Pressure means death; if the husband is already ill, it is better that Pressure be weak and not strong. Pressure with Pressure hidden beneath it, or a Peer line hidden beneath it, surely means two surnames living off the husband. The husband's poverty or wealth, rank, illness and fortune are judged as for the Self line.

> [!note] 按
> 这一段紧接在「断妻妾」「附占女婿」后面，是同一个方法用在妻子身上：自占的女人把应爻当丈夫。「贴夫营活」是靠着丈夫谋生；「正夫」「偏夫」是名分上的正偏，括号里书里自己注了：成了婚的是正，口头说说没成的是偏。命属木、午酉戌亥的吉，是说这个鬼爻生旺的五行和女方命宫五行的配合。
>
> *Note:* This passage follows 'Judging wives and concubines' and 'Appendix: divining a son-in-law' and applies the same method to the husband: a woman divining for herself takes the Other line as the husband. 'Living off the husband' is making a living by depending on him; 'principal' and 'collateral' husband are about standing, and the book itself glosses it in brackets: one whose marriage has been made is principal, one only spoken of and not made is collateral. 'A man of the Wood fate and a woman of Wu, You, Xu or Hai is auspicious' is about how the element in which the Pressure line is strong fits the element of the woman's fate.
