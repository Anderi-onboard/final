# 何知章·妻：妻有灾（虎临兄弟动伤财）、妻有孕（青龙财临喜神）、有妻妾（内外两财旺相）、损妻房（财爻带鬼落空亡）

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 「何知章」是一问一答的口诀，问的是一家人的各样事。其中四问是关于妻子的：妻子有灾，是白虎临着兄弟爻发动，伤克财爻；妻子有孕，是青龙、财爻临着喜神；这家有妻有妾，是内外卦两个财爻都旺相；这家要损妻房（丧妻），是财爻带着官鬼又落空亡。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The 'how to know' rhyme is a rhyme of questions and answers on the affairs of a household. Four of its questions concern the wife: the wife has misfortune when the White Tiger sits on a moving Peer line and injures Wealth; the wife has conceived when the Azure Dragon and Wealth arrive with a spirit of joy; the household has a wife and concubines when the two Wealth lines, inside and outside, are both strong; the household will lose its wife when the Wealth line carries Pressure and falls into Void.

> [!note] 按
> 「何知章」一共问了三四十件事（父母有病、有没有子孙、讼事、财富、田地、屋宇、坟墓、六畜、口舌、盗贼……），都是家宅的事；这里只收关于妻子的四问，其余与婚姻无关，不收。「损妻房」就是丧妻。《卜筮大全》本第三问作「青龙财临天喜神」，第四问的头一个字作「休」（应是「何」字之误），《卜筮正宗》本作「添喜神」「何」。
>
> *Note:* The 'how to know' rhyme asks thirty or forty things (illness of the parents, whether there are children, lawsuits, wealth, land, houses, graves, livestock, quarrels, thieves and more), all about the household; only the four questions about the wife are taken here, and the rest have nothing to do with marriage. 'Loses its wife' means bereavement. The Bushi Daquan text has 'the Azure Dragon and Wealth arrive with the Tianxi spirit' for the second, and 'xiu' for the first word of the fourth question (a slip for 'he'); the Bushi Zhengzong text has 'an added spirit of joy' and 'he'.
