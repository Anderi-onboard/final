# 逢龙而化败兄，蔡琰聪明而失节：财遇青龙本主聪明，但不宜化出兄爻及败爻，皆主不贞洁；化出刑爻、病爻、害爻及玄武同临亦然

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻遇到青龙，本来主聪明，但是不宜化出兄弟爻，也不宜化出败爻（沐浴之类），那样都主不贞洁，还兼不长寿。化出刑爻、病爻、害爻，以及玄武同临的，也是这样。像蔡琰，文章冠绝一时，却在胡人那里失了节。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth meeting the Azure Dragon means cleverness in itself, but it should not change out into a Peer line or into a ruined stage such as the Bath; either means she is not chaste, and short-lived besides. The same for changing out into a punishing, sick or harming line, and for the Dark Tortoise sitting with it. Like Cai Yan, whose writing surpassed all others of her time and who lost her chastity among the barbarians.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1749 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 财遇青龙本主聪明，如化兄弟及沐浴，皆主不贞洁兼不寿，如蔡琰文章绝世，失节胡人。 | 财爻遇到青龙，本来主聪明，如果化出兄弟爻以及沐浴，都主不贞洁，还兼不长寿，像蔡琰文章绝世，却在胡人那里失了节。 | Wealth meeting the Azure Dragon means cleverness in itself, but if it changes out into a Peer line or the Bath stage, she is not chaste and short-lived besides, like Cai Yan, whose writing was without equal and who lost her chastity among the barbarians. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「败」指十二长生里衰败的几位，卜筮正宗举的是沐浴。蔡琰（文姬）是蔡邕的女儿，被匈奴掳去，嫁给匈奴左贤王，后来被曹操赎回。
>
> *Note:* 'Ruined' means the falling stages of the twelve life stages, of which the Zhengzong names the Bath. Cai Yan (Wenji) was the daughter of Cai Yong; she was carried off by the Xiongnu and married to a Xiongnu prince, and was later ransomed by Cao Cao.
