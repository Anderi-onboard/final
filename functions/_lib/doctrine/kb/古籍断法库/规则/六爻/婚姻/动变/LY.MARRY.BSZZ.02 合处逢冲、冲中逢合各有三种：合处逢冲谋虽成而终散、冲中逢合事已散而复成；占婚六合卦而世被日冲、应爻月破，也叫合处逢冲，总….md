# 合处逢冲、冲中逢合各有三种：合处逢冲谋虽成而终散、冲中逢合事已散而复成；占婚六合卦而世被日冲、应爻月破，也叫合处逢冲，总不言吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 合处逢冲有三种：六合卦变成六冲卦；日辰、月建冲爻；动爻变成冲。冲中逢合也有三种，方向相反。合处逢冲，谋划虽然成了最后还是散；冲中逢合，事情已经散了又成。占婚姻时，卦是六合，可是世爻被日辰冲、应爻月破，也是合处逢冲，不说吉；书中例：辰月丁酉日自占婚姻得否卦，当月自己大病，未月世爻和财爻入墓，那个女子病死。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* There are three kinds of a combination meeting a clash: a six-combine hexagram changing into a six-clash hexagram; the Day or Month clashing a line; a moving line changing into a clash; and three kinds of a clash meeting a combination, in the opposite direction. With a combination meeting a clash, the plan is made but in the end scatters; with a clash meeting a combination, the matter has scattered and is then made. In a marriage divination, if the hexagram is six-combine but Self is clashed by the Day and the Other line is under a Month-break, that too is a combination meeting a clash and is not called lucky; the case in the book: on a Ding-You day in the Chen month a man divined for himself about a marriage and got Pi; that month he fell gravely ill himself, and in the Wei month, Self and Wealth entering the tomb, the girl died of illness.

> 这一节讲的三种合处逢冲，《易冒》里的「合冲」（LY.MARRY.YM.25）只是其中第二种（六合的卦被日辰冲害在世应上）。卦例的卦图不收。
>
> *Note:* Of the three kinds of a combination meeting a clash set out here, the Yi Mao's 'combination-clash' (LY.MARRY.YM.25) is only the second (a six-combine hexagram whose Self or Other is clashed or harmed by the Day). The case's hexagram diagram is not included.
