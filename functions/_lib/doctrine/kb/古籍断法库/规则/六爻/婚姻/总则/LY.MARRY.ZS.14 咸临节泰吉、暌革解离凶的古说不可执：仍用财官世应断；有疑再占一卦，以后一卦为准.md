# 咸临节泰吉、暌革解离凶的古说不可执：仍用财官世应断；有疑再占一卦，以后一卦为准

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 古人把咸、临、节、泰当作占婚的吉卦，把暌、革、解、离当作凶卦。觉子引《易冒》：得咸、临、节、泰，只要不遇到合冲、变冲，即使财官衰陷也不算凶；暌、革、解、离是凶兆，用神齐全也不算吉。觉子自己试了：得泰卦，可是兄弟持世、财爻月破旬空又被世爻克、日辰刑，他不敢拿别人的婚姻大事去试；让人再占一卦得坤卦，六冲大凶，才知道前面的泰卦不是吉，还是以财、官、世、应来断为对。有疑问不要死执古法，以再占的卦为准：后卦吉就按吉断，后卦凶就按凶推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The ancients took Xian, Lin, Jie and Tai as auspicious hexagrams for marriage and Kui, Ge, Xie and Li as ill ones. Juezi quotes the Yi Mao: with Xian, Lin, Jie or Tai, unless it meets a combination-clash or a change into a clash, it is not ill even if Wealth and Pressure are weak and fallen; Kui, Ge, Xie and Li are ill omens, and it is not auspicious even if the Subject is complete. Juezi tried it himself: he obtained Tai, but with a Peer line on the Self, the Wealth line under a Month-break and Void and also controlled by the Self line and punished by the Day, and he dared not test it on someone's great matter of marriage; when the person divined again and got Kun, a Six-clash of great ill, he knew the earlier Tai was not auspicious, and that judging by Wealth, Pressure, Self and Other remains right. When in doubt do not cling to the old methods; decide by the hexagram divined again: if the later one is auspicious judge it auspicious, if it is ill reckon it ill.

> [!note] 按
> 《易冒》引文里「若不遇合冲变冲」标点不明，可以读作「合、冲、变冲」，也可以读作「合变冲、冲变冲」；这里照字面译，没有替书选一种读法。
>
> *Note:* The Yi Mao quotation reads 'unless it meets he chong bian chong' without clear punctuation: it may be 'combination, clash, change into a clash' or 'combination changing to a clash, clash changing to a clash'. It is translated literally without choosing a reading for the book.
