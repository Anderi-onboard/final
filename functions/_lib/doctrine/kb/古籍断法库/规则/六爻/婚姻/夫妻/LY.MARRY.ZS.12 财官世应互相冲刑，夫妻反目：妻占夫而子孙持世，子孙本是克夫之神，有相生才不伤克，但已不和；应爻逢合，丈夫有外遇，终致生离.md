# 财官世应互相冲刑，夫妻反目：妻占夫而子孙持世，子孙本是克夫之神，有相生才不伤克，但已不和；应爻逢合，丈夫有外遇，终致生离

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻、官爻、世爻、应爻互相冲刑，夫妻就会反目。书中的例子（妻子占夫妻不和，将来能不能和好）：妻占夫不宜子孙持世，因为子孙是克夫的神；这卦幸好寅木、子水互相生，伤克不了，所以是不和而不是死别；又因为应爻代表丈夫，应爻逢子丑合，丈夫已经有了外遇。结果虽然不至于死别，生离是一定的。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When Wealth, Pressure, the Self and the Other lines clash with and punish each other, husband and wife fall out. The book's case (a wife divining whether the couple, now at odds, will be reconciled): in a wife's divination it is bad for an Output line to hold the Self, because Output is the spirit that controls the husband; here, luckily, Yin Wood and Zi Water generate each other so no harm is done, and the result is discord rather than death; and because the Other line stands for the husband and meets a Zi-Chou combination, the husband already has another attachment. The couple would not part by death, but parting alive was certain.
