# 财鬼一同生合，庆及婚姻：财爻官鬼一同来生合，家里有婚姻的喜庆；兄弟爻两处见刑冲，灾祸牵连眷属

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻和官鬼一同来生合（世身），家里有婚姻的喜庆；兄弟爻在两处被刑冲，灾祸就牵连到家眷。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Wealth line and the Pressure line together come to generate and combine (with the Self and Body), there is the joy of a marriage in the household; if the Peer line meets punishment and clash in two places, calamity extends to the family members.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句夹在一首家宅口诀里，前后没有注。原文没有说生合的是谁，按家宅口诀的惯例，是财、鬼一起生合世爻和宅爻（见同书家宅部分「财鬼二爻同动来生合世爻宅爻者，皆主婚姻之喜」），括号里的「世身」是译者补的。
>
> *Note:* The sentence is in a household rhyme with no note before or after. The text does not say what the Wealth and Pressure lines generate and combine with; by the convention of the household rhymes they generate and combine with the Self line and the household line (the Yiyin says in its household section that when the Wealth and Pressure lines move together to generate and combine with the Self and household lines, it always means the joy of a marriage), and 'Self and Body' in brackets is the translator's addition.
