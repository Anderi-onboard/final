# 应生世，悦服成亲；世克应，用强劫娶

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 应爻生合世爻，是女家贪图男家，容易成；世刑克应爻，男家不会早来求；世旺应衰，是倚仗富有欺负贫弱、用强抢娶。世上见鬼和螣蛇、财爻旺相、青龙得地，多半是看上了女子的姿色，想设谋娶她；世上见玄武、兄弟，是因为缺钱还债，想谋娶这个女子。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Other line generating or combining with the Self means the woman's family covets the man's and it is easy to bring about; the Self punishing or controlling the Other means the man's family would not have come early to ask; the Self thriving and the Other weak is relying on wealth to bully the poor and taking her by force. With the Self carrying Pressure and the Coiling Snake, Wealth thriving and the Azure Dragon well placed, it is probably because the girl is good-looking and someone means to scheme to marry her; with the Self carrying the Dark Tortoise or Peer, it is because the family lacks money to repay a debt and wants to take the girl in.

> | 应动或旺，以旺动断。 | 应爻发动或旺相，就以旺相发动来断。 | If the Other moves or thrives, judge by the thriving, moving line. |
