# 男临女子互爻，内多淫欲：男的身爻临女的命爻、女的身爻临男的命爻叫互，尊卑失序主淫乱；夫妻互相合主先奸后娶

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男子的身爻起来临到女子的命爻，或女子的身爻起来临到男子的命爻，叫做互，尊卑失了次序，主有淫乱的事；如果夫妻互相合，主先奸后娶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the man's Body line rises onto the woman's Life line, or the woman's Body line rises onto the man's Life line, this is called mutual: seniority and rank lose their order, and it means lewd conduct; if husband and wife combine with each other, it means adultery first and marriage after.

> [!note] 按
> 「互爻」是男女两边的爻互相临对方的位置，书里只给了这个说法；卜筮正宗把「互爻」写作「互交」。
>
> *Note:* A 'mutual line' is one in which the lines of man and woman sit on each other's places, which is all the book says; the Bushi Zhengzong writes the term with a different character.
