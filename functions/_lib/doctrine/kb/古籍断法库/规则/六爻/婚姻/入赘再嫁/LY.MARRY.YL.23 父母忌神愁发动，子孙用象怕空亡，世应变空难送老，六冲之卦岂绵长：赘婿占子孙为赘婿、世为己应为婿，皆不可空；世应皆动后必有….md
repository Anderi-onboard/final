# 父母忌神愁发动，子孙用象怕空亡，世应变空难送老，六冲之卦岂绵长：赘婿占子孙为赘婿、世为己应为婿，皆不可空；世应皆动后必有更，六冲丝毫莫用

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 招婿入赘：父母爻是忌神，不动为吉；子孙爻代表入赘的女婿，空了就不确定，又不宜动，动了还怕妨碍丈夫。子孙爻虽然动，但财爻也动来帮助鬼爻，就无妨。世爻是自己、应爻是女婿，都不可空：世空，自己有打发他回去的意思；应空，女婿没有久留的心；世应都动，以后必有变更；遇到六冲，丝毫不要用。合处逢冲，或变出六冲，起初和谐，后来分拆。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Taking a son-in-law in: the Resource line is the Adversary and is auspicious when it does not move; the Output line stands for the son-in-law, and if Void it is unsettled, and it should not move either, since moving may still harm the husband. Even if Output moves, if Wealth also moves and helps the Pressure line there is no harm. The Self line is oneself and the Other line the son-in-law, and neither may be Void: if the Self line is Void, one has a mind to send him back; if the Other line is Void, the son-in-law has no heart to stay long; if both move, there will surely be change later; a Six-clash is not to be used at all. A combination meeting a clash, or changing into a Six-clash, means harmony at first and separation afterwards.
