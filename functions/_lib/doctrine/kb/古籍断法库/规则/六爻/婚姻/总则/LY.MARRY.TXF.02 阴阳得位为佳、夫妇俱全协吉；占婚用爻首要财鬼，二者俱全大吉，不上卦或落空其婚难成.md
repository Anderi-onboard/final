# 阴阳得位为佳、夫妇俱全协吉；占婚用爻首要财鬼，二者俱全大吉，不上卦或落空其婚难成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 阴阳各在其位为好，夫妻双方都在（财、官俱全）就和谐吉利。阴阳得位有两种：卦象外阴内阳，或者应爻阴、世爻阳。占婚以财、官为首要的用爻，俱全大吉；男占女以财为主，女占男以官鬼为主。财、官不上卦或落空，婚事难成，成了也不吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Yin and yang each in its place is good, and husband and wife both present (Wealth and Pressure complete) is harmonious and lucky. Being in place takes two forms: a hexagram with yin outside and yang inside, or the Other line yin and the Self line yang. In a marriage divination Wealth and Pressure are the first Subject lines, and both present is very lucky; a man divining a woman takes Wealth as the main line, and a woman divining a man takes Pressure. If Wealth and Pressure are absent from the hexagram or fall Void, the marriage is hard to make, and even if made it is not lucky.

> [!note] 按
> 注里的「封象」多半是「卦象」之误，译文照卦象译。「纯阴纯阳之谓鬼」的「鬼」书里没有解释，照字面译作 ghost。
>
> *Note:* 'Feng xiang' in the note is most likely a slip for 'gua xiang', the hexagram image, and is translated so. The book does not explain 'ghost' in 'pure yin or pure yang is called a ghost', which is translated literally.
