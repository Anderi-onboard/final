# 绝处逢生·占婚：世应绝处逢生，事将解而后成，意先淡而后浓；男女两家贫乏无力得人扶策亦可成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用爻遇绝，却有一个生它的爻发动来生，又变得有用，叫绝处逢生。占婚姻遇到世爻、应爻绝处逢生：事情快要散的时候又成了，心意先淡后浓；或者可以说男女两家贫乏无力，但得到人扶助，这件事也能成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A line that meets extinction but has a moving line generate it becomes useful again: this is birth in a place of extinction. In a marriage divination, if Self and Other meet birth in a place of extinction, the matter is about to fall apart and then is made, and the intent is at first cool and then warm; or it may be said that the two families are poor and powerless but helped and supported by someone, the matter can still be made.

> | 且如申日占，卦遇用爻属木， | 比如在申日占卦，卦里遇到的用爻属木， | Suppose the divination is on a Shen day and the Subject line is Wood: |
> | 木见申则绝，木爻无用矣。 | 木遇到申就绝了，木爻没有用了； | Wood meets Shen and is cut off, and the Wood line is of no use; |
> | 此同无也。 | 这等于没有。 | this is the same as having none. |
> | 若得水爻发动，水长生动来相生，木爻仍复有用。 | 如果有水爻发动，水在申长生，动来相生，木爻又重新有用了。 | But if a Water line moves, Water being born at Shen and coming to generate it, the Wood line is useful again. |
> | 譬如人当困穷之际，得遇贵人扶持， | 就像人在穷困的时候，遇上贵人扶持， | It is like a person at a time of poverty and hardship who meets a noble patron's help: |
> | 必有寒谷回春之象，此乃是绝处逢生也。 | 一定有寒谷回春的样子，这就是绝处逢生。 | there is surely the look of spring returning to a cold valley; this is 'meeting life where there was severance'. |
