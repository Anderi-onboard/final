# 世爻入墓只验三种（随鬼入日墓、入动墓、动而化墓），且世爻用爻休囚被克才是真；占婚姻，世旺得地或财爻有气生世，冲墓之月日成，空破休囚断难许允

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻入墓，野鹤试验后只有三种应验：世爻随鬼入日墓、世爻入动爻的墓、世爻动而化入墓。自己占的看世爻，世爻旺相的不是真入墓；替别人占的看用神，用神旺相的也不是真。只有世爻、用爻休囚被克又入墓的才是；墓被日月和动爻冲破的，也不是真，因为墓破就像网破，很容易出来。占婚姻：世爻旺、得地，或者财爻有气去生世爻，在冲开墓的月日婚事成；世爻空、破、休囚的，断定难以答应。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Of the Self line entering a tomb, only three kinds have proved true on repeated trial: the Self line following Pressure into the Day's tomb, the Self line entering a moving line's tomb, and the Self line moving and turning into a tomb. In a divination for oneself look to the Self line, and if it is strong it is not the real thing; in a divination for another look to the Subject, and if it is strong it is not the real thing either. Only when the Self or Subject line is resting or trapped and controlled and also enters a tomb is it real; if the tomb is clashed and broken by the Day, Month or a moving line it is not real either, since a broken tomb is like a torn net and one gets out easily. In divining a marriage: if the Self line is strong and well placed, or the Wealth line has qi and generates the Self line, the match is made in the month or day that clashes the tomb open; if the Self line is Void, broken, resting or trapped, judge that consent is hard to obtain.

> [!note] 按
> 第二句「空破休囚者」没有写是哪一爻；同一章占功名、占求财、占身命都是「世旺得地……世若休囚空破」，所以译作世爻。同一章里其余各问（功名、求财、疾病、行人、家宅等）另有断语，这里只收婚姻一条。
>
> *Note:* The second sentence, 'where it is Void, broken, resting or trapped', does not say which line; in the same chapter the entries for fame, wealth and fortune all read 'Self strong and well placed ... if the Self line is resting, Void or broken', so it is read as the Self line. The other entries of that chapter (fame, wealth, illness, travellers, the household and others) are not included; only the one on marriage is.
