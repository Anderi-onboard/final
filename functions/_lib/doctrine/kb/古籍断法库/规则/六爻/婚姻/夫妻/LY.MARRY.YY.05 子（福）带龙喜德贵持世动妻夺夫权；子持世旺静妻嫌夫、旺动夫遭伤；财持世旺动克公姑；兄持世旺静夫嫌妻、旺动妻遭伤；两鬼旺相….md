# 子（福）带龙喜德贵持世动妻夺夫权；子持世旺静妻嫌夫、旺动夫遭伤；财持世旺动克公姑；兄持世旺静夫嫌妻、旺动妻遭伤；两鬼旺相而财被刑冲克害必生离改嫁；世应财鬼带刑刃鳏寡杀动夫妻不久

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 子孙爻带青龙、喜神、德神、贵人，持世而动，妻子夺丈夫的权；子孙持世，旺而静，妻子必定嫌丈夫；旺而动，丈夫必定遭伤。财爻持世，旺而动，克公婆；兄弟爻持世，旺而静，丈夫嫌妻子；旺而动，妻子遭伤。两个鬼爻旺相，而月建、日辰和动爻刑冲克害财爻的，一定生离改嫁。世应财鬼带刑、刃、鳏寡煞而动，夫妻不长久。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Output line carrying the Azure Dragon, the Joy star, Virtue and the Nobleman, holding the Self line and moving: the wife seizes the husband's authority; Output on the Self line, strong and still: the wife surely dislikes her husband; strong and moving: the husband is surely harmed. The Wealth line on the Self line, strong and moving, harms the parents-in-law; a Peer line on the Self line, strong and still: the husband dislikes his wife; strong and moving: the wife is harmed. Two Pressure lines, strong, while the Month, Day and moving lines punish, clash, control or harm the Wealth line: surely parting alive and remarriage. The Self, Other, Wealth and Pressure lines carrying the punishment, blade and Widower-Widow stars and moving: the couple will not last.
