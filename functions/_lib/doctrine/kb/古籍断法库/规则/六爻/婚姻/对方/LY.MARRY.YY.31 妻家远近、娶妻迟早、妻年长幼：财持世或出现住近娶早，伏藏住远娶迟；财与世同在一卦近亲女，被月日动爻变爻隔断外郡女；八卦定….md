# 妻家远近、娶妻迟早、妻年长幼：财持世或出现住近娶早，伏藏住远娶迟；财与世同在一卦近亲女，被月日动爻变爻隔断外郡女；八卦定方二十八宿定郡；财在二爻坐宅必养媳妇否亦邻女；财合世身命娶必早；妻阳世阴妻年长、世阳妻阴妻年幼；妻居辰戌丑未长女、子午卯酉中女、寅申巳亥少女

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问妻家远近、娶妻早晚、妻子年长年幼：妻财爻持世或出现，一定住得近、娶得早；伏藏不现，住得远、娶得迟。财爻和世爻同在一个卦，是近亲的女儿；财爻和世爻被月日、动爻、变爻隔断的，一定是外郡人的女儿——比如乾卦戌世、寅财，而申、午、辰爻动，或月日并动申、午、辰爻，都是隔断。用八卦定方位，用二十八宿定是哪个郡；卦空爻空，就用财爻的长生方位来定。财爻在二爻（二爻是宅爻）叫坐宅，必是养媳妇，不然也是紧邻的女子。财爻合世爻、身爻、命爻的，娶妻一定早。妻爻属阳、世爻属阴，妻子年长；世爻属阳、妻爻属阴，妻子年幼。妻爻在辰、戌、丑、未是长女，在子、午、卯、酉是中女，在寅、申、巳、亥是少女。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask how far the wife's family lives, how soon he will marry and whether the wife is older or younger: if the Wealth line holds the Self line or appears, she surely lives near and the marriage comes early; if it is hidden and does not appear, she lives far and the marriage comes late. Wealth and the Self line in the same trigram: a daughter of a close relative; cut off from each other by the Month, Day, moving or changed lines: surely a daughter of someone from another prefecture (for example in Qian with the Self on Xu and Wealth on Yin, a moving Shen, Wu or Chen line, or the Month and Day together moving on Shen, Wu or Chen, all cut them off). Use the eight trigrams to fix the direction and the twenty-eight lodges to fix the prefecture; if the hexagram or line is Void, fix it by the direction of the Wealth line's Birth stage. Wealth on the second line (the house line) is called sitting in the house: surely a child bride raised in the family, or else a girl from next door. Wealth combining with the Self, Body or fate line: he marries early. A yang wife's line with a yin Self line: the wife is older; a yang Self line with a yin wife's line: the wife is younger. The wife's line on Chen, Xu, Chou or Wei: the eldest daughter; on Zi, Wu, Mao or You: a middle daughter; on Yin, Shen, Si or Hai: the youngest daughter.
