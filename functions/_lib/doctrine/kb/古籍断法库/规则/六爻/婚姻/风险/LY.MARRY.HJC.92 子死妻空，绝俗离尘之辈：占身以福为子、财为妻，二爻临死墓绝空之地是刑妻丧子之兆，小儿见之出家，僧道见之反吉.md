# 子死妻空，绝俗离尘之辈：占身以福为子、财为妻，二爻临死墓绝空之地是刑妻丧子之兆，小儿见之出家，僧道见之反吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占身命，子孙爻是儿子，财爻是妻子。这两个爻如果落在死、墓、绝、空的地方，是刑妻丧子的征兆。小孩子见到这种卦，一定超脱世俗、出家；僧人道士见到反而是吉兆，没有世俗的牵累。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining one's person, the Output line is the son and the Wealth line the wife. If these two lines fall into Death, Tomb, Extinction or Void, it is the sign of a wife punished and a son lost. A child meeting this will surely leave the world and become a monk or nun; a monk or Taoist meeting it finds it lucky, with no worldly ties to hold him.

## 《黄金策·身命》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6039 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Person and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 子死妻空，绝俗离尘之辈； | 子孙死了、妻财落空，是绝俗离尘的人； | Output dead and Wealth void: one who cuts off worldly ties; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6040 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 占身以福为子，财为妻。 | 占身命，以福德爻为儿子，以财爻为妻子。 | In divining one's person, take the Output line as the son and the Wealth line as the wife. |
> | 二爻若临死墓绝空之地，乃是刑妻丧子之兆。 | 这两爻如果落在死、墓、绝、空的地方，就是刑妻丧子的征兆。 | If these two lines fall into Death, Tomb, Extinction or Void, it is the sign of a wife punished and a son lost. |
> | 小儿见之，必绝俗离尘，出家辈也。 | 小孩子见到，一定超脱世俗、出家的人。 | A child meeting this will surely be one who leaves the world and becomes a monk or nun. |
> | 若僧道见之，反为吉兆，必无俗累之牵惹矣。 | 如果是僧人道士见到，反而是吉兆，一定没有世俗的牵累。 | If a monk or Taoist meets it, on the contrary it is lucky, and surely he has no worldly ties to hold him. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1675 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 以福为子，财为妻，二爻若临死墓绝空之地，乃是刑丧妻子之兆，必绝俗离尘辈也。 | 以福德爻为儿子，财爻为妻子，这两爻如果落在死、墓、绝、空的地方，是刑伤妻子、丧失儿子的征兆，一定是超脱世俗的人。 | Take the Output line as the son and the Wealth line as the wife; if these two lines fall into Death, Tomb, Extinction or Void, it is the sign of a wife injured and a son lost, and he is surely one who leaves the world. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 这一条是「占身命」里说的，不是专门占婚姻；收在这里是因为「刑妻」是婚姻的后果之一。卜筮大全在同一首赋的另一处（第 9617 行）重复了这一句。
>
> *Note:* This entry is said of divining one's person and not specially of marriage; it is included because a wife punished is one of the consequences of a marriage. The Quanshu repeats the line in another place in the same rhapsody (line 9617).
