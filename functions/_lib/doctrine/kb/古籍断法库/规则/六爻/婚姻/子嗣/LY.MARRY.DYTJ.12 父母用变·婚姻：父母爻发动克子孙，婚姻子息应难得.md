# 父母用变·婚姻：父母爻发动克子孙，婚姻子息应难得

六爻 · 婚姻 · 子嗣 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 父母爻一发动就克子孙：病人无药、沉昏；婚姻和子息都难得；买卖劳心，利不存。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Resource line moves it controls Output: a sick person has no medicine and sinks into stupor; marriage and offspring are hard to get; in trade, effort is spent and profit does not stay.

## 《断易天机·散见各章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[断易天机]] 第 818 行 · Duanyi Tianji (Heavenly Secrets of Judging the Changes), "Scattered sections", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 父动当头克子孙， | 父母爻发动，当头克子孙， | Resource moving and squarely controlling Output: |
> | 病人无药主沉昏； | 病人没有药可用，昏沉不醒； | the sick have no medicine and sink into stupor; |
> | 婚姻子息应难得， | 婚姻、子嗣难得； | marriage and offspring are hard to get; |
> | 买卖劳心利不存。 | 做买卖，劳心费力，没有利。 | in trade one wears out the mind and no profit remains. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句只有「婚姻子息应难得」说到婚姻，前后是病和买卖。
>
> *Note:* Only 'marriage and offspring are hard to get' speaks of marriage; the clauses around it are about illness and trade.
