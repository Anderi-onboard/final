# 世应不和，仗冰言而通好：世应相冲相克难成，得间爻生合解救，靠媒人两边说合可成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应相冲相克，本来难成亲，如果得间爻生合来解救，终归要靠媒人两边说合来通好，事也可以成；解救的办法，比如应动克世，间爻合住应爻、生扶世爻，其余类推。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self and Other clashing or controlling each other makes the match hard, but if a between line rescues them by generating or combining, in the end the go-betweens must speak to both sides to bring them together, and then it can succeed; the way of rescue is, for example, when the Other moves to control the Self, a between line combines with the Other and generates the Self, and the rest by analogy.
