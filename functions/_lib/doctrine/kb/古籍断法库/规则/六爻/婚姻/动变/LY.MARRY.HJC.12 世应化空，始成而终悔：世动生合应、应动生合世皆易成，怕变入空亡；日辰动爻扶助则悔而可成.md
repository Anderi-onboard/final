# 世应化空，始成而终悔：世动生合应、应动生合世皆易成，怕变入空亡；日辰动爻扶助则悔而可成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世动生合应爻，男家愿成；应动生合世爻，女家愿嫁；都是容易成的象；只怕变入空亡，一定有退悔的意思；如果有日辰、动爻扶助，虽悔也可成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self moving to generate or combine with the Other means the man's family is willing; the Other moving to generate or combine with the Self means the woman's family is willing to give her; both are images of easy success; only turning into Void is feared, which means a wish to withdraw and regret; if the Day or a moving line supports it, it can still succeed despite regret.

> | 世应化空，始成而终悔。 | 世应变成空亡，开始成了，最终反悔。 | The Self and Other turning into Void: it succeeds at first and ends in regret. |
