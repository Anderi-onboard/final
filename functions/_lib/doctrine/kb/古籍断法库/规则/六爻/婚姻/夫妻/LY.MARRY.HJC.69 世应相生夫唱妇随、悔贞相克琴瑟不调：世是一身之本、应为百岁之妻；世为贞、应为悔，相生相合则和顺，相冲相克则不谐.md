# 世应相生夫唱妇随、悔贞相克琴瑟不调：世是一身之本、应为百岁之妻；世为贞、应为悔，相生相合则和顺，相冲相克则不谐

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻是自己一身的根本，应爻是相伴百年的妻子。世应相生相合，一定夫唱妇随（汉朝鲍宣娶桓少君）；世（贞）应（悔）相冲相克，一定夫妻不谐（唐朝郭暧招升平公主）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self line is the root of one's whole person and the Other line is the wife of a hundred years. Self and Other generating and combining means husband leads and wife follows (like Bao Xuan of Han and Lady Huan Shaojun); Self (Zhen) and Other (Hui) clashing and controlling means the couple is out of tune (like Guo Ai of Tang and Princess Shengping).

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1698 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世是一生之本，应为百岁之妻子，若见生合，必然夫唱妇随，若见冲克，必然琴瑟不调。 | 世爻是一生的根本，应爻是百年相伴的妻子，如果看到相生相合，一定夫唱妇随；如果看到相冲相克，一定夫妻不和谐。 | The Self line is the root of a whole life and the Other line is the wife of a hundred years; if they generate and combine, husband leads and wife follows, surely; if they clash and control, the couple is surely out of tune. |

^q5

> [!note] 按
> 「悔贞」在这里指世应两爻：注说「世为贞、应为悔」，不是内卦外卦。鲍宣、桓少君是汉朝的一对贫贱夫妻，妻子不要陪嫁、随夫过朴素日子；郭暧是唐代宗的女婿，和妻子升平公主吵过架。卜筮大全写「郭暖」，卜筮正宗写「郭曦」，人名是郭暧，译文按卜筮大全。这一句在卜筮全书里紧挨着「世是一生之本、应为百岁之妻」，那句的出处是这里的旧注（见 LY.MARRY.ZS.04）。
>
> *Note:* 'Hui and Zhen' here means the Self and Other lines: the commentary says 'the Self line is Zhen and the Other line is Hui', not the inner and outer trigrams. Bao Xuan and Huan Shaojun were a poor Han couple, the wife giving up her dowry to live simply with her husband; Guo Ai was a son-in-law of Emperor Daizong of Tang who quarrelled with his wife Princess Shengping. The Quanshu writes 'Guo Nuan' and the Zhengzong 'Guo Xi'; the name is Guo Ai, and the translation follows the Quanshu. The line 'the Self line is the root of a life, the Other line the wife of a hundred years' quoted at LY.MARRY.ZS.04 comes from the old commentary here.
