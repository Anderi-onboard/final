# 应财受制原配难偕老，动妻旺相续弦可白头；妻财克世不刑则悍，应财生世非内助即外财

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中财爻出现几个、要分正室和偏房时，以应爻上的财爻作正妻的位置：被日月、动爻冲克，或者动而化成凶象，就是原配妻子有伤；如果别的爻上的财旺相，或动而化吉，或者别的爻变出来的财旺相来生合世爻，就是续娶的妻子反而能白头到老。财爻不在应爻上，就用本卦的财当正妻，变爻里的财当再娶的。妻财克身，不是刑伤就一定是妒悍的妻；妻财克世而世爻空、破、休囚，或入墓、绝，或动而变凶，或随鬼入墓，只要碰上一条，丈夫就短命早亡；如果是旺财克世而世爻得地，虽然没有刑伤，也一定遇上悍妇。应爻上的财爻生世，不是得到贤内助，就是得到外财；财爻生合世爻也不能只按和美来断，间或有应在得到妻家财力帮助的，不然的话妻子一定善于持家。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When several Wealth lines appear and one must tell the principal wife from the secondary ones, take the Wealth on the Other line as the principal wife's position: if it is clashed or controlled by the Day, Month or a moving line, or moves and changes into ill, the wife of first marriage is harmed; if the Wealth on another line is strong or moves and changes into good, or a Wealth line changed out from another line is strong and generates or combines with the Self line, the wife of a second marriage, on the contrary, fulfils the wish of growing old together. If the Wealth line is not on the Other line, take the Wealth in the original hexagram as the principal wife and the Wealth in the changed lines as the second marriage. If the wife (Wealth) controls the Self, it is either injury or a jealous and fierce wife; if Wealth controls the Self line while the Self line is Void, broken, resting or trapped, or in tomb or extinction, or moves and changes into ill, or follows Pressure into a tomb, meeting any one of these means the husband dies young; but if a strong Wealth line controls the Self and the Self line holds its ground, there is no injury but he will surely meet a shrew. Wealth on the Other line generating the Self means either a helpful wife at home or wealth from outside; when a Wealth line generates or combines with the Self, do not judge it simply as harmony, for at times it answers as gaining the wife's family's financial help, and otherwise the wife will be good at running the household.
