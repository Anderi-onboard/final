# 用神绝处逢生：男婚用财、女姻用鬼，遇之者起初如参辰、后来成泰晋

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 用神落在绝地，又遇到生它的，叫「绝处逢生」（这一章列了五种情形）。用在婚姻上：男子的婚事用妻财、女子的婚事用官鬼当用神；用神绝而逢生的，婚事起初像参星和辰星那样两不相见，后来成了泰、晋那样的顺遂。要注意这一章同时列了三种「生不了」的情形：用神自己被破、被散；生它的飞爻或动爻被破、被散；生它的变爻被破、被绝——遇到这三种，绝处也逢不了生。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Subject stands in its extinction and meets a line that generates it, this is 'extinction meeting birth' (the chapter lists five forms). Applied to marriage: for a man's marriage the Subject is Wealth, for a woman's it is Pressure, and when it is in extinction and meets birth, the marriage is at first like the stars Shen and Chen that never meet, and afterwards turns out as well as Tai and Jin. Note that the same chapter lists three cases where birth cannot come: the Subject itself is broken or scattered; the flying or moving line that generates it is broken or scattered; the changed line that generates it is broken or in extinction. In these three, even in extinction it does not meet birth.

## 《易冒·绝生章第三十三》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 569 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 33: Extinction meeting birth", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身命世绝而逢生，虽困必亨，虽夭必寿； | 占身命，世爻绝了又逢生，虽然困顿也一定亨通，虽然短命也一定长寿； | In divining life and fate, if the Self line is in extinction and meets birth, though troubled it will surely prosper, and though short-lived it will surely live long; |
> | 文科重父，武试重官，遇之者几落孙山，终登龙虎； | 文科看重父母爻，武试看重官鬼爻，遇到这种情形的人，差一点名落孙山，最后终于登上龙虎榜； | for the civil examinations the Resource line counts, for the military examinations the Pressure line; one who meets this nearly falls off the list and in the end mounts the Dragon-and-Tiger list; |
> | 男婚用财，女姻用鬼，遇之者后成泰晋，始则参辰。 | 男子的婚事用妻财，女子的婚事用官鬼，遇到这种情形的，后来成了泰、晋那样的顺遂，起初却像参星和辰星那样见不到面。 | for a man's marriage use Wealth, for a woman's marriage use Pressure; one who meets this ends in the good fortune of Tai and Jin, though at the start they are like the stars Shen and Chen that never meet. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「遇之者」指遇到「绝处逢生」这种情形的人：同一句里先是身命（世爻绝而逢生），再是文科武试，最后才是婚姻，句式相同。「泰晋」是泰卦和晋卦的名字，取通泰、上进的意思；「参辰」是参星和辰星，一个在西一个在东，此出彼没、永不同见，是分隔的老比喻（常说「参商」）。这一章前面（第 565 行）还列了「绝处逢生」的五种形式，是通例，不专讲婚姻，没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'One who meets this' means one who meets 'extinction meeting birth'; the sentence runs in the same form for life and fate (the Self line in extinction meeting birth), then the civil and military examinations, and last marriage. 'Tai and Jin' are the hexagram names Tai (peace) and Jin (advance), meaning ease and progress; 'Shen and Chen' are two stars, one in the west and one in the east, one rising as the other sets and never seen together, the old figure for separation (usually 'Shen and Shang'). Earlier in the chapter (line 565) five forms of extinction meeting birth are listed; they are general and do not speak of marriage in particular, and are not taken.
