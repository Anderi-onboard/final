# 辟《易林补遗》婚姻嫁娶之谬：用神要按占的人与被占的人是什么关系来取，不能一概以内外卦、世应、财鬼论夫妇

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[卜筮正宗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《易林补遗》一概拿内外卦、世应爻、阴阳财鬼来论夫妇，不管是谁在占、为谁占，这是大谬。王洪绪主张：世爻是我家、应爻是对方家；女人自己占嫁人，以官爻为丈夫，官世相生相合、官阳世阴才叫得地；男人自己占娶妻，以财爻为妻子，财世相生相合、财阴世阳才叫得地；父母尊长为子孙的婚事占，以子孙爻为用神，世上的子孙或阳象是我家的男孩，应上的子孙或阴象是对方的女孩；无冲破而有生合就可配、夫妻和谐，无生合而有冲克就夫妻不睦，休囚或受克就不长寿；为弟弟娶妻、妹妹出嫁占，都以兄弟爻为用神。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Yilin Buyi judges husband and wife by the inner and outer trigrams, the Self and Other lines, and yin, yang, Wealth and Pressure alike, without regard to who divines and for whom; this is a great mistake. Wang Hongxu holds: the Self line is my family and the Other line the other family; when a woman divines for herself about marrying, Pressure is the husband, and only if Pressure and Self generate and combine, with Pressure yang and Self yin, is it called being in place; when a man divines for himself about marrying, Wealth is the wife, and only if Wealth and Self generate and combine, with Wealth yin and Self yang, is it in place; when parents or elders divine the marriage of a child, Output is the Subject, with Output on Self or on a yang image the boy of my family and Output on Other or on a yin image the girl of the other family; with generation and combination and no clash or break they can be matched and are harmonious, with clash and control and no generation or combination they are at odds, and resting or controlled they will not live long; for a younger brother's marriage or a younger sister's, take the Peer line as the Subject.

> | 无冲破有生合，自然可配，夫妇和谐，无生合有冲克，自然夫妇不睦，或休囚或受克自然不寿。 | 没有冲破、有生合，自然可配，夫妻和谐；没有生合、有冲克，自然夫妻不睦，休囚或者受克，自然不长寿。 | With no clash or break and with generation or combination, they can naturally be matched and husband and wife are harmonious; with no generation or combination but clash and control, husband and wife are naturally at odds, and if resting or trapped or controlled, they will naturally not live long. |

> | 或为弟娶妹嫁者，皆以兄弟爻为用神也。 | 或者替弟弟娶妻、妹妹出嫁占，都以兄弟爻为用神。 | Or for a younger brother's marriage or a younger sister's, always take the Peer line as the Subject. |

> [!warning] 打架
> 这是王洪绪对《易林补遗》婚姻说法的正面批评（LY.MARRY.YL.05、YL.06、YL.20、YL.21）：《易林补遗》一概按内外卦、世应、财鬼论夫妇，王洪绪要求先分清是谁在占、替谁占，再按这层关系取用神。《增删卜易》也讲代占与自占取用不同（LY.MARRY.ZS.04）。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This is Wang Hongxu's direct attack on the Yilin Buyi's teaching on marriage (LY.MARRY.YL.05, YL.06, YL.20 and YL.21): the Yilin Buyi judges husband and wife throughout by the inner and outer trigrams, Self and Other, and Wealth and Pressure, whereas Wang Hongxu requires first telling who is divining and for whom, and choosing the Subject by that relation. The Zengshan Buyi likewise distinguishes taking the Subject for a proxy from taking it for oneself (LY.MARRY.ZS.04).

> [!note] 按
> 张星元即《易林补遗》的作者张世宝（字星元）。「先天之妙旨」指易的本意，书里没有再解释。第三、四、五段是两个卦例，卦图（六爻的排列）不收，只收卦图前后的文字；第二个卦例的前一句在卦图之前。
>
> *Note:* Zhang Xingyuan is the author of the Yilin Buyi, Zhang Shibao. 'The fine purport of the primal Way' means the original intent of the Changes and is not explained further. The third to fifth passages belong to two cases, whose hexagram diagrams (the arrangement of the six lines) are not included, only the text before and after the diagrams; the second case's first sentence comes before its diagram.
