# 婚姻卦男占妇以财为主、女占夫以鬼为先：单看用爻，不可将卦名而断、不可凭星煞而推；「结婚推财鬼」是俗言，用神只有一爻，不是二象

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占卜不要乱说：一不可以按卦名来断，二不可以凭星煞来推，只看用爻，就一点疑惑也没有。婚姻卦里，男子占女子以财爻为主，女子占男子以官鬼为先。俗话说「结婚推财鬼」（财、鬼两样都要看），这不对：用神只有一个爻，怎么能说是两个象？
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining do not speak wildly: one must not judge by the hexagram's name, nor reckon by stars and spirits; look only at the Subject line and there is no doubt at all. In a marriage hexagram, when a man divines a woman Wealth is the chief, and when a woman divines a man Pressure comes first. The common saying 'for marriage reckon Wealth and Pressure' (look at both) is wrong: the Subject is one line, so how can one speak of two images?

> [!warning] 打架
> 这一段直接反对两件在别处通行的做法：一是「占婚财鬼双全」——《天玄赋》的注说「占婚用爻首要财鬼，二者俱全称为大吉」（LY.MARRY.TXF.02），阐奥歌章说「财鬼双全便可成」（LY.MARRY.HZL.22），而这里说用神只有一爻；二是按卦名断婚——《易冒》以咸、恒、节、泰为吉、睽、革、解、离为凶（LY.MARRY.YM.03），《增删卜易》里觉子引它（LY.MARRY.ZS.14），而这里说一不可将卦名而断。
>
> *Where they disagree:* This passage plainly opposes two practices that were current elsewhere. One is 'a marriage needs both Wealth and Pressure': the note to the Tianxuan Fu says 'in divining marriage the Subject lines are first Wealth and Pressure, and having both is greatly lucky' (LY.MARRY.TXF.02), and the Chan Ao Ge Zhang says 'with both Wealth and Pressure complete it can be made' (LY.MARRY.HZL.22), while this passage says the Subject is one line. The other is judging marriage by the hexagram's name: the Yi Mao takes Xian, Heng, Jie and Tai as lucky and Kui, Ge, Xie and Li as ill (LY.MARRY.YM.03), quoted by Juezi in the Zengshan Buyi (LY.MARRY.ZS.14), while this passage says one must not judge by the name.

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮全书里男占婚、女占婚各以哪一爻为用神，各书大同小异：男占取财、女占取鬼是最通行的（LY.MARRY.ZS.01）。这里的特别处是把它说成「唯一」，并且反对「财鬼并看」。
>
> *Note:* Which line the books take as the Subject for a man's or a woman's marriage divination differs little from book to book: Wealth for a man and Pressure for a woman is the most common (LY.MARRY.ZS.01). What is distinctive here is that it is said to be the only one, and looking at Wealth and Pressure together is opposed.
