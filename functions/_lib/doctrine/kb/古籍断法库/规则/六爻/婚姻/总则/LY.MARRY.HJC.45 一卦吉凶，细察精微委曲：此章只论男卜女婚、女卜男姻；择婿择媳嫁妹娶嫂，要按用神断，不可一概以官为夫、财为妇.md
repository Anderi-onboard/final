# 一卦吉凶，细察精微委曲：此章只论男卜女婚、女卜男姻；择婿择媳嫁妹娶嫂，要按用神断，不可一概以官为夫、财为妇

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 一卦的吉凶，要细察其中精微曲折；夫妇百年，才知道到底能否团圆。王洪绪特别说：这一章只是讲男子占女方婚事、女子占男方姻缘；今天的术士不辨清楚，凡择婿、择媳、嫁妹、娶嫂，都不按用神断，一概以官为夫、财为妇，大大误人；应当按用神的吉凶来推断，不可一概只论财官。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The good and ill of a hexagram is to be examined for its fine and winding detail; only over a lifetime as husband and wife does one know whether they come together in the end. Wang Hongxu adds especially: this chapter speaks only of a man divining about a bride or a woman divining about a bridegroom; today's practitioners, not seeing the distinction, for choosing a son-in-law or daughter-in-law, marrying off a younger sister or taking a brother's wife, do not judge by the Subject but always take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife, which misleads people greatly; one should judge by the good or ill of the Subject and never speak of Wealth and Pressure alike in every case.

> [!warning] 打架
> 这一章的财官各指谁：旧注一路按「官为夫、财为妇」讲（见 HJC.11、HJC.24）；王洪绪在章末明说这是「男卜女婚、女卜男姻」的意思，别人代占、兄占弟婚之类要按用神断，不可一概；觉子（《增删卜易》）也说男家占女以财为用、女家占男以官为用，不拘父母亲朋代占都同。
>
> *Where they disagree:* Whom Wealth and Pressure stand for in this chapter: the old commentary throughout speaks by 'Pressure the husband, Wealth the wife' (see HJC.11, HJC.24); Wang Hongxu says at the chapter's end that this is what applies when a man divines about a bride or a woman about a bridegroom, and that divinations made on another's behalf or by an elder brother for a younger must be judged by the Subject and not all alike; Juezi (Zengshan Buyi) also says that the man's family divining about the woman takes Wealth as the Subject and the woman's family divining about the man takes Pressure, the same whether parents or relatives divine on their behalf.
