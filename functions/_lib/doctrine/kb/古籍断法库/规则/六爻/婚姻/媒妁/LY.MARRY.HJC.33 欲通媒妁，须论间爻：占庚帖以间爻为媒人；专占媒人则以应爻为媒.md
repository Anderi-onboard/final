# 欲通媒妁，须论间爻：占庚帖以间爻为媒人；专占媒人则以应爻为媒

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 占庚帖，以间爻为媒人；如果是专为媒人这件事占，就不按间爻论，一定以应爻为媒。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In divining the horoscope card, the between lines are the go-between; if the divination is solely about the go-between, do not go by the between lines but take the Other line as the go-between.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6420 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 欲通媒妁，须论间爻。 | 想通过媒人说合，须论间爻。 | To go through a go-between, consider the between lines. |
