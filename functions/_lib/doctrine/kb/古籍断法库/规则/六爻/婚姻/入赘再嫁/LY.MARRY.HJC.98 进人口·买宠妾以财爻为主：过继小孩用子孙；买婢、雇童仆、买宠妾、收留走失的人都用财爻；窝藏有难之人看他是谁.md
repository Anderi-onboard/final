# 进人口·买宠妾以财爻为主：过继小孩用子孙；买婢、雇童仆、买宠妾、收留走失的人都用财爻；窝藏有难之人看他是谁

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 《黄金策》「进人口」一章讲收养、买人、雇人。用神要另外占一卦来定：过继小孩用子孙爻；买婢女、雇童仆、买宠妾、收留走失的人，都用财爻；窝藏有难的人，看那个人是谁——朋友用兄弟爻、尊长用父母爻、妇人用妻财爻、官府中人用官鬼爻。买宠妾用的就是财爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Huangjin Ce's chapter on taking in persons covers adoption, buying people and hiring. The Subject is fixed by a separate divination: for adopting a child use the Output line; for buying a maid, hiring a servant boy, buying a favoured concubine or taking in a lost person use the Wealth line; for sheltering a person in trouble look at who he is: a friend the Peer line, an elder the Resource line, a woman the Wealth line, a man in office the Pressure line. Buying a favoured concubine uses the Wealth line.

> [!note] 按
> 原文的「宠妾」是「嬖」（受宠爱的侍妾）。「螟蛉」是过继的孩子，这一章开头的注解：老而无子叫独，过继别人的儿子叫螟蛉。这一章后面的各联都按这里定的用爻去断，「用爻」就是被收留、被买、被雇的那个人；本库只收其中讲到妻妾、妇人的几联。
>
> *Note:* 'Favoured concubine' in the original is 'bi', a concubine who is loved. 'Mingling' is an adopted child; the opening note of the chapter says that an old man with no son is called 'alone' and adopting another's son is called 'mingling'. The couplets that follow in the chapter are all judged from the Subject line fixed here, which is the person taken in, bought or hired; only the couplets that speak of wives, concubines and women are taken into this library.
