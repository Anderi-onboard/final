# 纳妾重贤用财、重嗣用子孙；冲喜兼详六亲，为长用父母、为幼用子孙；不谐而问去留：生合可留、克世宜去，命随鬼墓者夭、贞冲合冲者散

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 纳妾：看他看重什么，重贤德就用财爻，重子嗣就用子孙爻。冲喜：要一并详看六亲，为长辈冲喜用父母爻，为晚辈冲喜用子孙爻。如果不和谐而问去还是留：生合就可以留，克世就宜去；命跟着官鬼入墓的短命，贞卦冲、合处遇冲的会散。小注说：人心已去而留不住，就像泼出去的水，硬留就会生出变故，占筮的人应当明白告诉他。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Taking a concubine: look at what one values; if virtue, use Wealth, if heirs, use Output. Marrying to drive off illness: examine the six relations as well, using the Resource line for an elder and Output for a younger person. If things are not harmonious and one asks whether to keep or dismiss: generating or combining, it can stay; controlling Self, it is better to let go; the Life following Pressure into the tomb dies young; the Zhen hexagram clashing, or a combination meeting a clash, means they part. The note says: when a heart has gone and will not stay it is like spilled water; if it is kept, changes will arise, and the diviner should say so plainly.

## 《易冒》括号小注(书里没有标注者)

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 「冲喜」是家里有人重病，借办喜事（通常是娶亲）来冲掉病气。「去留」问的是去还是留，书里没有说是问妾还是问这门亲事，译文不指明。
>
> *Note:* 'Marrying to drive off illness' is holding a wedding, usually taking a bride, when someone in the family is gravely ill, to 'clash away' the sickness with joy. 'Keeping or dismissing' does not say whether it is the concubine or the marriage that is in question, and the translation does not say either.
