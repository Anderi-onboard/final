# 命旺则荣华可拟，时衰则发达难期：本命爻生旺有气则荣华，休囚死绝则无发达；本命爻所临六亲六神各主一种性好

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 本命爻生旺有气，日后有荣华；休囚死绝，没有发达的日子。财旺命衰，妻子相貌虽美命却平常；鬼衰命旺，丈夫虽愚蠢衣食丰足。命临父母好技艺，加青龙好诗书，临兄好赌博、爱花钱、不好学，临官喜迎官府，临财福善于持家。王洪绪反对拿四季论旺衰，主张看本命爻是否临财福、青龙、贵人，或得日辰动爻生扶。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the natal line is born or thriving and has qi, glory lies ahead; when it is weak, dead or cut off, there is no rise. With Wealth thriving and the natal line weak, the wife is handsome but her fate is ordinary; with Pressure weak and the natal line thriving, the husband is dull but well fed and clothed. The natal line on Resource means a liking for skills, with the Azure Dragon added for poetry and books, on Peer for gambling, spending and no love of study, on Pressure a welcome to officials, on Wealth or Output a good housekeeper. Wang Hongxu objects to judging thriving and weakness by the four seasons and looks instead at whether the natal line sits on Wealth, Output, the Azure Dragon or a noble, or is generated and supported by the Day or a moving line.

> [!warning] 打架
> 旺衰怎么论：旧注按月令、日时（四季）；王洪绪明说古法按四季论旺衰是错的，改看本命爻临什么六亲六神、有没有日辰动爻生扶；两家在这一条上直接不同。
>
> *Where they disagree:* How to judge thriving and weakness: the old commentary goes by the month and by the day and hour (the seasons); Wang Hongxu says outright that the old method of judging by the four seasons is wrong, and looks instead at what relation and spirit the natal line sits on and whether the Day or a moving line supports it; the two differ directly here.
