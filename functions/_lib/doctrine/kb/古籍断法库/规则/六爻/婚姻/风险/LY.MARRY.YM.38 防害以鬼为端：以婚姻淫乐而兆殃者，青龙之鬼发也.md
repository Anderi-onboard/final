# 防害以鬼为端：以婚姻淫乐而兆殃者，青龙之鬼发也

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 戒防章里总说防祸害以官鬼为开端：因为言语音信出祸的，是朱雀临着的官鬼发动；因为婚姻、淫乐而预兆祸殃的，是青龙临着的官鬼发动。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The chapter on precautions says that in guarding against harm Pressure is where to begin: where harm comes through words and messages, it is the Pressure line on the Vermilion Bird moving; where disaster is foretold through marriage and lustful pleasure, it is the Pressure line on the Azure Dragon moving.

## 《易冒·戒防章第八十八》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1925–1926 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 88: Precautions", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 概言防害， | 总的说来，防祸害， | In general, in guarding against harm, |
> | 以鬼为端。 | 以官鬼为开端。 | Pressure is where to begin. |
> | 以言语音信而产祸者， | 因为言语音信而出祸事的， | Where harm comes through words and messages, |
> | 朱雀之鬼发也； | 是朱雀临着的官鬼发动； | it is the Pressure line on the Vermilion Bird moving; |
> | 以婚姻淫乐而兆殃者， | 因为婚姻、淫乐而预兆祸殃的， | where disaster is foretold through marriage and lustful pleasure, |
> | 青龙之鬼发也； | 是青龙临着的官鬼发动； | it is the Pressure line on the Azure Dragon moving; |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这一句在戒防章开头，讲各种祸害由哪个六神临着的官鬼发动来。它把「婚姻」和「淫乐」放在一起，说的是因婚姻和淫乐的事出祸，不是说婚姻本身是祸；紧接着的一句讲勾陈、螣蛇、玄武、白虎各主什么祸，不属婚姻，没有收。
>
> *Note:* This sentence stands at the head of the chapter on precautions and tells which six-spirit's Pressure line, moving, brings which kind of harm. It puts 'marriage' and 'lustful pleasure' together and speaks of harm coming through matters of marriage and pleasure, not of marriage itself as a harm; the sentence that follows, on what harm the Hook Earth, Coiling Snake, Dark Tortoise and White Tiger each bring, is not about marriage and is not taken.
