# 世值凶而应克，愿听鸡鸣：世爻自带刑害兄官虎蛇等凶神，反喜应爻来克世，去我之病，主有贤妻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世爻自己带着刑害、兄弟、官鬼、白虎、螣蛇这些凶神，应爻来克它，反而是对方来救我的过失、去我的毛病，不是伤我，主有贤惠的妻子（像齐国国君得到贤妃日夜规劝）。和上一条对照：世爻本来没事而被应爻克制，是怕老婆；世爻自己带凶而被应爻克制，是有人纠正我。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the Self line itself carries punishment, harm and ill spirits such as Peer, Pressure, the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake, and the Other line comes to control it, then the other party is coming to save me from my faults and cure my ailment and is not hurting me; it means a virtuous wife (as the ruler of Qi was warned day and night by a virtuous consort). Compare the previous entry: a Self line that is sound but held down by the Other line means a henpecked husband; a Self line that itself carries ill and is held down by the Other line means someone is correcting me.

> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 倘世爻自带兄官虎蛇等凶神者，反喜应来克世，谓之克我之凶，去我之病，主有贤妻，如齐襄公荒怠慢政，得陈贤妃，有夙夜警戒相成之道，故诗有《鸡鸣篇》。 | 倘若世爻自己带着兄弟、官鬼、白虎、螣蛇这些凶神，反而喜欢应爻来克世爻，这叫做克我的凶、去我的病，主有贤妻，就像齐襄公荒废懈怠政事，得到陈国的贤妃，有日夜警戒、彼此成全的规矩，所以《诗经》里有《鸡鸣》篇。 | If the Self line itself carries ill spirits such as Peer, Pressure, the White Tiger or the Coiling Snake, one is glad that the Other line comes to control it; this is called a control of me that removes my ill and cures my ailment, and it means a virtuous wife, as Duke Xiang of Qi, dissolute and slack in government, obtained the virtuous consort of Chen, who kept the way of warning him day and night and helping him toward success, and so the Odes have the poem 'Cock-crow'. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 同一个应克世：上一条（LY.MARRY.HJC.71）把它断成怕老婆，这一条断成有贤妻。分界在世爻自己：世爻本身平安而被应克，是被妻子压住；世爻本身带凶而被应克，是被妻子救回来。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The same Other line controlling Self is judged a henpecked husband in the previous entry (LY.MARRY.HJC.71) and a virtuous wife here. The dividing line is the Self line itself: a sound Self line held down by the Other line is pressed under the wife; a Self line that itself carries ill and is controlled by the Other line is rescued by the wife.

> [!note] 按
> 卜筮大全写「愿听鸡鸣」，卜筮正宗写「顾听鸡鸣」；注里的国君，卜筮大全是齐哀公，卜筮正宗是齐襄公。《诗经·齐风·鸡鸣》的小序说的是哀公，译文照各书原样。「鸡鸣」是妻子在天亮前催丈夫起来理事的诗。
>
> *Note:* The Quanshu writes 'glad to hear the cock-crow' and the Zhengzong 'willing to listen to the cock-crow'; the ruler in the note is Duke Ai of Qi in the Quanshu and Duke Xiang of Qi in the Zhengzong. The preface to 'Cock-crow' in the Odes (Qi Feng) speaks of Duke Ai; each book is translated as written. 'Cock-crow' is the poem of a wife urging her husband to rise before dawn and attend to affairs.
