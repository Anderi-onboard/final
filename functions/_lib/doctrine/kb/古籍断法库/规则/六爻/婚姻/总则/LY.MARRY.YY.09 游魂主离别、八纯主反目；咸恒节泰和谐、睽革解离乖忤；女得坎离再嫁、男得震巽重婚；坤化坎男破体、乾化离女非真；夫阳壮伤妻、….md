# 游魂主离别、八纯主反目；咸恒节泰和谐、睽革解离乖忤；女得坎离再嫁、男得震巽重婚；坤化坎男破体、乾化离女非真；夫阳壮伤妻、妇阴强欺夫；蒙不有其躬、渐夫征不复

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 游魂卦主离别，八纯卦主反目。咸、恒、节、泰的和谐，睽、革、解、离的乖违。女子得坎、离的再嫁，男子得震、巽的重婚。坤变坎，男子破体；乾变离，女子不真。丈夫阳气壮盛就伤妻子，妻子阴气强就欺丈夫。蒙卦是「不有其躬」，渐卦是「夫征不复」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Wandering Soul hexagram means separation, and a pure eight-palace hexagram falling out. Xian, Heng, Jie and Tai are harmonious; Kui, Ge, Xie and Li are discordant. A woman who gets Kan or Li marries again; a man who gets Zhen or Xun marries again. Kun changing into Kan: the man's body is broken; Qian changing into Li: the woman is not genuine. A husband with strong yang harms his wife; a wife with strong yin overrides her husband. Meng is 'not to have a body'; Jian is 'the husband goes on the expedition and does not return'.
