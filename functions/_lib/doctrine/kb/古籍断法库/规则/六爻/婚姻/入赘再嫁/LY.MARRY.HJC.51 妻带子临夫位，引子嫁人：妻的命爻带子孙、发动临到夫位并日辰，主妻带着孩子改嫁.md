# 妻带子临夫位，引子嫁人：妻的命爻带子孙、发动临到夫位并日辰，主妻带着孩子改嫁

六爻 · 婚姻 · 入赘再嫁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻子的命爻带着子孙、发动临到丈夫的位置，并且遇到日辰，主妻子带着孩子改嫁。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the wife's Life line carries Output, moves and sits on the husband's place, together with the Day, it means the wife brings her child along and marries.

## 《黄金策·家宅》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8071 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Chapter 19: The household", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻带子临夫位，引子嫁人； | 妻子带着子孙临到丈夫的位置，带着孩子嫁人； | The wife carrying Output and sitting on the husband's place: she brings her child and marries; |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8072 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻命带子孙，动临夫位，并日辰，主妻引子嫁人是也。 | 妻子的命爻带子孙，发动临到丈夫的位置，又遇日辰，主妻子带着孩子嫁人。 | If the wife's Life line carries Output, moves and sits on the husband's place together with the Day, it means the wife brings her child along and marries. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 3128 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 妻命带子孙，动临夫位并日辰，主妻引子嫁来是也。 | 妻子的命爻带子孙，发动临到丈夫的位置并遇日辰，主妻子带着孩子嫁过来。 | If the wife's Life line carries Output, moves and sits on the husband's place together with the Day, it means the wife brings her child along and comes to marry in. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注作「引子嫁人」，王洪绪作「引子嫁来」，一个是嫁出去，一个是嫁进来，字面不同。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary has 'brings her child and marries' and Wang Hongxu 'brings her child and comes to marry in', one going out and the other coming in; the wording differs.
