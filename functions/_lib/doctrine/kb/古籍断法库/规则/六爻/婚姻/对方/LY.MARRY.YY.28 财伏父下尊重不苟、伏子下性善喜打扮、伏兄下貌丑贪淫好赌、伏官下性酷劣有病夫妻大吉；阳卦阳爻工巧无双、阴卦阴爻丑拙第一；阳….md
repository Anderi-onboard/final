# 财伏父下尊重不苟、伏子下性善喜打扮、伏兄下貌丑贪淫好赌、伏官下性酷劣有病夫妻大吉；阳卦阳爻工巧无双、阴卦阴爻丑拙第一；阳化入阴幼巧长拙、阴化入阳小陋大姣

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻伏在父母爻下，为人庄重不苟；伏在子孙爻下，性善不损物，喜打扮；伏在兄弟爻下，貌丑，贪淫好赌；伏在官鬼爻下，性情残酷恶劣，有病，但夫妻大吉。阳卦阳爻，工巧无双；阴卦阴爻，丑拙第一。阳变入阴，小时灵巧、长大笨拙；阴变入阳，小时丑陋、长大美好。详见性情容貌篇。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth hidden beneath the Resource line: dignified and not careless; beneath Output: good-natured, does not waste goods, likes to dress up; beneath a Peer line: ugly, lustful and given to gambling; beneath Pressure: cruel and bad-tempered, ill, yet the couple is greatly auspicious. A yang trigram and yang line: skilled and clever beyond compare; a yin trigram and yin line: the ugliest and clumsiest. Yang changing into yin: clever when young and clumsy when grown; yin changing into yang: plain when small and lovely when grown. See the section on nature and looks.
