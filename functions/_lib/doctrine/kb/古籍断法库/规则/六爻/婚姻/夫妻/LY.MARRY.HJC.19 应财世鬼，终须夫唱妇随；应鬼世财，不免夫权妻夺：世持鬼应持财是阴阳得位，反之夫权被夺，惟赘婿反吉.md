# 应财世鬼，终须夫唱妇随；应鬼世财，不免夫权妻夺：世持鬼应持财是阴阳得位，反之夫权被夺，惟赘婿反吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世持鬼、应持财，是阴阳得位，丈夫掌男权、妻子守妇道，夫唱妇随；应持鬼、世持财，是阴阳失位，丈夫的权被妻子夺去，只有入赘的女婿反而吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self holding Pressure and the Other holding Wealth is yin and yang in their places, with the husband holding the man's authority and the wife keeping to her place, he leading and she following; the Other holding Pressure and the Self holding Wealth is yin and yang out of place, with the husband's authority seized by the wife, and only a son-in-law who marries into the wife's family finds it favourable.
