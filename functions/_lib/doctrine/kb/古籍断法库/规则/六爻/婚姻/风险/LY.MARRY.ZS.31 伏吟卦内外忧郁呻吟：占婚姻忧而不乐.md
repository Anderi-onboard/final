# 伏吟卦内外忧郁呻吟：占婚姻忧而不乐

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 伏吟（卦变之后每一爻的地支还是原来那个，例如无妄变大壮，戌仍是戌、申仍是申）主里外忧郁呻吟：内卦伏吟，里面不舒服；外卦伏吟，外面不安宁。占婚姻，是忧愁而不快乐。它比反吟轻，因为反吟有冲有克，伏吟没有，要看用神旺衰（见 LY.MARRY.ZS.32）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Hidden Chant (after the hexagram changes, every line still has the branch it had before; for example Wuwang into Dazhuang, where Xu stays Xu and Shen stays Shen) means gloom and groaning within and without: an inner trigram that is a Hidden Chant is ill at ease within, an outer one is unsettled without. For a marriage it means worried and unhappy. It is lighter than a Reversed Chant, because the Reversed Chant has clash and control and the Hidden Chant has neither; what matters is whether the Subject is strong (see LY.MARRY.ZS.32).

> [!note] 按
> 原文「伏吟卦者，有内外伏吟，如无妄变大壮」这一句没有句号，后面就是排盘图，这里只引这一句。无妄变大壮里，壬戌变庚戌、壬申变庚申、庚辰变甲辰、庚寅变甲寅，地支都没变，所以叫伏吟。
>
> *Note:* The sentence 'a Hidden Chant hexagram can be one with inner and outer both Hidden Chant, such as Wuwang into Dazhuang' has no full stop in the text and is followed by a chart, and only this sentence is quoted. In Wuwang into Dazhuang, Ren-Xu turns into Geng-Xu, Ren-Shen into Geng-Shen, Geng-Chen into Jia-Chen and Geng-Yin into Jia-Yin: the branches do not change, which is why it is called a Hidden Chant.
