# 身爻合处逢妻，娶婚必早：夫身爻合妻命爻则娶妻必早，妻身爻合夫命爻则出嫁必早

六爻 · 婚姻 · 应期 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 丈夫的身爻起来合妻子的命爻，娶妻一定早；妻子的身爻起来合丈夫的命爻，出嫁一定早。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the husband's Body line rises to combine with the wife's Life line, the marriage will surely come early; if the wife's Body line rises to combine with the husband's Life line, she will surely marry early.

## 《黄金策·家宅》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 8080 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Chapter 19: The household", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 身爻合处逢妻，娶婚必早。 | 身爻在相合的地方遇到妻，娶妻一定早。 | The Body line meeting the wife where it combines: marriage will surely come early. |
