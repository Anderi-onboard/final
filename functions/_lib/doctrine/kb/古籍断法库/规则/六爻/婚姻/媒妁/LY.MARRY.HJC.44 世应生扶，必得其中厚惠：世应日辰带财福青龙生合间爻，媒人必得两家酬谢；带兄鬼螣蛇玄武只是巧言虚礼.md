# 世应生扶，必得其中厚惠：世应日辰带财福青龙生合间爻，媒人必得两家酬谢；带兄鬼螣蛇玄武只是巧言虚礼

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应、日辰带财福、青龙，生合间爻，媒人一定得到两家的酬谢，旺相多、休囚少；带兄、鬼、螣蛇、玄武生合的，不过巧言虚礼，没有实惠；化出财福生合也吉；男看世爻，女看应爻；世应都生合，两家都有酬谢；世旺男家多，应旺女家多。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* When the Self and Other or the Day carry Wealth, Output or the Azure Dragon and generate or combine with the between line, the go-between surely receives thanks from both families, more if thriving and less if weak; if what generates or combines carries Peer, Pressure, the Coiling Snake or the Dark Tortoise, it is no more than fine words and empty courtesy without real benefit; changing out into Wealth or Output that generates or combines is also auspicious; a man looks at the Self line and a woman at the Other line; the Self and Other both generating or combining means both families give; the Self thriving means more from the man's family and the Other thriving more from the woman's.
