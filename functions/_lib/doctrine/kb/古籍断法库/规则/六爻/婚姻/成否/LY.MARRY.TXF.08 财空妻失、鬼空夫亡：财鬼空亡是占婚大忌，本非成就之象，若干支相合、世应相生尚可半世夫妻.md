# 财空妻失、鬼空夫亡：财鬼空亡是占婚大忌，本非成就之象，若干支相合、世应相生尚可半世夫妻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 财爻空是妻子失去，官鬼空是丈夫亡故。财、官同时空亡是占婚的大忌，本来不是能成的象；但若干支相合、世应相生，还能做半辈子夫妻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Wealth Void, the wife is lost; Pressure Void, the husband dies. Wealth and Pressure both Void is a great taboo in a marriage divination and by itself is not an image of success; but if the branches combine and Self and Other generate each other, they can still be husband and wife for half a lifetime.
