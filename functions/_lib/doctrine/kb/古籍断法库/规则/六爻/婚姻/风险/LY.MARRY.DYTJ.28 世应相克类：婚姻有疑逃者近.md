# 世应相克类：婚姻有疑逃者近

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世应相克的卦，断语是：宅墓高低，生孩子难；病多反复，出行艰难；婚姻有疑虑，逃跑的人就在近处；诉讼拖延，被牵连羁留。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* For a hexagram in which Self and Other control each other the verse says: with houses and graves high or low, childbirth is hard; illness advances and retreats and travel is hard; a marriage has doubts and the runaway is near; a lawsuit drags on and means being bound and held.
