# 刑合克合，终见乖淫：合中有刑有克，合而有克毕竟不和、合而有刑终成乖戾；午带自刑名刑合，占妻妾多不正；克合如丑子，占妻妾始合终背

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 合是和合，凡占见到都吉利。但人不知道合里面还有刑有克：合而有克，终究不和；合而有刑，终究乖戾。比如财爻是未、福德是午，午和未合，但午带自刑，叫刑合；又如财爻是子，子和丑合，丑土能克子水，叫克合。占妻妾，遇到刑合、克合，旧注说「多不正」，王洪绪说「始合终背，诸事终乖戾」。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A combination is a harmony and is lucky whenever it is seen in a divination. But people do not know that within a combination there are punishments and controls: a combination with a control is in the end not in accord; a combination with a punishment ends in perversity. For example, if Wealth is Wei and Output is Wu, Wu and Wei combine, but Wu carries self-punishment, which is called a punishing combination; or if Wealth is Zi, Zi and Chou combine, but Chou Earth can control Zi Water, which is called a controlling combination. In divining a wife or concubine, the old commentary says a punishing combination means she is 'mostly improper', and Wang Hongxu says a controlling combination means 'combined at first and turning against in the end, everything going perversely'.

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 同样的意思，旧注把「占妻妾多不正」说在刑合上，并且把例子里的午、未身份说反了（午火生未土，午应是福爻、未应是财爻）；王洪绪改对了身份，把「占妻妾始合终背」说在克合上。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The two notes attach the wife-or-concubine sentence to different cases: the old commentary puts 'mostly improper' on the punishing combination, and reverses the roles of Wu and Wei in its example (Wu Fire generates Wei Earth, so Wu should be Output and Wei Wealth); Wang Hongxu corrects the roles and puts 'combined at first and turning against in the end' on the controlling combination.

> [!note] 按
> 「自刑」是辰午酉亥各自相刑的那一类：午遇午就是自刑。旧注里的「褔」是「福」的异体字。这一条讲的是一般的合，占妻妾只是它的一个用处，收在这里是因为它给了合并非都吉的理由，可以和 LY.MARRY.DYTJ.03（合处逢冲）、LY.MARRY.BSZZ.02 一起读。
>
> *Note:* 'Self-punishment' is the kind in which Chen, Wu, You and Hai each punish themselves: Wu meeting Wu is self-punishment. The character used for 'fortune' in the old commentary is a variant form. This entry is about combinations in general, with the wife or concubine only one of its uses; it is included because it gives the reason a combination is not always lucky, and can be read with LY.MARRY.DYTJ.03 (a combination meeting a clash) and LY.MARRY.BSZZ.02.
