# 吉连沐浴，败而不成：吉神、用爻化入沐浴（败神）则败而不成；占婚姻最忌，男择婚得财爻化沐浴兼生则败门风、兼克则因奸杀身

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 吉神、用爻和喜爻化入沐浴（也叫败神），败而不成。占婚姻最忌它：男人择婚，财爻化成沐浴又带生的，必定败坏门风；带克的，会因为奸情丧命。各种占也一样：世爻化入沐浴，带生的因贪色败坏名声，带克的因奸情丧命，有救的从险里逃生。所以说吉神不可以化沐浴。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A lucky spirit, the Subject line or a welcome line that changes into Bathing, also called the spirit of ruin, is ruined and does not succeed. In a marriage divination it is most to be avoided: when a man chooses a marriage and the Wealth line changes into Bathing with generation, it will surely ruin the family name; with control, he will lose his life over adultery. It is the same in all divinations: if the Self line changes into Bathing, with generation one ruins one's name through lust, with control one loses one's life through adultery, and with rescue one escapes from danger. Hence a lucky spirit must not change into Bathing.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1237 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 沐浴，其名败神，又称沐浴煞，乃无廉无耻之神，其性淫败，然而有轻重之分别。 | 沐浴，名叫败神，又叫沐浴煞，是无廉耻的神，它的性子淫败，不过有轻重的分别。 | Bathing is called the spirit of ruin, also the Bathing star: a spirit without integrity or shame, licentious and ruinous by nature, though there are lighter and heavier cases. |
> | 即如金败于午，败中兼克； | 比如金败在午，败中带克； | For example, Metal is ruined at Wu, where the ruin also brings control; |
> | 寅木败于子，败中兼生； | 寅木败在子，败中带生； | Yin Wood is ruined at Zi, where the ruin brings generation; |
> | 卯木败于子，败中兼刑； | 卯木败在子，败中带刑； | Mao Wood is ruined at Zi, where the ruin brings punishment; |
> | 水败于酉，败中兼生； | 水败在酉，败中带生； | Water is ruined at You, where the ruin brings generation; |
> | 土败于酉，败中兼泄气； | 土败在酉，败中带泄气； | Earth is ruined at You, where the ruin brings a draining of qi; |
> | 火败于卯，败中兼生。 | 火败在卯，败中带生。 | Fire is ruined at Mao, where the ruin brings generation. |
> | 唯占婚姻最宜忌之。 | 只有占婚姻最应该忌它。 | Only in divining a marriage is it most to be avoided. |
> | 倘夫择婚姻，得财爻而化沐浴兼生者，必败门风，兼克者，因奸杀身。 | 如果男人择婚姻，得到财爻化成沐浴又带生的，必定败坏门风，带克的，因为奸情丧命。 | If a man chooses a marriage and gets a Wealth line that changes into Bathing with generation, it will surely ruin the family name; with control, he will lose his life over adultery. |
> | 即如诸占，倘世爻化之，生者因色坏名，克者因奸丧身，有救者险里逃生。 | 就像各种占一样，如果世爻化成它，带生的因为贪色败坏名声，带克的因为奸情丧命，有救的从险里逃生。 | As in all divinations, if the Self line changes into it, with generation one ruins one's name through lust, with control one loses one's life through adultery, and with rescue one escapes from danger. |
> | 故曰吉神不可化沐浴也。 | 所以说吉神不可以化沐浴。 | Hence it is said that a lucky spirit must not change into Bathing. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 旧注本（卜筮大全）里这一联后面有一个作者自己的卦例，本库没有收；「沐浴」是十二长生里的一位，「败神」「沐浴煞」是它的别名。
>
> *Note:* In the old commentary text (Bushi Daquan) this couplet is followed by a case of the author's own, which is not included here; 'Bathing' is one of the twelve life stages, and 'spirit of ruin' and 'Bathing star' are other names for it.
