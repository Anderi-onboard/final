# 女卜男婚当用官，财兴子静是良缘：女占夫以官爻为主，官旺为佳，鬼空莫用；嫌子动伤官，喜财兴助鬼

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 女子占男方的婚事，以官鬼爻为主，以应爻的象为凭。财爻兴旺、子孙爻安静，是好姻缘。官旺为佳，鬼爻空就不要用；又嫌子孙爻动伤官鬼，喜的是财爻兴旺来帮助鬼爻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A woman divining a man's marriage takes the Pressure line as the main thing and the sign of the Other line as evidence. With Wealth flourishing and Output still, it is a good match. A strong Pressure line is good, and if it is Void do not use it; a moving Output line hurting Pressure is disliked, and what is welcome is a flourishing Wealth line helping the Pressure.

> *Note:* From this passage to the chapter on pregnancy (chapter 14) the transcription has three short passages, each with a bracketed outline line (a woman divining, taking a son-in-law, heirs) but without chapter titles; they should be chapters 11 to 13 whose titles are missing.
