# 妻与应爻相合，外有私通：妻的身命爻与应爻相合又带咸池玄武桃花，主妻有外情；夫带日辰克妻与应爻，主捉到妻子的奸情

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 妻子的身命爻与应爻相合，又带咸池、玄武、桃花，主妻子在外有私情；丈夫带日辰克妻子和应爻，主捉到妻子的奸情。妻子带财生应爻，是妻子用钱引诱外人；应爻带财生妻爻，是外人用钱引诱妻子；应爻从外宫来，是远方的人，从内来，是近亲的人。世爻动带鬼隔断，是家里人阻隔；应爻动带鬼隔断，是外人阻隔他们的感情。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If the wife's Body or Life line combines with the Other line and carries the Xianchi star, the Dark Tortoise and the Peach Blossom star, the wife has a lover outside; if the husband, with the Day, controls the wife and the Other line, he catches his wife in adultery. If the wife carries Wealth and generates the Other line, the wife lures the outsider with money; if the Other line carries Wealth and generates the wife's line, the outsider lures the wife with money; if the Other line comes from an outer palace the man is from far away, if from within, a near relative. If the Self line moves carrying Pressure to cut things off, family members stand in the way; if the Other line moves carrying Pressure to cut things off, outsiders block the affection.

> [!note] 按
> 旧注里「夫并世应在三合之爻，主从良为娼」一句文字不通（从良本是娼妓嫁人），译文照字面写，含义不能确定。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary's sentence 'the husband and Self and Other on a three-harmony line means going from respectable to prostitute' does not read through, since the term for 'respectable' normally describes a prostitute leaving her trade to marry; it is translated literally and its meaning cannot be settled.
