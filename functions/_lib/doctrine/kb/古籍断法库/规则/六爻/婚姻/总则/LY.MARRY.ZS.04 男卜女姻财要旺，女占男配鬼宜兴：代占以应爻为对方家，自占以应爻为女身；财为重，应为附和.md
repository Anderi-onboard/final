# 男卜女姻财要旺，女占男配鬼宜兴：代占以应爻为对方家，自占以应爻为女身；财为重，应为附和

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 男方占，妻财要旺；女方占，官鬼要兴旺。代占的：男家占女，用财爻，应爻算女家；女家占男，用官鬼，应爻算男家。财、官要旺相，动了要化出吉象；应爻不该空、破，也不该克世（女家代占的应爻还不该入墓、绝）。男子自占娶妻：也用财爻，应爻兼算女子本人；财爻和应爻生世、持世、合世都吉，最忌破、墓、绝、空；财旺生世而应爻破空还可取，应爻生世而财爻破绝就不要求。野鹤补一句：财、应一起来生合世更吉；大体上财为重，应只是附和。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A man divining wants Wealth strong; a woman divining wants Pressure flourishing. For a divination on someone's behalf: when the man's family asks about the woman, use the Wealth line and take the Other line as her family; when the woman's family asks about the man, use the Pressure line and take the Other line as his family. Wealth or Pressure should be strong, and if it moves it should change into good; the Other line should not be Void or broken, nor control the Self line (in the woman's family's divination it should not be in a tomb or extinguished either). A man divining his own wife-to-be also uses the Wealth line and takes the Other line as the woman herself; Wealth and the Other line are good when they generate, hold or combine with the Self line, and most to be feared when broken, entombed, extinguished or Void; strong Wealth generating the Self while the Other line is broken or Void can still be taken, but if the Other line generates the Self while Wealth is broken or extinguished, do not seek it. Yehe adds: it is better still when Wealth and the Other line both come to generate or combine with the Self line; in general Wealth carries the weight and the Other line only follows along.

> | 若见相生相合，必然夫唱妇随。 | 如果看到相生相合，必定夫唱妇随。 | If they generate or combine with each other, the husband leads and the wife follows without fail. |

^q3

> [!warning] 打架
> 「应为百岁之妻」这句在《黄金策》「身命」章（占一生的那一章）的注里，不在它的婚姻章里。野鹤驳的是把它拿来占婚：重应而不重财是错的。夫妇章的正文替《黄金策》解释了为什么那样定（身命章一个卦要断六亲，财爻另有用处），也说占妻以财为重、应次之。两边都承认要兼看财爻，争的是谁为主。
>
> *Where they disagree:* The line 'the Other line is the wife of a hundred years' stands in the commentary of the Huangjin Ce's chapter on one's person and fate, not in its marriage chapter. Yehe attacks using it for marriage: stressing the Other line and not Wealth is wrong. The main text of the Zengshan Buyi's chapter on husband and wife explains why the Huangjin Ce fixed it that way (the person-and-fate chapter judges the six kin from a single hexagram, so Wealth had other work to do) and also says that in divining a wife Wealth carries the weight and the Other line comes second. Both sides agree that Wealth should be looked at as well; what they dispute is which comes first.
