# 十二宫断·夫妇宫：喜旺相得生，财临子值来助自身则吉；杀刃临爻多怪疾；衰空难保百年之姻

六爻 · 婚姻 · 夫妻 · 出处 [[易隐]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 十二宫里的夫妇宫：喜欢旺相、得到生扶，财爻临着、子孙爻值着来帮助自身；煞、刃临在这个爻上，多有怪病；衰弱落空，就难保百年的婚姻。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The husband-and-wife palace among the twelve: it is glad to be strong and generated, with Wealth on it and Output holding it to help the person; punishment and blade stars on the line bring much strange illness; weak or Void, a marriage of a hundred years is hard to keep.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 这是《易隐》引《黄金策》的一大段十二宫诗里的一句：先按生月、生时排出命宫，再排十二宫（命宫、兄弟、夫妻、子息、财帛、疾厄、迁移、奴婢、官禄、田宅、福德、父母），夫妇宫是第三位。排法在同一章前面，这里不收；这首诗其余各宫讲的是命、兄弟、子女、财帛等，也没有收。
>
> *Note:* This is one line of a long rhyme on the twelve palaces that the Yiyin quotes from the Huangjin Ce: first the life palace is set from the month and hour of birth, then the twelve palaces are laid out (life, brothers, husband and wife, children, wealth, illness, travel, servants, office, property, fortune, parents), the husband-and-wife palace being the third. The method of laying them out is earlier in the chapter and is not included here, nor are the lines of the rhyme on the other palaces (the life palace, brothers, children, wealth and so on).
