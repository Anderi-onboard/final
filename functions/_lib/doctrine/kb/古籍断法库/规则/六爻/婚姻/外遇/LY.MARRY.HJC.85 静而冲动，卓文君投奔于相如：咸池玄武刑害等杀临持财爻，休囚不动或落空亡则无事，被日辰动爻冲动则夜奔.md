# 静而冲动，卓文君投奔于相如：咸池玄武刑害等杀临持财爻，休囚不动或落空亡则无事，被日辰动爻冲动则夜奔

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 卦中咸池、玄武、刑害这类煞星临持在财爻上，如果休囚不动，或者落在空亡，大概没事；要是被日辰、动爻冲动，就像卓文君被司马相如用琴挑动，心动了，免不了夜里私奔相如，后来当垆卖酒。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* If ill stars such as the Xianchi star, the Dark Tortoise, punishment and harm sit on the Wealth line, then if it is resting and still or falls into Void it is probably safe; but if the Day or a moving line clashes and stirs it, she is like Zhuo Wenjun, whom Sima Xiangru stirred with the zither: her heart moves, and she cannot but elope to Xiangru by night, and later sell wine at the counter.

> [!note] 按
> 卓文君是卓王孙的女儿，新寡，听司马相如弹琴，夜里私奔与他成亲，后来在临邛开酒店，自己站柜卖酒。
>
> *Note:* Zhuo Wenjun was Zhuo Wangsun's daughter, newly widowed; hearing Sima Xiangru play the zither she eloped with him by night and married him, and later they kept a wine shop at Linqiong where she herself stood at the counter.
