# 娶妻先向财中觅，嫁夫可类鬼爻推：旺相得时成合配，休囚刑害不相宜；兄旺克妻妻不就，子旺伤夫夫有疑；八纯动者主离别，五世游魂损小儿

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[海底眼]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 娶妻先看财爻，嫁夫也照样看鬼爻。旺相得时就能配成，休囚或被刑害就不合适。兄弟爻旺克妻，妻子成不了；子孙爻旺伤夫，丈夫有疑。八纯卦动主离别，五世卦、游魂卦损害小孩。眉批的意思相同：财、鬼旺相成合，休囚不宜，兄旺克妻，子动伤夫。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To take a wife look first at the Wealth line; to marry a husband, look likewise at the Pressure line. If it is strong and in season the match is made; if resting, trapped, punished or harmed it is unsuitable. A strong Peer line controls the wife and she cannot be had; a strong Output line harms the husband and he is in doubt. A pure eight-palace hexagram that moves means parting; a fifth-generation or Wandering Soul hexagram harms the children. The marginal comment says the same: Wealth and Pressure strong, they combine; resting or trapped, unsuitable; a strong Peer line controls the wife; Output moving harms the husband.
