# 四刑：寅申巳亥为四刑，凡作百事一无成，婚姻官事俱不吉，纵得相生也不宁

六爻 · 婚姻 · 风险 · 出处 [[断易天机]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 寅、申、巳、亥是四刑：做什么事都不成，婚姻和官事都不吉，即使相生也不安宁（比如世爻寅、应爻申）。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Yin, Shen, Si and Hai are the four punishments: in doing anything at all nothing is accomplished, marriage and official matters are both unlucky, and even if they generate each other there is no peace (for example, Self at Yin and Other at Shen).
