# 来求乎：应病则不来；来辞乎：应实则不辞

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[易冒]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问对方会不会来求婚：应爻有病（受伤无力）就不会来；问对方会不会来推辞：应爻充实就不推辞。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* To ask whether they will come to ask for the match: if the Other line is sick, they will not come; to ask whether they will come to decline: if the Other line is solid, they will not decline.

## 《易冒·婚姻章第五十一》原文

> [!quote]+ [[易冒]] 第 1114 行 · Yi Mao, "Chapter 51: Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 来求乎， | 对方会不会来求婚？ | Will they come to ask? |
> | 应病则不来。 | 应爻有病就不会来。 | If the Other line is sick, they will not come. |
> | 来辞乎， | 对方会不会来推辞？ | Will they come to decline? |
> | 应实则不辞。 | 应爻充实就不推辞。 | If the Other line is solid, they will not decline. |

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「病」是爻受伤、无力，同一章里「官疾者」与「官实者」对举（LY.MARRY.YM.06）。「来求」「来辞」书里没说是哪一方来，译文不指明。
>
> *Note:* 'Sick' means a line that is injured and without strength; in the same chapter 'Pressure sick' is set against 'Pressure solid' (LY.MARRY.YM.06). The book does not say which side does the coming to ask or to decline, and the translation does not say either.
