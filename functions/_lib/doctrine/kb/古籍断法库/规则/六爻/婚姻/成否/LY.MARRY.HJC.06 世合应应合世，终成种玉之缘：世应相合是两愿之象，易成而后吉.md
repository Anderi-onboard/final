# 世合应应合世，终成种玉之缘：世应相合是两愿之象，易成而后吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 世和应相合、应和世相合，是男女两家都愿意的象，容易成，以后也吉利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* The Self and Other combining with each other is the image of both families willing; it is easy to bring about and auspicious later as well.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6324 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世合应应合世，终成种玉之缘。 | 世和应相合，应和世相合，终究能成种玉般的姻缘。 | The Self combines with the Other and the Other with the Self: in the end a match that plants jade is made. |

^q1

## 旧注(《卜筮全书》所载,注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6325 行 · Old commentary (printed in the Bushi Quanshu; commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世为男家，应为女家。 | 世是男家，应是女家。 | The Self is the man's family and the Other the woman's. |
> | 若得相合，是两愿之象，必主易成，后亦吉利。 | 如果得到相合，是两家都愿意的象，一定容易成，以后也吉利。 | If they combine, that is the image of both willing; it will surely be easy to bring about, and auspicious later. |

^q2

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 1996 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 男家卜，世为男家，应女家，若得相合是两愿之象，必主易成，后亦吉利。 | 男家占，世是男家，应是女家，如果得到相合，是两家都愿意的象，一定容易成，以后也吉利。 | When the man's family divines, the Self is the man's family and the Other the woman's; if they combine, that is the image of both willing, surely easy to bring about and auspicious later. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「种玉之缘」用杨伯雍种玉得妻的典故，指婚姻。
>
> *Note:* 'The match that plants jade' alludes to the story of Yang Boyong, who planted jade and won a wife, and stands for a marriage.
