# 世是平生之本，应为百岁之妻：应与青龙吉神并立则妻必贤，与咸池玄武并立则妻必淫；世爻无气受应克则妻夺夫权；应落空亡则妻宫有损

六爻 · 婚姻 · 对方 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡占，世爻是自己，应爻是妻子。应爻和青龙这样的吉神并立，妻子一定贤惠；和咸池、玄武并立，妻子一定淫乱。世爻没有气又被应爻克，一定是妻子夺了丈夫的权；应爻如果落在空亡，妻子这一宫有损失。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In every divination the Self line is oneself and the Other line is the wife. If the Other line stands together with the Azure Dragon or another lucky spirit, the wife is surely virtuous; if with the Xianchi star or the Dark Tortoise, the wife is surely lewd. If the Self line has no qi and is controlled by the Other line, the wife surely seizes the husband's authority; if the Other line falls into Void, the wife's place is damaged.

## 《天玄赋·身命章》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4132 行 · Tianxuan Fu (Rhapsody of the Heavenly Mystery), "The chapter on person and fate", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世是平生之本， | 世是一生的根本， | The Self line is the root of a whole life, |
> | 应为百岁之妻。 | 应是相伴百年的妻子。 | and the Other line is the wife of a hundred years. |

## 《天玄赋》注(注者不详)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 4135 行 · Commentary on the Tianxuan Fu (commentator unknown)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 世应二爻，乃一卦之主。 | 世爻和应爻两爻，是一个卦的主宰。 | The Self and Other lines are the masters of a hexagram. |
> | 凡占，以世为已，应为妻。 | 凡是占卜，世爻是自己，应爻是妻子。 | In every divination the Self line is oneself and the Other line is the wife. |
> | 若与青龙吉神并立，其妻必贤； | 如果（应爻）和青龙这样的吉神并立，他的妻子一定贤惠； | If it stands together with the Azure Dragon or another lucky spirit, the wife is surely virtuous; |
> | 咸池玄武并立，其妻必淫。 | 和咸池、玄武并立，他的妻子一定淫乱。 | if with the Xianchi star or the Dark Tortoise, the wife is surely lewd. |
> | 世爻无气受应克，必然妻夺夫权。 | 世爻没有气又被应爻克，一定是妻子夺了丈夫的权。 | If the Self line has no qi and is controlled by the Other line, the wife surely seizes the husband's authority. |
> | 应爻若落空亡，妻宫有损。 | 应爻如果落在空亡，妻子这一宫有损失。 | If the Other line falls into Void, the wife's place is damaged. |

^q2

> [!note] 按
> 这一段紧挨着的两段注（4133、4134）讲的是世爻本身的贵贱和世空，不涉及婚姻，没有收。同样一句「应为百岁之妻」，《黄金策》身命章里的注（LY.MARRY.HJC.69）和《增删卜易》夫妇章（LY.MARRY.ZS.24）另有说法。
>
> *Note:* The two notes directly between the verse and this one (lines 4133 and 4134) are about the rank of the Self line itself and a Void Self line, not about marriage, and are not included. The same phrase 'the Other line is the wife of a hundred years' is glossed differently in the Huangjin Ce chapter on person and fate (LY.MARRY.HJC.69) and in the Zengshan Buyi chapter on husband and wife (LY.MARRY.ZS.24).
