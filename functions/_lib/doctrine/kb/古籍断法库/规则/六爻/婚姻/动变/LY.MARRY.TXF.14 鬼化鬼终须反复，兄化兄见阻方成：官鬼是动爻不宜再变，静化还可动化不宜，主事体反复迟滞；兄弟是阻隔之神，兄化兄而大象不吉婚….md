# 鬼化鬼终须反复，兄化兄见阻方成：官鬼是动爻不宜再变，静化还可动化不宜，主事体反复迟滞；兄弟是阻隔之神，兄化兄而大象不吉婚难成

六爻 · 婚姻 · 动变 · 出处 [[天玄赋]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 官鬼化官鬼，终究反复；兄弟化兄弟，受阻才成。官鬼本来是动爻，不宜再变动，静化还行、动化不宜，必定事情反复迟滞；兄弟是阻隔之神，兄弟又化兄弟，如果大象不吉，婚事难成。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Pressure changing into Pressure ends in going back and forth; Peer changing into Peer is made only after obstruction. Pressure is a moving line to begin with and should not change further; changing from stillness may pass, changing while moving is unsuitable, and the matter surely goes back and forth and is delayed. Peer is the spirit of obstruction; if Peer changes into Peer and the overall image is not lucky, the marriage is hard to make.
