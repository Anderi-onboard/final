# 财官动合，先私而后公：财官两爻动合，先通后娶；临玄武眉目传情未通；财与旁爻合，与他人有情

六爻 · 婚姻 · 外遇 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 以官为丈夫、财为妻子；两爻都动而相合，一定是先私通后成婚；遇冲克，外人已经知道；临玄武，只是眉来眼去、还没有通情意；本人自占，财和世爻动而相合，也是这样；财爻与旁爻相合，是与别人有情；财遇合多、又化出子孙，一定是妓家出身，因为化子孙有从良的象。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Take Pressure as the husband and Wealth as the wife; if both lines move and combine, they were surely intimate in secret before they married; if a clash or control meets them, outsiders already know; on the Dark Tortoise, it is only glances and looks and feelings have not yet been exchanged; when one divines for oneself, Wealth and the Self line moving and combining is the same; if the Wealth line combines with another line, she has feelings for someone else; Wealth meeting many combinations and turning into Output means she is surely from a brothel, since turning into Output carries the image of leaving that life to marry.

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2006 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 夫占以财为妇，世与动合，是必先通而后娶，财与世爻动合亦然。 | 男子占，以财为妻子，世和它动而相合，一定是先私通后娶，财和世爻动而相合也是这样。 | A man divining takes Wealth as the wife; if the Self combines with it by moving, they were intimate in secret before marrying, and the same if Wealth and the Self line combine by moving. |
> | 财爻动与旁爻合，与他人有情，财遇合多亦然。 | 财爻动而与旁边的爻相合，是与别人有情，财遇到相合多的也是这样。 | If the Wealth line moves and combines with another line, she has feelings for someone else, and the same if Wealth meets many combinations. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 旧注把「财官」读作男女两方（官为夫，财为妇）；这一条只对男占女、女占男这一类占法成立，用神照占者身份定，见 HJC.45。
>
> *Note:* The old commentary reads 'Wealth and Pressure' as the two parties (Pressure the husband, Wealth the wife); this holds only for the ordinary man-divining-woman or woman-divining-man question, and the Subject is fixed by who divines, as HJC.45 says.
