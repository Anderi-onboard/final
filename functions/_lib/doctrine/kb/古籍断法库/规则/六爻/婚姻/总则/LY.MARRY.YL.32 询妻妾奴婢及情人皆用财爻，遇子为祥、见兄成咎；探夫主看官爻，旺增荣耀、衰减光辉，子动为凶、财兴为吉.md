# 询妻妾奴婢及情人皆用财爻，遇子为祥、见兄成咎；探夫主看官爻，旺增荣耀、衰减光辉，子动为凶、财兴为吉

六爻 · 婚姻 · 总则 · 出处 [[易林补遗]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 问妻妾、奴婢和情人，都用财爻，遇到子孙是吉祥，见到兄弟就成灾祸。探问丈夫和文武官员的身命，要察看官爻：旺就增加荣耀，衰就减少光辉；子孙动是凶，财爻兴旺是吉。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* In asking about a wife or concubine, maids and servants, or a lover, the Wealth line is always the Subject; meeting Output is lucky, meeting Peer brings blame. In inquiring after a husband and the person and fate of civil and military officials, look at the Pressure line: strong, it adds honor; weak, it dims the luster; Output moving is ill and Wealth rising is lucky.

^q1

> [!note] 按
> 「夫主」是丈夫；「子动云凶，财兴曰吉」是说官鬼这个用神怕子孙来克、喜财爻来生。同一段的前面是父母、尊长、子侄、僧道、兄弟朋友的用爻，后面是自身和他人，没有收。
>
> *Note:* 'Fu zhu' is the husband; 'Output moving is ill, Wealth rising is lucky' means that Pressure, as the Subject, fears being controlled by Output and is glad to be generated by Wealth. The earlier part of the passage gives the lines for parents and elders, sons and nephews, monks and Taoists, brothers and friends, and the later part oneself and others, none of which is included.
