# 应或相生，乃女家之瓜葛；世如相合，必男室之葭莩：间爻与世生合多男家亲、与应生合多女家亲；旺相新亲、休囚旧眷、本宫至亲、他宫外亲

六爻 · 婚姻 · 媒妁 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 间爻与世生合，多是男家的亲戚；与应生合，多是女家的亲戚；世应都被生合，两家都有亲；旺相是新亲，休囚是旧眷，本宫是至亲，他宫是外亲。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A between line generating or combining with the Self is mostly kin of the man's family, with the Other mostly kin of the woman's, and with both, both families are related to it; thriving means new relatives, weak means old ones, the home palace means nearest kin, another palace means outside kin.

## 《黄金策·婚姻》原文

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮大全]] 第 6423 行 · Huangjin Ce (The Golden Strategy), "Marriage", base text
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 应或相生，乃女家之瓜葛； | （间爻）若与应相生，是女家的亲戚关系； | If it generates the Other, it is a tie to the woman's family; |
> | 世如相合，必男室之葭莩。 | 若与世相合，必是男家的远亲。 | if it combines with the Self, it is surely a distant kinsman of the man's household. |

## 王洪绪注(《卜筮正宗》)

> [!quote]+ [[卜筮正宗]] 第 2056 行 · Wang Hongxu's commentary (Bushi Zhengzong)
> | 原文 | 译文 | English |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 间爻与世生合，言我家亲，与应生合言彼家亲，与世应俱生合，两家皆有亲也。 | 间爻与世生合，是说我家的亲戚，与应生合，是说对方家的亲戚，与世应都生合，两家都有亲。 | A between line generating or combining with the Self means kin on my side, with the Other kin on the other side, and with both, both families are related. |
> | 旺相新亲，休囚旧眷，本宫至亲，他宫外亲。 | 旺相是新亲，休囚是旧眷，本宫是至亲，他宫是外亲。 | Thriving means new relatives, weak means old ones, the home palace nearest kin, another palace outside kin. |

^q3

> [!note] 按
> 「瓜葛」是瓜蔓藤葛缠绕，比喻亲戚关系；「葭莩」是芦苇里的薄膜，比喻疏远的亲戚。经文里省了主语「间爻」，注里补出。
>
> *Note:* 'Melon and vine' is a tangle of creepers, standing for ties of kinship; 'reed membrane' stands for a distant relative. The verse omits its subject, the between line, which the commentary supplies.
