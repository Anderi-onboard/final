# 六合则易而且吉，六冲则难而又凶

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[黄金策]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 六合卦一阴一阳配合成象，世应相生、财官相合，容易成而且吉；六冲卦不是纯阴就是纯阳，像两个女人同住、两个男人并处，志向合不来，难成，成了也不利。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* A Six-Combine hexagram is one yin matched with one yang, with the Self and Other generating each other and Wealth and Pressure combining, and is easy to bring about and auspicious; a Six-Clash hexagram is pure yin or pure yang, like two women living together or two men side by side whose aims cannot agree, hard to bring about, and unfavourable even if it succeeds.
