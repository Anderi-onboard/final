# 凡得诸合，诸占皆吉、占婚必成，但要用神有气，用神失陷合也无济；吉事逢合必就，凶事逢合难结

六爻 · 婚姻 · 成否 · 出处 [[增删卜易]]

> [!abstract] 规则(整理,不是原文)
> 凡是得到各种合（日月合爻、爻与爻合、爻动化合、卦逢六合等），占什么都算吉，占婚姻一定成。但必须用神有气才相宜；用神如果失陷，合了也没有用。卜书说，万事得三合或六合，事情一定长久、有始有终；不过好事碰上合，事情一定做成，坏事碰上合，事情就难以了结。
>
> *Rule (our summary):* Whenever any kind of combination is obtained (a line combining with the Day or Month, a line with a line, a moving line changing into a combination, the hexagram itself being a Six-combine, and so on), every kind of divination counts it auspicious, and in a marriage divination the match is sure to be made. But it is fitting only when the Subject has qi; if the Subject is lost or fallen, the combination does not help. The divination books say that with a three-harmony or Six-combine in everything, matters will surely last, with a beginning and an end; but a good matter meeting a combination is sure to be made, whereas an ill matter meeting a combination is hard to bring to an end.

> [!note] 按
> 这一章开头把合分成六种：日月合爻、爻与爻合、爻动化合、卦逢六合、六冲卦变六合、六合卦变六合。第四种的原文放在最前面，第五种另见 LY.MARRY.ZS.27。「合起」「合绊」「合好」「化扶」四个名词在这一章里另有解释，这里不收。
>
> *Note:* The chapter opens by dividing combinations into six kinds: the Day or Month combining with a line, a line with a line, a moving line changing into a combination, a hexagram meeting Six-combine, a Six-clash hexagram changing into a Six-combine one, and a Six-combine hexagram changing into another. The text of the fourth kind is given first; the fifth is at LY.MARRY.ZS.27. Four terms the chapter explains further (a combination that raises, one that binds, one that makes friends, one that helps by change) are not included here.
