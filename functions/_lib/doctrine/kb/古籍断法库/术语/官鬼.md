# 官鬼 · Pressure

*guānguǐ*

六亲之一,克我者。求财时一般主阻隔、官非、盗贼;但它克兄弟,能护财;空手求财、公门求财时反而以它为主。

One of the six relations: the element that controls the Self. In money questions it usually means obstruction, officials, lawsuits or thieves; but it controls Peer and so can protect the money, and for skill-based or official money it becomes the main line.

原文里写作「官鬼」「鬼爻」「官爻」时,英文一律译作 **Pressure**。
