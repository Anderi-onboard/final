# 伏神 · hidden line

*fúshén*

卦里没有出现的六亲,到本宫首卦里找,伏在某一爻下面。

A relation missing from the hexagram, looked up in the head hexagram of the palace; it lies hidden under one of the lines.

原文里写作「伏神」时,英文一律译作 **hidden line**。
