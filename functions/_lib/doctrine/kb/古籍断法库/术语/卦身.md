# 卦身 · Body

*guàshēn*

按月卦身法取的一爻,代表所占之事的「体」。野鹤老人说它不验,只看世爻。

A line picked by the month-body method that stands for the matter itself. The Old Man of Yehe held it unreliable and used the Self line instead.

原文里写作「卦身」时,英文一律译作 **Body**。
