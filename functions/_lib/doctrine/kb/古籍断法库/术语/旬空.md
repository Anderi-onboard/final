# 旬空 · Void

*xúnkōng*

起卦那一旬里轮空的两个地支。落空的爻暂时不作数,出旬或逢冲才实。

The two branches left out of the current ten-day cycle. A void line does not count for now; it becomes real when the cycle passes or it is clashed.

原文里写作「旬空」「空亡」时,英文一律译作 **Void**。
