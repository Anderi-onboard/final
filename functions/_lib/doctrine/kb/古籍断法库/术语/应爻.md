# 应爻 · Other

*yìngyáo*

代表对方(对手、交易的人、被求的人)的那一爻。

The line that stands for the other party: the counterpart, the buyer or seller, the person being asked.

原文里写作「应爻」时,英文一律译作 **Other**。
