# 子孙 · Output

*zǐsūn*

六亲之一,我生者。它生妻财,所以求财时叫「财源」「福神」,是财的原神;又克官鬼,能解灾。

One of the six relations: the element the Self generates. It generates Wealth, so in money questions it is the source of money (the Support of the Wealth line); it also controls Pressure and so relieves trouble.

原文里写作「子孙」「福神」「福德」时,英文一律译作 **Output**。
