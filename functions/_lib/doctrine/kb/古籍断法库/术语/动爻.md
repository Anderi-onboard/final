# 动爻 · moving line

*dòngyáo*

摇出老阴(交)、老阳(重)的爻,会变。书上说「交重」就是发动。

A line cast as old yin (jiao) or old yang (zhong); it changes. The classics' jiaozhong simply means moving.

原文里写作「动爻」时,英文一律译作 **moving line**。
