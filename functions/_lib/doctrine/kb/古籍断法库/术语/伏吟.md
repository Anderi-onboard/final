# 伏吟 · Hidden Chant

*fúyín*

卦变之后每一爻的地支还是原来那个(如无妄变大壮,戌仍是戌),主忧郁呻吟、动如不动;比反吟轻,没有冲克。

A hexagram whose moving lines change into the very same branches they had (for example Wuwang into Dazhuang, where Xu stays Xu): groaning, gloom, movement that gets nowhere. Lighter than Reversed Chant, since there is no clash or control.

原文里写作「伏吟」时,英文一律译作 **Hidden Chant**。
