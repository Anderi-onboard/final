# 墓库 · tomb

*mùkù*

五行的墓地(水土墓辰、木墓未、火墓戌、金墓丑)。入墓主收藏、困住,冲开墓才出。

The tomb (storehouse) of each element: Water and Earth in Chen, Wood in Wei, Fire in Xu, Metal in Chou. Entering the tomb means being stored or shut in; it comes out when the tomb is clashed open.

原文里写作「墓库」时,英文一律译作 **tomb**。
