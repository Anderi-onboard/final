# 三合局 · three-harmony frame

*sānhéjú*

申子辰水、亥卯未木、寅午戌火、巳酉丑金四局,三爻合成一种五行。

Three branches that together form one element (Shen-Zi-Chen Water, Hai-Mao-Wei Wood, Yin-Wu-Xu Fire, Si-You-Chou Metal).

原文里写作「三合」时,英文一律译作 **three-harmony frame**。
