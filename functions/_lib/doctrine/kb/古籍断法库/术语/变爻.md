# 变爻 · changed line

*biànyáo*

动爻变出来的那一爻。「甲化乙」就是甲爻动,变出乙。动为事之始,变为事之终。

The line a moving line turns into. 'A turns into B' means line A moves and becomes B. The moving line is how the matter starts; the changed line is how it ends.

原文里写作「变爻」时,英文一律译作 **changed line**。
