# 劫财 · robber of wealth

*jiécái*

占财时对兄弟的叫法。注意:「劫杀」在《天玄赋》里另指一个神煞,不是兄弟,看上下文。

What money questions call the Peer line. Note: in the Tianxuan Fu the same word names a separate spirit-star, not the Peer line.
