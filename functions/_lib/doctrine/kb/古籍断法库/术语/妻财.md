# 妻财 · Wealth

*qīcái*

六亲之一,我克者。求财以它为用神;男占婚姻也以它为妻。

One of the six relations: the element the Self controls. The Subject line for money questions (and the wife in a man's marriage question).

原文里写作「妻财」「财爻」时,英文一律译作 **Wealth**。
