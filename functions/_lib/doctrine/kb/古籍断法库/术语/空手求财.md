# 空手求财 · earning without capital

*kōngshǒu qiú cái*

不出本钱,靠手艺、服务、差事、人情挣钱(九流、工匠、衙役、抽丰)。以官鬼为主顾。

Earning without putting money in: by a trade, a service, an office or a patron. Here the Pressure line is the paying client.
