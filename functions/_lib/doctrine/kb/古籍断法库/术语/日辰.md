# 日辰 · Day

*rìchén*

起卦那一天的地支,能生克冲合各爻,也定旬空。

The branch of the day of casting; it can generate, control, clash with or combine any line, and fixes which branches are void.

原文里写作「日辰」时,英文一律译作 **Day**。
