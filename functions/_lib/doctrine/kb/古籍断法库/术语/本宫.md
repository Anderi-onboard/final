# 本宫 · home palace

*běngōng*

卦所属的八宫之一。伏神从本宫首卦里取。

The palace (one of eight) the hexagram belongs to; hidden lines are taken from its head hexagram.

原文里写作「本宫」时,英文一律译作 **home palace**。
