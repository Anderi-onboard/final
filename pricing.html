Common questions

Everything you need before you commit a balance.

Do units expire?
Plan units refresh each billing month and do not roll over. One-time top-up units never expire while your account stays active.
What happens when I run out?
Inquiries pause until you add units or your plan renews. Nothing is lost—your history and open castings remain intact.
Can I change or cancel a plan?
Switch tiers or cancel anytime from your account. Changes apply at the next billing cycle; remaining units stay on your balance.
Are units refundable?
Once an inquiry is processed and a framework is generated, those units are spent and non-refundable. Unused balances follow the refund policy.
What's the difference between Stria and Sortis?
Stria 64 maps your immediate situation at 300 units. Sortis 6 evaluates dynamic permutations for high-stakes decisions at 1,500 units—powered by a more advanced reasoning engine.
Is my data private?
Inquiries are encrypted at rest, never sold, never used to train broad models, and purged on request.
◆  The fine print, stated plainly

Units are consumable digital assets. Once an inquiry is processed and a structural framework is generated, the analysis is delivered and the units are spent. They are non-refundable.
