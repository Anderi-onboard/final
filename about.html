About us

We built the part that cannot be talked out of its answer.

A reading here has two halves that never touch. Six lines fall, and they are fixed. Then Claude explains the figure that fell — the one you actually got, in the situation you actually described. Everything below is what follows from keeping those two apart.

One

What a reading is made of

Separation

Six lines fall before a single word is written about them. Nothing you typed moves them, and nothing written afterwards can move them back.

Evidence

Every answer carries the figure it was written from, so you can read the source of it and not only the conclusion.

Restraint

A view, not a verdict. It tells you what to go and check; it will not decide it for you.

Depth

You choose how much structure the question needs — one figure, or one figure and what it turns into.

Two

What you actually get

A whole reading

Your first one is on us, and it is a whole reading rather than a sample of one. It starts, and it finishes.

Your own words

Ask in whatever language you think in. The answer comes back in the one you asked in.

You pay for what ran

No plan to keep up and nothing held back in advance. A balance, and questions you spend it on. A reading that has started always finishes.
